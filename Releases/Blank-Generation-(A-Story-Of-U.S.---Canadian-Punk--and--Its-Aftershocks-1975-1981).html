Blank Generation (A Story Of U.S. / Canadian Punk & Its Aftershocks 1975-1981)

5-CD compilation surveying American and Canadian punk, proto-punk, and new wave from 1975 to 1981. Released 2023 on Cherry Red, cat# CRCDBOX147. Discogs release ID: 28954984.

Overview

130 tracks across 5 discs, sequenced roughly chronologically but mixing New York, Cleveland, San Francisco, Los Angeles, and Canadian scenes throughout. Compiled by Jon Harrison (who also wrote the foreword liner notes); mastered by Alec Palao. Design by Keith Davey; project management by Jon Harrison and John Reed.

The set functions as a wide-angle survey — anchored by canonical names (Ramones, Patti Smith, Television, Blondie, Suicide, Devo, Dead Kennedys) but covering deep cuts from regional scenes: Cleveland proto-punk (Pere Ubu, Electric Eels, Mirrors, Styrenes), Midwest (Zero Boys, Dow Jones And The Industrials, The Embarrassment), Canadian (Pointed Sticks, Young Canadians, Demics, Modernettes), Athens GA (Pylon, The Method Actors), Boston (Mission Of Burma, DMZ, Lyres), and LA hardcore adjacents (Germs, Adolescents, X, Weirdos, Black Randy).
