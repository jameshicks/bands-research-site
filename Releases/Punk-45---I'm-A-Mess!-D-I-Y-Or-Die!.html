Overview

Part of Soul Jazz Records’ long-running Punk 45 reissue series (sublabel ID: 660794), this volume focuses on the D-I-Y and art-punk end of the UK punk explosion — regional and independent releases from 1977–78, often one-off 45s pressed on tiny labels or self-financed by the bands themselves. The title track is “I’m A Mess” by Stormtrooper (4).

The compilation documents the geography of UK punk beyond the London centre: Birmingham (The Killjoys), Glasgow (Johnny And The Self Abusers), Coventry, and various regional scenes operating without major-label infrastructure. Several tracks appear here as their primary reissue context.

No compiler is credited on Discogs for any pressing. Soul Jazz’s Punk 45 series is typically curated by Stuart Baker (label founder), though this is not confirmed in the DB for this specific volume.

Notable Artists and Connections

Pre-fame appearances

  • Johnny And The Self Abusers — “Saints And Sinners” is the sole single by the Glasgow band, released the day they split in 1977. The full lineup — Jim Kerr (vocals), Charlie Burchill (lead guitar), Brian McGee (drums) — immediately reformed as Simple Minds. Already documented in vault from Action Time Vision (A Story Of Independent UK Punk 1976-1979).

  • The Killjoys — “Naive” features Kevin Rowland on vocals. The Killjoys were the immediate precursor to Dexys Midnight Runners. Also appears in Action Time Vision (different track: “Johnny Won’t Get To Heaven”).

  • The Electric Chairs — “So Many Ways” is produced by David Cunningham (of The Flying Lizards). Wayne/Jayne County’s band; Wayne County is in the vault at Wayne County.

  • Neon Hearts — “Venus Eccentric” features Paul Raven on bass (Discogs ID: 22534). Raven subsequently joined Killing Joke, Ministry, Godflesh, and Prong — a remarkable post-punk-to-industrial trajectory from this DIY 45.

  • Lucy (4) — “Feel So Good” features Phil Collen (Discogs ID: 367829, realname Philip Kenneth Collen) on guitar. Collen went on to join Def Leppard in 1982, becoming one of their principal guitarists.

  • Johnny Moped — “No One” (same track as in Action Time Vision). Early lineup included Ray Burns (Discogs ID: 762393), who became Captain Sensible of The Damned.

  • V2 (5) — “Speed Freak” features Steve Brotherdale (drums), who also drummed in The Panik (1977). Also appears in Action Time Vision.

Producer credit

  • David Cunningham (Discogs ID: 41910) — produced “So Many Ways” by The Electric Chairs. Cunningham is the founder of The Flying Lizards and a key figure in post-punk experimentalism; his appearance as producer on a punk 45 is an early credit.

Compilation Series Context

This volume sits within Soul Jazz’s Punk 45 series, which spans multiple volumes of UK and international punk 45s. Soul Jazz approaches this material the same way it approaches its reggae and funk reissues: sourced from original pressings, compiled thematically, with essay notes situating the tracks in their original context.

Companion compilations in the vault:

  • Action Time Vision (A Story Of Independent UK Punk 1976-1979) — Cherry Red 4xCD, 2016; broader scope, overlaps several artists (Johnny And The Self Abusers, The Killjoys, V2, The Carpettes, Johnny Moped, Neon Hearts, Social Security, The Art Attacks)
  • Moving Away From The Pulsebeat (Post-Punk Britain 1977-1981) — features The Art Attacks and The Electric Chairs
  • Optimism - Reject (UK D-I-Y Punk and Post-Punk 1977-1981) — Cherry Red, related D-I-Y territory
