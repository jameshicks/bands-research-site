Punk 45 — Approaching The Minimal With Spray Guns

Limited-edition box set of five 7” 45 RPM reissues documenting New York no wave, Cleveland art-punk, Los Angeles deathrock, and midwest punk. Released 2018 on Soul Jazz Records (sublabel: Punk 45), cat# SJR 408 Box. Discogs release ID: 11900175. A Record Store Day release.

Overview

Five records, each dedicated to a single artist or pairing of artists. The title track (Record One Side B) is an X-X original. The compilation takes its name from the X-X track “Approaching The Minimal With Spray Guns.” Music spans late 1970s / early 1980s US underground: Cleveland no wave (X-X), Akron Ohio punk (Bizarros, Rubber City Rebels), Los Angeles deathrock (The Flesh Eaters), and UK art-punk (The Scabs), plus LA punk (The Hollywood Squares).

The box is part of Soul Jazz’s ongoing Punk 45 series (sublabel ID: 660794), which has focused on obscure international punk 7” reissues and anthologies since the mid-2000s.
