Notes

Göransson produced and engineered half the album (tracks 1, 2, 5, 8, 12) and mastered the full record. He also played bass on tracks 2, 5, and 8, and electric guitar on track 12. The other tracks were engineered by Ben Blank (tracks 4, 6, 7) and Mark Phillips (4) (tracks 3, 9–11), with Gurf Morlix mixing tracks 4, 6, 7.

This is an Austin, TX-local project — all credited musicians are Austin-area figures. Jean Caffeine self-produced the overall album alongside Göransson’s track-level co-production.

Notable Austin musicians appearing: Jon Dee Graham, Joey Shuffield and Tony Scalzo (both of Fastball), Gurf Morlix, Champ Hood, Dennis Ku, Mick Buck.
