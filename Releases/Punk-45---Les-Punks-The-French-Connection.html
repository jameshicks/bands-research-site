Punk 45: Les Punks — The French Connection (The First Wave Of French Punk 1977–80)

Label: Soul Jazz Records / Punk 45 sublabel
Cat no: SJR LP354
Format: 2×LP, Compilation
Year: 2016
Country: UK
Discogs release ID: 9324491 — Master ID: 1111436
Genres/Styles: Rock / New Wave, Punk
Barcode: 5 026328 003542

Part of Stuart Baker’s Punk 45 series, released on the dedicated Soul Jazz Records Punk 45 sublabel (Discogs ID: 660794). Nineteen tracks across four sides, covering the first wave of French punk from 1977 to 1980. The compilation ranges from Paris-based no-wave-adjacent acts to Lyon post-punkers, with some tracks appearing here from obscure regional labels (Egg, Rebel Records, Cobra, Melodies Massacre) as well as early UK pressings on Rough Trade.

No compiler is separately credited on the Discogs release entry; as with the broader Punk 45 series, curatorial authorship is implied as Stuart Baker.

Artists

Marie Et Les Garçons (Discogs ID: 101677)

Lyon post-punk group; Michel Esteban’s first signing at SPY Records. Two tracks here: “Rien À Dire” (their debut 1977 single on Rebel Records) and “A Bout de Souffle.” Represented on both the opening and closing side. Members: Marie Girard, Philippe Girard, Erik Fitoussi, Christian Faye, Jean-Pierre Charriau, Jean-Marc Vallod, Patrick Vidal. Connected to Electric Callas via shared membership (Marie Girard, Jean-Marc Vallod, Philippe Girard). See full article.

Metal Boys (Discogs ID: 204079)

Also known as Métal Boys. Members: Charles Hurbier, Eric Débris, Hermann Schwartz. Connected to Métal Urbain — Hurbier, Débris, and Schwartz are all Métal Urbain members; Metal Boys is a side/related project. Two releases in Discogs: “Sweet Marylin” (Rough Trade, 1979) and Tokio Airport (Celluloid, 1980).

Fantomes (Discogs ID: 3202858)

Also known as The Phantoms. Members: Henri Paul, Paul Gray (2), Steve Nicol. Covers the Stooges track “I Wanna Be Your Dog.” Sparse Discogs footprint.

Gazoline (Discogs ID: 942815)

Paris punk group. Members include: Alain Kan, Fred Chichin (later of Les Rita Mitsouko), André Asse, Didier Laffont, Olivier Burger, Pierre-Jean Cayatte, Steve-Henry Peeters. Two releases: “Killer Man / Radio Flic” and “Sally” (both on Egg, 1977). Fred Chichin’s presence here connects the early punk scene to later French pop-rock.

A 3 Dans Les WC (Discogs ID: 1003264)

Also: A Trois Dans Les WC, Walter Closet, WC3. Nord-based group (Lille area). Members: Eric Comont, Janine (4), Jean-Christophe, Jeannot (2), Reno Isaac. Active website; noted in nordwaves.fr documentation of the northern French punk scene.

Asphalt Jungle (3) (Discogs ID: 932540)

Paris punk group. Members include Didier Laffont (also Gazoline), Pierre-Jean Cayatte (also Gazoline), Patrick Eudeline, Rikky Darling (also Métal Urbain), Riton (2), Patrice Le Morvan, The Mental Job, Chino (52). Three 7” singles 1977–78 on Cobra, Skydog, and Pathé. Patrick Eudeline went on to become a prominent French rock journalist.

Warm Gun (Discogs ID: 364884)

Members: François Camuzeaux (also Dogs), Frédéric Lemarchand, Jean-Paul Ruad, Patrick Lemarchand, Philippe Karlui, Thierry Dioniso. François Camuzeaux’s dual membership in Warm Gun and Dogs connects both groups.

Métal Urbain (Discogs ID: 683196)

Also known as Metal Urbain. Paris punk/industrial group; one of the most internationally known acts from the French first wave. Members: Charles Hurbier, Eric Débris, Hermann Schwartz, Oliver Votelet, Rikky Darling. “Paris Maquis” was also included on the Soul Jazz compilation Moving Away From The Pulsebeat (Post-Punk Britain 1977-1981). Debut single on Cobra (1977); Rough Trade pressed “Paris Maquis” for UK release (1977). LP Les Hommes Morts Sont Dangereux (Celluloid/Byzz, 1981). See vault stub.

Electric Callas (Discogs ID: 656093)

Lyon post-punk group; documented in full at Electric Callas. “Kill Me Two Times” was their Skydog Commando compilation track (1978). Members include Jangil Callas, Marie Girard, Jean-Marc Vallod, Philippe Girard.

Kas Product (Discogs ID: 155291)

Also known as KaS. Members: Mona Soyoc, Daniel Favre. Synth-punk duo, Strasbourg. Signed to RCA Victor (France) from 1981. “Mind” is their debut 7” on Punk Records (1980). Their run of RCA albums (1981–1990) represents one of the longest careers of any first-wave French punk act. Active into the 2020s (reissue 2022, new release 2025).

84 Flesh (Discogs ID: 331638)

Also: 1984 (22), 84. Members: Frédéric Cousseau, Henry Flesh, Jean-Louis Winsberg, Michel Bellocq, Pierre Cousseau. “Salted City” is their sole charted release. Jean-Louis Winsberg later had a jazz career.

Les Olivensteins (Discogs ID: 655609)

Paris punk group, named after controversial physician and author Léon Zitrone Olivenstein. Members: Alain Royer, Clément Lagrega, Eric Tandy, France Vitet, Gilles Tandy, Ludovic Groslier, Romain Denis, Vincent Denis. Self-titled LP (Mélodies Massacre, 1979). Reissued 2011 on Born Bad Records. Notable for aggressively French-language punk with literary/political edge.

Angel Face (2) (Discogs ID: 896990)

Members: Denis Alvarez, Eric Tende, Frédéric Cousseau (also 84 Flesh), Henry Flesh (also 84 Flesh), Hervé Zénouda (also Guilty Razors), Julien D.R. Farrey, Kim Nguyen, Pascal Bermond, Pascal Regoli, Riton Angel Face. The membership overlap with 84 Flesh and Guilty Razors suggests a tight-knit Parisian sub-scene.

Guilty Razors (Discogs ID: 444508)

Paris punk group; two tracks here (sides C and D). Members: Carlos Perez (13), Djill Bourezak, Hervé Zénouda (also Angel Face (2)), Jano Homicid, José Perez, Pascal Dequatremare. French Wikipedia article exists. Heroic for being one of the more multicultural-named groups in the French first wave.

Dogs (Discogs ID: 262774)

Also: The Dogs. One of the more substantial French punk acts by recorded output — around 20 releases 1977–2023 spanning Mélodies Massacre, Philips, Epic, and later reissue labels. Members include François Camuzeaux (also Warm Gun), Dominique Laboubée, Bruno Lefaivre, Christian Rosset, and others. “Charlie Was A Good Boy” (Mélodies Massacre, 1977) was their debut.

Charles De Goal (Discogs ID: 204080)

Also: CDG, C.D.G., De Goal. Members: Etienne Lebourg, Jean-Philippe Brouant, Patrick Blain, Thierry Leray, Vincent Guilluy. Long career — debut on New Rose Records 1980 (Algorythmes), then label home through the 1980s. Active into 2016 (Danger Records). Not strictly “first wave” in sound — more post-punk / cold wave, but chronologically covered by the 1977–80 window via their formation.

Calcinator (Discogs ID: 1306642)

Members: Jef Ettedguy, Mick Giani, Pat Vandenbroeck. Belgian-origin punk group; minimal Discogs footprint. “Électrifié” is their only catalogued release.

Cross-Scene Connections

Several personnel threads run across this compilation’s bands:

  • Métal Urbain / Metal Boys overlap: Eric Débris, Charles Hurbier, and Hermann Schwartz are members of both. Metal Boys was effectively a concurrent project out of the same group.
  • Gazoline / Asphalt Jungle overlap: Didier Laffont and Pierre-Jean Cayatte appear in both bands.
  • Métal Urbain / Asphalt Jungle overlap: Rikky Darling appears in both.
  • 84 Flesh / Angel Face (2) overlap: Frédéric Cousseau and Henry Flesh in both.
  • Angel Face (2) / Guilty Razors overlap: Hervé Zénouda in both.
  • Warm Gun / Dogs overlap: François Camuzeaux in both.
  • Marie Et Les Garçons / Electric Callas overlap: Marie Girard, Jean-Marc Vallod, Philippe Girard across both groups.
  • Gazoline / Les Rita Mitsouko: Fred Chichin (Gazoline) later co-founded Les Rita Mitsouko with Catherine Ringer.
