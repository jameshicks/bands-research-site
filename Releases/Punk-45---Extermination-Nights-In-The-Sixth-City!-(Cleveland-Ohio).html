Punk 45: Extermination Nights In The Sixth City! Cleveland, Ohio: Punk And The Decline Of The Mid-West 1975–82

Compilation LP/CD. Released 2015 on Soul Jazz Records. Compiled by Stuart Baker. Sleeve notes by Jon Savage.

Discogs: LP release ID 6584853 (cat# SJR LP300); CD release ID 6728006 (cat# SJRCD300)
Master ID: 807456
Country: UK
Genre: Rock / Punk

Overview

Part of Soul Jazz’s long-running Punk 45 series, this volume focuses exclusively on Cleveland, Ohio — one of the most distinctive and historically dense proto-punk and punk scenes in North America. The compilation covers 1975–1982 and documents the overlapping cluster of groups that emerged from Cleveland’s art school and bohemian underground: Pere Ubu, Electric Eels, Rocket From The Tombs, Mirrors, The Styrenes, Pagans, and a handful of lesser-documented acts.

The “Sixth City” of the title is Cleveland’s historical nickname. Sleeve notes by Jon Savage, the British music journalist and historian best known for England’s Dreaming (1991).

Artists On This Compilation

Pagans (3 tracks)

Cleveland punk band, active c. 1977–1979. Three tracks here: “Street Where Nobody Lives,” “Dead End America,” “I Juvenile.” Discogs ID: 426529. Members on Discogs include Mike Hudson and Mike Metoff.

Pere Ubu (2 tracks)

Cleveland avant-rock group, formed 1975. Two tracks: “Final Solution” (5:00) and “Heart Of Darkness” (4:45) — both proto-punk era pieces co-written by the founding lineup including Peter Laughner, David Thomas (2), Tom Herman, Scott Krauss, Tim Wright (2). “Final Solution” also credits Craig Bell and Dave Taylor. Discogs ID: 83071.

Electric Eels (2 tracks)

Cleveland proto-punk band, c. 1972–1975. Two tracks: “Splittery Splat” (written by Brian McMahon) and “Bunnies” (written by McMahon, John Morton, and Paul Marotta). Discogs ID: 252896.

The Styrenes (2 tracks)

Credited under two different names: as “Poli Styrene Jazz Band” on “Drano In Your Veins” (written by Paul Marotta) and as “Styrene Money” on “Everything Near Me” (written by Jamie Klimek). Discogs ID: 529193.

Scene Context

The Cleveland underground documented here is one of the most tightly interconnected proto-punk networks in the US. Several players appear across multiple acts:

  • Peter Laughner — co-founder of both Rocket From The Tombs and Pere Ubu; died 1977
  • John Morton — Electric Eels, x_x, The Styrenes
  • Jamie Klimek — Mirrors, Electric Eels, The Styrenes (wrote “Everything Near Me” here)
  • Paul Marotta — Electric Eels, Mirrors, The Styrenes; primary Electric Eels archivist
  • Dave E. McManus — Electric Eels, Jazz Destroyers
  • Craig Bell — Rocket From The Tombs, x_x; also co-writes “Final Solution” for Pere Ubu
  • Tim Allee — Pagans, Broncs
  • Anton Fier — Electric Eels (expanded lineup), The Styrenes, Pere Ubu
  • David Thomas (2) — Rocket From The Tombs, Pere Ubu

The overlap between Rocket From The Tombs (1974–75) and the subsequent formation of Pere Ubu is directly audible here: “Final Solution” and “Heart Of Darkness” were both Rocket From The Tombs-era compositions performed and recorded as Pere Ubu.
