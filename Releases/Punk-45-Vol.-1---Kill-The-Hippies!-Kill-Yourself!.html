Punk 45 Vol. 1 — Kill The Hippies! Kill Yourself! The American Nation Destroys Its Young

Full title: Punk 45: Kill The Hippies! Kill Yourself! The American Nation Destroys Its Young — Underground Punk In The United States Of America, 1973–1980 Vol. 1
Label: Soul Jazz Records (SJR LP272 / SJR CD272)
Format: 2xLP + CD
Released: 2013
Country: UK
Discogs master ID: 647438 | LP release ID: 5180346 | CD release ID: 5339466
Genre: Rock / Punk

Soul Jazz’s first volume in the Punk 45 series dedicated to American underground punk. Compiled by Stuart Baker (though Discogs lists no individual compiler credit; the series runs under the Soul Jazz / Punk 45 sublabel imprint). Spans 1973–1980 and draws from the Cleveland proto-punk scene, the Los Angeles punk wave, New York No Wave/punk, Texas, the Bay Area, and Philadelphia.

The LP edition is sequenced across four sides (A–D); the CD edition is a single continuous sequence of the same 21 tracks.
