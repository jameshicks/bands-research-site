Overview

March Records was a Chicago, Illinois indie and alternative rock label that operated from 1992 to 2004. Its catalogue of 50 listed releases is heavily concentrated in the 1990s (36 releases) and tapers into the early 2000s (14 releases), spanning indie rock, indie pop, synth-pop, and pop rock. The roster included producer Dave Trumfio, French pop artist Bertrand Burgalat, and veteran pop figure Terry Jacks alongside Chicago acts. No parent label or corporate structure has been recorded.
