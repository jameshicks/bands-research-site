Overview

Emergency Hearts (stylised eMERGENCY heARTS) is an Austin, Texas anarchist-run independent music collective and media project, operating across a deliberately wide stylistic brief: post-punk, darkwave, industrial, EBM, ambient, noise, trance, techno-hiphop, and experimental electronics, among others. The catalogue traces back to a single 1987 release before the label effectively went quiet, resuming in the 2010s with nine releases and then surging to nearly a hundred in the 2020s — an expansion that dwarfs everything that came before it. The roster connects to figures including Scott Crow (Austin anarchist organizer and author) and Mark Pistel, suggesting ties to both the activist left and the electronic/industrial underground.
