Federal Prisoner

Discogs ID: 1478886 Founded: 2018 (first Discogs releases) Based: Los Angeles, California Release count: 98 (Discogs)

Independent label founded by The Black Queen — primarily Greg Puciato — to release their second album Infinite Games (2018) and all subsequent Puciato solo and collaborative work. Described by Puciato as “an expression of passion and individualism” and “as much an act of refusal as it is a statement of intent.”

The label functions as the primary home for Puciato’s solo catalog post-Dillinger Escape Plan and also hosts releases by Jesse Draxler (as a primary artist — Reigning Cement, 2020) and other collaborators from the TBQ orbit.
