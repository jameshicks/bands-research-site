Overview

Sire Records Company is the corporate entity that owned the Sire brand from 1979 onward, distinct from the Sire label itself — releases bearing the Sire logo should be credited to that label rather than this entity. The catalogue attributed to Sire Records Company spans the 1980s and 1990s, covering styles including alternative rock, indie rock, synth-pop, house, and dance-pop, with roster credits that include Madonna and Babyface. It also appears under the variant name Sire Records Co.
