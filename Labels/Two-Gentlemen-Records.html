Overview

Two Gentlemen Records is a Swiss independent label founded in 2009 by Patrick David and Christian Fighera, operating under the Gentlemen Records umbrella with a sublabel also called Two Gentlemen. Its catalogue of around 42 releases spans alternative rock, industrial, folk rock, and indie, with activity concentrated sharply in the 2010s (32 releases) and continuing into the 2020s. The roster centres on prominent Swiss acts: The Young Gods and frontman Franz Treichler, Sophie Hunger, and drummer Bernard Trontin.
