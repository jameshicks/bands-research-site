Dutch label that mostly releases officially licensed reissues of classic albums on vinyl. It is a co-operation between distributor Bertus and Record Industry (who press all releases), which are all divisions of Artone Label Group.

Use Music On Vinyl B.V., when the company is credited on release as such.

— Discogs label profile, ID 169635

Overview

Music On Vinyl is a Dutch reissue label operating under the Artone Label Group umbrella, formed as a collaboration between the distributor Bertus and the pressing plant Record Industry. Active since 2007, its catalogue spans officially licensed vinyl reissues across rock, pop, soundtrack, and score recordings. The label operates several dedicated sublabels including Music On Vinyl Classical and the Ennio Morricone Classic Soundtrack Series, and its releases regularly carry mastering credits from engineers such as Bob Ludwig and George Marino.
