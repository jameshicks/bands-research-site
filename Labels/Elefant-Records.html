Elefant Records

Spanish indie-pop label based in Madrid. Discogs label ID: 5634. Parent entity: Elefant Records S.L.

Major Spanish label for indie-pop, with over 1,300 releases on Discogs. Home to The School (2), Trembling Blue Stars (The Seven Autumn Flowers, 2004), and numerous Spanish indie acts. Ian Catt has produced releases on Elefant continuously from 2000 through at least 2025, making it one of his longest-running label relationships outside Saint Etienne.
