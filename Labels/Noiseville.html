Label from Yonkers, NY, USA.

Owned by Jim Gibson and started in 1988 as a mail order company selling indie label records. In 1989 it started releasing records: mostly 7” singles and almost always limited editions. For several years Noiseville had a store in NYC. In later years, Noiseville released primarily experimental, noise, and doom projects.

Overview

Noiseville is an independent label founded by Jim Gibson in Yonkers, NY, beginning in 1988 as a mail-order outlet for indie records before launching its own releases in 1989 — concentrated heavily on limited-edition 7” singles. It ran a retail store in New York City for several years and built a catalogue across punk, noise, and psychedelic rock, with its peak output in the 1990s. In later years the label pivoted toward experimental, noise, and doom territory, reflected in sublabels including Doom-Mantra Records and Shitsville. Its last release came in 2013, and Noiseville formally ceased trading in 2015.
