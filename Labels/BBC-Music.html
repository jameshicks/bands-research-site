Overview

BBC Music is the BBC’s in-house label responsible for music released across its broadcast services, officially sitting within the BBC’s Radio operational division while also covering television and online output. Its catalogue spans over 180 releases from 1985 onward, with the heaviest output in the 1990s and 2000s, dominated by classical repertoire — Romantic, Modern, Baroque, and to a lesser extent Pop Rock — largely performed by BBC’s own orchestras: the BBC Symphony Orchestra, BBC Scottish Symphony Orchestra, and BBC National Orchestra of Wales. The label operates several imprints including BBC Music Magazine, the Archive Series, and Jazz Legends. It is a subsidiary of BBC Worldwide.
