Overview

Happy Happy Birthday To Me Records is an independent label founded in 1999 by Mike Turner in Panama City, FL, now headquartered in Athens, GA, and operating under the short-form name HHBTM Records. It grew out of Turner’s zine The Bee’s Knees, with its inaugural release a compilation CD bundled with the zine’s 12th issue; four subsequent zine issues (13–16) also received HHBTM catalogue numbers without accompanying CDs. The label released most heavily in the 2010s (53 releases) and spans indie pop, indie rock, lo-fi, and punk, with a roster that has included David Gedge and The Wedding Present alongside Jason Nesmith and Terry De Castro. It ran singles club sub-labels in 2000 and 2007.

Relocation: Panama City, FL to Athens, GA

What is known

Mike Turner moved the label to Athens, GA in 2004, and the move was personal — he relocated because most of his friends and his label’s roster were already living there. The exact wording from the Bloodbuzzed blog (January 2016), which is the clearest source on record: “since most of his friends & his label’s roster were already living there, Mike decided to move to Athens, Georgia, in 2004.” (Bloodbuzzed interview, Jan 2016)

The move appears to have been a formalisation of existing community ties rather than a pivot: the label’s roster was already Athens-adjacent before Turner himself relocated. On arrival he threw a four-day party to celebrate, which became the annual Athens Popfest — a festival he continued to organise, centred on the label’s roster, Elephant 6 artists, and Athens-local acts. (Bloodbuzzed interview, Jan 2016)

Turner also became manager of Wuxtry Records, described as “one of the top indie record stores in the country” and historically linked to R.E.M.’s early career, which deepened his integration into the Athens independent music scene. (Bloodbuzzed interview, Jan 2016)

Direction shift

No source found states an explicit change in the label’s direction after the move. The roster was already Athens-affiliated before 2004; the move consolidated Turner’s physical presence in the community he was already operating within. The founding of Athens Popfest post-move can be read as a deepening of that community identity rather than a new direction. The label’s stylistic focus (indie pop, indie rock, lo-fi, punk) was present from its Panama City origins and continued unchanged.

The Wedding Present connection

The Wedding Present and David Gedge first had documented contact with the Athens scene when the band played the 2010 Athens PopFest (Nashville Scene preview, 2010). Athens PopFest was organised by HHBTM’s Mike Turner (7) (Nashville Scene), so the festival appearance placed Gedge directly in Turner’s orbit roughly six years before the first release.

The formal label relationship began in 2016 with a run of three simultaneous releases: a vinyl reissue of The Wedding Present’s Take Fountain (HHBTM176), The Wedding Present’s new double album Going, Going… (HHBTM182), and a vinyl reissue of Gedge’s Cinerama project Valentina (HHBTM175). According to Gedge in a December 2016 interview with Big Takeover, the label’s role was specifically North American vinyl distribution, and the approach came from HHBTM’s side: “They’ve now put out several of our releases on vinyl for North America. They just approached us and seemed like good people!” (Big Takeover, Dec 1 2016)

Gedge also noted the label’s ethos appealed to him: “I think they do it for all the right reasons.” (Big Takeover, Dec 1 2016)

The relationship has been sustained and prolific. Subsequent HHBTM releases include the George Best 30 anniversary LP (HHBTM190, 2017), Tommy 30 (HHBTM203, 2019), Locked Down And Stripped Back (HHBTM210, 2021), Locked Down And Stripped Back Volume Two (HHBTM221, 2022), and the triple LP 24 Songs (HHBTM225, 2023).

Note on the brief: The original brief identified the label as run by Robert Schneider. Per the HHBTM about page and Discogs, the label is run by Mike Turner (HHBTM about page). Fred Schneider (of the B-52s) is an artist on the label. Robert Schneider is a separate figure (of The Apples In Stereo, also an HHBTM-adjacent artist). No connection between Robert Schneider and label management was found.
