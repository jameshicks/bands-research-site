Rune Grammofon

Discogs ID: 1502
Location: Akersgaten 7, 0158 Oslo, Norway
Founded: 1998
Founder: Rune Kristoffersen
Sublabels: Rune Arkiv (ID: 42800), The Last Record Company (ID: 150964)
Contact (historical): rune@grappa.no

Norwegian avant-garde, jazz, and experimental label. Founded by Rune Kristoffersen under the aegis of Manfred Eicher of ECM, though independently distributed. All releases issued in digipak format with exclusive design by Kim Hiorthøy (Discogs artist ID: 19822).

Founding and Overview

Rune Grammofon launched in 1998 with a cluster of releases that immediately established its aesthetic: free improvisation, electroacoustic experiment, and a distinctive graphic identity tied entirely to artist Kim Hiorthøy, whose cover designs appear on 492 of the label’s Discogs-credited releases. The label’s first catalog entry was Supersilent’s debut triple-CD 1-3 (RCD 2001), followed closely by composer Arne Nordheim’s Electric and the Biosphere/Deathprod split tribute Nordheim Transformed (RCD 2005), which established the label’s dual interest in contemporary Norwegian classical and electronic improvisation.

The founder Rune Kristoffersen is also a member of Fra Lippo Lippi (Discogs artist ID: 148395). His connection to ECM’s Manfred Eicher shaped the label’s commitment to quality recording and deliberate release cadence, though Rune Grammofon has never been an ECM subsidiary.

The label’s catalog as of 2025 spans roughly 170+ unique master entries on Discogs (817 total release entries including pressings). Releases use catalog number prefixes: RCD (standard CD), RLP (vinyl LP), RSI (single/EP), RCDS (compilation series), RALP/RACD (archival/reissue series for Motorpsycho), MPCD/MPLP (Motorpsycho-specific releases co-released with the band).

Design Identity

Every Rune Grammofon release carries sleeve work by Kim Hiorthøy (Discogs ID: 19822), a Norwegian graphic artist, musician, and filmmaker. His distinctive visual language — spare, typographically rigorous, often abstract or painterly — has made the label’s physical catalog instantly recognizable. Hiorthøy is credited across design, cover, illustration, photography, and even film directing roles on the catalog. His consistency across 26+ years is the label’s most distinctive visual signature.

Recurring Production Personnel

Helge Sten (Deathprod)

Discogs ID: 214775. The label’s most central production figure. Credited as engineer, mixer, mastering engineer, and producer across 158 releases as engineer and 41 as “other” contributor. Runs his Audio Virus Lab studio in Oslo. Produced the earliest Supersilent albums and has functioned as the label’s de facto house producer from 1998 onward. He is simultaneously an artist on the label himself as Deathprod.

Kim Hiorthøy

Discogs ID: 19822. Sole graphic designer for the entire catalog — 492 design credits. Norwegian artist/filmmaker who also has conductor/booklet editor credits on several releases. His visual identity is inseparable from the label’s brand.

Heino Leja / Sector 5 Studios

See Heino Leja. Lacquer cutting engineer at Sector 5 Studios (Optimal Media GmbH, Röbel/Müritz, Germany). Credited on 75 Rune Grammofon releases as “Lacquer Cut By.” The second most prolific cutter at Sector 5 for this label is Marko Kluge (ID: 12807917) with 49 credits. Combined, Sector 5 handles the majority of the label’s vinyl cutting.

Rune Kristoffersen

Discogs ID: 296377. Label founder and head. Producer credit on 83 releases (as executive producer, producer, or co-producer). Member of Fra Lippo Lippi.

Mats Gustafsson

Discogs ID: 184421. Swedish free jazz saxophonist; 59 “other” role credits and 27 writer credits on the label. Central figure in the Fire! and Fire! Orchestra projects. Appears as conductor, baritone/tenor saxophone performer, and composer.

Audun Strype

Discogs ID: 300685. Oslo-based mastering engineer; 51 mastering credits on the label, particularly concentrated in the early catalog (1998–2010s).

Bob Katz

Discogs ID: 383292. New York mastering engineer; 41 credits, primarily mid-2000s through 2010s.

Catalog Range and Scope

~170+ unique masters, 817 total Discogs release entries. Active 1998–present. Genre spread:

  • Free improvisation / free jazz: Supersilent, Bushman’s Revenge, Scorch Trio, Fire!, Spunk, MoHa!, Ultralyd, Paal Nilssen-Love
  • Jazz-rock / organ trio: Elephant9, Hedvig Mollestad Trio, Humcrush
  • Electroacoustic / dark ambient / drone: Deathprod, Phonophani, Alog, Helge Sten projects
  • Contemporary jazz: Espen Eriksen Trio, Arve Henriksen, Albatrosh
  • Art-pop / experimental song: Jenny Hval, Susanna, Susanna And The Magical Orchestra, Anneli Drecker
  • Prog rock / psychedelic rock: Motorpsycho (the label’s highest-volume artist at 69 releases)
  • Norwegian classical: Arne Nordheim, Fartein Valen
  • Avant-rock / noise: Stian Westerhus, Krokofant, The Last Hurrah!!

Compilation series: Runeology (RCDS prefix) — at least 3 volumes through 2007.

Sublabels

Rune Arkiv (ID: 42800)

Archive and reissue sublabel, primarily used for Motorpsycho back-catalog releases. Catalog prefixes RACD (archive CD) and RALP (archive LP). Active from at least 2003. Discogs catalog includes reissues of Fra Lippo Lippi, Motorpsycho (Demon Box, Timothy’s Monster, Blissard, etc.), and Motorpsycho-adjacent archival material (e.g., Roadwork live series). The RALP 3xx series on Rune Arkiv runs in parallel with the main RLP 3xx series.

The Last Record Company (ID: 150964)

Specialty sublabel. Only one Discogs entry: Stian Westerhus’s debut Galore (2009, TLRC 1). Appears to have been a short-lived or project-specific imprint.
