Overview

Nitro Records is an independent punk label founded in 1994 by Offspring members Dexter Holland and Greg Kriesel as a vehicle to sign and develop like-minded acts. Its catalogue of roughly 72 releases spans Punk, Hardcore, Pop Punk, and Alternative Rock, with output peaking in the 2000s (41 releases) after a solid founding decade in the 1990s (31 releases). AFI is the label’s most prominent alum, and roster credits for Jade Puget, Davey Havok, and Adam Carson reflect the band’s sustained relationship with Nitro across multiple releases. The label was acquired by The Bicycle Music Company in 2013 and its catalogue now sits under the Craft Recordings umbrella.
