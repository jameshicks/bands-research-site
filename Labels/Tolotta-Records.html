Tolotta Records

Discogs label ID: 61461. Independent record label based in Arlington, Virginia (mailing address: PO Box 4412, Arlington, VA 22204, USA). Founded and run by Joe Lally of Fugazi.

Overview

Tolotta Records was Joe Lally’s personal imprint, operated alongside his work in Fugazi and later solo projects. According to its Discogs profile, Lally used the label to reissue out-of-print albums and release new material by bands he was friendly with or admired. The label’s two primary active artists were Dead Meadow and Spirit Caravan; when Dead Meadow moved to another label and Spirit Caravan dissolved, Lally put the imprint to rest. The label was revived once, as a co-imprint on Lally’s 2011 solo album Why Should I Get Used To It, jointly released with Dischord Records and Desmonta.

The catalog spans 1994 to 2011 with 19 pressings across 14 distinct masters, covering stoner rock, doom metal, noise rock, and math/noise. The earliest releases (1994) include the Sevens EP and a Stinking Lizaveta single, suggesting Lally was operating the label even during Fugazi’s most active period. The bulk of the catalog appeared in 1999–2002, centered on Spirit Caravan (three releases, including Elusive Truth in both vinyl and CD formats), Dead Meadow (two albums), and Stinking Lizaveta (multiple releases). One-off releases include the Tolotta reissue of The Obsessed’s self-titled album (2000) and Orthrelm’s Iorxhscimtor (2001).

Lally served as executive producer on the Spirit Caravan and Obsessed releases. The production duties on most catalog items fell to Chris Kozlowski (engineer/producer across Spirit Caravan releases), with Shelby Cinca and Bruce Kane sharing mix duties on Dead Meadow records.

Key Artists

The Obsessed (Discogs ID: 311946)

Washington DC doom/heavy rock band led by Scott “Wino” Weinrich. The Tolotta release is a reissue of their 1990 self-titled album (originally Hellhound Records, Germany), augmented with live recordings captured by Kozlowski. Wino played guitar, sang, and produced the original sessions; bass by Mark Laue, drums by Ed Gulli. The Obsessed’s Discogs entry lists Spirit Caravan as an alias — the same core lineup (Wino, Sherman, Isom) continued in Spirit Caravan. The band has released material on Invictus Records (1983 demo), Hellhound Records (1990–91), Columbia (1993–94), Southern Lord (1999–2001), and Relapse Records (2017 reunion).

Members (Discogs): Wino (Scott Weinrich), Dave Sherman (4), Ed Gulli, Mark Laue, Guy Pinhas, Scott Reeder, Greg Rogers, and others across lineup changes.

Spirit Caravan (Discogs ID: 907855)

Stoner/doom band formed by Wino (Scott Weinrich) after The Obsessed. Core lineup on Tolotta releases: Wino (guitar, vocals), Dave Sherman (4) (bass, vocals), Gary Isom (drums, vocals). Discogs lists Shine (21) and The Obsessed as aliases of Spirit Caravan — all three share the same core membership. Tolotta released three Spirit Caravan records: Jug Fulla Sun (1999), Elusive Truth (2001), and the So Mortal Be / Undone Mind single (2002). The band also released on MeteorCity and other labels. Lally served as executive producer on Jug Fulla Sun, Elusive Truth, and So Mortal Be / Undone Mind. After Spirit Caravan dissolved, Tolotta went dormant.

Dead Meadow (Discogs ID: 460608)

Washington DC psych/stoner rock band. Core lineup on Tolotta records: Jason Simon (guitar, vocals), Steve Kille (bass, sitar), Mark Laughlin (drums, congas). Tolotta released their debut album Dead Meadow (2000) and second album Howls From The Hills (2001). The band self-designed artwork on Howls From The Hills alongside Steve Kille. After Howls, Dead Meadow moved to another label (Matador); their Tolotta-era departure prompted Lally to close the imprint. Name variation on Discogs: “Shivering King And Others.”

Members (Discogs): Jason Simon, Steve Kille, Mark Laughlin, Cory Shane, Stephen McCarty.

Stinking Lizaveta (Discogs ID: 651330)

Philadelphia-based instrumental/noise-rock trio. Three-piece: Yanni Papadopoulos (guitar), Alexi Papadopoulos (electric upright bass), Cheshire Agusta (drums). The earliest Tolotta release is a Stinking Lizaveta single (1994, Refinery / Crooked Teeth), recorded by Steve Albini. They then released III (2001), …Hopelessness And Shame. (undated), and Slaughterhouse (2002) on Tolotta — four releases in total, the most of any artist on the label.

Orthrelm (Discogs ID: 214040)

DC math/noise duo: Mick Barr (guitar) and Josh Blair (drums). Tolotta released Iorxhscimtor (2001) in both CD and vinyl formats. No additional credits on the release beyond the two members (Barr also did artwork).

Sevens (Discogs ID: 758393)

DC indie band including Joe Lally (bass), Bobby Sullivan, Mark Sullivan (4), Joshua Larue, and Christopher Farrall. Tolotta released their 777 EP (1994, cat# TRC101), produced by Brendan Canty. (Separate from a later 1996 Sevens release on Akashic Records.)

Shine (21) (Discogs ID: 1145109)

Pre-Spirit Caravan project listed separately on Discogs as an alias. Lost Sun Dance (1997, TRC103) features the same lineup as Spirit Caravan (Wino, Dave Sherman, Gary Isom) plus producer Chris Kozlowski and assistant John Koutsioukis.
