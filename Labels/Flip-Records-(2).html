Flip Records (2)

UK. Short-lived imprint of Fire Records, used in 1995 for a handful of archival/compilation releases. Discogs catalogs all FLIP catalog numbers under Fire Records (label ID 2051) — there is no separate Discogs entity for this imprint; it exists as a catalog sub-series rather than a registered sublabel. The FLIP prefix denotes Flip Records branding printed on the physical releases, mastered at Mayking.

The imprint issued exactly three masters in 1995: two archival compilations on CD only, and one on both CD and double-vinyl. All releases were tied to artists with prior history on Fire Records or its related imprint Paperhouse Records.

Release Notes

Yes Darling, But Is It Art? (Early Singles And Rarities) — Television Personalities

  • 24-track CD compiling early TV Personalities singles
  • Primary artist: Daniel Treacy (songwriter credit for all songs)
  • Credits: liner notes by Clive Solomon and John Reed; sleeve by Alison Wonderland (2); design by Roland Mince
  • Matrix: FLIPCD1: MASTERED BY MAYKING

Deep Fried Fanclub — Teenage Fanclub

  • 12-track CD compiling early Paperhouse-era recordings (Everything Flows, God Knows It’s True, etc.)
  • Producer: Don Fleming (tracks 5–12); Teenage Fanclub self-produced (tracks 1, 4–10)
  • Engineers: Wharton Tiers (tracks 5–10); Keith Hartley & Paul Chisholm (tracks 11–12); Ted Blakeway (track 4)
  • Remastered by Steve Rooke
  • Written-by credits include Norman Blake, Gerard Love, Brendan O’Hare, Raymond McGinley; covers of Lennon/McCartney, Neil Young, Alex Chilton, Beat Happening
  • Matrix: FLIPCD002 · ·MASTERED· BY MAYKING

Translucent Flashbacks (The Glass Singles) — Spacemen 3

  • 10-track CD (also 2xLP) collecting Spacemen 3’s Glass Records-era singles
  • Produced by Jason Pierce and Sonic Boom (credited as Sonic Boom (2))
  • Recorded by Carlo Morocco (tracks 1–3, 10) and Graham Walker (3) (tracks 4–9)
  • Additional performers: Pete Bassman (bass), Owen John (violin), Alex Green (saxophone), Nicholas “Natty” Brooker (percussion), Rosco (2) (percussion)
  • Cover versions include “Transparent Radiation” (Red Krayola), “Starship” (MC5/Sun Ra), “Rollercoaster” (Roky Erickson/Tommy Hall)
  • Design: Andy Smith (25); sleeve notes by Sonic Boom
  • Matrix: FLIPCD3 / FLIPDLP-003

Connections

  • Fire Records — parent label (London; founded 1985 by Johnny Waller and Clive Solomon); all FLIP releases cataloged under Fire Records in Discogs
  • Paperhouse Records — related imprint (1990–1993), joint venture of Clive Solomon (Fire) and David E. Barker (Glass Records); co-credited on Deep Fried Fanclub
  • Glass Records — where Spacemen 3 originally recorded; the Flip release collects their Glass-era singles
  • Fire Records Of London Ltd. — parent company entity (Discogs ID 672671); both Fire Records and Paperhouse are sublabels
  • Television Personalities — archival comp of early singles
  • Teenage Fanclub — comp of Paperhouse-era recordings
  • Spacemen 3 — comp of Glass Records-era singles
  • Don Fleming — produced tracks on Deep Fried Fanclub
  • Wharton Tiers — engineered tracks on Deep Fried Fanclub
  • Steve Rooke — remastering on Deep Fried Fanclub
  • Jason Pierce / J. Spaceman — produced Translucent Flashbacks
  • Sonic Boom — produced Translucent Flashbacks
  • Daniel Treacy — sole songwriter credit on Yes Darling, But Is It Art?
