DreamWorks Records

Discogs ID: 4887 Origin: USA

Major US record label. Released Jonathan Fire*Eater’s Wolf Songs For Lambs (1997, DRMD-50024) — the band’s major-label debut. Catalog eventually absorbed into Geffen/Universal. Also released associated promos and singles.

Overview

Co-founded c.1994 by Mo Ostin and Lenny Waronker after both left Warner Bros. Records. Operated approximately 1994–2003. Key signings and releases included:

  • Rufus Wainwright — self-titled debut (1998, exec. prod. Waronker), Poses (2001), Want One (2003)
  • Elliott Smith — XO (1998), Figure 8 (2000) — A&R by Waronker
  • The Prince of Egypt soundtrack (1998)
  • Shrek soundtrack (2001)
  • Jonathan Fire*Eater — Wolf Songs For Lambs (1997)

Catalog eventually absorbed into Geffen/Universal.
