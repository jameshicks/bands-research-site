Overview

Founded in 1977 by Brad Miller, Mobile Fidelity Sound Lab is an audiophile reissue label dedicated to high-fidelity remastering of classic recordings from major labels. Its catalogue is concentrated in the 1970s and early 1980s, weighted toward classic rock, folk rock, and blues rock, and spans nearly 1,900 credited releases across its imprints. The label moved into CD production in 1984 and developed its proprietary Gold Ultradisc format in 1987, followed by the domestic Gold Ultradisc II in 1992; all remastering is handled in-house, with engineers including Stan Ricker, Krieg Wunderlich, and Shawn R. Britton among its key staff. Sub-imprints cover specialist formats including ANADISQ 200™ and Digital Audio Cassette.
