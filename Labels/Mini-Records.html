Overview

Mini Records was a Haitian label and distributor operating out of Brooklyn, New York from 1973 through the early 2000s, functioning as an imprint under Prospo Records, Inc. Its catalogue of over 140 releases concentrates on Compas and Zouk, with additional threads of Bolero, Merengue, and Afro-Cuban music. Output peaked in the 1980s (66 releases) after a solid 1970s foundation of 36, reflecting the label’s central role in bringing Caribbean music to the Haitian diaspora in the northeastern United States. Credited personnel across the catalogue include engineers Greg Calbi and James Farber alongside the in-house Mini All Stars ensemble.
