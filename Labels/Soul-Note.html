Soul Note

Discogs ID: 26659 Parent label: CAM (Discogs ID 47332) Sibling label: Black Saint Label Code: LC 11120 Founded: 1979 Base: Tribiano, Italy Founder/Director: Giovanni Bonandrini Acquired by CAM: 2008

Italian jazz label established in 1979 by Giovanni Bonandrini as a companion to his Black Saint imprint. Where Black Saint specialized in free jazz and avant-garde, Soul Note was oriented toward modern jazz and post-bop — though the distinction was not always rigid and many artists appeared on both labels. Catalog reached over 350 albums. From July 1982, distributed in the US through a US distributor.

Both labels were acquired by CAM in 2008; CAM Jazz began reissuing the catalogs using original artwork. Bonandrini’s son Flavio Bonandrini succeeded him in management.
