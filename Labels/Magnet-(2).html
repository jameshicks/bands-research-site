Parent label: Magnet Records Ltd. (Discogs ID: 87589)

UK record label, subsidiary of Magnet Records Ltd. Home to Kissing The Pink for their 1982–1984 singles and first two LPs (Naked, What Noise). With 4,000+ releases, Magnet (2) was a substantial British independent of the early-to-mid 1980s.
