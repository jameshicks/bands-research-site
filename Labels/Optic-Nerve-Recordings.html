Optic Nerve Recordings

Discogs label ID: 487200
Location: 592 Blackpool Road, Preston PR2 1JA, Lancashire, UK
Founded: c. 2012
Contact: info@opticnerverecordings.com
Web: opticnerverecordings.com · opticnerverecordings.bandcamp.com

Preston-based UK reissue label specialising in vinyl and CD reissues of British indie, post-punk, C86, and indiepop from the 1980s and 1990s. One of the more systematically thorough archival operations in the UK independent scene — pulling together scattered 7”s, album sessions, demos, and unreleased material for artists who recorded for small or now-defunct UK indie imprints. Not a new-recordings label; virtually the entire catalog is archival.

Bio

Optic Nerve Recordings launched around 2012, initially putting out CD compilations of 80s UK indie material (early catalog numbers use the OPT4. prefix). The label is based in Preston, Lancashire. No named founder appears on Discogs; the label operates without a prominent public-facing individual identity, functioning more as a curatorial archive operation than a personality-led imprint.

From 2018 onward the label added a second catalog series (ON numbers) for 7” vinyl singles — part of their Optic Sevens subscription series, which ran in editions (Optic Sevens, Optic Sevens 2.0, Optic Sevens 3.0, Optic Sevens 4.0, Optic Sevens 5.0), each covering a different era or theme from the 80s/90s UK indie canon. Each Optic Sevens instalment offered 12 limited-edition coloured-vinyl 7”s over roughly six months, each with a poster and postcard insert.

By 2022 the label had expanded to a third series (ON4xx, ON5xx) covering later eras of the same core scene. By 2025 the catalog ran to well over 100 distinct releases.

Genre Focus

The catalog is overwhelmingly UK indie — specifically the post-punk/C86/janglepop/indiepop ecosystem of 1980–1995. Key scenes represented:

  • C86 / Sarah Records-adjacent indiepop: The Pooh Sticks, The Flatmates, The Siddeleys, East Village (2), Apple Boutique, One Thousand Violins, The Wallflowers (2)
  • Post-punk / anarcho / political: McCarthy, The Wolfhounds, Folk Devils, Girls At Our Best, Article 58, Josef K
  • Pre-history / early punk excavations: The Crazies (3) (pre-Sound), The Cigarettes, The Disco Zombies, Religious Overdose
  • Paisley Underground / jangle adjacents: 14 Iced Bears, Revolving Paint Dream, Razorcuts, The Wake
  • Left-of-centre / art pop: The Monochrome Set, Second Layer, Medium Medium, Laibach
  • Manchester / Northern scene: Dub Sex, Cud, Pop Will Eat Itself, The Wedding Present, Pulp (early)
  • Australian indiepop: Even As We Speak

The label also reissued some US indie touchstones (Beat Happening, Camper Van Beethoven) and scattered Scottish material (The Servants, Weeping Messerschmitts).

Key Artists

McCarthy (Discogs: 145670)

The most-reissued single act on the label (10 releases). McCarthy were a politically charged post-punk / C86 group from London active 1984–1990, fronted by Tim Gane and Malcolm Eden. Their Optic Nerve catalog spans multiple albums and singles compilations: Banking, Violence And The Inner Life Today, I Am A Wallet, Red Sleeping Beauty, The Enraged Will Inherit The Earth, In Purgatory. Key members — Tim Gane later co-founded Stereolab.

The Wolfhounds (Discogs: 286965)

Eight releases on the label, making them the second most-reissued band. North London post-punk / noise-pop group, mid-80s. Label issued Unseen Ripples From A Pebble (2014), Untied Kingdom (2017), The Stupid Poor (2017), Bright And Guilty (2022) among others.

The Monochrome Set (Discogs: 130826)

Seven releases. Art-pop / post-punk group with roots in the late 70s London scene; issued on Rough Trade, Cherry Red, and others originally. Volume, Contrast, Brilliance (2013), Eligible Bachelors (2014), The Jet Set Junta (2022) on Optic Nerve.

Tess Parks (Discogs: 3554944)

Seven Discogs entries, all pressings of one title: Blood Hot (originally released 2013 on Alan McGee’s 359 Music / Cherry Red). Optic Nerve licensed the vinyl rights and issued the first LP pressing in 2015 (OPT4.018); subsequent represses followed in 2019 (blue vinyl), 2023 (cassette), and 2024 (10th anniversary gold gatefold, OPT4.056). A licensed reissue relationship — no new or original Parks material was recorded for the label.

The House Of Love (Discogs: 107077)

Five releases. Janglepop / dream-pop group associated with Creation Records, led by Guy Chadwick. Their Optic Nerve reissues include Deluxe Vinyl Edition (2013), Christine (2020), Shine On (2019), Real Animal (2023).

The Pooh Sticks (Discogs: 243277)

Six releases including the Pooh Sticks 7” Box Set (2019), On Tape (2019), and Straight Up: Noise Pollution C88–90 (2023). C86 / Welsh indiepop group.

The Wedding Present (Discogs: 243285)

Four releases. Leeds janglepop group led by David Gedge. Optic Nerve reissued early singles.

Girls At Our Best (Discogs: 327015)

Four releases including Pleasure (2014) and Getting Nowhere Fast / Warm Girls (2021). Leeds post-punk group.

BOB (37) (Discogs: 1288848)

The single artist with the highest release count on the label (14), though most are likely different pressings/editions of a small number of masters. Indie/indiepop.

Hangman’s Beautiful Daughters (Discogs: 520528)

Ten releases. A group whose members — Emily Brown, Philip King, Gordon Dawson, Sandy Fleming, Ray Philpott — appear heavily across the label’s credits summary. Likely released primarily through Optic Nerve.

Folk Devils (Discogs: 630805)

Five releases. Post-punk band featuring Ian Lowery. Beautiful Monsters — Singles And Demo Recordings 1984–1986 (2016) and Forever EP (2020). Also acted as their own producers across much of the catalog.

The Monochrome Set / Medium Medium / Second Layer

Art-punk / post-punk adjacents pulled into the catalog.

Article 58 (Discogs: 893554)

Two releases including Event To Come (2022). Scottish post-punk.

The Crazies (3) (Discogs: 10381858)

A Simple Vision (2021, OPT4.043) — 1978 recordings by the pre-The Sound (2) South London group featuring Adrian Borland.

Connections

  • Creation Records — multiple Optic Nerve artists originally released on Creation (The House Of Love, The Primitives, The Vaselines); Joe Foster (Creation co-founder) produced 2 Optic Nerve releases
  • The Sound (2) — The Crazies (3) is the pre-Sound group featuring Adrian Borland
  • Television Personalities — label producer Daniel Treacy is TVP’s founder/frontman; deep overlap with the C86 scene the label documents
  • Stereolab — McCarthy’s Tim Gane co-founded Stereolab after McCarthy dissolved
  • Él — The Monochrome Set overlap with the Él Records roster
  • Cooking Vinyl — The Wedding Present connection
  • Southern Records — shared ecosystem for some post-punk reissues
