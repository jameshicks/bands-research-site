Monika Enterprise

Discogs search term: Monika Enterprise
Origin: Berlin, Germany
Founded by: Gudrun Gut

Berlin-based independent label founded by Gudrun Gut (of Malaria!). Active from at least 2000 onward. Released the “Kaltes Klares Wasser” reissue/remix campaign (2000) and co-released the M_Sessions box set (2021) with Moabit Musik.

The label’s website is listed in the Malaria! Discogs profile as m-enterprise.de. Gudrun Gut’s top labels on Discogs include Monika Enterprise as her primary home for solo and label work.
