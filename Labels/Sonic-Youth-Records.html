Overview

Sonic Youth Records is a record label based in Hoboken, NJ, founded in 1997. It functions primarily as a self-release vehicle for Sonic Youth and their immediate circle, with a roster that includes Jim O’Rourke, Wharton Tiers, Kim Gordon, and Chris Habib — all figures from the band’s experimental and noise-rock orbit. The catalogue is concentrated in the 1990s and 2000s (five releases each), with modest activity continuing into the 2010s and 2020s. Three sublabels — My So Called Records, Perspectives Musicales (2), and Sonic Knuth Records — operated under its umbrella, suggesting some organizational branching within the imprint.
