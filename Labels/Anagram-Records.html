— Discogs label profile, ID 10134

Overview

Anagram Records is a reissue-focused division of Cherry Red Records Ltd., built around what it claims is the world’s largest collection of classic Punk, Psychobilly, and Goth recordings, each release typically packaged with bonus tracks and rarities. Active since 1982, the label’s output peaked across the 1990s and 2000s with over 130 releases per decade. It operates several collector-targeted sub-series — dedicated Punk, Goth, and Psychobilly lines — and its roster centres on scene figures including Alien Sex Fiend, P. Paul Fenech, and Christine Wade.
