Overview

United Artists Records was founded in 1958 as a subsidiary of the United Artists film studio, created initially to release its own soundtrack recordings before rapidly expanding into pop, country, jazz, and easy listening. Transamerica Corporation acquired the label in 1967, then absorbed Liberty Records (purchased 1968, shut down and merged into UA in 1971), consolidating a wide catalogue spanning Ferrante & Teicher, Shirley Bassey, Don McLean, Bobby Goldsboro, and Jeff Lynne. The label enjoyed particular strength in jazz and mainstream pop through the 1970s before UA executives Artie Mogull and Jerry Rubinstein purchased it from Transamerica in 1979, a transaction that preceded its eventual passage into the EMI Group.
