Studio One

Discogs label ID: (to confirm — search needed; also known as Soul Jazz Studio One Series, ID: 391810 on SJR side)
Jamaican record label founded by Clement “Coxsone” Dodd. Based at 13 Brentford Road, Kingston, Jamaica. Active from the late 1950s; the foundational label of Jamaican popular music — ska, rocksteady, early reggae, dancehall, dub, and lovers rock all have major roots here.

Overview

Studio One is the most important label in the history of Jamaican music. Coxsone Dodd founded it as an outgrowth of his sound system operation. Key artists include Jackie Mittoo, The Skatalites, Alton Ellis, Hortense Ellis, Freddie McGregor, Sugar Minott, Burning Spear, Dennis Brown, Marcia Griffiths, and many others. The label’s catalogue has been extensively licensed and reissued by Soul Jazz Records since 2001, with Stuart Baker as compiler on more than 20 volumes.

Soul Jazz / Stuart Baker Compilations

Baker has compiled over 20 Studio One compilations on Soul Jazz, spanning:

  • Ska: Studio One Ska Fever! (2013), Independence Ska And The Far East Sound (2017)
  • Rocksteady: Studio One Rocksteady (2014), Vol. 2 (2017)
  • Reggae/roots: Studio One Rockers (2001), Studio One Classics (2004)
  • Dub: Studio One Dub (2004), Studio One Dub Fire Special (2016)
  • Funk: Studio One Funk (2004)
  • Dancehall: Studio One Dancehall (2014)
  • Lovers rock: Studio One Lovers Rock (2018)
  • Freestyle: Studio One Supreme (2017), Fire Over Babylon (2021), Studio One Power Mix! (2024)
