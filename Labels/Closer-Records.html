Overview

Closer Records is a French independent label founded in Le Havre by Philippe Debris in 1983, operating primarily in the garage rock, punk, power pop, and indie rock spaces. Its substantial 1980s catalogue — around 75 releases — was initially distributed by New Rose before switching to Virgin around 1984. After falling dormant in the early 1990s, the label sat quiet for roughly twenty years before Debris revived it in 2013; it has issued around two dozen further releases since then, continuing into the 2020s.
