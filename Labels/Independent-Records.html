Overview

Independent Records was a Dublin-based label that operated from 1994, building its catalogue around licensed US and Irish releases for the Irish market rather than signing acts directly. Its output centred on folk, folk rock, indie rock, and alternative rock, with the bulk of its roughly 58 releases appearing in the 2000s. The roster included US folk-indie acts such as Josh Ritter and Brett Sparks alongside Irish artists including Mark Geary. The label is now defunct, with virtually no releases recorded after 2010.
