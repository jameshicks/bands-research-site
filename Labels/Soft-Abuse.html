Soft Abuse

US independent record label. Discogs label ID: 48126. Based in San Francisco area. Active at least 2003–present (catalog runs to 2026). Contact: info@softabuse.com. Bandcamp: https://softabuse.bandcamp.com. No parent label; sublabel: Puissant (CD-R releases in handmade packaging).

Overview

Soft Abuse is a San Francisco-area independent label operating in the overlap between psych-folk, lo-fi indie, experimental, and free folk. The catalog opened in 2003 with a Frog Eyes 7” (cat. 001) and a CD by The Franciscan Hobbies (SAB 002) — the latter featuring core Jewelled Antler personnel — and grew to roughly 110+ master releases by 2026.

The label’s strongest aesthetic identity is in the SF psychedelic folk underground of the early-to-mid 2000s, with The Skygreen Leopards and Donovan Quinn & The 13th Month as its most emblematic artists. But the roster is deliberately eclectic: New Zealand lo-fi (Pumice, their most prolific artist by far), Australian and international artists, avant-folk, minimalist drone, and singer-songwriter material all appear. Geographically the label is US-based but not parochial.

The founding and ownership of Soft Abuse are not documented in the Discogs database. The label’s Discogs profile is a single line: “USA label.” The Jewelled Antler connection is established through personnel overlap rather than any formal affiliation.

Connection to Jewelled Antler

Jewelled Antler (Discogs ID: 60178) was a separate SF-area cassette/CDR collective active from roughly 2000, associated with Glenn Donaldson, Steven R. Smith, Thuja, The Blithe Sons, The Birdtree, and related projects. It is not a parent label of Soft Abuse — they are independent entities.

The overlap is substantive at the personnel level:

  • The Franciscan Hobbies (Soft Abuse SAB 002, 2003) is a Jewelled Antler-adjacent project: members include Glenn Donaldson, Loren Chasse, Christine Boepple, and others from that orbit
  • Glenn Donaldson (also credited as Edmund Xavier) appears across Soft Abuse’s early catalog — as performer on The Franciscan Hobbies and The Skygreen Leopards, and under the Edmund Xavier alias for producer credits
  • Steven R. Smith, another Jewelled Antler core figure (Hala Strana, Ulaan Khol, Ulaan Markhor), became one of Soft Abuse’s most heavily documented artists from 2019 onward (at least 4 primary credits plus contributor credits totaling 11 releases)
  • Hala Strana, also a Jewelled Antler artist, appears on Soft Abuse (2004–2005 releases)

The Franciscan Hobbies SAB 002 (2003) serves as the clearest single-release bridge between the two communities.

Sublabel: Puissant

Puissant (Discogs ID: 100626) is a Soft Abuse sublabel for CD-R releases in handmade packaging. Donovan Quinn releases on Puissant (top label in his Discogs profile). No other roster information in the database.

Key Cross-connections

  • Carey Mercer / Frog Eyes orbit: Soft Abuse’s first release was Frog Eyes (2003), and Mercer appears on Blackout Beach (his solo project, 6 releases) plus the Destroyer 12” (as guitarist). The Vancouver-to-SF connection via Mercer is significant.
  • Glenn Donaldson: Appears as performer (Skygreen Leopards, Franciscan Hobbies), as producer (Edmund Xavier alias), and as bandleader (Horrid Red, 6 releases). The Jewelled Antler-to-Soft Abuse bridge.
  • Steven R. Smith: One of the most documented contributors. Hala Strana (2004–05), Ulaan Khol (2008–15), Ulaan Markhor (2012–18), solo releases (2019–24). Also appears as contributor on others’ Soft Abuse releases.
  • Jason Quever (Papercuts): Produced Donovan Quinn’s 13th Month records; also played on Sonny And The Sunsets (which features several SF underground regulars).
  • Ben Chasny (Six Organs of Admittance): Mixed By credit on one release; closely connected to Quinn (New Bums) but no primary Soft Abuse release.
