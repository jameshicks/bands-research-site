Record Collection

Discogs ID: 43406
Origin: Santa Monica, California, USA
Address: 1223 Wilshire Blvd. #811, Santa Monica, CA 90403
Parent label: Warner Bros. Records
Website: recordcollectionmusic.com
Years active: 2002–present (most catalog 2004–2014)

Independent label and management company based in Southern California. A sublabel of Warner Bros. Records, giving its releases WEA/Warner distribution across the US. Despite the Warner parentage, Record Collection operated with a boutique indie character — anchored most visibly by The Walkmen’s mid-period output and a cluster of John Frusciante solo and side-project records.

Overview

Record Collection’s earliest releases date to 2002 (Har Mar Superstar’s You Can Feel Me and Power Lunch / EZ Pass). The label peaked in activity between 2004 and 2007, when it issued a dense run of records spanning indie rock, hip-hop, and experimental guitar music. The Warner affiliation provided catalog numbers in the WEA range and professional distribution, while the label’s Santa Monica management operation allowed it to sign artists across genres that a traditional Warner imprint would not.

Key creative relationships:

  • The Walkmen — four studio/EP releases (2004–2006), the label’s highest-profile indie rock signing
  • John Frusciante — most prolific artist on the roster; multiple solo albums plus the Ataxia project with Joe Lally and Josh Klinghoffer
  • Har Mar Superstar — early catalog (2002–2004), with European licensing via Warner
  • Blake Mills — later signing; debut Break Mirrors (2010) and subsequent EPs through the early 2010s

The label continued issuing Frusciante releases well into the 2010s and 2020s (including The Empyrean in 2009, Letur-Lefr and PBX Funicular Intaglio Zone in 2012, Enclosure in 2014). A 2019 credit for Colin Stetson’s The First (Original Soundtrack Vol. 1) shows the imprint still functional.

The Walkmen on Record Collection

Four releases 2004–2006, during the band’s most critically productive period.

Bows + Arrows (2004) — cat. 48680-2

Self-produced by the band, with one exception: “The Rat” (track 2) produced by Dave Sardy and engineered by Greg Gordon. Remaining tracks engineered and mixed by Stuart Sikes. Mastered by Fred Kevorkian. Lineup: Hamilton Leithauser (vocals, guitar), Paul Maroon (guitar, piano), Peter Bauer (bass), Matt Barrick (drums), Walter Martin (organ). Cover photo by Anna Leithauser.

Little House Of Savages EP (2004) — cat. 7-16463

7” EP; same band lineup. Mixed by Stuart Sikes and the band.

”Pussy Cats” Starring The Walkmen (2006) — cat. 2-44498

Full cover of Harry Nilsson’s 1974 Pussy Cats album (originally produced by John Lennon). Self-produced and recorded. Kevin McMahon as primary engineer/mixer. Features Ian Svenonius (vocals on three tracks), Quentin Stoltzfus (featuring on “Mucho Mongo”), string arrangements by Alex Waterman. Includes Bob Dylan, Jimmy Cliff, Doc Pomus/Mort Shuman, and Harry Nilsson written-by credits across tracks.

A Hundred Miles Off (2006) — cat. 44223-2

Twelve tracks, all self-produced and self-mixed, with engineering split: Don Zientara recorded/mixed multiple tracks, Kevin McMahon on others, the band itself on several. Walter Martin credited with multiple instruments across nearly every track (bass, organ, piano, djembe, lap steel). Mastered by Fred Kevorkian. Tom Peloso (trumpet) guests on “Louisiana.” “Another One Goes By” written by Quentin Stoltzfus.

John Frusciante on Record Collection

Frusciante is Discogs’ single most-credited artist on the label (114 release credits). His solo catalog on Record Collection runs from The Will To Death and Inside Of Emptiness (both 2004) through Enclosure (2014). The Empyrean (2009) is his most expansive solo statement on the label — orchestral and choral, featuring the Sonus Quartet, the Donald Taylor And The New Dimension Singers gospel choir, and engineers Ryan Hewitt and Adam Samuels.

The Ataxia side project (with Joe Lally of Fugazi and Josh Klinghoffer) produced two LPs on the label: Automatic Writing (2004) and AW II (2007). Both engineered and mixed by Ryan Hewitt, mastered by Bernie Grundman.

The A Sphere In The Heart Of Silence collaboration (2004, John Frusciante / Josh Klinghoffer) was co-produced and co-written by both, engineered and mixed by Ryan Hewitt. Mastered by Bernie Grundman.

Distribution and Business Structure

Record Collection is listed in Discogs as a sublabel of Warner Bros. Records (parent ID: 1000). The Warner relationship provided US distribution, reflected in WEA-range catalog numbers throughout the catalog. The label operated independently on A&R and management. Address of record: 1223 Wilshire Blvd. #811, Santa Monica, CA 90403.
