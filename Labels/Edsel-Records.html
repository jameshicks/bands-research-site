Edsel Records

Discogs label ID: 22779. UK reissue label specialising in archival catalog and compilations. Parent: Demon Music Group Ltd. (sibling label to Demon Records within the same group). Originally associated with Andrew Lauder.

Overview

Released two significant The Sound (2) retrospective box sets in 2014–2015:

  • Jeopardy / From The Lion’s Mouth / All Fall Down …Plus (2014) — early Korova/WEA period
  • Shock Of Daylight / Heads And Hearts / In The Hot House / Thunder Up / Propaganda (2015) — Statik/PIAS period

Connections

  • The Sound (2) — major reissue program
  • Demon Records — sibling imprint under Demon Music Group Ltd.
  • Andrew Lauder — associated founder
