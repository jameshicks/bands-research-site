Overview

On Discogs, “4AD (2)” is a placeholder label entry used to aggregate bootlegs and unofficial releases falsely attributed to the genuine 4AD imprint. It carries no independent curatorial identity — the roughly 47 releases logged under it from 1996 onward are unauthorised pressings tied to real 4AD artists such as Dead Can Dance, Lisa Gerrard, and Brendan Perry. Its style tags (Modern Classical, Ambient, Ethereal, Experimental, Indie Rock) mirror the legitimate 4AD roster rather than representing any distinct label programme.
