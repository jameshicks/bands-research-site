Overview

Grand Hotel Van Cleef is a German indie label founded in Hamburg in 2002 when Marcus Wiebusch, Reimer Bustorff, and Thees Uhlmann merged their two prior imprints — Hotel van Cleef and B.A. Records — into a single operation. Its founding trio are also artists on its own roster: Wiebusch fronts Kettcar, and Thees Uhlmann releases solo material under his own name, making it an unusually founder-driven label. The catalogue covers indie rock, alternative rock, and punk, with its heaviest output in the 2010s (86 releases), and the label has remained active into the 2020s under its parent publishing arm, Grand Hotel Van Cleef Musikverlag GmbH.
