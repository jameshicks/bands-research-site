Overview

Malmlund Records is a small label run by Christian Malmlund, active from 2017 across sound art, noise, sound poetry, and industrial music. Its catalogue of around 61 releases spans the late 2010s and early 2020s, with the roster built heavily around the Kommissar Hjuler circle — Kommissar Hjuler Und Frau, Mama Bär, and Kommissar Hjuler himself — alongside Foltergaul and the prominent German visual artist Jonathan Meese. (Note: the Hjuler/Mama Bär practice is based in Flensburg, Germany, not Hamburg; see Notes (web) below.) A distinctive aspect of the label is Christian Malmlund’s licensed use of erotic photography from Viv Thomas and Scarlett O’Hara (via sub-licence from Keith Tanner) as cover artwork, rights reserved to Malmlund.

Notes (web) — Malmlund / Hjuler connection

Searched: 2026-11-16. Sources checked: Wikipedia (Kommissar Hjuler), The Wire interview (Nov 2010), asylum-lunaticum.de artist CV page, asylumlunaticum.bandcamp.com, Discogs artist page for Christian Malmlund, general web searches for Christian Malmlund biography/interviews.

Label location. One search-result snippet described Malmlund Records as “based in Vardo, Finland”; however Vardø is in Norway (Finnmark), not Finland. No source has been found that pins down Christian Malmlund’s location with certainty. His surname is Scandinavian. The Discogs label profile does not state a country. This may be a snippet error (Finland vs. Norway) or Malmlund may have relocated. (disco.market snippet)

Is Malmlund himself a performing artist? On Discogs, Christian Malmlund appears as a credited performer on at least two releases on his own label: NOTION 2 (SC11, 2019) — credited alongside Kommissar Hjuler and Mama Bär — and Jack Harrison - Set # JG03 (SC20, 2020), where he is the sole credited artist. The asylum-lunaticum.de artist CV for Kommissar Hjuler / Mama Bär also lists “Christian Malmlund & KHj. & MB - NOTION 2 - LP (Malmlund Rec. (Finland))” in the vinyl discography, naming him as a co-performer. This suggests Malmlund participates in recordings rather than functioning purely as a label administrator. (asylum-lunaticum.de artist CV, as of 2026-11-16)

Is he part of the Hamburg/Flensburg outsider scene? No source found that describes Malmlund as a member of the Kommissar Hjuler circle or the broader German sound-poetry/NO!art scene. Kommissar Hjuler (Detlev Hjuler) is based in Flensburg, not Hamburg, though he has exhibited and performed in Hamburg. Malmlund appears in the Hjuler CV only as a label issuing releases and as a named co-performer on NOTION 2; there is no description of him attending performances, contributing to the visual-art or NO!art dimension of the circle, or being geographically proximate. The relationship is consistent with a remote label operator who also contributes sound material to collaborative releases — a common pattern in the global noise/sound-poetry cassette-and-lathe-cut network. (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kommissar_Hjuler, as of 2026-11-16; asylum-lunaticum.de)

How the roster came to centre on the Hjuler circle. No interview, press piece, or statement was found that explains the origin of the relationship — no “how we met” account from either side. What can be inferred from the catalogue: the very first Malmlund Records releases (2017) already feature Kommissar Hjuler, Kommissar Hjuler Und Frau, and Mama Bär, meaning the connection pre-dates the label’s public launch or was simultaneous with it. The label appears to have been founded around this relationship rather than having discovered the Hjuler circle later.

Conflict / correction note. The existing Overview section refers to “the Hamburg outsider/sound-poetry scene.” Wikipedia and the asylum-lunaticum.de CV consistently locate the Hjuler/Mama Bär practice in Flensburg (on the German–Danish border), not Hamburg. Hamburg is mentioned in the Hjuler Wikipedia article only in connection with venues and exhibitions, not as their base. This is a geographic error in the Overview and should be corrected.

What could not be established: No web source gives Christian Malmlund’s nationality, home city, artistic biography, how he first contacted Kommissar Hjuler, or what, if any, prior label or artistic activity he had before 2017. No interviews with Malmlund were found. No social media, blog, or personal website for him was found.
