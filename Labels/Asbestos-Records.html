Overview

Asbestos Records is a Southern Connecticut label founded in 1998, built around punk, ska, and folk rock. Its catalogue grew steadily through the 2000s before accelerating sharply in the 2010s, which account for the bulk of its roughly 100 releases. The label’s most prominent acts are The Suicide Machines and Bomb The Music Industry!/Jeff Rosenstock, the latter becoming one of the more visible names in DIY punk. Asbestos also operated sub-imprints including More Bang For Your Buck and two volumes of the Ska Is Dead Subscription Series, a curated subscription format that reflected the label’s investment in the ska revival circuit.
