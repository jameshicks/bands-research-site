Overview

Alerce is a Chilean record label founded in 1976 by Ricardo García, operating under the parent company Alerce Producciones Fonográficas S.A. It specialises in Chilean music and South American folk and singer-songwriter traditions, with its catalogue centred on Nueva Canción and Nueva Trova — styles closely associated with politically engaged Latin American music of the late twentieth century. The label’s output peaked in the 1990s (117 releases) after a sustained 1980s run (63 releases), with major roster artists including Inti Illimani and Silvio Rodríguez; sublabels such as El Canto Nuevo and Alerta! Diskos covered distinct corners of the catalogue. Activity slowed markedly after 2000, with just 16 releases across the 2000s and a handful since.
