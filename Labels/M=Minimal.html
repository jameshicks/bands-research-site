M=Minimal

Berlin-based label and platform for minimal music of all different shapes. Discogs label ID: 204371. LC 15278. Parent label: Studio.Wannsee (ID: 350734). 52 unique master releases on Discogs (57 total release entries including pressing variants).

Overview

M=Minimal was founded in 2010 by Christian Borngräber and Jens Strüver, the electronic duo Borngräber & Strüver. The label is run under Cmpm Creative Music Productions & More GmbH, based at Chausseestraße 3a, 14109 Berlin (the same address as the parent operation). It sits alongside the sister imprint M=Maximal under the Studio.Wannsee umbrella, which itself is a sublabel of Syncron-Arts GmbH. Studio.Wannsee also runs the Ausfahrt imprint.

The label’s positioning is genuinely broad despite the “minimal” name — the catalog spans Berlin-school minimalism, experimental electronic music, contemporary classical composition, improv, South American percussive music, and post-industrial avant-garde. The founders serve as house executive producers and handle cover artwork (Borngräber) and photography (Strüver) across virtually the entire catalog.

A second executive producer, Wolfgang Schirp (Discogs ID: 3419363), appears on several 2012–2013 releases, suggesting a third investor or co-operator in the label’s middle period.

Founding Artists

The label launched with three simultaneous 2010 LPs:

  • mm-001lp — Conrad Schnitzler’s Zug — archival solo electronics, mastered by Stefan Betke (Pole) and Kassian Troyer
  • mm-002lp — Chico Mello / Helinho Brandão’s Água — Brazilian contemporary music, mastered by Kassian Troyer
  • mm-003lp — Borngräber & Strüver’s In G — the founders’ own minimal electronic album, mastered by Stefan Betke

The launch made clear the label’s dual identity: platforming archival and contemporary experimental music while also serving as a vehicle for the founders’ own recordings.

Key Releases

Conrad Schnitzler Series

Conrad Schnitzler (Discogs ID: 17135; real name Konrad Schnitzler) is the label’s most prominent featured artist, appearing on 24 performer credits across the catalog — nearly half the label’s output. He was an original member of Tangerine Dream and Kluster (3). M=Minimal released:

  • Zug (mm-001, 2010) — solo electronics produced and performed by Schnitzler
  • Ballet Statique (mm-004, 2011) — solo electronics; reissued in 2013 as Ballet Statique / Con (mm-004cd) with liner notes by Wolfgang Seidel and production credit to Peter Baumann (Tangerine Dream)
  • Endtime (mm-010, 2012) — solo electronics; vinyl cut by Stefan Betke (credited as Pole)
  • Con-Struct with Borngräber & Strüver (mm-007, 2011) — collaborative record where Schnitzler provided solo sounds and Borngräber & Strüver arranged and produced
  • Zug Reshaped And Remodeled (mm-012, 2012) — Schnitzler’s Zug material remixed by Ricardo Villalobos and Max Loderbauer; original mastering by Villalobos and Loderbauer, vinyl mastering and lacquer cut by Stefan Betke; notable as a bridge between the Schnitzler archival side and Berlin’s contemporary techno/minimal scene
  • Con-Struct with Andreas Reihse (mm-014, 2012) — Reihse arranged and produced Schnitzler solo sounds, parallel to the mm-007 concept

Schnitzler died in August 2011, so the 2012 releases drew on pre-existing recordings.

Fennesz / Brandlmayr / Dafeldecker — Till The Old World’s Blown Up And A New One Is Created (mm-011, 2012)

Originally released on Duophonic UHF Discs and Mego. The M=Minimal release is a vinyl LP reissue of this trio session: Fennesz (electric guitar, acoustic guitar, computer), Martin Brandlmayr (drums, percussion, vibraphone, computer, piano, editing, arrangement), Werner Dafeldecker (double bass, tape delay, computer). Borngräber and Strüver served as executive producers. Stefan Betke cut the vinyl at Scape Mastering; Nicholas Bussmann mastered the CD version. Fennesz, Brandlmayr, and Dafeldecker are all credited under the group name Till The Old World’s Blown Up And A New One Is Created on Discogs.

Andreas Reihse — Romantic Comedy (mm-008, 2011)

Solo record by Andreas Reihse (Discogs ID: 416309), best known as a member of Kreidler. Artwork by Thea Djordjadze; voice contributions from Max Dax and Post Industrial Boys; mastered by Christian Borngräber; lacquer cut by Stefan Betke.

Ernstalbrecht Stiebler — Three Releases (2012–2014)

Ernstalbrecht Stiebler (Discogs ID: 530902) is a German composer associated with minimalism and the Frankfurt radio tradition. M=Minimal issued his self-titled LP (mm-009, 2012), Ton In Ton (mm-017cd, 2013; performed by Ensemble Modern conducted by Franck Ollu, with organ by Hans-Peter Schulz), and Mit Der Zeit (mm-022cd, 2014). Stiebler composed all material; mastering split between Stefan Betke (mm-009 vinyl) and Christian Borngräber (other releases).

Denseland — Like Likes Like (mm-021, 2013)

The Berlin trio Denseland: Hanno Leichtmann (drums, electronics, synth), Johannes Strobl (bass, electronics), David Moss (voice, lyrics). Mastered by Christian Borngräber. Executive producer Wolfgang Schirp.

Chico Mello — Água (mm-002, 2010)

Brazilian composer Chico Mello with Helinho Brandão and a large ensemble of Brazilian musicians. Mastered by Kassian Troyer. One of the label’s most harmonically and rhythmically diverse releases, contrasting sharply with the minimal electronic focus of the surrounding catalog.

Recurring Personnel

Borngräber & Strüver as House Team

Beyond their own recordings, Borngräber and Strüver function as the label’s in-house production and visual team across nearly all releases:

  • Christian Borngräber (Discogs ID: 1995217): cover/artwork design on 18 releases; mastering engineer on 9 releases; executive producer on the majority of non-self-released titles
  • Jens Strüver (Discogs ID: 510653): cover photography on 16 releases; executive producer alongside Borngräber on most titles

Stefan Betke / Pole (Discogs ID: 311802)

Stefan Betke, who records as Pole and operates Scape Mastering in Berlin, is the label’s primary external mastering and lacquer-cutting engineer. He appears on at least 6 releases in mastering capacity and 7 in lacquer-cut capacity. His presence bridges M=Minimal to the broader Berlin dub/techno/minimal scene (Betke runs Scape records and is associated with Raster-Noton-adjacent aesthetics).

Kassian Troyer (Discogs ID: 793598)

Berlin-based mastering engineer at Dubplates & Mastering. Credited on the 2010 launch releases (Zug and Água). Connected to much of the Berlin electronic/contemporary music mastering world.

Nicholas Bussmann (Discogs ID: 394378)

Composer and cellist performing under both his own name and the alias Nicholas Desamory (Discogs ID: 2744597 — listed separately but confirmed alias). On M=Minimal: Like You (mm-013, 2012) credited to Nicholas Desamory (cello, voice), with cover by Lucile Desamory and Nicholas Bussmann; and CD mastering of the Fennesz/Brandlmayr/Dafeldecker LP (mm-011). Member of several Berlin experimental groups including Ich Schwitze Nie and Kapital Band 1.

Hanno Leichtmann (Discogs ID: 292406)

Drummer, electronic musician, member of Denseland. Credited on Like You (mm-013, hihat) and as core member of Denseland on mm-021. Records under aliases including Static (7), Chromacolor, Forest Jackson.

Rashad Becker (Discogs ID: 166726)

Mastering engineer at Dubplates & Mastering. Credited for mastering Like You (mm-013). A key figure in Berlin experimental mastering, associated with PAN, Editions Mego, and related labels.

Studio.Wannsee is described as a digital + analogue audio recording studio in the Babelsberg neighborhood of Berlin, formerly known under a different name and relocated from Halle to Berlin in 2010 — the same year M=Minimal launched. Producers listed for Studio.Wannsee: Christian Borngräber (ID: 1995217) and Ulrich Hornberg (ID: 480657, credited “von Pavenstädt”). Hornberg appears as mastering engineer on one M=Minimal release (Clouds, mm-015cd).
