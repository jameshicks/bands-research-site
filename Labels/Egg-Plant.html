Egg Plant

UK indie label co-run by Alan Jenkins and Terry Burrows. Discogs label ID: 73426. Parent label: Egg Plant Records (ID: 3716856).

A joint venture between Burrows’ Hamster Records And Tapes and Jenkins’ Cordelia Records. Founded 1987; used exclusively for early Chrysanthemums releases through 1990, when the band signed to Madagascar. Revived for the 2022 Chrysanthemums album. 14 releases on Discogs total (including reissues/pressings).

Catalog numbers use a playful egg-counting sequence: ONE EGG, Two Eggs, Three Eggs, Four Eggs … SEVENEGGS (2022).
