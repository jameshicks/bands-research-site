Anti-

Record label. Discogs label ID: 1873. Imprint of Epitaph (parent label ID: 61599).

Overview

Anti- operates as a more eclectic, artist-driven imprint under Epitaph, releasing music outside Epitaph’s punk/rock core — spanning experimental, avant-garde, folk, and noise. Distributed major releases for The Locust, Tom Waits, Neko Case, Merle Haggard, and many others.
