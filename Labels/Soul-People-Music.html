Soul People Music

Self-run New York deep house imprint founded and operated by Fred P. (Fred Peterkin). Discogs label ID: 74526. Contact: info@soulpeoplemusic.net. Bandcamp: soulpeoplemusic.bandcamp.com. Approximately 87 releases across the main label and three sublabels, spanning 2006–2024.

Overview

Soul People Music is the primary release vehicle for Fred Peterkin’s work under his two main personas — Fred P. and Black Jazz Consortium — along with a small but carefully curated roster of like-minded artists in the NYC deep house orbit. Peterkin is the sole operator; the label’s contact address and all URLs point back to him personally.

The label launched around 2006 with early 12”s from Vibrophonic and DJ Mourad (SPM003/SPM004), before establishing its core identity in 2007 when Black Jazz Consortium’s debut full-length RE:Actions Of Light appeared as SPMCD 01. That record set the template: introspective, jazz-informed deep house with Peterkin handling all production, mixing, and arrangement himself.

From 2007 through 2013 the label concentrated almost entirely on Peterkin’s own output under the Black Jazz Consortium name — a run of EPs (SPM014 through SPM023) and three studio albums (SPMCD 001–004). The Earth Tones compilation series (SPMETV001–005, 2010–2013) served as a periodic showcase for affiliated and emerging artists, functioning as the label’s main A&R window.

Around 2013–2015 the imprint expanded slightly via the Boards sublabel (SPMB prefix), which issued 12”s from producers including Jose Cabrera, Brendon Moeller, Naoki Shinohara, Lapien, Dubbyman, and others. This period also saw Peterkin reactivate the Anomaly (10) alias (his own alter-ego) for three more EPs.

The label’s LP output accelerated from 2015 onward: Jose Cabrera’s Portrait Of The Flying Sky (SPMBLP001, 2015), Fred P.’s Sound Destination (SPMBLP002, 2016), and The Incredible Adventures Of Captain P (Escapism) (SPMBLP003, 2017). Activity slowed after 2017; a 2024 digital release (Emotive Vibrations) shows the label still active in a limited capacity.
