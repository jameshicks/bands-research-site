Deewee

Belgian independent record label and studio complex based in Ghent. Discogs label ID: 901323. Founded by Stephen Dewaele and David Dewaele of Soulwax, launched 2016. Sublabel: Waffles (ID: 900623).

Overview

Deewee operates simultaneously as a recording and mixing studio and a release imprint. Every music release on the label was either recorded or mixed (or both) at Studio Deewee in Ghent, Belgium — a constraint that gives the catalogue a consistent production character. Creative direction across the full catalogue is handled by Ill-Studio, a Paris-based design studio. Mastering across nearly the entire catalogue has been done by Mike Marsh (Discogs ID: 325174), credited on 37 releases. The label issues music on vinyl and digitally on the same day. A distinctive house style marks every release: each side opens with a spoken narration giving the catalog number and side letter, and closes with a locked groove (a looped sample tied to that side).

The label launched in 2015 with early releases by Die Verboten, Klanken, and Emmanuelle Moutinho before its official 2016 founding. In 2017 it served as the home for Soulwax’s long-awaited return album From Deewee. Since then it has built a roster centred on Belgian and Ghent-adjacent acts, with Charlotte Adigéry and Bolis Pupul (together as Charlotte Adigéry & Bolis Pupul) as its most internationally prominent signings, and Marie Davidson joining as a key artist from 2024 onwards.

The sublabel Waffles (ID: 900623, also Ghent) runs a separate sequence of 7 anonymous club-oriented 12-inch releases (WAFFLES001–007, 2015–2018) issued under the umbrella artist name “Waffles.”

Non-musical catalog numbers include studio identification (DEEWEE001), merchandise for the 2015 Untold Eivissa event, and books (Incomplete Inventory, DEEWEE041).

Key Personnel

Producers

David Dewaele and Stephen Dewaele (Discogs IDs: 430762 / 435059) are credited as producer on 45 releases each — effectively co-producers on the entire Soulwax output plus large swaths of the roster. Boris Zeebroek (= Bolis Pupul, ID: 1078335 credited as Boris Zeebroek) produced 13 releases. Charlotte Adigéry is credited as producer on 11 releases (her own output). Pierre Guerineau produced 5 releases.

Engineers

Mike Marsh (ID: 325174) mastered 37 releases across the label — near-total mastering consistency. Assistant engineering across many releases by Oliver Geerts (ID: 4927700, also a member of Asa Moto) and Pim Gombeer (ID: 11681960). Stefaan Van Leuven (ID: 435063) handled additional engineering and also performed on From Deewee.

Creative Direction

Ill-Studio (ID: 1530716) credited as creative director / art direction on 82 releases — present across the entire catalogue without exception. Thomas Le Provost (ID: 6325524) handled graphic design on 21 releases; Giovanni Murolo (ID: 7672337) on 12. Camille Vivier (ID: 2241228), Maxime Guyon (ID: 2855357), and Nadine Fraczkowski (ID: 5128816) each photographed 11–12 releases.

Live/Session Performers

Igor Cavalera (ID: 254253) played drums on 13 Deewee releases (Soulwax albums). Blake Davies (ID: 4340944) played drums on 11 releases. Laima Leyton (ID: 996410) is credited as vocalist/performer on 11 releases. Stefaan Van Leuven (ID: 435063) performed on Soulwax releases. Victoria Smith (ID: 972338) performed on 4 releases.
