Overview

“Island Records (2)” is a Discogs administrative placeholder — not a real label — used to catalog unofficial releases (bootlegs, counterfeits, and pirate compilations) that fraudulently present themselves as genuine Island Records issues. No actual imprint operates under this name; all releases here were manufactured without authorization from Island Records. The 496 catalogued releases are heavily weighted toward the 2000s and 2010s, and they cluster around artists from the real Island Records roster — U2, PJ Harvey, Brian Eno, and The Edge among them — whose names were exploited for counterfeit product.
