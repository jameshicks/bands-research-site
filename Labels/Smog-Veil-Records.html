Overview

Smog Veil Records is a Chicago, Illinois independent label founded in 1991, working primarily in punk, hardcore, garage rock, and experimental music. The catalogue built slowly through the 1990s (six releases) before expanding substantially in the 2000s (25 releases) and maintaining a steady output through the 2010s (19 releases). It operates the Splitting Headache Series as a sublabel and has worked with artists including David Thomas, Paul Hamann, and Frank Mauceri across more than 140 credited releases.
