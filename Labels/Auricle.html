From Discogs

Auricle was originally a cassette label, established in 1986 by experimental musicians, collectors, journalists/writers Alan Freeman & Steve Freeman from Leicester, England. It was launched (replacing Dark Star) to coincide with the first issue of Audion Magazine. Later, the label was resurrected/re-invented as a CD-R label. Auricle has issued lots of exclusive releases by various Freeman brothers projects, as well diverse international talent in the realms of underground and experimental music. It has championed many unknowns that have since become famous. All Auricle releases are official and authorised by agreements/contracts the musicians and/or licensed from them or their agents. Most are made in small edition runs, many in numbered/limited editions.

— Discogs label profile, ID 36198

Overview

Auricle is a Leicester, England label founded in 1986 by experimental musicians, collectors, and writers Alan Freeman and Steve Freeman, launched to coincide with the debut issue of their Audion Magazine and superseding their earlier imprint Dark Star. Beginning as a cassette operation, it was later reconstituted as a CD-R label issuing small and often numbered limited-edition runs. The catalogue is weighted heavily toward the 2000s and 2010s — the two most active decades by far — and spans the Freeman brothers’ own projects alongside a wide range of international underground and experimental artists across five sublabels: Auricle Music, Auricle Official Bootleg, Endgame Live, Escape From The Cage, and Plasm.
