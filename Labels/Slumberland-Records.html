Slumberland Records

Discogs ID: 15076
Parent: Slumberland Records LLC (ID: 1354337)
Founded: 1989 (College Park, Maryland) → moved to Berkeley CA (c. 1995) → Oakland CA
Owner: Mike Schulman
Status: Active
Genre: Shoegaze, Dream Pop, Indie Pop, Lo-Fi, Twee Pop

US independent label headquartered in Oakland, CA. One of the defining US labels for early 1990s shoegaze and indie-pop — home to Black Tambourine, Velocity Girl, The Aislers Set, Swirlies, Weekend, and many others.
