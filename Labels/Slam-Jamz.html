Overview

Chuck D launched Slam Jamz in 1996 as a label distributed through Columbia Records, using it as a home for music from his own extended circle. The roster spans several Chuck D-affiliated acts — C-Doc (The WarHammer), Gary G-Wiz, Kyle Jason, and The Impossebulls — alongside Chuck D himself. The catalogue is anchored in conscious rap, jazzy hip-hop, and boom bap, with the bulk of releases arriving in the 2000s (17 releases) and a smaller founding run in the late 1990s; activity tapered in the 2010s with six further releases.
