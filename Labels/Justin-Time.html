Justin Time Records

Discogs label ID: 40153 (main label) Parent: Justin Time Records Inc. (ID: 291725) Sublabel: Justin Time Essentials Collection (ID: 997497) Based: 5455 Paré, Suite 101, Montréal, QC H4P 1P7 Label Code: LC 1121 / LC 01121 Contact: info@justin-time.com

Overview

Justin Time Records was founded in 1982 by Jim West as a Montreal-based independent label focused on the Canadian jazz scene. It is one of the longest-running and most significant independent jazz labels in North America, with a catalog spanning jazz, blues, gospel, tango, musique actuelle, world music, and vocal music.

West originally concentrated on Canadian jazz talent — particularly the Montreal scene built around Biddle’s Jazz Club — and blues artists, with gospel a strong early strand (the Montreal Jubilation Gospel Choir series ran from 1986 onward). In the 1990s the label diversified significantly: tango (Quartango, Astor Piazzolla reissues), musique actuelle (Tim Brady), Latin jazz, and a suite of avant-garde and free jazz releases that brought in key figures from the New York loft-jazz and post-AACM world.

The label also operated Just A Memory Records (parent ID: 291725) as a sublabel for historic reissues and archival material — including early blues recordings and a Dave Van Ronk live album. By the 2000s, Justin Time was distributing European catalog and issuing Enja Records material in Canada, and had accumulated over 774 releases in the Discogs database.

Founder and Label Head

Jim West (Discogs ID: 300711) is the Canadian producer and president who founded Justin Time and its parent company, Justin Time Records Inc. He also founded Distribution Fusion III Inc., the Canadian distributor. West is credited as executive producer on at least 163 releases across the label — the single most prevalent producing credit in the catalog — and appears as a hands-on producer (not just administrator) on many core jazz titles from the 1980s through the 1990s. He also contributed occasional photography (7 releases). The breadth of his executive producer credits, running continuously from Live At Biddle’s (1983) through releases as late as 2023, documents a rare multi-decade owner-operator relationship with a single catalog.

Catalog Identity and Genres

Justin Time’s catalog is anchored in mainstream and traditional jazz, with Canadian artists forming the label’s spine. The dominant format is the small-group acoustic jazz record — piano trio, vocal-and-guitar duo, saxophone quartet. Key aesthetic threads:

  • Montreal scene jazz: Oliver Jones, Charles Biddle, Ranee Lee, Michel Donato, Don Thompson, Dave Young — the label essentially documented the Biddle’s Jazz Club milieu of the 1980s–90s
  • Canadian blues: Bryan Lee (15 releases), Bryan Lee & The Jump St. Five, Sue Foley, D.O.A. (2) (an anomaly — the hardcore punk band’s Let’s Wreck The Party, 1985)
  • Gospel: Montreal Jubilation Gospel Choir (20 releases), spanning 1986–2010
  • Avant-garde and free jazz: Billy Bang, David Murray, World Saxophone Quartet, Hamiet Bluiett, Frank Lowe, Paul Bley, Kip Hanrahan, Cecil Taylor — a significant strand running through the 1990s–2000s
  • Tango and world music: Quartango, Astor Piazzolla, Al Di Meola, Deep Rumba, Mor Thiam, The Gwo-Ka Masters, Dhafer Youssef, Rabih Abou-Khalil
  • Vocal jazz: Diana Krall (20 releases, some of her earliest recordings), Jeri Brown, Ranee Lee, Cécile McLorin Salvant, Cassandra Wilson
  • Reissues: The Just A Memory sublabel handled archival blues (Lightnin’ Hopkins, Muddy Waters, John Lee Hooker, Big Mama Thornton live-at-Rising-Sun recordings), plus jazz (Stéphane Grappelli, Chet Baker)

Key Avant-Garde and Free Jazz Releases

Billy Bang’s entire late-career run was documented on Justin Time, constituting one of the label’s most artistically significant bodies of work.

Vietnam: The Aftermath (2001) was the first of Bang’s two Vietnam veteran suites; it notably included Frank Lowe on three tracks, a year before Above & Beyond. The conductor on both Vietnam suites was Butch Morris, the New York conduction specialist.

Above & Beyond circulated initially as a radio edit promo in 2003 (cat# none); the full album proper was issued in 2007 (JUST 208-2), by which point Lowe had died (2003). It is documented as Lowe’s final studio recording.

Vietnam: Reflections (2005) expanded the ensemble with Henry Threadgill and James Spaulding, and added Vietnamese traditional musicians (Co Boi Nguyen on vocals, Nhan Thanh Ngo on dan tranh zither).

David Murray

David Murray’s output on Justin Time (15 primary releases, 1997–2009) spans his most varied late-career period. Notable entries:

  • Fo Deuk Revue (1997, JUST 94-2) — Afro-Cuban/jazz crossover
  • Speaking In Tongues (1999, JUST 118-2) — with Fontella Bass
  • Octet Plays Trane (2000, JUST 131-2) — Coltrane tribute
  • Yonn-Dé (2002, JUST 140-2) — with The Gwo-Ka Masters (Guadeloupean gwoka percussion ensemble)
  • Gwotet (2004, JUST 200-2) — with Pharoah Sanders and The Gwo-Ka Masters (Discogs IDs 1145566 and 874058 are two pressings)
  • Sacred Ground (2007, JUST 204-2) — David Murray Black Saint Quartet feat. Cassandra Wilson; produced by Murray; Andrew Cyrille on drums, Ray Drummond on bass, Lafayette Gilchrist on piano; liner notes by Ishmael Reed

Murray is also the third-highest producer credit on the label (17 releases), and the second-most prevalent musician credit in the “other” bucket (24 appearances across the catalog in playing/arranging roles).

World Saxophone Quartet

World Saxophone Quartet appeared on Justin Time from 1996 to 2006 with 10 releases. Key figures in the WSQ — David Murray, Hamiet Bluiett, Oliver Lake, Julius Hemphill — intersect with much of the avant-garde lineage the label supported. Bluiett Baritone Nation (Hamiet Bluiett’s project) also appears with 2 releases (1998–2002).

Kip Hanrahan

Kip Hanrahan (5 releases, 1985–1999) — the downtown New York composer/producer whose American Clavé label output spans post-bop, Latin, and avant-garde territories. His Justin Time releases include:

  • Vertical’s Currency (1985, JTAC 1010-2)
  • Days And Nights Of Blue Luck Inverted (1998, JTAC 1012)
  • A Thousand Nights And A Night – Shadow Night 1 (1998, JTAC 1043-2) — ensemble includes Billy Bang (violin), Henry Threadgill (sax), Don Pullen (piano), Andy Gonzalez (bass), Carmen Lundy (voice), Fernando Saunders (bass), Steve Swallow (bass)
  • A Thousand Nights And A Night – Shadow Nights 2 (1999, JTAC 1043)
  • A Few Short Notes From The End Run (1999, JTAC 1011 EP)

Paul Bley

Paul Bley has 11 releases on Justin Time spanning 1989–2008, making him one of the label’s most prolific free jazz figures. Details not pulled to this depth — stub entry.

Cecil Taylor

Cecil Taylor appears with a single release: The Owner Of The River Bank (2003, JENJ 3317-2) — a collaboration with the Italian Instabile Orchestra, issued on the Canadian Enja/Justin Time imprint.

D.O.A. (2)

An anomaly in the catalog: D.O.A. (2) (the Vancouver hardcore punk band) released Let’s Wreck The Party (1985, JTR-8404) on Justin Time. This appears to be a very early catalog entry before the label fully solidified its jazz identity.

Diana Krall’s Early Career

Diana Krall’s first three albums were released on Justin Time before she moved to GRP/Verve:

  • Stepping Out (1993, JUST 2-50) — 15 pressings documented; her debut
  • Only Trust Your Heart (1995, JTR 8451-2)
  • All For You: A Dedication To The Nat King Cole Trio (1996, JTR 8458-2)

The label’s role in launching Krall’s career is one of its most commercially significant contributions to jazz history.

Enja / Justin Time Connection

Several releases in the early 2000s bear catalog numbers prefixed JENJ — a co-branding between Justin Time and Enja Records (the Munich free jazz and contemporary jazz label run by Matthias Winckelmann, ID: 344112). This arrangement brought European avant-garde material into the Justin Time catalog for Canadian distribution, including releases by Cecil Taylor, Maria Schneider Orchestra, Bugge Wesseltoft, Dhafer Youssef, and Charlie Mariano. Matthias Winckelmann is credited as producer or co-producer on 12 Justin Time releases.

Just A Memory (Sublabel / Archival Imprint)

Just A Memory Records handled reissues and archival material. Key titles include live recordings from the Rising Sun Celebrity Jazz Club (Montreal) — a series of archival blues recordings featuring Lightnin’ Hopkins, Muddy Waters, John Lee Hooker, Big Mama Thornton, and Sonny Terry & Brownie McGhee. Also: Dave Van Ronk live, Herb Ellis / Ray Brown / Oscar Peterson Trio archival sessions. Jim West is credited as executive producer on these reissues, indicating they were drawn from existing tapes rather than new recordings.

Cécile McLorin Salvant

Cécile McLorin Salvant appeared on Justin Time for her first three albums:

  • WomanChild (2013, JTR 8580-2)
  • For One To Love (2015, JTR 8593-2)
  • Dreams And Daggers (2017, JTR 8608/9-2)
  • The Window (2018, JTR 8615-2)

Her association with the label brackets a period in which she won the Grammy for Best Jazz Vocal Album three years running.

Catalog Notes

  • Label used multiple catalog number prefixes: JUST (jazz), JTR (JTR 84xx series — early numbering), JTAC (Kip Hanrahan / American Clavé releases), JENJ (Enja co-releases), JTC (early cassette designations), JAM (Just A Memory reissues), JTDVD (DVD releases)
  • The label issued a notable DVD series including authorized rock band documentaries (Cream, Jethro Tull, Yes, The Moody Blues) — these sit outside the jazz core but are part of the catalog
