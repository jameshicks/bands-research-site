Vortex Records (2)

Discogs Label ID: 69558 Country: USA Label Code: LC 0987 / LC 00987 Parent: ATCO Records (which is itself a sublabel of Atlantic Records) Active: 1968–1970 (original US releases); reissued extensively thereafter, especially in Japan

US jazz imprint operating as a division of ATCO Records, the Atlantic Records subsidiary. Vortex was ATCO’s dedicated jazz and avant-garde outlet, releasing eleven original LPs between 1968 and 1970 along with a handful of singles. The roster was heavy with talent soon to become canonical: Keith Jarrett (three albums), Chick Corea (debut LP), Joe Zawinul (pre-Weather Report), and Sonny Sharrock (debut LP). The label also served as an early home for the avant-garde fringe of the late-1960s jazz world, releasing records by Byard Lancaster and Milford Graves-era musicians alongside more accessible jazz-rock crossover records by Steve Marcus and Dave Pike.

The catalog was numbered sequentially beginning at 2001, with stereo variants prefixed “SD”. A short singles series ran with catalog numbers in the 45-300 range. After the original run ended around 1970, Japanese licensees (Polydor, Atlantic/WEA Japan) kept pressing and reissuing the catalog through the 1970s, 1990s, and 2010s, making Vortex titles particularly well-represented in the Japanese jazz reissue market.

Key Release: Black Woman (1969)

Catalog number: 2014 (also CS 2014 cassette; TP 2014) Artist: Sonny Sharrock Discogs master ID: 49347

Sonny Sharrock’s debut LP as leader, recorded in 1969. The most historically significant release on the label — a foundational document of free jazz guitar and free improvisation, with Sharrock’s wife Linda Sharrock as co-vocalist throughout. Produced by Herbie Mann, who had already featured Sharrock in his own ensemble for Memphis Underground (Atlantic, 1969).
