Cooltempo

UK dance music label. Discogs label ID: 3208. Parent: Chrysalis Records (later Parlophone Records Ltd.).

Overview

Chrysalis-owned dance imprint active primarily in the late 1980s and 1990s. A vehicle for bringing American house music to UK release, including licensing from Strictly Rhythm. Simon Dunmore joined as club promotions manager in December 1989 under Ken Grunbaum, later moving into A&R and pushing the label toward house with acts like Juliet Roberts and Zoo Experience. US artists released on the label include Adeva, Tribal House, and Urban Soul.

Key Personnel

  • Ken Grunbaum — label head
  • Simon Dunmore — club promotions manager, then A&R (1989–1994)

Connections

  • Chrysalis — parent label
  • Strictly Rhythm — US partner label (licensing deal brokered by Dunmore)
