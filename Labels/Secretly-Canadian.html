Overview

Secretly Canadian is an American independent record label founded in 1996 in Bloomington, Indiana, by Chris Swanson, Ben Swanson, Eric Weddle, and Jonathan Cargill — all students at Indiana University, where Chris Swanson and Eric Weddle met through campus radio station WIUX. The label’s first release was a reissue of an album by North Dakota artist June Panic (Glory Hole, SC1, 1996). Its second release was the self-titled debut by Songs: Ohia (Jason Molina, SC2/SC4, 1996–97), secured after the Swanson brothers tracked down Molina’s email and drove to his in-store at Adult Crash in New York.

Early co-founders Eric Weddle and Jonathan Cargill both eventually departed — Weddle to form Family Vineyard. After Weddle left, Chris Swanson befriended Darius Van Arman in 1999 and Secretly Canadian joined forces with Van Arman’s Jagjaguwar, which relocated its offices to Bloomington. In February 2007, both labels — along with Phil Waldorf (ex-Misra Records) — announced a third imprint, Dead Oceans. The three labels combined as the Secretly Group, sharing offices and staff in Bloomington while maintaining separate editorial identities. Numero Group joined the collective in 2013; Secretly Group acquired a 50% stake in Merge Records in 2025. All labels are distributed by Secretly Distribution.

The label’s catalog spans indie folk, post-rock, avant-folk, art-rock, and experimental pop. Its most internationally prominent release was Antony and the Johnsons’ I Am A Bird Now (2004/2005, SC105), which won the UK Mercury Prize and sold 100,000 copies in its first two months. The War On Drugs’ Lost In The Dream (2014, SC310) was another critical and commercial landmark before the band moved to Atlantic. Secretely Canadian was listed #8 on Paste Magazine’s top 10 labels of 2018.

The label’s address history: 1703 N. Maple St. (through late 2000/2001) → 1021 S. Walnut St. (through ~2004) → 1499 W. 2nd St. (current). Contact: info@secretlycanadian.com. (Source: Discogs profile and Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretly_Canadian)

Secretly Group Context

The Secretly Group (Discogs ID: 1051624) comprises:

  • Secretly Canadian (35538) — founded 1996; indie folk, art-rock, avant-folk focus
  • Jagjaguwar (29121) — founded 1996; joined forces 1999; Bon Iver, Angel Olsen, Okkervil River, Sharon Van Etten, Unknown Mortal Orchestra, Dinosaur Jr.
  • Dead Oceans (94740) — founded 2007; Phoebe Bridgers, Mitski, Japanese Breakfast, Khruangbin, Slowdive, The Tallest Man On Earth
  • Numero Group (62008) — joined 2013; Chicago reissue label (Duster, Syl Johnson, etc.)
  • All Flowers Group — new division (2022); includes Ghostly International, drink sum wtr; hip-hop/R&B focus
  • Secretly Distribution (2801879) — in-house distribution arm for all labels
  • Secretly Publishing (1281867) — music publishing arm

In 2025, Secretly Group acquired a 50% stake in Merge Records.
