Independent cassette label run by Alan Freeman & Steve Freeman of Leicester, UK circa 1983-1986. Most releases on the label are projects involving the Freeman brothers, in the realms of experimental noise, industrial and electronic music (“Difficult Music” as Dave Henderson called it in his Sounds “Wild Planet” features). In 1986, along with the launch of Audion Magazine, Dark Star was re-branded as Auricle but the Dark Star label has more recently been revived and used for some CDr and download reissues.

Clam case editions of Dark Star cassettes are September 1984 to March 1986. All the earlier clam case editions were reissues, whereas some were issued as regular and clam case at the same time. The exact chronology has been lost.

Many very limited novelty packages also exist.

Overview

Dark Star (2) was an independent cassette label run by brothers Alan Freeman and Steve Freeman from Leicester, UK, active circa 1983–1986. It specialized in experimental noise, industrial, and electronic music—what journalist Dave Henderson dubbed “Difficult Music” in his Sounds “Wild Planet” column—with most releases involving the Freeman brothers themselves. The catalogue spans over 50 selected releases concentrated in the 1980s, with sublabels including Dark Star Distribution, Dark Star International, Old Wounds, and Säbel. In 1986, coinciding with the launch of Audion Magazine, the label was rebranded as Auricle; the Dark Star name was later revived for CDr and download reissues in the 2010s and 2020s.
