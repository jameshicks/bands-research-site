Overview

EXAG’ Records is a Brussels-based independent label founded in 2015, issuing analogue and digital releases across psychedelic, garage, krautrock, post-punk, and experimental rock. Its catalogue has grown steadily across two decades — 15 releases in the 2010s, 18 in the 2020s — with a roster that spans borders: French psych trio Slift, Israeli guitarist Tamar Aphek, and Belgian-rooted acts You Said Strange, Jean Fossat, and Lo’Spider. With 109 total credits across 33 releases, the label occupies a small but consistent position in the European underground rock circuit.
