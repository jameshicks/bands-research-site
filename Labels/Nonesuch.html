Nonesuch

American record label, founded 1964, known for its eclectic catalog spanning classical, world music, jazz, and contemporary pop/rock. Distributed by Warner Music Group. A.T. Michael MacDonald handled full recording/mixing/editing/mastering on Passion Flower — The Music Of Billy Strayhorn (1996) and engineering/editing/mastering on Fred Hersch’s Thelonious (Fred Hersch Plays Monk) (1998).
