Overview

Fort Evil Fruit is a Cork, Ireland cassette and digital label founded in 2011, dedicated to releasing underground music across experimental electronics, free improvisation, drone, ambient, and metal. Its catalogue of around 40 releases is split roughly evenly between the 2010s and the 2020s, indicating sustained activity over more than a decade. Recurring artists on the label include Delphine Dora, Patrick Shiroishi, Andy Cunningham, David Lacey, and Graeme Swinton.
