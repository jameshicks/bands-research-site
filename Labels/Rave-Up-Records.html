Rave Up Records

Italian independent record label. Discogs label ID: 83026. Website: raveuprecords.com.

Overview

Rave Up Records is an Italian label specializing in punk, garage, and related underground styles. It has numerous sublabels covering specific niches (American Lost Punk Rock Nuggets/Gems, Euro Punk Serie, Shell Shock Rock, Synthetic Shadows, etc.). Released the 2006 Italian LP pressing of The Hollywood Squares’ Hillside Strangler: Restrangled (RUR 047).
