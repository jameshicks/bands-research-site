Overview

Radbab Records is a mail-order CDR label run by André Herman Düne, primarily a vehicle for releases from his own circle of collaborators across the lo-fi indie, folk, and acoustic spectrum. Its catalogue runs from the late 1990s through the 2020s, with the bulk of output concentrated in the 2000s. Beyond Herman Düne’s own projects, the roster draws in Clémence Freschard, Stanley Brinks, David Tattersall of The Wave Pictures, and Olav Christer Rossebø — pointing to a small, tightly networked community of artists spanning France, the UK, and Norway.

Notes (web)

David Tattersall / The Wave Pictures — connection to André Herman Düne and Radbab Records

The connection between David Tattersall and André Herman Düne began when Tattersall and his friends drove to see Herman Düne perform at the Leicestershire Arts Centre (date not given in source, but prior to late 2003 based on later timeline). Tattersall gave André a CD-R of songs he had recorded in his parents’ kitchen in Wymeswold. About two weeks later André called him from a pay phone on the road and told him “the CD I had given him was the greatest album ever made.” awavepicturesresource.blogspot.com, “Escape Your Rubbish Family: David Tattersall on Herman Dune and John Peel”, 2011

Tattersall first heard Herman Düne on John Peel’s radio show, which prompted him to buy their album Turn Off The Light — reportedly unusual for him as he typically bought secondhand records. [same source]

The first documented Radbab release from this partnership is the 2003 collaborative album Streets of Philadelphia (André Herman Düne and David Tattersall), written and recorded while Tattersall stayed at André’s home during Christmas 2003, in Cleveland, Philadelphia, Saint-Ouen, and Bourg-La-Reine. thewavepictures.com album page The Wave Pictures also released Catching Light: The Songs of André Herman Düne (2006) on Radbab. [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wave_Pictures, as of 2026-11-17]

Herman Düne also brought Tattersall into the John Peel orbit, with a Peel Session recorded in January 2003.

André Herman Düne became Stanley Brinks after leaving Herman Düne in December 2006 and moving to Berlin. Collaboration between Brinks and The Wave Pictures continued after that name change, with Stanley Brinks and The Wave Pictures (2008, on Radbab) and further releases through 2019. [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wave_Pictures, as of 2026-11-17]

Olav Christer Rossebø — connection to Radbab Records

Olav Christer Rossebø is a Norwegian musician (mandolin, violin) who entered the Radbab/Brinks network as a member of Stanley Brinks And The Kaniks (and related configurations: The Flying Kaniks, The Space Kaniks). [discogs.com/artist/2363855-Stanley-Brinks-And-The-Kaniks] His Radbab-linked credits include: violin on Freschard’s Shh… (Radbab, 2011); and appearances on Alligator Twilight (2012) and Hafna Xita (2012), both released on b.y_records / Radbab Records. [stanleybrinks.bandcamp.com] No separate Radbab solo release by Rossebø has been located; his presence on the label is as a session/band contributor to Brinks-network releases.

No source gives a specific date for when Rossebø first joined the Brinks circle.
