Overview

Cachalot Records was a New York City label that operated through the 1980s as a division of Whale Productions Inc. Its fifteen-release catalogue concentrated on the experimental and electronic fringes of the New York scene, spanning New Wave, Synth-pop, Minimal, and Art Rock. The roster drew from both sides of the Atlantic — UK electronic artist Thomas Leer appeared alongside American acts including Ralph Carney, Eric Dufaure, Robert Derby, and Bill Ylitalo — suggesting a label with transatlantic reach unusual for a small independent of that era.

Founder and operation

Cachalot Records was founded and run by Eric Dufaure, a French national operating from a SoHo loft and later from 611 Broadway at Houston Street, New York. Dufaure ran the label under the corporate umbrella Whale Productions Inc. from approximately September 1977 through November 1986. After Cachalot, he went on to become Managing Director of the French music rights body SACEM and then President of EMI Publishing France. He is also credited as a producer of albums by French artists including Lio (who appears on the Cachalot catalogue). (comateens.com/the-band-2/associates/, as of 2026-10-03; bradspurgeon.com/tag/eric-dufaure/, as of 2026-10-03)

Thomas Leer / Cherry Red — transatlantic link

Letter From America (1982, cat. A RIPE 1) was not a standalone Cachalot signing: it was a co-release with Cherry Red Records, with the catalogue number matching Cherry Red’s UK series. The release compiled tracks from Leer’s two Cherry Red EPs — 4 Movements (1981) and Contradictions (1982) — onto a double 12-inch LP for the US market. (wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Leer, as of 2026-10-03)

The same Cherry Red / Cachalot arrangement is documented for other Cherry Red acts on the label’s roster. Medium Medium’s Hungry, So Angry 12-inch (1981) carries sleeve credits for both Cherry Red Music and Cachalot Music (ASCAP), with phonographic rights shared by Cherry Red Records and Cachalot Records / Whale Productions Inc. — confirming a formal US licensing deal between the two labels, not merely parallel distribution. Medium Medium’s album The Glitterhouse also appeared on Cachalot. (wikipedia.org/wiki/Medium_Medium, as of 2026-10-03)

Summary of the Leer connection: Leer was signed to Cherry Red in the UK; Cherry Red had a standing licensing arrangement with Eric Dufaure’s Cachalot for US releases of its roster. Letter From America was the vehicle Cherry Red chose to introduce Leer to US audiences, compiled specifically to package his two UK EPs as a single album-length release. Leer himself had no independent deal with Cachalot — the transatlantic link runs through Cherry Red.

Cachalot’s broader roster — not exclusively a Cherry Red outlet

Alongside Cherry Red acts, Cachalot also licensed or signed material from non-Cherry Red European sources. Malaria! (Berlin) released New York Passage on Cachalot (BIG 6, 1982), recorded at Sorcerer Sound in New York and mastered at Sterling Sound and Europadisk. The Comateens (NYC) had their debut self-titled LP on Cachalot (1981), originally issued by Barclay in France, suggesting Dufaure’s French connections also fed the label. (wikipedia.org/wiki/Medium_Medium; rateyourmusic.com/release/ep/malaria/new-york-passage-2/, as of 2026-10-03)
