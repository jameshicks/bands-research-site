Vinyl-on-demand

Discogs label ID: 21757 Country: Germany Sublabels: 3 (per Discogs) Total releases in DB: 637

German archival reissue label specialising in limited-edition box sets compiling rare and out-of-print experimental, industrial, and noise recordings, often pressed on vinyl for the first time.

Key Connection: Merzbow

In 2012 issued Lowest Music & Arts 1980–1983 by Merzbow (cat. VOD108; Discogs ID 3993515), a 10-LP + 7” numbered box set compiling the full catalog of Masami Akita’s self-released cassette imprint Lowest Music & Arts. Produced in limited, numbered edition; remastered.

Connections

  • Lowest Music & Arts — issued the 2012 box reissue of their full catalog
  • Merzbow — primary artist on the 2012 box
  • Masami Akita — credited for music, composition, and artwork on VOD108
