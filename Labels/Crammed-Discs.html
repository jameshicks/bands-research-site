Crammed Discs

Belgian independent record label, Brussels. Discogs label ID: 3950. Label code: LC 8689 / LC 08689. Address: 43 rue Général Patton, 1050 Brussels (Ixelles). Website: crammed.be.

Overview

Founded in 1981 by Marc Hollander (co-founder of Aksak Maboul), Crammed Discs emerged from the same late-1970s Brussels art-rock underground that produced Aksak Maboul and the affiliated ensemble around Vincent Kenis. The label’s first releases were tied directly to Hollander’s circle — Aksak Maboul’s Onze Danses Pour Combattre La Migraine (reissued CRAM 011, 1981), The Honeymoon Killers’ debut (CRAM 013, 1981), and Minimal Compact’s self-titled LP (CRAM 015, 1981).

Over four decades the label has moved through several distinct phases: post-punk and new wave (1981–1985), a world/African music turn through the CramWorld/Cramworld sublabel and collaborations with Congolese artists (mid-1980s onward), the Made To Measure ambient-and-cinema-music commissioning series (1984–present), a Brazilian/bossa nova period via the Ziriguiboom sublabel (late 1990s–2000s), a Congotronics phase anchored by Konono Nº1 (2004–2010s), and a continuing contemporary roster of global-influenced artists through the 2010s–2020s. Discogs records 1,368 releases and 17 sublabels as of 2025.

The label is owned by Crammed Discs S.P.R.L. Publishers on the catalog are Crammed Music (label ID 273839) and Les Editions De La Bascule (label ID 280008).

Founders and Key Personnel

Marc Hollander (Discogs ID: 69857)

Belgian musician and producer. Active in Belgian music from 1976 (Cos, various ensembles). Founded Crammed Discs in 1981 and has run it continuously since. Performer on many early Crammed releases — bass clarinet, saxophone, organ, percussion across Minimal Compact, The Honeymoon Killers, Bel Canto, Colin Newman, and others. Also produced or co-produced Zazou Bikaye’s Mr. Manager (1985), Bel Canto’s Birds Of Passage (1990, with IRS), Bebel Gilberto’s Tanto Tempo (A&R, 2000), and numerous compilations including the Freezone series (compiled/sequenced through the 1990s). As a performer he is also credited in Aksak Maboul, Les Tueurs De La Lune De Miel / The Honeymoon Killers, By Chance, Los Descontrolados, Mr. Big Mouse, and Cos (3). Aliases: Al Akefak, The Arc (11).

Vincent Kenis (Discogs ID: 69856)

Co-founder of Aksak Maboul with Hollander. Primary producer and engineer at Crammed across the label’s entire history. Produced Zap Mama’s debut Adventures in Afropea 1 (CramWorld, 1991/1992); produced, recorded, and mixed Taraf de Haïdouks’ catalog on Cramworld/Crammed (from Musique des Tziganes de Roumanie, 1991, through multiple releases to 2007); produced, recorded, and mixed Konono Nº1’s Congotronics (2004) and Congotronics 2 (2005); produced Staff Benda Bilili’s Très Très Fort (2009) and Bouger Le Monde! (2012); produced Kasai Allstars; produced The Honeymoon Killers’ debut and other early Crammed releases. Also mastered the Ziriguiboom Brazilian catalog extensively through the early 2000s. Alias: Bwana Mpuku.

Connections

  • Marc Hollander — founder and owner; performer, A&R, producer, mix engineer across entire catalog
  • Vincent Kenis — co-founder of Aksak Maboul; resident producer; responsible for world music catalog including Zap Mama, Taraf de Haïdouks, Konono Nº1, Kasai Allstars, Staff Benda Bilili
  • Aksak Maboul — Hollander and Kenis’s band; first Crammed release (CRAM 011)
  • Tuxedomoon — San Francisco art-rock group; largest single presence on Cramboy sublabel; also on Crammed proper (2004–present) and Made To Measure
  • Minimal Compact — Israeli post-punk group; most releases on main Crammed label (116 total across pressings); backbone of early catalog
  • Hector Zazou — French producer/composer; second-most prolific artist on Crammed (49 releases); central to Made To Measure series and world music strand
  • Steven Brown — Tuxedomoon member; solo and collaborative releases on Crammed/MTM
  • Blaine L. Reininger — Tuxedomoon member; MTM 3
  • Peter Principle — Tuxedomoon member; MTM 4 and 18
  • Colin Newman — Wire member; Commercial Suicide (1986), It Seems (1988)
  • Fred Frith — The Top Of His Head (MTM 21, 1989)
  • John Lurie — Lounge Lizards; film score commissions MTM 7, 14
  • Benjamin Lew — key MTM contributor; multiple collaborative releases
  • Bebel Gilberto — Ziriguiboom/Crammed; Tanto Tempo (2000) breakthrough
  • Yasmine Hamdan — Lebanese singer; contemporary Crammed artist since 2013
  • Jozef Van Wissem — MTM 40 (2014)
  • Cramboy — sublabel for Tuxedomoon (Discogs ID 9303)
  • Ziriguiboom — Brazilian music sublabel (Discogs ID 794)
  • SSR Records — dance/electronic sublabel (Discogs ID 812)
  • Les Disques du Crépuscule — sister label in the Brussels post-punk scene (independent, not a sublabel)
  • Factory Benelux — another Brussels-connected post-punk imprint of the era

Open Questions

  • Discogs profile text for Crammed is minimal (no founding narrative beyond year and founder name) — full label history would need web sources
  • Ludus (The Fall-adjacent Manchester group) appears in the research brief as an early Crammed artist but does not appear in the Discogs catalog for label ID 3950 — may be on a related imprint or the connection is via compilation
  • The Honeymoon Killers Discogs band entity (ID 186991) is listed as including Hollander and Kenis as members — but the first release (CRAM 013, 1981) is credited to both French and English name variants; band’s full membership not pulled in this research pass
