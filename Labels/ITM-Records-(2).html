ITM Records (2)

Discogs ID: 37719 Parent company: Ulli Blobel Schallplatten GmbH (Discogs ID: 572381) Label Code: LC 8301 / LC 08301 Origin: Wuppertal, Germany Active years: c. 1985–2014 (per catalog) Release count: ~208 releases (Discogs) Sublabels: 4

German jazz and free improvisation label founded and run by Ulli Blobel (born 1950, Peitz). Operating as the primary imprint of Ulli Blobel Schallplatten GmbH out of Wuppertal, ITM built a catalog spanning avant-garde jazz, free improvisation, free funk, blues, world music, and occasional classical reissues. Some releases are branded simply as ITM without the “Records” suffix. The label’s international reach was genuine: it recorded South African exiles (Chris McGregor, Johnny Dyani, Harry Beckett), New York no-wave and downtown artists (Last Exit, Bill Laswell, Peter Brötzmann, Sonny Sharrock), and West African percussionists alongside Wuppertal-area musicians. The house producers were Blobel himself and Jan Kazda, who arranged, played bass/guitar/keyboards, and co-produced across roughly 20 releases.
