EmArcy

American jazz sublabel of Mercury Records. Discogs label ID: 19900

Mercury’s dedicated jazz imprint, launched 1954. Named as an acronym/phonetic rendering of “MRC” (Mercury Record Corporation). Published major hardbop and mainstream jazz records of the 1950s–1960s. Key producers included Jack Tracy. Artists included Sarah Vaughan, Max Roach, Buddy Rich, Quincy Jones, Terry Gibbs, and others.
