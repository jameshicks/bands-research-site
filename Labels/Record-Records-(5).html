Overview

Record Records is a small Reykjavík independent label that launched in late 2007 as a distribution outlet for emerging Icelandic artists. Its catalogue peaked sharply in the 2010s — 39 of its 46 releases — across indie rock, pop rock, and synth-pop. Its roster included Of Monsters and Men, Mammút, Retro Stefson, and Júníus Meyvant, a cluster of acts that attracted considerable international attention during the early-2010s Icelandic indie wave. Activity has been sparse since 2020, with only four releases logged in the following decade.
