Not Not Fun Records

Discogs Label ID: 56819
Origin: Los Angeles, California
Run by: Amanda Brown (2) and Britt Brown
Catalog prefix: NNF
Contact: demos@notnotfun.com
Active: 2004–present (693 Discogs releases as of 2026)
Sublabels: 100% Silk, Bored Fortress 7” Club

Los Angeles DIY experimental label run by Amanda Brown and Britt Brown. One of the defining American underground labels of the 2000s and 2010s: began as a CDr/tape micro-label for Pacific Northwest and LA noise/psych, expanded into vinyl, hit a commercial peak c.2010–2013 with synth-pop and lo-fi italo crossovers, and has continued issuing experimental and ambient records into the 2020s. Spun off the dance sublabel 100% Silk and the singles club Bored Fortress 7” Club.

Founders

Amanda Brown (Discogs ID: 673479) — co-runs the label; performs music under the name LA Vampires (16 NNF releases, 2010–2014) and collaborates widely on the label. Also credited for artwork and design on 13 releases.

Britt Brown (Discogs ID: 812286) — co-runs the label; does the majority of physical design work (75 releases as designer/layout/artwork). Also plays on some releases (guitar, bass, instrumentation).

History By Era

2004–2006 — Founding / CDr Years (NNF001–NNF054)

The label launched in Los Angeles in 2004. Per the label’s own discography page, NNF001 was Have An Uptight Party — a cassette compilation (numbered edition of 150), confirmed as the debut release. (notnotfun.com/disco/nnf-001-050-2/) NNF002 was A-live by My Sexual Dad (CDR). NNF003 was Shark by My Sexual Dad (CDR and LP). NNF004 was So I’m Dude In This Equation by Weirdo/Begeirdo (CDR and LP). NNF005 was Lovers & Drunkards by Jacob Smigel (7”).

The label drew from the Pacific Northwest and broader US freak-underground from the outset. Earliest releases were CDrs and small-run tapes/cassettes. Key early artists: My Sexual Dad, Weirdo/Begeirdo, Foot Village, Bobby Birdman, Davenport, Abe Vigoda. The 2004 compilation Tapeworms Eat Bookworms (NNF015) — 259 Discogs pressings entries — functioned as an early scene document. By 2006 the label had crossed into split 7”s and LPs, with artists including Magik Markers, Raccoo-oo-oon, and Sword Heaven.

2007–2009 — Pacific Underground Peak (NNF070–NNF179)

The label’s most critically discussed phase. Vinyl releases proliferated; the catalog expanded rapidly. Core roster included:

  • Yellow Swans — Pete Swanson and Gabriel Mindel Saloman; 5 releases, 2007–2008
  • Eternal Tapestry — Nick Bindeman’s Portland group; 6 releases, 2007–2009
  • Raccoo-oo-oon — Iowa psych collective; 6 releases, 2006–2008
  • Barn Owl — Jon Porras and Evan Caminiti; 2007–2008
  • Sun Araw — Cameron Stallones; 13 releases total, beginning 2008
  • Pocahaunted — Amanda Brown and Bethany Cosentino (pre-Best Coast); 21 releases, 2006–2010
  • Inca Ore — Eva Saelens; Birthday Of Bless You (NNF107, 2008) and Silver Sea Surfer School (NNF175, 2009)
  • Peaking Lights — Aaron Coyes and Indra Dunis; 8 releases, 2009–2012
  • Ducktails — Matthew Mondanile (later of Real Estate); NNF152 (2009)
  • Heavy Winged — 5 releases, 2007–2009
  • Dolphins Into The Future — …On Sea-Faring Isolation (NNF155-LP, 2009)
  • Magic Lantern — 8 releases, 2007–2011
  • Robedoor — Britt Brown, Alex Brown (7), and M. Geddes Gengras; debuted 2005 (NNF018); 28 total releases — most of any NNF artist

2010–2012 — Synth / LA Vampires Era (NNF180–NNF270)

Amanda Brown’s LA Vampires project became the label’s most prolific. Cross-genre collaborations with Zola Jesus (LA Vampires Meets Zola Jesus, NNF195, 2010), Matrix Metals, and Psychic Reality pushed the label toward lo-fi italo disco and darkwave. Umberto (3) (16 releases, 2010–2016) emerged as a horror-synth anchor. Dylan Ettinger (13 releases, 2009–2012), Xander Harris (7 releases, 2011–2013), and Ensemble Economique (6 releases, 2010–2013) extended the synthesizer focus.

Weyes Blood (then Weyes Bluhd) released The Outside Room (NNF218-LP, 2011) — Natalie Mering’s early LP before major-label recognition.

2011–2014 — Maria Minerva / International Expansion (NNF210–NNF307)

Maria Minerva (Estonian artist; 14 releases, 2011–2014) became one of the label’s most internationally recognized names. Label also issued Sapphire Slows (Japanese, 5 releases), Blues Control, Gnod (UK), Torn Hawk, and Profligate. 100% Silk formalized as the dance sublabel around this period.

2013–2019 — White Poppy Era / Consolidation (NNF278–NNF360)

White Poppy (Crystal Dorval, Vancouver; 18 releases, 2012–2024) became the label’s longest-running active artist relationship. The roster contracted toward more focused experimental and ambient work. Notable: X.Y.R. (13 releases, 2015–2026, still active), Les Halles (8 releases, 2016–2018), Filthy Huns (8 releases, 2013–2021), H. Takahashi (Japanese ambient, 2 releases). 2814 (vaporwave/ambient duo) — NNF issued a cassette reissue (NNF314, 2015, purple chrome, edition of 200, with bonus track) of 新しい日の誕生, one of the most-circulated releases of the vaporwave era. The original had been released on HKE’s own Dream Catalogue as a CDR earlier in 2015. NNF co-founder Britt Brown called it “amazing cyberfuture ambient vaporwave.” (RA news)

2020–present — Ongoing (NNF361+)

The label continues issuing experimental LPs and cassettes. Recent artists: Acid Twilight, Voyage Futur, Dravier, Masahiro Takahashi, Coral Club, New Mexican Stargazers, Frunk29, Multi-Surface, metra.vestlud. White Poppy remains active on NNF.
