Lovitt Records

Washington DC area independent label. Discogs label ID: 68413. 229 releases on Discogs (unique masters). Website: lovitt.com / lovitt.bandcamp.com. Sublabel: Lovitt Archives (ID: 4303477).

Overview

Lovitt Records is the primary label for much of the DC-area post-hardcore / math-rock / indie-rock orbit of the late 1990s and 2000s. Key early signings include Frodus, Monorchid, Sleepytime Trio, Engine Down, and Milemarker. Later roster expanded to Pygmy Lush, Des Ark, The Mercury Program, Haram, and international acts including Trupa Trupa and Sóley. Both The Black Sea / Decahedron releases appeared on Lovitt (Love 32, LOV33).
