Ixor Stix

Independent record label based in Washington, DC. Discogs label ID: 135048. Bandcamp: https://ixorstixrecords.bandcamp.com/

Run by Christina Billotte, who used it to self-release her own projects from 1997 onward. No parent label. No sublabels. Mailing address on Discogs: P.O. Box 21811, Washington, DC 20009-1811.

Overview

Ixor Stix is Christina Billotte’s personal imprint, used across three distinct phases of her career. It debuted in 1997 with the first Crom-Tech 7” — an experimental post-hardcore project featuring Mick Barr — then became the home label for all of Billotte’s band Quix-o-tic through 1999. After a 23-year gap, the label re-activated in 2022 to release two LPs by The Casual Dots: a vinyl reissue of their 2004 debut and a new full-length, Sanguine Truth.

The catalog numbering shifted style between eras. The 1997–1999 releases used informal designations (“Ixor Stix #1,” “Ixor Stix 2,” “IXOR STIX III”). The 2022 reactivation used the compact “IX” prefix, at positions IX06 and IX07 — suggesting catalog numbers IX04 and IX05 (and possibly IX01–IX03 under the new scheme) exist but are not on Discogs, or that the IX numbering was a deliberate restart/renaming of the earlier series with the III position mapped onto a new numbering.

Guy Picciotto appears as producer or recorder on every label release except the first Crom-Tech 7”. Don Zientara engineered both 2022 Casual Dots LPs. Mastering on the 2022 LPs was by John Golden.

Open Questions

  • Catalog numbers IX01–IX05 are unaccounted for. The 2022 releases jump to IX06/IX07 while the 1997–1999 releases use a different naming convention. It is unclear whether there are unreleased or undocumented releases in the IX01–IX05 range, or whether the early “Ixor Stix #1 / 2 / III” releases were retrospectively assigned IX01–IX03 and IX04–IX05 remain open.
  • Recording location for both 2022 Casual Dots LPs: Don Zientara engineered, but Inner Ear Studios closed in October 2021. Needs web confirmation.
  • Full history and context of the Crom-Tech project: short-lived, avantgarde, featuring Mick Barr; no further Discogs detail beyond the single 7”.
  • Whether any releases exist between 1999 and 2022 that are not yet catalogued on Discogs.
