Suicide Squeeze

Discogs ID: 39381 (imprint); parent: Suicide Squeeze Records (75727)
From: Seattle, Washington
Founded: 1996
Founder: David Dickenson
Address: PO Box 80511, Seattle, WA 98108
Contact: david@suicidesqueeze.net
Active: 1996–present

Seattle independent label founded by David Dickenson in 1996. Started as a Pacific Northwest indie/lo-fi imprint with early releases from Modest Mouse, 764-Hero, and Elliott Smith; grew into one of the defining American indie labels of the 2000s–2010s, anchored by Minus The Bear, Russian Circles, The Coathangers, Pedro The Lion, and others. Catalog now exceeds 846 releases spanning indie rock, math rock, post-rock, garage, and experimental.

History

Pacific Northwest Origins (1996–2000)

The label launched in 1996 with S001 — 764-Hero’s “Now You’re Swimming” 7”. S002 followed the same year with The Scenic Vermont, and S003 was a two-song Modest Mouse 7”, A Life Of Arctic Sounds (1996), featuring the early lineup of Isaac Brock, Jeremiah Green, Eric Judy, and Dann Gallucci, recorded by Steve Wold. The early catalog defined a tight Pacific Northwest indie-rock orbit.

The Elliott Smith connection arrived quickly: “Division Day / No Name #6” (S-005, 1997) was one of his earliest 7” releases. A 1998 split 12” with 764-Hero and Modest Mouse, Whenever You See Fit (S008, co-released with Up Records), added remixes from DJ Dynomite D and Sientific American. Pedro The Lion appeared with Progress (S-011, 2000).

Expansion and Defining Roster (2001–2006)

Minus The Bear debuted on the label with “This Is What I Know About Being Gigantic” (S-017, 2001). Their debut LP Highly Refined Pirates (S-021, 2002) — produced and engineered by Steve Fisk, mixed by Fisk and Troy Tietjen on most tracks, mastered by Ed Brooks — established them as a label anchor. The band’s five-piece lineup: Jake Snider (vocals/guitar), Matt Bayles (keys), David Knudson (guitar/design), Erin Tate (drums), Cory Murchy (bass).

Other pivotal releases of this era: The Six Parts Seven’s Things Shaped In Passing (S-018, 2002); Hella’s Bitches Aint Shit But Good People (S-026, 2003) and The Devil Isn’t Red (S-031, 2004); The Aislers Set’s How I Learned To Write Backwards (S-025, 2003); and The Unicorns: 2014 EP (S-037, 2004).

Elliott Smith’s posthumous 7” “Pretty (Ugly Before)” b/w “A Distorted Reality Is Now A Necessity To Be Free” (S-028, 2003) — with sleeve design by Jesse LeDoux — appeared after his death in October 2003. Further Smith reissues and archival releases have continued to appear on the label.

Les Savy Fav appeared with a 7” in 2004 (S-034). Black Mountain’s Stormy High (S-055, 2006) and David Bazan’s first Suicide Squeeze release followed in 2007.

2007–2012: Post-Rock, Math Rock, Garage

Minus The Bear’s Planet Of Ice (S-065, 2007) — produced by Matt Bayles and Chris Common, mastered Ed Brooks — and Planet Of Ice cemented Bayles as the label’s in-house sonic architect. Bayles also produced and engineered Russian Circles’ breakthrough Station (S-070, 2008), featuring Brian Cook (bass), Dave Turncrantz (drums), Mike Sullivan (guitar), with Morgan Henderson on additional double bass; mastered Ed Brooks.

The Coathangers debuted with their “Shake Shake / Dreamboat” 7” (S-069, 2008) and have remained on the label ever since — now with 91 releases, the second most-prolific act on the label. This Will Destroy You’s Tunnel Blanket (S-093, 2011) and Black Dunes (S-098, 2011) followed. Cotton Jones’ Paranoid Cocoon (S079, 2009) and Tall Hours In The Glowstream (S-086, 2010) made them a cornerstone of the label’s more pastoral wing.

Julianna Barwick’s Pacing (S111, 2013), Dirty Beaches’ Lone Runner (S097, 2011), and La Luz (2)‘s debut Brainwash (S119, 2013) broadened the catalog further.

2013–Present: Garage, Psych, and Ongoing

The label moved into garage and psych territory with Guantanamo Baywatch, King Tuff, and L.A. Witch (debut LP SSQ155, 2017; 48 releases to date). Death Valley Girls signed with Darkness Rains (SSQ164, 2018) and became one of the label’s most active current acts (37 releases). Notable recent releases include a Courtney Barnett / Kurt Vile split (SSQ225, 2023) and ongoing work from Chastity Belt, Holy Wave, Michael Nau, and Night Beats.

Minus The Bear’s final album Farewell (SSQ175, 2021) closed out their 20-year run on the label.
