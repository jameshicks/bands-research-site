Overview

Dirty Water Records is an independent label founded in 2003, self-described as run by two committed individuals. Its catalogue of over 100 releases is concentrated in the 2010s and spans garage rock, punk, beat, and psychedelic rock — a resolutely retro-leaning set of sounds. The roster features acts including Muck And The Mires, MFC Chicken, and Mike Mariconda, reflecting a consistent alignment with raw, reverb-forward rock and roll. Activity has been lighter in the 2020s compared to the label’s peak decade.
