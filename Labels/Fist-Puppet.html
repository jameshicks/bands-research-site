Fist Puppet

US independent record label based in Chicago, Illinois (3058 N. Clybourn Ave., Chicago, IL 60618). Active roughly 1989–1996. The label’s name was taken from a song by Drunks With Guns, a Chicago noise-rock band. Fist Puppet operated two catalog series: the main FIST series (larger/more prominent releases) and a parallel PUP series (typically smaller or cassette-oriented releases). The label functioned as a US licensee and distributor for international artists — most notably as the North American imprint for several The Ex records from Ex Records — while also signing US-based artists outright.

The label’s Chicago address and the concentration of Chicago-area artists on the FIST series (Pitchblende, Coral (2), Hardvark, Sugar Shack (2)) suggests it was run out of Chicago’s independent music community. Steve Albini engineered four releases on the label, Brad Wood three, and Paul Leary produced two — consistent with the label’s reach into the Austin and Chicago scenes. Producer Dolf Planteijdt (The Ex’s longtime producer) appears on six releases, all of them Ex Records co-releases.

Connections

  • The Ex — the label’s most prominent artist; 11 primary-credited releases across both series; Fist Puppet functioned as The Ex’s North American licensing partner for Ex Records releases
  • Tom Cora — two collaborative LPs with The Ex (FIST 011, FIST 015); also co-producer on both; Fist Puppet is one of his top 3 labels by release count on Discogs
  • Ex Records — Dutch label; co-released several The Ex titles with Fist Puppet for the North American market
  • RecRec Music — Swiss co-release partner on Scrabbling At The Lock
  • Bad Livers — Austin string-punk band; Lust For Life (PUP 001, 1991) produced by Paul Leary
  • Pitchblende — Chicago noise-rock; 3 releases (FIST 012, FIST 022, and unnumbered Psychic Power Control)
  • Simply Saucer — Canadian proto-punk band; Cyborgs Revisited (FIST 004, 1989) is the earliest dated Fist Puppet release
  • Toiling Midgets — San Francisco post-punk; Sea Of Unrest (FIST 016, 1993)
  • Union Carbide Productions — Swedish psych-punk; two releases (FIST 014, PUP010)
  • Drunks With Guns — Chicago noise-rock; the label’s name comes from their song “Fist Puppet”
  • Steve Albini — recurring engineer; 4 releases
  • Dolf Planteijdt — recurring producer on all Ex co-releases
