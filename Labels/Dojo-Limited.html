Overview

Dojo Limited was a UK company serving as copyright owner for the Dojo label, incorporated in July 1990 as Jewelfuture Limited and renamed that September, then dissolved in April 2010. Its ownership passed through Castle Communications PLC (later Castle Music Ltd.) before ending under Sanctuary Records Group Ltd. The catalogue runs to around 174 releases concentrated almost entirely in the 1990s, covering punk, ska, Oi, psychobilly, and new wave, with roster credits including The Exploited and The Undertones. A sublabel, Dojo Early Years, also operated under its umbrella.
