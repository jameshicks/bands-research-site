Overview

Wax Records is a Japanese imprint of Tokuma Japan Communications Co., Ltd., dedicated to punk and new wave from its founding in 1987. The catalogue spans avantgarde and post-punk alongside the core styles, with output concentrated in the 1990s (56 releases) after a solid 1980s foundation (32 releases). The roster centers on key figures from the Japanese punk underground: The Stalin and its frontman Michiro Endo appear alongside Aburadako and Shozo Shiba. The label also maintained the Wax Great CD Masterpiece Series sublabel, pointing to a reissue and archival function within the catalogue.
