Bang! Records

Discogs ID: 130369
Origin: Spain
Websites: bang-records.net, munsterama.com, bangrecords.bandcamp.com

Spanish indie label with a focus on garage rock, punk, and underground rock reissues and new releases. Released Chrome Cranks’ retrospective The Murder Of Time (1993–1996) (2009) and the reunion album Ain’t No Lies In Blood (2012). ~285 releases in the Discogs database. Connected to the Munster Records universe (shares URLs with munsterama.com).

Note: Distinct from Bang Records (ID 21764), the 1960s US label co-founded by Bert Berns, and from Bang! Records (3) (ID 204864).
