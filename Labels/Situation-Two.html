Situation Two

Discogs ID: 5116 Parent: Beggars Banquet (parent_id: 2056) Active: 1981–1990s
Origin: UK

UK independent label founded by Peter Kent after he departed 4AD (where he had co-founded the label as Axis with Ivo Watts-Russell in 1980). Funded by and operated under the Beggars Banquet / Beggars Group umbrella. Artists included The Associates, The Cult (as Southern Death Cult / Death Cult), Gene Loves Jezebel, Alien Sex Fiend. Its first release was Biting Tongues’ Don’t Heal LP (SITU 1, 1981).
