New Rose Records

Independent record label. Paris, France. Active approximately 1980–1996.

French label that documented American and British punk and post-punk for the European market. Released Mission Of Burma’s live LP The Horrible Truth About Burma (1985). Also known for releasing material by The Gun Club, The Cramps, and other American underground acts.
