Tapete Records

German independent record label based in Hamburg, Germany. Parent label of Bureau B. Both labels founded by Gunther Buskies. Discogs label ID: 28060.

Overview

Tapete Records is the sister/parent imprint to Bureau B, run by the same Hamburg operation under Gunther Buskies. While Bureau B focuses on electronic, krautrock/Kosmische reissues and avant-garde electronic releases, Tapete covers a broader indie/alternative range.
