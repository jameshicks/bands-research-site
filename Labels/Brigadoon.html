Overview

Brigadoon is a Dutch independent record label based in Hilversum, operating as a sublabel of Flow Records B.V. (which also owns Idol Media). Founded in 1991 (per sublabel Brigadoon Vocal’s profile), the label specialised in Dutch vocal and cabaret/theatre music — jazz-inflected chanson, cabaretlied, and theatre songs performed primarily by Dutch artists. The label’s catalog runs from 1992 through at least 2010 and sits squarely in the tradition of Dutch kleinkunst (cabaret art song).

The label shares its Hilversum address and telephone number across its sublabels, and the house designer Egbert Luys handled visual identity across most releases. The executive producer Hubert Poell appears on 16 releases across main label and Brigadoon Vocal, suggesting a tight central operation.

The BIS catalog number series (BIS 001 onward) runs continuously across the main Brigadoon label and its Brigadoon Vocal sublabel — they share the same numbering scheme.

Catalog Highlights

Inaugural release — BIS 001 (1992) Lieder & Chansons by Marjol Flore. Germany pressing. The label’s first entry.

Astrid Seriese — Eclipse (BIS 005, 1993) Astrid Seriese’s debut on the label; produced by Seriese and Hubert Poell. Arranged by Berend van den Berg and Joep Lumeij. Recorded and mixed by Fir Suidema with engineering assistance from Holger Schwedt and Hans van Gelderen. Artwork by Egbert Luys. Features Gerbrand Westveen (saxophone), Evert Hekkema (trumpet), Joep Lumeij (bass), Peter Meuris (drums on one track).

Astrid Seriese — Secret World (BIS 010, 1994) Second Seriese solo album; co-produced with Hubert Poell. Musical director: Joep Lumeij. Arranged by Lumeij and Peter Meuris. Mixed by Fir Suidema and Holger Schwedt. Artwork/design by Egbert Luys.

Astrid Seriese Band — Trouble Of The World (BIS 012, 1995) The only Brigadoon release credited to the band unit. Band members: Astrid Seriese, Harm Wijntjes, Leo Bouwmeester, Marc Mommaas, Martijn van Agt, Peter Meuris, Theo Bodewes.

Wende Snijders — Au Suivant (BIS 086, 2005) Snijders’s major early statement on the label; features the Metropole Orchestra alongside a small ensemble (Bart Wolvekamp piano, Ro Krauss viola, Jeanne Biessen violin, Katja Didychenko cello, Rogier Bosman accordion, Sanne Van Delft bass). 40+ track live/studio set.

Wende Snijders — Chante! (BIS 115, 2008) CD + DVD format. Orchestra conducted by Jurjen Hempel; Metropole Orchestra on the DVD. Piano: Bart Wolvekamp; drums/percussion: Arthur Lijten; bass: Guus Bakker.

Wende Snijders — Nº9 (BIS 120, 2009) The label’s highest-pressing-count late release (3 pressings on Discogs), appearing across both main Brigadoon and Brigadoon Vocal.

House Design

Egbert Luys handled design, artwork and photography across the majority of Brigadoon releases (15+ releases on main label alone), making him the defining visual voice of the label. The design firm Tornado Vormgevers also recurs (3 main, 4 Vocal).

Scene Connections

  • Metropole Orchestra — the Netherlands’ premier light music/jazz orchestra appears on at least 6 main-label and 5 Vocal releases. Conductor Dick Bakker led the ensemble on multiple Brigadoon recordings.
  • Orkest De Volharding — Astrid Seriese is listed as a member on her Discogs artist page; this connects Brigadoon’s early period to the Dutch new music/leftist cabaret scene around De Volharding.
  • Tingel Tangel — another group Astrid Seriese belongs to per Discogs.
  • The Mathilde Santing Ensemble — Sebastiaan Koolhoven is a member; connects Brigadoon’s production to the broader Dutch art-pop/jazz chanson world.
  • KRO television — the label produced the soundtrack for the KRO series Wij Alexander (BIS 037, 1999) and other broadcast-linked recordings (KRO - Het Gevoel Blijft, Brigadoon Vocal).
  • Portret (3) series (sublabel of Brigadoon Vocal, ID 1278387) — “dedicated to the musical careers of important Dutch theatre personalities.” Artists covered: Jasperina De Jong, Jenny Arean, Willem Nijholt, Conny Stuart, Frans Halsema, Liesbeth List, Frits Lambrechts. This series is a significant archive of Dutch kleinkunst heritage.
  • Elektra Nonesuch — Astrid Seriese appeared as a voice credit on the US release De Stijl / M Is For Man, Music, Mozart (1994), cross-pollinating with the international classical/contemporary world.
