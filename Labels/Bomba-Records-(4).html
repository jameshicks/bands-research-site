Overview

Bomba Records (4) is a Japanese label under Bomba Records, Inc., built around reissuing jazz and Latin recordings for the domestic market. Its catalogue of over 400 releases concentrates on Brazilian and Latin styles — MPB, Bossa Nova, Samba, Latin Jazz, and Contemporary Jazz — with activity running from 1986 through the 2010s and peaking in the 1990s and 2000s. To serve distinct niches within that focus, the label spawned several imprints including Brasil 1500, Latin 1500, and Caribbean Classics (2). Artists such as Eumir Deodato and Arnaldo DeSouteiro appear prominently alongside the broader reissue catalogue.
