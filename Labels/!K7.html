Overview

!K7 is a German label founded in 1991, releasing and distributing across techno, downtempo, house, electro, and drum & bass. It operates a family of sub-labels including Stud!o K7 Records and 3Lux, and has also functioned as distributor or licensor on releases outside its own imprint. The roster has included Ken Ishii and Kruder & Dorfmeister; output peaked in the 1990s with 41 releases and has continued steadily into the 2020s. The label trades under several related names — !K7 Records (when the logo appears as !K7®) and the legacy variant Studio !K7 — reflecting its extended history and branding evolution.
