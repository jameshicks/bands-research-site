Overview

Carbon Records is an independent label founded and run by Joe Tunis since 1994, dedicated to noise, improv, drone, and heavy or otherwise strange rock. The catalogue opened in 1995 and has grown steadily across four decades — five releases each in the 1990s and 2000s, expanding to nine per decade through the 2010s and 2020s, suggesting a label that has remained active and productive rather than fading after an initial burst. Beyond Tunis himself, the roster includes Joe+N, Will Veeder, Muler, and Anthony Ferrari. No parent label or sublabels are recorded.
