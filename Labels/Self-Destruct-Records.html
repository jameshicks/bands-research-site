Self-Destruct Records

Independent punk/hardcore label from Louisville, Kentucky. Discogs label ID: 129123.

Founded around 1986 — catalog number 1 is Solution Unknown’s Taken For Granted E.P. The label ran through at least 1993 (last known Discogs entry is from that year), with Mike Bucayu of Solution Unknown serving as a central figure: he played on the founding releases and went on to produce most of the label’s catalog through the early 1990s.

Key Figures

  • Mike Bucayu (Discogs ID: 1413980) — guitarist/bassist on the two Solution Unknown releases; co-produced or produced the Big Deal (12) EP, and produced Undermine, Kinghorse, Dybbuk (2), Antman (5), Erchint, Blaze, Evergreen (5), Lather (4), and Plunge across 1990–1993. The label’s in-house production figure.
  • Howie Gano (Discogs ID: 323888) — engineer on the two Solution Unknown records (1986, 1987) and the Big Deal (12) EP; continued as engineer through the label’s entire run, credited on Louisville Sluggers I, Undermine, Dybbuk (2), Erchint, Evergreen (5), Lather (4), Plunge, and both Louisville Sluggers compilations.
  • David Pajo (Discogs ID: 254025) — drums/guitar on the two Solution Unknown releases; no further Self-Destruct production credits.

Scene Context

Self-Destruct Records is one of the Louisville hardcore/punk underground’s key institutional structures in the late 1980s–early 1990s. Its catalog documents the transition from the first-generation Louisville hardcore circle (Solution Unknown, 1986–87) through later acts (Kinghorse, Undermine, Dybbuk (2)) that carried the DIY infrastructure forward after Slint and Squirrel Bait had dissolved. The Louisville Sluggers compilation series (I, II, III — 1988, 1992, 1993) functions as a scene survey across those years.
