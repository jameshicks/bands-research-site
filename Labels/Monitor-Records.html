Overview

Monitor Records launched in 1999 in Maryland before relocating to California, operating as an independent outlet for indie rock, math rock, and experimental music. Its catalogue is almost entirely concentrated in the 2000s — 17 of its 18 releases fell in that decade. The roster spans markedly different corners of independent music: Cass McCombs’s singer-songwriter work, the Baltimore math-rock instrumentalism of Oxes, and the post-hardcore lineage of Jeff Mueller.
