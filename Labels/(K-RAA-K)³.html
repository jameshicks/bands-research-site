Overview

(K-RAA-K)³ is a Belgian experimental label founded in 1997 in Zedelgem as the direct successor to Toothpick, later relocating to Brussels. It operates as a broader cultural platform alongside its record output: staging concerts and festivals across Belgium and hosting a biweekly programme on Brussels community station Radio Panik. Its catalogue spans four decades — nine releases in the 1990s, growing through the 2000s, and peaking in the 2010s — in styles running from ambient and abstract to avant-garde and leftfield, built around a core roster that includes Köhn, Christophe Albertijn, and Jürgen De Blonde, with a sublabel, Trim, extending its range.
