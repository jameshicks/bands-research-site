Overview

Clay Records was an independent British label launched by Mike Stone in 1980, born out of his record shop in Stoke-on-Trent. The label’s first signing, local punk band Discharge, saw their debut single distributed from Stone’s car boot yet still crack the indie chart top 10 in Sounds. Through the 1980s — accounting for the bulk of its roughly 80 catalogued releases — Clay built a roster spanning punk, hardcore, heavy metal, and goth rock, with key acts including G.B.H., The Lurkers, Play Dead, and Demon. Members of the label operation went on to found Jungle Records in 1984, overlapping with Clay’s still-active run.
