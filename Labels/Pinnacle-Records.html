Overview

Pinnacle Records was a UK independent label that began releasing in 1974, originally operating under the parent company Pinnacle Electronics Ltd., later reorganized as Pinnacle Entertainment Ltd. Its catalogue is weighted toward the 1970s (42 releases) spanning disco, funk, pop rock, and soul, with a secondary cluster of 26 releases in the 1980s as the company expanded its operations as a UK distributor. The label ran several imprints including Pinnacle Disco, Rialto, Firebird, A&R Records (3), and Street Beat (2). Artists on its roster included Flintlock, Mike Holoway, Nick Straker, Don Gallacher, and Mankind (3).
