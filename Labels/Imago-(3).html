Overview

Imago is a sublabel of The Imago Recording Company, active from 1990 and concentrated almost entirely in that decade with 92 of its 94 releases. The catalogue ranges across alternative rock, pop rock, hard rock, alternative metal, and hardcore. Its roster was notably eclectic, housing both Henry Rollins and Rollins Band alongside pop-inflected acts like Paula Cole and Australian rockers Baby Animals. Activity effectively ceased after the 1990s, with only isolated releases appearing thereafter.
