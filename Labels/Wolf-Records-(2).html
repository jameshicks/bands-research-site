Wolf Records (2)

Discogs ID: 79109 Location: Vienna → Schöngrabern, Austria Founded: 1982 Website: wolfrec.com

Austrian independent blues and roots label, founded in Vienna in 1982 by four members of the Vienna Blues Fan Club. One of Europe’s most dedicated specialist outlets for Chicago blues, country blues, and Delta reissues, as well as new recordings by American blues artists. The label eventually relocated its base to Schöngrabern, a village north of Vienna. CEO and owner Hannes Folterbauer has run the operation since its founding; co-founder Herbert Pessiak was a core partner until his death in 2011.

History

Founding (1982)

Wolf Records grew directly out of the Vienna Blues Fan Club. Folterbauer and three fellow members — including Herbert Pessiak — turned their collector network into a label to document artists they felt were underrecorded in the US and unknown in Europe. The first catalog releases appeared in 1982, with Brewer Phillips’s Ingleside Blues (W 82 007) among the earliest studio sessions. Early catalog entries include Homesick James / Snooky Pryor (1979) and Henry Townsend sides (1981). Albums followed a 120.xxx catalog number scheme; various archival sublabels used their own series codes.

Chicago Blues Sessions and Core Catalog

The flagship series Chicago Blues Session (sublabel Chicago Blues Session) became the label’s defining output: live and studio recordings made in Vienna or Chicago, documenting working electric blues bands. Magic Slim & The Teardrops became the most prolific roster act with 30 releases. The series stretched across at least 13 volumes; Vol. 3 (1986, 120.849) — with John Primer on guitar, Nick Holt on bass — is an early landmark.

Archival Reissues and Chronological Series

Alongside new recordings, Wolf maintained multiple archival sublabels covering pre-war and postwar country blues: Best Of Blues, Best Of Country Blues, Blues Classics (2), The Best Of Country Blues, Louisiana Swamp Blues, Texas Blues Party, and The La Salle Chicago Blues Recordings. These released sides by Big Bill Broonzy, Yank Rachell, Charley Patton, and others. A Collector’s Series (11) sublabel ran to at least 11 volumes.

International Growth

The label formed a corporate subsidiary, Wolf Records International GmbH, and distributed widely across Europe. Dana Gillespie (9 releases) and Eddie Taylor Jr. (8 releases) represent the label’s reach beyond the core Chicago artists. Al Cook (2) (11 releases) is a notable Austrian blues artist on the roster.
