Overview

Beggars Banquet Germany was a defunct sub-division of SPV GmbH, created to distribute releases licensed from Beggars Banquet Records Ltd. for the German market. It operated exclusively through SPV’s catalogue numbering system (label code LC 5485) and was active primarily in the 1990s, issuing 32 of its roughly 33 releases in that decade. The catalogue leaned heavily into goth rock, indie rock, and alternative rock, with key licensed artists including Fields of the Nephilim, Carl McCoy, Peter Murphy, and Buffalo Tom. The label’s fortunes were tied to SPV GmbH, which later went through insolvency proceedings, and the division has since ceased activity.
