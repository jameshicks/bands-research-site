Overview

No. 6 Records was a boutique independent label founded by Terry Tolkin, whose path through the music world ran from the floor of Manhattan’s legendary 99 Records store on Macdougal Street through a stint at Of Factory New York before he moved on to A&R at Elektra, where he signed Pixies and Luna. The label operated almost entirely in the 1990s, with one title in 1989 and twenty-five releases across the following decade. Its catalogue covered indie rock, lo-fi, noise, and experimental territory, with roster figures including engineer and producer Wharton Tiers, Mark Robinson, Unrest, and Phil Krauth. A related sublabel, No. 6 (2), also operated under the imprint.
