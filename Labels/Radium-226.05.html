Overview

Radium 226.05 launched in 1983 as an art gallery in Gothenburg, Sweden, co-founded by Carl Michael von Hausswolff and Ulrich Hillebrand, before expanding into a multidisciplinary operation: record label, magazine, and organizer of film and computer music festivals. Erik Pauser joined as a co-director in 1986. The label operated independently through the 1980s — its most active period, with 30 releases — before being sold to MNW Records in 1991, after which a further 19 releases appeared into the 1990s. Its catalogue spans alternative rock, punk, experimental, synth-pop, and new wave; its roster included Union Carbide Productions, whose members Ebbot Lundberg and Chips Kiesbye later gained wider recognition in Swedish rock. The label also spawned a German imprint, Radium Germany 226.05.
