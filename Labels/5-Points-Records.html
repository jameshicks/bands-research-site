5 Points Records

American independent record label based in New York. Parent label: Nudgie Music LLC (Discogs ID: 1450615), which also owns the sibling imprint 5 Points VIP (Discogs ID: 4449331). Discogs label ID: 25716.

Overview

5 Points Records is a small New York independent active from approximately 2004 to 2010 (with a 2024 reissue of its most famous release). Its catalog of roughly 31 Discogs entries is eclectic: North Indian classical music, Turkish/Middle Eastern world fusion, indie pop singer-songwriters, electronic / IDM, progressive house, hardcore punk, and NYC indie-rock compilations. The label is best known retrospectively for being the home of Lana Del Rey’s debut album — released while she was still operating under the name Lana Del Ray — but that release was in some respects uncharacteristic of the catalog around it.

The label’s only known URL was www.5pointsrecords.com; it is also documented on the Lana Del Rey fan wiki. No personnel behind the label (founders, A&R) are identified in the Discogs database.

Catalog

World / Classical

The label’s earliest releases, beginning in 2004, were world music titles. Most prominent was Indian sarangi master Ustad Sultan Khan (Discogs ID: 132582), whose material appeared across multiple configurations:

The 2004 promo mini featured remixes from Thievery Corporation and Ralphi Rosario — an early indication of the label’s interest in dance-floor reworkings of world material.

Turkish multi-instrumentalist and composer Omar Faruk Tekbilek (Discogs ID: 28903) contributed a 2009 album, Rare Elements (FBT0152/FPT0152), which the label released as a remix package featuring contributions from Amon Tobin, DJ Cheb I Sabbah, Flosstradamus, Joe Claussell, Nickodemus, Tommie Sunshine, and Kodomo (label-mate Chris Child).

NYC Indie Compilations (Grand Life series)

5 Points released two branded compilations tied to the Grand Life boutique hotel group in Manhattan — a fairly common mid-2000s arrangement pairing upscale hospitality brands with emerging indie rock:

Grand Life Soho New York (FPT0125, 2005, CD Compilation) Featured: !!!, Ted Leo / Pharmacists, TV on the Radio, Lansing-Dreiden, Ambulance LTD, French Kicks, The Postal Service, White Hassle, The A.M., Earl Greyhound, The Rogers Sisters, Downtown (5), These Bones, Chris Leo’s Vague Angels. Art direction by Greg Foley. A snapshot of mid-2000s NYC/Brooklyn indie rock.

Grand Life Tribeca New York (FPT0132, 2006, CD Compilation) Featured: M83, Gang of Four, Maxïmo Park, Hot Chip, Out Hud, Tom Vek, Beat Pharmacy, The Frames. A somewhat wider international net than the Soho edition, tilting more electro/post-punk.

Electronic / IDM

Genres: Electro, IDM, Minimal. Remixers on the Concept 11 EP included Taylor Deupree and Jordan Lieb. Kodomo also contributed a remix to Omar Faruk Tekbilek’s Rare Elements (2009), cementing the cross-roster connection.

Pop / Soul / Country

Kassie DePaiva (real name Katherine Virginia DePaiva; Discogs ID: 2689064) — an American actress best known for daytime television — released two projects on 5 Points:

  • No Regrets (2005, CD Album) — 12-track country/pop album; catalog number not assigned
  • I Want To Love You (FPT0137, 2007, digital) — single

Emilia Sosa (also credited as Emilia (13); Discogs ID: 950680) released:

  • Burning In The Sun (FPTO136, 2006, digital single)
  • Rhythm Of Life (FPT0138, 2007, CD Promo) — 11-track progressive house / electronic album
  • Rhythm Of Life (Remixes) (FPTO142, 2007, digital)

Lizzy Grant / Lana Del Rey

The label’s two credits under the Lana Del Rey/Lizzy Grant name are:

Kill Kill EP (2008, digital, 3 tracks)

  • Credited to: Lizzy Grant (Discogs artist ID: 2456896)
  • Tracks: “Kill Kill,” “Yayo,” “Gramma (Blue Ribbon Sparkler Trailer Heaven)”
  • Producer: David Kahne (uncredited in original release)
  • Photography: Caroline “Chuck” H. Grant (uncredited) — Lana Del Rey’s sister
  • Design: Michael Kahne (Mike Kahne, uncredited) — David Kahne’s son

Lana Del Ray A.K.A. Lizzy Grant (January 15, 2010, digital MP3 download, 13 tracks)

  • Credited to: Lana Del Rey (as “Lana Del Ray” — with ‘a’)
  • Cat #: none; Label: 5 Points Records
  • Producer: David Kahne
  • Photography: Caroline “Chuck” H. Grant (Chuck Grant, uncredited)
  • Genres: Folk, Indie Pop, Pop
  • Tracklist: “Kill Kill,” “Queen Of The Gas Station,” “Oh Say Can You See,” “Gramma (Blue Ribbon Sparkler Trailer Heaven),” “For K Part 2,” “Jump,” “Mermaid Motel,” “Raise Me Up (Mississippi South),” “Pawn Shop Blues,” “Brite Lites,” “Put Me In A Movie,” “Smarty,” “Yayo”

The album was released exclusively as a digital download and gained minimal commercial attention at the time. Del Rey subsequently bought back the rights and withdrew it from sale. A 2024 European vinyl reissue appeared on the same master (Discogs ID: 31676525, cat# 65938459), evidently authorized as Del Rey’s commercial profile grew.
