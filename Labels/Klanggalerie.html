Overview

Klanggalerie is a Viennese independent label founded in 1997 by Walter Robotka as the successor to his earlier imprint Syntactic. It specialises in CD releases of both new material and reissues of works previously unavailable in digital format — a dual mandate that has made it one of the most significant archival outlets for post-punk, industrial, and experimental music from the 1980s and 1990s, alongside an active programme of new recordings. As of 2025 the catalogue runs to well over 500 releases, numbered sequentially under the prefix “gg” (e.g. gg2, gg527).

The label is essentially a one-family operation: Walter Robotka handles A&R, production, and liner notes; Lisa Robotka is responsible for the overwhelming majority of artwork, layout, and design work (credited on approximately 180 releases). The house mastering engineers — Christian Loebenstein and Martin Bowes — between them have mastered or remastered around 136 releases. Bowes (of Attrition) is the primary engineer for the post-punk and industrial side of the catalogue; Loebenstein handles the more experimental and new-music end.

Genres and Aesthetic

The catalogue spans:

  • Industrial / power electronics / noise — Konstruktivists, Nocturnal Emissions, Bourbonese Qualk, Blackhouse, The New Blockaders, Hastings Of Malawi, Scattered Order
  • Post-punk / synth / art rock — Eric Random, Eric Random And The Bedlamites, Thomas Leer, Section 25, Chakk, Blurt, Hula (2), C Cat Trance
  • Avant-garde / electro-acoustic / improv — Asmus Tietchens, Fred Frith, Jac Berrocal, Sophie Agnel, Frank Gratkowski, Rodrigo Amado, Joke Lanz, Charles Hayward
  • Art pop / outsider — The Residents (31 releases — the label’s single largest artist), Renaldo & The Loaf, Snakefinger, Charles Bobuck, Hardy Fox
  • Ambient / drone / tape-loop — Rapoon, Vidna Obmana, Edward Ka-Spel, The Legendary Pink Dots
  • Neue Deutsche Welle / krautrock-adjacent — Sprung Aus Den Wolken, Hans-Joachim Roedelius
  • Free jazz / new music — Un Drame Musical Instantané, Étant Donnés, Nurse With Wound (adjacent)
  • Austrian domestic acts — Non Toxique Lost (11 releases), GRAF+ZYX, Cadaverous Condition, Tábor Radosti, Gottfried Distl

The reissue programme is focused principally on acts whose original releases were cassette-only, small-run vinyl, or otherwise unavailable on CD or digital, making Klanggalerie an essential source for the post-industrial and post-punk underground of the 1980s.

Key Personnel

Label side

  • Walter Robotka (Discogs ID: 524875) — founder, label owner; credited as producer on 13 releases, executive producer, compiler, liner notes. Name variations: W. Robotka, Walter. Also a musician: member of Sandy & Boris (ID 125440), Statik (2) (ID 88492), and The Altai Chamber Orchestra (ID 184376).
  • Lisa Robotka (Discogs ID: 756296) — artwork, layout, design; credited on approximately 180 releases. The visual identity of the label.

House mastering / engineering

  • Christian Loebenstein (Discogs ID: 756297) — mastered or remastered approximately 74 releases; handles much of the new-music and experimental catalogue.
  • Martin Bowes (Discogs ID: 249281) — mastered or remastered approximately 62 releases; primarily the post-punk and industrial reissue programme. Of Attrition.
