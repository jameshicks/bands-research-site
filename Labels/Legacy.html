Overview

Legacy is Sony Music Entertainment’s dedicated catalog and reissue imprint, with roots in a Columbia Records imprint introduced in 1960 for archival recordings. CBS Records relaunched it in 1990 as a focused reissue and repackaging operation, and it has continued under CBS’s successive corporate owners ever since. With nearly 19,000 Discogs credits, its catalogue draws heavily from Columbia’s holdings — dominated by Pop Rock, Blues Rock, Country, Classic Rock, and Folk Rock — and swelled notably during the 1990s reissue boom. The label operates sub-imprints including Buddah Records and Certified Classics, with production work handled by figures such as Vic Anesini and Mark Wilder.
