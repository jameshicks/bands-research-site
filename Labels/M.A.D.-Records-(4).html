Overview

M.A.D. Records (4) was a Russian pirate label based in Moscow, active from at least 1993 through the 2000s, with the bulk of its catalogue concentrated in the 2000s (52 releases versus 12 in the 1990s). Its roster drew heavily from Western experimental and industrial artists — including Coil and The Residents — whose official releases were rarely distributed in Russia during that period. The label operated three sublabels (Diskus Originalikus, Huge Online, and Wonga Records) and ranged across experimental, industrial, ambient, alternative rock, and hardcore.
