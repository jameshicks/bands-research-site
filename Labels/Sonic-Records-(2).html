Overview

Sonic Records is a Polish label founded in 1991 under parent company Sonic Sp. z o.o., releasing Polish pop, rock, and alternative music. Its catalogue was most active in the 1990s (112 releases) before tapering through the 2000s and 2010s, with only one release logged in the 2020s. Beyond domestic signings, the label acted as exclusive Polish distributor for a wide roster of international indie imprints — Beggars Group (XL Recordings, 4AD, Beggars Banquet, Matador, Rough Trade), Domino, Epitaph, Anti-, Warp, City Slang, and others — bringing artists like Dead Can Dance, Cocteau Twins, and Lisa Gerrard into the Polish market. The label also appears on some releases simply as “Sonic” (third logo), and operates the sublabel Musicola.
