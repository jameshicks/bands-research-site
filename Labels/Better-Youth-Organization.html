Overview

Better Youth Organization (BYO Records) is an American punk and hardcore label founded in Los Angeles in 1982 by Youth Brigade brothers Shawn Stern and Mark Stern. Its catalogue spans three active decades — 24 releases in the 1980s, 32 in the 1990s, and 43 in the 2000s — before the label ceased releasing new music in 2009. The roster drew from the US punk and hardcore underground, with 7 Seconds and Kevin Seconds among its most prominent acts. In 2022, Trust Records announced a formal partnership with BYO to reissue the catalogue across digital streaming platforms and physical media.
