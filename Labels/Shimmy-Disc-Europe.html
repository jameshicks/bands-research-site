Overview

Shimmy Disc Europe was the Netherlands-based licensing and distribution arm of the New York independent label Shimmy Disc, operated through Semaphore Records of the Netherlands. Its catalogue spans from 1988 into the late 1990s, with the bulk of its roughly 40 releases coming in the 1990s, covering alternative rock, lo-fi, experimental, and psychedelic rock. The roster drew from the same pool as its parent label, with Kramer, Don Fleming, David Licht, and Dave Larr among the most credited artists. The imprint became inactive when Semaphore went out of business in the late 1990s.
