Hausmusik

German record label. Associated with the Munich experimental/indie scene. Released records by Alles Wie Gross (1997 split with A Million Mercies) and Carlo Fashion (Pieces For Acoustic Instruments And Synthesizers, 2001). Doro Tachler appears on both. Markus Acher of The Notwist also appears on the 1997 Alles Wie Gross releases. Carl Oesterhelt (of Slut / The Notwist circle) is connected via the Carlo Fashion release.
