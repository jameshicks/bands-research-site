rooArt

Sydney-based Australian independent record label, active primarily 1989–late 1990s. Distributed by Phonogram, later BMG. Parent company: rooArt Pty. Ltd. (Discogs label ID: 34489). Sublabels include rooArt Jazz and Young Blood (6).

Overview

rooArt was one of the key labels of the early-1990s Australian alternative boom, operating out of Sydney with Phonogram distribution giving its releases national and some international reach. The label signed Ratcat (whose Tingles was also issued on Mercury in the US), as well as other acts in the Australian indie/alternative space. A 2001 BMG-distributed compilation series suggests the label remained a catalog entity into the early 2000s.
