Definitive Jux

Discogs ID: 3847 Also known as: Def Jux; Definitive Jux, Inc. Origin: New York City, USA (45 John Street Suite 711, later 199 Lafayette St.) Active: c. 1999–2010 (hiatus); 393 releases in Discogs Co-founders: El-P (Jaime Meline) and Amaechi Uzoigwe

Note: Discogs ID 358791 is the parent corporate entity (Definitive Jux, Inc.); ID 322344 is El-P’s separate production/publishing company — neither should be confused with this label.

Independent underground hip-hop label co-founded by El-P and Amaechi Uzoigwe after El-P’s exit from Company Flow. For most of its active decade it was the central node of NYC underground rap, releasing the first records by Aesop Rock, Cannibal Ox, RJD2, Mr. Lif, Murs, Cage, Camu Tao, The Perceptionists, and others. El-P simultaneously served as house producer, executive producer, and the label’s most prominent performing artist. The label went on hiatus around 2010; El-P subsequently formed Run The Jewels with Killer Mike.

Pivotal LPs in detail

Labor Days — Aesop Rock (2001, DJX-13 CD). Aesop’s commercial debut; produced largely by Blockhead. One of the earliest and most impactful Def Jux full-lengths.

The Cold Vein — Cannibal Ox (2001, Def Jux). Duo from Harlem: Vast Aire (Shamar Gardner) and Vordul Mega (Theodore Arrington). Entirely produced by El-P. Widely regarded as one of the label’s landmark releases.

Fantastic Damage — El-P (2002, Definitive Jux). El-P’s solo debut LP. 16 tracks, all produced by El-P. Established the label’s sonic identity: dense, industrial hip-hop.

I Phantom — Mr. Lif (2002, DJX37). Produced across tracks by El-P, Fakts One, Insight (2), and NASA (3). Features Aesop Rock, Jean Grae, Akrobatik, and El-P. Mastered by Emily Lazar.

Black Dialogue — The Perceptionists (2005, DJX103CD). Mr. Lif and Akrobatik. El-P executive producer; producers include Fakts One, El-P, Camu Tao, Cyrus Tha Great, Willie Evans Jr. Features Guru, Humpty Hump, Phonte.

I’ll Sleep When You’re Dead — El-P (2007, DJX137, 2xLP). El-P’s second solo LP. Notable for eclectic guest list: Cedric Bixler-Zavala and Omar Rodriguez-Lopez (The Mars Volta) on “Tasmanian Pain Coaster”; Trent Reznor (co-producer) and Atticus Ross on “Flyentology”; Chan Marshall (Cat Power) on “Poisenville Kids No Wins”; Mr. Lif, Cage, Murs, Aesop Rock, Camu Tao also appear. Engineer/mix: Joey Raia; mastered by Michael Sarsfield.
