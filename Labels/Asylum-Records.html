Asylum Records

Discogs ID: 33574

American record label founded by David Geffen in 1971 in Los Angeles. Home to the LA singer-songwriter scene of the early 1970s; artists including Jackson Browne and Neil Young had work recorded at Sunset Sound Recorders during this era. Judee Sill was the first artist signed and the first to release on the label — her promo single “Jesus Was A Cross Maker” (AS 45-11000, 1971) and debut LP Judee Sill (SD 5050, 1971) preceded the Eagles, Linda Ronstadt, and the rest of the Laurel Canyon roster on the label.

Key Releases (Band/Danko-related)

Released Bob Dylan’s Planet Waves (1974) and Before The Flood (1974, with The Band), both engineered by Rob Fraboni. Rick Danko on bass throughout both releases. Also released Blondie Chaplin’s solo debut (1977, produced by Fraboni).
