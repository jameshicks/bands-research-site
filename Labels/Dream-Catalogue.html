Dream Catalogue

UK-based digital and physical label. Discogs label ID: 919110. No parent label. Total Discogs releases: 683. Active from at least 2014 to 2023+.

Central label in the vaporwave/lo-fi electronic scene. Primary home for HKE, Hong Kong Express, 2814, and related artists.

Notes (web)

Founding: January 2014 by HKE (David Russo). Launched the same day as HKE’s debut Hong Kong Express release 浪漫的夢想. Conceived as a Bandcamp collective — “a hobbyist thing to put out mine and some friends releases on Bandcamp, to present kind of a ‘collective’ aesthetic.” (Red Bull Music Academy interview, 2014; MMJ interview with HKE)

Breakthrough: 新しい日の誕生 (Birth of a New Day, January 2015) was the label’s first physical release and became the commercial and critical breakthrough. Catalog numbers use a DREAM_ prefix (DREAM_86 for vinyl/cassette of that album; DREAMCD_1 for CD-R). Not affiliated with Not Not Fun’s NNF prefix — NNF314 is a separate NNF cassette reissue of the same album. (Discogs release entries)

Role in vaporwave: In its early years Dream Catalogue was regarded as the preeminent vaporwave label. Early roster included Telepath テレパシー能力者, death’s dynamic shroud, and VAPERROR. (vaporwave.wiki/wiki/Dream_Catalogue)

Later evolution: The label gradually moved toward a self-described “dreampunk” aesthetic and distanced itself from the vaporwave tag. Russo removed several sampled albums from the catalog to reduce copyright exposure. (Cohost: “Dream Catalogue’s Impact on Vaporwave and the Aftermath”)
