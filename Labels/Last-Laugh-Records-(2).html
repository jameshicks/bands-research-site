Last Laugh Records (2)

American punk reissue and new-release label. Discogs label ID: 197658. Sublabel of Almost Ready Records (ID: 115944). Mailing address: P.O. Box 310376, Brooklyn, NY 11231. Self-described as “American made punk reissues.” 105 releases on Discogs as of 2025, active 2010–present.

Overview

Last Laugh Records (2) is the punk-specialist imprint of Almost Ready Records, a Brooklyn-based independent label. It operates alongside the Almost Ready Records name and a sister sublabel, Mighty Mouth Music (ID: 187939). Catalog numbers follow the “HAW-” series, running from HAW-001 (2010) into the HAW-050s as of 2025–2026.

The label launched in 2010 with a concentrated burst of 7” reissues spanning American punk, power pop, and proto-punk from the late 1970s — bands like Freestone, The Eat, The Injections, The Reactors (6), The Fingers (4), The Joe Hebert Band, and The Zeros. Most of these were first-time vinyl reissues of obscure regional singles from 1977–1981. From 2011 onward the catalog widened to include reissues of UK punk bands (Puncture, The Incredible Kidda Band), Midwestern groups (The Embarrassment, Skunks (2)), and Chicago outfits (Mentally Ill).

The label is most closely associated with two long-running reissue relationships: Mentally Ill (Chicago, 1979–present; 14 releases, 2012–2026, the label’s most prolific artist) and The Incredible Kidda Band (UK, 1978–present; 11 releases, 2011–2016). The Normals (2) (New Orleans) and The Insults (2) each account for 9 releases. The Embarrassment (Wichita, KS) have 7 releases across both reissue and new material.

Though the address is Brooklyn, NY, the roster reflects no single regional scene — it spans New Orleans (The Normals (2)), Chicago (Mentally Ill, The Shitdogs), Texas (Skunks (2), Ed Nasty And The Dopeds), Detroit (The Dogs (3)), Wichita (The Embarrassment), and the UK (The Incredible Kidda Band, Puncture). The connective thread is a shared devotion to first-wave punk and power pop archaeology.

Key Artists

Mentally Ill (Discogs ID: 430481)

Chicago hardcore/punk group, active 1979–present. The label’s most prolific artist with 14 releases (2012–2026). The Discogs realname is “The Mentally Ill.” Members include Sado Marquis, Special Ed (3), Klaus Trofobic, Reggie McFadden (2), Arthur E. MacQuilkin III, and Sven Herman. Releases on Last Laugh include the Gacy’s Place single (HAW-022), Strike The Bottom Red 12” (HAW-030), the Gacy’s Place (Starbeat Sessions) LP (HAW-041), and Gacy’s Place LP (HAW-044), plus a 2026 single (HAW-050). Also on Alternative Tentacles and Autistic Records.

The Incredible Kidda Band (Discogs ID: 1732474)

UK power pop/punk group, active 1978–present. 11 releases on the label (2011–2016). Members: Alan Hammonds (guitar, vocals), Les Rollason (bass, vocals), John Rollason (guitar, vocals), Dick Millington (drums), Dave Lister (guitar, vocals), Graham Hammonds, Paul Gardner (17). Based in the UK (website: kiddaband.co.uk). Also on Red Lounge Records and Detour Records (3).

The Insults (2) (Discogs ID: 2288456)

Punk group, active 1979–2021. 9 releases on the label (2013–2021). Members: Brendan Tracey, Brian Frus, Johny Tomaro, Richard B. Shively, Richard Watts (6). Also on Sick Sound Records and Ric-Mar Records.

The Normals (2) (Discogs ID: 321458)

New Orleans punk group, active 1978–2018. 9 releases on the label (2010–2018), including all reunion-era material. See The Normals (2) for full coverage.

The Embarrassment (Discogs ID: 109906)

Wichita, KS post-punk/art-punk group, active 1980–2025. 7 releases on the label (2011–2021), including both reissues and new material. Members: Bill Goffrier, Ron Klaus, B. Woody Giessmann, John Nichols (2). The band also co-produced their own Last Laugh reissues alongside engineer Michael McGee. Also on Bar/None Records and Restless Records.

The Dogs (3) (Discogs ID: 310820)

Detroit punk group. 4 releases on the label (2017–2022). Members: Ken Mundy, Loren Molinare, Tony Matteucci, Ron Wood (3), Mary Kay Dodson. Also described as “The Detroit Dogs” in some contexts.

The Shitdogs (Discogs ID: 443918)

4 releases on the label (2011–2025). Members use pseudonyms: I.B. Fugdup, Johnny Snotten, Ratz Marvin, Wheelie Bloodtongue.

The Eat (Discogs ID: 315919)

New Orleans punk group, active 1979–2010. 2 releases on the label (HAW-005, 2010). Members: Chris Cottie, Edward O’Brien, Michael O’Brien, Glenn Newland, Ken Lindahl. Also on Whappow and Moss Music (3). A key figure in the New Orleans punk scene alongside The Normals (2).

Skunks (2) (Discogs ID: 321451)

Austin, TX punk group (realname: The Skunks), active 1979–2016. 2 releases on the label (HAW-019, 2011). Members included Jesse Sublett (bass, vocals, writer), Eddie Muñoz (later of The Plimsouls), Billy Blackmon (drums), Jon Dee Graham, Bill Mansell, Doug Murray, Greg Murray (2). Also on Skunks Records and Republic Recording.

The Zeros (Discogs ID: 261758)

Chicano punk group from San Diego/Los Angeles, active 1977–2024. 2 releases on the label (HAW-007, 2010). Also on BOMP!, Munster Records, and Rockville.

The Fingers (4) (Discogs ID: 1953959)

Active 1978–2010. 3 releases on the label (HAW-003, 2010). Also on Rave Up Records and Paradox Records (8).

Recurring Personnel

Design

Jason Litchfield (ID: 4162428) is the dominant layout/design presence across the label’s catalog, credited on 25 releases — accounting for roughly a quarter of all Last Laugh output. Credits include layout for standard sleeves, reissue sleeves, and test pressing sleeves under various role strings (Layout; Layout [Reissue]; Layout [Test Pressing Sleeve]; etc.).

Other design contributors: Trey Lindsay (5 releases, artwork/design/sleeve), Artrouble (2 releases), Kid Sauce (2 releases, artwork), Ron Rat (2 releases, cover/front cover and vocals), Richard Luckett (2) (2 releases, artwork/design), Dave Wormley (2 releases, photography), Mike Fizer (2 releases, photography).

Engineering and Mastering

Josh Bonati (ID: 631289) is credited as Mastered By on 2 releases and Lacquer Cut By on 5. Wayne Vlcan (2 releases, engineer), Joe Zavaletta Jr. (2 releases, engineer), Michael McGee (2 releases as engineer; 3 as producer), Alan Knapp (2 releases, engineer), Charles Mueller (3) (2 releases, mixed by).

Production

Michael McGee (ID: 545173) is the most credited producer across the catalog (3 releases as producer). The Embarrassment co-produced their own Last Laugh reissues (3 releases). John Harris (ID: 255980) credited as co-producer on 3 releases. Des Dolan and George Hammerlein each credited as producer on 2 releases. Jim Becker (2) and Jim Buckingham (both 2 releases as co-producer/producer) also appear.
