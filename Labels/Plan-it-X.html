Plan-it-X was started in 1994 by Sam and Christopher Johnston (AKA, Chris Clavin), and released tapes by bands from Indiana. Chris began running Plan-it-X and in 1995, the label released its first record. The label was based out of Bloomington, Indiana until late 2005, when it relocated to Olympia, Washington.

Most non-Chris-Clavin-affiliated bands pulled their music from the Plan-It-X catalog in August of 2017 after Clavin was outed as a serial sexual predator.

Overview

Plan-it-X was co-founded in 1994 in Bloomington, Indiana by Sam and Christopher Johnston (Chris Clavin), initially releasing cassette tapes by Indiana bands before putting out its first record in 1995. The catalogue spans primarily the 2000s (42 releases) and runs across punk, acoustic folk, and indie rock — genres closely tied to Clavin’s own projects, which dominate the roster alongside Ghost Mice and Operation: Cliff Clavin. In late 2005 the label relocated from Bloomington to Olympia, Washington. Following Clavin being publicly identified as a serial sexual predator in August 2017, most non-Clavin-affiliated bands withdrew their music from the catalogue.
