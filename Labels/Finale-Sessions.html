Finale Sessions

US deep house and electronic label based in the New York area. Discogs label ID: 198815. Run by Michael Zucker. Active 2010–2018. Has two sublabels: Finale Sessions Limited (ID: 902221) and Future Sessions (ID: 1041315).

Overview

Finale Sessions was founded and operated by Michael Zucker, a New York-area DJ and producer who also released music on the label under his own name. The label launched in 2010 with a various-artists compilation (Finale Sessions, cat# Finale01) and ran until at least 2018, building a catalog of roughly 45 unique releases (masters) across the parent imprint and its sublabels.

The label’s output sits squarely in the tradition of US deep house and underground techno, drawing on connections to Detroit (Terrence Dixon, Alan Oldham, Santiago Salazar via Underground Resistance, Boo Williams via Chicago) and the broader transatlantic underground (Luke Hess, Motorcitysoul, Simoncino). Fred P. (Fred Peterkin) is the most prominent recurring artist, contributing five solo or co-credited releases and producing the inaugural compilation.

Three catalog lines run in parallel: the main “FS” series of 12-inches and EPs (FS-001 through FS038), the curated “Finale Select” subscription/vinyl series (FSS001–FSS006), and the “Finale Underground” series of various-artist compilations spotlighting Detroit and Chicago scenes.

The sublabel Finale Sessions Limited handled more club-oriented or experimental output (six releases, 2015–2018), while Future Sessions appears as a further imprint with no separate catalog in the database.

Scene Connections

The label’s geographic and stylistic range reflects a New York-anchored but outward-looking perspective:

  • Detroit pipeline: Terrence Dixon (Two Worlds), Alan Oldham (aka DJ T-1000, Illuminati Child EP), Santiago Salazar (a member of Underground Resistance, Rise EP), the “Detroit Visionaries” and “Future Chicago” underground volumes.
  • Chicago: Boo Williams (Finale Select 002), Finale Underground Vol. 3: Future Chicago.
  • Transatlantic underground: Luke Hess and Motorcitysoul (Germany, Community EP); Simoncino (Italy, The Other Side); Brothers’ Vibe / youANDme.
  • Fred P. network: Fred P. (real name Fred Peterkin) is the label’s most prominent house artist, appearing as Fred P., FP197, and credited as compilation producer on the debut. His Finale Revisited Vol 1 (FSR001, 2012) and split LP Two Worlds with Terrence Dixon (FS022, 2014) are the label’s most-noted releases.
  • Hakim Murphy / Innerspace Halflife: Murphy (operator of the Machining Dreams imprint) appears on New Roots with Amir Alexander and as one half of Innerspace Halflife (with Ike Release) on Village Hidden In The Sound.
  • Mastering: Mathieu Berthet handled mastering/cutting on multiple releases; Tim Xavier mastered the debut compilation.
