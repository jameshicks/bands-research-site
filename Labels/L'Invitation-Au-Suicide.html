L’Invitation Au Suicide

French experimental post-punk independent label. Discogs label ID: 32356. Active 1981–1986, headquartered in Le Havre, France.

Overview

L’Invitation Au Suicide (LAS) was founded and run by Yann Farcy from Le Havre, France. The label operated from 1981 to approximately 1986 and released around 50 catalog items (Discogs lists 48 unique releases across two pressing series). It occupies a distinctive position in early-1980s post-punk and industrial: a small French label that built an international roster connecting American no wave and deathrock acts, British post-punk, Irish experimental rock, and French goth — bound together by Farcy’s curatorial vision and a consistent, high-concept design program he executed with collaborators Gérard Rabel and Marie Lemeur.

The label is most often cited for issuing the first European pressings of Christian Death’s Only Theater Of Pain and Catastrophe Ballet, making it a key conduit for Californian deathrock into France and wider Europe. It was equally attentive to the avant-garde: the 1982 double-10” compilation Entrave Et Étouffement, From Australia… introduced Australian industrial and post-punk (Severed Heads, Scattered Order, Systematics, Pel Mel) to French audiences, and the 1983 The Gift Of Noise LP gathered American noise acts (No Trend, Smersh, F/i, Psyclones) on a single record.

After LAS wound down circa 1986, Yann Farcy went on to found White Noise (2) (1995), which became Noise Museum (1995–2001), and later Alice In Wonder (2001–2002).

A separate Discogs entry, L’Invitation Au Suicide (2) (ID: 150832), exists only to flag bootleg releases counterfeiting the original label’s sleeve identity. It is not a related imprint.

Contact address (per Discogs): L’Invitation Au Suicide / Yann Farcy / B.P. 1056 / 76062 Le Havre Cedex / France

Connections

  • Christian Death — primary roster anchor; LAS issued the first European pressings of their defining albums (1983–1985)
  • Virgin Prunes — Heresie (1982) box set; one of the label’s most elaborate early productions
  • 3 Teens Kill 4 — European pressing of No Motive (1984); David Wojnarowicz connection
  • Certain General — deepest relationship among NYC acts; 6 releases 1984–1986
  • Jad Wio — key French act; debut single ID 9; continued with ID 15
  • Sordide Sentimental — parallel French post-punk label; Jean-Pierre Turmel (its co-founder) contributed liner notes to multiple LAS releases
  • Yann Farcy — sole operator; after LAS founded White Noise (2) → Noise Museum → Alice In Wonder
  • Severed Heads — appeared on Entrave Et Étouffement (1982) Australian comp
  • No Trend — appeared on The Gift Of Noise (1983) and had their own LP Too Many Humans… (1984, SD 6)
