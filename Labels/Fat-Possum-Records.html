Fat Possum Records

Origin: Oxford, Mississippi, USA
Active: 1991–present

US independent label, originally known for blues (R.L. Burnside, Junior Kimbrough) and later expanded into indie rock. Released The Nighty Nite’s (John Congleton and the Nighty Nite) Until The Horror Goes (2016).
