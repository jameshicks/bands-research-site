Overview

Infectious Records was founded by Korda Marshall following his departure from RCA and became part of his Mushroom Records UK operation (later rebranded as A&E Records). The label ran through the 1990s with a catalogue centred on Indie Rock, Britpop, and punk-adjacent acts — most prominently Ash, whose members Tim Wheeler, Mark Hamilton, and Rick McMurray all appear on its roster alongside producer Owen Morris. Infectious Records was succeeded by Infectious Music in January 2009.
