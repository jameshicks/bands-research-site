From Discogs

Independent label founded in 1984 in San Francisco and active under that name until 1996. In 1987 one partner broke off and formed Penultimate Records. In 1996 there was a lawsuit/split between the US branch (then located in Seattle, WA) and the German branch (then located in Nuernberg). They subsequently separated into Empty Records (US) and Empty Records Europe. After the lawsuit between the entities, Empty Europe “released” a ‘bootleg’ series. These were Empty Records US releases with label details covered by a sticker of Empty Records Europe, not official releases.

Overview

Empty Records was an independent label founded in San Francisco in 1984, releasing punk, garage rock, grunge, and experimental music. A partnership split in 1987 sent one co-founder off to establish Penultimate Records, while Empty Records continued expanding into an international operation with branches in Seattle and Nuernberg, Germany. The 1990s were the label’s most prolific decade by far, accounting for the bulk of its catalogue; key roster figures included Seattle producer Jack Endino, experimental act Doc Wör Mirran, Gas Huffer, and Joseph B. Raimond. A 1996 lawsuit between the US and German branches dissolved the unified label into Empty Records (US) and Empty Records Europe, with the European entity subsequently issuing a disputed series of US releases bearing stickers over the original label details.
