Overview

Tree Records was a Chicago indie label founded in the mid-1990s by Ken Shipley and Dana Lomacchio, with a catalogue concentrated in emo, indie rock, and hardcore. Its output peaked in the 1990s (14 releases) before trailing off sharply in the 2000s and virtually disappearing in the 2010s. Notably, five releases appear in the 2020s, suggesting reissue activity or a late-stage revival. Roster figures include Jen Wood, Rob Sevier, Ethel Meserve, and Nick Pimentel alongside co-founder Shipley.
