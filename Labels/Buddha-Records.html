Overview

Buddha Records is a BMG Special Products reissue imprint, effectively a revival of the original Buddah Records label (and its Kama Sutra subsidiary), whose catalogue BMG acquired and began reissuing from early 1996. The name was deliberately corrected from the old “Buddah” spelling, and the logo replaced the Shiva statue with an image of the Buddha. Catalogue management sits under Legacy, and the label’s output is concentrated in the 1990s and 2000s — 54 and 86 releases respectively — spanning Pop Rock, Classic Rock, Blues Rock, Country, and New Wave reissues.
