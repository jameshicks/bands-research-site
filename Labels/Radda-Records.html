Radda Records

Discogs label ID: 743054

Independent electronic / house music label based in Portland, Oregon, USA. Founded in 2014 by Jacob Poe and Jesse Poe, the siblings who record together as Jac N’ Jill. Active through at least 2017, the label released 28 catalog entries across digital singles, EPs, and one vinyl test pressing, with a focus on deep house, tech house, and Chicago-inflected house.

History

Radda Records launched in 2014 out of Portland, OR. The founders — Jacob and Jesse Poe — operated the label as an outlet for their own material under the Jac N’ Jill alias and as a platform for like-minded producers in the house underground. The first release, RADDA001 (Easy Up by Busy (2)), appeared as a digital EP; the first vinyl pressing was RR 001, the FLB EP, a collaboration between established UK house act Inland Knights and US producers Jacob Poe and Aarta.

The catalog numbered consistently through RR029 (2017), with the bulk of activity in 2015–16. Radda drew on both local Portland talent and international house names — UK producer Dave Spoon contributed From Cha To PDX EP (RR015, 2015), and Chicago/US deep house stalwart Natural Rhythm released two singles on the label in 2016. The Poe brothers’ own Jac N’ Jill project was the label’s most consistent presence, with four releases across 2015–17.

No parent label or sublabels are recorded in Discogs. The label appears to have wound down after 2017 — the last catalogued release is RR029 by Jac N’ Jill.

Sound & Aesthetic

Radda’s catalog sits squarely in the deep house / house tradition. Genres credited across releases: Electronic (primary), with styles ranging across Deep House, House, and Tech House. Tracks run long — typically 6–8 minutes — in the DJ-tool format standard for underground house. Remixers called in include Art Of Hot, Jeromy Nail, Jonathan Cates (as remixer on RR012), 4peace, and Precious Roy, suggesting a network rooted in the US and UK house underground of the mid-2010s.

The label’s vinyl output was minimal (one confirmed test pressing, RR 001); the bulk of the catalog was distributed digitally (MP3 and WAV formats). The Blue Morning EP (RR012) was issued for USA, Canada, and Europe, suggesting some international digital distribution reach.

Key Artists

Jac N’ Jill (Discogs: 4683023)

The label’s flagship act and founding duo. Jacob Poe and Jesse Poe released four EPs on Radda (RADDA008, RR012, and two 2017 titles), making them the most active presence on the roster. Their style is deep house and electronic.

Aarta (Discogs: 2116519)

Three releases across 2014–16 (RR 001, RR020, RR025), making Aarta the second most prolific artist on the label. No member information on file in Discogs.

Inland Knights (Discogs: 3184)

UK deep house duo of Andy Riley and Laurence Ritchie (also recording as Larry Fives and Toka Project). Appeared on the FLB EP (2014), the label’s only known vinyl release — a notable early co-sign from an established act.

Natural Rhythm (Discogs: 183)

Deep house duo Pete Williams and Thomas White, also recording as Blaine Copperfield, Starbuck Jones, and Tavern On The Green. Two singles on Radda in 2016 (RR022, rr026).

Dave Spoon (Discogs: 270057)

UK house / tech house producer (real name Simon Bird), also known as Shadow Child. From Cha To PDX EP (RR015, 2015) — the title references Portland (PDX) and suggests a touring connection. Jacob Poe contributed a remix.

Art Of Hot (Discogs: 199669)

Duo of Chris Demetras and David Richardson. Appeared as remixers on Jac N’ Jill’s Blue Morning EP and as primary artists on Western Edition (RR024, 2016).

Alexander East (Discogs: 10876)

Featured vocalist/performer on Jacob Poe’s SOS (RR018, 2016). Member of Sonik Boom of Love and Unifunkation.
