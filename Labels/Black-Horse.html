Overview

Black Horse was a Brazilian record label operating under the Tapecar parent imprint, active chiefly through the 1970s with around 42 releases. Its catalogue spanned Disco, Soul, Funk, Ballad, and Easy Listening, reflecting the international popular-music currents of the era. Artists on the roster included jazz vocalist Freddy Cole, saxophonist Ronnie Ross, arranger Jerry Van Rooyen, Voyage, and David L. Miller.
