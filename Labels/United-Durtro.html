Overview

United Durtro is a joint-venture label founded specifically to release collaborative work between David Tibet of Current 93 and Steve Stapleton of Nurse With Wound — the name itself is a portmanteau of their respective home labels, United Dairies and Durtro. Active from 1996, the catalogue runs to around ten releases, with three appearing in the 1990s and the majority concentrated in the 2000s; styles range across experimental, drone, abstract, and avantgarde territory. The roster is tight — primarily the two founders plus Johannes Vester and Sand (2) — keeping the label’s remit narrow and collaborative throughout its run.
