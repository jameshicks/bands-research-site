Grabaciones Accidentales (company: Grabaciones Accidentales, S.A., [L=GASA], GA, S.A. or GG.AA.) is an independent spanish label formed in the end of 1981 by Paco Trinidad, by the group Esclarecidos and by some members of the group Décima Víctima.

Acquired by DRO in 1984.

— Discogs label profile, ID 52362

Overview

Grabaciones Accidentales was an independent Spanish label founded in late 1981 by promoter Paco Trinidad alongside the band Esclarecidos and members of Décima Víctima, rooted in Madrid’s early-eighties new wave scene. The label operated under the corporate name Grabaciones Accidentales, S.A. (abbreviated GASA) and was acquired by DRO in 1984. Its catalogue is concentrated in the 1980s (235 releases) with a secondary run through the 1990s (100 releases), covering Pop Rock, New Wave, Synth-pop, Indie Rock, and Alternative Rock. It also operated the sublabel El Cometa De Madrid.
