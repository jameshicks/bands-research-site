Overview

BGO Records (Beat Goes On Records) is a British independent reissue label founded in 1987 by Andy Gray, who also operated Andy’s Records — for much of the 1990s one of the largest independent record retailers in the United Kingdom. The label specialises in reissuing classic recordings from the 1950s through the 1980s, with a catalogue spanning blues rock, folk rock, psychedelic rock, hard rock, and pop rock. Its Discogs profile runs to over 1,800 credits, with catalogue activity concentrated in the 1980s. Reissued artists include blues legend John Lee Hooker and jazz guitarist Ian Carr.
