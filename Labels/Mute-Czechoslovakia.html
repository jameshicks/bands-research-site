Overview

The Czechoslovak branch of Mute Records Ltd., established in November 1992 and liquidated in 2006. Its lifespan straddled the dissolution of Czechoslovakia — it launched in the country’s final weeks, meaning virtually all of its activity fell under the Czech Republic, though the “Mute CS” logo persisted on releases past January 1993. The catalogue is overwhelmingly concentrated in the 1990s (81 releases against just 6 in the 2000s), spanning synth-pop, industrial, EBM, and alternative rock. Roster coverage mirrors the parent label’s flagship acts: Depeche Mode, Erasure, Martin L. Gore, Alan Wilder, and Flood.
