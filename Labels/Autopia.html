Autopia

US micro-label, Bay Area. Active approximately 1995–1999. Self-distributed imprint used by Steven R. Smith and associated acts during the earliest phase of his recording career. Discogs label ID: 73345.

About / History

Autopia was a San Francisco Bay Area self-release vehicle — the kind of small-circulation imprint common to the mid-1990s experimental underground, where artists pressed short runs of cassettes or vinyl and distributed through personal networks, record fairs, and sympathetic shops. The label released eight known titles between 1995 and 1999.

Steven R. Smith is the label’s central figure. His first known release under his own name, Log The Man Dead (AVT 1, 1995), appeared on Autopia, along with three further solo records across the label’s run. The variant catalog prefix — “AVT” on the debut versus “AUT” on subsequent releases — may indicate an early rename or a one-off numbering error; all other releases use “AUT.”

Alongside Smith’s solo work, Autopia served as the debut platform for Mirza, the short-lived Bay Area psych/drone quartet Smith co-founded with Glenn Donaldson, Brian Lucas, and Mark Williams. Mirza’s Number One (AUT 2, 1996) is the label’s only non-Smith primary release. The label also issued Topography Of Indefinite Impressions (AUT 7, 1999), a various-artists LP that doubles as a snapshot of the Bay Area experimental underground circa 1999 — assembled by Donaldson and Tim Green (2), with woodcut artwork by Smith, and featuring tracks from Thuja, Mirza, Loren Chasse, and several other acts from the same loose network.

By 1999–2001 Smith’s releases began appearing on Darla Records and then Emperor Jones, and the Autopia imprint went dormant. Smith later founded Worstward Recordings as his ongoing self-release label.

Key Artists

Steven R. Smith (Discogs artist ID: 99956) — the label’s founder and primary artist. Issued four solo records on Autopia between 1995 and 1999, covering solo guitar and drone territory. Concurrently a member of Mirza. Later active in Thuja, Hala Strana, Ulaan Khol, and numerous other projects; founded Worstward Recordings.

Mirza (Discogs artist ID: 123727) — Bay Area psych/experimental quartet, active 1996–2001. Members: Steven R. Smith, Glenn Donaldson, Brian Lucas, Mark Williams. Later signed to Darla Records and Ba Da Bing!. Also recorded under the alias Ursa Minor (9).

Connections

  • Steven R. Smith — founder; primary recording artist on the label
  • Mirza — co-founded by Smith and Glenn Donaldson; only non-Smith primary release
  • Glenn Donaldson — Mirza member; co-editor of Topography Of Indefinite Impressions; also in Thuja, The Blithe Sons, The Skygreen Leopards, and many other Bay Area acts
  • Tim Green (2) — co-editor of Topography; guitarist in The Fucking Champs, Citay, and former member of The Nation Of Ulysses
  • Loren Chasse — appeared on Topography; associated with Bay Area experimental/Jewelled Antler network
  • Thuja — appeared on Topography; collaborative project including Smith and Donaldson
  • Worstward Recordings — Smith’s later self-release label, successor to Autopia
  • Darla Records — Smith and Mirza both appeared on Darla releases in this period
  • Emperor Jones — subsequent label for Smith solo work

Open Questions

  • The “AVT 1” prefix on Log The Man Dead vs. “AUT” on all subsequent releases: possible early label rename (Autopia Victoria? Autopia Version T?) or a one-off error — no confirmed explanation.
