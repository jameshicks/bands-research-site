Overview

Resurgence was a UK sublabel of Voiceprint — the Durham-based progressive rock and fringe-pop independent run by Rob Ayling — operating from 1994 through the mid-2000s. The label’s stated purpose, echoed in its promotional sampler title (Isn’t It About Time You Had A Little Resurgence In Your Collection, 1996), was to release new recordings and reissues by veteran prog and Canterbury-adjacent artists who had fallen between major-label chairs. It sits within the broader Voiceprint group alongside Blueprint (4), Temple Records, and a dozen other imprints.

The catalog has two distinct strands:

  1. Canterbury/RIO-connected new recordings — the artistically weightiest part of the label, clustering around John Greaves, Peter Blegvad, Jakko M. Jakszyk, Bill Nelson, Dagmar Krause, and Lindsay Cooper. These releases draw directly on the Henry Cow/Soft Machine/Slapp Happy lineage.

  2. Mainstream prog and AOR catalog — a commercially broader strand covering Steve Howe (Yes), Asia (2) (the Geoff Downes/John Payne configuration), Ultravox (Midge Ure-era), Anthony Phillips (ex-Genesis), and Bill Nelson’s more ambient output.

Robin Ayling is credited as label coordinator on at least 17 releases, functioning as the operational backbone of the imprint within the Voiceprint infrastructure.

Key Artists

Bill Nelson

The single most prolific Resurgence artist by release count (10 entries as primary; 6 as producer), with catalog spanning ambient-experimental (Crimsworth, My Secret Studio Vol. 1), electronic collaboration (Culturemix With Bill Nelson), and the solo acoustic After The Satellite Sings / Four Songs cluster. Nelson self-produced virtually all his work, and on several releases also engineered and mastered. Known for Be Bop Deluxe and Red Noise (2); Resurgence concentrated his mid-1990s output.

Asia (2)

The Geoff Downes / John Payne configuration of Asia — not the original Howe/Wetton/Wetton classic lineup — placed 10 releases on the label’s dedicated LV sub-series (LV103–LV108), covering new recordings (Arena, Ingenuity) and archival compilations (Archiva 1, Archiva 2, Rare). This is distinct from the LV101/LV102 slot for Mike Lindup (ex-Level 42) and Level 42’s Forever Now.

Jakko M. Jakszyk

Jakszyk released his first three solo/band albums on Resurgence (Kingdom Of Dust RES101CD, Mustard Gas And Roses RES103CD, ‘Are My Ears On Wrong?’ RES110CD), as well as a later title as Jakko M. Jakszyk (The Road To Ballina RES127CD). Kingdom Of Dust features the Japan core trio of Mick Karn (bass), Steve Jansen (drums), and Richard Barbieri (keyboards). Jakszyk later joined King Crimson and is a recurring collaborator of John Greaves.

John Greaves

Four Resurgence entries across eleven years: Songs (RES112CD, 1995), Tambien 1-7 (1995) (RES141CD, 2005), and contributions to the Canterbury-connected Choices Under Pressure and Smell Of A Friend (The Lodge (2)). Songs is the label’s most discussed Canterbury document — see below.

Peter Blegvad

Three releases, all clustered around 2000–2001: Choices Under Pressure (An Acoustic Retrospective) (RES143CD, 2000) and The 3 Ages Of Magick (RES144CD, 2001, co-billed with Oliver Wakeman and Steve Howe). Choices Under Pressure was produced by Jakko M. Jakszyk and features John Greaves (bass) and Danny Thompson (bass), with written-by credits split between Blegvad, Anthony Moore, and Andy Partridge (XTC).

Anthony Phillips

Original Genesis guitarist, two titles: Sail The World (RES102CD, 1994/1997 Japan) — self-written, self-produced, self-engineered. A quiet presence but a signal of the label’s Genesis-era prog scope.

Steve Howe

Yes guitarist: Quantum Guitar (RES130CD, 1998) — entirely solo, featuring his son Dylan Howe on drums — and Pulling Strings (RES132CD, 1999), plus the joint Live At The Town & Country Club (LV107CD) with Asia. Also co-credited on The 3 Ages Of Magick (RES144CD, 2001).

Peter Knight & Danny Thompson

A single co-billed album (RES108CD, 1995). Knight was the Steeleye Span violinist; Thompson the legendary double bassist (John Martyn, Nick Drake sessions, etc.).

Dagmar Krause & Marie Goyette

A Scientific Dream And A French Kiss (RES-139-CD, 1998). Krause (Henry Cow, Art Bears, Slapp Happy vocalist) paired with Canadian composer/vocalist Marie Goyette. Jim Lowe engineered.

Lindsay Cooper & Charles Gray

Pia Mater (RES124CD, 1997). Cooper (Henry Cow, News From Babel) on sopranino saxophone and bassoon; Charles Gray on modified synthesisers and samplers. A rare solo-credited post-Henry Cow release from Cooper.

Notable Releases in Detail

Songs — John Greaves (RES112CD, 1995)

The label’s most Canterbury-connected release. A song-cycle produced by Greaves and Alain Cluzeau, recorded in Paris/France with an international cast:

This release connects Resurgence directly to the Soft Machine/Canterbury core (Wyatt, Dean), the post-RIO network (Cunningham, Sophia Domancich), and the Blegvad family orbit.

Choices Under Pressure (An Acoustic Retrospective) — Peter Blegvad (RES143CD, 2000)

An acoustic retrospective produced and arranged by Jakko M. Jakszyk. Personnel:

PerformerRole
Peter BlegvadGuitar, Clarinet, Vocals
Danny ThompsonAcoustic bass
John GreavesElectric bass
Jakko M. JakszykProducer, Arranged By, Engineer, Guitar, Keyboards, Percussion, Flute, Backing Vocals

Written-by credits split between Blegvad (majority), Anthony Moore (tracks 2, 9), and Andy Partridge (tracks 7, 11, 12). Mastered by Chris Thorpe.

Kingdom Of Dust — Jakko (RES101CD, 1994)

The label’s launch title. Features Mick Karn (bass), Steve Jansen (drums/programming), Richard Barbieri (keyboards) — the rhythm section of Japan — alongside Jakszyk on guitar/vocals. Links the label to the post-Japan art-rock world.

Pia Mater — Lindsay Cooper & Charles Gray (RES124CD, 1997)

Lindsay Cooper’s most prominent post-Henry Cow release in the Discogs catalog, paired with synthesizer/sampler work by Charles Gray. Short on credits but notable as a rare Cooper solo entry outside the Henry Cow reissue orbit.

Dizrhythmia — Dizrhythmia (RES117CD, 2003)

Group led by Jakko M. Jakszyk (Discogs artist ID 1241968), featuring Gavin Harrison (drums/percussion/marimba), Danny Thompson (double bass), Pandit Dinesh (Indian drums/percussion), and Owen Morris (engineer). Produced by Harrison and Jakszyk.

Recurring Personnel

Robin Ayling (Discogs ID: 1869512)

Coordinator for Resurgence across 17 releases. Functioned as the operational label representative from within the broader Voiceprint organization. His credit appears on Anthony Phillips (Sail The World), Jakszyk, John Greaves, Peter Blegvad, Dizrhythmia, and others.

Chris Thorpe (Discogs ID: 162728)

Mastered 4 releases including Choices Under Pressure (Blegvad) and Tambien 1-7 (Greaves).

John Spence (Discogs ID: 328078)

Engineered and mixed 4 releases.

Owen Morris (Discogs ID: 86125)

Engineered Dizrhythmia (2003) and at least one other release; a busy UK rock engineer of the period.

  • Tambien 1-7 (1995) (RES141CD, 2005) title implies the recordings are from 1995 — confirm whether this is an archival release of previously unissued Greaves sessions from that year or a new 2005 recording.
  • RES122CD (Smell Of A Friend by The Lodge (2)) has no year in Discogs — confirm approximate release date.
