Overview

A&M Video was the home-entertainment arm of A&M Records, releasing VHS and video titles from 1983 onward. Its 248-release catalogue spans the 1980s and 1990s in roughly equal measure, reflecting A&M’s mainstream roster of the era — pop rock, soft rock, new wave, and classic rock are the dominant styles. Artists including Sting, Janet Jackson, and Bryan Adams appear on the label, alongside director-producers Jonathan Dayton and Valerie Faris.
