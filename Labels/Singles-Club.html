Singles Club

Sublabel of Creeping Bent. Discogs label ID: 448107. Parent label ID: 17333.

Overview

Singles Club is a sublabel of Creeping Bent, the Glasgow independent label founded by Douglas MacIntyre. It was used as a numbered series of 7” vinyl and CD singles pairing roster artists in split or compilation format — functioning as a sampler series that cross-pollinated the Creeping Bent roster. Releases carried the standard Creeping Bent “Bent” catalog sequence rather than a separate Singles Club numbering; the “Singles Club” designation appears in the release title, not the cat number.

The series ran from 1997 to at least 2000, producing at least 12 numbered entries. A given Singles Club release typically featured two acts, each contributing one original track. The CD editions of early releases (particularly #1) added additional tracks beyond the 7” version’s two-sided pairing.

All Singles Club releases were issued on Creeping Bent (label ID 17333). The Discogs sublabel entry (ID 448107) carries no releases directly — releases are catalogued under the parent label and identified through their titles.

Connections

  • Creeping Bent — parent label; all Singles Club releases appeared under the Creeping Bent imprint and catalog sequence
  • Douglas MacIntyre — Creeping Bent founder; the Singles Club was his initiative
  • The series served as a cross-promotion mechanism within the Creeping Bent roster, pairing acts with different fanbases on the same release
  • The CD upgrade of #1 (four acts vs. two on the 7”) shows the format was used to maximize roster exposure beyond the 7“‘s AB constraint
  • Singles Club #3, #4, and #7 are unaccounted for in the Discogs database — may exist as physical releases not yet catalogued

Open Questions

  • Singles Club #3, #4, and #7 are absent from Discogs — release identities unknown
  • The Bentism compilations (Bent 009, Bent 022, Bent 040) were a separate various-artists series; relationship to Singles Club in terms of intent/format unclear
  • No producer/engineer credits are documented in Discogs for most Singles Club entries
