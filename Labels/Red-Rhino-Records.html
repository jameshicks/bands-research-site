Red Rhino Records

Discogs ID: 5403 Location: York, England Active: 1979–1988 (shop opened June 1977; first release RED 1, 1979; last release RED 92, 1988) Founders: Tony Kostrzewa (Tony K) and Peter Carnell Sublabels (Discogs): Red Rhino Distribution (288039), Ediesta Records (11180), Double R Records (9) (2733065), Charm, Ceremony, Chance, Prophecy (744083) Belgian/European division: Red Rhino Europe (Discogs 3262) — sublabel of Play It Again Sam Records

Overview

Red Rhino Records occupied a twin role unusual in the UK indie landscape: it was simultaneously an independent record label with its own catalog and the operating base of Red Rhino Distribution, one of the six regional nodes of The Cartel — the national indie distribution network that sustained UK independent music through the 1980s.

The label emerged from a record shop Tony Kostrzewa opened in York in June 1977 at 9 Gillygate. The shop became a hub for the post-punk scene in Yorkshire. The label pressed its first 7” single (RED 1, The Odds) in 1979. From the same Gillygate address the distribution operation grew into a regional pillar of indie logistics covering Yorkshire and the north.

By the mid-1980s the operation moved to The Coach House, Fetter Lane, York (1985–1987), and finally to The Grain Store, Eldon St (1988). The financial collapse of Red Rhino Distribution in December 1988 brought both the distribution arm and the label to a close; RED 92 was the final catalog release.

The Cartel

The Cartel was a network of six UK regional independent distributors that collectively provided national coverage for indie labels outside major-label channels:

The Cartel allowed a label like Red Rhino to publish records in York and have them stocked nationally. When Red Rhino Distribution collapsed in December 1988, the ripple effects were felt across the whole Cartel network — other distributors lost a node that covered a large portion of England’s north.

Red Rhino also distributed several independent labels beyond its own roster, including Fetish Records (Discogs 4880) and Illuminated Records.

Notable Connections and Personnel

Mother Juno (RED LP 84, 1987) — The Gun Club

The label’s most internationally significant release. The Gun Club’s fifth album was produced by Robin Guthrie of the Cocteau Twins, recorded in Brussels. Personnel: Jeffrey Lee Pierce (guitar, vocals), Kid Congo Powers (guitar), Romi Mori (bass), Nick Sanderson (drums), Lincoln Fong (engineer).

Akrylykz (RED 2, 1980) — Roland Gift

The Hull ska/post-punk group Akrylykz featured Roland Gift on vocals and tenor saxophone before he co-founded Fine Young Cannibals. Engineered by John Brierley at Red Rhino’s studio.

Pulp (REDLP 29, 1983) — First Album

It was Jarvis Cocker’s first LP, released when he was 19, on Red Rhino. The lineup included Simon Hinkler (later of The Mission) and David Hinkler.

‘Til Things Are Brighter (REDCD 88, 1988)

One of the label’s final releases was an unexpected gem: a Johnny Cash tribute compilation executive-produced by Jon Langford (2) of The Mekons and Marc Riley, with contributions from across the British indie scene.

Collapse (1988)

Red Rhino Distribution went into receivership in December 1988. The collapse was attributed to overextension of credit to retailers and the structural fragility of the Cartel model — each regional distributor was holding debt from a network of small independent shops that paid slowly or not at all. The failure removed a critical node from the national indie distribution network and directly impacted artists and labels that depended on northern England’s distribution reach.

The label itself issued its last release (RED T92 by The Rhythm Sisters) shortly before the collapse. Red Rhino Europe, the Belgian division operated in partnership with Play It Again Sam Records, continued separately after 1988 and is a distinct entity on Discogs (ID 3262).
