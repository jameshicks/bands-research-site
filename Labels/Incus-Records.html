Incus Records

Discogs label ID: 1264835 (as “Incus”; also see ID: 23055 for “Incus Records” variant entry) Founded: c. 1970, London, UK Founders: Derek Bailey, Evan Parker Website: http://www.incusrecords.force9.co.uk/ (archived)

One of the first artist-run independent record labels in British music, predating the punk DIY wave by several years. Founded by Derek Bailey and Evan Parker to document their own work and the broader British free improvisation scene without commercial mediation. Initially released as vinyl LPs (catalog numbers INCUS 1 through INCUS 51+); later moved to CDs (catalog prefix “CD”, then the “SG” Solo Guitar Series and “MC” series). The label ran continuously from 1970 to the mid-2000s, accumulating over 60 releases.
