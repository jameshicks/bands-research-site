In Tape

Discogs Label ID: 27977
Location: Sale, Cheshire, UK (Unit 3 104, Northenden Road)
Active: 1980s
Sublabel: Sidetrack Music (ID: 654308)

UK independent label run by Marc Riley (formerly of The Fall). 136 releases in the Discogs database. Primary label for Yeah Yeah Noh’s 1984–1985 output, including both early singles and the debut album Cutting The Heavenly Lawn Of Greatness…Last Rites For The God Of Love (IT 021, 1985). Catalog numbers used IT prefix for 7” singles and ITTI for 12” releases.
