Livephish.com

Phish’s official archive label and download platform, releasing high-quality soundboard recordings of Phish concerts from 2002 onward, plus archival shows and side-project releases. Discogs label ID: 137005 (as “Livephish.com”). Also appears on Discogs as “LivePhish” (ID: 1706892), which covers both the original Elektra-distributed CD series and subsequent digital releases.

Overview

LivePhish began as a physical-media series distributed by Elektra Records and launched on September 18, 2001 — during Phish’s first hiatus (October 2000 – December 2002). The original run consisted of twenty numbered multi-disc CD sets (Volumes 01–20), released in a distinctive slipcase/foldable-plastic-page format. Volumes 01–06 shipped simultaneously on launch day; the remainder followed in batches through May 2003.

After the hiatus ended, the platform shifted toward digital delivery via livephish.com, with Phish offering soundboard recordings for download starting in 2003. The Livephish.com label entity on Discogs (ID 137005) covers this post-Elektra phase: it is described as offering “high quality soundboard recordings of every show played by Phish since 2002 plus releases from the band’s archives, and webcasts of most concerts.” Physical releases during this era were handled through Jemp Records, Phish’s own imprint.

The parent label listed for Discogs ID 137005 is 212, which does not resolve to an active Discogs label record — likely a legacy artifact of the original Elektra distribution arrangement.

Catalog Overview

Discogs entity 137005 (Livephish.com): 110+ releases, spanning 2001–2024. All primary artists are Phish, with a small number of side-project credits (Trey Anastasio solo, Mike Gordon solo, Mike Gordon / The Benevento Russo Duo joint releases).

Discogs entity 1706892 (LivePhish): 104+ releases, spanning 1999–2024. Primarily Phish, with one notable outlier (see below).

Year range across both entities: earliest Discogs entry is a 1999-dated Phish show (Live Phish 10.31.90, filed under LivePhish ID 1706892); the most recent entries are from 2024 (Sphere, Las Vegas residency).

Catalog Number Schemes

The catalog numbers across both label IDs are inconsistent and evolved over time:

  • LP-prefix series (e.g. LP0379, LP0864, LP0942, LP01070, LP02276): incremental show numbers assigned to specific concerts. These appear to be internal LivePhish download identifiers; numbers reach at least LP02276 (a 2024 release), suggesting numbering tied to the cumulative count of shows released on the platform.
  • 101LP / 102LP / 103LP: early physical CD series numbering (2003), used for the first post-Elektra physical releases.
  • PA1002LP – PA1005LP: Island Tour CD series (2005), covering the April 1998 Nassau/Providence run.
  • T1 P2004-2 01: one-off catalog number on the 2004 release What I Did On My Summer Vacation.
  • PRCD-1689: promo catalog number on the 2001 Selections From LivePhish Volumes 01–06 sampler.
  • none / –: the majority of releases carry no catalog number, consistent with digital-first distribution.
  • Sphere-era: 2024 Sphere (Las Vegas) releases use a different scheme (0.2252, CWLHT-1744 BC32, etc.), possibly reflecting a new distribution or ticketing-platform arrangement.

Formats

The catalog is predominantly digital downloads (MP3/FLAC), with physical CD releases concentrated in two periods:

  1. 2001–2005: the original Elektra Volumes 01–20, plus the Island Tour CD set and early post-hiatus archive CDs.
  2. 2003 physical releases: 101LP/102LP/103LP series, individual show CDs.

The “Live Bait” series (Vols. 01–20, running 2010–2023) is a recurring sampler/compilation series released under both label IDs, collecting highlights from recent touring years.

Notable Outlier: Sci-Fi Soldier

Two 2022 LivePhish releases (Discogs ID 1706892) credit primary artist Sci-Fi Soldier for the release Get More Down. Discogs lists Sci-Fi Soldier (artist ID 12024280) as an alias of Phish — confirming this is not a non-Phish release but rather a Phish project or joke act credited under an alias.

Relationship to Elektra and Jemp Records

The original 20-volume LivePhish CD series was a Elektra Records project, launched while Phish was on hiatus. Elektra reissued several volumes in standard jewel cases in the mid-2000s. After Phish returned and the band moved away from Elektra, physical releases migrated to Jemp Records, their self-run imprint (Discogs ID: 72582). The livephish.com platform itself continued as the primary distribution vehicle for digital releases, with the Discogs label entity (137005) serving as the catalog record for that ongoing activity.

Connections

  • Phish — sole primary artist across the entire catalog
  • Jemp Records — Phish’s physical-release imprint, companion to the download platform; Discogs label ID 72582
  • Elektra Records — original distributor of the Volumes 01–20 CD series
  • Trey Anastasio — solo releases appear on the platform alongside Phish shows
  • Mike Gordon — solo and collaborative releases on the platform
  • Kasvot Växt — Phish Halloween costume alias; í rokk released on LivePhish (2018)

Open Questions

  • The parent label ID 212 on Discogs entity 137005 does not resolve to a named label record in the DB — may be a legacy Elektra placeholder or data artifact; worth checking if the Elektra Discogs ID matches.
  • Exact LP-prefix numbering logic: whether the numbers represent sequential show releases chronologically or are assigned by recording date.
  • Whether the Sphere-era catalog numbers (CWLHT-1744 series) reflect a new distribution partner or a venue/platform ticketing system.
