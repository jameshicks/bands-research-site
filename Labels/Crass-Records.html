Crass Records

Discogs Label ID: 13168 Origin: Essex, UK Founded: 1979 (by Crass) Closed: 1992; revived 2019 Address (historical): P.O. Box 279, London N22 4NR

Anarcho-punk label founded by Crass in 1979. The catalogue numbering system was a countdown to 1984 — e.g. 521984 = “5 years to 1984,” 421984 = “4 years,” etc. Multiple releases in one year appended /1, /2, etc. From 1985, standard sequential catalogue numbers were used. Closed in 1992, restarted 2019 with archival releases and the Normal Never Was series. Released both of Kukl’s studio albums.
