Rykodisc

Discogs label ID: 818
Label code: LC 02619
Founded: 1983
Base: Cambridge, Massachusetts; later Salem, MA (Pickering Wharf); later Beverly, MA
Status: Defunct as independent; used by Warner Music Group as reissue imprint
Parent (current): Rhino Records (2) (Discogs ID: 33244), a WMG division

Overview

Rykodisc was founded in 1983 as the world’s first CD-only music label. The name derives from the Japanese word for lightning, 雷光 (Raikō), which the founders translated as “sound from a flash of light.” For the first four years the label issued only compact discs across a wide range of genres, adding CD-quality LPs and cassettes only after proving the format viable. From a base in Cambridge and later Salem, Massachusetts, Rykodisc grew into one of the most important American independents of the late 1980s and 1990s, known equally for prestige back-catalog reissue programs and an eclectic active roster spanning alternative rock, world music, comedy, and jazz.

The label is most closely associated with two massive reissue programs: the complete Frank Zappa back catalog (beginning in 1984–86 and eventually spanning hundreds of releases in coordination with the Zappa Family Trust) and the David Bowie back catalog (a run of remastered titles beginning in 1989–1990 with the landmark Sound + Vision box set). Alongside the catalog work Rykodisc signed original acts including Morphine, Sugar (Bob Mould’s power-pop band), Galaxie 500, Mission Of Burma (reissues and later new material), and a varied roster of world music, spoken-word comedy, and alt-country artists.

A trademark of the Rykodisc CD era was the green-tinted jewel case, registered as a trademark by the label in 1992; it remained in use until around 1997, when transparent boxes gradually replaced it.

Key In-House Production / Engineering Personnel

The label built a core of recurring engineers and designers across its catalog:

  • Bob Stone — primary remaster engineer for the Zappa catalog (596 release credits); handled digital EQ and remixing from original tapes at UMRK (Zappa’s studio)
  • Toby Mountain (Northeastern Digital Recording, Boston) — mastering engineer for much of the active roster (456 credits); also credited on the Bowie reissues
  • Paul Q. Kolderie — producer/engineer for Morphine, Sugar, and much of the Boston-area indie rock roster; 113 producer credits, 87 engineer credits
  • Spencer Chrislu — Zappa transfer/mastering engineer at UMRK
  • Kerry McNabb — recording engineer on many Zappa live titles
  • Dick Kunc — original Zappa studio engineer (Whitney Studios); credited on the earliest Mothers reissues
  • Jonathan Wyner — mastering; Bowie Sound + Vision and many catalog titles
  • Cal Schenkel — Zappa’s longtime visual collaborator; 356 design/artwork credits across the Zappa reissue catalog
  • Ferenc Dobronyi — CD repackage design across the label (322 credits)
  • Steven Jurgensmeyer — package design (285 credits)
