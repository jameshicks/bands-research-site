Fresh Records (14)

UK independent record label. Discogs ID: 344059. Active 1978–1982, London.

Parent label: Jungle Records (Discogs ID: 14333) — absorbed by Jungle Records in 1982. Sublabel: Fresh Tunes (3) (Discogs ID: 344191) — publishing company tied to Fresh Records.

History

Fresh Records was founded in 1978 by Alex Howe out of his Wretched Records stall in Soho Market, London. Howe also ran a mail-order service; demand from shops for the Buzzcocks’ Spiral Scratch EP led him to establish Fresh Distribution. Bands including The Art Attacks, Second Layer, UK Decay, and Manufactured Romance came to the stall looking for an outlet for their self-pressed records.

In 1979 Alan Hauser joined Howe, bringing his Parole Records imprint with him — and along with it Family Fodder, Cuddly Toys, The Wall, and Bernie Tormé. The two combined operations to form the Fresh Records label proper. The FRESH single series launched immediately, with Family Fodder’s debut double-A-side Playing Golf (With My Flesh Crawling) / My Baby Takes Valium (FRESH 1) and The Art Attacks’ First And Last (FRESH 3) among the earliest issues.

The roster through 1979–1982 covered post-punk, goth, new wave, and early Oi! territory, with a notably diverse range of acts: from the art-damaged experiments of Family Fodder and Second Layer to the glam-punk of Cuddly Toys, the anarcho/punk-metal crossover of UK Decay, the pub-rock and R&B tradition of Wilko Johnson, and the punk/hardcore of Chron Gen. Wasted Youth’s Rebecca’s Room (FRESH 30, 1981) was produced by Martin Hannett, one of the label’s few outside-name production credits.

The label also had an international distribution relationship — multiple Fresh titles appeared in Portugal on the RIS imprint (RISS and RISLP cat numbers), and one Cuddly Toys single was pressed in Portugal and Spain.

In 1982 Fresh was absorbed into the Jungle Records family. The FRESH catalog series ended, but the FREUD album numbering — which had begun under Fresh (e.g. FRESH LP 1 through FRESH LP 8) — was carried forward as Jungle Records’ primary album imprint, running from FREUD 1 (D.T.K. (Live At The Speakeasy) by The Heartbreakers (2), 1982) through at least FREUD CD 137 (Play Dead: The Collection, 2023). A farewell sampler, A Fresh Selection (FRESH LP8), appeared in 1982.

Fresh Tunes (3)

Fresh Tunes (3) (Discogs ID: 344191) was a publishing company attached to Fresh Records, used for music publishing credits on releases. It issued no releases of its own.

Key Artists

Family Fodder

The label’s second-most prolific act by release count (13 releases), and one of its founding acts via Alan Hauser’s Parole Records connection. The debut single FRESH 1 (1979) was produced by Charles Bullen (side A) and Billy Bagg (side B). The album Monkey Banana Kitchen (FRESH LP 3, 1980) featured a large rotating cast of contributors including Bullen, Mick Hobbs, Martin Harrison, Ian Hill, and Alig, recorded across multiple London studios; produced by the collective credit “People In Control.” Film Music (FRESH 32, 1981) and the 12” ScHiZoPhReNiA pArTy! (FRESH 37/12, 1981) continued the label run through to the final single The Big Dig (FRESH 42, 1982).

Cuddly Toys

The label’s single most prolific act (14 releases), a glam-punk band led by vocalist Sean Purcell and bassist Tony Baggett. Brought to the label via Alan Hauser’s Parole Records. The debut Fresh album Guillotine Theatre (FRESH LP 1, 1981) was self-produced, engineered by Bob Broglia. Sean Purcell also functioned as remixer and, later, as producer and engineer for The Dark’s releases (see below).

The Art Attacks

Among the earliest signings: the single First And Last (FRESH 3, 1979) featured backing vocals from Ricky Slaughter, Steve Spear, Marion Fudger, and Edwin Pouncey (of Savage Pencil), with Robert Gotobed (later of Wire) appearing on drums on the B-side. Cover design by Savage Pencil.

Second Layer

Flesh As Property E.P. (FRESH 5, 1979) was an early post-punk/industrial release from the duo who would later become The Sound (2). The same recording had previously appeared on Tortch Records (TOR 001); Fresh’s involvement was a co-issue or distribution pickup.

UK Decay

Five releases across 1980–1981, including the album For Madmen Only (FRESH LP5, 1981), produced by John Loder and UK Decay. The band self-produced and engineered several of their singles. Personnel included Steve Spon (guitar), Steve Harle (drums), Martyn Smith (bass), and Steve Abbott (“Abbo,” vocals/guitar). For Madmen Only was engineered at Southern Studios with John Loder.

The Wall

Seven releases (four singles, two albums, and various pressings). Personal Troubles & Public Issues (FRESH LP 2, 1980) featured Jimmy Pursey (of Sham 69) producing one track, with Peter Wilson producing the remainder. The lineup was Andy Griffiths (bass), Rab Fae Beith (drums), Nick Ward (guitar), and Ivan Kelly (vocals).

Play Dead (2)

Two singles: Poison Takes A Hold (FRESH 29, 1981) and T.V. Eye (FRESH 38, 1981). A early goth/post-punk band who later moved to Jungle Records and beyond.

The Dark

The label’s most prolific single act by release count (9 releases), including the album Chemical Warfare (FRESH LP 9, 1982), which was self-produced alongside Sean Purcell. The band’s later material was closely intertwined with Cuddly Toys — Sean Purcell (Cuddly Toys vocalist) produced The Dark’s 1982 output. Personnel included Phil Langham, Jim Bryson, Jim Kane, and Andy Riff.

Chron Gen

Punk/hardcore band whose Puppets Of War EP (FRESH 36, 1981) was self-produced. The lineup was Glynn Barber, Pete Dimmock, John Johnson, and Jon Thurlow.

Wilko Johnson

Blues-rock guitarist who had a separate agreement with Fresh, issuing the live album Ice On The Motorway (FRESH LP4, 1980) and at least one single. A Portuguese pressing of the album exists on a separate catalog number.

The Lines

Post-punk group whose album Therapy (FRESH LP7, 1981) was self-produced (credited to members Joe Forty, Mick Linehan, Nick Cash, and Rico Conning) and engineered by Eric Radcliffe, with John Fryer as assistant engineer. The vault has an existing page.

Manufactured Romance

Five releases on the label. The Time Of My Life (FRESH 16, 1980) was produced by Peter Bite, with Nina Spencer on vocals.

Wasted Youth (3)

Rebecca’s Room (FRESH 30, 1981) — produced by Martin Hannett, mixed by John Holliday. One of the label’s more notable outside-producer credits.

Brygada Kryzys

Polish post-punk band whose album Brygada Kryzys = Crisis Brigade (FRESH LP 13, 1982) was mastered by Roy Rowland. Issued near the end of the label’s independent run.

Open Questions

  • Alex Howe’s full biography is not documented in Discogs; the founding story comes from the label’s own Discogs profile text. No People page exists for him in the vault — would need web research to confirm dates, later career.
  • The FRESH catalog has a gap between FRESH 43 and FRESH 46 — FRESH 44 and FRESH 45 are not accounted for in the Discogs data. May be unregistered releases or test pressings.
  • Portuguese RIS/RISLP pressings represent a licensing or distribution deal; the specific nature of the Fresh–RIS Portugal relationship is not clarified in Discogs data.
  • The “FB A/B” catalog number for the Wilko Johnson single (Back In The Night) sits outside the main FRESH sequence; its relationship to the standard numbering is unclear.
  • Parole Records (Alan Hauser’s prior label) has a Discogs entry; it is listed as a sublabel of Jungle Records in the Jungle Records vault page but its own page has not yet been researched.
