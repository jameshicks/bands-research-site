Experimental Sound Studio

Discogs ID: 301110 Type: Arts organization / recording facility / live venue Location: 5925 N. Ravenswood Ave, Chicago, IL Also known as: ESS

Overview

Experimental Sound Studio is a Chicago-based arts organization functioning primarily as a live venue and recording facility. It is co-directed by Alex Inglizian, who also serves as its chief recording engineer. ESS has been a central infrastructure node for Chicago’s experimental, improvised, and electronic music scenes, facilitating recordings that have appeared on labels including International Anthem, Corbett vs. Dempsey, Relay Recordings, and others.

Its Discogs label entry carries one commercial release: Three Circles & A Speaker by Steve Leaf & The Ex Pats. The bulk of ESS’s output is not released under its own imprint but rather as recording location/facility credits on releases by other labels.
