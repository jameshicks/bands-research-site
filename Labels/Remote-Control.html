Remote Control

Australian independent record label and distributor based in Melbourne, Victoria. Founded in 2001 by Harvey Saward and Steve Cross. Operates as the principal Australian imprint of the parent company Remote Control Records Pty Ltd. (Discogs ID: 1725066), which is 50% owned by Beggars Group. Distribution handled primarily by Inertia. Discogs label ID: 55075.

Overview

Remote Control was incorporated on 17 January 2001 by Harvey Saward — formerly International A&R Manager at Shock Records for ten years — and Steve Cross, previously General Manager at a division of Festival Mushroom Records. The label’s primary function from the outset was representing major international independent labels for release and distribution in Australia and New Zealand, acting as their local licensee.

Its founding international portfolio included some of the most significant indie and post-punk imprints of the period: Beggars Banquet, XL Recordings, 4AD, Mo Wax, Mantra Recordings, Wiiija Records, Too Pure, Matador Records, Domino Recording Company, and Bella Union. This gave Remote Control immediate reach across a wide spectrum — from electronic dance (Basement Jaxx, The Prodigy) through post-rock and alt-rock (Pixies, The White Stripes, Interpol, Radiohead) to the more adventurous end of independent music (Dirty Three, The Mountain Goats, Yo La Tengo, Cat Power).

In 2004 Remote Control launched the sub-label Dot Dash exclusively for Australian artists — one of the clearest expressions of a shift from pure distribution toward active domestic A&R. Dot Dash has since carried releases by Courtney Barnett, Client Liaison, Methyl Ethel, Donny Benet, Carla Geneve, and others. A second Australian-focused imprint, Dot Dash Recordings (ID: 406723), is listed separately on Discogs with an overlapping scope. The gaming/soundtrack label Boss Battle Records (ID: 1398515) is also a Remote Control sublabel.

By the mid-2010s Remote Control’s domestic Australian roster had grown substantially. The Smith Street Band have the label’s largest release count (47 releases, 2017–2025, with production credits from Jeff Rosenstock). Donny Benet (15 releases, 2020–2025), Courtney Barnett (9 releases, 2016–2021, via Milk! Records co-releases), Magic Dirt (9 releases, 2019–2023), and Ed Kuepper (10 releases, 2021–2025) are among the most prominent recurring names.

The label’s registered office is in Collingwood, VIC 3066. Mailing address: PO Box 182, Ascot Vale, Victoria 3032.
