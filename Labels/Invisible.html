Overview

Invisible Records is an independent label based in Chicago, founded and operated by Martin Atkins since 1989. The catalogue leans heavily industrial and experimental — with strands of ambient, alternative rock, and drum & bass — and peaked across the 1990s (79 releases) and 2000s (66 releases), with the roster anchored by Pigface, Chris Connelly, and William Tucker. The label serves as the primary organiser of Underground, Inc., a collective of independent labels. Sublabels include Deezal Records, Invisible China, and Origin Of The Species.
