Dauw is a Ghent based tape label. The aim of the label is to combine the graphic work of Femke Strijbol and some music we like. We want to create an unique hand-made piece of art that flatters both the ear and the eye.

— Discogs label profile, ID 660021

Overview

Dauw is a Ghent-based tape label founded in 2014, conceived as a partnership between graphic artist Femke Strijbol and a shared love of quiet, considered music — each release positioned as a hand-crafted object that pairs her visual work with the listening experience. The catalogue spans ambient, modern classical, electroacoustic, and drone, with 36 releases across the 2010s and a further 24 into the 2020s. Recurring artists include Ian Hawgood, Taylor Deupree, and The Humble Bee, placing Dauw at a crossroads between Belgian and international slow-music scenes.
