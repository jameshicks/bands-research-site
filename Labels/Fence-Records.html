Fence Records

Discogs ID: 54206
Origin: East Neuk of Fife, Scotland (mailing address: PO Box 14261, Anstruther, Fife KY10 3YF)
Catalog prefix: FNC
Active: 1997–present
Founded/run by: King Creosote (Kenny Anderson)

Fence is a collective of musicians, artists, craftsfolk, chancers and slackers based in Fife, Scotland. The label began as a cassette and CDR micro-operation run by Kenny Anderson out of Fife, issuing his own prolific solo output from 1997 onward before expanding into a genuine roster label in the early 2000s. The label became a focal point for a distinctive Scottish lo-fi folk/indie scene, mixing home-recording aesthetics with communal DIY values. Several key Fence artists — notably James Yorkston and Lone Pigeon (Gordon Anderson) — were picked up by Domino in the early 2000s; Fence itself continued as an active independent through at least 2022.
