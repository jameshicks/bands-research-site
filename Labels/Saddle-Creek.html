Overview

Saddle Creek is an Omaha, Nebraska indie label founded in 1993 — originally under the name Lumberjack Records (2) — by Mike Mogis and Justin Oberst, with Mogis soon handing his operational role to Robb Nansel. Its catalogue runs from 1996 onward, concentrated in the 2000s across roughly 40 selected releases, with indie rock, folk rock, and alternative rock as the dominant modes alongside threads of synth-pop and country rock. The label operates two sub-imprints, Document Series and Saddle Creek Europe, and its credited personnel lean heavily on producers and engineers including Mike Mogis, A.J. Mogis, and Andy LeMaster.
