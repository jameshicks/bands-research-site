Overview

Parole Records was an Australian record label that operated through the 1980s, releasing 26 titles across synth-pop, new wave, power pop, and pop rock — with a small Aboriginal music strand alongside those mainstream styles. Its releases were handled by Festival Records Pty. Ltd., whose catalogue numbering system they shared, making the two companies closely intertwined in practice; the label’s own legal entity is Parole Records Pty. Limited. The roster centred on acts including The Venetians, Tim Powles, and Rik Swinn.
