Moers Music

German independent record label founded in 1977 by Burkhard Hennen in Moers, Germany. Discogs label ID: 38874. Label code: LC 6059 / LC 06059. Publisher: Burkhard Hennen Publishing (Discogs ID: 301112). Sublabel: Baff (Discogs ID: 80918).

Contact: Moers Music Burkhard Hennen GmbH, P.O. Box 30 01 20, D-47426 Moers, Germany.

Overview

Moers Music began as Ring Records in 1974, but Burkhard Hennen renamed it three years later after a conflict with a Canadian label of the same name. From 1977 onward the label operated under the Moers Music name and became the primary document of the Moers Festival (also known as the Moers New Jazz Festival), one of Germany’s leading festivals for avant-garde and improvised music.

The label’s catalog runs from 1976 into the 2000s, with its core output concentrated in the late 1970s through the early 1990s. It documented both live festival recordings and studio sessions, drawing on American free jazz (AACM-affiliated artists, free funk), European improvised music, and cross-genre experiments bridging jazz, rock, and electronics. Burkhard Hennen produced the vast majority of releases himself and is credited on over 90 of the label’s ~173 catalog entries.

The house designer for nearly all releases was Jürgen Pankarz, who handled cover design on at least 56 releases. Volker Biesen provided artwork on 29 releases. Recording engineer Hans Schlosser (Discogs ID: 361201) was the main technician on 24 releases through the early period.

Festival Connection

The label was formerly related to the Moers Festival (Discogs label ID: 447808), a relationship that gave it privileged access to festival performances. Many releases are credited as live recordings at Moers, and artists such as Anthony Braxton, World Saxophone Quartet, Evan Parker, and Vienna Art Orchestra appear partly through festival documentation. The festival’s international reach — pulling in American free jazz players, European improvisers, and global percussionists — directly shaped the label’s eclectic roster.

Key Artists / Roster

The label’s roster reflects its twin poles of American free jazz and European improvised music.

American free jazz axis:

  • Anthony Braxton (Discogs 200818) — 4 primary releases, 1977–1983; solo and quartet
  • World Saxophone Quartet (Discogs 309757) — foundational 1977 LP
  • Music Revelation Ensemble (Discogs 341074) — 4 releases 1980–1982; James Blood Ulmer’s no-wave/free-funk ensemble
  • Ronald Shannon Jackson And The Decoding Society (Discogs 862460) — 4 releases 1981–1993; free funk; featured Vernon Reid
  • Ornette Coleman (Discogs 224506) — 1 release 1981
  • Phalanx (4) (Discogs 915919) — 2 releases 1986–1987; James Blood Ulmer, George Adams
  • Music Revelation Ensemble — overlaps with Ulmer’s Decoding Society orbit
  • Sunny Murray Trio (Discogs 1381826) — 4 releases 1979–1985
  • John Carter (Discogs 383859) — 3 releases 1979 (solo clarinet and quintet)
  • Odean Pope (Discogs 598920) — 3 releases 1982–1990
  • Jamaaladeen Tacuma (Discogs 171780) — 3 releases 1992–2005; also produced
  • Rhys Chatham (Discogs 584) — 3 releases 1983–1991; guitar minimalism/brass

European improvised music axis:

  • Günter Christmann (Discogs 297025) — 5 releases 1977–1995; trombone/contrabass; also produced
  • Vienna Art Orchestra (Discogs 321481) — 10 releases 1984–1990; led by Mathias Rüegg
  • Vienna Art Choir (Discogs 1921566) — 4 releases 1983–1984
  • Evan Parker & Paul Lytton (Discogs 1465405) — 1 release 1977
  • Rova Saxophone Quartet (Discogs 377693) — 2 releases 1980
  • Ned Rothenberg Double Band (Discogs 1366921) — 3 releases 1991–2004; also produced
  • Urszula Dudziak (Discogs 354194) — 4 releases 1983–1984; Polish vocalist
  • Greetje Bijma / Noodband (Discogs 990553) — 2 releases 1982

David Moss (Discogs 198935) — 4 releases 1984–1993; percussionist/vocalist who bridged both worlds; his Full House (1984) assembled John Zorn, Fred Frith, Arto Lindsay, Bill Laswell, Christian Marclay, David van Tieghem, and Phil Minton across 19 tracks.

Curlew (Discogs 366109) — 2 releases 1985 and 1991; New York avant-rock band; North America (1985) produced by Fred Frith, recorded by Martin Bisi.

Connections

  • Burkhard Hennen — founder and primary producer; label is essentially his personal project
  • Moers Festival (Discogs label 447808) — formerly affiliated; supplied live recordings across the catalog
  • Fred Frith — produced North America (Curlew) and Full House (David Moss); appears as performer on multiple releases
  • Rhys Chatham — 3 releases; bridged NYC minimalism and European free improvisation
  • Anthony Braxton — among the label’s earliest and most documented artists; AACM connection
  • World Saxophone Quartet — landmark 1977 early document
  • Vienna Art Orchestra — largest single-artist run on the label (10 releases)
  • Ornette Coleman — 1 release 1981
  • James Blood Ulmer — appeared via Music Revelation Ensemble and Phalanx (4); core free-funk figure
  • Martin Bisi — recorded North America; Bisi’s BC Studio (Brooklyn) was the hub for much NYC avant-garde work
  • John Zorn — guest on David Moss Full House (1984)
  • Arto Lindsay — guest on David Moss Full House (1984)
