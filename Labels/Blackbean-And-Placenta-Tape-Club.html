Overview

Blackbean And Placenta Tape Club was an independent cassette and record label based in Oceanside, California, owned and operated by Mike Landucci. Its catalogue runs primarily through the 1990s — 27 releases across that decade — before tapering to 6 in the 2000s, spanning lo-fi, indie rock, experimental, and noise. The label released work by Gang Wizard, Orange Cake Mix, The Mondo Crescendo, Greg Wadley, and Rose For Bohdan, and circulated under variant names including “Blackbean And Placenta” and “Black Bean Placenta Records.”
