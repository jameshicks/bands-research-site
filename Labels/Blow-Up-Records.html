From Discogs

London-based independent record label, started in 1994 by Blow Up Club founder Paul Tunkin, the year after the clubnight began. Music on the label ranges from 60s to New Wave, from Pop to Electronica; more or less reflecting and complementing the sensibility of Blow Up.

The Blow Up label is responsible for compilations such as the ‘Blow Up presents Exclusive Blend’ music library series, ‘Blow Up A-Go-Go: Dancefloor Classics’ and several series of limited seven inches featuring bands from across Europe and the UK. The label has released for artists including Add N To (X), Big Boss Man, The Bongolian, Baltic Fleet, Silvery, Mockingbird, Wish Me Luck, Neon Plastix, Alfa 9, David Woodcock and more reflecting the label’s eclectic output.

— Discogs label profile, ID 16811

Overview

Blow Up Records is a London-based independent label founded in 1994 by Paul Tunkin, the year after he launched the Blow Up club night — the label was conceived as a natural extension of the club’s curatorial identity. Its output spans a deliberately wide range: 60s pop, New Wave, funk, electronica, and indie rock, mirroring the eclectic programming of the club itself. The catalogue is weighted toward the 2000s and 2010s, with roughly 32 and 26 releases respectively in those decades, tapering off after 2020. Artists associated with the label include Add N To (X), Big Boss Man, The Bongolian, and Baltic Fleet, and the imprint runs sublabels Exclusive Blend — known for a music library compilation series — and the 1000 Series.
