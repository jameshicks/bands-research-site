EMI

Major UK record label. Discogs label ID: 26126.

Overview

One of the largest British record companies, active from the early 20th century through its absorption into Universal Music Group in 2012. Signed Gang of Four in 1979, releasing Entertainment! (1979), Solid Gold (1981), and Songs of the Free (1982), as well as multiple singles.
