Cinevox Record

Discogs label ID: 63092
Origin: Italy

Italian film soundtrack label. Released Evan Lurie’s score for Il Piccolo Diavolo (MDF 33.188, 1988). Primarily known for Italian genre film scores (giallo, horror, poliziottesco) from the 1960s–1980s, including work by composers such as Ennio Morricone, Riz Ortolani, and many others.
