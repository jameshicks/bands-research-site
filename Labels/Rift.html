Rift

New York City-based independent record label. Discogs ID: 43480. Subsidiary of Rift Records (Discogs ID: 23485). Active c.1979–1992. No profile text on Discogs; no URLs listed.

Overview

Rift was an NYC avant-garde imprint associated with the downtown experimental scene of the early 1980s. Its catalog (14 unique masters; cat# RIFT 1 through Rift CD16) spans free improvisation, experimental song, noise, and contemporary composition, with releases by Bob Ostertag, Phil Minton, Fred Frith, John Zorn, Skeleton Crew (2), and Tim Hodgkinson’s group The Momes. The label co-released Skeleton Crew (2)‘s debut LP Learn To Talk (1984, RIFT 08) with Swiss label RecRec Music, and issued the follow-up The Country Of Blinds (1986, RIFT 11) solo.

The catalog also includes John Zorn’s Locus Solus (1983, RIFT 7) — a landmark document of the NYC improvised music scene featuring Zorn’s game-piece approach — and the Bob Ostertag / Fred Frith / Phil Minton trio Voice Of America (1982, RIFT 4).
