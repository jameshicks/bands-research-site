Label, first credited in Discogs 2007, sublabel of All Flowers Group. Founded in Brooklyn, NY in 2007 by Caleb Braaten.

Founder Background — Caleb Braaten

Braaten grew up in Denver, CO, where he became a regular at the Twist & Shout record shop after school and later worked there for approximately six years. [source: slugmag.com]

In 2003 he relocated to New York City and took a job at Academy Records, a record shop in Williamsburg, Brooklyn. [source: bandcamp.com/2017 tenth-anniversary interview] Alongside the record store work, he and a friend ran a small merchandise side business selling bootleg horror movie t-shirts and patches at conventions. [source: bandcamp.com/2017 tenth-anniversary interview]

Braaten had no prior label or formal music-industry experience before starting Sacred Bones. The label originated in 2007 when friends wanted to put out a record and he decided to figure out how; the early operation was based in the basement of Academy Records. [source: bkmag.com/2014] His years working in record stores directly shaped the label’s visual identity approach — he credited the impulse-buy appeal of well-designed sleeves displayed on shop walls as a guiding principle for Sacred Bones aesthetics. [source: thecreativeindependent.com]
