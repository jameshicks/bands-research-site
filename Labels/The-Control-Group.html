Overview

The Control Group is a Brooklyn-based independent record label active since 2002. It is best known for releasing albums by El Perro Del Mar, Lykke Li, and Cate Le Bon, while also handling vinyl releases and reissues for higher-profile acts including The Killers, Kings of Leon, Spinal Tap, Grandaddy, Giorgio Moroder, and PJ Harvey. Its catalogue of roughly 67 selected releases is concentrated in the 2000s and 2010s, spanning indie rock, indie pop, synth-pop, and ambient styles. The combination of small-roster artist development and major-act reissue licensing is an unusual position for an independent label of its size.

Founder and background

The Control Group was founded by Nabil Ayers in Seattle, Washington in 2002 and relocated to Brooklyn in 2008. Ayers is the son of jazz vibraphonist Roy Ayers and the nephew of saxophonist Alan Braufman. Alongside the label, Ayers co-owned the Sonic Boom Records retail stores in Seattle (sold in 2016). He became US General Manager of 4AD in 2009, and President of Beggars Group US in early 2022 — roles he held concurrently with running The Control Group. (Wikipedia: Nabil Ayers, as of 2026-11-01; controlgroupco.com/about)

How The Control Group came to handle major-act vinyl releases

The mechanism was opportunistic licensing from major labels during a period of industry indifference to vinyl (mid-2000s). A contemporaneous Seattle Weekly feature (April 2010) explains the sequence:

“Ayers has been the beneficiary of major labels’ lack of interest in issuing vinyl releases for some of their stars.”

The chain of events:

  1. The Control Group pressed the vinyl edition of The Killers’ Hot Fuss (Island Records, 2004) — the article does not detail how this first deal was struck, but it established Ayers as a reliable vinyl operator.
  2. A colleague at RCA then approached Ayers and asked whether he would be interested in releasing Kings of Leon’s Aha Shake Heartbreak (2005) on vinyl. He agreed, and went on to issue the band’s subsequent records, including Because of the Times (2007) and Only by the Night (2008).
  3. Only by the Night sold roughly 1.9 million copies overall, with approximately 9,000 on vinyl through The Control Group. As the article notes: “It hasn’t made Ayers rich, but it has earned his label meaningful exposure.”

The pattern repeated with other acts (Grandaddy, PJ Harvey, Giorgio Moroder, Spinal Tap): major or legacy rights-holders licensed vinyl-only rights to The Control Group because vinyl was not a priority format for them at the time. Ayers’ credibility as a record-store owner and label operator made him an attractive, low-friction partner for these deals. (Seattle Weekly, “Both Sides of the Record”, 13 April 2010)

Valley of Search

In 2018 Ayers launched a sister imprint called Valley of Search. (controlgroupco.com/about)
