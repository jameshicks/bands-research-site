Overview

Nugs.net is a digital platform for professionally recorded live concerts, developed by Brad Serling and active since 2001. It hosts tens of thousands of free and paid downloads across formats from MP3 to MQA, with major touring artists routinely releasing recordings of every show they play. Beyond downloads the platform offers on-demand streaming and live concert broadcasts. The catalogue spans alternative rock, blues rock, country rock, indie rock, and heavy metal, with notable roster entries including Bruce Springsteen & The E Street Band.
