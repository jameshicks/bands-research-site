Skin Graft Records

Chicago-based independent record label. Discogs label ID: 33275

Overview

Chicago noise-rock and avant-garde label founded in the early 1990s. Known for releasing bands in the noise rock, no wave, and experimental rock spheres. 371 releases in Discogs, 1 sublabel (Skin Graft Records Poland, ID: 218739).

Note: Don Caballero does not appear in Skin Graft’s catalog per Discogs — any connection is peripheral or pre-Discogs.
