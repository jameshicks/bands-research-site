Overview

The Pink Label was a short-lived UK indie imprint founded by Simon Down in 1984, with Paul Sutton and his brother Ed joining shortly after launch. The label operated for roughly three years before folding in 1987, releasing 17 records that sit entirely within the 1980s and span indie rock, indie pop, and punk. Its roster included The Wolfhounds, McCarthy, and The June Brides — acts central to the mid-decade UK indie scene. Paul Sutton went on to launch September Records immediately after The Pink Label closed.
