Overview

Bad News Records is a Tokyo-based independent label incorporated on June 11, 1990, with president Kazutoshi Chiba at the helm. It began as a music magazine publishing company before shifting to record releasing, eventually assembling a catalogue that spans indie rock, punk, bass music, and jazzy hip-hop. Distribution moved from Bandai Music Entertainment (1995–2000) to Victor Entertainment, with a JetSet Records deal added in 2001 and a MIC LIFE imprint launched in 2002 for adult-oriented foreign hip-hop. A 1994–1997 tie-up with the US Latin label RMM brought that catalogue to Japan, and sublabels including Bass野郎 and La Mecca Records extended the label’s range; the catalogue is densest in the 2000s, with 135 of roughly 193 tracked releases falling in that decade.
