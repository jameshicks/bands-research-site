Overview

Berlin-based independent label founded 1999, specialising in indie electronics, IDM, folktronica, ambient, and experimental pop. Operates at the intersection of two geographic scenes: the Weilheim, Bavaria underground (The Notwist, Tied & Tickled Trio, Lali Puna — overlapping rosters anchored by Markus and Micha Acher) and the Icelandic scene (múm, Sin Fang, Sóley, Seabear, Pascal Pinon). As of 2025–26 still releasing new material (The Notwist, múm, Robert Lippok).

Weilheim Connection

A defining axis of the label’s founding era. Several core Morr artists share origins in or around Weilheim in Oberbayern, a small town that generated a disproportionate cluster of German experimental acts in the 1990s:

  • The Notwist: Markus Acher, Micha Acher, Martin Gretschmann (also known as Console), Cico Beck, Andreas Haberl
  • Tied & Tickled Trio: Markus Acher, Micha Acher, Christoph Brandner, Ullrich Wangenheim, Johannes Enders, Andreas Gerth
  • Lali Puna: Markus Acher, Christoph Brandner (+ Valerie Trebeljahr)

Markus Acher (Discogs ID 137761) is the central figure — credited on Notwist, Lali Puna, and Tied & Tickled Trio releases, and appears as a remixer on Morr compilations.

Icelandic Connection

Morr became a key European outlet for Icelandic artists through its long relationship with múm:

  • múm (Gunnar Örn Tynes, Gyða Valtýsdóttir, Kristín Anna Valtýsdóttir, Sigurlaug Gísladóttir, Örvar Þóreyjarson Smárason) — 51 releases 2001–2025
  • Sin Fang — Örvar from múm’s solo project; 41 releases 2008–2021
  • Sóley — 40 releases 2011–2021
  • Seabear — 2007–2022
  • Pascal Pinon — 2010–2016
  • FM Belfast — 2011
  • JFDR — 2018
  • Team Dreams (2017, mm 150) — collaboration LP: Sin Fang / Sóley / Örvar Þóreyjarson Smárason

Putting The Morr Back In Morrissey (MM012, 2000)

2CD label showcase documenting the founding-era roster. Disc 1 (new tracks): Manufracture, The Notwist, Christian Kleine, Herrmann & Kleine, Kevin & Paul, Styrofoam, E*Vax (Evan Mast — later of Ratatat), Phonem, Arovane (Uwe Zahn), Kandis, Blond, Lali Puna, Solvent (Jason Amm). Disc 2 (remixes): same roster plus Schneider TM, Flowchart, Hessen, Wunder.

Vertigo Days — The Notwist (mm 180, 2021)

The Notwist’s return after a long absence, released on Morr. Extensively collaborative: guests include Saya Ueno (Tenniscoats), Juana Molina, Angel Bat Dawid (clarinet), Ben Lamar Gay (vocals), and a full ensemble of Japanese musicians on “Into Love Again.” Mastered by Geoff Pesche at Abbey Road.

Lali Puna — Faking The Books (MM044, 2004)

Core lineup: Valerie Trebeljahr (vocals), Markus Acher (compositions), Christoph Brandner, Christian Heiß. Produced by Mario Thaler. Guests: Max Punktezahl (guitar, also in The Notwist), Osamu Nambu (violin), Sebastian Hess (cello). Mastered by Chris Blair.

Note on Savath & Savalas

No Savath & Savalas releases appear on Morr Music in Discogs — their catalog runs through Hefty Records, Warp, Anti-, and Stones Throw. The connection may be a web-sourced misconception or a one-off compilation track not indexed in the DB.
