Flemish Eye

Independent record label based in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. Discogs label ID: 115820. Sublabel of Flemish Eye Records (ID: 685394).

Overview

Key outlet for Calgary’s indie and post-punk scene. Home to Women and Preoccupations (both Viet Cong-era and Preoccupations-era releases). 111 releases on Discogs.

Key Releases

  • Women — catalog
  • Preoccupations — Viet Cong (2015, co-release with Jagjaguwar), Key / Off Duty Trip (2016), Arrangements (2022, FLCR052-LP)
