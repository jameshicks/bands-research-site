Mantra Recordings

UK independent label. Discogs label ID: 2238. Parent: Mantra Recordings Ltd. (Discogs ID: 320157).

Overview

UK indie label that released Six By Seven’s first four studio albums between 1998 and 2002, as well as their singles during that period. Also distributed releases co-credited with MFS (2). A 2016 Six By Seven greatest hits compilation also appeared on the label. ~626 releases on Discogs.

Key Artists / Releases

  • Six By Seven — primary artist 1998–2002, 2016
    • The Things We Make (1998)
    • The Closer You Get (2000)
    • Eat Junk Become Junk (2000)
    • These Compulsory Meatless Days (2001)
    • All My New Best Friends (2002)
    • Greatest Hits (2016)

Connections

  • Six By Seven — flagship act
  • Beggars Banquet — US distribution partner for Six By Seven’s debut and second album
