Overview

Epitaph (3) is a bootleg imprint that counterfeits the well-known Los Angeles punk label Epitaph, issuing unofficial releases under its name. Its catalogue spans 1989 into the 2020s across punk, alternative rock, ska, metalcore, and hardcore, with roughly 54 releases carrying 48 credits. Artists whose names appear on its releases — Bad Religion, Brett Gurewitz, Greg Graffin, Tom Waits, and Lars Frederiksen — are all associated with the legitimate Epitaph label, making the deception plausible.
