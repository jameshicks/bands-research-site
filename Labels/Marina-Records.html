Overview

Marina Records is a German independent label co-founded by Stefan Kassel and Frank Lähnemann (Label Code: LC 05093), active since 1993. The catalogue leans heavily into indie and alternative rock with acoustic and pop-rock crossovers, and the label operates a sublabel, The In-Kraut. Its peak output came in the 1990s and 2000s — 27 and 23 releases respectively — with activity tapering in the 2010s and 2020s. The roster includes David Scott, Douglas MacIntyre, and Shack (3) alongside its founders.
