Overview

People In A Position To Know (PIAPTK) is an American indie label founded in 2006 in Olympia, Washington and relocated to Tucson, Arizona by 2013, operated by Michael Dixon, who also runs LatheCuts.com, co-manages Mobile Vinyl Recorders with Kris Dorr, and co-runs Soild Gold Records with Dimitri Manos. The label is defined by severely limited lathe-cut releases — some pressed in editions of 20 or fewer — in elaborate handmade packaging, distributed by The Business (2). Its catalogue of around 149 releases is concentrated in the 2010s and 2020s, spanning indie rock, lo-fi, folk, and psychedelic styles, with roster artists including Jason Quever, Floating Action, Ash Reiter, and Seth Kauffman.
