Overview

Agit Prop was a UK record label founded and run by Chumbawamba, active from 1985 to 1992 as the band’s self-release imprint. The catalogue splits evenly across its two decades of operation—seven releases in the 1980s and seven in the 1990s—spanning punk, folk, pop rock, and leftfield alternative. Beyond Chumbawamba’s own output the roster included individual members Boff Whalley and Harry Hamer as solo or side-project credits. The label spawned two subsidiary imprints, Agit-Matter Records and Agit-Pap Records, suggesting a deliberate internal infrastructure rather than a purely ad-hoc DIY operation.
