Overview

Captain Oi! is a UK punk rock label founded in 1993 by Mark Brennan, who played bass in The Business. Its catalogue spans punk, oi, new wave, hardcore, and post-punk, peaking in the 2000s with 169 releases and building on a strong 1990s foundation of 109; output declined sharply from the 2010s onward. The label operates under the Cherry Red Records umbrella and runs a sublabel, Captain Mod, pointing to a parallel interest in mod-adjacent strands of British street music. Roster mainstays include UK Subs and Cock Sparrer, two of the genre’s most enduring acts.
