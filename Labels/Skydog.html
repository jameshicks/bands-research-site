Skydog

Discogs ID: 35054. Sublabel of Jungle Records (Discogs ID 14333). French garage-rock and proto-punk imprint associated with Marc Zermati (Paris); connected to the French punk scene of the 1970s–80s. Sub-imprints include Kind Of Groove (ID 16627), Skydog International (ID 222677), and Skydog Roots Of Garage Rock (ID 729770). Released material by The Flamin’ Groovies and others; SKY catalog numbers.
