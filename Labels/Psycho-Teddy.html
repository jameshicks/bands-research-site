Oct 19, 20266 min read

Psycho Teddy

Discogs IDs: 50751 (Psycho Teddy), 722341 (Psycho Teddy!), 1260397 (Psycho Teddy Records)
Founded: New York City, USA
Active: 1988–present
Primary Artist: Thomas Truax

Self-run imprint of Thomas Truax, an American musician and maker of homemade instruments. Founded in New York in 1988 with the Spook Show 12” EP (PT-1001), the label has remained Truax’s primary release vehicle across his entire career — from New York new wave through his relocation to the UK and into the 2020s. It appears in Discogs under three slightly different names (Psycho Teddy, Psycho Teddy!, Psycho Teddy Records), most likely reflecting informal re-registrations or stylistic variations rather than distinct corporate entities.

The label is not a roster label in any conventional sense: virtually every release is by Truax himself or by his band Like Wow. From 2012 onward, Truax co-released his albums with UK independent Blang, which handled European distribution while Psycho Teddy remained the originating imprint.

History

New York Origins, 1988–2000

The label launched with Spook Show (1988, PT-1001), a 12” vinyl EP issued under the name Thomas Truax & His Dark Tricks — an early name variation before Truax settled on his current identity. The EP is tagged New Wave and carries no additional personnel credits in Discogs beyond Truax himself.

From 1993 to 2000, the label issued a run of releases by Like Wow, the band Truax formed with Scott Hartley and Carlos Vivanco:

  • Whacked In Waco! (1993, US, PT-1002)
  • Ear Infection (1994, Canada, PT-1003)
  • Night Creature, Day Job Blues EP (1994, Canada, PT.1004)
  • Drinking Blues Classic Split Single (1996, US, PT1005) — split with Bliss Blood
  • Burn World Burn (2000, US, PT1006) — credited to Like Wow; composed by Truax and Carlos Vivanco throughout; Vivanco plays and produces

The Like Wow releases document Truax’s early creative partnership with Vivanco, who is credited as composer, electric guitar, bass, drums, keyboards, engineer, and producer on parts of the catalog.

UK Period and Mature Solo Work, 2004–2011

After Truax’s relocation to the UK, Psycho Teddy continued as his primary label alongside licensing deals with Breakin’ Beats and SL Records:

  • Full Moon Over Wowtown (2004, Canada, PT-1008) — Truax’s first solo album under this catalog; Canadian pressing; European editions on Breakin’ Beats
  • Audio Addiction (2005, UK, PT-1009) — CDr release
  • Songs From The Films Of David Lynch (2009, UK & US, PT1010) — co-released with SL Records; ten covers of songs from David Lynch films; Truax plays all conventional and invented instruments; mastered by Dirk T. Jambor; cover co-designed with Philip Lethen
  • Sonic Dreamer (2010, UK & US, PT-1011) — co-released with SL Records; 11-track LP

A series of digital/CDr singles followed in 2011 under the Psycho Teddy / SL Records imprints:

  • March Winds (PT-1015)
  • A Gold Star For Miss July (PT-1018)
  • Lost On The Moon In June (PT-1018)
  • Midnight In August (PT-1020)
  • Grandmother’s Advice (PT-1021, also on master 985701)
  • November In Berlin (PT-1023)

The 2011 monthly singles series is a Monthly Journal precursor — Truax releasing one track per month through the year, each assigned its own catalog number.

Blang Partnership, 2012–2023

From Monthly Journal onward, Psycho Teddy co-released with Blang, the UK independent label:

  • Monthly Journal (2012, Europe, BLANG 41) — 12-track concept album; sole Truax performer credit; catalog numbers reflect Blang’s primary role in European distribution
  • More! More! More! (2014, UK, no catalog number)
  • Trolls, Girls & Lullabies (2014, UK, PT-1026) — issued under Psycho Teddy! variant
  • Jetstream Sunset (2015, UK, PT-1028) — issued under both Psycho Teddy and Psycho Teddy! entries; features Brian Viglione on drums and Pete Harvey on cello
  • Save Me (2018, UK, PT-1031) — split single with Gemma Ray; Ray on vocals, Truax on instruments and vocals, mastered by Pete Maher
  • All That Heaven Allows (2018, UK, PT-1032) — issued under Psycho Teddy Records variant; 10-track LP
  • Dream Catching Songs (2023, UK, BLANG104LP / BLANG104CD) — co-billed with Budgie (2) (Peter Clarke, long-time drummer of Siouxsie And The Banshees) and Mother Superior (9) (Truax’s own homemade instrument, treated as a co-performer; Discogs entity ID 13786693)

Geographic Context

Founded in New York; the early catalog (1988–2000) is predominantly US and Canadian pressings. From 2004 onward Truax was UK-based, and releases are predominantly UK or “UK & US” co-issues. The Blang partnership (from 2012) formalised the UK distribution angle — Blang is a UK independent (Discogs ID 128945; sublabel: Blang Records, ID 207986) that also works with SL Records and similar UK indie artists.

Connections

  • Thomas Truax — founder and primary artist
  • Like Wow — early Psycho Teddy act; Truax, Hartley, Vivanco
  • Blang — UK co-release partner from 2012
  • SL Records — UK co-release partner 2007–2011
  • Breakin’ Beats — European co-release partner for Full Moon Over Wowtown (2004)

Open Questions

  • Truax’s biographical timeline (exact dates of NYC-to-UK relocation, origin of the Psycho Teddy name) would require web sources.
  • More! More! More! (2014) carries no catalog number in Discogs; its format is not specified.
