Overview

Winter & Winter is an independent Munich record label founded in 1995 by Stefan Winter, releasing jazz, classical, and contemporary music under an explicitly cross-border, cross-genre philosophy. Its catalogue is heavily weighted toward the 2000s — around 94 of its roughly 120 selected releases appeared in that decade — with activity tapering sharply in the 2010s. The label operates through several distinct imprints including Film Edition, JMT Edition, and Artist Edition, each carrying its own catalogue number series. Uri Caine is among the artists most closely associated with its roster.
