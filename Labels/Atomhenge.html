Overview

Atomhenge is a Cherry Red imprint established in 2008 under Esoteric Recordings management, dedicated to reissuing Hawkwind’s catalogue from 1976 to 1997 alongside solo projects by band members including Dave Brock and Nik Turner. Its releases are remastered editions of previously out-of-print material, often expanded with bonus tracks. The label has issued 56 releases across three decades of activity — with the bulk concentrated in the 2010s — covering space rock, psychedelic rock, and progressive rock.
