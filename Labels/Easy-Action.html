Easy Action

British archival and new music record label. Discogs label ID: 43180. Parent: Easy Action Recordings Ltd. (Discogs ID: 68578). Website: easyaction.co.uk.

Overview

Easy Action releases archival recordings alongside new music from emerging artists. The label has an extensive catalog of reissues and compilations covering UK punk, post-punk, and related genres.
