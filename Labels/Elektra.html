Elektra

American record label. Discogs label ID: 651. Parent: Warner Music Group (Discogs ID: 2345). Founded 1950 by Jac Holzman in New York.

Oysterhead connection: Released The Grand Pecking Order (2001, cat# 62677-2) and the Mr. Oysterhead single.

Also the label for Phish during their primary studio run (1992–2004), creating an indirect connection between Oysterhead and Phish’s label home.

Notable sublabels: 9 sublabels documented in Discogs. 58,488 releases on this label in the database.

Luna (5) on Elektra

Luna (5) released their first four studio albums and several singles on Elektra (1992–1997): Lunapark (1992), Bewitched (1993), Penthouse (1995), Pup Tent (1997), plus Slash Your Tires, Slide, Smile, and other singles.
