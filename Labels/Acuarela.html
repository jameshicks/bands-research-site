Overview

Acuarela is a Spanish independent label founded in 1993 by poet and translator Jesús Llorente (alias Jesús Malsonando), music journalist Victor Lenore, and José Luis Villalobos. In its early years the label was assisted by Luis Calvo of Elefant Records, placing it within the overlapping networks of the Spanish indie scene. The catalogue—85 releases concentrated heavily in the 2000s—runs across indie rock, folk rock, lo-fi, and indie pop, with artists such as Migala, Sr. Chinarro, and Coque Yturriaga among its roster. Publishing is handled through Ediciones Acuarela S.L. (formed March 2000), and since 2010 the operating company has been Acuarela Producciones S.L., which also serves as the parent entity.
