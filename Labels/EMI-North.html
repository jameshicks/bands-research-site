Overview

EMI North is a Leeds-based imprint of EMI Records, launched in January 2023 and run by Clive Cawley out of Duke Studios — the first major British label to operate a physical space outside London. Its catalogue sits squarely in indie and alternative rock, with post-rock and shoegaze textures, and all eleven releases to date fall within the 2020s. The roster is anchored by Doves and the band’s individual members — Jimi Goodwin, Jez Williams, and Andy Williams — alongside producer Ben Hillier.

Launch rationale and Leeds base

EMI North’s January 2023 launch was driven by a broader regional investment strategy, not by any specific artist signing. Doves do not appear in any launch-day announcement and were not a catalyst; they joined the roster sometime between launch (January 2023) and the album announcement for Constellations For The Lonely (announced late 2024, released February 2025).

The stated drivers for the label’s creation:

  • EMI Records’ decentralisation push. Co-presidents of EMI Records Jo Charrington and Rebecca Allen framed EMI North as an extension of discovering and nurturing talent “right across the UK”, making it the first major British label to operate a physical space outside London. (Music Business Worldwide, 2023-01-27)
  • Clive Cawley’s Yorkshire roots. Cawley, appointed President of EMI North, is originally from Yorkshire and began his career as a sales rep for Universal Music in North Yorkshire. His appointment was described as a “full circle” return. (Music Business Worldwide, 2023-01-27; Music Week, 2023)
  • Leeds City of Culture 2023. The launch was timed to coincide with Leeds Year of Culture 2023, positioning the city as “centre stage of the UK’s cultural scene”. (NME, 2023-01-27; Rolling Stone UK, 2023)
  • Local infrastructure. Leeds was chosen partly because of “initial partners” available on the ground: Clue Records and Come Play With Me (a Leeds-based non-profit music development organisation). (Come Play With Me / CPWM, via cpwm.co)
  • Bridging the North–South gulf. Cawley’s stated goal was to demonstrate “that career development and most importantly, breaking artist success, is now absolutely possible to achieve outside London.” The label operates from Duke Studios, a creative hub in Leeds city centre. (Music Week, later profile)

No specific artists were announced at the January 2023 launch. The early catalogue (2023–2024) includes releases distributed or co-released via Clue Records and Come Play With Me — The Wedding Present, Yowl, LYR, Green Gardens — before Doves’ Constellations For The Lonely (Feb 2025) became the label’s most prominent release. The oversight structure includes Nicola Spokes (from Lancashire), who leads “The Collective” department overseeing EMI North, with label manager Scott Lewis, coordinator Jasmine Sidhu, and marketing lead Tasha Pert on the ground in Leeds. (Music Week profile)

Sources checked: Music Business Worldwide (launch announcement), NME (launch), Music Week (launch + later profile), Rolling Stone UK (launch), Come Play With Me website, Digital Music News, Far Out Magazine, Celebrity Access, Music Biz Association. None name Doves at launch or suggest the band was a catalyst.

Corporate structure

No parent or sublabel recorded on Discogs. EMI North is a sub-label of EMI Records, which is itself part of Universal Music Group (UK). (Music Business Worldwide)
