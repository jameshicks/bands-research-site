Overview

Three Little Girls Recordings was an independent label from Louisville, Kentucky, founded in 1991 by Ed Lutz and Jason Hayden. The imprint grew out of an early “Aftereffects Of Insomnia” compilation self-released without a label name by Hayden, Mike Jarboe, and Sean McLoughlin; Lutz and Hayden then partnered to reissue it and establish the label proper. All 14 releases fell within the 1990s decade, spanning punk, hardcore, indie rock, and math rock — a range that reflected Louisville’s fertile underground scene of the era. The label closed in 1996, with its roster including Tara Jane O’Neil and Mike Jarboe among others.
