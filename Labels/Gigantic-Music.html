Gigantic Music

Discogs ID: 90341
Origin: 59 Franklin Street, Suite 403, New York, NY 10013 (TriBeCa)
Active: 2003–2011 (per catalog)
Catalog prefixes: GM / GM0 / G7 / GTC

Independent New York label founded by Brian Devine and operated in close conjunction with Gigantic Studios — the recording studio at the same TriBeCa address run by house producer Chris Zane. The label-studio symbiosis is its defining feature: virtually every release in the catalog was produced, engineered, or mixed by Zane and/or his studio engineer Alex Aldi, with Billy Pavone as the third leg of the production infrastructure. The most prominent release is The Walkmen’s You & Me (GTC17, 2008), recorded at Gigantic Studios and at Sweet Tea Studios and Brutalis Studio with Zane and John Agnello.

The label’s signing range was eclectic — Brooklyn indie rock, freak-folk, noise, international acts (Norwegian, Swedish, British) — unified more by the Gigantic Studios production house than by a single sound. The catalog numbers shifted over time: early singles used GM70xxx and GM0xxx; a G7xxx series appears around 2007 for 7-inch singles; the GTC prefix became dominant for full-lengths from 2007 onward.
