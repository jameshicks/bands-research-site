Overview

Zealrecords is an independent label operated by Geert Mets, active from 1997 with its catalogue concentrated heavily in the 2000s and 2010s across indie rock, folk, post-rock, and alternative styles. Its roster includes Isbells, Marble Sounds, and Max Avery Lichtenstein. The label is accompanied by three sublabels — Zealdigital, Zealectronic, and Ziel — suggesting distinct format or genre arms, though the principal imprint accounts for the bulk of its roughly 43 releases and 120 catalogue credits.
