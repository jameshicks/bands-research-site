I.R.S. Records

American independent record label founded in 1979 by Miles Copeland III. Distributed through A&M Records (parent label ID: 904 on Discogs). Home to a wide range of post-punk, new wave, and alternative artists through the 1980s. Discogs label ID: 4087.

Overview

I.R.S. (International Record Syndicate) was one of the defining US indie labels of the post-punk era. Its roster included R.E.M., The Go-Go’s, The English Beat, Squeeze, Oingo Boingo, and Wall of Voodoo, among many others.

Sublabels include: Faulty Products, I.R.S. Metal, I.R.S. No Speak, Juice (2).

General Public Connection

Released US editions of General Public’s albums: …All The Rage (1984) and Hand To Mouth (1986). UK/European editions were on Virgin. The Beat (2) catalog was also on I.R.S. in North America.

Fine Young Cannibals Connection

Released the US editions of both Fine Young Cannibals studio albums: Fine Young Cannibals (IRS-5683, 1985) and The Raw & The Cooked (IRS-6273, 1988). US distribution was through MCA Records.
