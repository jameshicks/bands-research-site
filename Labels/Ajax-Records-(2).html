Ajax Records (2)

Chicago-based independent record label, active 1988–1999. Discogs label ID: 58017. Owned and operated by Tim Adams (11) (Discogs artist ID: 13664341). Mailing address: PO Box 805293, Chicago IL 60680-4114. See also 3 Beads Of Sweat (related imprint).

Not to be confused with Ajax Records (the UK dance label, Discogs ID: 10010).

Chicago Reader profile: https://www.chicagoreader.com/chicago/ajax-wont-quit/Content?oid=896922

Overview

Ajax Records was a Chicago indie label founded by Tim Adams, who combined it with a mail-order one-stop distribution business (also called Ajax) that began as a home operation selling small and micro-label indie records, eventually growing into a record store. The label is described on Discogs as a “1980s–1990s indie rock label” but its catalog spans a wide range of underground styles: noise rock, lo-fi singer-songwriter, post-punk, New Zealand underground, and avant-rock.

The label’s catalog runs from 1988 (AJAX 001, Joe Young’s Bury The Needle) to 1999 (AJAX 060, The Mountain Goats’ Bitter Melon Farm), with approximately 60 catalog numbers. It punched well above its weight in terms of international reach: the roster includes acts from New Zealand (Peter Jefferies, This Kind Of Punishment, The Renderers, Graeme Jefferies), Australia (The Cannanes), the Netherlands (Furtips), and across the US.

Ajax is particularly notable for being the primary label for The Mountain Goats during John Darnielle’s formative cassette-to-vinyl transition years, releasing five records between 1993 and 1999 — including his first proper vinyl albums.

Key Artists

The Mountain Goats

The label’s most internationally recognized act. Ajax released five records across the label’s active life: Chile De Árbol (AJAX 028, 1993), Zopilote Machine (AJAX 036, 1994), Nothing For Juice (AJAX 056, 1996), Protein Source Of The Future…Now! (AJAX 059, 1999), and Bitter Melon Farm (AJAX 060, 1999). These span John Darnielle’s lo-fi cassette-boombox era through his early vinyl output. Zopilote Machine in particular is widely cited as a landmark of the period.

Peter Jefferies

The most-released artist on Ajax (11 credits), with albums, EPs, and collaborative releases across 1990–1996. Jefferies is a New Zealand musician whose work on Ajax covers solo records (The Last Great Challenge In A Dull World, Electricity, A Chorus Of Interludes), duo records with Robbie Muir (Catapult, Swerve), and a split with Stephen Kilroy. His brother Graeme Jefferies also released on the label (Messages For The Cakekitchen). Peter Jefferies also served as mastering engineer on several Ajax releases by other NZ artists.

The Cannanes

Australian indie band with the second-most releases on the label (9 credits, 1992–1998), including Stumpvision, Short Poppy Syndrome, A Love Affair With Nature, and Arty Barbecue. Core lineup: David Nichols, Frances Gibson, Gavin Butler, Stephen O’Neil.

Antiseen

The label’s earliest major signing after the debut. Three releases 1989–1991 (Blood Of Freaks, Two Headed Dog, Walking Dead / Haunted House). North Carolina noise/punk band; Jeff Clayton (2) as primary vocalist/producer, Joe Young on guitar, Doug Throgmorton on drums.

Thinking Fellers Union Local 282

San Francisco avant-rock group; two Ajax releases (The Natural Finger 1990, The Funeral Pudding 1994), both produced by Greg Freeman.

Unrest (2)

DC indie band’s Imperial F.F.R.R. (AJAX 019, 1992) was produced by Mark Robinson, Phil Krauth, and Wharton Tiers, with design by TeenBeat Graphica. The album was reissued on Ajax in 2023, making it one of the label’s longest-lived catalog items.

God Is My Co-Pilot

NYC avant-garde act. Two Ajax releases (How I Got Over 1992, Kittybait 5Song EP 1993). How I Got Over features John Zorn on alto saxophone and Jad Fair on guitar; produced by Craig Flanagin and Don Fury.

Shrimp Boat

Chicago band; Small Wonder (AJAX 021, 1992). Members include Sam Prekop (vocals/guitar/bass), Ian Schneller, Brad Wood (drums/saxophone), Eric Claridge, David Kroll. Prekop and Schneller later formed The Sea And Cake.

East River Pipe

F.M. Cornog’s lo-fi solo project; Shining Hours In A Can (AJAX 041, 1994), one of the earlier East River Pipe vinyl releases.

This Kind Of Punishment

New Zealand post-punk duo (Peter Jefferies and Graeme Jefferies, among others). Two releases on Ajax in 1993 (A Beard Of Bees, In The Same Room / 5 By Four).

The Renderers

New Zealand band: Brian Crook, Maryrose Crook, Denise Roughan, Robbie Yeats. Two Ajax releases (That Dog’s Head In The Gutter Gives Off Vibrations 1994, The Surface Of Jupiter 1996), both engineered by Tex Houston.

Ashtray Boy

Chicago band with three Ajax releases 1994–1996. Randall Lee as core member; recordings split between Chicago and Sydney sessions.

David Kilgour

New Zealand guitarist (of The Chills); First Steps & False Alarms (AJAX 040, 1995), solo home demos compilation.

Roy Montgomery

NZ guitarist; Just Melancholy (AJAX 051, 1996).

Seam

Chicago band (Sooyoung Park); Sukiyaki (AJAX 053, 1999), one of the label’s final releases.

Refrigerator

Pomona, CA lo-fi band (Allen Callaci vocals, Dennis Callaci instruments); Down With (AJAX 032, 1993).

Notable Production Credits

Several recurring producers and engineers across the catalog:

  • Greg Freeman — engineered/produced both Thinking Fellers Union Local 282 albums
  • Paul Mahern — produced Modern Vending records (early catalog)
  • Joe Carducci — produced Repulse Kava’s Flow Gently Sweet Alpha (AJAX 008)
  • Wharton Tiers — engineered Unrest’s Imperial F.F.R.R.
  • Ira Kaplan — produced Run On (AJAX 037); yes, the Yo La Tengo frontman
  • Don Fury — co-produced God Is My Co-Pilot’s How I Got Over
  • Brad Wood — drummer on Shrimp Boat; also part of Chicago recording scene (later engineered major indie records)
  • Chris Xefos — produced Wonderama releases (AJAX 006, AJAX 014)
  • Peter Jefferies — served as mastering engineer on multiple NZ-connected releases beyond his own records

The New Zealand Connection

A striking feature of the Ajax catalog is its deep roots in the New Zealand underground. Tim Adams evidently had close ties to the NZ Flying Nun / post-Flying Nun scene. Artists with NZ ties on the label:

  • Peter Jefferies (solo + duo with Robbie Muir + duo with Stephen Kilroy)
  • Graeme Jefferies (Messages For The Cakekitchen)
  • This Kind Of Punishment (Peter + Graeme Jefferies + others)
  • David Kilgour (of The Chills)
  • Roy Montgomery
  • The Renderers (Brian Crook, Maryrose Crook, Denise Roughan, Robbie Yeats)
  • Wreck Small Speakers On Expensive Stereos (Michael Morley, of The Dead C)

Several of these releases were mastered to DAT by Peter Jefferies and Barry McConnachie, establishing a consistent NZ-side production chain feeding into Ajax’s Chicago distribution base.
