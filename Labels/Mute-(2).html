Overview

Mute (2) is a Discogs catalogue device rather than a real imprint — it collects unofficial releases (bootlegs, counterfeits, and pirate compilations) that falsely present themselves as genuine Mute Records releases. The catalogue spans from 1986 through the 2020s, with the bulk of entries concentrated in the 2000s and 2010s, clustering around prominent Mute artists including Depeche Mode, Nick Cave, and Mick Harvey. Styles range across Synth-pop, Electro, and Alternative Rock, reflecting the acts most frequently bootlegged under the Mute name.
