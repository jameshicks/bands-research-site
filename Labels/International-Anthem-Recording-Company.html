International Anthem Recording Company

Chicago-based record label focused on adventurous jazz, improvised music, and experimental work. Founded by Scott McNiece. Home to Jaimie Branch’s fly or die series, Exploding Star Orchestra later releases, and many key Chicago creative-music artists.
