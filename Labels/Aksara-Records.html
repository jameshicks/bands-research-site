Overview

Aksara Records is an Indonesian independent label founded in 2002, occupying a central position in the country’s indie rock and pop scene of the 2000s. Its catalogue of around two dozen releases from that decade spans indie pop, indie rock, alternative rock, electro, and punk, with roster names including Mondo Gascaro, Fay Ismail, Aghi Narottama, and David Tarigan. The label was largely dormant after the 2000s before a small cluster of releases appeared in the 2020s. Its corporate structure and parent-label relationships remain undocumented.
