Overview

Contempo Records is a Florentine label founded around 1982 by Giampiero Barlotti, specialising in industrial, new wave, goth rock, experimental, and post-punk. Its output peaked in the 1990s — its most active decade at 90 releases — before the label suspended operations in 1995; it relaunched in 2005, reissuing its own back catalogue alongside new productions. Until 1992 the label also functioned as a distributor, a role subsequently spun off as Contempo International. Beyond the main imprint, the operation extended into sublabels covering library music, soundtracks, and Italian pop, with a roster that drew in UK industrial and post-punk acts including Clock DVA and Adi Newton.
