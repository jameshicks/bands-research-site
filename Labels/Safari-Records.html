Safari Records

UK record label. Discogs label ID: 35018. Parent label: Cherry Red Records Ltd. (ID: 269699). Release count: 684.

Overview

Safari Records was the primary label for The Electric Chairs (Wayne County & The Electric Chairs) through their main 1978–82 output. The label is now archived under Cherry Red Records Ltd., which also reissued some of the Safari catalog. Safari’s release count of 684 suggests a moderately sized independent with a broader roster beyond the Electric Chairs.
