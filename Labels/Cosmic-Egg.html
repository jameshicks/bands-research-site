Overview

Cosmic Egg is a CD imprint run by Ultima Thule through their Ultimate Transmissions outlet, dedicated to Krautrock reissues. The name comes from Steve Freeman and Alan Freeman’s Krautrock encyclopedia The Crack In The Cosmic Egg, and both Freemans appear on the roster. The catalogue spans the 2000s and 2010s, with the great majority of its releases concentrated in the 2010s, covering Krautrock alongside experimental, fusion, and psychedelic rock.
