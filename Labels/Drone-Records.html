From Discogs

German Industrial / Dark Ambient label started to release 7” vinyl in 1993. Those releases are mostly available in their first edition with handmade covers. Some releases were re-released with standard covers. The main theme for the 7”-series is drone / dark ambient music.

Most, if not all, records are also made available as promos. These are the same vinyls, but lack the packaging; instead they come in either a white paper or clear plastic sleeve with the record insert, and often have a promo stamp inked on the center-label.

— Discogs label profile, ID 1533

Overview

Drone Records is a German underground label specialising in drone, dark ambient, and industrial music, best known for its handmade 7” vinyl series launched in 1993. Its catalogue spans four decades — peaking in the 2000s with 33 releases and building on 26 in the 1990s — with a further trickle of releases into the 2020s, totalling around 72 selected releases across 259 credits. The label has spawned several sublabels, including Substantia Innominata and Drone-Mind // Mind-Drone, extending its reach within the same dark ambient underground. Many records were simultaneously issued as promos in plain sleeves, a practice that speaks to the label’s deep-underground, non-commercial orientation.
