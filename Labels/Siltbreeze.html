Siltbreeze

US independent label based in Philadelphia. Discogs label ID: 24980. 249 releases documented.

Key imprint for American underground, noise rock, lo-fi, and international underground releases in the 1990s–2000s. Released Venom P. Stinger’s Tearbucketer (1996), the band’s final studio release. Also released three Charalambides albums: Union (SB26, 1994), Market Square (SB46/47, 1995), and Houston (SB 67, 1998).
