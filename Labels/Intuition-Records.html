Overview

Intuition Records is a German jazz and world music label imprint founded by Vera Brandes in Cologne in the mid-1980s, with a parallel U.S. branch based in New York. Originally a division of the veraBra Music Group, the label also operated under the shortened name Intuition; it subsequently became a subsidiary of Intuition Music & Media GmbH before being integrated into Schott Music & Media GmbH. The catalogue of roughly 150 releases is concentrated heavily in the 1990s and spans Contemporary Jazz, Fusion, Experimental, Ambient, and Salsa — with roster artists including Jon Hassell, Eddie Palmieri, and Mike Mainieri. The label also operates several sublabels, among them European Jazz Legends and the Intuition Master Series.
