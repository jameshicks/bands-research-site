Geffen Records

US major/independent label. Founded 1980 by David Geffen. Discogs ID: 821.

One of the first solo acts signed to Geffen was Adele Bertei, whose “Build Me A Bridge” (produced by Thomas Dolby) was released on the label in 1983. The label’s early roster mixed artists across rock, pop, and new wave. Sold to MCA in 1990; later absorbed into Universal Music Group / Interscope.

The DGC imprint (David Geffen Company, Discogs ID 86487) was created in 1990 as a Geffen subsidiary focused on alternative and progressive sounds. DGC was home to Nirvana, Sonic Youth, Beck, Hole, Weezer, and Jawbreaker among others through the 1990s. After the Universal/PolyGram merger (1999) Geffen became part of Interscope Geffen A&M; DGC was permanently merged back into Geffen in 2021.
