Overview

Trouble in Mind is a Chicago record label founded in 2009 by Bill Roe and Lisa Roe, with a catalogue anchored in psychedelic rock, garage rock, indie rock, and post-punk. The label launched modestly before expanding sharply through the 2010s — 118 releases in that decade alone — and has remained active into the 2020s with a further 59 releases. It operates a sublabel, Explorers Series, and has developed ongoing working relationships with artists including Mikey Young, Klaus Johann Grobe, and Jason Ward.
