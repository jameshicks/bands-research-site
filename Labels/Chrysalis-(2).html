Overview

Chrysalis (2) is a Discogs identifier for counterfeit pressings that fraudulently use the real Chrysalis Records imprint. The bootlegs collected under this entry impersonate the legitimate UK label by reproducing its branding on unauthorised releases, with the catalogue centred on artists genuinely associated with Chrysalis — chiefly Jethro Tull and related personnel (Ian Anderson, Martin Barre) and UFO. Activity spans the 1970s through the 2020s, with the heaviest concentration of known fakes appearing in the 1990s and 2000s across prog rock, hard rock, and folk rock styles.
