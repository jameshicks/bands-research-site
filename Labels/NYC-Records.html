Overview

NYC Records is a US jazz imprint operating under the parent company NYC Records Inc. (label code LC 05833), active from 1992 and concentrated heavily in the 1990s — 29 of its 32 catalogued releases appeared in that decade, with three more in the early 2000s before the label went quiet. The catalogue spans contemporary jazz, fusion, jazz-funk, smooth jazz, and soul-jazz, with a roster anchored by Mike Mainieri, Michael Brecker, and Marc Antoine among others. A separate entity, NYC Records (4), handles unofficial releases under the same name.
