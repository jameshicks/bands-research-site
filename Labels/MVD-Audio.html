Overview

MVD Audio is a subsidiary of MVD Entertainment Group focused on exclusive distribution and CD representation. It operates through content agreements with labels across Europe, including in-akustik in Germany, Secret Records Limited in the UK, and Metal Mind Productions in Poland, alongside a roster of independent artists. The catalogue tilts toward experimental and punk-adjacent material — The Residents and their business entity The Cryptic Corporation are among the distributed acts, as is Curt Kirkwood of the Meat Puppets. Activity built steadily from a modest 2000s start to a peak in the 2010s, with over 160 releases in that decade alone.
