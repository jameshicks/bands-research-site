Raster-Noton

Discogs Label ID: 2525
Origin: Chemnitz / Berlin, Germany
Founded: 1999 (merger); predecessor labels active from 1994/1996
Active: 1999–2017 (split back into Raster (2) and Noton in 2017)
Label Code: LC 01293
Genres: Electronic, Glitch, Minimal, Electroacoustic, Modern Classical

raster-noton.archiv für ton und nichtton — the full formal name — was one of Germany’s most influential experimental electronic labels, spanning minimal-glitch sound art to conceptual composition. It operated out of Chemnitz (and later Berlin), and its catalog holds roughly 355 releases as of the Discogs snapshot.

Founding History

The label emerged from the fusion of two independent labels that had been co-releasing material since 1996:

Rastermusic (later styled “Raster Music”) was founded by Olaf Bender (recording as Byetone) and Frank Bretschneider in 1996, in Chemnitz (formerly Karl-Marx-Stadt). Both had roots in the post-reunification East German underground scene — Bretschneider notably as a member of AG Geige and Signal. The label’s philosophy centered on minimal and repetitive music with considered pop leanings, released in a highly systematic, design-conscious format.

Noton.archiv für ton und nichtton was founded by Carsten Nicolai in 1994 (active from 1996, first release N-001 Spin + Käfig in 1995). Nicolai conceived it as a platform for conceptual and experimental projects at the intersection of music, visual art, and science. Noton launched the ongoing clear series and in 1999–2000 developed the celebrated 20’ to 2000 CD-magazine series — conceptual CDs each exactly 20 minutes long.

The two labels had collaborated on releases from 1996 onward. In late 1999 they formally merged into Raster-Noton. The merger united Bender and Bretschneider’s rhythmic minimalism with Nicolai’s art-science conceptualism, making the combined imprint the central node of a global glitch-electroacoustic community.

In 2017, following 20th anniversary celebrations, the founders announced the label would restructure. Raster-Noton split into two independent entities: Raster (2) (run by Olaf Bender, handling the back catalog and new music in the Rastermusic tradition) and Noton (run by Carsten Nicolai / Alva Noto, representing all past and future Nicolai/Noto projects). Both labels continue independently.

Catalog Highlights

Collaborative Series (Alva Noto + Ryuichi Sakamoto)

The most internationally celebrated body of work on the label. Nicolai and Ryuichi Sakamoto collaborated across five albums of piano and electronics, beginning with Vrioon (2002, R-N 50) and continuing through Insen (2005, R-N 65), Revep (2006), Utp_ (2009, with Ensemble Modern), and Summvs (2011). All credited jointly as Alva Noto + Ryuichi Sakamoto (Discogs ID 66732). The series blends Sakamoto’s acoustic grand piano with Nicolai’s laptop-generated glitch and sine-wave micro-structures.

Design Philosophy

The label’s visual identity was exceptionally consistent. In-house design (credited to “Raster-Noton” as entity, and to designer Nibo across 9 releases) used austere typographic layouts, monochrome or minimal-color palettes, and systematic catalog numbering (R-N 50 onwards for standard releases; CDR prefix for earlier CD-R editions). Carsten Nicolai and Olaf Bender also took direct design credits on multiple releases.
