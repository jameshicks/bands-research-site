Overview

Tiny Global Productions is an independent label that began releasing music in 2015, working primarily in indie rock, post-punk, alternative rock, folk rock, and new wave. Its catalogue spans 179 credits across two decades, with 17 releases in the 2010s giving way to a more prolific 47 in the 2020s. The roster centres on veterans of the British post-punk and DIY scenes, including Martin Bramah and Blue Orchids alongside Stuart Moxham of Young Marble Giants fame, and James Stephen Finn and Andreas Schmid.
