Overview

Magniph is an independent record label based in Tokyo, Japan, founded in 2012. Its catalogue spans indie rock, post-rock, and experimental music, with releases from notable Japanese acts including Jesse Ruins, Mono, and The NOVEMBERS alongside Cairophenomenons and American industry figure Jeremy DeVine. The label built a focused catalogue of 35 releases through the 2010s before activity dropped sharply; only three releases are recorded in the 2020s.
