Der Schöne Hjuler-Memorial-Fond

Discogs label ID: 63362 Country: Germany Address: Weizenstieg 11, D-24941 Flensburg, Germany Email: mamabaer-hjuler@versanet.de Web: http://www.asylum-lunaticum.de Total Discogs releases: 931

Self-release imprint run by Kommissar Hjuler from his Flensburg home address. All releases are in very limited editions with handmade artworks. Official catalog numbers start with SHMF-; items given informally to friends are catalogued as SHMF-none.

Distinct from Psych.KG (which is the collaborative/network label): Der Schöne Hjuler-Memorial-Fond is Hjuler’s primary vehicle for solo output and very small-run releases, and also serves as the label for later collaborative releases with Family Fodder and others (particularly from the early 2020s onward, when some collab releases migrated from Psych.KG to this imprint).
