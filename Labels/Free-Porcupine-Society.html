Overview

Free Porcupine Society (FPS) is a small US independent label founded in 2002 by Rob Fisk and his wife Kelly Goode. It was started initially to release output from their own band, 7 Year Rabbit Cycle, but quickly expanded into a platform for hand-crafted, limited-edition releases across experimental rock, noise folk, free improvisation, neofolk, and avant-garde electronic music.

The label’s self-description: releases for “no wave hermit folk- dirt pirate dance- avant breadmaking field recordings.” A hallmark of FPS was Rob Fisk’s custom hand-made packaging — elaborately designed CD, LP, and book packages that were collectible objects as much as musical releases.

The catalog is notable for releasing early recordings by Grouper (Way Their Crept, 2005; Wide, 2006), pressing Deerhoof’s Milk Man (2004) on vinyl, and putting out Current 93’s Earth Covers Earth (2005) on LP. Xiu Xiu had the most releases on the label (five entries). Rob Fisk later co-founded the label Breath Sun Bone Blood; he has since relocated to Berlin.

Key Releases — Detail

Animal People — 7 Year Rabbit Cycle (FPS 001, 2003)

CD album. Primary lineup: Rob Fisk (guitar, violin, artwork), Miya Osaki (bass), Steve Gigante (drums, guitar). Vocal duties split across Kelly Goode, Rob Fisk, Miya Osaki, Steve Gigante per track.

Milk Man — Deerhoof (FPS 007, 2004)

Vinyl LP. One of several labels to press this album. Connected to Rob Fisk’s dual role as both FPS founder and Deerhoof member.

Wind Machines — 7 Year Rabbit Cycle (FPS 008, 2004)

Full lineup on this release: Rob Fisk, Kelly Goodefisk, Miya Osaki, Steve Gigante, Jamie Stewart (of Xiu Xiu), Adam Werner, Cain Blanchard. Mixed and mastered by Greg Saunier and John Dieterich (both of Deerhoof). Includes a cover of “Gimme Gimme Gimme” (written by Greg Ginn).

Earth Covers Earth — Current 93 (FPS 011, 2005)

Vinyl LP reissue. Historical lineup credited as “Current 93 Then And Never Again” — includes David Tibet, Steven Stapleton (NWW), Douglas Pearce (Death In June), John Balance (Coil), Rose McDowall, Diana Rogerson, Ian Read, Tony Wakeford, among others. Mixed by Steven Stapleton; cover art by David Michael (Bunting).

Stories For Owls — Badgerlore (FPS 012, 2005)

CD. Performers: Ben Chasny (Six Organs of Admittance), Pete Swanson (Yellow Swans), Rob Fisk, Tom Carter (Charalambides).

Way Their Crept — Grouper (FPS 014, 2005)

CD. Grouper’s debut full-length on CD. Liz Harris sole performer, recorder, and (with Pete Swanson) mastering engineer. Cover photography by Peter Harris.

Congs for Brums — Ches Smith (FPS 015, 2006)

CD. Jazz/free improvisation. Recorded by Eli Crews; mixed by Alex Newport; mastered by Tardon Feathered. Artwork by Miya Osaki.
