Overview

Factory Once was a short-lived reissue imprint launched by Tony Wilson in 1996 as a companion to Factory Too, dedicated to anthologising and repackaging archive material from the Durutti Column and Happy Mondays through a co-publishing arrangement with London Records. Its nine-release catalogue spans exclusively the late 1990s before the label quietly folded around 1998. Among its more unusual releases was a promo VHS — “The Third Member of the Durutti Column” — packaged in an oversized clamshell box alongside a run of early Durutti Column CD reissues, containing a 13-minute documentary about producer Martin Hannett directed by Tosh Ryan.
