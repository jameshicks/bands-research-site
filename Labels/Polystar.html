Overview

Polystar is a Japanese record label founded in 1980 as the third PolyGram company established in Japan, initially distributed by Nippon Phonogram Co., Ltd. and pressed by Polydor K.K. Its catalogue is concentrated in the 1980s and spans Synth-pop, J-pop, Disco, Pop Rock, and Indie Rock. The label’s roster has included domestic acts such as Wink and Keigo Oyamada (later Cornelius) alongside Japanese pressings for international artists including U2. It operates as part of Polystar Co., Ltd. and has spawned several sublabels — Atmark Corporation, Go! Cinemania, and Poplot among them.
