Axiom

Discogs Label ID: 1072 Parent: Island Records Active: 1989–c.1997 (later releases via Palm Pictures) Head: Bill Laswell Release count: ~236 (Discogs)

Overview

Bill Laswell’s label imprint, operating as a sublabel of Island Records. Founded around 1989 as a vehicle for Laswell’s world music, avant-jazz, dub, and experimental production work. Ran until roughly 1997; some later releases appeared under Axiom via Palm Pictures into the early 2000s.
