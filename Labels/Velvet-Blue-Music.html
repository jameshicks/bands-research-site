Overview

Velvet Blue Music is an independent label founded in 1996 in Huntington Beach, California, anchored in indie rock, shoegaze, dream pop, and synth-pop. Its roster has consistently drawn from the Christian alternative music world, with key acts including Starflyer 59, Jason Martin, Ronnie Martin of Joy Electric, Frank Lenz, and Jeff Cloud. The catalogue grew modestly in its first two decades — around 13 releases in the 1990s and 12 in the 2000s — before expanding sharply in the 2010s (23 releases) and again in the 2020s (34 releases), making the present decade the label’s most active period. It also operates a sublabel, Small Sounds Press.
