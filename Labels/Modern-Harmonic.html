Modern Harmonic

US reissue and archival label. Discogs label ID: 997548

Specialists in unearthing and reissuing cult and obscure recordings from the 1970s–80s underground. Released the posthumous Pulsallama compilation Pulsallama (MHCD-216, 2020), compiled by Jay Millar and Brian Thompson.
