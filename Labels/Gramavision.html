Gramavision

American independent record label, New York City, 1980s–1990s. Discogs label ID: varies by pressing.

Overview

New York independent focused on jazz and experimental music. Released records by figures in the downtown NYC scene including Jamaaladeen Tacuma. Anton Fier provided DMX drum programming on So Tranquilizin’ (1985) by Cosmetic with Jamaaladeen Tacuma — a session that also featured Adele Bertei, Maripol, Ornette Coleman, and John Robie.

Rykodisc Connection

Listed as a sublabel of Rykodisc (Discogs IDs: 12057 and 1023447 for two distinct Gramavision entries). The Rykodisc relationship likely reflects a distribution or acquisition arrangement for Gramavision’s catalog.
