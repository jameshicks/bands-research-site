Overview

Naked Brain was a small independent label whose entire documented catalogue falls within the 1990s, encompassing six releases across indie rock, punk, psychedelic rock, electro, and experimental territory. Its roster mixed UK acts — most notably Gaye Bykers On Acid — with The Jackofficers, the electro side project of Gibby Haynes (Butthole Surfers) and Jeff Ward, an unusual transatlantic pairing for a label of this size. No parent label or corporate structure is on record.

Notes (web)

Naked Brain as a band-run label

Wikipedia confirms that after leaving Virgin Records, Gaye Bykers On Acid set up Naked Brain as their own label to release their final two studio albums in 1990 (Cancer Planet Mission and Pernicious Nonsense). Wikipedia — Gaye Bykers on Acid, as of 2026-11-16. The Mary Byker Wikipedia article corroborates this, stating the band “released a further two albums in 1990 on their own label Naked Brain.” Wikipedia — Mary Byker, as of 2026-11-16.

The Jackofficers transatlantic connection

The Jackofficers released Digital Dump (1990) on two separate labels for the two territories: the US release appeared on Rough Trade US (catalog RUS 100-1 vinyl / RUS 100-2 CD / RUS 100-4 cassette); the UK release appeared on Naked Brain (NBX 003 vinyl / NBXCD 003 CD). Wikipedia — The Jackofficers, as of 2026-11-16; Discogs search result snippets (multiple entries for Discogs release IDs 250346, 250361, 2032875, 401960, 60092).

The split of territories between Rough Trade US and a small UK band-run label strongly suggests a licensing arrangement, but no interview, press article, or label biography found in this research explicitly states the mechanism. The specific personal or business connection that led Gaye Bykers On Acid to license or co-release the Jackofficers record on their own imprint is not documented in any accessible public source checked. Sources consulted without result: Wikipedia (Jackofficers, Gaye Bykers on Acid, Mary Byker, Rough Trade Records), Caught in the Crossfire (Gibby Haynes interview — failed to load), Headpress GREBO! book page (no relevant detail), official Gaye Bykers website (Wix, no readable content), Vinyl Exchange product listing (lists Rough Trade as label against NBX 003 cat#, likely a data entry error or reference to licensor).

The book GREBO!: The Loud and Lousy Story of Gaye Bykers On Acid and Crazyhead (Headpress, 2021) would be the most plausible print source for this question, but its content is not available online. Note: the book is authored by Rich Deakin, not Ian Glasper as sometimes mis-attributed. Headpress product page; AbeBooks listing.

What is confirmed vs. inferred

  • Confirmed: Naked Brain was the Bykers’ own label; Digital Dump UK release is on Naked Brain; US release is on Rough Trade US — these are distinct pressings.
  • Inferred but unsourced: A licensing deal between Rough Trade US and Naked Brain, or a direct band-to-band arrangement, accounts for the UK pressing on a Bykers-owned imprint. No source states this.
