Overview

Lost And Found Records began in January 1988 as a German record label before broadening into a mailorder company specializing in collectible records and CDs. Its catalogue is overwhelmingly concentrated in the 1990s — roughly 180 of its 191 releases — spanning hardcore, punk, thrash, Oi, and grindcore. Output fell sharply after 2000, with only a handful of releases in the following decades. The label runs the sublabel Check Your Head Records and counts Günter Pauler, Gigantor, and Achim Schaffrinna among its associated names.
