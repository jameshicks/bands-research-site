Overview

Human Highway is a Japanese label that released post-rock, experimental, and modern classical music, operating almost entirely in the 2000s — 32 of its 33 known releases fall in that decade, with just one entry in the 2010s. The catalogue centres on Mono, the Tokyo post-rock band, and their guitarist Takaakira Goto; Jeremy DeVine, founder of Temporary Residence Limited, also appears on the roster, pointing to a transatlantic relationship that may explain how Mono’s work circulated in North America during the same period.
