Salsoul Records

New York dance and disco label. Discogs ID: (search needed — main label). Founded 1974–1975 by the Cayre brothers (Ken, Stanley, and Joseph Cayre). Based in New York City.

One of the foundational labels of the disco era. Home of the Salsoul Orchestra (Vincent Montana Jr., arranger/conductor), Loleatta Holloway, First Choice, Double Exposure, Shalamar, and others. Known for early 12-inch singles and extended disco mixes. Closely associated with Sigma Sound Studios (Philadelphia).

Tom Moulton has the highest Discogs credit count on the label — approximately 480 release credits (1975–2022), including mixing, production, engineering at Sigma Sound, and his ongoing role as official Salsoul archivist overseeing digital remastering of the full catalog.

Key label personnel: Vincent Montana Jr. (Salsoul Orchestra arranger/conductor), Arthur Stoppe (Sigma Sound engineer).
