From Discogs

Defunct American division of Play It Again Sam that was run in partnership with Wax Trax! Records during the late 1980s.

Play It Again Sam Records USA releases with a catalog number starting with BIUS are from the period of the partnership with Wax Trax! Records. Play It Again Sam Records USA releases that were released in the US via Caroline Records after the Wax Trax! deal was terminated had the same BIAS catalog numbers as the European version.

This label should not be confused with PIAS America, the label’s current American division. Releases with a PIASA catalog number should go there.

— Discogs label profile, ID 87494

Overview

Play It Again Sam Records USA was the defunct American division of the Belgian label Play It Again Sam, active from 1987 and concentrated in industrial, EBM, and experimental electronic music. Its initial US releases — identifiable by BIUS catalog numbers — were distributed in partnership with Wax Trax! Records during the late 1980s; after that deal ended, distribution shifted to Caroline Records, with releases reverting to the same BIAS numbering as the European parent. The roster featured acts already on the European label: The Legendary Pink Dots, Borghesia, Click Click, Edward Ka-Spel, and Rodney Orpheus. It is a separate entity from PIAS America, the label’s current American operation, which used distinct PIASA catalog numbers.
