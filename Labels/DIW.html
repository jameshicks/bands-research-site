From Discogs

Japanese label belonging to the DIW Products Group. The label was established in 1982. Label Code: LC 16557 The label specializes in releases and reissues of mostly jazz character, from bop to avant garde but also other peripheric fields and other non-jazz related territories, covered by “sister” labels Diwphalanx Records or DIWClassics (classical music). Please do not use DIW Records for the label, keep it only for the record company (copyright, producer, distribution credits, etc…).

— Discogs label profile, ID 42689

Overview

DIW is a Japanese jazz label founded in 1982 under the DIW Products Group (label code LC 16557), covering a broad stylistic range from hard bop and post-bop through free improvisation and avant-garde. Its catalogue of over 300 releases was most productive through the 1980s and 1990s — 132 and 139 releases respectively — reflecting its peak era. The label is flanked by sister imprints: Diwphalanx Records handles peripheral and non-jazz adjacent genres, while DIWClassics covers classical music. DIW also maintains several sublabels of its own, notably an analog reprint series and the striking DIW on METAL imprint. It is distinguished from DIW Records, a separate entity used only for copyright, producer, and distribution credits.
