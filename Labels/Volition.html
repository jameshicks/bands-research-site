Australian label, founded in 1984 by Andrew Penhallow (who also operated GAP Records and Factory Records Australasia). Volition wound down its operations during the mid-1990s (96/98). For legal purposes as copyright indications, please use the company/entity ‘Volition Records’.

By researching Volition’s relationship to its distributors (as shown on the releases), it is possible to ascertain that:

  • From 1984 to 1988 Volition releases were distributed by CBS Records Australia Limited.
  • It’s not clear during 1989 which company distributed Volition releases.
  • From 1990 to approx. mid-1992 Volition releases were either distributed by BMG Arista / Ariola Limited and/or co-released with BMG.
  • From approx. mid-1992 to 1998 Volition releases were exclusively licensed to Sony Music Australia Limited and often co-released with Sony Music.

— Discogs label profile, ID 2131

Overview

Volition was an Australian independent label founded in 1984 by Andrew Penhallow, who simultaneously operated GAP Records and Factory Records Australasia. Its catalogue runs to 72 selected releases across roughly a decade, with the bulk (48 releases) appearing in the 1990s and spanning indie rock, synth-pop, house, and trance — a range that reflects Australian alternative music’s mid-period breadth. Distribution arrangements shifted over the label’s life: CBS Records Australia handled releases through 1988, BMG Arista/Ariola took over from 1990 into early 1992, and Sony Music Australia held an exclusive licensing deal from mid-1992 until Volition wound down around 1996–98. Sub-labels Second Nature and Volition Music operated under its umbrella, and the roster included acts such as Falling Joys, Boxcar, and Suzie Higgie.
