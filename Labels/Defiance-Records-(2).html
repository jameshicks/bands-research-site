Overview

Founded in 1994 by Lars Hoffmann and operating through 2012, Defiance Records (Label Code: LC 11475) was run out of the Underdog record store — a shop that still existed as of 2019. The catalogue centres on the 2000s, when the label released the bulk of its 84+ titles, spanning indie rock, emo, alternative rock, hardcore, and punk. Its roster included Rocky Votolato, Portugal. The Man (John Baldwin Gourley), and Casey Foubert, placing it at the heart of the Pacific Northwest indie underground during that decade.

Notes (web)

The Underdog Recordstore and label structure

Defiance Records was not a standalone label but one leg of an integrated operation run by Lars “Hoffi” Hoffmann in Cologne, Germany. The other legs were the Underdog Recordstore (opened 3 March 1997 at Ritterstrasse, later relocated to a larger space) and concert promotion. Hoffmann himself described the combination as “the right mix — the store, my label Defiance Records, and concert events” [Ox Fanzine interview with Underdog Recordstore, issue #106: https://www.ox-fanzine.de/interview/underdog-recordstore-4582 (in German)]. Defiance was distributed in Germany by Cargo Records [search snippet from Discogs/Cargo distribution records]. Hoffmann also ran a warehouse-sharing arrangement with Green Hell mailorder (Münster) and Burnout (Hamburg) for import purchasing.

Hoffmann was well-networked in the US emo and post-hardcore scene before Portugal. The Man existed as a band: he organised Jimmy Eat World and Hot Water Music’s first European performances in Cologne in September 1999, and later brought International Noise Conspiracy and At the Drive-In to the city [Cologne Tourism cultural feature: https://magazine.cologne-tourism.com/culture/spinning-around-record-stores-in-cologne/].

Defiance Records’ role with Portugal. The Man: European licensing, not primary signing

Multiple sources confirm that Portugal. The Man’s first two albums — Waiter: “You Vultures!” (2006) and Church Mouth (2007) — were released in the US on Fearless Records, not Defiance. The band then moved to their own label Approaching AIRballoons (in partnership with Equal Vision Records) for Censored Colors (2008), The Satanic Satanist (2009), and American Ghetto (2010) [Wikipedia, Portugal. The Man: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal.The_Man, as of 2026-10-27; Wikipedia, Waiter: “You Vultures!”: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waiter:%22You_Vultures!%22, as of 2026-10-27].

Defiance Records’ Discogs credits for the same albums (catalog numbers LXXVI through XCI) represent the German/European licensed pressings, consistent with the label’s established function as a European licensing and distribution partner for American indie, emo, and post-hardcore acts. This is the same pattern Defiance used with bands like Jimmy Eat World, Hot Water Music, and The Get Up Kids — all of whom appear in its catalogue with European pressings of albums their US labels released domestically.

No source found describes a primary signing relationship between Hoffmann/Defiance and Portugal. The Man, or explains how Defiance came to license the band’s catalogue from Fearless and later Approaching AIRballoons. The specific mechanism — who initiated the licensing deal, whether it came through existing relationships (e.g. Cargo distribution, Equal Vision contacts, or Hoffmann’s booking network) — is not documented in any interview, press release, or label history found in this search.
