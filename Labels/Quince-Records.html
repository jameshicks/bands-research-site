Overview

Quince Records is a Japanese independent label founded in 1995, focused on indie pop, shoegaze, and indie rock. Its catalogue spans roughly 73 releases concentrated heavily in the 2000s, with a smaller run in the 1990s and a tapering presence into the 2010s. The roster bridges Japanese and Scandinavian talent: alongside Japanese artists such as Hiromi Araki, the label released work by Swedish indie pop figures Johan Angergård and Club 8, suggesting a licensing or distribution relationship with the Scandinavian indie scene.
