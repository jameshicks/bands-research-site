Dead Oceans

Discogs ID: 94740
Founded: 2007
Origin: Bloomington, Indiana / Los Angeles, USA
Status: Active
Parent: Secretly Group

US independent label (part of Secretly Group alongside Jagjaguwar and Secretly Canadian). Founded by Chris Swanson, Ben Swanson, Darius Van Arman, Jonathan Cargill, and Phil Waldorf (ex-Misra Records). Released Slowdive’s reformation albums: Slowdive (2017, DOC132) and Everything Is Alive (2023, DOC232).

Also home to: Phoebe Bridgers, Mitski, Japanese Breakfast, Khruangbin, The Tallest Man on Earth, Snail Mail, and others.
