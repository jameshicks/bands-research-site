Optimo Music

Glasgow-based record label founded in 2009 by JD Twitch (Keith McIvor) and Jonnie Wilkes. Discogs label ID: 140001.

Grows out of the Optimo (Espacio) DJ night, which McIvor and Wilkes ran at the Sub Club, Glasgow from 1997 — one of the most influential and long-running club nights in the UK, known for collapsing genre barriers between techno, post-punk, disco, no wave, and leftfield electronics. The label name itself honours Liquid Liquid’s track “Optimo” (from the Optimo EP, 99 Records, 1983).

Overview

Optimo Music launched in 2009 with OM 001, Big Ned’s “Bad Angel” 12”. The label’s catalog is deliberately eclectic, spanning post-punk, experimental electronics, dub, dance floor, noise, and archival reissues — matching the ethos of the night. The label is based in Glasgow and releases almost exclusively on vinyl.

McIvor is the label boss (Wilkes is less directly involved as a label operator). JD Twitch also runs or part-owns several associated imprints under the Optimo umbrella (see Sublabels). He has been prolific as a compiler and DJ-mixer for other labels (Strut, Domino, R&S, Glasgow Underground) while running Optimo Music.

JD Twitch died on 19 September 2025 in Glasgow from terminal brain cancer. He was one of the most respected DJs in UK club music.

The DJ Night: Optimo (Espacio)

The Optimo night began in 1997 at Sub Club, Glasgow. McIvor and Wilkes ran it jointly as Optimo (2), also known as Optimo (Espacio). Their approach — playing post-punk alongside techno, African music alongside industrial noise — was celebrated for its lack of genre orthodoxy. The night ran until around 2010 (the duo formally separated their DJ careers after that). A 25th anniversary double compilation, We Love Your Ears (Above Board Projects, 2024), was released close to the end.

Key Figures

JD Twitch (Keith McIvor, 1966–2025)

Discogs artist ID: 315160 (also 166882). Born in Glasgow. Began DJing as “Twitch” running the Pure techno night in Edinburgh from 1990, one of the UK’s foundational techno nights. Adopted the “JD” prefix in the late 1990s with the Optimo nights. Ran Pi Recordings and T&B Vinyl before founding Optimo Music in 2009. Known for an exceptionally wide-ranging musical knowledge and for championing obscure post-punk, no wave, and world music alongside electronic music. Also ran Autonomous Africa and had involvement with Selva Discos (2017–2019). Aliases include Abel Paz (2), Kelvin Bridge, Betty Botox, This Is Belgium, Tomorrow The Rain Will Fall Upwards.

Discogs credits as compiler/mixer beyond Optimo Music include:

  • Zevolution Serie #1 (ZE Records, 2003) — ZE Records retrospective
  • How To Kill The DJ Part Two (Kill The DJ Records, 2004) — as Optimo (Espacio)
  • Sleepwalk (Domino, 2008)
  • In Order To Edit (R&S Records, 2009)
  • The Underground Sound Of Glasgow (Glasgow Underground, 2013)
  • [Cease & Desist] DIY! (Optimo Music, 2015) — post-punk compilation 1978–82
  • Kreaturen Der Nacht (Strut, 2018) — German post-punk 1980–85
  • Polyphonic Cosmos: Sonic Innovations In Japan 1980–1986 (Cease & Desist (2), 2022)

He also produced for Factory Floor (Mirror Mirror, Domino, 2011) and Warp artists.

Died 19 September 2025, Glasgow.

Jonnie Wilkes

Discogs artist ID: 474813. Real name Jonathan G. Wilkes. Born in Belfast, based in Glasgow. DJ and producer; co-founder of the Optimo (Espacio) night with Keith McIvor. Less prolific on Discogs than McIvor as a label operator, but co-ran Optimo (2) and produced under the alias Naum, and as part of Naum Gabo (which released on Optimo Music, 2011). Also a member of Vodershow alongside McIvor. Continued DJing after the Optimo night ended; releases on Feel My Bicep, International Feel, and The Vinyl Factory.

Key Roster Artists

  • Golden Teacher — Glasgow electronic/post-punk collective (Cassie Ojay, Sam Bellacosa, Oliver Pitt, et al.). The label’s most prominent homegrown act. Four EPs 2013–2015, including Bells From The Deep End (OM 18, 2013) and a collaboration with Dennis Bovell (Golden Teacher Meets Dennis ‘Dubmaster’ Bovell At The Green Door, OM 026, 2015). Discogs artist ID: 3108074.
  • Chris Carter (2) — Throbbing Gristle member. Two releases: The Spaces Between (OM CC01, 2010) and Moonlight (OM014, 2011). First vinyl since TG era for Carter.
  • Peter Zummo — New York avant-jazz trombonist associated with Arthur Russell. Zummo With An X (OM Zummo 01, 2012) reissue.
  • The Golden Filter — electronic duo. Multiple releases 2015–2018 including three LPs.
  • Factory Floor — London post-industrial trio. ~(Real Love) (OM013, 2011) — early release, before their DFA period.
  • Coil — A Cold Cell In Bangkok (OM 38, 2017). Archival unreleased material.
  • Robert Rental — post-punk electronic artist. Different Voices For You Different Colours For Me Demos 1980 (OM RENTAL 01, 2018); Paralysis reissue (2020).
  • Dennis Bovell — dub producer. Appears on Golden Teacher collaboration and other catalog.
  • Daniel Avery — early release Magnetic (OM 17, 2012) with The Deadstock 33’s.
  • Sex Judas — Norwegian group; six releases across Optimo Music including collaborations with Ricky (43).
  • Naum Gabo — Glasgow project (includes Jonnie Wilkes). Songs From A Great City (OM012, 2011).
  • Tornado Wallace — Australian producer. Midnight Mania (OMLP17D, 2020).
  • Bergsonist — producer. Middle Ouest (OMLP16D, 2020).
  • O Yuki Conjugate — Sheffield ambient/world group. Sunchemical (OM 46, 2021).
  • De Ambassade (2) — Rotterdam group. The Fool. (OM LP 25, 2023).
  • GǼG — two Anarcho Disco volumes in 2024.

Archival / Compilation Focus

A recurring thread in the catalog is archival reissues and compilations championing overlooked or obscure music:

  • [Cease & Desist] DIY! (2015) — post-punk cult classics 1978–82
  • Miracle Steps: Music From The Fourth World 1983–2017 (OM LP 09, 2017) — a comp JD Twitch described as a personal obsession
  • Kreaturen Der Nacht (STRUT196CD, 2018) — German post-punk subculture 1980–85 (on Strut, co-presented by JD Twitch)
  • Robert Rental archival demos (OM RENTAL 01, 2018)
  • Polyphonic Cosmos: Sonic Innovations In Japan 1980–1986 (Cease & Desist (2), 2022)
  • JD Twitch OMT series (2021) — eight cassette/digital mix tapes spanning dub, goth, cosmos, Caribbean music

South American Series (OMSD)

An unusual subset of the catalog focuses on Brazilian and South American artists:

  • Maria Rita (4), Brasileira (OMSD 001, 2017)
  • Barbatuques, Baianá (OMSD004, 2018)
  • Fernando Falcão, Memória Das Águas and Barracas Barrocas (OMSD 006/007, 2019)

Open Questions

  • The Optimo DJ night’s exact end date (circa 2010–2012) is not confirmed in Discogs data; web sources would clarify.
  • Jonnie Wilkes’s Discogs footprint as a label operator is sparse — his role at Optimo Music vs. just the night is unclear from DB alone.
  • Several OMSD (South American) artists are thinly documented on Discogs.
  • The “OM Zummo” catalog series suggests a planned Peter Zummo sub-series that didn’t continue beyond one release — context unclear.
  • Pi Recordings and T&B Vinyl (McIvor’s earlier labels) are not yet researched.
