Mr. Lady

US independent label focused on feminist/queer indie and electronic music. Durham, North Carolina. Active c. 1997–2002. Discogs label ID: 9581.

Overview

Mr. Lady (sometimes credited “Mr. Lady Records and Videos”) was based in Durham, NC (P.O. Box 3189, Durham NC 27715). It was the primary US home for Le Tigre during their first two studio albums and EP. The label also released music by The Butchies, Electrelane, Kaia, Sarah Dougher, Tami Hart, Tara Jane O’Neil, and others. The catalog runs approximately MRLR-01 to MRLR-27, with entries spanning 1997–2002; the label appears to have wound down around 2002.
