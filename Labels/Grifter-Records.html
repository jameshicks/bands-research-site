Grifter Records

Discogs ID: 70150 Address: 350 Bay Street, Suite 100, PMB 279, San Francisco, CA 94133

San Francisco independent record label founded in 1984 by Tom Mallon. Operated through at least 1988 (the California LP). Mallon — studio owner, engineer, producer, and multi-instrumentalist — ran the label as a vehicle for his own production work out of the Bay Area underground scene.

The label’s primary roster was American Music Club and Flying Color. In practice, many releases were co-credited with Frontier Records (a Los Angeles indie), which appears to have handled pressing and distribution; Frontier catalog numbers (FLP-xxxx / FCX-xxxx) appear on the Flying Color LP and the AMC California cassette alongside Grifter’s imprint. The Engine UK edition was licensed to Zippo Records (cat. ZONG 020), a sublabel of Demon Records.

Tom Mallon produced every release on the label and also played on most of them (guitar and backing vocals on Engine; drums on California). He passed away on 9 January 2014 after a brain cancer diagnosis in 2012.

Connections

  • Tom Mallon — founder and sole producer on the label; also a member of AMC (1986–1989) and later Toiling Midgets and Negative Trend
  • American Music Club — flagship artist; first three albums all produced by Mallon (the third, United Kingdom 1989, moved to Demon Records)
  • Frontier Records — Los Angeles indie that co-released Flying Color and California; Frontier catalog numbers appear on Grifter pressings, suggesting a distribution arrangement
  • Zippo Records — UK sublabel of Demon Records that handled the UK Engine pressing (ZONG 020)
  • Demon Records — UK label that later signed AMC directly (California UK on Demon; United Kingdom 1989 on Demon)

Open Questions

  • Exact cessation date — last original release was California 1988; CD reissues appear on Grifter through at least 1991, suggesting the imprint persisted on paper beyond the LP era
  • Tom Mallon’s own studio name (Discogs profile mentions he “built two studios” — the Grifter Records address may correspond to one of them; web needed to confirm)
  • Full story of the Frontier Records distribution deal — how formalized it was and whether Frontier initiated the connection
