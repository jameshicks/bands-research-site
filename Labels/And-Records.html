Overview

And Records is a Japan-based independent label operating under the parent imprint Youth Inc. Founded in 2003, its catalogue centres on indie rock and alternative rock, with touches of downtempo, post-rock, and folk rock — most output came in the 2000s (34 releases) with the label continuing into the 2010s (19 releases). The roster includes James Baluyut and +/- (Plus / Minus) alongside Chris Deaner, Marc Bianchi, and Patrick Ramos, suggesting a tight network of US-linked indie artists released through a Japanese outlet.
