Overview

Insane Music is a Belgian experimental cassette label founded in 1981 and operated by Alain Neffe. Its catalogue concentrates overwhelmingly in the 1980s — 27 releases across Industrial, Minimal, Ambient, and New Wave — with a small cluster of further output in the 2010s. The label served as the primary home for Neffe’s own projects alongside Bene Gesserit, Pseudo Code, Human Flesh, and Daniel Malempré, and split its releases across two sublabels: Insane Music For Insane People and Home-Made Music For Home-Made People.
