Overview

Conspiracy International is the self-managed imprint founded by Chris Carter and Cosey Fanni Tutti — the former Throbbing Gristle duo operating as Chris & Cosey — in 1982, serving as the primary release vehicle for their industrial, ambient, and synth-pop output. The label concentrated its densest activity in the 1990s (16 releases) after a quieter 1980s start (9 releases), then tapered through the 2000s and 2010s while remaining sporadically active into the 2020s. Its roster is effectively a family catalogue: beyond the founding Chris & Cosey partnership it covers their associated projects CTI and Carter Tutti, making it the definitive single-label home for the full Carter/Tutti body of work across five decades.
