Overview

Gilgongo Records is an independent experimental and noise label founded in Tempe, Arizona in late 2004, owned and operated solely by James Fella. Its catalogue spans the 2000s through the 2020s — with 14 releases in the 2000s, 28 in the 2010s, and 8 in the 2020s — covering noise, experimental, post-punk, and no wave. The label operates through four sublabels (Alien Summer, C-Salt, Deliberate Indifference, and Weird Machine) and has released work by artists including John Wiese, Sissy Spacek, and Soft Shoulder.
