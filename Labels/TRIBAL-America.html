TRIBAL America

US record label. Sub-imprint of I.R.S. Records, run by Rob Di Stefano. Issued Guerilla Records titles in the United States between 1991 and 1994 under a distribution deal.

When William Orbit launched Guerilla Records in 1990, I.R.S. struck a US distribution deal (citing their prior relationship with Orbit as an artist). Di Stefano managed which Guerilla titles were released in the US through Tribal America. By 1994, when the deal came up for renewal, Di Stefano declined (partly due to high licensing fees), precipitating Guerilla’s financial difficulties and eventual closure.

US Guerilla catalog numbers under this arrangement used “Y-” prefixes (e.g., Y-13875, Y-13899, Y-13904) and “US-GRRR” series.

Connections

  • Rob Di Stefano — ran the label
