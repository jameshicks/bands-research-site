Overview

Miasmah is the physical-release arm of the Miasmah Recordings imprint, founded in early 2006 by Norwegian musician and graphic designer Erik Skodvin (born 1979, Langesund, Norway; based in Berlin since some point after Oslo). Skodvin runs the label alongside his work as a recording artist under his own name and as Svarte Greiner, and as one half of ambient duo Deaf Center (with Otto A. Totland). He is also the trio’s designer: Discogs credits him with artwork and design on over 85 releases in the label’s catalog.

Miasmah Music, a free MP3 download label launched in 1999, predates the physical imprint and is tracked separately by Discogs as a sublabel. The physical Miasmah label began releasing CDs in 2006 and shifted increasingly to vinyl LP format from around 2010. The catalog spans ambient, drone, dark experimental, electroacoustic composition, and austere chamber music. Most releases come out of Norway, Germany, and Belgium, reflecting Skodvin’s own base in Berlin and the label’s network of European experimental musicians.

Three engineers share mastering credits heavily across the catalog: Nils Frahm (27 releases), Loop-o (27 releases), and Andreas Tilliander (22 releases).

Label Structure

Miasmah sits under the parent imprint Miasmah Recordings (Discogs ID: 165219), which has three sublabels:

  • Miasmah — the physical LP/CD imprint (this label)
  • Miasmah Design — Skodvin’s graphic design arm
  • Miasmah Music — the original free MP3 label (est. 1999)

Erik K Skodvin

Discogs IDs: Erik Skodvin (as artist): 607835 | Svarte Greiner: 216297
Real name: Erik Knive Skodvin (born 1979, Langesund, Norway; based in Berlin)

Skodvin operates under multiple aliases across the catalog:

  • Svarte Greiner — his primary solo recording project (drone ambient); 22 releases on Miasmah
  • Erik Skodvin — credited on Schächten (MIALP054, 2022) as a solo LP artist; also credited on releases as musician/performer
  • Xhale — early alias used on the Miasmah Music MP3 imprint (1999–2004)

He is a member of:

  • Deaf Center (with Otto A. Totland)
  • B-B-S- (with Aidan Baker and Andrea Belfi)
  • Miasmah Quartet
  • Norwegian Noise Orchestra
  • SGAR

As graphic designer, Skodvin holds design/artwork credits on 85 releases across the Miasmah catalog, making him the dominant visual voice of the label.
