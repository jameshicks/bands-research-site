Burger Records

Discogs ID: 114019 Location: Fullerton, California, US Founded: c. 2007

Independent cassette and vinyl label based in Fullerton, California. One of the defining labels of the 2010s US lo-fi / garage-rock revival. Operates a physical record store and puts out a high volume of cassette and vinyl releases annually. Known for supporting artists like Gap Dream, White Fence, Wavves, King Tuff, Part Time, and hundreds of others.

Overview

Burger Records built its identity around affordable cassette releases before expanding to vinyl. The label’s catalog runs into the thousands of releases and includes a large number of compilation tapes documenting the broader scene. Gap Dream’s primary catalog — from the 2012 self-titled debut cassette through This Is Gap Dream (2016) — was released on Burger.
