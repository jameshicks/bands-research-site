Acute Records

US independent reissue label based in New York. Discogs label ID: 14239. Active early 2000s. Specialised in archival releases of obscure/overlooked post-punk, no wave, and international punk from the late 1970s–early 1980s.

Overview

Acute Records (cat prefix ACT) released Anarchy In Paris! (2003, ACT003) by Métal Urbain — a 24-track CD retrospective covering the band’s original singles and album tracks alongside Metal Boys material. The release was coordinated by David Fakrikian and remastered by Eric Débris and Sean Cotter.
