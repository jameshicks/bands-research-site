Overview

Times Music is an Indian record label established in 1993 as a division of Bennett, Coleman & Co. Ltd., the media conglomerate behind the Times of India. Its catalogue of over 200 releases centres on Hindustani and Indian classical music, Bollywood soundtracks, Bhangra, and devotional recordings, with output peaking sharply in the 2000s before tapering after 2010. Sublabels Times Music Spiritual and World Beat reflect its range from sacred music to international sounds. The roster includes leading classical instrumentalists — flautists Hariprasad Chaurasia, Rakesh Chaurasia, and Ronu Majumdar, and vocalist Bhimsen Joshi — giving the label a particular strength in north Indian classical performance.
