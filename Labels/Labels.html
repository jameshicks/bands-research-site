Overview

Labels was a French independent distribution label founded in 1990 by Virgin France S.A. and operated by Alain Artaud, with a mandate to handle French distribution for a roster of indie-adjacent imprints — Mute/Blast First, 4AD, Beggars Banquet, Creation Records, City Slang, Domino, and Soma Quality Recordings among them — while also signing artists directly, including Daft Punk, Yann Tiersen, and Dominique A. Its active catalogue falls almost entirely within the 1990s, ranging across indie rock, house, synth-pop, downtempo, and alternative rock — a breadth that reflects its aggregator role rather than a single editorial identity. After Virgin France S.A. merged with EMI Music France in January 2002 the label became an EMI division, and it expanded into a European network with regional arms in Italy, Benelux, Germany, Spain, and Greece before going defunct in June 2008.
