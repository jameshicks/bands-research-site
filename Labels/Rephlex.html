Catalog number Rephlex Shop 1 is an envelope with release sticker, catalog number, and Rephlex stamp on backside from 1997. Overview

Rephlex was an independent UK label co-founded in 1991 by Richard D. James (Aphex Twin) and Grant Wilson-Claridge, starting out in Cornwall before relocating to London. The catalogue is anchored in IDM, Electro, Acid and Experimental Techno, with core roster acts including Aphex Twin, DMX Krew, and Cylob. Activity peaked across two decades — 98 releases in the 1990s and 103 in the 2000s — before tailing off sharply to 13 in the 2010s; the label closed in 2014. Rephlex also operated several sublabels, among them AP Musik, Audiovisual Designs, Electric Dance Music, Fire For Effect, and Men Records.
