Overview

Casa del Puente Discos is an independent label founded in 2002 and operating between Mar del Plata and Buenos Aires, Argentina. It has assembled an eclectic catalogue spanning shoegaze, indie rock, ambient, and IDM — a mix of releases by local Argentine artists and international licenses secured for the South American market. The catalogue’s centre of gravity is the 2010s, which accounts for the large majority of its roughly 53 releases, with modest activity in both the 2000s and early 2020s. Its roster is an unusually wide-ranging one for an Argentine indie label, running from local acts such as Altocamet and Emisor to UK post-rock figure Graham Sutton.
