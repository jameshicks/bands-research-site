Overview

Big Love Records is an independent record shop and label co-founded by Masashi Naka and Haruka Hirata, operating out of Harajuku, Tokyo. Active since 2009, its catalogue is concentrated in the 2010s (38 releases) and continuing into the 2020s (21 releases), spanning synth-pop, lo-fi, post-punk, and experimental indie rock. The label’s roster mixes domestic Japanese artists — Nana Yamato and Sapphire Slows among them — with notable international acts including Copenhagen post-punk band Iceage, Los Angeles artist Nite Jewel, and Canadian musician Alex Zhang Hungtai, positioning it as a curatorial bridge between Tokyo’s independent scene and the wider underground.
