Overview

Vertical Form was a London-based label releasing IDM, electronica, experimental, and dub music from 1999 until late 2004. Its catalogue of 37 releases concentrated almost entirely in the 2000s and leaned toward minimal, abstract, and ambient electronics. The roster mixed London-rooted acts such as Dylan Kendle and Corker/Conboy with notable Canadian artists Sixtoo and mcenroe, suggesting cross-Atlantic connections that were unusual for a small experimental imprint of that era.

Notes (web)

Canadian signings — Sixtoo and mcenroe

No source found that names a specific person, booking relationship, or intermediary who brokered either signing. The following is what the web record does and does not establish.

Sixtoo: Wikipedia states that “In 2000, Sixtoo moved to Montreal where he signed on with UK-based record label Vertical Form” (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sixtoo, as of 2026-11-04). The timing coincides with his relocation from Halifax to Montreal and his creative shift from MC to sampler-based work. His earlier catalogue (the “Psyche” records, 1996–2002) was later compiled on Vertical Form as Almost A Dot On The Map (VFORM032CD, 2004), suggesting the label had retrospective interest in his pre-signing work. No interview or press piece examined (R4NT Magazine 2004 interview, Exclaim!, Fakeforreal.net review) explains how the connection arose.

mcenroe: Released Working In The Factory (VFORM036, 2004) and 5 Years In The Factory (VFORM038CD, 2004) on Vertical Form. mcenroe founded and ran Peanuts and Corn Records in Winnipeg/Vancouver and was already releasing independently in Canada. Both his Vertical Form releases appear late in the label’s run (its final year), suggesting he was added to the roster after Sixtoo was already established there. No source establishes who made the introduction.

Shared context: Sixtoo and mcenroe were both part of the same early-2000s Canadian experimental/abstract hip-hop scene (along with Buck 65, Pip Skid, and others in the Peanuts & Corn orbit). It is plausible that Sixtoo’s signing opened the door for mcenroe — the label may have pursued mcenroe through its existing Canadian connection — but no source states this directly.
