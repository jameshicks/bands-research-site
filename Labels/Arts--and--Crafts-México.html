Overview

Arts & Crafts México is the Mexican arm of Arts & Crafts International, launched in 2009 and operating under the entity Producciones Arts & Crafts, S.A. de C.V. The label is also known as Arts & Crafts MX. Its catalogue of around 86 releases is concentrated in the 2010s and leans toward indie rock, alternative rock, and indie pop. The roster mixes Mexican artists such as Torreblanca with international names licensed for the Mexican market — including Kurt Vile, No Age, and Moby — reflecting the label’s dual role as both a domestic imprint and a regional distributor for the broader Arts & Crafts International group.
