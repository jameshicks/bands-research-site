Overview

Xtra Mile Recordings is an independent label based in London, founded by Charlie Caplowe in 2003 as a subsidiary of Xtra Mile Recordings Ltd. It operates across indie rock, folk rock, punk, and alternative rock, and is most closely associated with Frank Turner, whose output accounts for a substantial share of the catalogue. The label built its strongest period in the 2010s (133 releases), following a productive opening decade (61 releases), and has continued releasing into the 2020s (31 releases). It also runs a sublabel, Xtra Mile High Club, and its active roster includes Turner alongside Matt Nasir, Ben Lloyd, Frank Arkwright, and Tarrant Anderson.
