Texas Hotel Records

Independent American record label. Discogs label ID: 38950

Active approximately mid-1980s to mid-1990s. Originally based in California (offices at various points in Santa Monica and Davis, CA), though closely associated with the Athens, Georgia music scene. The label’s most significant legacy is the early catalog of Vic Chesnutt, but its actual roster was broader and more eclectic — spanning post-hardcore spoken word (Henry Rollins), indie rock (Rollins Band, Poi Dog Pondering), and Athens-adjacent outfits. European releases were co-distributed through Caroline Records and Rough Trade.

No parent label or sublabels on Discogs.

History and Identity

Texas Hotel launched in 1986 with the self-titled debut EP by Los Angeles art-pop group Downy Mildew (catalog number TEXAS HOTEL 1). Within a year the label had signed Henry Rollins for his solo debut Hot Animal Machine (1987, cat. TEXAS HOTEL 2) and released the Drive By Shooting EP by Henrietta Collins And The Wifebeating Childhaters — the pre-Rollins Band project featuring Rollins, guitarist Chris Haskett, and the lineup that would become the core Rollins Band. The label’s catalog numbers were initially informal (“Texas Hotel 2,” “Texas Hotel 3,” etc.) before settling on a “TXH.” prefix by 1988.

The Rollins connection was formative. Texas Hotel released his entire early solo catalog (Hot Animal Machine, the spoken word record Big Ugly Mouth, and the combined Hot Animal Machine… Plus), then became the label of record for the Rollins Band’s first three studio albums — Do It (1988, produced by Ian MacKaye), Life Time (1988, also MacKaye), and Hard Volume (1989) — as well as the spoken word document Sweatbox (1989). Ian MacKaye of Fugazi and Dischord Records produced the two Rollins Band studio albums; his name is the label’s most notable production credit from that era.

From 1988 onward Texas Hotel also released eclectic Athens-adjacent material: the Hawaiian/folk/world hybrid Poi Dog Pondering’s first two LPs (Poi Dog Pondering, 1988; Circle Around The Sun, 1989), the post-punk Athens band Hetch Hetchy (2) (Make Djibouti, 1988; Swollen, 1990 — the latter produced by Jimmy Sommers and recorded by Paul Q. Kolderie and Sean Slade), the blues-folk duo Chickasaw Mudd Puppies (White Dirt, 1990), and the short-lived Chicago art-pop act Baby Flamehead (Life Sandwich, 1990).

The label’s production history also includes Michael Stipe producing Make Djibouti for Hetch Hetchy (2) (1988) and White Dirt for Chickasaw Mudd Puppies (1990) — both before his most famous Texas Hotel credits with Vic Chesnutt.

In 1990 Vic Chesnutt debuted on the label with Little, produced by Stipe. Three more Chesnutt albums followed: West of Rome (1992, Stipe producing again), Drunk (1993/1994, self-produced with Caz Mack), and Is The Actor Happy? (1995, produced by John Keane). These four records are now the best-remembered part of the Texas Hotel catalog, in part because of Stipe’s involvement and in part because of Chesnutt’s subsequent reputation.

By the mid-1990s the label wound down. Chesnutt moved to Capitol/Caroline and then Rough Trade; the Rollins Band had already moved to Imago/Dreamworks by the early 1990s; Poi Dog Pondering had gone to Columbia. The last significant Texas Hotel catalog number for Chesnutt is TEX 24 (Is The Actor Happy?, 1995) and a 1995 European single (Key Of F, TXH0233).

Vic Chesnutt — The Texas Hotel Albums

The four Chesnutt albums are the label’s most enduring releases. All were recorded in Athens, Georgia.

Little (1990)

Cat. TXH 20 / 20 CD. Produced by Michael Stipe; recorded by Stipe and John Keane. Chesnutt plays guitar and sings, accompanied only by his wife Tina Chesnutt on some tracks. Backing vocals by Lynda Limner. Back cover painting by Chesnutt himself; front cover photo by J. T. Chesnutt. Discogs master: 183249.

Drunk (1993 US / 1994 Europe)

Cat. TEX 22. First album not produced by Stipe — Chesnutt co-produced with Caz Mack. Recorded at multiple studios; engineers include Don Zientara, Peter Fancher, Rick Fowler, Scott Stuckey. Chesnutt drew the cover artwork himself. Discogs master: 183252.

Is The Actor Happy? (1995)

Cat. Texas Hotel 24 / TEX 24 (US); TXH0231/TXH0232 (Europe). Produced by John Keane, who also plays guitar, pedal steel, and 12-string. The first Chesnutt album not produced by Stipe, signaling a new phase. Photography on the European edition includes Polaroids by filmmaker Jem Cohen and a photo by Shirley Manson (of Garbage). Discogs master: 173504.

Rollins Band — The Texas Hotel Albums

Do It (1988)

Cat. TXH.13. Produced by Ian MacKaye. Classic early Rollins Band lineup: Chris Haskett (guitar), Andrew Weiss (bass), Sim Cain (drums). Live sound by Theo Van Eenbergen. Cover photos by Joe Cole (2). Discogs master: 43176.

Life Time (1988)

Cat. TXH.8. Also produced by Ian MacKaye; engineered and mixed by Geoff Clout. Same lineup. Cover art by Stephen Myers (2). Photography by Joe Cole (2). Discogs master: 43178.

Hard Volume (1989)

Cat. TEX.18. Produced by Theo Van Rock; engineered and mixed by Rae DiLeo; mastered by Wally Traugott. Discogs master: 43179.

Notable Connections

  • Michael Stipe / R.E.M. — Athens, Georgia nexus; Stipe produced four Texas Hotel releases
  • Ian MacKaye / Dischord Records — produced both key Rollins Band studio LPs
  • John Keane — Athens-based engineer/producer; worked with Chesnutt, Hetch Hetchy, Chickasaw Mudd Puppies; later produced Widespread Panic
  • Don Zientara — Dischord-affiliated engineer (Inner Ear Studio); brought in for Drunk
  • Henrietta Collins And The Wifebeating Childhaters — pre-Rollins Band project; also an alias of Henry Rollins
  • Caroline Records — US co-distribution partner for Chesnutt’s European releases
  • Rough Trade — European co-distribution partner
