In-Poly-Sons

French independent label based in Rennes. Discogs label ID: 65064. Sublabel: Early Times (ID: 388465).

Contact / address (as of Discogs): 34 rue de Chatillon, 35000 Rennes, France — inpolysons@free.fr
Web: http://inpolysons.free.fr/ · https://inpolysons.bandcamp.com/

Overview

In-Poly-Sons positions itself explicitly against commercial interests — the Discogs profile describes it as proposing “an alternative to wholesale music, a different musical story… an uncommercial one. Nostalgic tenderness.” The catalog reflects this: art-brut tributes, Robert Wyatt tribute compilations, percussion solos, toy-instrument records, and chamber-eccentric song cycles. The label is the primary home for Klimperei (16 releases across 2000–2024), and has close ties to the overlapping Rennes/Reims experimental milieu that also spawned Toupidek Limonade and Look De Bouk — both of whose core members (Denis Tagu, Kwettap Ieuw, Jean Caël, Etienne Himalaya) recur throughout the In-Poly-Sons roster as performers, engineers, and producers.

The earliest release in the Discogs catalog is Ubu Et La Merdre (1989), a various-artists compilation, and Rubber Cage (1989) by The Work — the British avant-rock group whose members include Tim Hodgkinson (Henry Cow) and Mick Hobbs. The label was issuing records on both sides of the Channel before settling into its French experimental core in the 2000s.

The label has 68 releases documented in Discogs (including pressings/editions). Unique master titles: at least 11 in the primary catalog plus further releases under the Early Times sublabel.

Key Personnel (recurring across catalog)

Denis Tagu (ID: 544266) is the single most recurrent figure in the label’s catalog — credited on 7 releases as engineer/mixer, and across at least 6 more in other roles (keyboards, percussion, accordion, producer). He is a member of both Toupidek Limonade and Look De Bouk, and the label’s go-to mastering engineer.

Christophe Petchanatz (ID: 594090) — Klimperei’s core member — appears on at least 6 releases in composer/performer/engineer roles beyond simply being the credited primary artist.

Kwettap Ieuw (ID: 544294) appears on at least 4 releases in performer and artwork roles; also a member of both Toupidek Limonade and Look De Bouk.

Pierre Bastien (ID: 53779) appears on 5 releases in various guest/featured roles (mbira, saw, trumpet, painting, words).

Dominique Grimaud (ID: 544904) appears on 3 releases as featured performer (sampler, synthesizer, noises) and on 2 as illustrator/sleeve designer.

Patrick Muller / Patrick Müller (IDs: 316431 / 466372) — recur as mastering engineers across 4 releases each (likely the same individual under two spellings).

Open Questions

  • No founding year or label-head name documented in Discogs. The address (Rennes) and email are listed but no named individual is credited as operator.
  • Who runs In-Poly-Sons? Denis Tagu’s recurrence as engineer and performer suggests deep involvement; Christophe Petchanatz of Klimperei is another candidate.
  • The 1989 releases (Rubber Cage, Ubu Et La Merdre) predate the label’s apparent Rennes/French experimental focus — unclear if the label had different origins/ownership in the late 1980s.
  • MW Pour Robert Wyatt (2001) has no additional credits in Discogs — contributing artists on this tribute compilation unknown from DB alone.
  • Hardis Bruts (Hommage À L’Art Brut) (1992) — the 52 Discogs pressings count is anomalously high; likely a data artifact (multiple format entries) rather than genuinely 52 editions.
  • Patrick Muller (ID: 316431) and Patrick Müller (ID: 466372) appear as separate Discogs entities both credited as mastering engineers across In-Poly-Sons releases — almost certainly the same person.
  • Early Times sublabel content not researched; no releases visible under that ID in the data retrieved.
