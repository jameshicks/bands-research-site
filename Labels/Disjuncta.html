Disjuncta

Discogs ID: 25221 | Active: 1972–1975 (sold late 1975) | Country: France

French independent label founded by Richard Pinhas in 1972, operated out of Paris (56 rue Saint-Louis en l’île, 75004). Widely regarded as the first wholly independent French record label. Issued 18 releases in total (per Discogs), with its most active period concentrated in 1974–1975. In late 1975 Pinhas sold the label to his manager in exchange for a Big Moog synthesizer; the manager renamed it Urus Records (Discogs ID: 25235), though Disjuncta catalog references were often retained on the reissued pressings.

Overview

Pinhas founded the label — formally “Disjuncta Record” — initially to release a 1972 single by his pre-Heldon group Schizo (4). The label operated from Pinhas’s home studio, which gave it unusual self-sufficiency: at the outset they ran their own manual lacquer-cutting and pressing facilities in-house, only moving to outside pressing companies when output volume became too great. Distribution was deliberately non-commercial: records were sold exclusively to independent stores and through mail-order within France. In the UK the label was distributed by TOL (The Other Labels).

The creative peak was 1974–1975, when Disjuncta issued seven albums: three by Heldon (Pinhas’s main vehicle), one by guitarist Alain Renaud (then a Heldon member), one by the Zeuhl-adjacent group ZAO (3), one by Brazilian guitarist Lourival Silvestre, and one by Balinese gamelan ensemble Gede Manik — the last produced by journalist/activist Jean-François Bizot. The gamelan release stands apart from the rest of the catalog and reflects the label’s willingness to issue recordings that had no obvious commercial home elsewhere in France.

A notable recurring figure across the Heldon releases is philosopher Gilles Deleuze, who contributes spoken-word and uncredited vocal performances on at least three releases. This connection reflects Pinhas’s deep engagement with Deleuze’s philosophy, particularly concepts from Anti-Oedipus and A Thousand Plateaus, which informed Heldon’s aesthetic and texts.

Pinhas’s sale of the label to his manager — trading it for a Big Moog — is a well-documented episode that underlines both the financial precariousness of the operation and Pinhas’s priorities as an instrument-driven composer. Under the successor name Urus Records, most Disjuncta releases were reissued, sometimes retaining the original catalog numbers.

Producers of note:

  • Richard Pinhas produced or co-produced four releases on the label
  • “Parano’s Limited” (collective pseudonym) produced Electronique Guerilla
  • “Joe Chip” produced Third — Joe Chip is a character from Philip K. Dick’s Ubik, typical of Heldon’s sci-fi naming practice
  • Jean-François Bizot (journalist, founder of Actuel magazine) produced the Gede Manik gamelan LP

Connections

  • Richard Pinhas — founder and primary artist; aliases on this label include Moogi-Le-Moog
  • Heldon — label’s anchor act; first three Heldon LPs issued here
  • Schizo (4) — pre-Heldon band; label’s inaugural release (1972)
  • Alain Renaud — guitarist; both Heldon member and solo artist on the label

Open Questions

  • The Discogs profile states 18 releases but the catalog table above accounts for 14 unique Discogs entries (some masters with 2 pressings). The discrepancy may reflect additional pressings or releases not yet in the DB, or the “18” figure counting all pressing variants individually.
  • “Parano’s Limited” (producer credit on Electronique Guerilla) and “Joe Chip” (producer on Third) are collective/pseudonymous credits — exact membership of Parano’s Limited is unconfirmed in Discogs.
  • The 1976 Alain Renaud single (catno 103) postdates the claimed 1975 sale to Urus — whether this was issued under Disjuncta or is a Urus release that retained the Disjuncta numbering is unclear.
  • Jean-François Bizot’s involvement with the Gede Manik gamelan LP is a surprising outlier. His connection to Pinhas’s circle (if any) is undocumented in Discogs.
  • TOL (The Other Labels) handled UK distribution — no further Discogs data on this distributor.
