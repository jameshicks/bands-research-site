Black Truffle

Australian experimental music label. Discogs ID: 141293

Founded in 2009 by Oren Ambarchi, Sydney, Australia. Now internationally distributed (USA and Europe).

Overview

Black Truffle began with CD reissues of Ambarchi’s early out-of-print vinyl, then reissued Afternoon Tea (the 2000 Ritornell collaboration with Fennesz, Pimmon, Peter Rehberg, and Keith Rowe). Built rapidly into a significant curatorial imprint spanning new releases from Ambarchi’s collaborators, archival documents, and historic reissues.

By 2025 the label has 130+ releases (205 Discogs entries including pressings). Artwork for all releases is designed by either Stephen O’Malley or Lasse Marhaug.
