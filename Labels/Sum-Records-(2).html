Overview

Sum Records (2) was an Argentinian music label operating across Latin America and Spain through subsidiaries and offices in Mexico, Brazil, Uruguay, Chile, Colombia, and beyond, under parent company Sum Records S.A. De C.V. Its entire documented catalogue falls within the 1990s — 40 releases spanning heavy metal, death metal, alternative rock, and synth-pop — with a roster that included major international acts such as Slipknot, Depeche Mode, and Sepultura, suggesting a regional licensing or distribution role for larger labels rather than a purely independent operation. The network wound down in stages: Argentina went dark in 2001, Chile and Mexico followed in 2004, and the Brazilian operation lasted until 2014. Sublabels CDI Music Ltda., Smash Records (27), and Sum records Chile extended its reach across the region.
