Zickzack

Hamburg-based independent record label, central to the Neue Deutsche Welle underground. Discogs label ID: 9144. Parent label: What’s So Funny About.. GmbH (ID: 845905). Sublabel: Scratch ‘n’ Sniff (ID: 66782). 341 releases in Discogs.

Overview

Zickzack was one of the key Hamburg independent labels of the early 1980s NDW scene, releasing early material by Palais Schaumburg (debut single “Rote Lichter / Macht Mich Glücklich Wie Nie”, ZZ 23, 1981; “Telephon / Kinder Der Tod”, ZZ 33, 1981) as well as compilations and singles from the surrounding Hamburg avant-garde community. The Zickzack catalog also includes material by Timo Blunck (Die Zimmermänner, various), Holger Hiller-related projects, and numerous other NDW and post-punk acts.

Connections

  • Palais Schaumburg — debut singles ZZ 23 and ZZ 33 (1981)
  • Timo Blunck — Die Zimmermänner releases
