Overview

An independent label founded in Manchester in 1988 by Paula Greenwood, Playtime released indie and indie dance records for a cluster of acts closely linked to the Madchester scene — Inspiral Carpets, New Fast Automatic Daffodils, The Rainkings, and Molly Half Head among them. The catalogue built steadily through the late 1980s before accelerating sharply in the 1990s, spanning indie rock, alternative rock, and leftfield styles. The label relocated to London in 1994 and continued operating until 1997, adding new acts such as Elliot Green and TC Hug to its roster after the move.
