Overview

Miasmah Recordings is an audio-visual umbrella company run by Norwegian musician Erik Skodvin from Berlin, housing two distinct arms: the Miasmah record label and Miasmah Design, a graphic design studio. Skodvin launched it in the late 1990s as a deliberate departure from demo-scene conventions, using it to distribute his own music freely — 63 tracks and albums released under Creative Commons via archive.org, catalogued as Miasmah Music. In 2006 the operation expanded into physical formats (CDs and vinyl), with the label developing a catalogue that spans ambient, dark ambient, field recording, and contemporary composition. The artist roster connects Skodvin to collaborators including Joana Guerra, Aaron Moore, Axel Dörner, and Asalif Tewold.
