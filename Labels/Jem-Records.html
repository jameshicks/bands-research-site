Overview

Jem Records takes its name from its three founders — Jeff Tenenbaum, Ed Grossi, and Marty Scott — whose initials form the acronym. The label launched in 1976 and built a modest catalogue through the late 1970s and 1980s spanning power pop, country, psychedelic rock, and bluegrass before closing in 1987 when parent company Jem Records, Inc. folded. Marty Scott reactivated the imprint in the 2010s, and the revived label has since issued a larger body of work than the original run — concentrated in power pop and pop rock — through artists including Nick Piunti and Kurt Reil. Passport Records operates as a sublabel.
