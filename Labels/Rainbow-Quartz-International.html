Overview

Rainbow Quartz International is a U.S. indie label founded by music attorney Jim McGarry, built around guitar-pop and rock with jangly psychedelic and power-pop leanings, alongside cerebral singer-songwriters. The catalogue is heavily concentrated in the 2000s (61 releases), bookended by a modest 1990s run (15 releases) and a small batch in the 2010s (7 releases). Core roster names include Cotton Mather, The Grip Weeds, The Asteroid #4, Robert Harrison, and Matthew Smith, with the label’s 226 total credits spanning indie rock, psychedelic rock, power pop, and garage rock.
