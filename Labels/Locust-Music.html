Locust Music

Discogs ID: 13047 Location: Chicago, Illinois (P.O. Box 220426, Chicago IL 60622) Founded: Late 2000 Run by: Dawson Prater Sublabels: Ikef (ID: 61154); Latitude (ID: 14320); Met Life Location Sound Series (ID: 413489) Release count: ~167 (Discogs)

Chicago-based independent label run by Dawson Prater since late 2000. Home to early-2000s Chicago experimental, folk, and avant-garde releases. The label’s website (locustmusic.com) is no longer active.
