Overview

Very Small Records is a California punk label founded and run by David Hayes, with a catalogue spanning punk, hardcore, pop punk, indie rock, and ska. The label’s main output ran from the late 1980s through the 1990s, with the bulk of its releases concentrated in that decade. In 1994 the label briefly operated as Too Many Records before reverting to the Very Small Records name in 1997.

Sources checked without result:

  • East Bay Punk Digital Archive (eastbaypunkda.com) — 403, inaccessible.
  • Very Small Records Kickstarter (hoodie campaign) — 403, inaccessible.
  • Multiple searches combining “Very Small Records”, “Too Many Records”, “David Hayes”, “1994”, “rename”, “history”, “interview”, “zine” — returned no source that addresses the reason.

A “History of East Bay Punk: An Interview with David Hayes” was documented on Transition Video Magazine (video format), per a Wikipedia footnote, but that video is not indexed or accessible online in a way that search or fetch could reach.

Conclusion: No publicly indexed web source explains the reasoning behind either name change.
