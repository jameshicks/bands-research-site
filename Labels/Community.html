Overview

Community is a German independent label (LC 5149) active from 1993, with its catalogue heavily concentrated in the 1990s — 37 releases in that decade — before tapering to around ten in the 2000s. The roster is anchored by The Notwist and key collaborators from that circle, including Markus Acher, Andreas Gerth, and Mario Thaler, alongside Funky Chris. The catalogue ranges from indie rock and alternative rock into electro and abstract styles, reflecting the eclectic output of the Weilheim-associated artists at its core. It operates under the umbrella of Community Records.
