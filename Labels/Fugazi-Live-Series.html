From Discogs

Between 1987 and 2003, Fugazi played over 1000 concerts in all fifty of the United States, plus all over the world. Over 800 of these shows were recorded by the band’s sound engineers. Most recordings were made from a combination of board mixes and live mic-ing, and over the years the band amassed hundreds of taped shows. Initially (2004 / 2005) thirty of these recordings were transferred onto CD-Rs. The first twenty CDr volumes were outfitted with a uniform front insert style, but had individual concert info and track listings. Volumes 21 to 30 included a different uniform front insert style from volumes 1 to 20. Since December 1st 2011 the Dischord Records web site started offering all of the available Fugazi live recordings as digital downloads (more than 800 performances). The goal of this project is to make each of these recordings available to download for a small fee. A searchable archive lists each show along with available photos, flyers and general show info.

— Discogs label profile, ID 248018

Overview

Fugazi Live Series is a Dischord Records imprint dedicated to archiving the band’s extensive touring history, drawing on over 800 recordings made by their own sound engineers between 1987 and 2003. The project launched in 2004–2005 with thirty CD-R volumes documenting individual concerts, then expanded in December 2011 to a fully digital archive on the Dischord website offering all available recordings as pay-per-download files. The catalogue sits entirely in the 2000s and carries credits for all four members — Brendan Canty, Ian MacKaye, Joe Lally, and Guy Picciotto — alongside Fugazi as a whole.
