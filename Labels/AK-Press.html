AK Press

Worker-run anarchist book publisher and distributor, founded in Edinburgh, Scotland; North American operations based in Oakland, California. Discogs label ID: 54998.

Operates two audio/video sublabels:

  • AK Press Audio (Discogs label ID: 55785) — spoken word and music
  • AK Press Video (Discogs label ID: 56942) — video releases

Overview

AK Press is organized around anarchist principles: all decision-making, including which titles are published and distributed, is made collectively by the workers. Their catalog spans radical political theory, history, fiction, and audio, with a focus on titles from independent presses rather than corporate publishers. Founded in Edinburgh in the late 1980s, the press expanded to the United States and established its primary North American warehouse and office at 674-A 23rd Street, Oakland, CA 94612. The European operation continues as AK Distribution, PO Box 12766, Edinburgh EH8 9YE, Scotland.

The audio catalog (AK Press Audio) began in the early 1990s and is heavily oriented toward spoken word: political lectures, readings, and radio-format essays by figures including Noam Chomsky, Howard Zinn, Mumia Abu-Jamal, and Ward Churchill. AK Press Audio also released music from politically aligned acts including The Ex, Chumbawamba, Utah Phillips, and Jello Biafra.

The label has long-running co-release relationships with Alternative Tentacles (Jello Biafra’s San Francisco label) and with Allied Recordings (3), John Yates’s former Bay Area label. Many releases bear dual AK Press / Alternative Tentacles catalog numbers.

Web: akpress.org / akuk.com

AK Press Audio

Discogs label ID: 55785 | Parent: AK Press (54998)

AK Press Audio is the spoken word and music imprint, active from at least 1994 through the mid-2000s. It carries the catalog prefix AKA (e.g. AKA001CD through AKA047CD and beyond). With 48 releases cataloged on Discogs, it is the main audio output of the AK Press operation.

Key producer

David Barsamian is the single most prominent producer across the AK Press Audio catalog, credited on 17 releases. He runs Alternative Radio, the Boulder, Colorado-based political audio production outfit that records and distributes lectures by Chomsky, Zinn, Edward Said, and others. Barsamian is effectively the production pipeline for the majority of the Chomsky and Zinn spoken word releases.

Design

John Yates (Stealworks) is credited on 8 AK Press Audio releases and 3 AK Press releases — the most consistent design voice across the audio catalog. His credit lines follow his characteristic construction-process language: “CD Package Designed And Built By,” “Design & Built By,” “Design, Graphics [Built By].”

Winston Smith (5) contributed disc art and illustration to 2 AK Press Audio releases.

Key Releases

The Clinton Vision - Old Wine, New Bottles (1994)

AKA 001CD | AK Press | Noam Chomsky | Canada

The label’s first audio release. A Chomsky lecture produced by David Barsamian, designed by John Yates, and mastered at SF Audio. Covers NAFTA, Clinton’s health plan, labor, and corporate power. Spoken word / speech / political format.

Better Read Than Dead: A Benefit For AK Press (1996)

AKA 004 | AK Press Audio / Epitaph | Various Artists | Europe

Co-released with Epitaph (cat: 6474-2). A benefit compilation for the press, mixing punk and politically-aligned acts. Contributors include NOFX, Napalm Death, Propagandhi, Chumbawamba, Tribes Of Neurot, Wayne Kramer (of MC5), Pitchshifter, The Levellers, and Spazz. Genres span punk, grindcore, industrial, drum & bass, ska, and folk.

Less Rock More Talk (1996)

AKA 007CD | AK Press / Alternative Tentacles / Allied Recordings | Various Artists | US

Triple co-release (AK Press / AT VIRUS 254 / Allied No. 87CD). A spoken word compilation featuring Noam Chomsky, Jello Biafra, Penny Rimbaud (of Crass), Michael Gira, James Kelman, and Scottish poet Tom Leonard (2). A key document of the early 1990s politicized spoken word/punk crossover.

1936: The Spanish Revolution (1997)

AKA 010CD | AK Press Audio / Allied Recordings / Ex Records | The Ex | UK, Europe & US

Double EP / CD reissue of The Ex’s Spanish Civil War-themed recordings. Co-released with Ex Records (EX 028D / EX 029D) and Allied Recordings. Produced by Dolf Planteijdt and The Ex. Lineup: Luc Ex (bass, guitar), Terrie Hessels (guitar), Katherina Bornefeld (drums), Dolf Planteijdt (guitar), GW Sok (voice). The release aligns with AK Press’s anarcho-historical publishing interests — the Spanish Revolution and the CNT-FAI are frequent AK Press book topics.

All Things Censored: Volume 1 (1998)

AKA 013CD | AK Press Audio / Alternative Tentacles | Mumia Abu-Jamal | US

Co-released with Alternative Tentacles (VIRUS 221CD). Mumia Abu-Jamal’s death row essays and commentaries, framed with introductory remarks from Cornel West, Howard Zinn, Alice Walker, William Kunstler, Assata Shakur, Dorothy Allison, and others. One of the most notable releases in the AK Press Audio catalog — Mumia had been on death row since 1981 and was a cause célèbre across the anarchist and punk left.

The Return Of The Read Menace (1999)

AKA 014CD | AK Press Audio / Honest Don’s | Various Artists | US

Co-released with Honest Don’s (Fat Mike’s pop-punk sublabel; DON 023-2). Punk covers compilation. Design by John Yates. Contributors include Screeching Weasel, Propagandhi (covering Cro-Mags), J Church, No Use For A Name, Pitchshifter, Hot Water Music, Avail, The Weakerthans, D.O.A., The Levellers, Chumbawamba, and Atom And His Package.

Become The Media (2000)

AKA017CD | AK Press / Alternative Tentacles | Jello Biafra | US

3-CD spoken word set co-released with Alternative Tentacles (VIRUS 260CD). Produced by Biafra himself. Lectures covering the WTO protests (“K.O. The W.T.O”), media concentration (“Hack The Planet”), GMO food, and the 2000 Philadelphia RNC (“Philadelphia Stories”). The title Become The Media anticipates the post-internet media democratization discourse.

A Singsong And A Scrap (2006)

AKA047CD | AK Press / Trade Root Music | Chumbawamba | UK & US

Chumbawamba’s late-period acoustic folk album, co-released with Trade Root Music (ROOT-CD-0003). Self-produced and self-arranged by the band. Guests include Coope Boyes & Simpson (vocals on several tracks), Andy Cutting (melodeon), Ian Telfer (fiddle), and James O’Grady (whistle). Tracklist includes a cover of The Clash’s “Bankrobber” (written by Joe Strummer and Mick Jones) and “Bella Ciao” (traditional, arr. Chumbawamba).

Connections

  • Alternative Tentacles — the most frequent co-release partner; Jello Biafra’s label; shared many spoken word and punk titles (VIRUS catalog numbers alongside AKA)
  • Allied Recordings (3) — John Yates’s Bay Area label; co-released Less Rock More Talk and 1936: The Spanish Revolution
  • Honest Don’s — Fat Mike’s pop-punk sublabel; co-released The Return Of The Read Menace (1999)
  • Epitaph — co-released Better Read Than Dead (1996); European pressing
  • Ex Records — co-released 1936: The Spanish Revolution with The Ex
  • G7 Welcoming Committee Records — Canadian anarcho-punk label; John Yates also designs for them; political/anarchist overlap in catalog ethos
  • PM Press — fellow Oakland-based radical publisher; overlapping roster and political sphere; John Yates designs for both
  • John Yates / Stealworks — house designer across 11+ catalog entries; the visual identity of AK Press Audio is largely his work
  • David Barsamian / Alternative Radio — the production infrastructure behind almost all the Chomsky and Zinn releases
  • Chumbawamba — two releases (1996 benefit comp; 2006 A Singsong And A Scrap); aligned politically as anarcho-punk collective
  • Noam Chomsky — the single most released artist in the AK Press Audio catalog (14 releases on AK Press Audio alone)
