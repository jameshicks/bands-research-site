Overview

Pinkflag is the self-managed label operated by Wire, the British post-punk and art rock group, with its catalogue beginning in 2000. The roster is essentially the band itself — Colin Newman, Graham Lewis, Bruce Gilbert, and Matthew Simms — alongside Wire as a unit. Activity peaks in the 2010s, which account for the majority of its nearly fifty selected releases and reflect Wire’s most sustained period of self-released output since their late-1970s debut. A sublabel, Pink Flag Archive Research, sits beneath it, suggesting a parallel strand dedicated to archival or catalogue work distinct from new recordings.
