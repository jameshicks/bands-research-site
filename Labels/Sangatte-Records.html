Bootleg label which sells unlicensed recordings made by French Radio DJ Bernard Lenoir (Lenoir >>> ‘Le Noir’ means ‘The Black’ in English) for ‘The Black Sessions’ and also other unlicensed recordings by Radio France . Overview

Sangatte Records is a French bootleg label founded on unauthorized recordings captured by Radio France DJ Bernard Lenoir for his long-running “The Black Sessions” concert program, along with other unlicensed Radio France material. Despite being marketed as CDr pressings limited to 2,500 copies, the releases are in fact unlimited CDr duplications. The catalogue is heavily weighted toward the 2000s, with 35 of 45 releases appearing in that decade, tapering off sharply after 2010. Acts on the label include Dominique A., Tindersticks, Weezer, The Boo Radleys, and The Divine Comedy, reflecting the alternative and indie rock programming of Lenoir’s sessions. It operates a sublabel, Elysée Montmartre (2).
