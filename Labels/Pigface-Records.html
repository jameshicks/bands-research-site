Overview

Pigface Records is a small independent label based in Portland, Oregon, operated out of the home/studio address of Smegma member Michael Lastra (aka Dr. Id, aka Smegma Mike). It functions primarily as the in-house imprint for Smegma — the long-running Los Angeles/Portland free-improvisation collective — and for solo and splinter projects by Smegma members and Portland-adjacent artists.

The label launched in 1979 with three Smegma releases and a Ju Suk Reet Meate solo tape, and has issued releases continuously (if sporadically) ever since. While Smegma’s catalog dominates, Pigface has also served as an outlet for related acts: Rancid Vat (early 1980s), Hellcows (1987), and a cluster of Portland experimental artists in the 2010s including The Tenses, Lee Rockey, Guzo, and MSHR.

Ju Suk Reet Meate (real name Eric Stewart) is the most consistently credited non-performer figure — handling recording, editing, mixing, art direction, and cover layout across dozens of releases. Michael Lastra is credited as producer and engineer on multiple releases and his Smegma Studios URL appears on his Discogs artist page. Mike King (3) handles sleeve/artwork design on several early releases.

There is no profile text in the Discogs database for Pigface Records, and no label website is listed.

Notable Releases

Early run (1979–1980): The label opened with three Smegma tapes/records in a single year — Pigface Chant (001), Flashcards (002), and the split Disco Diarrhea (003) — plus Ju Suk Reet Meate’s solo Solo 1978 / 79 (004), making it clear from the start that the label was a direct outlet for Smegma members’ work.

Compilation: Flies Like Holidays (Pig 005, 1982) is the only proper Various Artists compilation in the catalog. Tracklist credits are sparse in Discogs.

Smegma’s Spontaneous Sound (1983, Pigface Records 008): Unusually large cast of 34 credited performers, including Tom Recchion, Wild Man Fischer (credited as “Wild Man Fisher”), Ken Butler, Monty Cantsin, and Richard Meltzer, suggesting this was recorded at an open Smegma event.

Hellcows (1987): Hellcows — lineup of Carl Annala (bass), Mike King (3) (drums), Eric Stotik (guitar), Gerry Ostrem (guitar), Brian Koelling (voice) — released G Spot Crush (012), co-produced by Michael Lastra.

2010s expansion: After a long gap, Pigface resumed heavy activity 2011–2017 with a run of Portland-scene releases: The Tenses (Ju Suk Reet Meate + Oblivia (3) as a duo), MSHR (Birch Cooper + Brenna Murphy), Lee Rockey (three solo records), Guzo, and Arttu Partinen (Finnish musician, one split appearance).

2022: Infringements by Smegma pressed in Italy (cat# 033), the most recent Discogs-listed release.

Label Context

Pigface Records sits within the orbit of the Los Angeles Free Music Society (LAFMS), the loose collective from which Smegma emerged in the 1970s. The label shares aesthetic territory with other micro-imprints run by LAFMS-adjacent artists. No distribution relationship is documented; the label appears to have operated purely through direct sale and mail order.
