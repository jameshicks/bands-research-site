Overview

Friendship Fever is a Sacramento, California record label co-founded by Chris Watson (22) and his wife Sabrina Watson. Its catalogue is entirely rooted in the 2010s, with seven releases since 2016 spanning indie rock, lo-fi, garage rock, and indie pop. The label’s most prominent act is Grandaddy and frontman Jason Lytle, with the roster also including Aaron Burtch and Geoff Barnett.
