Astro Lab Recordings

Discogs ID: 44271 Founded by: Laurent Pastor Contact: laurent.pastor@gmail.com Country: France (some releases pressed in Germany and UK) Active: 2005–2020 (as documented on Discogs) Sublabels: Treasure Hunting (compilation series imprint, Discogs ID: 1002333) Catalog prefix: ALR (vinyl), ALRCD (CD), ASTROUSCD (US comp)

Overview

Astro Lab Recordings is a French independent electronic label founded and run by Laurent Pastor, who is also a member of La Decadanse (2) and Mapa (5). Despite a relatively compact discography of around 38 releases across 15 years, the label operated as a curatorial hub for a wide range of European electronic music — spanning Parisian edit culture, Detroit-influenced techno, post-punk electronics, and dark synth-driven sounds.

The label’s earliest years (2005–2006) centered on the Cookie & Brownie EP series (ALR001–004), French-market various-artists compilations likely tied to a DJ/party night. From 2007 onward it brought in artists with wider profiles: Parisian edit cult figure Pilooski for three releases, Portuguese producer Photonz, Detroit veteran Anthony Shakir, and UK producer Tim Fairplay. The label’s later phase (2013–2020) shifted toward darker territory with ongoing artists Fkclub and Vox Low, alongside the Treasure Hunting compilation series.

Astro Lab is notable in Tim Fairplay’s biography as the home of his first two solo EPs (2011–2013), released under his sleeve name “Timothy J Fairplay.”

Roster

Key artists

  • Pilooski (Discogs ID: 140130) — French edit and disco-influenced producer; 3 releases (2007–2008), the label’s first recurring artist after the VA series. Real name unreported on Discogs.
  • Tim Fairplay (Discogs ID: 659308) — UK producer. 4 releases on ALR (2011–2013), including his first two solo EPs. Credited throughout as “Timothy J Fairplay.” Mixed on ALR014 with Steve Boardman.
  • Fkclub (Discogs ID: 3377441) — French electronic act; 4 releases (2015–2020), the label’s last recurring artist. Live band format: Marc Lapeyre (voice, guitar, effects) and Antoine Germain (drums).
  • Ana Helder (Discogs ID: 2110743) — 2 releases (2012). Engineered by Bruno Rey.
  • Hardway Brothers (Discogs ID: 1118413) — 2 releases (2011).

Treasure Hunting Series

The Treasure Hunting compilation series was issued under the sublabel Treasure Hunting (Discogs ID: 1002333), described as “Series of compilations released by Astro Lab Recordings.” The series ran across both vinyl 12”s and CDs:

  • Treasure Hunting (ALRCD01, 2013) — CD, compiled by Laurent Pastor
  • Treasure Hunting Vol. 2 (ALRCD002/ALRCD02, 2013) — CD, compiled by Laurent Pastor
  • Treasure Hunting Vol. 3 (ALR029, 2016) — 12” split, Autarkic / Vox Low
  • Treasure Hunting Vol.3/7 (ALR31, 2017) — 12”, Maestro (23) / Uncle O
  • Treasure Hunting Vol.3 (no cat#, 2017) — Fkclub / Red Axes / הילה רוח
  • Treasure Hunting Vol.3 (no cat#, undated) — Fantastic Twins / La Decadanse (2) / Yan Wagner

The catalog numbering in the later vinyl series (all labeled “Vol.3” with variant suffixes) appears intentional or was used to distinguish individual 12” volumes within a series arc.
