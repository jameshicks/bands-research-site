Some Bizzare

Discogs ID: 49453

British independent record label founded by Stevo Pearce in 1980. Early champion of the post-punk electronic underground; released the Some Bizzare Album (1981) compilation that introduced Soft Cell, Depeche Mode, and others. Key label for Psychic TV’s first two albums: Force The Hand Of Chance (1982) and Dreams Less Sweet (1983). Also home to The The, Soft Cell, Cabaret Voltaire, and Einstürzende Neubauten in this period.

Connections (web-sourced)

In 1982–83 Stevo signed Michael Anthony Keane of Royal Family And The Poor after a tape was passed to him by Eddie Lundon of China Crisis. Stevo planned to release “I Love You” as a mainstream single. Keane recorded at Trident Studio, London (January 1983) with Matt Johnson (The The) providing backing vocals on tracks including “Moonfish Is Here” and “Motherland.” Keane ultimately rejected the major-label strategy; the sessions were not released.
