Voices Of Wonder

Norwegian independent record label based in Oslo. Active from the early 1990s.

Primary label for Motorpsycho’s early catalog: Lobotomizer (1991), 3 Songs For Rut (1992), 8 Soothing Songs For Rut (1992), Soothe (1992), Demon Box (1993), Mountain EP (1993), Another Ugly EP (1994). A foundational imprint in the Norwegian alternative/noise rock scene of the early 1990s.
