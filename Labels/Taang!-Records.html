Overview

Taang! Records was founded by Curtis Casella in 1983 in Boston, Massachusetts — the name standing as an acronym for “Teen Agers Are No Good!” The label built its reputation as a home for Boston hardcore and punk, with its catalogue growing steadily through the 1980s (38 releases) before peaking in the 1990s (84 releases) as the roster broadened to encompass ska, Oi, and indie rock alongside its hardcore roots. In 1995 the operation relocated to San Diego, California. Key names associated with the label include The Mighty Mighty Bosstones, Slapshot, and producer Lou Giordano; output has continued at reduced volume into the 2010s and 2020s.

Notes (web) — relocation to San Diego

The relocation from Boston to San Diego was driven primarily by Curtis Casella’s personal and professional move to California, with the label following him rather than the label’s West Coast expansion precipitating the move.

Causal chain (per Rebel Noise label history): In 1992 Casella took on an archival release for San Diego-based Battalion Of Saints. Around the same time he was asked to serve as music supervisor and first DJ for the opening of the Viper Room in Los Angeles (owned by a friend). He relocated from LA to San Diego, where he continued working with Battalion of Saints on their reformation. After approximately two years commuting between coasts he decided to settle in California and open offices in San Diego and LA while continuing to operate the label out of Boston during the transition. (rebelnoise.com/labels/taang-records)

Date conflict: Discogs label profile states “Relocated to San Diego, California in 1995.” Wikipedia places the move in “the early 1990s.” Rebel Noise’s account implies the decision to settle was made roughly two years after the 1992 California work began, pointing to ~1994. Discogs address records are consistent with a gradual transition: the Newton MA PO Box ran through August 1994; the San Diego (706 Pismo Court) address is listed from August 1996. A single “move year” of 1995 may reflect when the Boston operations formally closed rather than when Casella himself arrived in California. No source gives a month-and-year for the formal transfer of label headquarters.

West Coast roster expansion (ska/punk) followed rather than caused the move: the Buck-O-Nine albums and the British Oi/punk catalogue (The Business, Cock Sparrer, The Exploited) were signed and released after Casella was already based in San Diego. (rebelnoise.com/labels/taang-records)
