Ernest Jenning Record Co.

Independent record label. Discogs label ID: 82757.

Released Beauty Pill’s Blue Period (2023, EJRC193), a double LP retrospective/compilation. Also appears in Chad Clark’s wider engineering network — the label had prior dealings in the DC/NYC indie sphere.
