Overview

550 Music was an American record label founded in 1992 under Sony Music Entertainment, operating exclusively through the 1990s with a catalogue of around 40 releases. Despite its relatively compact roster, the label covered broad commercial ground — from Céline Dion’s adult pop to Ben Folds Five’s indie rock, with production regulars including Humberto Gatica and Vlado Meller. The imprint is closely tied to Sony 550 Music, a related label that appears to represent either a rebranding or parallel structure within Sony’s organisation.
