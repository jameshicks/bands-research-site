Tym Records is a label associated with Tym Guitars, an instrument shop and recording space in Brisbane, Australia. Best known for its range of custom effects pedals, championed by the likes of J Mascis, Bob Mould, and Future Of The Left.

Note that Tym’s TYMxxx catalog numbers are not always chronologically aligned with their actual release date. For instance, TYM026 was released more than 3 months before, and in the year before, TYM025 was released. Therefore catalog number alone should not be used to determine a year of release in cases where a record numerically falls between two others with known release dates.

— Discogs label profile, ID 489279

Overview

Tym Records is a Brisbane-based label that grew directly out of Tym Guitars, a boutique instrument shop and recording space. The label leans into the guitar-centric aesthetic the shop embodies — its 36-release catalogue spans punk, shoegaze, psychedelic rock, and indie rock, with one early release in the 2000s and the bulk of its output concentrated in the 2010s. Its roster has included Swervedriver and local acts such as Sounds Like Sunset, and the shop’s custom effects pedals have attracted endorsement from artists like J Mascis, Bob Mould, and Future of the Left. Cataloguing is complicated by the label’s own TYMxxx numbering, which does not always follow chronological release order.
