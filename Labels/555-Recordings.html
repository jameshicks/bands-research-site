Overview

555 Recordings is an indie label founded and operated by Stewart Anderson, known for his work with Boyracer, Hulaboy, and related projects. It began life in Leeds, UK, relocated to Philadelphia in January 2001, and moved again to Flagstaff, AZ in November 2003, each shift tracking Anderson’s own migrations. The catalogue of 44 releases spans indie rock, lo-fi, punk, and experimental/IDM territory, with the bulk of output — 30 releases — concentrated in the 2000s. The roster is tightly orbited around Anderson’s circle, with Boyracer, Jen Turrell, Greg Wadley, and Russ Fox among the credited artists.
