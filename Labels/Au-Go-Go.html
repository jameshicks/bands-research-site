Au Go Go

Melbourne-based independent record label. Discogs label ID: 14106. One of the most significant Australian independent labels; 435 releases documented on Discogs. Has three documented sublabels: Au Go Go Records, Au Go Go Records Pty. Ltd., AuGoGo Music.

Released Venom P. Stinger’s Waiting Room (1991). Key label for the Melbourne underground and broader Australian independent scene.
