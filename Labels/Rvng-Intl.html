RVNG Intl.

New York-based independent label founded by Matt Werth in 2003. Known for experimental, electronic, and avant-garde releases. Operated several serial sub-series including the NRDS 12” edits series, the RVNGMXS mix-CD series, and the FRKWYS artist-collaboration series.

NRDS Series

The “RVNG of the NRDS” series was RVNG Intl.’s noise/experimental/edit sub-series, running from approximately 2006 to 2010 across 10 volumes of 12” releases. The final volume (Vol. 10) was a Pilooski edit, released with the tagline “In the end there were ten.” (igetrvng.com) Seven RVNGMXS mix CDs ran in parallel with the 12” series.
