PVC Records

American independent record label, based in South Plainfield, New Jersey. A sublabel of Passport Records (itself a division of Jem Records), formed in 1978 and active through approximately 1988 when the parent Jem Records group collapsed. Discogs label ID: 13206.

Overview

PVC Records operated out of the same South Plainfield, NJ address as Passport Records (3619 Kennedy Road). It functioned as a more adventurous imprint within the Passport/Jem group, licensing and distributing US editions of post-punk, progressive, new wave, and alternative titles from British, European, and Australian sources, alongside some domestic signings.

The label had affiliations with Some Bizzare (Foetus / JG Thirlwell’s Self Immolation releases), ZE Records (Alan Vega solo debut; James White / The Contortions’ Second Chance), and the Cachalot imprint. Its catalog design passed through two phases: a blue-and-white bulls-eye design used September 1979–December 1981, and a yellow/blue split-color design from approximately January 1982 through 1986.

Key signing achievements include US releases for early Siouxsie & The Banshees albums, the US debut of Simple Minds, early US availability of Brian Eno’s Ambient 1 (Music For Airports), the flagship Bad Brains album Rock For Light (1983, produced by Ric Ocasek), multiple Swans LPs, and US editions of Einstürzende Neubauten and The Fall.

According to Wikipedia, the Passport Records Group also included Import Records and Visa Records alongside PVC and Passport Jazz. Passport/Jem went out of business in 1988; Marty Scott subsequently took Paradox Records (another sublabel) to MCA.
