Overview

Firestation Tower Records was a German indie label founded in 1998 by Annikki Kretschmer, Uwe Weigmann, and Jan Kühn, releasing indie pop, indie rock, and synth-pop material through the late 1990s and into the early 2000s. Its catalogue of around 26 releases was weighted toward the 2000s, with roster acts including Avocadoclub, Goldstoned, and Lato. In 2003 the label renamed itself Firestation Records, under which it has continued operating.
