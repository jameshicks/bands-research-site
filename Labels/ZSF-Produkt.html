ZSF Produkt

Discogs label ID: 16450

Japanese cassette/experimental label operated by Masami Akita (Merzbow). 113 releases in the Discogs database. Primary outlet for Merzbow through the 1980s and 1990s. A related entity ZSF Produkt USA (ID: 56803) was a sublabel of Anomalous Records.

Key releases include: Mechanization Takes Command (1983), Antimonument (1986), Ecobondage (1987), Pornoise 1 KG (1987), Metal Acoustic Music (1989).
