Sky Records

German independent record label originally based in Munich. Founded in the 1970s. Primary original home of Cluster, Moebius, Roedelius, Conrad Schnitzler, and other Kosmische/Krautrock artists. Discogs label ID: 10414. Parent entity: Sky Records GmbH (Discogs ID: 278126).

Overview

Sky Records was the originating label for much of the Berlin/Hamburg electronic underground in the 1970s and early 1980s — releasing key works by Cluster, Dieter Moebius, Hans-Joachim Roedelius, and Conrad Schnitzler. Bureau B has systematically reissued the Sky Records catalog, and the Bureau B Kollektion 01 (2014) was explicitly a Sky Records compilation curated by Tim Gane of Stereolab.
