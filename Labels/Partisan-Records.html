Overview

Partisan Records is a Brooklyn-born global independent label co-founded by Tim Putnam, with offices in New York, London, and Los Angeles and worldwide distribution through Universal’s Virgin Music Group. Launched around 2008, it built its catalogue primarily across the 2010s, concentrating on indie rock, post-punk, and punk, with a roster that includes IDLES and Fontaines D.C. The label operates two sublabels, Desert Daze Sound and section 1, and is itself a subsidiary of the parent entity Partisan Records LLC.
