Because Music

French independent record label and distributor. Discogs label ID: 54379. Parent label of Vietnam (2).

Distributed and/or co-released several H-Burns albums including Kid We Own The Summer (2017) and Midlife (2018), and the digital single Opposite Way (2013). Because Music is one of the major French independent labels, also known for its international roster.
