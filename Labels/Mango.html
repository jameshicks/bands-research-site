Overview

Mango was a subsidiary label of Island Records, founded by Chris Blackwell in 1972 to concentrate on music from the Caribbean. Its catalogue spans roots reggae, dub, dancehall, and African styles, with a roster anchored by Sly & Robbie, Linton Kwesi Johnson, and Aswad. The label spawned two imprints of its own — Mango Street and The Tropical Sound Of Colombia — and accumulated over 2,400 credited releases before its US branch closed in 1993 and the label itself was discontinued in 1997.
