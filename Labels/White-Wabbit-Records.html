小白兔唱片 (White Wabbit Records; WWR) is an independent record store/label in Taipei, Taiwan. It’s established in 2002. Besides its own label business, it also imports and handles licensing deals abroad. Sometimes WWR organizes music events and invites foreign artists to perform.

— Discogs label profile, ID 89199

Overview

White Wabbit Records (小白兔唱片; WWR) is a Taipei-based independent record store and label operating since 2002 as both a retail outlet and a cultural conduit — importing international music into Taiwan and handling outbound licensing. Its catalogue runs toward post-rock, indie rock, and indie pop, with the bulk of its releases concentrated in the 2000s (35 releases) and 2010s (29 releases). The label has also programmed live events, bringing overseas artists to perform in Taiwan. Its roster spans local acts such as 鄭宜農 and the Danish project Sleep Party People, reflecting its cross-regional licensing reach.
