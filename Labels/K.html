Overview

K is an independent label and distributor founded in Olympia, Washington in 1982 by Calvin Johnson, beginning as a cassette-only outlet for local Olympia-area acts before expanding into vinyl through the International Pop Underground series of 7” singles and eventually into full-length albums on both vinyl and CD. The catalogue spans the 1980s and 1990s across indie rock, lo-fi, punk, and indie pop, with roster artists including Phil Elvrum, Steve Fisk, and Mirah Yom Tov Zeitlyn. K operates sublabels including Dub Narcotic Disco Plate and International Pop Underground, and is distributed by The Business (2).
