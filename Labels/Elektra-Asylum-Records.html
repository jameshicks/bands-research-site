Overview

Elektra/Asylum Records was formed in August 1973 as an administrative umbrella when Warner Communications merged the Elektra and Asylum labels — functioning as a company-level entity for copyright and publishing rather than as a consumer-facing release brand. Individual releases should be attributed to Elektra or Asylum Records directly; the combined credit spans roughly 100 entries concentrated in the 1970s and 1980s across Pop Rock, Classic Rock, Hard Rock, Country, and Folk Rock. The roster reflects the combined strengths of both parent labels, with acts including The Doors, Eagles, Jackson Browne, Steve Goodman, and Grover Washington Jr. A sublabel, Spun Gold, also falls under this entity.
