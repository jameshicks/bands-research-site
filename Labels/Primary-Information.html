Primary Information

Type: Non-profit publisher / record label Based: Brooklyn, New York (45 Main Street, Suite #515, Brooklyn, NY 11201) Founded: 2006 Discogs label ID: 130646 Website: primaryinformation.org

Primary Information is a Brooklyn-based non-profit organization devoted to publishing artists’ books, artists’ writings, out-of-print publications, and limited art editions. Founded in 2006, it was created to foster intergenerational dialogue through the publication of work by emerging, mid-career, and established artists. The organization’s period of focus runs from the early 1960s to the present, with an emphasis on conceptual and post-conceptual practice — treating the publication itself as an exhibition space (Wikipedia). Funding comes through grants from the Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts, the National Endowment for the Arts, and other organizations (Wikipedia).

On Discogs, Primary Information appears primarily as a record label for the audio and sound-art components of its publishing program: vinyl LPs, cassettes, flexi-discs, and CDs accompanying or reissuing archival sound material. Its catalog spans performance documentation, field recordings, experimental composition, and avant-garde music spanning No Wave punk to musique concrète.

Research Queue

  • Who are the founders of Primary Information beyond Miriam Katzeff? An Art21 Magazine interview (2010) with Katzeff is cited on Wikipedia — what does it say about the organization’s founding structure and co-founders?
  • XXX Macarena (2010) is co-released with From The Nursery (cat# FTN 002) — is this From The Nursery the Sheffield industrial/ambient label, or a different entity? Confirm from the label’s own Discogs page.
  • Live From The Commodore Ballroom (UJ3RK5, 2016) — was this the first commercial release of the Commodore Ballroom recording, or had it appeared on a prior Vancouver compilation?
  • Cannibal (2014) credits “Cannibal (7)” as primary artist — who is this group? The Discogs page lists Cameron Jamie, Cary Loren, and Dennis Tyfus as performers. Confirm group composition and whether this was a one-off project or recurring collaboration.

This discrepancy reflects the unique_masters_only flag vs. pressings — confirm whether all 13 are documented or if there are additional releases not yet in Discogs.
