The Curatorial Club

Discogs ID: 188527
URL: thecuratorialclub.blogspot.com
Country: USA
Active: 2010–2011 (per catalog)
Operator: Michael McGregor (3) (founded by Michael McGregor, co-founder of Chocolate Bobka music blog)

US cassette and limited-edition release imprint based in Brooklyn. Active 2010–2011, the label released approximately 18 documented catalog items (TCC001–TCC018, plus one unnumbered Various compilation). It sits squarely within the early-2010s hypnagogic pop and lo-fi electronic cassette scene, overlapping with the Hippos In Tanks / Software (2) orbit through the presence of Games (3) (Joel Ford and Daniel Lopatin), Airbird (Joel Ford’s solo alias), and Chuck Person (a Lopatin alias).

Operator

The Curatorial Club was founded and operated by Michael McGregor (Discogs: Michael McGregor (3), ID: 3722478), a music producer, artist, and blogger then based in the Los Angeles / New York indie underground. He co-founded the Chocolate Bobka music blog. He also recorded as M/M and Meadowlands (cassette/ambient) and was a member of groups ATM (14) and Free Spirit (25). His later work is documented at mcgregor.world.

Key Releases

Spend The Night With — Games (3) (TCC008, 2010)

26-track re-edit / mix compilation on 2× cassette, limited edition. Credited to Games (3) (the project of Joel Ford and Daniel Lopatin). Games is credited as “Edited By (Re-edits).” Artwork and layout co-credited to KBG Man (Discogs ID: 14573401) and Christian Oldham under the Megazord alias. The tracklist draws on a wide range of sources: Aphex Twin, Derrick May, Dopplereffekt, Enya, Herbie Hancock, Mobb Deep, and various soft soul / R&B artists (Michael Franks, Roger Troutman, Joyce Sims, Atlantic Starr, The Manhattans, 52nd Street).

Chuck Person’s Eccojams Vol. 1 — Chuck Person (TCC011, 2010)

Cassette release of what became one of the founding texts of the vaporwave aesthetic. Chuck Person is an alias of Daniel Lopatin (also known as Oneohtrix Point Never). Lopatin is credited Mixed By (uncredited). Artwork credited to Christian Oldham (as Christian “Megazord” Oldham, uncredited). This release puts both Games (3) members — Lopatin directly and Ford via TCC008 and TCC016 — on the label’s catalog.

Airbird / Megafortress — Airbird / Megafortress (TCC016, 2011)

Split cassette. A-side (tracks A1–A4): Airbird (Joel Ford’s solo alias), composed by Ford (uncredited). B-side (tracks B1–B4): Megafortress (Bill Gillim), composed by Gillim (uncredited). Cover art and layout by Christian Oldham (uncredited). This was the debut release for the Airbird alias; Megafortress later signed to Software (2) for their 2012 LP, produced by Ford & Lopatin.

Airbird tracklist:

  • A1: “Part Of The Game” / A2: “Surfer Girl” / A3: “Somewhere In A Field” / A4: “King For The Night”

Megafortress tracklist:

  • B1: “Alleluiah” / B2: “Omega L.I.F.E.” / B3: “Never-Returner” / B4: “Megafortress IV”

Scene Context

The Curatorial Club operated at the junction of several early-2010s micro-scenes:

  • Hypnagogic pop / NYC cassette underground: Twin Sister (2), Alex Bleeker, Run DMT, Future Shuttle all appear on the roster alongside Games (3) and Airbird
  • Hippos In Tanks / Software orbit: Both members of Games (3) appear independently — Lopatin as Chuck Person (TCC011), Ford as Airbird (TCC016) — and Games (3) collectively (TCC008). Christian Oldham (Megazord) provided artwork for TCC008, TCC011, and TCC016, making him the visual throughline across the most significant releases
  • Vaporwave prehistory: The Chuck Person Eccojams tape (TCC011) is now recognized as a foundational vaporwave document; it debuted on this label in cassette form before its wider circulation as a free download
