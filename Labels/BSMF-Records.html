Overview

BSMF Records is a Japanese label whose name stands for Blues & Soul Music Factory, active since 2006. Its catalogue spans electric blues, blues rock, modern electric blues, rhythm & blues, and soul — with the core of its output concentrated in the 2010s (over 300 releases) and continuing into the 2020s with around 140 more. The label runs several themed imprints including the Blues Rock Guitar Series, Blues Women Series, SGGW Series, and Red Now Records. Its roster mixes Japanese artists with international figures such as American harmonica player Bob Corritore and Thomas Ruf, founder of the German blues label Ruf Records.
