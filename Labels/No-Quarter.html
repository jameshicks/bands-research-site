No Quarter

US independent record label based in New York. Discogs label ID: 44042. Home label for Endless Boogie and other underground rock acts. Website: noquarter.net; Bandcamp: noquarterrex.bandcamp.com.

Overview

No Quarter was founded by participants who would also co-found Endless Boogie. The label’s catalog encompasses psychedelic rock, noise rock, and heavy/stoner rock. It has released all of Endless Boogie’s commercial LPs: Focus Level (2008), Full House Head (2010), Long Island (2013), Vibe Killer (2017), The Gathered and Scattered (2020), Admonitions (2021), and the compilation Vol. I, II (2019).

The label has approximately 261 releases in the Discogs database. A separate label, No Quarter (2) (Discogs ID: 795791), is a different entity.
