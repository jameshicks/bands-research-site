Overview

Luv Luv Luv Records is a London-based independent label whose stated mission is finding and developing new talent. Its catalogue is concentrated entirely in the 2010s, spanning indie rock, pop rock, and alternative rock across 44 releases. The label is closely associated with Florence and the Machine and her core circle — its roster includes Florence Welch, drummer Christopher Lloyd Hayden, harpist Tom Monger, and producer Paul Epworth, making it effectively a home for that project’s principal collaborators.
