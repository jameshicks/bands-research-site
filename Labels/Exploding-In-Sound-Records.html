Overview

Exploding In Sound Records is an indie label based in Brooklyn, New York, founded in October 2011 by Dan Goldin and Dave Spak. Its catalogue skews toward the 2010s (72 releases) but has remained active into the 2020s (28 releases), with a sound rooted in indie rock, post-punk, and math rock. The roster has included Pile, Ovlov, Rick Maguire, and Steve Hartlett, among others, with 334 total Discogs credits across roughly 100 catalogued releases.
