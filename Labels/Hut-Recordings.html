Overview

Hut Recordings is a British record label that functioned as an alias of Hut Records (5), operating under parent company VC Records Ltd. Its entire catalogue is concentrated in the 1990s, with the imprint’s output spanning Alternative Rock, Britpop, and Indie Rock alongside strands of Psychedelic Rock and Trip Hop. The roster included major acts of the era on both sides of the Atlantic: The Verve, Placebo, Richard Ashcroft, The Smashing Pumpkins, and Billy Corgan. The label also operated the Meccico sublabel.
