Fuzz Club Records

UK independent label. Discogs label ID: 389382. Release count: 1,143+.

Known for releasing psychedelic, noise-rock, and shoegaze-adjacent music. Released The Jesus And Mary Chain’s Sunset 666 (2023) and Glasgow Eyes (2024), the band’s most recent studio album.
