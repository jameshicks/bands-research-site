City Slang

Origin: Berlin, Germany (address: Dieffenbachstr. 33, D-10967 Berlin) Founded: 1989 Founder: Christof Ellinghaus Label Code: LC 06853

Overview

City Slang is an independent German record label founded in 1989 by Christof Ellinghaus. Initially Hamburg-based, later based in Berlin, it became one of the most important European indie labels of the 1990s and 2000s through a combination of licensing US underground rock bands for European release and signing significant European and international acts directly.

In its early years (1990–1995) City Slang served as a European home for American underground rock: the label’s first catalog numbers carried releases by The Lemonheads, Das Damen, Yo La Tengo, The Flaming Lips, Hole, Superchunk, Unsane, Sebadoh, Seam, Tortoise, Built To Spill, and Guided By Voices — effectively an encyclopedia of 1990s US indie. This European-licensing model ran through the EFA distribution network (catalog prefixes EFA 04xxx, then 08xxx, then 20xxx).

By the late 1990s the label shifted toward a more artist-development model, signing Calexico, Lambchop, and Schneider TM for long-term relationships that would anchor its catalog well into the 2000s. The Notwist’s Neon Golden (2002), Calexico’s Feast Of Wire (2002), and Lambchop’s sustained presence became landmark releases. The label also released To Rococo Rot from 1996 onward — their most consistent label relationship — and in the 2000s expanded its international scope with Canadian (Broken Social Scene, Stars, Caribou, Arcade Fire) and Scandinavian (Junip/José González, Anna von Hausswolff, Efterklang) acts alongside ongoing German-scene commitments.

City Slang has several sublabels: Souterrain Transmissions (sublabel id 167593), How Do You Are? Recordings (857251), 20 Years Of City Slang (522440), CEUSO Music Publishing (1337805), City Slang Musikverlag (324873), and City Slang Records (2) (1345008). The publishing company is City Slang Musikverlag (label id 324873); the copyright-holding company is City Slang Christof Ellinghaus Musikproduktion (label id 727734).

As of the Discogs dump, City Slang has approximately 2,753 releases across all formats.

Notable Roster

Long-term / Core Acts

  • Lambchop (artist id 10551) — 188 releases on City Slang, 1994–2023; label’s most-released artist. Kurt Wagner’s Nashville orchestral-pop project. First release: I Hope You’re Sitting Down (1994).
  • Calexico (artist id 9856) — 179 releases, 1998–2025. Joey Burns and John Convertino’s Tucson desert-rock project. The Black Light (1998) through Edge Of The Sun (2015) and beyond.
  • Caribou (artist id 275841) — 98 releases, 2007–2025. Dan Snaith’s electronic/psych project (Andorra, Swim, Our Love).
  • Tindersticks (artist id 15259) — 83 releases, 2012–2025. UK orchestral-pop; joined City Slang after leaving V2.
  • Hole (2) (artist id 105728) — 72 releases, 1991–1997. Courtney Love’s band; European releases including Live Through This.
  • The Notwist (artist id 616) — 63 releases, 2001–2023. Weilheim indie-electronic; Neon Golden (2002) is their career-defining work on City Slang.
  • To Rococo Rot (artist id 435) — 44 releases, 1996–2025. Berlin post-rock trio; label’s most durable early European signing. Releases: Paris 25, Veiculo, Cars, The Amateur View, Pantone EP, Music Is A Hungry Ghost, Instrument.
  • Broken Social Scene (artist id 138844) — 43 releases, 2005–2022. Canadian indie collective; European partner.
  • Get Well Soon (artist id 849866) — 43 releases, 2008–2019. German project of Konstantin Gropper; Rest Now, Weary Head! (2008) was an ambitious debut.
  • Superchunk (artist id 198667) — 43 releases, 1991–1997. Early US licensing cornerstone.

Significant Artist Relationships (1990s)

  • Tortoise (artist id 4727) — 42 releases, 1994–1999. Post-rock founding fathers; Rhythms, Resolutions & Clusters and Millions Now Living Will Never Die on City Slang.
  • Yo La Tengo (artist id 50984) — 35 releases, 1990–1995. Hoboken indie legends licensed for Europe.
  • Sebadoh (artist id 68467) — 32 releases, 1992–1999. Lou Barlow’s project.
  • Schneider TM (artist id 627) — 31 releases, 1998–2006. German electronic/post-rock; Moist (1998), Zoomer (2002).
  • Built To Spill (artist id 260124) — 29 releases, 1995–2000.
  • Salaryman (artist id 13052) — 12 releases, 1996–1999. Chicago post-rock supergroup with members of Tortoise.
  • Trans Am (2) (artist id 40385) — 9 releases, 1996–1998. DC post-rock/electro.
  • Gallon Drunk (artist id 86537) — 18 releases, 1993–1996. UK garage rock.
  • Seam (artist id 330670) — 14 releases, 1992–1995. Midwest indie.
  • Unsane (artist id 138729) — 7 releases, 1991–1994. NYC noise rock.
  • Dim Stars (artist id 273182) — 3 releases, 1992. NYC supergroup.
  • Jawbox (artist id 252314) — 7 releases, 1994–1996.
