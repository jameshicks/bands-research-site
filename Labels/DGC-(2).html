Overview

DGC (2) is a Discogs convention label used to catalog counterfeit and unofficial releases that falsely claim to be issued on DGC Records. Its 87 credits span the 1990s through the 2010s and fall predominantly in grunge and alternative rock — styles synonymous with DGC’s commercial peak — with the roster dominated by Nirvana-associated names: Kurt Cobain, Krist Novoselic, Dave Grohl, and producer Butch Vig, reflecting the high volume of Nirvana bootlegs catalogued under this entry.
