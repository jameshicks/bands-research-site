Overview

No Man’s Land was an experimental and avant-garde label founded by Jürgen Königer in 1984, launched at the same moment he renamed Recommended Music to Review Records — the two imprints then ran in parallel. The label operated its own distribution arm (Recommended No Man’s Land) and publishing entity (Edition No Man’s Land), suggesting a deliberately structured micro-infrastructure. Its catalogue is concentrated in the 1980s (19 releases), with a sharp drop into the 1990s (5) and sparse activity into the 2000s (2); its roster drew from the international improvised and industrial underground, including Jad Fair, Lindsay Cooper, and Tom Cora.
