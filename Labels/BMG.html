Overview

BMG — short for Bertelsmann Music Group — was a global music conglomerate that emerged from a 1985 joint venture between Bertelsmann AG and RCA Corporation. Bertelsmann bought out General Electric’s stake the following year, acquiring both the RCA and Arista labels outright, and formally renamed the group Bertelsmann Music Group on 26 March 1987. The label’s Discogs catalogue is concentrated in the 1980s and spans Pop Rock, Alternative Rock, Synth-pop and House, operated through a network of sublabels including Ariola and several collector imprints. The entity has since restructured as BMG Rights Management GmbH, which continues to trade under the BMG brand.
