Overview

CREAM Records (3) is a jazz label founded in 1983 by Jean-Marie Salhani, focused on fusion, jazz-rock, jazz-funk, and contemporary jazz. Its catalogue of 75 releases runs across four decades, peaking in the 1990s (30 releases) and remaining substantial through the 2000s (24 releases), with a smaller initial run in the 1980s. The roster leans heavily toward Québécois artists — UZEB and its members Alain Caron and Michel Cusson are central figures — alongside British fusion guitarist Allan Holdsworth. The label also operated the sublabel Ice Cream Music (3).
