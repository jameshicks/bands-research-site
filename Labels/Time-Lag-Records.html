Time-Lag Records

Discogs ID: 49080
US independent record label. 197 releases documented on Discogs.

Known for releasing experimental folk, free folk, psych, and related underground American music in the 2000s. Released the split Songs From The Entoptic Garden Volume Two (2001, Time-Lag 002) with Six Organs of Admittance and Charalambides, and the Terrastock 5 compilation (2002) featuring Charalambides, Bardo Pond, Sonic Youth, The Iditarod, and Stone Breath.

Also released Six Organs of Admittance’s For Octavio Paz (2003).
