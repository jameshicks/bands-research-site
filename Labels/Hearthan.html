Hearthan

Self-release / vanity label used by Pere Ubu and associated Cleveland acts. Discogs label ID: 66581. Sublabel of Hearpen Records (parent label ID: 74508).

Overview

The original outlet for Pere Ubu’s groundbreaking early 45s — “30 Seconds Over Tokyo” (1975), “Final Solution” (1976), and “Street Waves / My Dark Ages” (1976) — released before any major or indie label deal. These records are now regarded as foundational documents of American proto-punk and post-punk.

The imprint also issued a one-off single by Cleveland contemporaries Mirrors (“Shirley / She Smiled Wild”, 1977) and a single by The Girls (7) (“Jeffrey I Hear You / The Elephant Man”, 1979, produced by David Thomas (2)), as well as the Pressler-Morgan One Plus One single (1979).

The label name continued as an archival imprint through the 2000s: the Hearpen Singles compilation (1995, with Tim/Kerr Records), The Shape of Things (2000, live 1976 recordings), and multiple David Thomas and Pere Ubu live/archival releases.

Name note: Hearthan is the visual/design name for what is legally called Hearpen. See Hearpen Records for the full explanation of the naming convention.
