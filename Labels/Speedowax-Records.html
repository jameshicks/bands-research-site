Overview

Speedowax Records is a UK punk label founded in 1997, operating across punk, hardcore, garage rock, and thrash. After a measured first two decades — around 32 releases spread across the 1990s and 2000s — the label’s output surged in the 2010s, which account for over 90 of its roughly 143 catalogue entries. Its roster includes The Hip Priests, Dead Cuts, and Weight Of The World, among others.
