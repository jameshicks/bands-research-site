Overview

Penguin is a Greek record label operating as an imprint of A.S. Penguin Ltd., which also handled distribution duties. Its catalogue is concentrated in the 1980s — roughly 50 of its 65 releases date from that decade — before activity sharply fell off across the 1990s and beyond. Despite its Greek base, the label’s roster skews heavily international, drawing from the experimental and industrial underground: Edward Ka-Spel, Savage Republic, Bruce Licher, Stephan Barbery, and Jeremy Oxley all appear. Stylistically it ranges across experimental, industrial, new wave, and alternative rock.
