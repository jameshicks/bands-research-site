V. M. L. Records was a record label started in Franklin Park, Illinois, by The Vindictives in the beginning of the 1990s (V. M. L. stands for Vindictives Music Limited). Although the label put out studio recordings by a number of punk bands, it is probably best remembered for the “V.M.L. Live Series”, a collection of live 7” EPs known for their plain, uniform sleeves and the ample amount of crowd noise in the recordings.

— Discogs label profile, ID 36112

Overview

A Franklin Park, Illinois punk label founded by The Vindictives in the early 1990s — V.M.L. standing for Vindictives Music Limited — V.M.L. Records built a catalogue of roughly 49 releases concentrated entirely within that decade. While it issued studio recordings across punk, hardcore, pop-punk, and ska, the label’s signature output was the V.M.L. Live Series: a run of live 7” EPs distinguished by plain, uniform sleeves and recordings heavy with crowd noise. Its roster included Ben Weasel alongside several Vindictives-affiliated artists, keeping the operation tightly bound to the Chicago-area punk scene of the era.
