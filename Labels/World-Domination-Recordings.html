World Domination Recordings

Seattle-based independent label, distributed by Capitol Records. Active c. 1989–1999 (Capitol phase c. 1992–1996). Discogs label ID: 4935. Parent label: Capitol Records (Discogs label ID: 654).

Mailing address on catalog: P.O. Box 5618, London, W9 3WT, UK (UK office).

Overview

World Domination Recordings was founded by Dave Allen (ex-Gang of Four, Shriekback, King Swamp) and Belgian musician Luc Van Acker (ex-Revolting Cocks, My Life With the Thrill Kill Kult, Arbeid Adelt!). Allen served as the label’s primary A&R and executive/direct producer across the catalog; Van Acker contributed as executive producer and producer on a significant portion of releases, and released his own Catalogue LP (1992, catno WD 6665) as the label’s inaugural Belgian-phase release.

The label’s prehistory begins in 1989–1990 with three Mussolini Headkick albums on WD catalog numbers (Belgium pressing), and a Danceable Weird Shit compilation, suggesting a Belgian/European origin before the Capitol deal. The Capitol-distributed US phase launched in 1992 with Contagion, Sky Cries Mary, Shriekback, and Low Pop Suicide. After the Capitol relationship ended around 1996, the catalog continued under the World Domination Music Group (WDMG) banner — Allen’s partnership with Canadian label True North — active through approximately 1999. The final releases (Sky Cries Mary, 60 Channels, Perfume Tree, Makyo) appeared in 1998–1999.

Discogs catalogs 279 releases under this label ID across the full active span.

Founders

Dave Allen (Discogs ID: 842383)

Real name David Geoffrey Allen (born 23 December 1955, died 5 April 2025). Bass player and co-founder of Gang of Four (1977–1981) and Shriekback (1982–). After King Swamp (Virgin, 1988–1990), Allen relocated to Seattle and founded the label. He is credited as A&R, executive producer, or direct producer on 47 releases across the catalog — the single most-credited producer. He also performed as a band member on Low Pop Suicide releases (bass) and as a principal in The Elastic Purejoy (multi-instrumentalist, writer).

See Dave Allen for full biography and discography.

Luc Van Acker (Discogs ID: 29223)

Belgian musician (real name Luc Maria René Van Acker). Active in the Belgian new wave / industrial nexus: Arbeid Adelt!, Revolting Cocks, My Life With The Thrill Kill Kult, Noise Abroad (the latter shared with Allen). Also credited under aliases God (11) and Gumby. Co-founder/executive producer on WDR; credited as executive producer or producer on 16 releases. His solo Catalogue (1992) was one of the label’s early releases.

Phase Chronology

Belgian / Pre-Capitol Phase (1989–1991)

The earliest WD catalog numbers (WD 6661–WD 6665) are Belgian pressings, concentrated on Mussolini Headkick (three records: Themes For Violent Retribution, Blood On The Flag, Get Out) and Danceable Weird Shit. Luc Van Acker’s Catalogue closes this phase (WD 6665, 1992, Belgium pressing). These predate the Capitol distribution agreement and suggest the label’s roots in the Belgian / European industrial-adjacent underground.

Capitol-Distributed US Phase (1992–c. 1996)

Capitol parent label (ID: 654) distributed the US releases. Capitol catalog numbers (CDP 0777…, CDP 7…) appear on the 1992 core releases. The label shifted to its own WD/WDOM/WDM catalog series shortly after. Core acts: Sky Cries Mary (largest roster presence, 32 releases), Low Pop Suicide, Stanford Prison Experiment, Contagion, The Psyclone Rangers, Shriekback, The Elastic Purejoy. Allen and Van Acker served as executive producers; Ian Caple and Michael Vail Blum as recurring engineers; Tom Baker (3) as primary mastering engineer.

World Domination Music Group Phase (c. 1996–1999)

After the Capitol deal ended, the label rebranded as World Domination Music Group in partnership with Canadian label True North. WDM-prefixed catalog numbers (WDM 10060 onward) characterize this phase. Roster shifted: Loop Guru, Lizard Music, Penthouse (2), He Said Omala, Makyo, Wendy & Lisa, 60 Channels, Perfume Tree, For Against. Final releases appear in 1998–1999.

Sky Cries Mary — Anchor Act

Sky Cries Mary (Seattle; Discogs ID: 59296) was the label’s most-released act with 32 entries. Led by vocalist Anisa Romero and multi-instrumentalist Roderick Wolgamott Romero, with a large rotating cast including Gordon Raphael (guitar/keyboards/sampler), Joseph E. Howard (bass), Bennett James (drums/percussion), and at various points Ken Stringfellow, Jon Auer, Jack Endino, and others. Producer Rick Boston was closely involved across many SCM releases. The band’s 1992 debut Exit At The Axis was co-executive-produced by Allen and Van Acker. Their final WDR release was Until The Grinders Cease (1999).
