Spanish record label and Spanish distributor for labels like Mute Records, 4AD, XL, Beggars Banquet and many more, created by Mark Kitcatt and Martin Mills, among others. For company credits use Everlasting Records S.L.. Most of distribution done by Popstock, created at the same time.

Overview

Everlasting Records was a Spanish record label and distributor co-founded in 1995 by Mark Kitcatt and Martin Mills—the latter being the head of Beggars Group—positioning it as the Spanish home for Mute Records, 4AD, XL, and Beggars Banquet, among other imprints. Distribution was handled by its sister operation Popstock, launched simultaneously, while releases were formally credited to the corporate entity Everlasting Records S.L. The catalogue is weighted toward indie rock, alternative rock, downtempo, and experimental music, with output peaking in the 2000s (141 releases) and tapering sharply after 2010. The roster includes Echoboy, Guadalupe Plata, Moby, and Darren Hayman, with a sublabel, Recordings From The Other Side, extending its activity.
