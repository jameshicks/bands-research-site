Concentric Circles

US reissue label based in Portland, Oregon. Founded circa 2017–2018 by Jed Bindeman, drummer of Eternal Tapestry, co-founder of Freedom To Spend (RVNG Intl. sub-label), and co-owner of Little Axe Records (Portland). Discogs label ID: 1513183. Bandcamp: https://concentriccircles.bandcamp.com/

Specialises in vinyl reissues of obscure and lost recordings from the 1980s–90s independent underground — cassette releases, private presses, and previously unreleased material spanning experimental, art rock, lo-fi, and post-punk. As of 2026, the catalog runs to twelve numbered releases (CC-001 through CC-012), with at least one co-release and several additional pressings.

Overview

Concentric Circles grew out of Jed Bindeman’s record-hunting practice — sourcing obscure cassettes and private-press LPs from thrift stores, private collections, and direct submissions, then licensing them for vinyl reissue. The label’s stated philosophy, per Bindeman: “release albums that people aren’t necessarily waiting around for.” The debut release (2018) was The Story of Valerie by UK artist Carola Baer, a synth-pop / art-pop set originally self-released on cassette in the early 1990s that Bindeman reportedly found in a Portland thrift store.

The label operates entirely alongside Bindeman’s other activities: Freedom To Spend (the reissue arm he co-runs with Pete Swanson under RVNG Intl.) and his membership in Eternal Tapestry and several other Portland-area bands. Concentric Circles is narrower in scope than Freedom To Spend — more cassette-era oddities, more lo-fi and private-press material, less overlap with the freedom-jazz / new-age reissue territory Freedom To Spend has staked out.

The label has broadened from straight reissues into new material as well: the 2024 release Ulrika by Swedish multi-instrumentalist Sternpost (Petter Herbertsson) is an original album, as is the follow-up unworld.afterpop (2026), both receiving their first-ever vinyl editions through Concentric Circles. Similarly, the 2022 co-release Pedestal by Pedestal (2) appeared on cassette via All Night Flight before the Concentric Circles LP edition. The catalog therefore straddles archive reissue and first-time vinyl for under-distributed artists.

Recurring contributors across the catalog include mastering engineer Max Wolf (3), who handled both Tea Room (CC-006) and May Never Happen (CC-005), and designer / artist Julianna Bach, who contributed to both For the Moment (CC-003) and Heterophonious Fool (CC-004).

Key Connections

  • Jed Bindeman (Discogs artist ID: 402296) — label founder and operator. Drummer in Eternal Tapestry, Heavy Winged, Bloodbiker, Gärden Söund, Operative, The Greys, Helen (36). Co-runs Freedom To Spend with Pete Swanson. Name variations on Discogs: “Concentric Circles,” “J. Bindeman,” “Space Jed Bindeman.”
  • Freedom To Spend (Discogs label ID: 111874) — RVNG Intl. sub-label, co-run by Bindeman with Pete Swanson. Sister operation to Concentric Circles, also focused on archival reissue but with a different aesthetic territory.
  • RVNG Intl. (Discogs label ID: 52921) — parent label of Freedom To Spend; broader connection to Bindeman’s reissue activity.
  • Eternal Tapestry — Bindeman’s main band (Portland psych collective; members include Nick Bindeman, Dewey Mahood, Bob Jones (8), Krag Likins, Ryan Carlile).
  • Crawling With Tarts — long-running US experimental group (Michael Gendreau, Suzanne Dycus-Gendreau, Cliff Neighbors); active 1984–2023; major American cassette-underground figures whose catalog overlaps with labels like ASP and Sound of Pig.
  • Shizuka (2) (静香) — Japanese psychedelic rock group; CC-007 marked expanded Western distribution after prior releases on An’archives and Black Editions (2).
  • Ixna — Bay Area experimental group (Jay Cloidt, Marina La Palma, William Winant); active 1981–2019; previously on Dumb Records.
  • All Night Flight (label ID: 1702068) — co-released Pedestal with Concentric Circles.
