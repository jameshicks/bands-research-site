Jetset Records

American independent record label. Discogs label ID: 3327.

New York-based indie label active in the late 1990s and 2000s. Home to Luna (5)‘s final three releases of their first run and early Dean & Britta releases.

Key Releases

  • Luna (5): Live (2001, cat. ARE-017-2 — note: Arena Rock sub), Romantica (2002), Rendezvous (2004), Close Cover Before Striking (2002)
  • Dean & Britta: L’Avventura (2003), Sonic Souvenirs (2003)
