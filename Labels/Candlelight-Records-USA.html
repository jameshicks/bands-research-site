Candlelight Records USA (also credited as Candlelight USA), is the American office of English metal label Candlelight Records, opened in January 2001.

— Discogs label profile, ID 34854

Overview

Candlelight Records USA is the American arm of the British metal label Candlelight Records, established in January 2001 to handle North American releases and distribution. Its catalogue was most active in the 2000s — over 400 releases that decade — before tapering sharply in the 2010s, spanning black metal, death metal, doom metal, thrash, and heavy metal. The roster included prominent acts such as Opeth, Emperor, and Ihsahn, reflecting the parent label’s strength in Scandinavian extreme metal.
