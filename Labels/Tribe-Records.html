Tribe Records

Discogs ID (label): 100848 (as Tribe (3)); parent: Tribe Records (14), ID 1450692
Address: 81 Chandler St., Detroit, MI 48202
Founded: 1972
Active: 1972–c. 1976
Founders: Phil Ranelin (trombone) and Wendell Harrison (saxophone/clarinet)

American jazz record label, production company, and publishing house, founded in Detroit, Michigan. Motto: “Music is the healing force of the Universe.” The Tribe collective also published a black awareness magazine alongside the record label. Releases span spiritual jazz, soul-jazz, and jazz-funk. The collective included Marcus Belgrave, Harold McKinney, Charles Moore, Doug Hammond, Faruq Z. Bey, and others.

Key releases:

  • Message From The Tribe (1972) — Wendell Harrison & Phil Ranelin
  • An Evening With The Devil (1973) — Wendell Harrison
  • The Time Is Now! (1974) — Phil Ranelin
  • Gemini II (1974) — Wendell Harrison
  • Vibes From The Tribe (1976) — Phil Ranelin
  • Detroit Jazz Composers Ltd. (1976) — collective

An anthology, Message From The Tribe: An Anthology of Tribe Records 1972–1976, was originally issued in 1996 and reissued 2010 (Barely Breaking Even / BBE).

The Tribe LPs were reissued and remixed via Hefty Records in the early 2000s (coordinated by John Hughes / Slicker).
