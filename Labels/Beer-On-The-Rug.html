Beer On The Rug

Independent cassette/digital label. Discogs label ID: 242586.

Overview

Beer On The Rug was active in the early 2010s as part of the internet-based vaporwave and bedroom-electronic cassette scene. Released the split cassette Napolian / Computer Dreams (BOTR007, 2011). No parent label; no sublabels listed on Discogs.
