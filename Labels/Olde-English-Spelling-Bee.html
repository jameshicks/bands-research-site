Olde English Spelling Bee

US independent label. Discogs label ID: 65320. Sublabel: Series 200 (ID: 1149240).

Mailing address: P.O. Box 928, New York, NY 10009. Contact: oesbee@gmail.com.

Founded and run by Todd Ledford. Catalog spans 2004–2017+, with the bulk of activity 2008–2013. Total Discogs release entries: ~104. No parent label.

Profile

Olde English Spelling Bee (commonly abbreviated OESB or OESBee) was a New York-based DIY cassette and CDr label founded by Todd Ledford. It operated primarily as a vehicle for the post-blog-era underground — noise, lo-fi psychedelia, new age drift, vaporwave, and experimental electronics — releasing hand-duplicated tapes alongside occasional vinyl and CDr editions.

The label’s geographic base was New York City (Lower East Side PO Box), but its roster was geographically scattered: artists from the UK (Forest Swords, from Liverpool), Australia (Grey Daturas), Japan (K2 (17)), and across the US. The label had a recognizable visual identity: hand-stamped sleeves, collaged artwork, and minimal graphic design largely handled by Ledford himself (credited across multiple releases for layout, photography, and front-cover art).

OESB operated in the same orbit as labels like Not Not Fun Records, Woodsist, and Mexican Summer — all running cheap-format releases for overlapping experimental/indie communities in the late 2000s. Catalog numbers followed the pattern OESB-XX, with some entries using a plain number prefix (#5, #12) in early years.

The label’s most commercially significant release was Forest Swords’ Dagger Paths E.P. (2010, OESB-48), which helped break Matthew Barnes’ project internationally and led to a deal with Tri Angle. OESB also issued early material by Autre Ne Veut (2010, OESB-49), Ducktails (2009, OESB-40), and James Ferraro across multiple releases spanning 2008–2017 — Ferraro being by far the label’s most-documented artist (13 primary credits on Discogs).

In its later phase (2015–2017) OESB pivoted toward a harder vaporwave/PC Music-adjacent sound, releasing Ash Koosha, HKE, Macintosh Plus (the project behind the canonical vaporwave record フローラルの専門店), and Tanning Salon. The sublabel Series 200 (Discogs ID: 1149240) was used for some of this later output.

Mastering across the catalog was handled most frequently by Carl Saff (11 releases) and Rick Essig (5 releases); Pete Swanson (of Yellow Swans) is credited as mastering engineer on 3 releases. VEKTROID (Ramona Andra Xavier) appears as producer, mixer, and mastering engineer on 4 releases — particularly in the vaporwave-era output.

Key Roster Artists

  • James Ferraro (artist ID: 502318) — 13 primary credits, 2008–2017. Most prolific OESB artist. Noise/hypnagogic releases in the early period; later conceptual electronics. Also credited as producer and writer on several.
  • HKE (artist ID: 4968276) — 7 releases, 2016–2017. Vaporwave-era presence.
  • Macintosh Plus (artist ID: 2663189) — 6 releases. Vektroid alias; フローラルの専門店 is the defining vaporwave artifact.
  • Alice Cohen (artist ID: 740127) — 4 releases, 2009–2015. Credited for artwork as well as performing.
  • Ash Koosha (artist ID: 3742168) — 3 releases, 2015–2016.
  • Big Troubles (artist ID: 1678155) — 3 releases in 2010; indie pop.
  • Bruce McClure (artist ID: 1430626) — 3 releases in 2009; expanded cinema / light-and-film artist.
  • Mouthus (artist ID: 460035) — 3 releases, 2005–2007. Noise duo.
  • Tanning Salon (artist ID: 3418909) — 3 releases in 2017.
  • Autre Ne Veut (artist ID: 1609994) — 2 releases in 2010; self-titled debut on OESB before wider recognition.
  • Ducktails (artist ID: 1217375) — 2 releases; Matthew Mondanile’s solo project.
  • Forest Swords (artist ID: 1550411) — 2 releases in 2010; Dagger Paths the label’s best-known title internationally.
  • Julian Lynch (artist ID: 1499009) — 2 releases in 2009–2010.
  • Stellar OM Source (artist ID: 1256150) — Christelle Gualdi project; 2010 release.
  • Yellow Swans (artist ID: 285904) — 1 release; split with Grey Daturas.
  • Robedoor (artist ID: 407630) — 2 releases in 2008.
  • Susan Alcorn (artist ID: 547684) — 2 releases in 2006; avant-garde pedal steel.
  • Monopoly Child Star Searchers (artist ID: 1001175) — alias of Spencer Clark (not VEKTROID); 1 vinyl LP (Bamboo For Two, OESB-53, 2010).
