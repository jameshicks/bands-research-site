Overview

Hostess Entertainment Unlimited is a Japanese music company that operates as a producer, importer, distributor, and advertiser of recorded music and video, with Hostess K.K. as its legal copyright entity. Active from at least 2002, it assembled a catalogue of around 40 releases through the 2000s, focused primarily on indie rock, art rock, and experimental music licensed from international acts. Its roster spans a notable range of artists including Radiohead, Mogwai, Nigel Godrich, and Wally De Backer, reflecting the label’s role as a conduit for bringing internationally significant independent and alternative music to the Japanese market.
