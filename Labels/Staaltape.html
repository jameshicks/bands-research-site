Overview

Staaltape is the cassette imprint of Staalplaat, the Dutch experimental music label founded by Geert-Jan Hobijn. Originally launched in 1981 from a P.O. Box in Amsterdam (1001 GL), the label ran until 1995, documenting the European industrial and experimental underground through cassette releases at a time when the format was the primary vehicle for that scene’s self-distribution. Its catalog concentrated in the years 1983–1992, issuing live recordings, studio works, and split cassettes by artists from the UK, Netherlands, Belgium, Spain, and beyond.

The roster reads as a cross-section of the post-industrial international network of the 1980s: Laibach (their 1983 Dutch live document), Current 93 / Nurse With Wound split, NON (Boyd Rice), Z’EV, Nocturnal Emissions, The Hafler Trio, Chris & Cosey, Zoviet France, Sleep Chamber, The Legendary Pink Dots, and Last Few Days, among others. The Propagandum compilation (Staal Tape 00C, 1984) featured Belgian acts including Club Moral alongside Dutch acts, reflecting Hobijn’s connections with the Antwerp post-punk underground. Geert-Jan Hobijn himself appears on the Propagandum cassette as an interviewee.

In 2010 the label was revived by Rinus Van Alebeek, an Amsterdam-born tape artist then based in Berlin (c/o Staalplaat, Kienitzer Strasse 108, 12049 Berlin). Van Alebeek operates the revival as both curator and producer — his own releases appear on the label alongside new work by Midori Hirano, Charles Moneypenny, and others. The revival issues smaller-run cassettes and has developed a secondary imprint, Tape Run, for curated tape-run editions.

Geert-Jan Hobijn is a member of Staalplaat Sound System (the performance/installation project linked to Staalplaat) and contributed recorded engineering to at least one Staaltape-adjacent release (the Staalplaat live recording of Zoviet France at Melkweg, 1987). His Discogs artist page (ID 579370) lists only minimal primary release credits, with his main role being as the institutional founder rather than a recording artist.

Rinus Van Alebeek (ID 239160) has primary releases on Zeromoon (US) and other European tape labels, and is a member of several small ensembles. His website staaltape.com serves as the label’s current presence.

Catalog

The label issued releases under several catalog series. The original series ran with “Staal Tape 00X” numbering through the 1980s (e.g., 00A–00Z), then shifted to “STAALTAPE” with extended numbering and a CD series (“ST C.D.” / “STCD”). The revival uses unnumbered or individually numbered releases.

Research Queue

  • Was the “Documentatie Serie” sublabel actually used for releases (the NL-Documentatieserie tape series referenced in the Staalplaat article) or is it an administrative category only?
  • The Blue Murder cassette (1982, no cat#) is the earliest Discogs entry — is Blue Murder (Discogs artist ID: 264085) a Dutch act, and was this among the label’s inaugural releases?
  • Brion Gysin’s Recorded Live In France / Interview June 21 1981 (STAALTAPE 00P, 1988) — was this recorded at the American Center Paris or another French venue?
  • Jim O’Rourke’s Disengage (S.T.CD 048, 1992) — is this the same recording later reissued, or a Staaltape-exclusive session?
