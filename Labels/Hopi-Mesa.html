Hopi Mesa

Discogs ID: 105447 Country: France Active: 1991–2010 (documented) Parent label: None (independent)

French independent label based in France, active from at least 1991 through 2010. Its catalog divides cleanly into three artist strands: René Aubry (the dominant presence, with 25 releases spanning the full label life), the duo Henry Torgue / Serge Houppin (9–8 releases), and Jean-Philippe Goude (6 releases, 1992–2001). Total documented catalog: approximately 40 release entries (some pressings/editions of the same master), collapsing to around 25 distinct titles.

The label appears to have been self-run — “Hopi Mesa” appears as a producer credit on at least six releases (credited as an entity, Discogs ID 3369058), and there is no parent or sublabel structure in the DB.

Artists

René Aubry (Discogs ID: 1014192)

The label’s primary artist by volume. Composer and multi-instrumentalist (acoustic guitar, bouzouki, mandolin, banjo, cuatro, balalaika, accordion, banjolin, harmonica, keyboards, vocals), Aubry wrote, produced, and often mixed his own records. He is also credited as engineer on four releases. His catalog on Hopi Mesa spans film and stage soundtracks alongside independent albums.

Henry Torgue / Serge Houppin (Discogs IDs: 1140478 / 1472363)

Composer duo, often co-credited on joint releases. Henry Torgue (real name: Henri Torgue) composed, played keyboards, and produced; Serge Houppin contributed composition, synthesizer, drum programming, guitar, keyboards, percussion, voice, and co-produced. Both also mixed their own records. Houppin is a member of the group Local (6).

Jean-Philippe Goude (Discogs ID: 97971)

See Jean-Philippe Goude for full biography. Goude’s Hopi Mesa releases document his chamber ensemble work. He composed and directed all four albums; the label appears to have been at least partly his own vehicle during this period, as “Hopi Mesa” appears as co-producer on the Goude releases.

De Anima (1992)

Cat: HMK 006 (cassette), HMCD 006 (CD), 452025. One of the label’s earliest releases.

A 15-track work drawing on pavane, allemande, élégie, Salve Regina, and Libera Me forms. Scored for a mixture of period instruments (viol consort) and modern chamber forces. Produced by Pierre-Yves Roupin and Jean-Paul Darras (mastering/editing).

Open Questions

  • Whether Hopi Mesa was formally founded by Goude or Aubry — the label entity credit “Hopi Mesa” (Discogs ID 3369058) appears as producer but no founding information is in the DB.
  • René Aubry’s film/theatre work context for some releases (e.g., Malabar Princess, Killer Kid) — possible soundtrack connections not fully documented in Discogs credits.
  • Lorenzo Mattotti’s illustration work on 3 releases — Mattotti is a noted Italian graphic novelist/illustrator; his specific contributions to Hopi Mesa aesthetics may be worth documenting if the vault covers visual artists.
