Overview

Antena Krzyku is a Polish independent label and distributor specialising in alternative music — Punk, Post-Punk, Hardcore, and Noise — that also publishes a magazine under the same name. Founded around 1984, it maintained a substantial release schedule through the 1990s before going relatively quiet in the 2000s, then rebounding sharply in the 2010s with over 90 catalogue entries that decade. The 2014 Singles Subscription Club — issuing one limited-edition 7” per month to mark the label’s 30th anniversary — reflects a sustained commitment to physical formats. Its sublabels include C.O.D.E. Records and the anniversary Singles Subscription Club.
