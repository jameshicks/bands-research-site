Overview

Static Caravan is an English independent label founded in 1998 and run by Geoff Dolman, operating across experimental, leftfield, IDM, ambient, and folk rock. Its catalogue was most prolific in the 2000s, with 79 releases in that decade followed by 53 in the 2010s, before tapering to a handful in the 2020s. The VAN numbering sequence is notably inclusive: alongside records it tracks limited-edition merchandise — T-shirts, shot glasses, badges, tote bags, a mug — as formally catalogued items, giving the label a distinctly DIY, community-oriented character. Roster credits centre on Tunng and its members Mike Lindsay and Sam Genders, alongside Ben Javens and Montelimar (2), pointing toward the British folk-electronica scene as a gravitational core.
