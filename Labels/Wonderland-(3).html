Label set up in 1983 by the group Siouxsie & The Banshees. Initial releases were by Banshees related artists The Creatures and The Glove. Releases were manufactured and distributed by Polydor in the UK. First release on the label was (SHE 1) in April 1983, last one was (SHE 23) in February 1995. Overview

Wonderland was a vanity label founded in 1983 by Siouxsie & The Banshees, with manufacturing and distribution handled by Polydor in the UK. It launched with releases from two Banshees side projects — The Creatures (Siouxsie Sioux and Budgie) and The Glove (Steven Severin and Robert Smith) — before serving as the primary home for the Banshees’ own catalogue through the mid-1990s. The label ran a sequential catalogue from SHE 1 to SHE 23, and each physical release carried a distinct variant of the Wonderland logo. Its output was concentrated in the 1980s (28 releases), tapering through the 1990s (12) and into the 2000s (4), where the remaining entries likely reflect reissues and retrospectives.
