Overview

Tandori is a DIY label based in Lille, France, active since 2009. Its catalogue of around 36 releases sits primarily in the 2010s and spans experimental and noise music, art rock, avant-garde, and math rock. The roster centres on a small group of recurring artists including T.Raznor, Jean-Baptiste Rubin, Maria Goretti Quartet, TOC, and Peter Orins.
