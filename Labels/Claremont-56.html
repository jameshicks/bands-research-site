Claremont 56

Discogs ID: 82166
Founded: 2007
Origin: UK
Founder: Paul Murphy (Mudd) (Paul N. Murphy)
Contact: claremontinc@googlemail.com

UK independent label set up by Paul ‘Mudd’ Murphy in 2007. The house aesthetic is Balearic, downtempo, ambient house, and dub-influenced dance music — centred on long, unhurried tracks made for listening or late-night DJ use. Murphy runs the label as an extension of his own output: the flagship act Smith & Mudd was the first release, and Murphy’s various project names (Mudd, Smith & Mudd, Mudd & Pollard) account for a substantial share of the catalog across its 198 releases. The label is notable for hosting a late-career run of material from krautrock pioneer Holger Czukay (17 releases, 2009–2024), extending well beyond his death in 2017 through archival and reissue activity.

Catalog prefix: C56. Active and ongoing as of 2025.

Founder — Paul ‘Mudd’ Murphy

Paul N. Murphy (Discogs IDs 603289 / 15075) is the label’s sole operator, primary producer, designer, and principal artist. He records as Mudd (solo downtempo/ambient house), as one half of Smith & Mudd (with Ben Smith), and as one half of Mudd & Pollard (with Kevin Pollard). His production credit appears across 32 releases; his writer credit across 43. He also handles sleeve design on 27 releases, often using artwork by painter Mark Warrington.

Roster and Key Artists

Smith & Mudd (ID 1110724) — Ben Smith and Paul N. Murphy. The label’s flagship act, debuting on C56CD001 in 2007 and producing 24 releases through 2019. The single “The Start” / “Tanglewood” (C56004, 2008) is the most-pressed release on the label (76 pressings).

Holger Czukay (ID 3480) — German krautrock bassist and composer, CAN co-founder. 17 releases on Claremont 56, 2009–2024, making it his most prolific label in his final decade and beyond. Key release: A Good Morning Story (C56LP02, 2010) — a 2LP ambient/experimental set with a 22-minute centrepiece (“Mirage”). Jaki Liebezeit (CAN drummer) appears on several sessions.

Almunia (ID 2104576) — 11 releases, 2011–2021. Trio of Italian producers; albums New Moon (2011) and Pulsar (C56LP005, 2013).

Bison (4) (ID 1715961) — 10 releases, 2010–2022. Albums include Travellers (C56LP007, 2014).

Hillside (3) (ID 2519416) — 10 releases, 2019–2022. Albums Sunday In June (2021), Alba Sol (2020); Joe Claussell remixed their “Walpole Days” (C56075, 2020).

Mudd & Pollard (ID 1168694) — Murphy with Kevin Pollard. 9 releases 2007–2017; opened the label catalog with Villa Stavros (C56001, 2007 — the first Claremont 56 release).

Hear & Now (ID 5544067) — 9 releases, 2017–2022. Three albums: Aurora Baleare (2018), Alba Sol (2020), Milvus (2021).

Ahmed Fakroun (ID 613821) — Libyan vocalist/musician. Drago (C56011, 2009), co-credited “Ahmed Fakroun / Mudd.”

Fist Of Facts — NYC post-punk/dub group (Salvatore Principato, Ken Caldeira). Fist Of Facts EP (2009). One of the few non-UK acts in the early catalog.
