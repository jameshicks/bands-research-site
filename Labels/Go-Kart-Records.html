Overview

Go-Kart Records is an independent punk and hardcore label based in New York, active since 1991. Its catalogue leans into punk, pop punk, and hardcore, with two peak decades — 36 releases in the 1990s and 53 in the 2000s — before trailing off significantly after 2010. The label operates two associated imprints, Go-Kart Records Europe and High Speed Recordings, and counts Lunachicks and Gina Volpe among its New York–area roster.
