Cougouyou Music

Discogs ID: 1026988
Address: 15 Rue De La Cloche D’Or, 66000 Perpignan, France
Parent label: Staubgold
Active: 2016–present
Catalog range: Cougouyou Music 1 – cougouyou 26 (2016–2025)

French independent label based in Perpignan, in the Pyrénées-Orientales department — the heart of French Catalonia. A sublabel of Staubgold, which relocated from Berlin to Perpignan in 2011 (founded by Markus Detmer). Cougouyou Music launched in 2016 to serve as the local/regional music outlet within that umbrella.

The anchor artist is Pascal Comelade — Catalan-French musician born in Montpellier in 1955, known for cover songs of rock and easy listening standards played on toy piano, singing saw, and other unusual instruments. He accounts for 9 of the label’s releases (2016–2023) and appears on the label’s most high-profile release, Velvet Serenade (2023), a Velvet Underground covers LP with Lee Ranaldo and Ramón Prats.

A recurring feature of the catalog is documenting the Perpignan region’s local underground: the Back From The Canigó compilation series covers the city’s garage punk, freakbeat, country punk and city rock scenes from 1989 to 2010 (volumes in 2019, 2021, and a third collection Perpignan Burning in 2024).

Key Releases

Velvet Serenade (cougouyou 22 / staubgold 169, 2023)

Velvet Underground covers LP recorded by Lee Ranaldo (guitar, vocals), Pascal Comelade (keyboards), and Ramón Prats (drums). Five Lou Reed compositions plus one original by Ranaldo (“Lou’s Blues”). Recorded and mixed by Christian A. Korn; mastered by Raph Dumas; lacquer cut by Kassian Troyer. Executive produced and annotated by Ignacio Julià. Co-released on Staubgold (staubgold 169).

Back From The Canigó series

Regional documentary series capturing Perpignan’s underground scenes. Vol. 1 (cougouyou 11, 2019): garage punks and freakbeat mods, 1989–1999. Vol. 2 (cougouyou 19, 2021): country punks and city rockers, 1999–2010. Perpignan Burning (cougouyou 24, 2024) extends the series. The Canigou (Catalan: Canigó) is a peak in the eastern Pyrenees, a symbol of Catalan identity.

Le Plan De Paris (cougouyou 16, 2020)

Collaboration between Pascal Comelade and Richard Pinhas — the Heldon founder and philosopher-student (studied under Gilles Deleuze and Jean-François Lyotard).

Carré Noir Sur Fond Noir (Cougouyou Music 1, 2016)

Label debut, co-produced by Armand Miralles and Pascal Comelade. Miralles on electric guitars; Comelade on EMS/AKS synthesizer and instruments. Design by Marie Destandau; mastered by Raph Dumas.

Pascal Comelade at Cougouyou

Comelade’s Cougouyou releases represent a late-career chapter; his main discography runs from 1979 (debut Séquences Païennes on Parasite) through dozens of albums on Les Disques Du Soleil Et De L’Acier, Delabel, and Because Music. His Bel Canto Orchestra — a seven-piece ensemble built around toy instruments — appears on the catalog too (cougouyou 13, cougouyou 25). Members of Bel Canto Orchestra include Mark Cunningham (DNA/no-wave), Pierre Bastien, Cathy Claret, and Victor Nubla.

Label Context

Cougouyou Music shares an address city with Staubgold (both in Perpignan). Markus Detmer relocated Staubgold there in 2011 after years in Cologne and Berlin. The name “Cougouyou” is regional Catalan-French dialect; Perpignan sits in Occitan/Catalan territory. The Limiñanas — garage-psych husband-and-wife duo from Cabestany (just outside Perpignan) — appear on the very first year of the catalog (cougouyou 3, 2016).
