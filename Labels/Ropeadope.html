Overview

Ropeadope is a US independent record label rooted in Ropeadope Records, which was founded in 1999; the current entity emerged around 2004, operating under Ropeadope LLC and later Ropeadope Music Entertainment LLC on earlier releases. CEO Louis Marks has steered the label toward contemporary jazz, fusion, and jazz-rock, with sublabels AfricArise and Ropeadope Select broadening the imprint’s scope. Its catalogue of roughly 85 selected releases is weighted toward the 2010s (41 releases) and remains active into the 2020s (31 releases), with roster artists including Christian Scott, Michael League, Mark De Clive-Lowe, Lawrence Fields, and Weedie Braimah among its recurring names.
