A&M Records

Major independent label founded by Herb Alpert and Jerry Moss. Distributed through major networks; home to a wide range of rock, pop, and singer-songwriter acts.

Jeff Gold served in A&R and then art direction at A&M through most of the 1980s (c. 1982–1990), art directing releases by Suzanne Vega, John Hiatt, Bryan Adams, Robyn Hitchcock, the Neville Brothers, Split Enz, and many others.

The A Very Special Christmas franchise originated on A&M (vol. 1, 1987).
