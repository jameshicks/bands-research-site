Les Disques Du Soleil Et De L’Acier

Discogs ID: 11635
French independent record label; important early European home for God Is My Co-Pilot and other avant-garde/experimental acts. 180 releases documented on Discogs. Has two sublabels (Le Soleil Et L’Acier SARL, Editions Le Soleil Et L’Acier).

Overview

Based in France, the label released GodCo’s I Am Not This Body (DSA 54236), Speed Yr Trip (DSA 54237), Sex Is For Making Babies (CDSA 54033), and a French edition of Puss 02 (c-dSA 54041). The label was associated with Pascal Blua and was an important conduit for downtown NYC experimental music in Europe during the early-to-mid 1990s.
