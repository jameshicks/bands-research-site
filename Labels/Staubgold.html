Staubgold

Jul 26, 20264 min read

Staubgold

Discogs ID: 3510
Label Code: LC 10252
Address (current): 11 rue des Lices, 66000 Perpignan, France

Independent German/French experimental label founded 1998 by Markus Detmer in Cologne. Specialises in electroacoustic, microsound, sound art, and avant-rock — loosely orbiting the late-1990s German electronic underground (Force Inc / Mille Plateaux milieu) while maintaining strong Krautrock and noise-rock connections. Relocated Cologne → Berlin 2003, then Berlin → Perpignan, France 2011. 309 releases in Discogs.

History

Detmer, a musician (member of Klangwart and Amrol) and journalist, launched Staubgold in Cologne in 1998. The label’s earliest releases centred on the nascent German microsound/turntablist scene; catalog #5, Institut Für Feinmotorik’s Wenig Information: Kein Titel (1998), exemplifies the founding aesthetic — turntables and found objects (rubber bands, cardboard, adhesive tape, wire, miscellaneous detritus) treated as instruments.

The roster expanded through the early 2000s to include Ekkehard Ehlers (15 releases, 2001–2012), Klangwart itself (14 releases, 2008–2019), Mapstation (12 releases, 2000–2009), Reuber (11 releases, 2000–2023), and Kammerflimmer Kollektief (20 releases, 2003–2015) — the label’s most prolific relationship.

International scope developed early: Rafael Toral (Lisbon; 9 releases, 2001–2017), Oren Ambarchi (5 releases), Dälek (3 releases), Keith Rowe, No-Neck Blues Band, and Lee Ranaldo. The label also hosted Faust (6 releases), bridging the classic Krautrock generation with the post-digital scene.

In 2009, Alec Empire curated Rauschgold (staubgold 92), a DJ compilation drawing entirely from the Staubgold back-catalog, functioning as a label retrospective.

Key Release Notes

Patchwork 1971–2002 — staubgold 37 (2002)

Faust retrospective spanning the band’s original Polydor era through the 1990s reactivation. Markus Detmer served as compilation/executive producer; Hans Joachim Irmler co-produced, mastered, and edited. Featured the full extended Faust lineup: Irmler, Jean-Hervé Peron, Werner Diermaier, Arnulf Meifert, Gunther Wüsthoff, Rudolf Sosna, Lars Paukstat, Michael Stoll, Steven Wray Lobdell.

Derbe Respect, Alder — staubgold 50 (2004)

Collaborative album between Faust (Irmler, Diermaier, Paukstat, Stoll, Steven Wray Lobdell) and US hip-hop act Dälek (MC Dälek + Oktopus). Co-produced by Klangbad (Irmler’s label) and Staubgold. Liner notes by Dälek and Ralf Bei Der Kellen. Engineering credited to Irmler Productions; sound work by Bruno Gebhard.

Rauschgold: Alec Empire Plays Staubgold — staubgold 92 (2009)

Alec Empire (Atari Teenage Riot) compiled, mixed, and edited material from across the Staubgold catalog. Detmer as compilation producer; mastered by Kassian Troyer. Functions as an unofficial label retrospective.

Label Founder

Markus Detmer (Discogs ID 503864) — musician and journalist. Member of Klangwart (formed with Timo Reuber, Cologne 1996) and Amrol and Detmerkugel. Served as compilation/executive producer on multiple Staubgold releases including Patchwork 1971–2002 and Rauschgold.

Connections

  • Franz Hautzinger — Austrian quartertone trumpeter; Soundchambers (with Ekkehard Ehlers and Joseph Suchy, 2004) and sideman credit on A Life Without Fear (2006)
  • Mille Plateaux / Force Inc — Ekkehard Ehlers worked at the Force Inc. Frankfurt office; Staubgold shares aesthetic DNA with the German microsound milieu
  • Faust — 6 releases; Staubgold served as a home for the band’s archival and new work from 2002
  • Klangbad — Hans Joachim Irmler’s label; co-produced staubgold 50
  • Jaki Liebezeit — Can drummer; collaborated with Holger Mertin on staubgold 139
