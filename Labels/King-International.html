Overview

King International is a Japanese distributor imprint associated with King Records, functioning as a co-label on Japanese pressings of recordings licensed from European sources including CGD, Versailles, and Music. Active since at least 1959, the catalogue leans heavily on vocal, chanson, and ballad material in the 1960s — twelve releases in that decade alone — before a long gap and a resumption from the 1990s onward that brought in hardcore and punk through licensed releases by Black Flag, Greg Ginn, Henry Rollins, and Bill Stevenson. The label operates as a licensing conduit rather than an originating imprint, bridging continental European pop and American hardcore for the Japanese market across six decades.
