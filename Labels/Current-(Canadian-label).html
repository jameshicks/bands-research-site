Current (Canadian label)

Canadian independent record label based in Toronto. Discogs label ID: 34577. Parent entity: Current Records Ltd. (Discogs ID: 856759).

Overview

Current was a Toronto-based independent label founded in 1982 by Gerry Young (Discogs ID: 1007615), Joseph Fodor, and Brian Keevil. It operated under several related corporate names — Current Records Ltd., Current Musical Enterprises Inc., Current Records and Management — with copyrights frequently assigned to a related entity, The Current Entertainment Corp. (Discogs ID: 353444). The label’s parent registration on Discogs is Current Records Ltd. (ID: 856759); sublabels include Current Musical Enterprises Inc. (ID: 353444), Current Sounds Limited (ID: 277671), and The Current Entertainment Corp. (ID: 512620).

The label’s founding anchor acts were Martha And The Muffins / M + M and The Parachute Club, both of whom launched their current-era catalogs on Current in 1983. From the outset Current occupied a specific niche in the Canadian new-wave/synth-pop/world-beat space of the early 1980s, releasing records that were distributed and co-released through RCA Canada and later PolyGram Canada for international reach.

Gerry Young, one of the three founders, is credited on The Parachute Club’s debut album as their manager, confirming that Current operated as both a label and a management entity for its primary acts — a common arrangement for Canadian independents of the period.

The label’s most visible international release was M + M’s Mystery Walk (1984, WAVE 3), and the most commercially notable was The Parachute Club’s debut LP The Parachute Club (1983, WAVE 2). Daniel Lanois co-produced many of the M+M and Martha And The Muffins sessions during this period.

Active years on Discogs span 1983 to 2019 (with a dormant gap between the early 1990s and a resumption in 2017–2019 for three Canadian releases). The main operational period was 1983–1991.

Catalog Number System

Current used at least three distinct catalog series, likely corresponding to format:

  • WAVE — LP albums (e.g. WAVE 1, WAVE 2, WAVE 3…)
  • WASH — 12” singles (e.g. WASH-1, WASH-2, WASH-3…)
  • WAKE — 7” singles (e.g. WAKE-1, WAKE-2, WAKE-3…)
  • WIND — alternate LP or pressing variant (e.g. WIND-1, WIND-2, WIND-3)
  • CRL / CRD / CRT — later catalog series, used into the late 1980s and early 1990s

Production / Personnel Recurring Credits

Daniel Lanois appears as producer or co-producer on three of the label’s earliest and most prominent releases: Danseparc (WAVE 1, 1983), The Parachute Club LP (WAVE 2, 1983), and Cooling The Medium (WAKE-8/WASH-5, 1984). His involvement in the Current orbit predates his international profile (Eno’s The Unforgettable Fire, 1984) and represents some of the best-documented early evidence of his production work in Canada.

Gerry Young — co-founder; credited as management on The Parachute Club debut LP (1983). Discogs artist ID: 1007615.

Distribution / International Releases

Discogs data shows several Current-labeled releases appearing with different catalog prefixes suggesting distribution or co-release arrangements:

  • Danseparc appeared on RCA in France (PL 37756, 1983) and under WAVE 1 in Canada — indicating RCA handled European/international distribution for the M+M/Martha And The Muffins releases.
  • Mystery Walk appeared in Europe/UK on PK 70246 (1984) with no separate label listed other than Current.
  • The Parachute Club LP appeared on KKL1-0553 (Canada, 1984) — an RCA Canada catalog number — alongside the WAVE 2 pressing, and on 5743-2-R / 5743-4-R (US, RCA/BMG, 1986 reissue).
  • Alta Moda appeared in Germany with catalog CUCD 9.00984 O (1990) as did The Distance Between (Strange Advance), The Black Earth, Human Sexuality (Mystery Romance), and Uptown (Machinations), all with CUCD series numbers — indicating a German distribution deal, likely PolyGram or a sublicense, for the late 1980s catalog.

Open Questions

  • Joseph Fodor and Brian Keevil (co-founders with Gerry Young) have no Discogs artist entries — their roles at the label beyond the founding are undocumented in the DB.
  • The precise distribution deal structure (RCA Canada for M+M/Martha And The Muffins, the CUCD German series for the late 1980s roster) — names of the distribution companies and contract dates are not in the Discogs data.
  • Gerry Young’s broader career — whether he managed other acts beyond The Parachute Club and whether he had prior label experience — is not determinable from Discogs alone.
  • Cooling The Medium is listed as a 12”/7” single in the DB; it is not a full album, despite the brief’s reference to it as if it might be (the 1984 Mystery Walk LP contains the song “Cooling The Medium” — the single is a remix-only release of that track).
  • Alta Moda, Strange Advance, Doubledare, Mystery Romance, The Black Earth, and Machinations are all on the roster but have not been researched in depth in this vault.
