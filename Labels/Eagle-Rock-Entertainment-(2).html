Overview

Eagle Rock Entertainment (2) is a Discogs label entry covering unofficial releases that falsely carry the Eagle Rock Entertainment imprint without authorization. The catalogue runs from 1998 through the 2010s, with the bulk of its roughly 288 credited releases concentrated in the 2000s and 2010s. Styles span Pop Rock, Hard Rock, Heavy Metal, Synth-pop, and Alternative Rock — a range that mirrors the genuine Eagle Rock Entertainment’s catalogue of legacy rock and pop titles.
