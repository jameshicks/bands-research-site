Modern Recording

Discogs Label ID: 309048 Type: Recording studio / small label imprint Location: Chapel Hill, North Carolina, USA Owner / Operator: Chris Stamey Phone: 919.929.5008 Website: chrisstamey.com Active (as Discogs credit): 1996–present

Overview

Modern Recording is the Chapel Hill, North Carolina recording studio owned and operated by Chris Stamey (guitarist/vocalist/producer, The dB’s). On Discogs, studios are registered as labels, so Modern Recording carries label ID 309048 and surfaces in release credits as “Recorded At,” “Mixed At,” “Produced At,” “Mastered At,” or “Engineered At.” The imprint also served as an official label for a small number of releases, most notably Stamey’s 2021 collaboration A Brand-New Shade Of Blue with The Fellow Travelers.

According to notes in Chris Stamey’s vault page, the studio was founded around 1996, reportedly with encouragement from producer Scott Litt, and is sited in a century-old house approximately one mile from Stamey’s birthplace in Chapel Hill. It functions as Stamey’s primary production facility and as the venue for his mixing and arranging work for outside clients.

The Discogs company-credit footprint spans 1996–2013 (the range covered by the database), with the heaviest activity 1997–2009. Over that window the studio appears on more than 150 release entries — counting all pressings and formats — representing a concentrated body of work in the Chapel Hill / Triangle alt-country, indie rock, and singer-songwriter scenes.

Client / Artist Connections

Artists who recorded or mixed at Modern Recording, grouped by relationship:

Core Chapel Hill / Triangle scene (Stamey as full producer):

  • Whiskeytown — Ryan Adams’s alt-country band; Stamey mixed bonus tracks on expanded Faithless Street reissue
  • Chatham County Line — Stamey co-produced self-titled debut
  • The Mayflies USA — Stamey produced, recorded, mixed Summertown (1999)
  • The Backsliders (2) — early client; mixed at Modern Recording (1996)

National clients:

  • Le Tigre — Kathleen Hanna / Johanna Fateman / Sadie Benning; Stamey co-produced debut LP and played on it
  • Alejandro Escovedo — two albums (Bourbonitis Blues, A Man Under The Influence); the studio’s most sustained outside relationship in the data
  • Sue Garner & Rick Brown — NYC-based no-wave-adjacent duo; Still (2000)
  • The Rosebuds — Durham, NC duo; Loud Planes Fly Low (2011)
  • American Aquarium — NC alt-country band; Dances For The Lonely (2009)

Mastering / mixing clients (post-production only):

  • Various NC acts appear in the Discogs data with Modern Recording listed only as Mastered At or Mixed At, without Stamey personally credited by name.

Connections

  • Chris Stamey — owner and operator; producer/engineer/mixer for virtually all sessions
  • The dB’s — Stamey’s primary band; the studio’s founding extends his post-dB’s production career
  • Mitch Easter — fellow Chapel Hill veteran; occasional co-engineer; both were in Sneakers (12) before the dB’s, and both in Big Star’s Third
  • Caitlin Cary — Whiskeytown alumna; recurring session performer at Modern Recording (violin, vocals) and contributed artwork to We Are Not For Sale
  • Ryan Adams — Whiskeytown; connected through Faithless Street sessions
  • Alejandro Escovedo — recurring client; two full albums produced at the studio
  • Le Tigre — Stamey co-produced debut LP; notable for bridging Chapel Hill and NYC riot-grrrl scenes
  • Chatham County Line — self-titled debut produced here
  • The Rosebuds — Durham, NC; Loud Planes Fly Low
  • Scott Litt — reportedly encouraged Stamey to open the studio circa 1996
