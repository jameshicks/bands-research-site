Illuminated Records

Based: UK (London; label founder from Manchester) Active: c. 1980–1987 Discogs ID: 4290 Trading as: Illuminated Productions Ltd.

Overview

Illuminated Records was founded by Nigel Bagley, a Manchester music-scene figure who had worked alongside promoter Alan Wise at Rafters and The Russell Club (1978–1985) and subsequently managed Nico. He moved into record production in London and launched Illuminated as what one contemporary source describes as “an adventurous little label.” He later left the music industry entirely for a corporate career at Unilever. [source: Manchester Digital Music Archive, https://www.mdmarchive.co.uk/artefact/34617/Nico-Rafters-Russell-Club-Illuminated-Records-Nigel-Bagley-Photograph-2023, photograph/interview dated 2023]

The label operated as Illuminated Productions Ltd. and funded or distributed subsidiary/affiliated labels including Criminal Damage Records and S/Phonograph (Sexual Phonograph). [source: Discogs label page, https://www.discogs.com/label/4290-Illuminated-Records — noted in aggregated search results]

The label was active from approximately 1980 through to at least 1987 (the year of 23 Skidoo’s Just Like Everybody compilation on the Bleeding Chin imprint, which Illuminated released). Its catalog spans post-punk, goth, industrial, EBM, and avant-garde electronic music — a genuinely eclectic spread for an independent of its size.

Curatorial logic

No documented A&R philosophy has surfaced in available sources. The roster (see below) suggests opportunistic eclecticism rather than a single guiding aesthetic: gothic rock (Sex Gang Children), avant-percussion (23 Skidoo), industrial electronics (Portion Control, 400 Blows, Dormannu, Data), post-punk (Normil Hawaiians), and licensed European electronic material (DAF). The Normil Hawaiians connection is complicated by reported distribution problems — Illuminated apparently failed to pay bills, which meant More Wealth Than Money (1982) was distributed in Europe but not the UK. [source: Normil Hawaiians fan/band site, https://normilhawaiians.com/ and aggregated search results]
