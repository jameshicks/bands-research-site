Numero Group

Discogs ID: 62008
Founded: 2003, Chicago, IL
Address: 2533 S Troy St., Chicago IL 60623 (formerly 2348 S. Marshall Blvd.)
Parent: Secretly Group (ID 1051624)
Catalog size: ~2,249 releases (Discogs), 32 sublabels

Chicago archival and reissue label founded 2003 by Tom Lunt, Rob Sevier, and Ken Shipley. Specializes in privately issued, out-of-print, and overlooked recordings across soul, funk, gospel, folk, psychedelia, and post-punk — typically issued in heavily researched box sets and compilations with extensive liner notes. Absorbed Tiger Style and has become the primary home for canonical indie post-rock reissues (Karate, Codeine, Unwound, Duster, Hüsker Dü).

Founding and Identity

Ken Shipley had been working as A&R manager at Rykodisc when Rob Sevier conceived the idea for what became Eccentric Soul: The Capsoul Label. After Shipley lost his Rykodisc job in 2003, he crossed paths with Sevier and the two recast the concept as a direct-to-consumer CD label focused on rediscovering lost and forgotten music. Tom Lunt — a former VP/creative director at an advertising agency (through 2000; DDB Warsaw 2001) — provided approximately $23,000 in initial capital. The label started in a Bucktown basement in 2003, later moving to 2348 S. Marshall Blvd (Little Village), then to its current address at 2533 S. Troy St.

Shipley also came to the venture with an Antena reissue in hand from his Rykodisc days — this became Camino Del Sol (002, 2004), the second catalog release.

The first LP was Eccentric Soul: The Capsoul Label (001, 2004) — a compilation of recordings from the Capsoul label, founded in Columbus, Ohio by William Roger “Bill” Moss. It established the label’s hallmark: meticulous documentation of obscure regional recordings with premium packaging. The founders modeled their approach on Soul Jazz Records and Honest Jon’s.

Early operating rule: releases had to be “old enough to drink” — at least 21 years old. This was later relaxed, eventually allowing original recordings (most notably Duster’s Together, 2022, their first album in two decades).

The label has since won and been nominated for multiple Grammy Awards specifically for packaging and liner notes quality. The Syl Johnson: Complete Mythology booklet contains a 35,000-word essay.

Ken Shipley had previously founded his own label (Discogs ID 42747, 1995–2000) and is a member of the band September (10). Rob Sevier also records under the alias “Uncle Rob.”

Business History

  • 2003: Founded in Bucktown basement, Chicago
  • 2004: First LP release (Eccentric Soul: The Capsoul Label)
  • 2013: Partnership with Secretly Canadian (later Secretly Label Group / Secretly Group) — distribution and investment; announced June 2013. By this point catalog was ~150 releases with 3 Grammy nominations
  • 2017: Jackie Shane: Any Other Way (067) — Grammy nominated for Best Historical Album; confirmed Shane’s identity publicly for the first time
  • 2019: Duster full catalog reissued (Stratosphere, Contemporary Movement, Capsule Losing Contact box)
  • 2021: I Shall Wear A Crown (Pastor T.L. Barrett, 5LP box) — culmination of 12+ year working relationship begun 2008
  • 2021–22: Full Karate back-catalog reissued (900 series)
  • 2024: Tiger Style partnership announced April 2, 2024 — 40+ albums, ~12 EPs absorbed into Numero ecosystem; grew from Ida reunion at Numero Twenty (20th anniversary festival, 2023)
  • 2024: Brick-and-mortar record store opened at 2533 S. Troy St., Little Village; offices also in Los Angeles and London
