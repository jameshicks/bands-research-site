Overview

Brain (2) is not a genuine record company but a Discogs cataloguing label grouping counterfeit pressings of releases originally issued on Brain, the real German label. The fakes began surfacing in the mid-1990s and continued through the 2020s, with the bulk concentrated in the 2010s; 53 releases are logged against 43 individual credits. Artists affected include key names from Brain’s original Krautrock and hard-rock roster — among them Eroc, Conny Plank, Grobschnitt, and Accept — spanning styles from Krautrock and Prog Rock through Heavy Metal and Experimental.
