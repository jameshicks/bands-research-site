Overview

No Front Teeth Records is a London-based DIY punk rock label founded in 2001, with releases beginning in 2002. Its catalogue spans punk, power pop, garage rock, post-punk, and hardcore, growing most heavily through the 2010s (163 releases) and continuing actively into the 2020s (over 80 releases). The roster includes The Gaggers, Brandy Row, Marco NFT, Daniel Husayn, and Marco Palumbo, with the label accumulating nearly 860 credits across 268 catalogued releases — making it one of the more prolific independent punk operations out of the UK.
