Anchor & Hope

Independent record label founded in 1996 by Mick Turner of Dirty Three. Based in Brunswick North, Melbourne, Victoria, Australia (P.O. Box 5042). Releases are sometimes credited as “An Anchor & Hope Production.” The label functions primarily as the home for Dirty Three’s catalog and Mick Turner’s solo work, but has also released records by a small number of associated Australian artists and licensed/co-released titles for international distribution.

Discogs label ID: 39862. Sublabel: Anchor & Hope Records (Discogs ID: 376207).

Overview

Anchor & Hope was established the same year as Dirty Three’s first major international breakout — Horse Stories (1996) was the label’s inaugural release. The label has operated intermittently across three decades, with long gaps between releases, functioning more as a self-releasing vehicle than an active roster-building operation. Distribution and co-release arrangements have varied by era: Shock (Australia) handled domestic distribution in the early 2000s; Touch And Go handled North American release of Cinder (2005); Remote Control handled Australasia for Love Changes Everything (2024).

The archive label at the web address (now preserved via Wayback Machine) was anchorandhope.com.

Catalog Number Scheme

Primary scheme: AH prefix + sequential number + format suffix (CD, LP). Earlier vinyl-only releases used AHX prefix. Later releases drop the format suffix (AH010, AH2012-1, AH2024-1LP). The sublabel Anchor & Hope Records used AH numbers in the same sequence (AH004CD) and also ANHLP for vinyl.

Distribution & Co-Release Arrangements

  • Australia domestic (c. 2000): Shock (2) — credited on Whatever You Love, You Are (AH006CD)
  • USA & Canada (2005): Touch And Go co-released Cinder as TG293/tg293cd
  • Australia & New Zealand (2024): Remote Control co-released Love Changes Everything (AH2024-1LP)
  • Japan (2024): Love Changes Everything pressed as PCD-25405 (separate Japanese label)

Key Artists

Dirty Three

The label’s anchor act. Eight studio albums, two EPs, a live album, and a reissue are credited to the label. Members: Mick Turner (guitar, bass, organ, artwork), Warren Ellis (violin, viola, piano, mandolin), Jim White (2) (drums). Recurring engineer/mixer: Casey Rice.

Mick Turner

Solo debut Tren Phantasma (1997, AH002CD) is the only Mick Turner solo release on the label in the Discogs catalog.

Conway Savage & Suzie Higgie

Soon Will Be Tomorrow (1998, AH004CD) — Australian artists, released on the Anchor & Hope Records sublabel. Conway Savage is also known for work with Nick Cave. Produced and mixed by Matt Crosbie and Suzie Higgie.

Corrupt Fruit

Lessons In Love (1998, AH005CD) — art rock album, performers include Dave Richards, Narelle Geerckens, Ruzica Dujmovic. Only release in the Discogs catalog; Corrupt Fruit’s only known label is Anchor & Hope.

River City Extension

New Jersey folk-rock group. Two releases appear on the label: a split 7” with Kevin Devine (2011, no cat#) and Deliverance (2015, ANH007). Their primary label is Asbestos Records. The connection to Anchor & Hope is not documented in Discogs.

Squeeze (2)

UK new wave/pop group. Live At The Fillmore (2012, ANHLP03) appears on the Anchor & Hope Records sublabel. No obvious Australian or Dirty Three connection; likely a one-off licensing arrangement.
