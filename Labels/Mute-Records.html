Mute Records

Discogs ID: 26391 (as “Mute”; parent: Mute Artists Ltd. ID 298991)
From: London, England
Active: 1978–present
Founded by: Daniel Miller

London independent label founded by Daniel Miller in 1978 with his own single “Warm Leatherette” as The Normal. Long-term home of Depeche Mode, Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds, Erasure, Yazoo, Moby, and many others. Sold to EMI in 2003; Miller bought it back in 2010 and relaunched as an independent. A Certain Ratio signed to Mute in 2018, releasing ACR:MCR (2019) and continuing active.

Key post-punk and indie artists on the label include These Immortal Souls (two studio albums, 1987 and 1992), Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds, Crime & The City Solution, Barry Adamson, The Birthday Party, Fad Gadget, and many others.

Laibach have been on Mute since 1987 (Opus Dei, Macbeth, Kapital, NATO, Spectre, The Future, Alamut, and many more) — one of the label’s longest-running relationships, continuing through the EMI years and the 2010 re-independence to the present.

Fever Ray Connection

Karin Dreijer’s Fever Ray project moved to Mute for Radical Romantics (2022, cat. MTE-1110-1; co-released with Rabid Records) and subsequent releases: I’m Not Done (Therapy Session) (2025) and The Year Of The Radical Romantics (2025).
