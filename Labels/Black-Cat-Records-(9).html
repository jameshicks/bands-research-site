Overview

Black Cat Records (9) is a New York City CDR bootleg label that issues releases with color-copied artwork, nearly all of which share a fake UPC code (5 419990 000498) lifted from an existing Beach Boys silver CD bootleg. Its catalogue covers classic and alternative rock, punk, and psychedelic rock, with artists including Love, Motorpsycho, and the Minutemen. Despite a first appearance in 1997, the label’s output surged in the 2010s — accounting for 16 of its roughly 20 listed releases — with a handful more in the early 2020s. The listed address (420 Lanquidity Drive, New York) appears to be fictitious.
