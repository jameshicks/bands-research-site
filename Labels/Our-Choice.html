From Discogs

German label active in a short period 1983 and then reformed in 1991 under Rough Trade Records GmbH after the takeover by Pinnacle UK. In July 1999, Rough Trade Germany GmbH became Zomba Records GmbH and Our Choice moved along with the new branding.

In 2002, BMG purchased the entire Zomba Group and Zomba Records GmbH was integrated into the German BMG office. Our Choice largely disappeared after that.

The catalog# scheme is RTD xxx.xxxx.x The two additional digits appearing on the sleeve are the price code, don’t add them to the catalog#.

— Discogs label profile, ID 7682

Overview

Our Choice is a German label with a brief founding run in 1983 before being revived in 1991 under the Rough Trade Records GmbH umbrella. Its catalogue is overwhelmingly concentrated in the 1990s — 118 of 137 releases — spanning industrial, EBM, experimental, and alternative rock, with roster names including Die Krupps, Jürgen Engler, Ralf Dörper, and Blixa Bargeld. When Rough Trade Germany was rebranded as Zomba Records GmbH in July 1999 following a Pinnacle UK takeover, Our Choice moved with it; the label largely disappeared after BMG acquired the entire Zomba Group in 2002 and folded the German operation into its own offices.
