Overview

Club AC30 is a London-based record label and live music promoter run by Robin Allport, Nick Allport, and Duncan Jones, operating since 2004 around a focus on shoegaze, dream pop, post-rock, and electronica. Alongside the label they run regular London club nights spotlighting noisy guitar acts and dedicated DJs. The catalogue is heavily weighted to the 2010s (67 of roughly 98 selected releases), with a logical numbering system that encodes the release year and format directly in the catalogue number. Roster highlights include international acts Pinkshinyultrablast (Russia), Flyying Colours (Australia), and Ringo Deathstarr (USA), plus sub-imprints As & When 7″ Series and Never Lose That Feeling.
