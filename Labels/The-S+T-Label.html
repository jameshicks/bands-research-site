The S+T Label

Discogs ID: 64882
Origin: Leicester, England, UK
Active: c. 1979–1982
URL (archive): http://punkygibbon.co.uk/labels/sandtrecords.html

Overview

Small independent label run as a sideline alongside full-time jobs by four Leicester-area individuals. The operating model was straightforward: bands financed their own recording; the label covered production and pressing costs; profits split 50/50. The label also produced two associated publications: 0533 (1980–1981) and Biggles up The Fosse (1981–1982).

No engineer, producer, or sleeve credits appear in Discogs for STAB 1. Of the twelve acts, only The Deep Freeze Mice had a substantial independent career before and after the compilation (ten LPs, 1979–1989). New Age (5) went on to release two singles and an LP on Dining Out Records (1981–1982). The Amber Squad also had a subsequent career on Detour Records and Queen Mum Records. Most other acts appear nowhere else in Discogs.

The S+T Singles Collection (ST 1–3, 1981)

A bundle of the three S+T singles (ST 1, ST 2, ST3) packaged together; Discogs ID 29237269; format: 3× 7”, 45 RPM. No additional production credits beyond those on the individual releases. Treated as a distinct release entry in Discogs rather than a master compilation.

Publications

  • 0533 — label-associated publication, 1980–1981. (0533 was Leicester’s telephone area code at the time.)
  • Biggles up The Fosse — label-associated publication, 1981–1982. (The Fosse Way is the Roman road running through Leicestershire.)

These are referenced in background sources but no Discogs entries exist for them as releases.

Connections

  • Chris Freer — co-founder
  • Paul Betts — artwork co-founder; co-ran Terminally Blitzed fanzine with Freer
  • Kev Reverb — co-founder; went on to run Stayfree Studio / Memphis Studio; member of Crazyhead and The Swinging Laurels
  • Mick Dawkeye — co-founder; photographer
  • The Deep Freeze Mice — most prominent act on STAB 1; ran own labels Mole Embalming Records and Cordelia Records
  • New Age — from Groby (western Leicester); moved to Dining Out Records after the compilation
  • Dining Out Records — Leicester-adjacent label (Dave Henderson) that signed New Age after STAB 1

Open Questions

  • No engineer or producer is credited anywhere in Discogs for STAB 1. Were the tracks recorded at multiple studios, or centrally at Stayfree Studio / Memphis Studio (Kev Reverb’s Leicester facility)?
  • Paul Betts’s Discogs presence: the label profile spells his name “Paul Lacia-Betts” — is this a middle name, a stage usage, or a data error?
  • The publications 0533 and Biggles up The Fosse — no physical copies or further descriptions traced in Discogs.
  • Future Toys member names: the ST3 single credits only surnames (Boyle, Comley, Cullen) as songwriters. No full member list exists in Discogs.
  • Were there any unreleased recordings or further planned catalog entries beyond ST3?
  • The roster entry for artist_id 194 (“Various”) with two releases in 1981 covers both STAB 1 and the Singles Collection box — no additional unlisted comp.
