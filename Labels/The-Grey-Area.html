Overview

The Grey Area is a Mute Records sublabel established in 1989 and dedicated to reissues. Its catalogue peaks in the 1990s (61 releases) and draws heavily from Krautrock, Experimental, Industrial, and Ambient material — Can and its individual members (Holger Czukay, Irmin Schmidt, Michael Karoli, Jaki Liebezeit) are the most prominent names on the roster. Output continued at a lower pace through the 2000s and 2010s, with a handful of releases in the 2020s. The label carries a sublabel, Golden Hammer, and is occasionally credited in full as “The Grey Area of Mute Records Ltd.”
