Overview

Cobblestone was a New York City jazz label founded around April 1972 by Joe Fields, operating as a subsidiary of Buddah Records with the associated Cobblestone Jazz Series sublabel. Its roster drew predominantly from Prestige alumni — a pipeline that shaped its Post Bop and Soul-Jazz identity — and many of those same artists later followed Fields when he moved on to Muse Records. The catalogue is concentrated in the 1970s (37 releases) with a smaller body of earlier material, spanning styles from Post Bop and Soul-Jazz through Funk and Rhythm & Blues. Producers Don Schlitten and Paul Goodman, along with photographer Chuck Stewart, were among the key behind-the-scenes figures associated with the label.
