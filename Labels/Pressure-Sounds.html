Pressure Sounds

UK specialist reggae reissue label based in London (Beatback Ltd., PO Box 12757, London E8). Discogs label ID: 2410. Over 1,095 releases on Discogs as of 2025. Active from 1995 to the present.

Overview

Pressure Sounds began in the mid-1990s as a subsidiary of Adrian Sherwood’s On-U Sound label, initially coordinated and run by Pete Holdsworth (Discogs artist ID: 389258). It subsequently became a self-supporting independent operation. The label’s primary mission is to restore, remaster, compile, and reissue classic Jamaican roots reggae, rocksteady, ska, and dub recordings — often sourcing material that had been commercially unavailable for decades — accompanied by detailed archival sleeve notes. It is not exclusively a reissue operation: some releases draw on master recordings believed lost or forgotten, and the label has also issued original productions.

The catalog is structured around a series of catalogue number prefixes: PSLP (LP reissues), PSCD (CD), PSS (7-inch singles), PSTI (12-inch singles), and PSB (box sets). Runs now extend well past catalogue number 115+.

Pete Holdsworth is the central figure behind virtually every Pressure Sounds release, appearing in Discogs credits on well over 230 releases in roles including Compiled By, Coordinator, Reissue Producer, Project Manager, and Liner Notes. Diggory Kenrick (ID: 1205059) is a frequent collaborator credited as annotator and sleeve notes writer, and has also appeared as a recording artist on original Pressure Sounds productions from 2015 onwards in partnership with dub musicians including Jah Wobble.

Note: the brief co-credits Adrian Sherwood and Steve Barrow as co-founders. Discogs data supports Sherwood’s early foundational role (as the On-U Sound parent from which Pressure Sounds emerged), but Steve Barrow is a member of the rival reissue label Blood & Fire (Discogs 5624) and has only minimal credits on Pressure Sounds releases (one sleeve notes credit, 2013). The “co-founder” attribution for Barrow is not confirmed by Discogs evidence and may be conflation with Blood & Fire. Sherwood’s direct credits on Pressure Sounds releases are likewise sparse — the label’s practical operation was Holdsworth’s domain from inception.

The label’s heaviest artist concentration is the Yabby You / Prophets catalogue — across 89+ releases as primary artist, plus dozens more where Yabby You appears as featured or producer. This represents the most thorough archival excavation of any single artist on the label.

The Bunny Lee production universe — covering The Aggrovators, Tommy McCook, Prince Jammy, and King Tubby as mixing engineer — forms a second major cluster, traceable across dub albums such as Dubbing In The Back Yard (King Tubby / The Aggrovators, 2017) and multiple singles.

Sublabels

Green Tea (ID: 2411)

Founded 1997 as a spin-off from On-U Sound; later run by Pete Holdsworth. Now inactive. 12 confirmed releases. Green Tea occupied a different niche from the parent label — contemporary and crossover acts rather than vintage Jamaican reissues. Key releases:

  • Universe Crew — What’s Inside Your Afro? (GTT 001 / GTCD001, 1997)
  • Headfake — Cycles (GTT 002, 1997)
  • Audio Active / Universe Crew — What’s Inside Your Afro? (GTT 003, 1998) — collaboration
  • Dry & Heavy — One Punch (GTCD002, 1999); Full Contact (2000); In The Jaws Of The Tiger feat. King Jammy (GTCD004, 2000); New Creation (GTCD005, 2002)
  • Frankie Paul — Anytime (GTT 006, 2000)
  • Adrian Sherwood — Zero Zero One / Pass The Rizla (GTT008, 2001)
  • Jeb Loy Nichols — The October EP (GTSI002, 2003)
  • LVX Collective — 50.5.10 (GTCD006, 2007) — last known release

Maximum Pressure (ID: 21054)

Sister label focused on recent and contemporary Jamaican music (modern dancehall and reggae) rather than archival reissues. 7 releases identified:

  • Flag Flown High: The Best Of Bobby Digital’s Roots Productions (MPLP 001, 2002) — Various
  • Rough Inna Town (The Xterminator Sound) (MP002CD, 2002) — Various
  • King Jammy: The Rhythm King (MP003LP, 2003) — Various
  • Winston Riley Productions: Dancehall Techniques 1986–1991 (MPLP 004, 2003) — Various
  • Plus several Jamaica-pressed 7-inch singles (T.O.K., Elephant Man, Mr. Vegas, Frenchie, 2002)

Sounds & Pressure (ID: 373520)

Pressure Sounds’ own compilations series imprint. Used for the Sounds & Pressure Volume series (e.g. PS CD005, 1995).

Research Queue

  • The Discogs profile says Pressure Sounds “began as a subsidiary of Adrian Sherwood’s On-U Sound label run by Pete Holdsworth” — was Sherwood directly involved in programming/A&R, or was his role purely structural (On-U Sound as the corporate parent)? One interview with Holdsworth or Sherwood from a specialist reggae press source (e.g. Riddim, The Wire) should resolve this.
  • The brief describes Steve Barrow as co-founder of Pressure Sounds. Discogs credits Barrow to Blood & Fire, not Pressure Sounds, with only one sleeve-notes credit on a Pressure Sounds release (2013). Was there a founding-period involvement before the Blood & Fire split, or is this a factual error in the brief? The label’s founding history on its own website (pressure.co.uk) would confirm.
