Overview

Dance To The Radio is a UK independent record label founded in 2005, best known for its mid-2000s indie rock roster — The Pigeon Detectives, ¡Forward, Russia!, and Grammatics among its key signings. The catalogue is heavily weighted to its founding decade, with 37 releases in the 2000s against ten in the 2010s and seven in the 2020s. Its output is predominantly indie rock, alternative rock, indie pop and dream pop, with a grime credit appearing as an outlier against the otherwise guitar-led roster. Dance To The Radio Ltd operates as a sublabel.
