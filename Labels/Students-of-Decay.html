Overview

Students of Decay is an independent label founded in 2005 that issues drone, ambient, electronic, and experimental music across a range of formats. Its catalogue of roughly 46 releases weighted toward the 2000s and 2010s consistently runs through styles of abstract noise, drone, and ambient composition. The roster has drawn notable artists in those fields — including Sarah Davachi, James Plotkin, and Anne Guthrie — placing the label at a recognisable point in the North American experimental underground. Activity has tapered through the 2020s, though the label remains without a documented parent or sublabel structure.
