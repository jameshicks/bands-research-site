Overview

Tuff Gong is a Jamaican record label founded by Bob Marley in 1965 — operating under the Tuff Gong International parent — with catalogue releases beginning in 1967. It started on Orange Street in Kingston before relocating to 56 Hope Road (now the Bob Marley Museum and Theatre) and later to 220 Marcus Garvey Drive, where it grew into a vertically integrated operation covering recording, mastering, pressing, cassette production, distribution, and artist booking. The catalogue is concentrated in the late 1960s and 1970s and spans roots reggae, rocksteady, dub, and dancehall, built around Bob Marley & The Wailers and associated Marley solo output. Sublabels including 56 Hope Road, Ghetto Youths United, and 220 Marcus Garvey Drive extend the family, alongside affiliate entities for Rita Marley Music and Ghetto Youths International.
