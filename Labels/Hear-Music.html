From Discogs

Originally a music retail/catalog company, Hear Music was founded by Don MacKinnon and two partners in Cambridge, Massachusetts, in 1990. It was sold in 1992 to CML Group, which undertook an unsuccessful rollout. MacKinnon and a partner bought it back from CML in 1996. In 1999, the company, which was then headquartered in San Francisco, was purchased by Starbucks Coffee Company, and the headquarters were relocated to Seattle. Starbucks used it to release to their own compilations and other releases. In 2007, it became a joint venture between Concord Music Group and Starbucks. Hear Music was an assumed business name of Starcon, LLC, mentioned as ‘StarCon, LLC d/b/a Hear Music’ on some releases.

Overview

Hear Music began as a music retail and catalogue company, founded in Cambridge, Massachusetts in 1990 by Don MacKinnon and two partners. After changing hands twice — sold to CML Group in 1992 and repurchased by MacKinnon in 1996 — it was acquired by Starbucks Coffee Company in 1999 and relocated to Seattle, where it became the vehicle for Starbucks’ in-store compilations and exclusive releases. In 2007 it became a joint venture between Concord Music Group and Starbucks, operating as StarCon, LLC d/b/a Hear Music. Its catalogue of 131 releases is concentrated in the 2000s and 2010s, leaning toward accessible styles — Pop Rock, Folk Rock, Vocal, Easy Listening — with high-profile signings including Paul McCartney, Elvis Costello, and Paul Simon.
