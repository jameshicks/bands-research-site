Last Year’s Youth Records

German reissue and archival punk label. Discogs label ID: 178592. Sublabels: Boot Records (3) (ID: 220849), Crush Records (6) (ID: 781291).

Overview

German label responsible for the 2000s retrospective output of The Now (4): the 2002 “Into The 80’s” EP (LAST 12) and the 2003 LP Here Come The Now (LAST BIG 4). Its sublabel Boot Records (3) issued the 2005 demo LP Into The 80’s! - The 1978 Demos.
