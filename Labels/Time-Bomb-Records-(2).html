Overview

Time Bomb Records (2) is an Osaka-based Japanese independent label, run out of a record shop in downtown Osaka and dedicated to underground punk, rockabilly, and garage from the Kansai region. Active since 1992, it built its core catalogue heavily in the 1990s (46 releases) before sharply curtailing output in subsequent decades, though it has continued issuing records into the 2020s. Its roster includes The 5.6.7.8’s and Yoshiko “Ronnie” Fujiyama, connecting the label to Japan’s internationally recognised garage-punk underground. No parent label or sublabel structure is on record.
