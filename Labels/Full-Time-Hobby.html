Overview

Full Time Hobby is an independent label founded in London in 2004 by Nigel Adams and Wez Westley (label code LC 14591). Its catalogue leans toward indie rock, folk rock, and psychedelic pop, growing steadily from 73 releases in the 2000s to 104 in the 2010s, with a further 46 in the 2020s. Key roster names include Tunng, Mike Lindsay, Malcolm Middleton, Micah P. Hinson, and Dana Gavanski. Hassle Records operates as a sublabel within the same group.
