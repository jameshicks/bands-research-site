Hotel2Tango

Montreal recording studio. Discogs label ID: 278346. Website: http://www.hotel2tango.com

Overview

Hotel2Tango is the recording studio co-founded by Radwan Moumneh in Montreal, Quebec. It functions as the primary recording facility for the Constellation Records Records scene, appearing in the credits of the vast majority of Constellation releases from the mid-2000s onward. Harris Newman and Howard Bilerman are among the other engineers associated with the Montreal studio ecosystem. The studio is responsible for recordings by Godspeed You Black Emperor!, Matana Roberts, Fly Pan Am, A Silver Mt. Zion, Clues, Black Ox Orkestar, Big Brave, and many others.
