Overview

Ambiguous Records was a small Boston indie label active in the early 1980s, issuing just two releases in 1982. Its roster was unusually eclectic for such a compact operation: acoustic/experimental pairing Kath Bloom and Loren Mazzacane Connors shared catalogue space with Aimee Mann, then years away from her mainstream breakthrough with ‘Til Tuesday. Styles spanned avant-garde and acoustic experimentalism through new wave and alternative rock, suggesting a label that signed on feel rather than genre.

Aimee Mann on Ambiguous Records — The Young Snakes

Aimee Mann’s credit on Ambiguous Records is as lead vocalist and bassist of The Young Snakes, a Boston trio she co-founded after dropping out of Berklee College of Music after roughly 18 months. The other members were guitarist/vocalist Doug Vargas and drummer Mike Evans (original drummer Dave Bass Brown left in autumn 1981 to join Boston hardcore band Negative FX).

The band released a five-song 7” EP, Bark Along With The Young Snakes (Ambiguous Records, cat # 001, 1982), produced by Andy Breslau. Tracklist (per Discogs):

Connection to the label — Boston scene overlap via The Dark: No source gives a direct account of how The Young Snakes approached Andy Breslau, but the Boston scene cross-connections make the route visible. Ambiguous Records issued at least three releases in 1982: the Young Snakes EP (001), the Kath Bloom / Loren Mazzacane Connors LP (AMB-002), and an LP by Boston new-wave band The Dark (Darkworld, AMB-003). The Dark’s singer Jace Wilson was romantically involved with Aimee Mann during the period when she fronted The Young Snakes; their breakup was part of what triggered Mann’s departure to form ‘Til Tuesday. The Dark’s drummer Michael Hausman subsequently became Mann’s partner and co-founded ‘Til Tuesday with her. With The Dark on the same label as The Young Snakes, and the two bands connected through a personal relationship between Wilson and Mann, the most plausible route for the Young Snakes–Breslau connection is a direct Boston-scene referral through the shared Ambiguous/The Dark network — though no source makes this explicit. For the Kath Bloom / Loren Mazzacane Connors album, Breslau is documented as having discovered them through a home recording he found “deeply moving and transfixing” (Gothamist, 2026); a comparable origin story for the Young Snakes has not surfaced. Breslau himself later went on to work in communications; as of the mid-2020s he runs communications at the Alliance for Downtown New York (Gothamist, ibid.).

Mann described The Young Snakes as “a little punk noise-art outfit”; she left in early 1983 citing creative differences (bandmates resisted her love songs and melodic writing) and went on to form ‘Til Tuesday. A 14-track retrospective, The Young Snakes Featuring Aimee Mann, was issued by Lemon Recordings in 2004.
