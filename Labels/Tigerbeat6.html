Tigerbeat6

San Francisco-based independent electronic label founded and run by Kid606 (Miguel Manuel De Pedro). One of the central US imprints of the early 2000s laptop-music underground, operating alongside Schematic and Sublight Records in the same scene. Catalog number prefix: MEOW. Contact: tb6biz@gmail.com.

Discogs ID: 552 | Discogs release count: ~266 unique masters

History

Tigerbeat6 grew out of Kid606’s own output in San Francisco in the late 1990s. The label’s first documented release — Kid606 And Friends Vol. 1 (MEOW001, 2000) — established its DIY, irreverent aesthetic immediately. Early releases leaned hard into glitch, breakcore, and post-IDM (Cex, Electric Company, DAT Politics, Blectum From Blechdom) with an undercurrent of punk energy and pop-culture provocation.

By 2001–2002 the catalog had expanded significantly, bringing in noise experimenters (Merzbow, Black Dice), No-New-York-adjacent acts (Numbers, Erase Errata), and grime/dubstep crossover figures (The Bug, DJ -rupture). The Dälek collaboration Ruin It EP (MEOW040, 2002) connected the label to the experimental hip-hop underground.

The 2003–2004 peak period yielded some of the label’s most durable records: Kid606’s The Illness (MEOW088) and Who Still Kill Sound? (MEOW102); The Bug’s Pressure (MEOW076); and the three-way DJ /rupture / The Bug / The Rootsman Split EP (MEOW072). Kid606’s Kill Sound Before Sound Kills You (2003) was co-released on Ipecac Recordings. The Shockout sublabel launched in 2003 as a dedicated channel for bass-music hybrids drawing on dancehall, grime, and soundsystem culture.

From 2005 onward the label diversified: indie-pop touches emerged with White Williams and Drop The Lime’s club-facing electro-punk. The catalog continued sporadically through the 2010s with releases from Christoph De Babalon, DAT Politics, Cex, and Kid606 himself, but the era-defining intensity of 2001–2005 was not recaptured. The most recent Discogs entry is from 2019.

Scene Context

Tigerbeat6 sits at the intersection of several early-2000s underground currents:

  • US laptop IDM/glitch — shared roster ground with Schematic (Miami: Richard Devine, Phoenecia) and Sublight Records (Canadian glitch)
  • Breakcore/speedcore — Christoph De Babalon, Kid606’s more abrasive output
  • Noise/art damage — Black Dice, Erase Errata, one-off Merzbow appearance
  • Soundsystem/grime/dubstep — The Bug, DJ /rupture, and the Shockout sublabel bridged laptop music to UK bass culture well before those terms solidified
  • SF underground — Numbers, Erase Errata, Blectum From Blechdom, Wobbly all part of the Bay Area experimental scene of that moment

The label’s house aesthetic was DIY agitprop collage design (Slanginternational.org credited on 9 releases) and a deliberately scuzzy, lo-fi presentation that pushed against IDM’s cleaner tendencies.
