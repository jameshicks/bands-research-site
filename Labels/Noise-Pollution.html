Overview

Noise Pollution is an independent record label based in Louisville, Kentucky. It has operated from the late 1990s through at least the mid-2010s, cataloguing releases across indie rock, punk, post-punk, noise, and thrash. Its output is spread across three decades — three releases in the 1990s, three in the 2000s, and five in the 2010s — with a roster that has included Nate Smallwood, Trip Barriger, Sean Roberts, Chris Owens, and Andrew Padon.
