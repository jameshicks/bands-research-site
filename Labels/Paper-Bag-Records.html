Paper Bag Records

Canadian independent record label. Based in Toronto. Co-released Broken Social Scene’s You Forgot It In People (2002, PAPER001) with Arts & Crafts Productions — the founding release of both labels’ involvement with BSS.

Connections

  • Arts & Crafts Productions — co-released BSS debut (2002)
  • Broken Social Scene — shared release You Forgot It In People
