Grand Royal

Discogs label ID: 2236 (sublabel under Grand Royal Records, ID: 584293, which is a Capitol Records sublabel).

Record label founded by Beastie Boys. Operated as a sublabel/imprint of Capitol Records (Discogs: Grand Royal imprint ID 2236; parent entity Grand Royal Records ID 584293). Active 1992–2001.

Founding & Closure: First release was Luscious Jackson’s In Search Of Manny EP (1992, GR 001). The label ultimately went bankrupt; assets were auctioned online for $65K. The label has since partially revived, with catalog titles (e.g. Liquid Liquid’s LP) returning to availability.

Sublabels: G-Son Studios (ID: 270678) — the Beastie Boys’ recording studio imprint — and The Blow Up Factor (ID: 770247).

Mario Caldato Jr. was principal engineer/producer for the label’s Beastie Boys releases.

Beastie Boys Core Releases

Check Your Head (1992), Ill Communication (1994), Root Down EP (1995), Hello Nasty (1998), Anthology: The Sounds Of Science (1999). Earlier Paul’s Boutique (1989) was Capitol only, pre-Grand Royal imprint.

Broader Roster (from Discogs)

  • At The Drive-In — Relationship of Command (2000); produced by Ross Robinson, mixed by Andy Wallace. Their major-label breakthrough.
  • Liquid Liquid — Compilation LP reissue (1997, GR 049); produced by Ed Bahlman. No-wave dance archive reissued here after 99 Records folded.
  • De Facto (2) — 456132015 (2001, GR 096); Omar Rodriguez-Lopez and Cedric Bixler-Zavala side project.
  • Luscious Jackson — In Search Of Manny EP (1992, GR 001, their first release), CitySong (1994), Deep Shag (1994), Natural Ingredients (1994), Fever In Fever Out (1996), Electric Honey (1999).
  • Money Mark — Mark’s Keyboard Repair (1996, GR 028), Push The Button (1998, GR 059). Keyboard tech for Beastie Boys, solo artist.
  • Atari Teenage Riot — Not Your Business E.P. (1996, GR 039), Burn, Berlin, Burn! (1997, GR 042). See Alec Empire.
  • Buffalo Daughter — Multiple releases 1996–1999 (GR 030 through GR 078). Japanese art-rock/electronic trio.
  • Bis — This Is Teen-C Power! (1996), The New Transistor Heroes (1997), multiple singles 1996–2000. Scottish indie-pop/post-punk trio.
  • Moistboyz — GR 004 (1994) through Moistboyz II (1996, GR 037).
  • Sean Lennon — Into The Sun (1998, GR 058 area), Home - 5/8 (1998).
  • Propellerheads — History Repeating (1997, GR 057, with Shirley Bassey), Decksandrumsandrockandroll (1998, GR 055).
  • Noise Addict — Young & Jaded (1994, GR 005). Ben Lee’s pre-solo band.
  • Ben Lee — Grandpaw Would (1995, GR 015), Away With The Pixies (1995), Breathing Tornados (1998).
  • Hurricane (2) — Several US hip-hop releases 1994–1995.
  • Ween — Chocolate And Cheese (1994, GR 010).
  • BS 2000 — BS 2000 (1997, GR 046), Buddy (2000, GR 092). Mike D’s side project.
  • Shizuo — Sweat / Stop It (1996, GR 032), Shizuo Vs. Shizor (1997, GR 048). Digital hardcore.
  • Techno Animal — Demonoid (1997, GR 050).
  • Ec8or — All Of Us Can Be Rich… (1997, GR 047). DHR-adjacent.
  • Jimmy Eat World — Bleed American (2001, GR 099); distributed through Grand Royal in some territories.
  • Bran Van 3000 — Discosis (2001); Montréal collective.
  • Russell Simins — Public Places (2000). Jon Spencer Blues Explosion drummer’s solo record.
  • Scapegoat Wax — Okeeblow (2001).
  • Mr. Lif — Farmhand (1999, GR 085). Boston underground hip-hop.
  • Kostars — Hey Cowboy (1995), Klassics With A “K” (1996).
  • Josephine Wiggs Experience — Bon Bon Lifestyle (1996, GR 035). Post-Breeders project by Josephine Wiggs.
  • Sukpatch — Tie Down That Shiny Wave (1999, GR 079).

Grand Royal also published a magazine (Grand Royal magazine) alongside the record label, covering music, culture, and the Beastie Boys orbit.

Label closed circa 2001 alongside the broader shakeup in the music industry and the Beastie Boys’ shift toward independent releases.
