Fontana

Discogs label ID: 205. UK/European record label founded in France in 1956 by Philips Phonografische Industrie (PPI); launched in the UK in 1958. A subsidiary of Mercury Record Corporation / PolyGram, later absorbed into Universal Music Group. Label Code: LC 0211.

Overview

Fontana launched as a variety imprint initially licensing US recordings (notably from Epic) for European release. Its UK identity expanded substantially through the 1960s — the Spencer Davis Group’s formative singles appeared here — and the label carried on through the full PolyGram era as a home for international pop and rock. By the late 1980s and early 1990s it had become an active British alternative/rock imprint, signing and developing acts including James, Tears For Fears, The House Of Love, Catherine Wheel, Cocteau Twins, Was (Not Was), Pere Ubu, Swing Out Sister, and Lloyd Cole. The label also held European rights to several jazz and Brazilian catalogue reissues during the PolyGram era.

The key A&R figure across the alternative/rock roster was David Bates, credited as executive producer on multiple significant releases. Fontana’s independent distribution circuit was sold to Ingrooves in 2013. The label was revived in 2025 under Universal Music UK, repositioned as the UK home for affiliated jazz labels Blue Note, Verve Records, and Impulse!.

In 2025–2026 Fontana was revived again alongside a restructure of Universal Music UK’s labels, serving as home to affiliated jazz labels and pop artists moving from Decca.

Connections

  • Mercury Record Corporation — parent label throughout PolyGram era
  • PolyGram — overarching corporate structure; distribution and funding
  • Universal Music Group — absorbed PolyGram 1998; current owner
  • Creation Records — The House Of Love moved from Creation to Fontana 1988–89
  • 4AD — Cocteau Twins moved from 4AD to Fontana c.1993
  • Rough Trade — James on Rough Trade before Fontana (Come Home, 1989)
  • Factory Records — James’ earliest label; moved to Fontana 1990
  • Ingrooves — acquired Fontana’s independent distribution circuit, 2013
