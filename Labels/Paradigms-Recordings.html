Paradigms Recordings

UK-based experimental / dark ambient / metal label. Discogs label ID: 56994. No parent label; no sublabels. Approximately 102 releases catalogued. Online presence: MySpace (paradigmsrecordings) and a now-archived blog (paradigmsrecordings.blogspot.com).

Overview

Paradigms Recordings was a UK independent releasing primarily experimental, dark ambient, drone, black metal, and avant-garde material in the mid-2000s through mid-2010s. Its catalog numbers run from “Number One” / “paradigms number one” (2006) through at least PARADIGMS089 (2016). Notably, the label released Amber Asylum’s GardenOfLove as its first release, placed the Jarboe / Kris Force collaboration The Path at PARADIGMS037, and issued The Sweet Meat Love And Holy Cult’s debut single as PARADIGMS008. The label also had an extended relationship with psychedelic space rock act The Cosmic Dead (multiple releases 2012–2016).
