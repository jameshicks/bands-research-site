Atonal Records

German label associated with the Berlin Atonal festival. Parent label: Konnex Records (ID: 52008). Discogs label ID: 11769. 47 releases in DB.

Overview

Released the TAGC document The Delivery (1985), recorded live at the 2nd Atonal Festival, Ballhaus Tiergarten, Berlin, February 1985. The festival was organized by Dimitri Hegemann (later founder of Tresor club). The Atonal connection places The Anti Group at the center of early European industrial/noise activity.
