Overview

Warner Music is a multinational label operating under Warner Music Group, identifiable on releases by the “W” logo or the text “Warner Music” beneath it, with label code LC 14666 appearing on some pressings. Its Discogs-tracked catalogue dates from 1978, with activity picking up in the 1980s and the bulk of credited releases falling in the 1990s, spanning Pop Rock, Alternative Rock, Synth-pop, Hard Rock, and Classic Rock. The roster includes major acts such as Led Zeppelin, Jimmy Page, Robert Plant, John Paul Jones, and New Order. Sublabels operating under the Warner Music banner include regional entities like Monitor-EMI s.r.o. and Parlophone Czech Republic s.r.o., reflecting its function as both a release imprint and a structural node within the broader Warner group.
