Sonido Costa Verde

Spanish independent record label based in Spain. Discogs label ID: 1061002. Two releases in the catalog (as of the Discogs database), both on vinyl, spanning 2016–2019. The label has no documented parent label, sublabels, or URLs in Discogs.

The catalog is small and tightly focused: one vinyl reissue of a Spanish indie-pop LP (El Primer Disco by La Estrella De David, SCV-01, 2016) and one double LP pressing of experimental solo material by UK artist Ian Crause (The Vertical Axis / The Song of Phaethon, SCV-02, 2019). The two releases share a recurring mastering and layout team (Bernardo Calvo, Elisa Pérez), which suggests an organized if very small operation rather than a one-off imprint.

The label’s connection to Crause — a UK-based artist rooted in the early-1990s UK post-rock scene as founder of Disco Inferno — appears to have come through his Spanish production work: he produced Movimientos (2016) for Juventud Juché on Sonido Muchacho, a more prominent Spanish indie label. The Sonido Costa Verde physical release of Crause’s Bandcamp LP in 2019 likely grew from the network established by that connection.

No owner, founder, or staff credits appear in Discogs for the label itself; the label profile field is blank.

Release Details

SCV-01 — La Estrella De David: El Primer Disco (2016)

Vinyl reissue and remaster of La Estrella De David’s debut album, originally issued in 2007 on CD as La Estrella De David (El Ejército Rojo, EER008). The reissue retitles it El Primer Disco (“The First Record”). 18 tracks across two sides (A1–A9, B1–B9). Barcode: 7713042323206; Depósito Legal: M-11272-2016.
