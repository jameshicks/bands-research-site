Overview

Book Records is a Seattle-based independent label that has been active since 1997, working primarily in indie rock, power pop, and alternative rock with touches of garage and folk rock. Its catalogue is modest but durable — two releases in the 1990s, six in the 2000s, five in the 2010s, and four into the 2020s. The roster is striking for a small imprint: it includes Kurt Bloch of the Fastbacks, Scott McCaughey of the Young Fresh Fellows and the Minus 5, Rusty Willoughby of Flop, and R.E.M. guitarist Peter Buck — a cluster that maps closely onto the Pacific Northwest indie underground of the 1990s and the orbit around McCaughey and Buck’s collaborative projects.
