Overview

Times Square Records is a Silva America imprint centred on Indian percussionist and composer Trilok Gurtu, serving as the primary outlet for his Indo-jazz fusion work. The label’s roughly 60-release catalogue is concentrated in the 2000s, with a handful of 1990s titles including the Gurtu remix album Izzat. Styles range across Indian Classical, Jazz-Rock, Fusion, and Bollywood, reflecting the cosmopolitan character of Gurtu’s projects. Roster credits also reach Michael Shrieve, Walter Quintus, Kurt Renker, and Yusuf Gandhi, indicating the imprint extended modestly beyond Gurtu alone.
