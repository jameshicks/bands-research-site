Vinyl Films

US indie label. Discogs label ID: 36524. One sublabel: Vinyl Records (7) (ID: 1066163). Website: theuncool.com/vinyl. 155 releases in Discogs database.

Released the Sun Kil Moon debut Ghosts Of The Great Highway (2003) and the “Carry Me Ohio” single (Jetset Records co-release). Also co-released several Mark Kozelek compilations and live albums with Caldo Verde Records.
