Overview

Splendour is an Oslo-based record label founded in 2011, operating through the 2010s with a catalogue of 19 releases. Its sounds span indie rock, shoegaze, krautrock, psychedelic rock, and ambient, giving it a cohesive experimental-guitar identity within the Nordic scene. The roster centres on Finnish post-rock group Siinai alongside Norwegian artists Bjarne Stensli, Pål Espen Kapelrud, Lewi Bekkesletten, and Rebekka Maria Markstein. No parent company or sublabel structure has been recorded.
