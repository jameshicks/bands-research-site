Red Eye Records

Australian independent label. Discogs label ID: 35939. 391 releases.

Overview

Sydney-based indie label that was Steve Kilbey’s primary home for solo output from 1985 onwards. Co-released many titles with Polydor. Sublabel: Black Eye Records (ID: 62903). Website: redeye.com.au.

Key Artists / Releases

  • Steve Kilbey solo: Unearthed (1986), Earthed (1987), The Slow Crack (1987), Remindlessness (1990)
  • Jack Frost (5): Jack Frost (1990)
  • Curious (Yellow): Charms & Blues (1990)
