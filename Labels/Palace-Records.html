Palace Records

American record label. Discogs label ID: 33386. Sublabel of Drag City (parent ID: 9269). 177 releases in Discogs catalog.

Will Oldham’s primary imprint for Bonnie “Prince” Billy releases, often co-credited alongside Drag City on the same releases. Key releases include I See A Darkness (1998), Ease Down The Road (2001), Master And Everyone (2003), Sings Greatest Palace Music (2004), Superwolf (2005), Beware (2009), and Keeping Secrets Will Destroy You (2023).

Website archived at: https://web.archive.org/web/20000620164905/http://www.palacerecords.com/newfront2.html

Also issued the debut 7” by Appendix Out — Ice Age (PR10, 1996) — an early Scottish folk/lo-fi release, predating the label’s formal absorption into Drag City’s distribution structure.
