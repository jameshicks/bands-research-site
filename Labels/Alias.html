Overview

Alias is a label imprint that began in the suburbs of Los Angeles and later relocated to Kentucky; for the broader company see Alias Records. Its catalogue opened in 1988 and is concentrated almost entirely in the 1990s — 85 of 94 releases — centred on indie rock and alternative rock with touches of power pop and folk rock. The roster includes Archers of Loaf, Knapsack, Bruce Kaphan, and Scott Miller.
