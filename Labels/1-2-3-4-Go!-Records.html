Overview

1-2-3-4 Go! Records is an independent label and record store owned by Steve Stevenson, originally founded in Seattle and now based in Oakland, California. Its catalogue leans heavily on punk, garage rock, power pop, and hardcore, with steady output from 2001 through the 2000s and 2010s — roughly 30 releases per decade — continuing into the 2020s. The roster features prominent Bay Area punk figures including Pinhead Gunpowder and Aaron Cometbus, anchoring the label firmly in the Oakland DIY scene.
