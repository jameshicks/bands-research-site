Hidden Agenda Records

Discogs ID: 110171 Parent label: Parasol Label Group Cat # prefix: AHA!

US indie label (part of the Parasol Label Group). 108 releases on Discogs. Released two key The Gerbils titles: the “Glue” / “Is She Fiona” 7” (AHA!001, 1997) and the debut LP Are You Sleepy (AHA!006, 1998). The label name appears on the Wikipedia infobox for The Gerbils alongside Orange Twin.
