Overview

Wire Records was a defunct Swedish independent label operating under parent MNW, with Per Kviman serving as managing director and founder from January 1982 to January 1992. The catalogue runs from 1983 into the early 1990s, with 31 releases concentrated in the 1980s and a further 13 in the 1990s — a shape typical of an indie imprint that flourished during the Swedish new wave and alternative rock boom before winding down after its founder’s exit. Styles across the catalogue range from indie and alternative rock to new wave and pop rock, and the roster included The Leather Nun and All That Jazz among others. A sublabel, WIRE IL, operated alongside the main imprint.
