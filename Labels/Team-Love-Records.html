Overview

Team Love Records is an American independent label co-founded by Nate Krenkel and Conor Oberst in fall 2003, initially based in NYC’s East Village. In 2009 it relocated to New Paltz, NY, where it opened the Team Love Ravenhouse Gallery — a combined art gallery, record store, and live event space run in partnership with Ravenhouse Management. Originally distributed by Saddle Creek (Oberst’s Nebraska home base), the label later shifted to Alternative Distribution Alliance; by 2012 Krenkel had acquired full ownership. The catalogue runs to 70-plus releases, split roughly evenly across the 2000s and 2010s, rooted in indie rock, folk, and lo-fi, with artists including Tilly and the Wall, Mike Mogis, and Kendl Winter.
