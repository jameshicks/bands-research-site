Overview

Chunklet Magazine is a Georgia-based American humor and music publication founded by Henry H. Owings in 1993, known for its irreverent coverage of independent music culture. As a label entity on Discogs it encompasses around 30 releases running from the mid-1990s through the 2010s, weighted toward the 2000s and 2010s, with a catalogue spanning indie rock, punk, experimental, and surf. Its associated sublabel is Chunklet Industries, and recurring names on releases include Les Savy Fav, Steve Albini, and Man Or Astro-Man?.
