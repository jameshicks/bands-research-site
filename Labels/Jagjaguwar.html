Jagjaguwar

Discogs label ID: 29121
Origin: Bloomington, Indiana
Active: 1996–present
Parent: Secretly Group (alongside Dead Oceans, Secretly Canadian)
Founded by: Darius Van Arman

US independent label. Founded 1996 by Darius Van Arman; formally joined the Secretly Canadian team and relocated to Bloomington in 1999. Part of the Secretly Group umbrella from 2007. Key roster: Bon Iver, Angel Olsen, Sharon Van Etten, Okkervil River, Unknown Mortal Orchestra, Dinosaur Jr., Foxygen, Moses Sumney, Japanese Breakfast (pre-Dead Oceans), Black Mountain.

Artists with John Congleton credits via Jagjaguwar: Okkervil River (I Am Very Far, 2011), Sharon Van Etten (Remind Me Tomorrow, 2019), Angel Olsen (All Mirrors, 2019).
