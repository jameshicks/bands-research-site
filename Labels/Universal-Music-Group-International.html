Created at the start of 1997, Universal Music Group International (UMGI) is headquartered in High Street Kensington, London. UMGI manages UMG’s offices in most countries outside of North America. Please use it as label only if Universal Music Group International logo appears.

Jørgen Larsen became President in 1993 of MCA Music Entertainment International Ltd. This was renamed Universal Music Group International Ltd. on 24 December 1996 and Larsen oversaw the rebranding of MCA as Universal globally over the next few months. In June 1998, in preparation for the acquisition of PolyGram by Universal parent Seagram (completed in January 1999), he was appointed Chairman and Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of UMGI until his retirement on June 30, 2006. Lucian Grainge became Chairman and CEO of UMGI on July 1, 2006. On January 1, 2013 Max Hole was appointed Chairman and CEO of UMGI until October 21, 2015.

For the company Dutch office see Universal Music Group International B.V..

Overview

Universal Music Group International (UMGI) emerged from the December 1996 renaming of MCA Music Entertainment International Ltd., formally opening as a new division in January 1997 with headquarters in High Street Kensington, London. It coordinates UMG’s label operations in most countries outside North America, carrying a catalogue concentrated heavily in the 2000s and spanning Pop Rock, Alternative Rock, Hard Rock, and Vocal styles across roughly 3,700 credited releases. Jørgen Larsen guided the entity from its MCA origins through the 1999 PolyGram integration and served as Chairman and CEO until mid-2006, when Lucian Grainge succeeded him; Max Hole then took the role from January 2013 until October 2015. Sublabels operating under the UMGI banner include FUN (6), Lifestyle², and Rock ‘N’ Roll Legends.
