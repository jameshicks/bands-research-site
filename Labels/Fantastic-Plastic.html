Overview

Fantastic Plastic Records is an independent UK label that began in Northern Ireland, growing out of a record store of the same name before relocating to London in the early 2000s, where it remains based. Its catalogue stretches from 1991 but is centred on the 2000s — over 80 releases in that decade — drawing on indie rock, alternative rock, art rock, and Britpop. The roster is a broad sweep of British indie: Television Personalities and Blue Aeroplanes sit alongside contemporaries like Guillemots, Ikara Colt, The Futureheads, Luke Haines, and I Like Trains, with Help She Can’t Swim, Buffseeds, and Astrid among the closer-aligned roster acts.

Move from Northern Ireland to London — research outcome

Question: What prompted the label’s move from Northern Ireland to London in the early 2000s — was it tied to a specific signing or deal?

Conclusion: no source found. Searched across Wikipedia, cherryredlicensing.co.uk (Cherry Red sync licensing profile), general web queries targeting Darrin Robson interviews, NME/Guardian coverage, and queries pairing the label’s early-2000s signings (Ikara Colt, Ash) with the relocation. No source — including the Cherry Red profile page, which is the closest thing to an official label history publicly available — names a reason, deal, or signing as the catalyst for the move.

Incidental finding — Brighton, not London: The Cherry Red licensing page states that founder Darrin Robson “eventually relocated to Brighton” (not London). Discogs and Wikipedia both say “London.” This may reflect a later address change, or one of the two sources being imprecise. Cherry Red page: https://cherryredlicensing.co.uk/plastic-is-fantastic/ (fetched 2026-11-01). Discogs says “London” in label profile ID 33530. Wikipedia article https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantastic_Plastic_Records (as of 2026-11-01) also says “London.”
