Overview

3 Loop Music is a UK-oriented independent label that launched in 2012, with its entire catalogue concentrated in the 2010s. The roster leans heavily on veterans of the 1990s British alternative scene: Luke Haines (The Auteurs), the band Curve with Dean Garcia, and producer-engineer Flood all appear among its credits, alongside producer Phil Vinall, who built his name working with Elastica and Pulp. Stylistically the label spans Indie Rock, Alternative Rock, Britpop, Glam, and Art Rock — a profile that reads less like a genre-focused imprint and more like a home for critically regarded figures from the post-Britpop generation returning to record in the 2010s.
