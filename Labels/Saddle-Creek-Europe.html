Overview

Saddle Creek Europe is the UK-based European arm of Saddle Creek, the Omaha, Nebraska indie label co-founded in 1993 by Conor Oberst and his brother Justin Oberst. Its catalogue is concentrated almost entirely in the 2000s — 64 of its 66 releases — spanning indie rock, folk rock, emo, and alternative rock. The imprint’s own SCE catalogue numbers distinguish its pressings from earlier European distributions of Saddle Creek releases, which carried the parent label’s LBJ-XX numbering. Key roster figures include producers Mike Mogis and A.J. Mogis alongside Andy LeMaster, Doug Van Sloun, and Jadon Ulrich.
