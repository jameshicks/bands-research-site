DFA Records

Discogs ID: 6486 (label); 66245 (parent company) Founded: 2001, New York City Founders: James Murphy, Tim Goldsworthy, Jonathan Galkin Sublabels: DFA White Out Series (608808), Death From Abroad (95949), Plantain Recording House (396494) Total Discogs releases: 1,574 (1,480 unique masters)

New York City dance-punk and electronic label. Founded by James Murphy, Tim Goldsworthy, and Jonathan Galkin. Name origin: Murphy coined “Death From Above” as a nickname for his punishing live PA setup when working as sound engineer on tour for Six Finger Satellite (Providence RI, Sub Pop). Juan MacLean was also a Six Finger Satellite member — the direct link between Murphy and his future DFA collaborator. Murphy and Goldsworthy recorded together as The DFA (Discogs ID 64961) — the production alias under which most early label releases are credited.

Goldsworthy previously recorded with UNKLE (as “Tim From UNKLE”) and came from a UK trip-hop/electronic background. Murphy’s main recording project was LCD Soundsystem. Jonathan Galkin managed the label until 2020.

Founding and Early Period (2001–2004)

DFA launched with a run of 12” singles that became touchstones of the early 2000s NYC dance-punk revival. First catalog entry: The Rapture — House Of Jealous Lovers (dfa 2121, 2002), produced by The DFA, which broke the label internationally. Simultaneously, LCD Soundsystem’s Losing My Edge (dfa 2123, 2002) established Murphy’s voice.

Early label identity: post-punk energy over disco/house grooves. The DFA production alias (Murphy + Goldsworthy) anchored nearly the entire early catalog.

Distribution was handled through major label deals (EMI for European releases); catalog numbers on UK/Europe pressings often carried dfaemi prefixes.

Key Acts

The Rapture (82 releases, 2002–2023)

Post-punk/dance act. Core members: Luke Jenner (vocals/guitar), Gabriel Andruzzi (bass/synths/sax), Vito Roccoforte (drums), Mattie Safer. Echoes LP (2003) was the commercial breakout.

Hercules & Love Affair (48 releases, 2007–2008)

Founded by Andrew Butler (Discogs 904908). Debut Hercules And Love Affair (2008) produced by Butler and Goldsworthy. Featured Antony Hegarty on “Blind.” Members across releases: Kim Ann Foxman, John Grant, Nomi Ruiz, Tyler Pope.
