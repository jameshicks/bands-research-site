Wichita

Discogs ID: 2005. UK independent record label. Co-released several Her Space Holiday records with Mush for the UK market: Bright Eyes Vs. Her Space Holiday split 7” (WEBB005S, 2000), Manic Expressive (WEBB022, 2001), The Young Machines (WEBB047CD, 2003), The Past Presents The Future (WEBB089CD, 2005). Also home to Bright Eyes UK releases.
