Overview

AED — short for Analogue Enhanced Digital — is a UK indie label co-founded in 2001 by Edwyn Collins and industry figure James Endeacott, operating as a subsidiary of Analogue Enhanced Digital Limited. Though established at the turn of the millennium, its Discogs catalogue begins in 2011, with 22 releases through the 2010s and a further five into the 2020s, concentrated in indie rock, indie pop, folk rock, and Britpop. The roster is built around Collins himself alongside artists including Sebastian Lewsley, Joe McAlinden, Sean Read, and Duncan Cowell.
