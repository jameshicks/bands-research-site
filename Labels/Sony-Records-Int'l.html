Overview

Sony Records Int’l (officially ソニー・レコーズインターナショナル) is a Japanese imprint under Sony Music Labels Inc., releasing licensed international catalogue material — primarily classic rock, folk rock, and blues rock — for the Japanese market. Active from 1996, its catalogue peaks in the 2000s, with artists such as Miles Davis, Bob Dylan, Bruce Springsteen, and Herbie Hancock among its leading roster names. The imprint was previously administered jointly by Sony Music Japan International Inc., Sony Music Direct (Japan) Inc., and Sony Music Records Inc., all of which were folded into Sony Music Labels Inc. in April 2014; from that point the spine credit shifted from “Sony Records International” to “Sony Music Labels.” It now operates as a pure imprint — no longer a division — with several sub-series including specialist reissue lines for jazz, hip-hop, and heavy metal.
