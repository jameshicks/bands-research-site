PCP Entertainment

Discogs ID: 16654 Origin: New York City, USA Address: PO Box 1689, NY, NY 10009-8908 Contact: pk@pcp-entertainment.com Website: pcp-entertainment.com Sublabels: P.C.P. Productions (Discogs ID 282579) Active: primarily 1991–2001

Overview

PCP Entertainment was a New York City experimental/noise-rock indie label operating primarily through the 1990s. It served as the home label for Chrome Cranks’ entire core catalog — six releases from 1992 to 1996 covering both their EPs and all three studio albums — and also issued key early records by Unsane, Speedball Baby, Slug (6), Jonathan Fire*Eater, and Poem Rocket, among others. The label’s address was a Lower East Side PO box; PCP’s initials are not publicly explained on Discogs.

The sublabel P.C.P. Productions (ID 282579) appears on two early Unsane 7” pressings (1991), predating the main PCP Entertainment catalog, and has no independent profile in Discogs.

The catalog spans roughly PCP001 through PCP-034 (2001), with a gap after PCP-031/033 (1995–97) and a final release in 2001. The label issued vinyl 7”s, LPs, and cassettes; several releases also had UK pressings on the same cat numbers. A 1995 sampler, PCP Generics (PCP-022), functioned as a label showcase. The recurring producer across the catalog was Reginald Barnes (Slug (6) releases, 1992–96) and Matt Verta-Ray (Speedball Baby, 1994–95); the house engineer was Tom Grimley (8 releases), who was also a member of Slug (6).

Sublabel: P.C.P. Productions

P.C.P. Productions (Discogs ID 282579) is listed as a sublabel of PCP Entertainment. Its only Discogs entries are two pressings of the Unsane 7” Jungle Music / Blood Boy / My Right (1991), both without catalog numbers. It appears to be an earlier operating name or a pre-label imprint used before the PCP Entertainment name and numbering scheme were established.
