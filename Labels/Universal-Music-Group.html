Please note the takeover of the PolyGram group did not happen until early 1999 so if a label from that group (Mercury, Polydor, Decca, Verve Records, Philips etc) appears it is 1999 or later. Also, a 7 digit catalogue number xxxxxx-x is listed it indicates the release is using the adopted PolyGram system so it’s 1999 or later.

This should only be used as a label if the release carries a logo with the exact wording “Universal Music Group” as part of the logo.

History The Universal Music Group was formed in December 1996 by Seagram. Seagram, who had acquired MCA Inc. in April 1995, renamed their MCA Music Entertainment Group to Universal Music Group. In 1998 Seagram acquired Polygram and subsequently merged it with the Universal Music Group to form the world’s largest recorded music business. The takeover was in January 1999 so any PolyGram derived catalogue and the PolyGram styled catalogue numbers (xxxxxx-x) are 1999 or later.

Overview

Universal Music Group was formed in December 1996 when Seagram rebranded its MCA Music Entertainment Group following the 1995 acquisition of MCA Inc. In January 1999, the group absorbed PolyGram — acquired by Seagram in 1998 — merging labels like Mercury, Polydor, Decca, Verve, and Philips to become the world’s largest recorded music business. This Discogs entry tracks releases physically bearing the exact “Universal Music Group” logo, a catalogue concentrated in the 2000s and spanning Pop Rock, Ballad, Alternative Rock, and Europop, with artists such as Rammstein and Zucchero among its roster. The formal music operations entity is UMG Recordings, Inc.
