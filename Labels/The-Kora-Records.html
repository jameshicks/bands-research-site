Overview

Small US independent label and distributor active 2004–2012. Based in Washington, DC. The label released approximately 38 releases across that period, with its strongest identity centred on Richmond, Virginia post-rock and related chamber/ambient music. Primary home of Gregor Samsa (3) for all three of their major label albums. Also released records by the Swedish duo Fredrik (7), who appear on the roster more than any other artist (six releases), and served as a US outlet for the UK indie-pop act Stricken City and the Portland project Ramona Falls. Its final release was a Philip Glass remix compilation in 2012.

No label founder or operator is credited by name in any Discogs release records; who ran the label is not determinable from the database alone. A recurring engineer presence is Chad Clark (Beauty Pill, Silver Sonya studio, Arlington VA), who mixed or recorded four releases.

Key Releases in Detail

Gregor Samsa (3) — 55:12 (TKR 04, 2006)

First full-length by the label’s flagship act. Self-produced by the band; engineered by Jason Laferrera. Title encodes the album’s runtime. Three pressings (US + European). European edition on Own Records.

Gregor Samsa (3) — Rest (TKR 011, 2008)

The band’s most elaborately crewed record: mixed by Alan Weatherhead, engineered by Alex Aldi and Champ Bennett, mastered by Mark Christensen. Performers include Toby Driver and Mia Matsumiya (both of Kayo Dot). Eight pressings across US/South Korea/Poland/Europe.

Gregor Samsa (3) — Over Air (TRK 015, 2009)

Enhanced limited CD; alternate versions of Rest tracks. Produced by Dutch duo Berry Kamer and Maurice Woestenburg. Limited/numbered. Contains Paris 2008 live video directed by Rick Alverson.

Fredrik (7) — Na Na Ni (TKR 013, 2008)

Swedish duo Fredrik Hultin and Ola Lindefelt. No additional credits in Discogs beyond the primary artists. Released both as LP and CD.

Fredrik (7) — Trilogi (TKR017, 2009) / Flora (TKR021, 2011)

Two further Fredrik albums before the label folded. The duo handled recording and mixing themselves.

Stricken City — Songs About People I Know (TKR 018, 2009)

London indie-pop. Fronted by Rebekah Raa (vocals, piano, lyrics). Production divided across Andy Chatterley, Ian Davenport, Jeremy Warmsley, and Michael Hyland. UK pressing on DKR 003 as Losing Colour (2011).

Aidan Baker — Scalpel (TKR 008, 2007)

Solo acoustic album by Canadian ambient artist Aidan Baker: acoustic guitar, recorder, violin, vocals — all self-performed and recorded. Layout by Greg Stein, photography by Martina Verhoeven.

Ramona Falls — Prophet (TKR 025, 2012)

Solo project of Brent Knopf (Menoptera/Menomena). Mixed by Tony Lash and David Newfeld; mastered by Jamal Ruhe. Guest performers include Dana Janssen, Dave Depper, John Askew (2), Matt Sheehy, Paul Alcott, Dave Lowensohn. Viola/violin by Amanda Lawrence.

REWORK_Philip Glass Remixed (TKR026, 2012)

Remix compilation featuring Glass’s music reworked by various artists. Executive-produced by Don Christensen and Jim Keller (2). Original recordings produced by Glass with Kurt Munkacsi. Mastered by Dave McNair. This was among the label’s last releases; it marks a significant step up in profile from the label’s usual indie-scale output.

Label Context and Scene

The Kora Records sits at the intersection of several scenes:

  • Richmond, Virginia post-rock: Gregor Samsa (3) were the flagship act; connections to the Richmond indie underground via Champ Bennett’s side projects (Ghastly City Sleep, Tiempo, Excitebike) and producer Rick Alverson (filmmaker in the Richmond/Brooklyn orbit)
  • DC-area recording: Chad Clark’s Silver Sonya studio (Arlington, VA) provided recurring production services; J. Robbins (of Burning Airlines) produced two TKR releases
  • Swedish chamber indie: Fredrik (7) (Hultin + Lindefelt) became the label’s most-released act, connecting TKR to a Scandinavian quiet/folk scene
  • UK indie-pop: Stricken City (London) brought a UK presence; their Songs About People I Know had UK-side production by Andy Chatterley, Ian Davenport, and Jeremy Warmsley
  • Canadian ambient/drone: Aidan Baker (also in Nadja (5)) released two solo records; the quieter end of the label’s catalog
  • Portland indie: Ramona Falls (Brent Knopf of Menomena) appeared on the label’s final major release in 2012

The Philip Glass remix compilation in 2012, co-released under TKR026, is stylistically anomalous for the label — possibly a licensing pickup rather than a primary label initiative.
