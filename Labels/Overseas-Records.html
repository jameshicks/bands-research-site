Overview

Overseas Records was a registered trademark of Teichiku Records Co., Ltd. in Japan, serving as the company’s imprint for international licensed repertoire. Active from 1964, the label’s output concentrated in the 1970s — 36 of its roughly 40 listed releases fall in that decade — across chanson, pop rock, disco, vocal, and soundtrack styles. Its roster included international names such as Mireille Mathieu and The Beatles alongside smaller acts, and it operated several specialist sublabels including jazz series (All Time Jazz 1500 and 1800) and a Gregorian chant series, pointing to a curated approach to bringing diverse foreign catalogues to Japanese listeners.
