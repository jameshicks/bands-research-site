Overview

Arctic Rodeo Recordings is an independent label founded in 2007 that built its catalogue across indie rock, alternative rock, emo, and punk. Activity peaked strongly in the 2010s, which account for 63 of its 83 releases, before tapering to a handful of titles in the 2020s. Its roster has a notable post-hardcore thread: both J. Robbins and his band Burning Airlines appear as credited artists alongside Chamberlain and mastering engineer Dan Coutant. No parent label or distribution arrangement is documented in Discogs.
