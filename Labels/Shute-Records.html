Shute Records

Independent label based in Kensington, Maryland (Washington D.C. area). Discogs label ID: 92953. Active approximately 1994–1998. Catalog of 13 distinct releases across 7-inch vinyl, cassette, and CD formats.

Shute operated in the orbit of the DC post-hardcore/emo scene without being formally affiliated with Dischord Records, though several of its releases were co-issued with Dischord (cat# 98.75 / Shute 7 and the Corm full-length) and one with Lovitt Records. The label’s roster connects directly to the lineage that produced Q And Not U: Corm — the central Shute band — spun off into The Elusive, and members of both groups went on to found Q And Not U. John Davis (11) appears as a performer or compiler on virtually every Shute release. The label also served as a regional platform for Frodus, Chisel (featuring Ted Leo), and Velocity Girl, bridging the indie-rock and hardcore ends of the mid-90s DC spectrum.

No confirmed founder or label operator on record in Discogs. John Davis (11) is credited as compiler on the Vehicle compilation (Shute No. 6) and Charles Bennington appears as producer on the Corm debut and sequencer on Vehicle, suggesting both were closely involved in the label’s operation.

History

Shute launched in 1994 with Corm’s debut 7-inch Custom Cool (cat# 4). The catalog numbering begins at 4, suggesting earlier releases may exist or the numbering reflects a different scheme. The label’s first three years (1994–1996) are dominated by Corm and Jenhitt, two Kensington-area bands sharing multiple members. The 1995 Vehicle compilation (Shute No. 6) captures the broadest cross-section of the DC indie underground the label touched: Holy Rollers, Corm, Edsel, and Trusty each contribute one track.

A significant co-release relationship with Dischord Records emerged in 1995–1996: Corm’s second 7-inch The Conservation Of Momentum (Shute 7 / Dischord 98.75) and their full-length Audio Flame Kit (Shute No. 11 / Dischord no. 108.5) both carry dual imprints. The Lovitt Records co-release with Frodus (Sound Laboratories, Shute 09 / LOV002) connects the label to the harder hardcore side of DC.

By 1997 the label broadened with a Velocity Girl / Chisel split 7-inch (Shute No. 8), featuring Ted Leo on the Chisel side. The final release in the Discogs catalog is The Elusive’s Sometimes Sounds Collapse (SRC013, 1998), a CD EP that marked the end of both the label and the short-lived band.

Roster

Corm

DC post-hardcore/emo band, the label’s anchor act. Members: John Davis (11) (guitar/vocals/drums), Alex Ficker (vocals), Dan Chu (drums/guitar), Rob Anthony (guitar), Ben Winters (bass), Scott Stenger. Released three records on Shute: debut 7-inch Custom Cool (1994), split 7-inch The Conservation Of Momentum co-issued with Dischord (1995), and full-length Audio Flame Kit co-issued with Dischord (1996). The band’s breakup in 1997 led directly to The Elusive. Discogs artist ID: 48590.

Jenhitt

The other main Shute act, releasing four titles between 1995 and 1997. Members: Tamara Black (vocals), Ian Chu (bass/vocals), Costas Nakassis (guitar/piano), Andrew Black (drums). Despite the name, “Jenhitt” or “Jen Hitt” was a band name, not a person’s name (no realname on Discogs). Their 1997 CD In The Cold Light Of Winter (14 tracks) was produced by Davis White, who also recorded The Elusive’s EP. Ian Chu appears on both Jenhitt records and the Elusive EP. Discogs artist ID: 1827086.

The Elusive

Short-lived DC post-hardcore band formed in summer 1997 from Corm’s breakup. Members include John Davis (11), Chris Richards (4), Ian Chu, Dan Chu, Alex Ficker. Released one record on Shute — Sometimes Sounds Collapse (SRC013, 1998, CD EP) — before splitting in April 1998. Davis and Richards subsequently co-founded Q And Not U. The EP was reissued in 2025 on Expert Work Records. Discogs artist ID: 2051404.

Frodus

DC/Kensington hardcore band. Members: Shelby Cinca (vocals/guitar), Howard Pyle IV (bass/vocals), Jason Hamacher (drums), plus others. Released Sound Laboratories (Shute 09, 1996) co-issued with Lovitt Records — a 2-track 7-inch produced by Howard Pyle. Frodus are the hardest-sounding act on the Shute roster, operating closer to the hardcore/noise-rock end of the spectrum. Discogs artist ID: 397463.

Chisel

DC post-punk/indie rock band featuring Ted Leo (guitar/vocals), Chris Norborg, John Dugan, Chris Infante. Contributed “Morley Timmons” to the split 7-inch with Velocity Girl (Shute No. 8, 1997). Led directly to Ted Leo’s subsequent career. Discogs artist ID: 99951.

Velocity Girl

DC indie rock/dream-pop band on Sub Pop Records. Members: Sarah Shannon, Jim Spellman, Archie Moore, Brian Nelson, Kelly Riles. Contributed “Breaking Lines” (written by Brian Taylor / Stephen Pastel) to the Velocity Girl / Chisel split (Shute No. 8, 1997). Discogs artist ID: 378135.

Connections

  • Dischord Records — co-released Corm’s The Conservation Of Momentum (7”) and Audio Flame Kit (CD) — Shute’s closest institutional partner
  • Lovitt Records — co-released Frodus Sound Laboratories 7”
  • Q And Not U — direct successor band formed by John Davis (11) and Chris Richards (4) after The Elusive dissolved
  • Paint Branch — later band sharing Davis and Richards
  • Georgie James — post-Q And Not U project of John Davis
  • Title Tracks — John Davis’s subsequent band
  • Expert Work Records — reissued The Elusive’s Sometimes Sounds Collapse in 2025
  • J. Robbins — did graphics for Vehicle comp; prominent DC scene figure
