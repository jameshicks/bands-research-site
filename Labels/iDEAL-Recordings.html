Overview

iDEAL Recordings is a Swedish label for experimental sound and art, founded in Gothenburg in 1998 by Joachim Nordwall. It spans noise, electro-acoustic improvisation, drone, minimal techno, and acid, releasing on vinyl, cassette, and CD. The catalogue grew sharply in the 2010s — 33 releases in that decade, the label’s most productive — and continued into the 2020s, with the core roster including Kali Malone, John Duncan, Philip Marshall, and Henrik Rylander. Beyond records, iDEAL also runs festivals, exhibitions, concerts, and club nights, and operates three sublabels: Dub On Arrival, Ideal Inner Circle, and iDEAL Secret Circle.
