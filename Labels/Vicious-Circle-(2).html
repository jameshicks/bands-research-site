Overview

Vicious Circle is a French independent label founded by Philippe Couderc in Bordeaux and operating as a division of the Abus Dangereux music magazine, with a shop in Toulouse. Its catalogue spans all four decades from the 1990s onward — heaviest in the 2000s and 2010s — covering indie rock, alternative rock, noise, punk, and garage rock across around 130 releases. The roster has included Shannon Wright, Chokebore, Troy Von Balthazar, and Mansfield.TYA; a digital arm operates under the sublabel www.reverberation.fr.

Notes (web) — Chokebore / Vicious Circle connection

Question: Was the Chokebore signing made via Abus Dangereux coverage, or via a specific tour or festival?

What is established from web sources:

  • Chokebore toured Europe extensively in 1995–1996; Abus Dangereux covered them in issue 47 (Jul–Sep 1996), with a feature interview and live reviews from their March 1996 European tour dates. This is the earliest documented point of contact between the band and the Abus Dangereux orbit. chokebore.net/reviews/fr/abus47.html
  • The band’s original Amphetamine Reptile records (1993 onward) were not on Vicious Circle. The Vicious Circle relationship dates from their 2009 reunion onward: the band announced their comeback in November 2009, played European shows and festivals in 2010, and the first Vicious Circle release — the EP Falls Best (REVERB116) — appeared in October 2011. en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chokebore; chokebore.net/discography/singles/falls-best.html
  • Three of the five Falls Best tracks were recorded in France (Coxinhell Studio, Saint-Aygulf) in July 2010, suggesting Vicious Circle’s involvement was in place during the 2010 European activity, not after the EP was finished. coreandco.fr
  • Chokebore later recorded A Taste for Bitters (2020) at Black Box Studios in France — the first full album recorded there — indicating a deepening France tie over time. chokebore.net/biography/

What could not be confirmed:

No source found — in French or English — that describes the specific mechanism of the signing: whether Philippe Couderc approached the band following their 1990s European touring, whether Abus Dangereux coverage directly led to the deal, or whether contact was made at the 2010 reunion shows. The following were checked and contained no account of the signing: Wikipedia (Chokebore article), chokebore.net biography, w-fenec.org profiles, Abus Dangereux face-47 article, hartzine.com interview, exitmusik.fr interview, noisemag.net Couderc obituary, rue89bordeaux.com Couderc tribute, fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicious_Circle_Records, viciouscircle.fr label news.

Working hypothesis (unconfirmed): The most plausible sequence is that Abus Dangereux’s 1996 coverage of the band (issue 47) established early awareness, but the actual label relationship was not formalised until the 2009–2010 reunion, when Chokebore returned to Europe, recorded partly in France, and Vicious Circle stepped in as their label for new material. Whether there was also a 1990s licensing arrangement (none appears in Discogs) or whether Couderc simply reached out at the reunion is unknown.
