Overview

The Vinyl Factory is a British independent arts enterprise founded in 2001, running a record label, vinyl pressing plant, gallery spaces, a record shop, and a music magazine as a single integrated operation. The catalogue—168 releases across Discogs, concentrated heavily in the 2010s—spans experimental, house, ambient, and leftfield music, with the label specialising in limited edition vinyl tied to accompanying exhibitions and audiovisual events. Roster artists include Róisín Murphy, Robert Del Naja, Trevor Jackson, and Christian Marclay, and the operation encompasses sublabels including Bel-Air Glamour Records, FACT Magazine, Fear Anger Love, and Independent Label Market.
