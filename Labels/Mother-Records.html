Overview

Mother Records is a UK label founded in 1983 by the band U2, formally launched on 1 August 1984 and initially distributed by Island Records. After a 1990 relaunch as an independent company, the label built its most substantial catalogue — 105 of its 129 releases date from the 1990s — before ceasing operations in 2000. Its roster skews heavily toward the electronic and experimental end: Björk, Nellee Hooper, Mark Bell, Guy Sigsworth, and Marius de Vries account for its core output, giving the label a distinct Trip Hop and Leftfield character well removed from its U2 origins.
