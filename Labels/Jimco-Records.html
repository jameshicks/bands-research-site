Overview

Jimco Records is a Japanese label — also marketed as J!MCO Records — founded in 1985 under Jimco Japan Corp. Its catalogue is overwhelmingly concentrated in the 1990s, when it issued around 400 of its roughly 424 releases across a wide genre span: soul, jazz-rock, fusion, punk, and rockabilly. To organise that range it built out dedicated imprints including All That Blues, Best Works Collection, Grind Punk, Heavy Metal Industry, and Jimco Progressive Rock Selection. International artists on the roster include Curtis Mayfield, Rick Wakeman, Jonas Hellborg, and David Torn.
