Tzadik

Origin: New York City, USA Founded: 1995 Active: 1995–present

Independent label founded and run by John Zorn. Focuses on avant-garde, experimental, and Jewish music. Released Toby Driver’s debut solo album In The L..L..Library Loft (2005) and Kayo Dot’s debut Choirs Of The Eye (2003); Zorn served as executive producer on Driver’s album.

Discogs label ID: 3650. 1,135+ releases; 16 sublabels documented on Discogs.
