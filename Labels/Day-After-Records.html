Overview

Day After Records is a Czech hardcore and punk label founded in 1993 and run by Miroslav Pátý. Its catalogue spans hardcore, punk, emo, alternative rock, and indie rock, with the bulk of its output concentrated in the 1990s (22 releases) and 2000s (29 releases) before tapering into the 2010s and continuing into the 2020s. The roster includes Czech acts such as Lvmen, Sunshine, Kalle, Kay, and Ondřej Ježek, pointing to a consistent focus on domestic talent across more than three decades of activity.
