Overview

Communion Records was formally launched in September 2009 by Ben Lovett of Mumford & Sons, bassist Kevin Jones (formerly of Cherbourg), and producer Ian Grimble, building on the Communion music community they had established at London’s Notting Hill Arts Club in 2006. Operating under Communion Group Ltd with a companion Communion Singles Club imprint, the label built a reputation as a close-knit independent home for emerging alternative and indie talent, with its catalogue concentrated overwhelmingly in the 2010s. The label expanded to the US in 2011 and Australia in 2012. Early signings included Michael Kiwanuka and Catfish And The Bottlemen — both acts who went on to significantly greater mainstream success, raising questions about roster retention at the label.
