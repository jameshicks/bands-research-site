Wiiija Records

UK independent record label founded in 1988. London, UK. Discogs label ID: 1768. Label Code: LC 05485. Parent legal entity: Wiiija Records Ltd. (Discogs: 281728, Company No. 02242495, dissolved 2019). Sublabel: Whole Car Records (Discogs: 70714).

Overview

Wiiija Records was founded in 1988 by staff of the Rough Trade shop in Notting Hill (Talbot Street/Talbot Road, London W11 1JA — the name is a corruption of that postcode). It was initially run by Gary Walker, then became a subsidiary of Beggars Banquet Records in 1996, ultimately controlled by Martin Mills through his ownership of Beggars Group Ltd. The label remained active into the early 2000s, releasing around 564 releases in the Discogs database.

Wiiija is closely associated with the early 1990s UK riot grrrl scene, as home to Huggy Bear (3) and Blood Sausage, and through releases on the Some Hearts Paid To Lie EP (featuring Comet Gain, Skinned Teen, Linus, and Pussycat Trash). It was also a vital early outlet for Bikini Kill’s UK releases. The label’s commercial breakthrough came via Cornershop, whose 1997 album When I Was Born For The 7th Time — featuring the Norman Cook remix of “Brimful Of Asha” — was a major UK chart hit. Later significant signings included Le Tigre’s UK debut in 1999 and the pop-electronic trio Bis. The label introduced Therapy? and released the first records by Silverfish and Cornershop. (Source: Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiiija)

Physical address: 130 Talbot Road, London W11 1JA (later registered: 17/19 Alma Road, London SW18 1AA).
