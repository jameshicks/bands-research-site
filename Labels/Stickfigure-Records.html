Overview

Stickfigure Records is an Atlanta-based independent label and distributor founded by Gavin Frederick in 1992, operating under the scene of emo, hardcore, math rock, and experimental underground music — also listed as Stickfigure Recordings. The catalogue runs from 1995, with the weight of activity concentrated in the 2000s across 21 releases, and continues into the 2020s. The label operates two sub-imprints, Stickfigure CDR and Stickfigure Distro, reflecting separate release and distribution functions within the same operation.
