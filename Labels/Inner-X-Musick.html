From Discogs

Label run by John Zewizz of Sleep Chamber.

Starting in 1979, the name of this label began as Triple-X (or sometimes XXX Organisation). Sometimes it was also called Innersleeve. The name Inner-X did not solidify as the main name for this label until around 1986.

The early cassette releases were numbered “XXX-###” and then sometime during 1984 there seems to have been a new cassette series begun numbered “XXX “. The old series did not immediately stop; there were still “XXX-###” cassettes released in 1985. Please be aware of this and do not update any of the releases unless you have the release in hand. Also, if submitting a release please write the catalogue number as it is on the release.

Some of the cassettes do not feature catalogue numbers. In the tradition of Throbbing Gristle, Sleep Chamber attempted to record all of their live concerts and release them on cassette. Twelve of those that didn’t make it onto releases in the XXX series were released in more basic packaging and were just given numbers (such as “#5”). These twelve tapes were also released together in the “Best Ov The Rest” boxset.

There also appears to have been some slight inconsistency in the numbering of vinyl releases.

— Discogs label profile, ID 11975

Overview

Inner-X-Musick is an industrial and experimental label founded and run by John Zewizz of Sleep Chamber, operating under the name Triple-X (or XXX Organisation) from 1979 before the Inner-X name took hold around 1986. Its catalogue spans industrial, noise, ambient, and electro styles, with the overwhelming bulk of its 31 documented releases concentrated in the 1980s and a trickle continuing into the 2000s. Taking a cue from Throbbing Gristle, the label committed to taping and issuing Sleep Chamber live performances on cassette, building an extensive XXX-prefixed tape series whose numbering conventions evolved mid-decade. The roster beyond Zewizz — Jonathan Briley, Lawrence Van Horn, and Darline Victor — suggests a tight Sleep Chamber extended circle rather than a broader open roster.

Notes (web) — Triple-X → Inner-X-Musick rename

Researched: 2026-11-12. No direct statement from Zewizz explaining the rename has been found in any available source.

What the sources say: Every secondary source (Wikipedia, tape-mag.com, theultimateminorty.com, Chaos Control, Cyber-Psychos AOD interview excerpt) simply states the descriptive fact that Discogs already carries: the label operated as Triple-X / XXX Organisation from 1979, with “Inner-X” not solidifying until ~1986. None of these sources offer a cause, and none quote Zewizz on the subject.

Circumstantial context worth noting: Triple X Records (Los Angeles — founded late 1985 by Dean Naleway, Peter Heur, and Charley Brown, three former employees of the bankrupt Greenworld Distribution) is a distinct US label that emerged at exactly the moment Zewizz’s label began consolidating around the Inner-X name. The chronological overlap (Triple X Records: late 1985; Inner-X name solidifies: ~1986) raises the possibility that Zewizz renamed to avoid confusion with or conflict over the Triple-X name, but no source states this. It remains speculative. Triple X Records Wikipedia
