Xeric

Sublabel of Table Of The Elements. Discogs label ID: 37490. Based in Atlanta, Georgia. Active 1997–2008. Founded by Jeff Hunt as a focused imprint within the Table Of The Elements family, dedicated primarily to free improvisation, live and archival recordings, and New Zealand experimental music — areas that sat slightly to one side of the parent label’s minimalist-composition center of gravity. The label issued releases across three overlapping zones: the landmark Grow Fins archival series documenting early Captain Beefheart and The Magic Band recordings; a cluster of contemporary free-improv documents with Mats Gustafsson and John Corbett; and a New Zealand underground strand (2005–2008) anchored by Alastair Galbraith and related artists.

Xeric’s catalog numbers use two distinct systems. Early releases (1997–2001) carry “x 99.x” or “XER-LP-9x” codes, with the LP series running backwards (XER-LP-98 = Vol. I, XER-LP-97 = Vol. II, XER-LP-96 = Vol. III). The CD series runs XER-CD-100 onward. Co-releases with the parent Table Of The Elements label were issued simultaneously under their respective periodic-table catalog codes (e.g., the Vol. I LP also carries Table Of The Elements cat. “Sn 50”).

Catalog Notes

The Grow Fins series is the label’s most prominent archival project. All three volumes — and a separate box set of all volumes that appeared across other Table Of The Elements releases — document pre-Safe As Milk and Trout Mask Replica-era Captain Beefheart recordings: early 1965–68 demos, acetates, and live recordings (Vol. I), Trout Mask house sessions (Vol. II), and live and radio recordings 1969–82 (Vol. III). John Corbett contributed liner notes to Vols. I and II alongside John French and David Fricke. Mastering on Vol. I was split: Bill Schnee handled the early-demo material; David Glasser (Airshow Mastering) edited the final version. Scott Colburn served as audio technician on both Vols. I and II.

The New Zealand strand (XER-CD-103 through XER-CD-113) represents a distinct editorial focus, curating the post-Dunedin Sound underground: Alastair Galbraith, Peter Jefferies, Plagal Grind, Bruce Russell, Birchville Cat Motel, and Pumice. Need For A Crossing: A New New Zealand Vol. 1 (XER-CD-113) is the clearest statement of this focus, a ten-artist compilation acting as a sampler of the early 2000s NZ experimental underground.

Catalog number XER-CD-106 and XER-CD-107 do not appear in Discogs — these may be unissued, uncatalogued, or appear under different entries. XER-CD-111 is similarly absent. The Superlocalhellfreakride catalog number is listed as “Xerix112” rather than XER-CD-112, suggesting a co-release variant.

Connections

  • Jeff Hunt — founder and consistent executive producer / designer across the catalog
  • Audio ArtKive — sibling sublabel, similarly archival in focus
  • Radium (3) — sibling sublabel
  • Captain Beefheart — dominant presence; the Grow Fins series is Xeric’s largest single project
  • Alastair Galbraith — anchors the New Zealand strand with three releases (XER-CD-103, -104, -105)
  • Mats Gustafsson — two releases (XER-CD-100, -101)
  • John Corbett — producer and co-artist on the Gustafsson releases; later founded Corbett vs. Dempsey
  • Lee Ranaldo — appears on Mmmr (1997) and The Celestial Answer (2005)
  • David Daniell — primary artist on Coastal; also appeared as guest on Formal Sleep
  • Badgerlore — Xeric’s most ensemble-heavy later release (Ben Chasny, Glenn Donaldson, Liz Harris, Pete Swanson)
  • Ateleia — James Elliott project; appeared on Coastal and released Formal Sleep on Xeric
  • Crouton — co-released Topography (XER-CD-116)
  • Numero Zero Audio — French label co-credited on Mmmr (nza 02)

Research Queue

  • Grow Fins was described at the time as a five-volume series. Vol. IV and Vol. V do not appear in the Discogs Xeric catalog — confirm whether they were issued on Table Of The Elements proper, abandoned, or released elsewhere.
  • XER-CD-106, XER-CD-107, and XER-CD-111 are absent from Discogs — confirm whether these catalog numbers were assigned and issued but not yet entered, or whether they represent unissued projects.
  • Confirm whether Need For A Crossing had a planned Vol. 2 (the “Vol. 1” title implies a series).
  • Morse / Gaudylight (XER-CD-104) credits Plagal Grind and The Rip as separate acts alongside Galbraith solo material — confirm whether this is a split or a compilation, and whether Plagal Grind and The Rip are distinct bands or Galbraith aliases.
  • Confirm whether Shayne Carter’s appearance on Talisman (track 2, “one note lead”) was a guest session or an archival recording — Carter is credited as “S.P. Carter.”
