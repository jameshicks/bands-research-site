From Discogs

Main label of Sony Music Entertainment. Use this entry for releases from between 1991 and 2005 and 2008 onwards that show the ‘Sony Music’ logo or releases in general which credit “Sony Music”. Please note that the logo was different before 2005. Also please do not confuse with the Japanese SonyMusic.

New logo design from around October 2023: See also here: https://www.underconsideration.com/brandnew/archives/new_logo_for_sony_music.php

Releases from between 1991 and 2005 may also mention “Distribution: Sony Music”. This entry can be used for those ‘Distributed By’ credits as well. For releases crediting “Sony Music Entertainment” for manufacturing, marketing and/or distribution, use Sony Music Entertainment.

The “xx-xxxxxx-xx” format codes found on releases are not distribution codes and should not be arbitrarily assigned to Sony Music when listed as a distributor. Please add these codes as “Other” in the BaOI section with “Manufacturing code” as description. In 1999, these were replaced by “xxxxxxxxxx” codes. Please note that above codes related to label cat# may also appear in disc matrices in “Sxxxxxxxxxx-yyzz” format (these must not be confused with sequential plant codes appearing in S0x00xxxxxx-yyzz patterns, see further below).

Overview

Sony Music is the principal label imprint of Sony Music Entertainment, one of the largest music conglomerates in the world. The Discogs entry covers releases bearing the “Sony Music” logo from 1991 to 2005 and from 2008 onward — a gap in the middle reflects a period when the logo was not in use — with a further visual identity update introduced around October 2023. Across more than 71,000 credited releases, the catalogue leans heavily toward pop rock, ballad, vocal, alternative rock, and synth-pop, with a roster that has included Michael Jackson, Bob Dylan, and Bruce Springsteen. It is distinct from the Japanese SonyMusic imprint and sits above an extensive network of sub-labels.
