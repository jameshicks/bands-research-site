Overview

BMM Records (formerly Black Milk Music) is a French independent label founded in 2010 by Louis Treffel — who records as Le Serveur — and Joseph Petitpain, evolving from a blog and radio show into a label through the release of the Funkanozor compilations. Operating from a studio in Vandoeuvre-lès-Nancy, it bridges jazz, hip-hop, and electronic music, moving between abstract and funky rhythms — downtempo, funk, jazz-funk, and ambient are its primary styles. The catalogue is concentrated in the 2010s and 2020s, with roughly equal output across both decades and a roster drawn largely from the Nancy scene.
