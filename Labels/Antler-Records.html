From Discogs

Independent alternative music label from Belgium (Aarschot) founded in the early 1980s by Roland Beelen and Maurice Engelen. Initially they had launched the label Kleo, which then evolved into Antler Records. Sometime later, they merged with Subway around 1989, to form a new independent company: Antler-Subway Records, in order to follow the most recent dance movements of the time. They also did appropriate new beat clothing. Since 2023 the label has been revived via a collaboration with Jetaime.

The catalogue numbers have either the prefix ANT or Antler. On labels sometimes often appears as “Antler”.

— Discogs label profile, ID 14951

Overview

Antler Records was an independent alternative music label founded in the early 1980s by Roland Beelen and Maurice Engelen in Aarschot, Belgium, growing out of their prior label Kleo. Its catalogue is concentrated almost entirely in the 1980s — 69 releases spanning EBM, Industrial, Synth-pop, Electro, and New Wave — with roster acts including Klinik and Siglo XX. Around 1989 the label merged with Subway to create Antler-Subway Records, a move aimed at tracking the emerging new beat and dance scene; the label also briefly extended into new beat merchandise. After a long dormancy, Antler was revived in 2023 through a collaboration with Jetaime.
