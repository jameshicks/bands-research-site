Sam Shepherd’s own imprint, launched in 2015 alongside the debut LP Elaenia. Initially co-released Elaenia with Luaka Bop (US) and handled the UK/European edition entirely through Pluto. Subsequent releases — Kuiper (2016), Reflections - Mojave Desert (2017), Ratio (2017) — were Pluto-only. Later releases moved to Ninja Tune (Crush, LateNightTales, Cascade), though Pluto remained the co-label on digital releases like Someone Close (2022). All releases are primary-artist Floating Points; no other acts have appeared on the label.

Eglo Records, Shepherd’s earlier home, was co-founded with Alexander Nut in late 2008. Pluto represents Shepherd’s move to full ownership and control over his own catalog.

Overview

Pluto (4) operates as a strictly artist-run, single-artist imprint — every release is by Floating Points. The label’s catalog is small and deliberate: one debut LP, a 12” single, a live film/record hybrid, an abstract 12” for DJs, and a series of reissues. Distribution and co-release partnerships (Luaka Bop for US, Beat Records for Japan, Ninja Tune later) are handled outside the label identity; the Pluto name represents Sam Shepherd’s curatorial and aesthetic control over his physical catalog.

The recurring team of John Dent (mastering), Matt Colton (lacquer), Matthew Cooper (design), and Jason Evans (photography) gives the Pluto catalog a consistent visual and sonic identity. The label’s label code LC50496 appears across vinyl pressings.
