Ninja Tune

UK independent record label. Founded 1990 by Coldcut (Matt Black and Jonathan More).

Released Homelife material in the early 2000s, including Flying Wonders (2002), the Flying Wonders / Wobbly Jack EP (2003), and Guru Man Hubcap Lady (2004), all featuring Graham Massey as a member.
