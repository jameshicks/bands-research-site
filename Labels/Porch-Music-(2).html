Porch Music (2)

Discogs label ID: 2331550. Contact address: 2202 Lake Austin Blvd., Austin, Texas 78703.

Overview

Porch Music (2) was a small Austin, Texas independent label active from 1990 to 1992, operating during the cassette underground era when self-released tapes were a primary distribution channel for regional alt-country and roots music. The label is documented as the sole imprint on both studio cassette albums by Jean Caffeine’s All-Nite Truckstop, the rotating roots-rock ensemble led by Jean Caffeine (2) (real name Jean Leider). The Lake Austin Boulevard address suggests a small private operation rather than a commercial office.

The label sits squarely within the early-1990s Austin independent scene that predated “alternative country” as a recognized genre — Jean Caffeine was already drawing on folk-rock, honky-tonk, blues, and roots rock when the Nashville mainstream and the college-rock press were not yet paying attention to that territory. Porch Music (2) existed to release those records on cassette, with a roster that never extended beyond the one band.

Related Release (Not On Porch Music)

A vinyl 7” single, Hard Work And A Lot Of Hairspray / Hoedown In The Sky, was released in 1992 on Diesel Only Records (cat: DO8426) and Singles Only Label (cat: SOL-240-7) — a separate release from the cassette album, with a slightly different lineup including Louis Jay Meyers (banjo). Gurf Morlix produced. This 7” is not part of the Porch Music (2) catalog.
