Albion Records

UK independent record label. Discogs label ID: 13396. Parent entity: Albion Records Ltd. (Discogs: 277379). Had one sublabel: Ultra! Noise (Discogs: 115524). Approximately 401 releases documented on Discogs.

Released two APB (2) twelve-inch singles in 1984: “Danceability” (12 ION 160) and “What Kind Of Girl?” (12 ION 170), both notable for involving New York production connections.
