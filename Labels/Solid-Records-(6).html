Overview

Solid Records is a Japanese reissue label operating under the Ultra-Vybe, Inc. umbrella, active from 1986. Its catalogue concentrates on soul, disco, funk, and jazz (bop and post bop), with the bulk of releases — around 25 — appearing through the 1990s before output tapered off in the 2000s. The label heads a cluster of thematically focused sublabels including the Bethlehem Album Collection 1000, Black & Blue Real Jazz Classics, and Brasilian Music Love Affair, pointing to a focus on licensing classic American jazz and soul, as well as Brazilian recordings, for the Japanese market.
