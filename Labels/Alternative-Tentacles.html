Alternative Tentacles

San Francisco-based punk and hardcore label founded by Jello Biafra of the Dead Kennedys. One of the defining independent labels of the US punk underground. Discogs label ID: 10389. Parent: Alternative Tentacles Records (Discogs ID: 129500).

Long-running roster includes Dead Kennedys, Nomeansno, Lard, Neurosis, and many others. John Yates worked there as designer/layout person for approximately a decade.
