Overview

Cooperative Music was an association of independent labels founded in April 2005 under V2 Music Group, providing distribution and promotion services to a roster of UK and European indie imprints including Bella Union, Heavenly, City Slang, DFA, Transgressive Records, Moshi Moshi Records, and Memphis Industries. After V2 came under Universal Music Group’s control, the entity was caught up in regulators’ scrutiny of UMG’s acquisition of EMI’s recorded-music assets; the European Commission mandated its divestment, and [PIAS] Entertainment Group acquired it in February 2013. Under [PIAS] it evolved into [PIAS] Cooperative, continuing to serve the independent sector. Its catalogue credits span artists including Interpol, The Black Keys, The Knife, and Mumford & Sons, largely concentrated in the 2000s across indie rock, electro, and alternative rock.
