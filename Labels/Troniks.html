Troniks

Discogs label ID: 5156
Release count: 371
Role: US noise / power electronics / experimental imprint

US underground noise label, one of the more prominent American noise imprints of the 2000s. Released and co-released several Yellow Swans-era records (including These Graves, 2005, with PACrec and Scratch And Sniff Entertainment) for which Pete Swanson handled mastering. Active across a wide roster of US noise and power electronics artists.

Connections

  • Yellow Swans — These Graves (2005, co-release with PACrec)
  • Pete Swanson — mastering credits on Troniks releases (2005–2007)
