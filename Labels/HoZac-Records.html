Overview

HoZac Records is a Chicago-based independent label whose name derives from the punk and garage-rock zine Horizontal Action. Operating since 2006 and distributed by The Business, it has amassed roughly 180 releases concentrated in the 2010s, working in garage rock, punk, lo-fi, and psychedelic rock. The label runs two imprints — HoZac Archival and Hookup Klub — and its roster has included the French garage-psych duo The Limiñanas alongside Chicago acts such as Heavy Times.

Notes (web)

How HoZac came to sign The Limiñanas

The connection was not brokered by any intermediary — it was an unsolicited cold contact triggered by a MySpace upload. Lionel Limiñana has described the sequence consistently across interviews: the duo recorded two songs (“I’m Dead” and “Migas 2000”) informally, for their own pleasure, and posted them on MySpace over a weekend without actively promoting them or contacting any labels. The following Tuesday, HoZac Records in Chicago reached out and proposed releasing the tracks as a 45 rpm single. Two days after that, Trouble In Mind Records — another Chicago label — also contacted them independently. Both labels released singles; HoZac put out I’m Dead / Migas 2000 (HZR-054, 2010).

Limiñana’s framing in interviews is explicit: “We didn’t solicit anyone here.” There was no known intermediary, music blogger, or journalist who made the introduction. HoZac found them through MySpace directly.
