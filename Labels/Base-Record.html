From Discogs

Italian (Bologna) indie label operating between January 1979 and April 2010. In the 1980s specialized in licensing/pressing of well-known new wave and metal acts as well as acts on Factory, Rough Trade (both since 1981) and Les Disques Du Crépuscule (since 1982). The company first resided at Via Castiglione 109 but moved to Via Collamarini 26 in October 1981. So, all releases carrying Via Collamarini (since 1987 also with a fax number), cannot have been released earlier than 1981 or are reissues. Since 2001 the label was residing at Via Giuseppe Massarenti. The re-issues they did of the material (Fugs, Godz, and Sun Ra) were NOT licensed by Bernard Stollman, owner of the music and of ESP Disk.

— Discogs label profile, ID 34753

Overview

Base Record was a Bologna-based Italian independent label — the flagship imprint of Base Record S.R.L. — that operated from January 1979 through April 2010. The catalogue is overwhelmingly concentrated in the 1980s (148 of roughly 202 releases), when the label carved out a niche licensing and pressing new wave, metal, and experimental acts for the Italian market, including titles tied to Factory, Rough Trade, and Les Disques Du Crépuscule from 1981 onward. It also reissued material by ESP Disk artists including The Fugs, Godz, and Sun Ra, though those reissues were made without authorization from ESP Disk owner Bernard Stollman. After a near-dormant 1990s the label issued almost nothing further before closing in April 2010.
