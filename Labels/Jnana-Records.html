Overview

Jnana Records is a UK imprint run by Mark Logan, active since 2004, focused on experimental, neofolk, industrial, folk rock, and ambient releases. The label’s output is concentrated heavily in the 2000s (48 releases), tapering to 8 in the 2010s before a modest resumption with 9 releases in the 2020s. Its catalogue is closely tied to a core circle of UK underground figures — David Tibet and Current 93, Steven Stapleton, Michael Cashmore, and mastering engineer Denis Blackham — reflecting a tight alignment with the post-industrial neofolk scene. The label also operates a sublabel, United Jnana.
