Overview

Ray Gianchetti founded Kool Kat Musik as both a label and a distribution channel for melodic pop music. Despite a solitary release in the 1980s, the label rebuilt momentum from the 2000s onward — 10 releases that decade, 51 in the 2010s, and 73 in the 2020s — suggesting a deliberate expansion into catalogue-label territory. The sound runs from Power Pop and Indie Pop through Indie Rock and Psychedelic Rock, and recurring roster names include Richard Turgeon, Ed Ryan, Tomas Nilsson, and John Howard.
