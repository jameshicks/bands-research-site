Postcard Records

Glasgow independent record label. Discogs label ID: 6051. Founded by Alan Horne and Steven Daly, c.1979. Tagline: “The Sound of Young Scotland.” Address: 185 West Princes St., Glasgow, G4 9B2. Sublabel: I Wish I Was A Postcard (ID: 1730782). Total catalog: ~82 releases.

Overview

Postcard Records launched in Glasgow in 1979 on the back of the punk explosion and quickly became one of the most feted independent labels in Britain. Founded by Alan Horne with Orange Juice drummer Steven Daly, it signed Orange Juice (featuring Edwyn Collins), Josef K, and Aztec Camera in rapid succession. The label’s initial run ended in 1981 after the departure of Orange Juice and Aztec Camera to major labels — releasing just a dozen singles and one LP (Josef K’s The Only Fun In Town). Planned releases by The Bluebells and others were shelved.

Horne reactivated the label in 1992, releasing an Orange Juice compilation alongside new material from Paul Quinn And The Independent Group, Vic Godard, and The Nectarine No. 9. The label ceased again in the mid-1990s. In 2021, now based in Dundee, Postcard released a comprehensive box set of Paul Quinn’s solo work.

Key Connections

  • Orange Juice (3) — flagship early act; Edwyn Collins; Steven Daly co-founded the label
  • Josef K — only LP on the label (The Only Fun In Town, 1981); five singles
  • Aztec Camera — two singles (1981); went on to WEA/Rough Trade
  • The Go-Betweens — one single (I Need Two Heads, 1980); Australian post-punk band
  • The Nectarine No. 9 — debut released on revival Postcard 1992 (Davy Henderson, ex-Fire Engines)
  • Paul Quinn And The Independent Group — revival era; three releases 1992–2021
  • Vic Godard — revival era; Subway Sect frontman
