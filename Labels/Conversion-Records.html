Overview

Conversion Records was a hardcore and punk imprint founded by Dennis Remsing in 1989, with its catalogue concentrated almost entirely in the 1990s — 24 of its 26 releases appeared across that decade. The label worked across hardcore, punk, emo, and post-hardcore, releasing material by Ignite, Outspoken, Metroschifter, Battery, and Man Will Surrender, among others. It has since closed, though some of its titles have been repressed by New Age Records.
