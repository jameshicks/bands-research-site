Soleilmoon Recordings

Discogs ID: 175

Active: 1987–present
Total releases: 393 (Discogs)

Independent label specializing in dark electronic, industrial, ambient, ethnic electronica, and experimental music. Soleilmoon is one of the key US distribution and publishing hubs for the international post-industrial underground, with the Muslimgauze and Legendary Pink Dots catalogs as its twin anchors.

History

1987–1994: Cassette Origins, Early CD Era

Soleilmoon launched in 1987 as a cassette label, operating from the back of a small Portland record shop called the Ooze. The first four releases established the aesthetic immediately: Nocturnal Emissions, Muslimgauze, Coil, and Nocturnal Emissions again. Compact disc releases followed in increasing numbers; the cassette catalog was eventually deleted entirely. The label also issued vinyl records, video tapes, and DATs.

The opening catalog run (SOL 1–21) reads as a founding document of UK/US post-industrial: Smegma (Morass, SOL-01, 1988), Muslimgauze’s first Soleilmoon appearance (Untitled, SOL 2, 1988, written and performed by Bryn Jones), Coil (The Consequences Of Raising Hell / Hellraiser, SOL 4, 1988, artwork by Trevor Brown), and Nocturnal Emissions dominating the early numbers.

The label’s first Various Artists compilation, Absolute (SOL 3, 1989), gathered the wider scene. Lustmord’s Heresy (SOL-9, 1990) brought Brian Williams’ dark ambient project into the catalog, with post-production by Chris Carter (2) and programming by Andrew Lagowski (Lagowski). The Hafler Trio debuted on the label the same year with Intoutof (SOL 14 CD, 1990).

1995–1999: Peak Muslimgauze / Legendary Pink Dots Era

The mid-1990s saw Soleilmoon consolidate around two dominant presences. The Legendary Pink Dots — led by Edward Ka-Spel — released a torrent of albums beginning with From Here You’ll Watch The World Go By (SOL 28 CD, 1995), producing more than a dozen titles in five years. Muslimgauze matched them in output, with releases including Vote Hezbollah (SOL 17 CD, 1993), Salaam Alekum, Bastard (SOL 25 CD, 1995), Syrinjia (SOLV 007, 1998 — 10 pressings, the most-repressed single title in this period), and Uzi Mahmood (SOLV 009, 1998).

Rapoon joined from 1996, with Robin Storey releasing his processed tape-loop work. Zoviet France contributed Digilogue (SOLV 004, 1996 — 5 pressings).

The label’s relationship with sister company Staalplaat (Netherlands, then Germany; ID 1371) — mutual distribution, each distributing the other in its home territory — deepened through this period. The partnership ended in 2000 following the death of Bryn Jones (Muslimgauze) on January 14, 1999 (born June 17, 1961). Jones had recorded at an extraordinary pace; posthumous Muslimgauze releases on Soleilmoon continued for fifteen more years, extending the catalog to 62 entries on this label alone.

1999–2009: Post-Muslimgauze, Bass Communion, Sublabels

In 1999, Soleilmoon launched the sublabel Caciocavallo (ID 14443). The same year saw Bass Communion v Muslimgauze (SOL 89 CD) — a posthumous collaboration between Bass Communion (Steven Wilson, also known as Porcupine Tree) and the Muslimgauze archive, completing a session Jones had worked on before his death. Bass Communion had 9 releases on the label between 1999 and 2009, including Loss (SOLV 011, 2006 — 5 pressings).

Daniel Menche had 10 releases on Soleilmoon through this period, beginning with Incineration (SOL 21, 1993). Randy Greif released several titles. Z’EV contributed Ghost Stories (SOL 64 CD, 1998) and Opus 3.1. Mick Harris (Napalm Death, Scorn) appeared on the label in 1999.

The LPD catalog continued its steady output; Edward Ka-Spel began solo releases alongside the group work (The Blue Room, SOL 87 CD, 1998).

2010–present

Death In June appeared with Burial and Occidental Martyr in 2010 (two separate catalog numbers in one year). Merzbow (Merzbient, SOL 171 CD, 2010 — 4 pressings). Simon Fisher Turner — a long-running Soleilmoon artist — released The Great White Silence (SOL 176 CD, 2011).

2013 brought a cluster of Coil-adjacent releases: Peter Christopherson (posthumous, 2018 catalog supplement), and Nightmare Culture (SOL 180) credited to Sickness of Snakes — the project of Geoff Rushton (John Balance of Coil, ID 548529; real name Geoffrey Nigel Laurence Rushton, died November 2004, i.e. these are posthumous). Geoff Rushton himself has a standalone Soleilmoon entry (2013). Boyd Rice appeared with a single release in 2013.

Zoviet France returned with The Tables Are Turning (SOL 182 CD, 2013). Ostara released two albums (2013, 2016). The label reached catalog number SOL 200 CD with Clouds Of War by Mona Mur / Yishai Swearts in 2024.

Sister Label

Staalplaat (ID 1371, Netherlands / Germany) — Soleilmoon’s sister company for most of the label’s first decade. Each distributed the other in its home country. Partnership ended in 2000 following Bryn Jones’ death in January 1999.

Key Personnel / Artist Notes

Muslimgauze / Bryn Jones (ID 484): Born June 17, 1961; died January 14, 1999. British ethnic electronica artist whose entire project was shaped by conflicts in the Muslim world, particularly the Israel-Palestine conflict. Name is a pun on “muslin” (a type of gauze) combined with “Muslim.” Recorded prolifically; posthumous Soleilmoon releases ran until 2014 (62 entries on this label). Aliases: Bryn Jones, E.g Oblique Graph.

Bass Communion / Steven Wilson (ID 529): Real name Steven John Wilson. Also records as The Porcupine Tree (the band name appears as his alias), Steven Wilson solo, and many pseudonyms (I.E.M., Mr Jelly, Sir Tarquin Underspoon, etc.). Bass Communion is his ambient/drone/electronics project; Soleilmoon run 1999–2009.

Lustmord / Brian Williams (ID 583687): Real name Brian Williams. Started assisting SPK in the early 1980s; Lustmord often a duo with John Murphy (2) before becoming solo. Also records as Isolrubin BK (3 Soleilmoon releases, 1993–2009) and Arecibo (1 Soleilmoon release, 2009) — these are the same person. Geoff Rushton (John Balance, Coil) was also listed as a Lustmord member.

Nocturnal Emissions / Nigel Ayers (ID 10872/98233): The label’s founding artist. Nigel Ayers is the real name; also releases as The Pump, Unit 07. Members include Caroline Kaye Walters and Daniel Ayers.

Geoff Rushton / John Balance (ID 548529): Real name Geoffrey Nigel Laurence Rushton (née Burton). Primary alias: John Balance (Coil). Also: Jenny De’Ath, Murderwerkers, etc. Groups: Coil, Psychic TV, Lustmord, Sickness of Snakes, Time Machines, Zos Kia, Rosa Mundi, Black Light District. Died November 2004. Sickness of Snakes (Soleilmoon, 2013) and standalone Geoff Rushton release (Soleilmoon, 2013) are posthumous.
