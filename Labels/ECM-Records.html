ECM Records

Discogs ID: 6785
Parent: ECM Records GmbH (Discogs ID: 33313)
Founded: 1969, Munich, Germany
Sublabels: 12 (including ECM New Series, Japo, and others)
Release count: ~15,900 in Discogs

German jazz and improvised music label founded by Manfred Eicher. Known for its distinctive high-fidelity recording aesthetic (primarily at Rainbow Studio, Oslo, with engineer Jan Erik Kongshaug) and extensive catalog spanning avant-garde jazz, European improvisation, minimalist classical, and world music. Steve Lake was a key producer and annotator for much of the label’s catalog.

Relevant releases (Chicago free jazz)

  • The Finnish/Swiss Tour (1991) — NRG Ensemble, produced Steve Lake
  • Hal’s Bells (1992) — Hal Russell, produced Steve Lake
  • The Hal Russell Story (1993) — NRG Ensemble, posthumous; Steve Lake

Wadada Leo Smith on ECM

Wadada Leo Smith has five ECM releases spanning 1979–2025, produced by Manfred Eicher (and Steve Lake for Kulture Jazz):

  • Divine Love (1979) — ECM 1143; with Bobby Naughton, Dwight Andrews; guest tracks with Kenny Wheeler, Lester Bowie, Charlie Haden
  • Kulture Jazz (1993) — ECM 1507; solo multi-instrumentalist date; produced by Steve Lake
  • A Cosmic Rhythm / With Each Stroke (2016) — ECM 2486; duo with Vijay Iyer
  • Lebroba (2018) — trio with Bill Frisell and Andrew Cyrille
  • Defiant Life (2025)

Relevant releases (Manu Katché solo catalog)

Manu Katché recorded five solo albums as leader for ECM, all produced by Manfred Eicher:

  • Neighbourhood (2005) — with Jan Garbarek, Marcin Wasilewski, Tomasz Stańko
  • Playground (2007) — Wasilewski Trio plus David Torn, Trygve Seim, Mathias Eick
  • Third Round (2010) — with Pino Palladino, Jason Rebello, Tore Brunborg
  • Manu Katché (2012) — with Jim Watson, Nils Petter Molvær, Tore Brunborg
  • Last Decade (2022)
