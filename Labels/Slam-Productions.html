Slam Productions

Discogs label ID: 89815 Label code: LC 5526 / LC 05526 Founded: 1989 by George Haslam Parent label (Discogs): My Only Desire Records (ID: 988521) Total releases: ~266 Genre: Free jazz, contemporary jazz, improvised music

Overview

Slam Productions is a British free jazz and improvised music label founded by baritone saxophonist George Haslam in 1989. Based primarily in the UK, it has released over 250 albums across three decades, functioning both as Haslam’s own outlet and as a curatorial imprint for allied musicians across the UK, Italy, Argentina, Scandinavia, and Eastern Europe.

Online presence: slamproductions.bandcamp.com / slamproductions.net / myonlydesirerecords.com/slam
