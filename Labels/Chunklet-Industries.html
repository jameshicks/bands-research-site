Overview

Chunklet Industries is an Atlanta, Georgia label that grew out of Chunklet Magazine, the long-running music zine associated with Henry H. Owings. The catalogue leans into indie rock, post-punk, surf, and psychedelic rock, with just one release before 2010 followed by sustained output across the 2010s and 2020s — roughly 40-plus releases in each decade. Regular roster presences include Man Or Astro-Man?, Honey Radar, Mugstar, Drew Crumbaugh, and Owings himself.

Notes (web) — Chunklet Magazine to Chunklet Industries

Origins of the magazine

Owings started Chunklet as a zine in Athens, Georgia in 1993, initially dissatisfied with how his writing was handled at the local alternative paper Flagpole: “The main reason the mag started was due to my dissatisfaction with how my writing was handled in the Flaghole.” Wikipedia, Chunklet (magazine), as of 2025-10-22 The first issue was a single sheet of paper folded into quarters, distributed around Athens haunts like the 40 Watt Club. The magazine ran twenty issues, broadly 1993–2008, with 150+ page issues modelling itself on ’80s zines like Forced Exposure and Conflict, blending “enthusiasm and cynicism.” Bandcamp Daily, Underground Medicine #6

How the label grew from the zine — organic, not a formal pivot

No single founding moment for the label has surfaced across the available sources. Owings’ own framing consistently presents both activities — publishing and putting out records — as running under the Chunklet name since the outset rather than as sequential phases. In the Chickfactor interview he says he “kept doing stuff under the Chunklet moniker out of laziness,” framing the continued use of the brand as pragmatic continuity rather than deliberate expansion. When asked directly about the label’s status he described it as “just a hobby that just keeps going forward. I just haven’t met many people who have told me ‘no’ when I ask if I can put out a record with them.” Chickfactor, Plus 1 Athens interview

The Chunklet.com About page presents both the magazine and record releases as aspects of the same “rabid love of music,” noting that after relocating Chunklet HQ from Athens to Atlanta in 1997 and ramping up his freelance graphic design work, Owings “released more than 100 records by artists such as Man or… Astro-man?, Pylon, The Jesus Lizard, the Oblivians, and the Apples in Stereo.” The page treats them as a single, continuous operation from 1993 onward. Chunklet.com/about

Curatorial continuity — writing about music, then doing it

The most direct statement of the zine-to-label relationship comes from Owings in the Bandcamp Daily feature: he describes his current work as “not too far removed from what I was doing in Chunklet, it’s just more about doing.” The Bandcamp piece characterises the label as a natural outgrowth: the same editorial taste that drove the magazine’s championing of underground acts was redirected into actually producing and releasing those acts’ records — “putting his money and effort behind the artists and projects he championed editorially.” Bandcamp Daily, Underground Medicine #6

Discogs first release date vs. label founding

Discogs records the first Chunklet Industries release as 2007 (Whirlyball Single #1, Various), but Owings consistently dates the label to 1993 (coincident with the magazine), and the Chunklet.com About page makes no distinction between a pre-Atlanta and post-Atlanta label phase. The 2007 Discogs start likely reflects when the label acquired a Discogs profile rather than when it began releasing records. Chunklet.com/about; Discogs label ID 413597
