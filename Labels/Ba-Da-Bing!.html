Ba Da Bing!

Discogs ID: 6606 Parent: BA DA Bing Records (ID: 213522) Sublabels: Badaboom Gramophone (ID: 8616), Even More Important Records (ID: 690822) Active: Brooklyn, NY indie label

Independent label responsible for Beirut’s debut Gulag Orkestar (2006, BING048) and the Lon Gisland EP (2006, BING052); co-released The Flying Club Cup (2007) alongside 4AD.
