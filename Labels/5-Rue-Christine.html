5 Rue Christine

5 Rue Christine

Discogs ID: 23648
Parent label: Kill Rock Stars (ID 5661)
Address: PO Box 1190, Olympia, WA 98507, USA
Contact: WEBMASTER@5RC.COM
Catalog prefix: GER (numbers GER001–GER078+ confirmed)
Total releases: 114 (Discogs)
Active: 1997–c.2008; revived 2022

Kill Rock Stars sublabel founded in Olympia, Washington, operating as a dedicated imprint for experimental, math rock, noise, and avant-garde releases. While housed in Olympia under the Kill Rock Stars umbrella, the artist base skewed toward the San Francisco Bay Area and Sacramento scenes — Deerhoof and Hella are both California acts. The name references a street in Paris’s Saint-Germain-des-Prés neighborhood; no stated connection to the French address beyond the name.

Profile

5 Rue Christine was the home of Deerhoof from the label’s founding (1997) through Friend Opportunity (2007), providing the early catalog infrastructure for one of the US’s most enduring experimental rock bands. Hella — the two-piece math rock project of guitarist Spencer Seim and drummer Zach Hill — had their most concentrated run of studio releases on the label (2002–2006). Xiu Xiu released their first four studio albums on 5RC (2002–2006) before moving to Kill Rock Stars proper and then Polyvinyl.

Beyond those three anchors, the label cultivated a diverse catalog: noise electronics (No-Neck Blues Band, Excepter), NES-cover math rock (The Advantage), singer-songwriter experimentation (Wooden Wand, BARR / Brendan Fowler), lo-fi art-punk (The Mae Shi, The Seconds), and Unwound-lineage avant-rock (Replikants). The Charm (Motion Picture Soundtrack) compilation (2001, GER014) represents a rare Various Artists entry.

Catalog by Era

Founding period (1997–2001)

The label launched with two simultaneous pillars: Replikants — the synth-and-sample project of Unwound’s Justin Trosper and Brandt Sandeno with Joe Plummer — and the early Deerhoof. GER001 (This Is Our Message, 1997) and GER002 (The Man, The King, The Girl, 1997) were released in the same year; GER002 was also co-issued on Kill Rock Stars proper as KRS4444, signaling early label alignment. The Deerhoof lineup at this time was credited pseudonymously as “The Man, The King, The Girl” (bass/guitar, drums, vocals). Replikants followed with Slickaphonics (1999, GER005).

Out Hud — the Bay Area / NYC electronic group featuring Nic Offer, Tyler Pope, Molly Schnick, Phyllis Forbes, and Justin Van Der Volgen (Offer, Pope, and Van Der Volgen were also in !!!) — released “The First Single Of The New Millennium” (2000, GER007), a 7” that became the label’s most-pressed single item (24 pressings catalogued in Discogs). Semiautomatic (GER008, 1999) and Schema (GER011, 2000) and XBXRX (GER012, 2000) filled out the catalog before the Charm soundtrack compilation (GER014, 2001).

Breakthrough (2002–2004)

The label’s strongest cluster of output:

  • Deerhoof — Reveille (GER017, 2002; also KRS380) marks the first release credited to the canonical trio of Greg Saunier, John Dieterich, and Satomi Matsuzaki; recorded by the band with Ian Pellicci, Jay Pellicci, and (bits) Jamie Stewart. Apple O’ (GER025, 2003) and Milk Man (GER033, 2004) followed.
  • Hella — Hold Your Horse Is (GER018, 2002) introduced the duo in the Discogs DB; Falam Dynasty (GER022, 2002); The Devil Isn’t Red (GER027, 2004; recorded/mixed by Jr. Thompson and Tony Cale, mastered by JJ Golden; credited as “Hella Is” Spencer Seim and Zach Hill).
  • Xiu Xiu — Knife Play (GER016, 2002), A Promise (GER024, 2003), Fabulous Muscles (GER031, 2004).
  • The Advantage (GER028, 2004) — Sacramento math rock quartet covering NES video game soundtracks; members Carson McWhirter (bass) and Spencer Seim (drums) were simultaneously Hella members; also Ben Milner (2) and Nick Rogers (2) on guitars.
  • Young People (GER021, 2002) — members Jarrett Silberman, Jeffrey Rosenberg, Katie Eastburn.
  • The Seconds — Y (GER015, 2002).
  • Nervous Cop (GER029, 2003).

Peak (2005–2006)

Densest year for releases:

  • Deerhoof The Runners Four (GER048, 2005; USA & Canada; also KRS-issued).
  • Hella Concentration Face & Homeboy (GER044, 2005) and Acoustics (GER074, 2006).
  • Xiu Xiu La Forêt (GER045, 2005) and The Air Force (GER063, 2006).
  • The Advantage Elf-Titled (GER050, 2006) — lineup change from debut: Nick Rogers (2) out, Robby Moncrieff in on guitar; McWhirter, Seim, and Milner returned.
  • BARR (Brendan Fowler) — Beyond Reinforced Jewel Case (GER046, 2005, Canada) and Summary (GER065, 2007).
  • Devendra Banhart / Xiu Xiu split 7” (GER038, 2005) — 4 pressings; early Banhart 5RC connection.
  • Wooden Wand (James Jackson Toth) — Harem Of The Sundrum & The Witness Figg (GER057, 2004), Buck Dharma (GER051, 2005, with The Vanishing Voice), Gipsy Freedom (GER053, 2006, with The Vanishing Voice).
  • Metalux Victim Of Space (GER040, 2005).
  • No-Neck Blues Band Qvaris (GER052, 2005) — NYC noise institution.
  • Need New Body Where’s Black Ben? (GER041, 2005).
  • Men’s Recovery Project The Very Best Of (GER026, 2005).
  • The Mae Shi Terrorbird (GER034, 2004) and Heartbeeps (GER049, 2005).
  • The Robot Ate Me On Vacation (GER042, 2005) and Good World (GER060, 2006).
  • Excepter Alternation (GER047, 2006) and Sunbomber (GER055, 2006).
  • The Punks Unanimous Bangers (GER076, 2006).
  • Amps For Christ (GER, 2004–2006).
  • Zach Hill solo release (GER, 2006).
  • Inca Ore (GER, 2006).

Later years (2007+)

  • Deerhoof Friend Opportunity (GER078, 2007; also KRS472) — recorded by Eli Crews, Ian Pellicci, Jay Pellicci, Michael Zelner; cover art by David Shrigley; last Deerhoof on 5RC.
  • BARR Summary (GER065, 2007).

2022 Revival

Kill Rock Stars deliberately relaunched 5RC in 2022, framing it as the label’s 25th-anniversary return. The stated rationale, per KRS press materials, was that the imprint’s original mission — “to invent the 21st century” — still had more work to do. [source: killrockstars.com/blogs/news/experimental-imprint-5rc-relaunches-mv-carbon-charlemagne-palestine-announce-album, scenepointblank.com/news/labels/2022/10/19/mv-carbon-and-charlemagne-palestine-relaunch-5rc/]

The inaugural release of the revival was:

  • MV Carbon & Charlemagne Palestine — Liquiddd Changesss (GER079, November 4, 2022) — limited edition clear blue with black & white smoke vinyl. Described as “a clashing sonic exploration by two highly esteemed multidisciplinary artists.” Lead single: “Glass from Sand” (featuring piano, cello, and celestial electronics). [source: killrockstars.com/blogs/news/experimental-imprint-5rc-relaunches-mv-carbon-charlemagne-palestine-announce-album]

Why these two artists: MV Carbon was previously one half of Metalux, which released Victim of Space (GER040, 2005) on 5RC during the label’s original run — so the 2022 release was explicitly framed as a homecoming for Carbon. Charlemagne Palestine is a veteran avant-garde composer with no prior 5RC or Kill Rock Stars history; he entered the picture as Carbon’s collaborator on Liquiddd Changesss, not through an independent relationship with the label. [source: scenepointblank.com/news/labels/2022/10/19/mv-carbon-and-charlemagne-palestine-relaunch-5rc/]

No further 5RC releases have been identified past GER079 as of the time of this research (July 2026).

Key Release Credits

Replikants — This Is Our Message (1997, GER001)

Justin Trosper, Brandt Sandeno — sounds; guest bass (Aaron Hartman) and sax (Benjamin Hartman) on select tracks. 25 tracks. Genres: Electronic, Experimental, Industrial.

Deerhoof — The Man, The King, The Girl (1997, GER002 / KRS4444)

Personnel credited pseudonymously: The Man (bass, guitar), The King (drums), The Girl (vocals). Also co-released on Kill Rock Stars as KRS4444. Styles: Experimental, Indie Rock, Lo-Fi, Noise.

Hella — Hold Your Horse Is (2002, GER018)

Spencer Seim (guitar), Zach Hill (drums). Engineered by Aaron Prellwitz (also mixed); assistant: Galen Baudhuin. Mastered: George Horn. 9 tracks. Styles: Chiptune, Indie Rock, Math Rock.

Deerhoof — Reveille (2002, GER017 / KRS380)

Greg Saunier, John Dieterich, Satomi Matsuzaki — self-recorded (also written-by all three); additional recording: Ian Pellicci, Jay Pellicci; “Bits and pieces” recorded by Jamie Stewart. Art by Satomi Matsuzaki. Guest: Chris Cooper (3) on guitar (“Cooper”).

Hella — The Devil Isn’t Red (2004, GER027)

Lineup: Spencer Seim, Zach Hill (credited as “Hella Is”). Recorded and mixed by Jr. Thompson and Tony Cale. Mastered: JJ Golden. Cover painting: Zach Hill; back cover: Justin Stewart (2).

The Advantage (2004, GER028)

Carson McWhirter (bass), Spencer Seim (drums), Ben Milner (2) (guitar), Nick Rogers (2) (guitar). Mastered: Eric Broyhill. NES video game covers.

The Advantage — Elf-Titled (2006, GER050)

Carson McWhirter (bass, autoharp, SK-1 keyboards, Hammond organ, percussion, vocals), Spencer Seim (drums), Ben Milner (2) (guitar, Hammond organ, electric sitar, percussion, vocals), Robby Moncrieff (guitar, organ, piano, electric sitar, percussion, vocals) — Nick Rogers (2) did not return; Robby Moncrieff replaced him. Engineered by Antreo Pukay. Mastered: John Golden.

Deerhoof — Friend Opportunity (2007, GER078 / KRS472)

Greg Saunier, John Dieterich, Satomi Matsuzaki. Recorded by: Eli Crews, Ian Pellicci, Jay Pellicci, Michael Zelner, Deerhoof. Mixed by: Ian Pellicci, Deerhoof. Cover: David Shrigley (also provided lyrics on “Kidz Are So Small” from Worried Noodles songbook).

Cross-Label Connections

Several 5RC releases were co-issued on Kill Rock Stars proper:

  • Deerhoof The Man, The King, The Girl → KRS4444
  • Deerhoof Reveille → KRS380
  • Deerhoof Friend Opportunity → KRS472

Spencer Seim (drums) and Carson McWhirter (bass) appear simultaneously in both Hella and The Advantage — the only two Sacramento bands on the roster. Zach Hill (Hella drummer) released a solo album on 5RC in 2006, and appears as a credited collaborator on The Advantage sleeve artwork.

Jamie Stewart (Xiu Xiu) recorded “bits and pieces” of Deerhoof’s Reveille (2002), one of the few direct cross-band studio collaborations in the 5RC catalog.

Connections

  • Kill Rock Stars — parent label; co-released several Deerhoof titles
  • Polyvinyl Record Company — later home for Xiu Xiu after 5RC
  • !!! — Out Hud members Nic Offer, Tyler Pope, and Justin Van Der Volgen were simultaneously in !!!
  • Unwound — Justin Trosper and Brandt Sandeno (Replikants) were Unwound members; Joe Plummer later drummed for Modest Mouse
