Soul Jazz Records

Discogs label ID: 27182
Parent: Soul Jazz Records Ltd. (ID: 364250)
UK independent label and record shop based in London. Founded by Stuart Baker. Specialises in reissues and compilations of reggae, funk, soul, no wave, Latin, Brazilian, German electronics, hip-hop, jungle, and global music, alongside new artist releases. Over 1,400 editions catalogued on Discogs (1993–2026).

Selected Compilations / Box Sets

  • Punk 45 Vol. 3 - Sick On You! (2014, SJR LP279 / SJRCD279) — 21-track proto-punk compilation 1969–77; Debris’, Death (8), Hollywood Brats, Cabaret Voltaire, Electric Eels, Mirrors, The 101’ers (Joe Strummer), Crime (2), Simply Saucer, Zolar X, Killer Kane Band (Arthur Kane), and others
  • Punk 45 Vol. 2 - There Is No Such Thing As Society (2013/2014, SJRCD278 / SJR LP278) — 24-track UK punk/post-punk compilation 1977–81; Swell Maps, Television Personalities, Josef K, The Mekons, The Cravats, The Prefects, Eric Random, and others
  • Punk 45 - Approaching The Minimal With Spray Guns (2018, SJR 408 Box) — 5×7” box set; X-X, Bizarros, Rubber City Rebels, The Flesh Eaters, The Scabs (2), The Hollywood Squares
  • Punk 45 - I’m A Mess! D-I-Y Or Die! (2022, SJR LP505 / SJR CD505) — UK D-I-Y punk 45s 1977–78; 19 tracks; The Art Attacks, Johnny And The Self Abusers, The Electric Chairs, Neon Hearts, The Killjoys, Johnny Moped, V2 (5), and others
  • Punk 45 - Les Punks The French Connection (2016, SJR LP354) — 19 tracks, first wave French punk 1977–80; Métal Urbain, Marie Et Les Garçons, Kas Product, Les Olivensteins, Electric Callas, Dogs, and others
