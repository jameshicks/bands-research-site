Smekkleysa

Also known as: Bad Taste Origin: Reykjavík, Iceland

Icelandic record label imprint associated with The Sugarcubes. Credited on at least one pressing of Kukl’s Holidays In Europe (The Naughty Nought) reissue.

Released Sigur Rós’s first three records: Von (1997), Von Brigði = Recycle Bin (1998), and the original Icelandic pressing of Ágætis Byrjun (1999, cat. SM 79 CD).
