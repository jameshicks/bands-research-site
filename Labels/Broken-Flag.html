Overview

Broken Flag was a Power Electronics and Industrial label founded in 1982 by Gary Mundy of Ramleh and Debbie Thomas, based around the UK noise and industrial underground. Mundy shut the label down in 1988, leaving its roughly 39 releases — primarily cassettes and vinyl — out of print; it revived in 1994. Beyond records, it extended into fanzine publishing, producing magazines including King Krown and Kountry and Even When It Makes No Sense. Its roster drew from both the British scene (Ramleh, Skullflower) and European figures including Maurizio Bianchi and Giancarlo Toniutti.
