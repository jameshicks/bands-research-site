Doppler Records

Independent label operating in two distinct phases: a Sacramento-area indie-rock period (2002–2004) and a UK-based electronic/tech house period (2009–2025). The connection between the two phases is unclear from Discogs data — same label ID (156880), same name, no profile or contact information on file.

Phase 1 — Sacramento Indie Rock (2002–2004)

The label’s earliest documented releases are a promotional single for The High Speed Scene and two full-length CDs for Golden Shoulders, a project led by Sacramento vocalist/multi-instrumentalist Adam Kline. The catalog numbers follow a date-coded scheme (DR0302 = March 2002; Dr1102 = November 2002; dr0804 = August 2004) rather than a sequential numbering system, suggesting a small-run, self-distributed operation.

Both Golden Shoulders records drew heavily from the Sacramento/Bay Area indie orbit of the era, with guest contributions from Joanna Newsom, Josh Klinghoffer, Todd Roper, and Dana Gumbiner (the latter two also members of Deathray). Dana Gumbiner served as mastering engineer on Let My Burden Be and as producer/engineer on Friendship Is Deep, making her the key production figure across the Sacramento-era catalog.

Key release: Let My Burden Be (2002, Dr1102)

Debut Golden Shoulders CD. Recorded and mixed by Adam Kline, Greg Brown (7), and Victor Damiani. 13 tracks. Mastered by Dana Gumbiner.

Notable track-level contributors: Joanna Newsom (vocals and piano on four tracks), Josh Klinghoffer (bass on “Goodbye Hummingbird”), Neal Morgan (vocals on multiple tracks), Todd Roper (drums/percussion), Jason Graham (7) (bass and lead guitar). Sleeve design and illustration by Keara Fallon; additional illustration by Sam Salisbury.

Full release credits — see Let My Burden Be (Golden Shoulders).

Key release: Friendship Is Deep (2004, dr0804)

Second Golden Shoulders CD. 12 tracks. Produced by Dana Gumbiner and Golden Shoulders. Engineered and mixed by Dana Gumbiner; mastered by Tim Pratt. Kline arranged all tracks and played rhythm guitar, piano, percussion, vocals.

Personnel includes Josh Klinghoffer and Marc Snegg (bass), Todd Roper and Neal Morgan (drums), Ehren Haas and Greg Brown (7) (guitar), Lee Bob Watson (piano, trumpet, vocals), Dan Elkan (vibraphone), Dave Brockman (piano), Brett Shady and Charla Ausman (additional vocals). Design by Adam Kline and Keara Fallon.

Full release credits — see Friendship Is Deep (Golden Shoulders).

The High Speed Scene (2002, DR0302)

Two-track promo CD single (“For the Kids” / “Fuck and Spend”) by The High Speed Scene, a three-piece (Adam Aaronson, Domen Vajevec, Max Hart). No production credits in Discogs. The release predates the Golden Shoulders catalog entries and may represent the label’s first release.

Phase 2 — UK Electronic / Tech House (2009–2025)

Beginning in 2009, the label issued a series of electronic releases under catalog numbers prefixed DOPP. The dominant artist is Jacksonville, a project by UK-based producer Chris Lyth (Discogs ID: 1159556; also credited separately as artist ID 59262). Lyth operates under numerous aliases including Sparkhouse, Capricorn One (5), Heads Of State, Dominica, Juan Recoba, Mecanique, Pierre Cavert, Pump Action, and Curtis hope.

Jacksonville’s two core Doppler releases are Trix (DOPP01, 2009) and Valparaiso EP (DOPP02, 2009), both digital MP3 releases in the tech house genre. Lyth is credited as producer and written-by across three Doppler releases, making him the most recurrent production figure in the label’s electronic-era catalog. The Inbetween / Sub Rosa EP (DOPP03, 2010) splits credit between Jacksonville and Sparkhouse — both Lyth aliases — indicating a solo artist releasing under two names simultaneously.

The connection between the Sacramento indie phase and the Chris Lyth/UK electronic phase is not documented in Discogs. Possibilities include: separate labels using the same name, a label sold or transferred, or a label that pivoted genre and geography. No contact info, URLs, or profile text are on file in Discogs for either iteration.

The most recent entry, Showtime by Creative Creatures / Zivo (DOPR009, 2025, Australia), represents a third geography and may indicate the label passed through further hands or expanded.
