Overview

Okka Disk is an American independent label founded in 1995 and operated by Bruno Johnson from Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Despite the Milwaukee mailing address, the label’s identity is inseparable from the Chicago free jazz and improvised music scene of the 1990s–2000s: virtually every release documents musicians based in or passing through Chicago, and the label’s recurring liner-notes writer, graphic designer, engineers, and session recordists were Chicago figures.

The label is the primary document of Peter Brötzmann’s extended Chicago period. Brötzmann, working primarily as a free agent from Wuppertal, assembled and sustained the Peter Brötzmann Chicago Octet and the expanded Peter Brötzmann Chicago Tentet through Okka Disk, producing some of his most celebrated later recordings there. Alongside Brötzmann, the label drew together the core of the Chicago free-improv community: Hamid Drake, Ken Vandermark, Kent Kessler, Joe McPhee, Michael Zerang, and Fred Anderson, as well as visiting European and American improvisers.

As of Discogs, 98 releases are catalogued. The catalogue runs to at least OD12093 (2013), though the label appears to have reduced activity after that date; a 2024 reissue of a Sonny Sharrock title indicates it has not fully closed.

Connections

  • Peter Brötzmann — the label’s central figure; more releases than any other artist; also contributed cover art and design to many releases
  • Bruno Johnson — operator; virtually every release carries his producer or executive-producer credit
  • DKV Trio (Hamid Drake, Kent Kessler, Ken Vandermark) — core Chicago ensemble, 8 releases
  • Malachi Ritscher — captured most of the label’s live recordings; his personal archive was the source for posthumous releases after his death in 2006
  • Mats Gustafsson — early contributor (Parrot Fish Eye, For Don Cherry, 1/2/3); later continued parallel career on other European labels
  • Sonny Sharrock — Fragments (2003) is a posthumous LP documenting recordings made before Sharrock’s death in 1994

Open Questions

  • Bruno Johnson’s full background — no biographical detail in Discogs; relationship to Milwaukee vs. Chicago is undocumented
  • Whether the label is still actively releasing or has wound down (last Discogs activity ~2013 for new material; 2024 Sharrock reissue suggests it remains functional)
  • The Maleem Mahmoud Ghania release (1997) — gnawa master from Morocco; unclear how this came about in the Okka Disk context; possibly a Brötzmann collaboration session
  • Full scope of Territory Band series (Territory Band-1 through Territory Band-6 all have 1 Okka Disk release each); these are Ken Vandermark large ensemble recordings
  • Parrot Fish Eye (1995, OD12006) had 64 pressings listed in Discogs — unusually high; unclear whether that’s a metadata error or reflects genuine international distribution reach
