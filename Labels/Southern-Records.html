Southern Records

Discogs ID: 12148
Founded: 1990
Location: Wood Green, North London (10 Myddleton Road, London N22 8NS); US office: Chicago IL
Parent: Southern Studios Ltd. (ID 767746)
Sublabels: Black Diamond (3) (ID 750927), Latitudes (ID 43106), Midwich Records (ID 128225), Truth Cult (ID 125358)
Total releases: 417

Independent label founded 1990 as the record label arm of John Loder’s Southern Studios — a recording studio in Wood Green, North London that operated from the mid-1970s until 2013. Parent company Southern Studios Ltd. was originally incorporated as ICECROFT LIMITED (1980), renamed REGULAR MUSIC LIMITED (1980–1982), with John Loder as original director until his death in 2005. Current director: Allison Schnackenberg (since 15 Sep 2004).

Note: “Southern” as distributor refers to a separate entity — Southern (3) (ID 269886) — not Southern Records.

Era by Era

1990–1993 — Early signings, grunge and post-punk adjacency

First release: Silverfish Fat Axl (18502-2, 1990). Babes In Toyland arrived immediately: Spanking Machine (18505-1, 1990) co-produced by Jack Endino and the band; To Mother (18506-1, 1991); Fontanelle (18501-9, 1992; highest catalog number implies later pressing run). Therapy? signed in 1991 (Babyteeth EP, 18507-2), remaining through 2001. Chumbawamba placed three releases 1992–1994, including Shhh (18515-1, 1992). Big Black placed one release (1992). Silverfish debuted the catalog.

1994–1998 — Punk catalog and post-rock emergence

Anarcho-punk and hardcore catalog deepened: Antisect In Darkness, There Is No Choice (18524-2, 1994); Crucifix (2) Dehumanization (18523-2, 1994); Jah Wobble Psalms (18522-2, 1994). Karate (Boston, formed 1993) debuted with their self-titled LP (18534-2, 1995) and became the label’s most-represented artist at 36 releases through 2015. UI (New York — Sasha Frere-Jones, Clem Waldmann, Wilbo Wright) debuted with Sidelong (18535-1, 1995). Rex placed four LPs 1995–1997. Prong placed Force Fed (18542-2, 1998).

1999–2004 — Post-rock and math rock peak

A dense cluster of post-rock, math rock, and art-punk: Les Savy Fav (10 releases, 1999–2002); Sweep The Leg Johnny (10 releases, 1999–2021); Dianogah (13 releases, 2000–2008); 90 Day Men (9 releases, 1998–2004); The Paper Chase (10 releases, 2000–2009; produced by John Congleton); Joan Of Arc (2 releases, 1997); The Black Heart Procession (2 releases, 1999–2000). William Elliott Whitmore debuted with Hymns For The Hopeless (28106-2, 2003), placing 22 releases through 2009. The Desert Sessions vol. 7 & 8 preview (2001) and The Mars Volta (2002) and Sage Francis (2002) each placed one-off releases.

2005–2013 — Late period and closure of the studio

Post-John Loder era (Loder died August 12, 2005). Dälek placed two experimental hip-hop releases (2004, 2009). Neurosis placed Given To The Rising (NR050, 2007). Nomeansno placed All Roads Lead To Ausfahrt (2006). Mouse On Mars placed Varcharz (2006). Chrome Hoof (10 releases, 2007–2010). Rudimentary Peni No More Pain EP (BOOBOO 09, 2008). Asva (8 releases, 2008) produced by Randall Dunn. Crass reissue of The Feeding Of The 5000 (The Second Sitting) (2011). Penny Rimbaud Acts Of Love (EXT001, 2012). Southern Studios itself closed in 2013.

Key Personnel

John Loder (ID 232794) — founder, engineer, mastering

Born 7 April 1946; died 12 August 2005. Founded Southern Studios in Wood Green in the mid-1970s. 70 engineering and mastering credits on Southern Records — the most of any person across the catalog. Father of Tasha Loder.

Harvey Birrell (ID 73361) — engineer, producer, mastering

24 engineering/mastering credits + 13 producer credits + 12 recording credits. Became the primary in-house engineer as the label matured. Also Therapy?’s main producer (14 releases self-produced by the band, with Birrell frequently involved).

Mike Hagler (ID 310567) — mastering

25 mastering credits. Key late-period mastering engineer.

John Congleton (ID 265534) — producer

15 producer credits, primarily for The Paper Chase.

Karate — label cornerstone

Karate (Boston, formed 1993) is the label’s most-represented act at 36 releases over two decades. Lineup: Geoff Farina (guitar/vocals, ID 480330), Gavin McCarthy (drums, ID 480332), Jeff Goddard (bass, ID 323662), Eamonn Vitt (guitar, ID 1075914 — departed 1997). Notably, Farina is also the label’s primary visual designer (28 design credits). Disbanded July 2005 after Farina developed hearing problems; reunited 2022.

Babes In Toyland — early flagship

Babes In Toyland (Minneapolis, formed 1987): Kat Bjelland (guitar/vocals, ID 275284), Lori Barbero (drums/vocals, ID 440026), Michelle Leon (bass, ID 439979) → later Maureen Herman (bass, ID 626838). Three LPs on Southern Records 1990–1993. Fontanelle (1992) produced by Bjelland and Lee Ranaldo; cover photograph by Cindy Sherman. Split 2001; reunited 2014; split again 2020.

UI — New York post-rock

UI: Sasha Frere-Jones (ID 196011 — bass, guitar, keys, electronics), Clem Waldmann (ID 528408 — drums), Wilbo Wright (ID 528407 — bass/guitar/cello). Sidelong (1995) debut; 17 releases through 2002. Frere-Jones later became the pop music critic for The New Yorker.

Design

Geoff Farina (Karate) is the dominant designer (28 credits). Jon Resh (19 credits) is second. Luke Tweedy (16 credits) known for sculptural cover work (“front cover sculpture” credits). Cindy Sherman (ID 2244302) contributed 7 cover photographs — including the iconic Fontanelle cover (Babes In Toyland, 1992). Tom Recchion (ID 65713) handled art direction and design on Fontanelle and 6 other releases. Richard McGuire (ID 426259) — cartoonist and designer (“Here”) — contributed 6 design credits.
