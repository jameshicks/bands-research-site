Smells Like Records

Discogs ID: 35015
Founded: 1995 by Steve Shelley of Sonic Youth
Location: Hoboken, NJ (PO Box 6179, NJ 07030)
Contact: info@smellslikerecords.com
Continuation label: Discogs label ID 208672

Independent label founded to release music by artists in Shelley’s orbit and beyond. First releases actually date to 1993 (catalog numbers suggest pre-founding activity). Notable for early releases by Blonde Redhead and Cat Power, and for a run of Lee Hazlewood reissues in 1999–2000.

Notes

Shelley produced both Blonde Redhead LPs on the label. Myra Lee (Cat Power) is an early Cat Power LP predating her Matador deal. The Lee Hazlewood reissues (SLR 037–041) were the label’s most prominent licensing project. The Raincoats’ Extended Play (SLR 012) was the only Raincoats US release in the mid-1990s.
