Trance Syndicate Records

Austin, Texas independent label. Discogs label ID: 33260. Active approx. 1990–1999. Founded by King Coffey (drummer, Butthole Surfers) and co-run with his husband Craig Stewart (2). One of the key Austin noise rock / psychedelic labels of the 1990s.

Overview

Distributed through Touch And Go distribution network. Released records by Austin-area acts and broader US underground acts. Known for a particularly uncompromising noise-rock aesthetic alongside Austin psychedelia.
