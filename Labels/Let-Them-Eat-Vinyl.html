Overview

Let Them Eat Vinyl is a reissue label active since 1996, working primarily in punk, post-punk, hardcore, new wave, and alternative rock. Its catalogue was modest through the 2000s (around 24 releases) before expanding sharply in the 2010s to over 400 releases, making that decade the core of its output. The roster centres on British acts from the post-punk era — The Fall, Killing Joke, and The Exploited feature prominently — though the presence of Jeff Lynne points to a broader reissue mandate. No parent label or corporate structure is recorded on Discogs.
