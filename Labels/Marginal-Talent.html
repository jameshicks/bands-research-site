Overview

Marginal Talent is a label founded in 1989, operating under the MT Undertaiment umbrella alongside MT3, Empty Records Europe, Musical Tragedies, and ManifesT, with Empty Records as its direct parent. Its catalogue runs from 1992 through the 2020s — most densely in the 1990s — and skews toward experimental, ambient, industrial, and avant-garde work. The roster centers on Joseph B. Raimond and his project Doc Wör Mirran, with Michael Wurzer and Adrian Gormley among the recurring contributors.
