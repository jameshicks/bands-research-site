Overview

Sinnbus is a Berlin-based independent label, publisher, and occasional concert promoter that grew out of a loose circle of local musicians and artists and was formally founded in 2003. From a city-local operation it built a strong reputation across the German-speaking market—Germany, Austria, and Switzerland—known as much for the quality of its sleeve design and cover art as for the music itself. The catalogue, which extends back to 2002 and hit its peak output in the 2010s, spans post-rock, post-punk, indie rock, synth-pop, and electronica, with roster acts including Bodi Bill, Hundreds, and Einar Stray. Total credits across the label run to 238, spread across roughly 70 documented releases.
