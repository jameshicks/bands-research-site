Overview

OnderStroom Records is a Belgian reissue label specialising in new wave, cold wave, synth pop, minimal synth, and punk, and operates as the sister label of Aguirre Records. Active since 2009, the label built most of its catalogue across the 2010s — 27 of its roughly 36 releases — with a further handful in the 2020s. Its roster is anchored by key Belgian post-punk and new wave names including Siglo XX, Aroma Di Amore, De Brassers, and Micky Mike, making it one of the few dedicated outlets for that scene’s archival reissue work.
