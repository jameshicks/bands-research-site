Overview

Ronin Records was a British hip hop label co-founded in late 1989 or early 1990 by brothers Alex and Johnny Turnbull, who were also members of the post-punk and industrial group 23 Skidoo. The label’s catalogue reflects their eclectic background, spanning breaks, dub, funk, cut-up/DJ and industrial styles rather than a narrow hip hop sound. Active until 2002, Ronin built its roster around artists including Skitz, Deckwrecka, and Agzilla alongside 23 Skidoo itself, issuing the bulk of its 27 releases during the 2000s.
