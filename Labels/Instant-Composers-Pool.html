Instant Composers Pool (Label)

Discogs ID: 99922 Release count: 122 Country: Netherlands Founded: 1967 Website: http://www.icporchestra.com / https://icporchestra.bandcamp.com

Overview

The Instant Composers Pool label is the self-release imprint of the Dutch free improvisation collective ICP and ICP Orchestra, founded in Amsterdam in 1967 by Han Bennink, Misha Mengelberg, and Willem Breuker alongside the ICP collective itself. The label’s profile on Discogs states simply: “Label associated with and formed by the founders of ICP.”

The label operates without a parent company or major-label affiliation. Catalog numbers run sequentially from ICP 001 (1967) through at least ICP 069 (2025), documenting nearly sixty years of continuous self-publication. Distribution has varied across territories; some releases have appeared as co-releases with labels such as AdLib (Italy), DIW (Japan), and hatOLOGY (Switzerland).

Discogs links: http://www.efi.group.shef.ac.uk/labels/icp/cicp.html and https://icporchestra.bandcamp.com
