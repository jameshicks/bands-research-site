Overview

A Turntable Friend is a small independent label with two distinct lives: an original run from 1990 to 1999 and a revival launched in 2017 under the motto “Keep vinyl alive!” Its catalogue of around 34 releases leans into indie rock, indie pop, shoegaze, and lo-fi, with the bulk of its nineties output (14 releases) clustered around the label’s founding artists and a fresh wave of releases across the 2010s and 2020s since the revival. The roster — centred on Boyracer, Stewart Anderson, and Tim Rippington, figures who overlap heavily with the same post-DIY indie scene — suggests a tightly artist-driven operation rather than a conventional label.
