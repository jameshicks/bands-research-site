Chiswick Records

British independent record label. London, UK. Founded c. 1975. Discogs label ID: 37789

Overview

One of the earliest UK independent labels, co-founded by Roger Armstrong and Ted Carroll, growing out of the Rock On record stall at Camden Market (and another location in Soho). Chiswick released music spanning pub rock, punk, R&B, and early new wave — at the transition point between pub rock and punk in 1976–1977.

Parent label: Ace Records Ltd. (Discogs ID: 228088). Has a sublabel: Chiswick Chartbusters (Discogs ID: 1490421).

Discogs lists 1,267 releases on the label. A separate entry, Chiswick Records (2) (ID: 477609), covers a different entity.

Key Artists (early catalog)

  • The Count Bishops — debut EP Speedball (1975, SW 1) was among the label’s first releases; full catalog through 1979
  • The 101’ers — “Keys To Your Heart” (1976, S 3); their debut single
  • The Damned (early single)
  • Motörhead (early single)
  • The Cannibals — post-Count Bishops project of Mike Spenser

Co-founders

  • Roger Armstrong — co-founder, producer (The Count Bishops debut EP, The 101’ers)
  • Ted Carroll — co-founder, executive producer; ran Rock On record stalls with Armstrong
