Jul 07, 20262 min read

Red Star Records

Discogs ID: 39551
Parent: Red Star Records Inc. (Discogs ID 295524)
Sublabels: Martin Thau (Discogs ID 393025)

New York independent label founded by Marty Thau (Martin Thau), former manager of the New York Dolls. Active in the late 1970s. Primary release: Suicide’s debut album Suicide (1977; cat. RS 1), co-produced by Marty Thau and Craig Leon, recorded at Atlantic Studios. “Cheree” b/w “I Remember” (1978, UK, Bronze/Red Star) was the label’s main single. Red Transistor recorded Not Bite / We’re Not Crazy for the label c. 1978; the release was shelved and did not appear until Ecstatic Peace! issued it in 1990.
