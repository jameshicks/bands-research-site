Cobra

French record label. Discogs label ID: 25222. Sublabel of Cezame.

Founded by Frédéric Leibovitz as the rock and experimental imprint within the Cezame group. Notable releases include the self-titled Weidorje album (1978) and Black Stamp by Factory (8) (1977), both recorded by Laurent Thibault. Also released Métal Urbain’s debut single “Panik” / “Lady Coca Cola” (1977, COB 47004), as well as early singles by Asphalt Jungle (3).

Not to be confused with Cobra—group (the John Zorn game-piece ensemble).
