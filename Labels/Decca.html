Decca

British record label, founded 1929. One of the major UK labels of the twentieth century.

Holds the UK catalog of The Rolling Stones from 1963 to 1969, including the debut album through Let It Bleed. The Stones famously had a contentious relationship with Decca — the label refused to release “Let It Bleed” with a toilet on the cover and required the band to record “We Love You” after drug arrests. After the Stones’ deal ended, their pre-1971 UK masters remained with Decca and are now administered via ABKCO Records.

Also the UK home of the Moody Blues, Tom Jones, and many others. Famous for rejecting the Beatles in 1962 (Decca’s A&R Brian Epstein famously said “guitar groups are on the way out”).
