Overview

Beste! Unterhaltung is a small independent label founded in October 2008 by Christian Pliefke in Langenzenn, near Nuremberg, Germany, operating under the CPL-Music parent (label code LC 19104). Its catalogue of around 41 releases is concentrated almost entirely in the 2010s and tilts strongly toward acoustic, folk, and indie pop. The roster has a notable Nordic character — Faroese singer-songwriter Guðrið Hansdóttir, Icelandic troubadour Svavar Knútur, and electronic act Janus Rasmussen all released here alongside the German circus-folk ensemble Gankino Circus and Finnish singer Aino Venna.
