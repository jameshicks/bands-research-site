Overview

Universal Music is a branded label imprint under Universal Music Group, distinct from both the plain “Universal” imprint and the publishing arm Universal Music Publishing. The brand traces to late December 1996 when MCA’s corporate entity was renamed Universal Music Group, with the Canadian rollout following in early 1997. Discogs carries 31,000+ credits under this imprint spanning a wide roster — Caetano Veloso, U2, Rammstein, and Queen among them — across Pop Rock, MPB, Alternative Rock, House, and Ballad. The catalogue, defined as releases explicitly bearing the “Universal Music” logo or text, dates back to 1993, with the B00xxxxxx sequence of catalogue numbers introduced in early 2003.
