Overview

Linear Obsessional Recordings is a South London-based label founded by improvising musician Richard Sanderson (2), releasing limited-edition physical runs alongside free or name-your-price digital downloads of experimental and improvised music from artists worldwide. The catalogue is concentrated in the 2010s — 51 of its 60 tracked releases — spanning styles including free improvisation, field recording, minimal, and abstract music. Roster contributors include Dave Clarkson, Mark Browne (3), Kev Hopper, and Viv Dogan Corringham. Activity in the 2020s has dropped to 8 releases, suggesting the label has scaled back significantly.
