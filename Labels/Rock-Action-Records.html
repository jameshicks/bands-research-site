Rock Action Records

Glasgow-based independent record label founded by Mogwai. Discogs label ID: 7763. Address: PO Box 15107, Glasgow G1 1US.

Overview

Rock Action Records is Mogwai’s own imprint, established in Glasgow in 1996 as a vehicle for the band’s early singles and expanded into a fully functioning independent label releasing associated and unrelated acts. The label name comes from “Rock Action,” a track by New York proto-punk duo Suicide (2) — a title Mogwai later applied to their own 2001 LP.

From its first release — the Mogwai single Tuner / Lower (catalog 01, 1996) — the label grew into one of Scotland’s most important post-rock and experimental imprints. Over nearly three decades it has released everything from Japanese hardcore (Envy (2)) and Miami sludge metal (Torche) to Glasgow art-pop (Errors), singer-songwriters (Kathryn Joseph), and post-punk veterans (Arab Strap, Swervedriver). As of 2025 the Discogs catalog lists over 560 individual release entries.

Key Personnel and Production

Tony Doogan is the label’s most recurring engineer and producer, credited on 60+ releases. He has been Mogwai’s principal studio collaborator from Happy Songs For Happy People (2003) onward, and also produced or mixed records by Sacred Paws, Out Lines, Aidan Moffat / RM Hubbert, and others. Paul Savage (of The Delgados and Chemikal Underground) is the second major studio figure, producing Mogwai’s Hardcore Will Never Die, But You Will. (2011), Rave Tapes (2014), and other label releases; he also mastered and recorded records for Arab Strap and Aidan Moffat / RM Hubbert.

Dave Fridmann produced Mogwai’s Every Country’s Sun (2017) and As The Love Continues (2021), working with his assistant Matt Estep at Tarbox Road Studios. John Congleton produced The Bad Fire (2025). Frank Arkwright at Abbey Road is the label’s most-used mastering engineer (62 release credits), followed by Greg Calbi (27 credits).

John Cummings (Mogwai guitarist) produced several non-Mogwai releases on the label, including Part Chimp’s early records and Errors singles. Niall McMenamin at Castle of Doom (Mogwai’s own Glasgow studio) has been an engineer/assistant producer on Mogwai and label releases from the 2010s onward.

DLT (3) is the label’s house designer, credited on over 115 releases — the visual identity of Rock Action Records is largely his work.

Roster Overview

Mogwai

The label’s primary artist with 260 catalog entries. Mogwai released all their studio albums from Rock Action (2001) onward on Rock Action Records, with joint distribution/licensing arrangements for various international territories (Matador in the US for some periods, Pias for Europe). The Twilight Sad

Scottish post-rock/shoegaze band from Kilsyth. Joined Rock Action for It Won’t Be Like This All The Time (2019), produced by Andy MacFarlane and mixed by Chris Coady. Their 2026 album It’s The Long Goodbye features Robert Smith of The Cure (guitar/bass) and is mixed by Chris Coady. Discogs artist ID: 578828. Full page: The Twilight Sad.

Arab Strap

Aidan Moffat and Malcolm Middleton’s reformed duo. Released As Days Get Dark (2021) and I’m Totally Fine With It 👍 Don’t Give A Fuck Anymore 👍 (2024) on Rock Action, both recorded and mixed by Paul Savage. Discogs artist ID: 15449. Full page: Arab Strap.

Errors

Glasgow synth/post-rock group. One of the label’s most prolific non-Mogwai acts with 41 catalog entries across 2005–2015, including the albums It’s Not Something But It Is Like Whatever (2008), Come Down With Me (2010), and Have Some Faith In Magic (2012). John Cummings mixed several early releases. Discogs artist ID: 305059.

Part Chimp

London noise-rock band. Released four albums on Rock Action between 2002 and 2017, with John Cummings producing the early records. Lineup included Tim Cedar, Iain.H, and (on later records) Joe McLaughlin. Discogs artist ID: 125191.

Kathryn Joseph

Glasgow singer-songwriter. Released three albums on Rock Action: From When I Wake The Want Is (2018), For You Who Are The Wronged (2022), and We Were Made Prey. (2025). Earlier albums produced by Marcus MacKay; later albums co-produced with Lomond Campbell. Discogs artist ID: 4199019.

Sacred Paws

Glasgow/London duo: Eilidh Rodgers (drums/vocals) and Rachel Aggs (guitar/bass/vocals). Released Six Songs EP (2015, mixed by Tony Doogan), Strike A Match (2016), Run Around The Sun (2019), and Jump Into Life (2025) on Rock Action; all produced by Tony Doogan. Discogs artist ID: 3033385.

Blanck Mass

Solo project of Benjamin John Power (of Fuck Buttons). Self-titled debut released on Rock Action in 2011. Discogs artist ID: 2307335. Full page: Blanck Mass.

bdrmm

Hull-based shoegaze/post-punk group. Joined Rock Action for I Don’t Know (2023) and Microtonic (2025), both produced by Alex Greaves. Discogs artist ID: 7255372. Full page: bdrmm.

Aidan Moffat / RM Hubbert

Glasgow singer-songwriter and guitarist. Collaborative releases on the label: Here Lies The Body (2018) and Ghost Stories For Christmas (2018), Cut To Black (2019). Here Lies The Body features Rachel Grimes (piano), Siobhan Wilson (vocals/cello), and John Burgess (saxophone); recorded by Paul Savage and mixed by Tony Doogan. Discogs IDs: Aidan Moffat 58275, RM Hubbert 2514952.

Envy (2)

Tokyo post-hardcore band. Released four albums on Rock Action between 2003 and 2015: A Dead Sinking Story (2003), All The Footprints You’ve Ever Left… (2004), Insomniac Doze (2006), and Atheist’s Cornea (2015). All recorded by Takashi Kitaguchi. Discogs artist ID: 155390.

Remember Remember

Project of Glasgow multi-instrumentalist Graeme J.D. Ronald. Released self-titled debut (2008), The Dancing (2009), and The Quickening (2011) on Rock Action. First album features Mick Cooke (trumpet) and James Hamilton (drums, also of Errors). Discogs artist ID: 1317340.

Swervedriver

Oxford shoegaze veterans. Released Future Ruins (2018) on Rock Action. Discogs artist ID: 159997.

Torche

Miami metal/sludge band. Released self-titled LP (2007) and In Return EP (2007) on Rock Action; sleeve art by John Dyer Baizley. Discogs artist ID: 912768.

Out Lines

Collaboration between Marcus MacKay, James Graham (The Twilight Sad), and Kathryn Joseph. Released Conflats (2017), mixed by Tony Doogan; artwork by Scott Hutchison (Frightened Rabbit). Discogs artist ID: 6082449.

Fuck Buttons

Bristol electronic duo. Split 12” Colours Move / Mogwai Fear Satan released 2008. Discogs artist ID: 1083695.

Catalog Note

Catalog numbers run sequentially with the “ROCKACT” prefix (e.g. ROCKACT55LP for Hardcore Will Never Die, But You Will., ROCKACT140LP for As The Love Continues). Early releases used informal numbering (“rock act 05 cd,” “rockactcd 06”). The label reached ROCKACT170 with Gruff Rhys’s Dim Probs in 2025.
