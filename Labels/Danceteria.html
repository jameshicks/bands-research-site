Overview

Danceteria was a Paris record shop and company (rue du Cardinal Lemoine, 5th arrondissement) that licensed and issued records while also serving as a French distributor for international independent labels from the mid-1980s to the mid-1990s. The bulk of the catalogue — around 118 releases — falls in the 1990s, with a smaller run of 23 in the late 1980s, spanning Indie Rock, Alternative Rock, Punk, New Wave, and Electro. The label’s most prominent distribution relationship was with ROIR, the American cassette label. Danceteria operated a sublabel, Act (2), and built a roster that included Philippe Fichot and Norscq.
