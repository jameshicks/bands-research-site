Ariola

German/European major label, part of the BMG / Bertelsmann group. Discogs label ID: 5410 (as used on Dutch/European releases).

Overview

Ariola was the European pop subsidiary of Bertelsmann Music Group (BMG). Active across Europe from the 1960s through the 1990s, releasing pop, rock, and mainstream releases. Leo Bouwmeester appeared as a session pianist on a 1993 Dutch alternative rock album released on this label.
