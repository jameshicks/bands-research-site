Cheree Records

Discogs label name: Cheree
Discogs ID (label): 53327
Discogs ID (parent company): 21337 (Cheree Records)
Active: 1988–1992
Base: South Woodford / Leytonstone, London, UK
Successor: Ché Trading
Umbrella: Rocket Girl (retrospective grouping)

UK independent label founded by Paul May (of SkullF**K) and Nick Allport, growing directly out of their Sowing Seeds fanzine — described in the Discogs profile as “one of London’s best known and loved Fanzines” of the late 1980s indie scene. Vinita Joshi (later of Ché Trading and Rocket Girl) was also centrally involved, managing The Telescopes as a teenager and running day-to-day operations alongside Allport from their flat in Leytonstone.

Cheree is best known as the label that launched The Telescopes, releasing their first three vinyl records, but the catalog is broader: Bark Psychosis’s first two singles, The Pooh Sticks’s most prolific release run, early Disco Inferno, and a surprise early release by Barenaked Ladies (before their Canadian breakthrough).

History and Closure

Cheree ran until approximately 1992, when it was liquidated after falling “prey to a pair of crooked financial backers” (per Vinita Joshi’s later accounts). The label did not wind down gradually — it was forced to close.

Nick Allport and Vinita Joshi immediately founded Ché Trading directly out of the ashes, continuing with much of the same roster: Disco Inferno, who had just appeared on Cheree’s final releases, became Ché’s first signing (CHE01–04). Joshi is also quoted describing herself as having been a teenage Telescopes manager who co-ran Cheree before transitioning to Ché.

Joshi later grew frustrated with diminished creative control at Ché Trading and founded Rocket Girl independently in 1997, after Ché wound down. Rocket Girl retrospectively groups Cheree, Ché, and related imprints (i, Indus Sonica, PASK) in its related-labels catalog.

Origins

The label emerged from the Sowing Seeds fanzine culture. Paul May was already active in the UK underground as a member of SkullF**K. The first Cheree release came in summer 1988: a 7” flexi split between Loop (“Soundhead”, live at ULU London) and The Telescopes (“Forever Close Your Eyes”) — catalog number CHEREE1/CHERREE1. The Telescopes’ “Kick The Wall” 7” followed in 1989 as CHEREE2, launching both band and label.

Key Release Detail

The Telescopes — Taste (1989, CHEREE9CD)

The Telescopes’ debut album, produced by Richard Formby with the band. Core lineup: Stephen Lawrie (vocals, guitars, words, music), David Fitzgerald (guitar), Joanna Doran (guitar, French horn), Robert Brooks (bass), Dominic Dillon (drums). Engineers: Chris Bell (4), Ken MacPherson. Also features Rose Emery (piano) and Julian Overton (violin). All songs written by Lawrie.

Bark Psychosis — Nothing Feels / I Know (1990, CHEREE10T)

Self-produced by the band: Graham Sutton, John Ling, Mark Simnett, and Steven Page (2) share production, arrangement, and writing credits. Engineer: Scott Rushbury.

Disco Inferno — Summer’s Last Sound (1992, CHEREE28CD)

Ian Crause (guitar, vocals, sample programming, sleeve design), Paul Willmott (bass), Robert Whatley (drum programming, bass patterns). Co-produced with Charlie McIntosh.

The Pooh Sticks — The Great White Wonder (1991, CHEREE18CD)

Produced by Steve Gregory (2). Lineup includes Hue Williams (vocals), Alison (7) (bass), Stephanie (12) (drums), Paul (181) (guitar), Trudi Tangerine (tambourine, Arp & Moog, piano). Notable guest: Amelia Fletcher on vocals.
