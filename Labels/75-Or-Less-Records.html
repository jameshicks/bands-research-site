We obviously couldn’t compete in the modern music business, so we aren’t going to try. Convincing anyone of anything positive has become too difficult. By putting this music out there, there’s hope the right music fans will find it. The label specializes in small print runs on vinyl or compact disc, with hand printed/assembled packaging, and offers each album for sale through the label webpage. Although we distribute promotional copies and downloads for our bands to places they will most be appreciated by various like-minded media outlets, we do not distribute our cds in stores outside the Providence, RI/ Southeastern Mass area. Typical releases have between 50-500 copies made. We also offer advice on affordable custom packaging creation and design, recording, live booking, equipment hook-ups, promotion and a shoulder to cry on. 99% of our bands aren’t on tour, but perform live in the RI/Mass area, hence the traditional role of ‘label’ is quite overstated.

It was recognized by Time Magazine, USA Today, Yahoo!, and The Bloggies back when print media was still king.

Overview

75 Or Less Records is a Providence, RI–based DIY label founded in February 2006, releasing indie, punk, garage, and lo-fi music in small hand-assembled editions of 50–500 copies on vinyl and CD, distributed locally within the Rhode Island and southeastern Massachusetts area rather than through national retail. It grew out of a music-review website of the same name that ran 2001–2013, publishing capsule reviews under 75 words each and earning recognition from Time Magazine, USA Today, and Yahoo!. The catalogue totals 465 credits across 179 selected releases, concentrated in the 2010s, with roster artists — including Kraig Jordan, Sick Pills, Mark Cutler, Six Star General, and Minky Starshine — active primarily in the local live scene.
