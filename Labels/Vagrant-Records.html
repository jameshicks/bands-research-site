Vagrant Records

Vagrant Records is an American independent record label founded in Los Angeles, California. Discogs label ID: 33933. The label launched in 1995 with a catalog rooted in California punk and hardcore, then pivoted sharply into emo and indie rock in the late 1990s, becoming one of the central labels of the early-2000s emo wave. It released defining records by The Get Up Kids, Dashboard Confessional, Alkaline Trio, Saves The Day, The Hold Steady, Thrice, and Senses Fail, among others. The label operated independently until November 2014, when it was acquired by BMG Rights Management. Label code: LC 14593.

Overview

Vagrant Records was co-founded by Rich Egan — who also served as manager and is credited on the large majority of the label’s releases — and Jon Cohen. It initially released California punk records under the “Vagrant Records” branding, with early catalog entries including Face To Face (joining in 1996), The Hippos, and No Motiv. The 1997 comp Before You Were Punk and its 1999 follow-up helped establish the label’s identity in the genre.

The label’s commercial and critical center of gravity shifted in 1999 with the release of The Get Up Kids’ Something To Write Home About (cat. VR340), a record now credited as a touchstone of the second-wave emo era. The Anniversary, Reggie And The Full Effect, and The New Amsterdams followed in quick succession, solidifying Vagrant’s reputation as the home of Midwestern emotional rock. The next two years saw Dashboard Confessional’s breakthrough (the acoustic The Swiss Army Romance in 2000; The Places You Have Come To Fear The Most in 2001) and Alkaline Trio’s move to the label with From Here To Infirmary (2001).

Through 2001–2005 Vagrant operated at the center of post-hardcore and emo’s mainstream moment. Saves The Day’s Stay What You Are (2001) and Dashboard Confessional’s MTV Unplugged v2.0 (2002) both crossed over to mainstream audiences. Alkaline Trio remained the label’s most consistent sales anchor across multiple albums through the 2000s and beyond. The label also signed artists further outside the punk sphere during this period, including Rocket From The Crypt, Eels (from 2005), and Paul Westerberg (from 2002).

In the mid-to-late 2000s Vagrant expanded into harder post-hardcore (Thrice, Protest The Hero, Senses Fail, From Autumn To Ashes, Alexisonfire) while also bringing in indie rock and singer-songwriter acts. The Hold Steady signed in 2006 and released Boys And Girls In America to wide critical acclaim. School Of Seven Bells, Edward Sharpe And The Magnetic Zeros, City And Colour, and PJ Harvey (US releases) joined the roster in the label’s next phase. Edward Sharpe’s Up From Below (2009) was a crossover success.

After BMG acquired Vagrant in November 2014, the label continued operating under the Vagrant name while its catalog moved under BMG Rights Management GmbH (Discogs parent ID: 281003). Post-acquisition releases include work by Bad Suns, L.P., Mayer Hawthorne, Albert Hammond Jr., Mark Lanegan Band, and the Bloc Party album Hymns (2016). The label sublabel “Vagrant” (ID: 39982) carried some releases under the shorter logo branding; additional associated imprints include Hotly Wanting (ID: 757275) and Startime Intl Fader Label Enterprises (ID: 19394).

Key recurring personnel across the catalog: producers Brian McTernan and Jerry Finn (each on ~46–50 releases), Rob Schnapf, and Matt Allison; mastering by Ramón Bretón, Howie Weinberg, Bob Ludwig, Ted Jensen, and Don C. Tyler; design by Joby J Ford and Ben Goetting (each credited on 90+ releases); management by Rich Egan throughout.

Open Questions

  • Founding year: The Discogs profile does not specify an exact founding year. The label’s earliest catalog entries on Discogs are from 1995 (cat. VR3160-VR320), and the roster’s first proper releases (Face To Face, etc.) are from 1996. Year of formal incorporation and exact founding story need web sources.
  • Founders: Rich Egan is widely associated with the label’s founding but the identity of co-founders (Jon Cohen is commonly cited but not confirmed in Discogs data) requires verification.
  • Hot Hot Heat and The Gaslight Anthem: Both bands are sometimes associated with Vagrant in outside sources, but neither appears in the Discogs Vagrant catalog as primary artists. Hot Hot Heat was on V2/Sub Pop; The Gaslight Anthem was on SideOneDummy. Their connection to Vagrant (if any) may be limited to licensing or regional distribution deals not captured in Discogs.
  • Startime Intl Fader Label Enterprises (ID: 19394) and Hotly Wanting (ID: 757275) sublabels: nature and scope of these imprints not fully explored.
  • The label’s address history (Santa Monica CA, then Sunset Blvd LA, and a Methuen MA address) suggests possible operational splits — not clarified from Discogs data alone.
