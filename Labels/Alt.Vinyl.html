Overview

Alt.Vinyl was a small UK label run by Graham Thrower, originally out of a physical shop in Newcastle and later operating online from Northumberland, that released drone, noise, psychedelic, acid folk, and experimental recordings exclusively on analogue formats — vinyl and tape — over roughly thirteen years before closing in May 2017. Its catalogue of around 33 releases is concentrated almost entirely in the 2010s, with just one release from the 2000s. The roster included notable acts such as Richard Dawson and the influential industrial/noise group Zoviet France, and the label spun off a dedicated sublabel, Alt.Vinyl 8” Lathe Cuts Series, for small-run lathe-cut releases.
