Overview

No Threes Records is a punk label out of Seattle, Washington, founded in 1979. Its roster is essentially the Fastbacks circle — Kurt Bloch, Kim Warnick, Lulu Gargiulo, and Al Bloch all feature — suggesting a band-run imprint rather than an independent operation. The catalogue began with a handful of releases in the late 1970s and 1980s spanning punk, power pop, and hardcore, then lay dormant for roughly two decades before resurging strongly in the 2010s and 2020s, which account for twelve of the label’s eighteen known releases.
