Cleopatra

US independent record label based in Los Angeles. Discogs label ID: 1004.

Overview

Long-running US indie with a broad catalog spanning punk, goth, metal, and rock reissues. Released the Waldos’ Wacka Lacka Loom Bop A Loom Bam Boo (CLO0852, 2018) and various Johnny Thunders / Heartbreakers related archival releases.
