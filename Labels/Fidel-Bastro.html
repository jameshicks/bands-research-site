Overview

Fidel Bastro is a German label founded in 1992 with a focus on noise rock and experimental sounds at the margins. Its catalogue started slowly in the 1990s — five releases — and expanded through the 2000s before accelerating into the 2010s and 2020s with over twenty releases across those two decades, suggesting unbroken activity across thirty-plus years. Its roster spans Pyrolator alongside less-known acts such as Boy Division, Stephan Mensger, Dirk Simmig, and Potato Fritz, drawing on noise, post-punk, punk, and experimental styles throughout.

Notes (web)

Pyrolator / Fidel Bastro connection

Pyrolator (Kurt Dahlke) does not appear to have released material as a recording artist on Fidel Bastro. His credits on Fidel Bastro releases are as a mastering engineer at Ata Tak Studio Berlin, not as a performing or recording artist. Multiple FREISPIEL (Stephan Mensger) albums on Fidel Bastro carry the mastering credit “Pyrolator (Ata Tak Studio Berlin)”:

  • Dangerous Eiertanz (FB 92, 2016) — mastering: Pyrolator, Ata Tak Studio Berlin [https://freispiel.bandcamp.com/album/dangerous-eiertanz]
  • Degeneration Deluxe (FB 97, 2017) — mastering credit confirmed via Discogs [https://www.discogs.com/release/11054881-FREISPIEL-Degeneration-Deluxe]
  • Nonsens Konsens (FB 101, 2018) and Die goldene Mitte (FB 104, 2019) — same mastering arrangement [https://freispiel.bandcamp.com/album/die-goldene-mitte]
  • Revue — mastering: Pyrolator, Ata Tak Studio Berlin [https://freispiel.bandcamp.com/album/revue]

The route is therefore a professional studio connection rather than a personal scene relationship: Stephan Mensger (FREISPIEL) used Pyrolator’s mastering services at Ata Tak Studio Berlin for a run of Fidel Bastro releases in the mid-to-late 2010s. No documented personal friendship, NDW/Düsseldorf scene overlap, or Ata Tak-to-Fidel-Bastro label tie has been found in published sources.

The Discogs credit that placed Pyrolator in the Fidel Bastro “roster” (and that the Overview above reflects) almost certainly reflects these mastering credits, not artist credits. The Overview’s phrase “Its roster spans Pyrolator” is therefore misleading.
