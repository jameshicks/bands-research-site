US label. Label Code: LC 3456 / LC 03456. New Albion records was a new-music label founded in 1983 by Foster Reed. It was named after what Sir Francis Drake named the San Francisco Bay after being the first European to see the area, which was near where the label was founded. During the mid-2000s, after over 20 years there, Reed relocated the label and his family to the upper Hudson valley of New York state. In 2010, the label ceased its retail operations. Overview

New Albion was a San Francisco-based new-music label founded in 1983 by Foster Reed, its name drawn from Sir Francis Drake’s designation for the Bay Area — the region where the label was established. Its catalogue of roughly 55 releases concentrated on contemporary classical, experimental, and ambient music, with artists including Stuart Dempster and John Adams; output was heaviest across the 1980s and 1990s before dropping sharply in the 2000s. Reed relocated the label to New York’s Hudson Valley in the mid-2000s, and after more than two decades in operation New Albion ceased retail in 2010 and closed entirely in 2012, returning unsold stock to its artists.
