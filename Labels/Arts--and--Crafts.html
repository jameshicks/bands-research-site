Overview

Arts & Crafts Productions is a Toronto-based independent label founded in 2002, operating across recording, management, merchandising, and publishing as a full-service artist services company. Widely regarded as one of Canada’s most important indie labels, it has accumulated 21 JUNO Awards. The roster centres on Broken Social Scene and its constellation of members — including Kevin Drew, Justin Peroff, and Charles Spearin — with a catalogue spanning indie rock, folk rock, and experimental styles across 268 releases, the majority concentrated in the 2010s. It operates under the parent Arts & Crafts International and has issued releases through sublabels Arts & Crafts Sampler and Madic Records.
