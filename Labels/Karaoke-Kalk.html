Karaoke Kalk

Discogs ID: 852

German independent electronic label. 379 releases in Discogs. Sublabel: Kalk Pets (Discogs ID: 37536).

Overview

Cologne/Berlin-based label active from the early 2000s, home to experimental electronic, post-techno, and avant-pop. Key artists include März (Ekkehard Ehlers + Albrecht Kunze) and others in the German experimental underground.

Key Artists (Partial)

  • März — four releases 2001–2004
  • The National Jazz Trio Of Scotland (Bill Wells + Lorna Gilfedder) — Standards series Vol. I–VI (2007–2023) and Christmas Album (2012)
  • Bill Wells — solo releases incl. Osaka Bridge (2006), Paper Of Pins (2009), Pianotapes (2010)

Connections

  • März — four releases 2001–2004
  • Ekkehard Ehlers — via März releases
  • Bill Wells — solo releases and National Jazz Trio of Scotland
