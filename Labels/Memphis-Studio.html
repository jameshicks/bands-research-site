Memphis Studio

Discogs ID: 350212
Location: Lillie House, 1a Conduit Street, Leicester LE2 7PU (near Leicester train station)
Also known as: Stayfree Studio (the broader rehearsal complex)
Active: c. early 1990s – 2008/9
Run by: Kev Reverb (Kevin Bayliss)

Overview

Memphis Studio was the recording studio component of the Stayfree Studio rehearsal rooms complex on Conduit Street, Leicester, a short walk from Leicester train station. The building was Lillie House at 1a Conduit Street (later abbreviated to “Stayfree, Conduit Street” in contact listings). The two names — Memphis Studio and Stayfree Studio — are used interchangeably in Discogs, both pointing to the same physical space run by Kev Reverb.

The studio closed around 2008/9 when the Stayfree rehearsal rooms relocated to 2–4 Frog Island, Leicester LE3 5AG. The Frog Island premises continued as Stayfree Music (stayfreemusic.co.uk); the recording studio component does not appear to have transferred with it.

The connection to the earlier The S+T Label (c. 1979–1982) is through Kev Reverb personally: he was one of the S+T Label’s four co-founders. However, the S+T sessions pre-date the Stayfree/Memphis Studio infrastructure — the S+T singles used separate engineers (Derek Tompkins on ST 1; Simon Jones on ST 2; Colin Richardson on ST 3). Memphis Studio is a later operation, traceable primarily to the 1990s–2000s.

One Discogs release credit explicitly names “Memphis Studios” as the recording location: Andy Griffiths’ Strawberry Girl (Oblong Music, 2004), where Kev Reverb is credited as “Engineer [Memphis Studios]” on tracks 3 and 7.

Key Acts Recorded Here

Volcano The Bear (Discogs ID: 119766)

By far the most documented act recorded at Stayfree/Memphis Studio. Leicester-based experimental group: Aaron Moore, Daniel Padden, Laurence Coleman, Nick Mott (core lineup; Paul Warrener and Sarah Cawkwell also members). Kev engineered or recorded at least seven distinct VTB releases between 1998 and 2008, spanning their Volucan, United Dairies, Pickled Egg, and Beta-lactam Ring Records periods. Kev Reverb and Paul Warrener share co-production credit on the Spine (5) single (1993), which predates the VTB recordings.

G.R.O.W.T.H. (Discogs ID: 238953)

Appears on two Stayfree label releases (STAY004CD For Lack Of Horses They Saddle Dogs, 1993; STAY007 $TAR$, 1994). Kev is credited as Producer and Engineer on $TAR$. Global Cut (Discogs ID: 206188) is credited for programming.

The Ugly Music Show (Discogs ID: 7781426)

Apron (Stayfree, STAY008CD, 1994) — Kev Reverb and Rob Vom credited as “Recorded By.”

Cornershop (Discogs ID: 7203)

Cornershop used Kev for engineering on single tracks from two releases: “Hold On It Hurts” (1994, Wiiija) and “Brimful Of Asha” (1997, Alternation). Rob Reverb (Discogs ID: 6058594) appears alongside Kev on both of these.

Andy Griffiths (2) (Discogs ID: 3926670)

Strawberry Girl (Oblong Music, 2004) — tracks 3 and 7 recorded at Memphis Studios by Kev Reverb. The remaining tracks were recorded at Tiny Studios by Neil Segrott.

Kansas City Faggots (2) (Discogs ID: 7760266)

…Knock One Off In Memphis (KCF Record Company, undated) — country covers album recorded by “Rev. Rooster Reverb” (a name variation of Kev Reverb). The title is a direct nod to the studio name.

Spine (5) (Discogs ID: 1782205)

Tiny Bones / Jack’s Hit (Fluff (2), 1993) — produced and engineered by Kev Reverb and Paul Warrener (with Rob Vom and the band). This is one of the earliest documented sessions.

Po! (2) (Discogs ID: 548657)

A Page A Day (Elefant Records, Spain, 1996) — engineered by Kev Reverb. Band fronted by Ruth Miller.

Relationship to The S+T Label

The S+T Label (active c. 1979–1982) was co-founded by Kev Reverb alongside Chris Freer, Paul Betts, and Mick Dawkeye. The S+T catalog (ST 1, ST 2, ST 3, STAB 1) predates the Stayfree/Memphis Studio infrastructure; those sessions used different engineers and locations. The connection is biographical rather than operational: Kev Reverb’s career as an engineer runs from the S+T era through the Memphis Studio period, but the two are distinct chapters.

No Discogs credits link any S+T act directly to Memphis Studio.

Relationship to Stayfree (Label, ID: 31048)

Rob Reverb

Rob Reverb (Discogs ID: 6058594) appears alongside Kev on the Cornershop sessions. No real name or further profile is documented in Discogs. The “Reverb” surname suggests a close association with the Stayfree/Memphis circle, possibly a family member or long-term studio associate.

Connections

  • Kev Reverb — studio proprietor and primary engineer; also co-founder of The S+T Label
  • Stayfree Studio — the broader rehearsal rooms complex; Memphis Studio was the recording studio within it
  • The S+T Label — earlier Leicester independent label; co-founded by Kev Reverb; distinct operation predating Memphis Studio
  • Volcano The Bear — most extensively documented act recorded here; multiple albums 1998–2008
  • Cornershop — used the studio for individual tracks in 1994 and 1997
  • Rob Reverb — recurring co-engineer on Cornershop sessions
  • Paul Warrener — Volcano The Bear member; co-produced Spine (5) with Kev
  • Volucan — Volcano The Bear’s primary label; many Volucan releases recorded here
  • Pickled Egg Records — released Volcano The Bear material recorded here
  • Beta-lactam Ring Records — US label; released several VTB albums recorded at this studio
  • United Dairies — Steven Stapleton’s label; released VTB material recorded here

Open Questions

  • No Discogs credits connect the S+T Label sessions (ST 1–STAB 1) to Memphis Studio or Stayfree by name. Was Kev Reverb involved as engineer on the S+T singles, or only as label co-founder? The S+T engineers on record are Derek Tompkins (ST 1) and Simon Jones (ST 2).
  • The “Stayfree” releasing label (ID 31048) published several 1992–1996 releases. Were all of these recorded in the Conduit Street facility, or did the label occasionally license/distribute outside recordings?
  • Rob Reverb: real name, relationship to Kev, and whether he had any independent engineering work beyond the Cornershop sessions.
  • The undated …Knock One Off In Memphis (Kansas City Faggots) — no released_year in Discogs; likely recorded before the studio’s 2008/9 closure.
  • Were any acts from the Where The Hell Is…Leicester? compilation (STAB 1, 1981) later recorded at Memphis Studio?
