Off Course Records

Independent Swiss record label. Discogs label ID: 15964. Parent company: Off Course Music (ID: 296919). Address: Postfach 1, 3906 Saas Fee, Switzerland.

Overview

Off Course Records is the Swiss independent label that documented the Basel/Bern punk and new wave scene starting in 1979–80. It is the label home of Grauzone, Stephan Eicher, and Liliput, among others, and serves as both a document of the Swiss underground and the launching point for Eicher’s eventual crossover into Francophone pop.

The label is physically based in Saas Fee (canton of Valais), and is affiliated with SUISA (Swiss music rights society). Off Course Music (the publishing arm) is also registered with ASCAP and BMI in the US, suggesting some American rights infrastructure.

A sublabel, Off Course Rhythm Records (ID: 970055), handled rockabilly-oriented releases in the early 1990s.

NYC Connections

The label’s primary artist, Stephan Eicher, had his 1983 LP Les Chansons Bleues (ASL 3310) produced and arranged by Ed Bahlman, founder of 99 Records (2) in New York. Mastering on that LP was done at Masterdisk, NYC (engineer: Bill Kipper). This is the only documented NYC production-side involvement on the Off Course catalog. No other Off Course releases carry NYC production or engineering credits.
