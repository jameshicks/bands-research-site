Overview

Outpost Recordings was a record label that launched in 1995 and built its catalogue almost entirely across the second half of that decade, issuing 41 releases in the 1990s before trailing off to 6 in the 2000s. The roster sat at an unusual crossroads of genres: Days of the New and Travis Meeks occupied alternative rock territory, while The Crystal Method brought Big Beat and Breakbeat electronics to the label, and Hayden contributed a more acoustic, country-leaning sound. Producer Scott Litt also appears among the credited names. Its corporate parentage and distribution arrangements are not documented on Discogs.
