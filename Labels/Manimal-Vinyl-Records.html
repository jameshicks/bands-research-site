Overview

Manimal Vinyl Records is an independent label based in Los Angeles, founded by Paul Beahan in 2007. Its catalogue spans indie rock, experimental, electro, and synth-pop, growing from 12 releases in the 2000s to 25 in the 2010s. The label operates under the Manimal Group, LLC umbrella and also runs the sublabel L.O.O.S.E Music. Its roster has included Natasha Khan (Bat for Lashes), Josh Pearson, David Kosten, Caroline Weeks, and David Muller.
