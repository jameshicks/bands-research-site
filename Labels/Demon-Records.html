Demon Records

Discogs label ID: 8522

UK independent record label founded in London in 1980 by Jake Riviera (born Andrew Jakeman), Elvis Costello, and Nick Lowe. The legal entity behind the label was Demon Records Ltd. (UK company number 01495331, incorporated 6 May 1980), which operated as the immediate subsidiary of F-Beat Records Ltd. Demon launched as a home for the founders’ own back catalog and new signings, and through the 1980s became one of the UK’s most significant importers and licensors of American roots, blues, New Wave, and indie music. Its primary album series, the FIEND catalog, runs from FIEND 1 (Dr. John Plays Mac Rebennack, 1982) through to FIEND 940+ in the late 1990s, representing the core of the label’s licensed and original releases.

On 6 July 1998, Demon Records Ltd. was acquired by Crimson Productions Ltd. (parent: Demon Music Group Ltd.), and the label was merged into Westside Records operations. The Demon Music Group Ltd. later passed through Kingfisher plc and Woolworths Group plc before ending up as part of BBC Worldwide / 2 Entertain. The label now operates under Demon Music Group Ltd. as a reissue imprint based at Television Centre, London.

Key Artists

Elvis Costello and the Stiff/F-Beat Circle

The label’s first major function was as a catalog home for the back catalog and reissues of its founders and their circle. Elvis Costello and The Attractions are the single largest primary-credit presence on Demon, with releases beginning in 1984 and continuing through the mid-1990s. Key reissues include Armed Forces (FIEND 21, 1984), My Aim Is True (IMP FIEND 13, 1986), Get Happy!! (IMP FIEND 24, 1986), and Trust (IMP FIEND CD 30, 1986). Costello compilations, box sets, and rarities — including Out Of Our Idiot (X FIEND 67, 1987) and the 5-CD 2½ Years (DPAM BOX 1, 1993) — also appeared here. Nick Lowe has a parallel presence, with albums including 16 All-Time Lowes (FIEND 20, 1984), Basher: The Best Of Nick Lowe (D-FIEND 142, 1989), and The Impossible Bird (FIENDCD757, 1994). Graham Parker is heavily represented from 1988 onwards, with albums from The Mona Lisa’s Sister (FIEND 122, 1988) through to the mid-1990s. Rockpile (Seconds Of Pleasure, FIEND 28, 1988) and The Attractions appeared on the label in their own right.

American Roots and Blues (FIEND series)

From the first FIEND release — Dr. John Plays Mac Rebennack (FIEND 1, 1982) — Demon established itself as the UK conduit for New Orleans R&B, Texas blues, and American roots music. The pattern was consistent: license albums from smaller US independents for UK (and sometimes European) release, with minimal re-packaging. Key recurring artists in this vein include:

US Indie / Alternative

  • American Music Club — California (FIEND 134, 1988) and United Kingdom (FIEND 151, 1989), both licensed from Grifter Records. These were the band’s UK debut releases; California was produced by Tom Mallon and features Bruce Kaphan on pedal steel. United Kingdom (FIEND 151) followed in 1989, also produced by Mallon.

Sublabels and Related Imprints

Demon Records operated a cluster of sublabels, all of which now sit under the Demon Music Group umbrella:

  • Imposter Records (ID: 49871) — primarily used for certain Elvis Costello releases; cat nos prefixed “IMP FIEND”
  • Code 90 (ID: 45500) — specialist imprint, limited releases
  • Diablo Records (ID: 95469) — reissues and budget-line releases
  • Drop Out Records (2) (ID: 71418) — small UK imprint under Demon
  • Mau Mau Records (ID: 20101) — later reissue/compilation imprint (e.g., What’s The Matter Boy? by Vic Godard / Subway Sect, MAUCD 645, 1996)
  • Zippo Records (ID: 50420) — used for some American Music Club and related US indie licensed releases in the UK; see Zippo Records
  • Classic 45s (ID: 1027660)
  • Demon Records Deluxe (ID: 1491172)

The FIEND and VEX catalog series were not separate sublabels but catalog prefixes within the main Demon imprint. “VEX” appears primarily on The Damned releases and a small number of American imports (T-Bone Burnett, The Connells, Aaron Neville). The “TRANDEM” prefix was used for UK folk reissues (Bert Jansch, Pentangle, John Renbourn). “REU” appears on Cajun/zydeco and roots releases (Nanci Griffith, Barrence Whitfield, Duke Robillard).

Edsel Records (ID: 22779) is a sibling label — also under Demon Music Group Ltd. — specializing in classic rock and pop reissues. Edsel was not a sublabel of Demon Records itself but a co-equal imprint within the group.

Recurring Personnel

Key behind-the-scenes figures across the label’s 1980s–1990s catalog:

  • Nick Lowe — most prolific producer in the Demon/FIEND catalog; produced Elvis Costello’s early albums and many licensed US releases
  • Roger Bechirian (ID: 206137) — house engineer and remastering figure, 21 releases
  • Michael Krage — principal designer across 75+ releases
  • Phil Smee — sleeve design on 24+ releases
  • Porky (5) (ID: 473994) — lacquer cutting on 100+ releases
  • Pete Thomas and Steve Nieve — The Attractions sidemen recurring across Costello catalog releases
