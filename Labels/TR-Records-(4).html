TR Records (4)

Discogs ID: 1108292 Location: USA Active: 2008–2020 (Discogs catalog range) URLs: knm-music.com / soundcloud.com/knm_music

Soulful house label based in the US, functioning as the parent/holding entity for Tony Records (Discogs ID: 76429) and Yellorange (Discogs ID: 721). Formed when Tony Humphries left Tony Records in October 2016; his two business partners — DJ Jacko (2) and Maurizio Clemente — continued the enterprise as TR Records, carrying forward the Tony Records and Yellorange back catalogs. Humphries remained credited as A&R and executive producer on TR Records (4) releases from 2008 through at least 2015, before the formal split.

Label History

The lineage runs from Yellorange (1998–2003, active soulful house imprint folded after distributor Strictly Rhythm collapsed) → Tony Records (est. 2006, East Orange NJ, Humphries’ rebuild under his own name) → TR Records (4) (post-2016, new operating entity after Humphries’ departure).

Per the Yellorange Discogs profile: “Its catalog is being revived through Humphries’s new label, Tony Records. As of October 2016, the label’s catalogue is controlled by TR Records (4).”

Per the Tony Records profile: “In October 2016, Tony Humphries left the label, and his two business partners, DJ Jacko (2) and Maurizio Clemente, started a new label called TR Records, which now controls the Tony Records and Yellorange catalogues.”

The label has strong Italian connections — many artists and producers are Italy-based (Peppe Citarella, Walter Ricci, DJ Jacko (2) / Giacomo Berarducci). This Euro-soulful-house axis is a defining feature of the post-Humphries catalog.
