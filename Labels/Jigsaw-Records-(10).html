Overview

Portland, Oregon indie label founded in 1995 and operated by Chris McFarlane (2), doubling as a physical record shop in the city. The roster skews toward indiepop, jangle pop, power pop, and lo-fi twee, with a handful of shoegaze and indie rock releases rounding out the catalogue. Activity was sparse through the late 1990s and 2000s, then surged sharply in the 2010s — 67 releases in that decade alone — before continuing at a steadier pace into the 2020s. Signed acts include Cozy Slippers (2), Animal Daydream, Makoto Gomi, and Joy Cleaner.
