Overview

La Castanya is a record label and show promoter founded in 2008 and based in Barcelona, Spain. Its catalogue leans toward indie rock, experimental, punk, and indie pop, with a single release in the 2000s giving way to a productive 2010s (45 releases) and a sustained presence into the 2020s (19 releases). The label’s roster includes Santi García, Víctor García (3), The Van Pelt, La Paloma (2), and ALIMENT. North American distribution is handled by The Business (2).
