Overview

Saint Marie Records is an independent label launched in 2011 that specialises in shoegaze, dream pop, indie pop, and electronica. Its catalogue is heavily concentrated in the 2010s, with 98 releases in that decade, before activity dropped sharply to just two releases into the 2020s. The label operates Static Waves as a sublabel and has released material by artists including Secret Shine, Seasurfer, Wyatt Parkins, Karl Skivington, and Matt Brown.
