Mordam Records

Discogs label ID: 34255

US independent distribution company and in-house record label based in Sacramento, CA (P.O. Box: San Francisco, CA 94142). Founded in 1983 by Ruth Schwartz — one of the original co-editors of Maximumrocknroll — Mordam became the exclusive North American distributor for dozens of punk rock and independent record labels through the 1980s and 1990s, including Alternative Tentacles, Lookout! Records, Asian Man Records, and Sympathy For The Record Industry, among many others.

In addition to its distribution arm, Mordam operated an in-house record label (catalog prefix MDR) from approximately 1985 to the late 1990s, releasing albums by Faith No More, Victims Family, Rhythm Pigs, The Ex, and others. The label was sold to / merged with Lumberjack Distribution in 2005 to form Lumberjack Mordam Music Group.

A sublabel imprint, Mordam (Discogs label ID: 424451), was used for some releases in parallel with the main Mordam Records imprint.

Founder

Ruth Schwartz (Discogs artist ID: 374791) — co-editor of Maximumrocknroll, founded Mordam in 1983. Credited as producer on several Victims Family releases. No separate vault page yet.
