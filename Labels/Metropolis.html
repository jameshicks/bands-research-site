Overview

Founded by Dave Heckman, Metropolis is an American record label, distributor, and mail-order store rooted in the electro-industrial and darkwave corridor, with additional reach into EBM, synthpop, futurepop, and goth rock. Its catalogue traces to 1995, with 40 releases in its first decade and a total credit footprint of 2,676 reflecting a densely interconnected industrial-scene roster. Anchoring acts include KMFDM (via Sascha Konietzko), Front Line Assembly, and Raymond Watts, with producer Greg Reely appearing as a recurring credit across the catalogue.
