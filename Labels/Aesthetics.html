Overview

Aesthetics is an independent art organization and record label founded in 1996 by Kenneth James Dyber at age 21. While primarily a music operation, it blurs into broader arts practice — its roster, including Pulseprogramming, Damon Locks, Wayne Montana, Daniel Givens, and Marc Hellner, works across painting, film, photography, and design, with a consistent political undercurrent across releases. The catalogue of 29 releases is concentrated in the 2000s, spanning experimental, post-rock, ambient, dub, and abstract styles. Beyond its own releases, the organization distributes seven other independent labels.
