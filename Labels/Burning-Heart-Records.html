Swedish independent rock label, based in Örebro. Discogs label ID: (to be confirmed).

Overview

One of Sweden’s most prominent independent rock/punk labels, active from the early 1990s. Distributed internationally; home to a range of Swedish punk, hardcore, and indie rock acts.
