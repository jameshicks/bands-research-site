Overview

Wave (7) was a Japanese record label tied to the Wave chain of record stores and managed by Masanori Akashi. Active from 1983 through the 1980s and into the 1990s — roughly even splits across both decades — it released across experimental, synth-pop, avant-garde, and alternative rock styles. The roster mixed Japanese artists such as Makoto Kubota and Rinken Teruya with international acts including UK singer-songwriter Tot Taylor and US psychedelic band Rain Parade. The label operated through several sublabels, among them Cinéphile, Mighty Sixties, and Eva Records (3), and had structural ties to Wave Records (13), Diskport, and Diskport-Seibu.
