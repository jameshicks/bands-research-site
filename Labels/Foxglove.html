Foxglove

Discogs label ID: 37482. US CDr imprint based in Tulsa, Oklahoma. Sublabel of Digitalis Recordings, the broader imprint run by Brad Rose (also known as The North Sea). Active primarily 2003–2007, with a handful of stray releases as late as 2018. The label also spawned its own sublabel, Wailing Bones, which released a long-running compilation series.

Foxglove operated squarely in the American experimental underground of the mid-2000s — hand-duplicated CDr releases in small editions, distributed largely through tape/CDr trading networks and mail-order. The catalog mixes drone, free folk, noise, improvised music, and avant-psych from both US and international artists. The Wailing Bones compilation series (at least fourteen volumes across 2004–2007) served as a sampler of the broader Foxglove/Digitalis universe.

Mailing address: PO Box 700810, Tulsa, OK 74170, USA.

Parent Label

Digitalis Recordings (Discogs ID: 59378) — the umbrella imprint run by Brad Rose out of Tulsa, OK. Digitalis maintained several sublabels alongside Foxglove: Arroyo Subscription Series, Arts & Crafts Editions, Bottled Smoke Series, Dial Square Tapes, Digitalis Limited, and Foxy Digitalis (its webzine/review wing).

Sublabels

  • Wailing Bones (Discogs ID: 694525) — compilation sublabel; at least 14 volumes released 2004–2007 (catalog numbers comp002–comp015)

Catalog

The catalog numbering runs roughly foxglove001–foxglove176 plus the comp00x compilation series. The earliest entries (foxglove001–foxglove020) date to 2003–2004. Two earlier releases (FG1001 from 1995 and FG9603CD from 1996) appear to be pre-history or miscatalogued entries predating the Digitalis-era label.

Key Spotlight: Two Dreams Tucked In Tight — W-S Burn (2004)

Catalog: Foxglove 049. CDr, limited edition. Eight tracks (33 min total). Genres: Rock / Experimental. W-S Burn is the project of Steve Gigante. No additional credits on the release; a solo work.

Tracks: “Sometimes,” “Facing North,” “Forgive Me,” “Augustine,” “Sharp,” “Someday,” “Fade,” “Blameless Child.”

Open Questions

  • Who ran Foxglove day-to-day? Brad Rose (The North Sea) is the likely operator given the Digitalis connection, but this is not confirmed in the Discogs data.
  • What were the typical edition sizes? CDr format strongly implies small runs (often under 100 copies) but no print-run data in Discogs.
  • The two apparent pre-history releases (FG1001/1995, FG9603CD/1996) — are these actually a different label sharing the Foxglove name, or an earlier incarnation under Brad Rose?
  • What is the Randal Bays 2014 release (cat# 0360) — genuine late Foxglove output or catalog error?
  • Relationship between Foxglove and the Foxy Digitalis webzine; did the label and zine share personnel?
