From Discogs

“AMPLIFY 2020: quarantine” was an online festival organized and curated by Jon Abbey with the assistance of Vanessa Rossetto and Matthew Revert as a direct response to the 2020 Coronavirus pandemic. Sound artists around the world had been invited to record “aural postcards” from their respective states of isolation, creating a collective journal of the times. Starting from March 20, the fest was put on hold between May 31 and June 2 in solidarity with the protesters against George Floyd’s killing by Minneapolis police on May 25. “AMPLIFY 2020: quarantine” ended after six months with the release of the encore – an overdub of every track in the entire festival – on September 21. The fest was dedicated to Sean Baxter, George Floyd, Simon Fell and Cor Fuhler.

— Discogs label profile, ID 1800239

Overview

Amplify 2020: Quarantine was an online festival and Erstwhile Records sub-imprint organized by Jon Abbey, with Vanessa Rossetto and Matthew Revert, as a direct response to the COVID-19 pandemic lockdowns. From March through September 2020, sound artists worldwide submitted short recordings from isolation — a collective document of that moment — generating 242 releases spanning experimental, electroacoustic, musique concrète, and free improvisation. The festival briefly suspended activity from May 31 to June 2 in solidarity with the George Floyd protests, and concluded with an encore overdub of every track from across the entire run. The project was dedicated to Sean Baxter, George Floyd, Simon Fell, and Cor Fuhler.
