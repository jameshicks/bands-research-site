Overview

Vice Records is a New York-based music label launched in 2002 as an offshoot of Vice Magazine, which had been in operation since 1994, making it one of the first media-brand imprints to cross into record releasing. The catalogue is weighted heavily toward the 2000s — 90 releases in that decade against 31 in the 2010s and near-silence since — and runs across indie rock, garage rock, punk, and alternative rock, with an electro strand threaded through. Notable roster signings include The Black Lips and Bloc Party alongside The Stills and Dimitri Coats; the label sits under the VICE Music, Inc. umbrella and spun off the Wolfgang Morden sublabel.
