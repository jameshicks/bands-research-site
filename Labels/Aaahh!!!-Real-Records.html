Overview

Aaahh!!! Real Records is a Cambridge, UK independent label founded in 2010 by Ian Perry (4) and distributed through Code 7. Its catalogue of around 66 releases skews heavily toward the 2010s, drawing on punk, hardcore, folk, and acoustic indie; the label has remained active into the 2020s at a reduced pace. It runs two related imprints — a dedicated tape series and Conchairto Records — and has built its roster around artists including Tim Holehouse, Naomi Randall, Dan O’Dell, and B-Sydes.
