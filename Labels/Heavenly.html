Heavenly

UK independent record label. Home to Saint Etienne from their debut in 1990 through most of their career. Ian Catt appears on Heavenly releases continuously from 1990 onward.

Key Saint Etienne releases on Heavenly: Foxbase Alpha (1991), So Tough (1993), Tiger Bay (1994), He’s On The Phone (1995), Casino Classics (1996), Words And Music By Saint Etienne (2012), and others.

Also home to Manic Street Preachers, Doves, Amelia Fletcher’s projects, and other UK indie acts. Q-Tee released the solo single “Gimme That Body” on Heavenly in 1995 and also appears on the label as a featured artist on Saint Etienne’s “Filthy” (1991).
