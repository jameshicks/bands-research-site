Overview

DuPage County Hardcore is a Chicago, Illinois label founded in 2013 by Dave Hofer, dedicated to re-releasing and preserving rare music from the region’s punk and hardcore scenes. The label retains copyright jointly with its artists, spanning styles from hardcore and punk through pop punk, emo, and ska. Its entire catalogue of 40 releases falls within the 2010s, with roster acts including Apocalypse Hoboken alongside individuals such as Phil Bonnet, Matt Allison, Chuck Uchida, and Douglas Ward. The label also operates a sublabel, D.C.Live.
