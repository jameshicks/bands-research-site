Overview

Snowstorm is a UK independent label that launched in 1998 and concentrated most of its activity across the 2000s, assembling a catalogue of roughly 26 releases in indie rock, folk rock, folk, and acoustic styles. Its roster skews toward singer-songwriters and acoustic-leaning acts, most notably former Belle and Sebastian cellist Isobel Campbell, who recorded solo material in this period, alongside UK folk-punk performer Chris T-T. The presence of both Ian Painter and Clive Painter on the roster suggests possible familial or band ties among the label’s artists.

Isobel Campbell – Snowstorm signing story

Searched for how Isobel Campbell came to sign with Snowstorm for Amorino (2003) and Time Is Just The Same (2004). No source found that explains the connection.

What is established from web sources:

  • Campbell left Belle and Sebastian in spring 2002 during their North American tour. Stuart Murdoch stated she “said that the band hadn’t really been her thing for a couple of years now, and I think she was much more interested in her solo career.” en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isobel_Campbell
  • Her two Gentle Waves records (1999, 2000) were on Jeepster Records, the same label as Belle and Sebastian. When she went solo under her own name, she did not remain with Jeepster — she moved to Snowstorm for the first two proper solo LPs. en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isobel_Campbell
  • Amorino was recorded between 2000–2002 and released 7 October 2003 on Snowstorm (UK) / Instinct Records (US). en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amorino_(album)
  • Snowstorm is described as a London-based independent label. en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_T-T
  • Chris T-T signed to Snowstorm after his 1999 debut, so the label was already active and signing acts when Campbell came aboard. No source names who ran Snowstorm or brokered the Campbell deal.
