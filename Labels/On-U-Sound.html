On-U Sound

Discogs label ID: 2409 (parent: On-U Sound Records And Music Ltd., ID 469461) Founded: 1980, London Founder / primary producer: Adrian Sherwood (born Adrian Maxwell Sherwood, 1958) Status: Active; relaunched 2002, catalog numbers above ON-U CD 1001 onwards Label code: LC 8619 / LC 08619

Independent UK label specialising in experimental reggae, dub, post-punk/dub crossover, and industrial dancehall. Run almost entirely as a vehicle for Adrian Sherwood’s production work. Not to be confused with the US DJ/club remix label ON-USound (no space).

Related labels: Pressure Sounds, Green Tea. Sublabels include Pay It All Back, Sound Boy, Noah Records, Reggae Archive, Sherwood At The Controls, ON-U Sound 10” Disco Plate, On-U Sound MasterRecordings, On-U Sound Records.

Overview

On-U Sound was launched in 1980 by Adrian Sherwood as a platform for his dub-infused production work. Its first release (cat. ON-Usound 1) was a split 7” between London Underground (band) and New Age Steppers. From the outset the label blurred the line between UK post-punk and Jamaican sound-system culture: punk and reggae musicians shared studios, album credits, and touring bands in configurations that Sherwood called “Punks & Rastas, Rebels, Rockers.”

Sherwood’s production method — heavy tape manipulation, multi-generational echo, found sounds, unconventional stereo imaging, and live-band dub mixed in real time — defined a recognisable On-U house sound applied consistently across every project on the roster. The same core pool of musicians (the “Maffia”) played on releases credited to African Head Charge, Creation Rebel, Dub Syndicate, Singers & Players, New Age Steppers, Tackhead, Gary Clail, and Mark Stewart And The Maffia.

The label ran strongly through the 1980s with a dense catalog of LPs and 12” disco plates, then went quiet in the late 1990s before relaunching in 2002 with Little Axe’s Hard Grind. Since the relaunch Sherwood has continued to release new material, archive excavations, and reissue series while collaborating with younger artists (Pinch, Nisennenmondai, Speakers Corner Quartet).

Discogs records 736+ unique releases across the On-U Sound label ID, making it one of the larger independent label catalogs in the DB.

Adrian Sherwood — Founder and Producer

Adrian Sherwood (Discogs ID 4969) is the label’s sole constant. He produced 427 releases across the On-U catalog (by credit count in the DB) and is listed under aliases including Crocodile, Prisoner (3), and Eddie Echo. He is a member of Singers & Players, Tackhead, Barmy Army, Missing Brazilians, Gary Clail & On-U Sound System, The Circuit, Voice Of Authority, and others — most of which are in effect studio projects assembled around him.

Before On-U Sound he ran the Hitrun label (1978–80) through which Creation Rebel first appeared. He co-founded Carib Gems and later Pressure Sounds.

Singers & Players

Discogs ID 40537. 40 releases on On-U. The first major On-U Sound collective: a rotating cast of Jamaican vocalists (Prince Far I, Bim Sherman, Mikey Dread, Congo Ashanti Roy, Jah Woosh) and UK post-punk instrumentalists. Members on record include Keith Levene (PiL) and Ari-Up (The Slits) on War Of Words (1981), directly bridging punk and reggae. US rights held by 99 Records. Full personnel documented in Singers & Players.

Key albums: War Of Words (1981, ON-U LP5 / 99 Records US), Revenge Of The Underdog (1982, ON-U LP11), Staggering Heights (1983, ON-U LP23), Leaps & Bounds (1984, ON-LP33), Vacuum Pumping (1988, ON-U LP39).

Creation Rebel

Discogs ID 85421. 37 On-U releases; first appeared on Sherwood’s earlier Hitrun label (1978). Members: Eskimo Fox, Peter Stroud (2), Eric “Fish” Clarke, Clifton “Bigga” Morrison, Desmond “Fatfingers” Coke, and others. Pre-On-U Sound, the band issued Close Encounters Of The Third World and Dub From Creation (both 1978, Hitrun) and Starship Africa (1980, 4D Rhythms) before moving to Statik Records for Psychotic Jonkanoo (1981). On-U Sound releases from 1982 onward. Recent: Hostile Environment (2023), High Above Harlesden 1978–2023 (2024 archive set).

New Age Steppers

Discogs ID 23698. 45 On-U releases. Began on Statik Records (1980–81) before migrating to On-U. Core members: Ari-Up (Ariane Forster; The Slits), Bruce Smith (The Pop Group, PiL), Mark Stewart, Keith Levene, George Oban, Eskimo Fox, Neneh Cherry, Sean Oliver, Jarrett Tomlinson, Lincoln Valentine Scott. Action Battlefield (2000, On-U CD 0003) collects main-era recordings.

Mark Stewart And The Maffia

Discogs ID 23183. 7 On-U releases. Mark Stewart (ex-The Pop Group) worked with the On-U/Maffia collective — Tackhead rhythm section (Keith LeBlanc, Doug Wimbish, Skip McDonald) plus Sherwood production. Jerusalem 12” (1982, ON-U DP05), Learning To Cope With Cowardice LP (1983, ON-U LP24) are the main On-U works; later releases moved to Mute (This Is Stranger Than Love, 1987).

Tackhead

Discogs ID 40228. 8 On-U releases. The industrial-funk unit formed around the Sugarhill Gang rhythm section: Keith LeBlanc (drums), Doug Wimbish (bass), Skip McDonald (guitar), with Adrian Sherwood as mixologist. The trio had been the house band at Sugar Hill Records before Sherwood brought them to London. Tackhead is effectively the On-U Sound house band for the mid-1980s industrial crossover material. Also recorded under aliases Fats Comet and Maffia. Key On-U releases: Mind At The End Of The Tether 12” (1985), What’s My Mission Now? 12” (1985), Hard Left 12” (1986, with Gary Clail), Reality 12” (1988, with Gary Clail). Crossed over to 4th & Broadway (The Game, 1987), SBK Records (Strange Things, 1990). 2006 retrospective Tackhead Sound Crash: Slash & Mix on On-U/EMI.

Gary Clail

Discogs ID 40520 (solo); ID 30764 (Gary Clail & On-U Sound System). MC/vocalist who became Tackhead’s frontman and a significant crossover figure in the late 1980s. 5 solo On-U credits (1985–88) plus 4 as Gary Clail & On-U Sound System (1989–99). Half Cut For Confidence 12” (1985, ON-U DP12) was his debut. End Of The Century Party LP (1989, ON-U LP49) with the On-U Sound System is his main statement.

Sleeve Design

Kishi Yamamoto (Discogs ID 133405) designed and photographed the sleeves for the majority of the 1980s On-U catalog (152 release credits), defining the label’s visual identity. Coneyl Jay took over as primary sleeve designer/photographer into the 1990s and 2000s (59 credits). Die Gestalten and Jaffa (6) handled reissue design.

Lee Perry and the Jamaican Connection

Lee Perry (ID 41272) — the legendary Jamaican producer — appears on 47 On-U releases, primarily from 1987 onward. Time Boom X De Devil Dead (Dub Syndicate / Lee Perry, 1987, ON-U LP43 / Syncopate) is the key collaboration. The 2008–11 period saw a sustained run of Perry releases: The Mighty Upsetter (2008), Dub Setter (2010, with Sherwood), Nu Sound & Version (2011), plus the Jahtari collaboration 12” (2016). His presence anchors the label’s connection to first-generation Jamaican dub.

Vocalist Bim Sherman (ID 9866) also bridges both worlds: 20 On-U releases spanning 1982–2017, from Across The Red Sea (1982) through collaborations with Dub Syndicate and Strange Parcels.

Prince Far I (ID 10352) — another Jamaican vocalist central to the early On-U sound — appears on 9 On-U releases (1982–2024, the later ones posthumous reissues). He was shot dead in September 1983.

Horace Andy (ID 3850) joined the label later; Midnight Rocker (2022, ONULP152) became one of the label’s best-selling recent releases.

Compilations

The Pay It All Back series is the label’s primary compilation sequence:

  • Vol. 1 (1984, ON-U LP37), Vol. 2 (1988, ON-U LP42), Vol. 3 (1991, ON-U LP53), Vol. 4 (1993, ON-U LP63), Vol. 5 (1995, ON-U LP75), Vol. 6 (1996, ON-U CD96), Vol. 7 (2019, ONUCD143), Vol. 8 (2022)

Roots Of Innovation — 15 And X Years On-U Sound (1996, ON-U LP92) — 15th anniversary retrospective.

Sherwood At The Controls Volume 1: 1979–1984 (2015, ONULP128) and Volume 2: 1985–1990 (2016, ONUCD132) — comprehensive archival overview of Sherwood’s productions.

Trevor Jackson Presents Science Fiction Dancehall Classics (2015, ONUCD129) — curated selection by graphic designer Trevor Jackson.
