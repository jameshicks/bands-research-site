Overview

Dindisc was a Virgin Records subsidiary active primarily in the early 1980s, issuing synth-pop and new wave releases under its own imprint before going inactive. The bulk of its catalogue — 46 of 50 known releases — appeared between 1980 and 1989, with a handful of titles dating to its 1979 launch. Its core roster centred on Orchestral Manoeuvres in the Dark and associated personnel including Andy McCluskey, Paul Humphreys, and producer Mike Howlett. The label also ran a sublabel, Snatzo Records. Though the imprint is no longer operational, CD reissues have continued to carry Dindisc catalogue numbers.
