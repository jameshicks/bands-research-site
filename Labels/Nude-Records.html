Nude Records

UK independent record label, founded 1991. Discogs label ID: 28841. Parent company: Nude Records Ltd. (Discogs ID: 335030).

Overview

Nude Records was founded in 1991 by Saul Galpern, a former A&R man at major labels, operating initially from his flat in northwest London. The label launched with Suede, whose self-titled debut album topped the UK charts in 1993 and established Nude as a central address of the Britpop era. Through the mid-1990s the roster expanded to include Sharkboy (2), Goya Dress, Geneva (2), Mainstream, Ultrasound (5), Black Box Recorder, and Lowgold, among others. Billy MacKenzie’s posthumous final album Beyond The Sun (1997) was released on Nude after his death in January of that year.

As the label neared its tenth anniversary, Nude Records went into voluntary liquidation in the early 2000s; the company (Nude Records Ltd.) was formally dissolved on 13 July 2007. Suede’s catalog was picked up by long-term backer Sony Music. Part of the Nude catalog was acquired by One Little Indian, and several artists continued on that label. The brand was relaunched in 2016 under a new company registration (originally incorporated in 2002 as Troubadour Recordings Ltd., renamed Altered States Ltd., then Nude Records from 24 February 2016), with Galpern again at the helm.

Ownership and Distribution

  • Legal entity: Nude Records Ltd., original UK Company No. 02634428, incorporated 1 August 1991
  • Founders: Saul Galpern and Peter Felstead (Peter John Felstead; Discogs artist ID: 1480976)
  • Label code: LC 05632 / LC 5632
  • Distribution (1990s): Primary distributor was Sony Music (appears on ~165 releases in the Discogs DB), with 3MV (122 releases) and Pinnacle (93 releases) also listed as distributors at various points; Vital (2) (81 releases) distributed later catalog. PolyGram Music Ltd. appears as a publishing/rights entity across the early catalog.
  • Publishing: Bare Tunes Ltd. (Nude’s publishing arm) and PolyGram Music Publishing Ltd. handled most rights administration.

Key Personnel Behind the Label

Production: Ed Buller was the dominant in-house producer/engineer, credited on 232 releases and producing every major Suede release through Coming Up (1996). Gary Stout served as assistant producer/engineer on 125 releases. Steve Osborne produced 38 releases; Nigel Godrich produced two tracks on the Ultrasound double album. Mike Hedges produced Geneva (2)‘s Further.

Design: The visual identity of the 1990s catalog was handled primarily by Howard Wakefield (85 releases), Andrew Biscomb and Peter Barrett (3) (each ~64 releases), and Brian Cannon of Microdot (62 releases — Cannon was the go-to designer for the Britpop era, also known for Oasis sleeves). Peter Saville (2) (Discogs ID: 1069010) directed art on ~88 Suede releases, including Coming Up.

Mastering: Bob Ludwig (34 releases) and Bunt Stafford-Clark (32 releases) handled the bulk of mastering.
