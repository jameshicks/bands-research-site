Overview

Urtovox is an Italian independent label founded in 2001, releasing music across indie rock, alternative rock, indie pop, and chanson. Its catalogue built steadily through the 2000s and peaked in the 2010s, with output tapering in the 2020s. The roster has centred on Italian acts including Cesare Basile, Beatrice Antolini, …A Toys Orchestra, Giacomo Fiorenza, and Francesco Donadello — a cluster of artists with strong ties to Italy’s indie and experimental scenes.
