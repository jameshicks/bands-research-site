Overview

Choke was a noise rock label and distributor based in Chicago, Illinois, active through the 1990s under the parent company Choke, Inc. Its catalogue of seven releases, beginning in 1993, spanned the Chicago underground’s math rock, noise, and hardcore currents. The roster was small and tightly focused, with Craw and Milkmine among its key acts.

Notes (web)

Craw–Choke connection

No source directly documents how Craw came to sign with Choke. The most substantive statement from the band appears in a 2021 interview: “We were on a Chicago label for the first two records, before they defaulted to being a distribution label, and they were helpful in getting those records out there and reviewed.” — Machine Music, January 2021

The clearest circumstantial thread is Steve Albini. Choke signed bands that recorded at Albini’s Chicago home studio, regardless of their home city:

  • Craw — Cleveland, Ohio. Debut LP recorded by Albini, June 1993; released on Choke, October 1993. Second LP Lost Nation Road also engineered by Albini and released on Choke, 1994. crawband.com
  • Morsel — Ann Arbor, Michigan (not Chicago). Signed to Choke in summer 1993; debut Noise Floor recorded by Albini, released 1994. Choke collapsed before Morsel’s follow-up EP could appear. Wikipedia: Morsel (band)

Both non-Chicago acts on Choke’s earliest releases were Albini-recorded. This strongly suggests Albini’s studio served as a de facto talent pool or referral pipeline for the label, though no interview or press piece found confirms this explicitly.

What was not found: No interview with Craw band members, Choke label personnel, or contemporaneous press names the person who initiated contact or explains whether the approach came from the label, the band, or a third party (e.g. Albini). The Choke roster page on Discogs credits David McClelland, Jay Wilson (2), and Joe McTighe but their specific roles (label staff vs. band members) are not distinguished in any web source found.
