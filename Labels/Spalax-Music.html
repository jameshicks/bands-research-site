Spalax Music

French reissue and archival label based in Paris. Discogs label ID: 6219. Active under the Spalax Music name from approximately mid-1994 through 2009 (finally wound down 2014).

Overview

Spalax Music is the successor imprint to the earlier Spalax label (Discogs ID: 68848), which ran from 1974 to mid-1994. The split is branding-based: releases up to mid-1994 carry the original Spalax logo with its blind mole-rat (Spalax ehrenbergi) mascot; releases from mid-1994 onward carry the Spalax Music branding. Both share the same catalog number sequence (14xxx).

The label was the vehicle of Gabriel Ibos (Discogs artist ID: 1152766) — previously a promoter and executive at Vamp Records, Barclay, and other French majors. Ibos had strong ties to the German krautrock scene, and this shapes the label’s identity profoundly: Ash Ra Tempel, Klaus Schulze, Agitation Free, Amon Düül, Guru Guru, Popol Vuh, and The Cosmic Jokers dominate the catalog numerically. Around 1994, Spalax as a partnership dissolved when MSI distribution ran into trouble; Ibos continued as sole proprietor under the Spalax Music name. He later moved to the Far East and allowed the label to wind down post-2004; trading ceased in 2009 and it formally folded in 2014.

Spalax Music also functioned as a publisher (established 1988) and was distributed by a succession of companies: MSI, Socadisc, WMD, Mélodie Distribution, Wagram, and finally Multiwaves (1999–2009).

Contact address on record: 10 rue des Feuillantines, 75005 Paris.

Character of the Catalog

The catalog (471 releases on the Spalax Music imprint alone; 362 on the earlier Spalax) breaks into several distinct clusters:

German kosmische / krautrock reissues — the largest single segment by volume. Ash Ra Tempel and Manuel Göttsching alone account for 28 and 18 primary-credited releases respectively. Other pillars: Klaus Schulze (11), The Cosmic Jokers (13), Agitation Free (4), Guru Guru (5), Amon Düül (11), Amon Düül II (5), Popol Vuh (10), Wallenstein (5), Cluster (4), Hoelderlin (3), Annexus Quam (3), Limbus 4 (2), Emtidi (3), Yatha Sidhra (1).

British prog and space rock — Gong / Planet Gong / Daevid Allen / Mother Gong (collectively 20+ releases), Hawkwind (3), National Health (6), Soft Machine (4), Soft Heap (3), Van Der Graaf Generator (2), Gilgamesh (3), New York Gong (1).

French avant-garde and experimental — a smaller but distinctive cluster tightly connected to the vault:

  • Heldon / Richard Pinhas — Heldon’s Stand By (1991, catno 14233) was one of the earliest Spalax CD reissues; Pinhas appears with 5 primary credits (De L’Un Et Du Multiple, Iceland, Rhizosphere) and as producer of the Heldon reissue alongside François Auger.
  • Lard Free — Unnamed (1997, SPALAXCD14915), featuring Gilbert Artman (drums, piano, bugle) and Robert Wood (vibraphone).
  • Vidéo-Aventures — Musiques Pour Garçons Et Filles reissue (1997, SPALAXCD14970) and Camera (In Focus) / Camera (Al Riparo) (1997, SPALAXCD14973).
  • Camizole — Camizole (1999, SPALAXCD14549), compilation of 1970s recordings.
  • Mahogany Brain — Smooth Sick Lights (1997, SPALAXCD14266).
  • Fille Qui Mousse — Trixie Stapelton 291 (1998, SPALAXCD14919).
  • Catalogue (Gilbert Artman / Jac Berrocal / Jean-François Pauvros) — 3 releases (catno 14966, 14920, and others).
  • Gaby Bizien / Jean-François Pauvros — No Man’s Land (1996, SPALAXCD14506).
  • Areski - Brigitte Fontaine — L’Incendie (1995, SPALAXCD14815).
  • Bernard Szajner — Some Deaths Take Forever (1999, SPALAXCD14545).
  • Art & Technique — Clima-X (1998, SPALAXCD14914).
  • Lard Free-adjacent: Robert Wood vibraphone appears as session player across several Spalax releases.

American garage / psychedelia — 13th Floor Elevators (12 releases, including Roky Erickson), Red Krayola (2).

Italian film music — Ennio Morricone (4), Armando Trovaioli (1), Bruno Nicolai (1); mostly horror/genre soundtrack reissues.

Native American / world music — an extended series (Authentic Music Of The American Indian sublabel) covering Navajo, Cheyenne, Lakota, and other traditions; Jorge Reyes Mexican pre-Hispanic music (4 releases).

Japanese prog — Ryuichi Sakamoto (3), Yukihiro Takahashi (3), Bi Kyo Ran (2), Asturias (2), Kenso (1), several Japanese prog acts in the 2001 cluster.

German experimental / electronic — Einstürzende Neubauten Kollaps (1998 reissue), Terry Riley / Pierre Mariétan collaboration.
