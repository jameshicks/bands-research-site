Overview

Bent Window Records is an experimental electronic and noise imprint based in Vancouver, Canada, founded by Nicholas Gibas. The label launched in 2021 and accumulated 25 releases within its first few years, working across noise, dark ambient, abstract, and ambient electronic forms. Its roster spans Gibas himself alongside artists including Tim Olive, N. Antoni, Bradung, and Giraffe People — a mix of local Vancouver figures and names associated with broader experimental circuits.

Notes (web) — Tim Olive / Bent Window connection

Tim Olive was born in Moose Jaw, Canada and has long resided in Kobe, Japan, where he releases on a wide range of North American, European, and Asian experimental labels and runs his own imprint 845 Audio. He tours Canada periodically and maintains an active presence in the broader Anglophone experimental/noise circuit. (timolive.org/about, retrieved 2026-10-11)

His relationship with Bent Window predates the solo Smelt (BWR059, Aug 2023): he contributed track “III” to the label compilation Sounds of the Ruins Vol. 1, a 27-track survey running from extreme hardcore/grind through dark ambient and harsh noise. (bentwindowrecords.bandcamp.com/album/sounds-of-the-ruins-vol-1, retrieved 2026-10-11)

Smelt was recorded October 2022 in Kobe using magnetic pickups, metal plates, twine, electric cockroach, modified music boxes, and spring reverb; mixed by Olive, mastered by James Armstrong. It was released August 10, 2023, with a reissue September 8, 2023 on cassette (cover art by Olive). (bentwindowrecords.bandcamp.com/album/smelt, retrieved 2026-10-11)
