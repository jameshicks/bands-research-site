Overview

Festival Records was an Australian and New Zealand record label established in 1952 under Festival Records Pty. Ltd., operating primarily as a distribution company that partnered with international labels including Atlantic, Chrysalis, Island, A&M Records, and Virgin — licensing labels rather than signing acts outright. In New Zealand it was distributed by G.A. Wooller & Co. Ltd. from 1952 until 1966, when the joint-venture entity Festival Records (NZ) Ltd. was formed with cinema chain Kerridge Odeon. In 1999 Rupert Murdoch’s Newscorp consolidated both the Australian and NZ operations — along with Mushroom Records and Mushroom Distribution Services — into what became Festival Mushroom Records. The label’s catalogue spans Pop Rock, Vocal, Country, Rock & Roll, and Synth-pop, with roster names including Johnny O’Keefe, John Williamson, Bing Crosby, and Herb Alpert & The Tijuana Brass.
