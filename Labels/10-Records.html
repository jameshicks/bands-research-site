Overview

10 Records was a British label founded in 1983 as a Virgin-affiliated imprint — early pressings carried the Virgin logo inside the zero. Its 1980s catalogue spanned hard rock, synth-pop, soul, and house, with a roster running from Gary Moore to Soul II Soul, Jazzie B, Nellee Hooper, and Detroit house figure Kevin Saunderson, making it one of the more stylistically eclectic UK independents of the era. Mick Clark handled A&R. After 1990, a corporate name change from 10 Records Ltd. to Ten Records Ltd. saw the imprint rebranded as Ten Records.
