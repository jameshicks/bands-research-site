Overview

Constrictor is a German independent label founded in 1986 by Phillip Boa and Pia Lund, serving as both the home imprint of Phillip Boa & The Voodooclub and a conduit for UK indie and post-punk acts including The Membranes, Television Personalities, and Inca Babies. Its catalogue is concentrated almost entirely in the late 1980s, built through close ties with British labels such as Dreamworld, In Tape, and Red Flame; designer Dirk Rudolph shaped the visual identity of the Voodooclub releases during this period. In 1991 the label was transferred to Matthias Gülicher and briefly continued under the name Historia, releasing German acts like Brosch and Speed Niggs before folding within eight months. Phillip Boa revived the Constrictor name around 2009 for new physical and digital Voodooclub releases, and the label also operates publishing subsidiaries Constrictor Musikverlag and Film Noir.
