Overview

Integral (2) is a marketing division of [PIAS] UK Ltd., formed in 2006 to provide marketing structure and development backing for smaller labels and rights holders looking to introduce and promote their acts. It occasionally appears credited as [Integral]. Its catalogue is heavily concentrated in the 2020s — 32 of its 33 listed releases date from that decade — spanning Indie Rock, Alternative Rock, New Wave, and Punk. Associated artists include David Sylvian, Gomez, Simon Jeffes, and Burning Heads.
