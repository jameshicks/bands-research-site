Memo

Berlin electronic music sublabel of BPitch Control, founded as a vehicle for new and emerging talent. Discogs label ID: 44520. Website: memomusik.com.

Overview

Memo was established as an explicit “new talents” imprint operating under the BPitch Control infrastructure at Oranienburger Str. 91, 10178 Berlin. The parent label — founded and owned by Ellen Allien — had by the mid-2000s become one of Berlin’s most prominent techno and electro imprints; Memo served as its lower-stakes proving ground where artists could debut without the full commercial weight of the BPitch name.

The label’s catalog runs from 2005 to at least 2017, with its core activity concentrated in 2005–2010. Catalog numbers follow a spelled-out scheme: MEMO one, MEMO two, MEMO three, etc. (occasionally written numerically on later pressings), reaching at least MEMO ten as the main sequence. The ten-release sequence covers roughly five years of activity with a consistent all-vinyl 12” format before a late outlier — a 2017 CD EP on the label — suggests a brief revival attempt.

Parent Label Context

BPitch Control (Discogs ID: 1466) is owned by Ellen Allien and operates under the BPitch Control GmbH holding entity (ID: 305080). Its other sublabels include Spielwiese (Discogs ID: 14974), BPitch Control Music Publishing (ID: 298476), and We Are Not Alone (2) (Discogs ID: 2448583). Memo is the only dedicated new-talent imprint in the family.

Key Artists

Ben Klock

Discogs ID: 77025. The label’s launch artist, contributing the first two releases in 2005. Klock would go on to become one of Berlin’s most respected techno DJs and producers, founding his own Klockworks imprint. His Memo debut (four tracks: “Earthquake”, “Frozencity”, “Lumo”, “Land Of Milk & Honey”) established the label’s minimal/techno character from the outset.

Zander VT

Discogs ID: 461975. The most prolific Memo artist, with three releases (MEMO three, four, six) spanning 2006–2007. A duo comprising Fritz Zander (ID: 505309) and Sven Von Thülen (ID: 691931). Their sound sits in the minimal/tech house zone; MEMO six’s B-side features a remix by Redshape, showing the label’s connections within the Berlin underground.

Lee Van Dowski

Discogs ID: 135657. Real name Renaud Lewandowski. French producer based in the Berlin scene; also records as NOPE and is a member of Extensio and How To Make Sense Of A Window. His Memo release (MEMO seven, 2008) features a collaboration track with Tassilo Ippenberger on the B-side.

Mark Broom

Discogs ID: 459. Veteran UK techno and tech house producer with an enormous discography across many labels. His MEMO eight release (2009) is a remixes EP of his track “Twenty Nine”, with versions by Broom and Mihalis Safras.

Andre Crom

Discogs ID: 538924. Berlin-based producer (also records as ACR). MEMO five (2006) was co-produced and co-written with Alex Krüger on three of its four tracks, representing a collaborative approach unusual for the label.

Cormac (2)

Discogs ID: 1881742. Also known as Cormac McAdam. His MEMO ten (2010) is the label’s final numbered entry, featuring Deepgroove and a remix by Thomas Muller.

Sound and Aesthetic

Memo’s core catalog (2005–2010) is firmly in the Berlin minimal techno and tech house tradition — long, functional 12” cuts built for club play, sparse production, and muted sleeve designs typical of the era’s Berghain-adjacent underground. The label code and contact details match BPitch Control’s, confirming full operational integration with the parent. The catalog number scheme (written words, not numerals) was a small but characteristic stylistic touch.

The 2017 outlier — a CD EP by de Turquoise from Portugal, tagged as Blues and pressed on CD rather than vinyl — stands apart from everything else on the label and may represent either a personal favor or a brief attempt to expand scope.
