Dead Broke Rekerds is an independent, D.I.Y. record label and distro run out of Long Island, New York, United States by a group of friends. The label was started to record and distribute the label’s own bands, their friends’ bands, and anything else they felt people should hear.

— Discogs label profile, ID 154793

Overview

Dead Broke Rekerds is a DIY independent label and distro founded in 2005 by a circle of friends on Long Island, New York. Its catalogue spans punk, pop-punk, emo, and indie rock, with the bulk of its roughly 191 releases concentrated in the 2010s and a continued output into the 2020s. Iron Chic is among the flagship roster acts; Carl Saff has recurred as a mastering engineer across many of the label’s releases.
