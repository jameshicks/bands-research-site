Overview

Mortarhate Records is an anarcho-punk label founded in 1983 and owned outright by the band Conflict, who have run it as a direct vehicle for their own releases and associated acts throughout its existence. The catalogue is heaviest in the 1980s — 29 releases in that decade — and spans anarcho-punk, hardcore, dub, and folk rock, mirroring Conflict’s own range; output continued through the 1990s and has appeared sporadically into the 2020s. A pressing-and-distribution deal with Jungle Records, established in 1984, underpinned the label’s early infrastructure, with further distribution arrangements following after 2000. The label has operated under several related imprints — Fight Back Records, Mortarhate, Mortarhate Inc., and New Army Records — with Conflict members Colin Jerwood, Paul Hoddy, and Mark Pickstone among its principal credited artists.
