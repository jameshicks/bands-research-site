Overview

Cadiz Music is a UK label founded in 2007, operating under the parent company Cadiz Music Ltd. Its catalogue built steadily through the 2010s (45 releases) and has remained active into the 2020s (32 releases), covering punk, post-punk, dub, and goth rock. The roster centres on Killing Joke and their bassist/producer Youth, alongside Wonk Unit and Michael Rendall, giving the label a clear grounding in the post-punk underground.
