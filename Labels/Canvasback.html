Overview

Canvasback Music is an independent-leaning imprint founded in 2006 by Steve Ralbovsky that became a division of Atlantic Records in 2009. Its catalogue of around 70 releases skews heavily toward the 2010s and centres on indie rock, folk rock, and acoustic music. The roster has included Glen Hansard, Marketa Irglova, Grouplove, The Joy Formidable, and Hannah Hooper, placing the label within the mid-2000s to 2010s indie-folk mainstream. Activity tapered sharply in the 2020s, with only a handful of releases after 2019.
