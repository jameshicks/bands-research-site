Overview

Audiotree is a Chicago-based artist discovery platform that parlayed its live-session video series — including Audiotree Live, Far Out, Live From Lincoln Hall, and Shows From Schubas — into a full record label operation under the Audiotree Music parent. Its catalogue, built from 2010 onward with the bulk of releases in the 2010s, sits in indie rock, folk, and indie pop territory, with artists like Indigo De Souza and Matt Andersen among its signed roster. The Audiotree Live sublabel runs alongside as a dedicated imprint for the session recordings that first gave the platform its profile.

Label founding and the live-session pivot

Audiotree was originally conceived as a record label, with the live in-studio sessions intended as a rigorous audition process for acts it might sign. Michael Johnston (audio engineer, President) and Adam Thurston (business background, Director of Operations) — childhood friends from Portage, MI — founded the company together with Chris May; advisers Ira Antelis and Chris Watkins are also named in the founding press release. Johnston’s motivating observation was that existing live-session options either “sounded good and looked horrible, or looked really good and sounded horrible.” [https://www.24-7pressrelease.com/press-release/196420/new-independent-music-label-audio-tree-plants-roots-in-chicago] [http://www.lornebehrman.com/audiotree]

Once Johnston and Thurston started posting session videos online, their inboxes were flooded with band inquiries, and producing live performances became the primary passion rather than signing acts. By 2011 (their first full year) they had generated 150 sessions, cementing the live series as the core product. The label ambition did not disappear, however — it was refashioned into what a Chicago Reader article called a “cafeteria label”: Audiotree planned to sign one or two artists per year to traditional deals, while offering a much larger pool of unsigned artists an a la carte menu of services (licensing, management, promotion, etc.) for upfront fees with no further label relationship required. [https://www.chicagoreader.com/chicago/adiotree-chicago-record-label/Content?oid=3392761 — snippet only, article rate-limited]

Official label launch: March 10, 2011 — a concert at Chicago’s Lincoln Hall headlined by Atlantic recording artist Portugal. The Man, alongside Audiotree’s first formally signed artist, Kellen & Me. [https://www.24-7pressrelease.com/press-release/196420/new-independent-music-label-audio-tree-plants-roots-in-chicago]

Audiotree also launched a management wing to work with artists who had appeared on Audiotree Live, deepening artist relationships beyond the session itself. [http://www.lornebehrman.com/audiotree]

The transition to signing and releasing full records under the Audiotree Music imprint therefore appears to be an organic outgrowth of the platform rather than a single pivotal moment or external partnership: the label was always part of the founding intent; the live-session business just grew faster and larger than anticipated, and the label arm was retooled around the a la carte model before eventually becoming the more conventional releasing entity visible in the Discogs catalogue from 2010 onward.
