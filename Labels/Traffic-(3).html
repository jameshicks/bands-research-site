Overview

Traffic (3) is a Japanese record label operating under Traffic Inc., active since 2007 and focused primarily on licensed catalogue releases for the Japanese market. Its roster is dominated by Can and Can’s individual members — Irmin Schmidt, Holger Czukay, Jaki Liebezeit, and Michael Karoli — to the extent that the label runs a dedicated sublabel, CAN:ライヴ・シリーズ, for Can live recordings. A second sublabel, Sub Pop名盤シリーズ, handles Japanese-market reissues drawn from the Sub Pop catalogue. Output has been sustained across three decades, peaking in the 2010s with 81 releases, and spanning Krautrock, experimental, and indie rock styles.
