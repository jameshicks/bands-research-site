Overview

Orb Tapes is a cassette label founded in 2015 and based in central Pennsylvania, dedicated to fringe music across experimental, noise, ambient, drone, and abstract styles. The catalogue grew from 15 releases in the 2010s to 31 in the 2020s, keeping a consistent DIY tape aesthetic throughout. Its roster centers on Dave Petersen alongside acts including Teuthis Galore, James Searfoss, Abdul Hakim-Bilal, and Matthew Crowe.
