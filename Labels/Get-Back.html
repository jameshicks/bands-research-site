Overview

Get Back is an Italian label (also credited as Get Back Records), operating under parent Abraxas, that launched in 1993 and issued over 500 releases across the 1990s and 2000s. Its catalogue spans psychedelic rock, punk, free jazz, prog rock, and blues rock, with artists including Sun Ra, Pere Ubu, and Curtis Mayfield among its roster. The label’s output is organized across four imprints — Get Back Avantgarde, Get Back Jazz Series, Get Back Roots, and Lo-Cost — suggesting deliberate genre segmentation within a broad reissue-oriented programme.
