Overview

TKO Records is an independent punk and Oi label founded in San Francisco in the late 1990s by Mark Rainey, with a catalogue spanning Punk, Oi, and Hardcore alongside Garage Rock and Rock & Roll. Output peaked sharply in the 2000s — over 100 releases in that decade alone — before settling into a steadier pace of roughly 35–37 per decade through the 2010s and 2020s. In 2007 the label opened a record shop in Huntington Beach, California, and in 2014 relocated its operations to Portland, Oregon. Its roster has included Portland hardcore institution Poison Idea and vocalist Jerry A., as well as Southern noise-punk outfit Antiseen, with the sublabel Punch Drunk (2) operating under the same umbrella.

Notes (web)

TKO Records 2014 relocation to Portland — Poison Idea connection?

Question: Did TKO’s existing relationship with Portland-based Poison Idea (signed 2007) influence or precipitate the label’s move from Orange County to Portland in Fall 2014?

What was checked: TKO Records blog (tkorecords.blogspot.com, January 2017 post); Portland Mercury feature on Cascade Record Pressing (portlandmercury.com, June 2015); Hey-PDX interview (failed — socket hang up); Portland Interview Magazine (redirected to suspicious domain, not fetched); player.fm podcast listing for “Punk Till I Die” ep. 213 with Mark Rainey (403); Spotify listing for “Dan O Says So” ep. 14 (not fetched — no snippet content); web searches for “TKO Records Mark Rainey Portland relocation 2014” and “Mark Rainey TKO Records Cascade Record Pressing Portland interview”.

Findings:

No source states that the Poison Idea relationship drove or influenced the relocation. The available evidence points in a different direction:

  • The TKO Records blog post (January 2017) states only that “at the end of 2014, TKO RECORDS founder and owner Mark Rainey moved with his family up to Portland, OR where he continues to operate the label.” No reason for the move is given. tkorecords.blogspot.com

  • The Portland Mercury feature (June 2015) is the most informative source found. It places the relocation discussion in December 2013, when Rainey began talking with Adam Gonsalves (who was already Portland-based) about moving there. When Rainey floated the idea of opening another record store, Gonsalves told him the Portland retail market was saturated. This pivoted the conversation toward a vinyl pressing plant — which became Cascade Record Pressing (located in Milwaukie, OR). The article does not mention Poison Idea as a factor in the move. portlandmercury.com, 2015-06-24

Assessment: The preponderance of evidence suggests the relocation was driven by the opportunity to co-found Cascade Record Pressing with Adam Gonsalves and Steve Lanning — not by TKO’s pre-existing relationship with Poison Idea. The signing of Poison Idea in 2007 predated the move by seven years; no interview or press piece found links the two. The connection to Gonsalves (who appears in TKO’s Discogs roster and is already in this file) is the more plausible connective tissue.

Dead end note: The longest available interview with Mark Rainey on this topic (Punk Till I Die podcast ep. 213, 2h36m, January 2023) could not be accessed (player.fm returned 403). The Apple Podcasts and Spotify listings for it and the “Dan O Says So” ep. 14 were not fetched. If a transcript or summary of either podcast surfaces, it may contain a direct answer.
