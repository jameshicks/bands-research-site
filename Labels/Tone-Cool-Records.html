Tone-Cool Records

US blues label, based in Boston, MA. Discogs label ID: 354305. Release count: 198.

Overview

Boston-area independent blues label active from at least 1986 through approximately 2006. Home to several key Boston-area and nationally distributed blues artists. The label issued early recordings by Monster Mike Welch and Susan Tedeschi, as well as records by North Mississippi Allstars, Rod Piazza, Paul Rishell, and others. Ducky Carlisle worked on multiple releases through this label. By the early 2000s the catalog was being distributed more widely — the North Mississippi Allstars releases appeared on Warner imprints in Europe.
