Overview

WERGO is a German avant-garde record label founded in 1962 by Dr. Werner Goldschmidt, now operating under the umbrella of Schott Music & Media GmbH. Its catalogue is anchored in contemporary classical, experimental, and musique concrète, with a roster that has included György Ligeti, Karlheinz Stockhausen, and John Cage. With over 1,100 credits accumulated across more than six decades, the label has developed several sublabels — among them Ars Acustica and the Earle Brown Contemporary Sound Series — reflecting its sustained commitment to post-war and serialist avant-garde music.
