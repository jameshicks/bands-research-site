Overview

Cargo Records was a Montreal-founded independent label and distributor (est. 1987) that became a significant force in Canadian rock, punk, and metal through the early 1990s. Its roughly 40-release catalogue skews heavily toward the 1990s, covering punk, alternative rock, indie rock, hard rock, and hardcore, with roster names including Phillip Boa, Drive Like Jehu, and Mark Trombino. The company expanded into the US (as Cargo Music), the UK, and Germany before financial difficulties emerged after 1995; the Canadian parent filed for bankruptcy in December 1997. The European offices — operating largely as independent distributors — were insulated by prior ownership changes and survived, with the German arm (Cargo Records Germany GmbH) continuing as a going concern.
