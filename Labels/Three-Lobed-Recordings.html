Three Lobed Recordings

US independent label based in Jamestown, North Carolina (mailing address: P.O. Box 2345, Jamestown, NC 27282). Discogs label ID: 32939. Website: threelobed.com; Bandcamp: threelobed.bandcamp.com.

Overview

Three Lobed Recordings is an American independent label specializing in limited-edition vinyl releases spanning psychedelic rock, free folk, acoustic guitar music, drone, noise, and experimental improvisation. The label’s roster runs from heavy psych stalwarts like Bardo Pond to fingerstyle guitarists (Jack Rose, Daniel Bachman, William Tyler), free folk players (Six Organs of Admittance, Wooden Wand), harp and ambient music (Mary Lattimore), and experimental noise projects (Sunburned Hand Of The Man, Yellow Swans). Since its first release in 2000 (Bardo Pond’s Slab, TLR 001), the label has built a catalog of roughly 326 releases — all on vinyl or in limited physical formats — while maintaining relationships with a core group of recurring artists across decades.

The label operates five sublabels: Modern Containment, Oscillation III, Parallelogram (3), Purposeful Availment, and Three Lobed Recordings 20th Anniversary. None of these appear with independent release records in Discogs; they function as imprint identifiers within the main catalog. The label has also issued a notable series of collaborative split releases — most visibly the 2015 “Parallelogram” series, a multi-volume run of paired artist EPs issued simultaneously (TLR-109 through TLR-113).

The house mastering partnership is the label’s most consistent technical thread: Patrick Klem has mastered 95 releases on the label; Carl Saff has mastered 60. Primary sleeve designers Darryl Norsen (46 releases) and Scott Caligan (33 releases) give the label’s visual identity its consistency.

Connections

  • Bardo Pond — founding and longest-running artist on the label; Michael Gibbons also served as engineer and producer on other TLR releases
  • Sonic Youth — eight releases (2021–2022); In/Out/In (2021) documented improv sessions with Jim O’Rourke
  • Six Organs of Admittance — Ben Chasny’s project; multiple TLR releases including “Parallelogram” split (2015) and The Veiled Sea (2021)
  • Steve Gunn — among the label’s most prolific artists; solo debut Boerum Palace (2009) through ongoing Gunn-Truscinski Duo and solo releases into 2025
  • Daniel Bachman — fingerstyle guitarist; major label presence from 2015 onward with landmark self-titled LP and Axacan
  • Patrick Klem — mastered 95 TLR releases; longest-running technical collaborator
  • Carl Saff — mastered 60 TLR releases including the Sonic Youth In/Out/In LP
  • Aaron Dilloway — appeared on Body/Dilloway/Head (TLR-135, 2021) with Kim Gordon and Bill Nace
  • Bill Nace — on roster as performer and frequently contributes artwork and layout
  • Body-Head — Kim Gordon + Bill Nace; issued “come on” e.p. (TLR147, 2023)
  • Thom Monahan — recurring producer; credits include Ghost Forests and other releases
  • Drag City — label home for some overlapping roster artists (Six Organs of Admittance, Bill Orcutt)
  • Thrill Jockey — overlapping experimental/psych orbit; Rachel’s released on both
  • Time-Lag Records — shared experimental Americana/psych scene space
