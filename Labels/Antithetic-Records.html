Antithetic Records

Discogs Label ID: 217998 Origin: USA Contact: antitheticrecords@gmail.com Active: 2010–2014 (per catalog) Genre focus: Avant-garde metal, progressive rock, black metal, experimental

Independent US DIY label founded to release limited, high-quality vinyl and CD editions by underground and original artists. Best known as the label that reissued the maudlin of the Well back catalog on vinyl and released Kayo Dot’s Gamma Knife (2012). The roster cuts across avant-metal, progressive rock, black metal, and post-hardcore, with a strong emphasis on limited-edition pressing quality. Most pressings were manufactured at Pirate’s Press (San Francisco). The label operated primarily between 2010 and 2014, releasing approximately 24 catalog titles across that window.

Overview / History

Antithetic Records launched in 2010 with its first release — a reissue of maudlin of the Well’s Part The Second (Anti-001), a double LP that had never previously appeared on vinyl. The label positioned itself as a home for high-quality limited pressings of underground and original music. Releases were typically pressed in small runs and sold direct via Storenvy and Bandcamp storefronts, alongside the label’s own site.

The label’s early catalog mixed Toby Driver-adjacent material (maudlin of the Well, Kayo Dot) with heavier bands across progressive, black metal, and post-hardcore: East Of The Wall (co-released with Translation Loss Records), Young And In The Way, and the French act Terzij De Horde. Several releases were co-pressed with other labels, including Hydra Head Records (Kayo Dot’s Stained Glass) and Blood Music (maudlin of the Well’s Bath and Leaving Your Body Map reissues).

By 2012 the catalog had expanded to include the South African black metal act Wildernessking (The Writing Of Gods In The Sand, ANTI-006), the Lovecraftian French black metal band The Great Old Ones (Al Azif, ANTI-011), and the debut LP from Nick Hudson (ANTI-013). The label also took on the technical death/prog supergroup Blotted Science, releasing both The Machinations Of Dementia (ANTI-019) and The Animation Of Entomology (ANTI-020) in 2013.

A 4-way split (ANTI-021, 2013) featured four US screamo/post-hardcore acts: Coma Regalia, Locktender, Oaken, and Wounded Knee. By 2014 the label had issued repress editions of the maudlin of the Well vinyl titles (ANTI-007R, ANTI-008R) and appears to have wound down activity.

Key behind-the-scenes figures recurring across the catalog: Mell Dettmer mastered or remastered 17 releases; Jim Fogarty engineered and mixed 12 releases (core to the maudlin of the Well and Kayo Dot sessions); Jacob Hansen mastered 8 releases (mainly the heavier end of the roster).

Catalog Highlights

maudlin of the Well — Part The Second (Anti-001, 2010)

First-ever vinyl appearance of the 2003 album, remastered by Mell Dettmer. Double LP at 45 RPM. Band lineup on this pressing: Toby Driver, Greg Massi, Jason Byron, Sam Gutterman, Terran Olson, Josh Seipp-Williams. Guest cello by Madeleine Craw, Hammond organ by Jim Fogarty, violin by Mia Matsumiya. Lacquer cut by Kyle Bayens.

Kayo Dot — Stained Glass (ANTI-002, 2011)

Co-released with Hydra Head Records (HH666-213). Remastered by Mell Dettmer, mixed by Jim Fogarty. Notable guest: Trey Spruance (guitar solo). Lineup: Toby Driver (vocals, guitar, bass, keyboards, glockenspiel), Terran Olson (Rhodes, Hammond, harmonium, Korg Mono/Poly), David Bodie (drums), Mia Matsumiya (violin), Daniel Means (sax/clarinet), Russell Greenberg (vibraphone). Side B is a remix by Driver, Olson, and Fogarty.

East Of The Wall — The Apologist (ANTI-004, 2011)

Co-released with Translation Loss Records (TL53-2). Recorded and mixed by Andrew Schneider. Mastered by Nick Zampiello and Rob Gonnella. Double LP.

Wildernessking — The Writing Of Gods In The Sand (ANTI-006LP, 2012)

South African band’s debut LP on Antithetic. Double LP, 45 RPM. No additional credits listed in Discogs; self-produced.

The Great Old Ones — Al Azif (ANTI-011LP, 2012)

Debut LP from Bordeaux-based Lovecraftian black metal act. Recorded and mixed by Cyrille Gachet. Mastered by Alan Douches. Double LP. Member Jeff Grimal handled artwork.

Kayo Dot — Gamma Knife (ANTI-012LP, 2012)

LP. Styles: Avant-garde, Black Metal, Prog Rock. The band’s most metal-leaning release of the era. Limited edition vinyl.

Blotted Science — The Animation Of Entomology (ANTI-020, 2013)

12” EP reissue. Lineup: Ron Jarzombek (guitar), Alex Webster (bass), Hannes Grossmann (drums). Mastered by Jacob Hansen. Produced by Jarzombek; drums recorded by V. Santura.
