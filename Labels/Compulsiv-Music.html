Overview

Compulsiv Music is a record label based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, active from 1991 through the 1990s. Its catalogue of around ten releases spans indie rock, psychedelic rock, stoner rock, punk, and alternative rock — a mix that reflects Philadelphia’s underground rock scene of that era. The label is best known as an early home for Bardo Pond, the city’s signature psych-rock outfit, alongside a small roster of local acts including Art Difuria, Greg Vaughn, Caterpillar, and Alexi Papadopoulos. No parent or sublabel relationship has been recorded, suggesting it operated as an independent venture.
