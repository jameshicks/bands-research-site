Aggressive Rockproduktionen (AGR) was a punk label from West Berlin, formed in 1980 by Karl-Ulrich Walterbach. AGR was a division of Modern Music Records GmbH. The company is usually referred to as just Modern Music on releases by the labels that it owned. Since 2001 was AGR a.k.a. AGR Records a label of Sanctuary Records Group Ltd.

Overview

Aggressive Rockproduktionen (AGR) was a West Berlin punk label founded in 1980 by Karl-Ulrich Walterbach, structured as a division of Modern Music Records GmbH — the parent company typically credited on releases in place of individual imprint names. The label was most productive in the 1980s (44 releases) and remained active into the 1990s (21 more), spanning punk, hardcore, Oi, industrial and alternative rock; its roster included German punk fixtures Slime and Daily Terror as well as Killing Joke members Geordie Walker and Jaz Coleman. In 2001, AGR passed into the Sanctuary Records Group, operating thereafter as AGR Records under that umbrella.
