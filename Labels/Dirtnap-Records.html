Overview

Dirtnap Records is a Portland, Oregon punk and power pop label run by Ken Cheppaikode, founded in 1999. Its catalogue of over 150 releases is concentrated in the 2000s (75 releases) and 2010s (65 releases), spanning punk, garage rock, power pop, and pop punk. The label became a focal point of a mid-2000s West Coast punk-pop scene through records by The Briefs, The Epoxies, The Exploding Hearts, and The Soviettes, with The Marked Men’s Mark Ryan and Jeff Burke and Dave Williams among its most recurring figures. Dirtnap also operates a sublabel, Green Noise Records.
