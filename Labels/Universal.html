Label brand used by the Universal Music Group and its affiliated companies, and former sister company Universal Studios.

The Universal brand/label name dates from March 1996 when the newly named Universal Music Group rebranded their Rising Tide label Universal (leaving the earlier label’s country acts on a much reduced Nashville-based Rising Tide subsidiary). However, it did not become more widely used in the group until the rebranding of MCA Records Inc. and its subsidiary operations in late December 1996. Universal as a label brand beyond the US dates to after that, in 1997, when the rebranding went around the world. The current (2023) black and white simplified logo with the word in a flat plane was introduced at that time.

Please note: the presence of a six-digit plus one digit cat # (xxxxxx-x) makes a release post-PolyGram merger, so 1999 or later.

Overview

Universal is a label brand operated by Universal Music Group (and historically its former sister company Universal Studios), created in March 1996 when UMG rebranded its Rising Tide imprint — with country acts remaining on a reduced Nashville-based Rising Tide subsidiary. The name spread globally in 1997 following the December 1996 rebranding of MCA Records Inc. and its operations. The PolyGram acquisition in early 1999 brought major imprints including Mercury, Polydor, Decca, Verve, and Philips into the fold, with the six-digit-plus-one catalogue number format (xxxxxx-x) marking post-merger pressings and the B00xxxxxx sequence denoting releases from 2003 onward. Discogs records over 40,000 credits under this brand, concentrated in the 1990s across Pop Rock, Alternative Rock, Europop, and Ballad styles, with roster acts including Rammstein, Aqua, U2, and Robert Smith.
