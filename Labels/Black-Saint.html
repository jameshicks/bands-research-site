Black Saint

Discogs ID: 30835 Parent label: CAM (Discogs ID 47332) Sibling label: Soul Note Founded: 1975 (by Giacomo Pellicciotti); taken over by Giovanni Bonandrini 1977–1978 Base: Milan / Rome, Italy (C.A.M., Via Cola di Rienzo 152, 00192 Rome) Acquired by CAM: 2008

Italian avant-garde and free jazz label, one of the most significant independent jazz imprints of the late twentieth century. Founded in 1975 by Giacomo Pellicciotti (first release: Billy Harper’s Black Saint, BSR 0001); Bonandrini took over as partner/co-owner around 1977–1978 and dramatically expanded the roster. The label had over 190 albums in its catalog by the time it was acquired by CAM.

Distribution history: 1975–1979 distributed by various companies; from 1979, Carosello distributed Black Saint releases; from July 1982, US distribution through a dedicated US distributor.

Bonandrini’s son Flavio Bonandrini subsequently managed the label. In 2008 Black Saint and sibling Soul Note were acquired by CAM, which began reissuing under the CAM Jazz imprint using original artwork.
