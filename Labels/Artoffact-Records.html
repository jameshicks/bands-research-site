Overview

Artoffact Records is a Toronto-based independent electronic label launched on 1 September 1999 as the in-house imprint of Storming the Base. Its catalogue concentrates on industrial, EBM, synth-pop, and darkwave, and its roster gravitates toward established Canadian electronic acts: Front Line Assembly, Bill Leeb, cEvin Key, and Download sit alongside newer darkwave acts such as ACTORS. The catalogue is almost entirely post-2000, with over 2,100 total Discogs credits across roughly 40 catalogue entries.
