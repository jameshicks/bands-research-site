Overview

Casete is a Mexican independent label that launched in 2011, also appearing in its releases as Casete Agricultura Digital and Casete Digital Agriculture. Its catalogue is heavily concentrated in the 2010s with 122 releases spanning Alternative Rock, Indie Rock, Cumbia, Indie Pop, and Experimental music. Its roster is strikingly cosmopolitan for a regional indie, listing international experimental acts such as Björk, Arca, The Haxan Cloak, and James Merry alongside Chris Elms — suggesting a possible licensing or distribution role for Mexico. Activity has been minimal since 2020, with only two releases in the current decade.
