House Of Silk

Discogs ID: 570522 Parent Label: 100% Silk (Discogs ID: 232335) Grandparent Label: Not Not Fun Records Run by: Amanda Brown (2) Active: 2013–2014 (initial run); 2022 (one revival release) Catalog prefix: HOS Total releases (Discogs): 5 (unique masters) Origin: Los Angeles, CA (US)

Overview

House Of Silk is a sublabel of 100% Silk, itself a dance-oriented sublabel of Not Not Fun Records. Where 100% Silk proper runs a broad catalog of lo-fi house, italo-influenced electronics, and experimental dance music, House Of Silk appears to have been conceived as a more vocal and R&B-inflected offshoot — its first releases lean toward deep house and club-soul rather than the beatmaker minimalism typical of the parent label.

The catalog is extremely small: five releases total over roughly nine years, with a concentrated burst of four in 2013–2014 and then a single revival release in 2022. No Discogs profile text exists for the label. Its catalog prefix (HOS001–HOS005) is sequential and complete with no gaps visible in the Discogs data.

The sublabel appears to have been an experimental vehicle rather than a sustained project — a space for artists in the 100% Silk orbit who were making more vocal-forward or soul-influenced material. The 2022 return (Torrello, Out Of Office, HOS005) suggests some continued intent to use the imprint, though at low volume.

HOS005 — Out Of Office — Torrello (2022)

Cassette album. 11 tracks. The label’s only release after an 8-year dormancy. Represents the revival of the House Of Silk imprint within the current 100% Silk operational period.
