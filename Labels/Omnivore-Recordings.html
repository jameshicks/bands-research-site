Overview

Omnivore Recordings is a Los Angeles independent label founded in 2010 by music industry veterans Cheryl Pawelski, Greg Allen, Dutch Cramblitt, and Brad Rosenberger, with a focus on reissues, archival releases, and previously unissued recordings from heritage and catalog artists. The label operates under the parent company Omnivore Entertainment Group and expanded its holdings substantially in 2017 by acquiring the catalogs of Nighthawk Records, Blackheart Music, and Ru-Jac Records. Its output of over 380 releases spans the 2010s and 2020s, drawing across power pop, indie rock, country, and alternative rock. Omnivore Music Publishing rounds out the group’s imprints alongside Nighthawk and Blackheart.
