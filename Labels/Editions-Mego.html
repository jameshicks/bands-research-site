Editions Mego

Discogs ID: 61632 Based: Vienna, Austria Founded: 1 January 2006 Founder/owner: Peter Rehberg (Pita) Predecessor: Mego (Discogs ID: 2440, 1994–2005)

Overview

Editions Mego was founded on 1 January 2006 by Peter Rehberg with the twin mandate of maintaining and reissuing the catalog of the defunct Mego label, and releasing new work. It became the central hub for post-digital experimental, noise, and electroacoustic music throughout the 2000s and 2010s — a small Viennese operation with global reach and consistent curatorial vision. Discogs lists 694 releases.

Where Mego was defined by glitch and harsh digital electronics, Editions Mego broadened that foundation without abandoning it: the label moved fluidly between electroacoustic improvisation, drone, noise, computer music, and noisier strands of post-minimalism, and also took on significant archival work through the Recollection GRM sub-series (reissuing INA/GRM composers). Rehberg died in August 2021; the label has continued operating under his estate and colleagues.

House Personnel

Design: Stephen O’Malley (82 releases — primary house visual identity, cover art, design); Dave Coppenhall (38 releases); Tina Frank (30 releases); Nik Colk Void (23 releases). O’Malley’s design work is the most visible non-musical throughline of the label.

Mastering: Rashad Becker (102 releases — principal mastering engineer, almost all vinyl). Russell Haswell (52 releases — both as artist and mastering engineer). Stephan Mathieu (35 releases). James Plotkin (19 releases).

GRM coordination: François Bonnet (2) (aka Kassel Jaeger) and Christian Zanési — liner notes and coordination on Recollection GRM series.

Historical Context

Mego (1994–2005) was founded by Ramon Bauer, Peter Meininger, and Andreas Pieper. Peter Rehberg joined in 1995 when Meininger left. The original label was associated with Vienna’s post-digital glitch scene and issued key early works by Fennesz, Mika Vainio (Pan Sonic), Pita, Hecker, and others. It operated from Vienna’s Meidling district.

After Mego closed at the end of 2005, Rehberg relaunched it on 1 January 2006 as Editions Mego. Rather than treating it as a clean break, the new label maintained catalog continuity — many eMEGO catalog numbers directly continue the Mego numbering, and early Editions Mego releases include reissues of Mego back-catalog items.

Rehberg died in August 2021. Editions Mego has continued releasing material since, including posthumous and archival KTL releases, and new works from ongoing roster artists.
