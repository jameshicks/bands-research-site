Overview

Indigo is a Hamburg-Harburg-based German independent distribution company founded in 1992 by Albrecht Boehm, Nikel Pallat, and Jörn Heinecker — all three veterans of the EFA label — with distribution operations beginning on 1 January 1993. The company serves labels including Strange Ways Records, appending its own catalogue numbering system to releases. Its catalogue spans indie rock, alternative rock, folk rock, punk, and electro, with output peaking in the 2000s and continuing into the 2020s. The roster connects Indigo to prominent artists such as Einstürzende Neubauten members Blixa Bargeld and Alexander Hacke, Hamburg punk act Slime, and American songwriter Chris Eckman.
