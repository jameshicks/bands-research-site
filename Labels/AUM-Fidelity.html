AUM Fidelity

Discogs ID: (needs lookup)

American independent record label based in New York. Founded by Steven Joerg (Discogs ID: 311034) in the mid-1990s. A key imprint for downtown New York free jazz and improvised music.

Released multiple Matthew Shipp recordings including Wisdom Of Uncertainty (1997) and Seraphic Light (2018). Also home to releases by William Parker, Rob Brown (4), and many other figures in the same scene.
