Stash Records

American jazz reissue and new-release label. Based in New York; active from the 1970s through the 1990s. Known for reissuing classic jazz recordings and releasing new material by contemporary artists. Discogs label ID: varies (listed as “Stash Records Inc.” and “Stash Records”).
