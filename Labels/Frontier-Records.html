Frontier Records

Discogs ID: 33807

Los Angeles independent record label. Co-released the Flying Color LP (1987) and American Music Club’s California (1988) with Grifter Records; Frontier catalog numbers (FLP-xxxx / FCX-xxxx) appear on those Grifter pressings, suggesting Frontier handled pressing and distribution for the San Francisco label’s releases.

Key SoCal hardcore releases: Circle Jerks Group Sex (1980, FLP-1001); Adolescents debut LP (1981); Suicidal Tendencies debut LP (1983). Lisa Fancher credited as “Caused By” on Group Sex — she is widely identified as the label’s founder. The Group Sex / Wild In The Streets compilation (1988 onward) paired Circle Jerks’ first two LPs on Frontier. Glen E. Friedman credited across at least 10 unique Frontier masters. Also reissued The Middle Class’s catalog: Out of Vogue – The Early Material LP (2008, cat no. 31078-1), repressed 2012 (with Burger Records) and 2017.
