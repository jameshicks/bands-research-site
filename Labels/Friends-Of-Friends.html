Friends Of Friends

Los Angeles-based independent electronic label founded by Lazy Brow, active from 2009 to the present, specializing in beat music, downtempo electronica, ambient, dream pop, and left-field hip-hop. One of the defining imprints of the early-to-mid 2010s LA beat scene.

History

Friends Of Friends launched in 2009 with a split digital release, Friends Of Friends Volume 1 (FOF001), featuring Daedelus and Jogger — two established LA electronic acts that set the label’s early tone of IDM-inflected, eclectic downtown electronics.

The label’s identity crystallized quickly around Shlohmo (Henry Laufer), whose Camping EP (FOF104, 2010) and debut LP Bad Vibes (FOF109, 2011) established the hazy, lo-fi beatmaking aesthetic that became Friends Of Friends’ signature. Shlohmo went on to become the label’s most prolific and prominent artist, releasing 38 releases on the label across its entire active period.

The early 2010s saw rapid expansion: Ernest Gonzales, Paul Salva (a.k.a. Salva), and LOL Boys arrived by 2011–12, followed by Tomas Barfod, Groundislava, Keep Shelly In Athens, Kyson, and Perera Elsewhere. The 2012–2014 window was arguably the label’s creative peak, with Tomas Barfod’s Salton Sea (FOF115, 2012) introducing a more polished, European-leaning pop-house sound, and Perera Elsewhere’s Everlast (FOF-127, 2013) adding experimental dub textures.

The five-year anniversary compilation 5oFoF (2014) documented the breadth of the roster: 36 tracks drawing on essentially every artist the label had signed, with guest spots from Freddie Gibbs, How To Dress Well (feat. on Shlohmo), Nicolas Jaar (remix), Baths, and Machinedrum.

Deru (Benjamin Wynn) became a long-term presence from 2014 onward, his ambient LP 1979 (FOF-131) one of the label’s most critically noted records; he returned with We Will Live On (FOF208LP, 2022), extending the label’s arc into the 2020s.

The mid-to-late 2010s brought a second wave: Anenon, Indian Wells (2), M.O.O.N., Sweatson Klank, and Lorenzo BITW. Shlohmo’s double LP The End (FOF175LP, 2019), co-released with WeDidIt Records, was the flagship release of this era.

Into the 2020s the label continued at a slower pace, releasing work from Somni (3), Afternoon Bike Ride, Shrimpnose, and Deru’s 2022 LP, signaling an enduring commitment to mood-based electronic music rather than any single genre trend.

Scene Connections

Friends Of Friends emerged from the same Los Angeles underground that produced the Low End Theory and Brainfeeder scenes, though it occupied a distinct niche — cooler, more atmospheric, and less jazz-inflected than Brainfeeder, with stronger ties to European club music and dream pop. The label overlapped heavily with the wider beat music circuit: early associates included Baths, Shigeto, and Machinedrum, all of whom contributed remixes to FOF releases. The five-year comp documented clear connections to Nicolas Jaar, Freddie Gibbs, Ryan Hemsworth, and the Chicago-adjacent world of How To Dress Well.

Tomas Barfod’s presence bridged FOF to the Scandinavian electronic scene (connections to Nina Kinert, Kasper Bjørke, Jeppe Kjellberg of WhoMadeWho), while Perera Elsewhere (Sasha Perera of Jahcoozi) brought a Berlin/experimental dub angle. Shlohmo’s later work co-released on WeDidIt Records (his own imprint) reflects a broadened distribution strategy while maintaining the FOF roster.

The label’s consistent use of a small group of mastering engineers — particularly Taylor Deupree (12k) and Heba Kadry — reflects a deliberate, audiophile approach to the final sound of its records.
