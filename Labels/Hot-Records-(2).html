Hot Records (2)

Discogs ID: 50384

Australian independent record label; became the primary home for Laughing Clowns from 1984 onward and the keeper of their catalog posthumously.

Released Eternally Yours (HOT 12001), Law Of Nature (HOT 1004), History Of Rock N’ Roll — Volume One (HOT 1010), Ghosts Of An Ideal Wife (HOT 1013), and the definitive 3-CD retrospective Cruel, But Fair (The Complete Clowns Recordings) (HOT 1088, 2005). Also released work by associated Australian acts. The “(2)” disambiguates from another Discogs entity.
