Overview

Skyline Studios was a commercial recording facility in New York City, founded in 1979 by Paul Wickliffe — who served as president and chief engineer for sixteen years — as a single-room, 16-track setup at 36 West 37th St. It expanded over time into a three-room, 48-track SSL/Studer complex before shutting down in 1994. A later attempt to reopen the studio, led by Jonathan Mover and Ron Allaire, ended in 2010 when a federal district court ruled against Skyline in a suit brought by software developer Waves; the space subsequently passed through Chung King Studios before becoming Reservoir Studios, NYC under Pat Dillett and Steve Salett. Discogs traces a single release to the label imprint — a 1985 session touching Big Band, Gospel, Swing, and Vocal styles — with the recording roster including James Blackwood and The Masters V.
