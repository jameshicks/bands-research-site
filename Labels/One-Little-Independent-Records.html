Overview

One Little Independent Records (OLI) is the 2020 rebrand of One Little Indian, a UK independent label founded around 1986 by members of anarcho-punk band Flux Of Pink Indians. Founder Derek Birkett announced the rename in June 2020, issuing a public apology for the old name’s contribution to racism. The label was built on DIY and anarchist ideals inspired by Crass, and has always given artists full creative control — a philosophy that underpinned a roster anchored for decades by Björk. Under the OLI name, activity accelerated sharply: 99 of the 117 listed releases appeared after the 2020 rebrand, spanning Leftfield, Experimental, Ambient, and Abstract styles, with a sublabel also operating as One Little Independent.
