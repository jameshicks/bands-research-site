Twin/Tone Records

Minneapolis independent record label. Named as “Twin/Tone Records” in Discogs sources.

Home to early The Replacements, Soul Asylum, The Jayhawks, and Ween. Acquired by Restless Records in 1992, bringing a significant Minneapolis indie back-catalog onto the Restless imprint. The Replacements catalog (14 releases) appears under Restless from 2002 forward in Discogs.

Soul Asylum’s Twin/Tone releases: Say What You Will… (1984, produced by Bob Mould), Made To Be Broken (1986, produced by Bob Mould), While You Were Out (1986), Clam Dip & Other Delights (1988). Engineers on the Bob Mould–produced records: Steve Fjelstad.

Note: filename uses hyphen for the / per vault convention. The label’s actual name is Twin/Tone Records.
