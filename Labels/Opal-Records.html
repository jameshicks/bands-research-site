Opal Records

Warner Bros. Records imprint active in the late 1980s–early 1990s, associated with Brian Eno and Daniel Lanois. Discogs label — top releases include Acadie (Lanois, 1989) and other ambient/alternative projects.

Overview

Opal served as the label for Lanois’s solo debut Acadie (1989) and associated singles (Jolie Louise, The Maker, Under A Stormy Sky, Still Water). Functioned as a Warner imprint positioned for left-of-center productions.
