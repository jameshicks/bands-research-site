Overview

Solid Brass (2) is an independent label launched in 2022 under the Solid Brass Records umbrella with a dual mandate: reissuing archival recordings from the 1990s DIY underground and releasing new work from contemporary artists. Its catalogue of twenty releases falls squarely in the emo, post-hardcore, and indie rock traditions of that era. The roster brings together veterans of the ’90s scene — including J. Robbins and The Black Heart Procession — alongside newer names such as Erik Denno and Darren Zentek.
