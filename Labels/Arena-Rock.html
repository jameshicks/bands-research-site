Arena Rock Recording Company

Discogs ID: 28226
Full name: The Arena Rock Recording Company (ARRCO)
Parent entry ID: 170598
Founded: mid-1990s, New York City (Lower East Side)
Founders: Greg Glover and Dan Ralph
Later address: 17 SE 3rd Ave. #405, Portland, OR 97214
Distribution: Redeye (US), Koch (Canada)
Website: arenarock.com

US independent label, founded by Greg Glover and Dan Ralph in a Lower East Side bar over pints and meager tax return checks. The name beat out “Stiff Goat Records” at the last moment. First release was a Superdrag 7” in 1995. Catalog ran across indie rock, post-rock, emo, and Elephant Six adjacency throughout the late 1990s and 2000s, operating out of NYC and later Portland.

Founding and Catalog Series

Two parallel catalog series run through the ARRCO discography:

  • ARE-XXXXX — the label’s own series (AR-001 onward, later formatted ARE-00XXX-2)
  • RCD-XXXXX — releases distributed/co-released through Rykodisc

Greg Glover’s name appears in credits throughout the catalog (photos, liner notes, coordination), indicating hands-on involvement.

Flagship Artist: Superdrag

The label’s anchor act and first signing. Knoxville, Tennessee alternative rock band formed 1992; hiatus 2003–2007; reformed 2007. 14 releases on ARRCO across their whole career.

Elephant Six Connection: Elf Power

Athens, Georgia band, core members of the Elephant Six Collective. Nine ARRCO releases 1997–2001. Their label-period records are notable for the density of E6 guest contributors.

Core Elf Power lineup:

  • Andrew Rieger — vocals, guitar, bass, keyboards (principal songwriter)
  • Laura Carter — keyboards, drums, vocals
  • Dave Wrathgabar — guitar, vocals
  • Aaron Wegelin — drums (early period)
  • John Fernandes — violin, clarinet

Harvey Danger

Seattle indie/college rock; formed 1992. Sean Nelson (vocals), Aaron Huffman (bass), Jeff Lin (guitar/organ/violin), Evan Sult (drums). Their ARRCO debut Where Have All the Merrymakers Gone? (AR-006, 1997) contained “Flagpole Sitta” — their signature song and a minor hit. Produced by John Goodmanson.

Emo and Post-Emo: Mineral and The Gloria Record

Mineral (Discogs ID: 573453) — Houston emo band, 1994–1997, reformed 2014. Members: Chris Simpson (vocals), Jeremy Gomez (bass), Matt Hammon (guitar), Scott McCarver (guitar), Gabriel Wiley (drums). EndSerenading (ARE-089, 2014) — reunion release on ARRCO.

The Gloria Record (ID: 615446) — Austin; formed 1997 as a side-project of Mineral’s Chris Simpson and Jeremy Gomez (Mineral bassist). Their ARRCO debut Start Here (RCD16010, 2002) was produced by Mike Mogis. The label thus bridged Mineral’s original run and its post-band offshoot.

Wilco Adjacency: The Autumn Defense

Chicago duo composed of John Stirratt (bassist of Wilco and Uncle Tupelo) and Pat Sansone (guitarist/multi-instrumentalist of Wilco). Circles (ARE00039-2, 2003) produced by the band with Brad Jones (bass) and Greg Wieczorek (drums); Pat Sansone engineering credits.

Calla

New York City indie rock/post-rock; formed 1997. Aurelio Valle (guitar, vocals), Sean Donovan (bass, keyboards), Wayne B. Magruder (drums). Two ARRCO albums: Televise (ARE00025-2, 2003, produced by Chris Zane) and self-titled Calla (2004).

Talkdemonic

Portland/Brooklyn duo: Kevin O’Connor (synthesizer, guitar, multi-instrumentalist) and Lisa Molinaro (viola, synthesizer). Post-rock, cinematic slowcore. Beat Romantic (ARE 00053-2, 2006); Eyes At Half Mast (ARE 061, 2008).

Mono

Japanese post-rock quartet (Discogs ID: 208568). Two ARRCO releases: One Step More And You Die (ARE 00031-2, 2003) and New York Soundtracks (2004).
