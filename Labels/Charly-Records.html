Overview

Charly Records was founded in 1975 by Jean-Luc Young and Joop Visser, operating simultaneously from London (Visser) and Paris (Young). It was conceived primarily as a reissue label specialising in archival material from major American catalogues — most notably Sun Record Company (rockabilly and early rock ‘n’ roll: Elvis Presley, Jerry Lee Lewis, Carl Perkins, Johnny Cash, Roy Orbison, Charlie Rich) and Vee Jay Records — but from its opening years it also issued a significant run of original recordings under the CRL 5000 series, spanning Canterbury scene progressive rock, Gong-adjacent psychedelia, rockabilly, country, and early punk-adjacent acts.

The label operated across UK, French, and pan-European pressings throughout the late 1970s and 1980s, with separate licensing deals generating editions in Italy, Netherlands, Spain, and Germany. Charly established subsidiary entities including Charly Records International APS (Denmark) and Charly Holdings Inc. (USA). In the 1990s the label shifted heavily into CD reissue activity, using Movieplay Portuguesa for proxy manufacture while retaining UK market distribution.

According to producer Giorgio Gomelsky — who worked extensively with the label in the 1970s — Charly repeatedly released and sub-licensed recordings without artist approval, a practice that generated significant controversy. Charly Records (UK) Limited was formally dissolved in 2001, after which the catalogue passed under new management and became part of the Snapper Music group. The label name has remained active for ongoing reissue work through at least 2025–2026.

CRL 5000 Series — Original Releases (1976–1980)

The most musically notable portion of Charly’s output. The CRL 5xxx catalog series covers original (not archival reissue) UK albums issued from 1976 onward. Despite the label’s overall reissue identity, this series shows a surprisingly eclectic mix of live acts signed or licensed for new recordings.

Canterbury Scene Releases

Charly was a significant early UK label for the Canterbury scene in the late 1970s, issuing both original recordings and archival material for key acts in the orbit of Gong and related groups.

Key Reissue Catalogue Areas

Sun Records / Early Rock ‘n’ Roll

The core of Charly’s identity. Charly held extensive licensing rights to Sun Record Company material, driving their largest rosters:

  • Jerry Lee Lewis — 129 releases on Charly
  • Carl Perkins — 72 releases
  • Johnny Cash — 54 releases
  • Roy Orbison — 28 releases
  • Elvis Presley — 27 releases
  • Charlie Rich — 23 releases

British Invasion / British R&B

Extensive archival reissue of 1960s British material, much of it produced by Giorgio Gomelsky:

Neo-Rockabilly (Original Signings)

Charly was a significant UK home for the neo-rockabilly revival, with original recordings rather than just reissues:

  • Crazy Cavan And The Rhythm Rockers — 92 releases; the label’s most prolific original act
  • Hank Mizell — 27 releases
  • Sleepy La Beef — 31 releases
  • The Bollock Brothers — 55 releases (punk/rockabilly hybrid; also acted as producers)

Open Questions

  • Exact founding year: Discogs profile says 1975, earliest releases in DB are dated 1974 (Jerry Lee Lewis, IF (6), Charlie Rich via CR 300002–300004). May be 1974 or early 1975.
  • Giorgio Gomelsky’s specific claims about unlicensed releases — names artists affected but doesn’t specify which releases. Worth cross-referencing against specific artists.
  • The “Immediate Records” catalog (referenced in Glyn Johns’ credits as “original Immediate recordings”) suggests Charly also held rights to Andrew Loog Oldham’s Immediate Records catalog — not confirmed in Discogs label profile.
  • Relationship to Ace Records: Discogs does not show a direct parent/child relationship; they are separate companies, though both specialise in UK archival reissue work.
  • CRL 5000 series gaps: Numbers CRL 5002, CRL 5023–5024 not found in DB — may be missing entries or unissued catalog numbers.
  • Whether the 2001 dissolution and relaunch under Snapper altered the catalog rights or just the operating company.
