Domino

Independent British record label, the primary imprint of Domino Recording Company (Domino Recording Co. Ltd., UK company no. 03229546). Founded 1993 in Putney, South West London, by Laurence Bell and his partner. Discogs label ID: 26465. Parent company ID: 69345.

Label code: LC 10192. Catalog number schemes: WIG (albums) and RUG (singles and EPs) for UK/European releases; DOMUS then DNO (until 2013) for US division releases.

Total releases in Discogs DB: 7,713 (across 1992–2026).

Overview

Domino is one of the most consistently significant British independent labels of its era. Founded by Laurence Bell — a former member of the bands Perfect Daze and Sink (4) — the label began as a UK licensing vehicle for American indie and lo-fi artists (Sebadoh, Smog, Palace, Silver Jews, Royal Trux) and grew through the 1990s into a broad catalog covering US indie, post-rock, electronic, and experimental music. The mid-2000s breakthrough of Franz Ferdinand and Arctic Monkeys established Domino as a major commercial force while maintaining independent status. From the 2010s onward, Domino expanded further into electronic music, US singer-songwriter territory, and contemporary indie-pop.

The company operating the label, Domino Recording Co. Ltd., incorporated 25 July 1996, also runs a publishing arm, a US division (Domino USA), and several sister imprints including Weird World (2), Ribbon Music, Double Six Records, and Series 500.

The US office was initially located on Washington Street, Brooklyn; it moved to Jay Street, Brooklyn in late 2014. US-aimed pressings are often identifiable by the Brooklyn address appearing first on the sleeve.

Laurence Bell

Discogs artist ID: 694357 (real name: Laurence Bonamy Simon Bell; aliases include Blind Lorenzo Hoss and Laurence Repo). Bell founded the label out of his own music background — he had been in the post-punk/shoegaze-adjacent group Perfect Daze and Sink (4) before pivoting to label work. He remains ultimate controller of the Domino Music Group Limited parent entity.

Roster by Era

1993–1999: American Indie Foundation

The label launched in 1993 primarily licensing US indie and noise-pop acts for UK release. The early catalog was anchored by a cluster of Drag City and lo-fi American acts with strong Domino relationships:

  • Sebadoh (Discogs ID: 68467) — the label’s first major relationship; Bubble & Scrape (1993, WIGMC4), Bakesale (1994), Harmacy (1996), The Sebadoh (1999)
  • Royal Trux (ID: 65828) — Cats And Dogs (1993), Twin Infinitives (1994), Accelerator (1998), Veterans Of Disorder (1999)
  • Smog / Bill Callahan (ID: 56147) — The Doctor Came At Dawn (1996), Red Apple Falls (1997), Knock Knock (1999); relationship continued through A River Ain’t Too Much To Love (2005) and Supper (2003)
  • Palace / Bonnie “Prince” Billy (ID: 135307 / 66089) — Hope (1994), Viva Last Blues (1995), Arise Therefore (1996); Will Oldham became one of the label’s longest-running artists
  • Silver Jews (ID: 65827) — Starlite Walker (1994), The Natural Bridge (1996), American Water (1998)
  • Pavement (ID: 7578) — Brighten The Corners (1997), Terror Twilight (1999); catalog reissues through the 2000s
  • Elliott Smith (ID: 243300) — UK releases of Either/Or (1997), Elliott Smith (1998), Roman Candle (1998); posthumous From A Basement On The Hill (2004)
  • Flying Saucer Attack (ID: 66963) — extensive mid-1990s catalog; Distance (1994), Chorus (1995), Further (1995)
  • The Pastels (ID: 2805) — Mobile Safari (1994), Illumination (1997); ongoing reissue relationship
  • Come (2) (ID: 89573) — Near Life Experience (1996), Gently Down The Stream (1998)
  • Pram (ID: 3542) — North Pole Radio Station (1998), Space Siren (1999), The Museum Of Imaginary Animals (2000)
  • Jim O’Rourke (ID: 3550) — Bad Timing (1997), Eureka (1999)
  • Mouse On Mars (ID: 2540) — Distroia (1999), Niun Niggung (1999)
  • Hood (ID: 5047) — Rustic Houses Forlorn Valleys (1998), The Cycle Of Days And Seasons (1999)
  • Quasi (2) (ID: 242940) — Featuring “Birds” (1998), Field Studies (1999)
  • The Third Eye Foundation (ID: 212837) — Ghost (1997), You Guys Kill Me (1998)
  • Robert Wyatt (ID: 5049) — EPs (1999); ongoing catalog presence from 1999 onward, including Comicopera (2007)
  • Gastr Del Sol — Camoufleur (1998, WIGCD44)
  • Papa M (ID: 30391) — Live From A Shark Cage (1999)
  • The Folk Implosion (ID: 297458) — One Part Lullaby (1999)

The Series 500 sub-label (also under Domino) released more experimental and electronic material in this period.

2000–2002: UK Talent Emerges

By the early 2000s, Domino began signing British acts for the first time:

  • Clinic (ID: 42650) — signed 1999; debut single Second Line (1999) on Domino, Internal Wrangler (2000, WIG/DOMUS01), Walking With Thee (2004), Winchester Cathedral (2004); eight studio albums on the label through 2012
  • Four Tet / Kieran Hebden (ID: 3543) — Pause (2001), Rounds (2003, WIGCD126), Everything Ecstatic (2005), Remixes (2006), There Is Love In You (2010); plus the Kieran Hebden & Steve Reid Exchange Sessions (2006–2008)
  • Fridge (ID: 30361) — Happiness (2001), The Sun (2007)
  • The Kills (ID: 202601) — signed 2002; Keep On Your Mean Side (2003, WIGCD124), No Wow (2004), ongoing relationship through 2024
  • Max Tundra (ID: 418) — Cakes (2000), Some Best Friend You Turned Out To Be (2000), Mastered By Guy At The Exchange (2002)
  • Hood — Outside Closer (2004)

2003–2006: Commercial Breakthrough

The label’s commercial and critical fortunes changed dramatically with two Scottish signings:

Franz Ferdinand (ID: 170994) — Darts Of Pleasure debut single (2003, DNO22CD); self-titled debut album (2004, WIGCD136, 40+ pressings in DB); You Could Have It So Much Better (2005, WIGLP161, 51 pressings); became Domino’s best-selling act of the era. Singles Take Me Out and Do You Want To were mainstream UK hits.

Arctic Monkeys (ID: 391170) — signed after a grassroots demo campaign; Whatever People Say I Am, That’s What I’m Not (2005) became the fastest-selling debut album in UK chart history at the time; Favourite Worst Nightmare (2007); continued through Tranquility Base Hotel + Casino (2018) and beyond. The highest-release-count artist in Domino’s catalog (596 releases in DB, from 2004 to 2026).

Other key signings of this period:

  • The Notwist (ID: 616) — Neon Golden (2003, DOMUS10CD)
  • Junior Boys (ID: 144679) — Last Exit (2004), So This Is Goodbye (2006)
  • Sons And Daughters (ID: 220657) — Love The Cup (2004), The Repulsion Box (2005)
  • Juana Molina (ID: 187553) — Segundo (2003), Tres Cosas (2004), Son (2006)
  • James Yorkston (ID: 95409) — debut Moving Up Country (2002), extensive ongoing catalog
  • Stephen Malkmus & The Jicks (ID: 97614) — Pig Lib (2003), Face The Truth (2005)
  • Caribou (ID: 275841) — The Milk Of Human Kindness (2005), Yeti (2005)
  • Ulrich Schnauss (ID: 22152) — A Strangely Isolated Place (2004, DNO32)
  • Adem (2) (ID: 216643) — Homesongs (2004)
  • Test Icicles (ID: 347124) — For Screening Purposes Only (2005)
  • Archie Bronson Outfit (ID: 363248) — Derdang Derdang (2006)
  • Psapp (ID: 129228) — The Only Thing I Ever Wanted (2006)
  • Clearlake (ID: 116255) — Cedars (2003), Amber (2005)

2007–2012: Electronic Expansion and Indie Maturity

The label consolidated and diversified, signing key electronic and experimental artists alongside continuing its indie base:

  • Animal Collective (ID: 207958) — Strawberry Jam (2007, WIGCD199), Merriweather Post Pavilion (2009), Centipede Hz (2012); flagship experimental act
  • Wild Beasts (ID: 679225) — Limbo, Panto (2008), Two Dancers (2009), Smother (2011), Present Tense (2014); critically acclaimed throughout
  • Hot Chip (ID: 159840) — joined Domino from 2012 for their later catalog
  • Dirty Projectors (ID: 400992) — Bitte Orca (2009), Swing Lo Magellan (2012)
  • Lightspeed Champion (ID: 850000) — Falling Off The Lavender Bridge (2007)
  • These New Puritans (ID: 640009) — Beat Pyramid (2007), Hidden (2010)
  • Von Südenfed (ID: 793128) — Tromatic Reflexxions (2007; Mark E. Smith of The Fall with Mouse On Mars)
  • Bonde Do Role (ID: 447023) — With Lasers (2007)
  • The Cinematic Orchestra (ID: 3384) — Ma Fleur (2007)
  • Anna Calvi (ID: 1908565) — debut album (2011); ongoing
  • Jon Hopkins (ID: 24056) — Insides (2009), Immunity (2013), Singularity (2018)
  • Austra (ID: 2101535) — debut 2011, extended relationship
  • Real Estate (2) (ID: 1415073) — Days (2011), Atlas (2014)
  • Julia Holter (ID: 1475750) — Loud City Song (2013), Have You In My Wilderness (2015)
  • Owen Pallett (ID: 284436) — signed 2010
  • Cass McCombs (ID: 394536) — Dropping The Writ (2007), ongoing
  • Blood Orange (2) (ID: 2372814) — Dev Hynes; ongoing from 2011
  • Panda Bear (ID: 218809) — on Domino from 2010
  • The Last Shadow Puppets (ID: 1099843) — Arctic Monkeys/Rascals supergroup; The Age Of The Understatement (2008)
  • Robert Wyatt — Comicopera (2007, WIGCD202P)
  • Tricky (ID: 3140) — Mixed Race (2010), False Idols (2013)
  • The Fall (ID: 2228) — Your Future Our Clutter (2010)
  • My Bloody Valentine (ID: 6516) — catalog/reissue relationship from 2021
  • John Cale (ID: 66358) — Shifty Adventures In Nookie Wood (2012); ongoing

Archival/reissue highlights: Young Marble Giants Colossal Youth (2007 reissue), Orange Juice The Glasgow School (2005), Galaxie 500 Peel Sessions (2005), Neutral Milk Hotel In The Aeroplane Over The Sea (2005 European reissue), Josef K Entomology (2006), The Triffids Born Sandy Devotional (2006).
