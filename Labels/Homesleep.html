Overview

Homesleep was an Italian independent label that operated from 1999 to 2009, releasing 32 records across its single decade of activity — all of them in the 2000s. Its catalogue leaned toward indie rock, post-rock, and alternative rock, with folk and pop shadings, and its roster included Giardini Di Mirò, Yuppie Flu, and Andrea Rovacchi. The label also ran a dedicated singles imprint, Homesleep Singles Club.
