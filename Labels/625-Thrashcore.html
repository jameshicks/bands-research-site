Overview

625 Thrashcore is an American hardcore punk label founded in 1993, sometimes credited as 625 Productions. Its catalogue spans power violence, grindcore, thrash, and hardcore, peaking sharply in the 2000s with over 114 releases before continuing at a steadier pace through the 2010s and 2020s. The label operates two sublabels — Barbaric Thrash and Possessed To Skate — and counts Spazz and Punch (13) among its key roster acts.
