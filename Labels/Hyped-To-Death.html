Hyped To Death

Discogs ID: 80378 Origin: US Founded: c. 2000s Sublabels: 4 (per Discogs) Release count: 137 (per Discogs)

US archival label run by Chuck Warner (Discogs ID: 1793197), specialising in documenting the UK DIY / post-punk underground of the late 1970s and early 1980s. Best known for the Messthetics series — numbered compilation CDs compiling obscure cassettes, self-released singles, and small-run vinyl from the post-punk era. The label’s URL was hyped2death.com.
