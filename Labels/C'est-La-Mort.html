Overview

C’est La Mort was an American independent label based in Baker, Louisiana, active from 1986 through the mid-1990s and run by label head Woodrow Dumas. It concentrated on gothic, ethereal, and “artsy” pop and rock — spanning synth-pop, goth rock, and indie rock — with its thirty-release catalogue split evenly across the 1980s and 1990s. The roster drew from a compact circle of artists including East Ash, Scott Rodger, John Golden, and James Pinker. The label is now defunct, and its corporate structure — any parent or sublabel relationships — remains unrecorded.
