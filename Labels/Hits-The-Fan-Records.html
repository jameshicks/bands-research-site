Hits The Fan Records

Glasgow-based independent record label. Discogs label ID: 108576. Contact: hitsthefanrecords@gmail.com. Website: hitsthefanrecords.co.uk.

Overview

Hits The Fan Records was conceived in 2006 by Marcus MacKay and Claire MacKay. Its first release was the debut album by Frightened Rabbit — Sing The Greys (HTF01, 2006) — produced by Marcus MacKay and the band. The label then lay essentially dormant for nearly a decade before re-emerging in 2015 as the home for Kathryn Joseph’s debut album Bones You Have Thrown Me And Blood I’ve Spilled, which won the Mercury Music Prize that year. In 2015–2016 the label briefly expanded its roster to include two additional artists: JR Green (an EP) and Lost Left (an album and a single). All creative releases across the label’s lifespan were produced by Marcus MacKay, with the single exception of the JR Green EP, which was co-produced with Joe McAlinden.

The label has no parent and no sublabels on Discogs. The 18 Discogs entries represent multiple pressings and formats of five distinct recordings across four artists.

Artists on the Label

Frightened Rabbit

Debut album Sing The Greys (HTF01, 2006) was the label’s founding release. The band self-produced alongside MacKay. Members at the time included Scott Hutchison (songwriter, vocalist), with the core lineup that would go on to sign to FatCat Records. Discogs artist ID: 820682.

Kathryn Joseph

Primary artist of the label’s 2015–2016 activity. Three releases: the debut album (HTF003), a 7” single (HTF004), and a follow-up 7” single (HTF009). Produced entirely by Marcus MacKay. Moved to Rock Action Records for her second album in 2018. Discogs artist ID: 4199019.

JR Green

Duo of siblings Jacob Green (accordion, vocals, songwriter) and Rory Green (guitar, vocals, songwriter). Released one EP, Bring The Witch Doctor (HTF005/HTF005c, 2015), in CD and cassette formats. Co-produced by Marcus MacKay and Joe McAlinden. The band has one further self-released recording (Tiger Lily, 2017) in Discogs. Discogs artist ID: 5505089.

Lost Left

Released the album Levollinen (HTF007, 2016) and the single Thank You For The Lung (HTF008, 2016). Recorded by Marcus MacKay. Their entire Discogs catalog is on Hits The Fan. Artwork by Dan Aldous and Kate Lyons. Discogs artist ID: 5505005.
