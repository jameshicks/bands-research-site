From Discogs

UK label run by Mark E. Smith, founded 1987, dissolved 5 May 1992. The first appearance of the Cog Sinister label was in 1987 when The Fall used it as the imprint for their self-released anthology album “Palace Of Swords Reversed”.

The imprint returned in 1990 when the band signed to Fontana and used Cog Sinister as a vanity label for their releases. When they moved from Fontana to Permanent Records, they continued to use the Cog Sinister brand on their releases.

Since leaving Permanent in 1995, The Fall no longer used Cog Sinister on their new material. However, since 1997, the imprint has become part of the Voiceprint label group, and is being used for reissues of the group’s back-catalogue.

After the demise of Voiceprint, Cog Sinister is now part of Gonzo Multimedia

Overview

Cog Sinister is a UK label created by Mark E. Smith in 1987 as a vanity imprint for The Fall’s self-released anthology “Palace Of Swords Reversed.” It returned as an active imprint in 1990 when The Fall signed to Fontana, and continued through their subsequent deal with Permanent Records; after they departed Permanent in 1995, the label was no longer used for new material. Since 1997 it has functioned as a reissue imprint, first within the Voiceprint group and now under Gonzo Multimedia, with its back-catalogue activity producing a notable second wave of releases in the 2010s (17 releases) that exceeds even its active-band peak in the 1990s (26 releases).
