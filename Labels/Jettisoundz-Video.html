Overview

A video imprint of Jettisoundz Ltd., founded and operated by John Bentham and Karen Bentham, which released VHS and video material from the UK post-punk and industrial underground beginning in 1983. Output was concentrated in the 1980s (47 releases) with a smaller run into the early 1990s (17 releases), spanning punk, industrial, experimental, and psychobilly. Its roster centred on key figures from that scene — Throbbing Gristle, Genesis P-Orridge, Psychic TV, Alien Sex Fiend, and Chris Carter. In 1992 the label was reconstituted as Visionary Communications.
