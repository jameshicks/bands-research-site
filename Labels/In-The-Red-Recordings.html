In The Red Recordings

Los Angeles-based independent record label founded in 1991 by Larry Hardy. Discogs label ID: 34157 (parent). Sublabels: In The Red Records (ID: 161626), Explosion Juke Box Series (ID: 298285).

Overview

In The Red Recordings is the Los Angeles independent label founded by Larry Hardy in 1991, specialising in garage rock, lo-fi, punk, and underground rock from the US and internationally. Hardy started the label out of Anaheim, California (early address: 2627 E. Strong Pl.), later operating with a P.O. Box in Los Angeles (CA 90050).

The label’s first release was a single by The Morlocks (ITR 1001, 1990), and its second was Kings Of Rock (ITR 1002, 1991). According to Hardy (via Wikipedia), he wished he had waited for Mick Collins’s The Gories material to be ready for the debut slot, but the recording moved slowly and he pressed ahead — the Gories’ Here Be The Gories became ITR 1003 (1991) regardless.

Through the 1990s the label built its identity around American primitive garage and junk-blues: early stalwarts included Cheater Slicks, Bassholes, The Jon Spencer Blues Explosion, The Dirtbombs, Demolition Doll Rods, Andre Williams, Pussy Galore (2), Boss Hog, and Blacktop.

In the 2000s the roster widened to include international garage (Country Teasers, King Brothers, Black Time), Toronto’s The Deadly Snakes, Detroit’s Reigning Sound, Memphis-connected Lost Sounds and Jay Reatard (solo as well as the Reatards), and early releases by The Black Lips. Greg Cartwright of Reigning Sound became one of the label’s most prolific producers across this period.

From the late 2000s onward, In The Red became closely associated with the San Francisco/Los Angeles garage revival centred on Thee Oh Sees (John Dwyer’s primary vehicle), Fuzz (16), Meatbodies, Wand (2), and Ty Segall, who also recorded and produced for the label. Kid Congo Powers (formerly of The Gun Club and The Bad Seeds) has sustained a long relationship with the label through Kid Congo & The Pink Monkey Birds.

The label’s catalog runs to over 1,100 releases. Its catalog numbers follow an ITR-NNN sequence, with a handful of special series (ITR 1313 for Halloween records; ITR 2.13.61 for punk reissue series; the Explosion Juke Box Series sublabel). The sublabel In The Red Records (ID 161626) is used interchangeably with the parent on many releases.

Key recurring production figures across the catalog include engineer/producer Chris Woodhouse (48 releases, Sacramento-based), mastering engineer JJ Golden (72 mastering credits), producer Jim Diamond (2) (Detroit, 18 credits), and Australian mastering engineer Mikey Young (39 credits).
