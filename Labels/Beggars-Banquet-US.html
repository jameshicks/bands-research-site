US division of the venerable UK indie label Beggars Banquet. They mainly issue North American editions of releases from Beggars Banquet and affiliated labels (4AD, Too Pure, XL Recordings, etc.) that are identical to the UK editions (same catalog number, tracks, etc.). Overview

Beggars Banquet US is the North American arm of the UK independent label Beggars Banquet, established in 1989 to handle US releases for the broader Beggars Group — including affiliated labels 4AD, Too Pure, and XL Recordings. The majority of its catalogue mirrors UK editions, sharing catalogue numbers and track listings; however, exclusive North American titles carry BBN or BBNYC prefixes. Its catalogue runs from the early 1990s through the mid-2000s, with the bulk of releases concentrated in the 2000s, and spans Alternative Rock, Indie Rock, Synth-pop, and Downtempo. The roster draws heavily from post-punk and goth-adjacent lineages, with Gary Numan, Love And Rockets, Daniel Ash, and David J among the credited artists.
