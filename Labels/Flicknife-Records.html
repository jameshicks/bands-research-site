Overview

Flicknife Records was a UK independent label founded in 1980 by Frenchy Gloder, carving out a niche in punk and Hawkwind solo projects during its first decade — 62 of its 72 selected releases came in the 1980s, spanning space rock, psychedelic rock, punk, and hard rock. Its roster centred on Hawkwind-associated acts including Dave Brock, Huw Lloyd-Langton, and Porky (5), making Flicknife one of the key outlets for that scene’s extended universe. Cherry Red took over the label in 1992, ending its original run, but Gloder revived it in 2010 and has released a small number of titles since. It sits under the Soho Girl parent imprint and operated the Lip Service Magazine sublabel.
