Overview

Rossil was a Portuguese record label founded in 1977 by José António Crespo, operating under the parent company Edições Rossil, Lda. Its catalogue spans the late 1970s and early 1980s in roughly equal measure — around 89 releases in the 1970s and 85 in the 1980s — covering Fado, Disco, Pop Rock, Rock & Roll, and Vocal styles. Alongside the main imprint the company operated several sublabels including Fonodis, Lis, Pintainho, Vidiofono, and Voyage, with its Portuguese roster featuring artists such as Fernando Correia Martins, António Sala, and Paulo Alexandre. By the mid-1980s the operation had pivoted toward television programming under the Vidiofono identity, with the last known Videofono production dated 1987.
