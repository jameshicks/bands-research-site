Overview

Guernica was a short-lived sub-label of 4AD launched by Ivo Watts-Russell in 1992, conceived as a home for one-off projects reflecting his founding vision for 4AD. Its name honoured Picasso’s 1937 anti-war painting, and its visual identity was kept deliberately separate from the parent label by commissioning all sleeve design from London studio The Senate (3). The entire catalogue is concentrated in a single two-year window — ten releases between 1992 and 1994 — with styles spanning indie rock, lo-fi, minimal, and synth-pop from acts including Insides, Unrest, Bettie Serveert, and that dog. Almost all artists moved on after one release; only Insides returned with a follow-up, making Guernica one of the most compact, self-contained imprints in the 4AD orbit. All vinyl pressings came with a bonus 7” of additional tracks not available separately.
