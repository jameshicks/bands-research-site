Overview

Beat Records is a Japanese electronic label founded in 1994, built around licensing and reissuing Western albums for the Japanese market, operating interchangeably under the names Beatink and Beatink Inc. (the latter reserved for publishing credits). Its catalogue concentrates in the 1990s — 28 releases in that decade against 12 in the 2000s — and skews toward experimental, downtempo, IDM, ambient, and dub styles. The roster includes Adrian Sherwood, Brian Eno, and Audio Active; a dedicated sublabel, Legendary Classics From On-U Sound, points to a particular relationship with Sherwood’s UK dub imprint.
