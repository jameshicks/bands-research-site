After Hours

Discogs ID: 5842
Origin: Tokyo, Japan (3-9-11 Kitazawa, Setagaya-ku, Tokyo 155-0031)
Run by: “After Hours” magazine; website afterhoursmagazine.jp
Also referred to as: Afterhours, Afterhours Inc.

Note: The previous stub incorrectly identified this as a German label. After Hours (ID 5842) is a Japanese label. A separate “After Hours (2)” (Discogs ID 45907, 29 releases) also exists; the German co-release of Kammerflimmer Kollektief Hysteria may involve one of these or a different label entirely — needs checking.

Overview

After Hours is a Tokyo-based independent label with approximately 108 releases documented on Discogs, active from at least 1997 into the 2010s. The label has a strong connection to Adam Pierce’s Bubble Core Records network, co-releasing multiple Mice Parade and associated records for the Japanese market.

Connections

  • Bubble Core Records — long-running co-release partner; Mice Parade, HiM, and Igloo (2) all appeared on both labels
  • Mice Parade — multiple releases; Collaborations (AH-008LP, 2000) was first co-release with Bubble Core
  • Igloo (2) — Igloo (AH-046, 2003); Adam Pierce / Doro Tachler duo
  • HiM — four Japanese releases on After Hours (AH-018, AH-068, AH-084, AH-104)
  • Kammerflimmer Kollektief — Hysteria (AH-017, 2000); German jazz-experimental group
  • The Notwist — Close To The Glass (AH-127, 2014)

Open Questions
