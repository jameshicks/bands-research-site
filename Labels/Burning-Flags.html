Burning Flags

Discogs label ID: 3289905. Photography studio and publishing imprint operated by Glen E. Friedman; Los Angeles and New York. Website: burningflags.com.

Burning Flags functions as Friedman’s commercial operation and the vehicle for his photobook publishing. Discogs describes it as “[a225397]‘s photo studio and company” and links it directly to his artist profile. The Discogs label entity exists but carries no release entries — Friedman’s photobooks are not catalogued as Discogs releases. The label appears in Discogs primarily as a workplace / studio credit in Friedman’s artist page rather than as a traditional record label with its own catalog.

Overview

Friedman founded Burning Flags as the infrastructure for his independent publishing and commercial photography practice. The studio name appears on his photobooks — published outside standard music-industry distribution channels — and has served as the operational entity for commissioned work across punk/hardcore and hip-hop. The imprint is distinct from any record label relationship; it publishes print matter (books, prints, limited editions) rather than phonographic releases.

The name echoes the confrontational rhetoric that runs through Friedman’s body of work. His Discogs artist profile (active span 1981–2025, 146 unique masters, ~1,500+ release credits across pressings) lists “Worked at Burning Flags” as a biographical fact, placing it as a continuous employer/studio rather than a one-time venture.

Photography Work Published Through / Associated With Burning Flags

Friedman’s commercial photography — conducted through Burning Flags as studio — encompasses the defining images of American punk, hardcore, and early hip-hop. Representative credits documented in Discogs:

Punk / Hardcore:

  • Adolescents — debut LP back cover and insert (1981, Frontier Records)
  • Circle Jerks — Wild In The Streets (1982); multiple BYO and Frontier releases
  • Germs — What We Do Is Secret (1981, Slash)
  • Minor Threat — insert photography; Salad Days; Complete Discography center spread (Dischord Records)
  • Suicidal Tendencies — 1983 self-titled debut (Frontier Records); Friedman credited as Producer, Photography By, Design
  • Fugazi — inner photos across most Dischord releases 1988–2002
  • Bad Brains — front cover photograph (2021 reissue, Bad Brains Records / ORG Music)

Hip-Hop:

  • Run-DMC — “Walk This Way” cover (1986); Raising Hell reissue booklet
  • Public Enemy — Yo! Bum Rush The Show cover concept (1987); It Takes A Nation Of Millions (1988, Def Jam)
  • LL Cool J — Bigger And Deffer cover (1987, CBS / Def Jam)
  • Ice-T — 11 album masters spanning Rhyme Pays (1987) through O.G. Original Gangster (1991) and beyond
  • Beastie Boys — Check Your Head cover (1992, Capitol / Grand Royal)
  • Slick Rick — The Great Adventures Of Slick Rick cover (1988, Columbia / Def Jam)
  • Russell Simmons — introduced Friedman to the hip-hop world; gateway to Def Jam orbit

Other:

  • System Of A Down — self-titled (1999) and Toxicity (2001, American Recordings / Columbia)
  • Skateboarders: A Look Back: Dogtown & Z-Boys (2021, At The Dojo Records) — directed and photographed
