Ex Records

Discogs ID: 33157 Location: Amsterdam, Netherlands (P.O. Box 635, 1000 AP) Contact: mailorder@theex.nl / ex@theex.nl Website: theex.nl/discography.html

Record label owned and operated by The Ex. The catalog code “EX” was in use on one-off labels from at least 1981 (EX 006); Ex Records became the consolidated label name from approximately 1986 onward.
