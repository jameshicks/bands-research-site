Out Of Breath Records

Discogs label ID: 831457

Independent cassette label run out of California (self-described as “a sunny bedroom in California”). Active primarily 2013–2018, releasing limited-edition cassettes in the lo-fi, emo, indie rock, and experimental pop space. Catalog prefix OOB; at least 20 catalog numbers documented, though several gaps exist (see Open Questions below). The label co-released several titles with Funeral Sounds and Driftwood Records. Its anchor artist was Pill Friends, who accounts for six releases across the run.

Contact: outofbreathrecords@gmail.com
Web: outofbreathrecords.bandcamp.com / outofbreathrecords.rip (archive)

History

Out Of Breath Records launched in 2013 with Pill Friends’ Blessed Suffering (OOB011 — the earliest Discogs-documented release, though the OOB003 catalog number for Haunt (5)‘s Portraits suggests earlier catalog existed). The label operated during the early-to-mid 2010s microwave of bedroom cassette culture, releasing short-run tapes that intersected lo-fi emo, chiptune, shoegaze, and indie pop.

The 2014 period was the label’s busiest: four separate artists (Haunt (5), Light Black, Shadynasty, Basin (2) / Sonic4ever) all received releases, with the Headcold / Nouns split Downer (OOB012) co-issued with Funeral Sounds. Light Black’s Tom Hanks and Shadynasty’s ✌Curtis✌ were both co-released with Driftwood Records.

Activity slowed after 2014 with a gap before three 2016 releases — Candy (60)‘s Wrapped In Plastic (OOB015), Pill Friends’ Child Sacrifice (OOB-016), and Elli (22)‘s Woof (OOB017). The final documented releases came in 2017–2018: Pill Friends’ b-sides/covers compilation Let’s Be Nice (catalogued as OOB-020), Stevie Dinner’s Pet Detective (OOB019), and the Fog Lake / Euphoria Again split cassette EP (also OOB020 — a duplicated catalog number, possibly an error).

Open Questions
