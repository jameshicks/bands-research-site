Overview

Resonance is a New York City independent label that operated under the Semaphore imprint, releasing records from 1986 through the mid-1990s. Its catalogue of roughly 33 releases clusters heavily in the late 1980s (22 releases) before thinning across the 1990s (11 releases), and spans garage rock, psychedelic rock, indie rock, and punk. Roster acts include Sister Ray and Peter Stuart (3), with recurring credits from Günter Pauler and Elan Portnoy.
