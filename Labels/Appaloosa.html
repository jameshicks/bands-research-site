Overview

Appaloosa is an Italian independent label founded in 1979 by Franco Ratti and distributed through the IRD Records group. Its catalogue centres on folk rock, blues rock, country, and harmonica blues, with a roster that has included Andy J. Forest, Mary-Ann Brandon, Fred James, and Bob Hall. Output peaked in the 1980s and 1990s (31 and 40 releases respectively), then contracted sharply in the 2000s before a sustained revival from 2010 onward that has produced nearly 40 further titles. The label operates a sublabel, Maremmano Records.
