Loathsome Reels

Glasgow imprint. Discogs label ID: 149240. No parent label; no sublabels. One release documented.

Overview

Loathsome Reels is a Glasgow-based self-run imprint, almost certainly operated by Bill Wells as a vehicle for releasing the debut recording by The Bill Wells Octet. It has one release on Discogs: Live ‘93 - ‘94 (1995, cat. LR 001), a limited edition CD drawn from live performances in 1993 and 1994. The label name echoes one of the recording’s own track titles — “The Loathsome Reel #1” and “The Loathsome Reel #2” — suggesting that the imprint was created specifically around this release rather than being a pre-existing operation.

No profile, contact information, parent label, or URL is listed for Loathsome Reels in Discogs. No further releases under this label ID exist in the database. The catalog number LR 001 implies the imprint was intended as a continuing series, though no LR 002 or subsequent release has been documented. Whether it was ever used again remains unknown.

The release bears a barcode (5028350950320), indicating it was commercially distributed rather than purely hand-sold at gigs, but Discogs lists no distributor credit and the pressing count in Discogs stands at 4 distinct entries, suggesting modest but genuine circulation.

Glasgow Context

Loathsome Reels belongs to the small-run, self-released stratum of the mid-1990s Glasgow experimental and jazz underground. The Octet’s network overlapped with Geographic (Domino sublabel, which released the Octet’s 1998 LP and Bill Wells Trio records), Creeping Bent (Glasgow DIY punk/indie/pop imprint), and the broader community of musicians connected to The Pastels and Belle & Sebastian via shared personnel. None of these were involved in the Loathsome Reels release, which preceded those connections.
