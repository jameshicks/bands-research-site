From Nov. 23rd, 1992, Jettisoundz (and their label Jettisoundz Video) partnered with Iain McNay to become Visionary Communications (aka Visionary), in partnership with Cherry Red Records Ltd. The catalog number system from the previous Jettisoundz Video label was continued.

Overview

Visionary Communications (also known simply as Visionary) was established on 23 November 1992 when Jettisoundz and its subsidiary Jettisoundz Video partnered with Iain McNay under the Cherry Red Records umbrella, continuing the Jettisoundz Video catalogue numbering system. The label concentrated its activity in the 1990s, when the overwhelming majority of its roughly 59-release catalogue appeared, with two earlier releases tracing back to the Jettisoundz years of the 1980s. Its roster drew from post-punk and alternative fringes — Hawkwind, Psychic TV, Genesis P-Orridge, and One Way System among the most prominent names — across styles spanning punk, Oi, space rock, hardcore, and goth. The Wide Angle Collection operated as a sublabel imprint.
