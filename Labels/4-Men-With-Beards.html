US record label founded by Filippo Salvadori in San Francisco, California in 2001, and specializing in 180 gram vinyl reissues. Overview

4 Men With Beards is a San Francisco-based US reissue label founded by Filippo Salvadori in 2001 as part of the Runt label group, with a focus on 180-gram vinyl reissues of classic recordings. The label’s self-deprecating name was chosen as a nod to the record-collecting demographic it serves. CD editions were issued in parallel by Water until 2014, after which the label appears to have continued as a vinyl-only operation. Its catalogue of over 215 releases spans the 2000s and 2010s in roughly equal measure, covering psychedelic rock, folk, soul, and punk — with roster names including George Clinton, Funkadelic, John Fahey, Tom Verlaine, and Iggy Pop.
