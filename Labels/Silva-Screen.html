Overview

Silva Screen is a UK-based soundtrack and movie theme label, founded in 1983 under the parent company Silva Screen Records Ltd. Its catalogue centres on soundtrack, score, theme, ambient, and modern classical music, with a substantial presence across film music from the 1980s onward. The label’s roster has prominently featured the City of Prague Philharmonic Orchestra alongside individual contributors including Reynold da Silva, David Stoner, and James Fitzpatrick. Silva Screen has extended its reach through dedicated sublabels: Digital Film Scores, Primetime Collection, Primetime Records, and The Essential Film Music Collection.
