M’lady’s Records

Brooklyn-based DIY independent label. Discogs label ID: 132042

Overview

M’lady’s Records is a Brooklyn indie label with 113 primary releases on Discogs and three sublabels. Active in the late-2000s and 2010s, it released music across punk, lo-fi, experimental, and indie genres. Released City Center’s Cops Don’t Care 7” in 2009.
