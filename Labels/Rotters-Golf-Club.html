Rotters Golf Club

UK independent record label. Discogs label ID: 4928. Run by Andrew Weatherall. Address: 2 Prowse Place, Camden, NW1 9PH, UK. Contact (historic): claire@rottersgolfclub.com. SoundCloud: soundcloud.com/rotters_golf_club.

Overview

Weatherall’s primary label from 2001 through his death in February 2020. Described on Discogs as “UK electro label run by Two Lone Swordsmen.” Releases span his own solo work, Two Lone Swordsmen material (with Keith Tenniswood), The Asphodells (with Tim Fairplay), The Woodleigh Research Facility (with Nina Walsh), Radioactive Man (Keith Tenniswood solo), and associated acts.

Early releases carried the imprint R.G.C. Records (ID 352456) before standardising as Rotters Golf Club. The label’s SoundCloud page remained active as an archive.

Roster Summary

Primary artists on the label: Andrew Weatherall (solo), Two Lone Swordsmen, The Asphodells, The Woodleigh Research Facility, Radioactive Man (Keith Tenniswood), Klart, Aramchek, Decal, Remote, The Boardroom.

Klart, Aramchek, and Hidden Library are alias project names shared by Weatherall and Tenniswood. The label functioned in practice as a house label for Weatherall and his immediate collaborators.
