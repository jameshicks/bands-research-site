Overview

Manifesto is an independent US record label founded in 1993 by Evan Cohen, established as the successor to Bizarre Records and Straight Records. Its roster ties together California punk — Dead Kennedys, Jello Biafra, and East Bay Ray all appear — with older catalog material including Tim Buckley, alongside reissue-oriented figures such as mastering specialist Bill Inglot. The catalogue is concentrated in the 2000s and 2010s (roughly 80 of ~100 selected releases), pointing to sustained catalog and reissue activity through those decades rather than a single founding-era burst. The label also operates the sublabel Blixa Sounds.
