Self Immolation

Self-run imprint of JG Thirlwell, used for all early Foetus output 1981–1989. Catalog prefix: WOMB. Discogs label ID: 12244.

Overview

Founded by Thirlwell in London as a vehicle for releasing his own music entirely independently. Often co-released with Some Bizzare (Stevo Pearce’s label) for UK and European distribution. 14 releases documented on Discogs (1981–1990).
