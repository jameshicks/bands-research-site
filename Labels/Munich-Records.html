Munich Records

Origin: Netherlands

Dutch independent label that released the bulk of Centro-Matic and South San Gabriel’s European catalog, including Distance And Clime (2001), Love You Just The Same (2003), Welcome, Convalescence (2003), and The Carlton Chronicles (2005).
