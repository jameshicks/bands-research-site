Overview

Green UFOs is a Spanish label specialising in the distribution of post-rock, IDM, and off-centre pop, bridging English and American titles into the Spanish market while also releasing domestic artists working in the same genres. Its catalogue was concentrated almost entirely in the 2000s, with styles spanning indie rock, indie pop, alternative rock, and chanson. The label’s most internationally notable role was as the primary release home for Piano Magic, whose titles were in some cases exclusively available through Green UFOs — a relationship that connects roster member Glen Johnson directly to the label’s catalogue. Johan Angergård and Franck Alba round out a roster that reflects the label’s taste for left-field European and Anglo-American indie.
