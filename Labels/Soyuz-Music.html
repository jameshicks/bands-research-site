Overview

Soyuz Music is a Russian record label operating under the parent entity ООО “Союз Мьюзик”, active since at least 2001. Its catalogue is concentrated in the 2000s and weighted heavily toward licensed metal releases — Heavy Metal, Death Metal, Thrash, and Power Metal predominate, with Alternative Rock also represented. The label umbrella covers several sublabels including Chaotic Noiz, Zakat, and Среда, suggesting deliberate genre or market segmentation beneath the main imprint. Its credited roster mixes Russian artists such as Евгений Хавтан and Браво with European metal producers like Jens Bogren and Charlie Bauerfeind, the latter two likely appearing as credited personnel on licensed international releases.
