Overview

Play It Again Sam is a major independent distribution company and label operating within the [PIAS] Entertainment Group, with roots going back to 1982. The label proper relaunched under the revived “Play It Again Sam” name in 2010 from the group’s London offices, having previously operated under variants including Pias, Play It Again Sam!, and Play It Again Sam [PIAS] — the last used for distributed labels like Antler Records and Antler-Subway. Its catalogue is heaviest in the 1990s (21 releases) and leans toward indie rock, alternative rock, and indie pop, with folk and electro also represented; the roster has included Agnes Obel, Editors, and Nick Cave.
