Overview

Subterranean Records is a San Francisco label co-founded by Michael Fox and Steven Tupper in August 1979, historically notable as both the city’s first hardcore imprint and a claimant to the title of America’s first industrial label. Its catalogue spans punk, experimental, industrial, hardcore, and avant-garde, with the bulk of its roughly 55 releases concentrated in the 1980s; output dropped sharply in the 1990s and has been sporadic since. The label also operated as a distributor and ran associated entities including Subterranean Studios and the Thermidor sublabel, with Flipper among its most prominent roster acts.
