Caldo Verde Records

San Francisco-based indie label founded in 2005 by Mark Kozelek (frontman of Red House Painters and Sun Kil Moon). Discogs label ID: 57319. No parent label; no sublabels. 233 releases cataloged on Discogs (as of 2024). Website: caldoverderecords.com.

The label functions primarily as Kozelek’s personal imprint for all Sun Kil Moon and Mark Kozelek solo output from 2005 onward, as well as a home for collaborators, Kozelek-produced acts, and a handful of artists in his orbit. Publicity (US): Robert Vickers (robert@caldoverderecords.com); publicity (Europe/UK): Fergus Denham.

Overview

Caldo Verde was created to release Tiny Cities (2005), Sun Kil Moon’s second album — an all-covers Modest Mouse record. Since then it has issued every major Sun Kil Moon LP and the bulk of Kozelek’s solo catalog, including live recordings, holiday releases, collaborative albums, and the sprawling 2017 double LP Common As Light And Love Are Red Valleys Of Blood. The catalog also encompasses several Kozelek-produced non-Kozelek artists (Retribution Gospel Choir, Desertshore, Corrina Repp) as well as distributed or co-released records by acts in the extended Kozelek circle (Jesu, Kath Bloom, Petra Haden, Advance Base, Jimmy LaValle, Ben Boye, Jim White).

Catalog numbering runs CV001 (Tiny Cities, 2005) through CV056 and beyond, with occasional parallel CV PRO- prefixes for promo live albums and CDCV-/LPCV- format-specific variants.

Production Patterns

  • Self-produced throughout. Kozelek produces every Sun Kil Moon and solo album, with no outside producers brought in for the main catalog. Guest co-production appears only on fully collaborative releases (LaValle, Broadrick, Desertshore, Yeaton).
  • Hyde Street Studios, San Francisco is the primary recording location implied by engineer credits (Aaron Prellwitz “Engineered By, Hyde Street Studios”). Well Recording also appears for some sessions (Geoff Stanfield, H.T. Waterman).
  • Red House Painters personnel continuity. Phil Carney, Anthony Koutsos, Jerry Vessel, and Geoff Stanfield — all Red House Painters members — appear in significant numbers across the Caldo Verde catalog, providing continuity from the 4AD era.
  • John Golden is the house mastering engineer, credited on 29 releases.
  • Brian Azer as art director across 105 releases gives the catalog a consistent visual identity.

Notable Collaborations

  • Retribution Gospel Choir (2008, CV 005): Low’s Alan Sparhawk’s louder project, produced by Kozelek. Engineering by Aaron Prellwitz; photography by Nyree Watts — indicating these were brought into the wider Caldo Verde infrastructure.
  • Perils From The Sea (2013, CV024): Co-credit with Jimmy LaValle (The Album Leaf). LaValle wrote all music; Kozelek wrote lyrics. A genuine dual-composer collaboration.
  • Jesu / Sun Kil Moon (2016, CV039) and 30 Seconds To The Decline Of Planet Earth (2017, CV043): Two LPs with Jesu / Justin K Broadrick. Broadrick wrote, played, engineered, and produced the Jesu instrumentation; Kozelek wrote lyrics and sang.
  • Mark Kozelek With Ben Boye And Jim White (2017, CV045): Three-way credit with pianist Ben Boye and Australian drummer Jim White (2).
  • Joey Always Smiled (2019, CDCV050): Collaboration with Petra Haden.
  • Sun Kil Moon Featuring Amoeba (2024, CV056): With Hungarian band Amoeba (14).
