Overview

BloodLust! is a Chicago label founded in 1993 and run by Mark Solotroff, who also records as an artist on the imprint. It specializes in Power Electronics, Noise, Industrial, and experimental electronic music — a catalogue that peaks heavily in the 1990s (66 releases) before tapering sharply through the 2000s and 2010s. The label operates two sub-series, the BloodLust! Cassette Series and a Private 7” Series, and has released work by BLOODYMINDED, Isidro Reyes, Jonathan Canady, and Super Eight Loop alongside Solotroff’s own projects.
