Ghost Box

Discogs ID: 56772
Co-founders: Julian House, Jim Jupp (Belbury Poly)
Origin: UK
Active: 2004–present

UK independent label co-founded by Julian House and Jim Jupp. Specialises in psychogeographic, hauntological, and library-music-influenced electronic music. Home to House’s The Focus Group, Belbury Poly, The Advisory Circle, and others. Released Broadcast + The Focus Group collaboration Familiar Shapes And Noises (GBX704, 2010). Roj Stevens (ex-Broadcast) also released on the label.

Jim Jupp is the label’s other co-founder and co-owner. He records as Belbury Poly (and alias Eric Zann). The most prolific artist on the roster with 46 releases (2005–2023). No separate People/Jim Jupp.md stub yet — see Belbury Poly.
