Cult Records (3)

Independent record label founded and run by Julian Casablancas, based in New York City. Discogs label ID: 552048. Parent entity: Cult Records, LLC (Discogs ID: 749861), a holding structure with no independent release history. 169+ releases documented in Discogs across pressings and editions; approximately 28 unique masters by primary release count.

Bio

Cult Records was established by Casablancas as an outlet for his own work and that of artists he wanted to champion outside the major-label system. The label launched in 2009 with Casablancas’ solo debut Phrazes for the Young, released on Cult Records / RCA — an arrangement that put Casablancas in creative control while using RCA’s distribution infrastructure. This co-release model (Cult Records for creative ownership, RCA or Sony for distribution in major markets) has been a recurring structural pattern throughout the label’s life.

The earliest documented wave of non-Casablancas Cult Records signings came in 2013, when the label released albums and singles by The Virgins (3), Har Mar Superstar, Cerebral Ballzy, Exclamation Pony, Rey Pila, and Reputante, and an EP by Albert Hammond Jr. — a diverse slate leaning toward NYC rock, power pop, and surf-adjacent sounds. Also in 2013, Karen O (of Yeah Yeah Yeahs) joined the roster; her Crush Songs LP followed in 2014.

From 2014 onward the label also handled Casablancas’ primary experimental outlet, Julian Casablancas+The Voidz (later billed as The Voidz (2)), whose records — Tyranny (2014), Virtue (2018), Like All Before You (2024), MęĞż øF rÅm (2025) — are among the label’s most critically engaged releases.

The Strokes came to Cult Records in 2016 with the Future Present Past EP, their first release outside the RCA umbrella. The label’s highest-profile release to date is The New Abnormal (2020), co-released with RCA — produced by Rick Rubin, it became the Strokes’ commercial and critical peak of their later career.

Other notable mid-to-late 2010s signings include The Growlers (2) (City Club, 2016, produced by David Andrew Sitek), Surfbort (Friendship Music, 2018), and a 2015 collaboration single between Jehnny Beth and Julian Casablancas (“Boy/Girl”), co-released with Pop Noire.

The label’s visual identity — particularly through its consistent use of designers Liz Hirsch (28 releases), Warren Fu (25 releases), Tina Ibañez (15 releases), and Sarah Schmitt (17 releases) — is among the most cohesive of any indie operation of its size. The Jean-Michel Basquiat painting used on The New Abnormal is the label’s most prominent cover acquisition (Basquiat’s estate is credited across 14 Discogs pressings of that release).

Management

Ryan Gentles (Discogs ID: 1667816), Jack Rovner (1664296), and Richard Priest (5082131) each appear across 22–23 Cult Records releases in management credits — the core management structure for both The Strokes and The Voidz throughout the label’s catalog.

Catalog Number System

The label used a CLT-prefix catalog number system from its early releases (CLT001-1 through at least CLT028-12). Later releases used Sony/RCA-formatted catalog numbers for co-releases. The 2025 Voidz LP MęĞż øF rÅm reverted to a CLT-prefix format (CULT-500), suggesting a deliberate return or restart of the internal numbering.
