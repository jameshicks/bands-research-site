Orchid Tapes

Discogs ID: 204780 Founded: 2010 Location: Toronto, Ontario (founded) → New York, NY (relocated) Genres: Lo-Fi, Bedroom Pop, Ambient, Shoegaze, Experimental Electronic Status: Active (releases through at least 2021) Founded by: Warren Hildebrand (and formerly Brian Vu)

Independent cassette and digital label founded in Toronto in early 2010 by Warren Hildebrand — who records as Foxes In Fiction — and co-operated for a period by Brian Vu. Orchid Tapes became a definitive home for lo-fi bedroom pop, ambient, and DIY electronic music in the early 2010s, launching or hosting artists including Ricky Eat Acid, Alex G (2), Fog Lake, Elvis Depressedly, Soccer Mommy, Balam Acab, and Katie Dey (2).

Profile

Orchid Tapes was started in Toronto in early 2010 by Warren Hildebrand, who runs it alongside his own recording project Foxes In Fiction. The two ventures launched in tandem: the label’s first release (OCT001) was Foxes In Fiction’s Swung From The Branches, a cassette album of ambient, lo-fi, and shoegaze-inflected home recordings. In those first months Hildebrand released music from a tight cluster of Canadian and US artists who shared the same aesthetic sensibility — hazy bedroom production, emotionally raw songwriting, cassette-first distribution — including Trans-Bedroom Sound, Mickey Mickey Rourke, Teenage Reverb, The Concrete Thoughts of Felix Tengen, and Lazy Dre.

The label expanded rapidly through 2011–2013, picking up artists outside the immediate Toronto circle: Ricky Eat Acid (Sam Ray, Virginia-based ambient-electronic), Happy Trendy, Elvis Depressedly (Mat Cothran), and Evenings (2). The format mix widened from cassette to digital, and Orchid Tapes began making its catalog available on Bandcamp in an archival mode that would define its long-term reach.

The label’s most commercially significant period came in 2014, when it released Alex G (2)‘s DSU (OCT042) — the Philadelphia indie songwriter’s breakout album, pressed on vinyl in a limited edition — alongside the Boring Ecstasy: The Bedroom Pop Of Orchid Tapes compilation (OCT039), a 14-track vinyl survey of the label’s extended roster. That year also saw Ricky Eat Acid’s Three Love Songs (OCT035), a widely praised ambient LP with track titles running to full sentences. The Boring Ecstasy comp captured the label at peak roster depth, with Ricky Eat Acid, Alex G, R.L. Kelly, Home Alone (4), Julia Brown (2), Infinity Crush, Elvis Depressedly, Happy Trendy, Yohuna, Euphoria Again, Meishi Smile, and Foxes In Fiction all contributing exclusive tracks.

By 2016 Hildebrand had relocated to New York (the label began listing “US” as release country), and Orchid Tapes entered a new phase. That year saw the label release Balam Acab’s Child Death (OCT056) — a vinyl LP mastered by Hildebrand and mixed by Sam Haar, Balam Acab’s sole Orchid Tapes outing — alongside Soccer Mommy’s For Young Hearts (OCT059, mastered by Hildebrand), Katie Dey (2)‘s Flood Network (OCT062), and Yohuna’s Patientness (OCT061). The Radiating Light: Orchid Tapes & Friends compilation (OCT060) served as a second label overview, drawing contributions from Soccer Mommy, Katie Dey, Emily Reo, Benoit Pioulard, Owen Pallett & Foxes In Fiction, Alex G, Ricky Eat Acid & Blithe Field, Ghost Orchard, Infinity Crush, Fog Lake & Home Alone (4), and Yohuna.

Activity thinned through the late 2010s but continued into 2019–2021: Fog Lake’s Tragedy Reel (OCT072, 2021), Foxes In Fiction’s Trillium Killer (OCT068, 2019, mastered by Rafael Anton Irisarri), and Yohuna’s Mirroring (OCT066, 2019) were among the later vinyl releases.

Hildebrand mastered many Orchid Tapes releases in-house, as well as the Balam Acab and Soccer Mommy albums. The label maintained a numbered catalog system (OCT001–OCT072+) and pressed consistently limited vinyl and cassette editions.
