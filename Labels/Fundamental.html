Overview

US independent label founded in 1984 by Richard Jordan, who had prior roots in Red Records (2) and Red Music; both became sublabels alongside Delta Recording Co. and Nate Starkman & Son under the Fundamental Music parent. The catalogue is heavily concentrated in the 1980s (85 releases) and spans alternative rock, punk, industrial, and indie rock — with Savage Republic and Eugene Chadbourne among its key artists. The label wound down in the mid-1990s but was relaunched in 2003 when Tim White joined as managing director, adding a small second wave of seven releases. During its first run Fundamental operated a European office at the same Brussels address as Play It Again Sam, pointing to a close distribution relationship with one of the continent’s most influential indie infrastructure companies.
