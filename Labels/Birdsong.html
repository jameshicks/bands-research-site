Overview

Birdsong is an experimental and avant-garde label operating under the Hayabusa Landings umbrella, active from 2007. Its catalogue spans Experimental, New Wave, Avantgarde, Psychedelic Rock, and Art Rock, with releases by both Japanese artists — Souichirou Nakamura, Seiichiro Murayama, and Toshikazu Ohtaka — and internationally recognized acts including The Residents and A Certain Ratio. The label’s output grew substantially across its first two decades, with 17 releases in the 2000s expanding to 46 in the 2010s.
