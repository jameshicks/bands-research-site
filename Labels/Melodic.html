Overview

Melodic is a Manchester-based British independent label founded in 1999 by David Cooper, rooted in indie rock and alternative rock with a catalogue that also spans downtempo and experimental releases. Output peaked through the 2000s (53 releases) and held steady into the 2010s (48 releases) before tapering off in the 2020s, suggesting a label that had a strong two-decade run rather than a single-era burst. The roster mixes UK acts — W. H. Lung, Standard Fare, The Cool Greenhouse — with the Minneapolis band Dark Dark Dark, an unusual transatlantic reach for a Manchester imprint. The label operates a sublabel, Melodic European Labels.
