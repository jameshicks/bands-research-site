Projekt

US ambient/darkwave/ethereal/neoclassical label. Discogs label ID: 2905 (imprint), parent entity “Projekt Records” ID: 1634251. Founded and run by Sam Rosenthal of black tape for a blue girl. Brooklyn, NY (formerly Garden Grove, CA).

Overview

Projekt Records is one of the central US independent labels for ethereal, dark ambient, neoclassical, and darkwave music. It was founded by Sam Rosenthal in 1983 in South Florida as a vehicle for his own solo electronic music and early cassette releases. The label grew through the late 1980s to become the primary home for black tape for a blue girl (Rosenthal’s own band), and by the early 1990s had expanded into a broader roster covering ambient, goth, and neoclassical artists.

Rosenthal coined the term “darkwave” for use on his label’s catalog after encountering it in the German music magazine Zillo in the early 1990s. In 1993 he launched Projekt: Darkwave as a mail-order division stocking Projekt releases alongside similar labels (Tess Records, Hyperium Records, and others).

The label’s catalog number sequence runs from PRO00001 upward, reaching into the 400s by the mid-2020s. As of 2025–26, Projekt remains active with new releases from ongoing roster artists. Total release count on the main Projekt imprint: approximately 1,258 releases (across all pressings/editions) per Discogs.

Genre positioning: Projekt occupies a distinctive niche combining US ambient (Steve Roach lineage), 4AD-influenced ethereal/goth pop (Love Spirals Downwards, lovesliescrushing), Belgian dark ambient (Vidna Obmana / Dirk Serries), and neoclassical/world-inflected atmospheric music (Alio Die, Amelia Cuni). It is not a metal or industrial label, despite darkwave adjacency; the house sound leans toward texture, drone, and melody over aggression.

Projekt: Archive95356Current reissue sublabel; formerly Relic; same catalog numbering system

Relic / Projekt: Archive lineage: Relic was Rosenthal’s dedicated reissue imprint. When a lawsuit threatened the Relic name, he renamed it Projekt: Archive while retaining the numbering system. This label has handled reissues of Vidna Obmana archive material, Projekt catalog titles, and various retrospective compilations (e.g. “Free Electronic & Ambient Sampler” ARC00081, “The Hues Of Infinity” arc00134).

Label History & Evolution

  • 1983: Founded by Sam Rosenthal in South Florida. First release: Projekt Electronic South Florida Vol. 1 (PESF 1, 1983 cassette compilation).
  • Mid-1980s: Rosenthal releases solo electronic music as Projekt Electronic Amerika (Islands 1985, Tanzmusik 1985). black tape for a blue girl emerges as the label’s main act (The Rope 1986).
  • Late 1980s–early 1990s: Label expands beyond Rosenthal’s own projects. Attrition (UK, first US Projekt release 1990), O Yuki Conjugate (1991), Love Spirals Downwards (1992), Alio Die (1993), lovesliescrushing (1994).
  • 1993: Darkwave mail-order division launched; Rosenthal begins using “darkwave” as genre descriptor for his label.
  • Mid-1990s: Steve Roach and Vidna Obmana enter the roster (Well Of Souls 1995), beginning the most commercially successful period. Lycia expands its catalog. Reissue imprint Relic established.
  • Late 1990s–2000s: Voltaire joins (The Devil’s Bris 1998). Label adds international artists (Arcana, Rajna, Amelia Cuni). Robert Fripp appears on collaborative releases. Label address moves to Brooklyn, NY.
  • 2010s: Relic renamed Projekt: Archive following a naming lawsuit. Dirk Serries begins releasing under his own name (post-Vidna Obmana). Erik Wøllo becomes one of the most active roster artists. Label continues primarily digital/CD model with ongoing Bandcamp presence.
  • 2020s: Label remains active; Steve Roach dominates the release schedule. New signings include Theadelaidean, Arin Aksberg, Deepspace (3). Archive reissues of classic catalog continue.

  • Vidna Obmana / Dirk Serries — Belgian dark ambient; continuous presence 1995–2025
  • Lycia — key early-90s darkwave signing
  • Love Spirals Downwards — defining ethereal/dream pop act on the label
  • Alio Die — Italian ambient; frequent collaborative partner (with Vidna Obmana, Amelia Cuni, Lorenzo Montanà)
  • Robert Fripp — appears on A Temple In The Clouds (2000) and Trance Spirits (2002) with Jeffrey Fayman/Momodou Kah/Steve Roach
  • German music magazine Zillo — indirect influence; Rosenthal borrowed “darkwave” from its pages
