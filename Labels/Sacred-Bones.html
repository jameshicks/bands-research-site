Sacred Bones Records

Discogs ID: 98317 Full name: Sacred Bones Records Origin: Brooklyn, New York Active: 2007–present Founded by: Caleb Braaten (Discogs ID 3650706) Parent: All Flowers Group (Discogs ID 3995343) Address: 276 Greenpoint Avenue, Building 8, Floor 3, Suite 8302, Brooklyn NY 11222 Catalog prefix: SBR

Overview

Brooklyn independent label founded in 2007. Began as a lo-fi/post-punk DIY imprint releasing cassettes and 7”s by Blank Dogs, Factums, Nice Face, and Zola Jesus; expanded through the 2010s into a full-service indie covering experimental electronic (Pharmakon, Blanck Mass), darkwave/EBM (Lust For Youth, The Soft Moon), dream-pop/psych (Moon Duo, The Holydrug Couple, Amen Dunes, Föllakzoid), art pop (Jenny Hval, Marissa Nadler, Spellling), film scores (John Carpenter, Mark Korven, Robin Carolan), and retrospective/archival reissues (Mort Garson, Psychic TV, Alan Vega, X Mal Deutschland, Julee Cruise). As of 2026, 2,564 releases across the main label and 9 sublabels.

Team (per Discogs profile): Taylor Brode (general manager); Caleb Braaten (owner/operator); in-house design arm Sacred Bones Design.

Josh Bonati appears as mastering engineer / lacquer cutter across hundreds of releases throughout the label’s catalog — the de facto house mastering engineer.

Catalog History by Era

Founding era — lo-fi, post-punk, noise (2007–2010)

Launched with SBR-001 — The Hunt One Thousand Nights (2007). Early catalog built around the Brooklyn/NYC underground: Blank Dogs (SBR-002, SBR-008) — the label’s first prolific act; Factums (SBR-004, SBR-025); Nice Face (SBR-007); Gary War; His Electro Blue Voice. Mike Sniper credited on sleeve design for multiple early releases (Dead Luke SBR-006, Nice Face SBR-007) — Sniper also co-runs Captured Tracks.

Zola Jesus entered at SBR-009 (Soeur Sewer, 2008) — one of the label’s longest-running artist relationships (97 releases through 2022). Moon Duo debuted with Killing Time EP (SBR-024, 2009), launching a similarly long-running association (100 releases through 2022).

Zola Jesus New Amsterdam (SBR-018, 2009): produced by Jacqueline Castel; Dead Luke on synth; Max Elliott (2) on tom.

Expansion — independent rock, darkwave, psych (2011–2014)

2011 marked a step-change in catalog breadth: The Men (2) (Leave Home, SBR-057; engineered by Ben Greenberg; 4 pressings), Moon Duo Mazes (SBR-050; 7 pressings; mastered by James Plotkin), Crystal Stilts, Cult Of Youth, Religious Knives, Naked On The Vague, Amen Dunes, Föllakzoid.

Pharmakon (Abandon, SBR-099, 2013, 7 pressings) — Margaret Chardiet; recorded/mixed by Sean Ragon; mastered Josh Bonati. Pharmakon becomes one of the label’s signature experimental acts.

David Lynch entered the label with In Your Nature (w/ Zola Jesus, SBR-073, 2012) and solo The Big Dream (SBR-109, 2013, 4 pressings), eventually becoming one of the top-release-count artists on the roster.

Chilean psych group Föllakzoid (SBR-044 onward, 2011–2023), Chilean duo The Holydrug Couple (SBR-045 onward), and Norway’s Lust For Youth (2012–2025) extended the label’s international reach.

Zola Jesus Versions (SBR-102, 2013, 8 pressings) — orchestral reworkings; string arrangements by JG Thirlwell, performed by MIVOS Quartet at the Guggenheim.

Marissa Nadler joined with July (SBR-103, 2014, 9 pressings), establishing a long-running association through 2025.

John Carpenter and film score pivot (2015–2018)

John Carpenter (Lost Themes, SBR-123, 2015, 27 pressings — among the most-pressed on the label) transformed the label’s commercial profile. Self-produced and engineered with his son Cody Carpenter and son-in-law Daniel Davies; design by Jay Shaw. Lost Themes II (SBR-150, 2016, 19 pressings) followed; the Carpenter / Davies / Cody Carpenter trio became the label’s top-count roster entry (270+ releases across solo, splits, and soundtrack work).

Jenny Hval (Apocalypse, Girl, SBR-134, 2015; Blood Bitch, SBR-161, 2016, 11 pressings; The Practice Of Love, SBR-229, 2019, 9 pressings) — major art-pop relationship through 2022.

Blanck Mass (Dumb Flesh, SBR-136, 2015; World Eater, SBR-174, 2017, 12 pressings; Animated Violence Mild, SBR-220, 2019, 12 pressings; Ted K, SBR-286, 2022, 11 pressings) — electronic/noise project of Benjamin John Power (Fuck Buttons).

The Soft Moon (Criminal, SBR-192, 2018, 14 pressings; Exister, SBR-292, 2022, 17 pressings) — darkwave/noise-pop.

Thou (2) (Magus, SBR-205, 2018, 10 pressings) joined for a multi-album run through 2024.

Carpenter soundtrack work expanded: John Carpenter’s Halloween (2017 rework, w/ Atticus Ross / Trent Reznor, SBR-190, 9 pressings); Halloween (2018) (SBR-213, 29 pressings) — the most-pressed individual title in the Carpenter run.

Retrospectives, viral breakthrough, and film scores (2019–2022)

Mort Garson Mother Earth’s Plantasia (SBR-3030, 2019, 36 pressings) — the single most-pressed release in the entire catalog. Remastered by Josh Bonati; electronic engineering original by Eugene Hamblin; liner notes Andy Beta. Originally a 1976 promotional-only Moog record given away with houseplants; the Sacred Bones reissue drove a massive retrospective for Garson. Led to further Garson catalog releases (Didn’t You Hear?, SBR-3031; Music From Patch Cord Productions, SBR-3032; Journey To The Moon And Beyond, SBR-3042).

Молчат Дома — Belarusian post-punk trio (Yegor Shkutko vocals, Roman Komogortsev guitar/synth/drums/production, Pavel Kozlov bass/synth). Back-catalog releases Этажи (SBR-3037, 22 pressings) and С крыш наших домов (SBR-3036, 14 pressings) went viral; Monument (SBR-262, 2020, 16 pressings) — produced entirely in-house by Komogortsev; lacquer Josh Bonati; design Andrey Yakovlev. 80 Discogs releases through 2025.

Spellling (Mazy Fly, SBR-221, 2019; The Turning Wheel, SBR-243, 2021, 15 pressings; Portrait Of My Heart, SBR-347, 2025, 9 pressings).

Alan Vega posthumous releases: Mutator (SBR-271, 2021, 8 pressings); further singles and reissues through 2026 including the solo debut Alan Vega (SBR-3063, 2026, 9 pressings) and Collision Drive (SBR-3062, 2026, 7 pressings) — part of Vega Vault sublabel activity.

Film scores continued: The Lighthouse (SBR-232, 2019, 12 pressings) by Mark Korven; The Northman (SBR-307, 2022, 13 pressings) and Nosferatu (SBR-355, 2024, 8 pressings) by Robin Carolan (and with Sebastian Gainsborough on The Northman).

Halloween Kills (SBR-263, 2021, 23 pressings) and Halloween Ends (SBR-315, 2022, 21 pressings) continued the franchise run.

SQÜRL (Jim Jarmusch + Carter Logan, 44 releases, 2017–2025) — running film soundtrack collaboration.

X Mal Deutschland reissues (2024): Early Singles (1981–1982) (SBR-3050, 10 pressings), Schwarze Welt, Incubus Succubus — major archival goth-rock reissues.

Julee Cruise catalog licensed: The Voice Of Love (SBR-3026, 2018), Floating Into The Night (SBR-3041, 2023).

Thought Gang (David Lynch / Angelo Badalamenti) Thought Gang (SBR-214, 2018, 10 pressings).

Khanate reissues and new album (Khanate, SBR-3043, 2023; To Be Cruel, 2023; Capture & Release, SBR-3045, 2024) — sludge/drone metal reunion.
