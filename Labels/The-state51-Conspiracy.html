The state51 Conspiracy

Independent music company, distributor, and record label based in London (Hereford Street, E2). Founded 1991 by Paul Sanders. Discogs label ID: 313070. Also trades as state51 Music Group following a 2018 corporate restructure.

Overview

The state51 Conspiracy is one of the UK’s longer-running independent music infrastructure businesses, operating simultaneously as a physical and digital distributor, an e-commerce platform (Greedbag), and a record label. Sanders founded it in 1991, and through the 1990s state51 developed early digital retail work including the Virgin Records website “The Raft.” It also co-founded Consolidated Independent (CI), a digital supply chain business that became significant in UK independent distribution.

In 2018 the group was consolidated under a single board as The state51 Music Group, with offices extending beyond London to Vilnius, Budapest, Da Nang, and New York — reflecting an international operational footprint that the roster’s Lithuanian and Eastern European acts partly illustrate.

As a label, state51 runs a diverse catalogue of predominantly UK independent artists spanning post-punk, folk, electronic, and experimental music. The label’s catalog number series uses the prefix CON (e.g. CON147, CON150). It operates a sublabel, Singularity Series, which issues single-sided lathe-cut releases in numbered editions of 50 — used for the Family Fodder Singularity EPs (2011) and later for Vanishing Twin (2023).

The early catalog includes archival and contemporary releases from Chris & Cosey and CTI (their joint project with additional members), as well as reissues of Wire. The label has distributed or released work by artists across generations: Donovan (multiple reissues and new material, 2018–2025), Tom Robinson, Kevin Godley (of 10cc / Godley & Creme), and Malcolm McLaren (Duck Rock reissue, 2023).

The most-released artists on the label by Discogs release count are Dan Michaelson & The Coastguards (21 releases, 2013–2016), Gabrielle Papillon (17 releases, 2017–2021), Donovan (16 releases, 2018–2025), and Family Fodder (15 releases, 2010–2013). Other recurring names include Psapp, Guillemots, Steve Jansen (ex-Japan), and She Makes War (Laura Kidd).

In-house production / design

Designer Louise Mason handled artwork and layout on at least 22 releases — the most prolific in-house creative contributor. Engineer/mastering credits recur across multiple albums for Peter Fletcher (3) (mastered 8 releases), mixer Dan Austin (7), and engineer TJ Allen (7). Producer Mickie Most appears on 13 releases, all archival Donovan material he originally produced.
