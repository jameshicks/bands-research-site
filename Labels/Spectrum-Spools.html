Spectrum Spools

Sublabel of Editions Mego, focused on synth, drone, and ambient music. Run by John Elliott (John Elliott (4) of Emeralds (2)). Discogs sublabel of Editions Mego (ID: 61632).

Known for releasing work by artists adjacent to the American cassette underground and kosmische/synth revival scenes of the early 2010s, including work from the Emeralds circle and related artists.
