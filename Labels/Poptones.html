Poptones

UK independent record label founded by Alan McGee following the closure of Creation Records (c. 2000). Roster included The Hives, The Boxer Rebellion, Pure Reason Revolution, King Biscuit Time (Steve Mason), and others. Pablo (featuring Iain Slater of APB (2)) was also on the label. (source: Wikipedia — Poptones, as of 2025-05-16)
