Abbey Road Studios

Recording and mastering studio at 3 Abbey Road, St John’s Wood, London. Discogs label ID: 217694.

Overview

Abbey Road Studios is one of the most famous recording facilities in the world, opened in 1931 by EMI. The name is synonymous with The Beatles, who recorded most of their catalog there, but the facility has continued as a working studio and mastering house through the present day. The studios passed from EMI to Universal Music Group (2012) and then were acquired by an independent trust arrangement. Abbey Road’s mastering suites have been used by a vast range of artists across all genres.

Notable engineers based at Abbey Road in the modern era include Frank Arkwright (mastering, 2011–present), who handles a large volume of indie, post-rock, and electronic music from the facility.
