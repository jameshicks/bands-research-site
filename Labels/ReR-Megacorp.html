ReR Megacorp

Discogs ID: 5606 Founded: March 1978 (as Rē Records / Recommended Records) Founders: Chris Cutler, Nick Hobbs Origin: Thornton Heath, Surrey, UK Address: 79 Beulah Road, Thornton Heath, Surrey CR7 8JG Total releases: ~666

Founded in March 1978 by Chris Cutler (drummer of Henry Cow) and Nick Hobbs (Henry Cow’s manager) as a non-profit music company. Operated as two parallel structures from the start: Rē Records (Cutler’s own releases) and Recommended Distribution (handling other artists). In 1979 the Recommended label was added for releases by artists other than Cutler.

Timeline:

  • 1978 — Rē Records + Recommended Distribution founded
  • 1979 — Recommended label added for non-Cutler releases
  • 1987 — Rē and Recommended merged into RēR
  • 1989 — Debts from Recommended Distribution forced restructure; became RēR/Recommended, then RēR Megacorp (website + online shopping)
  • 1994 — Turnover: £180,000
  • Recommended Distribution → became These Records (affiliated distribution label)

Mission statement: “Not ever concerned with commercial viability, only with distributing the music we feel close to.”

Published File Under Popular (essays by Cutler, 1984) as a companion imprint. Co-published RēR Quarterly sound-magazine with November Books (1985–1997; see below).

Catalog number system:

  • Re / Rē prefix → Rē Records releases (pre-1988)
  • R.R. / RR prefix → Recommended Records releases
  • ReR prefix → ReR Megacorp releases (1988 onward)

RēR Quarterly (1985–1997)

Sound-magazine co-published with November Books, edited by Chris Cutler. Each issue combined an A4 magazine (42–112 pages) with an LP (later CD) of previously unreleased music — commissioned pieces, live recordings, works by emerging international artists. Distributed by mail order and specialist record shops.

13 issues total:

  • Vol. 1: 4 issues (1985–1986)
  • Vol. 2: 4 issues (1987–1989)
  • Vol. 3: 3 issues (1989–1991)
  • Vol. 4: 2 issues (1994–1997) — LP replaced by CD; magazine retitled unFILEd: The RēR Sourcebook
  • Plus 2 compilation “Selections” CDs (1991)

Notable contributors: Robert Wyatt, This Heat, Thinking Plague, John Oswald, Henry Kaiser, Bill Frisell, Iva Bittová, Cornelius Cardew.
