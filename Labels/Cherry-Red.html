Cherry Red

Discogs ID: 5174 (sublabel of Cherry Red Records Ltd., ID: 269699)
Origin: London, UK
Active: 1978–present

UK independent label. Known for post-punk, indie, and new wave releases in the early 1980s; also maintains a large reissue catalog. Released Second Layer’s sole album World Of Rubber (1981, B red 14) and reissued it on CD (2009, CDM RED 390). Released the Television Personalities compilation Part Time Punks (The Very Best Of Television Personalities) (1999). Also released The Sound’s This Cover Keeps Reality Unreal (1983). The label has 14 sublabels including Cherry Red Archive Series, Morello Records, and Dissonance Productions.

Laibach released Die Liebe (1985) and Nova Akropola (1986) on Cherry Red — their main UK label before signing to Mute Records in 1987.
