Overview

Trio Records was a Japanese record label founded in September 1969 by Trio Electronics, Inc. — the audio manufacturer later known as Trio-Kenwood Corporation and now simply Kenwood — operating until it ceased in 1984. Though primarily associated with jazz (contemporary jazz, free jazz, and post bop), the label also issued bluegrass and kayōkyoku titles. Virtually all of its catalogue falls in the 1970s, with sublabels covering licensed Arhoolie blues and American folklore series alongside dedicated bluegrass collections. Roster figures include Manfred Eicher and Alan Bates, suggesting licensing or distribution relationships with European jazz labels during that era.
