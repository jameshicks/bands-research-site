Overview

Forge Again Records is a Chicago-based DIY label founded and operated by Justin Wexler since 2000, working across punk, hardcore, emo, and indie rock. Its catalogue ran heaviest in the 2000s with 32 releases, quieted to 16 in the 2010s, and has resumed pace with 17 releases in the 2020s. The label operates a sublabel, Cold Slither Tapes, and its roster includes mastering engineer Carl Saff alongside bands such as Fordirelifesake and Hewhocorrupts.
