Temple Records

Discogs ID: 3298 (sublabels include TOPY and T.O.P.Y. - World Network System; distributed/acquired by Voiceprint)

Psychic TV’s own imprint, operating approximately 1984–1994. Released the bulk of their 1980s catalog: live albums, studio records, the Jack series acid house 12”s, and TOPY-affiliated material. Closed around 1994 as Genesis P-Orridge relocated to the United States.
