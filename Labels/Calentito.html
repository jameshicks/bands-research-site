Overview

Calentito is a music distribution and PR company based in Tokyo, operating with a self-described mission to champion music that has “strayed off road” — material that falls outside mainstream channels. Its catalogue of around 38 releases is concentrated in the 2010s and spans Downtempo, Ambient, Experimental, House, and Cumbia, reflecting a deliberately eclectic remit. The label operates a sublabel, Ritmo Calentito, and has worked with a range of artists including Stuart Argabright, Live Skull, Michael Diekmann, and Kenneth Compton.

Notes (web)

Live Skull / Calentito connection

Calentito’s two Live Skull releases in 2013 — the self-titled EP reissue (CLTCD-2030) and Bringing Home the Bait reissue (CLTD-2031) — were co-releases with Desire Records (Paris), founded by Jerome Mestre. The pattern is consistent across Calentito’s early catalogue: Desire Records appears to have originated and packaged these NYC post-punk reissues, with Calentito serving as the Japanese CD edition licensee/distributor. The same Desire–Calentito co-release structure applies to Ike Yard’s Nord (CLTCD-2002, 2011) and the Ike Yard self-titled reissue (CLTCD-2020, 2012).

The scene connection linking these acts is concrete: Stuart Argabright of Ike Yard collaborated directly with Mark C of Live Skull on a project called Outpost (described in a 2010 Diskotopia interview as “our new psych electro dub group formed with Mark C from Live Skull”). Both bands orbit the same NYC no-wave/post-punk world, and the reissue programme appears to have been assembled by Desire Records as a curatorially coherent bloc, with Calentito handling Japanese distribution of those titles.

No named individual at Calentito has been publicly identified. Calentito’s own “about” page (calentitomusic.tumblr.com, as of 2026-10-18) states only that it is “a music distribution + PR company based in Tokyo” with a mission to “pave way for the music strayed off the road, regardless of genre and nationality” — consistent with a distributor that licenses curated Western catalogue rather than signing artists directly.
