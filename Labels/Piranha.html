Overview

Piranha is a Berlin-based world music label founded in 1987, best known by its motto “Swimming among sharks since 1987.” Specialising in African, Klezmer, Romani, and Gypsy Jazz traditions, it built a substantial catalogue across four decades with output peaking in the 1990s and 2000s. The label has been home to artists including The Klezmatics, Stella Chiweshe, Frank London, and Mahmoud Fadl. Originally held by Piranha Musik GmbH, it is now owned by Piranha Arts AG, which also oversees sublabels Heimatklänge and Noir.
