Overview

Meat Records is an independent label from Tacoma, Washington, active primarily in the early 1990s with one further release in the 2010s. Its small catalogue is rooted in the Pacific Northwest underground, spanning indie rock, punk, hardcore, and emo. The roster draws from regional acts such as Fitz of Depression alongside figures with wider US indie connections, most notably Mac McCaughan of Superchunk and Merge Records.

Notes (web)

Mac McCaughan / Wwax connection

Mac McCaughan appears on the Meat Records roster via Wwax, his pre-Superchunk band from Raleigh, NC. Wwax was a trio: McCaughan (guitar/vocals), Brian Walsby (drums/vocals), and Wayne Taylor (bass/vocals). Their recordings were made at Duck Kee Studios, Raleigh, in 1987–88.

Two of those tracks — “Pumpkin” and “Inn Town” — were first issued as a 7” single in 1989 on Leopard Gecko, a Tacoma label run by one Steve Skrzyniarz. That same 7” was reissued in 1991 on Meat Records (also Tacoma) under the title Pumpkin (cat. FLESH76). The reissue is what ties McCaughan to Meat Records: it is a straight repress of the Leopard Gecko original, not a new recording.

The Tacoma thread therefore runs Leopard Gecko → Meat Records, both operating out of the same city. McCaughan himself was in Chapel Hill/Raleigh throughout; the Tacoma releases predate Merge Records and Superchunk’s wider recognition.
