Overview

Materiali Sonori is an Italian label founded in 1977 under the parent company Materiali Sonori SC, specializing in experimental electronics, modern classical, and future jazz. Its catalogue is concentrated in the 1980s and 1990s — 45 and 60 releases respectively — with activity continuing into the 2020s at a lower rate. The label spawned several sublabels including Fuzz Dance, MatSon Studio, and Centro Attivita’ Musicali, and its roster brought together Italian artists such as Arlo Bigazzi, Giampiero Bigazzi, and Lucia Baldini alongside German experimental figures like Christian Burchard and Embryo.
