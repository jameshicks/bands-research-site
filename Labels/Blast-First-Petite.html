Blast First Petite

Discogs ID: 27869 Website: blastfirstpetite.com Parent label: None (independent imprint) Sublabels: Alan Vega 70th Birthday Limited Edition EP Series (Discogs ID: 370520) Operated by: Paul Smith (Discogs: 2366783)

UK independent experimental imprint set up by Paul Smith (52) — the same Paul Smith who ran Blast First, the Mute subsidiary that was home to Pan Sonic, Sonic Youth, Band Of Susans, and others. Blast First Petite operates as a label for titles Smith wants to release but that Mute/EMI declines to put out. It launched around 2004 (first releases PTYT 001–PTYT 003) and continued through at least 2019. The Discogs DB shows 92 releases across the label.

The catalog covers a narrow but distinctive arc: Finnish electronics (Pan Sonic, Mika Vainio), New York punk heritage (Suicide, Alan Vega, Martin Rev), Melbourne post-punk (HTRK), UK underground rock (Factory Floor, Michael Chapman, Dean McPhee), Japanese experimentalists (Nazoranai collaborations with Keiji Haino), and international noise/industrial (Monte Cazazza, Excepter). Reissues sit alongside new releases throughout.

Sublabel: Alan Vega 70th Birthday Limited Edition EP Series

Discogs ID: 370520

A series of seven-inch releases celebrating Alan Vega’s 70th birthday in 2008. Vega was born in 1938 (died 2016). The series collected new versions and covers of Suicide songs by UK and international acts, with radio promo CD compilations tied to each batch of vinyl releases (PTYT/PCD001, PCD002, PCD003, PCD005). Artists across the series included Suicide themselves, Nik Colk Void, The Horrors, Primal Scream, Lydia Lunch, Klaxons, and others. Catalog numbers carry the PTYY prefix (vs. PTYT for standard releases).

The sublabel’s releases are credited to the parent label in the Discogs DB; the series entry (370520) shows no standalone release rows but is documented as a sublabel of BFP.

Songwriters (Suicide catalog)

Alan Vega and Martin Rev are credited as songwriters across 8 releases each — the birthday series and subsequent Suicide-adjacent releases. This reflects the volume of covers and archival material surrounding the 70th birthday series.

Connections

  • Blast First — predecessor label (Mute subsidiary); Paul Smith ran both. BFP was created specifically for releases Mute/EMI declined.
  • Mute — parent of Blast First; institutional context for Smith’s operation
  • Pan Sonic — anchoring artist across BFP’s entire lifespan; 13 releases 2007–2019, every major Pan Sonic album from Katodivaihe onward
  • Alan Vega / Suicide — 70th birthday series (2008) was a defining event for the label’s identity; Vega solo and split releases continue 2009–2010
  • HTRK — second most prominent act by release count (8); Melbourne group’s early discography largely built on BFP
  • Factory Floor — crucial early releases (2010–2011) before the band moved on
  • Stephen O’Malley — recurring design credit; connects to [[Bands/Sunn O)))] (who appears on Che)
  • Denis Blackham — consistent mastering engineer, present from 2007 to 2016
  • Rowland S. Howard — produced HTRK’s Marry Me Tonight (2009)
  • Keiji Haino — two live collaboration releases with Pan Sonic (2009, 2010)
  • Mika Vainio — solo Kilo LP (2013) in addition to Pan Sonic; died April 2017
  • Lindsay Gravina — HTRK producer/mixer; also credited on The Birthday Party sessions via Howard connection

Open Questions

  • The Alan Vega 70th Birthday EP Series sublabel (370520) has no standalone release rows in the Discogs DB — exact vinyl catalog numbers for each individual birthday 7” are embedded in the parent label’s PTYY-prefixed entries rather than in the sublabel
  • No founding year documented in DB; first releases appear 2004 (PTYT 001–003), but label may have been formally registered earlier
  • Paul Smith (Discogs 2366783) has a Wikipedia entry but no Discogs artist profile with discography data — his operational role at BFP is confirmed in the label profile text only
  • Gravitoni (PTYT 045, 2010) — listed on roster but appears to be a 12”/EP rather than a full album; format not confirmed from DB alone
