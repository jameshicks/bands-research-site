Astral Spirits

Independent record label based in Austin, Texas. Discogs label ID: 757266. Parent entity: Astral Spirits Records (ID: 2229175).

Overview

Astral Spirits is an avant-jazz, free improvisation, and experimental music label founded in September 2014 by Nate Cross (Nathan Cross) as a sublabel of Monofonus Press, the Austin-based experimental/punk imprint. When Monofonus Press closed its doors in 2019, Astral Spirits continued independently under the Astral Spirits Records umbrella.

The label operates on a cassette-first model — nearly all releases appear on cassette with concurrent or subsequent LP and digital editions. Catalog numbers use the prefix AS (AS001 onward); the catalog had reached at least AS249 by 2025. Nate Cross is himself a musician active in several Austin-area bands (Shit And Shine, USA/MEXICO, Water Damage (3), Marriage, Expensive Shit, When Dinosaurs Ruled The Earth).

The label’s aesthetic spans free jazz, creative improvisation, post-Albert Ayler fire music, electro-acoustic chamber work, and noise-adjacent free music. It draws heavily from the Chicago and New York free jazz scenes but also releases international artists from Europe (Scandinavia, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Switzerland), Japan, and Canada.

Sister Labels

In late 2019 Astral Spirits launched Astral Editions (ID: 1866664), a sister label focused on composed and more textural work; as of 2022 it is co-curated by Matthew J. Rolin and Jen Powers (Powers/Rolin Duo). In early 2022 the label launched cow: Music (ID: 2687966), run by Gabriel Jermaine Vanlandingham-Dunn.

Notable Release Spotlights

AS001 — Hexane (Icepick (4), 2014): The label’s inaugural release. Personnel: Nate Wooley (trumpet), Chris Corsano (drums), Ingebrigt Håker Flaten (bass). Executive produced by Nate Cross. Sets the template: international-caliber improvisers, concise format, cassette edition.

AS100 — Invitation to a Dream (Susan Alcorn / Joe McPhee / Ken Vandermark, 2019): Pedal steel (Alcorn), soprano sax/trumpet (McPhee), tenor sax/clarinet (Vandermark). All compositions credited to the trio.

AS112 — Zoning (Nick Fraser / Kris Davis / Tony Malaby with Ingrid Laubrock & Lina Allemano, 2019): Five-way international session: drums, piano, saxes, trumpet. Mixed/mastered by Sandro Perri.

AS125 — Good Days (Chicago Underground Quartet, 2020): Rob Mazurek (piccolo trumpet, electronics), Chad Taylor (drums), Jeff Parker (electric guitar), Josh Johnson (organ/piano/synth). Produced by Chris Schlarb.

AS139 — …And Then There’s This (Artifacts (5), 2021): Tomeka Reid (cello), Nicole Mitchell (flute, electronics), Mike Reed (2) (drums). Recorded by Cooper Crain and others.

AS145 — The Ritual And The Dance (Roscoe Mitchell & Mike Reed (2), 2021): Roscoe Mitchell on reeds, Mike Reed on drums/electronics. A meeting of AACM lineage and contemporary Chicago free.

AS166 — Sun Beans Of Shimmering Light (Wadada Leo Smith / Douglas Ewart / Mike Reed (2), 2021): AACM elders (Smith: trumpet; Ewart: woodwinds) with Reed (drums). All compositions by the trio. Cover art by Sonnenzimmer (Nadine Nakanishi / Nick Butcher).

AS223 — Timing Birds (Silvia Bolognesi / Dudu Kouate / Griffin Rodriguez, 2024): Italian bassist Silvia Bolognesi (bass, voice, percussion), Dudu Kouate (percussion, voice, ngoni, kalimba), Griffin Rodriguez (electronics, voice). Recorded/mixed/mastered by Rodriguez.

AS231 — The Separatist Party (Mike Reed (2), 2023): Large-ensemble production with Ben Lamar Gay (cornet/flugelhorn), Rob Frye (tenor sax/flute), Cooper Crain (guitar/synth), Dan Quinlivan (synth), Marvin Tate (vocals). Reed as producer, drummer, and editor.

Recurring Production Credits

David Zuchowski — Mastering engineer on multiple releases (AS139, AS145, AS166). The label’s most recurring mastering figure on Discogs.

Cooper Crain — Recording engineer and performer; appears on …And Then There’s This (AS139) and The Separatist Party (AS231).

Jaime Zuverza — Layout and cover art; credited on Multiring (AS124), Zoning (AS112), and others. The label’s primary visual identity contributor.
