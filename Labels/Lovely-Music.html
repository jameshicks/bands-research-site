Lovely Music, Ltd.

Independent record label. New York City. Discogs ID: 2111 (as “Lovely Music, Ltd.”). Note: Discogs also lists “Lovely Music” (id 113854) as a publisher/copyright entity — the actual record label is id 2111.

Founded June 28, 1977, by Mimi Johnson (Discogs artist ID: 453529; also known from Art Services). Legally renamed to Lovely Communications, Ltd. (Discogs id 102005) in April 1981, though releases continued under the Lovely Music, Ltd. imprint. Address on file: 260 West Broadway, New York, NY 10013 (later 325 Spring Street).

Per Discogs label profile: “dedicated to releasing the best in avant-garde and experimental music, from electronics and computer music to new opera and extended vocal techniques.” One of the longest-lived independent labels in American experimental music.

Sublabel: Vital Records (VR catalog prefix, used 1979–mid-1980s). LML prefix = early pressings; VR = Vital Records sublabel; LCD = later CD releases; LMC/LVHS = video/cassette releases.

Catalog Structure

  • LML prefix (e.g. LML 1001): founding-era pressings, 1977–1978
  • VR prefix (e.g. VR 1002): Vital Records sublabel, 1979–mid-1980s
  • LVHS prefix (e.g. LVHS 1–9): video/cassette releases, notably the Music With Roots In The Æther portrait series
  • LCD prefix (e.g. LCD 1003): CD-era releases
  • LMC prefix: cassette releases

Music With Roots In The Æther (1987)

A key document in the catalog: a series of video portraits of American experimental composers, produced and directed by Robert Ashley. Separate Discogs entries for each subject (LVHS 1–7+):

  • LVHS 1 — David Behrman
  • LVHS 2 — Philip Glass
  • LVHS 3 — Alvin Lucier
  • LVHS 5 — Pauline Oliveros
  • LVHS 6 — Terry Riley
  • LVHS 7 — Robert Ashley
