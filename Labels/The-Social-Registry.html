Overview

The Social Registry is a New York-based independent label founded in 2002, releasing experimental, indie rock, and post-rock material primarily through the 2000s. Its catalogue of around 37 releases is concentrated in that decade, with Gang Gang Dance as its most prominent act and a broader roster spanning psychedelic rock and abstract experimental music. The label also operated a sublabel, The Social Club. Output dropped sharply after 2010, with only a handful of releases in the following decade.
