Overview

Constellation is a Canadian independent record label founded in Montreal in 1997 by Don Wilkie and Ian Ilavsky. It became one of the defining homes of post-rock and experimental music in Canada, anchored by its longstanding relationship with Godspeed You! Black Emperor and associated figures including Efrim Menuck, Thierry Amar, and Radwan Moumneh. The catalogue grew steadily from seven releases in the 1990s to a peak of 76 in the 2010s, spanning post-rock, ambient, folk rock, and experimental styles. Harris Newman, a frequent presence on the roster, reflects the label’s broader investment in avant-garde and genre-crossing work.
