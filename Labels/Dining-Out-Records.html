Dining Out Records

Founded: 1979 Closed: ~1982 (cash flow collapse) Based: London, UK Founder: Dave Henderson Catalog prefix: TUX

A London-based DIY indie label started by Dave Henderson, vocalist of Disco Zombies. Released classic post-punk/new wave singles in the late 1970s and early 1980s, several of which charted on the UK Independent Chart.

The label collapsed due to cash flow problems — distributors didn’t pay upfront, depleting the label financially despite strong sales. Henderson relaunched it briefly in 1981.
