Force Inc. Music Works

Discogs ID: 269
Label code: LC 6001 / LC 06001
Parent: Kennen Ltd
Founded: September 1991
Founder: Achim Szepanski
Location: Frankfurt, Germany
Active: 1991–2004 (original run); revived 2006, relaunched November 2017

Force Inc. Music Works is the primary imprint of the Achim Szepanski-run Frankfurt label collective. Founded in September 1991, it served as the launching pad for early 1990s German rave, acid, and techno, then expanded through the mid-decade into deep house, tech house, and harder forms of electronic club music, before eventually connecting to the more experimental directions explored by its sister label Mille Plateaux. The roster covered an exceptional span — from Alec Empire’s earliest rave 12-inches and Ian Pooley’s Frankfurt house, to the dub-techno minimalism of Porter Ricks, the Chicago house cross-pollination of Gene Farris and DJ Rush, and the turn-of-millennium microhouse of Akufen and Sutekh. The label ceased operations in early 2004 when distributor EFA-Medien collapsed; it was revived in 2006 under Disco Inc. Ltd., and relaunched again in November 2017.

Overview

Origins (1991–1992)

Force Inc. launched in September 1991 with a cluster of Frankfurt rave and techno 12-inches. The first release, Rhyme 2 The Rhythm (FIM 001), credited Spee / Station 99. Immediately following were releases from T’N’I, Age, Space Cube, Skydiver, and Outrage (2) — several of which were aliases of Ian Pooley (born Ian Pinnekamp, 1973) and DJ Tonka. Alec Empire debuted the same year as part of the T’N’I project (FIM 005, Trip Men) and returned as a solo artist with the SuEcide EPs (FIM 023, FIM 029) in 1992. N.R.G., Car & Driver, Biochip C., and Nexus & Blowback all appeared in 1992, establishing the label as a broad hub for the German rave underground.

Mid-period: house crossover and expansion (1993–1997)

From 1993 onward, Force Inc. diversified. Ian Pooley emerged as a core artist with releases under his own name, and the catalog pulled in Chicago connections — Gene Farris (from 1995), DJ Rush (from 1995), and later Akufen, Sutekh, and Tomas Jirku. Mike Ink (a Wolfgang Voigt alias) contributed 16 releases between 1993 and 2019. Biochip C. became one of the label’s most prolific artists (23 releases, 1992–1999). Porter Ricks (Thomas P. Heckmann and Andy Mellwig) issued four key EPs and the Vol 1 / Vol 2 12-inches (1997) — dub-techno works later compiled on their self-titled Mille Plateaux album. Cristian Vogel appeared in 1994–1995 with early EPs. Ian Pooley’s album The Times (FIM-1-019, 1996) and Chord Memory (FIM 107, 1996) became key Force Inc. LPs.

Love Inc. was the label’s most commercially prolific act with 30 releases spanning 1992–2019, including New Jack City (FIM 064, 1994) and Life’s A Gas (FIM-1-021, 1996).

The experimental turn (1998–2003)

The label’s final pre-bankruptcy years saw an influx of artists associated with the wider microhouse and minimal techno movements. Stewart Walker debuted with the Nevermore EP (FIM 173, 1999) and the full-length Stabiles (FIM-1-034, 1999 — shared release with Mille Plateaux). Akufen arrived in 2002 with Deck The House (FIM 223) and the album My Way (FIM-1-060, 2002). Sutekh contributed Miasma EP (FIM 182, 2000) and Periods.Make.Sense. (FIM-1-039, 2000). Donnacha Costello appeared with Growing Up In Public (FIM-1-043, 2000). Yagya contributed Rhythm Of Snow (FIM-1-057, 2002). Baby Ford appeared in 2003 with two full-lengths.

Ekkehard Ehlers’s Auch alias released four 12-inches on Force Inc. in 2000–2001 (International Breakfast EP, Kiss Tomorrow Goodbye, Laptop Ethics, Remix Tomorrow Goodbye), produced while Ehlers was working at the Force Inc. Frankfurt office and curating material for Mille Plateaux.

Collapse and revivals

In early 2004, the German independent distributor EFA-Medien collapsed, taking Force Inc. and the other labels in the constellation with it. In 2006, the Force Inc. label family (Force Inc., Mille Plateaux, and Force Tracks) was taken over by Disco Inc. Ltd. and distributed by Intergroove, Rough Trade, and Discomania. The label relaunched in November 2017. A pair of 2020 releases — the Rauschen 2020.01 compilation and galcid’s Bucket Brigade Device — confirmed the relaunch was active.

The Label Family

Force Inc. Music Works operated as the club/dancefloor arm of a broader label constellation run by Achim Szepanski:

  • Mille Plateaux (ID: 270) — founded 1994 as a sublabel of Force Inc., dedicated to experimental and abstract electronic music (glitch, minimal techno, sound art). Home to Gas, Oval, Cristian Vogel, Alva Noto, Vladislav Delay, and Terre Thaemlitz, among many others. Its Clicks & Cuts series (2000–2002) was a landmark of glitch music. Mille Plateaux’s parent entity is now listed as Edition Mille Plateaux.
  • Ritornell (ID: 761) — sublabel of Mille Plateaux, active 1999–2008, dedicated to abstract sound art and lowercase electronics. Releases from Stephan Mathieu, Taylor Deupree, Fennesz / Keith Rowe ensemble, Dean Roberts, Marvin Ayres, and Stilluppsteypa.
  • Force Tracks (sublabel ID: 220) — distributed more commercial house and techno material.
  • Force Lab (ID: 2270), Force Inc. UK (ID: 3843), Intense Recordings (ID: 11032) — further sub-imprints.
  • Disco Inc. (ID: 38614) — the company entity that took over the label family in 2006.

Key Production Credits

Thomas P. Heckmann was the label’s most prolific behind-the-scenes figure (31 producer credits, 6 mix credits), as well as an artist on Mille Plateaux (Raum, MP 068, 1999). Ian Pooley was a credited producer on 26 releases in addition to his performer credits. Thomas Gerlach produced 14 releases. Distorter handled mixing and recording on 6 releases. SST (8) mastered many releases.

Connections

  • Achim Szepanski — founder; died September 2024 at age 67
  • Mille Plateaux — sister/sublabel; experimental counterpart to Force Inc.’s club focus; shared artists include Cristian Vogel, Porter Ricks, Stewart Walker, Alec Empire, Love Inc., and Donnacha Costello
  • Ritornell — sublabel of Mille Plateaux; microsound / sound art
  • Ian Pooley — cornerstone Frankfurt house artist; 27 primary credits 1994–2020
  • Alec Empire — early Force Inc. artist (LX Empire, Jaguar); later moved to Mille Plateaux for experimental solo work and founded Digital Hardcore Recordings (DHR)
  • Ekkehard Ehlers — worked at the Force Inc. Frankfurt office in the late 1990s, curating Mille Plateaux releases; his Auch alias released four EPs on Force Inc. 2000–2001
  • Hanin Elias — first solo release on Force Inc. (FIM 017, 1992); later co-founded Atari Teenage Riot and Fatal Recordings
  • Porter Ricks — Thomas P. Heckmann and Andy Mellwig; key dub-techno act; 13 releases on Force Inc. 1997–1999; also released self-titled LP on Mille Plateaux (MP CD 42, 1997)
  • Gene Farris — Chicago house artist; 12 releases 1995–1998
  • DJ Rush — Chicago; 13 releases 1995–1999
  • Akufen — Marc Leclair; key microhouse artist; My Way (2002) became an influential release in the genre
  • Sutekh — Josh Eustis; San Francisco microhouse
  • Cem Oral — Jammin’ Unit, Ultrahigh, Dr. Walker aliases all on Force Inc.
  • Sebastian Meissner — associated with Force Inc. / Mille Plateaux Frankfurt network; released on Mille Plateaux and Ritornell as Random_Inc and Autopoieses (with Ekkehard Ehlers)
  • Thomas Köner — released on Mille Plateaux; Porter Ricks co-founder
  • Staubgold — loosely parallel German experimental label; shared aesthetic milieu with Force Inc. / Mille Plateaux Frankfurt scene
