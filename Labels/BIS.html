Overview

BIS is a Swedish classical music label founded in 1973 by Robert von Bahr and based in Åkersberga, operating under parent company BIS Records AB (formerly Grammofon AB BIS). The catalogue centres on Nordic repertoire — Jean Sibelius most prominently — while also championing less-documented composers such as Eduard Tubin; across styles from Baroque to Contemporary, the label’s releases are noted for wide dynamic range that attempts to reproduce concert-hall acoustics. Alongside the main imprint, BIS operates several dedicated sublabels including BIS Northern Lights, a Harald Sæverud series, and BIS-Twins. In September 2023 the company was acquired by Apple Music (2) for an estimated $7.6 million.
