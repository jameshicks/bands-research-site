Overview

Cube Records was a British label launched in May 1972 as the direct successor to Fly Records, both owned by David Platz. Its entire catalogue falls within the 1970s and spans classic rock, pop rock, psychedelic rock, glam, and blues rock. Key figures on the roster included Joe Cocker, Marc Bolan, and Procol Harum, with prominent producers Denny Cordell and Tony Visconti also credited across its releases.
