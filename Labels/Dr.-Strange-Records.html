Overview

Dr. Strange Records is a Southern California punk label that began in 1988 as a mailorder distro before formally launching as a record label in 1989; a retail store followed in 1997. The catalogue runs to over 130 releases spanning punk, hardcore, ska, and pop-punk, with its densest output in the 1990s (67 releases that decade) and a steady if smaller presence through the 2010s and 2020s. Voodoo Glow Skulls and Rhythm Collision are among the most prominent names on the roster. Catalogue number 14 was reserved for a Bolsheviks LP that was recorded but ultimately never released, leaving a deliberate gap in the numbering sequence.
