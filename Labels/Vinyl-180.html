Overview

Vinyl 180 is a UK-based label that launched in 2008, distributed domestically through Proper/Weatherbox and in the United States through Virtual/Rykodisc. Its roster — which includes Dead Can Dance, Brendan Perry, and Thompson Twins members Tom Bailey and Alannah Currie — points toward a specialisation in post-punk, New Wave, and ethereal acts from the 1980s, whether via reissues or new recordings. The catalogue spans roughly 80 releases, with the label most active through the 2010s (63 releases that decade) across styles including Synth-pop, Alternative Rock, and Indie Rock, before activity dropped sharply after 2020.
