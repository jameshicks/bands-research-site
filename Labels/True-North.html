True North

Canadian independent record label. Partnered with Dave Allen’s World Domination Music Group (WDMG) following the end of World Domination Recordings’ Capitol Records distribution deal c. 1996.

Context: True North co-released late-period World Domination catalog including The Elastic Purejoy’s The Clutter Of Pop (1996) and other WDMG-phase releases. The partnership extended the WDR catalog’s life through approximately 1998–1999.
