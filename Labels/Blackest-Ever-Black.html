Overview

Blackest Ever Black was a London-born independent label founded and run by Kiran Sande, active from 2010 to 2019. The label spent part of its run based in Berlin before relocating back to London in 2016. Its catalogue of around 75 releases sits squarely in the 2010s, spanning experimental, industrial, ambient, dark ambient, and techno, with recurring credits for mastering engineers Matt Colton and Noel Summerville and core artists including Raime and Carla dal Forno. It operated four sublabels: A14, Confessions, Id Mud, and Krokodilo Tapes.
