Overview

UMe (stylized UMᵉ) is Universal Music Group’s centralized global catalogue division, operating as a label brand under Universal Music Enterprises since 2001. It serves as an umbrella for several reissue and archival series — including Hip-O Records, Twentieth Century Masters Millennium Collections, and Universal Chronicles — sold through retail and UMG’s worldwide network of websites. The catalogue spans pop rock, classic rock, soul, and alternative rock, reflecting the breadth of UMG’s back-catalogue holdings, and the label has accumulated over 5,700 Discogs credits. Unless otherwise indicated, UMe releases are treated as worldwide.
