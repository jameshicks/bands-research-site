Overview

Atomic Action Records is a Rhode Island hardcore and punk label that launched in 1989 under the name Constant Change before rebranding in 1993. It ran through 2000, then relaunched in 2012, with the revived imprint proving considerably more prolific — 31 releases in the 2010s and 16 in the 2020s against 8 in the original run. The catalogue spans hardcore, punk, noise, power violence, and indie rock, with roster figures including Will Killingsworth and Fucking Invincible.
