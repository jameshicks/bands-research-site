Overview

Blackout! Records is a New York City punk and hardcore label co-founded by Bill Wilson and Jim Gibson in 1988. Its catalogue of roughly 71 releases is concentrated in the 1990s — 45 of them — with the roster anchored by NYHC and punk acts including Sheer Terror, Killing Time, and Kill Your Idols, and extending into metalcore and Oi. The label ran until 2005, then relaunched in 2016 focusing on vinyl reissues; its full digital catalogue is now on streaming platforms.
