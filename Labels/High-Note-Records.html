Overview

High Note Records (映象唱片) is a Taiwanese label operating under High Note Int’l Multi-Media Corp., formerly known as Crossline Records, with a catalogue concentrated in electronic music alongside occasional other genres. The bulk of its releases are licensed from prominent electronic imprints — Renaissance, Hed Kandi, Ministry of Sound, Global Underground, and Nukleuz — giving it a strong house, progressive house, and downtempo footprint. Active since 1999, the label’s output peaked in the 2000s with 263 releases and continued steadily into the 2010s with 236 more, amounting to nearly 500 selected Discogs entries.
