Overview

Lost & Lonesome is a Melbourne, Victoria-based record label operated by Mark Monnone, functioning as an imprint of The Lost And Lonesome Recording Co. Founded in 2000, the label centred on Australian indie pop and jangle pop, with its catalogue shaped by styles running from indie rock and shoegaze through to pop rock. Activity peaked in the 2000s (35 releases) and remained substantial through the 2010s (29 releases) before tapering in the 2020s. The roster is built around Monnone’s immediate circle, most notably The Lucksmiths alongside Marty Donald, Tali White, and Craig Pilkington.
