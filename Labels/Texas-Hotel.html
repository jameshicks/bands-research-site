Overview

Texas Hotel was a California-based independent record label active from the mid-1980s to the mid-1990s, releasing material across alternative rock, folk rock, hard rock, country rock, and acoustic styles. Its catalogue of roughly 30 releases split between the 1980s (17) and 1990s (13), with output weighted toward the earlier decade. The label’s roster centred on Henry Rollins and his immediate collaborators — including Rollins Band and guitarist Chris Haskett — alongside singer-songwriter Vic Chesnutt and mastering engineer John Golden. Its corporate structure and founding context remain unrecorded.
