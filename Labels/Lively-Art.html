Overview

A French sublabel of New Rose Records, Lively Art was founded in 1987 by Louis Thévenon — a co-founder of New Rose — to focus on new wave, electronic, and industrial sounds. The label became the hub of the so-called “Touching Pop” movement between 1989 and 1991, built around four bands: Little Nemo, Asylum Party, Mary Goes Round, and later Babel 17. Its catalogue divides almost evenly across the 1980s and 1990s, reflecting the transition that followed Thévenon’s 1991 departure to establish Single KO.; Lively Art pressed on independently from catalogue number ARTY 35 onward, spanning coldwave, darkwave, and EBM alongside its new wave core.
