Overview

Radiation Reissues is an Italian reissue imprint operating since 2010 as a subsidiary of Radiation Records. Specialising in punk, hardcore, Oi, and new wave, the label has assembled a catalogue of over 330 releases, with output accelerating through the 2020s. Its roster spans British punk acts like The Exploited and Toy Dolls alongside American figures such as Lydia Lunch and J Mascis, indicating a broad scope across the post-punk and hardcore canon. The sublabel Hey Suburbia operates within the same group.
