Fire Records Of London Ltd.

UK registered company — the legal entity behind Fire Records (Discogs ID: 2051). Discogs ID: 672671. Not a separate imprint or sub-label; it appears as the ℗ (phonogram) and © (copyright) credit on Fire Records releases when the sleeve prints the full legal suffix “Fire Records Of London Ltd.” rather than the abbreviated trading name “Fire Records”. The two Discogs entries together represent a single independent label operating from London since the mid-1980s.

Incorporated 9 December 1994 as Agecall Limited; renamed Fire Records Of London Limited on 28 March 1995. UK Companies House number: 03000256. The company’s registered addresses track the label’s operational moves across London over three decades — from Loudoun Road (NW8) in 1995 through various addresses to the current office at 4 Tyssen Street, London E8 2FJ (from 21 September 2023).

Ownership

Owned (as of the Discogs profile) by Clive Solomon (90%, Discogs ID: 1909264) and James Nicholls (10%, Discogs ID: 600128). Previously jointly owned by Clive Solomon and Gerald Solomon until 30 November 2020. James Nicholls took over day-to-day operation of the label around 2002 and has been its director since.

Relationship to Fire Records (ID: 2051)

Fire Records lists Fire Records Of London Ltd. as its parent label in Discogs. The distinction is purely a sleeve-credit convention: when a release prints the full legal company name (e.g. ”℗ Fire Records Of London Ltd.”), Discogs credits that to ID 672671; when the short form “Fire Records” appears, it credits ID 2051. All releases are part of the same catalog.

Sister companies listed in the Discogs profile: Fire Songs (ID: 475856) and Paperhouse Records (ID: 38366).
