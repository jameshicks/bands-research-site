Verve Records

American jazz label. Founded 1956 by Norman Granz in Los Angeles; home to Ella Fitzgerald, Oscar Peterson, Stan Getz, and dozens of other jazz giants. Sold to MGM in 1961; later passed through various ownership (Polygram, then Universal Music Group). Discogs label ID: 5041.

Active as a prestige jazz imprint continuously through to the present under Universal. In the 1990s–2000s released major albums by contemporaries including Steve Turre.

Connection to Horace Silver

Horace Silver recorded for Verve in the later stages of his career, returning to the major label world after his self-run Silveto Records period.
