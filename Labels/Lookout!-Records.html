Lookout! Records

Berkeley, California punk label central to the Bay Area pop-punk scene of the late 1980s and 1990s. Founded by Larry Livermore and David Hayes. Discogs label ID: 33263.

Key early releases include Green Day’s first releases (1,000 Hours EP 1989, 39/Smooth 1990, Slappy 1990, 1,039/Smoothed Out Slappy Hours 1991, Kerplunk! 1991) and the Operation Ivy discography. John Yates designed multiple releases including Green Day’s 1,039/Smoothed Out Slappy Hours and the Operation Ivy CD. Pinhead Gunpowder also released several records on Lookout!. Lawrence Livermore, who co-founded the label, previously played in Lookouts with Green Day drummer Tré Cool.
