Dangerbird Records

Independent American record label. Los Angeles, California. Founded 2004. Discogs label ID: 37148.

Address: 3801 Sunset Boulevard, Los Angeles, CA 90026.

Overview

Dangerbird Records was founded in 2004 by musician Peter Walker (5) (Discogs artist ID: 1429040) and executive Jeff Castelaz (Discogs artist ID: 1675738). The label launched with Peter Walker’s own album Landed (DGB 001) and rapidly built a roster anchored in indie rock, alt-rock, and power-pop. Its flagship act from the beginning was Silversun Pickups, who remained on the label through multiple albums into the 2020s. Over two decades Dangerbird has released approximately 694 releases (Discogs count) spanning over 100 unique masters, ranging from emerging LA bands to high-profile UK acts (Beady Eye) and indie veterans (Grandaddy, Swervedriver, Sebadoh).

Peter Walker also records and performs under the name Arthur King and is a member of Eulogies and Broadheds. His roles on Discogs span A&R (82 releases), guitar, percussion, vocals, and production work across the catalog.

Jeff Castelaz is credited across 93 releases in A&R, management, and licensing roles — effectively the label’s business and A&R backbone.

Founding & Catalog Overview

  • Founded: 2004
  • Location: Los Angeles, CA (3801 Sunset Blvd)
  • Founders: Peter Walker (musician) and Jeff Castelaz (executive)
  • First release: Landed by Peter Walker (5) — DGB 001, 2004; produced mainly by Joe Chiccarelli with Tony Hoffer on select tracks
  • Catalog size: ~694 releases (Discogs); catalog numbers run DGB001 through at least DGB276 (as of 2024)
  • Genres: Indie rock, alternative rock, shoegaze, synth-pop, acoustic, lo-fi, psychedelic rock

Notable Releases

Landed — Peter Walker (5), 2004 (DGB 001)

The label’s inaugural release. Produced by Joe Chiccarelli (most tracks) and Tony Hoffer (tracks 1, 5). Acoustic rock. Established Peter Walker as both an artist and the label’s primary voice.
