Defected

Discogs ID: 98 (label) | Parent company: Defected Records Ltd. (Discogs ID: 272745) Founded: 1 January 1999 Origin: London, UK (first office: 12 D’Arblay Street, Soho; later 23 Curtain Road, EC2A) Founder: Simon Dunmore

British house music label founded by Simon Dunmore, former A&R at Cooltempo and AM:PM. Launched with partial funding from Ministry Of Sound on the day PolyGram formally merged into Universal Music Group — 1 January 1999. Grew into one of the UK’s most durable independent house imprints, absorbing a string of heritage catalog labels in the 2000s–2010s and building a diversified sublabel network.

Founding & Early Years (1999–2002)

Dunmore incorporated the parent entity Defected Records Ltd. in September 1998 (UK company no. 03643252) while still active in the A&M / PolyGram ecosystem. His promotions partner from AM:PM, Janet Bell, co-launched the label with him and ran it until December 2001, when she departed to found Big Love.

The Ministry Of Sound connection was structural at launch: Defected co-issued its early “Sessions” compilation series with MOS. The first two — Sessions Ten (Subliminal Sessions) (2000, cat. DEFSESS1) by Erick Morillo, Jose Nuñez, and Harry “Choo Choo” Romero, and Sessions Eleven (The R-Senal Sessions) (2000, DEFSESS2) by Roger Sanchez — both appeared on Defected rather than the main MOS imprint. Sessions Twelve (The Magic Sessions) (2001, DEFSESS3) followed, credited to Tony Humphries, Louie Vega, and Tedd Patterson; Kings Of Tomorrow Sessions (2001, DEFSESS4) was credited to Sandy Rivera. The series continued with Defected-branded editions through 2002 (DEFSESS5 / DEFSESS6).

Growth & Peak Crossover (2003–2008)

ATFC (whose “In And Out Of My Life” had been a 1999 smash) and the Kings Of Tomorrow project (Sandy Rivera) were cornerstones through the early 2000s. The Defected catalog numbering shifted to the DFTD prefix around this era, signaling a more deliberate brand identity.

Bob Sinclar’s “World, Hold On (Children Of The Sky)” feat. Steve Edwards (2006, DFTD132) became the label’s commercial highwater mark — a global chart hit reaching #1 in several European territories.

Martin Solveig released extensively on Defected from 2003–2007 (45 releases as primary artist). His “Jealousy” (2006, DFTD121) was a significant club hit.

Dennis Ferrer joined the roster in 2004 (“Hit It Off / Jero”, “Church Lady” 2006, “Touched The Sky” 2007, 35 releases total). The Blaze track “Most Precious Love” feat. Barbara Tucker (2006, DFTD125), with Freemasons remixes, was a UK top-20 crossover.

The Defected In The House compilation/mix series launched in parallel, with editions tied to Pacha Ibiza (2005 onward), positioning the label at the centre of the Ibiza circuit. Sandy Rivera mixed the inaugural edition (2005, DEFPACH01CD); subsequent editions featured Simon Dunmore and ATFC.

Catalog Acquisitions

From the late 2000s onward Defected systematically acquired heritage house catalogs, which now constitute a significant portion of its sublabel network:

  • Nu Groove Records (Discogs ID: 608) — NYC deep house label, 1988–1992
  • Fourth Floor Records / 4th Floor Records (ID: 903) — NYC, home of Blaze and NJ house
  • Soulfuric Recordings (ID: 723) — Miami soulful house, run by Marc Pomeroy
  • Slip ‘N’ Slide Records (ID: 602) — UK/EU house
  • Yoruba Records (ID: 2744) — Afro-house / Afrobeat house, Osunlade’s label
  • Strictly Rhythm (former sublabel) — classic NY house

These acquisitions gave Defected ownership over catalogue that included some of the foundational 1990s NYC deep house recordings.

Management & Ownership History

  • 1999: Launched by Simon Dunmore with funding from Ministry Of Sound; Janet Bell co-runs promotions.
  • 2001: Janet Bell departs to found Big Love.
  • 2004–2019: Andy Daniell serves as A&R Manager.
  • 2022 (July 29): Wez Saunders (former MD) acquires the label via management buyout; becomes CEO. Parent entity becomes “Optimum Defected Holdings Limited.”
  • 2024: Ownership transferred to “Firebird Music” group; holding company renamed Firebird Defected Holdings Limited. Firebird Music Holdings LLC becomes ultimate parent.
  • 2023: Allan Nicoll (aka Kid Fonque) appointed Head of A&R.
  • 2024: Andy Daniell returns as Director of Music. 25th anniversary celebrated with special logo by Trevor Jackson.

Ministry Of Sound Connection

The MOS relationship was more than financial. Defected co-issued the “Sessions” series — MOS’s flagship DJ mix compilations — starting from Sessions Ten in 2000. The Sessions series had run on MOS from Sessions One through Nine; from Sessions Ten onward, two or three editions appeared on the Defected imprint before the series migrated back or ended. Lohan Presencer (associated executive) is noted in the stub as connected to both entities, though Discogs does not list him in production credits on specific releases.

25th Anniversary (2024)

The label marked its 25th year in 2024. Trevor Jackson designed a special anniversary logo. Moby joined the roster (19 releases 2024–2025). Inner City, Candi Staton, Todd Edwards, and other legacy names remained active on the label.
