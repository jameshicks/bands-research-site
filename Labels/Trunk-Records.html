Overview

Trunk Records is a London-based independent label founded in 1996 by Jonny Trunk, built around soundtracks, library music, easy listening, experimental recordings, and musique concrète — much of it archival and reissue material. The catalogue grew steadily through the 2000s and expanded most heavily in the 2010s, accounting for 68 of the label’s roughly 123 releases. It operates four sublabels — Bonk Records, Britxotica, Jonny Records, and OST — that divide its territory by mood or format. Recurring roster names include Jon Brooks, Basil Kirchin, and John Cameron, all associated with British library and film music traditions.
