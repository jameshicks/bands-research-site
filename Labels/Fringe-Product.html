Overview

Fringe Product was a Canadian independent label founded in 1981 as an offshoot of Ben Hoffman’s Record Peddler, itself a spinoff of Ready Records in Toronto. Through the 1980s it served as a primary outlet for Canadian and international punk, hardcore, thrash, and speed metal, issuing 77 releases in that decade alone before tapering to 27 in the 1990s. In 1988 the operation was restructured into a separate incorporated entity, Fringe Product Inc.; that same year the label and Record Peddler faced RCMP criminal obscenity charges stemming from Dayglo Abortions releases, a case that ran through the courts until the charges were cleared in 1990. Roster acts ranged from Dead Kennedys and Dirty Rotten Imbeciles to Jello Biafra’s solo work.
