London Records

American record label; the US imprint used by Decca for British artists’ US releases, including The Rolling Stones from 1963 to 1969.

The Stones’ US albums from England’s Newest Hit Makers (1964) through Let It Bleed (1969) were released on London Records. Not to be confused with the later London Records (UK) which was a separate entity. The pre-1971 US Stones catalog is now controlled by ABKCO Records.

London Records (UK) — Discogs ID 1060

Separate entity from the US imprint above. A major UK record label, active from the 1960s, later owned by PolyGram and then Universal Music Operations Ltd. Home to Fine Young Cannibals throughout their career (1985–1996), releasing both studio albums (Fine Young Cannibals 1985, LONLP 16; The Raw & The Cooked 1988/89, 828 069-1) and UK/European singles. FFRR was a London Records sublabel that handled FYC’s 1996 best-of The Finest. The label was relaunched under Universal in the 2020s and issued FYC’s FYC40 anniversary set in 2025.

Notable UK Signings (partial)

  • Fine Young Cannibals — both studio albums (1985–1989)
  • Siobhan Donaghy (ex-Sugababes solo, 2003 singles)
