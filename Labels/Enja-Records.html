Enja Records

Discogs ID: (search needed)

German jazz label, Munich. Long-running independent with a catalog covering mainstream jazz, free jazz, and improvised music. Issued James Emery’s solo albums Standing On A Whale Fishing For Minnows (1997) and Spectral Domains (1998, James Emery Septet). Executive producer on those releases: Matthias Winckelmann.

Connections

  • James Emery — two late-1990s solo albums
  • Cecil McBee — 19 releases as primary credit; leader dates (Live At The Jazz Showcase Vol. 1 & 2, Live At Sweet Basil) and sideman work for Abdullah Ibrahim, Mal Waldron, and others
  • Abdullah Ibrahim — African Space Program (1974), Children Of Africa (1976)
