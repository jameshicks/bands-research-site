Overview

Rockville was a US indie label founded in 1988 under the umbrella of Dutch East India Trading, with a catalogue built almost entirely in the 1990s — 49 of its roughly 51 tracked releases come from that decade. Its styles run from Alternative Rock and Indie Rock to Country Rock, Punk, and Garage Rock. The label is most closely associated with Uncle Tupelo (Jay Farrar, Jeff Tweedy, Mike Heidorn), the pioneering alt-country act whose early recordings appeared here, alongside producer Sean Slade. No Discogs profile exists, leaving the label’s full history and eventual fate undocumented in the database.
