Real World Records

British independent label founded in 1989 by Peter Gabriel. Based at Real World Studios, Mill Lane, Box, Wiltshire, UK. Distributed by Virgin until 2007; subsequently independent and Rykodisc (US).

Discogs ID: 4066 Parent label: Real World Records Ltd. (ID 281512) Sublabels: Eclectic Art Records (829659); Peter Gabriel Full Moon Club (3978807); Real World X (2942420) Total Discogs releases: ~3,117

Context

Founded to support and document the world-music artists associated with the WOMAD festival. Real World Studios became one of the UK’s premier recording facilities. The label is consistently associated with world music, ambient, and cross-cultural collaborations.
