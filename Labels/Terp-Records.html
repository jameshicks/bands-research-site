Terp Records

Discogs ID: 75553 Location: Amsterdam, Netherlands Website: http://www.terprecords.nl/ Release count: 64

Amsterdam-based independent label closely associated with The Ex and Terrie Ex. Releases Dutch free improvisation, African music (particularly Ethiopian), and experimental work. Operates three sublabels: African Series (AS), Improv. Series (IS), and Other Series (OS).

Sublabels

  • African Series (AS) (Discogs ID: 281536) — Ethiopian and African music
  • Improv. Series (IS) (Discogs ID: 796373) — free improvisation duo/small-group albums; Terrie Ex’s primary solo/duo catalog
  • Other Series (OS) (Discogs ID: 1730805) — miscellaneous releases
