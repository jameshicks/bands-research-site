Overview

Фирма Грамзаписи Никитин (also known as Никитин) is a Russian recording company founded in 2000 by Alexey Nikitin. Its catalogue, concentrated in the 2000s, spans pop rock, alternative rock, Europop, synth-pop, and punk — with domestic artists including Вячеслав Бутусов, Андрей “Князь” Князев, Инна Желанная, and Гости Из Будущего alongside licensed international repertoire. From 2008 to 2013 the label served as the Russian licensee for Warner Music International, a relationship that gave it access to Warner’s global catalogue during that period. The company has diversified through several sublabels, including Magic Classics and Жемчужины Классической Музыки for classical music and Город Не Спит for other domestic releases.
