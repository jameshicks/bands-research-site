Overview

Clearview Records is a punk label founded in 1994 in Garland, Texas, operating as a subsidiary of Selfless Records. Its catalogue covers punk, pop punk, hardcore, and power violence, with key roster acts including The Lillingtons, Teenage Bottlerocket, and Kody Templeman. The label was most active in the 1990s with 18 releases that decade, then dropped sharply to five in the 2000s before resuming output in the 2010s and continuing into the 2020s — a lifespan that belies the “1990s–2000s” description attached to it.
