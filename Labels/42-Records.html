Overview

42 Records is an Italian independent label established in 2007, with a catalogue centred on indie rock, indie pop, alternative rock, psychedelic rock, and post rock. After a quiet start with three releases in its first years, it expanded steadily through the 2010s (28 releases) and into the 2020s (27 releases to date), reaching its 100th release in 2025. Its roster has included notable Italian acts such as Colapesce, I Cani, Cosmo, Andrea Suriani, and Giacomo Fiorenza.
