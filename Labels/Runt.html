Overview

Runt is a small label group founded in 1996, based transatlantically between Florence, Italy and San Francisco, operating as an umbrella for sublabels including 4 Men With Beards, DBK Works, Plain Recordings, and Water. Its catalogue clusters almost entirely in the 1990s across indie rock, punk, hardcore, and noise, with artists including Chan Marshall and Lifter Puller among its roster. Distribution has been handled by City Hall Records since 2007.
