Overview

Double Double Whammy is a Brooklyn, New York independent record label co-founded by Dave Benton and Mike Caridi in October 2011 while both were students at SUNY Purchase. After Benton’s departure in autumn 2016, Caridi has continued running the label with Mallory Hawkins, who joined as a collaborator in January 2018. The catalogue spans roughly 75 releases across the 2010s and 2020s, weighted toward indie rock, lo-fi, folk, and indie pop. Prominent roster members include Florist, Emily Sprague, Hovvdy, and Hatchie, with Josh Bonati appearing across multiple credits as a frequent collaborator.
