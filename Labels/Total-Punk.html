Overview

Total Punk is a punk and garage rock label founded in 2011 by Rich Evans in Orlando, Florida, relocating to Portland, Oregon in 2020. Its catalogue runs across punk, garage rock, hardcore, and post-punk, with 44 releases in the 2010s and 28 more from 2020 onward. The label operates three sublabels — Mind Meld Records, Order of the Dirty Plates, and Total Punk Archives — and its roster has drawn in figures from outside Florida including Australian producer Mikey Young and Canadian musician Joe Chamandy.
