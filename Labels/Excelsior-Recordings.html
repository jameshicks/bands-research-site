Overview

Excelsior Recordings is a Dutch indie label and distribution and promotions company founded in 1996 by Ferry Roseboom and Frans Hagenaars, who had launched their first venture, Electrolux, just the year before. The catalogue leans into indie rock, alternative rock, indie pop, and garage rock, with output concentrated in the 1990s and accelerating through the 2000s. Alongside the main imprint the label developed a cluster of sublabels — Concordia Recordings, I Love My Label, Re, and Tender Records — and its roster has included prominent Dutch acts such as Spinvis and Danny Vera.
