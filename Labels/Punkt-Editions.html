Punkt Editions

Norwegian experimental label. Discogs label ID: 2884424. Parent: Jazzland Recordings (ID 28640), which is itself owned by Jazzland Publishing (ID 513698). No sublabels. URL: https://punktfestival.no/punkt-editions

Punkt Editions is the dedicated release platform for the Punkt (2) project and its extended network — described in the Discogs profile as “an archive of two decades of sonic experimentation, as well as a future-focussed label and arthouse.” It operates as a sublabel of Bugge Wesseltoft’s Jazzland Recordings, curated and produced by Jan Bang and Erik Honoré. Releases include solo albums by the two founders, duo collaborations, and albums by other artists in the Punkt orbit.

Punkt Editions is distinct from the earlier Punkt Recordings imprint (ID 72660), which carried only the 2006 debut Crime Scenes and its 2007 German reissue.

Overview

Punkt Editions formally launched in the early 2020s and had 13 releases in the Discogs catalog as of the current data dump. The profile states the label releases “live and studio recordings from the vaults, as well as brand new recordings produced and/or curated by Jan Bang and Erik Honoré, including solo releases by the two Punkt founders, various collaborations, and albums by other artists in the Punkt network.” There will also be “audio-visual installations, visual design and book publications.”
