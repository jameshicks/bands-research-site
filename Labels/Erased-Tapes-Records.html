Erased Tapes Records

UK independent label founded 2007. Discogs — search “Erased Tapes” to confirm label ID. Specialises in post-classical and contemporary composition; associated with the London/northern European ambient/orchestral scene.

Overview

Erased Tapes has released work by Ólafur Arnalds, Nils Frahm, Peter Broderick, and others. Several of these artists cross-reference Icelandic and Reykjavík-scene performers in their recording credits.
