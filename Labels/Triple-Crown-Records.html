Overview

Triple Crown Records is a New York-based rock label founded in 1997 by Fred Feldman, later folded under the Round Hill Records umbrella. It concentrated on emo, indie rock, and pop punk, building a catalogue that grew steadily through the 2000s and peaked in the 2010s with 64 releases that decade. The roster has centred on acts like Brand New, Foxing, and Caspian, with producer Matt Bayles and mastering engineer Alan Douches as recurring contributors to its sound.
