Step-Forward Records

UK punk independent label, London, 1977–1983. Discogs label ID: 14962 (as “Step-Forward Records”). Part of the Faulty Products family of indie labels.

Overview

Step-Forward Records was set up by Miles Copeland (2) alongside Sniffin’ Glue fanzine founders Harry T. Murlowski and Mark Perry. It was one of the defining UK punk independents of the late 1970s, releasing debut singles and albums by Chelsea (2), The Fall, and Sham 69, as well as releases by The Cortinas, The Models, and Lemon Kittens.

Address (1977): 27 Dryden Chambers, 119 Oxford Street, London W.1.
