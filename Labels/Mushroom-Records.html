Mushroom Records

Australian independent record label, founded in Melbourne in 1972 by Michael Gudinski. Discogs label ID: 23489. Parent: Warner Music Australia. Had 14 documented sublabels on Discogs; over 6,000 releases in the Discogs catalogue.

Overview

Mushroom was the dominant Australian independent rock and pop label through the late 1970s and 1980s. Primary home label for Renée Geyer (1980–1999), Midnight Oil, Split Enz, and many other Australian artists. Sublabels include Mushroom 25 (Discogs ID: 522798).

The label released Renée Geyer’s core catalogue including Hot Minutes (1980), I Can Feel The Fire, Say I Love You, and So Lucky (all 1981), Love So Sweet (1982), Faves and Trouble In Paradise (1983), and the retrospective The Best Of Renée Geyer 1973–1998 (1998).

Also released the Jimmy Barnes solo catalogue including For The Working Class Man (1985), on which Geyer contributed backing vocals.
