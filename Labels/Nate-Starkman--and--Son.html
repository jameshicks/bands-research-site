Overview

Nate Starkman & Son was an American independent label operating under the Fundamental umbrella, active from 1986 with an even split of releases across the 1980s and 1990s. Its roughly two-dozen-release catalogue spans indie rock, alternative rock, new wave, goth rock, and folk rock. The roster included notable underground figures such as Ethan James, Bruce Licher, and Shiva Burlesque. The label also operated a Greek sublabel, Nate Starkman & Son Greece, indicating some licensed international activity.
