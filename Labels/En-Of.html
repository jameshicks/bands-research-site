Overview

En/Of is a Bottrop-Boy sublabel founded in 2001, releasing experimental, ambient, and noise works in editions strictly limited to 100 hand-signed, numbered, and dated copies, each paired with an individual signed artwork by a visual artist — positioning each release as much fine-art object as music release. The catalogue runs to 24 entries, overwhelmingly concentrated in the 2000s (22 releases) with only two appearing in the 2010s, suggesting the label is functionally dormant. Its roster mixes Bottrop-Boy and Kompakt figures — Jan Jelinek, Stephan Mathieu, Wolfgang Voigt — with visual artists Liam Gillick and Sarah Morris, reflecting the imprint’s deliberate crossover between sound and contemporary art.
