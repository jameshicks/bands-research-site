Brain

German record label, sublabel of Metronome. Discogs label ID: 9261.

Brain was the krautrock and progressive rock sublabel of German Metronome, active primarily in the early-to-mid 1970s. It issued landmark albums in the krautrock canon, including releases by Neu! and Amon Düül.
