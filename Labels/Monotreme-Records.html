Overview

Monotreme Records is a London-based independent label founded in 2002 by Kim Harrison-Lavoie. Its catalogue is rooted in post-rock, indie rock, experimental, shoegaze, and IDM, with 65daysofstatic and Barzin among its most prominent acts. Output peaked across the 2000s and 2010s — 40 and 36 releases respectively — before tapering sharply in the 2020s. No parent label or subsidiary structure is recorded.
