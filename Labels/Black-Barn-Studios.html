Black Barn Studios

Private recording studio in Surrey, UK. Discogs label/company ID: 302356.

Address: 3 Dunsborough Cottage, Dunsborough Park, Woking, Surrey, GU23 6AL. Website: blackbarnstudio.com

Owned by Paul Weller from around 1999. Charles Rees has been studio manager and chief engineer from the outset. Black Barn is the primary recording facility behind Weller’s entire solo output from Heliocentric (2000) onward, and has operated as a destination studio for numerous artists in and around Weller’s orbit.

Overview

Pre-Weller history (1987–1999)

The Discogs company record (ID 302356) pre-dates Weller’s purchase. Under its earlier iteration, Black Barn Studios hosted recording sessions from 1987 onward for a striking range of artists:

  • Jethro Tull — Crest Of A Knave (1987) was recorded and produced here; the album won the 1989 Grammy for Best Hard Rock/Metal Performance
  • Gary Numan — Metal Rhythm (1988) recorded and mixed at Black Barn
  • The Dogs D’Amour — In The Dynamite Jet Saloon (1988) and related singles
  • Manic Street Preachers — Generation Terrorists (1992) tracked here
  • Ride — Going Blank Again (1992), Today Forever EP (1991), Leave Them All Behind (1992), Twisterella (1992)
  • Procol Harum — The Prodigal Stranger (1991)
  • Chrome Molly — Angst (1988), Thanx For The Angst (1988)
  • Patrick Moraz — Human Interface (1987)
  • Cutting Crew — The Scattering (1989)

By the mid-1990s the studio’s profile was shifting, and by 1999 Weller had acquired it and installed Rees as resident engineer.

The Weller era (1999–present)

From 2000, Black Barn becomes essentially synonymous with Paul Weller’s recording operation. The first major Weller album fully tracked here is Heliocentric (Island, 2000). Every subsequent Weller studio album through Find El Dorado (Parlophone, 2025) has been recorded at Black Barn in whole or in part, with Rees serving as engineer on all of them.

The studio operates as a private facility with no commercial hire advertising, but artists with Weller connections regularly use it. The Discogs record documents sessions from at least the following non-Weller clients post-1999: John Martyn, Ocean Colour Scene, The Rifles, The Moons, Steve Pilgrim, Graham Coxon, P.P. Arnold, Stone Foundation, Declan O’Rourke, Steve Cradock, The Charlatans, Miles Kane, Bruce Foxton, Dot Allison, Carleen Anderson, The Spitfires (7), and others.

Roster Highlights

Paul Weller

Paul Weller (Discogs ID: 14906; born John William Weller Jr, Sheerwater, Surrey) is Black Barn’s owner and dominant presence. His entire solo output since 2000 originates here. The studio is effectively Weller’s creative headquarters, and several members of his working band — Cradock, Pilgrim, Crofts — are frequent presences. Beyond his own recordings, Weller has produced or co-produced records for other artists at Black Barn, including Stone Foundation, P.P. Arnold (executive producer role on The Turning Tide), and The Rifles.

Charles Rees

Charles Rees (Discogs ID: 381623) is studio manager and chief engineer. He is credited on virtually every release recorded at Black Barn in the Weller era, typically as engineer, mixer, or “recorded by.” On Sonik Kicks (2012) he is also credited as drummer/percussionist and co-writer — indicating his role can extend beyond the desk. He also produced several non-Weller sessions at the studio, including Stone Foundation’s Let It Go (2010), The Rifles’ Freedom Run (2011) and None The Wiser (2014), and Bruce Foxton’s Back In The Room (2012).

Steve Cradock

Steve Cradock (Discogs ID: 306224) is guitarist in Weller’s live band and a frequent presence at Black Barn as a session player, co-producer, and occasional engineer. His deepest involvement at the studio is on P.P. Arnold’s The New Adventures Of… (2019), on which he is listed as producer, arranger, engineer, and multi-instrumentalist. He also recorded his own solo albums The Kundalini Target (2008) and Last Days Of The Old World (2011) at Black Barn.

The Moons

The Moons (Discogs ID: 1266546), the power-pop band led by Andy Crofts, have recorded multiple releases at Black Barn. Crofts is also a member of Weller’s touring band, which explains the studio relationship. Releases recorded here include Let It Go (2010), Nightmare Day (2010), Something Soon (2012), Double Vision Love (2012), Mindwaves (2014), Pocket Melodies (2020), and several singles.

The Rifles

The Rifles (Discogs ID: 567301) are the Black Barn band with the most sustained relationship outside Weller himself, recording multiple albums at the studio between 2011 and 2024. Rees served as their producer on Freedom Run (2011) and None The Wiser (2014), with the Weller connection (via Solid Bond productions and Cooking Vinyl) facilitating studio access.

Stone Foundation

Stone Foundation (Discogs ID: 2083407), the Coventry soul/R&B collective, began recording at Black Barn around 2010. Paul Weller produced their Street Rituals (2017) and Everybody, Anyone (2018) at the studio. Members include Neil Jones, Neil Sheasby, Steve Trigg, and others; Trigg is also credited as brass arranger on Weller’s On Sunset and Fat Pop.

P.P. Arnold

P.P. Arnold (Discogs ID: 30212; real name Patricia Ann Cole) made significant use of Black Barn for her comeback recordings. The Turning Tide (2017, Kundalini Music / Universal) was mixed by Rees and executive-produced by Weller, while The New Adventures Of… (2019, Ear Music) was a double LP produced and engineered by Cradock, also mixed at the studio. Former Ikette and Small Faces-era veteran who signed to Immediate Records in the 1960s.

Other Notable Artists

  • John Martyn — On The Cobbles (2004, Independiente) was among the last studio albums from the veteran Scottish folk/jazz guitarist before his death in 2009
  • Ocean Colour Scene — OCS recorded I Just Need Myself (single, 2003) and Painting (2013) at Black Barn; Cradock is a member of both the Weller band and OCS
  • Carleen Anderson — Soul Providence (2005) recorded at Black Barn, assisted by Rees
  • The Charlatans — Different Days (2017) engineered by Rees at Black Barn
  • Miles Kane — First Of My Kind (2012) recorded at the studio
  • Bruce Foxton — Back In The Room (2012, Absolute) produced by Rees
  • Declan O’Rourke — Irish singer-songwriter with an extended relationship at the studio; 11 shared credits with Rees, including Arrivals (EastWest, 2021)
  • Steve Pilgrim — Weller band drummer with solo recordings at Black Barn; Rees engineered Morning Skies (2017) and The Magic Strings Session (2019)
  • Dot Allison — Room 7 1/2 (2009) recorded and mixed at Black Barn by Rees

Open Questions

  • Exact date Weller acquired the studio is not confirmed from Discogs alone (circa 1999 based on credit patterns; first Weller-era Discogs credit is Heliocentric, 2000)
  • Pre-Weller ownership history is not documented in Discogs — the 1987–1999 sessions suggest the facility was commercially active before Weller
  • Whether the studio has ever been available for external hire under Weller’s ownership, or operates strictly for Weller and invited artists, cannot be confirmed from Discogs
  • Some non-Weller credits (e.g. Jethro Tull reissues from 2005–2018 citing Black Barn) may reflect archival credits for recordings made in the studio pre-1999, rather than new sessions
  • TXT (5) credit (2023) — K-pop group The Name Chapter: Freefall cites Black Barn; likely a sample clearance or archival credit rather than an actual session
