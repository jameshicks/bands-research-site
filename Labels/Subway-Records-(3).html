Overview

Subway Records (3) is an independent punk and alternative rock label founded in 1990 in Wuppertal, Germany, run by Michael Schuster and Katrin Disselhorst and distributed through the German branch of Cargo Records. Its catalogue peaked sharply in the 1990s — 37 releases across punk, hardcore, garage rock, and emo — before activity fell off through the 2000s and 2010s, then returned with five releases in the 2020s. The roster is closely tied to the Wuppertal indie scene, with The Notwist and siblings Markus and Micha Acher among its key acts. The label also operates the sublabel Active Records Ac.
