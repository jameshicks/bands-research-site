Extreme

Discogs label ID: 1100

Australian experimental/noise label. 257 releases in DB; has 7 sublabels. Home of the landmark Merzbow release Merzbox (2000) — a 50-disc retrospective box set that consolidated Akita’s career profile internationally. Also released Music For Bondage Performance (1991), Music For Bondage Performance 2 (1996), Collaborative (1988), A Taste Of… (2002 on sister label Mego).

Key in the international distribution of Japanese experimental music to Australia and Europe in the 1990s–2000s.
