No Masters Voice

Melbourne independent label, active 1984–1990. Discogs label ID: 117507. Run by Paul Conroy (2), who was also a member of End Result (3) — one of the label’s earliest acts. The label operated in Melbourne’s hardcore punk and noise rock underground, releasing six known titles across cassette, vinyl, and compilation formats. Its most prominent release is Venom P. Stinger’s debut LP Meet My Friend Venom (1986).

Profile

No Masters Voice was a small Melbourne DIY operation with no parent label and no sublabels. The Discogs profile describes it as “Melbourne label, active in the 80s and early 90s, run by Paul Conroy.” Conroy was a member of End Result (3), a Melbourne hardcore punk band whose cassette album Eeeeee (1984) was the label’s first documented release (catalog #1). The name is a pun on “His Master’s Voice,” the HMV trademark — a gesture consistent with the label’s anarcho-punk and hardcore milieu.

The catalog spans 1984 to 1990: it opens in the Melbourne hardcore underground, passes through the noise rock moment with Venom P. Stinger, and closes with a single release by God Guns And Guts in 1990. No further activity is documented after that date on Discogs.

The Eat Your Head compilation (1984) was co-released with Greville Records (cat. GR 4/5), suggesting an early connection to that Melbourne record shop/label, which was a significant local institution. The compilation collected nine Melbourne hardcore bands across a double 12” at 45 RPM.
