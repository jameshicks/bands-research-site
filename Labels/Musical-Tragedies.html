Overview

German independent label, sublabel of Empty Records. Issued novelty and collectible vinyl shaped singles pairing artists from the 1970s–80s new wave/punk/rockabilly orbit. Catalog of approximately 427 releases. Notable for the 2001 series of 6-inch shaped limited edition split singles.
