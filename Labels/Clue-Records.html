Overview

Clue Records is an independent DIY label, podcast operation, and promoter based in Leeds, UK, run by Scott Lewis alongside Steven Langton and Tom Roche. Founded in 2013, it spent the 2010s building a modest catalogue of nine releases across indie rock, shoegaze, and alternative rock before accelerating to seventeen releases in the 2020s. Its roster spans newer acts such as NARCS and Pit Pony alongside legacy Leeds figures — The Wedding Present and David Gedge both appear among its credits, giving the label an unusual connection to the city’s indie heritage. The label explicitly frames itself as community-minded, focused on promoting bands it believes in rather than operating at arm’s length.
