Overview

C.A.M. Jazz is an Italian jazz label (Label Code: LC 21226) active since 2000, covering contemporary jazz, post bop, free improvisation, Latin jazz, and soundtrack releases. Its catalogue was most prolific through the 2000s (49 releases), continued steadily through the 2010s (35 releases), and extends into the 2020s. The roster has included pianist Enrico Pieranunzi and violist Danilo Rossi, among others. Notably, the label operates a sublabel titled “The Complete Remastered Recordings On Black Saint & Soul Note,” indicating a licensing or reissue relationship with those two historically significant Italian jazz imprints.
