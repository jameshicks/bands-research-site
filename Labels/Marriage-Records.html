Overview

Marriage Records was founded in 2001 by Curtis Knapp and Adrian Orange in Portland, Oregon, releasing a diverse catalogue spanning poetry, improvisational electronics, northwest folk, and lo-fi punk. The label was most prolific in the 2000s, putting out 42 releases in that decade, with activity tapering sharply in the 2010s. Its sub-label Rad was curated by Little Wings (Adrian Orange’s project), and the label was distributed by The Business. In January 2015, both Marriage and Rad were acquired by Gnar Tapes and relocated to Los Angeles. Beginning in 2022, Addendum began reissuing selected titles via Bandcamp; by 2025 Addendum had acquired most remaining stock, moved operations to Olympia, and assumed the role of parent label.
