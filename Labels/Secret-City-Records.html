Overview

Secret City Records is a Montreal-based independent record label and publishing company (Label Code: LC 30745), active since 1998. Its catalogue skews strongly toward indie rock, folk rock, and indie pop, with output growing sharply across the 2010s (42 releases) and continuing into the 2020s (36 releases). The roster centers on artists with deep Montreal ties — Patrick Watson, Basia Bulat, Leif Vollebekk, and Robbie Kuster — accumulating over 340 total credits across 93 selected releases.
