Emanem

Discogs label ID: 38942 Founded: early 1970s, UK Key figure: Martin Davidson (producer, recorded by — Discogs ID: 293824)

British independent label specialising in free improvisation and British avant-garde jazz. Founded and run by Martin Davidson, who was also a recording engineer closely involved with the Incus scene (he is credited on recordings at “Bob Woolford” sessions and directly on Bailey and Parker records). Emanem functions as an archival and contemporary documentation label — releasing both new recordings and historical materials from the 1960s–70s British free improv scene.

273+ releases in the Discogs database. Two sublabels documented.
