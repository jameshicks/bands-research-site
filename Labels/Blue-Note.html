Aug 25, 20261 min read

Blue Note

Discogs ID: TBD
Origin: USA (New York)
Active: 1939–present (classic era: 1939–1967)

American jazz record label. Co-founded by Alfred Lion and Francis Wolff in New York in 1939. The definitive hard bop label: released essential recordings by Horace Silver, Art Blakey, Hank Mobley, Kenny Dorham, Lee Morgan, Freddie Hubbard, Wayne Shorter, Herbie Hancock, and many others. Recordings made almost exclusively by Rudy Van Gelder at his New Jersey studios; covers designed by Reid Miles. Sold to Liberty Records in 1965; dissolved and revived multiple times; currently a subsidiary of Universal Music Group.

Cecil Taylor’s Conquistador! (1968, BST 84260) — featuring Bill Dixon on trumpet alongside Jimmy Lyons (2), Alan Silva, Henry Grimes, and Andrew Cyrille, recorded by Rudy Van Gelder — was released on Blue Note.

Discogs ID: 281 (parent: Capitol Music Group, ID: 126439). 38,582 releases; 92 sublabels.

Late-career releases for Tony Allen on Blue Note include A Tribute To Art Blakey And The Jazz Messengers (2017) and The Source (2017).
