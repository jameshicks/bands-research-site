Overview

Adolescent Records was a San Francisco Bay Area imprint run by V. Vale in the early 1980s, operating as an offshoot of his Search & Destroy punk zine, which later evolved into RE/Search Publications. The catalogue is concentrated entirely in the 1980 decade across seven releases, with a sound running from new wave and darkwave through experimental and industrial minimalism. Roster acts included the industrial duo Factrix alongside Michael Belfer and Eric Paul Fournier.
