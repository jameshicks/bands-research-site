Liberation Music

Independent Australasian record label headquartered in Albert Park, Victoria (9 Dundas Lane, Albert Park, Victoria 3206, Australia). Discogs label ID: 74258. Also known simply as “Liberation.”

Overview

Liberation Music was founded in November 1999 by Michael Gudinski and Warren Costello. It is the recorded-music arm of Michael Gudinski’s Mushroom Group (Discogs label ID: 1031759). The label’s founding followed Gudinski’s sale of his earlier Mushroom Records and subsidiaries — including White Label Records and Flying Nun Records — to Rupert Murdoch’s News Corp.

The label operates across Australia and New Zealand, with offices in Sydney, Melbourne and Auckland. Discogs notes that releases on Liberation before 1999 should be attributed to the earlier Liberation Records page (a separate entity).

Catalog is active from 1999 through at least 2018 in the Discogs DB, with over 300 unique masters documented. Catalog numbers follow patterns: LIBCD / LIBSP / LIBDVD (early era, 1999–c.2007), then LMCD / LMLP / LMSP / LMDVD (later era, c.2007 onward). Singles use the SP suffix; DVDs use DVD; vinyl LPs use LP. Sub-series BLUE and BOOK appear on releases by house-managed artists (Hunters & Collectors back catalog, Mark Seymour, Not Drowning Waving, etc.).

Parent Label and Sublabels

  • Parent: Mushroom Group (Discogs ID: 1031759) — also home to Ivy League Records, I Oh You, Illusive Sounds, Bloodlines, and others
  • 2Q Records (sublabel, Discogs ID: 17214)
  • Liberation Blue (sublabel, Discogs ID: 74910) — reissue/catalog series
  • Liberation Music DVD (sublabel, Discogs ID: 3866804)
  • Liberator Music (sublabel, Discogs ID: 67137) — used for some Nizlopi UK releases
