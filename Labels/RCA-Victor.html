RCA Victor

Major US record label. Discogs ID: 11358.

One of the dominant American major labels of the 20th century, operating as a division of RCA Corporation. Nancy Sinatra & Lee Hazlewood moved to RCA Victor from Reprise Records for their second LP, issued as Did You Ever? (Germany) / Nancy & Lee Again (US/Australia/UK) in 1971–72 (catalog LSP 4645).

Bill Dixon released Intents And Purposes here in 1967 (LSP-3844) — one of the very few avant-garde free jazz records issued on a major US label in that period.

Ann Margret recorded her entire early career on RCA Victor (1961–1967), including her debut LP And Here She Is (1961, prod. Dick Peirce, arr. Marty Paich), Bachelors’ Paradise (1963, prod. Steve Sholes), Beauty And The Beard with Al Hirt (1964), and Songs From The Swinger And Other Swingin’ Songs (1966, arr. John Williams (4) and Quincy Jones). Steve Sholes was also the RCA executive who signed Elvis Presley.
