Posh Boy

Discogs ID: 33510
Website: http://www.poshboy.com
Based: Los Angeles, California

LA independent label founded by Robbie Fields, primarily known for early hardcore punk and new wave. Key roster: Circle Jerks, TSOL, Adolescents, Simpletones. Also released Johanna Went’s Hyena (PBS 139, 1982), demonstrating occasional openness to art-noise outsiders.
