New Alliance Records

Discogs ID: 35723
Parent label: SST Records (ID 9335)
Release count: ~239

SST Records sublabel that released indie rock, noise, and avant-garde recordings. Issued two Blue Humans records: Clear To Higher Time (1992, Discogs 1811501) and To Higher Time (1993, Discogs 1323821), both featuring Rudolph Grey, Alan Licht, and Tom Surgal.
