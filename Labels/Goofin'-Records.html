Overview

Goofin’ Records is Sonic Youth’s in-house label, serving as a vehicle for vinyl and CD reissues of their regular albums alongside exclusive recordings. The name fuses “Goo” — the band’s 1990 major-label debut — with “Geffen,” under which it sits as a sublabel. Catalogue activity runs from 1990 through the 2020s, with the heaviest output in the 2000s (12 releases) and 2010s (6 releases), suggesting continued operation well past Sonic Youth’s 2011 dissolution. The roster is effectively the core band — Thurston Moore, Lee Ranaldo, Kim Gordon, and Steve Shelley — working across indie rock, art rock, and experimental territory.

Notes (web)

Current ownership and post-2011 status

Goofin’ Records is owned and controlled by Sonic Youth collectively as a band entity — it was never a Geffen Records corporate sublabel in the formal sense. The Discogs “sublabel of Geffen” designation reflects that Goofin’ was created during the band’s DGC era (1990–2006) and functioned alongside it, not that Geffen held ownership. The label name itself is a portmanteau of “Goo” (the 1990 DGC debut) and “Geffen,” underscoring its origin as an in-house band imprint running parallel to the major-label deal. (Bandcamp Daily, 2024)

After Sonic Youth disbanded in 2011 (following Thurston Moore and Kim Gordon’s separation), the label continued operating under band management. Steve Shelley (drummer) took on the primary role of managing Goofin’ alongside Sonic Youth Recordings (SYR), digging through and curating the band’s archive of live recordings for release. (Bandcamp Daily — Steve Shelley feature; confirmed by aggregated search references citing Shelley’s management of both labels post-2011)

Shelley works alongside sound engineer Aaron Mullan, who curates the live tape archive alongside him. (Aquarium Drunkard, 2021)

Post-2011 Goofin’ releases confirm ongoing independent operation:

  • Smart Bar Chicago 1985 (2012, GOO-016) — first Goofin’ release after the band’s breakup; self-released on the label without any Geffen/Universal involvement (Wikipedia: Smart Bar Chicago 1985)
  • Bad Moon Rising (2015, GOO-018), EVOL (2015, GOO-019), Sister (2016, GOO-020), Spinhead Sessions 1986 (2016, GOO-021), Confusion Is Sex (2016, GOO-022) — all archival reissues on Goofin’ independently
  • Walls Have Ears (2024, GOO-023) — most recent release; 1985 live recording, released officially “under the band’s auspices” on Goofin’ (Bandcamp Daily, 2024)
  • Live in Brooklyn 2011 (2023) — co-released on Goofin’ and Silver Current Records (Wikipedia: Live in Brooklyn 2011)

Separately, the DGC/Geffen-era studio catalog (Goo through Rather Ripped, nine albums) was reissued in remastered vinyl and HD digital by UMe (Universal Music Enterprises) from 2016 onward — this is the Universal/Geffen corporate catalog arm, entirely distinct from Goofin’. (PR Newswire, 2016)

Conflict note: Discogs lists Goofin’ as a sublabel of Geffen Records. Web sources consistently describe it as the band’s own imprint, not a Geffen corporate entity. The most likely resolution: Discogs uses “sublabel” loosely to reflect co-branding/simultaneous operation during the DGC years, not formal corporate ownership. No web source indicates Geffen ever owned Goofin’ or retained rights over it post-2011. The label appears to have always been band-controlled.
