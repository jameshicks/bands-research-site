Overview

Fan Club is a sublabel of New Rose Records, named after the song by The Damned, established to re-release rare, out-of-print, and historically significant material. Its catalogue is split across the 1980s (73 releases) and 1990s (51 releases), leaning toward punk, garage rock, new wave, and rock & roll. The roster includes artists such as Chris Spedding, Elliott Murphy, and Sylvain Sylvain — figures whose back catalogues fit the reissue mandate. Copyright credits on some releases may reference the related imprint Fan Club Records (8).
