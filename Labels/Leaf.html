Leaf (The Leaf Label)

UK independent record label. Discogs label ID: 202. Officially: The Leaf Label. Parent legal entity: The Leaf Label Ltd. (Discogs ID: 275854, registered as The Leaf Label Limited, UK Company No. 04402147, incorporated 22 March 2002). Label code: LC 12877.

  • Founded: 1995
  • Founder: Tony Morley (3) (real name: Anthony David Morley; Discogs ID: 1167051)
  • Based: Leeds / UK
  • Active: 1995 – present (releases documented to 2026)
  • Discogs catalog: 976+ releases (unique masters basis)

Overview

Leaf was founded in 1995 by Tony Morley out of Leeds, initially with a UK drum and bass / breakbeat orientation (the first release, Boymerang’s Boymerang EP, cat DOCK 1, 1995, signals a jungle/drum-and-bass starting point). Through the late 1990s the label mutated steadily toward ambient, IDM, and experimental electronic music. By the early 2000s it had established itself as one of the UK’s most distinctive independent electronic imprints, with a catalog that ranged from Mexican modern-classical electronics (Murcof) to Canadian psych-noise-pop (Manitoba / Caribou), Japanese percussion ensembles (Asa-Chang & Junray), French minimalism (Colleen), and Danish post-rock choral music (Efterklang).

The label built a reputation for quality vinyl presentation: in-house and recurring designers (Split (11), Oli Bentley, Ruben Tamayo, Non-Format) gave the catalog a coherent visual identity. Its catalog numbering uses two main series: DOCK (12”/EP/single) and BAY (album-format, CD and vinyl). Sub-series include REEL (the Invisible Soundtracks compilation run), PRAGUE, KLANG, HAWK, CARIBOU, and ONO.

From the mid-2010s onward the label evolved again — The Comet Is Coming’s jazz/electronic fusion (debut EP Prophecy, 2015, DOCK 61), the krautrock-adjacent noise of Snapped Ankles (from 2017), the anonymous industrial/ambient synthwave of Craven Faults (from 2019), and jazz-adjacent artists like Sarathy Korwar, Melt Yourself Down, and Szun Waves extended the palette into jazz-punk, Indian classical, and post-punk territory.

Sublabels:

  • BIAS (Discogs ID: 82460)
  • Invisible Soundtracks (Discogs ID: 340501) — the series of ambient/electronic compilation compilations (REEL 1–5 + CD editions)
  • Leaf 20 (Discogs ID: 958950) — 20th anniversary sublabel (2015, BAY 100XX, a 5xLP box compilation)

Notable Individual Releases

Pole v Four Tet EP / Four Tet v Pole EP (2000, DOCK 20)

Split 12” pairing German minimal dub pioneer Stefan Betke (Pole, ID: 611) with then-emerging UK electronic producer Four Tet (Kieran Hebden, ID: 3543). Side A: Four Tet’s remix of Pole’s “Heim” + Four Tet’s own track “Cload.” Side B: Pole’s remix of “Cload” + Pole’s original “Heim.” Design: Ekhornforss. Reissued 2015 as DOCK 20E (digital). Genre: Downtempo, Dub, IDM.

Start Breaking My Heart — Manitoba (2001, BAY 16CD)

Debut Manitoba LP. Dan Snaith, written and produced by himself. Styles: Breaks, IDM, Leftfield. The record that established the Manitoba / Caribou presence on Leaf, a relationship that lasted into the 2020s (Caribou Vibration Ensemble releases).

Martes — Murcof (2002, BAY 23CD)

Fernando Corona’s debut LP; all tracks begin with “M.” IDM / Modern Classical. Art direction: Ruben Tamayo; photography: Karina Villalobos. Launched a decade-long Murcof/Leaf relationship spanning five studio albums (Martes, Ulysses, Utopía, Remembranza, Cosmos, The Versailles Sessions, The Alias Sessions).

Everyone Alive Wants Answers — Colleen (2003, BAY 31CDP)

Debut LP by Cécile Schott (Colleen). Experimental / Leftfield. Mastered by Emiliano Flores. One of the formative documents of early-2000s European miniaturist electronics.

Tripper — Efterklang (2004, BAY 40CD)

Debut LP by Danish post-rock/choral ensemble Efterklang. Leftfield, Minimal, Post Rock. Established a sustained Efterklang/Leaf relationship through Parades (2007) and Caravan (2008).

The Milk Of Human Kindness — Caribou (2005, BAY 42CDP)

First Caribou LP (Dan Snaith, previously Manitoba). Krautrock, Indie Rock, Leftfield. Written and produced by Dan Snaith.

Cosmos — Murcof (2007, BAY 59V)

Triple 12” vinyl album at 45 RPM. Dark Ambient, Drone, Modern Classical. One of the most ambitious vinyl productions on the label.

Melt Yourself Down — Melt Yourself Down (2013, BAY 85V)

Debut by UK jazz-punk collective. Personnel: Pete Wareham (sax), Shabaka Hutchings (tenor sax), Ruth Goller (bass), Tom Skinner (drums), Kushal Gaya (vocals), Satin Singh (percussion), Leafcutter John (electronics, producer). African, No Wave, Post-Punk, Freestyle.

Prophecy / Channel The Spirits — The Comet Is Coming (2015, 2016)

Debut EP (DOCK 61) and debut LP (BAY 104CDP) by The Comet Is Coming: King Shabaka (Shabaka Hutchings), Danalogue (Dan Leavers), Betamax (9) (Max Hallett). Channel The Spirits was Mercury Prize nominated (2016). Psychedelic Rock / Jazz / Electronic.

Erratics & Unconformities — Craven Faults (2019, BAY 120V)

Debut LP by anonymous UK artist Craven Faults. Long-form analog synthesizer tracks titled after Northern English industrial geography. Ambient, Experimental. Mastered by Bo Kondren. Became one of the label’s most critically-regarded 2010s signings.
