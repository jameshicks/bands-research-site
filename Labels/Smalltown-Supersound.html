Smalltown Supersound

Independent label. Oslo, Norway. Discogs label ID: 975. Founded and run by Joakim Haugland (aka Smalltown). One of Norway’s most internationally significant independent labels, spanning electronic music, free jazz, experimental, and pop across three decades. Mailing address: Box 2069 Grünerløkka, N-0505 Oslo.

Overview

Smalltown Supersound emerged in the mid-1990s as a platform for Oslo-area electronic and noise acts, gradually building one of Norway’s most credible independent rosters. By the 2000s it had become the primary home for the Norwegian cosmic disco scene — Lindstrøm, Prins Thomas, Bjørn Torske — and an internationally recognized outlet for jazz and experimental music through its sublabel Smalltown Superjazzz. In the 2010s the label expanded into UK and international pop with Neneh Cherry and Kelly Lee Owens, while maintaining roots in noise, ambient, and improvised music.

Kim Hiorthøy is the defining visual presence across the catalog, credited with 163 unique design roles on the label — the covers are his for most of its landmark releases.

The label is distributed through various partners by territory; international distribution has historically included ties to Ninja Tune (who co-released several Jaga Jazzist albums) and Feedelity (Lindstrøm’s personal imprint). The label has not listed a parent label on Discogs, operating as a fully independent operation.

Founder

Joakim Haugland (born 1975, Norway) founded Smalltown Supersound and runs it alongside a day job as promotion manager for Voices Music & Entertainment AS in Oslo. He also works as a producer, DJ, and concert promoter. On Discogs he appears with 42 executive-producer and co-producer credits across the catalog, and 20 A&R credits — making him the single most consistently credited non-performer across the label. He also runs Smalltown Superjazzz, Smalltown Supernoise, and Smalltown Superbooks (book publishing, not indexed on Discogs). Discogs artist ID: 446153.

Smalltown Superjazzz is the most active and historically important sublabel, with releases from The Thing (2), Lasse Marhaug, Paal Nilssen-Love, Ken Vandermark, Diskaholics Anonymous Trio, Original Silence, Peter Brötzmann, and Soft Machine (archival 1971 live). Catalog prefix: STSJ.

Catalog Overview

The main label catalog runs from cat no. STS13CD (1995) into STS453 (2025), with approximately 140+ unique masters on the main label and a further 15 on Smalltown Superjazzz. Total Discogs release entries across all pressings: ~750.

Early catalog — 1995–2003

The earliest releases are Norway-only electronic and experimental singles. Key early acts:

  • Swim (2) — Swimsical (1995, STS13CD) — first catalogued release
  • Stuntbike — Stuntbike E.P. (1997); Gone (1998) — industrial / noise rock
  • Loophole (3) — Super. I Love You. (1997)
  • Kim Hiorthøy — Hei (2000, STS 046) — first Kim release on the label
  • Jazkamer — joint live release with Merzbow (2001) on the main label; solo output on Smalltown Supernoise
  • Lasse Marhaug — Nothing But Sound From Now On (2001)

Jaga Jazzist era — 2002–2005

Jaga Jazzist released their earliest albums on Smalltown Supersound before transitioning to Ninja Tune for wider distribution. Key Smalltown-originated titles:

The Stix and What We Must appear in the DB with Ninja Tune catalog numbers, indicating they were co-releases or licensed out. Kim Hiorthøy designed all the sleeves. The band’s extended lineup — led by Lars Horntveth with Martin Horntveth, Mathias Eick, Andreas Mjøs, Even Ormestad, Harald Frøland, Lars Wabø, and Line Horntveth — are among the most heavily credited performers in the label’s history.

Cosmic / space disco era — 2006–2010

This period defined the label’s international profile. The Norwegian cosmic disco/nu-disco sound coalesced around three core artists:

Lindstrøm (Hans-Peter Lindstrøm, Discogs ID: 152312) — the label’s most prolific artist with 78 primary release credits. Key titles:

Lindstrøm has also released collaborative projects on the label: Christabelle / Lindstrøm (2009–2010); Runddans (2015) with Emil Nikolaisen and Todd Rundgren; and the Lindstrøm & Prins Thomas III LP (2020). His aliases on Discogs include Six Cups Of Rebel, L Storm, Randaberg Ego Ensemble, and others. He also operates Feedelity, his own imprint closely associated with STS.

Prins Thomas (Thomas Moen Hermansen, Discogs ID: 111067) — 47 primary release credits. Oslo DJ and producer. Key titles: Principe Del Norte (2016), Principe Del Norte Remixed (2016), Ambitions (2019), Ambitions Remixes I & II (2019). Collaborates often with Bjørn Torske (Arpa, Square One, Bjørn Torske / Prins Thomas series) and with Bugge Wesseltoft (Bugge Wesseltoft & Prins Thomas, 2018). The 25th anniversary compilation Smalltown Supersound 25: The Movement Of The Free Spirit (2018) was compiled and mixed by Prins Thomas. Realname Thomas Moen Hermansen.

Bjørn Torske (Discogs ID: 679) — 31 primary release credits. Bergen/Oslo-based. Key titles: Feil Knapp (2007), Kokning (2010), Nedi Myra (2015), Trøbbel (2015, archival), Byen (2018). Member of multiple groups including Ismistik, Anon (2), Krater, Open Skies.

diskJokke (Joachim Dyrdal) — Staying In (2007), En Fin Tid (2010), 1987 (2010). Oslo-based nu-disco. Discogs artist ID: 278309.

Mungolian Jet Set — duo of Knut Sævik and Pål Nyhus. We Gave It All Away (2009), Moon Jocks N Prog Rocks (2010), Schlungs (2011), Mungodelics (2012), A City So Convenient (2016). Discogs artist ID: 511117.

120 Days — 120 Days (2006), Come Out, Come Down, Fade Out, Be Gone (2006), Sleepwalking (2007). Norwegian rock/electronic.

The Cherry Thing and free jazz crossover — 2012

The Thing (2) (via Smalltown Superjazzz since 2004) crossed over to the main label with The Cherry Thing (2012, STS229) — a landmark free jazz / pop collision with Neneh Cherry. The album included covers of songs by Suicide, Patti Smith, MF Doom, and Neneh Cherry, and brought the label significant UK and European press attention. The Cherry Thing Remixes followed the same year.

Todd Terje released It’s The Arps EP (2012) and remixed Lindstrøm. Discogs artist ID: 132082.

Neneh Cherry solo era — 2014–2020

Neneh Cherry became one of the label’s most important artists across two full solo albums:

  • Blank Project (2014, STS248) — produced by Kieran Hebden (Four Tet); 10 pressings; UK/European release. Stark, minimalist album.
  • Broken Politics (2018, STS343CD) — produced by Kieran Hebden; 6 pressings.

Cherry also contributed singles Everything (2014), Out Of The Black (2014, with Robyn), Spit Three Times (2014), Kong (2018). Discogs ID: 11449.

Kieran Hebden (Four Tet) is the single most-credited outside producer on the label — 16 production credits across the catalog — centered on the Cherry records. Discogs ID: 60342.

Kelly Lee Owens era — 2017–2022

Deathprod / reissues — 2017–2024

Deathprod (Helge Sten, Discogs ID: 48347) received major reissue/archival treatment: Treetop Drive (2017, STS279LP), Morals And Dogma (2017, STS280LP), Imaginary Songs From Tristan Da Cunha (2017, STS278LP) — archival works reissued on LP. New album Occulting Disk (2019) and Sow Your Gold In The White Foliated Earth (2022, STS402LP) — a composition for Ensemble Musikfabrik using Harry Partch instruments. Compositions anthology (2023). He is also credited as Helge Sten and is the label’s most-used mastering/mixing engineer after commercial engineers.

Other key artists

Carmen Villain (Carmen Hillestad, Discogs ID: 2621684) — Sleeper (2013), Infinite Avenue (2017), Both Lines Will Be Blue (2019), Only Love From Now On (2022), Music From The Living Monument (2023). Ambient / drone / electronic.

Bendik Giske (saxophone / electronics, Discogs ID: 876612) — Surrender (2019), Cracks (2021), Adjust EP (2018). Norwegian saxophonist working in minimalist electronic territory.

Biosphere (Geir Jenssen) — Departed Glories (2016, STS281LP, 8 pressings). Discogs ID: 3956.

Dungen — Allas Sak (2015), Häxan (2016), Häxan (Versions By Prins Thomas) (2017). Swedish psych rock act on the label alongside Norwegian electronic artists.

Key Creative Personnel

Visual / Design

Kim Hiorthøy — 163 design credits across the catalog. Designed sleeves for virtually every significant Smalltown Supersound release. Sole credited designer for the label’s early catalog; continues to design extensively through 2023. His visual identity is inseparable from the label’s physical presence. See his page for full scope. Discogs ID: 19822.

Secondary designers include Rune Mortensen (24 credits), Blank Blank (23 credits), Jacob Grønbech Jensen (19 credits), and photographer Wolfgang Tillmans (6 credits on Cherry-era releases).

Engineering / Mastering

Chris Sansom — 50 engineer/mastering credits; the most-used mastering engineer. Also credited Mixed By on select releases.

Helge Sten (Deathprod) — 32 credits as engineer / mastered by. The label’s de facto in-house mastering/mixing voice for the noise/experimental wing.

Audun Strype — 30 mastering/mixing credits.

Rashad Becker — 18 mastering credits (Berlin-based; handled much of the electronic output).

Mandy Parnell — 14 mastering credits.

Ingar Hunskaar — 20 mastering credits.

Jørgen Træen — 15 credits; also a producer on several releases.

Production

Joakim Haugland — 42 production credits (executive producer / co-producer across the catalog). The backbone of the label’s production administrative infrastructure.

Kieran Hebden (Four Tet, Discogs ID: 60342) — 16 production credits; entirely centered on the Neneh Cherry albums.

Cameron McVey — 10 production credits; credited on Neneh Cherry records.

Jørgen Træen — 14 production credits across various electronic acts.

Open Questions

  • Exact founding year: the earliest catalogued release is 1995 (Swimsical, STS13CD), but the catalog number suggests earlier releases (STS1–12) may predate Discogs coverage or were unreleased.
  • Loophole (3) catalog number STS020 in 1997 and Swim (2) at STS13 in 1995 suggest the label was operational from at least 1995.
  • Distribution deals: specific distribution agreements with territory partners (e.g. ties to Rough Trade distribution, Ninja Tune’s distribution arm) are not documented in Discogs.
  • Smalltown Superbooks: the book publishing arm is not covered in Discogs.
  • Smalltown Supermusic (label ID 278046): no releases indexed — may be a dormant or administrative entity.
