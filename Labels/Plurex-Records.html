Plurex Records

Amsterdam-based independent label, active 1977–1983. Founded by Wally van Middendorp (also of Minny Pops) and Sam Tjioe. Central to the Dutch post-punk scene and the broader “Ultra” underground arts movement.

Overview

Plurex began as a DIY outlet for van Middendorp and Tjioe’s own music and expanded to release other like-minded acts. The label’s singles catalog (cat nos 0001–0019, 1977–1980) documents the early Dutch post-punk underground. Notable acts include Minny Pops, Nasmak, The Musical Janeens, The Twinkeyz, and The Mumbles.

In 1983 Plurex licensed David Thomas And The Pedestrians’ Variations On A Theme for Benelux distribution (cat. PLUREX 3301), alongside simultaneous regional releases on Rough Trade (UK/Germany) and Sixth International (US). This appears to be one of the label’s last releases before it ceased operations in 1983.

A 2024 retrospective compilation From Punk To Ultra: The Plurex Story was released on Sundazed/Modern Harmonic.
