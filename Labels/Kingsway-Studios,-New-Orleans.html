Kingsway Studios, New Orleans

Recording studio founded by Daniel Lanois in New Orleans, Louisiana. Referenced in Discogs as the recording location for several of Lanois’s most significant productions in the late 1980s and 1990s.

Overview

Lanois established Kingsway in the late 1980s; it became the recording site for Oh Mercy (Bob Dylan, 1989), Wrecking Ball (Emmylou Harris, 1995), Time Out Of Mind (Dylan, 1997), and portions of Achtung Baby (U2). The studio’s ambient-acoustic character — a nineteenth-century mansion — shaped the textural sound associated with these records.
