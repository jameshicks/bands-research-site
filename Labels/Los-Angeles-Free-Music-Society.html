Los Angeles Free Music Society

Discogs ID: 30059 Also known as: LAFMS

Cooperative experimental music collective and label based in Pasadena, California, active from the mid-1970s onward. Smegma were early participants; Glamour Girl 1941 (1979) was released on LAFMS (cat. LAFMS-008). Tom Recchion and the Doo-Dooettes were also part of the network.

Included on the Nurse With Wound list (1979).
