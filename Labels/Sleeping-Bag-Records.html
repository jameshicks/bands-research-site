Sleeping Bag Records

Independent label. New York City. Active 1981–1992. Label ID: 3379. Label code: LC 7241.

Co-founded by Arthur Russell, William Socolov, and Juggy Gales in 1981.

Overview

Eclectic NYC dance and hip-hop label. The first release was Dinosaur L’s 24→24 Music (1981) — produced by Russell, with Peter Gordon (tenor sax), Julius Eastman (keyboards, vocals), Peter Zummo (trombone), and Ed Tomney (guitar) in the ensemble. The label then expanded into freestyle, funk, and hip-hop: Kurtis Mantronik’s Mantronix, Joyce Sims, Nocera, EPMD, Cash Money & Marvelous, and The Todd Terry Project were all on the roster.

Kurtis Mantronik served as A&R executive and recorded for the label as Mantronix beginning in 1985.

Sub-label: Fresh Records (ID 11356, founded 1984) — dance and hip-hop releases under the same ownership.

Closure and Aftermath

Closed 1992. Catalog sold to Warlock Records in 1996. Now controlled by Phase One Network (ID 963064).
