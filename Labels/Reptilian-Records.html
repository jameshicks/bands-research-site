From Discogs

Reptilian Records was founded by Chris X. in 1989 as an underground music shop located at 403 South Broadway in Baltimore, MD, USA. In 1993, Reptilian began releasing records from local bands at first, and eventually unheard and established bands nationwide.

Reptilian has released a diverse catalog of punk rock, metal, noise rock, hardcore, grind, indie rock, and random strangeness. Several sub-labels and partnerships developed over the years, such as Adversary Recordings, Complete Distort, and Empirical Recordings, as well as the publishing house Scapegoat Publishing.

The Reptilian Records store in Baltimore was shuttered in 2009 but the label is currently active, now based in Texas.

— Discogs label profile, ID 33828

Overview

Reptilian Records grew out of an underground record shop opened by Chris X. in Baltimore in 1989; the label side launched in 1993, initially pressing releases by local acts before expanding to bands across the US. Its catalogue spans punk, noise rock, hardcore, grind, and metal — 121 releases across four decades, with output peaking in the 2000s and remaining steady into the 2020s. Over the years the operation spun off three sub-labels (Adversary Recordings, Complete Distort, and Empirical Recordings) and a publishing arm, Scapegoat Publishing. The Baltimore storefront closed in 2009 and the label relocated to Texas, where it continues to operate.
