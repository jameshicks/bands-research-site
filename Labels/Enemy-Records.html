Enemy Records

German-registered independent label (Label Code: LC 8144), Munich-based, active approximately 1986–1996. The primary vehicle for the Last Exit catalog and the solo output of Sonny Sharrock and Bill Laswell in the late 1980s, Enemy Records expanded from a free-jazz/avant-garde imprint into a broader roster covering noise rock, downtown New York experimentalism, funk, and alternative rock. Its US address (Enemy Productions Inc., 234 6th Avenue, Brooklyn, NY) served the American market alongside the Munich headquarters. Parent entity: Enemy GmbH (Discogs ID: 259576). Sublabel: Brake Out Records (Discogs ID: 18432), a German rock imprint with its own OUT-prefixed catalog. Related company: Discogs lists a third connected entity (label ID 609600, unlisted by name in the profile). Label administrator across the catalog was Michael Knuth (Discogs ID: 984544), whose “Executive-Producer” and “Enemy Administration” credits appear on the majority of releases.

The Knitting Factory Series

A distinct sub-catalog running to at least four volumes (plus a European tour compilation), the Live At The Knitting Factory series documented New York’s downtown venue during its peak years. Produced by Bob Appel, with Michael Dorf as executive producer/director. Engineers included Hahn Rowe, Yoram Vazan, Francis Manzella, Doug Rice, and Kurt Heidinger. Mastering on the series by Joachim Hinsch (Vol. 2) and Mike Reese (Vol. 4).

Brake Out Records (Sublabel)

Sublabel of Enemy Records (Discogs ID: 18432), LC 6811, Munich. Uses OUT-prefix catalog numbers. Roster skews toward American alternative rock and indie rather than Enemy’s downtown/avant-jazz focus. Notable artists include Giant Sand (Howe Gelb), Steve Wynn, Gutterball, Suicide (2), Barkmarket, False Virgins. At least 30 unique masters documented on Discogs, running from OUT 101 (1989) to OUT 128 (1996).
