Tricatel

French independent record label. Founded and run by Bertrand Burgalat. Paris.

Overview

Tricatel is Bertrand Burgalat’s label, known for releasing sophisticated French pop, lounge, and orchestral recordings. In 2002 the label issued The David Whitaker Songbook, a compilation/overview of David Whitaker’s arrangements and compositions, compiled with Burgalat’s assistance.
