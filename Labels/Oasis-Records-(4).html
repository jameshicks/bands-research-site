Overview

Oasis Records (4) is a South Korean record label founded in 1952, operating under the parent entity Oasis Record Co. and registered with the Ministry of Culture & Information as number 16. Its Discogs-documented catalogue spans the mid-1950s through the 1970s, with the bulk of its 40 releases concentrated in the 1970s and ranging across ballad, vocal, disco, and synth-pop styles. The label’s roster is anchored by prominent Korean entertainers including 나훈아, 정훈희, and 현인. It is also affiliated with Oasis Video Production, a related video cassette label.
