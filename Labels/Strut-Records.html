Strut Records

Independent UK reissue and archival label founded in 1999 by Quinton Scott. Strut was one of the first UK labels to treat dance music history with the attention to documentation and primary-source research previously reserved for academic releases — original tapes where available, extensive sleeve notes, rare photographs. The label launched with releases covering Nigerian Afrobeat, UK library music, early hip-hop, original breaks, and leftfield disco. After a dormant period it was reactivated in January 2008 and acquired by the !K7 Label Group, which provides global distribution. Strut operates the sublabel Afro Strut (Discogs ID: 17748). The label’s 944-release catalog (as of 2025) spans funk, soul, Afrobeat, South African township music, free jazz, italo disco, Factory Records, industrial/EBM, Haitian music, Peruvian psych, and beyond.

Overview

Strut’s editorial identity across both its eras has been shaped by Quinton Scott, who appears on roughly 279 releases as A&R, compiler, coordinator, and liner-notes author. Secondary figures include Joel Davies (A&R, ~42 releases), Hugo Mendez (compiler/coordinator, ~47 releases), and Duncan Brooker (research/compilation consultant, ~43 releases). The house designer across the bulk of the catalog is Matt Thame (~223 releases). Primary mastering engineers were Frank Merritt (~113 releases) and Peter Beckmann (~76 releases).

The 1999–2003 phase built on dancefloor archaeology: KPM and Chappell music-library editions, Nigerian 70s funk comps, the Disco Not Disco and Block Party Breaks series, and Larry Levan’s Live At The Paradise Garage. The 2008 relaunch shifted toward artist albums (Mulatu Astatke, The Heliocentrics, Ebo Taylor, Sun Ra Arkestra) alongside deeper archival compilations covering ZE Records, Compass Point, Factory Records, Celluloid, and Dance Mania.

A&R and Key Figures

  • Quinton Scott — founder; A&R, compiler, liner-notes author across virtually the entire catalog. Discogs ID: 468714.
  • Joel Davies — A&R and product management from approx. 2008 onward. Discogs ID: 1041836.
  • Hugo Mendez — compiler and reissue coordinator on African and Latin releases. Discogs ID: 857355.
  • Duncan Brooker — research and reissue consultant. Discogs ID: 682860.
  • Matt Thame — principal graphic designer across the catalog. Discogs ID: 1843725.
  • Frank Merritt — primary mastering engineer. Discogs ID: 858903.
  • Peter Beckmann — mastering engineer. Discogs ID: 300815.
  • Bill Brewster — recurring compiler and liner-notes contributor; co-compiled Disco Not Disco, Blank Generation, Fac. Dance, and others.
  • Frank Broughton — co-compiled Blank Generation (2010); liner notes on Grandmaster Flash and Larry Levan.
