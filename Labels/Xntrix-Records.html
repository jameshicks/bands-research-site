Xntrix Records

UK indie label. Discogs label ID: 45721. Sublabel imprint “Xntrix”: label ID 503398. Parent: Rough Trade (label ID: 51167). Contact c/o Rough Trade, 137 Blenheim Crescent, London W11.

Sublabels: Xntrix (503398), Xntrix Music (307214), Xntrix Publishing (389533), Xntrix Studios (504959 — studio operation, not a release imprint).

Overview

The Poison Girls band label, distributed through Rough Trade. The primary release imprint was “Xntrix” (503398); the parent entity “Xntrix Records” (45721) held the publishing and sub-labels. The label operated from c.1979 through the late 1980s, with Pete Fender (Daniel Sansom, Vi Subversa’s son) running Xntrix Studios as the recording facility for many of its releases.

Beyond Poison Girls, Xntrix was effectively the hub label for the south London / Brighton anarcho-punk network clustered around the Poison Girls family: Fatal Microbes (the Sansom children’s pre-Rubella band), Rubella Ballet, and Conflict all released on the imprint. Recordings by the Lenya Hobnoobs Theatre Company (1989–1992) were also distributed via Xntrix.
