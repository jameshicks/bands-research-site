Overview

Super Electro Sound Recordings was founded in 1992 by Mudhoney guitarist Steve Turner after Sub Pop co-founder Bruce Pavitt declined to release records from two of Turner’s side projects, instead encouraging him to start his own imprint with Sub Pop handling pressing and distribution. By 1993, Turner’s girlfriend Caryn Palmier had come aboard as a partner, and Sub Pop’s distribution role became only intermittent. The label’s entire catalogue of 14 releases falls within the 1990s, clustered around garage rock, punk, and grunge, with roster acts including The Fall-Outs, The Kent 3, and Conrad Uno. Super Electro has been out of business for some time.
