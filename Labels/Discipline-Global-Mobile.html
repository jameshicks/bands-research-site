Discipline Global Mobile is an independent British record label co-founded by guitarist Robert Fripp and producer/engineer David Singleton as a direct response to the protracted royalty dispute with E.G. Records Ltd. The label’s founding premise — that artists retain all copyrights, with the label acting as a transparent service organization — was explicitly anti-industry in intent. Fripp described the model as “a way of doing business in the music industry which is honest.” DGM is primarily the home of King Crimson from 1992 onward and of the full range of Fripp’s solo, collaborative, and archival output.

History

Discipline Records (1992–1994)

The label began as Discipline Records in 1992, operating under the catalog prefix DR. Its earliest releases were archival and reissue-oriented — the King Crimson live box The Great Deceiver (1973–74 material, DR catalog, 1992) and releases by The Robert Fripp String Quintet (The Bridge Between, DR 9303, 1993) and California Guitar Trio (Yamanashi Blues, DR 9301). These initial years established the label’s aesthetic: direct-to-fan archival releases, lean packaging, and total production control retained by Fripp and Singleton.

The transition point came in 1994 with the release of The Robert Fripp String Quintet’s Live In Japan VHS (DGM 9401-3), the first release bearing the DGM catalog prefix, and the label formally became Discipline Global Mobile.

Discipline Global Mobile (1994–present)

The first DGM-era King Crimson release was the VROOOM EP (DGM 0004, 1994), signaling the Double Trio lineup’s formation. The label’s output in the mid-1990s expanded rapidly: in 1994–1996 alone it issued King Crimson’s THRAK album, the THRAK-related ProjeKcts, Fripp solo soundscape records, Adrian Belew solo material, Bill Bruford / Tony Levin collaborations, and artist-curated archival releases.

From approximately 2000, DGM became increasingly focused on King Crimson-related material — reissues, live recordings, the King Crimson Collectors’ Club series (CLUB prefix), and the 21st Century Edition series. The ProjeKcts — King Crimson subunits (ProjeKct One through Four, ProjeKct X) — all had dedicated releases through DGM in 1998–2000.

From the 2010s onward, DGM issued the large-scale boxed-set reissues tied to King Crimson’s 40th and 50th anniversary series, and continued releasing Fripp’s ongoing soundscape recordings and Fripp & Eno archival material. The label remains artist-controlled.

DGM Live (DGMLive.com, Discogs sublabel ID: 64149) serves as the digital/download arm, issuing live recordings directly to fans.

Guitar Craft / League of Crafty Guitarists Releases

Several DGM releases document Robert Fripp’s Guitar Craft network: the California Guitar Trio, The League Of Crafty Guitarists (Intergalactic Boogie Express, DGM 9502, 1995), and related satellite ensembles.
