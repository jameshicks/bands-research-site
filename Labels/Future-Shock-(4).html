Overview

Future Shock (4) is a European reissue label founded in 2021 operating under legally dubious circumstances. Its catalogue of roughly 26 releases concentrates in the 2020s decade and spans psychedelic rock, hard rock, blues rock, and prog-rock material. The roster draws on 1960s–70s names including Jorge Ben, Lynn Carey, and Neil Merryweather, indicating a specialization in vintage rock and psych reissues — likely without proper licensing.
