Cloud Recordings

American independent label based in Athens, Georgia. Founded 2001, closely tied to the Elephant 6 collective. Primary home for Circulatory System, the post-The Olivia Tremor Control project of Will Cullen Hart. Distributed work by A Hawk And A Hacksaw, Tall Dwarfs, Robert Schneider, and a broad range of experimental and psych-pop artists through the 2000s–2020s.

Discogs ID: 47569 | Website: cloudrecordings.com

Background

The label launched in 2001 with the self-titled debut of Circulatory System (cat: cld-001), which Will Cullen Hart had formed after The Olivia Tremor Control dissolved in 1999. The core of the label’s early years grew directly from the Athens Elephant 6 milieu: John Fernandes, Eric Harris, Derek Almstead, Jeff Mangum, Julian Koster, and Scott Spillane all appear on early releases, as does Robert Schneider (in engineering and mixing roles).

The second release (cld 002) was the debut album by A Hawk And A Hacksaw, the Eastern European-influenced project of Jeremy Barnes (then known as drummer for Neutral Milk Hotel). The album assembled an extended cast including Jeff Mangum and Scott Spillane for several tracks.

Sublabel: New Music From Mathematics

Cloud Recordings operates New Music From Mathematics (Discogs ID: 3795650) as a sublabel, described as a collaboration with Michigan Technological University’s Mathematics and Music Lab (MML). The series is co-sponsored by Michael G. Maxwell and Robert Schneider. MML’s stated goals are producing futuristic works of music and installation art, exploring music theory using mathematics, and inventing new audio hardware and software.

The Black Foliage reissue (CLD004) was mixed by Robert Schneider and features Julian Koster, Scott Spillane, Kevin Barnes of Of Montreal, and J. Kirk Pleasant. The original had been released on Flydaddy/Overcoat in 1999.

In 2005, the label issued US editions of two Tall Dwarfs albums (Weeville and Fork Songs). The New Zealand lo-fi duo — Chris Knox and Alec Bathgate — toured the US that year with The Olivia Tremor Control, who had cited them as an influence.

Signal Morning (CLD010, 2009) remains the most personnel-dense release in the catalog: the full Circulatory System line-up alongside Jeff Mangum, Julian Koster, Gary Olson (Ladybug Transistor), Heather McIntosh, Bill Doss, Nesey Gallons, and Derek Almstead.

Connections

  • Elephant 6 Collective — effectively a label imprint serving that collective’s core members
  • The Olivia Tremor Control — Will Cullen Hart, John Fernandes, Eric Harris, Derek Almstead, Pete Erchick all involved
  • Neutral Milk Hotel — Jeff Mangum and Jeremy Barnes both appear on early releases
  • The Apples In Stereo — Robert Schneider engineers/mixes; co-runs New Music From Mathematics sublabel
  • Gary Olson / Ladybug Transistor — engineering on Signal Morning
  • Michigan Technological University — institutional partner for New Music From Mathematics series
