Overview

Dark Operative is an American record label founded in 2018 by Brent Eyestone, with a sublabel Pax Aeternum Digital. Its catalogue spans post-rock, hardcore, emo, and punk — anchored by prominent roster acts This Will Destroy You and Caspian — and has grown steadily, with 18 releases in the 2010s and 27 in the 2020s. Credits trace back to 2009, suggesting Eyestone’s involvement in the scene predates the label’s formal establishment.

Notes (web)

Dark Operative founding and early signings

Dark Operative launched in 2018 immediately after Eyestone closed Magic Bullet Records, which he had run since 1996 (some sources say 1997). The punknews.org snippet confirms the sequence directly: “Magic Bullet Records closes, Brent Eyestone launches Dark Operative” (punknews.org snippet — page blocked, cited from search-result title/snippet only). Magic Bullet’s closure happened at the end of 2017 per Wikipedia’s Magic Bullet Records article (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_Bullet_Records, as of 2026-11-22).

This Will Destroy You — prior relationship confirmed

This Will Destroy You had a long prior relationship with Eyestone through Magic Bullet Records. According to Wikipedia’s TWDY article (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/This_Will_Destroy_You, as of 2026-11-22), Eyestone discovered the band via their self-recorded demo, invited them to contribute to a Magic Bullet compilation (It Came From The Hills Vol. 1), then signed them to Magic Bullet — Young Mountain was reissued on Magic Bullet in June 2006, and the self-titled was released on Magic Bullet in 2008. The relationship operated on a verbal agreement, no written contract. When Magic Bullet closed, the band’s catalogue moved with Eyestone to Dark Operative: the self-titled 10th Anniversary reissue and Young Mountain both appeared on Dark Operative in 2018 (OPS010-1 and OPS001-1 respectively), and New Others: Part One/Two were the first new TWDY material on the label (OPS013-1 / OPS014-1, September 2018). The Dark Operative Discogs entry credits go back to 2009, consistent with Eyestone’s catalogue carrying over from Magic Bullet.

Caspian — no prior relationship with Eyestone

Caspian (3) had no prior connection to Eyestone or Magic Bullet. Their early albums appeared on Dopamine Records (You Are The Conductor, 2005; The Four Trees, 2007) and later on The Mylene Sheath (Tertia, 2009; subsequent records). The search-result aggregate for the 2020 reissues notes the band signed with Dark Operative after facing difficulties with The Mylene Sheath regarding delivery of back-catalogue reissues (idioteq.com — page confirmed unhelpful on signing context; cited from aggregated snippet). Dark Operative announced the formal reissue of their first four albums in 2020: You Are The Conductor, The Four Trees, Tertia, and Live At Old South Church — all had been out of print physically for five to seven years at that point (earsplitcompound.com press release). No interview source found explaining the introduction between Caspian and Eyestone.
