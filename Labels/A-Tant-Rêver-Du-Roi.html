Overview

A Tant Rêver Du Roi is a noise label based in Pau, in the French Basque Country. Founded in 2001, it built its catalogue slowly through the 2000s before expanding significantly in the 2010s and 2020s, releasing across noise, post-punk, noise rock, and indie rock. Its roster includes La Jungle, Ingrina, and The Guru Guru, among others working in experimental and abrasive guitar territory. No parent label or distribution structure is recorded on Discogs, suggesting an independent or artist-run operation.
