Goodnight Records

US independent label based in North Carolina. Discogs label ID: 92938. Active from at least 2000 through 2015. Website: goodnightrecords.com (no longer active). 50 releases on Discogs. Released early material by The Rosebuds and The Close, and later a mix of indie rock acts including The Big Sleep, Heaven (30), Friend Roulette, Nightmare Air, Sun Club, and Heliotropes.
