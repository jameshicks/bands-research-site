Overview

An alternative-rock imprint based in Norcross, Georgia, Sky Records (7) operated under the Sky Records Inc. umbrella from the late 1980s through the 1990s. Its catalogue is concentrated in the 1990s — 26 releases against 4 in the prior decade — spanning alternative rock, industrial, folk rock, pop rock, and indie rock. Despite its Georgia address, the label’s most prominent roster entries were Swans and its principals Michael Gira and Jarboe, bringing a downtown New York avant-garde presence to a suburban Atlanta imprint. John Keane and Clinton Steele round out the roster, reflecting ties to Georgia’s broader alternative and studio scene.

Swans connection — web research

Sky Records (7) / Sky Records Inc. served as the US manufacturer and marketer of Swans’ Young God Records releases in 1991–1992. The relationship was a distribution/manufacturing arrangement, not a direct signing, and arose in the aftermath of Rough Trade Inc.’s 1991 US bankruptcy.

Background

When Michael Gira founded Young God Records in 1991 — created specifically to release Swans material after the band exited its MCA contract — the label initially used Rough Trade Inc. for US distribution. Rough Trade’s US arm collapsed in 1991 shortly after the release of White Light from the Mouth of Infinity, leaving Young God without a US outlet. In a 2003 Exclaim! interview Gira described the fallout directly: “We were going through Rough Trade for distribution at the time, and we shipped out a huge amount of that record, and then they went under.” (exclaim.ca, as of 2026-09-26)

Sky Records Inc. subsequently took over US CD manufacturing and marketing for the next cluster of Young God releases. The Discogs credits for the 1992 US CD pressing of Love of Life (cat. 7-5064-2 / 344.0350.2) carry a “manufactured and marketed by Sky Records, Inc.” credit, and the same arrangement is documented for Body to Body Job to Job (cat. 7-5063-4), White Light from the Mouth of Infinity (cat. 7-5060-4), and the 1992 live album Omniscience (cat. 7-5160-2). The vinyl editions of these albums were distributed separately through Rough Trade and Revolver in the UK/Europe.

In the Exclaim! interview, Gira referred to Sky as “the obscure German distributor Sky” — suggesting he may have understood the arrangement as running through a German entity, or that Sky Records Inc. (Norcross, Georgia) operated as a US licensee/subsidiary arm rather than an entirely independent company. The German Sky Records GmbH (Discogs 278126) and Sky Records Inc. (Discogs 354388 / parent of Sky Records (7) label 192066) are catalogued as separate Discogs entities, and no public documentation has been found confirming or denying a corporate link between them.

What the arrangement was not

No interview, liner note, or press source found to date describes a personal connection between Gira and any named principal at Sky Records Inc., a creative partnership, or a conventional A&R signing. The relationship appears to have been purely functional: Sky manufactured and moved US CDs for the Young God catalogue during a window when Rough Trade had collapsed and before Gira found a more stable solution.

Legal fallout

The Sky arrangement deteriorated. The Exclaim! interview notes “legal complications with Sky” that left Swans releases floundering and contributed — alongside broader label issues — to the three-year gap between Love of Life (1992) and The Great Annihilator (1995). The nature of those legal complications (rights disputes, unpaid royalties, licensing terms) has not been documented in any publicly available source.

Other Sky Records (7) roster context

Sky Records (7)‘s other 1991–1992 roster — Flat Duo Jets, The Jazz Butcher, Vigilantes of Love, Insane Jane — indicates the label was active across the broader US alternative and Georgia indie scenes, not solely a conduit for Swans material. Whether those acts also came via distribution sub-licensing or direct signings is undocumented in web sources found to date.
