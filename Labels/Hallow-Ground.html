Overview

Hallow Ground is a Lucerne, Switzerland-based label and arts platform founded in 2013 that operates across publishing, event hosting, and performance. Its catalogue spans experimental, ambient, drone, and industrial music — 35 releases across the 2010s, followed by 21 more since 2020, sustaining a consistent output rather than tapering off. The roster draws from an international pool of electroacoustic and experimental artists including Lawrence English, Giuseppe Ielasi, Siavash Amini, and Marja Ahti.
