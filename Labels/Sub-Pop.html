Sub Pop

Independent record label. Seattle, Washington. Discogs ID: 77343. Founded 1988 by Bruce Pavitt and Jonathan Poneman.

Landmark Seattle label; key releases in grunge (Nirvana, Mudhoney, Soundgarden pre-major) and indie rock across multiple decades. Signed The Album Leaf for In A Safe Place (2004) and A Chorus Of Storytellers (2010). Also released Mark Kozelek’s Red House Painters material (Old Ramon, 2001).
