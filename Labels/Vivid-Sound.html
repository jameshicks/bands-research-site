Overview

Vivid Sound is a Japanese record label operating under the Vivid Sound Corporation umbrella, active from 1977 through the 1980s with a catalogue of roughly 40 releases. Its output spans psychedelic rock, folk rock, prog rock, soul, and pop rock, and the shape of its sublabel series — British Beat Explosion, British Folk Premium Collection, British Jazz Premium Collection, and British Psychedelic Revolution — points to a primary function as a Japanese licensing and reissue imprint for classic British recordings. The parent corporation also launched several thematic series under the Vivid Sound name, suggesting a catalogue-driven model built around genre-curated compilations and licensed titles rather than original signings.
