Doublevision

Discogs ID: 4814
From: Nottingham, England (30 Chatsworth Avenue, New Basford, Nottingham NG7 7EW)
Active: 1982–c.1987
Co-founded by: Cabaret Voltaire and Paul Smith

Label started by Cabaret Voltaire and Paul Smith in 1982, initially to release affordable video works (CV’s own live/visual material, and others) at around £15 when the industry norm was £40–70. Expanded into records from 1983.

Video Catalog (DV series)

The DV series was the original video imprint — VHS/Betamax releases issued from 1982 onward, separate from the DVR record series. DV7 is not cataloged on Discogs (gap confirmed).

Kirk and Mallinder served as producers/engineers for several Doublevision acts in 1984: both produced Eric Random’s Mad As Mankind (DVR7); Kirk produced The Box’s Muscle In (DVR10).
