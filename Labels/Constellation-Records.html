Constellation Records is a Canadian independent label founded in 1997 by Ian Ilavsky and Don Wilkie in Montreal’s Mile End neighbourhood. Home to Godspeed You Black Emperor!, A Silver Mt. Zion, Do Make Say Think, Fly Pan Am, and dozens of other post-rock, experimental, and avant-garde artists, the label has operated continuously for nearly three decades with a distinctively hand-crafted aesthetic and a strong commitment to artist autonomy and political independence.

Profile

Founding and Early Years (1997–2001)

Constellation was co-founded by Ian Ilavsky (formerly of Montreal indie band Sofa) and Don Wilkie in 1997. The label launched simultaneously with a monthly Montreal concert series called Musique Fragile, which served as a community-building vehicle for the experimental and post-rock scene then forming in the city’s Mile End. The first release (CST 001) was a 7” by Sofa. CST 003 — Godspeed You! Black Emperor’s debut vinyl F# A# ∞ (1997), recorded and mixed by Don Wilkie and Ian Ilavsky, with Ian Ilavsky also credited for engineering on many subsequent releases — established the label’s international profile immediately.

Early years were defined by a cluster of artists closely intertwined with the GY!BE milieu: A Silver Mt. Zion (Efrim Menuck’s chamber-folk project, debut CST009, 2000), Do Make Say Think (debut CST005, 1998), Fly Pan Am (debut CST008, 1999), Exhaust (CST004, 1998), and Sackville (CST007, 1999). Many of these releases were recorded at informal home studios or by collective self-production, with Wilkie and Ilavsky handling engineering.

Hotel2Tango and Studio Infrastructure (2000–2006)

Around 2000, Radwan Moumneh (later the nucleus of Jerusalem In My Heart) established Hotel2Tango as a recording studio in Montreal. Hotel2Tango became the de-facto recording home for Constellation releases through the 2000s and beyond, with Moumneh serving as primary engineer and mixer. In 2006, Constellation and Hotel2Tango co-purchased a building in Mile End, cementing the studio-label relationship institutionally.

Howard Bilerman also emerged as a key recording engineer at Hotel2Tango during this period, recording much of GY!BE’s Yanqui U.X.O. (2002, with Steve Albini tracking), ‘Allelujah! Don’t Bend Ascend (2012), and numerous other Constellation records. Harris Newman — Montreal mastering engineer — has mastered the majority of the label’s catalog since the early 2000s, appearing on 269 releases per the Discogs credits summary.

Jace Lasek (of The Besnard Lakes) is also a recurring mix engineer across many Constellation releases (34 releases credited). Ian Ilavsky himself maintained an active engineering role across at least 36 releases, and Don Wilkie across 19.

Catalog Broadening (2004–2011)

Through the mid-2000s, Constellation expanded significantly beyond the original GY!BE-adjacent cluster. Notable additions:

  • Black Ox Orkestar — Yiddish-inflected folk project featuring Thierry Amar, Jessica Moss, Scott Levine Gilmore, and Gabriel Levine; debut Ver Tanzt? (CST029, 2004)
  • Carla Bozulich — American avant-rock vocalist/composer; debut Evangelista (CST041, 2006)
  • Vic Chesnutt — American singer-songwriter; North Star Deserter (CST046, 2007) and At The Cut (CST060, 2009)
  • Tindersticks — UK chamber-rock group; several releases including The Hungry Saw (CST055, 2008), Falling Down A Mountain (CST065, 2010), and The Something Rain (CST086, 2012); also a retrospective of their Claire Denis film scores
  • Colin Stetson — Montreal-based saxophonist; New History Warfare Vol. 2: Judges (CST075, 2011) and subsequent albums
  • Matana Roberts — Chicago/New York saxophonist/composer; began the multi-volume Coin Coin series with Chapter One (CST079, 2011), recorded by Radwan Moumneh

2010s Onward

The label continued to grow its roster with acts including Suuns (who appear in the research vault though signed later), Jessica Moss (solo work from 2017), Efrim Menuck (solo from High Gospel, CST078, 2011), Sandro Perri, Eric Chenaux, and newer artists such as Joni Void, Automatisme, and Kee Avil. The Godspeed comeback album ‘Allelujah! Don’t Bend Ascend (CST081, 2012) was followed by Asunder, Sweet And Other Distress (CST111, 2015), Luciferian Towers (CST126, 2017), G_d’s Pee At State’s End! (2021), and No Title As Of 13 February 2024 28,340 Dead (CST183, 2024).

Radwan Moumneh — operating under the moniker Jerusalem In My Heart for solo/collaborative work — released his debut Mo7it Al-Mo7it (CST093, 2013) on the label, with subsequent volumes following through 2021.

The label’s design sensibility has been largely shaped by Ian Ilavsky (credited for layout/design on 33 releases), John Tinholt (cover drawings and etchings, 30 releases), Nadia Moss (artwork, 29 releases), and photographer/filmmaker Jem Cohen (16 releases).
