Peterbilt Records

Washington DC independent label. Discogs label ID: 66962. Founded 1988 by Guy Picciotto (of Rites Of Spring, Happy Go Licky, One Last Wish, Fugazi).

Mailing address: P.O. Box 10021, Arlington, VA 22210, USA.

Most releases issued in conjunction with Dischord Records. Has one sublabel: Peterbilt CD Projects (Discogs ID: 72238).

Overview

Peterbilt Records is a micro-label personal to Guy Picciotto, launched in 1988 to document bands in his immediate circle that predated or ran alongside Fugazi. The label operated with virtually no infrastructure: no in-house production staff, minimal graphic identity, and a direct pipeline to Dischord Records for manufacturing and distribution on most releases. The Arlington, VA PO Box address maps it geographically to the same cluster of homes and rehearsal spaces that defined the Dischord orbit.

The catalog is small — six primary catalog numbers across roughly twelve years, plus one CD sublabel release in 2001 — but its scope is unusually archival. Four of the six primary releases document bands that had dissolved before the record appeared: the Happy Go Licky LP appeared in 1988 by which point the band was already defunct; the Deadline (5) live recording documents an August 2, 1982 show released in 1989; the One Last Wish 1986 was released thirteen years after recording. Peterbilt is, in effect, a vault label for the generation of DC bands between Revolution Summer and Fugazi.

The recurring creative personnel — Ian MacKaye and Don Zientara as engineers/producers, Brendan Canty as drummer across multiple releases, Jeff Nelson for graphic work — reflect the tight shared infrastructure of Dischord-adjacent DC. The label’s mailing address migrated by the early 2000s to a Washington DC PO Box (PO Box 21603, Washington DC 20009), and the sublabel Peterbilt CD Projects carried a contact email (Peterbiltinfo@aol.com).

Release Notes

Peterbilt 001 — Happy Go Licky (Happy Go Licky, 1988)

Self-titled debut LP, the label’s founding release. Lineup: Guy Picciotto (guitar, vocals), Eddie Janney (guitar, vocals), Mike Fellows (bass), Brendan Canty (drums). No producer or engineer is credited on Discogs. Matrix runout stamped “PETERBILT-001-A 8x349 MASTERDISK” — lacquer cut at Masterdisk (New York). Styles: Art Rock / Experimental / Punk.

Peterbilt 2 — 8/2/82 (Deadline (5), 1989)

Live capture of an August 2, 1982 DC hardcore show — the title is the date. Deadline (5) lineup: Ray Hare (vocals), Chris Caron (guitar), Terry Scanlon (bass), Brendan Canty (drums); backing vocals from Chipsterwick, Harvestfellows, and Urchin (3). Recorded by Ian MacKaye and Don Zientara. Jeff Nelson provided graphic assistance. Matrix runout jokes: “D.O.D. is Innocent” (Side A), “Chevy Chase Was Blamed” (Side B). Styles: Hardcore / Punk. Reissued 2007 as a CD.

Peterbilt 3 — La Vache Qui Rit (Rain (18), 1990)

Six-track EP by Rain (18), an emo/post-hardcore band from Washington DC active 1986–1990. Lineup (uncredited on sleeve): Jon Kirschten (guitar, vocals), Scott McCloud (guitar, tracks A1–B1, B3; vocals on A3), Eli Janney (drums), Bert Queiroz (bass). Produced (uncredited) by Ian MacKaye; engineered (uncredited) by Don Zientara (most tracks) and Geoff Turner (B2). Backing vocals by Amy Pickering (uncredited). Graphics/layout: Kurt Sayenga. Styles: Emo / Post-Hardcore. Reissued 2007 on CD.

Peterbilt 4 — Will Play (Happy Go Licky, 1997)

CD archival collection of 21 tracks, including live and studio recordings. Some tracks overlap with or expand the Peterbilt 001 LP (Ansol, White Lines, Torso Butter, Pastel Blue Eyes, Suzuki, Boca Raton appear here in longer or alternate versions). Also includes tracks not on the LP: Brigham Young, Casing, Twist and Shout, Abandon Me, 13 Months of Sunshine, Born Like Steam, Battery, Don’t Bone Me, Cutthroat Answer, Casing (Edit), D.I.Y. Ansol. Mixed by Happy Go Licky and Ian MacKaye. Co-released Dischord DIS 109.

Peterbilt 5 — 1982–1997 (The Black Light Panthers, 1998)

12” EP by The Black Light Panthers, a side project of Guy Picciotto and Brendan Canty. “Side 82” contains five tracks (Hey Hey Hey! ×2, Planet What!, Fuck You My Latino Lover!, Go!); “Side 97” contains one track (Golden Vanity). Only release under The Black Light Panthers name. Styles: Indie Rock / Lo-Fi / Punk.

Peterbilt 6 — 1986 (One Last Wish, 1999)

CD of 12 songs recorded in 1986 by One Last Wish — Picciotto (guitar, lead vocals, songwriter), Eddie Janney (bass, backing vocals, songwriter), Canty (drums, songwriter), Michael Hampton (3) (guitar, backing vocals, songwriter). Engineered by Don Zientara; produced by Ian MacKaye and the band. Graphics: Jason Farrell, Guy Picciotto, Jeff Nelson. Photography: Bert Queiroz, Jeff Nelson, Tina Atkinson. Co-released as Dischord DIS 118. Eddie Janney sings lead on tracks 4 and 9. Styles: Emo / Hardcore / Punk.

Peterbilt CD Projects 001 — Uppragn Srilimia Ixioor Ocrilim Nollfithes Mrithixyl (Octis / Or:12r3, 2001)

Experimental 2×CD split (shape disc format) with 72 ultra-short tracks (most under 35 seconds). Mick Barr credited for guitar and drum programming (uncredited on sleeve). Stylistically in avantgarde / experimental / grindcore territory. The only release on the Peterbilt CD Projects sublabel. Octis is a Mick Barr project; Or:12r3 is an associated act. The connection to Picciotto’s label orbit is unclear — this release stands apart from the rest of the catalog thematically.

Sub-labels

Peterbilt CD Projects (Discogs ID: 72238) — one-release CD imprint. Contact: Peterbiltinfo@aol.com / PO Box 21603, Washington DC 20009. Sole release is the Octis / Or:12r3 split (2001).
