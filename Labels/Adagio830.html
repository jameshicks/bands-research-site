Overview

Adagio830 is a Berlin independent label founded in 1999 by Robert ‘The Knife’ Schulze of the band Zann and Norman Dietze, working primarily in hardcore, emo, punk, and post-punk. Its catalogue grew steadily through the 2000s before accelerating in the 2010s, which account for nearly 100 of its roughly 150 releases. The label’s production credits feature prominent North American engineers including Jack Shirley and Will Killingsworth, pointing to transatlantic ties within the underground hardcore and emo world.
