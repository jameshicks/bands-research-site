Lectric Eye Records

New Orleans, Louisiana independent punk label. Discogs label ID: 42912. Active 1978–1979. Four 7” singles on the catalog, all pressed at Masterfonics in Nashville.

Overview

Lectric Eye Records was a small New Orleans imprint that documented a cluster of the city’s earliest punk acts across two years. The label issued four 7” singles between 1978 and 1979, all on seven-inch vinyl and all manufactured at Masterfonics. No parent label, no sublabels, and no profile text survives in the Discogs database — the label’s history is reconstructed almost entirely through the releases themselves and the personnel credits they carry.

The catalog opens in 1978 with two singles: The Skinnies (EYE 1) and The Normals (2) (EYE 2). The Skinnies’ single was produced by Carlos Boll, who was also the band’s guitarist — he went on to do artwork for the Men In Black (6) release (EYE 3) and is separately known for later projects Duparc and The Mystery School. The Normals’ single was produced by Charles Hanson and has gone on to become the label’s most-documented release, reprinted multiple times and included on the Soul Jazz Punk 45 compilation. The 1979 releases are Men In Black (6) (cat# “Lectric Eye 3”) and Buns (2) (EYE 4) — the latter self-produced by Michael Fell (4), who handled vocals, guitar, drums, and production. Men In Black’s single was produced by Duval Russell, who played guitar and vocals on it; Jay Hagman contributed piano, synthesizer, and wrote the songs.

The label used the “EYE” catalog prefix for three of four releases; EYE 3 is listed on Discogs with the longer form “Lectric Eye 3” on the label. No EYE 5 or subsequent releases are known. Whether the label was run by a band member, a manager, or an outside party is not recorded in the Discogs data.

Artists

The Skinnies (Discogs artist ID: 414008)

New Orleans punk band. Their sole Lectric Eye single, released as the EP Kill The Beat (EYE 1, 1978), contains the tracks “I’m A Dullard” (A) and “Out Of Order” (B). The band was anchored by Carlos Boll (guitar, producer, songwriter, sleeve) alongside Mandeville Mike (vocals), Bobby Brennan (bass), and Ricky Polizzi (drums). A later Italian compilation, Out Of Order (Rave Up Records, 2000), bears the same title as their B-side and collected their recordings. Members: Carlos Boll (guitar), Mandeville Mike (vocals), Bobby Brennan (bass), Ricky Polizzi (drums), Michael Pastor (2).

The Normals (2) (Discogs artist ID: 321458)

See The Normals (2) for full entry. New Orleans punk band formed around the songwriting partnership of Charles Hanson and David Brewton. Their 1978 Lectric Eye single “Almost Ready” / “Hardcore” (EYE 2) is the label’s most-documented release — produced by Hanson, A-side written by Brewton, B-side by Hanson. Included on the Soul Jazz Punk 45 Vol. 1 (2013) compilation. The band reunited decades later and released material on Last Laugh Records (2) in 2011 and 2017.

Men In Black (6) (Discogs artist ID: 1858568)

New Orleans punk/post-punk act. Their single “S&M Bar” / “The Gypsy Lid” (Lectric Eye 3, 1979) was produced by Duval Russell (guitar, vocals) with Jay Hagman on piano, synthesizer, and songwriting, and Jimi Fusulier on drums. Artwork by Carlos Boll and Norma Jones (2). Engineered by Al Taylor. Only one release in the Discogs database. The Discogs band entry (1858568) has no member list.

Buns (2) (Discogs artist ID: 8850904)

New Orleans punk act. Their single “Anti-Matter” / “Raise The Roof” (EYE 4, 1979) is a self-contained production: Michael Fell (4) handled lead vocals, rhythm guitar, drums, and production; David Gostl played bass and vocals and engineered; Ron James (8) played lead guitar and vocals. Fell also did the cover art (credited as “M.F.”). The band is listed on Discogs as “The Buns” in the personnel credits. Photography by Russ (Discogs ID: 88462). BMI rights society listed. Masterfonics runout etchings (VMFRP-1385/1386).

Carlos Boll is the most prominent recurring figure in the label’s orbit: he produced and performed on the Skinnies single and contributed artwork to the Men In Black release. Norma Jones (2) contributed sleeve/artwork to both EYE 1 and Lectric Eye 3. Al Taylor (2) / Al Taylor engineered the Skinnies and Men In Black sessions.

Pressing Details

All four singles were pressed at Masterfonics (Nashville), as confirmed by runout etchings on EYE 1 (stamped “Masterfonics,” etched VMFRP-1170/1171) and EYE 4 (etched VMFRP-1385/1386). The Normals’ single (EYE 2) also carries the Masterfonics credit on its original pressing. This was a common indie pressing destination for Southern independent labels in the late 1970s.
