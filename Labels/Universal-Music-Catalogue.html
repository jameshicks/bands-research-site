Overview

Universal Music Catalogue was the reissue and catalogue marketing label of Universal Music Group, operating primarily under that name and logo from 2006 to 2016. Its releases draw heavily from UMG’s archive across pop rock, synth-pop, new wave, and alternative rock, with notable catalogue acts including Mike Oldfield, Pete Townshend, and Robert Smith. Around 2016, the imprint was rebranded as Universal UMC, which continues as a sublabel alongside the stripped-down UMC identity.
