Overview

Sophomore Lounge is a US independent record label founded in 2006 and distributed by The Business (2). Its catalogue runs heavily toward indie rock, experimental, and garage rock, with a secondary country rock strand; output picked up sharply in the 2010s (26 releases) and has continued at a steady pace into the 2020s (17 releases). The label operates a sublabel, Loin Seepage, and its recurring roster includes Ryan Davis (4), Ryan Davis & The Roadhouse Band, Carl Saff, Jim Marlowe, and Dominic Cipolla.
