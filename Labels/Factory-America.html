Overview

Factory America was the short-lived US offshoot of Factory Records, active from 1981 to 1983 and run by Michael Shamberg. Its inaugural catalogue designation — FACT A First — was assigned to a New Order/Ike Yard joint show at the Ukrainian National Home in New York on 18 November 1981, with the gig’s poster co-designed by Ike Yard’s Stuart Argabright; the New Order performance was later issued as a Factory video (FACT 77). The label’s catalogue of three releases spans electro, minimal synth-pop, and experimental territory, with its roster built around Ike Yard, A Certain Ratio, and Be Music.
