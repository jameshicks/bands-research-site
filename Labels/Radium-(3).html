Radium (3)

American experimental/indie rock label operating 2006–2010. Discogs label ID: 106679. Sublabel of Table Of The Elements (parent ID: 12701). Also known as R88. Website shared with parent: myspace.com/tableoftheelements.

Founded and operated by Jeff Hunt, who ran all Table Of The Elements imprints. Radium catalogued twenty-five releases in roughly four years (2006–2010), with catalog numbers prefixed TOE-CD- and TOE-LP- in the 800-series. The label served as the contemporary/indie-facing wing of Table Of The Elements, releasing new recordings alongside the parent label’s retrospective and minimalist catalog; it was the primary vehicle for Hunt’s work with Rhys Chatham in the mid-2000s.

Context: Table Of The Elements

Table Of The Elements was founded by Jeff Hunt in 1993. Each catalog number is composed using a symbol from the periodic table paired with its atomic number — a conceptual design system running across all imprints. Radium (element 88, symbol Ra) maps to catalog numbers in the 800-series. The Table Of The Elements family of sublabels includes Xeric, Lanthanides, Audio ArtKive, Azoth Schallplatten Gesellschaft, and four Guitar Series imprints.

Personnel Behind the Label

Jeff Hunt (Discogs ID: 399900) is credited as Executive Producer across nine Radium releases — essentially every non-artist-produced record in the catalog. He also handled art direction on most releases and wrote liner notes for the Chatham and Collections Of Colonies Of Bees records. His name variation “J. Hunt” appears on some credits.

Bradly Brown (Discogs ID: 2101232) was the label’s primary graphic designer, credited on five releases for graphic design, art direction, photography, and layout. He designed the Jonathan Kane solo albums, the Chatham Guitar Trio set, the Helen Money record, and the Impala Eardrums sampler.

Susan Archie (Discogs ID: 1827891) handled design on three releases including the Guitar Trio All-Stars and Peg Simone album.

Catalog

2006

TOE-CD-802 / TOE-LP-802 — An Angel Moves Too Fast To See (For 100 Electric Guitars, Electric Bass, And Drums) — Rhys Chatham The label’s flagship release. CD (release ID 1030063) and LP (release ID 1142858) versions issued simultaneously. Documents a Paris performance of the 1989 large-ensemble composition. Chatham as composer, conductor, performer. Mixed by Chatham and Martin Wheeler (3). CD version additionally credits four electric guitarists: Dominique Pichon, Jean-François Pauvros, Kent Condon, Robin Lyon. Drums: Jonathan Kane. Electric bass: Ernie Brooks (credited as “Ernest Brooks III”). Executive production and art direction: Jeff Hunt. Cover photography by Robert Longo (“Running Man”).

TOE-CD-801 — Die Donnergötter — Rhys Chatham Release ID: 1589325. Issued the same year as the 2006 Radium catalog, documenting an earlier Chatham ensemble work. The recording spans multiple compositions and large personnel. Performers include Anton Fier (drums, track 5), David Linton (drums, tracks 3–4), Conrad Kinard (electric bass), Michael Brown (5) (electric bass), Ben Neill (electric guitar and trumpet), Bill Brovold (electric guitar), Joe Dizney (electric guitar), Karen Haglof (electric guitar), Mitch Salmen (electric guitar), Ned Sublette (electric guitar), Nina Canal (electric guitar), Robert Poss (electric guitar), Tim Schellenbaum (electric guitar), Don Yallech (percussion). Brass ensemble on track 5: George Lewis (trombone), Jim Staley (trombone), Steve Freeman (2) (trombone), Ben Neill (trumpet), Frank Gordon (trumpet), Olu Dara (trumpet), Ron Tooley (trumpet), Sinclair Acey (trumpet), Bob Stewart (tuba). Mixed by Martin Bisi (track 1), J.P. (7), Peter Gordon (tracks 3–4), Chatham, and Wharton Tiers.

TOE-CD-803 — I Looked At The Sun — Jonathan Kane Release ID: 701960. Kane’s solo debut on Radium. Kane played guitar, bass, and drums; co-produced with David Daniell (who also played guitar and pedal steel) and Paul Duncan (4) (who recorded and mixed). Gene Paul mastered. Igor Cubrilovic credited for pre-production and advice.

TOE-CD-804 — Transmission — Transmission (12) Release ID: 1641347. Band featuring Jonathan Kane on drums, Daniel Galli-Duani on saxophone, and Sal Palazzo on bass (track 5).

Sampler (cat: 57) — Rhys Chatham Sampler — Rhys Chatham Release ID: 2465709. Promo sampler, catalog number 57.

2007

TOE-CD-805 — Arkansaw Man — Arkansaw Man Release ID: 1231886. CD EP, reissue. Four tracks. Lineup: Stephen Clarke (guitar), Patrick LoVullo (bass), Glenn Sorvisto (drums). Produced by Arkansaw Man and Richard Kelly; Ron Morgan engineered. Executive produced by Cindy Buff. Jeff Hunt credited as Reissue Producer. Styles: Post Rock, Punk.

TOE-LP-807 — Face To Face On High Places — School Of Seven Bells Release IDs: 1387500 (pressing 1), 1038784 (pressing 2). The debut release by School Of Seven Bells. Limited-edition single-sided 12” EP with etched B-side. Members: Alejandra Deheza, Claudia Deheza, Benjamin Curtis, James Elliott (all credited as Performer). James Elliott also co-produced, recorded, and mixed alongside Curtis. Roberto Carlos Lange recorded drums. Dave Cooley mastered. Jeff Hunt executive produced.

TOE-CD-808 — Nightly — Ateleia Release ID: 1215317. James Elliott (of School Of Seven Bells) composed and mastered three of the four tracks; Greg Davis (7) mastered track 2.

TOE-CD-809 — The Little Drummer Boy — Jonathan Kane Release ID: 1464607. Kane played guitar, bass, and drums. Produced and recorded by Jon Crider; Gene Paul mastered; Bradly Brown designed.

TOE-CD-813PROMO — Guitar Trio Is My Life! (promo) — Rhys Chatham & His Guitar Trio All-Stars Release ID: 11159328. Promo-only version; sole additional credit is Tim Iseler (recording engineer, tracks 1-2, 3-2).

2008

TOE-CD-810 — Birds — Collections Of Colonies Of Bees Release ID: 1502734. Milwaukee post-rock / experimental ensemble. Lineup: Chris Rosenau (guitar, recorded by), Thomas Wincek (Fender Rhodes, electronics, recorded by), Jon Mueller (drums, percussion), Daniel Spack (guitar), Jim Schoenecker (synthesizer/bass, electronics, recorded by). Self-produced; James Plotkin mastered. Jeff Hunt executive produced, wrote liner notes.

TOE-LP-811 / TOE-CD-811 — Gong Lake — Neptune (5) Release IDs: 1775710 (LP), 1534676 (CD). Portland-based experimental group. Lineup: Daniel Paul Boucher (drums, xylophone, synthesizer, percussion, vocals), Kevin Micka (percussion, recorded by, mixed by), Mark Pearson (3) (percussion, synthesizer, baritone guitar, vocals), Jason Sanford (synthesizer, lamellophone, baritone guitar, vocals), Kenneth Linehan (synthesizer on B1, B5). Neptune (5) also composed, mixed, and photographed the album. Scott Craggs mastered; Jeff Hunt executive produced.

TOE-CD-812 / TOE-LP-812 — Impala Eardrums: A Radium Sampler — Various Release IDs: 9343078 (CD), 2916850 (LP). Eight-track sampler surveying the Radium roster, one track per artist. Track listing:

  8. School Of Seven Bells — “Limb By Limb” (4:27) LP: executive produced by Jeff Hunt and Bradly Brown; mastered by James Plotkin. Limited edition LP pressing.

TOE-CD-813 — Guitar Trio Is My Life! — Rhys Chatham & His Guitar Trio All-Stars Release ID: 1290668. Three-CD set. Recording engineers Eric Block (3) (tracks 1-1, 1-3 to 3-1, 3-3) and Tim Iseler (tracks 1-2, 3-2). Jeff Hunt executive produced, art directed, and wrote liner notes; Susan Archie assisted design; Bradly Brown (graphic design). Chris Griffin (2) mastered.

TOE-CD-814 — Specimens — Melissa St. Pierre Release ID: 3052008. St. Pierre played prepared piano. Collections Of Colonies Of Bees credited as producer and performer.

Sampler (no cat) — Jon Mueller sampler Release ID: 4369091. Catalog listed as “none.” Jon Mueller (drummer of Collections Of Colonies Of Bees).

2009

TOE-818-LP — Birds — Collections Of Colonies Of Bees Release ID: 2047112. LP reissue of the 2008 CD release. Same album, different format/catalog number.

TOE-CD-819 — In Tune — Helen Money Release ID: 3176971. Solo cello album by Alison Chesley (performing as Helen Money). Mixed by Sanford Parker; mastered by Collin Jordan; Greg Norman engineered. One track (“7”) credited to Mike Watt (written-by). Jeff Hunt served as art director.

TOE-CD-816 / TOE-LP-816 — Jet Ear Party — Jonathan Kane Release IDs: 5701226 (CD), 1868820 (LP). Kane’s third solo full-length on Radium. No additional credits logged in Discogs.

2010

TOE-CD-820 — Secrets From The Storm — Peg Simone Release ID: 2983954. Produced and recorded by Igor Cubrilovic (who also engineered) and Simone. Gene Paul mastered; Susan Archie designed. Jonathan Kane credited for photography.

Open Questions

  • Table Of The Elements / Radium discography does not appear to extend past 2010 on Discogs; unclear whether the label formally closed or simply went inactive. Web research needed to confirm.
  • The catalog gap between TOE-CD-815 and TOE-CD-816 (Jet Ear Party) is unaccounted for — no release with 815 appears in the DB.
  • Die Donnergötter on Radium (2006) vs. the earlier Die Donnergötter on Dossier Records (1987): the 2006 release appears to be a reissue or alternate presentation of the same material; full relationship between the two editions not confirmed from Discogs alone.
