Overview

Pastel Music is a South Korean independent label founded in 2001 under the parent company Pastel Music Inc., with a catalogue spanning indie rock, post-rock, indie pop, shoegaze, and ethereal styles. Its output was heaviest in the 2000s — 69 releases that decade — before tapering sharply to 17 in the 2010s, pointing to either a strategic shift or a gradual wind-down. Roster acts include Ninaian, 어른아이 (Adult Child), Kim Min Hong, Sound Providers, and 불싸조, positioning the label as an early incubator of Korean indie and post-rock.
