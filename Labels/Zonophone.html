Zonophone

British record label, EMI subsidiary (parent: Parlophone Records Ltd.). Discogs label ID: 35129.

Overview

Zonophone was an EMI imprint active in the early 1980s, releasing new wave, punk, and pop material. It was used as a home for artists signed in the post-punk pop crossover wave. Honey Bane was signed to Zonophone for a five-year contract starting in 1980, releasing four singles between 1981 and 1983.

Key Zonophone catalog numbers in the Honey Bane discography: Z 15 (“Turn Me On Turn Me Off”), Z 19 (“Baby Love”), Z 23 (“Jimmy… (Listen To Me)”), 12Z 32 (“Wish I Could Be Me”), Z 36 (“Dizzy Dreamers”).
