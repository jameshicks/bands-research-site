Overview

Blocks Recording Club is a Canadian independent label that began releasing music in 2003, working primarily in indie rock, lo-fi, and experimental sounds. Its catalogue is weighted heavily toward the 2000s, with 22 releases that decade tapering to 9 in the 2010s. The roster centres on a small cluster of Toronto-adjacent artists, most notably Owen Pallett (also active under the alias Final Fantasy) and Bob Wiseman, alongside Leon Taheny and George Graves. The label takes its name literally in its catalogue numbering, substituting block symbols for the word “BLOCKS” in release identifiers.
