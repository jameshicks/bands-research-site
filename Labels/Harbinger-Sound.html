Overview

Harbinger Sound was a UK independent label run by Steve Underwood, focused on avant-punk, post-punk, and experimental noise. Active from 1997, it published steadily through the 2000s and reached peak output in the 2010s — 40 of its 67 releases — with a roster that included Sleaford Mods, Consumer Electronics, and Pisse. It also operated a sublabel, Harbinger Rågsved. The label went into liquidation in spring 2021, with a Record Store Day release for Girls In Synthesis as its final output.
