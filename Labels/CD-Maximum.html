One of the leading Russian labels, active since 1997. Unlike many of its contemporaries, their label adheres more to policy of complying with both International and Russian copyright laws. They have, however, issued many bootlegs and pirated material as well.

When signing exclusive manufacturing, marketing & distribution deals with foreign labels in Europe, Asia & North America, CD-Maximum agrees to sell their legally licensed, re-packaged, re-labeled merchandise within Russia, CIS and the Baltic States only and will not export their moderately priced products abroad.

CD-Maximum’s A&R department has signed numerous Russian bands to their label, and promotes and distributes those works internationally.

Known in Russian as СД-Максимум.

Overview

CD-Maximum is one of Russia’s leading music labels, founded in 1997 and legally registered as ООО “СД-Максимум”. Working through exclusive regional licensing agreements with foreign labels across Europe, Asia, and North America, the imprint distributes re-licensed catalogue strictly within Russia, the CIS, and the Baltic States at below-import prices. Despite a stated commitment to respecting international and Russian copyright law, the label has also issued bootleg and pirated material — a duality apparently acknowledged in its sublabel name Украдено Из Студии (“Stolen from the Studio”). Its catalogue centres on classic rock, hard rock, and heavy metal, with particular depth around Nazareth and related artists.
