Overview

Detroit Underground is a Detroit-based multidisciplinary arts collective founded in 1997, operating primarily as a record label releasing IDM, techno, experimental, electro, and glitch music. Credits begin appearing in Discogs from 2003, and the catalogue is heavily concentrated in the 2010s (61 of 86 total releases), with a smaller initial run through the 2000s. The roster spans electronic artists such as Dietrich Schoenemann, Kero, and Dmas3, alongside a notable entry from The Designers Republic — the Sheffield visual design studio whose aesthetic is closely associated with Warp Records and 1990s IDM.
