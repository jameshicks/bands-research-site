Overview

Sounds Haarlem Likes Vinyl is a Dutch independent label run out of the Sounds Haarlem record shop in Haarlem, Netherlands. Active since 2013, it has accumulated over 40 releases spanning pop rock, post-punk, new wave, and pop rap, with output picking up noticeably in the 2020s. Its roster mixes Dutch artists — including rapper Brainpower and local performers Jean-Paul van Mierlo and Reinier Rietveld — with posthumous releases by British post-punk figure Adrian Borland.

Adrian Borland releases — authorization chain (web research)

The Borland releases on Sounds Haarlem Likes Vinyl flow through Stichting Opposite Direction (also written “Stichting In The Opposite Direction”), a Dutch foundation (stichting) based in the Netherlands. The foundation was created by Jean-Paul van Mierlo, a long-standing Borland devotee who had earlier co-produced a fan memorial publication (“Happy Book of Memories”) and thereby established working relationships with Adrian’s family and associates. IndieGoGo campaign page, archived snippet

The foundation’s first major output was the 2016 documentary Walking in the Opposite Direction, directed by Marc Waltman and produced by van Mierlo, which premiered at IDFA (International Documentary Festival Amsterdam) in 2016 and featured Adrian’s father Bob Borland among the interviewees. IMDB tt6139984

Archive transfer: Adrian’s father Bob Borland had been the primary custodian of the recordings — he had served as home recording engineer for the young Sound and continued archiving his son’s material after Adrian’s death in 1999. Multiple sources indicate that after Bob Borland’s death, the entirety of Adrian’s official and unofficial recordings passed to Stichting Opposite Direction, which then began sorting and releasing the material in collaboration with Sounds Haarlem Likes Vinyl. stichtingoppositedirection.com (homepage, JavaScript-heavy — content confirmed via search-result snippets) The precise date of Bob Borland’s death has not been confirmed from a primary source; the first Sounds Haarlem Borland release (Beautiful Ammunition, SHLV014) appeared in 2017, consistent with the archive transfer occurring around or shortly before that year.

Current activities: As of 2026, Stichting Opposite Direction/van Mierlo also released a book — All The Words: The Handwritten Lyrics of Adrian Borland (250 pp., April 2026) — alongside exhibitions and concerts at Broedplaats De Volharding in Haarlem. The label and foundation appear to operate as a unit: Stichting Opposite Direction holds/licenses the rights; Sounds Haarlem Likes Vinyl handles physical production and retail. [Search result snippet from stichtingoppositedirection.com, 2026-10-27]

Note on sourcing: The stichtingoppositedirection.com and adrianborlandthesound.com sites both run on Wix and returned only JavaScript on fetch; the above is confirmed via IMDB, IndieGoGo campaign snippets, the soundshaarlem.nl catalogue page title, and consistent cross-referencing across multiple search-result summaries.
