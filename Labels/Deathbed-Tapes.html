Overview

Deathbed Tapes is a US harsh noise, industrial, and power electronics label run by Alex Ford. Founded in 2018, it has built a focused roster that includes Controlled Death, Maso Yamazaki, King Cobra, Grant Richardson, and Death Cult Ritual. Its output accelerated sharply into the 2020s — roughly 79 releases in that decade against 27 in its founding years — placing it among the more prolific small imprints in the American harsh noise scene.
