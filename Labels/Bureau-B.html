Bureau B

German independent record label, music publisher, and booking agency based in Hamburg. Founded 2005 by Gunther Buskies as a sister label to Tapete Records. Discogs label ID: 111528. Label code: LC 13875.

Bureau B (sometimes spelled Bureau-B) operates as a sibling imprint to Tapete Records, both run out of Hamburg by Buskies. The label focuses on the spectrum from pop to avant-garde, and has become a primary home for reissues of classic German electronic music — especially Krautrock and Kosmische — alongside new recordings by both veteran and contemporary artists.

Overview

Bureau B’s catalog falls into two overlapping streams:

Reissue program (core identity): The label has systematically reissued the back catalog of Sky Records (the original Munich-based home of Cluster, Moebius, Roedelius, Schnitzler and others), as well as albums from related artists. Key reissue subjects include Cluster, Hans-Joachim Roedelius, Dieter Moebius, Conrad Schnitzler, Kluster (3), Harmonia, Michael Rother, Faust, Asmus Tietchens, Günter Schickert, Heldon (Richard Pinhas), and Qluster. Reissues are typically remastered and produced by Gunther Buskies and Thomas Worthmann, with Willem Makkee as the recurring remastering engineer.

Contemporary signings: Alongside the archival work, Bureau B has developed a live roster of active artists, including the long-running Düsseldorf group Kreidler, Die Wilde Jagd, Camera (10), Karl Bartos, Camouflage, Der Plan, Pyrolator, Martin Rev, Schneider TM, and Finnish artist Jimi Tenor.

The Kollektion series (launched c. 2014) collects archival material by label artists, compiled by guest curators including Tim Gane, Holger Hiller, Thomas Fehlmann, and Richard Fearless.

Conrad Schnitzler

The single most represented artist on the label. Bureau B issued an extensive run of Schnitzler archive releases from 2012 onward, including Blau (BB103, 2012), Con 3 (BB122), Consequenz (BB121), Gold (BB150, 2013), Silver (BB149, 2013), Con-Struct (CD110702, 2015), Kollektion 05 compiled by Thomas Fehlmann (BB188, 2015), Con (BB350, 2020), and many more. Over 114 release entries on the label.

Key Reissue Source: Sky Records

Bureau B has systematically reissued much of the back catalog of Sky Records (Discogs ID: 10414), the original Munich label that was the home of Moebius, Roedelius, Cluster, Schnitzler and others in the 1970s–80s. The Kollektion 01 (2014) was explicitly a Sky Records compilation curated by Tim Gane.

Open Questions

The catalog shows releases from 2005 (BB01), and the profile explicitly says “founded in 2005.” The 2009 date may refer to when the krautrock reissue focus began in earnest (the Cluster/Eno and Faust reissues both hit in 2009).
  • Gunther Buskies does not yet have a People stub — worth creating once his broader work is researched.
  • Thomas Worthmann (co-reissue producer) not yet stubbed.
  • Several sublabels (Grosse Freiheit, Selekt, Silberland, Sowas Von Egal.) show 0 releases in Discogs — may be structural/imprint entries or very new.
  • La Düsseldorf appears as “La-Duesseldorf.de” in vault Bands — may need cross-check.
