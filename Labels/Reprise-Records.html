Reprise Records

Discogs ID: 157
Origin: Burbank, California, USA
Founded: 1960 (Frank Sinatra); acquired by Warner Bros. 1963

Major label subsidiary of Warner Bros. Records. Home to The Meters from 1972–1976, across four studio LPs: Cabbage Alley (MS 2076), Rejuvenation (MS 2200), Fire On The Bayou (MS 2228), Trick Bag. The Meters moved to Warner Bros. proper for New Directions (1977).

Reprise’s broader catalog includes Frank Sinatra (founder), Neil Young, Jimi Hendrix, Joni Mitchell, and many other major acts. One of the most significant US major-label subsidiaries.

Nancy Sinatra & Lee Hazlewood on Reprise

The label was co-founded by Frank Sinatra, whose daughter Nancy Sinatra recorded there. All primary Nancy Sinatra & Lee Hazlewood duo recordings were released on Reprise 1966–1971: “Summer Wine,” “Jackson,” “Some Velvet Morning,” “Lady Bird,” “You Only Live Twice / Jackson,” the Nancy & Lee LP (RS 6273, 122+ pressings), and the “Did You Ever” single. The duo then moved to RCA Victor for the Did You Ever?/Nancy & Lee Again LP (1971–72).

Waronker Productions on Reprise

Lenny Waronker was the primary staff producer at Reprise through the 1960s–1970s. Key Reprise artists he produced: Randy Newman (full catalog 1968–1983), Gordon Lightfoot (1970–1978 peak years), Ry Cooder (1970–1974), Maria Muldaur (1973–1974, with Joe Boyd), Arlo Guthrie (multiple LPs).

Templeman Productions on Reprise

Ted Templeman produced several key Reprise LPs: Little Feat’s Sailin’ Shoes (1972) and Dixie Chicken (1973), and Captain Beefheart and His Magic Band’s Clear Spot (1972).
