Fresh Records

NYC dance and hip-hop label. Sub-label of Sleeping Bag Records. Discogs label ID: 11356.

Founded 1984 by William Socolov as a sublabel of Sleeping Bag Records. Part of the same operation until Sleeping Bag closed in 1992. Catalog now controlled by Phase One Network.

Not to be confused with Fresh Records (14) (UK post-punk/indie label, 1978–1982).
