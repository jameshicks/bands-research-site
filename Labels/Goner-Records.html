Overview

An independent punk and garage rock label — and record store — founded in Memphis, Tennessee in 1993 by co-owners Eric Friedl and Zac Ives. The catalogue spans five decades of activity, growing from a handful of releases in the 1990s to a concentrated peak in the 2010s (90 releases) before a steady presence into the 2020s. Its roster has passed through notable names including Jay Reatard and Ty Segall, and the label operates a sublabel, Goner Jr. Stylistically the output circles garage rock, punk, indie rock, post-punk, and rock & roll.
