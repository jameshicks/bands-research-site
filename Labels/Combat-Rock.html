Overview

Combat Rock is a French punk rock label founded in 1990 by Claude Alain Chervet — who also records as Caps and appears on its own roster — and based in Metz. The catalogue centres on punk, Oi, new wave, and alternative rock, with consistent output across all four decades: roughly 20–28 releases per decade from the 1990s through the 2020s. The label navigated a long succession of distributors, from Danceteria in its opening years to Musidisc through the late 1990s and 2000s, and L’Autre Distribution from 2017 onward. Its roster includes Charge 69, UK Subs, Pkrk, and Laurent Hees, and it has spawned two sublabels: French Connection (4) and Via Combat Rock.
