Overview

Daytrotter is a music website that hosts exclusive, specially-recorded live sessions captured at the Horseshack studio in Rock Island, Illinois, most offered as free downloads alongside streaming. Its catalogue leans heavily on indie rock, folk, folk rock, and acoustic acts, with all 40 of its listed releases falling in the 2000s. The operation was run by a small team anchored by Sean Moeller, Patrick Stolley, and Mike Gentry, and expanded into two related imprints — Daytrotter Presents and Daytrotter Session — accumulating over 1,500 Discogs credits across its run.
