ABOUT JACKPOT RECORDS: Jackpot Records is an independent record label based in Portland, Oregon, that focuses on vinyl reissues of classic as well as obscure titles. Care is paid to giving each album the most authentic and true-to-form representation, with recordings taken from the original master tapes whenever possible.

We’ve reissued records by The Meters, The Wipers, Etta James, Dead Boys, Willie Nelson, Devo, Bill Evans, Nirvana, Martin Denny, Mudhoney, Silver Apples & James Brown, to name just a few.

The reissue label started in 2004, but Jackpot Records actually began as an independent record store in 1997 (which is still operating at 3574 SE Hawthorne Blvd in Portland, OR). Jackpot has offered free, all-ages in-store performances, signings, meet-and-greets, and is the go-to spot for vinyl obsessives.

— Discogs label profile, ID 112252

Overview

Jackpot Records is a Portland, Oregon independent label that grew out of a record store opened in 1997 on SE Hawthorne Boulevard. The reissue arm launched in 2004, with an emphasis on sourcing from original master tapes and a catalogue spanning punk, soul, psychedelic rock, and easy listening — touching artists from the Wipers and Dead Boys to Etta James and Martin Denny. Output has expanded sharply decade over decade, from eight releases in the 2000s to more than fifty in the 2020s, with mastering engineers Kevin Gray and Carl Saff among the most consistent contributors across the catalogue.
