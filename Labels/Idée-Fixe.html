Idée Fixe

Toronto-based Canadian independent label. Discogs label ID: 393150. Also known as Idée Fixe Records. Founded 2011 by Alex Durlak and Jeff McMurrich. Specialises in experimental, avant-garde, folk-adjacent, and jazz-inflected music from the Toronto scene and wider Canada.

Overview

Idée Fixe launched in 2011 with its first two releases nearly simultaneously: the self-titled debut by Deloro (IF002) and The Luyas’ Too Beautiful To Work (IF001CD). From the start the label was shaped by two men: co-founder Jeff McMurrich (also a member of Les Mouches and Deepspace (2)), who produces and engineers many of the label’s records and has production credits on 19 releases; and Alex Durlak (member of Kid Sniper and The Connoisseurs (4)), who handles the majority of the artwork and layout, credited across 23 releases.

The label’s catalog number scheme is sequential with an “IF” prefix (IF001–IF048 as of 2025). Most releases are Canadian pressings; a handful are co-credited Canada/USA. Formats include vinyl LP, CD, and cassette.

The roster is rooted in the Toronto indie-experimental milieu. Recurring primary artists include Bry Webb (formerly of The Constantines), Jennifer Castle, Joseph Shabason and Nick Krgovich (often together), The Highest Order, and The Cosmic Range. ROY (101) has become one of the label’s most prolific acts with 10 primary credits.

Key recurring collaborators across the catalog include Harris Newman (mastering, 6 releases), Sandro Perri (mastering/mixing, 4 releases), and Owen Pallett who arranged strings on Jennifer Castle’s Pink City.

Key Artists

Bry Webb — Former frontman of The Constantines. Released Provider (IF004, 2011) and Free Will (IF008, 2014) on the label; produced by McMurrich and Webb jointly. Recurring contributor/backing vocalist on other label releases.

Jennifer Castle — Toronto singer-songwriter. Three studio albums: Pink City (IF009, 2014), Angels Of Death (IF018, 2018), Monarch Season (IF027, 2020). Pink City featured string arrangements by Owen Pallett. Castle also appears as a collaborator across the catalog (backing vocals on Bry Webb’s Provider, contributor to multiple other releases).

Joseph Shabason & Nick Krgovich — Both top primary-credit performers on the label (12 releases each). Collaborated on Philadelphia (IF026, 2020, with Chris Harris (5)), At Scaramouche (IF038, 2022), and Shabason, Krgovich, Sage (IF045, 2024, with Matthew Sage). Shabason also serves as recording/mixing engineer on his own releases.

The Highest Order — Roots-country-inflected Toronto ensemble. Three LPs: Rainbow Of Blues (IF006, 2012), If It’s Real (IF007, 2013), Still Holding (IF013, 2016). Key members include Paul Mortimer (2), Simone TB, Kyle Porter.

The Cosmic Range — Discogs artist ID: 5363419. Experimental/cosmic jazz collective. Two LPs: New Latitudes (IF015, 2015, produced by Jeff McMurrich and Matthew “Doc” Dunn) and The Gratitude Principle (IF019, 2019).

ROY (101) — 10 primary credits including Peace Love And Outer Space (IF024, 2020) and the compilation Roy’s Garage (IF029, 2021), which featured guest vocalists Desiree Das Gupta, Caitlin Woelfle-O’Brien, and Sam Maloney (2), plus guest musicians Robin Hatch (keyboards) and others.

Absolutely Free — Toronto electronic/krautrock-influenced trio. Geneva Freeport (IF022, 2019).

Mauno (2) — Two LPs: Rough Master (IF014, 2016) and Tuning (IF017, 2017, co-pressed with USA).
