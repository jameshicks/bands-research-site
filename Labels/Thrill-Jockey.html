Thrill Jockey

American independent record label. Discogs ID: 500. Founded 1992 in New York City by Bettina Richards (former Atlantic Records A&R representative). In 1995 Richards relocated the label to Chicago, Illinois, where it has remained. Mailing address: PO Box 08038, Chicago IL 60608. Also maintains a Berlin office.

Primary identity: post-rock, experimental, electronic, alt-country, free jazz, and adjacent underground music. The label’s early Chicago years were closely tied to the post-rock scene centered on Tortoise, The Sea And Cake, and Isotope 217, but the roster has always been broader — spanning German electronic acts (Oval, Mouse On Mars, Microstoria), alt-country (Freakwater), and noise/metal in later years.

Sublabel: Thrill Jockey Records (ID 33968) — used for selected releases.

History

New York Origins (1992–1994)

Bettina Richards launched Thrill Jockey in New York in 1992 with a debut catalog that led with Midwest punk and indie rock acts: H.P. Zinker (Perseverance, THRILL 001, 1992), Gaunt (Whitey The Man, THRILL 002, 1992), and Gorilla (2) (Deal With It, THRILL 003, 1993). The label pivoted quickly to the Chicago experimental scene, signing Tortoise for their Lonesome Sound EP (THRILL 006, 1993) and Freakwater (Feels Like The Third Time, THRILL 010, 1993), establishing its two primary early aesthetic poles: post-rock and Americana.

Chicago Move and Post-Rock Ascendancy (1995–2001)

After relocating to Chicago in 1995, Thrill Jockey became the de facto home label for the city’s experimental music community. Key releases in this period:

  • Tortoise — Tortoise (THRILL 013, 1994) and all subsequent albums (except Standards, which went to Warp)
  • The Sea And Cake — signed from the start; debuted with THRILL 016 (1994)
  • Trans Am (Bethesda, MD) — joined with their self-titled debut (THRILL 024, 1995)
  • Microstoria — Init Ding (THRILL 031, 1995); German electronic duo (Jan St. Werner / Oval member)
  • Isotope 217 — The Unstable Molecule (THRILL 049, 1997)
  • Sam Prekop — self-titled solo debut (THRILL 061, 1999)
  • Brokeback — Field Recordings From The Cook County Water Table (THRILL 070, 1999)
  • Pullman — Turnstyles & Junkpiles (THRILL 055, 1998)
  • Oval — catalog from 94 Diskont onward (THRILL 036, 1996); German glitch/electronic
  • Mouse On Mars — catalog from Autoditacker (THRILL 045, 1997); German electronic duo

Also in this era: Bobby Conn, The Lonesome Organist, A Minor Forest, Eleventh Dream Day, Chicago Underground Duo, and Directions (Bundy K. Brown, THRILL 033, 1996).

Broadening the Catalog (2001–2010)

The label expanded genre-wise while retaining its experimental core: Giant Sand (Howe Gelb), Tom Verlaine (Television), The Fiery Furnaces, Future Islands (early catalog), Califone, Matmos, OOIOO (Yoshimi, Boredoms), and Colleen.

Noise, Metal, and Further Expansion (2010–present)

By the early 2010s Thrill Jockey had incorporated heavier sounds: Lightning Bolt, The Body (3), Sumac, Wrekmeister Harmonies, and Liturgy (2). Simultaneously continued releasing folk guitarists (Glenn Jones, Marisa Anderson, Jack Rose, William Tyler) and electronic/ambient work (Emptyset, Moor Mother). Remains active as of 2026.
