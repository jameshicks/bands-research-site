Ediesta Records

UK independent record label; sublabel of Red Rhino Records. Discogs label ID: 11180.

Overview

Sublabel of Red Rhino Records, the York/Leeds-based indie distributor and label central to the Northern UK post-punk and goth scenes. Released Pink Peg Slax’s second LP 12 Songs Never Recorded By Frank Sinatra in 1987 (CALCLP 027).
