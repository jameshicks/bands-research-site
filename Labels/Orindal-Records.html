Overview

Orindal Records is a Chicago-based independent label co-run by brothers Owen Ashworth and Gordon Wilson Ashworth, with distribution through The Business (2). Active since 2011, it has accumulated a steady catalogue of around 68 releases split nearly evenly across the 2010s and 2020s, skewing toward indie folk, lo-fi, and experimental sounds. Owen Ashworth releases his own music on the label as Advance Base alongside outside artists including Karima Walker and Gia Margaret.
