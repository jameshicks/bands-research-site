Twisted Nerve

Manchester-based independent record label. Discogs label ID: 2287. Parent label: Finders Keepers Records.

Released PlanningToRock’s debut, Topics On A Foreign Mind (2004; catalog TN I 004). Also home to artists including Badly Drawn Boy (early releases) and others in the Manchester indie/experimental scene. Catalog runs to 421 releases in the Discogs database.
