Overview

Popfrenzy Records is an independent label based in Sydney, Australia, operating since 2004 and previously known as Gifted Records. Its catalogue runs heaviest in the 2000s (77 releases) and continues into the 2010s (48 releases), spanning indie rock, alternative rock, experimental, lo-fi, and garage rock. Despite its Australian base, the label’s roster includes prominent UK acts: Wire members Colin Newman, Graham Lewis, and Matthew Simms appear alongside Alasdair MacLean and Mel Draisey of The Clientele, giving the label an outsized connection to British post-punk and indie circles.
