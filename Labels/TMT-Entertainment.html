TMT Entertainment

Discogs ID: 1800
Origin: Iceland (Reykjavík)
Parent label: Thule Musik (Discogs ID: 480237)
Active: 1999–2003 (primary catalog); scattered later reissues
Genres: Electronic, Experimental, IDM, Electro, Synth-pop

Icelandic independent imprint operating under the Thule Musik umbrella (Eikjuvogur 29, 104 Reykjavík). TMT Entertainment was the electronic/experimental wing of the Thule Musik group in the late 1990s and early 2000s, functioning as one of the earliest domestic homes for the Reykjavík electronic scene. The label is best known as the original home of múm and as the launching pad for Apparat Organ Quartet and Trabant.

Overview

TMT Entertainment emerged at a moment when Iceland’s experimental electronic scene was consolidating around a loose network of Reykjavík musicians. The label opened its catalog in 1999 with múm’s debut 12” and then their first album, before expanding to cover a range of related acts — from Trabant’s electro-influenced material to Apparat Organ Quartet’s Krautrock-adjacent organ minimalism. Most releases were manufactured in Iceland and pressed at MPO (the French pressing plant), distributed primarily domestically and through European channels under the Thule Musik group structure.

Thule Musik’s other sublabels during this period included Thule Records, Thule Traks Records, Uni:form Recordings, 66 Degrees Records, Plast Trax, Tissju, and Kitchen Motors — forming a cluster of imprints that collectively covered the range of Icelandic electronic, dance, and experimental output in the era.

The catalog runs from TMT01 (1999) through approximately TMT15 (2002), with a 2023 compilation (Alsæla, TMT15) appearing as an apparent revival. Catalog numbers largely follow a TMT##CD convention for CDs and TMT## for vinyl.

Key Releases in Detail

múm — The Ballað Of The Broken Birdie Records (TMT01, 1999)

Discogs ID: 205996. 12” vinyl. The label’s first release. Five-track 12” built around the title track from what would become the debut album, with remixes by Biogen, Ruxpin, and Ilo. Lacquer cut by Loop-o at D&M.

múm — Yesterday Was Dramatic - Today Is OK (TMT02CD, 1999)

Discogs ID: 7354. CD album, 10 tracks. Recorded and mixed by Finnur Björnsson and Finnur Hákonarson, along with múm themselves. Artwork by Arnaldur Hilmisson and the band. Orchestral guests on “Awake On A Train” include Hildur Guðnadóttir and Gróa Margrét Valdimarsdóttir (strings), Eiríkur Orri Ólafsson (trumpet), and Stefán Már Magnússon (guitar); Helga Björg Arnardóttir plays clarinet on two tracks. Genres: Electronic / Experimental, Glitch, IDM, Synth-pop. Pressed at MPO (matrix: TMT 02 CD MPO 01 @@ 4 03).

The album received a vinyl repress as TMT02LP in 2001 and two further Icelandic pressings in 2005 (cat# “none”).

múm — Remixed (TMT04CD, 2001)

Discogs ID: 109178. CD album, 9 tracks. Remix album drawing on Yesterday Was Dramatic, with contributions from: Bix, Ruxpin (two remixes), Ilo, Traktor (3), µ-Ziq (two remixes), Biogen, and El Hombre Trajeado. Written-by credits confirm the four-person múm lineup: Gunnar Örn Tynes, Gyða Valtýsdóttir, Kristín Anna Valtýsdóttir, Örvar Þóreyjarson Smárason. A parallel pressing exists as TMT 03 (Discogs ID: 254084).

Trabant — Moment Of Truth (TMT08CD, 2001)

Discogs ID: 237747. CD album, 15 tracks. Trabant’s debut full-length on the label. Electro/IDM/Leftfield. Members included Ragnar Kjartansson, Viðar Hákon Gislason, and Þorvaldur H. Gröndal (who also appears in Apparat Organ Quartet). No additional personnel credited in Discogs.

Apparat Organ Quartet — Apparat Organ Quartet (TMT11CD, 2002)

Discogs ID: 187231. CD album, 9 tracks. Four-organ-and-drums ensemble featuring Jóhann Jóhannsson (organ, producer, engineer), Hörður Bragason (organ), Sighvatur Ómar Kristinsson (organ), Úlfur Eldjárn (organ), and Arnar Geir Ómarsson (drums). Engineers: Aron Arnarsson, Finnur Björnsson, Viðar Hákon Gislason. Mastered by Axel “Flex” Árnason. Vocals on tracks 3 and 6 by Adda Ingólfsdóttir. Genres: Electronic, Rock / Alternative Rock, Electro, Krautrock, Leftfield, Synth-pop. A 2022 repress appeared on FROG 192-1 (different label).

Ampop — Made For Market (TMT13CD, 2002)

Discogs ID: 261591. CD album, 9 tracks. Written and produced by Birgir Hilmarsson and Kjartan F. Ólafsson. Guest guitar by Nói Steinn Einarsson and Stafrænn Hákon. Ambient/Downtempo/IDM/Synth-pop.

Worm Is Green — Automagic (TMT12CD, 2003)

Discogs ID: 198668. CD album, 12 tracks. Produced by Árni Teitur Ásgeirsson, Bjarni Thor Hannesson, and Thorsteinn Hannesson. Guest vocals on “Shine” by Ragnar Kjartansson (also in Trabant). Mixed and mastered by Equalizer Axel.

Recurring Personnel

Several names recur across the label’s catalog as engineers and producers, pointing to a tight studio network:

  • Finnur Björnsson — recorded and mixed múm’s debut; engineered the Apparat Organ Quartet album
  • Viðar Hákon Gislason — Trabant member; also engineered and mixed the Apparat Organ Quartet album
  • Jóhann Jóhannsson — organ, producer, and engineer on Apparat Organ Quartet
  • Ragnar Kjartansson — Trabant member; guest vocalist on Worm Is Green’s Automagic
  • Þorvaldur H. Gröndal — appears in both Trabant and Apparat Organ Quartet

Connections

  • Thule Musik — parent company; address: Eikjuvogur 29, 104 Reykjavík. Other sublabels include Thule Records, Kitchen Motors, Uni:form Recordings, 66 Degrees Records, Plast Trax, Tissju, Thule Traks Records, Dizorder Recordings, Strobelight Network, OZ Interactive Inc.
  • múm — the label’s flagship act; departed to FatCat Records after the TMT period
  • Apparat Organ Quartet — later associated with Kitchen Motors (also a Thule Musik sublabel)
  • Jóhann Jóhannsson — member of Apparat Organ Quartet; his solo career went through multiple other labels
