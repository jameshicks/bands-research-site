Overview

Black Water is a Portland, Oregon record label and distribution operation founded in 1999 by Keith Testerman, taking its name from an early-1980s track by Japanese punk band Execute. The label concentrates on punk, hardcore, crust, and thrash, with a catalogue spanning 2003 through the 2020s — 25 releases in the 2000s, 44 in the 2010s, and 35 so far in the 2020s. Beyond the catalogue, Black Water operates a physical record store and, since 2025, an all-ages venue, restaurant, and bar at 5115 NE Sandy Blvd that functions as a node for Portland’s underground scene. Its sublabel Red Lantern Studios and roster regulars including Hellshock and Long Knife anchor its Pacific Northwest identity.
