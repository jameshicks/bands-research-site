Overview

Cherry Red Films is the video and DVD division of Cherry Red Records, established in March 1982. The legal entity was dissolved in 1992, but the imprint continued operating under the Cherry Red umbrella and accounts for the vast majority of its output in the 2000s, when it issued 83 releases — a dramatic expansion from the handful of VHS titles it released in the 1980s. Its catalogue runs heavily toward punk and new wave, reflecting the parent label’s roots, with additional coverage of psychobilly, indie rock, and hard rock. Roster credits include Buzzcocks, Howard Devoto, designer Malcolm Garrett, and producer John A. Rivers.
