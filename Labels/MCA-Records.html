MCA Records

Discogs ID: 878
From: United States (UK division active)
Active: 1960s–2003 (absorbed into Geffen/Interscope/Universal)

Major US record label with active UK division. Signed Chakk in 1985 following the indie chart success of their Fon Records singles; released 10 Days In An Elevator (MCG 6006, 1986). The size of the advance was “something of a legend” — sufficient to fund construction of FON Studios in Sheffield. Chakk left after the album failed to meet commercial targets.
