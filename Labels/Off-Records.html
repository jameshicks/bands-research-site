Overview

Off Records is an independent label operated by Chris Slusarenko, launched in 2001 and active mainly through the 2000s with seven releases that decade and two more in the 2010s. The catalogue sits at the intersection of indie rock, lo-fi, and avant-garde art rock, and the roster reflects Slusarenko’s wider musical circle — it includes Robert Pollard, Richard Meltzer, Jeff Stuart Saltzman, and Phantom Tollbooth alongside Slusarenko himself.

Notes (web)

Richard Meltzer on Off Records — the Tropic of Nipples project

Richard Meltzer moved to Portland, Oregon in the 1990s (Wikipedia, Richard Meltzer, as of 2026-11-04), placing him in the same city as Chris Slusarenko and his label. Slusarenko is a Portland-based musician and entrepreneur — a member of Guided By Voices and Boston Spaceships, founder of Off Records, and longtime operator of Clinton Street Video in Portland (Discogs artist page).

Slusarenko’s label connection to Robert Pollard predates the Meltzer project: Slusarenko met Pollard after a Guided by Voices show in the mid-1990s. Off Records launched in 2001. The two 2002 “Tropic of Nipples” releases were conceived by Slusarenko specifically as a pairing of Pollard with unlikely collaborators — and Meltzer, as a legendary rock critic who had also done musical work (fronting the LA punk band VOM in 1977–78 and writing lyrics for Buck Dharma / Blue Öyster Cult), was his chosen foil.

Slusarenko explained his role directly: “I just asked if they wanted to work on something together since they admired each others work so much and then I cut and spliced and fucked with the whole thing.” — he functioned as architect and editor rather than performer, sequencing the tracks as a poetic duel between Meltzer and Pollard, with Meltzer backed by experimental noise group Smegma. (The Constant Bleeder, “Robert Pollard-Mania! #67: The Tropic of Nipples EP”, 2021-02-15)

The expanded CD release (The Completed Soundtrack For The Tropic Of Nipples, OFF 998) further foregrounded Meltzer by including five tracks from his 1970s VOM recordings alongside new material. The Constant Bleeder notes: “The real auteur here looks to be Off Records founder Chris Slusarenko.” (The Constant Bleeder, chris-slusarenko tag)

In short: Meltzer’s route onto Off Records was geographic proximity (both Portland-based), Slusarenko’s pre-existing relationship with Pollard, and Slusarenko’s knowledge that the two men admired each other’s work. Slusarenko initiated the collaboration and shaped the finished releases.
