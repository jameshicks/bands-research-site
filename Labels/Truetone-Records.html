Overview

Truetone Records was an Australian independent label founded by Michael Crawley in late 1985, operating under the company entity True Tone with a separate publishing arm, True Tone Music. Its catalogue is concentrated almost entirely in the 1980s, with 35 releases in that decade versus 7 in the 1990s, spanning indie rock, alternative rock, and synth-pop. The label launched with early releases by GANGgajang, The Go-Betweens, and The Rockmelons, later expanding its roster to include Ed Kuepper and Stephen Cummings — a lineup that reads as a thoughtful cross-section of Australian indie and post-punk. Distribution originally ran through PolyGram, shifted to EMI between 1987 and 1990, then returned to PolyGram.
