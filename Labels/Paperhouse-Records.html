Overview

Paperhouse Records was a short-lived UK indie label, a joint venture between David E. Barker of Glass Records and Clive Solomon of Fire Records, operating under the Fire Records umbrella during the 1990s. Its name was drawn from “Paperhouse,” the opening track on Can’s 1971 album Tago Mago — a nod to the adventurous, left-of-centre sensibility that would define its catalogue. All 40 of its releases came in that single decade, spanning indie rock, indie pop, lo-fi, and experimental styles. The roster brought together a distinctive mix of UK indie acts and US underground figures, including Teenage Fanclub, The Pastels, Jad Fair, and Don Fleming.
