Blanco Y Negro

UK record label. Discogs label ID: 9626. Parent: Warner Music UK Ltd. (ID: 22328)

Overview

Blanco Y Negro was a WEA sublabel active from 1985 through the late 1990s that served as the UK home for The Jesus and Mary Chain’s entire core catalog. The label was run by Mike Alway. Beyond JAMC, it released records by other artists in the UK alternative and indie spectrum.

The label issued 2,576 releases on Discogs and is distinct from the Spanish Blanco Y Negro (2) imprint (ID: 1967).
