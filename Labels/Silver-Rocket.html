Overview

Silver Rocket is a Prague-based independent label active since 1997, operating in alternative rock, lo-fi, indie rock, noise, and punk. Its catalogue grew steadily through the 2000s and reached its densest period in the 2010s, accumulating over 47 releases across nearly three decades. The label operates a sublabel called Sekta and has built a roster that includes Ondřej Ježek, Yoz, Aran Epochal, Bourek, and Lyssa.
