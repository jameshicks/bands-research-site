Overview

El Segell Del Primavera was the record-label arm of Primavera Sound, S.L., the Barcelona company that runs the Primavera Sound festival. It operated from 2013 and was rebranded in October 2018 as El Segell, with Discogs directing catalogue queries to that successor entry. Its roughly 39 releases are heavily concentrated in the 2010s and span indie pop, indie rock, folk and post-rock, with roster artists including Joana Serrat and Núria Graham. The presence of Montreal-based producer Howard Bilerman on the roster suggests at least one internationally sourced signing alongside the label’s predominantly Spanish and Catalan acts.
