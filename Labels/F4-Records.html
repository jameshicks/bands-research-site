Overview

F4 Records was the fourth and final iteration of Tony Wilson’s Factory Records lineage, launched in Manchester in 2004 and active until the end of 2006. Built around three core acts — The Durutti Column, Raw-T, and The Young Offenders Institute — the label bridged post-punk experimentalism with grime and indie rock across a small catalogue of three releases. Its numbering system was distinctively local, pairing a serial “R” prefix with the Manchester postal code of each artist, anchoring every release to a specific district of the city.
