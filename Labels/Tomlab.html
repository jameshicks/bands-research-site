Overview

Tomlab was an independent record label founded by Tom Steinle in Cologne at the end of 1997, later relocating to Berlin. Its catalogue of roughly 80 releases skews heavily toward the 2000s and spans indie rock, experimental, folk, leftfield, and synth-pop. The label built an unexpectedly international roster — signing UK artists Patrick Wolf and Jherek Bischoff alongside US acts Casiotone for the Painfully Alone and Owen Ashworth — and ran two named singles series, the Alphabet Singles Series and the Umlaut Singles Series.
