Chrysalis

UK independent label. Founded 1968 by Chris Wright and Terry Ellis. Discogs ID: 3198.

Adele Bertei released solo material on Chrysalis 1985–1988: “When It’s Over” / “Every Little Bit Hurts” (Chrysalis CHS 2907, 1985, produced by David Gamson and Fred Maher); and her debut LP Little Lives (Chrysalis FV/FVT/VK 41634, 1988). The Jellybean single “Just a Mirage” (UK #13, 1988) was also on Chrysalis. Sold to EMI in 1991; later passed to Warner Music Group; relaunched independently from 2020.
