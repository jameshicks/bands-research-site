Cooking Vinyl

Origin: London, UK
Active: 1986–present
Label Code: LC 7180 / LC 07180
Parent (corporate): Exceleration Music Partners, LLC (acquired June 2025)
Address (current): PO Box 1845, London W3 0ZA

Overview

Cooking Vinyl is a British independent record label incorporated on 11 August 1986. It was originally managed by Pete Lawrence and operated out of west London. The label began by championing British folk-rock and roots acts — most notably Oysterband and Michelle Shocked — before expanding across two decades into post-punk, singer-songwriter, electronic, and mainstream rock territory. By the 2020s it had become one of the UK’s largest genuinely independent labels, with a back catalogue of over 5,600 releases across several decades and sublabels.

In June 2025 the label’s holding company, Cooking Vinyl Ltd., was acquired by US indie group Exceleration Music Partners, LLC (which also owns Alligator Records, Kill Rock Stars, SideOneDummy, and others).

Founding and Early Years (1986–1991)

The label launched in 1986 with a 7” flexi sampler (cat. ROOTS 01) featuring Oysterband, Michelle Shocked, Rory McLeod, and The Mekons — an accurate snapshot of its founding aesthetic: British folk-rock, American roots music, and left-leaning independent spirit.

Pete Lawrence (Discogs artist ID: 33631) is credited on the label’s earliest releases as recorder and sleeve-notes writer — his name appears on the 1986 UK pressing of Michelle Shocked’s The Texas Campfire Tapes (COOK 011 area / FRY series). The label ran two main catalogue series in this period: the COOK prefix for LPs/CDs and the FRY prefix for singles.

Key founding-era signings included:

  • Oysterband (Discogs ID: 89461) — first release Hal-An-Tow (FRY 001, 1986); remained on the label until the 2020s (164 Cooking Vinyl-era releases)
  • Michelle Shocked — The Texas Campfire Tapes (1986, recorded by Pete Lawrence at a campfire in Texas), followed by Short Sharp Shocked (1988), Captain Swing (1989), and Arkansas Traveler (1992) before leaving for Mercury
  • Cowboy Junkies — The Trinity Session (COOK 011, 1988), a landmark ambient country album recorded in one session at the Church of the Holy Trinity, Toronto; produced by Peter Moore
  • The Mekons — appeared on the 1986 sampler; released several albums through the label’s early years
  • Tom Robinson — signed late 1992 (War Baby, FRY C022; Living In A Boom Time)
  • The Ukrainians — Ukrainian folk/rock band, debuted 1991 with Oi Divchino (FRY 019T) and Pisni Iz The Smiths (Smiths songs in Ukrainian, FRY CD 023, 1992)
  • June Tabor and Edward The Second And The Red Hot Polkas — folk stalwarts from the label’s early folk-world music focus
  • Sweet Honey In The Rock, Boiled In Lead, Bruce Cockburn, Bhundu Boys, and African roots acts including The Four Brothers, Machanic Manyeruke, and S.E. Rogie

The early Cooking Vinyl aesthetic was defined by its connection to the British folk revival, world music, and the American singer-songwriter tradition. Designer John Liepins handled sleeve work on several early releases (including Step Outside).

Mid-Period Expansion (1992–2002)

Through the 1990s the label diversified its roster significantly while retaining its folk and roots core. Key developments:

Billy Bragg (Discogs ID: 83242) became the label’s most commercially significant artist during this period. The Cooking Vinyl pressings of his catalogue (beginning with back-catalogue reissues and William Bloke, 1996) gave him a home after Go! Discs. He remains the label’s longest-standing and most-released artist with 164 releases. Producer Grant Showbiz (ID: 249748) — who recurs as one of the label’s most active producers (64 release credits) — worked extensively with Bragg. Don’t Try This At Home (1991, COOK CD 062) featured appearances by Johnny Marr, Michael Stipe, Kirsty MacColl, and Peter Buck.

Ani DiFranco (Discogs ID: 11442) — appeared on the label 1996–1999 for UK/European releases of her Righteous Babe albums, including Dilate (1996).

XTC — the Swindon art-pop group signed with Cooking Vinyl for their post-Virgin output, releasing Apple Venus Volume 1 (COOK CD 172, 1999) and Wasp Star (Apple Venus Volume 2) (2000), plus the BBC sessions box set Transistor Blast (COOKCD 152, 1998). XTC’s chief songwriters Andy Partridge and Colin Moulding both appear extensively as writers in the label’s credits.

The Wedding Present (ID: 243285) — appeared on the label 1996–2010, including Saturnalia (1996).

Pere Ubu / David Thomas (2) — significant long-term relationship; David Thomas appears as one of the label’s top “other” credited contributors with 63 release credits.

Ryan Adams — Heartbreaker (2000) and Gold (2001) were released on the label in the UK.

Pixies — catalog reissues beginning 2002 (COOKCD 234 and related), including the compilation Pixies (2002), making Cooking Vinyl the UK home for the Pixies back catalogue.

Frank Black / Frank Black And The Catholics (IDs: 22376, 274749) — Black Francis solo and Catholics recordings on the label from 2000 onward.

2000s: Post-Punk and Rock Expansion

The mid-2000s saw Cooking Vinyl extend into post-punk revival, alternative rock, and electronic:

  • Echo & The Bunnymen (ID: 45115) — on the label 2001–2008; key production credit from Ian McCulloch and Will Sergeant, who also acted as producers (30 and 29 release credits respectively in the label’s catalogue)
  • The Orb (ID: 54286) — joined the label in 2004; became the second-most-released artist on the label with 108 releases running through 2025
  • Killing Joke (ID: 13408) — catalog from 2005, including Pandemonium (COOKCD 344, 2005) and XXV Gathering: Let Us Prey (COOKCD 358, 2005)
  • They Might Be Giants — on the label 1998–2004
  • Ron Sexsmith — long-running relationship from 2001 to 2025
  • Janis Ian — 2003–2006
  • Hayseed Dixie — 2004–2010
  • Richard Thompson — 2003–2006
  • Camper Van Beethoven — 2002–2025
  • The Charlatans — 2008–2016
  • Dropkick Murphys — 2007–2015

Producer Stephen Street (ID: 85779) — known for his work with The Smiths and Blur — appears as one of the label’s most active producers with 45 release credits. Electronic acts Youth (ID: 2656) and The Prodigy also feature in this period.

2010s–Present: Mainstream Reach

By the 2010s Cooking Vinyl had signed a roster spanning mainstream rock to pop-adjacent acts:

  • Marilyn Manson (ID: 12208) — Born Villain (COOKCD 554, 2012) and associated releases; produced by Manson himself (credited as Marilyn Manson (2), ID: 392594)
  • Alison Moyet (ID: 19674) — The Minutes (COOKCD 585, 2013), her first album in 17 years; long-running relationship through 2025
  • Suzanne Vega (ID: 41182) — joined 2010 for the Close-Up acoustic series (Vols. 1–4); continues through 2025 with 86 releases on the label
  • The Cult (ID: 15266) — from 2012
  • The Darkness (ID: 154624) — 2017–present; one of the label’s most active current acts (69 releases)
  • The Prodigy (ID: 66852) — catalog releases 2008–2023
  • Sophie Ellis-Bextor (ID: 37594) — 2018–present (51 releases)
  • The Psychedelic Furs (ID: 125410) — Made Of Rain (2020, COOK LP/CD), their first studio album in 29 years; produced by Richard Fortus, David Maurice, and the band themselves; personnel included Tim Butler (bass), Paul Garisto (drums), Mars Williams (saxophone), and Richard Butler (vocals)
  • The Proclaimers — 2012–2022
  • Roger Waters — catalog 2023–2025
  • Will Young — 2019–2021
  • Gogol Bordello — 2016–2025
  • Babymetal — 2021–2025

Connections

  • The Psychedelic Furs — Made Of Rain (2020); see Made Of Rain
  • Billy Bragg — flagship artist; produced primarily by Grant Showbiz
  • Pete Lawrence — founding manager (also associated with Big Chill Recordings under alias Chilled By Nature)

  • Cactus Design / Andrew Swainson — primary visual identity of the label across decades

Open Questions

  • Exact founding circumstances: who cofounded the label alongside Pete Lawrence, and when did Lawrence depart? The Discogs profile says “formerly managed by Pete Lawrence” but gives no date.
  • Precise timeline of when Cooking Vinyl shifted from folk/roots to broader rock/pop (internal A&R changes around mid-1990s to early 2000s).
  • The relationship with The Cartel distribution (appears as co-label on the 1986 flexi ROOTS 01) — was The Cartel Cooking Vinyl’s original distributor?
  • Whether there was a separate US licensing deal for early releases (some early Michelle Shocked releases appear on Mercury/Polydor in the US simultaneously).
  • Current status post-Exceleration acquisition (June 2025): roster and A&R changes unknown.
