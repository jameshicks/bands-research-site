Jemp Records

Band-owned record label of Phish, formed in 2005 in Burlington, Vermont. Also stylized as JEMP Records. Discogs label ID: 72582.

Note: JEMP Records has no connection to Jerry Garcia or the Grateful Dead. Some Discogs release entries appear to carry an incorrect “Jemp Records” label credit on Garcia-related releases — this is a data error. Garcia’s posthumous archival releases were issued on Jerry Made, Round Records, and ATO Records. (Confirmed via web research 2025-07-15: Wikipedia: Jerry Garcia discography; jerrygarcia.com)

The label serves as the primary home for all Phish output from 2005 onward — new studio albums, archival live releases, and reissues of the full back catalog originally released on Elektra. It also functions as the distribution vehicle for the Livephish.com digital download series and the physical LP-on-LP vinyl subscription program. The name “JEMP” is derived from the initials of the four band members: Jon (Fishman), (Trey) Anastasio, Mike (Gordon), and Page (McConnell) — with the E standing for (Trey) Anastasio’s given name, Ernest.

Catalog Overview

  • Founded: 2005 (Burlington, Vermont)
  • Discogs releases: 237 unique masters / 38 unique titles at master level
  • Year range: 2005–2026 (scheduled) on Discogs
  • Catalog number scheme: JEMP#### sequential (e.g. JEMP1002, JEMP1051 through JEMP1115+); some non-JEMP-prefixed catalog numbers for Mike Gordon solo (MG-CU03), Trey/Page EPs (cat# 1099, JEMP1109), and early Livephish downloads (catno: “none”)
  • Parent label: None (independent)
  • Sublabels: None on Discogs

Live and Archive Program

Jemp is the label home for Phish’s extensive archival live release program, distributed via the Livephish.com platform. Releases span:

  • Earliest archive: 1993 (NYE Worcester, sched. 2026)
  • LP on LP series: Subscription vinyl program launched 2021, pressing extended jams to vinyl for the first time. Releases include LP On LP 01: “Ruby Waves” 7/14/19 (JEMP1096, 2021) and LP On LP 02: “Waves” 5/26/11 (JEMP1098, 2021)
  • Early milestone live releases: New Year’s Eve 1995 – Live At Madison Square Garden (R2 73275, 2005), Vegas 96 (JEMP1002, 2007)
  • Livephish Download series: Dozens of show-specific download releases (catno: “none”) dating from 2007–2012 covering 1993–2010 shows

The live catalog covers roughly 1993–2019 on physical media, with the download series extending further.

  • John Siket — recurring recording/mixing engineer

  • Paul Languedoc — Phish’s long-time sound engineer; recorded by credit on live releases

  • Ben Collette — engineering and post-production on 55+ releases

Connections

  • Phish — sole band owner; primary artist on 233 of 237 releases
