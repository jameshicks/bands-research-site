Sargent House

Discogs ID: 116554 Founded: June 2006 Founder: Cathy Pellow Based: Los Angeles, California Type: Management company and record label Catalog prefix: SH-### (primary); occasional variant prefixes (SHRC, PRC-SH, etc.) Parent: none; no sublabels

American management company and record label founded in Los Angeles in June 2006 by Cathy Pellow. Operates simultaneously as an artist management firm and a vinyl/CD imprint. Primary genre identity: post-metal, post-rock, noise rock, math rock, progressive metal, doom — with notable outliers in folk/spiritual (Woven Hand), art-pop (How To Dress Well), and extreme classical/neo-folk (Lingua Ignota).

As of March 2023, Pellow stepped down from management duties following Mylets (Henry Kohen) posting a video on Reddit alleging widespread abuse at the label. Chelsea Wolfe, Lingua Ignota, and King Woman — all of whom had previously cut ties with the label — released statements on social media alluding to similar negative experiences.

Era Overview

2006–2010 — Founding / Post-Hardcore Base

First full catalog entry: Russian Circles Enter (SH009, 2006, 7 pressings) — though SH had previously released Russian Circles vinyl on a limited basis. SH001 was Maps And Atlases Tree, Swallows, Houses (2007). First-wave signings included RX Bandits, This Town Needs Guns, Red Fang, Good Old War, Tera Melos, and Fang Island. Genre range was broad — indie, post-hardcore, math rock — before the label’s post-metal focus hardened.

Red Sparowes appeared in this period: Aphorisms EP (SH016, 2009) and The Fear Is Excruciating, But Therein Lies The Answer (SH034CD, 2010, with Toshi Kasai recording/mixing and Brian Gardner mastering). The 2010 LP also featured Emma Ruth Rundle as a new member, an early Sargent House-world association for her before her own signing. Red Sparowes’ The Fear Is Excruciating was reissued on vinyl by SH in 2020.

Woven Hand (a.k.a. David Eugene Edwards, ex-16 Horsepower) appeared in the catalog from 2002 (prior catalog folded in); SH’s Woven Hand releases ran through 2025.

2011–2014 — Chelsea Wolfe + Russian Circles Anchor Era

Chelsea Wolfe signed in 2011 (Ἀποκάλυψις, 10 pressings) and quickly became the label’s dominant artist. Russian Circles formalized their SH relationship for studio albums in 2011 (Empros, SH-067, 11 pressings). Deafheaven debuted on SH with Roads To Judah (2011). Boris (3) appeared 2011–2017.

Key moment: Bosnian Rainbows — Omar Rodriguez-Lopez’s post-Mars Volta project — released their debut (2013, 14 pressings) on SH. Russian Circles Memorial (SH 111, 2013, 17 pressings) featured a guest vocal by Chelsea Wolfe; Brandon Curtis produced. Chelsea Wolfe Pain Is Beauty (SH106, 2013, 17 pressings) co-produced by Wolfe, Ben Chisholm, and Lars Stalfors.

Emma Ruth Rundle joined in 2014 (Some Heavy Ocean, SH119, 11 pressings) and became the third pillar alongside Wolfe and Russian Circles.

2015–2019 — Peak Pressings / Hardcore Pivot

Chelsea Wolfe’s Abyss (2015) is the most-pressed SH release at 24 pressings. Emma Ruth Rundle Marked For Death (SH 163, 2016, 17 pressings) and Russian Circles Guidance (SH159, 2016, 13 pressings — produced by Kurt Ballou of Converge) continued strong numbers.

Brutus (23) (Belgian post-metal trio) debuted on SH in 2017 (Burst, SH 182, 7 pressings) and grew into one of the label’s most-pressed newer acts. The Armed appeared from 2019. Chelsea Wolfe Birth Of Violence (SH217, 2019, 13 pressings); Russian Circles Blood Year (SH 215, 2019, 8 pressings).

2020–2023 — Lingua Ignota + Controversy

Lingua Ignota (Kristin Hayter) released Sinner Get Ready (SH253, 2021, 12 pressings) — one of the label’s most critically noted releases of the era. Deafheaven Infinite Granite (SH251, 2021, 14 pressings) produced by Justin Meldal-Johnsen (293885). Brutus Unison Life (2022) at 19 pressings — second-highest pressing count in the catalog.

March 2023: abuse controversy breaks; Pellow steps down from management. Lingua Ignota, Chelsea Wolfe, and King Woman had already left.

2024–present

The Armed remain active on SH. How To Dress Well signed for one album (I Am Toward You, SH285, 2024, 3 pressings). Botch returned (061524, SH297, 2025, 4 pressings). Woven Hand continues.
