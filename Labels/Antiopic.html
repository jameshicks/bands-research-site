Antiopic

American experimental music label. New York City (later Chicago). Discogs label ID: 11925. Website: antiopic.com.

Co-founded by James Elliott (of Ateleia, School Of Seven Bells) and guitarist David Daniell. Active 2002–2011 based on Discogs catalog. The label was home to a cluster of NYC experimental and drone musicians; several Antiopic releases also credit Table of the Elements imprints for distribution or co-release. Sublabels: Allegorical Power Series (ANMP catalog), Antiopic Live Series (ANSI catalog).
