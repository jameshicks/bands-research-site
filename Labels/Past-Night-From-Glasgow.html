Overview

Past Night From Glasgow is a reissue sub-label of Last Night From Glasgow, established in 2020 to handle archival releases from the parent label’s Scottish indie-pop and alternative rock orbit. Its roster is anchored by The Trash Can Sinatras and their associated members — Paul McGeechan, John Douglas, and Colin Cassells — alongside American indie-pop artist Eric Matthews, an outlier that points to the label’s reach beyond its Scottish base. All 56 catalogue entries fall within the 2020s, making it a purely retrospective imprint with no original releases.
