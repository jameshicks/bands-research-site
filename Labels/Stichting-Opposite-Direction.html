Stichting Opposite Direction

Dutch foundation (stichting = foundation). Based in Nootdorp, Netherlands. Discogs label ID: 1930675. Active 2010s–2020s. Dedicated to preserving the legacy of Adrian Borland and The Sound (2).

Contact: Willem Pijperstraat 1, 2631 BG Nootdorp, The Netherlands. Run by Jean-Paul van Mierlo (Discogs artist ID: 5072983).

Overview

Stichting Opposite Direction is a Dutch non-profit foundation that handles archival and posthumous releases by Adrian Borland and his associated acts — The Sound (2), Adrian Borland & The Citizens, and White Rose Transmission. All catalog releases are co-produced with Sounds Haarlem Likes Vinyl (Discogs label ID: 633657), which handles physical manufacture and distribution under the SHL catalog numbering system. The foundation operates two internal series under its umbrella:

  • AB Series (Discogs sublabel ID: 1937591) — Adrian Borland-related releases
  • MJR Series (Discogs sublabel ID: 1930674) — purpose not documented in DB; no releases logged under this sublabel ID separately

The foundation’s catalog numbers follow two tracks: releases with SOD’s own “AB series 00X” numbering, and releases that carry only the SHLV/SHLCD numbering from Sounds Haarlem Likes Vinyl when the co-release cat# is shared. Jean-Paul van Mierlo is credited on multiple releases as concept originator and sleeve designer.

Key Releases

Will And Testament — The Sound (2) (2021/2022)

A posthumous archival collection of The Sound (2). Released first as a 2×CD (SHLCD 042, 2021), then as a 2×LP special edition (AB series 006 / SHLV042, 2022). Personnel documented on the vinyl pressing: Adrian Borland (vocals, guitar), Graham Bailey (bass), Michael Dudley (drums), Colvin Mayers (keyboards), Reinier Rietveld (engineer, tapes digitized), Stefan Bornhorst (mastered by). Jean-Paul van Mierlo and Mike Dudley share concept credit. The 2×CD pressing carries 20 tracks across two discs including four demo versions.

2 Meter Sessions 1987–1995 — Adrian Borland (2021)

CD compilation (AB series 007 / SHLCD 043) drawing from Dutch radio sessions. Covers three configurations: The Sound (2) (tracks 1–2 with Max Mayers on keyboards), Adrian Borland solo/acoustic (tracks 3–13 with Wouter Planteijdt on guitar), and Adrian Borland & The Citizens (tracks 14–16). Remastered by Reinier Rietveld; sleeve by Jean-Paul van Mierlo; liner notes by Mark Ritsema (2).

Walking In The Opposite Direction — Adrian Borland (2021)

DVD documentary (AB Series 005 / SHLDVD 039), 96 minutes. No additional personnel credits in DB.

5:00 AM — Adrian Borland (2022)

2×LP (SHLV050) and CD (SHLCD50). Produced by Adrian Borland. Co-produced and engineered by Tim Smith (credited also for drum programming and backing vocals on “I’m Your Freedom”). Other contributors: Pat Rowles (bass, backing vocals), Don Alexis (drums), Diana Overton (layout, cover art), Michael O’Gorman (trumpet, flugelhorn).

700 Miles Of Desert — White Rose Transmission (2024)

2×LP (AB series 013 / SHLV076). Fourteen tracks; most written by Adrian Borland. Two tracks have separate credits to Florian Bratmann (music/programming) and Carlo Van Putten (lyrics). Marks the foundation’s first release outside the Borland solo / The Sound catalogue proper.

Alexandria — Adrian Borland & The Citizens (2024)

LP (SHLV068) and CD (SHLCD068). Released as a Record Store Day limited edition. Full orchestral production: co-produced and engineered by Nick Robbins; strings arranged by John Metcalfe; mixed by Pat Collier (select tracks); remastered by Stefan Bornhorst. Sleeve by Jean-Paul van Mierlo.

Co-Release Partner

Sounds Haarlem Likes Vinyl (Discogs ID: 633657) — Haarlem-based Dutch vinyl/CD label. All nine Discogs-listed releases carry both labels. Catalog numbering follows SHL’s scheme (SHLV = vinyl, SHLCD = CD, SHLDVD = DVD); SOD adds a parallel “AB series” number for Adrian Borland-focused releases.
