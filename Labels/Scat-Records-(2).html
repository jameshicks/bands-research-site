Overview

Scat Records is a Cleveland-based independent label founded in January 1989 by Robert Griffin, guitarist of Prisonshake. It peaked in the 1990s with 32 releases concentrated in indie rock, lo-fi, and garage rock, making it a significant platform for acts including Guided By Voices, Robert Pollard, Tobin Sprout, and Jim Pollard. The label relocated to St. Louis in 1995 before returning to Cleveland in late 2022, and has continued releasing material into the 2020s with a modest second wave of output.
