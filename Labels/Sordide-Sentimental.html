Sordide Sentimental

French post-punk independent label, based in Rouen. Co-founded by Jean-Pierre Turmel.

Overview

One of the most significant French independent labels of the post-punk era, best known internationally for releasing Joy Division’s Atmosphere / Dead Souls 7” in 1980 (catalog: SxS 6). Operated roughly in parallel with L’Invitation Au Suicide; Turmel contributed liner notes to multiple LAS releases, indicating cross-label dialogue between the two principal French post-punk imprints.
