Mint Records (12)

Discogs ID: 1170471 Origin: Vancouver, BC, Canada

Canadian independent label based in Vancouver, BC. Parent entity: Mint Records Inc. (Discogs ID: 96660). Sublabel: Hot Heros. Home to Tough Age for all main studio releases (2013–2020) and to Dumb (8) from 2018 onward. Also home to Cub, whose material Tough Age covered on Plays Cub’s Hot Dog Day (2015). A long-running Vancouver indie label with a strong post-punk and indie rock roster.
