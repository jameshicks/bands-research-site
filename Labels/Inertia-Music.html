Inertia Music

Discogs label ID: 41372
Country: Australia (Sydney, NSW)
Address: Unit 2/198 Young Street, Waterloo, Sydney NSW 2017 (street); PO Box 84, Surry Hills NSW 2010 (postal)
Active years: c. 1999–2023 (Discogs catalog span)
Releases on Discogs: ~390 (main label); ~340 across major sublabels
Archived URL: web.archive.org/web/20240721130131/http://inertiamusic.com

Australia’s leading independent music company, specialising in marketing, promoting, distributing, and licensing recordings. Inertia operated simultaneously as a label in its own right and as the dominant Australian independent distributor for international releases, making it a central node in the country’s indie music infrastructure from the late 1990s through the 2010s.

History

Origins and Dual Role (late 1990s–2002)

Inertia’s Discogs footprint begins in 1999 with a Smog Australian pressing and a Lambchop issue in 2000 (cat# URA017), suggesting the company was active as a distributor from at least that period, licensing releases from US labels for Australian/New Zealand territory. Early catalog numbers in the URA series (used on Bonnie “Prince” Billy, Bright Eyes, Yeah Yeah Yeahs, Mogwai, Smog, The Handsome Family) indicate a consistent arrangement with American indie labels across the early 2000s.

Expansion Era (2003–2009)

The catalog peaks in activity around 2007–2009 (28 releases per year in 2007–2008, 35 in 2009 on the main label alone). By this period Inertia had established several active distribution imprints:

  • Pod (ID: 101577) as the primary conduit for international indie-electronic and indie-rock titles (UNKLE, Frightened Rabbit, M83, Girls, Wavves, The Knife, Japandroids, Destroyer)
  • Rogue Records (ID: 70968) as a boutique imprint carrying singer-songwriter and Americana titles (CocoRosie, Vashti Bunyan, Bon Iver, The Hold Steady, Caribou, Alela Diane, Pinback, El Perro Del Mar)
  • Inertia Recordings (ID: 27700) focusing on electronic, hip-hop, and global beats (Pepe Deluxé, Chungking, Ben Lee, Concord Dawn, Transglobal Underground, Pink Martini)

During this era Inertia was the Australian distributor for Warp Records (Strange Weather Isn’t It?/!!!, Grizzly Bear’s Veckatimest, PODCD releases), XL Recordings (Friendly Fires, Holly Miranda), Downtown Records (Miike Snow, Santogold, Mos Def/The Ecstatic, Eagles of Death Metal), and likely others.

Australian Artist Development (2006–2016)

Alongside distribution, Inertia actively signed and developed Australian artists, particularly out of the Sydney/Melbourne scenes. Key Australian acts on the main Inertia label in this period include:

  • Midnight Juggernauts (Sydney electro-rock, 4 releases 2007–2010, cat# SIB)
  • Bridezilla (Melbourne, 4 releases 2009–2010, including the Tren Brothers split IR7001)
  • Gotye (Melbourne, 4 releases 2011, including Like Drawing Blood and Boardface)
  • Passenger (Michael Rosenberg, UK-born but Australian-based; 11 releases 2010–2014, including All The Little Lights which was a major international breakthrough)
  • Sia (Australian; 8 releases 2008–2012 including Some People Have Real Problems)
  • Ali Barter (8 releases 2017–2019)
  • Elizabeth Rose (4) (electronic/pop, 16 releases 2012–2014)
  • Body Type (Sydney indie-rock, 6 releases 2018–2019)
  • Dirty Three (3 releases 2005–2009, including Cinder)

Later Period (2016–2023)

Activity declined after 2017, though the label continued issuing releases through 2023. The catalog from the late 2010s onward reflects fewer Australian originals and more selective international licensing (Lord Huron, American Football, Cigarettes After Sex). The Good Cop Bad Cop (ID: 116361) and Inertia Access (ID: 597784) sublabels appear to be more recent operational entities, likely administrative rather than curatorial.

Scene Connections

Inertia functioned as the primary gateway for international independent music entering the Australian/New Zealand market through the 2000s and 2010s. Its distribution network brought to Australia:

  • Warp Records catalog (!!!, Grizzly Bear, UNKLE via Pod)
  • XL Recordings titles (Friendly Fires, Holly Miranda)
  • Downtown Records roster (Miike Snow, Santogold, Eagles of Death Metal, Mos Def)
  • Sub Pop / American indie via Rogue Records (Bon Iver, The Hold Steady)
  • Frenchkiss / other US indie via Rogue (Caribou, Pinback, CocoRosie, El Perro Del Mar)
  • Bella Union / European indie via Pod (Tunng, Malcolm Middleton, Frightened Rabbit)

As a domestic label, Inertia was closely connected to:

  • The Midnight Juggernauts / Gotye Melbourne electronic/indie scene
  • The Bridezilla / Tren Brothers Sydney DIY scene (Inertia issued their 2009 split 7”)
  • The Dirty Three circle via Shane O’Mara and Mick Turner artwork
  • The Australian hip-hop scene (The Herd, Horrorshow, Macromantics, Katalyst)
  • Remote Control — parallel Australian indie distributor; Inertia and Remote Control were the two dominant independent distribution companies in Australia during the 2000s
