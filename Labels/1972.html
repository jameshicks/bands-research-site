Overview

1972 is a reissue label launched in 2011 specialising in ambient, indie, post-punk, and new wave music. Its catalogue is heavily weighted toward the 2010s (39 releases) with continued activity into the 2020s. The roster is dominated by The Sound and its members — Adrian Borland, Graham Green, and Michael Dudley — pointing to a focus on that band’s legacy; Tim Gane also appears among its credited artists.
