Midnight Music

British independent record label, active primarily 1983–1993. Based in London. Key label for UK post-punk and indie music of the mid-1980s. Artists include Cindytalk (Camouflage Heart, 1984), Robyn Hitchcock (I Often Dream Of Trains, 1984), Sad Lovers And Giants (“In The Breeze”, 1984), and others. John Dent at The Sound Clinic cut the lacquers for much of their early catalogue.
