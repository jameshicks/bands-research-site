Rouska

Leeds (UK) indie label founded by Richard Rouska, who also published a fanzine of the same name. Active mid-to-late 1980s. Discogs label ID: 13680. Sublabel: Rouska Records (ID: 1080483).

Overview

Rouska operated out of Leeds, first from 19 Stansfeld Chambers, Gt. George St. (1986), then from the Electric Press Factory, 39 Cookridge Street, Leeds LS2. The label had a US satellite address at Lehigh University, Bethlehem PA (“Rouska’s Great Satanic Subsidiary”). Catalogue spanned post-punk, indie rock, goth-tinged noise, and experimental releases across approximately 70 releases.
