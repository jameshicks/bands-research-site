Overview

An independent UK label founded in 2006 by Jack Clothier (also known as Jack Pop or Jack pOp) and Kevin Douch, Alcopop! Records focuses on indie rock, alternative rock, punk, post-punk, and indie pop. The catalogue spans roughly 118 releases: a modest 17 in its founding decade, a peak run of 55 through the 2010s, and 46 continuing into the 2020s, suggesting a label that gained momentum steadily and has remained active. Its roster has included Johnny Foreigner, DITZ, and several solo artists, and the label also operates the Alcopop! Singles Club sublabel.
