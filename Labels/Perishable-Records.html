Overview

Chicago independent label operating from the early 1990s through the mid-2000s. The contact email (ben@perishablerecords.com) points to Ben Massarella as the label’s operator or co-founder — Massarella is also the most recurring producer across the catalog (3 production credits) and a performer on 8 releases. No formal founder credit appears in the DB.

The label is rooted in the Red Red Meat / Califone extended network. Tim Rutili and Brian Deck appear on the very first release (Hot Nikkety Trunk Monkey, 1990) and remain fixtures through to the Califone cluster in 2001–2003. The catalog spans alt-rock, chamber folk, experimental and post-rock, loosely unified by overlapping Chicago-area personnel.

Perishable is significant as the original home of Red Red Meat (1990–1992), the launch pad for Orso (1998–2004), and — crucially — the US imprint for three Califone releases (Roomsound, Deceleration One, Deceleration Two, Sometimes Good Weather Follows Bad People) that are incorrectly attributed to Glitterhouse in some sources. HiM placed two of their major records here (Sworn Eyes 1999, Our Point Of Departure 2000) alongside the UK-licensed FatCat versions.

Release Notes

Red Red Meat (1990–1992): Two uncatalogued releases (Hot Nikkety Trunk Monkey 1990; Ream Tar Tar 1992) plus the self-titled LP (PER002 for Buzzmuscle, catno “none” for RRM). Brad Wood produced the 1990 record; Brian Deck is already on board as drummer and co-recording engineer. The classic Red Red Meat lineup: Tim Rutili (vocals/guitar), Brian Deck (drums), Glenn Girard (guitar), Glynis Johnson (bass/vocals).

Loftus (1998, PER003): A supergroup session drawing on the whole Perishable network — Massarella, Deck, Bundy K. Brown, Doug Scharin, Phil Spirito, Tim Rutili, Tim Hurley, Curtis Harvey. No individual composer credits; listed as “Performer” for all.

Orso (1998–2004): Three albums produced across the label’s active years. Spirito-led chamber folk ensemble with shifting personnel. Ben Massarella and Brian Deck co-produced the debut; Long Time By (2000) expanded to include Gillian Lisee and a wider string/winds ensemble. My Dreams Are Back (2004) replaced Deck with Doug Scharin and Griffin Rodriguez as engineers.

HiM (1999–2000): Two Perishable releases (Sworn Eyes PER009, Our Point Of Departure PER012), the latter simultaneously issued by FatCat in the UK (FATCD12) and Polydor Japan. Guest contributors on Sworn Eyes include Bundy K. Brown, Rob Mazurek, Jeff Parker, Julie Liu, and Martin Perna — essentially the Chicago jazz/experimental roster.

Califone (2001–2005): Califone’s US label during their busiest period. Roomsound (PER 015) and Sometimes Good Weather Follows Bad People (PER019) were co-released with Glitterhouse in Germany. Deceleration One (PER020) and Deceleration Two (PER023) are Perishable-only EPs/LPs. Everybody’s Mother (PERISHLTD01CD, 2005) was a limited-release compilation.

Joan Of Arc (2003, PER 024): In Rape Fantasy And Terror Sex We Trust — the most densely credited release on the label, with Califone serving as executive producers and Graeme Gibson engineering. Features Tim Kinsella, Sam Zurick, Mike Kinsella, Todd Mattei, Rob Mazurek, Jim Becker, Azita Youssefi, and a remix by 90 Day Men.

Sinister Luck Ensemble (2002, PER018): Anniversary — composed and arranged by Charles Kim; features Ken Vandermark (clarinet), Rob Mazurek (cornet), Kent Kessler (double bass), Glenn Kotche (percussion), Andrew Bird (violin, tracks 4, 5, 8), Jason Adasiewicz (vibraphone/glockenspiel).
