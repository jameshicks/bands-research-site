6months

Sublabel of Anticon. Discogs label ID: 25836. Parent: Anticon (ID: 1105).

Described in Discogs as a “distribution company formed by Anticon.” In practice, 6months functioned as a secondary imprint that allowed Anticon to release records by artists in the extended collective who were not strictly Anticon core — including Canadian acts and label-adjacent producers — alongside a handful of full Anticon-family members. All releases are CDs (plus one vinyl LP), all US (except one Canadian co-release), concentrated in 2001–2002, with a single straggler in 2004. No 7”s, no cassettes, no unique format niche; it was content-differentiated rather than format-differentiated: looser ties to the mothership, more room for fringe-scene collaborators.

No URLs or contact info on record. Catalog numbers follow the scheme 6m000X / 6M000X.

What the Label Was For

6months was Anticon’s overflow / affiliate valve in the 2001–2002 period. All eight releases share the same Anticon barcode prefix (751937…) and were pressed at the same plant (CDForge), confirming they ran through Anticon’s manufacturing and distribution infrastructure. The imprint gave the label a way to:

  1. Release fringe-Anticon members — Sixtoo (Halifax, Nova Scotia) was a regular Anticon collaborator and touring figure but not a core Oakland resident; 6months was his primary label home. Similarly, Josh Martinez (Vancouver) had Canadian ties and his 2004 release was co-issued with Camobear, suggesting the imprint was a handshake arrangement for Canada-adjacent acts.

  2. Put out reissues and archival material — Left Handed Straw (Controller 7) and Imaginarium (L*Roneous) are both credited as “Reissue” on Discogs, meaning 6months functioned partly as a reissue channel for label-adjacent underground records that deserved wider circulation within the Anticon network.

  3. House full-roster collaborations — Learning To Walk (Sole, 6m0003) is catalogued as a “Compilation,” a retrospective or odds-and-ends collection rather than a standalone studio album.

  4. Give Alias a dedicated EP home — Three Phase Irony Double EP is a remix/production showcase distinct from Alias’s main Anticon studio LP sequence.

The label appears to have been inactive after 2004. No web presence, no founding statement documented in Discogs.
