Ici D’Ailleurs

Discogs ID: 24065 Founded: 1996/1997 (as independent label; name derived from a 1996 compilation) Location: Nancy, France Contact: 3 Rue Fabert, 54000 Nancy

Ici D’Ailleurs is an independent French record label founded in Nancy by Stéphane Grégoire, growing out of the dissolution of his prior associative label Sine Terra Firma. It has operated since the late 1990s as an eclectic, aesthetics-driven imprint with no fixed genre agenda, building a catalog that spans post-rock, neo-classical, electronic, contemporary composition, experimental pop, and French indie song. Its most internationally recognized release is the Amélie Poulain soundtrack by Yann Tiersen (2001).

Profile

Origins: Sine Terra Firma (1995–1996)

Ici D’Ailleurs grew directly out of Sine Terra Firma, an associative (non-profit) label Stéphane Grégoire ran in Nancy. Sine Terra Firma released Yann Tiersen’s first two albums — La Valse des Monstres (1995) and Rue des Cascades (1996) — and saw escalating success. A compilation Grégoire issued in 1996 under the title Ici D’Ailleurs gave the future label its name. When Sine Terra Firma dissolved, the independent commercial entity Ici D’Ailleurs carried on that identity.

Launch and Yann Tiersen (1997–2003)

The label launched with Tiersen’s third album Le Phare (1998), which became a commercial breakthrough and gave the label a platform. The catalog expanded quickly: Bästard (whose Radiant, Discharged, Crossed-Off was on IDA 004), The Married Monk, Fugu, and early singer-songwriter and post-rock acts from France and internationally. The Amélie Poulain (2001) release — Tiersen’s soundtrack for Jean-Pierre Jeunet’s film — became the label’s highest-profile release and circulated in multiple international pressings, though Grégoire’s role was listed on that release as coordinator rather than full executive producer.

Expansion and Sublabels (2002–2010)

In 2002 Grégoire founded 0101 (Discogs ID: 18379), an electronic subdivision to distinguish artists working primarily through digital/electronic processes from the label’s wider catalog. In 2004 the label added OuMuPo (Discogs ID: 563410) — an acronym for “Ouvroir de Musique Potentiel,” consciously modeled on the literary movement OuLiPo (itself an outgrowth of the Surrealist tradition). The OuMuPo concept asks artists to remaster a portion of the Ici D’Ailleurs catalog under a pre-established formal constraint. The visual/design component was assigned to JC Menu, a founding member of the comic-publishing association OuBaPo, who produced a 16-page comic book (itself constrained by OuBaPo rules) for each OuMuPo release. The collection reached six albums. Other sublabels include More Over (ID: 467266), Mind Travels Serie (ID: 685121 — a contemporary/new music curated series operating into the 2020s), and Ici d’ailleurs Publishing (ID: 599714).

A major collective project was inaugurated in late 2004: This Immortal Coil, conceived by Grégoire as a tribute to Coil. The project gathered Yaël Naim, Bonnie Prince Billy, Yann Tiersen, Matt Elliott, DAAU, Chapelier Fou, Sylvain Chauveau, Christine Ott, Oktopus, Nightwood, David Donatien, and Nicolas Jorio to re-interpret Coil compositions. The resulting album The Dark Age of Love appeared in 2009 (IDA060), produced by Grégoire, mixed by Oktopus, with compositions credited to Coil (Peter Christopherson and Jhonn Balance). This Immortal Coil became a recurring entity on the label, with The World Ended A Long Time Ago (2022) and Twisted By Love (2022) continuing the catalog.

Key domestic artists signed in this period included Matt Elliott (from 2004, starting with Drinking Songs), Programme, Thomas Belhom, Bed, and Zëro (from 2007).

Consolidation and Mature Roster (2010–present)

The 2010s saw the label deepen its commitment to long-running artist relationships. Chapelier Fou became one of its most active signed artists (40 releases across multiple albums and formats by the mid-2020s). Mendelson, Michel Cloup Duo / Michel Cloup, Ez3kiel, and Diabologum were key signings or reissue projects of the decade. Matt Elliott remained among the most consistently active catalog artists through releases including The Broken Man (2011), Only Myocardial Infarction Can Break Your Heart (2013), The Calm Before (2016), Farewell To All We Know (2020), The End Of Days (2023), and Drinking Songs Live 20 Years On (2024).

The Mind Travels Serie sublabel emerged as a distinct curatorial strand focused on contemporary composition, featuring artists including Melaine Dalibert, Tomasz Sroczyński, Mathias Delplanque, Geins’t Naït / Laurent Petitgand, David Chalmin, and — in 2025 — a collaboration between David Sylvian and Melaine Dalibert (Vermilion Hours, MT022) and CoH / Midori Hirano (Sudden Fruit, MT021).

The label continues to operate from Nancy. Its Bandcamp (icidailleurs.bandcamp.com) is the primary digital retail point.

Catalog Structure

The main catalog runs sequential IDA numbers (IDA 004 beginning around 1996 through IDA 162+ by the mid-2020s). The Mind Travels Serie uses MT prefix numbering (MT09 through MT022+ documented). The 0101 sublabel used its own sequence.

Credits Infrastructure

The label has a small number of recurring technical and design collaborators across its catalog:

Mastering: Uwe Teichert (88 releases), Matt Colton (37 releases), Alan Ward (9 releases), Ivan Chiossone, Anders Peterson.

Mixing / Engineering: David Chalmin (25 releases as engineer/mixer), F. Lor, Louis Warynski, Gwen Roujanski, Triboulet, Fred Norguet.

Artwork / Design: Francis Meslet (42 releases — photography and artwork), Anne Boissez (27 releases — cover paintings, drawings, photography), Uncle Vania (19 — drawings/artwork), Gregory Wagenheim (17 — layout), Frank Loriou (15), Iconographik.com (15), Yann NGuema (14 — also electronics player in some contexts), Anne Boissez / François Wirz.

Label founder / executive producer: Stéphane Grégoire (Discogs ID: 426208) — credited as producer or executive producer on 38 releases.
