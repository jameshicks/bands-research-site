The Tapeworm

UK cassette and limited-edition label based in London. Label ID: 158893. Active from 2009 onward; 203 releases in Discogs. Motto: “Unfading moths, immortal flies, and the worm that never dies.” Specialises in experimental, electroacoustic, noise, drone, and improvised music — releases typically cassette or small-run format. Run by Mark Van Hoen (also known as Locust). Sublabels include The Digital Archive Of Tapeworm, The Wormhole, and Vanity Publishing.

Overview

The Tapeworm operates as a curatorial imprint, pairing artists for one-off releases. Its catalog is catalogued with TTW# numbers. By 2017, the series had passed TTW#100, with the centenary release A Can Of Worms (TTW#100, 2017) — a Various Artists compilation. The label’s scope ranges from concrete poetry (Jean Baudrillard, TTW#02) to noise (Daniel Menche, Merzbow-adjacent releases), free jazz (Apiary), ambient, and the UK post-industrial tradition. Artists have included Philip Jeck, Fennesz, Dylan Carlson, Stephen O’Malley, Oren Ambarchi, Carl Michael Von Hausswolff, Simon Fisher Turner, Jay Glass Dubs, Pinkcourtesyphone, and many others.
