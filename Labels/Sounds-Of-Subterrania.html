Overview

Sounds Of Subterrania is a small DIY punk rock label founded in Kassel in 1998 by Gregor Samsa, built around a core philosophy of artistic independence from the mainstream. The operation relocated to Hamburg in 2008 and by March 2009 had settled into the premises of the Hafenklang Studio. Its catalogue of over 115 releases spans punk, garage rock, indie rock, noise, and soul, with the bulk of its output concentrated in the 2000s and sustained activity continuing into the 2020s. The label has expanded under three sublabels — Chestnut Classics, Happy Mono, and In Gute Hände — reflecting its broad stylistic reach beyond its punk origins.
