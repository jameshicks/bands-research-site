Small but devoted record label, formed by John Kemler, Jason Gagovski (Suicide Note, Sweet Cobra, Stabbed by Words, Old Devil Moon) and Iain Hursey (who ran the label Stability Records before). Their newest addition is Craig Sopata.

From their website: “We like to think Hawthorne Street is a little different from the average label. We don’t buy into trends. We don’t release music from bands because we think it will sell. We only release music that we love, and by people who love the music they create.

— Discogs label profile, ID 64663

Overview

Hawthorne Street Records is a small independent label founded around 1999 by John Kemler, Jason Gagovski (Sweet Cobra, Suicide Note), and Iain Hursey, who had previously run Stability Records. Operating by a philosophy of releasing only music its founders genuinely love rather than chasing trends or commercial viability, the label built a 32-release catalogue spanning hardcore, post-rock, punk, and indie rock. Activity peaked in the 2000s with 18 releases and continued strongly into the 2010s with 11 more, with two releases in the 2020s. Key roster names include Sweet Cobra, The Life And Times, and Allen Epley.
