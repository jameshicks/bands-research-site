Muscle Shoals Sound Studios

Recording studio at 3614 Jackson Highway, Sheffield, Alabama, founded circa 1969 by the four core members of the Muscle Shoals Rhythm Section: Barry Beckett, Roger Hawkins, Jimmy Johnson (4), and David Hood. The address is referenced in Lynyrd Skynyrd’s “Sweet Home Alabama.” One of the most important American recording studios of the 1970s–80s. The studio also ran its own label, Muscle Shoals Sound Records.

Key Session Musicians

Beyond the four founders, recurring session players at Muscle Shoals Sound include:

  • Pete Carr — lead guitarist; appears on Bob Seger, Paul Simon, Art Garfunkel, Rod Stewart records; solo albums on Big Tree (1976, 1978)
  • Spooner Oldham — keyboards; early FAME alumnus who continued at Muscle Shoals Sound
  • Harvey Thompson — saxophone; appears on Pete Carr solo albums and many sessions
  • Will McFarlane — guitar (later MSRS member)
  • Steve Nathan — keyboards (later MSRS member)
