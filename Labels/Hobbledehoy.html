Overview

Hobbledehoy is an Australian independent record label founded in Melbourne in 2006 by Tom Majerczak and now based in Adelaide, South Australia. Its catalogue of roughly 56 releases skews heavily toward the 2010s and spans indie rock, post-rock, emo, shoegaze, and acoustic music. The roster mixes local Australian acts with international names: US post-rock band Caspian and Seattle producer–engineer Matt Bayles both appear alongside home-grown artists such as Arrows and Luke Howard.

Caspian / Waking Season Australian release — how the deal came about

What is established from public sources:

Caspian’s Waking Season was originally released in September 2012 on Triple Crown Records in the US. Hobbledehoy issued a separate Australia/New Zealand pressing (HOB029, 2×LP, limited to 250 copies in Seafoam Blue) in January 2014, timed to coincide with Caspian’s first-ever Australian tour (five shows in March 2014 as part of a broader Asia–Pacific run). The release is credited jointly to Hobbledehoy and Triple Crown on the pressing, suggesting a standard regional licensing or sub-licensing arrangement rather than a full signing. Caspian also appear on Hobbledehoy’s artist roster page, and the label subsequently released Dust And Disquiet (2015, no catalog number recorded on Discogs).
