SmallsLIVE

Discogs ID: 676130
Website: https://www.smallslive.com/
Location: New York City, Greenwich Village
Active: 2008 – present (Discogs entries through 2018)
Catalog prefix: SL-0001 through SL-0059 (documented on Discogs)

Recording label founded by jazz pianist and Smalls Jazz Club co-owner Spike Wilner to document live performances at Smalls Jazz Club, a beloved basement jazz club at 183 West 10th Street, Greenwich Village, NYC. The label’s releases are almost uniformly titled Live At Smalls (with occasional Live At Mezzrow releases from Wilner’s second club). Every recording is drawn from actual club performances, making SmallsLIVE a sustained document of the NYC mainstream jazz scene from 2008 onward.

Background: Smalls Jazz Club

Smalls opened in 1994 and quickly became an after-hours institution in Greenwich Village, known for marathon jam sessions running through the night. After a closure in the mid-2000s, the club was reconceived and reopened under new ownership including pianist Spike Wilner. The label was launched shortly after the reopening as a way to capture and sell the club’s live recordings — initially as downloads and physical CDs. Mezzrow, a smaller and more intimate room next door at 163 West 10th Street, was added later and also became a recording venue for the label.

Key Personnel

Producer / Executive Producer: Spike Wilner (real name Michael Wilner) produced the vast majority of the catalog — credited on 47 of the label’s 67 releases on Discogs. As the club’s co-owner and house pianist, Wilner set the artistic direction and served as the connective tissue across the entire label.

Engineer / Recording: Jimmy Katz (Discogs ID: 518823) engineered 11 releases. Glen Forrest (ID: 1053780) engineered 15 and co-produced several early releases. Tyler McDiarmid (ID: 1042666) engineered 5 releases.

Mix / Production: Ben Rubin (2) (ID: 3785488) mixed and co-produced 18 releases — second only to Wilner in overall producer credits. Derek Nievergelt (ID: 769493) mixed 5 releases.

Mastering: Gene Paul (Discogs ID: 339468) mastered 23 releases, the dominant mastering presence on the label. Jamie Polaski (ID: 578875) mastered 12 releases. Ian Hendrickson-Smith (also a principal recording artist) handled mix/master duties on several releases he was not leading.

Art / Design: Michelle Watt (Discogs ID: 3776900) designed covers and provided photography for 32 releases — a defining visual identity across the catalog. Una Stade (ID: 4966653) handled graphic design on 4 releases.

Character and Significance

SmallsLIVE is one of the most sustained documentary efforts in mainstream acoustic jazz of the 2000s–2010s. Rather than signing artists to multi-album contracts or recording in a conventional studio, the label captures working groups in the actual performance environment — the intimate, low-ceilinged room that Smalls is famous for. The catalog spans straight-ahead post-bop, bebop piano trios, contemporary hard bop, and a few outliers (Ari Hoenig’s Punkbop, Cyrille Aimée’s vocal set). Most artists on the label are based in or connected to the NYC jazz scene. Several releases also appeared in Japan via licensing, reflecting continued international interest in the NYC acoustic jazz tradition.

The label’s infrastructure is lean: Spike Wilner as consistent executive producer, a small rotating team of engineers (Glen Forrest, Jimmy Katz, Tyler McDiarmid), a pair of primary mixing engineers (Ben Rubin, Derek Nievergelt), and Gene Paul as the predominant mastering engineer. Michelle Watt’s cover photography and design gives the physical releases a consistent visual identity.
