Damaged Goods

UK independent record label. Discogs label ID: 7428. Parent: Damaged Goods Records Ltd (ID: 683191). Approximately 1,615 releases in DB.

Founded 1988 by Ian Ballard (aka Ian Damaged, Discogs artist ID: 1650541), operating out of east London. Originally conceived as a punk reissue label; by the early 1990s pivoting to contemporary acts across garage punk, indie pop, and new wave. Still active as of 2026.

LC 13056. Contact: info@damagedgoods.co.uk.

Sublabels: DG 30th Anniversary Singles Club (ID: 1503460); Damaged Books (ID: 236515); Damaged Goods Records (ID: 105637).

Overview

Damaged Goods formed in 1988 from a living room in east London. The label’s first release was a reissue of the 1977 Slaughter and the Dogs single “Where Have All the Bootboys Gone?” (FNARR 1), followed by their debut album Do It Dog Style (FNARR LP 2). Early catalog also included reissues and new material from Adam And The Ants, The Pork Dukes, The Snivelling Shits, and The Killjoys.

By 1990 the label shifted from a pure reissue operation to releasing contemporary bands, scoring a significant early coup with the debut EP by Manic Street Preachers (New Art Riot E.P., 1990, YUBB 4). In 1991 they began a sustained relationship with Billy Childish (initially with Thee Headcoats) that would become the core of the label’s identity for three decades. They subsequently issued the debut single by Thee Headcoatees.

The mid-1990s saw the label embed itself in the UK garage punk scene centred on Toe Rag Studios in London and Slim Chance’s Wild Western Rooms in Archway. In 1995 they released the debut album by Holly Golightly (The Good Things, DAMGOOD CD-65), who went on to record fifteen albums for the label. The late 1990s and 2000s brought Buzzcocks singles and albums (2003–2006), a series of Television Personalities releases, Penetration (2) comeback singles, and sustained output from the Billy Childish succession of bands (Billy Childish And The Blackhands, Wild Billy Childish & The Musicians Of The British Empire, CTMF).

2018 marked the label’s 30th anniversary, celebrated with the retrospective compilation 1988–2018 Damaged Goods and a 7” singles club (DG 30th Anniversary Singles Club). More recent signings include Amyl And The Sniffers (whose debut UK releases appeared on Damaged Goods, 2018), The Courettes (from 2020), and The William Loveday Intention.

The label is noted for its consistent aesthetic — DIY design sensibility, strong links to the Medway/Chatham garage rock axis, and a willingness to document the whole arc of an artist’s career rather than cherry-picking commercial prospects. Billy Childish alone accounts for over 100 primary credits; Wolf Howard, Bruce Brand, and Julie Hamper appear across scores of releases as core session players.

(Wikipedia: Damaged Goods (record label))

Connections
