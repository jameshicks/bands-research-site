Overview

Weird World is a UK independent label founded in 2011 as an imprint within Domino Recording Company. It is an official Domino sublabel — confirmed by Discogs parent relationship and the contact address routing to dominorecordco.com. The Discogs profile says “established in 2011 in partnership with Domino Record Co.” but no named individual co-founder appears anywhere in public-facing materials; the label’s own About page simply repeats the same line and routes enquiries to Domino. The most likely interpretation is that “in partnership with Domino” is standard language for a Domino in-house imprint — comparable to Domino’s other sublabels (Double Six, Ribbon Music, Vault). No evidence of an outside co-owner exists. It is not a Sub Pop imprint; Sub Pop has no documented relationship to this label or to Within And Without. The label operates under its own identity and roster but distributes through Domino infrastructure.

The label launched with three simultaneous signings — Smith Westerns, Washed Out, and Peaking Lights — in 2011, establishing a chillwave/dream-pop/psych identity from the outset. Over the following decade it developed into a home for experimental and avant-folk acts, particularly Richard Dawson, who has released five studio albums on the label (2014–2025) and become its most prolific and critically prominent act.

Notes on Discogs Disambiguation

Discogs lists this label as “Weird World (2)” to distinguish it from:

  • Weird World (ID: 37852) — unrelated 1970s imprint under T.K. Productions
  • Weird World (3) (ID: 1748094) — one release, no parent

The WEIRD003LP catalog number for Within And Without unambiguously points to Weird World (2) / Domino.

Departures: Washed Out and Peaking Lights

Washed Out never “left” Weird World in a conventional label-switch sense. The Weird World relationship was always a UK/Europe territorial licence — Sub Pop handled North America for both Within And Without (2011) and Paracosm (2013), while Weird World held the UK pressing rights and Domino handled Germany. After Paracosm, Washed Out moved to Stones Throw (Mister Mellow, 2017) globally, ending the Weird World territorial deal. The Weird World roster entry (24 releases) reflects all UK pressings of those two albums’ many formats. See Washed Out.

Peaking Lights departed by founding their own label: Two Flowers Records (Baywood, Los Osos, California), conceived in 2013 specifically to re-release 936 and handle all subsequent output. The move was explicitly artist-driven — the band cited “an art as life ethic where the artist takes control of their work through DIY mechanisms.” Later releases also appeared on Dekmantel. See Peaking Lights.
