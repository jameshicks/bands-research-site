Beggars Banquet

UK independent record label. Discogs label ID: 2056. Parent: Beggars Group.

Overview

Beggars Banquet Records was a British independent label launched in 1977 by Martin Mills and Nick Austin, growing out of the Beggars Banquet record shops (first opened 1973 in Earls Court, London). Inspired by the DIY ethics of the UK punk boom, Mills and Austin pressed their first release — The Lurkers’ “Shadow”/“Love Story” 7” (BEG 1) — the same year. Over the following three decades the label ranged across punk, post-punk, goth, synth-pop, dream-pop, indie rock, and alternative, building one of the most eclectic catalogs in British independent music.

The label’s early commercial breakthrough came via Tubeway Army and Gary Numan, whose run of late-1970s synth-pop records secured the label’s finances and national profile. Through the early 1980s Beggars became a home for goth and post-punk: Bauhaus, Tones On Tail, Love And Rockets, and The Associates all recorded for the label or its sublabels. The mid-to-late 1980s added The Cult, The Fall, Fields Of The Nephilim, The Go-Betweens, Gene Loves Jezebel, and The Icicle Works. Into the 1990s and 2000s came The Charlatans, Mercury Rev, Tindersticks, Biffy Clyro, The National, and Oceansize.

Along the way Beggars spun off or invested in numerous sublabels — most significantly 4AD, Situation Two, XL Recordings, and City Beat — and acquired stakes in Too Pure, Mo Wax, and Matador Records, eventually forming the Beggars Group, now one of the largest independent label networks in the world (Wikipedia: Beggars Group).

In April 2008 the Beggars Group retired the Beggars Banquet imprint, migrating key artists to 4AD and other group labels. The catalog is now maintained through the Beggars Arkive (Discogs sublabel ID: 1888522). Label Code: LC 5485. Business Registration No. 1414047.

Address (as of active years): 17–19 Alma Road, London SW18 1AA.
