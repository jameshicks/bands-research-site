Overview

BlocSonic is an independent hip-hop label founded and curated by Michael Gregoire, with operations spanning the USA, Canada, and UK. Active since 2007, the label built its catalogue primarily through the 2010s — 27 of its 32 logged releases land in that decade — working in Boom Bap, Conscious, Instrumental, Jazzy Hip-Hop, and Downtempo. Its roster includes C-Doc (The WarHammer), The Impossebulls, Marcus J Gilligan, and Stu Beetle. The label operates through four sublabels — BlocSonic Originals, FortyFives, MaxBlocs, and NetBlocs — suggesting a structured approach to different release formats or tiers.
