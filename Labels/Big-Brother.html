From Discogs

Big Brother Recordings was set up in 2000 by the British band Oasis to handle their UK and Ireland releases. Independent distributor 3MV/Pinnacle handles domestic sales in UK and Ireland. Sony BMG/Epic Records continues to handle the band’s international distribution. The name refers to Oasis guitarist and songwriter Noel Gallagher, the elder brother of singer Liam Gallagher. Each release’s catalogue number is prefixed “RKID”, a reference to Liam. (“Our kid” is a term widely used in North-West England to refer to a younger sibling) The first release on Big Brother was the single “Go Let It Out” (with a catalogue number of ‘RKID 001’) on February 7, 2000. Big Brother have subsequently issued releases by other artists, including British band Happy Mondays.

— Discogs label profile, ID 34032

Overview

Big Brother Recordings is Oasis’s self-owned label, founded in 2000 to control UK and Irish releases while Sony BMG/Epic continued handling international distribution and 3MV/Pinnacle managed domestic sales. The name is a reference to guitarist and primary songwriter Noel Gallagher as the elder of the two brothers, while the catalogue prefix “RKID” nods to Liam, drawn from the North-West English phrase “our kid” for a younger sibling. The label launched with Oasis’s single “Go Let It Out” (RKID 001) in February 2000 and later extended its roster to other acts including Happy Mondays. Activity is heavily concentrated in the 2000s, with the catalogue thinning through the 2010s into the 2020s.
