Ntone

London-based experimental/electronic sublabel of Ninja Tune, active 1994–2001. Originally named Ninja Tone, the label was renamed to avoid confusion with its parent. Contact address: PO Box 4296, London SE11 4WW. Discogs label ID: 322.

Overview

Ntone was Ninja Tune’s outlet for more experimental, abstract, and downtempo electronic material that sat outside the main label’s hip-hop-oriented aesthetic. It launched in 1994 alongside Ninja Tune’s early years (Ninja Tune itself was founded 1990 by Coldcut — Matt Black and Jonathan More). The label ran for roughly seven years, issuing around 32 catalog numbers before going dormant around 2001.

The roster was dominated by Neotropic (Riz Maslen), whose six releases across 1995–2001 make her the label’s central figure. Other core acts: Cabbage Boy, Flanger (Bernd Friedmann + Uwe Schmidt), Journeyman, Animals On Wheels (Andrew Coleman), Fink (Fin Greenall), Hexstatic (Robin Brunson + Stuart Warren Hill), and Hex (Ntone) (Matt Black of Coldcut, Rob Pepperell, Miles Visman, Karel Dander). Flanger’s presence connected the label to a strand of German electronic music — both Bernd Friedmann and Uwe Schmidt (aka Atom Heart) were key figures in the Frankfurt/Cologne experimental scene.

In 1997 the label made an unusual step into US avant-garde territory with two releases by Rhys Chatham: Neon (with Martin Wheeler) and Septile (with Jonathan Kane and DJ Elated System). These releases brought a New York minimalist/post-punk sensibility — Chatham on trumpet, Kane on drums — into contact with London electronic production.

House mastering engineer across most of the catalog: Voda (Jonathan Moore, Discogs ID: 3984). House designer: Openmind (Discogs ID: 1844845), credited on 24 releases. The 1994 compilation Tone Tales From Tomorrow (NTONE CD5) — mixed by Coldcut — served as an early label sampler and documents the founding-era roster: Journeyman, Hex, Drome, Real Life (3), Nonplace Urban Field, Incarnate.

Key Releases in Detail

Antistatic — Hex (1994, NTONE tone cd1)

The label’s first full release. Five tracks of ambient/techno; one track (“Glass”) co-produced by Coldcut. Hex here = Matt Black (of Coldcut), Rob Pepperell, Miles Visman, Karel Dander (Discogs artist ID: 4957).

Tone Tales From Tomorrow — Various (1994, NTONE CD5)

Label sampler mixed by Coldcut. Tracks from Journeyman, Hex (Ntone), Drome, Real Life (3), Nonplace Urban Field, Incarnate. Artwork credited to Hex.

15 Levels Of Magnification — Neotropic (1996, NTONE CD 17)

Neotropic’s debut album on Ntone; 16 tracks spanning abstract, breaks, drum n bass, trip hop, downtempo. Producer: Neotropic. Written by Riz Maslen. Engineer: Small Fish With Spine (another Maslen alias).

Neon — Rhys Chatham / Martin Wheeler (3) (1997, NTONE 15)

Four-track 12” (A-sides: “Charm,” “Hornithology”; B-sides: “Ramatek,” “Neon”). Electronic abstract. Design by Openmind. This is Chatham’s trumpet-based experimental work in a UK-electronic context.

Septile — Rhys Chatham / Jonathan Kane / DJ Elated System (1997, NTONE CDS 28)

Four-track CD. Personnel: Chatham (trumpet), Kane (drums, turntables), DJ Elated System (keyboards, turntables, electronics), Danny Hamilton (7) (keyboards, turntables, electronics). “Rotate” features Holly Anderson (lyrics) and Tamar Kotoski (voice). Photography by Art Kane. Styles: Abstract, Techno.

Mr Brubakers Strawberry Alarm Clock — Neotropic (1998, NTONE CD30)

Third Neotropic album. 15 tracks; guest guitar by Gary Lucas on “Vent.” Paul Jason Fredericks on voice (tracks 4, 11). Produced by Riz Maslen.

Templates — Flanger (1999, ntone cd33)

Flanger = Atom Heart (Uwe Schmidt, Discogs ID: 519207) + Bernd Friedmann (Discogs ID: 58045). Core Frankfurt/Cologne electronic duo; styles: Downtempo, Future Jazz.

Nuvol I Cadira — Animals On Wheels (1999, ntone cd36)

Animals On Wheels = Andrew Coleman (Discogs ID: 3798). 11-track IDM/downtempo album. Mastered by Voda.

Rewind — Hexstatic (2000, Ntone CD 43)

Hexstatic = Robin Brunson (ID: 99154) + Stuart Warren Hill (ID: 172929). Audio+CD-ROM package. Includes collab with Coldcut (“Timber [Hardwood Mix]”).
