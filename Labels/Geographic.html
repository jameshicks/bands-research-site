Geographic

Discogs label ID: 13732 Parent label: Geographic Music (Discogs ID: 732775) Associated with: Domino Records (URL: dominomusic.com/releases/labels/geographic) Active: 2000–present (153 releases)

Glasgow-affiliated independent imprint operated by The Pastels (Stephen McRobbie and Katrina Mitchell) through Domino Records. Originally launched to release music from the Glasgow experimental/post-rock scene around the Pastels’ circle; the label subsequently expanded to become one of Scotland’s most significant indie imprints, releasing artists including Future Pilot A.K.A., International Airport, Bill Wells Trio, Maher Shalal Hash Baz, Telstar Ponies, Tenniscoats, Lightships, Spinning Coin, and Crescent.
