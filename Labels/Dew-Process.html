Overview

Dew Process is an Australian independent record label founded in 2002, operating across indie rock, alternative rock, folk rock, and indie pop. Its catalogue built steadily through the 2000s (57 releases) before peaking in the 2010s (90 releases), with output tapering in the 2020s. The roster has included high-profile Australian artists such as Sarah Blasko and Bernard Fanning alongside international acts like Mumford & Sons, suggesting licensing or distribution arrangements that extended the label’s reach beyond domestic signings. Despite 461 total Discogs credits across 167 releases, the label’s corporate structure — any parent company or sublabel arrangement — is unrecorded.
