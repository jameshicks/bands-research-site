Pod

Sublabel of Inertia Music. Discogs label ID: 101577. Australia.

Primary international indie-rock, electronic, and art-pop distribution imprint for Inertia, active from at least 2007. Largest Inertia sublabel by volume (156 releases). Used PODCD catalog numbering. Distributed titles from Warp Records, XL Recordings, Bella Union, and many others in the Australian/NZ market.
