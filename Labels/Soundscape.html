Overview

Soundscape is the personal imprint of ambient and drone composer Robert Rich, occasionally credited as “Soundscape Productions” on some releases. The label debuted in the early 1980s but remained sparse through the 1990s before finding its stride from the 2000s onward, when the bulk of its forty-plus catalogue entries appeared. Its roster extends beyond Rich to include John Bergin, Brad Cole, Luca Formentini, and Markus Reuter, with the catalogue centred on ambient, tribal, experimental, minimal, and drone styles. A sublabel, Soundscape Studio, operates alongside the main imprint.
