Dais Records

Independent American record label. Discogs label ID: 93202

Founded 2007 by Gibby Miller (Discogs: 257106) and Ryan Martin (2) (Discogs: 886779). Based in Los Angeles, California and New York, New York. Distributed exclusively by Secretly Distribution. Over 1,300 releases on Discogs.

Overview

Dais describes itself as genre-agnostic, operating across experimental, avant-garde, darkwave, industrial, noise, and ambient territory. The label issues new material from active artists and also curates reissue projects for rare and out-of-print recordings.

Gibby Miller is also the co-founder who appears in groups Louderbach, Harmony Set, Panic (14), and The Trouble. His Discogs profile links to daisrecords.com, confirming he is one of the two principals. Miller is also credited for Layout on Drab Majesty’s Careless (2015).

Scene Identity

Dais sits at the intersection of:

  • 1980s gothic/post-punk reissue (Coil, Psychic TV, Nocturnal Projections)
  • Contemporary LA darkwave (Drab Majesty, Cold Showers, Body Of Light, High-Functioning Flesh)
  • Industrial/noise (Youth Code, ADULT., Merzbow, Prurient)
  • Ambient/drone (Drew McDowall, Time Machines, Tor Lundvall)
