Les Enfants Du Paradiddle

Discogs ID: 115736
Location: Aberdeen, Scotland, UK
Run by: Alan Davidson (4)

Self-run micro-label operated by Alan Davidson (4) (Kitchen Cynics) as the primary outlet for his own solo recordings and those of close collaborators. One of the most prolific CDr/cassette imprints in the UK folk underground, with 135+ releases on Discogs.

Catalog spans Kitchen Cynics solo albums (from 1989), Matricarians, Kitchen Cynics Orchestra, Kitchen Cynics Drone Trio, and Barrett’s Dottled Beauty, plus compilations and releases by affiliated artists.
