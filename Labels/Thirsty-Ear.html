Thirsty Ear

Discogs ID: 932

American independent record label based in New York. Founded in the early 1990s; transitioned into a major free jazz and experimental imprint through the 2000s.

Home of the Blue Series (2001–c.2006), curated by Matthew Shipp as Artistic Director, which produced crossover releases bridging free jazz, hip-hop, and electronic music. Key releases include Shipp’s Pastoral Composure, Optometry (with DJ Spooky), Equilibrium, Art Of The Improviser, and many more. Executive-Producer across the catalog: Peter Gordon (3).
