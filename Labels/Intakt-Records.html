Intakt Records

Swiss avant-garde jazz and improvised music label. Based in Zurich.

One of the key European labels for creative improvised music since the 1980s. Released a substantial portion of Fred Frith’s late-career output including The Compass, Log, And Lead (2006), Backscatter Bright Blue (2014), Edge Of The Light (2014), It Rolls (2015), and You Are Here (2017). Run by Patrik Landolt (ID: 864591).
