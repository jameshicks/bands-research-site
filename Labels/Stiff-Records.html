Stiff Records

UK independent record label. Founded London, 1976. Discogs label ID: 11617. Parent label (current): Universal Music Group (38404).

Overview

One of the most important British independent labels of the punk and new wave era. Founded by Dave Robinson (3) and Jake Riviera in 1976. Stiff was known for its irreverent marketing (“If it ain’t Stiff, it ain’t worth a fuck”), sharp visual identity (many sleeves designed by Barney Bubbles), and a roster that encompassed pub rock, punk, new wave, ska, and pop.

Key Stiff artists include Madness (five studio LPs, 1979–1984), Elvis Costello (early albums), Ian Dury and the Blockheads, Nick Lowe, Lene Lovich, The Damned (debut), and Devo (licensed). Madness were the label’s most commercially successful UK singles act in the early 1980s.

Stiff had 11 sublabels on Discogs. The label went through financial difficulties and ownership changes through the 1980s; its catalog eventually came under Universal.
