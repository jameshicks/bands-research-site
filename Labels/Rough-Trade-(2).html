Overview

Rough Trade (2) is Discogs’ designator for counterfeit or bootleg releases falsely attributed to the genuine Rough Trade label. The concentrated roster — The Smiths and all four of their individual members — indicates that forgers overwhelmingly targeted that band’s catalogue, unsurprising given The Smiths’ commercial profile and cult status. Fake pressings surface from 1984 onward, with the bulk of documented entries concentrated in the 2000s and 2010s, spanning styles from Indie Rock and New Wave to Shoegaze and Garage Rock in line with Rough Trade’s authentic output.
