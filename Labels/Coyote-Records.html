Overview

Coyote Records was an indie label founded by Bill Ryan and Steve Fallon, based in the New Jersey/New York orbit, that joined forces with Minneapolis’s Twin/Tone Records in 1985. Its catalogue is concentrated in the 1980s — 23 of its 31 releases fell in that decade — with indie rock and alternative rock at its core and a country-rock thread running through. The label is most closely associated with The Feelies: all five members of that band’s classic lineup (Glenn Mercer, Bill Million, Brenda Sauter, Stanley Demeski, Dave Weckerman) appear on its roster. Staff included Glenn Morrow and Julie Panebianco, both figures in the broader New Jersey indie scene. A small cluster of releases in the 2010s and 2020s points to some form of later activity, likely tied to the Feelies’ reunion era.
