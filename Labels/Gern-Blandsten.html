Overview

Gern Blandsten is an independent New Jersey label owned by Charles Maggio, operating under the motto “fine musical products for the socially unaccepted.” Founded in 1991, it built its catalogue around hardcore, punk, and emo, later widening into indie rock and alternative — releasing 28 titles through the 1990s and 33 more through the 2000s before tapering off. Its roster has included Rorschach and Alap Momin, among others.
