Passport Records

US-based independent record label, 1973–1988. Parent: Jem Records. South Plainfield, NJ. Discogs label ID: 23950.

Overview

Founded in 1973 by Jem Records (co-founded 1971 by Martin L. Scott, Jeff Tenenbaum, and Ed Grossi) in partnership with Sire Records; that partnership ended in 1977 when Sire was sold to Warner Bros. Passport became the flagship label of the Passport Records Group, which grew to include PVC Records, Passport Jazz, Audion Recording Co, Import Records, and Visa Records. Distributed by Jem in the US and GRT Records in Canada.

Artists included Larry Fast (Synergy), FM, Al Stewart, Nektar, Gerry Rafferty, Link Wray, Human Sexual Response, Wendy O. Williams, Brand X, Anthony Phillips, Mike Rutherford, Robbie Krieger, David Johansen, Richard Barone, Utopia, Bill Nelson, and Camel.

In 1986 Passport began operating separately from Jem internally while still distributed through it. The entire group collapsed with Jem’s bankruptcy in 1988. Marty Scott subsequently took Paradox Records (another sublabel) to MCA.

(Source: Wikipedia, “Passport Records”)
