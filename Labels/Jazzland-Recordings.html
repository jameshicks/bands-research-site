Jazzland Recordings

Norwegian jazz and experimental label. Discogs label ID: 28640. Label Code: LC 00808 / LC 0808. Parent: Jazzland Publishing (ID: 513698). URLs: http://www.jazzlandrec.com/

Founded by Bugge Wesseltoft and associated with key figures in the Norwegian jazz-electronica scene including Eivind Aarset, Sidsel Endresen, and Nils Petter Molvær. The Discogs profile credits the label as “fronted by” Bugge Wesseltoft, Wibutee (Jan Bang’s pre-Punkt group, Discogs ID 32275), Sidsel Endresen, Beady Belle (ID 15263), and Eivind Aarset.

Overview

One of the central Norwegian jazz labels from the late 1990s through the 2020s, with 629+ releases. Key artists include Bugge Wesseltoft, Beady Belle, Eivind Aarset, Sidsel Endresen, and Torun Eriksen. The label also carries significant Jan Bang solo output (Narrative From The Subtropics, 2013; Snow Catches On Her Eyelashes, 2020) and the Punkt (2) project (Live Remixes Vol. 1, 2008).
