Fat Wreck Chords

San Francisco punk and pop-punk label founded by NOFX frontman Fat Mike (Mike Burkett). One of the central labels for 1990s US melodic hardcore and punk. Discogs label ID: 33296.

Key artists: NOFX, Propagandhi, No Use for a Name, Bad Religion, Strung Out, Lagwagon. John Yates handled design and layout on multiple releases including Bad Religion’s The Longest Line, Propagandhi’s How To Clean Everything, and Today’s Empires, Tomorrow’s Ashes.
