Sixth International

US-based independent label associated with David Thomas (2) (Pere Ubu). Held the phonographic copyright on Variations On A Theme (1983) by David Thomas And The Pedestrians, suggesting Thomas used it as his own US imprint. Licensed the album to Rough Trade (UK/Germany) and Plurex (Benelux) for those territories.
