Overview

Flannelgraph Records is an independent label from Bloomington, Indiana, whose catalogue is almost entirely concentrated in the 2010s — one release dates to 2002, while the remaining 32 span that decade. The label is best known for Laminar Excursion Monthly, a 3″ CD-R subscription series co-produced with fellow Bloomington label Crossroads Of America in 2010. Its roster skews toward indie rock and folk with experimental and synth-pop threads, and includes Don Muro, Mike Bridavsky, Jared Cheek, and Mike Adams (who appears both under his own name and as Mike Adams At His Honest Weight).
