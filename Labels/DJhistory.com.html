Overview

DJhistory.com is the label imprint of music journalists and historians Bill Brewster and Frank Broughton, extending the reach of their long-running website of the same name. Active from 2008, the label issued releases across the 2000s and 2010s — 28 in total — with a focus on archival compilations and curated sets drawing from disco, house, acid house, leftfield, and new wave. Featured artists include Bob Blank, Quinton Scott, and Simon Francis alongside the co-founders themselves.
