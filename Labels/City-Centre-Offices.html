City Centre Offices

Discogs ID: 191

Berlin-based independent electronic label active in the late 1990s–2000s.

Released the Duckie / Glued Smile split 7” (BLOCK 003, 1999) by Solvent and Lowfish, among other releases in the IDM and electro space.
