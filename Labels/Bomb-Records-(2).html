Overview

Bomb Records was a Canadian independent label launched in 1978 by Wolfgang Spegg and Phil Lubman, both veterans of distributor P.J. Imports, which also served as its initial distributor. Its catalogue of roughly 31 releases is concentrated in the 1970s and spans an eclectic range: punk and new wave acts such as Battered Wives share space with power pop from Bob Segarini and Krautrock from Klaus Schulze. In 1979 the label escalated its reach by signing a global distribution deal with CBS Records Canada, releasing through the Epic imprint, but that arrangement collapsed within a year and Bomb switched to Rio Records in 1980.
