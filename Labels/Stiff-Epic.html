Overview

Stiff-Epic was a short-lived joint venture between Stiff Records and CBS, Inc., operating from 1978 to 1981 under the tagline “The world’s most flexible label.” The arrangement placed Stiff’s punk-adjacent New Wave roster — including Ian Dury, Lene Lovich, and Chas Jankel — under a major-label distribution umbrella. The catalogue is split evenly across the late 1970s and early 1980s, drawing on New Wave, Pop Rock, Synth-pop, Disco, and Dub, reflecting both the label’s origins in the post-punk moment and its brief reach into the emerging synthesiser era. The venture was defunct by 1981.
