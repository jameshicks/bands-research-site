Overview

Rattlesnake Records was an Australian independent label that operated through the 1980s and 1990s, distributed domestically by Survival Music and in Germany by Normal Records. Its catalogue of roughly 21 releases leaned toward the 1990s and centred on garage rock and punk, with grunge and indie rock also in the mix. The roster included Cosmic Psychos, Psychotic Turnbuckles, and guitarist Chris Masuak, placing the label at the heart of Australia’s raw, guitar-driven underground. The Normal Records arrangement gave it a foothold in the German indie market — an uncommon reach for a small Australian imprint.
