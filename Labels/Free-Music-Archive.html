Overview

The Free Music Archive began as an initiative of WFMU, the freeform New Jersey radio station, launching in 2009 as a curated library of legally downloadable audio. In 2019 it passed to Tribe Of Noise, a commercial music licensing platform. Its catalogue of 51 releases is concentrated in the 2010s and skewed toward Experimental, Noise, and Avantgarde styles, with roster artists including People Like Us, Ergo Phizmiz, and Delphine Dora. Two sublabels document specific events tied to its WFMU origins: All Tomorrow’s Parties NY 2010 on WFMU and PS11 On WFMU.
