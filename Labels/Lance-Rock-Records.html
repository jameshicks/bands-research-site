Overview

Lance Rock Records is a Canadian indie label founded in 1990 and operated by Jack T. Its catalogue is concentrated in the 1990s — 26 releases in that decade alone — spanning punk, garage rock, alternative rock, and country rock, with output tapering to a handful of releases per decade after 2000. The roster includes Neko Case, Kurt Bloch, Darryl Neudorf, and Craig Schumacher, placing the label squarely within the Vancouver indie and alt-country milieu of the era.
