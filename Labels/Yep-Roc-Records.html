Yep Roc Records

Discogs ID: 37255
Origin: Haw River, North Carolina, USA
Founded: 1997

American independent label. Home to Robyn Hitchcock from Spooked (2004) through Robyn Hitchcock (2017). Also released material by Nick Lowe, Dave Edmunds, The Minus 5, and various Americana and power pop artists.

Chris Stamey Connection

Chris Stamey has both released solo records on Yep Roc (V.O.T.E. 2004, Lovesick Blues 2013, Euphoria 2015) and produced multiple Yep Roc releases (Summertown 1999, Waltzie 2000, While You Weren’t Looking 2002, I’m Staying Out 2003, Route 23 2005, IV 2008).
