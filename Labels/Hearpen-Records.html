Hearpen Records

Independent label. Cleveland, Ohio. Founded 1975 by David Thomas (2) (David Ormonde Thomas). Discogs label ID: 74508. Parent label of Hearthan, Hearpen, and Hearpen Music.

Overview

Hearpen Records was created by David Thomas (2) in 1975 to put out Pere Ubu records. It is the legal and administrative umbrella above several sub-imprints (see below) and over time became a production and management entity, operating as part of the Ubu Projex framework in America. The label was the primary self-release vehicle for the Cleveland proto-punk scene’s most important documents, and continued functioning into the 2000s and 2020s as an archival and reissue imprint.

The label’s name derives from an Anglo-Saxon word for harp; the “p” in “Hearpen” is a transliteration of the Anglo-Saxon thorn (a character pronounced “th”), making the name cognate with its sublabel “Hearthan” (pronounced hay-are-than, rhyming with “van”). The rule that evolved: in written form the name is “Hearpen”; integrated into a graphic design it becomes “Hearthan.” The logo was designed on a day it read “Hearthan.” Discogs describes this as “Confusion was the foundation on which their business grew.”

On 11 December 2007, hearpen.com went online (associated with the sublabel Hearpen.com) as a site dedicated to high-quality audio downloads from Pere Ubu, David Thomas, Rocket From The Tombs, 15-60-75, and related bands.

Per Wikipedia’s Cleveland punk article, Thomas founded Hearpen in 1975 as “a partial document of the Cleveland scene.” (Wikipedia: Cleveland punk)

Hearthan vs Hearpen: Hearthan is a sublabel of Hearpen Records, not a separate umbrella. All early Pere Ubu singles were physically released under the Hearthan imprint. Hearpen Records is the legal parent that holds the catalog, and later releases — compilations, live albums, reissues, co-releases — appear under the Hearpen or Hearpen Records name directly.

Connections

  • Pere Ubu — primary associated act; Hearpen was created for and around Pere Ubu
  • David Thomas (2) — founder; also credited as producer across multiple releases
  • Hearthan — sublabel; original 1975–1977 singles imprint
  • Hearpen — sublabel (Discogs ID: 334322); mid-period and digital releases
  • Hearpen Music — sublabel (Discogs ID: 431741)
  • Hearpen.com — sublabel of Hearpen (ID: 1294800); digital download imprint from 2007
  • Mirrors — Cleveland contemporaries; released “Shirley / She Smiled Wild” (HR 105) in 1977
  • 15-60-75 — Cleveland blues-rock act; released Jimmy Bell’s Still In Town (HR112, 2000)
  • Pressler-Morgan One Plus One — Cleveland project; released HR107 (1979)
  • Rocket From The Tombs — predecessor band to Pere Ubu; archival release co-issued via Hearthan
  • Paul Hamann — recurring engineer/mastering credit across Hearpen releases
  • Ken Hamann — recurring engineer on early Hearthan singles
  • Peter Laughner — guitarist on first two Hearthan singles; died 1977
