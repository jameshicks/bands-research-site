Overview

Mode Records is a New York-based label founded in 1984 by Brian Brandt, devoted to contemporary classical and experimental music. Its catalogue centres on the major figures of the American avant-garde and European spectralism — John Cage, Iannis Xenakis, and Morton Feldman — and has expanded through dedicated sub-imprints for Christian Wolff, Horatiu Radulescu, and Roger Reynolds. Output grew steadily from the first releases in 1985, peaking in the 2000s with 19 catalogue entries that decade, and the label remains active into the 2020s. Several low-numbered catalogue slots were reserved for US-distributed releases rather than Mode originals, including early LPs by Kirk Nurock and Petr Kotik.
