Chess Club

UK independent record label. Discogs label ID: 112076 (also “Chess Club Records”, ID: 561930). London-based; 339+ releases on main label ID.

Overview

Early home of Wolf Alice, releasing their first four singles (Fluffy, Bros, Blush, She) in 2013 before the band signed to Dirty Hit. Has also released early material by a range of UK indie acts.
