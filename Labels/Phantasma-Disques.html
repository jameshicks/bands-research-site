Phantasma Disques

German experimental/witch-house/okkvlt label founded by Cosmotropia de Xam. Discogs label ID: 223703. Active from 2011 to the present. 880+ releases in the Discogs database.

Overview

Phantasma Disques is the primary label vehicle of Cosmotropia de Xam and the central infrastructure of the German “okkvlt” and witch-house scene in the early 2010s. Releases span CDr, DVDr, cassette, vinyl, and digital formats, running the gamut from solo DVDr film works by CdX (directed) to audio releases by dozens of affiliated artists worldwide. Nearly all physical releases feature CdX’s handmade collage cover card artwork as a visual signature.

The label functions simultaneously as a self-publishing system, an archival compilation project (the A Sound Installation Tribute To… series, A Witch House & Okkvlt Guide To… series), and a distribution point for international witch-house affiliates. Artists on the label include MSV, How I Quit Crack, and a wide network of US, European, and Japanese acts.
