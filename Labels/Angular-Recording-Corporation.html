Overview

Angular Recording Corporation is a UK independent label, a subsidiary of Angular Recording Corporation Ltd., that began releasing music in 2003. Its catalogue of roughly 59 releases skews heavily toward the 2000s and spans indie rock, new wave, synth-pop, darkwave, and leftfield sounds. The label is best known as the home of These New Puritans, whose principal member Jack Barnett also appears on the roster in a solo capacity; other signed acts include The Violets, Gyratory System, and Anthony Barratt. Output thinned in the 2010s, with 21 releases catalogued against 38 in its first decade.

Decline and Closure

On 8 August 2011, during the England riots, the Sony DADC warehouse in Enfield — which also served as PIAS’s primary UK distribution centre — was destroyed by fire. Angular lost all of its physical stock and its entire back catalogue in the blaze, along with over 100 other independent labels distributed through PIAS. (Wikipedia — Angular Recording Corporation, as of 2026-08-23; The Line of Best Fit.) No public statement from Angular’s founders Joe Daniel or Joe Margetts about the fire’s impact on the label has been located.

The label produced only sporadic releases after the fire (five in 2012 across This Many Boyfriends, Eleven Pond, Fresh Touch, and Society), and went defunct in 2012. The final catalogued release was This Many Boyfriends (ARC 097, October 2012).

These New Puritans — Label Transition

TNP’s last Angular releases were in 2010: Hidden (ARC065CD), We Want War (ARC064), Attack Music (ARC072), Hologram (ARC076), and Hidden Remixes (ARC079). No releases followed on Angular.

The contractual picture by 2010 was already divided: Hidden was co-released by Angular and Domino Recording Company, with Domino holding the master copyright and Angular holding an exclusive UK licence — ”℗ & © 2010 Domino Recording Co. Ltd. Under exclusive license in the UK to Angular Recording Corporation.” (Wikipedia — Hidden, as of 2026-08-23.) This suggests TNP’s primary deal was with Domino by the time of their second album, with Angular acting as a UK sub-licensee rather than the band’s direct label.

For their third album Field of Reeds (June 2013), TNP moved entirely to Infectious Music (a Domino affiliate) for UK releases, with Domino handling other territories. (Wikipedia — Field of Reeds, as of 2026-08-23.)

No direct statement from the band or from Angular’s founders about the departure has been located. The available evidence points to a structural transition rather than an announced split: by 2010 Domino effectively held the rights to TNP’s recordings, Angular’s capacity to function collapsed after the August 2011 warehouse fire, and the band’s next release simply appeared on an Infectious/Domino imprint. Whether the band formally exited an Angular contract before or after the label’s closure is not documented in any source found.
