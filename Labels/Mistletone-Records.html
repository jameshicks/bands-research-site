Overview

Mistletone Records is an independent Melbourne label founded in 2006 by Ash Miles and Sophie Best (formerly Sophie Miles), operating as the recorded-music arm of a broader Mistletone enterprise that also ran tour promotion, booking, and publicity out of Australia. The catalogue of roughly 62 releases is concentrated in the 2010s, spanning dream pop, experimental indie, and synth-pop. Its roster drew notably from American acts — Beach House members Victoria Legrand and Alex Scally, and Chaz Bundick — alongside Australian noise duo HTRK, suggesting an international reach unusual for an independent label of its size and geography.
