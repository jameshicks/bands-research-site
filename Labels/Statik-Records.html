Statik Records

UK independent label. West London. Active c. 1980–1988+. Discogs ID: 4869. Label Code: LC 7924. Parent company: Statik Records Ltd. (ID 354522). Founded by Laurie Dunn.

Named after the Second City Statik — A Glasgow Compilation (1980, catno 01), a Glasgow new wave sampler that was the label’s first release. The first act signed was Positive Noise, a Glasgow synthpop band — establishing a Glasgow-to-West-London pipeline that defined the label’s early identity. Distributed by Virgin throughout most of its run, which ironically disqualified label acts from the UK independent charts (the Chameleons’ Discogs profile specifically notes this frustration).

Over roughly a decade, Statik released ~872 releases across post-punk, new wave, synth-pop, hardcore, no wave, dub, and art-rock, signing US acts (Dead Kennedys, Men Without Hats), British acts (The Chameleons, The Sound, New Age Steppers, Tom Robinson), and international acts (Gina X, TC Matic, Yukihiro Takahashi, DAF).

Founding and Name

The label was named after the Second City Statik — A Glasgow Compilation (1980), a Various Artists release (catno 01) featuring Glasgow new wave acts including Positive Noise. Photography and artwork on the comp by Tom Cannavan. The first proper catalog release was Yukihiro Takahashi’s Murdered By The Music (1980, Stat S 17), an early solo outing from the YMO drummer.

Roster

Positive Noise

Glasgow new wave/synthpop band. Discogs ID: 293706. The label’s first signing and most prolific early act (79 releases, 1981–1985). Brothers Ross, Fraser, and Graham Middleton with Les Gaff and Russell Blackstock. Key releases: Heart Of Darkness LP (1981, catno stat lp1), Charm / Give Me Passion singles (1981).

Dead Kennedys

San Francisco hardcore band. Discogs ID: 239092. 77 Statik releases 1981–1989. Statik was their primary UK licensee. Key releases: In God We Trust, Inc. EP (1981, catno STAT EP2, 26 pressings); Bleed For Me (1982, 13 pressings); Halloween (1982); Plastic Surgery Disasters LP (1982, catno STAT LP 11, 29 pressings — the label’s most-pressed master). Plastic Surgery Disasters / In God We Trust, Inc. CD (1985).

Men Without Hats

Canadian new wave band. Discogs ID: 24170. 160 releases on Statik — the label’s single highest release count. Based in Montreal; led by Ivan Doroschuk. Rhythm Of Youth LP (1982, catno STAT LP 10, 39 pressings) contained “The Safety Dance” — the label’s biggest commercial hit. Personnel on Rhythm Of Youth: Ivan Doroschuk (vocals, guitar, percussion, piano), Colin Doroschuk (guitar), Stefan Doroschuk (guitar, violin), Martin Cartier (percussion), Alan McCarthy (piano, percussion), Michel Jerome (castanets), Daniel Vermette (acoustic guitar), Anne Dussault (voice); produced by Marc Durand. “The Safety Dance (Extended Club Mix)” (1982, catno TAK 1-12, 43 pressings) was their biggest UK 12”.

The Chameleons

Post-punk band from Middleton, near Manchester. Discogs ID: 137234. 72 releases, 1983–1987. Members: Mark Burgess (vocals), Reg Smithies (guitar), Dave Fielding (guitar), John Lever (drums, d. 2017). Previously one single on CBS; CBS dropped them after the first single and they signed to Statik. Script Of The Bridge LP (1983, catno STAT LP 17, 23 pressings) was their debut; recorded in Rochdale over six weeks. What Does Anything Mean? Basically (1985). Statik’s Virgin distribution made them ineligible for the indie charts; they were unhappy with the label and left after their second album to sign to Geffen. Manager Tony Fletcher died suddenly in 1987, leading to the band’s breakup.

The Sound (2)

Post-punk band from South London. Discogs ID: 171148. 52 releases, 1984–1987. Led by Adrian Borland (d. April 26, 1999); also Colvin “Max” Mayers (d. Boxing Day 1993), Graham “Greene” Bailey, Michael Dudley. Key releases: Shock Of Daylight EP (1984, 16 pressings); Heads And Hearts LP (1985); In The Hothouse LP (1985).

New Age Steppers

Post-punk dub collective. Discogs ID: 23698. 15 releases, 1981–1991. Produced by Adrian Sherwood. Action Battlefield LP (1981, catno STAT LP 2) featured Ari-Up (The Slits) on lead vocals and piano/organ/percussion; George Oban on bass; Tony Phillips on guitar; engineered by John Walker (2) (“Tightrope” Walker) and Bob Zimbler. Also: The New Age Steppers LP (1981, catno STAT LP 9); My Love single (1981). Last release 1991.

Yukihiro Takahashi

Japanese musician. Discogs ID: 58981. Drummer of Yellow Magic Orchestra. 19 Statik releases, 1980–1983. Murdered By The Music (1980, catno Stat S 17) was the label’s first catalog release; second pressing was Murdered By The Music LP (1982, catno STAT LP 6).

The Dance

NYC no wave band. Discogs ID: 47969. 17 releases, 1981–1982. See The Dance. Key releases: In Lust LP (1981, catno LP 3, 8 pressings); In Lust remix (1982, catno STAT 712, remixed with John Walker (2)); Soul Force LP (1982, catno STAT LP 12, also licensed to Teldec for Germany); Stay Down single (1982, catno STAT 16).

Jeffrey Lee Pierce

American singer-songwriter and guitarist. Discogs ID: 262749. Born June 27, 1958; died March 31, 1996. Founding member of The Gun Club. 24 solo releases on Statik, 1985–1986. Key releases: Flamingo (1985, catno STAB 5); Wildweed LP (1985, catno CDST 25, 13 pressings); Love & Desperation (1985).

Gina X

Electronic/new wave artist. Discogs ID: 52866. 24 releases, 1984–1986. Drive My Car (1984); Harley Davidson (1984); Yinglish LP (1984, catno STAT LP 21); No G·D·M (1985); Nice Mover LP (1986, catno STAT LP 30).

The Flying Lizards

Art-pop group. Discogs ID: 24165. 16 releases, 1984–1985. Key releases: Dizzy Miss Lizzie (1984, catno TAK 25/12); Sex Machine (1984, catno TAK 19/12, 5 pressings); Top Ten LP (1984, 8 pressings).

Thomas Dolby

British synth musician. Discogs ID: 24046. 2 early releases on Statik 1981–1983, predating his Parlophone/EMI career. Urges single (1981, catno Stat-29).

TC Matic

Belgian post-punk band. Discogs ID: 193545. 11 releases, 1981–1983. Early Statik signing.

New Race

Australian supergroup. Discogs ID: 637853. 5 releases, 1983. The First And The Last LP (1983, catno STAT LP16). Featuring ex-Radio Birdman and ex-MC5 members.

Luna Twist

Discogs ID: 266663. 20 releases, 1982–1983. UK new wave act. African Time single (1982); Luna Twist LP (1983).

Tom Robinson

British singer-songwriter. Discogs ID: 250581. Post-TRB solo work on Statik, 8 releases 1982–1988.

The Blockheads

The Birthday Party

Distribution and Business

Distributed by Virgin throughout most of the label’s run. This arrangement gave Statik mainstream distribution reach — enabling German licensing (Teldec for Soul Force), Spanish, Portuguese, and Greek territory pressings — but it had a significant cost: acts on Virgin-distributed labels were ineligible for the UK independent charts. The Chameleons specifically noted this as a grievance. The label used multiple catalog sub-series (STAT, TAK, STAB, CDST) suggesting evolving internal organization across the decade.
