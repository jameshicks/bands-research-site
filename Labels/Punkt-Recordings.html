Punkt Recordings

Norwegian independent label. Discogs label ID: 72660. No parent label; no sublabels. No profile text or contact info in Discogs.

Punkt Recordings is the original release imprint of the Punkt (2) project — the Kristiansand-based live-remix trio of Jan Bang, Erik Honoré, and Arve Henriksen. It exists solely to carry the project’s debut album Crime Scenes (2006), with two entries in the Discogs database: the Norwegian original pressing and a German reissue the following year. No other releases were issued under this name. By the time Punkt released further recordings, the home had shifted to Jazzland Recordings (Live Remixes Vol. 1, 2008) and eventually to the dedicated Punkt Editions sublabel from 2022 onward.

Crime Scenes (2006)

Genres: Electronic, Jazz, Pop. Styles: Abstract, Contemporary Jazz, Experimental.

A 13-track document assembled from early Punkt Festival live recordings, capturing the live-remix method in its earliest documented form. Bang and Honoré produced and recorded the bulk of the album; Sidsel Endresen is credited as a co-recorder on three tracks (1, 6, 12). The album uses source performances as raw material — instrument recordings from festival appearances are sampled, processed, and reassembled, with original performers contributing to specific tracks.

Context: The Punkt Label Sequence

Punkt Recordings sits at the start of a three-label trajectory for the project:

  1. Punkt Recordings (ID 72660, 2006–2007) — self-released imprint; sole release is Crime Scenes
  2. Jazzland Recordings (ID 28640, 2008) — Live Remixes Vol. 1, the project’s first album on an established Norwegian label
  3. Punkt Editions (ID 2884424, 2022–present) — dedicated sublabel of Jazzland, launched as a formal archive and ongoing platform; run by Bang and Honoré

The reason Punkt Recordings remained a one-release label is most likely structural: Crime Scenes was self-released while the trio established its identity, and subsequent releases found a home within the existing Norwegian jazz infrastructure via Jazzland and the Bugge Wesseltoft network. Punkt Editions, which the Discogs profile describes as “organised as a sub-label of Bugge Wesseltoft’s Jazzland Recordings,” formalised this arrangement decades later.
