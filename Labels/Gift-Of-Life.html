Overview

Gift Of Life Records is a punk and alternative rock label that launched in 1987 and built most of its catalogue across the 1990s, amassing 36 releases. Its roster is tightly clustered around the Wipers and associated figures — Greg Sage (the Wipers’ frontman), the Wipers themselves, and Greg Keith and Chris Keith all appear as roster acts. The label’s output spans punk, hardcore, indie rock, and garage rock, suggesting a home base in Portland’s post-punk underground.
