From Discogs

Czech independent label from Brno. Established in 1990, active until 2006. Divided into three labels in January 2007: “Indies Scope” (Milan Páleš), “Indies Happy Trails” (Jaromír Kratochvíl) and “Indies MG” (Miloš Gruber), all sharing the same distributor.

— Discogs label profile, ID 42854

Overview

Indies Records was a Czech independent label based in Brno, operating from 1990 to 2006. Its roughly 160-release catalogue concentrates heavily in the 1990s and covers folk, folk rock, alternative rock, indie rock, and avant-garde styles, making it one of the more stylistically broad Czech independents of the era. The roster included prominent figures in Czech alternative and world-folk music: Iva Bittová, Zuzana Navarová, Radůza, Vladimír Václavek, and Milan Cais among others. In January 2007 the label dissolved into three successor operations sharing a common distributor — Indies Scope under Milan Páleš, Indies Happy Trails under Jaromír Kratochvíl, and Indies MG under Miloš Gruber.
