Shandar

Discogs ID: 21072

French avant-garde music label, active 1971–1979. Co-founded by Daniel Caux and Chantal Darcy, financed by Aimé and Marguerite Maeght (founders of the Fondation Maeght). Much of the catalog derived from recordings of Maeght-sponsored concerts (“Nuits de la Fondation Maeght”). Shandar closed in 1979 after a flood damaged its vinyl stocks and recordings. [sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandar, as of 2026-07-13; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Caux, as of 2026-07-13]

Founding and the Fondation Maeght Connection

Daniel Caux organized the “Nuits de la Fondation Maeght” concert series starting in 1969 (Cecil Taylor, 29 July 1969) and expanding in 1970 (Albert Ayler, Sun Ra). The concerts were financed by Aimé Maeght, and recordings were issued on the Shandar label that Caux and Darcy subsequently founded to release them. Shandar is therefore both institutionally and financially connected to the Fondation Maeght; the Maeghts provided the backing. [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandar, as of 2026-07-13]
