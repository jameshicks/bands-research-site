Overview

EE Tapes is a Belgian independent label founded in Sint-Niklaas in 1987 by Eriek van Havere, focused on experimental, ambient, and noise music. After a slow start with a handful of releases in the late 1980s and 1990s, the label grew more active through the 2000s and reached its most prolific phase in the 2010s with nineteen releases. It operates two sublabels — Notre Dame (2) and The Walls Are Whispering… — and its roster has included figures such as Alain Neffe, Guy Marc Hinant, and Jan Van Den Broeke, working across minimal, industrial, and synth-pop territory.
