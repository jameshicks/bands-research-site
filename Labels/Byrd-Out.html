Byrd Out

UK independent record label, 2017–present. Discogs label ID: 2324854. Parent company: Byrd Out Limited (ID: 1850762). Founded and run by Andrew Weatherall in the later phase of his career. Named as a tip of the hat to American jazz trumpeter Donald Byrd.

Email: byrdout@gmail.com. Bandcamp: byrdout.bandcamp.com.

Overview

Byrd Out launched in 2017 and served as the primary outlet for Weatherall’s late-period original work, while simultaneously championing a broad and eclectic roster that stretched from free jazz to acid techno to Kenyan experimental electronics. The label’s name is a pun on the trumpeter Donald Byrd’s surname, signalling from the start that it was not going to be narrowly genre-defined.

Weatherall released three EPs on the label under his own name — Kiyadub EP (BYR005, 2017), Blue Bullet EP (BYR012, 2018), and Unknown Plunderer / End Times Sound (BYR023, 2020) — and a fourth body of work under his joint alias The Woodleigh Research Facility, which he ran with longtime collaborator Nina Walsh. All Weatherall-credited material carries co-writing credits for both Weatherall and Walsh.

Weatherall died in February 2020. Unknown Plunderer / End Times Sound (BYR023) had already been scheduled and was released posthumously in spring 2020, becoming one of the most-noted final statements in his discography. The label did not close with his death; releases continued through 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, and into 2025, suggesting the operation passed to whoever managed Byrd Out Limited or was taken over by trusted associates.

The label’s profile page describes its range as “from electronic to experimental.”

Catalog Structure

Discogs lists 83 release entries for the label, but these collapse to a substantially smaller number of distinct titles — many entries represent separate pressings of the same release (vinyl, digital, different territories). Unique masters number around 35–40.

Catalog numbers run BYR001–BYR049 (with some gaps and variant suffixes such as BYR 47V, BYR40V). The series is broadly chronological: BYR001–BYR014 cover 2017–2018, BYR015–BYR025 cover 2019–2020, BYR028–BYR039 cover 2021–2022, and BYR040–BYR049 cover 2023–2024.

Notable: BYR003 was co-released with Ariwa, Lee Perry’s dub label, for the Mad Professor compilation.

Key Artists

Andrew Weatherall (Discogs ID: 2657)

Andrew Weatherall released three EPs under his own name (BYR005, BYR012, BYR023) and additional material as The Woodleigh Research Facility. His eight primary-credited releases on Byrd Out span 2017–2020 and represent his most concentrated body of late-period original studio work.

The Woodleigh Research Facility (Discogs ID: 4689420)

Duo project of Andrew Weatherall and Nina Walsh. Active as primary credited artists on Byrd Out through at least 2024 (BYR048, Vous Du EP), making it one of the longest-running threads on the label. The WRF alias was used for more experimental/collaborative directions.

DMX Krew (Discogs ID: 983)

Edward Upton, UK electronic producer, is the joint top-credited artist on the label alongside Weatherall with eight releases spanning 2017–2024 (BYR014 through BYR047). His continued presence post-2020 is one of the anchors of the label’s ongoing identity.

Evan Parker / John Edwards / John Russell (Discogs IDs: 42708 / 292492 / 292491)

Free jazz trio whose Walthamstow Moon (‘61 Revisited) (BYR002) was one of the label’s founding releases in 2017. Evan Parker returned with Binker Golding, John Edwards, and Steve Noble on Moon Day (BYR030, 2021) and solo on Winns Win (BYR031, 2021).

Binker Golding (Discogs ID: 2590111)

UK jazz saxophonist, six releases on the label across 2019–2021 including the duo album Ex Nihilo (BYR016) with Elliot Galvin, and the Evan Parker trio. Also credited for artwork on some releases.

KMRU (Discogs ID: 7020237)

Kenyan electronic/experimental artist Joseph Kamaru. Erased EP (BYR017, 2019) was an early UK release for him; returned in 2023 for a split with Hibotep (BYR040).

Label Structure and Context

Byrd Out operates as a sublabel of Byrd Out Limited (ID: 1850762), the holding company. Byrd Out Limited has no profile text or additional sublabels in the Discogs database, and appears to be purely a legal/corporate entity for the label.

In the context of Weatherall’s wider output, Byrd Out sits alongside Rotters Golf Club as one of his primary imprints in the 2010s. Where Rotters Golf Club focused more on Weatherall’s DJ and remix output, Byrd Out served as the home for original productions and a wider curatorial remit. The label was notably eclectic even by Weatherall’s standards: the roster in 2017–2019 ranged from free jazz (Evan Parker) to dub (Mad Professor, Lee Perry) to UK club music (Roska, Mark Archer) to African electronic music (KMRU, Faizal Ddamba Mostrixx) to acid techno (Hardfloor).

Post-2020 Continuity

After Weatherall’s death in February 2020, the label did not cease. At minimum the following happened:

  • Unknown Plunderer / End Times Sound (BYR023) was released as scheduled in 2020
  • The Woodleigh Research Facility continued releasing on the label through 2024 under the WRF name, with Vous Du EP (BYR048) — the personnel question is unresolved (whether Nina Walsh operates WRF solo or with other collaborators post-Weatherall is not answered by Discogs data)
  • New artists appeared on the roster (Anatolian Weapons, Santaka, Betamax/Clive Bell collaborations expanding, Hibotep)
  • Catalog numbers continued to BYR049 as of 2024; a 2025 release (Space Blanket (2)) also appears

Who controls the label after Weatherall’s death is not documented in the Discogs database.
