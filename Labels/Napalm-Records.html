Discogs catalog size: ~6,700 releases (as of 2025)

Austrian independent heavy music label. Founded in Eisenerz, a small industrial town in the Austrian Alps, it began as a black/death metal imprint and evolved over three decades into one of Europe’s largest independent metal labels spanning symphonic metal, gothic/doom, folk metal, stoner/doom, hard rock, and power metal.

History & Overview

Napalm Records issued its first release in 1992 — Where The Blood For Ever Rains by Austrian death metal band Disastrous Murmur (cat. NPR 001). Through the mid-1990s the label concentrated on underground black metal from the emerging Central European and Scandinavian scenes, signing early Austrian black metal acts Abigor and building a catalog with Norwegian and Swedish acts.

By the late 1990s the label had diversified into symphonic and gothic metal, signing Tristania (Norway), Summoning (Austria), and early folk metal acts. The 2000s saw further expansion into the nascent folk/Viking metal genre (Korpiklaani, Týr, Alestorm) and the symphonic metal boom (Leaves’ Eyes, Visions Of Atlantis, Sirenia).

The 2010s brought a significant roster upgrade: Powerwolf (the label’s most commercially successful act by release volume), Alter Bridge, Monster Magnet, and Jinjer (2) all joined. By this period Napalm had offices in Berlin and North America and was distributing globally.

The parent corporate entity — Napalm Records Handels GmbH (Discogs ID: 271784) — also oversees unrelated sublabels including Iron Avantgarde Publishing (music publishing) and SPV GmbH (the German rock/metal distributor).

The early catalog is notable for concentrating Austrian and Scandinavian black/gothic metal at a time when these scenes had few dedicated labels. Abigor and Summoning, both from Vienna/Lower Austria, were the label’s flagship domestic acts in this period.

Notable Roster (Selected)

Black / Pagan / Folk Metal

  • Abigor — Austrian black metal; on Napalm from 1994 through 2001 (NPR 005 through NPR088). Among the most catalog-dense early signings.
  • Summoning — Austrian Tolkien-influenced black metal; continuously on Napalm from 1995 (Lugburz) through their later career. Oath Bound (2006, NPR 185) and Let Mortal Heroes Sing Your Fame (2001) are defining releases.
  • Falkenbach — Pagan/viking metal; …Magni Blandinn Ok Megintiri… (1998) through Ok Nefna Tysvar Ty (2003) and Heralding - The Fireblade (2005).
  • Einherjer — Norwegian viking metal; Dragons Of The North (1996).
  • Korpiklaani — Finnish folk metal; on Napalm from Spirit Of The Forest (2003) through the 2020s.
  • Týr — Faroese folk/metal; Eric The Red (2006), Ragnarok (2006), Land (2008), By The Light Of The Northern Star (2009).
  • Alestorm — Scottish “pirate metal”; Captain Morgan’s Revenge (2008) through the 2020s. Frontman Christopher Bowes is credited on 93 Napalm releases.
  • Aркона (Arkona) — Russian pagan metal; on Napalm from Ot Serdca K Nebu (2008) through at least 2023.

Gothic / Symphonic / Doom Metal

  • Tristania — Norwegian gothic/symphonic metal; debut through World Of Glass (2001) and later Rubicon (2010).
  • Leaves’ Eyes — Germanic/Norwegian symphonic metal, fronted by Liv Kristine Espenæs Krull; Lovelorn (2004) through 2019.
  • Visions Of Atlantis — Austrian symphonic metal; on Napalm since Cast Away (2004), with A&R head Thomas Caser also serving as the band’s drummer.
  • Sirenia — Norwegian gothic metal project of Morten Veland; At Sixes And Sevens (2002) through 2025.
  • Draconian — Swedish doom/gothic metal; Where Lovers Mourn (2003) through 2025.
  • Lacrimas Profundere — German gothic rock/metal; one of the label’s longest-tenured acts, from Memorandum (1999) through The Grandiose Nowhere (2010) and beyond.
  • Ahab (4) — German funeral doom; The Call Of The Wretched Sea (2006) through 2025. Considered one of the defining acts of the funeral doom genre.
  • Candlemass — Swedish epic doom metal legends; on Napalm from 2012 through 2025.
  • Katatonia — Swedish progressive doom; joined 2022.

Hard Rock / Stoner / Doom

  • Monster Magnet — American stoner rock; joined Napalm with Mastermind (2010, NPR 336) and remained through 2022. The label’s highest-profile stoner rock signing.
  • Karma To Burn — American instrumental stoner rock; Appalachian Incantation (2010, NPRLP330).
  • The Sword — American stoner/doom; on Napalm 2012–2014.
  • Brant Bjork — Desert rock veteran (Kyuss); solo and Brant Bjork And The Low Desert Punk Band releases, 2014–2017.

Power Metal / Heavy Metal

  • Powerwolf — German power metal; the label’s most release-prolific act (215 releases 2013–2026), including major European festival staples.
  • Grave Digger (2) — German heavy metal veterans; on Napalm from 2008 through 2025.
  • Alter Bridge — American hard rock/metal (Myles Kennedy, Mark Tremonti); joined 2016, with 101 releases through 2026.
  • HammerFall — Swedish power metal; on Napalm from 2016.
  • W.A.S.P. — American hard rock/metal; Blackie Lawless produced all W.A.S.P. releases on the label.

Modern Metal / Extreme Metal

  • Jinjer (2) — Ukrainian progressive metalcore; 102 releases 2016–2025; one of the label’s fastest-rising international acts.
  • Moonspell — Portuguese gothic/black metal; on Napalm from 2012.
  • Satyricon — Norwegian black metal; joined 2015.
  • Cradle Of Filth — British extreme metal; joined 2023.
  • Destruction — German thrash metal; joined 2021.

Key Creative Staff & Recurring Collaborators

A&R / Management

  • Thomas Caser — Head of A&R; also drummer of Visions Of Atlantis. Credited on 60+ Napalm releases in booking, management, and A&R roles (Discogs ID: 563375).

Production (Most-Credited Producers)

  • Alexander Krull — German producer/vocalist (Atrocity, Leaves’ Eyes); credited on 97+ Napalm releases as producer and on 65+ in other roles. The label’s single most-prolific production credit.
  • Michael “Elvis” Baskette — American rock producer; 90 releases, primarily Alter Bridge and associated acts.
  • Lasse Lammert — German producer/engineer; 78 releases.
  • Terje Refsnes — Norwegian producer; 76 releases.
  • Dave Wyndorf — Monster Magnet frontman; self-produced all Monster Magnet releases on Napalm (50 credits as producer/writer).
