Calypso Now

Swiss independent cassette label founded 1983 by Rudi Tüscher (operating as Hotcha) in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland. Discogs label ID: 147065. Active from 1983 to approximately 1989, releasing around 200 cassette productions, then reactivated autumn 2010 and still intermittently active into the 2020s. Address: P.O. Box 12, Obergässli 4, CH-2500 Biel 3 (later POB 3525, CH-2500 Biel 3). Web: calypsonow.ch / calypsonow.bandcamp.com.

Overview

Calypso Now operated on a punk-inflected copying-on-demand model: rather than pressing large runs, it licensed cassette masters from other labels and bands worldwide and duplicated copies as needed, using whatever tape stock was available at the time. Covers frequently omitted both the label name and a catalog number — a deliberate anti-commercial stance (“Be there or be square” in the Discogs profile). The label’s geographic position in Biel/Bienne, a bilingual Swiss city, placed it at a node between the German-language experimental underground (industrial, noise, EBM, post-punk) and the UK DIY cassette culture scene centred on acts like the Cleaners From Venus.

The catalog spans 250 releases (per Discogs) and covers an unusually wide range: UK lo-fi guitar pop (Cleaners From Venus, Stray Trolleys), German industrial/noise (P16.D4, Permutative Distorsion), Belgian avant-garde (Bene Gesserit), Dutch noise/post-punk (The Ex, later The Legendary Pink Dots), North American experimental (1/2 Japanese, Eugene Chadbourne, Throbbing Gristle), and Swiss acts found nowhere else. The copying-on-demand mechanism is what enabled this breadth — the label acted as a European distribution node for cassette culture as much as a traditional label.

After 1989 the label became home exclusively to Hotcha’s own band Pull My Daisy (2) until about 1992. Activities then stopped. Reactivation in autumn 2010 brought new releases from Swiss acts including Years On Earth, Marc Stalder, and new compilations. The 2020s releases include Puts Marie (2022).

Sublabels: Calypso Now Deutschland (Discogs label ID: 961362) — a German branch with at least one confirmed release, Marin by Pull My Daisy (2) (7-CN-001, 1991); Die Pleite (Discogs label ID: 2481589) — no releases catalogued on Discogs.

Research Queue

  • The label profile says “around 200 cassette productions from 1983 to 1988” but Discogs has 250 entries — confirm whether the discrepancy is post-1988 releases, reissues, or Discogs counting pressings as separate releases.
  • The Final Taboo (1985) is credited to Dr. Timothy Leary — confirm whether this is the actual Timothy Leary (the psychologist) or a pseudonym used by a Swiss artist.
  • The Segments cassette (SI-38, 1981) predates the stated 1983 founding date — confirm whether the label was operating informally before 1983, or whether this release has a misdated year on Discogs.
