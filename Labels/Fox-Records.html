Overview

Fox Records was a subsidiary of 20th Century Fox, operating primarily in the early 1990s and distributed through Arista Records. The catalogue of roughly 50 releases skews heavily toward that decade, spanning hip hop, funk/soul, indie rock, and soundtrack material — a range that reflected the parent studio’s breadth. The label closed in 1995 and was succeeded by Fox Music. A single release traced to 1977 suggests an earlier or incidental use of the imprint before the label took its 1990s form. The roster included entertainer Jamie Foxx and film composer James Horner, tying the label closely to 20th Century Fox’s film and television output.
