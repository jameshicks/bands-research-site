EFA stands for ‘Energie Für Alle’ (Energy For All). Founded in 1982 in Hamburg as a distribution network for the independent label scene, historically developed from the musicians’ association April / Schneeball. EFA started out in Germany but had a number of labels from other countries in their stable as well. 1993 the administration and accounting department moved from Hamburg to Friedland near Göttingen. The company went bankrupt in spring 2004 and doesn’t exist any more.

Overview

EFA (Energie Für Alle, “Energy For All”) was a Hamburg-founded independent distribution network established in 1982, tracing its roots to the musicians’ cooperative April/Schneeball. It grew into a full-service label and distribution hub for the European indie scene, carrying releases across indie rock, punk, dub, and synth-pop, with over 250 releases in the 1990s representing its commercial peak. Its international roster ranged from dub figures Adrian Sherwood, Skip McDonald, and Bim Sherman to the Finnish indie band 22 Pistepirkko, and it operated several sublabels including 3 Finger Records, E:Motion, and Here2Hear Music. The company relocated its administration from Hamburg to Friedland near Göttingen in 1993, operated under the EFA Medien GmbH legal entity, and declared bankruptcy in spring 2004.
