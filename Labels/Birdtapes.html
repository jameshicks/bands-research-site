Birdtapes

Discogs ID: 512321 Location: Brooklyn, New York / Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, USA Founded: December 2012 Contact: hello@birdtapes.com Formats: Cassette, Vinyl (7”), limited editions Genres: Lo-Fi, Bedroom Pop, Indie Pop, Indie Rock Active: 2011–2017 (on Discogs; earlier releases pre-date formal label launch) URLs: birdtapes.com · blog.birdtapes.com

Brooklyn/Philadelphia-based tape and vinyl label launched in December 2012. Part of the early-2010s US bedroom pop and lo-fi cassette underground, with a roster closely overlapping with Orchid Tapes and the Philadelphia/New York DIY scene. Home to early-career recordings by Alex G (2) and a cluster of related acts including Teen Suicide, Elvis Depressedly, LVL UP, Julia Brown (2), and Porches (3).

History

Birdtapes formally launched in December 2012, operating out of Brooklyn with ties to Philadelphia — both cities that were central to the lo-fi/bedroom-pop underground of the early 2010s. The label’s Discogs catalog extends back to 2011, including an early cassette release of Alex G (2)‘s SALT and LVL UP’s Space Brothers (cat. BRD000), suggesting pre-label activity before the December 2012 formal launch.

The label’s core activity ran 2012–2014, releasing a concentrated run of 7” singles and cassettes by acts who were defining the lo-fi bedroom pop sound of that moment. Many of these artists had parallel or overlapping releases on Orchid Tapes (the Toronto-founded, NY-relocated label run by Warren Hildebrand): Alex G, Elvis Depressedly, R.L. Kelly, Fog Lake, Julia Brown (2), and Infinity Crush all appear on both rosters, indicating Birdtapes and Orchid Tapes occupied the same scene and occasionally co-orbited the same acts without direct formal affiliation.

The label’s most distributed release by pressing count is the Alex G / R.L. Kelly split 7” (BRD010, 2013), with 9 documented pressings/editions — a signal of genuine demand for Alex G material in this period, before his subsequent signing to Domino Records. Alex G (2) alone accounts for 11 releases across the Birdtapes catalog (including multiple cassette issues and compilations), more than any other act.

By 2016 the label issued a second wave of releases under BRD016–BRD020, featuring newer acts including Arm Candy, Littler, and Patio (3). Activity appears to have tapered off after 2017 (last Discogs entry: Boosegumps, Maneater (3)).

The label operated with minimal public personnel detail; no founder or curator is identified by name in the Discogs credits. The contact email domain (birdtapes.com) and the label’s web presence (blog, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube) suggest a small operation run by one or two people.

Scene Connections

Birdtapes occupied the same lo-fi/bedroom-pop ecosystem as Orchid Tapes during 2012–2014. Several artists appear on both labels:

  • Alex G (2) — Birdtapes cassettes 2011–2013; Orchid Tapes DSU vinyl 2014
  • Elvis Depressedly — Birdtapes Holo Pleasures 7” (2013); multiple Orchid Tapes releases
  • R.L. Kelly (Rachel Levy) — Birdtapes; contributed to Orchid Tapes Boring Ecstasy comp (2014)
  • Fog Lake — single release on each label
  • Julia Brown (2) — Birdtapes; also on Orchid Tapes comp
  • Infinity Crush — Birdtapes; also on Orchid Tapes

The LVL UP / Porches split (BRD015) was co-released as DDW015, suggesting a second label co-release relationship. Teen Suicide and Julia Brown (2) share members (Sam Ray (2), Alec Simke, Caroline White, Sean Mercer), with Julia Brown functioning essentially as a Teen Suicide offshoot.

Alex G (2)‘s Birdtapes period (2011–2013) directly precedes his Orchid Tapes DSU (2014) and subsequent Domino Records signing — placing Birdtapes at the ground floor of one of indie rock’s more notable mid-2010s breakthroughs.
