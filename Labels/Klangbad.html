Klangbad

Discogs ID: 7670 Label Code: LC 2160 / LC 02160 Based: Scheer, South Germany (c/o Cornelia Paul, Hauptstr. 12, 72516 Scheer) Founded: 1996 Website: klangbad.de

German independent label closely related to Faust. Founded in 1996 and run by Hans Joachim Irmler and Cornelia Paul. Operationally tied to the Faust Studio in Scheer, which Irmler owns and engineers. Also organises the annual Klangbad Festival in Scheer. Approx. 178 releases on Discogs.
