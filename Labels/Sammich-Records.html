Overview

Sammich Records was a Washington, DC label co-founded by Amanda MacKaye and Eli Janney, operating from 1985 and primarily active through the late 1980s. The catalogue sits squarely in the DC hardcore and punk tradition, with work touching Swiz, Jason Farrell, and engineer Don Zientara, and a secondary thread of indie rock and emo. Six releases appeared in the 1980s and one more followed in the 1990s before the label went quiet.
