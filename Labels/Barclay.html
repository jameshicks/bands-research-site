Barclay

French record label. Discogs ID: 3304. Distributed by PolyGram.

Major French pop/chanson label that released Caroline Loeb’s breakthrough period (1986–87): “C’est La Ouate” (885 341-1), Loeb C.D. (833 865-1), and all associated singles. Parent structure under PolyGram enabled wide European distribution.

Founded by Eddie Barclay (1949); historically associated with French chanson artists including Serge Gainsbourg, Juliette Gréco, and Michel Polnareff.
