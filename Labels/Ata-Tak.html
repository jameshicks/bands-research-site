Ata Tak

Origin: Düsseldorf, Germany

Independent record label co-founded by Kurt Dahlke (Pyrolator) and Frank Fenstermacher. Primary outlet for the Düsseldorf NDW/experimental scene: Der Plan, Pyrolator solo, and associated artists. Website: atatak.com.
