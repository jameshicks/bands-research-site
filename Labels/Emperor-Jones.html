Emperor Jones

US independent record label based in Austin, Texas. Discogs label ID: 20912. Website: http://www.emperorjones.com. Sublabel: Emperor Jones Records (Discogs ID: 1307809).

Tagline: “Chicken soup for your third eye.”

Overview

Emperor Jones was founded in Austin, Texas and began releasing records in 1995–1996, focusing on lo-fi, post-rock, and underground genres. The label was run by Craig Stewart (2) (Discogs artist ID: 757464), who also appears in the band Rubble. King Coffey (Jeffrey Coffey, Discogs ID: 600400) — drummer of Butthole Surfers — is documented as having also been involved with the label.

The label’s mailing address was PO Box 49771, Austin TX 78765; contact: ejrecords@aol.com.

The label’s catalog spans roughly 1995–2008, with the most active period being 1995–2003. Its first release (EJ-01) was My Dad Is Dead’s For Richer, For Poorer (1995), and the first two releases established that Emperor Jones would draw from US indie underground and post-rock as well as New Zealand underground (Alastair Galbraith, Peter Jefferies) and German/European experimentalists (To Rococo Rot). The American Analog Set became the label’s flagship act, with 18 releases through 2001.

Label History

1995–1996 — Launch. The label debuted with My Dad Is Dead (EJ-01, EJ-05) and The Mountain Goats’ Nine Black Poppies (EJ-02CD, 1995), then in 1996 issued the American Analog Set’s debut Diana Slowburner II (EJ-03) and The Fun Of Watching Fireworks (EJ-04LP). Early catalog also brought in Peter Jefferies (New Zealand post-rock; EJ-09LP), Alastair Galbraith (New Zealand outsider folk/rock; EJ-08CD), and the post-rock band Home (EJ-10CD).

1997 — Expansion. A strong year: the American Analog Set got two more releases (From Our Living Room To Yours, Magnificent Seventies), and the catalog added the Berlin-based electronic group To Rococo Rot (Veiculo, EJ-15; Paris 25, EJ-19CD), Half Japanese’s Heaven Sent (EJ-12CD), and The Mountain Goats’ Full Force Galesburg (EJ-11LP).

1998–1999 — Peak run. Trumans Water, Roky Erickson’s Never Say Goodbye (EJ-26CD, 1999 — liner notes by John Darnielle), and the American Analog Set’s The Golden Band (EJ-28CD) and The Only Living Boy Around (EJ-25) anchored this period. Alastair Galbraith’s Mirrorwork (EJ-22CD, 1998) also appeared.

2000–2001 — Transition. Steven R. Smith began appearing on the label; Peter Jefferies returned with Closed Circuit (EJ-40CD, 2001). The American Analog Set’s final Emperor Jones material (Through The 90s: Singles And Unreleased, EJ-42CD) came out in 2001.

2002–2008 — Later catalog. The label continued into the mid-2000s with Austin-adjacent acts including The Mountain Goats’ All Hail West Texas (EJ-41, 2002), Thuja, ST 37, Rusted Shut (Rehab, EJ-67, 2004), I Love You But I’ve Chosen Darkness (EJ-61, 2003), and The Crack Pipes. Later catalog (EJ-70s and up) includes Lord Fyre, Rahdunes, The Golden Boys, and Grey Daturas, suggesting continued activity through at least 2008.

Notable Roster Artists

The American Analog Set (ID: 64767) — 18 releases, 1996–2001. The label’s flagship act; Austin slowcore/post-rock group. Their full catalog on Emperor Jones is the label’s commercial and artistic spine.

The Mountain Goats (ID: 95417) — 5 releases, 1995–2002. Lo-fi project centered on John Darnielle; early cassette-era recordings through All Hail West Texas (2002). Rachel Ware co-credited on early recordings.

My Dad Is Dead (ID: 311148) — 8 releases, 1995–1997. Cleveland lo-fi post-punk project. Three albums on Emperor Jones: For Richer, For Poorer, Shine(r), and Everyone Wants The Honey But Not The Sting.

Alastair Galbraith (ID: 56202) — 8 releases, 1996–2003. New Zealand outsider guitarist/vocalist, associated with $100 Band, A Handful Of Dust, and The Rip. Emperor Jones issued five solo albums and a collaborative record (Radiant, EJ-57CD, with Constantine Karlis).

Peter Jefferies (ID: 268850) — 5 releases, 1996–2001. New Zealand post-rock songwriter; former member of This Kind Of Punishment and Nocturnal Projections. Producer Jean Smith (of Mecca Normal) worked on Elevator Madness (1996). Also connected to Dimmer, Plagal Grind, Cyclops, and 2 Foot Flame.

To Rococo Rot (ID: 435) — 4 releases, 1997. Berlin electronic/post-rock trio (Robert Lippok, Ronald Lippok, Stefan Schneider). Two albums on Emperor Jones (Veiculo, Paris 25) before moving to major European labels.

Pip Proud (ID: 289986) — 5 releases, 1998–2002. Australian singer-songwriter.

Trumans Water (ID: 259618) — 3 releases, 1998–2001. San Diego noise-pop group.

Steven R. Smith (ID: 99956) — 4 releases, 2000–2005. US guitarist/composer; appeared on multiple releases as both primary artist and session instrumentalist.

Roky Erickson (ID: 306494) — Never Say Goodbye (EJ-26CD, 1999). Archival release; liner notes written by John Darnielle. Erickson is the legendary founder of 13th Floor Elevators, an Austin psychedelic rock touchstone — making this release a pointed statement about the label’s Austin roots.

1-2 Japanese (ID: 178047) — Heaven Sent (EJ-12CD, 1997). Jad Fair’s lo-fi outfit; Dallas Good (of The Sadies) played bass and engineered.

100% Storms Ensemble (ID: 608740) — 3 releases, 2001–2003. Canadian ensemble performing the music of Eric D. Morrison.

Thuja (ID: 68002) — 3 releases, 2002. Experimental drone/folk collective.

I Love You But I’ve Chosen Darkness (ID: 465357) — EP (EJ-61, 2003). Austin band; early release on Emperor Jones before signing to Secretly Canadian.

Rusted Shut (ID: 287263) — Rehab (EJ-67, 2004). Houston noise/punk band.

Key Personnel (Label-Wide)

Craig Stewart (2) (Discogs ID: 757464) — Label co-founder and primary operator. Member of Rubble. Previously co-ran Trance Syndicate Records with King Coffey. Passed away December 18, 2023, aged 53.

King Coffey (Discogs ID: 600400; real name Jeffrey Coffey) — Butthole Surfers drummer, said to have been involved with the label’s operation.

Jerry Tubb (Discogs ID: 448830) — Recurring mastering engineer; handled 15+ releases across the catalog, including most American Analog Set records. Based at Terra Nova Mastering, Austin, TX.

Adam Wiltzie (Discogs ID: 93848) — Engineer and mixer on 3 releases; co-founder of Stars of the Lid.

Britt Daniel (Discogs ID: 273944) — Produced one release on the label; frontman of Spoon, also Austin-based.
