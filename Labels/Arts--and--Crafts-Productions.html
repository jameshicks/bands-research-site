Arts & Crafts Productions

Arts & Crafts Productions (operating label name: Arts & Crafts) is a Canadian independent record label, management firm, publisher, and artist services company based in Toronto, Ontario. Discogs label ID: 35704 (Arts & Crafts); parent entity Arts & Crafts International: 30545. Founded in 2002 with the release of Broken Social Scene’s You Forgot It In People, the label was co-founded by Kevin Drew and Jeffrey Remedios. Address: 460 Richmond St W #402, Toronto, ON M5V 1Y1. The label has earned 21 JUNO Awards and is widely regarded as one of Canada’s most significant independent labels of the 2000s–2010s.

Overview

Arts & Crafts launched in 2002 as the vehicle for Broken Social Scene’s landmark second album You Forgot It In People, initially co-released on Paper Bag Records. The label grew directly out of the Toronto indie scene that coalesced around BSS’s collective membership — Kevin Drew and Brendan Canning as core principals, with co-founder Jeffrey Remedios handling the business side. The label’s early catalog was essentially a map of that scene: Jason Collett, Feist, Apostle of Hustle (Andrew Whiteman’s project), Stars, The Most Serene Republic, Young Galaxy, and The Hidden Cameras all appeared in the label’s first few years. Do Make Say Think share several personnel with BSS (Charles Spearin is in both) but are a Constellation Records act and do not appear in the A&C catalog directly.

The 2004–2007 period was the label’s commercial and critical peak. Feist’s Let It Die (2004) and The Reminder (2007) achieved international recognition; The Reminder and its single “1234” became a mainstream crossover, aided by an Apple iPod advertisement. Broken Social Scene’s self-titled 2005 record and the Kevin Drew and Brendan Canning solo records (both credited as “Broken Social Scene Presents…”) extended the brand.

From 2008 onward, Arts & Crafts diversified well beyond the BSS circle. Los Campesinos! (Wales), Constantines, Timber Timbre, Trust (15), Chilly Gonzales, Dan Mangan, and Gordon Downie all became significant roster presences. The label took on Gord Downie’s solo catalog extensively from 2014 through posthumous releases into the 2020s.

The label’s geographic reach also expanded: sublabels Arts & Crafts Europe (ID: 78673), Arts & Crafts Europe GmbH (ID: 718806), and Arts & Crafts México (ID: 341124) handled regional distribution. In Canada, the label functioned as a full artist-services company combining label, management, merchandise, and publishing under the Arts & Crafts International umbrella.

By the 2010s–2020s, the roster had broadened considerably to include Jean-Michel Blais, Japandroids, BadBadNotGood, FRIGS, Andy Shauf, Frazey Ford, Sarah Harmer, and international signings including Steven Wilson (2021 distribution).

Key in-house producers and engineers: David Newfeld (house producer for early BSS-related records, 46 production credits), Kevin Drew (31 production credits), Nyles Spencer, Howie Beck, Marcus Paquin, and Dave Hamelin. Mastering engineer Noah Mintz appears on 97 releases — the single most-recurring credit across the entire catalog. Designer Robyn Kotyk (65 design credits) and illustrator/BSS drummer Justin Peroff (51 design credits) defined the label’s visual identity.
