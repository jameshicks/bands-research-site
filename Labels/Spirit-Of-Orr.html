Overview

Spirit Of Orr is an American independent label founded in 1995, most active through the 2000s when it issued the bulk of its roughly 28-release catalogue. Its output ranges across experimental, psychedelic rock, indie rock, and lo-fi territory, issued under two imprints — a CD-R Series and the Quiet Weather Singles Series. The roster has included Moviola, Joshua Burkett, Ron Schneiderman, Buttercup (4), and Danish ambient-psych artist Jonas Munk, with release activity tapering sharply after 2010.

Notes (web)

Jonas Munk / Spirit of Orr connection

Jonas Munk does not appear to have released any solo material (as Manual or under his own name) on Spirit of Orr. His presence in the Spirit of Orr catalogue is as a member of Causa Sui, the Danish psych-rock band he co-founded with Jakob Skott.

Spirit of Orr — operated by Ron Schneiderman (Twitter: @spiritoforr) — co-released two Causa Sui records jointly with El Paraiso Records, the Danish label that Munk and Skott run:

  • Pewt’r Sessions 1 (April 2011, limited 500-copy LP on 180g vinyl) — Spirit of Orr cat. EPR005 / El Paraiso Records https://www.discogs.com/release/2852628-Causa-Sui-Pewtr-Sessions-1
  • Pewt’r Sessions 2 (July 2011) — Spirit of Orr cat. EPR006 / El Paraiso Records https://www.discogs.com/release/3042742-Causa-Sui-Pewtr-Sessions-2

The connection is therefore a label-to-label partnership on these two co-releases: Spirit of Orr and El Paraiso Records jointly issued the records. Because Jonas Munk co-founded and co-runs El Paraiso Records, the relationship is essentially between the two labels rather than a personal signing of Munk as a solo artist to Spirit of Orr.

No web source found explains the specific personal or business contact that initiated the Spirit of Orr / El Paraiso partnership.
