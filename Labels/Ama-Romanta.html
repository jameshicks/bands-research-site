Overview

Ama Romanta was a Portuguese independent label founded in 1986 by João Peste, vocalist of Pop Dell’Arte. Its catalogue is concentrated almost entirely in the late 1980s — 11 of its 14 entries fall in that decade — with just three releases carrying into the early 1990s. The roster spans experimental, new wave, and indie rock, with Pop Dell’Arte, Rafael Toral, Nuno Rebelo, and Luís San-Payo among its acts. No parent company or distribution arrangement has been documented.
