Konnex Records

German record company, Berlin. Founded 1984 by Manfred Schiek. Discogs label ID: 52008. Label Code: LC 08718.

Overview

Parent company for several sublabels including Dossier Records (the primary experimental/avant-garde imprint) and Atonal Records (documenting the Berlin Atonal festival). Schiek’s address listed as Schonacher Straße 23, 13469 Berlin. Contact: konnex-dossier@gmx.de.
