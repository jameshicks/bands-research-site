Prince Melon Records

Oct 30, 20268 min read

Prince Melon Records

Discogs ID: 71979 Country: Australia Founded: 1980 Founder: Ed Kuepper Sublabel: The Prince Melon Bootleg Series (Discogs ID: 447120)

Australian independent record label founded in 1980 by Ed Kuepper, then guitarist and primary songwriter of Laughing Clowns. It served as the band’s primary domestic Australian outlet from 1980 to 1983, issuing their debut EP, two LPs, and several singles. Beyond the Clowns, it released one single each by Hugo Klang and Out Of Nowhere (2) in 1982. Kuepper reactivated the label in 2008 as an imprint for The Prince Melon Bootleg Series of mail-order live and archival releases, then revived the main Prince Melon imprint proper in 2021 for a run of commercial reissues carrying the “PM 21st Century” catalog prefix, continuing through 2024.

History

1980–1983: Laughing Clowns era

Prince Melon was established by Ed Kuepper in 1980 to release music by Laughing Clowns, the Brisbane-rooted post-punk/jazz-rock group he had formed after leaving The Saints (2). The label’s first release — catalog number PM-01 EP — was the debut Laughing Clowns 7”, Sometimes, The Fire Dance… (1980), a three-track vinyl with the lineup of Kuepper (guitar/vocals), Jeffrey Wegener (drums), Ben Wallace-Crabbe (bass), Dan Wallace-Crabbe (piano), and Bob Farrell (saxophone). Engineers were Tony Cohen.

Later the same year came Reign Of Terror, Throne Of Blood (PM-2000), a compilation LP gathering eight tracks and expanding the band’s lineup to include Peter Doyle (2) on trumpet. Engineers were Tony Cohen and John Bee.

In 1981 the label released the 12” EP 3 (PM-05-LP), a five-track record, and the 7” single Theme From “Mad Flies, Mad Flies” / Mr. Uddich-Schmuddich Goes To Town (PM 020). The EP expanded the lineup further, with Kuepper, Wegener, Ben Wallace-Crabbe, Dan Wallace-Crabbe, Bob Farrell, and Peter Doyle (2) — engineering by John Bee, photography by Clinton Walker.

In 1982 Prince Melon issued two singles by artists outside the Laughing Clowns orbit: Grand Life For Fools And Idiots / Beat Up The Old Shack (P.M. 010) by Hugo Klang, with songs written by Ollie Olsen; and Remember, Remember / No Resistance (PM 015) by Out Of Nowhere (2), featuring Peter Milton Walsh (guitar/vocals), Jeffrey Wegener (drums), and others. Both were post-punk/experimental singles, the Hugo Klang release leaning minimal electronic.

The label’s final Laughing Clowns original release from this era was the LP Mr Uddich-Schmuddich Goes To Town (PM5000, 1982) — the band’s debut full-length studio album. The lineup at this point comprised Kuepper, Wegener, Leslie Millar (bass), Louise Elliott (saxophone/flute), and Peter Doyle (2) (trumpet). Producers were Kuepper, Wegener, and Peter Doyle (2); engineers were Peter Doyle (2) and Peter Walker. Cover art by Judi Dransfield.

A German pressing of the Laughing Clowns LP Everything That Flies (PM 03012, 1983) also carried the Prince Melon catalog number, as did an Australian pressing the same year, extending the original catalog sequence into 1983.

2008–2010: The Prince Melon Bootleg Series

Kuepper reactivated the Prince Melon name in 2008 through a mail-order sublabel, The Prince Melon Bootleg Series (Discogs ID: 447120), issuing live recordings of himself, Laughing Clowns, and The Aints under a “year zero” catalog scheme (year zero 001 onward). These were limited archival releases available directly from Kuepper, including collaborative live material with Jeffrey Wegener and pianist Alister Spence. The series also included a release by Ascension Academy (year zero 013, 2010), the only non-Kuepper/Clowns act in the bootleg series.

2012–2015: Occasional imprint releases

During this period Prince Melon issued a handful of Ed Kuepper studio and live releases outside the mail-order series, including Second Winter (2012), a collaboration credited to Ed Kuepper / Mark Dawson (2); The Return Of The Mail-Order Bridegroom (2014); and Lost Cities (2015, catalog EDK50TH, a 50th-anniversary release).

2021–2024: Reissue era

In 2021 Prince Melon undertook a significant reissue campaign under the “PM 21st Century” catalog prefix, co-released with Fatal Records (2). Key releases:

  • Golden Days — When Giants Walked The Earth (PM 21st Century) — Laughing Clowns anthology LP drawing on multiple lineups, with full personnel documentation including Louise Elliott, Ben Wallace-Crabbe, Leslie Millar, Peter Doyle (2), Dan Wallace-Crabbe, Bob Farrell, Peter Milton Walsh, Louis Tillett, Chris Abrahams, Glad Reed, and Diane Spence.
  • Singles ‘86–‘96 (PM 21ST CENTURY DOUBLEPLUSGOOD) — Ed Kuepper solo singles compilation.
  • The Exploding Universe of Ed Kuepper (2021) — 8-LP box set (4 double-LPs) credited jointly to Ed Kuepper, Laughing Clowns, and The Aints, collecting Singles ‘86–‘96, Golden Days — When Giants Walked The Earth, and The Aints Live At Marrickville Bowlo, co-released with Fatal Records (2).

The label continued reissuing Kuepper’s back catalog through 2023–2024, releasing vinyl and CD editions of Electrical Storm (1985, PMR 1985LP/CD), Honey Steel’s Gold (1991, PMR 1991CD), Frontierland (1996, PMR 1996LP), Mr Mirakle (1997, PMR1997LP/CD), and Black Ticket Day (1992, PMR1992LP). In 2024 a new vinyl reissue of Laughing Clowns’ Law Of Nature (PMR1983LP/CD) also appeared.

Recurring engineer for the reissue era: Don Bartley (mastering/remastering, 11 releases) and Rick O’Neil (mastering, 6 releases). Design and layout for reissues handled by Ultra Vivid. Judi Dransfield Kuepper provided original artwork reproduced across multiple reissue sleeves.

Connections

  • Laughing Clowns — the label’s primary act and reason for existence; Kuepper founded Prince Melon to release their records domestically
  • Ed Kuepper — founder and controlling force across all eras
  • Jeffrey Wegener — Laughing Clowns drummer; co-producer on original releases; also appeared on Out Of Nowhere (2) single
  • The Saints (2) — Kuepper’s preceding group; Prince Melon represents his post-Saints independent infrastructure
  • The Aints — Kuepper’s later group, included in the 2010 bootleg series and the 2021 box set
  • The Prince Melon Bootleg Series — sublabel (Discogs ID: 447120) for 2008–2010 mail-order live releases
  • Fatal Records (2) — co-released the 2021 reissue campaign (The Exploding Universe of Ed Kuepper box set)
  • Hugo Klang — Melbourne post-punk/minimal act; released one single on Prince Melon (1982), written by Ollie Olsen
  • Out Of Nowhere (2) — released one single on Prince Melon (1982); included Peter Milton Walsh (later of The Apartments)
  • Peter Milton Walsh — appeared on the Out Of Nowhere (2) single (PM 015) and on the 2021 Laughing Clowns compilation
  • Missing Link — Australian indie label of the same era, context label for Australian post-punk

Open Questions

  • Whether PM-1000 (Reign Of Terror, Throne Of Blood) is a legitimate alternate first pressing or a mislabeled entry in Discogs — the PM-2000 number is more widely cited.
  • The catalog gap between PM-05-LP (1981) and PM 020/PM 015/P.M. 010 (1981–1982) suggests numbering was not strictly sequential; no known releases for PM 06–PM 014 range.
  • Everything That Flies (1983) appeared on both an Australian and a German pressing under PM 03012 — unclear whether Prince Melon was licensing or co-releasing with a German partner; label for German pressing may need verification.
  • Nature of the Hugo Klang connection to Kuepper/Prince Melon is not documented on Discogs; may require web research.
  • Ascension Academy (year zero 013) identity not confirmed beyond Discogs ID 2781222 — unclear if connected to Kuepper or incidental to the mail-order series.
