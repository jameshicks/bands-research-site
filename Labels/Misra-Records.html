Misra Records

Discogs ID: 41272
Origin: USA (Austin, Texas)
Release count: 175

Independent label. US label for Bablicon’s releases: In A Different City (1999, MSR001), The Orange Tapered Moon (2000, MSR005), The Cat That Was A Dog (2000, MSR008). Home to Centro-Matic and South San Gabriel for their mid-2000s run: Fort Recovery (2006), Triggers And Trash Heaps (2006), Dual Hawks (2008). Also released Summer Hymns, The Mendoza Line, Destroyer (4) (Streethawk: A Seduction, 2001), Shearwater, Phosphorescent (Aw Come Aw Wry, 2005), Great Lake Swimmers, and Hallelujah The Hills. Matt Pence frequently engineered and produced releases on the label.
