Mute

British independent record label. Discogs ID: 26391. Founded 1978 by Daniel Miller (as The Normal). Parent: Mute Artists Ltd. (ID 298991).

Home label for DAF, Depeche Mode, Nick Cave, Yazoo, Erasure, and many others. Key imprint for electronic and post-punk acts through the 1980s–90s.

Key Artists (relevant to this vault)

  • Depeche Mode — signed 1981; 13 studio albums through Spirit (2017); left for Columbia after Spirit
  • Erasure — Andy Bell / Vince Clarke; Vince Clarke’s post-DM project
  • Recoil — Alan Wilder project; full catalog on Mute, 1986–2012
  • Deutsch Amerikanische Freundschaft — Die Kleinen Und Die Bösen (1980); Der Mussolini reissue (1998)
  • Robert Görl — solo singles Mit Dir. (1983), Night Full Of Tension LP (1984)
  • Martin L. Gore — solo material including Counterfeit E.P. (1989), Counterfeit² (2003), The Third Chimpanzee EP (2021)
  • Dave Gahan — solo: Paper Monsters (2003), Hourglass (2007)
