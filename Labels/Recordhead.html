Overview

Recordhead is an indie rock label that launched in 1996, built around the output of Guided By Voices members Robert Pollard and Tobin Sprout. The catalogue is weighted heavily toward the 2000s — 30 releases against 10 in the 1990s — and spans lo-fi, garage rock, and alternative rock. Beyond Pollard and Sprout, the roster includes George Ingram, Wigtea Collective, and John Shough, suggesting the label functioned as an outlet for Pollard’s wider artistic circle. No parent label or corporate structure is on record.
