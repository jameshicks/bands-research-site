Overview

PIAS France — also known as Play It Again Sam France — is the French branch of the [PIAS] Entertainment Group, handling releases credited without the bracketed form of the name (distinct from the related [PIAS] France imprint). The label was most active through the 1990s and 2000s, issuing 74 and 80 releases in those decades respectively, with a catalogue spanning indie rock, alternative rock, chanson, and electro and downtempo styles. It operated the sublabel A Donf’ and worked with French acts including Ludwig Von 88, Burning Heads, and Tue-Loup.
