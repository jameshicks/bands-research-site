Overview

Crunchy Frog is a Copenhagen-based indie label founded in 1994 by the members of THAU. Its catalogue spans indie rock, synth-pop, garage rock, and surf, peaking sharply in the 2000s with 57 releases before tapering through the 2010s and into the 2020s. The label is best known for launching Junior Senior, whose dance-pop drew international attention, and maintains a companion imprint, Crunchy Frog Soundtracks, for film-adjacent work. Thomas Troelsen and Jesper Reginal are among the recurring names on its roster.
