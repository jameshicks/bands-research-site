Total Discogs releases: ~1,000+ (capped at 500 in individual queries)

Independent extreme music label. Founded by Matthew F. Jacobson in 1990 in Upper Darby, PA — a suburb immediately west of Philadelphia. Began as a death metal and grindcore imprint and expanded significantly through the 1990s–2000s to encompass noise rock, post-metal, sludge, doom, mathcore, noise/industrial (Merzbow, Bastard Noise), and experimental heavy music. One of the most comprehensive independent metal labels of the era, with a deliberately curated catalog that crossed underground genre lines more freely than typical genre-specific labels.

Later Period (2010–present): Post-Mastodon Diversification

Mastodon departed for Reprise Records after Crack The Skye (2009). The Dillinger Escape Plan left after One Of Us Is The Killer (2013). Relapse continued with Baroness, Neurosis, YOB, Pig Destroyer, and expanded into shoegaze/dream pop with Nothing (12) and Myrkur (black metal/folk).

Key Release Credits

Transcendence Into The Peripheral — diSEMBOWELMENT (1993)

Australian funeral doom/death. Catalog: RR6065 (2012 reissue). Cult record; one of the slowest and most influential doom-death LPs of the period.

Destroy Erase Improve — Meshuggah (1994)

Swedish polyrhythmic metal. First major label exposure for Meshuggah in North America. Co-produced by the band.

Through Silver In Blood — Neurosis (1996)

Post-metal landmark. Produced by the band; Recorded by Steve Albini at Pachyderm Studios. Vinyl: HWCY-1042 (repress).

Calculating Infinity — The Dillinger Escape Plan (1999)

Produced by Steve Evetts. Engineered by Steve Evetts and DEP. Mixed at Inner Ear. 500 BPM drumming; introduced mathcore to a wider audience.

Leviathan — Mastodon (2004, RR 6622-1)

Produced and engineered by Matt Bayles. Mixed by Matt Bayles. Concept album based on Moby-Dick. Artwork by Paul Romano.

Blood Mountain — Mastodon (2006, 9362-44364-2)

Produced by Matt Bayles. Licensed to Warner Bros. for major-label distribution. Artwork by Paul Romano.

Crack The Skye — Mastodon (2009, 2-459132)

Produced by Brendan O’Brien. Final Relapse album; Mastodon moved to Reprise after.

Sacred — The Obsessed (2017)

The Obsessed reunion album. Self-produced by the Weinrich/Sherman/Costantino lineup. First Obsessed studio record in 23 years.

Yellow & Green — Baroness (2012, RR7190)

Double LP. Design by John Dyer Baizley. Departure from pure metal toward prog/psych rock.
