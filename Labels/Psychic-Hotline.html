Overview

Psychic Hotline is an artist-run independent label out of Durham, North Carolina, co-founded in 2018 by Sylvan Esso duo members Amelia Meath and Nicholas Sanborn alongside their longtime manager Martin Anderson. The label took off primarily in the 2020s, with 29 of its roughly 34 catalogued releases appearing after 2020, spanning folk, indie pop, indie rock, and folk rock. Its roster stays close to the Durham indie scene—Phil Cook and Ryan Gustafson both appear—while also including Brazilian singer-songwriter Tim Bernardes, suggesting the founders have pursued connections well beyond their local base.

Tim Bernardes — label connection

Searched for interviews, label announcements, or profiles explaining how Tim Bernardes came to sign with Psychic Hotline. No source found that directly explains the connection.

What is documented:

  • Bernardes’ second solo album Mil Coisas Invisíveis was released on Psychic Hotline on 14 June 2022 — his first release with the label. (Psychic Hotline release page; Broadway World, 31 May 2022)
  • In summer 2022 Bernardes toured the US opening for Fleet Foxes. (Broadway World, 10 Aug 2022)
  • In 2022 Newport Folk Festival commissioned Psychic Hotline to put together a collective set; Bernardes performed alongside Amelia Meath and other label artists. The rehearsals were later released as Emmanuel Rehearsals – Newport, RI (Psychic Hotline, 2024). (Psychic Hotline release page)
  • The label’s own journal page announcing the Recomeçar reissue (psychic-hotline.net) and the “BB” single announcement do not discuss the origin of the relationship.
