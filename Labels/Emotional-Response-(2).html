Overview

Emotional Response (2) is an independent record label based in Cottonwood, Arizona, founded in 2013 and operated by Stewart Anderson and Jennifer Anderson alongside co-founder Jen Turrell. Its catalogue of roughly 77 releases spans indie rock, indie pop, punk, and post-punk, with the bulk of output — 62 releases — concentrated in the 2010s and a further 15 appearing in the 2020s. The label’s roster centres on Anderson’s own long-running project Boyracer, with additional releases from Jen Turrell, Julian Knowles, and Zac Denton.
