From Discogs

Swedish record label mainly releasing italo- and euro disco in the mid- to late 1980’s.

Vinyl Mania, a record shop placed in the centre of Stockholm, that focused on dance/DJ stuff in the early 1980’s came up with a label of their own; Beat Box. They were three shareholders, of whom Fredrik Ramel was one. Ramel asked the mixing engineer Gunnar Silins if he would like to help out with a mix that Ramel considered a bit weak. This instituted the start of a co-operation which lasted for a few years and together they did over 50 remixes that were released on Beat Box.

— Discogs label profile, ID 42763

Overview

Beat Box was a Stockholm-based record label operated under the dance-specialist shop Vinyl Mania, which had served the city’s DJ community since the early 1980s. The label grew out of a chance collaboration: co-founder Fredrik Ramel recruited mixing engineer Gunnar Silins to shore up a weak mix, and the partnership went on to produce more than 50 remixes released on Beat Box. Its catalogue is almost entirely from the 1980s (276 releases) with a small tail into the 1990s (15 releases), centred on italo-disco and euro disco with strands of synth-pop and early house. Among the roster’s notable names are Italian dance producers Gianfranco Bortolotti and Stefano Cundari, pointing to strong trans-European connections in the italo-disco circuit.
