American Recordings

Record label founded by Rick Rubin. Discogs label ID: 2569. Parent label: Republic Records. Based in Los Angeles.

Home to System Of A Down (1998–2006, 2020) across all five of their studio albums, co-released with Columbia. Also home to Johnny Cash (American Recordings series), Slayer, The Black Crowes, and a broad roster spanning rock, metal, country, and hip-hop.

Founding and History

Founded by Rick Rubin c.1988 as Def American Recordings after his departure from Def Jam Recordings. The “Def” was dropped in 1993 after Rubin’s objection to the word’s associations, becoming American Recordings. Distribution has shifted across major labels over the decades; current parent per Discogs is Republic Records.
