Overview

Sanctuary is a British record label operating under the Sanctuary Records Group Ltd. umbrella, catalogued under label code LC 23251. Active from 1996, it concentrated its output in the 1990s (29 releases) before a smaller run into the 2000s (11 releases). Its catalogue skews heavily toward heavy metal and hard rock — with artists including Black Sabbath, Tony Iommi, and Geezer Butler on its roster — alongside Pop Rock, Prog Rock, and Blues Rock. The label is related to Sanctuary Studios Ltd and serves as the catch-all imprint for releases bearing either a “Sanctuary” or “Sanctuary Records” logo where no more specific subsidiary role applies.
