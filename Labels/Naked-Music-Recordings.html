Naked Music Recordings

Discogs ID: 10 Parent company: Naked Music NYC Recordings, LLC (Discogs ID: 272720) Based: San Francisco, California (mailing address: 4663 18th Street, SF CA 94114) Incorporated: 21 October 1998 (parent LLC, NY company number 2308668) Active: 1999–2014 (catalog evidence; final release Signs & Wonders, 2014)

Overview

Naked Music Recordings is a San Francisco-based soulful deep house label co-founded by producer Jay Denes (also recording as Blue Six) and executive David Boonshoft. The label grew out of their production company and parent entity Naked Music NYC, incorporated in New York in October 1998. From 1999 onward, Naked Music Recordings became the primary commercial imprint, issuing singles, EPs, LPs, and mix compilations.

Label manager Bruno Ybarra ran A&R, legal, manufacturing, distribution, and promotion from late 1998 to July 2003, and is co-credited as executive producer across roughly half the catalog. The distinctive sleeve artwork on early releases was created by Stuart Patterson (8) at the design studio Art Dept. NYC; Stuart Patterson is credited on over 33 releases.

The label’s signature sound is warm, song-focused soulful deep house built around live instrumentation — guitar, bass, keys, and saxophone — with featured vocalists rather than faceless track production. Jay Denes (as Blue Six) is the creative center; David Boonshoft plays bass and electric bass on many Blue Six recordings. The primary mastering engineers across the catalog were J. Mark Andrus (credited on ~50 releases, also DJ mixing compilations) and Emily Lazar (credited on ~28 releases, particularly the 2002–2005 Astralwerks co-releases).

Key artists signed or frequently featured: Blue Six (Jay Denes’s primary alias), Miguel Migs (also as Petalpusher), Aquanote (Gabriel René), Lisa Shaw, Gaelle Adisson, Lovetronic (Jay Denes + Dave Warrin), Andy Caldwell, and singer Aya (3) (Lysa Aya Trenier). The label also introduced Monique Bingham to the wider soulful house audience via the 2000 Blue Six single “Pure.”

Several major 2002 releases were co-issued with Astralwerks (EMI): Beautiful Tomorrow (Blue Six), Colorful You (Miguel Migs), and The Pearl (Aquanote) — a period of mainstream crossover visibility. Trentemøller’s 2003 US debut EP (NMS025) is a notable outlier in the catalog, placing a future Danish post-rock/electronic artist on a US soulful house label early in his career.

Key Artists

Blue Six (Jay Denes) — Discogs ID: 33 / 32

The label’s artistic core. Blue Six is the main recording alias of Jay Denes (also credited under his own name and as Lovetronic with Dave Warrin). Denes wrote, produced, played keyboards and percussion, and mixed virtually all Blue Six material. David Boonshoft (co-founder) plays bass on Blue Six albums. The arc runs from Music And Wine (NM002, 1999) through the Astralwerks crossover Beautiful Tomorrow (2002) to the late albums Aquarian Angel (2007), Noesis (2010), and Signs & Wonders (2014). Before Naked Music, Blue Six appeared on Wave Music (1998).

Miguel Migs (also Petalpusher) — Discogs ID: 34 / 669

Realname Miguel Sundance Steward. The label’s second most prolific artist, releasing both under his own name and as Petalpusher. Key releases: Nude Dimensions – The Petalpusher Session (1999), Take Me To Paradise (2000), Colorful You (2002, co-Astralwerks), Nude Tempo One (2002 mix CD). After leaving Naked Music he founded his own Salted Music imprint and continued extensively on OM Records and NRK Sound Division. Petalpusher’s “Breakin’ It Down” (NM001) was the label’s inaugural single.

Aquanote (Gabriel René) — Discogs ID: 38 / 3459

Gabriel René’s Aquanote alias produced three Naked Music LPs: True Love (2000), Only (2000), Nowhere (2002), and The Pearl (2002, co-Astralwerks, produced and performed by René). Gabriel René also served as compiler for the Carte Blanche compilation series and as producer for additional production on several label releases.

Lisa Shaw — Discogs ID: 670

Realname Lisa Shaw-Winsett. Vocalist and artist. Appeared as vocalist on Lovetronic’s You Are Love (1999) and Central Living’s Everyday (2001), then fronted her own releases Always (2000) and the LP Cherry (2005). A recurring vocal presence on Blue Six’s Aquarian Angel (2007). Has a substantial solo career beyond Naked Music (OM Records, Salted Music, Defected, Anjunadeep).

Gaelle Adisson — Discogs ID: 64616

French-American singer-songwriter who recorded Rain (2001), the LP Transient (2004), and several singles. Also a member of Speakeasy. Has a significant independent Discogs presence outside Naked Music.

Andy Caldwell — Discogs ID: 3337

Producer and DJ, also a member of Soulstice. Released All I Need (2001) on Naked Music; also co-credited on Give A Little (2003). Has a wider discography on OM Records and elsewhere.

Aya (3) (Lysa Aya Trenier) — Discogs ID: 178750

Vocalist and co-writer who appears under two names in the catalog: as “Lysa Aya Trenier” (featured vocalist on Beautiful Tomorrow, 2002) and as “Aya (3)” for her own releases (Strange Flower, Uptown, Sean, all 2004). Also on Aquarian Angel (2007) and Noesis (2010).

Monique Bingham — Discogs ID: 99026

Featured on Blue Six’s “Pure” (NM008, 2000) as vocalist and co-writer — her first significant placement in the US soulful house underground. Also on Beautiful Tomorrow (2002). Went on to a long international career in deep house. See Monique Bingham for full profile.

Groove Collective — Discogs ID: 30

New York jazz-funk collective who contributed Everything Is Changing (NM004, 1999). An early cosmopolitan signing that signaled the label’s connections beyond SF; Groove Collective members include Genji Siraisi, Itaal Shur, Jonathan Maron, Jay Rodriguez, and others.

Trentemøller — Discogs ID: 117914

Danish electronic producer (real name Anders Trentemøller). His 2003 US debut Trentemøller EP (NMS025/NAK25) appeared on Naked Music — a notable outlier in the label’s soulful house catalog, and an early international credit for a producer who later became prominent in post-rock and electronic music.

Kaskade — Discogs ID: 19116

American electronic producer (Ryan Raddon). Appeared on the Jet Lag EP (NAK21, 2002) co-credited with Petalpusher — an early label credit for Kaskade before his mainstream EDM career.
