Psych.KG

Discogs label ID: 127571 Country: Germany Parent label: Multi National Disaster Records (ID 17801) Contact: c/o Matthias Horn, Kleine Hoehle 12, 53881 Euskirchen, Germany; & c/o Kommissar Hjuler, Weizenstieg 11, 24941 Flensburg, Germany Email: psychkg@online.de Web: https://limitierte-schallplatten-1.jimdosite.com | https://psychkg.bandcamp.com Total Discogs releases: 859

German experimental / sound-art / mail-art label, co-operated by Kommissar Hjuler from his Flensburg address and Matthias Horn in Euskirchen. Functions as the primary vehicle for Hjuler’s collaborative releases with external artists worldwide — while Der Schöne Hjuler-Memorial-Fond (see below) is the self-release imprint, Psych.KG is the network label.

The roster is a cross-section of international noise, experimental, Fluxus-adjacent, mail-art, and sound poetry practice. Releases are typically issued in small/limited editions as vinyl, with handmade elements.
