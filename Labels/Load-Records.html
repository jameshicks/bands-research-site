Load Records

Providence, Rhode Island-based independent label. Discogs label ID: 17358. Active in the 2000s–2010s, specializing in noise, experimental, and avant-rock.

Overview

Load Records released Mouthus’ Saw A Halo (2007, Load 103), produced by Mouthus and Samara Lubelski.
