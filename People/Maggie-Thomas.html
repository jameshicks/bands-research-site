Maggie Thomas

Sound engineer, graphic designer, and photographer. Discogs artist ID: 406391

Also credited as E.M. Thomas, E.M. Thomas, EM Thomas, Maggie T., Margaret Thomas. Aliases: Art Bear IV, EMT (3). Married to Bob Drake.

Overview

Maggie Thomas’s connection to music began around 1972, when she worked at The Manor (Virgin’s residential recording studio). There she first encountered Gong and Henry Cow, forming lasting relationships with the members of both. From that point she remained closely embedded in the Henry Cow orbit — working as roadie, designing album covers, and eventually becoming the live sound engineer for these groups and their related projects.

Her Discogs credit history spans five decades (1973–2022), predominantly in design and artwork roles for the ReR Megacorp catalog — she is one of the most consistent visual artists across Chris Cutler’s entire label and network. She also has scattered sound engineer credits (live mixing) for Art Bears, Cassiber, and related groups.

Key Roles

  • Live sound engineer for Henry Cow, Art Bears, Cassiber, and related groups
  • Graphic designer / visual artist — cover designs for the ReR Megacorp catalog
  • Photographer — credited on multiple ReR releases
  • Performer — credited on The Black Sheep’s Alive In Beograd (1987)

Connections

  • Chris Cutler — longtime creative partner; both in The Black Sheep and throughout the ReR catalog
  • Henry Cow — first major musical connection; artwork and roadie work
  • Art Bears — sound engineering and design
  • Cassiber — live mixing and artwork
  • Gong — early cover design (1973)
  • Bob Drake — husband; also in the ReR orbit
  • ReR Megacorp — her primary outlet for design and artwork across four decades
  • The Manor — where she first encountered the musicians she would work with
  • The Black Sheep — performer credit, Alive In Beograd (1987)

Notes

The alias “Art Bear IV” references Art Bears — the post-Henry Cow trio of Chris Cutler, Fred Frith, and Dagmar Krause. Thomas was a fourth, non-recording member of that project in a live/technical capacity.
