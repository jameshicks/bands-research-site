Molly Neuman

Drummer, songwriter, and music industry figure. Born June 18, 1972, Washington D.C. area. Discogs artist ID: 607646. Alias: Molly Germs (used in the Frumpies).

Overview

Molly Neuman is best known as the co-founding drummer of Bratmobile, one of the central bands of the early-1990s riot grrrl movement, alongside vocalist Allison Wolfe and guitarist Erin Smith. Bratmobile formed out of the Olympia/D.C. nexus and became a key fixture on the Kill Rock Stars roster. Neuman also drummed in the Olympia supergroup-collective Frumpies — a rotating cast drawn from Bikini Kill and Bratmobile orbits — where she performed under the alias “Molly Germs.”

After Bratmobile’s initial break-up (following The Real Janelle EP in 1994), Neuman moved into the music industry, working at Lookout! Records in various roles including co-ownership and as a coordinator on their sampler releases. She also played in post-riot-grrrl bands: The Peechees (Kill Rock Stars, Lookout! Records, 1996–1999) and Love Or Perish (2007–2009). In parallel with those performing years, she operated Indivision Management, managing The Donnas through their Atlantic Records peak (2002–2005) and The Locust (2005–2007), as well as The Tyde (Rough Trade America, 2006).

Bratmobile reunited in 1999 and released two further albums on Lookout! Records before disbanding again. Neuman has remained listed as manager for Bratmobile reissue activity as recently as 2025.

Bands and Projects

Bratmobile (1991–1994, 1999–2003)

Bratmobile (Discogs artist ID: 267486) formed in the early 1990s with Neuman on drums, Allison Wolfe on vocals, and Erin Smith on guitar. The band also rotated additional members including Jen Smith and Christina Billotte at various points. Their debut full-length Pottymouth (Kill Rock Stars, 1993) is the definitive statement; Neuman is credited on drums and cover photography. After breaking up in 1994 following the The Real Janelle EP, they reunited in 1999 and released Ladies, Women And Girls (Lookout! Records, 2000) and Girls Get Busy (Lookout! Records, 2002) before disbanding again.

Frumpies (1992–1998)

The Frumpies (Discogs artist ID: 276323) were an Olympia collective drawing members from Bikini Kill (Tobi Vail, Kathi Wilcox, Billy Karren) and Bratmobile. Michelle Mae was also a member. Neuman performed as “Molly Germs.” Two releases on Kill Rock Stars: Babies And Bunnies (1993), a 7”/cassette where all members were credited as “Frumpies Equal,” and the compilation Frumpie One-Piece (1998).

The Peechees (1995–1999)

The Peechees (Discogs artist ID: 888588) were a Kill Rock Stars/Lookout! Records band formed by Neuman with Carlos Canedo (guitar), Rop Vasquez (bass), and Christopher Appelgren (vocals). Producer credits on their releases include John Reis (Scented Gum) and Mark Trombino (Do The Math). Three main releases: Cup Of Glory 7” (Kill Rock Stars, 1994), Scented Gum (Lookout! Records, 1996), Do The Math (Kill Rock Stars, 1996), and Games People Play (Kill Rock Stars, 1997). Christopher Appelgren was also a designer on several Bratmobile and Lookout! releases and later president of Lookout! Records.

Love Or Perish (2007–2009)

Love Or Perish (Discogs artist ID: 1616044) was a duo of Neuman (drums) and Carlos Canedo (guitar, bass), with Massimo Scoditti on vocals. Released Start From Zero (Simple Social Graces, 2007) and a split 7” (Exo Records, 2009, Australia).

Lois (session, 1992)

Neuman played drums on two releases by Lois Maffeo (recording as Lois, artist ID: 459516): Press Play And Record (K Records, 1992) and Butterfly Kiss (K Records, 1992). Both produced by Stuart Moxham.

Lookout! Records

Neuman’s Discogs profile identifies her as a co-owner of Lookout! Records until it ceased operations. Her Discogs credits at Lookout! span the label’s peak years: she co-compiled the 1996 sampler Heide Sez (alongside Cathy Bauer and Christopher Appelgren), coordinated the 1999 VHS Turn-On…Turn In…Lookout!, and her Bratmobile albums in the 2000–2002 window were released there. The Peechees’ 1996 Scented Gum 7” was also on Lookout!. Her co-ownership role is not credited directly on individual releases but is noted in the artist profile.

Indivision Management

From 2002 onward, Neuman ran Indivision Management (Discogs entity ID: 3686375), operating alongside co-manager Joey Minkes. Primary clients visible in Discogs:

  • The Donnas — managed through the Atlantic years, 2002–2005 (Spend The Night, Who Invited You, Take It Off, Gold Medal, I Don’t Want To Know)
  • The Locust — 2005–2007 (Safety Second, Body Last on Radio Surgery; New Erections on Anti-)
  • The Tyde — 2006 (Three’s Co. on Rough Trade America)
