Overview

Anne-Marie Hurst is a British vocalist whose career moves through three bands at the heart of the UK goth-rock scene. She sang with The Elements until 1982, then co-founded Skeletal Family in December of that year, recording six releases on Red Rhino between 1983 and 1985. After departing Skeletal Family in 1985, she co-founded Ghost Dance with Gary Marx — formerly of The Sisters of Mercy — a band that eventually signed to Chrysalis in 1989. Her sixty-plus credits span goth rock, post-punk, and deathrock, with activity continuing into the 2010s and 2020s via Cherry Red and Restless Empire releases.

Notes (web) — Ghost Dance / Chrysalis 1989

After exhausting the Karbon Records deal, Ghost Dance’s tour manager Simon “Sparky” Parker transitioned to a management role and secured a recording contract with Chrysalis in 1989. (Wikipedia, Ghost Dance (band), rev. 2025-11-16)

Chrysalis assigned producer Mark Dodson — described by Gary Marx as “an out and out rock producer” — to the lead single “Down to the Wire.” Marx recounted that Dodson asked Hurst to “sing more like Tina Turner,” a directive that signalled the creative tension the label relationship would generate. The single reached No. 66 on the UK Singles Chart in June 1989. (Gary Marx interview, Part 2, angelsinexile.com)

The label then pushed for a re-recording of the early song “Celebrate” as the follow-up single. Its commercial reception was described as “disastrous.” (Wikipedia) Chrysalis subsequently dropped the band. Marx reflected: “Without the immediate promise of more money there wasn’t much to keep us together after the differences in direction the whole Chrysalis experience had served to highlight.” He noted that band members John and Steely were “much more straight-ahead rockers” than himself, and that the Chrysalis period exposed those creative differences. (Gary Marx interview, Part 2, angelsinexile.com)

The band slowly disintegrated; their final concert was in Amsterdam on 4 December 1989. The official Ghost Dance site records they disbanding “after completing a tour in Germany with The Ramones.” (ghost-dance.com/about)
