Forbes Graham

Discogs ID: 476393
Also credited as: Forbes “Corneater” Graham, Forbes, Graham
Origin: Boston, USA

Trumpet player, euphonium player, and electronic musician. Active in experimental rock, free jazz, and improvised music scenes spanning Boston and New York. Best known as a member of Kayo Dot (appearing on Dowsing Anemone With Copper Tongue, 2006). Also a visual artist — painted the cover of his 2022 release Another Day, Another Vector. Runs the Polyrhythmatics collective/website (polyrhythmatics.net).

Career

Amalgamation (late 1990s–2001)

Early band: Amalgamation (Discogs ID 826776), active on the Rice Control label in the late 1990s. Releases include a split with Left In Ruins (1997), a split with Jeromes Dream (1998), and Again To The People. (1998). Forbes credited with “Design [Sleeve Design]; Vocals” on the Left In Ruins split and as performer on the Jeromes Dream split, suggesting a hardcore/post-hardcore context. Members included Adam Witt, Chad Matheny, Chris Chang (2), Eazy (3), Jacob Long, Matt Safer (2), Pat Vogel, and andrew black.

Kayo Dot (2006)

Forbes appeared as a core member of Kayo Dot on Dowsing Anemone With Copper Tongue (2006, Robotic Empire robo 058), playing trumpet, euphonium, percussion, and voice. The double LP was produced by Toby Driver; recorded and mixed by Jim Fogarty, assisted by Chris Fortin; mastered by Alan Douches. Lyrics by Jason Byron (tracks A, B2, C, D) and Driver (B1). Festival of New Trumpet Music / Anthony Braxton (2007, 2011)

Forbes performed in Anthony Braxton’s ensemble at the Festival of New Trumpet Music in 2007, playing Composition No. 103. The recording was released in 2011 on New Braxton House (NBH007) as Two Compositions (Festival Of New Trumpet Music) 2007. Personnel for Composition No. 103: Forbes Graham, John McDonough, Nate Wooley, Nicole Rampersaud, Sam Hoyt, Tim Byrnes (all trumpet); Taylor Ho Bynum (cornet); Braxton conducting. Composition No. 169 used different instrumentation (trombone, French horn, tuba). Liner notes by Taylor Ho Bynum.
