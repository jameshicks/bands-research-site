Jeff Parker

Guitarist, composer, producer. Born Bridgeport, CT. Based in Chicago (arrived 1991). Discogs ID: 44570.

Studied at Berklee College of Music. Active member of the AACM (Association for the Advancement of Creative Musicians). Joined Tortoise in the mid-1990s as lead/rhythm guitarist; also a founding or early member of Isotope 217. Has worked extensively in jazz and improvised music, including the Chicago Underground Quartet and collaborations with Brian Blade.
