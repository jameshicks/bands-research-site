Kelly Clarkson

Full name: Kelly Brianne Clarkson Born: April 24, 1982, Fort Worth, TX Role: Singer-songwriter Discogs ID: 201543 Labels: 19 Recordings / RCA / BMG / S Records (2003–2016); Atlantic (2017–present)

American pop-rock singer-songwriter. First winner of American Idol (Season 1, 2002), signed immediately to 19 Recordings (Simon Fuller’s management label) and RCA. Over a 20-year career she moved from polished pop-rock through a more singer-songwriter-driven phase (My December, 2007) to Atlantic-era contemporary R&B/pop. She is also the longtime host of The Kelly Clarkson Show (NBC, 2019–).

Overview

Clarkson grew up in Burleson, TX. After an unsuccessful attempt to break into the music industry in Los Angeles she returned to Texas and entered American Idol in 2002, winning the first season. Her debut single “A Moment Like This” went to #1 on the Billboard Hot 100 in 2002. She was signed jointly to 19 Recordings (Simon Fuller’s label) and RCA/BMG, a structure that would shape her label relationships for her first decade.

Her commercial peak came with Breakaway (2004), a multi-platinum crossover album built around contributions from Max Martin, Dr. Luke, John Shanks, and Ben Moody. The follow-up My December (2007) was a deliberate artistic pivot — heavier, more personal, substantially co-written by Clarkson — and became the main point of connection between her catalog and the vault’s core world, specifically through the involvement of David Kahne (producer/engineer/keyboards), Mike Watt (bass), and engineer Joe Barresi.

Career by Era

2002–2003 — American Idol and Thankful

Signed simultaneously to 19 Recordings and RCA. Debut album Thankful (2003) was produced by a large committee of pop specialists: Evan Rogers & Carl Sturken (who had broken Ricky Martin), Rhett Lawrence, Clif Magness, Matthew Wilder, Babyface and The Underdogs, Biancaniello & Watters, Desmond Child/Cathy Dennis. Clive Davis served as executive producer. Track 8 (“You Thought Wrong”) features fellow Idol contestant Tamyra Gray.

“Miss Independent” (co-written with Christina Aguilera and Rhett Lawrence) was the breakout single.

2004 — Breakaway

The major commercial breakthrough. Produced by multiple teams:

  • Max Martin and Dr. Luke (Lukasz Gottwald) — “Since U Been Gone,” “Behind These Hazel Eyes” (tracks 2–3)
  • John Shanks — “Breakaway,” “Gone,” “You Found Me” (tracks 1, 5, 9); also produced tracks on All I Ever Wanted (2009)
  • Ben Moody and David Hodges (ex-Evanescence) — “Because of You,” “Addicted” (tracks 4, 6)
  • Chantal Kreviazuk and Raine Maida (Our Lady Peace) — “Where Is Your Heart,” “Walk Away” (tracks 7–8)
  • Clif Magness — “I Hate Myself for Losing You,” “Hear Me” (tracks 10–11)

Title track “Breakaway” was written by Avril Lavigne. Clarkson is credited as co-writer on five tracks. Serban Ghenea mixed the bulk of the album. Clive Davis was again executive producer.

Genres/styles catalogued: Pop Rock, Alternative Rock, Ballad, Soft Rock.

2007 — My December

The most artistically distinctive album of the RCA era. Clarkson fought RCA to release it with minimal outside-songwriter involvement; she is credited as co-writer on all 13 tracks (plus the hidden track “Chivas”). The production team was lean:

Release-level credits:

  • David Kahne — Producer, Engineer, Keyboards, Programmed By (full album credit)
  • Jimmy Messer — Co-producer, Guitar (tracks 1–12), Bass (tracks 9, 11), Engineer [Additional]
  • Jason Halbert — Co-producer, Keyboards, Programmed By
  • Shawn Pelton — Drums (tracks 1–12)
  • Mike Watt (Minutemen / fIREHOSE) — Bass (tracks 3 “Hole,” 10 “How I Feel,” 12 “Can I Have a Kiss”)
  • Billy Mohler — Bass (tracks 1, 2, 4–8, 10, 11, 13a)
  • Joe Barresi — Engineer
  • Andy Wallace — Mixed By
  • Bob Ludwig — Mastered By
  • Mick Rossi (2) — Orchestral arrangements and conductor
  • Aben Eubanks — Guitar/keyboards/programming (tracks 8, 9, 13a); co-wrote “Be Still,” “Maybe,” “Irvine”
  • Erik Friedlander — Cello (track 4 “Sober” and others)

Track-level co-writers include Chantal Kreviazuk and Raine Maida (track 2), Kara DioGuardi (track 2), and Swedish writers Fredrik Rinman and Malcolm Pardon (tracks 5, 11 — via a Scandinavian pop songwriting camp element).

The album stalled commercially — partly due to label conflict, partly because it was a deliberate genre shift toward harder rock and personal songwriting. It was not a failure, reaching #2 on the Billboard 200, but sold significantly less than Breakaway.

Vault connections on My December: Mike Watt’s bass credit is the clearest bridge into the vault’s core world — he is the founding bassist of Minutemen and a central figure in the SST / post-punk underground. Joe Barresi has produced or engineered work by Tomahawk, Isis, and Queens of the Stone Age (see Joe Barresi). David Kahne’s prior credits include The Strokes (First Impressions of Earth, 2006), Regina Spektor (Begin to Hope, 2006), and Rank & File (see David Kahne).

2009 — All I Ever Wanted

Return to a more commercial sound. Release-level Discogs credits are thin for this album — limited to Serban Ghenea (mixed by) and Chris Gehringer (mastered by). Track-level production credits not captured at release level in DB. John Shanks was involved per other sources (web would be needed to confirm full producer list). My Life Would Suck Without You” (#1) and “Already Gone” were the singles.

2011 — Stronger

Again produced outside the DB’s release-level credits. Serban Ghenea (mixed by) and Chris Gehringer (mastered by) are the only credits captured in Discogs. “Stronger (What Doesn’t Kill You)” was the lead single. Label: 19 Recordings / RCA.

2012 — Greatest Hits – Chapter One

Compilation. Released on 19 Recordings / RCA.

2013 — Wrapped in Red

Christmas album. RCA.

2015 — Piece By Piece

Fifth studio album, last for RCA/19 Recordings. No additional credits captured in Discogs at release level.

2017–present — Atlantic Era

Moved to Atlantic Records with Meaning of Life (2017), which leaned into classic soul and R&B influences. Chemistry (2023) followed. Discogs release-level credits for both albums are empty beyond the primary artist credit — track-level production detail not captured in DB for these records.

Connections

  • David Kahne — produced My December in full; the primary reason this entry exists in the vault
  • Mike Watt — bass on three My December tracks; connects Clarkson’s catalog to the Minutemen / SST / post-punk underground
  • Joe Barresi — engineered My December; also worked with Tomahawk, Isis, QOTSA
  • Minutemen — Mike Watt’s core band
  • Max Martin — Swedish pop producer; principal Breakaway architect alongside Dr. Luke
  • Chantal Kreviazuk and Raine Maida (Our Lady Peace) — produced and wrote on both Breakaway and My December; unusual indie-pop-rock crossover
  • Andy Wallace — mixed My December; also mixed The Strokes First Impressions of Earth (2006) in the same David Kahne cluster
  • Bob Ludwig — mastered My December; recurring across Kahne productions
