Overview

Chuck Johnson is a composer and multi-instrumentalist originally from North Carolina, now based in Oakland, California. He works primarily as a pedal steel guitarist and synthesizer player — with additional roles as a recording and mastering engineer — placing his output across ambient, folk, drone, and experimental territory. His catalogue runs from the mid-1990s but accelerated sharply through the 2010s, with Three Lobed Recordings serving as his most consistent label home across more than a decade. His closest recorded collaborator by shared credits is Marielle Jakobsons (23 releases together), and his broader Bay Area network includes Jason Hoopes, Phil Manley, and Daniel Bachman; he also holds membership in Blood Wedding, Idyll Swords, Loki (64), Micro-East Collective, and Saariselka.
