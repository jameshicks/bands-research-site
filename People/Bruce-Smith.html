Bruce Smith

Drummer, producer, songwriter, remixer. Born 14 December 1960, California, USA; raised in Bristol, England. Real name Bruce Neal Smith. Discogs ID: 33813.

One of the central figures in early-1980s British post-punk and dub. Moved through The Pop Group, The Slits, New Age Steppers, Rip Rig & Panic, African Head Charge, and Public Image Limited. Married Neneh Cherry for three years; moved to Sweden. Father of Naima Karlsson (Cherry’s daughter). Session work for Björk. Currently lives in Soho, New York.
