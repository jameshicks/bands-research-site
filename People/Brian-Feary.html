Overview

Brian Feary is a drummer, producer, and label operator based in Christchurch, New Zealand. He founded and runs Melted Ice Cream, the Christchurch indie label that accounts for the large majority of his 117 Discogs credits and has been active from 2010 through at least 2025. As a musician and engineer he has worked steadily within the Christchurch alternative, punk, and indie-rock scene, appearing in Salad Boys, ShackLock Meth Party, Kool Aid, and other local acts. His most frequent collaborator across releases is Joseph Sampson, followed by Johnny Fast and Benjamin Odering.
