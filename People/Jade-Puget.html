Overview

Jade Errol Puget (born November 28, 1973, Santa Rosa, CA) is an American musician and producer best known as guitarist for AFI, which he joined in 1998. Beyond AFI he co-founded the synth-driven electronic duo Blaqk Audio with AFI vocalist Davey Havok, and has played guitar and contributed writing to the straight-edge hardcore project XTRMST. His catalogue spans punk, alternative rock, hardcore, and synth-pop, running from Nitro Records releases in the mid-1990s through major-label work on DreamWorks and Interscope in the 2000s and 2010s, with over 430 Discogs credits alongside long-running collaborators Davey Havok, Adam Carson, and Hunter Burgan. He also carries earlier credits with Redemption 87, Loose Change, and Twinsign.
