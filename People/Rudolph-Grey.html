Rudolph Grey

Discogs ID: 269252
Name variations: Grey; R. Grey; R.Grey
Active: c. 1977–2017+
Role: Guitar, design, liner notes, photography

American jazz/noise guitarist. Co-founder of Red Transistor (NYC no wave, 1977–78). Better known as the long-running leader of Blue Humans, a free improvisation and noise-rock project that drew in a rotating cast of downtown players across three decades.

Discogs describes him as “American jazz guitarist.” He also served as a visual/design contributor on multiple releases — artwork, cover design, liner notes — particularly on Ecstatic Peace! and New Alliance releases.

Web Notes

Added from web research; sources cited inline.

Mars association — confirmed brief membership

Wikipedia’s Mars (band) article states: “They were joined briefly by guitarist Rudolph Grey of Red Transistor.” This confirms the Discogs group-membership association is not an error — Grey did have a brief stint with Mars. Timing relative to Red Transistor (before, during, or after) is not specified in available sources; the Wikipedia phrasing “of Red Transistor” suggests it was concurrent or immediately adjacent. (Wikipedia — Mars (band), as of 2025-06-06)

Conflict note: The Discogs member list for Mars (4) (ID 277107) flags this association; the prior vault entry marked it “may be an error.” Wikipedia confirms it was real. Duration and exact role remain unclear.

AllMusic description

The AllMusic entry identifies Grey as first coming to notice “in the late-1970s New York City post punk and art scene which also produced Sonic Youth and Swans, and played in the influential no wave band Mars.” (AllMusic) This is consistent with the Wikipedia confirmation above.
