Caleb Scofield

Discogs ID: 434370 Role: Bassist, vocalist Active: ~1995–2018 Also known as: Secret C

Bassist/vocalist in Cave In (Hydra Head Records). Guest additional vocals on Isis’s In The Absence Of Truth (2006), track “1000 Shards.” Died March 2018 in a car accident. The October 2018 Isis reunion concert (performed as “Celestial”) was a benefit in his tribute. Also a member of Old Man Gloom alongside Aaron Turner.

Side Projects
