Daniel Kessler

Discogs ID: 329414
Real name: Daniel Alexander Kessler
Groups: Interpol, Big Noble Sources: discogs, web, drownedinsound.com, culturesofresistancefilms.com

Lead guitarist and backing vocalist of Interpol (1997–present). Co-founded the band with Paul Banks at NYU. Also a founding member of Big Noble (with Joseph Fraioli), an ambient/cinematic project launched in 2014.

Caipirinha Music (pre-Interpol label management)

Before Interpol signed to Matador Records, Kessler worked as manager of Caipirinha Music, a New York-based left-field electronic music label operating under Caipirinha Productions (approx. 1998–2003). It was there that he first met Joseph Fraioli, who recorded for the label as Datach’i. Kessler described the label’s output as “very left field kinda Richard D. James type records, very skitzy drum beats but quite melodic at the same time.” [source: drownedinsound.com/in_depth/4148805-interpols-daniel-kessler-on-his-ambient-ish-side-project-big-noble]

Caipirinha Music released over 35 albums from dozens of artists, including full-length albums, concept compilations, film soundtracks, and experimental electronic projects. Datach’i’s debut 10110101 = (Rec+Play) and the follow-up Wearealwayswellthankyou (2000) were among the label’s releases. The label went out of business circa 2003. [source: culturesofresistancefilms.com/caipirinha-music/]

Note: Kessler is confirmed as manager of the label (his own word in the Drowned in Sound interview), not founder or owner. The founder of Caipirinha Productions/Music has not been identified in available sources; Béco Dranoff is documented as a compiler/A&R contributor but not as the label’s founder.

Big Noble

Big Noble is Kessler’s ambient/cinematic side project, co-founded with sound designer Joseph Fraioli (who records solo as Datach’i). The two had known each other for roughly fifteen years before forming the project — they originally connected when Kessler managed an electronic music label on which Fraioli released work. [source: drownedinsound.com/in_depth/4148805-interpols-daniel-kessler-on-his-ambient-ish-side-project-big-noble]

Character and sound

Big Noble occupies ambient and cinematic territory: New York City audio field recordings filtering softly through unhurried guitar lines, with tension used as its own compositional element rather than as something to resolve. Kessler has described the intended listening context explicitly — not background music for a desk, but for commutes, train windows, and walks where exterior sounds interact with the tracks. The second album moves somewhat closer to song structure: “The first record was more improvised. For this record, it was a bit more song-oriented.” [source: stereogum.com/2317024]

Kessler’s stated aim for the project: “I want It’s Later Than You Think to be the soundtrack to the listener’s own personal movie, when they’re walking down the street, when they’re looking out the window of a train, when they’re watching people walk by a window in a café. It’s their movie.” [source: thelineofbestfit.com/news/big-noble-announce-second-album-its-later-than-you-think]

The debut was not conceived as an album: “We never decided to make an album. It was more about collaboration for collaboration’s sake.” [source: drownedinsound.com]

Formation and process

Kessler initiated the project after Interpol’s fourth album, seeking work outside his comfort zone: “I wanted to try and push myself into new boundaries and do things out of my comfort zone; improvising on the spot.” Early sessions were recorded by Fraioli in Kessler’s living room. The second album was made long-distance — Kessler based between Barcelona and New York, Fraioli in Los Angeles. [source: stereogum.com/2317024; drownedinsound.com]

Collaborators and guest contributors on It’s Later Than You Think

  • Tunde Adebimpe (TV on the Radio) and Wilder Zoby — radical reworking of “All the Marbles”. Kessler and Adebimpe are long-standing friends who attended university together; Kessler notes he has known Adebimpe longer than anyone in Interpol. [source: thelineofbestfit.com]
