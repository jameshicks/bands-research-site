Overview

Daron Hollowell is a musician active from 1992 through the mid-2000s, associated with Bats & Mice, Four Hundred Years, and Soulfish (2). His catalogue spans hardcore, emo, and indie rock — primarily through Lovitt Records between 1998 and 2002 — alongside neo soul work via Soulfish, an unusual stylistic pairing. His closest collaborators are Ash Bruce, Dave Jackson, Erin Housholder, and the Four Hundred Years collective, pointing to a tight-knit scene clustered around the Lovitt orbit.
