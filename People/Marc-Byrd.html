Overview

Marc Byrd (born Carey Marcus Byrd) is an American musician, producer, and songwriter whose career spans alternative rock, ambient, and post-rock. He fronted the alternative rock band Common Children through the 1990s and recorded as GlassByrd with his wife Christine Glass before co-founding Hammock with Andrew Thompson in the mid-2000s — a duo that became his primary vehicle, running their own Hammock Music imprint continuously from 2005 through the present. He joined The Choir as musician and producer in 2005, deepening an already close working relationship with Steve Hindalong; together they co-wrote “God of Wonders” in 2000 for the City On A Hill worship series, a song that went on to wide coverage in Christian music. Beyond the Christian alternative world, Byrd developed a significant ambient collaboration with Taylor Deupree, reflecting Hammock’s drift toward post-rock and shoegaze textures over their two-decade catalogue.
