Richard Schuler

Louisville, Kentucky drummer, multi-instrumentalist, visual artist, and vocalist. Discogs ID: 470050. Also credited as “Rich Schuler,” “Rich Schuller,” and simply “Rich” on various releases.

Overview

Richard Schuler is a Louisville underground veteran who was present at two distinct moments in the city’s music history. He played drums on the earliest Squirrel Bait recordings (1982–1983), before the teenage hardcore band that would seed Slint and Bastro settled into its better-known lineups. He then re-emerged in 1990 as drummer for King Kong (3) — the experimental rock collective founded by Ethan Buckler — appearing on the band’s first three releases from 1990 through 1993, including the Drag City debut Funny Farm.

After leaving King Kong’s regular lineup following Funny Farm, Schuler remained within the Louisville / Drag City orbit through the 2000s, contributing drums to releases by Bill Callahan (Smog) and Will Oldham, and joining his wife Carrie Yury in the duo Dolce Volante. He also contributed artwork and visual work to several releases — the Funny Farm cover, the Breeding Ground King Kong demo compilation, the Hot Dog Days 7” — suggesting a creative practice that extended beyond drumming. The Discogs profile notes he made cover art for both Squirrel Bait and King Kong.

Per his Discogs profile: “Schuler was the original drummer for Squirrel Bait and King Kong and made cover art for both bands. He is married to Carrie Jury [sic Yury].”

Dolce Volante

Dolce Volante is a small ensemble Schuler formed with his wife Carrie Yury. Discogs artist ID: 4137417. The group’s Discogs page lists Schuler and Yury as members, with one confirmed release:

  • Dolce Volante (2000, Not On Label, release 9974966): Schuler on Drums/Vocals; Carrie Yury on Vocals/Bass; Zare Na Asumi on Guitar/Vocals; Mary Lass Stewart on Vocals/Guitar. Four-piece on this record.
  • Ben Hur (1998, Not On Label, release 6388486): self-titled debut — no additional credits listed on Discogs.

Mary Lass Stewart also appears on David Grubbs’s The Thicket (1998, Drag City) — a Grubbs album on which Schuler also contributed “wood finishing” artwork — suggesting a shared orbit.

Connections

  • King Kong (3) — drummer 1990–1993 (Bring It On, Old Man On The Bridge, Funny Farm); artwork on Hot Dog Days (1994) and Breeding Ground (2001)
  • Ethan Buckler — King Kong bandleader; primary collaborator across three records
  • Dolce Volante — duo with Carrie Yury (his wife); Discogs ID 4137417
  • Carrie Yury — wife; shared member of Dolce Volante; also member of Fashion Island; Schuler plays drums on her solo album Mutter (2005)
  • David Grubbs — Squirrel Bait co-founding guitarist; Schuler contributed “wood finishing” to Grubbs’s The Thicket (Drag City, 1998)
  • Smog / Bill Callahan — drums on Dongs Of Sevotion (2000, two tracks) and Tit-Related Death (2000, one track); both Drag City
  • Will Oldham — drums/featuring on All Most Heaven (2000, Drag City); Schuler also drums on Mutter (2005), which features Will Oldham on guitar and backing vocals
  • Brian McMahan — Squirrel Bait co-member; engineered Old Man On The Bridge (1991)
  • Britt Walford — Squirrel Bait successor on drums; returned as King Kong guest keyboardist on Funny Farm
  • Drag City — primary label across eight credits spanning 1993–2005
