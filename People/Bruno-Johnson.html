Bruno Johnson

Discogs ID: 323676

Producer and label operator based in Chicago; the driving force behind Okka Disk from its founding in the mid-1990s through the 2010s. His credit appears almost exclusively on Okka Disk releases, where he typically served as Executive-Producer and/or Producer. One exception: a 2016 producer credit on a self-released Mats Gustafsson tribute to Don Cherry (Not On Label).

Overview

Johnson’s profile on Discogs is minimal — no realname, no URLs — but his credits tell the story: he produced or executive-produced nearly every significant release on Okka Disk, the Chicago-based free jazz label. He co-produced key recordings with Peter Brötzmann and Ken Vandermark, and regularly worked alongside engineer/recordist Malachi Ritscher and designer Louise Molnar.

The Okka Disk operation ran from at least 1995 (Mats Gustafsson’s Parrot Fish Eye) through the early 2010s. Johnson’s credits dry up after Pescado (2013), which may indicate the label wound down or reduced activity at that point.
