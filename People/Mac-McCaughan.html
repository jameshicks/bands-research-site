Mac McCaughan

Discogs ID: 228961

American musician; co-founder of Superchunk and Merge Records. Also played drums in Seam on the debut lineup (1990–1992, Days Of Thunder and Headsparks).

Real name: Ralph Lee McCaughan. Name variations: Jabber McCaughan, Mac MacCaughan, Ralph Lee McCaughan III. Groups: Superchunk, Portastatic (solo project, Discogs ID: 23954), Seam, Bricks, Go Back Snowball, Metal Pitcher, Slushpuppies, Wwax, A Number Of Things. Solo LPs: Non-Believers (Merge, 2015), The Sound of Yourself (Merge, 2021).

Played Moog Rogue synthesizer on track 5 (“Castanets”) of Alejandro Escovedo’s A Man Under The Influence (2001), recorded at Modern Recording in Chapel Hill.
