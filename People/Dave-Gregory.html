Dave Gregory

Guitarist, keyboardist. Real name David Charles Gregory. Discogs artist ID: 276091.

Joined XTC in 1979 (first Discogs credits on Drums and Wires); remained through recording of Apple Venus Vol. 1 (1999), leaving before Wasp Star was completed. After XTC, became a core member of Big Big Train (ID: 1208309), appearing on all their major albums through 2023.

Aliases / Dukes Persona

Lord Cornelius Plum (his Dukes of Stratosphear alias — see Lord Cornelius Plum). Also: Bongo (10), Colin Midnite, David Dreams, Jet Pastorius, Rex Rapier, Terry (33).

Post-XTC: Big Big Train

Extensive credits with Big Big Train from English Electric Part One (2012) through Ingenious Devices (2023) and Wild River (2023). Also contributed string arrangements and guitar to Porcupine Tree (Lightbulb Sun, 2000; In Absentia, 2008) and various other UK prog/rock artists.
