Greg Lake

Born 10 November 1947, Bournemouth, Dorset; died 7 December 2016. Bassist, vocalist, guitarist. Discogs artist ID: 259120.

Co-founder of King Crimson (bass, lead vocals on the debut) and of Emerson, Lake & Palmer.

Overview

Sang and played bass on In the Court of the Crimson King (1969) alongside Michael Giles, Ian McDonald, Robert Fripp, and Peter Sinfield. Left to form ELP. Released a self-titled solo album in 1981 (Chrysalis) on which Michael Giles played drums — a post-KC reunion credit. Also associated with various Roger Glover / Purple Records projects.

Discogs years active: 1967–2026 (reflecting posthumous releases). Top labels: Chrysalis, Manticore, Atlantic.

Final Years: Manticore Revival and Moonchild (2015–2016)

In 2015, Lake revived his Manticore label (originally founded by ELP in 1973) together with Italian producer Massimo Marchini. Two productions followed on the relaunched imprint:

  • Live in Piacenza (2017, Manticore) — a live concert album at which Lake first encountered Annie Barbazza, who sang backing vocals
  • Moonchild (2018, Manticore, cat. MANLP002 / MAN002) — a vocal-and-piano duo album by Barbazza and pianist Max Repetti, which Lake had originally conceived for himself; he redirected it when he became ill, and produced it alongside Marchini

Moonchild was one of the last projects Lake completed as a producer. He died 7 December 2016, before the album’s release. After his death, Marchini took over artistic direction of Manticore; the label is now owned by Lake’s widow Regina Uta Lake.

Lake is also credited as producer on track 13 of Annie Barbazza’s solo album Vive (2020, Dark Companion) — another session from his final period.
