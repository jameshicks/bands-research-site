Odd Nosdam

Discogs ID: 32301
Real name: David Madson
Aliases: David Madson (2753281), The Anticon Art Director
Active: 1998–present
Origin: USA

Music producer, DJ, visual artist, and live performer. Co-founder of Anticon and the label’s longtime art director. Member of cLOUDDEAD (with Dose One and Yoni Wolf). Discogs profile describes his aesthetic as “super-saturated, no-fi weirdness that intersects British IDM, West Coast hip-hop and ethereal drone.”

Biography

Early Cassettes and Anticon Origins (1998–2001)

Nosdam’s first documented releases are self-released cassette compilations from 1998: Anecdoticselfportrait and an appearance on Dose One’s Slow Death (The Permanent Cry) — both self-released, pre-label. Plan 9… Meat Your Hypnotis. followed in 1999. These circulated within the small West Coast underground hip-hop tape network that would coalesce into Anticon.

By 1999 he was already handling artwork and graphic design for the Anticon debut: Dose One’s The Taste Of Rain… Why Kneel (Anticon, 1999). The art director credit would become one of his most consistent roles across the label’s catalog through the 2000s.

In 2001 he issued a Split EP! on Anticon (his first official label release) and the self-released Reject Odd Nosdam compilation.

cLOUDDEAD (2001)

cLOUDDEAD — his trio with Dose One and Yoni Wolf — released their self-titled compilation album on Mush (US) and Big Dada Recordings (UK) in 2001, compiling ten 10” singles. The record is a cornerstone of the abstract/experimental hip-hop canon. Nosdam produced the beats.

Anticon Producer Period (2002–2005)

Throughout the early Anticon years Nosdam was ubiquitous across the label as producer, mixer, and designer:

  • No More Wig For Ohio (2002, self-released) — early solo beat collection
  • Layout and design credits on Dose One’s Personal Journals (2002, Anticon) and The No Music (2002, Anticon)
  • Produced and mixed Sole’s Selling Live Water (2003, Anticon)
  • Your American Bonus - Buy David Odd Nosdam (2004, Anticon) — brief in-store bonus release
  • Burner (2005, Anticon) — first major solo album; entirely self-produced, recorded, and mixed; mastered by George Horn at Fantasy Studios

Role at Anticon

Beyond performance and production, Nosdam served as Anticon’s art director for much of the label’s key period (c.1999–2009), with design, layout, and artwork credits across dozens of releases. His visual aesthetic — collage-heavy, saturated, physically worn — was as central to Anticon’s identity as the music.
