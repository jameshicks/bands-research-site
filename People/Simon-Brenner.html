Simon Brenner

Keyboards. Discogs ID: 474111.

Keyboardist and founding member of Talk Talk (1981–1984). Present on the first two albums: The Party’s Over (1982) and It’s My Life (1984). Departed before The Colour of Spring; replaced in practice by Tim Friese-Greene as the band’s keyboard presence and co-writer.

Brenner retains songwriting credits on Talk Talk material from the It’s My Life era: he is credited as co-writer on the Blue Section Two single “Strange Fascination” / “Nothing to Say” (EMI, 12 EMI 5456, 1984) and on the Woyehyeh single “Give This Heart” / “I Hope and I Pray” (Chrysalis, 1985). His interpolation credit on Alex Gold’s “L.A. Today” (Xtravaganza, 2003) alongside Hollis is for Talk Talk-era material.
