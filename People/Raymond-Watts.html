Raymond Watts

Discogs ID: 101943
Real name: Raymond George Watts
Primary alias: Pig (also: Nains, Ray Scaballero)

British industrial rock musician, producer, and vocalist. Member of Zos Kia (as Nains), Coil-adjacent underground, and long-term collaborator of John Gosling.

Overview

Raymond Watts is best known as the primary force behind Pig, his long-running industrial rock project. He has also been a member of KMFDM, Schaft, and Schwein, and has worked extensively in the industrial/electronic underground. His alias “Nains” appears in Zos Kia credits.

His connection to John Gosling spans the early Zos Kia period (as Nains, a Zos Kia member) through to co-production work on Mekon’s later albums.
