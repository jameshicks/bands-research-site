Karizma

Real name: Christopher (Chris Theadore) Clayton Born: March 28, 1970, Baltimore, Maryland Discogs artist ID: 10053 (as Karizma); 319513 (as Kris Klayton) Also records as: Kris Klayton, KaytroniK (ID 976481), K-Man, K2 (12), Da Mutha Funka, Kohesive, Kayorcan, Kristical, Kristikal, Krystopher, Feel Kollyns, Knevrm!nd, Unruly Mass Choir, The Very Ol’ Dirty Bastards

American DJ and producer from Baltimore, Maryland. Best known for a rich blend of house, broken beat, jazz, and hip-hop influences developed across three-plus decades. A founding member of the legendary Baltimore production collective The Basement Boys, he formed one of house music’s most enduring duos with DJ Spen — releasing together as DJ Spen & Karizma (Discogs ID 14216) throughout the late 1990s and 2000s. His solo work found an international platform on R2 Records (UK) starting in 2007. A parallel electronic/experimental project, KaytroniK, has operated since at least the mid-2000s and culminated in a full-length album, Thee Album (R2 Records, 2016).

Biography

Baltimore Origins and The Basement Boys (early 1990s–1997)

Karizma came up in Baltimore’s underground house scene in the early 1990s, working within the orbit of The Basement Boys — the city’s foundational production unit responsible for bringing gospel-inflected, deep house to national attention. His production alias at this stage was largely Kris Klayton, the name under which he is credited as a member of both The Basement Boys and the DJ Spen & Karizma duo. An early production credit appeared in 1994 on “Blow” (Unruly Records), followed by engineering and production work on releases via the short-lived Hardhead Records in 1995. By 1997 he was contributing vocals to Solid Ground on Basement Boys Records, the label co-run by the collective.

DJ Spen Partnership and Basement Boys Records (1997–2005)

The creative partnership with DJ Spen (Sean Spencer) was the defining axis of Karizma’s career through the late 1990s and early 2000s. As DJ Spen & Karizma, the duo produced, remixed, and arranged a large volume of material for Basement Boys Records and beyond. Key production credits include:

  • In The Sky (1999, BBR) — produced, arranged, engineered, mixed
  • Praisin His Name (2002, BBR) — produced
  • Another Day (Blaze Remixes) (2003, BBR) — produced

Outside BBR, the duo remixed and produced for Defected (Believe Part 2, 2000; Freak E.P., 2002), Nervous Records (Paradise, 2001), Slip ‘n’ Slide, NuLife, VC Recordings, Oyster Music, and Ocean Trax, among others. Their sole primary-credited duo release is 4 The Love (The Collective Minds Theme) (2005, Code Red Recordings).

Karizma also logged solo production credits at BBR during this period: He’s The One (2001), Shades Of “K” EP (2000), and engineering on Gotta Keep Tryin (2000) and Dancin (2000).

Solo Career and International Profile (2006–2014)

Karizma’s solo profile shifted international around 2006–2007. He co-produced and co-wrote “The Cube” (2006) on Objektivity with Dennis Ferrer — a New York-based connection that extended his network into the Defected/Objektivity axis. That same year the Strings Emotional EP and In Tha D.ee.p appeared on Ricanstruction Label.

His debut album A Mind Of Its Own (2007, R2 Records, UK) established him as a solo artist on the international deep house circuit. The album was reissued and expanded as A Mind Of Its Own V2.0 - The Upgrade (2009, R2). Follow-up singles Good Morning (2010, featuring Monique Bingham) and Wall Of Sound (2013) continued on R2. The 2013 album Wall Of Sound was mastered by Atjazz (Martin Iveson) and marked a sustained period of collaboration with Iveson’s Atjazz Record Company.

Also in 2007 he compiled and DJ mixed Coast 2 Coast (NRK Sound Division, UK) and the Soul Heaven DJ mix (Soul Heaven Records), the latter alongside Kenny “Dope” Gonzalez.

KaytroniK and Later Work (2015–present)

The KaytroniK alias has been active as a distinct Discogs entity (ID 976481) since at least the mid-2000s. The project centers on electronic and broken-beat territory with a more experimental edge than the Karizma house catalog. The full-length Thee Album (2016, R2 Records) was produced and written by KaytroniK, with Karizma serving as executive producer alongside Kyri Patsalidis, and mastering by Martin Iveson (Atjazz). A 2015 remix for Ninja Tune (Kaytronik / Yoruba Soul Mixes) and later work for Atjazz Record Company round out the alias’s traceable credits.

On the Karizma side, Tech This Out Pt. 1 (2018, Atjazz Record Company) continued the R2/Atjazz axis. A 2022 remix for Nervous Records (Love Like This) and Can’t Call !t (2025, R2 Records) confirm ongoing activity into the mid-2020s.
