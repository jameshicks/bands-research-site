Steve Marker

Guitarist, engineer. Discogs artist ID: 177032. Madison, Wisconsin. Co-founder of Smart Studios with Butch Vig. Member of Garbage.

Overview

One of the three Wisconsin-based musicians (with Vig and Duke Erikson) who formed Garbage around 1993–1994. At Smart Studios, Marker engineered sessions for many of the same Touch And Go and Sub Pop bands that Vig produced. Credited as additional engineer on L7’s Bricks Are Heavy (1992, Slash).

Marker is the one who spotted Shirley Manson’s band Angelfish (2) on MTV’s 120 Minutes, which led to Manson’s recruitment into Garbage.

On Garbage releases Marker plays guitar, keyboards, bass, samples, loops, synthesizer, programming, and noise — contributing across the full range of the band’s electronic-rock texture. He also co-wrote all songs and co-produced from the debut onward.

Name variations on Discogs: Marker, Markes, S. Marker, S. W. Marker, Steve, Steve Marjer, Steve Market.
