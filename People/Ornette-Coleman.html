Ornette Coleman

American saxophonist, composer, free jazz pioneer. Discogs artist ID: 224506. Born 9 March 1930, Fort Worth, Texas; died 11 June 2015, New York City.

Signed to Atlantic Records in the late 1950s by Nesuhi Ertegun. His Atlantic recordings were foundational documents of free jazz:

AlbumYear
Something Else!!!!1958
Tomorrow Is the Question!1959
The Shape of Jazz to Come1959
Change of the Century1960
Free Jazz: A Collective Improvisation1961
Ornette on Tenor1962

Engineered by Tom Dowd for most of these sessions. The Ornette Coleman Quartet (Discogs ID: 317878) and Ornette Coleman Double Quartet (ID: 317886) also appear in the Atlantic catalog.
