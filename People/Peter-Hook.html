Peter Hook

English bassist, songwriter, and co-founder of Joy Division and New Order. Born Peter Woodhead, 13 February 1956, Salford, Greater Manchester. Known universally as “Hooky.” Discogs ID: 120532. Discogs Peter Hook And The Light ID: 4693825.

His signature style — melodic bass lines played high on the neck, often in the upper register while Bernard Sumner handled rhythm guitar — defined the sonic architecture of both Joy Division and New Order.

Biography by Era

Pre-Joy Division: Warsaw and Stiff Kittens (1976–1978)

Hook formed the group that became Joy Division with Sumner, Ian Curtis, and Stephen Morris in Salford/Manchester. The band went through early names — first Stiff Kittens, then Warsaw — before settling on Joy Division in early 1978. Under the Warsaw name they released The Ideal Beginning EP (Anonymous Records, 1978) and recorded the Warsaw sessions (later released in bootleg and archival form). Hook played bass and contributed backing vocals throughout.

Joy Division (1978–1980)

Joy Division — Hook, Sumner, Curtis, Morris — recorded all studio material with producer Martin Hannett on Factory Records:

Ian Curtis died 18 May 1980. The remaining trio — Hook, Sumner, Morris — regrouped as New Order, with Hook co-writing “Ceremony” and “In A Lonely Place” (the final Joy Division recordings, credited to New Order).

New Order (1980–2007)

Hook was a primary member and co-writer for New Order’s entire classic run. Key albums and significant Hook credits:

Singles include “Blue Monday” (1983, best-selling 12” single of all time in the UK), “True Faith” (1987), “Bizarre Love Triangle” (1986), “Regret” (1993). Hook co-wrote virtually all of them.

Hook left New Order in 2007 (some sources say 2010; the Discogs credits show his participation through the 2000s). A protracted legal dispute over the New Order name followed, with Hook asserting joint ownership. The other members continued under the New Order name with replacement members including Tom Chapman (2) on bass.

Revenge (1989–1993)

Hook’s first post-New Order (concurrent) side project. Revenge was a band unit on Factory Records and Capitol Records, with Hook on vocals and bass alongside:

  • David Potts (guitar) — also later in Monaco
  • Dave Hicks (guitar, keyboards)
  • Chris Jones (keyboards)
  • Brian Whittaker

Discogs ID: 44608. Key release: One True Passion (Capitol Records, 1990) — Hook: vocals, bass, keyboards, written-by; art direction by Peter Saville. Singles included “7 Reasons,” “Pineapple Face,” “Slave,” “Gun World Porn.”

Production Work — The Stone Roses (1988–1989)

Hook produced The Stone Roses’ debut single “Elephant Stone” (Silvertone Records, ORE T 1, 1988), mixed by John Leckie. He is also credited as producer on The Stone Roses album (1989, Silvertone) and its retrospective releases (The Complete Stone Roses, 1995; Turns Into Stone, 2012). This is a notable outlier credit — Hook as producer for a band he had no performance connection with.

Monaco (1995–2000)

Duo of Hook and David Potts (Discogs ID: 23667). Less industrial than Revenge; the project leaned into Britpop, trip-hop, and progressive house. Albums on Polydor:

Freebass (2009–2012)

A supergroup of Manchester bassists: Hook, Andy Rourke (The Smiths), and Gary Mounfield (The Stone Roses / Primal Scream). Discogs ID: 1280925 (Freebass (9)). Additional members: Gary Briggs, Nat Wason, Paul Kehoe, Phil Murphy. One album released: It’s A Beautiful Life (Haçienda Records, 2010); Hook credited as bass and producer.

Peter Hook And The Light (2010–present)

After departing New Order, Hook formed Peter Hook And The Light to perform Joy Division and New Order catalog material live in full — often performing complete albums in sequence. Discogs ID: 4693825.

K÷93 (2021)

One-off supergroup project with Jaz Coleman (Killing Joke) and Geordie Walker (Killing Joke). Discogs ID: 8993392. Released K÷93 (Cadiz Entertainment, CADIZ12198, 2021); Hook credited as bass and sleeve notes. Three tracks: “Remembrance Day,” “Giving Up The Ghost,” “Scrying.”

Haçienda Classical / Joy Division Orchestrated

Hook has organized orchestral reworkings of the Factory Records / Haçienda catalog:

  • Haçienda Classiçal (Sony Classical, 2016) — Hook: executive producer, soloist; features Graeme Park, Mike Pickering, the Manchester Camerata, Rowetta, and others
  • Dreams (Peter Hook Presents Joy Division Orchestrated) (Haçienda Records, 2021) — Hook: primary credit; features Jack Bates, Rowetta, Natalie Findlay, Paul Kehoe, Mica Millar, and credits Stephen Morris, Bernard Sumner, Ian Curtis as written-by
