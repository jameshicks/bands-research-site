Overview

Kevin McMahon (Kevin S. McMahon) is a producer, engineer, and musician based in upstate New York, where he operates Marcata Recording. He is also a member of The Cross Sea. His production career stretches back to 1995 and spans more than 430 credits across indie rock, punk, experimental, and post-rock. His most sustained relationships are with the Swans camp — Michael Gira, Norman Westberg, Chris Pravdica, Thor Harris, Phil Puleo, and Kristof Hahn all rank among his top collaborators through Young God Records — and with Titus Andronicus via a long-running partnership with Patrick Stickles; his broader work touches Domino, XL Recordings, Merge, and Captured Tracks.

McMahon–Gira connection: what is known and what is not

What is confirmed by sources:

McMahon was already operating Marcata Recording in upstate New York (Gardiner/New Paltz area) and had an established experimental/indie production career before the Swans reunion. When Swans reconvened in 2010, McMahon traveled to Berlin to handle recording at Studio P4 and at Andere Baustelle (the former Einsturzende Neubauten studio), then continued recording and mixing back at Marcata. Michael Gira served as producer throughout; McMahon was the engineer/mixer.

A Stereogum news item from 2010 describes Gira’s existing production plans and names McMahon as handling the engineering; the same item notes “the recordings will continue at Marcata in early October after Swans complete their current tour.” [https://stereogum.com/759521/swans-recording-new-album-preparing-live-record/news] A contemporaneous short piece titled “Kevin McMahon is mixing the first Swans album in 14 years” appeared at justmanaging.com (now sparse), confirming McMahon was at Marcata mixing My Father Will Guide Me up a Rope to the Sky (2010) — the first reunion album. [http://justmanaging.com/kevin-mcmahon-is-mixing-the-first-swans-album-in-14-years/]

One search-result snippet (not independently fetchable) characterises McMahon as Gira’s “trusted friend and engineer,” implying a pre-existing relationship, but no primary source was located that uses this phrase or expands on it.

A full-length interview with McMahon appeared in Tape Op issue #114 (interviewer: Zac Meyer), titled “Kevin McMahon: Indie Recording Pioneer,” published July/Aug 2016. [https://tapeop.com/interviews/114/kevin-mcmahon] The page rendered in a second fetch attempt (2026-11-06) but the interview body is paywalled after the opening section. The visible preview text names Swans only in a credential list (“he has produced Swans, Real Estate, Titus Andronicus, Widowspeak, and more excellent artists”) without touching the origin of his Gira relationship. The portion of the interview that is freely readable covers Marcata’s origins with The Walkmen and McMahon’s background as an audio technician; the Swans-specific section, if it exists in the full interview, is behind a subscription wall. It cannot be confirmed from the available text whether the interview discusses how McMahon first came to work with Gira.
