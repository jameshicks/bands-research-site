Biography

Danny Farrant is a British drummer. His earliest Discogs-documented credit is on Kings Of London - Live (2001), a Spear Of Destiny live album released by Kirk Brandon’s band on a self-released/Not On Label basis. That session also connected him to Kirk Brandon and bandmates Warren Wilson and James Yardley — a network that would recur later through Theatre Of Hate.

In April 2005 Farrant replaced Philip Barker on drums in Buzzcocks, joining the lineup of Pete Shelley (guitar/vocals), Steve Diggle (guitar/vocals), and Tony Barber (bass). Flat-Pack Philosophy (Cooking Vinyl, 2006) — recorded with that lineup — was released shortly after Barker’s departure, and Discogs credits the drumming on that album to Barker, meaning it was tracked before or shortly after the transition. Farrant’s first formally credited Buzzcocks recording is 30 (Cooking Vinyl, 2007), a 30th-anniversary live/compilation set.

Tony Barber departed in April 2008 and was replaced by Chris Remington on bass. The Shelley–Diggle–Remington–Farrant lineup remained stable through the 2010s, recording The Way (Pledge Music, 2014) — the final Buzzcocks studio album with Pete Shelley. The album lists Farrant as drums alongside Shelley (guitar, vocals), Diggle (guitar, vocals), and Remington (bass). A 2014 single, “It’s Not You” (1-2-3-4 Records), also carries a written-by credit for Farrant, one of only a few songwriting credits in his Discogs record.

Pete Shelley died on 6 December 2018. Diggle continued the band, with Farrant and Remington remaining as the rhythm section. Mani Perazzoli joined as a touring guitarist/vocalist. The post-Shelley studio catalog includes Sonics In The Soul (Cherry Red, 2022) — produced and co-mixed by Steve Diggle and Laurence Loveless — and Attitude Adjustment (Cherry Red, 2026), both featuring Farrant on drums.

Alongside Buzzcocks, Farrant is a credited member of Theatre Of Hate, the post-punk band fronted by Kirk Brandon. He appears on Kinshi (Pledge Music, 2016), their studio album, with band member credit and co-writing credit alongside Brandon, Stan Stammers, Adrian Portas, and John Lennard. He also appears on the undated The Dark Star Project (Not On Label), another Spear Of Destiny release featuring overlapping personnel from both bands.

A separate credit appears on the 2020 single “Don’t Go Changing Soho” by Tim Arnold (TA Music), which features an assortment of British pop/rock figures including Boy George, Chrissie Hynde, Marc Almond, Glen Matlock, Guy Chambers, and Gary Kemp.
