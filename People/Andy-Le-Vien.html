Andy Le Vien

Real name: Andrew Le Vien. UK recording engineer, producer, and studio owner based in South London. Discogs artist ID: 307687.

Overview

Andy Le Vien is the founder and long-time owner of RMS Studios (Discogs label ID: 274539), a recording studio located in South Norwood / Crystal Palace, South London, operating since 1977 — originally under the name Pukka Recorders, co-founded with Wally Brill, then renamed RMS in 1978 and co-owned for a period with Ranald MacDonald. Le Vien has been its sole owner since MacDonald’s departure.

RMS Studios functioned as an affordable recording facility serving the UK post-punk, anarcho-punk, goth, alternative, and DIY scenes throughout the 1980s and 1990s. A large number of early UK punk and post-punk bands recorded there, including Blyth Power, Bone Orchard, and many Oi! and anarcho-punk acts. In parallel, Le Vien worked outside RMS as a recording engineer for UK independent and major labels (Mercury, A&M, Cherry Red, Vinyl Solution, Jungle Records, All The Madmen Records).

From the late 2000s, Le Vien shifted significantly towards sound restoration and remastering work, becoming a recurring technician for Pressure Sounds (UK reggae reissue label), restoring classic Jamaican recordings by Augustus Pablo, Lee Perry, King Tubby, Bunny Lee, and others. He also remastered for Optic Nerve Recordings and smaller specialist labels.

Le Vien also records under the alias A Fuckin’ Hippy (listed on Discogs as an alias).
