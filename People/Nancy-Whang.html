Nancy Whang

Discogs ID: 216729 Name variations: N. Whang, Nancy Wang, Whang New York vocalist and keyboardist. Core member of LCD Soundsystem and primary vocalist of The Juan MacLean on DFA Records.

Overview

Nancy Whang is a New York-based vocalist and multi-instrumentalist who became a fixture of the DFA Records universe in the early 2000s. She is best known as the live keyboardist and frequent on-record vocalist for LCD Soundsystem across both of the band’s active periods (2002–2011 and 2015–present), and as the primary vocalist and co-writer for The Juan MacLean’s house-influenced output.

Her first documented Discogs credit is a photography credit on a 1997 Flake Music / Scared of Chaka split (702 Records/Science Project), suggesting she was embedded in indie rock circles before the DFA era. By 2002 she appears on both the DFA’s two founding releases: LCD Soundsystem’s debut single Losing My Edge (additional vocals) and The Juan MacLean’s debut single You Can’t Have It Both Ways (vocals).

On LCD Soundsystem recordings, Whang is credited under a variety of instrumental roles — synthesizer, keyboards, organ, piano, electric piano (Wurlitzer), sampler — as well as vocals. On The Juan MacLean she is the consistent voice of the project across every major release, and a writing credit co-author on most of the catalog from 2008 onward.

Beyond the two home projects, she has been a featured or collaborative performer with Munk, Shit Robot (Marcus Lambkin), and appeared on Idles’ Tangk (2024) alongside James Murphy.

LCD Soundsystem

Whang’s LCD Soundsystem role evolved from an early session/additional-vocals presence (2002–2004) into a fully credited band member with defined instrumental duties.

The live farewell concert document (The Long Goodbye, released 2014) lists her playing synthesizer, vocals, piano, organ, sampler (samples), and Wurlitzer electric piano — the most detailed accounting of her full instrumental scope. The London Sessions (2011) credits her as “Keyboards, Vocals” alongside the full live band lineup of Tyler Pope, Patrick Mahoney, David Scott Stone, Matt Thornley, Gavin R. Russom, and Murphy.

The Juan MacLean

Whang is the primary vocalist on virtually every The Juan MacLean release and a recurring co-writer from Happy House (2008) onward.
