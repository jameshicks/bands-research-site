Lotic

Real name: J’Kerian Morgan Discogs artist IDs: 3566928 (Lotic), 7925607 (J’Kerian Morgan) Name variations: @Lotic, Lōtic Origin: Texas, US; Berlin-based Active: 2011–present

Overview

Lotic is the primary recording alias of J’Kerian Morgan, a Texas-born, Berlin-based electronic producer and artist. Morgan emerged from the early 2010s underground internet music scene — self-releasing DJ mixes and EPs on small labels including FEELINGS, Sci-Fi & Fantasy, and Janus Berlin — before signing to Tri Angle for the Heterocetera EP (2015) and the album Power (2018). Power is among Tri Angle’s final major releases before the label closed in 2020. Morgan’s 2021 album Water (Houndstooth) marks a move toward R&B and soul textures alongside the deconstructed club and experimental electronics of earlier work.

Morgan also records under the name J’Kerian Morgan (a secondary Discogs alias pointing back to the Lotic entry). The existing Discogs entry for “J’Kerian Morgan” (ID: 7925607) is listed as an alias of Lotic and carries a 2013–2018 activity window.

Note on Wife (4): Early vault entries and the Tri Angle article incorrectly attributed the alias “Wife (4)” to J’Kerian Morgan / Lotic. Wife (4) is a separate artist — real name James Kelly (also recording as James Kelly (5)) — who released What’s Between (TRI026LP, 2014) on Tri Angle. Morgan and Kelly are distinct persons on Discogs. Morgan does not appear to have used the Wife alias.

Notget Remix (2015, One Little Indian 1304tp12)

Lotic provided the “Fromdeath Version” remix of Björk’s “Notget.” The release also credits Arca (4) as Written-By/Composed By. Limited 12”, single-sided with etched B-side bearing the inscription “go matriarchs go” (in Björk’s handwriting). A separate “Keptsafe Version” exists (mastered by Jeremy Cox (4)) — that single does not list Lotic as mixer.
