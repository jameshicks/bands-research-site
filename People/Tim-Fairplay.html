Biography

Tim Fairplay is a London-based producer and multi-instrumentalist who emerged from the orbit of Andrew Weatherall’s post-Two Lone Swordsmen scene in the late 2000s. He plays guitar, bass, keyboards, and handles programming; his credits span solo electronic releases, collaborative projects, and session/co-production work across psych, electro, kosmische, and post-punk-influenced electronics.

Early involvement with Weatherall (2007–2011)

Fairplay’s earliest Discogs credit is a 2004 limited release on Not On Label. His first major association is with Weatherall’s Kill The DJ Records releases — he played guitar on the French label’s 2007 and 2009 singles (credited as guitar on Battant’s Kevin [1989]. Blah Blah and Battant’s No Head). He also played guitar and bass on Weatherall’s solo album A Pox On The Pioneers (2009, Rotters Golf Club), joining a recording band that included Bobby Gillespie (backing vocals), Steve Boardman (programming/keys), and Chris Mackin.

Through Battant — a UK four-piece also including Andy Baxter, Chloé Raunet, and Joel Dever — Fairplay was already operating in the Kill The DJ / post-punk electronic space before The Asphodells formed.

His first solo EP appeared on Astro Lab Recordings (Germany) in 2011: Timothy J Fairplay EP, on which he handled writing, production, and mixing.

The Asphodells (2012–2014)

In 2012, Fairplay co-founded The Asphodells (Discogs ID 2733213) with Andrew Weatherall. The duo released one main studio album: Ruled By Passion, Destroyed By Lust (2012, Rotters Golf Club; also released on Beat Records in Japan). The album features Fairplay on keyboards, guitar, bass guitar, and programming, with Weatherall on keyboards, vocals, melodica, and percussion; both are credited as co-writers throughout. A remixes collection, Remixed (2013, Rotters Golf Club), followed. A further single, Before Your Eyes / Velo (2014, Telophase), rounded out the catalogue. Andy Baxter (Battant) played bass guitar and baglama on the album.

The Asphodells were closely tied to the Rotters Golf Club aesthetic — kosmische-influenced deep techno with live instrumentation. The name references the mythological asphodel, a flower associated with the underworld.

Solo and alias work, expanding output (2012–2020)

During and after The Asphodells, Fairplay built a consistent solo discography under his own name and a range of aliases:

As Tim Fairplay (solo):

  • Somebody, Somewhere (2012, Emotional Response) — solo EP on Weatherall’s associate label
  • B-Ultras (2014, [Emotional] Especial) — solo EP
  • The Cat Prowls Again / A Strange Servant (2016, Höga Nord Rekords, Sweden)
  • Mindfighter EP (2017, Höga Nord Rekords)
  • The Way is Opened and Closed (2017, Work For Love)
  • Where Is The Champion? (2017, Charlois)
  • DX Marks The Spot EP (2018, Body Works, USA) — with design by The Designers Republic
  • Skylark II (2019, Mystic & Quantum, Spain)

As Antoine Rouge (Discogs ID 3559863; active 2013–2015):

  • Spook Juke EP (2013, Crimes Of The Future)
  • Eye Of The Star Warrior (2015, Centurians Of Rome) This alias operated in darker, more industrial-adjacent territory, released on small boutique UK labels.

As Junior Fairplay (Discogs ID 3998348; active 2014–2018):

  • Sugar Puss (2014, Crimes Of The Future)
  • How Do You Like Me Now? (2015, [Emotional] Especial) — also appears in his main-name discography as co-producer
  • Faxes From The Future (2018, [Emotional] Especial) His most active alias for [Emotional] Especial releases.

Effectron II (ID 12490375), Leon Kaiser (ID 14686116), and Roland Telescopes (ID 14841460) are registered on Discogs but have no credited releases in the database.

Session / production credits during this period:

  • Engineer and programmer on Weatherall’s St Anthony: An Ode To Anthony H Wilson (2015, Skinny Dog)
  • Engineer, additional guitar/keyboards on Primal Scream’s More Light (2013, First International/Love Da Records) — working in the wider Weatherall-Primal Scream circle
  • Co-producer on Dot Allison’s Bizarre Feeling (2013, [Emotional] Especial) and EP#2 (2014, [Emotional] Especial)
  • Engineer/programmer on Chewing The Fat (2014, Cherry Red)
  • Producer on How Do You Like Me Now? (2015, [Emotional] Especial)

Dungeon Module era (2021–present)

From 2021, Fairplay became closely associated with Dungeon Module (Discogs ID 2159458), a UK label that appears to be his own or co-run imprint. The output here shifted toward a darker, more abstract electronic and kosmische style, often with artwork credits alongside writing credits, suggesting Fairplay is handling the full visual and sonic identity.

Key Dungeon Module releases:

  • A Snowstorm In The Tropics (2021) — with frequent collaborator Alden Tyrell (Martijn Hoogendijk, ID 8201; 20 shared releases total)
  • Dungeon Module Volume One (2023) — compilation/anthology featuring Alden Tyrell, Jamie Paton, Computerdisc, and others
  • I Lay Awake At Night Scheming (2023)
  • Rule The Streets (2023)
  • Dungeon Module Volume Two (2024) — with James Bell (35) / Computerdisco, Alden Tyrell, Jamie Paton
  • Local Networks (2024)
  • Home Of The Cone (2025)

His single most frequent collaborator across the entire discography is Alden Tyrell (aka Martijn Hoogendijk, The Tyrell Corporation; ID 8201) with 20 shared releases, centred on the Dungeon Module catalogue. Jamie Paton (ID 815247, also known as Ape Into Jam) appears on 7 releases; Computerdisc (ID 13343061) on 6.

Other later output: Convictions That Stick (2024, Pinkman, Netherlands), DDS09 (2024, Dalmata Daniel, Hungary — with Anders Peterson, Daniel Jani, Max Sinclair, Norwell, Angela Santa), Chariots In The Sky EP (2022, Archaic Future Sounds, Austria — with Luca Reiter-Haibach, Grey People, Johnny Yen DJ).
