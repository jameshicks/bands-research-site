Oct 10, 20269 min read

Jaimie Branch

Born: 17 June 1983, Huntington, NY
Died: 22 August 2022, Chicago, IL
Role: Trumpet, composer, bandleader, electronics, vocals
Discogs ID: 914585
Name variations: Branch; J. Branch; Jaimie; Jamie Branch

American trumpeter and composer who became one of the most distinctive voices in post-free jazz and creative music during the 2010s. Rooted in the Chicago improvised music scene before relocating to New York, then back to Chicago, Branch led the fly or die quartet whose four records on International Anthem Recording Company established her as a major figure. She died in August 2022 at age 39, leaving a completed final album released posthumously in 2023.

Biography

Early Life and Chicago Scene (pre-2010)

Branch was born in Huntington, New York in 1983 and moved to Chicago, where she embedded herself in the city’s post-AACM improvised music world. She became a member of the large collective Who Cares How Long You Sink, a sprawling Chicago ensemble whose membership included Jason Ajemian, Jeff Parker, Josh Berman, Frank Rosaly, Tim Daisy, Aram Shelton, Matt Bauder, Jessica Pavone, Kyle Bruckmann, Emmett Kelly, and many others — effectively a node in the city’s entire creative music network.

Her earliest Discogs credit is a 2003 compilation on To Define Records, performing with The Indecisives. A 2011 session credit on Marketa Irglova’s Anar (Anti-) places her in a folk/chamber context alongside Fredrick Lonberg-Holm (cello) and Frank Rosaly (drums), showing the range of Chicago-adjacent work she took on before her own projects crystallized.

Other documented group memberships from this period include:

  • Bullet Hell — group; limited Discogs documentation
  • Galactic Unity Ensemble — large-ensemble context
  • New Fracture Quartet — quartet
  • Predella Group — Chicago-based group
  • Keefe Jackson’s Project Project (as Keefe Jackson’s Project Project) — documented Chicago ensemble

New York Period and Rising Profile (2013–2016)

Branch also spent time in New York, building connections with the city’s downtown and experimental jazz scenes. The 2013 credit on Viscous (Molk Records) and the 2015 Streams (Mole-Tree Music) document steady sideman activity in this period. She became a member of Heroes Are Gang Leaders, a poetry-music collective, and Mofaya!, a group operating in New York free jazz circuits.

She also participated in Christopher Parker & The Band Of Guardian Angels alongside drummer Christopher Parker.

fly or die Debut and International Anthem (2017)

Branch’s debut as a leader, Fly Or Die (2017, International Anthem Recording Company), announced a fully formed artistic vision. The record showcased her core quartet — Branch (trumpet, composer), Jason Ajemian (bass), Chad Taylor (drums), and Tomeka Reid (cello on the debut) — alongside additional voices:

fly or die II and Expanded Quartet (2019)

Fly Or Die II: Bird Dogs Of Paradise (2019) marked a key transition: Lester St. Louis (cello, percussion) replaced Tomeka Reid as the permanent fourth voice, cementing the classic lineup. Branch’s instrumentation expanded to include voice, synth, bells, whistle, and memorably — sneaker squeaks. All four members shared composition credits across tracks.

Anteloper with Jason Nazary (2018–2022)

Alongside the fly or die work, Branch co-founded Anteloper (Discogs ID: 6485546), a duo with drummer and electronic musician Jason Nazary. The project takes a more electronics-forward approach than fly or die, blending trumpet with synthesizers, drum machines, and processed sound. Anteloper released four records:

Pink Dolphins is notable for featuring Jeff Parker as producer, guitarist, bassist, percussionist, and Korg MS-20 player — a substantial guest contribution. Branch co-composed tracks; Nazary and Parker shared writing credits.

Zurich Concert and European Work (2020)

Branch extended into European touring and collaborative sessions. Zurich Concert (2020, Intakt Records) is co-credited to the Dave Gisler Trio (Dave Gisler — guitar, Raffaele Bossard — bass, Lionel Friedli — drums) with Branch on trumpet. The record was mixed by David Torn — an unusual production choice that suggests creative latitude in how the sessions were handled.

fly or die Live (2021)

The live document Fly Or Die Live (2021, International Anthem Recording Company) captures the core quartet — Ajemian (bass, vocals, shaker), St. Louis (cello, vocals, cymbal), Taylor (drums, vocals, mbira), Branch (trumpet, vocals, vibraslap) — in full performance. All four members contribute voice. Branch handled cover photography and collage design herself.

Pink Dolphins and Final Year (2022)

The final Anteloper album Pink Dolphins was released in 2022, with Branch also contributing cover art and cover design alongside the music. Also in 2022 she contributed painting to the Medicine Singers release (Joyful Noise / Stone Tapes) and liner notes to Marvin Tate’s D-Settlement (American Dreams Records), showing ongoing engagement with Chicago’s wider creative community until the end.

Death and Posthumous Release

Jaimie Branch died on 22 August 2022 in Chicago. She was 39. The cause of death was not publicly specified. Fly Or Die Fly Or Die Fly Or Die ((World War)), completed before her death, was released in 2023 on International Anthem Recording Company.

Fly Or Die Fly Or Die Fly Or Die ((World War)) (2023)
