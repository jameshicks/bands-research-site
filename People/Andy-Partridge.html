Oct 09, 20262 min read

Andy Partridge

Discogs ID: 229492
Real name: Andrew John Partridge
Origin: Malta (raised Swindon, England)
Aliases: Animal Jesus, Sandy Sandwich, Sir John Johns, Terry (31)

Singer-songwriter and guitarist. Co-founder of XTC (1972–2000). Principal songwriter across the XTC catalog; co-songwriter with Colin Moulding. 2 shared Discogs releases with Robyn Hitchcock, including the collaborative EP Planet England (2019, Ape House). Known for Anglophile psychedelia, complex arrangements, and his APE (Andy Partridge Enterprises) label.
