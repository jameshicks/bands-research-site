Skip McDonald

Real name: Bernard Alexander. Born 1 September 1949, Dayton, Ohio. Discogs IDs: 40858 (Skip McDonald), 565130 (Bernard Alexander), 14564 (Little Axe). American guitarist, bassist, keyboardist, producer, and arranger, based in New York and London.

One of the most-deployed musicians in the On-U Sound catalog — 187 release credits across 66 unique masters, the largest role clusters being Producer (57 releases), Guitar (46), and Written-By (43). Alongside Keith LeBlanc and Doug Wimbish, McDonald formed the Sugar Hill Records house rhythm section (formally the Sugarhill Gang Band) from around 1979, playing on some of hip-hop’s most significant early recordings. Adrian Sherwood brought all three to London, where they became the rhythmic engine of the On-U Sound collective and the core of Tackhead and Mark Stewart And The Maffia. Simultaneously McDonald built a solo career under the alias Little Axe, blending acoustic Delta blues, dub, and psychedelia across a series of albums on Wired Recordings, Real World Records, and Echo Beach.

Early Career: Wood, Brass & Steel and Sugar Hill (1976–1983)

McDonald, LeBlanc, and Wimbish first played together in Wood, Brass & Steel, a New York funk outfit that recorded for Turbo Records. McDonald played lead guitar and produced on their 1976 album. The trio then became the in-house rhythm section at Sugar Hill Records — the label founded by Sylvia Robinson that effectively launched hip-hop on record — working as the Sugarhill Gang Band.

McDonald’s guitar is central to “The Message” (1982), one of the defining early hip-hop recordings. The house band trio recorded under a range of credit names; McDonald appears on Sugar Hill releases both as “Skip McDonald” and as “Bernard Alexander” (writer/composer credits), with the latter ID (565130) carrying most of the songwriting attributions.

Meeting Sherwood: On-U Sound and the Maffia (1982–)

Adrian Sherwood encountered the trio through the Sugar Hill / early-hip-hop New York scene and brought them into the On-U Sound orbit. McDonald’s Discogs profile references him working closely with Sherwood beginning in the mid-1980s. His earliest On-U Sound credits are from 1985–1986.

Tackhead (1985–present)

Tackhead — LeBlanc (drums), Wimbish (bass), McDonald (guitar), Sherwood (production/mixing), with vocalist Bernard Fowler — is McDonald’s longest-running band credit. The group also recorded as Fats Comet and Maffia. McDonald is credited as guitarist and co-writer across the full Tackhead catalog, and appears on associated Gary Clail releases.

Solo Career as Little Axe (1993–2024)

McDonald’s solo project, Little Axe (Discogs ID: 14564), blends acoustic Delta blues guitar with dub bass, drum programming, sampling, and electronics. Sherwood has been a frequent production collaborator. Most albums were recorded in London and feature McDonald on virtually all instruments: guitar, bass, keyboards, vocals, programming.

Sugar Hill Credits Summary

  • Sugarhill Gang (debut album, 1980) — guitar
  • Grandmaster Flash & The Furious Five — The Message (1982) — guitar
  • The Sequence — 1982 LP — guitar
  • West Street Mob — 1981 (as Bernard Alexander) — musician
