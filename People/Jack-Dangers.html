Jack Dangers

Discogs ID: 2538 Real name: John Stephen Corrigan (born 11 January 1965, Swindon, England) Real name note: Confirmed by Brainwashed biography (brainwashed.com) and Wikipedia (Jack Dangers, as of 2026-06-22). The earlier stub value “John Garside” is incorrect — no primary source supports it, and the name produces no music-relevant results. “John Garside” appears to have been a data-entry error. Discogs (ID 2538) lists “John Stephen Corrigan”, which is correct. Active: ~1987–present Origin: UK Aliases: Meat Beat Manifesto (primary project)

UK electronic musician, producer, and remixer. Best known as Meat Beat Manifesto, a long-running industrial/electronic project. Contributed a remix to Merzbow’s Merzbeat (Important Records, 2002) — the sole outside remix on that album. Later collaborated with Merzbow on the joint album Extinct (Cold Spring, CSR330CD/LP, released 26 April 2024), composed and recorded November 2023 – January 2024.

Bella Union / Series 7

Released Variaciones Espectrales (BELLA705, 2002) as Part 5 of Bella Union’s Series 7 concept series — seven instrumental albums, seven tracks each, recorded in seven days. Recorded at Tape Lab, California, October 2001. The album is Dangers’ first proper solo credit outside the Meat Beat Manifesto name.

Seven tracks: “Nano”, “Every Technique”, “No Secrets No Surprises”, “Zxero”, “Short Heavies”, “3:00 min”, “Echo in Space”. Lynn Farmer plays drums on tracks 2, 3, 4. Also issued on Instinct Records (INS7005) in the US. [Sources: brainwashed.com; Wikipedia: Variaciones Espectrales]

Merzbow Collaboration

Dangers initiated contact with Merzbow himself — distinguishing this from his usual pattern of being approached by others. In a SLUG Magazine interview he stated: “Well, of those artists, Merzbow is the only one I actually got in touch with. I usually get a phone call or e-mail and we hook up that way. I don’t put myself out there that much.” [source: slugmag.com]

The earliest documented collaborative product is the Dangers remix of “Amlux” on Merzbow’s Merzbeat (Important Records, 2002) — the sole outside remix on that album, hidden as a bonus track after 65 tracks of silence. By the Quietus interview (undated but post-2005), Dangers mentions that Merzbow attended an MBM Tokyo show in 2005: “He came to our Tokyo show in 2005 and he liked it; that made my day!” [source: thequietus.com]

Two decades after Merzbeat, the two released the full collaborative album Extinct (Cold Spring, CSR330CD/LP, 26 April 2024), composed and recorded November 2023 – January 2024. Dangers on the experience: “We may not speak the same language, but in the vortex of sound, there’s a raw, primal understanding that transcends words.” [source: coldspring.co.uk]

No source details how the Merzbeat remix commission was arranged beyond Dangers having initiated contact with Merzbow, nor how the Extinct project was specifically proposed or brokered; Cold Spring Records is the label on Extinct but no source confirms whether Cold Spring introduced them or they approached Cold Spring with the completed work.
