Discogs ID: 932374 Real name: Jonathan Robert James Nash Also known as: JN, Jon Nash, Jonathan R.J. Nash Origin: Leeds, England

Leeds multi-instrumentalist — guitar, drums, and vocals. Active across a wide network of Leeds bands since the early 2000s. Best known as a member of Hookworms (from The Hum, 2014) and Holodrum (the post-Hookworms collective formed in 2022). No solo releases; all output is through collaborative projects.

Hookworms

Hookworms were a Leeds indie/psychedelic rock band. Nash joined (or was first formally credited) on The Hum (2014, Weird World) — credited as “JN” in the collective roster alongside MJ (Matthew Johnson (12)), MB (Matthew Benn), JW (J.W. (8)), and SS (Sam Shipstone). He continued through the final album Microshift (2018, Domino, 9 pressings — the group’s most-pressed release). On Microshift he is credited for drums, percussion, and drum programming on the remixes.

The band dissolved on 31 October 2018 following public allegations of sexual and mental abuse against frontman Matthew Johnson (12).

Holodrum

Holodrum formed after Hookworms disbanded — notably without Matthew Johnson (12). The seven-piece collective includes Nash alongside Matthew Benn (also recorded and mixed the record), J.W. (8), Sam Shipstone, Christopher Duffin, Emily Garner, and Steve Nuttall. Their self-titled debut (2022, Gringo Records) was engineered by Ross Halden and mastered by Robert Weston.

Cowtown

Cowtown is a Leeds trio with Nash, Hilary Knott, and David Shields (6). Active from at least 2005 (a split 7” on The Bloodloss Collective) through 2024 (Fear Of.., Gringo Records). Also released on Too Pure (Castleman, 2016) and Happy Happy Birthday To Me Records (Paranormal Romance, 2016).
