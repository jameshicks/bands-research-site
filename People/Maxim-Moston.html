Violinist, string arranger, songwriter and producer. Born in Moscow, emigrated to America in 1979. Lives and works in New York. Maxim Moston plays with numerous bands, both classical and pop. He has done orchestral arrangements of the music of Pink Floyd and writes music as part of electronic group The Government. He is one of the founding members of an electro-acoustic group Slow Six. Maxim Moston has also collaborated and written music for Joan As Policewoman, Lou Reed and Rufus and Martha Wainwright. Production début for Baby Dee’s “Book Of Songs”.

Overview

Moscow-born violinist, string arranger, songwriter and producer who emigrated to the United States in 1979 and has worked out of New York ever since. His career, stretching from the late 1990s and encompassing nearly 340 Discogs credits, spans both classical and pop contexts: he is a member of Antony And The Johnsons, Edison Woods, Joe Gallant & Illuminati, The Harmonix Ensemble, and The Unremembered Orchestra, and co-founded the electro-acoustic group Slow Six and the electronic outfit The Government. His most sustained collaborations are with fellow string players Anja Wood, Joan Wasser, and Antoine Silverman, placing him at the centre of New York’s chamber-pop and art-rock circles. Beyond ensemble work he has arranged strings for Lou Reed, Rufus and Martha Wainwright, and Joan As Policewoman, and produced Baby Dee’s “Book Of Songs”; he has also arranged Pink Floyd’s music for orchestra.
