Sam Britton

Electronic musician, composer, producer, engineer, and mastering engineer. UK-based. Discogs artist ID: 499915. Also records and performs as Isambard Khroustaliov (Discogs ID: 226637). Cousin of Ollie Bown.

Name variations on Discogs: Britton, S Britton, S.Britton.

Biography

Sam Britton trained as an architect at the Architectural Association in London but moved into music full-time after securing a recording contract as an undergraduate. He and his cousin Ollie Bown form the electronic duo Icarus (2), whose first release appeared in 1997 on Recordings Of Substance. The project ran through the early-to-mid 2000s on Output Recordings, Leaf, and the Danish label Rump Recordings, and returned in 2011 (the split EP Flensburg with Badun) and 2024 (the LP An Ever-Growing Meridional Entertainment Transgression At The Edge Of The Multiverse on Not Applicable).

Under the solo alias Isambard Khroustaliov he released Five Loose Plans (2006, as a duo with Maurizio Ravalico) and performed with The Human Arm (2012, improvisation). In 2006 he completed a masters in electronic music and composition at IRCAM in Paris. He has also been selected to work with the London Sinfonietta (Writing the Future commissioning scheme, 2011), is an associate of The New Radiophonic Workshop, and was researching a PhD in composition at Brunel University under Richard Barrett, Peter Wiegold, and Christopher Fox (status as of profile writing).

Beyond Icarus (2), Britton is a member of three further groups:

  • Fiium Shaarrk — free-jazz / electronics trio with Maurizio Ravalico and Rudi Fischerlehner; released No Fiction Now! (2012, Not Applicable).
  • Elegiac (2) — trio with Ted Milton (Blurt) and Graham Lewis (Wire); released Elegiac (2021, Upp Records (2)). Britton co-arranged and mixed.
  • Leverton Fox — group with Alex Bonney and Tim Giles (alias Isambard Khroustaliov credited as member).

His production and engineering work for Blurt represents the longest-running outside collaboration in his credits. He produced Cut It! (2008, The Orchestra Pit Recording Co.; reissued 2010, LTM) — the first Blurt studio album in many years — and subsequently handled the 2024 reissue of My Mother Was A Friend Of An Enemy Of The People (mastered, mixed, restored, liner notes) and the 2025 studio album The Mecanno Giraffe (recorded, mastered, artwork/graphic design), both on All City Records (3).

He also served as mixer on Tujiko Noriko’s Kuro (2019, Pan (3)) alongside Will Worsley, and appears as an electronics performer on the The Odes (2) live document Live (2013, New Ideals/Not Applicable) with Ted Milton.
