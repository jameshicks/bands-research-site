Brooks Harlan

Austin/DC-scene musician. Discogs artist ID: 1287234.

Overview

Brooks Harlan is a member of several bands spanning the DC hardcore/post-hardcore and Austin indie worlds. His most prominent credit is as a member of Jawbox (Discogs ID: 252314). He is also a member of Grizzly Adams Family alongside Munaf Rayani of Explosions In The Sky.
