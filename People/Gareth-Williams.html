Gareth Williams

Born: Gareth John Williams, 23 April 1953, Cardiff, Wales, UK. Died: 24 December 2001. Discogs artist ID: 196210.

Multi-instrumentalist (keyboards, guitar, bass, voice, tape, organ, electronics, drums) and co-founder of This Heat. After departing This Heat in late 1981 — before the band’s final 1982 European tour — Williams released the cassette album Flaming Tunes (1985) as Flaming Tunes with lifelong friend Mary Currie, and continued to work as a DJ. He also studied Kathakali dance drama in Kerala, India; completed a degree in Indian religious studies and music at SOAS; contributed to the first Rough Guide to India; and worked as a travel guide. He died in 2001.

Name variations used on releases: G.W., Williams.

This Heat (1976–1981)

Williams co-founded This Heat with Charles Bullen (guitar, clarinet, viola, voice, tape) and Charles Hayward (percussion, keyboards, voice, tape) in 1976. The trio set up their own studio — a rented meat-locker in Brixton nicknamed Cold Storage — and built a practice of continuous, self-funded recording that became central to their sound: dense tape layering, loop composition, multi-track free improvisation, and overtly political lyrical content.

Both studio albums were produced by David Cunningham and recorded at Cold Storage.

Departure (1981)

Williams left This Heat in late 1981. Trefor Goronwy joined as touring bassist for the final 1982 European tour, after which the band dissolved. The Discogs Live cassette (1986, Germany, Independance (2)) was recorded with the original three-member lineup; Live 80/81 (2006) also documents the Williams-era band.

Flaming Tunes (1985)

After leaving This Heat, Williams and Mary Currie recorded and released Flaming Tunes as Flaming Tunes — a cassette-only album on Williams’s own Contagious Tapes label (1985). Williams wrote or co-wrote all material; Currie co-wrote tracks A1, A2, A6, B1, B3, and B5. Williams engineered tracks A1–B1 and B3–B5; Charles Bullen engineered and performed on B2 (“B To A”). The album was reissued on CD by Life And Living Records in 2009.

Contagious Tapes (Discogs ID: 232207) was a small imprint with just two releases: the Flaming Tunes album and a 1982 cassette by Missing Persons (3) (M.P. — A Representative).

Other Credits

Nid Yw’n Gyfrinach (1993)

Williams played bass on Nid Yw’n Gyfrinach (1993, Sain, UK) — a Welsh-language release by Timothy Evans (4). Other personnel: Annette Bryn Parri (accompanied by), Charli Britton (drums), Emyr Rees (engineer), Hefin Elis (keyboards, guitar, producer). Discogs ID: 11052676.

Repeat (1993)

Credited for Tape [Loops] on Repeat (1993, These Records) — a This Heat archival release of Cold Storage loop material. Discogs ID: 438817.

The Cult Files: Re-Opened (1997)

Williams is credited Mastered By on this US compilation (Silva America). Discogs ID: 6218505.

My Kind Of Christmas (2020)

Post-mortem credit: Williams credited as Co-producer, Mixed By, Mastered By on a Greek pop release (My Kind Of Christmas, 2020, Not On Label, by Aliki). Discogs ID: 16637274. The date (2020) suggests this is either a reissue of a pre-2001 recording or a data anomaly.
