Jason Farrell

Discogs ID: 515974. Guitarist, vocalist, graphic designer, short film maker. Washington DC. Active from the mid-1980s through the present.

Farrell is a dual-practice figure in the DC hardcore and post-hardcore world: a frontman/guitarist across a string of bands spanning 1987–2018, and simultaneously the dominant graphic designer of Dischord Records’ mid-to-late catalog. His design work — hundreds of credits across Dischord and elsewhere — is as central to his output as his music.

Background

Farrell came up in the DC hardcore scene in the mid-1980s. By 1987 he was playing guitar and handling artwork for Swiz, a straight-edge hardcore band on Hellfire and Sammich Records. That parallel role — musician and visual designer on the same release — became his signature across every subsequent project.

He runs Hellfire Records (hellfirerecords.net), a small label associated with his solo and band output.

Bands

Swiz (1987–1993)

Farrell played guitar in Swiz, a DC hardcore outfit. Lineup: Shawn Brown (4) (vocals), Nathan Larson (bass, later replaced by Dave Stern), Alex Daniels (drums), Farrell (guitar). Ramsey Metcalf also appeared.

Produced by Eli Janney (also of Girls Against Boys). Farrell handled all artwork across the discography.

Lineup: Farrell (vocals, guitar), Dave Stern (guitar), Jake Kump (bass), Dave Bryson (drums). Later additions: Brian Clancy (guitar), Zac Eller, Joe Gorelick, Areif Sless-Kitain.

Graphic Design

Farrell is one of the defining visual voices of Dischord Records across the 1990s–2020s. His credit “Cover Mechanic” appears on Fugazi’s In On The Kill Taker (1993), Red Medicine (1995), End Hits (1998), Instrument Soundtrack (1999), and Ian MacKaye/Amy Farina’s The Evens (2005), among many others. He handled layout and/or graphics for a substantial portion of the Dischord back-catalog as it was reissued through the 2000s and 2010s.
