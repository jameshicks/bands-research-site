Ian Williams

Guitarist, keyboardist. Full name: Ian T. Williams. Pittsburgh PA / New York. Discogs artist ID: 187429. Active 1992–present.

Overview

Williams is primarily known as a guitarist in two influential post-hardcore/math-rock acts: Don Caballero (1992–2001) and Battles (2002–present). His technique is built around finger-tapping used rhythmically rather than melodically, with heavy use of delay pedals — no conventional lead solos. In Don Caballero he was one of two guitarists in a four-piece; in Battles he expanded into keyboards and the band’s studio-production role. He also ran the Pittsburgh side project Storm And Stress concurrently with Don Caballero in the late 1990s, and was a member of Sludgehammer alongside Pittsburgh indie figures Karl Hendricks and Brian Welcker.

Don Caballero Era (1992–2001)

Williams joined Don Caballero at the band’s founding, appearing on all four studio albums released through Touch And Go Records. The debut For Respect (1993) established the band’s interlocking, drumbeat-led guitar style. The lineup through the first two albums was Williams and Mike Banfield on guitars, Damon Che on drums, and Pat Morris on bass (replaced by Matt Jencik on Don Caballero 2, then by Eric Emm on American Don). Producer Al Sutton worked on Don Caballero 2 and What Burns Never Returns.

Williams left (or the band dissolved around him) in 2001; reported interpersonal tension with Damon Che is cited as a factor. Che subsequently revived the Don Caballero name with different personnel.

Storm And Stress (1997)

Concurrent Pittsburgh side project. The self-titled album (1997, Touch And Go, Discogs 765915) featured Williams (guitar, vocals), Eric Emm (bass, credited under the alias “M. Eric Topolsky”), Kevin Shea (drums), and Micah Gaugh (vocals, piano — track C1 only). The project overlapped the Don Caballero years; Emm also appeared in Don Caballero’s final lineup on American Don.

Sludgehammer

Pittsburgh-area project. Discogs artist ID: 1540299. Members: Ian Williams (drums), Karl Hendricks (guitar, Discogs 506891), Brian Welcker (bass, Discogs 1540301). One release in Discogs: Big Water 7” EP (1990, Cubist Productions, Cat: Cube 026, Discogs release ID: 1889599). Tracks: “Big Water,” “Step On Me,” “Love Cake,” “Close To You.” Produced by Matt Marcus; sleeve/label design by Wayno. Notably, Williams played drums here — predating his guitar role in every subsequent band. See Sludgehammer for full details.

Battles Era (2002–present)

After Don Caballero dissolved, Williams co-founded Battles with Dave Konopka, John Stanier (ex-Helmet (2)), and Tyondai Braxton. The band signed to Warp Records and released the landmark debut Mirrored (2007).

Tyondai Braxton departed before the recording of Gloss Drop (2011), leaving Battles as a trio: Williams, Konopka, and Stanier. The trio format has continued through La Di Da Di (2015) and Juice B Crypts (2019). On the latter two albums Williams is explicitly credited for guitar and keyboards; on Juice B Crypts both credits appear as “Uncredited” in the liner notes despite standard sleeve omission.
