Overview

Joey Santiago (born June 11, 1965 in Manila, Philippines) is a Filipino-American guitarist and composer who built his career almost entirely as the lead guitarist of the Pixies, a band he has shared credits with across 873 releases dating back to 1987. His catalogue flows through 4AD, Elektra, and Rough Trade as the dominant labels, with Cooking Vinyl and Demon Records carrying later-era Pixies output into the 2020s. Outside the Pixies, Santiago has performed with The Martinis — a project involving his former wife Linda Mallari — and The Everybody. His most frequent studio collaborators beyond bandmates Dave Lovering, Black Francis, and Kim Deal are producers Gil Norton and Paul Q. Kolderie and engineer Steven Haigler.
