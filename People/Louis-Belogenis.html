Louis Belogenis

Discogs ID: 273483
Name variations: Louie Belogenis, Belogenis

American jazz saxophonist (tenor, soprano) based in New York City. A core figure in the downtown NYC improvised music scene since the early 1990s, Belogenis operates at the intersection of free jazz, no-wave, and post-Coltrane spiritualism. He has maintained a long creative partnership with drummer Rashied Ali and leads or co-leads multiple ongoing ensembles. His output spans the punk-affiliated queerpunk collective God Is My Co-Pilot through to Tzadik releases with John Zorn’s extended circle.

Overview

Belogenis came up in the early-1990s downtown New York scene, contributing tenor saxophone to the large rotating ensemble of God Is My Co-Pilot (the queer/punk/noise outfit led by Craig Flanagin and Sharon Topper) across three major releases between 1993 and 1995. Simultaneously he was building a parallel identity in avant-jazz, joining the Rashied Ali–anchored outfit Prima Materia and producing their 1994 Knitting Factory Works release Peace On Earth (Music Of John Coltrane). His partnership with Ali deepened into a duo/trio setting, producing Rings of Saturn (1999, Knitting Factory Records) and Live at Tonic (2001, DIW) with bassist Wilber Morris.

He also led his own projects: the Louie Belogenis Trio (with Sunny Murray and Michael Bisio), which released Tiresias on Porter Records (2011), and Flow Trio (with bassist Joe Morris and drummer Charles Downs), which has released on ESP-Disk’ and Ayler Records. Later work gravitated toward Tzadik, where the supergroup Blue Buddha (with Dave Douglas, Bill Laswell, and Tyshawn Sorey) appeared in 2015, and Terton (with Trevor Dunn and Ryan Sawyer) released Outer, Inner, Secret in 2023.

The 1978 credit for liner notes on School (Parachute label) is anomalous and may reflect early editorial/writing work rather than musical performance.
