Simon Edwards

Bass player and multi-instrumentalist. Active primarily as a session musician and band member from the early 1980s through the 2020s. Discogs artist ID: 270607. Name variations: S Edwards, S. Edwards, Simon, Simon Edward, Simon J. Edwards.

Overview

Simon Edwards is one of the more quietly prolific session bassists working out of the UK from the 1980s onward. His career spans an unusually wide range: British indie-folk (Fairground Attraction), post-punk art rock (Talk Talk, Shriekback), French chanson (Alain Bashung), protest folk (Billy Bragg), Portishead-adjacent solo material (Beth Gibbons), Celtic-influenced folk (Eddi Reader, Loreena McKennitt), and world music sessions. He is distinctive for his fluency on non-standard bass instruments — particularly the guitarrón (Mexican acoustic bass), sintir, marimbula, and double bass — alongside conventional electric bass.

He was a founding member of Fairground Attraction and appeared on their debut album The First of a Million Kisses (1988), which went to #1 in the UK. He was also an early member of Shriekback and later became a core member of Billy Bragg & The Blokes.

Biography

Early Career — Red Box and Shriekback (1981–1986)

Edwards first appears in Discogs credits on a 1981 single “Only You” (May Ann Records), playing bass. By 1983 he was playing bass guitar and backing vocals on Foot In The Door (Paladin Records, 1983). He is listed as a member of Shriekback, the art-funk group that also included Barry Andrews and Dave Allen; his credited Shriekback appearance is on The Circle & The Square (WEA, 1986). He also appeared on T·R·O·U·B·L·E (Upside Records, 1986) and the Stiff Records release You Caught Me Out (1984).

Around this time he played with Red Box, contributing to their top-ten singles “Lean on Me” and “America” (neither individually catalogued in this session).

Fairground Attraction (1987–1990)

In 1987, Edwards joined singer Eddi Reader, guitarist Mark E Nevin, and drummer Roy Dodds to form Fairground Attraction. The group’s debut album The First of a Million Kisses (RCA, 1988) was a major UK success; it topped the charts and produced the #1 single “Perfect.” Edwards played bass (credited as “guitaron”) on the album. The band also included vibes player Roger Beaujolais and accordionist/pianist Kim Burton on session.

The group’s live document Kawasaki Live In Japan (RCA, 2003) confirms his role in their touring lineup. Fairground Attraction’s 2024 release Beautiful Happening (Absolute / Sony Records Int’l) — and the subsequent Beautiful Happening Live In Japan (Sony Records Int’l, 2025) — show the band still active with Edwards as a primary member, now credited on guitarrón and mandolin.

Talk Talk Sessions (1988, 1991)

Edwards is credited on both of Talk Talk’s final two studio albums:

  • Spirit of Eden (EMI, 1988) — guitarrón (Mexican bass). A celebrated ambient/jazz-influenced record produced by Tim Friese-Greene, featuring an enormous cast of session players including Danny Thompson (double bass), Nigel Kennedy (violin), Mark Feltham (harmonica), and Robbie McIntosh (dobro, twelve-string guitar).
  • Laughing Stock (Polydor, 1991) — acoustic bass. Also featuring Danny Thompson, Henry Lowther, Martin Ditcham, and Lee Harris (drums). Both records were engineered by Phill Brown.

He also appears on the Talk Talk compilation Natural History 1982–1988 (Parlophone) playing Mexican bass.

Billy Bragg & The Blokes (late 1990s–ongoing)

Edwards is a listed member of Billy Bragg & The Blokes, the backing group that supported Billy Bragg from the late 1990s. The band’s core included Edwards (bass/marimbula/sintir), Martyn Barker (drums), Lu Edmonds (oud, guitar), Ben Mandelson (mandolin), and Ian McLagan (organ/piano).

Key releases in this configuration:

  • England, Half English (Cooking Vinyl, 2002) — bass
  • Mr Love & Justice (Cooking Vinyl, 2008) — double bass, electric bass, marimbula, handclaps; also engineer (“Butchers”)
  • The Roaring Forty | 1983–2023 (Cooking Vinyl, 2023) — retrospective featuring Blokes-era tracks; Edwards plays double bass, marimbula, handclaps, electric bass

The Bragg connection also generated session appearances: Six Songs From Pressure Drop (2010), Volume II (2006), and live/promo releases.

Alain Bashung (1998–2008)

Edwards became a recurrent presence on the records of French rock icon Alain Bashung, contributing to multiple studio and live albums:

  • Fantaisie Militaire (Barclay, 1998) — bass, percussion, harpsichord. Featuring Adrian Utley (guitar) and Ian Caple (producer/engineer).
  • Bleu Pétrole (Barclay, 2008) — bass, guitar (dobro), percussion
  • Caravane (Capitol/EMI, 2005), Vegetal (Barclay, 2006), La Marche De L’Empereur (Barclay, 2005), Scandale Mélancolique (Sony, 2005), The Flower Book (Barclay, 2006), Les Champs Arides (Naïve, 2007)
  • Live - Résistance À La Nuit (Capitol, 2006), Live - Une Nuit Au Châtelet (Capitol, 2007)
  • Mue (Barclay, 2014) — posthumous release

Also involved with other French recordings: La Réalité (Capitol, 2003), Aimez-vous Les Uns Les Autres Ou Bien Disparaissez… (Polydor, 2003), Tout Sera Comme Avant (Labels, 2004), Je Sais Que La Terre Est Plate (Delabel, 2008).

Loreena McKennitt (2008–2009)

Edwards appears across several releases by Canadian folk/Celtic artist Loreena McKennitt:

  • A Midwinter Night’s Dream (Quinlan Road, 2008) — acoustic bass, electric bass, marimbula, sintir (sentir)
  • A Mediterranean Odyssey (Quinlan Road, 2009) — bass
  • From Istanbul To Athens (Quinlan Road, 2009) — bass
