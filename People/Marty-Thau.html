Marty Thau

Marty Thau

Aug 08, 20266 min read

Marty Thau

Discogs ID: 286954
Born: December 7, 1938
Died: February 13, 2014, Petersburg, Virginia (renal failure; aged 75)
Role: Manager, record producer, label founder

Biography

Early Career (1963–1971)

New York music industry figure. Attended New York University (1956–1960, communications). Worked in the advertising department at Billboard magazine from 1963. Moved to Cameo-Parkway Records, then Buddah Records, where he is credited as administrator on the 1968 Ohio Express single “Chewy, Chewy” (UK Buddah, master 247276) and as Key Plays administrator on the Lemon Pipers’ “Green Tambourine” (Buddah Canada, 1968). Contributed concept to the Atlantic spoken-word record The Way To Become The Sensuous Woman By “J” (1971) and served as project co-ordinator on the Synergy LP Synergy (Decca, 1971).

New York Dolls Management (1972–1975)

Left an A&R position at Paramount Records in 1972 to manage the New York Dolls, where he served as executive producer on the band’s debut New York Dolls (Mercury, 1973; master 13185) and on The Facedancers (Paramount, 1973; master 575949). Remained with the Dolls through their breakup in 1975. Also produced the Bay City Rollers’ “Rock And Roll Love Letter” (Sire, 1975; master 841643) during this period. Thau appears in a minor credit on the Blondie self-titled debut (Chrysalis, 1977) as “Cocktail Music” performer — likely reflecting a personal/cameo connection to the New York scene rather than a production role.

Red Star Records (1977–c. 1986)

Co-founded Red Star Records in 1977. The label’s first release was Suicide’s self-titled debut (RS 1, 1977), co-produced by Thau and Craig Leon; engineer was Larry Alexander. Craig Leon departed after four days of tracking; Thau completed remixes and collaborated with Leon on the final “Frankie Teardrop” mix. He also organized Suicide’s first European tour and later published a memoir excerpt on the experience in Sensitive Skin magazine (December 2012).

The second Red Star release was The Real Kids’ self-titled debut (RS 2, 1977), co-produced by Thau and the band with Larry Alexander engineering. In 1980, Thau issued Marty Thau Presents 2x5 (RED 100), a compilation featuring two tracks each from five New York acts: The Fleshtones, Revelons, Bloodless Pharaohs (Brian Setzer’s pre-Stray Cats band), Comateens, and Student Teachers. Cover design was by Peter Zaremba (Fleshtones); the album was produced by Jimmy Destri (Blondie). Red Star also released two Walter Steding LPs (RSS-2, 1979; RED 101, 1980), both produced by Chris Stein (Blondie).

In 1982, Thau served as executive producer on Richard Hell & The Voidoids’ Destiny Street (RED 801), produced by Alan Betrock with Robert Quine and Naux on guitars and Fred Maher on drums.

Also in 1982, Thau produced the New York Dolls live document Lipstick Killers (ROIR, A102) and the Fleshtones’ Blast Off! (ROIR, A107). Both ROIR releases were engineered by Wayne Vlcan.

A 1985 Red Star release, The X-Citerz (catno: none), was also issued. Martin Rev’s solo album Clouds Of Glory (1985/1996, New Rose / Mau Mau) was co-produced by Thau and Rev; a Red Star reissue appeared on Mau Mau Records (MAUCD 648, 1996).

In 1996, Red Star released a Fleshtones EP and the compilation Songs Of The Naked City (RS 7004-2). In 1998, Red Star issued the New York Dolls live document Live In Concert (Paris 1974) (RS-7006) with Thau as executive producer.

Later Career (1982–2014)

Continued working as producer and business administrator into the 1990s. Served in a business administration capacity for several Restless Records releases (Material’s One Down, 1992; Massacre’s Killing Time, 1992; Last Poets reissue, 1992; Tackhead’s World Destruction, 1992; Sonny Sharrock’s Ask the Ages, 1992). Managed re-issue creative consulting for various catalog projects. Produced “I Wanna Be Your Boyfriend” (Norton Records, 1997; master 408721) and “Better Be Good” (Norton Records, 1999), both for Real Kids-related projects. Produced a reissue of the Comateens’ Endless Party (Get Hip, 2000). Executive produced the NY Dolls Attempted: Live at Max’s Kansas City 1980 (Sympathy For The Record Industry, 2004). In 2022, Thau’s original production credit carried forward to the Suicide Surrender reissue (BMG/Mute; master 2585498).
