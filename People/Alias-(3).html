Alias (3)

Discogs ID: 48536
Real name: Brendon Whitney
Aliases: Brendon Whitney, Mysterious (2)
Name variations: Alias, Allias
Born: April 5, 1976, Portland, Maine
Died: March 30, 2018 (heart attack, age 41)

Producer, beatmaker, and multi-instrumentalist. One of the core artists on Anticon from 1999 onward, with 44 releases on the label across two decades. Primarily an instrumental producer working in abstract hip-hop and experimental electronic music; also credited with guitar, keyboards, drum programming, sax, and photography on various releases. Met Sole in 1993, performed with The Live Poets (Sole’s group) around New England from 1996. Taught himself MPC 3000 and ADAT. Born to a jazz drummer father and a church organist mother; discovered hip-hop at 13 via Yo! MTV Raps. Member of Deep Puddle Dynamics.

Biography

Grew up in Hollis, Maine — a small suburb outside Portland. Isolated from any local hip-hop scene, he encountered Yo! MTV Raps at 13 and bought The Source monthly at the one shop in Maine that carried it. By 14 he was writing raps; his parents gave him a drum machine for Christmas. He moved from his bedroom to a friend’s basement studio, taught himself MPC 3000 and ADAT, and by 1993 had connected with Sole, then recording and performing. From 1996, he performed with Sole’s group The Live Poets around New England. He became an early Anticon signee and transitioned from rapper to full producer/beatmaker.

He died of a heart attack on March 30, 2018, aged 41. His final album Less Is Orchestra (with Dose One) was released that same year.

Key Release Details

The Other Side Of The Looking Glass (2002, abr0022)

  • 2xLP, 13 tracks, entirely self-produced except “Inspirations Passing” (prod. DJ Mayonnaise; Ehren Whitney on saxophone)
  • “Opus Ashamed” features Dose One
  • Mastered by Jonathan Wyman; layout by The Anticon Art Director (Odd Nosdam)
  • Artwork by Thesis Sahib

Muted (2003, abr0036)

  • Fully self-produced instrumental album
  • “Unseen Sights” features Markus Acher (vocals, guitar) and Dax Pierson (melodica); lyrics by Acher and Valerie Trebeljahr
  • “The Physical Voice” features The Pedestrian (rap)
  • Art direction: Roger Bacon (2); mastered Jeremy Goody; exec. prod. Baillie Parker

Lillian (2005, abr0054)

  • Credited as “Alias & Ehren” — collaborative album with Ehren (Ehren Whitney; alto/soprano sax, flute, clarinet, keys, drum programming)
  • Alias plays guitar, keyboards, drum programming alongside production duties
  • Mastered Jeremy Goody; exec. prod. Baillie Parker

Brookland / Oaklyn (2006, abr0059)

  • Credited as Alias & Tarsier (Alias & Tarsier, Discogs ID 462858)
  • Tarsier (ID 542019): vocalist, pianist, organist, lyricist — real name Rona Rapadas; Brooklyn-based, one half of cinematronic duo Healamonster & Tarsier
  • Guests: Dose One (“Luck And Fear”), Dax Pierson (organ, “Picking The Same Lock”), Telephone Jim Jesus (guitar, “Dr C.”), Matt McCullough (guitar)
  • Mastered George Horn
  • Album title: “Brookland” encodes Brooklyn (Tarsier’s base); “Oaklyn” encodes Oakland (Alias’s base — he spent ~8 years in Oakland before returning to Portland, ME in August 2007). The portmanteau title thus maps the geographic separation of the collaboration. The two worked entirely by email for 21 months, exchanging music without meeting in person until shortly before the album’s release. aliasofficial.com; corroborated by multiple reviews and press summaries — no single artist interview located that states this explicitly, but the Oakland/Brooklyn mapping is consistently reported across sources (e.g. Amazon product page, Wikipedia article as of 2026-07-12)

Resurgam (2008, ABR0087)

  • First album Alias both produced and mastered himself
  • “Well Water Black” features Yoni Wolf (vocals, Rhodes, bass)
  • “Weathering” features Hrishikesh Hirway (The One AM Radio; vocals, guitar, bells, trumpet)
