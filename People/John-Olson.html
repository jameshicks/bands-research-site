John Olson

Discogs ID: 439840

American noise musician, member of Wolf Eyes (electronics, tape, horns). One of the most prolific alias-users in the US noise underground — operates under over 150 aliases including Spykes, John R. Spykes, and many others. 5 shared releases with Aaron Dilloway. Also has solo releases on Hanson Records.
