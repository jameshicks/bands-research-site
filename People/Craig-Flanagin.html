Craig Flanagin

Discogs ID: 273482
Guitarist, multi-instrumentalist, producer; co-founder and core member of God Is My Co-Pilot (GodCo). Also a member of The Hattifatteners.

Overview

Craig Flanagin is the primary instrumentalist of God Is My Co-Pilot, playing guitar, bass, drums, keyboards, and producing virtually all of their output from 1991 onward. He co-founded the band with vocalist Sharon Topper in New York City around 1990–1991, and the two ran the band’s own imprint The Making Of Americans throughout their most active period.

Flanagin’s role in GodCo was consistently that of the studio engine: he produced the records, tracked most of the instrumental parts, and arranged the revolving-door cast of up to 30-odd collaborators that populated the band’s recordings. His production approach — dense, lo-fi, confrontational — remained consistent across more than a dozen releases spanning hardcore-inflected punk, free jazz, Klezmer, Finnish song, Irish trad, and queer pop.

Outside GodCo he appeared as guitarist on John Zorn’s Cobra: Live At The Knitting Factory (Knitting Factory Works, 1995), a landmark live document of Zorn’s game-piece compositional system recorded at the Knitting Factory. He also played keyboards on the debut release by The Hattifatteners and contributed guitar to Jad Fair’s Half Monster (Misc. Music, 2008).

Known name variations on releases: C Flanagin, C. Flanagin, CF, Craig, Craig F, Craig F., Craig Flanigan, Flanagin.

Key Release Credits in Detail

Straight Not (1993, Outpunk Records)

The most explicitly queercore-identified GodCo release. Craig Flanagin: guitar, bass, drums, producer. Don Fury (“Don Fury Der Hexer”): recorded by. Full band: Alex Klein (bass), Elliott Sharp (bass), Fredrick Lonberg-Holm (cello), Andy Haas (didgeridoo, alto saxophone), Christine Bard (drums), David Simons (drums), Michael Evans (2) (drums), Sharon Topper (drums), Siobhan Duffy (drums, vocals), Anthony Coleman (sampler, keyboards), Louis Belogenis (tenor saxophone), Mia Keinanen (vocals), Tanja Lahtinen (vocals), Juli Brown (bagpipes).

How To Be (1994, The Making Of Americans)

23-track CD. Flanagin: guitar, bass, drums, vocals, samples. Full cast includes Jad Fair (guitar, vocals), Elliott Sharp (bass), James Garrison (bass), Jenny Wade (bass), Fredrick Lonberg-Holm (cello), Chris Pellettieri (drums), David Simons (drums), Michael Evans (2) (drums), Siobhan Duffy (drums, vocals, saxophone), Anthony Coleman (piano), Louis Belogenis (saxophone), Andy Haas (saxophone, didgeridoo), Daryl Kahan (vocals), Fly (9) (vocals), Mia Keinanen (vocals), Tanja Lahtinen (vocals), Sharon Topper (vocals, drums).

John Zorn’s Cobra: Live At The Knitting Factory (1995, Knitting Factory Works)

Flanagin appears on track 9, playing guitar alongside John Zorn (saxophone), Sharon Topper (voice), John King (6) (guitar), Michael Evans (2) (drums), Anthony Coleman (sampler), David Shea (sampler), Mark De Gli Antoni (sampler), Roy Nathanson (saxophone), and Jim Pugliese (percussion). The full release features a large rotating ensemble across 14 tracks; producers were Anthony Coleman and Norman Yamada.

The Peel Sessions (1996, Strange Fruit)

Three BBC sessions. Flanagin: guitar on all sessions, Hammond organ (sessions 1 and 3), vocals and “Performer [Clown Toy]” (session 2), Written-By on most tracks. Full credits show three configurations:

  • Session 1 (BBC producer Mike Robinson): Alex Klein (bass), Siobhan Duffy (drums), Margaret Fiedler (guitar, vocals, sampler), Michael Evans (2) (drums/percussion), Fly (9) (jew’s harp)
  • Session 2 (BBC producer Miti Adhikari): Ann Rupel (bass, vocals, Hammond organ), Christine Bard (drums), Michael Evans (2) (drums), Marion Coutts (trumpet, vocals), Sharon Topper (melodica, Hammond, clarinet, vocals, synthesizer [Wee Faux Mini-moog])
  • Session 3 (BBC producer Adam Askew): Fly (9) (bass, vocals), Dan Brown (10) (drums), Sharon Topper (vocals, clarinet)

Vogue Bambini — The Hattifatteners (1993, Dark Beloved Cloud)

Flanagin: keyboards. Full band: Sharon Topper (drums, vocals), James Kavoussi (guitar), Tanya Small (guitar), Alexa Firat (guitar, bass), Tanja Lahtinen (vocals).

Half Monster — Jad And Nao (2008, Misc. Music)

Flanagin contributes guitar on track 7 alongside Sharon Topper (vocals, track 7) and Gilles Rieder (drums on multiple tracks). The release is credited to Jad And Nao, a project of Jad Fair and Naofumi Ishimaru.

Non-GodCo Collaborations

  • John Zorn’s Cobra: Live At The Knitting Factory (1995) — Guitar, track 9. This was a live performance of Zorn’s long-running game piece at the Knitting Factory, NYC. Flanagin participated alongside a large downtown NYC ensemble including Sharon Topper, Anthony Coleman, David Shea, Fredrick Lonberg-Holm, Marc Ribot, Zeena Parkins, and many others.
  • The Hattifatteners — Keyboards on Vogue Bambini (1993, Dark Beloved Cloud). The band was a side project of the GodCo core with additional members Alexa Firat, James Kavoussi, Tanya Small, and Tanja Lahtinen. Three releases total: Vogue Bambini (1993), Constantly On The Move With (1993, Funky Mushroom Records), Rabbit Rabbit (1999, Dark Beloved Cloud).
  • Fantasy Band (A Shrimper Compilation) (1994, Shrimper) — Guitar, cello.
  • Half Monster by Jad And Nao (2008, Misc. Music) — Guitar on track 7; alongside Sharon Topper on vocals.
  • Gunfighter Ballads And Songs Of The Old West by Jad Fair (1993, The Making Of Americans) — Guitar, percussion; written-by. Released on the GodCo label with Flanagin as credited collaborator.
  • Yo! Baby, ‘Sup (1992, Drunken Shaolin Records) — Credited as “Performer [Game Show Announcer].”
