Sly Dunbar

Discogs ID: 30419 (also listed as “Lowell Dunbar,” ID 508282)
Drummer, producer. Born Lowell Charles Dunbar, 10 May 1952, Kingston, Jamaica. Half of the Sly & Robbie production duo (with bassist Robbie Shakespeare).

Overview

One of the most recorded drummers in reggae and dub history. With Robbie Shakespeare, formed a production partnership that dominated Jamaican music from the late 1970s and crossed into post-punk and avant-garde through Compass Point Studios, Nassau.

Compass Point All Stars

Core member of the Compass Point All Stars — the Nassau session collective. Worked with Grace Jones, Robert Palmer, Joe Cocker, and many others through the Compass Point facility.

Key Credits

  • Lizzy Mercier Descloux — Mambo Nassau (Philips/ZE, 1981): drums; associated with the Compass Point sessions alongside Wally Badarou and Steven Stanley
  • Grace Jones — Nightclubbing (Island, 1981) and others
  • The Slits — worked with them
