Overview

Cate Le Bon (real name Cate Timothy, born 4 March 1983 in Penboyr, Carmarthenshire) is a Welsh singer-songwriter and producer whose catalogue spans indie rock, art rock, folk, and avantgarde tendencies. Her output peaked in the 2010s across a string of labels including The Control Group, Heavenly, Drag City, and later Mexican Summer and 4AD. She has built a parallel career as a producer for other artists, with Samur Khouja appearing as her most constant collaborator across 93 shared credits — a working relationship that extends well beyond her own releases. Jeff Tweedy’s presence among her top collaborators (17 shared credits) points to production work beyond her solo discography.
