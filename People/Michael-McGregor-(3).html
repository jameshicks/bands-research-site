Music producer, artist, and blogger. Co-founder of the Chocolate Bobka music blog and founder/operator of The Curatorial Club (Discogs label ID: 188527), a US cassette and limited-edition imprint active 2010–2011 that released early material by Games (3), Airbird (Joel Ford), and Chuck Person (Daniel Lopatin’s Eccojams alias). Personal site: mcgregor.world.

The Curatorial Club

McGregor ran The Curatorial Club as a vehicle for the early-2010s cassette underground he was curating through the Chocolate Bobka blog. The label’s 18-entry catalog (TCC001–TCC018) spans 2010–2011 and includes some of the most historically significant releases of the hypnagogic pop / vaporwave adjacent moment: Chuck Person’s Eccojams Vol. 1 (TCC011), Games (3)‘s Spend The Night With re-edit compilation (TCC008), and the Airbird / Megafortress split cassette (TCC016).

Music Projects

As Meadowlands, released cassette/ambient material on Exo Tapes, Patient Sounds Intl., and Stadiums & Shrines (2012–2015 per Discogs). As M/M, released on Czaszka Records, Speaker Footage, and Spring Break Tapes (2016–2018).
