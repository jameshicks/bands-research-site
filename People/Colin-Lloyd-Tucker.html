From Discogs

Colin Lloyd Tucker began a solo career aged 17, with ‘Toybox’, on which he wrote, produced and played all the instruments himself. His record releases have been infrequent, his live shows rare and his sound not easily definable. He recorded 3 albums with Matt Johnson (of ‘The The’) in the early eighties. Colin was also in groups Bushtucker, The Gadgets, Jeremy’s Secret and Plain Characters. Colin did not sign a major deal but on Kate Bush’s “Red Shoes” his name mingles with Eric Clapton, Jeff Beck and Prince.

Overview

A self-taught polymath who launched his recording career at 17 with Toybox — writing, producing, and performing every part himself — Colin Lloyd-Tucker spent the 1980s at the intersection of UK experimentalism and indie pop. He made three albums with Matt Johnson of The The in that decade, and later contributed vocals to Kate Bush’s The Red Shoes, appearing on the same record as Eric Clapton, Jeff Beck, and Prince. His longest creative partnership has been with Simon Fisher Turner — principally as the duo Deux Filles — accounting for his largest cluster of shared credits (47); he also logged extensive contributions to The Go-Betweens catalogue across nearly their entire core lineup. His label trail moves from early UK independents (Final Solution, Él) through Plastic Head in the mid-1980s and into Glass Modern, which has issued work as recently as 2023.
