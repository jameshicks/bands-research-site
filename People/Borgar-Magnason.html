Borgar Magnason

Discogs ID: 1093579
Origin: Iceland

Icelandic bassist (contrabass). Co-primary artist on Skeylja (Denovali Records, 2014) with Eiríkur Orri Ólafsson, The Alvaret Ensemble, Kira Kira, and Ingi Garðar Erlendsson. Also credited on Sigur Rós’s Kveikur (XL Recordings, 2013) as strings.
