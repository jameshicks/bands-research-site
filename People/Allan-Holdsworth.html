Allan Holdsworth

British jazz-rock guitarist. Born August 6, 1946, Bradford, Yorkshire. Died April 15, 2017. Discogs artist ID: 254012.

Guitarist in The New Tony Williams Lifetime (1975–76) — the second version of Tony Williams’s fusion group, alongside Alan Pasqua (keyboards) and Tony Newton (bass). The band recorded Believe It (1975, Columbia) and Million Dollar Legs (1976). Holdsworth’s legato single-note runs and unusual chord voicings made him a distinctive voice in fusion guitar; his work with the New Lifetime is among his most-cited early recordings. Also played with Soft Machine, Gong, UK, and later as solo artist. Cited as influence by Eddie Van Halen, among others.
