Heman Pathak

Discogs ID: 364063
Realname: Heman Pathak
Groups: Nurse With Wound, Hastings Of Malawi

Co-founder of Nurse With Wound alongside Steven Stapleton and John Fothergill. Of Indian background, he met Stapleton at school in North London and shared a passion for obscure European rock and experimental music. Described by Stapleton as “against his family wishes” becoming obsessed by European rock; notably one of the few other people Stapleton had met who was a fan of Floh De Cologne.

Pathak performed keyboards, piano, and effects on the first NWW albums (1979–1980) and co-compiled the “Categories Strain” NWW list insert on the debut. He also performed on the 2001 CD reissue of Chance Meeting… (credited separately from the original 1978 recording).

Beyond NWW, Pathak appears in Hastings Of Malawi (Discogs group ID 1137580).
