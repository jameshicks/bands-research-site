Overview

Valentina Magaletti is a London-based Italian drummer and percussionist, one of the most prolific and widely credited figures in UK experimental, electronic, and improvised music. She is best known as co-founder of Tomaga (with Tom Relleen) and as a member of an unusually large number of concurrent groups and projects.

Her group memberships on Discogs include: AVVITAGALLI, Better Corners, Buttonhead, CZN, Frequency Disasters, Holy Tongue, London Improvisers Orchestra, Moin (2), Teclo, Tomaga, UUUU (2), V/Z, and Vanishing Twin.

Instrument range spans: drums, percussion (prepared and found-object), vibraphone, marimba, piano, timpani, electronics, oscillator, toys, field recordings, and voice.

Notes

Wikipedia (June 2026 snapshot) confirms: Magaletti was born in Bari, Italy; started drumming at 12; teachers included Agostino Marangolo (Goblin) and jazz drummer Michele Di Monte. Her V/Z project is a duo with Susumu Mukai (also Vanishing Twin); the duo released Suono Assente (AD 93, 2023) to which Cathy Lucas contributed vocals/lyrics on track A3. Holy Tongue (with Al Wootton) is described as dub-influenced. UUUU also includes Graham Lewis (Wire) and Thighpaulsandra (Coil). In 2017 she played drums for surviving Can member Irmin Schmidt’s Can Project. She and Fanny Chiarello founded the imprint Permanent Draft in 2023, dedicated to promoting female, non-binary, and transgender artists in experimental music.
