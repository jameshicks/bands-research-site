Kishi Bashi

Discogs ID: 2446539
Real name: K Ishibashi (also credited as “K Ishibashi” on songwriting, ID 1051049)
Label: Joyful Noise Recordings

Solo artist and multi-instrumentalist. Most-represented artist on Joyful Noise Recordings with 123 releases from 2012–2026. Debut 151a (JNR92, 2012) produced by Ishibashi himself, mixed by Drew Vandenberg and Kishi Bashi, mastered by Joe Lambert. String and violin-forward indie pop aesthetic; also handles production and arrangement on much of his catalog. Carries 34 producer credits on JNR releases including work for other label artists.
