Michael Lastra

Discogs ID: 277708 (also credited as Dr. Id, Discogs ID 491950)

Core member of Smegma and primary producer/engineer for Pigface Records, operated out of Portland, OR. His Discogs aliases include “Dr. Id,” “Smegma Mike,” and “Martini Mike Lastra.” Also a member of The Beast (2).

Producer and engineer on Smegma releases including Pigs For Lepers (1982), 1973-1998 Vol. I (1999), and G Spot Crush by Hellcows (1987). The Pigface Records mailing address (2123 NE Rodney, Portland OR) corresponds to his home/studio (Smegma Studios, referenced in his Discogs URL).
