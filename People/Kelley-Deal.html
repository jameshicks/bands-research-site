Kelley Deal

Born June 10, 1961, Dayton, Ohio. Guitarist, vocalist, songwriter, producer. Discogs artist ID: 273942. Identical twin sister of Kim Deal. Co-founder of The Breeders (lead guitarist, co-vocalist, 1992–present). Also co-leads R. Ring with Mike Montgomery. Name variations on record: Deal, Kelley, KelleyDeal, Kelly Deal.

Biography

Early Life and The Breeders (1989–1994)

Kelley Deal grew up in Dayton, Ohio alongside her twin sister Kim Deal. Kim co-founded The Breeders in 1989 with Tanya Donelly (Throwing Muses) as a side project from the Pixies, and Kelley joined as lead guitarist. By the time of Safari (4AD, 1992) — the band’s first release as a four-piece — Kelley was fully credited for guitar and vocals. The band’s commercial breakthrough came with Last Splash (4AD, 1993), recorded with producer/engineer Mark Freegard. That album reached platinum in the US, driven by the single “Cannonball.” Kelley’s guitar work was central to the band’s loud-quiet-loud aesthetic; she is also notably credited with operating a “Kenmore 12-stitch” sewing machine as a noise instrument on that record.

The Kelley Deal 6000 (1996–1997)

While The Breeders went on hiatus following the Last Splash cycle, Kelley assembled her own band, The Kelley Deal 6000, based in Dayton. The lineup included Marty Nedich (bass), Nick Hook (drums), Steve Salett (guitar), and Todd Mund and Todd Johnson. The band released three records: the Canyon EP (1996, Nice Records / Bitter Sweet), the full-length Go To The Sugar Altar (1996, Nice Records), and Boom! Boom! Boom! (1997, New West/Nice Records). Kelley produced all three, handled artwork and logo design, and played guitar and sang. Guest contributors on Go To The Sugar Altar included David Shouse (guitar, lap steel, Hammond, Casio), Jesse Roff (guitar, drums, trumpet), and Jimmy Flemion (vocals, guitar — later her collaborator in The Last Hard Men). A European single, Brillo Hunt, appeared on Play It Again Sam in 1997.

The Last Hard Men (mid-1990s–2001)

The Last Hard Men was a supergroup combining Kelley (vocals, guitar, bass, writing), Jimmy Flemion (guitar, vocals, bass, piano), Jimmy Chamberlin (drums, of Smashing Pumpkins), and Sebastian Bach (vocals, formerly of Skid Row). The project recorded in the mid-1990s but its sole self-titled album appeared in 2001 on Spitfire Records. Kelley sang lead vocals on a significant portion of the tracklist and co-wrote several songs. The record also included covers of Alice Cooper, Scorpions (“Rock You Like A Hurricane”), and a reading of a Rodgers & Hammerstein standard.

The Breeders: Return and Later Eras (2002–present)

A The Breeders reunion LP, Title TK, followed in 2002 on 4AD, with the lineup of Kim Deal, Kelley Deal, José Medeles, and Mando Lopez. The original Last Splash lineup — Kim, Kelley, Josephine Wiggs, and Jim MacPherson — reunited for Mountain Battles (4AD, 2008), with Steve Albini among the recording engineers. That same lineup returned for All Nerve (4AD, 2018), recorded partially by Mike Montgomery and partially by Albini; Matt Boynton mixed most tracks. A lead single, Wait In The Car, appeared on 4AD in 2017. Nobody Loves You More (4AD, 2024) continues the band’s activity.

R. Ring (2011–present)

R. Ring is the duo project Kelley co-leads with Mike Montgomery (also of Ampline and Thistle). They record and produce everything themselves. Releases include the self-titled 7” (Not On Label, 2011), Fallout And Fire (Misra, 2012), the Singing Tower / Tip To Trick The Tide 7” (Sofaburn Records, 2015), the full-length Ignite The Rest (Sofaburn Records, 2017), and War Poems, We Rested (Don Giovanni Records, 2023). Lori Goldston (cellist, known from Nirvana’s MTV Unplugged) appears as a featured musician on War Poems, We Rested. Kelley and Mike share recording, mixing, and mastering duties throughout the catalog.

Guest Credits and Collaborations

Kelley co-wrote track B1 on Protomartyr’s Consolation E.P. (Domino, 2018). The EP was recorded and mixed by Mike Montgomery, giving R. Ring’s immediate orbit a direct production link to that record. She also contributed backing vocals to Courtney Barnett’s Tell Me How You Really Feel (Milk! Records / Remote Control, 2018), and to Motel Beds’ Understanding Land (Electric Ragtime, 2014). She appeared on a split 7” with Torres and Motel Beds (Misra, 2014). The Sonic Ecstasy compilation (Octopus, Italy, 1994) captured early Breeders-era live material.

Freedom Cruise

Freedom Cruise is a project grouping Kelley Deal, Kim Deal, Robert Pollard (Guided by Voices), and Jim Greer. Its Discogs entry exists but no releases appear to have been documented in the database.
