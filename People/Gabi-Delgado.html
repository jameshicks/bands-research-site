Gabi Delgado

Discogs ID: 23762
Real name: José Gabriel Delgado López
Born: 18 April 1958, Córdoba, Spain
Died: 22 March 2020

Spanish-born, Germany-based vocalist, lyricist, and producer. Co-founder of Deutsch Amerikanische Freundschaft (DAF) with Robert Görl in 1978. His family moved from Córdoba to Germany in 1966. He was a founding force of the Rhine/Ruhr punk scene and helped create the first German punk fanzine “The Ostrich.” Associated with Mittagspause — credited as co-writer of “Militürk” (credited “Delgado-Lopez” on their 1979 EP) and “Militürk 1 / Militürk 2” on the posthumous Punk Macht Dicken Arsch LP; not a performing member. Also associated with Deutschland Terzett.

Provided all vocals and lyrics for the DAF Virgin trilogy (1981–82). After DAF’s first split, released solo album Mistress (1983). Around 1996 partnered with Wotan Wilke on DOS. Also ran Berlin club/dance projects including Delkom (with Saba Komossa) and associated labels (Delkom Club Control, BMWW - The Delkom Corporation, Sunday Morning Berlin). Later solo albums: 1 (2013), 2 (2015).

Died of a heart attack, March 22, 2020; DAF had been planning a new album.

Aliases: Delboy (5), Disco Garage, Latino Tanz Machine, Santos Leon, and others
Groups: Deutsch Amerikanische Freundschaft, DAF / DOS, Delkom, Mittagspause, Deutschland Terzett, Futur, Future Perfect, others
