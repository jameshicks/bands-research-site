Elvis Costello

Discogs ID: 55029
Real name: Declan Patrick Aloysius Macmanus

UK singer-songwriter, guitarist, and producer. Wrote Shipbuilding (1982, Rough Trade) for Robert Wyatt — music by Clive Langer, lyrics by Costello; addresses the Falklands War. Later appeared on the 7” Asylums In Jerusalem / Jacques Derrida (1982, Rough Trade, ID 29302) with Wyatt. Produced The Specials’ debut album Specials (1979) — his first major production credit. Known for aliases including Napoleon Dynamite, The Imposter (2), Nick Lowe Not To Blame For This One, and others.

Key Credits

  • Producer: Specials (1979) — The Specials
  • Writer: “Shipbuilding” (1982) — Robert Wyatt
  • Co-writer: Flowers in the Dirt (1989) — Paul McCartney; co-wrote 4 tracks (credited MacManus/McCartney)
  • Momofuku (Lost Highway, 2008, Discogs release ID: 1345128) — co-produced with Jason Lader, who also engineered, mixed, and did studio photography. The Imposters (Davey Faragher, Pete Thomas, Steve Nieve). Lyrics on two tracks by Loretta Lynn and Rosanne Cash.

Label History (brief)

  • Radar Records (1978–79) — This Year’s Model (RAD 3), Armed Forces (RAD 14), “Oliver’s Army” (ADA 31, UK #2); produced by Nick Lowe
  • F-Beat (1980–86) — co-founded with Lowe, Jake Riviera, Andrew Lauder; see F-Beat for full discography
