Scott Krauss

Drummer. Cleveland, Ohio. Born 1950. Discogs artist ID: 365052.

Name variations in credits: Scott Kraus, Scott Krause, R. Scott Krauss, R. Scott Kraus, S. Krauss, Kraus, Krause, Krass.

Overview

Scott Krauss is the founding drummer of Pere Ubu, the Cleveland avant-garage group formed in September 1975 from the ashes of the short-lived collective Rocket From The Tombs (though Krauss is not confirmed as a member of that band in Discogs; he appears instead in adjacent pre-Ubu circles including Cinderella Backstreet, Hy Maya, and The Finns). He played on Pere Ubu’s debut Hearthan singles in 1975–76, all five of the classic-era albums through The Art Of Walking (1980), and returned for the reformation albums The Tenement Year (1988), Cloudland (1989), Worlds In Collision (1991), Story Of My Life (1993), and on selected tracks of Ray Gun Suitcase (1995), which marked his final departure from the group.

Outside Pere Ubu, Krauss co-founded Home And Garden with Tony Maimone during Pere Ubu’s first dissolution (1982–1987), releasing two LPs. He also contributed drums to the Cleveland experimental collective Hy Maya (whose 1972-era recordings were eventually released in 2017 as The Mysticism Of Sound & Cosmic Language on Smog Veil Records) and played with the avant/noise collective Scarcity Of Tanks and the post-punk group Neptune’s Car.

His drumming is characterized by propulsive, locked-in playing that anchors Pere Ubu’s most experimental textures — a grounding element against Allen Ravenstine’s EML synthesizer noise and David Thomas (2)‘s eccentric vocals.

Pere Ubu: Classic Era (1975–1982)

Krauss was recruited by David Thomas (2) and Peter Laughner in September 1975 for what was originally intended as a one-single project. The founding lineup was Thomas (vocals), Laughner (guitar), Tom Herman (guitar), Tim Wright (2) (bass), Krauss (drums), and Allen Ravenstine (synthesizer). After the debut single Laughner departed (he died in June 1977); Tony Maimone replaced Tim Wright on bass. This five-piece — Thomas, Herman, Maimone, Ravenstine, Krauss — recorded the two canonical early albums and continued through New Picnic Time (1979). For The Art Of Walking (1980) Mayo Thompson replaced Tom Herman on guitar. After Song of the Bailing Man (1982) — on which Krauss was replaced by Anton Fier — Krauss and Maimone left to form Home And Garden.

Home And Garden (1982–1984)

After Pere Ubu’s first dissolution, Krauss and Tony Maimone co-founded Home And Garden in Cleveland. The project drew in other ex-Ubu associates and released two self-produced LPs.
