Biography

Detroit Origins (mid-1990s)

Michael Buchanan, operating as House Shoes and sometimes as Smokey The Cat, emerged from the Detroit underground hip-hop scene in the mid-1990s. He was part of the extended collective around J Dilla (then credited as Jay Dee) during the period when Dilla was producing remixes and building his reputation outside of Slum Village. Buchanan was also a member of the large Detroit hip-hop collective Thirtyone (Discogs ID: 4895213), a sprawling multi-member Detroit crew with alumni including Bugz (2), Harvard Blue, and others.

His first significant move as a record man was founding House Shoes Recordings (Discogs ID: 30209) and pressing the Jay Dee Unreleased EP in 1996 — an 8-track white-label 12” (cat# SHOES 001) collecting Jay Dee remixes for De La Soul, Busta Rhymes, Das EFX, D’Angelo, and others that had been circulating. The EP is among the earliest vinyl documents of Dilla’s remix work.

The 1999 Phat Kat 12” Dedication To The Suckers was executive-produced by Buchanan alongside Brian Gillespie, with Jay Dee providing production and scratch work (alongside DJ Dez, a.k.a. Andrés). The 2003 collection showcased the full Detroit underground roster of the time — Slum Village, Phat Kat, Royce Da 5’9”, Proof (3), Dabrye, Finale, and others — with Buchanan credited under both the House Shoes and Smokey The Cat aliases.

DJ Career and Mix Work (2004–2011)

Through the late 2000s Buchanan established himself as a touring and recording DJ. His primary solo statements in this period were DJ mix projects:

  • Loungin’ Volume One (2004) — live set from the Buddha Lounge
  • The King James Version (2009, Not On Label) — 5-part mixed CD clocking over 75 minutes; recorded by Rhettmatic of the Beat Junkies; artwork by Black Kerouac. Spans classical, jazz, funk, soul, rock, Latin, and electronic — a genre-agnostic statement of his range as a selector.

He also held an executive producer credit on the well-regarded Elmatic (2011, Jae B. Group) — Elzhi’s rapping-over-Illmatic-beats project.

Street Corner Music and The Gift Series (2013–2022)

Around 2013 Buchanan launched Street Corner Music (2) (Discogs ID: 601672; appears as “SCM”), which became his primary vehicle for the next decade. The label’s flagship project was The Gift, a long-running vinyl beat-tape series pairing Buchanan as presenter/curator with individual producers:

SCM has two sublabels: Flips (ID: 816830) and The Gift (2) (ID: 834640).

Bits & Pieces Mixtape (2015)

Bits & Pieces (The House Shoes Mixtape) (2015, Mello Music Group) was mixed/DJ’d by Buchanan and fronted by Detroit rapper Finale. The production roster across 22 tracks includes Flying Lotus, Black Milk, Waajeed, Nottz, Oddisee, Knxwledge, and others — functioning as a survey of underground hip-hop production circa 2015.

Big Shoes (2019)

Big Shoes (2019, SCM cat# SCM133) is Buchanan’s most prominent solo album proper, a full collaborative LP with Detroit rapper Big Tone (2). Buchanan handled beats (credited under the Michael Buchanan (4) Discogs alias for the beat credits) and art direction; Big Tone handled raps. Features include Denmark Vessey, Quelle Chris, Ta’Raach, Blu (2), Jimetta Rose, and 87 (artist). Mixed and mastered by Jared Gosselin; recorded by Scott Martin (a.k.a. Tenacity) and Jared Gosselin.

J Dilla’s The Diary (2016)

House Shoes served as A&R alongside J Rocc on J Dilla’s posthumous album The Diary (2016, Mass Appeal / Pay Jay Productions, cat# PJ007). The album had been recorded largely before Dilla’s 2006 death and was shepherded to release by Eothen Alapatt. Buchanan’s name appears in the production credits of the opening track “The Introduction” (co-produced by House Shoes and J Dilla). Mastering and archival research by Dave Cooley.
