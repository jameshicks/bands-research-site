Overview

Eddy Sayer is a drummer and percussionist with Discogs credits dating to 1984, working primarily across ambient, tribal, and experimental music. A member of Lights In A Fat City and Stillpoint, his catalogue concentrated in the 1990s with City Of Tribes Records as the principal label relationship (1992–1999). His heaviest collaborations are with Stephen Kent (14 shared releases) and Adrian Borland and the Citizens ensemble (12 shared credits each), an unusual overlap of tribal and aboriginal percussion work with the post-punk world Borland inhabited.

Sayer’s role in Adrian Borland & The Citizens

The Citizens were not a standing band but a loose aggregation of session musicians assembled for the 1989 album Alexandria, recorded January–February 1989 at Elephant Studios, London, and mixed at Greenhouse Studio. Sayer was brought in as the album’s percussionist — credited alongside Graham Ward (drums, timpani). Other session players on the same recording include Anthony Thistlethwaite (sax, harmonica, tambourine; ex-The Waterboys), Danny Thompson (string bass; ex-Pentangle), Ivan Groznyc (cello), John Metcalfe (viola/strings arrangement), and Colvin Mayers (an ex-The Sound (2) colleague of Borland’s). The album was produced by Adrian Borland and Nick Robbins and released on Play It Again Sam. [Search result snippet from discogs.com / en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandria_(album), retrieved 2026-11-20]

The 12 shared Discogs credits between Sayer and Borland / the Citizens almost certainly reflect Alexandria appearing across multiple pressings and reissue formats (at least five separate Discogs release entries exist for Alexandria) plus any associated singles, rather than 12 distinct collaborative projects. No second Borland–Citizens recording session featuring Sayer has been identified.
