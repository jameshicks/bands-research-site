Overview

Tim Midgett (real name Tim Midyett) is a Chicago-based multi-instrumentalist—primarily bassist and vocalist—who came up through Seattle and Missoula, MT, and has been recording since at least 1988. He is best known as a core member of Silkworm and its post-Silkworm successor Bottomless Pit, and continues to record with Mint Mile. His catalogue spans indie rock and heavier territory: the Matador and Touch And Go years align with guitar-driven alternative rock, while a substantial body of work alongside Stephen O’Malley, Greg Anderson, and Sunn O))) draws him into drone and doom metal circles. Comedy Minus One accounts for the largest share of his output, covering releases from 2013 through the mid-2020s, and he also co-founded Ox Optimal, a Milwaukee-based internet marketing consultancy.
