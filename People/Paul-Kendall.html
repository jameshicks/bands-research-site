Paul Kendall

Producer, engineer, remixer, visual artist, composer. UK. Discogs artist ID: 26841. Also credited as PK, P.K., Paul K. Aliases: Pablo Castrati (Discogs ID: 77211), Piquet (Discogs ID: 31054). Band memberships: Dry Rib, Kendall Turner Overdrive, The Digital Intervention, Troika (8), Tot (17).

Overview

Paul Kendall is a UK producer and engineer whose career traces two major arcs. The first — running from the mid-1980s through the mid-1990s — is rooted in the Mute/Blast First orbit: he was the house engineer at Worldwide Studios, London, and became one of the key production figures for Nitzer Ebb, Wire, Barry Adamson, I Start Counting, Loop (3), and the early catalogue of Depeche Mode (in an engineering capacity). His most prominent early credits are producing and engineering both Ut albums for Blast First (Early Live Life, 1987; In Gut’s House, 1987). The second arc — running from the late 1990s onward — is broader and more varied: French chanson (Bertrand Burgalat / Tricatel circle), electronica, industrial/EBM, his own projects, and continued remix work for Mute.

He is also a visual artist and in 2003 co-founded The Digital Intervention with vocalist Olivia Louvel; since 2011 has released music on his own Cat Werk Imprint label.

Members: Andrew Goodwin (2), Joni Sackett, Mike Mulholland (2), Paul Kendall, Robert Vasey. Released The Dry Season (1979, Clockwork Records (3)) — their earliest documented credit with Kendall as producer.

Kendall Turner Overdrive (Discogs ID: 108878)

Members: Paul Kendall and Simon Fisher Turner. Released Displaced Links (1997, Mute / Parallel Series, cat. CDPSST 4) — an ambient/abstract/experimental CD. Tracks are credited as written by both Kendall and Fisher Turner; also credited with liner notes.

The Digital Intervention (Discogs ID: 172908)

Members: Paul Kendall and Olivia Louvel. Founded 2003. Released:

  • Capture (2003, Parallel Series, cat. PSST 5) — co-produced, programmed, mixed and co-written by Kendall; Louvel on vocals; also released “Coma Idyllique (Special Edit)” (2003, 0101)
  • Continued releases under the Cat Werk Imprint label through 2017 (Data Regina, 2017, includes Kendall as co-producer/mix consultant)

Troika (8) (Discogs ID: 3558674)

Members: Andrew Goodwin (2), Joni Sackett, Paul Kendall. Released 3 Voices (1985, Plum Records (8)).

Tot (17) (Discogs ID: 11917739)

Members: Paul Kendall and Tony Martin (7). No primary-credit releases found in the database.

Alias Credits

Piquet

Solo project; released The Faulty Caress (1996, Mute / Parallel Series, cat. CDPSST 2) — a 7-track CD of experimental noise/electronic material. Written by Kendall (tracks 1–3, 5–7); track 4, “Alone In Stone,” co-written with Douglas McCarthy (Nitzer Ebb) and Bon Harris (uncredited). Mastered by Denis Blackham. Sleeve by FilthArt / Roland Brown. This is the only primary Piquet release in the database.

Pablo Castrati

Credited on one remix: a contribution to a Mute Compilation (2010). No primary Pablo Castrati releases in the database.
