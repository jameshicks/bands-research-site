Alexander Hacke

German guitarist, bassist, vocalist, composer, filmmaker. Born Alexander von Borsig, 11 October 1965, West Berlin. Discogs artist ID: 107203.

Overview

Alexander Hacke joined Einstürzende Neubauten in 1980 at age fifteen and has remained a member since. He played guitar through the mid-1990s, then switched to bass, which Blixa Bargeld has described as making Hacke “a musical director of the band.” His wide catalogue of collaborations includes Crime & the City Solution, Miranda Sex Garden, Tiger Lillies, FM Einheit, Robert Rutman, Phew, Danielle de Picciotto (his partner and wife since 2006), and David Yow. Since 2010 his main side project is hackedepicciotto with de Picciotto. He also composed music for Fatih Akın’s films Head-On (2004) and Crossing the Bridge: The Sound of Istanbul (2005), in which he appears as a central character. (Source: Wikipedia: Alexander Hacke)

He is a part-time member of Big Bottom, Susan Stenger’s New York five-bass-guitar ensemble (formed 1997).
