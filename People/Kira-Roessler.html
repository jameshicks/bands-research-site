Kira Roessler

Full name: Kira Roessler Origin: USA Role: Bassist, vocalist

Bassist best known as a member of Black Flag (1983–1985) and the bass duo DOS (4) with Mike Watt (her then-partner). She and Watt drove to the East Coast together following D. Boon’s death in December 1985 — she was beginning an internship at Yale; Watt continued to New York City, which led to his involvement in Ciccone Youth.
