Overview

T La Rock (Clarence “Terry” Ronnie Keaton) is an American rapper from The Bronx, New York City and an older brother of Special K and Tony Tone. His recording career is concentrated almost entirely in the 1980s — 13 releases spanning Electro, Hip Hop, and Hip-House — with Fresh Records as his primary home and a notable early credit on Def Jam Recordings. His closest collaborators include Kurtis Mantronik (21 shared credits), Jazzy Jay, and Rick Rubin (both 18), placing him at the intersection of early hip-hop’s key production networks.
