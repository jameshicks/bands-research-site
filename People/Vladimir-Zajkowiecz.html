Vladimir Zajkowiecz

Bass and bassoon player, real name Martin Howells. Core member of The Chrysanthemums (1987–c.1990) and The Creams, and a participant in the band’s 2020s revival. Also recorded as a solo artist under his own name on Mermaid Records.

  • Discogs ID: 965003
  • Real name: Martin Howells
  • Also credited as: Martin Howells (2) (Discogs ID: 1694475), Nils Phlamethrowar (Discogs ID: 2857912)

Biography

Vladimir Zajkowiecz is the performing name of Martin Howells, a UK-based multi-instrumentalist whose principal instrument is bass (electric and double). He was a founding member of The Chrysanthemums, the English art-pop group co-founded by Alan Jenkins and Terry Burrows, and appeared on every Chrysanthemums release from their 1987 debut through to the Madagascar era in 1990.

The Chrysanthemums — Egg Plant period (1987–1990)

Zajkowiecz played bass and bassoon on the debut LP Is That A Fish On Your Shoulder Or Are You Just Pleased To See Me? (1987, Egg Plant), the group’s first full statement, alongside Alan Jenkins (lead guitar, backing vocals), Geraldine Minou-Sullivan (drums), Yukio Yung (lead vocals, piano), and Jane Laing (hammered dulcimer), with The Acton Horns featuring as guests. It was a core lineup of five that would recur across the Egg Plant run.

He contributed bass, backing vocals on the XXXX Sessions EP (1988, Egg Plant), also produced by the band collectively, with Jonathan Staines adding ukulele and R. Stevie Moore credited for tape. On the double LP Little Flecks Of Foam Around Barking (1989, Egg Plant) he expanded to bass, double bass, and backing vocals — Staines also joined for ukulele and vocals, and R. Stevie Moore again contributed tape. This was the most expansive Egg Plant release, with the Actonian Schonberg Quintet and The Acton Horns both guesting.

The Chrysanthemums then signed to Madagascar and released Odessey And Oracle (1990, Belgium pressing), where Zajkowiecz played bass guitar. Yukio Yung also produced. Jonathan Staines moved to melodica and backing vocals; Geraldine Minou-Sullivan continued on percussion.

Solo — The Jes-U-Like (1992)

In 1992 Zajkowiecz released a solo CD on Mermaid Records (cat. Vlad 011) credited simply as “Vladimir.” The Jes-U-Like is a 12-track album (Electronic / Rock, styles: Experimental, Indie Rock, Lo-Fi) with no additional credited personnel in the Discogs entry. Tracks include “Mr. Nevil,” “Wichita Lineman,” “World Of Interiors,” and “Harvest Moon.”

The Creams (1994–1996)

Running in parallel with his Chrysanthemums work, Zajkowiecz was a member of The Creams, a related Alan Jenkins vehicle also on Cordelia Records-adjacent labels. He played bass on The Creams And Nico (1994, Raffmond) alongside Jenkins (guitar), Robyn Gibson (drums), Blodwyn P. Teabag (keyboards), Alison Mackinder (vocals), and Peter Pengwyn (vocals). He also appears on Pluto (1996, Raffmond) as bass guitarist.

The Chrysanthemums — Jarmusic period (1995)

By 1995 the Chrysanthemums had moved to Jarmusic (Germany) with a slimmer lineup. Zajkowiecz played bass guitar and vocals on Insekt Insekt (1995) alongside Jenkins, Yukio Yung, Robyn Gibson, and Jonathan Lemon (organ, keyboards, vocals). The same lineup appears on Two Thirty-Gallon Drums Of Banana Purée (1995, Jarmusic / Pink Lemon Records), with Zajkowiecz on bass and vocals.

The Loch Ness Monster (as Nils Phlamethrowar)

Under the alias Nils Phlamethrowar, Zajkowiecz was a member of The Loch Ness Monster, a two-person group with Steven Plessiosaur (Discogs ID: 2857913). Releases under this alias were on Hamster Records And Tapes and predate the Chrysanthemums: Industrial Poppies (1985, credited as “Performer [All Material Created And Executed By]”) and Mosaic 44 (1988, credited as “Written-By”).

Martin Howells (2) alias credit

The Martin Howells (2) Discogs entry carries one additional credit not captured under the Vladimir Zajkowiecz ID: the pressing of Little Flecks Of Foam Around Barking (1989) where the role is listed as “Bass, Acoustic Bass, Backing Vocals” — a slightly different instrument listing (acoustic bass rather than double bass) for what appears to be an alternate pressing of the same LP.

2022 revival

Zajkowiecz returned as a “Performer” on Decoy For A Dognapper! (2022, Egg Plant), the Chrysanthemums’ return to their original label after more than 30 years. The lineup for this release includes Alan Jenkins, Yukio Yung, Robyn Gibson, Jonathan Lemon, Steven Plesiosaur (drums), Louis Burrows (clarinet), Jack Dunn (horn), Lane Steinberg (backing vocals), Illy Birkenwald (backing vocals), Geraldine McKewen (violin, backing vocals), and Joe O’Connor (voice).
