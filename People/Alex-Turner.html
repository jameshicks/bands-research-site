Alex Turner

Discogs artist ID: 408091 Real name: Alexander David Turner Born: January 6, 1986, Sheffield, Yorkshire, England Role: Vocalist, guitarist, lyricist, composer, producer Aliases: The Martini Police Name variations: A. Turner, Alexander David Turner, Alexander Turner, Turner

Primary songwriter, vocalist, and guitarist of Arctic Monkeys (2002–present). Co-founder and vocalist/guitarist of The Last Shadow Puppets with Miles Kane, James Ford, and Zachary Dawes. Wrote the soundtrack for Richard Ayoade’s film Submarine (2011) under his own name — the only release in the Discogs DB credited to Alex Turner as primary artist (outside the Vertigo EP). Also co-produced the Alexandra Savior debut Belladonna Of Sadness (2017) with James Ford. Member of Death Ramps (Discogs ID 986372), the informal Arctic Monkeys alias. Member of The Newell Octet.

Biography

Sheffield Beginnings (2002–2005)

Turner grew up in High Green, a suburb of Sheffield, where he formed Arctic Monkeys around 2002 with school friends Jamie Cook, Andy Nicholson, and Matt Helders. The band self-distributed demo CD-Rs — documented in Discogs as Beneath The Boardwalk (2004, Not On Label) — before any industry involvement. The grassroots circulation of these recordings and further demos via file-sharing built a local and online following ahead of label interest.

Arctic Monkeys signed to Domino Records in 2005. Their debut single “I Bet You Look Good On The Dancefloor” (Domino, October 2005) went to UK #1. A second single “When The Sun Goes Down” (2005) also reached #1. Turner’s lyrics — observational, working-class Sheffield vernacular, sharp and novelistic — were immediately recognised as distinctive.

Whatever People Say I Am and Rapid Ascent (2006–2007)

Whatever People Say I Am, That’s What I’m Not (Domino, 2006) became one of the fastest-selling debut albums in UK chart history. Produced by Jim Abbiss with one track (“Mardy Bum”) by Alan Smyth, mixed by Barny Barnicott and Abbiss, all lyrics by Turner. Discogs credits Turner as “Vocals, Guitar.”

Favourite Worst Nightmare (Domino, 2007) introduced James Ford as producer — a relationship that would define the next fifteen years of the band’s output. Ford co-produced with Mike Crossey, with Alan Moulder mixing. Turner continued as sole lyricist; Discogs credits “Words By” on this album onwards. The single “Brianstorm” opens the record.

The Last Shadow Puppets — First Album (2008)

During a lull in Arctic Monkeys activity Turner co-founded The Last Shadow Puppets with Miles Kane (then of The Rascals (2)). The Age Of The Understatement (Domino, 2008) was produced by James Ford, with orchestral arrangements and conducting by Owen Pallett, the London Metropolitan Orchestra performing, mixed by Ford and Richard Woodcraft. Turner and Kane share writing credits. The record — orchestral pop in a Scott Walker / John Barry mode — was a major departure in register from Arctic Monkeys.

Humbug and Collaboration with Josh Homme (2009)

Humbug (Domino, 2009), the third Arctic Monkeys album, was co-produced by Josh Homme (seven tracks) and Ford (three tracks). Recorded partly in Joshua Tree, California. Homme also contributed backing vocals to AM (2013) tracks 3 and 11. Turner’s lyrics grew more psychedelic and Southern Gothic in register.

Submarine Soundtrack — Solo Credit (2011)

Turner’s first and most prominent sole primary-artist credit: Submarine — Original Songs From The Film By Alex Turner (Domino, 2011), the soundtrack EP for director Richard Ayoade’s debut feature. Six songs, produced by Ford (mixed by Ford), with Bill Ryder-Jones on guitar (tracks A2, B1), string arrangements by Owen Pallett, conducted by Andrew Hewitt, strings recorded by Jake Jackson. Turner credited as composer (“Music By”), performer, and writer. The record represents Turner working in a more intimate, folk-influenced mode — acoustic guitar, confessional lyrics.

Suck It And See (Domino, 2011) also appeared this year, produced by Ford, mixed by Craig Silvey.

AM and Production Involvement (2013)

AM (Domino, 2013): produced by Ford (tracks 1, 3–12) and Ross Orton (tracks 1–2, with Orton producing track 2 outright). Turner designed the artwork alongside Matthew Cooper (2). Turner’s lyrics cover all tracks 1–11; the closing track, “I Wanna Be Yours,” sets John Cooper Clarke’s Sheffield poem to music — the only non-Turner lyric in the Arctic Monkeys catalog. Josh Homme contributed backing vocals on tracks 3 and 11. Mixed by Tchad Blake. Bill Ryder-Jones plays additional guitar on track 8. Ford plays keyboards on tracks 3, 6–12.

Vertigo EP — Mini Mansions Collaboration (2015)

Turner co-headlined the Vertigo EP (Capitol Records / Electromagnetic Recordings, 2015) with LA band Mini Mansions, under the credit “Mini Mansions With Alex Turner.” Discogs lists Turner as primary artist. Executive-produced by T-Bone Burnett, with Woody Jackson as associate producer and engineer Michael Harris (10). The Great Pretenders single (2015) features Turner on additional vocals and songwriting.

Alexandra Savior Production (2017)

Turner co-produced (with Ford) and contributed guitar, keyboards, and synth to Alexandra Savior’s debut album Belladonna Of Sadness (Columbia, 2017). Mastered by Bob Ludwig. A significant production credit outside his own projects.

Tranquility Base Hotel + Casino — Full Co-Production (2018)

Tranquility Base Hotel + Casino (Domino, 2018) marked Turner’s first full co-producer credit on an Arctic Monkeys album alongside Ford. Largely piano-driven and self-played: on the European pressing Turner is credited for vocals, backing vocals, piano, bass, organ, harpsichord, and Orchestron. Side B has Turner credited for “All Instruments.” Recorded at La Frette, Paris (engineers Anthony Cazade, Jimmy Robertson, Jonathan Ratovoarisoa, Nicolas Quéré) and Los Angeles (Turner as engineer at “Lunar Surface LA” with Loren Humphrey, and Michael Harris (10) for vocals). Ford engineered the London sessions and mixed the album. Turner also designed the artwork. A concept-album departure — literary, lounge-inflected, science-fiction themed.

The single “Four Out Of Five” (2018) was produced and written by Turner.

The Last Shadow Puppets — Reunion (2016)

Everything You’ve Come To Expect (Domino, 2016) reunited Turner, Kane, Ford, and now Zachary Dawes on bass. Produced by Ford, mixed by Tchad Blake, orchestrations by Owen Pallett. Mastered by Brian Lucey. Several singles: “Aviation,” “Bad Habits,” “Miracle Aligner,” The Dream Synopsis EP (2016).

The Car and String Arrangements (2022)

The Car (Domino, 2022): produced and mixed by Ford. Turner co-arranged the strings alongside Ford and Bridget Samuels. Turner is credited as Performer and Designer (with Matthew Cooper (2)). Matt Helders contributed photography. Recorded by Anthony Cazade and Loren Humphrey. More orchestral and cinematic than any prior record; Turner credited for strings arranging.
