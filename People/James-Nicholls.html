James Nicholls

Real name: James Lewis Nicholls. UK label director and musician. Discogs ID: 600128.

Managing Director of Fire Records (2002–present). Founder and Director of Earth (13) (Fire sublabel, est. c.2014). Also active as a performing musician — member of Virgin Passages (Discogs ID: 1196601) and Savaging Spires (Discogs ID: 2336115).

Overview

James Nicholls took over Fire Records from co-founders Clive Solomon and Johnny Waller around 2002, relaunching the dormant London independent with a new signing strategy and a substantial reissue programme. Under his direction, Fire expanded from its original UK post-punk/indie base into a broad international roster spanning US indie rock, post-rock, experimental folk, and contemporary singer-songwriters, while simultaneously building one of the most active vintage-folk and folk-rock reissue catalogs in the UK via the Earth (13) sublabel.

He brought creative hands-on involvement to the label from the start — early credits include sleeve design and layout work on Fire releases from 2003 onward, before executive-producer and A&R credits became the dominant mode in the 2010s–2020s. According to Wikipedia (Fire Records UK article), “The label was relaunched in the late 1990s by James Nicholls, and has gone on to release albums by Guided By Voices, Giant Sand, Pere Ubu, Black Lips, Jane Weaver, Josephine Foster, The Lemonheads and Islet.”

Nicholls is also credited as a recording engineer on the 1984 soul/funk album Combat Zone by Prince Charles And The City Beat Band (Virgin Records, Spain), suggesting some pre-Fire studio work earlier in his career.

As a musician, he is the primary songwriter and producer for both Virgin Passages and Savaging Spires.

The self-titled LP (2011, CRITTER03) is the key release: 12 tracks across two sides, credited “Written-By, Performer: Savaging Spires”, mastered by Brian Pyle (who also mastered extensively for Fire Records). Genres span abstract electronics through neofolk and folk rock.

We Should Be Dead Together (2013) brings in guest players on track 11, including Alex Hornsby on vibes — Hornsby is also Fire Records’ principal sleeve designer (84 releases on Fire per Discogs credits summary).

Critical Heights (Discogs ID: 241285, 71 releases) is an independent UK label with a broader roster including DIVA (32), Scott & Charlene’s Wedding, Virginia Wing, Dead Rat Orchestra, and others.
