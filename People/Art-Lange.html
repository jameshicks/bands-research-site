Biography

Art Lange is a Chicago-based American writer whose work sits at the intersection of jazz criticism, poetry, and avant-garde music documentation. Born in Chicago in 1952, he became one of the most prolific annotators in the post-bop and free improvisation worlds, writing liner notes for labels including hat ART, hatOLOGY, Soul Note, Black Saint, Muse Records, Intakt Records, Les Disques Victo, and Verve Records.

He was the founding President of the Jazz Journalists Association. Beyond criticism, Lange wrote essays, reviews, articles, and interviews on both music and poetry.

Production Work

Alongside his writing, Lange took on producer credits at hatOLOGY from the late 1990s through the 2000s — a relatively small but notable body of production work concentrated on that label:

  • By The Law Of Music (1997, hat ART) — Matthew Shipp String Trio; Lange co-produced with Werner X. Uehlinger and supplied liner photography
  • A Memory Of Vienna (1997, hatOLOGY) — Ran Blake & Anthony Braxton; co-produced with Pia & Werner X. Uehlinger
  • The Multiplication Table (1998, hatOLOGY) — Matthew Shipp Trio (with William Parker & Susie Ibarra); co-produced with Werner X. Uehlinger
  • Something To Live For (1999, hatOLOGY) — Ran Blake solo; co-produced with Werner X. Uehlinger
  • Haunted Heart & Other Ballads (2001, hatOLOGY) — Marc Copland Trio (with Drew Gress & Jochen Rückert); producer
  • Scattered Clouds (2001, hatOLOGY) — Anthony Ortega Trio; producer and cover credit
  • Tales Out Of Time (2004, hatOLOGY) — Peter Brötzmann / Joe McPhee / Kent Kessler / Michael Zerang; producer

Liner Notes — Major Strand

Lange’s liner notes span four decades and effectively map the canon of European and American free jazz documentation. The heaviest concentration is on hat ART / hatOLOGY (Switzerland), with significant work also for Black Saint and Soul Note (Italy), Muse Records and Evidence (US), Verve Records, and Canadian labels including Les Disques Victo and Sackville Recordings.

His liner note credits run from 1980 (Brahma, Sackville) through 2024 (multiple ezz-thetics reissues), with the hat label family accounting for the bulk of the output.
