Adam Hoskins

Guitarist and multi-instrumentalist based in St. Louis, Missouri. Discogs artist ID: 2716793. Name variations: A. Hoskins, A.Hoskins, Adam “Lightnin’” Hoskins, Adam “Lightning” Hoskins.

Overview

Adam Hoskins is a guitarist and multi-instrumentalist best known as a founding member of The South City Three, the core backing trio for American roots musician Pokey LaFarge. Hoskins played guitar, lap steel, guitjo (guitar-banjo), and archtop acoustic guitar across LaFarge’s key albums on Third Man Records (2011–2013) and Rounder Records (2015–2017), and also served as associate producer on Central Time (2013) and the self-titled Pokey LaFarge (2013). Outside the LaFarge context, he is credited as lead guitarist on Tears Of Rage (2012) by Maximum Effort, a release on Problem Solved Industries. His Discogs footprint is entirely in collaborative and session roles — he has no primary solo releases in the database.

Career

The South City Three and Free Dirt Records (2010–2012)

The South City Three — Hoskins (guitar), Joey Glynn (double bass), and Ryan Koenig (harmonica, drums, washboard, vocals) — were the primary backing unit for Pokey LaFarge from at least 2010. The trio appears as a credited co-billed act on LaFarge’s earliest Discogs entries:

  • Pokey LaFarge And The South City Three — Riverboat Soul (2010, Free Dirt Records, R3827340): the group’s first appearance together on record; self-produced by LaFarge with producer Phil Harris (19) assisting.
  • Pokey LaFarge And The South City Three — Middle Of Everywhere (2011, Free Dirt Records, R3099479): Hoskins is specifically credited as playing a 1945 Silvertone Archtop acoustic guitar, a 1900 Guitjo (guitar-banjo), and a 1960 Fender Lap Steel — the vintage-instrument specificity is a hallmark of LaFarge’s early period. The session also featured additional brass players Shaye Cohn (cornet), Charlie Halloran (trombone), and Andrew Ratcliffe (2) (drums).
  • Pokey LaFarge And The South City Three — Live In Holland (2012, Free Dirt Records, R5536368): a live document of the early touring lineup, credited to Hoskins on guitar and vocals.
  • Pokey LaFarge And The South City Three — Riverboat Soul (2012 pressing, Free Dirt Records, R34938650): Hoskins credited on guitar and vocals.

The South City Three also released their own records on Free Dirt Records, with the 2012 7” Fan It / Shenandoah River appearing on the UK label Evangelist Record Co.

Third Man Records Period (2011–2013)

The trio’s association with Jack White (2) led to two years of Third Man Records releases:

  • Pokey LaFarge And The South City Three — Chittlin’ Cookin’ Time In Cheatham County (2011, Third Man Records, R2796281): Hoskins and the full South City Three performed on this Jack White-produced session. White and Vance Powell handled mixing; Vance Powell recorded. Hoskins is credited on the release-level band credit (backing vocals; guitar).
  • Jack White — Blunderbuss (2012, Third Man Records, R3549849): Hoskins is credited for acoustic guitar on this White solo LP, alongside fellow South City Three members Joey Glynn (bass) and Ryan Koenig (backing vocals). A notable crossover credit placing the St. Louis roots trio inside White’s core recording circle.
  • Pokey LaFarge — Pokey LaFarge (2013, Third Man Records, R4687192): Hoskins played electric guitar, slide guitar, archtop acoustic guitar, and backing vocals, and served as Associate Producer alongside co-producers Ketch Secor and LaFarge. Other musicians included Chloe Feoranzo (clarinet), Teddy Weber (cornet, lap steel), and Andrew Fletcher (7) (piano).
  • Pokey LaFarge — Central Time (2013, Third Man Records, R4836364): Hoskins again serves as Associate Producer; engineers Morgan Jahnig and Doug Storm (2) handled tracking; Ketch Secor and LaFarge co-produced.

Rounder Records Period (2015–2017)

  • Pokey LaFarge — Something In The Water (2015, Rounder Records, R6883464): Hoskins plays electric guitar on tracks A2, A3, A5, A6, B8, B10, and backing vocals on A2, A5. The session featured a large Chicago jazz ensemble alongside the St. Louis core — Alex Hall (4) (drums, piano, organ), Joel Paterson (guitar), Scott Ligon (piano), Paul Asaro (piano), Andy Schumm (cornet), and Chloe Feoranzo (reeds); produced by LaFarge and Jimmy Sutton.
  • Pokey LaFarge — Manic Revelations (2017, Rounder Records, R10281832): Hoskins plays lead guitar, vibraphone, and rhythm guitar — the vibraphone credit is a notable expansion of his instrumental range. The album was produced by South City Collective (Discogs ID 5670684, a production entity) and mixed by Tony Hoffer; fellow South City Three members Joey Glynn and Ryan Koenig also appear.

Other Credits

  • Maximum Effort — Tears Of Rage (2012, Problem Solved Industries, R6950444): Hoskins is credited as lead guitarist on all 12 tracks of this release. Maximum Effort (Discogs ID: 3341319) is otherwise poorly documented in the database.

The South City Three

The South City Three (Discogs ID: 2180809) was the three-piece backing group that accompanied Pokey LaFarge from 2010 through at least 2017. Members:

  • Adam Hoskins — guitar, guitjo, lap steel, slide guitar, archtop acoustic guitar, backing vocals
  • Joey Glynn (Discogs ID: 2716794) — double bass (upright), backing vocals. Also a member of Rum Drum Ramblers and The Vultures (5).
  • Ryan Koenig (Discogs ID: 2608912) — harmonica, drums, washboard, guitar banjo, fiddle, mandolin, vocals. Multi-instrumentalist based in St. Louis; also a member of Rum Drum Ramblers, Ryan Koenig & The Goldenrods, Southwest Watson Sweethearts, The Vickroids, and Lonesome Cowboy Ryan & His Dried-Up Teardrops.

The trio is billed as co-primary artists on the early Pokey LaFarge releases (Free Dirt Records and the first Third Man Records release); from the self-titled 2013 LP onward, they appear as session contributors rather than co-billed artists.
