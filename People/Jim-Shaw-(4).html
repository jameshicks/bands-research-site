Jim Shaw (4)

American visual artist. Realname: Jim Shaw. Discogs artist ID: 1150028. Name variations: J. Shaw, JS, Jim.

Co-founding member of Destroy All Monsters (with Mike Kelley, Cary Loren, and Niagara), active in the original 1973–1976 Detroit period. Along with Kelley, departed for CalArts (Los Angeles). Subsequently active in the LA art world as a painter and installation artist. Also a member of The Perfect Me (Discogs ID: 2667232).
