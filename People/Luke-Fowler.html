Luke Fowler

Scottish artist, filmmaker, and musician based in Glasgow. Discogs artist ID: 465679. Active on Discogs 2001–2025. Website: https://www.luke-fowler.com

Overview

Luke Fowler operates across moving image, sound art, and music performance. He is primarily known as a filmmaker working in documentary and portrait film — particularly films about figures in the British experimental music underground — but he also functions as an active musician and collaborator, appearing on records as instrumentalist, field recordist, and performer. His Discogs credits span compiled compilations, collaborative improvised recordings, performance on folk sessions, and group work across several ongoing bands.

His most significant Discogs-documented musical relationship is with Glasgow-based musician Richard Youngs, with whom he has shared four releases across more than a decade (2012–2024), including collaborative LPs on Fourth Dimension Records and Dekorder, and a 2024 digital release. Fowler also co-produced the 2003 film Xentos (Fray Bentos) Jones: Found Documents Concerning The Life And Work Of Xentos Jones with Kosten Koper — a documentary portrait of Jim Welton (aka Xentos Jones), available on UbuWeb. This film is representative of his broader practice of portrait films on marginal experimental figures, though the film itself does not appear as a Discogs release entry.
