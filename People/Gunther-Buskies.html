Overview

Gunther Buskies is a Hamburg-based music industry figure who joined Polygram (later Universal Music) in 1994, building expertise in back-catalogue management and reissue licensing across labels including Brain, Sky Records, and Revisited Rec. In 2002 he co-founded Tapete Records with Dirk Darmstaedter, and later established Bureau B, which became his primary outlet for reissues and new releases in experimental, Krautrock, and ambient music. His production and compilation credits — 424 in total, running from 2001 to 2025 — centre heavily on the classic Krautrock generation: Conny Plank, Hans-Joachim Roedelius, Dieter Moebius, Conrad Schnitzler, and Michael Rother all appear among his closest collaborators. Alongside his label work he is a guitarist and member of Bureau Buskies, Darlo, and Die Liga Der Gewöhnlichen Gentlemen.
