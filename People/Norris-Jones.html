Norris Jones

Also known as: Sirone
Discogs IDs: 417149 (Norris Jones), 297037 (Sirone)
Born: September 28, 1940, Atlanta, Georgia
Died: October 21, 2009, Berlin, Germany

Double bassist. One of the central figures of the American free jazz avant-garde, active from the mid-1960s through the 2000s. From the early 1970s performed almost exclusively under the alias Sirone. Co-founder of The Revolutionary Ensemble (1971–1977, revived 2004). Later a member of the Cecil Taylor Unit.

The Revolutionary Ensemble (1971–1977, 2004)

Jones co-founded the trio with Leroy Jenkins (violin) and Jerome Cooper (drums, piano). One of the most distinctive free jazz chamber groups of the era — three players generating an unusually wide textural range. Active 1971–1977, then disbanded. Revived as a reunion in 2004 for one further record.

See The Revolutionary Ensemble for full discography.
