Don Cherry

Discogs ID: 42757 Real name: Donald Eugene Cherry Born: November 18, 1936, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma Died: October 19, 1995, Malaga, Spain

American avant-garde jazz trumpeter and pocket trumpet player, co-founder of Ornette Coleman’s classic quartet and one of the most influential figures in both free jazz and world music. Cherry’s career spans from the late 1950s Los Angeles scene (he played with La Monte Young, Billy Higgins, and Dennis Budimir in high school) through the New York free jazz revolution of the 1960s, then into a prolonged period of global exploration in the 1970s–80s drawing on African, Asian, Middle Eastern, Scandinavian, and Indian music. His pocket trumpet and cornet became synonymous with free melodic invention; his later work introduced the doussn’gouni (a West African six-string hunter’s harp), Tibetan bells, Indian flutes, and other instruments. He settled in Sweden with textile artist and collaborator Moki Cherry, whose visual work appeared on several of his record covers. He is the stepfather of Neneh Cherry and the father of Eagle-Eye Cherry and David Ornette Cherry.

Codona

Codona was a trio formed with Collin Walcott (ECM/Oregon) and Naná Vasconcelos (berimbau/percussion), all produced by Manfred Eicher on ECM. The three LPs (1979, 1981, 1983) are cornerstones of ECM’s intercultural jazz strand. Walcott died in a traffic accident in November 1984, ending the group.
