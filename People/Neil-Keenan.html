Overview

Neil Keenan is a UK musician who played guitar and keyboards in Blyth Power, the folk-punk outfit that formed in 1985. His credit cluster is anchored around the band’s core lineup — Curtis Youe, Joseph Porter, Sarah Lewington, and Andy Morgan — with the bulk of his recorded work appearing on All The Madmen Records across the mid-to-late 1980s. Beyond the core band he accumulated credits with producer Grant Showbiz and guitarist Justin Adams, and later with Jamie Hince, pointing to a continued presence in the UK indie scene well past the band’s initial run; a Demo Tapes Records entry in 2010 confirms activity into the following decade.

Jamie Hince / Blyth Power connection

Jamie Hince (later guitarist of The Kills) was a member of Blyth Power as guitarist from approximately 1987 to 1990, one of many musicians to pass through the band’s ever-shifting lineup around a core of Joseph Porter. Wikipedia — Jamie Hince (as of 2026-11-03); Wikipedia — Blyth Power (as of 2026-11-03).

The three Discogs credits shared between Hince and Keenan fall in this 1988–1990 window. The specific releases Wikipedia attributes to Hince in this period are: Up from the Country (1988, Midnight Music), Goodbye to All That (1988), The Barman and Other Stories (1988), Alnwick & Tyne (1990, Midnight Music), and Better to Bat (1990, Midnight Music). Of these, Alnwick & Tyne is confirmed in liner credits: Hince is listed as “James Hince — Guitar.” Wikipedia — Jamie Hince (as of 2026-11-03). Keenan’s label table shows a Midnight Music entry dated 1989, which is consistent with the overlap — the three shared Discogs credits likely include Alnwick & Tyne and two further Midnight Music singles or releases from 1988–1990.

No source found explains how Hince came to join Blyth Power. Wikipedia describes the band as having “many line-up changes” with Porter as the constant; Hince’s recruitment is not addressed in any interview or press piece found. The connection appears to have been a straightforward band membership in the late-1980s UK post-punk/folk-punk scene, predating his subsequent bands Scarfo (formed 1994) and Fiji (1999) and The Kills (formed 2000).
