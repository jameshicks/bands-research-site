Kenny Barron

American jazz pianist, born Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, June 9, 1943. Discogs artist ID: 65746.

Veteran bebop-rooted pianist with a long recording career. Appears on Kenny Garrett’s early session Moon Alley (Criss Cross Jazz, 1986), billed as “Tom Harrell Quintet Featuring Kenny Garrett And Kenny Barron,” recorded by Rudy Van Gelder.
