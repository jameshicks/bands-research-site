Overview

Fin Greenall is a Cornish-born singer-songwriter, guitarist, producer and DJ who records as Fink, currently splitting his time between Berlin and London. His work ranges across acoustic, folk rock, indie rock and blues rock, with early downtempo output giving way to a predominantly singer-songwriter catalogue that peaked in the 2010s (over twenty releases that decade). His main label home was Ninja Tune from 2001 to 2017, with later output channelled through the R’COUP’D imprint. Recurring production and performance collaborators include Tim Thornton, Guy Whittaker and Blair Mackichan.
