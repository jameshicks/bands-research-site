A.T. Michael MacDonald

Discogs ID: 889143 Name variations: A T Michael McDonald, A. T. Michael MacDonald, A. T. Michael McDonald, A.T. Michael McDonald, Michael MacDonald, Michael McDonald, Mike MacDonald, Mike McDonald (and several other spellings) Websites: algorhythmsmastering.com; Peabody Conservatory faculty page (Johns Hopkins University)

American sound engineer specializing in acoustic jazz recording and digital mastering. Active from 1979 across a career that spans three distinct phases: staff engineer at Skyline Studios, New York (1979–1984), chief engineer at Classic Sound, New York (1984–1989), and owner/chief engineer of his own digital mastering studio AlgoRhythms (2) since 1998. Across his career he has been the dominant engineering presence in the Sunnyside catalog, with credits from the label’s earliest releases through the 2010s, and from the 2000s onward became the principal mastering engineer for Palmetto Records. Also teaches at the Peabody Conservatory at Johns Hopkins University.

Career

Skyline Studios, New York (1979–1984)

MacDonald’s first Discogs credit is as engineer on Flatbush Waltz (1980, Rounder Records). Through this period he worked across a range of sessions: In A City Groove (1982, Cotillion), the Tender Mercies film soundtrack (1983, Liberty — the Robert Duvall film), and early Sunnyside and Concord Jazz dates. His first Sunnyside credit is Alter Ego (1984, Sunnyside). The 1984 period also brought work for Elektra Musician (In Performance At The Playboy Jazz Festival, Prime Time Workout) and Concord Jazz (A Beautiful Friendship, Isn’t It Romantic, Plays The Music Of Luiz Bonfá And Cole Porter).

Classic Sound, New York (1984–1989)

As chief engineer at Classic Sound — described in his Discogs profile as dedicated to recording acoustic jazz — MacDonald established himself as a go-to engineer in the New York jazz scene. Key sessions from this phase:

  • Sunnyside Records: It’s Your Dance (1985), Progress Report (1985), Seven Minds (1985), William The Conqueror (1985), Sung Heroes (1986), Everything Is Changed (1987), Sarabande (1987), Wind Inventions (1987), Elusive (1988), Give And Take (1988), The Cove (1988), The Double Cross (1988), Alone But Not Forgotten (1989), Rumba Para Monk (1989, mixing — Jerry Gonzalez), Waiting In The Wings (1989)
  • JMT Records: As One (1985), Transparency (1985), Dedication (1989)
  • Concord Jazz: Havin’ Fun (1985), Horizons (1985), Only Trust Your Heart (1988), Back To The Beautiful (1989), Plays Ballads (1989)
  • Soul Note: The Sixth Sense (1985), You Make Me Smile (1985), ETC (1990)
  • Black Saint: The Sixth Sense (1985)
  • Owl Records: Cold Blues (1985), From The Heart (1985)
  • Storyville: Daybreak (1989)
  • Atlantic: Short Stories (1989)

Transition period and freelance work (1990–1997)

Following the Classic Sound years, MacDonald continued working independently — recording, mixing, editing, and mastering sessions for a diverse range of labels:

  • No Question About It (1988, Blue Note), The Turning Point (1992, Verve/Birdology), Watch Your Step (1992, Antilles), Journey (1993, Verve/Birdology), Live In Paris 1992 (1993, Birdology)
  • CTI Collections mastering (1993, Windows Volume One and Volume Two)
  • Passion Flower — The Music Of Billy Strayhorn (1996, Nonesuch) — full package: recorded by, mixed by, edited by, mastered by
  • A Tribute To Wes Montgomery volumes (1992–1993, Evidence/Sound-Service Bellaphon)
  • Chesky Records editing (Best Of Chesky Classics & Jazz Vol. 3, 1994; Maiden Voyage, 1994; Plays…, 1994)
  • Thelonious (Fred Hersch Plays Monk) (1998, Nonesuch) — engineering, editing, mastering (Fred Hersch)

AlgoRhythms (2): mastering studio (1998–present)

In 1998 MacDonald founded AlgoRhythms, a digital mastering studio and listening room in New York (later relocated to Santa Fe, New Mexico in 2016). From this point his role shifts increasingly toward mastering and digital editing, though he continues full-package recording/mixing work for key clients.

Sunnyside Records continued as a primary client:

  • Speaking Of Jobim (2000), Love Is For The Birds (2001), Songs & Lullabies (2003, producer + full recording/mixing/mastering), North And South (2003, mastering), Duos II (2005, mastering), Dust (2006, editing + mastering), Fred Hersch Plays Jobim (2009, recorded by + mastered by), @ The Village Vanguard (2018, recorded by)

Palmetto Records became a dominant mastering relationship through the 2000s–2010s:

  • Dusk (2000, recorded by + mastered by), Riding The Nuclear Tiger (2001), A Beautiful Day (2002, recorded by), Peace Pipe (2002), Buzz (2004), Horizon Reassembled (2004), Lullabluebye (2004), Too Damn Hot (2004), La Espada De La Noche (2005), Leaves Of Grass (2005, producer + recorded by + full engineering), News On The Rail (2005), Una Noche Inolvidable (2005), Fantasy (2007), From The Heart (2007), Spark! (2007), Once Upon A Melody (2008), Whirl (2010), Jungle Soul (2006), Play (2006), The Scenic Route (2006), Quartet (2006), @ The Village Vanguard (2018)

Other significant late-career work:

  • Music Written For Monterey 1965 (2006, Sunnyside/Sue Mingus Music) — digital transfer
  • All That Is Tied (2006, Tompkins Square)
  • A Thousand Beautiful Things (2006, Telarc)
  • Truth And Beauty (2007, Origin Records)
  • Community Immunity (2011, Greenleaf Music)
  • Barcode Poetry (2024, Infrasonic Press) — most recent Discogs entry

Production credits (beyond engineering): Songs & Lullabies (2003, Sunnyside), Leaves Of Grass (2005, Palmetto).
