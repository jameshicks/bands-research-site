Biography

Daniel Lopatin (born July 25, 1982 in Boston, Massachusetts) is an American electronic music producer, composer, singer, and songwriter, best known under the alias Oneohtrix Point Never (OPN). The son of Russian-Jewish émigrés with musical backgrounds, Lopatin grew up in Winthrop, Massachusetts. His father owned a Roland Juno-60 synthesizer that Lopatin would inherit and use extensively in his own music. In high school he played synthesizer with friends including his future collaborator Joel Ford. He later attended Hampshire College in Massachusetts before moving to Brooklyn to study archival science at Pratt Institute — a field of study that informed aspects of his artistic practice. During his time in Brooklyn he became involved in the underground noise music scene. (Source: Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oneohtrix_Point_Never)

The alias Oneohtrix Point Never is a verbal play on the name of Boston FM station Magic 106.7 — “Magic one-oh-six point seven” — heard during his childhood. The project was originally called Magic Oneohtrix Point Never.
