Tatsuya Yoshida

Oct 06, 20265 min read

Tatsuya Yoshida

Discogs ID: 223249 (realname: 吉田 達也)
Active: 1984–present
Origin: Japan
Role: Drummer, composer, producer

Japanese drummer; founding member and constant core of Ruins (the avant-prog power duo). Guest drummer for PainKiller (2) during the 2004–2006 period, filling in after Mick Harris’s initial departure. Also records as Ruins alone (solo project alias).

Projects Beyond Ruins

Early bands (pre-Ruins / concurrent)

  • Phaidia — early Japanese punk/rock group, credited drummer from 1984 (City Rocker Records); also In The Dark (1985, City Rocker Records)
  • あぶらだこ (Aburadako) — Japanese hardcore punk, drums 1985 (Japan Record)
  • YBO² — Japanese noise/avant band on Transrecords (2); releases include Alienation (1986), Kingdom Of Familiydream (1986), 太陽の皇子 (1986), Doglamagla (1987), 光の国 (1987), Pale Skin, Pale Face (1988), Whole Lotta Live Bootleg (1988), Starship (1989, Invitation) — drums, voice, piano, keyboards across these releases
  • Vasilisk — Italian tribal/ambient group; Yoshida guests on drums for the live side of Acqua (1989, Musica Maxima Magnetica, Italy)
  • Akaten (赤天) — Yoshida project, 1995 (Magaibutsu); he played drums, bass, guitar, and vocals

Mastering / engineering (selected)

Yoshida also mastered many releases for artists in his circle including Acid Mothers Temple (multiple), Koenjihyakkei, Korekyojinn. He ran his own label Magaibutsu as home base for his releases.
