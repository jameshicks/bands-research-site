Steve Halliwell

British rock / new wave keyboardist and multi-instrumentalist. Member of King Swamp and Shriekback. Discogs artist ID: 657599.

Name variations on record: “Halliwell”, “Steve ‘The Count’ Halliwell”, “Steve Haliwell”.

Overview

Steve Halliwell is a British musician primarily associated with the post-Shriekback wave of British rock. He served as keyboardist and co-writer on both of King Swamp’s Virgin Records albums (King Swamp, 1988; Wiseblood, 1990), co-producing the debut and sharing primary writing credit with Dave Allen across both records. Prior to King Swamp he appeared with Shriekback as part of the “Shriekback Big Live Band” on Big Night Music (1986) and Gunning For The Buddha (1986), and as keyboardist on the video compilation Jungle Of The Senses (1987).

After King Swamp dissolved he made a guest appearance on East Village (2)‘s Drop Out (Heavenly, 1993) playing Hammond organ. In 2011 he appeared on Madam Life by Little Machine (2) — a record also featuring former King Swamp bandmate Walter Wray — playing vocals, bass, keyboards, guitar, and mandolin.

Discogs lists him as a member of three groups: King Swamp, Shriekback, and Little Machine (2).

King Swamp

King Swamp was formed in London in 1988 after Dave Allen and Martyn Barker parted ways with Shriekback. Halliwell (also ex-Shriekback) joined alongside vocalist Walter Wray, guitarist Dominic Miller, and Barker on drums. The group signed to Virgin Records.

Halliwell and Allen served as co-producers and co-writers on both albums. On the debut they are the sole credited songwriters (tracks 1–10). On Wiseblood the writing credit expands to include Wray on most tracks. Additional guitarist Mike Cozzi (Michael Cozzi) — another ex-Shriekback member — appeared on both records.

The debut was mixed by Bob Clearmountain and recorded/co-produced by Ian Caple and Nick Launay. Wiseblood was again mixed and produced (with the band) by Clearmountain, recorded by Caple and Andy Bradfield.
