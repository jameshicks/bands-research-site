Biography

Nathan Cross is an Austin, Texas-based bassist, percussionist, and label founder. On Discogs he appears as “Nate” (name variation) and holds multiple roles: performer, executive producer, and, most prominently, proprietor of Astral Spirits.

Cross has been active in the Austin noise/rock/experimental scene since at least the mid-2000s. His musician credits span a range of roles — bass (credited or uncredited), electric piano, dual Rhodes, electronics, percussion, and “Snarehorn” — and he appears across projects that run from heavy noise rock to avant-jazz to free improvisation.

Austin Bands

Cross is or has been a member of six groups documented on Discogs, all Austin-connected:

  • Shit And Shine — noise rock / psych outfit also featuring Craig Clouse and Jeffrey Coffey. Active since 2004; principally led by Craig Clouse. Cross appears on at least Chakin’ (2015, AS009 / Astral Spirits / Monofonus Press), the only Shit And Shine release cross-released on Astral Spirits.
  • USA-MEXICO — heavy bass/noise quartet with Craig Clouse, Jeffrey Coffey, and Owen McMahon. Released three albums on Riot Season (Laredo 2017, Matamoros 2019, Live In Paris 2025) and one on 12XU (Del Rio 2021). Cross plays bass (credited uncredited on Laredo).
  • Water Damage (3) — Austin drone/noise group with eight documented members including George Dishner, Greg Piwonka, Jeff Piwonka, Mari Maurice, Mike Kanin, Thor Harris, and Travis Austin. Active 2022–present; releases on 12XU and Cardinal Fuzz.
  • Marriage — Austin group with Greg Piwonka, Jeff Piwonka, Mike Kanin, and Alex Laroche.
  • Expensive Shit — larger Austin ensemble (nine members on Discogs): Cory Plump, Dave Youmans, George Dishner, Greg Piwonka, Jah Weed, Jeff Piwonka, Klint Teston, Mike Kanin, and Cross.
  • When Dinosaurs Ruled The Earth — Austin group with Brandon Crowe, Cory Plump, Daniel Francis Doyle, George Dishner, Jesse Hodges (2), and Marc Voorhees.

Astral Spirits

Cross founded Astral Spirits in September 2014, initially as a sublabel of Monofonus Press, the Austin experimental/punk imprint. When Monofonus Press closed in 2019, Astral Spirits continued independently under the parent entity Astral Spirits Records (ID: 2229175). Cross is the sole operator.

His direct production credits on Astral Spirits releases include Executive Producer on Hexane (AS001, 2014) and Amaranth (AS018, 2016) by Icepick (4), and Producer on Hiljaisuus (AS065, 2018) by Kuzu (4), and Executive Producer on No Place To Fall (AS103, 2019) and Ascua (AS081, 2020) by Anáhuac.

Cross also has a credit as “Management [Talent Scout]” on Shit And Shine’s Reality (2022, Drag City) — an indication of his broader A&R activity beyond the Astral Spirits catalog.

Astral Spirits Label

Discogs ID: 757266 Founded: September 2014 Originally: Sublabel of Monofonus Press Independent since: 2019 Contact: astralspiritsrecords@gmail.com Web: https://astralspirits.bandcamp.com · https://www.astralspiritsrecords.com

Astral Spirits releases avant-jazz, free improvisation, electro-acoustic, and experimental music. The aesthetic spans fire music in the Albert Ayler lineage, post-AACM Chicago free, European electroacoustic improvisation, and noise-adjacent free music. The catalog draws from Chicago, New York, the European improv circuits (Scandinavia, Spain, Portugal, Switzerland, Italy), Japan, and Canada, as well as Austin-local acts.

Catalog numbers use the AS prefix, sequential from AS001 (2014). By 2025 the catalog had reached at least AS249.

Sister Labels

  • Astral Editions (ID: 1866664) — launched late 2019; co-curated by Matthew J. Rolin and Jen Powers (Powers/Rolin Duo) from 2022. More textural and composed work.
  • cow: Music (ID: 2687966) — launched early 2022; run by Gabriel Jermaine Vanlandingham-Dunn.
