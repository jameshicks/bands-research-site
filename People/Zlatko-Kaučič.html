Biography

Zlatko Kaučič (born 1953) is a Slovenian percussionist and drummer based at the intersection of free jazz and European free improvisation. His work encompasses acoustic percussion, extended techniques, objects, and amplified zither — a deliberately broad sonic palette that places him closer to the European improv tradition than to mainstream jazz drumming.

Early and foundational work (1990s)

His Discogs record begins in 1994 with Round Trip (Le Parc), a leader credit from Italy, where he was already crediting himself as composer, performer of kitchen utensils and home-made instruments, and mixer — signalling the multidisciplinary approach that would define his career. By 1998 he had two significant releases: Emotional Playgrounds (Iktius) — a large-scale orchestral work featuring Kenny Wheeler, Gianluigi Trovesi, and the Komorni zbor RTV Slovenija chamber choir — and Glasba Za Romana, a more intimate self-released project. The Iktius label release is notable for his composing, arranging, and mixing credits alongside the performance.

Splasc(h) Records period and the Zlati Čoln projects (2000–2010)

Through the 2000s Kaučič became a consistent presence on the Italian Splasc(h) Records, releasing a sequence of albums that cemented his European reputation. Key releases from this period include:

  • Squarci (2000) — with Mauro Bardusco, Mauro Negri, Luigi Naro, Peppo Spagnoli, Stefano Amerio (engineer)
  • Zlati Čoln = The Golden Boat (2002) — a large ensemble project bringing together Steve Lacy, Paul McCandless, Jean-Jacques Avenel, Emanuele Cisi, Iztok Zupan, and the Ljubljanski Madrigalisti choir
  • Pav (2006)
  • Zlati Čoln 2 = The Golden Boat 2 (2006) — sequel with Ares Tavolazzi, Emanuele Cisi, Paul McCandless, Mariborska filharmonija
  • Tolminski Punt (2007) — with Peter Brötzmann and Luigi Naro
  • Koncerti Ob 30-Letnici = 30th Anniversary Concerts (2010) — a 30th anniversary retrospective featuring Giovanni Maier, Javier Girotto, Saadet Türköz, Emanuele Cisi, and the Doline Trijo

Also during this period: Vibrato Tišine (2006, Založba Sanje), a collaboration with Slovenian poet Srečko Kosovel and poet-translator Barbara Korun; and Vizionarja (2007, Goga), a multimedia/theatrical project with Irene Aebi.

The Emigrants release (2011, Leo Records, CD LR 604) marks a notable connection to the international free improvisation circuit, appearing on Martin Davidson’s celebrated London imprint alongside Claudio Zambenedetti and Lora Denis.

Group projects: Trio, Dreiländer, Disorder at the Border, Jubileum

Kaučič has sustained several ensemble vehicles simultaneously:

Zlatko Kaučič Trio — with Stefano Battaglia (piano) and Paolino Dalla Porta (bass). Release: December Soul (2013, Not Two Records).

Dreiländer Trio — with Giovanni Maier (bass) and Claudio Cojaniz (piano), released Dreiländer Trio (2012, Palomar Records). The name references the three-country (Italy/Slovenia/Austria) border region that characterises much of Kaučič’s collaborating territory.

Disorder At The Border — with Giovanni Maier and Daniele D’Agaro. Released Disorder At The Border (2014, Palomar Records) and Disorder At The Border Plays Ornette (2016, Not Two Records).

Zlatko Kaučič Cerkno Jubileum Orchestra — a large ensemble featuring Massimo De Mattia, Giovanni Maier, Cene Resnik, Giorgio Pacorig, Vid Drašler, Jošt Drašler, Vitja Balžalorsky, Marko Lasič, Luigi Vitale, Boštjan Simon, Gabriele Cancelli, Mimo Coliandro.

The Klopotec / Not Two period and Polish connections (2014–2022)

A cluster of albums on Slovenian label Klopotec (run by Iztok Zupan) documented ongoing activity across many formats: Round Trip (2014 reissue), Ena / One (2015), East West Daydreams (2016), Second Round (2016), Blasts Of Chance (2020), Arkosberg (2020), Live At Achteckstadl (2021), Electroscope (2022), Father, Son & Holy Sound (2024).

Concurrently he became a regular presence on the Polish Not Two Records roster: Sonic Party (2014), Canvas (2015), Without Borders (2017, Fundacja Słuchaj!), Diversity (2018), Agrakal (2018), One Foot In The Air (2023), The Steps That Resonate (2022) — these projects featuring a recurring network of Polish and European improvisers including Małgorzata Lipińska, Maciej Karłowski, Krzysztof Penarski, Krzysztof Machowina, Ab Baars, and Agustí Fernandez.

The Fundacja Słuchaj! label (Kraków-based free improvisation imprint, ID: 924193) became a key outlet: Without Borders (2017, FSR05|2017), Morning Patches (2019, FSR 11|2019), Live In St. Johann (2024, FSR 13/2024), Zlatko Kaučič @ 70 Inklings (2024, FSR 27|2024).

The Zlatko Kaučič Quintet and Morning Patches (2019)

Zlatko Kaučič Quintet (Discogs ID: 7381450) is the most internationally prominent ensemble. Members:

  • Michael Moore (4) — alto saxophone, clarinet
  • Marco Colonna — clarinet, bass clarinet
  • Albert Cirera — tenor saxophone
  • Silvia Bolognesi — double bass

Morning Patches (2019, Fundacja Słuchaj!, FSR 11|2019) is the Quintet’s only documented album on Discogs. Released as a digital file (FLAC/Album), it contains 10 tracks with Slovenian titles: “Koprena”, “Bilke”, “Svit”, “Mlaj”, “Jutranja”, “Arabeska”, “September”, “Delirij”, “Strehe”, “Altar”. All music composed collectively (each member credited “Music By”). Kaučič credited for “Percussion [Ground Sounds]“. Recorded, mixed, and mastered by Iztok Zupan; executive producer Maciej Karłowski. Genre: Avant-garde Jazz.

Duo with Tomaž Grom (2019–2021)

A sustained duo partnership with Tomaž Grom (double bass, objects) produced:

  • Uho Je Senca Očesa = The Ear Is The Shadow Of The Eye (2019, Zavod Sploh, ZASCD 020) — Kaučič credited for percussion, objects, amplified zither
  • Raztrgana Folklora Spomina = Torn Memories Of Folklore (2021, Zavod Sploh, ZASCD26) — with Martin Küchen

Late period and 70th anniversary documentation (2024)

Zlatko Kaučič @ 70 Inklings (2024, Fundacja Słuchaj!, FSR 27|2024) marks his 70th birthday with contributions from Barry Guy, Axel Dörner, Rodrigo Amado, Torben Snekkestad, Agustí Fernandez, and others.

Live In St. Johann (2024, Fundacja Słuchaj!, FSR 13/2024) documents a trio with Joëlle Léandre (bass, voice) and Elisabeth Harnik (piano, objects).
