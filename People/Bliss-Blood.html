Bliss Blood

Born Lisa Kay Peterson, Omaha, Nebraska. Vocalist, songwriter, ukulele, musical saw, percussion, guitar. Discogs artist ID: 256312.

Overview

Bliss Blood is an American musician and songwriter best known as a co-founder of the Houston noise-rock band Pain Teens (1985–1995) and as leader/vocalist of the New York Hawaiian-jazz group The Moonlighters (2) (formed 1998). After Pain Teens disbanded in 1995, she relocated to New York City and pivoted to ukulele-led jazz and original songwriting in a retro-twenties and thirties style. Her work spans noise-rock brutalism and intimate pre-war jazz, making her a distinctive figure across both scenes.

Blood co-founded Pain Teens in Houston in 1985 with guitarist Scott Ayers; the band grew from a punk alignment into a chaotic hybrid of noise, tape loops, found sound, sampling, exotic instrumentation, and experimentation, eventually signing to Trance Syndicate Records (King Coffey’s Austin label, home of Butthole Surfers). She contributed lyrics, vocals, percussion, and guitar across the full Pain Teens catalog. Following the band’s dissolution, she began playing solo ukulele gigs in New York, formed The Moonlighters in 1998, and built a parallel solo partnership with guitarist Al Street. In 2001 she appeared on Angels of Light’s How I Loved You, contributing backing vocals, ukulele, and musical saw alongside Michael Gira and his regular collaborators.

Name variations on releases: B. Blood, Bliss, Blood.
