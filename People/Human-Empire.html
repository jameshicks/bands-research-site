Human Empire

Hamburg-based graphic design and illustration studio. Discogs artist ID: 1846438. Website: humanempire.com (formerly humanempire.de).

Founded in 2003 by Jan Kruse (2) and Malte Kaune. Operated primarily as a sleeve design studio serving the European indie-electronic scene from the mid-2000s onward, with the bulk of their credited Discogs work concentrated on Morr Music releases between 2004 and 2008. Also worked with Bureau B, A Number Of Small Things, and smaller German indie labels.

Discogs credits Human Empire as a single artist entity; on some releases the credit appears as www.humanempire.de or www.humanempire.com (early credits used the .de domain; later ones switched to .com). On the Duo505: Late (2004) and the Faust (7): C’est Com… Com… Compliqué (2009) releases, Jan Kruse (2) is credited separately alongside Human Empire in the same artwork role, suggesting the two credits are sometimes used interchangeably for the same work.

Key Labels and Client Relationships

Morr Music (primary client, 2004–2008)

Human Empire’s dominant client. At least 11 of their 16 Discogs credits are on Morr Music releases. They designed covers for a wide cross-section of the label’s mid-2000s roster:

  • Duo505 — Late (2004)
  • Lali Puna — I Thought I Was Over That (2005)
  • Populous — Queue For Love (2005)
  • B. Fleischmann — The Humbucking Coil (2006)
  • ISAN — Plans Drawn In Pencil (2006)
  • Fat Jon & Styrofoam — The Same Channel (2006)
  • Couch — Figur 5 (2006)
  • Electric President — s/t (2006)
  • People Press Play — People Press Play (2007)
  • Seabear — The Ghost That Carried Us Away (2007)
  • Populous & Short Stories — Drawn In Basic (2008)

This run spans the label’s peak activity years (roughly 2004–2008) and covers most of the label’s key artists in that window. Human Empire’s clean, typographic aesthetic became closely associated with Morr Music’s visual identity during this period.

Bureau B

One credit: Faust (7), C’est Com… Com… Compliqué (2009). Bureau B is a Hamburg-based label (sublabel of Tapete Records), suggesting a local Hamburg connection. Label ID: 111528.

A Number Of Small Things

One credit: ISAN, Trois Gymnopedies (2006). This is a sublabel / sister imprint associated with Morr Music; label ID: unknown from this session. A Number Of Small Things page exists in the vault.

Loob Musik / Hafenschlamm Rekords / Insular

Smaller German indie labels with one credit each (2009 and 2012). These represent the studio’s later, more sporadic Discogs-visible work after the main Morr Music run concluded.

Groove Magazine

Credited with illustration and design on Groove #98 / N°7 (2005), a German electronic music magazine compilation. This suggests the studio’s work extended into editorial/print design beyond just record sleeves, though no other magazine credits appear in Discogs.

Notable Individual Releases

Duo505 — Late (2004, Morr Music) Human Empire’s first Discogs-credited release. The cover credit is listed as www.humanempire.de. Jan Kruse (2) is credited separately in the same Cover role. B. Fleischmann arranged the record; Christoph Amann recorded, mixed, and mastered.

B. Fleischmann — The Humbucking Coil (2006, Charhizma / Morr Music) Co-released on Charhizma and Morr Music. Human Empire credited for Cover; Christof Kurzmann and Christoph Amann co-produced with Fleischmann.

ISAN — Plans Drawn In Pencil (2006, Morr Music) Cover design. Antony Ryan and Robin Saville are the ISAN duo.

ISAN — Trois Gymnopedies (2006, A Number Of Small Things) Cover design on this Erik Satie reinterpretation EP, on the Morr Music sister imprint.

Faust (7) — C’est Com… Com… Compliqué (2009, Bureau B) Artwork credited to both Human Empire and Jan Kruse (2) in the same role — a pattern also seen on the Duo505 Late (2004) credit, suggesting the studio name and the principal’s name were used interchangeably at times.

Klez.e — Vom Feuer Der Gaben (2009, Loob Musik) Artwork credit among a large collective of designers on this release; both Human Empire and Jan Kruse (2) listed.

Open Questions

  • Founding date precision: Discogs profile states “Founded in 2003” but the earliest Discogs credit is 2004. Pre-2004 work is not reflected in Discogs.
  • Malte Kaune: Co-founder per the profile, but not credited independently on any Discogs release. No separate Discogs artist entry found.
  • Relationship between studio credits and Jan Kruse (2) solo credits: Jan Kruse (2) has pre-2003 credits (ISAN covers 1999–2004) that predate the founding of Human Empire. On some 2004+ releases (Duo505 Late, Faust (7), Klez.e) both names appear — it’s unclear whether these are genuinely joint credits or redundant double-crediting.
  • Non-Discogs clients: humanempire.com almost certainly documents a much larger body of design work (poster design, merch, editorial) that is outside Discogs scope. No web research done.
  • Post-2012 activity: The last Discogs credit is 2012. Whether the studio is still active and what they’ve been working on since is unknown from Discogs.
  • Frisky He Said (B. Fleischmann): Mentioned in the research brief as a Human Empire-designed release, but no Discogs credit for this title appears under Human Empire (ID 1846438). Either it’s credited differently, on a pressing not in the DB, or the brief may be referring to a different release.
