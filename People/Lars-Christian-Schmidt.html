Lars Christian Schmidt

German musician based in Düsseldorf; co-founder and primary songwriter for Subterfuge (5). Discogs artist ID: 2127645. Also records under the name variation Lars Schmidt. Active since at least 1992.

Overview

Lars Christian Schmidt is one of two core songwriters in Subterfuge (5), the Düsseldorf indie pop band he has co-led with Thomas Baumhoff since 1992. He plays guitar, bass, vocals, synthesizer, and electric piano (Wurlitzer) across the band’s catalog. Outside Subterfuge, he co-founded the collaborative project Quent (2) with Cologne musician Veit König (of Mikrovolt) around 2006, releasing on Less Records; and launched the lockdown homerecording project The Radio Field around 2020, which expanded into a full band and has released an EP and two albums (2022–2025) on Less Records / Subjangle. He also does visual work — credited with artwork on Quent (2)‘s 2020 debut album and on The Radio Field releases.

On Subterfuge releases through 2001, he is credited in Discogs under his full name “Lars Christian Schmidt.” On the 1997 split 7” with Soap (3) and on The Radio Field releases he is credited as “Lars Schmidt” (name variation).

Bands & Projects

Subterfuge (5) — 1992–present

Subterfuge (5) is Schmidt’s primary band. He and Thomas Baumhoff co-write the songs, both playing guitar and singing leads; Schmidt additionally handles bass, synthesizer, and electric piano (Wurlitzer). The band’s core lineup through the 2000s also included Kai Blankenberg (bass/keyboards/mastering) and Daniel Klingen (drums); earlier releases feature Mark Specht on drums. See Subterfuge (5) for the full band article.

Quent (2) — formed 2006

Quent (2) is a collaboration between Schmidt (Düsseldorf / Subterfuge) and Veit König (Cologne, associated with Mikrovolt). Formed in 2006; released on Less Records. Discogs ID: 7714223. Two releases documented on Discogs: the 2020 album Quent and single Batteriebetrieb (both Less Records). On the Quent album Schmidt is credited with Band, Composed By, and Design; Kai Blankenberg mastered.

The Radio Field — 2020–present

The Radio Field started as a lockdown homerecording project by Schmidt and quickly became a full band. Jangle pop from Düsseldorf. Discogs ID: 12073771. Released on Less Records and Subjangle. Members on released recordings: Schmidt (guitar, bass, vocals, artwork, songwriter), Mark Specht (drums — an And Again-era Subterfuge member returning), Christoph Schneider (2) (bass, mixed by on Don’ts And Dos), Michael Krannich (performer), Philipp Breuer (guitar, vocals), and Hanne-Lotta Blum (vocals, brass). Kai Blankenberg masters; Lorenz Naumann engineered the Simple EP.
