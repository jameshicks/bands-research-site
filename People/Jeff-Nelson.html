Jeff Nelson

Born: Jeffrey K. Nelson Discogs ID: 386114 Alias: Drive-Thru Nelson Active as performer: 1980–1992; returned ca. 2007 (Fast Piece Of Furniture) Origin: Washington, DC (later Toledo, Ohio) Role: Drummer, label co-founder, graphic designer

Co-founder of Dischord Records alongside Ian MacKaye; drummer in The Teen Idles and Minor Threat; producer and graphic designer across the Dischord catalog. Brother of Brian King Nelson.

Overview

Jeff Nelson grew up in Washington, DC, where as a teenager he formed The Slinkees (ca. 1979) with MacKaye and Geordie Grindle — the immediate precursor to The Teen Idles. When The Slinkees dissolved after vocalist Mark Sullivan left for college, the remaining members recruited Nathan Strejcek and became The Teen Idles in 1979–80.

The Teen Idles toured California in summer 1980 and returned to DC flush with $600 in door money — the seed capital for Dischord. Nelson and MacKaye used the funds to press the Teen Idles’ Minor Disturbance EP (1980), Dischord’s inaugural release. Nelson designed the sleeve, establishing the visual identity he would carry through most of the early Dischord catalog.

After The Teen Idles dissolved in late 1980, Nelson co-founded Minor Threat with MacKaye (vocals), Brian Baker (bass), and Lyle Preslar (guitar). Minor Threat became the defining band of the American hardcore/straight edge movement. Nelson drummed on every studio release: the Minor Threat EP (1981), In My Eyes (1981), Out Of Step (1983), and the posthumous Salad Days EP (1985). He also took on graphics for those releases and produced/co-produced several of them.

When Minor Threat went on hiatus in 1981–82, Nelson and MacKaye formed the short-lived Skewbald - Grand Union with Eddie Janney and John Falls, recording a demo with Don Zientara that was later archived as Dischord’s 50th release (1991).

Minor Threat disbanded definitively in 1983. Nelson formed Three (3) (listed on Discogs as Three (3)) and later The High-Back Chairs, continuing to drum and handle design on both bands’ releases for Dischord. In 1986 he also contributed drums, gum-chewing, and vocals to Egg Hunt’s Me And You single, the record that bridged MacKaye’s period between Embrace and Fugazi.

By 1992 Nelson had retired from performing; he dissolved The High-Back Chairs and concentrated on Dischord operations and a smaller label he founded, Adult Swim Records. He continued contributing graphics and design to Dischord releases through the 1990s and 2000s.

In 2003 Nelson relocated from DC to Toledo, Ohio, retaining his stake in Dischord. He returned to performing around 2007, drumming for Fast Piece Of Furniture, which released Adventures In Contentment that year on Adult Swim Records / Dischord.

Nelson was also a member of The Slinkees, Rozzlyn Rangers, and Senator Flux.

Dischord Records Role

Co-founder alongside Ian MacKaye in 1980. Nelson and MacKaye started the label to release the Teen Idles’ debut EP using money the band had earned on its California tour. From the beginning Nelson served as the label’s primary graphic artist, designing sleeves and coordinating visual identity for a large portion of the catalog.

The Dischord Records file documents 92 design credits for Nelson across the label’s catalog. He also operated Adult Swim Records (Discogs ID: 90599), a smaller imprint he founded independently; it released material by Rozzlyn Rangers, The High-Back Chairs, Fast Piece Of Furniture, and others.

In 2003 Nelson moved to Toledo, Ohio but retained his co-ownership stake in Dischord.
