Lomond Campbell

Scottish musician, songwriter, and producer. Discogs artist IDs: 3386249 (Lomond Campbell), 424080 (Ziggy campbell). Also releases and records as Ziggy Campbell — a separate Discogs entry that cross-lists “Lomond Campbell” as an alias, confirming both names map to the same person.

Overview

Lomond Campbell is a Scottish musician and producer whose career spans solo work, band membership, and production for other artists. He first appeared on Discogs in 2006 under the Ziggy Campbell name via Fence Records, the Fife-based community label run by King Creosote. Under that alias he was a member of the art-pop band Found (Discogs: 424084) alongside Tommy Perman, Kev Sim, and Alan Stockdale, releasing on Chemikal Underground (2011’s Factorycraft, 2011’s Machine Age Dancing) and contributing to Fence Records compilations and other label releases. Found also released Terra Nova (2016) on Chemikal Underground, their final album.

Parallel to his Found work, Campbell has maintained a solo career under his given name Lomond Campbell. His early solo records came out on the Dewar House imprint and The Narrows (2013). He then moved to Triassic Tusk Records — a label he appears to operate himself — for the LP Black River Promise (2016, later picked up by Heavenly Recordings in 2017) and subsequent releases. His 2020–2021 LP LŪP was initially self-released before a proper issue on One Little Independent Records. He continued with Under This Hunger Moon We Fell (2022) and Lost Loops (2022) on One Little Independent, and Transmission Loss (2025), also on One Little Independent.

From 2022 onward Campbell has been a close collaborator of Scottish artist Kathryn Joseph, co-producing her albums on Rock Action Records and contributing guitar, modular synthesizer, drum programming, design, and layout. He also remixed her 2022 album For You Who Are The Wronged in full (released as an EP in 2024). Outside that partnership, he has produced for TeenCanteen (2014) and provided additional recording for King Creosote’s I Des (2023, Domino) and Any Port In A Storm (2025, Domino). In 2025 he provided two remixes for Dot Allison’s Subconsciousology on Sonic Cathedral.
