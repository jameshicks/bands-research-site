Overview

Boff Whalley (born Allan Mark Whalley) is a musician, author, and playwright from Yorkshire, best known as a vocalist and guitarist with Chumbawamba, the anarchist collective he shared with Neil Ferguson, Harry Hamer, Lou Watts, Danbert Nobacon, Alice Nutter, Dunstan Bruce, and others. His recorded output dates from 1983, with early releases on the collective’s own Agit Prop imprint before the band moved to One Little Indian and then Virgin for their commercial breakthrough in the mid-1990s. Since the mid-2000s he has remained active through the No Masters label, with credits running to at least 2024. Alongside his music career he has worked as an author and playwright.
