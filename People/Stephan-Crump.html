Stephan Crump

American bassist, composer, and producer. Discogs artist ID: 279632. Married to artist ID 279631 (unnamed in DB). Active from at least 1995. Website: stephancrump.com.

Name variations: S. Crump, Stephen Crump, Stephan Crumblé Crump, ステファン・クランプ.

Biography

Stephan Crump has been a working bassist across the spectrum of creative jazz since the mid-1990s, most prominently as a long-standing member of the Vijay Iyer Trio — a cornerstone of American jazz’s post-millennium wave — and as co-leader of his own ensembles. He plays acoustic and electric bass and frequently handles engineering, mixing, and production on his own records.

Early Credits (1995–2003)

His earliest Discogs credits are as bassist on Big Ass Truck’s album (Upstart Records, 1995), the Vijay Iyer Quartet’s Panoptic Modes (Red Giant, 2001), and Vijay Iyer’s Blood Sutra (Artists House, 2003). The Iyer connection became central: Crump was bassist on Iyer’s acclaimed In What Language? (Pi Recordings, 2003) and Tactiles (Pi Recordings, 2003), helping establish the Pi Recordings nexus around Iyer’s work.

Vijay Iyer Trio (2001–2017+)

Crump was one of the core members of the Vijay Iyer Trio alongside pianist Vijay Iyer and drummer Marcus Gilmore. The trio recorded the widely-praised Historicity (ACT, 2009) and Accelerando (ACT, 2012), both engineered, mixed, and mastered by a consistent team (Scotty Hard on mixing; Mike Fossenkemper mastering). He also appeared in the Vijay Iyer Sextet (artist ID: 5922897). The Iyer-Crump-Gilmore trio was one of the most critically discussed jazz piano trios of the 2000s–2010s.

Stephan Crump’s Rosetta Trio

Crump leads Stephan Crump’s Rosetta Trio (Discogs artist ID: 4858868), a guitar-bass-guitar trio with Liberty Ellman (acoustic guitar) and Jamie Fox (electric guitar). Releases on Sunnyside Communications:

  • Thwirl (2013, Sunnyside Communications) — all music by Crump; Crump recorded, mixed, and engineered; Liberty Ellman also mixed.
  • Outliers (2019, Papillon Sounds) — Crump produced and recorded; mastered by Scott Hull.

Borderlands Trio (2017–present)

Crump is a co-leader and bassist in the Borderlands Trio (Discogs artist ID: 6010043), a piano-bass-drums trio with pianist Kris Davis and drummer Eric McPherson. Released Asteroidea on Intakt Records (2017). On that record, Crump served as bassist, composer (all members contributed compositions), mixer, and producer.

Planktonic Finales / Channels Trio with Laubrock & Smythe (2017–2019)

Crump co-led a trio with saxophonist Ingrid Laubrock and pianist Cory Smythe, releasing two albums on Intakt Records:

  • Planktonic Finales (2017, Intakt) — recorded by Ryan Streber; mastered by Liberty Ellman; Crump mixed, played bass, composed, and co-produced (alongside Laubrock, Smythe, and Patrik Landolt of Intakt). Photography by Crump and Laubrock; plankton photography by Richard A. Ingebrigtsen.
  • Channels (2019, Intakt) — recorded by Martin Pearson at Radio SRF 2 Kultur (Swiss radio); mastered by Liberty Ellman; Cory Smythe also mixed. Produced by Anja Illmaier, Florian Keller (5), Patrik Landolt, and Peter Bürli.

Secret Keeper (with Mary Halvorson)

Crump is a member of Secret Keeper (Discogs artist ID: 3178253), a duo with guitarist Mary Halvorson. The duo’s Emerge (Intakt, 2015) listed Crump as bassist, recording engineer, mixer, cover photographer, and co-producer.

Solo Output and Papillon Sounds

Crump has released solo and leader records on his own label, Papillon Sounds:

  • Rhombal (2016, Papillon Sounds) — bass solo or small ensemble; mixed by Ron St. Germain; with Tyshawn Sorey (drums), Ellery Eskelin (tenor saxophone), Adam O’Farrill (trumpet).
  • Outliers (2019) — Rosetta Trio
  • Rocket Love (2022, Papillon Sounds)
  • Slow Water (2024, Papillon Sounds) — produced by Crump; ensemble includes Jacob Garchik (trombone), Kenny Warren (trumpet), Patricia Brennan (vibraphone), Joanna Mattrey (viola), Yuniya Edi Kwon (violin); mixed by Eli Crews.

Other Session Work

Notable session credits include:

  • Linger (2004, Hybrid Recordings) — Crump credited with electric bass, acoustic bass, arranged strings, glockenspiel, balafon, berimbau, mbira, production, and recording; also co-produced Me Be Me (2004, Hybrid Recordings).
  • Rez Abbasi’s Reimagining (Pi Recordings, 2005) — double bass
  • Joel Harrison’s Harrison On Harrison (HighNote, 2005)
  • Revisions - Songs Of Stevie Wonder (Chesky Records, 2009)
  • Break Stuff by Vijay Iyer Sextet (ECM, 2015)
  • Far From Over (ECM, 2017) — likely Vijay Iyer Sextet
  • Still Point: Turning World (Whirlwind Recordings, 2019)
  • Love Came Down (Sweet Soul Records, Japan, 2019)
