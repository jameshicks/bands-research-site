Daniel Miller

Daniel Otto Joachim Miller (born 14 February 1951, Marylebone, London) is a British record producer, musician, and founder of Mute. Discogs artist ID: 9857. Records under the aliases The Normal (ID: 2704), Silicon Teens (ID: 79941), Darryl, Diane (3), Dr. Death (3), Jacki (2), Larry Least, and Paul (32). Member of Duet Emmo (with Bruce Gilbert and Graham Lewis), Sunroof (with Gareth Jones), Come (ID: 58512), Djxdj (ID: 69500), and Populist (2) (ID: 7993116). Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Miller_(music_producer)

Overview

Miller is the son of two Austrian-Jewish refugees — actor Martin Miller and Hannah Norbert-Miller. He studied film and television at Guildford School of Art (1969–1972), developing an interest in synthesizer music and the German krautrock bands (Can, Faust, NEU!, Kraftwerk). He worked as a DJ in Switzerland before returning to England at the height of punk, drawn by its DIY energy. The combination of punk attitude and influence from Throbbing Gristle and Cabaret Voltaire prompted him to buy a miniKORG 700S synthesizer and a TEAC four-track, leading to the creation of The Normal. After the success of The Normal’s debut single, demo tapes began arriving at the address printed on the sleeve — the birth of Mute Records as a going concern. The label grew from Miller’s North-West London flat into an international operation by the early 1980s on the back of Depeche Mode and Yazoo. Miller sold Mute to EMI in 2003 but bought it back in 2010 and relaunched it as an independent. In 2017 he co-authored Mute: A Visual Document (Thames & Hudson) with Terry Burrows.

“Warm Leatherette” (Mute 001, May 1978) was the label’s first release — 2,000 copies initially pressed via Rough Trade. The B-side “T.V.O.D.” and A-side were written solely by Miller. The sleeve designer (uncredited on original) was Simone Grant. Following the single Miller performed live as a duo with Robert Rental, including shows with Throbbing Gristle and Cabaret Voltaire, and a Rough Trade tour supporting Stiff Little Fingers.

Silicon Teens

Silicon Teens was a fictitious four-person synth-pop band conceived by Miller. The four persona names — Darryl (played by Frank Tovey per Wikipedia; Miller also credited uncredited vocals/synth/percussion on LP), Jacki, Paul, and Diane — are all Miller aliases. Engineer on all sessions: Eric Radcliffe at Blackwing Studios (and Eric Hine at Magritte). “Larry Least” (another Miller alias) credited as producer on Music For Parties.

Duet Emmo

One-off collaborative project with Bruce Gilbert and Graham Lewis (both of Wire). The name is an anagram of Mute and Dome (Lewis/Gilbert’s duo project). All three had been using Blackwing Studios with shared engineer Eric Radcliffe. Engineered by Radcliffe and John Fryer.

Sunroof

Ongoing project with Mute-associated producer Gareth Jones. Started with a remix of “Oh Yeah” for the 1997 Can remix album Sacrilege (Mute). Also remixed Neu!, Kreidler, Pizzicato Five, Goldfrapp, Faust, MGMT, Charles Wilp, Ellis Island Sound, Future Bible Heroes, To Rococo Rot. Three albums on Mute:
