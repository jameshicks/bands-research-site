Overview

A musician based in Gent, Belgium, De Blonde works across experimental, drone, ambient, and free improvisation idioms, contributing as performer, electronics operator, and mastering/mixing engineer. He is a member of several ensembles — 1% Slasher, Burda Moden, Cinéma Invisible, De Portables, and Dead End (27) — giving him a broad footprint across the Belgian underground and electropop scenes. Active since 2001, his catalogue spans releases on (K-RAA-K)³ and Knotwilg as his primary labels, with recurrent collaborators including Hellvete, Köhn, and Michael Beckett.
