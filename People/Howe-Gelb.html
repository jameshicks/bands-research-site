Howe Gelb

Real name: Howard Gelb
Born: October 22, 1956, Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania
Base: Tucson, Arizona
Discogs artist ID: 179559
Roles: Vocalist, guitarist, pianist; record producer

Howard “Howe” Gelb is an American singer-songwriter, musician, and record producer. He is the founder and constant creative centre of Giant Sand, the Tucson-based alternative country / roots rock collective that began as Giant Sandworms in the late 1970s and has operated, with shifting membership, ever since. Outside Giant Sand, Gelb has maintained a substantial solo career across several labels and issued a long-running series of informal piano recordings (“Some Piano” cassettes/releases). He has collaborated with flamenco musicians in Spain (Alegrías, 2010), with Ottawa-based gospel choirs (‘Sno Angel Like You, 2006), with Austrian post-rock group Radian (Radian Verses Howe Gelb, 2014), and with Belgian chamber ensemble The Colorist Orchestra and Pieta Brown (Not On The Map, 2021, Dangerbird Records). He is also active as a film actor and soundtrack composer. His brother is Ricky Gelb. (Wikipedia: Howe Gelb)
