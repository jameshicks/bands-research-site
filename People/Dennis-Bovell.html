Dennis Bovell

Dub producer, musician, songwriter. Born 1953, Barbados; raised in London.

Overview

One of the most important British dub and reggae producers. Also played in Matumbi. Production work stretched into post-punk and experimental music — the Slits, Linton Kwesi Johnson, The Fall, and others.

Key Credits

  • The Pop Group — produced Y (Radar, 1979) and co-produced “She Is Beyond Good And Evil” (1979); 50 shared releases
  • The Slits — produced Cut (Island, 1979)
  • Linton Kwesi Johnson — produced multiple LKJ albums
  • Lizzy Mercier Descloux — mixed Calypso Moguls (Polydor, France, 1986)
  • The Fall — produced Grotesque (Rough Trade, 1980)
