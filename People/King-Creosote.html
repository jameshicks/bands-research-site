King Creosote

Discogs ID: 360659
Real name: Kenny Anderson
Born: January 1967
Origin: Fife, Scotland (East Neuk / Anstruther area)
Active: 1997–present
Labels: Fence Records, Domino, Names (mid-2000s)
Aliases: Kwaing Creasite (Discogs ID 429371); also issued releases as Jataak Mulk & Hudiv Ahken, KY-10, King Menace

Singer-songwriter and founder of the Fence Records collective. Anderson began releasing self-issued cassettes and CDRs under the King Creosote name in 1997 (FNC 01: Queen Of Brush Country), establishing a prolific lo-fi folk/acoustic practice rooted in the East Neuk of Fife. He is the brother of Lone Pigeon (Gordon Anderson). He is also a member of Scottish-Canadian band The Burns Unit.
