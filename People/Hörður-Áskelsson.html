Hörður Áskelsson

Discogs ID: 996768 Role: Conductor, organist, cantor Based: Reykjavík, Iceland Name variations: H.Á.

Icelandic conductor and cantor. Founder and permanent artistic director of Schola Cantorum Reykjavík (est. 1996). Serves as cantor at Hallgrímskirkja (Hallgrím’s Church), Reykjavík’s dominant Lutheran landmark. Appears on all Schola Cantorum Reykjavík Discogs releases from 1999 to 2023 — 13 shared credits in total. Also plays organ on selected Schola recordings (e.g. Sálmar Í Gleði, track 12).
