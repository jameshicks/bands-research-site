Overview

Gavin Friday (born Fionán Martin Hanvey, Dublin, 1959) is an Irish singer-songwriter, composer, actor, and painter whose Discogs credits number nearly 660 across a career stretching from 1981 to the present. His recorded output — concentrated in the 1990s and spanning pop rock, new wave, experimental, and soundtrack work — has been released primarily on Island Records. His most substantial collaborative relationships are with U2 and its individual members: he shares over 170 credits with Bono and more than 120 each with The Edge, Adam Clayton, and Larry Mullen, alongside frequent work with producers Maurice Seezer and Flood. Virgin Prunes appears as one of his closest credited collaborators with 66 shared works, though the nature of that relationship — membership or close association — is not resolved by the Discogs data alone.
