Kurtis Mantronik

DJ, producer. Born Kurtis el Khaleel, 1965, Jamaica; raised in New York City and Canada.

Overview

One of the pioneering hip-hop/electro producers. As Mantronix (with MC Tee), produced a string of influential records in the mid-1980s that married hip-hop rhythms to electro and house textures. Also worked as A&R.

Key Role

  • A&R executive at Sleeping Bag Records — the label co-founded by Arthur Russell, William Socolov, and Juggy Gales. Mantronik both worked in A&R and recorded for the label with Mantronix.
  • Mantronix — debut album on Sleeping Bag Records (1985); Music Madness (1986)
