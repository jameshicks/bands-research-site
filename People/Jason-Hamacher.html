Jason Hamacher

Discogs artist ID: 576445. American drummer, photographer, and documentarian based in Washington DC. Best known as the drummer for Frodus and Decahedron / The Black Sea, the latter a project with Joe Lally (of Fugazi) and Shelby Cinca. Also drummed for DC straight-edge band Battery (3), Florida grindcore/chaotic hardcore act Combatwoundedveteran, and sang under the pseudonym Ponan in the DC punk band Mancake. Post-music career shifted to documentary work and field recording, most notably capturing ancient Syriac Christian chant in Aleppo, Syria through his company Lost Origin Productions LLC.

Aliases: Ponan (vocals alias used in Mancake). Name variations: Hamacher, Hamacher Jason, Jason H., Jason Hamaker, Jay Rotten.

Overview

Hamacher came up in the Washington DC and surrounding hardcore/post-hardcore scene in the early 1990s. He joined Frodus — formed with Shelby Cinca — by the time of their early EPs on Gnome Records (2). Through the mid-to-late 1990s he was one of the busiest drummers in the DC/Florida chaotic hardcore orbit: Frodus on Tooth & Nail and Lovitt Records, Battery (3) on Conversion and Revelation Records, and guest credits with Reversal Of Man and Combatwoundedveteran.

After Frodus dissolved, Hamacher co-founded Decahedron / The Black Sea with Cinca and Joe Lally (bass), releasing The Black Sea EP (2002, Lovitt Records) and the full-length Disconnection_Imminent (2004, Lovitt Records). He also co-produced a self-titled EP for Majority Rule (1999, Submit Records) and performed with Regents alongside J. Robbins and David NeSmith of Sleepytime Trio.

Beyond music, Hamacher became an accomplished photographer and documentary filmmaker. He filmed the 2008 International Documentary Challenge-winning short Old Soul and subsequently traveled to Syria to document the ancient Aramaic-speaking Syrian Orthodox Church, producing field recordings of early Christian chant released on the Lost Origin Sound Series / Electric Cowbell Records imprint.
