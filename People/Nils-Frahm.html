Nils Frahm

German pianist and composer. Based in Berlin. Known for piano-based ambient and contemporary classical work; releases on Erased Tapes. Discogs artist ID: 411447. One of the three primary mastering engineers across the Miasmah catalog, credited on 27 releases (alongside Loop-o and Andreas Tilliander).

Appears on The Silence Set’s Teeth Out (Mini50 Records, 2015), same release that credits Heather Woods Broderick for flute and vocals.

Also credited for Instrumentation By on Plus (Morr Music, 2018) by Future 3 operating as System — the same three-member Danish trio (Thomas Knak, Anders Remmer, Jesper Skaaning).
