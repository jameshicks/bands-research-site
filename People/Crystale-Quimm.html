Overview

Crystale Quimm is a stage name and project of Diana Rogerson, whose principal Discogs alias is Chrystal Belle Scrodd — the biographical profile below is Diana Rogerson’s. The Crystale Quimm catalogue is rooted in the 1980s UK experimental-industrial underground: two releases on L.A.Y.L.A.H. Antirecords between 1984 and 1988, with Performer credits resurfacing on Durtro, Jnana Records, and Cashen’s Gap from 2008 through 2025. The density of shared credits with David Tibet, Steven Stapleton, Current 93, Steve Ignorant, and Nurse With Wound — ranging from 3 to 19 overlapping releases — positions this alias squarely in the mid-1980s post-industrial network that coalesced around L.A.Y.L.A.H. and the early Current 93 era.

Notes (web)

How Diana Rogerson entered the L.A.Y.L.A.H. / Current 93 circle

The route was through Steven Stapleton. Stapleton met David Tibet at the Equinox Event in Camden in June 1983 — a showcase of industrial-inclined performers — specifically in the pub after Stapleton had left the venue in anger. Tibet revealed he was a fan; from that point the two contributed to most of each other’s outputs. This is the bridge that brought Rogerson into the orbit: she met Stapleton in 1984 as a fellow artist working in London (she was part of Fistfuck, an S&M performance art duo with Jill Westwood), and by virtue of that relationship became a participant in the network already established between Stapleton/Nurse With Wound and Tibet/Current 93. (The Quietus — “A Repertoire Of Mistakes: The Strange World Of… Nurse With Wound”, accessed 2026-11-06)

On L.A.Y.L.A.H. Antirecords: Rogerson appears as Crystale Quimm on two 1984 L.A.Y.L.A.H. releases: Brained By Falling Masonry (a Nurse With Wound release also featuring David Tibet and Jim Thirlwell) and Dogs Blood Rising (Current 93, also featuring Steven Stapleton). These credits confirm her active role as performer in the L.A.Y.L.A.H. micro-network at its earliest point. No source located explains how she came to hold the Crystale Quimm alias specifically. (Brainwashed discography — Brained By Falling Masonry, accessed 2026-11-06; Brainwashed discography — Dogs Blood Rising, accessed 2026-11-06)

Nature of collaboration with David Tibet: Rogerson appears in the personnel of Dogs Blood Rising (Current 93, 1984) and on subsequent Current 93 releases through her shared network with Stapleton and Tibet. The Brainwashed entry for her solo album The Inevitable Chrystal Belle Scrodd Record (United Dairies, 1985) notes she collaborated with Current 93 “both live and on record.” (Brainwashed — The Inevitable Chrystal Belle Scrodd Record, accessed 2026-11-06)

Nature of collaboration with Steve Ignorant: Ignorant and Rogerson share credits on Dogs Blood Rising (1984). Ignorant’s specific contribution to that record is documented: he delivered a spoken/shouted vocal performance on the track “Falling Back In Fields of Rape,” a prose-poem set to dark industrial backing. Rogerson’s parallel role on the same recording is listed in personnel but not separately described in available sources. No source found describes direct collaboration between Rogerson and Ignorant outside of this release. (search result snippet citing progarchives and Brainwashed review; Wikipedia — Dogs Blood Rising, as of 2026-11-06)
