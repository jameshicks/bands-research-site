Hamid Drake

Discogs ID: 273086
Real name: Hamid Drake
Born: August 3, 1955, Monroe, Louisiana, USA
Based: Chicago, Illinois, USA

American jazz drummer and percussionist. In addition to the drum set, Drake plays frame drum, goblet drums, and a wide range of hand percussion. One of the central figures in the post-1980 Chicago free jazz scene and a prolific international collaborator. His playing integrates free improvisation with African, Caribbean, and South Asian rhythmic traditions; his reggae and dub sensibilities are explicit in projects like Spaceways Incorporated and sessions with Bill Laswell.

Early Work (1987–1993)

The earliest primary-credited releases in Discogs are from 1987: Jazz Africa (NEC Avenue, Japan) and La Luna (Olufsen Records, Denmark), suggesting early international gigging and recording. The titles signal the Afro-diasporic interests that run throughout his career.

Die Like A Dog Quartet (1994–)

The Die Like a dog quartet (Discogs ID 598165) is one of Drake’s most documented groups: Peter Brötzmann (reeds), Toshinori Kondo (trumpet, electronics), William Parker (bass), and Drake (drums, frame drum). Their debut, Die Like A Dog (Fragments Of Music, Life And Death Of Albert Ayler) (FMP CD 64, 1994, Germany), is a live recording that established the group’s rawness. The name and dedication to Albert Ayler sets the conceptual frame. The group’s complete FMP recordings were anthologised on The Complete FMP Recordings (Jazzwerkstatt, 2007).

Chicago Groups

DKV Trio (Discogs ID 326487) — Drake, Kent Kessler (bass), and Ken Vandermark (reeds). The name is their initials. Core Chicago free improvisation trio, active and recording extensively.

Peter Brötzmann Chicago Tentet (ID 393176) — Drake is a core member of Brötzmann’s large-ensemble Chicago formation, alongside Vandermark, Kessler, Mars Williams, and others.

Spaceways Incorporated (ID 351804) — Drake and Vandermark’s band that explicitly incorporates reggae and dub; one of the clearest expressions of Drake’s non-free-jazz interests.

Drake Duo (ID 12776612) — sustained duo with Peter Brötzmann, documented separately from the larger groups.

Fred Anderson Partnership

Fred Anderson (ID 582509) — Chicago tenor saxophonist, founder of the Velvet Lounge — is Drake’s most prominent Chicago-based co-leader partner (10 shared releases). Their clearest document is From The River To The Ocean (Thrill Jockey, thrill 183, 2007): a session recorded by John McEntire featuring Harrison Bankhead (bass/cello/piano), Joshua Abrams (bass/guimbri), and Jeff Parker (guitar). Anderson writes most of the material; Drake contributes frame drum on the title track alongside standard drumset work throughout.

William Parker

William Parker (ID 222600) — New York bassist — is Drake’s single most frequent collaborator with 54 shared releases. Their connection runs through the Die Like a dog quartet, the Indigo Trio, and numerous William Parker-led groups (Quartet, Violin Trio, Orchestra, Double Quartet, and others). Parker’s vast network places Drake at the centre of the New York–Chicago free jazz interchange.

Global Percussion Projects

Hu Vibrational (ID 41589) and Mandingo Griot Society (ID 1867445) — both with Adam Rudolph (ID 8590), percussionist and composer. These projects foreground world music and polyrhythm over free jazz.

Material (ID 8796) — Bill Laswell’s studio/live project. Drake’s membership connects him to the dub-rock-jazz fusion that Laswell developed from the late 1980s onward.

PainKiller (2) (ID 69896) — grindcore-meets-free-jazz trio; Drake is a member alongside noise/free music contexts.
