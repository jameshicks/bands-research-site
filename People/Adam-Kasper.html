Overview

Adam Kasper is a Seattle-area record producer and engineer based at Robert Lang Studios, with over 2,000 Discogs credits spanning from 1990 across alternative rock, grunge, and hard rock. He is most closely associated with Pearl Jam and Soundgarden — virtually every member of both bands appears among his top collaborators — and worked frequently alongside producer Brendan O’Brien. His catalogue touches major labels including A&M, Epic, Geffen, Sony, and Sub Pop, placing him at the centre of Seattle rock’s commercial output through the 1990s and beyond. He is also credited as a member of the Dave Grohl metal project Probot and of L.S.D. Rexxx.
