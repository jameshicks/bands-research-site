Shoeb Ahmad

Discogs ID: 880656 Real name: Shoeb Ahmad Based: Canberra (later Sydney), Australia Active: 2006–present Roles: Guitar, vocals, laptop/computer, field recordings, keyboard, electronics, clarinet, synth

Australian musician and sound artist. Began with toy microphones and feedback manipulation, moving through minimal organ drone and punk experimentation before developing a practice centred on fragile tone structures and spacious songforms. Uses guitar, voice, field recordings, keyboard and laptop as core instruments.

Also performs and records as Sia Ahmad and Alicia Sparkles.

Name variations used on releases: Ahmad, SA, Shoeb, Shoeb Spartak, Sia Ahmad.

Biography

Shoeb Ahmad is based in Canberra. His solo practice since the mid-2000s emphasises delicate layered songwriting — guitar, voice, and electronics combined into what Discogs describes as “fragile tone structures and spacious songforms.” His debut solo album Roomsound (2006, hellosQuare Recordings) established the blueprint: solo writing, guitar, vocals, percussion, computer.

Alongside the solo work he co-founded Spartak (3) with drummer Evan Dorrian — a Canberra duo (later trio with bassist Matthew Lustri) producing largely improvised material ranging from glitchy free jazz to noise. Spartak released three albums between 2007 and 2014.

He is also a member of Tangents (3), the Sydney post-jazz/electronics ensemble featuring Ollie Bown, Peter Hollo, Adrian Klumpes, and Evan Dorrian. Tangents released four albums on Not Applicable / hellosQuare Recordings (2013) and Temporary Residence Limited (2016–2021). Ahmad’s role in Tangents spans guitar, vocals, and computer.

Later solo releases — Watch / Illuminate (2012, Mystery Plays Records), Quiver (2018, Art As Catharsis Records), Quiver Versions (2019, Provenance), A Body Full Of Tears (2020, self-released), and Facade (2021, self-released) — show a continued trajectory toward spare, personal songwriting.

He also contributed to Peter Hollo’s Young Man Old Man (2012, Poison City Records) as strings/electronics, and to Lawrence English’s Watch / Illuminate session as field recording contributor.
