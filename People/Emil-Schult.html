Emil Schult

German painter, graphic artist, and lyricist. Born 1946 in Cologne. Discogs artist ID: 42121. Long-time collaborator of Kraftwerk as cover artist, lyricist, and tour manager.

Overview

Schult met Kraftwerk after the recording of their first three albums and became a regular collaborator from Autobahn (1974) onward. His roles included:

  • Painting and designing album covers (Autobahn’s motorway landscape is his work)
  • Co-writing lyrics from Radio-Activity through Computer World
  • Serving as tour manager for live shows in the 1970s

He appears credited on Autobahn (Painting [Cover]), Trans-Europe Express (Artwork [Inner Sleeve, Poster]), and Electric Café (CGI Artist [Computer Graphics]), and as lyric co-author on multiple albums. After Kraftwerk, Schult continued as an independent artist; he also participated in the 3-D Catalogue project (credited on 2016–2017 releases with “Emil has researched extra contemporary drawings, graphics, and photographs” for the planned early-albums box, per Ralf Hütter).
