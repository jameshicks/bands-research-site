Sam Coomes

Born: April 23, 1964, Sherman, Texas
Active: 1987–present
Real name: Samuel J. Coomes
Discogs artist ID: 260123
Aliases: Blues Goblins, Fra. Treepig

Portland, Oregon-based multi-instrumentalist, songwriter, vocalist, and producer. Primary creative force in Quasi (2), the duo he co-founded with drummer Janet Weiss (his former spouse). Also a former member of Heatmiser, the mid-1990s Portland indie rock band that also featured Elliott Smith. Over a thirty-plus-year career Coomes has worked as a session player, producer, and engineer for a range of Pacific Northwest artists, most notably Built To Spill and The Go-Betweens.

Background

Coomes grew up in Texas and eventually settled in Portland, Oregon, where he became embedded in the indie rock scene of the late 1980s and 1990s. His primary instrument across most of his career has been the Rocksichord — a keyboard with a percussive, attack-heavy timbre that mimics guitar-like sustain — which became the sonic signature of Quasi. He also plays bass, guitar, piano, synthesizer, mellotron, and theremin.

Heatmiser (1993–1996)

Coomes joined Heatmiser as bassist for their final and most commercially successful album. The band had formed in Portland in 1991 around Elliott Smith and Neil Gust (guitars/vocals), Tony Lash (drums), and Brandt Peterson (bass). Peterson departed, and Coomes stepped in for Mic City Sons (1996, Caroline Records), produced by the band alongside Rob Schnapf and Tom Rothrock, who had also worked on Elliott Smith’s early solo records. Heatmiser disbanded in 1996, largely because of the rising profile of Elliott Smith’s solo work.

Quasi (1993–present)

Quasi is Coomes’s primary and longest-running project. He co-founded the duo with Janet Weiss around 1993; the two were married at the time and the band continued after their divorce. Coomes is the primary songwriter, handles keyboards/Rocksichord, bass (on record), and lead vocals, while Weiss plays drums and contributes vocals. Bassist Joanna Bolme joined the live lineup in later years.

The band’s sound is stark and raw by design — Coomes and Weiss intentionally strip arrangements down to the two of them, the Rocksichord creating a wall-of-sound illusion from a single instrument. Coomes writes the majority of the material; Weiss is credited on selected tracks beginning with The Sword Of God.

Solo Work

Coomes released the album Bugger Me in 2016 on No Quarter — a sparse, largely solo release with no additional credits listed on Discogs.

Session Work and Production Credits

Coomes has been a recurring presence as a session player and producer in the Portland music community, working repeatedly with Lawrence Crane (Jackpot! Recording Studio) and Doug Martsch of Built To Spill.
