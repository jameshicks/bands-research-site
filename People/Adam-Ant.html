Adam Ant

Vocalist, bandleader. Born Stuart Leslie Goddard, 3 November 1954, London.

Projects

  • Adam and the Ants — founder and frontman (1977–1982)
  • Adam Ant (solo) — 1982–present

Key Facts

  • Early Adam and the Ants lineup included members who went on to form The Monochrome Set — the split and overlap are documented in The Monochrome Set research notes.
  • Young Parisians (1978, Decca) — early single co-produced with Jo Julian, who also played piano. Personnel: Stuart Goddard (vocals/guitar), Matthew Ashman (guitar/piano), Andy Warren (bass), Dave Barbarossa (drums).
  • Long-running production/performance association with Jo Julian: Julian produced the Antmusic compilation (1994, Arcade) and played piano on the Antbox box set (2000, Columbia).
  • Early Ants were part of the first-wave UK punk scene; by 1979–80 the band had moved toward a more tribal/new romantic sound.
  • Malcolm McLaren briefly managed the Ants (1979–80) and effectively raided the band, taking most of the lineup to form Bow Wow Wow; the remaining Adam Ant recruited a new lineup and went on to massive commercial success.
  • “Antmusic” / Kings of the Wild Frontier (1980) — breakthrough; Prince Charming (1981) — UK #1 album.
