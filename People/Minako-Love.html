Minako Love

Vocalist. Discogs artist ID: 3283034. Also credited as “Minako” on sleeve.

Overview

Minako Love is a vocalist whose documented output sits entirely within the Black Jazz Consortium orbit on Soul People Music, Fred Peterkin’s New York deep house imprint. She appears on three releases spanning 2012–2015, all tied to the Codes And Metaphors album cycle. Her contribution to track 1, “Free Your Mind”, is the most prominent: it appears on the pre-release 12” Codes And Metaphors 1 (2012), on the Simbad remix of the same track on that same 12”, and then in its definitive form — with full Lyrics By and Vocals credits — on the Codes And Metaphors LP (2013). A second original contribution, “You Take Me Higher”, surfaces on the outtakes/reshapes 12” Recoded: Reshapes And Outtakes Pt.1 (2015).

Her name variation on all three releases is “Minako” (without the surname). No solo releases, no other primary credits, and no additional collaborations outside the BJ Consortium context are present in the Discogs database. The profile and realname fields are empty; her background is not documented at the Discogs level.
