Overview

Ray Barbee is a professional skateboarder and multi-instrumentalist from San Jose, California, born October 5, 1971, who built a parallel music career running alongside his skateboarding work. He plays guitar, electric bass, synthesizer, and percussion across styles ranging from downtempo and indie rock to free jazz and surf. His catalogue is concentrated in the 2000s through bands including Blktop Project, Black Chalk, and Coldwatercrane, with releases primarily on Japanese imprints Galaxia, Rush! Production, and AWDR/LR2 — a label alignment that persisted from 2001 into the 2020s. His most frequent musical partner across roughly two decades of recordings is Tommy Guerrero, another skater-musician from the same San Jose scene, with whom he shares twenty credits.
