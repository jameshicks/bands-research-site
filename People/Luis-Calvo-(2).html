Overview

Luis Calvo Soler is a Spanish music entrepreneur best known as the founder of Elefant Records, the Madrid-based indie label he has operated from 1991 through to the present. Alongside the label, he built much of the infrastructure of Spanish indie culture in the 1990s: editing the fanzines La Línea del Arco and Spiral, hosting the radio programme Viaje a los Sueños Polares with Joako Ezpeleta, and co-founding the Festival Independiente de Benicàssim — later the Festival Internacional de Benicàssim — with Ezpeleta and two unnamed partners. His 199 Discogs credits are concentrated almost entirely on Elefant releases across indie rock, indie pop and synth-pop, with notably deep involvement in Camera Obscura’s catalogue and recurring work alongside Swedish producer Jari Haapalainen.
