Bio

Steve Pearce is a British bassist, engineer, mixer, producer and songwriter. His earliest Discogs credit is as bassist on the Observers’ This Age / Suicide 7” (ST 2, The S+T Label, 1980), the Leicester post-punk band’s sole single. From there he went on to become one of London’s most in-demand session bassists over the following three decades.

His session credits span a wide range of major-label and high-profile recordings across pop, rock, and orchestral pop, including:

  • Van Morrison: Poetic Champions Compose (Mercury, 1987), Avalon Sunset (Mercury, 1989), Enlightenment (Polydor, 1990), Hymns To The Silence (Polydor, 1991)
  • Seal: Seal (ZTT, 1991), Seal II (ZTT) — credited as Musician
  • Enya: Watermark (1988, Idlewild — Blanco Y Negro/Sire, 1988)
  • Scott Walker: Tilt / The Drift (4AD, 2006)
  • Westlife: multiple albums 1999–2011 on RCA/BMG, including Westlife (1999), Coast To Coast (2000), World Of Our Own (2001)
  • Ronan Keating: multiple singles and albums (Polydor)
  • Gareth Gates: What My Heart Wants To Say (RCA, 2002)
  • Elvis Presley tribute work with the Royal Philharmonic Orchestra (Legacy/RCA, 2017)
  • The Beach Boys with the Royal Philharmonic Orchestra (Capitol, 2018)
  • Film work: Evita (Warner Bros., 1996), Trance OST (Universal, 2013), Doctor Strange OST (Hollywood Records, 2016)
  • Brother Strut (as a group member)

Also holds a mastering credit on the The Swinging Laurels’ Young Men Gone West / Book Early reissue (Lemon Recordings, 2015).
