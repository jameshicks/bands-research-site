Page McConnell

American multi-instrumentalist, keyboardist, and vocalist. Born May 17, 1963, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Discogs artist ID: 349029. Founding member of Phish (keyboards, vocals) and member/co-founder of Vida Blue. Active in recorded music from 1989 to the present.

Career

Phish (1983/1986–present)

Page McConnell joined Phish at the University of Vermont in 1983, completing the quartet alongside Trey Anastasio (guitar, vocals), Mike Gordon (bass, vocals), and Jon Fishman (drums). He became the permanent keyboardist after founding guitarist Jeff Holdsworth’s departure in 1986. Phish recorded their self-released debut cassette Junta in 1989, signed to Elektra Records in 1992, and produced a run of seven studio albums through Undermind (2004). McConnell’s keyboard work spans piano, organ, synthesizer, and Rhodes, and he contributes lead and harmony vocals throughout the catalog.

After Phish’s 2004 break-up he continued writing and recording independently. The band reunited in 2009 and has remained active, releasing Joy (2009), Fuego (2014), Big Boat (2016), The Baker’s Dozen Live At Madison Square Garden (2018), Sigma Oasis (2020), and Evolve (2024), the latter on ATO Records. A parallel archival live-release program under Livephish.com and Jemp Records spans dozens of concert recordings.

Vida Blue (2001–2019)

Vida Blue is an atmospheric jazz-funk trio McConnell co-founded with bassist Oteil Burbridge (Allman Brothers Band, Dead and Company) and drummer Russell Batiste (The Funky Meters). Guitarist Adam Zimmon also appears as a credited member. The trio released their self-titled debut on Elektra in 2002, followed by the remix EP Most Events Aren’t Planned + CJ3 - Selected Remixes (also 2002) and The Illustrated Band (2003, Sanctuary/When! Recordings). After a long gap, they returned with Crossing Lines in 2019 on ATO Records, which expanded the lineup to include members of Miami’s The Spam Allstars. McConnell produced, wrote most of the material, and provided the cover illustration.

Solo Work

McConnell released a self-titled solo album in 2007 on Legacy (Discogs release ID: 3884151), co-produced and engineered with Jared Slomoff and Bryce Goggin. A second solo album, Unsung Cities & Movies Never Made (2013), appeared on his own Keyed Records imprint, with Reed Mathis on bass, Gabe Jarrett on drums, and Adam Zimmon on guitar; Jared Slomoff co-produced and John Siket engineered. A third solo release, Something Will Land (2025), also on Keyed Records, rounds out the solo catalog at the time of writing.

Guest Appearances and Sessions

  • Gov’t Mule — Rising Low (2002, ATO Records): Keyboards on this all-star bass tribute album alongside a large ensemble including Oteil Burbridge, John Medeski, Bootsy Collins, Les Claypool, and many others.
  • Tenacious D — Tenacious D (2001, Epic): Keyboards on the debut album by Jack Black and Kyle Gass.
  • Widespread Panic — Space Wrangler (1992 reissue, Capricorn Records): Organ credit as additional musician.
  • The Green Sparrow (2008, Rounder Records): Organ.
  • Tweezer 7.31.13 (2015, The Royal Potato Family): Written-By credit.
