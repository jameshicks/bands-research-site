Danielle Dax

Born Danielle Gardner, 23 September 1958, Southend-on-Sea, Essex, England. Experimental rock musician, producer, visual artist. Active c.1979–mid-1990s. [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danielle_Dax, as of 2026-06-29]

Joined The Lemon Kittens c.1979 as keyboardist, flautist, saxophonist, and vocalist. Met Karl Blake after he placed an advertisement in Industrial News (September 1979); initially joined as visual artist before becoming a full performer. Created cover artwork for all Lemon Kittens releases.

Central figure in the John Fothergill–Steven Stapleton split: Fothergill fell in love with Dax, causing a permanent rift and approximately one year of parallel United Dairies operations (c.1980–1981). No interview with Dax discussing these circumstances has been located as of June 2026.

Contributed cover design and appeared on tracks of Bombay Ducks LP Dance Music (United Dairies UD 05, 1981). [source: http://mutant-sounds.blogspot.com/2007/01/bombay-ducks-dance-music-lp-1981.html]

Also painted the cover for Robert Fripp’s Let the Power Fall; performed (as “Hamsprachtmusic”) on The League of Gentlemen’s 1981 album.
