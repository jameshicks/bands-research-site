Doro Tachler

Discogs ID: 173673 Real name: Dorothea Tachler Name variations: Doro, Dorothea Tachler

German composer and violinist based in New York City. Active across indie, experimental, and post-rock contexts from the mid-1990s through the 2010s. Career spans session violin work on German avant-pop and post-rock records, a duo project with Adam Pierce (Igloo (2)), solo and co-credited releases, and later vocal contributions on recordings by HiM and Chimp Beams.

Website: https://dorotheatachler.com/

Overview

Tachler appears in Discogs credits under both “Doro Tachler” and the full form “Dorothea Tachler.” Her earliest documented session credits are from 1997 in Germany, playing violin on releases by Alles Wie Gross (on Hausmusik and the Belgian label (K-RAA-K)³) alongside Markus Acher of The Notwist. By 2001 she was contributing violin to Mice Parade’s Mokoondi and to Carlo Fashion’s Pieces For Acoustic Instruments And Synthesizers on Hausmusik.

Her most substantive compositional credit comes from the Igloo (2) duo project with Adam Pierce (Mice Parade), which released a self-titled album in 2003 on Bubble Core Records / After Hours. She co-wrote and co-recorded the entire record.

From 2003 onward she also contributed additional vocals to Mice Parade’s Obrigado Saudade, appeared as a featured vocalist on HiM’s Peoples (2005), and played violin on Dis-ka’s We Only Have Music (Echokammer, 2005). In 2011 she co-released a 7” split with Japanese group Chimp Beams on Concent Productions — credited jointly as a primary artist under the ANV “Dorothea Tachler.” In 2012 she contributed vocals to ASDR, a collaboration between Shing02 and Chimp Beams.
