Mark Nelson

Discogs ID: 416169 (listed as “Mark Nelson (2)”; real name Mark Nelson) Also credited as: Mark K. Nelson, Nelson Active: 1991–present (Richmond, Virginia / Chicago)

Guitarist, vocalist, tape-loop artist. Founding member of Labradford alongside Carter Brown (1991). Primary instrument on Prazision LP (1993) was guitar (Epiphone Genesis, Ovation Preacher 12-string, Yamaha 6-string) and tape loops. Runs the solo project Pan-American (Discogs: 12542), which has been his primary ongoing vehicle since 2001 and remains active into the 2020s, primarily on Kranky.
