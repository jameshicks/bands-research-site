Mike Watt

Full name: Michael David Watt Discogs ID: 77192 Origin: San Pedro, California Role: Bassist, vocalist, guitarist, producer

Bassist and co-founder of Minutemen (SST Records, 1980-1985) and fIREHOSE (SST Records, 1986-1994). After D. Boon’s death in December 1985, Watt formed fIREHOSE with Ed Crawford (vocals/guitar) and George Hurley (drums). Prolific solo artist and session collaborator throughout his career.

Also a credited member of Ciccone Youth — functioned as a fifth member alongside Sonic Youth’s four core players, producing and home-recording “Burnin’ Up (Mike Watt Orig. Demo)” on The Whitey Album (1988/1989), and producing the “Burnin’ Up” track on the 1986 single (New Alliance Records / Blast First). Greg Ginn of Black Flag contributed lead guitar to that recording.

The Ciccone Youth project originated partly from Watt’s personal circumstances: following D. Boon’s death in December 1985, Watt fell into depression and drove to the East Coast, staying with Kim Gordon and Thurston Moore in New York City. He participated in Sonic Youth’s EVOL sessions (playing bass on “In the Kingdom #9” and a cover of “Bubblegum”). Sonic Youth conceived the Ciccone Youth project in part as a way to encourage Watt’s return to making music. (Wikipedia — The Whitey Album, as of 2025-12-14)

Also a member of Wylde Ratttz, where he played bass and provided vocals (credited as “Mike ‘Pipe’ Watt”), with Bil Emmons as engineer/mixer on the 2020 LP.
