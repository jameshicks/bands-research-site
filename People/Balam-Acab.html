Solo electronic project of Alec Koone from Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania. One of the defining acts of the early witch-house movement, Balam Acab launched on Tri Angle with the label’s very first release and delivered its earliest full-album statement. After departing Tri Angle, Koone continued releasing self-produced material — including slowed-and-chopped edit tapes and a darker ambient album — through his own channels into the 2020s.

Biography

Origins and Tri Angle (2010–2011)

Alec Koone was born in 1991 in Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania. Under the name Balam Acab he became one of the first artists signed to Robin Carolan’s newly founded Tri Angle imprint in 2010. The debut EP See Birds (TRI ANGLE 01) was the label’s inaugural release, a five-track 12” of heavily reverbed, beat-driven electronic music built around pitched-down samples and slow, fog-drenched drum patterns — immediately establishing both the artist’s sound and the label’s aesthetic identity. The single-sided B-side included “See Birds (Sun),” a companion piece to the opening track “See Birds (Moon).”

In 2011, Koone expanded the project into a full-length album with Wander / Wonder (TRIANGLE07). The eight-track LP was produced and written by Koone (as “Alec Koone” in the credits), co-mixed with Sam Haar (one half of Brooklyn duo Blondes), and photographed by Emmette Murkett. The album deepened the witch-house approach: glacial tempos, reverb-saturated textures, and vocals processed into near-abstraction. It remains the fullest realization of Tri Angle’s first wave and is widely considered one of the label’s essential releases alongside Holy Other’s Held and the oOoOO debut.

These two releases concluded Koone’s association with Tri Angle. He did not appear on the label again.

Self-Released Period and Stylistic Drift (2013–2017)

After Tri Angle, Koone moved to self-releasing under the Balam Acab name. Slopped & Wetted Vol. 1 (2013) was a three-track digital release demonstrating a pivot toward the slowed-and-chopped “chopped not slopped” aesthetic — reworking existing material at screw-influenced tempos. Styles listed on Discogs (Downtempo, Leftfield, Screw) mark the shift from the Tri Angle witch-house template.

Child Death (2016, Orchid Tapes OCT056) was a darker, more ambitious release: five tracks of ambient and leftfield electronic music, pressed on limited numbered vinyl. Additional vocal contributions came from Josie Hendry, Kylyn Swann, Liz Yordy (also known as Coinstarr/DJ Alina/YUNGストレス), and Morgan Laubach. Sam Haar returned to mix the album; Warren Hildebrand (Foxes In Fiction) mastered it.

Also in 2015–2016, Koone appeared as a producer-for-hire on Awful Records releases. He produced track “B10” (“Lately”) on Slug Christ’s Sometimes Even The Moonlight Hurt My Eyes (Awful Records / Blackhouse Records, 2016), and produced It’s Colder At The Bottom of The Shower (Awful Records / Blackhouse Records, 2017) — a hip-hop LP, placing him in the Atlanta-adjacent cloud rap/trap orbit that Awful Records operated in.

Slopped & Wetted Vol. 2 (2017) followed the same format as Vol. 1 — seven tracks of self-released screwed edits of existing material, including a track titled “Drake X Pulse Emitter X Color Dream.”

Koone also contributed a DJ mix to the Weird Magic compilation Mother Earth (2011) and appeared on Clan Destine Records’ Downergaze 1.0 compilation (2017).

Later Work (2020–2023)

Just Bliss Right Now (2020) was a collaborative release credited jointly to Balam Acab and Stewart Keller, self-released by both. The five-track digital album saw Koone contributing music, lyrics, and vocals, with Keller handling music composition, mixing, mastering, and artwork. Styles listed: Ambient, Experimental — suggesting a continued drift from the witch-house template.

Discogs years_active runs to 2023, though no specific 2021–2023 release appears in the DB under the Balam Acab name.
