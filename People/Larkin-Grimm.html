Larkin Grimm

Singer-songwriter, experimental folk / freak-folk / psychedelic folk. Born 1981, Memphis, Tennessee. Discogs artist ID: 509618.

Overview

Larkin Grimm grew up in Dahlonega, Georgia, in the foothills of the Appalachians, in a family of singers and fiddlers that included her brother Joseph Grimm. At around age 20 she dropped out of Yale University and moved to Alaska, where she hiked and began experimenting with singing. A friend persuaded her to return to Yale; she graduated with a degree in Art. During her Yale years she began recording her debut album Harpoon, partly influenced by her experiences performing with the Dirty Projectors. (Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larkin_Grimm)

After graduating she moved to Providence, Rhode Island, where she recorded The Last Tree (2006) and eventually Parplar (2008). Parplar was her first major-label release, produced with Michael Gira on his Young God Records imprint. She is listed on Discogs as a formal member of Angels of Light and provided vocals on that band’s final studio album, We Are Him (2007).

Her 2013 album Soul Retrieval (Bad Bitch Records) was co-produced by Tony Visconti, who also played bass and guitar on the record. Her 2017 album Chasing an Illusion (Northern Spy Records) was recorded in a natural cave and used a mixing board previously used for David Bowie’s Young Americans. (Wikipedia)

Discogs profile note: based in Providence, Rhode Island at time of entry; also described as based in New York City per Wikipedia.
