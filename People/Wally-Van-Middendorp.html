Overview

Wally Van Middendorp is a Dutch vocalist, producer, and composer active since 1978, best known as the principal force behind Minny Pops — a minimal synth and experimental New Wave act that released on Factory Records and Factory Benelux between 1982 and 1985. He has also been a member of Streetlife (4), Tits, and White Birds, and has sustained long-running creative partnerships with Wim Dekker, Pieter Mulder, and Toon Bressers across dozens of shared releases. Alongside his recording career he co-ran Plurex Records, through which the Minny Pops catalogue has been reissued and extended as recently as 2024.
