Overview

Jason Corsaro was a Grammy Award-winning recording engineer and producer born in New York City on July 27, 1959, who died on August 16, 2017 from cancer complications at age 58. His catalogue of over 3,100 credits spans four decades, anchored by an extraordinarily close working relationship with the Chic organisation: Bernard Edwards and Nile Rodgers alone account for more than 1,200 shared credits, with Tony Thompson and Rob Sabino further deepening that axis. Beyond Chic, he was a core collaborator with Bill Laswell, appearing on nearly 500 shared releases, and his label footprint runs from major pop and rock imprints — EMI, Capitol, Atlantic, Warner Bros. — to Laswell’s experimental Axiom imprint. His final work was a pair of anthologies for the Cars and two 1980s compilation projects.
