Overview

Jon Brooks (real name Cate Brooks) is a British composer, producer, and mastering engineer who runs the Cafe Kaput label and has served as long-term engineer for Trunk Records and others. Their recorded output sits in experimental, ambient, and synth-pop territory, with catalogue concentrated in the 2010s through projects including The Belbury Circle, The Pattern Forms, Black Mustang, and Woodbines & Spiders. Brooks is a fixture of the Ghost Box world — 29 works on that label across 2005–2025 — and shares the most credits with Moon Wiring Club, Jonny Trunk, and The Advisory Circle.
