Overview

Colin Meloy is an American singer-songwriter and author, the frontman and primary songwriter of Portland, Oregon’s The Decemberists — an indie folk-rock act with prog and acoustic elements whose output spans the 2000s through the 2020s. Before The Decemberists, he played in Tarkio, and later extended his reach through the Olivia Chaney collaboration Offa Rex. His work has appeared across Capitol Records, Rough Trade, and indie imprints including Jealous Butcher and Kill Rock Stars, with Tucker Martine and Chris Walla as recurring production-side figures alongside the core Decemberists lineup of Jenny Conlee, Chris Funk, Nate Query, and John Moen.
