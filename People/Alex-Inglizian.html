Alex Inglizian

Discogs ID: 608851 Name variants: Alex Inglesian, Alex Inglezian, Alex Inglisian Based: Chicago, IL Active: 2010s–present Roles: Composer, electronics performer, recording/mixing/mastering engineer

Overview

Alex Inglizian is a Chicago-based musician, composer, and recording engineer. He is co-director and chief recording engineer at Experimental Sound Studio (ESS), 5925 N. Ravenswood Ave, Chicago IL — one of the city’s primary hubs for experimental, improvised, and electronic music. His work straddles two modes: as a performing artist in his own right (electronics, composition) and as a behind-the-board engineer whose credits span a wide range of Chicago and international experimental/improv releases.

His engineering work places him at the intersection of Chicago’s experimental music community — labels like International Anthem, Corbett vs. Dempsey, Relay Recordings, and Room40 — while his performance work is comparatively sparse on record, with the 2019 duo release “Looking Out” (with Tim Daisy) being the most visible document.

Experimental Sound Studio

Inglizian is co-director and chief recording engineer at Experimental Sound Studio (ESS), a Chicago arts organization and recording facility at 5925 N. Ravenswood Ave. ESS functions primarily as a live venue and recording space; its Discogs label entry (ID 301110) shows one release: “Three Circles & A Speaker” (Steve Leaf & The Ex Pats). Much of ESS’s output is not released commercially but rather facilitates other artists’ recordings — Inglizian’s extensive engineering credits are substantially a product of his ESS role.
