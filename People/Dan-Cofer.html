Overview

Dan Cofer is an American drummer best known as a member of the Parasites, a punk and pop-punk band active primarily through the 1990s. His 18 Discogs credits cluster heavily in that decade (six releases), with a single entry in the 2000s, spread across labels including Shredder, Lookout! Records, and Go-Kart Records. Recurring collaborators include bandmates Nikki Parasite, Scott Cofer, and Dave Parasite, alongside producers Kevin Army, Kurt Bloch, and Mass Giorgini.
