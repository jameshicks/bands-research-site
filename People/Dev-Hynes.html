Dev Hynes

Real name: Devonté Hynes Born: 23 December 1985, London, England Discogs IDs: Blood Orange (2) = 2372814 · Lightspeed Champion = 850000 · Devonte Hynes (credits) = 853545 Active: 2007–present Labels: Domino Recording Company · Domino · Terrible Records (2) · Loyauté · RCA

British-American singer, composer, songwriter, producer, and author. Raised in Essex, England; relocated to New York in the late 2000s. Began recording career as Lightspeed Champion on Domino with Falling Off The Lavender Bridge (2008), a country-inflected indie pop album produced by Mike Mogis. Dissolved the Lightspeed Champion project around 2010 and re-emerged as Blood Orange, a solo vehicle drawing on 1980s R&B, soul, and post-punk — shaped heavily by the New York scene he had moved into. From 2011 onward he has operated simultaneously as a recording artist (under Blood Orange) and as an in-demand outside producer and songwriter, placing credits on records by Solange, Caroline Polachek, Neneh Cherry, Connan Mockasin, and Ryuichi Sakamoto, among others.
