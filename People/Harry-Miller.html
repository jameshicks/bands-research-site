Harry Miller

Real name: Harold Simon Miller
Discogs artist ID: 272603
Born: South Africa
Died: 1983, Netherlands (car accident)
Instrument: Double bass, flute, percussion

South African-born bassist who settled in London; co-founder of Ogun with his wife Hazel Miller and engineer Keith Beal in 1973. A central figure in the South African exile jazz community in London and in British free improvisation.

Overview

Miller arrived in London as part of the broader South African exile community that included Chris McGregor, Dudu Pukwana, Louis Moholo, Johnny Dyani, and Mongezi Feza. He co-founded Ogun to document this community’s music. His own debut album Children At Play (1974, OG 200) was the label’s second release. He led Harry Miller’s Isipingo and was a member of Chris McGregor’s Brotherhood Of Breath, Keith Tippett’s Ark, Elton Dean’s Ninesense, Ovary Lodge, and dozens of other groups.

When the Millers relocated to the Netherlands in the early 1980s, Ogun went dormant. Harry Miller was killed in a car accident there in 1983.
