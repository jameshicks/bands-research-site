Linton Kwesi Johnson

Discogs ID: 20702 Born: 23 August 1952, Chapelton, Jamaica; raised in Brixton, London

Jamaican-British poet, dub reggae musician, and activist. Pioneer of “dub poetry” — spoken word set against reggae bass lines. Associated with Island Records (Mango imprint) through the late 1970s and 1980s. Making History (Island/Mango, 1984) appears repeatedly in John Dent’s credits across multiple pressings.

Key albums: Dread Beat An’ Blood (1978), Forces Of Victory (1979), Bass Culture (1980), Making History (1984). Worked extensively with producer Dennis Bovell.
