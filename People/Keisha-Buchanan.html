Keisha Buchanan

Singer-songwriter. Born 30 September 1984 in Westminster, London, England; parents of Jamaican descent. Distant cousin of Simon Webbe. Co-founder and longest-serving original member of Sugababes (Mk1–Mk3, 1998–2009; reunion 2019–present).

Discogs ID: 631957

Biography

Formation and early Sugababes (1998–2001)

Keisha Buchanan co-founded Sugababes with Mutya Buena and Siobhan Donaghy in 1998 while all three were schoolgirls in London. The trio signed to London Records and released their debut album One Touch (2000/2001). The record was a commercial underperformer on initial release, though later reissued and reassessed. Singles from this era include “Overload”, “New Year” and “Run for Cover” — the latter credited with all vocals performed by the Sugababes.

Siobhan Donaghy left the group in 2001 and was replaced by Heidi Range.

Mk2 era — Island Records and mainstream breakthrough (2002–2006)

With the Mk2 lineup of Buchanan, Buena and Range, the group moved to Island Records and entered their commercial peak. The 2002 album Angels with Dirty Faces and the 2003 single “Hole in the Head” (UK #1) established the group as a major pop act. Production across this period was handled primarily by Xenomania — the collective fronted by Brian Higgins alongside Tim Powell, Miranda Cooper, and Matt Gray — as well as outside collaborators.

Taller in More Ways (2005) brought in additional producers: Dallas Austin (tracks 1, 2, 6, 7, 9), Jony Rockstar (tracks 3, 4, 11), Guy Sigsworth (track 8), and Xenomania (tracks 5, 10). Writers on the album included Cathy Dennis, Nick Coler, and Cameron McVey. The 2006 compilation Overloaded: The Singles Collection closed the chapter.

Mutya Buena departed in late 2005 and was replaced by Amelle Berrabah.

Mk3 era — Change and Catfights (2007–2009)

The Mk3 lineup — Buchanan, Range, and Berrabah — recorded Change (2007) and Catfights and Spotlights (2008). Keisha co-wrote across both records. The 2008 single “Denial” and 2009’s “Get Sexy” were among the final releases with her involvement.

In September 2009, Buchanan was replaced by Jade Ewen, making her the last of the three founders to leave the lineup. The group continued under the Sugababes name with the Mk4 lineup.

Band Aid 20 (2004)

Keisha appeared as a performer on the 2004 charity single “Do They Know It’s Christmas?” recorded under the Band Aid 20 banner, alongside Mutya Buena, Heidi Range, and many other UK artists. A 2024 reissue of the track on Universal also carries her contributor credit.

Mutya Keisha Siobhan reunion (2013–2019)

In 2011–2012, Buchanan reunited with the original three founding members — Mutya Buena and Siobhan Donaghy — forming Mutya Keisha Siobhan (also known as MKS). They signed to Polydor and released the single “Flatline” (2013), co-written by all three and produced by Dev Hynes (Blood Orange). The Flatline (Remixes) EP followed. A full album was recorded but not commercially released during this period.

The trio also contributed a co-written track (“Now I’m All Cried Out”) to Bananarama’s 2019 album In Stereo on In Synk.

Reclaiming the Sugababes name (2019–present)

After legal proceedings to reclaim the trademark, the original trio relaunched as Sugababes in 2019 and performed at Glastonbury. They released “Flowers” (2021) on London Records — a re-recording of the One Touch-era track “Overload” retitled — and signed to BMG for the 2023 single “When the Rain Comes”, co-written and co-performed by all three and produced by George Moore. The track featured live bass (Aaron Powell), drums (Matthew Brown), and was engineered by Alex Killpartrick, Dan Parry, and Joe LaPorta.

The 2024 single “Situation” (with A Little Sound, on Ministry Of Sound) was co-written by Buchanan, Buena, and Donaghy alongside Cameron McVey, Felix Howard, John Lipsey, and Paul Simm, and produced by Billen Ted and Kove.
