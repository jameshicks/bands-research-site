Felix Morel

Montreal-based drummer and graphic designer. Discogs artist ID: 632109. Member of Fly Pan Am. Name variations: Félix Morel, Flex, Sex Pistol Moe.

Overview

Plays drums and scrap cymbals in Fly Pan Am; also credited with graphic design on Frontera (2021). Active in the Montreal experimental scene across multiple projects.
