K. K. Barrett

Real name: Keith Barrett Discogs artist ID: 1025827 Role: Drummer, later film production designer

Overview

K. K. Barrett (Keith Barrett) was the drummer in several key Los Angeles punk acts of the late 1970s, most notably Screamers and The Randoms. He is also credited on releases by Black Randy & The Metrosquad and Thelonious Monster. After the punk era, he transitioned into film production design, becoming an acclaimed Hollywood production designer. His film credits include Being John Malkovich (dir. Spike Jonze, 1999), Her (dir. Spike Jonze, 2013), and others.

Suburban Lawns — no confirmed connection (researched 2026-07-19)

The vault’s prior note that Barrett worked with Suburban Lawns before his film career is unverified and likely incorrect.

Sources consulted:

  • Wikipedia article for K. K. Barrett (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K._K._Barrett, checked 2026-07-19) — names only The Screamers as his band; no mention of Suburban Lawns.
  • Wikipedia article for Suburban Lawns (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suburban_Lawns, checked 2026-07-19) — lists five members: Su Tissue (vocals/keyboards), Vex Billingsgate (bass/vocals), Frankie Ennui (guitar), John Gleur (guitar), Chuck Roast (drums). No mention of Barrett.
  • Surface magazine interview with K. K. Barrett (https://www.surfacemag.com/articles/production-designer-kk-barrett/) — Barrett describes his band career as The Screamers only (“synthesizers, a distorted electric piano, and myself on drums”).
  • Something Else Reviews — Suburban Lawns interview (2015) (https://somethingelsereviews.com/2015/10/21/suburban-lawns-interview/) — confirms Chuck Roast as sole drummer throughout the band’s existence; no Barrett mention.
  • The Outline — “Searching for Su Tissue” (https://theoutline.com/post/7399/searching-for-su-tissue-suburban-lawns) — no Barrett mention.
  • Vice — “Looking Back on Suburban Lawns” (2015) — no Barrett mention.
  • MusicBrainz entry for K. K. Barrett (https://musicbrainz.org/artist/94a2c1fb-21f4-4c17-a033-bddfb7f8124a) — no Suburban Lawns association listed.

Conclusion: No press source, band history, Wikipedia article, or interview corroborates a Barrett–Suburban Lawns link. The prior vault note appears to have been an error (possible confusion with The Screamers, which were a contemporaneous LA punk-adjacent act). The Discogs entry for Barrett likewise contains no Suburban Lawns credit. The claim should be treated as unsubstantiated until a primary source is found.
