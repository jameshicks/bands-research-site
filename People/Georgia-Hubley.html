Connection to Dining Out Records

Via Martha Tilson: Tilson passed through the Dining Out Records scene (Twilight Zoners, Occult Chemistry) → A Certain Ratio / Factory Records → returned to New York and co-led Magnetic Pull with Pam Meyer, with Georgia Hubley on drums.

Magnetic Pull was an all-female unrecorded band (~1982–1983). No recordings exist.

Notes

Georgia Hubley was embedded in the New York underground art/music scene in the early 1980s before co-founding Yo La Tengo in 1984 with Ira Kaplan (whom she later married). She had played in at least one other pre-YLT project (The Dangerous Curves). Magnetic Pull fits this pre-YLT period.

Key Connections

  • Magnetic Pull — with Martha Tilson and Pam Meyer
  • Yo La Tengo — co-founder (1984–)
  • Martha Tilson — Magnetic Pull bandmate

Tortoise Connection

Co-wrote and sang “Yonder Blue” on Tortoise’s The Catastrophist (2016, Thrill Jockey) — one of the album’s two vocal tracks.

Notes (web research — 2026-07-04)

Searched extensively across available interviews (DRUM! Magazine, Tom Tom Magazine, Chickfactor, Interview Magazine, Loud And Quiet, Jambase, Wikipedia, drummerphotographer.com) for any mention of Magnetic Pull or Martha Tilson. None found. In every interview where Hubley discusses her pre-YLT drumming, she references only an unnamed all-female band (which she had already left when they became The Dangerous Curves) — and does not name bandmates. She does not mention Magnetic Pull, Martha Tilson, or any other pre-YLT project by name in any located public statement.

The most detailed account comes from Tom Tom Magazine 2012 (source) where Hubley describes meeting a fellow art school student who invited her to play: “She was like, ‘Oh I’ve been playing with some people. Do you want to come play?’ I was like, ‘Ok sure.’ So I ended up joining this all girl band.” On the band’s name and activity: “We didn’t have a name for a long time, and by the time I left we still had never had a show…They eventually called themselves The Dangerous Curves, but that was kind of after I left and quit.” DRUM! Magazine (source) adds that The Dangerous Curves “pitched in to help her buy her first kit, an old Slingerland.” These two sources establish a clear timeline: Hubley played in an unnamed all-girl band at art school → left before they had a name or shows → band later became The Dangerous Curves → Hubley then met Ira Kaplan → Yo La Tengo formed 1984.

The Magnetic Pull connection therefore has no primary-source citation in any located interview. It rests entirely on the vault’s existing research chain (Twilight Zoners Discogs release notes r15406580 sleeve notes).
