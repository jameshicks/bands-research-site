Aug 22, 20262 min read

Carl Saff

Discogs ID: 436674

Mastering engineer. 58 mastering credits on Joyful Noise Recordings. Chicago-based; one of the most active mastering engineers in US indie rock and experimental music.

Also mastered How To Dress Well’s Love Remains (2010, Lefse Records) — early indie/R&B crossover work.

Also mastered Hookworms’ self-titled cassette (2011, Sun Ark Records) and Pearl Mystic (2013, Gringo Records).

Also the primary mastering engineer for Forest Swords across virtually the entire discography — 27+ shared releases including Dagger Paths E.P. (2010), Engravings (2013, Tri Angle), Shrine: Original Dance Score (2016), and Compassion (2017, Ninja Tune).

Second most frequent mastering engineer across Temporary Residence Limited’s catalog — 71 TRL releases. Mastered many of the label’s Envy (2), Young Widows, Coliseum (2), and Eluvium releases. Often credited alongside Robert Weston (vinyl lacquer cuts) on the same TRL records.

Also mastered H-Burns’s How Strange It Is To Be Anything At All (2008, Boxson) and West Country Night - Session One (2010, Not On Label) — among his French indie credits.

Also the top mastering engineer on Locust Music (Chicago), with 23 releases credited — Begushkin, No-Neck Blues Band, Jay Bolotin, Amen Dunes, Paul Metzger, and others. Label operated by Dawson Prater.

Also mastered 3 releases on Birdtapes (Brooklyn/Philadelphia lo-fi cassette label, 2012–2017).
