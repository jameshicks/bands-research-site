Overview

Dave Burrell (born Herman Davis Burrell, September 10, 1940, Middletown, Ohio) is an American pianist, keyboardist, and organist working across free jazz, avant-garde jazz, post-bop, and soul-jazz. His recorded output runs from the mid-1960s into the 2020s, with the bulk concentrated in the 1970s — a decade when he documented heavily for BYG Records, Impulse!, and Freedom. He has been a long-standing associate of Archie Shepp, sharing credits on over 270 releases and appearing in the Archie Shepp Quartet; his other closest collaborators include drummer Beaver Harris, trombonist Grachan Moncur III, and bassist Malachi Favors. Later in his career he contributed to the David Murray Octet and David Murray Quartet.
