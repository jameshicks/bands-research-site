Angel Deradoorian

Discogs ID: 952820 Real name: Angel Deradoorian Also known as: Deradoorian (solo project, Discogs artist ID: 1714484) Origin: Northern California Role: Vocalist, multi-instrumentalist, producer, songwriter

Multi-instrumentalist and singer-songwriter from Northern California. Best known internationally as a core vocalist of Dirty Projectors during the Bitte Orca era (2009–2012), her voice — alongside Amber Coffman and David Longstreth — defines the complex three-way vocal counterpoint of that band’s classic period. After departing Dirty Projectors she launched the solo project Deradoorian, releasing two LPs on Anticon (The Expanding Flower Planet, 2015; Find The Sun, 2020) and a third on Fire Records (Ready For Heaven, 2025), each self-produced. She also belongs to Avey Tare’s Slasher Flicks (with Avey Tare and Jeremy Hyman), the Black Sabbath-themed noise project BSCBR, and the duo Decisive Pink (with Kate NV).

Biography by Era

Early Credits (2005–2008)

Deradoorian’s earliest Discogs-traceable credits are as a multi-instrumentalist. She played piano, flute, synth, organ, harmonium, accordion, and contributed artwork to an unnamed Drive-Thru Records release (We Can Breathe Under Alcohol, 2005). She added vocals to a second Drive-Thru release (The Truth Is That You Are Alive, 2007) and contributed accordion, toy piano, and vocals to Tigers On Fire (Burst & Bloom Records, 2007). A further vocal credit appeared on The Island Moved In The Storm (La Société Expéditionnaire, 2008). These pre-Dirty Projectors recordings establish her range across instruments before the full-band context.

Dirty Projectors: Bitte Orca Era (2008–2012)

Deradoorian joined Dirty Projectors in time for Bitte Orca (Domino, 2009) — the album that made the band internationally known. The core credited lineup was David Longstreth, Amber Coffman, Deradoorian, and Brian McOmber, with Haley Dekle contributing additional harmonies and Nat Baldwin on bass on two tracks. A string quartet — Jordan Dykstra (viola/director), Andrew Todd and Caleb Russell (violin), Anna Fritz (cello) — appeared on four tracks. The album was produced by Longstreth with Nicolas Vernhes as additional producer; mixed by Longstreth and Vernhes; mastered by Joe Lambert.

Mount Wittenberg Orca (self-released, 2010) was a full collaborative record with Björk, jointly credited to Dirty Projectors + Björk. The Bitte Orca lineup — Longstreth, Coffman, Deradoorian, McOmber, Dekle, Baldwin — performed alongside Björk throughout. Mixed by Longstreth, Björk, and Vernhes; mastered by Joe Lambert.

Deradoorian also contributed voice to Inter Arbiter (Twosyllable Records, 2010) during this period.

She is not credited on Swing Lo Magellan (2012), which appears to be the effective endpoint of her tenure in the group.

Prefuse 73 and Guest Work (2011–2014)

Scott Herren’s all-female-vocalist project The Only She Chapters (Warp Records, 2011, WARPLP208) features Deradoorian on track D3 — both as vocalist (Voice) and co-writer (Written-By). The track appears alongside contributions from Zola Jesus, Trish Keenan, Shara Worden, Alejandra Deheza, and Niki Randa. It is one of her more substantial guest credits in this period.

Avey Tare’s Slasher Flicks (2014)

Avey Tare’s Slasher Flicks is a trio of Avey Tare (Dave Portner, of Animal Collective), Deradoorian, and drummer Jeremy Hyman. Their sole LP Enter The Slasher House (Domino, 2014) was produced by the band and Sonny DiPerri (additional production), engineered and co-mixed by DiPerri, mastered by Joe Lambert. The band also released a single, Little Fang (Domino, 2014).

The project shares Sonny DiPerri as engineer/producer with the early Deradoorian solo work, and Joe Lambert (who mastered Bitte Orca) as mastering engineer — connecting it to the Dirty Projectors production world.

Deradoorian Solo: The Expanding Flower Planet (2015)

The debut Deradoorian LP The Expanding Flower Planet (Anticon, 2015) was self-produced and extensively self-performed: Deradoorian played vocals, drums, guitars, bass, banjos, synthesizers, flutes, and piano. She also co-mixed and co-produced with Kenny Gilmore (who also played guitar and synthesizer), with Sonny DiPerri contributing additional mixing. Additional drummers: Jeremy Hyman and Mike Lockwood. Guest vocalists: Niki Randa and Arlene Deradoorian (presumably a family member). Cover artwork by Daniel Higgs. Mastered by Dave Cooley. Layout and design by Deradoorian and Bryant Rutledge.

The record positions her as a solo auteur — writing, playing nearly every instrument, producing, and controlling the visual presentation.

BSCBR (2020–2022)

BSCBR is a New York-based group of Black Sabbath devotees: Deradoorian, Brad Truax (bass), Greg Fox (2) (drums), Mick Barr (guitar), and Nick Zinner (guitar, also mixed). Their album Master Of Rehearsal (Famous Class, 2020) was recorded by Tim O’Sullivan (4) and mastered by Heba Kadry. Deradoorian is credited as vocalist. A second volume, Master Of Rehearsal Vol2, appeared on Famous Class in 2022.

Other notable BSCBR-adjacent credits: Deradoorian contributed vocal arrangements to Vampire Weekend’s Modern Vampires Of The City (XL Recordings, 2013) and vocals to LA Vampires’ Moody Coup (Software (2), 2013).

Deradoorian Solo: Find The Sun (2020)

The second Deradoorian LP Find The Sun (Anti-, 2020) expanded to a small band. Deradoorian played producer, lead vocals, flute, guitar, synthesizer, and bass (tracks 1, 4, 7, 9). Dave Harrington (3) contributed bass on the remaining tracks (2, 3, 5, 8, 10) and electronics, congas, and percussion throughout. Samer Ghadry: drums, timpani, and percussion. Sonny DiPerri co-produced, engineered, and mixed. Mastered by Adam Gonsalves.

The shift to Anti- (from Anticon) placed the record on a more mainstream independent platform.

She also contributed vocals to Sunn O)))‘s Life Metal (Southern Lord, 2019) — specifically credited for “Vedic Astrologue Charts Generated By,” a non-musical but cosmological contribution noted in the release credits.

Decisive Pink (2022–2023)

Decisive Pink is a duo of Deradoorian and Kate NV (Kate Shilonosova, Moscow-based). Signed to Fire Records. The EP Haffmilch Holiday (Fire Records, 2022) credits both as musicians. The LP Ticket To Fame (Fire Records, 2023) credits both with “Music By.” A brief project description on Discogs identifies Deradoorian as “former member of art-pop iconoclasts Dirty Projectors.”

Deradoorian Solo: Ready For Heaven (2025)

Ready For Heaven (Fire Records, 2025) is the third Deradoorian LP. Deradoorian played bass, guitar, synthesizer, piano, and lead vocals, and produced. The band: Steven Martens (bass), Andrew Maguire (drums), Dylan Fujioka (drums), Cole Berliner (guitar), Pedrum Siadatian (guitar), Steve Marion (guitar), Patrick Shiroishi (saxophone). Sonny DiPerri engineered. Mastered by Dave Cooley. Continuing the Fire Records relationship established with Decisive Pink.

The label shift to Fire Records for solo work represents a new chapter, with the fuller live band and saxophone suggesting movement toward a more expansive sound.

Personnel: Key Releases

Bitte Orca (Dirty Projectors, Domino, 2009)

Core performers: David Longstreth, Amber Coffman, Angel Deradoorian, Brian McOmber. Additional: Haley Dekle (vocals, A1–A3, B3–B4); Nat Baldwin (bass, A2/B1); string quartet: Jordan Dykstra (viola/director), Andrew Todd, Caleb Russell (violins), Anna Fritz (cello) on A4, A5, B3, B4. Produced by Longstreth; Nicolas Vernhes (additional producer); mixed by Longstreth and Vernhes; recorded by Robby Moncrieff and McOmber; mastered by Joe Lambert.

Mount Wittenberg Orca (Dirty Projectors + Björk, 2010)

Performers: Amber Coffman, Angel Deradoorian, Björk, Brian McOmber, David Longstreth, Haley Dekle, Nat Baldwin. Produced and written by Longstreth. Mixed by Björk, Longstreth, Nicolas Vernhes. Mastered by Joe Lambert.

The Only She Chapters (Prefuse 73, Warp, 2011)

Deradoorian credited Voice and Written-By on track D3. Also on the record: Zola Jesus (C1), Trish Keenan (B2), Niki Randa (B5), Adrienne McCann (C3), Shara Worden (A5), Alejandra Deheza (C4), Claudia Deheza (C4). Guitar on C4: Benjamin Curtis. Producer on C4: Roberto Carlos Lange. Overall producer and written-by: Scott Herren (Guillermo S. Herren). Mastered by Noel Summerville.

Enter The Slasher House (Avey Tare’s Slasher Flicks, Domino, 2014)

Performers: Angel Deradoorian, Avey Tare (Dave Portner), Jeremy Hyman. Produced by Avey Tare’s Slasher Flicks; additional production, engineering, co-mixing: Sonny DiPerri. Mastered by Joe Lambert.

The Expanding Flower Planet (Deradoorian, Anticon, 2015)

Primary performer: Deradoorian (vocals, drums, guitars, bass, banjos, synthesizers, flutes, piano). Additional: Jeremy Hyman (drums), Mike Lockwood (drums), Kenny Gilmore (guitars, synthesizer), Niki Randa (vocals), Arlene Deradoorian (vocals). Produced by Deradoorian and Gilmore; mixed by Deradoorian, Gilmore, and Sonny DiPerri. Mastered by Dave Cooley. Cover art: Daniel Higgs.

Master Of Rehearsal (BSCBR, Famous Class, 2020)

Members: Angel Deradoorian (vocals), Brad Truax (bass), Greg Fox (2) (drums), Mick Barr (guitar), Nick Zinner (guitar, mixed by). Recorded by Tim O’Sullivan (4). Mastered by Heba Kadry.

Find The Sun (Deradoorian, Anti-, 2020)

Deradoorian: producer, vocals, flute, guitar, synthesizer, bass (tracks 1, 4, 7, 9). Dave Harrington (3): bass (tracks 2, 3, 5, 8, 10), electronics, congas, percussion. Samer Ghadry: drums, timpani, percussion. Co-produced, engineered, and mixed by Sonny DiPerri. Mastered by Adam Gonsalves.

Ready For Heaven (Deradoorian, Fire Records, 2025)

Deradoorian: producer, lead vocals, bass, guitar, synthesizer, piano. Band: Steven Martens (bass), Andrew Maguire (drums), Dylan Fujioka (drums), Cole Berliner (guitar), Pedrum Siadatian (guitar), Steve Marion (guitar), Patrick Shiroishi (saxophone). Engineered by Sonny DiPerri. Mastered by Dave Cooley.

Key Groups

Avey Tare’s Slasher Flicks (Discogs ID: 3753171)

Trio: Dave Portner (Avey Tare, Animal Collective), Angel Deradoorian, Jeremy Hyman (drums). One LP (Enter The Slasher House, Domino, 2014) and one single (Little Fang, Domino, 2014). The project connects Deradoorian to the Animal Collective world — Portner and Deradoorian appear to have been in a relationship, per broader context.

BSCBR (Discogs ID: 8089512)

New York-based five-piece formed around Black Sabbath fandom. Members: Deradoorian (vocals), Brad Truax (bass), Greg Fox (2) (drums), Mick Barr (guitar), Nick Zinner (guitar). Two albums on Famous Class (2020, 2022). The lineup is a cross-section of Brooklyn avant-metal and experimental circles: Fox and Barr from Krallice-adjacent heavy music, Zinner from Yeah Yeah Yeahs.

Decisive Pink (Discogs ID: 12604649)

Duo: Angel Deradoorian + Kate NV (Kate Shilonosova). Signed to Fire Records. EP Haffmilch Holiday (2022) and LP Ticket To Fame (2023). The pairing bridges Deradoorian’s California art-rock background with Kate NV’s Moscow-based electronic pop world.
