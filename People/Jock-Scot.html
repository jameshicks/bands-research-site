Overview

Jock Scot (John Graham Manson Leslie, 1952–2016) was a Scottish poet and performer born in Leith who became a fixture of the British post-punk and pub rock scenes after moving to London and forging a close friendship with Ian Dury. He worked at the Stiff and Charisma labels, toured with Rip Rig & Panic, and warmed up shows for acts including The Clash, Blondie, Talking Heads, Elvis Costello, and the Libertines. His recorded work, clustered on Postcard Records in the 1990s, centres on spoken-word and lyric writing, with seven co-credits alongside The Nectarine No. 9 marking his deepest studio partnership. He published one book, Where is My Heroine? (Castle-Star, 1992), and a documentary on his life was being crowdfunded at the time of his death in London in April 2016.
