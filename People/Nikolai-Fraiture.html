Nikolai Fraiture

American bassist. Born November 13, 1978, New York City, NY. Founding member and bassist of The Strokes from their formation (1998). Also released a solo album under the alias Nickel Eye and leads the collective Summer Moon. Discogs artist ID: 430105.

Biography

Early Life and The Strokes (1998–present)

Fraiture grew up in New York City and co-founded The Strokes in 1998 alongside Julian Casablancas, Albert Hammond Jr., Nick Valensi, and Fabrizio Moretti. The band self-released a demo in 2000 before signing to RCA (via Rough Trade in the UK). He has played bass on every Strokes studio album and the majority of their singles across a span of two decades.

Nickel Eye (2008–2009)

Under the alias Nickel Eye (Discogs artist ID: 1555859), Fraiture released a full solo album on Rykodisc. On The Time Of The Assassins (2008) he wrote ten of the eleven tracks, produced the album himself, and played bass, guitar, harmonica, and double bass, in addition to providing lead vocals. The record featured contributions from Jack Dishel (backing vocals on two tracks), Nick Zinner (guitar on “Dying Star”), Regina Spektor (piano on “Where The Cold Wind Blows”), and Illy Fraiture (backing vocals on “Fountain Avenue” and “Hey, That’s No Way To Say Goodbye”). The sole cover was a Leonard Cohen song. Drummer Brett Shaw co-engineered alongside Chris Ribando, who also mixed. A single, You And Everyone Else (2008), and a remix EP, Brandy Of The Damned (Remixed) (2009), followed on Rykodisc.

Summer Moon (2015–present)

Around 2015, Fraiture began building Summer Moon (Discogs artist ID: 5337260), described as a music and arts collective. The lineup includes Stephen Perkins (drums; also of Jane’s Addiction), Camila Grey (keys/vocals; also of Uh Huh Her), and Noah Harmon (guitar; also of The Airborne Toxic Event). The group released the single “With You Tonight” on UK label Kissability (2016) before issuing a full-length LP of the same name in 2017 on Dtf Records (2). A further release, Casino Days, appeared in 2023 on a not-on-label basis. The 2017 LP was written and produced by Fraiture (with additional production from Brad Bell on select tracks), mixed by Gus Oberg, and featured backing vocals from Erika Spring and Lewis Lazar.
