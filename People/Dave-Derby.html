Overview

Dave Derby (born David Philander Derby) is a New York-based indie bassist, vocalist, and producer active since the early 1990s. He has worked across multiple projects including Lloyd Cole & The Negatives, Gramercy Arms, Mascott, Kam Lung, and Derby & Kotch, with an especially deep collaborative history with Joan Wasser (36 shared credits) and Lloyd Cole (30 shared credits). His label trajectory moved from major-adjacent EastWest Records America in the mid-1990s through a cluster of indie imprints — Tapete, Reveal, and Ear Music — continuing into the 2020s. His song “Pilot Light” serves as the outro music for the US political talk show The Majority Report with Sam Seder.
