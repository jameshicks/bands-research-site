Walt Dickerson

Discogs ID: 487219
Born: April 4, 1928, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania
Died: May 15, 2008

Vibraphonist. Co-led the trio on Life Rays (Soul Note, SN 1028, 1982) with Norris Jones (bass) and Andrew Cyrille (drums). One of the few free jazz vibraphonists of his era.
