Biography

Cory Smythe is a New York-based pianist and composer whose practice spans contemporary classical performance, free improvisation, and genre-crossing composition. He is a long-standing member of the International Contemporary Ensemble (ICE), the New York-based collective whose roster includes Dan Lippel, Claire Chase, Jacob Greenberg, Joshua Rubin, Tyshawn Sorey, and many others.

Smythe’s earliest Discogs credit is a 2006 self-released disc (Repose, Not On Label) credited to Kyle Quass. By 2007 he appears as composer and electric/acoustic pianist on Ingrid Laubrock’s That / Not (Firehouse 12 Records), a relationship that would extend across more than a decade. He also contributed piano and celesta to the International Contemporary Ensemble’s 2011 Nonesuch release Son Of Chamber Symphony / String Quartet (John Adams) and participated in multiple Anthony Braxton New Braxton House sessions in 2011 (Composition 30, Trillium E).

His mainstream-classical profile expanded with appearances on Hilary Hahn’s In 27 Pieces: The Hilary Hahn Encores (2013, Deutsche Grammophon) and Vijay Iyer’s Radhe Radhe (Rites of Holi) (2014, ECM) — both high-profile crossover projects. He contributed piano to Steve Lehman’s Alloy (2014, Pi Recordings) and Tyshawn Sorey’s The Inner Spectrum of Variables (2016, Pi Recordings).

The trio project with Ingrid Laubrock and bassist Stephan Crump produced two albums on Intakt Records: Planktonic Finales (2017) and Channels (2019), both recorded by Ryan Streber and mastered by Liberty Ellman. Smythe co-composed and co-produced both records alongside Laubrock and Crump.

His debut solo album, Circulate Susanna (2018, Pyroclastic Records, cat. PR 04), is his most prominent leader statement. It features Dan Lippel on detuned acoustic guitar and electronics, and Sofia Jernberg on voice. Smythe composed all tracks except a Dan Lippel/Sofia Jernberg improvisation and a closing piece based on Lewis Allan’s “Strange Fruit.” The record was produced by Smythe and David Breskin, mixed by Ron St. Germain, and mastered by Liberty Ellman.

Accelerate Every Voice (2020, Pyroclastic Records) followed, featuring vocalists Kari Francis, Kyoko Kitamura, Raquel Acevedo Klein, and Michael Mayo, again produced with David Breskin and recorded by Ryan Streber. Smythe mixed the record himself alongside Streber.

The Cloud Sounds Trio — with Ingrid Laubrock and drummer Devin Gray — yielded a 2021 self-titled release on Rataplan Records. Smythe also appears on Matana Roberts’s Coin Coin Chapter Five: In the Garden (2023, Constellation), contributing piano, vocals, and tin whistle. He appears on Joshua Modney’s Ascending Primes (2024, Pyroclastic Records), maintaining his connection to the label as both sideman and leader.

Smythe also contributed piano to the Ingrid Laubrock Small Ensemble recording Something To Hunt (2020, Sound American) and appears on Lesley Mok’s The Living Collection (2023, self-released).
