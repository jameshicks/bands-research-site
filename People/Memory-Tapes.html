Memory Tapes

Discogs ID: 1519054
Real name: Dayve Hawk
Aliases: Memory Cassette, Weird Tapes
From: New Jersey (previously Philadelphia)
Active: 2009–2015
Labels: Loog (2009); Something In Construction (2009–2010); Carpark Records (2011–2015)

Solo recording project of Dayve Hawk, former frontman of Philadelphia band Hail Social. Operates under three aliased identities in Discogs — Memory Cassette (early EPs and tracks), Weird Tapes (earlier experiments), and Memory Tapes (the main LP alias). Blends lo-fi electronics, synth-pop, and dream pop. Associated with the 2009–2010 “chillwave” moment. Released a split single “Real Life” with Tanlines in 2010.

Background: Hail Social

Before Memory Tapes, Hawk was frontman of Hail Social, a Philadelphia-based band with members Dan Henry, Dayve Hawk, Matt Maraldo, and Richie Roxas. Hail Social’s timeline and discography are separate from the Memory Tapes project.

Discography

Seek Magic (2009)

Debut LP (Something In Construction SICNOTE 049, UK). Nine tracks plus a bonus CDr disc containing the 22-minute piece “Treeship.” Styles: disco, downtempo, experimental, synth-pop. Tracks: “Swimming Field,” “Bicycle,” “Green Knight,” “Pink Stones,” “Stop Talking,” “Graphics,” “Plain Material,” “Run Out,” + bonus “Treeship.” No personnel credits beyond primary artist on this pressing.

Lead single “Bicycle” was issued separately as a promo CDr on Loog (UK) and remixed by The Horrors (“Bicycle (Horrors Cosmic Dub)”).

Early Singles and Remixes (2009–2010)

  • “Heartbeats” remix (2009, self-released)
  • “Nadine” remix (2009, self-released)
  • “Magic Sequence” DJ mix (2009, self-released, 12 pressings across various formats)
  • “1901” remix (2010, self-released)
  • “Graphics (Re-modal Edit)” promo (2010, Something In Construction SICNOTE 064)
  • “Real Life” split single with Tanlines (2010, Not On Label)

Player Piano (2011)

Second LP (Carpark Records CAK64, US). Twelve tracks: “Musicbox(in),” “Wait In The Dark,” “Today Is Our Life,” “Yes I Know,” “Offers,” “Humming,” “Sunhits,” “Worries,” “Fell Thru Ice,” “Fell Thru Ice II,” “Trance Sisters,” “Musicbox(out).” Artwork by Kazuki Takamatsu; management David Laurie. Hawk credited as written-by, performer, and recorded-by. Styles: electro, leftfield, lo-fi, new wave.

Grace / Confusion (2012)

Third LP (Carpark Records CAK81, US). Six tracks: “Neighborhood Watch,” “Thru The Field,” “Safety,” “Let Me Be,” “Sheila,” “Follow Me.” Cover painting by JKB Fletcher; design by Rob Carmichael (2); mastered by Joe Lambert. Hawk credited as written-by and producer. Styles: electro, indie rock, leftfield, lo-fi.

”Fallout / House On Fire” (2015)

Digital double single (Carpark Records). Two tracks: “Fallout” (4:25) and “House on Fire” (3:59). Hawk credited as performer. Styles: downtempo, post-punk, synth-pop. Last release under the Memory Tapes name in the Discogs catalog.
