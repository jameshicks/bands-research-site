George Lucas

American film director, producer, screenwriter, and chairman of Lucasfilm Ltd. Creator of the Star Wars and Indiana Jones franchises. Born 14 May 1944 in Modesto, California. Discogs artist ID: 369287. Real name: George Walton Lucas Jr.

Overview

George Lucas appears on Discogs primarily as a soundtrack producer, executive producer, liner-notes contributor, and (for the German-language audiobook market) as a primary credited artist. His most significant contribution to recorded music was commissioning and producing the original Star Wars (1977) soundtrack LP with John Williams (4) and the London Symphony Orchestra, establishing the template for Neo-Romantic orchestral scoring in blockbuster cinema. That relationship continued across the entire saga he directed — Episodes IV through VI and I through III — and also extended to his executive-producer role on films directed by others (Indiana Jones, Mishima, Powaqqatsi).

His Discogs credit footprint is modest but spans 1974–2023. The shared-release count with John Williams (4) runs to 7 on Discogs (cross-label pressings aside), and collaborators skew heavily toward German-language audiobook cast members across the dubbed Krieg der Sterne releases.

Notes on the Original Star Wars LP (1977, release 528627)

The 1977 Star Wars LP is credited as Produced by George Lucas on the Discogs record. Primary performers: John Williams (4) (Composed By, Conductor) and London Symphony Orchestra. Recording engineer: Eric Tomlinson. The record was a commercial smash with 159 pressing variants documented on Discogs. This is the release where Lucas’s producer role is most explicitly credited.

Notes on the 1993 Anthology Box (release 660972)

The four-disc Star Wars Trilogy: Original Soundtrack Anthology credits Lucas as Producer on discs 1 and 4 (the Star Wars and Return of the Jedi material). Disc 2-3 (Empire) credits Williams as sole producer. Nick Redman served as Compilation Producer. John Neal handled remix engineering; Eric Tomlinson original recording; Lionel Newman supervised recordings.

Mishima (release 2369955)

Lucas served as executive producer alongside Francis Ford Coppola on Paul Schrader’s Mishima: A Life In Four Chapters (1985). The Kronos Quartet-performed Philip Glass score was released on Nonesuch. This is Lucas’s most notable credit outside the Lucasfilm franchise context.

Scene Connections

Lucas sits at the intersection of the New Hollywood director generation and the film score revival of the 1970s. His decision to commission a live orchestral score (rather than an electronic or rock-oriented one) for Star Wars — and to record it with the LSO at Abbey Road — was a deliberate counter-programming move against the then-dominant trend of pop/rock film soundtracks. The LP’s commercial and critical success helped rehabilitate the film score as a standalone product.

Adjacent figures in this ecosystem, several of whom appear in the Discogs credits chain:

  • Steven Spielberg — co-created Indiana Jones; parallel producing career in the same era
  • Francis Ford Coppola — co-produced Mishima; New Hollywood peer
  • Lawrence Kasdan — screenwriter credited on the Raiders of the Lost Ark story LP (release 4103708)
  • Philip Glass — intersection point via Mishima and Powaqqatsi; different aesthetic tradition

Open Questions

  • The brief mentions 242 shared releases with John Williams (4) — the Discogs collaborators tool returned 7 direct shared releases at distance 1. The 242 figure likely reflects a broader cross-pressing count or a different query methodology. The actual creative overlap is the six films and their compilations, well-documented here.
  • Alex North’s unused THX 1138 score — the brief references this, but no Discogs evidence found. North’s unused 2001 score is a separate matter; Lucas’s THX 1138 (1971) had a score by Lalo Schifrin; no Discogs credit for Lucas on that release was found in this trawl.
  • The Return of the Jedi OST proper (RSO, 1983) — the main US pressing credits are not pulled here. The French pressing (release 5134274) credits Lucas as Executive-Producer; the US pressing credits may differ.
  • No Indiana Jones main OST credits for Lucas found in the DB — the Spielberg-directed Indiana Jones films (Lucas as executive producer) may credit him differently across pressings. The 2023 Dial of Destiny (release 36683401) is the only Indiana Jones OST where his credit is confirmed here.
  • THX 1138 and American Graffiti original US soundtrack LPs — Lucas’s credit on those releases (if any) was not retrieved in this trawl. The Italian American Graffiti pressing (1974) credits him as Film Director only.
