Overview

A Northern California indie and experimental musician and recording engineer whose catalogue is almost entirely a 2020s phenomenon — one release from 2012 gives way to thirteen from 2020 onward. He performs, writes, records, and mixes across five bands: Cindy (57), Flowertown, Hospital (11), The Cherries (6), and April Magazine, working in indie rock, lo-fi, jangle pop, and Britpop-adjacent territory. His deepest collaborative ties are with Karina Gill and Mikey Young, each sharing eighteen releases with him, and his output has centred on Paisley Shirt Records and Tough Love Records.
