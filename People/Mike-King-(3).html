Portland-based drummer, keyboard player, visual artist, and graphic designer. Active as a musician from the late 1970s through the late 1980s, primarily in the Pigface Records orbit; subsequently built a sustained career as a commercial graphic designer through his company Crash Design (also appearing as “Crash”), serving Portland-area bands and labels from the early 1990s onward.

Overview

Mike King appears in the Discogs record under at least ten name variations — most commonly “M.X. King” or “Michael X King” — suggesting deliberate stylistic variety in how he credited himself across projects. Discogs lists no realname, but his design company URL (crashamerica.com) and the Crash Design label entry both confirm a Portland, Oregon base. His Discogs profile states he “now mainly an artist, layout designer for local Portland, Oregon bands and record labels” and that he is the founder of Crash Design.

His musical work clusters entirely in the 1979–1987 period, all within the Pigface Records/Portland experimental scene. His design career begins in earnest around 1992 and extends continuously to at least 2026, taking in clients from Portland indie-punk, Pacific Northwest folk, major-label mainstream (Ben Harper, Jack Johnson), and the long-running Pink Martini catalog.

Early Period: Pigface Records and the Portland Experimental Scene (1979–1987)

Artwork credit: Disco Diarrhea (1979)

Mike King’s earliest Discogs credit is artwork (credited as “MXK”) on Disco Diarrhea (Pigface Records 003, 1979), a split release by Smegma and Cheez-It-Ritz. This places him in the Smegma/Michael Lastra orbit from the label’s very first year of operation.

Rancid Vat — Stampeding Cattle (1982)

Played keyboards and percussion (credited as “Michael X King”) in Rancid Vat on their debut LP Stampeding Cattle (Pig 006, 1982). Rancid Vat was a Portland punk/experimental group formed on New Year’s Eve 1981 by Phil Irwin, Marla Vee, and Steve Wilson. The Stampeding Cattle lineup: Marla Vee (bass), Phil Irwin (guitar), Eric Larsen (guitar/keyboards), Mike King (keyboards/percussion), Steve Wilson (vocals), Tammy Cates (vocals/guitar). Mike King is not listed among Rancid Vat’s official members in Discogs — his involvement appears to have been on this release only; later Rancid Vat lineups do not include him.

Hellcows — G Spot Crush (1987)

Played drums in Hellcows on G Spot Crush (Pigface Records 012, 1987), co-produced by Michael Lastra and the band. Full lineup: Carl Annala (bass), Mike King (drums), Eric Stotik (guitar), Gerry Ostrem (guitar), Brian Koelling (voice). He is listed as a member of Hellcows in Discogs. The Hellcows went on to release Toothless (1988) and Idiodyssey (1989) on Black Label Records and Rockport Records respectively, but Mike King’s Discogs credits do not include those releases; his stint with the band may have ended after the Pigface session.

Design and Visual Art Career (1980–present)

Mike King’s design work spans from early cover credits on Portland indie releases to high-profile commercial album design for nationally distributed artists. He founded Crash Design (also appearing simply as “Crash”), a Portland-based graphic design company. His URL on Discogs is crashamerica.com.

Mid-period: Portland indie scene (1993–1998)

Worked on releases for Portland-adjacent labels including Singles Going Steady (Eager Boy, 1993), Lucky Records (Under The Streetlights, 1995), and scattered one-offs in the Netherlands (photography, 1997) and Switzerland. This period is sparser in Discogs credits.

Film direction

Listed as “Film Director” on Introducing (2015, Bad Diet label) — the only film/video credit in his Discogs record.

Songwriting

Credited as songwriter on one track (B3) of John Shirley & The Screaming Geezers (Reprehensible Records, 2020) — his only songwriting credit outside his performance work. The Screaming Geezers were also engineered by Michael Lastra, maintaining his connection to that circle into 2020.

Later design (2021–2026)

We Must Burn (2024, American Leather / TKO Records) and Let’s Mutate (2026, Sealed Records) show continued activity. The 2026 photography credit (Let’s Mutate, UK label Sealed Records) extends his Discogs record into the future relative to the research date.
