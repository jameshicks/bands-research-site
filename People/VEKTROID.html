VEKTROID

Artist name of Ramona Andra Langley (born Ramona Andra Xavier), born August 19, 1992, Washington State; based in Portland, Oregon. Electronic producer, engineer, composer, graphic designer. Discogs artist ID: 2097749. Over 40 full-length albums across dozens of aliases since 2005.

Note: Monopoly Child Star Searchers is NOT a VEKTROID alias — it is an alias of Spencer Clark. This was a prior misattribution in this vault.

Aliases (per Discogs)

Macintosh Plus, New Dreams Ltd., PrismCorp Virtual Enterprises, Laserdisc Visions, Tanning Salon, Sacred Tapestry, Sapporo Contemporary, DRVG CTRL, Dstnt, ESC 不在, Omegalpha, Peace Forever Eternal, Vectorfray, Vektordrum, Vktrfry, fuji grid tv, 情報デスクVIRTUAL, CTO (2), new dreams ltd initiation tape.

Groups

Palacio Del Rio.

Key Releases

Primary label: PrismCorp. Also released on Olde English Spelling Bee (2 vinyl records), Aguirre Records (2), Beer On The Rug, Afternoons Modeling, Chronos & Vermilion.

  • Telnet Erotika (2010, PrismCorp) — debut
  • フローラルの専門店 (as Macintosh Plus, 2017 OESB pressing, OESB-92) — canonical vaporwave LP
  • Dream Castle (as Tanning Salon, 2017, Olde English Spelling Bee OESB-89) — VEKTROID credited as Recorded By

Connections

  • Macintosh Plus — alias; フローラルの専門店 the defining vaporwave release
  • Tanning Salon — alias; Dream Castle (2017) on OESB
  • Olde English Spelling Bee — production credits and vinyl releases as Macintosh Plus and Tanning Salon
  • PrismCorp — self-run label; primary home of most releases
