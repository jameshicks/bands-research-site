Tim Herzog

Oct 20, 20261 min read

Tim Herzog

Discogs ID: 418072
Also credited as: Timothy Herzog
Based: Montreal, Quebec, Canada
Instruments: Drums, glockenspiel, drone

Drummer and member of Godspeed You Black Emperor! in the post-reunion period (2012–present). First credited on ‘Allelujah! Don’t Bend Ascend (2012) as photographer (“Atonal Canada” photo), then joined as full performer on Asunder, Sweet And Other Distress (2015) and subsequent releases, serving as second drummer alongside Aidan Girt.
