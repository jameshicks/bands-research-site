Todd Tobias is a recording engineer, producer and musician working in Northeast Ohio. Todd is a solo instrumental artist on Tiny Room Records and Hidden Shoal Recordings. He is also a member of the bands Circus Devils (with Guided by Voices’ Robert Pollard) and Clouds Forming Crowns (with brother Tim Tobias).

Overview

Todd Tobias is a Northeast Ohio recording engineer, producer, and multi-instrumentalist whose 520-plus Discogs credits span production, engineering, and keyboards work across more than three decades. As a founding member of Circus Devils alongside Robert Pollard — his most frequent collaborator by a wide margin, with 226 shared credits — and of Clouds Forming Crowns with his brother Tim Tobias, he sits at the centre of the Ohio indie underground. The bulk of his output is tied to the Guided By Voices Inc., Happy Jack Rock Records, Fading Captain Series, and Recordhead labels, while his solo instrumental work has appeared on Tiny Room Records and Hidden Shoal Recordings.

How Todd Tobias first connected with Robert Pollard

No interview or article found explicitly states how Todd Tobias first met or was introduced to Robert Pollard. The following is the best reconstruction from available sources, with the key inference noted as unconfirmed.

What the sources establish:

  • Tim Tobias (2) joined Guided By Voices as bassist in 2000, one year before Circus Devils formed. Wikipedia lists him as a GBV member 2000–2003. (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guided_by_Voices, accessed 2026-10-25)
  • Circus Devils formed in 2001 as a psychedelic rock side project, consisting of Pollard (vocals/lyrics), Todd Tobias (music/production), and Tim Tobias (music). First album Ringworm Interiors (2001). (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circus_Devils, accessed 2026-10-25)
  • Todd Tobias then began producing GBV’s Universal Truths and Cycles (2002), his first GBV production credit. (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_Tobias, accessed 2026-10-25)
  • Todd is based in Northeast Ohio; Pollard in Dayton, Ohio — both within Ohio’s indie underground. (Discogs artist profile; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_Tobias)

Most plausible inference (unconfirmed by any source):

Tim Tobias’s membership in GBV from 2000 is the most plausible bridge: Tim likely introduced his brother Todd to Pollard, leading to the Circus Devils formation in 2001 and then Todd’s GBV production role from 2002. No interview confirms this chain explicitly.
