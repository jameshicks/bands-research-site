Overview

Courtney Taylor-Taylor is an American singer-songwriter, guitarist, and producer born in Portland, Oregon on 20 July 1967, best known as the frontman and primary creative force of The Dandy Warhols. His Discogs credits number over 630 spanning from 1994, covering alternative, indie, psychedelic, and stoner rock territory. The band’s catalogue moved from Tim/Kerr in the mid-1990s through a long run on Capitol Records and Parlophone into the 2010s, with their own Beat The World Records handling much of the output from 2004 onward. Beyond the core Dandy Warhols lineup of Zia McCabe, Peter Holmström, and Brent DeBoer, recurring production collaborators include Tony Lash, Dave Sardy, and Tchad Blake.

Beat The World Records — origins and Capitol friction

Two-phase founding. Beat The World Records (BTW) appears first as a label credit in April 2004 on The Black Album / Come On Feel The Dandy Warhols (BTW CD-001A), a double-disc set released initially only through the band’s website. That release collected a Capitol-rejected 1996 demo album (disc one) and B-sides/outtakes (disc two). Wikipedia describes the demo as “rejected by Capitol Records and dismissed by the band themselves.” https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Black_Album/Come_On_Feel_The_Dandy_Warhols (as of 2026-11-20)

The label was then formally constituted in 2007–2008: the band conceived it in 2007, signed initial artists (Logan Lynn, The Upsidedown), and officially launched it in 2008 as a Caroline Records/EMI third-party label when they split with Capitol. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_the_World_Records (as of 2026-11-20)

Capitol friction. The proximate cause of the Capitol split was the Odditorium or Warlords of Mars cycle (recorded April 2004–January 2005, released September 2005). Taylor-Taylor later characterised it as “a fuck you record. Drop us, please, we want off your label.” Alternative Press read the album as “a giant ‘fuck you’ to [their] record label.” An earlier grievance — Capitol remixing material without the band’s involvement — reportedly pushed them toward the exit before that. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odditorium_or_Warlords_of_Mars (as of 2026-11-20)

After the split. …Earth to the Dandy Warhols… (2008) was the first full album on BTW. The label cut ties with Caroline/EMI in 2010 and began working with The End Records in 2011. Taylor-Taylor acknowledged the operational challenges, saying in a 2010 interview: “We’re terrible at business, terrible. We don’t know what we’re doing. It’s like trying to have children run a household.” Logan Lynn (a BTW signee) noted the label lacked “radio, no real distribution, no licensing, no PR.” https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_the_World_Records (as of 2026-11-20)

Conflict note. Wikipedia’s Beat The World Records article states the label was “founded in 2008,” but both Discogs and Wikipedia’s own Black Album article confirm the name was used as early as April 2004 (catalog number BTW CD-001A). The 2004 use appears to be an informal/pre-label imprint credit; the 2008 date marks formal incorporation as a Caroline/EMI third-party label.
