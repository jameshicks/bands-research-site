Felix Cavaliere

Keyboardist, vocalist, producer. Discogs artist ID: 304241. Born 1942 in Pelham, NY. Best known as primary creative force of The Rascals (organ, piano, lead vocals, co-producer). Inducted into Rock and Roll Hall of Fame with the Rascals in 1997.

Overview

Principal songwriter (with Eddie Brigati) and producer for the Rascals across their full Atlantic and Columbia runs (1965–1972). Served as sole producer on the two Columbia albums (Peaceful World, The Island Of Real) after Brigati’s departure. Key instrumental voice: Hammond organ was central to the band’s blue-eyed soul sound.

Also played organ and acted as co-producer on Side A of Laura Nyro’s Christmas And The Beads Of Sweat (Columbia, 1970). Also played keyboards/piano on tracks 7 and 10 of Nyro’s Nested (Columbia, 1978).
