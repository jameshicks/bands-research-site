Ryan Hadlock

Discogs ID: 305476 Active: 1996–2024 Origin: Pacific Northwest, USA Studios: Bear Creek Studio (Woodinville, WA), Studio Litho (Seattle, WA) Roles: Engineer, Mixer, Producer Name variations on sleeves: Hadlock, Ryan Hadloc

Son of Joe Hadlock (Discogs ID: 611795). Based in Woodinville, Washington, near Seattle. Ryan Hadlock came up as an assistant engineer at Bear Creek Studio in the mid-1990s — most visibly on Foo Fighters’ The Colour And The Shape (1997) — before taking on full production and engineering responsibilities from 1998 onward. He built a body of work that bridges the Pacific Northwest indie underground (The Black Heart Procession, Blonde Redhead, Stephen Malkmus & The Jicks) and later mainstream folk and rock (The Lumineers, Brandi Carlile, Vance Joy), with Bear Creek remaining his primary recording home throughout.

Notes:

  • On The Black Heart Procession 2 (1999), Hadlock handled tracks 1–9; Rafter Roberts handled tracks 10–11. Zack Reinig (credited as “Zach Reinig”) served as second engineer across both sessions.
  • On Three (2000), Hadlock and the band co-produced and co-mixed; Reinig again credited for additional recording.
  • On Melody Of Certain Damaged Lemons (2000), Hadlock co-produced alongside Guy Picciotto (Fugazi). Howie Weinberg mastered.
  • On Pig Lib (2003), Lawrence Crane recorded additional tracks (1–5, 9, 11); Hadlock recorded and mixed the remainder.
  • On The Lumineers (2012), Kevin Augunas mixed; Bob Ludwig mastered.
  • On The Firewatcher’s Daughter (2015), co-produced with Brandi Carlile, Phil Hanseroth, Tim Hanseroth, and Trina Shoemaker. Mike McCready (Pearl Jam) plays electric guitar on one track.

Studio Context

Bear Creek Studio

Located in Woodinville, Washington. Hadlock’s primary home base for virtually his entire career. His father Joe Hadlock is associated with the facility. The studio was the site of early assistant work (Foo Fighters’ The Colour And The Shape, 1997 — credited as “Assistant Engineers, Bear Creek”) and later of the full productions including The Lumineers’ self-titled debut (2012) and Brandi Carlile’s The Firewatcher’s Daughter (2015).

Studio Litho

Seattle recording facility also associated with Hadlock, mentioned in biographical sources. Less frequently cited in Discogs credits than Bear Creek.
