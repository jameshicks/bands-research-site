John Hanti

Discogs ID: 309135
Born: 5 June 1951, Farrell, Pennsylvania, USA

American record producer, venue operator, and backline/touring industry entrepreneur. Co-founder of ROIR with Neil Cooper (3). Later founder and president of SST Studios & Rentals (Weehawken, NJ).

Background

Born in Farrell, Pennsylvania, where his parents owned Club Haiti, a chitlin’ circuit nightclub. Grew up hearing Ray Charles, Sam Cooke, Little Richard, Jackie Wilson, and Faye Adams from infancy. Learned organ as a child; performed in high school rock bands and later in a Pittsburgh-area band called Virgin.

1971: relocated to South Florida, working as a booking agent at Miami–Fort Lauderdale clubs including the Flying Machine, the She, and Big Daddy’s Clubs. 1978: moved to New York City, mentored by industry figures Nat Weiss, Walter Hofer, and Roy Cicala.

In Manhattan, opened the Ugly Door Studio on West 30th Street — a rehearsal space frequented by John Cale and Patti Smith. Acquired vans and instruments to support touring musicians, laying the groundwork for SST.

ROIR

Co-founded ROIR with Neil Cooper (3) as “America’s first all-cassette record label” (described as “only the second in the world” at the time of launch in 1979/1981). Credited as Executive Producer throughout the early ROIR cassette catalog.

The ’80s Club

Co-operated The ’80s, a live music venue on the Upper East Side of Manhattan, with Neil Cooper. Also co-opened Plexus, a performance arts space, with Louis Tropea and Sondro Dernini.

Recorded the live performance that became Meredith Monk’s Turtle Dreams album.

SST Studios & Rentals

1983: Founded Studios, Systems, and Transport (SST) in Manhattan. The trigger: Ruth Polsky — Danceteria’s talent buyer — needed logistical solutions for British post-punk bands making their first US appearances. SST provided backline and transport for the wave of British acts arriving during the early 1980s.

Acts served include: The Smiths, New Order, Happy Mondays, Sisters of Mercy, Quando Quango, Gene Loves Jezebel, The Pixies, The Cure, Killing Joke, Nick Cave & the Bad Seeds, The Damned. Hanti also served as front-of-house sound engineer for Sisters of Mercy and Killing Joke.

In the late 1980s, relocated SST to a converted warehouse in Weehawken, New Jersey (west bank of the Hudson). Expanded from backline rental to full-service recording, rehearsal, and touring operations.

2012: involved in a serious auto accident; the following month, Superstorm Sandy devastated the company. Rebuilt and restored operations; remains president of SST Studios & Rentals.
