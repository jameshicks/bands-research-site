Joe Tomino

US drummer and percussionist, based in New York City. Discogs artist ID: 512339. Also credited as “Jo Tomino” and “Tomino.” Website: joetomino.com.

Overview

Joe Tomino is primarily known as the drummer of Dub Trio, the NYC instrumental trio he co-founded with guitarist Dave Holmes (4) (also credited as DP Holmes) and bassist Stu Brooks. The group make mostly live dub music with minimal overdubs, first touring the NYC area unnamed before settling on the descriptive tag “Dub Trio.” Tomino has played drums, percussion, melodica, keyboard, piano, and toy piano across the Dub Trio catalog — a wider instrumental range than his primary role suggests.

Outside Dub Trio, he is a listed member of Battle of Mice, the post-metal duo of Julie Christmas and Josh Graham, and played drums on their 2008 Jesu split. He also co-led Birth, a jazz-influenced trio with saxophonist Joshua Smith (2) and bassist Jeremy Bleich, active in the late 1990s and returning for a 2019 release. He played in Like Yesterday alongside Dave Holmes (guitar) and Seva Granik (guitar) — a precursor outfit to Dub Trio — and is listed as a member of Like Yesterday.

A session credit of note: Tomino co-wrote the Dub Trio track “B5” on Mike Patton’s Peeping Tom (3) self-titled album (Anticon, 2006). In 2011 he drummed on alternating tracks of Outernational’s Eyes On Fire (Outernational Music), sharing drum duties with Gil Sharone on a record that features Tom Morello on one track.

Scene Connections

Tomino sits at an intersection of several NYC underground networks:

  • Dub-influenced heavy rock: Dub Trio are explicitly dub-first, making live dub with minimal overdubs. Their ROIR connection ties them to the label’s long reggae/avant-punk history.
  • Post-metal / Neurosis orbit: Via Battle of Mice, Tomino overlaps with the Neurosis/Neurot ecosystem — Josh Graham (touring Neurosis guitarist), Joel Hamilton (A Storm of Light, Book of Knots), Tony Maimone (Pere Ubu veteran), and Justin K Broadrick (Jesu, ex-Godflesh).
  • Ipecac / Patton network: Dub Trio released Another Sound Is Dying on Ipecac Recordings (Mike Patton’s label), and Patton wrote lyrics for one track. Dub Trio co-wrote a song on Patton’s Peeping Tom LP.
  • Jazz-inflected avant-rock: Birth (1999–2019, with saxophonist Joshua Smith) reflects a more jazz-leaning side to Tomino’s work.
