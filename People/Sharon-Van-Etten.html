Sharon Van Etten

American singer-songwriter. Discogs artist ID: 1611151. Released on Jagjaguwar. Contributed vocals to Beirut’s “A Candle’s Fire” and “Payne’s Bay” on The Rip Tide (2011, Pompeii Records (2)).

Heather Woods Broderick contributes backing vocals on Remind Me Tomorrow (Jagjaguwar, 2019).
