Sid Vicious

Born John Simon Ritchie (1957–1979). British punk musician. Member of Flowers of Romance (1976–77, vocals and saxophone); bassist and vocalist for Sex Pistols (1977–1978). The band name “Flowers of Romance” was also used as an on-stage jam title by the Sex Pistols, and later by Public Image Ltd for their 1981 album.
