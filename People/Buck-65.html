Buck 65

Real name: Richard Terfry
Origin: Mount Uniacke, Nova Scotia, Canada

Canadian rapper, DJ, and broadcaster. Known for a literary, abstract style rooted in alt-country, hip hop, and spoken word. Long-running solo career; collaborated with Dose One on North American Adonis (2023, Handsmade).
