Overview

Kris Wheat is a New York-based musician who played bass in the Dallas, Texas slowcore/post-rock bands Bedhead and Day of the Double Agent. His released catalogue runs from the mid-1990s through the 2020s, with Bedhead credits forming the dominant thread — those recordings appeared on Trance Syndicate and Rough Trade in the 1990s and were later compiled and reissued by Numero Group across 2014–2023. Beyond the core Bedhead circle of Trini Martinez, Bubba and Matt Kadane, and Tench Coxe, he shares ten credits with Adam Wiltzie, suggesting a significant collaboration outside that group.
