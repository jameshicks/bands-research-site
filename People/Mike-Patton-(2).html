Mike Patton (2)

Discogs ID: 464755
Role: Bass, Producer
Also credited as: Patton
Note: Distinct from Mike Patton of Faith No More (different Discogs entry)

Bassist and producer based in Southern California. Founding member of The Middle Class (Santa Ana, CA), playing bass on their landmark Out of Vogue EP (1978). After The Middle Class became a significant production figure in LA/OC hardcore and SST-orbit indie rock.
