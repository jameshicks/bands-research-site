Howard Ashman

American lyricist and playwright. Born Howard Elliot Ashman, Baltimore, MD, May 17, 1950. Died New York City, March 14, 1991. Discogs artist ID: 546816.

Core creative partner of composer Alan Menken (Discogs ID: 546819). Together they wrote the songs for the off-Broadway musical Little Shop of Horrors (1982) and the Disney animated features The Little Mermaid (1989) and Beauty and the Beast (1991). Ashman also contributed songs to Aladdin (1992) before his death, with Tim Rice completing the lyric work. He died of AIDS-related complications at age 40, before Beauty and the Beast was released. He also served as executive producer on Beauty and the Beast.

Won two Academy Awards (including a posthumous win for “Beauty and the Beast”), two Grammy Awards, and two Golden Globe Awards. Nominated five additional times for Academy Awards.

Biography

Early Career and Theater (1970s–1981)

Ashman studied at Indiana University and later at Boston University, then moved to New York City to pursue theater. He became artistic director of the WPA Theater off-Broadway in New York in the late 1970s. He worked on several musicals before the breakthrough that would define his career, writing both book and lyrics for productions including God Bless You, Mr. Rosewater (based on the Kurt Vonnegut novel). God Bless You, Mr. Rosewater (1979) was his first collaboration with Menken, predating Little Shop of Horrors. Discogs does not carry a cast recording for this show.

Little Shop of Horrors (1982)

The off-Broadway musical Little Shop of Horrors, based on the 1960 Roger Corman film, opened at the WPA Theater in 1982 and transferred to the Orpheum Theatre off-Broadway for a long run. Ashman wrote book and lyrics; Menken composed the music. The original cast recording was released on Geffen Records (ID: 821) in 1982. Ashman and Menken are co-billed as primary artists on the album (Discogs release ID: 1577814).

Credits on the 1982 original cast recording:

  • Primary artists: Howard Ashman (lyrics) & Alan Menken (music)
  • Producer: Phil Ramone
  • Musical Director / Keyboards: Robert Billig
  • Arrangements / Keyboards: Robbie Merkin
  • Mastered By: Ted Jensen
  • Engineer: Bradshaw Leigh

When the 1986 film adaptation directed by Frank Oz was released — starring Rick Moranis and Ellen Greene — a new soundtrack album appeared on Geffen Records (Discogs release ID: 1324453). Key cast vocalists: Rick Moranis, Ellen Greene, Tichina Arnold, Tisha Campbell, Levi Stubbs (as Audrey II’s voice), and Michelle Weeks. Musical arrangements by Robbie Merkin; produced by Bob Gaudio.

The Disney Renaissance (1986–1991)

Ashman and Menken were recruited by Walt Disney Pictures in the mid-1980s to write songs for a new animated feature. The result was The Little Mermaid (1989), which marked the beginning of the Disney Renaissance — the revitalization of Disney’s animated musical tradition after a period of commercial decline.

The Little Mermaid (1989) — Ashman wrote lyrics; co-produced the songs with Menken; also produced the soundtrack album. Released on Walt Disney Records (ID: 30630). The title song “Under the Sea” won the Academy Award for Best Original Song. Discogs release ID: 769185 (141 pressings, 1989–).

Additional producer on the soundtrack: Robert Kraft. Conductor: J.A.C. Redford. Arranger: Robbie Merkin.

Ashman was then brought into the development of Beauty and the Beast and Aladdin simultaneously. He developed several songs for Aladdin (including early versions that were later reworked) before his health deteriorated severely.

Beauty and the Beast (1991) — Ashman was credited as executive producer of the film itself — an unusual production credit for a lyricist — in addition to writing the lyrics and co-producing the songs. He died on March 14, 1991, before the film’s theatrical release in November 1991. The title song won the Academy Award for Best Original Song (posthumous), and Menken won Best Original Score.

OST released on Walt Disney Records (Discogs release ID: 2160344). Songs produced by Ashman and Menken jointly. Score produced by Menken. Voice cast on the soundtrack: Paige O’Hara (Belle), Robby Benson (Beast), Jerry Orbach (Lumière), Angela Lansbury (Mrs. Potts), David Ogden Stiers (Cogsworth), Richard White (Gaston), Jesse Corti (LeFou). Orchestrations: Danny Troob.

Aladdin (1992, Posthumous)

Ashman had written lyrics for several songs intended for Aladdin before his death, including early drafts of “Proud of Your Boy” (cut from the 1992 film) and other material. After Ashman died, Tim Rice was brought in to complete the lyric work. Ashman is credited as primary artist on the 1992 OST alongside Menken and Rice (Discogs release ID: 1446510, 135 pressings). His contributions were documented and later released more fully on the 1994 box set The Music Behind the Magic.

The Music Behind the Magic (1994)

A 4-CD box set released on Walt Disney Records (Discogs release ID: 7744213), titled The Music Behind the Magic, documented the making of The Little Mermaid, Beauty and the Beast, and Aladdin, including work tapes and demos. Ashman and Menken are both credited with “Vocals [Work Tape And Demo]” — Ashman’s voice appears on early recordings made during the songwriting process. This release represents the most complete documentation of the Ashman/Menken creative process available on Discogs.

Scene Connections

  • Alan Menken — composer on all Ashman-credited works; the partnership is Discogs’ most documented Ashman relationship (772 shared releases including all international pressings and reissues)
  • Tim Rice — lyricist who completed Aladdin after Ashman’s death; shares 267 releases across the Menken/Disney catalog
  • Phil Ramone — produced the Little Shop of Horrors original cast recording; Ramone brought Broadway recording expertise to the project
  • Robbie Merkin — musical arranger who bridged both the theater and Disney phases; worked on Little Shop (1982) and The Little Mermaid (1989)
  • Angela Lansbury — performed the title song of Beauty and the Beast; her recording was the definitive version credited on the OST
  • Bruce Botnick — core audio engineer/mixer for the Disney soundtracks; appears on nearly all Menken/Ashman releases on Walt Disney Records
  • Danny Troob — primary orchestrator across the Disney Renaissance; appears on Beauty and the Beast, Aladdin, and The Music Behind the Magic
  • Walt Disney Records — primary release home for the Disney Renaissance; Ashman is among the label’s most-credited primary artists
  • Geffen Records — released both Little Shop of Horrors recordings (1982 OCA and 1986 film soundtrack)

Name Variations (Discogs)

Ashman’s name appears in numerous variants across international pressings: “H. Ashman,” “Howard E. Ashman,” “Howard Elliott Ashman,” “A. Ashman,” as well as Cyrillic (Ховард Эшман) and Japanese (ハワード・アシュマン) transliterations. The canonical Discogs name is “Howard Ashman” (realname: Howard Elliot Ashman).

Open Questions

  • God Bless You, Mr. Rosewater (1979, first Menken/Ashman collaboration) — no cast recording appears in Discogs; unclear if one was ever released commercially
  • Ashman’s specific lyric contributions to the early Aladdin development (pre-Rice) — the 1994 Music Behind the Magic box set contains work tapes, but a complete accounting of which songs he wrote fully vs. partially is not determinable from Discogs alone
  • Stage work prior to Little Shop of Horrors (1970s WPA Theater productions) — not represented in Discogs
  • The posthumous Academy Award for “Beauty and the Beast” — Discogs does not track awards; this is confirmed from the Discogs profile note but not sourced to a specific release entry
  • Whether Ashman had any involvement in Aladdin songs beyond what appeared on the OST (e.g., “Proud of Your Boy,” which was cut from the 1992 film and only appeared on the 1994 Broadway cast recording) — the 2014 Broadway OCA does credit Ashman as primary artist, confirming “Proud of Your Boy” was retained for the stage version
