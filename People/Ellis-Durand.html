Ellis Durand

Discogs artist ID: 16376497 Role: Bassist, additional vocalist Band: Wet Leg Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/ellisdurand

Ellis Durand is the bassist and an additional vocalist in Wet Leg, joining the band’s live and studio lineup ahead of their second album Moisturizer (2025). Durand replaced Michael Champion, who held the bass role on the debut album Wet Leg (2022). As a member of the full five-piece — alongside Rhian Teasdale, Hester Chambers, Joshua Mobaraki, and Henry Holmes — Durand contributed both as a performer and as a co-writer on Moisturizer.

No realname, aliases, or prior discographic credits are listed on Discogs outside of Wet Leg. Discogs offers no profile text or background information; the Instagram link is the only external reference recorded.

Wet Leg

Wet Leg was founded in 2019 by Rhian Teasdale and Hester Chambers on the Isle of Wight. The debut album Wet Leg (2022) was produced primarily by Dan Carey and shortlisted for the Mercury Prize; Michael Champion played bass on that record. By the time of Moisturizer, the studio lineup had expanded to the full five-piece with Durand on bass.
