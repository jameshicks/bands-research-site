Stewart McFayden

Scottish drummer. Discogs artist ID: 1335923. Also credited as Stewart McFadyen.

Drummer and occasional backing vocalist of Close Lobsters (Paisley, Scotland). Active throughout the band’s original run and the post-2013 reformation. Co-wrote several tracks including “Foxheads” and “Mother Of God”.
