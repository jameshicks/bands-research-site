Julia Shapiro

Seattle-based vocalist, guitarist, and multi-instrumentalist. Discogs artist ID: 3440482. Lead member of Chastity Belt, solo artist on Hardly Art, and co-founder of Childbirth (2) and Who Is She?. Name variations in credits: “JS”, “Julia”.

Biography

Formation and Early Work (2010–2013)

Julia Shapiro formed Chastity Belt while studying at Whitman College in Walla Walla, Washington alongside Annie Truscott, Gretchen Grimm, and Lydia Lund. The band’s first release was the self-released cassette Dude (2012, Not On Label), credited to Shapiro on guitar and vocals. Their debut LP No Regerts followed in 2013 on Help Yourself Records, with Shapiro playing guitar and sharing lead vocals.

Childbirth and Chastity Belt Peak Years (2014–2017)

In 2014 Shapiro co-founded the comedic punk side project Childbirth (2) with Bree McKenna (of TacocaT) and Stacy Peck. The trio released the LP It’s A Girl! that year (Not On Label); Shapiro played guitar and vocals and also handled the artwork. Childbirth released a second LP, Women’s Rights, on Suicide Squeeze in 2015.

Chastity Belt signed to Hardly Art for Time To Go Home (2015) and I Used To Spend So Much Time Alone (2017). On the 2017 record Shapiro played lead vocals and guitar across most tracks, plus drums on one track (“Stuck”). Producer Matthew Simms (Mount Sims) recorded and mixed the LP.

Solo Debut and Chastity Belt Self-Titled (2019)

2019 was a dual-release year. Shapiro’s solo debut Perfect Version (Hardly Art, HAR-114) appeared alongside Chastity Belt’s self-titled LP (HAR-117). Perfect Version was substantially a solo construction: Shapiro wrote all songs, played all other instruments, recorded most tracks herself, and handled mixing on most of the record (with assistance from David Hrivnak on select tracks). Ian LeSage recorded and mixed the remaining tracks. Heba Kadry mastered. Back cover and label photos by Eleanor Petry; front cover photo by Barbara Shapiro (2). The album was recorded partly at home and partly with LeSage.

For the self-titled Chastity Belt LP, the band co-produced with Melina Duterte (Jay Som), who had an emerging reputation as an in-demand indie producer. Andrew Sarlo mixed; Heba Kadry again mastered. Shapiro handled the layout design.

Zorked, Who Is She?, and Later Work (2021–present)

Shapiro released Zorked in 2021 (Not On Label) as her second solo LP. It appears on Discogs with no additional credits — a sparse, self-contained release.

Around this period she was also active in Who Is She?, an all-female Seattle group featuring Bree McKenna, Emily Nokes, and Robin Edwards (3) (Lisa Prank). The project’s LP Goddess Energy came out in 2023 on Daughter Records; Shapiro played drums, keyboards, and backing vocals — a role shift away from her usual frontperson position. Jenn Champion mixed the album.

In 2022 Shapiro played electric guitar and sang on Kurt Vile’s (Watch My Moves) (Verve Records). She also continued design/layout work for several other artists and labels (Life Tax 2022, various Suicide Squeeze releases 2024–2025).

Chastity Belt’s Live Laugh Love appeared on Suicide Squeeze in 2024, the band’s move away from Hardly Art.
