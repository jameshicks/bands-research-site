Keith LeBlanc

Real name: Keith A. LeBlanc. Discogs artist ID: 40521. American drummer and producer. Born November 10, 1954, Bristol, Connecticut. Died April 4, 2024, aged 69. Obituaries published by On-U Sound and Pitchfork.

Founding member of the Sugar Hill Records house band alongside Doug Wimbish and Skip McDonald (born Bernard Alexander). Core member of Tackhead and Mark Stewart And The Maffia in the On-U Sound orbit. Founded Blanc Records as his own production imprint. His first solo album Major Malfunction (1986) is cited as the earliest Tackhead recording and was influential in the industrial scene.

Aliases on Discogs: DJ Spike, Raw (17), Sugarhill All-Stars.

Drumming Style

LeBlanc’s approach was considered modern at the time: exceptionally precise 16th-note bass-drum patterns and syncopated hi-hat work rather than straight 8th or 16th notes. He moved fluidly between acoustic kit, Simmons electronic drums, and DMX drum programming as the 1980s production landscape shifted.

Early Career: Wood, Brass & Steel and Sugar Hill (c.1976–1983)

LeBlanc, Doug Wimbish, and Skip McDonald first played together in Wood, Brass & Steel, a funk ensemble that became the nucleus of the Sugar Hill Records session band, known formally as the Sugarhill Gang Band.

As the house rhythm section from around 1979 through 1983, the trio played on the vast majority of Sugar Hill’s output, including:

In 1983 LeBlanc produced and arranged “No Sell Out” for Tommy Boy Records — a track built around archival Malcolm X speeches, with LeBlanc credited for music and production. The track was a pivotal early example of sampling political speech as musical raw material.

Tommy Boy Period (1984–1986)

In 1984 LeBlanc left Sugar Hill and moved to Tommy Boy Records as producer and arranger. Credits from this period include:

Meeting Adrian Sherwood and On-U Sound (1984–)

In 1984 LeBlanc met Adrian Sherwood and began working with him on On-U Sound and associated projects. Wimbish and McDonald followed; Sherwood described the three as the rhythmic foundation of On-U’s output. Their collaboration ran continuously from 1984 through the 2010s.

Tackhead

Tackhead — LeBlanc (drums/keyboards), Doug Wimbish (bass), Skip McDonald (guitar), Adrian Sherwood (production/mixing) — is Discogs-listed with vocalist Bernard Fowler and occasional vocalist Gary Clail.

LeBlanc’s solo debut Major Malfunction (World Records, 1986) is considered the proto-Tackhead record. Produced as Fats Comet (Sherwood + LeBlanc + Wimbish + McDonald), it featured all three rhythm section members plus Nick Plytas (keyboards).

Notable Session and Production Credits

Nine Inch Nails

LeBlanc produced alongside Adrian Sherwood on two key NIN releases:

ReleaseYearRole
”Down In It” 12”1989Producer (Reproduced By), Remix
Pretty Hate Machine1989Producer

The Pretty Hate Machine production team also included Flood, John Fryer, and Trent Reznor. LeBlanc and Sherwood contributed one of the record’s key industrial-funk production textures. These credits made LeBlanc one of the few people who spans the Sugar Hill hip-hop world and the TVT industrial/alternative world in a single decade.
