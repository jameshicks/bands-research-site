Kurt Ballou

Discogs ID: 261312
Real name: Kurt Parker Ballou
Born: February 1, 1974
Origin: Massachusetts, USA
Aliases: Dale Nixon (5)
Active: ~1992–present

Guitarist of Converge and owner/engineer of GodCity Studio (Salem, MA — Discogs label ID 284882). One of the most prolific producers in American heavy music; production credits span 1996 to 2025 across hardcore, metal, grindcore, and adjacent genres. Production career is roughly parallel with his performance career — both originating in the Massachusetts hardcore scene in the early-mid 1990s.

Formative Period (1996–2002)

Connection to Full Of Hell

Produced both solo studio albums Full Of Hell made in their peak Profound Lore / Relapse period:

  • Trumpeting Ecstasy (2017, Profound Lore PFL 184) — 25 shared Discogs releases total
  • Weeping Choir (2019, Relapse RR7421)

Also credited on Trumpeting Ecstasy Remixes (2017) where a JK Broadrick remix appears. Brad Boatright handled mastering on both records; Ballou handled recording and production.

Labels

Long association with several key labels: Hydra Head Records (early Cave In), Epitaph (Converge), Relapse Records (Full Of Hell, Torche), Sargent House (Russian Circles), Southern Lord (Nails), Deathwish (various hardcore acts).
