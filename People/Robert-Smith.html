Robert Smith

UK vocalist, guitarist, lyricist, and co-producer. Born 21 April 1959, Blackfield, Hampshire. Discogs artist ID: 73783. Founder and only constant member of The Cure, active from 1976 to the present.

Overview

Smith is the primary creative force behind The Cure — songwriter, vocalist, and co-producer across the band’s entire catalog. During the 1984-1992 commercial peak he co-produced all studio albums jointly with David M. Allen. Also had a parallel involvement with Siouxsie And The Banshees (guitarist 1982-1984) and briefly with The Glove (a side project with Steve Severin).
