Aug 08, 20262 min read

Carlos Niño

Discogs ID: 273087
Origin: Los Angeles, CA

Producer, percussionist, and curator. Co-founder of Build An Ark (2001) alongside vocalist Dwight Trible. Member of the LA creative music scene connecting jazz, spiritual music, and experimental electronics. Appeared on Phil Ranelin’s Infinite Expressions (2022, ORG Music). Known for connecting older generations of jazz (Ranelin, Nate Morgan) with younger LA improvisers.
