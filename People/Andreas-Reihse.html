Andreas Reihse

Discogs ID: 416309
Alias: April (Discogs artist ID: 24433)
Name variations: A.R., Andreas, Andreas Rheise, Reihse, Reise
Origin: Düsseldorf, Germany
Website: reihse.de · andreasreihse.bandcamp.com

Musician, composer, and visual artist; keyboardist and core member of Kreidler (Düsseldorf minimal/electronic rock group). Also a member of La! NEU? — connecting him directly to Klaus Dinger’s post-Neu! orbit. Beyond Kreidler, maintains an extensive parallel career spanning solo records, duo collaborations, several side projects, and one-off outside credits.
