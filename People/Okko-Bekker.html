Overview

German multi-instrumentalist (sitar, synthesizer, keyboards) and producer, born 30 July 1947 in Heidelberg and based in Hamburg. He has been active in experimental and krautrock-adjacent music since 1970, with early recordings on Polydor and BASF giving way to a long association with the Hamburg experimental imprint Die Stadt (11 works, 1999–2025) and Auf Abwegen. His most substantial working relationship is with Asmus Tietchens, with whom he shares 98 credits — by a wide margin his closest collaborator — suggesting a sustained duo or co-production partnership rather than incidental sessions. He has been a member of Liliental and several overlapping ensemble credits under the “Okko, Lonzo…” banner.

Notes (web)

Connection to Discos Esplendor Geometrico (Spain, 1986–1990)

Bekker’s three credits on Discos Esplendor Geometrico are all as producer on Asmus Tietchens releases, not as a performing artist in his own right. The label, founded in Madrid in 1985 by members of the Spanish industrial band Esplendor Geométrico, released work by several non-Spanish artists from the industrial/experimental underground. The three Tietchens LPs Bekker produced for the label are:

ReleaseCat. No.YearNotes
Geboren um zu dienenEG 0031986Tietchens’ “industrial” LP; recorded at Audiplex Studios, Hamburg
NotturnoEG-0111987Originally released on DEG; later reissued on Die Stadt (DS 102)
Stupor MundiEGD 0181990

All three were recorded in Hamburg (at Audiplex Studios, per the “Geboren um zu dienen” listing). Bekker’s production role on Tietchens releases — which started in 1979 and continued for decades — brought these Hamburg recordings to the Spanish label rather than any direct relationship between Bekker and Esplendor Geométrico independently. The connection was Tietchens, not Bekker himself.
