Andy Metcalfe

Andy Metcalfe

Aug 02, 20263 min read

Andy Metcalfe

Discogs ID: 391377
Origin: UK

Bassist, keyboards player, and vocalist. Member of The Soft Boys and a founding member of Robyn Hitchcock & The Egyptians (1984–1994). Notable absence from the 2001 Soft Boys reunion. Also worked with Squeeze (appears on Hitchcock’s Globe Of Frogs-era circle). 4 shared Discogs releases with Hitchcock in the Egyptians/Midnight Music era.

2001 Soft Boys Reunion — Absence

Metcalfe was not part of the 2001–2002 Soft Boys reunion that produced Nextdoorland (Matador, 2002). The reunion lineup was Robyn Hitchcock, Kimberley Rew, Morris Windsor, and Matthew Seligman on bass. Wikipedia’s Soft Boys article states the band “briefly came together again in 2001 without Metcalfe” but gives no reason. (Wikipedia — The Soft Boys, accessed 2026-06-28.)

Structural context: The 2001 reunion was explicitly framed around the 20th anniversary of Underwater Moonlight (1980). Seligman — not Metcalfe — played bass on that album; Metcalfe had left the band by late 1979 before it was recorded. Reuniting the Underwater Moonlight lineup therefore meant Seligman rather than Metcalfe by default. The Harvard Crimson’s 2001 preview describes the reunited lineup as “Hitchcock, guitarist Kimberley Rew, bass player Matthew Seligman and drummer Morris Windsor” without comment on Metcalfe. (Harvard Crimson, 2001-04-20.) Trouser Press likewise lists Seligman and Windsor for the reunion with no mention of Metcalfe. (Trouser Press.)

Groundwork for the 2001 reunion: A 2001 interview notes that Rew and Hitchcock had repaired their relationship via Hitchcock’s 1999 album Jewels for Sophia (on which Rew played), and the Underwater Moonlight reissue provided the occasion. No source discusses any outreach to or decision about Metcalfe. (Durian Apocalypse, 2001.)

Metcalfe’s activities around 2001–2002: His official bio records production work with Glenn Tilbrook (The Incomplete Glenn Tilbrook, 2000) and a tour/live album with Julian Dawson in Germany in 2002 (Cologne Again Or). No public statement from Metcalfe or any other band member addresses his absence from the reunion. (andymetcalfe.com/bio, accessed 2026-06-28.)

Earlier reunion (1994): Metcalfe was involved in 1994 UK reunion shows tied to a Soft Boys box set release. That lineup included Rew, Seligman, and Jim Melton alongside Hitchcock, Windsor, and Metcalfe — an expanded configuration rather than one definitive bass player.

Conclusion: No public source — interviews with Hitchcock, Rew, Windsor, Seligman, or Metcalfe himself — explains the reason for Metcalfe’s absence in 2001. The most plausible structural explanation is that the 2001 reunion was specifically the Underwater Moonlight lineup, in which Seligman was the bassist. Whether Metcalfe was asked and declined, not asked, or unavailable is not on the public record.
