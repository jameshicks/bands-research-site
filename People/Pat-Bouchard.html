Overview

Pat Bouchard is a French recording engineer, producer, musician and composer whose output is concentrated in the late 1970s through the 1980s, with archival reissues appearing on SouffleContinu Records (2018–2020) and Replica Records (2022). He was a member of Les Asociaux Associés and accumulated 48 credits spanning engineering, mixing, production and effects work, primarily across Experimental, Industrial, Avantgarde and Art Rock releases on smaller French imprints such as Gratte-Ciel and L’Invitation Au Suicide. His deepest collaborations were with Philippe Doray, Clair Obscur, Gilbert Artman and Lard Free, placing him at the centre of the French experimental underground of that era.
