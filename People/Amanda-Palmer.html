Amanda Palmer

Born: Amanda MacKinnon Palmer, 30 April 1976
Discogs ID: 575536
Also known as: AFP, A.F.P., AP, “Amanda Fucking Palmer”
Instrument: Piano (primary), ukulele

American performer, composer, and vocalist. Co-founder and pianist/vocalist of The Dresden Dolls, the Boston “Brechtian punk cabaret” duo. After the Dolls’ 2008 hiatus she built a prolific solo career through direct-to-fan funding (Kickstarter, Patreon) and her own 8ft. Records imprint. Key sideline projects include Evelyn Evelyn (with Jason Webley), Amanda Palmer & The Grand Theft Orchestra, and 8in8 (with Ben Folds, Neil Gaiman, Damian Kulash Jr.). Her 2017 LP I Can Spin A Rainbow was co-created with Edward Ka-Spel of The Legendary Pink Dots. Married to writer Neil Gaiman 2011–2022.
