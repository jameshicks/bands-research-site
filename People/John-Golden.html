John Golden

Discogs ID: 265502
Role: Mastering engineer, lacquer cutting

Mastering and lacquer-cutting engineer. Cut and mastered …And The Machines Are Winning (1999, Magister Ludi Records) — the earliest The Paper Chase release with an outside mastering credit. Credited as “JG” for lacquer cut and “Golden” for mastering in the runout matrix.

Temporary Residence Limited

Most frequent mastering engineer across Temporary Residence Limited’s catalog — credited on 89 TRL releases. Primary mastering engineer for MONO across their core TRL era (from Walking Cloud And Deep Red Sky 2004 through the 2010s), and for Explosions In The Sky and Maserati. Often paired with Steve Albini’s recording/mixing work on the same records.

Key TRL releases mastered: Walking Cloud And Deep Red Sky, Flag Fluttered And The Sun Shined (MONO, 2004), You Are There (MONO, 2006), Palmless Prayer / Mass Murder Refrain (MONO / World’s End Girlfriend, 2005), Hymn To The Immortal Wind (MONO, 2009), Inventions For The New Season (Maserati, 2007).

Family

Son JJ Golden joined him at Golden Mastering in Ventura, California in 1998, having previously worked as a recording and mixing engineer at Pathway Studios in North London. Both father and son work as mastering engineers at the same studio; their credits overlap significantly and Discogs lists many of JJ’s name variations as the same as John’s (including “John Golden,” “J.J. Golden,” etc.).
