David Campbell

Canadian-American string arranger, conductor, and composer. Real name: David Richard Campbell. Discogs artist ID: 341947. Also credited as Dave Campbell, D. Campbell, David R. Campbell.

Born: 7 February 1948, Toronto, Canada.

Overview

David Campbell is one of Hollywood’s most prolific orchestral arrangers and conductors, with credits spanning pop, rock, country, film soundtracks, and Japanese J-pop from the early 1970s through the 2020s. He began his career as a session viola and violin player (Carole King’s Tapestry, Jackson Browne’s debut album), then transitioned into full arranging and conducting work from the mid-1970s onward. His credits include Adele, Beyoncé, Johnny Cash, Michael Jackson, Taylor Swift, U2, Green Day, Metallica, Aerosmith, Rush, The Rolling Stones, Leonard Cohen, and Beck — the last being his son.

Campbell is credited under a number of ensemble names on Discogs: The David Campbell String Section (ID: 1586762), The Campbell-Kurban String Section (ID: 1302571), The Clockwork Angels String Ensemble (ID: 4630362, for Rush), and the “Joe Bonamassa Live At The Hollywood Bowl” Orchestra (ID: 14736580).

The TGR / LA Early-1990s Connection

Beyond his mainstream Hollywood work, Campbell appears in the orbit of the early-1990s Los Angeles indie scene centered around the This Great Religion core. He produced TGR’s second single Nothing Is Wrong • The Clot (Intermission Records, 1991) and served as musical arranger and director on the Nanaki And The Spice mini-album (Victor Japan, 1992) — the latter also featuring Justin Meldal-Johnsen (bass), Tony Hoffer (guitar), and Matt Gunnell (drums). The Nanaki And The Spice project was a Japanese-market pop-rock album, but its personnel — the TGR core rhythm section plus Campbell — suggests an ongoing collaborative relationship with this LA musician circle in the years immediately before Beck’s career broke through.

Career Arc

Session Musician (1971–1974)

Campbell appears on records as a viola and violin player from the outset. Key early credits:

  • Carole King — Tapestry (1971, Ode/A&M), Rhymes & Reasons (1972), Fantasy (1973): viola and strings arranged/conducted
  • Jackson Browne — debut self-titled album (1972, Asylum): viola
  • Jimmie Spheeris — Isle Of View (1971, Columbia), I Am The Mercury (1972): strings arranged, conducted; this partnership continued through Spheeris’s career
  • Kris Kristofferson & Rita Coolidge — Full Moon (1973, A&M): strings arranged

Arranger and Conductor (1975 onward)

From the mid-1970s Campbell shifted his primary role to orchestration and conducting. Early production credits include work for Jimmie Spheeris (Ports Of The Heart, 1976), Mayumi Itsuwa (Japan, 1977), and The Quick (1978). His arranging output accelerated through the 1980s with a wide genre range:

  • James Taylor — JT (1987): strings arranged and conducted
  • Dolly Parton / Emmylou Harris / Linda Ronstadt — Trio (1987): orchestration and conducted
  • 10,000 Maniacs — In My Tribe (1987): strings arranged
  • Art Garfunkel — Scissors Cut (1988): strings
  • Cher — Heart Of Stone (1989): strings arranged and conducted
  • Diana Ross — Cry Like A Rainstorm (with Aaron Neville, 1989): arranged and conducted Skywalker Symphony Orchestra

1990s — Peak Mainstream Volume

The 1990s saw Campbell’s work appear on some of the decade’s most commercially significant records:

  • Leonard Cohen — The Future (1992, Columbia): strings
  • Aerosmith — Amazing (1993), Crazy (1994), Nine Lives (1997), I Don’t Want To Miss A Thing (1998): strings arranged and conducted throughout
  • Rolling Stones — Out Of Tears (1994): strings arranged
  • Bon Jovi — Always (1994), These Days (1995–96), Have A Nice Day, Bounce: strings throughout
  • Willie Nelson — Healing Hands Of Time (1994): strings and conductor
  • Céline Dion — Just Walk Away (1995): strings arranged and conducted
  • Linda Ronstadt — Winter Light (1993), Dedicated To The One I Love (1996): arranged and conducted
  • Neil Diamond — The Christmas Album Volume II (1994): conductor (orchestra, choir, horns, brass)
  • Aaron Neville — recurring collaborator through 1990s: Ave Maria (1991), The Grand Tour (1993), Can’t Stop My Heart (1995)
  • Journey — Trial By Fire (1996): strings arranged
  • Manowar — Louder Than Hell (1996): orchestra arranged and conducted
  • Green Day — Nimrod. (1997): string and horn arrangements
  • Hanson — Middle Of Nowhere (1997): strings arranged and conducted
  • Aerosmith — Nine Lives (1997): strings/horns/conducted
  • Hole — Celebrity Skin (1998): strings
  • Goo Goo Dolls — Dizzy Up The Girl (1998): strings arranged
  • Alanis Morissette — Supposed Former Infatuation Junkie (1998): strings arranged
  • Beck — Mutations (1998): strings arranged and conducted — first confirmed credit with his son

Japanese Market Work

Campbell has a notably sustained connection with Japanese artists, particularly from the mid-1980s onward:

  • Anri (2) — recurring string arrangements across multiple albums: Apricot Jam (1986–88), Boogie Woogie Mainland (1988–96), Circuit Of Rainbow (1989), Mind Cruisin’ (1990–96), Neutral (1991)
  • Kahoru Kohiruimaki — Time The Motion (1989 and 2024)
  • Miyuki Nakajima — Love Or Nothing (1994), Paradise Cafe (1996)
  • 이승환 (Lee Seung-hwan) — Human (1995, producer/orchestrator/conductor), Cycle (1997, producer)
  • Kaze — Moony Night (2022)

Film Work

Campbell has contributed to several film soundtracks:

  • Dead Man Walking — The Score (1996): orchestrated and arranged
  • Grace Of My Heart (1996–97): orchestrated and arranged
  • Brokeback Mountain (2005/2025 reissues): strings arranged (Gustavo Santaolalla score)
  • Rock Of Ages (2012): strings arranged
  • Coyote Ugly (2022): orchestra arranged

Connections
