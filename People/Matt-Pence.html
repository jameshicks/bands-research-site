Matt Pence

Real name: Mathew Louis Pence
Role: Drummer, recording engineer, producer
Base: Denton, Texas
Studio: The Echo Lab, Denton TX
Discogs ID: 292250

Drummer, engineer, and producer based in Denton, Texas. Best known as the drummer in Centro-Matic (and its quieter sister project South San Gabriel), both led by singer-songwriter Will Johnson. Also runs The Echo Lab, a studio in Denton that has become a significant hub for Texas and Americana recording. Credit list spans alt-country, indie rock, folk, and post-rock.

Centro-Matic and South San Gabriel

Centro-Matic (Discogs ID 289472) was founded in Denton in 1995. Lineup: Will Johnson (vocals, guitar), Matt Pence (drums), Scott Danbom, and Mark Hedman. The band released prolifically through the 2000s, primarily on Munich Records and later Misra Records and Undertow Music.

South San Gabriel (Discogs ID 445919) is a parallel project featuring the same four members with added guests — quieter, more introspective music. The name first appeared on Centro-Matic’s South San Gabriel Songs/Music (2000); the first proper SSG release was a split 7” with Okkervil River (2002, Tight Spot Records).

New Multitudes (2012, Rounder Records)

Particularly notable credit: Pence mixed the Will Johnson–helmed sessions of this Woody Guthrie centennial album. Primary artists: Jay Farrar, Will Johnson, Anders Parker, and Jim James (Yim Yames). Lyrics by Guthrie; music co-written by each contributor per track.

The Texas-Jerusalem Crossroads reissue (2017, Mute)

Pence mixed the Mute reissue of Lift to Experience’s debut double album. Centro-Matic bandmate Scott Danbom played fiddle on the reissue sessions.
