Paul B. Cutler

Discogs ID: 306007
Name variations: Paul Cutler, P. B. Cutler
Instrument: Guitar, vocals
Scene: Paisley Underground

LA guitarist, engineer, and producer. Produced The Middle Class’s Homeland LP (Pulse Records, 1982). Engineered the The Dream Syndicate’s self-titled debut EP (Down There Records, 1982). Later joined the Dream Syndicate as guitarist replacing Karl Precoda, appearing on Out Of The Grey (1986) onward as producer and guitarist alongside Steve Wynn.
