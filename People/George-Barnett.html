Overview

George Barnett is a British drummer (born 9 August 1988) and brother of Jack Barnett, best known as the drummer of These New Puritans. His discographic footprint runs from 2005 onwards, spanning art rock, post-rock, avantgarde, and experimental styles, with primary label relationships at Angular Recording Corporation (2006–2010) and Domino (2008–2025). His most frequent collaborators beyond the band itself include producer Graham Sutton and arranger Thomas Hein, both central figures in These New Puritans’ orchestral later work.
