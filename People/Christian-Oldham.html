Overview

Christian Kōun Alborz Oldham is a US visual artist and designer who operated at the center of the early-2010s hypnagogic pop and experimental electronic scene, primarily through design and artwork credits. He is best known for his work on both Games (3) releases on Hippos In Tanks and for being among the four credited performers on Death Grips’s founding mixtape Exmilitary (2011), listed under his Info Warrior alias.

He worked under three distinct Discogs identities — Christian Oldham (design credits), Megazord (early music/art alias), and Info Warrior (performer credit with Death Grips) — all confirmed as the same person (realname: Christian Kōun Alborz Oldham on all three). The Megazord profile also explicitly describes him as “former projectionist for” Death Grips.

As of the mid-2010s onward, his career shifted toward institutional visual art contexts — he is documented by the Asia Foundation, SFMOMA’s OpenSpace platform, the University of Washington, and the Library of Congress authority file (no2021049048). Later credits use the name “Christian Alborz Oldham” and are associated with projects such as an ikebana-related device site (options.device.gmbh/ikebana) and a Berlin Instagram presence (@flowerplanetberlin).

Scene Connections

Hypnagogic Pop / Early-2010s Experimental Electronic:
Oldham’s design work was tightly embedded in the Hippos In Tanks / The Curatorial Club cluster. Both Games (3) LPs on Hippos In Tanks carried his uncredited work; The Curatorial Club released both the Chuck Person Eccojams tape (where he is again uncredited but documented) and the Games Spend The Night With comp and the Airbird / Megafortress split (where Joel Ford is the Airbird side).

Death Grips:
The Info Warrior credit on Exmilitary places him inside the founding Death Grips lineup. Discogs credits that release to four performers: MC Ride, Zach Hill, Flatlander (2), and Info Warrior. Whether his role was primarily visual/projectionist or extended to audio production is not resolved by Discogs data alone.

Software (2) / Napolian:
The Rejoice EP (SFT 009, 2012) connects him to the Software (2) label ecosystem.

West Mineral Ltd. / Later Experimental Electronic:
Collex by Exael (West Mineral Ltd., 2018) and Furi (Motion Ward, 2020) show Oldham active in a later wave of experimental electronic design work into the early 2020s.

Institutional / Fine Art:
Multiple URLs (Asia Foundation, SFMOMA OpenSpace, University of Washington, WorldCat, Library of Congress authority file, Contemporary Art Library) place him in a fine art / curatorial context by the late 2010s.
