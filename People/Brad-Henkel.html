Brad Henkel

American trumpeter, composer, and improviser, Berlin-based. Discogs artist ID: 1715295

Active across free improvisation, contemporary jazz, and experimental music. Works primarily in ensemble contexts — small groups through large-scale orchestras — often sharing compositional duties or operating in collective settings. His Discogs credits span 2006–2025 across US, German, Belgian, Slovenian, Portuguese, UK, and Austrian releases.

Overview

Henkel operates in the Berlin free-improvisation and new-music scene and maintains connections to the US experimental jazz world. He plays trumpet (sometimes amplified) and has mixing credits on several releases, suggesting hands-on involvement in the production side of his recordings. His longest-running ensemble is S P O I L E R (with Julia Reidy, Liz Kosack, and Samuel Hall (2)). He also fronts the Brad Henkel Quartet, whose 2025 album Overstory is his most recent Discogs-documented release as a leader.

Name variations credited on releases: B. Henkel, Henkel.
