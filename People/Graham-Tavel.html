Overview

Graham Tavel is a recording engineer, mixer, and producer whose credits span punk, post-punk, hardcore, folk rock, and dream pop, active since 2014. He has accumulated over 100 credits across numerous small independent labels, most frequently Total Punk, In Poor Taste Records, and Chunklet Industries. In addition to his production work he plays in several bands, including Blammo, Chairhouse, Glittering Insects, Hospice, and Riboflavin. His most sustained studio relationship is with Nag (10), sharing 21 releases, followed by Jesse Mangum (11) and a cluster of collaborators around Brannon Greene, Ryan Bell, and Ryan Fetter.
