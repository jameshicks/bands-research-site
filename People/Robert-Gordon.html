Robert Gordon

Role: Electronic producer, sound engineer, DJ — co-founder of Warp Records Based: Sheffield, England Discogs ID: 27036

Overview

Sheffield-based producer and engineer. One-third of Forgemasters (with Sean Maher and Winston Hazel). Co-founded Warp Records in 1989 with Steve Beckett and Rob Mitchell. Shaped Warp’s early “bleep and bass” aesthetic through A&R and production. Cut his teeth at FON Studio in Sheffield working with Mark Brydon as FON Force, engineering and producing Age Of Chance, Pop Will Eat Itself, Krush and others. Collaborated with Richard H. Kirk as XON.

Forgemasters’ “Track With No Name” was an early Warp release. After Warp, released solo material on David Moufang’s Source label (1996), including work with Pete Namlook (Fax label). Runs Trash & Ready Records.
