Overview

Simon Machan is a programmer, producer, and mixer whose work is almost entirely bound up with Happy Mondays — he shares over 100 credits with the band and each of its core members across their releases on Factory, Elektra, and London Records. His credited roles (Programmed By, Sampler, Mixed By, Recorded By) place him at the heart of the band’s sample-driven and electronically textured production. He appears repeatedly alongside producers Paul Oakenfold & Steve Osborne on the same releases, positioning him within the team behind the band’s early-1990s commercial peak. His earliest credits run back to 1987, predating the Mondays, with appearances on Abstract Sounds and FM.
