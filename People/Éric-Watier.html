Éric Watier

Discogs ID: 662918 Active: 1987–2020 (per credits) Origin: France Roles: Visual artist, graphic designer, musician

French visual artist and musician active in the European cassette underground from 1987. Best documented on Discogs as a cover designer and visual collaborator for Klimperei and the Underground Productions orbit. Also released at least one solo cassette as a performer (1987). His name appears on releases spanning thirty-three years, from the first Klimperei cassette in 1987 through a 2020 Tribe Tapes release.

Solo Release

Les Miniatures (1987, Underground Productions, SUB 11) is Watier’s only Discogs entry as a primary artist. A cassette album of 20 tracks, all untitled sessions credited to Watier alone — no additional personnel are listed. The label, Underground Productions, is the same French cassette imprint used by Klimperei throughout this period, suggesting close proximity within that network. Genre: Electronic; Style: Minimal.

Track titles include “Chanson,” “William,” “Nuit Africaine,” “Slavery,” “L’Hippopotame,” “La Danse À 7 Temps” — suggesting playful, small-scale, vaguely narrative pieces in the naive-minimal mode common to this milieu.

Visual Work for Klimperei

Watier contributed cover art to at least five Klimperei-adjacent releases between 1987 and 2020:

  • Brötchen Et Petits Pains (1987) — credited “Artwork [Cover Drawings]” on the label’s debut cassette split with Los Paranos
  • Deux Lapins (1988) — “Sleeve [Design De Couverture]” on the Underground Productions compilation (credited as “E.Watier”)
  • Sucres d’Orge (1989) — “Cover” (credited as “É. Watier”)
  • Gris-Nez (1990) — “Design [Cover]” on the SHM Tapes cassette, compiled by Guido Erfen
  • Vilain Chien (1993) — “Design” on the Klimperei / Claude Seyve split cassette
  • Presque Fini (2020) — “Artwork” (credited as “Eric Watier”) on the Tribe Tapes cassette

The gap between 1993 and 2020 suggests either an extended hiatus or activity outside Discogs documentation.
