JJ Golden

Discogs ID: 379566
Role: Mastering engineer, lacquer cutting
Studio: Golden Mastering, Ventura, California
Active: 1997–present

Mastering engineer based at Golden Mastering in Ventura, California. Son of mastering engineer John Golden, whom he joined at the family studio in 1998. Before that, JJ Golden freelanced in London as a recording and mixing engineer at Pathway Studios in North London. Over a career spanning nearly three decades, he has built an exceptionally broad portfolio — spanning indie rock, avant-folk, psych, garage, soul, post-rock, and experimental music — with particularly deep ties to Drag City and a recurring presence on releases by the west-coast underground labels In The Red Recordings and Daptone Records.

Background

JJ Golden’s Discogs credits begin in 1997 (a remaster for Justin Roberts on Carpet Square Records). He joined his father John Golden at Golden Mastering in 1998, initially handling mastering while his father continued to focus on lacquer cutting, although JJ has taken on both roles throughout his career. The father-son relationship between the two engineers is explicitly noted on Discogs, where name-variation confusion is common due to shared credits and overlapping name variants (both appear in JJ’s listed name variations, including “John Golden” and “John J. Golden”).

Broader Catalog

Beyond Drag City, JJ Golden’s mastering and lacquer-cutting work spans a wide range of indie imprints and labels across nearly thirty years:

Early career (1999–2005): Nebula (To The Center, Sweet Nothing Records, 1999), Calexico (Feast Of Wire, City Slang/Labels, 2003 — across multiple pressings), Shipping News (Quarterstick Records, 2001–2003), Spoon (Kill The Moonlight, Merge Records, 2002), American Music Club (Love Songs For Patriots, Merge, 2004), Cass McCombs (A, Monitor Records, 2003/2004), Rachel’s (Systems/Layers, Quarterstick, 2003), Silkworm (It’ll Be Cool, Touch And Go, 2004), Hella (The Devil Isn’t Red, Suicide Squeeze, 2004), Iron and Wine with Calexico (In The Reins, Overcoat/Touch And Go, 2005).

2006–2010: Neurosis (Live at Roadburn 2007, Roadburn/Neurot, 2010), Swans (My Father Will Guide Me Up a Rope to the Sky, Young God Records, 2010), The New Pornographers (Together, Matador, 2010), Neko Case (Middle Cyclone, Anti-, 2010), Fucked Up (Couple Tracks, Matador, 2010), Thee Oh Sees (multiple In The Red releases from 2010), OFF! (First Four EPs, Vice Records, 2010), Mission of Burma (Signals, Calls, and Marches, Matador, 2010).

2011–present: Sharon Jones & The Dap-Kings (Soul Time!, Daptone, 2011 and subsequent reissues), Daptone Records catalog (recurring — El Rego Et Ses Commandos, The Altons, Antibalas, The Budos Band, Cochemea Gastelum), Daptone Records vinyl and lacquer work; Thee Oh Sees / Osees (recurring In The Red releases through the 2010s–2020s); Matador vinyl cuts (Kurt Vile, Horsegirl, Mdou Moctar); Charles Moothart (Chaotic Shimmer, In The Red, 2025); …And You Will Know Us by the Trail of Dead (Madonna, 2000 on Domino and again in 2025 on Arising Empire/Century Media).
