Evian Christ

Oct 10, 20265 min read

Evian Christ

Real name: Joshua Leary
Discogs artist ID: 2604384
Name variations: Evian, Evian Chirst
Origin: Ellesmere Port, Merseyside, England
Born: 1990

Electronic producer. Part of the first wave of Tri Angle artists (2012–2014), where he released seven titles that applied a hip-hop beatmaking sensibility to the label’s atmospheric electronic context. His debut Kings And Them (2012) attracted attention from Kanye West’s production circle; he went on to receive a production credit on Yeezus (2013). Post-Tri Angle, he moved to Warp Records, releasing Ultra (2020) and the album Revanchist (2023).

Biography

2012 — Emergence / Tri Angle Debut

Leary emerged in 2012 with two releases. The Duga-Three Concept Mix appeared on Dummy Records as a written-by credit, and Kings And Them (TRI ANGLE 12) arrived on Tri Angle as his proper debut — eight tracks across Electronic, Hip Hop, Ambient, Juke, and Leftfield styles. The EP’s title tracks (“Drip,” “MYD,” “Horses In Motor,” etc.) distinguished him from the label’s prevailing hauntological and witch-house aesthetics: the palette was wider, the rhythmic logic closer to Chicago footwork and hip-hop construction than to the reverb-saturated drone of labelmates. He also released No Experiences (2012, Forty Ounce), a DJ mix.

The Kings And Them debut circulated widely enough to reach Kanye West’s production circle — the connection that would lead to the Yeezus credit a year later.

2013 — Yeezus and Duga-3

Leary received a producer credit on Kanye West’s Yeezus (2013, Getting Out Our Dreams), alongside a densely populated production roster that included Arca, Hudson Mohawke, Gesaffelstein, Daft Punk, Rick Rubin (executive producer), Lunice, Brodinski, and others. The precise track(s) he produced are not individually attributed in Discogs release-level credits.

Also in 2013, his Tri Angle follow-up Duga-3 (TRIANGLE19) appeared: a single-sided 12” with one 19-minute piece, mastered by Sebastian Gainsborough (Vessel) and with design/artwork by Dr. Me. The extended, drone-ambient format was a sharp departure from the choppy beat constructions of Kings And Them, placing him closer to the label’s experimental end.

2014–2015 — Waterfall and Production Work

Waterfall (TRIANGLE22, 2014) was his final Tri Angle release, mixed by Leary himself (credited as Joshua Leary) and Noah Rubin, with mastering by Heba Kadry and artwork/typography by David Rudnick. The EP marked the end of his Tri Angle relationship.

In 2015 he produced tracks on Le1f’s Riot Boi album (Terrible Records), alongside Lunice, Dubbel Dutch, Salva, and SOPHIE.

2016 — Danny Brown’s Atrocity Exhibition

Leary produced track C2 on Danny Brown’s Atrocity Exhibition (2016, Fool’s Gold / Warp Records) — an album with a notably diverse production slate including Alchemist, Black Milk, Paul White, and Petite Noir.

2020–2023 — Warp Records

After a multi-year gap in primary-credited releases, Leary signed to Warp Records. Ultra (2020, WAP449D) was a single-track digital single. Revanchist (2023, WARPDD 273) was his debut album proper: eight tracks, digital-only, in an Experimental/Electronic vein. Collaborators on Revanchist include Bladee (on “Yxguden”) and Merely (featured on “With Me”). Artwork and typography across multiple releases by David Rudnick.

In 2024 he compiled TranceParty: English Heritage, a self-released compilation (Not On Label) featuring labelmate Seretide among others.
