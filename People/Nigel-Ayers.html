Overview

Nigel Ayers founded Nocturnal Emissions in London in 1980, creating one of the UK’s most sustained experimental/post-industrial projects. Over four decades he has shifted fluidly between noise, musique concrète, industrial electronics, ambient, and dub-inflected beat work — always within a conceptual frame he calls “guerrilla sign ontology.” He co-ran Sterile Records (ID 9411) with Caroline K from 1979 to 1986, then launched Earthly Delights (ID 11873) as Nocturnal Emissions’ primary outlet through the 1990s. Now based in Cornwall on the Mary-Michael ley line.

Nocturnal Emissions — phase by phase

(See full band article at Nocturnal Emissions)

Ayers is the continuous creative core of Nocturnal Emissions from 1980 onward. His specific instrument credits across releases reveal a hands-on range: voice, guitar, violin, tape, church harmonium, chimes, music box, car-wiper rhythms, digital and analogue effects, sampling. He also acts as executive-producer, lyricist, designer, and photographer on most releases.
