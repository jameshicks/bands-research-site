Jack Monck

Discogs ID: 1031305 Real name: Adam John Monck

British bassist. Active from the late 1960s; played in the Cambridge scene in the early 1970s alongside Syd Barrett, Kevin Ayers, and others. Contributed to David Thomas And The Pedestrians’ Variations On A Theme (Plurex, 1983) and More Places Forever (Rough Trade, 1985). Solo work and Dutch releases in the 2000s–2010s.

Cambridge Scene

The sole Discogs-documented Cambridge-era release is Six Hour Technicolour Dream Cambridge 1972 (Easy Action, EARS052, released 2014, release ID 5858776) — a live CD by the Last Minute Put Together Boogie Band recorded in Cambridge in 1972. Monck plays bass alongside Twink (drums) and Bruce Paine (vocals); Syd Barrett and Fred Frith guest on electric guitar for tracks 5–7.

No other Cambridge-era releases are catalogued on Discogs. Monck’s connections to Syd Barrett (a178489), Fred Frith (a78003), and Chris Cutler (a177209) during this period appear to have been largely live/informal rather than documented on record.
