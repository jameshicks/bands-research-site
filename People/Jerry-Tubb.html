Jerry Tubb

Mastering engineer, CEO of Terra Nova Mastering (Austin, Texas). Discogs artist ID: 448830. Name variations in Discogs records: J. Tubbs, Jerry Tubbs, Jerry Taub, Jerry Tub.

Active 1990–2025 (35-year career). Over 1,400 release credits on Discogs, predominantly US indie rock, alt-country, Americana, and Austin-scene acts. Closest association is The American Analog Set (23 shared releases), mastering their entire major-label-era catalog through Tiger Style and Emperor Jones.

Overview

Tubb operates out of Austin, Texas, where Terra Nova Mastering is one of the city’s established independent mastering houses. His catalog is anchored in the Austin music ecosystem, with strong ties to Americana, country, blues, and Texas roots music alongside a parallel thread of indie rock clients. The breadth is unusual: on the same roster as Willie Nelson reissues and Austin City Limits archival releases, he mastered the full catalogs of post-rock/indie bands like The American Analog Set, Ola Podrida, and The Wooden Birds, and handled both David Gordon Green film soundtracks scored by Explosions In The Sky and David Wingo.

The most numerically dominant relationship in his Discogs credits is with New West Records (146 release appearances), largely from the long-running Live From Austin TX concert series filmed for Austin City Limits. He is the default mastering engineer for that archive.

Austin City Limits / New West Records Archive

Tubb is the house mastering engineer for the Live From Austin TX concert series (New West Records). Credits span multiple volumes 2004–2021, covering artists including Steve Earle, Richard Thompson, Susan Tedeschi, Widespread Panic, Neko Case, and many others. New West Records is his most frequent label relationship by raw release count (146 credits).
