Overview

Kassie Carlson is a vocalist, lyricist, and producer primarily known as a member of Guerilla Toss, the Boston-to-New York avant-garde group that worked across No Wave, Art Rock, and Rhythmic Noise. Her 39 Discogs credits run almost entirely through the 2010s, beginning on small cassette imprints — Feeding Tube Records, NNA Tapes, Spooky Town, and Sophomore Lounge — before the band landed at DFA Records for three releases between 2016 and 2018. Her tightest collaborative cluster is the Guerilla Toss core: Arian Shafiee, Peter Negroponte, Ian Kovac, and Simon Sheldon Hanes each share over a dozen credits with her. A 2022 Sub Pop appearance and a 2024 Bufu Records credit signal continued activity past the DFA period.
