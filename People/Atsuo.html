Atsuo

Real name: Atsuo Mizuno Discogs artist ID: 431875 Production alias: FangsAnalSatan (Discogs ID 435812) Name variations: Atsuoabaddon, 敦夫, A Instagram: @fangsanalsatan

Drummer, percussionist, vocalist, label operator, recording engineer, and visual designer. Core member of Boris (3) since 1996; the band’s de facto producer, engineer, and art director under the FangsAnalSatan alias. Runs Fangs Anal Satan (label ID 95874), Boris’s primary self-release imprint, which has issued 128+ releases spanning 1994–present.

Biography by Era

Early 1990s — Hakaiders and Pre-Boris

Before Boris, Atsuo played drums and sang in Hakaiders (Discogs ID 12986358) — a band documented by a 1992 release on Yu-Wa Label. Other Hakaiders members: Hara (8) (ID 2260023), Sera (16) (ID 12623756), and 加藤泉 (ID 12623756). The band has a web presence at potziland.com/pages/hakaiders. He also started Tacos U.K. in the 1990s, described as a Chaos U.K. cosplay/tribute band — no dedicated Discogs presence.

1992–1995 — Boris Formation

Boris formed in Tokyo around 1992–1993 with Nagata on drums. The first recorded documentation under Atsuo’s credited involvement comes from Demo Vol. 3 (1994, Fangs Anal Satan) — meaning the label was already running by the time Boris’s early demos were being cut. The band name is drawn from the Melvins song “Boris.” Nagata departed and Atsuo assumed the drum seat by 1996.

1996–2001 — Drone Doom, Foundational Records

With Atsuo on drums, Boris became a three-piece: Takeshi (Boris) on bass, Wata on guitar. Engineer Kakinuma handled sound on Absolutego (1996, Fangs Anal Satan — release ID 1082117), the debut proper — a two-track drone doom record reportedly spanning 60+ minutes, self-produced.

Amplifier Worship (1998, Mangrove — ID 393645) expanded the approach; here Atsuo played wave drum, cymbal, and contributed electronics alongside conventional kit work. FangsAnalSatan provided artwork for this release — the first documented credit under the alias.

Flood (2000, Fangs Anal Satan / Midi Creative — ID 417179): Atsuo credited for Drums and Gong, marking the first appearance of gong as a named instrument — a recurring element from here on.

2002–2005 — Prolific Expansion

Boris entered a densely productive period. Heavy Rocks (2002, Fangs Anal Satan/Quattro UK Discs — ID 417084): Atsuo as Drums, Percussion, Vocals; FangsAnalSatan as Design. The Merzbow collab thread begins: Megatone split (2002, Inoxia Records — ID 376588) credits Atsuo under “Effects [Feedback Conduction]” — one of several non-kit roles he takes on non-standard releases.

Akuma No Uta (2003, Diwphalanx — ID 1334231) and Boris At Last -Feedbacker- (2003, Diwphalanx — ID 394172): breakthrough domestic releases mixed by Souichirou Nakamura; FangsAnalSatan handled design on both.

Pink (2005, Diwphalanx — ID 656463): 29 pressings, international breakthrough. Atsuo: Drums. FangsAnalSatan: Artwork. The aRCHIVE series (2005) documented earlier live material across multiple volumes with Atsuo playing guitar and wave drum on Volume Two (“Drumless Shows”) — documenting format flexibility.

Sun Baked Snow Cave (2005, Hydra Head/Double H Noise Industries — ID 561021): Boris/Merzbow collab; Atsuo credited for “Electronics [Feedback Conduction], Computer” — pure electronics, no percussion.

2006–2009 — Southern Lord, Altar, High-Profile Collaborations

Altar (2006, Southern Lord — ID 818145) with [[Bands/Sunn O)))] is the defining collaborative statement of this era. Atsuo, Takeshi, and Wata appear as band members alongside Stephen O’Malley and Greg Anderson. FangsAnalSatan shared Art Direction credit with O’Malley (as SOMA); artwork by Aaron Horkey. Recorded and mixed by Randall Dunn and Mell Dettmer.

Rainbow (2006, Pedal Records — ID 867520): Atsuo on Percussion; FangsAnalSatan on Design and Photography — the photography credit is notable as it extends the FAS alias beyond pure graphic design. This release introduced Michio Kurihara into Boris’s orbit.

Speed, Thrill, Stupid (2006, Diwphalanx — ID 8483316): Atsuo credited as “Mixed By [Re-Mixed By], Mastered By [Mastering By], Producer [Produced By]” — revealing the full depth of his production role when working without Nakamura.

Smile (2008, Southern Lord — ID 1254973): Atsuo on Vocals, Drums, Percussion; FangsAnalSatan as Recorded By and Mixed By — he tracked and mixed the album himself.

Statement (2008, Southern Lord — ID 1253470): FangsAnalSatan as Recorded By; Atsuo on Vocals, Drums, Percussion.

2010–2014 — Ensemble Pearl, Sargent House, Continued Label Work

Boris & Ian Astbury (2010, Southern Lord — ID 2411963): BXI project — Atsuo on Drums, Percussion; also credited as Engineer [Recording].

Ensemble Pearl (2012, Drag City — ID 4144362): Atsuo as Performer alongside Stephen O’Malley, Michio Kurihara, Bill Herzog, Eyvind Kang (track 3), and Timb Harris (track 3). Mixed by Randall Dunn; mastered by Bob Weston. The solo Atsuo project A/N【eɪ-ɛn】 with Takeshi issued two releases: -素描集Ⅰ- (2018) and 素描集Ⅱ (2019), both on Fangs Anal Satan.

Sargent House period: Attention Please (2011 — ID 2899770), Heavy Rocks (2011 — ID 2899089), New Album (2011 — ID 2771730), Noise (2014 — ID 5798355). FangsAnalSatan handled recording and design across all of them.

Lou Lou… In Tokyo (2011, Daymare — ID 2798411): Atsuo credited for Gong, Percussion, Effects [Reverb Box, Tape Echoes]; FangsAnalSatan as Recorded By, Mixed By, Producer, and Film Director — directing the video component.

Mama (2014, Daymare — ID 6351446): Atsuo as Producer. Shade Themes From Kairos (2014, Drag City — ID 5708869): Atsuo as Recorded By — tracking for another act, demonstrating his recording work outside Boris.

2015–2019 — Solo Label Activity, Third Man, O.N.I / A/N

EROS (2015, Fangs Anal Satan/G.G.R.R. — ID 6966138): Atsuo: Drums, Vocals; FangsAnalSatan: Design.

Gensho (2016, Relapse — ID 8219145): Boris/Merzbow. Atsuo: Vocals, Percussion, Electronics; FangsAnalSatan: Artwork, Recorded By.

Dear (2017, Sargent House — ID 10555075): Atsuo: Vocals, Drums, Percussion, Electronics; FangsAnalSatan: Layout, Recorded By.

Live At Third Man Records (2017, Third Man — ID 11170806): Atsuo: Drums, Gong, Electronics, Vocals.

O.N.I project (ID 6697781) — members: Atsuo, Takeshi, Gazelle (5), Seiichiroh Morikawa, Toyoki Kawaguchi. One documented release: Parsley, Sage, Rosemary, And Thyme (2019, Fangs Anal Satan).

A/N【eɪ-ɛn】 (ID 6857134) — members: Atsuo, Takeshi, Seiichiroh Morikawa, Shinji Kuroki. Releases: -素描集Ⅰ- (2018, Fangs Anal Satan) and 素描集Ⅱ (2019, Fangs Anal Satan).

2020–present — Sustained Output, Sacred Bones, Relapse

No (2020, Fangs Anal Satan — ID 15568129): Atsuo: Vocals, Percussion, Electronics; FangsAnalSatan: Recorded By, Design. 10 pressings.

2R0I2P0 (2020, Relapse — ID 16406364): Boris/Merzbow. FangsAnalSatan: Mixed By [Total Mix], Design.

W (2022, Sacred Bones — ID 21709525): Atsuo: Vocals, Drums, Percussion, Electronics; FangsAnalSatan: Design, Recorded By. 10 pressings.

Heavy Rocks (2022, Relapse — ID 24137201): third album with this title. Bright New Disease (2023, Sacred Bones — ID 27368037): FangsAnalSatan as Recorded By, Translated By, Design.

And 2 Years Later (2024, Fangs Anal Satan — ID 31433216): Atsuo: Vocals; FangsAnalSatan: Mixed By, Mastered By, Design — one of the rare cases where Mastered By credit falls to FAS/Atsuo rather than an external mastering engineer.

Live Noise Alive (2025, Fangs Anal Satan — ID 34585945): Atsuo: Vocals, Drums, Electronics; FangsAnalSatan: Design, Mixed By.

Role in Boris

Drums / Percussion / Gong / Electronics / Vocals. Standard kit from 1996. Gong first documented on Flood (2000); returns on Variations + Live In Japan (2010), Live At Third Man Records (2017), 素描集Ⅰ (2018). Electronics and wave drum appear from the Amplifier Worship era onward. Lead vocals become more prominent from the Smile era; by the 2017–2020 releases he is routinely credited for Lead Vocals on individual tracks (e.g., “Ibitsu” re-recording 2023 credits him as Lead Vocals). On Volume Two of the Archive series (“Drumless Shows”) he plays guitar and wave drum, showing the full-band interchangeability.

FangsAnalSatan — Production and Design Alias

FangsAnalSatan (ID 435812) is Atsuo’s credited persona for all non-performance work. The alias covers:

  • Recording / Tracking — self-records Boris in their own studio from at least 1998 (Amplifier Worship artwork) through the present; by the 2000s he is the primary tracking engineer on all Boris sessions
  • Mixing — documented from Sun Baked Snow Cave (2005) onward as de facto mixer; shares mixing credit with Nakamura on many releases
  • Design / Artwork / Typography — credited as designer and art director from Black: Implication Flooding (1998) through Live Noise Alive (2025); designs virtually all Boris release packaging
  • Art Direction — full art direction credit on Altar (2006), Vein (2020), and others
  • Photography — credited for photography on Rainbow (2006) and Sound Track From Film “Mabuta No Ura” (2005)
  • Film Direction — “Direction By” credit on Lou Lou… In Tokyo (2011)
  • Mastered By — appears on “Ibitsu” Re-Recording 2023 and And 2 Years Later (2024); otherwise mastering usually goes to Nakamura or an external engineer
  • Logo Design — credited for logo on Devil / Rebirth (2024)

The alias has its own Discogs page with name variations: FAS, Fangs Anal Satan, Fangsanalsatan, ファングスアナルサタン. The connection to the label name is direct: the label and the alias share a name, collapsing the production/label/design operation into one identity.
