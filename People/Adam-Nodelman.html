American bassist Adam Nodelman began playing with Borbetomagus at age 18 in 1984. He appeared on “Live In Allentown”, “7 Reasons For Tears” and “Fish Thy Sparkling Bubble”. He joined Missing Foundation in 1987 appearing on various recordings, was in Motherhead Bug with David Ouimet, played with the War Hippies and joined west coast industrial outfit Crash Worship for part of a tour in 1996 and 1997. Since 2005, Nodelman has been a core member of Sunburned Hand Of The Man & Franklin’s Mint and appears on ton’s of each band’s recent recordings and live shows. Adam has been a sometimes member of Bunny Brains in the past year, and In 2008, Adam became the front man of the pioneer valley assault band.

Overview

Adam Nodelman (b. April 12, 1966, NYC; d. August 25, 2008, Woodstock) was an American bassist who worked across noise, free jazz, free improvisation, and experimental punk over roughly two decades. He joined Borbetomagus at age 18 in 1984, forging a close working relationship with Donald Miller, Jim Sauter, and Don Dietrich that produced eight shared releases; he also played with Missing Foundation from 1987 and briefly joined west-coast industrial outfit Crash Worship on tour in the mid-1990s. In the 2000s he became a core member of both Sunburned Hand Of The Man and Franklin’s Mint, with Jacques Kralian and Phil Franklin among his regular collaborators in that period. His label relationships ranged from Agaric Records (his most frequent home, 1988–2007) to Vermiform and Restless, and his catalogue is concentrated in the 1980s and 1990s, with a small cluster of later recordings before his death at 42.
