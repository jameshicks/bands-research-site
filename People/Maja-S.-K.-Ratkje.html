Maja S. K. Ratkje

Discogs ID: 65603 Born: December 29, 1973, Trondheim, Norway Full name: Maja Solveig Kjelstrup Ratkje Also known as: Maja Ratkje, Maja S.K. Ratkje, Tatke, Tennessee Patsy, Solveig Kjelstrup Website: ratkje.no Husband: Frode Haltli (accordionist, Discogs ID: 629711)

Overview

Maja S. K. Ratkje is a Norwegian composer, vocalist, and electronics artist born in 1973 in Trondheim. Her practice centres on the extended human voice as both a sound source and a compositional material, which she subjects to live processing, sampling, and electronic transformation. Her output spans solo concert music, free improvisation, ensemble work, noise, and electroacoustic composition, and she has occupied a central position in the Norwegian experimental music scene since the late 1990s.

She is a core member of the free improvisation quartet Spunk (with Hild Sofie Tafjord, Kristin Andersen, and Lene Grenager), one of the earliest acts on Rune Grammofon (debut 1999). Her solo debut Voice (2002, Rune Grammofon, RCD 2028) — produced with noise duo Jazkamer — uses her own voice as the sole sound source and stands as a landmark of European electroacoustic vocal music. She co-leads the duo MAJAAP with Dutch sound poet Jaap Blonk, with two albums on Kontrans (2004–2005). She is also a member of the noise/heavy metal project Trinacria, the duo Fe-mail (with Hild Sofie Tafjord), and has appeared across all three formations of Phantom Orchard (the duo, Ensemble, and Orchestra — Tzadik, 2008–2014).

Her closest recurring production collaborator is Lasse Marhaug (12 shared releases), and her husband Frode Haltli co-produces and appears on several of her records. She also works frequently with Bjørn Hatterud (alias Maskinanlegg, 10 shared releases). She has been active as a producer and mixing engineer on her own releases and those of others, including mastering work for Hubro artists.

Trinacria

Norwegian avant-metal/experimental project. Discogs ID: 1104103. Travel Now Journey Infinitely (2008, Indie Recordings, INDIE010CD). Members include members of Enslaved (Grutle Kjellson, Ivar Bjørnson, Arve Isdal, Iver Sandøy) plus Ratkje and Hild Sofie Tafjord. Ratkje credited for music, vocals, and electronics. A second Discogs entry, digital single “In The Sky” (2019, Classroom Records (2)), is filed anomalously under Electronic/Dance-pop. See Trinacria.
