Phil Spirito

Discogs ID: 340576

Musician, producer. Bandleader of Orso (banjo, tenor guitar, tenor banjo, vocals). Core member of Rex (bass) and HiM (US). Also produced the first two Orso albums for Perishable Records. Part of the Chicago post-rock/chamber folk orbit around Doug Scharin.
