Wayne Shorter

Jazz saxophonist (tenor, soprano) and composer. Born August 25, 1933, Newark, New Jersey. Died March 2, 2023. Discogs artist ID: 29979.

Key member of Miles Davis’s second great quintet (1964–70) alongside Tony Williams, Herbie Hancock, and Ron Carter. Shorter was the group’s primary composer. Co-founded Weather Report (1971) with Joe Zawinul — one of the defining jazz-fusion bands of the 1970s. Extensive Blue Note discography as leader including Speak No Evil, Juju, Infant Eyes, Schizophrenia. Returned to acoustic small-group work in the 2000s with his own quartet. 73 shared Discogs releases with Tony Williams.

Super Nova (Blue Note, 1970) featured Sonny Sharrock on guitar alongside John McLaughlin, Chick Corea, Jack DeJohnette, and Airto Moreira — one of Sharrock’s most prominent early sideman credits. Produced by Duke Pearson.
