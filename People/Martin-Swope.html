Martin Swope

Tape manipulations. Publishing entity: “Oscine Music.” Discogs ID: 446496.

Original member of Mission Of Burma (1979–1983); operated live tape loops from the side of the stage, a defining structural element of the band’s sound. Declined to participate in the 2002 reunion — Bob Weston took over the tape role. Also a member of Birdsongs Of The Mesozoic (alongside Roger Miller) and The Shop Coolies.

Oscine / Oscine Music

“Oscine” is not a performance alias but a music publishing entity — “Oscine Music” — associated with Swope’s songwriting credits. It appears in the liner-note publishing credits of Faultline (Birdsongs of the Mesozoic, 1989, Cuneiform Records), listed alongside the other members’ publishing imprints (Foot Foot-Bug Juice / Your Music / Ziva-Ziva Music). (source: Discogs release 1699819; confirmed by search results citing Discogs data, 2026-07-06)

The same publishing entity reportedly appears on Dawn of the Cycads, a 2008 Cuneiform Records compilation drawing on earlier recordings. (source: Discogs release 1942352; unverified directly — Discogs page returned 403)

There is no evidence of “Oscine” functioning as a performance alias or of any releases issued under the name “Oscine” as a standalone artist. The Discogs artist page for Martin Swope (ID 446496) lists no releases under that name. The label on the existing vault entry of “Alias: Oscine” likely arose from the publishing credit being misread as a performance alias.

Note: an AI-generated search summary claimed a track by “Oscine” appeared on the Arf! Arf! Records sampler These Dogs Live In The Garage (1987, AA-021). The actual tracklist does not include any “Oscine” track. The Shop Coolies (of which Swope was a member) do appear on that sampler with “Guess Who.” The Oscine claim is a hallucination and should be disregarded.
