Malcolm Middleton

Scottish singer-songwriter, guitarist. One half of Arab Strap alongside Aidan Moffat. Discogs artist ID: 280016.

Overview

After Arab Strap dissolved in 2006, Middleton pursued a solo career. His album A Brighter Beat (2007, Full Time Hobby) was co-produced with Tony Doogan, as were the associated singles “Beep Beep, I Love You,” “A Brighter Beat / Point Of Light,” and “We’re All Going To Die.”
