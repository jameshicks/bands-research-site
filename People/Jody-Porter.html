Jody Porter

American guitarist and songwriter. Discogs artist ID: 274943.

Co-founder and primary songwriter of The Belltower (guitar, vocals, 1991–c.1995). Later co-founded Fountains Of Wayne with Adam Schlesinger. Also member of The AstroJet (ID 485660).
