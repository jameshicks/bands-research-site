Ian Masters

Vocalist, bassist, songwriter. UK. Discogs ID: 290173. Aliases: Friendly Scientist, I’m Sore, Oneironaut, Pail Saint.

Lead vocalist and co-founder of Pale Saints, a 4AD dream-pop/shoegaze band (1988–1996). Appeared as co-writer on all tracks 1–14 of His Name Is Alive’s Stars On E.S.P. (1996, 4AD) — as part of a collective group-written credit alongside Mark Kozelek, Erika Hoffmann, Denise James, and others.

Stars On E.S.P. / ESP Summer collaboration — origin

The collaboration between Masters and Warren Defever (His Name Is Alive) was brokered by Ivo Watts-Russell, founder of 4AD. In an interview Masters recalled Watts-Russell asking him directly: “Would you like to go to America and record with Warren Defever?” — Masters accepted, describing the trip as “one of the most enjoyable paid holidays I’ve ever had.” (Dicksonianinstitute Substack interview with Ian Masters)

Both artists were on 4AD at the time — Pale Saints since 1990, His Name Is Alive since 1990 — so the label connection was the direct link. Watts-Russell personally facilitated the introduction rather than it arising organically between the two acts.

Masters travelled to the US and they worked together for approximately two weeks at Defever’s studio in Livonia, Michigan, recording in 1994 on early ProTools. Songs were built either from demos or through jamming; Masters wrote melodies over Defever’s musical ideas and they co-wrote lyrics. (Dicksonianinstitute Substack)

The primary output was the ESP Summer LP Mars Is A Ten, released in 1995 under the duo name ESP Summer on small-scale formats — Defever’s minimal acoustic guitar against Masters’s high falsetto vocal. (Boomkat product page; Wikipedia — Ian Masters as of 2026-10-05)

The co-writing credits Masters holds on tracks 1–14 of Stars On E.S.P. (the 1996 His Name Is Alive full-length on 4AD) appear to have grown out of this same collaboration period — Defever drew on the songs written jointly during those 1994 sessions for both the ESP Summer release and the HNIA album. (Wikipedia — Stars on E.S.P. as of 2026-10-05)

Masters and Defever later regrouped as ESP Summer in 2020–2021, releasing Kingdom of Heaven (a cover of the 13th Floor Elevators track), confirming the relationship as ongoing. (Wikipedia — Ian Masters as of 2026-10-05)
