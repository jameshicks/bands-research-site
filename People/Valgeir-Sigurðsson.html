Valgeir Sigurðsson

Discogs ID: 671064
Born: 1971, Iceland

Icelandic record producer, mixer, composer, engineer and programmer. Founder of Bedroom Community label (Discogs ID 76509). Key producer/engineer for múm — credited on Finally We Are No One (2002) for “recorded by, mixed by, other [good ideas].” 11 shared Discogs releases with múm. Worked across the Icelandic experimental scene. Co-mixed Tim Hecker’s Virgins (2013, Kranky KRANK 183) alongside Hecker.

Sigur Rós Credits

Recorded strings on Valtari (2012) and Kveikur (2013) for Sigur Rós. 26+ shared Discogs entries.

  • Draumalandið (HVALUR8, 2009)
