Aug 11, 20266 min read

Steve Knutson

Discogs ID: 261418
Name variations: Esteban Luis, S. Knutson, Steven Knutson
Active: 1981–present
Base: Portland, Oregon (from 2014); previously New York City; originally Denver

Record label founder, archivist, and music industry figure. Founded Audika Records in 2004 and has since produced the most sustained posthumous archival project for Arthur Russell — over 20 releases spanning Russell’s dance, folk, classical, and experimental output, continuing through 2025.

Career Overview

Denver — Wax Trax

Knutson began his career at Denver’s Wax Trax! record shop, which preceded and overlapped with the Chicago industrial label of the same name. The Denver store was a formative environment for independent music culture in the early 1980s. He also had early band activity in Denver as part of Front (4) (with David Hill (7), Phil Blaine, and Randy Caldwell) and Young Weasels (with Cooper Brougher, Jeff Froyd, and Kevin Meilinger). A 1981 live recording features him as uncredited performer; a 1982 single “Twist & Burn / Happy Feathers” on Local Anesthetic Records credits him on vocals.

Tommy Boy Records — New York (c. 1982–2001)

Moving to New York in the early 1980s, Knutson joined Tommy Boy Records, where he spent approximately 18 years and witnessed — and contributed to — the commercial rise of hip-hop. Credits at Tommy Boy range across arranger, performer, executive producer, and compilation producer. Key credits include:

  • 1985: arranged “He’s Got The Beat” (Tommy Boy)
  • 1986: performer on “Beware (The Funk Is Everywhere)” — as The Zulu Funkatron Singers And Players
  • 1992: executive producer, Don’t Stop… Planet Rock / The Remix EP (Afrika Bambaataa)
  • 1998: compiled Tommy Boy Greatest Beats Volumes 1–4
  • 2001: developed and coordinated the Tommy Boy 20th Anniversary Series — a wave of reissues covering De La Soul (3 Feet High And Rising), Afrika Bambaataa (Looking For The Perfect Beat 1980–1985), Stetsasonic (In Full Gear), Digital Underground (No Nose Job), Information Society, Planet Patrol, Force MDs, and others

Rough Trade North America — New York (2007–2013)

From 2007 to 2013, Knutson served as General Manager of Rough Trade North America, the US arm of the UK label Rough Trade — one of the most historically significant independent labels in the English-speaking world. During this period he continued running Audika in parallel.

Audika Records — Portland (2004–present)

Knutson founded Audika Records in 2004, initially in New York, and relocated it to Portland, Oregon in 2014 when he moved there. The label’s Discogs profile states its mission simply: “Arthur Russell archive. Namaste. PDX 97214.” The label operates as both a standalone imprint and in co-release partnerships, primarily with Rough Trade.

Arthur Russell Archival Work

The scope of Knutson’s Russell archive is unmatched: as of 2025, the Audika catalog contains over 50 Russell release entries in Discogs, spanning original solo cassette-era recordings (1970s–early 1990s), live recordings, instrumental collections, folk/country sessions, dance material, and more.

Calling Out Of Context (2004) — key credits

The first and most influential release. Twelve tracks of dance-oriented electronic pop recorded in the late 1970s and 1980s. Co-producers: Steve Knutson, Melissa Jones (2), Tom Lee (3). Knutson also wrote liner notes.

Recurring personnel on the source recordings:

  • Peter Zummo — trombone (multiple tracks)
  • Mustafa Ahmed — percussion (multiple tracks)
  • Walter Gibbons — remix (“Calling All Kids”)
  • Killer Whale — production/mixing (tracks 1, 2, 4)
  • Mikel Rouse — post-production and digital transfers

Love Is Overtaking Me (2008) — key credits

21 tracks of country, folk, and soft-rock recordings from across Russell’s career. Compilation producers: Knutson, Ernie Brooks, Tom Lee (3). Liner notes by Tom Lee (3).

Notable performers on source recordings:

  • Ernie Brooks — bass (multiple tracks)
  • David Van Tieghem — drums
  • Peter Gordon — saxophone
  • Peter Zummo — trombone
  • John Hammond — producer (multiple tracks)
  • Jerry Harrison — organ (track 12)
  • Bob Blank — engineer (multiple tracks)
  • Steven Hall — guitar/vocals (multiple tracks)
