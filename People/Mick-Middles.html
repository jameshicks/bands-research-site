Mick Middles

British music journalist and author based in Flixton, Greater Manchester. Discogs artist ID: 2031434. Active in print from the 1970s onward; Discogs credits span 1996–2020.

Overview

Mick Middles is a freelance writer and journalist from North-West England who served as the Manchester correspondent for Sounds throughout the 1970s and 1980s. He has also contributed to The Face, The Guardian, Daily Telegraph, Mail on Sunday, and Daily Express. He is one of the most prolific chroniclers of the Manchester post-punk and independent music scene in print, with books covering The Fall, Mark E. Smith, Factory Records, Tony Wilson, Joy Division, Ian Curtis, New Order, and Mick Hucknall, among others. He is also the authorised biographer of comedian Chris Sievey (Frank Sidebottom). Beyond music, he has written illustrated biographies of comedians Les Dawson and Frankie Howerd. (Sources: Discogs artist profile; Wikipedia: Mick Middles)

His Discogs presence is exclusively as a credited liner notes and sleeve notes writer — he does not appear as a primary (performing) artist. Twenty credits are confirmed in Discogs, ranging from 1996 to 2020, across Cherry Red, Silvertone, Ozit-Morpheus, Mute, Document Records, Third Man Records, and Warner Music.

Note on the Document / Third Man Records Blues Series (2013–2014)

Middles contributed sleeve notes to a substantial run of historical blues reissues issued jointly by Document Records and Third Man Records. These appear to be a single ongoing series (possibly Blind Blake, Blind Lemon Jefferson, or similar archival series — artist names not confirmed in Discogs records retrieved). The credits represent around 10–12 individual release IDs, though several are alternate pressings of the same masters.

Connections
