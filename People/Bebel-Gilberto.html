Bebel Gilberto

Discogs ID: 17090
Real name: Isabel Gilberto de Oliveira
Born: May 12, 1966, New York City

Brazilian-American singer. Daughter of João Gilberto and Miucha. Note: Astrud Gilberto is not her mother — a widely repeated misconception flagged explicitly in her Discogs profile. Raised between Brazil and New York; her music sits at the intersection of bossa nova, Brazilian pop, and electronic production.

Career Overview

1980s — Brazil: WEA Period

Started writing songs with Cazuza in the mid-1980s. Released an EP on WEA Brazil containing “Mais Feliz” (lyrics by Cazuza) and “Eu Preciso Dizer Que Te Amo.” A full solo LP followed on WEA Brazil in 1986. This period is sparsely documented in Discogs; primary releases are Brazilian pressings with limited international circulation.

1991 — Japan: MELDAC LP

De Tarde, Vendo O Mar (MELDAC, Japan, 1991). Released into the Japanese market during a period of heightened Brazilian music interest there. This predates her international breakthrough by nearly a decade.

1991 onward — New York

Relocated to New York City in 1991. Worked with Massive Attack (ID 3918), Jarvis Cocker (ID 10293), and DJ Chase (ID 10234, formerly of Massive Attack). These collaborations shaped the electronic-bossa hybrid sound she developed through the decade.

2000 — Tanto Tempo (Breakthrough)

Tanto Tempo (Ziriguiboom/Six Degrees/Crammed Discs, 2000) was the international breakthrough. Main producer: Suba (Mitar Subotić, alias Rex Ilusivii) — he died before the album’s release. Co-producers: Béco Dranoff and Antoine Midani. Mixed by Antoine Midani, Vincent De Bast, and Suba. Mastered by Alan Ward (ID 276459).

Tanto Tempo has 100+ pressings across the world. Its bossa/downtempo sound became a reference point for the early-2000s lounge revival.

2001 — Tanto Tempo Remixes

Tanto Tempo Remixes (Ziriguiboom, 2001). Remixers included Kruder & Dorfmeister, Amon Tobin, and Arling & Cameron.

2004 — Bebel Gilberto (Self-Titled)

Bebel Gilberto (Warner Jazz France, 2004; label ID 294950). Primary producer: Marius de Vries on most tracks. Guy Sigsworth produced “Cada Beijo.” Includes “All Around,” later remixed by Telefon Tel Aviv for their 2007 compilation Remixes Compiled.

2007 — Momento
