Overview

Aaron Snow is a multi-instrumentalist and recording engineer active since 1999, playing guitar, drums, bass, and vocals across several projects. He is a central member of Landing, with whom he has accumulated over 110 shared Discogs credits alongside close collaborators Daron Gardner and Adrienne Snow. His work spans ambient, shoegaze, space rock, and experimental styles, distributed across Strange Attractors Audio House, Music Fellowship, K, and a handful of smaller imprints. He also records under the alias Turning, a project rooted in the sounds of mid-1990s rural psychedelia.
