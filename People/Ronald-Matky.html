Overview

Ronald Matky is an Austrian recording and mastering engineer whose credits span the European jazz scene from the mid-1990s through the 2000s. He is closely associated with the Vienna Art Orchestra and its extended circle, with shared credits across releases by Mathias Rüegg, Klaus Dickbauer, Andy Scherrer, Florian Bramböck, Karl Fian, and Matthieu Michel, among others. His work appeared on prestige labels including Verve Records, EmArcy, and Deutsche Grammophon, placing him at the recording desk for some of the most internationally distributed Austrian jazz of that era.
