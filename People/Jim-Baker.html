Jim Baker

Discogs ID: 379933
Origin: Chicago, Illinois, USA

Chicago pianist and electronics player. Member of Witches & Devils (with Ken Vandermark, Mars Williams, Fredrick Lonberg-Holm, Kent Kessler, Steve Hunt). One of the more active figures in the Chicago improvised music scene. Also member of Exploding Star Orchestra, Territory Band-1 through Territory Band-6, and Fred Anderson Quartet, among many others.

Co-led The Earth Sessions Vol. I/II (1997, BOXmedia, BOXCD004A/B) with Michael Zerang — a Redmoon Theater-associated double album. Vol. I: piano and drum kit; Vol. II: percussion and electronics (Baker used Arp 2600 synthesizer and Yamaha FB01 modules). Engineered by John McCortney.
