Jon Wurster

American drummer and humorist. Born 31 October 1966. Discogs artist ID: 257888. Real name: Jonathan Patrick Wurster.

The permanent drummer of Superchunk (from 1992) and The Mountain Goats (from 2007–08), both on Merge Records, making him arguably the central rhythmic figure of the Chapel Hill indie rock scene across three decades. Parallel career as a comedy performer — best known as one half of Scharpling & Wurster (with Tom Scharpling) on WFMU’s The Best Show.

Biography

Early career: The Accelerators (1982–1991)

Before Superchunk, Wurster played drums in The Accelerators (Discogs ID: 601129), an American rock band from Raleigh, North Carolina active 1982–2000. Their discography on Discogs runs from a 1983 single (“Leave My Heart,” Dolphin Records) through a 1988 self-titled LP and 1991 album Dream Train (both Profile Records), and a 2007 reunion album Road Chill (Sound Asleep Records). Wurster appears across these releases as drummer.

Superchunk (1992–present)

Superchunk formed in Chapel Hill in 1989, with Mac McCaughan (vocals, guitar), Laura Ballance (bass), and Jim Wilbur (guitar). Their first drummer was Chuck Garrison, who appears on the debut LP Superchunk (Matador, 1990) and No Pocky For Kitty (Matador / City Slang, 1991). Wurster replaced Garrison and is first credited on releases from 1992 onward — the Hit Self-Destruct Australian 7” (1992) and the Mower EP (City Slang, 1992) are his earliest confirmed Superchunk credits. He is listed as full band member on Foolish (Merge, 1994) and every subsequent release.

From 1992 forward Wurster has been on virtually every Superchunk release — a run of over 30 years. Key albums he played on:

McCaughan and Ballance co-founded Merge Records (Chapel Hill), which became the primary home for Superchunk from the mid-1990s onward. The Clambakes self-released live series (Vols. 1–10, 2002–2020) also documents Wurster-era performances.

The Mountain Goats (2007–present)

Wurster joined The Mountain Goats — the long-running project of John Darnielle — as the band’s drummer around 2007–08. His first Mountain Goats credit on Discogs is Heretic Pride (4AD, 2008), where he is explicitly credited with “Drums” alongside Peter Hughes (bass) and Erik Friedlander (cello), with Scott Solter and John Vanderslice producing. All subsequent Mountain Goats albums list him as a core member.

The move to Merge Records in 2011 (All Eternals Deck) consolidated the Wurster axis: from that album onward, The Mountain Goats and Superchunk shared the same label, the same drummer, and often the same Chapel Hill orbit.

BobMouldBand (touring)

The BobMouldBand (Discogs ID: 1082926) is the live touring unit for Bob Mould — described by Discogs as “live backing band for Bob Mould’s touring.” Members include Jason Narducy, Jim Wilson (6), Jon Wurster, Matt Hammon, Michael Cerveris, and Richard Morel. The only Discogs release under this credit is LiveDog98 The Forum, London UK (Granary Music, 2002) — a live document from Bob Mould’s touring band.

Split Single (2012–present)

Split Single (Discogs ID: 3822097) is a two-piece consisting of Jason Narducy (lead vocals, backing vocals, guitar, keyboards; also co-produced) and Jon Wurster (drums, percussion). Narducy — who also appears in the BobMouldBand — leads the project. Split Single released on Inside Outside Records and later Rough Trade:

Scharpling & Wurster (1999–present)

The comedy duo of Jon Wurster and Tom Scharpling (Discogs ID: 1698160), whose material consists primarily of phone-call bits: Wurster voices characters — including “Philly Boy Roy” (his main alias on Discogs) — calling in to The Best Show on WFMU (later The Best Show with Tom Scharpling), hosted by Scharpling. Scharpling & Wurster’s website is scharplingandwurster.com; their material is also cataloged at stereolaffs.com. The duo’s Discogs name variations include “The Best Show On WFMU.”

Wurster is also credited as Executive Producer on Rock, Rot & Rule (1999) under his own artist ID (257888), separate from the duo credit.

Grand National (3) and Psychotic Norman

Grand National (3) (Discogs ID: 4875101) is a Chapel Hill, NC rock band with members Ed Crawford, Jenni Snyder, Jon Wurster, and Laura King (2). No releases appear in the Discogs discography under this group.

Psychotic Norman (Discogs ID: 3748184) and Test Pattern (4) (Discogs ID: 7024509) are also listed as Wurster group memberships with no Discogs discography data.

The Pinetops (Discogs ID: 1430192) is another listed group membership with no Discogs discography data.
