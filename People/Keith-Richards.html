Keith Richards

Discogs ID: 166570. British guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter; co-founder of The Rolling Stones.

Born 18 December 1943, Dartford, Kent. Core member and musical engine of the Stones from 1962 to the present. Principal guitar voice and co-author (with Mick Jagger) of the Jagger-Richards songwriting partnership, which also records as The Glimmer Twins in production contexts. Plays bass on several tracks across the Stones catalog. In 1979 co-led The New Barbarians with Ron Wood on a North American tour.

Connections

  • Rob Fraboni — produced/engineered Wingless Angels (Island Jamaica, 1996) and Wingless Angels Volumes I & II (2010); recorded and mixed tracks on Bridges To Babylon (Rolling Stones, 1997)
