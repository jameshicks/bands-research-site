Erlend Skomsvoll

Norwegian pianist, tuba player, conductor, and arranger. Born 12 May 1969 in Bærum, Norway. Discogs ID: 238344

Overview

Erlend Skomsvoll is one of Norway’s most versatile jazz musician-arrangers: equally at home as a performing pianist in small-group settings and as the long-serving conductor and chief arranger of the Trondheim Jazz Orchestra (TJO). He has been a member of the TJO since its founding, and his name appears on every major TJO release in a conducting or arranging capacity.

Beyond the orchestra, he has been a member of the groups Wibutee, Come Shine, and the Erlend Skomsvoll Trio. His session keyboard work spans everything from Norwegian hip-hop (Det E Hardt Å Være Mainn, 1996, Kirkelig Kulturverksted) to contemporary jazz and classical crossover. As a co-leader he released Louder Than You (2017, Øra Fonogram) with vocal collective Molecules.

His primary label affiliations across three decades are Jazzland Recordings, MNJ Records (the TJO’s house label), and Rune Grammofon (via the Hedvig Mollestad / TJO collaboration).

Maternity Beat (2022) — Key Personnel

Produced by Hedvig Mollestad Thomassen. Recorded by Dag Erik Johansen. Mixed by Bård Ingebrigtsen. Mastered by Helge Sten (Deathprod). Sleeve design by Kim Hiorthøy.

TJO players on the session: Ellen Brekken (double bass), Torstein Lofthus (drums), Ståle Storløkken (Hammond, Rhodes, synths), Trine Knutsen (flutes), Ingvald André Vassbø (percussion), Martin Myhre Olsen (soprано/alto/baritone sax), Petter Kraft (tenor sax), Thomas Johansson (trumpet), Adrian Løseth Waade (violin), Ingebjørg Loe Bjørnstad and Mai Elise Solberg (vocals).

Scene Connections

  • Norwegian jazz / Trondheim scene — TJO is based in Trondheim; Skomsvoll is one of its founding creative pillars alongside Eirik Hegdal and others.
  • Rune Grammofon — Appears via TJO collaborations with Mollestad (Maternity Beat) and the Motorpsycho crossover The Death Defying Unicorn.
  • Jazzland Recordings — Recurring presence as session pianist across multiple artists.
  • ECM-adjacent Norwegian jazz — Circles overlap with Jan Erik Kongshaug (Rainbow Studio engineer), Arve Henriksen, and the broader Manfred Eicher production network, though Skomsvoll’s own releases sit outside ECM proper.
  • Crossover / orchestral jazz — The TJO model — large ensemble, rotating guest soloists — connects to the European tradition of composer-led jazz orchestras (ACT label, Hubro).
