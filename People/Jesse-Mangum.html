Overview

Jesse Mangum is an audio engineer and producer based in Athens, GA, operating The Glow Recording Studio. Active since 2013, he has accumulated over 130 Discogs credits spanning recording, mixing, mastering, and production across indie rock, psychedelic rock, and adjacent styles. His catalogue shows an early connection to Orange Twin Records (2013–2017) deepening into a concentrated run with Scare Flair Records (seven releases, 2022–2023) and Mystic Vault (2023–2025), suggesting The Glow has become a central node in the current Athens recording scene. His most frequent collaborators — Graham Tavel, Andrew Rieger, and Elf Power — reflect sustained ties to that local community.
