Brix Smith

Discogs ID: 73155
Real name: Laura Elisse Salenger
Origin: USA (Chicago)

Guitarist and vocalist. Member of The Fall 1983–1989, joining after marrying Mark E. Smith. Primary guitarist on the Beggars Banquet run — from The Wonderful And Frightening World Of… (1984) through I Am Kurious Oranj (1988). Co-writer on several key tracks across This Nation’s Saving Grace and The Frenz Experiment. Left The Fall in 1989 following divorce from Smith; later had solo releases and returned to The Fall briefly.

Has released solo material: Hurdy Gurdy Man (1992, Nettwerk), Happy Unbirthday EP (1997, Strangelove Records), Neurotica (2007, Loser Friendly Records), Lost Angeles (2021, Cadiz Entertainment), Valley Of The Dolls (2023, Grit Over Glamour Records).
