Austrian producer, multi-instrumentalist, and electronic musician. Born Vienna, 1975. One of the earliest and most persistent figures in European laptop-pop and ambient electronics. Best known as the debut artist on Morr Music — Poploops For Breakfast (MM001, 1999) was the label’s inaugural release, co-issued with Charhizma out of Vienna. Has maintained a presence across two labels and two cities (Vienna / Berlin) for over two decades, evolving from glitchy IDM-inflected bedroom pop to more song-based indie and ambient forms.

Overview

Fleischmann emerged from the late-1990s Viennese underground, where he was involved with the post-punk band Sore! and the improvisational group The Year Of. His solo career began with twin 1999 releases: Pop Loops For Breakfast (Charhizma/Morr) established him as a practitioner of melodic loop-based electronic composition rooted in the Oval/Dntel aesthetic, and drew immediate attention as the opening statement of a new Berlin label.

The period 2000–2003 saw three more solo LPs — A Choir Of Empty Beds (2000), Nico (2001 EP), TMP (2001) — released via Fuzzy Box, Morr’s sublabel A Number Of Small Things, and Charhizma respectively. The 7” Sidonie EP (MM006/Charhizma 006, 2000) appeared very early in Morr’s catalog. Welcome Tourist (MM041/Charhizma025, 2003) marked a more politically engaged turn: several tracks were explicit statements about Austrian politics and modern democracy.

The Humbucking Coil (MM063/Charhizma 035, 2006) was the most collaborative of his studio albums — co-produced and co-mixed by Christoph Amann and Christof Kurzmann, the two producers who appear most often in his discography. The same year he contributed drums, electronics, and piano to The Year Of’s Slow Days (MM066), a group release featuring Werner Dafeldecker, Martin Siewert, and Kurzmann.

Angst Is Not A Weltanschauung! (MM085, 2008) was a largely self-produced, self-recorded and self-mixed effort — the credits listing Fleischmann himself as recorder, mixer, and masterer alongside Amann — and was released in five pressings. A quieter period followed, with Duo505 releases on Konkord and a contribution to a Tied & Tickled Trio-adjacent project.

I’m Not Ready For The Grave Yet (mm119, 2012) marked a return to solo albums on Morr. Stop Making Fans (MM158, 2018) was a fuller-band recording, adding drummer Valentin Duit and guitarist Markus W. Schneider (who also joined Fleischmann in the group B.and Fleischmann). The most recent solo LP, Music For Shared Rooms (Morr Music, 2022), continues in an ambient/electronic direction.

Outside solo work, Fleischmann has worked in three ongoing group contexts: Duo505 (with Herbert Weixelbaum, released on Morr and Konkord), The Year Of (with Kurzmann, Burkhard Stangl, Martin Siewert, and Werner Dafeldecker — improv/post-rock ensemble), and Sore! (early Vienna punk/indie group).

The recurring technical collaborator throughout nearly his entire career is Christoph Amann (Amann Studios, Vienna): mastering, mixing, recording, and production on releases from 1999 to at least 2018.
