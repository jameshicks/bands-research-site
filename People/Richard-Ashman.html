Overview

Richard Ashman is an audio engineer and producer whose career traces a geographic arc from Washington, DC — where he built his early credits in the indie rock scene — through Phoenix, Arizona, where he ran Mastercraft, and finally to Bat Cave, North Carolina, home of his MasterCraft Studios. His 35 Discogs credits stretch from 1985 to the 2010s and are weighted toward engineering and production. The bulk of his work sits within the Teenbeat orbit: he accumulated eight credits alongside Unrest (2) and appears repeatedly with Mark Robinson, Phil Krauth, Tim Moran, and Chris Thomson (2). Alongside that indie rock thread he logged seven credits with ambient and new age musician Laraaji, giving his catalogue an unusually wide tonal range.
