Britta Phillips

American musician, singer, bassist, and multi-instrumentalist. Born June 11, 1963, Boyne City, Michigan. Discogs artist ID: 256620. Alias: Jem (9) (Discogs ID: 1567684) — the singing voice of the title character in the 1985–1988 animated series Jem.

Overview

Britta Phillips has operated across two distinct phases of public life: an early career as a session/voice actress (most famously providing the singing voice of Jem in the Hasbro animated series from 1985) and a sustained career as a performing musician from the early 1990s onward. Her music career began with New York shoegaze/alternative act The Belltower (1991–c.1995) and the 1990s indie group Ultra Baby Fat (also rendered Ultrababyfat; Discogs ID: 1476281). She joined Luna (5) as bassist and vocalist in 2001, replacing Justin Harwood, and remained through the band’s original 2005 dissolution and the 2014 reunion. From 2003 onward she has recorded and toured as one half of Dean & Britta alongside husband Dean Wareham. The duo also scored films for directors Noah Baumbach (The Squid and the Whale) and Morgan J. Freeman (Just Like the Son), and composed the live score for the Dean & Britta presentation of Andy Warhol’s screen tests (13 Most Beautiful…, 2010). Her solo debut Luck Or Magic appeared in 2016 on their own Double Feature Records.

Phillips also participated in The Million Dollar Bashers (Discogs ID: 982142), the large ensemble assembled for the 2007 Todd Haynes film I’m Not There; The Mystery (3) (Discogs ID: 2571768 — no primary releases in Discogs DB); and Babyfat (2) (Discogs ID: 3866496), which released Dragons Go Away (Sister Ruby, 1994).

Career by Era

Jem (1985–1988)

Phillips provided the singing voice of Jerrica Benton / Jem in the Hasbro animated series that ran from 1985 to 1988 in US first-run syndication. The speaking voice was provided by actress Samantha Newark. Under the alias Jem (9) (Discogs ID: 1567684), she is credited on the animated series’ soundtrack releases. The Jem And The Holograms soundtrack (Magic Star, 2025) lists her on backing vocals, indicating continued association with the property decades later.

An early solo/demo recording, Through The Eyes (Not On Label, 1984), predates the Jem role.

Satisfaction soundtrack (1988)

Phillips appears on vocals on the Satisfaction original motion picture soundtrack (AJK Music, 1988, Discogs ID: 5641496).

The Belltower (1991–c.1995)

Phillips was a co-founder and primary vocalist/guitarist of The Belltower, a New York-based shoegaze/alternative band. She appears on all core releases:

Producer Terry Bickers (ex-The House Of Love) produced the debut EP and several Popdropper tracks.

Babyfat (2) / Ultra Baby Fat (1990s)

Phillips was a member of Babyfat (2), which released Dragons Go Away (Sister Ruby, 1994, Discogs ID: 7086015). She also appears in Discogs groups as a member of Ultra Baby Fat (ID: 1476281), which released Bent On and Silver Tones Smile (both Velvel, 1998). On Silver Tones Smile she is credited as Bass, Vocals, Performer.

Luna (5) (2001–2005, 2014–present)

Phillips replaced Justin Harwood as bassist and vocalist in Luna (5) from 2001. Her first appearance on record is the Live album (Arena Rock, 2001) — a transitional document where Harwood and Phillips split the set. She is credited Bass, Vocals on all subsequent Luna releases.

Dean & Britta (2003–present)

Phillips and Wareham began recording as Dean & Britta in 2003, while Luna was still active. The duo uses the name variously as “Dean & Britta,” “Britta Phillips & Dean Wareham,” and “Dean Wareham & Britta Phillips” in Discogs credits. They self-operate Double Feature Records (Discogs ID: 133173).

Key producers on Dean & Britta releases: Tony Visconti (produced L’Avventura and Back Numbers, recorded and mixed both); Eric Broucek (mixed and co-produced Luck Or Magic). Engineer Emily Lazar mastered L’Avventura and Back Numbers.

13 Most Beautiful… was a live performance score commissioned by the Andy Warhol Foundation to accompany 13 of Warhol’s silent screen tests. Additional musicians: Anthony LaMarca and Matt Sumrow. Recorded by Bryce Goggin.

The Mistress America soundtrack credits Phillips specifically as Engineer and Arranged By, with Wareham and Phillips jointly credited as “Score Composed And Performed By.” Mixed by Eric Broucek.

A Peace Of Us (Carpark, 2024) is a collaboration with Sonic Boom (2) (Pete Kember); also features Noah Lennox (Panda Bear) on additional percussion on one track.

Film scores (no standalone Discogs release found)

Discogs references the Dean & Britta profile noting compositions for Noah Baumbach’s The Squid and the Whale and Morgan J. Freeman’s Just Like the Son. Neither film score appears to have a standalone Discogs release entry.

Solo: Luck Or Magic (2016)

Phillips released her solo debut Luck Or Magic (Double Feature Records, 2016, cat# CD-DBL-0012, Discogs ID: 8446577). She is credited as producer, vocals, bass, keyboards, and guitar (tracks 3, 5, 7, 10). Co-produced and mixed by Eric Broucek. Dean Wareham plays guitar on tracks 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9. Drums by Jeff Brodsky. Mastered by Emily Lazar.

A single Love / Fallin’ In Love (Formosa Punk Records, 2014, Taiwan) preceded the album (Discogs ID: 5585906).
