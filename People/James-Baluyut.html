Overview

James Baluyut is a New York guitarist, producer, and recording engineer whose career stretches back to 1992 across four bands: Versus, +/- (Plus/Minus), The Melba, and Whysall Lane. His catalogue sits in indie rock and indie pop, with Teenbeat Records as his primary label home from 1995 through 2014 and more recent releases on Ernest Jenning Record Co. and And Records. His closest working relationships are with Richard Baluyut, Patrick Ramos, and Fontaine Toups — recurring collaborators across multiple projects — alongside frequent studio partners Nicolas Vernhes and Emily Lazar. Beyond performing and songwriting, he contributes recording and production credits throughout the catalogue.
