Overview

Mark Elliott is a Dallas-based record producer who co-owned Leaning House Jazz with Keith Foerster. His production credits are concentrated in the 1990s Texas indie underground, most notably through deep collaboration with Bedhead and both Kadane brothers — Bubba and Matt — making him a recurring figure in the Dallas slowcore and post-rock scene alongside Adam Wiltzie. His label affiliations span Trance Syndicate Records, Touch And Go, and A&M, reflecting work that reached beyond local circuits, and his catalogue extends from 1987 through the 2010s via archival reissues on Numero Group.
