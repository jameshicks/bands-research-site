Devin Hoff

Real name: Devra Hoff Discogs ID: 326722 Instruments: Double bass, bass guitar, contrabass, fretless bass, bass VI, cigar box guitar Origin: Colorado (born); San Francisco Bay Area (based) Bandcamp: https://devinhoff.bandcamp.com/ Name variations on record: D. Hoff, D Hoff, Devon Hoff, Dave Hoff, Devin, Hoff

Bassist and songwriter born in Colorado, based in the San Francisco Bay Area. Hoff is one of the most broadly connected session and ensemble players in West Coast experimental music, spanning free jazz, avant-rock, improvised music, folk, and orchestral pop. Active since at least the late 1990s across a remarkably diverse cast of artists, Hoff’s credit trail runs from intimate improvised settings to major label orchestral pop (Julia Holter, Julia Holter), and from anarcho-punk tributes (The Emma Goldman Bust-Out Brigade with Mike Watt) to Xiu Xiu’s long-running avant-pop arc.

Biography

Early Credits (1999–2003)

Hoff’s earliest Discogs trace is as primary artist on A Necessary Conversation (1999, Music In The Vines), a US release that predates the Bay Area scene work. By 2002 she was appearing in The Nels Cline Singers debut Instrumentals on Cryptogramophone, the LA-based free improv / jazz label that would host much of her best-documented work through 2010. The Nels Cline Singers lineup on that record comprised Nels Cline (guitar), Scott Amendola (drums/percussion/electronics), and Hoff (contrabass) — a tight trio format retained across the group’s most active recording period.

The same period brought her into Xiu Xiu’s orbit: she played double bass (credited as “Devon Hoff”) on the 2003 EP Fag Patrol (Free Porcupine Society) alongside Jamie Stewart and Cory McCulloch, and was part of the sprawling 7 Year Rabbit Cycle collective — a Jamie Stewart side project with a rotating cast including Ches Smith, Rob Fisk, Miya Osaki, Steve Gigante, John Dieterich, and others — whose debut Animal People also appeared on Free Porcupine Society in 2003. Free Porcupine Society was a key early SF-scene imprint connecting many of these collaborators.

Mid-2000s: Nels Cline, Xiu Xiu, Ben Goldberg (2004–2007)

The most concentrated period of ensemble activity. Hoff contributed to the Nels Cline Singers’ The Giant Pin (2004, Cryptogramophone) and appeared on Nels Cline’s solo tribute New Monastery (A View Into The Music Of Andrew Hill) (2006, Cryptogramophone), alongside Ben Goldberg (clarinet), Bobby Bradford (cornet), Scott Amendola, and Alex Cline (percussion). Also in 2006 she was in the Ben Goldberg Ben Goldberg Quintet on The Door, The Hat, The Chair, The Fact (Cryptogramophone).

7 Year Rabbit Cycle released Wind Machines (2004, Free Porcupine Society), with production/mixing by Greg Saunier and John Dieterich of Deerhoof. The 7YRC lineup for Wind Machines is credited across Hoff, Jamie Stewart, Rob Fisk, Steve Gigante, Miya Osaki, Kelly Goodefisk, with Adam Werner and Cain Blanchard as accompanied performers.

In 2006 Hoff also played double bass on The Air Force (Xiu Xiu, 5 Rue Christine / Chthonic Records), produced by Caralee McElroy, Greg Saunier, and Jamie Stewart — one of Xiu Xiu’s most widely noted records.

The Redressers, a chamber-folk quartet with Carla Kihlstedt, Ches Smith, and Marika Hughes, released “To Each According…” on Free Porcupine Society (2004). This is one of several groups where the Smith–Hoff axis is central.

Nels Cline Singers Draw Breath and Initiate (2007–2010)

Draw Breath (2007, Cryptogramophone) and Initiate (2010, Cryptogramophone) mark the culmination of the Nels Cline Singers’ Cryptogramophone run. On Initiate, Hoff is credited as composer on several tracks alongside Scott Amendola and Nels Cline, confirming her compositional role in the group. The extended lineup by 2010 included Brian Marsella, Cyro Baptista, Eric Walton, and Trevor Dunn alongside the core three. Also on Initiate: Carla Bley and Joe Zawinul compositions appear — the group was engaging the wider jazz canon.

Also in 2010, Dirty Baby (David Lichtenstein / Ed Ruscha project, Cryptogramophone) has Hoff on contrabass, bass guitar, and cigar box guitar — an unusual texture credit that suggests flexibility beyond pure bass roles.

Xiu Xiu Women As Lovers, wider pop sessions (2008–2013)

Women As Lovers (2008, Kill Rock Stars) listed Hoff as a full “Performer [Xiu Xiu]” member alongside Caralee McElroy, Jamie Stewart, and Ches Smith — the broadest membership credit she receives on a Xiu Xiu record. This marked one of the more stable Xiu Xiu lineup configurations.

Outside the core scene: Hoff played double bass on Good City For Dreamers (2008, Discograph) by a French release artist, and electric upright bass on Tara Jane O’Neil’s self-titled 2017 LP (Gnomonsong). Julia Holter’s Loud City Song (2013, Domino) has Hoff on double bass and fretless bass — one of the most high-profile pop-crossover credits. She continued with Holter across Have You In My Wilderness (2015, Domino), In The Same Room (2017, Domino), and Aviary (2018, Domino), establishing a long working relationship.

Made To Break (2013–2019)

Made To Break is a free-improvisation quartet led by reed player Ken Vandermark, with Hoff on bass (often bass VI), Tim Daisy on drums, and Christof Kurzmann on electronics (Lloopp). The group is based around international residency and festival work, releasing on Trost Records (Vienna) and Clean Feed (Lisbon):

  • Provoke (2013, Clean Feed)
  • Cherchez La Femme (2014, Trost Records)
  • Before The Code (2015, Trost Records)
  • Trébuchet (2017, Trost Records)
  • F4 Fake (2019, Trost Records)

This is Hoff’s most prolific single-group recording run on record, and the group’s consistent use of Trost Records made them a flagship act for that label.

Solo Production and Anne Briggs Tribute (2021)

Voices From The Empty Moor (Songs Of Anne Briggs) (2021, Kill Rock Stars) is credited to Devin Hoff as primary artist, with Hoff serving as producer and mixer. The album features Rob Jones (3) on layout/design and cover art by Tivona. This is Hoff’s most prominent solo release by Discogs credit, a tribute to the English folk singer Anne Briggs.

The Emma Goldman Bust-Out Brigade (2021)

A three-way collaboration with Mike Watt (Fender Bass, mixed by, producer) and Joseph Berardi (drums), all three credited as songwriters. The record appeared on Nomad Eel Records (2021). Hoff plays Ampeg Baby Bass — a specific tonal choice that distinguishes it from her usual double bass work. This is a distinct political-folk/punk project, named after the anarchist Emma Goldman.

Mendoza Hoff Revels and Recent Work (2023–2024)

Mendoza Hoff Revels is a quartet with Ava Mendoza (guitar), Ches Smith (drums), and James Brandon Lewis (tenor saxophone), releasing Echolocation (2023, AUM Fidelity). This continues the Hoff–Smith axis and adds Mendoza, who brings the group into the orbit of radical NYC/Bay Area guitar improvisation.

Xiu Xiu’s Girl With Basket Of Fruit (2019, Polyvinyl) brought Hoff back into a core session role (double bass and bass guitar) on one of the band’s best-reviewed recent records. The 2024 credit on Julia Holter’s Something In The Room She Moves (Domino) confirms that Holter relationship ongoing into the mid-2020s.

Additional recent credits include Ches Smith’s projects (Reasons For Moving, Calling All Portraits), Shannon Lay’s Living Water (2017, Woodsist), and appearances on film soundtrack work (Bleed For This OST, 2016, Milan).
