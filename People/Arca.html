Arca

Producer, composer, vocalist, multi-instrumentalist. Born 14 October 1989 in Caracas, Venezuela. Raised partly in the US; studied at New York University. Later based in London, then Barcelona. Non-binary; she/her and they/them pronouns used in different contexts. Real name: Alejandra Ghersi Rodríguez. Also credited as Alejandro Ghersi and under the alias Nuuro. Discogs ID: 2868236 (listed as Arca (4)).

One of the most influential voices in experimental electronic music and art pop from the early 2010s onward. Emerged through the NYC underground label Hippos In Tanks before breaking through as a production collaborator for Björk and others, then developed an ambitious and singular solo discography across Mute and XL Recordings.

Origins and Early Work (2011–2013)

Arca’s first release on Discogs is Baron Foyel (2011), a DJ mix and production showcase released via DIS Magazine (the online platform/label). This was followed by Stretch 2 (2012) on UNO (cat no. UNO007), a 12” EP with genres spanning Abstract, Bass Music, Deconstructed Club, and Glitch Hop — an early signal of the fractured, post-club sound she would become known for.

The Hippos In Tanks phase followed, with the early mixtape Safety Net (2012, mixed by Arca) and the EP &&&&& (2013, Hippos In Tanks, Discogs ID: 4761195). These records established her reputation in the art-electronic underground — alongside figures associated with the Tri-Angle and PAN scenes.

Around this same period came the pivotal Kanye West connection.

Kanye West / Yeezus (2013)

Arca received a Production Consultant credit on Yeezus (2013, Def Jam Recordings, Discogs ID: 4665945), alongside Hudson Mohawke and Young Chop. The album’s abrasive industrial textures were widely noted, and Arca’s consultancy role — while not a direct producer credit — placed her name in one of the year’s most prominent releases. This accelerated interest in her solo work and other collaborations.

Xen (2014) — Mute

Xen (2014, Mute Records, UK, Discogs ID: 6257028, master 754416) was Arca’s debut album proper. The title was taken from a name she had used for herself — an alter-ego that predated her public coming out as non-binary. Released on Mute, which would remain her home for the first phase of her solo career. Discogs credits are sparse (the release carried no additional credits), suggesting tight solo authorship.

Styles: Electronic, Experimental.

Björk’s Vulnicura (2015) — One Little Indian

Vulnicura (2015, One Little Indian, France pressing, Discogs ID: 6768364, master 789040) is the album most associated with the Arca–Björk partnership. Arca’s specific credit is Programmed By. The production team also included The Haxan Cloak (Mixed By) and Mandy Parnell (Mastered By). Björk self-produced and arranged strings and vocals.

Arca’s programming work on Vulnicura defined the album’s dissonant, disintegrated electronic textures — a sound that departed sharply from the orchestral pop of Biophilia.

Vulnicura Strings (2015)

A companion album consisting of string-only reworkings. Arca contributed Music By credit on track 8.1. Also features Mandy Parnell (Mastered By) and Björk (Mixed By, among many other roles). Discogs ID: 7568195.

Vulnicura Live (2016)

Arca received substantial credits on the live album: Arranged By [Rearranged By], Remix [Remixed By] (tracks C3, C4, CD-8, CD-9); Composed By (tracks B2, C1, CD-5, CD-6); Electronics, Beats, Adapted By [Electronic Beat Adaptations]; and Mixed By. The Haxan Cloak held parallel credits (Rearranged/Remixed: tracks D3, D4, CD-12, CD-13; Electronic Beat Adaptations). Mastered by Mandy Parnell. Discogs ID: 8651418.

Mutant (2015) — Mute

Mutant (2015, Mute Records, UK, Discogs ID: 7695838, master 916386). Follow-up album on Mute. Credits name Jesse Kanda (artwork) and Alejandro Ghersi (performer / songs). Jesse Kanda is a visual artist and close collaborator who would continue working with Arca across multiple releases (see below).

Björk’s Utopia (2017) — One Little Indian

Utopia (2017, One Little Indian, UK, Discogs ID: 11154831, master 1270127). Arca’s credits: Concept By [Conceived By] (jointly with Björk); Electronics, Synth [Synth Melodies], Beats. The album featured a large ensemble of Icelandic flute players, a choir, and mixed by Heba Kadry (tracks 2, 5, 6, 7, 8, 11) and Marta Salogni (tracks 1, 3, 4, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14). Mastered by Mandy Parnell. Visual credits shared between Jesse Kanda and M⁄M (Paris); James Merry as visual co-creative director.

Utopia represented a shift from Vulnicura’s grief toward something more utopian and poppy, with Arca’s electronic contributions blending with dense choral and flute arrangements.

Arca (2017) — XL Recordings

Arca (2017, XL Recordings, UK, Discogs ID: 5039041, master 1161309). First album on XL Recordings — a move from Mute. Self-produced, performed, and mixed. Credits: Programmed By, Instrumentation By, Vocals, Producer, Mixed By (all Arca); Written-By (as Alejandro Ghersi); Artwork (Jesse Kanda); Mastered By (Matt Colton). Marked a shift toward more overtly emotional and vocal material.

Björk — Collaboration Continues (2018)

Arca produced Blissing Me (2018, One Little Indian, Discogs ID: 11600988) and wrote/composed Family (Bloom’s North Mix) (2015, One Little Indian) and both Lotic remix singles of Notget (2015). On the Utopia single Arisen My Senses (2018, Discogs ID: 11745428), Arca is credited as Featuring.

FKA Twigs — Magdalene (2019)

Arca contributed Effects [Vocal Processing], Programmed By [Synth Programming] to the track Holy Terrain on FKA twigs’ album Magdalene (Discogs release ID: 14123426; album master: 1630931, 2019, Young Turks). Note: Arca is not credited on FKA Twigs’ earlier LP1 (2014) or M3LL155X (2015) in the Discogs database — the primary Twigs collaboration on record is this 2019 track.

The KiCk series (five volumes) represented an ambitious multi-album project released across 2020–2021, featuring collaborators including Rosalía and others (confirmed by other sources; Discogs credits for KiCk volumes are sparse).

Oneohtrix Point Never / Magic (2021)

Arca contributed Vocals to track 15 of Magic Oneohtrix Point Never (Blu-Ray Edition) (2021, Warp Records, Discogs ID: 21251941). Other contributors to this release included Caroline Polachek, Four Tet, A. G. Cook, and Elizabeth Fraser.
