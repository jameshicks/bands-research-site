Walter Martin

Discogs ID: 491962
Real name: Walter R. Martin
Origin: USA

Keyboardist, bassist, and multi-instrumentalist; member of The Walkmen (2000–2013; reunion 2022+) and previously Jonathan Fire*Eater. Credited with “Organ, Pandemonium” on Bows + Arrows. Solo career post-hiatus.
