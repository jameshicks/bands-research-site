Overview

Ernst Glerum is a Dutch double bassist born in 1955, embedded at the heart of Amsterdam’s free improvisation and avant-garde jazz scene. With 180 Discogs credits beginning in 1979, he has worked across the Amsterdam String Trio, Available Jelly, BBGtrio, and the Benjamin Herman and Curtis Clark quartets. His closest collaborators form the axis of the Amsterdam improvised music world: Han Bennink (60 shared releases), Wolter Wierbos (48), Ab Baars (39), Tobias Delius (33), and Tristan Honsinger (33), with 29 shared releases alongside ICP Orchestra. His catalogue appears primarily on Dox Records, hatOLOGY, and DIW Records.
