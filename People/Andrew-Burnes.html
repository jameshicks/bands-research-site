Andrew Burnes

Atlanta-based guitarist. Discogs artist ID: 187885. Originally played electric bass in Barrel (3) (early 1990s Atlanta post-hardcore), then transitioned to guitar for San Agustin (1996–present, with David Daniell and Bryan Fielden). Also plays lap steel; released a solo lap steel album Telescope in 2008 on Table Of The Elements.

Name variations in credits: “Burnes.”
