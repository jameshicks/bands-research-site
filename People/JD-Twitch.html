JD Twitch

Glasgow-based DJ, producer, compiler, and label founder. Discogs artist ID: 315160 (also Keith McIvor alias page: 166882).

Real name Keith McIvor. Born and based in Glasgow. Died 19 September 2025 from terminal brain cancer.

Overview

Half of the DJ duo Optimo (2) (Optimo Espacio) with Jonnie Wilkes. Co-ran the Optimo night at Sub Club, Glasgow from 1997. Founded Optimo Music in 2009. Previously ran the Pure techno night in Edinburgh from 1990 (as “Twitch”) and the labels Pi Recordings and T&B Vinyl.

Known for extreme eclecticism: techno alongside post-punk, no wave, African music, industrial. Celebrated for mixing skill and encyclopedic musical knowledge. Champion of ZE Records, Liquid Liquid, German post-punk, and South American music.
