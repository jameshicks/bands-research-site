Overview

Ian Holford is a Scottish drummer and vocalist active from 1992 to the present, best known for his membership in The Nectarine No. 9 and The Sexual Objects — both key projects of Glasgow post-punk figurehead Davy Henderson, and both fixtures of the Creeping Bent label. He is also a member of Gareth Sager And The Hungry Ghosts and The Bible For Dogs, extending his ties across the broader post-punk lineage into the Rip Rig + Panic circle. His catalogue spans three decades — sparsely in the 1990s via Postcard Records, concentrated through the 2010s, and continuing into the 2020s — with Creeping Bent as his primary label home from 1999 through 2025. His closest collaborators, David Henderson and Simon Smeeton, place him firmly within the same tight Glasgow indie-art scene across all eras.

The Gareth Sager connection

The most likely bridge between Holford’s Glasgow/Edinburgh world and Gareth Sager is Creeping Bent, the Glasgow independent label founded by Douglas MacIntyre in 1994. Sager’s relationship with Creeping Bent predates the Hungry Ghosts project by nearly a decade: he signed to the label around 2009 and released Slack Slack Music with his CBO ensemble that year, followed by The Last Minute of Normal Time (2004 per the “Creeping Bent Triptych” sequencing) and Juicy Rivers (2018), which he describes as completing that triptych. Holford is a long-standing Creeping Bent artist via his work with Nectarine No. 9 and the Sexual Objects, and Douglas MacIntyre (the label’s founder) is himself a Sexual Objects bassist — meaning Holford and MacIntyre are bandmates. Sager arriving on Creeping Bent placed him directly inside the same tight label/social ecosystem that Holford already occupied. (sjsmastering.com, garethsager.bandcamp.com/album/juicy-rivers)

A second thread is shared Edinburgh roots. Sager was born in Edinburgh in 1960; Henderson’s circle (Fire Engines, Nectarine No. 9, Sexual Objects) is also centred in Edinburgh. Sager left Edinburgh for Bristol and became a founding member of The Pop Group and then Rip Rig + Panic, but his origins place him within the same generation and city as the Henderson/Holford milieu, even if he operated in different orbit for decades. (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gareth_Sager, as of 2026-10-22; freaksrus.net)

By the time of Juicy Rivers (2018), Holford is described in press materials as a “serial Sager collaborator,” which implies the working relationship had been established before that album. The 2024 Creeping Bent release Maelstrom In The Bare Garden (on which Holford plays drums and percussion and serves as assistant producer) confirms the collaboration continued. Davy Henderson himself contributes vocals to Juicy Rivers, further collapsing the two worlds. The band made its debut live performance at Celtic Connections in Glasgow (February 2019), reinforcing the Glasgow/Creeping Bent axis as the operational home of the project. (glasgowmusiccitytours.com, louderthanwar.com)

Summary of the connection: Sager is an Edinburgh-born artist who returned to Scotland via the Creeping Bent label network. Holford was already embedded in that network through the Henderson bands. The Creeping Bent label — run by Douglas MacIntyre, who is Holford’s bandmate in the Sexual Objects — is the institutional bridge.
