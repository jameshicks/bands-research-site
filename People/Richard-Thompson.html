Richard Thompson

Discogs ID: 241755 Real name: Richard John Thompson Born: April 3, 1949, Notting Hill, London Aliases: Agnes Mirren, Marvyn Prestwick, Woolfe J. Flywheel (pseudonyms used on sleeve credits)

British guitarist, singer-songwriter. Co-founder of Fairport Convention (debut 1967). Later in Richard & Linda Thompson duo. Recipient of OBE. One of the most respected guitarists in British folk-rock.

Unexpectedly played guitar and dulcimer on David Thomas And The Pedestrians’ debut The Sound Of The Sand And Other Songs Of The Pedestrian (Rough Trade, 1981) and is co-primary credited artist on Variations On A Theme (Plurex, 1983) — a pairing with American post-punk that sits outside his folk-rock reputation.

Also in: French Frith Kaiser Thompson (295848) with Fred Frith and Henry Kaiser.

Clive Gregson and Christine Collister both appeared as backing vocalists on Shoot Out The Lights (Hannibal, 1982), Hand Of Kindness (Hannibal, 1983), and Across A Crowded Room (Polydor, 1985); all three were also members of the Richard Thompson Band touring unit in the late 1980s. Gregson appears on electric guitar on Clock Without Hands (Elektra, 2001).
