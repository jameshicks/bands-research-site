Overview

Seattle guitarist and bassist Jim Sangster has been active in the Pacific Northwest indie scene since at least 1981, accumulating 127 Discogs credits across indie rock, garage rock, and power pop. He has played in The Minus 5, Fun At The Zoo, Mark Pickerel And His Praying Hands, Roy Loney And The Longshots, and Sgt. Major, with his deepest recurring partnerships being with Scott McCaughey (79 shared credits), Tad Hutchison (68), and Kurt Bloch (54) — the core of the Young Fresh Fellows circle. He is also the owner of Cruddy Record Dealership, a Seattle-based enterprise that Discogs lists as a label entity alongside his performance credits. Key label affiliations span Frontier Records (1987–2004), Yep Roc Records (2009–2026), and the early PopLlama imprint (1985–1990).
