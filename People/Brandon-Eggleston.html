Overview

Brandon Eggleston is an American freelance recording engineer and producer based in Portland, Oregon, whose credits run from 2003 into the 2020s across indie rock, folk rock, and country rock. He has worked most extensively with The Mountain Goats camp — Sam Kassirer, John Darnielle, Peter Hughes, Jon Wurster, and Matt Douglas all rank among his most frequent collaborators, with over 40 shared releases connecting him to that orbit. His label credits span Merge Records, 4AD, Young God Records, and Drag City, alongside a consistent run of work for Josh Ritter on V2 and later imprints.
