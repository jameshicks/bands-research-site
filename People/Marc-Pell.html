Marc Pell

Real name: Marc David Pell Discogs artist ID: 1428489 Also credited as: Marc Withasee (Discogs ID: 4528317), Suitman Jungle (Discogs ID: 6530001), Marc Origin: London, UK IPI Code: 00590360746 (PRS)

Percussionist and drummer from London. One of the three core members of Micachu And The Shapes (and its continuation Good Sad Happy Bad) alongside Mica Levi and Raisa Khan. Beyond the Micachu axis, he has worked as a session drummer on a range of UK indie and film score recordings, most notably Mica Levi’s film scores for Under The Skin (2014) and Jackie (2016), and on albums by Mount Kimbie and King Krule. He also records solo as Suitman Jungle and as Marc Withasee. Member of the chamber-ensemble collective Orchestrate.

Overview

Marc Pell has been active since at least 2009 as the drummer in Micachu & The Shapes, the London experimental pop group formed around Mica Levi. The group (also active under the alias M.A.T.H.E.S.) released four studio albums on Rough Trade — Jewellery (2009), a live record with the London Sinfonietta (Chopped & Screwed, 2011), Never (2012), and Good Sad Happy Bad (2015) — before renaming themselves Good Sad Happy Bad. Under that name Pell co-wrote and co-composed material; the band released Looking Up At The Sun (2016, self-released), Shades (2020, Textile Records), and All Kinds of Days (2024, Textile Records).

Parallel to the band work, Pell has played sessions for an unusually varied range of artists. He provided drums on Mica Levi’s film score for Jonathan Glazer’s Under The Skin (2014, Milan/Rough Trade) and performed as part of the Orchestrate ensemble on Levi’s score for Pablo Larraín’s Jackie (2016, Milan). He appears as a percussionist on King Krule’s double LP The Ooz (2017, XL/True Panther) and as a performer on both Mount Kimbie albums Love What Survives (2017) and The Sunset Violent (2024), both on Warp Records. He contributed additional drums to Movements (2014) by We Have Band and Rebirth (2018) by Farai (2) on Big Dada. A 2021 credit on Finnish jazz group Elifantree’s Hachi (Eclipse Music) places him in a percussionist collective alongside several international experimental drummers.

As a writer, Pell received a co-writing credit on a track for Tanita Tikaram’s LIAR (Love Isn’t A Right) (2025, Cooking Vinyl).

Under the Suitman Jungle alias he appeared on a 2017 four-way split 7” on the London label Curl (with Kwake Bass, Bianca Scout, and Jacob Samuel). As Marc Withasee he co-produced tracks on singer-songwriter Tanya Auclair’s Semaphore EP (2014, Kit Records).

Orchestrate is a London-based chamber ensemble that has performed Mica Levi’s film scores; Pell is listed as a member alongside cellists, violinists, and other string players. The group is also credited on the Jackie soundtrack as “Score Performed By Orchestrate.”
