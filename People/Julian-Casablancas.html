Julian Casablancas

American singer, songwriter, and label founder. Born August 23, 1978, New York City. Realname: Julian Fernando Casablancas. Discogs artist ID: 430106.

Overview

Julian Casablancas is the lead vocalist and primary songwriter of The Strokes, the New York City rock band whose 2001 debut Is This It became one of the defining albums of the 2000s garage rock revival. He wrote all the songs on the first two Strokes albums and the bulk of subsequent ones. Outside The Strokes he has released a solo album (Phrazes for the Young, 2009) and fronts Julian Casablancas+The Voidz (also listed as The Voidz (2)), a more experimental outfit responsible for Tyranny (2014) and Virtue (2018). He runs Cult Records (3) (Discogs ID: 552048), his own label through which The Strokes released the Future Present Past EP (2016) and The New Abnormal (2020).

Biography

Background and Formation of The Strokes (1978–2000)

Julian Fernando Casablancas was born on August 23, 1978, in New York City. His father, John Casablancas, founded Elite Model Management. He attended the Dwight School in Manhattan, where he met several of the future Strokes members, and reportedly also attended the Institut Le Rosey in Switzerland for a period.

By 1998 he had assembled what would become The Strokes: Casablancas on vocals, Nick Valensi and Albert Hammond Jr. on guitars, Nikolai Fraiture on bass, and Fabrizio Moretti on drums. The band self-released a four-track cassette demo (Discogs release ID: 4396593, 2000) before attracting label interest — signing to Rough Trade in the UK and RCA in the US.

The Strokes: Is This It and Room On Fire (2001–2003)

Is This It (2001, Discogs release ID: 367912) was produced by Gordon Raphael and mastered by Greg Calbi. All songs written by Casablancas. The album launched the garage rock revival and was rapturously received in the UK in particular. In the US the track “New York City Cops” was replaced following the September 11 attacks.

Room On Fire (2003, Discogs release ID: 368350) again brought in Raphael as producer, with head engineering by Toshikazu Yoshioka and mastering by Greg Calbi and Steve Fallone. Casablancas again credited with all words and music. Singles included 12:51, Reptilia, and The End Has No End.

The Strokes: First Impressions Of Earth (2005–2006)

The third album (Discogs release ID: 607174) shifted production to David Kahne for most tracks, with Raphael returning for three. Andy Wallace mixed. Casablancas retained overall songwriting credit, though Valensi, Moretti, and Fraiture each received individual “Music By” credits on specific tracks — the first signs of broader collaborative writing within the band. Singles: Juicebox, Heart In A Cage, You Only Live Once.

Solo Album: Phrazes for the Young (2009)

During The Strokes’ extended hiatus, Casablancas recorded a solo album with producer and multi-instrumentalist Jason Lader (Discogs ID: 359731). Released in 2009 on Cult Records (3) / RCA (Discogs release ID: 1993310). Casablancas performed all instruments alongside Lader. Additional production on one track by Mike Mogis. Guest musicians included Blake Mills (guitar on multiple tracks), Lenny Castro (percussion), Nate Walcott (horns), and Adam MacDougall (organ). Mixed by Brian Sperber; mastered by Brian Gardner and Steve Fallone.

Tracklist: “Out Of The Blue” / “Left & Right In The Dark” / “11th Dimension” / “4 Chords Of The Apocalypse” / “Ludlow St.” / “River Of Brakelights” / “Glass” / “Tourist”

The lead single “11th Dimension” received wide attention and established his solo voice as more synth-forward and New Wave-influenced than the Strokes aesthetic. The album was preceded by a UK promo single, and a live version from Electric Lady Studios for WRXP was released in 2010 (Discogs release ID: 2237010).

Collaborations and Guest Appearances (2008–2013)

  • My Drive Thru (2008, Discogs release ID: 1375921): A Converse-sponsored collaboration with Pharrell Williams / -NSYNC side act N-E-R-D and Santogold (Santigold). Produced by Pharrell Williams, co-produced by Chad Hugo.
  • Dark Night Of The Soul (2009, Discogs release ID: 1834227): Featured on the Danger Mouse and Sparklehorse collaborative album, alongside Iggy Pop, Black Francis, Gruff Rhys, The Flaming Lips, Suzanne Vega, and others.
  • Random Access Memories (2013, Discogs release ID: 4570366): Featured on the Daft Punk album, credited as “Featuring [Starring]” and “Musician.” Appeared on the track “Instant Crush,” which was also released as a standalone UK single (Discogs release ID: 5274359).

Julian Casablancas+The Voidz: Tyranny (2014)

The Voidz debuted as Julian Casablancas+The Voidz with Tyranny in 2014 (Discogs release ID: 6181149) on Cult Records (3). The album is dense, abrasive, and sprawling — a significant departure from Strokes minimalism. Produced and mixed by Shawn Everett, with additional engineering by Ryan Fagman. Mastered by Brian Gardner, Dave Kutch, and Emily Lazar.

Band lineup on Tyranny:

  • Julian Casablancas — vocals
  • Amir Yaghmai (Discogs ID: 572047) — guitar
  • Jeramy Gritter (Discogs ID: 1358961) — guitar
  • Jake Simon Bercovici (Discogs ID: 1651184) — bass, synthesizer
  • Alex Carapetis (Discogs ID: 408108) — drums
  • Jeff Kite (Discogs ID: 1788106) — keyboards

Additional musician: Mike Bloom (additional guitar, “Dare I Care”). Artwork by Warren Fu, Liz Hirsch, El Teneen (chessboard art), and Sam Adoquei (cover painting).

The Strokes: Angles, Comedown Machine, Future Present Past EP (2011–2016)

The Strokes returned in 2011 with Angles (Discogs release ID: 2769624), co-produced by Gus Oberg and Joe Chiccarelli with the band. Casablancas’ vocal role — and collaborative songwriting — had expanded, though writing credits went to the full band. Comedown Machine (2013, Discogs release ID: 4412919) was entirely produced and mixed by Gus Oberg.

The Future Present Past EP (2016, Discogs release ID: 8600976) marked the Strokes’ first release on Cult Records (3), also produced by Gus Oberg.

Julian Casablancas+The Voidz: Virtue (2018)

The Voidz released Virtue in 2018 on Cult Records (3) / RCA (Discogs release ID: 11756638), credited to The Voidz (2). Produced primarily by Shawn Everett with additional production by Alana Da Fonseca and Chris Tabron. Mixed by Andy Wallace (most tracks), Shawn Everett, and Ben Baptie. Mastered by Ben Baptie.

Lineup same core five plus a new addition: Beardo (Discogs ID: 1017761) — guitar.

Fifteen-track double LP spanning garage rock, synth-pop, and post-punk. Standout cuts include “Leave It In My Dreams,” “Qyurryus,” and “Aliennation.”

The Strokes: The New Abnormal (2020)

Fifth Strokes studio album, produced by Rick Rubin — the most significant production shift of the band’s career. Engineered primarily by Jason Lader, who also mixed most tracks alongside Ben Baptie. Mastered by Stephen Marcussen and Stewart Whitmore. Cover art is Bird on Money (1981) by Jean-Michel Basquiat. Art direction by Tina Ibañez.

Casablancas has lyrics credits on most tracks (A1–A3, A5–B4); the track “Bad Decisions” (A4) carries co-writing credits to Billy Idol and Tony James (of Generation X) alongside the band. “At the Door” (A5) carries Music By credits to Richard Butler and Tim Butler of the Psychedelic Furs. Released on Cult Records (3) / RCA (Discogs release ID: 15049564).

Later Work and The Voidz (2022–2025)

The Voidz contributed “Daft Pomp” to a Pomplamoose self-release in 2022. The band continued as a unit, with Casablancas credited on Like All Before You (2024, Discogs release ID: 32027807) and MęĞż øF rÅm (2025, Discogs release ID: 35536117), both on Cult Records (3).

Casablancas also served as executive producer and producer on The Future Sugar (2015, Discogs release ID: 13158833), a Mexican release on Arts & Crafts México / Cult Records (3), and as producer on Best Summer Ever (2016, Discogs release ID: 8381545).
