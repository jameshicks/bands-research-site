Mike Pickering

Born 21 February 1954, Manchester. DJ, musician, producer, songwriter, A&R executive. Discogs artist ID: 21154.

Co-founder of Quando Quango and T-Coy with Simon Topping; key Haçienda DJ from 1984 who introduced house music to the UK at the Nude and Hot nights; co-founder of Deconstruction Records; founder of M People; later long-tenured A&R at Columbia/Sony BMG.

Biography

Early life and punk years

Pickering grew up in Manchester listening to Northern Soul, Motown, and soul through his parents, with early clubbing at Blackpool Mecca’s Highland Room. In 1977 he was singing in Manchester punk bands and was part of the milieu around Joy Division and manager Rob Gretton. He then spent time in Rotterdam, where he began DJing, playing Chic and other dance records. (Clash Magazine, as of 2026-06-25)

Factory Records A&R

On returning to Manchester, Pickering took an A&R role at Factory Records in the early 1980s. In this capacity he signed Happy Mondays (1985), James, and To Hell with Burgundy, helping Factory become one of the most influential independent labels of the decade. (Decoded Magazine, as of 2026-06-25)

Quando Quango (1982–1985)

Simultaneously Pickering co-founded Quando Quango with Gonnie Rietveld (Hillegonda Rietveld), contributing saxophone, vocals, writing, and production. The band released on Factory and achieved an underground US dance hit with “Love Tempo,” championed by Larry Levan and Mark Kamins in New York. See Quando Quango for full discography.

The Haçienda DJ career (1984–1997)

Pickering became a resident DJ at the Haçienda, where he stayed for approximately eleven years. His key nights:

Nude (Fridays, from c. 1984/1986). Pickering launched or took over the Nude Friday night — sources differ slightly on the exact start date, with some placing it at October 1984 and others noting he became established there from c. 1986. He initially played soul, funk, hip-hop, electro, and Motown; from around 1986 he began introducing Chicago house imports, making Nude one of the first UK nights to programme house music systematically. Within weeks of Nude’s house-focused era, the 1,200-capacity venue was drawing 1,600 people on Fridays. (FAC51 The Haçienda, as of 2026-06-25; The Haçienda Wikipedia, as of 2026-06-25)

Hot (from July 1988). Co-hosted with Jon Dasilva, Hot was an acid house night that launched in July 1988, directly inspired by the Ibiza club experience. It became a focal point of the emerging Madchester scene. (The Haçienda Wikipedia, as of 2026-06-25)

Pickering also ran a Shine night (dates less documented in sources). The Haçienda closed in 1997.

T-Coy and Deconstruction Records (1987–1989)

After Quando Quango dissolved (~1986), Pickering and Simon Topping formed T-Coy with session keyboardist Ritchie Close. Working anonymously, they recorded on 8-track in Didsbury and released “Cariño” (1987) on Deconstruction Records — now regarded as one of the first British house records and a Haçienda classic. (“T-Coy” reportedly stands for “take care of yourself.“) The direct connection to Pickering’s Haçienda DJ work is explicit: T-Coy’s sound was shaped by the Chicago and New York imports he was playing at Nude, and the band headlined the Northern House Review tour in February 1988, with a Haçienda date on 26 February featuring DJ sets by Pickering and Graeme Park. Further T-Coy singles: “I Like to Listen” (November 1988), “Night Train” (August 1988). (LTM Recordings T-Coy biography, as of 2026-06-25)

Concurrently Pickering co-founded Deconstruction Records in 1987 with Pete Hadfield and Keith Blackhurst (one source says 1986; majority of sources give 1987). The label became a home for UK dance music through the late 1980s and 1990s. (Deconstruction Records Wikipedia, as of 2026-06-25)

M People (1990–1999)

In 1990 Pickering formed M People with vocalist Heather Small (b. 20 January 1965, London; ex-Hot House), keyboardist Paul Heard (b. 5 October 1960, London; ex-Ace of Clubs/Orange Juice), and later percussionist Shovell (Andrew Lovell, b. 11 February 1969). The group signed to Deconstruction and released their debut single “Colour My Life” in May 1991.

Awards: Mercury Prize 1994 (Elegant Slumming); Brit Award Best British Dance Act 1994 and 1995. Over 11–14 million records sold worldwide (sources vary slightly). M People entered an extended hiatus after 1999, with reunion activity from 2005 onward. (M People Wikipedia, as of 2026-06-25)

Post-M People: Columbia/Sony BMG A&R

After M People, Pickering worked for approximately 20 years as an A&R executive within Columbia Records (Sony BMG), signing Calvin Harris, Kasabian, The Ting Tings, and Gossip, among others. (Decoded Magazine, as of 2026-06-25)

He has continued DJ work and participated in Haçienda Classical concerts, including a Glastonbury Festival appearance.
