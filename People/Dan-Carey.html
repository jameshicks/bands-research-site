Dan Carey

Discogs artist ID: 101581 Role: Record producer, engineer Based: London, UK

British record producer and engineer. One of the most active producers across Domino Records’ catalog (107 production credits). Runs Speakeasy Studios (later Urchin Studios). Founder / primary producer of Speedy Wunderground (Discogs ID: 512148). Known for work with Bat For Lashes, Kate Tempest, Speedy Ortiz, Fontaines D.C., New Order. Also worked under the alias Quiet Village with Matt Edwards. Real name: Daniel De Mussenden Carey.

Groups/bands: Danmass, Heroes For Hire, Howls, Lazyboy, Miss Tiny, Moog (3), Sexwitch, Sunnyroads, The Meditations (2).

Produced Black Midi’s debut single bmbmbm (Speedy Wunderground, 2018) and debut album Schlagenheim (Rough Trade, 2019).
