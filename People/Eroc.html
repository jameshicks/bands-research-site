German musician and producer, born 15 November 1951 in Weimar, GDR. Best known as the madcap drummer with Grobschnitt, he has also been an innovative sound explorer since the mid-60’s, and a talented multi-instrumentalist. As early as 1967, Eroc had developed his own methods of multi-tracking and phasing effects, with voices and instruments, and he explored other creative types of music. In 1970 he recorded the revolutionary echo vocal collage work Horrorgoll for his solo debut, very much like a piece of Dadaist sound-poetry (yet with a rock aesthetic). His solo work includes a wild mix of synthesizer music, space-rock, comic theatrics, schlager and folk musics, with the touch of Eroc’s humour thrown in for good measure. Since the 1970’s, Eroc has also worked as an engineer and producer at Eroc’s Heimstudio and Eroc’s Mastering Ranch.

Overview

Eroc (Joachim Heinz Ehrig, born 15 November 1951 in Weimar, GDR) is a German drummer, producer, and sonic experimenter best known as the drummer of Grobschnitt. His explorations in sound began in the mid-1960s, long before Grobschnitt’s formation, and included multi-tracking and phasing experiments he developed independently; his 1970 recording Horrorgoll — an echo-vocal collage with Dadaist inflections — signals how far his sensibility reached beyond straightforward rock. His solo catalogue spans synthesizer music, space rock, schlager, folk, and comic theatrics, while his studio work as engineer and producer at Eroc’s Heimstudio and Eroc’s Mastering Ranch has given him a significant behind-the-scenes presence in German rock across five decades. His 2,000+ Discogs credits cluster heavily around Grobschnitt associates Lupo, Wildschwein, Mist, and Toni Moff Mollo, with a substantial later-career thread running through Phillip Boa and psych-leaning labels such as Tonzonen and Sulatron.
