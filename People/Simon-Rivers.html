Simon Rivers

Discogs ID: 421814. UK postpunk vocalist, guitarist, keyboardist. Alias: Poor Performer.

Overview

Postpunk singer and multi-instrumentalist with a long career in the UK independent scene. Profile note: “Was in Last Party and its later incarnation the Bitter Springs. Also present when the latter version of the band morphed into a reincarnation of Subway Sect, which at that point was essentially Vic Godard’s backing band.”

Groups: Last Party (ID: 808599), The Bitter Springs (ID: 127618), Subway Sect (ID: 83073), Oldfield Youth Club (ID: 7454772).

Alias Poor Performer has no Discogs releases.
