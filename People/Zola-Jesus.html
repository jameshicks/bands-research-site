Zola Jesus

Oct 15, 20266 min read

Zola Jesus

Born: April 11, 1989, Phoenix, Arizona
Real name: Nika Rose Hummel (per Discogs); also credited as Nika Roza Danilova, Nika (33)
Discogs IDs: 1249728 (Zola Jesus), 1444277 (Nika Roza Danilova alias)
Role: Vocalist, songwriter, producer
Labels: Sacred Bones (primary); Souterrain Transmissions, Mute, Not Not Fun, Troubleman Unlimited, self-released
Groups: Former Ghosts

Overview

Solo project built around Nicole (Nika) Danilova’s operatically trained soprano over industrial electronics and minimalist synth arrangements. Emerged 2008 on cassette imprints and Sacred Bones; achieved wide critical notice with Stridulum II (2010) and Conatus (2011) before signing to Mute for Taiga (2014). Returned to Sacred Bones with Okovi (2017) and Arkhon (2022). Parallel work includes orchestral reworkings (Versions, 2013), the Former Ghosts collaborative project, and film/visual art contexts.

Career by Era

Early releases — cassettes and Sacred Bones (2008–2009)

Debut Poor Sons (2008, Die Stasi) preceded Sacred Bones involvement. Soeur Sewer (2008, SBR) and New Amsterdam (2009, SBR) established the label relationship. The Spoils (2009, SBR-022) closed the first chapter — 9 pressing variants. Tsar Bomba (2009, Troubleman Unlimited) was a concurrent release on a different imprint.

During 2009–2010 also appeared with Former Ghosts — a collaborative project with Freddy Ruppert, Jamie Stewart (2), and Yasmine Kittles — releasing Fleurs (2009) and New Love. (2010) on Upset! The Rhythm.

Breakthrough — Souterrain Transmissions and Stridulum era (2010–2011)

LA Vampires Meets Zola Jesus (2010, Not Not Fun) — split/collaboration LP with Amanda Brown (2) / LA Vampires.

Stridulum EP (2010, SBR) and Stridulum II (2010, Souterrain Transmissions, SOU016LP) — the latter produced by Alex DeGroot (sides A-B1) with Danilova self-producing B2-B4; 8 pressings. Photo by Indra Dunis.

Valusia (2010, SBR) further ep material.

Conatus (2011, Souterrain Transmissions, SOU027LP) — 17 pressing variants, the most-pressed early LP. Produced and engineered by Brian Foote (alias Leech 13); drums Nick Johnson (6); strings: Sean McCann (4) (violin/viola), Ryan York (3) (cello/double bass); mastered Josh Bonati; Dave Rieley additional engineering; DMM cut Hendrik Pauler.

Collaborations and side projects (2012–2013)

In Your Nature (2012, SBR) — collaboration with David Lynch.

New France (2012, ACP Recordings) — collaboration with Orbital.

Versions (2013, SBR, SBR-102) — orchestral reworkings of prior material. Produced, arranged strings, and mixed by JG Thirlwell (Discogs ID 15025); performed at the Guggenheim with MIVOS Quartet: Mariel Roberts (cello), Victor Lowrie (viola), Joshua Modney (violin), Olivia De Prato (violin). Engineered/mixed by Al Carlson; mastered/lacquer Josh Bonati. 8 pressings.

Former Ghosts continued with Whitman / Former Ghosts (2013, Folktale).

Mute era (2014)

Taiga (2014, Mute, CDSTUMM373) — 13 pressings; the largest-budget production to this point. Produced, engineered, and mixed by Dean Hurley (David Lynch’s studio engineer, Asymmetrical Studio). Additional production Chris Coady. Mastered Brian Lucey. Brass: four trombonists, three trumpeters, flugelhorn, tuba; horn contractor Joe Exley. Strings: Yuiko Kamakari (viola), Gillian Rivers (violin), Kim Free (violin), Justin Kantor (cello), Kyra Sims (French horn). Mix assist Blake Mares; additional engineering Brian Rosemeyer + David Tolomei.

Dangerous Days (2014, Mute) — 5 pressings.
Go (Blank Sea remixes) (2014, Mute) — 3 pressings.
Hunger (2014, Mute) — 3 pressings.

Wiseblood (Johnny Jewel Remixes) (2018, Italians Do It Better / Sacred Bones) — 7 pressings. Remixes by Johnny Jewel on Italians Do It Better co-release.

Return to Sacred Bones — Okovi and Arkhon (2017–2022)

Okovi (2017, SBR, SBR-184) — 12 pressings. Co-produced with Alex DeGroot (also mixed); mastered Heba Kadry; lacquer Josh Bonati. Strings: Shannon A. Kennedy (cello/electronics), Julia Sonmi Heglund (double bass), Lauren Baba, Leah Zeger, Rachel Grace (2) (violins); percussion Ted Byrnes. “Siphon” co-produced by James Kelly (5) (alias Wife (4)). Artwork Jesse Draxler.

Okovi: Additions (2018, SBR) — 4 pressings.

Krunk (2020, self-released) — 3 pressings; mastered Heba Kadry.

Live At Roadburn 2018 (2020, Roadburn Records) — 8 pressings.

Arkhon (2022, SBR, SBR-297) — 15 pressings, the most-pressed album. Drums Matt Chamberlain (aliases Mattress Oxide, Spaceboy (7); session drummer).

Recent work (2024)

Plyve Kacha (2024, self-released) — 2 pressings; produced by Randall Dunn. Ukrainian song; reflects Danilova’s Ukrainian ancestry focus.

Also appeared on Scott Herren / Prefuse 73’s The Only She Chapters (2011, WARPLP208) — featured and written-by credit on C1 “The Only Direction In Concrete.”
