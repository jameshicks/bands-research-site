Overview

Florian Von Keyserlingk is a recording engineer, mixer, and producer whose work is concentrated in the 2010s and gravitates toward experimental, krautrock, and art rock. His most sustained collaboration is with Guy Sternberg — 20 shared credits, far outpacing anyone else in his catalogue — and he has also worked repeatedly with the Kreidler ensemble (Andreas Reihse, Detlef Weinrich, Stefan Schneider, Thomas Klein), suggesting a steady presence in the Düsseldorf/Berlin experimental underground. Label credits include Bureau B, Alien Transistor, City Slang, and Frenchkiss Records, with additional appearances alongside Arto Lindsay, Robert Lippok, and Dirk Dresselhaus.
