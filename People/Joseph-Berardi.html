Overview

Joseph Berardi is a drummer, percussionist, and keyboard player based in Los Angeles whose credits stretch back to 1982 and total over 146 releases across alternative rock, punk, experimental, and free improvisation. He has been a member of several groups including Congo Norvell, Double Naught Spy Car, James White & The Blacks, and the Brad Dutz Obliteration Percussion Quartet. His densest collaborative ties run to Stan Ridgway and Pietra Wexstun — each accounting for more than 35 shared releases — pointing to a long-running involvement in Ridgway’s world. His earlier catalogue was rooted at I.R.S. Records through the late 1980s and into the 1990s, with later work spread across smaller labels including Dis-Information Records and Vibro-Phonic Recordings.
