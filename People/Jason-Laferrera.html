Jason Laferrera

Bassist and recording engineer. Discogs artist ID: 503872. Name variations: Laf, Lafferrera.

Member of Gregor Samsa (3) (Discogs group credit). Active as a recording/mix engineer in the Richmond, Virginia indie and punk scene, 2002–2008.

Overview

Laferrera played bass and handled recording duties for Gregor Samsa (3) on their earliest releases (2002–2006). In parallel he worked as an independent engineer and producer for a cluster of Richmond-area bands — AVAIL, The Silent Type, Brainworms, Ultra Dolphins, VCR, Snack Truck, Stop It!!, and Wow, Owls!. His engineering credits span Fat Wreck Chords and several regional indie/DIY labels (SideOneDummy Records, Rorschach Records, The Perpetual Motion Machine, Life In A Box). The Gregor Samsa work is the most visible internationally; the rest is rooted firmly in the Richmond scene.

Notes

  • The Front Porch Stories session (AVAIL, 2002) was produced and engineered by Brian Paulson; Laferrera served as assistant engineer — the same Brian Paulson who later mixed the Gregor Samsa tracks on the 2005 Red Sparowes split.
  • On the The Silent Type Of Writing / Of Violence (2005), Laferrera shared the session with Amber Blankenship and Nathan Altice, both of whom were simultaneously Gregor Samsa members — reflecting the tight overlap between the two bands.
  • His Rorschach Records work (Brainworms, Snack Truck) places him in the DC-adjacent hardcore/punk world alongside that label’s other recording engineers.
  • Listed on Discogs as a member of Gregor Samsa (3) (no other group memberships listed).
