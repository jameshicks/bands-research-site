Bill Frisell

American guitarist. Born 18 March 1951, Baltimore. One of the most recorded jazz and Americana guitarists of his generation; wide range of styles from jazz to country to avant-garde.

Discogs artist ID: 144927.

Appeared on Ambitious Lovers’ Greed (1988) — “Steel Wool” and “Para Não Contrariar Voce” — alongside Joey Baron. Also on Arto Lindsay’s O Corpo Sutil (1995).

Key collaborator of Danny Barnes: guitar on Dirt On The Angel (Terminus, 2003) and Man On Fire (ATO, 2020); 6 shared releases in the Discogs DB.
