Scott Davidson (2)

Bassist of Protomartyr, the Detroit post-punk band active from around 2010. Discogs artist ID: 3220382. Credited as “Scott Davidson (2)” on Discogs to distinguish from other artists of the same name; credited simply as “Scott” on the 2012 cassette debut.

Career

Scott Davidson is the bass player of Protomartyr, the Detroit post-punk band that formed around 2010 and released their first material in 2012. He appears on every studio album from the cassette debut onward, making him one of the four continuous members of Protomartyr’s lineup alongside vocalist Joe Casey (2), guitarist Greg Ahee, and drummer Alex Leonard (2).

Davidson’s role across the Protomartyr catalog is purely performative — no production, engineering, or writing credits appear on Discogs beyond band membership. He is listed as “Written-By” only via the collective band credit (Protomartyr as a whole), not individually. On the Consolation E.P. (2018, Domino) he is credited specifically as Guitar rather than Bass — a departure from his typical role, consistent with the EP’s orchestrated, chamber-inflected arrangements that recruited outside players including Kelley Deal, Evan Ziporyn, and Lori Goldston.

The Formal Growth In The Desert (2023) credits mark the first time he is explicitly tagged as “Band” alongside “Bass Guitar,” consistent with Domino-era credits formalizing the four-piece lineup identity.

Discogs lists no credits outside Protomartyr. No side projects, session appearances, or solo releases appear in the database. His name variations on record are “S. Davidson” and “Scott.”
