Sunna Gunnlaugs

Born Sunna Gunnlaugsdóttir, 11 May 1970. Icelandic jazz pianist and composer. Discogs artist ID: 2394225. Website: sunnagunnlaugs.com.

Her husband is American drummer Scott McLemore, born 1 February 1973 in Norfolk, Virginia — who appears on nearly every release in her catalog as co-producer and bandmate.

Overview

Sunna Gunnlaugs has been active since the mid-1990s, recording primarily in Iceland and the United States. Her output combines original composition with jazz standards and Icelandic folk material. Most of her catalog is on her own Sunny Sky Records, a label she co-runs with Scott McLemore via D’Steam Creative. She works in trio format most consistently (piano, bass, drums), though she has also led a quartet, made duo recordings, and appeared as a sideperson on pop and spoken-word projects.

Her trio’s long-running rhythm section pairs McLemore with Icelandic bassist Þorgrímur Jónsson (born 29 May 1976), who replaced American bassist Drew Gress in the lineup around 2010–11. Her 2010 album The Dream marked the transitional moment — Eivind Opsvik played bass on that record — before the Gunnlaugs / McLemore / Þorgrímur Jónsson trio settled as the working unit for Long Pair Bond (2011) through Becoming (2023).

Her US connections are primarily through drummer McLemore and through New York-based session players (Drew Gress, Tony Malaby, Loren Stillman, Eivind Opsvik) who appeared on earlier recordings. Recording engineer and mixer Kjartan Kjartansson is her primary technical collaborator on Iceland-recorded material.

Groups

  • Sunna Gunnlaugs Quartet — Discogs ID 5652662. Members: Sunna Gunnlaugs, Scott McLemore, Matt Pavolka (bass), Ohad Talmor (saxophone). Recorded Live In Europe (2003).
  • Sunna Gunnlaugsdóttir Trio — Discogs ID 6729257 (also listed as “Sunna Gunnlaugs Trio”). Members: Sunna Gunnlaugs, Scott McLemore, Dan Fabricatore / Þorgrímur Jónsson. Entity spans two different bass players across different eras.
