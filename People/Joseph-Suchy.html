Joseph Suchy

Cologne-based experimental guitarist and mastering engineer. Discogs artist ID: 32888. Active since the late 1980s; primarily known for his long-running work with Ekkehard Ehlers, the Staubgold label, and Burnt Friedman & Jaki Liebezeit.

Overview

Joseph Suchy is a Cologne-based experimental guitarist whose career spans improvised music, electroacoustic performance, and mastering engineering. The Discogs profile describes him as “MIDI-centric” and notes he “often accompanies experimental electronic artists as Ekkehard Ehlers, F.X. Randomiz, Hrvatski.” His work is tightly concentrated in the German experimental scene from around 2000 onward, with Staubgold as his primary home as both a performer and house mastering engineer.

As a guitarist Suchy plays a Lag Telecaster (often with Morley pedals and distortion processing), a credit that appears consistently across the Burnt Friedman sessions. He is also credited on balafon (West African xylophone) on Ekkehard Ehlers’ A Life Without Fear (2006), suggesting a broader instrumental range.

Suchy’s mastering work — sometimes credited as “Klangmeister,” “Mastermeister,” or “Mastering By” — runs across much of the early-to-mid Staubgold catalog and is a parallel career line to his performing work. He also co-produced and produced several Klangwart albums in this capacity.

He is credited as a member of four groups in Discogs: Burnt Friedman & The Nu Dub Players, Four Square Logos, Metall-Assemblague, and Spooky Attraction From A Distance.
