Overview

Wm. Berger (William M. Berger, 1964–2017) was a New York-based sound artist, guitarist, and keyboards player who served as a radio host at WFMU and founded Prison Tatt Records. His recording activity was concentrated in the 1990s, appearing on Shimmy Disc, Teenbeat, Funky Mushroom Records, and the small Nur Sch. Rec! imprint, with band memberships spanning Gamma Rays, Judenfrei, Sinistre, and Jailbird. His tightest recording partnerships were with James Kavoussi, Michael Anzalone, and Uncle Wiggly, reflecting a close circle of downtown experimental, noise, and indie rock collaborators. A single credit surfaced in the 2010s, capping a catalogue that ranges across experimental, psychedelic rock, and art rock.
