Fred Smith (7)

Discogs ID: 1550506
Born: April 10, 1948, New York City, USA
Died: February 5, 2026 (age 77)
Role: Bassist, vocalist
Groups: Television, Blondie, The Fleshtones

American bassist. Was the original bassist of early Blondie (and its precursor Angel and the Snake), departing March 7, 1975. Replaced Richard Hell in Television in 1975 and remained through all three of the band’s active periods (1975–1978, 1991–1993, 2001–2023). Later played with The Fleshtones (1988–89). Also contributed to solo projects by both Tom Verlaine and Richard Lloyd.

Blondie Stint

Smith was a founding member of the band that became Blondie, joining when Chris Stein and Debbie Harry formed a new group with ex-Stillettoes members in July 1974. The band played two shows in August 1974 billed as “Angel and the Snake,” then renamed to “Blondie and the Banzai Babies,” then simply “Blondie” by October 1974. Smith was the bassist throughout this early formation period.

He quit on March 7, 1975 to join Television, replacing Richard Hell. He explained the move: “Blondie was like a boat that was sinking and Television was my favourite band.” (Wikipedia, as of 2026-07-02)

Nature of membership: Pre-debut formation era only — Blondie’s first album was not released until 1976, after Smith had already departed. No known studio recordings with Blondie are documented in available sources; his role appears to have been as a live/rehearsal member during the band’s pre-label period.

Tenure: approximately July 1974 – March 7, 1975 (roughly 8 months)
