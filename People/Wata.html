Wata

Oct 24, 20267 min read

Wata

Discogs ID: 431874 Name variations: Watamantas, ワタ Instruments: Lead guitar, E-bow, effects (Space Echo, Orange amp), accordion, musical box, vocals Active: 1994–present (earliest Discogs credit: Demo Vol. 3, 1994) Groups: Boris (3), BXI

Japanese guitarist and vocalist; one of three core members of Boris (3). Credited on every Boris release from the 1994 demo onward. Her guitar work spans the full range of Boris’s stylistic identity — sculpted drone on Absolutego, post-hardcore leads on Pink, anthemic shoegaze on Smile, and layered noise on 2R0I2P0. Also credited on the 2022 Boris album W (Sacred Bones Records), which, while released under the Boris name, was a primarily Wata-centered songwriting project. Outside Boris, her most significant credit is BXI, the Boris + Ian Astbury collaboration.

Career Overview

Early Boris (1994–2001)

Wata first appears on the Demo Vol. 3 cassette (1994, Fangs Anal Satan) and the debut LP Absolutego (1996), which established Boris’s drone-doom foundation. Her role on Absolutego and Amplifier Worship (1998) was primarily textural — sustained guitar drones alongside Takeshi’s bass and Atsuo’s drums. The Flood LP (2000) is one of her most prominent early credits, a 70-minute single-movement drone piece where her guitar and echo effects are central to the architecture.

Key early credits: Guitar, Effects [Echo] across all releases; E-bow is introduced on Amplifier Worship (1998) and becomes a recurring tool.

Mid-period: Genre expansion (2002–2009)

Boris’s stylistic explosion through the 2000s maps directly to Wata’s expanding role. Heavy Rocks (2002) and Pink (2005) pushed into stoner rock and pop-metal territory where her distorted leads are foregrounded. On Altar (2006, Southern Lord) — the collaboration with [[Bands/Sunn O)))] — she played lead guitar on “Etna” and guitar/Space Echo on “The Sinking Belle (Blue Sheep),” alongside Stephen O’Malley, Greg Anderson, Kim Thayil, and Randall Dunn.

Rainbow (2006) added glockenspiel to her credit alongside guitar and vocals — an indication of her range within Boris’s more melodic side. Smile (2008, Southern Lord) is the record on which her shoegaze-influenced guitar writing is most central; she received lead songwriting and vocal credits across that album.

Walrus / Groon (2007, Hydra Head Records) and Sun Baked Snow Cave (2005, Hydra Head Records) represent the noisier, experimental wing of this era.

BXI (2010)

The band BXI — Boris + Ian Astbury of The Cult — released one LP, Boris & Ian Astbury (2010, Southern Lord; Discogs ID 2411963, master 270340). Wata is credited with Vocals and Guitar. The project united all four Boris members (Atsuo, Takeshi, Wata) with Astbury for a set of melodic heavy rock songs. It remains her most prominent non-Boris primary-credit collaboration.

Later Boris and W (2011–2022)

Through the 2010s Wata remained a constant. Notable credits:

  • Attention Please (2011, Sargent House): credited separately for Guitar/Keyboards and Vocals — one of her most prominent vocal-lead credits.
  • Noise (2014, Sargent House): Vocals, Guitar, Effects.
  • Gensho (2016, Relapse Records): collaboration with Merzbow; Vocals, Guitar, Effects.
  • Dear (2017, Sargent House): Vocals, Guitar, Effects.
  • 2R0I2P0 (2020, Relapse Records): Vocals, Guitar, Effects.
  • No (2020, Fangs Anal Satan): Vocals, Guitar, Effects.

W (2022, Sacred Bones Records; Discogs release ID 21709525, master 2469985): Released under the Boris (3) name, but structured around Wata’s songwriting. She is credited with Vocals, Guitar, Accordion, and Musical Box. Additional contributors: Takeshi (Boris) (Vocals, Bass, Guitar), Atsuo (Vocals, Drums, Percussion, Electronics), guest bassist Tokie (track B3), and Sugar Yoshinaga (Sound Producer; also credited Vocals, Guitar, Synth). Mixed and mastered by Koichi Hara. Issued on Sacred Bones — a significant label relationship for Boris’s later catalog.

Bright New Disease and beyond (2023–present)

Bright New Disease (2023, Sacred Bones Records; Discogs release ID 27368037, master 3131781): A full-band collaboration between Boris and Uniform (5). Wata credited with Guitar. The Uniform lineup on the record includes Michael Berdan (Synth, Vocals) and Ben Greenberg (Guitar, Mixed By). Additional contributors: Randall Dunn (Synth on selected tracks), and Steve Moore (3) (Bass on selected tracks). Recorded by FangsAnalSatan. Mastered by Matt Colton.

Further 2023–2025 activity under Boris’s self-releasing Fangs Anal Satan imprint: “Ibitsu” Re-Recording 2023, And 2 Years Later (2024), -Performing “Fade” At High 20230402- (2024, where she is credited Guitar and Bass), Hello There (2024, Dog Knights Productions), Live Noise Alive (2025, Fangs Anal Satan).

Scene Connections

Wata is a central figure in the overlapping worlds of Japanese heavy psych, drone-doom, and noise rock that Boris has occupied since the mid-1990s. Key nodes:

  • Japanese heavy/experimental scene: Boris emerged from the same Osaka/Tokyo underground that produced Merzbow (multiple shared releases), Nazoranai, and later intersections with Ensemble Pearl (Atsuo’s other group).
  • Drone/doom international network: Altar connects her to the Southern Lord/Sunn O))) axis — Stephen O’Malley, Greg Anderson, Randall Dunn, Dylan Carlson.
  • Hydra Head Records orbit: Boris’s mid-2000s US presence ran through Hydra Head Records, connecting to the broader extreme/experimental American scene.
  • Sacred Bones (2022–present): Boris’s current label home positions them alongside post-punk/goth adjacent artists.
