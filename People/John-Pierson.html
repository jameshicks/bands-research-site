Jughead is the alias of John Pierson, guitarist and, along with Ben Weasel, co-founder of Screeching Weasel. John played with Screeching Weasel from 1986 through 2001, as well as the band’s reunion shows in 2004, but was excluded from the band’s 2009 resurrection. Following Screeching Weasel’s demise in 2001, Jughead formed the acoustic pop-punk band Even In Blackouts, who existed until 2009.

He is also an actor and playwright, performing with Chicago’s Neo-Futurists group and writing plays under the name “Ian Pierce”. He has authored a book, “Weasels in a Box”, which was largely informed by his time in Screeching Weasel. Jughead was also the former co-owner of Panic Button Records.

Overview

John Pierson, known by the alias Jughead, co-founded Screeching Weasel alongside Ben Weasel in 1986 and played guitar for the band through its 2001 dissolution and 2004 reunion shows, though he was not included when the group was revived in 2009. Following Screeching Weasel’s end he launched Even In Blackouts, an acoustic pop-punk project that ran until 2009, and subsequently appeared with Gore Gore Girls (2), Semi Famous, and The Mitochondriacs — a 2018 Stardumb Records credit indicating continued musical activity. Outside music, Pierson performs and writes plays under the name Ian Pierce with Chicago’s Neo-Futurists, authored the Screeching Weasel memoir “Weasels in a Box,” and was a co-owner of Panic Button Records.
