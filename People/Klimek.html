Klimek

Ambient/experimental electronic alias of Sebastian Meissner (born September 27, 1969, Częstochowa, Poland). Discogs artist ID: 34008. Active 2002–2015. Described by a Spex magazine writer as “violently sad sounding music.”

The Klimek project was Meissner’s longest-running and most prominent alias, running from a debut 12” on Kompakt in 2002 through a major burst of self-released material on GOULDEN in 2015.

Biography

Origins and debut (2002)

Klimek emerged as Meissner’s ambient vehicle after several years of glitch/microsound work under other monikers (Random Industries, Random_Inc, Bizz Circuits) in the Force Inc / Mille Plateaux Frankfurt network. The name Klimek is taken from Meissner’s own family name (his mother’s surname appears on the Dedications tracklist: “For Zofia Klimek & Gregory Crewdson”).

The debut was Milk & Honey (Kompakt, 2002), a double-sided 12” (cat: KOMPAKT 61 / KOM 61) with two long-form ambient tracks: “Milk” (8:54) and “Honey” (8:47). Meissner is credited as “Concept By” as well as primary artist. Two tracks — “Milk & Honey” and “Sun(Rise)” — were compiled on Pop Ambient 2003 (Kompakt), placing Klimek in the annual ambient series curated by Wolfgang Voigt that was the label’s primary ambient document.

Kompakt years (2004–2006)

Milk & Honey (LP, Kompakt, 2004; cat: KOM 98 / KOMPAKT CD 33) expanded the 12” concept into a nine-track full-length. The tracklist reworked the original sides into “(Sun)rise (Edit),” “Sand,” “Wind,” “Home,” “Milk (Edit),” “Honey (Edit),” “(Sun)fall,” “Blood & Tears,” and “Back Against The Sea,” plus a bonus video track “(Sun)fall Upwards.” Styles catalogued by Discogs: Abstract, Ambient, Electroacoustic, Experimental, Leftfield, Neofolk, Post Rock.

Solo Swim (Alien Transistor, 2004; cat: N05) was a 12” three-way split with The Notwist and Console (Martin Gretschmann’s project, also a member of The Notwist). The Notwist contributed “Solo Swim” and “Solo Swim Dub” (both Markus Acher / Micha Acher compositions); Console contributed “Mount Everest Horizontal” (written by Martin Gretschmann); Klimek contributed “Expo-Park, Sevilla (Parts I–V) (Solo Swim Rmx)” — a 9:36 remix of The Notwist’s track, credited as “Remix [Not Credited As Remixer]” with a co-writing credit to Sebastian Meissner alongside the Acher brothers. Cover art by Mario Wagner (2). Released on Alien Transistor, the label run by the Acher brothers / Notwist circle in Weilheim, Bavaria.

Klimek tracks also appeared on Kompakt’s Pop Ambient series in the 2004, 2005, 2006, 2007, and 2008 installments.

Listen, The Snow Is Falling (Kompakt, 2005; cat: KOM 125) was a 12” EP. Five tracks arranged across two sides, all credited to Klimek as “Arranged By [Concept / Sound-process / (re)-arrangements)].” Two “Snowfall” pieces use geographic coordinates as subtitles (33°53’ N / 35°31’ E; 42°54’ N / 74°46’ E). The B-side features two pieces “Based On Fragments” of Claude Debussy’s Danses profanes (similarly geo-tagged) and a reading of Erik Satie’s Gymnopédie No. 1. Harp recordings by Katja Leber; recorded by Peter Fey. Design by Sensodyne.

Music to Fall Asleep (Kompakt, 2006; cat: KOMPAKT CD 50) was the second full-length. Twelve tracks, produced and written by Klimek. German/English bilingual parenthetical track titles throughout (e.g., “Standing (Ausser) On The Beach (Gefahr),” “Verlust (White, No Trails) Der Motorik”). Artwork by Sensodyne; front cover photography by Sabine Siegmund.

Anticipate Recordings (2007–2010)

In 2007 Meissner moved the Klimek project to Anticipate Recordings, the New York independent run by Ezekiel Honig.

Dedications (Anticipate Recordings, 2007; cat: Anticipate 004) is an eight-track album composed by Sebastian Meissner. Each track is titled as a dedication to a pair of names — musicians, filmmakers, artists, and personal figures: Jim Hall & Kurt Kirkwood; Ezekiel Honig & Young (Pan) Americans; Zofia Klimek & Gregory Crewdson; Eugene Chadborne & Henry Kaiser; Michael Gira & Vladimir Ivanovich; Marvin Gaye & Russell Jones; Mark Hollis & Giacinto Scelsi; Steven Spielberg & Azza El-Hassan. Cover drawings by Jan Rohlf; packaging design by Ingredient (3); mastered by Twerk.

Movies Is Magic (Anticipate Recordings, 2009; cat: Anticipate 008) is a ten-track album. Track titles in this period are more overtly expressive: “Abyss Of Anxiety (Unfolding The Magic),” “Exposed To Life In It’s Brutal Meaninglessness,” “Exploding Unbearable Desires,” “Pathetic And Dangerous,” “Greed, Mutation, Betrayal,” “A Lament,” “True Enemies And False Friends,” “Sound Of Confusion,” “For Whom The Bells Toll,” “Tears Of Happiness (Dismissed Into Mundanity).” Catalogued as Abstract, Contemporary, Modern Classical.

An extended version of Movies Is Magic followed in 2010 (Anticipate 010).

The live titles (Live at Berghain, Live in Ramallah) suggest an active live performance history. GOULDEN appears to be a self-release vehicle or micro-label. Whether this material was newly recorded or archival/live captures is not determinable from Discogs alone.

Open Questions

  • GOULDEN label — self-release or micro-label? The 11-release burst in one year suggests possible archival/live dump alongside new recordings. Whether any were new studio material is unclear from Discogs.
  • Live history — Live at Berghain and Live in Ramallah indicate an active live practice; no other documented shows in Discogs.
  • Pop Ambient track-level detail — exact tracks contributed to each installment (2003–2008) not fully pulled from Discogs; a track-level query per compilation would retrieve them.
  • Post-2015 activity — Discogs shows no Klimek releases after 2015. Meissner has been writing/directing radio features for Deutschlandradio and WDR3 since c. 2010. Whether the alias has been formally retired is unknown.
