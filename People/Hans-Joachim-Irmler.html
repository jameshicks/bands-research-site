Hans Joachim Irmler

Discogs ID: 74866 Real name: Hans-Joachim Irmler Born: 1950, Germany Instruments / Roles: Organ, synthesizer, electronics, voice; producer, mixer, mastering engineer, label owner

Founding member of Faust (from the 1971 debut). Engineer and owner of Faust Studio (Discogs label ID: 319809) in Scheer, South Germany. Runs the Klangbad label (Discogs ID: 7670) and organises the annual Klangbad Festival in Scheer, both co-operated with Cornelia Paul (a5688187 on Discogs). Credited under numerous name variants including “Jochen Irmler,” “H.J. Irmler,” and simply “Irmler.”

Biography

Faust — Polydor / Virgin Years (1971–1974)

Irmler was among the original six-member Faust lineup assembled in Hamburg/Wümme in 1971 under producer Uwe Nettelbeck and engineer Kurt Graupner. On the self-titled debut (Polydor, 1971) he is credited as performer (billed as “Joachim Irmler”). On So Far (Polydor, 1972) he is co-composer and performer across the whole record. His most specific early instrument credit appears on Faust IV (Virgin, 1973), where he is listed as organist — while Rudolf Sosna handled guitar/keyboards and Gunther Wüsthoff covered synthesizer and saxophone. He also appears uncredited as performer on The Faust Tapes (Virgin, 1973).

The classic Polydor/Virgin-era lineup alongside Irmler: Jean-Hervé Peron (bass), Werner Diermaier (drums), Arnulf Meifert (drums), Rudolf Sosna (guitar, keyboards), Gunther Wüsthoff (synthesizer, saxophone).

Post-Faust and Reformation (1975–1995)

Following Faust’s dissolution in the mid-1970s, Irmler maintained the Faust Studio in Scheer — which became the operational base for the band’s 1990s reformation and the subsequent Klangbad label. His activity during this period is not well documented in the Discogs DB.

Klangbad Founded / Faust Reformation (1996–2002)

Irmler co-founded Klangbad in 1996 with Cornelia Paul. The first Klangbad release was Faust’s Untitled (1996) — a studio album on which Irmler is credited as writer, arranger, performer, producer, mixer, remixer, and masterer. The reformed Faust unit on that release consisted of Irmler, Peron, and Diermaier.

You Know Faust (Klangbad, 1996) followed, with Irmler as mixer and producer. The live document Edinburgh 1997 (Klangbad, 1997) captures a concert performance from that touring period, with Irmler handling post-production and production credits.

In 2002 Irmler served as compilation producer, mastering engineer, and editor on Patchwork 1971–2002 (Staubgold, staubgold 37) — the career-spanning Faust retrospective co-produced with Markus Detmer. The release featured an expanded Faust lineup: Irmler, Peron, Diermaier, Meifert, Wüsthoff, Sosna, Lars Paukstat, Michael Stoll, Steven Wray Lobdell.

Collaboration and Label Expansion (2003–2009)

Irmler released his first credited solo album Lifelike on Staubgold in 2003. The record was engineered by Bruno Gebhard (“Klangarbeiter”) at Faust Studio, with photography/design by Rebecca Harris (2).

In 2004 Derbe Respect, Alder (staubgold 50 / Klangbad 23) paired Faust against US hip-hop duo Dälek (MC Dälek + Oktopus). Irmler performed and produced via Irmler Productions; the release was co-produced by Klangbad and Staubgold, with engineering handled again by Bruno Gebhard.

Klangbad expanded its roster through this period: Finnish acts Markku Peltola and Circle (the Alotus release, 2002), Audiac, Ole Lukkøye, and the compilation Klangbad First Steps (co-released with Wire Magazine, 2002).

In 2007 Irmler compiled and mastered the Klangbad Festival 2007 sampler (Klangbad / Gonzo Circus). He also contributed loops to a release on the Norwegian Go To Gate Records / Save Our Sperms Records imprint (V:S/T + [v:s/t]², 2007).

The 2009 record No Apologies (Klangbad, cat: 44lp) is a studio collaboration with F.M. Einheit (Frank Martin Strauß, ex-Einstürzende Neubauten). Irmler and Einheit share primary billing and co-production; Bruno Gebhard engineered.

To Rococo Rot Collaboration (2009–2010)

Irmler contributed to the To Rococo Rot sessions at Faust Studio. On the Forwardness Fridays EP (Domino, 2010, credited as “Jochen Irmler”) he plays organ on tracks 1 and 2 and is co-writer. On Speculation (Domino, 2010, WIGCD234P) he is credited as co-writer (with Robert Lippok, Ronald Lippok, Stefan Schneider); Andreas Schmid engineered the sessions. Note: Discogs credits Irmler as “Written-By” rather than “Organ” on Speculation itself; the organ performance may be on the EP tracks only.

Continued Collaborations and Solo Work (2010–2018)

Faust Is Last (Klangbad, 2010) — a Faust release produced by Irmler “for Faust & Klangbad.”

500m (Bureau B, 2014) is a co-billed album with Gudrun Gut; Irmler and Gut share writing/production, with Stefan Betke (Pole) handling mastering, Jan Wagner (2) and Andreas Schmid recording, and Gudrun Gut mixing select tracks.

Flut (Klangbad, 2014, cat: KLANGBAD 65) is a duo album billed as Irmler / Jaki Liebezeit (Can drummer). Irmler plays organ and produces; Liebezeit plays drums; Andreas Schmid mixed.

Bestandteil (Klangbad, 2015, cat: Klangbad 68) is the second Irmler / F.M. Einheit collaboration. Both play and co-produce; Schmid mixes.

Formen (Klangbad, 2016, cat: 70LP) is a co-billed album with Carl Oesterhelt. Irmler plays organ, synthesizer, and mixes. The release involves a chamber ensemble: violinists Jörg Widmoser and Winfried Zrenner, violist Andreas Höricht, cellist Jost Hecker, clarinettist/saxophonist Stefan Schreiber, percussionist Salewski. Jan Wagner (2) and Andreas Schmid recorded.

Die Gesänge Des Maldoror (Klangbad, 2017, cat: klangbad 75) extends the Irmler / Oesterhelt partnership, this time with the Stadtkapelle Scheer brass ensemble (conducted by Viktor Schill), vocalist Schorsch Kamerun (Golden Parachutes/Golden Apples of the Sun), and the same chamber strings. Irmler plays organ (Orgel), analog synthesizer, and provides voice.

Anguish (RareNoise Records, 2018) is a record by supergroup Anguish (13), in which Irmler (synthesizer, vocals) appears alongside Mats Gustafsson (tenor saxophone, electronics), Will Brooks (Dälek’s MC Dälek — vocals, sampler, electronics), and Andreas Werliin (drums, percussion).

Faust — Bureau B Era (2021–present)

Faust’s most recent studio work has appeared on Bureau B. Punkt. (Bureau B, 2022) features Irmler, Peron, Diermaier, Wüsthoff, and Sosna — essentially the classic-era nucleus — engineered by Kurt Graupner. The Momentaufnahme series (Bureau B, 2023–2024, volumes I–IV) continues the band’s activity with Irmler as performer.

Klangbad Label

Founded 1996. Based in Scheer, South Germany. Discogs ID: 7670. Label Code: LC 2160. Co-run by Irmler and Cornelia Paul. The label is operationally attached to Faust Studio and the annual Klangbad Festival. The label has released c. 178 items on Discogs.

Connections

  • Faust — founding member; the entire arc of Faust’s discography is Irmler’s primary creative spine
  • Klangbad — co-owner and operator; Faust Studio in Scheer is the physical base
  • Staubgold — released Lifelike solo album (2003); co-produced Derbe Respect and Patchwork with Markus Detmer
  • Bureau B — home for recent Faust output (500m collab 2014, Faust Punkt. 2022, Momentaufnahme series)
  • To Rococo Rot — organ and songwriting contribution to two 2010 Domino releases; sessions held at Faust Studio with Andreas Schmid engineering
  • F.M. Einheit — two duo albums on Klangbad (2009, 2015); Einheit is the former Einstürzende Neubauten percussionist
  • Jaki Liebezeit — duo album Flut (2014); Liebezeit was Can’s drummer
  • Gudrun Gut — 500m collaboration on Bureau B (2014); Gut is associated with the Berlin experimental scene (Malaria!, Monika Enterprises)
  • Carl Oesterhelt — recurring duo partner from 2016 onward; Formen, Die Gesänge Des Maldoror
  • Mats Gustafsson — co-billed on Anguish (13) Anguish (RareNoise, 2018)
  • Dälek — Derbe Respect, Alder (2004) cross-genre collaboration with the NJ hip-hop duo
  • Dieter Moebius — Klangbad label artist; overlap via B.I.L.L. and compilation appearances
