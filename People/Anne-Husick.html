Anne Husick

Guitarist and backing vocalist based in New York City. Discogs artist ID: 450346.

Overview

Anne Husick is a NYC-based guitarist best known as a member of Band Of Susans during the band’s final studio era (1991–1994). She joined alongside fellow guitarist Mark Lonergan, replacing the departing Page Hamilton and Karen Haglof, and became part of the core quartet — Robert Poss, Susan Stenger, Husick, and Lonergan, with Ron Spitzer on drums — that recorded all of the Restless Records period albums. Outside Band Of Susans, Discogs credits show her appearing as a session guitarist and backing vocalist on releases by Bill Popp And The Tapes (1989), Deni Bonet (2005), and Ronnie Spector (2006), indicating an ongoing presence in the broader NYC pop and rock session world.

Discogs lists no aliases and no solo releases under her name. Her primary-credit discography is empty; all credits are as a contributor.

Other Projects

Bill Popp And The Tapes — A 1989 US release on 121st Street Records. Husick plays guitar and backing vocals on four of the ten tracks, alongside guitarist Jerry Barnas, bassist/flutist Stefanie Seskin, and bassist Emily Shane. Bill Popp handled lead vocals and keyboards.

Deni Bonet (Acoustic, OK?, 2005, MR2 Records) — Husick contributes acoustic bass, acoustic guitar, and backing vocals. The album was produced by Paul Bevan (3) and engineered/mixed/mastered by Kevin Salem.

Ronnie Spector (The Last Of The Rock Stars, 2006, Go! Entertainment) — Backing vocals on tracks 1, 3, and 6. Producers on those tracks include Daniel Rey and Angela Piva.
