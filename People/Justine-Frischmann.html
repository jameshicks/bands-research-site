Justine Frischmann

British musician and visual artist, born 16 September 1969, Twickenham, Greater London. Discogs artist ID: 353585. Real name: Justine Elinor Frischmann. Active as musician 1989–2001; painter/artist from c. 1999 onwards.

Overview

Daughter of Hungarian Holocaust survivor and consulting engineer Willem Frischmann. Attended St Paul’s Girls School; studied at the Bartlett School of Architecture, UCL (1989–1993). Co-founded Suede with Brett Anderson (whom she met at UCL in 1988); left Suede in October 1991. Then founded and fronted Elastica (2) (1992–2001).

Post-Elastica, she co-produced and co-wrote early M.I.A. demos (including “Galang”, 2003) for M.I.A.’s debut Arular. Moved to Boulder, Colorado in 2005 to study visual arts at Naropa University. Became a painter; shortlisted for the UK Marmite Prize for painting (2012). Married Ian Faloona (professor, UC Davis) in 2008; lives in the San Francisco Bay Area. (Sources: Discogs; Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justine_Frischmann)
