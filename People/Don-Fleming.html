Don Fleming

Discogs ID: 234554 (alias: Dr. Frankenstein (2))

Musician and producer based in New York. Member of Dim Stars (1991–1992) alongside Robert Quine, Richard Hell, Thurston Moore, Steve Shelley. Sub-producer on Sonic Youth’s Goo (1990, DGC) alongside J Mascis. Additional producer on A Thousand Leaves (1998) and Sonic Nurse (2004) vocal production.

Also played additional percussion on Goo. Part of the NYC underground rock production network alongside Wharton Tiers and others.

Biography (summary)

Born Donald Gene Fleming, September 25, 1957, Valdosta, Georgia. Started in The Stroke Band (Adel, GA, late 1970s), then Citizen 23 (Norfolk, VA, 1979–1981), then The Velvet Monkeys (Washington DC, 1981 onward), a long-running psychedelic/post-punk project with Fleming as constant core member. Jay Spiegel (alias “The Rummager”) became the Velvet Monkeys’ drummer early on; the two remained longtime collaborators.

By 1988, Fleming and Spiegel had relocated to New York City, where they joined forces with Kramer (2) and David Licht to form B.A.L.L. (1987–1990, Shimmy Disc). After B.A.L.L. dissolved, Fleming and Spiegel briefly joined Dinosaur Jr (releasing “The Wagon” 45 on Sub Pop, 1990), then co-founded Gumball (2) (1990–1995), which signed to Columbia Records. The Velvet Monkeys’ final lineup — also featuring Thurston Moore, J Mascis, Julia Cafritz, and John Hammill — recorded Rake (Rough Trade, 1990), a blaxploitation-parody soundtrack. (Wikipedia)

As a producer, Fleming has worked with Sonic Youth, Hole (2), Teenage Fanclub, The Posies, Screaming Trees, Alice Cooper, Joan Jett, Nancy Sinatra, The Dictators, Andrew W.K., and many others. He has also served as executive director of the Association for Cultural Equity, working since 1995 to conserve and publish the Alan Lomax musical collection. (Wikipedia)
