Career

Tom Dixon dropped out of Chelsea School of Art to play bass in Funkapolitan, a post-punk funk band that:

  • Toured with The Clash (US tour, 1981), Rita and Ziggy Marley, Simple Minds
  • Appeared on Top of the Pops

By 1983, a series of motorcycle accidents ended his music career. During recovery he taught himself to weld and became fascinated with transforming scrap metal. This led to a career as one of Britain’s most celebrated industrial/furniture designers, known for the S-Chair and his Tom Dixon brand (founded 2002, based in Kings Cross, London). Made a CBE in 2025.

Connection to Dining Out Records

Played bass and piano on Occult Chemistry’s 1980 EP on Dining Out Records alongside Martha Tilson (vocals), Justin Langlands (drums), Selima Guirey (flute), and Gordon Twilight / Glenn Carmichael (whistling).
