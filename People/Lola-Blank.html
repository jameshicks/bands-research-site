Lola Blank

Discogs ID: 90563
Name Variations: L. Blank · Lola
Groups: Lola (group) · Blank Productions (ID 752944)

Vocalist, co-producer, and wife of Bob Blank. Documented by Discogs as a former vocalist for James Brown. From the early 1980s onward she co-produced sessions at Blank Tape Studios alongside Bob Blank, appearing across R&B, dance-pop, and house-adjacent productions for a range of NYC labels. She co-leads Lola (group) (Discogs ID 61264) — an NYC house/disco project active 1987–2020 releasing on Jump Street Records, Syncopate, and Vinylmania — and is a member of the Blank Productions unit alongside Bob Blank and their son Kenny Blank.

Overview

Lola Blank’s Discogs footprint spans 1982–2017 across fifteen distinct releases. She operates primarily in a dual vocalist/co-producer role. The Discogs profile note (“Vocalist for James Brown”) is the only biographical hook present in the database; no further detail about her James Brown tenure is recorded there. Her studio work runs in parallel with Bob Blank’s engineering career: every credit she holds from 1982 onward is either shared with Bob Blank or issued through their shared production umbrella.

Her compositional credit on the 2016 Wolfgang Voigt GAS rework (Embassy One) is notable — it is the only Discogs entry linking the Blank Productions world to the European electronic reissue circuit and indicates that earlier Lola-credited compositions were commercially exploitable into the streaming era.

Blank Productions

Blank Productions (Discogs artist ID 752944) is listed as a group entity with Bob Blank, Kenny Blank, and Lola Blank as members. It has no primary-credited releases of its own; it appears as a producer credit on the Debby Blackwell-Cook single “Changing Up” (1989, Sunshine Music LTD). This suggests it functions as a production umbrella for outside commissions rather than a standalone recording project.
