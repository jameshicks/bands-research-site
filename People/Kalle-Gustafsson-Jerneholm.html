Kalle Gustafsson Jerneholm

Swedish bassist, producer, mixer, and audio engineer from Gothenburg. Born April 27, 1970; full name Åke Karl “Kalle” Gustafsson Jerneholm. Discogs artist ID: 369631.

Owner of Svensk Grammofonsstudio (label ID: 301804) in Gothenburg. Best known as the bassist and co-founder of The Soundtrack Of Our Lives, but equally active as a recording engineer and mixer across the Scandinavian rock and indie scene from the mid-1990s onward.

Overview

Kalle occupies a dual position in Swedish music: he is a performer/band member at the centre of a major Swedish psychedelic rock group, and independently a go-to producer-engineer for artists across Sweden, Norway, Finland, Denmark, and the Netherlands. His studio work spans roughly 1995–2024, with the heaviest concentration in the 2000s and early 2010s. He operates mostly out of Gothenburg but has worked with Scandinavian artists across the region.

His production/mixing work consistently leans toward guitar-driven indie rock and singer-songwriter material. The most internationally prominent single credit outside The Soundtrack Of Our Lives is his mixing work on José González’s In Our Nature (2007), but his catalogue runs far wider — The Hives, The Datsuns, Fireside, Division of Laura Lee, Nicolai Dunger, and many others.

He appears on Discogs under a large number of name variations: Kalle Gustafsson, Kalle G, Kalle G:son, Gustafsson Jerneholm, K. Gustafsson Jerneholm, Karl Gustafsson, Kalle Gustavsson, and several others — all the same person.

Band Membership

The Soundtrack Of Our Lives (1994–)

Bass, double bass, harpsichord, backing vocals. One of the founding members. The band is on Telegram Records Stockholm and later Telegram’s licensing partners. Kalle appears on all major albums: Welcome To The Infant Freebase (1996), Extended Revelation For The Psychic Weaklings Of The Western Civilization (1998), Behind The Music (2001), Origin Vol. 1 (2004), A Present From The Past (2005), Communion (2008), Throw It To The Universe (2012).

Electric Eskimoes (Discogs ID: 1879779)

Member.

Ulf Dageby Band (Discogs ID: 7137488)

Bass player. Credited on the band’s 2019 self-titled LP (Gamlestans Grammofonbolag).

Studio Career

Active years

First credited production/engineering work: 1995 (The Talking Picture, bass/editing). Engineering and production credits begin in earnest around 1998–1999. Active continuously through at least 2024 (Sun Dog by Silver Dagger (2)).

Primary roles

  • Mixed By — his most frequent credit in commercial contexts
  • Producer — full production across many Swedish indie/rock albums
  • Recorded By / Engineer — tracking and studio operation
  • Co-producer — collaborative production on select projects

He occasionally plays instruments on records he produces: bass, percussion, accordion, harp, baritone guitar.

The José González / In Our Nature session (2007)

On In Our Nature Kalle was one of four credited mixers alongside Don Alsterberg, Mattias Nyberg, and José González himself. He mixed specific tracks rather than the whole album. The same team handled the associated singles on Peacefrog Records and Imperial Recordings (4). Backing vocals on the album came from Yukimi Nagano (Little Dragon); mastering by Hans Olsson.

Nicolai Dunger collaborations (1999)

On This Cloud Is Learning he co-produced with The Soundtrack Of Our Lives vocalist Ebbot Lundberg and played bass. Ian Person (TSOOL guitarist, Discogs ID: 429402), Fredrik Sandsten, and Martin Hederos also appear. Something In The Way (same year) was produced and mixed by Kalle with Virgin/Dolores distribution.

Division Of Laura Lee — Black City (2002)

Full producer/mixer credit. Co-produced with Don Alsterberg (who was co-producer and mixed some tracks). On Burning Heart Records.

Fireside — Get Shot (2003)

Multi-role credit: producer, mixer (partial), engineer, additional musician. Released on Startracks / V2 Records.

The Hives — Lex Hives (2012)

Engineer credit (alongside many others) on the band’s self-released album on Disques Hives. Mixing handled by Andrew Scheps, Dave Sardy, and Joe Zook.

Other Notes

  • On Foals’ Total Life Forever (2010) and the related single This Orient he appears as “Engineer [Assistant At S.G.S.]” — i.e. assistant at Svensk Grammofonsstudio, confirming the studio affiliation.
  • On the TSOOL Communion album (2008) his bass, recording, and mixing are listed as uncredited.
  • He photographed the cover of a 2019 release (Western Stars, Columbia Canada).
  • Transferred and restored audio for Live At CBGB New York 1988 (2018, Bengans).
  • Played additional musician / featured vocalist on a handful of releases beyond TSOOL.
