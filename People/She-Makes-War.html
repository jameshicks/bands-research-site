She Makes War

Solo project of Bristol-based multi-instrumentalist and producer Laura Kidd. Discogs artist ID: 2748203. Also records as Laura Kidd and Penfriend.

Overview

Laura Kidd writes, performs, and produces all of her own music as She Makes War. The project began as a fully self-released operation — Kidd plays nearly every instrument herself and co-produced the first two albums with Myles Clarke (also of Sparkadia and Kid Harpoon). The sound has been tagged “gloom-pop,” drawing on influences from Echo & The Bunnymen, Belly, The Breeders, PJ Harvey, Elastica (2), Suede, Bikini Kill, Nirvana, Radiohead, Cat Power, and Bon Iver.

From 2016 she signed to The state51 Conspiracy for a run of singles and the third album Brace For Impact (2018), returned to her own My Big Sister Recordings imprint for later work, and launched a subscription series (Supersub Club) in 2019. In parallel she runs the Breakfast With Apollo music nights (London and Bristol, from 2012), promoting better gig economics for DIY artists.

She also has an alias, Penfriend, under which she has released more recent material.

Key Personnel — Album Credits

Disarm (2010)

Co-produced with Myles Clarke. Kidd performs all instruments; Clarke contributes drum programming (tracks 1, 3, 4, 10, 13), synth, piano, and acoustic guitar. Additional: harp by Kat Arney; mastered by Dick Beetham.

Little Battles (2012)

Co-produced with Myles Clarke; Clarke also mixed and engineered. Kidd plays all instruments; Clarke adds drum programming, piano, and Morse code. Additional contributions: harp — Kat Arney; piano and vocals — Chris T-T; violin — Milly McGregor; vocals — Tasmin Archer, Anna Madeleine, Annie Gardiner, Dana Jade; lyric co-write on track 8 — Usuyak Bassey; mastered by Dick Beetham.

Direction Of Travel (2015)

Fully self-produced. No additional credited contributors in Discogs data.

Cold Shoulder (2016 single)

Produced by Laura Kidd. Kidd: bass, guitar, ukulele, keyboards, omnichord, vocals. Cello: Martin McCarrick; drums: Andy Sutor; engineer: TJ Allen; mixed by Dan Austin; mastered by Dick Beetham.

Brace For Impact (2018)

Kidd sole producer and engineer — credited for: producer, engineer, vocals, bass, guitar, synth, all arrangements, music and lyrics. Live drums: Dan Whitfield. Additional engineer/mix: Dan Austin. Mastered by Dick Beetham.

Production Role

Across the discography Kidd moves from co-producer (with Myles Clarke on the first two albums) to sole producer and engineer by Brace For Impact (2018). Discogs does not credit her as engineer or producer on any releases by other artists in the database — the brief’s note about state51 engineering credits does not appear in the DB data for other artists’ releases.

Touring Associates

Kidd has supported or toured with: Viv Albertine, Tricky, Suede, Babybird, The Levellers, tUnE-yArDs, Gruff Rhys, The Magic Numbers, Midge Ure, New Model Army, Glen Matlock, Nadine Shah, Chris Helme, and others.
