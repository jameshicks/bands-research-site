Tim Rutili

Musician, filmmaker, visual artist. Real name: Temistocles Hugo Rutili. Born 1967 or 1968. Discogs ID: 259374. Alias: Dexter Methoropham.

Founder of Red Red Meat and Califone. Full-member of Boxhead Ensemble (confirmed on Discogs; credited as “Band [Boxhead Ensemble is]” on The Unseen Hand, 2014). Also a member of Loftus and Ugly Casanova.

Contributed guitar, slide guitar, violin, and vocals to Ugly Casanova’s Sharpen Your Teeth (Sub Pop, 2002), appearing alongside Brian Deck. Co-wrote “Spilled Milk Factory” with Isaac Brock.

Film & Visual Art Work

Rutili studied at Columbia College Chicago’s film school before band life took over. He has maintained parallel careers in filmmaking and visual art throughout.

Feature Films (Director)

  • All My Friends Are Funeral Singers (2010) — wrote and directed; budget ~$30,000; stars Angela Bettis (now his wife) as a medium living with ghosts. Shot April–December 2009. Premiered Sundance Film Festival January 26, 2010; also screened at SXSW (with Califone performing live score), IFC Center NYC, and MCA Chicago. Distributed by Better Angel Films / IndiePix Films. (Wikipedia, as of 2026-04-25; Filmmaker Magazine interview)

Documentaries / Short Films (Subject or Director)

  • Made a Machine by Describing the Landscape (2011) — documentary directed by Solan Jensen and Joshua Marie Wilkinson; follows Califone on tour in Europe and the US c. 2004–2008. Rutili is main subject. Released by IndiePix 2011. (IMDB; Amazon)
  • Music videos directed for Veruca Salt, Iron & Wine, David Yow, Bill Fay, and Califone. (Wikipedia, as of 2026-04-25)

Film Scoring / Soundtrack Work

Boxhead Ensemble Involvement Timeline

Rutili is listed as a current member of Boxhead Ensemble on Wikipedia (as of 2026-04-25). His Discogs credit (The Unseen Hand, 2014) is his only explicit studio credit in the Discogs database, but his involvement began earlier:

  • Not present on Atavistic-era studio records (1997–1999): The credited lineups for Dutch Harbor (1997) and The Last Place to Go (1998) do not include Rutili. The Dutch Harbor lineup was: David Grubbs, Joseph Ferguson, Charles Kim, Michael Krassner, Douglas McCombs, Will Oldham, Jim O’Rourke, David Pavkovic, Rick Rizzo, Ken Vandermark. (Wikipedia)
  • Also not on Quartets (2003): Forced Exposure personnel listing: Jessica Billey, Michael Colligan, Ryan Hembrey, Glenn Kotche, Fred Lonberg-Holm, Scott Tuma — no Rutili. (Forced Exposure)
  • September 2001 European tour — confirmed in person. Rutili (guitar, organ) toured with Krassner, Curry, Lonberg-Holm, Tuma, Turner, and White on the “Stories, Maps and Notes from the Half-Light” tour across Netherlands, Belgium, France, UK, and Ireland. (Astria Suparak, 2001-09-19). Rutili later described it as “one of the best musical experiences i have ever had.” (Illabirinto interview, undated)
  • The Unseen Hand (2014) — confirmed studio credit; lineup: Bruce Licher, Eric Heywood, Michael Krassner, Rob Moore, Tim Rutili, Wil Hendricks.
  • Here: Chicago Sessions (2017) — confirmed member; lineup: Jim Becker, Shahzad Ismaily, Michael Krassner, Fred Lonberg-Holm, Tim Rutili, Jim White. Recorded at Electrical Audio, Chicago, c. 2008–2011. (Dusted, 2017; Wikipedia, as of 2026-04-25)
  • Ancient Music (2016 CDr / 2023 expanded CD) — confirmed participant. (AllMusic)
