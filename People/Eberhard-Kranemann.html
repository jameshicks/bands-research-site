Eberhard Kranemann

Born 1945 in Germany. Bassist, cellist, guitarist, vocalist, visual artist, and experimental musician. Key figure in the Düsseldorf avant-garde scene of the late 1960s and a founding participant in the earliest formations of what became Kraftwerk and NEU!. Performs and records under the alias Fritz Müller. Discogs artist IDs: 541898 (as Eberhard Kranemann), 730521 (as Fritz Müller).

Overview

Kranemann studied classical double bass at the Dortmund Conservatory before moving to Düsseldorf to study painting at the Kunstakademie. His practice has always fused sound and visual art. In 1967, with fellow art students, he co-founded Pissoff, an improv noise group that operated as deliberate acoustic chaos — performances ran three hours of free improvisation, avoiding harmony, pitch norms, and structure entirely.

Florian Schneider heard Pissoff and joined the group, beginning a collaboration with Kranemann. Through this circle Kranemann connected with Klaus Dinger and Michael Rother. He is listed as a member of both Kraftwerk (Discogs ID: 4654) and NEU! (Discogs ID: 12636) on Discogs, placing him in the generative moment — circa 1971 — when the two bands separated and crystallized from overlapping personnel.

Joseph Beuys, then a professor at the Düsseldorf Kunstakademie, was an enthusiast of Pissoff and organised a performance with the group at the Creamcheese Club — Düsseldorf’s central meeting point for the avant-garde. A 1968 recording of this event was eventually released in 2021 as Creamcheese Düsseldorf – 1968 (Rotorelief).

Kranemann later invented the stage identity Fritz Müller, under which he did studio work, concert tours, radio and television appearances, and formed the Fritz Müller Band. In 2017 he released Krautwerk in collaboration with Harald Grosskopf, an album that explicitly revisited the motorik/electronic language of the early Düsseldorf scene. He continues to work as a visual artist, musician, and performer.

Key Releases: Personnel Detail

Fritz Müller Rock (1977, Roth-Händle)

Kranemann’s debut album under the Fritz Müller alias. Engineered and co-produced by Conny Plank at his studio. Band lineup included:

  • Egon Stüwe — guitar, choir, music (A7, B1)
  • Dirk Fleck — bass, choir
  • Rüdiger Braune — drums, choir
  • Oliver Petry — piano, synthesizer, choir
  • Conny Plank — engineer, producer, lyrics (A1), music (A6)

Krautwerk (2017, Bureau B)

A full collaborative album with Harald Grosskopf (of Ashra, Wallenstein, The Cosmic Jokers). Both handled all composition, production, recording, editing, and mixing. Kranemann: all instruments, electronics, electric guitar, lap steel guitar, cello, vocals. Grosskopf: all instruments, electronics, electric drums, percussion. Mastered by Andreas Kolinski.

Freedom Of Sound (2023, Bellerophon Records)

Collaborative album with Jochen Oberlack. Produced and recorded by Rudi Kronenberger. Kranemann credited for voice, guitar, Hawaiian guitar, double bass, cello, drum machine, electronics, piano, and painting.

Creamcheese Düsseldorf – 1968 (2021, Rotorelief)

Archive performance document. Primary credit: Joseph Beuys + Pissoff. Kranemann credited for recording, composition, and sounds. Engineered by Andreas Lubich. Documents the Creamcheese Club scene that Beuys helped catalyse.
