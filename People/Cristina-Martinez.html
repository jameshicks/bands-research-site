Cristina Martinez

Cristina Martinez

Nov 08, 20263 min read

Cristina Martinez

Discogs ID: 299999
Origin: New York City (raised Washington, DC)

Vocalist and co-leader of Boss Hog alongside Jon Spencer. Credited as “Reduced By” on the Boss Hog LP (1995, Geffen) — also credited as performer. The two Boss Hog Geffen albums (Boss Hog 1995, Hunk 1996) were both engineered by Bil Emmons.

Other Groups

Also a member of Pussy Galore (2) (NY period, 1986–1990) — credited as guitar and vocals on Exile On Main St and later releases. Photography credit on Groovy Hate Fuck (1986). Also in The Honeymoon Killers (2) and Gibson Bros.

Notes (web) — Pre-Pussy Galore background

Upbringing and early influences

Martinez grew up in Washington, DC — she describes it as “a very rural part of WDC” with lots of trees and backyards. Her parents had immigrated from Spain and Cuba in the 1960s; she was raised in a Spanish-speaking Catholic household she describes as existing in “this weird half-light between an American and a very religious Catholic upbringing.” She graduated high school at 16. thequietus.com/interviews/boss-hog-interview/ (fetched 2026-11-08); scaruffi.com/interv/bosshog.html (fetched 2026-11-08)

Formative musical influences (self-reported): DC hardcore / Dischord shows at Fort Reno Park (including seeing Half Japanese); The Birthday Party’s Junkyard at age 14 as a turning point; then The Swans, Throbbing Gristle, The Residents, Tuxedo Moon, Funkadelic / Parliament, Sugar Hill and Trouble Funk, and the Butthole Surfers (“Cherub” specifically cited as the moment she wanted to join a band). scaruffi.com; thequietus.com

Education and work before Pussy Galore

After high school she briefly attended the University of Colorado at Boulder to study film — specifically because experimental filmmaker Stan Brakhage was teaching there. She left after one semester, returned to DC, and enrolled at The Corcoran School of the Arts and Design to study photography. She worked at Olsson’s Books & Records in DC, working as an import record buyer. thequietus.com; clevescene.com

How she entered the scene and met Jon Spencer

The pivotal event: 8 December 1985, Jesus and Mary Chain show at the 9:30 Club, Washington DC. Martinez attended to shoot live photography. The opening act was Peach of Immortality, a noise band fronted by her Olsson’s co-worker Tom Smith. That night, Jon Spencer and Julia Cafritz of Pussy Galore (2) were sitting in with Peach of Immortality as guest musicians. After the set, Martinez went backstage and spoke to Spencer about Scraping Foetus Off the Wheel’s Nail; she later described finding “some sort of kinship with the confusion and aggression” in him. Spencer and Martinez mark 8 December (the Feast of the Immaculate Conception) as their anniversary. pop-catastrophe.co.uk (fetched 2026-11-08); thequietus.com

Path into Pussy Galore

Spencer needed a photographer for the first Pussy Galore single; Martinez, who was studying photography at the Corcoran, took the photograph for the 7” (Groovy Hate Fuck, 1986). Spencer moved in with her approximately two weeks after the 9:30 Club meeting. She then dropped out of the Corcoran and took on duties as a third guitarist in Pussy Galore — she had no prior playing experience. clevescene.com; Wikipedia en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pussy_Galore_(band) (noted as already exhausted per brief)
