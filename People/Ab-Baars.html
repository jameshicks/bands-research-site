Overview

Ab Baars (born Abraham Baars, 1955) is a Dutch multi-instrumentalist and composer based in Amsterdam, central to the Dutch free improvisation scene since the 1980s. He plays tenor saxophone, soprano saxophone, clarinet, and shakuhachi (Japanese bamboo flute); the nohkan (a related Japanese flute) also appears in his later recording credits. He is a member of the ICP Orchestra (Instant Composers Pool Orchestra) alongside Han Bennink, Misha Mengelberg, Wolter Wierbos, Guus Janssen, and others, and has been one of its most consistent reeds players across decades of recordings.

Outside the ICP, Baars operates primarily through small-group and duo formats. His most documented ongoing partnership is with Terrie Ex, producing the duo albums Hef (2000, Terp IS-02) and Shifting Sands (2016, Terp IS-26). He co-leads the Ab Baars Trio (Discogs ID: 2134071) and Ab Baars Quartet (Discogs ID: 2134132), and the duo Duo Baars - Henneman (Discogs ID: 6412849) with violist Ig Henneman, documenting a long-running collaboration that has produced Stof (2007, Wig) and Trandans (2017, Wig), the latter with pianist Dave Burrell.

Baars is also a member of Double Tandem (with Ken Vandermark and Paal Nilssen-Love), Corkestra (the large free-improv ensemble including Andy Moor (2), Cor Fuhler, Xavier Charles, Tony Buck, and others), and a substantial roster of Dutch collaborative projects (Groep Ohm, Orkest De Volharding, Nieuw Artis Orchestra, Guus Janssen Septet, Maarten Altena Nonet, and more).

His name variations in Discogs credits include: A. Baars, Ab, Abraham Baars, Baars.
