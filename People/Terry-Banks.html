Overview

Terry Banks is a guitarist and vocalist from Washington, DC, active in indie pop and jangle pop circles from the early 1990s through the 2020s. He is best known as a core member of Dot Dash, sharing credits with Hunter Bennett, Danny Ingram, Dan Searing, and Geoff Sanoff across 18 releases. His earlier work includes The Saturday People and Glo-worm, and he also appears as a member of St. Christopher — a notable UK jangle pop act — suggesting transatlantic connections rare for a DC-based player. His catalogue peaks in the 1990s through Slumberland Records, K, and The Bus Stop Label, with steady output continuing into the 2020s via The Beautiful Music.
