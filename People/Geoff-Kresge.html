Overview

Geoff Kresge (real name Geoff Tilander) is a bassist whose career traces a path from early-90s Bay Area punk and hardcore — co-founding Loose Change and playing a formative role in AFI from 1992 to 1997 — through the psychobilly and rockabilly underground. He spent the bulk of the 2000s as the upright bassist for Tiger Army and as co-writer and guitarist for HorrorPops, then led Viva Hate through 2013. His 323 Discogs credits span Nitro Records and Hellcat Records in the punk era and Atom Age Industries in later years, with a final chapter as bassist for FEAR from 2018 to 2024.
