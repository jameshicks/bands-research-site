Scott Benedict

Drummer. Discogs artist ID: 650017.

Overview

Scott Benedict is a drummer based in the US, best known as a member of Pere Ubu in the mid-1990s to early 2000s. He is the primary drummer on Ray Gun Suitcase (1995), the album that introduced Michele Temple and Robert Wheeler as core long-term members of Pere Ubu. He also shared drum duties on that record with founding drummer Scott Krauss, who played on selected tracks (3, 6, 13, 15). Wikipedia confirms Benedict’s name in Pere Ubu’s past-members list.

Before Pere Ubu, Benedict drummed for The Vivians, a US band that also featured Michele Temple and was produced by Jim Jones (3) — giving him direct prior connections with two future Pere Ubu bandmates. His earliest Discogs credit is on Re-Entry (1987) by Wilbur Niles and Thrust, a funk/rock act from the same Cleveland-adjacent scene. His post-Ray Gun Suitcase Pere Ubu involvement is confirmed by the Drive, He Said 1994–2002 box set (Fire Records, 2017), which credits him as “Performer” across material spanning both the Ray Gun Suitcase and Pennsylvania eras.

The Vivians

The Vivians were a US band (Discogs artist ID: 771193) comprising:

MemberRole
Diane DuncanVocals
Michele TempleGuitar, Backing Vocals

They released two records on Hit & Run Records (2): Vivicide (1989) and I Fear (1991). Jim Jones (3) produced I Fear and appeared as a guest artist. The Vivians predate Benedict’s time in Pere Ubu, and the shared membership with Temple (and Jones’s production credit) shows how tightly woven this network was before both Temple and Benedict joined Ubu for Ray Gun Suitcase.
