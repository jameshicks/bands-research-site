Rivers Cuomo

American singer, guitarist, and songwriter. Born June 13, 1970, New York City. Discogs artist ID: 300542. Alias: Peter Kitts (1985–87).

Primary member and creative driver of Weezer (1992–present), where he writes the majority of the material, sings lead, and plays guitar. Moved to Los Angeles from Connecticut in 1989 with an earlier metal band; cofounded Weezer on February 15, 1992.

Overview

Cuomo studied at Harvard University (enrolling 1995, taking a break, graduating 2006 with a degree in English and entry to Phi Beta Kappa). Harvard influenced the confessional tone of Pinkerton (1996). He married Kyoko Ito in 2006.

His songwriting spans the full Weezer catalog plus solo releases and side projects. He also collaborated with T.I. on the T.I. single “Magic” (2010, Atlantic) and with Scott Murphy of Allister on the Scott & Rivers project (Japanese-language albums, 2013, 2017).
