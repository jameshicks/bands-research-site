Overview

Tony Bonner is a British engineer and producer whose recording work runs from 1983 onward, concentrated in the mid-to-late 1980s UK independent scene. His catalogue spans alternative rock, new wave, indie rock, punk, and country rock, with label ties centred on Rouska, Unamerican Activities, In Tape, and Abstract Records, and extending through Cooking Vinyl into the 2000s. His densest collaborations are with Jon Langford and The Three Johns (reflecting the same Leeds post-punk network), alongside Ian Caple, Brendan Croker, and The Cassandra Complex — a cluster that maps a specific stratum of UK independent recording in that era.
