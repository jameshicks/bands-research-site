Szende Gábor

Hungarian drummer and percussionist, also credited as Gabor Szende and G. Szende. Discogs artist ID: 1418198. Active from at least 1982 through 2007.

Overview

Szende Gábor is a Hungarian drummer and percussionist who worked across jazz fusion, pop, and world music contexts over at least two and a half decades. His career spans the Hungarian-German jazz corridor of the 1980s and a long thread of domestic Hungarian work thereafter.

His most internationally visible credits are as drummer for Tony Lakatos’ Bacillus (on the 1983 Aliso Records LP Sing-Sing Song, produced by Toto Blanke) and as a member of the broader Toto Blanke’s Electric Circus ensemble. The Electric Circus was a Frankfurt-based international fusion outfit led by German guitarist Toto Blanke; Szende entered the group’s lineup after original drummer Heinrich Hock, placing him alongside saxophonist Tony Lakatos, keyboardist Stu Goldberg, and percussionist Trilok Gurtu.

On the Hungarian side, Szende’s most significant creative affiliation is with the jazz fusion group Dimenzio (Dimenzió), where he is credited as drummer, percussionist, and co-composer on the 1987 Krém label LP Avec Plaisir. The group also included saxophonist Dés László (László Dés) and pianist Szacsky Róbert. His recurring appearances with Dés László — on Avec Plaisir, the 1994 compilation Running Time, the 1982 Supergroup (4) live LP, and the 2007 Udvaros Dorottya album Átutazó — make Dés László the single most consistent axis of his Discogs-documented career.

His 2000 credit on Face To Face (11)‘s Emberdal is unusual: he is credited on piano, not drums — suggesting multi-instrumental facility or a crossover into pop session work.

Nationality is not stated in Discogs, but is inferred as Hungarian from release country, label associations (Pepita, Krém, Hungaroton), and band memberships.

Tony Lakatos’ Bacillus — Sing-Sing Song (1983)

Personnel: Tony Lakatos (tenor/soprano sax, compositions), Szende Gábor (drums), László Gárdonyi (piano), Lattmann Béla / Pál Vasvári (bass). Produced by Toto Blanke and Heiner Beringmeier. Released on Aliso Records (Germany).

Dimenzio — Avec Plaisir (1987)

Personnel: Dés László (soprano/tenor sax, producer), Szacsky Róbert (piano, synth), Tamás Tóth (bass guitar), Szende Gábor (drums, percussion). Szende co-wrote track A2. Released on Krém (Hungary).

Supergroup (4) — Supergroup Live (1982)

Pepita LP recorded live in Hungary. Personnel includes Dés László (music director, sax), Tátrai Tibor (guitar), János Karácsony (guitar), Lerch István and Másik János (keyboards), Tamás Tóth (bass), János Solti and Szende Gábor (percussion).

Udvaros Dorottya — Átutazó (2007)

Hungaroton album. Music direction by Dés László; Szende on drums for most tracks alongside Tamás Tóth (bass), Béla Szakcsi Lakatos (piano), Horváth Kornél (electronic drums/percussion). Hungarian pop/jazz crossover.
