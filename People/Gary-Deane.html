Gary Deane

Gary Deane

Jun 19, 20268 min read

Gary Deane

Real name: Gary Charles Ahrens Born: February 9, 1957, Buffalo, New York Died: January 22, 2024 Discogs ID: 306363 Also billed as: G. Ahrens, Gary Ahrens, Gary Deane Ahrens, Gary Dean, G. Deane Active: c. 2004–2022

DJ, producer, mastering engineer, songwriter, and label operator. Resident in Baltimore, Maryland, where he co-ran Code Red Recordings with DJ Spen from approximately 2004 to 2011. Member of The MuthaFunkaz — the Baltimore house production collective that also included Gary Hudgins (keyboards) and Irvin Madden (bass, engineer) — and the associated combined project DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz. His primary roles across the Discogs credit record are executive producer, mastering engineer, and songwriter; he is credited as co-producer on Gabryelle (Defected, 2005), the group’s most prominent international release.

Biography

Background

Gary Deane (Gary Charles Ahrens) was a DJ and producer originally from Buffalo, New York who relocated to Baltimore, Maryland. The Discogs profile notes he was Baltimore-resident during his years running Code Red Recordings. No earlier career credits appear in the Discogs database — his full documented activity begins around 2004 with the launch of Code Red and The MuthaFunkaz as active entities.

Code Red Recordings and The MuthaFunkaz (2004–2011)

From 2004 Deane co-ran Code Red Recordings (Discogs ID 27684) alongside DJ Spen. The label was the primary platform for DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz — the combined unit of DJ Spen, Deane, Gary Hudgins, and Irvin Madden — as well as for DJ Spen & Thommy Davis and various vocalist-led projects.

Within this setup Deane’s credit pattern across Code Red releases was consistent:

  • Executive Producer — heading up sessions and overseeing releases
  • Mastered By — handling mastering (sometimes credited as “pre-mastering”) on Code Red output and on releases from adjacent labels (Next Moov Records, BPM King Street Sounds, Hustle Music)
  • Written-By — co-writing credits across Code Red and associated Spiritually Sound Records releases
  • Design, Layout — Deane also took design credits on some Code Red releases (e.g. Nightrain, 2005, Code Red Recordings CR12-510)

The earliest single credited across Deane’s full credits is Gabryelle (Code Red Recordings, 2004, US pressing) and Rock The Bellz (Code Red, 2004). The flagship 2004 release was The Best I Can (Code Red CR12-001) — 13 pressings, the label’s most distributed release.

Key songwriting credits under Deane’s authorship in this period:

  • It’s All Jesus (Spiritually Sound Records, 2004) — DJ Spen Proudly Presents Byron Stingily; Deane credited as “G. Ahrens” for written-by alongside Gary Hudgins, Irvin Madden, Byron Stingily, and Sean Spencer; the full MuthaFunkaz production team is named (DJ Spen, Gary Hudgins, Irvin Madden as producers and arrangers)
  • Holy Ghost (Code Red, 2005) — DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz; Deane listed as executive producer, mastered by, and written-by; Kelly Tribble Spencer appears on vocals
  • 4 The Love (The Collective Minds Theme) (Code Red, 2005) — executive producer, mastered by, written-by
  • Nightrain (Code Red, 2005) — DJ Spen & Thommy Davis; design, layout, executive producer, mastered by, written-by
  • Right Away (Spiritually Sound Records, 2005) — executive producer, mastered by, written-by
  • Don’t Give Up (Code Red, 2006) — co-producer, executive producer, mastered by, written-by
  • Love Is (Love Breeze) (Code Red, 2006) — executive producer, written-by
  • Shine (Code Red, 2006) — mastered by, written-by
  • It’s So Easy (Code Red, 2007) — DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz feat. Ann Nesby; written-by as “G. Ahrens”

Defected Releases (2005–2008)

The DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz axis also released internationally on Defected (UK). Deane’s Defected credits center on co-production and songwriting:

  • Gabryelle (Defected, 2005) — presented as “DJ Spen Presents DJ Technic”; Deane, DJ Spen, and Gary Hudgins (as “Gary Hudge”) are all co-producers; DJ Technic (Glenn Brand) is listed as the primary producer and mixer. This was the group’s most widely distributed release.
  • Church Lady (Defected, 2007) — mastered by
  • Given Me Joy (Defected, 2008) — DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz Present Marc Evans; Deane listed as written-by (“Gary Ahrens”)
  • Reach Out For Love (Defected, 2008) — DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz Present Marc Evans; written-by; The MuthaFunkaz collectively credited as producer and arranged by

The 2008 Defected album The Way You Love Me (Discogs ID 1476886) is the major group statement — DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz credited as primary artist on the Defected pressing, with Deane’s involvement through his songwriter and executive producer credits on underlying tracks.

Deane also mastered several Defected-adjacent releases for other labels during this period: Esperanza (Defected, 2005), In The Sun (Defected, 2006), The Way You Love Me (Hustle Music, 2005).

Later Work (2009–2022)

After the Code Red era Deane’s credit footprint reduced. He is not among the primary listed members of later DJ Spen productions on Quantize Recordings, though his songwriter credit reappears on:

  • The Supernatural Communication EP (Quantize Recordings, 2013) — Marc Evans; The MuthaFunkaz credited as producer on tracks 2–3–4–5–6; Deane listed as written-by (“Gary Charles Ahrens”) on those tracks alongside Hudgins and Madden; the EP also features Joey Negro co-production and Sheila Ford vocals
  • Until You (Quantize Recordings, 2022) — N’Dinga Gaba & DJ Spen featuring Marc Evans; DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz listed as producer; Deane listed as written-by (“Gary Ahrens”); final documented credit in the database before his death in January 2024

He also held a mastering credit on The L Word (Crosstown Rebels, 2011) — written-by credit for lyrics — and on Changed My Life (BPM King Street Sounds, 2009).

Gary Deane died on January 22, 2024.
