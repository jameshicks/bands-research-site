Glenn Branca

Composer, guitarist. Born 6 October 1948, Harrisburg, Pennsylvania. Died 13 May 2018, New York City.

Discogs artist ID: 55972.

Key Projects

  • Theoretical Girls — early no-wave group, 1977–c.1979; self-released single on Theoretical Records (1978). Members: Jeffrey Lohn, Margaret DeWys, Wharton Tiers.
  • The Static — next band after Theoretical Girls (1979), with Barbara Ess and Christine Hahn (No Wave); released on Theoretical Records and Audio Arts.
  • Glenn Branca Ensemble — guitar symphony ensemble; Thurston Moore and Lee Ranaldo both performed in it.
  • 99 Records — first release: “Lesson No. 1” (99-01, 1980); also The Ascension (99-02, 1981). He pushed Ed Bahlman to start the label.
  • Neutral Records — co-founded with Josh Baer (White Columns) c.1982 after falling out with Bahlman over the Sonic Youth signing refusal. Published Symphony No. 3 (Gloria) (NEUTRAL 4, 1983) on the label.

Key Releases / Compositions

  • “Lesson No. 1” (99-01, 99 Records, 1980)
  • The Ascension (99-02, 99 Records, 1981)
  • Symphony No. 1 (Tonal Plexus) (ROIR, 1983)
  • Symphony No. 3 (Gloria) (NEUTRAL 4 / Les Disques Du Crépuscule, 1983)
  • Symphony No. 6 (Devil Choirs At The Gates Of Heaven) (Blast First, 1989)
  • Symphony No. 13 (Hallucination City) For 100 Guitars (Atavistic, 2016)

Key Facts

  • Instrumental in the No Wave scene and in launching both 99 Records and Neutral Records.
  • Sonic Youth emerged from his guitar ensemble; Thurston Moore and Lee Ranaldo both played in Branca’s ensembles.
  • Produced Native Dance (#3, Neutral) by Jules Baptiste / Red Decade as executive producer alongside Josh Baer.
  • Produced Tidal (N5, Neutral) as executive producer.
  • Also released on Les Disques Du Crépuscule (Belgium): Symphony No. 3 and The World Upside Down (ballet).
  • Last major work: Symphony No. 13 (2016); died 2018 of throat cancer.

Web Sources Notes

  • Died of throat cancer, May 13, 2018, age 69. (NPR obituary)
  • Married Reg Bloor; she joined the ensemble c.1999–2000 and served as concertmaster. (Wikipedia, Glenn Branca, as of 2026-05-22; Please Kill Me interview)
  • Symphony No. 3 on Neutral Records (NEUTRAL 4) was the original US pressing; Les Disques Du Crépuscule (TWI 151) issued a simultaneous Belgian pressing in August 1983 — not a license, two co-equal pressings of the same recording.
  • Symphony No. 4 (Physics) was never commercially released as audio during his lifetime. A VHS docu-collage (dir. Arleen Schloss, 1984) is the only existing document. A Kickstarter by Reg Bloor for an audio release on Systems Neutralizers was funded in January 2026. (Kickstarter)
  • Symphony No. 7 (1989 performance, Graz Festival Orchestra) was released on CD in 2010 on Systems Neutralizers (SN03CD). (Discogs 2557060)
  • Symphonies No. 11 and No. 12 were never commercially released. No. 11 is for traditional orchestra. No. 12 (Tonal Sexus) was performed but unissued; Branca mentioned in 2016 Red Bull might record it. (Red Bull Music Academy Daily 2016)
  • Thurston Moore and Lee Ranaldo both left the ensemble in 1983, during/after the European Symphony No. 4 tour; a dispute over Neutral Records royalties contributed to the split. Both appear on Symphonies 1, 2, 3 — not on any release after 1983. (nirvana-legacy.com)
  • Composed 16 symphonies total (plus additional non-symphony works). (Systems Neutralizers bio)
