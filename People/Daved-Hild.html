Daved Hild

American drummer and multi-instrumentalist. Discogs artist ID: 775894.

Member of David Thomas And The Wooden Birds (drums on April U Beogradu cassette and Monster Walks The Winter Lake); also The Pale Orchestra, The Girls (7), and Farmers (3). Credited as accordion player on “Monster Walks The Winter Lake” (the title track of the 1986 LP) and as co-writer of that track alongside David Thomas and Garo Yellin.
