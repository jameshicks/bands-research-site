Aug 21, 20264 min read

Thom Yorke

Discogs ID: 4854 Real name: Thomas Edward Yorke Born: 7 October 1968, Wellingborough, Northamptonshire, UK Aliases (recording names): Dr Tchock, Sisi Bakbak, The White Chocolate Farm, Zachariah Wildwood

English singer, multi-instrumentalist, and songwriter. Lead vocalist and primary lyricist of Radiohead (1985–present). Also core to Atoms For Peace (2) (2009–2014) and The Smile (2021–present). Extensive solo catalog alongside frequent film-score work. Closest long-term collaborator: Nigel Godrich (producer and engineer across virtually the entire solo and group output).

Pre-Radiohead

Formed On A Friday at Abingdon School in 1985 with Colin Greenwood, Jonny Greenwood, Ed O’Brien, and Phil Selway. Renamed Radiohead in 1991 at the suggestion of their management. First single Creep (1992).
