Michael Gira

Born Michael Rolfe Gira. Vocalist, guitarist, songwriter, label founder. New York City. Active from c.1981–present. Discogs artist ID: 79651.

Founder and constant leader of Swans (1982–1997, 2010–present). Also founded and ran Angels of Light (1999–2010) and Young God Records (c.1990–present). Previously in Circus Mort and the Glenn Branca Ensemble.

Overview

Gira’s sound in Swans evolved from brutal noise-minimalism through orchestral post-rock to an expansive, hymn-like style in the reformed era. As label head of Young God Records, he has signed and championed Devendra Banhart, Akron-Family, and others alongside his own releases.

Skin (2) / The World Of Skin

Side project with Jarboe, Clinton Steele, and Steve McAllister. See Skin (2). Active c.1987–1990. Four releases on Product Inc. (a Mute sublabel) and Ten Songs For Another World (1990) on Young God Records. Gira produced all releases and co-wrote lyrics throughout; the sessions directly preceded and influenced Children Of God.
