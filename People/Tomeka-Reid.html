Tomeka Reid

American cellist, composer, and educator. Born 1977, Washington D.C.; based in Chicago, Illinois. Discogs artist ID: 839131. Name variations on record: “Reid”, “T. Reid”, “Tomeka Reed”.

Biography

Early Career and Chicago Arrival (pre-2010)

Reid’s earliest Discogs credit places her in 2002 as cellist on Dreamtime Records’ Afrika Rising compilation. By 2005 she appeared as a featured guest on HiM’s Peoples (Bubble Core Records), playing cello on tracks 4, 8, and 10 — a session organized by HiM’s core of Doug Scharin (drums, percussion), Griffin Rodriguez (bass, vocals, synth), and Adam Pierce (guitar, vibraphone). The Peoples session also included Martin Perna, Julie Liu, and Jordan McLean.

During this period Reid also contributed cello to releases on Thrill Jockey (Kings & Queens, 2006), Anti- (Golden Pollen, 2007), Lujo Records (The Simp, 2007), and Polyvinyl Record Company (Ghost Town, 2011). These indicate steady work as a string collaborator across indie, post-rock, and experimental contexts before her jazz work fully crystallized.

Quartet and AACM Orbit (2010–2016)

Reid became embedded in Chicago’s AACM-adjacent avant-jazz world through membership in Nicole Mitchell’s ensembles — Nicole Mitchell’s Black Earth Ensemble and Nicole Mitchell’s Black Earth Strings (alongside Joshua Abrams and Renée Baker). She also joined Mike Reed’s Loose Assembly with Mike Reed (2), Jason Adasiewicz, Joshua Abrams, and Greg Ward (2).

Her debut as a leader under the Tomeka Reid Quartet name came with the self-titled Tomeka Reid Quartet (2015, Thirsty Ear). The quartet — Mary Halvorson (guitar), Jason Roebke (double bass), Tomas Fujiwara (drums) — recorded nine original Reid compositions plus a cover of Eric Dolphy’s “Hat and Beard”; Mike Reed (2) produced. The same year she appeared on Anthony Braxton’s Trillium E (2011, New Braxton House) as cellist, and became a member of the Roscoe Mitchell Quartet (alongside Roscoe Mitchell, George Lewis, and others from the AACM lineage).

She was also a founding member of the all-cello trio Hear In Now with Mazz Swift and Silvia Bolognesi, and contributed to Living By Lanterns — Mike Reed’s large-ensemble project (New Myth / Old Science, 2012, Cuneiform Records), which united Taylor Ho Bynum, Mary Halvorson, Tomas Fujiwara, Ingrid Laubrock, Jason Adasiewicz, and Joshua Abrams.

International Anthem and Wider Recognition (2017–2020)

Reid’s profile rose significantly with a run of releases on International Anthem Recording Company. She appeared on Jaimie Branch’s landmark Fly or Die (2017), a session that also included Jason Ajemian, Chad Taylor, Ben Lamar Gay, and Matt Schneider. The following year she was on Makaya McCraven’s double-album Universal Beings (2018, International Anthem), contributing cello to the Chicago sides (tracks A and B), recorded alongside Dezron Douglas, Junius Paul, Brandee Younger, Shabaka Hutchings, and others from the international new jazz scene.

Also in this period: she contributed cello to the Rob Mazurek / Exploding Star Orchestra album Dimensional Stardust (2020, International Anthem / Nonesuch), playing beside Nicole Mitchell, Jaimie Branch, Jeff Parker, Chad Taylor, Damon Locks, and Joel Ross (3).

She was co-primary artist with Alexander Hawkins on Shards And Constellations (2020, Intakt Records) — a piano-and-cello duo she produced alongside Hawkins. Liner notes were provided by Anthony Davis (2).

She also appeared on Junius Paul’s Ism (2019, International Anthem), a session produced and edited by Makaya McCraven.

Prolific Leadership and Collaboration (2021–present)

Reid became a primary credited artist with increased frequency. The trio Artifacts — with Nicole Mitchell (flute, electronics) and Mike Reed (2) (drums) — released …And Then There’s This (2021, Astral Spirits). The quartet release Geometry Of Trees (2022, Relative Pitch Records) was co-led with Kyoko Kitamura, Taylor Ho Bynum, and Joe Morris.

She co-led Let Our Rejoicing Rise (2022, Corbett vs. Dempsey) with veteran saxophonist Joe McPhee, produced by John Corbett and recorded/mixed by Alex Inglizian.

Reid collaborated with Ken Vandermark in The Margots, a band that also included Tim Daisy, Joe McPhee, and Nick Macri, releasing Pescado (2013, Okka Disk).

In 2025, The Hemphill Stringtet Plays Music Of Julius Hemphill (Out Of Your Head Records) was released, a project dedicated to the AACM composer Julius Hemphill. A 2026 Out Of Your Head Records release (Dance! Skip! Hop!) credits her as composer and producer.
