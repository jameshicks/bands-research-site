Mark Gane

Canadian guitarist, keyboardist, and songwriter. Discogs ID: 224716. Co-founder of Martha And The Muffins.

Overview

Co-founder of Martha And The Muffins alongside Martha Johnson; the duo contracted the band to just the two of them in 1983 (operating as M+M, Discogs ID: 79790). Plays guitar, Synthi A synthesizer, and keyboards across the catalog.

Primary songwriter: on Metro Music (1980), his writing credits cover A1, A5, and all of Side B (B1–B5). “Echo Beach” — the band’s breakthrough single — is written solely by Gane. He continued as principal co-composer with Johnson through the M+M era.

Spent the 1990s writing music for television and film. Designed the cover for Danseparc (1983). Photography credited on Mystery Walk (1984) and Marthology (2021).

Name variations on Discogs: M Gane, M. Gane, Marc Gane, Mark Thomas Gane.
