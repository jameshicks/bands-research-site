Overview

Sophie Sleigh-Johnson is a writer, local journalist, and artist based in Southend-on-Sea, UK, born in 1988, who holds a PhD from Goldsmiths College where she serves as an Associate Lecturer in Fine Art Critical Studies. On record she appears as a keyboardist, synthesizer player, and vocalist, with membership in These New Puritans anchoring her catalogue — nine shared credits with both Jack Barnett and George Barnett mark her as a central presence in that project. Her recorded work clusters in two distinct phases: five releases in the 2000s on Angular Recording Corporation and Domino, and a return in the 2020s through smaller experimental outlets including Crow Versus Crow, Infant Tree, and scatterArchive.
