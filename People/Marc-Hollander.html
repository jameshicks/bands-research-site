Marc Hollander

Discogs ID: 69857
Role: Musician, label founder, producer
Known for: Aksak Maboul, Crammed Discs (founder)
Aliases: Al Akefak, The Arc (11)
Name variations: M. Hollander, MH, Mark Hollander
Active: 1976–present

Belgian musician and label operator. Co-founder (with Vincent Kenis) and leader of Aksak Maboul. Founded Crammed Discs (Brussels, 1981) and has run it continuously since. Also co-founded Cramboy sublabel specifically for Tuxedomoon’s catalog.

Groups and Ensembles

  • Aksak Maboul (ID 143563) — co-founder with Kenis
  • The Honeymoon Killers / Les Tueurs De La Lune De Miel (ID 186991) — Brussels group
  • By Chance (ID 314544) — early Belgian group
  • Cos (3) (ID 612102)
  • Los Descontrolados (ID 86812)
  • Mr. Big Mouse (ID 85717)

Production and Performance at Crammed

Hollander played on early Crammed releases as performer (bass clarinet, saxophone, organ, percussion) and served as producer or co-producer on dozens of records. Key credits:

  • Bass clarinet and saxophone on Minimal Compact’s debut (1981) — also producer
  • Producer on Zazou Bikaye’s Mr. Manager (1985) and Guilty! (1988)
  • Keyboards and producer: Douzième Journée with Benjamin Lew / Steven Brown (1982)
  • Woodwind on Bel Canto’s White-Out Conditions (1987)
  • Producer on Bel Canto’s Birds Of Passage (1990)
  • A&R on Bebel Gilberto’s Tanto Tempo (Ziriguiboom, 2000)
  • Compiled and sequenced the Freezone series (6 volumes, 1994–2001)
  • Performer on Aksak Maboul’s Ex-Futur Album (2014) and Figures (2020) — resumed band activity
