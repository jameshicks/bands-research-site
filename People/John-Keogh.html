Overview

John Keogh was a British musician active from 1980 until his death in 1994, working across keyboards, production, and performance. He was a member of Max (31) and Wide Boy Awake, and his catalogue spans leftfield, downtempo, dub, trip hop, and abstract styles across releases on Sire (1980), Virgin, and Red Dot Records (1992). His collaborator network overlaps heavily with the post-Adam and the Ants circle — Kevin Mooney and Matthew Ashman each share 12 credits with him, alongside Helen Terry and John Reynolds (10 each). He also accumulated 10 shared credits with Leslie Winer, placing him at the edge of early 1990s trip-hop and experimental UK underground scenes.
