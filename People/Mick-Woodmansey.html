Overview

Mick Woodmansey (born 4 February 1950, Driffield, Yorkshire, England) is the drummer best known as the rhythmic engine of the Spiders From Mars, David Bowie’s glam-rock backing unit of the early 1970s. He appears throughout the central Bowie catalogue of that era alongside guitarist Mick Ronson and bassist Trevor Bolder, with producer Ken Scott and engineer Tony Visconti shaping much of that sound. Beyond the Spiders he featured in the Arnold Corns and Ronno projects, and later in the Screen Idols and the Cybernauts.
