Scott Friedlander

Discogs ID: 1718217 Role: Sound engineer, photographer, graphic designer Active (per credits): 1994–2025 Associated acts: Andy Haas, Ask The Oracle, GOTHgsb

Overview

Scott Friedlander is a New York-based sound engineer and photographer whose Discogs profile spans over 100 credits from 1994 to 2025. His work sits entirely in the avant-garde jazz and experimental music world. He is a recurring technical and visual collaborator on releases by Andy Haas, appearing on six Andy Haas releases (all on Resonant Music (2) or its variant imprint Resonantmusic), primarily handling cover art and design.

His work divides into two streams:

  1. Sound engineering / recording: Live and studio recording for jazz and improvised music releases on labels including Ictus Records, Infrequent Seams, Slam Productions, and others.
  2. Photography and graphic design: His most extensive credit type. He has supplied band and artist photography for dozens of releases on labels including Clean Feed, Tzadik, Cuneiform Records, Intakt Records, Enja Records, and Whirlwind Recordings.

His Discogs artist profile describes him as “Sound engineer. and photographer.” He has no primary artist credits (no releases where he is the headline act), and the find_collaborators tool returns no results for him as a graph seed — consistent with someone who works entirely in support roles.
