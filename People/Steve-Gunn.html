Overview

Steve Gunn is an American guitarist, singer, and songwriter from Lansdowne, Pennsylvania, working across psychedelic rock, folk, and experimental acoustic territory. Active since 2005, he built his early catalogue through Three Lobed Recordings and smaller imprints before signing to Matador in the mid-2010s for his most widely distributed solo work; Three Lobed has remained an outlet alongside it through 2025. His most consistent collaborative relationship is with drummer John Truscinski, with whom he records as the Gunn-Truscinski Duo, and he has a broad network of recurring partners including Mary Lattimore, Nathan Bowles, James Elkington, and Meg Baird. He has also participated in several group projects including GHQ and Black Dirt Oak.
