Credit Details

The Discogs credit is classified under Written-By, with the specific qualifier Postscript By. This means Silk contributed text — a postscript, i.e. an afterword or appendix — that is treated as a writing credit rather than a performance credit. The nature of the contribution (spoken word on the recording, printed text in the liner notes, or both) cannot be determined from Discogs data alone.

“Footprints In Snow” is the only track on D. I. Go Pop that carries any individual writing credit beyond the blanket “All Tracks By Disco Inferno” attribution. The album otherwise credits all compositions to Disco Inferno collectively. The “Postscript By” therefore marks something structurally different: an external text or voice appended to the composition, not part of its core authorship.

Context: “Footprints In Snow”

The track is the closing piece on D. I. Go Pop and the longest on the album at 5:29. The album’s sound is built around Ian Crause’s technique of routing guitar through a Roland S-750 sampler, with environmental recordings (water, wind, crashes, voices) rather than conventional instruments. The addition of a named external “postscript” to the album’s final track is consistent with a literary or reflective gesture — a closing text appended after the music.

The album was produced by Charlie McIntosh, with design by Fuel (9) and photography by David Spero (2).
