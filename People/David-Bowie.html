David Bowie

Born David Robert Jones, 8 January 1947, Brixton, London. Died 10 January 2016. British rock musician, actor, and visual artist. Discogs artist ID: 10263.

Overview

One of the central figures in rock’s art-crossover. Multiple persona phases (Ziggy Stardust, Aladdin Sane, The Thin White Duke). The Berlin Trilogy — Low (1977), “Heroes” (1977), Lodger (1979) — recorded at Hansa Studios, Berlin, with producer Tony Visconti and collaborator Brian Eno — represent his most experimental period and remain canonical art-rock texts.
