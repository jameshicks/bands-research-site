Salvatore Principato

Vocalist and percussionist. Core member of Liquid Liquid. Discogs ID: 331352. Also credited as Sal P, Sal Principato.

Overview

Lead vocalist for Liquid Liquid across all four original 99 Records EPs (1981–1985). His voice was central to the band’s identity: oblique phrasing, chant-like repetition, and a dry, percussive delivery that sat inside the rhythm rather than above it.

After Liquid Liquid dissolved in 1984, Principato continued making music across a range of collaborations and solo projects. His outside credits span Detroit techno (Ectomorph on Interdimensional Transmissions), hip-hop adjacent funk (Rong Music), and deeper house (Compost Records). He also contributed liner notes to the definitive Arthur Russell retrospective (2011), placing him in a network of figures who bridged downtown NYC post-punk with what became dance music.

Groups: Bison (4), Fist Of Facts, Visitors (14), The Idiot Orchestra.

On Big City (The Idiot Orchestra, 1980), Principato is credited on violin — an unusual instrument credit compared to his later role as vocalist and percussionist.

The Arthur Russell Connection

The 2011 24→24 Music retrospective is notable for the company it places Principato in. The liner notes include testimonials from Bob Blank, DJ Spinna, Freddy Fresh, Jill Kroesen, and Joey Negro — a cross-section of disco, house, and downtown NYC left-field pop figures who all circled Arthur Russell’s world. The release also features Peter Gordon (tenor sax), Julius Eastman, and Peter Zummo on the recordings themselves. Russell’s “Corn” (from the original 24→24 Music sessions) used the Liquid Liquid rhythm axis as a reference point; that Principato appears here is an acknowledgment of that kinship.
