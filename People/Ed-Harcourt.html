Overview

Ed Harcourt (born Edward Henry Richard Harcourt-Smith, 14 August 1977, Wimbledon, London) is an English singer-songwriter whose debut album Here Be Monsters earned a Mercury Prize nomination in 2001. His catalogue sits across indie rock, soft rock, and acoustic styles, with the bulk of his output concentrated in the 2000s and a long primary association with Heavenly Recordings (2000–2020). Beyond his solo work he has been a prolific collaborator as songwriter, producer, and pianist — most extensively with Sophie Ellis-Bextor (35 shared credits) and Jari Haapalainen — and holds band memberships in Loup Garoux and Snug.
