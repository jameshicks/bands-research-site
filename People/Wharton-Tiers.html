Wharton Tiers

Engineer, producer, drummer. New York City. Discogs artist ID: 234510.

Overview

Wharton Tiers emerged from the late-1970s No Wave scene as drummer in Theoretical Girls (1977–c.1979), then became one of the central producer-engineers of the NYC underground through the 1980s and 1990s, operating Fun City Studios. Also a member of Glenn Branca Ensemble, A Band (2), Glorious Strangers, New Detroit Inc., The Giant Mums, and The Wharton Tiers Ensemble.

Key Roles

  • Drummer — Theoretical Girls (1977–c.1979)
  • Producer/Engineer — Sonic Youth Confusion Is Sex (Neutral Records, 1983) and Kill Yr. Idols (Zensor, 1983); their first major label records
  • Operated Fun City Studios, NYC — de facto home for NYC underground recording, 1980s–1990s
