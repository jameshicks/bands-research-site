John Bergin

Composer, producer, designer, photographer, comics creator. Discogs artist ID: 266200. Runs Stompbox13 (stompbox13.com). Alias: C17H19NO3 (Discogs ID: 73098). Name variation on records: Bergin.

Overview

Multi-disciplinary artist based in Illinois. Co-founder of Blackmouth with Jarboe and Brett Smith. Bergin handles composing, mixing, design, photography, and mastering across his projects. Ran Grinder Books, a comics/publishing imprint connected to Grinder Books And Recordings and Grinder Tool & Die labels. Shares three band memberships with Brett Smith: Blackmouth, Tertium Non Data, Trust Obey, Western Devils.
