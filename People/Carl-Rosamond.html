Overview

Carl Rosamond is a Leeds-based producer and engineer who has worked out of The Billiard Room in Leeds since 1984, accumulating 185 credits across indie rock, punk, post-punk, and alternative rock. His catalogue spans four decades of UK independent music, with his most sustained relationships being with Cyanide Pills (36 shared releases) and The Wedding Present’s core members — David Gedge, Peter Solowka, Keith Gregory, Shaun Charman, and Jamie Lockhart — across 19–28 releases each. His label ties are strongest with Damaged Goods (11 releases, 2009–2023) and the earlier Reception imprint (5 releases, 1985–1992).
