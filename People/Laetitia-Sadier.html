Overview

Lætitia Sadier (born 6 May 1968) is a French musician primarily known as co-founder and vocalist of the London-based avant-pop band Stereolab, which she formed with Tim Gane after meeting him while singing backing vocals for McCarthy. She also co-founded the Duophonic label. While Stereolab was active she launched the side project Monade in 1996, then transitioned to solo and ensemble work in 2009 under her own name via the Laetitia Sadier Source Ensemble; she also performs in Little Tornados. Her catalogue is weighted toward the 2010s and 2020s, spanning Indie Rock, Krautrock, Post Rock, Space Rock, and Experimental styles, with long-running label ties to Duophonic Ultra High Frequency Disks and Drag City and close musical relationships with Gane, Andy Ramsay, Sean O’Hagan, Mary Hansen, and John McEntire.
