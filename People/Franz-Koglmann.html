Overview

Franz Koglmann is an Austrian jazz composer and flugelhorn/trumpet player, born 22 May 1947 in Mödling near Vienna. His work is rooted in avant-garde jazz and third stream, drawing on both improvisation and through-composed structures. He has collaborated extensively with European and American improvisers including Steve Lacy, Bill Dixon, Lee Konitz, Paul Bley, Tony Coe, Misha Mengelberg, and Burkhard Stangl. Koglmann released his first records on his own Pipe Records label in the early-to-mid 1970s before moving to the Swiss hat ART / Hat Hut Records family, where the bulk of his 1980s–90s catalog appeared. From the late 1990s onward he worked mainly with the German label Between The Lines and the Italian Ictus Records. In 2003 he received the Hans Koller Prize (highest Austrian jazz award), category Album of the Year. (Source: Wikipedia)

He has also served as artistic director / creative director for other artists’ releases on Between The Lines, including Enrico Rava’s Duo En Noir (1999), Gebhard Ullmann’s Essencia (2001), and Michel Wintsch’s Road Movie (1998).
