Kristín Anna Valtýsdóttir

Discogs ID: 1035284
Born: 5 January 1982, Iceland
Aliases: Doctess, Kría Brekkan
Name variations: Kristin Anna Valtysdottir, Kristín Anna, K. Valtysdóttir (and others)
Based: New York (as of at least 2012)

Icelandic vocalist and classically trained multi-instrumentalist. Co-founder of múm. Twin sister of Gyða Valtýsdóttir. Ex-wife of Avey Tare (Animal Collective). Active as a solo artist, producer, and session player across the Reykjavík experimental music scene and internationally.

Biography

Origins and múm (1997–2006)

Kristín Anna was born 5 January 1982 in Iceland, twin to Gyða Valtýsdóttir. Along with Örvar Þóreyjarson Smárason and Gunnar Örn Tynes, the twins co-founded múm in Reykjavík in 1997. The group released on the small Icelandic label TMT Entertainment from 1998 before breaking internationally via FatCat Records with Finally We Are No One (2002).

Kristín Anna contributed vocals and played multiple instruments across múm’s early period, including on the acclaimed Finally We Are No One (2002) and Many In High Places Are Not Well (2003). She is also credited as songwriter on Please Smile My Noise Bleed (Morr Music, 2004 SACEM uncredited credit). She stopped active work with múm from June 2005; the departure was officially announced in November 2006. Gyða Valtýsdóttir had already departed after Finally We Are No One to return to studies in Reykjavík.

Animal Collective connection (2008–2010)

During and around her marriage to Avey Tare (David Portner of Animal Collective), Kristín Anna contributed recording and production work to several Paw Tracks releases. She is credited as producer and recording engineer on Black Habit (2008, Paw Tracks PAW 20), an album by Rings (Animal Collective offshoot featuring Abby Portner). She also appears as a recording engineer on the Animal Collective / Danny Perez visual album ODDSAC (2010, Plexifilm), credited as “Kristin Anna Valtysdottir.”

Reykjavík session work and Stórsveit Nix Noltes (2005)

Outside múm, Kristín Anna played accordion on the Stórsveit Nix Noltes album Orkiduer Hawaí (Bubble Core Records, 2005) — a Balkan and Eastern European folk repertoire record featuring an all-star Reykjavík cast: Hildur Guðnadóttir (cello), Ólöf Arnalds (violin), Orri Jónsson (recording), and Mandy Parnell (mastering). The album’s repertoire consists entirely of Bulgarian and Macedonian dance forms (krepatka, rachenitsa, gankino, etc.).

Mice Parade (2007)

She contributed uncredited vocals to the track “Double Dolphins On The Nickel” on Mice Parade’s self-titled album (FatCat, 2007), alongside Adam Pierce and other performers. The Discogs credit is listed as “Vocals [Uncredited].”

Solo work: Rekjavik (2012)

In 2012, Kristín Anna and twin sister Gyða Valtýsdóttir collaborated on Rekjavik (Petites Planètes, 2012) — a joint release credited to both sisters (“Gyda & Kristin Anna Valtysdottir”). The release has no additional credited contributors in Discogs.

Solo work: Howl and Doctess (2015)

Kristín Anna released her debut proper solo album Howl in 2015 on Bel-Air Glamour Records - The Vinyl Factory (cat# VF174LP), released under her own name. The album is a 10-track LP with no additional credited contributors — a wholly self-produced solo statement. Genres on Discogs: Electronic / Non-Music / Rhythmic Noise. Track titles employ deliberate misspellings (“Hazy Omnizcience,” “Dowzing Curiouzity,” “Velvet Breeze Pinkz the Zky”), a stylistic marker distinct from the múm material.

The same label released The Visitors (2016), a release in which she appears as a musician alongside other contributors.

I Must Be The Devil (2019)

Her second solo album I Must Be The Devil (Bel-Air Glamour Records / The Vinyl Factory, 2019) — credited as “Kristín Anna” on the sleeve — was co-produced and recorded by Kjartan Sveinsson (former member of Sigur Rós) and Birgir Jón Birgisson (mixer, also known as Biggi Pool Boy). Mastered by John Davis (4). The album represents a significant step up in production scope compared to Howl, with two producers attached and professional mastering.

Megas (2019)

The same year, Kristín Anna contributed backing vocals to Megas Syngur ÓSÓMALJÓÐ Þorvaldar Þorsteinssonar by Megas (Mengi, 2019), alongside Gyða Valtýsdóttir (backing vocals and cello) and Ólöf Arnalds (backing vocals and guitar). The album is a tribute to the poetry of Þorvaldur Þorsteinsson.
