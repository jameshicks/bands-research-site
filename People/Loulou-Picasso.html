Overview

Loulou Picasso is the stage name of Jean-Louis Dupré, a French visual artist and member of Bazooka, the Paris punk art collective that emerged in the late 1970s. His recorded catalogue runs from 1977 into the 2020s, spanning punk, post-punk, synth-pop, new wave, and chanson; the earliest releases appeared on Sordide Sentimental, Skydog, and Celluloid, with reissue activity concentrated on Born Bad Records in the 2000s and 2010s. Elli Medeiros is his most frequent collaborator by shared credit count, with eleven appearances together. He is the brother of Lawrence Dupré.
