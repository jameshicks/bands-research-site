Harris Newman

Harris Newman

Harris Newman

Discogs ID: 310148
Origin: Montreal, Quebec, Canada
Role: Mastering engineer, guitarist, bassist
Studio: Grey Market Mastering, Montreal
Years active: 1998–present
Labels (as artist): Strange Attractors Audio House, Constellation Records, Okraïna

Harris Newman is a Montreal-based mastering engineer and guitarist, operating Grey Market Mastering in the same building as the Constellation Records office and the Hotel2Tango recording studio. He is the single most-credited person across the Constellation catalog — credited on approximately 267 releases for that label alone — and one of the most prolific mastering engineers in Canadian independent music, with over 2,100 mastering credits in Discogs spanning 1998 to the present. He is also an active performer, known for his acoustic solo guitar work (in the tradition of John Fahey and American primitive guitar) and as a member of several Montreal groups including Hrsta, Sackville, Triple Burner, and Esmerine. (Source: Wikipedia)

Grey Market Mastering

Newman opened Grey Market Mastering in Montreal and by the mid-2000s had become a go-to engineer for independent labels across Canada and beyond. His credit volume grew steadily: roughly 20 releases in 2000, climbing to 80–90 per year by 2007–2009, and peaking near 150 per year in 2011–2018 before settling in the 70–130 range through the early 2020s.

Beyond Constellation Records, his mastering footprint is wide: roughly 50 releases for Sub Pop, 37 for Secret City Records, 33 for Paper Bag Records, 27 for You’ve Changed Records, 26 for Jagjaguwar, 23 for HoloDeck, 22 for Arts & Crafts, 18 for Dare To Care Records, 16 for Southern Lord, 15 for Thrill Jockey, 15 for Full Time Hobby, 18 for Sub Rosa, and 11 each for Bella Union and Strange Attractors Audio House, among dozens of smaller Canadian and international independents.

The studio shares a building with Constellation Records and Hotel2Tango, placing Newman at the physical center of the Montreal post-rock scene from the very beginning.

Performer and Bandmember

Sackville (mid-1990s–2001)

Newman played bass in Sackville, a Montreal group led by singer-guitarist Gabe Levine and co-founded by Geneviève Heistek, who charted a path through damaged roots music, country, and avant-folk. He is credited on bass on These Last Songs (1998, Mag Wheel Records) and The Principles Of Science (1999, Constellation) — which was also one of his earliest mastering credits. Sackville slowly disbanded at the turn of the millennium; members went on to play in Hangedup, Black Ox Orkestar, Set Fire To Flames, and A Silver Mt. Zion.

Hrsta (2001–present)

Newman is a member of Hrsta, the post-rock group founded by Godspeed You Black Emperor! member Mike Moya. Hrsta released L’Éclat Du Ciel Était Insoutenable (2001, Fancy), Stem Stem In Electro (2005, Constellation), Ghosts Will Come And Kiss Our Eyes (2007, Constellation), and the self-released Autumn Sketchbook 2008. Newman is credited as bassist.

Triple Burner (2006)

Newman played guitar in Triple Burner, releasing the self-titled Triple Burner (2006, Madrona Records). The band also included the Dark Was The Night project (Kning Disk, 2006), on which Newman is credited for guitar, writing, and mixing.

Solo Guitar

Newman has released acoustic guitar albums under his own name on Strange Attractors Audio House: Non-Sequiturs (2003), Accidents With Nature And Each Other (2005), Decorated (2007). His playing has been compared to John Fahey’s American primitive approach (Wikipedia). He also released a split with Mauro Antonio Pawlowski (Glasvocht Records, 2007). A later recording as Ignatz & Harris Newman, Bring You Buzzard Meat, appeared in 2009 (self-released) with the studio version on Okraïna in 2013.
