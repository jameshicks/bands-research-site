Bratko Bibič

Slovenian accordionist and film-music composer. Discogs artist ID: 524571.

Overview

Member of Nimal and Begnagrad, and later of Accordion Tribe. His surname sometimes appears as Bibič-Faninger. He became a core Nimal member from Voix De Surface (1990), co-mixed the album alongside François Dietz, and is the dominant instrumental voice on Dis Tanz (1992). Also a composer and film-music writer.

Key Projects

  • Begnagrad — Slovenian avant-garde/folk group; core member across all four releases (1982–1993); primary composer; accordion, melodica, piano
  • Nimal — Swiss-based RIO collective; accordion, harmonica, voice; member 1990–1993
  • Accordion Tribe — later international accordion ensemble
  • Bratko Bibič & The Madleys
  • Die Wiener Ziehharmoniker
