Philipp Münch

Electronic musician and producer based in Germany (Nuremberg area). Discogs artist ID: 488971. Real name: Philipp Münch. Extensive career in European industrial/EBM/noise electronics spanning multiple aliases and groups. Listed as a member of The Present Moment.

Overview

Philipp Münch has one of the most wide-ranging discographies in the European industrial/experimental electronics underground. He operates under numerous aliases (see below) and is a member of many groups. His collaboration with Scott Milton on the early The Present Moment records (2010–2019) connected the US witch-house scene to European body-music tradition.
