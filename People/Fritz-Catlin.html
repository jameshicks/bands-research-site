Fritz Catlin

Discogs ID: 448649 Real name: Geoffrey Simon Mark Catlin Also credited as: Fritz Häaman, FC Harmony, Hashashan, Schizo-P, F. Catlin, GS Catlin, Geoffrey Catlin Role: Drummer, percussionist, recording/mixing engineer, songwriter Active: 1979–present (years on Discogs: 1982–2026)

Overview

British new wave musician and engineer from London. Best known as co-founder and drummer of 23 Skidoo (1979). Also co-founded Current 93 in its earliest incarnation, played session drums for Laibach in 1983–86, and performed with Last Few Days, Sugardog, and Firebomb Fritz’ Stylites. In later career: active as freelance drummer, percussionist, mixing engineer, and producer across UK indie, reggae/dub, and experimental scenes; notably produced/performed on posthumous Lee Perry recordings (2025). Also a wildlife gardener.

The Current 93 Connection

Fritz Catlin is one of the three founding members of Current 93, alongside David Tibet and Jhonn Balance (later of Coil). His role makes him the direct personnel link between 23 Skidoo and the early UK neofolk/industrial underground.

LAShTAL was “a spur of the moment studio session” — recorded 1982, first solo Current 93 release (1984). “LAShTAL” and “Salt” later appeared on the compilation Nature Unveiled.

The Laibach Connection

In April 1983, 23 Skidoo and Last Few Days were invited by Laibach to perform at the Zagreb Music Biennial. A split cassette was jointly released: Laibach / Last Few Days (1983, recorded at Radio Študent, limited to 400 copies). The concert was interrupted at 5am by police.

On a subsequent Laibach tour (late 1983), Catlin played drums on recordings that became Laibach’s debut single — Boji / Sila / Brat Moj (1984).

In September 1986, Catlin performed with Laibach in choreographer Michael Clark’s show “No Fire Escape In Hell” at Sadler’s Wells Theatre, London (also Brighton and Los Angeles).

Last Few Days

An esoteric/experimental collective from 1981 centred around Daniel Landin (“Stan Bingo”), Si Joyce, and Keir Wahid — using megaphones, tape loops, and harsh sonics. Catlin performed with them and toured Europe with Laibach.

Solo alias: Hashashan

Catlin recorded under the alias Hashashan in the 1990s. No formal releases confirmed — research queue item.

Sugardog

Member of Sugardog — a London live breakbeat act from Hackney. Members: Tim Whelan, Jim Whelan, Gila, Fritz Catlin, and John Gosling (of the Mekons). Released “That’s Heavy Baby” on Temple Records (TOPY 021, 1987). Temple/TOPY was Genesis P-Orridge’s label — same thread as TG producing Seven Songs.

Commercial songwriting (1988–91)

During the period when 23 Skidoo were largely on hiatus, Catlin placed songs with mainstream acts:

  • “Intuition 88” — by David Grant (Fresher Records, FREST 1, 1988). Produced by Assassins With Soul; co-written with Alex Turnbull, Johnny Turnbull, Peter Martin, and David Grant. Sleeve design by Neville Brody.
  • “Intuition 89” — by David Grant (Beat Box, Sweden, BB 8213, 1989). Swedish reissue of same song.
  • “Your Face In My Mind” — by Lizzie Tear (EMI, EM 187, 1991). Co-written with John Gosling and Lizzie Tear. Produced by Catlin under his FC Harmony alias, alongside Lizzie Tear and Sugar J.

The David Grant co-writes are notable: Grant was a UK R&B/soul chart act. The Lizzie Tear session is a Catlin production under his FC Harmony alias — one of the few documented uses of that name.

Skintologists

Big Daddy (37)

Catlin co-founded Big Daddy (37) (Discogs ID: 12850523) with Simon Crab of Bourbonese Qualk. The project released the LP Bomb Culture (Offen Music, OFFEN 025, 2023). Catlin played drums, percussion, bass, guitar, and melodica; wrote, recorded, and produced. Crab handled electronics, synthesizers, drum programming, and graphic design. The two-man lineup is confirmed on the Discogs entry.

Vic Godard connection (2024)

Catlin produced track A1 on Everybody’s Scared Now (Gnu Inc. Recordings, 2024) by Vic Godard (Subway Sect). Kevin Harris (3) produced the remaining tracks. A direct post-punk lineage connection: Godard/Subway Sect are canonical first-wave UK punk.
