Howard Walmsley

Manchester-based musician and filmmaker. Discogs artist ID: 504961.

Saxophonist, keyboards player, and founding member of Biting Tongues (Discogs ID: 12089), active in the group across all periods from 1979 to the 2020 reunion. He also played saxophone on 808 State’s Gorgeous (1993). Director and editor of the short film Feverhouse (1985, Factory/Ikon; release 1298776), co-edited with Dave Horsfield.

Name variations: Howie, Walmsey, Walmsley.

Biography

Walmsley was the originating force behind Biting Tongues. In 1979, he gathered musicians including Ken Hollings and Eddie Sherwood at the Factory Club in Hulme to perform live accompaniment to a 16mm silent film he had made — also titled “Biting Tongues.” This one-off performance at Tony Wilson’s club was the group’s founding act, and within weeks Graham Massey and Colin Seddon (from their previous band, the Post Natals) joined to complete the five-piece.

Walmsley was the constant presence across all of Biting Tongues’ phases — from the raw post-punk of Don’t Heal (1981) through the Factory-period lineup changes to the late-period electronics of Love Out (1989), and through all reunion activity from 2003 onwards. He was also a co-writer on the Feverhouse soundtrack (alongside Massey and Seddon) and the CGI artwork co-credit on 808 State’s Gorgeous (1993) suggests an ongoing relationship with the Massey/Manchester network after Biting Tongues’ initial hiatus.

Feverhouse (1985)

Feverhouse is a black-and-white short film written by Ken Hollings and directed and edited by Walmsley, co-edited with Dave Horsfield. Set in “an all-purpose institution that might be part hospital, part museum, part asylum and part morgue,” the film follows three characters — a thief (Graham Massey), a nurse (Joanne Hill), and a blind man (Patrick Nyland) — in relationships of desire and power. The film was partially financed by the North West Arts Association and released on VHS and Betamax in 1985 through Ikon FCL (Factory Records’ video offshoot), catalogued as Factory FAC 105. The accompanying soundtrack was recorded by the new Biting Tongues lineup (Derby, Kirby, Barnish, Massey, Walmsley) and released as an LP on Factory Records (FACT 105). In 1988, Walmsley contributed to the compilation video Wall of Surf (Ikon, IKON 26).
