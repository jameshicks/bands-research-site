Chris Haskett

US guitarist, born 1962 in Washington D.C. Discogs artist ID: 79631. Also credited as DJ Linux (2).

Overview

Guitarist in Surfin Dave And The Absent Legends (Leeds, mid-1980s), where he co-wrote and co-produced the 1985 LP In Search Of A Decent Haircut on Crammed Discs. Subsequently achieved international recognition as the guitarist of Rollins Band, Henry Rollins’ post-Black Flag band, serving through their most prominent commercial period. Also appeared in Henrietta Collins And The Wifebeating Childhaters and Iron Cross, and later Pigface and The Joy Thieves.
