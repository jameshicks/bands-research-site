Overview

Doug Wieselman is a Los Angeles-born reed player, guitarist, and composer whose career stretches from 1985 across avantgarde, experimental jazz, and art rock contexts. He plays clarinet, tenor saxophone, and bass clarinet, and has been a member of Kamikaze Ground Crew, Badawi Quintet, and DarkMatterHalo, among other ensembles. With over 535 credits, his work is anchored by dense collaborative relationships with Steven Bernstein (131 shared releases) and Paul Shapiro, situating him firmly in New York’s downtown experimental scene. He also sustained a long association with Antony and the Johnsons, appearing across releases on Rough Trade, Secretly Canadian, and Zoë Records.
