Eric Drew Feldman

Keyboardist, bassist, synthesizer player, producer, and multi-instrumentalist from the USA. Discogs artist ID: 223296.

Overview

Eric Drew Feldman is one of the more quietly essential figures connecting several distinct currents of American experimental and post-punk music. A multi-instrumentalist equally comfortable on synthesizer, bass, piano, Rhodes, Mellotron, and Moog, he is best known for two major band memberships: Captain Beefheart’s The Magic Band (1976–1982) and Pere Ubu (from 1988). After the Magic Band dissolved on Beefheart’s retirement, Feldman worked briefly with Snakefinger’s Vestal Virgins — through which he met The Residents, later touring with them nearly two decades on. He also worked extensively with Frank Black as both bassist and co-producer across Frank Black’s most prolific solo period (1993–2004+), and played on two landmark Pixies albums in 1991. From the mid-1990s he became a prominent producer, working with PJ Harvey, dEUS, and a range of other artists. He also recorded on PJ Harvey’s White Chalk (2007). His alias project is listed as Black Jew Kitabo, and his solo/duo project was Knife & Fork (with Laurie Hall).

Name variations used on records: E. Feldman, E.D. Feldman, E.D.F., Eric Drew, Eric Drew Feldmen, Eric Feldman, Feldman, Mr. Feldman, Professor Eric Drew Feldman, eDf.
