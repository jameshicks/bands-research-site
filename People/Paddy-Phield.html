Overview

Paddy Phield (real name Takeshi Tsujino) is a drummer and percussionist whose career is rooted in the late-1970s British punk and new-wave scene. His catalogue is heaviest in that decade — four releases between 1977 and 1981 — spread across Parole Records, Overseas Records, and Fresh Records (14), with a lone 1990s credit and then Vivid Sound appearances in 2006–07 that suggest a later Japanese chapter. His tightest creative circle aligns with Cuddly Toys and its predecessor band Raped, sharing double-digit credits with Faebhean Kwest, Sean Purcell, Tony Baggett, and Billy Surgeoner. Alongside drumming he holds Written-By credits and in 2019 self-released on his own Phield Music imprint.
