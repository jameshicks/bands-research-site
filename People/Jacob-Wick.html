Jacob Wick

Trumpeter and improviser, US-based. Discogs artist ID: 1857378

Name variations: J. Wick, Wick.

Overview

Jacob Wick is a free improvisation trumpeter whose Discogs record spans 2007–2024. He works primarily in small-group improvisation — trios, quartets, and collective contexts — and has appeared in a wide range of ensembles from minimal chamber-like trios (Kimmel, Moré, Wick Trio; Tres Hongos) through jazz-rock bands (Kenosha Kid) to large orchestras (DNA Orchestra (2)). His most consistent duo partner is Brad Henkel, with whom he shares Trumpet Trumpet Synthesizer and the duo release I Saw A Lightbulb Flickering I Moved Towards It And It Was Morning (2017). The bulk of his credited output sits in free improvisation and avant-garde jazz on small US labels; his most recent Discogs credit is a trumpet appearance on Mabe Fratti’s Sentir Que No Sabes (2024, Tin Angel Records).

His Discogs profile text is just “Trumpet player” — no biographical detail on training, location, or career origins is recorded there.
