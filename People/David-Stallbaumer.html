Overview

David Stallbaumer is a US-born drummer and engineer who has been active in the UK since the late 1970s. He is the brother of Clete Stallbaumer and appears as a member of the band Toys (2). His credits cluster tightly around the Peter Gabriel production circle — top collaborators include Daniel Lanois, David Rhodes, David Bottrill, Kevin Killen, Tony Levin, and Manu Katché — indicating sustained work on Gabriel-related projects across multiple decades. His catalogue spans Pop Rock, Synth-pop, Art Rock, and Ambient styles, with credits on labels including Geffen Records, Real World Records, and Charisma.
