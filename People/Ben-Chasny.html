Ben Chasny

Ben Chasny

Oct 18, 20266 min read

Ben Chasny

Discogs ID: 274470
US guitarist and composer based in Santa Cruz / San Francisco area. Best known as Six Organs of Admittance, his primary vehicle since 1998. Also a founding member of noise-psych band Comets On Fire, improv trio Rangda, and folk duo New Bums. One of the most prolific figures in the American underground folk/psych/experimental continuum, active from the mid-1990s through the present.

Biography

Origins and Early Recordings (1996–2001)

Chasny’s first credited appearance is on The Wicker Image (1996, Holy Mountain), a tribute/compilation. He launched Six Organs of Admittance as his solo vehicle around 1998–1999, initially releasing cassettes and CDRs under that name. The earliest Six Organs titles — self-titled debut (2000, Pavilion) and Dust & Chimes (2000, Holy Mountain) — established the project’s foundation: fingerpicked acoustic guitar, Indian raga influence, drones, and a mystical folk atmosphere.

Around the same time, Chasny joined Comets On Fire in Santa Cruz — a five-piece heavy psych/noise rock outfit with Ethan Miller (guitar/vocals), Noel Von Harmonson (Echoplex), Ben Flashman (bass), and Utrillo Kushner / Utrillo Kushner (drums). Comets released a self-titled debut in 2001 and quickly developed a reputation for ferocious live shows.

Prolific Mid-Period: Six Organs and Comets (2002–2007)

This period is defined by an extraordinary output on both fronts. Six Organs released a string of albums that deepened and diversified Chasny’s aesthetic:

  • Dark Noontide (2002, Holy Mountain) — solo drone experiments
  • For Octavio Paz (2003, Time-Lag Records) — acoustic/electric guitar
  • School Of The Flower (2005, Drag City) — first Drag City album; Chris Corsano on drums
  • The Sun Awakens (2006, Drag City) — more electric, co-produced with Tim Green (2)
  • Shelter From The Ash (2007, Drag City) — co-produced with Tim Green

Comets On Fire moved to Sub Pop and hit their peak with Blue Cathedral (2004, Sub Pop) and Avatar (2006, Sub Pop) — the latter widely considered their best. The band effectively dissolved after 2006, with Chasny pursuing solo work and other collaborations.

During this stretch, Chasny also began appearing on releases by Current 93 (contributed to Black Ships Ate The Sky, 2006) and other left-field British artists, expanding his network internationally. He participated in the Black Ships project (2005) with Steven Stapleton (Nurse With Wound) and David Tibet.

Collaborative Explosion and Hexadic System (2008–2016)

The RTZ compilation (2008/2009, Drag City) documented this period’s range. Key releases:

  • Luminous Night (2009, Drag City) — recorded and produced solo; electronics, tape, Korg keyboards introduced
  • Asleep On The Floodplain (2011, Drag City) — mixed and recorded by Chasny himself
  • Ascent (2012, Drag City) — guitar and vocals, produced by Chasny

In 2010, Chasny formed Rangda with Richard Bishop (Sir Richard Bishop, ex-Sun City Girls) and Chris Corsano. The trio released False Flag (2010, Drag City), Formerly Extinct (2012, Drag City), and The Heretic’s Bargain (2016, Drag City), plus a split with The Dead C (2013, Ba Da Bing!). Rangda is purely improvisational, more abrasive and free than Six Organs.

Also formed New Bums with Donovan Quinn (of The Skygreen Leopards), releasing Voices In A Rented Room (2014, Drag City) — acoustic folk duo with a lo-fi, intimate feel.

In 2011, Chasny participated in 200 Years — a duo with Elisa Ambrogio — releasing a self-titled album on Drag City (2011) and Holyoke (2011, Grapefruit Records).

Parallelogram (2015, Three Lobed Recordings) is a notable split/collaboration with fingerpicker William Tyler.

The Hexadic album (2015, Drag City) was a watershed: Chasny had developed his own compositional system, the “Hexadic System,” derived from a card-game-like arrangement of intervals. The album is heavily electric and dissonant, a departure from folk-drone aesthetics.

Basalt Fingers (with Elisa Ambrogio and Brian Sullivan) released one record on Three Lobed Recordings (2007).

August Born was a duo with Hiroyuki Usui (Japanese guitarist; member of Ghost (2) and Fushitsusha); released on Drag City (2005, DC284CD).

Later Period (2017–present)

  • Burning The Threshold (2017, Drag City) — all sounds by Chasny
  • Losing Tracks Of Time (2019, Hermit Hut) — self-recorded
  • Companion Rises (2020, Drag City) — photography, recording, mixing all by Chasny
  • The Intimate Landscape (2021, Drag City) — composed by Chasny; mastered by Carl Saff
  • Waves (2023, Voix Records (2)) — a collaborative album, co-credited guitar release
  • Time Is Glass (2024, Drag City) — performed, written, and recorded by Chasny
  • Jinxed By Being (2024, Drag City) — full collaboration album with Shackleton (producer credit)

New Bums returned with Last Time I Saw Grace (2021, Drag City).
