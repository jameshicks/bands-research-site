Michael Duane

British guitarist/vocalist. Leader and core member of Dustdevils throughout their existence (Leeds period, 1986–88; New York period, 1989–96). Alias on Discogs: Michael Dust Devil. Discogs artist ID: 1227969.

Overview

Duane formed Dustdevils in Leeds in the mid-1980s with Australian-born Jacqui Dulany. After relocating to New York he continued leading the band through their Matador-era releases. He handled guitar and vocals, co-produced the UK Rouska releases with Geoff Clout, and took an uncredited cover design role on Struggling Electric And Chemical (1990).

Post-Dustdevils he recorded under various project names including Cameras In Cars, Atlantic Drone, Ancients (2), Crippled And The Burnout, and Dustdevil & Crow.
