Overview

Monte Vallier is a musician, engineer, and producer who has been active since 1986, best known as a member of Half Church, Jet Black Crayon, and Swell. His 473 Discogs credits span bass guitar, recording, mixing, and production across indie rock, post-punk, shoegaze, and downtempo. His most sustained working relationship is with Tommy Guerrero, sharing credits on over 100 releases, followed by a long collaboration with David Freel and the Swell collective. His catalogue cuts across Beggars Banquet, Slumberland Records, Rush! Production, and a range of smaller imprints, with output extending from the mid-1980s into the mid-2020s.
