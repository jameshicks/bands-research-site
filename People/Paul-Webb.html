Paul Webb

Bassist, musician. Born Paul Douglas Webb, 16 January 1962, Essex, England. Also records as Rustin Man. Discogs ID: 60623.

Bassist for Talk Talk (1981–1991). After the band dissolved, formed .O.Rang with drummer Lee Harris. As Rustin Man, released Out of Season (2002, Domino) — a collaborative album with Beth Gibbons of Portishead, recorded across several years in the late 1990s.
