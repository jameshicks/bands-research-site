Overview

Johnny Sangster is a Seattle-based producer, recording engineer, and multi-instrumentalist whose credits stretch back to 1986 and total nearly 600 releases. Originally from Seattle, he relocated to Denmark in 1989 — a move that appears connected to his membership in the Danish band De Hårde Få — before returning to the Pacific Northwest, where he operates his Crackle & Pop facility. His production work is tightly woven into the Seattle punk and garage-rock scene: his most frequent collaborators include the core Mudhoney lineup (Mark Arm, Steve Turner, Dan Peters, Guy Maddison) alongside The Cute Lepers and Kicks, with Sub Pop accounting for his most sustained label relationship, spanning 1999 through 2023.
