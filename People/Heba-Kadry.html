Heba Kadry

Discogs ID: 1338550. Mastering engineer. Credits include remastering work on Explosions In The Sky’s How Strange, Innocence reissue and mastering on Big Bend (2021). Mastered both of Slowdive’s reformation-era albums: Slowdive (2017, Dead Oceans DOC132) and Everything Is Alive (2023, Dead Oceans DOC232).

Mastered Julianna Barwick’s Healing Is A Miracle (2020, Ninja Tune) and Tragic Magic (2026, Infiné), 10 shared releases in total per Discogs. Consistent mastering partner with Marta Salogni across 10+ releases — the pair appear together on Ela Minus, Björk, Holly Herndon, English Teacher, Westerman, Sharon Van Etten, The Orielles, and others from 2019 onward. Also mastered Music For Open Spaces (Hands In The Dark, 2023) by Marta Salogni & Tom Relleen.

Mastered FIS (2)‘s two Tri Angle EPs: Preparations (A1, B1, B2) and Iterations (A2, B1), both 2013/2014.

Fred Thomas connection

Mastered Fred Thomas’s Aftering (Polyvinyl, 2018). 2 shared releases on Discogs.
