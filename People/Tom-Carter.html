Tom Carter

Discogs ID: 196446
US guitarist based in Texas. Core member of Charalambides (with Christina Carter, 1991–2011). Prolific solo improviser and participant in the American free folk / psych underground.

Overview

Tom Carter is primarily known as one half of the Houston/Austin experimental folk drone duo Charalambides, which he co-founded with Christina Carter in 1991. Alongside that work, he has maintained an extensive solo guitar career and participated in numerous improv and psychedelic rock projects. His Discogs groups list is long: Badgerlore, The Mike Gunn, Primordial Undermind, Kyrgyz, Sarin Smoke, Spiderwebs, Friday Group, Mudsuckers, Turnstone, Zaika, Eleven Twenty-Nine, Sky City (2), Savage Sons Of Wallonie, Morning Scales The Mountain, and Tom Carter & Robert Horton.

Badgerlore

Member of Badgerlore (with Rob Fisk, Ben Chasny, Pete Swanson, Liz Harris). Co-credited on Beats For The Beast (FPS 005, 2004) split with Scorces. Performed on Badgerlore’s Stories For Owls (FPS 012, 2005).

The Mike Gunn

Member of The Mike Gunn, a Houston-based psych/noise band (other members: Curt Mackey, John Cramer (2), Scott Grimm). Likely pre-dates or overlaps with early Charalambides.

Primordial Undermind

Member of Primordial Undermind, a large-ensemble international experimental group with rotating membership including Eric Arn and Vanessa Arn. Carter is one of many guitarists in this orbit.
