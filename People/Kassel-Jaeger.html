Kassel Jaeger

French composer and sound artist. Real name: François Bonnet. Born 1981, Limoges. Paris-based. Discogs artist ID: 1037551. Also active as François Bonnet (2) (Discogs ID: 1466604) — the two are listed as separate but aliased entries on Discogs. Director of Groupe De Recherches Musicales (INA-GRM).

Overview

Kassel Jaeger is the performing and composition alias of François J. Bonnet — composer, sound engineer, radio producer, and director of the Institut National de l’Audiovisuel Groupe de Recherches Musicales (INA-GRM) in Paris. His work sits in the electroacoustic tradition: tape-based, acousmatic, and deeply indebted to French musique concrète. He works in the lineage of GRM’s founders (Pierre Schaeffer, Pierre Henry, Bernard Parmegiani) while maintaining an active contemporary recording presence through Editions Mego, Black Truffle, Shelter Press, and Latency.

His output divides into two streams. As Kassel Jaeger he releases solo electroacoustic work and collaborative albums with improvisers and experimental musicians. As François Bonnet (2) he handles GRM curation work — coordinating, writing liner notes, and producing the Recollection GRM and Portraits GRM archival sub-series on Editions Mego. The Cylene project with Stephen O’Malley is credited under the François Bonnet (2) name.

He also theorizes: his book The Order of Sounds (2016, Urbanomic) is a philosophical treatise on listening and sonic ontology.
