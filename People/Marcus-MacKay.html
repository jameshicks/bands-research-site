Marcus MacKay

Glasgow-based musician, producer, and recording engineer. Discogs artist ID: 327353. Real name: Marcus Mackay. Studio: Diving Bell Lounge (divingbelllounge.co.uk).

Overview

Marcus MacKay is a Glasgow-based musician, producer, and recording engineer whose career spans band membership, session drumming, and studio production across the Scottish independent music scene from the mid-1990s onward. He operates the Diving Bell Lounge studio in Glasgow, which carries its own credit on some releases.

As a band member he has played in The Reindeer Section (Gary Lightbody’s large-scale Scottish indie collective, 2001–2002), The Zephyrs (Glasgow indie group, active around 2000–2010s), Our Lunar Activities (duo with Charlie Clark), The P&R Social Club (duo with Tom Simpson), and Out Lines (trio with James Graham of The Twilight Sad and Kathryn Joseph). His Discogs name variations include “Marcus”, “Marcus Mackay”, and “Marcus McKay”.

As a producer and engineer his most sustained relationships have been with Frightened Rabbit (debut album Sing The Greys in 2006, Christmas single 2007, EP in 2011) and Kathryn Joseph (debut album Bones You Have Thrown Me And Blood I’ve Spilled in 2015 through multiple singles and albums to at least 2019). The bulk of his production work has appeared on Rock Action Records and Hits The Fan Records. He also recorded Snow Patrol’s Final Straw (one track) in 2004 and has credits across Trembling Bells, Sparrow And The Workshop, Alasdair Roberts, and others.

His earliest documented Discogs credit is drums on “Dreaming” (1996) by Blind (13), a Glasgow group. His Reindeer Section contribution was programming on Son Of Evil Reindeer (2002).

Band Memberships

The Reindeer Section

Discogs artist ID: 83605. Large rotating Scottish indie collective assembled by Gary Lightbody (Snow Patrol). MacKay is listed as a member; his documented contribution is “Programmed By” on Son Of Evil Reindeer (2002, Bright Star Recordings). The collective also included Aidan Moffat (Arab Strap), John Cummings (Mogwai), Richard Colburn (Belle & Sebastian), Malcolm Middleton (Arab Strap), Roddy Woomble (Idlewild), Mick Cooke (Belle & Sebastian), Iain Archer, Bob Kildea, Jonny Quinn, and others. Both albums produced by Tony Doogan.

The Zephyrs

Discogs artist ID: 269755. Glasgow indie band. MacKay is listed as a member alongside Gordon Kilgour, David Nicol, Stuart Nicol, Malcolm Cochrane, Caroline Barber, Charlie Clark, David Jeans, Emily Hall, Jonathan Kilgour, Robert Dillam, and Stewart Campbell. MacKay is not credited with a specific production/instrument role in any documented release credits — the early EP It’s Okay Not To Say Anything (2000, Evol Records) was engineered and mixed by Michael Brennan Jr. with no MacKay credit. Later releases (from the 2010 revival period, Club AC30 and Acuarela) also appear to predate or run parallel to his production work.
