Jack Callahan

American experimental musician, drummer, producer, and mastering engineer. Also performs as Die Reihe (Discogs artist ID: 4061470). Based in the US. Discogs artist ID: 1629772.

Active across noise, free improvisation, and experimental electronics since at least 2009. A member of several overlapping experimental/noise ensembles and a consistent presence in the underground tape and small-label circuit. Since roughly 2019 has also worked as a mastering engineer across a wide range of independent releases.

Scene Connections

Callahan sits at an intersection of several overlapping American underground scenes:

  • Free improvisation / noise: The Klangfarben debut and Music From Airports place him in early-2010s US tape-circuit free improv. Both Bhob Rainey (free saxophone, NE Winds) and James Twig Harper (electronics/synthesis) are core figures in this circuit.
  • Sunburned Hand Of The Man: The large Northampton/Boston collective also includes John Moloney, who shares the Egg, Eggs connection. Sunburned is documented elsewhere in this vault.
  • Home Blitz: A New Jersey garage/punk project led by Daniel DiMaggio; Callahan’s role as a member connects his experimental work to the indie/underground rock world.
  • Ascetic House: The Phoenix label associated with industrial/goth/noise electronics; Die Reihe’s 2014 cassette situates Callahan in that milieu alongside his free-improv roots.
  • Mastering work: From 2019 on, Callahan’s mastering credit list spans Cloud Nothings, Wolf Eyes, The Trilogy Tapes, Astral Spirits, ESP-Disk’, Cardinal Fuzz — suggesting a working relationship across noise, drone, garage, and indie underground labels.
  • Rozart Mix (2024): A realization of John Cage’s Rozart Mix (originally 1965) with Aaron Dilloway, James Twig Harper, Quintron, Robert Turman, John Wiese, Rose Actor-Engel, and C. Lavender — a significant gathering of the American experimental underground.
