Asmus Tietchens

German electronic and musique concrète composer. Born 1947 in Hamburg. Discogs artist ID: 15560.

Overview

Tietchens is a Hamburg-based composer associated with the electronic and industrial avant-garde. He wrote the liner notes for the Bureau B reissues of Cluster & Eno (2009) and After The Heat (2009), situating him as both a commentator on the scene and a participant. Bureau B has issued a substantial body of his solo work from 2012 onward (31+ entries), including Biotop (BB141, 2013), In Die Nacht (BB143, 2013), and Litia (BB144, 2013).

Terry Burrows Collaborations

Tietchens collaborated with Terry Burrows on three releases spanning 1986–2017:

  • Watching The Burning Bride (Hamster HAM 16, 1986) — joint LP of abstract industrial pieces, on Burrows’ Hamster Records And Tapes
  • Burning The Watching Bride (Disaster Area DA 001, 1998) — split LP; one side each
  • Watching The Burning Bride / Burning The Watching Bride (Auf Abwegen / Die Stadt, 2017) — compilation of both works
