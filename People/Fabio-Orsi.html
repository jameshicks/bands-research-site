Overview

Fabio Orsi is an electronic musician and composer from Taranto, in Southern Italy, working in ambient, drone, and experimental modes with a strain of Berlin-School influence. His recorded output stretches from 2006 through the present, with broadly consistent volume across three decades and a focus on guitar, composition, and electronic processing. His most extensive working relationship is with Gianluca Becuzzi, with whom he shares 19 releases — a collaboration that sits at the centre of his catalogue alongside contributions from Valerio Cosi, Claudio Rocchetti, and Giuseppe Ielasi. He is a member of the groups GREY HISTORY, Modus (27), and OP (9), and has released primarily on 13 (3) and Backwards.
