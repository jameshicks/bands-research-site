Phil Vinall

Discogs ID: 227231
Real name: Phillip Vinall
Died: December 8, 2025
Origin: London, UK (later relocated to Mexico)

Producer and engineer. 55+ shared releases with Television Personalities — primary producer from Privilege (1989, Fire Records) through the 1990s, including Closer To God (1992). Also produced Close Lobsters’ second album Headache Rhetoric (Enigma (4), 1989) and the Nature Thing single (Fire Records, 1989), with Howard Turner engineering both. Produced the final two The Perfect Disaster Fire Records releases: Heaven Scent (1990, Fire CD27) and the Rise EP (1990), both co-produced with the band. Later produced Placebo (Pure Morning, 1998; Without You I’m Nothing, 1998; Every You Every Me, 1999), Elastica (2) (Elastica — mixed), Gene, Black Box Recorder, and others. After relocating to Mexico, produced extensively for Zoé and related Latin alternative acts. Member of Fuse (45). Discogs credits span 1980 (tape op on XTC’s Black Sea) through 2020+.
