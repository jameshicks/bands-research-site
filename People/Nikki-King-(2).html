Real name: Nikki King Bennett (born Nikki King) Role: Vocalist, strings arranger Discogs artist ID: 510108 Years active (DB): 2002–2009 (Gregor Samsa releases); 2025 (GS reunion unclear) Name variations on sleeves: “King” (2002 EP), “Nk Bennett” (Over Air, 2009)

Biography

Nikki King Bennett is a slowcore vocalist and strings arranger from New York. She was a core member of Gregor Samsa (3), the Richmond-formed post-rock/slowcore ensemble, appearing on their releases from the 2002 self-titled EP through the 2009 Over Air disc.

She married Champ Bennett, Gregor Samsa’s primary vocalist and guitarist, in September 2007. Her sleeve credits track the name change: she appears as “King” on the 2002 EP (pre-marriage), and as “Nk Bennett” on Over Air (2009). On the 2005 Red Sparowes split she is credited under her full name Nikki King.

Her most distinctive credited role beyond vocalist is strings arranger: on the 2005 Red Sparowes / Gregor Samsa split she is explicitly credited “Vocals, Arranged By [Strings]” on the two GS tracks (B1, B2). The Rest lineup (2007–2008) was Gregor Samsa’s largest, and she is listed among its ensemble performers alongside Mia Matsumiya (violin), Amber Blankenship (cello/violin), Toby Driver, and others.

Outside Gregor Samsa, she appeared as a guest vocalist on Ghastly City Sleep’s debut album (2007, Robotic Empire), contributing additional sounds and vocals. She is also a member of Tiempo, a trio with Champ Bennett and Earl Yevak — the group appears credited on track 8 (“XXX”) of Over Air (2009), though Tiempo has no standalone releases in the Discogs database.
