Overview

George Cabaniss (August 15, 1956 – July 17, 2020) was an American punk rock guitarist and vocalist born in Decatur, Illinois and based in Ohio. He is best known as a member of The Dead Boys, the influential Cleveland punk outfit fronted by Stiv Bators, and also played in Color Me Gone, Hammer Damage, and Illuminatus. His discographic footprint spans the mid-1980s through the 2010s across Power Pop, Punk, and Indie Rock, with original releases on A&M Records and later reissues on BOMP!, Munster Records, and Radiation Reissues. His closest recorded collaborators were Bators, Frank Secich, and David Quinton, pointing consistently to the Dead Boys orbit.
