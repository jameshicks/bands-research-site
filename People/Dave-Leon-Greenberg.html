Recording, mixing and mastering engineer, currently at Sonopod Mastering (St. Petersburg, Florida), and formerly at Boomtown, Sausalito (California).

He started his career in the late 1980s by working as an assistant engineer in two Boston recording studios, while he was still an art school student making experimental music, electro-acoustic instruments and sound processors. While he eventually became chief engineer, he also freelanced and continued to equip and develop his warehouse studio in the South of Market area. Overview

Dave Leon Greenberg is a recording, mixing, and mastering engineer currently operating Sonopod Mastering in St. Petersburg, Florida. He began his career in the late 1980s as an assistant engineer at Boston recording studios while studying at art school and building experimental electronic instruments, before relocating to San Francisco in the early 1990s and joining the staff at Boomtown in Sausalito, where he rose to chief engineer. Alongside studio work he maintained his own warehouse facility in the South of Market district, shifting focus toward mastering and eventually launching Sonopod formally before moving to Florida in the early 2000s. His 109 Discogs credits span leftfield, post-punk, downtempo, and indie rock, with his most sustained collaboration being the UK electronic act Lamb and its core members Andrew Barlow and Louise Rhodes, who account for his three highest-shared-credit relationships.
