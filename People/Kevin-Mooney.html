Kevin Mooney

UK bassist, vocalist, and songwriter. Real name: Kevin Paul Mooney. Discogs artist ID: 307830

Also credited as: Black. Moon E., K. Moon E., Moon.E, and related variants.

Overview

Kevin Mooney is best known as the bassist for Adam and the Ants during the Kings of the Wild Frontier period (1980), appearing on the album and associated singles on CBS. After leaving the Ants, he co-founded Wide Boy Awake with John Keogh, releasing a string of singles and a mini-album on RCA (1982–1984). He subsequently joined Max (31) (also known as Lomax), a group also featuring Matthew Ashman, Leslie Winer, John Reynolds, and Tex Axile, releasing on Chrysalis (1987) and ZTT (1991).

Later credits include uncredited bass and guitar on Sinéad O’Connor’s The Lion and the Cobra (1987, Chrysalis/Ensign) and bass on O’Connor’s Sold (1987, Virgin). He is also credited as a performer and contributor on Leslie Winer’s projects (3 Bags Full, 2013). In 1992, he appeared on the Red Dot Records single “Hold On” (as a band member of Silence Running). He contributed to Transglobal’s Witch (1993) and is credited with bass, vocals, and songwriting on the Adam and the Ants retrospective Antbox (2000, Columbia).
