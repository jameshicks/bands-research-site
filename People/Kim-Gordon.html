Kim Gordon

Discogs ID: 187676
Born: Kim Althea Gordon, April 28, 1953, Rochester, NY, USA
Groups: Sonic Youth (co-founder, 1981–2011), Free Kitten, Body-Head, Ciccone Youth, Harry Crews, Glitterbust

American musician, artist, and writer. Co-founder, bassist, and vocalist of Sonic Youth. Married to Thurston Moore 1984–2011. Their separation in 2011 triggered the band’s indefinite hiatus.

Solo Work

  • No Home Record (2019, Matador) — debut solo LP, critically acclaimed
  • The Collective (2024, Matador) — second solo LP

Side Projects

  • Free Kitten — with Julie Cafritz (ex-Pussy Galore (2)); four albums 1992–1997
  • Body-Head — duo with Bill Nace; Coming Apart (2013), The Switch (2018), “come on” e.p. (2023); also Body/Gate/Head (with Michael Morley, 2012) and Body/Dilloway/Head (with Aaron Dilloway, 2021)
  • Glitterbust — duo with Alex Knost
  • Harry Crews
  • Ciccone Youth — SY alter-ego project

Visual Art and Writing

  • Memoir Girl in a Band (2015, Dey Street Books)
  • Visual artist: paintings, sculpture, installations shown internationally

Connections (additional)

  • Hole (2) — co-produced debut LP Pretty on the Inside (1991, Caroline Records) with Don Fleming
