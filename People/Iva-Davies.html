Overview

Iva Davies (born Ivor Arthur Davies, 22 May 1955, Wauchope, New South Wales) is an Australian multi-instrumentalist, songwriter, and producer who founded the band Flowers in the late 1970s — renamed Icehouse in 1981 — which became effectively his sole vehicle, with Davies the only original member still in the project. Across a catalogue stretching back to 1975, he has occupied every principal role: lead vocals, guitar, keyboards, writing, and production. His commercial peak ran through the 1980s on Chrysalis and Regular Records, trading in synth-pop and new wave, and he has continued issuing material on Diva Records into the 2020s. A “Score” credit running through his catalogue signals a strand of film and television composition work alongside his recording career.
