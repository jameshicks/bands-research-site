Alexis Fleisig

Drummer and photographer from Washington, DC. Core member of Girls Against Boys and Soulside (2); also in Edsel, Obits, Bellini (4), and Paramount Styles.

Overview

Fleisig came up in DC post-hardcore as drummer in Soulside (2) alongside Scott McCloud and Johnny Temple. He joined Girls Against Boys with those bandmates around 1990, serving as the band’s sole drummer through their entire run (1990–present). His photography appears in GVSB sleeve credits, including design and photography on Freak*on*ica (1998) and You Can’t Fight What You Can’t See (2002).

Outside GVSB, Fleisig drummed for Moby on the 1996 album Animal Rights. He is a listed member of Edsel (the DC post-hardcore band whose frontman Sohrab Habibion also toured with GVSB in 2002). He joined the Italian band Bellini (4) in 2002, whose output was produced by Steve Albini. He later co-founded the supergroup Obits with Drive Like Jehu frontman Rick Froberg, Greg Simpson, Sohrab Habibion, and Scott Gursky. He was also briefly a member of Edsel from 2012 to 2013.

Fleisig co-founded Paramount Styles with Scott McCloud in 2007; the band released three albums on Cycle/Konkurrent Records (Netherlands) and Silver Rocket (Czech Republic). He also plays in Lünch Meat with McCloud. (Source: Wikipedia)
