Oct 06, 20261 min read

Steve Hauschildt

Discogs ID: 1121448

Cleveland-based synthesist; founding member of Emeralds (2); prolific solo artist.

Left Emeralds in January 2013 weeks after McGuire. Also a member of Mental Prism (2007, five-piece Drone/Experimental collective). Solo discography includes S/H (Editions Mego, 2013; eMEGO 178), as well as Kranky (Tragedy & Geometry 2011, Sequitur 2012, Where All Is Fled 2015, Strands 2016) and Ghostly International (Dissolvi 2018, Nonlin 2019). Alias: The Celestial Sprinkler (earlier recordings). Active through 2025 (Simul Records).
