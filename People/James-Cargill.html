James Cargill

Active: 1995–present

Birmingham-based musician, bassist, and producer. Co-founder of Broadcast alongside Trish Keenan. After Keenan’s death in January 2011, Cargill has continued to operate the Broadcast name and archive, contributing to Warp reissues and archival releases through 2024. Also active in Children Of Alice (with Roj Stevens and Julian House) from 2017, and as a session contributor to albums by Deerhunter and others.

Early Career and Broadcast Formation

Cargill met Trish Keenan at a 1960s psychedelic revival club in Birmingham. Their shared musical interests led to the formation of Pan Am Flight Bag, which played two gigs before they reconceived the project as Broadcast in 1995 alongside Roj Stevens (keyboards), Tim Felton (guitar), and Steve Perkins (2) (drums).

Broadcast’s first releases were on Duophonic Super 45s (the Stereolab-run label): Living Room / Phantom 7” (1996) and The Book Lovers EP (1996). This placed them alongside Stereolab and associated artists at the intersection of electronic pop and vintage-library reference.

Broadcast: Warp Era (1997–2010)

Broadcast signed to Warp Records and released Work And Non Work (1997), a compilation of their early recordings that established their aesthetic: BBC Radiophonic Workshop-influenced electronics, references to Ennio Morricone and John Barry, and Keenan’s distinctive singing.

Tender Buttons (2005)

The group contracted to a duo for this album — Keenan and Cargill as sole credited performers, producers, engineers, and writers. Guest appearances are minimal: Roj Stevens on feedback (“Black Cat”), Tim Felton on fuzz guitar (“Corporeal”). Julian House provided artwork uncredited.

Broadcast & The Focus Group (2009–2010)

Broadcast collaborated with Julian House’s project The Focus Group on two releases: the mini-album Investigate Witch Cults Of The Radio Age (Warp WARPCDD189, 2009) and the 7” Familiar Shapes And Noises (Ghost Box GBX704, 2010). These connected Broadcast explicitly to the Ghost Box aesthetic — a link already implicit through House’s long visual association with the band.

Berberian Sound Studio (2013)

Broadcast’s score for Peter Strickland’s horror film was recorded before Keenan’s death in January 2011, and released on Warp posthumously (WARPLP233). The score draws on Italian giallo film music traditions. Notable contributions beyond Broadcast:

After Keenan’s Death (2011–present)

Children Of Alice (2017)

A trio formed by Cargill, Roj Stevens, and Julian House as a tribute to Trish Keenan — the group’s name references Lewis Carroll’s Alice in Wonderland, a major influence on Keenan (especially Jonathan Miller’s 1966 BBC TV adaptation). Released one album: Children Of Alice (Warp WARPLP279, 2017), four extended tracks of abstract/ambient electronic music. All three credited equally as writers, producers, and engineers.
