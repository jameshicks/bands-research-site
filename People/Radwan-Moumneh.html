Radwan Moumneh

Radwan Moumneh

Real name: Radwan Ghazi Moumneh
Also credited as: R. Moumneh, R.G.M., Radwan Ghazi, Radwan Ire, Radwan G. Moumneh
Origin: Montreal, Quebec, Canada (Lebanese-Canadian)
Active: 1996–present
Roles: Recording engineer, mixer, producer, vocalist, multi-instrumentalist (buzuk, zurna, guitar, synthesizers, harp)

Radwan Ghazi Moumneh is a Montreal-based Lebanese-Canadian recording engineer, producer, and musician, best known as the sole official member of Jerusalem In My Heart, an experimental project fusing Arabic vocal traditions with electronic textures. He co-founded the Hotel2Tango recording studio in Montreal — a hub for the city’s experimental and post-rock scene — and has engineered, mixed, or produced well over one hundred releases spanning experimental, free jazz, post-rock, metal, and Arabic music. His production work is inseparable from Constellation Records Records’ catalog, where he has been a central studio presence for two decades.

Career

Early Years: Ire and Hardcore (1996–2004)

Moumneh’s earliest Discogs credits are as a performer and engineer with the hardcore/post-hardcore band Ire (2) (credited as “Radwan Ire Moumneh”), active from the mid-1990s and releasing material on Fetus Records, Schema Records, and Scorched Earth Policy. During this period he accumulated early engineering work — recording and mixing hardcore records for bands including Born Dead Icons (New Scream Industry, 1999, Not On Label), Left For Dead (Part Of Something Larger Than Ourselves, 1999, Deranged Records), and various international acts on Scorched Earth Policy and similar labels.

He also performed in The Black Hand during this era, and later became a member of Cursed (2), the Montreal metallic-hardcore band where Chris Colohan and others recorded.

Hotel2Tango and the Constellation Circle (2006–2015)

By the mid-2000s Moumneh had co-founded Hotel2Tango (Discogs label ID: 278346), a studio in Montreal that became the primary recording home for the Constellation Records scene. His first prominent Constellation credits arrive in 2006–2007: recording and mixing Black Ox Orkestar’s Nisht Azoy (2006, CST069) and Feu Thérèse’s Ça Va Cogner (2007). From this point onward his name appears on virtually every Constellation release for the following decade.

Key Constellation credits from this phase:

  • 2008: A Silver Mt. Zion (13 Blues For Thirteen Moons, Recorded By); Clues (Clues, Recorded By & Mixed By, 2009)
  • 2009: Vic Chesnutt (At The Cut, Assistant Engineer); Eric Chenaux (Against The Day, Recorded By + Voice, Lyrics By); Efrim Menuck (Plays High Gospel, various); Clues (Clues, 2009)
  • 2010: Sam Shalabi / Land Of Kush (Warm Weather With Ryan Driver, Recorded By); Godspeed You Black Emperor! (Kollaps Tradixionales, Recorded By); Eric Chenaux (Monogamy, Recorded By, Mixed By, Voice & Verse, Cassettes)
  • 2011: Matana Roberts (Coin Coin Chapter One, Recorded By); Godspeed You Black Emperor! work continues
  • 2012: Shalabi Effect (Guitar & Voice, Producer, Recorded By, Mixed By); Fly Pan Am (Devastates, Mixed By); Eric Chenaux (Brûlez Ce Coeur, Mixed By)
  • 2013–2014: Land Of Kush (The Big Mango, Recorded By & Mixed By); multiple Constellation releases

He also performed as a guest or contributor outside Constellation during this period, appearing on Grizzly Bear’s Shields (2012, additional vocals on Sound Kapital compilation, Sub Pop) and lending bouzouki/engineering to projects in the wider international experimental orbit.

Jerusalem In My Heart (2013–present)

In 2013 Moumneh launched Jerusalem In My Heart as a vehicle for his own solo work, releasing on Constellation. The project weaves processed Arabic vocals (Moumneh’s own voice), buzuk (or buzuq — a long-necked lute), zurna, modular synthesizers, and 16mm film projections into a live and recorded practice rooted in Lebanese maqam traditions.

Expanded Production Work (2015–2025)

Moumneh’s engineering and production work extended well beyond Constellation through this period. He recorded and mixed albums for artists across genre lines:

  • Matana Roberts: Coin Coin Chapter Two (2013), Chapter Three (2015), Chapter Four (2019), Chapter Five (2023) — a sustained multi-release relationship on Constellation
  • Big Brave: Ardor (2017, Southern Lord) — recorded and mixed
  • Fly Pan Am: C’est ça (2019) and Frontera (2021) — produced and mixed, both Constellation
  • Jessica Moss: Pools Of Light (2017) and Phosphenes (2021) — Constellation
  • Sarah Pagé: Trouble Fête (via Constellation)
  • Suuns: Felt (Sun Coming Down, 2015, Constellation) — Mixed By
  • Tim Brady and various Québécois artists
  • Oiseaux-Tempête: Tower (2015, BLWBCK)
  • Protomartyr: Relatives In Descent (Renegade Breakdown credits, Ninja Tune, 2020)
  • Arabic/Middle East-oriented releases: Mazen Kerbaj, Raed Yassin, Mashrou’ Leila’s Raasük (2013, Lebanon), TARAB (2018, Sub Rosa), and appearances with the Praed Orchestra

He has also appeared as performer or instrumentalist on numerous releases — playing buzuk, zurna, synthesizers, or contributing vocals as a guest across the Montreal scene and internationally.
