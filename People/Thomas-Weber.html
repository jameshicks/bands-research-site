Thomas Weber

Discogs ID: 488983
Origin: Germany
Website: studiokindacloudy.com
Groups: Kammerflimmer Kollektief, Kissin Cousins, The Schwarzenbach

German musician, composer, and producer. Founder and constant through-line of Kammerflimmer Kollektief, the long-running ensemble whose catalog spans jazz, drone, post-rock, and chamber electronics. Weber’s pre-Kammerflimmer work as an executive producer of jazz home-video documentation suggests a production background before the group was founded; from the mid-2000s the Kammerflimmer records are increasingly his compositional statements, shaped around rotating collaborators.

Early Career (1991–1996)

Appeared as guitarist and pianist on Lautermuschel (1991, L’Age D’Or). Executive producer on jazz concert videos: a Billy Cobham / Louie Bellson production (1991), the Gil Evans Orchestra live video, and World of Rhythm Live (both 2002). Kammerflimmer Kollektief’s first release, Untitled, appeared on Payola in 1996.

Kammerflimmer Kollektief

Founded 1997. The lineup has shifted across three decades; Weber has been the constant.
