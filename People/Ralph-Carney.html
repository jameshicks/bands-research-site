Ralph Carney

Discogs ID: 216657 Saxophonist, multi-reed player. Akron, Ohio, USA. Name variations: Carney, R. Carney, Ralf Carney, Thee Mighty Ralph Carney, Hudson, Mr. Ralph Carney.

Overview

One of the most connected figures in the Akron punk/new-wave scene. Core member of both Tin Huey and The Waitresses — two of the defining bands of the late-1970s Akron sound. Solo track “Closet Bears” appears on Punk 45 - Burn Rubber City Burn! (Akron Ohio).

Beyond the Akron scene, Carney became a prolific session player and sideman across jazz, avant-garde, rock, and film scoring. He was a recurring member of Tom Waits’s live and studio band from the mid-1980s through the 2010s, and contributed to albums by the B-52’s, Allen Ginsberg, Jim Carroll, They Might Be Giants, and many others.

Wide range of groups across career including Kamikaze Ground Crew, Oranj Symphonette, Labatomy, Clarinet Thing, Nive Nielsen & The Deer Children, Swollen Monkeys.
