The Twilight Zoners

Carmichael founded the Twilight Zoners in London in March 1978 with guitarist John McGowan (aka Johnny Limbo). The band’s label ZIP Records (cat. prefix ZIP) stood for “Zoners in Plastic” — his own imprint.

See Twilight Zoners for full details.

The White Brothers (1980–1982)

After the Twilight Zoners wound down, Carmichael and Kevin Evans formed the White Brothers — described as “rapping over drum machines and bass,” influenced by the Last Poets and Grandmaster Flash. Active 1980–1982. This places them squarely in the earliest wave of UK hip-hop/proto-rap, contemporaneous with the Sugarhill Gang’s UK breakthrough and well before the mid-1980s hip-hop explosion.

No releases have been documented online.

Cardiff Connections

Carmichael grew up in Cardiff and witnessed the “Anarchy in the UK” tour there in December 1976 — his entry point into punk. He managed the Cardiff band The Tax Exiles in late 1977 before leaving for London. No documented musical connection to the Cardiff post-punk acts (Young Marble Giants, Reptile Ranch, Datblygu) — by the time those bands were active he was already in London.
