Meredith Monk

Composer, vocalist, performer, director. Full name: Meredith Jane Monk. Born November 20, 1942, New York City. Discogs ID: 245341.

A central figure in American avant-garde performance since the mid-1960s. Voice is her primary compositional medium — she developed an extended vocal technique using the voice as an instrument free from text, exploring timbre, register, and breath as musical material. Work integrates music, movement, film, and installation; she coined the term “interdisciplinary performance.” Rooted in the Judson Dance Theater / Judson Church circle of the 1960s (alongside Yvonne Rainer, Trisha Brown, Steve Paxton).

Part of the broader Downtown Music Scene fabric through her long presence at The Kitchen and Brooklyn Academy of Music. Recorded primarily for ECM New Series from the 1980s: Dolmen Music (ECM, 1981), Turtle Dreams (ECM, 1983), Book of Days (ECM, 1990), Mercy (ECM, 2002). Julius Eastman appeared on Dolmen Music — voice and percussion — connecting the Ashley/SUNY Buffalo strand to Monk’s world.

Also leads Meredith Monk And Vocal Ensemble (ID 867041) for larger-scale works. Member of Aunt Jamina And The United Pancakes (ID 1382727 — likely a collective project).
