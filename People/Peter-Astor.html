Peter Astor

Discogs ID: 159996. Born 13 August 1960, Hammersmith, London. British singer-songwriter and academic. Formerly of The Loft and The Weather Prophets (both Creation Records acts, mid-1980s). Currently a senior lecturer at the University of Westminster teaching music. (Wikipedia, as of 2026-07-02)

Sang “Waking Up” on Piano Magic’s Low Birth Weight (1999, Rocket Girl).

Solo Career (post-Weather Prophets)

The Weather Prophets disbanded in 1989. Astor immediately continued as a solo artist on Creation, then moved through several labels across three decades.

The Wisdom of Harry (1997–2003)

A side project active from approximately 1997. Pre-Matador: Staying in with the Wisdom of Harry 10” EP (Lissy’s, 1998). Early singles also released on Static Caravan, Wurlitzer Jukebox, and Astor’s own Faux-Lux label. Signed to Matador Records for three releases:
