Thierry Amar

Thierry Amar

Oct 23, 20268 min read

Thierry Amar

Discogs ID: 359596 Born/Based: Montreal, Quebec, Canada Instruments: Double bass, contrabass, electric bass, cello, violin, piano, vocals

Montreal-based bassist and multi-instrumentalist active from the mid-1990s to the present. A foundational member of Godspeed You Black Emperor! (joined 1997) and a core performer on the debut of Esmerine (2003). Also co-founder of Black Ox Orkestar (2000) and A Silver Mt. Zion (1999). In parallel, he has functioned as a producer, engineer, and mixer throughout the Montreal Constellation scene, working behind the board for many of the label’s key releases over the last twenty-five years. He co-runs Hotel2Tango, the recording studio, practice space, and performance venue in Montreal that serves as the hub of the Constellation network.

Biography by Era / Project

Mid-1990s — Early Montreal Underground

Amar’s first documented Discogs credit is as bassist on The Psychotramps (1996, Not On Label), placing him in the Montreal underground before the Constellation scene coalesced. By 1997 he had joined the expanding GY!BE collective, credited on their debut release F♯ A♯ ∞ (1997, Constellation).

Late 1990s–2002 — Godspeed You! Black Emperor Core Period

Amar is credited on all the major GY!BE recordings through the original run, playing bass guitar or contrabass across:

  • F♯ A♯ ∞ (1997) — bass guitar
  • Slow Riot For New Zerø Kanada E.P. (1999, Kranky)
  • Lift Yr. Skinny Fists Like Antennas To Heaven! (2000, Kranky) — double-LP peak of the first period
  • Yanqui U.X.O. (2002) — final record before the long hiatus

During this era he also began recording and mixing work: credits on A Silver Mt. Zion’s Sédatifs En Fréquences Et Sillons (2000, Constellation) — “Recorded By, Mixed By” — mark the start of his parallel engineering career.

1999–2000 — Co-founding A Silver Mt. Zion and Black Ox Orkestar

Efrim Menuck co-founded A Silver Mt. Zion in 1999; Amar was part of the ensemble from the outset, credited on contrabass and electric bass across their entire Constellation discography through at least 2014’s Fuck Off Get Free. He also co-founded Black Ox Orkestar in 2000, contributing contrabass (and later recording and mixing) to both of their Constellation albums: Ver Tanzt? (2004) and Nisht Azoy (2006).

2003–2006 — Esmerine Debut and Guest Credits

Amar contributed contrabass and electric bass to Esmerine’s debut album If Only A Sweet Surrender To The Nights To Come Be True (2003, Resonant). He is listed as a performer on that release alongside core members Bruce Cawdron and Rebecca Foon, though he does not appear in the Discogs member listing for Esmerine and is absent from their subsequent studio albums as a performer. In 2011 he contributed mixing and recording to their La Lechuza (Constellation).

In 2004 he played acoustic bass as a guest on Sam Shalabi’s Shalabi Effect album Pink Abyss (Alien8 Recordings), in a Montreal supergroup session that also included Sophie Trudeau (violin), Gabriel Levine (clarinet), and Charles Spearin (trumpet).

2004 — Arcade Fire / Funeral

Amar served as assistant recording engineer on Arcade Fire’s Funeral (2004, Merge Records), recording at Hotel2Tango alongside Howard Bilerman. This was an early high-profile overlap between the Constellation network and the wider Montreal indie scene.

2007 — Vic Chesnutt’s North Star Deserter

Co-produced and co-recorded (alongside Efrim Menuck and Guy Picciotto) Vic Chesnutt’s album North Star Deserter (2007, Constellation), with A Silver Mt. Zion as the backing band. This is one of the highest-profile production credits in his discography.

2008–present — Lhasa, ASMZ Late Period, GY!BE Return

Mixed and recorded Lhasa de Sela’s self-titled album Lhasa (2008, Audiogram), one of the most prominent mainstream Quebecois releases he’s touched. Continued as contrabass/bass player and co-recording engineer on A Silver Mt. Zion through Fuck Off Get Free We Pour Light On Everything (2014) and as a core GY!BE member through all their post-reunion recordings: ‘Allelujah! Don’t Bend Ascend (2012), Asunder, Sweet And Other Distress (2015), Luciferian Towers (2017), G_d’s Pee At State’s End! (2021), and No Title As Of 13 February 2024 28,340 Dead (2024).

On the 2012 GY!BE return ‘Allelujah!, he is credited as Band, Electric Bass, Contrabass, Cello — and also as co-recorder and co-mixer on sides C/D.

Beyond GY!BE he continued a busy sideman and production schedule: contrabass on Matana Roberts’s Coin Coin Chapter One (2011, Constellation), bass on Coin Coin Chapter Four: Memphis (2019), contrabass on William Tyler’s Ardor (2017, Southern Lord), Gave In Rest by Ryley Walker (2018, Ba Da Bing!), A Gaze Among Them by William Tyler (2019, Southern Lord), and contrabass/double bass on Light Falls by Ben Frost (2016, Thrill Jockey).

Also a member of: Land Of Kush, Balai Mécanique, Molasses (2).
