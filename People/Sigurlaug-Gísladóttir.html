Sigurlaug Gísladóttir

Discogs ID: 1807776 (real name); solo alias ID: 1250916 (Mr. Silla)
Alias: Mr. Silla
Active: 2006–present

Icelandic singer, songwriter, musician, and visual artist. Member of múm (from Go Go Smear The Poison Ivy, 2007); also in Fræ. Releases solo work under the alias Mr. Silla on Icelandic labels Rafraf, 12 Tónar, and Making Records.

Mr. Silla Solo Career

Foxbite (2007, Rafraf)

Debut album, co-credited to Mr. Silla & Mongoose (4) (Magnús Skarphedinsson). 15 tracks; electronic/IDM/downtempo. Artwork (exterior) by Sigurlaug herself. Additional players: Magnús Trygvason Eliassen (drums on tracks 7, 10, 13, 15), Eiríkur Orri Ólafsson (brass on track 15); mastered by Styrmir Hauksson; interior artwork by Korka.

Track listing: “Gua,” “Noodlefeet,” “Raggedypack,” “I Don’t,” “Wanna Million,” “How Do You,” “Ten Foot Bear,” “Save It For Later,” “The Devil Has A Prosthetic Leg,” “Foxbyte,” “I’ve Been Hit,” “Do Not Know,” “I Only Want Chicken,” “Say,” “Organ Deviltry.”

Mr. Silla (2015, 12 Tónar, 12T068)

Self-titled second album under the alias. 9 tracks; indie pop / trip hop / downtempo. Produced by Mike Lindsay and Sigurlaug Gísladóttir. Mixed by Friðfinnur Sigurðsson; mastered by Alan Douches. Artwork by Kevin Bourland; handwriting by Halldór Örn Ragnarsson; design assistance by Klara Arnalds.

Guest credits: Tyler Ludwick (guitar, tracks 2, 3, 9); Ingibjörg Elsa Turchi (bass, track 5); Sam Genders (lyrics, track 4 “Breathe”); Una Stigsdóttir (lyrics, track 1 “Dressed Lightly”).

Track listing: “Dressed Lightly,” “Reach For Me,” “Holding On,” “Breathe,” “Baobab,” “Down The Hill,” “One Step,” “Cold,” “I Want All.”

Hands On Hands (2019, Making Records, MR001)

Third album. 8 tracks; vinyl LP; no individual session credits listed in Discogs.

Track listing: “Water,” “Look At The Light,” “Underneath,” “Coal,” “Butter On It,” “Hands On Hands,” “Gloria,” “Naruto (Say You Wanna Run Away).“

múm

Joined the Reykjavík experimental group múm from their fifth studio album onward. múm was founded 1997 by Gunnar Örn Tynes and Örvar Þóreyjarson Smárason; earlier members include Kristín Anna Valtýsdóttir and Gyða Valtýsdóttir.
