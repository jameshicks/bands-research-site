Simon Lord

Vocalist, guitarist, drum programmer, producer. Real name Simon William Lord. UK. Discogs ID: 581124. Aliases: Garden (2), Lord Skywave.

Three distinct projects: Simian (2000–2005, Manchester), The Black Ghosts (2006–2013, with Theo Keating), and Roberts & Lord (2011, with Rafter Roberts on Asthmatic Kitty). The first made him one of the figures behind a song that defined mid-2000s French electro; the last paired him improbably with a San Diego post-rock engineer on a synth-pop LP.

Simian (2000–2005)

Lord co-founded Simian in Manchester around 2000 with Alex MacNaghten (bass), James Shaw (keyboards, drum programming), and James Ford (drums). The group recorded and self-produced, releasing on Source Records and Astralwerks. Lord played vocals, lead guitar, and drum programming.

Their catalog runs:

  • Drop And Roll 7” (2000, Not On Label)
  • Watch It Glow (2000, Source)
  • Chemistry Is What We Are LP (2001, Source / Astralwerks)
  • One Dimension (2001, Source)
  • The Wisp EP (2001, Source)
  • Mr. Crow (2002, Source)
  • Never Be Alone (2002, Source) — later remixed and released on Ed Banger Records (2003) and International Deejay Gigolo Records (2004)
  • “We Are Your Friends” (2002, Astralwerks) — the signal track
  • La Breeze (2003, Source)

“We Are Your Friends” was a modest release on its original run. Justice remixed it in 2006, renaming their version “We Are Your Friends” and using it as the opener of their debut album † (Cross, Ed Banger/Virgin, 2007). The Justice remix became one of the defining tracks of the Ed Banger era of French electro-house; the Simian original gained retroactive exposure from it. The 2006 reissue cycle on 10 Records and Source capitalized on the remix’s success.

Simian disbanded in 2005. Ford and Shaw regrouped as Simian Mobile Disco, going on to become one of the most prolific UK electronic production outfits of the 2010s. Lord went a different direction.

The Black Ghosts (2006–2013)

Lord formed The Black Ghosts with Theo Keating (also known as Fake Blood; previously one half of The Wiseguys). The duo released on Southern Fried Records. Their sound shifted toward electro-pop and indie electronic, further from Simian’s live-band rock feel.

Discography:

  • Face (2006, Southern Fried)
  • Anyway You Choose To Give It (2007, Southern Fried)
  • The Repetition Kills You (2007, Southern Fried)
  • The Black Ghosts LP (2008, Southern Fried)
  • When Animals Stare LP (2011, Southern Fried)
  • Forgetfulness EP (2013, Southern Fried)

The Twilight connection: “Full Moon” appeared on the Twilight soundtrack (2008, Atlantic/Chop Shop), giving them their widest US exposure.

Roberts & Lord (2011)

See Roberts & Lord.

Lord teamed with San Diego’s Rafter Roberts for two releases on Asthmatic Kitty Records: Eponymous LP (AKR086) and Covers EP (AKR326). Both credited as Electronic/Pop, Electro/Synth-pop. The connection point between a UK electronic act and a San Diego post-rock engineer is unclear from Discogs — Asthmatic Kitty is the obvious bridge but how they found each other is not documented here.
