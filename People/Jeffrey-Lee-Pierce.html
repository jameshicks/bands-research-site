Jeffrey Lee Pierce

Singer, songwriter, guitarist. Born June 27, 1958; died March 31, 1996. Discogs ID: 262749.

Born: June 27, 1958
Died: March 31, 1996 (Oxford, England — stroke, cirrhosis complications, aged 37)
Discogs ID: 262749

Founding vocalist, guitarist, and primary songwriter of The Gun Club. Also a founding member of The Red Lights (2) before forming the Gun Club. Other band associations: Tex & The Horseheads, Soulsuckers, The JLP Sessions Project. Solo releases on Statik Records 1985–1986: Flamingo (1985, catno STAB 5), Wildweed LP (1985, catno CDST 25, 13 pressings), Love & Desperation (1985). 24 total solo releases.

Name variations: J.L. Pierce, Jeffrey Lee, Jeff Pierce, Ramblin’ J.L.P.

Pierce co-produced the Death Party EP (1983) and wrote or arranged the vast majority of the Gun Club’s original material, including selecting the Robert Johnson and Tommy Johnson covers on Fire Of Love. He relocated to Europe (London, then Geneva) in the mid-1980s, where he died in 1996.

Co-credited on I Knew Buffalo Bill (1987, Flicknife Records) alongside Epic Soundtracks, Nikki Sudden, Rowland S. Howard, and Jeremy Gluck.

Jeffrey Lee Pierce Sessions Project

Posthumous collaborative project initiated by Cypress Grove after Pierce’s death. Cypress Grove found a cassette of three unfinished songs and recruited collaborators via Myspace; the project expanded to include Nick Cave, Debbie Harry, Lydia Lunch, Mark Lanegan, Isobel Campbell, Jim Sclavunos, Mick Harvey, Kid Congo Powers, Iggy Pop, Thurston Moore, Warren Ellis, and Barry Adamson, among others. Four albums released on Glitterhouse Records, 2010–2023 — per Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Lee_Pierce).
