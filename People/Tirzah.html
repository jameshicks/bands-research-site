Tirzah

Discogs artist ID: 508796 Real name: Tirzah Mastin (also credited as Tazir Mastin; performs as Taz) Active: 2013–present Origin: England, UK Labels: Greco-Roman (2013–2015), Domino Records (2018–present) Key collaborator: Mica Levi (producer, co-writer, mixer)

English singer and songwriter. Tirzah emerged through London’s Greco-Roman label with minimal electronic singles in 2013, then built a close creative partnership with Mica Levi (Micachu) that defined her two critically acclaimed Domino albums. Her work sits at the intersection of leftfield R&B, minimal electronic, and indie pop — spare production, intimate vocals, long textures.

Biography by Era

2013–2015: Greco-Roman Singles

Tirzah debuted in 2013 on Greco-Roman with the 12” single I’m Not Dancing, which included a dub remix by Greco-Roman Soundsystem. The single drew notice for its low-key, bass-forward aesthetic. She followed it with two digital EPs on the same label: No Romance (2014) and Make It Up (2015), both five-track digital releases in a bass music / house-adjacent vein. The Greco-Roman period credits no additional producers on Discogs — the production team behind these releases is not documented there.

In 2015 she appeared on a collaborative single Thinking Of You alongside Micachu (Mica Levi), Mumdance, and South African producer Nozinja, released on The Vinyl Factory.

2016: Guest Features

In 2016 Tirzah appeared as a featured vocalist on Baauer’s album Aa (LuckyMe), alongside a large cast that included Future, M.I.A., Pusha T, G-Dragon, Rustie, Leikeli47, Novelist, and TT The Artist.

2018: Devotion (Domino)

Devotion was Tirzah’s debut album proper, released on Domino Records (cat. WIGLP394). All music written and produced by Mica Levi (credited as Micachu on this release), with co-writing by Tirzah throughout. The album was mixed jointly by Kwes. and Micachu, and mastered by Christian Wright. The title track “Devotion” features Coby Sey on vocals and co-writing credit — the only guest on the record.

The album is eleven tracks, predominantly in the 3–4 minute range, across Electronic / Funk/Soul / Pop genres with styles tagged Contemporary R&B, Indie Pop, and Leftfield.

2021: Colourgrade (Domino)

Colourgrade (Domino, cat. WIGCD476) marked a shift in the production credit from Micachu to the Mica Levi alias, confirming the long-running partnership across her solo work. Mica Levi produced nine of the ten tracks and mixed them; Dean Blunt and Kwake Bass co-produced track 4 (“Recipe”). Kwes. mixed tracks 4 and 9. Mastered by Heba Kadry.

Coby Sey co-wrote five tracks (2, 3, 7, 8, 9) and features on track 3 (“Hive Mind”). Dean Blunt and Giles Kwakeulati King-Ashong (= Kwake Bass) co-wrote track 4. Design by Leah Walker, Matthew Cooper (2), and Tirzah herself; photography by Leah Walker.

2022: Highgrade (Domino)

Highgrade (Domino, cat. WIG515D) is a remix album of Colourgrade tracks, released digitally. Remixers include Speakers Corner Quartet, Lafawndah, Anja Ngozi, Arca, Fauzia, Actress, Loraine James, Wu-Lu, Still House Plants, and TØNE.

2023: Trip9love…??? (Domino)

Trip9love…??? is an eleven-track digital album (Domino, no physical cat. no.) with all production by Mica Levi. Shorter tracks, similar leftfield R&B territory. Only additional credits listed on Discogs are Mica Levi as producer.
