Tobi Vail

Drummer, guitarist, vocalist, and writer. Realname: Tobi Celeste Vail (born July 20, 1969, Auburn, Washington). Discogs artist ID: 438738.

Overview

Tobi Vail is a central figure in the Olympia, Washington DIY/punk scene, best known as the founding drummer of Bikini Kill — the band credited alongside Bratmobile with starting the riot grrrl movement. She coined the spelling “grrl” and launched the feminist zine Jigsaw in 1989, which ran in print through 1999 and migrated online. She worked at Kill Rock Stars from 1992 to 2011 (part-time 1992–1997, mail order department 1998–2011).

Her musical career began in the mid-1980s with The Go Team, a K Records project she co-founded with Calvin Johnson, where she first played alongside Billy Karren. After early stints with Some Velvet Sidewalk, she co-founded Bikini Kill in October 1990 with Kathleen Hanna and Kathi Wilcox. During the Bikini Kill years she also ran the Frumpies (1992–2000), a looser collective with overlapping membership. Post-Bikini Kill, she founded Spider And The Webs (2004), drummed with The Old Haunts (2006–2008), and formed girlSperm / gSp (c. 2015) with Layla Gibbon and Marissa Magic. Her most recent primary-credit release is M.O.D.S. (2022) with Morgan & The Organ Donors on K Records.

Wikipedia (offline snapshot) notes that Vail also co-organized the first Ladyfest (Olympia, 2000) with her sister Maggie Vail, Allison Wolfe, Cat Power, and members of Sleater-Kinney. Her relationship with Tim Armstrong inspired the Rancid song “Olympia, WA” (…And Out Come the Wolves, 1995). Kurt Cobain played guitar on the Go Team’s 1989 “Scratch It Out / Bikini Twilight” single (credited as “Kurdt Kobain”); the pair briefly dated in 1990, and Kathleen Hanna’s “Kurt smells like Teen Spirit” graffiti on Cobain’s wall — a reference to Teen Spirit deodorant that Vail used — directly inspired the Nirvana song title.

Writing & Publishing

  • Jigsaw zine, 1989–1999 (print); online continuation from 2001. Issues 1–7 in print; issue 8 online (2003). 1990s issues archived at Harvard University as of 2013.
  • Freelance music criticism: NPR, Artforum, The Believer, Punk Planet, Maximum Rock-N-Roll, eMusic (monthly column as of Wikipedia’s last update)
  • Published by The Feminist Press in Pussy Riot: A Punk Prayer for Freedom and The Riot Grrrl Collection
  • Co-organized Ladyfest (Olympia, 2000) with sister Maggie Vail, Allison Wolfe, Cat Power, and Sleater-Kinney members; performed at Ladyfest as “Frenchie and the German Girls” with Maggie
  • Graduated from Evergreen State College, 2009
