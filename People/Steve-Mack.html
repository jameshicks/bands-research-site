Steve Mack

Vocalist, audio engineer, and producer from Seattle, Washington. Discogs artist ID: 220077. Best known as the sole North American member of That Petrol Emotion, the Derry, Northern Ireland post-punk band he fronted from 1985 until their dissolution in 1994 and again during a 2008–2010 reunion. Pre-TPE he played in The Thrown Ups in Seattle. After TPE disbanded he maintained an active production and engineering practice centred on the early-1990s London indie/alternative scene, recording and producing for artists on Clawfist, Deceptive, Wiiija, and Cherry Red. By the early 2000s he had returned to the Pacific Northwest, where he became involved in streaming media and later sang in Stag (6) (Seattle, 2010–2017) and in the mid-1990s projects Anodyne (6) and Marfa Lights. He owned and operated Bang Bang Studios (Discogs label ID: 293557).

Name variations on record: Mack, S. Mack, S.Mack, Steve, Steve “Dreads” Mack.

Overview

Mack’s career falls into three distinct phases. The first (1985–1994) is dominated by That Petrol Emotion, where he was the band’s vocalist and by the Koogat era was also contributing as engineer on their own recordings. The second phase (1991–1995) overlaps with and extends beyond TPE: a prolific run of production and engineering work in London, primarily at Clawfist-adjacent studios, covering indie-punk, shoegaze-adjacent, and post-grunge acts. The third phase (from circa 1996) sees him in the Pacific Northwest — songwriting and recording with Anodyne (6) and Marfa Lights, then from 2010 as vocalist of Stag (6).

A notable feature of his engineering period is the company he kept: two sessions with Stereolab (the Lo Boob Oscilator Sub Pop 7” and the Crumb Duck Clawfist split with Nurse With Wound, both 1993), a co-production credit alongside Jason Pierce (The Spectrum Zero, 1992), and a drum-programming and production credit on The Membranes’ To Slay The Rock-Pig (1989). He frequently co-engineered with Geoffrey Perrin.

Band Memberships

That Petrol Emotion (1985–1994, reunion 2008–2010)

Discogs artist ID: 92580. Formed in Derry from the ashes of The Undertones (O’Neill brothers). Mack was recruited from Seattle as vocalist, the only non-Irish member. The band released five studio albums and extensive singles output across Demon Records, Polydor, Virgin, and their own Koogat imprint before splitting in 1994. A retrospective compilation, Final Flame (Fire, Detonation & Sublime Chaos), appeared in 2000 on Essential! Records; Mack sang on it and took a mixed-by and written-by credit (track 14).

Members alongside Mack: Damian O’Neill (guitar/bass, Discogs: 221886), John O’Neill (guitar 1985–1988, Discogs: 310797), Réamann Ó Gormain (guitar, Discogs: 549292), Ciaran McLaughlin (drums, Discogs: 527666), Brendan Kelly (bass, Discogs: 388653), John Marchini (bass 1988–1992, Discogs: 288909).

The Thrown Ups (Seattle, pre-1985)

Discogs artist ID: 265404. Seattle noise-punk band on Amphetamine Reptile Records. Members included Steve Turner (Discogs: 112603; later of Mudhoney), Mark McLaughlin (Discogs: 868896), Ed Fotheringham (Discogs: 439985), Leighton Beezer (Discogs: 775503), Scott Schickler (Discogs: 720980). Two releases in Discogs: Smiling Panties (1987) and Melancholy Girlhole Box (1990), both on Amphetamine Reptile. Mack is listed as a member but carries no individual production or songwriting credits on either release.

Anodyne (6) (mid-1990s)

Discogs artist ID: 1080747. US alternative rock group. Members: Harris Thurmond (Discogs: 1080746), James Atkins (Discogs: 589747), Marcus Piña (Discogs: 2040199), Steve Mack. One release: Kiss Of Fire 7” (1996, Summershine, cat Shine-US14 / Shine 51). Both tracks (“Kiss Of Fire” / “Incandescent”) written by Thurmond and Mack. Discogs lists Anodyne (6) as an alias of Marfa Lights.

Marfa Lights (late 1990s)

Discogs artist ID: 3713825. Closely related to Anodyne (6) — Discogs lists them as mutual aliases. Members: Harris Thurmond, Marcus Piña, Steve Mack (James Atkins absent from this lineup). One release: Tensor (1998, Independent Records, INDCD 009), a 10-track CD album released in Ireland. No additional production or engineering credits attached to this release in Discogs.

Stag (6) (2010–2017)

Discogs artist ID: 3292519. Seattle rock group. Core members: Ben London (Discogs: 323618, guitar/vocals, primary songwriter), Rob Dent (Discogs: 1775824, drums), Steve Mack (vocals). Additional players include Pete Everett (bass), Lincoln Barr (guitar/vocals), John Randolph (guitar). Five releases catalogued in Discogs.
