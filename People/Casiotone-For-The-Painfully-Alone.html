Overview

Casiotone For The Painfully Alone is the solo project of Owen Ashworth, built around beats and keyboard-driven lo-fi arrangements spanning synth-pop, indie pop, and experimental electronics. Active from 1998, the project’s output was concentrated in the 2000s — twelve releases across that decade — before tapering to a handful in the 2010s. The catalogue was primarily issued on Tomlab between 2001 and 2013, with later work appearing on Orindal Records. Frequent collaborators include Jherek Bischoff, Jason Quever, and Joseph Suchy.
