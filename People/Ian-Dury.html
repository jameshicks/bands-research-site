Ian Dury And The Music Students

Discogs artist ID: 263016. The Music Students were a group of young musicians assembled for the album 4,000 Weeks’ Holiday (Polydor, January 1984). According to Wikipedia, Polydor suggested Dury work with younger musicians; the Blockheads declined after learning they wouldn’t be paid. The core group:

MemberRole
Michael McEvoyBass, keyboards, synthesizers, brass arranger; lead guitar and drums on select tracks
Merlin Rhys-JonesGuitar; continued co-writing with Dury until Dury’s death
Tag LamcheDrums, percussion
Jamie TalbotSaxophones, clarinet
Ray CooperPercussion

Additional guests on the album included Chaz Jankel (lead guitar), Rico Rodriguez (trombone), Davey Payne (saxophones), Rod Melvin (piano/backing vocals), and Ed Speight (lead guitar). Adam Kidron produced; Barney Bubbles did the design. (Source: Wikipedia, 4,000 Weeks’ Holiday article.)
