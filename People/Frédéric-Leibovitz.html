Frédéric Leibovitz

French record producer, label founder, and music publisher. Discogs artist ID: 394217. Also credited as Terry Lipton (Discogs alias ID: 612305).

Active across five decades: began at Editions Chappell in 1963, moved through copyrighting roles at Barclay and Editions Vogue, then signed acts for Philips (including Aphrodite’s Child, Vangelis, and Alan Stivell). Co-founded Cezame with Jean-Michel Gallois-Montbrun in 1975. Cezame’s rock/experimental sublabel Cobra produced the progressive and RIO recordings most associated with his name, including the sole album by Weidorje (1978). Later co-founded library-music label Koka Media in partnership with RCA, and founded Rhinocéros.

Name Variations

Credited variously as: F. Leibovitz, Frederic H. Leibovitz, Frederic Leibovitz, Frédéric Leibowitz, Frédérick Leibovitz, L. Frederic, Leibovitz, Leibowitz. The alias Terry Lipton (Discogs ID 612305) appears on a handful of credits 1974–1987, mainly writer and producer roles in France and Germany.

Production Career

Early Work — Philips / Pathé / Polydor (1969–1977)

First production credit on record is Musica Spiritualis (Philips, Canada, 1969). Through the early 1970s he produced several French and Spanish releases on Pathé (including singles attributed to acts on the Belter and Telefunken labels). A notable 1977 session was Disco Rhapsody by the Jupiter Sunset Band (Polydor, Canada), a disco/orchestral project featuring Jannick Top on bass alongside British session players.

F.R. David — Words and International Breakthrough (1982–1986)

Leibovitz co-produced and co-arranged the debut album Words (1982) by F.R. David on Carrere, alongside co-producers Jean-Michel Gallois-Montbrun and Daniel Darras. The title track “Words” became a major European hit. He continued as executive-producer or producer across F.R. David’s subsequent Carrere albums through 1986:

Cezame Later Period (2008–2013)

Returned to hands-on production credit for a 2008 Cezame compilation Electro Minimalism (co-produced with Françoise Marchesseau; compiled by Christian Zanési). Also co-produced Silence … On Tourne (2008, Cezame/Iris Music). Held executive-producer credit on Méditerranées (2010, Enja Records) and 334 Distance (2013, Cézame Carte Blanche).

Alias: Terry Lipton

The Discogs alias Terry Lipton (ID: 612305) appears on credits 1974–1987, with songwriter credits on a 1974 release (Summer Ways), a 1975 Philips single, and composer credits on two 1986–1987 Koka Media library releases (Minimal, Eureka). The pseudonym was also used for a 1974 production credit on a Canadian Bellaphon release and a 1984 RCA Media compilation.
