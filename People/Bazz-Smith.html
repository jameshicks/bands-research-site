Bazz Smith

British drummer, percussionist, vocalist, and occasional keyboardist. Also credited as Baz Smith and Bas Smith. Discogs artist ID: 360281.

Core member of Family Fodder (post-punk, early 1980s), and the only member to span four distinct projects across four decades: Killing Floor (2) (British blues-rock, 1969), Family Fodder (1980–2008), Bill Fay Group (archival reissue project, 2005), and The Roustabouts (2) (undated).

Biography

Early Career — Killing Floor (c. 1969)

Smith’s earliest Discogs credit is as drummer and co-writer on Killing Floor (1969), the debut LP by the British blues-rock group Killing Floor (2) on Spark. The band featured vocalist and harpist Bill Thorndycraft, guitarists Mick Clarke (5) (credited as Mike Clarke) and Michael Stewart, keyboardist Lou Martin, and bassist Stuart McDonald. Smith co-wrote tracks B2 and B6. The group recorded a second album, Out Of Uranus (1988, SPM Records), with Smith returning on percussion (credited as Bas Smith). A further studio set, Zero Tolerance (2004, Appaloosa), saw him back on drums, and the live/archival album Rock’n’Roll Gone Mad.. (2012, Rockfold) completed Killing Floor’s documented output with Smith behind the kit.

Family Fodder — Core Years (1980–1983)

By 1980–81, Smith was a core member of Family Fodder, the London-based post-punk collective formed around principal songwriter and multi-instrumentalist Alig (John Pearce). The band’s lineup was fluid — Greatest Hits (1981, Crammed Discs) listed at least four different drummers across its sessions (Mark Doffman, Rick Wilson, Charles Bullen, and Smith) — but Smith and Graham Painting formed the rhythmic core for the 1981 mini-album ScHiZoPhReNiA pArTy !.

ScHiZoPhReNiA pArTy ! (Fresh Records, 1981) was the release where Smith’s contribution was deepest. Credited as “Baz Smith,” he played drums, percussion, and voice alongside Graham Painting (bass, guitar, percussion, voice), Alig (keyboards, voice), and Ian Hill (keyboards, voice), with additional vocals by Lynn Alice. Produced by Chris Mansbridge and Family Fodder collectively, the record is notable for the nine-minute “Dinosaur Sex.” Smith holds shared written-by credits across the album alongside Alig (as Henry Fodder/Fodder), Ian Hill, and John Pearce.

All Styles (Jungle Records, 1983) was the final Family Fodder LP of this era. Smith’s role expanded here — drums, percussion, Casiotone keyboards, and vocals — alongside Felix Fiedorowicz (keyboards, vocals, autoharp, percussion, recorder, bassoon, tapes), Alig (piano, keyboards, bass, vocals, tapes), and Lynn Alice (vocals, Casiotone).

Bill Fay Group — Archival Sessions (2005)

Smith appears on tracks 3, 4, and 17 of Tomorrow Tomorrow And Tomorrow (2005, Durtro/Jnana Records), an archival compilation credited to the Bill Fay Group. The album documents sessions from the early 1970s with singer-songwriter Bill Fay, alongside Bill Stratton (drums, percussion), Rauf Galip (electric bass), and Gary Smith (3) (guitar). Smith’s presence on specific tracks indicates he drummed on select Bill Fay sessions during that era, predating or overlapping with his Killing Floor work.

Family Fodder — Return (2008)

Smith is credited as a performer on More Great Hits! (Jungle Records, 2008), a compilation/retrospective that assembled most of the Family Fodder alumni roster: Alig, Graham Painting, Ian Hill, Dominique Levillain, Felix Fiedorowicz, Mick Hobbs, Judy Carter, Jan Beetlestone, Mark Doffman, Martin Harrison, Naresh Singh Kaul, Rick Wilson, and Remy Laporte.
