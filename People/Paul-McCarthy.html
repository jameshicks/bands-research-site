Paul McCarthy

American visual artist and performer based in Los Angeles. Discogs artist ID: 458636.

Member of Extended Organ and Gobbler alongside Mike Kelley. McCarthy is a major figure in LA-based body art and installation art. His Discogs presence is as a musician/performer in these art-music collectives.
