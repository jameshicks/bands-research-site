Overview

Simon Le Bon is an English musician, songwriter, and lead vocalist born near Bushey, Hertfordshire on 27 October 1958, best known as the frontman and lyricist of Duran Duran. His catalogue spans from the early 1970s through the 2020s, with the bulk of his 1,586 credits concentrated in the 1980s — the era of the band’s commercial peak in synth-pop and new wave — continuing steadily across EMI, Capitol Records, and Parlophone into the present day. Beyond Duran Duran he contributed to the offshoot Arcadia alongside Nick Rhodes and Roger Taylor, and appeared on the Band Aid charity single. His closest creative partners by shared credits are Nick Rhodes, John Taylor, and Roger Taylor (2), reflecting the long-running core of the band.
