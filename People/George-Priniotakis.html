George Priniotakis

Mastering engineer, mixer, producer, and multi-instrumentalist based in Athens, Greece. Real name Γεώργιος Πρινιωτάκης (Georgios Priniotakis). Discogs artist ID: 650562. Active from at least 1993 through 2026.

Also credited under the name variations: G. Priniotakis, Giorgos Priniotakis, Yiorgos Priniotakis, Yorgos Priniotakis, Γ. Πρινιωτάκης, Γιώργος Πρινιωτάκης.

Website: http://www.artracks.gr/the-artrackers/george-priniotakis/

Overview

Priniotakis is a Greek recording engineer and producer whose career spans three decades. His Discogs footprint covers mastering, mixing, recording, and production across a remarkably varied range of artists — from Greek alternative rock and post-punk to indie pop, jazz, world music, ambient, and metal. While his name is perhaps most internationally visible through his mastering work for Keep Shelly in Athens, the breadth of his credits shows a consistent presence in the Athenian independent music scene.

He is also a performer in his own right: a member of the Athens dream-pop / darkwave act Blue Birds Refuse To Fly (with whom he plays guitar, keyboards, and backing vocals) and the earlier Greek alt-rock outfit Wasteland (14). His technical career and performing career have run in parallel since the early 1990s.

Early Career and Performing Work (1993–2007)

Priniotakis appears as vocalist and guitarist on the 1993 Wasteland (14) EP Dream Drops and the 1994 single Moon On Fire / Lonesome Yesterdays, both released on Pegasus Records (4). Wasteland (14) also counts Alkis Priniotakis (likely a relative) and Kyriakos Poursanides among its members — Poursanides would later be a key collaborator in Blue Birds Refuse To Fly.

The mid-1990s also saw him taking on engineering and production roles: in 1999 he co-produced and recorded/mixed the Χρήστος Αλεξόπουλος album Η Άλλη Πλευρά (Hitch-Hyke Records), and the same year co-produced Κύκλος (also Hitch-Hyke Records), with engineering and mixing credits on both. In 2001 he engineered, co-produced, and co-mixed the Greek power metal band Wolfcry’s Power Within (Black Lotus Records).

Blue Birds Refuse To Fly, his main performing vehicle, released three records:

  • ..Give Me The Wings… (1998, Physis Records) — he was a band member
  • Anapteroma (2004, Decadance Records) — engineer and additional vocals
  • Xenomorph Angel (2007, Decadance Records) — engineer, mixer, guitar, keyboards, backing vocals, and a full performer credit

The Blue Birds were a four-piece: Eva K. (lead vocals, keyboards), George Dedes (vocals/guitar), Kyriakos Poursanides (keyboards), and Priniotakis (guitar/keyboards/backing vocals).

Engineering and Mastering Career (2004–present)

From the mid-2000s onwards his credit list shifts increasingly toward the studio side. Key projects:

Keep Shelly in Athens (2013–2017)

His most internationally documented connection. He mastered all three of the band’s studio albums:

Keep Shelly in Athens is the project of RPR and Sarah P. (who also releases under her own name — see below). The mastering of all three LPs through Priniotakis establishes him as their go-to engineer.

Sarah P. (2017)

Priniotakis co-produced, mixed, and mastered Sarah P.’s solo EP Who Am I (EraseRestart, Germany, 2017), suggesting a closer working relationship than pure mastering — he was brought in as a producer/mixer on her solo material, not just the Keep Shelly releases.

The Puzzlemusik axis is notable for the co-production work with Alex Bolpasis (Discogs artist ID: 1275650), who appears repeatedly as co-producer, mixer, and engineer alongside Priniotakis on these releases — particularly on the Socos-associated projects and Lüüp’s Meadow Rituals (Experimedia, 2011, US).

Marva Von Theo (2018–2021)

He mastered both of Marva Von Theo’s self-released releases: Dream Within A Dream (2018) and Afterglow (2021). Mixed By and production credits on these go to Θοδωρής Φοινίδης (Theo Foinidis).

Monsieur Minimal (2020)

Mixed and mastered Easteria (Mo.Mi. Records, 2020) for the Greek electronic/pop project Monsieur Minimal.

Wave Records (4) — Brazil (2014–2025)

An unexpected international thread: Priniotakis mixed and mastered releases for Brazilian artists on Wave Records (4) in multiple years:

  • Stains Of Love (2014) — also recorded acoustic guitars
  • Dystopia (2022)
  • Human Cries (2025)

Jazz and World Music (2020–2026)

Later credits expand into jazz and world music:

  • Strovilos by Trio Tekke (Riverboat Records / World Music Network, UK, 2020) — mastered. This release was mixed by Alex Bolpasis.
  • Έρκυνα - From Darkness To Light by Nassia Gofa & The Jazz Tales (Jazz Breeze Records, 2023) — engineer, recording supervisor, mastered. This session also features Gilad Atzmon on tenor saxophone.
  • Everything In Between by Jako Quartet (XJAZZ Music, Germany, 2026) — mixed and mastered.
