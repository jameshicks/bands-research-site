Kathleen Hanna

Vocalist, musician, activist, zine writer. Born November 12, 1968, Portland, Oregon. Discogs artist ID: 58331. Central figure of the riot grrrl movement; founder of Bikini Kill, Le Tigre, and The Julie Ruin.

Overview

Kathleen Hanna is one of the defining voices of 1990s feminist punk and the riot grrrl movement. She has operated across four main musical projects — Bikini Kill, a solo Julie Ruin album, Le Tigre, and The Julie Ruin band — as well as numerous guest appearances and production credits. She is also known as an activist, zine writer, and visual artist. Married to Adam Horovitz (Ad-Rock of Beastie Boys) in 2006.

Era 1: Bikini Kill (1990–1997, reunited 2019)

Hanna co-founded Bikini Kill in Olympia, Washington in 1990 alongside Kathi Wilcox (bass), Tobi Vail (drums), and Billy Karren (guitar). The band became the flagship act of the riot grrrl movement, signing to Kill Rock Stars for most of their output. Key albums:

  • Revolution Girl Style Now (1991, self-released cassette)
  • Bikini Kill EP (1992, Kill Rock Stars KRS-204) — includes “Double Dare Ya” and “Liar”; recorded in part by Ian MacKaye and Don Zientara
  • Pussy Whipped (1993, Wiiija Records) — LP; includes “Rebel Girl”
  • Reject All American (1996, Kill Rock Stars KRS-260) — final studio LP; produced by John Goodmanson
  • The Singles (1998, Kill Rock Stars) — compilation

The band disbanded in 1997, reunited in 2019, and continue to release and tour on their own Bikini Kill Records imprint.

Era 2: Julie Ruin — Solo (1998)

After Bikini Kill dissolved, Hanna recorded a lo-fi solo album under the name Julie Ruin, released on Kill Rock Stars (KRS 297) in 1998. She played and recorded almost everything herself (instruments, vocals, programming), with mixing assistance from Paul Schuster. The album — 15 tracks of electro-punk bedroom pop — prefigured the electronic direction she would take with Le Tigre. The solo Julie Ruin entity carries Discogs artist ID 390236 (listed as an alias of Hanna, ID 58331).

Era 3: Le Tigre (1998–2005, reunited 2023)

Hanna formed Le Tigre in New York City in 1998 with Johanna Fateman and Sadie Benning. Benning left in 2001 and was replaced by JD Samson. The band mixed punk politics with electronic dance, samples, and performance art. Key releases:

  • Le Tigre (1999, Wiiija Records) — debut LP; produced by Le Tigre and Chris Stamey; includes “Deceptacon” and “Hot Topic”
  • Feminist Sweepstakes (2001, Mr. Lady MRLR-19) — second LP; JD Samson’s first album with band
  • This Island (2004, Universal/Le Tigre Records) — major-label LP; produced with Nick Sansano; Ric Ocasek co-produced “Tell You Now”

See Le Tigre for full discography and personnel.

Era 4: The Julie Ruin (band, 2010–present)

Hanna formed The Julie Ruin as a full band (Discogs artist ID: 3439059), retaining Kathi Wilcox from Bikini Kill on bass. The band self-released their debut on their own The Julie Ruin imprint, then signed to Hardly Art.
