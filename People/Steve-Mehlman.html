Steve Mehlman

Drummer. Born 1971. Discogs artist ID: 376466.

Overview

Steve Mehlman is a drummer based in the American rock underground, best known as the long-serving drummer for Pere Ubu from the mid-1990s through 2017 — one of the most stable tenures in the band’s famously rotating membership. He also appeared on both reunion albums of Rocket From The Tombs and contributed drums to several smaller Ohio-connected projects including Terminal Lovers and a band called Roue.

Name variations on Discogs: “Mehlman”, “Melman”.

Pere Ubu Tenure (c. 1995–2017)

Mehlman first appears in the Discogs record on a 1996 Pere Ubu release (Beach Boys See Dee +, Tim/Kerr), placing him in the lineup as early as the Ray Gun Suitcase era — likely the drummer who performed the live material on that CD/video compilation. He went uncredited on the studio album Pennsylvania (1998, Tim/Kerr Records), where his role as drummer and percussionist was confirmed only in Discogs credits, not the sleeve itself — consistent with David Thomas’s preference for removing individual credits from that period’s artwork.

He remained with Pere Ubu through the Glitterhouse period and the shift to UK labels, appearing on a continuous run of studio albums spanning two decades:

His credit on “Long Live Père Ubu!” (2009) is notable: in addition to drums and vocals, he took an “Arranged By” credit — one of the few non-Thomas arrangement credits in the band’s catalog. On Carnival Of Souls (2014) he shared a “Written-By” credit, suggesting increased compositional involvement in the later Fire Records period. He also plays organ on St. Arkansas and Lady From Shanghai, and contributes piano on the live Jarocin album — indicating a broader instrumental role than drummer alone.

He appears not to have played on The Long Goodbye (2019) or Trouble On Big Beat Street (2023), suggesting his tenure with the band ended around 2017–2018. Confirmation of exact departure would require checking those albums’ personnel.

Rocket From The Tombs (2004, 2011)

Mehlman played drums on both of the Rocket From The Tombs reunion albums. The original band (active 1974–1975) had dissolved before his time, but he was recruited for the reunion lineup alongside original members David Thomas (2), Craig Bell, Cheetah Chrome, and Richard Lloyd (Television).

Terminal Lovers (2003–2005)

Mehlman played drums on two releases by Terminal Lovers, an Ohio indie/art-rock band led by guitarist/vocalist Dave Cintron (2). The band had a rotating cast of players and also included Jim Jones (3) (Pere Ubu guitarist) among its members.

Roue (2005)

Mehlman is listed as a member of Roue (also spelled Roué), a small US project that released one album, Upward Heroic Motive (2005, Exit Stencil Recordings). Other members include Jeff Harris (11), John Kalman, and Justin Coulter. No further Discogs credits for this project.

The Heathers

Mehlman is listed as a Discogs member of The Heathers, alongside Gina J., Lisa Mehringer, Rae Martello, and Rebecca Yody. No releases appear in the DB under this name.
