Michael Thieke

Clarinettist and improviser. Born 1971 in Düsseldorf, Germany; based in Berlin and Rome. Discogs artist ID: 395542. Active approximately 1992–present. Top labels: scatterArchive, Al Maslakh, Sound Anatomy.

Website: michael-thieke.de | SoundCloud: soundcloud.com/thieke

Overview

Michael Thieke is a clarinettist working at the intersection of free improvisation, contemporary composed music, jazz, and noise. He has been a central figure in Berlin’s electroacoustic and free-improvisation scene since the early 2000s, with a discography spanning some three decades and appearing on releases from Tokyo (Ftarri, Meenna) to Beirut (Al Maslakh) to Germany (Staubgold, Sound Anatomy, Gagarin Records) and beyond. Thieke is unusual in combining an active performance practice with hands-on production work — he regularly self-records, mixes, and produces his own projects.

His most sustained creative partnership has been with fellow clarinettist Kai Fagaschinski in two long-running duo/group contexts: The International Nothing (clarinet duo, ongoing since at least 2006) and The Magic I.D. (quartet with Christof Kurzmann and Margareth Kammerer). Beyond these, Thieke maintains an unusually wide web of collaborations — from Beirut’s Al Maslakh circle to Tokyo’s Ftarri community to the Berlin avant-garde at large.

Bio by Era / Project

Early career (1992 — early 2000s)

The Discogs record reaches back to 1992, where Thieke appears on a Jazz Haus Musik release playing saxophone — an indication that his early practice included saxophone before he specialised on clarinet. By the mid-2000s he was embedded in the Berlin free-improvisation scene.

The Magic I.D. (2005–2011)

Thieke was a founding member of The Magic I.D., a quartet with Kai Fagaschinski (clarinet), Christof Kurzmann (vocals, computer/Lloopp software), and Margareth Kammerer (vocals, guitar). The group released two albums on major experimental imprints:

  • Till My Breath Gives Out (2008, Erstwhile Records) — produced by Jon Abbey and Yuko Zama; recorded and mixed by Christoph Amann
  • I’m So Awake / Sleepless I Feel (2011, Staubgold)

Thieke played clarinet (left channel) throughout, with the two clarinets panned hard left/right. The group’s approach blended extended techniques, voice, and laptop processing.

The International Nothing (2006–present)

Thieke’s most central long-term project is the clarinet duo The International Nothing with Kai Fagaschinski. The duo has released six records, primarily on the Kyoto/Tokyo label Ftarri, with consistent self-production (Thieke records and mixes, Werner Dafeldecker has mastered several releases):

The duo’s music works with long sustained tones, microtonal relationships between the two clarinets, and minute parametric variations — a form of spectral and process-oriented improvisation.

Nashaz and the Al Maslakh connection (2016)

In 2016 Thieke appeared on Nashaz (Al Maslakh, MSLKH 19), a quartet improvisation recorded with Sharif Sehnaoui (guitar), Andrea Neumann (inside piano, mixing desk electronics), and Michael Vorfeld (percussion). Thieke recorded the session; mixing and mastering was handled by Fadi Tabbal. The release connects Thieke’s Berlin practice to the Beirut free-improvisation circle centred on Al Maslakh, and also to the journal-label’s international-visitor strand. Mazen Kerbaj and Michael Vorfeld handled artwork/design. Thieke also co-appears on The Three Things You Can Hear (2016, Annihaya Records) with Sehnaoui and others.

As Found and other co-leader releases (2017–present)

As Found (2017, Sound Anatomy) is a trio record co-led by Thieke with violinist Biliana Voutchkova and electro-acoustic artist Roy Carroll. David Sylvian handled design and photography; Adam Asnan recorded the live session; Richard Scott mastered. The Sound Anatomy label is listed among Thieke’s top three labels by volume, suggesting this is an imprint he is closely associated with.

Other notable appearances from this period:

  • Concurrences (2018, Trouble In The East) — with Der lange Schatten (a group Thieke is a member of): Antonio Borghini (bass), Håvard Wiik (piano), Werner Dafeldecker (mastering)
  • Mem, Aleph, Lassis Performed By The Pitch & Bondi ​- ​D’Incise​ - ​Majkowski (2018, Astral Spirits/Monofonus Press) — with The Pitch (2)
  • A Biology Of Shadows (2018, In Aulis, Belgium)
  • Live At Ftarri Festival 2015 (2019, Meenna) — quartet with Ju Sei (vocals), Utah Kawasaki (electronics), Masahiko Okura (contrabass clarinet); recorded/mixed/mastered by Chihei Hatakeyama
