John McLaughlin

Aug 23, 20263 min read

John McLaughlin

Born: January 4, 1942, Doncaster, Yorkshire, England
Discogs ID: 136179
Aliases: Mahavishnu (2), A. Hall (2)

British jazz-fusion guitarist. Founded the Mahavishnu Orchestra in New York in 1971; one of the premier fusion groups of the 1970s. Original lineup: McLaughlin (guitar), Jerry Goodman (violin), Jan Hammer (keyboards), Rick Laird (bass), Billy Cobham (drums). Revived the band in 1984 with Jonas Hellborg on bass, Mitchel Forman (keyboards), Danny Gottlieb (drums), Bill Evans (saxophone). Also worked with Miles Davis and led the acoustic Shakti ensemble. Shares 4 releases with Hellborg in Discogs.
