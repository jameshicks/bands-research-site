Islamiq Grrrls

Discogs ID: 6511393. Real name: Alisa Beširević. Also known as Anuk Rocha and Asia Nevolja. Musician, vocalist, producer, visual artist, and painter based in Germany. Active on Discogs 2016–2018 as a solo/collaborative entity, primarily as co-artist and co-producer with oOoOO (3) (Christopher Dexter Greenspan) on the album Faminine Mystique (2018, Nihjgt Feelings, NFCD 1002). Together with Greenspan she forms the duo Drab City, which signed to Bella Union and released the LP Good Songs For Bad People (2020).

Overview

Alisa Beširević operates under the alias Islamiq Grrrls (name variation: Islamiq Grrls) and presents as a multidisciplinary artist — singing, writing, producing, and also contributing visual work (photography, artwork, layout) to her releases. Her Discogs footprint is small but dense: a 2016 self-released DJ mix, a handful of 2018 recordings made with oOoOO (3), and the Drab City project that grew out of those sessions.

2016 — Solo DJ Mix

Her first listed release is Pulse Mix (2016), a self-released DJ mix put out under the “Not On Label (Islamiq Grrrls Self-released)” flag. No catalog number; Germany country designation. This establishes her DJ / electronic roots before the collaborative work with Greenspan.

2018 — Collaboration with oOoOO (3)

The main body of released work centers on her partnership with Christopher Dexter Greenspan (oOoOO). The Dazed Mix (2018) is a joint DJ mix self-released via oOoOO’s own imprint. The single “The Stranger” (2018, Nihjgt Feelings, digital) was co-composed and co-performed by both, with Beširević taking vocal duties and co-writing the lyrics; on Discogs it also carries a credit to her legal name (“Alisa Beširević” / artist ID 16279792) as Composed By and Lyrics By, alongside “Christopher Greenspan” (artist ID 16279708) — a parallel entry for Greenspan — suggesting a formal publishing credit layer distinct from their performance personas.

The album Faminine Mystique (2018, Nihjgt Feelings, NFCD 1002) is the centrepiece. It is co-billed as oOoOO & Islamiq Grrrls and released on CD. Of the 13 tracks, Beširević is primary or featured vocalist and co-composer on at least five (“All Of Me,” “The Stranger,” “True Blue,” “You Don’t Love Me,” “Yr Love,” “I Want To Be Alone”); she also recorded and mixed the album jointly with Greenspan, and handled all album artwork, photography, and layout herself. Greenspan handles the remaining tracks as solo oOoOO material. Genres: Electronic, Pop; Styles: Contemporary R&B, Experimental, Leftfield, Witch House.

2020–Present — Drab City

The Islamiq Grrrls / oOoOO partnership evolved into the formal duo project Drab City, signed to Bella Union and releasing Good Songs For Bad People (BELLA1020V, 2020) — a Dream Pop / Psychedelic-leaning LP. The Drab City name represents a softer, more song-oriented incarnation of the collaboration compared to the atmospheric witch house textures of Faminine Mystique.
