Overview

Franz Treichler (born Francis José Conceição Leitão Treichler) is a Swiss composer, vocalist, guitarist, programmer, and record producer, best known as the creative force behind The Young Gods. His work spans industrial, alternative rock, experimental, ambient, and electro territory, with credits stretching back to 1981 across projects including /A\ (2), Jof & The Ram, and the Schizoids. He has worked most consistently with Roli Mosimann, Al Monod, Bertrand Siffert, and other long-standing Young Gods associates, with releases through Play It Again Sam Records and the band’s own Two Gentlemen Records label.
