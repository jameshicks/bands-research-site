Shankar

Real name: Lakshminarayanan Shankar Also known as: L. Shankar Discogs ID: 158759 Instrument: Violin, Electric Violin (Double Violin), Vocals, Kanjira Active: 1970s–2000s

South Indian Carnatic violinist; founder of the double violin (two-necked electric violin). Founding member of Shakti (2) (1975–1977) alongside John McLaughlin, Zakir Hussain, Vikku Vinayakram, and Ramnad Raghavan.

Overview

Shankar was central to the original Shakti lineup’s sound, playing violin across all three studio albums (Shakti With John McLaughlin, A Handful Of Beauty, Natural Elements). After Shakti he pursued an international fusion career, releasing a series of records on ECM Records (produced by Manfred Eicher) including Nobody Told Me (1989) and M.R.C.S. (1991), and on the Axiom label including Soul Searcher (1990, produced by Bill Laswell) and the compilation-heavy Soul Searcher ensemble sessions.

He regularly worked with a core trio of himself, Zakir Hussain, and Vikku Vinayakram on ECM and Moment Records releases including Panca Nadai Pallavi (1989, CBS/ECM), Eternal Light (2000, Moment Records). His group “The Epidemics” (with vocalist Caroline (2)) was a recurring production vehicle.
