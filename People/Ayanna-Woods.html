Ayanna Woods

Discogs ID: 5151703

Performer, composer, and bandleader based in Chicago. Plays electric bass and contributes voice across the Chicago experimental/jazz scene. Discogs credits span 2012–2025; no solo or co-leader releases are documented in the DB as of this writing. Her profile lists a B.A. in music from Yale University (class unknown). She is a core contributor to Ben Lamar Gay’s ensemble work and appears on releases by Jamila Woods and Macie Stewart. Closely affiliated with International Anthem Recording Company.

Background

Woods’s earliest Discogs credit is a 2012 vocal role with the Yale Women’s Slavic Chorus (credited as Yale Slavic Chorus, Discogs ID 5867292) on I’m Not Afraid = N’e Bayus’a = Не Боюся (Not On Label). This is consistent with her Yale B.A., placing her there circa 2012 or earlier.

Her subsequent Chicago credits situate her within the city’s post-AACM experimental ecosystem — the same network anchored by International Anthem, The AACM Great Black Music Ensemble, and artists like Ben Lamar Gay, Tomeka Reid, Rob Frye, and Macie Stewart.

She is also credited as arranger on Macie Stewart’s Mouth Full Of Glass (2022, Full Time Hobby), indicating compositional and arrangement work beyond bass and voice.
