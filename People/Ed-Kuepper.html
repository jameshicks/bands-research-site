Ed Kuepper

Discogs ID: 381695. Real name: Edmund Kuepper. Australian guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter. Co-founder of The Saints (2) (Brisbane punk band, mid-1970s). Left The Saints in 1978 and formed Laughing Clowns in Sydney in 1979. Also credited as Edmund Kuepper on Discogs. Prolific solo and collaborative career through subsequent decades with numerous groups and alias configurations.
