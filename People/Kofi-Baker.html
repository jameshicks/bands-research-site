Kofi Baker

Real name: Kofi Streatfield Baker
Born: 28 March 1969, Hampstead, London, England
Discogs ID: 691666
Name variations: Kaufi Baker (Japan releases)
Web: kofibaker.com

English-born, US-based drummer. Son of Ginger Baker (Cream, Blind Faith) and Liz Baker. His drumming career spans hard rock, fusion, jazz-rock, and experimental improvisation, with touchstones in the Hellborg/Lane collaboration, the LA-based fusion trio OHM:, and multiple Cream tribute projects running his father’s legacy forward.

Early Career and The Now (1989)

Baker’s earliest Discogs-documented recording is with The Now (11) — a UK hard rock band whose members also included Malcolm Bruce (son of Jack Bruce), Rue Philips (vocals/guitar), and Lee Faulkner (bass). The band’s only album, Overnight Stay = 戦慄のメタル・ナイト (1989, Metal Mania, Japan-only CD), documents this lineup. Baker is credited as “Kaufi Baker.” Dave Morgan (2) and Martin Smith (3) produced; Colin Owen engineered. The record is twelve tracks of hard rock with covers including “I Heard It Through The Grapevine” (Marvin Gaye). The genealogy is notable: Baker and Bruce are both second-generation musicians from the original Cream.

Lost City (1993)

Lost City (Discogs ID: 2160783) was a US-based rock band whose core was Baker, Malcolm Bruce, Rue Phillips (Rue Philips in earlier credits — same person), and Slobo Svrdlan. Their album Watching You (1993, Scotti Bros. Records, US) was produced by the band and Larry Hirsch, with Rick Parashar (Pearl Jam, Alice in Chains producer) contributing additional production and mixing on several tracks. Doug Sax mastered. The album credited “The Players” as guest performers — a large ensemble including Ric Fierabracci (bass), Bob Glaub (bass), Roland Cayce (keys), Rose Stone, Alfie Silas, and Olivia McClurkin (backing vocals). Baker played drums and percussion throughout.

Abstract Logic (1995)

The most acclaimed recording of Baker’s career. Abstract Logic (Day Eight Music, DEM 035) is the first album in the celebrated Hellborg/Lane trilogy — three records pairing Swedish bassist Jonas Hellborg with Memphis guitarist Shawn Lane. Each album used a different drummer; Baker was the choice for this first volume.

Recorded in Sweden by Stephane Jean (Discogs ID: 691667); mixed by Scud Noonan; produced by Hellborg. Released 1995 on CD (pressed at DADC Austria). Two pressings documented.

Guitarminator (1992)

A UK 12” single (Mirror Image Management) credited to Guitarminator (2). Baker appears as one of two drummers alongside the act’s own drummer. Big Beat/Industrial in style — an early-1990s UK indie release, minor context.

Kofi Baker’s Karisma (2003)

Baker’s first solo-credited album. Released 2003 on True Hustlin’ Entertainment (US). He plays drums and keyboards throughout and takes songwriter credit on multiple tracks. The lineup drew from his established West Coast network:

Mojo (56) and Tapestry (2002)

Mojo (56) (Discogs ID: 6777682) was a trio of Baker, Ric Fierabracci (bass/producer), and Brett Garsed (guitar/producer). Their album Tapestry (2002, Paranormal Records, US) is jazz-rock fusion — 7 tracks running up to 10 minutes. Co-produced by Fierabracci and Garsed. Baker and Fierabracci would continue working together in subsequent projects.

OHM: (2004–2008)

OHM: (Discogs ID: 2179743) is a Los Angeles–based fusion/jazz-rock group built around former Megadeth guitarist Chris Poland. Baker joined the band’s lineup alongside Robertino Pagliari (fretless bass).

“Live” On KPFK 90.7 FM (2004, Lion Music, LMC 091) — live radio recording featuring Baker on drums, Poland on guitar, Pagliari on fretless bass. 15 tracks including a KSPC session set.

Amino Acid Flashback (2005, Blacknote Records) — studio album. Baker, Poland, and Pagliari as core trio. Produced and engineered by Poland and Petar Sardelich. Nancy Matter mastered.

Circus Of Sound (2008, Irond, Russia) — Baker plays drums on tracks 8–13 only; Joel Taylor (tracks 1–3) and Frank Briggs (tracks 4–7) are the other drummers on the record. Poland produces and engineers; Petar Sardelich engineers; Ralph Patlan mixes.

OHM: membership: Chris Poland, Robertino Pagliari, Kofi Baker, Nick Menza (also former Megadeth), David Eagle (2).

The Music Of Cream and Cream Tribute Work

The Music Of Cream (Discogs ID: 6791910) — trio tribute featuring Baker (drums), Malcolm Bruce (keys/vocals), and Will Johns (2) (guitar/vocals). This project performs the Cream catalog, directly invoking the sons-of-Cream-members lineage. Malcolm Bruce is son of Jack Bruce; Baker is son of Ginger Baker. Will Johns (2) is the son of engineer Andy Johns and nephew by marriage of Eric Clapton.

Kofi Baker’s Cream Experience (Discogs ID: 12079201) — Baker’s own Cream tribute band. Members: Kofi Baker, Ric Fierabracci (bass), and Tony Spinner (vocals/guitar — also a member of Toto). Active into the 2020s (Facebook page active as of data cutoff).

Other Groups

Riddlehouse (2) (Discogs ID: 5169339) — project listed under Baker’s memberships; no releases documented on Discogs.

Steve Marriott & The Next Band (Discogs ID: 4487109) — Baker listed as a member. Steve Marriott was the vocalist of Small Faces and Humble Pie; Baker’s connection here has not yet been confirmed with a specific release.
