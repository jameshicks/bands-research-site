Andy Hernandez

Real name: Andy Hernandez. Stage name: Coati Mundi. Born January 3, 1950, New York City. Musician, arranger, composer, producer. Discogs IDs: Andy Hernandez = 73414; Coati Mundi = 30097.

Also credited as: “Sugar Coated” Andy Hernandez, Sugar-Coated Andy Hernandez.

Overview

Andy Hernandez played vibraphone, marimba, and accordion with Dr. Buzzard’s Original Savannah Band (1976–1979) and was a core member and co-producer of Kid Creole And The Coconuts under his stage name Coati Mundi. He also led Aural Exciters and released solo material through ZE Records and Virgin (1980–2010).

He has been described in Discogs materials as a cousin of August Darnell (Thomas Browder) — a relationship that has not been independently confirmed in other sources. (See August Darnell page for research note on this point.)
