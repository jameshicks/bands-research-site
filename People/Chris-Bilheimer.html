Chris Bilheimer

Chris Bilheimer

Oct 19, 20263 min read

Chris Bilheimer

Discogs ID: 609312

Graphic designer based in Athens, Georgia. Longtime art director for R.E.M. — credited on virtually every R.E.M. release from Monster (1994) through Radio Free Europe 2025 (2025), spanning studio albums, singles, compilations, live releases, and reissues. Also known for art direction on Neutral Milk Hotel’s In The Aeroplane Over The Sea (Merge Records, 1998). Extensive work across the Athens indie scene and beyond, including Green Day, Weezer, and Sacred Bones Records soundtrack releases.
