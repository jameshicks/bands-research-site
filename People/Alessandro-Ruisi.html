Overview

Alessandro Ruisi is a UK-born violinist pursuing a career as a chamber musician and soloist, best known as first violinist of the Ruisi Quartet, which he performs in alongside his brother Max Ruisi. He is also a member of the 12 Ensemble and the London Contemporary Orchestra. His recorded output is concentrated in the 2020s, spanning film soundtracks and indie-leaning releases on XL Recordings, 4AD, and Invada; his closest recurring collaborators are fellow string players Eloisa-Fleur Thom, Luba Tunnicliffe, and Venetia Jollands.
