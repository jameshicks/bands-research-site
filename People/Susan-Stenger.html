Susan Stenger

Bassist, vocalist, composer, flautist. New York City. Discogs artist ID: 154043.

Overview

Susan Stenger is a New York-based bassist, vocalist, composer, and multi-instrumentalist (also flute and guitar). She was a core member of Band Of Susans from the band’s formation in 1986 until their disbanding in 1996, playing bass and sharing vocal duties throughout the band’s run. From the 1992 LP Now onward she co-wrote all Band Of Susans material with Robert Poss, her closest long-term creative partner. After Band Of Susans she formed Big Bottom in 1997 — an ensemble of five electric bass guitars and a drum machine — and has continued to work in experimental music as a performer and collaborator. She also appears in the Coh Da Guitar Quartet alongside Poss, David First, and Seth Josel. Her earliest documented credit is a liner-note contribution to a 1984 Phill Niblock release on India Navigation, placing her in the New York downtown experimental scene before Band Of Susans began.
