Engineer, producer, and musician (guitarist).

Steve Watson has worked as chief engineer and co-producer for Noise New Jersey (the relocated home of indie guru Kramer and his original Noise New York studio). There he honed his indie production skills working with King Missile, Jad Fair, and Galaxie 500 to name but a few. As Noise New Jersey ran into community zoning Issues, he made a quick transition to becoming an in-demand music engineer in the TV and film world. His years of experience mixing live music for TV is a passion he shares with a love for indie music. Some of Steve’s live recording credits include Mariah Carey, Tom Petty, The B-52’s, Korn, Lauryn Hill, and Silverchair, and he has mixed live for broadcast for Michael McDonald, Charlie Daniels, Willie Nelson, Al Jarreau, Chick Corea, Sammy Hagar, and Trace Adkins.

A musician as well, he has toured and played internationally with Kramer (Bongwater), Hugh Hopper (Soft Machine), Damon & Naomi (Galaxie 500), and Dogbowl.

Overview

Steve Watson is an engineer, producer, and guitarist who built his indie credentials through the 1990s as chief engineer and co-producer at Noise New Jersey — Kramer’s relocated New York studio — where he worked extensively with King Missile, Jad Fair, and Galaxie 500, among others, accumulating 191 Discogs credits from 1991 onward. His closest collaborative relationship was with Kramer (120 shared releases), and his label work centred on Shimmy Disc, Mute, and Infectious Records. When community zoning issues shut down Noise New Jersey he pivoted to TV and film, mixing live-to-broadcast performances for Mariah Carey, Tom Petty, The B-52s, and others. A working musician as well, he has toured internationally with Kramer, Hugh Hopper of Soft Machine, Damon & Naomi, and Dogbowl.
