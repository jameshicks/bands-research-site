Anthony Braxton

Anthony Braxton

Aug 04, 20262 min read

Anthony Braxton

Discogs ID: 200818
Origin: Chicago, Illinois, USA

Composer, multi-instrumentalist (reeds), conductor. Central figure in American avant-garde jazz; associated with the AACM (Association for the Advancement of Creative Musicians) and later Tri-Centric Foundation. Forbes Graham performed in Braxton’s ensemble at the Festival of New Trumpet Music 2007, playing Composition No. 103; released on New Braxton House in 2011. Conducted by Braxton; cornet by Taylor Ho Bynum.

Phillip Wilson appeared on his large-ensemble Creative Orchestra Music 1976 (Arista, 1976), playing marching cymbals, drums, and percussion alongside Roscoe Mitchell, Dave Holland, Muhal Richard Abrams, Barry Altschul, George Lewis, Wadada Leo Smith, and others.

Braxton appeared on Dave Holland’s landmark Conference of the Birds (ECM, 1973) — the David Holland Quartet with Sam Rivers, Barry Altschul, and Holland, produced by Manfred Eicher. He and Holland also co-founded Circle (5) with Chick Corea and Barry Altschul in 1970–71. Discogs lists Braxton as a member of the Anthony Braxton Quartet (ID: 361203), which also featured Holland.

Also on Futura 2000’s Down By Law (Deadline / Celluloid, 1985): painting credit by Futura 2000.

Kurt Munkacsi recorded Alto Saxophone Improvisations 1979 (Arista, 1979), produced by Michael Cuscuna. Braxton is also listed as a member of the The Philip Glass Ensemble on Discogs (and by extension associated with Philip Glass).

Delmark Records Releases

Braxton’s debut LP was recorded for Delmark Records in 1968 — one of the first commercial releases for an AACM artist. He returned to the label multiple times through the 2000s.
