Mark Robinson

Musician, producer, label founder, and graphic designer. Real name: Mark Richard Robinson. Born 21 February 1967, Mineola, New York; raised and based in Washington, D.C.; later Cambridge, Massachusetts. Discogs artist ID: 127628.

Robinson is the founder and sole operator of Teen Beat Records (founded 1984), DC’s longest-running indie label. He is best known as the principal songwriter and driving force behind Unrest (2) (1985–1994) — the band that put Teen Beat on the map — and its successor project Air Miami (1994–1998), both of which featured Bridget Cross as co-vocalist and instrumentalist. Under his own name and a series of aliases and group projects, Robinson has maintained a continuous recording output from 1985 to the present. Wikipedia describes his recordings as “typically sparse, often featuring a carefully controlled guitar.” (Source: Wikipedia, “Mark Robinson (musician)”)

He became a DJ at the student-run radio station WMUC while studying at the University of Maryland. His solo song “Catalog & Classify” (from Tiger Banana) was used frequently on the public radio program This American Life.

Unrest (2) — Key Releases

Unrest was Robinson’s primary band, founded with Phil Krauth at the inception of Teen Beat. The band issued their first cassette/7” in 1985 and ran through multiple lineups; the 1991–1994 period with Bridget Cross is the most widely documented. The band was signed to 4AD (UK) for their final phase.

Teen Beat Records

Robinson founded Teen Beat Records in 1984 (first release 1985: Extremism In The Defense Of Liberty Is No Vice, Teenbeat 1, a Various Artists comp). The label has issued 555 documented releases across all pressings (Discogs count), reaching catalog number 599 by 2024. Full label article: Teen Beat Records.

Key artists on Teen Beat beyond Robinson’s own projects: Eggs, Versus, Tuscadero, Blast Off Country Style, Gastr del Sol, +/- (Plus/Minus), True Love Always, The Rondelles, Aden, Tracy Shedd, Luna (5), Jonny Cohen, Scaley Andrew, Clarence (4), Dustdevils, Phil Krauth, Gamma Rays, Uncle Wiggly.

Sublabels: Teen-Beat Originals (ID: 3455911), Teenbeat Annual Sampler Series (1470402), Teenbeat Originals Series (632793), Wakefield. The TeenBeat Story (1470407).
