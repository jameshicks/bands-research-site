Liam Watson

UK recording engineer, producer, and musician. Discogs artist ID: 265027. Proprietor of Toe Rag Studios in London. Central technical figure behind the Damaged Goods garage punk scene from the mid-1990s.

Overview

Engineered and produced a large portion of the Damaged Goods catalog from the mid-1990s. Toe Rag Studios — his recording facility — became a focal point for UK garage punk, also hosting recordings by The White Stripes (Elephant, 2003) and others outside the Damaged Goods orbit. On label releases: 26 engineer credits, 49 producer credits, and 57 “other” credits (as bassist, guitarist, or performer).
