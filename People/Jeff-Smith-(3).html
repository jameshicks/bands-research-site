Jeff Smith (3)

San Antonio, Texas vocalist, producer, label founder, and co-founder of Hickoids. Real name Jefferey J. Smith. Discogs artist ID: 589305. Aliases in DB: “Mofungo (2)”, “Wounded Turkey”.

Overview

Jeff Smith is primarily known as the vocalist and co-founder of Hickoids, the cowpunk band formed in Austin in 1984 with guitarist Jukebox (2) around the concept of “hardcore punk meets hardcore country.” He also briefly fronted an earlier punk band called The Dwarves (unrelated to the San Francisco band of the same name) before the Hickoids. Smith ran the Hole in the Wall, an Austin music venue on Guadalupe Street, as co-owner from the late 1990s into the early 2000s before eventually leaving Austin and returning to San Antonio.

He founded the Saustex Media label in San Antonio in 2003, which became home to Hickoids, Piñata Protest, Copper Gamins, Churchwood, Snowbyrd, Glambilly, T. Tex Edwards, and others. He regularly promotes the “A Town South of Austin” showcase of San Antonio bands at South by Southwest.

Smith has also helped international artists find an Austin foothold, including Australian country band Chuck’s Wagon and German country/roots group The Boss Hoss, both of whom connected with him via the internet. He is involved with the South Texas Popular Culture Center. His brother is credited on Discogs as artist ID 8127850.

Career

Early punk / The Dwarves (pre-1984)

Before the Hickoids, Smith played in a punk band called The Dwarves — not to be confused with the San Francisco Dwarves. No Discogs releases documented from this period.

Hickoids (1984–present)

Smith co-founded Hickoids in Austin in 1984 with guitarist Jukebox (2). The band’s premise — “hardcore punk meets hardcore country” — placed them squarely in the cowpunk wave. They broke up in 1989 but toured with varying lineups until 1991, then played sporadic reunion shows before resuming regular recording and touring in 2006.

Compiler / label work (1984–1985)

Smith produced the Hickoids debut Metal Moo Cow (1984, Matako Mazuri Records) and compiled and edited Bands On The Block (1985, Matako Mazuri Records), an Austin punk compilation for which he also designed the cover art.

Smart Dads / St. Nick’s Farm (1994)

Smith contributed vocals and production to St. Nick’s Farm (1994, Only Boy Records) by Smart Dads (Discogs ID: 2909190). Discogs release ID: 2804518.

Texacala Jones & Her T.J. Hookers (1998)

Smith co-produced the self-titled debut CD of Texacala Jones & Her T.J. Hookers (1998, Honey Records (2), HON-CD03) alongside Mick Buck. His Discogs credit on this release is Producer only — no guitar credit appears in the database. Other production/engineering credits on the album: Mick Buck (Guitar, Producer, Mixed By) and John Hancock (2) (Engineer, Producer, Mixed By). Players: Roger Prescott (Guitar), Tim Kinard (Bass), Rob Gaines (2) (Drums), Walter Daniels (Saxophone, Harmonica). Discogs release ID: 2907217.

Saustex Media (2003–present)

Smith founded Saustex Media (Discogs label ID: 82024) in San Antonio in 2003. The label and its sublabel Saustex Records (ID: 996136) became a hub for San Antonio and Texas underground country-punk. Key roster includes Hickoids, Piñata Protest, Copper Gamins, Churchwood, and others.
