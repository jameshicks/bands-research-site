Erin Smith

Guitarist, zinester, and co-founder of Bratmobile. Discogs artist ID: 621934. Real name: Erin Susannah Smith.

Overview

Erin Smith is a musician and zinester from Bethesda, Maryland. In the early 1990s she co-edited the zine Teenage Gang Debs with her brother Don Smith and John Huston — a fanzine devoted to pop culture and independent music, based in Bethesda. The zine briefly had a musical side: Teenage Gang Debs recorded a song for the Teen Beat Records compilation Teen-Beat Fifty.

Smith then joined Bratmobile alongside fellow zine editors Allison Wolfe (vocals) and Molly Neuman (drums), forming one of the central bands of the riot grrrl movement and based out of the Olympia/DC circuit. Bratmobile released singles and full-lengths from 1992 through their first disbanding, reunited in the 2000s for further touring and two albums on Lookout! Records, then split again after Girls Get Busy (2002).

During the gap between Bratmobile runs, Smith and Wolfe continued working together in Cold Cold Hearts, a looser D.C. project also featuring Mark Robinson (of Unrest (2) and Teen Beat Records) as co-producer and contributor. Cold Cold Hearts released a 7-inch (1996, as “Baby Donut”) and a self-titled LP (1997) on Kill Rock Stars.

Beyond performing, Smith has contributed photography to several releases (Bratmobile’s Pottymouth, Unrest’s Perfect Teeth, the 2019 Bikini Kill reissue) and liner notes to riot grrrl and indie pop archival releases. Her Vampi Soul credits (liner notes on Pachuco-Soul!, 2004; Spanish translation on Sensacional Soul, 2007) reflect the broader pop culture and music journalism interests established through her zine work.

Discogs ID Note

The Strife (Victory Records hardcore) “In This Defiance” (1997) backing vocals credit listed under this Discogs ID (621934) is almost certainly a misattribution — the credit list on that release is a large open-call backing chorus with no evident connection to the DC/Olympia scene.
