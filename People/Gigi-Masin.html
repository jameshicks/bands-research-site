Overview

Gigi Masin is a Venetian composer and multi-instrumentalist born on 24 October 1955, working in ambient, experimental, and modern classical idioms. His 1986 debut brought early recognition before a long period of relative quiet — just three releases across the 1990s and 2000s — followed by a significant return in the 2010s driven by renewed interest in his early catalogue. That second period has been the more prolific, with eleven releases across the 2010s and 2020s on labels including Music From Memory, Apollo, and Strut. He performs piano and synthesizer, belongs to the groups Gaussian Curve and Wind (7), and has collaborated most extensively with Tempelhof, Alessandro Pizzin, and Jonny Nash.
