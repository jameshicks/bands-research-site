Discogs ID: 2460644 Website: http://www.davidspero.co.uk/

British photographer. Active across the UK indie and post-rock scene from the early 1990s, primarily associated with Rough Trade releases by Disco Inferno and Trade 2 releases by Spring Heel Jack. Also has credits on French rock, US classic rock compilations, and a Cat Stevens comeback album.

Overview

David Spero (2) is a UK-based photographer whose most concentrated period of work in the Discogs record is 1993–1997, centred on the Rough Trade / Trade 2 ecosystem. His most significant run is four photography credits across the Spring Heel Jack catalog on the Island/Trade 2 label in 1997. Prior to that he shot all three of Disco Inferno’s Rough Trade releases between 1993 and 1994.

The “(2)” disambiguation indicates there is at least one other person named David Spero on Discogs — notably David Spero (Discogs ID: 2089309), who has an A&R credit on Yusuf Islam’s Roadsinger (2009). These are distinct individuals. David Spero (2) shares only a photography credit on that same album.

Connections by Artist

Disco Inferno

Three consecutive Rough Trade releases, 1993–1994. This is his densest cluster with a single band — he shot the EP The Last Dance (1993), the album D.I. Go Pop (1994), and the EP Second Language (1994). The design on all three was handled by Fuel (9), making this a recurring creative pairing on Disco Inferno packaging.

Spring Heel Jack

Four releases in 1997, all on the Island Records / Trade 2 imprint. Spring Heel Jack was the drum’n’bass / jazz-influenced duo of John Coxon and Ashley Wales. The full sleeve design on these releases was handled by System Gafa and Yuki Miyake; Spero provided the photography component. Mads Bjerke was engineer across all four.

Noir Désir

One credit: One Trip / One Noise (1998, Barclay), a live album by the French rock band. A relatively isolated connection — no other French artists in his known credits.

Joe Walsh

One credit: The Best Of Joe Walsh (MCA Records, 2000), a US compilation. His credit is specifically for “Outside And Inside Inlay” photography, suggesting archive/documentary use rather than a fresh commission.

Yusuf Islam

One credit: Roadsinger (UMe, 2009), Cat Stevens’s comeback album. Multiple photographers are credited; Spero is one of several.
