Jack Bruce

Bassist, vocalist, composer. Born May 14, 1943, Glasgow; died October 25, 2014. Discogs artist ID: 106909.

Overview

Best known as bassist and co-vocalist of Cream. In the 1980s appeared as a guest vocalist on The Golden Palominos records produced by Anton Fier — most prominently on “Silver Bullet” from Visions Of Excess (1985, Celluloid), where he sang lead and played blues harp alongside Richard Thompson. 5 shared releases with Fier on Discogs.

Connections

  • Anton Fier — Golden Palominos (5 shared releases)
  • Tony Williams / The Tony Williams Lifetime — bassist on (Turn It Over) (Polydor, 1970); joined post-Emergency! for live dates and the second studio album
