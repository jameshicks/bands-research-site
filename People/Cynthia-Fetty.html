Overview

Cynthia Fetty is an American art director and designer whose visual work spans free jazz, experimental, and contemporary jazz releases from 1995 into the 2010s. Her output is concentrated around the Thirsty Ear label (18 releases, 1998–2004), with additional work for ROIR and In The Red Recordings, giving her catalogue a strong downtown New York axis. Her most frequent collaborators are Matthew Shipp and William Parker (27 shared credits each), reflecting deep ties to the free improvisation scene built around those musicians. Across 53 total credited releases she worked primarily as art director and creative director.
