Sep 27, 20267 min read

Jonas Hellborg

Real name: Jonas Fredric Hellborg
Born: June 7, 1958, Gothenburg, Sweden
Role: Bassist, producer
Labels: Day Eight Music (own), Axiom, Bardo Records
Discogs ID: 33737

Swedish electric bassist in the jazz/fusion tradition. Debuted internationally with a solo bass performance at the Montreux Jazz Festival in 1981, where his slapping technique was noted as groundbreaking. Since then has moved across electric fusion, acoustic chamber jazz, noise rock, and South Indian classical contexts — often simultaneously. Founded and runs Day Eight Music as both his own label and production vehicle.

Early Career and Solo Work (1981–1986)

The Bassic Thing (1981, Amigo) — solo bass debut recorded following the Montreux appearance; 28 pressings confirm it reached a large audience for a European jazz record.

All Our Steps… (1983, Day Eight Music) — early collaborative record featuring Swedish musicians including Lave Lindholm (engineer) and Michael Shrieve.

Elegant Punk (1984, Day Eight Music) — self-produced; Hellborg on fretted and fretless bass, acoustic guitar; also engineered. The title reflects the tension between technical precision and provocative presentation that would define his image.

Mahavishnu Orchestra — John McLaughlin revived the band in 1984 with a new lineup: Hellborg on bass, Mitchel Forman (keyboards), Danny Gottlieb (drums), Bill Evans (saxophone), Billy Cobham (percussion). This produced Mahavishnu (1984, WEA), the one Warner Bros. album from this incarnation.

Axis (1986, Day Eight Music) — key early New York-facing record. Recorded partly at Electric Lady Studios (NY) and partly in Sweden. Personnel:

The Downtown New York and Axiom Period (1988–1994)

Bass (1988, Day Eight Music) — solo bass record. Co-produced with Kenneth Rasmusson. Bernie Worrell on keyboards; Bernard Fowler contributes. Ginger Baker appears here.

Material connection: Hellborg appeared on Middle Passage (1990, Axiom) — Bill Laswell’s world-music-inflected Material collective — and on Hallucination Engine (1994, Axiom/Island Records), the record featuring Sly Dunbar, Bootsy Collins, Ornette Coleman, and others.

The Word (1991, Axiom/Mango) — acoustic bass album. Co-produced with Bill Laswell. Engineers: Jason Corsaro and Oz Fritz. Tony Williams on drums; The Soldier String Quartet (David Soldier, Laura Seaton, Ron Lawrence, Mary Wooten). Covers Jimi Hendrix’s “Cherokee Mist”; co-writes with Tony Williams. Spiritual/classical register.

Deadline — Hellborg is a core member of this downtown project with Bill Laswell. On Down By Law (1985, Celluloid): bass on “Baliphone Dub” and “Gammatron.” On Dissident (1991, Day Eight Music): bass, principal writer, producer — essentially his own record released under the Deadline name.

Acoustic and Percussion Collaborations (1991–1993)

The Silent Life (1991, Day Eight Music) — with Ginger Baker; Wechter acoustic bass.

Unseen Rain (1992, Day Eight Music) — trio with Ginger Baker and Jens Johansson. Acoustic bass. Scud Noonan engineers/mixes throughout this period.

Shining Path (Discogs ID 212508) — project producing No Other World (1992, Day Eight Music).

Octave Of The Holy Innocents (1993, Day Eight Music) — Hellborg with Buckethead (acoustic guitar) and Michael Shrieve (drums). Recorded by Oz Fritz and Paul Berry. Five long-form tracks.

Shawn Lane Trilogy (1995–1997)

The most celebrated chapter of Hellborg’s discography: three records with Memphis guitarist Shawn Lane and rotating drummers, on Day Eight Music and then Bardo Records.

Abstract Logic (1995, Day Eight Music, DEM 035) — with Shawn Lane (guitar) and Kofi Baker (drums, son of Ginger Baker). Scud Noonan mixes; Stephane Jean records. Seven extended improvisations.

Temporal Analogues Of Paradise (1996, Day Eight Music) — with Shawn Lane and Jeff Sipe (Apt. Q-258).

Time Is The Enemy (1997, Bardo Records) — with Shawn Lane and Jeff Sipe. First release on Hellborg’s Bardo Records imprint.

Shawn Lane (born March 21, 1963, Memphis; died September 26, 2003, Memphis) was noted for exceptional speed and melodic invention on guitar. He had been a member of Black Oak Arkansas before meeting Hellborg. These three records are the primary documentation of his mature work.

Indian Music Phase (2000–2002)

After Lane’s increasing health problems limited touring, Hellborg shifted toward South Indian classical contexts.

Good People In Times Of Evil (2000, Bardo Records) — with V. Selvaganesh (Carnatic mridangam / kanjira percussionist). Produced by Hellborg.

Icon - A Transcontinental Gathering (2002, Bardo Records) — with V. Selvaganesh, V. Umamahesh, V. Umashankar. Hellborg on bass, writer, producer.

Personae (2002, Bardo Records) — composed and produced.

Art Metal and Later Work

Art Metal (Discogs ID 7801114) — trio with Mattias Ia Eklundh (guitar) and Anders Johansson (drums, brother of Jens Johansson). Art Metal (Vyakhyan-Kar) (2007, Bardo Records).

Ginger Baker Plus Special Friends Live @ The Jazz Cafe London 2009 (2010, Voiceprint) — Hellborg on bass guitar.

Let Their Hearts Desire (2020, self-released) — credited to Exit North; bass guitar.

The_Puzzle (2021, HevyDevy Records) — Devin Townsend project; bass credit.

RAF (6) (Discogs ID 247698) — duo with Jens Johansson; no releases in Discogs.
