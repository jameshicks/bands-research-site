Overview

Ron Reyes, born July 24, 1960 in Puerto Rico, is an American vocalist best known as the second singer of Los Angeles hardcore pioneers Black Flag, stepping in after Keith Morris and before Dez Cadena. Beyond Black Flag he has been a member of Redd Kross alongside the McDonald brothers and Piggy (6), with his recorded work spanning punk, hardcore, and occasional bluegrass across four decades. His catalogue clusters in two distinct eras — a handful of early-1980s appearances on Posh Boy and a renewed output through SST Records and smaller independents from the 2010s onward. Core collaborators from the Black Flag years — Greg Ginn, Chuck Dukowski, Brian Migdol, and Robo — account for the bulk of his shared credits, supplemented by the Redd Kross contingent of Jeff and Steven McDonald and Greg Hetson.

How Reyes came to join Red Cross (later Redd Kross)

The McDonalds’ band had started as the Tourists in Hawthorne in 1978, with Steve still in middle school. Greg Hetson came in as guitarist — reportedly a connection through Hawthorne High School. The original drummer was John Stielow (age 13 at the time of the first recordings).

The Tourists / early Red Cross orbit overlapped with the Black Flag circle centred on “the Church,” Black Flag’s rehearsal space in Hermosa Beach. Jeff and Steve McDonald attended Black Flag shows and became part of that South Bay scene. Reyes was already a presence at the Church, and that is where the McDonalds encountered him. The practical hook was straightforward: Reyes owned a drum kit. As the LA Beat article summarises, “the band assumed that he must know how to play it.” Reyes himself acknowledged that access to the Church’s free rehearsal space was an added incentive for agreeing to join.

Within roughly five weeks in the late summer–fall of 1979 the band renamed itself Red Cross, slotted Reyes in on drums in place of Stielow, played a gig with Black Flag at the Hong Kong Cafe in Chinatown, and recorded the debut EP at Media Art Studio in Hermosa Beach and Shelter Studios in Hollywood. The released lineup was Jeff McDonald (vocals, guitar), Steven McDonald (bass, vocals), Greg Hetson (guitar), Ron Reyes (drums). Reyes was 19 at the time of recording.

Reyes later recalled the session with dry candour: the producer removed his hi-hat and some cymbals to constrain what he could play, leaving him relying on “the only beat I knew, the ‘My Sharona’ beat.” Despite his self-described limitations as a drummer, he later reflected: “Best band I was ever in. It was all downhill from there.” (thelosangelesbeat.com, 2020-04-xx)

Both Hetson and Reyes departed soon after the EP: Hetson to form the Circle Jerks with Keith Morris, Reyes to join Black Flag.
