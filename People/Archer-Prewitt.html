Archer Prewitt

American musician and illustrator. Full name: Allen Archer Prewitt. Discogs ID: 239475. Also performs as “Lord Henry Wanton.”

Member of The Coctails (Chicago pop-jazz group) and The Sea And Cake. Also in Bangtails (ID: 1760204) and Mudhead (ID: 797414).

Provided cover illustration for the Evergreen (5) Pants Off / The Queen Song 7” (Hi-Ball Records, 1994), connecting his Coctails world to the Louisville punk band via the shared Hi-Ball Records relationship.

Released two solo records on Hi-Ball Records: In The Sun (1997) and White Sky (1999).

Hi-Ball Solo Records

In The Sun (1997, HBLP0497) — LP plus bonus 7”. Self-produced with Ken Sluiter. Chamber-pop / acoustic; strings, brass, woodwinds. Notable guest: Chris Ware (cartoonist) plays piano on “Leave It Gone” and electric piano on “I’m All You Know.” Trumpet by Dave Max Crawford; clarinet/flute by Paul Mertens; cello by Marina Peterson and Poppy Brandes. Former Coctails bandmates Mark Greenberg (bass) and John Upchurch (clarinet) also appear. Mastered by Roger Seibel.

White Sky (1999, HBLP-1099) — LP. Ken Sluiter engineered. Vocals by Edith Frost (tracks A4, A5, B4). Strings (Alison Chesley, Susan Voelz, Inger Carle, Shelley Weiss), trumpet/organ/electric piano by Dave Max Crawford, flute/sax/harmonica by Paul Mertens. Styles: Indie Rock, Post Rock. Final Hi-Ball release.
