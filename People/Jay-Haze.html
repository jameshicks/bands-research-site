Jay Haze

DJ, producer, and label owner. Real name Justin McNulty. Discogs artist ID: 71701. Born/based Philadelphia; relocated to Berlin in the early 2000s. Passed away August 2025.

Also records as: Dub Cord, Dub Surgeon, Montagespiral, The Architect, The Red Light Shining. Groups: Fuckpony (with Samim Winiger), Bearback (with Samim Winiger), Social Being (with Ricardo Villalobos), The Basstards (with Argenis Brito and Cem Oral), Sub Version (2).

Own labels: Contexterrior (ID: 10607, parent label) and its imprints Tuning Spork Records (ID: 3911), Future Dub (ID: 9689), Junion (ID: 57110), Textone (ID: 19114).

Biography

Philadelphia Origins and Early Berlin Period (2001–2004)

Justin McNulty grew up in Philadelphia and was active in the city’s underground electronic music scene before relocating to Berlin in the early 2000s. His earliest Discogs-documented releases appear on the Dutch label Future Dub, where he recorded under three of his aliases simultaneously: as Montagespiral (Future Dub 1, 2001), Dub Surgeon (Future Dub 2, 2002), and Dub Cord (Future Dub 3, 2003). This simultaneous multi-alias approach characterised his output throughout the decade.

Around the same time — with Berlin now as his base — he launched Contexterrior, a label with an explicit aesthetic statement: sub-bass, click, glitch, dub-influenced sparseness, and psychedelia. The first Contexterrior releases (cat. CNTX 01 and CNTX 02, 2002) were issued under the Jay Haze name and credited to the US. The label quickly became the hub of his own imprints, ultimately housing Tuning Spork Records, Future Dub, Junion, Textone, and ineedsuperfreak.fr as sublabels.

Simultaneously he was putting out under The Architect alias on the nascent Tuning Spork Records, which he co-founded with Someone Else (2) and Björn Hartmann. The first Architect release (My Shoes Are On Backwards And My Socks Don’t Match, 2001, spork 04) arrived before the label had even moved its operations from the US to Germany.

Contexterrior, Collaboration With Villalobos, and the Fuckpony Years (2004–2008)

The mid-2000s were his densest period. Two key moments defined his wider profile:

  1. Prefer Summer (Contexterrior, CNTX 08, 2004): A collaborative 12” with Ricardo Villalobos billed as “Ricardo Vs. Jay” — three tracks including the 12-minute title track. This cemented a working relationship that would span at least nine shared Discogs entries over the decade.

  2. Love For A Strange World (Kitty-Yo, KY05095CD, 2005): His debut album on the eclectic Berlin imprint Kitty-Yo, produced and mixed entirely by Haze with mastering by Stefan Betke (Pole). The record featured vocal contributions from D:exter and Argenis Brito, and documented his move toward a more song-centred, soul-influenced strand of minimal electronic music.

In parallel he formed Fuckpony with Swiss producer Samim Winiger. The project released extensively on Get Physical Music (2006–2010) and BPitch Control (2008–2010), with releases including Children Of Love, Ride The Pony, Switch The Lights (Cocoon Recordings), and two volumes of The Dark Side Of The Pony. The Fuckpony alias leaned toward a more club-functional tech-house sound.

He and Villalobos also recorded together as Social Being, releasing Free Your Mind (Tuning Spork, “tsextra”, 2007) — two untitled sides totalling over 26 minutes, firmly in the deep-dub-minimal style.

Tuning Spork Records grew during this period into a genuine imprint with a stable of artists including Michal Ho, Dan Curtin, Shyza Minelli, and Alex Celler. His own Tuning Spork output of the era includes Berlin Pimping Part 2 (2006) and the provocatively titled Ass To Mouth (spork 32, 2008), the B-side of which (“Free Ride”) is credited to “Ricardo vs. Jay”.

Fabric 47 and Mature Period (2009–2014)

In 2009 Haze compiled and mixed Fabric 47 for the London series — an 18-track mix CD (Fabric fabric93) spanning minimal, tech house, and techno, with tracks by Fuckpony, himself, and collaborators including Pheek, Alex Celler, and Miss Fitz. The tracklist included the Jay Haze / Ricardo Villalobos co-production “Mellow Dee”, which also appeared on the split 12” Mellow Dee / Sunday Prayer (Contexterrior CNTX 25, 2008).

His relationship with Desolat (Loco Dice’s imprint) produced Mama Coca (DESOLAT 005, 2008), with a remix by SIS. He placed work on other European imprints during this stretch: Leftroom, Supernature, Welcome To Masomenos, and Sonora Records (US).

The 2014 album Finding Oriya — released as Jay Haze & ESB (6) (Elan Benaroch) on Leftroom (LEFTCD006) — was his last major long-form statement, a 10-track CD that mixed deep house, tech house, and ambient with vocal guest Tyler (3) on “Refine To Deepness”.

Final Years (2016–2025)

The 2016 1840 EP (Contexterrior/Tuning Spork, CNTX/TS 1003) with Kaan Bulak marked a return to his own label after a quieter period. The Social Being project with Villalobos produced 40 AGES (2018, Contexterrior/Tuning Spork), the last recorded output currently in the Discogs database.

Jay Haze passed away in August 2025.
