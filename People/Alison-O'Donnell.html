Overview

Born Alison Bools in Dublin on 5 October 1952, she is an Irish singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist who performs and records under several names, including Alison Leigh, Alison Williams, and Assie O’Donnell. Her Discogs-tracked work begins in 1998, running across three decades in folk, folk rock, psychedelic rock, and experimental ambient territory. She is a member of Oeda and United Bible Studies, with her most consistent collaborators being David Colohan (22 shared releases), Steven Collins, and The Owl Service. Her releases are spread across a network of small independent imprints, with Fruits de Mer Records and Talking Elephant Records among the recurring homes.
