Overview

Fisher Rose is a Canadian multi-instrumentalist — primarily a drummer but also credited on vibraphone, trumpet, and percussion — best known as the original drummer of The New Pornographers and a long-standing member of Destroyer (4). Rose came up in the Vancouver indie scene with The Lamps in the mid-1990s before joining both bands. Their catalogue spans from 2000 onward across 71 credits, centred on Merge Records and Matador releases, with Daniel Bejar, David Carswell, and A.C. Newman among their closest recurring collaborators.
