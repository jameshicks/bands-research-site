Igor Cavalera

Born Igor Graziano Cavalera, September 4 1970, Belo Horizonte, Brazil. Drummer. Co-founder of Sepultura (with brother Max Cavalera). Left Sepultura in 2009 after two decades as their drummer. Active in electronic/experimental music as half of Mixhell (with Laima Leyton) and Petbrick (with Wayne Adams). Touring and studio drummer for Soulwax since 2016. Releases solo experimental music under the alias Mudo (3).

Discogs artist ID: 254253. Aliases on Discogs: Alucinações Sônicas, Igor Skullcrusher, Mudo (3).

Early Career — Sepultura (1984–2006)

Igor co-founded Sepultura in Belo Horizonte in 1984 alongside his brother Max Cavalera, bassist Paulo Jr., and guitarist Andreas Kisser (who joined in 1987, replacing original guitarist Jairo Guedz). The band spent the late 1980s on the Brazilian label Cogumelo Produções before signing to Roadracer/Roadrunner Records.

Igor drummed on every Sepultura studio album from Morbid Visions (1986) through Dante XXI (2006). Across that span the band moved from raw thrash-death metal into crossover and groove metal territory (Chaos A.D., 1993; Roots, 1996), then post-Max experimentation (Against, 1998; Nation, 2001; Roorback, 2003). Igor contributed to more than drums on several releases — he co-designed the “Sepultribe Chaos Logo” (credited on Refuse/Resist 1994, Against 1998, and the 2013 Max Cavalera box set), and received co-writing credits on numerous albums.

Cavalera Conspiracy (2007–ongoing)

After Max left Sepultura in 1996 and Igor departed in 2006, the two brothers reunited as Cavalera Conspiracy in 2007. The band also operates under the alias Cavalera. Members have included Marc Rizzo (guitar), Johnny Bechtel (bass), and Joseph Duplantier (guest). On Discogs the project is also listed as the revived Cavalera name.

Mixhell (2009–2018)

Mixhell was Igor’s electronic duo with his wife Laima Leyton (a Brazilian DJ/producer). The project released records on Boysnoize Records and Southern Fried Records, sitting at an intersection of electro, techno, and industrial. Also included Max Blum as a collaborator. Discography on Discogs:

Soulwax (2016–ongoing)

Igor joined Soulwax as touring drummer in 2016. He is credited as a performer (“Playing On This Record Are”) on the studio album From Deewee (2017, Play It Again Sam / [PIAS]), alongside fellow performers Blake Davies, Laima Leyton, Stefaan Van Leuven, Victoria Smith, and the Dewaele brothers. He is also credited on All Systems Are Lying (2025, Because Music / Deewee) under the spelling “Iggor Cavalera.”

Petbrick (2018–2022)

Petbrick is a noise/industrial duo with UK producer Wayne Adams (4) (also of Big Lad, Death Pedals, DEAFBRICK). The project operates in harsh electronic / noise-metal territory. Released on Rocket Recordings and Neurot Recordings.

DEAFBRICK (2020)

DEAFBRICK is a larger collective that grew out of the Petbrick world, adding Brazilian musicians Douglas Leal, Marcelo Dos Santos, and Mariano Sarine alongside Igor and Wayne Adams. Their self-titled debut (2020) was released jointly on Neurot Recordings and Rocket Recordings.

Absent In Body (2022)

Absent In Body is a sludge/doom supergroup featuring Igor alongside Scott Kelly (Neurosis), Colin H. Van Eeckhout (Amenra), and Mathieu Vandekerckhove (Amenra/Oathbreaker). Album Plague God released 2022 on Relapse Records.

Mudo (3) — Solo Electronic Work

Igor releases experimental/ambient electronic music under the name Mudo (3) (Discogs artist ID: 8087495). One official Discogs release traced: Gristleism (2020, Tartarus Records, Netherlands) — a tribute/covers project referencing Throbbing Gristle.
