Julie Cafritz

Guitarist, vocalist, producer. Discogs artist ID: 377472. Real name: Julia Cafritz. Also credited as J. Cafritz, J.C., Juicy Cafritz, Julia Cafritz, Julie Caflitz. Wikipedia: Julia Cafritz.

Overview

Julia Cafritz (born May 5, 1965, Washington, D.C.) is one of the defining guitarists of the NYC noise-rock underground of the late 1980s and 1990s. She co-founded Pussy Galore (2) with Jon Spencer at Brown University in 1985, was a driving force in that band through roughly 1988–89, then co-founded Free Kitten with Kim Gordon in 1992. Between and around those two anchors she played with Action Swingers, The Velvet Monkeys, STP (5), and Halo Of Kitten, and produced the entirety of Guv’ner’s studio catalog (1993–1996) — one of the more underrated production runs of the Wiiija Records / US noise-indie overlap era.

Her father was Washington, D.C. real estate developer Conrad Cafritz; her grandfather was the philanthropist Morris Cafritz. Her younger sister is Daisy Von Furth (also member of Halo Of Kitten). Cafritz attended Brown before dropping out when Pussy Galore formed, later completing B.A. and M.A. degrees at New York University (Wikipedia). After relocating with her husband Bob Lawton (booking agent; Labor Board) to Florence, Massachusetts, she taught English at Holyoke Community College (Wikipedia).

Wikipedia describes her as “a cult figure from the New York City noise music scene of the 1990s.”

Pussy Galore (1985–1988/89)

Cafritz co-founded the band with Jon Spencer and John Hammill while at Brown University in 1985. After the debut 7” Feel Good About Your Body, the band moved to New York City, expanding to include Neil Hagerty, Cristina Martinez, and Bob Bert (drums, ex-Sonic Youth). Cafritz played guitar and occasional vocals through the band’s most confrontational period: the Groovy Hate Fuck EP, the Exile on Main St cassette cover, Pussy Gold 5000, Right Now!, and Sugarshit Sharp.

Her departure came around the time of Dial ‘M’ For Motherfucker (1989) — Wikipedia notes “Cafritz appears on some of this album but not the entirety. Dial M marks a point at which Cafritz and the band began fighting, ultimately leading to her leaving the band.”

The 1992 double-LP retrospective Corpse Love - The First Year (Caroline Records / Hut Recordings) documents both the Hammill-era and Bert-era lineups in which she appears.

STP (5) (1989–1990)

Cafritz formed the short-lived all-girl band STP after leaving Pussy Galore in 1989. Members: Cafritz (guitar), Melissa Huffsmith-Roth (guitar), Jacqui Nimitz (guitar, vocals), Sally Edroso (drums). Their sole release was the Smoke ‘Em 7” (1990, Circuit Records (2)), produced by Don Fleming and Kim Gordon, engineered by Jim Waters, with photography by Michael Lavine. The band toured with Nirvana and Sonic Youth (Wikipedia).

Action Swingers (1989–1991)

After meeting fellow CBGB Record Canteen clerk Ned Hayden (Wikipedia), Cafritz joined his band as lead guitarist. The initial unit with Don Fleming (production, drums) recorded the debut single Bum My Trip / “Kicked in the Head” (1989, Noiseville). The Fear Of A Fucked Up Planet 7” (1990, Primo Scree) followed. The self-titled debut LP (1991, Primo Scree) featured Cafritz (lead guitar, vocals, credited as Julia Cafritz), Ned Hayden (vocals, guitar), Pete Shore (bass), and Bob Bert (drums); engineered and co-produced by Wharton Tiers and Jim Waters, with photography by Michael Lavine. J Mascis also played drums on two early tracks (Wikipedia). Cafritz left by early 1992 to form Free Kitten. A BBC Radio 1 John Peel session was recorded in March 1992 just after her departure. The band continued under Hayden through 1998.

The Velvet Monkeys (1990)

Cafritz played guitar and keyboards on the The Velvet Monkeys album Rake (1990, Rough Trade), a Don Fleming-led project. The Rake lineup also included Thurston Moore (bass, guitar), J Mascis (guitar, vocals, drums), Jay Spiegel (drums), John Hammill (drums, guitar), and Malcolm Riviera (guitar, keyboards). Her credit is confirmed in release credits as “Julia Cafritz.”

Free Kitten (1992–1997, 2007–2008)

In 1992, Cafritz and Kim Gordon formed Free Kitten (initially called Kitten), joined by Yoshimi (Boredoms) on drums and Mark Ibold (Pavement) on bass. The band recorded and toured sporadically through 1997, releasing three studio albums and several EPs, primarily on Wiiija Records (UK) and Kill Rock Stars (US). Cafritz is credited as guitarist, vocalist, and co-writer (songs jointly credited to Free Kitten, Kim Gordon, and Julie Cafritz from Sentimental Education onward).

Key production collaborators: Don Fleming and Jim Waters (early releases); Wharton Tiers (engineering/production on Sentimental Education and the Japan tour EP); Justin Pizzoferrato (producer/engineer, Inherit 2008). J Mascis appears as a session drummer on Sentimental Education (1997).

The 2008 reunion LP Inherit (Ecstatic Peace!) was co-produced by Free Kitten and Justin Pizzoferrato.

Halo Of Kitten (1995)

A short-lived project. Members: Cafritz, Daisy Von Furth (her sister), Tim Mac, and Tom Hazelmyer. Their only Discogs release is the Porn 1 split 7” (1995, Amphetamine Reptile Records) shared with Hammerhead (2).

Production Work: Guv’ner (1993–1996)

Cafritz produced the complete studio output of Guv’ner, an NYC angular pop/noise-rock trio fronted by Pumpkin Wentzel (Cafritz’s childhood friend) and Charles Gansa. Cafritz brought the band to Thurston Moore’s attention, leading to the debut EP’s release on his Ecstatic Peace! label.
