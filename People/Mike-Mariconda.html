Overview

Mike Mariconda is an American garage rock guitarist and producer who began playing in the New York area in 1977 and joined the Raunch Hands in 1984, later relocating to Austin, Texas and then Spain. Beyond the Raunch Hands he has played in The Devil Dogs, The A-Bones, South Filthy, Los Ass-Draggers, and Crackhouse. His 584 Discogs credits — spanning production, recording, mixing, and guitar — make him one of the more prolific behind-the-boards figures in the underground garage and punk world. His densest collaborative cluster runs through Albert Caiati, Jorge Explosion, Marc Tena, and The New Bomb Turks, while his longest continuous label relationship is with Crypt Records (1989–2023); Folc Records and Sympathy for the Record Industry account for much of the rest of his catalogue footprint.
