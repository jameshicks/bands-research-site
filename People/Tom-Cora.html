Tom Cora

American cellist, improviser, and composer. Born Thomas Henry Corra, 14 September 1953, Yancey Mills, Virginia. Died 9 April 1998 in France. Discogs artist ID: 198937.

Overview

Tom Cora was one of the central instrumental voices in the NYC downtown/experimental scene of the 1980s and early 1990s. A cellist by training, he extended the instrument into avant-rock, free improvisation, and noise, playing on and mixing records by an extraordinarily wide range of collaborators. He was a founding member of Skeleton Crew (2) (with Fred Frith and, on The Country Of Blinds, Zeena Parkins) and produced/engineered Parkins’s first two solo records. His most visible collaborative work was two full LPs co-credited with The Ex — Scrabbling At The Lock (1991) and And The Weathermen Shrug Their Shoulders (1993) — which brought him to European audiences. He was also a long-standing member of Curlew, and appeared on records by John Zorn, Eugene Chadbourne, Hans Reichel, Christian Marclay, Chris Cutler, and many others.

Tzadik released “Hallelujah, Anyway — Remembering Tom Cora”, a compilation in his memory, in 1999.

Name variations used on records: Cora, T. Cora, T.Cora, TC, Tom, Tom Corra, トム・コラ.
