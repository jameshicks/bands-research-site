Overview

Swiss keyboardist and songwriter Juliana Müller was active in the post-punk and experimental underground from 1983 through the 1990s, contributing piano, keyboards, sampler, and electronics across 14 Discogs credits. Her catalogue is concentrated in the 1980s and 1990s, with releases on Rough Trade (1983), RecRec Music, UX Records, and Off Course Records among others. The density of her overlap with Liliput and that band’s core members — Astrid Spirig, Klau Schiff, Marlene Marder, Beat Schlatter — places her firmly within the Zürich post-punk and avantgarde circle.
