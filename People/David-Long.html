Overview

David Long is a guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter best known for his membership in Into Paradise and The Sentimental Tourists, working across alternative rock, indie rock, shoegaze, and indie pop. His recorded output stretches from 1988 through the 2020s, with the core of his catalogue concentrated in the early 1990s on Ensign and Setanta — labels central to the Irish alternative scene of that era. His closest working relationships were with Into Paradise bandmates James Eadie, Rachel Tighe, and Ronan Clarke, with producers Dick Meaney and Chris Sheldon also recurring across sessions. Notably, he shares five credits with Adrian Borland, a collaboration that bridges the Irish indie world with the broader post-punk circuit.
