Discogs ID: 106812 Role: Percussionist Location: Frankfurt, Germany Active: 2000–2012 (Discogs credits) Name variations: Heiko Himminghoffen, Himmighoffen

Biography

Heiko Himmighoffen is a Frankfurt-based percussionist who worked primarily within the INFRACom! jazz-house scene through the 2000s. His credit trail runs from a Dub session in 2000 through a decade of ensemble work, almost entirely in a session/sideman role rather than as a primary artist.

Early work (2000)

His first documented credit is on Tornado Warning (Elektrolux, 2000) by Syncromatic Dubsters, playing timbales, tumbas (percussion), and triangle alongside producer Marcus Darius and the Syncromatic Dubsters collective.

[re:jazz] (2002–2012)

The bulk of Himmighoffen’s recorded career is tied to -re-jazz- (Discogs: 114832), the Frankfurt ensemble assembled around Matthias Vogt on the INFRACom! label. He is one of the band’s core members, credited on every studio album from the debut through 2012:

  • Re:Jazz (INFRACom!, 2002) — percussion
  • Point Of View (INFRACom!, 2004) — percussion
  • Donaueschingen (INFRACom!, 2005) — percussion (live concert recording)
  • Push Push (INFRACom!, 2005) — percussion (single)
  • Expansion (INFRACom!, 2006) — percussion
  • Kaleidoscope (INFRACom!, 2012) — percussion

The [re:jazz] lineup around Himmighoffen also included Andreas Manns (bass), Hanno Busch (guitar), Volker Schmidt (drums), Oliver Leicht (saxophone), Inga Lühning (vocals), and — by 2004 — Matthias Vogt on piano and production.

Motorcitysoul sessions (2004, 2007)

Because several [re:jazz] members doubled as session players for the Motorcitysoul duo (Matthias Vogt + Christian Rindermann), Himmighoffen appears on both Motorcitysoul releases where live percussion was used:

  • Did You Expect That? (INFRACom!, IC 113-2, 2004) — percussion on tracks 1, 2, 3, 6, 9 (“It’s On,” “Written In The Stars,” “The Dream,” “With You ‘92,” “Take Care”)
  • Aura (STIR15 Recordings, 2007) — percussion; produced by C-Rock and Matthias Vogt

On the debut album he is a track-level credit only (not in the overall release credits), underlining his role as a hired hand rather than a full band member.

ASP session (2005)

An outlier in his discography is a credit on Aus Der Tiefe (Schmetterling Zyklus, Teil 4) (Trisol, 2005) by German gothic-rock band ASP (Alexander F. Spreng). He plays bodhrán, djembe, and tabla — a broader percussive palette than his jazz-house work. Also notable: the drummer on this record is Oliver Himmighoffen, likely a relative.

Cosmic Orient and Sound System Players

Himmighoffen is listed as a member of two further ensembles on Discogs, though neither has credits in the database:

  • Cosmic Orient (Discogs: 142571) — also including Herb-T (Tino Pavlis), Bernhard Faltermeier, Sham (10), and Walter Dorn
  • Sound System Players (Discogs: 151828) — also including Frederik Feindt, Peter Koch, Robert Lochmann, Christian Reuning, and Seppl Niemeyer

Scene Connections

  • INFRACom! Frankfurt network: Himmighoffen is embedded in the tight circle of musicians who recorded for INFRACom! throughout the 2000s. The same players — Vogt, Manns, Busch, Schmidt, Leicht, Lühning — appear together across [re:jazz] albums and Motorcitysoul sessions.
  • Matthias Vogt: The common thread across nearly all of Himmighoffen’s credits. Vogt produced both the [re:jazz] and Motorcitysoul material where Himmighoffen appears, and is central to Motorcitysoul.
  • Oliver Himmighoffen (Discogs: 452693) — listed as drummer on the ASP Aus Der Tiefe session; likely a relative. No further DB context.
  • Syncromatic Dubsters: The earliest credit places him in a Frankfurt dub/electronic context predating the [re:jazz] relationship.
