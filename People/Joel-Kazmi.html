Joel Kazmi

Discogs ID: 462058
Name variation: Joel Kasmi

Canadian recording engineer based primarily out of Metalworks Studios in Mississauga, Ontario. Active from 1999, Kazmi built his early career as an assistant engineer on the Toronto session circuit before developing a long-running professional relationship with Sum 41. He has also accumulated credits across Canadian rock, R&B, jazz, and heavy metal, and from the mid-2000s onwards took on production roles for independent releases.

Biography

1999 — Early Years as Assistant Engineer

Kazmi’s first Discogs-documented credits date to 1999, when he was working as an assistant engineer at multiple Toronto-area studios. That year he appeared on:

  • Kim Stockwood — 12 Years Old (EMI Music Canada, 1999): assistant engineer on an album produced and engineered by Michael Phillip Wojewoda, mixed in part by Mike Shipley. A large-scale project with string arrangements and a full band roster.
  • The Sidemen — Dig In (Kingnote Records, 1999): assistant engineer. The blues/roots group produced by the band themselves, with mixing by Fergus Jemison Marsh and Paul Reddick.
  • Richard Egües & Friends — Cuban Sessions (Latin World Entertainment Group, 1999): mastering assistant on a Venezuelan release — suggests early cross-studio utility work.
  • Various — What A Wonderful World (EMI Music Canada / Heartland Music, 1999): assistant mixer and assistant recording engineer on a Canadian compilation.

He also received a “Musical Assistance” credit on several Solitudes label ambient/nature recordings (Avalon Music) in 2000 — a minor utility credit suggesting studio floor work rather than formal engineering.

2000 — Sum 41 and the Metalworks Connection

The key break came in 2000 with Sum 41’s debut EP, Half Hour Of Power (Island Records / Big Rig, 2000). Kazmi is credited as assistant engineer on tracks 1–3, 6, 7, and 9–11, working under primary engineers Blair Calibaba and Ed Kroutner. Producers were Deryck Whibley and Greig Nori; mixing was split between Calibaba, Jerry Finn, L. Stu Young, and Matt Hyde. Mastering: Howie Weinberg and Roger Lian.

Also in 2000, Kazmi assisted on Geddy Lee — My Favorite Headache (Anthem/Atlantic, 2000) — a high-profile Canadian project with Lee, Ben Mink, and David Leonard as producers. Kazmi’s credit: “Mixed By, Engineer [Assistant, Metalworks],” alongside Ian Bodzasi. This is a significant early credit: the album also featured Matt Cameron on drums.

Additional 2000 credits:

  • Various — Only Be In Love (Universal/Universal Urban, 2000): additional recording credit
  • Various — Sleepless (BMG Music Canada / Vik Recordings, 2000): assisted mixing and all-vocals recording

2001 — All Killer No Filler and *NSYNC

Sum 41 — All Killer No Filler (Island Records, 2001) is Kazmi’s most commercially prominent engineering credit. He is listed as “Engineer [Assisted By At Metalworks]” alongside other Metalworks assistants. Main engineer: Joe McGrath. Mixer: Tom Lord-Alge. Mastering: Brian Gardner. Producer: Jerry Finn.

In 2001 he also received an “Engineer [Assistant Vocal Recording]” credit on the *NSYNC single “Pop” (Jive), produced and engineered by BT and Justin Timberlake. This appears to be a session at a Toronto studio rather than a core production role.

Additional 2001:

  • Dan Hill — Collection: Illuminations (EMI Music Canada, 2001): digital editing and remixed assistant; Kazmi worked as assistant on a remix/compilation DVD project.
  • Various — What A Wonderful Christmas (EMI Music Canada / StraightWay, 2001): assistant engineer.

2002 — Rush, Vapor Trails, and Does This Look Infected?

2002 saw two major credits:

Rush — Vapor Trails (Anthem/Atlantic, 2002): “Mixed By [Assisted By]” alongside primary mixer David Leonard. This is Kazmi’s most artistically significant peer-association — Rush produced the album themselves alongside Paul Northfield. A high-pressure record made after an extended hiatus; Kazmi’s involvement at Metalworks connects directly to the Leonard–Metalworks relationship.

Sum 41 — Does This Look Infected? (Island Records, 2002): “Engineer [Additional, Metalworks Studios].” Primary recording engineer: Greg Gordon. Mix: Andy Wallace (main album), Tom Lord-Alge (disc 1). Producer: Greig Nori. Several other Metalworks assistants shared the additional engineering credit (Ian Bodzasi, Darius Szczepaniak, L. Stu Young, Ed Krautner).

Additional 2002:

  • NSYNC — “Motivation” (Mercury, Europe): “Musical Assistance” — a peripheral utility credit on a European single.
  • Various — Tender Lullabies (Somerset Entertainment, 2002): assistant engineer.

2003 — Jacksoul, Triumph Live, and First Solo Mix

Jacksoul — Resurrected (Vik Recordings, 2003): Kazmi is credited as both “Recorded By” and “Mixed By [Uncredited]” (alongside Brad Haehnel). Producer: Haydain Neale and K-Cut. This is one of his first appearances as a credited recording and mix engineer (rather than assistant) on a major-label release.

Triumph (2) — Live At The US Festival (TML Entertainment, 2003): “Mixed By [Assistant]” under primary mixer Richard Chycki.

Sum 41 — “The Hell Song” (Mercury, UK, 2003): additional engineer — a single pulled from Does This Look Infected?.

Four Square — Three Chords…One Capo (Bad Taste Records, Sweden, 2003): credited as “Mixed By” — his first full, credited mix on an album-length release. Producer and main engineer: Simon Head.

He is also credited as “Recorded By” on A Sides Win: Singles 1992–2005 (Imperial Records Japan, 2005) — a Rush compilation, reflecting his Metalworks work on Vapor Trails.

2006 — Engineer / Recorder on Jacksoul’s mySOUL

Jacksoul — mySOUL (Sony BMG, 2006): “Recorded By” across the full album (tracks 2–14). Primary producer and mixer: Brad Haehnel. A Toronto-recorded R&B project with a full band and horn section. This is among Kazmi’s most complete studio recording credits on a major Canadian R&B release.

Other 2006:

  • Various — Love Stories (In+Out Records, Germany, 2006): “Recorded By, Mixed By” — a German jazz release.
  • Various — Airless Alarm (Kirtland Records, US, 2006): “Engineer” — a US project, demonstrating his reach beyond Canadian sessions.

2017–2020 — Production Credits (Possessed Steel)

The most substantial shift in Kazmi’s Discogs record comes with his work on two albums for Canadian heavy metal act Possessed Steel:

  • Possessed Steel — Order Of The Moon (self-released, 2017): credited as “Producer” — his first full production credit on record.
  • Possessed Steel — Aedris (Temple Of Mystery Records, 2020): “Producer, Recorded By, Mixed By” — a full-service production role covering all studio stages.

These credits mark a transition from session engineering to independent production, focused on the underground Canadian metal scene.
