Overview

Dylan Cristy is an American musician and visual artist best known as the co-founder of The Dylan Group alongside Adam Pierce. The duo released seven records on Bubble Core Records between 1997 and 2000, plus one on FatCat Records, making them among the most-documented artists on the label Pierce founded. Cristy’s instrument credits on The Dylan Group recordings — vibraphone, percussion, and Korg MS20 synthesizer — situate the project in the percussion-and-electronics post-rock terrain the label favored in its formative years.

Beyond The Dylan Group, Cristy continued to appear as a peripheral member of the wider Adam Pierce orbit: credited as a performer on Mice Parade’s 2003 LP Obrigado Saudade (vibraphone on one track), listed explicitly as a member of the Mice Parade ensemble on the 2007 self-titled album and the 2010 LP What It Means To Be Left-Handed, and featured on Lymbyc Systym’s 2007 album Love Your Abuser (Mush). He also contributed artwork to several Bubble Core releases.

Discogs lists Cristy as a member of two overlapping entities: The Dylan Group (Discogs ID: 12536) and its alias Dylan & Adam 1 (Discogs ID: 14708).

The Dylan Group (1997–2000)

The Dylan Group formed around the partnership of Cristy and Adam Pierce. The project ran in parallel with HiM (the experimental collective led by Doug Scharin) and with the early phase of Pierce’s Mice Parade project, all orbiting Bubble Core Records.

Ur-Klang Search (2000) is the most extensively documented Dylan Group release in Discogs: it shows a project that by its final album had expanded well beyond a two-person duo, drawing in a rotating cast of players. Track 9 is a cover credited to Paterson, Weston, and Fehlmann — the writing credits behind The Orb.

The 1997 split with HiM (release ID: 334017) is also the earliest release on which Cristy receives an individual songwriter credit, confirming he was writing-credited on The Dylan Group material from early in the project’s run.

The Dylan Group also appeared on early Bubble Core compilations: The Resonance Found At The Core Of A Bubble (1996, BC-007) included both The Dylan Group and the alias Dylan & Adam 1; the 1995 1st Bubble Core Records Sampler (BC-006) featured Cristy under his own name alongside Pierce-related acts.

The ensemble credit on What It Means To Be Left-Handed is the fullest articulation in Discogs of Mice Parade as a collective rather than a Pierce solo project. Cristy is among eight named musicians.

Outside Collaborations

Lymbyc Systym — Love Your Abuser (2007, Mush)
Cristy is credited as “Featuring” on this album by Lymbyc Systym (Jared Bell, Michael Bell), a Phoenix-based electronic/post-rock duo. The album also features Jimmy LaValle (Album Leaf), Kevin Tangney, and Ryan Breen. This is Cristy’s only Discogs credit outside the Pierce/Bubble Core/FatCat axis.

Visual Art Credits

Cristy contributed artwork to multiple Bubble Core releases:

  • More Adventures In Lying Down… (The Dylan Group, 1999) — artwork alongside Bodie Chewning, Rudi Reitberg, and Shane Deblasio
  • Ur-Klang Search (The Dylan Group, 2000) — artwork alongside Rachel Grayson and Rudi Reitberg; Reitberg also handled art direction

This places Cristy in a consistent role across the label’s visual production, alongside Rudi Reitberg (who served as art director across much of the Bubble Core catalog).
