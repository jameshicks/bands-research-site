Biography

Daniel Villarreal (full name Daniel Villarreal-Carrillo) is a Panamanian-born musician and DJ based in Chicago. He operates in the city’s post-jazz / Latin-experimental underground, bridging Afro-Latin percussion traditions with the broader International Anthem ecosystem of improvised and hybrid music.

His main band contexts are Dos Santos Anti-Beat Orquesta — a Chicago Latin-experimental group whose members include Alex Chavez and Nathan Karagianis, who released City Of Mirrors (2021) on International Anthem — and The Los Sundowns, a duo with Chicano guitarist Beto Martinez, which released the self-titled The Los Sundowns EP (2021) on Lechehouse Music. Villarreal plays drums and percussion in both groups and handled photography on the Sundowns release.

As a solo artist Villarreal released Panamá 77 (2022, International Anthem) — a fully credited production effort on which he served as producer, editor, and mixer alongside co-producer Dave Vettraino and Scott McNiece. The album documents session recordings that drew in a wide cast of International Anthem–adjacent players: Aquiles Navarro (of Irreversible Entanglements), Marta Sofia Honer, Kellen Harrison, Bardo Martinez, Ben Lumsdaine, Doug Malone, and others as recording engineers/performers. The record was mastered by David Allen (10) and photographed by Ariana Lebedev and Carolina Sánchez.

In 2023 he appeared as a percussionist (congas) on Jaimie Branch’s posthumous Fly Or Die Fly Or Die Fly Or Die ((World War)) — appearing on tracks A2 and B1–B3 alongside the core band of Jason Ajemian, Chad Taylor, and Lester St. Louis, with contributions from Rob Frye, Nick Broste, and Akenya Seymour. The same year he played drums on Rudy De Anda’s Closet Botanist (Karma Chief Records), produced by Adrian Quesada of Black Pumas.

Also in 2023, he released Lados B (International Anthem) — a trio record with Chicago guitarist Jeff Parker and bassist Anna Butterss, produced with Dave Vettraino and Scott McNiece. The album features a guest turn by pianist Neal Francis. Lados B is his most compositionally credited solo release, with Villarreal, Parker, and Butterss sharing writing credits throughout.

Labels / Scene

Villarreal is primarily associated with International Anthem Recording Company (Chicago, IL; label ID: 799458), which has released all three of his primary-credited records (City Of Mirrors via Dos Santos, Panamá 77, Lados B) and the Jaimie Branch posthumous album. International Anthem is the anchor label for Chicago’s contemporary jazz-adjacent experimental scene, home to Makaya McCraven, Jeff Parker, Irreversible Entanglements, Dos Santos, and many others.

His one release outside the International Anthem orbit is The Los Sundowns EP on Lechehouse Music (Texas-based Chicano/Latin roots label), and a session credit on Rudy De Anda’s Closet Botanist for Karma Chief Records (soul/funk subsidiary of Colemine Records).

The scene he inhabits is a Chicago post-jazz / Latin-experimental nexus: players who move between structured composition, free improvisation, and Afro-Latin rhythm traditions — overlapping heavily with the International Anthem roster, the Tortoise / Isotope 217 lineage (via Jeff Parker), and the New York free jazz world (via Aquiles Navarro / Irreversible Entanglements).
