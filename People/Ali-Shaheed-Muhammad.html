Ali Shaheed Muhammad

Discogs ID: 10378 Real name: Ali Shaheed Muhammad Jones Origin: Brooklyn, NY Active: 1989–present

DJ and producer. Co-founder and DJ of A Tribe Called Quest. Co-founder of the production collective The Ummah with Q-Tip and J Dilla (James Yancey, Discogs ID: 246270), which produced ATCQ’s final two albums: Beats, Rhymes And Life (1996) and The Love Movement (1998). Member of Native Tongues collective.

Post-ATCQ: co-founded Lucy Pearl with Raphael Saadiq and Dawn Robinson (2000, Penalty Recordings). Co-composed and co-produced scores for Netflix series Luke Cage (2016) and Luke Cage Season 2 (2018) on Mondo. Co-runs Jazz Is Dead label with Adrian Younge, a prolific ongoing series of collaborations with jazz legends (Jazz Is Dead 001–24+, 2020–present).
