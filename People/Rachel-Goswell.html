Rachel Goswell

Oct 27, 20268 min read

Rachel Goswell

Discogs ID: 228094 Born: May 16, 1971, Fareham, Hampshire, England Active: 1989–present

British vocalist, guitarist, and songwriter. Co-vocalist of Slowdive (1989–1995, 2014–present) and co-founder of Mojave 3 (1995–2006). Also released a solo album on 4AD (2004) and has participated in numerous collaborative and guest projects including Minor Victories, The Soft Cavalry, Beachy Head, and guest work with Mark Kozelek / Sun Kil Moon and American Football.

Biography

Early Years & Slowdive (1989–1995)

Goswell met Neil Halstead as a teenager in Reading, Berkshire, and the two formed the nucleus of what would become Slowdive. The band’s earliest release under the pre-Slowdive name The Pumpkin Fairies (1989) predates their signing to Creation Records. Slowdive proper debuted with the self-titled EP in 1990 and their first album Just For A Day in 1991, on which Goswell shared lead vocal and guitar duties with Halstead.

Goswell and Halstead functioned as a dual-vocal partnership throughout the band’s original run. On Just For A Day (1991) and Souvlaki (1993), both are credited as Vocals, Guitar; on the ambient and more inward-facing Pygmalion (1995), Goswell is credited as Performer and additionally as Lyrics By on tracks 3 and 7 — one of her rare explicit co-writing credits during the Slowdive period. Composition credit on Pygmalion went formally to Halstead alone, but Goswell’s lyrical contribution to two tracks is documented in the Discogs credit. All five members performed on Pygmalion; production was shared between the band and Chris Hufford.

Slowdive were dropped by Creation following Pygmalion and dissolved in 1995.

Mojave 3 (1995–2006)

Immediately after Slowdive’s dissolution, Goswell, Halstead, and drummer Ian McCutcheon formed Mojave 3, pivoting from shoegaze into a country-folk direction heavily indebted to American roots music. They were joined by Simon Rowe (guitar), Alan Forrester (piano/keyboards), and Christopher Andrews (piano) — Andrews, credited on Ask Me Tomorrow, is also listed in the Mojave 3 member roster on Discogs; he later played in Cuba.

Goswell’s role in Mojave 3 evolved across the catalog: on Ask Me Tomorrow she is credited only as Vocals, while by Spoon And Rafter and Puzzles Like You she is also credited on Bass Guitar. All songwriting credit on the main albums belongs to Halstead (sometimes co-credited to McCutcheon on the 2003 EP Bluebird Of Happiness). Mark Van Hoen produced Out Of Tune and Excuses For Travellers.

Solo Work (2004)

Goswell released her sole solo album Waves Are Universal (2004, 4AD, cat. CAD 2414CD), preceded by The Sleep Shelter EP (2004, 4AD). Both are credited to Rachel Goswell as primary artist.

Waves Are Universal is a twelve-track acoustic-folk/indie-rock album. Goswell plays acoustic guitar, accordion, lead guitar, and rhythm guitar across different tracks, and handles all vocals. The album was produced, engineered, and mixed by Dave Naughton, with contributions from:

  • Joe Light — acoustic guitar, electric guitar, 12-string, slide guitar across most tracks
  • William D. Drake — piano, organ/harmonium
  • Ashley Bates — drums
  • Jerome Farrell — uilleann pipes, whistle
  • Jeremy Perl — cello (tracks 5 and 10)

Goswell also handled the album’s typography. The front cover oil painting is by T. Jackiewiecz. Mastered by Tony Cousins.

A follow-up single, Coastline / Plucked (2005, 4AD), rounded out the solo era.

Slowdive Reunion (2014–present)

Slowdive reunited in 2014 and released two albums on Dead Oceans:

  • Slowdive (2017) — self-titled reunion album. Produced by Halstead; mixed by Chris Coady; mastered by Heba Kadry. Goswell credited Vocals. Engineering by Ian Davenport, with additional vocal engineering from Steve Clarke (22).
  • Everything Is Alive (2023) — Goswell credited Vocals; produced by Halstead; mixed by Halstead (tracks A1, B3) and Shawn Everett (remaining tracks); mastered by Heba Kadry.

Minor Victories (2016)

Goswell was a full member of Minor Victories, a supergroup formed with Stuart Braithwaite (Mogwai), and brothers Justin Lockey and James Lockey. The self-titled debut Minor Victories (2016, Fat Possum Records) credits Goswell as Band, Engineer (Additional), and Written-By. Steve Clarke (22) (later her Soft Cavalry co-founder) also served as additional engineer.

Orchestral variations of the album appeared in 2017 on Play It Again Sam.

The Soft Cavalry (2019–present)

Goswell co-founded The Soft Cavalry with Steve Clarke (22) (Discogs realname: Steve Clarke); the duo is registered under the Discogs realname “Steve Clarke & Rachel Goswell.” Their self-titled debut (2019, Bella Union) credits Clarke as vocalist, guitarist, producer, engineer, mixer, and lyrics writer alongside Goswell on vocals. Additional players: Jesse Chandler (keyboards), Stuart Wilkinson (drums), Tom Livemore (guitar, engineer).

Beachy Head (2021)

Beachy Head (Discogs ID: 9118375) is another project featuring Goswell. Their self-titled album (2021, Graveface Records) credits Goswell Vocals alongside Christian Savill (Slowdive guitarist), Ryan Graveface, Matt Duckworth, and Steve Clarke (22).
