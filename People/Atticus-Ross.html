Atticus Ross

English musician, composer, programmer, and producer. Born 16 January 1968, Ladbroke Grove, London. Real name: Atticus Matthew Cowper Ross. Discogs artist ID: 259284. Married to Claudia Sarne; brother of Leopold Ross.

Name variations: A. Ross, Atticus, Atticus Matthew Ross, Ross.

Biography

UK Electronic Scene — Mute Records Era (1992–2000)

Ross’s earliest documented Discogs credits are as a programmer and beat-maker in the British electronic/post-punk scene centred around Mute Records. His primary relationship in this period was with Barry Adamson — ex-bassist of Magazine and founding member of Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds — who was pursuing a solo cinematic-noir aesthetic on Mute throughout the 1990s.

Ross contributed “additional beats and sounds” to Adamson’s The Negro Inside Me (Mute, 1993), additional programming and sounds to Oedipus Schmoedipus (Mute, 1996), and additional programming/sounds to the Set The Controls For The Heart Of The Pelvis single (Mute, 1996). By 1998 he was serving as full producer on tracks from Adamson’s As Above So Below (Mute, 1998), and produced tracks on the Murky World Of Barry Adamson (Mute, 1999) compilation alongside Flood and Rob Kirwan. Discogs logs 39 shared releases between Ross and Adamson across this period.

Simultaneously, Ross was a member of 12 Rounds (Discogs ID: 170295) — a project also featuring his brother Leopold Ross, Claudia Sarne, Andy Crisp, Adam Holden, and Kirk Hellie. 12 Rounds released on Polydor mid-1990s: the 12 Rounds E.P. (1995), and singles “Business / Pleasant Smell” (1996) and “Jitter Juice” (1996). Ross is credited on bass, programming, and songwriting for this period.

He also worked beyond Mute in the 1990s: assistant programmer on Sinéad O’Connor’s “You Made Me The Thief Of Your Heart” (Island, 1994) and on the In The Name Of The Father soundtrack (Island, 1994).

Transition Period — Nine Inch Nails and Korn (2003–2007)

By the early 2000s Ross was working in the US rock/alternative sphere. He is credited as engineer (additional) and loops provider on Pink’s Try This (Arista, 2003) and follow-up single Last To Know (2004). He was a member of Error (3) (Discogs ID: 279183) alongside his brother Leopold Ross, Brett Gurewitz (Epitaph founder), and Greg Puciato (The Dillinger Escape Plan) — the band’s self-titled album came out on Epitaph in 2004. He also programmed on Bad Religion’s The Empire Strikes First (Epitaph, 2004).

His most significant pre-NIN credit is as producer and programmer on Korn’s See You On The Other Side (Virgin, 2005, 45 pressings) and the associated single “Twisted Transistor” (Virgin, 2005), for which he is also credited as writer. He produced and programmed for Nine Inch Nails’s With Teeth (Interscope, 2005) as “additional producer” and sound designer — his first NIN association. He continued on NIN’s Year Zero (Interscope, 2007) and The Slip (Null Corporation, 2008), growing from engineer/programmer to full co-producer.

Alongside this, Ross produced tracks for Saul Williams’s The Inevitable Rise And Liberation Of NiggyTardust (2007), worked on the Korn surrounding singles Capital G (2007) and Survivalism (2007), and was fully credited producer/mixer/writer on the Todd Terry-related “Untitled” (Virgin, 2007).

Nine Inch Nails — Full Membership (2008–present)

Ross became a full member of Nine Inch Nails around the time of Ghosts I-IV (Null Corporation, 2008), where he is credited as arranger, producer, programmer, and writer. He co-produced and engineered The Slip (Null Corporation, 2008) alongside Trent Reznor and Alan Moulder. The live band for The Slip era included Alessandro Cortini, Josh Freese, and Robin Finck.

Hesitation Marks (Columbia/Polydor, 2013) saw Ross credited as arranger, engineer, producer, and programmer — the full creative team alongside Reznor and Moulder. The Add Violence EP (2017) and Bad Witch (2018) continued his full production and writing credits. Ghosts V-VI (Null Corporation, 2020) credits him as performer.

He is also listed as NIN member on Tron: Ares (Interscope/Null Corporation, 2025), where he has full writing, producing, and mixing credits alongside Reznor.

How To Destroy Angels (2010–2013)

Ross was a founding member of How To Destroy Angels (Discogs ID: 1806412), a supergroup formed with Trent Reznor, Trent’s wife Mariqueen Maandig, and designer Rob Sheridan (2). The debut self-titled EP (Null Corporation, 2010) was followed by An Omen EP_ (Columbia/HTDA LLC, 2012) and the full-length Welcome Oblivion (Columbia, 2013). The band’s lineup contributed to The Girl With The Dragon Tattoo soundtrack (2011), performing and producing the closing track.

Reznor & Ross — Film Scoring Partnership (2010–present)
