Overview

Pernell Saturnino is a Latin jazz percussionist whose career spans the mid-1990s through at least the 2010s. He works primarily within the Latin jazz and post-bop worlds, bringing conga, timbales, shekere, guiro, claves, and general hand percussion to studio dates and live engagements. His work sits at the intersection of Caribbean, Afro-Cuban, and American jazz traditions — a position that made him a natural fit for the Paquito D’Rivera orbit and for Edward Simon’s Criss Cross Jazz albums.

The bulk of Saturnino’s discographic footprint is as a contributor rather than a leader: he appears on major-label Columbia dates (David Sánchez, Danilo Pérez), Impulse! (Antonio Hart, Danilo Pérez), Concord Picante, Chesky Records, Heads Up International, Sunnyside, and the Italian boutique label C.A.M. Jazz. His two most documented long-form associations are with saxophonist/clarinetist Paquito D’Rivera — including membership in the The United Nation Orchestra — and with pianist Edward Simon, whose Criss Cross Jazz sessions Simplicitas (2005) and Océanos (2007) feature him prominently.

He does not appear to have released a leader date in the Discogs database.

Group Membership

  • The United Nation Orchestra — Paquito D’Rivera’s large-ensemble Latin jazz vehicle. Saturnino is listed as a full member (Discogs group ID: 1256759), alongside Danilo Pérez, Diego Urcola, Conrad Herwig, Avishai Cohen, Airto Moreira, Flora Purim, Giovanni Hidalgo, Claudio Roditi, Mark Walker, Oscar Stagnaro, and others.

Connections

  • Edward Simon — most sustained single-artist association in the Discogs record: four joint releases across Criss Cross Jazz, C.A.M. Jazz, and Red Record, 2005–2011.
  • Paquito D’Rivera — longest orbital association; Saturnino is a named member of D’Rivera’s United Nation Orchestra and appears on multiple quintet and large-ensemble dates.
  • David Sanchez (3) — five Columbia/Concord dates between 1998 and 2008, the most prolific single-leader relationship by release count.
  • Criss Cross Jazz — Saturnino appears on two Edward Simon–led dates for this Amsterdam-based label: Simplicitas (2005) and Océanos (2007).
