Overview

Robert Palmer (1949–2003) was a British pop-rock vocalist, songwriter, and musician from Batley, Yorkshire whose career stretched from the late 1960s through the early 2000s across solo work and band projects including The Alan Bown Set, Dada, and The Power Station. His catalogue is anchored by a long association with Island Records (65 releases across five decades), with peak output in the 1980s spanning pop rock, synth-pop, funk, soul, and new wave. His most recurring collaborators include Bernard Edwards and Tony Thompson — both central to The Power Station — alongside long-running studio partners Rhett Davies, Eric Thorngren, and Jason Corsaro. He relocated to Lugano, Switzerland in 1986, became a naturalised Swiss citizen in 1993, and died in Paris from a heart attack in September 2003 at the age of 54.
