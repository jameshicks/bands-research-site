Overview

Steven Peter Stapleton is an English experimental musician, visual artist, record label operator, and the founding and sole constant member of Nurse With Wound. Born in Finchley in 1957 and now resident in County Clare, Ireland, Stapleton has been active in experimental music since 1975 (Wikipedia). He met NWW co-founders John Fothergill and Heman Pathak through shared record-hunting in London second-hand shops; together they recorded the NWW debut in September 1978 at B.M.S. Studio, Wardour Street, London W1. After the first three NWW albums Stapleton became the group’s sole permanent presence, assembling different collaborators for each release.

Beyond performing, Stapleton functions as the project’s producer, mixer, compiler, and primary designer. Under his Babs Santini alias he has created the visual artwork for most NWW releases and for records by Coil, the The Legendary Pink Dots, and Current 93. He is also a painter whose work has appeared on album covers across the wider UK experimental underground and has been exhibited independently (most recently at GalleryX, Dublin). He runs United Dairies (Discogs ID 8625), NWW’s primary label from the 1979 debut onward. Wikipedia notes that United Dairies also released records by Current 93, the The Lemon Kittens, and Volcano The Bear, as well as krautrock reissues and other experimental artists.

Nurse With Wound (1978–present)

Primary vehicle. Stapleton is the only constant member. See Nurse With Wound for full detail.

Inflatable Sideshow (Discogs ID 95997)

Black Ships (Discogs ID 716598)

Members: Ben Chasny (Six Organs of Admittance), David Tibet, Steven Stapleton.

  • Black Ship Time (2005, Hand/Eye) — ID 876364, San Teeny alias used for mixing credit
  • Black Ships Ate The Sky (2006, Durtro/Jnana Records) — ID 699362, Stapleton: “Mixed By; Musician [All Other Musics By]; Performer [Goatheard]“

The Murray Fontana Orchestra (Discogs ID 245297)

A large collective credited across one release: The Murray Fontana Orchestra Plays The Hafler Trio (1995, Staalplaat). Membership list is enormous (50+ people), including David Tibet, Edward Ka-Spel, J.G. Thirlwell, Diana Rogerson, John Fothergill, William Bennett, David Jackman, Geoff Rushton, Robert Haigh, Graeme Revell, and many others. Stapleton is credited for Artwork.

The Nodding Folk (Discogs ID 178656)

Members: David Tibet, Geoff Rushton, James Mannox, Ossian Brown, Peter Christopherson, Sam Mannox-Wood, Steven Stapleton. Release: The Apocalyptic Folk In The Nodding God Unveiled (1993, Durtro) — a Current 93 adjacent release; Stapleton credited as Performer.

Whitehouse (Discogs ID 58520)

Listed as a group member on Discogs. William Bennett’s power electronics project. Stapleton’s connection dates to the early 1980s Come Organisation / United Dairies era overlap.

Alias Discographies

Babs Santini (ID 1084248)

Primarily a visual art alias — cover art, illustration, collage, design. Used across virtually the entire NWW catalog and for numerous collaborators (Current 93, Coil, The Legendary Pink Dots, Play It Again Sam releases, etc.). Notable external credits include Shadow Weaver and Malachai (Current 93, Play It Again Sam, 1992), and A Thousand Lights In A Darkened Room (Coil, Eskaton, 1996). No independent music releases under this name.

El Cod (ID 1320296)

Rare alias. Discogs records only two credits under this name: cover artwork for Chemical Playschool Volumes 8 & 9 (Terminal Kaleidoscope, 1995) and Hallway Of The Gods (Soleilmoon, 1997). No independent releases.

San Teeny (ID 716600)

Rare alias. Sole credit: mixing on Black Ship Time (Hand/Eye, 2005) — the Black Ships project with David Tibet and Ben Chasny.

Sylvie And Babs (ID 91333)

Art alias with a more active Discogs footprint, covering cover design and sleeve work from the early 1980s through the 2000s. The name is derived from the NWW release The Sylvie And Babs Hi-Fi Companion (L.A.Y.L.A.H., 1985). Discogs credits include art direction on Lex Talionis (Tursa, 1990), Island (Durtro, 1991), To Be An Angel Blind, The Crippled Soul Divide (Nettwerk, 1996), and many NWW/Current 93 adjacent releases. No music releases under this name.

Visual Art Practice

Stapleton trained as a signwriter before NWW and has maintained a parallel career as a visual artist throughout. Working primarily as Babs Santini, he produces paintings, collages, and sleeve designs. His visual work spans:

  • Cover art and illustration for the entire NWW catalog
  • Artwork for Current 93, Coil, The Legendary Pink Dots, and many others
  • Independent exhibition; most recent show at GalleryX, Dublin (2017 per Wikipedia)
  • Painting commissions appear as album cover credits on Coil (Love’s Secret Domain, 1991), Current 93 (Chimæra, 1993), and many others

The Babs Santini Discogs entry (ID 1084248) records over 100 distinct artwork/design credits.
