Overview

Nick Cash (Nicholas John Cash) is a British drummer and percussionist whose career began in 1978 with pragVEC before he joined The Lines the following year, a group he continues to perform with. He is a central figure in the early British synth-pop and post-punk underground, most prominently through a six-year association with Fad Gadget and Frank Tovey on Mute Records (1980–1986). His wider collaborative circle includes John Fryer, David Simmonds, and Barbara Frost, and he has been a member of several other groups including Caveman TV, The Members (rejoining their reunion in 2007), and the early-1990s duo Unmen with Giles Perring. In later years Cash has developed a parallel career as a visual artist, serving as CEO of Paste Table Gallery in London.
