Dwight Trible

Discogs ID: 244725
Origin: Los Angeles, CA

Jazz vocalist and spiritual music performer. Co-leader (vocalist) of Build An Ark (2001) alongside producer Carlos Niño; the Discogs profile describes the band as “lead by producer Carlos Niño and vocalist Dwight Trible.” Member of The Pan-Afrikan Peoples Arkestra (PAPA) from c. 1995; rose to vocal director.

Pan-Afrikan Peoples Arkestra

Trible joined PAPA as a vocalist around 1995, with Horace Tapscott as his mentor. He became vocal director and revived The Great Voice of UGMAA — a 12-person choir originally active in PAPA from the late 1960s until the mid-1970s that Tapscott had dissolved due to internal tensions. Trible reconstituted the choir, which performed alongside PAPA at the Los Angeles County Museum of Art on July 24, 1998 (recorded as Why Don’t You Listen? - Live at LACMA, 1998, released on Dark Tree). After Tapscott’s death in 1999, Trible continued leading the UGMAA choir. panafrikanpeoplesarkestra.com, All About Jazz review, Alta Online
