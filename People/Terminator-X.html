Overview

Terminator X (born Norman Rogers) was the DJ and turntablist for Public Enemy, contributing scratches and turntable work across the group’s catalogue from 1987 through the 1990s. His Discogs footprint clusters almost entirely in that decade, with 291 total credits tied overwhelmingly to Public Enemy and The Bomb Squad production team — Hank Shocklee, Eric Sadler, Chuck D, and Flavor Flav account for his heaviest collaborations. Primary label relationships were with Columbia and Def Jam during his active years, alongside the Rush Associated Labels and P.R.O. Division imprints; UMe and Def Jam credits extending to 2025 reflect subsequent reissue activity. He also released work under his own name, Norman Rogers, and had solo output through MCA Records and Chaos Recordings in the early-to-mid 1990s.
