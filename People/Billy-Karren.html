Billy Karren

Born William Francis Karren. American guitarist from Olympia, Washington. Discogs artist ID: 438737. Also credited as “Bill Karren,” “Billy Boredom,” and “Billy The Stinker.”

Overview

Billy Karren is the founding guitarist of Bikini Kill, the Olympia riot grrrl band he co-founded in October 1990 with Kathleen Hanna (vocals), Kathi Wilcox (bass), and Tobi Vail (drums). He was present on every Bikini Kill studio release from 1991 through the band’s 1997 disbandment. He did not participate in the 2019 reunion; touring guitarist Erica Dawn Lyle replaced him in the live lineup.

Before Bikini Kill, Karren played guitar and vocals in Snakepit, a Eugene, Oregon punk band (1987–1988) whose other alumni include Mike Johnson, Joe Preston (later of Melvins and Thrones), and Al Larsen (Some Velvet Sidewalk). He also contributed guitar and voice to a 1989 single by The Go Team, the K Records Olympia outfit associated with Calvin Johnson and Tobi Vail, placing him inside the Olympia DIY network before Bikini Kill formed.

Alongside Kathi Wilcox and Tobi Vail, Karren co-founded Frumpies in 1992, a lo-fi side project that also included Molly Neuman (Bratmobile) and Michelle Mae (later of Make-Up / Weird War). The Frumpies were active 1992–2000 and released their catalog primarily on Kill Rock Stars. On Frumpies releases, Karren is credited under the alias Billy The Stinker.

Karren gave his musical influences as The Slits, The Ronettes, Chrissie Hynde, and Wire (per a Bikini Kill interview cited in Wikipedia). Post-Bikini Kill, he is reported to have played in a project called Ghost Mom (Wikipedia, Bikini Kill article).
