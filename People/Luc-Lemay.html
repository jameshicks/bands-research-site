Luc Lemay

Discogs ID: 346754
Origin: Sherbrooke, Quebec, Canada
Role: Guitar, vocals, composer, lyricist

Founder and sole constant member of Gorguts, the Canadian avant-death metal band active since 1989. Solely responsible for maintaining the Gorguts name through the band’s hiatus (2005–2012) and the 2012 reformation. Primary composer and lyricist across all six studio albums; credited with logo design and artwork concept on Colored Sands (2013).

Era 1 — Considered Dead / The Erosion of Sanity (1991–1993)

Gorguts formed in Sherbrooke in 1989. The debut Considered Dead (1991, R/C Records) and The Erosion of Sanity (1993, Roadrunner Records) placed the band in the Florida-influenced death metal tradition.

Era 2 — Obscura (1998)

Obscura (1998, Olympic Recordings / Slipdisc / Mercury) marked a radical stylistic shift — dissonant counterpoint, atonality, microtonal guitar tunings, and extreme rhythmic displacement. Widely considered one of the defining avant-garde death metal records. Produced by Gorguts + Pierre Rémillard.

Negativa (2006)

During Gorguts’ hiatus, Lemay formed Negativa with former Gorguts guitarist Steeve Hurdle. Released one self-titled EP (2006, Prodisk). Members: Luc Lemay + Steeve Hurdle + Miguel Valade (bass) + Etienne Gallo (drums). Produced by Negativa + Pierre Rémillard + Jean-Yves Thériault.

Era 4 — Colored Sands / Pleiades’ Dust (2013–2016)

The 2012 reformation assembled an entirely new lineup around Lemay. Signed to Season of Mist.
