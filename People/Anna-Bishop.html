Anna Bishop

Violinist, Richmond, VA. Member of The Richmond Symphony and The Spacebomb Strings. Discogs artist ID: 3596929. Profile on Richmond Symphony site: https://www.richmondsymphony.com/profile/anna-bishop/

Overview

Anna Bishop is a Richmond-area violinist who joined The Richmond Symphony in 2015 and is a core member of The Spacebomb Strings, the house string ensemble at Spacebomb Studios. Through Spacebomb she has appeared on recordings by Natalie Prass, Bedouine, and many others across the label’s orbit and beyond. Her credit trail runs from 2013 to at least 2023, spanning Spacebomb releases and outside sessions on Domino, Jagjaguwar, Matador, Sacred Bones, Secretly Canadian, and Ghostly International.

Spacebomb Context

The The Spacebomb Strings ensemble — the house string section at Spacebomb Studios, Richmond — is the context for most of Bishop’s recorded output. The group’s members as listed on Discogs:

  • Anna Bishop (violin) — Discogs 3596929
  • Adrian Pintea (3) (violin) — Discogs 5837699
  • Elizabeth Gopal (violin/viola) — Discogs 5837695
  • Ellen Cockerham-Riccio (violin) — Discogs 5942727
  • Jason McComb (2) (cello) — Discogs 5942728
  • Kim Ryan (viola) — Discogs 3596926
  • Schuyler Slack (cello) — Discogs 5837697
  • Treesa Gold (violin, contractor) — Discogs 3131413

The strings are consistently arranged and conducted by Trey Pollard, and sessions are recorded and engineered by Adrian Olsen at Spacebomb Studios. Matthew E. White produces the majority of Spacebomb releases on which Bishop appears.
