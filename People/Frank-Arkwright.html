Frank Arkwright

Oct 27, 20268 min read

Frank Arkwright

Mastering engineer. Discogs artist ID: 339601.

Overview

Frank Arkwright is a London-based mastering engineer with a career spanning 1991 to the present. He has been based at Abbey Road Studios since 2011, following stints at The Town House (1993–2004), Whitfield Street Studios (2004–2005), and Metropolis Mastering (2005–2010). Across more than 2,700 distinct release credits, he has worked across post-rock, electronic, indie rock, punk, reggae, and Goa trance. He is the most frequently credited mastering engineer in the Rock Action Records catalog (97 release credits by label, with approximately 62 distinct mastering credits). He is also the primary mastering engineer for Frank Turner across the Xtra Mile Recordings catalog and for Belle & Sebastian throughout most of their career.

On several early Goa trance releases he is credited as “Heinous Frank” or “Frank.” His one documented alias on Discogs is “The Heinous One.”

Career by Studio Era

The Town House, 1993–2004

Arkwright began his career at The Town House in London. His earliest documented credits in the DB date to 1991 (mastering Reggae reissues on Front Line / Virgin), but Town House is confirmed from 1993. During this period he worked extensively with Warp Records acts — an association that would define his early reputation:

  • Squarepusher: Hard Normal Daddy (1997), Big Loada (1997), Music Is Rotted One Note (1998), Budakhan Mindphone (1999/2006)
  • Autechre: LP5 (1998), EP7 (1999) series, Confield (2001), Gantz Graf (2002) and the EPs 1991–2002 compilation (2011)
  • Plaid: Not For Threes (1997, Direct Metal Mastering), Rest Proof Clockwork (1999), Booc (2000), Double Figure (2001)
  • Red Snapper: Making Bones (1998), The Sleepless (1998)
  • Seefeel: Succour (1995) — credited as digital editor
  • µ-Ziq: In Pine Effect (1995)
  • Prefuse 73, Nightmares On Wax, Jimi Tenor, Two Lone Swordsmen

He also worked with Jeepster Recordings in this era, mastering early Belle & Sebastian albums: Fold Your Hands Child, You Walk Like A Peasant (2000), Sing Jonathan David (2001), Storytelling (2002), and early Snow Patrol (When It’s All Over We Still Have To Clear Up, 2001). He mastered Gomez’s first three albums on Virgin/Hut (Bring It On, 1998; Liquid Skin, 1999) and Arab Strap’s Monday At The Hug & Pint (2003) on Matador.

Town House also brought early pop and mainstream credits: Echo & The Bunnymen (What Are You Going To Do With Your Life?, 1999), Culture Club (Don’t Mind If I Do, 1999), and various reggae/world reissue projects.

Whitfield Street Studios, 2004–2005

A brief period before moving to Metropolis. Overlapping credit trails with Jeepster and early Rough Trade work suggest continuity of client relationships.

Metropolis Mastering, 2005–2010

At Metropolis, Arkwright continued the Belle & Sebastian relationship (moving with the band to Rough Trade and Matador), worked with Biffy Clyro’s early catalog on 14th Floor Records, and began long-running associations with Frank Turner (Xtra Mile Recordings) and Deltasonic Records acts (The Coral, The Dead 60s). Notable credits from this era:

  • Belle & Sebastian: Dear Catastrophe Waitress (2003, Rough Trade), Books (2004), The Life Pursuit (2006), Write About Love (2010)
  • Biffy Clyro: Folding Stars (2007), Mountains (2008), Lonely Revolutions (2010)
  • The Coral: The Invisible Invasion (2005, Deltasonic)
  • Frank Turner: Campfire Punkrock (2005), Sleep Is For The Week (2007), Love Ire & Song (2008), Poetry Of The Deed (2009)
  • Jarvis Cocker: Fat Kid (2007, Rough Trade)
  • Baxter Dury: Len Parrot’s Memorial Lift (2002, Rough Trade)

Abbey Road Studios, 2011–present

Arkwright joined Abbey Road in 2011 and has remained there since. Abbey Road has brought the most wide-ranging period of his career, spanning continued indie/post-rock work alongside major-label reissues and catalog projects.

Lacquer Cutting

In addition to mastering, Arkwright is credited as “Lacquer Cut By” on many vinyl releases — indicating he personally cut the lacquer at Abbey Road from a mastered source, sometimes (but not always) the same master he made. Both roles appear on several Mogwai vinyl editions (Atomic, Every Country’s Sun, As The Love Continues). When only the lacquer cutting credit appears without a mastering credit on the same release, another engineer mastered the audio.

Name Variations

The Discogs entry lists an unusually large set of name variations (over 20), including misspellings (Frank Artwright, Frank Arkright, Frank Arkweight), formal variations (Francis Arkwright), abbreviations (F.A., FA, F. Arkwright), and the Goa trance credit Heinous Frank. The alias “The Heinous One” is his sole Discogs alias entry.

Connections

  • Rock Action Records — most-credited mastering engineer in the catalog; primary label association from ~2013
  • Mogwai — mastered every studio album from Rave Tapes (2014) through As The Love Continues (2021) and multiple soundtracks; also lacquer cutting on vinyl editions
  • Sacred Paws — Strike A Match (2016/17), Run Around The Sun (2019), Jump Into Life (2025)
  • bdrmm — I Don’t Know (2023)
  • Out Lines — Conflats (2017); connection to Kathryn Joseph and The Twilight Sad via this release
  • Belle & Sebastian — primary mastering engineer throughout career; Jeepster → Rough Trade → Matador
  • Autechre — Warp catalog from LP5 (1998) through EPs 1991–2002 reissue (2011)
  • Squarepusher — Warp catalog late 1990s
  • Plaid — Warp catalog late 1990s–early 2000s
  • New Order — Music Complete (2015) and Factory/Mute catalog vinyl reissues
  • The Twilight Sad — indirect connection via Out Lines; their It Won’t Be Like This All The Time was mastered by Greg Calbi, not Arkwright
  • Kathryn Joseph — vocalist on Out Lines Conflats (2017), which Arkwright mastered
