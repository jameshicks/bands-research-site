Steve Fisk

Discogs ID: 49143. American producer, engineer, musician, and keyboardist based in Seattle/Pacific Northwest. Active 1980–present.

Overview

Steve Fisk is one of the central producers and engineers of the Pacific Northwest underground, active across four decades. He worked extensively with Sub Pop, K (Calvin Johnson’s label), Kill Rock Stars, SST Records, and a range of indie and major labels. His production style emphasizes texture, atmosphere, and unconventional arrangement — he frequently contributes keyboards, organ, Mellotron, theremin, and sampler to sessions as a player, not just a knob-twiddler.

As a performer, he was/is a member of Pell Mell (the SST-era instrumental indie/alt-rock band he helped define) and Pigeonhed (Sub Pop electronic duo with Shane Endsley). He has also appeared in Anonymous (3), Customer Service (2), Cut-Out, Duck Hunt, Fiskfibers, Freeplay (2), His Mortal Enemies, Jad Fair And Friends, The Action Suits, The Halo Benders, Tiny Holes, Westside Lockers, and Witchypoo.

His KRS connection runs deep: he produced Wipers (Groaning Machine, 1991), co-produced and mixed Unwound’s Fake Train (1993), produced the Unwound albums New Plastic Ideas (1994) and Repetition (1996), Challenge For A Civilized Society (1998), and the career retrospective A Single History 1991–1997 (1999), plus a string of other KRS productions through the 1990s and beyond.

Biography

Early Career and SST Years (1980–1988)

Fisk’s earliest production credit is a 1980 single on Flat Records. His profile as a musician-producer emerged through the SST Records circuit: he produced Screaming Trees’ Even If And Especially When (1987, SST), Invisible Lantern (1988, SST), and Anthology (1991, SST). He recorded the Sub Pop 100 compilation (1986, “Technical Whatever” engineering credit) and produced the early Beat Happening / Screaming Trees split (1988, K / Homestead).

His earliest personal releases are the 448 Deathless Days LP (1987, SST Records) — an album of keyboards, optigan, sampler, drum machine, and tape manipulation — and Life Elsewhere (1980). He contributed to Negativland’s Escape From Noise (1987, SST) and later to their The Park Is Mine assemblage credit (1991).

K Records and the Olympia Scene (1988–1994)

Fisk had a longstanding relationship with K (Calvin Johnson’s Olympia label). He produced multiple Beat Happening records: Hit It Or Quit It (1988), Jamboree (1988), Black Candy (1989), You Turn Me On (1992), God Don’t Make No Junk (1994, mixed with Calvin Johnson). He also produced the The Halo Benders debut God Don’t Make No Junk (1994) — a group he was himself a member of — as well as Karp, Long Hind Legs, Mocket, and others in the Olympia orbit.

Additional K productions include: K Whirlpool, Nancy Sin, Alien I.D. (Unwound), Bet The Sky, Shipwreck, the Mirah LP Music To Climb The Apple Tree By (2003, mixed), and K’s Catharsis In Crisis (2007) and Share This Place (2007).

Sub Pop and Grunge (1989–1997)

Fisk’s Sub Pop credits are extensive. He mixed the early Screaming Trees Between The Eyes (1990) and engineered multiple Soundgarden-adjacent tracks. He co-produced the Screaming Trees’ Change Has Come b/w Days (1989, Sub Pop). He also engineered or produced for Mudhoney (Frenching The Bully, 1992, C/Z Records), Boss Hog (Boss Hog, 1995, Geffen), and served as arranger and producer for the lo-fi electronic Pigeonhed project (The Full Sentence, 1997; Battle Flag, 1997, both Sub Pop), for which he was also a songwriter and performer.

Notably, Fisk produced both the Screaming Life / Fopp reissue (1990, Sub Pop) and the original Fopp EP (1988). He mixed Interstate (Screaming Trees, 1995, DGC) — adding piano, organ, and noises — and later contributed to Nirvana archival material (Incesticide, 1992, produced one track; credited on With The Lights Out box set, 2004).
