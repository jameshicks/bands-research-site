Ovasoul7

Vocalist and lyricist. Real name: Delvin Sabela Grimes. Also credited as D. Sabela Grimes (Discogs artist ID: 739443). Discogs artist ID: 13070. Name variations on sleeve: Ovasoul 7, Ovalsoul7, Ovaosul 7. Active in Discogs credits from approximately 2003 to 2019.

Overview

Ovasoul7 is a vocalist and lyricist who operated primarily as a featured artist on releases in the deep house, vocal house, and jazz-influenced electronic music scenes. The Discogs record shows eight credited releases across a sixteen-year span (2003–2019), with the heaviest cluster in the mid-to-late 2000s. The alias D. Sabela Grimes also appears as a songwriter and producer credit on several of the same releases, indicating that Grimes contributed creatively beyond pure performance — writing lyrics, co-producing, and co-composing across multiple projects.

The longest-running collaboration is with Swiss producer Deetron (Sam Geiser), with whom Ovasoul7 shared eight releases including the I Cling series (Music Man Records, 2007) and Out Of My Head (Music Man Records, 2013). Two releases with German duo Slope on Sonar Kollektiv (2006–2007) represent the other sustained partnership. One-off collaborations span New York DJ/producer DJ Spinna (Wonderwax, 2008), Motorcitysoul (Simple Records, 2008), TJ Kong (Compost Records, 2014), Ezel (Bayacou Records, 2018), and Mark De Clive-Lowe (Foliage, 2019).

The earliest credit — How’s Life with The Society on Nite Grooves (2003) — shows Ovasoul7 in a fully co-billed primary role alongside a live production involving strings, guitar, percussion, and Rhodes, produced by Thomas Hass and Tommy Gee.

Scene Connections

Ovasoul7’s credit trail sits at the intersection of several overlapping scenes:

  • Deep house / vocal house: The Deetron / Music Man Records axis is rooted in deep and techy house. The Motorcitysoul / Simple Records releases connect to the UK deep house scene. Sonar Kollektiv is a Berlin label associated with jazz-inflected house and broken beat.
  • Broken beat / nu-jazz: The You Should Be Loving Me session (DJ Spinna, Mark De Clive-Lowe, Finn Peters on flute) and the live-instrument texture of How’s Life (strings, Rhodes, guitar) place Ovasoul7 squarely in the broken-beat/nu-jazz vocal tradition of the mid-2000s.
  • Compost Records: The 2014 TJ Kong collaboration on Compost Black Label links to the Munich-based Compost stable, one of the leading European deep/jazzy house labels.
