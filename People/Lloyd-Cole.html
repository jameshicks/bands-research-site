Lloyd Cole

Scottish singer-songwriter and guitarist. Discogs artist ID: 45641.

Overview

Known primarily for Lloyd Cole and the Commotions (1980s). Produced multiple tracks on Certain General’s Jacklighter (Barclay, 1990) and played harmonica and backing vocals on the single “Baby Are You Rich?“. Fellow Commotions member Blair Cowan also appeared on the album (accordion, Hammond organ). The connection came through Barclay Records, which had arranged for Certain General to open for Cole on a European and American tour in 1990; Barclay pulled Certain General from the tour one week before it was to start. soul-kitchen.fr interview, 2018
