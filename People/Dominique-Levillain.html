Dominique Levillain

Original lead vocalist of Family Fodder, active on their recordings from 1980 through the mid-1980s and on several later archival and reunion releases. Discogs artist ID: 360283. Name variations on record: D. Levillain, Dominique, Leisllain, Levillain.

Overview

Levillain was the voice of Family Fodder’s earliest and most widely heard material. She sang on Monkey Banana Kitchen (1980, Fresh Records (14)) and Greatest Hits (1981, Crammed Discs), the two releases that established the band’s profile in the UK post-punk and Belgian new-wave circuits. She was succeeded as the band’s primary vocalist by Lynn Alice.

Despite stepping back from active performance, her work remained in circulation. She was credited as Performer on the 2008 retrospective More Great Hits! (Jungle Records), returned as a vocalist on Sunday Girls (Director’s Cut) (2015, Staubgold) — a large-ensemble recording released as “Family Fodder And Friends” — and contributed vocals to a track on Foreverandever + Sex Works (2016, Jungle Records), on which Lynn Alice also appeared.

She also wrote liner notes for the 1998 compilation Savoir Faire: The Best Of (Dark Beloved Cloud / Jungle Records).

Her daughter Darlini (with Alig) sang on Family Fodder’s Classical Music album (2010, The state51 Conspiracy). (Source: Wikipedia, Family Fodder article.)

Teaching Practice

After her performing years, Levillain built a parallel career as a music and movement teacher based in the Uzès region of southern France. Her practice combines two strands:

  • Méthode des Rythmes Premiers (“Primary Rhythms” / Prymary Rhythms): a rhythm-based music teaching method of her own devising.
  • Feldenkrais Method: she trained as a Feldenkrais practitioner and integrates somatic awareness into her music teaching. Her platform is called “L’Écoute en Mouvement” (Listening in Motion / Mind in Motion). Website: l-ecoute-en-mouvement.com.

A secondary French-language site (ecoute-en-mouvement-feldenkrais.com) documents her Feldenkrais-specific offerings. She also maintains practice as a visual artist working in textiles and photography, and literary work, all under the same “L’Écoute en Mouvement” umbrella.
