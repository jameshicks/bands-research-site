Grant McLennan

Australian singer-songwriter and guitarist. Discogs artist ID: 479679

Co-founder and co-primary songwriter of The Go-Betweens alongside Robert Forster. Tony Cohen engineered and co-produced the Go-Betweens’ debut album Send Me A Lullaby (1982, Rough Trade). McLennan also had a prolific solo career. Died 6 May 2006.

Jack Frost (5)

McLennan co-formed Jack Frost (5) (Discogs ID: 636488) with Steve Kilbey. Their self-titled debut Jack Frost (1990, PolyGram/Polydor/Red Eye Records) was co-produced by both with Pryce Surplice; McLennan played vocals, bass, and guitar. A second album Snow Job followed in 1996 (Beggars Banquet/Karmic Hit). McLennan died 6 May 2006.
