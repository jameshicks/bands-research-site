Dieter Moebius

Born 16 January 1944, Luven, Switzerland; died 20 July 2015. German electronic musician and composer. Discogs artist ID: 105273. Core member of Cluster.

Overview

Moebius (along with Hans-Joachim Roedelius) formed the duo Cluster, later known as Kluster in early incarnations. He was the closest long-term creative partner of Conny Plank: the Discogs collaboration graph shows 62 shared releases between them — the highest count in Plank’s entire network. Together they made four duo albums at Conny’s Studio (Rastakraut Pasta 1980, Material 1981, Zero Set 1983, and the Begegnungen volumes 1984–85).

He also participated in Harmonia (with Michael Rother) and in multiple sessions with Brian Eno.
