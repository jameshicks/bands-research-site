Peter Saville (2)

English graphic designer. Born 1955, Manchester. Discogs artist ID: 1069010.

Disambiguation Note

Discogs lists two entries under the name “Peter Saville.” This entry — ID 1069010, “Peter Saville (2)” — is the Factory Records designer, as confirmed by Discogs’ own profile text (“Best known for the design of record sleeves for Factory Records artists, most notably for Joy Division and New Order”). The other entry, ID 202866 “Peter Saville” (no number), is a percussionist; its Discogs page explicitly redirects users to this (2) entry for the graphic designer.

The vault’s primary page for this person is Peter Saville, which carries the full biographical article. That page correctly records Discogs ID 1069010. This page documents the credits specifically filed under the (2) Discogs entry and provides cross-reference context.

Overview

Peter Saville created the visual identity of Factory Records from the label’s first release in 1979 and went on to design sleeves across Factory’s entire catalogue through the early 1990s. Simultaneously he worked for a range of non-Factory labels — most notably Dindisc (Virgin’s post-punk/new wave subsidiary), EG, Chrysalis, and Polydor — producing sleeve designs for Orchestral Manoeuvres In The Dark, Martha And The Muffins, Ultravox, Roxy Music, and many others.

He operated under several studio names over the years, all of which appear as Discogs “groups” linked to this artist ID:

D/R Institute and the Martha And The Muffins Connection

The D/R Institute credit connects Saville to Martha And The Muffins’ early Dindisc releases. On This Is The Ice Age (1981) the cover credit reads “15% D/R Institut (Saville/Wickens)” — confirming both Saville and Brett Wickens as the designers. Wickens (Discogs ID 298718) was Saville’s design partner at Peter Saville Associates during the early 1980s. D/R Institute appears to be the name under which they operated their joint practice before or alongside the Associates entity.

Open Questions

  • Who exactly is “Wickens” in D/R Institute? Discogs lists only Peter Saville (2) as a member of that entity; the Saville-Wickens name variation strongly implies Brett Wickens (Discogs ID 298718) but Discogs has not added him as a member. Needs verification.
  • The “SpA10” suffix in some ANVs (e.g. “P. Saville - SpA10”) is unexplained — likely an internal Peter Saville Associates job code or catalogue system.
  • Discogs primary entry ID 202866 (“Peter Saville,” percussionist) — unclear who this actually is; it may be an error or placeholder entry.
