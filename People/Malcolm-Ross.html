Malcolm Ross

Scottish guitarist and songwriter. Born 31 July 1960, Blantyre, Malawi. Discogs artist ID: 269125.

Overview

Malcolm Ross’s career began with Josef K, the Edinburgh post-punk group active 1979–1982 on Postcard Records. After Josef K dissolved, he joined Orange Juice (3), playing guitar and co-writing material for the Rip It Up and Texas Fever albums. He then joined Aztec Camera as second guitarist, recording and touring the Mark Knopfler-produced Knife album (1984). Subsequently he worked with Paul Haig, Momus, Edwyn Collins, Dave Graney and The Coral Snakes, Blancmange, and Barry Adamson (ex-Magazine and The Bad Seeds). He was musical consultant on the Beatles biographical film Backbeat (1993) and contributed guitar to the score of Chocolat (2000, alongside Johnny Depp). He joined the final UK-based lineup of The Moodists alongside David McClymont (also ex-Orange Juice) c. 1986–87, and subsequently played in the original Dave Graney & The Coral Snakes lineup, appearing on I Was The Hunter…And I Was The Prey (1991, Fire Records). He appeared on The Nectarine No. 9’s last album (Davy Henderson’s post-Fire Engines project). Ross plays with his wife Syuzen Buckley in Buckley’s Chance. In 2025 he and Paul Haig formed Land Surveyor (another Kafka reference), debuting live on BBC 6Music, 14 January 2026. (Sources: Wikipedia Malcolm Ross (musician); Discogs ID 269125)
