Ollie Bown

Electronic musician, programmer and academic researcher born in London, England. Discogs artist ID: 499914. Cousin of Sam Britton. Moved to Australia in 2008. Name variations on Discogs: O Bown, O.Bown, Ollie Bown Electric Duo, Ollie Brown.

Overview

Ollie Bown is best known as co-founder and co-producer of Icarus (2), the electronic duo he formed with his cousin Sam Britton in London in 1997. The pair signed to Recordings Of Substance (a sublabel of Hydrogen Dukebox) and issued a run of 12” singles in the late 1990s before moving to Output Recordings for UL-6 (2000) and then founding their own imprint, Not Applicable, in 2002.

The Icarus (2) catalog spans late-1990s UK post-club electronics through to more compositionally open work in the 2010s and 2020s. Their 2004 album I Tweet The Birdy Electric on Leaf remains the most widely distributed release. After a long gap following the Danish LP Sylt (2007) and its remix companion (2009), Icarus (2) returned with the split Flensburg (2011, with Danish duo Badun) and in 2024 with the full-length An Ever-Growing Meridional Entertainment Transgression At The Edge Of The Multiverse on Not Applicable — the most expanded incarnation of the project, bringing in guest players (bass clarinet: Lothar Ohlmeier; cello: Peter Hollo; drums: Rudi Fischerlehner; soprano sax: Frank Paul Schubert; percussion: Maurizio Ravalico).

After relocating to Australia in 2008, Bown became a member of the Sydney post-jazz/electronics ensemble Tangents (3), alongside Peter Hollo (cello), Evan Dorrian (drums), Shoeb Ahmad (guitar/computer), and Adrian Klumpes (piano). Tangents released four albums on Not Applicable and Temporary Residence Limited between 2013 and 2021. Bown’s role in the group is computer/software/synths and field recordings, essentially the electronic processing and textural layer of a band that otherwise plays acoustic and semi-acoustic instruments.

He also formed the duo Crackler (2) with Adem Ilhan (of Fridge). No primary releases from Crackler (2) are indexed in Discogs. Separately, as a solo artist Bown co-released Erase (2011, Not Applicable) — a CD of experimental electronic music — as a primary duo credit alongside Australian composer and clarinettist Brigid Burke.

Beyond performing, Bown has an academic research career in human-computer interaction, machine listening, and generative systems, based in Australia.
