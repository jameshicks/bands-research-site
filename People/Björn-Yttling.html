Björn Yttling

Swedish musician, producer, and songwriter. Born 16 October 1974 in Umeå, Sweden (grew up in Måggliden, near Bjursele in Norsjö municipality). One third of Peter Bjorn And John. Also known as engineer at Högalid Studio. Discogs artist ID: 352198

Full name: Björn Daniel Arne Yttling.

Biography

Yttling grew up in northern Sweden and attended the music program at Carlforsska gymnasium in Västerås, where he studied alongside Marit Bergman. He started writing and recording music at age eight. His father played in the 1960s band Spotlites.

He primarily plays keyboard instruments — piano, organ, mellotron, synthesizer — but also plays electric bass, guitar, and sings. He has produced and engineered most of his work out of Högalid Studio in Stockholm.

Peter Bjorn and John

Yttling forms, with Peter Morén and John Eriksson, the trio Peter Bjorn And John, which has been active since the late 1990s. The band released their self-titled debut in 2002 on Beat That!, then signed to Wichita (UK) and Merge (US), achieving international breakthrough with Writer’s Block (2006) and its single “Young Folks.” Later albums include Living Thing (2009), Gimme Some (2011), Breakin’ Point (2016), Darker Days (2018), and Endless Dream (2020), all on their own INGRID label. Yttling serves as primary producer, engineer, and multi-instrumentalist for all PBJ recordings.

INGRID Collective

Together with the other members of Peter Bjorn and John, Joakim Åhlund (Teddybears Sthlm), and Lykke Li, Yttling co-founded the Swedish artist collective and record label Ingrid. The collective serves as an umbrella for members’ projects and releases.

Smile (12)

Yttling forms the duo Smile (12) with Joakim Åhlund of Teddybears Sthlm. The project released A Flash In The Night (2013, Ingrid) and Phantom Island (2022, Chimp Limbs Recordings / Ingrid).

Yttling Jazz

Yttling has also run a jazz side project under the name Yttling Jazz (listed as alias on Discogs).

Lykke Li — Three Albums

Yttling was the primary producer for all three of Lykke Li’s early albums, which established her internationally:

He also produced or co-wrote several Lykke Li singles including “Little Bit” (2007), “Get Some” (2010), “I Follow Rivers” (2011), and “Breaking It Up” (2008, written-by credit).

Primal Scream — Chaosmosis (2016)

Co-producer and co-writer on Primal Scream’s Chaosmosis (2016, First International; Discogs release ID: 8256120). His involvement was track-specific: primary producer on tracks 1 and 6; additional producer on tracks 2, 5, 8, and 10; written-by credit on tracks 2, 8, and 10. The other core producers were Bobby Gillespie and Andrew Innes. The album was engineered in part by Innes and mixed by Lasse Mårtén.

The Hives — Tyrannosaurus Hives (2004)

Strings arranger on The Hives’ Tyrannosaurus Hives (2004, Polydor; Discogs release ID: 377050). Yttling wrote the string arrangements; the individual string players were Andreas Forsman, Christopher Öhman, Henrik Söderquist, Johan Morén, and Rebecka Karlsson. The album was produced and recorded by Pelle Gunnerfeldt.

Neko Case — Hell-On (2018)

Co-producer on Neko Case’s Hell-On (2018, Anti-; Discogs release ID: 12068031).

Camera Obscura — String Arrangements

Arranged strings on Camera Obscura’s Let’s Get Out Of This Country (2006, Merge Records) and several associated singles including “Lloyd, I’m Ready To Be Heartbroken” and “If Looks Could Kill” (Elefant Records editions).

Caesars Palace — 39 Minutes Of Bliss (2003)

Played organ on 39 Minutes Of Bliss (In An Otherwise Meaningless World) (2003, Virgin) by Caesars Palace.

Anna Ternheim

Produced Open Field (2007, Rough Trade) for Anna Ternheim — also playing bass, piano, guitar; mixing, recording, and providing string arrangements and written-by credits. One of his most multi-role external production credits.

Moneybrother

Co-produced Soul Rush (2001, Dolores Recordings/Virgin) and arranged strings/horns for Moneybrother. Later produced Real Control (2009, Hacka Skivindustri).

The Soundtrack Of Our Lives / Caesars Palace — Organ (Paper Tigers, 2005)

Played Farfisa and Philicordia organ on Paper Tigers (2005, Astralwerks) — this is the Caesars Palace album.

Madrugada (2016)

Provided arrangements for the Madrugada release (2016, Music On Vinyl/Warner Music; Discogs release ID: 8063105).

Chrissie Hynde — Stockholm (2014)

Co-wrote and played extensively on Chrissie Hynde’s Stockholm (2014, Caroline Records / Will Travel; Discogs release ID: 5789571): Electric Bass, Acoustic Guitar, Piano, Synth, Electric Guitar, Organ, Percussion, Mellotron, Celesta; Producer and Written-By. Also produced the 2000 Miles (Stockholm 2014 Version) single. Co-wrote “Dark Sunglasses” and “Never Gonna Love Again” from this period.

Sahara Hotnights

Arranged strings and co-produced Blood Panic (2003, Burning Heart Records) for Sahara Hotnights (Discogs release ID: 602123).

The International Noise Conspiracy

Horn arrangements on Capitalism Stole My Virginity (2001, Burning Heart Records) and played horn on A New Morning, Changing Weather (2001, Burning Heart/Epitaph).

Marit Bergman

Produced 3:00 A.M. Serenades (2002, Sugartoy Recordings) for Marit Bergman.
