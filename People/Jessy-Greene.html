Jessy Greene

Discogs ID: 571336
Origin: Western Massachusetts; career based in Los Angeles, then St. Paul, Minnesota (1997–), then Los Angeles again

Multi-instrumentalist, singer, and songwriter. Started playing violin as a four-year-old prodigy. One of the most extensively cross-genre session violinists in the Discogs database: her credits run from alt-country (The Geraldine Fibbers, The Jayhawks) through Chicago indie (Wilco) and Minneapolis Americana/hip-hop (Doomtree Records, Rhymesayers Entertainment) to arena pop (P!NK) and rock (Foo Fighters, Ghost (32)).

In 1997 she relocated to St. Paul where she founded and operated two entities: Ravenstar Records (ID 221366 — record label) and Ravenstar Studios (ID 2720036 — recording studio). Her only solo album, A Demon & Her Lovers (2006), was released on Ravenstar Records.

Name variations on sleeve: Greene, J. Greene, Jesse Greene, Jessee Greene, Jessie Greene, Jessy, Jessy Green

Label: Ravenstar Records · Discogs ID: 9838546 · 2 pressings (CD)

Core band: Sean McPherson (bass), Peter Leggett (drums), Steve McPherson (guitar), Devon Gray (keyboards). Design and additional production by Kii Arens.
