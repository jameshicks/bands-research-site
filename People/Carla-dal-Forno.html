Overview

Carla dal Forno is an Australian singer, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist who has been active since 2012, working across synth-pop, minimal, new wave, ambient, and drone idioms. She came up through the Melbourne underground as a member of cult outfit Mole House, and continues to work in F Ingers and Tarcar. Her solo catalogue runs heavily through the 2010s, anchored by a run of five releases on UK label Blackest Ever Black between 2014 and 2017, followed by a move to Kallista Records from 2019. Her most sustained collaborators are Tarquin Manek (11 shared releases), Amir Shoat (9), and Noel Summerville (8).

What the evidence does support (from search snippets and successful fetches):

The band’s last known release, Be Around, was recorded and released in 2013. Dal Forno began solo work in late 2014 in Melbourne, having said she “started working on [the solo album] mostly because she wanted to prove to herself that she had the skills to do things on her own terms” (Vice, 2016 — snippet only, full text unverifiable). She had a brief stint in Berlin before returning to Melbourne in December 2015, then relocated to Berlin again around 2015–2016 when Blackest Ever Black released her debut single “Fast Moving Cars.” The available evidence is consistent with the band simply winding down naturally as members moved on to other projects — dal Forno to solo work and eventually F Ingers and Tarcar, Zulicki continuing with Quemada Records — rather than any documented dramatic split or specific precipitating event.

Dead end confirmed. No published interview or press source (as of October 2026) gives a stated reason for Mole House’s disbanding.
