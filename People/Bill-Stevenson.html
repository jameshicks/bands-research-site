Overview

Bill Stevenson (born John William Stevenson) is a drummer, songwriter, and producer whose career spans the full arc of American punk — from early-1980s hardcore with Black Flag and the Descendents through decades of producing punk and pop-punk records at The Blasting Room, the Fort Collins, Colorado studio he co-owns. His closest studio partner, Jason Livermore, shares over 1,000 credits with him, reflecting the sheer volume of outside production work he takes on alongside his ongoing role in the Descendents. His label history moves from SST Records and New Alliance through Fat Wreck Chords, Epitaph, and SideOneDummy, tracing the infrastructure of independent punk from hardcore roots to its commercial expansion.
