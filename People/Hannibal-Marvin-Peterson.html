Hannibal Marvin Peterson

Discogs artist ID: 272598

American jazz trumpeter. Born 11 November 1948 in Smithville, Texas. Active in the New York avant-garde and post-bop scene from the late 1960s onward. Best known for the album Hannibal (1975, MPS / BASF), recorded in Europe with an ensemble billed as “Hannibal And The Sunrise Orchestra.” That session featured Michael Carvin on drums, percussion, vocals, whistle, and timpani; produced by Joachim Ernst Berendt for MPS Records.

Associated with the loft-jazz generation; appeared on sessions connected to the wider post-Coltrane New York scene.
