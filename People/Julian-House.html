Overview

Julian House is a British graphic designer and musician. He is a co-founder and co-owner of Ghost Box (with Jim Jupp, who records as Belbury Poly). As a designer he worked extensively through the studio Intro, credited as “House@Intro” on many releases. As a musician he records as The Focus Group, producing collage-based experimental electronics drawing on library music and radiophonic sound.

His most sustained design relationship has been with Broadcast (from their 1996 debut forward), for whom he designed virtually every release. He also did extensive work for Stereolab, Primal Scream, and many Warp Records artists. In 2017 he co-founded Children Of Alice with former Broadcast members James Cargill and Roj Stevens, as a tribute to Trish Keenan.

House is a member of design collective Intro (11).

As The Focus Group

See full article: The Focus Group

The Focus Group is House’s solo recording alias — collage electronics drawing on library music, radiophonic sources, and British educational film sound. All releases on Ghost Box except Investigate Witch Cults Of The Radio Age (Warp, 2009, with Broadcast). Active from 2005; last DB entry is the 2017 Stop-Motion Happening LP.
