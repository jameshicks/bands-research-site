Kassian Troyer

Discogs ID: 793598 Discogs name variations: Kassian, Kassian Von Troyer, Kassian von Troyer, Kessi, Troyer, K URLs: kassiantroyer.com · dubplates-mastering.com

German electronic musician and mastering/lacquer-cutting engineer based in Berlin. One of the most prolific vinyl mastering and cutting engineers active in the European experimental, electronic, and contemporary-classical underground. Trained under Stefan Betke (aka Pole) at Scape Mastering — where he helped establish the vinyl-cutting facility — before moving through Schnittstelle (2) and a period working from his own studio. Since 2018, based at Dubplates & Mastering (D&M), Berlin’s premier independent vinyl mastering house. His lacquer cuts carry the runout engraving: Kassian @ D&M.

Career Overview

Kassian Troyer’s career spans from at least 2004 (earliest Discogs credit) through to the present (2026 credits confirmed), making him one of the most consistent and long-running mastering engineers in the Berlin-centered experimental underground. His total footprint on Discogs runs to over 860 unique releases — a number that puts him among the highest-volume mastering/cutting engineers in the database.

Career Phases

2004–2009 — Early Credits / Scape Mastering era First traceable credits from 2004 onward. Early work appears on ~scape (the Stefan Betke-run label), Staubgold, and Kranky. This period reflects his apprenticeship under Stefan Betke and the Scape network. Volume is modest (roughly 20 releases before 2010), but the clients are immediately at the serious end of the experimental spectrum.

2010–2017 — Schnittstelle era / Own studio After leaving Scape Mastering, Troyer moved to Schnittstelle (2) and eventually worked from his own studio. Release volume approximately doubles each four-year block: ~67 credits 2010–2013, ~68 credits 2014–2017. Clientele expands substantially — Faitiche, Rough Trade, Staubgold, Dial, Room40, Hubro, 4Bit Productions, and many smaller labels come on board.

2018–present — Dubplates & Mastering Since 2018 at D&M, Troyer’s output explodes: ~334 releases 2018–2021, ~450+ releases 2022 onward. This phase corresponds with D&M’s expanded role as the go-to lacquer-cutting facility for international vinyl releases. He takes on an enormous range of genres and labels, from extreme noise (Hospital Productions) to jazz reissues (Honest Jon’s Records) to African experimental (Nyege Nyege Tapes) to techno (Ostgut Ton, Delsin, Afterlife).

Studio / Facility

  • Training: Scape Mastering, Berlin — under Stefan Betke aka Pole
  • Intermediate: Schnittstelle (2) / own studio
  • Since 2018: Dubplates & Mastering (D&M), Berlin

D&M is Berlin’s most prestigious independent vinyl mastering and cutting house, and Troyer is one of its core engineers. The facility’s client roster crosses into virtually every corner of contemporary independent music.

As a Musician

Troyer identifies as an electronic musician as well as an engineer. The Tesseract (2012, Home Normal) release for a Japanese label credits him as a primary artist playing Guitar and Electronics in an ensemble with Masayoshi Fujita, Simon Harris, Derek Shirley, and Jan Thoben. This appears to be his only known primary-artist release in the Discogs database.
