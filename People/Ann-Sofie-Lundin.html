Overview

Ann-Sofie Lundin is a Swedish singer, keyboardist, and songwriter born in Gävle on 15 February 1981. She came up through the indie band Leiah alongside David Lehnberg, taking the group on several European tours before forming the duo Idiot Kid with Linus Lutti. When Idiot Kid broke up she began recording on her own, eventually settling on the solo name Min stora sorg. Her catalogue spans the late 1990s through the 2010s, largely on Genet Records, working across Indie Rock, Indie Pop, and Folk with regular collaborators including Jari Haapalainen, Johan Angergård, and Karolina Komstedt of Club 8.
