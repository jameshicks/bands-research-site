Kramer (2)

Discogs ID: 239431
Real name: Mark Kramer (born Stephen Michael Bonner)
Active: 1980–present (studio/touring musician 1980–1994; Shimmy Disc 1987–2000/2020–; Noise Studios 1985–)

American musician, producer, and label owner. Founded and ran Shimmy Disc (1987–2000; revived 2020 in partnership with Joyful Noise Recordings). Operated Noise New York and Noise New Jersey recording studios (1985–1998), now Noise Miami (mastering, 2006–present). Associated with the New York avant-garde / downtown scene.

Biography

Kramer was a core figure in New York’s experimental underground across multiple roles. As a performer he was a member of Shockabilly (with Eugene Chadbourne and David Licht), played with B.A.L.L., toured with Butthole Surfers, was part of New York Gong, and co-founded Bongwater with Ann Magnuson (1987–1993). As a label founder, Shimmy Disc released foundational records by Bongwater, King Missile, Galaxie 500 (distributed/licensed), Ween, Naked City, and many others.

As a solo artist on Shimmy Disc, Kramer released albums including Happiness Finally Came To Them (1987), Roll Out The Barrel (1988, Shimmy Disc Europe), Real Men (1991), The Guilt Trip (1992), Who’s Afraid? (1992), A Remark Hugh Made (1994), Black Power (1994), The Secret Of Comedy (1994), Huge (1997, with Knitting Factory Works), Songs From The Pink Death (1998). After a long hiatus, resumed solo releases with The History Of Crying (2017, Shimmy 500) and continued through 2025.

Side Projects & Groups

Brainville (Discogs ID: 1593917)

Supergroup formed with Daevid Allen (Gong), Hugh Hopper (Soft Machine), and Pip Pyle (Hatfield and the North / Gong). Active 1998–2004 with archival releases continuing to 2017. All releases on Voiceprint / Bananamoon Obscura imprints.

Brainville is the most prominent Kramer side project outside Bongwater — a genuine Canterbury/prog-rock crossover collaboration bringing Kramer into the Gong and Soft Machine orbit.

Captain Howdy (Discogs ID: 1550678)

Duo with Penn Jillette (Penn & Teller). Released two singles on Shimmy Disc: The Best Song Ever Written (1993) and Tattoo Of Blood (1994).

Egomaniacs (Discogs ID: 1901604)

Group with Jamie Harley and Kim Fahy. One self-titled album (Shimmy Disc, 1993).

Glen or Glenda (Discogs ID: 1096896)

Duo with Tammy Lang. One release: Reasons In The Sun (Shimmy Disc, 1997).

The New York Superscum (Discogs ID: 728247)

Group with J Mascis (Dinosaur Jr) and Steve Dansiger. No releases found on Discogs (credited appearances only).

SQUANDERERS (Discogs ID: 15450387)

Group with David Grubbs and Wendy Eisenberg. Recent Shimmy Disc releases: Psalms (2023), If A Body Meet A Body (2024), Skantagio (2025).

Let It Come Down (Discogs ID: 8080772)

Duo with Xanthe Tyler. One release: Songs We Sang in Our Dreams (Joyful Noise / Shimmy Disc, 2020).

Bank Of Sodom (Discogs ID: 364919)

Group with David Licht and Eric Boucher (Jello Biafra). No releases found on Discogs as primary artist.
