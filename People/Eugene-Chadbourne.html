Eugene Chadbourne

Real name: Eugene Alexander Chadbourne. American avant-garde guitarist, banjoist, and vocalist. Discogs ID: 181485. Also credited as “Dr. Chad,” “Dr. Eugene Chadbourne,” and under aliases including Doctor (12), Hank Gonzalez, Fiddlin’ U-jean, and Eddie Chatterbox Trio. Active from at least the 1970s onward.

Overview

Eugene Chadbourne is a prolific free-jazz and experimental guitarist associated with the no-wave, new music, and acoustic avant-garde scenes. He has recorded extensively for small labels worldwide — especially Parachute (his 1970s solo guitar records), Fundamental (through the late 1980s), and a string of self-released and micro-label records. A frequent collaborator with Kramer (2), David Licht, John Zorn, and Tom Cora, he is also co-founder and guitarist of Shockabilly and a member of The Chadbournes (the precursor quintet also featuring Zorn and Cora). His solo work spans acoustic guitar improvisation, country parody, political songwriting, and free jazz. Wikipedia cites comparisons between Chadbourne’s vocal style and Les Claypool (Primus).
