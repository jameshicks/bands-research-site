Keith Dobson

UK drummer, guitarist, vocalist, and engineer. Discogs artist ID: 255630 (real name: Keith Henry Dobson). Also performs as Kifkif Le Batteur (Discogs ID: 772156) — confirmed same person by shared realname in DB.

Overview

Keith Dobson is a UK musician active from the late 1970s. He is best known as a member of World Domination Enterprises, the noise-funk post-punk band active mid-1980s onward, but has a much longer history in the UK experimental / free music underground. He was a core member of The 012 (guitar, vocals, as Kifkif Le Batteur) and has also played in Here & Now (3), Planet Gong (2), The Sell-Outs, The Love Bringers, and Ici Maintenants.

His alias Kifkif Le Batteur (sometimes spelled “Kif Kif Le Batter” or “KK”) is used on The 012 and World Domination Enterprises releases. Name variations include Dobson, K Dobson, K. Henry Dobson, Keith Keith, Kif-Kif Dobson.
