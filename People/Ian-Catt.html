Ian Catt

Ian Catt

Oct 19, 20269 min read

Ian Catt

Discogs ID: 48747
Alias: Katmandu (2) (Discogs ID: 197034)
Studio: Cat Music (5) — his own facility at cat-music.co.uk
Name variations: Cat, Catt, Ian Cat, Yan Catt

Producer, engineer, guitarist, bassist, programmer, and multi-instrumentalist. The single most frequent collaborator in the Saint Etienne catalog — 298 shared releases on Discogs — and a pivotal figure in the UK indie-pop underground from the early 1990s onward. Also ran his own recording studio, Cat Music (5), which became a hub for the Shinkansen Recordings roster and a string of Spanish indie artists.

Career Overview

Early Work (1988–1990)

The earliest Discogs credit is a 1988 self-released UK release titled So So Scorpio (Not On Label), where Catt appears as engineer. By 1990 he was working with the nascent Saint Etienne, engineering their debut Heavenly singles: Only Love Can Break Your Heart (1990) and Kiss And Make Up (1990). An uncredited producer credit on the Sarah Records release Snowball (1990) — for a band unnamed in Discogs — suggests he was already operating as a producer for hire at this point.

Around the same time he engineered 7 Ways To Love (1991, Arista) for Cola Boy, demonstrating that his scope extended beyond Saint Etienne from the very start.

Saint Etienne Core Years (1991–1998)

Catt is listed on virtually every Saint Etienne release from Foxbase Alpha (1991) onward. His role evolved steadily across the first phase of the band’s career:

  • Foxbase Alpha (1991): Engineer, guitar, keyboard programming. The record credits him alongside the core trio (Bob Stanley, Pete Wiggs, Sarah Cracknell) with hands-on playing — Wiggs on Prophet 5 and Emax, Stanley on Jupiter and Korg M1, Catt on guitar and keyboard programming.
  • So Tough (1993): Engineer, guitar, keyboards — also credited for “Whistling Jack,” an index of the band’s playful sleeve humor. Bob Stanley and Pete Wiggs produce under the Saint Etienne name.
  • Tiger Bay (1994): Engineer, keyboards, guitar, bass, plus the credit “Bodlondeb” (another in-joke). Co-wrote track 9 (“Hug My Soul”). This record marked the height of his instrumental contribution to the core albums, with orchestral arrangements handled by David Whitaker.
  • He’s On The Phone (1995): Engineering continues.
  • Good Humor (1998): Pre-production alongside Gerard Johnson; the main production went to Swedish producer Tore Johansson.

Catt is credited as Engineer; Producer on the 1993 single Hobart Paving / Who Do You Think You Are and Who Do You Think You Are (Warner Bros.) — his first explicit production credit for Saint Etienne proper.

He also wrote or co-wrote several tracks across the discography. His co-writing credit on the Tiger Bay track “Hug My Soul” is the most prominent single composition.

Shinkansen Recordings (1996–2003)

Catt’s most sustained non-Saint-Etienne production work ran through Shinkansen Recordings (label ID: 15449), the UK indie-pop label whose roster was dominated by Trembling Blue Stars (Bobby Wratten’s post-Field Mice project). He produced every Trembling Blue Stars album on the label:

He also produced Diary Of An Antibody (2002) by Fosca on Shinkansen, and a California single (1999) for Harvey Williams on Shinkansen.

The Shinkansen relationship reflects Catt’s alignment with the overlapping circles of the Field Mice diaspora and UK jangle-pop scene — Bobby Wratten being the key shared figure.

Saint Etienne Return and Full Producer Role (2001–2012)

From Finisterre (2002) onward, Catt is credited as Producer rather than just engineer on Saint Etienne studio albums:

ReleaseYearRole
Finisterre2002Producer (tracks 2, 3, 6, 8, 12); “Musical Talents” performer
Tales From Turnpike House2005Producer, Mixed By, String Arrangements

On Finisterre he shared production duties with Mr. Joshua and the band itself. On Tales From Turnpike House he handled the majority of production (all tracks except 3 and 8, which went to Xenomania) and arranged the strings. He also produced A Good Thing (2005) and Side Streets (2005).

The 2006 What Have You Done Today Mervyn Day? film soundtrack (Foreign Office) credits him as producer and as a player of guitar, bass, and keyboards — a full musical direction role.

This strand represents consistent work across more than two decades and shows Catt operating as a full-service producer-engineer-musician for international clients, not merely a Saint Etienne satellite.

Katmandu (2) — Solo Project (1994–1995)

Under the alias Katmandu (2), Catt released:

  • “Creation / Welcome To California” (7”, Handlebar Records / Vinyl Japan, 1994)
  • “Katmandu” (album, Handlebar Records / Vinyl Japan, 1995) — composed with Govind Sharma; featured Julie Felix on vocal effects, Paul Pritchard on acoustic percussion, Michelle Hillier as performer and Sanskrit translator. Catt engineered, mixed, and made the “final reduction.”

The album appears to blend ambient/world-influenced sounds with his pop production aesthetic. Released on Vinyl Japan and a small UK label (Handlebar Records), it was a modest cult release with limited international distribution.

Dylan Mondegreen and Nordic Work (2012)

Catt produced and played on the Norwegian indie-pop act Dylan Mondegreen’s self-titled album (2012, Shelflife Records / Division Records). He handled keyboards, sampler, melodica, guitar (atmospheric), mix, recording, and string arrangements — effectively acting as the band’s musical co-director alongside frontman Børge Sildnes.

Later Work (2013–2025)

Active through the 2010s and 2020s, Catt continued producing for Elefant Records artists and for the Saint Etienne orbit:

  • I Love To Paint (2017, Heavenly) — Saint Etienne; Engineer, Guitar, Bass, Keyboards
  • Nice Price (2017, Foreign Office) — guitar, bass, keyboards
  • Quiet Actions (2017, The Tapeworm) — Producer, Engineer
  • Various Tiny Fireflies releases (2015–2018) — mix, production
  • Fairfax High (2022, Foreign Office) — Engineer
  • Singles 1991–1998 (2023, A Colourful Storm) — Remastered By
  • Understood (2025, Needle Mythology) — Producer
  • Colours Yet To Be Named (2025, Elefant) — full production role

He also did mastering work for LTM (4) compilations including Auteur Labels: Factory Benelux 1980-1985 and Auteur Labels: Les Disques Du Crépuscule 1980-1985 (both 2008).
