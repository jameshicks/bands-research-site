Pat Flegel

Visual artist and musician from Calgary, Alberta. Discogs artist ID: 3799172. Real name: Patrick John Flegel. Alias: Cindy Lee (experimental music project).

Overview

Sibling of Matt Flegel (Preoccupations / Women). Member of Women (guitar/vocals). After Women dissolved, continued as Cindy Lee, releasing experimental/folk recordings. Provided inside artwork for Preoccupations’ self-titled LP (2016) and cover art for the Cassette (2017, credited as Patrick Flegel).
