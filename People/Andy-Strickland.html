Overview

Andy Strickland is an English guitarist and singer, best known for his involvement in UK indie and jangle pop acts of the 1980s — The Loft, The Chesterf!elds, The Caretaker Race, and The Living Room. His recording catalogue clusters in the mid-to-late 1980s, with Creation Records and Roustabout Records as his main labels, and consistent close collaborators in Bill Prince, Peter Astor, and Dave Morgan. Alongside music he worked as a journalist for Record Mirror and Sounds, co-founded the football magazine 90 Minutes, and edited the webzine Dotmusic in the late 1990s. After a long gap from active recording he returned in the 2020s with a run of releases across several small labels including Mr. Mellow’s Music, Precious, and Tapete Records.
