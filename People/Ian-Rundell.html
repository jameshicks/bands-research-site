Overview

Ian Rundell is an engineer and musician based in Austin, Texas, active since at least 2012 with over 130 credits concentrated in the Texas underground punk and noise scene. His technical work spans recording, mixing, mastering, and production, primarily for Austin-rooted labels — 12XU, Monofonus Press, Super Secret Records, and Astral Spirits — across styles running from punk and post-punk to noise and industrial. He also performs as a musician in Ghetto Ghouls and The Infinites. His closest working relationships are with Evan Kleinecke, the post-punk outfit Spray Paint, Mikey Young, and Steve Pike.
