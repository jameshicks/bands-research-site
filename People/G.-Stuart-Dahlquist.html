G. Stuart Dahlquist

Discogs ID: 482964
Real name: George Stuart Dahlquist
Aliases: Bootsy Kronos, G:Subharmonia
Origin: Seattle, Washington

Bassist and multi-instrumentalist. Founder and leader of Asva, the drone/doom project that included Trey Spruance and William Winant among its contributors. Also a member of Burning Witch, Goatsnake, and associated doom/drone circles. Web Of Mimicry co-released Asva’s Futurist’s Against The Ocean (2005).

Sunn O))) Connection

Dahlquist played bass on the 2011 Southern Lord reissue of ØØ Void (the Sunn O))) debut, credited as “Stuart Dahlquist.” He is also listed among the core performers on that release under his alias G:Subharmonia. He is not listed as a formal group member of Sunn O))) in Discogs.
