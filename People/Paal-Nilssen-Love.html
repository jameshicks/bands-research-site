Paal Nilssen Love

Paal Nilssen-Love

Oct 18, 20266 min read

Paal Nilssen-Love

Discogs ID: 231168
Born: 24 December 1974, Norway
Role: Drummer, composer, bandleader
Active: 1993–present

Norwegian free jazz drummer and prolific bandleader. One of the most recorded improvisers in the Nordic and international free jazz circuit. Leader of Paal Nilssen-Love Large Unit (a 20-piece ensemble), co-founder of the power trio The Thing (2), member of Scorch Trio, Atomic (2), Boneshaker (3), and dozens of other projects. Runs his own imprint PNL. Also credited as PNL, Paal N-L, and Paal Nilssen Love (various sleeve spellings).

Biography

Early career — Norway in the 1990s

Nilssen-Love’s earliest documented appearance on Discogs is the 1993 Circulasione Totale compilation Enten Eller (Circulasione Totale label). He was active in the Oslo free jazz underground through the mid-1990s, appearing on Norwegian sessions through NORCD and related imprints.

His first significant international breakthrough came via Scorch Trio, the Norwegian free trio featuring Ingebrigt Håker Flaten on bass and Raoul Björkenheim on guitar. That ensemble’s records appeared on Rune Grammofon and brought him into contact with Swedish and European players.

The Thing (2) — 2000s breakthrough

In 2000, Nilssen-Love co-founded The Thing (2) with alto/tenor saxophonist Mats Gustafsson and bassist Ingebrigt Håker Flaten. The self-titled debut appeared on Crazy Wisdom / Universal (Sweden). The trio became one of the defining Nordic free jazz units, releasing through Smalltown Superjazzz (the jazz sublabel of Smalltown Supersound), Crazy Wisdom, and later The Thing Records (Trost imprint). The Cherry Thing (2012), a collaboration with Neneh Cherry, was their widest-exposure release.

Gustafsson duo

In parallel, Nilssen-Love developed an ongoing duo partnership with Mats Gustafsson. I Love It When You Snore (2002, Smalltown Supersound) was produced by both; further duo recordings continued through the decade.

Chicago connections — Vandermark, Territory Bands, Boneshaker

Nilssen-Love became embedded in the Chicago improvised music scene through Ken Vandermark. He appears on multiple Territory Band recordings (Territory Band-3, -4, -5, -6) in the early-to-mid 2000s and co-leads the duo Milwaukee Volume (2009, Smalltown Superjazzz) with Vandermark directly. He is also a member of Boneshaker (3) (with Mars Williams and Kent Kessler), FME, and School Days, all Chicago-Norwegian crossover groups.

Clean Feed / Portugal — 2005–2007

Several important leader dates appeared on Clean Feed (Lisbon): Townorchestrahouse (2005, with Evan Parker, Ingebrigt Håker Flaten, and Sten Sandell), Concentric (2006), and 4 Corners (2007). These placed him firmly in the European post-Brotzmann conversation.

Brötzmann orbit

Nilssen-Love is a recurrent partner of Peter Brötzmann. His most documented work in this circle is the group Hairy Bones — a quartet with Brötzmann, Massimo Pupillo (Zu), and Toshinori Kondo — documented on Hairy Bones (2009, Okka Disk). He also appears in Powerhouse Sound and other Brötzmann-adjacent ensembles.

PNL imprint and Large Unit — 2010s

Around 2010–2012 Nilssen-Love launched the PNL self-release label (Discogs label ID: 88075), which became the primary home for his leader recordings and archival releases. Over 80 releases appear under PNL on Discogs. He also convened Paal Nilssen-Love Large Unit, a ca. 20-piece ensemble, releasing Jacana (2014, Rune Grammofon) and More Fun Please (2018, PNL, recorded with the “Extra Large Unit” variant featuring incorporated student ensemble Intuitive People).

Key Large Unit members include: Lasse Marhaug (electronics), Ketil Gutvik, Klaus Ellerhusen Holm (reeds), Christian Meaas Svendsen (bass), Jon Rune Strøm (bass), Per Åke Holmlander (tuba), Mats Äleklint (trombone), Marthe Lea (reeds), Julie Kjær (reeds), Andreas Wildhagen (drums), and others.

Arashi / Japan connections

The group Arashi (5) pairs Nilssen-Love with Japanese alto saxophonist Akira Sakata and Swedish bassist Johan Berthling. Recordings include Arashi (2014, Trost) and Semikujira (2016, Trost). His ongoing Japan connections also include duo work with Otomo Yoshihide (Otomo Yoshihide & Paal Nilssen-Love, 2014, Jvtlandt) and Joe Talia.

Death Rattle, Bocian, noise crossovers

Death Rattle (2013, Rune Grammofon) documents a guitar-drums duo with James Plotkin, crossing into heavy noise/metal territory. He also recorded prolifically for Bocian Records (Poland), including Chiapaneca (2012), Con Gas, Sin Gas (2013), and You Can Be Mine (2015).

2020s — continued output

Activity continues into 2024–25. Butterfly Mushroom (2024, Trost), Chicken Shit Bingo (2024, Trost), I Love Noise (2024, PNL), and Hyperentasis (Live In Thessaloniki) (2025, Defkaz) document his undiminished pace. A large Japan 2019 box set appeared on PNL / Audiographic Records (2024).
