Angelo Verploegen

Angelo Verploegen

Oct 21, 20265 min read

Angelo Verploegen

Discogs ID: 398069 Born: 1961, Oss, Netherlands Instruments: Trumpet, flugelhorn Name variations: Verploegen

Dutch trumpet and flugelhorn player and composer. Active across the full breadth of the Netherlands music scene — from major pop and musical-theatre sessions in the 1980s–90s to avant-garde free improvisation and large-ensemble jazz. Member of 21 recorded groups including Bik Bent Braam, Splinks, the Jazz Orchestra Of The Concertgebouw, and the Soo Cho Quartet. Also served as A&R and producer at the Dutch jazz label A-Records for several years in the late 1990s–2000s.

Career

Early career and Frank Grasso Big Band (1983–1989)

Verploegen’s earliest Discogs credit is as session trumpet on Maiden Voyage (1983, JR Records, Netherlands). He joined the Frank Grasso Big Band, a Dutch jazz ensemble on the Timeless label, appearing on their self-titled LP (1985, Timeless) and Live (1986, Timeless), both credited with trumpet and flugelhorn. He also appears on Mind Trouble (1986, Double Trouble Records).

The late 1980s brought high-profile commercial session work. He played first trumpet on the Dutch-language cast recording of Cats (Cats Nederlandstalige Versie, 1987, Mercury). In 1989 he contributed trumpet to Niemand In De Stad (Mercury 836 985-1) by De Dijk, a landmark Dutch rock album (10 pressings), and appeared on Bijna Shocking (1989, CNR).

Timeless Records and early jazz leadership (1990)

Live At Paradox (1990, Timeless Records) carries both his trumpet/flugelhorn and a producer credit — one of his earliest recorded production roles.

Splinks and avant-garde work (1993)

The recordings by Splinks — a large-format free improvisation ensemble led by vocalist Jaap Blonk, also including Tobias Delius, Mary Oliver, and Bart Van Der Putten — were documented in 1993 and later released on The Splinks And BRAAXTAAL Recordings | 1993 (2011, Audition Records, Spain). Verploegen plays trumpet, flugelhorn, and voice on the record and the associated digital release.

Pop sessions and musical work (1995–2001)

Throughout the mid-to-late 1990s Verploegen continued major session work:

  • Stormvogels (Live 1990–1995) (1995, Ariola/BMG) — trumpet, flugelhorn; likely the soundtrack/live document for the Dutch TV/theatre production
  • Acda En De Munnik (1997, S.M.A.R.T., SMT 487274 2) — debut album of the iconic Dutch pop duo Acda En De Munnik (Thomas Acda and Paul de Munnik); Verploegen plays trumpet
  • De Ballade Van De Dromedaris (1999, Columbia) — flugelhorn and trumpet; 4 pressings
  • Heden (2001, V2) — trumpet; three V2 releases in 2001 suggest a sustained relationship with an artist on that label (De Liefde Gaat and Tijd also in 2001)

A-Records A&R and production role (1998–2008)

From the late 1990s Verploegen took on an industry role at A-Records (Amsterdam), a leading Dutch jazz label:

  • A&R: Señor Tambó (1998, A-Records), Hommage (1999, A-Records), Saturn’s Finger (1999, BUZZ-Records)
  • Producer: Get In (1999, A-Records), One More Time (2000, A-Records), Solo (2000, Buzz-Records), Benjamin Herman Plays Jaki Byard (2002, A-Records), Congo Square: Tribute To New Orleans (2002, Munich Records/Radio Nederland), Silk Rush (2008, 55 Records Japan)
  • Featured on A-Records compilation Tomorrow’s Jazz Classics Vol. 1 (1998)

Solo leadership: The Night And The Daydreamer (2003)

The Night And The Daydreamer (2003, A-Records AL 73234) is his sole primary-leader release on Discogs, co-credited to the trio of Verploegen, guitarist/producer Ed Verhoeff, and double bassist/producer Eric Van Der Westen. The album is a mix of original compositions by all three plus a Dave Holland cover (“Lazy Snake”) and a Schwartz & Dietz standard. Chris Weeda produced, recorded, mixed, and mastered. Genre: jazz.

Continued ensemble work and later career (2003–present)

Verploegen continued active membership across multiple Dutch jazz formations:

  • Soo Cho Quartet (led by Korean-Dutch saxophonist Soo Cho) — appeared on Prayer and Little Prince
  • Jazz Orchestra Of The Concertgebouw — large-scale prestigious ensemble
  • Bik Bent Braam — major Dutch experimental-jazz large ensemble
  • The New Art Orchestra, New Concert Big Band, Amsterdam Jazz Trio

Later recordings include Waltzing With Zoe (2002, Challenge Records, trumpet/flugelhorn), Goes Bonsai (2003, BBB), Tribute To Ray Charles (2005, Munich Records), Unleashed And Remixed (2007, Social Beats), Live At Royal Theatre Carré (2020, Electrosaurus Records/Suburban Records), and (More Of) A Good Thing (2021, SoNo Recording Group).
