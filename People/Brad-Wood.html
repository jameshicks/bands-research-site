Brad Wood

American record producer, engineer, mixer, and multi-instrumentalist, based in Los Angeles (formerly Chicago). Discogs artist ID: 55122. Active from 1988 to present. Member of Shrimp Boat and Cranky George; also credited as member of The Walker Roaders.

Overview

Brad Wood is one of the defining production figures of the early-1990s Chicago indie scene. His work at Idful Music Corporation (his Chicago studio) shaped the sound of that city’s post-punk/alternative underground, most famously as the producer and engineer of Liz Phair’s debut Exile In Guyville (Matador, 1993). He also produced Sunny Day Real Estate’s self-titled second album (Sub Pop, 1995), Veruca Salt’s American Thighs (DGC/Minty Fresh, 1994), and Placebo’s self-titled debut (Virgin/Elevator Music, 1996), establishing a range that spanned lo-fi indie rock, post-hardcore, and British alternative.

Wood is also a capable multi-instrumentalist — he played drums, saxophone, keyboards, bass, and vocals across many of the records he produced or engineered, particularly in the Shrimp Boat years and on his collaborative Shrimper/Ajax-adjacent work.

After relocating to Los Angeles in the late 1990s–2000s he continued producing across Christian rock (mewithoutYou, mewithoutYou), post-hardcore, and indie, and in the 2010s–2020s built a second prolific run producing and engineering for [[Bands/Sunn O)))] on Southern Lord, as well as Portrayal of Guilt, Inter Arma, and related heavy underground acts.

Biography by Era

Early Chicago Work (1988–1992)

Wood’s earliest Discogs credit is a saxophone performance on That Love Thang (Demon Records, UK, 1988). By 1990 he was engineering records in Chicago — the Shrimp Boat LP Flow Gently Sweet Alpha (Ajax Records, 1990), the Cows Roundhouse EP (Amphetamine Reptile, 1990), and Social Graces Social Graces (Emigre, 1990).

Through 1991–1992 he accumulated a dense roster of Midwest indie engineering credits — Amphetamine Reptile bands (Cows), Sympathy For The Record Industry singles, Drag City sessions, and his own performances as a member of Shrimp Boat (drums, saxophone) on records released by Ajax Records and Bar/None/Rough Trade. His name appears on Drag City’s Lounge compilation (1992), Perishable Records releases (Red Red Meat’s self-titled debut, 1992), and City Slang singles.

Idful Studio and the Chicago Indie Peak (1993–1996)

The key years. Wood’s production company (Idful Music Corporation, Discogs label ID: 4378903) was the operational base for his most celebrated work.

Liz Phair — Exile In Guyville (Matador, 1993, release ID: 387600): The cornerstone credit. Wood produced and engineered. The record became one of the decade’s most discussed indie debuts; a 2018 box set Girly-Sound To Guyville (Matador, 2018, release ID: 11929962) presented additional archival material with Wood again credited.

Veruca Salt — American Thighs (DGC/Minty Fresh, 1994, release ID: 398327): Recorded and mixed by Wood. Spawned “Seether” and “Number One Blind.” The band’s debut LP.

Liz Phair — Whip-Smart (Atlantic/Matador, 1994, release ID: 387607): Recorded and mixed by Wood. Follow-up to Exile.

Sunny Day Real Estate — self-titled (“the Pink Album”) (Sub Pop, 1995, release ID: 397025): Produced by Wood. The band’s second album, a pivot toward melodic post-hardcore.

Placebo — Placebo (Virgin/Elevator Music, 1996, release ID: 370050): Produced by Wood. UK alternative rock breakthrough; 54 pressings on Discogs — the most widely distributed single release in Wood’s catalog.

Other significant 1993–1996 credits: Sea and Cake self-titled (Rough Trade/Thrill Jockey, 1994, mixed/recorded); Trenchmouth Vs. The Light Of The Sun (EastWest, 1994); Poster Children (Touch and Go, 1993/1995); The Spinanes Strand (Sub Pop, 1996, mixed/additional production); Veruca Salt singles on Minty Fresh; Combustible Edison.

Late 1990s Transitions (1997–1999)

Liz Phair — Whitechocolatespaceegg (Capitol/Matador, 1998, release ID: 1325212): Produced by Wood. Third Phair LP, her most mainstream-leaning.

Smashing Pumpkins — Adore (Virgin/Hut, 1998, release ID: 367899): Wood received “Additional Production” credit. He also produced/engineered several Adore-era singles — “Ava Adore” (release ID: 1605144), “Perfect” (release ID: 1089992) — and is credited as multi-instrumentalist (keyboards, saxophone, vocals, whistle, handclaps) on the b-side collection All Disco Dance Must End In Broken Bones (release ID: 11190) and other Hut singles.

Hedwig and the Angry Inch original cast recording (Atlantic, 1999, release ID: 2244114): Produced by Wood.

Pete Yorn — musicforthemorningafter (Columbia, 2001, release ID: 633983): Co-produced and mixed. Yorn’s debut; a Columbia/major-label breakthrough, 13 pressings.

Major Label and Wider Scope (2000–2006)

Work expanded to include:

  • The Fire Theft — self-titled (Rykodisc, 2003, release ID: 626385): produced, engineered, mixed. Jeremy Enigk’s post-SDRE band.
  • The Bangles — Doll Revolution (Down Kiddie/Liberty EMI, 2003, release ID: 401628): engineered, mixed, produced.
  • mewithoutYou — Catch For Us The Foxes (Tooth & Nail, 2004, release ID: 466343) and Brother, Sister (Tooth & Nail, 2006, release ID: 894452): produced, recorded, mixed. Two of the most critically regarded records from the Christian post-hardcore scene.
  • Ben Lee — Awake Is The New Sleep (Ten Fingers/Inertia, 2005, release ID: 543994): Wood plays bass, drum programming, saxophone, vocals, and more; also produced, engineered, mixed.
  • Various Pete Yorn follow-up projects.

Southern Lord Era and Later Work (2013–present)

From around 2013 Wood became a key production partner for Southern Lord, working primarily with drone/doom and sludge metal acts:

  • Touché Amoré — Is Survived By (Deathwish, 2013, release ID: 4930687): produced, engineered.
  • Portrayal of Guilt — Forest Nocturne (Southern Lord, 2022, release ID: 22985930): produced, engineered, mixed, mastered.
  • Portrayal of Guilt — Devotional (Southern Lord, 2022, release ID: 24850148): produced, mixed, mastered.
  • Sunn O))) — upcoming (Sub Pop, 2026, release ID: 36926526): produced, recorded, mixed.
  • Inter Arma — Sulphur English adjacent credits; multiple Southern Lord artists 2021–2025.

He also continued engineering/mixing for Frightened Rabbit (The Midnight Organ Fight follow-ups), Say Anything, and various indie/rock acts.
