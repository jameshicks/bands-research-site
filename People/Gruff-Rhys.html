Overview

Gruff Rhys (full name Gruffudd Maredudd Bowen Rhys) is the lead vocalist, guitarist, and principal songwriter of Super Furry Animals, the Welsh psychedelic indie outfit. Before SFA he played in several Welsh groups including Machlud, Ffa Coffi Pawb, and the Creation Records-signed Emily (3). As a solo artist from 2005 onward his catalogue is prolific and concentrated in the 2010s, frequently recorded in Welsh and issued mainly through Rough Trade and Lex Records; much of it has a concept-album character, typified by his Neon Neon collaborations with Boom Bip, which built concept records around John De Lorean and Italian publisher Giangiacomo Feltrinelli. His closest credited collaborators are the SFA core — Dafydd Ieuan, Guto Pryce, Cian Ciaran, and Huw Bunford — alongside long-term producer Gorwel Owen.
