Jean-Paul Goude

Real name: Jean-Paul Goude Discogs ID: 250086 Born: 8 December 1938, Montreuil, France Role: graphic designer, illustrator, photographer, advertising film director, event designer Name variations: J. P. Goude, J.P. Goude, Jean Paul Goude, Jean-Paul, Jean-Paul Goode, John Paul Goude

French graphic designer, illustrator, photographer, and event director. Best known for his long-term visual partnership with Grace Jones — he photographed or designed the sleeves of her entire Compass Point trilogy and beyond, and directed her 1982 television special — and for staging the choreography, art direction, and concept for the 1989 French Bicentennial parade (“La Marche des Mille”). Also provided cover art for Cristina’s Sleep It Off (Mercury, 1984). Forms part of the group Dream Makers with Virginia Astley.

Biography

Early career and illustration work

Goude began his career in illustration and graphic design. His earliest Discogs credit dates to 1968, when he contributed a drawing to the sleeve of Françoise Hardy’s self-titled album (Disques Vogue, France). A 1969 back cover credit on Italian artist Lucio Battisti’s Il Pretesto (CGD) and a 1975 painting for the compilation Born To Die (Capitol/EMI Electrola, Germany) show early work spanning national markets and media — drawn illustration, back-cover layout, and painted cover art.

A 1978 painting credit on a Canadian RCA release (Flame) rounds out the pre-Island phase.

Grace Jones and the Island Records era (1980–1986)

Goude’s defining professional collaboration was with Grace Jones, whom he was in a long-term personal relationship with and with whom he has a son, Paulo Goude (Discogs ID: 1275577). The partnership produced some of the most distinctive album covers of the early 1980s.

On Warm Leatherette Goude combined photography with the full album design. On Nightclubbing he supplied a cover painting. On Living My Life the credit splits between Goude (sleeve photography) and co-designer Rob O’Connor.

Television direction:

Goude directed A One Man Show / A Contemporary Musical Entertainment For Television (Island Visual Arts / PolyGram Music Video, 1982) — Grace Jones’s concert film, produced by Eddie Babbage. This represents his only director credit in the Discogs data.

The Island Life greatest-hits cover and the Slave To The Rhythm cover art are among his most widely reproduced images. Slave To The Rhythm was produced by Trevor Horn for ZTT Records and also appears on Manhattan Records and Island Records pressings; Goude’s imagery appeared across all editions.

Film direction:

Goude directed State Of Grace (RCA / Columbia Pictures Home Video, 1986), a music video or short film (concept and direction credited to Goude).

1989 French Bicentennial parade

The release 1989 - La Marche Des Mille (Island Records, France, 1989) documents the musical recording from the parade Goude staged in Paris for the bicentennial of the French Revolution. He is credited with choreography, art direction, and concept. The music was produced and performed by Wally Badarou.

Dream Makers

Goude is a member of the group Dream Makers alongside Virginia Astley (Discogs realname: “Virginia Astley, Jean-Paul Goude”). No primary releases are credited to Dream Makers in the Discogs data; the group appears to have been a project without a recorded output indexed in Discogs.
