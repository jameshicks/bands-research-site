Penny Rimbaud

Discogs IDs: 213075 (Penny Rimbaud); 7107354 (J J Ratter) Real name: Jeremy John Ratter

Drummer, writer, and co-founder of Crass (formed 1977, Essex). Central lyricist and ideological voice of the band. Co-founded Crass Records in 1979. Published Acts Of Love as a solo album on Southern Records (EXT001, 2012). Crass’s reissue of The Feeding Of The 5000 (The Second Sitting) also appeared on Southern Records in 2011.

Produced Flux Of Pink Indians’ Neu Smell EP (1981, Crass Records 321984/2) and Strive To Survive Causing Least Suffering Possible (1983, Spiderleg Records) — credited on both as alias “Shaw Ram Buoy.” Produced Kukl’s Holidays In Europe (The Naughty Nought) (1986, Crass Records).

Aliases (Discogs): Elvis Rimbaud, Arfer ‘Bonzo’ Rimbaud, Elvis Rimrod, Monsignure Penelope Rainbow, Penny Zenvy, Puny Rumble, Shaw Ram Buoy, J J Ratter

Post-Crass Groups

Donna & The Kebabs, Kebabs, Exit (79), Last Amendment, The New Banalists Orchestra, Crass Agenda
