Overview

Robin Holcomb is an American singer, songwriter, and pianist born in 1954, married to fellow musician Wayne Horvitz. Her recorded output is concentrated in the late 1980s and 1990s, released primarily on Elektra Musician and Nonesuch, spanning contemporary jazz, experimental, and ballad idioms — with roles extending to composition and lyric writing. Horvitz is her most frequent collaborator by far (59 shared releases), followed by Bill Frisell, Bobby Previte, and Doug Wieselman; she has also been a member of the New York Composers Orchestra and Varmint.
