Charles Hanson

Guitarist, vocalist, bassist, drummer, keyboardist, and producer. New Orleans, Louisiana (originally); later New York City. Discogs artist ID: 590912.

Name variations on record: C. Hanson, Charles Hansen, Charles Harison, Charley HansonA, Charlie Hansen, Charlie Hanson, Hanson.

Overview

Charles Hanson is a multi-instrumentalist whose career spans New Orleans punk, Philadelphia new wave, New York noise rock, and engineering work into the 2020s. He co-founded The Normals (2) in New Orleans in the late 1970s — producing their sole original-era release, the 1978 single “Almost Ready” / “Hardcore” (Lectric Eye Records), a key document of Southern punk. After the Normals’ initial run, he moved to Philadelphia and co-founded The Vels with vocalist Alice Cohen (as Alice DeSoto) and keyboardist Chris Larkin — a new wave / synth-pop trio that released three singles and an LP on Mercury (1983–1986). During the same era he contributed a co-written song to a Kym Ryder single on Invasion Recordings (1988).

By the early 1990s Hanson had moved to New York, where he joined Loudspeaker (a noise-rock outfit on Patois/Cargo) as bassist, replacing Jens Jurgensen. He then became part of the rotating drummer pool for Chrome Cranks, appearing across multiple releases between 1993 and 1995 and on the 2009 retrospective The Murder Of Time. He rejoined The Normals (2) for two reunion releases in 2011 and 2017. His most recent Discogs credit is as engineer on tracks 6–10 of Smash Palace’s 87 (Think Like A Key Music, 2025).
