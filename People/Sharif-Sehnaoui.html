Sharif Sehnaoui

Discogs ID: 465384
Active: 2003–present
Based: Beirut, Lebanon
Instruments: Electric guitar, acoustic guitar, drums/percussion
Genres: Free improvisation, free jazz, experimental

Free improviser and guitarist based in Beirut. Curator of the Irtijal festival of experimental and improvised music. Core member of the Beirut free-improv scene centred around Al Maslakh, the label and cultural project founded in association with Mazen Kerbaj and others. Operates across solo guitar practice, small-group improvisation, and large international collectives.

Name variations: S. Sehnaoui, SS, Sehnaoui, Sharif Sehnoui.
