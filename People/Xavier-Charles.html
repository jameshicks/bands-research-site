Xavier Charles

French clarinettist active in free improvisation and electroacoustic music. Discogs artist ID: 214620.

Name variations on record: Charles, X. Charles, X.Charles, XC, Xavier.

Overview

Xavier Charles is a French clarinetist whose work sits at the intersection of free improvisation, electroacoustic music, and extended technique. He has been a consistent presence in the European improvised music scene since the mid-1990s, active across French, Norwegian, Dutch, and international contexts. His collaborations span intimate duos and trios through to large ensembles such as ONCEIM (the Paris-based large-scale improvisation orchestra), Corkestra (Amsterdam), and Dans Les Arbres (his long-running Norwegian quartet with Christian Wallumrød, Ivar Grydeland, and Ingar Zach on ECM/Hubro). He has a long-standing duo and quartet relationship with Austrian trumpeter Franz Hautzinger, documented most fully on Tsstt! (2012, Monotype Records) and Torche ! (2017, Tour De Bras). He also has an ongoing connection to the Makkum Records / Zea orbit (Arnold de Boer), appearing on multiple releases across two decades.
