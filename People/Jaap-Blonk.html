Biography

Jaap Blonk is a self-taught composer, voice performer, and sound poet born in Woerden, Holland. His studies in physics, mathematics, and musicology went unfinished, though they instilled a penchant for Dada-inflected approaches to language and sound. A series of unsuccessful office jobs reinforced a disposition toward anti-institutional experimentation.

In the late 1970s Blonk took up the saxophone and began composing music. The decisive turn came a few years later when he discovered the voice as his primary instrument. By the mid-1980s he had developed into a specialist in the performance of sound poetry — a practice he traces to the Dada tradition of Hugo Ball and Kurt Schwitters — distinguished by a powerful stage presence and an improvisational freedom verging on the childlike.

His first major recorded statement was a 1986 performance of Kurt Schwitters’s Ursonate (BV Haast Records), produced by Gert-Jan Blom, which brought his voice work to documented form. He followed this with the 1993 solo album Flux De Bouche on Staalplaat, a fully produced set of voice compositions, and Bek (2002, Staalplaat), a duo with electronic artist Radboud Mens.

A central thread is the trio album Blonk, Gustafsson & Zerang (1996, Kontrans), improvised sessions with Swedish saxophonist Mats Gustafsson and Chicago percussionist Michael Zerang — an encounter of free improvisation across European and American traditions. Blonk and Zerang continued this relationship on Blonk, Smith & Zerang.

Blonk founded and runs his own record label, Kontrans (Discogs label ID: 24663), which has been the primary outlet for his work since 1993. The label’s catalog covers his solo albums, his group BRAAXTAAL, and a series of documented collaborations and improvised duo/trio sessions. Kontrans has two sublabels: Electronic Improvisor Series and Improvisors.

Alongside his free improvisation work, Blonk founded two ongoing ensembles:

  • BRAAXTAAL — an avant-rock trio with synthesizer and drums, with Rob Daenen and Theo Bodewes
  • Splinks — a 15-piece orchestra playing his compositions; members include Angelo Verploegen, Bart Van Der Putten, Tobias Delius, Mary Oliver, and others

He has also worked with electronics and the computer (Powerbook), sampling and processing his own voice. His duo with Norwegian vocalist Maja S. K. Ratkje generated two Kontrans releases (Majaap, 2004; Post-Human Identities, 2005), documented under their collective name MAJAAP.

Other significant collaborators include Cor Fuhler (EMS synthesizer, turntables; Pre-Zoic Cellways, 2005), Carl Ludwig Hübsch and Claus van Bebber (Hübsch, Van Bebber & Blonk, 2003), Norwegian musicians Ingar Zach and Ivar Grydeland (Blonk, Zach & Grydeland, 2004), guitarist Terrie Ex (Thirsty Ears, 2018), Munich-based reed player Udo Schindler (Lakefront Discussions, 2021), and Chicago improvisers Ken Vandermark, Lou Mallozzi, and Frank Rosaly.

Blonk’s performances are described as simultaneously music, poetry, and performance art. He has toured extensively across Europe, the US, Canada, Indonesia, South Africa, and Latin America. Commissioned works include a solo voice piece for the Donaueschinger Musiktage 2002 and several radio plays. He has performed in compositions by German composer Carola Bauckholt including a work for voice and orchestra. His graphic scores have been exhibited as large-scale drawings.

He is also active as a workshop leader, with a particular emphasis on work with children.

A name variation, Reverof Zrem, appears in certain credits.
