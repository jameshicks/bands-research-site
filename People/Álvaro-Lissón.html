Overview

Álvaro Lissón is the founder and director of Meritorio Records and Cuarto Sonoro, working as a music publisher and mastering/mixing engineer. His 34 Discogs credits, all from 2022 onward, are exclusively in mastering and remastering roles across indie rock, jangle pop, and lo-fi releases. Meritorio Records is his primary association (six releases, 2022–2025), with Dust and Memory emerging as a second key label relationship from 2025. His collaborator network includes a tight-knit cluster of artists — among them Mikey Young, Gemma Fleet, and Andrew Robert Doig — each sharing three releases with him.
