Mick Turner

Discogs ID: 286071
Born: 1960, Melbourne, Victoria, Australia
ANV: The Marquis De Tren
Instruments: Guitar, bass, organ, melodica, loops

Australian guitarist and visual artist. Core member of Dirty Three (1992–present). Founded Anchor & Hope in 1996. Extensive solo catalogue on Drag City: Tren Phantasma (1997), Marlan Rosa (1999), Moth (2002), Seven Angels (2002, Three Lobed Recordings), Blue Trees (2007), Don’t Tell The Driver (2013). Paintings appear on multiple Dirty Three covers. Former/concurrent groups: Venom P. Stinger, The Moodists, Fungus Brains, Boxhead Ensemble, Tren Brothers, Bonnevill, Mess Esque.

Tren Brothers

With Dirty Three drummer Jim White (2), forms Tren Brothers (1998–2009) — instrumental duo. Their catalog: Gone Away / Kit’s Choice 7” (1998, Secretly Canadian SC 014); Tren Brothers EP (1998, Drag City DC146CD); Swing Pts. 1 & 2 7” (2001, Chapter Music CH34); The Swimmer CD EP (2005, Western Vinyl West 020); Blue Trees CD (2007, Drag City DC344CD / King Crab Records KC 007); Forth & Fine / Sometimes split 7” with Bridezilla (2009, Inertia Music IR7001). Blue Trees is credited jointly to Mick Turner / Tren Brothers and includes eight solo Turner tracks alongside six Tren Brothers pieces. See Tren Brothers for full article.

Notes (web)

Anchor & Hope Records — founding

Anchor & Hope was established by Dirty Three (all three members: Turner, Warren Ellis, Jim White (2)) to release Horse Stories (1996, AH001CD) — the band’s third album. The label was set up as an independent imprint with regional distribution: Shock Records (Australia), Big Cat Records (Europe), Touch and Go Records (USA). encyclopedia.com

Note: Discogs and Wikipedia credit Turner as the individual founder/operator of the label; encyclopedia.com frames it as a band collective decision for Horse Stories. No primary-source interview with Turner has been found that gives his personal account of why the label was started — multiple interviews checked (Scaruffi, Mess+Noise/Andrew McMillen, Beat Magazine, Digital in Berlin, Tempo Blog/Substack) did not address this. The Aquarium Drunkard 2025 interview may contain relevant material but is behind a paywall. Wikipedia: Mick Turner (as of 2026-10-07)
