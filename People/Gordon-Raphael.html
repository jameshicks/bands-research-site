Gordon Raphael

American record producer, musician, and engineer. Discogs artist ID: 252201. Also credited as G. Raphael, Gordon Raphaël, Gordy, Raphael.

Born in the US; has lived in Seattle, Los Angeles, London, and Berlin; currently based in West Yorkshire, UK.

Overview

Gordon Raphael is best known as the producer of The Strokes’ first two albums — Is This It (2001) and Room On Fire (2003) — and three tracks on First Impressions Of Earth (2006). The Strokes records established his reputation for a compressed, deliberately lo-fi aesthetic: close-miked guitars, dry drums, and a sense of controlled claustrophobia that became a touchstone of the early-2000s garage rock revival. He recorded the early Strokes sessions at Transporterraum, his studio in New York (later also a Berlin operation; see below).

Beyond The Strokes, Raphael has maintained a diverse production and engineering career stretching from a 1978 Seattle punk session through to 2026. Key clients include Regina Spektor (2003–2006), The Wildhearts (2004), Skin (2006), Hinds (2018), Colleen Green (2021), and a 2019 session with Green River for a Sub Pop archival reissue. He is also a prolific songwriter and multi-instrumentalist who claims roughly 2,000 composed pieces.

Career Timeline

Late 1970s — Seattle Origins

Raphael’s earliest Discogs credit is a 1978 keyboard and backing vocals appearance on Peasant/Slave, a 7” EP by Seattle punk/poetry outfit Chinas Comidas (Exquisite Corpse Records). He is credited as “Gordy.” This situates him in the Seattle underground well before the city’s grunge moment — the same milieu that would later produce Jack Endino, with whom Raphael would share bandmates in Sky Cries Mary.

He also appears as an assistant engineer and assistant recorded-by on Skin Yard’s self-titled debut (C/Z Records, 1986), again in the Seattle context.

1986–1999 — Musician Credits and Early Production

Through the late 1980s and 1990s Raphael accumulated credits primarily as a performer rather than producer:

  • Exit At The Axis (Capitol / World Domination, 1992) — Guitar, Keyboards, Sampler. This is a World Domination Recordings release; Raphael appears as a session/contributor.
  • A Return To The Inner Experience (Capitol, 1993) — Keyboards, Noises.
  • This Timeless Turning (World Domination, 1994) — Guitar (Les Paul, SG, Stratocaster), synthesizer (Pro-One, Moog Source, Micro Moog, JD-990, ARP Odyssey), ASR-10 sampler, organ (Vox), sequenced by, written-by. A densely credited performance role.
  • Every Iceberg Is Afire (World Domination, 1994) — Written-By.
  • Moonbathing On Sleeping Leaves (Warner Bros., 1997) — Written-By.
  • Stars (Restless Records, 1999) — Keyboards.

He also maintained a solo project: I Sleep On The Radio (Ars Divina, 1989) is listed under his own primary credit.

In 1995 he contributed additional keyboards to Sybil Vane (Island Records).

Sky Cries Mary

Raphael was a member of Sky Cries Mary, the Seattle psychedelic/alternative rock band. The group’s Discogs member list includes Raphael alongside Jack Endino, Jon Auer (Posies), Ken Stringfellow (Posies), and others — a convergence of Seattle figures from several scenes. His specific recording credits with Sky Cries Mary are not broken out in the DB at the release level, but his membership is documented.

Transporterraum — Studio and Collective

Raphael ran Transporterraum as a recording studio, first in New York City (where the early Strokes sessions were recorded) and later in Berlin. Discogs lists Transporterraum (ID: 196159) as a collective entity with named members including Raphael, Moses Schneider, Michael Ilbert, Ben Lauber, and others — figures from the Berlin studio and production world. Raphael contributed a “Recorded By” credit to Mélodie Citronique (Touch and Go, 2000) around this period.

He also formed the duo Absinthee (with Anne Hadlock) and was a member of Mental Mannequin.

2001–2003 — The Strokes

The relationship with The Strokes is Raphael’s most defining credit. He produced:

The Modern Age (Rough Trade, 2001) — Recorded By credit. The debut single that broke the band in the UK.

Hard To Explain / New York City Cops (RCA Europe, 2001) — Producer, Engineer, Mixed By.

Last Nite (RCA Europe, 2001) — Producer, Engineer, Mixed By.

Is This It (RCA / BMG, 2001) — Producer. Full lineup: Julian Casablancas (vocals, all songs written by), Albert Hammond Jr. (guitar), Nick Valensi (guitar), Nikolai Fraiture (bass), Fabrizio Moretti (drums). Mastered by Greg Calbi. 71 pressings/editions documented on Discogs.

Someday (Rough Trade, 2002) — Producer. Single from Is This It campaign.

Room On Fire (RCA, 2003) — Producer. Head engineer Toshikazu Yoshioka, 2nd engineer Will Kelly. Mastered by Greg Calbi and Steve Fallone. 59 pressings/editions documented.

12:51 (Rough Trade, 2003) — Producer.

Reptilia (BMG/RCA, 2003) — Producer.

The End Has No End (RCA, 2003) — Producer.

The compressed, analogue-leaning sound Raphael crafted for these records — tracked at Transporterraum — became a reference point for a generation of garage rock and post-punk revival acts. The combination of Julian Casablancas’ detached baritone and Raphael’s dry, close production aesthetic is the defining texture of both albums.

2003–2006 — Regina Spektor

Raphael produced a substantial portion of Regina Spektor’s early catalogue on Shoplifter Records (her self-run imprint) and later for Sire/Warner:

  • Soviet Kitsch (Shoplifter Records, 2003) — Producer (co-produced with Regina Spektor and Alan Bezozi). This is the album that broke Spektor into wider consciousness, driven by the NYC anti-folk scene.
  • Carbon Monoxide (Sire / Transgressive, 2005) — Producer (side A, co-produced with Bezozi).
  • Mary Ann Meets The Gravediggers And Other Short Stories (Sire / Transgressive, 2005) — Producer on tracks 3, 4, 11 (co-produced with Spektor and Bezozi).
  • Us (Sire / Transgressive, 2006) — Producer on track 1 (alongside Alan Bezozi).

The Spektor connection extended Raphael’s New York anti-folk network into the major-label sphere and demonstrated that his productions could serve intimate singer-songwriter material as effectively as band rock.

2003–2005 — Broader Production Work

During the Strokes’ Room On Fire period and after, Raphael took on a range of UK and European clients:

  • All American Girl (Shoplifter Records, 2003) — Producer. (Spektor-adjacent Shoplifter release.)
  • Not Another Pop Queen (Shoplifter Records, 2003) — Engineer; Producer.
  • Red & Black (SUBverse Recordings, 2003) — Producer.
  • The Rock And Roll EP (Tri-Tone, 2003) — Producer.
  • Stormy In The North - Karma In The South (Round Records, 2003) — Producer.
  • Exit / Ambien (Hummer, 2003) — Producer.
  • Riff After Riff (The Wildhearts, Gearhead Records, 2004) — Producer on tracks 4, 8–10, 12. (The album had multiple producers; Ginger, Russ Russell, and Simon Efemey also produced tracks.)
  • Massachusetts Murder Medallions (SUBverse Recordings, 2004) — Producer.
  • Coupled With (Punk’n Drunk Recordings, 2004) — Producer.
  • Burn All The Flags (Motor Music, 2005) — Co-producer.
  • Spitting Off Tall Buildings (Aggressive Rockproduktionen, 2005) — Producer (Vocals).
  • Stolen Horse (Four Music, 2005) — Lyrics By, Vocals.
  • Decadence & Isolation (Motor Music, 2005) — Keyboards (some); Mixed By.

He also received a “Vocal Coach [Vocal Security]” credit on the Hives’ Smack Smash (Epitaph/WEA, 2004) — an unusual non-production credit reflecting his proximity to the garage rock world of the period.

2005–2006 — First Impressions Of Earth and Skin

First Impressions Of Earth (The Strokes, RCA/Sony BMG, 2006) — Producer on tracks 4, 8, and 9. The bulk of the album was produced by David Kahne; Raphael was brought in for three tracks, suggesting a deliberate bridge between the old and new production regimes. Mixed by Andy Wallace.

Fake Chemical State (Skin, V2 Records, 2006) — Producer (whole album, co-produced with Skin); also played keyboards on track 5 and organ/synth on track 4. This is the third solo album by Skin (lead singer of Skunk Anansie). A dense co-production involving Linda Perry (track 7), Paul Draper (track 1), and Alan Moulder (mixing).

Other 2006 productions: Just Let The Sun (V2, 2006) — Producer; Purple (V2, 2006) — Producer; Super700 (Motor Music, 2006) — Producer and illustrator; Us (Regina Spektor, Sire/Transgressive, 2006) — Producer (track 1); Bending The Willow (Pop-U-Loud/PIAS, 2006) — Mixed By; Hands And Arrows (Pop-U-Loud/PIAS, 2006) — Mixed By.

2006–2010 — Berlin Period and European Work

Raphael’s base shifted toward Berlin and the European circuit. Credits from this period:

  • Electric Zeppelin (Mamasweed, 2006) — Mixed By.
  • Out Now (Blue Pearls Music/Indigo, 2006) — Recorded By, Other (Vibe).
  • Deportivo (Barclay/Le Village Vert, 2007) — Mixed By.
  • Fatherhood / Motherhood (Transgressive Records, 2007) — Producer, Mixed By.
  • .Limbo Messiah (Warner Music Group Germany, 2007) — Other (Vocal Security). (The Hives again, same role as 2004.)
  • Dissatisfied EP (Framingo Records, 2008) — Producer.
  • The Mondrians (Not On Label, France, 2008) — Producer.
  • Reality Check (XL/Merok, 2008) — Recorded By (Additional Vocals).
  • 7/1 (Too Pure, 2009) — Producer; Recorded By.
  • EP (Klee, Japan, 2009) — Producer.
  • Close The Evil Eye (62 TV Records/PIAS, 2010) — Recorded By, Producer, Mixed By.
  • Pull In Emergency (Pull In Emergency, Mute Records, 2010) — Producer.

2011–2018 — Continued Output

  • Einer Will Immer Mehr (Pflanz einen Baum, 2011) — Recorded By, Mixed By.
  • Talk (Moshi Moshi Records, 2011) — Producer.
  • Wer Bist Du? (Pflanz einen Baum, 2011) — Mixed By; Recorded By (Vocals).
  • Escape From Black Cloud (Nasoni Records, 2012) — Mixed By.
  • Numbers: 1-13 (Blood And Biscuits, 2012) — Producer.
  • The Real Thing (Nomad Songs, 2013) — Organ (Hammond), Keyboards (Wurlitzer); Recorded By, Producer.
  • Wolfy (Not On Label, Mexico, 2014) — Mixed By; Producer; Recorded By.
  • Ghost Into The Fog (Bughlt Records, 2015) — Producer.
  • Cultura Generale (GodzillaMarket/Warner Music Italy, 2015) — Piano; Producer, Recorded By.
  • I Don’t Run (Hinds, Lucky Number, 2018) — Co-producer. The Spanish indie-rock band’s second album. Engineered by Paco Loco; mixed by Shawn Everett.

2019–Present — Archival and Recent Work

  • Dry As A Bone (Green River, Sub Pop, 2019) — Producer, Recorded By on tracks C9–C14. This is a comprehensive archival reissue of the pre-Mudhoney / pre-Pearl Jam Seattle band. Raphael’s tracks appear to document previously unissued or alternate recordings from early in his career, connecting his Seattle origins to the present.
  • Sleep On The Radio (Zero Hours, 2018) — Primary artist credit; solo release.
  • New Song (King Forward Records, 2020) — Voice.
  • Cool (Colleen Green, Hardly Art, 2021) — Producer, Engineer. The Los Angeles pop-punk artist’s album for the Sub Pop subsidiary.
  • Howling At A Concrete Moon (Not On Label, 2023) — Producer.
  • You Can’t Eat The Moon And Be A Werewolf Too (Needlejuice Records, 2025) — Engineer (Sound Engineer); Producer, Mixed By.
  • Selling A Vibe (Play It Again Sam / Sonic Blew, 2026) — Producer.
