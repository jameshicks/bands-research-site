Overview

Alfonso Thornton, known as Fonzi Thornton, is an American singer, session vocalist, and vocal contractor from East Harlem, New York, active in the music industry since the mid-1970s. He built his reputation chiefly as a core part of the Chic orbit, working alongside Nile Rodgers and Bernard Edwards, and sustained parallel long-running relationships with Luther Vandross and Bryan Ferry / Roxy Music, as well as Aretha Franklin. His nearly 2,000 credits span a wide range of major labels — EMI, Epic, Atlantic, RCA, and Warner Bros. among them — reflecting a career anchored in session work and vocal contracting rather than recording under his own name. He did maintain a brief solo recording career in the first half of the 1980s, the decade that accounts for the bulk of his own release activity.
