Overview

Del Crabtree is a British multi-instrumentalist — primarily trumpet, but also bass and vocals — known as a member of Animals That Swim, the London post-rock/indie group active in the 1990s. His 42 Discogs credits cluster heavily in that decade, spread across labels including 3rd Stone, Circa, Elemental Records, and Fire Records, with a handful of later entries extending into the 2000s and a solitary 2026 Apollo credit. His closest collaborators are Graham Sutton (24 shared) and Lee Harris (21 shared), both central to Bark Psychosis, with 19 credits shared directly with that group — pointing to a tight overlap between the Animals That Swim and Bark Psychosis circles. His work sits at the intersection of post-rock, experimental, and indie styles.
