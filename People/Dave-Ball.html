Dave Ball

Dave Ball

Oct 28, 20266 min read

Dave Ball

Real name: David James Ball
Born: 3 May 1959, Blackpool, Lancashire, England
Died: 22 October 2025
Discogs ID: 62914
Aliases: David Jamez, Lance Rock, Luv Bass, Mista Luv, Ronnie B (3)

British electronic musician and producer. Co-founder (with Marc Almond) of Soft Cell; later co-founder (with Richard Norris) of The Grid. Married to violinist Gini Ball (Virginia Hewes, credited as “Gini Hewes” on early releases). Published memoir Electronic Boy (2020, Omnibus Press), in which he described himself as “the Backcover Man.”

Early Career and Soft Cell (1978–1984)

Ball met Marc Almond while both were students at Leeds Polytechnic. Their early recordings — synthesizer-and-vocals duo work — appeared on the Mutant Moments E.P. (1980, A Big Frock Rekord), with Ball credited on Synthesizer, Tape, and Electronics. Soft Cell signed to Some Bizzare and achieved a UK #1 with their cover of “Tainted Love” (1981), with Ball on synthesizer and percussion.

Ball wrote or co-wrote most of Soft Cell’s material throughout this period. He also produced the 12-inch single “What!” (1982, Vertigo) and the box set The Twelve Inch Singles (1982).

Solo: In Strict Tempo (1983)

Ball’s debut solo album on Some Bizzare, produced, performed, and written entirely by him. A largely instrumental electronic record with chamber textures added by session players.

Psychic TV / Genesis P-Orridge Connections (1990–2013)

Ball has 21 shared Discogs credits with Genesis P-Orridge, spanning compilation songwriting credits and direct studio collaboration.

Towards Thee Infinite Beat (1990, Temple Records)

Psychic TV’s acid house LP. Ball contributed keyboards and sampler on five tracks (A1, A4, A5, B1, B4). Core production by Fred Giannelli and Genesis P-Orridge, with Matthew Best and Paula P-Orridge also contributing.

Jack The Tab (Tekno Acid Beat) (1990, Wax Trax!)

Ball co-wrote tracks on this Psychic TV-adjacent acid house compilation alongside Genesis P-Orridge and Richard Norris (his future Grid partner). The Ball/Norris co-write credits here appear to be an early point of contact between the two.

  • Thee Fabulous Feast Ov Flowering Light (2013, Cold Spring) — same ensemble performer credit

Cabaret Voltaire: The Crackdown (1983)

Ball played Keyboards, Drum Programming (credited “Rhythm Programmes”), and Tape on Cabaret Voltaire’s The Crackdown (Some Bizzare/Virgin), the Sheffield duo’s pivotal move toward funk-influenced industrial. Other contributors included Flood (engineer/producer) and Stephen Mallinder.

English Boy On The Loveranch (late 1980s)

Discogs group ID: 19753. A Ball side project active in the late 1980s; issued soundtrack recordings Sex Vigilante (1988) and The Man In Your Life (1987) on New Rose Records. Ball credited as songwriter and performer. Engineers on Sex Vigilante included Flood.

The Grid (1989–1995, 2007–present)

After Soft Cell disbanded (1984), Ball formed The Grid with Richard Norris, initially as M.E.S.H. The duo signed to EastWest (Warner) and later Deconstruction/BMG, achieving mainstream UK chart success.

The Grid also remixed David Bowie’s “Hallo Spaceboy” (additional production/remix, 1996, BMG). Leviathan (2021) was issued on Robert Fripp’s Discipline Global Mobile label — a King Crimson label connection.

Soft Cell Reunion and Late Career (2001–2025)

Soft Cell regrouped for a series of reunion activities beginning around 2001:

  • Cruelty Without Beauty (2002, spinART) — Ball: Electronic Instruments, Backing Vocals, Producer, Written-By
  • Live (2003, Cooking Vinyl) — Ball: Synthesizer (Mini Moog, Mooger Fooger, Roland SH-101, Roland Juno 106, Yamaha QS3000, Yamaha CS5), Producer, Mixed By, Written-By
  • Keychains And Snowstorms (The Soft Cell Story) (2018, Mercury/UMC) — Ball: Executive Producer, Photography
  • *Happiness Not Included (2022, BMG) — Ball: Moogs, Synths, Analogue Sequencing, Upright Piano, Additional Vocals
  • Ecce Homo (2024, BMG) — Ball: Moog and Roland Synthesizers, Bass, EFX Boxes, Producer, Written-By

Ball also provided 2020 remixes of Cruelty Without Beauty tracks for A Big Frock Rekord, his own imprint.

Photosynthesis (2016)

Solo/duo album co-credited to Ball and Jon Savage (4), released on Cold Spring. Ball: Mini Moog, Moogerfoogers, Sequential Circuits Prophet 5. Also on Cold Spring: Lost In Blue (2019) — Bass Guitar, Synthesizer, Producer.
