Tom Coyne

Discogs ID: 272899 Real name: Thomas Paul Coyne Born: December 10, 1954, Union, New Jersey, USA Died: April 13, 2017 Role: Mastering engineer, lacquer cutting Active: 1963–2017 (Discogs credit range) Primary studios: Dick Charles Recording Inc., Frankford-Wayne Mastering Labs, The Hit Factory, Sterling Sound Associated labels: Alligator Records, Jive, Columbia, Def Jam Recordings, Atlantic, RCA, Tommy Boy

Overview

Tom Coyne was one of the most prolific mastering engineers in American popular music, with over 14,000 release credits on Discogs spanning six decades. Born in Union, New Jersey, he died on April 13, 2017 at age 62.

His career moved through a succession of New York’s premier mastering facilities. His first job was at Dick Charles Recording Inc. (Discogs ID: 196522), where he first worked alongside Lee Hulko. In 1978 he moved to Frankford-Wayne Mastering Labs, where he built expertise in dance and R&B throughout the 1980s. In 1989 he joined The Hit Factory, cementing his reputation in hip-hop and contemporary R&B. In 1994 he moved to Sterling Sound (Discogs ID: 264060). In 1998, Coyne co-founded the new Sterling Sound partnership with Ted Jensen, Greg Calbi, Murat Aktar (Absolute Audio, Discogs ID: 271189, co-founder), and the UK-based Metropolis Studios (Discogs ID: 265000), buying Sterling Sound from Lee Hulko.

His Grammy wins included mastering Adele’s 21 and 25, and Taylor Swift’s 1989. One of his distinctive marks was the cents symbol ¢ hand-etched in vinyl runout grooves — on Discogs, runout appearances of ¢ are credited as “Lacquer Cut By [Runout Etching ¢] - Tom Coyne.” When DMM appears alongside the ¢ symbol, the credit shifts to “Direct Metal Mastering By.”

The original stub for this page cited ~330 Alligator Records credits. The actual Discogs count for Alligator Records is ~380 release credits. Total Discogs credits across all roles exceed 14,200 individual release appearances (unique-master count: ~14,204).

Open Questions

  • Exact cause and circumstances of death (April 13, 2017): Not available from Discogs; would require web sources.
  • Earlier credits before 1978: The Discogs record shows a 1963 year on one release but Coyne’s profile says he started at Dick Charles (first job) — early credits may be attributed via runout ¢ identification and may include work he did as an assistant or under Lee Hulko’s supervision.
  • Grammy Award years and categories: Discogs does not record Grammy Award metadata; confirmed from profile text only (no specific year data).
  • Full list of Frankford/Wayne-era work: Several credits carry the note “[Frankford Wayne]” in the role string (~5 credits explicitly noted), but the bulk of his 1978–1989 work at that facility is credited generically as “Mastered By” or “Lacquer Cut By.”
  • Murat Aktar’s role at Sterling Sound: Profile mentions him as Absolute Audio co-founder involved in the 1998 buyout; his specific ongoing role at Sterling is unclear from Discogs.
  • Relationship with Hit Factory (1989–1994): Only one credit explicitly notes “@Hit Factory” in the role string, though his bio places him there for five years.
