Ólöf Júlía Kjartansdóttir

Icelandic violinist/violist. Discogs ID: 372461. Name variation credited as “Ólöf Júlía.” Founding member of Amiina, active on their earliest session work and the 2006 “Seoul” single. Session strings player on several key Icelandic post-rock and ambient records in the 2000s–2010s.

Biography

Ólöf Júlía Kjartansdóttir is one of the four founding members of Amiina, the Icelandic string quartet formed in Reykjavík. Discogs lists her directly as a member of Amiina (artist ID 779865), which confirms formal membership rather than merely extended-collaborator status.

Her first traceable Discogs credit is from 2000 on Motorlab #2 (Kitchen Motors / Smekkleysa), a compilation featuring múm (tracks 1–5), Apparat Organ Quartet (tracks 6–8), and Big Band Brútal (tracks 9–11). She plays violin on the múm tracks, alongside fellow Amiina member Stefanía Ólafsdóttir (viola) and Guðný Guðmundsdóttir (violin). The compilation was mastered and mixed by Jóhann Jóhannsson.

In 2002 she contributed uncredited viola to Sigur Rós’s ( ) (FatCat Records, release ID 69857). In 2005 she is credited on Takk… (Geffen Records, release ID 515622) playing violin, where Amiina are listed collectively for strings alongside a larger ensemble of Icelandic brass and string players. The 2006 Hoppípolla UK single (EMI) carries a further violin credit.

The 2006 “Seoul” single — released on the Amiina self-titled label — credits her as Written-By and Performer alongside the other three Amiina members: Edda Rún Ólafsdóttir, María Huld Markan Sigfúsdóttir, and Sólrún Sumarliðadóttir. Mixing and mastering on that single involved Birgir Jón Birgisson (Biggi) and Kjartan Sveinsson of Sigur Rós.

The Heima live documentary film (2007, EMI, release ID 1138887) credits her on viola — reflecting Amiina’s role as Sigur Rós’s live string accompanists for the Heima concerts.

Outside the Amiina/Sigur Rós orbit, she appears as session violin on Get It Together (2010, Kölski) by Icelandic indie-pop band Dikta (release ID 4408907), and as a “Performer” on Living Room Songs (2011, Erased Tapes Records) by Ólafur Arnalds — a large ensemble recording where she is credited under the name variation “Ólöf Júlía.”
