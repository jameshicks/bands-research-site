Farin Urlaub

Real name: Jan Ulrich Max Vetter Discogs IDs: 253010 (as Farin Urlaub); 478864 (as Jan Vetter) Born: 27 October 1963, West Berlin Roles: guitarist, vocalist, songwriter, producer

Guitarist and co-vocalist of Die Ärzte, one of Germany’s biggest and longest-running punk/rock acts. Co-founded the band in 1982 with Bela B. (Dirk Felsenheimer). His solo career began in the early 2000s and evolved into the ensemble project Farin Urlaub Racing Team from 2006. His pre-Ärzte career included the early Berlin punk band Soilent Grün (1980–1984) and the new wave group King Køng (1990–1992).

Early Years: Berlin Punk Scene (1980–1982)

Before Die Ärzte, Jan Vetter was active in the West Berlin underground. He was a member of Soilent Grün (Discogs ID: 358674), a punk band that recorded as early as 1980 (demo tapes, “Proberaumdemos 1980”) and released the Die Fleisch EP on Screen (1982) and the live tape Live 26.9.81 on Graf Haufen Tapes. The band also included Bela B. (Dirk Felsenheimer). Soilent Grün is documented as a Die Ärzte precursor/alias; their 1984 tape In Memorandum was also released on Graf Haufen Tapes.

He is also credited on the 1982 live tape Live Im Flöz (007 Tapes) in an artwork capacity, showing involvement in Berlin’s tape-trading circuit.

Die Ärzte (1982–1988, 1993–present)

Farin Urlaub and Bela B. co-founded Die Ärzte in 1982, initially with Hans Runge (Sahnie) on bass. The band signed to CBS for a run of albums through the mid-to-late 1980s.

The band split in 1988. Various compilations and archival releases continued to appear through Vielklang and other labels throughout 1989–1990, including Die Ärzte Früher! (1982–‘83) Der Ausverkauf Geht Weiter! (1989, Vielklang), for which Farin wrote liner notes.

The early period also spawned a notable side project: Die Ulkigen Pulkigen (Discogs ID: 167014), an alias credited only to Farin Urlaub (Jan Vetter) and Bela B., which appeared as producer on the 1989 Vielklang archival release.

King Køng Interlude (1990–1992)

During the Die Ärzte hiatus, Jan Vetter was a full member of King Køng (Discogs ID: 423506), a new wave / rock group on Ariola. King Køng’s lineup also included Christian Florie, Conny Mathieu, Hans-Jürgen Reznicek, and Uwe Hoffmann (who would later be a key engineer/mixer on Farin’s solo work). He also appeared on a 1991 Polydor release (Wir Brauchen… Werner, credited as Jan Vetter as primary artist) and a 1992 Life Itself Is Sweet, Sweet, Sweet! release.

Reunion and the Metronome Era (1993–1997)

Die Ärzte reunited in 1993 with Rod Gonzalez (Rodrigo González) replacing the original bassists. The reunion LP Die Bestie In Menschengestalt (Metronome, 1993) contains split songwriting across all three members; Farin wrote or co-wrote the majority of tracks. “Schrei Nach Liebe” — one of their best-known songs, famously used as an anti-fascism anthem — credits Farin for music and lyrics.

Hot Action Records Era (1998–present)

From 1998, Die Ärzte operated primarily through their own label Hot Action Records (Discogs ID: 33213; URL: bademeister.com), with Farin’s solo work channelled through the sublabel Völker Hört Die Tonträger (Discogs ID: 33283).

On Jazz Ist Anders (2007), track-level credits show a consistent three-way split: Farin wrote the majority of songs (Himmelblau, Lasse Redn, Junge, Nur Einen Kuss, Heulerei, Deine Freundin, Allein, Living Hell, Vorbei Ist Vorbei), Bela B. wrote several others, and Rod Gonzalez contributed to a handful including Breit.

Farin also took cover photography credit on the 2006 live album Livealbum Of Death (Völker Hört Die Tonträger) under the Farin Urlaub Racing Team name.

Solo Career

Endlich Urlaub! (2001)

Farin’s debut solo album, released under his own name on Völker Hört Die Tonträger. He wrote all music and lyrics, produced, and mixed (alongside Gørdi Gerhardt and Uwe Hoffmann). A brass section — Hardy Appich (trumpet), Hans-Jörg Fischer (tenor saxophone, brass arranger), Robert Göhring (trombone), Peter Quintern (alto saxophone) — was a core sound element. String arrangements on “Phänomenal Egal” by Ralf Hübner (2). Mastered by Howie Weinberg.

Singles from this period: “Glücklich,” “Sumisu,” “OK,” “Phänomenal Egal” (all on Völker Hört Die Tonträger / Universal).

Am Ende Der Sonne (2005)

Second solo album on Völker Hört Die Tonträger. Farin produced and mixed again with Gørdi Gerhardt and Uwe Hoffmann. The same brass ensemble (Appich, Fischer, Göhring, Quintern) returned, with backing vocals by Annette Steinkamp, Simone Richter (2), and Vanessa Mason — all of whom became core Farin Urlaub Racing Team members. Mastered by Alex Klier. “Mehr” features Akiko San.

Singles: “Porzellan,” “Sonne,” “Dusche.”

Berliner Schule (2017)

A solo double-LP (29 tracks, vinyl only, Völker Hört Die Tonträger). Farin is credited for all roles: Composed By, Lyrics By, Arranged By, Vocals, Recorded By, Producer, Mixed By. Cover photo credited under the anagram alias “Niraf Alburu.” Rod Gonzalez appears on drums for the “Intro” track outro. Artwork/mastering by Chris Lippert. One of the most fully self-produced of his projects.

Other Group Projects and Aliases

Die Diabolischen 2 (2004)

Side project of Farin Urlaub and Bela B. only (Discogs ID: 2569648). Released one album: Never Mind The Ärzte Here’s Die Diabolischen 2 (2004, Elvis Schmiedekamp Entertainment). The entity shares aliases with Die Ärzte, Die Ulkigen Pulkigen, Laternen-Joe, and Nudo Tra I Cannibali.

Laternen-Joe (2011–2019)

A full-band alias used by all three Die Ärzte members (Farin, Bela B., Rod Gonzalez; Discogs ID: 2981273). Releases include The Best Of (2011, Hot Action Records), Ist Das Noch Punkrock? (2012, Hot Action Records), and Das Comeback Live In Zwickau (2019, self-released). The entity is an official alias on the Die Ärzte Discogs page.

Nudo Tra I Cannibali (2003–2005)

Another alias for all three Die Ärzte members (Discogs ID: 8247299). Italian-named (“Naked Among Cannibals”). Releases include Buona Notte (2003, self-released) and Nackt Unter Kannibalen (Kegeln Für Kreuzberg Tour 2003) - Terzo Edizione (2005, DÄB Records, Italy).

030 (2) (1985)

A large Berlin ensemble (Discogs ID: 4672770) that included both Farin (Jan Vetter) and Bela B. as members, alongside George Kranz and others. Released one single: Mitten Drin Und Voll Daneben (1985, ST 45).

Production and Writing Credits Outside Die Ärzte

Farin produced the King Køng catalog across three albums on Ariola / Gringo Records (1990–1992).

He has a credited role on the Soilent Grün tribute A Tribute To Soilent Grün (2005, Evil Killing Records) as Written-By.

A 2004 film soundtrack release (Notaufnahme, Subversive Rock Productions) credits him for Guitar and Vocals.

A 2006 release Ich Kann Auch Ohne Dich (Vertigo Berlin) credits him for Music By, Lyrics By (compilation context).

Widergeburt (2021, Cargo Records GmbH) credits him for Bass.

Yours (2017, Beatstuff / Warner Music Central Europe) credits him as Producer.
