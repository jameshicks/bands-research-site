Fred Thomas

Multi-instrumentalist, songwriter, producer, label operator, and prolific collaborator from the Detroit/Ann Arbor underground. Discogs artist ID: 305642

Overview

Fred Thomas is one of the most densely connected figures in the Michigan indie scene, with primary credits on releases dating from 1996 to 2024 and member credits in at least 28 groups catalogued on Discogs. He is the creative engine behind Saturday Looks Good To Me (the project that brought him to Polyvinyl) and City Center (3) (duo with Ryan Howard, released on Type Records, 2009–2011), and has had stints in His Name Is Alive and Tyvek. His solo catalog under his own name runs from 2002 to at least 2024, with five albums on Polyvinyl Record Company.

Beyond performing, Thomas has run a succession of small labels — Westside Audio Laboratories, hereforeveralways, Ypsilanti Records, Invisible Friend, and Life Like — that between them have released records by dozens of Michigan artists over three decades. He also records and mixes extensively for others, and has done mastering work since the mid-2010s.

Aliases on Discogs: All Energy Must Continue Upward, Child (10), Ecoatm, Everyone Name variations on sleeve credits: F. Thomas, FT, Fred, Fred Thermos, Tape Head Fred, Thoma, Thomas Bandcamp: fredthomasmusic.bandcamp.com
