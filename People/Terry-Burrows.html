Terry Burrows

Oct 25, 202610 min read

Terry Burrows

Real name: Terence Ashley Burrows
Born: Ipswich, Suffolk, England
Based: London (later career)
Discogs artist ID: 272201
Aliases: Jung Analysts (ID: 164878), Yoo-Ko (ID: 302728), Yukio Yung (ID: 84448)
URLs: terryburrows.com · onoma.co.uk · Wikipedia

Multi-instrumentalist, producer, and prolific author. Classically trained pianist from age five; self-taught guitarist from twelve. Also plays bass, drums, saxophone, theremin, and VCS3. Described by AllMusic as “a classically trained keyboardist with an advanced degree in computer engineering.” His recording career spans four decades from post-punk industrial funk in 1982 to noise/drone, art pop, and electronic dance music. Simultaneously one of the world’s best-selling authors of music tuition books, with close to one hundred titles published across sixteen countries.

Biography

Early Career and Hamster Records (1979–1985)

Still in his teens, Burrows founded Hamster Records And Tapes in Leicester, a DIY cassette-and-vinyl imprint releasing non-commercial acts including R. Stevie Moore, Rimarimba, Loch Ness Monster, and Attrition alongside his own work.

His first recordings appeared under the banner Push-Button Pleasure — a duo with Mark Beaumont (3) — and its sub-alias Jung Analysts (the ”(‘Jung Analysts’ Branch Of) Push-Button Pleasure”), billed as “post-punk industrial funk.” The debut LP (The Penal Collada Effect) / A Leading Surgeon Speaks! (Hamster HAM 008, 1982) split sides between Jung Analysts and Push-Button Pleasure tracks. A second cassette release, Bring Me The Leg Of Arthur Askey!! (Even), followed the same year.

Under the Jung Analysts name, Don’t Panic - Low Flying Norwegians (cassette, 1982) and The Wishing Balloons LP (Hamster HAM 12, 1984) extended the post-punk/experimental direction. The Jung Analysts alias also had one Cordelia Records release, the cassette Sprockendidootch? (1985).

Tietchens Collaboration and Abstract Industrial Work (1986–1989)

From 1986 Burrows recorded a series of “abstract industrial” LPs in collaboration with Hamburg Krautrock/electronic composer Asmus Tietchens (a former collaborator with Brian Eno and Cluster). The first, Watching The Burning Bride (Hamster HAM 16, 1986), consists entirely of untitled pieces: seventeen short tracks of abstract, minimal, industrial electronics, co-credited to both composers equally. A follow-up, Burning The Watching Bride (Disaster Area DA 001, 1998), appeared twelve years later and collected solo pieces by each composer on split sides. A 2017 compilation, Watching The Burning Bride / Burning The Watching Bride (Auf Abwegen / Die Stadt), combined both works.

Burrows also contributed a remix to Third Mind Records’ In The Realm Of The Hungry Ghosts (1986) compilation and received a mastering credit on 4 From The Madding Crowd (Third Mind, 1986).

His solo ambient/minimal LP The Whispering Scale (Hamster HAM 26, 1989) — fourteen untitled “Loop” pieces — appeared on his own label and stands as his most austere solo statement.

Yukio Yung and First Chrysanthemums Era (1986–1991)

In 1986, Cordelia Records — run by Alan Jenkins, leader of The Deep Freeze Mice — released Burrows’ first LP under the Yukio Yung alias: Tree-Climbing Goats (And Other Analysing Shanties) (Cordelia Ericat 019). The record runs to twenty-nine tracks across two sides (labeled “Bit Boring Side” and “Not Quite So Boring Side”), with Burrows performing all instruments and writing most material; Paul Smith (181) guests on two tracks.

Shortly after, Jenkins and Burrows co-founded Egg Plant as a joint venture between Hamster Records And Tapes and Cordelia Records, dedicated exclusively to the early releases of their new band The Chrysanthemums. Egg Plant used playful catalog numbering: One Egg, Two Eggs, Three Eggs, Four Eggs.

The Chrysanthemums’ debut LP Is That A Fish On Your Shoulder Or Are You Just Pleased To See Me? (Egg Plant, Two Eggs, 1987) features Burrows as Yukio Yung (lead vocals, piano), Jenkins on lead guitar, Vladimir Zajkowiecz on bass and bassoon, Geraldine Minou-Sullivan on drums, Jane Laing on hammered dulcimer, and R. Stevie Moore contributing vocals on one track (“The Holocaust Parade”). Songs are divided between Burrows (as Yung) and Jenkins as writers.

The XXXX Sessions (Egg Plant Three Eggs, 1988) and the ambitious double LP Little Flecks Of Foam Around Barking (Egg Plant Four Eggs, 1989) followed. The latter — thirty tracks across four sides — adds Jonathan Staines (ukulele, vocals) and Moore (tape), with Burrows credited as Yukio Yung for all lead vocals, keyboards, saxophone, guitar, orchestral/brass arrangements, and recording. The band’s cult following grew almost entirely outside the UK.

The Chrysanthemums signed to Belgian label Madagascar and released Odessey And Oracle (1990) — an LP-length cover of The Zombies’ 1968 album, with Burrows producing and playing all keyboard/wind instruments. Porcupine Quills (Madagascar, 1991) followed, an EP.

Egg Plant released a 1987 debut single, Another Sacred Day / Mouth Pain (catalog: ONE EGG), and a flexi sampler (EggFlexOne) before the full LPs.

In 2010, German music magazine MusikExpress placed The Chrysanthemums at number 23 in their list of the most under-rated bands of all time.

Yoo-Ko and Electronic Dance Music (1991–1992)

In the early 1990s Burrows briefly entered electronic dance music under the alias Yoo-Ko on the Belgian label Zazaboem. The 12” single Matrix (Zazaboem ZZB004, 1991) — released in Belgium with bleep/house/techno styles — reached the Top Ten in Germany’s Network Dance Chart. Burrows is credited as writer and programmer. A second Yoo-Ko release also appeared on Zazaboem.

Solo Yukio Yung and Chrys&themums (1993–1997)

Burrows resumed solo activity as Yukio Yung with Art Pop Stupidity LP (1993) and CD A Brainless Deconstruction of the Popular Song (1993), followed by a single and four EPs between 1993–1997 on Jarmusic and other labels. The Jarmusic LP Objectivity (1997, ID: 1148380) credits him directly under the Yukio Yung alias.

The Chrysanthemums continued through Jarmusic with Insekt Insekt and Two Thirty-Gallon Drums Of Banana Purée (both 1995), featuring Burrows alongside Jenkins.

In 1997 Burrows reunited with ex-Chrysanthemums member Vladimir Zajkowiecz (credited as Martin Howells) to form a new version of the group renamed Chrys&themums (a visual pun to distinguish from the original line-up).

Publishing Career and Musical Hiatus (1998–2003)

Between 1998 and 2004 Burrows was largely silent musically — a period he attributed to poor health and an increasingly demanding publishing schedule. By 2003 he had over 50 books published and was recognized as one of the world’s best-selling authors of music tuition titles, with over two million books sold in the US alone. He also began a parallel career as a university lecturer. His books include The Art of Sound (Thames & Hudson), Mute: A Visual Document (co-authored with Daniel Miller, Thames & Hudson), Total Guitar Tutor (Barnes & Noble), and the ITV Visual History of the 20th Century (Carlton).

Return: Yung & Moore, Tonesucker (2004–present)

In 2004 Burrows returned with a collaboration LP with R. Stevie Moore released as The Yung - Moore Show. Yung & Moore Versus The Whole Goddam Stinkin’ World (Orgone Records ORGONE1, 2006) is a 15-track album compiled by Burrows on Apple Macintosh; Moore and Burrows share songwriting throughout with additional contributions from Andy Ward (2) (drums) and Krystyna Olsiewicz (backing vocals). The duo also released The Yung and Moore Show on Orgone Records.

In 2006 Burrows launched Tonesucker, a noise/drone project described as “fundamentalist.” Members include John Bowers (2) and Steven Elsey. The debut CD Slaughterhouse (Onoma Research ONOMA1, 2006) runs four tracks totaling over an hour of drone metal/experimental material. Tonesucker has performed widely at festivals across Europe over the following fifteen years. A second album, Memento Mori (Onoma Research, 2017), followed. Burrows also performed theremin and VCS3 at the UK’s Aldeburgh Festival.

The Chrysanthemums returned to action with Decoy For A Dognapper! (Egg Plant SEVENEGGS, 2022) — a 90-minute concept album based on a single 1969 episode of the children’s cartoon Scooby-Doo. The label catalog number continues the Egg Plants sequence.

Family Yung (Discogs ID: 11144684) formed in the 2020s as a collaboration with Burrows’ son Louis Burrows. Their debut The Lost World Of Family Yung Parts One And Two (Moloko+ / Onoma Research, 2022) appeared the same year as the Chrysanthemums return.
