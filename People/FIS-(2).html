FIS (2)

Real name: Oliver Peryman
Discogs artist ID: 2546290
Alias on Discogs: Oliver Peryman (listed as separate artist entry, ID: 3151061)
Origin: New Zealand (profile says “New Zealand producer”; active primarily in the UK scene)
SoundCloud: soundcloud.com/fisnz
Active: 2011 – at least 2019

Electronic producer from New Zealand, operating under the name FIS. Active across drum and bass, halftime, abstract electronic, dark ambient, and experimental music from 2011 onward. Associated with a network of UK labels in the early-to-mid 2010s, with the most visible output on Tri Angle (2013–2014) and then Subtext (2016–2017). Peryman produces, writes, and masters his own material; on several releases he is also credited for illustration and artwork.

Discography Overview

2011 — Debut Releases

FIS’s earliest documented output is a digital single, Skin Beats / Nu (2011, Haunted Audio Recordings, HAR123), released as a digital WAV download — two tracks in an abstract drum and bass / IDM vein. The same year he co-released The Dares / The Truths with Tokyo Prose on Samurai Horo (HORO001), a 10” white-label test pressing in a drum and bass / halftime style. Samurai Horo (Discogs ID: 323765) is a sublabel of Samurai Music.

2012 — Duckdive EP and The Commons

Duckdive EP (2012, Samurai Horo, HORO007) was a 12” test pressing; it later received a proper release in 2015. Track “DMT Usher” from this EP was recycled (same title) on Preparations the following year.

The Commons (2012, Exit Records (7)) gave FIS a release on a third label — also in the drum and bass / experimental space.

2013 — Preparations and Homologous

Preparations (Tri Angle, TRIANGLE 21D / TRIANGLE 21) was FIS’s debut on Robin Carolan’s label. Released in digital form in 2013 (US pressing, cat TRIANGLE21D) and on vinyl in 2014 (UK pressing, TRIANGLE 21, 12”, 45 RPM). Four tracks: “Magister Nunns,” “DMT Usher,” “Mildew Swoosh,” “CE Visions.” Styles registered as Abstract, Dark Ambient, Drum n Bass, Experimental.

Production credits on the vinyl pressing: Written By and Producer — Oliver Peryman; Mastered By — Heba Kadry (tracks A1, B1, B2), Lewis Hopkin (track A2); Illustration — FIS (2); Design — Dr. Me.

Also in 2013: Homologous (Void Coms, Voidcoms001) — a one-sided etched 12” with three abstract tracks, released on the small UK label Void Coms.

RA.388 (2013, Resident Advisor) is a DJ mix released through Resident Advisor’s mix series — not a studio release.

2014 — Iterations and Speech Spirits

Iterations (Tri Angle, TRIANGLE 24) was FIS’s second and final Tri Angle release. Vinyl 12” (33⅓ RPM). Four tracks: “Iterations,” “Womb Dreams,” “Fever Sweats,” “Her Third Eye (Drum Rain).” Same stylistic territory as Preparations. Credits on the vinyl: Written-By, Producer, Illustration — Oliver Peryman; Mastered By — Heba Kadry (tracks A2, B1), Oliver Peryman (tracks A1, B2); Design — Dr. Me.

Note: FIS self-mastered two of the four tracks on Iterations — indicating increasing technical self-sufficiency.

Speech Spirits (2014, Loopy, XX0LPY) — a 12” EP on the small UK label Loopy. Two original tracks plus two remixes: “Speech Spirits (Kassem Mosse’d)” remixed by Kassem Mosse, and “Speech Spirits (‘The Nagger’ by Oren Ambarchi)” remixed by Oren Ambarchi. Mastered by Helmut Erler; layout by Christopher Leckie.

2015 — Fabriclive and The Blue Quicksand

FIS appeared on Fabriclive 80: Sampler 2 (2015, Fabric (2)), contributing a track to the sampler release for the Fabriclive mix series.

The Blue Quicksand Is Going Now (2015, Loopy, 000LPY) is FIS’s debut full-length album. A double-sided LP with nine tracks. Credits: Artwork — Brian DeGraw; Layout — Christopher Leckie; Mastered By — James Meharry. DeGraw (Discogs ID: 839997, also known as bEEdEEgEE) is an American musician and visual artist associated with the band Gang Gang Dance. The album’s title track runs 3:10; the longest piece, “Pedal,” runs 5:09. Styles: Abstract, Experimental.

2016 — From Patterns To Details (Subtext)

From Patterns To Details (2016, Subtext, SUB018) is FIS’s debut album on Subtext (sublabel of Multiverse Ltd., Discogs ID: 26421). A 7-track LP (vinyl). Credits: Written-By, Producer — Oliver Peryman; Artwork — James Ginzburg (of Emptyset); Design — John Coulthart. No external mastering credit; Peryman appears to have handled mastering himself. Styles: Abstract, Experimental. Subtext catno SUB018 places it as the 18th release on the label.

2017 — Clear Stones (with Rob Thorne)

Clear Stones (2017, Subtext, SUB022) is a collaborative LP with Rob Thorne (3) (also known as Man Alone, from New Zealand). Both are credited as primary artists, writers, and producers. Credits: Artwork — Pyur (Sophie Schnell); Design — John Coulthart; Mastered By — Oliver Peryman. The matrix runout on Side B reads “Stardelta” — an alias of Lewis Hopkin (who also mastered track A2 of Preparations). Genres: Electronic, Folk/World/Country; Styles: Aboriginal, Ambient, Experimental. The “Aboriginal” style tag and Rob Thorne’s New Zealand-based URL (robthorne.co.nz) suggest New Zealand indigenous musical elements may be present in this release, though this cannot be confirmed from Discogs alone.

2019 — Emergence Vol. 1

Emergence Vol. 1 (2019, Saplings Records, SCIONWOOD002) is a collaborative digital release with Renick Bell on the German label Saplings Records. FIS is credited for “Sounds [Sound Work, Sound Design Live Processing, Post Production].” 19 tracks; styles Ambient, Experimental, Sound Collage.
