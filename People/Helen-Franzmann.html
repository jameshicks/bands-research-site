Biography

Early Career and Redbreast (2006)

Franzmann’s earliest Discogs-documented activity is the 2006 self-titled album by Redbreast, a Brisbane ensemble whose other members were Angela Gadd (trombone), Ben Schiller (vocals, guitar), and Simon Pitchers (2) (drums, organ). The album was released independently (Not On Label) and was mixed and produced collectively by Redbreast, with tracks also produced by Matt Morphett and Naut Arse. Marty Brown (who would later appear on Mess Esque’s self-titled album) was the recording engineer on several tracks.

The same year Franzmann appeared as a session pianist and backing vocalist on Lady Juliana by Jacob S. Harris & The Morning Belles, produced by Jamie Trevaskis (who later engineered and mixed McKisko’s debut Glorio).

McKisko — Solo Work (2009–2019)

Franzmann has released three solo albums under the moniker McKisko:

Glorio (2009, El Nino El Nino, ENEN002, CD) — Debut album, recorded and mixed by Jamie Trevaskis, mastered by Matt Redlich. Predominantly self-written (all tracks credited to Franzmann except track 5, “Undertow”, co-written with Ben Schiller and Simon Pitchers — both Redbreast bandmates). Genres: Rock / Ethereal / Post Rock. Key session players: Ben Tolliday (cello), Simon Pitchers (2) (drums), Angela Gadd (trombone, credited as “Ange Gadd”), Pete Hunt (3) (trumpet), Adam Storey (2) (double bass), Jamie Trevaskis (guitar/noises on track 1). Franzmann played guitar, vocals, piano, melodica, drums, and glockenspiel across tracks.

Eximo (2013, El Nino El Nino, ENEN007, LP) — Second album, vinyl only (limited edition). Co-produced by Franzmann and Kurt Read, who also arranged, mixed, recorded, and played instruments throughout. Mastered by Joe Lambert. Guest vocals by Paddy Mann on track A3 “Turning Curving”. Genres: Folk / Ethereal / Post Rock. Pressed by Pirates Press.

Southerly (2019, Little Lake Records (2), MCKS001, LP) — Third album, limited edition vinyl. Recorded by Nick Huggins. Franzmann credited for all music. Photography by Marek Rygielski and Nick Huggins. Guest feature: Seagull (7) on track A4 “Leaning Out (feat. Seagull)“. Genre: Folk. No mastering credit listed in Discogs.

Mess Esque (2020–present)

In 2020 Franzmann co-founded Mess Esque with Mick Turner (guitarist of Dirty Three). The duo released two albums in 2021 and a third in 2025. See Mess Esque for full detail.
