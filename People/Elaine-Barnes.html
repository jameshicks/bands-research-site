Overview

US-American vocalist and keyboardist who was a member of The Velvet Monkeys and Citizen 23, rooted in the indie rock, psychedelic rock, and punk scenes of the early 1980s. Her catalogue clusters in the 1982–1989 window, with releases on small independent imprints including Big Circus, Fountain Of Youth Records, and Shimmy Disc. Her closest recurring collaborators include Don Fleming, Jay Spiegel, Don Zientara, and Charles Steck. A credit on Thick Syrup Records in 2011 suggests continued involvement in the same network more than two decades after her core 1980s output.
