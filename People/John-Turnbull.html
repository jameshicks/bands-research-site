From Discogs

Veteran guitarist born in Newcastle upon Tyne, England, on 27 August 1950, not to be confused with Johnny Turnbull from 23 Skidoo or Johnny Turnbull (2) from The Buzz (Scotland). John Turnbull has played in various bands, including Skip Bifferty, The Chosen Few, Arc, Loving Awareness, Glencoe, Nick Lowe, Dave Stewart and the Spiritual Cowboys, Eurythmics, Talk Talk, Londonbeat, Paul Young, Bob Geldof, World Party, Kaos Band and Ian Dury and the Blockheads. He has also played and sung on a number of film soundtracks, including Get Carter (1971).

Overview

John Turnbull is a veteran guitarist born in Newcastle upon Tyne on 27 August 1950 who built a long career in British rock and pop, passing through Skip Bifferty, The Chosen Few, Glencoe, and Arc before finding his most enduring home as a member of Ian Dury and the Blockheads. His 588 Discogs credits are dominated by the Blockheads axis — Micky Gallagher, Norman Watt-Roy, Davey Payne, Charley Charles, and Ian Dury each share more than 200 releases with him — while a parallel strand of session and live work took him through Dave Stewart and the Spiritual Cowboys, Eurythmics, Talk Talk, Paul Young, World Party, and Londonbeat. His catalogue appears across Stiff, Epic, RCA, and a cluster of reissue labels, and his earliest credits include the Get Carter (1971) soundtrack, pointing to a film and TV thread running alongside his band work.
