Ninety

Designer and illustrator. Discogs artist ID: 946271. Also credited as “9T”, “N. Ninety”, and “Ninety (9T)“. Based at Studio OneDrinPen (Discogs label ID: 1067211; web presence at cloud9t.com and onedrinpen.com). Active across UK hip-hop, reggae, and Bristol-connected soul/downbeat scenes from approximately 2000 to 2013. Ninety’s credits are exclusively visual — design, illustration, and artwork — with one notable exception: a choir performance credit on Overproof Sound System’s Pull It Up! (2012). The core working relationship is with Groove Corporation and the Overproof Sound System cluster around Different Drummer, but Ninety’s design work extended considerably wider, covering UK hip-hop (London Posse, Unsung Heroes), reggae compilations (Roots Records), and independent poetry/spoken-word imprints (Melankolic, Wordplay Records).

  • On It’s All Happening Now, Ninety shares the design credit with Lewis Parker himself, suggesting a collaborative or layout-support role.

Studio OneDrinPen

Ninety’s Discogs profile states “Works for [Studio OneDrinPen]” (Discogs label ID: 1067211), and the email address listed as a name variation — ninety@onedrinpen.com — confirms an active affiliation. The studio’s web presence at cloud9t.com and myspace.com/ninetynemo was active during the 2000s. Studio OneDrinPen does not appear to have its own label releases on Discogs; it functions as a design studio rather than a record label.

Scene Context

Ninety’s work clusters around two overlapping networks:

Bristol / Different Drummer orbit: The relationship with Groove Corporation (who produced and mixed Nothing To Proove and Pull It Up!) and Overproof Sound System places Ninety at the edge of the Bristol reggae-meets-downbeat scene centred on Different Drummer and later Collision: Cause Of Chapter 3.

UK hip-hop / spoken word: Credits for Unsung Heroes (75 Ark / Scenario Records), the London Posse best-of (Wordplay Records / later Tru Thoughts), Lewis Parker’s Melankolic releases, and Roger Robinson’s Illclectica (Altered Vibes) connect Ninety to the UK underground hip-hop and spoken-word scene of the early-to-mid 2000s.

Reggae compilations: Three credits on Roots Records (4) releases — The Lion Roars, U Brown’s Hit Sound, and In A Revival Dub — show a parallel strand of roots/reggae compilation work, likely through the same Bristol/UK reggae network.

Broadcite Music: Two credits for the Broadcite label (The Bush Man EP by Tempo (8) and Broadcite Unplugged Volume Three) suggest a working relationship with that imprint, which is connected to DJ/selector TRoy (9) and Afterculture.

Connections

  • Groove Corporation — primary design/illustration collaborator; credited on Remixes From The Elephant House (2002) and as producer on Nothing To Proove (2004) and Pull It Up! (2012)
  • Overproof Sound System — design on Nothing To Proove (2004); artwork and choir on Pull It Up! (2012)
  • Different Drummer — associated label; home of the Overproof Sound System
  • Guidance Recordings — released Remixes From The Elephant House (2002)
  • Collision: Cause Of Chapter 3 — released Pull It Up! (2012)
  • Roger Robinson — illustrated and designed Illclectica (2005, Altered Vibes)
  • Lewis Parker — shared design credit on two Melankolic releases (2002); Parker is both primary artist and co-designer on those releases
