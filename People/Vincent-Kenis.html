Jul 26, 20262 min read

Vincent Kenis

Discogs ID: 69856
Role: Producer, engineer, guitarist, bassist
Known for: Crammed Discs, Aksak Maboul, Congolese music production
Alias: Bwana Mpuku
Name variations: Kenis, V. Kenis, VK, Vincent Van Vogt, Vincent Kennis, Bwana Mpuku
Active: 1976–present

Co-founder of Aksak Maboul with Marc Hollander. Resident producer at Crammed Discs from its founding; described in the Discogs profile as “Cramworld resident producer” responsible for the Congolese/African music strand including Zap Mama, Taraf de Haïdouks, and the Congotronics series. Also a member of The Honeymoon Killers, Los Descontrolados, Mr. Big Mouse, and Sacher Musak.

Key Production Credits at Crammed

Post-punk / new wave

  • Recorded and produced Onze Danses Pour Combattre La Migraine (Aksak Maboul, 1977/Kamikaze; co-arranged)
  • Bass and produced The Honeymoon Killers debut Les Tueurs De La Lune De Miel (CRAM 013, 1981)
  • Produced and recorded Made To Measure Vol.1 (1984)
  • Guitar/bass/accordion and consultant on Zazou Bikaye Mr. Manager (1985)
  • Remix on John Lurie Down By Law (MTM 14, 1987)

World / African music

  • Producer, engineer, mixed: Zap Mama Adventures in Afropea 1 (CramWorld, 1991/1992) — breakthrough world music release
  • Recorded, mixed, produced: Taraf de Haïdouks Musique des Tziganes de Roumanie (1991) and subsequent albums through Maškaradă (2007)
  • Produced and recorded: Koçani Orkestar Alone At My Wedding (2002); The Ravished Bride (2008)
  • Producer, recorded, mixed: Konono Nº1 Congotronics (2004); Congotronics 2 (comp., 2005); Assume Crash Position (2010); co-produced Konono N°1 Meets Batida (2016, with Pedro Coquenão)
  • Producer, recorded, mixed: Staff Benda Bilili Très Très Fort (2009) and Bouger Le Monde! (2012)
  • Producer, recorded, mixed: Kasai Allstars In The 7th Moon… (2008); Beware The Fetish (2014)

Mastering (Ziriguiboom/Brazilian catalog, 2000s)

Mastered numerous Ziriguiboom releases including Bebel Gilberto, Zuco 103, Cibelle, and compilation releases.
