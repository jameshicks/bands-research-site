Monster Mike Welch

Born June 11, 1979. American electric blues guitarist, singer, and songwriter. Discogs artist ID: 1427822. Real name: Mike Welch.

Overview

Monster Mike Welch is a Boston-area electric blues guitarist who began recording professionally in his mid-teens. He released his debut album Axe To Grind on Tone-Cool Records in 1996 at age 16, followed immediately by These Blues Are Mine the same year on the same label. His third Tone-Cool album, Catch Me (1998), was produced by his backing band The Mike Welch Band and mixed by Ducky Carlisle. After a gap in solo output he returned with Just Like It Is (BGB Records, 2007), which he co-produced with engineer Matt Malikowski. A collaboration with Duke Robillard — Independently Blue (Stony Plain Records, 2013) — broadened his audience. Right Place, Right Time (Delta Groove Music, 2017) is a three-way collaboration with vocalist Mike Ledbetter and guitarist Laura Chavez. His most recent solo album is Nothing But Time (Gulf Coast Records, 2023), produced by Kid Andersen and featuring a large ensemble of California blues sidemen.

Beyond his solo work, Welch has appeared as a guest guitarist on a wide range of blues records across his career: with Sugar Ray & The Bluetones (Severn Records, 2003), on Cryin’ Hey! Monster Mike Welch Plays The Blues (DixieFrog, 2005), on his own band outing Adding Insight To Injury (95 North Records, 2004), and on releases by Victor Wainwright, Laura Chavez, and others. He also appeared on a Buddy Guy album (I’m A Bluesman, Virgin America, 2004) as rhythm guitarist.

Welch’s name variations in use across releases include “Monster” Mike Welch, M. Welch, M.M. Welch, and The Monster Mike Welch Band. He is a member of two Discogs-listed groups: The Mike Welch Band (Discogs artist ID: 4198642) and The Soul Band (Discogs artist ID: 13899388).

Early Releases: Mike & The Mellotones

In 1997, Welch appeared as a featured artist on the release Mike & The Mellotones (Blue Blood Records (2) / New Road Music), credited alongside the Dutch group Mike & The Mellotones (Discogs artist ID: 980345). This was a Dutch/Netherlands-market release and reflects early international touring activity. (20 pressings registered, the highest of any Welch-related release.)
