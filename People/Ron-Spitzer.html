Ron Spitzer

Drummer, bassist, vocalist, pianist. New York City (grew up in Buffalo, NY). Discogs artist ID: 450344.

Overview

Ron Spitzer was the backbone rhythm section player in Robert Poss’s orbit from the very start of Poss’s recording career through to the end of Band Of Susans in 1994. A multi-instrumentalist credited on Discogs as bass player, vocalist, and songwriter, he also played piano (on Poss’s 1986 solo single) and guitar (on the 1989 Shimmy Disc release Bobby). According to Wikipedia, Poss, Susan Stenger, and Spitzer grew up together in Buffalo, New York, which explains the core trio’s longevity — they were the three constants across every Band Of Susans lineup from 1986 through the final studio LP.

Spitzer played drums on every Band Of Susans studio album and EP from Blessing And Curse (1987) through Here Comes Success (1994). Before Band Of Susans he was bassist and lead vocalist in Poss’s earlier NYC groups: Tot Rocket And The Twins (1980–1981, punk rock) and Western Eyes (2) (1984). He also appears as a member of When People Were Shorter And Lived Near The Water, a Shimmy Disc–associated collective that included Kramer (2), Dave Rick, Steve Shelley, and others.
