Nicky Skopelitis

Discogs ID: 60086
Born: 2 February 1949
Origin: Greek / New York, USA
Alias: Ekstasis
Name variations: Nicki Skopelitis, Scopelitis, Skopelitis

Downtown NYC guitarist and multi-instrumentalist. Core figure in the Bill Laswell orbit across Material, Axiom Records, and dozens of sessions from 1981 to the present. Fluent in electric/acoustic guitar, baglama/saz, bouzouki, coral sitar, banjo, and Fairlight CMI programming — a toolkit that made him indispensable on records bridging jazz, hip-hop, dub, and world music.

Career

Early Work — Curlew and the Downtown Scene (1981–1983)

Skopelitis appeared on the debut LP by Curlew (1981, Landslide), the downtown jazz/improv group led by George Cartwright with Tom Cora and Bill Laswell on bass. The record placed him in the same post-No Wave improvisational space as Fred Frith, John Zorn, and the Celluloid Records cluster.

He then joined The Golden Palominos — Anton Fier’s rotating collective — for their self-titled Celluloid debut (1983), playing guitar alongside Laswell, John Zorn, Arto Lindsay, and Michael Beinhorn.

Celluloid / Material / Axiom Era (1984–1994)

Skopelitis became a permanent satellite of the Material axis during its Celluloid years. Credits include:

  • Sound-System (1984, CBS) — guitar; Afrika Bambaataa / Material collaboration
  • World Destruction (1984, Celluloid) — guitar; Time Zone (Bambaataa + John Lydon)
  • Visions Of Excess (1985, Celluloid) — writing credits; Golden Palominos
  • Electric Africa (1985, Celluloid) — guitar; Mandingo
  • Rhythm Killers (1987, 4th & Broadway/Island) — guitar and Fairlight programming; Sly Dunbar and Robbie Shakespeare production showcase
  • Seven Souls (1989, Virgin) — guitar, baglama/saz, coral sitar, Fairlight; Material collaboration with William S. Burroughs
  • Middle Passage (1990, Axiom Records) — guitar, sitar, banjo, Fairlight; Material
  • Hallucination Engine (1994, Axiom/Island/Mango) — 6-str and 12-str acoustic/electric guitar, coral sitar, baglama, Fairlight; Material

He also appeared on two Swans records in this period: The Burning World (1989, UNI; baglama, bouzouki) and White Light From The Mouth Of Infinity (1991, Young God; acoustic/electric guitar, baglama, bouzouki, banjo).
