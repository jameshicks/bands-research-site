Brendan Kelly

Bassist and vocalist. Discogs artist ID: 388653. Name variation: B. Kelly.

Overview

Brendan Kelly is a bassist and vocalist based in Northern Ireland. He joined That Petrol Emotion as the band’s final bassist, replacing John Marchini, and appeared on their last studio album Fireproof (1993) on Koogat. Discogs credits for Chemicrazy (1990) list Marchini — not Kelly — as the bassist, placing Kelly’s entry into the band sometime between the Chemicrazy sessions (1990) and the recording of Fireproof.

After That Petrol Emotion disbanded, Kelly appeared as vocalist on a 1993 self-released single by The Id (15) and was a member of the informal Derry project Wavewalkers alongside Damian O’Neill and Réamann Ó Gormain — no Wavewalkers releases have Discogs entries. In 2018 he was part of Damian O’Neill And The Monotones (alongside O’Neill, Sean O’Hagan, and Dave Hattee), who issued Refit Revise Reprise on Dimple Discs.

His most sustained post-TPE work is The Everlasting Yeah, which he co-founded in 2013 with Damian O’Neill, Réamann Ó Gormain, and Ciaran McLaughlin — the same core four who had played together in That Petrol Emotion’s final lineup. The Everlasting Yeah released Anima Rising (2014) and Staying Cool Staying Free With The Everlasting Yeah (2024), both on the Infinite Thrill label.
