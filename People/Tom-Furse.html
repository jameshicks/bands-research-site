Overview

Tom Furse (Thomas Furse Cowan) is a London-based musician and producer. He is best known as the keyboardist of The Horrors, the UK post-punk/psychedelic band he has been part of since their debut in 2006. Across The Horrors’ discography he has developed from a straight-up bass player on the early punk-garage records into a core synthesizer and keyboard voice on the band’s more expansive later work.

Outside The Horrors, Furse records solo under his own name and maintains a parallel production identity as Tom Cowan (2), under which he has produced for other bands (notably S.C.U.M), and is a founding member of Innerspace Orchestra alongside Cathy Lucas and Rose Elinor Dougall. He was also a member of Spider And The Flies, a side project with Horrors bassist Rhys Webb.

Across his career Furse has accumulated an eclectic set of credits — solo ambient/electronic releases, a commissioned soundtrack, a label compilation, a remix, a co-production for a Spanish garage band — suggesting a genuinely broad musical range beyond his day job as keyboardist in a rock band.

The Horrors

Discogs artist ID: 517333. Active 2006–present (as of 2025). Labels: Loog (early EPs and debut), XL Recordings (2009–2014), Wolf Tone (2017–2021), Fiction Records (2025).

Furse is credited on The Horrors’ releases from the first singles forward. On the early Loog-era records (2006–2007) he is credited as Bass [Danelectro Longhorn] — the band’s lineup was fluid in the early days. By Skying (2011) and onward he is the keyboards credit. On V (2017) he is credited with Synthesizer, Keyboards, Drum Programming.

S.C.U.M (Discogs ID: 1273916) were a Loog-signed London post-punk band. Furse producing their 2008 debut single under the Tom Cowan (2) alias is a direct link between the early Horrors circle (all on Loog at the time) and a peer act.

Connan Mockasin — Furse played synthesizer on Side B of Faking Jazz Together (Because Music / Phantasy Sound, 2011), an album that also passed through Erol Alkan for mixing.

The Parrots (5) — Madrid garage-rock band. Furse produced their second album Dos (Heavenly, 2021), taking a hands-on role (also played synth, piano, backing vocals).
