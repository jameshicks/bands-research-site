Overview

Thomas Worthmann is a producer and coordinator with 251 credits spanning 2004–2024, working almost exclusively in the reissue and compilation sphere for the Krautrock and experimental electronic canon. He operates closely with Gunther Buskies under the Bureau Buskies name and has been central to Bureau B’s output — the Hamburg label devoted to reissuing the German electronic underground — accounting for 88 works there alone. His collaborator list is essentially a roll-call of the scene he has spent two decades archiving: Conny Plank, Hans-Joachim Roedelius, Dieter Moebius, Cluster, Conrad Schnitzler, Michael Rother, Harmonia, Asmus Tietchens, and Mani Neumeier.
