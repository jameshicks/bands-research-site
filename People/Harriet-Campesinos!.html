Overview

Harriet Coleman (credited as Harriet Campesinos!) is a UK-based violinist and keyboard player, a core member of Los Campesinos! across the full span of the band’s recorded output. Her credits run from 2008 through the early 2020s, concentrated in the late 2000s with continued appearances into the following decades. Wichita and Arts & Crafts served as her primary release labels, and her credits align closely with bandmates Gareth Campesinos! and Tom Campesinos!, as well as producers John Goodmanson and Guy Davie.
