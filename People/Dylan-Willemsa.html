Overview

Dylan Willemsa is a viola and violin player whose recording career ran primarily through the 2000s, working in folk rock, indie rock, and blues-influenced contexts on labels including Touch And Go, Matador, and FatCat Records. The catalogue clusters strongly around Cat Power’s recording ensemble: Willemsa shares credits with Chan Marshall, Gregg Foreman, Judah Bauer, Mabon “Teenie” Hodges, Spooner Oldham, and Stuart Sikes across around twenty releases each, placing them inside the loose collective that backed Marshall through her mid-2000s period. Drummer Jim White carries the single largest overlap at 29 shared credits, reinforcing the Cat Power axis. Outside that circle, Nina Nastasia is a notable recurring collaborator with 14 shared releases. Willemsa is also a member of Dimestore Dance Band and The Jump Arts Orchestra, the latter documented on Jump Arts Records.
