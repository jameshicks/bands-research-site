Overview

David Odlum is a Dublin guitarist, producer, and engineer who started out busking with his brother Karl before joining Kíla. He and fellow Kíla alumnus Colm Mac Con Iomaire co-founded The Frames alongside Glen Hansard, the band that formed the spine of his early career. After departing The Frames, he moved into production and engineering work for other artists, with Gemma Hayes and Nina Hynes among the notable names. His 570-plus credits run from 1991 onward across acoustic, folk rock, and indie rock material, with his heaviest label presence on Anti- and Plateau Records, and his closest ongoing studio relationships with Hansard and Peter Deimel.
