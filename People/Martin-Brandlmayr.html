Martin Brandlmayr

Austrian composer, percussionist, and visual artist, born 1971 in Bad Ischl. Based in Vienna. Discogs artist ID: 224705. Works at the frontier between improvisation and composition, acoustic and electronic music.

Website: martinbrandlmayr.com | Thrill Jockey page: thrilljockey.com/artists/martin-brandlmayr

Name variations on record: Brandlmayr, Martyn Brandlmayr, Mats Brandlmayr.

Overview

Brandlmayr occupies a central position in the Viennese electroacoustic and free-improvisation scene. He is a core member of Radian, Polwechsel, Trapist, and Autistic Daughters, plus smaller formations including Kapital Band 1 and Till The Old World’s Blown Up And A New One Is Created. Across those contexts his instrument palette runs from drums and vibraphone (including marimbaphone) to electronics, synthesizer, computer processing, and transducer — reflecting the scene’s characteristic blurring of acoustic percussion and live electronics.

He has composed music for dance, opera, film, video, and sound installation, and received the SWR Karl Sczuka Prize for radio play twice: Vive les fantômes (2018) and Interstitial Spaces (2023).

Groups / Projects

Radian (Discogs ID: 36006)

Core trio with Martin Siewert (guitar, electronics) and Stefan Németh (synthesizer, computer), plus bassist John Norman. Active from the late 1990s. Seven albums, primarily on Thrill Jockey. Brandlmayr plays drums, vibraphone, and electronics; on later records he also takes producing, recording, and mixing credits. John McEntire (Tortoise) recorded and mixed the early albums.

Polwechsel (Discogs ID: 65735)

Core member. Current lineup also includes Burkhard Beins (percussion), Werner Dafeldecker (double bass), Burkhard Stangl (guitar), Michael Moser (cello), John Butcher (saxophone), and Radu Malfatti (trombone). Brandlmayr composed “Chains And Grain” for the 2023 album of that name (Ni-Vu-Ni-Connu, release ID 36669658) and co-mixed the record.

Trapist (Discogs ID: 226312)

Trio with Martin Siewert and bassist Joe Williamson. Two albums: Ballroom (2004, Thrill Jockey) and The Golden Years (2012, Staubgold). Brandlmayr plays drums, percussion, vibraphone, synthesizer, and piano; co-wrote and co-mixed both records.

Autistic Daughters (Discogs ID: 236657)

Trio with Dean Roberts (guitar, vocals) and Werner Dafeldecker (bass). Two albums on Kranky and Staubgold. Brandlmayr co-produced and engineered Jealousy And Diamond (2004, Kranky) alongside Roberts, Dafeldecker, and Valerio Tricoli. Chris Abrahams (The Necks) contributed compositions to Uneasy Flowers (2008, Staubgold).

Till The Old World’s Blown Up And A New One Is Created (Discogs ID: 1244950)

Trio with Werner Dafeldecker and Fennesz. One self-titled release (2008, Mosz, mosz 016). Brandlmayr plays drums, percussion, vibraphone, and computer; co-handled additional recordings.

Kapital Band 1 (Discogs ID: 255819)

Duo with Nicholas Bussmann. Active as of the early 2010s.

El Infierno Musical (Discogs ID: 2477420)

Larger ensemble led by Christof Kurzmann. One album (2011, Mikroton Recordings). Also features Ken Vandermark (saxophone/clarinet), Clayton Thomas (bass), and Eva Reiter (viol, recorder, voice).

Mapstation

Session drummer on two Mapstation albums: Version Train (2003, Staubgold) and Distance Told Me Things To Be Said (2006, ~scape). Mapstation is Stefan Schneider of To Rococo Rot. Brandlmayr co-wrote several tracks on Version Train.

Velvet Lounge (Discogs ID: 2201859)

Listed as member. No releases in the Discogs database under this project.

the klingt.collective (Discogs ID: 16472025)

Listed as member. No releases in the Discogs database.

Fake The Facts + 2 (Discogs ID: 6142102)

Listed as member. No releases in the Discogs database.
