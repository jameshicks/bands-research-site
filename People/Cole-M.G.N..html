Cole M.G.N.

Discogs ID: 1271318 Real name: Cole Marsden Greif-Neill Born: March 7, 1985, Los Angeles, California Role: Producer, mixer, engineer, songwriter, musician Active: ~2007–present Management: Red Light Management

Producer and engineer on Washed Out’s Mister Mellow (Stones Throw, 2017) — handled production, mixing, and recording for the entire visual album. 4 shared Discogs credits with Washed Out. The Mister Mellow credit is a notable shift away from the Ben H. Allen production model of prior Washed Out records.

Background

Greif-Neill graduated from NYU’s Tisch School of the Arts (inaugural class of the Clive Davis Department of Recorded Music) in 2007, then relocated to Los Angeles. Wikipedia, as of 2026-07-12 He became embedded in the LA indie scene through work at The Smell, a DIY venue where he met artists including Julia Holter around 2008 (roughly ten years before a 2018 interview). gorilla vs. bear, 2018

He was a member of Ariel Pink’s Haunted Graffiti, contributing guitar during sessions for Before Today (October 2009–February 2010). Wikipedia He also leads a band called The Samps. gorilla vs. bear

As of 2021, he holds six Grammy Awards, all for his work with Beck. Red Light Management

Selected Credits

Notable production/mixing/engineering work beyond Washed Out: Julia Holter (Ekstasis 2012, Loud City Song 2013, Have You In My Wilderness 2015, Aviary); Nite Jewel; Real Estate; Ariel Pink; Beck; Christine and the Queens; Blood Orange; DāM-FunK; Thurston Moore; NxWorries; Charlotte Gainsbourg; The Vaccines; Carly Rae Jepsen; The Lonely Island; Stephen Malkmus and the Jicks; Dwight Yoakam; Bat for Lashes; Jenny Lewis; Laurel Halo; Dawn Richard; Snoop Dogg. Wikipedia

Notes (web) — Greene/Cole M.G.N. Collaboration

Multiple 2017 press sources describe Cole M.G.N. as the sole outside collaborator on Mister Mellow, variously called “mix engineer” (Stones Throw press release) and “co-producer” (Wikipedia infobox). The album was conceived over approximately two years. Stones Throw Records announcement

How the collaboration formed is not documented in any publicly available source. Interviews with Ernest Greene from the album cycle (AU Review, The Bakery Atlanta, Line of Best Fit, MusicRadar) focus on his burnout recovery, the sample-heavy aesthetic, and the Stones Throw signing — but none mention how he and Cole M.G.N. connected. The Stones Throw signing itself came via Greene’s manager, who played in an amateur basketball league with Stones Throw’s general manager; Peanut Butter Wolf and the Stones Throw team reviewed the completed work before signing. The Bakery Atlanta This suggests the album — including the Cole M.G.N. collaboration — may have been substantially complete before the Stones Throw deal, but that is an inference, not a stated fact.

Cole M.G.N.’s prior credits with LA-based indie artists in the Stones Throw orbit (DāM-FunK, Nite Jewel, Blood Orange) make a label or scene referral plausible, but this is circumstantial.
