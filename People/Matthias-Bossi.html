Matthias Bossi

Matthias Bossi

Aug 07, 202610 min read

Matthias Bossi

Discogs ID: 735952
Born: 1979, Boston, USA
Based: Bay Area, California (later Falmouth, Massachusetts)
Name variations: MB, Mb, mb, Matthias, Matthias “Maushops” Bossi

American drummer, percussionist, vocalist, and multi-instrumentalist. Long-term musical partner of Carla Kihlstedt; together they are co-founders of Sleepytime Gorilla Museum, The Book Of Knots, and the duo Rabbit Rabbit (3). Also a member of Cosa Brava (Fred Frith’s ensemble) and the video-game soundtrack duo Ridiculon. Earlier member of Skeleton Key (Erik Sanko’s NYC project).

Career

Early Work: Skeleton Key (mid-1990s–early 2000s)

Before his move to the Bay Area, Bossi was a member of Skeleton Key, the New York band fronted by bassist/singer Erik Sanko. Skeleton Key operated from 1995 onwards, releasing material on Motel Records, Capitol Records, and later Ipecac Recordings. The band built its sound around antique microphones, primitive guitars, and unconventional percussion — an approach that would prove formative for Bossi’s later aesthetic. Discogs lists him as a member alongside Erik Sanko, Chris Maxwell, and others through their active period into the early 2000s.

Sleepytime Gorilla Museum (1999–2010, reunion 2024)

Sleepytime Gorilla Museum was formed in 1999 in Oakland, California. The core lineup assembled around Bossi, Carla Kihlstedt, Nils Frykdahl, and Dan Rathbun, with additional members including Michael Mellender, David Shamrock, Frank Grau, and Moe! Staiano. The band operated in avant-garde rock territory, employing hand-built instruments, extreme dynamics, and theatrical presentation — absorbing influences from death metal, chamber music, free improvisation, and industrial noise without settling into any of them.

SGM released on Chaosophy/Seeland (Grand Opening And Closing, 2001), Sickroom Records (Live, 2003), and Web Of Mimicry (Of Natural History, 2004) — Trey Spruance’s Bay Area avant imprint. The 2007 album In Glorious Times appeared on The End Records. Bossi played drums, piano, glockenspiel, xylophone, and vocals throughout the catalog, contributing to SGM’s signature practice of building custom instruments and treating the drum kit as one component of a larger percussive system rather than its center.

A reunion recording, Of The Last Human Being, appeared on Avant Night in 2024 — over a decade after the band had gone quiet.

The Book Of Knots (2003–2011)

Bossi co-founded The Book Of Knots in 2003 alongside Joel Hamilton and Tony Maimone, with Carla Kihlstedt as the fourth core member. Unlike SGM — a live band with theatrical ambitions — The Book Of Knots was conceived from the start as a studio collective based in New York City. The project revolved around assembling guest vocalists and performers around a dense, multi-layered instrumental core.

The self-titled debut (Arclight Records, 2004) saw Bossi credited with a remarkable range of instruments: Rhodes piano, drums, vocals, Gregorian chant choir, May Bell guitar, whistling, pipe organ, snare, melodica, distorted drums, vibraphone, backing vocals, EPIRB vocal, cymbal rope, dumbek, and flute. This promiscuous multi-instrumentalism is a consistent thread across his work.

Traineater (Anti-, 2007) brought in guest vocalists including Tom Waits, Carla Bozulich, David Thomas (2), Mike Watt, and Jon Langford (2); the band’s core four played drums, piano, bass, guitar, violin, synthesizer, and harmonium. Garden Of Fainting Stars (Ipecac Recordings, 2011) was the final Book Of Knots release. Bossi is credited as producer alongside Kihlstedt, Maimone, and Hamilton; Joel Hamilton also engineered and mixed.

Cosa Brava (2009–2012, archival 2024)

Bossi is a member of Cosa Brava, the avant ensemble led by Fred Frith alongside Kihlstedt, Zeena Parkins, and Shahzad Ismaily. The group recorded two albums for the Swiss label Intakt Records — Ragged Atlas (2010) and The Letter (2012) — and an archival release, Z Sides (Klanggalerie, 2024). Bossi’s credit on The Letter lists “drums, percussion, voice, electronics [Mayhem], whistling.” The “Mayhem” label for electronics is characteristic of his tendency to name his contributions descriptively rather than technically.

See Cosa Brava for full personnel and tracklist detail.

Carla Kihlstedt: Tales For The Stage (2011–2012)

Bossi co-authored and performed on two installments of Kihlstedt’s theatrical Tales For The Stage series, released self-sufficiently under their joint imprint:

  • Still You Lay Dreaming — Tales For The Stage, II (2011): Bossi co-credited as primary artist alongside Kihlstedt. His instrument list here is among the most expansive in his discography: vocals, drums, piano, organ, field organ, tambourine, laundry sneaker, bathtub, windowsill (as percussion), legislative interjection (voice), under-age choir, harmonica, bass harmonica, music boxes, melodica, surdo, whistling. Also credited with words and music, and as recorded by. Mixed by Joel Hamilton; mastered by Dan Rathbun.
  • Niagara Falling — Tales For The Stage, III (2012): Again co-credited as primary artist.

Rabbit Rabbit Radio / Rabbit Rabbit (3) (2013–present)

The most commercially accessible project in Bossi’s catalog, Rabbit Rabbit Radio is the duo he and Kihlstedt formed in Falmouth, Massachusetts. The project began as a monthly subscription music release — a song-a-month model — before the material was collected into albums. Five collections have appeared to date on Rabbit Rabbit Records and Klanggalerie.

The duo’s Discogs profile notes they are also collectively founding members of Tin Hat (Tin Hat Trio), 2 Foot Yard, and Causing a Tiger — projects not individually credited to Bossi in the DB but confirming the continuity of his working relationship with Kihlstedt across over two decades.

Ridiculon (2010s–2021)

Bossi formed Ridiculon with Jon Evans (2) as a video-game soundtrack project, releasing material on Yetee Records. The Super Meat Boy Forever soundtrack (2021) credits Bossi with drums, programmed by, synth, and voice; Carla Kihlstedt appears on violin on two tracks. The project represents a significant genre departure from the rest of his work — game audio with electronics and pop-adjacent structures.

Session and Collaborative Work

Beyond his main projects, Bossi has appeared as a session drummer and collaborator on a range of recordings:

  • Book Of Silk (Ropeadope/Rykodisc, 2004): Percussion credit
  • Tanya Donelly And The Parkington Sisters (American Laundromat Records, 2020): Drums, percussion — crossing into indie/folk rock territory
  • Tom Waits — Orphans (Brawlers, Bawlers & Bastards) (Anti-, 2009): Credited as “Bonus Musicians,” drums and finger cymbals
  • St. Vincent — Actor (4AD, 2009): Additional drums
  • Conor Oberst — Romanian Names (Dead Oceans, 2009): Drums, percussion, keyboards
  • John Vanderslice — Green Grow The Rushes (self-released, 2011): Drums, piano, Hammond B3, surdo
  • Garden Of Fainting Stars (Ipecac, 2011): Producer credit (for The Book Of Knots)
  • Secrets Of Secrets (Tzadik, 2012): Drums
  • Tanya Donelly (2020): Drums, percussion
  • Grace Morrison — Daughter (self-released, 2021): Drums
  • Surplus Industries releases (2022, 2023): Backing vocals; vocals — Surplus Industries being the label of John Vanderslice

He also produced Last Show On Earth (2009), a self-released recording.
