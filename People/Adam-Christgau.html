Overview

Adam Christgau is a drummer and percussionist whose Discogs credits stretch back to 2005, working primarily in indie rock, folk, and acoustic settings. He is a member of Fire Flies and has built a dense web of collaborations, counting Saul Simon-MacWilliams, Kenny Warren, Dan Romer, Meredith Godreau, and Blake Mills among his most frequent partners. His 52 credits span a range of independent labels including Absolutely Kosher Records, Mind Over Matter Records, and Graveface Records.
