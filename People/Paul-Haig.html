Paul Haig

Scottish songwriter, musician, and singer. Born 1960, Edinburgh. Discogs artist ID: 47952.

Overview

Paul Haig began his career as frontman and primary songwriter of Josef K, the Edinburgh post-punk group active 1979–1982 on Postcard Records. After Josef K dissolved in 1982, Haig launched a solo career that moved steadily toward electronic and synth-pop territory — signing to Les Disques du Crépuscule and its UK sublabel Operation Twilight, then to Island Records for wider distribution. His debut LP Rhythm Of Life (1983) was recorded in New York with a cast that included Tom Bailey (of Thompson Twins), Bernie Worrell, and Anton Fier, produced by Alex Sadkin. Subsequent solo albums, most released through Crépuscule, deepened the electronic production with Alan Rankine (formerly of The Associates) as his main collaborator. The mid-1980s LP The Warp Of Pure Fun (1985, FünfUndVierzig) is particularly noted for its contributions from Bernard Sumner (New Order), Martin Rev (Suicide), and Alan Vega. A late-career collaborative album with Billy MacKenzie (Memory Palace, 1999) was released posthumously (MacKenzie died in 1997); Haig had written and produced all tracks. He continued releasing music into the 2010s on his own Rhythm Of Life Records imprint and via Crépuscule’s revival.

The existing stub misidentified a key collaborator as “Carlo Moroder” — no Giorgio Moroder credit appears anywhere in the Discogs record; this may be a web-sourced claim requiring separate verification.

Connections

  • Josef K — his primary band; core member 1979–1982
  • Postcard Records — Josef K’s label (Haig: written-by on early releases)
  • Les Disques du Crépuscule — primary solo label; anchored his entire 1980s output
  • Operation Twilight — Crépuscule UK sublabel; released “Justice” (1982); run by Philip Hoare
  • FünfUndVierzig — released The Warp Of Pure Fun (1985); label also housed Dunkelziffer, John Cale, Anna Domino
  • LTM Recordings — issued Josef K archival material (Haig written-by); LTM has biography page at ltmrecordings.com/phaigbio.html
  • Alan Rankine — The Associates guitarist; became Haig’s chief collaborator 1984–1989
  • Bernard Sumner — New Order; guitar on The Warp Of Pure Fun (credited as himself, not as Be Music)
  • Billy MacKenzie — The Associates singer; posthumous collaboration Memory Palace (1999)
  • Blaine L. Reininger — Tuxedomoon; violin on The Warp Of Pure Fun; shared Crépuscule orbit
  • Alex Sadkin — producer of Rhythm Of Life LP
  • Josef K members: Malcolm Ross (guitar), David Weddell (bass), Ronnie Torrance (drums)
  • The Durutti Column — shared Crépuscule / Factory Benelux connections; split single 1985
  • Propaganda — Haig has a Written-By credit on the US pressing of A Secret Wish (Island, 1985)
