Overview

Aron Arnarsson is a recording engineer based at Studio Sýrland in Iceland, credited on over 150 releases since 2002. He is best known as the principal engineer for Of Monsters And Men, sharing more than 30 credits with the band alongside frequent collaborators Ari Bragi Kárason and Greg Calbi. His work spans folk rock, indie rock, and synth-pop, appearing on releases through Bella Union, Island Records, Universal Republic, and Partisan Records internationally, as well as the Icelandic labels Smekkleysa and 12 Tónar domestically.
