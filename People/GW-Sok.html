GW Sok

Discogs ID: 254023 | Jos Kley alias ID: 1948667 Real name: Jos Kleij (also spelled Jos Kley) Born: 1957, Netherlands Aliases: IJsbrand Koudvuur; Jos Kley Name variations: G.W. Sok, G W Sok, G.W.Sok, Sok, S, G.W. Sok Of The Ex

Dutch vocalist, writer, and graphic designer. Founding member and primary vocalist of The Ex from 1979 until 2009 — a 30-year tenure spanning the band’s entire anarcho-punk, post-punk, and global free improvisation periods. Post-2009, he has worked as a solo artist under the GW Sok name and as a member of several collaborative ensembles.

Overview

GW Sok (born Jos Kleij, 1957) was the voice that defined The Ex across their first three decades. He joined at the band’s founding in Amsterdam’s squatter-punk scene in 1979 and appeared on every major Ex album through Moa Anbessa (2006, with Getachew Mekuria) and the DVD/live release Building A Broken Mousetrap (2007). His voice and words — often explicitly political, rooted in anarchist and anti-war sentiment — became inseparable from the Ex’s identity across more than 20 studio and live records.

He departed The Ex in 2009 and was succeeded by Arnold de Boer. Since then he has operated as a solo artist, as a member of King Champion Sounds, Gran Kino, Ex Orkest, Surplus 1980, and The And (3), and as a guest lyricist and vocalist on releases across European improvised, post-punk, and experimental contexts. He is also credited as a graphic designer (artwork, layout) on multiple releases. The alias IJsbrand Koudvuur appears in two early 1983–1986 credits (contributions to Miljoenen Magen and Emma). The Discogs entry Jos Kley (ID: 1948667) is the same person — that profile captures his group memberships (King Champion Sounds, Gran Kino, Ex Orkest, Surplus 1980 Collectiv Ensembl With G.W. Sok, The And (3), OMA (11), Cannibales & Vahinés).

Group Memberships (post-Ex)

Key LPs: Different Drummer (2013), Songs For The Golden Hour (2014), To Awake In That Heaven Of Freedom (2016), For A Lark (2018), Between Two Worlds (2021).
  • Gran Kino (Discogs ID: 2644528) — French-Dutch collective; members: Jos Kley, Morgan Arnault, Robin Genetier, Sara De Sousa, Rémi Velotti, Jean-Charles Doublet. Key releases: 1989 (2011, Alt Musique), Un Mur Qui Tombe (2012, Du Goudron & Des Plumes).

  • Surplus 1980 / Surplus 1980 Collectiv Ensembl With G.W. Sok (Discogs ID: 2478701) — US experimental ensemble. Joint releases incl. Kremlin Gremlin (A Dull Fiddler) (2022, Surplus Industries).
  • The And (3) (Discogs ID: 5085600) — trio with Nicolas Lafourest and Forêt (3). Straw/Town Of Stone (2015, Tractor Notown).
