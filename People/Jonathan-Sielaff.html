Overview

Jonathan Sielaff is a woodwind player based in Portland, Oregon, specializing in clarinet, bass clarinet, and contra-alto clarinet, with credits stretching back to 2004 across experimental, ambient, drone, and folk contexts. He is best known as a member of Golden Retriever alongside Matt Carlson — his most frequent collaborator by far, with 17 shared releases — and also belongs to Dreamboat (4) and the PDX Ecstatic Singers. His label history ranges from Root Strata and Thrill Jockey in the 2010s to Beacon Sound and, notably, Deutsche Grammophon in the 2020s, the latter an unusual home for an artist of this profile. Output across 39 total credits has grown decade by decade, with the 2020s already his most productive period.
