Danas Mikailionis

Danas Mikailionis

May 07, 20265 min read

Danas Mikailionis

Discogs ID: 1981491

Lithuanian producer; the primary operator and executive-producer behind NoBusiness Records (Vilnius, Lithuania) from its founding in 2008 through at least 2025. One of the most prolific producers in contemporary free jazz documentation, with over 120 credited releases on Discogs — almost all on NoBusiness Records.

Overview

Mikailionis built NoBusiness Records into a major documentation label for improvised and free jazz, pressing vinyl editions of concert recordings and archival sessions by American, European, and Japanese improvisers. His recurring co-producer at the label is Valerij Anosov; the label’s house mastering engineer is Arūnas Zujus; its recurring designer is Oskaras Anosovas. Mikailionis also contributed photography on several early releases.

The label launched with a burst of releases in 2008 — including live recordings of Joe McPhee with Trio-X at Vilnius — and expanded rapidly through the 2010s, documenting American free jazz (Sun Ra Arkestra members, AACM figures, New York downtown scene) alongside European and Japanese improvisers.
