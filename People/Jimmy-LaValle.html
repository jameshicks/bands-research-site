Jimmy LaValle

Musician, producer, multi-instrumentalist. San Diego, California (later Los Angeles). Discogs ID: 360159. Operates under the alias The Album Leaf.

Background — San Diego Hardcore / Experimental Scene (1996–1999)

Before The Album Leaf, LaValle was embedded in the San Diego underground: drumming and playing guitar across a cluster of hardcore, post-hardcore, and experimental bands that shared personnel and released on labels like Gold Standard Laboratories, Three One G, and Goldenrod Records.

  • Guyver-One — earliest credit (1996, inchworm. label)
  • The Crimson Curse — appeared on Goldenrod (1997–98), Three One G (1999, 2001, 2012)
  • Swing Kids (2) — San Diego post-hardcore
  • GoGoGo Airheart — San Diego group
  • The Locust — Gold Standard Laboratories (1997 comp), one of the defining San Diego noise/hardcore outfits of the era
  • Tristeza — ambient/post-rock group; LaValle appeared on Spine And Sensory (Makoto, 1999) and Dream Signals In Full Circles (Tiger Style, 2000); he left Tristeza to focus full-time on The Album Leaf after touring with Sigur Rós

The Album Leaf (1999–present)

Founded 1999 as a solo studio project, gradually evolving into a full live band. See The Album Leaf for full discography, personnel, and label history.

Black Heart Procession connection

After leaving Tristeza in early 2003, LaValle joined The Black Heart Procession as their touring bassist. In a Tape Op interview (issue 41) he states: “I had just done a tour with Blackheart Procession [as the bassist] and I invited him to come to Iceland with me” — referring to Pall Jenkins, who then came to the In A Safe Place sessions and contributed guitar, saw, and vocals. The touring relationship with BHP deepened into studio work: LaValle plays on six of eleven tracks on The Spell (2006, Touch And Go) — piano, bass, organ, and reverb tank. The creative exchange ran both directions, with Jenkins returning as a recurring lyricist and vocalist across three Album Leaf albums.

Sigur Rós connection

After One Day I’ll Be On Time (2001), Sigur Rós discovered LaValle and took The Album Leaf on two US tours and a European tour. LaValle then left Tristeza and traveled to Iceland to record In A Safe Place (2004) at Sigur Rós’s Sundlaugin studio. Several Sigur Rós members played on the record: Kjartan Sveinsson (keyboards, bells, guitar, glockenspiel, accordion), Orri Páll Dýrason (drums), Gyða Valtýsdóttir (cello), Jón Þór Birgisson (vocals, acoustic guitar, sampler on “Over The Pond” and “Streamside”). Birgir Jón Birgisson (Sigur Rós’s engineer) mixed and co-produced.

Perils From The Sea (2013) — collaboration with Mark Kozelek

LaValle wrote all music; Mark Kozelek wrote lyrics and sang. Mixed by Aaron Prellwitz. Released on Caldo Verde Records (CV024). The closing track “Somehow The Wonder Of Life Prevails” features strings by Peter Broderick, Marcel Gemperli, and Vanessa Ruotolo.

Film scoring

  • The Endless (2018, Eastern Glow Recordings)
  • 3022 (2020, self-released)
  • Synchronic (2021, Milan)
  • Man Finds Tape (2025, self-released)
