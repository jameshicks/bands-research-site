Hunter Lea

Discogs ID: 2917347 Name variations: H. Lea, Lea Website: hunterleamusic.com

American record producer, songwriter, musician, and music curator. Grammy-nominated. Co-owner and lead producer/recording engineer at Uptone Recorders, Tacoma, WA (with Rick King and Wendell Ratliff). Member of The Low Humbles. Best known in archival/reissue work as the primary curatorial producer behind Light In The Attic’s extensive Lee Hazlewood and Nancy Sinatra reissue programs, spanning over a decade (2012–2025) and encompassing more than 20 releases.

Background

Hunter Lea is based in the Pacific Northwest (Tacoma, WA). His involvement in recording and production predates his curatorial work — an early credit (2005) shows him as bass guitarist, mixer, art director, guitarist, keyboardist, and co-producer on The Voltage Control EP (Not On Label), indicating an active career as a working musician. By 2008 he is credited across multiple performance and production roles on Mono In VCF (Stylomusic), which added string arrangements, backing vocals, and primary recording duties.

His entry into the archival world came through Light In The Attic Records, where he became the label’s principal researcher and liner-note writer for the Hazlewood catalog — a body of work that gave him both curatorial and fan-access knowledge of one of American pop’s more eccentric canons.

Light In The Attic Records Affiliation

Light In The Attic (label ID: 24105) is a Seattle-based reissue label founded by Matt Sullivan (4). It operates under the parent entity Light In The Attic Records & Distribution, LLC (label ID: 364585) and has spawned a number of archival sublabels, among them the Lee Hazlewood Archive Series (sublabel ID: 516841) and the Nancy Sinatra Archival Series (sublabel ID: 2051662).

Hunter Lea’s role at LITA is primarily as Compilation/Reissue Producer and Liner Notes writer. On every major Hazlewood/Sinatra LITA release from 2012 onward, he appears alongside co-producers Matt Sullivan (4) (label founder/executive producer) and, on later releases, Ryan Wilson (9). The label’s recurring mastering engineer across these projects is John Baldwin (5), and design duties have been handled by Henry H. Owings (early releases) and Darryl Norsen (2021 onward).

Lee Hazlewood Reissue Project (2012–2025)

Hunter Lea has been the central curatorial figure in the most comprehensive reissue program ever mounted for Lee Hazlewood’s catalog. The 2013 box set There’s A Dream I’ve Been Saving: Lee Hazlewood Industries 1966–1971 represents the apex of the project — a multi-LP/CD compilation drawing on the full LHI Records catalog, for which Lea served as box set producer alongside Matt Sullivan (4) and Patrick McCarthy.

The 2021 Start Walkin’ 1965–1976 is notable for Lea’s role as interviewer: he conducted the Q&A with Nancy Sinatra included in the package — reflecting the depth of his relationship with the subject matter.

Separately, in 2017, Lea contributed liner notes to Rose Colored Corner on the LHI Records label itself (not LITA), indicating direct engagement with the Hazlewood estate/trust beyond purely the reissue label context.

Other Curatorial Work

Beyond the Hazlewood/Sinatra canon, Lea’s Discogs credits show one non-LITA production credit:

  • 2024 — New Ghosts (Fat Wreck Chords): Engineer. This is the only documented credit outside the Light In The Attic orbit, suggesting his broader recording engineering practice continues alongside his archival work.

An early Discogs credit (2001) lists him as “Camera Utility” on a live DVD (Live Evolution, Sanctuary/Warner Vision Australia) — likely unrelated to his later curatorial career.

The Low Humbles

Hunter Lea is a member of The Low Humbles (Discogs artist ID: 4841784), alongside Andrew Sandoval (ID: 433429) and Jeff Noyes (ID: 3094126). No releases are currently documented under this group name in Discogs.
