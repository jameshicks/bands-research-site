Overview

John Gordon Lewis (born 14 March 1947, Southampton) is a British vocalist and multi-instrumentalist who began as a session pianist before joining Brett Marvin & The Thunderbolts, the group that also performed under the alias Terry Dactyl and the Dinosaurs. His recording career is densest across the 1970s and 1980s, working across Pop Rock, Synth-pop, and New Wave, with a “Holiday” tag pointing to at least one seasonal crossover. He maintained a long and productive relationship with Stiff Records, accumulating 18 releases with them between 1978 and 2007, and collaborated most extensively with Bob Andrews and label figure Dave Robinson across more than 30 shared credits apiece.
