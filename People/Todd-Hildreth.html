Todd Hildreth

Louisville, Kentucky organist, keyboardist, and accordionist. Discogs ID: 695423. Active at least 1994–2011. Also credited as “Tod Hildreth,” “Todd Hilddreth,” and “Hildreth” on various releases; name variation “Todd Hildreth Trio” also on Discogs.

Overview

Todd Hildreth is a Louisville-based keyboard player best known as the organist for King Kong (3) during the mid-to-late 1990s. He appears on the band’s two mid-period Drag City LPs — Me Hungry (1995) and Kingdom Of Kong (1997) — as well as the earlier 7” Hot Dog Days (1994). His Discogs credit trail extends beyond King Kong into a loose set of Louisville and cross-regional collaborations: the Java Men (2) trio with King Kong drummer Ray Rizzo and guitarist Craig Wagner; a self-released accordion trio project on his own groovytodd records imprint; the one-release project Squeeze-bot; and a 2011 guest appearance on a Bonnie “Prince” Billy release for Drag City. A 2000 credit on the Eugene Chadbourne album Young And Innocent Days (Swamp Room Records, Germany) shows him reaching outside the Louisville orbit, playing organ, piano, and accordion for the avant-garde guitarist’s ensemble.

The Discogs entry shows no realname, profile text, or URLs — all knowledge of Hildreth comes from his release credits.

Projects and Groups

King Kong (3) (c. 1994–1998+)

Louisville experimental rock collective led by Ethan Buckler. Hildreth joined around 1994 as organist, appearing on the Hot Dog Days 7” and the two subsequent LPs. The stable core lineup for those records was Buckler (guitar, vocals), Willy MacLean (bass), Ray Rizzo (drums), and Hildreth (organ), with Amy Greenwood on vocals. He is not credited on the post-1997 King Kong releases (The Big Bang 2002, Buncha Beans 2007).

Java Men (2) (c. 1995)

A trio with Ray Rizzo (drums, percussion) and Craig Wagner (guitar). Released one self-titled 7” in 1995. Rizzo was also the drummer in King Kong (3) during the Kingdom Of Kong era, making Java Men a simultaneous side project. No further releases on Discogs.

Todd Hildreth Accordion Trio (2005)

Self-released on his own groovytodd records label (Discogs label ID: 802318 — one release only). Discogs credits no other performers; the ensemble makeup is unknown from Discogs data alone.

Squeeze-bot (2008)

One-release self-titled project. No additional credits or personnel on Discogs.
