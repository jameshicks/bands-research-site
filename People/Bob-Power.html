Bob Power

American recording engineer and mixer. Discogs ID: 67370

Mixed portions of A Tribe Called Quest’s Beats, Rhymes and Life (Jive, 1996, tracks 3, 4, 6–8, 10–13, 15), working alongside The Ummah and Tony Smalios. A recurring engineer in the Native Tongues / New York hip-hop and R&B scene of the 1990s.
