Dan Koretzky

Co-founder of Drag City (Chicago, 1990). Discogs artist ID: 587939. Also credited as performer in Desert Storm (4). Active in Discogs credits 1993–2019.

Overview

Dan Koretzky co-founded Drag City with Dan Osborn in 1990 (Discogs profile says 1989; earliest catalog release DC1 is dated 1989). The label grew from a Chicago underground vehicle for early Pavement and Royal Trux singles into one of the defining American independent labels of the 1990s and 2000s — home to Smog, Silver Jews, Royal Trux, Joanna Newsom, Bonnie “Prince” Billy, and dozens more.

Koretzky’s Discogs credit history reflects his dual role as label operator and occasional participant. His production credits are sparse but notable: he co-produced Will Oldham’s Joya (1997) alongside Rian Murphy, and served as sole producer on John Mulaney’s Kid Gorgeous At Radio City (2018). He held executive producer credits on the Purple Mountains self-titled (2019), All My Happiness Is Gone (Wand, 2019), and Margaritas At The Mall (2019). His appearance as Tea Chest Bass and handclap player on Smog’s Knock Knock (2019 reissue) alongside Jim O’Rourke and Bill Callahan is one of his most visible performer credits. He also appears in the choir on King Kong (3)‘s Funny Farm (1993) and holds a “Technician [Special Ops]” credit on Royal Trux’s Accelerator (1998) — a tongue-in-cheek billing typical of the label’s approach.

Koretzky is also a member of Desert Storm (4), a Chicago indie rock group that released one 7” — Wake-Up Call (1995) — on Sea Note (SN-1), which is itself a Drag City sublabel. The other Desert Storm (4) members include Brendan Murphy (2), Gene Booth, and Rian Murphy — the last of these also the label’s primary in-house producer.
