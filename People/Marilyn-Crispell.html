Marilyn Crispell

Aug 26, 20262 min read

Marilyn Crispell

Discogs ID: 195813

American jazz pianist, born 1947 in Baltimore. One of the most significant pianists in post-Coltrane free jazz and improvised music. Associated with the AACM and the work of Anthony Braxton (was a long-standing member of his quartet). One release on Okka Disk (1996).
