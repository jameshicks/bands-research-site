Overview

Dez Cadena (born June 2, 1961 in Newark, New Jersey) is a guitarist and vocalist, and the son of jazz producer Ozzie Cadena. He is best known as a member of Black Flag, where his 258 Discogs credits cluster tightly around the band and the SST Records ecosystem — his top collaborators are Greg Ginn, Chuck Dukowski, Spot, and Robo. When Henry Rollins joined Black Flag in 1981, Cadena shifted from vocalist to rhythm guitarist, a role he continued across punk and hardcore releases throughout the decade. Beyond Black Flag his work spans D.C. 3, Bulimia Banquet, Duff McKagan’s Loaded, and a substantial run of credits alongside the Misfits circuit — 66 shared with Jerry Only — touching styles from hardcore and punk through doom metal and horror rock.
