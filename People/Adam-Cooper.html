Adam Cooper is a textural guitarist, songwriter, mix engineer and producer based in the Arizona desert. He is most widely known for his work within the Shoegaze and Dreampop genres with mixing and production credits on Alison’s Halo debut album, “Eyedazzler,” Astrobrite’s groundbreaking “Crush” and “8 Candy” EP, and more recently on Tribes Of The City’s soon to released fourth album, “Rust and Gold.” Some of his earliest production and mixing work involved a fledgling Jimmy Eat World who recorded their first demos with Cooper. His current musical project is an ambient guitar based project called Heavy Sunrise.

Overview

Adam Cooper is an Arizona-based guitarist, songwriter, mix engineer, and producer whose credits stretch back to 1994 across shoegaze, dreampop, ambient, and indie rock. He is best known for mixing Alison’s Halo’s debut album Eyedazzler and production work on Astrobrite’s Crush and the 8 Candy EP; he was also a member of Alison’s Halo alongside Scott Cortez, one of his most frequent collaborators. Early in his career he recorded the first demos for a then-nascent Jimmy Eat World before the band broke through. His current project, Heavy Sunrise, pursues an ambient guitar direction, and his label relationships include Independent Project Records and a range of small US imprints.
