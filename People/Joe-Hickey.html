Overview

Joe Hickey is a UK-based live sound engineer and studio technician whose Discogs catalogue is almost entirely built around The Wedding Present and their extended circle — he worked with the band from around 1989 to 1995, accumulating credits alongside David Gedge, Simon Smith, Keith Gregory, Peter Solowka, and Paul Dorrington. Alongside his engineering work he served as a picture editor for VOX magazine in the early 1990s. He left the music industry in 1998 and subsequently took a role with London Underground. His catalogue runs from 1988 into the 2010s, with the later credits reflecting reissue programmes on Edsel Records and Scopitones rather than new active work.
