Overview

Carlos De La Garza is a Los Angeles-based record producer, engineer, mixer, drummer, and multi-instrumentalist whose career spans three decades and two distinct phases. In the 1990s and early 2000s he was active as a drummer — performing with ska-punk outfits F.Y.P., Reel Big Fish (full member), and others in the Southern California punk circuit. By the mid-2000s he had shifted into the production and engineering seat, building a portfolio that now covers indie rock, alt-pop, pop-punk, and punk. His most sustained relationships are with Paramore (engineer/mixer on Paramore 2013 and After Laughter 2017; producer on This Is Why 2023), The Linda Lindas (producer on all their major releases from 2020 to 2024), Cherry Glazerr (producer on Apocalipstick 2017 and Stuffed & Ready 2019), and Bad Religion (producer/mixer on Age Of Unreason 2019). He co-produced Best Coast’s Always Tomorrow (Concord Records, 2020) with Justin Meldal-Johnsen. His most frequent studio collaborator is mastering engineer Dave Cooley (16 shared master releases). Mila De La Garza (drums) and Lucia De La Garza (guitar) of The Linda Lindas are his daughters.
