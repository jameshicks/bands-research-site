Overview

Eric Eble is a multi-instrumentalist — principally bassist — whose credits span alternative rock, garage rock, folk, and experimental noise from the mid-1990s through the early 2010s. He has been a member of The Heroine Sheiks, The Stinky Puffs, Ringolevio (2), Cordero (4), and the Reid Paley Trio, placing him inside overlapping circles of the New York underground. His most frequent collaborators include Sheenah Fair and Simon Fair Timony — siblings at the centre of The Stinky Puffs — alongside Swans guitarist Norman Westberg, with further shared credits with Cody Ranaldo. His recorded output is concentrated in the 2000s, with releases spread across Bloodshot Records, Rubric Records, and All Hands Electric.
