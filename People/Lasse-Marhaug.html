Lasse Marhaug

Nov 15, 20262 min read

Lasse Marhaug

Discogs artist ID: 44779
Aliases include: Crazy Steigen, Denver Dixon Jr., Egoproblem, Herb Mullin, Living Tissue (2), Otto von Kleist, Zonked Scum

Norwegian noise/experimental musician. Born 1974. Active from the early 1990s. Has collaborated widely in the international noise scene. Also a key producer and mixing engineer in the Nordic free jazz world.

Notable Credits

  • Member of Paal Nilssen-Love Large Unit
  • Mixed, mastered, and co-produced Medemer (2020, PNL) — Lean Left
  • Appears on Territory Band-4, -5, -6 with Ken Vandermark
  • Runs labels TWR Tapes, Pica Disk, Prisma Records

Connections

  • Paal Nilssen-Love — 68 shared releases; Large Unit; many duo/group sessions
  • Ken Vandermark — Territory Bands
  • Lean Left — mixed/mastered Medemer (2020)
  • Merzbow — 16 shared releases
