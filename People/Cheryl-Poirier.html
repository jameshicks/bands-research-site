Cheryl Poirier

Vocalist; core member of Kid Creole And The Coconuts and The Coconuts. Discogs artist ID: 376631. Name variations on record: Cheril Poirier, Cherly Poirier, Cheryl Lee Poirier, Poirier.

Overview

Cheryl Poirier was one of the principal Coconuts — the female vocal group at the centre of August Darnell’s Kid Creole And The Coconuts project — from at least 1980 through the 1990s. She appears on every major Kid Creole LP from Me No Pop I (1980) through Private Waters In The Great Divide (1990), with her most prominent billing on Tropical Gangsters (1982), where she is listed individually as “Featuring.” The Discogs profile for The Coconuts describes the core 1982 trio as Poirier, Adriana Kaegi, and Taryn Hagey, with Poirier taking the lead vocalist role on the standalone Coconuts album Don’t Take My Coconuts (1983, EMI America).

Beyond the Coconuts, Poirier co-founded Boomerang (4) — a side project with Kaegi and Perri Lister — releasing one album on Atlantic in 1986, on which she is credited for vocals, vocal arrangement, and artistic direction. She also has scattered session-vocalist appearances across French pop and Japanese releases in the late 1980s–mid 1990s, including credits on U2’s War (1983, backing vocals) and a handful of French and Japanese releases in the Côté/Ascot orbit.

Key Release Personnel Notes

Fresh Fruit In Foreign Places (1981) — produced by August Darnell; co-produced/directed by Andy Hernandez (Coati Mundi); engineered by Michael Frondelli; executive produced by Michael Zilkha. Cast included Adriana Kaegi, Carol Coleman, Coati Mundi, Lori Eastside, Steve Kroon, Yogi Horton, Dutch Robinson, Winston Grennan.

Tropical Gangsters (1982) — produced/directed by August Darnell; engineering by Bob Blank and Michael Frondelli; photography by George DuBose; string/horn arrangements by Darnell and Carlos Franzetti. Cheryl listed individually as “Featuring” alongside Adriana Kaegi and Carol Coleman (“Co-Starring”).

Doppelganger (1983) — produced by August Darnell; mixed by Michael H. Brauer; mastered by Greg Calbi. Cheryl credited as part of “The Boys In The Band.”

Don’t Take My Coconuts (1983) — standalone Coconuts album; produced by August Darnell; engineered by Michael Frondelli; mastered by Ted Jensen. Core Coconuts trio of Poirier, Kaegi, and Taryn Hagey.

Boomerang (1986) — produced by David Kershenbaum; arranged by Jeff Lorber; mastered by Bob Ludwig. Cheryl credited for vocals, vocal arrangement, and artistic direction alongside Kaegi; also featuring Perri Lister on vocals.

Private Waters In The Great Divide (1990) — produced by August Darnell; mixed by Steven Stanley. Cheryl credited on specific tracks (4, 6, 10).
