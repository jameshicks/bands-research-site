Ennio Morricone

Italian composer and conductor, born November 10, 1928, Rome; died July 6, 2020, Rome. Discogs artist ID: 15900

Received sole composer credit on the Teorema (1968) film soundtrack despite Ted Curson reportedly having written some of the music; one of the most prolific and celebrated film composers of the 20th century.
