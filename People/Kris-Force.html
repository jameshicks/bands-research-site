Kris Force

Born Kris T. Force. Composer, violinist, producer, and mastering engineer based in the San Francisco Bay Area. Discogs artist ID: 196312. Name variations on record: Cris Force, Force, Kris T Force, Kris T. Force. Alias: Frozeninamber.

Overview

Kris Force occupies an unusual position in the US underground: a classically informed string player equally at home as a noise-drone producer, a mastering engineer, and a visual artist. She founded Amber Asylum in San Francisco in 1990, and that project has been the primary vehicle for her compositional voice over more than three decades — from the early self-released cassettes through Relapse-associated releases to the 2025 Prophecy Productions album Ruby Red. Amber Asylum is listed on Discogs as an experimental neoclassical/darkwave/dark ambient project.

Beyond Amber Asylum, Force has maintained a sustained cross-scene presence as a session violinist/violist and as a producer-engineer. Her longest collaborative thread is with vocalist Jarboe: violin on Mahakali (2008), co-primary artist on The Path (2010, Paradigms Recordings), mastering on Jarboe & Helen Money (2015), and mix production plus mastering on The Cut Of The Warrior (2018). She was a core member of the all-female experimental collective AEAEA (with Jarboe, Annie Hogan, Meredith Yayanos, and Zoë Keating) and of the large ensemble project The Sweet Meat Love And Holy Cult.

Her session and production credits span Neurosis (Souls At Zero, 1992; A Sun That Never Sets, 2001), Swans (Soundtracks For The Blind, 1996), Neurosis & Jarboe (355 as Culper Ring co-producer, 2003), and Prophecy Productions acts well into the 2020s.
