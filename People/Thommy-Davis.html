Thommy Davis

Real name: Walter Thomas Davis Also known as: Thommy Gypsy, Thomas Davis, Tommy Davis Discogs ID: 45851 Location: Baltimore, Maryland Active: early 1980s–present

Baltimore house producer and DJ. Founding member of The Basement Boys collective. Co-founded Quantize Recordings (2011) with DJ Spen and Spen’s wife Kelly Tribble Spencer. Long-running production partner with Spen as DJ Spen & Thommy Davis (Discogs ID 423703).

Biography

Pre-House Career (1983–1987)

Thomas Davis’s earliest Discogs credit is a drum performance on the 1983 gospel LP Come Together (Myrrh Records) — likely under the Thommy Gypsy alias. From 1987 he appears as arranger and producer on Thommix Records compilations (Thomix Records Vol. 1).

R&B / Commercial Production (1988–1994)

Davis served as co-producer on a series of RCA-distributed releases through Popular Records in the late 1980s and early 1990s, including:

  • Black Rock And Ron (1988)
  • Stop The World (1989)
  • True Feelings (1989)
  • You Can’t Do Me None (1990)

He is also credited as written-by on Crystal Waters’ “100% Pure Love” (A&M, 1994) — one of the larger US house crossover hits of the decade.

The Basement Boys (late 1980s–early 2000s)

Davis was a founding member of The Basement Boys, the Baltimore production collective that also included DJ Spen, Teddy Douglas, Karizma, Maurice Fulton, Jay Steinhour, Kris Klayton, Neal Conway, Victor Williams, and Wayne Cooper. The collective ran Basement Boys Records, which became one of the defining American soulful/gospel house labels of the 1990s.

His Basement Boys credits include written-by work on their early major label output via WEA/Eternal (Blue Notes In The Basement, Europe, 1991).

Code Red Recordings Era (2005–2008)

Davis began releasing music under his own name during DJ Spen’s Code Red Recordings period, with writing and production credits on:

  • Holy Ghost (2005, CR12-506)
  • Nightrain (2005, CR12-510) — the first release as DJ Spen & Thommy Davis
  • Don’t Give Up (2006, CODE01) — written-by
  • Shine (2006, CODE07) — written-by; duo release

Quantize Recordings Era (2011–present)

Davis co-founded Quantize Recordings with Spen and Kelly Tribble Spencer in 2011, serving as executive producer across large swaths of the catalog. He has his own solo primary releases on the label and its unquantize sublabel:
