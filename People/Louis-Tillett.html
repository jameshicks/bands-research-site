Louis Tillett

Discogs ID: 285692. Real name: Louis Rohan Tillett. Born 13 March 1959, died 6 August 2023. Australian singer-songwriter, pianist, keyboardist, and saxophonist based in Sydney. Active 1984–2025 across post-punk, art-rock, and blues-influenced styles. Known equally as a solo artist on Citadel (7) and Normal and as a hired sideman on piano for key figures of the Sydney post-punk scene, most notably Laughing Clowns and Ed Kuepper.

Biography

Early Activity and Sydney Scene (1984–1986)

Tillett’s recorded presence begins in 1984. He appeared on piano for No Dance on their EP Carnival Of Souls (Hot Records (2), 1984) — a trio with Brett Myers (guitar) and Damien Lovelock (12-string guitar), produced and engineered by David Connor and Kent Steedman. In the same year he provided vocals and piano on the Laughing Clowns compilation From The Archives (Hot Records (2), 1984), which drew on earlier band recordings.

His highest-profile session credit of the period was on Laughing Clowns’ final LP Ghosts Of An Ideal Wife (Hot Records HOT 1013, 1985), replacing Chris Abrahams on piano. The album also featured Ed Kuepper (guitar, vocals, banjo, producer), Jeffrey Wegener (drums), Louise Elliott (saxophone), Diane Spence (saxophone), and Glad Reed (trombone). Engineer: Steve Crane.

Also in 1985 he appeared on piano on Ed Kuepper’s first solo LP Electrical Storm (Hot Records (2)), co-produced by Kuepper and Bruce Callaway. The following year he contributed piano to Kuepper’s Free Dirt (Citadel Records (5), New Zealand, 1986).

First Solo Work and The Aspersion Caste (1987–1990)

Tillett’s debut release as a primary artist was Ego Tripping At The Gates Of Hell (Citadel (7), Germany, 1987), a solo LP that established his relationship with the German imprint. Personnel details for this release are sparse in the DB.

The EP/single Condemned To Live / Midnight Witch (Blue Mosque / Festival Records, Australia & New Zealand, 1989) was credited jointly to Louis Tillett and The Aspersion Caste. He co-produced with Charlie Owen, and the session band was essentially a fixed ensemble: Owen on guitar, Penny Ikinger on guitar, Lenny Bastiaans on bass, Louis Burdett on drums, Jason Morphett on saxophones, Jackie Orszaczky (brass arrangements), James Greening on trombone, Miroslav Bukovsky on trumpet. This lineup carried over to the debut full album.

A Cast Of Aspersions (Citadel (7) / Normal, Germany, 1990) was his first full solo album with the Aspersion Caste lineup credited. Personnel: Owen (guitar), Ikinger (guitar), Bastiaans (bass), Burdett (drums), Mark Simmonds (tenor saxophone), Morphett (saxophone), Greening (trombone), Bukovsky (trumpet and flugelhorn), Mary-Ellen Stringer (vocals). Tillett sang, played piano, Hammond organ, and harmonica. The album also carried piano contributions from other sessions.

He also appeared on piano on The Turgid Miasma Of Existence (Rough Trade / Hot Records (2), 1986 US pressing) as a guest, and on the Dutch Megadisc compilation Roman Beach Party / The Turgid Miasma Of Existence (1989), both credited as additional personnel.

Sideman credit: piano on Wet Taxis release V For Vendetta (Ursula Records, Australia, 1989).

1990s: Sustained Solo Output

Letters To A Dream (Citadel (7) / Normal / Rough Trade Germany, Europe, 1992) retained a core of recurring players: Orszaczky (bass), De Silva (percussion — Sunil De Silva), Stringer (female vocals), Boyd (Peter Boyd, alto and soprano saxophone), Peet (Sarah Peet, cello), with Tillett again on vocals, piano, Hammond organ, and — an unusual credit — hacksaw, axe, and boltcutters. Produced and engineered by Barry Wolifson.

The Ugly Truth (Return To Sender, Germany, 1993) was a duo album with Charlie Owen, co-produced by Owen. Credits: Owen (guitar, percussion, producer), Tillett (piano, vocals, Hammond organ, percussion). Engineer: David McCluney.

Midnight Rain (Hitch-Hyke Records, Greece, 1995) was another Tillett / Charlie Owen co-credit: a spare two-hander recorded by and mixed by Tony Cohen. Tillett’s instrumental palette here was at its widest on record: vocals, piano, Hammond organ, synthesizer, soprano saxophone, harmonica, triangle.

Cry Against The Faith (Normal, Germany, 1998) was a further solo LP. Discogs carries no additional credits.

He also appeared on piano on Golden Days — When Giants Walked The Earth (Hot Records (2), 1995), the major Laughing Clowns retrospective compilation.

He also appeared on piano and as a band member (organ, backing vocals) on an undated release for Red Eye Records / Polydor (Australasia), listed in the DB as Monday Morning Coming Down….

2000s: The Hanged Man and Return To Sender

The Hanged Man (Normal, Europe, 2005) was a solo album with a small credited ensemble: Rachael Slattery, ‘O’ (2), James Gadski, Robin Gist, Daniel Phelan. Discogs roles for these contributors are not enumerated in the current DB entry.

He contributed piano to The Beasts Of Bourbon Box Set (Inertia Recordings, Australia & New Zealand, 2009) and appeared on the Beasts Of Bourbon-related compilation ecosystem in this period.

Later Work and Death (2010s–2023)

To Ride A Dead Pony (Louis Tillett Music, 2018) was a self-released compilation drawing on career recordings. Further digital pressings appeared in 2019 on Not On Label.

Tillett died on 6 August 2023. A posthumous compilation, The Burning Of Rome: Selected Works (Wild Honey Records / Folc Records, 2025), appeared across multiple territory pressings as a career retrospective.
