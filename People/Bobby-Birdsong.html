Bobby Birdsong

Discogs ID: 515550 Active: 1987–2003 (from Discogs credits) Origin: Austin, Texas Roles: Lap steel guitar, pedal steel guitar Name variation: Bobby Lee Birdsong

American steel guitar player active in the Austin, Texas music scene from the mid-1980s through the early 2000s. He played both lap steel and pedal steel, appearing on records spanning Austin country-punk (Hickoids), downtown DC avant-punk (No Trend), Austin roots-country (Jean Caffeine’s All-Nite Truckstop), and an early-2000s indie/punk reissue. A Discogs-listed member of both the Hickoids and Jean Caffeine’s All-Nite Truckstop.

Release Notes

Tritonian Nash - Vegas Polyester Complex — No Trend (1987)

LP on Touch And Go. No Trend was a Washington DC area avant-punk/noise-rock band. Birdsong contributed pedal steel alongside a large ensemble including a horn section (Nick Smiley, Johnny Ontego, Paul Henzey), cello (Rogelio Maxwell), keyboards (Dean Evangelista), and percussion (Chris Pestelozzie). Lyrics for one track co-written by Lydia Lunch. Produced and engineered by Ken Mora.

Jean Caffeine — Jean Caffeine (2) (1989)

Self-released four-track demo cassette (single-sided). Produced by JD Foster. Core band included Fred KRC (drums), Mick Buck (lead guitar), Ponty Bone (accordion), Wash Hamilton (acoustic guitar/bass), Scott Blesener (fiddle). Four tracks from this cassette were re-recorded for or carried over to the 1990 LP. Birdsong on lap steel.

Waltz A Crossdress Texas — Hickoids (1989)

LP on Toxic Shock. Austin country-punk band; Birdsong is listed as a Discogs member of the Hickoids. Engineered and produced by Spot (Greg Ginn’s engineer at SST, known for Black Flag, Minutemen records). Other players: Davy Jones (2) (guitar), Jeff Smith (3) (lead vocals), Dick Hays (bass, piano, flugelhorn), Wade Driver (drums, vocals), Tim Cole (2) (trumpet), Jeff Daniels (8) (harmonica).

Jean Caffeine’s All-Nite Truckstop — Jean Caffeine’s All-Nite Truckstop (1990)

10-track cassette LP on Porch Music (2). Self-produced by Jean Caffeine with co-production from Brent Wilson (6). Mixed by JD Foster. Full ensemble: Ponty Bone (accordion), Mark Rubin (acoustic bass, backing vocals), Mick Buck (lead guitar, slide guitar, backing vocals), Fred KRC and Tom Lewis (10) (drums), Scott Biesener (fiddle), Lisa Pankratz (percussion), “Bongo Don” Stroud (bongos), Julie Grady (backing vocals), photography by Jane Levine.

Hickoid Heaven — Hickoids (1993)

CD compilation (remastered) on Westworld / Musical Tragedies, collecting earlier Hickoids material. The second half of the disc (tracks 20–28) consists of the full Waltz A Crossdress Texas LP, on which Birdsong played. Remastered by Roger Seibel. Engineer for the Waltz portion: Spot. First-half material engineered by Stuart Sullivan.

Trendy Candies For Happy Tourists — Barnacle Choir (2003)

CD on Gray Area Records (GA027). Birdsong contributes pedal steel on track 8 (“Scenario”) only. Primary band: Anatol Sucher (guitar, bass, beats, recorded by), Dan Bottrell (guitar, bass), Dave Ward (7) (drums), Gary Gray (6) (vocals, organ, piano). Guest guitar on track 3 by Chris Hart; track 1 by Justin Bishop.
