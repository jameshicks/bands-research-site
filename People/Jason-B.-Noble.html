Jason B. Noble

Louisville, Kentucky musician, engineer, and designer. Discogs ID: 345115. Died 2012.

Core member of Rachel’s (guitar, bass, vocals, engineering, design), Rodan (3) (guitar, vocals), Shipping News (bass, vocals), and The Sonora Pine.

One of the most prolific presences across the entire Quarterstick Records catalog: credited on 36 releases as engineer/mixer and 27 releases as designer/layout artist, in addition to his performing credits. With Rachel Grimes, he produced Rachel’s records and co-designed the label’s signature fine-art visual identity (letterpress, gravestone-rubbing imagery).
