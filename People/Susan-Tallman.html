Susan Tallman

Guitarist, visual artist, art critic, and editor. New York City. Discogs artist ID: 466908.

Overview

Susan Tallman was a founding member of Band Of Susans, playing guitar on the group’s first two releases (1987–1988). She was part of the original all-Susan guitar lineup assembled by Robert Poss, alongside Susan Stenger, Susan Lyall, and vocalist Alva Rogers. Tallman’s Discogs footprint is small — four credited releases — but she appears across two projects in the Trace Elements orbit: Band Of Susans and Nicolas Collins.

Outside music, Tallman became a prominent art critic and curator. She served as editor of Arts Magazine from 1989 to 1992, and later founded Art in Print (2011–2019), an international bimonthly journal devoted to the history and culture of the printed image. According to the Wikipedia article on Art in Print, the magazine was established following the 2009 closure of Art on Paper, received funding from the National Endowment for the Arts and several private foundations, and published until 2019. She was also credited as “Cultural Attaché” on the 1984 Western Eyes (2) LP — the Poss-led group that was the direct precursor to Band Of Susans — linking her social circle to Poss and Spitzer before Band Of Susans formed.

Tallman is Nicolas Collins’s wife, which accounts for the visual/design credits she contributed to several of his Trace Elements releases.
