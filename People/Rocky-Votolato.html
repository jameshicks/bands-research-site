Overview

Rocky Votolato is a Dallas-born singer-songwriter, active since 1996, working primarily in folk, acoustic, and indie rock. The elder brother of Cody Votolato, he has been part of several bands — most notably Waxwing — while maintaining a solo career anchored almost entirely by Second Nature Recordings across more than two decades, with additional releases through Barsuk Records. His most frequent collaborators include Casey Foubert and Seth Warren, with production credits from Matt Bayles, Troy Glessner, and Chris Walla, the latter forging a notable link to the Pacific Northwest indie scene.
