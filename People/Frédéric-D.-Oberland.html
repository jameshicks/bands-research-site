Overview

Frédéric D. Oberland is a French musician and photographer working in experimental, ambient, and post-rock territory, based at Magnum Diva Studio. He co-runs the NAHAL Recordings label and is a member of several ensembles — Oiseaux-Tempête, Farewell Poetry, Foudre!, Le Réveil Des Tropiques, and The Rustle Of The Stars — playing electric guitar, alto saxophone, and mellotron. Active since 2007, his catalogue across Sub Rosa, Gizeh Records, and NAHAL accelerated noticeably into the 2020s. His closest collaborator is Stéphane Pigneul (43 shared releases), with the circle extending to Harris Newman, GW Sok, Mondkopf, and Gareth Davis.

Notes (web) — Constellation Records connection

Oberland has two credits on Constellation spanning 2019–2026, but these are distinct in nature:

1. Photography credit (2019): The 2019 entry is not a music release. Oberland contributed 35mm B&W photographs for the insert of Sand Enigma by Land Of Kush (Sam Shalabi’s project), released on Constellation as a double vinyl on 8 November 2019. This was a visual/art contribution alongside photographer Hicham Chadly. Source: fredericdoberland.com/publications.html (retrieved 2026-11-19).

2. Music release (2026): The music connection came through Radwan Ghazi Moumneh — already one of Oberland’s closest collaborators (17 shared Discogs credits). Moumneh is a Montreal-based musician known for his project Jerusalem In My Heart, a long-standing Constellation artist; he also co-owns Hotel2Tango studio in Montreal. Oberland and Moumneh had collaborated repeatedly over the years via Oiseaux-Tempête (Moumneh was a recurring guest and sometimes producer on Oiseaux-Tempête records; Oberland reciprocally contributed to a Jerusalem In My Heart album). Those prior collaborations opened the door to a duo project. In summer 2023 they began recording focused duet sessions at Hotel2Tango; over roughly two years the sessions grew into the album Eternal Life No End (ليلة ظلماء ملعونة، كحياة طالبيها), released on Constellation as CST191 in April 2026. Sources: cstrecords.com/pages/artist/rgmfdo (retrieved 2026-11-19); castthedice.org (retrieved 2026-11-19).

How the connection formed: The Constellation relationship is essentially routed through Moumneh. Moumneh brought Constellation familiarity (as Jerusalem In My Heart) and the Hotel2Tango recording context; the prior Oiseaux-Tempête collaboration provided the existing creative trust. No interview found states exactly when or where Oberland and Moumneh first met. The 2019 photography credit for the Land Of Kush sleeve shows Oberland was already in the Constellation orbit before the duo music project — possibly via the same Montreal experimental network around Moumneh and Sam Shalabi.

Sources checked (web, 2026-11-19): cstrecords.com artist page, fredericdoberland.com publications, castthedice.org press release, nichemtl.com Moumneh interview (no origin-of-meeting detail), brainwashed.com (not fetched — snippet only). No source gives an explicit account of the first meeting between Oberland and Moumneh.
