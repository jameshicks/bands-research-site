Jenn Champion

Born Jennifer Lauren Hays, 1 March 1977. Seattle-based singer-songwriter, guitarist, and vocalist. Performed as Jenn Ghetto through the Carissa’s Wierd era and early solo years; adopted the name Jenn Champion around 2018 for her Hardly Art releases. Discogs IDs: Jenn Ghetto = 421369; Jenn Champion = 5130107.

Biography

Carissa’s Wierd (1996–2003)

Jenn Ghetto was a co-founding member of Carissa’s Wierd (Discogs ID: 401060), a Seattle slowcore/folk-rock band she shared with guitarist and co-songwriter Mat Brooke (ID: 421368). The band’s lineup across its run also included Ben Bridwell (drums; later Band of Horses), Sera Cahoone (drums; later solo career), Jeff Hellis (piano, accordion), Robin Perringer (drums), and Sarah Standard (violin). Jenn played guitar and sang.

Key releases during the active years:

  • Ugly But Honest: 1996–1999 (2000, Brown Records — archival comp of early recordings)
  • You Should Be At Home Here (2001, Brown Records)
  • Songs About Leaving (2002, Sad Robot SR 001) — produced and recorded by Chris Walla (Death Cab for Cutie); Jenn credited Guitar, Vocals
  • Scrap Book (2003, Not On Label) — final release before disbanding

The band dissolved in 2003. Their recordings were made cheaply on 4-track in some cases, with Jenn occasionally recording tracks herself alongside Walla’s production work. Iron And Wine contributed additional lyrics to one track on Songs About Leaving.

Solo as Jenn Ghetto (2001–2010)

Concurrent with and following Carissa’s Wierd, Jenn released solo material under the name Jenn Ghetto:

  • Sadstyle (2001, Brown Records) — recorded, mixed, and written by Jenn
  • I Before E (2004, Sad Robot)
  • Puking And Crying (2004, Suicide Squeeze)
  • I’m Not As Good At It As You (2010, Own Records — Luxembourg release)

Carissa’s Wierd Archival Reissues (2010)

Hardly Art (Sub Pop’s Seattle-based sublabel) reissued the Carissa’s Wierd catalog in 2010, including They’ll Only Miss You When You Leave: Songs 1996–2003 (HAR-015), a 16-track compilation with full band personnel. Chris Walla engineered the bulk of the compiled recordings. This reissue cycle introduced Jenn’s work on Carissa’s Wierd to a new audience and established her relationship with Hardly Art ahead of her solo career on the label.

Cool Choices (2014, Hardly Art) — credited to Carissa’s Wierd’s lineup — also appeared in the mid-2010s with Jenn on guitar, piano, vocals, synth, and drums.

As Jenn Champion (2018–present)

Adopting the Jenn Champion name, she released synth-leaning indie pop through Hardly Art and other labels:

  • Single Rider (2018, Hardly Art HAR-104) — LP; primary collaborators Levi Seitz and Jimmy Bazan; Brian Fennell (aka SYML) also credited
  • Owner of a Lonely Heart (2018, Hardly Art) — likely associated single/EP release
  • The Blue Album (2019, Turntable Kitchen SD014) — full LP cover of Weezer’s debut Blue Album (1994), performing all ten tracks; written-by credits to Rivers Cuomo, Pat Wilson, and Jason Cropper. Released on vinyl with Levi Seitz and Arwen Nicks collaborating.
  • The Last Night Of Sadness (2023, Gay Forever) — most recent LP

She also holds a mixing credit on Goddess Energy (2023, Father/Daughter Records).

Other Projects

  • Crictor (Discogs ID: 1101788) — a duo of Jenn Ghetto and Creighton Barrett (drummer; Grand Archives). No releases in Discogs; appears to be an informal or unreleased project.
  • Silly Goose (2) (Discogs ID: 4649677) — a Blink-182 cover band fronted by Jenn Ghetto with members of Grand Archives. Released one 7” vinyl EP (Silly Goose, 2013, End Of Time Records, EOT-003; indie rock) covering “Anthem Part Two,” “Talking,” “All The Small Things,” and “Dammit.”
