Keith Moliné

Discogs ID: 515496
Instruments: Guitar (primarily MIDI guitar), electronics, violin, bass, vocals
Active: 1996–2023 (documented on Discogs)

British guitarist and electronics musician. Best known as one half of Two Pale Boys alongside Andy Diagram (trumpet, electronics), and as a member of David Thomas And Two Pale Boys — the trio that served as David Thomas (2)‘s primary touring and recording unit throughout the late 1990s and 2000s. Moliné went on to join Pere Ubu proper (from around 2009), appears on five Pere Ubu studio albums, and has contributed to several other David Thomas–led ensembles including The Pale Orchestra. He has a solo release under his own name (with Andy Diagram) on the Entr’acte label (2012), and is a member of British art-rock group Prescott (4).

Name variations on Discogs: Keith, Keith Molin, Keith Moline, Keith Molinè, Moline, Moliné.

David Thomas And Two Pale Boys (1996–2004)

Moliné’s primary vehicle through this period. The group — Thomas (vocals, melodeon, producer), Diagram (trumpet), and Moliné (MIDI guitar) — recorded four studio albums, all produced by Thomas and engineered largely by Paul Hamann at Suma Recording in Cleveland.

Two Pale Boys duo — Ley (2012)

Moliné and Diagram released Ley (2012) as a duo on the experimental label Entr’acte (cat# 125). Credited as “Diagram & Moliné.” Seven tracks of free improvisation / experimental electronics; mastered by Giuseppe Ielasi.

Prescott (4) — One Did (2014)

Moliné is a member of British art-rock band Prescott (4), which released One Did (2014) on Slowfoot Records (SLOCD023). The band is a quartet: Moliné (guitar/electronics), Kev Hopper (bass), Frank Byng (drums), and Rhodri Marsden (keyboards, guitar).

Pere Ubu compilation — 93-03 (2007)

Moliné is credited as Musician on the Cooking Vinyl compilation 93-03 (2007), which draws from the David Thomas And Two Pale Boys era material.
