Gini Ball

Real name: Virginia Ball, née Virginia Hewes
Also credited as: Gini Hewes (pre-marriage); Virginia Ball; Ginni Ball; Ginny Ball; Jinnie Ball; Jinnie Hewes; V. Ball
Discogs IDs: 141736 (Gini Ball) · 481260 (Gini Hewes)
Groups / ensembles: The Venomettes · Brilliant Strings · The False Harmonics String Ensemble · Other People · Psychic TV · The Millennia Ensemble · Thee Wand · Virginia (7)

British violinist, viola player, singer, and composer. Sibling of Martin McCarrick (cellist; Siouxsie & the Banshees, This Mortal Coil). Graduate of the Royal College of Music. Was married to Dave Ball of Soft Cell. A fixture of the UK post-punk and alternative session world from 1983 onwards, working across the Some Bizzare stable, 4AD, Beggars Banquet, and beyond.

Background and Training

Virginia Hewes — known professionally as Gini — trained at the Royal College of Music. After graduating she formed the punk-classical string group Humoresque with Anne Stephenson, and later co-founded Brilliant Strings with Stephenson and Sally Herbert, a London-based session string collective that worked extensively in pop and alternative recording throughout the 1990s and 2000s.

She married Dave Ball and was thereafter credited as Gini Ball on Discogs, though her maiden name Gini Hewes appears on the earliest releases (1983–1984). Both Discogs entries (IDs 141736 and 481260) carry the same realname and are effectively aliases of each other.

Her brother Martin McCarrick was a parallel figure in the same milieu — McCarrick (cello, keyboards) worked extensively with Marc Almond and The Willing Sinners, Siouxsie & The Banshees, and This Mortal Coil, and appears alongside Gini on several key releases.

The Venomettes (1983)

Gini’s earliest documented session work places her within The Venomettes, a string section listed on Marc Almond’s Marc And The Mambas album Torment And Toreros (1983, Some Bizzare). The Venomettes were Gini Hewes (violin), Anne Stephenson (violin), Martin McCarrick (Venomettes credit, also cello), and Billy McGee. The group is also credited as arranger of the string parts on that album. The Venomettes appear to have been a short-lived entity specific to the Some Bizzare / Marc Almond orbit.

Some Bizzare Sessions (1983–1985)

Gini’s session calendar in 1983–85 was anchored by the Some Bizzare label.

In Strict Tempo — Dave Ball (1983, Some Bizzare)

Credited as Gini Hewes: Violin, Viola. Dave Ball’s debut solo album — largely instrumental electronic music with chamber textures from session strings. Other players included Anne Stephenson (violin), Virginia Astley (flute), Gary Barnacle (saxophone). Mixed by Flood at Trident and Wessex. Genesis P-Orridge and Gavin Friday contribute vocals to individual tracks.

Torment And Toreros — Marc And The Mambas (1983, Some Bizzare)

Credited as Gini Hewes, both as a Venomettes member and individually (violin). Brother Martin McCarrick also credited (cello, Venomettes). Produced by Flood and Marc Almond.

Sebastiane (soundtrack) (1983, Illuminated Records)

Violin credit under Gini Hewes. The Derek Jarman film soundtrack.

Showpeople (1983, The Compact Organization)

Violin, Viola credit under Gini Hewes.

This Last Night In Sodom — Soft Cell (1984, Some Bizzare / Sire)

Credited as Gini Ball: Vocals [Big Appetite + Big Hair] — an unusual vocal credit on the final Soft Cell studio album. The phrasing is a sleeve in-joke rather than a conventional backing vocal credit. Mixed by Flood.

Vermin In Ermine — Marc Almond & The Willing Sinners (1984, Some Bizzare)

String arrangements (co-arranged with Billy McGee and Martin McCarrick) and violin (credited “Ginni Ball” and “Ginni”). Produced by Mike Hedges.

Tenderness Is A Weakness (1984, Some Bizzare / Phonogram)

String arrangements; violin.

You Have (1984, Some Bizzare)

String arrangements; violin.

Stories Of Johnny — Marc Almond (1985, Some Bizzare / Virgin)

String arrangements (co-arranged with Martin McCarrick); no solo violin credit listed separately. Produced by Mike Hedges.

Love Letter — Marc Almond (1985, Some Bizzare / Virgin)

String arrangements; violin.

The House Is Haunted By The Echo Of Your Last Goodbye — Marc Almond (1985, Some Bizzare / Virgin)

String arrangements.

Melancholy Rose — Marc Almond (1987, Some Bizzare / Virgin)

String arrangements.

4AD / This Mortal Coil (1986–1997)

Filigree & Shadow — This Mortal Coil (1986, 4AD)

Violin, Viola. Produced by Ivo Watts-Russell and John Fryer. Martin McCarrick credited (cello, arrangements) alongside Simon Raymonde. A significant 4AD appearance placing Gini within the core TMC session ensemble.

Blood — This Mortal Coil (1991, 4AD)

Violin (French pressing credit).

Children Of God / World Of Skin — Swans (1997, Atavistic / Young God)

2nd Violin.

Siouxsie & The Banshees (1987)

Through The Looking Glass (1987, Polydor / Wonderland)

Violin — the Banshees’ covers album. Produced by Mike Hedges and the band. Martin McCarrick credited (cello, keyboards, string arrangements). Jocelyn Pook on viola.

This connection anticipated McCarrick’s later full membership in the Banshees (he joined the touring band shortly after).

Marc Almond: The Stars We Are (1988, Some Bizzare / Capitol)

Violin as member of The False Harmonics String Ensemble — also a Discogs group entity (ID 393605), overlapping membership with Brilliant Strings. Gini is listed as a member of both The False Harmonics and Brilliant Strings on Discogs.

Psychic TV (1991–1994)

Direction Ov Travel (1991, Temple Records)

Violin.

Allegory & Self — Thee Starlit Mire (1994, Cleopatra)

Credited as Performer “for thee realisation ov this project Psychic TV were.” Also a Discogs member of Psychic TV (ID 17926).

Manic Street Preachers (1996–1998)

Everything Must Go (1996, Epic)

Violin — alongside Anne Stephenson and Sally Herbert, all three Brilliant Strings members. Produced by Mike Hedges.

This Is My Truth Tell Me Yours (1998, Epic)

Violin (tracks 1, 5, 11) — again alongside Stephenson and Herbert, strings arranged by Sally Herbert. Produced by Mike Hedges.

Hedges was a recurring production link connecting the Some Bizzare / Marc Almond world to the Manic Street Preachers work.

McAlmont & Butler (1995)

The Sound Of… McAlmont & Butler (1995, Hut / Virgin)

Violin. Produced and string arrangements by Bernard Butler. Alongside Anne Stephenson, Claire Orsler, Jocelyn Pook.

Brilliant Strings (1997–present)

Gini is a Discogs member of Brilliant Strings (ID 314336), the London session string collective she co-founded with Anne Stephenson and Sally Herbert. The group’s name and membership overlap extensively with The False Harmonics String Ensemble. Brilliant Strings credits as a named ensemble appear on releases from the late 1990s onwards, including:

  • People Move On — Bernard Butler (1998, Epic): “Violin [The Brilliant Strings]”
  • Jack O The Green: Small World Big Band Friends 3 — Jools Holland (2003, Radar)
  • More Friends (Small World Big Band Volume Two) — Jools Holland (2002, Warner)
  • Small World Big Band — Jools Holland (2001, Warner)
