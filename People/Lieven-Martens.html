Lieven Martens

Belgian conceptual artist and composer, born 1981. Discogs artist IDs: 1055026 (Lieven Martens), 957466 (Dolphins Into The Future). Primary project name: Dolphins Into The Future (2007–c.2020, transitional). From c.2013–2014 onward, released increasingly under his own name. Runs the Belgian private-press label Edições Cn. Earlier associated with Cetacean Nation Cassettes and Dreamtime Taped Sounds (both sublabels of Taped Sounds).

Overview

Lieven Martens emerged from the Belgian underground tape scene in the mid-2000s, primarily under the Dolphins Into The Future alias. Early work drew on New Age, field recording, and kosmische-adjacent improvisation, released on micro-labels and cassette runs. The cetacean (whale/dolphin) theme was not merely aesthetic but conceptually central: several aliases, label names, and release titles foreground it (Cetacean Nation Cassettes, Cetacean Nation Institute For Environmental Sounds, Dolphins Of East Belgium, Ke Mirning Pu’uwai, Swimming With The Hawaiian Spinner Dolphins).

A first wave of cassette releases (2007–2009) came out on Dreamtime Taped Sounds and Cetacean Nation Cassettes — both sublabels of the Belgian umbrella label Taped Sounds. In 2009 the debut LP …On Sea-Faring Isolation appeared on Not Not Fun Records (NNF155-LP), situating him in the American psych/drone underground. A Belgian cassette edition had appeared the same year on Cetacean Nation Cassettes.

From 2010 he worked closely with the Belgian label (K-RAA-K)³ (Ke Ala Ke Kua LP, Music From The Guardhouse) and with the Canadian label Beniffer Editions (Two Romantic Landscapes). Around 2012–2014 the project name began shifting: early Edições Cn releases still foregrounded Dolphins Into The Future but increasingly credited “Dolphins Into The Future / Lieven Martens” jointly (Songs Of Gold, Incandescent, 2014) or led with the birth name. By Gardens, Fire And Wine (2017) and Idylls (2017) the Martens name was primary; Insular Monographs (2020) still carried both IDs in the primary credit.

Edições Cn (Discogs ID 606577, “Private Press run by Lieven Martens”) became from 2013–2014 the main outlet for both his own work and a curated circle of Belgian and international experimental artists: Antti Tolvi, Floris Vanhoof, Francesco Cavaliere, Christina Vantzou, Christophe Piette, Spencer Clark, SUGAI KEN, Andrew Pekler, Piotr Kurek, Matthew Sage, Florence Cats, Amber Meulenijzer, Gavin Vanaelst, Tomutonttu, and others.

Alias Identity and Naming Pattern

Discogs carries two separate primary artist entries for the same person (IDs 957466 and 1055026). The Dolphins Into The Future entry (957466) lists Lieven Martens as an alias; the Lieven Martens entry (1055026) lists Dolphins Into The Future as an alias. In practice:

  • 2007–2012: Almost all releases credited Dolphins Into The Future. The Lieven Martens name appears on a handful of early Belgian releases (Bolderplaats, Eternal Landscapes 2010) as L.R.J. Martens.
  • 2012–2014: Transition period. Lieven Moana and Lieven Martens Moana variants appear on Canto Arquipélago (2012), A Star Maker (2012), Songs Of Gold, Incandescent (2014).
  • 2014 onward: Own-name releases predominate at Edições Cn, though Dolphins Into The Future still appears on some Edições Cn releases through 2020 (Insular Monographs).
  • Duncan Cameron (3) is an additional alias used for at least two releases (2008, 2009) via Cetacean Nation Cassettes.
  • The Cetacean Nation Institute For Environmental Sounds alias appears on The Kealakekua Bay (2010).

Edições Cn — Label Context

Edições Cn (Discogs: “Private Press run by Lieven Martens”) operates from Belgium with the URL edicoescn.com / edicoescn.bandcamp.com. 108+ releases cataloged as of the Discogs dump. Catalog numbers run ECN10 (roughly Complementary Music) through ECN45+ (2024–2025). The label functions both as a vehicle for Martens’s own music and as an edited, curated catalog of collaborators and affiliates — Finnish (Antti Tolvi, Tomutonttu), Japanese (SUGAI KEN), Belgian (Roman Hiele, Floris Vanhoof, Christina Vantzou, Christophe Piette), Polish (Piotr Kurek), and international experimental artists.
