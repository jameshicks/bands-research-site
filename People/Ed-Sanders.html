Ed Sanders

American poet, singer, and producer. Real name: Edward Sanders. Discogs artist ID: 256643. Active: 1960s–present.

Overview

Co-founder and primary leader of The Fugs (1964–1969, 1984–2003+). Poet, publisher of Fuck You / A Magazine of the Arts (a mimeograph poetry magazine, 1962–65), and author. Sanders wrote much of The Fugs’ catalog and served as producer from Tenderness Junction (1967) onward. He operated as a solo recording artist on Reprise Records in the early 1970s.
