Hedvig Mollestad Thomassen

Discogs ID: 1619029
Born: 4 February 1982, Ålesund, Møre og Romsdal, Norway
Active: 2001–present
Also known as: Pøggs (alias); credited as “Hedvig Mollestad” on most releases
Primary label: Rune Grammofon

Norwegian guitarist, singer, and composer. One of the leading voices in contemporary Norwegian jazz-rock. Leads or co-leads multiple ensembles — most prominently Hedvig Mollestad Trio — and releases solo works under her full name. Her sound combines heavy, amplified guitar work rooted in hard rock and blues with free jazz improvisation and Nordic lyricism. Awarded “Young Jazz Talent of the Year” at Moldejazz 2009.

Biography

Origins and Training

Born and raised in Ålesund on Norway’s west coast. Trained at the Norwegian Academy of Music (Norges musikkhøgskole) in Oslo. Her early credits before the Trio period include a 2001 session (electric guitar on En Dråpe Lime, Mistral Lydstudio) and contributions to compilation and ensemble releases.

Early Career (2001–2010)

Before establishing her trio, Thomassen worked across the Oslo and Bergen music scenes as a session and ensemble player. She joined the bands of Jarle Bernhoft and Hilde Marie Kjersem in a central role. She was also involved with Trondheim Jazz Orchestra early on (member listed on Discogs). In 2009 she contributed piano to A Life Less Necessary on Rodeostar Records and glockenspiel to the Black Debbath 10th-anniversary compilation on Duplex Records/Grappa.

Hedvig Mollestad Trio: Core Years (2011–2021)

The Trio — with Ellen Brekken (bass) and Ivar Loe Bjørnstad (drums) — debuted on Rune Grammofon in 2011 with Shoot! (RCD 2115). This guitar–bass–drums power trio quickly became one of the label’s most prolific and internationally recognized acts, releasing eight studio albums across a decade: the debut was followed by All Of Them Witches (2013), “Enfant Terrible” (2014), Black Stabat Mater (2016), the live Evil In Oslo (2016), Smells Funny (2018), and Ding Dong. You’re Dead. (2021). The lineup on all trio records is unchanged: Thomassen (guitar), Brekken (bass), Bjørnstad (drums). Songwriting is shared across all three members, with Thomassen contributing the largest share.

The Trio returned in 2025 with Bees In The Bonnet (4 pressings on Discogs).

Solo Expansion (2020–2023)

Thomassen’s first solo release under her own name — Ekhidna (2020) — marked a decisive step outward from the trio format. The album featured a sextet: Torstein Lofthus (drums), Erlend Slettevoll (electric piano, synthesizers), Marte Maaland Eberson (electric piano, synthesizers), Ole Mofjell (percussion), and Susana Santos Silva (trumpet). All music by Thomassen; she also produced. Recorded and mixed by Bård Ingebrigtsen; mastered by Helge Sten.

Tempest Revisited (2021) expanded further: Thomassen plays guitar, vocals, handclaps, and upright piano, with a small chamber-jazz ensemble including Ivar Loe Bjørnstad (drums), Trond Frønes (bass), Marte Maaland Eberson (vibraphone, synth), Martin Myhre Olsen (saxophones), Peter Erik Vergeni (tenor sax, flute), and Karl Hjalmar Nyberg (alto sax). Special guest: Per Oddvar Johansen (drums, on A1). Co-produced by Thomassen and Bård Ingebrigtsen; mastered by Helge Sten.

Maternity Beat (2022) is a large-scale orchestral project: Thomassen as composer, producer, guitarist, and vocalist (one track), collaborating with Trondheim Jazz Orchestra under conductor Erlend Skomsvoll. The orchestra features Ellen Brekken (double bass), Torstein Lofthus (drums), Ståle Storløkken (Hammond, Rhodes, synthesizers), Trine Knutsen (flutes), Martin Myhre Olsen (saxophones), Petter Kraft (tenor sax), Thomas Johansson (6) (trumpet), Adrian Løseth Waade (violin), Ingvald André Vassbø (percussion), with vocalists Ingebjørg Loe Bjørnstad and Mai Elise Solberg. Commissioned jointly by Midtnorsk Jazzsenter and Molde International Jazz Festival. Recorded by Dag Erik Johansen; mixed by Bård Ingebrigtsen; mastered by Helge Sten.

Hedvig Mollestad Weejuns (2023)

A new trio project with Ståle Storløkken (Hammond, synth, Continuum) and Ole Mofjell (drums) — released Weejuns (2023) on Rune Grammofon. Thomassen produced alongside Rune Kristoffersen. Mixed by Daniel Wold; mastered by Helge Sten. Sleeve and design by Kim Hiorthøy.

Cortex Collaboration (2025)

Co-leads Did We Really? (2025, Sauajazz) with Cortex (17), a Norwegian jazz group. Both parties are credited as arrangers, composers, and co-producers. Bård Ingebrigtsen recorded and mixed; Fridtjof A. Lindeman mastered.
