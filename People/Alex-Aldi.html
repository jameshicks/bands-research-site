Oct 19, 20264 min read

Alex Aldi

Discogs ID: 525592
Name variations: Alex, Alex (NYC)
Origin: New York City, USA

Recording engineer, mixer, and producer. Based at Gigantic Studios, NYC. Career spans from 2005 assistant engineer credits through to full production. Defined primarily by a long partnership with producer Chris Zane, with whom he engineered and mixed three Passion Pit albums on Frenchkiss / Columbia — the backbone of his discography. Unusually, also listed as a performer on Gregor Samsa (3)‘s Rest (2008) alongside engineering duties.

Career Arc

2005–2007 — Early Engineering

Entered the credit trail as engineer on The Fall’s Fall Heads Roll (2005, Slogan Records), credited as “Alex (NYC).” Spent 2006–2007 as assistant/2nd engineer across Gigantic Studios sessions, including:

  • White Rabbits — Fort Nightly (2007, Academy Fight Song) — mix
  • Les Savy Fav — Let’s Stay Friends (2007, Frenchkiss Records) — 2nd engineer
  • Gregor Samsa (3) — Rest (2007/2008, Talk To Me Records) — performer and engineer; lineup included Toby Driver, Mia Matsumiya, Rick Alverson, Alan Weatherhead, and others
  • Ghastly City Sleep (2007, Robotic Empire) — first full producer + mixer credit

2008–2009 — Consolidation and Passion Pit

  • Tokyo Police Club — Elephant Shell (2008, Saddle Creek) — additional engineering
  • The Walkmen — You & Me (2008, Talitres) — 2nd engineer alongside primary engineer/producer Chris Zane
  • Passion Pit — Manners (2009, Frenchkiss Records / Columbia) — recorded by, mixed by; producer Chris Zane; 19 pressings; includes “Sleepyhead,” “The Reeling,” “Little Secrets”

2010–2012 — Expansion

  • The Walkmen — Lisbon (2010, Fat Possum) — 2nd engineer at Gigantic Studios
  • The Hundred In The Hands — self-titled (2010, Warp Records) — recorded/mixed specific tracks (with Chris Zane); DFA-adjacent release with contributions from Jacques Renault, Richard X, Eric Broucek
  • DFA adjacent: engineer on Say My Name (The Revenge Remixes) (2010, DFA); Hold On / On Board (2010, DFA/XL)
  • Passion Pit — Gossamer (2012, Columbia/Frenchkiss) — engineer, additional producer, mixed by; notable session cast: Nico Muhly (string arrangements, piano on several tracks), Diplo (additional programming on “Where We Belong”), Clarice Jensen (cello/flute), Rob Moose (violin)

2014–2015 — Full Production

  • Before The Waves (2014, Columbia/Neon Gold) — producer, recorder, mixer, programmer
  • Passion Pit — Kindred (2015, Columbia) — engineer and producer; Benny Blanco co-produces tracks 1, 2, 4, 7; Hans Zimmer co-wrote “Until We Can’t (Let’s Go)“

2016–2023 — Mixing and Scattered Production

  • Mixing credits for various artists through 2019
  • Grey Daze — Amends (2020, Loma Vista) — producer (Chester Bennington’s pre-Linkin Park band; this credit was previously misattributed in this vault to Grace VanderWaal — that was an error)
  • Chaos In Bloom (2023, Warner Records) — written-by credit
