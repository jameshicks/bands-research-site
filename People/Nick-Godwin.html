Overview

Nick Godwin is a North London-born multi-instrumentalist and recording engineer whose career threaded through early UK punk, post-punk, and folk-adjacent circles from the mid-1970s onward. He joined Zounds near the band’s inception around 1976, taking part in their UK Weird Tales touring runs in 1979 and 1980, then relocated to west London where he co-founded The Idiot Ballroom, a club night at the Hammersmith Clarendon Hotel. After that he joined Real Imitations, then moved back to North London and fell in with the Lee Thompson Band, which eventually became The Silencerz. The heaviest cluster in his credit history—six shared releases apiece with Ewan MacColl, Peggy Seeger, and their children Calum, Neill, and Hamish—points to a sustained involvement with the MacColl/Seeger world, most likely through his membership in The World Service; his catalogue skews sharply toward the 1980s with scattered returns in the 2000s and 2022.
