Stuart Hallerman

Producer, engineer, studio owner. Discogs artist ID: 281169. Active 1987–present.

Overview

Stuart Hallerman runs Avast! Recording Co. (also referred to as Avast! Studios, Avast Recording, Avast Sound), a Seattle-based recording facility. The original “Avast! Classic” location at 1325 N. 46th St. was active from around 1990 until 2008; a new facility opened in 2005 at 601 NW 80th St. with two separate units (Studio A and B). Avast became one of the key PNW indie studios of the 1990s–2000s, hosting Soundgarden, Death Cab for Cutie, Unwound, Bikini Kill, Mudhoney, Earth, and dozens of others.

His early career (pre-Avast) included engineering work at Ironwood Studios (Seattle). He is associated with Kill Rock Stars primarily through his 1993 work on Bikini Kill’s Pussy Whipped and Unwound’s Fake Train, but his studio drew a much wider circle of Seattle-area artists across the grunge and post-grunge eras.

Studio Notes

Avast! Recording Co. (Discogs label ID: 276755) is credited on releases simply as “Avast,” “Avast! (Seattle),” “Avast II,” “Avast Sound,” or “Avast Recording, Seattle, WA.” Hallerman appears in credit fields as “Engineer [at Avast! Studios]” or “Engineer [Avast Assistant]” on records where he was assistant rather than principal engineer.
