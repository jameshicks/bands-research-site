Tom Herman

Born Thomas E. Herman, 1949, Buffalo, New York. Guitarist, bassist, keyboardist, vocalist. Discogs artist ID: 321545.

Overview

Tom Herman is the original guitarist of Pere Ubu, present from the band’s founding in September 1975 through New Picnic Time (1979) — three studio albums and a run of Hearpen singles that established the Cleveland avant-rock sound. He was recruited by David Thomas (2) and Peter Laughner directly into Pere Ubu (not via Rocket From The Tombs, which had already disbanded before Ubu formed). Herman was not a member of RFTT.

His guitar work on the early Ubu records is abrasive and anti-melodic — closer to industrial texture than conventional rock lead guitar. He co-wrote much of the band’s early catalog and his approach was central to what distinguished Pere Ubu from the New York punk scene developing simultaneously. He departed after New Picnic Time and the band briefly reconstituted around Mayo Thompson.

Herman returned to Pere Ubu in 1995 as a touring guitarist during the Ray Gun Suitcase cycle (covering for Jim Jones), then recorded Pennsylvania (1998), playing guitar, bass, tack piano, and snare — all without sleeve credit. He appeared again on St. Arkansas (2002) as a full credited member before departing once more. Wikipedia notes he rejoined the touring band in 2016 to select and perform material from the 1979–1982 era and appeared at specific live events, including a 2011 full-album performance of The Modern Dance at the Beachland Ballroom in Cleveland.

Beyond Pere Ubu, Herman led the solo project Tripod Jimmie (with Lenny Bove), released one solo album Wait For It (2004, Return To Sender), contributed lead guitar to the Robert Bensick Band’s French Pictures In London (2016, Smog Veil Records), produced Make ‘Em Cry (2021, Reminder Records), and is a member of the large-ensemble free music collective Scarcity Of Tanks.

Discogs lists his birth city as Buffalo, New York (not Cleveland, Ohio as sometimes cited in press).
