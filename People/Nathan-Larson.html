Oct 30, 20262 min read

Nathan Larson

Discogs ID: 345447
Real name: Nathan Peter Larson
Origin: Washington, DC, USA

DC-born guitarist, bassist, composer, and producer. Early career as bassist in Swiz (1987–1989); later guitarist and co-writer in Shudder To Think (c. 1992–1998). After Shudder To Think dissolved, built a sustained career in film/TV scoring and record production, with a long working relationship with Nina Persson’s A Camp project.

Overview

Larson joined Shudder To Think in time for the Get Your Goat era (1992) and is the guitarist on both Epic albums: Pony Express Record (1994) and 50,000 B.C. (1997). He co-wrote substantial portions of the High Art film score (1998) with Craig Wedren. After the band wound down, he moved primarily into scoring and production.
