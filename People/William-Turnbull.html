William Turnbull

Born: 11 January 1922, Dundee, Scotland Died: 15 November 2012, London (aged 90) Occupation: Sculptor, painter Connection to this network: Father of Johnny and Alex Turnbull of 23 Skidoo

Biography

Left school at 15 during the Depression; worked as a labourer and film poster painter, then as a commercial illustrator at DC Thomson (Dundee). Served as an RAF pilot 1941–1945, stationed in Canada, India, and Ceylon. After the war, attended the Slade School of Fine Art from 1946 — where he met Eduardo Paolozzi and Nigel Henderson. Moved to Paris 1948–1950, visiting Brancusi’s studio and meeting Giacometti. Returned to London 1950 and taught at the Central School of Art for the rest of his career.

Married Singaporean sculptor Kim Lim in 1960. Alex and Johnny Turnbull (23 Skidoo) are their sons.

The Independent Group

William Turnbull was a founding participant in the Independent Group — a loose collective of artists, architects, and critics who met at the ICA in London in two sessions: winter 1952–53 and 1953–54.

Fellow members:

  • Eduardo Paolozzi (sculptor — close friend since Slade days)
  • Richard Hamilton (artist)
  • Nigel Henderson (photographer)
  • Lawrence Alloway (critic — coined the term “pop art”)
  • Reyner Banham (design critic/historian)
  • John McHale (fine artist)
  • Alison and Peter Smithson (architects)
  • Frank Cordell (music producer — an early art/music bridge)

The IG challenged modernist elitism by taking American movies, science fiction, advertising, car design, and pop music seriously. This was the conceptual root of British Pop Art.

Key IG exhibitions:

  • Parallel of Life and Art (ICA, 1953)
  • This Is Tomorrow (Whitechapel Gallery, August 1956) — the watershed show; Turnbull contributed Sun Gazer

Connection to 23 Skidoo

The connection is direct and documented:

  1. Alex Turnbull co-directed Beyond Time (2011/2012), a documentary film about his father, with Pete Stern. Narrated by Jude Law.

  2. 23 Skidoo composed the entire soundtrack, combining newly composed pieces with reworked older material. Released by Les Disques du Crépuscule (with the documentary DVD) in 2014 — the band’s first new material in 15 years.

  3. Alex Turnbull (direct quote): “Johnny and I thought 23 Skidoo’s anti-commercial tendencies came from a punk sensibility. But it turns out we had a genetic predisposition to anti-establishment practices.”

  4. Johnny Turnbull described their musical approach as “almost sculptural — layering, refining, and removing elements.” Alex described their father as “a polymath at a time when that was a dirty word, shifting between sculpture and painting and putting both in a symbiotic relationship.”

  5. The household record collection — “spanning jazz, ethnic, and classical music” — is cited as broadening both brothers’ musical horizons from childhood.

The ICA Thread

The same institution where William Turnbull helped launch British Pop Art in the early 1950s later hosted COUM Transmissions’ Prostitution exhibition (1976) — which directly spawned Throbbing Gristle, widely considered the founding act of industrial music. TG then produced 23 Skidoo’s debut album. The ICA is thus both the origin point of William Turnbull’s career and the institutional birthplace of the genre his sons would define.
