Josh Hill (3)

Guitarist, drummer, and keyboardist. Discogs ID: 1214876. Cousin of Zach Hill (Hella, Death Grips). Based in the Sacramento/Davis, California area in the late 1990s–2000s.

Overview

Josh Hill is a multi-instrumentalist who moves between guitar, drums, and keyboards. He is best known as a member of Legs On Earth — the pre-Hella group he shared with Zach Hill, Spencer Seim, and Julian Imsdahl — and for his guitar credit on Hella’s 2007 Ipecac album There’s No 666 In Outer Space. His family connection to Zach Hill placed him at the center of the Sacramento math-rock/noise-rock scene that generated Hella.

Discogs lists him as a member of five groups: Legs On Earth, Hella, Boyz IV Men, E S S, and Playing To The Grandstand.
