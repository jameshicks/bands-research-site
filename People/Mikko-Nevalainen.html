Mikko Nevalainen

Finnish bassist, born 1952. Real name: Mikko Tapani Nevalainen. Also known as “Vaari”. Discogs artist ID: 532202

Biography

Overview

Mikko Tapani Nevalainen (born 1952) is a Finnish bassist whose career spans two of the most significant Finnish punk and new wave acts of the late 1970s and 1980s. He is best known under the alias “Vaari” — a Finnish word roughly meaning “grandpa/old man”, used informally as a nickname. His name variations on Discogs include: M. Nevalainen, Mikko “Vaari” Nevalainen, Mikko N., Nevalainen, Nevalainen M., and Vaari.

Kollaa Kestää (1978–1979)

Nevalainen joined Kollaa Kestää in spring 1978, after their debut single “Musti, Sotakoira” / “Tähtien Rauha” (Poko Rekords, 1978). He appeared as bassist and backing vocalist on the four-track EP Kollaa Kostaa (1978, Poko Rekords — produced by Mikko Syrjä, recorded by Mika Sundqvist) and on the band’s landmark debut LP Jäähyväiset Aseille (1979, Johanna). That album is regarded as one of the high points of Finnish punk-era music, self-produced by the band and recorded/mixed by Reino Iso-Aho; it features guest tenor saxophone from British artist Lora Logic (of X-Ray Spex and Essential Logic).

He also appeared on the 1979 single / EP Kirjoituksia Kellarista (Poko Rekords), again on bass and backing vocals (recorded and mixed by Mikko Syrjä).

Nevalainen departed Kollaa Kestää after the LP cycle — the band’s classic lineup dissolved in the early 1980s.

Solo Single (1980)

In 1980, Nevalainen released a solo single on Poko Rekords: “Meijän Isi” / “Kuin Eilen” (Discogs release 3369532). He is credited as bass, vocals, and producer — the only release where he appears as a primary artist. The single also features Matti Nevalainen (guitar, vocals) and Pasi Raunio (drums), suggesting a family or close-circle collaboration.

Eppu Normaali (1980–1989)

Nevalainen became a member of Eppu Normaali circa 1980, where he served as bassist through 1989 — their most commercially productive decade. Eppu Normaali, centred around the Syrjä brothers (vocalist Martti Syrjä, guitarists Mikko Syrjä / Pantse Syrjä, and drummer Aku Syrjä) with guitarist Juha Torvinen, became one of Finland’s biggest rock acts.

Nevalainen played bass on every Eppu Normaali studio album through this period. He is credited in the 2009 retrospective compilation Jackpot - 101 Eppu-klassikkoa 1978-2009 specifically as “Bass [1980–1989]”, confirming the exact dates of his membership. He also contributed producer credits on the 1987 mini-LP “Soolot” (two tracks: A5 and B5), which was credited to Aku, Juha, Martti & Mikko².

Apua! Orkesteri (1985)

Nevalainen is listed as a member of Apua! Orkesteri, a large Finnish collective act (38+ performers) assembled around songwriter Juice Leskinen. He appears as a performer on their 1985 album Maksamme Velkaa (Poko Rekords). The ensemble also included Martti Syrjä, Aku Syrjä, Juha Torvinen, Pantse Syrjä, Ismo Alanko, Pave Maijanen, Pate Mustajärvi, and many others — effectively a who’s-who of Finnish rock of the era. The album was produced by Anssi Tikanmäki, Pantse Syrjä, and Mika Sundqvist.

Mikko² (1987)

Nevalainen is one of two members of Mikko² — the other being Mikko Syrjä (the guitarist/songwriter of Eppu Normaali). The duo’s one Discogs release is the mini-LP “Soolot” (1987, Poko Rekords), released under the joint credit “Aku, Juha, Martti & Mikko²”. Nevalainen produced tracks A5 and B5 on that release.

Later Activity (2007, 2024)

In 2007, Nevalainen appeared on Tuuliajolla (Eli Ballaadeja Saimaalta), a joint release credited to Hassisen Kone, Juice Leskinen Slam, and Eppu Normaali (Poko Rekords). He plays bass on tracks 1, 4, 9, 11, and 12 (the Eppu Normaali set), with Pantse Syrjä also on guitar and vocals, Aku Syrjä on drums, Juha Torvinen on guitar, and Martti Syrjä on vocals.

In 2024, he appeared on Kahdeksas Ihme (Backstage Rock Shop), an Eppu Normaali release — confirming a return to the band after a long gap.

Open Questions

  • Exact departure from Kollaa Kestää — Discogs profile says he was in the “classic lineup” 1978–79. Whether he stayed into 1980 is not confirmed; the solo single is from 1980 and may postdate Kollaa Kestää.
  • Matti Nevalainen (Discogs 431921) — credited on Nevalainen’s solo single on guitar/vocals. Surname match suggests possible family relation, but Discogs has no profile data confirming this.
  • Eppupopedanormaali (Discogs 383770) — Nevalainen is listed as a member. This appears to be a Finnish supergroup / collective; its release history or activity dates are not confirmed from the available data.
  • Apua! Orkesteri context — The collective has a Wikipedia link (fi.wikipedia.org) not accessible via Discogs tools; fuller background on the project’s purpose and Nevalainen’s specific role within it requires web research.
  • 2024 Kahdeksas Ihme context — The 2024 Eppu Normaali release suggests Nevalainen rejoined the band at some point after 1989. The circumstances of this return are not captured in Discogs metadata.
  • “Vaari” nickname origin — not explained in Discogs profile text; would need Finnish-language sources.
