Victor Van Vugt

Dutch record producer and engineer. Discogs artist ID: 224957.

Co-produced and engineered Epic Soundtracks’ Rise Above (1992, Rough Trade). Seven shared releases with Epic on Discogs. Known for work with Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds and PJ Harvey.

Red Flame work

House producer for Red Flame in its second phase. Credited on 12 releases on the label — primarily for Ruby Blue (the label’s longest-running act, 1987–1993) and The Moodists (Australian post-punk, 1983–1985).
