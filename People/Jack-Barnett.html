Overview

Jack Barnett is a British songwriter, producer, vocalist, and multi-instrumentalist, primarily known as the creative lead of These New Puritans, the art-rock group he has shaped across a catalogue spanning angular post-punk, orchestral experimentation, and darkwave. Working since 2005, he has accumulated 150 credits, with his brother George Barnett appearing on 30 of them and producer Graham Sutton on a further 30, making both recurring anchors of the project. His label trajectory runs from Angular Recording Corporation (2006–2010) through Domino (a relationship stretching to 2025) and Infectious Music (2013–2023), reflecting a steady move toward larger independent infrastructure while maintaining an experimental scope.

How the Barnett–Tibet connection began

Jack Barnett initiated contact with David Tibet by sending him a question about the meaning of a lyric in one of Tibet’s songs. Tibet responded warmly and stated that he liked These New Puritans’ record. The relationship then deepened when These New Puritans appeared on the bill for Current 93’s “25 Years of Current 93 / David Tibet’s 50th birthday” concerts at the HMV Forum, Kentish Town, London, on 29 May 2010, alongside Comus and Rameses III.

George Barnett described the origin in a 2019 PopMatters interview:

“I think Jack asked him a question about something in one of his songs several years ago, and he came right back to him and was like, ‘Yeah, I really like your record.‘”

“These New Puritans ended up opening for Current 93 in South London in 2010 and the relationship developed from there.”

— George Barnett, Pushing Pop to Extremes: An Interview with These New Puritans, PopMatters, May 2019 (source)

The HMV Forum show is confirmed by setlist.fm (source) and the Last.fm event page (source).

I Am the Last of All the Field That Fell: A Channel (2014)

Jack Barnett contributed organ, sound design, and voices to this Current 93 album. It was recorded in 2013, debuted live at Union Chapel, Islington (12 February 2014), and released 4 March 2014 on The Spheres / Coptic Cat. Other contributors included Nick Cave, ANOHNI, John Zorn, James Blackshaw, Andrew Liles, Ossian Brown, and Jon Seagroatt. (Wikipedia, as of 2026-08-24)

These New Puritans’ own site hosts a page dedicated to the collaboration, including a group photograph of the participants. (thesenewpuritans.com)

Ongoing relationship

Tibet subsequently appeared as a vocalist on These New Puritans’ comeback single “Into the Fire” (2018) and on Inside the Rose (2019). Jack Barnett has noted: “I think people expect him to be a bringer-of-the-apocalypse, but I’ve found that the more ‘serious’ the music, the less seriously the people take themselves.” George has called Tibet “probably the best living lyricist.” (PopMatters 2019); (Loud and Quiet 2019)
