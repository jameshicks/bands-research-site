Atelewo

Collaborative project between Osunlade and Pastaboys. Discogs artist ID: 85760

Active 2002–2012, primarily on Yoruba Records. Released four singles across a decade; the name also appears as “Attelewo” on some pressings. The three Pastaboys members (Cristiano Rinaldi, Davide Santandrea, Dino Angioletti) are all credited alongside Osunlade (real name Christian Carlos Warren) in the Discogs member list, confirming this is a five-person collective/studio project rather than a solo act.

Overview

Atelewo is a studio collaboration uniting Kansas City–born, Yoruba-influenced DJ/producer Osunlade with Pastaboys, a house production team from Bologna, Italy. The project yielded four known releases between 2002 and 2012, all in the deep house / electronic idiom. There is no publicly documented information on Discogs about live performances or any other format beyond 12” vinyl singles.

The debut single “New Day” (2002) was among the earliest releases on Yoruba Records (cat no. YOR 001), also credited to Soul Jazz Records. A 2005 reissue/flipside (“New Day / Macaco”) re-pressed the track. A 2003 detour onto the Italian label Irma Unlimited (“Chimes Of Freedom”) credited the three-way collaboration explicitly: “Osunlade and Pasta Boys Presents Atelewo.” The final release “Infinity / Sendai” appeared in 2012.

No real name, solo biography, or identity separate from its two constituent acts is documented. Atelewo appears to function as a shared project name rather than a distinct band persona.

Open Questions

  • No compilation appearances found on Discogs
  • Christian Warren is listed in Discogs as both an Osunlade alias and an Atelewo member — this appears to be a data artefact (Christian Carlos Warren = Osunlade); not a separate person
