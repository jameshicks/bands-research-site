Dorothy Max Prior

Discogs ID: 2671553 Real name: Dorothy Prior. UK performer and writer. Released the single I Confess (IR0014, 1980) on Industrial Records.

Overview

Dorothy Max Prior released I Confess (b/w “Softness”) on Industrial Records in 1980, written with Alex Fergusson (who also produced) and with a B-side co-written by Genesis P-Orridge (credited as “Tony Graves” on the sleeve). She was subsequently a member of Rema-Rema and later Psychic TV. She is also credited as a member of The Monochrome Set.
