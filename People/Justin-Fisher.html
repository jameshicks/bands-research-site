Overview

Justin Fisher is a bassist, vocalist, and guitarist best known as a member of Nerf Herder, the Santa Barbara pop-punk band that formed around Parry Gripp, Angus Cooke, Dave Ehrlich, and Steve Sherlock. He has also been part of Avant Garde (16) and Shufflepuck. His recorded output reaches back to 1987 but concentrates heavily in the 2000s, with releases through Honest Don’s, Fat Wreck Chords, and Interscope Records; producer Ryan Greene and engineer Eddy Schreyer recur across his work alongside his core Nerf Herder collaborators.
