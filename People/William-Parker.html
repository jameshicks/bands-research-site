William Parker

Discogs ID: 222600
Origin: New York City, USA

American double bassist and composer. One of the central figures in post-1970s free jazz; an extraordinarily prolific collaborator and leader. Primary collaborator of Hamid Drake (54 shared releases). Member of the Die Like a dog quartet (with Brötzmann, Kondo, Drake). Runs his own ensembles: William Parker Quartet, Violin Trio, Orchestra, Double Quartet, Septet, and others. Also member of In Order To Survive and Cecil Taylor groups.

Key collaborator of Matthew Shipp — over 52 shared releases, serving as the bass anchor for Shipp’s trios and quartets from debut through the early 2010s. Appeared on hat ART By The Law Of Music (1997), hatOLOGY The Multiplication Table (1998), and the full Thirsty Ear Blue Series run.

Also wrote liner notes (English) for Hear In Now’s debut album Hear In Now (2012, Rudi Records RRJ 1007), the string trio of Tomeka Reid, Mazz Swift, and Silvia Bolognesi.

Key collaborator of Charles Gayle across many contexts: Consecration (1993, Black Saint), Touchin’ On Trane (1993, FMP), Kingdom Come (1994, KFW), More Live At The Knitting Factory (1993, KFW as Charles Gayle Quartet), and Webo (2024, Black Editions Archive, with Milford Graves).
