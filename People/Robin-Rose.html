Overview

Robin Rose is a synthesizer player best known as a member of Urban Verbs, the Washington D.C. new wave and art rock band that released two albums on Warner Bros. Records in 1980–1981, and Twisted Teenage Plot, a later project on TTP Records in 1984. The discography is concentrated entirely in the 1980s, with a core band cluster of Robert Goldstein, Danny Frankel, Linda France, and Roddy Frantz recurring across credits, alongside producers Mike Thorne and Steve Lillywhite. Reissues from Wounded Bird Records and Music On CD through 2024 point to sustained collector interest in the Urban Verbs catalogue.
