Carla Kihlstedt

Carla Kihlstedt

Discogs ID: 248127
Born: 1971, Lancaster, Pennsylvania, USA
Based: Bay Area, California
Instruments: Violin, nyckelharpa, accordion, voice, keyboards, autoharp, harmonium, bass harmonica

American violinist, accordionist, vocalist, and composer. One of the most versatile instrumentalists to emerge from the Bay Area experimental/improv scene, Kihlstedt has operated across contemporary classical, free improvisation, avant-rock, and theatrical composition. She is known as a founding or core member of Tin Hat Trio, Sleepytime Gorilla Museum, Cosa Brava, The Book Of Knots, and the duo Rabbit Rabbit (3) (with Matthias Bossi).

Biography

Early Career and Tin Hat Trio (1994–2007)

Kihlstedt first appears on record in 1994 on Nub City (Vaccination Records), a Charming Hostess project. Her central early vehicle was the Tin Hat Trio, a chamber-jazz ensemble pairing her violin with Mark Orton’s guitar/dobro and Rob Burger’s accordion/piano. The group recorded for Angel Records (EMI) and later Ropeadope/Rykodisc:

Sleepytime Gorilla Museum (2001–2007)

Kihlstedt was a founding member of Sleepytime Gorilla Museum, an Oakland-based avant-garde rock band formed 1999 with Matthias Bossi, Nils Frykdahl, Dan Rathbun, Michael Mellender, and others.
