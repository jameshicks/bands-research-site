Andy Connell

Keyboardist. Manchester. Real name Andrew Connell. Born 26 July 1961. Discogs ID: 33933.

Projects

  • A Certain Ratio — keyboards, synthesiser, sampler; full member c.1982–1985; listed in Discogs membership
  • Kalima — prominent guest/associate; piano on Four Songs (1985) and Night Time Shadows (1986)
  • Domino Park — side project with Corinne Drewery and Tony Quigley (Vivo Recordings, 1998–1999)
  • Swing Out Sister — co-founder with Corinne Drewery and Martin Jackson (ex-Magazine); pop/jazz crossover; “Breakout” (1986) was a UK and US hit

Key Facts

  • One of the more surprising trajectories in the Factory orbit — from ACR’s avant-funk to Swing Out Sister’s polished pop.
  • Swing Out Sister: signed to Mercury Records; “Breakout” reached #4 UK, #6 US.
