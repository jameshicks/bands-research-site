John Tchicai

Aug 22, 20262 min read

John Tchicai

Discogs ID: 82740

Danish-American alto saxophonist, a key figure in the mid-1960s New York free jazz scene.

Appeared on the Archie Shepp / New York Contemporary 5 side of the 1964 Savoy split LP shared with Bill Dixon’s 7-Tette.

Selected Credits

  • Always Born (1988, Silkheart) — Charles Gayle Quartet (tenor and soprano saxophone); co-led with Charles Gayle
  • John Tchicai With Strings (Treader trd005, 2005) — bass clarinet, alto saxophone, voice, composer; produced by John Coxon and Ashley Wales. Coxon played electric guitar, piano, sampler, harpsichord, and composed alongside Tchicai.

Connections

  • John Coxon — John Tchicai With Strings producer
