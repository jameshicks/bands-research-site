David Licht

Discogs ID: 266805
Also credited as: Dave Licht, D. Licht
Born: c. 1954, Detroit, Michigan
Origin: Grew up in Greensboro, North Carolina; later based in New York City

Drummer. Co-founder of The Klezmatics (NYC, 1986). Member of Shockabilly, Bongwater, B.A.L.L., Bank Of Sodom, The Chadbournes, David Krakauer’s Klezmer Madness!, Paws (2), Shekhina Big Band, The Moon (26). Performed with Ned Rothenberg, John Zorn, and Tom Cora in early 1980s. Also connected to Kapelye, Giora Feidman.

Biography

Licht grew up in Greensboro, North Carolina, where he played in a local jazz group called In-Time around 1977. In the early 1980s he worked with The Chadbournes. He also worked as a house painter alongside his music career during this period. (wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Licht, as of 2025-06-26)

In 1985 he moved to New York City to help manage a recording studio, and shortly after joined Bongwater. Around the same time — 1986 — he co-founded The Klezmatics with Frank London, Paul Morrissett, Lorin Sklamberg, and Alicia Svigals. The Klezmatics became one of the most prominent klezmer revival groups and won a Grammy Award. (wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Licht, as of 2025-06-26; allaboutjazz.com)

He also played with Shockabilly (the Kramer-led trio that preceded Bongwater on Rough Trade) and B.A.L.L. (the Shimmy Disc supergroup with Kramer (2) and Dave Rick). He has performed with When People Were Shorter and Lived Near the Water. (wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Licht, as of 2025-06-26)

Alan Licht — family connection?

A web search for a family connection between David Licht and guitarist Alan Licht returned an obituary for a David Frederick Licht (born 1963, Pigeon, Michigan; died December 28, 2023, Kissimmee, Florida) who was survived by a brother named Alan Licht — but that is a different person (a pest-control worker and deer hunter with no music career). (ransfordfuneralhome.com)

Guitarist Alan Licht (born June 6, 1968, New Jersey) mentions a brother in interviews who liked Dixieland jazz, but does not name him; no source connects that brother to the Bongwater drummer. (paristransatlantic.com) The two musicians share a surname but are believed to be unrelated. No source confirms or denies a family connection between David Licht (Bongwater drummer, b. 1954 Detroit) and Alan Licht (guitarist, b. 1968 New Jersey). This should be treated as unresolved pending a direct source.
