Overview

Ian Craig Marsh is a Sheffield-born musician and composer (b. 11 November 1956) who was a founding member of The Human League, contributing writing and synthesizer work to their first two albums before leaving in 1980. He departed alongside Martyn Ware, initially forming The Future (3) with Adi Newton of Clock DVA, then establishing the British Electric Foundation as a production vehicle and Heaven 17 as the performing act. The Virgin catalogue dominates his output of over a thousand credits, with Ware his closest collaborator across all three formations and Glenn Gregory a consistent presence through the Heaven 17 years. He gradually withdrew from Heaven 17’s activities around 2006, and in 2008 Ware publicly confirmed he was no longer an active member.
