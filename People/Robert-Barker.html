Overview

Robert Barker is a drummer and percussionist who played with UK new wave act Cuddly Toys during the group’s early-1980s run on Fresh Records (14), sharing credits with core collaborators Sean Purcell, Terry Noakes, David Kovacevic, S. Paul Wilson, and Alan Barson. His Discogs catalogue is small — three releases in the 1980s decade — but he reappears in the 2010s working in an afrobeat and funk direction, with credits alongside Big Mean Sound Machine, Bobby Spellman, and Alicia Aubin on Blank Slate and Peace & Rhythm.
