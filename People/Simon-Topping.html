Simon Topping

Vocalist, trumpeter, percussionist, and songwriter. Manchester. Discogs artist ID: 23692. Name variations on Discogs: S. Topping, Topping, Tupping.

Active continuously from 1979 through the 2010s, Topping is best known as an original member of A Certain Ratio and as a co-founder of Quando Quango and T-Coy. He was a significant node in the Manchester post-punk-to-dance transition centred on Factory Records and the Haçienda milieu.

Biography

Early ACR: 1979–1982

Topping was a founding performer in A Certain Ratio, appearing on the group’s debut single “All Night Party” (Factory, 1979, FAC 5), produced by Martin Zero (i.e. Martin Hannett). The same core lineup — Topping, Jez Kerr, Martin Moscrop, Peter Terrell, and Donald Johnson — appears on the mini-LP The Graveyard And The Ballroom (1980), a cassette-only release that mixed a studio side produced by Hannett with a live side mixed by Tony Wilson and Jeff Hooper. Topping is credited as a co-writer on the studio tracks alongside all five members.

The second ACR studio record, Flight (1980), also bears his Performer credit, again produced by Hannett. By Sextet (1982) — the ACR album that added vocalist Martha Tilson and completed the group’s move toward a more groove-based funk sound — Topping is listed as Musician alongside the rest of the band, with the record produced collectively.

Production and side work: 1981

While still in ACR’s orbit, Topping produced Swamp Children’s debut single Little Voices (Factory, FAC 49, 1981). This is his sole confirmed production credit outside his own groups in the Discogs data.

Quando Quango: 1982–1985

Topping co-founded Quando Quango with Mike Pickering, Gonnie Rietveld, Donald Johnson, Barry Johnson (8), Derek Johnson, and Reinier Rietveld. The group put out a string of Factory and Factory Benelux singles: Go Exciting / Tingle (1982), Love Tempo (1983, Factory Benelux — became a cult item and charted in Belgium), 2 From Quando (1984), Atom Rock (New York Remix) (1985), and Pigs + Battleships (1985).

The personnel on these records is a who’s-who of the Manchester/Factory axis: 2 From Quando features Johnny Marr on guitar, Vini Reilly on uncredited guitar, Bernard Sumner as uncredited producer, and Donald Johnson and Be Music producing — Topping plays percussion and trumpet. Pigs + Battleships brings in Andy Connell, Vini Reilly, and NYC DJ Mark Kamins as producer, with Beverly McDonald on vocals. The Latin Rascals did uncredited edits.

Theoretically Chinese (1985, Les Disques Du Crépuscule / Normal) — credited to Winston Tong — features Topping on percussion alongside Stephen Morris, Jah Wobble, Alan Rankine, and Dave Formula.

Quando Quango released a US-market album Genius (1985, Pow Wow Records).

Durutti Column guest: 1983

Topping appears on The Durutti Column’s Another Setting (Factory, 1983), playing trumpet. The album was produced by Vini Reilly and Chris Nagle.

T-Coy: 1987–1988

T-Coy is a trio of Topping, Mike Pickering, and Ritchie Close. All three releases on Deconstruction Records carry the songwriting trio credit Pickering/Topping/Close:

Cariño is one of the early British house crossover records. T-Coy is also registered on Discogs as aliases Frequency 9 and Masters Of Acid, suggesting the same trio operated under multiple names in the late-1980s dance market.

The group included Gerald Simpson (A Guy Called Gerald) in the broader T-Coy / Annette lineup context — Simpson appears in the Discogs member list for Annette, a related project with the same core (Topping, Pickering, Close, Simpson).

ACR returns and later work

Topping’s writing and performance credits continue to appear on ACR releases into the early 1990s:

  • Here Comes Everybody (1985, Factory / Virgin, France) — percussion
  • The Old & The New (1986, Factory Canada) — written-by
  • Force (1986, Factory Records Australasia) — written-by
  • Live In America (1987, Dojo) — written-by
  • The Guitar And Other Machines (1990, Factory Australia) — written-by, performer
  • Looking For A Certain Ratio… (1994, Creation Records) — written-by

He appears in the 2006 LTM documentary Shadowplayers (Factory Records & Manchester Post-Punk 1978–81) as an interviewee.

In 2016 he played bass and vocals on Section 25’s Alfresco (Factory Benelux), marking him as an active participant in the revived Factory Benelux ecosystem.

The 2018 ACR box set acr:set (Mute Records) includes Topping as Performer on tracks 1, 3–5, and 12–14, placing him back in the recording lineup alongside the core band.

He also appears on Renaissance. (2020, Edsel Records) credited as Additional Musician.
