Ullrich Wangenheim

Discogs ID: 68797 Born: 21 November 1973, Munich, Germany Based: Weilheim, Bavaria, Germany Instruments: Tenor saxophone, bass clarinet, alto saxophone, alto flute, concert flute Name variations: Ulrich Wangenheim, Ulli Wangenheim, Uli Wangenheim, Wangenheim

German woodwind multi-instrumentalist, best known as a core member of Tied & Tickled Trio since their formation in 1994. A recurring presence in the Weilheim scene, he has contributed bass clarinet, flute, or saxophone to releases by The Notwist, 13 & God, and numerous Bavarian jazz and chamber ensembles across three decades.

Biography

Early Activity (1994–1998)

Wangenheim is from Munich but became embedded in the Weilheim underground in the mid-1990s, when he joined the newly formed Tied & Tickled Trio alongside Markus Acher, Micha Acher, Christoph Brandner, Johannes Enders, and Andreas Gerth. The group debuted with a self-titled CD on Kollaps/Payola (1997), a record that established their future-jazz and dub aesthetic; Wangenheim was not listed in the credits on that debut — the earliest confirmed Discogs credit under his name on a TTT release is EA1 EA2 (1999). A 1996 live recording on Edition Collage documents his flute and alto saxophone playing in what appears to be a chamber or jazz context.

The Notwist — Neon Golden and Vertigo Days

Wangenheim appears as a guest musician on two of The Notwist’s landmark albums, more than a decade apart.

Neon Golden (2002) — Credited as “Guest, Bass Clarinet, Tenor Saxophone, Alto Saxophone.” The album also featured guest saxophonist Johannes Enders (tenor saxophone); both were drawn from the Weilheim circle. Neon Golden was released on City Slang / Big Store / Virgin and became the band’s most acclaimed work.

Vertigo Days (2021, Morr Music) — Credited for “Alto Flute, Clarinet, Bass Clarinet.” The album expanded The Notwist’s scope considerably with collaborators including Saya (of TENNISCOATS), Angel Bat Dawid, Ben Lamar Gay, and Juana Molina. A second Weilheim woodwind player, Theresa Loibl (clarinet, bass clarinet), also appears on this record.

13 & God (2005)

Wangenheim contributed clarinet and flute to the debut album by 13 & God, the collaborative project between The Notwist and Anticon collective members. His parts appear on tracks A1 (“Low Heaven”) and B2 (“Soft Atlas”). The album was released on Alien Transistor and Anticon.
