Andrew Lauder

Discogs ID: 1477914. UK record executive. Born 22 May 1947; died 26 November 2025, Seillans, France.

Overview

Originally an A&R man at the British divisions of Liberty Records and United Artists Records, where he championed American avant-garde and psychedelic acts for UK release. Co-founded Radar Records in 1977 with Martin Davis, funded and distributed by WEA. When Radar collapsed at end of 1979, co-founded F-Beat Records with Jake Riviera, Elvis Costello, and Nick Lowe.

Subsequently co-founded Demon Records and founded Edsel Records (a reissue label) and Silvertone Records. One of the key figures in shaping UK independent and semi-independent label culture through the post-punk era.
