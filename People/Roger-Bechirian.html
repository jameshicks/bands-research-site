Roger Bechirian

Discogs ID: 206137
Aliases: Count Freda, Soldier (3)

British record producer and engineer. Discogs realname: Roger Béchirian. Associated with the late-1970s new wave scene centred around Stiff Records, Radar Records, and Sire Records. Produced all three of the Undertones’ Sire Records-era albums (1979–1981), and continued with the band through several Ardeck singles including “All Wrapped Up” (1983). Also produced Jumpin’ In The Night (1979, Flamin’ Groovies, Sire), Elvis Costello & The Attractions’ East Side Story (1981, A&M) and associated singles, Squeeze’s East Side Story (same session — Bechirian was the production linchpin for that LP alongside Nick Lowe), and The Photos (Epic, 1980). His Stiff Records credits include engineering on Lene Lovich’s Stateless (1978) and Richard Hell’s Blank Generation orbit. By the mid-1980s he was working with The Monkees (Pool It!, Rhino, 1987) and other American acts.
