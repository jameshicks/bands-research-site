Andy Moor (2)

Discogs ID: 161031 Real name: Andrew Moor Dutch guitarist, member of The Ex (joined 1990). Also credited as Andy Ex, Andy Ex.

Overview

Post-punk/improvisation guitarist based in the Netherlands. Joined The Ex in 1990 and has been a core member since. Also a member of free improv quartet Lean Left (with Terrie Ex, Ken Vandermark, Paal Nilssen-Love). Earlier member of Scottish post-punk band Dog Faced Hermans.
