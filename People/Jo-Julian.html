Jo Julian

Real name: Joseph Richard Julian. Engineer, producer, multi-instrumentalist. Discogs artist ID: 261099. Member of Berlin.

Name variations in credits: Jo Jullian, Joe Julian, Joseph Julian, Joseph Julian (J.J.), Julian.

Overview

Jo Julian is an engineer and producer active from the late 1970s through at least the 2000s. His credit history cuts across post-punk, new wave, hard rock, and synth-pop, with a dense cluster of work in 1979–1982. He is listed as a member of the US synth-pop band Berlin, for whom he produced and engineered their first two releases and also contributed synthesizer, vocals, and songwriting — making him a key figure in Berlin’s early formation. Separate from Berlin, he engineered and mixed key releases for Blurt on Armageddon Records, produced the early Oingo Boingo EP, worked with Adam And The Ants before their commercial breakthrough, and later engineered and remixed releases for the NWOBHM band Samson (3).

His career bridges the LA synth-pop scene of 1979–1980 and the UK independent post-punk scene of 1981, an unusual pairing that reflects either transatlantic movement or the timing of his departure from Berlin.

Key Projects

Berlin (1979–1980)

Julian’s most substantial performing/songwriting role. He produced, engineered, and played synthesizer and sang on Berlin’s first two releases: the A Matter Of Time EP (1979, Renegade Records, US) and the Information EP (1980, Vinyl (4), Germany). He co-wrote multiple tracks alongside John Crawford, Chris Ruiz-Valesco, and Toni Childs; Terri Nunn sang on A Matter Of Time. His departure predated Berlin’s commercial breakthrough with Pleasure Victim (1982) and Take My Breath Away (1986).

Adam And The Ants (1978)

Co-produced and played piano on Young Parisians (Decca, 1978), an early Adam And The Ants single predating the Malcolm McLaren/Bow Wow Wow-era lineup. Personnel included Adam Ant, Matthew Ashman, Andy Warren, and Dave Barbarossa. Julian’s association with Adam Ant persisted for decades: he later produced the Antmusic compilation (1994, Arcade) and played piano on the Antbox box set (2000, Columbia).

Armageddon Records cluster (1981)

Three credits on Armageddon Records in a single year:

  • Mixed Blurt’s In Berlin (live recordings from West Berlin)
  • Co-produced and engineered Danny Adler’s Gusha-Gusha Music
  • Engineered Robyn Hitchcock’s solo debut Black Snake Diamond Röle

This clustering suggests a working relationship with the label or its recording environment during 1981.

Oingo Boingo (1980)

Produced two of the four tracks on the self-titled Oingo Boingo debut EP (I.R.S. Records, 1980) — tracks A1 and A2. Michael Boshears produced the other two. Julian is also credited on the companion Oingo Boingo Dance Record (1980, I.R.S.).

Samson (1982–1986)

Produced and engineered Before The Storm (Polydor, 1982) for the NWOBHM band Samson (3), and later engineered and remixed Head Tactics (Capitol, 1986), the album featuring Bruce Dickinson. Multiple other Polydor singles from 1982 carry Julian as producer/engineer.
