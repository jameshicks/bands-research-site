Jørgen Træen

Norwegian recording engineer, mixer, mastering engineer, producer, and musician. Discogs artist ID: 97329. Real name: Jørgen Træen. Alias: Sir Dupermann. Based in Bergen; operates Luftgrotten studio (sometimes credited as “Duper Luftgrotten”).

Overview

One of the most frequently recurring production figures across Hubro’s catalog. Credited as engineer, mixer, or mastering engineer on 71 Hubro releases, and as producer or co-producer on 31. Works alongside Andreas Meland (label manager / executive producer). Also contributes modular synthesizer, electronics, lap steel guitar, and other instruments as a performer on select recordings (e.g., Strandebarm by Stein Urheim; Krympende Klode (2021) with Stein Urheim).

He is also a founding member of Møster! and has group affiliations with Cosmolodic Orchestra, Kaptein Kaliber, House Of Hiss, The Golden Serenades, Ullan Gensa, and Vestnorsk Jazzensemble.
