Jherek Bischoff

Musician, arranger, composer. Real name: Jherek Brandon Bischoff. Discogs ID: 422428. Also records as “Ribbons.”

Overview

Seattle-based bassist, arranger, and composer with an unusually wide stylistic footprint. Bischoff is a member of Amanda Palmer & The Grand Theft Orchestra (double bass, guitar, vocals), Parenthetical Girls, Shudder To Think, and The Dead Science. Across the 8ft. Records catalog he appears on 19 releases in roles spanning double bass, guitar, vibraphone, prepared piano, arranger, conductor, and backing vocalist. He produced and arranged the inter-song instrumentals on Amanda Palmer’s There Will Be No Intermission (2019) and arranged the string quartet on Strung Out In Heaven: A Bowie String Quartet Tribute (2016, co-credited with Amanda Palmer). The 2016 Bowie EP is the one 8ft. Records release where Bischoff carries a primary-artist credit.
