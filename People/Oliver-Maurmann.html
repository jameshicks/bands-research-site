Swiss-German singer, songwriter, guitarist and sound engineer, born 2nd Dec. 1967 in Konstanz (Germany) and died 19th Jan. 2020 in Zürich (Switzerland). Overview

Oliver Maurmann (1967–2020) was a Swiss-German singer, songwriter, guitarist, and sound engineer who worked across indie rock, punk, rockabilly, and psychobilly. His principal vehicle was Die Aeronauten, though he also ran the underground alias Guz while embedded in the Hamburg scene, contributing to a wide circle of Hamburg-based artists alongside his Swiss collaborators. He owned Star Track Studio in Schaffhausen, which anchored much of his production and engineering work. With 114 credits spanning from 1985 through the 2010s, his catalogue ran primarily through Ritchie Records and L’Age D’Or, with Tom Etter and Dan Suter his most frequent collaborators.
