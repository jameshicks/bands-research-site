Franklin Bruno

Guitarist, pianist, songwriter; Los Angeles indie scene, active from the mid-1980s. Primary band: Nothing Painted Blue. Co-founder (with John Darnielle) of The Extra Glenns / The Extra Lens. Discogs artist ID: 263253. Website: http://franklinbruno.com/

Biography

Background and Early Work (1984–1990)

Franklin Bruno’s earliest documented recordings predate Nothing Painted Blue: Suggestion Box: Home Recordings 1984–1990 (Shrimper, SHR 09, 1990) is a solo cassette collecting home recordings from that six-year span. Credited instrumentation lists Bruno on drums, guitar, keyboards, piano, and vocals — a multi-instrumentalist working alone or with occasional collaborators (Kyle Brodie appears on drums on “Spring’s Newest Neutrals”; Don Munsil on “My Ex-Girlfriend’s Hairdo”). The cassette includes a cover of Grant McLennan’s “Newton Told Me.” It establishes Bruno’s DIY approach before the band formed.

Nothing Painted Blue — formed in Los Angeles in 1987, with Bruno (guitar, vocals) and drummer Kyle Brodie as the constant core — released their debut LP A Baby, A Blanket, A Packet Of Seeds on Jupa Records in 1990. Earlier Bruno had been in a band called Born Leaders with Brodie.

Nothing Painted Blue Years (1990–1998)

The band signed to Cleveland’s Scat Records for most of their catalog, releasing six records between 1991 and 1998. Bruno’s undergraduate background in mathematics inflected the band’s identity: the name was sometimes stylized “N∅thing Painted Blue” (empty set notation), songs bore titles like “Indices And Surds” and “Epistemophilia,” and the final LP The Monte Carlo Method (1998) took its name from the computational algorithm.

Peter Hughes (2) (later primary bassist for The Mountain Goats) played bass on four NPB releases from 1994 to 1998. Joey Burns (later of Calexico) appeared on the 1992 and 1993 records. Kramer (2) produced Power Trips Down Lovers’ Lane (1993) on Kokopop, a sublabel of Shimmy-Disc.

A solo compilation, A Bedroom Community (Simple Machines, 1995), is credited to Franklin Bruno alone — home recordings mixed by Bruno and Bob Durkee, covering most of side A–B with Bruno’s own compositions.

The Extra Glenns / Extra Lens (1993–2010)

Bruno co-founded The Extra Glenns with John Darnielle in 1993. In early credits both used aliases: Bruno as Glenn B, Darnielle as Glenn D. Their first release, Infidelity (7”, Harriet Records, 1993), was engineered by Bob Durkee. Through the 1990s the duo built a reputation as “something of an underground legend” (Discogs profile) recording tracks for singles and compilations while both concentrated primarily on their main projects.

A 2000 San Francisco Noise Pop Festival show — reportedly drawing fans who traveled cross-country specifically for it — convinced them to move ahead with a full-length. Martial Arts Weekend (Absolutely Kosher Records, 2002) followed: 12 tracks, co-written, with Darnielle and Bruno playing guitar and vocals together, backed by Daniel Brodo on double bass. The title sequence “Going To Morocco / Michigan / Marrakesh” carried Darnielle’s “Going To…” song convention into the Extra Glenns framework.

They returned in 2010 under the renamed Extra Lens for Undercard (Merge Records, MRG406). Bruno co-wrote, played instruments, and mixed; Jon Wurster (Superchunk/Mountain Goats) and Matt Houser contributed percussion on specific tracks; Jean Cook contributed strings. The album completes a deliberate trilogy of “Landscape” titles started on Infidelity: Malevolent Cityscape X → Malevolent Seascape Y → Ambivalent Landscape Z.

See the full article at The Extra Lens for complete track-by-track credits.

The Human Hearts (2006–2012)

The Human Hearts (Discogs ID: 1933779) is a project based around Bruno’s songwriting with a rotating cast, linked to his solo website. Members include Brandon Kalber, Matt Houser, and Pete Galub (Discogs ID: 802430). Their Discogs-catalogued releases are:

  • Civics (2006, Tight Ship label) — LP
  • Another (2012, Shrimper, SHR 158 area) — LP; extensive guest credits including Peter Hughes (2) (electric bass on six tracks), Jenny Toomey (vocals), Laura Cantrell (vocals), Mitch Rackin (recorded, mixed), Greg McMullen (pedal steel), John Wriggle (trombone, arranged), and a horn section.

Wckr Spgt and Other Collaborations

Bruno is a member of Wckr Spgt, the Claremont/Inland Empire lo-fi collective formed 1981 centered on Shrimper. He shares that membership with Kyle Brodie (also NPB’s drummer), Mark Givens, Joel Huschle, and David J. Carpenter.

Bruno appears as a session performer on several Shrimper-network releases, including the Dennis Callaci & Simon Joyner collaboration New Secrets (Shrimper, 2013), which also featured Kevin Morby and Jarvis Taveniere.

He contributed liner notes to the Wckr Spgt-adjacent Shrimper release 2 Steps From The Middle Ages (Omnivore Recordings, 2017).

Tsunami (6) (Arlington, Virginia)

Bruno is listed as a member of Tsunami (6), the DC-area indie rock band formed in 1990 by Jenny Toomey and Kristin Thomson. Tsunami were based in Arlington, Virginia, and released on Simple Machines (the label Toomey and Thomson co-ran). The connection is notable given the broader DC/LA indie network; Bruno’s A Bedroom Community (1995) was also released on Simple Machines.
