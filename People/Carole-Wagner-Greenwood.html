Carole Wagner Greenwood

Discogs ID: 2723257 Scene: Washington DC Active (documented): 2009–2010

Multi-instrumentalist and vocalist; co-member of The Beatings (5) with Stewart Lupton. The only Discogs credits for her are within The Beatings (5), where she played an unusually wide range of instruments across both releases.

Overview

Carole Wagner Greenwood’s documented musical life centers on The Beatings (5), the late-period project of Jonathan Fire*Eater frontman Stewart Lupton. She is credited on both of the band’s releases and handled a broader range of roles than simply vocalist — including marimba, piano, xylophone, organ, electric upright bass, additional recordings (production work), and cover design on the debut EP.

Her DC scene connection is confirmed by the company she kept: The Beatings (5) also featured Brendan Canty (Fugazi) on additional recordings and Cynthia Connolly (DC photographer/designer, Dischord-associated) on banjo. These were figures drawn from Washington DC’s post-hardcore world, placing Greenwood squarely within that orbit even if she was not part of the earlier wave.

No activity before or after The Beatings (5) is documented on Discogs.
