Toby Driver

Discogs ID: 476398 Real name: Toby Driver Origin: Boston, MA → New York City, USA

American composer, multi-instrumentalist (guitar, bass, keys, clarinet, voice), and singer-songwriter. The central creative force behind Kayo Dot and its predecessor maudlin of the Well. One of the more prolific and idiosyncratic voices in avant-garde progressive rock, chamber metal, and experimental song. Active solo since 2005 alongside continuous group work.

maudlin of the Well

Formed in Boston c.1996; pioneering progressive metal/avant-rock band predating Kayo Dot. Driver was the primary composer and multi-instrumentalist. Key members included Greg Massi (guitar), Jason Byron (lyrics), Sam Gutterman (bass), Josh Seipp-Williams, Maria-Stella Fountoulakis, Jason Bitner. Released on Dark Symphonies. Dissolved c.2002 after two simultaneous studio albums (Bath and Leaving Your Body Map, both 2001). Reunion in 2009 produced Part The Second (self-released, 11 pressings — most-pressed motW title).

Kayo Dot

Successor to maudlin of the Well, based in New York City from 2003. Driver leads a large rotating cast of avant-rock and new-music players. Evolving sound across the catalog — early records darker and more doom-inflected, later records shifting toward synth-driven textures and electronics. Key labels: Tzadik (debut), Robotic Empire, Hydra Head Records, Antithetic Records, Ice Level Music, Flenser Records, Prophecy Productions. Most-pressed Kayo Dot titles: Blue Lambency Downward (2008, 8p), Dowsing Anemone With Copper Tongue (2006, 8p), Hubardo (2013, 8p).
