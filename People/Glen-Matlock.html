Glen Matlock

Born: 1956, London Role: Bass, vocals

Original bassist of the Sex Pistols (1975–1977), co-writer of much of their debut album Never Mind the Bollocks. After being fired from the Pistols in February 1977, formed Rich Kids with Midge Ure, Steve New, and Rusty Egan.
