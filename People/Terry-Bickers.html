Terry Bickers

English guitarist and songwriter, born 6 September 1965 in Fulham, London. Discogs artist ID: 270245.

Name variations in Discogs: Bickers, T. Bickers, Terry, Terry Bikers.

Overview

Terry Bickers is best known as the lead guitarist of The House Of Love (1986–1989), whose shimmery, ominous interlocking guitar work with vocalist Guy Chadwick drew immediate critical acclaim and is widely cited as an influence on Catherine Wheel, Ride, and Adorable. He departed the band in late 1989 and went on to form Levitation (2) (1990–1993), a London-based psychedelic/progressive rock act that was similarly praised but commercially elusive. After leaving Levitation in May 1993 he cycled through several lower-profile projects — Monkey 7, Cradle (3), a production sideline — before returning to the House of Love for their 2005 reunion album Days Run Away. A collaboration with Piotr Fijalkowski (Pete Fij, ex-Adorable) yielded several self-released singles in the 2010s. His most recent group, Terry Bickers & The Inner Spiral, released Keepers in 2025.

He was previously a member of Colenso Parade before joining the House of Love.

Era 1: Colenso Parade and The House Of Love (1984–1989)

Bickers joined Colenso Parade, a London group that released three singles on Goliath and Fire Records (1984–1986). He joined The House Of Love in 1986. The band released their debut material on Creation Records — the self-titled mini-LP (1987), the singles Shine On and Christine — before signing to Fontana. Their Relativity-distributed self-titled LP (1988, US; also UK Creation pressing) was produced by the band with engineer Pat Collier. The band were writing for Fontana when Bickers departed in late 1989, mid-sessions for what would become the Fontana LP (1990).

Era 2: Levitation (2) (1990–1993)

In 1990, Bickers formed Levitation (2) with Laurence O’Keefe (bass), Robert White (4) (keyboards), Christian Hayes (guitar), and David Francolini (drums). The band released on Rough Trade and Ultimate (UK) and Capitol Records (US), produced by Tim Smith and Mike Dignam on the main album Need For Not. Bickers played his final Levitation concert on 14 May 1993 and was replaced by Steve Ludwin.

Era 3: Post-Levitation projects (1994–2004)

Cradle (3) (mid-1990s)

Bickers formed Cradle (3), a UK neo-psychedelia/space rock/ethereal group, with Caroline Tree (vocals), Ian Mundwyler (guitar), Richard Barber (bass), and Pete Moon (drums). Discogs credits him on guitar and bass. Released on WEA and Ultimate.

Era 4: House of Love reunion and collaborations (2005–present)

Bickers returned to The House Of Love for Days Run Away (V2, 2005), co-writing music with Guy Chadwick and playing guitar and vocals. The reunion lineup was: Chadwick, Bickers, Pete Evans (5) (drums), Matt Jury (bass); recorded and mixed by Pat Collier.

Collaboration with Piotr Fijalkowski (Pete Fij)

Bickers collaborated with Piotr Fijalkowski (ex-Adorable) on a series of self-released singles under their joint name. Fijalkowski is credited as co-primary artist on all releases.
