Susanna Hoffs

American singer, guitarist, and actress. Born Susanna Lee Hoffs, January 17, 1959, Los Angeles, CA. Discogs artist ID: 58358. Married to director Jay Roach.

Co-founder, rhythm guitarist, and lead vocalist of The Bangles. Extensive solo career running alongside and between Bangles activity. Member of the Paisley Underground super-session project Rainy Day and the Austin Powers comedy-band side project Ming Tea.

Biography

Early Career and The Bangles (1981–1989)

Susanna Hoffs co-founded The Bangles in Los Angeles in 1981 alongside Vicki Peterson and Debbi Peterson. The band initially performed as The Bangs; their earliest EP, The Bangs (1982, Faulty Products), was produced by Craig Leon and featured Hoffs on rhythm guitar and co-writing credits on multiple tracks alongside Annette Zilinskas on bass.

The band signed to Columbia Records and released All Over The Place (1984), produced by David Kahne, who would become the group’s primary producer through the decade. Hoffs contributed guitar, vocals, and songwriting across the album. The follow-up, Different Light (1985/1986), broke them internationally — reaching #2 on the US Billboard 200, with Kahne again producing. Hoffs co-wrote “Walking Down Your Street” and “If She Knew What She Wants” on that record. The album featured engineers Tchad Blake and Peggy McLeonard, with mixing by David Leonard.

During this same period, Hoffs participated in Rainy Day (1984), the Paisley Underground collective project featuring members of the Dream Syndicate, the Rain Parade, and The Three O’Clock, produced by David Roback.

The Bangles’ third album, Everything (1988), produced by Davitt Sigerson (not Kahne), contained “Eternal Flame” (co-written by Hoffs, Tom Kelly, and Billy Steinberg) — their second US #1 single. Session personnel on the album included David Lindley (lute, dobro, bouzouki), John Philip Shenale (keyboards), and Paulinho Da Costa (percussion).

The Bangles dissolved in 1989 following tensions around Hoffs’s increasingly prominent solo profile.

First Solo Phase (1990–1998)

Hoffs’s first solo record, When You’re A Boy (Columbia, 1991), was produced by David Kahne and featured a notable guest roster: Juliana Hatfield on backing vocals, alongside Donovan Leitch, Dan Navarro, and Rusty Anderson on guitar. The album yielded the single “My Side Of The Bed” (1990 European single, CBS).

Her self-titled 1996 album (Susanna Hoffs, London Records) was co-produced by Hoffs with Jack Joseph Puig, David Baerwald, and David Kitay. This marked a shift toward Hoffs taking a full producer role on her own material.

The Bangles Reunion and Doll Revolution (1998–2004)

The Bangles reunited in the late 1990s. Their reunion album Doll Revolution (2003, Down Kiddie Records) was self-produced by the band with Brad Wood as co-producer and engineer. Notably, Dave Grohl appeared in the “Bangles Boys Choir” ensemble credit alongside Peter Holsapple. Hoffs contributed acoustic and electric guitar, vocals, and co-songwriting. Down Kiddie Records — the band’s own imprint — handled the release.

Under The Covers Trilogy with Matthew Sweet (2006–2013)

Hoffs co-produced and co-released three covers albums with Matthew Sweet under the Shout! Factory imprint: Under The Covers Vol. 1 (2006), Vol. 2 (2009), and Vol. 3 (2013). All three were produced by Sweet and Hoffs together, with Sweet also handling engineering and remixing. Liner notes on Vol. 1 were written by Van Dyke Parks. The series also appeared as a compilation set, Completely Under The Covers (Demon Records, 2015).

The two are also members of Ming Tea — the fictional band from the Austin Powers films, featuring Mike Myers and Matthew Sweet.

Later Solo Work (2012–present)

From 2012 onward, Hoffs released a series of solo singles and albums on the Baroque Folk imprint: Someday (2012), From Me To You (2012), Take Me With U (a Prince tribute, 2017), Bright Lights (2021), The Deep End (2023), and The Lost Record (2024).

The Deep End (2023) was produced by Peter Asher and engineered/mixed by Nathaniel Kunkel and others — marking her first collaboration with Asher, the legendary Beatles-era and James Taylor producer.

She also contributed guest backing vocals to Fanny’s Fanny Walked The Earth (2018, Blue Élan Records) and appeared as a guest on Elvis Costello’s tour (The Return Of The Spectacular Spinning Songbook!!!, 2012).
