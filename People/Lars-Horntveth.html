Lars Horntveth

Norwegian multi-instrumentalist, composer, and producer. Discogs artist ID: 168772. Founded Jaga Jazzist in Tønsberg, 1994, at age 14. Primary songwriter and leader throughout the band’s career.

Overview

Lars Horntveth plays saxophones (tenor, baritone, soprano), flute, bass clarinet, guitar, lap steel, keyboards, and mellotron. He handles production on later Jaga Jazzist records (from Starfire onward, co-producing with Jørgen Træen). His siblings Martin Horntveth and Line Horntveth are also core Jaga members.
