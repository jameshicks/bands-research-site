Biography

Tom Relleen was a London-based musician and producer, best known as one half of the experimental electronic duo Tomaga alongside drummer Valentina Magaletti. He also played bass in psychedelic rock band The Oscillation, co-founded Phonica Records (the London record shop), ran the All Time Low label, co-ran Negative Days, and served as UK representative for the Julie Tippex booking agency.

Relleen died on 23 August 2020 at the age of 42. His death was noted on his Discogs profile and prompted the posthumous release of several works-in-progress.

Instruments and Production Style

Relleen’s instrument palette across Tomaga was broad: synthesizer, bass guitar, organ, mbira, cello, drum machine, electronics (oscillators), vibraphone, and found objects (springs, gravel, phonography). He handled recording and production duties for Tomaga throughout their run, typically with Marta Salogni mixing and engineers such as Chris Potter or Lawrence Dunster mastering.

The Oscillation

Relleen played bass in The Oscillation, a London psychedelic/krautrock group fronted by Demian Castellanos. Their shared membership also led to a further project, Autotelia (Relleen and Castellanos as a duo), which released I on Rocket Recordings in 2020, mixed by Salogni.

Papivores and Autotelia

Beyond The Oscillation and Tomaga, Relleen was a member of:

  • Papivores (with Agathe Max) — released Death And Spring (Hands In The Dark, 2019); mixed by Salogni
  • Autotelia (with Demian Castellanos) — released I (Rocket Recordings, 2020); mixed by Salogni
  • Staran Wake (Discogs group ID: 12722726) — few details in DB

All Time Low and Negative Days

Relleen co-founded All Time Low (Discogs label ID: 197354), which released compilations and The Oscillation archival material. He also co-ran Negative Days (Discogs label ID: 780449), which issued Tomaga’s earlier EPs.

Posthumous Releases

Several Tomaga recordings appeared posthumously:

  • Extended Play 2 (2020, Not On Label) — released after his death
  • Intimate Immensity (2021, Hands In The Dark) — the final Tomaga LP; Relleen is credited as written by/performer/producer on nine of ten tracks
  • Extended Play 1&2 (2022, Hands In The Dark) — compilation collecting EPs 1 and 2
  • Music For Open Spaces (2023, Hands In The Dark) — posthumous collaborative LP with Marta Salogni; both credited as writer, performer, and producer
