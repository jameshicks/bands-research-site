Dominique Grimaud

French multi-instrumentalist (synthesizers, guitars, alto saxophone), composer, and visual artist. Discogs artist ID: 544904. Active from the mid-1970s to at least 2020. Also known by the alias Grimo.

Website: http://dominique-grimaud.fr

Overview

Dominique Grimaud occupies an unusual position in the French experimental underground: he is both a working musician and a visual artist/illustrator, and his credits across three decades reflect both modes. As a performer his core instrument is synthesizer — specifically Synthi AKS, EMS equipment, Moog, Korg, and various Prophet models — alongside guitar (including slide and “guitare horizontale”), alto saxophone, and electronics. As an artist he has contributed cover design, illustration, typography, and liner notes to releases he was often performing on simultaneously.

His career threads through three main contexts: the free/electronic duo Vidéo-Aventures with Monique Alba (1981–1990, the primary early vehicle); the art-rock/free-improv ensemble Camizole (1975 recordings, 1999 reissue, 2018 live document); and a sustained relationship with the Rennes experimental label In-Poly-Sons that runs from 1993 through 2020, spanning performer roles, solo releases (under the alias Grimo), and design/annotation work for other artists’ records.

A parallel strand is as a curator and annotator: he directed the Musiques Electroniques En France 1974–1984 compilation series (Gazul Records, 2007) and produced the Fall Of Saigon 1981–1984 reissue anthology (Musea/Replica, 2016), positioning himself as a chronicler of the French post-punk/electronic moment in which he was himself a participant.

Design & Visual Credits Summary

Grimaud’s visual/design work appears on releases as:

  • Cover art / illustration (e.g. Camera (In Focus) / Camera (Al Riparo) — shared with Monique Alba)
  • Booklet / sleeve design (Camizole Camizole, 1999)
  • Typography / lettrage (Rag-time, 2002)

The 1997 Camera reissue credits both Grimaud and Monique Alba for cover art, suggesting shared or collaborative visual work within the Vidéo-Aventures partnership.
