Duncan Kilburn

Discogs ID: 428957 Real name: Duncan Leslie James Kilburn Origin: UK

British saxophonist and keyboardist. Founding member of The Psychedelic Furs (1977–1981), appearing on their first two studio albums. Departed alongside Roger Morris after Talk Talk Talk (1981). Made one documented post-Furs session appearance on a Georgia II single (1982).

The Psychedelic Furs (1977–1981)

Kilburn was part of the original lineup that formed in London in 1977 around brothers Richard Butler and Tim Butler. He played saxophone on the band’s debut single and both of their early studio albums, contributing the distinctive horn textures that characterized the band’s post-punk / new wave sound.

”We Love You” / “Pulse” (Epic EPC 8005, 1979)

The band’s debut single. Kilburn on saxophone. Produced by Howard Thompson and Ian Taylor alongside the band.

Talk Talk Talk (Columbia NFC 37339, 1981)

Second studio LP and Kilburn’s final album with the band. His role expanded to horns and keyboards on this record. Also produced by Steve Lillywhite. Engineer: Phil Thornalley and Will Gosling.

“Pretty In Pink” was co-written by Kilburn (credited as co-composer) and later re-recorded for John Hughes’s 1986 film of the same name; that re-recorded version appears on compilations as a Written-By credit for Kilburn.

Post-Furs Session Work

Georgia II — “The Flag” / “Tunnel Vision” (PRT 7P 257, 1982)

After leaving the Psychedelic Furs, Kilburn played saxophone on a single by Georgia II (a UK act on PRT Records). Other contributors included Peter Hammill (backing vocals, guitar) and David Rhodes (guitar).
