Biography

Early Career (1996–2004)

Brian Borcherdt is a Toronto-based Canadian musician, producer, and engineer. His earliest documented Discogs credit is a 1996 compilation (Nervous Wreck) on d-pression, where he contributed lyrics, performance, and photography. By the late 1990s and early 2000s he was a member of By Divine Right, the Toronto indie rock group led by José Miguel Contreras and home at various points to Brendan Canning, Leslie Feist, and Loel Campbell. Borcherdt played vocals, guitar, and keyboards on By Divine Right’s Sweet Confusion (2004, Linus Entertainment / spinART Records).

In 2002 he released the solo album Moth under his own name on a self-released basis. Recorded by David Newfeld, it featured John Hall (10) on drums — both would remain long-term collaborators. Moth is his first primary-credited release in the Discogs DB.

Holy Fuck Formation and Career (2003–present)

Around 2003 Borcherdt co-founded Holy Fuck in Toronto with Graham Walsh. The band’s core concept — replicating the textures of electronic music using live traditional instruments, pedals, mixers, toy keyboards, and a 35mm film synchronizer, without laptops or pre-programmed tracks — proved highly distinctive. The lineup was initially fluid, drawing on a rotating cast that included Mike Bigelow, Loel Campbell, Matt Schulz, Glenn Milchem, Kevin Lynn, and Kevin Lynn (2); for Latin (2010) and onwards the band settled as a four-piece with Borcherdt, Walsh, Matt McQuaid, and Matt Schulz.

Key releases on Young Turks (a Beggars Group sublabel):

  • Holy Fuck (self-released, 2007) — debut EP/album, Canada
  • LP (Young Turks, 2007) — breakthrough full-length; mixed by David Newfeld and Eli Janney; recorded live by Craig James (2)
  • Latin (Young Turks, 2010) — settled four-piece lineup; self-produced; mastered by Mandy Parnell
  • Congrats (Last Gang Records / Innovative Leisure Records, 2016) — Borcherdt credited for design concept, mixing, recording, and performance
  • Deleter (Holy Ef Music, 2020) — self-released; Borcherdt and Walsh co-wrote, co-produced, and co-recorded all material

Earlier releases also include the Lovely Allen single (Young Turks, 2008), the Vicious Circles Vol. 1 split (Hand Drawn Dracula, 2011) featuring Black Moth Super Rainbow and Shugo Tokumaru, and Cassette NO1 (self-released, 2014). A 2017 single, Bird Brains (Innovative Leisure), brought in Noah Mintz for mastering and credits Borcherdt for additional recording.

Holy Fuck also collaborated with Canadian rapper Shad (5) on the Holy Shad project (2014).

Solo and Production Work (2008–present)

Alongside Holy Fuck, Borcherdt pursued a solo career under his own name. Coyotes (2008, Hand Drawn Dracula) featured production by José Miguel Contreras, harmony vocals from Audrey Bankley, and strings by Randy Lee (9). It was followed by Torches (& The Ward Colorado Demos) (2009, Hand Drawn Dracula). He also maintained the project The Remains Of Brian Borcherdt.

As a producer and engineer Borcherdt has worked beyond Holy Fuck’s own catalog:

  • Lights (5) – Siberia (2011, Last Gang Records) — producer on the majority of tracks alongside Graham Walsh
  • No Joy – Drool Sucker (2016, Topshelf Records) — producer, mixer, and engineer alongside Graham Walsh
  • Dusted (4) – Blackout Summer (2018, Polyvinyl Record Company) — producer and performer; features Anna Edwards-Borcherdt on guitar and backing vocals

Dusted (4) and Later Work (2012–present)

Borcherdt and Leon Taheny record as Dusted (4), a Canadian dream-pop / shoegaze project. Key releases: Total Dust (2012, Hand Drawn Dracula / Polyvinyl), Blackout Summer (2018, Polyvinyl), and III (2022, self-released). On III Borcherdt played nearly all instruments, co-produced, and co-handled layout alongside Jud Haynes and Joshua Mitchell (2); mixing was split between David Newfeld, Jarvis Taveniere, and Shuta Shinoda.

Under the alias Chipmunks On 16 Speed (Discogs ID: 7200564) he self-released Vol. 1 and Vol. 2 in 2024, both on Not On Label, with himself as sole producer.

He also contributed a 35mm film sync credit to Wintersleep’s 2018 release on Dine Alone Records, and appeared on a 2021 Sub Pop single credited to Lids alongside Jennifer Castle (as “Lis Are”), handling vocals, guitar, drone, and tape delay.
