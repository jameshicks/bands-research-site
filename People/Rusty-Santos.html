Overview

Rusty Santos is a New York City-based musician, producer, and engineer with over 240 Discogs credits across mixing, mastering, recording, and production work. His deepest associations are with the Animal Collective circle — Panda Bear (47 shared releases), Avey Tare (33), and Animal Collective as a group (32) rank as his three most frequent collaborators — while his broader catalogue also spans Beach House, Owen Pallett, and Born Ruffians. His label footprint is anchored by Domino, Paw Tracks, and Warp Records, covering roughly 2002 through the mid-2020s. He has released four solo albums and is currently a member of The Present.
