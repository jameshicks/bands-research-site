Overview

Marion Fudger is a UK musician who played bass, synthesizer, and backing vocals, active from the late 1970s into the 2020s. She was a member of The Art Attacks and Tagmemics, working primarily in punk, new wave, and experimental styles. Her closest collaborators across her catalogue are Edwin Pouncey and Steve Spear, each sharing 16 releases with her, along with John Haney (10). Overground Records is her dominant label relationship, accounting for four works spanning 1996–2023, with earlier appearances on Albatross, Fresh, Index, and Virgin from the original late-70s/early-80s period.
