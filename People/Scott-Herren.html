Scott Herren

American musician and producer. Born 1976, Atlanta, Georgia. Discogs IDs: 229415 (Scott Herren), 3722 (Prefuse 73). Real name: Guillermo Scott Herren. Also credited as Guillermo S. Herren.

Best known as Prefuse 73 (Discogs ID: 3722), a pioneering glitchy hip-hop/electronic project on Warp Records. Career spans from 1997 (as Delarosa & Asora on Schematic) through the mid-2000s Warp peak into later releases on Lex Records and self-releases. Key collaborator network spans indie rock, hip-hop, and experimental electronics.
