Bent Sæther

Discogs ID: 226490
Origin: Trondheim, Norway
Active: 1989–present
Aliases: Bob LeBad, Charlie Bob Bent, Onkel Fuzzbass, The Baron (2)

Norwegian musician; primary bassist, vocalist, and principal songwriter of Motorpsycho. Has operated as the band’s de facto producer since the mid-1990s, credited as producer or co-producer on nearly every Motorpsycho album from Blissard (1996) onward. Active across multiple side bands and production roles; 55+ credits on Rune Grammofon as performer and producer.

Overview

Bent Sæther co-founded Motorpsycho in Trondheim around 1989 alongside guitarist Hans Magnus Ryan (Snah) and drummer Håkon Gebhardt. The band became the centre of his creative output for the next three-plus decades, with Sæther responsible for the majority of songwriting, production, bass, and lead vocals across a catalogue stretching from early Norwegian noise-rock to prog-inflected psychedelia and orchestral rock. His production work began formally with Blissard (1996) — where he produced alongside Deathprod (Helge Sten) — and has since expanded across the Norwegian scene, including work for artists on Rune Grammofon, Crispin Glover Records, and several other Norwegian indie labels.

Outside Motorpsycho he has played bass in Spidergawd (with Motorpsycho drummer Kenneth Kapstad and guitarist Per Borten), Ryanbanden, Sugarfoot, The Jürg Mager Trio, and All The Dead Pilots (2). He also produced and performed on the Motorpsycho / Ståle Storløkken collaboration The Death Defying Unicorn (2012), recorded with the Trondheim Jazz Orchestra and Trondheimsolistene.
