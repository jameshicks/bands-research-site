Colin Newman

English vocalist, guitarist, producer, and record label owner. Real name: Colin John Newman. Born 16 September 1954, Salisbury, Wiltshire. Discogs artist ID: 13719. Co-founder and primary vocalist/guitarist of Wire. Also known as Klive (3) (alias). Co-founder and co-runner of Swim ~ with partner Malka Spigel.

Overview

Colin Newman is best known as the lead vocalist and a principal songwriter of Wire, the London post-punk group he co-founded in 1976 alongside Graham Lewis, Bruce Gilbert, and Robert Gotobed. Wire’s debut trilogy — Pink Flag (1977), Chairs Missing (1978), and 154 (1979), all produced by Mike Thorne for Harvest/Warner — established the band as foundational figures in post-punk and art rock minimalism.

Newman has sustained a significant parallel solo career since Wire’s first dissolution. His debut solo LP A-Z (Beggars Banquet, 1980) — produced by Mike Thorne with Robert Gotobed and Desmond Simmons — showed a more direct, song-centred approach than the experimental direction he would pursue with later Crammed Discs releases. He subsequently recorded for 4AD (Provisionally Entitled The Singing Fish, 1981; Not To, 1982) before relocating his solo work to the Belgian label Crammed Discs, where he produced himself and collaborated with Brussels-based orchestral and jazz musicians on Commercial Suicide (1986) and It Seems (1988).

His personal and creative partnership with Malka Spigel (formerly of Minimal Compact) — they married in 1986 — led to the formation of the Swim ~ label in 1993 and multiple collaborative projects: the duo Immersion (2), the quartet Githead, and the live project Spigel*Newman*Colin*Malka. Newman and Spigel have operated Swim ~ as an artist-run label in London ever since. He also has a credit under the group name Intens (Discogs ID: 37961) and appeared on the Blair Witch 2: Book Of Shadows soundtrack (Priority Records, 2000) as producer of one track alongside members of Elastica (2), Graham Lewis, and Robert Gotobed.

Wire’s 2000s–2020s output — all on the band’s own Pinkflag label — has seen Newman take on production and mixing duties in addition to performing, giving him full creative control across the band’s recent LPs.
