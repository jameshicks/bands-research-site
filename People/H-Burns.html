H-Burns

Oct 21, 20267 min read

H-Burns

French singer-songwriter; real name Renaud Brustlein. Discogs artist ID: 782702. Also credited under the alias “Renaud Brustlein” (Discogs artist ID: 1444487) for performer/production roles on his own records.

Biography

Origins and early recordings (2006–2009)

Renaud Brustlein began releasing music as H-Burns in France in the mid-2000s, working in a Americana-leaning strand of indie folk and country — a tradition with few French precedents. His debut, Songs From The Electric Sky (2006, Noise Digger), was engineered and mixed by collaborator Mathieu Plancher and mastered by Alexandre Potteck. The record established the sparse, guitar-centred sound he would carry through subsequent work.

The second album, How Strange It Is To Be Anything At All (2008, Boxson), brought in a core band: Antoine Pinet (guitar, mandolin, bass, backing vocals), Stéphane Milochevitch (drums, lap steel, banjo, guitar, backing vocals), Jonathan Morali (guitar, piano, saw, Hohner Pianet), Fabien Canetto (contrabass), and his brother François Brustlein (harmonica). The album was co-produced by Brustlein and Syd Matters — the French indie band whose frontman Jonathan Morali was a member — and recorded by Guilhem Granier, with mastering by Carl Saff in Chicago.

We Go Way Back (2009, Pbox) followed as a third album, with the core cast largely intact.

West Country Night and the Chris Bailey collaboration (2010–2011)

In 2010 H-Burns appeared on West Country Night - Session One, a split/compilation release (Not On Label) pairing him with Texas singer-songwriter Josh Pearson (of Lift to Experience), Texan singer Bosque Brown (real name Mara Lee Miller), Thousand, and Tom Cooney. The release was produced by Erwan Le Floch, mixed by Guillaume Chalencon and Brustlein, and mastered by Carl Saff.

The 2011 album Stranger (Vicious Circle (2)) is the most notable cross-national collaboration of his career: a co-billed record with Australian punk legend Chris Bailey, lead singer of The Saints (2). The album is credited jointly as “Chris Bailey & H-Burns” (with H-Burns listed as “H.Burns” on the pressing). Artwork was by the Toulouse design studio Brest Brest Brest. The Vicious Circle (2) label is a Toulouse-based independent.

Vietnam years — Steve Albini sessions and mainstream visibility (2012–2014)

Six Years (2012) and Off The Map (2013) were both released on Vietnam (2), a sublabel of Because Music. Off The Map was recorded by Steve Albini at Electrical Audio in Chicago, with mastering split between Albini and Bob Weston (Robert Weston). The record features a notably larger ensemble: Antoine Pinet and Patrice Coeytaux on drums; Jean-Pierre Maillard on bass; Jonathan Morali on organ and piano; brass players Nick Broste and Dave Max Crawford (trombones), Ellis Seiberling (trombone), and Sam Johnson (trumpet). Artwork again by Brest Brest Brest.

In 2014 H-Burns appeared on the collaborative live/session release Bertrand Belin + H-Burns + Jonathan Morali (Vietnam (2) / Because Music / Cinq 7), co-billed with French songwriter Bertrand Belin and Syd Matters keyboardist Jonathan Morali. The record was mixed by Morali and features Brest Brest Brest artwork.

A digital single, Opposite Way (2013), was released through Because Music directly.

Rob Schnapf period — US production (2015–2018)

Night Moves (2015, Vietnam (2)) and Kid We Own The Summer (2017, Vietnam (2) / Because Music) were both produced by Rob Schnapf — the Los Angeles producer known for his work with Beck and Elliott Smith. Greg Calbi (Greg Calbi) mastered both. Kid We Own The Summer features the fullest band credit of his career: Antoine Pinet (guitar, bass, piano, drums), Jean-Pierre Maillard (bass), Pablo Ramos (guitar, organ), Denis Clavaizolle (keyboards, piano, drum machine — also engineer/recorder), Yann Clavaizolle (drums, backing vocals), Alma Forrer (backing vocals), Raphaël Séguinier, and Bertrand Belin contributing strings, keyboards, and backing vocals. Schnapf mixed the album; Calbi mastered.

Midlife (2018, Vietnam (2) / Because Music) continued on the same label, with Noah Georgeson and Agathe Watremez in the credits.

Leonard Cohen tribute and later work (2021–2023)

Burns On The Wire (2021, Play Two / Warner Music France) is an album-length tribute to Leonard Cohen, covering songs across Cohen’s catalog. Most tracks are Cohen compositions; two covers step outside Cohen: Anna Marly’s “La Complainte du Partisan” (with co-writer Emmanuel D’Astier De La Vigerie) and a Dick Blakeslee song. Personnel included Antoine Pinet (drums, guitar, bass, piano), Raphaël Séguinier (percussion), The Stranger Quartet (strings and backing vocals), and David Mestre (recording and mixing). Rémy Poncet of Brest Brest Brest handled artwork. Mastered by Chab (2) (Antoine Chabert). H-Burns is credited as producer. The album moved him to a major-distribution deal (Warner Music France / Play Two).

Sunset Park (2023, Yotanka Records) is the most recent Discogs-documented album, on a different independent (Yotanka Records, parent label Idol).

Connections

  • Chris Bailey — co-billed Stranger (2011); Bailey is the lead singer and co-founder of The Saints (2), the Australian proto-punk band
  • Syd Matters — French indie band whose members (esp. Jonathan Morali) orbit H-Burns; co-produced 2008 album
  • Bertrand Belin — French songwriter; appeared on Kid We Own The Summer; co-billed 2014 live/session release
  • Steve Albini — recorded Off The Map at Electrical Audio, Chicago; Albini’s involvement marks H-Burns as one of a small cohort of French artists to record there
  • Rob Schnapf — US producer bringing Los Angeles indie-rock production to his mid-period records
  • Josh Pearson — Texas songwriter; shared West Country Night - Session One (2010)
  • Leonard Cohen — primary compositional source for Burns On The Wire (2021); most covered songwriter in H-Burns’s catalog
  • Brest Brest Brest — Toulouse design duo (Arnaud Jarsaillon + Rémy Poncet) who provided artwork across multiple releases; Rémy Poncet also records as Chevalrex / Rémy Chante
