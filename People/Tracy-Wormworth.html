Tracy Wormworth

Discogs ID: 327926
Instrument: Bass, Electric Bass
Groups: The Waitresses

American electric bassist. Best known as the primary bassist for The Waitresses, anchoring both their studio albums and EP (1982–1983). After The Waitresses dissolved, Wormworth worked steadily as a session and touring bassist across jazz, pop, R&B, and rock — most notably with Sting on his 1988 world tour and with The B-52’s on Good Stuff (1992) and their 2011 Athens live record. Her later session work spans Lena Horne, Des’ree, David Lee Roth, Rachel Z, The Family Stand, and Joan Osborne. In 1997 she appeared as a primary co-credited artist on the jazz album The Opening Round (Savant), an organ-combo record led by saxophonist Houston Person alongside drummer Bernard Purdie and organist Joey DeFrancesco.

Discogs name variations: Miss Tracy Wormworth, T. Wormwort, T. Wormworth, Tracey Wormworth, Tracy Ann Wornworth, Tracy Wormsworth, Tracy Wormword. These all resolve to the same Discogs profile (ID 327926) — not separate entries.
