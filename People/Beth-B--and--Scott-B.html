Beth B & Scott B

Filmmakers, artists. Active New York late 1970s–early 1980s. Beth B Discogs ID: 830747 (real name: Beth Billingsley).

Collaborative filmmaking duo associated with No Wave Cinema. Made The Offenders (1979/1980), a Super 8 punk noir in which Adele Bertei played the character “Laura.” Also the primary artists on Vortex (The Motion Picture Soundtrack) (Neutral Records, 1982), for which Bertei co-produced tracks A3 and B6 alongside Lydia Lunch, Richard Edson, and Kristian Hoffman.

Note: Beth B and Scott B later worked separately. Beth B continued as a solo filmmaker.

The Offenders (1980) — Cast & Credits

Directors / Screenplay: Scott B and Beth B
Producer: Scott B (source)
Format: Super 8, color
Running time: 100 minutes
Filming locations: Club 57 and various Lower East Side Manhattan locations
Premiere: March 6, 1980, Film Forum, New York
Distribution / Preservation: Digitally restored by the Museum of Modern Art via the Celeste Bartos Fund for Film Preservation

Production notes

Originally conceived as a 10-minute short. Produced as a serial: directors “scripted on Wednesday, cast and filmed on Thursday and Friday, processed the film and made posters over the weekend, edited on Monday, and screened the new sequences on Tuesday nights at Max’s [Kansas City].” Weekly audience admissions funded each subsequent episode. Also screened serially at the Mudd Club. Full version premiered at Film Forum, March 6, 1980. (source)
