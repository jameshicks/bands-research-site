Overview

Matt Eaton is a British musician, DJ, sound designer, and mastering engineer from Harrogate, North Yorkshire. A school friend of Rosie Cuckston, he moved to Birmingham with her in the late 1980s to study, and the two began performing together as Friends Of The Family — a project connected to what would become Pram. His recorded output runs from 1987 into the mid-2010s, weighted toward the 2000s and 2010s, working across indie rock, leftfield, and synth-pop territory. His most frequent collaborators — Grandmaster Gareth (17 shared credits), Samuel Minnear, and Lucy and Hannah Baines — form a cluster centred on Birmingham’s leftfield indie scene, with Misty’s Big Adventure at its hub.
