Roger Eno

British musician, composer, and pianist; younger brother of Brian Eno. Discogs artist ID: 23859.

Overview

A more conventionally lyrical composer than his brother, Roger Eno works primarily with piano and chamber ensembles. Appeared on Brian Eno’s Apollo: Atmospheres And Soundtracks (1983, EG) as the third member (with Daniel Lanois) — his melodic keyboard lines give the album much of its plaintive quality. Extensive solo career on the EG and later All Saints and other labels. Long-running duo with Brian Eno that produced Mixing Colours (2020, Deutsche Grammophon).
