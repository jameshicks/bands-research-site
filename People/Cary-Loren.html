Cary Loren

Detroit-born artist, musician, and archivist. Discogs artist ID: 634759. Name variations: C. Loren, Carey Loren, Cary, Loren.

Co-founding member of Destroy All Monsters with Mike Kelley, Jim Shaw (4), and Niagara, active from the original 1973–1976 period through the band’s later rock-oriented incarnation and 1996 reunion. Unlike Kelley and Shaw, Loren remained with DAM through its duration rather than departing for CalArts. Also active in Monster Island (Discogs ID: 756550) and several other formations.
