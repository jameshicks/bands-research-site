Overview

John Doe (born John Nommensen Duchac, February 25, 1953, Decatur, Illinois) is a Los Angeles punk and roots-rock musician, best known as co-founder, bassist, vocalist, and primary songwriter of X (5). Before X he played bass in The Randoms (1977, Dangerhouse) and appeared in credits for Black Randy & The Metrosquad (wastebasket percussion on Pass The Dust, I Think I’m Bowie, 1979). X’s debut album Los Angeles (1980) was released on Slash Records and produced by Ray Manzarek; the band then moved to Elektra for a run of albums through the late 1980s. Alongside X, Doe has played in The Flesh Eaters (A Minute To Pray A Second To Die, 1981, Ruby Records), The Knitters (roots/folk supergroup, 1985), Divine Horsemen, and The Million Dollar Bashers. His solo career began with Meet John Doe (1990, DGC) and continued through albums on Yep Roc, Fat Possum, and other labels into the 2020s. Doe is also listed as an actor, poet, and occasional producer for other artists.

Note: Discogs splits his credits between two IDs — 158024 (the main “John Doe (2)” entry) and 1565899 (“John Duchac,” which lists all his band memberships). Both share the same realname. His wastebasket credits on Black Randy appear only under ID 1565899.
