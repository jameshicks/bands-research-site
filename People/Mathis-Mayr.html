Overview

Mathis Mayr is a German cellist born in 1972, active primarily in contemporary and experimental music. A member of Ensemble Mosaik, he has worked across styles ranging from contemporary classical and modern composition to indie rock and leftfield electronics. His catalogue is concentrated in the 2000s, with releases on Hausmusik and Kollaps as the main labels, and his tightest collaborative ties are with Michael Heilrath and Carl Oesterhelt. A single credit on Alien Transistor hints at crossover with Micha Acher’s Munich indie circle.
