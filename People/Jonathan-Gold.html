Jonathan Gold

Born: July 28, 1960, Los Angeles, CA
Died: July 21, 2018, Los Angeles, CA
Discogs ID: 852766
Alias: The Rack (2) (Discogs ID: 12705620 — no separate releases found)
Roles: Food critic, music journalist, cellist, record producer

Los Angeles food and music critic who in 2007 became the first food critic to win the Pulitzer Prize for Criticism. Before establishing himself as a writer, Gold was an active participant in the LA post-punk and experimental underground as a cellist and producer. He studied art and music at UCLA, which placed him squarely in the same milieu as the bands he would later cover.

Career Overview

Journalism

Gold began at LA Weekly in 1982 as a proofreader, where he met his future wife, editor Laurie Ochoa. He worked his way through the music section — initially writing about classical music, then hip-hop — before launching his restaurant column “Counter Intelligence” in 1986. The column moved to the Los Angeles Times (1990–1996) and eventually to Gourmet magazine in New York (from 1999). He returned to the LA Times for the final decade of his life. His work for Gourmet was twice shortlisted for the National Magazine Award. He also wrote music criticism for Blender, Spin, Rolling Stone, and Details.

He was a regular contributor to KCRW’s Good Food radio program.

Musical Activity

Gold’s musical activity is documented on Discogs primarily in the early-to-mid 1980s, when he was a practicing cellist embedded in the LA post-punk scene:

  • 1982 — co-produced Savage Republic’s debut LP Tragic Figures (on Independent Project Records) and played additional percussion on the track “Attempted Coup: Madagascar.”
  • 1985 — played cello on Gleaming Spires’ Welcoming A New Ice Age (Tabb Records).
  • 2007 — played cello on “The Box,” a track appearing in both the DVD and CD versions of Johanna Went’s Club Years retrospective (Soleilmoon Recordings).

The Went collaboration, twenty-plus years after his peak musical activity, indicates he maintained connections with the LA underground into the 2000s.

His alias The Rack (2) is registered in Discogs as a separate entry (ID: 12705620) pointing back to Jonathan Gold, but no releases have been issued under that name in the database.

Liner Notes / Critical Writing on Record

Gold contributed liner notes to several releases, extending his critical voice into the album format:

  • Chairs I Have Known (Jac Zinder, Catasonic Records, 1996)
  • Live At The Starwood Dec 3, 1980 (The Germs, Rhino Handmade, 2010)
  • Electronic Music From The Seventies And Eighties (Carl Stone, Unseen Worlds, 2016)
