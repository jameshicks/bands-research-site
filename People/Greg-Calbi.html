Aug 14, 20261 min read

Greg Calbi

Mastering engineer. Discogs ID: 244135.

Mastered Mission Of Burma’s The Sound The Speed The Light (2009) and Unsound (2012). Sterling Sound, New York. Long-tenured mastering engineer with credits across decades of rock and indie releases.

Also mastered Explosions In The Sky’s Take Care, Take Care, Take Care (2011) and The Wilderness (2016).

Mastered the entire Interpol catalog from Turn On The Bright Lights (2002) onward — 202 shared releases counting all pressings. The single most consistent technical collaborator in Interpol’s catalog.

Also mastered H-Burns’s Night Moves (2015) and Kid We Own The Summer (2017), both on Vietnam (2) / Because Music — produced by Rob Schnapf.
