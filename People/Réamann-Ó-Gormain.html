Réamann Ó Gormain

Guitarist and vocalist. Derry, Northern Ireland. Also known as Raymond Gorman. Discogs artist ID: 549292.

Overview

Réamann Ó Gormain (also credited under the Anglicised form Raymond Gorman, plus some two dozen spelling variants across both names) is a guitarist and vocalist from Derry, Northern Ireland. He is best known as lead guitarist of That Petrol Emotion from the band’s formation in 1985 through its dissolution in 1994, and again through the 2008–2010 reunion. On the earliest TPE material (Keen, 1985; Manic Pop Thrill, 1986) he also contributed keyboards and vocals.

Before TPE he played in Derry bands Bam Bam and The Calling and Derry Hitmakers; neither project has a Discogs presence.

In the mid-1990s he co-founded Wavewalkers (Discogs ID: 114601) alongside Damian O’Neill, Brendan Kelly, and drummer Kevin Sharkey (standing in for Ciaran McLaughlin). The project played roughly six gigs across London, Derry, and Paris but never released anything officially and disbanded without an official record.

In 2013 the four surviving members of TPE’s final lineup — Réamann, Damian O’Neill, Ciaran McLaughlin, and Brendan Kelly — reunited as The Everlasting Yeah. They released Anima Rising (2014, Infinite Thrill) and a second album Staying Cool Staying Free With The Everlasting Yeah (2024, Infinite Thrill). On Anima Rising he takes lead vocals on several tracks and is credited as “Raymond Gorman.”

An isolated non-TPE credit: he translated lyrics into Irish for The Young Gods’ self-titled 1987 debut on Product Inc., credited as “Reamann O’Gormain.”

Name Variations on Discogs

Discogs lists 24 name variations: the canonical Irish-language form Réamann Ó Gormain, the Anglicised Raymond Gorman, and variants including R. Gorman, R. O’Gormain, R. Ó Gormain, Reamann O’Gormain, Reámann Ó Gormain, Redmann O’Gormáin, and others. All resolve to Discogs artist ID 549292.
