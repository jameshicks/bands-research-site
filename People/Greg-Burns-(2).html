Greg Burns (2)

Bassist, pedal steel guitarist, photographer, and visual artist. Real name: Gregory Burns. Discogs artist ID: 419641. Name variations: G Burns, G. Burns, Gregory Burns.

Biography

Greg Burns is a Los Angeles-based multi-instrumentalist who first appeared on record in 1995 as bassist for Jasta 14, a Connecticut-based band that also featured guitarist Mick Barr (later of Ocrilim / Octis). That record, released on Centrifuge Recordings, was a five-person hardcore-adjacent outfit with no subsequent releases in the Discogs database.

By 2001 Burns was playing bass for Halifax Pier, a chamber-tinged indie ensemble whose sole release Put Your Gloves On And Wave came out on Temporary Residence Limited. The band included Nathan Salsburg on guitar and cello/violin players — an early sign of Burns’s pull toward acoustic textures alongside electric instrumentation.

The decisive chapter opened in 2003 when Burns co-founded Red Sparowes in Los Angeles alongside Bryant Clifford Meyer (of Isis (6)), Josh Graham (of Neurosis), Jeff Caxide, and Dana Berkowitz. Burns and Meyer are the only two members to appear on every Red Sparowes release across the band’s entire recorded history. Burns’s pedal steel playing became one of the defining sonic signatures of the band — a country-inflected, desert-quality timbre unusual for post-rock and the heavy underground, and unusual as a primary textural voice rather than an ornament.

Burns also took on engineering and visual roles within the band. On the 2005 split with Gregor Samsa (3) and on the As The Black Wind Withers… / Black Tar Prophecies Vol. I split with Grails (2006), he is credited as Recorded By for the Red Sparowes tracks — self-recording that sits alongside similar hands-on approaches from bandmates. By the final LP, The Fear Is Excruciating, But Therein Lies The Answer (2010), Burns is credited with Art Design and Photography By (under the name Gregory Burns) as well as bass, pedal steel, and vocals.

Alongside Red Sparowes, Burns was a member of MGR (also known as Mustard Gas and Roses), the experimental/drone project founded by Mike Gallagher (2) and also featuring Josh Graham. He contributed pedal steel guitar to MGR’s Nova Lux (2006, Viva Hate Records).

In 2008, Burns and Josh Graham released Requiem as The (Fallen) Black Deer, a duo ambient/drone piece put out by Latitudes (UK), with Burns credited for Written-By and Instruments.

After Red Sparowes wound down, Burns joined Marriages — the trio he formed with Red Sparowes drummer Dave Clifford and guitarist/vocalist Emma Ruth Rundle. Marriages released Kitsune (2012, Sargent House) and Salome (2015, Sargent House), with Burns on bass guitar and synthesizer, and credited for layout and art design on both records. Burns was not part of Marriages’ final dissolution lineup per later credits.

As a session contributor, Burns played bass on two tracks of Imaad Wasif’s The Voidist (2009, Tee Pee Records) and pedal steel on two others, appearing alongside Dale Crover (Melvins). He also contributed pedal steel to Emma Ruth Rundle’s debut solo LP Some Heavy Ocean (2014, Sargent House), and photography to her 2024 demo collection Some Heavy Demos (Errant Child Recordings). A guest appearance is also credited on Godswounds’ Death To The Babyboomers (2014, Sonichimaera, Australia) alongside Dale Crover, Eugene Robinson (Oxbow), and Carla Kihlstedt.

Burns also appeared on the Triad three-way split EP (2006, Neurot Recordings) alongside Battle of Mice and Made Out Of Babies, contributing bass and guitar to the Red Sparowes’ two tracks, alongside the full touring lineup.
