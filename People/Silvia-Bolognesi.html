Silvia Bolognesi

Italian double bassist and composer, born in Siena. Discogs artist ID: 1862057. Active 2005–present. Name variations on record: “Bolognesi”, “S.Bolognesi”, “Silvia Bolgnesi”.

Website: silviabolognesimusic.com

Overview

Silvia Bolognesi is a double bassist, composer, and bandleader rooted in the Italian free jazz and improvised music scene. She leads or co-leads multiple working groups — the Silvia Bolognesi Open Combo, Silvia Bolognesi Living Quartet, Silvia Bolognesi - Almond Tree, and other ensembles — and has been an active collaborator within Europe’s broader free improvisation circuit since the mid-2000s. Her most internationally visible project is Hear In Now, the string trio she shares with American cellist Tomeka Reid and violinist-cellist Mazz Swift, released on Chicago’s International Anthem Recording Company. Her recorded output spans labels from Italy, France, Poland, the UK, and the United States, reflecting a career that moves freely across national contexts. She has recorded alongside major figures including Sabir Mateen, William Parker, Roscoe Mitchell, and Nicole Mitchell, and in 2024 led a live Gil Scott-Heron tribute concert released on her home label Fonterossa Records.

Biography

Early Work and Open Combo (2005–2010)

Bolognesi’s first major Discogs credit is Introducing Open Combo (2005, 33 Records, cat. 33JAZZ130), credited to the Silvia Bolognesi Open Combo. The album featured Sergio Casale (alto sax, flute), Rossano Emili (baritone sax, clarinet, bass clarinet), Cristiano Arcelli (tenor/soprano sax), Ian Da Preda (vibraphone, one composition), and Owen Hart Jr. (drums). Bolognesi composed eight of the nine tracks and played double bass throughout. A follow-up on the same label, What Was I Saying? (2008, 33 Records, cat. 33JAZZ173), was produced and arranged by Bolognesi herself.

During this period she worked extensively with Italian ensembles connected to Tiziano Tononi, Daniele Cavallanti, and Tiziana Ghiglioni, appearing on releases for Splasc(h) Records, Nu Bop Records, Reflexio, and Silta Records — a dense network of Italian creative music players.

In 2007 she appeared on Solo Fight (Drycastle Records, cat. DR-012) and contributed to Varii - featuring Tiziana Ghiglioni (Reflexio, 2007) in a credited composer and bassist role.

Rudi Records and International Connections (2010–2015)

Bolognesi became closely associated with Rudi Records (Italy), releasing several key albums on the label:

  • Holidays In Siena (2011, Rudi Records RRJ 1002) — a duo with free jazz saxophonist Sabir Mateen (alto/tenor sax, flute). Both are credited as composers. Recorded and mixed by Antonio Castiello.
  • Hear In Now (2012, Rudi Records RRJ 1007) — debut album of the trio Hear In Now, co-led with Tomeka Reid and Mazz Swift. Liner notes by William Parker; recorded partly by Alan Camlet and partly by Antonio Castiello; mixed and mastered by Antonio Castiello.
  • Suoni Dal Carcere (2013, Rudi Records RRM1001) — featuring vocalist Maria Pia De Vito.
  • Faces And Tales (2013, Rudi Records RRJ 1013).

In parallel she appeared on Terre Sommerse releases, including If Not (Omaggio A Mario Schiano) (2012) and Dialogo (2013) with Angelo Olivieri (2).

Her Chicago Sessions (2015, Fonterossa Records) was recorded in Chicago with Tomeka Reid (cello), drummer Mike Reed (2), trumpeter Russ Johnson, and vocalists Dee Alexander and Emiliano Nigi as guests. Recording by Griffin Rodriguez; mixed and mastered by Antonio Castiello. This was Bolognesi’s most direct point of contact with the Chicago creative music world outside Hear In Now.

Also in 2015: Ghost Trio (Setola Di Maiale, SM2890), a co-leader release with Ghost Trio (Ivano Nardi, drums), Marco Colonna (clarinet, bass clarinet, baritone sax, flute), and Bolognesi (double bass). Executive produced by Stefano Giust.

Hear In Now and International Anthem (2016–2019)

The Hear In Now album Not Living In Fear (2017, International Anthem Recording Company, IARC 0012) was the trio’s breakthrough release. Bolognesi played double bass; Tomeka Reid played cello; Mazz Swift played violin and voice. Vocalist Dee Alexander guested on track 7. Mixed by Griffin Rodriguez; cover art by Damon Locks.

In 2016 Bolognesi appeared on La Main Qui Cherche La Lumière (Improvising Beings, ib49) alongside Marco Colonna, Stefano Giust, and other European improvisers.

Her appearance on the Art Ensemble of Chicago’s We Are On The Edge (A 50th Anniversary Celebration) (2019, Pi Recordings (2), PI80) placed her in an extraordinary ensemble that included Roscoe Mitchell, Famoudou Don Moye, Nicole Mitchell, Hugh Ragin, Tomeka Reid, Junius Paul, Jaribu Shahid, Dudu Kouate, and Moor Mother, among many others.

Morning Patches (2019, Fundacja Słuchaj!, FSR 11|2019) was led by the Zlatko Kaučič Quintet featuring Michael Moore, Marco Colonna, Albert Cirera, and Bolognesi — a pan-European improvisation album.

Sustained Output and Pandemic-Era Projects (2020–2022)

Bolognesi maintained high output through 2020–2022. Highlights include:

  • Cats In The Kitchen (2021, We Insist! Records, CDWEIN15) — trio co-led with Alberto Braida (piano, composer) and Cristiano Calcagnile (drums). Recorded and mixed by Griffin Rodriguez.
  • EraorA (2021, Fonterossa Records, cat. 321) — duo with Marco Colonna (bass clarinet, clarinet). Eight improvisations.
  • The Call: For A New Life (2021, Felmay) — with Nexus (46), the ensemble of Tiziano Tononi, Daniele Cavallanti, Tony Cattano, Emanuele Parrini, and Luca Gusella.
  • Don’t Shush Me! (2022, Fonterossa Records, cat. 01/22).
  • Ruah (2022, Fonterossa Records, cat. 3/22).

Recent Work (2023–2026)

In 2023 Bolognesi appeared on the Art Ensemble of Chicago’s The Sixth Decade - From Paris To Paris (Live At Sons D’Hiver) (Rogueart, ROG-0122/0123), again alongside Roscoe Mitchell, Famoudou Don Moye, Nicole Mitchell, Tomeka Reid, Junius Paul, Jaribu Shahid, Griffin Rodriguez (mastered by), and Moor Mother.

She appeared on Smiles (2023, Cloud Recordings, CLD035), credited as “Silvia Bolgnesi” (name variation), in a single track — an Icy Demons release with a sprawling cast.

Timing Birds (2024, Astral Spirits, AS223) is a co-leader trio recording with Dudu Kouate (percussion, voice, ngoni, kalimba) and Griffin Rodriguez (electronics, voice). Bolognesi plays acoustic bass, voice, and percussion across the album — her most prominent co-leader credit in the US market since Hear In Now.

Is That Jazz? Celebrating Gil Scott Heron Live (2024, Fonterossa Records, cat. 1/25) credits Bolognesi as double bass, conductor, and arranger — she led the ensemble for a live tribute concert that included Brian Jackson (Gil Scott-Heron’s longtime collaborator), vocalist Eric Mingus, and others.

In 2025: Jungle Duke (Caligola, 2362) with Emanuele Parrini, Nick Mazzarella, Tony Cattano, Marta Viviani, Guglielmo Santimone, and Francesco Martinelli (liner notes). Across The Lines (Dodicilune, ED579) and Uncaged Bird (Dodicilune, ED572) also appeared.

In 2026: Actionreaction 2 (Slam Productions, SLAMCD2118) with Bolognesi on bass and composition credit.
