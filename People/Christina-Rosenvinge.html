Overview

Christina Rosenvinge (born Christina Rosenvinge Hepworth, Madrid, 29 May 1964) is a Spanish singer, songwriter, and producer whose career stretches from the early 1980s Madrid scene through sustained indie-pop output into the 2020s. She came up through Ella Y Los Neumáticos and Magia Blanca before meeting Alex De La Nuez and forming the duo Alex & Christina in 1988; she launched a solo career in 1991, initially fronting a band called Los Subterráneos and later recording under her own name. Her catalogue of around 18 selected releases clusters in the 2000s across Alternative Rock, Indie Pop, and Folk Rock, anchored by a long relationship with WEA and, from 2006, her own Søster Records imprint. Among her most striking credit relationships is an extended run of 16 shared releases with Steve Shelley (Sonic Youth), alongside close collaborations with Nacho Vegas and Xel Pereda that place her firmly within the Spanish indie underground.

Steve Shelley collaboration (Notes — web)

The 16 shared credits with Steve Shelley stem from an ongoing working relationship that began around 1999–2001 and produced at least five or six studio albums.

How they connected. The bridge was Lee Ranaldo (Sonic Youth guitarist), who produced Rosenvinge’s 1994 album Mi Pequeño Animal — the first documented point of contact between Rosenvinge and the Sonic Youth world. [Search result snippet, eltriunfodearciniegas.blogspot.com, via Google 2026-11-11] After that production link was established, Rosenvinge relocated to New York City in 1999. Wikipedia’s article on her (as of 2026-11-11) states explicitly: “Lee Ranaldo, guitarist from Sonic Youth, produced one of her albums. Subsequently, she relocated to New York City in 1999, where she recorded three English-language albums released by Smells Like Records.” [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christina_Rosenvinge] Steve Shelley founded Smells Like Records (Hoboken, NJ) in 1993 — the same label that became Rosenvinge’s home for her English-language output. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smells_Like_Records; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Shelley] The Ranaldo connection almost certainly opened the door to Shelley and his label.

A 2010 Cooperativa.cl piece about a Chilean tour adds texture: Shelley “personally requested” to accompany Rosenvinge, described her as part of his New York-based circle, and had performed with her New York band for some years at that point. [https://cooperativa.cl/noticias/magazine/musica/shows-en-vivo/baterista-de-sonic-youth-acompanara-a-christina-rosenvinge-en-chile/2010-03-30/092446.html]
