Brian Blade

American jazz drummer, born Shreveport, Louisiana, July 25, 1970. Discogs artist ID: 252331.

One of the most sought-after drummers of his generation. Long-running member of Kenny Garrett’s groups from the early 1990s (Black Hope, 1992) through Beyond the Wall (2006) and beyond. Also closely associated with Wayne Shorter and Joshua Redman.

Key Collaborations (from Garrett records)

  • Kenny Garrett: Black Hope (1992), Triology (1995), Pursuance: The Music of John Coltrane (1996), Songbook (1997), Beyond the Wall (2006) — 31 shared releases
  • Pat Metheny: appears on Pursuance alongside Metheny

Larry Grenadier Connections

  • Larry Grenadier — Driftwood (ECM, 2014) co-leader alongside Wolfgang Muthspiel; also shared Joshua Redman sessions (Compass, Nonesuch 2009)
