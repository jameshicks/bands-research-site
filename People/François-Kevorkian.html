François Kevorkian

DJ, remixer, producer, engineer. Born 1954, France; based in New York City from 1975. Known as “FK.”

Overview

One of the architects of the NYC underground dance sound. Resident DJ at Danceteria and later the Paradise Garage. His edits and remixes of early 1980s dance records shaped what became house music. Distinguished between a “DJ mix” (performance) and an “edit” (physical reworking of a master tape) — the latter being the thing he did for Russell.

Key Credits

  • Arthur Russell / Loose Joints — “Is It All Over My Face?” (West End Records, 1980): uncredited edit. The edit was the version that circulated, not the original mix.
  • Arthur Russell / Dinosaur L — “Go Bang! #5” (Sleeping Bag Records, 1982): remix. This became the canonical version; foundational for house and electronic DJs.
  • Arthur Russell / Loose Joints — “Snake Charmer” (Island Records, 1983): producer.
  • Wide Boy Awake — “Billy Hyena” (RCA, 1983): producer. Extended Dance Mix and Instrumental Mix; the band’s most club-oriented release.
  • Extensive work for West End Records, Prelude Records, and other NYC dance labels in the early 1980s
  • Later founded Strictly Rhythm Records (1989)
