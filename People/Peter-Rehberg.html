Peter Rehberg

Austrian-British electronic musician, composer, and label owner. Also records as Pita. Discogs artist ID: 60079. Pita alias Discogs ID: 11160. Born 29 June 1968, London. Died 23 July 2021.

Biography

Peter Rehberg was born in London on 29 June 1968 and was based in Vienna for most of his adult life. He is described on Discogs as an Austrian-British composer and performer of electronic music. His primary alias was Pita, under which most of his solo work was released.

Early Career and Mego (1994–2005)

Rehberg appeared on the first Mego release in 1995 — Fridge Trax (MEGO 001), a 12” co-credited with General Magic (Ramon Bauer and Andreas Pieper). He also appeared on the label’s debut promotional pack (Mego 001-003 Promo-Pack, 1995).

His first solo release as Pita was Fridge Trax (1995, Mego), followed by Seven Tons For Free (1996, MEGO 009) — a CD with Fennesz contributing “extra sound” to the closing track “Seven Tons Revised”. The early Pita sound was abstract electronics and noise, closely associated with the Mego glitch/experimental aesthetic. Get Out (1999, Mego mego 029) is the foundational solo LP — nine untitled tracks, mastered by Christoph Amann, with cover by Tina Frank. Get Down (2002, Mego MEGO 049) followed, an LP with cover photography by Russell Haswell.

Rehberg was also involved in Mego from the beginning as more than a roster artist — he co-ran the label alongside Ramon Bauer and others through its active period, which closed around 2005.

During this period he also recorded as part of several smaller groups:

  • Rehberg & Bauer (with Ramon Bauer): two releases — Faßt (1997, Touch) and an untitled release (1997, Korm Plastics)
  • Le Zappalot (with Ramon Bauer and Samy Birnbach): one release — Erika Goes To Leopoldau / Life Is Cheap (1995, Syntactic)
  • Pop (Product Of Power) (with Zbigniew Karkowski): Album (2001, Tochnit Aleph) and Work Hard Play Harder (2003, absurd)
  • Rosenberg (with Paul Douglas): Single (2001, Bottrop-Boy)
  • DACM (with Tujiko Noriko): Showroom Dummies (2002, Mego)

Editions Mego and Mature Work (2006–2021)

After Mego closed, Rehberg founded Editions Mego in 2006 and ran it until his death. The label became the central hub of the post-Mego laptop/experimental scene in Europe, releasing work by Fennesz, Hecker, Russell Haswell, Kevin Drumm, and many others, and also overseeing the Recollection GRM sublabel (a collaboration with INA-GRM reissuing historical French musique concrète).

His most significant project of this period was KTL with Stephen O’Malley, formed in 2006. KTL combined Rehberg’s laptop/electronics (oscillators, apps, drives) with O’Malley’s electric guitar drone; they released nine albums between 2006 and 2020, mostly on Editions Mego, plus the film score The Pyre: Versions Distilled To Stereo (2018, Shelter Press). They also contributed music to the French choreographer/director Gisèle Vienne’s theatrical productions over many years.

Rehberg’s solo work as Pita continued with:

  • Work For GV 2004–2008 (2008, Editions Mego EDITIONS mego 092) — music made for Gisèle Vienne productions, with text by Dennis Cooper (spoken by Cooper), action by Catherine Robbe-Grillet and Jonathan Capdevielle, photography/doll art by Vienne, and design by Stephen O’Malley
  • Get In (2016, Editions Mego) — credited as “Recorded By, Mixed By” (implying a performance-capture rather than pure studio construction)
  • Get On (2019, Editions Mego EMEGO 269) — his final solo album in his lifetime

He participated in Fenn O’Berg (the laptop trio with Fennesz and Jim O’Rourke), which released The Return Of Fenn O’Berg (Mego MEGO 054, 2002), then reunited on Editions Mego for Magic & Return (eMego 3154, 2009), In Stereo (eMEGO 103, 2010), Live In Japan Part One (eMEGO 110.1, 2010), Live In Japan Part Two (eMEGO 110.2, 2010), and In Hell (eMEGO141, 2012; 2×LP). Rehberg is credited as Performer across all releases. The trio ended with his death.

He was also a member of MIMEO (Music In Movement Electronic Orchestra), a large-ensemble laptop/improvisation collective also including Fennesz, Keith Rowe, Marcus Schmickler, Rafael Toral, and others; MIMEO released Queue (1998, GROB) and Electric Chair + Table (1999, GROB).

Later group projects included:

  • R/S (with Marcus Schmickler): One (Snow Mud Rain) (2007, Erstwhile Records) and USA (2011, Pan)
  • NPVR (with Nik Colk Void): 33 33 (2017, Editions Mego) and 33 34 (2024, Editions Mego) — the latter released posthumously
  • Shampoo Boy (with Christina Nemec and Christian Schachinger): Licht (2013, Blackest Ever Black) and Nebel / Nadel (2014, Substance Recordstore)
  • Peterlicker (with Christian Schachinger, Franz Hergovich, Gregor Weissegger): Last Slave (2010, Editions Mego) and Nicht (2011, Editions Mego)

He also collaborated with Gisèle Vienne on the theatre/music piece Éternelle Idole (2015, Shelter Press SP060) — credited with music and performance alongside O’Malley, Steve Moore, Bill Herzog, Daniel O’Sullivan, Jesse Sykes, and Randall Dunn — and made a digital release for Amplify 2020: Piece For Amplify2020 Quarantine Parts I & II (2020).

A split release Pita / Friedl appeared posthumously on Karlrecords (2022).

Rehberg was also credited under his real name “Peter Rehberg” on a Syntactic release (Erika Goes To Leopoldau / Life Is Cheap, 1995) as a performer.

Peter Rehberg died on 23 July 2021.
