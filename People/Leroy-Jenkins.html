Leroy Jenkins

Discogs ID: 282232
Born: March 11, 1932, Chicago, Illinois
Died: February 24, 2007, New York City

Violinist and composer. Co-founder of The Revolutionary Ensemble (1971–1977, 2004) alongside Norris Jones (bass) and Jerome Cooper (drums/piano). Also associated with the Association for the Advancement of Creative Musicians (AACM).

Additional Credits (from James Emery research)

  • Organized For Players Only (1975, JCOA Records) — a large-ensemble recording featuring James Emery on guitar, Anthony Braxton (contrabass clarinet), Dave Holland, Dewey Redman, Sirone, Jerome Cooper, Wadada Leo Smith, Joseph Bowie, and many others. Jenkins was composer, conductor, and producer.
  • Guest violin on James Emery’s Artlife (1982, Lumina Records Ltd.) — two tracks.
  • Shared Leroy Jenkins/James Emery Duo (Discogs group ID 11278487) — no releases documented in Discogs.
  • Also: Leroy Jenkins’ Sting (shared group with Emery, Discogs ID 5832172).
