Damian O’Neill

Guitarist, bassist, and multi-instrumentalist. Born Stephen Damian O’Neill, 15 January 1961, Belfast, Northern Ireland. Discogs artist ID: 221886. Brother of John O’Neill.

Overview

Damian O’Neill is best known as lead guitarist of The Undertones (1978–1983) and bassist of That Petrol Emotion (1985–1994 and 2008–2010 reunion). In the Undertones he was the youngest member, and his lead guitar work underpinned the band’s energetic punk-pop sound across four studio albums. After The Undertones broke up in 1983, he co-founded That Petrol Emotion in 1985 with his brother John O’Neill, switching from lead guitar to bass — a shift he maintained across the band’s entire five-album run. His bass playing on TPE’s records evolved significantly as the band moved from garage-inflected post-punk through to dance-influenced alternative rock in the early 1990s: his credit on End of the Millennium Psychosis Blues (1988) specifies “Bass [2 String]”, suggesting deliberate tonal experimentation.

After TPE dissolved he pursued a series of smaller projects: a 1998 solo single mixed by Kevin Shields, the alias A Quiet Revolution (an album on Poptones, 2001), Wavewalkers (a mid-1990s Derry-scene supergroup that never released officially), and from 2013, both The Everlasting Yeah and Damian O’Neill And The Monotones. He also resumed his role as lead guitarist when The Undertones reunited from 2003 onwards (with new vocalist Paul McLoone replacing Feargal Sharkey), playing on Get What You Need (2003), Dig Yourself Deep (2007), Dig What You Need (2022), and the 2024 live record.

Name variations on record: D. O’Neil, D. O’Neill, D. O’Néill, D.O’Neill, Damian, Damian Ó Neill, Damian Ó Néill, Damien O’Neill, O’Neil, O’Neill

Aliases/project names: A Quiet Revolution, Tommy Tate & The Torpedoes, X-Valdez

Era 1: The Undertones (1978–1983)

The Undertones formed in Derry in 1975. Damian joined as lead guitarist alongside his brother John (rhythm guitar), Feargal Sharkey (vocals), Michael Bradley (bass), and Billy Doherty (drums). Their debut EP “Teenage Kicks” (1978, Sire, cat. SIR 4007) was self-produced; Damian co-wrote “Smarter Than U” (B-side) and “True Confessions” (also B-side) on that record. The EP famously captivated John Peel, leading directly to a deal with Sire Records.

The four studio albums were all produced by Roger Bechirian (first three) and then self-produced alongside Mike Hedges (The Sin Of Pride). Damian’s lead guitar is a credited role on the debut LP; by Positive Touch and The Sin Of Pride, he had also added keyboards to his instrumental palette.

The band split in summer 1983. Feargal Sharkey launched a solo career. The O’Neill brothers moved on to form That Petrol Emotion.

Era 2: That Petrol Emotion (1985–1994; reunion 2008–2010)

That Petrol Emotion formed in 1985, initially with both O’Neill brothers, plus three members from other Derry bands: guitarist Réamann Ó Gormain, drummer Ciaran McLaughlin, and Seattle-born vocalist Steve Mack. Damian switched to bass guitar for this band. John O’Neill departed after Babble (1987); bassist Brendan Kelly joined and appears on Chemicrazy and Fireproof. (On Fireproof the instrument credit for Damian switches back to “Guitar,” with Brendan Kelly taking bass.)

The band recorded for Demon Records (debut), Polydor (Babble), Virgin Records (End of the Millennium Psychosis Blues through Chemicrazy), and their own Koogat imprint (Fireproof, final two singles). See That Petrol Emotion for the full label and personnel picture.

Era 3: Post-TPE Projects (1995–present)

Wavewalkers (mid-1990s)

Wavewalkers was a project band featuring Damian O’Neill, Réamann Ó Gormain, Brendan Kelly, and drummer Kevin Sharkey (brought in while Ciaran McLaughlin was on sabbatical). They played six gigs in London, Derry, and Paris circa 1996 but never released anything officially. Discogs artist ID: 114601.

”No Flies On Frank” solo single (1998, Artefact)

Damian’s first credited solo release. Notably, it was mixed by Kevin Shields (My Bloody Valentine). Damian wrote and also mixed the record. Discogs release ID: 5267550.

A Quiet Revolution (2001, Poptones)

Under the alias A Quiet Revolution, Damian released an album on Poptones (Discogs release ID: 2586955). He is credited as arranger, producer, guitarist, organ player, and effects performer; drums/percussion on selected tracks by Kevin Sharkey. This project name also appears as a Discogs alias for Damian directly.

Songwriting

Damian O’Neill was a significant co-writer throughout the Undertones. His co-writing credits on the debut LP (1979) include “Teenage Kicks” (B-side “Smarter Than U”), and tracks including “Jimmy Jimmy”, “Here Comes The Summer” (partial), “Male Model”, “Get Over You”, and others. He wrote or co-wrote tracks across all four Undertones albums. His Written-By credits continue through the reunion era. On Refit Revise Reprise (Monotones, 2018), he is the sole credited writer on 11 of 12 tracks.
