Overview

Catherine Cooper is a vocalist, guitarist, and recording engineer active since 1994, primarily associated with the shoegaze and ethereal act Alison’s Halo. She has also worked within Insta, Kitten Factor, and The Pastry Heros, and her roles extend beyond performance into mixing and recording. Her catalogue runs from the 1990s through the 2020s — concentrated in the 1990s but with releases in each subsequent decade — spread across labels including Independent Project Records (a relationship lasting from 1994 to 2022), Burnt Hair, Hayden’s Ferry Records, Sunday Records, and Submersible Recordings. Her closest collaborator by credit count is Adam Cooper, with whom she shares 21 credits, pointing to a deep and sustained creative partnership.
