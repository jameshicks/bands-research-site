Overview

Anthony Barratt is a multi-instrumentalist and producer whose 59 Discogs credits since 2004 span keyboards, guitar, clarinet, and mixing. He has been a member of Billy Mahonie, Je Suis Animal, Klangverkstedet, S.L.Y.C., and Salvatore (2), working across indie rock, indie pop, and experimental music. His catalogue runs from early releases on Angular Recording Corporation and Lost & Lonesome (2008–2009) through to Sheep Chase Records in the 2020s. The bulk of his collaborators — Christian Næss, Daniel Meyer Grønvold, Elin Grimstad, Håvard Volden, Magnus Skavhaug Nergaard — point to a deep involvement in the Norwegian indie scene, making his membership in the UK-based post-rock band Billy Mahonie a notable cross-scene connection.
