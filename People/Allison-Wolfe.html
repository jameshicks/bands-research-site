Allison Wolfe

Vocalist, lyricist, and co-founder of Bratmobile. Realname: Allison C. Wolfe. Discogs artist ID: 621932. Alias: Baby Donut.

Overview

Allison Wolfe is a vocalist and lyricist who emerged from the Olympia/Washington D.C. underground in the early 1990s as a central figure in the riot grrrl movement. She co-founded Bratmobile with drummer Molly Neuman and guitarist Erin Smith, and the band’s debut 7-inch Kiss And Ride (1992) was one of the opening salvos of riot grrrl on record. Bratmobile formed in the orbit of the early Olympia scene and quickly migrated between Olympia and D.C., where many of the key riot grrrl bands were active.

After Bratmobile’s initial run ended in the mid-1990s, Wolfe remained active in the D.C. scene through side projects. She fronted Cold Cold Hearts — a project that also featured Erin Smith and was produced in part by Mark Robinson of Teenbeat/Unrest — releasing a 7-inch (1996, under her alias Baby Donut) and a self-titled LP (1997) on Kill Rock Stars. She also contributed vocals and artwork to Deep Lust (2000, Kill Rock Stars), with recording credited to Guy Picciotto.

Bratmobile reunited in the late 1990s and released two albums on Lookout! Records: Ladies, Women And Girls (2000) and Girls Get Busy (2002), before disbanding again. Wolfe then fronted Partyline, a D.C. punk band, whose debut Girls With Glasses appeared on Retard Disco (2005).

In the 2010s Wolfe appeared as a guest vocalist on both of Alice Bag’s solo albums on Don Giovanni Records — the self-titled Alice Bag (2016, backing vocals and handclaps on three tracks) and Blueprint (2018, backing vocals and a shared lead vocal spot on one track, alongside Kathleen Hanna). She also formed Sex Stains (2016, Don Giovanni), a Los Angeles punk group, contributing vocals. Her most recent Discogs-visible project is Cliquey Bitches, a trio with Alice Armendariz (Alice Bag) and Seth Bogart, whose debut Scorpio Scorpio appeared on Fettkakao in 2020. Wolfe holds a photography credit on that release in addition to her vocal role.

Beyond vocals, Wolfe holds songwriter credits on early Bratmobile material (Kiss And Ride, credited as a writer) and holds a photography credit on the Frozen Roses compilation (2006, K Records) as well as on the Skinned Teen release (2020).
