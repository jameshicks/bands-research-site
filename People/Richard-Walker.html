Overview

Richard Walker — better known as Richard Amp — is a British multi-disciplinarian working across music, visual design, and production, most prominently as the central figure of the post-rock, space rock, and ambient outfit Amp, with which he has been active since the late 1980s. His catalogue is heavily rooted in the Creation Records orbit of the 1990s, where he worked alongside Alan McGee, Edward Ball, Joe Foster, and Biff Bang Pow!. Since the 2000s he has continued releasing through his own Ampbase imprint, carrying his experimental approach into the 2020s. In addition to music, he works as a designer and illustrator — activities reflected in his FouldsWalker-Design project alongside Karine Charff.
