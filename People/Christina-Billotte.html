Christina Billotte

American singer, songwriter, and guitarist based in Washington, DC. Discogs artist ID: 594451. Active from 1990 to the present. Ran the Ixor Stix label. Name variations: “Billotte,” “Christina,” “Chris-teen-a Bil-lot-té.”

Central figure in the DC post-punk / riot grrrl scene across four bands spanning three decades: Autoclave (2) (1990–91), Slant 6 (1992–95), Quix-o-tic (1997–2002), and The Casual Dots (2004, 2022–). Sister of Mira Billotte, who played alongside her in Quix-o-tic.

1990–1991: Autoclave (2)

Short-lived all-female DC band, summer 1990 to May 1991. Billotte played bass and sang. The other members were Mary Timony (guitar, vocals), Nikki Chapman (guitar; Discogs ID 594452), and Melissa Berkoff (drums; Discogs ID 594453). After the band folded, Timony went on to Helium (3); Billotte formed Slant 6.

Also around this period, Billotte briefly played with an early Bratmobile lineup. She appears on the 1992 compilation The Embassy Tapes 1990 To 1992 (not-on-label cassette, release ID 5351462) credited as “Band [Bratmobile]” on four tracks (A5, A8–A10), alongside Allison Wolfe, Erin Smith, Jen Smith, and Molly Neuman. This reflects a very early, pre-official Bratmobile configuration; Billotte is not credited on any of Bratmobile’s commercial releases, and the canonical Bratmobile lineup on Kill Rock Stars is Wolfe/Neuman/Erin Smith.

1992–1995: Slant 6

All-female punk trio. Billotte on guitar and vocals; Myra Power (Discogs ID 897556) on bass and vocals; Marge Marshall (Discogs ID 897559) on drums and trumpet. All three releases on Dischord Records; produced with Ian MacKaye and engineered by Don Zientara at Inner Ear Studio. Billotte wrote all songs. Band active until November 1995.

1997–2002: Quix-o-tic

(Discogs name: Quixotic; file uses sanitized form Quix-o-tic.) DC alternative/indie band described as blending R&B and doo-wop with a gothic outlook. Core trio of Christina Billotte (guitar, vocals), Mira Billotte (drums, vocals), and Brendan Majewski (bass, vocals; ID 1316080). Later albums also credit Mick Barr (ID 559447) on various instruments. Guy Picciotto produced the debut LP. Label: Ixor Stix (which Billotte ran herself).

2004–present: The Casual Dots

DC punk trio co-founded with Kathi Wilcox (guitar; ex-Bikini Kill) and Steve Dore (drums; Discogs ID 1153136). Produced by Guy Picciotto; engineered/mixed by Don Zientara at Inner Ear. Released on Kill Rock Stars (2004) and the band’s own Ixor Stix (2022).

Open Questions

  • Billotte’s Bratmobile membership: Discogs lists her as a member, and she appears on the 1992 Embassy Tapes cassette. The exact nature and timeline of this participation is unclear from Discogs alone — web sources would clarify whether she was a founding member who left before the band signed to Kill Rock Stars or a looser early collaborator.
  • The Hole In The Ground (1993, Pop Narcotic) listed in some Autoclave sources: not found in the Autoclave (2) Discogs discography, but Discogs may be incomplete.
  • Quix-o-tic lineup changes: Mick Barr appears on Mortal Mirror (2002) but not the earlier releases; Brendan Majewski drops off Mortal Mirror. The transition between the two lineups is not documented in Discogs.
