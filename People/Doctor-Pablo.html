Doctor Pablo

Discogs ID: 132194 (Doctor Pablo) · also credited as Peter Stroud (2) Real name: Peter Stroud

Keyboards, melodica, guitar, and piano player. Core member of the On-U Sound session ecosystem from 1978 to the 2020s. Credited on releases by London Underground (band), Singers & Players, African Head Charge, Creation Rebel, and many others.
