URLs: danaschechter.com, Instagram, Vimeo

Musician, composer, visual artist, and animator. Primarily a bassist, but also plays lap steel guitar, keyboards, piano, and synthesizer. Schechter is the founder and main creative force behind Bee And Flower (1999–2012) and Insect Ark (2011–present), and has been a recording and touring member of Swans since 2019. She has additionally contributed to Angels of Light, American Music Club, Gnaw, Wrekmeister Harmonies, and Årabrot, among others. Her cross-disciplinary practice extends to animation and film scoring: she scored Craig Singer’s Dead Dogs Lie (2001) and A Good Night to Die (2003), and worked as an animator on the Scott Walker documentary 30 Century Man (2006).

Bee And Flower (1999–2012)

Founded in New York City in 1999 while Schechter was already a member of Michael Gira’s Angels of Light. The original core lineup included Ani Cordero (drums, vocals), Roderick Miller (piano, keyboards), Jon Petrow (viola, violin), and Lynn Wright (guitar, lap steel). The band recorded its first demo in 1999 with Sam McCall.

In 2004 Schechter and Roderick Miller relocated to Berlin with producer Toby Dammit (Larry Mullins) to record the second album, and Berlin became the band’s base from that point forward. In 2021 Schechter confirmed she considered the band effectively finished, though not formally dissolved.

Insect Ark (2011–present)

Started in 2011 in Brooklyn as Schechter’s one-woman solo project. Became a duo in 2015 with Portland drummer Ashley Spungin (Taurus, Purple Rhinestone Eagle), then with Andy Patterson (formerly of Subrosa). In 2022, Insect Ark officially relocated to Berlin; drummer Tim Wyskida replaced Patterson. The project sits at the intersection of doom, drone, and experimental metal.

Angels Of Light (2001–2003)

Schechter appears on two Angels of Light albums — How I Loved You (2001) and Everything Is Good Here / Please Come Home (2003) — playing bass guitar, piano, melodica, and voice. Both are extensively documented in Angels of Light.

American Music Club (reunion era, 2007–2010)

Schechter joined the reunited American Music Club as bassist during the final phase of the reunion era, after Sean Hoffman’s departure. She appears on the live recording Food, Sex And Shelter (Live Cambridge 2008) (2021, self-released, Discogs 18646090). According to the Wikipedia article on AMC, “a later line-up replaced Hoffman with a returning Dana Schecter on bass, and added Jonathan Heine as second guitarist.” The studio album The Golden Age (2008) credits Sean Hoffman as bassist; Schechter’s contribution was during subsequent live touring.

Gnaw

Noise / experimental metal band from New York. Members alongside Schechter include Alan Dubin, Brian Beatrice, Carter Thornton, and Jamie Sykes. Discogs 1379431. Releases include This Face (2009, Conspiracy Records), Horrible Chamber (2013, Seventh Rule Recordings), and Cutting Pieces (2017, Translation Loss Records).

Swans (2019–present)

Schechter joined Swans as a full touring and recording member ahead of Leaving Meaning. (2019, Young God Records). She is credited on Leaving Meaning. (Discogs 14289346) as “Sounds”, and on The Beggar (2023) for bass guitar, lap steel guitar, piano, sounds, and vocals. Also credited on Live Rope (2024) and Birthing (2025).

Årabrot

Norwegian rock band. Schechter is credited as a member (Discogs 545120). The releases she appears on include Sinnerman (2018, Pelagic Records) and Who Do You Love (2018, Pelagic Records) and The World Must Be Destroyed (2021, Pelagic Records), playing lap steel guitar and backing vocals.

Wrekmeister Harmonies

Chicago-based experimental collective led by J.R. Robinson. Schechter is listed as a member (Discogs 2058851) alongside J.R. Robinson, Esther Shaw, Mark Solotroff, and Tim Herzog. She is credited on There / Not There (2020, Consouling Sounds) playing bass.
