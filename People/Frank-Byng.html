Overview

Frank Byng is a drummer, percussionist, producer, and engineer based in Forest Hill, London, where he operates Snorkel Studios. He co-founded Slowfoot Records, which is his most sustained label relationship, spanning 2001 to 2025 with eight works. His 130-plus credits cut across experimental, psychedelic rock, prog rock, future jazz, and post-rock, and his most prominent collaborative relationships — 22 shared releases each — link him to Gong and the core of that group: Dave Sturt, Fabio Golfetti, and Kavus Torabi. He has been a member of Crackle, Lifetones, Prescott, Snorkel, and Spring Tides.
