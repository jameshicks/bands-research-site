Overview

Brad Johnson is a San Francisco-based multi-role contributor whose Discogs footprint spans two distinct functions: musician and visual artist. He played keyboards on American Music Club’s debut album The Restless Stranger (1985) as part of the band’s original lineup alongside Mark Eitzel, Vudi, Daniel Pearson, and Matt Norelli. After that early AMC period he shifted predominantly toward visual work — cover art, painting, and design — while also playing bass and contributing artwork as a member of Virginia Dare, the SF indie/folk group fronted by Mary O’Neil with Paula Frazer on bass/backing vocals.

He is listed as a member of two further bands: Virginia Dare (Discogs ID: 575020) and Wannabe Texans (Discogs ID: 638518), both San Francisco acts. His credit on American Music Club’s Love Songs For Patriots (2004) is for Painting and Design (credited as “Bradley Johnson”), not keyboards — the stub’s framing of that credit as a performance credit was therefore inaccurate.

Discogs lists his name variations as: Brad, Bradley Johnson, J Bradley Johnson, J. Bradley Johnson. No aliases or parallel solo projects appear in the database. No biography text is on file.
