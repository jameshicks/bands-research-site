Rashid Sinan

Free jazz drummer. Discogs artist ID: 408609. Active in New York’s loft jazz scene from the early 1970s through at least the 2010s.

Overview

Rashid Sinan was a drummer embedded in New York’s 1970s avant-garde and loft jazz networks. Discogs records his profile as “American free jazz drummer, active in the 1970s ‘Loft jazz’ scene.” He appears on eight releases across five decades — from ESP-Disk’ sessions in 1973 to a 2012 Frank Lowe reunion album and an undated UK release. He is credited under name variations including Rashid Shinan and Rashied Sinan.

His most documented affiliations are with saxophonist Frank Lowe, the collective group Abdullah (8) led by trumpeter Ahmed Abdullah, and the ensemble Arthur Doyle Plus 4. He also appeared on a 1975 Japanese release by Seikatsu Kojyo Iinkai (生活向上委員会ニューヨーク支部) — a New York-based Japanese free jazz group — alongside William Parker and Ahmed Abdullah.

On Alabama Feeling (1978, AK-BA Records) he is unusually credited as drums, engineer, and mastered by, indicating studio as well as performance roles on at least that one recording.

Black Beings — Frank Lowe (1973, ESP-Disk’)

Sinan’s earliest documented credit. Personnel: Frank Lowe (tenor saxophone, liner notes), William Parker (bass), Joseph Jarman (soprano and alto saxophone), The Wizard / Raymond Lee Cheng (violin), Scott Trusty (engineer). One of the key documents of ESP-Disk’s early-1970s run. Ten pressings documented on Discogs.

Seikatsu Kōjyō Iinkai (1975, ALM Records / self-released, Japan)

Japanese free jazz collective, New York chapter. Also credited: Kazutoki “Kappo” Umezu (alto saxophone), William Parker (bass), Yoriyuki Harada (piano, bass clarinet), Ahmed Abdullah (trumpet). Three pressings.

Alabama Feeling — Arthur Doyle Plus 4 (1978, AK-BA Records)

Sinan plays drums and also serves as engineer and mastering credit — one of the few records where he appears in a production capacity. Other personnel: Arthur Doyle (tenor saxophone, bass clarinet, flute, composed by), Charles Stephens (trombone), Richard Williams (bass), Bruce Moore (drums), Marzette Watts (mastering engineer). Four pressings.

Life’s Force — Abdullah (1979, About Time Records)

Abdullah (8) was a collective led by trumpeter Ahmed Abdullah. Personnel includes Ahmed Abdullah (trumpet, composer), Jerome Hunter (bass), Muneer Abdul Fataah (cello), Jay Hoggard (vibraphone), Vincent Chancey (French horn). Two pressings.

Live At Ali’s Alley — Abdullah (1980, Cadence Jazz Records)

Live recording. Same core Abdullah group: Ahmed Abdullah, Jerome Hunter, Muneer Abdul Fataah, Vincent Chancey, Chico Freeman (tenor saxophone, flute). Produced by Bob Rusch. Two pressings.

No More Crazy Women — Arthur Doyle (2005, Qbico, Italy)

Sinan appears on two tracks (A2, A4) alongside Wilber Morris (double bass). The other three tracks are electronics-based edits. Three pressings.

The Loweski — Frank Lowe (2012, ESP-Disk’)

Reunion of the Black Beings group nearly four decades on: Frank Lowe, William Parker, Joseph Jarman, Raymond Lee Cheng (The Wizard). Liner notes by Clifford Allen (September 2011). Two pressings.
