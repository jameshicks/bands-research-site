Hugh Hopper

Aug 19, 20262 min read

Hugh Hopper

Discogs ID: 235031

Canterbury scene bassist. Member of Soft Machine, playing on Third (1970, CBS) alongside Robert Wyatt (drums), Mike Ratledge (organ), and Elton Dean (sax). Also a member of Wilde Flowers and Soft Heap.

Also active in the British free jazz world connected to Ogun: produced Keith Tippett’s Ark’s Frames (Music For An Imaginary Film) (1978, OGD 003/004) and appears as bassist on Rogue Element (1978, OG 527) alongside Alan Gowen, Dave Sheen, and Elton Dean.
