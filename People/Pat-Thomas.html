Overview

London-based pianist and keyboardist, born 27 July 1960, with over 450 Discogs credits spanning free improvisation, experimental, and free jazz. His catalogue runs from early appearances on Incus (1993–2003) through to prolific output on 577 Records (14 works, 2019–2024) and Otoroku (8 works, 2016–2025), with his recorded presence peaking sharply in the 2020s. He is a member of several ensembles including About Group and (3) أحمد, and his most frequent collaborators include percussionists Mark Sanders, Antonin Gerbal, and Seymour Wright, alongside saxophonists John Butcher and Shaun Crook.
