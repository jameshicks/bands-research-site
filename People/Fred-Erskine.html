Fred Erskine

Discogs ID: 40396
ANVs: Fred “Timbales” Erskine, Freddie T., Freddie T. Erskine, Frederick T. Erskine, Frederick Erskine, F. Erskine, F Erskine, FE, Erskine, Fred Erskin

Multi-instrumentalist (primarily bass, trumpet, keyboards) rooted in the DC-area post-hardcore scene. Core member of Hoover (3) (1992–1994), The Crownhate Ruin (1994–1996), and June Of 44 (1994–2000; reunited 2018–present). Post-dissolution, appeared across the sprawling ensemble HiM alongside Doug Scharin, and through a cluster of Slowdime-adjacent projects including Abilene, The Boom (3), and Just A Fire.

Overview

Erskine’s career traces the arc of DC post-hardcore into post-rock and experimental jazz-adjacent territory. He came up through the early-1990s Dischord orbit — first in Hoover with guitarist Joseph McRedmond and drummer Christopher Farrall, then in The Crownhate Ruin (a direct continuation of Hoover with different personnel) — before becoming the bassist of June Of 44. As June Of 44 matured across five studio records on Quarterstick Records, his credited instrumentation expanded from bass alone to include trumpet, keyboards, and vocals, reflecting the band’s drift from post-hardcore into textural post-rock.

After June Of 44 dissolved around 2000, Erskine became a frequent contributor to HiM — the large rotating ensemble centred on Scharin’s drumming — and appeared on a series of DC-adjacent records released by Slowdime and related labels. He returned to June Of 44 for the 2020 reunion LP and subsequent touring.
