Overview

John Herod is a guitarist and bassist whose credits in the DC-area post-hardcore and emo scene stretch from 1993 to the early 2020s. He is a member of Lincoln (3), Enderhall, and Ice Fan, working across styles that span hardcore, math rock, and punk. His catalogue is weighted toward the 1990s, with early releases on Watermark Records and Art Monk Construction, before a long gap and a return to activity via Temporary Residence Limited in 2022–2023. Jay Demko and Justin Wierbonski are his two most constant collaborators, each sharing eleven releases with him.

Notes (web)

Lincoln (3) return to activity and Temporary Residence Limited, 2022–2023

The 2022 Temporary Residence Limited release Repair and Reward (TRR358, released 5 August 2022) is not a resumption of recording — it is a tape-recovery and reissue project. Lincoln had been inactive since the mid-1990s and no new studio recordings were made for this release. The “2022–2023” span in Discogs credits appears to reflect multiple vinyl pressings of the same album (the ocean-blue and clear/black mix LPs share the same content).

What led to the release: In 2021, Lincoln’s complete recorded history — the original analog tapes for all three 7-inch singles — was recovered, restored, and transferred. All eight songs (seven from the original 7-inches plus one previously unreleased track) were then newly mixed from those tapes by J. Robbins (Jawbox, Burning Airlines), who had institutional knowledge of the studios and equipment from the era. The recordings had been out of print and unavailable for over 25 years. Sources do not name who initiated the tape recovery or who approached whom regarding the label deal. https://newnoisemagazine.com/news-lincoln-discography-sees-release-august-5/

Temporary Residence Limited connection: No interview, press release, or label statement found explains how Lincoln came to Temporary Residence Ltd. specifically. The label’s existing roster overlaps with the post-hardcore/emo world Lincoln inhabited (Explosions in the Sky, Mono, etc.), making it a plausible fit, but no sourced explanation has been located. The TRL 2022 year-end compilation (released December 2022) also included Lincoln’s track “Benchwarmer,” suggesting an active working relationship with the label that year beyond just the album release. https://temporaryresidence.bandcamp.com/album/trl-2022

Reunion shows: The band played reunion live shows in Morgantown, WV in October 2023 (a show at 123 Pleasant Street on 15 October 2023 is referenced in Bandcamp comments). This is the only documented post-1990s live activity found. No new recordings tied to those shows have been located. https://lincoln-wv.bandcamp.com/album/repair-and-reward (comment section)

Mixing location and date: The mixing sessions took place in May 2021 in Baltimore, MD — J. Robbins’ home base. This is consistent with Robbins being the operational hub of the project, though no source names him as the one who initiated it. Source: Piccadilly Records product description snippet (https://www.piccadillyrecords.com/145073/Lincoln-Repair-And-Reward-Temporary-Residence-Ltd).
