The Architect

Alias of Jay Haze (Justin McNulty). Discogs artist ID: 77493. Active 2001–2006, primarily on Tuning Spork Records and Karloff Rekordings.

Overview

The Architect is one of several aliases used by Jay Haze (Discogs ID: 71701), the American minimal/tech house producer based in Germany. Under this name he released six 12” singles between 2001 and 2006, all on Germany-based labels connected to his own operations. The earliest releases appeared on Tuning Spork Records (spork 04, spork 05, spork 07), the label most closely identified with Jay Haze’s early catalog. From 2004 onward the alias shifted to Karloff Rekordings (a sublabel of Sub Static) and the one-off Friends Of Tomorrow imprint.

Stylistically the releases span Haze’s range during this period: the 2001 debut leans into dub-inflected tech house; 2002’s Pixelated Porn King (spork 07) pushes toward experimental minimal techno; the Karloff releases move into abstract minimal and glitch territory. No remixers, guest performers, or additional credited personnel appear on any of the six releases in the Discogs database — each is a solo production.

Other Jay Haze aliases active in overlapping years include Dub Cord, Dub Surgeon, Montagespiral, and The Red Light Shining.
