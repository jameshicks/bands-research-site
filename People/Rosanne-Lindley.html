Rosanne Lindley

Discogs artist ID: 401930
Name variations: R. Lindley, Rosaaane, Rosanne, Roseanne, Roseanne Lindley
Groups: The Mountain Goats (occasional member, 1991–1995), The Bright Mountain Choir

American vocalist, guitarist, and musician based in California. Daughter of David Lindley, the LA session guitarist and multi-instrumentalist. She performed as one of the four occasional women members of The Mountain Goats during the Claremont-era cassette period (1992–1994), and later worked directly with her father.

Biography

Early Life and the Mountain Goats Era (1991–1994)

Rosanne Lindley grew up in the orbit of her father David Lindley’s musical world — David Lindley was one of the most in-demand session and touring players in Los Angeles throughout the 1970s and 1980s, appearing on hundreds of records for artists including Jackson Browne and Linda Ronstadt. Rosanne herself appears on David Lindley And El Rayo-X’s Very Greasy (1988, Elektra) credited for Harmony Vocals on one track (B3), alongside Jackson Browne and Linda Ronstadt — this is her earliest Discogs credit, placing her in that professional milieu from at least age-equivalent of a late-1980s session.

In the early 1990s she was part of the loose collective around John Darnielle in Claremont, California, that formed the various-personnel incarnation of The Mountain Goats. Darnielle’s cassette-era Mountain Goats credited the full five-person performing group (Darnielle, Rachel Ware, Amy Piatt, Sarah Arslanian, and Rosanne Lindley) as “The Mountain Goats Are” or “Performer [We Are],” reflecting the collective nature of those recordings. She is credited on five Mountain Goats releases between 1992 and 1994:

  • Songs For Petronius (1992) — credited as “Roseanne,” role: Band [The Mountain Goats Are]
  • Chile De Árbol (1993) — credited as “Rosanne,” role: Performer
  • Hot Garden Stomp (1993) — credited as “Rosaaane,” role: Performer
  • Beautiful Rat Sunset (1994) — credited as “Rosanne,” role: Performer [We Are]
  • Zopilote Machine (1994) — credited as “Rosanne,” role: Performer [We Are]

The deliberately misspelled/variable sleeve credits on these releases (Rosaaane, Roseanne, Rosanne) were part of Darnielle’s lo-fi cassette aesthetic and house style at Shrimper and Ajax Records (2).

The four women who appeared alongside Darnielle in this formation — Lindley, Ware, Piatt, and Arslanian — are also grouped under the name The Bright Mountain Choir, which appears in the Discogs credits for Zopilote Machine (1994) as “Recorded By.”

Later Work With David Lindley

Rosanne Lindley appears in two releases directly credited to or featuring her father:

  • Big Twang (2007, Not On Label) — a David Lindley release; Rosanne credited for Guitar, Vocals. David credited as producer, lap steel, bouzouki, percussion, oud, twelve-string guitar, vocals.
  • Vienna 1995 (2019, Leftfield Media) — a live recording from 1995, released in 2019, credited jointly to Ry Cooder, David Lindley, Joachim Cooder, and Rosanne Lindley. She is a primary artist on this release, credited for Vocals, Guitar. Ry Cooder plays guitar, accordion, bouzouki, tambora, mandolin; David Lindley plays guitar, bajo sexto, bouzouki, tambora; Joachim Cooder plays drums, percussion.

The Vienna 1995 recording places Rosanne in an impromptu quartet with her father, Ry Cooder, and Cooder’s son Joachim — a gathering of the extended Cooder/Lindley musical family that had been collaborating since at least the early 1970s.
