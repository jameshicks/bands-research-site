Overview

Stefan Németh is an Austrian guitarist and electronic musician born in Baden and based in Vienna, known primarily for his work in the groups Radian, Lokai, Innode, and Velvet Lounge. He co-founded the Mosz label, and his output spans experimental, ambient, and minimal styles from the late 1990s through the 2020s, with the densest period of releases in the 2000s. The majority of his catalogue appeared on Thrill Jockey (six works, 2002–2009), with later releases on Editions Mego from 2013 onward. His most frequent collaborators — Martin Brandlmayr, John Norman, Martin Siewert, and John McEntire — point to both the Viennese experimental scene and Chicago post-rock connections.

Radian and Thrill Jockey — how the relationship started

Radian’s pre-Thrill Jockey releases (including their self-titled 1998 record on Rhiz Records and a release on Peter Rehberg’s Mego label) had limited US distribution, arriving as expensive imports. By early 2002 the band traveled to Chicago to record their second full-length with John McEntire at Soma Electronic Music Studios; Thrill Jockey released the result, Rec.Extern, later that year. thrilljockey.com/products/rec-extern | sonotope.org/radian

McEntire’s role on Rec.Extern (2002) was recording engineer and mixer (“recorded by John McEntire” per the Thrill Jockey product page). On Juxtaposition (2004) the band again brought their complete electronic arrangements to Soma, where McEntire recorded the drums and bass; two of the three-album trilogy were thus recorded and mixed by McEntire. sonotope.org/radian/radian-juxtaposition | radian.bandcamp.com/album/juxtaposition

The connection to Thrill Jockey fits a pattern: Thrill Jockey was already home to Tortoise (McEntire’s band) and other Chicago post-rock/experimental acts, and Soma was McEntire’s primary recording facility. The label’s founder Bettina Richards is listed in the Juxtaposition credits. musicexport.at

Bettina Richards’s credit — A&R, not just label-owner boilerplate

The 2002 skug interview (Christian König, German-language, published at skug.at/radian/) contains direct first-person accounts from all three band members about how the Thrill Jockey deal came together:

The relationship with Chicago preceded Richards by several years. John Norman recounts that Pita (Peter Rehberg) sent a rough mix to Chicago before their first CD was released; McEntire received it and liked it. In March 1998 Tortoise played in Wels (Austria) and Norman spoke to McEntire backstage; McEntire invited Radian to open in Vienna, and an ongoing friendship developed. McEntire also invited them to record at his studio, but travel costs and unfinished material prevented it at that stage.

The decisive step was the 2001 ATP (All Tomorrows Parties) festival in England, curated by Tortoise. Martin Brandlmayr, in the interview: “Bettina Richards, the label chief of Thrill Jockey, saw our concert there and offered us a record deal.” [skug.at/radian/]

Stefan Németh’s direct quote: “Wir haben gar nicht gewusst, dass von seiten Thrill Jockey Interesse besteht, aber offensichtlich haben sie sich schon informiert. Sie hat unsere Sachen gekannt und gewusst wo die Platten erhältlich sind, wollte aber noch mal die Liveshow sehen und die war offensichtlich überzeugend. Das war der letzte Schritt. Sie arbeitet relativ straight und weiß was sie tut.” (Translation: “We didn’t even know that Thrill Jockey was interested, but they had apparently already looked into us. She knew our stuff and knew where the records were available, but still wanted to see the live show and apparently it was convincing. That was the last step. She works fairly directly and knows what she’s doing.“) [skug.at/radian/]

Conclusion: Bettina Richards’s credit on Juxtaposition (and by extension Rec.Extern) reflects genuine, hands-on A&R work. She personally scouted Radian, researched their releases before approaching them, attended their ATP 2001 live set, and made the offer herself. The McEntire connection was the warm path that brought Radian into the Thrill Jockey/Chicago orbit, but Richards made the signing decision independently and directly.

The Willamette Week extended Q&A with Richards (wweek.com) also groups Radian among Thrill Jockey artists whose work has an “obtuse” quality she hears as a unifying trait — consistent with an owner who engaged closely with the band’s music rather than passively listing it. [wweek.com/portland/blog-29420-extended-q-a-with-thrill-jockey-founder-bettina-richards.html]
