Savage Pencil

Born Edwin Pouncey, June 1951, Leeds, West Riding of Yorkshire. British comics artist, illustrator, musician, and music journalist. Primary Discogs entry: Savage Pencil (ID: 196519). Secondary entry under his real name: Edwin Pouncey (ID: 548723).

Also known as: Sav X, Ether Hogg. Name variations: Edwin “Savage Pencil” Pouncey, SAVX, Sav Lab, Savage.

Overview

Edwin Pouncey, working under the nom de plume Savage Pencil, is one of the most consistently cross-disciplinary figures in the British post-punk and underground noise scenes. He is a comics artist and illustrator (best known for the Rock’n’Roll Zoo strip in Sounds and continuing work in Forced Exposure and The Wire), a record sleeve designer whose visual vocabulary shaped the aesthetic of multiple labels from the late 1970s through the 2020s, a musician and singer, a compiled music writer, and — through long-term collaborations with Blast First and Table Of The Elements in particular — one of the most recognisable visual artists in experimental and noise rock.

His sleeve design credits run from the earliest days of UK independent punk (The Saints’ (I’m) Stranded UK pressing, 1976; The Lurkers’ Freak Show, 1977; the Streets Beggars Banquet compilation, 1977) through to 2026 (Turner-Nordwall’s Malign Seeds). He was a member of early UK punk band The Art Attacks (appearing as vocalist and backing vocalist on their First And Last 7” on Fresh Records (14)) and later of the improvising drone-noise band Pestrepeller. As a primary recording artist he compiled and presented the 1988 biker-movie soundtrack compilation Angel Dust (Music For Movie Bikers) on Blast First’s Furthur sublabel, and the 1999 mix/compilation The Antiquack (A Dead Duck Selection) on EMI. (Source for biographical context: Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savage_Pencil)

As Musician / Performer

The Art Attacks (1977–1979)

UK punk group on which Pouncey appeared as vocalist and backing vocalist. He also designed the sleeve. Per Wikipedia, the band released two 7”s: I Am A Dalek b/w Neutron Bomb (Albatross Records (2), 1977/1978) and First And Last / Punk Rock Stars / Rat City (Fresh Records (14), FRESH 3, 1979). Robert Gotobed (later of Wire) played drums on the B-side of First And Last. On the Fresh single, Pouncey is credited under the name “Edwin” as vocalist on both sides and as backing vocalist on side A alongside Marion Fudger, Steve Spear, and Rick Slaughter. Pouncey also wrote Punk Rock Stars (co-written with Steve Spear).

Pestrepeller (c. 2003–)

Described by Wikipedia as an “improvising drone-rock noise band,” Pestrepeller was formed by Pouncey (credited under his Edwin Pouncey alias, Discogs ID 548723) alongside Peter Hope Evans (ex-Medicine Head), Ed Pinsent (Discogs: 1307053), Harley Richardson (2) (Discogs: 1499038), Nick Neocleous, and Rob Brown. The group released an album Isle Of Dark Magick in May 2006 on Important Records, described as “free-improvised-found-sound-collage enriched with dark pagan folky vibes and supernatural horror noise dedicated to H.P. Lovecraft, Clark Ashton Smith and Austin Osman Spare.” Earlier, they released The Cruel Sea (2003) on MR E Disk. The band operates under the alias “Ultrasonic Attack Wave Pestrepeller.” (Source: Wikipedia)
