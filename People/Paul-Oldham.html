Paul Oldham

American recording engineer, producer, and musician. Discogs artist ID: 254225. Born and based in Louisville/Shelbyville, Kentucky. Brother of Will Oldham and Ned Oldham, son of Joanne Oldham and Joe Oldham, husband of Amber Oldham. Owner and operator of Rove Studio in Shelbyville, KY (Discogs label ID: 273394).

Name variations on releases: P T Oldham, P. Oldham, P.O., Paul, Paul “Dieter” Oldham.

Primary engineer, mixer, and mastering engineer behind the bulk of the Palace / Bonnie “Prince” Billy catalog from 1995 onward. Doubles as a performer (bass, guitar, vocals) on many of those records. From roughly 2009, his role shifted from engineering toward mastering — he appears on 50+ releases in that capacity, serving as an informal house mastering engineer for a loose circle of Louisville, Nashville, San Francisco, and Chicago indie artists. Member of several active groups including Feast Of Smoke (with Amber Oldham), the Oldham Brothers (with Will and Ned), Havanarama (with Will Oldham, David Pajo, and others), and the short-lived The Broadcast Choir.

Biography

Louisville Beginnings and The Broadcast Choir (mid-1990s)

Paul Oldham enters the Discogs record in 1995 as a featured performer on Songs That Sank The Titanic (Palace Records), the only release credited to The Broadcast Choir — a loose ensemble that also included Peter Townsend and Chris Layton. This record plants him firmly in the Louisville orbit around his brother Will.

His earliest engineering credit is the electroacoustic work on Sweet Sixteen (1997, Virgin Records America) — a live album recorded at Chicago’s Steppenwolf Theatre. The same year he appears as a featured player on the Will Oldham compilation Lost Blues And Other Songs (Drag City/Palace Records, 1997).

The Palace/BPB Engineer Era (1998–2008)

Paul Oldham becomes the core engineer behind the Bonnie “Prince” Billy catalog starting with I See A Darkness (Palace Records, 1998) — the debut record under that name. He recorded and mixed the album, sharing mixing duties with David Pajo (“Pajo”) and Colin Gagon. He also appears as a performer (credited “Played By”), alongside Arellano, Gagon, Peter Townsend, and Will Oldham himself. Mastering on that record went to Konrad Strauss.

The Bonnie “Prince” Billy mini-album Travels In Constants Volume Seven (More Revery) (2000, Temporary Residence Limited) — Vol. 7 of the Travels In Constants series — was recorded by Paul, credited as “P T Oldham” under the alias “The Rove Unit.” The record consists of BPB covers of songs by Bill Withers, John Holt, John Phillips, PJ Harvey, The Renderers, and Tim McGraw.

Ease Down The Road (Palace Records, 2001), BPB’s first proper full-length, has Paul in the recording and mixing chair alongside Will Oldham and David Pajo (who co-mixed and played). Personnel included Mike Fellows (bass), Jon Theodore (drums), Bryan Rich (guitar), Matt Sweeney, Todd Brashear, Paul Greenlaw, Catherine Irwin, Harmony Korine, Ned Oldham, and Matthew Everett (violin).

Also in 2001, Paul produces and engineers The Anomoanon self-titled LP (Palace Records) — Ned Oldham’s band, whose lineup included Willy MacLean (bass), Jack Carneal (drums), Jason Stith (keyboards), and Aram Stith (guitar). He returned for The Anomoanon’s second album, Asleep Many Years In The Wood (2002, Temporary Residence Limited), again producing and engineering.

Master And Everyone (Drag City/Palace Records, 2003) is recorded by Mark Nevers rather than Paul, but Paul appears as a performer (bass, guitar, backing vocals) and handles the mastering. Other players include William Tyler (guitar), Tony Crow (keyboards, credited with “Blowing And Fingering Of Keys”), Gary Tussing (cello), Matt Swanson (tambourine), and Marty Slayton (backing vocals).

In 2002 Paul serves as full producer/engineer/mixer/bassist on Swedish singer Nicolai Dunger’s Tranquil Isolation (Dolores Recordings/Virgin), co-produced with Dunger and Will Oldham, with Peter Townsend on drums. This is one of the more prominent outside commissions of his early career.

Additional engineering/recording work during 2001–2005 includes:

  • More Revery (2001, TRL) — BPB “More Revery” full EP, recorded by Paul
  • Ode Music (2000, Drag City) — BPB recording
  • Get On Jolly (2000, Domino) — BPB, voices recorded by Paul
  • Amalgamated Sons Of Rest (2002, Galaxia) — Paul performs additional accompaniment and records
  • Envoi Villon (2002, Galaxia) — produces and engineers
  • Ala.Cali.Tucky (2003, Galaxia) — bass, producer, recorded by
  • Rock The Blockade: 2000 Incorruptible (2003, Secret Eye Records) — bass and vocals
  • I Gave You (2005, Drag City) — recording
  • No Earthly Man (2005, Drag City) — bass and mixing
  • Summer In The Southeast (2005, Sea Note) — bass, backing vocals, mixing

Superwolf (Drag City/Palace Records, 2005), the first Matt Sweeney / BPB collaboration, is produced and recorded by Paul, with Sweeney handling guitar/vocals/bass/keyboards and production alongside him, Peter Townsend on drums, and Will Oldham on vocals/bass/percussion.

Sings Greatest Palace Music (2004, Drag City/Palace Records) — mastered by Paul.

The Letting Go (Domino, 2006) and the two BPB EPs flanking it (Cold & Wet, Cursed Sleep) feature Paul as a performer on bass and electric piano, alongside producer Valgeir Sigurðsson’s Icelandic lineup.

Lie Down In The Light (Drag City/Palace Records, 2008): Paul plays bass and is credited as a performer “Realized With” — one of several albums where his role as both player and informal co-producer is acknowledged. Recorded by Mark Nevers.

Wilding In The West (2008, Spunk): Paul joins as a guest vocalist (“Joining Voice”) and recording engineer on this Australian BPB release.

Born Heller and Outside Sessions

The Born Heller self-titled LP (2004, Locust Music) — the project of Josephine Foster and Jason Ajemian — has Paul recording five tracks (1, 2, 4, 9, 10), with Devin Davis recording the other six. This is one of the more distinct outside sessions; Foster went on to a significant solo career.

Other recording work from this period includes:

  • Gang Gang Dance self-titled (2004, Fusetron) — recorded by Paul
  • Joji (2004, Temporary Residence Limited) — recorded by Paul
  • Moody Pike (2004, Agenda/Darla) — recorded by Paul
  • Honey For Dinner (2001, Galaxia) — engineer
  • More Revery (2001, TRL) — recorded by Paul

The Mastering Phase (2009–present)

From Beware (Drag City/Palace Records, 2009) onward Paul’s role on BPB records is primarily mastering, while recording duties pass to others (Neil Strauch on Beware). He continues mastering almost every BPB/Palace Records release through Superwolves (2021).

His mastering practice extended well beyond the Palace circle to include:

  • Funtown Comedown (2009, Sea Note)
  • The Wonder Show Of The World (2010, BPB & The Cairo Gang, Drag City) — mastered by Paul
  • Multiple Empty Cellar Records releases (2011–2014): The Glad Birth Of Love, The Pass And Crossings, Mrs. Jones’ Cookies, River Of Souls, Garagearray
  • Polyvinyl Record Company releases: Antenna To The Afterworld (2013), I Need You Bad (2013), Talent Night At The Ashram (2015)
  • Honest Jon’s Records: The Marble Downs (2012)
  • Burger Records titles: Cool Ghouls (2013), Double Exposure (Matt Kivel, 2013, also mixed)
  • Big White Cloud Records (Canada): In The Morning (2014), The Long Shadow (2015)
  • Bird’s Robe Records (Australia): A Broken Sail (2018)
  • Singer’s Grave A Sea Of Tongues / Barely Regal (2014, Drag City/Palace Records) — bass and mastering
  • Days Of Being Wild (Matt Kivel, 2014, Woodsist) — full producer/engineer/mixer credit
  • Little Wings self-released records: People (2019, mixed), Froggy’s (2020), Zephyr (2020)
  • The Name Of The Ghost Is Home (2022, Fluff And Gravy Records) — mixed and mastered

Feast Of Smoke and Later Projects

Feast Of Smoke (Discogs ID: 10353673) is a duo of Paul Oldham and Amber Oldham (his wife). Their self-titled LP (2021, Perpetual Doom) credits Paul for music, lyrics, and mastering.

The Oldham Brothers (Discogs ID: 774349) is a trio with Will and Ned Oldham — releases not documented as a standalone discography on Discogs, but the group credit appears across shared appearances.

Havanarama (Discogs ID: 577187) is a Louisville ensemble grouping Paul, Will Oldham, David Pajo, Peter Townsend, Bob Arellano, and a cat (Jasper). This appears to be an informal session/performance collective rather than a touring band.

Other groups in Discogs under Paul’s name: Lucky Eyes, Speed To Roam, The Monkey Boys, The Pale Horse Riders, Royal Trux — the Royal Trux credit is notable and likely reflects a session or one-off appearance rather than full membership.

Open Questions

  • Discogs lists Paul as a member of Royal Trux — the specific release or session context is unclear; Royal Trux is a Neil Hagerty / Jennifer Herrema project and the connection is not explained by any shared release in this DB pull.
  • The “Rove Unit” credited as recorder on Travels In Constants Vol. 7 is a pseudonym/collective name for Paul’s operation at Rove Studio — not separately documented.
  • The Galaxia label (releases: Honey For Dinner, Amalgamated Sons Of Rest, Envoi Villon, Ala.Cali.Tucky) appears to be a small Louisville imprint connected to Will Oldham’s circle; its ownership/structure is not clear from Discogs metadata alone.
  • Several of Paul’s Louisville group projects (Lucky Eyes, Speed To Roam, The Monkey Boys, The Pale Horse Riders) have no Discogs release credits visible in this pull — likely recordings exist outside the Discogs database or are self-released.
  • Rock The Blockade: 2000 Incorruptible (2003, Secret Eye Records): Paul credited with bass and vocals — identity of the primary artist on that release is not in this pull.
