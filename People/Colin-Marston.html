Colin Marston

Discogs ID: 600635
Real name: Colin J. Marston
Born: September 13, 1982
Origin: Queens, New York, USA

Producer, engineer, mastering engineer, and multi-instrumentalist. Runs Menegroth, The Thousand Caves studio in Queens, NY. Trained at NYU (BA, Music Technology, 2004). One of the most active engineers in contemporary avant-garde and extreme metal, with credits across hundreds of releases from 2005 onward. As a performer, central to Krallice (Warr guitar, bass) and Gorguts (bass), and a co-founder of Dysrhythmia and Behold… The Arctopus.

His signature instrument is the Warr guitar — a tapped instrument whose range covers both guitar and bass simultaneously — which he employs in Behold… The Arctopus and other projects. Aliases include Indricothere (solo project), Cancer (5), Urthshroud, Xazraug, and the Colony (8).

Biography

Early years and Infidel?/Castro! (2001–2004)

Marston’s first documented project is Castro! (ID 764203), a Canadian-adjacent experimental group whose self-released debut Case Studies In Bioentropy appeared in 2001. He is credited with artwork, composition, and recording. By 2003 he had enrolled at NYU, graduating in 2004 with a BA in Music Technology — a program focused on recording and production rather than performance, consistent with his subsequent career trajectory.

Behold… The Arctopus and studio founding (2005–2007)

Behold… The Arctopus (ID 668949) gave Marston his first visible footprint in the US avant-metal underground. A trio/quartet playing interlocking Warr guitar parts alongside standard guitar and drums, the band released Nano-Nucleonic Cyborg Summoning (2005, Troubleman Unlimited) and Skullgrid (2007, Black Market Activities / Metal Blade Records), both recorded, mixed, and mastered by Marston at what would become Menegroth. Members included Mick Barr (who would later co-found Krallice with Marston), Mike Lerner, and Charlie Zeleny; Weasel Walter (ID 216186) has also been credited.

By this point Menegroth, The Thousand Caves was operational in Queens and Marston was accumulating engineering credits: Cloak Of Love (Crucial Blast, 2005), Barriers And Passages for Dysrhythmia (Relapse, 2006), Trueno Oscuro (Load Records, 2007), Wake/Lift (Translation Loss, 2007). He also remastered the Pestilence back catalog for Relapse in 2005 (Elements, Piece Of Time, Unquestionable Presence).

Dysrhythmia (ID 334727) is a guitar-drums-bass trio with Kevin Hufnagel (ID 600634) and Jeff Eber (ID 757066), formed in the early 2000s. Barriers And Passages (Relapse, 2006) was their first Marston-engineered album; he subsequently played bass on every subsequent Dysrhythmia record.

Krallice and the Hydra Head / Profound Lore period (2008–2012)

Krallice (ID 1182892) was formed with Mick Barr, Lev Weinstein (ID 1182891), and Nick McMaster (ID 1112843). Their self-titled debut (Profound Lore PFL-036, 2008) — recorded, mixed, and mastered by Marston — established the band as a major force in avant-garde black metal: six tracks running 10–15 minutes each, dense with interlocking guitar lines. Dimensional Bleedthrough followed in 2009 (Profound Lore), Diotima in 2011 (Profound Lore). Marston played guitar and bass throughout and served as the band’s primary recording engineer.

Alongside Krallice, Marston’s engineering profile expanded rapidly:

  • Blue Lambency Downward — Kayo Dot (Hydra Head, 2008) — drums recording
  • Mahakali — Jarboe (The End Records, 2008) — guitar, mix, engineer, producer, arrangement
  • Annwn (Hydra Head, 2008) — mixed and mastered
  • Dimensional Bleedthrough — Krallice (Profound Lore, 2009) — performer + recording
  • Psychic Maps — Dysrhythmia (Relapse, 2009) — bass + recorded, mixed, mastered
  • Aesthethica — Battles (Thrill Jockey, 2011) — recorded, mixed
  • Diotima — Krallice (Profound Lore, 2011) — performer + recorded, mixed, mastered
  • Test Of Submission — Dysrhythmia (Profound Lore, 2012) — bass + recorded, mixed, mastered
  • Horrorscension — Behold… The Arctopus (Black Market Activities, 2012) — Warr guitar + recorded, mixed, mastered

The Agalloch remastering project (2016 reissues of Pale Folklore, The Mantle, Ashes Against The Grain on The End Records) and a 2020 Throne Records reissue series show his continued remastering work for the back catalogs of major underground bands.

Gorguts reformation and Colored Sands (2013–2016)

In 2013 Marston joined the reformed Gorguts — Luc Lemay’s (ID 346754) technical death metal band, inactive since 2005 — as bass player. Colored Sands (Season of Mist SOM 311, 2013) was their first album in 12 years. Marston played bass, co-produced, and handled mix and mastering; he also recorded the string orchestra and vocals at Menegroth. He composed music for track 3 (“Forgotten Arrows”), the only non-Lemay composition on the album. Kevin Hufnagel (also of Dysrhythmia) played lead guitar; John Longstreth (ID 474966) drummed; Martin Brunet and Pierre Rémillard tracked the basic sessions.

Pleiades’ Dust (Season of Mist, 2016) — a single-track 33-minute EP — continued the collaboration: Marston again on bass, recorded, mixed, mastered, produced. Grief Relic (Season of Mist Underground Activists, 2016) is a further Gorguts entry on which he is credited with bass.

During this period Marston also contributed keyboards, orchestral arrangement, and engineering to Panopticon’s Roads To The North (Bindrune Recordings, 2014) — one of his rare credits outside metal proper.

The Dysrhythmia catalog continued: The Veil Of Control (Profound Lore, 2016) — bass, recorded, mixed, mastered.

Imperial Triumphant and expanded profile (2017–present)

Imperial Triumphant became the defining production partnership of Marston’s later career. He engineered and mastered Vile Luxury (Gilead Media / Throatruiner, 2018), which became one of the most acclaimed avant-metal albums of the decade. He then mixed, produced, and mastered Alphaville (Century Media, 2020) — the band’s label breakthrough — and continued with An Evening With Imperial Triumphant (Century Media, 2021, mixed and mastered) and Spirit Of Ecstasy (Century Media, 2022, where he also appeared as a guest performing electronic drums via Simmons kit and as a producer).

Other significant credits from this period:

  • Labyrinth Constellation — Artificial Brain (Profound Lore, 2014) — recorded, engineer, producer
  • Infrared Horizon — Artificial Brain (Profound Lore, 2017) — producer, recorded, engineer
  • World’s Blood — Chthe’ilist (Profound Lore, 2018) — engineer, mixed, mastered
  • A Romance With Violence — Oranssi Pazuzu… wait, this is actually a different act — mixed (Profound Lore, 2020)
  • Terminal Threshold — Dysrhythmia (Translation Loss, 2019) — bass + performed, recorded, mixed, mastered
  • Coffin Of Conviction — Dysrhythmia (self-released, 2024) — bass, guitar, recorded, mixed, mastered
  • Parallels Of Infinite Perspect — with Eliane Gazzard (5848628) (Iluso Records, 2019) — a 75-minute avant-garde jazz/free improvisation record; four untitled tracks of 24, 12, 20, and 18 minutes. His only primary-credited release outside a metal context.

The Indricothere solo project (self-released, 2007; II 2013; III 2016) runs in parallel throughout his career — credited as “Performer [All Instruments],” with contributions extending to drum programming, Warr guitar, guitar, and production.
