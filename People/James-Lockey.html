James Lockey

Filmmaker and musician. Discogs artist ID: 1000634. Brother of Justin Lockey. Known name variations: J. Lockey, Jim Lockey, Lockey.

Overview

James Lockey is primarily known as a filmmaker who crosses into music. On Discogs his profile states “Film Maker and musician.” His musical work spans two distinct modes: engineering/recording for other artists’ releases, and full membership in the supergroup Minor Victories alongside his brother Justin Lockey, Rachel Goswell, and Stuart Braithwaite. He and Justin have collaborated on records since at least 2007, often with James handling engineering while Justin produces.

He is also a member of Mastersystem, which he shares with Justin Lockey (Discogs group ID: 6363165).

Minor Victories

Minor Victories was the defining project of James Lockey’s recorded career to date. The self-titled debut (Fat Possum Records, 2016) credits him as one of four band members, as an additional engineer, and as a co-writer alongside Justin Lockey, Rachel Goswell, Steve Clarke (22), and Stuart Braithwaite. Both brothers are credited as “Lockey” in the written-by field. Justin Lockey handled production and mixing (with Paul Gregory); James contributed engineering.

The 2017 Orchestral Variations (Play It Again Sam) credits all four original members under “Minor Victories Are:”, with Justin additionally taking orchestration and production credit.

Engineering Work (2007–2019)

James’s engineering credits cluster around the UK indie scene in the late 2000s and into the 2010s. Recurring context:

  • The British Expeditionary Force (2007, Erased Tapes): early collaboration with Justin, who wrote, performed, produced, and mixed the record; James performed low frequencies.
  • White Belt Yellow Tag (2009, Distiller Records): engineered both the Methods LP and the Remains E.P. / Tell Your Friends singles. Justin Lockey contributed writing on these releases, suggesting the brothers worked as a loose unit around this project.
  • Codes In The Clouds (2009, Erased Tapes): engineered Paper Canyon; Justin produced and mixed.
  • Cagework (2019, Physical Education Recordings): engineered the self-titled debut; Justin produced and mixed.

The pattern across 2009 and 2019 is consistent — James engineers, Justin produces — suggesting an ongoing informal working partnership.

Photography

In 2017 James provided artist photography for Neil McSweeney’s A Coat Worth Wearing (Hudson Records), credited as “Jim Lockey.” This is consistent with his stated background as a filmmaker.
