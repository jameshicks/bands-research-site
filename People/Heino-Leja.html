Heino Leja

Discogs ID: 4567417
Origin: Germany
Studio: Sector 5 Studios (in-house mastering facility of Optimal Media GmbH), Röbel/Müritz, Germany

German mastering and lacquer cutting engineer. One of the most prolific vinyl cutting engineers in Discogs, with over 6,600 credited releases spanning 1998 to the present (as of 2026). Identified in runouts by the etched/stamped initials HL.

Studio

Sector 5 Studios is the in-house audio mastering facility of Optimal Media GmbH, a major European pressing plant located in Villa B, Röbel/Müritz, Germany. The studio opened in 1997 and offers lacquer cutting, DMM cutting, premastering, restoration, and HD mastering services. Equipment includes Neumann VMS 70, VMS 80, and VMS 82 cutting lathes and a Studer A820 master tape recorder.

Studio manager: Thorsten Wyk (TW). Other colleagues at Sector 5 include Andre Muchow (AM), Björn Wolfram (BW), Jörg Peters (JP), Martin Krafft (KR), Marko Kluge (MK), Steffan Jeschek (SJ), Stefan Tenner (ST).

Career Scale

~6,607 releases in Discogs (1998–2026). Annual output peaked at 492 releases in 2020, with sustained high-volume periods around 2000–2003 (291–355/year) and 2016 (403). Active continuously across the full span.
