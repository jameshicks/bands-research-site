Biography

Claremont Collective, 1992–1994

Sarah Arslanian (née Sarah Coleman) was part of the loose collective of women who performed on early The Mountain Goats cassette releases during the project’s Claremont, California period. The Mountain Goats in this era was primarily John Darnielle’s project, but several of the 1992–1994 releases credit a rotating lineup of five performers — Darnielle, Rachel Ware, Amy Piatt, Rosanne Lindley, and Arslanian — variously as “Band [The Mountain Goats Are]” or “Performer [We Are].”

Her Discogs credits span five releases across 1992–1994, all on Shrimper or Ajax Records (2). These are cassette and small-press releases characteristic of the boombox lo-fi aesthetic Darnielle pursued through the mid-1990s. On Songs For Petronius (1992) she appears under the name variation “Sara”; on Chile De Árbol (1993) as “Saaa-rah”; on Hot Garden Stomp (1993) as “Sarah”; on Beautiful Rat Sunset (1994) as “Sara”; on Zopilote Machine (1994) as “Saaarah” — the elaborate vowel-stretched spellings are a running stylistic quirk on Mountain Goats releases of this period shared by all five credited performers.

The Bright Mountain Choir

Arslanian is one of four members of The Bright Mountain Choir (Discogs ID: 1012113) — a collective entity comprising the four women in the early Mountain Goats lineup: Arslanian, Ware, Piatt, and Lindley. The Bright Mountain Choir does not appear to have released music independently; on Discogs it appears solely as “Recorded By” on Zopilote Machine (1994, Ajax Records (2)). The credit is understood to mean the four women collectively handled the recording of that album, operating alongside a “Recorded By” credit also held by The Mountain Goats themselves, and partial assistance credits for Keith Fitz and Steve Kronk.

Solo Work as Ladytown

Under the alias Ladytown (Discogs ID: 10320811), Arslanian released a self-titled full-length CD in 2003 on Spinout Records (2) (cat# spinout #102). The record is a 12-track folk/country-adjacent album. Track listing:

  1. Glass You Laid (3:28)
  2. The Big Invisible (3:37)
  3. Lolita (4:17)
  4. Give You Peace (4:36)
  5. 4 Years 7 Days (5:11)
  6. Dirt Is Dirt (5:55)
  7. Easy Come / It’s Alright (4:40)
  8. Wo Xiang Ni (I Miss You) (3:21)
  9. I’ll Give To You (3:38)
  10. There’s A Little Girl (5:04)
  11. Understood (2:53)
  12. Wilted Rose (3:11)

In 2014, a single digital release titled Drive (4:22) appeared credited jointly to “Sarah Coleman (4) Is Ladytown” as a self-released MP3 (Discogs ID: 21055981, label: Not On Label / Ladytown Self-released). Discogs genre tag: Folk, World, & Country. No other solo releases are documented on Discogs.
