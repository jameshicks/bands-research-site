Bil Emmons

Discogs ID: 428260
Full name: William J. Emmons
Also known as: Doctor Bill
Base: New York City
Studio home: Sear Sound (graduate and longtime freelance; 353 West 48th St., NYC)

Freelance recording engineer, active from the early 1990s to at least 2023. Built his early career as assistant engineer at Sear Sound — the West 48th Street studio founded by Walter Sear — accumulating credits on landmark records across jazz, alternative rock, and indie. By the mid-1990s he had moved into primary engineering roles, working at the nexus of the NYC indie underground (Jonathan Fire*Eater, Boss Hog, Soul Coughing) and major-label alternative (Sonic Youth, Soul Asylum). Long career tail into the 2010s–20s.

Boss Hog (1995): Engineered the Geffen LP. Produced by Jon Spencer and Steve Fisk. Lineup: Cristina Martinez, Jon Spencer, Hollis Queens, Jens Jurgensen.

Jonathan Fire*Eater (1996): Recorded the basic tracks for the Deceptive EP. The full session chain: Emmons (basic tracks), John Siket (recorded/mixed tracks 1–5), Greg Talenfeld (engineered track 6). Band: Stewart Lupton (vocals), Thomas Frank (bass), Paul Maroon (guitar), Walter Martin (organ, lap steel, piano), Matt Barrick (drums). Note: this is the pre-Walkmen lineup — Maroon, Martin, and Barrick all went on to form The Walkmen.

Soul Coughing (1996): Tracked on half the album. Notably track 6 (“4 Out Of 5”) featured Walter Sear himself on theremin — recorded at his own studio.

Sear Sound connection

Sear Sound (ID 265437) was Emmons’ primary base. Founded by Walter Sear (also a theremin player and analog synthesizer manufacturer) at 353 West 48th St., New York. Its house engineers included David Schoenwetter and Chris Allen (4). Emmons is listed as a “graduate” on the studio’s website — an unusual honorific indicating certified training through the studio’s engineer development pipeline. Many of his credits from the 1990s explicitly name Sear Sound in the credit text.
