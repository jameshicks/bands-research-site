Overview

UK visual artist, producer, and film director born in 1951, Mike Coles is best known for his decades-long association with Killing Joke and their label Malicious Damage, for which he handled artwork from 1979 through 2018. Working under Studio C (2), he produced original album art and reissue and remaster design for the band across four decades, accumulating over 380 credits. His collaborations extend across the Killing Joke orbit — Youth, Jaz Coleman, Geordie Walker, Paul Ferguson — and into the ambient and electronic world via The Orb and Alex Paterson, with his work spanning post-punk, industrial, dub, and ambient aesthetics.
