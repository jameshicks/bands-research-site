Morten Joh

Discogs IDs: 10921444 (as Morten Joh) / 457501 (as Morten J. Olsen — primary entry) Also credited as: Morten J. Olsen, Morten Johan Olsen, Morten “Kasserolle” Olsen, MJO, M.J.O Real name: Morten Johan Olsen

Norwegian drummer, percussionist, and multi-instrumentalist based out of the Stavanger and later Berlin scenes. Also plays vibraphone, synthesizer, piano, computer (SuperCollider 3), jew’s harp, and langeleik. Active as performer, producer, and recording/mixing engineer. Discogs lists two entries for him — the fuller career page is under Morten J. Olsen (Discogs ID 457501); the “Morten Joh” credit (ID 10921444) appears primarily on his recent collaborative and solo releases.

Overview

Olsen emerged from the experimental rock and free improv community in Stavanger, Norway in the early 2000s. His foundational project is MoHa!, a noise/improv duo with guitarist Anders Hana, which released four albums on Rune Grammofon (2004–2008). Alongside MoHa!, he co-founded Ultralyd — a larger Stavanger group also involving Hana, Kjetil D Brandsdal, and initially saxophonist Frode Gjerstad — releasing on FMR Records and Load Records before moving to Rune Grammofon.

From around 2009 onward he relocated or split his activities toward Berlin, joining the structured-improvisation quartet The Pitch (2) with Boris Baltschun, Koen Nutters, and Michael Thieke; contributing to the large ensemble Splitter Orchester; and building a wide network of collaborators across the European experimental and New Music scenes. His later work — particularly under the “Morten Joh” credit — moves into solo and duo territory, working with Julia Reidy, Stine Janvin, and the Ryfylke-rooted folk/drone project Naaljos Ljom.

He has also been a regular recording and mixing engineer for projects associated with Anders Hana and the broader Stavanger/Motvind Records circle.
