Robert Görl

Robert Görl

Oct 10, 202611 min read

Robert Görl

Discogs ID: 15130
Real name: Robert Görl
Born: 15 June 1955, Munich, Germany
Roles: Drummer, electronic musician, composer, producer
Active: 1978–present

German musician. Co-founder and primary instrumentalist of Deutsch Amerikanische Freundschaft (DAF). On the three Virgin LPs (1981–82) — the core DAF catalog — Görl composed all music and played every instrument; Gabi Delgado provided vocals and lyrics, and Conny Plank produced. After Delgado’s death in March 2020, Görl assembled archival DAF material with new lyrics he wrote and sang himself for Nur Noch Einer (Grönland Records, 2021).

Biography by Era

1978–1979 — Formation and Debut

Robert Görl co-founded DAF in Düsseldorf in 1978, initially as a five-piece with Delgado, Kurt Dahlke, Michael Kemner, and Wolfgang Spelmans. The debut LP Produkt Der Deutsch-Amerikanischen Freundschaft (Warning Records NR. 00001, 1979) was self-released on their own imprint. 22 untitled tracks across two sides. Görl plays drums; the lineup reflects the punk-influenced multi-member formation before the group stripped down.

He was also briefly involved with Der Plan in the early period, through shared connections with Dahlke and Chrislo Haas (who joined DAF circa 1979, replacing Dahlke). Der Plan roster overlaps significantly: Dahlke, Haas, Frank Fenstermacher, Moritz R, and Görl all appear as members.

1980 — London, Mute, Die Kleinen Und Die Bösen

DAF relocated to London in 1980 and signed to Mute. With Conny Plank engineering and remixing, Die Kleinen Und Die Bösen (Mute STUMM 1, 1980) became the transitional record: still a multi-member band (Görl: drums, vocals; Haas: synthesizer/tape/saxophone; Spelmans: guitar/synthesizer; Delgado: vocals), produced and partly remixed by Plank at Conny’s Studio. Görl also played drums on the Eurythmics debut In The Garden (RCA, 1981, produced by Conny Plank) — he appears on track A2 alongside Jaki Liebezeit and Clem Burke as the album’s three drummers.

1981–1982 — The Virgin Trilogy (DAF’s Peak)

Signed to Virgin, DAF settled as a strict duo with Görl responsible for all composition and performance, Delgado for all words and vocals, and Conny Plank producing.

Alles Ist Gut (Virgin 203 644, 1981)
All music and instruments: Görl. Words/vocals: Delgado. Producer: Conny Plank. Contains “Der Mussolini” — DAF’s most remixed and re-released track — alongside “Der Räuber Und Der Prinz,” “Rote Lippen,” and “Tanz Den Mussolini.” Defines the EBM template: minimalist drum machine, single bass synth, Delgado’s provocative German-language delivery.

Gold Und Liebe (Virgin V2218, 1981)
All instruments: Görl. Lyrics/vocals: Delgado. Producer: Conny Plank. Contains “El Que,” “Absolute Körperkontrolle,” “Verschwende Deine Jugend.” Görl co-credited on lyrics for “Als Wär’s Das Letzte Mal” (A2) alongside Delgado. Cover concept credited jointly to both. 36+ pressings.

Für Immer (Virgin 204 956, 1982)
All instruments: Görl. Music by: Görl. Words/vocals: Delgado. Producer: Conny Plank (credited as C. Plank). Artwork concept: Görl + Delgado jointly. Photography: Peter Gruchot. Contains “Im Dschungel Der Liebe,” “Ein Bißchen Krieg,” “Kebab Träume,” “Verlieb Dich In Mich.” The band split before the album’s release.

Singles from this period include “Der Mussolini” (Virgin UK, 1981), “Der Räuber Und Der Prinz / Rote Lippen” (Virgin Germany), “Der Räuber Und Der Prinz / Tanz Mit Mir” (Mute UK), “Goldenes Spielzeug / Gold Gold Gold” (Virgin), “Als Wär’s Das Letzte Mal / Der Mussolini” (Virgin Germany), “Verlieb Dich In Mich / Ein Bisschen Krieg,” “Sex Unter Wasser / Knochen Auf Knochen,” “Kebab-Träume / Ein Bisschen Krieg.”

1983–1984 — Solo Debut (Mit Dir., Night Full Of Tension), Darling Don’t Leave Me

After the first DAF split Görl launched a solo career on Mute.

Mit Dir. (Mute, 1983) — debut solo single. Co-produced by Görl and Daniel Miller. Written by Görl. Multiple pressings across Europe; also issued in an English-language version as “With You (Mit Dir).”

Darling Don’t Leave Me (Mute/Powderworks, 1983) — solo single. Görl as performer, songwriter, and producer. Co-produced by Görl and Mike Hedges. Wide international distribution: Mute (UK), Powderworks (Australia), Elektra (US), Vogue (France), RCA Victor. The most geographically spread of his solo releases.

Night Full Of Tension (Mute, 1984) — debut solo LP. All music and performance by Görl. Co-produced by Görl and Mike Hedges. Design: Martyn Atkins. Photography: Sabine Raef. 18 pressings globally across Mute, Elektra, Vogue, Powderworks.

1985–1987 — DAF Reunion (Illuminated/Dean Records); Side Projects

DAF reunited in 1985 under Illuminated Records with Görl and Delgado self-producing:

  • Absolute Body Control (Mix Solid) 12” (Illuminated, 1985) — Görl: performer, producer, arranger, music
  • Brothers (Illuminated LEV 65, 1985) — Görl: written-by, producer, performer, arranger

Moved to Dean Records:

  • 1st Step To Heaven (1986) — Görl: music, recording, mixing (with uncredited DAF)
  • Voulez Vous Coucher Avec Moi Ce Soir (1986) — Görl: music
  • Pure Joy 12” (1986) — Görl: music
  • The Gun (1987)

Temple (2) (Discogs ID: 179618) — duo of Görl and E. Jäger (real name R. Görl & E. Jäger per Discogs). Released Keep Your Body In Trance and Your Mind Is Your Screen (both Disko B, 1993).

Elektro (Discogs ID: 292993) — duo of Görl and Peter Kuhlmann. Released Elektro LP (Fax +49-69/450464, 1996).

Heat (Discogs ID: 1163) — trio of Görl, Klaus Löschner, and Markus Löhr. Released Heat (Harthouse, 1996). Görl: producer, written-by.

Conny Plank died 17 December 1987.

1988–1992 — Lower Profile

The DAF D.A.F. compilation LP (Virgin, 1988) circulated widely (9+ pressings). Görl played drums and synthesizer on Tales Of Taboo (Pow Wow Art International, 1986) and The Truth Is Hard To Swallow (D-Day/Pow Wow Art International, 1988) — both associated with the Belgian scene. Electric Marilyn (Hangman Records, 1991) — Görl as producer and primary credit; co-written with D.C. LaRue; features Behaviør and Chanin Warakulnukroh.

1993–2000 — Solo on Disko B

A cluster of solo releases on Munich’s Disko B label:

  • Psycho (Disko B, 1993) — written with Kotai and Good Groove (Klaus Löschner)
  • Psycho (Remixes) (Disko B, 1993) — remixed by Richard Bartz, Kotai, Good Groove, SST (8)
  • (Psycho) Therapie compilation (Disko B, 1994) — collecting the 1993–94 Disko B solo material
  • Therapie (Disko B, 1994) — written with Kotai
  • Happy Gathering / U.F.N. (Disko B, 1995) — Görl as producer
  • Watch The Great Copycat (Disko B, 1996) — Görl: written-by, producer
  • Yip Yep (Disko B, 1996)
  • Sexdrops (Disko B, 1998) — remixes by Karl O’Connor (Regis), Robert Henke
  • Final Metal Pralinées (Disko B, 2000)

Dark Tool Symphony (Gift, Japan, 2007) — Japanese-label release.

2003 — DAF Return: Fünfzehn Neue DAF Lieder

A second DAF comeback. Görl wrote music for 14 of 15 tracks (Delgado wrote track 3); Delgado handled lyrics, vocals, recording, mixing, and production on his tracks; Görl recorded, mixed, performed, and produced throughout.

2017–2020 — Grönland Records, Final DAF Activity

  • Das Ist DAF (Grönland Records, 2017) — major retrospective compilation; Görl: all instruments (historical), cover/design concept credits
  • Reworx (Grönland Records, 2018) — DAF remix album
  • The Paris Tapes (Grönland Records, 2018) — solo Görl release; music by Görl; recorded with Walter Schönauer, Steffan Jeschek, Jens Balzer; 3 pressings
  • Part 1 (Vince Clarke Remix) (Grönland Records, 2018) — solo Görl single remixed by Vince Clarke
  • Ich Denk An Dich (Grönland Records, 2020) — DAF single; Görl: producer; co-produced with Sylvie Marks

Gabi Delgado died of a heart attack, 22 March 2020.

2021 — Nur Noch Einer

After Delgado’s death, Görl completed an album drawing on archival DAF material, writing new lyrics and singing them himself — the first time Görl sang lead on a DAF release. Released as DAF: Nur Noch Einer (Grönland Records, CD + vinyl, 2021). Credited to DAF but Görl-only production. Co-produced by Sylvie Marks. Inner sleeve text by Hanna Rollmann. Photography: Iain McKell. Design: Walter Schönauer.

Connections

  • Deutsch Amerikanische Freundschaft — co-founder; composed all music on the Virgin trilogy; continued as sole surviving member post-2020
  • Gabi Delgado — co-founder and sole long-term DAF partner; their division of labour (Görl: all music/instruments, Delgado: all words/vocals) defined the group’s identity
  • Conny Plank — producer of the 1980–82 DAF catalog; Die Kleinen Und Die Bösen through Für Immer
  • Daniel Miller — Mute Records founder; co-produced Mit Dir. (1983)
  • Mike Hedges — primary collaborator on early solo work (1983–84)
  • Der Plan — shared Düsseldorf/NDW network; Görl listed as member
  • Kurt Dahlke — DAF original member; shared Der Plan membership; co-founded Ata Tak
