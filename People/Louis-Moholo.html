Louis Moholo / Louis Moholo-Moholo

Discogs artist ID: 314299
Also credited as: Louis Moholo-Moholo (from around 2005 onward)
Born: 1940, Cape Town, South Africa
Instrument: Drums, percussion, vocals

South African drummer; member of the Blue Notes and the longest continuously active figure on Ogun. One of the most influential drummers in European free jazz and free improvisation, known for an approach that bridges South African township rhythms and free improvisation.

Overview

Moholo was the drummer in the Blue Notes when the group left South Africa in 1964. He remained in London after the Blue Notes disbanded as a working unit, becoming a central presence in both the South African exile community and the British free improvisation scene. He played on virtually every major Ogun release from 1974 onward — as sideman on Brotherhood and Blue Notes sessions, as leader with the Louis Moholo Octet, and through a series of groups under his own name.

From around 2005 he began crediting himself as Louis Moholo-Moholo, adding his clan name.
