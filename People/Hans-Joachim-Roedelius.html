Hans-Joachim Roedelius

Born 26 August 1934, Berlin. German electronic musician, keyboardist, and composer. Discogs artist ID: 105272. Core member of Cluster.

Overview

With Dieter Moebius, Roedelius formed Cluster (early spelling: Kluster), one of the foundational electronic duos of German music. Nearly all their major recordings were engineered or produced by Conny Plank. He also participated in Harmonia (with Michael Rother) and the celebrated collaborations with Brian Eno.
