Overview

Mark Knopfler is a British guitarist, singer, and songwriter who grew up in Newcastle, England after being born in Glasgow in 1949, and co-founded Dire Straits with his brother David Knopfler in the late 1970s. His Discogs catalogue runs from 1970 to the present, with output concentrated in the 1990s and 2000s as both a solo artist and film composer in the years following Dire Straits’ dissolution. His most frequent collaborators are drawn largely from the Dire Straits core — John Illsley, Guy Fletcher, Alan Clark, Pick Withers, and Terry Williams — alongside longtime engineer and producer Neil Dorfsman. Vertigo and Mercury have been his primary label homes since 1978, with Warner Bros. handling North American releases through his solo run.
