Chris Cutler

Chris Cutler

Sep 27, 20261 min read

Chris Cutler

Discogs ID: 177209 Born: January 4, 1947, Washington, D.C. (raised in England)

British-American drummer, writer, label head. Drummer in Henry Cow (1971–1978), Art Bears, News From Babel, Cassiber. Founder and director of ReR Megacorp. Also played with Pere Ubu, Aksak Maboul, and many avant-garde collaborations.

Member of The Black Sheep — an international collective bridging the UK avant-garde and the Dutch underground, with fellow Henry Cow alumnus Geoff Leigh and Netherlands-based musicians Colin McLure, Loek Van Saus, Maggie Thomas, Ronnie Krepel, and others. The Black Sheep released Alive In Beograd (1987, Nikad Robom, cat. 007) — a live cassette from a Belgrade performance fitting the ReR network’s Eastern European touring activity.

Joined David Thomas And The Pedestrians for three tracks on Variations On A Theme (Plurex, 1983) and as core member/composer on More Places Forever (Rough Trade, 1985) — bringing the Henry Cow compositional collective approach to the project. Also drummer and producer on David Thomas And The Wooden Birds’ Blame The Messenger (Rough Trade, 1987), alongside Jim Jones (3) and Allen Ravenstine.

Published “File Under Popular” (1984), essays on experimental music.
