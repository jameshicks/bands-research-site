JG Thirlwell

Discogs IDs: 15025 (as J.G. Thirlwell), 5125 (as Foetus), 222158 (as Clint Ruin)
Real name: James George Thirlwell
Born: January 29, 1960, Melbourne, Australia
Aliases: Foetus, Clint Ruin, Steroid Maximus, Manorexia, Xordox, Baby Zizanie, Wiseblood (duo with Roli Mosimann)

Australian-born avant-garde composer and producer. Moved to London around 1980, then relocated to New York City mid-1980s. Recorded prolifically as Foetus (and numerous Foetus-derived monikers) on his own Self Immolation imprint and Some Bizzare Records. As Clint Ruin, produced Fur Bible’s two New Rose Records releases (1985) — the short-lived London band formed by Kid Congo Powers and Patricia Morrison. Prolific collaborator across industrial, post-punk, and avant-garde scenes. Scored the animated series The Venture Bros. (Williams Street / Ectopic Ents, 2009–2025) and Archer (seasons 7–9). Long-running Wiseblood duo partnership with Roli Mosimann. See full article at Foetus (JG Thirlwell).
