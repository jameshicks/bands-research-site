William Socolov

Record label co-founder and music industry figure. New York City. Discogs artist ID: 263720 (listed as “Will Socolov”).

Overview

William Socolov is a New York-based record label entrepreneur who co-founded two of the defining NYC independent labels of the 1980s. His longest-running venture, Sleeping Bag Records, was one of the most eclectic independent labels of the era — launching with downtown avant-garde dance music and expanding through funk, freestyle, and hip-hop over its eleven-year run.

Sleeping Bag Records

Co-founded 1981 with Arthur Russell and Juggy Gales. Label ID: 3379.

The label’s first release was Dinosaur L’s 24→24 Music (1981, cat. TLX-0) — a 12-track LP made in the downtown NYC dance/loft music tradition, co-produced by Russell, with contributions from Peter Gordon (tenor sax), Peter Zummo (trombone), Julius Eastman (keyboards, vocals), and Ed Tomney (guitar). It remained an experimental outlier on the label; the bulk of the Sleeping Bag catalog quickly moved toward club-oriented dance and hip-hop.

Key elements of the label’s trajectory:

  • 1981–1983: Downtown dance and early NYC club music — Dinosaur L, Urban Blight, Loose Joints (“Tell You (Today),” 1983 — actually credited to Sleeping Bag/4th & Broadway), Class Action / Christine Wiltshire (“Weekend,” SLX-001, 1983, co-produced by Bob Blank).
  • 1984: Expansion into funk, post-punk cross-licensing — Konk (Your Life, SLX009), APB (2) (What Kind Of Girl?, SLX-12), Felix (2), Bonzo Goes To Washington (“5 Minutes,” SLX-13).
  • 1985–1987: Hip-hop breaks through. Kurtis Mantronik (Mantronix) joined as A&R executive and recorded for the label — “Needle To The Groove” (SLX-15, 1985), The Album (TLX-6, 1985), Music Madness (TLX-8, 1986). Joyce Sims and Nocera anchored the freestyle side.
  • 1988–1990: EPMD, Cash Money & Marvelous, The Todd Terry Project, and T La Rock gave the label genuine hip-hop credibility. Kariya’s “Let Me Love You For Tonight” (SBUK 4T, 1988) became a significant UK house/garage moment.
  • Sub-label Fresh Records (ID 11356, founded 1984) — operated under the same Phase One Network umbrella, focused on dance and hip-hop releases.

The label closed in 1992. Its catalog sat dormant until 1996, when Warlock Records purchased it and began re-releasing titles. The catalog is presently controlled by Phase One Network. Label code: LC 7241.

Freeze Records

Co-founded Freeze Records with Todd Terry. Discogs lists this separately from Sleeping Bag; Freeze Records (ID 2593) was later absorbed under In House Records.

Record Pressing (Post-Label)

After Sleeping Bag closed, Socolov moved into the record pressing business in Brooklyn. He was involved first with EKS Manufacturing (label ID 227959) and subsequently with Brooklyn Vinyl Works (label ID 985396).
