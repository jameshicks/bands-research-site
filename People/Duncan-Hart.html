Cellist based in Aberdeen. Member of Matricarians (Aberdeen improv quartet, Discogs ID 3137193), and a featured contributor on Alan Davidson (4)‘s Kitchen Cynics project. Named in the Kitchen Cynics Discogs profile as one of Davidson’s primary recurring collaborators on cello.

Overview

Hart appears in Aberdeen’s closely-knit experimental folk / free improvisation circle centred on Alan Davidson (4) and the Les Enfants Du Paradiddle micro-label. He is a founding member of Matricarians, a free-improvisation quartet whose other members — Davidson (credited as “Alan Cynic”), David Mackay (2), and Susan Matthew — all orbit the Kitchen Cynics project in various configurations.

His two documented Discogs credits span 2008–2009 and both fall within this Aberdeen network. Beyond Matricarians, he appeared as a featured cellist on the Kitchen Cynics release Disconnected (2009), alongside violinist Beth Anderson (3) and slide guitarist Philip Johnston.

No aliases or name variations are registered in Discogs beyond the single name variant “Hart”.

Release notes

Matricarians (2006, ENF 28, CDr)
Self-titled debut album by the quartet. 10 tracks of improvised folk/free music — titles drawn from Scottish folk material (“Jock Sheep”, “The Fraserburgh Meal Riot”, “By Yon Clear-Rinnin’ Burnie”, “The Hangman’s Wife”, “The Nightingale”, etc.). Genre tagged Folk, World, & Country / Lo-Fi on Discogs. Writing credits shared by Davidson and Susan Matthew.

An Absence Of Obstacles (2008, ENF 62, CDr)
Second Matricarians album. 10 tracks. All four members credited as “Music By”. Genre: Folk, World, & Country. Track titles include “By Aikey Side”, “Palette Of Circumstances”, “She Dived Down, He Swam Up”, “Little String”. Davidson is credited under his alias “Alan Cynic” on this release.

Disconnected (2009, enf 65, CDr)
Kitchen Cynics album (primary artist: Kitchen Cynics, credited as “The Kitchen Cynics”). Hart appears as featured cellist alongside Beth Anderson (3) (violin) and Philip Johnston (slide guitar). 16 tracks including “The Wind”, “Hoodie Craw”, “Oystercatchers”, “Black Is The Colour”, “Me & Arthur Farquhar”, “Now’s The Time / Clear Rinnin’ Burnie”.
