Guigou Chenevier

Discogs ID: 488540
French drummer, saxophonist, vocalist, and composer. Core member of Etron Fou Leloublan (1973–1986) and one of the most active figures in the French and international avant-rock scene across five decades. Name variations include “Guigou ‘Samba Scout’ Chenevier.”
