Alasdair Roberts

Discogs ID: 258097 Born: Swabia, Germany (raised in Scotland) Active: 1996–present Genres: Folk, Traditional Scottish Folk, Experimental Folk, Acoustic Primary Labels: Drag City, Stone Tape Recordings, Clay Pipe Music

Scottish folk musician and songwriter. Born in Swabia, Germany; raised in Scotland. Founding member and primary creative force of Appendix Out (1996–2001). Extensive solo discography on Drag City (2001–present), with additional releases on Stone Tape Recordings, Clay Pipe Music, and various collaborators’ labels. Also a member of International Airport, Amalgamated Sons Of Rest, Concerto Caledonia, Green Ribbons, The Fruit Tree Foundation, The Furrow Collective, and Three Queens In Mourning.

Biography

Early Work / Appendix Out (1996–2001)

See Appendix Out for the full Appendix Out history. Roberts served as primary songwriter throughout the group’s 1996–2001 run, responsible for all released material on Palace Records, Drag City, Creeping Bent, and Temporary Residence Limited. He played guitar, vocals, keyboards, mandolin, violin, piano, dulcimer, bulbul tarang, and various other instruments across the band’s recordings.

Solo Career — Drag City Period (2001–2018)

Roberts launched his solo career with The Crook Of My Arm (Secretly Canadian, SC54, 2001), followed rapidly by Farewell Sorrow (Rough Trade, RTRADCD152, 2003), which reached a wider UK audience. His move to Drag City began with No Earthly Man (2005), a traditional Scottish folk album drawing on unaccompanied ballads (dulcimer, guitar, keyboards). This marked a shift toward deep engagement with the Scottish ballad tradition.

Key solo albums on Drag City:

  • No Earthly Man (2005, DC305) — traditional trad. arr. material; dulcimer, guitar
  • The Amber Gatherers (2007, DC359) — written originals; acoustic guitar, dulcimer, sampling keyboard
  • Spoils (2009, DC420) — guitar, vocals
  • The Wyrd Meme (2009, DC433) — guitar (K Yairi), synthesizer (Yamaha), drums, feedback, vocals
  • Too Long In This Condition (2010) — arranged by Roberts
  • Urstan (2012, DC492) — guitar, voice
  • A Wonder Working Stone (2013, DC517) — acoustic guitar, voice; mixed by Roberts
  • Alasdair Roberts (2015, DC556) — self-titled; most fully produced; bass guitar, electric guitar, organ, snare
  • Plaint Of Lapwing (2016, Clay Pipe Music) — produced, mixed, and liner notes by Roberts; includes Nashville guitar, piano, cello, synth, percussion
  • Pangs (2017, DC613) — acoustic guitar, Nashville guitar, electric guitar, metallophone, synthesizer; botanical drawings by Roberts
  • What News (2018, DC643) — voice, electric guitar; final studio album for Drag City through this period

Collaborations and Affiliations

Roberts has appeared on releases well outside his folk core. Notable credits:

  • Amalgamated Sons Of Rest — group project; Amalgamated Sons Of Rest LP (Galaxia, 2002); Roberts credited as Performer
  • Nothing We Can Control (Geographic, 2000) — as International Airport; chanter, electronics, percussion, piano, bulbul tarang, violin, vocals; album mixed by John McEntire
  • Ghost Tropic / The Lioness / Protection Spells (Secretly Canadian, 2000) — “Music By” credit; likely overlap with solo/Appendix Out transitional work
  • Hirta Songs (Stone Tape Recordings, 2013) — central project; voice, acoustic guitar, electric guitar, keyboards, cello, drum; precentor for congregation; music by Roberts
  • A Selection Of Marches, Quicksteps, Laments, Strathspeys Reels And Country Dances (Room40, 2010) — guitar

Notable co-appearances and guest credits include work with Sean O’Hagan (producer on The Night Is Advancing), live/session work across the Scottish folk revival circuit, and liner notes for Whaur The Pig Gaed On The Spree (Drag City, 2011), a compilation of Alan Lomax’s 1951–57 Scottish recordings compiled and annotated by Roberts — reflecting his deep engagement with the scholarly side of the tradition.

Later Work (2019–Present)

  • The Fiery Margin (Drag City, 2019) — vocals, guitar; written by Roberts
  • The Old Fabled River (Drag City, 2021) — guitar, voice
  • Grief In The Kitchen And Mirth In The Hall (Drag City, 2023) — acoustic guitar, piano, voice; arranged by Roberts
  • Twa Brothers (L-13, 2024) — primary artist; vinyl release
  • Remembered In Exile (Songs And Ballads From Nova Scotia) (Drag City, 2025) — voice, guitar, synthesizer; arranged by Roberts

Roberts has also contributed vocals or guitar to a wide range of other artists’ recordings: Janus (Matt Kivel, Driftless Recordings, 2015/16, producer credit), To Awake In That Heaven Of Freedom (Excelsior Recordings, 2016), Wild Hog (Hudson Records, 2016), Head Above The Water (Fire Records, 2020, co-producer and electric guitar), If A City Is Set Upon A Hill (HomAleph, 2022), and more.
