Gregg Dickson

Executive Producer and co-founder of Large Records (Chicago, IL), one of the foundational Chicago deep house labels. Dickson co-launched Large Records in 1993 alongside Jeff Craven and simultaneously co-founded the related imprints 8th Bar and Swing Street as part of the same founding effort.

  • Discogs ID: 3481487
  • Name variations: G Dickson, G. Dickson
  • Roles: Executive Producer, Label Co-founder
  • Based: Chicago, IL
  • Years active (Discogs): 1993–1995

Career

Gregg Dickson entered the Discogs record as a co-founder and Executive Producer at the inception of Large Records in 1993. He shares the founding credit with Jeff Craven; the two are jointly listed as Executive Producer on every early Large Records release. Together they also launched two related imprints that became Discogs-recognized sublabels of Large Records: 8th Bar (Discogs ID: 597655) and Swing Street (Discogs ID: 143550).

Dickson’s first Executive Producer credit is on I’m Leavin’ You by Ulysses (LJ-003, 1993) — the label’s debut release, produced by Lil’ John Coleman. He continues in that role across the entire early Large Records catalog through 1995, covering releases on both the main Large Records imprint and the 8th Bar and Swing Street sublabels.

His credits run in lockstep with Jeff Craven’s through mid-1994. From late 1994 onward, some releases show Craven credited as A&R rather than Executive Producer (e.g., Dub International by Thrillseekers, Feel Love by David Brown (37), Vital Signs by DJ Fiasco), while Dickson retains the Executive Producer credit. His final documented credit in Discogs is Love Wonder (LJ-015, 1995) by Vital Signs (an alias of Johnny Fiasco / Juan Lopez) featuring Ulysses, produced by Johnny Fiasco.

No Discogs credits for Dickson appear after 1995. Whether he remained involved with the label informally or departed is not documented in the database.

One notable discovery from the credits: the Dub International (LJ-012) release credits its primary artist as “The Thrill Seekers” (Discogs: Thrillseekers), which is a production alias of Jordan Fields (Discogs ID: 161911) — not the UK trance act of the same name.
