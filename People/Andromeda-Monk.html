Biography

Andromeda Monk is a Vancouver-based woodwind player whose Discogs credits span 2011 to 2022 under two names: Ridley Veronicah Bishop (her longstanding credit identity on primary-ensemble releases) and Andromeda Monk (used on guest/session appearances and solo/Bandcamp work). The profile note under the Ridley Veronicah Bishop entry reads “often appears on releases as Ridley Bishop,” indicating a further name abbreviation in common use.

Her earliest documented ensemble work is with Thee AHs, a Vancouver-area group whose members include Davina Shell, Mareesa Holmes, and Sarah Lowenbot. Bishop played bass and alto saxophone on the band’s 2011 cassette Thee AHs Nation (Not On Label) and returned for the 2014 EP Corey’s Coathangers (Birdtapes) on bass guitar and vocals.

In parallel she was a founding member of Sprïng (members: Elliot Langford, Joseph Hirabayashi, Ridley Veronicah Bishop), whose 2014 self-released LP Celebrations features her on bass, vocals, and saxophone.

Her most substantial composer credit is Breathing Machines (2017), released as “Narwhal (4) Plays The Music Of Ridley Bishop.” This project — led by Narwhal (4) with Bishop as primary artist, composer, arranger, mixer, vocalist, bass clarinetist, and bassist — features a large ensemble of Vancouver musicians. Jared Burrows co-directed, produced, and co-arranged the record.

On the guest/session side, Bishop played alto saxophone on track 8 (“Dreams Come True”) of Dumb (8)‘s Beach Church (2016, Not On Label), and saxophone on Dumb (8)‘s Club Nites (2019, Mint Records (12)), credited as Ridley Veronicah Bishop on track A4. She also contributed bass clarinet to Gentle Party’s God Complex (2022, Phonometrograph), a harp-led Vancouver ensemble project led by Elisa Thorn.

Bishop also appears under her alias as saxophone player on Adrian Teacher And The Subs’ Terminal City (2016, You’ve Changed Records).
