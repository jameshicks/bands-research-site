Jeff Hunt

Music producer and graphic designer. Discogs artist ID: 399900. Name variation: J. Hunt.

Founder of Table Of The Elements (founded 1993) and all its sublabels including Radium (3). Based in Atlanta. Served as executive producer and art director across at least nine Radium (3) releases (2006–2010) and is the central figure behind the Table Of The Elements catalog, which spans minimalist composition, drone, and experimental music. Also wrote liner notes for several releases.
