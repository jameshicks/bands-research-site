Skin

Born Deborah Anne Dyer, 3 August 1967, Brixton, London. Lead vocalist, songwriter, guitarist, DJ. Discogs artist ID: 11539.

Overview

Skin is the lead vocalist and co-principal songwriter of Skunk Anansie, the UK rock band formed in 1994. One of the most prominent Black female rock vocalists of her era, she co-writes primarily with guitarist Len Arran and is credited across all six Skunk Anansie studio albums. Her powerful, wide-ranging soprano and shaved-head visual identity made her one of the defining figures of the mid-1990s Britrock scene. Skunk Anansie signed to One Little Indian in 1994 (UK releases) and Virgin (international), disbanding in 2001 before reforming in 2009.

After Skunk Anansie’s first split, Skin released two solo studio albums — Fleshwounds (2003, EMI) and Fake Chemical State (2006, V2) — and accumulated an extensive catalogue of guest vocal appearances spanning rock, house, techno, and Italian pop. She has been particularly active in Italy, collaborating with Italian rock and pop acts including Marlene Kuntz and Le Vibrazioni. From 2009 onwards she also pursued a DJ career under the moniker D-Dyer, playing techno and tech-house. In 2015 she co-released the Breed EP with techno producer Nicole Moudaber on Mood Records (4).

She was appointed OBE in the 2021 Birthday Honours for services to music. Her memoir It Takes Blood and Guts, co-written with Lucy O’Brien, was published by Simon & Schuster in September 2020. From January 2021 she has served as Chancellor of Leeds Arts University. (Wikipedia: Skin (musician))

Alias on Discogs: Deborah Anne Dyer (no separate Discogs artist entry). Name variations: Skin (Skunk Anansie), Skin Of Skunk Anansie. Discogs ID 11539 is the sole main entry.
