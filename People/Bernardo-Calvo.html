Overview

Bernardo Calvo Fernández is a Spanish audio technician, producer, and owner of Studio B Madrid, working primarily as a recording engineer, mixer, and mastering engineer. His credits are concentrated in the 2010s and 2020s, heavily centred on Sonido Muchacho (10 works, 2012–2024), Spain’s leading indie imprint, which anchors much of his catalogue. His closest collaborators include Juan Pedrayes, the members of Carolina Durante, and British producer Martin Glover (Youth), pointing to a range that stretches from Spanish indie rock to international post-punk production.
