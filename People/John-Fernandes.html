John Fernandes

Discogs ID: 374051
Name variations: J. Fernandes, J. K. Fernandes, John Kiran Fernandes, Jyoti Fernandez
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Fernandes_(musician)

American multi-instrumentalist (violin, clarinet, bass clarinet, bass guitar, vocals) based in Athens, Georgia. Co-founder of Cloud Recordings (with Will Cullen Hart, 2001). Core member of The Olivia Tremor Control and Circulatory System; long-running contributor across the Elephant 6 collective. Member of Dhana Jeera since 2024. One of the most prolific artists on Cloud Recordings, with releases as primary artist between 2011 and 2025.
