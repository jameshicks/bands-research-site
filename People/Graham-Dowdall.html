British percussionist and composer. Born 1954; died 16 June 2024. One of the most persistently connected figures in the Manchester post-punk underground, active across five decades.

Career Overview

Started drumming for Ludus in the early 1980s (Linder Sterling’s group on New Hormones). Became a member of Eric Random And The Bedlamites — credited on all three Bedlamites studio LPs (1982, 1984, 1986) as “Dids” (percussion / drums / electronics). Worked extensively with Cabaret Voltaire in their Sheffield orbit.

Perhaps most prominently: sustained touring and recording work with Nico (3) (the Velvet Underground vocalist), making several records with her including Camera Obscura (produced by John Cale).

Composed music for the African National Congress Choir; wrote the theme music for the Ugandan National Lottery. Composed for Fishpool Dance Theatre; a sound portrait of Moscow for BBC Radio 3 and a Channel 4 production company (Just TV).

Later projects included Suns Of Arqa, Moontwist, Bill Pritchard, Faction, Anglo-Japanese fusion group Mekong Zoo. Started his own Gagarin project in late 1995 (also traded as Pars Radio). Member of trance-pop band Sonexuno (released vinyl 12”s including on Yello’s Solid Pleasure label).
