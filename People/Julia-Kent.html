Julia Kent

Canadian cellist and composer. Based New York City. Discogs artist ID: 240833.

Overview

Julia Kent is a Canadian cellist and composer who has been a fixture of the New York experimental and chamber-folk scenes since the early 1990s. She is best known as a session cellist whose playing runs through the catalogs of Michael Gira’s Angels of Light, ANOHNI’s Antony and the Johnsons, and a wide range of adjacent experimental, post-rock, and gothic acts. Alongside her session work, she has built a substantial solo catalog of layered, loop-based cello music that ranges from quiet ambient minimalism to dense atmospheric drone — released primarily on Leaf (the UK imprint) and Important Records (2).

Kent appears on the Discogs member roster for Antony and the Johnsons, Rasputina, Motherhead Bug, black tape for a blue girl, Joe Gallant & Illuminati, and Parallel 41, reflecting a career that has crossed goth, new wave, chamber pop, drone, and avant-folk contexts. Her solo debut Delay (2007, Shayo) introduced a signature approach: cello looped and layered into ambient textures, combined with omnichord and field recordings. The Leaf albums — Character (2013), Asperities (2015), and Temporal (2018) — are the main statements of her mature solo work.

On Ultra Vivid Scene’s Rev (4AD, 1992), Kent appears alongside Melora Creager and Rasputina (entity credit) and Serena U. Jost as part of the cello section, with Kurt Ralske producing.

On the Jarboe compilation Pandora’s Box (2008), Kent played alongside Colin Marston (guitar, mixing, producing), Kevin Hufnagel, and Kris Force — reflecting the heavy/experimental crossover of the Jarboe orbit.

On the Larsen ABECEDA project (Important Records, 2007), Kent performed alongside Baby Dee (2), David Tibet (Current 93), Jóhann Jóhannsson, and Fabrizio Modonese Palumbo of Larsen — a rare meeting of new-classical, post-industrial, and experimental folk communities.
