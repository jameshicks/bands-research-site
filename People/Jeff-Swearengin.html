Jeff Swearengin

Experimental musician, producer, mastering engineer, and visual artist based in Los Angeles, CA. Discogs artist ID: 1112382. Active across industrial, EBM, and experimental electronics scenes primarily as a solo artist (Sleep Clinic, Brundle Fly) and as a member of several collaborative projects. Also contributes sound design and programming to larger productions.

Aliases & Projects

Solo aliases:

  • Sleep Clinic (Discogs ID: 559034) — primary solo vehicle; releases on Decisive Sound (2005) and Squarewav (2018)
  • Brundle Fly (Discogs ID: 756828) — alternate alias; no separate primary releases in DB, appears as alias attribution only

Groups:

  • -TENSE- (//TENSE//, Discogs ID: 1850893) — with Jake Schrock, Mariana Saldaña, Robert Lane, Seth Nemec. Released on Desire Records, Disaro, МИШКА (2007–2015); remixed by Beau Wanzer
  • Marching Dynamics (Discogs ID: 809002) — duo with Shane Talada. Released on Hymen Records and Waxtooth Records (2007–2014)
  • Warm Gadget (Discogs ID: 6452943) — duo with Colten Tyler Williams. Bandcamp/SoundCloud presence
  • Women Should Not Drink Alcohol (Discogs ID: 2962743) — with David Christian (2) and Rik Excev; released See You Next Tuesday (2012, Signifier)
