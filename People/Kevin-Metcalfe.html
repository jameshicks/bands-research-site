Kevin Metcalfe

Kevin Metcalfe

Oct 10, 20263 min read

Kevin Metcalfe

Discogs ID: 261923

Mastering engineer, operating primarily from Sounds Good studio (London). One of the most prolific mastering engineers in the Discogs database, with credits spanning from 1973 to 2025 — over 5,100 unique releases credited as “Mastered By” alone. Mastered every major Singers & Players album and is a long-standing regular in the On-U Sound production chain alongside Adrian Sherwood.

His earliest Discogs credit is a 1973 lacquer cut on David Bowie’s Pinups (RCA Victor). He began accumulating mastering credits from 1974 onward, working consistently across major and indie labels for over five decades.

Queen is his single largest client by a wide margin — the bulk of all Queen pressings and reissues across multiple decades passed through his hands, including The Miracle (1989), Innuendo (1991), and Greatest Hits II (1991). He also mastered the Freddie Mercury / Montserrat Caballé collaboration Barcelona (1988) and subsequent Queen, Paul Rodgers releases.

The Greensleeves Records relationship is particularly notable — 353 unique releases covering virtually the label’s entire reggae and dancehall catalog from 1978–2019. Similarly deep relationships with Mute (Depeche Mode, Erasure, Nitzer Ebb, Soft Cell) and On-U Sound (Creation Rebel, African Head Charge, New Age Steppers, Dub Syndicate, Keith LeBlanc).

Key Genre/Scene Concentrations

  • Queen / EMI / Parlophone orbit: the dominant strand of his career by volume. Essentially the in-house mastering engineer for Queen’s catalog through the late 1980s–2000s.
  • On-U Sound / reggae / dub: long-running relationship with Adrian Sherwood’s label and associated acts (Creation Rebel, African Head Charge, New Age Steppers, Singers & Players, Dub Syndicate, Keith LeBlanc, Horace Andy). Also the go-to engineer for Greensleeves Records and Blood & Fire (both major reggae reissue labels).
  • Mute Records: mastered core catalog for Depeche Mode, Erasure, Nitzer Ebb, Soft Cell, Renegade Soundwave.
  • Electronic/dance: Orbital, Underworld, Juno Reactor, Shpongle, Younger Brother (via Twisted Records/Ninja Tune), Bonobo, Friendly Fires.
  • UK indie/alternative: Carter The Unstoppable Sex Machine, Pulp, Siouxsie & The Banshees, The Creatures, Loop, The Wildhearts, The Wonder Stuff, Fairground Attraction, Curve, Felt.
  • Art/progressive rock: Steve Hackett, Can (reissues), Brian Eno (later catalog reissues).
  • International: Yello (Switzerland, 174 releases — second-largest single-artist client), Alphaville (Germany), Miguel Bosé (Spain/Latin America), a-ha (Norway).
