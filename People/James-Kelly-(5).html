Discogs ID: 1836531 Alias: Wife (4) — Discogs ID: 3009640 Role: Musician, vocalist, producer, engineer Active: 2007–2023 (Discogs span) Groups: Altar Of Plagues, Bliss Signal

Irish musician. Frontman and founder of black-metal band Altar Of Plagues (Dublin). Runs the electronic solo project WIFE — registered as Wife (4) on Discogs. Also a member of Bliss Signal (with Jack Adams (5)).
