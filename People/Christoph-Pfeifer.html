Overview

Christoph Pfeifer is a German vocalist, guitarist, songwriter, recording engineer, and producer whose output spans the late 1980s through the 1990s, with the bulk of his credits concentrated in indie rock and alternative rock contexts. He has been a member of several German acts — Bill Your Neighbour, Great Tuna!, The Story Of Acoustic Kitty, and The Surfin’ Dead — and built his strongest label relationship with Glitterhouse Records across five works between 1987 and 1998. Beyond his band work, Pfeifer accumulated a substantial engineering and production credit list, with recurring collaborators including Christopher Uhe, Sascha Unversucht, Schneider TM, and Wigbert Pieper — the Schneider TM connection alone spanning seven shared releases and bridging his indie-rock background with the German post-rock and electronic scenes.
