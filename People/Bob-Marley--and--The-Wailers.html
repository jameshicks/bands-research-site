Bob Marley & The Wailers

Discogs ID: 21764

Jamaican reggae band led by Bob Marley (Robert Nesta Marley, born 6 February 1945, died 11 May 1981). The Wailers were the primary vehicle through which reggae music reached a global audience in the 1970s. Signed to Island Records from 1972; Island’s UK pressings throughout the late 1970s were mastered/lacquer-cut by John Dent at Trident Studios.

Key Island-era LPs with Dent credits: Exodus (1977), Kaya (1978), Survival (1979), Uprising (1980). Singles: “Jamming / Punky Reggae Party” (1977), “Waiting In Vain” (1977). Legend compilation (1984) also bears Dent’s cutting credit.
