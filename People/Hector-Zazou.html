Hector Zazou

Real name: Pierre-André Job
Born: July 11, 1948, Sidi Bel Abbès, Algeria
Died: September 8, 2008, Paris, France
Discogs ID: 5114
Active: 1976–2008

Overview

French avant-garde composer, producer, and multi-instrumentalist who spent three decades building elaborate cross-cultural collaborations. Born in colonial Algeria, Zazou was associated with the French “lettriste” movement before pivoting to an eclectic world-music fusion practice in the late 1970s. He emerged as the most prolific individual artist on Crammed Discs — 49 releases — and became central to the label’s Made To Measure series, which commissioned soundtracks and experimental works alongside its main catalog. His output spanned Congolese soukous-meets-electronics (the Zazou Bikaye project), orchestral ambient song-cycles (Sahara Blue, Chansons Des Mers Froides), minimalist ambience with Harold Budd (Glyph, MTM 37), and Mediterranean/Irish sacred music. He produced and arranged virtually everything under his own name; Gilles Martin was the recurring mix engineer across most of his major albums.

Zazou’s alias on Discogs is Roquet Belles Oreilles. Name variations include H. Zazou, HZ, and the transliteration 艾克特薩蘇.

Groups and Projects

  • ZNR (Discogs ID: 216859) — French avant-garde group alongside Harvey Néneux, Joseph Racaille, Manfred Kovacic, and Patrick Portella. Active from 1976; two LPs on Isadora/Invisible Records (1976–78) plus a 2018 archive box on ReR Megacorp.
  • Zazou Bikaye (Discogs ID: 26165) — duo with Congolese vocalist Bony Bikaye. Active from 1985; LPs Mr. Manager (1985) and Guilty ! (1988) on Crammed Discs; reunion material Na Kenda (2018) on ensemble (2).
  • Zazou, Bikaye and CY1 (Discogs ID: 33937) — trio expanding the Zazou Bikaye core with electronics. Members: Bony Bikaye, Claude Micheli, Guillaume Loizillon, Hector Zazou. Two primary releases: Noir Et Blanc (CRAM 025, 1983) and M’Pasi Ya M’Pamba (CRAM 034, 1984).
  • Slow Music Project (Discogs ID: 2934817) — later group credit.
