Bob Ludwig

Discogs ID: 271098

American mastering engineer. One of the most prominent mastering engineers in the recording industry; Gateway Mastering, Portland, Maine. Credits span rock, pop, classical, and avant-garde.

Mastered Ambitious Lovers’ Lust (1991, Elektra).
