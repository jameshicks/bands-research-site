Jeff Noon

Born 24 November 1957, Droylsden, Greater Manchester, England. Novelist, playwright, musician, and visual artist. Guitarist with Manicured Noise (Manchester, 1978–c.1981). No Discogs artist ID.

Overview

Jeff Noon was an “early member” of Manicured Noise, playing guitar during their active period centred on the Hulme estate. He is described by Caroline True Records as contributing to the “particular retro-futurist edge” of the band’s sound. He subsequently pursued fine art and drama at Manchester University, then became playwright in residence at the Royal Exchange Theatre Manchester. His debut novel Vurt (1993) won the Arthur C. Clarke Award. He worked at Waterstone’s Manchester before writing Vurt. He moved to Brighton in 2000.

Wikipedia describes him as having been “musically active on the punk scene” before moving to theatre and fiction, but does not name Manicured Noise specifically.

Sources: carolinetruerecords.com; lostbands.blogspot.com; en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Noon (as of 2026-06-01)
