Mike Donovan (2)

US musician, guitarist, vocalist. Discogs artist ID: 625242. Also credited as M.E.D., Michael Donovan, Mike D. Based in San Francisco, California.

Overview

Best known as a member of Sic Alps, a San Francisco noise-pop / lo-fi rock duo active in the 2000s–2010s. Also central to Peacers (Drag City), Brigid Dawson And The Mothers Network, and a long list of Bay Area projects including Nam (4), The Ropers, The Trawgs, and others. Releases solo as Mike Donovan & Band. Has frequent lineup overlap with Shayde Sartin, Mike Shoun, and Bo Moore (2).
