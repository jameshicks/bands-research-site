Josh Klinghoffer

Discogs ID: 354590 (Josh Klinghoffer) · 7375070 (Pluralone alias) Real name: Josh Adam Klinghoffer Born: October 3, 1979, Santa Monica, California Origin: Los Angeles, CA

Multi-instrumentalist (guitar, drums, bass, keyboards, voice, synthesizer) and producer. Best known as the guitarist for Red Hot Chili Peppers (2009–2019), during which he recorded two studio albums and was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2012. Prior to RHCP he built an extensive session and collaborative career alongside John Frusciante, appearing on multiple Frusciante solo records and co-founding the experimental trio Ataxia. He fronts the alternative rock band Dot Hacker (formed 2008) and records solo material as Pluralone (Discogs ID: 7375070). Additional associations include The Bicycle Thief (Bob Forrest’s project, 1997–c.2001), Golden Shoulders, Headless Heroes, touring stints with PJ Harvey, Beck, Gnarls Barkley, Sparks, and Pearl Jam (since 2021), and session contributions to Iggy Pop, Elton John, Eddie Vedder, Tinariwen, and Redd Kross, among others.

Sources: Discogs (primary structure); Wikipedia (Josh Klinghoffer, offline snapshot June 2026) for career narrative.

Career Overview

Klinghoffer dropped out of high school at 15 (1995) to pursue music. He joined The Bicycle Thief at 17, recording You Come and Go Like a Pop Song (1999, Goldenvoice) — his first studio credit. It was during those sessions that he first met John Frusciante. Through the early 2000s he accumulated a dense session résumé: PJ Harvey (2004 BBC Sessions, touring), Beck (2003 tour), Sparks (2006–2008 tour), Gnarls Barkley (2006–2008 tour and The Odd Couple album), and the Frusciante collaboration cluster (multiple albums, 2002–2009). In 2007 he joined RHCP as touring rhythm guitarist on the Stadium Arcadium tour; in late 2009 he was officially named Frusciante’s permanent replacement. He co-wrote all songs on I’m with You (2011) and The Getaway (2016). RHCP let him go in December 2019, reinstating Frusciante. After departing he became a touring member of Pearl Jam (2021–present), briefly toured with Jane’s Addiction (2023, filling in for Dave Navarro), produced Redd Kross’s self-titled 2024 LP, and appeared with Chad Smith on Elton John & Brandi Carlile’s Who Believes in Angels? (2025). His Pluralone solo project launched in 2019 and has released four albums to date, all on ORG Music.

Ataxia (2004; 2007)

Experimental trio formed during six weeks of sessions in early 2004. Disbanded after two shows at the Knitting Factory, Los Angeles. Klinghoffer played drums, synthesizer, and voice on both records; all songs co-written by all three members. Producer: John Frusciante. Engineer and mixer: Ryan Hewitt.

John Frusciante collaborations (2002–2009)

Klinghoffer contributed to most of Frusciante’s output from this period. Co-credited on A Sphere in the Heart of Silence (2004, Record Collection) — produced jointly by both, engineered by Ryan Hewitt. Also credited on Shadows Collide with People, The Will to Death, Inside of Emptiness (all 2004), and mixed The Empyrean (2009).

Red Hot Chili Peppers (2007–2019)

Joined as touring rhythm guitarist March 2007; officially named permanent guitarist in early 2010. Co-wrote all songs on both studio albums recorded during his tenure. Inducted into Rock and Roll Hall of Fame with the band in 2012 (youngest inductee at the time).
