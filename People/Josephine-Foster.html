Josephine Foster

Discogs ID: 383904 Active: 2000–2025 (Discogs span) Origin: Colorado, US Roles: Vocals, guitar, harp, mandolin, ukulele, autoharp, zither, banjo, and many more; songwriter, producer

American folk and psychedelic singer-songwriter from Colorado. Prolific solo career spanning twenty-five years on multiple labels, with sustained work on Locust Music in the early period and Fire Records from 2009 onward. Member of Born Heller (duo with Jason Ajemian), Josephine Foster And The Supposed, The Children’s Hour, and Mendrugo. Also records as Jove (2) (alias). Married to artist credited on Discogs as artist ID 1235535.

Biography / Profile

Early Self-Released Work (2000–2002)

Foster’s earliest Discogs appearances are two self-released records:

  • There Are Eyes Above (2000, Not On Label) — her debut; songwriter credit
  • Little Life (2001, Not On Label) — ukulele, guitar, harp, autoharp, banjo, tin whistle among her instruments; songwriter credit

These set the template: Foster as sole author, performing on a wide array of acoustic instruments, self-releasing.

SOS JFK / Early Collaborations (2003)

Foster appears as a performer on SOS JFK (2003, Rough Trade UK), playing guitar, harp, piano, harmonium, and ukulele, and credited “Words By.” This is shared with Jason Ajemian (credited double bass on the same record). The primary artist of this release is not recoverable from Discogs alone.

Locust Music Period (2004–2006)

Three albums on Chicago’s Locust Music, run by Dawson Prater:

All The Leaves Are Gone (2004) — released under the name Josephine Foster And The Supposed, her backing band formation. Foster produced, wrote all songs, and played classical guitar, vocals, and tambourine alongside Rusty Peterson (drums) and Brian Goodman (electric guitar, bass, vocals). Design by Foster, Dawson Prater, and Plastic Crimewave; mastered by Ernst Karel; recorded by John Dawson.

Born Heller (2004) — duo record with Jason Ajemian, released the same year. Foster plays harp, mandolin, and voice; writes 8 of 11 tracks. See Born Heller for full details.

Hazel Eyes, I Will Lead You (2005) — solo record; Foster performs entirely alone, accompanying herself on guitar, harp, bells, zither (cittarina), castanets, kazoo, sandblocks, tin whistle, tambourine, ukulele, spoons, sitar, flute, and other items. Engineers Clay Ruby and Jeff Mann; mastered again by Ernst Karel; artwork shared with Plastic Crimewave. This album represents the fullest expression of her solo multi-instrumental approach.

A Wolf In Sheep’s Clothing (2006) — arranged, produced, and co-mixed by Foster. A step toward more developed arrangements; still on Locust Music.

Australian Release (2005)

Joey Will Come If You Invite Her (2005, Art School Dropout, Australia) — a home-recorded release.
