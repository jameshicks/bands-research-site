Lyra Pramuk

Vocalist, composer, and producer. Based in Berlin. Discogs artist ID: 7139378.

Lyra Pramuk makes music built entirely or primarily from processed and synthesized vocal samples, fusing classical vocalism, pop, performance, and contemporary club culture into what she describes as “futurist folk music.” She released her debut album Fountain in 2020 on Bedroom Community and has since built a parallel career as a session vocalist for film scores and experimental projects.

Biography

Background and Early Collaborations (pre-2020)

Before her solo debut, Pramuk was part of the ensemble credited on Holly Herndon’s landmark AI-assisted album Proto (4AD, 2019). The ensemble — also including Colin Self (2), Stine Janvin, Albertine Sarges, and others — provided the human vocal training data and performance layer for the record. Herndon and co-producer Mathew Dryhurst built Proto around an AI “baby” named Spawn, and the ensemble vocalists were central to that process. Marta Salogni mixed the record; Heba Kadry and Amy Dragon mastered.

Fountain and Debut on Bedroom Community (2020)

Pramuk’s debut solo album Fountain was released on Bedroom Community in 2020 (cat# HVALREKI17, Iceland). She composed, produced, and performed all vocals. The record was mixed by Ben Pramuk (also credited as backing vocalist) and mastered jointly by Valgeir Sigurðsson and Francesco Fabris. Artwork was by visual artist Donna Huanca; graphic design by Gergő Kovács.

Fountain appeared in four pressings and established Pramuk as a distinctive solo voice in experimental electronic music. The same year she released a DJ mix, Fact Mix 770, for FACT Magazine.

Knot Of Spirit and Delta (2021)

In 2021 Pramuk collaborated with Italian synthesist Caterina Barbieri (2) on Knot Of Spirit, released on Barbieri’s own label Light-years (Italy, two pressings). The release is a co-billed joint work: Barbieri on synthesizers, Pramuk on vocals; artwork by Dasha Kuznetsova.

Delta, her second solo album, followed on Bedroom Community (cat# HVALUR39, 2021). It became one of the label’s better-circulated recent releases, running to eight pressings across Iceland and Europe. Pramuk is credited as Creative Director and Art Direction on certain pressings in addition to her role as primary artist. Credits on the first pressing are minimal in the Discogs record; fuller personnel were documented on later editions.

Film Score Vocal Work (2022)

Pramuk appeared as vocalist on two high-profile film/TV scores in 2022:

  • The Northman (Robert Eggers, dir.) — original score by Robin Carolan and Sebastian Gainsborough (Vessel), released on Sacred Bones Records. Pramuk is one of several credited “Singers” alongside Björk, Dessislava Stefanova, and Adrian Peacock. Mastered by Heba Kadry.
  • 1899 (Netflix series) — score by Ben Frost, released on Invada. Pramuk credited for “Vocal Performances by” alongside Chrysa Tsaltampasi, Radie Peat, and Sofia Sarri.

Hymnal and move to 7k! (2025)

Hymnal (2025) marks Pramuk’s third solo album and her first for Berlin-based label 7k! (co-released with pop.soil). She composed, produced, and performed all vocals. The record expands her palette significantly: Sonar Quartett provided string improvisations (arranged by Francesca Verga), with individual players including Konstantin Manaev (cello), Alexander Tarbert and Samar Hafez (double bass), Ian Anderson (viola), and Artiom Shishkov and Wojciech Garbowski (violins). Dylan Kerr (2) played flute on one track and handled additional string re-amping and analog processing. Jordan Juras contributed computer processing using a UMT algorithm. Vocal reamplification was by Riccardo Sellan; strings were recorded by Erik Breuer; vocals recorded by Emanuele Frison. Mixed and mastered by James Ginzburg. Design by Johannes Schnatmann; photography by Leonardo Scotti.

Hymnal appeared in three pressings (worldwide digital and Germany physical).
