Julie Christmas

Discogs ID: 624599 Active: ~2001–present Origin: New York City

Vocalist and frontwoman of Battle of Mice (with Josh Graham) and Made Out Of Babies. Co-wrote the narrative concept for Red Sparowes’ Every Red Heart Shines Toward The Red Sun (2006) — the album’s Mao’s Great Leap Forward / Four Pests thematic arc is jointly credited to her and Josh Graham. Also released a solo album Mayhem (2010, Translation Loss).
