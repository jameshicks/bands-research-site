Patty Donahue

Discogs ID: 327927
Alias ID: Patty Darling (Discogs ID 4625286) — used on early Waitresses works
Real name: Patricia Jean Donahue
Born: March 29, 1956, Cleveland, Ohio, USA
Died: December 9, 1996, aged 40 (lung cancer)

Patty Donahue was the lead vocalist of The Waitresses, the Akron, Ohio post-punk / new wave group led by songwriter and guitarist Chris Butler. Her deadpan, nasal delivery — unnervingly flat, conversational, and dry — defined the band’s sound across their entire recorded output, from the 1978 debut single through the 1983 album Bruiseology. She is best known for two tracks: “I Know What Boys Like” (1980), a deadpan account of female agency that became a new wave staple, and “Christmas Wrapping” (1982), a narrative pop song that has become a perennial holiday fixture. Donahue holds no solo Discogs entries and no credits outside The Waitresses. She died of lung cancer on December 9, 1996, at age 40. Her early Waitresses credits appear under the alias Patty Darling (Discogs ID 4625286).
