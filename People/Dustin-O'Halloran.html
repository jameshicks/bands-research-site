Overview

American pianist and composer born 8 September 1971 in Phoenix, Arizona and raised in Los Angeles, who later relocated to the Emilia Romagna region of Italy, where he became part of indie duo Dévics. His output shifted decisively toward neo-classical and modern classical territory through the 2010s — the most productive decade of his catalogue — released across Bella Union, Erased Tapes, and Deutsche Grammophon, among others. He co-founded ambient ensemble A Winged Victory for the Sullen alongside Adam Wiltzie, and his closest studio collaborators include Francesco Donadello, Nils Frahm, Hildur Guðnadóttir, and Peter Broderick. His film work earned him an Emmy in 2015 for the Transparent theme song and an Oscar and Golden Globe nomination in 2016 for his score to Lion.
