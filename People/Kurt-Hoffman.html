Overview

Kurt Hoffman is a New York-based multi-instrumentalist, composer, arranger, producer, and visual artist whose principal instruments run across the woodwind and keyboard families — saxophone, clarinet, accordion, and horns. He played keyboards and woodwinds in the They Might Be Giants touring band from 1992 to 1994, and that association left a deep imprint on his catalogue: his closest Discogs collaborator is John Linnell with 123 shared credits, well ahead of Frank London (73) and John Flansburgh (67). Outside the TMBG sphere he has been a member of The Ordinaires, Firewater, Kurt Hoffman’s Band Of Weeds, Les Chauds Lapins, and Off Beach, and his Frank London connection ties him to New York’s downtown experimental and klezmer circles. His credits span from 1985 to the present across labels including Elektra, Restless Records, Matador, and 4AD.
