Glenn Kotche

American drummer, percussionist, and composer. Born 1970, Roselle, Illinois. Discogs artist ID: 523506.

Overview

Glenn Kotche is one of the most prominently placed drummers in American indie and experimental music of the 2000s onward. He joined Wilco as full-time drummer following his participation in the Loose Fur sessions (circa 2000) alongside Jeff Tweedy and Jim O’Rourke, and has remained in the band’s lineup through every record from Yankee Hotel Foxtrot (2002) to Hot Sun Cool Shroud (2024). In parallel, he has maintained an extensive network of collaborative and solo work spanning avant-jazz (Fred Lonberg-Holm Quartet, Sinister Luck Ensemble), bass-and-drums duo music (On Fillmore, with Darin Gray), free improv (Boxhead Ensemble), and composed contemporary music — the last documented on his solo Nonesuch album Mobile (2006), which sets a Steve Reich co-composition alongside his own pieces for percussion. His name variations on Discogs include Glenn Kotchke, Glen Kotche, GK, and “The Mighty Glenn Kotche.”
