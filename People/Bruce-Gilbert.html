Bruce Gilbert

Guitarist and electronic artist, born 1946 in Watford, UK. Core member of Wire from their formation in 1976 through 2004. Discogs artist ID: 11323.

Overview

Bruce Gilbert is one of Wire’s founding members, contributing a distinctive minimalist, angular guitar style and later electronic/noise work both within Wire and in solo projects. He appeared on all of Wire’s classic-era releases and continued through the band’s reformations. He appears as guitarist on Ear Trumpet’s Bring On The Dirt (Data Terminal Systems Records, 1987) alongside Tony Barber and Steven Severin.

Projects

In addition to Wire, Gilbert co-founded Dome (with Graham Lewis), releasing three LPs under that name (1980–1981) plus a fourth under the alias Cupol on 4AD. He co-performed on MZUI (Cherry Red, 1982) with Lewis and Russell Mills. Gilbert was also a co-member of Duet Emmo with Lewis and Daniel Miller. He departed Wire in 2004 and has continued solo work on Editions Mego and other labels.
