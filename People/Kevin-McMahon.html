Kevin McMahon

Discogs ID: 688321 (credited as “Kevin McMahon (2)“)
Full name: Kevin S. McMahon
Location: Upstate New York
Studio: Marcata Recording

Producer and engineer based in upstate New York. Owns and operates Marcata Recording, the studio where most of his work originates. Also a working musician — plays guitar, bass, keyboards, and more on many of the albums he produces, and fronts his own project The Cross Sea. Career spans 1995 to at least 2026. Most closely associated with Titus Andronicus (five studio albums, also performed on all of them) and Real Estate (2) (four records on Domino across 2011–2021). Also in the orbit of Young God Records and Swans.

Career by Era

Mid-1990s — Early 2000s

First Discogs credits appear in 1995 (Goods And Services, Brake Out Records, recorded). Worked on records for Jetset Records (1998) and File 13 Records (2004). Established Marcata Recording in upstate New York during this period.

The Monitor is McMahon’s most cited production — a 64-minute punk-rock concept album about the American Civil War, credited with relaunching Titus Andronicus. He played guitar, percussion, and sang on it while also producing and mixing. The Seer is Swans’ 2012 double LP; McMahon recorded and mixed it, and contributed guest electric guitar on “The Seer Returns.”

2026: The Cross Sea

McMahon as primary artist: The Cross Sea self-released an album in 2026 on which he played everything — vocals, electric guitar, keyboards, piano, bass, cello, Chapman Stick, and drums — in addition to producing, recording, mixing, and mastering.
