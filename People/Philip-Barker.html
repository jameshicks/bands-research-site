Philip Barker

Drummer. Discogs artist ID: 910094. Alias: Phil Phuture. Member of Buzzcocks from August 1992 to April 2005.

Overview

Philip Barker joined Buzzcocks alongside Tony Barber in 1992 and remained through four studio albums. He quit in April 2005 and was replaced by Danny Farrant.
