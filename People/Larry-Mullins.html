Larry Mullins

American musician (drums, percussion, keyboards), record producer, and composer. Born Lawrence Edward Mullins, 13 December 1966, Knoxville, Tennessee. Stage name: Toby Dammit. Discogs artist ID: 286494.

Overview

Larry Mullins is a multi-instrumentalist whose career has run through some of the most significant post-punk and experimental rock outfits of the last three decades. He is best known as a member of Swans and Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds, and as former drummer for Iggy Pop and The Stooges. He is also a core member of Angels of Light across their first three LPs. His range is unusually broad even by post-punk standards — credited across drums, vibraphone, glockenspiel, tabla, timpani, Farfisa organ, mellotron, piano, synthesizer, and vocals across the same albums. He is a co-founder of label l14307 (Discogs label reference), and has worked extensively in film and television scoring, including a performance role in the German cable series Babylon Berlin as big-band drummer Willy Schuricke.

On Discogs his alias “Toby Dammit” is cross-linked with a separate Discogs entry (ID 31841, realname Lawrence Edward Crooke) — this appears to be a Discogs data error; Toby Dammit is Mullins’s established stage name, and the other entry’s credits (Tricatel, The Residents, Stephan Eicher) overlap with Mullins’s real career. The “Larry Contrary” alias (Discogs 3548518) likewise appears as a variant credit name, not a distinct person. Wikipedia confirms Toby Dammit = Larry Mullins. (Source: Wikipedia, “Larry Mullins (musician)“)

Notes on Alias Discrepancy

Discogs lists “Toby Dammit” as a separate artist (ID 31841) with realname Lawrence Edward Crooke. However all three Discogs entries (Larry Mullins 286494, Toby Dammit 31841, Larry Contrary 3548518) cross-reference each other as aliases. Wikipedia is unambiguous: Toby Dammit is the stage name of Lawrence Edward Mullins. The Toby Dammit Discogs entry (31841) has its own credit history covering Tricatel, Stephan Eicher, and The Residents — it is possible this represents a Discogs merge error where two entities were conflated, or simply that Mullins used the Toby Dammit name for those French projects. This warrants verification.
