Chris Corsano

US free-jazz and experimental drummer. Discogs artist ID: 274468. Alias: Kristian Rädiätiön.

Played drums, percussion, and organ across School Of The Flower (2005, Drag City) by Six Organs of Admittance. Prolific collaborator in free improvisation and psych scenes; appeared on split releases with Ben Chasny (the Chris Corsano & Ben Chasny / Paul Metzger split, 2006, Roaratorio).
