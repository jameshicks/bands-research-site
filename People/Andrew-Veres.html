Overview

Andrew Veres is a recording engineer, mastering engineer, and performer active since 2008, working in experimental, ambient, abstract, and krautrock-adjacent territory. He is a member of King Tut (8), Organic Dial, and Outer Space (3), and his 63 total credits are anchored by an unusually deep tie to the Emeralds circle — John Elliott leads his collaborators with 23 shared releases, while Steve Hauschildt, Mark McGuire, and Imaginary Softwoods also recur. His output sits primarily on Spectrum Spools and Editions Mego, the Viennese imprint and its sister label that defined much of this scene through the 2010s; a 2025 credit on Diagonal suggests continued activity.
