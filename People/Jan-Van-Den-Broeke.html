Overview

Jan Van Den Broeke is a Belgian architect, musician, and visual artist who legally changed his name from Jean-Pierre Van Den Broeke. His recorded output begins in 2008 and runs continuously through the mid-2020s, with the great majority of his 26 EE Tapes releases placing him at the centre of that label’s minimal and experimental catalogue; Walhalla Records (4) accounts for a further nine works. He has worked across five projects — Absent Music, April & June, Canto De Mudo, June11, and The Misz — in roles spanning bass, instruments, recording, remastering, and sleeve design. His most sustained collaboration is with Alain Neffe, with whom he shares 23 releases.
