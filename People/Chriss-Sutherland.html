Overview

Chriss Sutherland is an American bassist and founding member of Cerberus Shoal, the experimental post-rock collective from Maine. His work spans from the mid-1990s through the 2010s—most densely in the 2000s—across post rock, psychedelic rock, folk, and experimental styles. Beyond Cerberus Shoal he has been a member of Fire On Fire, Olas, and SNAEX, with recurring collaborators including Caleb Mulkerin, Tom Rogers, and Steve Drown. His releases have appeared primarily on North East Indie and Temporary Residence Limited.
