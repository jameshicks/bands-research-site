Peter Asher

British record producer, A&R executive, and musician. Discogs artist ID: 265644. Born Peter Richard Asher, June 22, 1944, Park Royal, London, England. Name variations: P. Asher, Pete Asher, Peter Ascher.

One half of the 1960s British Invasion duo Peter & Gordon. Became head of A&R at Apple Records (1968–1969) where he signed and produced James Taylor’s debut. Went on to a long production partnership with James Taylor (Warner Bros., Columbia) and Linda Ronstadt (Asylum, Elektra), defining a strand of 1970s LA singer-songwriter rock. Also produced 10,000 Maniacs, Cher, Diana Ross, Neil Diamond, and others through the 1980s–1990s. Continued producing into the 2020s.

Career Overview

Peter & Gordon (1963–1968)

Asher and Gordon Waller formed Peter & Gordon in London. Their debut single “A World Without Love” — written by Paul McCartney (then dating Asher’s sister Jane Asher) — reached #1 in the UK and US in 1964 on Columbia (UK) / Capitol Records (US). The duo had a string of further hits through 1967: “Nobody I Know,” “I Don’t Want to See You Again,” “I Go to Pieces,” “True Love Ways,” “To Know You Is to Love You,” “Lady Godiva,” “Knight in Rusty Armour,” and “Woman” (the last three written by McCartney under the pseudonym Bernard Webb to prove songs could chart without his name attached).

Their albums appeared on Columbia in the UK and Capitol in the US. Asher handled arrangements on several releases alongside their vocal performances. The duo dissolved in 1968.

Apple Records A&R (1968–1969)

After Peter & Gordon disbanded, Asher became head of A&R at Apple Records, the Beatles’ newly formed label. His key signing was James Taylor, an American singer-songwriter he encountered in London. Asher produced Taylor’s self-titled debut (Apple Records, 1968), playing percussion and contributing backing vocals. He also photographed the Maybe Tomorrow (Badfinger) release and supervised the Apple Records release Space (1969). Asher left Apple in 1969 when the label’s operations became turbulent following the departure of the Beatles’ manager.

Linda Ronstadt Productions (1973–1993)

Asher co-produced Don’t Cry Now (Asylum, 1973) and from Heart Like A Wheel (Capitol, 1974) onward produced Ronstadt’s albums through the late 1980s. This run constitutes one of the most commercially successful production partnerships in 1970s pop-rock. Key personnel on these sessions included Andrew Gold (guitar, piano, drums), Kenny Edwards (bass), and engineer Val Garay.

Management Work

Asher co-ran Asher/Krost Management (Discogs group ID 2799222) and later Sanctuary Artist Management (Discogs group ID 2123697), managing Morrissey’s Attack Records releases in 2004–2005 (including You Are the Quarry, Live at Earls Court) and Nirvana’s box set (With the Lights Out, 2004, executive producer for Sanctuary). He also managed The Innocence Mission (A&M, 1989) and other artists.
