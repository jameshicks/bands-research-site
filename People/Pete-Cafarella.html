Pete Cafarella

Recording engineer, Washington, D.C. area. Discogs artist ID: 451312.

Credited as recording engineer on Q And Not U’s Power (2004, Dischord Records), alongside Rafael Cohen. That album marked Q And Not U’s shift away from Inner Ear Studios and their usual production team.
