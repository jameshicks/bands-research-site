Grandmaster Flash

Born Joseph Saddler; DJ and founding member of Grandmaster Flash & The Furious Five. South Bronx, New York. Active from late 1970s. Discogs artist ID: 21715 (stage name); also listed as Joseph Saddler (ID: 265393).

Overview

Pioneer of hip-hop turntablism. Developed the “clock theory” for cueing breakbeats and the “Quick Mix Theory” for extending breaks by cutting between two copies of a record. Assembled the Furious Five from the South Bronx DJ/MC circuit. First performed at Bronx block parties and park jams before any commercial release.

Key labels: Sugar Hill Records, Elektra, Strut. Years active per Discogs: 1980–2022.
