Trilok Gurtu

Indian percussionist / tabla player. Discogs artist ID: 12821.

Active internationally from the late 1970s; associated with jazz, world music, and fusion contexts. Worked with John McLaughlin (John McLaughlin Trio), Oregon, and others. On Day Eight Music, credited as primary artist on five releases in 1989, most notably Adfa (DEMLP 020), a duo album with Jonas Hellborg. Also associated with Aktuala (listed as member on Discogs), Christy Doran’s May 84, Embryo, Jan Fryderyk Creative Sound, and various other ensembles.

Connections

  • Aktuala — listed as member; the Milan world-improvisation ensemble (Walter Maioli, Lino Capra Vaccina) where Gurtu’s tabla playing is documented in early 1970s Italy
  • Lino Capra Vaccina — Aktuala bandmate
