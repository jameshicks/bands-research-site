Aug 05, 20263 min read

Neil Tennant

Discogs ID: 62963
Born: 10 July 1954, North Shields, Tyne and Wear
Real name: Neil Francis Tennant

Singer, lyricist. Co-founder of Pet Shop Boys with Chris Lowe. Before music, worked as a journalist at Smash Hits magazine.

Electronic Credits

Guest on three distinct Electronic releases (Discogs ID: 4868):

ReleaseYearLabelRoleNotes
”Getting Away With It” (single, release 28565)1989FactoryVocalsDebut Electronic single; co-wrote with Sumner/Marr
Electronic (album, release 77968) — track 51991Virgin/Warner Bros.Written-By”The Patience Of A Saint”; Tennant co-wrote but does not appear to perform on album release credits
”Disappointed” (single, release 37115)1992Warner Bros.Vocals, ProducerThird Electronic–Tennant collaboration; his most substantial production role

The 2006 Get The Message: The Best Of Electronic (release 789678) lists Tennant as Written-By on CD-2 (“Getting Away With It”) and CD-5 (“Disappointed”) — these are derivative compilation credits from the originals, not additional appearances.

Beyond the two tracks previously noted (“Getting Away With It” and “The Patience Of A Saint”), Tennant also sang on and co-produced the 1992 single “Disappointed.”
