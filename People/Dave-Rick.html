Overview

David Matthew Rick is an American guitarist and bassist whose career spans the mid-1980s underground rock scene through to the present. He is a core member of Phantom Tollbooth — where he plays guitar and sings — and appeared on key releases by Bongwater, King Missile, B.A.L.L., and When People Were Shorter And Lived Near The Water. His earliest Discogs credit is playing bass on the 1985 Yo La Tengo debut single “The River of Water.” Rick’s name-variation “F.D.M.R.” (a backronym of his full name initials) appears on some credits; he also appears as “Dave ‘Magic Finger’ Rick” on the Galaxie 500 “Rain” single. He is a recurring collaborator of Kramer (2) (Shimmy Disc), who produced the Bongwater, B.A.L.L., and King Missile records on which Rick played. Later projects include The Martinets, The Shapir-O’Rama (with Jad Fair), Wonderama, Atlantic Drone, Overcat, Stress Test, Wide Right, McLoud, Dew-Claw, and Bob Carol Ted (with artist/drummer Steve DiBenedetto). (Wikipedia)
