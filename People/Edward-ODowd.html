Edward “Edley” ODowd

Discogs ID: 866141

Drummer, percussionist, and producer. Core PTV3 member from at least 2008 onwards; credited on Mr. Alien Brain Vs. The Skinwalkers (2008), Snakes (2014), and likely the full run of Psychic TV PTV3-era records. Primary producer on Snakes. Also handles art direction and design on some releases.
