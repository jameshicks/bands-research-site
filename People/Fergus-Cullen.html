Overview

Fergus Cullen is a multi-instrumentalist working across saxophone, clarinet, guitar, and keyboards, with activity documented from 2003 through 2024. He has been a member of Amygdala (4), European Sensoria Band, and Weapons Of Mass Destruction, operating across experimental, free improvisation, and post-punk idioms. His most consistent label affiliation is with Deserted Village, the Dublin-based experimental imprint, though later releases spread across Fort Evil Fruit, Diet Of Worms, and Tor Press. Close collaborators include Jamie Davis (7), Damien Lennon, Gavin Prior, and ¡NO!, and his catalogue includes two releases shared with Damo Suzuki, pointing to connections beyond the Irish experimental network.
