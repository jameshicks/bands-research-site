Overview

Danny Federici (born January 23, 1950 in Flemington, New Jersey; died April 17, 2008 in Manhattan) was an American musician and founding member of Bruce Springsteen’s E Street Band, where he played organ, glockenspiel, and accordion — instruments that defined the band’s sound across four decades of Columbia releases. Known as “Phantom Dan,” he was one of the most constant presences in the Springsteen orbit alongside Clarence Clemons, Garry Tallent, Roy Bittan, and Max Weinberg. Before the E Street Band he was a member of Steel Mill, an earlier Springsteen-led New Jersey outfit. He was posthumously inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2014 as a member of The E Street Band.
