Hubert Poell

Dutch record producer, A&R, and compiler. Central to the Brigadoon operation across its full catalog history. Discogs ID: 1226346

Overview

Hubert Poell is the dominant production figure behind the Dutch vocal and cabaret label Brigadoon (and its Brigadoon Vocal sublabel), operating under parent company Flow Records B.V. based in Hilversum. He holds the highest release count of any credited producer across the combined Brigadoon catalog — 10 releases on the main label and 6 on Brigadoon Vocal, totalling 16 entries on Discogs. His roles range from hands-on co-producer on early Astrid Seriese albums through to executive producer and A&R consultant on later Wende Snijders records and compilation curator for the Portret retrospective series.

His Discogs footprint is entirely concentrated on Brigadoon/Brigadoon Vocal; one additional credit appears on Tathata Music (2011) in a non-production capacity (English subtitle translation for a Wende Snijders live DVD).

Career at Brigadoon (1993–2011)

Early Period — Astrid Seriese productions (1993–1994)

Poell’s first Discogs credits are as co-producer alongside Astrid Seriese herself on her first two solo albums for the label:

  • Eclipse (BIS 005, 1993) — Poell and Seriese jointly produced. Arranged by Berend van den Berg and Joep Lumeij; recorded and mixed by Fir Suidema with assistant engineering from Holger Schwedt and Hans van Gelderen. Features Gerbrand Westveen (saxophone), Evert Hekkema (trumpet), Joep Lumeij (bass), Bram Wijland (drums). Artwork by Egbert Luys.
  • Secret World (BIS 010, 1994) — Again joint production with Seriese. Musical director: Joep Lumeij. Mixed by Fir Suidema and Holger Schwedt. Design by Egbert Luys.

These two albums establish the core Brigadoon production team: Poell as executive/A&R presence, Seriese as creative lead, Lumeij as musical director, Suidema as engineer.

Portret Series (1997)

Poell produced and compiled Portret (Brigadoon Vocal, 1997), a career-retrospective compilation for Dutch cabaret legend Jasperina De Jong. The release was mastered and edited by Fir Suidema. This is part of the broader Portret (3) retrospective series on the Brigadoon Vocal sublabel, which documents major Dutch theatre/cabaret personalities.

Later Period — Executive Producer and A&R (2006–2009)

In the label’s later years Poell’s credit shifts from hands-on producer to executive/oversight roles:

  • Als De Herfst Je Overvalt — Brigitte Kaandorp (Brigadoon, 2006); Executive Producer. Produced by Hans Strikkers and Sebastiaan Koolhoven, with the Metropole Orchestra conducted by Dick Bakker.
  • Nº9 — Wende Snijders (Brigadoon, 2009); Executive Producer and A&R Consultant, alongside Arjo Klingens, Jan Van Eerd, Reyn Ouwehand, and Sonja Tabak. Produced by Jan Van Eerd and Reyn Ouwehand.

Post-Brigadoon (2011)

On Wende Snijders’s live DVD Carré (Tathata Music, 2011), Poell receives a single credit for translating lyrics into English for DVD subtitles. This is a support/editorial role rather than a production credit, and is the only entry outside the Brigadoon family in his Discogs record.

Label Context

Brigadoon’s production ecology was tiered: Poell sat at the A&R/executive level, with working producers like Sebastiaan Koolhoven, Hans Strikkers, Jan Van Eerd, Reyn Ouwehand, and Dick Kuijs handling hands-on sessions. On the early Seriese albums Poell appears to have been more directly involved (co-producer credit), while from 2006 onward his credit reflects an oversight/consultant function. The label was small and Hilversum-based; the same names — Egbert Luys for design, Hans Strikkers for engineering — recur across nearly all releases, suggesting a tight core team.
