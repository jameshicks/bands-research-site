Andy Heermans

Recording engineer, mixer, producer, and musician based in the East Village of New York City. Real name: Andrew Jay Heermans. Discogs artist ID: 226798. Owner of Polywog Recording Company. Also credited as “Heermans,” “A. Heermans,” “Andrew Heermans,” “Andrew J. Heermans,” “Andy Heermann,” and several other variants.

Overview

Heermans emerged from the New York downtown scene, playing bass in the post-punk band Cells (2) on their 1979 debut single “Uh-Oh” (Rude Records). He transitioned into studio work in the early 1980s, picking up assistant engineering credits at the ZE Records orbit before establishing himself as a primary engineer and mixer throughout the decade. His credit list spans downtown NYC post-punk/no wave adjacents, ZE Records artists, Latin jazz crossover, Japanese fusion, Argentine rock, R&B/hip-hop (Capitol Records), and into indie/garage in the 2000s via Polywog Recording Company, his own East Village studio. He remains active at least into the 2010s.
