Gary Clail

Real name: Gary Edward Clail. Born 1959. Discogs ID (solo): 40520. Discogs ID (Gary Clail & On-U Sound System): 30764. Discogs ID (Gary Clail’s Tackhead Sound System): 86246.

Overview

Gary Clail is a Bristol-born English MC, vocalist, DJ and record producer who became one of the most visible live performers in the On-U Sound stable from 1985. He operates as a sound-system toaster rather than a conventional singer, delivering spoken-word and rap-style vocals over dub and industrial-funk productions. His early work is inseparable from Tackhead — the New York funk/dub crew of Skip McDonald, Doug Wimbish, and Keith LeBlanc — and from producer Adrian Sherwood, who produced virtually the whole of Clail’s recorded output through the mid-1990s.

Clail’s association with Tackhead was formalised under the alias Gary Clail’s Tackhead Sound System (Discogs ID: 86246), a live-performance and recording entity built around him toasting over radically reworked Tackhead dub versions. From 1989 he released under the name Gary Clail & On-U Sound System, a credit that foregrounded his role as the live/vocal face of the broader On-U collective. His commercial breakthrough came in 1991 when “Human Nature” — produced by Sherwood with a Paul Oakenfold / Steve Osborne mix — reached the UK Top 10 on Perfecto, then distributed through RCA. The debut album The Emotional Hooligan (1991, Perfecto/RCA) drew on the full On-U roster as session players. Follow-up albums Dreamstealers (1993) and These Things Are Worth Fighting For (1993) continued on Perfecto/BMG. After the major-label period Clail moved to French imprint Yelen Musiques for Another Hard Man (1995) and Keep The Faith (1995), and later returned to sporadic self-released activity.
