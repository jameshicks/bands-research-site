Overview

Jean Beauvoir is a Chicago-born singer, multi-instrumentalist (guitar, bass, keyboards, drums), producer, and entertainment executive of Haitian descent — sibling of Marie-Louise Beauvoir and Pierre Beauvoir. His catalogue is concentrated in the 1980s across punk, hard rock, synth-pop, and AOR, built on stints with the Plasmatics and Little Steven And The Disciples Of Soul before a solo run on Sire, Virgin, and Mercury. His closest working relationship by credit count is with the Ramones and their immediate circle — Ed Stasium, Daniel Rey, Tom Erdelyi, Dee Dee Ramone — alongside heavyweight producers Phil Spector, Craig Leon, and Tony Bongiovi. He continued recording into the 2000s and 2010s via Frontiers Records and his own Voodoo Island Records imprint, with Crown Of Thorns as his most recent active project.
