Nick Worthington

Music industry executive; founder of 679 (679 Recordings) and 37 Adventures. Discogs artist ID: 60243.

Overview

Worthington co-founded Pure Groove Records, a record shop at 679 Holloway Road, Archway, North London, with school friends at age 19 — running it for roughly 20 years. He went on to become Head of A&R at XL Recordings, where he delivered two Platinum albums from Basement Jaxx and the Mercury Prize-winning album from Badly Drawn Boy. He also co-founded Locked On Records. oddnumbersmusic.co.uk; Wikipedia: 679 Artists

In 2001 he founded 679 (679 Recordings) in partnership with Warner Music Group, serving as MD and A&R Director. The label name derives from the Holloway Road address of Pure Groove. He remained MD after Warner’s full acquisition of 679 in 2008. On the label’s closure in 2013, he founded 37 Adventures as an independent successor. Wikipedia: 679 Artists

He co-founded Pure Groove Publishing — managing rights for artists including Example, The Streets, Metronomy, Frank Turner, and Plan B — before it was acquired by Universal Music Publishing. Subsequently he became a partner at Black Butter, and later co-founded Odd Numbers with Kieran Jay. stack.rostr.cc; oddnumbersmusic.co.uk

Richard Russell (XL Recordings founder) described Worthington as someone “involved in his low-key way, in innumerable success stories” — “an unusual type of behind-the-scenes player.” oddnumbersmusic.co.uk
