Overview

Karolina Sumali Komstedt (born 7 December 1976) is a Swedish vocalist, musician, and songwriter, primarily known as the lead voice of Club 8, the indie pop duo she has formed with Johan Angergård since the mid-1990s. The two share credits on 88 releases, almost all routed through Labrador Records, where the duo has been a mainstay from 1998 to 2018. She is also a member of Poprace. Her catalogue ranges across indie pop and soft rock into Afrobeat and synth-pop, tracing Club 8’s broad stylistic arc over two decades; the Discogs credits under Universal Records (2007–2010) reflect a Philippines regional distribution deal for Labrador, not a global major-label signing (see Notes (web) below).

Notes (web)

Universal Records 2007–2010: Philippines distribution, not a major-label deal

The five Discogs credits under Universal Records (7) for the 2007–2010 period do not represent a global signing to a major label. They are Philippines-market pressings licensed through Universal Records Philippines (a local independent label, unrelated to Universal Music Group globally), which distributed Labrador Records releases in the Philippines during this period.

Evidence:

  • The 2007 Philippines pressing of The Boy Who Couldn’t Stop Dreaming carries catalog number LAB108 — Labrador’s own catalog number — licensed to Universal Records for manufacturing, marketing, and distribution in the Philippines. (Discogs release 1601036 snippet)
  • The 2010 Philippines pressing of The People’s Record likewise uses catalog number LAB130, manufactured/distributed by Universal Records Philippines. (Discogs master 268975 snippet)
  • When Club 8 toured the Philippines in May 2010, Universal Records hosted a press conference as the band’s “record distributor in the Philippines.” (lilystars-records.com, “The Club 8 Experience”, retrieved 2026-11-16)
  • The Wikipedia article for Universal Records (Philippines), as of a 2011 snapshot, lists Labrador Records among its distributed foreign labels — confirming the relationship was a distribution agreement at the label level (Labrador → Universal Records Philippines), not a band-level signing. (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Records_(Philippines), as of 2026-11-16)

Conclusion: There was no “move to a major” and no departure from one. Club 8 remained on Labrador throughout. The Universal Records credits reflect Labrador’s regional distribution deal with Universal Records Philippines, covering at minimum The Boy Who Couldn’t Stop Dreaming (2007) and The People’s Record (2010). No interview or press source was found explaining when or why that distribution arrangement began or ended. The overview sentence “a brief period on Universal Records (2007–2010) suggests a wider commercial push” (written from Discogs data alone) is a misreading and should be treated as corrected by this note.

Sources checked: Wikipedia (Club 8, Universal Records Philippines, Labrador Records, The Boy Who Couldn’t Stop Dreaming), lilystars-records.com, search snippets from Discogs releases 1601036 and master 268975, QRO Mag / Line of Best Fit / Billboard interviews with Johan Angergård (none addressed the Philippines deal). No interview source found that discusses the distribution arrangement directly.
