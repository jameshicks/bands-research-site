Adrian Olsen

Musician, engineer, mixer, and producer based in Richmond, VA. Discogs ID: 2677790. Active 2006–present. Co-owner of Montrose Recording with his father Bruce Olsen. Core Spacebomb Studios engineer and long-term collaborator of Matthew E. White and Trey Pollard. Also a performing musician and member of Richmond band Avers.

Overview

Adrian Olsen operates out of Montrose Recording, a Richmond, Virginia studio he co-owns with his father Bruce Olsen. His name appears on Discogs under the name variation “Adrian Montross,” pointing to the Montrose Recording identity. Beginning around 2009 with mix credits on local releases, Olsen became a central figure in the Spacebomb Records ecosystem through the 2010s, engineering and mixing recordings at Montrose and at Spacebomb Studios for Matthew E. White’s label roster and beyond. His credits span engineering, mixing, production, tape transfer, modular synthesis, and drums — reflecting a versatile studio practice that extends from purely technical work to co-production and performance.

By the mid-2010s Olsen had become the default engineer for Spacebomb sessions, recording string and brass arrangements conducted by Trey Pollard for records by Natalie Prass, Bedouine, Foxygen, Nadia Reid, and Matthew E. White himself. He also engineers and mixes for acts well outside the Spacebomb orbit — The Dream Syndicate, Waxahatchee, Buck Meek, and others — reflecting a national reputation developed alongside his Richmond base.

As a performer, Olsen plays drums and percussion on select sessions, and contributes modular synthesizer to recordings by Matthew E. White (K Bay, 2021) and Buck Meek (Haunted Mountain, 2023). He also appears as a producer in his own right on records for Michael Nau, The Dream Syndicate, and others.

Montrose Recording

Olsen co-owns and operates Montrose Recording (Discogs label ID: 676206) alongside his father Bruce Olsen. The studio is frequently credited simply as “Montrose” on releases. String and brass sessions for many Spacebomb-adjacent records — including Foxygen’s Hang and Jeffrey Foucault’s Out Of All This Blue — are specifically credited as “Recorded at Montrose,” distinguishing this Richmond location from Spacebomb Studios proper.

Avers

Olsen is a founding member of Richmond indie band Avers (Discogs ID: 4093422), alongside Alexandra Spalding and Tyler Williams (3). Avers released Empty Light (2014, Not On Label) and Omega/Whatever (2016, EggHunt Records). Alexandra Spalding also appears as a studio assistant engineer on Matthew E. White’s K Bay (2021), suggesting the Avers circle overlaps with the Spacebomb studio community.

Role Breakdown

  • Engineering / Recording: Primary role across the bulk of his credits. Appears as engineer, recorded by, or both on the large majority of releases.
  • Mixing: Consistently mixes his own recordings and frequently mixes for others working in separate studios (The Dream Syndicate, Nadia Reid, Buck Meek, Faye Webster releases, etc.).
  • Production: Credited as producer on a smaller but growing subset — notably the three 2019 Sub Pop releases (Museum District, The Good News, Tropic Of Cancer), The Universe Inside for The Dream Syndicate (co-produced with John Agnello), and Michael Nau’s Accompany (2023, solo production credit).
  • Mastering: Appears occasionally — The Birth Of Stars (2014), Ampersand (2018), Marlowe’s Revenge (2016).
  • Performance: Drums on The Deliverance Of Marlowe Billings (2012); percussion on Whistling in the Dark (2006); drum machine and synthesizer on Accompany (2023); modular synths on K Bay (2021) and Haunted Mountain (2023); modular processing on The Life You Save (2025).
  • Tape Transfer: Rare Feeling (2017, Keeled Scales).
  • Remix: Days Of A Quiet Sun (2020, Feel It Records).
