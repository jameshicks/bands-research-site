Overview

Cory Younts is an American multi-instrumentalist best known as a member of Old Crow Medicine Show, where he plays mandolin, keyboards, and harmonica. Active in the country and Americana scene since at least 2000, he also plays with The Buzzards, The D.O.T.B., and The Swindlers. His session and collaborative work has placed him at the center of Nashville’s roots-music community, with deep ties to producer Vance Powell and extensive credits through Third Man Records alongside Jack White and a wide network of collaborators including Justin Townes Earle.
