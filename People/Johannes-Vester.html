The synthesist, multi-instrumentalist and lead singer with Sand, Hannes Vester continued with several projects after Sand’s demise. For instance, the curiously named Johannes Vester And His Vester Bester Tester Electric Folk Orchestra, who recorded an unreleased album, was his attempt to continue the spirit of Sand, blending folk and other musics most uniquely. As a soloist, he explored a very different direction, as a creative synthesist, notably inspired by Klaus Schulze. Recordings of these two facets are featured on the 2cd collection.

Overview

Johannes “Hannes” Vester is a German synthesist, multi-instrumentalist, and the lead vocalist of Sand, the Krautrock and space rock group active from the early 1970s. After Sand dissolved he took two divergent paths: the Johannes Vester And His Vester Bester Tester Electric Folk Orchestra, a folk-blending project whose album was recorded but never officially released, and a solo synthesizer direction heavily influenced by Klaus Schulze. His catalogue runs from 1974 into the 2020s, with a concentrated burst of releases on Rotorelief between 2010 and 2020, and his network of collaborators extends unexpectedly into the post-industrial underground through shared credits with Current 93 and Attrition.
