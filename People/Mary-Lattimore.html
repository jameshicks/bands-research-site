Mary Lattimore

Discogs ID: 1111064
Origin: USA

Harpist. Featured on “Bony Fish” from Lali Puna’s Two Windows (2017, Morr Music morr 156), contributing harp and co-writing the track with Valerie Trebeljahr and Christian Heiß.

Co-billed with Julianna Barwick on Tragic Magic (2026, Infiné). Also shares 8 releases with Barwick per Discogs collaboration graph, including Canyon Lights and Perpetual Adoration (likely Lattimore-primary releases where Barwick guests).
