Overview

Aki Tsuyuko is a Japanese vocalist, composer, organist, and harpist based in Japan. She is the primary vocal collaborator of Nobukazu Takemura and a core artist on his Childisc label, where she has released three solo albums. Her voice appears — credited both as live vocal and as processed sample — across Takemura’s most critically noted electronic works including Scope (1999), Meteor (1999), Songbook (2001), and 10th (2002). On the latter she is credited as lyricist rather than vocalist.

Beyond Childisc, she has released material on the US label Thrill Jockey (Hokane, 2006) and self-released through her own imprint. She records under the alias Ahi (written あひぃ in Japanese) for collaborative and group projects. Her instruments include organ (Kawai Dreamatone, Casiotone, fluteorgan, reed organ, pump organ), harp, and voice; she handles composition, production, and artwork on her solo work.

She is a member of three small-group projects based in Japan: Tonjay (duo with Ippei Matsui), Tondekebana (duo with Eddie Corman, also listed as “とんでけ花”), and Wasurerogusa (trio with Eddie Corman and Jules Marcon, also listed as “わすれろ草”).

Group Projects

Tonjay (トンジャイ)

Duo with Ippei Matsui (also written 一平). One CDr release: きら きら しずむ [Kira Killersism] (2008, Not On Label, limited edition). Credited alongside “Eddie Marcon” (Eddie Corman). Tsuyuko plays organ, Kawai Dreamatone, Casiotone, and sings. Styles: Acoustic, Psychedelic Rock.

Tondekebana (とんでけ花)

Duo with Eddie Corman (へでぃ “Heddie”). One CDr release under the Ahi alias: 宇日葉 [Uhiha] (2010, Not On Label, self-released). Four tracks. Styles: Acoustic Rock.

Wasurerogusa (わすれろ草)

Trio with Eddie Corman and Jules Marcon. One CDr release: Baby Works (2010, Not On Label, self-released). Six tracks; Tsuyuko plays organ and sings. Styles: Acoustic, Folk Rock.

Ahi Alias

Discogs registers Ahi (ID: 2040364, written あひぃ) as an alias with name variations “Aki Tsuyuko” and “Aki Tsuyoko.” The alias appears in the Tondekebana release Uhiha (2010), where she is credited as “あひぃ” (songwriter, performer). No solo releases are credited to the Ahi alias.
