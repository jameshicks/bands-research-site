Michael Bass

Discogs ID: 255920

Bandleader, percussionist, multi-instrumentalist. Led Michael Bass And His Moderately Sized Orchestra, which released Painting By Numbers (Random Radar Records, RRR 006, 1979). Closely tied to the D.C. avant-rock milieu and the The Muffins circle. Active on Discogs across releases 1978–2022.
