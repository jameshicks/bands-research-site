Eric Débris

Real name: Eric Daugu. French musician, Paris. Discogs artist ID: 302756. Core member and primary creative force of Métal Urbain; also a member of Metal Boys.

Overview

Eric Débris (born Eric Daugu) played synthesizer and drum machine in Métal Urbain from the band’s formation in June 1976 through its dissolution ca. 1980, and again at the 2006 reunion. Following Clode Panik’s departure (December 1978), he also took on lead vocals. He is credited as producer and mixer on most Métal Urbain recordings, and handled remastering for the 2003 retrospectives alongside Sean Cotter.

Outside Métal Urbain and Metal Boys, he ran the project Doctor Mix And The Remix and its offshoot Dr Mix And The House Of Noise (Discogs group IDs 369484 and 556259). Also listed as a member of Sex Sex Sex (3) (ID 3616249).
