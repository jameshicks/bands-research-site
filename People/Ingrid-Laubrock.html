Ingrid Laubrock

Discogs ID: 561153

German-British saxophonist (tenor, soprano) and composer based in New York. Long-term collaborator with Cory Smythe and Stephan Crump in the trio that produced Planktonic Finales (2017, Intakt) and Channels (2019, Intakt). Also leads the Ingrid Laubrock Small Ensemble. Member of Cloud Sounds Trio.
