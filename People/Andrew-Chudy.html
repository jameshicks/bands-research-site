Overview

Andrew Chudy (born 9 June 1957, New York) is an American percussionist and composer who became a long-standing member of the Berlin industrial group Einstürzende Neubauten, accumulating 113 shared credits with Blixa Bargeld, Alexander Hacke, and others in that circle. His catalogue stretches from 1980 through the 2010s, working principally on Mute Records (1991–2004) and Potomak (1996–2011) across experimental, industrial, and avant-garde styles. He holds roles as percussionist, composer, and performer, and is also credited as a member of Faust (54).

Notes (web)

Route from New York to Berlin, and into Einstürzende Neubauten

Family background and move to Berlin. Andrew Chudy was born on 9 June 1957 in New York to a German veterinarian mother and a Slovakian toolmaker father. The family relocated to West Berlin in 1963 when he was six years old. He grew up in Berlin and has remained based there. taz.de interview with N.U. Unruh (paywalled; details retrieved via search extract); confirmed by official band biography: “N.U. Unruh, was born in New York, USA and grew up in Berlin, Germany” — neubauten.org/en/nu-unruh/ (retrieved 2026-11-05).

School friendship with Blixa Bargeld. Chudy and Blixa Bargeld were classmates at the Paul-Natorp-Gymnasium in the Friedenau district of Berlin. taz.de interview. This is the basis for the Wikipedia phrase “school friend”; the specific school name is from the taz source.

Amsterdam interlude (post-Abitur, before 1980). After completing his Abitur, Chudy trained as a piano restorer at Piano Salon Christofori in Amsterdam. Bargeld visited him there in 1979 with his girlfriend; during those visits Bargeld proposed forming a musical group. taz.de interview; also referenced (without venue name) in web search extracts citing the same source.

Return to Berlin and founding of Einstürzende Neubauten. Chudy returned to Berlin at Bargeld’s invitation. In 1980, they co-founded Einstürzende Neubauten alongside Gudrun Gut and Beate Bartel. Per the taz source, Chudy noted that “we had already spent time together in a rehearsal space making music” before the band’s formal creation, and their early SO36 performances were envisioned during the Amsterdam conversations. taz.de interview.
