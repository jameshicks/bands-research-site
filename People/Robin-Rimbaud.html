Robin Rimbaud

Also known as: Scanner. UK electronic/sound artist. Discogs artist ID: unclear — see Discogs under “Scanner” or “Robin Rimbaud.” Active 1991–present.

Overview

Robin Rimbaud works under the Scanner alias. His early work (early 1990s) intercepted mobile phone conversations and integrated them into electronic music — making questions of surveillance, signal, and privacy central to his practice. He co-administered Ash International with Mike Harding (2) from its founding in early 1993 until early 1995, when he departed to focus on the Scanner operation. His early Scanner releases were among the first on Ash International.

Connections
