Michael Rother

Born 2 September 1950, Hamburg. German guitarist, keyboardist, composer. Discogs artist ID: 37955. Key figure of Krautrock through NEU!, Harmonia, and a solo career.

Overview

Rother briefly played in early Kraftwerk (1971) before co-founding NEU! with Klaus Dinger. All three NEU! studio albums were produced by Conny Plank. After NEU!, he formed Harmonia (1973–1976) with Dieter Moebius and Hans-Joachim Roedelius of Cluster, and also collaborated with Brian Eno (Harmonia 76 sessions). His solo career began with Flammende Herzen (Sky, 1977), also produced by Conny Plank.
