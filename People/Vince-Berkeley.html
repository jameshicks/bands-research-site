Vince Berkeley

Also known as: ‘Teenage’ Vince Berkeley, Berkeley, Vince Discogs ID: 1006501 Role: Guitarist, Vocalist, Songwriter Active: 1984–1987 (confirmed via Discogs credits) Associated Acts: Pink Peg Slax

Biography

Vince Berkeley was the lead guitarist, vocalist, and primary songwriter of Pink Peg Slax, a rockabilly-meets-indie band from Leeds that recorded and released between 1984 and 1987. Berkeley is the dominant creative voice across the band’s catalogue: on the debut single Self-Pitying Stan (1984, Half-Cut Records), he is the sole credited writer of the A-side “Self-Pitying Stan” and the B2 track “Pay Dough,” and co-wrote “Mud On My Clothes” (B1) with Ant Hanlon. All three writing credits use the name “Berkeley.”

On the band’s full-length debut Belting Out A Tuna (1986, Half-Cut Records), Berkeley is credited by his full theatrical alias “‘Teenage’ Vince Berkeley” for his roles as vocalist and lead guitarist. The LP was self-produced by Pink Peg Slax, placing Berkeley in the production chain as well, though no individual producer credit is assigned to him personally — the band is collectively credited. The title Belting Out A Tuna and the matrix etchings (“ABNER’S WOBBLING HULK,” “MARTIN IS TOO POSH”) suggest the surrealist humour Berkeley and the band brought to their releases.

The 1986 12” EP The Sound Of The Meanwood Valley (HC63) and the 1987 LP 12 Songs Never Recorded By Frank Sinatra (Ediesta Records, CALCLP 027) both list Pink Peg Slax as the primary artist; Discogs carries no individual performer credits for either, so Berkeley’s specific role cannot be confirmed from the database for those releases, though as lead vocalist and guitarist across the band’s entire documented history it is reasonable to assume continuity.

Discogs records no activity for Berkeley under any of his credited name variants after the band’s 1987 LP. No solo releases, no collaborations outside the band, and no post-Pink Peg Slax group credits appear in the database.

Pink Peg Slax’s profile notes multiple BBC Radio One sessions recorded with John Peel during 1984–1988, and daytime Radio One airplay during that window. Berkeley’s songwriting drove this output, but the Peel session recordings are not catalogued in Discogs.

A 2023 Cherry Red box set Where Were You- (Independent Music From Leeds 1978-1989) included the Pink Peg Slax track “Boy From Leeds” — originally from Belting Out A Tuna — placing Berkeley’s songwriting in the documented history of the Leeds independent music scene.
