Overview

Adam Pierce is an American musician and the founder of Bubble Core Records, the label he has used to self-release and release associated work throughout his career. He is best known as the sole creative entity behind Mice Parade — a project that is literally an anagram of his name. Pierce plays nearly all instruments himself on Mice Parade recordings (drums, guitar, marimba, vibraphone, mbira, vibes, various synthesis) and records and engineers much of the material, while handling production and mixing. Discogs lists Mice Parade (ID: 12539) as an alias of Adam Pierce (ID: 14704).

Before Mice Parade, Pierce played drums in the Boston-area lo-fi/shoegaze band Swirlies and was a co-founder of The Dylan Group alongside Dylan Cristy. He has also been a member of HiM (the experimental group led by Doug Scharin), Igloo (2), and The Philistines Jr.

Outside his own recordings, Pierce contributed harp to múm’s Summer Make Good (FatCat Records, 2004), one of six shared Discogs releases with the Icelandic group.

Early career and Swirlies (~1992–1997)

Pierce’s earliest Discogs credit is a 1992 release on Bubble Core Records (ID: 1809), indicating he founded the label very early in his career — likely as a self-release vehicle from the start. His playing on a 1995 Swirlies release, The Sinking Of The S.S. Danehower, documents his membership in that band.

Swirlies (Discogs ID: 136975) were a Boston-area band with an unusually large rotating membership including Damon Tutunjian, Andy Bernick, Christina Files, Kevin Shea, Rob Laakso, Ron Regé Jr., and others. Pierce is one of 17 credited Discogs members.

Pierce also co-founded The Dylan Group (Discogs ID: 12536, alias Dylan & Adam 1) with Dylan Cristy, a duo that released on Bubble Core Records. The Dylan Group’s Discogs page credits only Pierce and Cristy as members.

Mice Parade (1998–present)

Mice Parade launched in 1998 with its debut single My Funny Friend Scott (FatCat Records, UK) and the first album Ramda (P-Vine Records, Japan). From the start, Pierce engineered and wrote all material, with Peter Katis assisting on selected tracks on Ramda and the 1998 US debut The True Meaning Of Boodleybaye (Bubble Core Records). Alan Douches has mastered the majority of Mice Parade releases.

The Discogs member list for Mice Parade also credits Caroline Lufkin, Doug Scharin, and Joshua Larue as members, indicating that while Mice Parade is fundamentally Pierce’s solo project, a live/collaborative band form existed.
