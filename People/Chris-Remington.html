Chris Remington

Bass player from Canvey Island, UK. Discogs artist ID: 545824. Member of Buzzcocks from April 2008 to present; also plays in Steve Diggle & The Revolution Of Sound and previously with The Weekenders (2) and Sugarfree (3).

Overview

Chris Remington is a UK bassist who replaced Tony Barber as the Buzzcocks’ bass player in April 2008. His arrival coincided with the band’s continuing post-reunion activity, and he has remained in the lineup ever since — through the recording of The Way (2014), the death of Pete Shelley in December 2018, and the Steve Diggle-led Buzzcocks albums Sonics In The Soul (2022) and Attitude Adjustment (2026). He is from Canvey Island, Essex.

Prior to joining Buzzcocks, Remington was active in the 1990s–2000s UK indie/pop scene. He played bass and sang backing vocals with The Weekenders (2), a four-piece on Blow Up Records (UK, mid-1990s), and he went on to play bass with Steve Diggle on the solo album Some Reality (2000), co-producing alongside Diggle and drummer Gary Rostock. He also appears on Diggle’s Serious Contender (2005, EMI). He was a member of Sugarfree (3), a UK indie act on Little Giant Records, and is a member of Steve Diggle & The Revolution Of Sound — the group Diggle formed for his solo recordings — which released Air Conditioning (2010) and Inner Space Times (2016).

Remington’s name variation “Remington” (without first name) appears on the Some Reality production credits.
