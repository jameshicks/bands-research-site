Heather Leigh Murray

Discogs ID: 612557
US multi-instrumentalist and vocalist. Real name: Heather Leigh. Member of Charalambides from 2000; formerly of Ash Castles On The Ghost Coast.

Overview

Heather Leigh Murray joined Charalambides around 2000, adding pedal steel guitar, psaltery, and voice to the group’s drone-folk sound. She appears on Ana / Kata (2002, Beta-lactam Ring Records), the Untitled split with Scorces (2002, Wholly Other), and Joy Shapes (2004, Kranky).

Other projects include Scorces (with Christina Carter), Dream/Aktion Unit, Taurpis Tula, and Jailbreak. She also collaborated with Peter Brötzmann.
