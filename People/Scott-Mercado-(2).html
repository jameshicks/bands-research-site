Overview

Scott Mercado (not the Candlebox drummer of the same name) is a Scottish-born, San Diego-based multi-instrumentalist, songwriter, and producer who plays keyboards, drums, and guitar. His catalogue runs from 1993 through the mid-2010s across indie rock, post-rock, IDM, and folk styles, with the bulk of activity in the 2000s. He has been a member of Grimoon, Manuok, Mr. Tube And The Flying Objects, Shlümpf, and Via Satellite, and his closest recurring collaborators include Pall Jenkins, Jimmy LaValle (The Album Leaf), and multiple members of Sigur Rós — with Jón Þór Birgisson, Gyða Valtýsdóttir, Kjartan Sveinsson, Orri Páll Dýrason, and Birgir Jón Birgisson each sharing six releases with him.
