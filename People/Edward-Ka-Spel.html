Edward Ka-Spel

Discogs ID: 76764

UK vocalist, songwriter, core member and primary creative force of The Legendary Pink Dots. Also releases prolifically under his own name — 9 solo Soleilmoon entries (1995–2017) including The Blue Room (SOL 87 CD, 1998). Has been central to the LPD’s 54-release catalog on Soleilmoon Recordings.

Key Collaborations

  • Amanda Palmer — co-created I Can Spin A Rainbow (2017, 8ft. Records, cat 8FT035CD) and The Hands EP (2017, 8FT036EP); both credited jointly; mastered by Peter Van Vliet and Raymond Steeg; artwork by Judith Clute. 20 shared Discogs releases.

  • Is That You Santa Claus? (Oscar’s X-mas Carols Vol. 3) — Noel Noel 3, 1986
