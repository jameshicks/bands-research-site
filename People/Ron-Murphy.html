Ron Murphy

Discogs ID: 399415
Origin: Detroit, Michigan, USA

Mastering engineer and lacquer cutter. Based in Detroit; ran National Sound Corporation (NSC), a key facility for the Detroit techno scene. Cut lacquers for many foundational Detroit releases. 5 shared Discogs releases with Ectomorph, including Subsonic Vibrations (IT No. 1, 1995) and Stark EP (IT No. 2, 1995). The NSC logo appears in the runout etchings of many early IT releases.
