Overview

Pete Kember (born 19 November 1965) is an English musician and producer who records and performs as Sonic Boom. He co-founded Spacemen 3 in 1982, contributing vocals, guitar, and keyboards until the band dissolved in 1991; his deepest creative partnership has been with fellow founder Jason Pierce, with whom he shares over a hundred credits. Since then he has run two parallel solo projects — Spectrum and Experimental Audio Research — while building a substantial production portfolio for acts including Stereolab and Yo La Tengo. Now based in Sintra, Portugal, his catalogue spans psychedelic rock, space rock, ambient, and experimental music across labels including Space Age Recordings and Fire Records.
