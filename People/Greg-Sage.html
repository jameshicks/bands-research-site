Greg Sage

Guitarist, vocalist, producer, and songwriter. Real name: Gregory Kent Duff Sage. Born September 21, 1952, Los Angeles, California. Discogs artist ID: 257166.

Founding member and sole consistent presence in Wipers. Produced, recorded, and wrote essentially all Wipers output across two decades. Also an active producer for other Portland artists in the early 1980s, and released two solo albums under his own name.

Overview

Sage moved to Portland, Oregon and formed Wipers as a recording vehicle in late 1978. His approach was deliberately self-contained — he recorded and produced everything himself, often in non-commercial studio settings, and ran his own labels (Park Avenue Records, Brain Eater, Trap Records, and later Zeno Records) to retain control over the catalog. This model was unusual in punk at the time and presaged the Pacific Northwest DIY infrastructure that produced much of the 1980s–90s indie scene.

Particularly notable is the production of Beat Happening’s self-titled debut (1985) for Calvin Johnson’s K label — connecting Wipers directly to the Olympia, Washington indie scene.

Sage also appeared on the very early Beauregarde album (1971, F-Empire) credited as lead guitar, before Wipers formed.
