Pseudo Carol

Third member of The Rentals (2). Boston, active 1977–1980. Discogs ID: 657739.

Name variations on Discogs: “Pseudo Carol Angel” (as credited on the “I Got A Crush On You” single, 1979), “Pseudo Carol Meinke”. The surname Meinke may be her legal name; “Angel” possibly a middle name; “Pseudo Carol” is the stage name used across both variations.

Overview

Took video classes with Jeff and Jane Hudson; made little steel guitars and printed etchings of them as art objects — already working at the intersection of sound and visual culture that defined the Boston loft-building art milieu the three shared. Played guitar, drums, and vocals in The Rentals. Wrote the A-side “I Got A Crush On You” on the UK single (1979, Rental Records).

Discogs entry is sparse — only one release credited (The Rentals “I Got A Crush On You”). No solo releases or post-Rentals credits found in Discogs.

Notes (web)

Post-Rentals activity: The Dark Entries Records interview with Jeff and Jane Hudson states “Carol went on to pursue a solo career” — the only web claim of post-Rentals activity. No solo releases, recordings, or performances by any variant of “Pseudo Carol” or “Carol Meinke” have been located on Discogs, streaming platforms, or in press coverage. The contemporary gospel artist “Carol Angel” on Spotify is unrelated (gospel/worship music, albums 2013–2024). (Dark Entries interview)

Identity: No web source provides her legal surname. The Discogs name variation “Pseudo Carol Meinke” remains the only hint at a legal last name. “Pseudo Carol Angel” appears to reflect her stage name with possible middle name. No “Carol Meinke” or “Carol Angel” with Boston punk/art connections appears in any searched source (AllMusic, Last.fm, RYM, press archives).

Additional Rentals members: The Last.fm/Discogs aggregated data lists a Billy Bacon (organ) as a fourth Rentals member — not yet in the Discogs trawler findings. This may be a separate non-recording member or live player; not yet confirmed via primary source. Worth a Discogs lookup (no MCP call done here).

Label discrepancy: The Dark Entries interview describes the Rentals’ UK single as being on “Beggars Banquet Records.” Discogs credits the label as Rental Records. This is likely an error in the interview — Beggars Banquet was a major UK indie that released The Clash; Rental Records is a small private press imprint consistent with the single’s DIY nature. No independent web source supports the Beggars Banquet claim. (source)

MFA context: The Music Museum of New England describes Pseudo Carol as “one of their students” at SMFA — but Jeff and Jane only co-founded the video studies program there after leaving New York (post-1985). The Rentals were active 1977–1980. This framing likely reflects a general connection to the school, not that Carol was literally their student at the time. (source)
