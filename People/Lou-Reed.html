Lou Reed

Born Lewis Allan Reed, 2 March 1942, Brooklyn, NY; died 27 October 2013, Southampton, NY. Vocalist, guitarist, lyricist. Discogs artist ID: 11879.

Co-founder of The Velvet Underground with John Cale; one of rock’s most influential songwriters, known for street-level realism and avant-garde leanings.

Overview

Reed co-founded The Velvet Underground in New York City c.1964, contributing most of the band’s lyrics and lead guitar. Before VU he worked at Pickwick Records as a staff songwriter and played in early groups including The Primitives (3) and The Jades (2). He departed VU in 1970 before Loaded was released, reportedly unhappy with edits made without his input.

Solo career began in 1972. Key albums: Transformer (1972, produced by David Bowie and Mick Ronson), Berlin (1973), Metal Machine Music (1975), New York (1989), Magic and Loss (1992). Married Laurie Anderson in 2008. Later formed Metal Machine Trio.
