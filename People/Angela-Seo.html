Angela Seo

Multi-instrumentalist and vocalist. Long-term member of Xiu Xiu (2009–present); co-producer from 2017 onward. Also a member of XXL (4) and Deal$. Discogs ID: 2597132.

Biography

Background and early association with Xiu Xiu

Angela Seo’s first documented appearance in Xiu Xiu’s credits is on Tu Mi Piaci (2006, Acuarela) — a Spanish-label single credited to Xiu Xiu — where she contributed photography, credited under her Korean name “Seo Hyunhye.” This was a peripheral, non-performing role; the band at that point still centred on Jamie Stewart and Caralee McElroy.

The stub claim that she contributed additional lyrics to The Air Force (2006) is noted in the Xiu Xiu vault article and in prior research notes, but there is no confirming release_artist row for her on any Air Force pressing in the Discogs database. The photography credit on Tu Mi Piaci (also 2006) is the earliest confirmed DB entry.

Becoming a full member (2009–2011)

Caralee McElroy departed Xiu Xiu around 2009–2010. Angela Seo filled the space — by Always (2012, Polyvinyl), the first album of the Polyvinyl era, she was fully credited in the liner notes as a performer (“Music By”), alongside a large ensemble that included Ches Smith, Devin Hoff, Greg Saunier, Carla Bozulich, John Dieterich, Sam Mickens, and Zac Pennington. Mixed by John Congleton.

Instrumental and lyrical contributor (2012–2016)

On Angel Guts: Red Classroom (2014, Polyvinyl), Seo was credited with analog synth and microtonal synthesizer, and as one of three people credited for “Lyrics Edited By” (alongside Congleton and Vincent Van Gerven Oei). Thor Harris (Swans/Shearwater) contributed drums and percussion; the album was produced and mixed by John Congleton.

On Merzxiu (2015, self-released) — the Xiu Xiu collaboration with noise artist Merzbow (Masami Akita) — Seo played organ and contributed field recordings alongside Stewart on synth/harmonium/percussion.

Plays The Music Of Twin Peaks (2016, Bella Union) saw her playing piano, synth, and cymbal across the full Angelo Badalamenti cover set. The core quartet for that album was Stewart, Seo, Jherek Bischoff (bass/double bass), and Shayna Dunkelman (vibraphone/percussion/drums/synth).

Seo was also credited with “Sounds” on the Tempesta Atsepmet release in 2016 (a Xiu Xiu context, label: no info in DB).

Co-producer and core voice (2017–present)

Forget (2017, Polyvinyl) marks the first album on which Seo received a producer credit alongside Greg Saunier and John Congleton. Her instrumental role expanded: synth, organ, percussion. She has retained the producer credit on every subsequent Xiu Xiu studio album.

Girl With Basket Of Fruit (2019, Polyvinyl) — produced by Seo and Greg Saunier — is the fullest statement of her expanded role to that point. She played piano, vocals, electronic drums, organ, and harmonica. Devin Hoff played double bass; John Congleton mixed; Lawrence English mastered. Guests included Eugene Robinson (Oxbow) and Tara Jane O’Neil.

Oh No (2021, Polyvinyl) — a double LP with a different guest vocalist on nearly every track — listed Seo as producer alongside Greg Saunier and Lawrence English. No performing credits for Seo appear on this pressing in the DB (recorded by Stewart).

Ignore Grief (2023, Polyvinyl) trimmed the lineup to three: Stewart, Seo, and David Kendrick, each credited with “Vocals, Instruments.”

13” Frank Beltrame Italian Stiletto With Bison Horn Grips (2024, Polyvinyl) — her instrument palette on this album spans synth, organ, sheet-metal noises, vocals, percussion, piano, and autoharp. She produced alongside Stewart (recording) and Congleton (mixing).

Xiu Mutha Fuckin’ Xiu: Vol. 1 (2026, Polyvinyl) credits her with synth, percussion, vocals, and backing vocals. Other musicians on this release include David Kendrick (drums), Mary Halvorson (guitar), Tim Berne (alto saxophone), Tony Malaby (tenor saxophone), and Fabrizio Modonese Palumbo (viola/vocals).

Solo work

Seo has released two solo works under her birth name Hyunhye Seo:

  • Strands (2021, Room40) — credited as Hyunhye Seo; produced by Lawrence English; recorded by Jamie Stewart. Primary-artist credit.
  • Eel (2023, Room40) — again as Hyunhye Seo; produced by Seo and Lawrence English. Primary-artist credit.
  • Continuation (2025, label unclear) — as Hyunhye Seo; mixed by Giuseppe Ielasi. Primary-artist credit.

XMFX (2020–2021)

During 2020–2021, Xiu Xiu released a series of cover singles under the “XMFX” name. Seo appears on multiple entries with various roles — piano/vocals/producer on “Plain Gold Ring / In Heaven”; producer/vocals on “HALLOWEEN”; organ on “Less Than Human”; organ on “In Every Dream Home A Heartache”; organ/vocals on “Mama, You’ve Been On My Mind”; synth on “Reject All American.”

XXL (4) (2005–2018)

XXL (4) — full name Xiu Xiu Larsen — is a collaboration between Xiu Xiu (US) and Larsen (Italian). The Italian contingent consists of Fabrizio Modonese Palumbo, Marco Schiavo, Paolo Dellapiana, and Roberto Maria Clemente.

Seo’s involvement in XXL evolved over the project’s arc:

  • ¡Ciaütistico! (2005, Important Records) — Caralee McElroy and Jamie Stewart; no Angela Seo credit. Pre-dates her full Xiu Xiu membership.
  • ¿Spicchiology? (2007, Important Records) — Caralee McElroy, Stewart, and the Larsen contingent; no Angela Seo credit.
  • Düde (2012, Tin Angel Records) — first XXL album with Seo credited; she played synthesizer, guitar, and vocals. Caralee McElroy is not on this release.
  • XXL Selezioni (2005–2012) (2017, Xiu Xiu self-released) — compilation; Seo credited with synth, vocals, percussion.
  • Puff O’Gigio (2018, Bad Paintings) — Seo credited “Music By” alongside Stewart, Palumbo, Schiavo, Dellapiana, and Clemente.

Deal$ (2012)

Deal$ is a four-piece band: Angela Seo (keyboards, vocals, sleeve), Emily O’Sullivan (bass guitar, vocals), Hannah Spector (drums, vocals), and Princess Ojiaku (guitar, vocals). Their only Discogs entry is the self-released cassette Deals (2012), recorded by Matt De Bellis. Seo is credited under “Angela.”

Other guest credits

  • All Of My Bodies (2017, Heavy Lark) — piano.
  • The Svalbard Suite (2019, Hypershape Records) — field recording; primary artist is Blind Cave Salamander (Paul Beauchamp); also featuring Xiu Xiu, Julia Kent, Fabrizio Modonese Palumbo, and a Norwegian choir.
  • Moira (2021, Summer Interlude Records) — “Sounds”; a Black Leather Jesus & Xiu Xiu collaboration.
  • Two Real Nuts (2022) — piano.
  • The Inevitable Fork LP (2024, Melted Bodies self-released) — vocals. Primary artist: Melted Bodies.
  • Original Sin (2024) — Music By.
  • Killing Distance (2021) — synth.

Open Questions

  • The stub and Xiu Xiu article both cite Angela Seo contributing additional lyrics to The Air Force (2006) as “Seo Hyunhye,” but no Discogs release_artist row for her on any Air Force pressing was found. The only 2006 credit in the DB is the photography credit on the Spanish single Tu Mi Piaci. The Air Force credit may exist in a non-digitized LP pressing, or it may be an error that propagated through research notes.
  • Exact join date as a “full member” is not established by Discogs data alone; the DB shows her transitioning from peripheral to core between Always (2012) and the period 2009–2011. She appears in no Discogs entry between 2006 (Tu Mi Piaci) and 2012 (Always, Deals, Düde), so the stub’s “c.2009” join date cannot be confirmed from DB records.
  • Deal$ is documented only by a single 2012 self-released cassette. No further output is in the DB.
  • Solo releases Strands (2021) and Eel (2023) and Continuation (2025) are on Room40 (Lawrence English’s label); the label connection is significant but the Room40 label ID is not in the vault yet.
  • All Of My Bodies (2017, Heavy Lark), Two Real Nuts (2022), Killing Distance (2021), Original Sin (2024), and Zap Ya Dead (2018) — primary artists for these releases are not listed in Angela Seo’s credit rows in the DB; needs investigation.
  • The Inevitable Fork LP / The Inevitable Fork — two separate Discogs entries (2024); primary artist appears to be Melted Bodies.
