Tim Barnes

American percussionist and audio engineer based in Chicago. Discogs artist ID: 130719. Credited as mix engineer and mastering engineer on 14 Locust Music releases, making him one of the label’s most recurring technical collaborators. Also appears as a primary artist on one Locust release.

Overview

Barnes is active in Chicago’s experimental and free improv communities. On Locust Music he handled mix and mastering duties across a wide range of the label’s catalog. Also a performing percussionist with deep connections in the NYC and Chicago experimental scenes.

Text Of Light

Member of Text Of Light (Discogs ID 288448), the NYC film-improvisation ensemble. Barnes plays percussion on Reel 1 (2005, tracks 1–2) and Un Pranzo Favoloso / A Fabulous Lunch (2006, FinalMuzik Italy). The percussion slot in Text Of Light was shared with William Hooker, who appears on track 3 of Reel 1.
