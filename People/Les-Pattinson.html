Overview

Les Pattinson, born Leslie Thomas Pattinson on 18 April 1958 in Ormskirk, Lancashire, is an English bassist and songwriter best known as a founding member and co-writer of Echo & The Bunnymen. His recording career stretches back to 1979, accumulating over 1,000 credits almost entirely within the Bunnymen orbit — released predominantly on Korova and WEA across the 1980s, then on London Records and later Warner imprints for reunions and reissues. Beyond the Bunnymen he has figured as a member of Poltergeist, The Wild Swans, and The Children Of The Revolution. His most sustained collaborations are with fellow Bunnymen Will Sergeant and Ian McCulloch, with producers Hugh Jones, Laurie Latham, and Gil Norton recurring across the catalogue.
