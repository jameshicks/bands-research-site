Overview

Colin McLean is a Scottish bassist, engineer, and multi-instrumentalist associated with the Edinburgh/Amsterdam DIY-punk and free improv scenes. Best known as the bassist and multi-instrumentalist of Dog Faced Hermans (1986–1995), the Edinburgh post-punk group that relocated to Amsterdam, where he played bass, autoharp, slide guitar, and steel drums across the band’s full run from Unbend (1987) through Bump And Swing (1995).

After the Hermans dissolved, McLean’s activity shifted toward engineering and mastering, working closely with Amsterdam-based musicians in The Ex orbit and the Unsounds and Terp Records networks. Key credits in this second phase include recording and mastering the first two Lean Left discs (Volume 1 and Volume 2, Smalltown Superjazzz, 2010), remastering The Ex’s 30th anniversary box set 30 (2009), mixing and mastering the audio for The Ex’s documentary film Building A Broken Mousetrap (Ex Records / Touch And Go, 2007), and mastering records for Kaffe Matthews, Mats Gustafsson / Zu, Getachew Mekuria & The Ex, and others.

He also surfaces as a performer on Unsounds-released Everything But The Beginning (2009), a duo record with Andy Moor (2) where McLean is credited on computer and music, and appears as a guest bassist on the Getachew Mekuria & The Ex releases Moa Anbessa (2006) and Y’Anbessaw Tezeta (2012). An earlier outlier is a 1981 songwriter credit on the TV21 single Something’s Wrong (Deram), co-writing the B-side with Ally Palmer and Neil Baldwin — pre-dating his work with the Hermans.

Name Variations and Alias Disambiguation

Discogs lists McLean under four name forms across releases:

  • Colin McLean (primary)
  • McLean (as a songwriter abbreviation on the TV21 single, 1981)
  • Colin McClean (alternate spelling on Hef, 2001 — this is a separate Discogs artist entry, ID: 506153, but the credit context confirms same person)
  • Colin Mclean (lower-case variant on Belgian Tango, 2008)

There are no confirmed aliases pointing to separate projects or pseudonyms. The earlier brief’s mention of “Mr. Mclean” is listed as a Discogs name variation on his profile but has not been found attached to any released credit in the database.

Connections

  • Andy Moor (2) — Dog Faced Hermans bandmate; later collaborated on engineering credits, Everything But The Beginning duo (Unsounds, 2009), and co-mastered several releases
  • Marion Coutts — Dog Faced Hermans bandmate (vocalist, trumpet)
  • Wilf Plum — Dog Faced Hermans bandmate (drums, saxophone)

  • Lean Left — recorded and mastered Volumes 1 and 2 (2010); mix advisor on Live At Area Sismica (2014); mixed/mastered Live At Café Oto CD (Unsounds, 2012)
  • Unsounds — Amsterdam label closely associated with Andy Moor; McLean’s duo Everything But The Beginning released here; also mastered Break The Record for Chaton / Moor
  • Terp Records — Amsterdam label run by Terrie Ex; McLean mastered Selam (2004) and appeared as guest bassist on both Getachew Mekuria & The Ex releases
  • Konkurrent — parent label of Konkurrel; McLean engineered In The Fishtank (The Ex + Tortoise, 1999) for Konkurrent
  • Konkurrel — released Dog Faced Hermans records from 1990 onward
  • Alternative Tentacles — released the final two Dog Faced Hermans LPs
  • Demon Radge Records — the Hermans’ primary UK label
  • Kaffe Matthews — McLean mastered cd dd (Annette Works, 2001)
  • Getachew Mekuria — appeared as guest bassist on both The Ex collaboration albums (Moa Anbessa 2006, Y’Anbessaw Tezeta 2012)
  • Cactus Truck — Amsterdam free-jazz trio (Jasper Stadhouders, John Dikeman, Onno Govaert); McLean mixed and mastered Brand New For China! (Public Eyesore, 2012)
  • TV21 — Edinburgh new wave band; co-wrote B-side of Something’s Wrong single (Deram, 1981), produced by Ian Broudie
