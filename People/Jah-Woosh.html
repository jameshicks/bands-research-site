Discogs ID: 134030
Real name (Discogs alias): Neville Beckford (ID 65916)
Other aliases: Albert Classau, Jah Youth (8)

Jamaican vocalist. Credited on Singers & Players’ War Of Words (1981) and Revenge Of The Underdog (1982). 15 shared credits across On-U Sound releases.

Notes

One Germany release (GeeBeeDee, 1981) and one Ireland release (FORM, 1981) also on record.
