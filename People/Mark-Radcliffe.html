Mark Radcliffe

UK broadcaster, DJ, musician, and record producer. Discogs artist ID: 299257. Also known as Fat Harry White.

Overview

Mark Radcliffe is best known as a BBC Radio presenter (Radio 1, Radio 6 Music), but in the 1980s he was active as a producer and musician in the Manchester / northwest England independent music scene. He produced tracks for Vee VV (1989 cassette single “Prick up Your Ears / Flame On”; also credited on the 2019 Payola retrospective). He is listed as a member of several groups including The Shirehorses, The Family Mahone, Mark And Lard (with Marc Riley), and UNE (6).
