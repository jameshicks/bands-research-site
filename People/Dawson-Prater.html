Dawson Prater

Chicago-based label operator and designer, best known as the founder and sole operator of Locust Music since late 2000. Also credited as producer, reissue producer, designer, and art director across the label’s catalog and related imprints.

  • Discogs ID: 238301
  • Roles: Label Operator, Producer, Reissue Producer, Design, Layout, Art Direction
  • Based: Chicago, IL
  • Years active: 2000–2011 (Discogs-documented)

Career

Pre-Locust / Ampersand era (2000–2001)

Prater’s earliest Discogs credits are clustered around a label called Ampersand (ID: 9647), where he is credited as Art Director, Creative Director, and Reissue Producer on a run of US reissues in 2000–2001. Titles include John Cage’s Cheap Imitation, Empty Words (Parte III), Four Principles On Ireland And Other Pieces, and several others, alongside early credits on Jagjaguwar’s Rollover (2000, Art Direction). This suggests Prater was working in curatorial / reissue production before launching Locust.

Also in 2000–2001, he took on design roles for Ikef, a Chicago imprint he would later fold in as a Locust sublabel — credits include A “Poetic Excursion” Thru The Black Experience (2001), The Ark And The Ankh (2001), Move Ever Onward (2002), and Sound Awareness (2002).

Locust Music (2000–present)

Prater founded Locust Music in late 2000, running it out of Chicago (P.O. Box 220426, Chicago IL 60622). Over its documented run, the label issued approximately 167 releases (Discogs), with catalog numbers beginning at LOCUST 01. His personal production and design credits on Locust titles span from the label’s earliest releases through at least 2010–2011.

His role on individual releases ranges widely:

  • CD Production / Reissue Producer — the operational credit on archived and reissued material (e.g. Henry Flynt’s C Tune, Back Porch Hillbilly Blues volumes, Michael Hurley’s Blueberry Wine)
  • Design / Layout — sleeve and packaging design (see Full Credits below)
  • Liner Notes — wrote notes for Nico’s Desertshore reissue (4 Men With Beards, 2002, “Chicago, May 2002”) and the At The Helm (2004) reissue
  • Executive Producer — Intensifications by Gene Estribou / Jean-Paul Pickens (2004)
  • Sequencing / Editing / Track Selection — notably on The Voyage Of Icarus (Silmaril, 2007) alongside Rob Sevier and Alex Stimmel

The label’s catalog is eclectic: early American experimental music reissues (Tod Dockstader, Angus MacLise, Henry Flynt, Henry Jacobs), Chicago avant-folk (Born Heller, Josephine Foster, Espers), drone/free improv (No-Neck Blues Band, Catherine Christer Hennix), and international finds (Lau Nau, Cyann & Ben, Nalle, Begushkin). Carl Saff handled mastering on 23+ Locust releases; Tim Barnes on 14; Ernst Karel on 12.

Locust’s sublabels under Prater’s umbrella:

  • Ikef (ID: 61154) — earlier imprint, folded into Locust orbit
  • Latitude (ID: 14320)
  • Met Life Location Sound Series (ID: 413489)

The label’s website (locustmusic.com) is no longer active.

Locust Music

Locust Music (Discogs ID: 13047) is a Chicago independent label Prater founded in late 2000. With approximately 167 releases in the Discogs database, it is the primary vehicle for his curatorial and production work.

Genre focus: American experimental reissues, avant-folk, free improv, drone, psych-folk, international experimental. The label spans academic electronic music reissues (Tod Dockstader, Angus MacLise) through to contemporary folk-adjacent acts.

Top artists by release count:

  • Henry Flynt — 16 releases (2002–2009), by far the most-issued artist
  • Rick Bishop — 8 releases (2004–2009)
  • Begushkin — 6 releases (2007–2008)
  • Ethan Rose — 6 releases (2003–2007)
  • Espers — 5 releases (2004–2005)
  • Paul Metzger — 5 releases (2007–2010)
  • Josephine Foster — 4 releases (2005–2006), plus 1 as Josephine Foster And The Supposed
  • Lau Nau — 4 releases (2005–2008)
  • No-Neck Blues Band — 4 releases (2007–2009)

Catalog numbering: Sequential from LOCUST 01 (Christof Migone’s Crackers, 2001) through at least L123 (Paul Metzger’s The Uses Of Infinity, 2010).

Sublabels: Ikef (ID: 61154), Latitude (ID: 14320), Met Life Location Sound Series (ID: 413489).

Key recurring personnel: Carl Saff (mastering, 23 releases), Tim Barnes (mix/mastering, 14 releases), Ernst Karel (mastering, 12 releases), Noah Wall (design/layout, 5 releases), Plastic Crimewave (artwork, 4 releases).
