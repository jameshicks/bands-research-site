Bob Durkee

American guitarist, vocalist, recording engineer, producer, and label founder. Discogs artist ID: 319443. Aliases: Bob Fartblossom, Marshall H. Stack. Name variations include Robert Curtis Durkee, Robert C. Durkee Esq., Bob D., B. Durkee, Durkee, Bob (M.H. Stack) Durkee.

Overview

Bob Durkee is one of the most consistently credited engineers in the Shrimper catalog, working across the label’s 1991–1998 peak in roles ranging from recording and mixing to field recording, DAT transfer, tape assemblage, and sequence editing. His relationship with Shrimper — and with Dennis Callaci in particular — goes beyond technical work: on The Mountain Goats’ Sweden (1996) he plays “threnodic guitar” on track 19 and is credited alongside an assemblage role, a signal that Durkee was embedded in the scene as a collaborator, not merely a hired engineer.

Outside Shrimper, Durkee worked with Nothing Painted Blue (engineered and co-mixed two records for Scat Records), The Mountain Goats (sequencing on Beautiful Rat Sunset, Zopilote Machine; recording on Full Force Galesburg, Nine Black Poppies), Simon Joyner (mixing The Cowardly Traveller Pays His Toll), Franklin Bruno (recording and co-mixing A Bedroom Community for Simple Machines), Fu Manchu (re-mix engineering on Return To Earth 91-93 for Elastic), Primordial Undermind (engineer and guest guitarist on You And Me And The Continuum, Camera Obscura, Australia, 1997), and Claw Hammer (Poor Robert, 1989).

Before his engineering era, Durkee was a performer: he was a founding member of Pomona hardcore band Pillsbury Hardcore (guitar/vocals, active mid-1980s), and also played in Shoeface. He operated the tape label and release imprint Fartblossom Enterprizes (Discogs label ID: 107059) from the early 1980s, releasing hardcore and punk compilations (Empty Skulls, FBE-001, 1984) and singles, and serving as executive producer on several early releases. The related studio entity FBE (Discogs label ID: 135693) is the credit used for later recording work from the same space.

By the 2020s Durkee returned to performing with Pillsbury Hardcore reunion-era recordings (Ghosts of Straight Edge Past, 2021; Eighty SiXed: The 1986 Rough Mix Tape, 2024, both Black Claw Records).

Background: Fartblossom Enterprizes and Early Career (1982–1990)

Durkee founded Fartblossom Enterprizes in the early 1980s in the Southern California hardcore scene. The label’s earliest documented release is a Circle Jerks live tape (1982), followed by a Black Flag live tape (Olympic Auditorium L.A. 7-17-82, catno FBE-014). The first major catalog release is the Empty Skulls compilation (FBE-001, 1984) — Durkee took the “Against, All Photos” photography credit on that release, indicating he was already operating as a multi-role operator.

He was an executive producer and songwriter in Pillsbury Hardcore, contributing guitar and vocals to releases through 1986 (In A Straight Edge Limbo E.P., 1985; Baker’s Dozen + Bonus Batter, 1986; End The Warzone, 1986, One Step Ahead Records). Other Fartblossom releases he exec-produced include hardcore singles and EPs for Justice League (2) (Think Or Sink, 1985), Subculture (9) (I Heard A Scream, 1985), and a few co-released titles with Goon Records and No Core Records.

By 1989 Durkee was working as a recording engineer outside his own label: he recorded Claw Hammer’s Poor Robert (Grown Up Wrong!, 1989), an early credit outside the hardcore/SST orbit.

Shrimper Era (1991–1998)

The Shrimper relationship is the densest part of Durkee’s credit record. His first documented Shrimper work is a sequencing credit on the Ghost Of A Rollercoaster compilation (1991). He then appears continuously through 1997–98. Key credits:

The Mountain Goats’ core lineup for the Shrimper-era releases was John Darnielle, Rachel Ware, and supporting performers Amy Piatt, Rosanne Lindley, Sarah Arslanian.

Beyond Shrimper: Key External Credits

Nothing Painted Blue / Franklin Bruno

Durkee recorded and co-mixed two Franklin Bruno / Nothing Painted Blue releases:

  • After The House Warming (Nothing Painted Blue, Anyway Records, 1994) — Recorded By
  • A Bedroom Community (Franklin Bruno, Simple Machines, 1995) — Recorded By, Mixed By (credited as “Bob Durkeg” on one pressing — Discogs name variation)
  • Control Freak (Nothing Painted Blue, Scat Records, 1996) — Performer
  • The Monte Carlo Method (Nothing Painted Blue, Scat Records, 1998) — Engineer, Mixed By

On Control Freak he is listed as a Performer alongside Franklin Bruno, Kyle Brodie, and Peter Hughes (2) — this is not a studio credit but a live or collaborative recording where Durkee participated as a player. The Monte Carlo Method (1998) is the last Nothing Painted Blue record and one of Durkee’s most polished engineering credits.

Simon Joyner

  • The Cowardly Traveller Pays His Toll (Sing, Eunuchs!, 1994) — Mixed By. The primary recording was done by Joyner and Chris Deden; Durkee’s involvement was in post-production mixing.

Fu Manchu

  • Return To Earth 91-93 (Elastic Records, 1998) — Engineer [Re-mix], credited specifically on tracks 1, 2, 4, 6–8. Co-engineered the re-mixes with Scott Hill (2), the band’s guitarist. This is a significant departure from Durkee’s usual indie/lo-fi register — Fu Manchu were a mainstream stoner-rock act on a larger label.

Primordial Undermind

  • You And Me And The Continuum (Camera Obscura (2), Australia, 1997) — Engineer; Guest, Guitar. Durkee played guitar as a guest alongside Eric Arn, Brian Craft, and a roster of experimental psych contributors. This is one of his clearest performing credits outside his own bands.

The Extra Glenns / Extra Lens

  • Martial Arts Weekend (The Extra Glenns, Absolutely Kosher Records, 2001) — Edited By [Tape Editing]. This is a John Darnielle / Franklin Bruno collaboration. Durkee’s tape editing role here suggests his physical tape infrastructure was still being called upon in 2001, even after the cassette era had mostly passed.

Claw Hammer

  • Poor Robert (Grown Up Wrong!, 1989) — Recorded By (co-credited with Rusty Cavender). Claw Hammer were a Pomona rock band; this was their debut release. Durkee’s credit predates the Shrimper era and suggests he was active as an engineer in the local Pomona/LA underground scene throughout the late 1980s.

Goosewind

  • Goosegg (Shrimper/Baby Huey, 1992) — Engineer [Engineering Assistance]. Co-credited with Robert Vodicka. Dennis Callaci handled design and layout.

Open Questions

  • Identity of “FBE Studios” vs “FBE”: Discogs profile says founder/owner of “FBE Studios” and “Fartblossom Enterprizes” — but the Discogs label named “FBE Studios” (ID 350047) shows 0 releases, while “FBE” (ID 135693) has 28. The relationship between these entities and their physical location is undocumented in the DB.
  • “Threnodic guitar” on Sweden: The credit appears only on track 19 of the Mountain Goats’ Sweden LP (1996). What specific sound or technique this refers to, and whether it was Durkee’s own label for his playing style, is not explained anywhere in the Discogs record. Answerable from interviews or liner notes — one source.
  • Pomona/Inland Empire scene geography: Fartblossom Enterprizes, Pillsbury Hardcore, and Refrigerator all orbit the Pomona/Claremont area. Whether Durkee’s studio was physically in that area or elsewhere in LA County is undocumented.
  • Goony Tunes compilation (1985): Durkee is credited as Executive Producer on this 52-pressing Fartblossom/Goon Records release — the most-pressed item in his catalog. What this compilation contained is not detailed in Discogs.
  • “Control Freak” (Nothing Painted Blue, 1996): Durkee is listed as “Performer” with no instrument specified, alongside Franklin Bruno, Kyle Brodie, and Peter Hughes. Whether this is a live recording or studio performance session is unclear from the credit alone.
  • 2013 mastering credit: Single appearance as “Mastered By” on Comfort & Joy: A Holiday Compilation (Analog Ghost Recordings, 2013) — a one-off that may indicate Durkee was still operating FBE as a mastering facility into the 2010s, but this cannot be confirmed from Discogs alone.
