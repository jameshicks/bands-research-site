Karl Hendricks

Guitarist, bassist, vocalist, songwriter. Pittsburgh, PA. Discogs artist ID: 506891. Born July 17, 1970, Port Vue, near Pittsburgh; died January 21, 2017, Bloomfield, Pittsburgh, PA.

Overview

Karl Hendricks was the central figure in Pittsburgh’s indie-rock scene for roughly two decades. He fronted The Karl Hendricks Trio and The Karl Hendricks Rock Band, releasing records through his own Peas Kor imprint and through Merge Records and Comedy Minus One. He also appeared in The Speaking Canaries alongside Don Caballero drummer Damon Che and drummer Noah Leger, and served as bassist in The Speaking Canaries. His earliest Discogs credit is the 1990 Sludgehammer EP Big Water, which also features Ian Williams (later Don Caballero, Battles) on drums and Brian Welcker on bass. He began writing songs and releasing cassettes while still in his teens (the Jolly Doom cassette on Peas Kor, 1989).

Outside music Hendricks owned a Pittsburgh record store, taught English at the University of Pittsburgh, and wrote fiction. He died January 21, 2017, at age 46 (Wikipedia).
