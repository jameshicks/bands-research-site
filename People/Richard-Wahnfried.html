Richard Wahnfried

Recording alias of Klaus Schulze. Discogs artist ID: 18791.

Overview

Richard Wahnfried is the primary alias under which Klaus Schulze released collaborative electronic/experimental recordings outside his main solo discography. The name is a portmanteau of Richard Wagner and Haus Wahnfried (Wagner’s villa in Bayreuth).

The 1981 album Tonwelle (Innovative Communication) featured Manuel Göttsching on guitar alongside Schulze (keyboards), Michael Shrieve (percussion), Michael Garvens (vocals), and others.
