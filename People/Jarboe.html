Jarboe

Born Jarboe La Salle Devereaux. Vocalist, keyboardist, multi-instrumentalist. Active 1984–present. Discogs artist ID: 65694.

Core member of Swans from c.1985 to the band’s dissolution in 1997. Extensive solo career as The Living Jarboe. Also known under the alias Jyoti. Name variations on record: J., Jarbo, Jarboe Living, Living Jarboe, The Living Jarboe.

Overview

Jarboe joined Swans in the mid-1980s, initially contributing backing vocals (first credited on Greed, 1986), then becoming a full co-lead voice and arranger by White Light from the Mouth of Infinity (1991). Her presence broadened Swans’ sonic palette from purely brutal noise toward melodic, almost devotional territory. She co-produced and co-arranged several of the band’s most important records and is credited as primary songwriter on her own contributions throughout.

After Swans dissolved in 1997 she pursued a prolific solo and collaborative output spanning dark ambient, experimental rock, post-metal, and electronic territory. She has maintained her own imprint (The Living Jarboe) alongside releases on Young God Records, Alternative Tentacles, Translation Loss, The End Records, Consouling Sounds, and others. Her collaborative reach is wide — from Justin K Broadrick (Godflesh / Jesu) to Neurosis, from JG Thirlwell to cellist Julia Kent and violinist Kris Force.

Side Projects

Skin (2) — The World Of Skin

Discogs artist ID: 149804. Duo (later quartet) with Michael Gira, also featuring Clinton Steele and Steve McAllister. Released under several name variants: The World Of Skin, The World Of Skin (Swans). Key release: Ten Songs For Another World (1990, Young God Records). See Skin (2) for full discography and personnel.

Beautiful People Ltd

Discogs artist ID: 281005. Duo of Jarboe and Lary Seven. Released one self-titled album (1992, Sub Rosa, Belgium). Jarboe handled lead vocals, instrumentation, mixing, and most songwriting. Michael Gira mastered; Lary Seven engineered. Bryce Goggin handled engineering on mastering.

Blackmouth

Discogs artist ID: 73115. Trio: Jarboe (vocals, lyrics), John Bergin (composer, mixer, design, photography), and Brett Smith (composer, mixer). Self-titled album 2000 on Crowd Control Activities. Reconvened for Blackmouth: To Forget Time (2019, Stompbox13) — released as Jarboe / Bergin / Smith in trio credit.

AEAEA

Discogs artist ID: 3970645. All-female experimental collective: Jarboe, Annie Hogan, Kris Force, Meredith Yayanos, and Zoë Keating. Releases on Bandcamp.

The Sweet Meat Love And Holy Cult

Discogs artist ID: 3113062. Large ensemble including Jarboe and Kris Force among others (Brian Castillo, Chandler Rentz, Diana Obscura, Derek Bonner, Damon Young, Henry Derek, and more). Southeastern US base; some members in Brazil / Bali. Two Discogs releases: Introducing The Sweet Meat Love And Holy Cult (2007, 7”, Paradigms Recordings, PARADIGMS008) — credited “Jarboe Introducing The Sweet Meat Love And Holy Cult”; and “Mystagogue” (2013, 2×CD, Atavistic, ALP191) — credited “Jarboe Presents The Sweet Meat Love And Holy Cult”; 28-track double album with distributed songwriting across ensemble members; Kris Force: violin, mastered by.

Sacrificial Cake (1995)

Key solo album on Young God Records / Alternative Tentacles. Produced by Jarboe with Michael Gira (additional music, mixes, design) and Berry Kamer (track 4). Jarboe: vocals, keyboards, bass, guitar, tape. Lary Seven: electric guitar, double bass, percussion, engineer. Michael Evans (2): drums on tracks 2, 15, 16. Clinton Steele: mix engineer. This is the central statement of her solo voice.
