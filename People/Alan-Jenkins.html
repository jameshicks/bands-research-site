Alan Jenkins

Discogs ID: 289877
Origin: Leicester, UK
Active: 1979–present
Name variations: A. Jenkins, A.J., A.K.Jenkins, Alan Whatsit, Jenkins-ish

Guitarist, vocalist, and multi-instrumentalist (clarinet, piano, bongos). Founder and principal songwriter of The Deep Freeze Mice, the Leicester new wave / art-rock group he ran from 1979 to 1989. Founded and runs Cordelia Records, which issued the Mice’s later catalog and later became a recording studio serving the Leicester scene. Co-ran the Egg Plant label with Terry Burrows. After The Deep Freeze Mice dissolved, continued prolifically through The Chrysanthemums, Ruth’s Refrigerator, The Thurston Lava Tube, Yeah Yeah Noh, Jody And The Creams, and a 1994 solo/project release credited jointly to Alan Jenkins and The Creams. Also active as engineer and mixer for other Leicester acts well into the 2000s–2010s.

Biography

Origins and The Deep Freeze Mice (1979–1989)

Jenkins formed The Deep Freeze Mice in Leicester in 1979 with Sherree Lawrence (organ, keyboards) and Mick Bunnage (bass). The band’s first four records — My Geraniums Are Bulletproof (1979), Teenage Head In My Refrigerator (1981), The Gates Of Lunch (1982), and Saw A Ranch House Burning Last Night (1983) — came out on Jenkins’ own Mole Embalming Records imprint with drummer Graham Summers. These LPs established the band’s signature: absurdist literary titles, long experimental side pieces, lo-fi home-studio recording, and a wry pop sensibility sitting beneath the noise.

In 1984 Jenkins launched Cordelia Records and the Mice released their most developed work: the double LP I Love You Little BoBo With Your Delicate Golden Lions (1984), produced with Rick Willson, who served as engineer and producer on both that record and its follow-up. Peter Gregory replaced Summers on drums for the Cordelia era. Jenkins played guitar, vocals, and clarinet throughout, and frequently wrote all the songs. He produced and arranged the band’s final LP, The Tender Yellow Ponies Of Insomnia (1989), credited entirely to the band.

In 1985 Jenkins also directed and produced the Cordelia Records compilation Obscure Independent Classics: Volume 1 “Magnificent March Of The Dead Monkeys”, which collected tracks from the Leicester scene.

Yeah Yeah Noh and Parallel Activity (1984–1989)

Jenkins was a member of Yeah Yeah Noh, the Leicester indie band that recorded for In Tape and Strange Fruit in the mid-1980s. His fellow Deep Freeze Mice drummer Graham Summers was also in Yeah Yeah Noh. Other Yeah Yeah Noh members included Derek Hammond, Andy Nicholls (saxophone), John Grayland, Sue Dorey, Tom Slater, and Adrian Crossan.

Post-Deep Freeze Mice: The Chrysanthemums, Ruth’s Refrigerator, The Creams (1987–1995)

The Chrysanthemums began alongside (and continued after) the Mice, releasing on Egg Plant (1987–1989) and then Madagascar (1990–1991) and Jarmusic (1995). Jenkins’ co-conspirators there included Terry Burrows, Robyn Gibson, Geraldine Minou-Sullivan, Jonathan Staines, Andy Ward (2), and Martin Howells (2). The Chrysanthemums released two LPs for the Belgian label Madagascar (Odessey And Oracle, 1990; Porcupine Quills, 1991) and returned in 2022 with Decoy For A Dognapper! on Egg Plant.

Ruth’s Refrigerator was a Jenkins project with Robyn Gibson (vocals, drums), Blodwyn P. Teabag (keyboards), Terri Lowe (vocals, bass), and Ruth Miller (vocals, guitar). They released two LPs for Madagascar in 1990–1991 (Suddenly A Disfigured Head Parachuted, A Lizard Is A Submarine On Grass), with Jenkins producing and playing vocals and guitar.

Jody And The Creams — alias The Creams — also recorded for Cordelia Records (1988–1990), with Jenkins alongside Ariadne Metal-Cream Pie, Blodwyn P. Teabag, Jonathan Staines, and Ruth Miller.

In 1994 Jenkins released ie, a double LP credited to “Alan Jenkins and The Creams” on Cordelia Records (co-released by Raffmond in Germany). The record is an ambitious, multi-voiced song-cycle with Jenkins writing all music and words, playing guitar and voicing the character “E & some other animals”. Personnel included Sherree Lawrence (vocals), Alison Mackinder (vocals/violin), Blodwyn P. Teabag (keyboards), Robyn Gibson (drums), Andy Nicholls (tenor saxophone), and several character-named vocalists.

Cordelia Records Studio and Engineering Work (mid-1990s–2010s)

Jenkins opened the Cordelia Records studio in Leicester in the mid-1990s. Discogs credits show him engineering or mixing releases for various UK acts across this period: One Little Indian act Heal (assistant engineer, 1995); a Hideaway soundtrack (mixer, 1995); Yak Folks Y’Are (engineered by, 1999, Pickled Egg Records); and engineering for Where It’s At Is Where You Are and Fortuna Pop! releases (2000–2001). He also mixed releases as late as 2018 (Some Hearts Are Paid To Die on Dying Day).

The Thurston Lava Tube and Ongoing Work (1999–present)

The Thurston Lava Tube is Jenkins’ long-running psychedelic / surf instrumental project, releasing on Cordelia Records and Sorted. Members include Blodwyn P. Teabag, Marshall Cavendish, and Mat Bartram (2). They released Pour Quoi Pas Moo (1999) and The Pink Elephant With Nipples For Tusks (2005).

Jenkins continued as a solo performer and in various configurations; Discogs credits him as primary on Free Surf Music #4 (2016, Cordelia Records), a release also featuring Jonathan Lemon (accordion) and Mat Bartram (2). His most recent primary-credited release in the DB is Decoy For A Dognapper! (2022) with The Chrysanthemums.
