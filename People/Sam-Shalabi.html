Sam Shalabi

Discogs ID: 199971
Real Name: Osama Shalabi
Active: 1992–present
Origin: Montreal, Canada (Egyptian-Canadian)
Role: Guitar, oud, electronics, composer

Egyptian-Canadian composer and improviser based in Montreal. Began as a punk musician in the late 1970s; work evolved into a fusion of experimental music, modern Arabic music, noise, free improvisation, and jazz. Founder of Shalabi Effect.

Key Projects

  • Shalabi Effect — free improvisation quartet; core 1996–2005, periodic reunions
  • Land Of Kush — experimental 30-member orchestra (Discogs ID: 1404372); four albums
  • The Dwarfs of East Agouza (Discogs ID: 5044485) — Cairo-based trio with Alan Bishop and Maurice Louca
  • Karkhana (Discogs ID: 4230773) — sextet with musicians from Lebanon, Egypt, Turkey, USA
  • Moose Terrific (Discogs ID: 5346214)
