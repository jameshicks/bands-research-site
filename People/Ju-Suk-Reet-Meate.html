Ju Suk Reet Meate

Discogs ID: 555739 Real name: Eric Stewart Aliases / name variations: Eric Stewart (2) (Discogs ID: 1069917), Reet, Ricky Reet, JS, JSRM, Ju Suk, Reet Meate, Ricky Reet (Ju Suk) Role: Multi-instrumentalist, composer, recording engineer, mixer, photographer, designer Location: Pasadena / Los Angeles, California; later Portland, Oregon Active: 1973–present

Founding member and long-term anchor of Smegma (Discogs ID: 67352). More consistently present across Smegma’s recorded output than any other member — credited on 74+ shared releases in the DB. Also maintains a solo recording practice documented on at least two releases, and handles the group’s recording, mixing, and visual production work on many releases.

Biography

Origins and LAFMS, 1973–1979

Eric Stewart co-founded Smegma in Pasadena, California in 1973 under the name Ju Suk Reet Meate, a pseudonym he has used consistently across five decades of work. The group emerged from the same Pasadena experimental underground that produced the Los Angeles Free Music Society (LAFMS) — a cooperative network of improvisers and tape collagists active through the 1970s and early 1980s. Smegma were participants in the LAFMS from its earliest period; their debut LP Glamour Girl 1941 (1979) appeared on the LAFMS cooperative label (cat. LAFMS-008).

The earliest Discogs-documented recording under Ju Suk Reet Meate’s own name is Acnode One (1978, Not On Label), a co-release with Dr. Id (Mike Lastra), performed jointly and credited to both artists. This duo recording precedes the first Smegma LP and documents the experimental duo work happening within the collective context.

Solo Practice, 1975–1980

The release Solo 1978 / 79 (1980, Pigface Records, cat. Pigface Records 004) — and its 2017 expanded reissue Solo 1975-1980 (Alga Marghen, Italy, cat. TES 153LP) — gather Ju Suk Reet Meate’s solo recordings from the mid-to-late 1970s. These are the primary documents of his individual practice outside the group context during the formative LAFMS period. The Alga Marghen reissue extends the window back to 1975, indicating solo work predating Acnode One.

Core Smegma Member, 1979–present

Across Smegma’s full recorded history, Ju Suk Reet Meate is the constant. He plays guitar, bass, trumpet, keyboards (Farfisa, clavinet), oboe (credited as “mussette”), turntables (credited as “record skips”), percussion, tape loops, and vocals — often in combination on a single release. His instrument list on Ism (1993, Tim/Kerr Records) is representative: guitar, trumpet, oboe, clavinet, keyboards, bass, turntables, percussion, tape loops, and vocals.

Beyond performance, he takes on production and technical roles that Michael Lastra (Dr. Id) does not: recording, editing, and mixing on Wild Blue Yonders (2012, iDEAL Recordings); mixing and mastering on Smegma Vs. Black Leather Jesus (2017, Axis Mundi); and bass, organ, trumpet, recording, and mixing on Mirage (2010, Important Records). This dual performer/engineer role makes him the technical backbone of the group’s later production.

Visual work also runs consistently through his credits: cover photography and design on Morass (1988, Soleilmoon Recordings, the label’s inaugural release SOL-01); cover artwork on Swampdick (1991); photography on The Mad Excitement, The Barbaric Pulsations, The Incomparable Rhythms Of… (1996); illustration credit on Smell The Remains (1989); and cover and design on Smegma Vs. Black Leather Jesus (2017). Artwork and design on Folklore & Concepts (2025, Helicopter), shared with Ace Farren Ford and John Wiese.

He is listed as a composer on 40 Years Of Smegma (2015, Otoroku), which credits the five-person performing core — Ace Farren Ford, Dennis Duck, Ju Suk Reet Meate, Madelyn Villano, Rock And Roll Jackie — as collective composers.

Outside Projects and Contributions

Spontaneous Sound (1983, Pigface Records, cat. Pigface Records 008) is a group improvisation compilation on which Ju Suk appears as a non-primary contributor.

In 2011 he was credited as remastering engineer on Blorp Esette (Transparency, cat. 0022), a Various Artists compilation compiled by Ace Farren Ford and Rick Potts, with artwork by Don Van Vliet (Captain Beefheart). Joseph Hammer handled the tape transfer and initial mastering; Ju Suk’s credit is specifically “Remastered By [June 2011].”

He appears uncredited as an additional performer on Human Taste (2009, Ecstatic Peace!, cat. E#104D) by Little Claw, a live recording in which he participated alongside several other musicians.

In 2021 he received a photography credit on the box set Los Angeles Free Music Society -1974~1983+ (Vinyl-on-demand, Germany, cat. VOD171), a retrospective documentation of the LAFMS scene — confirming his photographic archive of that period.
