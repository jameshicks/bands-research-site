Jari Haapalainen

Finnish-Swedish record producer. Discogs artist ID: 258223.

Overview

Producer closely associated with the Swedish indie and punk scene of the late 1990s–2000s. Produced both The International Noise Conspiracy albums Survival Sickness (2000) and A New Morning, Changing Weather (2001), also contributing additional percussion on Survival Sickness.
