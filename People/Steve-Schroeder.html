Overview

Steve Schroeder is an American musician, producer, graphic artist, and vocalist based in Portland, Oregon. He founded States Rights Records in 2001, a label that issued work across indie rock, lo-fi, electro, and experimental styles through the mid-2000s. His Discogs credits are concentrated entirely in that decade, with his closest collaborative relationships being with Jona Bechtolt and Khaela Maricich/The Blow, alongside fellow Portland figures Adam Forkner and Zac Pennington. Beyond his own label, his work appeared on K, Not Not Fun Records, Tomlab, and Popfrenzy Records.
