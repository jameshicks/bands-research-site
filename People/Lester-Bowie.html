Lester Bowie

Born: October 11, 1941, Frederick, Maryland
Died: November 8, 1999, New York City
Instrument: Trumpet
Discogs artist ID: 95089

Trumpeter and co-founder of The Art Ensemble Of Chicago. One of the core figures of the AACM (Association for the Advancement of Creative Musicians). Long-standing collaborator with Phillip Wilson, with whom he shared 7 recorded releases across their careers.

Overview

Bowie grew up in St. Louis and came up through R&B and jazz before joining the AACM in Chicago. He co-founded the Art Ensemble of Chicago with Roscoe Mitchell, Malachi Favors, Joseph Jarman, and Don Moye. Bowie was known for his wide range of trumpet styles — from bebop to tailgate to pure noise — and for theatrical performance. He led several concurrent groups including Lester Bowie’s Brass Fantasy (a large brass ensemble) and the New York Organ Ensemble.
