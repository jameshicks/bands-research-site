Irene Schweizer

Discogs ID: 296710

Swiss jazz pianist, born 1941. Pioneer of European free jazz. Co-primary artist on Jazz Meets India (SABA, 1967) with Barney Wilen, Manfred Schoof, and the Dewan Motihar Trio. Led the Irene Schweizer Trio. Associated with FMP Records and the European free improvisation scene.
