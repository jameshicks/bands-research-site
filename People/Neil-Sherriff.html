Bio

Neil Sherriff was the rhythm guitarist in the Leicester post-punk band Observers. His sole confirmed Discogs credit is a co-writing and performance credit on the Observers’ 7” single This Age / Suicide (catalogue ST 2), released in 1980 on The S+T Label, a small Leicester-based independent label co-founded by Chris Freer and Kev Reverb (Kevin Bayliss).

The single — a two-track 7” on black vinyl — featured “This Age” on the A-side and “Suicide” on the B-side. Both tracks were credited as written by the Observers collectively. The record was engineered by Simon Jones (4) and the lacquer was cut by Porky (5) (George Peckham), whose characteristic humorous etchings appear in the runouts: “IN ANY AGE A PORKY PRIME CUT. (ALI)” (side A) and “IT’S A KILLER. A PORKY PRIME CUT. (ALI)” (side B). The single carries catalogue number ST 2 on The S+T Label (preceded by the The Amber Squad 7” ST 1, and followed by the Where The Hell Is… Leicester compilation LP on STAB 1).

Beyond this single, no further Discogs credits exist for Neil Sherriff under this name. No aliases, name variations, or group memberships are recorded.
