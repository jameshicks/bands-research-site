Terry Chambers

Drummer. Born 18 July 1955, Swindon, Wiltshire, England. Discogs artist ID: 316129.

Overview

Terry Chambers co-founded XTC in Swindon in 1972 under their original name Star Park, and played on every XTC studio recording through Mummer (1983). His drumming anchored the band’s early post-punk and art-rock periods, ranging from the spare angularity of Drums and Wires (produced by Steve Lillywhite, 1979) to the dense layering of Black Sea (1980) and the double-LP English Settlement (1982). He left during the Mummer sessions in 1982, married, and emigrated to Australia, contributing drums to only two tracks on that album before Pete Phipps finished the record.

After settling in Australia, Chambers joined Dragon (5) (the New Zealand–Australian rock band) in late 1983, appearing on their album Body and the Beat (Polydor, 1984) and the live record Live One (Liberation Records, 1985), then departed mid-1985. In the 2010s he reunited with XTC bassist Colin Moulding in TC&I, recording Great Aspirations (TC&I Music, 2017) and the live album Naked Flames (2019). He also plays in EXTC (2), an XTC tribute band he founded, who released EXTC Live (2022), mixed by John Leckie — the same producer who helmed XTC’s first two LPs.

Name variations on Discogs: “Colin Chambers”, “Eileen and Peter’s son”, “Terry”, “テリー・チェンバース”.
