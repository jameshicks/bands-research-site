Osunlade

Real name: Christian Carlos Warren Discogs ID: 5164 Born: St. Louis, Missouri, USA (raised in Chicago) Based: Paris, France Active: 1999–present Roles: DJ, producer, multi-instrumentalist, label founder

House DJ, producer, and label head. Founder and operator of Yoruba Records. Known for deep, spiritual, Afro-house built around live instrumentation — synthesizers, Rhodes, hand percussion, strings, brass — and a cosmology rooted in Yoruba religion. Discogs profile says St. Louis; he is widely identified with Chicago’s house scene.

Biography

Origins and Yoruba Records founding (1999–2001)

Osunlade launched Yoruba Records in 1999 out of the US, with catalog numbers prefixed YOR-. His debut release under his own name on the label was Natives Tongue (YOR-005, 1999) — mixed and edited by Ben Grassini alongside Osunlade. The label’s first year also included Closer I Get by Marie St. James (YOR-010) and releases by Ebbo. The Yoruba Records (El Primer Ano) compilation (YOR-040, 2000) collected the first year’s output.

Early releases under the Latina Café alias appeared in 2000 (Power To Conquer, YOR-025). By 2001 he was producing for the label’s growing roster — Cantos A Ochun Et Oya (YOR-050, 2001) featured a remix by Frankie Feliciano (“Ricanstructed By”) and an edit by Juan Valentine. The Que Vive El Canto release by Sambas (YOR-045, 2001) expanded the label’s spiritual-percussion direction.

UK expansion and international recognition (2002–2005)

Around 2002 Osunlade’s catalog shifted to UK pressing on Soul Jazz Records, releasing Paradigm (2001, Soul Jazz Records) and Rader Du / Blackman (Soul Jazz, 2001) — the latter credited as “Producer [Created By].” The Yoruba Records Cinco Anos Despue (Five Years On) anthology (2006, Soul Jazz Records) later documented this period.

2002 brought Pride (Set, France) — a vocal collaboration with Nadirah Shakoor that became one of his most recognized tracks. Osunlade produced and handled all instrumentation; Shakoor supplied vocals. The track was remixed extensively and Shakoor became a recurring Yoruba Records artist.

Also in 2002: Offering (R2 Records, UK), his first mix compilation for that imprint, which became a recurring series.

He contributed a remix to the Afefe Iku discography context during this era: Sakitayo (Osunlade Remixes) (Isma’a, France, 2003) — drums, balaphone, ARP Omni, Minimoog, Moog Source, additional vocals. The depth of instrumentation on remixes was already signature.

In 2004 The Yoruba Soul Mixes (Rapster Records / BBE, Germany/Europe) compiled his remix and production work as a DJ-mix album. The same year: Same Thing (Papa Records, UK), produced and engineered by Osunlade with vocals by Maiya James. He also began the The Year Of The Monkey (Yoruba Records, 2004).

Ibara (River Crossing) (Yoruba Records, YOR LP111, 2005) — a compilation he presented and executive-produced — marked a label milestone.

Aquarian Moon and BBE era (2006–2008)

Aquarian Moon (BBE, 2006) is widely regarded as one of his definitive statements. Osunlade played or programmed nearly everything: drums, tambourine, acoustic piano, electric piano (Rhodes), udu, Mini Moog, electric bass, shaker, shekere, analog synths, clavinet, ARP2600, bongos, sampler, Wurlitzer, voice, ethnic percussion — crediting himself also as conductor. Guest contributors: Skeeta (2) on electric and acoustic guitar, Nordic fiddle, fretless bass, and Middle Eastern santoor; Christian Carlos (Mr. Christian Carlos) on vibraphone, saxophone, flute, trumpet, clarinet; The I’lle Orchestra (his own alias) on strings and brass. Mastered by Shawn Joseph. A&R by Peter Adarkwah (BBE). Art direction by Thomas McCallion (Tommy Badshoes).

The same year: Soul Heaven (Soul Heaven Records, 2006) — a double-disc DJ-mix compilation co-mixed with DJ Spen. Each mixed their own disc. This was one of four shared releases between the two. The Soul Heaven Presents DJ Spen & Osunlade LP sets (two volumes, Soul Heaven Records, 2006) followed.

Elements Beyond (Strictly Rhythm, 2007) extended the live-instrument approach — Osunlade created and performed all instrumentation himself, with Simon Dunmore as co-executive producer.

April (Strictly Rhythm, 2007) — again all instruments, vocals, production, and executive production by Osunlade; Simon Dunmore co-executive produced; layout by Claudio Maike. Both Strictly Rhythm albums show his most fully realized self-contained studio approach of the period.

Passage (BBE, 2008) was a compiled and DJ-mixed retrospective set.

Afefe Iku deepens (2007–2012)

Under the Afefe Iku alias Osunlade released a cluster of more percussive, stripped-down records on Yoruba Records:

  • Bodydrummin (Yoruba Records, YS-009.5, 2007)
  • Artifacts Of Pottery Vessels (Yoruba Records, YS009, 2008)
  • Mirror Dance (Yoruba Records, YS0975, 2008) — 9 pressings; one of his most widely distributed releases
  • The Blues (Yoruba Records, YSD43, 2012)
  • Order Of Direction (Yoruba Records, YSD62, 2014)

Later LPs and Yoruba Records maturity (2009–2017)

Rebirth (Yoruba Records, 2009) was his first full album on the label under his own name since the compilation era. Butterfly (2010, Yoruba Records) and Pyrography (2011, Yoruba Records, YSD039CD) followed in quick succession.

We Call Love (2011) with remixes attracted wider attention via a DJ Harvey remix released on Crosstown Rebels (2011). Envision Remixes (Innervisions, 2011, Germany) — 8 pressings — brought him into the Innervisions orbit, with remixes by Frank Wiedemann (Soul FC).

A Man With No Past Originating The Future (Yoruba Records, YSD48L, 2013) — 5 pressings — is one of his most-pressed solo LPs.

Peacock (Yoruba Records, YSD69, 2014): Osunlade wrote and produced; The Yoruba Soul Orchestra supplied cello and vibraphone; Dwayne Carter (5) played guitar on two tracks.

Dedication EP (Yoruba Records, YSD82, 2017): Osunlade handled all instruments, programming, and arranging solo.

Recent work (2018–present)

Moss (Yoruba Records, YSD108D, 2020): Osunlade produced, performed, and recorded; the Afefe Iku alias contributed artwork; CoFlo mastered.

Basic Sketches For Beginners (Yoruba Records, 2020) was co-released.

Isolated (Flaneurecordings, 2022, Germany): vocals and written-by credit alongside Oliver $ and Stefan Braatz.

Prism (R2 Records, 2025): produced with Nathan Meyer.
