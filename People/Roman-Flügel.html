Roman Flügel

Discogs ID: 19758
Aliases: Ro70, Acid Test, Eight Miles High, List (2), Roman IV, Soylent Green
Origin: Frankfurt, Germany
Role: Producer, DJ

Frankfurt-based electronic music producer and DJ. Operating under various aliases, most notably Ro70 for his more experimental/ambient output and Roman Flügel for house and broader electronic work. Founder of Sister Midnight, his own imprint and party series (launched 2020). Recorded two collaborative EPs with Move D as Ro70 Meets Move D: Ro70 Meets Move D (1997) and Ro70 Meets Move D II (1998), both on Source Records (2).
