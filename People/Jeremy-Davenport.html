Discogs ID: 399206 Role: Trumpeter, Vocalist Origin: St. Louis, Missouri; based in New Orleans, Louisiana Active: 1987–present Associated Acts: Harry Connick Jr. Big Band, Todd Williams Quintet

Biography

Early Life and St. Louis Roots

Jeremy Davenport is a St. Louis, Missouri native who grew up immersed in that city’s classical and jazz orbit. His formative musical education took place through contact with members and guest artists of the St. Louis Symphony, which gave him early exposure to high-level musicianship. Among the most significant early influences was an introduction to Wynton Marsalis, whose advocacy for the jazz tradition was a model for Davenport’s own development as a brass player.

His earliest documented recording is Just In Time (1987, Not On Label), a small-run release that predates his professional big-band work.

Harry Connick Jr. Big Band Years (c. 1991–1999)

Davenport joined Harry Connick Jr’s big band and remained a core member for approximately six years. His presence is documented across several major Columbia Records releases, credited specifically as playing with the Harry Connick, Jr. Big Band:

  • Blue Light, Red Light (1991, Columbia) — trumpet
  • When My Heart Finds Christmas (1993, Columbia CK 57550) — trumpet, Harry Connick, Jr. Big Band
  • Star Turtle (1996, Columbia) — trumpet/horns
  • Come By Me (1999, Columbia) — trumpet, Big Band
  • The New York Big Band Concert (Columbia Music Video) — trumpet

He shares 53 release credits with Connick, placing him alongside core big band members Roger Ingram and Dan Miller as the trumpet section. The Connick big band was a working road and recording unit throughout this period, touring internationally and releasing albums on Columbia that ranged from big-band swing to New Orleans funk.

Solo Recording Career (1996–present)

While still active with the Connick band, Davenport launched his solo career on Telarc Jazz (a sublabel of Telarc), releasing two albums:

Jeremy Davenport (1996, Telarc Jazz CD-83376) — His self-titled debut. A contemporary jazz vocal-and-trumpet set in the tradition of the New Orleans-inflected Great American Songbook style. Personnel:

  • Glenn Patscha — piano (most tracks)
  • Christopher Thomas — bass (most tracks)
  • Neal Caine — bass (tracks 6, 7)
  • Martin Butler — drums
  • Shannon Powell — drums
  • Jeremy Davenport — vocals, trumpet

Maybe In A Dream (1997, Telarc CD-83409) — Second solo album. Featured the same core rhythm section plus a guest vocal appearance by Diana Krall on track 4 (“Let’s Leave”). Personnel:

  • Glenn Patscha — piano
  • Peter Washington — bass
  • Gregory Hutchinson — drums
  • Diana Krall — vocals (track 4)
  • Jeremy Davenport — trumpet, vocals

Both records present Davenport as a trumpet-playing vocalist in the lineage of Chet Baker and Louis Armstrong — melodic, intimate, and rooted in swing and ballad repertoire.

After a gap in solo recording, Davenport returned with We’ll Dance ‘Til Dawn (2009, Basin Street Records BSR 1101-2), his third solo album and first for the New Orleans-based independent label. The record continued in the contemporary jazz/easy listening mode, with twelve tracks including standards (“That Old Black Magic,” “The Lady Is A Tramp”) and originals (“Mr. New Orleans,” “We’ll Dance ‘Til Dawn”).

The search results from his Discogs page list Basin Street Records as one of his top three labels (alongside Telarc Jazz and Telarc), suggesting further releases may exist beyond what the main credits table captures.

Sideman and Scene Work

Beyond the Connick big band, Davenport appears on:

  • Louissiana (1995, Initial (2)) — trumpet sideman credit; a French release connected to the New Orleans music scene
  • Mood Indigo (1997, Rounder Records CD 2145) — trumpet in the New Orleans C.A.C. Jazz Orchestra alongside Nicholas Payton, Wessell Anderson, Victor Goines, Christopher Thomas, Herlin Riley, and others; vocalists Johnny Adams and Germaine Bazzle; produced by Dave Torkanowsky and Scott Billington

The Mood Indigo credit places Davenport firmly within the New Orleans traditional jazz community, playing alongside figures central to that scene.

Todd Williams Quintet

Davenport is listed as a member of the Todd Williams Quintet (Discogs: 10414342), alongside:

  • Todd Williams — leader (saxophone)
  • Peter Martin (6) — piano
  • David Berger — (instrument unspecified)
  • Mark Peterson — (instrument unspecified)

No release credits for this group appear in the Discogs data, suggesting it may be a live working band without a Discogs-documented recording.

New Orleans Base

Davenport has made New Orleans his home base after his years with the Connick band. He plays regularly in the city, tours internationally, and returns annually to St. Louis for a Thanksgiving engagement at Jazz St. Louis. His Basin Street Records affiliation places him within the label’s roster of New Orleans jazz artists that includes Kermit Ruffins, Irvin Mayfield, the Rebirth Brass Band, and Los Hombres Calientes.
