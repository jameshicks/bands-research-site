John Cummings

Scottish guitarist, producer, and engineer. Member of Mogwai from at least 1996, also a member of The Reindeer Section. Active as producer/engineer for Rock Action Records acts and others. Discogs artist ID: 308221.

Aliases on Discogs: Captain Meat (credited on Young Team), John Of Arc. Name variations include J. Cummings, John Cumming, Jon Cummings, Cummings.

Overview

John Cummings has been a guitarist in Mogwai since the band’s earliest releases in 1996. He is one of five core members alongside Stuart Braithwaite, Dominic Aitchison, Martin Bulloch, and Barry Burns (who joined slightly later). On Young Team (1997) he was credited under the pseudonym “Cpt. Meat” (as the alias Captain Meat). Alongside his performing work, Cummings established himself through the mid-2000s as a producer and recording engineer, working primarily with bands in the post-rock and noise-rock orbit — Part Chimp, Errors, and Fuck Buttons being the most significant.

He also played guitar on both albums by The Reindeer Section, the Scottish collective assembled by Snow Patrol’s Gary Lightbody.

The Part Chimp work is fully confirmed by Discogs credits — he produced their first two Rock Action albums and engineered the third. The Fuck Buttons credits are notable: Cummings and Part Chimp’s Tim Cedar (who was also in Fuck Buttons’ circle) co-recorded all three of the band’s early ATP releases including the debut Street Horrrsing.

The Reindeer Section

Cummings played guitar on both Reindeer Section albums, the sprawling Scottish indie collective led by Gary Lightbody of Snow Patrol, featuring members of Mogwai, Arab Strap, Idlewild, Belle & Sebastian, and others.
