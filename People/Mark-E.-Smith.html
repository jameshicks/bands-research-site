Mark E. Smith

Oct 29, 20262 min read

Mark E. Smith

Discogs ID: 267527
Born: 5 March 1957, Salford, Greater Manchester
Died: 24 January 2018

English vocalist, lyricist, and the sole constant member of The Fall, which he founded in Manchester in 1976. Fronted The Fall through dozens of lineup changes over four decades, maintaining a prolific and uncompromising output rooted in post-punk, krautrock, and rockabilly influences. Notoriously confrontational, lyrically idiosyncratic, and culturally indispensable to British post-punk.

Appeared on Ghostigital’s debut single “Not Clean / Crackers” (2005, Honest Jon’s Records HJP25) and the subsequent album In Cod We Trust (2006, Ipecac Recordings) — vocals on “Not Clean”. Also appears on the Antimatter Boutique retrospective (2013) on a track titled “Codomatopoeia”.
