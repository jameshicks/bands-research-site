Overview

Jon Langford is a Welsh musician, visual artist, and co-founder of The Mekons, who formed at the University of Leeds in 1977. He was originally the band’s drummer but shifted to guitar and vocals as the lineup evolved. Through the 1980s he became a key figure at the intersection of British post-punk and American country, helping steer the Mekons toward what became known as country-punk on the landmark Fear And Whiskey (1985) and The Edge Of The World (1986). He co-founded The Three Johns in the early 1980s with John Hyatt and John Brennan, and co-founded The Waco Brothers after relocating to Chicago in the early 1990s.

Beyond performing, Langford was an active producer for the British independent scene through the late 1980s, working with The Ex, The Three Johns, Big Flame, and several other bands on labels including In Tape, Red Rhino, and Rough Trade. He also co-executive-produced the influential 1988 Johnny Cash tribute ‘Til Things Are Brighter on Red Rhino.

Langford is also a prolific visual artist, particularly known for portraits of country music icons (Hank Williams, Johnny Cash, Elvis). He illustrated the comic strip Great Pop Things under the alias Chuck Death. Since 2005 he has co-hosted “The Eclectic Company” on WXRT 93.1 FM Chicago. He is closely associated with Bloodshot Records, where he championed many artists as a collaborator and cover painter.

The Three Johns (1981–1990)

Discogs ID: 151086. Drum-machine-driven punk trio with John Hyatt and John (Phillip) Brennan. Released on Abstract Records and Abstract Sounds. Key LPs: Atom Drum Bop (1984), The World By Storm (1986), The Death Of Everything (1988, Trance Records, Germany). Langford was a member alongside the two Johns.

The Waco Brothers (1995–present)

Discogs ID: 521901. Chicago alt-country/country-punk band co-founded by Langford after relocating to Chicago. Members include Dean Schlabowske, Tracey Dear, Alan Doughty, Mark Durante, and Mekons drummer Steve Goulding. Exclusively on Bloodshot Records. Key LPs: … To The Last Dead Cowboy (1995), Cowboy In Flames (1997), Electric Waco Chair (2000), Great Chicago Fire (2012), Going Down In History (2016).

The Pine Valley Cosmonauts

Discogs ID: 1582295. Chicago revolving ensemble used by Langford to back himself and others (Kelly Hogan, etc.) on country-oriented recordings. Several alternative country musicians guested on these recordings.
