From Discogs

Born 1956 Died 2001 Ian Paul Lowery was born on March 27, 1956 in Hartlepool in the North-East of England.

He formed and fronted many bands during a long and prolific musical career, gaining critical acclaim both within and outside the industry. Many claim that his music deserved and is still deserving of a much wider audience.

A well-honed gutter poet since the early days of punk, Ian developed an idiosyncratic style, fusing a natural gift for sly wordplay and a well-turned snarky phrase with his often vitriolic and poignant lyrics channelled through an explosive onstage persona. All of which gained him a loyal following throughout his tragically short musical life.

Overview

Ian Lowery (1956–2001) was a Hartlepool-born vocalist and lyricist who moved through British punk and post-punk across several bands — Folk Devils, King Blank, Drug Of Choice, F For Fake, and Phantom White Limb — building a cult reputation for caustic wordplay and an intense stage presence. His recorded output was concentrated in the late 1980s, primarily on Situation Two (five releases, 1987–89) and Beggars Banquet (two releases, 1988–90), with frequent collaborators Hugh Garrety, Kris Jozajtis, and Nigel Pulsford appearing across multiple projects. Reissue activity in the 2010s on labels including Dark Entries and Definitive Gaze suggests a belated reassessment, fitting for an artist whose critical standing apparently outpaced his commercial reach.
