Karl Precoda

Karl Precoda

Oct 18, 20267 min read

Karl Precoda

Discogs ID: 564356
Name variations: K. Precoda, Karl, Precoda
Instrument: Guitar (lead/rhythm); occasional bass
Active: 1981–c.1997 (documented credits)
Scene: Paisley Underground, LA experimental / art-noise

Guitarist and founding member of The Dream Syndicate, the Los Angeles group central to the early 1980s Paisley Underground. Precoda co-wrote at least one track on the band’s debut LP and played lead and rhythm guitar on their first two studio albums before departing ahead of the 1986 Out Of The Grey LP. Outside the Dream Syndicate he contributed bass to Johanna Went’s Hyena (Posh Boy, 1982) and Club Years (Soleilmoon, 2007), appeared on the Paisley Underground super-session LP as a member of Rainy Day (1984), and surfaced uncredited on the self-titled Last Days Of May EP (No-Fi Records, 1997).

Biography

Founding The Dream Syndicate (1981–1982)

The Dream Syndicate formed in Los Angeles in 1981, founded by Steve Wynn and Kendra Smith alongside Karl Precoda and drummer Dennis Duck. The band quickly became a cornerstone of the loose Paisley Underground coalition alongside Rain Parade, The Three O’Clock, and the Bangles.

Their debut self-titled EP (Down There Records, 1982) features the classic four-piece lineup: Precoda on guitar, Kendra Smith on bass, Dennis Duck on drums, Steve Wynn on guitar and vocals; Paul B. Cutler engineered. The EP circulated within the LA scene and served as the calling card that brought them wider attention.

Later in 1982 the band recorded The Days Of Wine And Roses (Rough Trade), produced by Chris D. (of The Flesh Eaters). Precoda is credited with guitar on the full album and with sole songwriting credit on one track (side A, track 5). This is the most extensive documentation of his compositional contribution to the band’s catalog.

Johanna Went Connection (1982)

In 1982, concurrent with the early Dream Syndicate activity, Precoda played bass on four tracks of Johanna Went’s debut LP Hyena (Posh Boy, PBS 139). The record was produced by Went and Mark Wheaton; Jeff McLane and Mark Wheaton engineered. Precoda appears on tracks A2, A3, B2, and B3 — the bulk of the LP’s musical backing. The connection between the Dream Syndicate camp and Went’s LA art-noise scene is direct: Posh Boy photographer Ed Colver also contributed cover work, and Lynda Burdick (whose cover photography also appears on the Dream Syndicate EP) is a recurring figure across both scenes.

Medicine Show and Departure (1983–1985)

The band signed to A&M Records and recorded Medicine Show (1984), produced by Sandy Pearlman. Precoda is credited with both lead guitar and rhythm guitar — the fullest guitar credit he receives in the catalog. Kendra Smith had departed by this point; Dave Provost took the bass role. Additional musicians on the session included Sid Griffin and Stephen McCarthy (The Long Ryders) on backing vocals, and Tom Zvoncheck on piano/organ. A live album, This Is Not The New Dream Syndicate Album… Live!, also appeared on A&M in 1984 with Precoda on lead guitar.

By the time of the 1986 Out Of The Grey LP (Chrysalis/Big Time), Precoda was no longer in the band. That album’s credits list Paul B. Cutler as producer and guitarist alongside Wynn, with Mark Walton on bass — no Precoda credit. His last documented studio session with the Dream Syndicate as far as Discogs records show is Medicine Show (1984). The live The Day Before Wine And Roses (Normal, 1994) — recorded at KPFK on September 5, 1982 — captures the original lineup and credits Precoda on guitar.

Rainy Day (1984)

Precoda was a member of Rainy Day, the Paisley Underground covers collective assembled from members of the Dream Syndicate, Rain Parade, The Three O’Clock, and the Bangles. The roster included Kendra Smith, David Roback, Steven Roback, Matt Piucci, Michael Quercio, Susanna Hoffs, Vicki Peterson, Will Glenn, and Dennis Mehaffey (Duck). David Roback produced the Rainy Day LP (Llama Records, 1984); Ethan James engineered. The LP is a set of cover versions central to documenting the Paisley Underground’s shared influences.

Post-Dream Syndicate: Last Days Of May (1997)

In 1997 Precoda appeared uncredited on guitar on the self-titled EP by Last Days Of May (No-Fi Records), a four-piece that also included James Ralston, Matt Datesman, and Thomas Howard. The credit being uncredited suggests an informal or guest contribution rather than full membership, though Discogs lists him as a member of the group.

Return to Johanna Went: Club Years (2007)

Twenty-five years after Hyena, Precoda returned to play bass on four tracks (CD-4, CD-5, CD-8, CD-9) of Johanna Went’s Club Years (Soleilmoon Recordings, 2007), a CD/DVD retrospective produced by Went and Mark Wheaton. The session reassembled much of the Hyena-era personnel — Brock Wheaton on drums, Jeff McLane engineering — alongside newer collaborators.

Dream Syndicate Reissue Era

Precoda’s guitar work is documented on the 2005 1982–1984: The Official DVD Bootleg Series: Vol. 1 (Blue Rose Records) and the 2022 expanded reissue What Can I Say? No Regrets… (Out Of The Grey + Live, Demos & Outtakes) (Fire Records), the latter crediting him with lead guitar. He also receives a “Written-By [Except] [By]” credit on the 2024 live album Live Through The Past, Darkly (Label 51 Recordings), indicating at least one track whose composition he shares credit for.

Scene Connections

  • Paisley Underground: Precoda was in at the founding of the movement’s defining band, and appeared on the coalition’s key covers LP (Rainy Day, 1984) alongside members of Rain Parade, The Three O’Clock, and the Bangles.
  • LA experimental / art-noise: The Hyena sessions (1982, 2007) connect him to Johanna Went’s circle, which overlapped with but was distinct from the guitar-pop Paisley Underground — more confrontational performance art.
  • Dream Syndicate reunion: The band reunited from 2012 onward without Precoda in the credited lineup; however, his compositions appear in the 2024 live set (Live Through The Past, Darkly).
