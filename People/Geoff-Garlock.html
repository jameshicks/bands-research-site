Overview

Geoff Garlock is an American bassist and vocalist from the Amherst, Massachusetts area, active in hardcore and screamo since the late 1990s. Best known as a member of Orchid — the influential screamo outfit whose tight recording circle included Jayson Green, Jeffrey Salane, and Will Killingsworth — he has remained active across two decades, playing in Panthers, Ritual Mess, and Low Estate while singing for All I Ask. Ebullition Records has been his most consistent label home, with releases spanning 2000 to 2026.
