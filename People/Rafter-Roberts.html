Rafter Roberts

Multi-instrumentalist, engineer, producer, solo artist. San Diego, California. Also credited as “Rafter.” Discogs ID: 360160.

Two parallel careers: (1) central recording/mastering engineer for the San Diego post-rock scene in the late 1990s–early 2000s, and (2) solo artist releasing under his first name on Asthmatic Kitty Records from 2005 onward. Both threads run simultaneously — the same person who mastered Spine And Sensory and One Day I’ll Be On Time is the one releasing experimental pop records for Sufjan Stevens’s label.

San Diego Engineering Work

Roberts was present at nearly every significant San Diego post-rock recording of the late 1990s:

  • Tristeza — Edited By, Mastered By on Spine And Sensory (Makoto Recordings, 1999); also Edited By, Mastered By on Macrame 7” (2000) and Insound Tour Support Series No. 1 (1999)
  • The Album Leaf — Recorded By, Mixed By on An Orchestrated Rise To Fall (1999); heavily involved on One Day I’ll Be On Time (Tiger Style / Better Looking Records, 2001) — Drum Programming, Recorded By, Mastered By
  • The Black Heart Procession — Recorded By, Mixed By on 2 (Touch And Go, 1999); Edited By, Extra Recording, “Helped Drop Some Beats” on Three (Touch And Go, 2000)
  • Modest Mouse — Mastered By on A 3 Song Recording EP (Up Records, 1999)

Also mastered releases on Swami Records (All Systems Go 2, Ghost Ship) and Gold Standard Laboratories releases, and holds a Recorded By credit on a Neurot Recordings compilation (2004).

Key Release Details

The Album Leaf — One Day I’ll Be On Time (2001)

Roberts’s deepest involvement with another artist: drum programming, recording, and mastering. The album came out on Tiger Style with Better Looking Records; Roberts was functionally co-producing alongside Jimmy LaValle at this stage.

The Black Heart Procession — 2 (Touch And Go, 1999)

Recorded By and Mixed By. His involvement on the BHP records alongside Pall Jenkins is the main link between him and the Touch And Go orbit.

Modest Mouse — A 3 Song Recording (Up Records, 1999)

Mastering credit situates him as a known quantity across the broader Pacific coast indie network, not just San Diego specifically.

Roberts & Lord (2011)

Roberts’s most unexpected collaboration. Roberts & Lord paired him with Simon Lord — British musician, co-founder of Simian (the UK electronic rock group whose “We Are Your Friends” was remixed by Justice and became a touchstone of the Ed Banger / French electro era) and The Black Ghosts. Both releases appeared on Asthmatic Kitty:

  • Eponymous LP (2011, Asthmatic Kitty AKR086) — twelve tracks; vinyl; credited as Electro/Synth-pop
  • Covers EP (2011, Asthmatic Kitty AKR326) — four covers: Marvin Gaye, Lennon-McCartney, D’Angelo, Prince

How a San Diego post-rock engineer met a UK ex-Simian member is not documented in Discogs. Asthmatic Kitty is the obvious bridge.

The Sufjan Stevens Connection

Roberts mastered Sufjan Stevens’s Enjoy Your Rabbit (Asthmatic Kitty, 2001) — the electronic album Stevens made between his folk-oriented records, which was later adapted into the Son Of Esau material. That credit preceded his own signing to the label by four years, suggesting he was in the Asthmatic Kitty orbit before becoming a roster artist.
