Overview

Ed Baluyut is an indie rock musician best known as a member of Versus, the group he has shared with Richard Baluyut and Fontaine Toups since at least 1988. He plays drums, guitar, and vocals; producers Adam Lasus and Nicolas Vernhes recur heavily across the band’s catalogue. His recorded output is concentrated in the 1990s (eight releases that decade), with a long primary relationship with Teenbeat spanning 1994 to the present and later activity on Ernest Jenning Record Co. in 2019–2020. He has also been a member of Flower and The Pacific Ocean.

Notes (web) — Versus 2019–2020 return

Versus had been essentially absent from recording since the 2010 Merge Records LP On the Ones and Threes. The 2019 EP Ex Nihilo and LP Ex Voto both appeared on Ernest Jenning Record Co., marking the band’s first new studio output in nearly a decade.

What sources confirm:

  • The two 2019 releases were drawn from the same recording sessions, engineered by Ian James and mixed by Ray Ketchem. (Ernest Jenning label page for Versus, as of 2026-10-26)
  • The Ex Nihilo Bandcamp page frames the EP as preceding “future cryptic work to be announced,” suggesting the material was already substantially ready when they surfaced. (versusny.bandcamp.com/album/ex-nihilo, as of 2026-10-26)
  • The Big Takeover review notes the releases “have been in the works sporadically for at least half of a decade,” implying the material gestated roughly from 2014 onward rather than being written in a concentrated burst. (bigtakeover.com, as of 2026-10-26)
  • Richard Baluyut described thematic concerns (escape, alternate dimensions, mortality, creation, devotional painting) but gave no explanation in available interviews for what ended the gap or why the band chose Ernest Jenning. (Exclaim!, as of 2026-10-26)
  • The band had previously worked with Teenbeat, Caroline, and Merge. Ernest Jenning represents a new label relationship; no source explains how it came about.
