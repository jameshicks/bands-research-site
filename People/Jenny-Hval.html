Jenny Hval

Discogs ID: 713862 Born: 1980 (approx), Norway Origin: Oslo, Norway Active: 2006–present Label: Sacred Bones (2015–2022)

Norwegian singer, songwriter, and novelist. Art-pop with conceptually rich, often explicitly feminist lyrical content. Sacred Bones output: Apocalypse, Girl (SBR-134, 2015, 7 pressings), Blood Bitch (SBR-161, 2016, 11 pressings), The Long Sleep EP (SBR-199, 2018, 5 pressings), The Practice Of Love (SBR-229, 2019, 9 pressings), Classic Objects (2022). 39 Discogs releases on Sacred Bones. Also writes prose fiction; first novel published in English as Paradise Rot.

Rune Grammofon Period (2011–2013)

Two albums on Rune Grammofon before moving to Sacred Bones:

  • Viscera (RCD 2108, 2011) — folk/ethereal; produced by Deathprod (Helge Sten); mixed by Helge Sten; mastered by Bob Katz; 4 pressings. Band: Håvard Volden (guitar), Kyrre Laastad (drums/keys).
  • Innocence Is Kinky (RLP 3142, 2013) — 7 pressings (the most-pressed of her Rune Grammofon titles); produced by John Parish; recorded largely in Bristol with John Parish and Ali Chant. Marks her international breakthrough.
