Carrie Brownstein

Born: 27 September 1974, Seattle, Washington, USA
Role: Guitarist, vocalist, songwriter
Discogs artist ID: 350406 (also listed as “Carrie Kinney,” ID: 1688921, on early releases)
Active: 1993–present

American guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter. Best known as co-founder and guitarist of Sleater-Kinney (1994–2006, reunited 2014–present), and co-founder of Wild Flag (2010–2012). A central figure in the Pacific Northwest riot grrrl / indie rock scene of the 1990s. Outside the Discogs scope: actor and co-creator of the IFC sketch comedy series Portlandia (2010–2018) with Fred Armisen.

Early Career: Excuse 17 (1993–1995)

Brownstein’s first recorded band was Excuse 17 (also listed as “Excuse Seventeen,” Discogs ID: 320266), an Olympia, Washington trio also featuring Becca Albee and C.J. Phillips. Active 1993–1995.

On early S-K releases (1995–1996), she was credited as Carrie Kinney rather than Brownstein.

Sleater-Kinney (1994–2006, 2014–present)

Brownstein co-founded Sleater-Kinney (Discogs ID: 218269) with Corin Tucker in Olympia, WA in 1994. The band’s name comes from a road in Lacey, WA. First drummer was Laura Macfarlane (Australian-born; joined late 1994). Janet Weiss replaced Macfarlane on drums in 1997, forming the classic trio lineup. The band announced an indefinite hiatus in summer 2006, reunited in 2014, and continues as a core duo (Brownstein + Tucker) after Weiss’s departure in 2019.

Producer Pattern

Goodmanson produced four Sleater-Kinney albums (Call The Doctor, Dig Me Out, One Beat, No Cities To Love) across two separate decades. Dave Fridmann produced The Woods (2005). St. Vincent (Annie Clark) produced the controversial The Center Won’t Hold (2019), after which Weiss departed. Little Rope (2024) went to John Congleton.

Other Band Affiliations

Wild Flag (2010–2012)

Supergroup formed with Janet Weiss (drums), Mary Timony (guitar, vocals), and Rebecca Cole (keyboards, piano, vocals). Released one self-titled album on Merge Records (2011) and a handful of singles. Brownstein plays guitar and handles lead vocals.
