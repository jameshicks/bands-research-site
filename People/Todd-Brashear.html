Todd Brashear

Bassist from Louisville, Kentucky. Discogs artist ID: 254075. Known primarily as the bassist on Slint’s Spiderland (1991) and all subsequent Slint activity. Also played in Solution Unknown (1986–1987), one of the early Louisville hardcore acts that connected several future Slint members.

Name variation on some releases: “Todd Brasher.”

Background

Brashear played guitar in Solution Unknown, a Louisville punk/hardcore band active 1986–1987 that also featured David Pajo and others from the pre-Slint Louisville scene. Solution Unknown self-released a cassette in 1984 (Justice For All) and put out two 7”s on Self-Destruct Records: Taken For Granted E.P. (1986) and Karen (1987), the latter recorded at Inner Ear Studio in Washington D.C. with Don Zientara engineering.

He replaced Ethan Buckler as Slint’s bassist around 1989, after the recording and release of Tweez. His first documented Slint-adjacent studio credit is as co-engineer (alongside Brian McMahan) on King Kong (3)‘s Old Man On The Bridge EP (Homestead Records, 1991) — a session that drew on the same Louisville social network.

Slint

Brashear is the bassist on Spiderland (Touch And Go, 1991), though like all band members he goes uncredited on the original sleeve. The album was engineered by Brian Paulson. He appears on the Untitled 10” (Touch And Go, 1994), recorded with Steve Albini engineering, though the 1994 pressing credits only the engineer, mixer (Paulson), and photographer — individual band members are not listed.

He participated in Slint’s reunion tours (2005, 2007, 2014) and played on the 2019 Record Store Day single Breadcrumb Trail / Good Morning, Captain (Touch And Go), which explicitly credits him, McMahan, Pajo, and Britt Walford by name and instrument.

Palace / Will Oldham sessions

Brashear appears on several Will Oldham / Palace recordings in the early-to-mid 1990s, reflecting the overlapping Louisville social circle:

  • There Is No-One What Will Take Care Of You (Palace Brothers, Drag City, 1993) — vocals (harmonies), drums, guitar (electric), bass, guitar (lap steel), shaker
  • An Arrow Through The Bitch (Palace Brothers, Domino, 1994) — bass on track 2 (alongside McMahan on guitar, Pajo on guitar, Walford on drums)
  • Lost Blues And Other Songs (Palace Music, Drag City / Palace Records, 1997) — credited as a player/writer/recorder; also credited for Photography By

The An Arrow Through The Bitch EP credit is particularly notable: four of the five Slint members appear on track 2 of that release, recording together as Palace Brothers sidemen.

He also appears as performer on the Palace Records single In My Mind / Spotlight (1997) and on Guarapero (Lost Blues 2) (Domino, 1999).

Other credits

March On (Reprise, 1993) — a various-artists compilation benefiting the National Civil Rights Museum — lists Brashear as “Mastered By [Assistant]” (credited as “Todd Brasher”), working under Hank Williams (2).
