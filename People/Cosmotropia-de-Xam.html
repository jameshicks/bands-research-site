Cosmotropia de Xam

Musician, composer, film director, designer, and label founder. Real name: Anna Luisa Syversen (also listed as alias). Discogs artist ID: 1917595. Based in Germany. Co-founder and primary visual/directorial force of Mater Suspiria Vision. Founder of the CDX and Phantasma Disques labels. Active from at least 2010 through the present.

Overview

Cosmotropia de Xam operates at the intersection of experimental electronic music, occult-flavored visual art, and underground film. The name functions as both a solo artistic identity and a production/curation persona tied tightly to Phantasma Disques — a label she founded that became the central hub of the German/international witch-house and “okkvlt” scene in the early 2010s. Her output divides into three overlapping streams: (1) solo and group DVDr/film releases in which she serves as director, with music contributed by MSV and collaborators; (2) audio contributions — electronics, synthesizer, echo tapes, production — to MSV releases and allied artists; (3) visual/design work — collage artwork, layout, photography, and mastering — across a vast catalog of Phantasma Disques releases by other artists.

She operates under an unusually large number of aliases and project names (see below), many appearing on Phantasma Disques releases as solo or collaborative audio projects.

Groups and Projects

Mater Suspiria Vision

Cosmotropia de Xam is a co-founder and core member of Mater Suspiria Vision (Discogs ID: 1820825), an Italian-German audiovisual collective. Other members include Aura (11) (also a member of all her side projects), Rachel Audrey, and Violette De Lestrange.

Her documented roles on MSV releases include electronics, synthesizer, echo tapes, producer, mastered by, artwork, photography, and design. On major MSV albums Inverted Triangle I (2011) and Inverted Triangle II (2011) she is credited as written-by, producer, and cover artwork/layout — suggesting a primary compositional and production role alongside Aura (11)‘s vocal contributions. On Hollywood Necronomicon (2013, cassette) she contributes electronics, echo tapes, artwork, photography, and mastering. On Antropophagus (2015, CD) she plays synthesizer and electronics and is credited as producer. On Scanners (2019) she handles electronics and artwork. On Baba Yaga (2018) and 666 (2018) she contributes electronic devices and mixing. On Cosmic Silver (2024) she contributes “electronic witchcraft” and mastering.

Madame O Und Ihre Kopffilm Bande

A duo or collective with Aura (11) (Discogs ID: 1820827). Name variations include Madame O, La Signora O e Il Piccolo Coro Tradizionale Di Brienno. Featured prominently as music contributor on the Diabolique film (2013).

The Videodrome Reality

A project with Aura (11) (Discogs ID: 4580462). Named after the Cronenberg film — consistent with the label’s deep engagement with Videodrome imagery (A Sound Installation Tribute To David Cronenberg’s Videodrome, 2012).

Pwin Teaks

The alias Pwin Teaks (Discogs ID: 5463075) appears as a distinct Discogs entry but shares the full alias list with Cosmotropia de Xam, confirming it as the same person. Pwin Teaks is credited on primary releases including Aoxomoxoa (2011, Brave Mysteries MYST005) — a vinyl LP on which Cosmotropia de Xam is credited for written-by, producer, artwork, photography, and mastering — and appears as a music contributor on many Phantasma Disques DVDr film releases.

Scene Context

Cosmotropia de Xam is the operational center of the German “okkvlt” witch-house scene as it coalesced around 2010–2013. Phantasma Disques — with 880+ releases — was the most prolific label in this micro-scene, functioning as a cassette/CDr/DVDr self-releasing infrastructure for dozens of acts worldwide. The label’s visual identity is inseparable from CdX’s collage aesthetic: nearly every release bears her handmade collage cover cards. The DVDr film series (under both her own name and Kosmische Puppen) set MSV’s music against occult/body-horror imagery directly referencing Argento (Suspiria), Lynch (Twin Peaks, Blue Velvet), Cronenberg (Videodrome, Scanners, Crash), and Italian genre cinema. The A Sound Installation Tribute compilation series and A Witch House & Okkvlt Guide To Twin Peaks comps are key documents of this approach.

External label appearances are limited but notable: Disaro (US, 2010 — the witch-house origin point), Living Tapes (US, 2010), Brave Mysteries (US, 2011), МИШКА (US, 2011), Clan Destine Records (UK, 2011), Dischi V (Italy, 2013–2014), O’69 (Japan, 2012), Black Porno Cassette (US, 2021), Dagger Forest (UK, 2023).
