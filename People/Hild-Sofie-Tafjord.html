Hild Sofie Tafjord

Discogs ID: 464839 Real name: Hild Sofie Tafjord Born: 4 January 1974, Langevåg, Møre og Romsdal, Norway Instrument: French horn, electronics Father: Stein Erik Tafjord (tuba player)

Overview

Norwegian French horn player, improviser, and composer. Active across jazz, experimental music, noise, improvisation, and live electronics. Studied at Toneheim folkehøgskole alongside Maja S. K. Ratkje, then continued at the University of Oslo and the Norwegian Academy of Music. Core member of Spunk (with Ratkje, Kristin Andersen, Lene Grenager); co-founder of noise duo Fe-mail (with Ratkje, from 2000); member of Trinacria; regular member of the Berlin-based ensemble Zeitkratzer (from 2009); and a member of Lemur (2) (quartet formed 2006 with Lene Grenager, Bjørnar Habbestad, Michael Duch). Appears on more than 130 albums. (Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hild_Sofie_Tafjord)
