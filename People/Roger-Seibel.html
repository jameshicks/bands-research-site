Biography

Roger Seibel is a US mastering and lacquer-cutting engineer based in Phoenix, Arizona. His career falls into three distinct phases: a formative period at QCA Custom Pressing in Cincinnati, Ohio (1979–1982), a transitional period at Wakefield Manufacturing in Phoenix (1982–1986), and a long independent phase at SAE Mastering, the studio he founded in Phoenix in 1986.

At QCA, Seibel was one of several in-house lacquer cutters (identified in runouts by the initials “RS”). QCA was a major Midwestern custom pressing and mastering plant that handled a substantial volume of independent and regional recordings; the roster of Seibel’s credits from the Cincinnati years (late 1970s to 1982) is dominated by gospel, country, regional rock, and Midwestern indie releases, alongside ECM Records pressings. After relocating to Phoenix and working briefly at Wakefield Manufacturing, he founded SAE Mastering in 1986, which became his primary credit from that point forward. At SAE, Seibel’s client base shifted toward independent rock, avant-garde, and experimental labels: ECM Records, Cuneiform Records, Flying Fish (2), Rounder Records, Thrill Jockey, Kill Rock Stars, Matador Records, Drag City, Barsuk Records, and Temporary Residence Limited, among many others.

Seibel’s Discogs credit count across all variants exceeds 3,500 individual release entries. His peak activity period was the mid-1980s through the 2000s. He mastered many landmark indie and post-rock releases, including Tortoise’s Millions Now Living Will Never Die (1996) and TNT (1998), Sleater-Kinney’s One Beat (2002), Unwound’s Leaves Turn Inside You (2001), Death Cab for Cutie’s Plans (2005), and Silver Jews’ Tanglewood Numbers (2005). In the avant-jazz and experimental sphere he was the go-to engineer for Cuneiform Records throughout the 1980s–1990s, and mastered extensively for ECM Records and Flying Fish during the vinyl era.

Open Questions

  • The Discogs profile credits include entries going back to 1965 — some of these early entries may be attribution errors conflating “RS” runout initials with Roger Seibel before his documented QCA tenure began in 1979.
  • The specific label at which Seibel worked in Phoenix between QCA and founding SAE Mastering: Discogs profile lists label ID 367883 (Wakefield Manufacturing), but Wakefield is primarily documented as a pressing plant, not a mastering studio — the exact nature of his role there (in-house mastering, lacquer cutting) is unclear without web sources.
