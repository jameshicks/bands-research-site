Zander Schloss

Bassist, musician, actor; Los Angeles. Discogs artist ID: 321764. Full name: Alexander Schloss. Credited variously as “Zander ‘Snake’ Schloss”.

Bassist of Circle Jerks from 1984 (first studio appearance: Wönderful, 1985) through 1990, 1994–1995, 2001–2011, and 2019–present. Also associated with Joe Strummer & The Latino Rockabilly War, The Weirdos, Thelonious Monster, Die Hunns, The Magnificent Bastards, Sean Wheeler & Zander Schloss, The Low & Sweet Orchestra, and others.
