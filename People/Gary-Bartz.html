Gary Bartz

American jazz alto saxophonist and flutist, born September 26, 1940, Baltimore, Maryland. Discogs artist ID: 45008.

Active from the early 1960s onward. Appeared on McCoy Tyner’s Blue Note albums Expansions (1969, alto sax and wooden flute) and Extensions (1972, alto sax). Also worked with Miles Davis (on Jack Johnson and related sessions), Max Roach, and Art Blakey. Led his own NTU Troop group in the 1970s.
