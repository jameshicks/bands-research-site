Alan Davidson (4)

Discogs IDs: Alan Davidson (4) = 3002144; Kitchen Cynics = 284480
Real name: Alan Davidson
Also known as: Kitchen Cynics, Alan Cynic, Dangerlamb?
Origin: Aberdeen, Scotland
Active: 1988–present

Solo multi-instrumentalist and songwriter working primarily as Kitchen Cynics — a lo-fi, home-recorded project spanning folk, drone, experimental pop, and neo-psychedelia. Davidson both performs and self-releases through his own imprint Les Enfants Du Paradiddle, one of the most prolific micro-labels in the UK cassette and CDr underground. His regular collaborators include violinist Beth Anderson, cellist Duncan Hart, and vocalist/keyboardist Susan Matthew.

Overview

Davidson began recording under the Kitchen Cynics name in Aberdeen in the late 1980s, releasing a stream of home-recorded cassettes on his own Les Enfants Du Paradiddle imprint as well as a handful of early vinyl releases on German tape-and-vinyl labels. The project’s aesthetic — intimate acoustic instruments, plainspoken melodic songwriting, occasional drone and electronics — remained broadly consistent for over thirty-five years, while Davidson accumulated an unusually large catalogue of collaborations, split releases, and guest appearances.

He runs Les Enfants Du Paradiddle (Discogs label ID 115736) as a vehicle for his own releases and those of close collaborators — 135+ releases on Discogs as of 2025.

He also performs as part of several group projects: the improv quartet Matricarians (with David Mackay (2), Duncan Hart, and Susan Matthew); Kitchen Cynics Orchestra (an orchestral/ensemble extension of the solo project); Kitchen Cynics Drone Trio; and the duo Barrett’s Dottled Beauty with Gayle Brogan (also known as Pefkin and Electroscope).
