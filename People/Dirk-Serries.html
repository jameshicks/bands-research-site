Oct 04, 20269 min read

Dirk Serries

Belgian guitarist and experimental musician, born in Belgium. Discogs artist ID: 605817. Working since the early 1980s across industrial tape music, ambient drone, and free improvisation, primarily under the aliases Vidna Obmana (1984–2005) and Fear Falls Burning (2005–2012). Since the early 2010s has worked predominantly as Dirk Serries, focusing on electric guitar drone, free jazz, and improvised music. Runs the A New Wave Of Jazz label.

Biography

Early Years: Industrial Tape Scene (1984–1991)

Serries began in the early-1980s Belgian DIY industrial and tape-music scene under the Vidna Obmana alias. His own label, Mechanical Orchestration Music, released several early cassette works including The Face That Must Die (1987) and Deathchamber - Trancedreamed (1988). The first recordings were harsh and industrial in character; the sound shifted gradually toward tribal primitivism and early ambient. US label Ladd-Frith picked up split releases in 1986–87, and in 1988–89 he appeared on the German ZNS Tapes label and the Belgian Death Pact Mailmusic imprint.

The transition out of pure industrialism was underway by 1989–1990, when releases like Compositions: Depression & Ideal (PBK Recordings, Canada) and Revealed By Composed Nature (Decade Collection) marked a move toward sustained atmospheric textures.

Vidna Obmana: Ambient and New Age (1991–2005)

Through the 1990s Vidna Obmana became a fixture of the American ambient/New Age underground, primarily through the Projekt label (Sam Rosenthal, proprietor). Key releases there include Well Of Souls (1995), The River Of Appearance (1996), Cavern Of Sirens (1997), and Crossing The Trail (1998). The relationship with Projekt ran through at least 2004 and extended to reissues and archive releases through the 2010s and 2020s. His own Decade Collection imprint handled much of the earlier Belgian output.

A parallel series of Steve Roach & Vidna Obmana collaborations emerged from this period; Steve Roach and Serries recorded together extensively, with archival live documents still surfacing in the 2020s. The ambient electronic label Hypnos released three solo Vidna Obmana records: Landscape In Obscurity (1999), The Contemporary Nocturne (2000), and Soundtrack For The Aquarium (2001).

Other labels from the Vidna Obmana period: Multimood Records (Sweden), Klanggalerie (Austria), Musica Maxima Magnetica (Italy), Extreme (UK/US), and N D (US). The Vidna Obmana project was formally wound down in 2005, though Serries has since revisited the alias for archival and self-released work.

Serries also collaborated with Asmus Tietchens under the Vidna Obmana name, documented on the split Asmus Tietchens. Vidna Obmana. (Syrenia, Italy, 1995).

Continuum (2005–2016)

Around the time Vidna Obmana wound down, Serries formed the ambient duo Continuum (2) with Steven Wilson (of Porcupine Tree / Bass Communion). The project produced two studio albums — Continuum (Soleilmoon, 2005) and Continuum 2 (Soleilmoon, 2007) — and two parallel “Recyclings” vinyl releases on Tonefloat in which the source material was reworked by external collaborators (Serries himself under Vidna Obmana for Vol. 1; Justin K Broadrick for Vol. 2). Anthology and box-set releases on Tonefloat followed through 2016.

Fear Falls Burning: Guitar Drone (2005–2012)

The Fear Falls Burning project launched in 2005 simultaneously with the retirement of Vidna Obmana, focusing on minimal electric guitar drone and noise. Serries released twelve albums under this name. Key labels include Tonefloat (the primary home), Conspiracy Records (2) (Belgium), Projekt, Auf Abwegen (Germany), Soleilmoon Recordings, and Die Stadt (Germany). Notable collaborations during this period:

  • Fear Falls Burning & Nadja (Conspiracy Records, 2007) — with the Canadian drone metal duo Nadja
  • Fear Falls Burning & Birchville Cat Motel (Conspiracy Records, 2007) — with the New Zealand noise artist Birchville Cat Motel
  • Final + Fear Falls Burning (Conspiracy Records, 2009) — with the French post-industrial project Final (Justin Broadrick)

The Fear Falls Burning project was closed in 2012, though self-released archival material appeared under the alias as late as 2020.

Microphonics and Transition (2008–2014)

Running concurrently with Fear Falls Burning was the Microphonics project, a more ambient guitar-focused practice. The Microphonics series was extensively documented by Tonefloat, running from Microphonics I-V (2008) through at least Microphonics XXVI-XXX Resolution Heart (2016), with individual volumes also issued on Tonefloat’s Ikon sublabel. Martina Verhoeven (violin, viola) appears as the most consistent collaborator across Microphonics releases, with 88 shared releases in the database. Ronald Mariën (as Stratosphere (2)) also appears frequently in this period.

Mastering work is a thread throughout — Serries regularly mastered his own releases and those of collaborators, particularly on Tonefloat.

Low Volume Music (Projekt, 2012) and In A Place Of Mutual Understanding (Projekt, 2013) represent Serries’s final sustained engagement with the Projekt ambient aesthetic before his direction shifted to jazz and improvisation. Steve Roach appears on Low Volume Music.

Free Jazz and Improvisation (2014–present)

By 2014–2015 Serries’s activity shifted markedly toward free jazz and free improvisation. He founded A New Wave Of Jazz (Discogs label ID: 701953), which has released work by YODOK III, Kodian Trio, Dikeman Lisle Serries Webster Quartet, Fantoom (2), and other improvisation-based groups he is part of.

Key ensemble projects in this phase:

  • YODOK III — free jazz trio active from at least 2014; released on A New Wave Of Jazz and Tonefloat
  • Kodian Trio — improvisation trio (Discogs ID: 4952011)
  • Dikeman Lisle Serries Webster Quartet — quartet with John Dikeman (saxophone), Andrew Lisle (drums), and Colin Webster (saxophone); also extended to quintet with Dikeman Lisle Serries Webster Wilkinson Quintet
  • Serries Verhoeven Webster Trio — with Martina Verhoeven and Colin Webster
  • Fantoom (2) — released Sluimer (A New Wave Of Jazz, 2015)
  • Ratbau — improvisation group
  • Scatterwound — (Discogs ID: 5546553)
  • Yodok III — (disambiguation: also listed as Yodok III, ID: 3871409)
  • The Eightfold Model — (Discogs ID: 1284661)

Alongside the ensemble work, Serries continued solo electric guitar releases under his own name, with Consouling Sounds (Belgium) issuing several key records: Unseen Descending And Lamentations (2015), Buoyant (2015), Epitaph (2018), This Earth We Walk Upon (2019), A Dreamer Ascends (2020).

The UK label Raw Tonk (Colin Webster’s imprint) became another consistent home for improvised work, including Etched Above The Bow Grip (2016), Night Realms (with Marlon Wolterink, René Aquarius, Lewis Mclean), Day Realms (2017), Outermission (2017), Garland In Grey (Playneutral, 2022).

German label Midira Records hosted multiple releases including Enomeni (2016), Hell (2019), Distant Curving Horizon… (2018), Fluctuation Of Being (2023). Italian label 13 (3) released soundtrack and improvisation work including The Devastation Chant (2015), The Disintegration Of Silence (2023), Defiance Of Self (2024).

Other recent labels: Glacial Movements Records (The Storm Of Silence, 2016; Black Frost, 2020); Denovali Records (Kehr, 2015); Confront (Vectorial Spaces, 2024; Dual Activity, 2025); Cloudchamber Recordings (Nocturnal Discord, 2023; At Future Dawn, 2024).

The Streams Of Consciousness series — individual numbered live recordings of improvised electric guitar sets — has run continuously since 2013 and into 2026, self-released on his own imprint.
