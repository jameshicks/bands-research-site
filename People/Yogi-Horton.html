Yogi Horton

Born Lawrence Yogi Horton, 1954; died 8 June 1987 (fell from 17th-floor hotel window, New York City). American drummer and one of the most in-demand session players in New York in the early 1980s. Member of Kid Creole And The Coconuts and Chew. Discogs ID: 238357.

Name variations: Y. Horton · Yogi · Yogi “Mr. Hock” Horton · Yogi (Mr. Hock) Horton · Yogi Hortan.

Overview

Horton was the go-to drummer for a remarkably wide range of NYC sessions spanning R&B, pop, new wave, avant-garde, dance, and Broadway cast recordings. In the early 1980s he played on hundreds of TV commercials as well as LPs by Diana Ross, Luther Vandross, Aretha Franklin, Gladys Knight, Dionne Warwick, Grover Washington Jr., Ashford & Simpson, Cheryl Lynn, and the Alessi Brothers — a core R&B/soul session circuit. His range extended well beyond that lane: he is the drummer on the B-52s’ Mesopotamia EP (produced by David Byrne), David Byrne’s Songs From The Broadway Production Of “The Catherine Wheel”, Jean-Michel Jarre’s Zoolook, the John Lennon/Yoko Ono posthumous LP Milk And Honey, Jerry Harrison’s Casual Gods, and Material’s One Down. He had been working since the early 1970s (sessions for All Platinum, Vibration, and Turbo Records) before becoming a fixture of the NYC studio scene.

His death at 33 ended a career still at its peak — the breadth of his last few years of credits (1984–1987) is remarkable.

Catherine Wheel Session (1981)

On Songs From The Broadway Production Of “The Catherine Wheel” (Sire, 1981), Horton played drums on A1–A4, B1, and B5. David Byrne produced and performed bass, guitar, vocals, and synthesizer. Choreography by Twyla Tharp. Engineer: Butch Jones. Mastered by Greg Calbi.

Mesopotamia Session (1982)

B-52s EP produced by David Byrne. Horton played full drums. Other performers: Ricky Wilson (guitar/keys/bass), Keith Strickland (keys/bass/drums/other), Kate Pierson, Fred Schneider, Cindy Wilson (vocals), Steve Scales (percussion), Ralph Carney (saxophone), Robert Aaron (saxophone). Engineer: Butch Jones.

Milk And Honey Session (1984)

Drums alongside Allan Schwartzberg and Andy Newmark. Bass: Neil Jason, Tony Levin, Wayne Pedzwater. Guitar: Earl Slick, Hugh McCracken, John Lennon, John Tropea. Engineers: Michael Barbiero, Jon Smith (3).

Zoolook Session (1984)

Jean-Michel Jarre’s landmark electronic/world music LP. Horton on drums alongside Marcus Miller (bass), Adrian Belew (guitar/effects), Frédérick Rousseau (keyboards). Mixed by David Lord.

Jerry Harrison: Casual Gods (1988)

Horton drummed on track 3. Other drummers: Rick Jaeger (most tracks), David Van Tieghem (track 2). Backing vocals on track 6: Arthur Russell. Songwriting credits on track 6 include Arthur Russell. Bernie Worrell on keyboards.
