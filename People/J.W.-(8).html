J.W. (8)

Discogs ID: 1514089 Real name: Jonathan Wilkinson Also known as: JW, JW (8) Based: Leeds, UK

Jonathan Wilkinson is a Leeds-based musician, best known as a member of psychedelic rock band Hookworms (all five studio releases, 2011–2018) and its successor collective Holodrum (formed 2022). He is credited on Discogs under the name JW (initials-only convention used throughout the Hookworms era) and as J.W. (8) / JW (8).

Biography

Hookworms (2011–2018)

Wilkinson was a founding member of Hookworms, the Leeds indie/psychedelic rock band that operated under a strict initials-only credit convention — he appeared as “JW” on all Hookworms records. The band formed around 2011, with frontman and studio operator Matthew Johnson (12) (MJ) at the centre. Other members at founding included Matthew Benn (MB, bass), EG (5) (Discogs ID 3594353), and SS (7) / Sam Shipstone (Discogs ID 3594352).

Hookworms debuted on the US label Sun Ark Records with a self-titled cassette (SA014, 2011). Wilkinson appeared as Performer. Their debut LP Pearl Mystic (2013, Gringo Records (2)) continued with the same lineup, with MJ recording at his Suburban Home studio in Kirkstall, Leeds.

By The Hum (2014, Weird World), the lineup shifted: EG (5) had departed, and Jonathan Nash (2) (JN) joined as a full member. Wilkinson’s specific role was listed as Performer throughout. The band continued with this core four-piece (MJ, MB, JW, JN) plus SS (7).

Microshift (2018, Domino Recording Company) was Hookworms’ final record and most explicitly credited album. Wilkinson is listed as Musician. The Microshift Remixes release (also 2018) credits him specifically as Guitar — the clearest statement of his instrumental role across the entire Hookworms discography.

Hookworms disbanded on 31 October 2018 following public allegations of sexual and mental abuse against frontman MJ.

Holodrum (2022–)

After the Hookworms dissolution, Wilkinson re-emerged as a member of Holodrum, a seven-piece Leeds collective formed around 2022 with overlapping Hookworms personnel. For the self-titled debut LP (2022, Gringo Records (2)), he is credited as Written-By, Performer — the first time he received a writing credit on a Discogs-documented release. The album was recorded and mixed by Matthew Benn, with mastering by Robert Weston.

Holodrum’s membership expanded beyond the Hookworms core to include Christopher Duffin (saxophone, previously a guest on Microshift), Emily Garner (Discogs ID 6388336), and Steve Nuttall (Discogs ID 3433929).
