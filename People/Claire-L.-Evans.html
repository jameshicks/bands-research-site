Overview

Claire L. Evans is a vocalist, songwriter, and programmer active since 2002, best known as one half of the synth-pop and dance-pop duo Yacht alongside Jona Bechtolt — a partnership reflected in 30 shared releases across three decades. She is also a member of The World Court. The bulk of her output appeared on DFA Records between 2008 and 2019, with her catalogue spanning dance-pop, indie rock, and experimental territory; she returned with new work as recently as 2024 on Paris By Night.
