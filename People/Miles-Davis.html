Miles Davis

American trumpeter and composer, born Alton, Illinois, May 26, 1926; died September 28, 1991. Discogs artist ID: 23755.

One of the most influential figures in jazz history. Led successive groundbreaking groups across bebop, cool jazz, modal jazz, jazz fusion, and 1980s electric funk. His final electric band (c. 1985–1991) included Kenny Garrett on saxophone.

Key Periods

  • 1940s–50s: Birth of cool; collaborations with Gil Evans; recorded prolifically for Prestige 1951–57 (903 releases in Discogs catalog) — including the four classic marathon Van Gelder sessions in 1956 (Cookin’, Relaxin’, Workin’, Steamin’) — before moving to Columbia
  • 1960s: Second great quintet (Herbie Hancock, Wayne Shorter, Ron Carter, Tony Williams); Kind of Blue (1959); Sam Rivers briefly toured as tenor saxophonist on the 1964 Japan tour (replacing George Coleman, before Wayne Shorter joined permanently); transition to electric with Dave Holland (replacing Ron Carter) and In A Silent Way (1969), Bitches Brew (1970)
  • 1970s: Heavy electric fusion; On the Corner; health issues
  • 1980s comeback: Albums Decoy (1984), You’re Under Arrest (1985), Tutu (1986), Amandla (1989); band included Kenny Garrett, Marcus Miller, Ricky Wellman, Foley
  • Died 1991

Key Release (with Kenny Garrett)

  • Amandla (Warner Bros., 1989) — Kenny Garrett plays alto and soprano saxophone; produced by Marcus Miller
