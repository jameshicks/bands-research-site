Kenny Dorham

Aug 07, 20263 min read

Kenny Dorham

Discogs ID: 254945
Origin: USA (Texas)
Active: 1940s–1972

American trumpeter and composer. Member of the original hard bop quintet alongside Horace Silver and Art Blakey, recorded on Horace Silver And The Jazz Messengers (Blue Note BLP 1518, 1956). Also a leader in his own right for Blue Note. 88 shared Discogs credits with Silver.
