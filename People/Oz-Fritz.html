Oz Fritz

Full name: Oswald George Fritz III
Discogs ID: 196031
Origin: USA
Role: Recording / mixing engineer, producer, live sound engineer

Recording and mixing engineer whose career spans roughly 1987 to the present. Best known as the in-house engineer for Bill Laswell’s production universe — Material, Axiom Records, and the wider Laswell circle — but with a second major thread as the primary recording/mixing engineer for Tom Waits on Mule Variations, Alice, and Blood Money.

Operates under the name High Velocity Sound Engineering (Discogs label ID: 478430). Primary studios in the formative years were Platinum Island Studios (Discogs: 274727) and Greenpoint Studios (Discogs: 277133), both core Laswell-affiliated rooms in New York.

Career Arc

1987–1989 — Apprentice at Platinum Island Studios

Fritz entered the profession as an assistant engineer at Platinum Island Studios, New York. Credits from this period consistently list him as “assistant” or “assistant at Platinum Island.” The studio was a hub for Laswell productions and mainstream sessions alike, and Fritz worked across both:

  • Greed (Material / Scritti Politti adjacent, Virgin, 1988) — assistant engineer
  • Seven Souls (Material, Virgin, 1989) — assistant engineer at Platinum Island alongside Robert Musso
  • Brain Drain (Ramones, Sire, 1989) — assistant engineer at Platinum Island
  • Strange Angels (Laurie Anderson, Warner Bros., 1989) — assistant engineer
  • Before We Were Born (Bill Frisell, Elektra Musician, 1989) — assistant engineer
  • The Burning World (Swans, UNI Records, 1989) — assistant engineer

The Seven Souls session is significant: it placed him in the same room as Martin Bisi (BC Studio engineer), Nicky Skopelitis, Sly Dunbar, William S. Burroughs, and the core Laswell team for the first time.

1990–1993 — Full Engineer, Axiom Records Core Team

By 1990 Fritz had graduated to lead engineer on Laswell productions and became the primary engineer for the newly founded Axiom Records imprint (Island Records). He credited under “High Velocity” on several releases. Key work:

  • Middle Passage (Bootsy Collins, Axiom, 1990) — engineer
  • New World Power (Nicky Skopelitis, Axiom, 1990) — engineer
  • Red Warrior (Axiom, 1990) — assistant engineer
  • The Music of Mohamed Abdel Wahab (Axiom, 1990) — engineer
  • Wrong Way Up (Brian Eno & John Cale, Opal/Warner, 1990) — engineer
  • Funk Of Ages (Axiom, Gramavision, 1990) — engineer

Ask The Ages (Sonny Sharrock, Axiom, 1991) — recorded by Fritz alongside Robert Musso and Jason Corsaro; produced by Laswell and Sharrock; personnel included Pharoah Sanders, Elvin Jones, Charnett Moffett. One of the most critically praised Axiom releases.

  • The Third Power (Axiom, 1991) — engineer
  • The Word (Axiom/Mango, 1991) — engineer
  • Ekstasis (Nicky Skopelitis, Axiom, 1993) — engineer/mix
  • Be Bop Or Be Dead (Axiom, 1993) — engineer/mix
  • Lord Of The Harvest (Rykodisc, 1993) — engineer
  • Too Much Sugar For A Dime (Axiom, 1993) — engineer
  • Reality (Axiom, 1993) — recorded by

Apocalypse Across The Sky (Master Musicians of Jajouka / Bachir Attar, Axiom, 1992) — engineer; session produced by Laswell with Skopelitis coordinating the Morocco field work; liner notes by William S. Burroughs and Brion Gysin.

Transmutation (Mutatis Mutandis) (Praxis, Axiom, 1992) — engineer credited as “Engineering: For High Velocity”; personnel included Buckethead, Bootsy Collins, Bernie Worrell, Bryan Mantia.

1992–1995 — Greenpoint Studios, Expanded Range

In parallel with Axiom work, Fritz shifted base to Greenpoint Studios (Brooklyn), another Laswell affiliate, expanding into death metal and experimental releases:

  • Guts Of A Virgin / Buried Secrets (Autopsy? — Earache, 1991–92) — recorded/mixed by
  • I’ll Strangle You (Crammed, 1992) — engineer at Greenpoint Studio
  • Ritual Beating System (Axiom, 1992) — recorded/mixed by
  • The Dark Fire (Axiom, 1992) — engineer
  • Sacrifist (Subharmonic, 1993) — engineer
  • Execution Ground (Subharmonic, 1994) — engineer

Hallucination Engine (Material, Axiom/Island, 1994) — recording and mixing engineer (with Robert Musso and Martin Bisi); personnel included Bootsy Collins, Bernie Worrell, Sly Dunbar, Jonas Hellborg, Wayne Shorter, Zakir Hussain, Trilok Gurtu, William S. Burroughs, Shankar, Simon Shaheen. One of the most densely-credited Axiom productions.

  • Giant Robot (Sony, 1994) — engineer/mix
  • Hallucination Engine above
  • Funkcronomicon (Axiom, 1995) — engineer

1996–2002 — Outside Laswell Circle Appears; Tom Waits

The mid-to-late 1990s brought Fritz’s biggest commercial credits and the first major work independent of Laswell:

  • Dreams Of Freedom (Ambient Translations Of Bob Marley In Dub) (Axiom/Island, 1997) — engineer
  • Arc Of The Testimony (Axiom, 1997) — additional engineering
  • Altered Beats (Axiom/Island, 1996) — engineer

Mule Variations (Tom Waits, Anti-, 1999) — recorded by and mixed by (with Jacquire King); produced by Tom Waits and Kathleen Brennan. Grammy-winning album; Fritz is a primary credited engineer on all tracks. One of his highest-profile non-Laswell credits.

  • Antipop (Primus, Interscope, 1999) — recorded by; session included Tom Morello

Alice (Tom Waits, Anti-, 2002) — recorded by and mixed by
Blood Money (Tom Waits, Anti-, 2002) — recorded by and mixed by
These two Tom Waits albums, both released simultaneously in 2002, cement Fritz as Waits’s go-to engineer for the Anti- era.

  • Wicked Grin (John Hammond, Pointblank/Virgin, 2001) — engineered and mixed by
  • The Grand Pecking Order (Oysterhead, Elektra, 2001) — recorded by [NB: previously misattributed to “Ozric Tentacles” in this file — confirmed as Oysterhead via Discogs release 501070]

2003–2010 — Continued Freelance; Live Sound; Smaller Labels

Fritz diversified into live sound, world music, and smaller independent sessions:

  • Orphans: Brawlers, Bawlers & Bastards (Tom Waits, Anti-, 2006) — “other engineers”
  • Meltdown (John Zorn, Tzadik, 2001) — concert sound and DRT recording
  • Live In San Francisco At Stern Grove (Tom Waits, Palm Pictures, 2002) — engineer
  • All Around The World (Sub Rosa, 2004) — producer, engineer, arranger, photographer; one of only two primary-credited releases on Discogs

2011–present — Bill Laswell Self-Release Era

Fritz remained active in Laswell’s late-career self-release period, handling full recording/editing/mixing/mastering duties on a series of concert and studio documents released on Laswell’s own label:

  • MOD Berlin Part 1 & 2 (Not On Label / Laswell self-released, 2020)
  • Milan (2020), Frankfurt (2021), Live In San Francisco (2021), Jazz Middelheim (2022), Maura Maura (2024), Troubadour (2024)

He also continued independent mixing and mastering for various artists through the 2010s–2020s (Ilo Veyou, Screamadelica live, Twin Solitude, etc.).
