Daniel Klingen

Musician, drummer, recording engineer. Discogs ID: 568678 Name variations: D, DK, Danny “Diamond” Klingen.

Member of Subterfuge (5) (drums, percussion) and Soap (3). Also a member of Klee (German indie pop), Blackpool (3), and Berend.

Played drums on Subterfuge (5)‘s I Do Birds (2001) and The Legendary Eifel Tapes (2005). Produced, recorded, and mixed the Soap (3) side of the 1997 split 7” (Subterfuge / Soap, Blurr Records).

Active as a session drummer, producer, and recording engineer across a wide range of German indie and rock releases through the 2020s. Selected credits include:

  • Soll Die Zeit Doch Vergehen (2017, Grand Hotel Van Cleef) — drums
