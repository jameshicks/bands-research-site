Ill-Studio

Paris-based creative studio specialising in art direction, graphic design, and photography. Active on Discogs from 2009 onwards. Discogs artist ID: 1530716. URL: http://www.ill-studio.com/ Also credited as “Ill Studio” (name variation).

Overview

Ill-Studio began their recorded-music work around 2009 as the house design collective for Institubes and its electronic sublabel Sound Pellegrino, handling artwork for a wide roster of French and international dance and club acts. Their early credits cover singles and EPs for artists including Para One, Surkin, Bok Bok, Todd Edwards, L-Vis 1990, Zombie Disco Squad, Harvard Bass, and Joakim, among others — essentially the full Sound Pellegrino catalogue from 2009 through around 2014.

From 2015 onwards, Ill-Studio shifted their primary relationship to Deewee, the label and studio run by David and Stephen Dewaele (Soulwax). They have served as Creative Directors on every major Deewee release from 2015 to the present, covering art direction, photography, and design. Their Deewee work spans Soulwax’s own albums and the full Deewee artist roster — including Charlotte Adigéry, Charlotte Adigéry & Bolis Pupul, Asa Moto, Phillipi & Rodrigo, Bolis Pupul, and Klanken. They also contributed photography alongside Younes Klouche on From Deewee and Transient Program For Drums And Machinery.
