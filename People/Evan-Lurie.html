Evan Lurie

Discogs ID: 257371
Born: 28 September 1954, Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA
Role: Pianist, composer, keyboardist

American composer and musician. Co-founder (with his brother John Lurie) of The Lounge Lizards in 1978. Alongside his Lounge Lizards work, maintained a parallel career as a solo composer and film-score writer, developing a personal style rooted in tango, neo-classical piano, and downtown New York jazz. Name variations: E. Lurie, Evan, Lurie.

The Lounge Lizards (1978–1998)

Evan co-founded The Lounge Lizards with John in 1978 in New York City. The group was an eclectic ensemble whose lineup shifted around the Lurie brothers as the only constant members, drawing from the wider New York avant-garde and No Wave scene. Evan played keyboards throughout, and wrote a small number of compositions for the group.

The Lounge Lizards (1981, r604647)

Debut LP. Produced and recorded by Teo Macero (Columbia Records staff producer, long associated with Miles Davis). Design by Peter Saville (2). Personnel: John Lurie (saxophone), Arto Lindsay (guitar), Steve Piccolo (bass), Anton Fier (drums), Evan Lurie (keyboards). Evan co-wrote “Conquest Of Rar” with John Lurie and Anton Fier. Released on Editions EG (EGS 108). Genres: Avant-garde Jazz, No Wave.

Stranger Than Paradise / The Resurrection Of Albert Ayler (1985, r604646)

Two Jim Jarmusch film soundtracks on one LP. John Lurie primary artist; Evan credited as “Orchestrated By.” Side A (Stranger Than Paradise) performed by The Paradise Quartet. Side B (Resurrection Of Albert Ayler) features John Lurie, Arto Lindsay, Dougie Bowne, Curtis Fowlkes, Jill Jaffe, E.J. Rodriguez. Released on Crammed Discs / Attitude (MTM 7 LP).

Big Heart (1986, r927824)

Lounge Lizards LP on Island Records (IMA 20). Evan contributed piano and wrote “Punch And Judy Tango” (B2, 12:55). Marc Ribot on guitar; Seigen Ono mixed and produced alongside John Lurie. Also features Curtis Fowlkes (trombone), Erik Sanko (bass), Dougie Bowne (drums), Roy Nathanson (saxophones).

Solo and Film Score Career

Evan maintained a consistent solo output from 1985, largely centred on tango-influenced chamber music for small ensembles. His core collaborators were Marc Ribot (guitar), Jill Jaffe (violin/viola), Alfredo Pedernera (bandoneon), Tony Garnier (bass), and Seigen Ono (production/recording).

Happy? Here? Now? (1985, r2444220)

Debut solo LP on Les Disques du Crépuscule (TWI 574). All compositions by Evan Lurie. Tracks include “Tesla’s Pigeons,” “Three Bills,” “Inevitable (Tango),” “P (For Harvey),” “Brazen Or Cavalier” (13:28), “Suite From Punch.” Sleeve artwork by David Wojnarowicz (backcover) and Susan Pitt (frontcover). Genres: Free Improvisation, Tango.

No band personnel listed in Discogs credits — likely a solo piano recording or small group without credited personnel.

Pieces For Bandoneon (1987, r2917044)

Original soundtrack LP, released in Japan (Japan Record, 28-JAL-3102). Composed, produced, and performed (piano) by Evan Lurie. Co-produced and recorded by Seigen Ono. Personnel: Marc Ribot (guitar), Alfredo Pedernera (bandoneon), Tony Garnier (bass), Jill Jaffe (violin). Genres: Latin, Tango, Soundtrack.

Track titles include “III Determination - For Rosa Parks,” “I Inevitable,” “VI The Colour Of The Sky,” “II Travel,” “IV The Punch And Judy Tango,” “VII Intention.”

Il Piccolo Diavolo (1988, r4061785)

Film score for Roberto Benigni’s Il Piccolo Diavolo (1988, Italy). Released on Cinevox Record (MDF 33.188). Evan Lurie: piano, composed by, arranged by. Personnel: Marc Ribot (guitar), Alfredo Pedernena (bandoneon), Enzo Pietropaoli (bass), Jill Jaffe (violin), Tom Lazarus (engineer). Genres: Soundtrack. Roberto Benigni credited as Film Director.

Selling Water By The Side Of The River (1990, r1669365)

LP on Antilles Records (AN 8754). Core ensemble of regular collaborators: Evan Lurie (piano), Marc Ribot (guitar), Alfredo Pedernera (bandoneon), John Beal (bass), Jill Jaffe (violin, viola). Mastered by Bob Ludwig. Genres: Classical, Jazz, Latin; Styles: Contemporary, Latin Jazz, Neo-Classical, Tango. Tracks include “The Spinster’s Waltz,” “Those Monkeys Weren’t Typing,” “Rintrah Roars,” “Ballad Of A New York Peasant,” “Alfredo’s Response.”

Joe Gould’s Secret (2000, r9819018)

Film score for Stanley Tucci’s Joe Gould’s Secret (2000). Released BMG / RCA Victor (09026 63602 2). Evan Lurie credited as Score. Producer: Susan Jacobs. The album is a mix of Evan’s original cues and period jazz recordings (Count Basie Orchestra, Coleman Hawkins, Charlie Parker / Woody Herman, Lester Young / Nat King Cole, Louis Armstrong, Perry Como, Don Byas), plus a dialogue track with Ian Holm and Stanley Tucci.
