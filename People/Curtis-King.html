Overview

Curtis King Jr. (born 11 May 1954) is an American session singer, songwriter, and arranger with over 1,400 Discogs credits stretching from 1979 to the present. Best known as a backing vocalist, he has worked with a wide range of major artists including Billy Joel, David Bowie, Madonna, Céline Dion, Meat Loaf, and Randy Crawford, placing him at the centre of the New York studio world across multiple decades. His top collaborators by shared credits — Brenda White King, Nile Rodgers, Fonzi Thornton, Cindy Mizelle, and Bernard Edwards — confirm deep involvement in the Chic studio orbit. He also appears as a member of The Sessions Band.
