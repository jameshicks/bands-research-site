Patricia Morrison

Discogs ID: 155571 (as Patricia Morrison) / 4272225 (as Pat Bag)
Real name: Patricia Anne Rainone (later Patricia Vanian)
Born: January 14, 1962, Los Angeles, California
Aliases: Pat Bag, Patricia Rainone, Patricia Vanian

American bassist, vocalist, and songwriter. Co-founded The Bags in 1977 as one of LA’s first-generation punk bands, passed through Legal Weapon and The Gun Club in the early 1980s, formed Fur Bible in London with Kid Congo Powers in 1985, briefly appeared on The Sisterhood’s Gift (1986), and joined The Damned from roughly 1986 — eventually marrying the band’s singer Dave Vanian. Released one solo album in 1994. A rare figure who connects the LA first-wave punk scene directly to the British post-punk underground.

History

The Bags (1977–c.1980)

Co-founded The Bags in Los Angeles with Alice Armendariz (Alice Bag) in 1977. The first lineup: Alice on vocals, Pat on bass, Janet Koontz and Geza X on guitars, Joe Nanini on drums. Also passing through on drums early on: Nicky Beat, Don Bonebrake, and Don Bolles (later of the Germs). First show at The Masque on September 10, 1977.

The lineup that recorded was Alice and Pat with Craig Lee and Rob Ritter on guitars and Terry Graham on drums. Released one single, “Survive” (1978/79, Dangerhouse Records) — The Bags’ only studio release. Pat left the band shortly after. In 1980 the remaining group (without her) was filmed by Penelope Spheeris for The Decline of Western Civilization; the film credits them as Alice Bag Band to avoid conflict with ex-member Pat.

After The Bags dissolved: Terry Graham and Rob Ritter joined The Gun Club; Craig Lee died in 1991 (AIDS); Rob Ritter (later Rob Graves) formed 45 Grave and died in 1991 of a heroin overdose.

Legal Weapon (1980–1982)

After leaving The Bags, Morrison joined Legal Weapon, an LA punk band fronted by vocalist Kat Arthur. Credited as “Patericia” on the band’s No Sorrow EP (1981, Arsenal Records LW-68B) — bass and vocals. Photographer Ed Colver shot the record.

The Gun Club (1983–1985)

Joined The Gun Club in time for the Las Vegas Story sessions — reuniting with Terry Graham. On The Las Vegas Story (1984, Animal Records / Chrysalis 206 445 — 32 pressings) she is credited for “Bass, Backing Vocals, Maracas, Other [Bacardi].” A live album Love Supreme (1985, Offence Records OFFENCE 9002) documents a transitional lineup with Morrison alongside Ward Dotson, Graham, and Pierce. A second live album, Danse Kalinda Boom - Live In Pandora’s Box (1985, Megadisc MD 7979), features the Las Vegas Story touring lineup (Pierce, Kid Congo, Morrison on bass, drummer uncredited). A French bootleg/unofficial release, Destroy The Country (1984, Not Just Jazz Records), documents the same lineup. Morrison is also credited on the Death Party reissue (2004, Sympathy For The Record Industry).

Fur Bible (1985)

After the Gun Club disbanded in its Las Vegas Story configuration, Morrison and Kid Congo Powers (Brian Tristan) formed Fur Bible in London — Discogs describes it as “a short-lived gothabilly band.” The core lineup: Kid Congo (vocals, guitar), Morrison (bass), Murray Mitchell (guitar), and a rotating drummer. Producer: Clint Ruin (JG Thirlwell). Two releases on New Rose Records:

  • Fur Bible 12” EP (1985, NEW 61) — “Plunder The Tombs” / “Headbolt” / “Fumble Fist”
  • Plunder The Tombs 7” (1985, NEW 63)

Both credited Morrison as bassist and co-writer; Kid Congo wrote lyrics.

The Sisterhood / mid-1980s (1985–1986)

In 1986, Morrison appeared (uncredited) as vocalist on “Jihad,” the opening track of The Sisterhood’s Gift LP (Merciful Release SIS 020). The Sisterhood was a side project by Andrew Eldritch (Sisters of Mercy) assembled primarily to block former bandmates Wayne Hussey and Craig Adams from using the Sisters of Mercy name. The record featured an eclectic cast: Alan Vega (Suicide), James Ray, Lucas Fox, and Morrison — all performing uncredited. She is also listed as an interviewee on a 1989 Sisters of Mercy interview disc (Not On Label, SIS 020-era).

Also in 1986, played bass on Silver Missiles And Nightingales (PVC Records PVC 8950) by The Suicide Twins — a glam/acoustic project by Andy McCoy of Hanoi Rocks.

The Damned (1986–present)

Joined The Damned (Discogs ID 152965) around 1986 and has been the band’s primary bassist for most of their activity since. Married singer Dave Vanian. Key studio credit: Grave Disorder (2001, Nitro Records 15844-2) — bass guitar, backing vocals, and co-wrote “Lookin For Action.” Lineup on that record: Dave Vanian (vocals), Captain Sensible (guitar), Andrew Pinching (drums), Monty Oxy Moron (keyboards), Morrison (bass). Produced by David Bianco. Also documented on several live releases: Danse Kalinda Boom (1985 — pre-Damned, Gun Club), Live At The Hacienda (1994), and the Cherry Red/Culture Factory archival series Live At The Hacienda ‘83 and Live At The Hacienda ‘84 (both 2022).

Solo work (1994)

Released one solo album, Reflect On This (1994, Rebel Rec. / SPV, CD 076-45632), in Germany. Goth Rock. Co-produced with Julian Beeston and Kenny Jones (3). Musicians: Jim Daley (bass), Franco Rogantin (drums), Andy Russell (3) (guitar). Morrison wrote all tracks and played bass on two of them (“The Living End,” “Reflection”).

Later appearances

Voice readings (prologue and epilogue) on Sanguivore II: Mistress Of Death (2025, Spinefarm Records) — a metal release with 4 pressings.
