Overview

Mark Fell is a Rotherham-based English music producer and artist working across experimental, glitch, and minimal techno. He records under his own name, as one half of the duo Snd alongside Mat Steel, and under the Sensate Focus alias. His catalogue is weighted toward the 2010s (10 releases), with Editions Mego serving as his primary label home from 2010 to 2016; more recent work has appeared on Black Truffle and Boomkat Editions. His closest recurring collaborators include Rashad Becker, Loop-o, Rian Treanor (his son), and Will Guthrie.
