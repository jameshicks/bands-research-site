Masaki Nakao

Discogs ID: 3275588
Name variations: Nakao
Origin: Japan

Percussionist, vocalist, lyricist, and photographer. Member of Japandorf. Known primarily through his collaboration with Klaus Dinger on the posthumous Klaus Dinger + Japandorf LP (2013, Grönland Records).

Biography

Nakao’s Discogs presence is small but spans nearly fifteen years of engagement with Klaus Dinger’s Japan orbit. His earliest documented connection is a visual/calligraphic credit on La! NEU?’s Live In Tokyo 1996 Vol.2 (1999, Captain Trip Records) — he contributed “Calligraphy [Japanese Sign For ‘Love’]” to the release, placing him at the 1996 Tokyo concert that marked Dinger’s major Japanese engagement. That event (or its aftermath) appears to have seeded the Japandorf project.

Japandorf — the group Nakao formed with Kazuyuki Onouchi, Miki Yui, and Satoshi Okamoto (also listed under the name variation “Prejapandorf”) — collaborated with Dinger on material that became Klaus Dinger + Japandorf. Dinger died in March 2008 before the record was finished; the album was completed and released posthumously in 2013 on Grönland Records (cat. LPGRON124).

On the LP, Nakao’s credited roles are specific and varied: percussion on “Kittelbach Symphony” (A4), lead vocals on “Immermannstraße” (A1), “Udon” (A3), and “Osenbe (Edit)” (B5), words (lyrics) on “Immermannstraße” and “Udon” (with Miki Yui), and photography. “Immermannstraße” and “Udon” credit both Nakao and Yui as lyricists alongside Onouchi and Dinger as composers.

No other releases are documented in Discogs.
