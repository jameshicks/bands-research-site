Overview

Aaron Dessner (born 23 April 1976, Cincinnati, Ohio) is a New York-based songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, and producer, best known as a founding member of The National. His catalogue runs from the early 2000s through the mid-2020s, centred on indie rock and indie pop, with 4AD as his primary long-term label (2009–2025) and additional releases on Republic Records and Jagjaguwar. His most frequent creative partners are his twin brother Bryce Dessner and engineer Jonathan Low, alongside National bandmates Matt Berninger, Bryan Devendorf, and Scott Devendorf. From 2020 onwards he became one of the most active figures in mainstream pop production, collaborating extensively with Taylor Swift and Jack Antonoff, and he also belongs to Big Red Machine alongside Justin Vernon of Bon Iver.
