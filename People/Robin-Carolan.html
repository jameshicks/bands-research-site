Robin Carolan

Discogs ID: 2163478 Role: Composer, label founder Labels: Sacred Bones (composer); Tri Angle Records (founder)

Composer and former label founder (Tri Angle Records — electronic/hauntology imprint, UK). Composed the score for The Northman (2022, Robert Eggers) with Sebastian Gainsborough — released SBR-307 (13 pressings) on Sacred Bones. Also composed Nosferatu (2024, Robert Eggers) — SBR-355 (8 pressings). 22 Discogs releases on Sacred Bones through 2025.

Connections

  • Sebastian Gainsborough — co-composer on The Northman
  • Sacred Bones — film score releases

Tri Angle Records

Carolan founded Tri Angle in 2010, operating it from Brooklyn and London through to early 2020. The label released ~40 titles by artists including Vessel (5), Holy Other, Forest Swords, The Haxan Cloak, Serpentwithfeet, oOoOO (3), Balam Acab, Lotic, and How To Dress Well. Carolan is credited as Executive-Producer, A&R, and Management on late-period releases such as Serpentwithfeet’s Soil (2018). Also records under the alias Lie (2) (Discogs artist ID: 4188838).

Film Scoring

The Northman (2022) — how the collaboration came about

The Northman was Carolan’s debut film score — he has described the experience as a “baptism by fire.” (readrange.com)

Carolan and Eggers were close friends before any professional collaboration. The origin of their friendship is not documented in available sources, but by the time The Northman was greenlit, Eggers described Carolan as “one of my closest friends.” (lwlies.com/interviews/robert-eggers-the-northman, as of 2026-06-27)

The friendship had already produced a significant chain of introductions: Carolan — who had an existing working relationship with Björk — introduced Eggers and his wife to Björk, reportedly during an Iceland holiday in 2016. Björk then introduced Eggers to Sjón (the Icelandic novelist/poet), who became his co-writer on The Northman. Eggers confirmed this in a Screen Rant interview: “Robin Carolan, the co-composer along with Sebastian Gainsborough, has worked with Björk; has a relationship with Björk. He introduced me and my wife to Björk, who introduced me to Sjón, my co-writer.” (screenrant.com)

When Eggers approached Carolan about scoring the film, Carolan — who had recently closed Tri Angle Records in 2020 — took roughly six weeks to agree to take it on. He has said he was “at a crossroads” about what to do after the label and that Eggers’ offer came at an unusual moment. (brooklynvegan.com; search-result summary corroborated across multiple interviews)

Carolan brought in Sebastian Gainsborough (Vessel) as co-composer. The score was developed over roughly 18 months during COVID-19 lockdown (beginning 2020), involving extensive ethnographic research into Viking music traditions and consultation with ethnographer Poul Høxbro.

Eggers’ brief to the composers was that the music should sound “caked in blood and mud” — deliberately harsh rather than conventionally listenable. (theblankmag.com)

Carolan went on to score Eggers’ Nosferatu (2024) solo, describing the growth from Northman to Nosferatu as “incredibly satisfying.”
