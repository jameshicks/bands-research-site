Sep 27, 20261 min read

Syl Johnson

Discogs ID: 49823
Origin: Holly Springs, Mississippi (based Chicago)
Active: 1959–present
Genres: Soul, funk, R&B

Chicago soul and funk artist. Extensive discography spanning gospel-influenced early soul through funk-era recordings for Twinight Records and Hi Records. Known for the proto-funk standard “Is It Because I’m Black” (1969) and “Different Strokes” (1969), both heavily sampled in hip-hop. His Hi Records albums — including Total Explosion (1975) and Bout To Make Me Leave Home (1975) — were produced and engineered by Willie Mitchell at Royal Recording Studios, using the full Hi Rhythm Section and The Memphis Horns.

Numero Group issued Complete Mythology (NUM032, 2010) — a massive 6LP + 4CD box set collecting his entire discography, with the catalog running from sides A through L on vinyl. 22 total releases on Numero Group, including individual Twinight 45 reissues on the JR (3) and ES sublabels.
