Overview

Bob Nanna (Robert Thomas Nanna) is a vocalist and guitarist best known as a co-founder of Braid, the influential mid-1990s emo and math rock band from Champaign, Illinois, whose tight-knit lineup — Bell, Atkinson, and Broach — has remained his primary creative circle across 186 Discogs credits stretching back to 1993. After Braid’s initial run wound down, he co-founded Hey Mercedes and has continued recording under multiple projects including Certain People I Know, Friction, and Jack & Ace. His primary label home has been Polyvinyl Record Company, a relationship spanning 1996 to the present (14 releases), with additional work on Vagrant, Topshelf, and Run For Cover. Producer J. Robbins ranks among his most frequent outside collaborators, reflecting a sustained tie to the broader DC/Midwest post-hardcore production network.
