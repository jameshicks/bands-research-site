Andy Smith (4)

Guitarist, active UK anarcho-punk scene early 1980s. Discogs artist ID: 683988. Real name unknown.

Overview

Andy Smith was a guitarist operating in the UK anarcho-punk scene of the early 1980s, credited on releases by both Rubella Ballet and Flux Of Pink Indians. His Discogs name variation lists him as simply “Andy.” No real name is recorded in the database.

His earliest traced credit is on Flux Of Pink Indians’ debut EP Neu Smell (1981, Crass Records), placing him in the Crass Records orbit at the height of the anarcho-punk scene. He is listed alongside fellow guitarist Neil Puncher and bassist/founder Derek Birkett on that record, with production by Penny Rimbaud.

By 1982 he had moved to Rubella Ballet, appearing on their debut release Ballet Bag (1982, Xntrix). He appears on Cocktail Mix (1987), the band’s second studio album, credited on specific tracks (A2–A5, B3–B6), sharing guitar duties with Pete Fender who handled the remaining tracks. This track-by-track split suggests he may have left and rejoined, or that the album was assembled from sessions across different lineup configurations.

In 2008 he reappeared on Anarchy In The U.V., Rubella Ballet’s retrospective/archival release on Overground Records, again sharing guitar credit with Pete Fender and Steve Cashman.

He is also credited on the 1997 Flux Of Pink Indians retrospective Not So Brave (Overground Records), on specific tracks (B1, B3–B8) — the overlap with Neil Puncher on that record (A1, B3–B8) suggests those were from an early shared lineup period.
