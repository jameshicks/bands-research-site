UK producer and engineer, active primarily in the 1990s alternative rock scene. Also plays keyboards, pedal steel guitar, and percussion as session musician.

Overview

Phil Wright (2) is listed in the Discogs DB as a UK producer/engineer active in the 1990s alternative rock scene. He is best known in this vault as the producer of Sharkboy (2)‘s entire Nude Records output (1993–1995), and for his engineering and production work with Stereolab at Elektra.

Note: There is a separate “Phil Wright” entry (Discogs ID: 229440) with 1969–1970 Capitol Records credits and two later credits on The Valentine Tapes and Valentine Singles — likely a data overlap or mis-merge in the DB. Phil Wright (2) (ID: 384646) is the 1990s UK producer.
