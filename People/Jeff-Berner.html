Jeff Berner

Discogs ID: 1358757 Also credited as: Jeff “Bunsen” Berner, J. Berner, Jeffrey Berner Website: jeffbernerrecording.com

Freelance producer, engineer, mixer, and multi-instrumentalist based in New York (label 583644 on Discogs). Guitarist and long-standing member of Psychic TV (PTV3 era from at least 2014). Also a member of the band Spin the Outside.

Overview

Berner operates primarily in indie rock, heavy psych, and experimental circles as a working engineer and producer, with a side identity as a performing guitarist. His Discogs credit trail runs from 2003 to at least 2025 — around 60 release credits total — spanning bands on Tee Pee Records, Matador, Northern Spy, Western Vinyl, Dais Records, and Heavy Psych Sounds.

Within Psychic TV he is credited under the “Bunsen” moniker and plays a genuinely multi-role part: performing instruments (guitar, synth, bass, backing vocals), programming, recording, and mixing. On both Snakes (2014) and Alienist (2016) he shares the production and mixing credit with Edley ODowd, making the two of them the de facto musical architects of the PTV3 sound in that period, operating under Genesis Breyer P-Orridge’s vocal direction.
