Chaka Khan

Singer. Discogs artist ID: 17035. Born Yvette Marie Stevens, March 23, 1953, Chicago, Illinois.

Lead vocalist of Rufus (1972–83); solo career from 1978 onward. Arif Mardin produced and arranged brass/woodwind/strings across 39 releases spanning 1978–2025, including her debut Chaka (1978) and I Feel For You (1984, which featured Stevie Wonder and Grandmaster Melle Mel). One of Mardin’s longest client relationships.
