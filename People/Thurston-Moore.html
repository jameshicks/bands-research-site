Thurston Moore

Discogs ID: 2108
Born: Thurston Joseph Moore, Coral Gables, FL, 1958
Groups: Sonic Youth (co-founder, 1981–2011), Ciccone Youth, Chelsea Light Moving, Dim Stars, Bark Haze, Glenn Branca Ensemble

Guitarist, vocalist, and solo artist. Co-founder of Sonic Youth. Performed in Glenn Branca’s ensembles before forming SY. Married to Kim Gordon 1984–2011; their separation triggered SY’s indefinite hiatus.

Also ran Ecstatic Peace! label from the mid-1980s.

Solo Discography (selected)

  • Psychic Hearts (1995, Geffen) — debut solo LP
  • Root (1998, Lo Recordings)
  • Trees Outside The Academy (2007, Ecstatic Peace!)
  • Demolished Thoughts (2011, Matador)
  • The Best Day (2014, Matador) — Thurston Moore Band

Side Projects

  • Ciccone Youth — with SY members; The Whitey Album (1988)
  • Chelsea Light Moving — post-hiatus group (2012–2013)
  • Dim Stars — with Richard Hell, Robert Quine, Don Fleming, Steve Shelley
  • Bark Haze — with bassist Kid Millions
  • Thurston Moore Band — solo touring/recording group
  • Blue Humans — produced Clear To Higher Time (1992, New Alliance Records) for Rudolph Grey’s noise/free-improv project; also listed as Blue Humans member on Discogs

Labels: Joyful Noise Recordings (2013–2016, 4 releases), Matador Records, Ecstatic Peace!
