oOoOO

Real name: Christopher Dexter Greenspan Discogs ID: 1765951 Also known as: Christopher Greenspan (Discogs ID: 16279708) Name variations: oOoO, O Oo Oo, o0o0o0o Active: 2010 – present Origin: New York, NY, USA (later based in Berlin, Germany) Genres: Electronic — Witch House, Darkwave, Synth-pop, Leftfield, Experimental R&B

Solo project of Christopher Dexter Greenspan, one of the founding acts on Tri Angle and a key figure in the 2010 witch-house moment. Greenspan’s work is characterised by heavy reverb, slowed or pitch-shifted vocals, slow-motion drum programming, and a pervasive atmosphere of late-night dread. After two EPs on Tri Angle, he relocated his output to his own imprint Nihjgt Feelings, expanding the palette toward darkwave and lo-fi R&B. He also records and performs as one half of Drab City alongside Islamiq Grrrls.

Biography

2010 — Emergence and Tri Angle

oOoOO surfaced in 2010 as part of a loose cluster of producers — clustered online under the “witch house” tag — releasing heavily processed, vapour-thick electronic music. The name itself plays on the visual illegibility of the genre’s artists (symbols, slashes, triangles in names), a deliberate SEO-hostile aesthetic gesture.

Greenspan’s earliest documented release was the Untitled CDr (2010, Disaro, CDR021) — a six-track self-released object credited with artwork and released in extremely limited edition. The tracklist includes “NoSummr4U” (with uncredited vocals later attributed to Miss Lisa (3)), “Sedsumting,” “Mumbai,” “Egyptynlvr” (with uncredited Nadia Oh vocals), “Pckrfcrmx” (sampling Lady Gaga), and “No Shore.” The CDr format and hand-made presentation were typical of early witch-house circulation.

That same year he split a 7” with fellow witch-house act White Ring on Sweden’s Emotion (2) (EMO001). The split — “Roses” by White Ring / “Seaww” by oOoOO — was one of the genre’s earliest physical documents, mastered by Henrik Sunbring (Oplen).

The self-titled EP oOoOO (Tri Angle, 2010, cat: TRI ANGLE 02) was his Tri Angle debut and the label’s second-ever release, following Balam Acab’s See Birds (TRI ANGLE 01). Six tracks on 12” vinyl (33⅓ RPM): “Mumbai,” “Burnout Eyess,” “Sedsumting,” “Hearts,” “Plains Is Hot,” and a closing remix of “Burnout Eyess” by Visions Of Trees. Cover photography by Alison Scarpulla. The EP was immediately identified as a defining example of the emerging Tri Angle sound: slow beats, tidal reverb, indistinct vocals treated as pure texture. Styles on Discogs: Leftfield, Synth-pop, Witch House.

2012 — Our Loving Is Hurting Us

The follow-up EP Our Loving Is Hurting Us arrived in 2012 (Tri Angle, TRIANGLE11CD, CD format). Five tracks, two featuring ButterClock (Laura Clock): “Springs” and “NoWayBack.” The remaining tracks — “TryTry,” “Starr,” “Break Yr Heartt” — were solo. The release broadened the palette slightly, with genres on Discogs spanning Electronic, Hip Hop, and Pop alongside styles of Glitch, Leftfield, Screw, and Synth-pop — indicating a consciously chopped-and-screwed influence alongside the witch-house DNA. This was Greenspan’s last release on Tri Angle.

2013 — Without Your Love and Nihjgt Feelings

Greenspan founded Nihjgt Feelings as his own imprint and debuted it with the LP Without Your Love (2013, Nihjgt Feelings 001 LP, Germany). Eleven tracks on vinyl, ranging across Darkwave, Leftfield, and Synth-pop. No featured vocalists — the record is purely solo. The move to self-release via a private label represented a step away from the tastemaker infrastructure and toward total control of the output. The imprint’s catalog number (Nihjgt Feelings 001 LP) suggests it was designed from the outset to continue.

2018 — Faminine Mystique and Islamiq Grrrls Collaboration

The second Nihjgt Feelings LP, Faminine Mystique (2018, NFCD 1002, CD), is a co-billed album with Islamiq Grrrls (Alisa Beširević, also known as Anuk Rocha and Asia Nevolja). Thirteen tracks; production, recording, mixing, photography, and artwork are shared between Greenspan and Beširević across the tracklist. The two divide songwriting duties: Greenspan sings lead on “Feeling Feelings,” “Be On Through,” “Later On,” “Y’re Gonna Love Me,” “When Yr’e All Alone,” “I Want To Be Alone” (co-featuring Islamiq Grrrls), and “Ordinary Words”; Islamiq Grrrls takes lead on “All Of Me,” “True Blue,” and “Yr Love”; “The Stranger” and “Jobim Goes Latenight” are additional co-credited or solo Greenspan cuts. Styles: Contemporary R&B, Experimental, Leftfield, Witch House. The album signals a maturation of the oOoOO aesthetic into something closer to lo-fi R&B and experimental pop.

2020 – Present — Drab City

Greenspan and Beširević formalised their collaboration as Drab City, a duo project signed to Bella Union. The debut album Good Songs For Bad People (2020, Bella Union, BELLA1020V) was released on 10-track vinyl LP and CD. Styles: Dream Pop, Psychedelic — a softer, more song-oriented direction than either solo project’s extreme atmospheric ends. The Drab City name has remained active into the early 2020s.
