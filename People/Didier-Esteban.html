Didier Esteban

Discogs ID: 1404872. French musician, arranger, and producer. Brother of Michel Esteban (ZE Records co-founder). Active primarily in the Paris–NYC post-punk / no wave orbit in the late 1970s and sporadically thereafter. Also records and is credited under the alias D.J. Banes (Discogs ID 523939).

Biography

Born January 13, 1955, in the northern suburbs of Paris. Grew up with one older brother (Michel), one sister, and two younger twin brothers. Early musical exposure came via a family record player with The Beach Boys and The Beatles; he has described “the revelation” as hearing the first Roxy Music album in 1972. His first significant concert was Todd Rundgren on October 14, 1975, at the Mutualité in Paris — he bought his first guitar the following day. Influences in this period included the Velvet Underground, Iggy and the Stooges, David Bowie, Lou Reed, and T. Rex.

European Son and Man Ray (Paris, 1976–1977)

Esteban’s first serious musical project was a two-guitar duo called European Son, formed with Jean-Louis Boulanger (later known as Hermann Schwartz). In September 1976, the pair and companions attended punk concerts at the 100 Club in London (Sex Pistols, The Clash, The Damned, Siouxsie and the Banshees).

Around late 1976, Esteban expanded the project into a band called Man Ray — named after the photographer — adding his girlfriend Anne on vocals and drummer Patrice “Ness.” They performed three concerts: MJC de Fresnes, the Mouffetard theatre (January 31, 1977), and the Gibus (March 21, 1977). On February 1, 1977, Esteban was called up for French military service; he received special permission to return for performances before being discharged on April 20, 1977.

Move to New York and Rosa Yemen (1977–1979)

In mid-1977, Esteban relocated to New York City with Lizzy Mercier Descloux, staying over a year. Before settling on the name Rosa Yemen, the duo performed at least one concert under the name Lola Rastaquère, at The Kitchen gallery in New York. The Rosa Yemen EP and Lizzy’s Press Color album were created simultaneously in early 1978 at Blank Tape Studios, engineered by Bob Blank. Seth Tillett (Lizzy’s companion at the time) contributed percussion and sound recordings to the sessions.

The EP included reimagined covers: “Mission Impossible,” “Jim on the Move,” “Fever” (transformed into “Tumor”), and Arthur Brown’s “Fire.” Esteban has described an earlier version as closer to the originals: “Michel was the producer [and] had seen the breaking disco wave and decided to re-record it with the contribution of studio musicians.” This producer intervention — his brother Michel pushing the material toward a disco-inflected sound — shaped the final release. Esteban mixed, produced, and arranged the EP as D.J. Banes, and handled the same roles on Press Color.

After Rosa Yemen

Following the end of his partnership with Descloux, Esteban continued composing and recording sporadically in Paris. He worked with Clode (formerly a vocalist with Métal Urbain) on a 45 released under the name Etat Major, featuring “Django Jet.” He also recorded covers with a project called Parisians Inc., and composed original tracks “L’adieu aux Larmes” and “Homme Vidéo” with Erik (likely Erik Fitoussi), though these remained unreleased. He eventually stepped back from professional performance, though he continued as an active listener.

Man Ray (9) reformation (2016)

In 2016, Esteban reunited with Jean-Louis Boulanger (Hermann Schwartz, by then known from Métal Urbain and Metal Boys) to revive the Man Ray name as Man Ray (9), releasing two 7-inch singles on Cameleon Records (2).

Rosa Yemen

Rosa Yemen was a short-lived avant-garde/no wave project formed in New York City in 1978. The self-titled EP was recorded there and released in 1979 on two pressings with differing credits and label attributions:

Celluloid pressing (France, 1979): Didier is credited as D.J. Banes — Guitar [Ritmic]. Produced by Michel Esteban. Engineered by Bob Blank.

ZE Records pressing (UK, 1979, Release ID 442764): Didier is credited as Didier Esteban — Guitar [Rhythm]. Produced by Michel Esteban and Rosa Yemen (i.e., the group as collective producer). Engineered by Bob Blank.

Lizzy Mercier Descloux sings lead vocals and plays guitar on both pressings.

Man Ray (9)

In 2016, Didier formed a punk duo called Man Ray (9) with Hermann Schwartz (real name Jean-Louis Boulanger; Discogs ID 494544). They released two records on Cameleon Records (2):

  • “Daily And News / Man Ray / Cancer / Folie Douce” (2016)
  • “De La Came Punk” (2016)

Hermann Schwartz is best known from Métal Urbain and Metal Boys.
