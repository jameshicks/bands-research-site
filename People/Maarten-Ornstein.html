Maarten Ornstein

Dutch saxophonist and clarinetist, born 1967. Discogs artist ID: 466201. Website: maartenornstein.com

Name variations in credits: M. Ornstein, Ornstein.

Overview

Ornstein is a Dutch reed player active in jazz and contemporary chamber settings from the late 1980s through at least the late 2010s. His main instruments are tenor saxophone, bass clarinet, and clarinet; he is also credited on tárogató (a Hungarian woodwind). He works across a wide range of Dutch jazz ensembles and has led or co-led recordings under his own name. His international profile is most visible through Unspoken (2016), a duo album with Icelandic pianist Sunna Gunnlaugs on her label Sunny Sky Records, on which he plays bass clarinet throughout.

He is a member of nine documented ensembles in the Discogs database, including long-running partnerships with bassist Tony Overwater and flutist Mark Alban Lotz.

Ensembles

Scapes

Discogs ID: 1429433. A jazz quintet also featuring Tony Overwater (bass), Erik Doelman (piano), Ben van den Dungen (tenor and soprano saxophone), Hans Verdoorn (drums). Ornstein plays tenor saxophone and bass clarinet. Their sole Discogs release is Changes In Time (1990, Timeless Records).

SFeQ

Discogs ID: 278796. A seven-piece ensemble. Members: Bart Suèr (alto/soprano saxophone), Peter Bergman (bass), Erwin Zimmerman, Jesse Van Ruller (guitar), Maarten Ornstein, Stefan Kruger (drums). Also known as SFEQ / SFEQ 3000. Bandcamp presence. Their Discogs release is Rumble (1992, VIA).

The Jazzmeteors

Discogs ID: 4261545. A larger jazz band. Members include Christof May (alto sax), Marcel Van Ettinger (bass guitar), Ruben Van Roon (drums), Paul Jan Bakker (guitar), Wiboud Burkens (keyboards), Manuel Hugas, Bob Fosko. Ornstein on tenor saxophone. Release: Hallo (1994, VIA Jazz).

Martin Fondse Oktemble

Discogs ID: 2019745. An octet led by composer/pianist Martin Fondse. Members: Eric Vloeimans (trumpet), Nils Wogram (trombone), Michael Moore (4) (alto sax/clarinet), Mete Erker (tenor sax), Maarten Ornstein (reeds), Miguel Boelens, Eric Van Der Westen, Pieter Bast. Released 8 x G ! (1995, Rollercoaster Records), a joint release co-credited to De 8 Baan.

Lotz Of Music

Discogs ID: 6164846. An ensemble organized around flutist Mark Alban Lotz. Large rotating membership; Ornstein appears alongside Albert Van Veenendaal, Marius Beets, Marc van Roon, Michael Vatcher, Eric Surménian, Jörg Brinkmann, Mark Alban Lotz, Alan “Gunga” Purves, and others. Discogs credits Ornstein on Le Coq Rouge (1996, VIA Jazz) and — by ensemble membership — the later Pum’kin Diaries (2002, LopLop Productions), though he is not listed in the 2002 personnel credits specifically.

Maarten Ornstein & W.A.R.P. Music Ensemble

Discogs ID: 3950137. Ornstein’s own ensemble, for which he is also the composer. Personnel: Marije van der Ende (bassoon), Ivar Berix (clarinet), David Kweksilber (clarinet/bass clarinet/saxophone), Michiel van Dijk (clarinet/bass clarinet/saxophone), Xenia Meijer (mezzo-soprano), Joshua Samson (percussion). Ornstein plays clarinet, bass clarinet, and tárogató. One release: the self-titled Maarten Ornstein & W.A.R.P. Music Ensemble (2000, Turtle Records).

Tony Overwater Kwintet

Discogs ID: 2020930. A quintet led by bassist Tony Overwater. Release: Kikker Swingt (2003, Jazz In Motion / Leopold), a children’s album narrated by Max Velthuijs. Ornstein plays saxophone and clarinet alongside Marc van Roon (piano), Angelo Verploegen (trumpet/flugelhorn), Joshua Samson (percussion).

Tony Overwater Trio

Discogs ID: 7549532. A trio: Tony Overwater (bass), Ornstein (bass clarinet/tenor saxophone), Yuri Honing (tenor saxophone). Release: Over The Rainbow (1999, First Impression Music — Japan-only release).

Tony Overwater Ensemble

Discogs ID: 7549538. An expanded group also including Rembrandt Frerichs (fortepiano/harmonium), Nizar Rohana (oud), Rosanne Philippens (violin), Ruven Ruppik (percussion). Release: Om De Oude Wereldzee (2015, Jazz In Motion Records). Ornstein credited on clarinet.
