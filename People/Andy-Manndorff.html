Overview

Andy Manndorff (24 February 1957 – 8 September 2017) was a Viennese composer and jazz guitarist active from the mid-1980s until his death at sixty. He worked across contemporary jazz, avant-garde, fusion, and free improvisation idioms, and was a member of the Manndorff Trio and Jacek Kochan Ajee. His most consistent collaborator was saxophonist Wolfgang Puschnig, with whom he shared eight releases; he also worked repeatedly with Christian Radovan, Erich Dorfinger, Hannes Kottek, Harry Sokal, Heiri Känzig, Herbert Joos, Jon Sass, Joris Dudli, Karl Fian, Lauren Newton, and Mathias Rüegg. His recordings appeared on Moers Music in the late 1980s, EmArcy in the 2000s, and Cracked AnEgg Records in his final years.
