Arne Van Petegem

Discogs ID: 14329
Origin: Belgium
Project: Styrofoam (also known as Tin Foil Star)

Belgian electronic musician performing as Styrofoam. One of Morr Music’s founding-era artists; 28 releases 2000–2008. Remixed múm on Please Smile My Noise Bleed (2001) and collaborated with Fat Jon on The Same Channel (2006).
