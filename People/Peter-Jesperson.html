Overview

Peter Jesperson is a Minneapolis-born music industry figure best known as co-founder of Twin/Tone Records and former manager of The Replacements. His Discogs footprint is dominated by Replacements-related work — he shares credits with all four core members across 130–147 releases, alongside frequent collaborator engineer Steve Fjelstad. He later relocated to Los Angeles, joining New West Records as a manager and accumulating a further 43 credits there between 2001 and 2025, working with acts including Drive-By Truckers, Jason Isbell, and Patterson Hood. His roles span producer, co-producer, reissue producer, mixer, and arranger, tracing a career arc from indie label co-founder through artist management to reissue and catalogue work.
