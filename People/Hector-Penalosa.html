Hector Penalosa

Discogs artist ID: 549088. Name variations: Hector Peñalosa, H. Penalosa.

Bassist and vocalist from the San Diego / Los Angeles area. Co-founding member of The Zeros (1976–1980; reunions through 2010s). Brother of Victor Penalosa.

Overview

Hector Penalosa played bass and sang on all The Zeros recordings, contributing songwriting on tracks including “They Say That (Everything’s Alright),” “Left To Right,” and “You, Me, Us.” After The Zeros’ initial breakup he went on to Flying Color (Frontier / Grifter Records, 1987), then continued with The Zeros reunion through the 1990s and 2000s.

Other bands and projects: The Riot Act!, MCM And The Monster, Wolvarines, Squiddo, Loud (17).
