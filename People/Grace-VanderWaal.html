Overview

VanderWaal came to national attention at age 12 when she won America’s Got Talent Season 11 in 2016, performing original songs on ukulele. She was signed to Columbia/Syco shortly after and released her debut EP and debut album before turning 14. Her early work is notable for being largely self-written — she co-wrote or sole-wrote all tracks on Perfectly Imperfect and Just the Beginning, with production handled primarily by Greg Wells. After a quieter 2018, she moved toward more pop-oriented singles in 2019 under Simco Limited before resurfacing in 2025 with the album Childstar on Pulse Records.

The brief that seeded this research contained a factual error: it attributed Amends (2020, Loma Vista) to Grace VanderWaal with production by Alex Aldi. That album is by Grey Daze (Chester Bennington’s pre-Linkin Park band). VanderWaal has no known association with Loma Vista or Alex Aldi. The research queue item about “how Aldi came to produce Amends” was based on incorrect information.

She also appeared as the lead in Disney+‘s Stargirl (2020) — biographical detail not verifiable via Discogs.
