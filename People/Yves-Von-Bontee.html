Overview

Yves Von Bontee is a figure in the late 1970s–1980s experimental and industrial underground, with ten Discogs credits spanning releases from 1979 to the mid-1980s. All three selected releases tie to the French label Sordide Sentimental, the Rouen-based imprint known for releasing early Joy Division and avant-garde industrial work. Von Bontee’s credit list places them squarely within the Psychic TV orbit — Genesis P-Orridge shares three credits, with Alex Fergusson, Paula P-Orridge, and John Gosling each appearing on two, suggesting a working relationship with the post-Throbbing Gristle circle around that label. Whether Von Bontee was a performer, producer, or a pseudonym for a known figure remains unresolved.

Notes (web) — Von Bontee / Genesis P-Orridge connection

Summary: The available evidence indicates that Von Bontee’s shared credits with Genesis P-Orridge arose through the Sordide Sentimental co-founding relationship, not from any prior direct personal link between Von Bontee and Genesis P-Orridge.

The personal bridge to Genesis P-Orridge was built by Jean-Pierre Turmel, the other Sordide Sentimental co-founder. Turmel ordered a copy of Throbbing Gristle’s Second Annual Report (mid-1970s), which led to a correspondence with Genesis P-Orridge; Turmel then wrote an extended philosophical review of the LP in his fanzine (also called Sordide Sentimental). The two became pen-pals, and that relationship directly shaped Turmel’s decision to convert the fanzine into a record label in 1978 — co-founded with Von Bontee. Letters from Genesis to Turmel spanning the mid-1970s to early 1990s are documented in The Last Slogan (Genesis Breyer P-Orridge and Jean-Pierre Turmel, Les presses du réel). [source: https://www.lespressesdureel.com/EN/ouvrage.php?id=11822&menu=0, as of 2026-10-28]

Von Bontee’s role across all three shared-credit releases is visual/artwork (collage, inside artwork, cover collage), consistent with the role of a design/art partner at the label rather than an independent correspondent or collaborator with the Genesis P-Orridge circle. No source documents any direct personal contact between Von Bontee and Genesis P-Orridge, Throbbing Gristle, or Psychic TV outside the label context. The Springerin 2008 article on Turmel mentions Von Bontee only as co-founder; the John Coulthart feuilleton tag page for Von Bontee makes no mention of Genesis P-Orridge at all. [source: https://www.springerin.at/en/2008/3/futures-pasts/ , as of 2026-10-28; https://www.johncoulthart.com/feuilleton/tag/yves-von-bontee/ , as of 2026-10-28]

Conclusion: The Von Bontee / Genesis P-Orridge connection was label-mediated. The Turmel–Genesis correspondence predated and motivated the label’s founding; Von Bontee co-founded the label and contributed artwork, but the pre-label personal link was Turmel’s alone. No earlier direct Von Bontee / Genesis P-Orridge relationship is documented in any source located.
