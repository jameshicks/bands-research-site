Howard Bilerman

Montreal recording engineer, producer, drummer, and actor. Discogs artist ID: 284434. Active in credits 1993–2026.

Biography

Howard Bilerman is a Montreal-based recording engineer, producer, and drummer who has been central to the city’s avant-rock and indie underground since the early 1990s. His profile ranges from the Constellation Records post-rock scene to major-label sessions, Quebec pop, and international indie work.

He started recording in 1993, initially working out of a Montreal studio he ran from 1996 to 2000 (Discogs label ID: 309926). Around 2000–2001 he co-founded Hotel2Tango studio in Montreal with Efrim Menuck and Thierry Amar, establishing the room that would become the primary recording home for most of Constellation’s roster through the 2000s and 2010s. Radwan Moumneh became a close collaborator and co-engineer there.

Beyond engineering, Bilerman is a working drummer. He played drums on Arcade Fire’s debut album Funeral (2004), also engineering it — a crossover that tied the Constellation world to what became one of the decade’s defining indie-rock breakthroughs. He left Arcade Fire after Funeral to focus on production work. He has also played drums and guitar for scattered smaller sessions (Sam Roberts Band, Wandering Eyes, and others) and appeared in acting credits.

His name variations in Discogs credits include H. Bilerman, H. Billerman, Howard Billerman, Howie Billerman, and H.B.

Hotel2Tango Studio

Hotel2Tango is the recording space co-founded by Bilerman, Efrim Menuck, and Thierry Amar in Montreal circa 2000–2001. It became the primary recording room for the Constellation ecosystem. Discogs release notes frequently name “Hotel2tango, Montreal” as the recording location. Radwan Moumneh is associated with the studio as a co-engineer and operator, particularly from the 2010s onward. Bilerman’s tenure as house engineer represents the studio’s formative period; the Leonard Cohen and British Sea Power credits suggest the room attracted outside bookings throughout the 2000s–2010s.

Scene Context

Bilerman sits at the intersection of Montreal’s post-rock underground (the Constellation axis — GY!BE, Silver Mt. Zion, Black Ox Orkestar, Do Make Say Think) and the broader Montreal indie ecosystem that produced Arcade Fire, Basia Bulat, The Barr Brothers, and the Secret City Records roster. Hotel2Tango was the physical locus of this convergence. His willingness to work across genres — from the klez-punk of Black Ox Orkestar to Quebec chanson to British indie — marks him as an engineer rather than a scene loyalist.

His collaboration with Harris Newman (mastering) on the majority of his recordings represents a consistent two-person post-production chain running through Montreal recording from 2000 onward.
