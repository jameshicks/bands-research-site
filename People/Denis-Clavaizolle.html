Denis Clavaizolle

French musician, producer, and composer. Discogs artist ID: 380961. Works as keyboardist, multi-instrumentalist, arranger, recording engineer, and producer. Has a website at denis-clavaizolle.com. Also credited in nicknames as “Denis ‘Big D’ Clavaizolle” and “Denis ‘Big D.’ Clavaizolle”. Member of the collective Fab Foundation. Brother of drummer Yann Clavaizolle.

Career Overview

Denis Clavaizolle’s Discogs credits span from the early 1980s to 2020, covering a wide range of French popular music — rock, chanson, indie folk, and pop. His career divides roughly into two strands: a long-running collaboration with singer-songwriter Jean-Louis Murat across multiple decades, and a parallel career as a producer and session musician for French indie and pop artists.

Early work — 1980s French rock (1982–1993)

Clavaizolle’s earliest Discogs credit is on the 1982 album Eyano (Paris Album), where he appeared as a guest on synthesizer and clavinet. Through the late 1980s he contributed keyboards to several French releases, including Notre Âme (Philips, 1989).

His association with Jean-Louis Murat begins in the early 1990s. On the 1991 album Le Manteau De Pluie (Virgin) he is credited for keyboards and programming — one of the most prominent records of that pairing. He also appeared on Murat En Plein Air (1991, Virgin/Libération), a live release, playing synthesizer and organ. The collaboration with Murat would continue across multiple decades.

Also in 1991, he played keyboards and guitar on Marie Audigier (Les Disques Du Crépuscule), an album by singer Marie Audigier produced jointly by Murat and Audigier herself — connecting Clavaizolle to the Belgian Crépuscule label. By 1993 he was serving as producer on Ces Étés (Les Disques Du Crépuscule), Audigier’s follow-up, and contributing guitar and keyboards.

Jean-Louis Murat — core collaborator (1995–2019)

Clavaizolle appears on Jean-Louis Murat’s Live (1995, Virgin) playing keyboards and guitar. His most intensive Murat work comes in the late 1990s and 2000s:

  • Mustango (1999, Virgin/Labels) — engineer and pre-production producer alongside Murat; a notably outward-looking record that brought in Marc Ribot (guitar), John Medeski (organ), and Calexico members Joey Burns (double bass) and John Convertino (drums) on several tracks.
  • Muragostang (2000, Labels) — keyboards, guitar, piano, and mixing.
  • Le Moujik Et Sa Femme (2002, Labels) — keyboards.
  • L’homme À Tête De Chou (2011, Barclay) — Bashung tribute; keyboards, organ (Hammond), piano, electric guitar, bass, programming, orchestration, arrangement, and multi-track recording. This is the Serge Gainsbourg song cycle re-interpreted by Alain Bashung’s posthumous collaborators. Yann Clavaizolle played drums; Pascal Mondaz co-recorded; Jean Lamoot mixed.
  • Innamorato (2019, Scarlett/[PIAS]) — arrangement, recording, mixing, and keyboards on the final LP section (tracks D1–D4).
  • Il Francese (2018) and Travaux Sur La N89 (2017) and Baby Love (2020, Scarlett/[PIAS]) also carry arrangement and performance credits.

Across this body of work, Clavaizolle appears in virtually every creative capacity: performer, arranger, engineer, mixer.

Producer for French indie artists (2007–2009)

In the mid-to-late 2000s, Clavaizolle produced a cluster of French indie rock and folk records, often as both producer and bassist:

  • My Friends All Died In A Plane Crash (2007, Sober & Gentle) by Cocoon (4) — bass guitar, producer, and string/wind/brass score. Yann Clavaizolle played drums; mixed by Pascal Mondaz. The album features members Mark Daumail and Morgane Imbeaud.
  • From Panda Mountains (2007, Sober & Gentle) by an unidentified act — bass and producer. Again with Yann Clavaizolle on drums.
  • The Last Memories Of My Old House (2009, 3ème Bureau/Wagram Music) by Zak Laughed — producer, recording, and mixing, credited as “Denis ‘Big D’ Clavaizolle.”

Alain Bashung — L’homme À Tête De Chou (2011)

Separately from the Murat strand, Clavaizolle was a primary studio contributor on L’homme À Tête De Chou (Barclay, 2011), a posthumous album for Alain Bashung. He performed keyboards, Hammond organ, piano, electric guitar, and bass, handled orchestration and arrangement, and recorded the keyboards, electric guitar, bass guitar, programming, strings, and lead vocals tracks. This placed him at the centre of a major French rock project.

H-Burns — Night Moves and Kid We Own The Summer (2015–2017)

Clavaizolle joined H-Burns’s band for two consecutive albums, both produced by Rob Schnapf and mastered by Greg Calbi:

Night Moves (2015, Vietnam (2) / Because Music)

  • Keyboards and organ (Farfisa & Rhodes) on tracks 1, 4, 8, 9, 11
  • Additional recording

Kid We Own The Summer (2017, Vietnam (2) / Because Music)

  • Keyboards, piano, drum machine
  • Recorded by (alongside Jean-Pierre Maillard)

His brother Yann Clavaizolle played drums and backing vocals on Kid We Own The Summer.

Other session work

  • Les Premiers Pas (2004, La Kandeur) — carillon, keyboards, lap steel guitar, shaker
  • Hang On (2014, Freemount Records) — keyboards and synthesizer
  • Boy Sentimental (2016, PBOX Music) — recorded by, piano
  • The Elderberries (2012, Sophiane) — organ and piano. Yann Clavaizolle played drums on the same release.
  • Grand Lièvre (2011, V2) — keyboards
  • Elisa Tovati’s Je Ne Mâche Pas Les Mots (2006, Mercury/Rapas) — keyboards, piano, Rhodes, and pre-production engineering

Fab Foundation

Denis Clavaizolle is listed as a member of Fab Foundation (Discogs ID: 15853076), a collective that also includes Yann Clavaizolle, Davy Sladek, Christophe Pereira, Frédéric Havet, and others — several of whom appear across his various recording credits.
