Overview

Colleen Ann Kinsella is an American multi-instrumentalist — vocalist, guitarist, accordionist, pianist, and dulcimer player — active since 1999. She is best known as a core member of Big Blood, the experimental folk project she shares with her partner Caleb Mulkerin, and also contributed to Cerberus Shoal and Fire On Fire. Her catalogue spans experimental, post rock, psychedelic rock, and folk territory, spread across labels including Young God Records, Feeding Tube Records, and Temporary Residence Limited. Beyond the Big Blood axis, her most notable collaborators include Michael Gira (17 shared credits, via Young God Records) and Alan Sparhawk of Low (11 shared credits).

Notes (web)

Fire on Fire and Young God Records

Signing and naming. Fire on Fire were originally called “Threads” (after the British post-apocalyptic film) while operating informally as a house band in South Portland, Maine. The band’s name was not finalised until the Young God Records deal arrived: per Chriss Sutherland in an undated Gothic Country feature, “when the Young God Records deal came along we had to make the tough choice.” (gothiccountry.se)

Gira’s framing of the relationship. In a 2022 Psychedelic Baby Magazine interview, Colleen Kinsella described the Young God work as engagement-by-hire rather than a mutual pitch to the label: “Michael Gira hired us for the Young God related projects, and Caleb was involved in the production of the other artist’s albums.” She also noted, “Once we signed with Young God records we had to make a name change and eventually with the birth of Quinnisa, Caleb and I were unable to keep a consistent practice schedule. We were making music as Big Blood while we were in Fire on Fire.” (psychedelicbabymag.com, as of 2026-10-20)

How the initial connection happened. No interviewed source (band member, Gira, or label) has described how Gira first encountered or reached out to the ex-Cerberus Shoal musicians. The announcement piece in ALARM Magazine (November 2007) confirmed the signing but offered no backstory. (alarm-magazine.com)

Gira’s stated enthusiasm. The Young God Records artist page for Fire on Fire carries a description written by Gira himself, calling them “a backwoods, fierce, psychedelic Mamas And The Papas,” and the label copy emphasises his “adamant belief in the music.” (younggodrecords.com/pages/fire-on-fire)

The 5 Song EP (November 2007). The first release on Young God was the self-titled five-song EP (catalog YGX01), limited to 1,000 handmade copies, available only via the Young God website and at live shows. It was co-produced by Caleb Mulkerin and Michael Gira. (alarm-magazine.com; gothiccountry.se)

The Orchard (December 2008). The full-length debut was recorded in early spring 2006 at the band’s home (tracks were already familiar to Portland audiences by the time of release). Gira co-mixed the record alongside Caleb Mulkerin. Colleen Kinsella designed the album artwork (she noted the imagery predated the band’s formation). A YGR press piece described the mixing contribution as significant: “the input of a talent like Gira (who mixed the record with Mulkerin) can’t be understated,” producing “a clean and well-balanced sound, but with enough rough edges to ensure the music’s authentic, homemade vibe comes through.” (younggodrecords.com press; gothiccountry.se)

Caleb Mulkerin’s broader YGR production work. Colleen’s 2022 interview indicates Caleb worked as a producer on other Young God artists’ albums beyond the Fire on Fire releases (consistent with the 17 shared Discogs credits between Kinsella and Gira). No source names which other YGR albums Mulkerin produced.
