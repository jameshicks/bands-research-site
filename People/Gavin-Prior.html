Overview

Gavin Prior is an Irish multi-instrumentalist active since at least 1997 in the experimental and psychedelic folk underground, working across guitar, electronics, percussion and effects over 45 credited releases. His catalogue is weighted toward the 2010s and is anchored primarily to the Deserted Village label, which released seven of his projects between 2003 and 2017. He has maintained dense collaborative ties within the same scene — particularly with David Colohan (14 shared releases), United Bible Studies (10), and Áine O’Dwyer and Richard Moult (8 each) — and has contributed to several bands including Amygdala, Tarracóir, The Magickal Folk Of The Faraway Tree, Toymonger, and Weapons Of Mass Destruction.
