Roger Sanchez

Discogs ID: 4719 Real Name: Roger René Sánchez Born: June 1, 1967, New York City, USA Roles: DJ, remixer, producer

American house DJ, producer, and label founder from the NYC underground scene. One of the defining figures of 1990s–2000s New York and Ibiza house music. Founded three labels: Narcotic (5) (1994), R-Senal (1999–2002), and Stealth Records (2) (2002–). Best known for “Another Chance” (2001, Defected, UK #1) and the long-running Release Yourself mix series.

Career

NYC Underground Origins (late 1980s–1993)

Began DJing in the late 1980s in the New York underground house scene. First releases appeared on Strictly Rhythm in 1990 — “Freedom” and “Luv Dancin’” — then continued on Strictly Rhythm through the early 1990s. Get Up (1991, Maxi Records (6)) was an early standalone single.

Narcotic Label and Mid-1990s (1994–1998)

Co-founded Narcotic (5) in 1994 with Marts Andrups. The label was a platform for deep NYC house and became closely associated with his The S-Man alias. Compiled Hard Times — The Album (1995, Narcotic), a compilation of the label’s output. I Want Your Love (1998, Narcotic, 13 pressings) was a breakout track.

Regular remixer throughout this period; notable 1997 commission: a remix of Michael Jackson’s “Blood On The Dance Floor.” Also appeared on Malcolm McLaren’s Buffalo Gals Stampede (1998, Virgin), sharing credits with Rakim.

R-Senal and Defected Breakthrough (1999–2001)

Launched the R-Senal imprint in 1999. I Never Knew (1999, R-Senal, 26 pressings) was a significant underground hit. Sessions Eleven (The R-Senal Sessions) (2000, Defected) was his first major DJ mix compilation, licensed to Defected.

“Another Chance” (2001, Defected, 32 pressings) reached #1 on the UK Singles Chart — his commercial peak. Follow-up “You Can’t Change Me” (2001, Defected, 30 pressings) featured N’Dea Davenport (vocalist associated with Brand New Heavies) and was co-produced with Armand Van Helden (28 shared release credits). “Nothing 2 Prove” (2002, Defected, 9 pressings) featured Sharleen Spiteri (Texas). Album Nothing 2 Prove (2002) collected this run of Defected singles.

Stealth Records and Release Yourself Series (2002–2011)

Founded Stealth Records (2) in 2002. The label’s first release was Release Yourself Ibiza 2002 (2002). The Release Yourself series became his flagship ongoing mix compilation, running to at least eleven volumes (2002–2011), all on Stealth. The series documented his Ibiza residency sets and NYC underground picks.

Further Stealth singles: “Turn On The Music” (2005, 26 pressings), “Lost” (2006, 24 pressings) — “Lost” featured Katherine Ellis (also recorded under alias Lisa Pure, 15/14 shared credits). “Come With Me” (2006, Ultra Records, 11 pressings). “2Gether” (2010, Stealth, 16 pressings) featured Far East Movement.

2010s–2020s

Continued releasing on Stealth and Ministry of Sound through the 2010s–2020s. Most recent tracked releases through 2025. Also appeared on Ministry Of Sound across 2010–2021.
