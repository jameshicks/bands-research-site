Neneh Cherry

Vocalist, songwriter. Stockholm, Sweden / UK. Discogs ID: 11449. Real name: Neneh Mariann Karlsson.

Stepdaughter of jazz trumpeter Don Cherry. Core vocalist of Rip Rig & Panic (1981–1982/83), the post-The Pop Group / The Slits band formed in Sweden. Left temporarily when pregnant with her first child; Andrea Oliver filled in. After Rip Rig & Panic dissolved she continued in Float Up CP (1984–1986) and God Mother & Country before going solo.

Nellee Hooper contributed arrangements to her debut album Raw Like Sushi (Circa / Virgin, 1989), which also featured production by Cameron McVey and Jamie Morgan. The album spawned “Buffalo Stance” and “Manchild”, two of the defining UK pop-soul singles of the era.

Also collaborated with Forest Swords (Matthew Barnes) on Bolted-era work (2023, Ninja Tune) — 2 shared release credits.
