Overview

Ben Flashman is a bassist from Eureka, California who co-founded Comets On Fire with Ethan Miller in 1999, helping shape the band’s heavy psychedelic and space rock sound. Beyond bass he contributed baritone guitar and organ across 32 credited releases, the bulk concentrated in the 2000s. His closest working partnerships outside Comets On Fire were with bandmates Noel Von Harmonson and Utrillo Kushner, alongside regular collaborator Ben Chasny of Six Organs of Admittance, with releases spread across Ba Da Bing!, Drag City, Sub Pop, and Alternative Tentacles.
