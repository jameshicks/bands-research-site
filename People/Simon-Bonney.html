Simon Bonney

Australian vocalist and lyricist. Discogs artist ID: 101666.

Lead vocalist and primary lyricist of Crime & The City Solution. Epic Soundtracks drummed on the band’s key Mute Records releases (Just South Of Heaven 1985, Room Of Lights 1986, The Kentucky Click. / Adventure 1986). Also appeared on the Wings Of Desire film soundtrack (1989).
