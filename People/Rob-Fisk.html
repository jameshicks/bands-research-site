Rob Fisk

Discogs ID: 556019
Experimental musician, visual artist, and label operator based in Berlin (originally US). Also known as Rob Bees Fisk; aliases include B.S.s. (2), Bees (2), Curse All Kings.

Overview

Co-founder of Free Porcupine Society (with Kelly Goode). Member of Deerhoof (bass/guitar), 7 Year Rabbit Cycle, Badgerlore, Common Eider King Eider, and Nitre Pit. Later co-founded Breath Sun Bone Blood. Also active in Egregore (with Yan Arexis).

Known for elaborate hand-made packaging on FPS releases. Credits span performance, guitar, violin, vocals, illustration/drawings, and artwork across many releases.
