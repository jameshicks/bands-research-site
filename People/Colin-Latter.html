Colin Latter

Vocalist with Flux Of Pink Indians, UK anarcho-punk scene. Discogs artist ID: 681985.

Overview

Colin Latter was the lead vocalist of Flux Of Pink Indians, appearing on the band’s 1981 debut EP Neu Smell (Crass Records) — credited on that release under the name variation “Colsk (The Terrible) Latter” — and on the 1997 retrospective Not So Brave (Overground Records).
