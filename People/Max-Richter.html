Max Richter

Discogs ID: 171698

German-British composer. Robert Wyatt appeared as narrator/reader on Songs From Before (2006, 130701, ID 852834 — 14 pressings).

Early in his career was a member of Piano Circus, the six-piano ensemble — appearing as pianist on Graham Fitkin’s Log · Line · Loud (Argo, 1992).
