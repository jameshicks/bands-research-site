Charles Hayward

English drummer, percussionist, vocalist, and composer. Born 1951. Discogs artist ID: 90225. Name variations: C. Hayward, CH, Charlie Hayward, Charis Hayward.

Overview

Charles Hayward is one of the key figures in British post-punk and avant-garde music. He is best known as a founding member and drummer of This Heat, the Cold Storage collective whose two studio albums — This Heat (1979) and Deceit (1981) — became touchstones for experimental rock. After This Heat dissolved in 1982, Hayward formed The Camberwell Now and maintained an extensive solo and collaborative career through the following four decades, working with figures including Fred Frith, Bill Laswell, Gigi Masin, Hugh Hopper, Keiji Haino, and Dälek. He has been a member of an unusually wide range of groups spanning art-rock, free jazz, improv, noise, and post-punk.

Early Career (pre–1976)

Before This Heat, Hayward played in Quiet Sun, the Phil Manzanera–led art-rock group alongside Bill MacCormick and Dave Jarrett. He also contributed percussion to Phil Manzanera’s solo album Diamond Head (1975, ATCO Records) — a recording that also featured Brian Eno, Robert Wyatt, John Wetton, and Andy Mackay.

This Heat (1975–1982)

Hayward co-founded This Heat with Gareth Williams and Charles Bullen in 1975. The band operated out of their self-built Cold Storage studio in Brixton.

The Camberwell Now (1982–1992)

After This Heat, Hayward formed The Camberwell Now with Trefor Goronwy (This Heat’s touring bassist) and Stephen Rickard.

Core lineup:

  • Charles Hayward — voice, drums, kazoo, autoharp, melodica, keyboards
  • Trefor Goronwy — bass, guitar, ukulele, voice, keyboards, erhu, jew’s harp
  • Stephen Rickard — tape, autoharp, noises, field recording

Additional musician on Greenfingers and All’s Well: Maria Lamburn (soprano and tenor saxophone, piccolo flute, viola).

Personnel notes by release:

Meridian (1983): Self-produced. The trio’s debut EP. Hayward: voice, drums, kazoo, autoharp, melodica, keyboards. Rickard credited as “Steve Rickard” for noises and tape.

The Ghost Trade (1986): Self-produced on Ink Records. Goronwy adds erhu. No additional musicians. Mastered by Dave Bernez and Tony Cousins.

Greenfingers (1987): Engineer Keith Hancock. Maria Lamburn joins on woodwinds and viola. Mastered by John Dent (“Jonz”). Words by Hayward (A, B1) and Goronwy (B2).

All’s Well (1992, RecRec Music): The most fully documented release. Track-by-track personnel assignments differ — Hayward plays on tracks 1–4, Rickard on 5–11, mixed sessions on 12–15. Engineer Keith Hancock (12–15). Maria Lamburn again on woodwinds and viola.

Meridian / Greenfingers (The EP Collection) (2016, Modern Classics Recordings): Reissue produced by Matt Sullivan and Patrick McCarthy. Liner notes by Hayward, Goronwy, and Rickard.

Survive The Gesture (1987, Ink Records): Hayward’s solo debut. Music and words by Hayward on all tracks except B1. Produced and arranged collectively by a large collaborating group drawn from the Camberwell Now circle and London experimentalists. Cover art by Tony Hayward.

Les Nouvelles Musiques De Chambre Volume 2 (1989, Sub Rosa): Collaboration with Italian ambient composer Gigi Masin. Also released as Les Nouvelles Musiques De Chambre 2 (different edition).

My Secret Alphabet (1993, Sub Rosa): Co-billed with Nick Doyne-Ditmas. Collaboration that became the nucleus of the Monkey Puzzle Trio. Six credited “technical assistants” including Sue Lawes, Ron Briefel, and alex x fraser.

Also contributed to Sub Rosa compilation Chaos In Expansion (1993) — six tracks produced with Nick Doyne-Ditmas.

Meridiem (1998, Materiali Sonori): Quartet with Percy Howard, Fred Frith, and Bill Laswell. Hayward plays drums and percussion; Frith plays guitar and violin; Laswell plays bass. Produced by Percy Howard. Engineer/mix: Oz Fritz.

Full Catastrophe (2000, Outer Music): Meridiem expands with Vernon Reid (guitar) and Trey Gunn (Warr guitar) added to the Meridiem nucleus. Engineering split between Oz Fritz and John Carr.

Numero D’Vol (2007, MoonJune Records): Quartet album with Soft Machine bassist Hugh Hopper, saxophonist Simon Picard, and keyboardist Steve Franklin.

White World (2010, Slowfoot Records): Monkey Puzzle Trio — Hayward, Nick Doyne-Ditmas (double bass), and Viv Dogan Corringham (voice, electronics).

HaywardxDälek (2025, Relapse Records): Co-billed collaboration with New Jersey hip-hop/noise duo Dälek.
