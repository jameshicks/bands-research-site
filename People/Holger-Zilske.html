Holger Zilske

Sep 27, 20267 min read

Holger Zilske

Berlin-based DJ, producer, and musician. Discogs artist ID: 63011. Alias: Holon Encoder. Core member of Smash TV (duo with Michael Schmidt); also records under his own name and as Yoko Duo.

Note on stub correction: An earlier stub described Zilske as part of “Toktok.” That is incorrect — Toktok (Discogs ID 9126) is a separate German project with no overlap with Zilske’s membership. His primary duo project with Michael Schmidt is Smash TV.

Biography

Early work and the Mindlab / Unit-E phase (mid-1990s)

Zilske and Schmidt began working together in the mid-1990s under various monikers that Discogs treats as a single alias cluster: Unit-E, Mindlab, Acrid Gain, and Triphex are all registered as the same entity. The earliest traceable release is the Mindlab EP (1995, Zulu 030 Records), credited to Unit-E with Holon Encoder (Zilske) and Mike Cybec (Schmidt) as co-writers. A second Zulu 030 release, Braindrop, followed in 1996. These are straightforward mid-90s Berlin techno productions with no wider distribution.

BPitch Control founding era and Smash TV (2000–2006)

By 2000 Zilske and Schmidt had formed Smash TV as their main working identity. Smash TV became one of BPitch Control’s anchoring acts from the label’s earliest years. The discography opened with Electrified (2001, BPitch Control), credited to Smash TV with both Zilske and Schmidt as producers and writers.

A key early credit outside the duo was on Ellen Allien’s Stadtkind (2001, BPitch Control), where Zilske and Schmidt co-wrote and co-produced the record alongside Allien — indicating a close working relationship with the label’s founder from the start.

In 2002 Zilske provided a remix (and additional production) for Rippin Kittin by Golden Boy & Miss Kittin — notably shared with an Ellen Allien remix, both appearing on the B-side of the French pressing.

Smash TV’s run of BPitch Control 12”s through this period includes:

  • Now (2001)
  • Rock On Boy (2001)
  • Nobody Rmxs (2002)
  • Hi-Jacked (2003)
  • Bits For Breakfast (2004) — Smash TV’s most elaborated release of the period; Zilske sang on several tracks, wrote all lyrics on his tracks, and collaborated with vocalist Raz Ohara and guitarist Kai Preussner
  • Queen Of Men (Rmx) (2004)
  • Tech-Tech-Talk (2005) — heavily pressed across 14+ editions/pressings
  • Air / Earth (2006)
  • Yellow Asteroids (2006)

Beyond Smash TV credits, Zilske also appeared as writer/producer on BPitch releases by other artists throughout this period: Ellen Allien’s Erdbeermund (2002), Alles Sehen (Remixe) (2003), Trashscapes (Rmxs) (2003), Berlinette (2003), Down Rmx (Part 3) (2006), and Thrills (2005), as well as contributing a co-writer credit to the Paul Kalkbrenner album I Com (2004, NovaMute/EMI).

A credit as “Music By, Producer” on Spaceship [Fly Away] (2000, B.I.G.) sits slightly outside the BPitch orbit — this is an early outlier.

Solo work on Playhouse (2006–2009)

From 2006 Zilske began releasing solo material under his own name on Playhouse, a Frankfurt label closely associated with Kompakt’s orbit. Three solo releases:

  • Rainshower EP (2006, Playhouse) — a collaboration billed as “Holger Zilske Meets Dave DK” (real name: David Krasemann, Discogs ID 10890), both credited as producers and writers
  • Enduro Disco (2007, Leena Music) — sole production credit, released on Leena Music (a sublabel of Mobilee)
  • Holz (2009, Playhouse) — solo LP/EP, with one track co-produced and co-written with August Landelius
  • Mes Yeux (2009, Playhouse) — solo, credited to Zilske as primary artist

Late Smash TV and Get Physical (2008–2012)

Smash TV resumed activity with Locomotive Breath (2008, BPitch Control) and expanded beyond BPitch for the first time as a primary act. Steroids To Heaven (2011, Get Physical Music) introduced Kai Preussner as a co-producer and writer alongside Zilske, indicating an evolution of the Smash TV line-up. World Wide Wet (2011, Leena Music) followed on Mobilee’s sublabel. Matthew Pervert (2012, Get Physical Music) continued the Get Physical relationship.

A co-producer credit on the BPitch Ellen Allien release Sool (2008) continued the long-running collaboration with the label’s founder.

Yoko Duo (2010–2013)

In 2010 Zilske formed Yoko Duo with Swedish musician August Landelius (Discogs ID 1418634), who sang on the project’s records. Yoko Duo released two records on Fauxpas Musik:

  • Behaving Like A Widower (2011)
  • Remixes (2013)

Both releases featured Freak Fabric for artwork and Christoph Grote-Beverborg for mastering.

Open Questions

  • What is the Holon Encoder alias used for specifically? The 1995 Mindlab EP lists “Holon Encoder” (artist ID 839633) and “Mike Cybec” as the credited names — both are Zilske and Schmidt’s respective aliases. Zilske’s artist page on Discogs lists Holon Encoder as an alias but it does not appear as a primary credit on any standalone releases in the database.
  • Zilske’s personal biography is thin in the DB (profile: “German DJ and producer”). Active period appears to tail off after 2013 in the Discogs data — no releases found after the Yoko Duo Remixes EP (2013).
  • The “Smash TV (Holger)” name variation on Discogs suggests the duo continued in some form with different lineups; Kai Preussner replaced (or joined alongside) Michael Schmidt by 2011.
