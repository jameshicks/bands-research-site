Steve Wynn

Oct 03, 20262 min read

Steve Wynn

Discogs ID: 179560
Instrument: Guitar, vocals
Origin: Los Angeles, California
Scene: Paisley Underground

Co-founder and primary songwriter of The Dream Syndicate (1981–1989, 2012–present). Wynn formed the Dream Syndicate with Kendra Smith, Karl Precoda, and Dennis Duck in 1981. Prolific solo artist and collaborator; also a member of The Hard Quartet (with J Mascis, Graham Maby, Linda Pitmon) and other projects.
