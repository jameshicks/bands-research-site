Overview

Han Buhrs is a Dutch vocalist and musician whose recorded career stretches back to 1982, working at the intersection of free improvisation, experimental music, and avant-garde art rock in the Netherlands. He has been a member of Ex Orkest, Ig Henneman Tentet, Motobs, Houtkamp’s Pow3, and Encore Plus Grande, with his most sustained network of collaboration running through The Ex and its extended circle — Luc Ex, Andy Moor, GW Sok, and Katrin and Terrie Ex each share eight or more releases with him. His credits span labels including Eksakt Records, BVHAAST, Ex Records, and Bimhuis Records, reflecting a long career tied closely to Amsterdam’s improvised and experimental music institutions.
