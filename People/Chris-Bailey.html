Chris Bailey

Born: 22 July 1957, Nanyuki, Kenya
Died: 9 April 2022, Haarlem, The Netherlands
Real name: Christopher James Mannix Bailey
Discogs artist ID: 100998

Australian singer, songwriter, guitarist, and record producer. Co-founder and long-running frontman of The Saints (2). Widely recognised as a co-creator of punk music — The Saints released “(I’m) Stranded” out of Brisbane in September 1976, the first punk single released outside the US/UK. Bailey led or reformed the band through numerous lineups across five decades, while also pursuing solo work and collaborative projects.

Biography

Origins (1957–1973)

Bailey was born in Nanyuki, Kenya, and grew up in Belfast, Northern Ireland until around age seven, when his family emigrated to Australia, settling in Brisbane. He did not grow up in a music centre — Brisbane in the early 1970s was relatively isolated — which may partly explain the fierce, self-reliant quality of The Saints’ early recordings.

The Saints — Formation and “(I’m) Stranded” Era (1973–1978)

The Saints formed in Brisbane circa 1973. The founding core was Bailey (guitar, vocals), Ed Kuepper (guitar), and Ivor Hay (drums). Bassist Kym Bradshaw completed the first recording lineup.

The band self-released “(I’m) Stranded” as a single in September 1976 on their own Fatal Records (2). It attracted attention from the UK press and led to a deal with Harvest (EMI). The band relocated to London.

Debut album lineup: Bailey (vocals), Kuepper (guitar), Hay (drums), Bradshaw (bass)

The Harvest years produced three albums in rapid succession:

The Saints after Kuepper — New Rose Period (1979–1985)

After Kuepper’s departure, Bailey rebuilt the band and relocated between Europe and Australia. The band signed with French indie New Rose Records, which released several albums throughout the early 1980s.

Bailey also released his first credited solo record: What We Did On Our Holidays (1984, New Rose Records) — a solo release produced by Bailey.

Casablanca (1983, New Rose) is the first release in Bailey’s discography credited to him as a primary/solo artist (not The Saints).

Mushroom Years — Mainstream Push (1985–1989)

The Saints signed with Mushroom Records (Australia) and Polydor (UK), with US releases on TVT Records. This was the band’s most commercially ambitious period, producing several charting singles in Australia.

Key members in this era: Bailey, Ivor Hay (drums, returned), Richard Burgman (guitar), Arturo Larizza (multi-instrumentalist). All Fools Day (1986) was produced by Hugh Jones.

Solo — Demons and the Memphis Sessions (1990–1991)

Bailey’s most notable solo album, Demons (1990, Mushroom/Sweden), was recorded in Memphis with producer Jim Gaines. The sessions used a range of Memphis session players: Jack Holder (guitar), Harry Peel (drums), Sam Shoup (bass), Joe Ciafalo (keyboards), Jim Spake (saxophone), Lester Snell (strings), Tony Thomas (piano). The album drew on Bailey’s soul and R&B influences.

He also released Bridges (1991, Mushroom), a solo LP.

Solo and Saints in Europe — New Rose & French Connections (1992–1995)

Bailey moved to continental Europe (primarily the Netherlands and France), where he maintained a loyal following. Savage Entertainment (1992, New Rose Records) was a solo record credited under his name. He reunited The Saints for activity through the mid-1990s.

54 Days At Sea (1994, Mushroom) is a particularly noted solo LP, recorded in Sweden with a Scandinavian ensemble: Magnus Börjeson (bass, keyboards), Victor Salazar (charango), Oscar Salazar (panpipes), Eddie Nyström (guitar), Berndt Andersson (3) (piano), Stellan Colt (drums). Produced by Bailey and Martin Hennel.

Chris Bailey & The General Dog (2000s)

By the 2000s Bailey was based in the Netherlands. He led The Saints under the working band name Chris Bailey & The General Dog, a stable lineup featuring Caspar Wijnberg (bass, organ), Peter Wilkinson (2) (drums), and Adam Bar-Pereg (piano). This lineup recorded:

Reunions and Final Years (2007–2022)

A notable 2007 reunion at Brisbane’s Pig City festival brought Bailey together with original members Kuepper and Ivor Hay. Recorded and released as Live At Pig City Brisbane 2007 (2009, Shock).

The Saints continued through the 2010s. King Of The Sun (2012, Highway125) and a double EP King Of The Sun / King Of The Midnight Sun (2014, Fire Records) appeared on Fire Records.

In 2011 Bailey recorded the collaborative album Stranger (Vicious Circle) with French artist H-Burns (Renaud Brustlein).

He contributed chorus vocals to Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds’ Nocturama (2012, Mute).

Bailey died 9 April 2022 in Haarlem, aged 64.

Posthumous: Long March Through The Jazz Age (2025, Fire Records) was released by The Saints after Bailey’s death. Bailey had contributed keyboards, vocals, guitar, bass, and acoustic guitar to the sessions. Additional contributions by Davey Lane (guitar), Sean Carey (guitar, banjo, mellotron), and others.
