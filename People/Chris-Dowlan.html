Chris Dowlan

Guitarist. Discogs artist ID: 3405049. Member of Memorial (2) (2008–2011), an American emo/melodic hardcore band from Virginia.

Biography

Chris Dowlan is documented in Discogs solely through his membership in Memorial (2), an American band that released material between 2008 and 2011. He is credited as a guitarist on the band’s 2008 12” vinyl The Creative Process / Berlin (Flight Plan Records, FPR-005) — the only Discogs release where individual personnel are listed.

Memorial (2) formed around a rhythm section drawn from The Silent Type, the Richmond, Virginia post-rock/folk band: bassist Billy Mutter and drummer Jared Laine both moved from The Silent Type into Memorial (2). Dowlan, along with Pete Appleby and Brendan Murphy, filled out the guitar section. The band operated under the genre tags Emo, Hard Rock, and Melodic Hardcore — a noticeably heavier register than The Silent Type.

The The Creative Process / Berlin LP was produced by Pete Appleby and by Chris Dowhan (Discogs ID 1133489), who also recorded the record. The name “Chris Dowhan” may be a Discogs spelling variant of Chris Dowlan’s own name, though this is unconfirmed.

Memorial (2)‘s Wikipedia URL (linked from the Discogs page) uses the slug Memorial_(band), and the Facebook URL contains memorialva, consistent with a Virginia base. Beyond these circumstantial details, no biographical information is available in Discogs for Dowlan individually.
