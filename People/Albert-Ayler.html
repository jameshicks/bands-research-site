Albert Ayler

Free jazz tenor saxophonist; born July 13, 1936, Cleveland, Ohio; died November 25, 1970. Discogs artist ID: 202309

Overview

One of the most radical voices in 1960s free jazz, known for ecstatic, hymn-like themes dissolved into torrential improvisation. Recorded extensively for ESP-Disk’ before signing to Impulse!. Core collaborators included his brother Donald Ayler (trumpet), Gary Peacock, Henry Grimes, Alan Silva, Call Cobbs, and Milford Graves.
