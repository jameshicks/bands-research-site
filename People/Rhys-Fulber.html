Rhys Fulber

Producer, composer, and electronic musician. Discogs artist ID: 10658. Real name: Nowell Rhys Fulber. Based in Canada. Core member of Front Line Assembly alongside Bill Leeb; also worked extensively in Delerium, Cyberaktif, Noise Unit, and solo. Solo releases on Sonic Groove (Germany).
