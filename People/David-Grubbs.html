David Grubbs

Discogs ID: 56194

Chicago-area guitarist, composer, and academic. Member of Bastro, Gastr del Sol, and solo artist. Also teaches at Brooklyn College.

Adam Pierce (Mice Parade) played drums and percussion on A Guess At The Riddle (FATCD33, FatCat Records, 2004).

Codeine / Slowcore Connection

Grubbs has a direct, documented Discogs credit on Codeine’s catalog — he is not merely scene-adjacent.

  • The White Birch (Codeine, 1994, Sub Pop) — Grubbs credited as Guitar on tracks 6 and 8. The other credited guitarist is John Engle; drummer is Doug Scharin. This is Codeine’s final album.
  • What About The Lonely- (Codeine, 2013, Numero Group) — Grubbs again credited as Guitar on tracks 6 & 7. This is a career retrospective/compilation; Scharin and Stephen Immerwahr also credited.

Beyond Codeine, no shared releases with Low, Bedhead, or Ida (3) appear in Discogs.

The Codeine connection is through Doug Scharin (Codeine’s drummer), who also co-appears with Grubbs on the 2013 Numero Group compilation. Scharin is a recurring figure in the post-slowcore underground (he went on to HiM and Rex).
