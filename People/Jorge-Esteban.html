Overview

Jorge Benito Esteban Moreno is a recording engineer, mixer, and producer whose Discogs credits run to 196 entries from 1984 onward. He is most closely identified with the Ramones circle — sharing credits on 89 releases with the band and logging extensive further work alongside producer Daniel Rey, Joe Blaney, and individual members Dee Dee and Joey Ramone — suggesting a long-running role as a trusted studio hand in their orbit. Beyond punk, his catalogue ranges into industrial and experimental territory, and his label history spans Sire, Columbia, Rhino Records, PVC, and the Chilean imprint Alerce, with activity continuing through the mid-2020s.

Notes (web)

Esteban / Beauvoir working relationship — origin

The collaboration between Esteban and Jean Beauvoir originated through the Ramones, specifically on Animal Boy (1986). Wikipedia’s credits for that album list Esteban as “engineer (Intergalactic)” and Beauvoir as producer and mixer. Recording took place at Intergalactic Studios, New York City (basic tracks) and Polar Studios, Stockholm (lead vocals). Esteban’s role was at the New York sessions only. Wikipedia — Animal Boy, as of 2026-08-25

Beauvoir’s only other Ramones production work is Brain Drain (1989, tracks 7 and 12), but Esteban has no engineer credit on that album — Brain Drain was engineered by Robert Musso, Martin Bisi, Fernando Kral, and Don Peterkofsky. Wikipedia — Brain Drain (album), as of 2026-08-25

The third Ramones album in this period, Halfway to Sanity (1987), was produced by Daniel Rey and the Ramones — not by Beauvoir. Esteban is credited as engineer on that album but Beauvoir is not involved. Wikipedia — Halfway to Sanity, as of 2026-08-25

Conclusion: The Esteban/Beauvoir relationship is rooted in the Ramones circle, with Animal Boy (1986) at Intergalactic Studios as the documented origin point. Their 56 shared Discogs credits extend well beyond these Ramones releases — Esteban also appears on Beauvoir’s own album Jacknifed as engineer — suggesting the relationship that began on Animal Boy continued into Beauvoir’s solo and production work independently of the Ramones.

Esteban career background

Per his SoundBetter profile, Esteban began as a studio assistant at Intergalactic Studios, New York City. His first significant engineering work was sessions for Ron Wood and Link Wray. He credits producer Giorgio Gomelsky as a key influence on his wanting to move into production. He has over 35 years in the industry. SoundBetter profile
