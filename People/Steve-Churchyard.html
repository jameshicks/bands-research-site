Steve Churchyard

Engineer and record producer. Discogs artist ID: 114779

Worked extensively with The Stranglers from the late 1970s through the early 1980s — engineer on The Raven (1979), engineer and mix on The Gospel According to the Meninblack (1981), producer on La Folie (1981) and Feline (1982). Also worked with other artists in the post-punk/new wave orbit.
