Bill Wells

Scottish pianist, bassist, guitarist, and composer. Born in Falkirk, c.1963. Based in Glasgow. Discogs artist ID: 14393. Active 1983–2025.

Overview

Bill Wells is a central figure in the Glasgow experimental music scene, operating simultaneously across jazz improvisation, chamber pop, song accompaniment, and avant-pop electronics. He plays piano, bass, guitar, sampler, autoharp, melodica, and electric piano. His catalog is built primarily through collaborative projects and ensemble formations rather than conventional solo records.

His two Falkirk-born collaborators form a notable thread: Aidan Moffat (Arab Strap) in the Bill Wells & Aidan Moffat duo (2011–2015), and Future Pilot A.K.A.’s Sushil K. Dade (based in Glasgow) on the 1998 Octet joint LP. Wells also worked closely with Isobel Campbell (ex-Belle & Sebastian) on a 2002 Billie Holiday tribute for Creeping Bent, and with Stevie Jackson (Belle & Sebastian) as a regular session collaborator and Bill Wells Trio member.
