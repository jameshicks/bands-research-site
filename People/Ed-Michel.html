American musician, engineer, producer, and record company executive from Los Angeles, California born in 1936. Mainly produced jazz and blues recordings since 1957. Collaborations with many jazz musicians, from Louis Armstrong to John Coltrane, Dave Brubeck, Chet Baker and Miles Davis. Also credited as photographer.

Started in industry as an assistant engineer to Richard Bock and Val Valentin. Joined Muntz Stereo-Pak, Inc. as Associate Director of the Music Department in February, 1966. He left do freelance producing and joined ABC-Dunhill Records, Inc. in early 1969 as their jazz and blues specialist. After Bob Thiele left to establish Flying Dutchman, Michel took over management of Impulse!, Bluesway and Apt Records in 1970. He was also Chief Producer and A&R Director for those labels until 1975. After leaving ABC, he did freelance production work, often in partnership with Eric Miller (6), notably at A&M’s Horizon (3), Warner Bros. Records, Artists House, and Fantasy’s sub-labels Galaxy and Contemporary Records.

Overview

Ed Michel is a Los Angeles-based producer, engineer, and label executive born in 1936 who began in the industry as an assistant engineer to Richard Bock and Val Valentin, accumulating credits in jazz and blues from 1957 onward. He joined ABC-Dunhill in 1969 as their jazz and blues specialist, then took over stewardship of Impulse!, Bluesway, and Apt Records following Bob Thiele’s departure in 1970, serving as Chief Producer and A&R Director across those labels until 1975. After leaving ABC he worked freelance — often alongside Eric Miller — at Horizon, Warner Bros., Artists House, and Fantasy’s Galaxy and Contemporary sub-labels. His nearly 2,900 Discogs credits, concentrated at Impulse!/ABC but extending through RCA, Galaxy, and Contemporary, span free jazz, post-bop, modal jazz, and soul-jazz, with top associations including John and Alice Coltrane, Pharoah Sanders, Charlie Haden, and B.B. King.
