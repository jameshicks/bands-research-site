Gary Hudgins

Discogs ID: 131723 Real name: Gary C. Hudgins Also billed as: Gary “Hudge” Hudgins, Gary Hudge, G. Hudgins, Hudge, Gary “Fruity” Hudge Origin: Baltimore, Maryland, US Active: 1979 – present (Discogs credits span 1979–2025) Role: Keyboardist, producer, arranger, songwriter

Baltimore-based keyboardist and producer. One of the most consistent session and production partners in Baltimore/US house music, with credits spanning the P-Funk orbit in the late 1970s through to the Quantize Recordings era in the 2010s–2020s. Member of The MuthaFunkaz and the associated project DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz. DJ Spen’s most frequently credited collaborator across the Discogs database (at least 39 co-credited releases by the time of the Quantize era), and a core contributor to the Basement Boys / BBR circle throughout the late 1990s–2000s.

Biography

Early Career — P-Funk Circuit (1979–1980)

Hudgins’s earliest documented credits place him deep inside the George Clinton extended family. He plays keyboards on two landmark P-Funk releases in 1979:

  • Brides Of Funkenstein — Never Buy Texas From A Cowboy (Atlantic, 1979) — keyboards alongside Bernie Worrell and others in the full Parliament-Funkadelic ensemble
  • Funkadelic — Uncle Jam Wants You (Warner Bros., 1979) — keyboards; the P-Funk landmark LP credited to Funkadelic featuring Bootsy Collins, Eddie Hazel, Dennis Chambers, and the entire P-Funk cast

He also appears on Wynne Jammin’ (Uncle Jam, 1980), a release on George Clinton’s own imprint. These credits establish that Hudgins was a working part of the P-Funk studio circle in its final Warner Bros. phase.

Mid-Career — Session Work (1991–1997)

Through the early 1990s Hudgins worked as a keyboard session player across R&B and dance:

  • Crystal Waters — 100% Pure Love (A&M PM, 1994) — organ solo; produced by The Basement Boys (Sean Spencer and Teddy Douglas). This is the production environment he would inhabit for most of the next decade.
  • Additional keyboards on Mercury and Columbia projects through this period (credits listed below)

Baltimore House Core — Basement Boys Records (1996–2003)

The center of Hudgins’s creative work through the late 1990s and into the 2000s is Basement Boys Records (BBR), the Baltimore imprint run by DJ Spen and Teddy Douglas. He became one of the label’s most relied-upon keyboard contributors and co-writers.

Key BBR credits:

  • Kenny Bobien — Let Me Show You (Soundmen On Wax, 1998) — piano, organ, synth; DJ Spen & Karizma remix; with Irvin Madden on bass and Wayne Cooper on guitar. One of Baltimore house’s landmark deep gospel-house recordings.
  • DJ Spen Presents Jasper Street Co. — God Helps Those (Who Help Themselves) (BBR, 1999) — keyboards (billed as “Gary Hudge”); produced by DJ Spen and Teddy Douglas; with The New Jersey Mass Choir and Martin Wilson on vocals
  • DJ Spen Presents Three Fierce Divaz (Fierce (2)) — Sweet Love (The DJ Spen & Karizma Remixes) (BBR, 1999) — keyboards
  • Byron Stingily — Till I Found U (BBR, 1999) — keyboards, co-writer
  • Various BBR releases 2000–2003: Gotta Keep Tryin, Last Dance, Lift Every Voice (Take Me Away), Praisin His Name, Sympathy For The Devil, Another Day, Best Of Me

At BBR, Hudgins frequently worked in production teams anchored by DJ Spen and Teddy Douglas. His keyboard contributions are credited across deep gospel-house, soulful house, and uplifting dance — BBR’s signature aesthetic of “uplifting dance music fused with Gospel, Jazz, and 70s Disco.”

Code Red Recordings and DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz (2004–2010)

From 2004, Hudgins’s primary production home shifted to Code Red Recordings, the Baltimore label co-run by DJ Spen and Gary Deane. This is the period in which his collective with Deane and Irvin Madden — The MuthaFunkaz — became an active release entity, and the combined project DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz charted releases on Defected Records and Code Red.

Key credits from this period:

  • DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz — Rock The Bellz (Code Red Recordings, 2004) — keyboards; The Best I Can (Code Red Recordings, 2004) — primary artist; Holy Ghost (Code Red Recordings, 2005)
  • DJ Spen Proudly Presents Byron Stingily — It’s All Jesus (Spiritually Sound Records / Code Red, 2004) — producer, arranger, composer, writer; with Gary Deane and Irvin Madden, a full MuthaFunkaz production team. Spiritually Sound Records is a Code Red sublabel.
  • DJ Spen Presents Angie Stone — Gabryelle (Defected, 2005) — co-producer; the Defected release that brought the DJ Spen / MuthaFunkaz axis to a wider UK audience
  • The Way You Love Me (Hustle Music, 2005 / Defected, 2006–2008) — keyboards across multiple pressings; The MuthaFunkaz credited as additional producer
  • Church Lady (Defected, 2007) — keyboards
  • Given Me Joy (Defected, 2008) — arranged by (backing vocals, keyboards, strings); writer; DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz presenter credit
  • Reach Out For Love (Defected, 2008) — arranged by; writer; DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz presenter

The Defected deal represents the peak of the DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz brand internationally — the group released two artist albums on Defected in 2008 and a string of EP/single releases.

He also played on:

  • Changed My Life (BPM King Street Sounds, 2009) — instruments, arranged vocals, writer
  • Under My Skin (DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz Remixes) (BPM King Street Sounds, 2009) — keyboards

Quantize Recordings Era (2012–present)

From 2013 onward, Quantize Recordings becomes the primary outlet. Hudgins is a recurring producer and keyboard player on the label across a wide range of vocal house, soulful house, and gospel-influenced releases.

Selected Quantize credits:

  • Dawn Tallman — Believe In Love (Quantize, 2013) — keyboards, producer, writer; co-produced with DJ Spen and Thommy Davis
  • DJ Spen & The Supernatural Communication EP (Quantize, 2013) — keyboards, horns (additional), writer
  • DJ Spen & John Morales Presents Spencer Morales & Randy Roberts (4) — Without Your Love (Quantize, 2016) — keyboards, music/arrangements, writer; a full studio collaboration with John Morales
  • Barbara Tucker — Think (About It) (Quantize, 2017) — keyboards; produced by DJ Spen & Thommy Davis
  • Randy Roberts (4) & Richard Burton (4) — Stairway To Heaven (Quantize, 2017) — keyboards
  • S.E.L & Gary Hudgins — The Sweetest Pain (Quantize, 2018) — primary artist (sole primary release as co-lead); with DJ Spen remix
  • Spencer Morales ft. Tasha LaRae — I Need Your Lovin’ (Quantize, 2018) — keyboards, producer; with DJ Spen and John Morales
  • Thommy Davis & Tracy Hamlin — Isn’t It A Shame (Quantize, 2019) — keyboards, producer; with DJ Spen
  • DJ Spen Presents — Wish I Didn’t Miss You (Quantize, 2019) — keyboards, orchestrated by
  • Hot Shot (Quantize, 2020) — instruments, producer
  • Try Jah Love (Quantize, 2020) — keyboards, bass
  • Get Here (Quantize, 2022) — keyboards, producer
  • Until You (Quantize, 2022) — keyboards, writer
  • Better (DJ Spen & Gary Hudgins Remixes) (Quantize, 2023) — remix
  • Shout (The DJ Spen and Gary Hudgins Mixes) (Quantize, 2025) — keyboards, producer, mixed by, arranged by

Hudgins also began co-crediting his name directly in the billing for DJ Spen releases during the 2010s–2020s, appearing as “DJ Spen & Gary Hudgins” on remixes and productions for Morehouse Records (2012), Maurice Joshua Digital (2014), SoulHouse Music (2017), Rhemi Music (2018), and Quantize itself.
