Spencer Clark

American experimental musician and label operator. Discogs artist ID: 404319. Primary alias: Monopoly Child Star Searchers (his most-documented project name). Runs the Pacific City label family (Pacific City Sound Visions, Pacific City Discs). Co-founder of The Skaters (with James Ferraro). Active 2004–present.

Aliases (per Discogs)

Monopoly Child Star Searchers, Monopoly Child, Monopoly Child Nightlife, Monopoly Child’s Nightlife Band, Star Searchers, Typhonian Highlife, Vodka Soap, Charles Berlitz, CB Cenobyte, Black Joker, HR Giger’s Studiolo, Fourth World Magazine, Melon Brains.

Groups
