Laurie Anderson

Real name: Laura Phillips Anderson. Born 5 June 1947, Glen Ellyn, Illinois. Performance artist, composer, violinist, vocalist.

Overview

One of the central figures in American avant-garde performance art since the early 1970s. Anderson’s work integrates violin, electronics, voice, film, sculpture, and spoken word. Though rooted in the visual arts (she studied at Barnard and Columbia), she crossed into popular music in 1981 when “O Superman” reached #2 on the UK charts — an eight-minute avant-pop piece built on a vocoder loop — and was immediately signed to Warner Bros. Records.

Her downtown NYC presence connects her to the same circles as Peter Gordon, Arthur Russell, Glenn Branca, and experimental scene more broadly. She collaborated with Peter Gabriel and William S. Burroughs on Mister Heartbreak (1984) and appeared on compilations across the art/avant-garde boundary.

Married Lou Reed in 2008; he died in 2013.
