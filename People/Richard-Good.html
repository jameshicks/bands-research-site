Richard Good

Full name: Richard Edward Henry Good
Born: 17 June 1974, Crawley, West Sussex, England, UK
Role: Guitarist, graphic designer
Discogs ID: 699531
Name variations: Rich Good, Good

Overview

Richard Good is a British guitarist and graphic designer based primarily in the US indie-rock scene. He plays guitar and has done extensive visual work — sleeve design, layout, photography — across the California indie circuit. His bands span a Joanna Newsom–adjacent Bay Area folk-rock world (The Pleased, Golden Shoulders) and the reformed UK post-punk institution The Psychedelic Furs. The combination is unusual: a Crawley-born musician who became embedded in San Francisco’s early-2000s bohemian scene and then, in his mid-forties, crossed back to contribute to the Furs’ long-delayed comeback album.

Career

Early 2000s — Bay Area indie: The Pleased and Golden Shoulders

Good arrived in the US indie scene around 2002. His first documented project is The Pleased, a Bay Area band whose only known release is the cassette/self-released compilation track One piece from the middle (Not On Label, 2002). The Pleased’s lineup is notable: alongside Good on guitar/vocals, it featured Joanna Newsom on keyboards, Noah Georgeson on guitar/vocals, and Luckey Remington on bass. This places Good at the early intersection of the Nevada City/Bay Area folk-rock community that would soon coalesce around Newsom’s Drag City career.

Simultaneously or shortly after, Good joined Golden Shoulders as lead guitarist. Golden Shoulders was a San Francisco indie-rock outfit led by singer-songwriter Adam Kline and featuring an overlapping cast: Todd Roper (drums), and guests including Joanna Newsom, Josh Klinghoffer, and Neal Morgan. Good played lead guitar on their debut Let My Burden Be (Doppler Records, 2002) and remained in the band through the 2019 album Could This Be the End (Golden Shoulders, self-released), on which he contributes electric guitar on one track (track 6).

2020 — Made Of Rain with The Psychedelic Furs

Good joined the reformed The Psychedelic Furs lineup for Made Of Rain (Cooking Vinyl, 2020), the band’s first studio album in 29 years (since World Outside, 1991). He is credited as Rich Good on the sleeve and plays guitar alongside the core Furs: Richard Butler (vocals), Tim Butler (bass), Paul Garisto (drums/percussion), Amanda Kramer (keyboards), and Mars Williams (saxophone). Richard Fortus served as additional producer (alongside Tim Palmer and the band collectively). The album was released in late July 2020.

2020, 2024 — Los Days

Good also contributed as a session guitarist to two albums by Los Days (3), the project of San Francisco skater-musician Tommy Guerrero and Josh Lippi:

  • Singing Sands (Too Good, 2020) — performer on tracks 11 and 12
  • Dusty Dreams (Too Good, 2024) — guitar on tracks A6 and B6

Both records have an instrumental/ambient-funk character consistent with Guerrero’s broader solo work.
