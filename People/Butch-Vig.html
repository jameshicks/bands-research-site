Butch Vig

Producer, engineer, drummer. Discogs artist ID: 42640. Born Bryan David Vigorson, 2 August 1955, Viroqua, Wisconsin. Co-founder and drummer of Garbage. Co-owner of Smart Studios, Madison, Wisconsin (1983–2010).

Biography

Wisconsin Roots and Smart Studios (1979–1990)

Vig began his career as a drummer in Madison, Wisconsin. His earliest Discogs-documented appearance is on Cruel School (1979, BOAT Records & Tapes) as a drummer/vocalist. In the early 1980s he played drums and sang on Every Corner Dance (1982, Mountain Railroad Records) with Spooner, and on In The Heart Of The Heart Country (1986, BOAT Records) and The Good Life (1989, Atlantic) with Fire Town, both Wisconsin-based bands. He also co-wrote songs for Fire Town’s The Good Life album.

By 1983 he had co-founded Smart Studios in Madison, Wisconsin (with Steve Marker), which became the base for nearly all of his production and engineering work through 2010. His first production credits appear on the same year: Acceptance (1983, Bone Air) and the following year Intellectuals Are The Shoeshine Boys Of The Ruling Elite (1984, Bone Air).

Through the mid-to-late 1980s Vig built a regional reputation producing and engineering bands on Touch And Go Records and adjacent labels. Key credits from this period:

  • Die Kreuzen — Century Days (1988), Gone Away (1989), For Ladies Only (1989), Twelve Point Buck (1989)
  • Killdozer — Snakeboy (1985), Burl (1986), Little Baby Buntin’ (1987), Merry Go Round (1987)
  • Tar — Jackson / Clincher (1989–1990)
  • Urge Overkill — Americruiser (1990)
  • The Jesus Lizard — Pure / Head EPs
  • Tad — 8-Way Santa (1991, Sub Pop) — engineer and producer
  • Cosmic Psychos — Blokes You Can Trust (1991, Survival), later Uncompromising War on Art (1994)
  • Mudhoney — Glue / Roadmouth (1990, Sub Pop)
  • Soul Asylum — Hang Time (1988, Twin-Tone) and several follow-ups
  • Wisconsin Hayride (1992, Columbia) — compilation he engineered/mixed/produced

By 1990–91 his production was consistently reaching national/international labels.

Nevermind and Breakthrough (1991–1992)

Vig produced and engineered Nirvana’s Nevermind (1991, DGC/Sub Pop), the album that broke alternative rock into the mainstream. Recorded primarily at Sound City Studios, Van Nuys, California, with mixing by Andy Wallace. The album also carried a co-production credit to Nirvana itself (Kurt Cobain, Dave Grohl, Krist Novoselic); mastering by Howie Weinberg. Nevermind became one of the best-selling albums of the decade. Individual singles — “Smells Like Teen Spirit,” “Come As You Are,” “In Bloom,” “Lithium” — all carried his producer/engineer credit.

Earlier in 1991 he had produced the Smashing Pumpkins’ debut Gish (Caroline Records), which also gave him entrée into that camp.

Also in 1992, Vig produced L7’s Bricks Are Heavy (Slash Records), engineering and co-producing with the band; Steve Marker (future Garbage collaborator) assisted. The same year he produced and engineered Sonic Youth’s Dirty (DGC), with Andy Wallace mixing most tracks and Vig mixing the closing track “Youth Against Fascism” himself.

Siamese Dream and Peak Alt-Rock Production (1993–1994)

Siamese Dream (1993, Hut/Virgin) with the Smashing Pumpkins was Vig’s second landmark album, co-produced with Billy Corgan and mixed by Alan Moulder and Corgan/Vig. Vig also co-arranged the string parts with Corgan. The album spawned several UK singles — “Cherub Rock,” “Today,” “Disarm,” “Rocket” — all with his production credit.

Also 1993: produced Sonic Youth’s Sugar Kane single (DGC). In 1994 Sonic Youth credited him on Experimental Jet Set, Trash And No Star in an unorthodox engineer/mixing role (listed as “Super Sound Capturing, Earth Tweaking, Dennis Adjusting / Speed Mixing”). He also worked on tracks for Babes In Toyland (Pumpkin Seeds, 1993 Australian single) and continued issuing compilation and catalog material through DGC.

Also in 1994: produced Depeche Mode single In Your Room (Mute) — engineer, mix, and additional production credit.

Founding Garbage (1993–2001)

Around 1993, Vig, Steve Marker, and Duke Erikson — all Madison-based musicians — began experimenting with drum machine and sample-heavy rock. They recruited Scottish vocalist Shirley Manson (formerly of Goodbye Mr. Mackenzie / Angelfish (2)). The band recorded their debut at Smart Studios.

Garbage (Discogs artist ID: 12332) debut Garbage (1995, Mushroom/BMG) — Vig as drummer, programmer, and effects. The album produced the singles “Vow,” “Subhuman,” “Only Happy When It Rains,” “Queer,” “Stupid Girl,” and “Milk.” Label: Mushroom in Europe/Australia, Almo Sounds in the US.

Version 2.0 (1998, Mushroom) was a commercial peak, producing “Push It,” “I Think I’m Paranoid,” “Special,” “When I Grow Up,” and “You Look So Fine.” During this period Vig also mixed the Ash album Nu-Clear Sounds (1998, DreamWorks).

Beautifulgarbage (2001, Mushroom/[PIAS]) — Vig co-wrote and co-produced throughout; singles included “Androgyny,” “Cherry Lips,” “Breaking Up The Girl,” “Shut Your Mouth.”

Bleed Like Me (2005, Geffen) — Vig credited as mixer, drummer, guitarist, effects, and programmer; last album before an extended hiatus.

Production Work Continues (2003–2011)

While active with Garbage through 2005, Vig continued outside production work:

  • AFI — Sing The Sorrow (2003, DreamWorks) — co-produced with Jerry Finn; Decemberunderground (2006, Interscope) — produced tracks 13–14 (remaining tracks produced by Finn)
  • Against Me! — New Wave (2007, Sire), White Crosses (2010, Sire)
  • Jimmy Eat World — Chase This Light (2007, Interscope), Invented (2010, Interscope), Damage (2013)
  • Green Day — 21st Century Breakdown (2009, Reprise/Warner), produced with the band; followed by single/EP credits including “Know Your Enemy,” “21 Guns,” “Last of the American Girls”
  • Foo Fighters — Wasting Light (2011, RCA/Roswell), recorded entirely on analog tape in Dave Grohl’s garage; mixed by Alan Moulder
  • Metric — Synthetica (2012)

Garbage Returns and Later Work (2012–present)

Garbage reconvened for Not Your Kind of People (2012, Stun Volume), self-released on their own Stun Volume label, followed by singles and the EP/remix cycle. Strange Little Birds (2016, BMG/Stun Volume), No Gods No Masters (2021, Infectious Music/Stun Volume), and Let All That We Imagine Be The Light (2025, Infectious Music/Stun Volume) continued the band’s run.

Vig also produced the Foo Fighters documentary/soundtrack Sound City: Real to Reel (2013, RCA/Roswell) and individual Foo Fighters album Sonic Highways (2014, RCA/Roswell) — each chapter recorded in a different city’s legendary studio.

He has also appeared in side projects including 5 Billion In Diamonds (2017, 100% Records), Emperors Of Wyoming, and Sonic Morticians.

Garbage

Garbage — Discogs artist ID: 12332

Formed ~1993, Madison, Wisconsin. Members:

  • Butch Vig (drums, programming, loops, effects, some guitar)
  • Shirley Manson (vocals) — recruited from Goodbye Mr. Mackenzie / Angelfish
  • Steve Marker (guitar, engineer) — co-founded Smart Studios with Vig
  • Duke Erikson (guitar, keyboards) — Discogs ID: 60047; not in Discogs members field but credited individually on all releases

Released on Mushroom (UK/Europe/Australia), Almo Sounds (US, early albums), Geffen Records (Bleed Like Me), and their own Stun Volume imprint from 2012 onward.

Open Questions

  • Smart Studios history: co-founded with Steve Marker (confirmed in profile); exact founding year and other co-owners not confirmed in Discogs
  • Duke Erikson (Discogs ID: 60047, real name Douglas Elwin Erikson) is not listed in the Discogs “members” field for Garbage (which only shows Vig, Manson, Marker) — he is individually credited on every Garbage release, so this appears to be a Discogs database gap
  • Depeche Mode In Your Room (1994, Mute) — full context of Vig’s involvement (remix vs. original production) not fully resolved; credited as engineer/mix/additional producer on the Benelux single
  • Soul Asylum connection: Let Your Dim Light Shine (1995) was his sole production credit for them — confirmed in Discogs. Earlier Twin/Tone albums (Hang Time 1988, Made To Be Broken 1986, Clam Dip 1988) are credited to other producers (Bob Mould, Ed Stasium/Lenny Kaye, the band itself). Vig’s earlier involvement is not supported by the database.
  • Fire Town / Spooner back-catalog may have additional credits not indexed under Vig’s Discogs ID
  • Butch Vig’s “Nevermind” recording location: Sound City confirmed from credits; Smart Studios also used for pre-production (not confirmed in DB)
