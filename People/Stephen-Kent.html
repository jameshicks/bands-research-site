Overview

Stephen Kent is a contemporary didgeridoo player and multi-instrumentalist whose catalogue of over 125 credits runs from the mid-1980s through the 2010s, concentrated in the ambient, tribal, experimental, and downtempo sounds of the 1990s West Coast scene. He was a member of several ensembles — most notably Beasts of Paradise, Lights In A Fat City, and Mandorla Mu — and recorded primarily for City of Tribes Records (10 works, 1992–2002) and Intuition Records (6 works, 1995–2002). His most sustained working relationships were with Kenneth Newby and Simon Tassano (19 shared releases each), with further recurring collaborations alongside Christian Jones, Eddy Sayer, and Beth Custer.
