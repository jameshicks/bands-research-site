Simon Underwood

English bassist, cellist, violinist, producer. Discogs ID: 349616. Name variations: S. Underwood, Simon, Underwood, サイモン・アンダーウッド.

Bass guitarist and producer. Best known as a founding member of The Pop Group and Pigbag. His connections to Disc O’Dell (manager, Y Records) were the direct pipeline through which Pigbag reached both the label and the Bristol scene.

The Pop Group (1977–1980)

Underwood was a founding member of The Pop Group, the Bristol post-punk band formed in 1977 around vocalist Mark Stewart. The band’s other core members were Gareth Sager (guitar/sax), John Waddington (guitar), Bruce Smith (drums), and Dan Catsis (bass alongside Underwood).

Key releases:

  • “She Is Beyond Good And Evil” (1979, Radar Records) — debut single
  • “We Are All Prostitutes” (1979, Rough Trade) — landmark post-punk single
  • Y (1979, Radar Records) — debut album; one of the defining records of British post-punk
  • For How Much Longer Do We Tolerate Mass Murder? (1980, Rough Trade / Y Records) — second album; co-released on Disc O’Dell’s Y Records, cementing the Y Records connection
  • We Are Time (1980, Rough Trade / Y Records) — live/archival collection

The Pop Group disbanded in 1980. Underwood moved straight to Pigbag.

Pigbag (1980–1983)

Underwood joined the nascent Pigbag (then a single-song jam session around what would become “Papa’s Got A Brand New Pigbag”) after being invited by founder Chris Hamlin. He brought tenor saxophonist Ollie Moore with him, and his relationship with Disc O’Dell secured both the band’s first gig (supporting The Pop Group at Bristol’s Romeo and Juliets) and a Y Records deal the following day.

After Pigbag

Pigbag split June 1983. Underwood, Angela Jaeger, and James Johnstone formed Instinct (7), signed to Sire Records; a single was recorded but never officially released. One track, “Swamp Out,” appeared on a 1985 compilation.

Underwood and Jaeger married in 1983 at Hammersmith Registry Office; among the notable attendees was John Lydon.

Conspirators Of Pleasure

Duo with Poulomi Desai (Discogs ID: 4887843). One documented release: The Usurp Tapes, Vol. I: Species Of Spaces (2017, Impeka). Website: conspire.org.uk.

The Pop Group Revival (2010–2023)

The Pop Group reformed for a 2010 tour. Underwood is credited as a performer on the archival compilations Cabinet Of Curiosities and We Are Time (both Freaks R Us, 2014). The band released new studio material — Citizen Zombie (2015, Freaks R Us) and Honeymoon On Mars (2016) — and the anniversary campaign around Y produced Y In Dub (2021, Mute). Both Mark Stewart and John Waddington died in 2023, effectively ending the second phase.
