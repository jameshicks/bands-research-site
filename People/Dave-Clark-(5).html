Dave Clark (5)

Toronto musician and record producer. Discogs artist ID: 559508. Real name: David John Clark.

Name variations: Clarkie, D. Clark, DC, Dave, Dave “William” Clark, DaveC, David Clark, Jimmy Buford, Mr. Clark.

Wikipedia: http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Clark_(Canadian_musician)

A central figure in the 1990s Toronto indie scene. Best known as drummer for Rheostatics from 1980–1995 and currently. Also member of The Dinner Is Ruined Band and The Country Of Miracles. The “(5)” suffix distinguishes him from other Dave Clarks in the Discogs database.
