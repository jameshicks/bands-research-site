Overview

Larry Fessenden is an American filmmaker — actor, writer, director, and producer — based in New York, and founder of the independent production company Glass Eye Pix. His Discogs presence is rooted in his work with the band Just Desserts and in producing audio drama, particularly Tales From Beyond The Pale, a horror anthology series connected to Glass Eye Pix. His closest recorded collaborators are Tom Laverack and producer Wharton Tiers, with releases spread across the 1980s, 1990s, and 2010s on labels including Sojourn Records, Fire Records, and Jagjaguwar.
