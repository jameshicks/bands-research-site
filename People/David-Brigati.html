Jul 17, 20263 min read

David Brigati

Vocalist. Discogs artist ID: 501970. Brother of Eddie Brigati. Not a full member of The Rascals, but appeared as a session backing vocalist on multiple albums:

  • Groovin’ (1967) — vocal background effects
  • Once Upon a Dream (1968) — vocals on tracks A8, B6
  • Freedom Suite (1969) — backing vocals; also artwork credit (inside right gatefold)
  • Search and Nearness (1971) — backing vocals (B5)

Pre-Rascals: Joey Dee and The Starliters (1962)

David appeared as a performer on two Joey Dee releases before the Rascals era:

  • Twistin’ With Joey Dee! Volume II (1962, Roulette)
  • Twistin’ At The Peppermint Lounge With Joey Dee! Volume III (1962, Roulette)

Producer Work: The Vagrants (1967, 1987)

David co-produced (credited as “Dave Brigati”) a 1967 ATCO single by The Vagrants — “I Love, Love You (Yes I Do) / Respect” — alongside Larry Vernieri. The Vagrants featured Leslie West (later of Mountain). He also produced two tracks (A1, B5) on The Great Lost Album (1987, Arista), a posthumous Vagrants compilation, sharing producer duties with Felix Pappalardi, Shadow Morton, and others.

The Lost In The Wilderness credit is notable as it was Eddie Brigati’s solo album on Elektra (1976), on which David contributed vocal and percussive effects alongside a heavyweight cast including Steve Gadd, Phil Ramone, Arif Mardin, and Herbie Mann.

The 1991 New York Rock And Soul Revue live album (Live At The Beacon, produced by Donald Fagen and Elliot Scheiner) featured both Brigati brothers prominently alongside Donald Fagen, Michael McDonald, Boz Scaggs, and Phoebe Snow. David is listed as Featuring, Percussion, and Vocals — one of his most prominent non-Rascals appearances.
