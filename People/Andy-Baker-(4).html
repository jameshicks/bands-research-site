Bass player and recording/mixing engineer from Athens, GA. Primary engineer for The Mercury Program across all their Tiger Style releases (12 shared releases, 2000–2003). Credited on Mercury Program releases with the band’s signature deadpan language: “Alignment Of Magnetic Particles Performed By” (recording) and “Level Adjustments And All Other Preparations For Production” (mixing/mastering).
