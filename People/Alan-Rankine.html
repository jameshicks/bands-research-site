Alan Rankine

Scottish guitarist, songwriter, and producer. Discogs artist ID: 114967. Best known as co-founder and guitarist of The Associates alongside Billy Mackenzie.

Overview

Alan Rankine (born 1958, Glasgow area) formed The Associates with Billy Mackenzie in 1979. After leaving The Associates in the mid-1980s, he worked as a producer and solo artist. In 1995 he produced the Moondials EP Can You See? (Electric Honey), featuring Stevie Jackson.
