Overview

A performer, guitarist, producer and vocalist with 45 Discogs credits running from 1993 through at least 2020, Cameron Jamie’s catalogue is centred heavily in the 1990s and closely tied to the Redd Kross orbit — he shares 20 credits with the band and 16 apiece with brothers Jeff and Steven McDonald, with Brian Reitzell and engineer John Agnello rounding out the recurring cast. He was a member of Cannibal (7) and Gobbler alongside that circle. Beyond the power-pop core, his label history shows a late arc toward experimental contexts: This Way Up and Sympathy For The Record Industry gave way to Editions Mego, Shelter Press and Primary Information in the 2010s and 2020s, suggesting a broadening practice beyond guitar-band work.
