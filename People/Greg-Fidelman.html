Greg Fidelman

Producer, mixing engineer, and recording engineer. Born Greg Louis Fidelman, September 4, 1965. Discogs artist ID: 268779. Also credited as Greg Fields (Discogs ID: 1273486, same person — used during his time as guitarist in Rhino Bucket). Name variants on releases include “Fidelman”, “Greg Fiddelman”, “Greg Fiddleman”, “Grag Fidelman”.

Overview

Fidelman began in music as the lead guitarist of Los Angeles hard rock band Rhino Bucket, recording two albums for Reprise Records in the early 1990s under the credit “Greg Fields.” He transitioned to studio engineering by the mid-1990s, working as an assistant engineer at Sound City and similar LA facilities, where he entered the orbit of Rick Rubin and American Recordings. His first decade of credit work runs from small assistant slots on Weezer’s Pinkerton (1996) and Marilyn Manson’s Holy Wood (2000) through to lead engineer/recorder on Slipknot’s Vol. 3 (2004) and Metallica’s Death Magnetic (2008).

By 2005 he had crossed into full production credit, delivering Slayer’s World Painted Blood (2009), Slipknot’s Vol. 3: The Subliminal Verses (recorded/mixed), and High On Fire’s Snakes For The Divine (2010) as sole or co-producer. The peak commercial credit is Metallica’s Hardwired…To Self-Destruct (2016), which he produced and mixed alongside the band — their first studio album since Death Magnetic and their highest-charting album in decades. He continued as Metallica’s primary producer through 72 Seasons (2023).

He also holds a significant role within Metallica’s archival and catalog operations, holding executive producer credits on reissues of Kill ‘Em All, Ride The Lightning, Master Of Puppets, and …And Justice For All, and on live releases under the Blackened label.

Career by Era

Early Career — Rhino Bucket (1990–1994)

As Greg Fields, Fidelman played lead guitar on Rhino Bucket’s self-titled debut (1990, Reprise) and Get Used To It (1992, Reprise). The band also included Reeve Downes on bass and Georg Dolivo (2) on vocals. A third album, Pain, appeared in 1994 on Concrete. A 2013 live album on Acetate Records (where he is credited as both guitar and vocals) documents a later reunion.

Assistant Engineer Phase (1995–2003)

Fidelman’s engineering credits appear first on releases produced at or adjacent to Rick Rubin’s American Recordings setup. Early assistant slots include:

  • Weezer, Pinkerton (1996, Geffen) — Sound engineer alongside a large pool including Joe Barresi and Dave Fridmann; Rubin not involved here but indicates Sound City connections
  • Frank Black, The Cult Of Ray (1996, American Recordings) — assistant engineer under Rubin
  • Johnny Cash, Unchained (1996, American Recordings) — assistant engineer under Rubin
  • Marilyn Manson, Holy Wood (In The Shadow Of The Valley Of Death) (2000, Nothing Records) — engineer and Pro-Tools engineer; mixed by Dave Sardy
  • Marilyn Manson, Renegades (2000, Epic) — digital editing
  • Audioslave, Audioslave (2002, Epic/Interscope) — digital editing under Rubin
  • RHCP, Californication (1999, Warner) — additional engineering; recorded/mixed by Brendan O’Brien
  • Marilyn Manson (Nothing Records, live) — mix engineer on The Last Tour On Earth (1999)

He also worked as assistant engineer on the System Of A Down self-titled debut (1998, American Recordings), produced by Rick Rubin with Sylvia Massy as primary engineer.

Lead Engineer / Recording Phase (2004–2008)

Fidelman ascended to full engineer/recording credit on several significant rock and metal albums:

  • Slipknot, Vol. 3: (The Subliminal Verses) (2004, Roadrunner) — Recorded By, Mixed By; produced by Rick Rubin
  • Slipknot, Duality single (2004) and The Nameless (2005) — mixed
  • Jet, Get Born (2003, Capitol) — engineer
  • Life Of Agony, Broken Valley (2005, Epic) — producer, recorded by, mixed by (first prominent full-production solo credit)
  • Metallica, Death Magnetic (2008, Vertigo/Warner) — Recorded By, Mixed By (portions; Andrew Scheps mixed other portions); produced by Rick Rubin
  • Metallica, All Nightmare Long single (2008) — Recorded By, Mixed By
  • Slayer, Soundtrack To The Apocalypse (2003, American) — mixed
  • Lou Reed & Metallica, Lulu (2011, Vertigo) — Producer, Mixed By, Recorded By; co-produced with Hal Willner, Lou Reed, and Metallica

Producer / Mixing Engineer Phase (2009–present)

From World Painted Blood onward, Fidelman appears as credited producer or co-producer:

  • Slayer, World Painted Blood (2009, American Recordings) — Producer, Mixed By, Recorded By; Rick Rubin holds Executive Producer credit
  • High On Fire, Snakes For The Divine (2010, E1 Entertainment) — Producer, Mixed By, Recorded By; co-recorded with Greg Gordon
  • Gossip, Music For Men (2009, Columbia) — Recorded By, Mixed By, Additional Percussion
  • Slipknot, .5: The Gray Chapter (2014, Roadrunner) — Producer; tracks 1 and 12 mixed by Fidelman, others by Joe Barresi
  • Slipknot, We Are Not Your Kind (2019, Roadrunner) — Producer, Engineer; mixed by Joe Barresi
  • Slipknot, All Out Life single (2018) — Producer; Unsainted (2019) — Engineer, Producer
  • Metallica, Hardwired…To Self-Destruct (2016, Blackened) — Producer, Mixed By (sole production credit alongside the band)
  • Metallica, S&M2 (2020, Blackened) — Producer, Mixed By
  • Metallica, 72 Seasons (2023, Blackened) — Producer
  • Metallica, Through The Never soundtrack (2013, Blackened) — Producer, Mixed By, Recorded By
  • Johnny Cash, American V: A Hundred Highways (2006, American Recordings) — Mixed By (portions); produced by Rick Rubin
  • Slayer, reissue/compilation work via American Recordings

Additional mixing credits (non-production): Gossip Born Free (2010), Joan Osborne Bring It On Home (2012 equivalent, Music For Men era), M.I.A. Born Free (2010, Atlantic), Goo Goo Dolls Golden State (2001).

Connections

  • Rick Rubin — Fidelman’s entire mid-career assistant phase (1996–2003) ran through Rubin’s American Recordings productions; Rubin held executive producer credit on World Painted Blood (2009) when Fidelman took the producer seat for the first time on a Slayer album
  • Metallica — primary working relationship from Death Magnetic (2008) through 72 Seasons (2023); also executive producer on major catalog reissues (Blackened label)
  • Slipknot — three studio albums as producer (Vol. 3, .5, We Are Not Your Kind) plus singles; longest-running outside production relationship
  • Slayer — World Painted Blood (2009); Soundtrack To The Apocalypse (2003, mix)
  • Sylvia Massy — primary engineer on System Of A Down (1998); Fidelman was her assistant
  • Dave Sardy — primary mixer on Holy Wood (2000); Fidelman was engineer
  • Andrew Scheps — shared mixing duties on Death Magnetic (2008)
  • Joe Barresi — shared mixing duties on Slipknot .5: The Gray Chapter (2014) and We Are Not Your Kind (2019)
  • Hal Willner — co-producer on Lou Reed & Metallica’s Lulu (2011)
  • Rhino Bucket — Fidelman’s band as guitarist (1990–1994), credited as Greg Fields
  • Roadrunner United — listed as member; appeared on the 2005 Roadrunner all-star project
  • Greg Gordon — frequent assistant/recording engineer alongside Fidelman on Slipknot and High On Fire sessions
  • Sara Killion — recurrent assistant engineer in the Rubin/Fidelman chain (Death Magnetic, Lulu, .5, World Painted Blood)
  • Dan Monti — recurrent digital editor/engineer in the same orbit (Death Magnetic, Lulu, .5, We Are Not Your Kind)
  • American Recordings — primary label context for assistant engineer phase; Rubin’s label
  • Roadrunner Records — Slipknot albums; also Roadrunner United project
  • Blackened — Metallica’s own label; Fidelman holds executive producer roles on catalog reissues in addition to production credits
