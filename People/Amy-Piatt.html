Amy Piatt

Amy Piatt

Discogs artist ID: 1218139
Married name: Amy Koza
Name variations on record: Aaa-my, Aaaamy, Amy
Groups: The Mountain Goats (Discogs ID: 95417), The Bright Mountain Choir (Discogs ID: 1012113)

Occasional performer on early The Mountain Goats cassette releases (1992–1994). One of four women who appeared as a collective alongside John Darnielle during the band’s lo-fi cassette era, the others being Rachel Ware, Rosanne Lindley, and Sarah Arslanian. The same four women are the entirety of The Bright Mountain Choir, a collective entity separately credited as “Recorded By” on Zopilote Machine (1994). Beyond the Mountain Goats orbit, Piatt has one documented credit in Discogs as a session backing vocalist on a major-label record: Ben Harper’s The Will To Live (1997, Virgin).

Biography

Early Mountain Goats Cassette Era (1992–1994)

Piatt first appears in Discogs on Songs For Petronius (1992, Shrimper), credited collectively as part of “The Mountain Goats Are” — a five-person formulation used on that release in lieu of individual performer credits. The five named are Darnielle, Piatt, Ware, Lindley, and Arslanian, each listed by a first-name variation (her credit reads “Amy”). The same five-person collective credits appear on Chile De Árbol (1993, Ajax Records (2)) and Hot Garden Stomp (1993, Shrimper), where the role is listed simply as “Performer.”

On the 1994 releases — Beautiful Rat Sunset (Shrimper, Discogs release ID: 852509) and Zopilote Machine (Ajax Records, Discogs release ID: 1449873) — the credit formulation shifts to “Performer [We Are].” On Zopilote Machine specifically, The Bright Mountain Choir is additionally credited as “Recorded By,” its members being identical to the collective: Piatt, Ware, Lindley, and Arslanian. This is the only release on which the Bright Mountain Choir name appears as a distinct credited entity.

Piatt’s Mountain Goats credits end with Zopilote Machine in 1994. Unlike Rachel Ware, who continued appearing on Mountain Goats releases through 1996, Piatt does not appear on Nine Black Poppies (1995), Songs For Peter Hughes (1995), or Sweden (1996).

The Bright Mountain Choir

The Bright Mountain Choir (Discogs ID: 1012113) is a collective entity in Discogs whose members are listed as Amy Piatt, Rachel Ware, Rosanne Lindley, and Sarah Arslanian — all four women from the early Mountain Goats occasional lineup, without John Darnielle. Its sole credit in Discogs is “Recorded By” on Zopilote Machine (1994). It has no releases of its own as a primary-credited act. Whether the name referred to a specific performing or recording configuration versus being a purely administrative grouping is not resolvable from Discogs data alone.

Ben Harper Session (1997)

The one non-Mountain Goats credit in Discogs is for backing vocals on Ben Harper’s The Will To Live (1997, Virgin Records, Discogs release ID: 638831), produced by J.P. Plunier. Piatt is credited as “Backing Vocals” on tracks 6 to 8. Two other vocalists appear in the same role on adjacent tracks: Agnes Baddoo (track 6) and Juan Nelson (track 11). How Piatt came to be on this session is not documented in Discogs.
