Michael Zerang

Discogs ID: 319251
Born: 1958, Chicago, Illinois, USA
ANV: M. Zerang, MZ, Zerang
Alias: Jimi Jihad

US percussionist, improviser, and composer based in Chicago. A core figure in Chicago’s free improvisation scene from the early 1980s onward. Best known as drummer in the Peter Brötzmann Chicago Tentet and as leader of several ensembles including Michael Zerang & The Blue Lights. Plays drums, percussion, marimba, goblet drum (darburka/dumbek), frame drum, and tambourine.

Chicago Scene and Key Groups

Zerang emerged from Chicago’s improvised music underground in the early 1980s. His longest-running affiliations are with the Brötzmann-McPhee-Kessler quartet (beginning by the early 2000s) and the Peter Brötzmann Chicago Tentet, both of which define him as a drummer in the post-free-jazz tradition.

Key Release Notes

Tales Out Of Time — hatOLOGY (2004)

Quartet with Peter Brötzmann (tenor/alto saxophone), Joe McPhee (tenor saxophone, pocket cornet, trumpet), Kent Kessler (double bass), and Zerang (drums/percussion). Compositions distributed among Brötzmann, McPhee, and Zerang, plus one traditional piece. Produced by Art Lange; executive producer Werner X. Uehlinger; engineered by John McCortney; mixed/mastered by Peter Pfister; liner notes by John Corbett.

Live In Beirut — Al Maslakh (2005)

Duo with Brötzmann (tenor sax, tárogató, B♭ clarinet); Zerang on drums, darburka, and percussion. Recorded by Marc Codsi; mixed/mastered by Lou Mallozzi; artwork by Mazen Kerbaj. Released on the Beirut experimental label Al Maslakh. Documents Zerang’s deep connection with the Beirut improv scene.

Guts — Okka Disk (2006)

Same Brötzmann/McPhee/Kessler/Zerang quartet as Tales Out Of Time. Produced by Bruno Johnson and Brötzmann. Recorded by Malachi Ritscher and Savage Sound Syndicate; mastered by Lou Mallozzi.

The Damage Is Done — Not Two Records (2009)

Same quartet again; recorded in Poland on the Kraków-based Not Two Records. Recorded by Michał Rosicki; mixed/mastered by Lou Mallozzi.

Creole Gardens (A New Orleans Suite) — NoBusiness Records (2011)

Duo with Joe McPhee (alto sax, pocket trumpet); both credited as composers. Released on the Lithuanian NoBusiness Records. Mixed by Todd Carter; mastered by Arūnas Zujus; produced by Danas Mikailionis.
