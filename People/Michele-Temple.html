Michele Temple

American musician (born 1959, Ohio). Bassist, multi-instrumentalist, and songwriter. Discogs artist ID: 647467. Known primarily as a core member of Pere Ubu from 1995 through their final album in 2023.

Overview

Michele Temple joined Pere Ubu in 1993 for the Story of My Life tour and became a full recording member from Ray Gun Suitcase (1995) onward — one of the longest-tenured members of the band’s later era. Over nearly three decades with Pere Ubu she played bass, slide bass, guitar, lute, piano, tambourine, and organ, and co-wrote material across multiple albums. Wikipedia’s Pere Ubu article notes she and Garo Yellin joined for the Story of My Life tour and appeared on the 1995 album.

Before Pere Ubu, Temple was active in the broader Cleveland post-punk / art-rock network. She played bass on Home And Garden’s Melville (1986, After Hours Records) — a project built around ex-Pere Ubu members Scott Krauss, Tony Maimone, Jim Jones (3), and Robert Wheeler. She also played guitar and backing vocals in The Vivians, an early-1990s indie rock band that included future Pere Ubu drummer Scott Benedict.

Temple holds a name variation credit on Discogs as “Temple.”

Notes on credits

  • On Pennsylvania (1998) all performer credits are listed as “Uncredited” on Discogs — the band worked without sleeve credits for that album.
  • On “Long Live Père Ubu!” (2009), a theatrical/live album recorded with Sarah Jane Morris, Temple is credited with “Bass, Vocals, Instruments [All]” — the most expansive single-release credit on her record.
  • Why I Hate Women (2006) is the only Pere Ubu album where Temple receives a lead vocals credit.
  • Temple co-wrote songs on Ray Gun Suitcase, Carnival Of Souls, and Trouble On Big Beat Street in addition to her instrumental roles.
