Biography / Career Overview

Theo Bodewes is a Dutch drummer and percussionist best known as a founding member of BRAAXTAAL, the avant-rock/experimental trio led by sound poet and vocalist Jaap Blonk. He has worked across three documented group contexts: the long-running BRAAXTAAL trio, the large-ensemble Splinks project (also Blonk-led), and the Astrid Seriese Band, a Dutch jazz-pop group on the Brigadoon label.

His Discogs credits span 1993–2011. Within BRAAXTAAL he played drums and percussion on all three of the group’s studio albums (1993, 1997, 2001), added electronic drums on the second (Speechlos, 1997), and is also credited as a co-writer on several tracks of Speechlos. He also contributed to the 2011 archival reissue that compiled the band’s 1993 recordings alongside Splinks material. Outside the Blonk orbit, he appears as recording engineer on Jaap Blonk’s 1993 solo cassette Liederen Uit De Hemel (Poëzie Perdu).
