Britt Brown

Discogs ID: 812286
Name variations: B Brown, B. Brown, Britt
Based: Los Angeles, California

Co-founder and primary designer of Not Not Fun Records. Core member of Robedoor (with Alex Brown (7) and M. Geddes Gengras). Also a member of Pocahaunted, Weirdo-Begeirdo, Barrabarracuda, Quintana Roo, Topaz Rags, and Vibes (4).

Role at NNF

Britt Brown co-runs Not Not Fun Records alongside Amanda Brown (2). His primary contribution to the label’s physical output is design: credited for artwork, layout, and graphic design on 75+ NNF and 100% Silk releases spanning 2004–2025. His design work defines the visual identity of both NNF and its sublabel 100% Silk — a consistent, minimal-to-psychedelic aesthetic in packaging, sleeve art, and typography. He has no documented formal design training on Discogs; the style developed organically alongside the label.

Robedoor

Robedoor is Britt Brown’s primary and longest-running musical project. See Robedoor for full discography and detail.

The project began around 2005 and has released on NNF continuously through 2024 — 28 NNF catalog entries, the most of any NNF artist. Outside NNF, Robedoor released on Arbor, Blackest Rainbow, Chondritic Sound, Deathbomb Arc, Hands In The Dark, Important Records, Olde English Spelling Bee, and many others.

Design: NNF Visual Identity

Britt Brown is the dominant designer behind NNF’s visual output. Amanda Brown (2) is credited on 13 releases; Crystal Dorval (White Poppy) designs her own covers (15 NNF releases) but that is artist self-design, not collaboration with Britt. Britt’s role is label-wide packaging: he is the house designer.

No direct Britt Brown credit appears on White Poppy releases. The “design collaboration with Amanda/Britt Brown” referenced in White Poppy research notes reflects the broader NNF house-design context — Crystal Dorval handles her own visual output, while Britt and Amanda handle the label packaging infrastructure around her. There is no documented Britt-Dorval visual co-credit in Discogs.
