Jon Theodore

Jon Theodore

Jul 27, 20265 min read

Jon Theodore

Discogs ID: 254223
Born: December 30, 1973, Baltimore, Maryland
Role: Drummer, percussionist, keyboardist

American drummer. Known primarily for his tenure with The Mars Volta (2002–2005/06) and as current drummer of Queens of the Stone Age (2013–present). Also appeared as percussionist/keyboardist on HiM’s Our Point Of Departure (2000) — confirmed as the same person (see below).

Identity Confirmation

The Jon Theodore (Discogs ID: 254223) listed on HiM’s Our Point Of Departure (2000) playing percussion and keyboards is confirmed to be the same Jon Theodore who drummed for The Mars Volta and Queens of the Stone Age. The identification is supported by:

  • Doug Scharin’s Bandcamp page for Our Point Of Departure credits Jon Theodore explicitly on percussion and keyboards. (dougscharinmusic.bandcamp.com, retrieved 2026-06-29)
  • Wikipedia’s Jon Theodore article lists his pre-Mars Volta career as: Golden (~1990–2000) → Royal Trux (~1998–1999) → The Mars Volta (2001–). The HiM appearance (album released 2000) fits squarely in this timeline, likely during or just after his Golden period. (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Theodore, as of 2026-06-29)
  • Doug Scharin is cited by Theodore as a drumming influence (Wikipedia), consistent with a collaborative relationship.
  • Discogs assigns the same ID (254223) to the HiM member credit and the Mars Volta member page.

No source explicitly states “Jon Theodore of HiM = Jon Theodore of The Mars Volta” in prose; the confirmation rests on Discogs ID identity, timeline fit, and network overlap (Doug Scharin / HiM overlapping with the post-rock underground Theodore moved through before joining TMV).

Biography

Early life and education. Born in Baltimore, MD. Father is of Haitian descent, which Theodore credits with influencing his rhythmic vocabulary. Began piano at age seven; took up drums around age 15. Attended Gilman School (Baltimore) and subsequently studied at Oberlin Conservatory of Music, Ohio. (remo.com/profile/jon-theodore, retrieved 2026-06-29; en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Theodore, as of 2026-06-29)

Golden (~1990–2000). Joined near the end of high school; recorded and toured with the band for approximately ten years. Releases include Here Comes the Golden Fuzz (1997, Slowdime), self-titled (1998, Trans Solar), Super Golden Original Movement (1999), Golden Summer (2000). (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Theodore, as of 2026-06-29)

Royal Trux (~1998–1999). Played with Royal Trux for approximately eighteen months, appearing on their 1998 EP and Veterans of Disorder (1999). (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Theodore, as of 2026-06-29)

HiM (c. 2000). Credited on Our Point Of Departure (Perishable PER012 / FatCat FATCD12, 2000) playing percussion and keyboards, alongside Doug Scharin (drums/percussion/electronics), Fred Erskine (bass/trumpets/keyboards/guitar), Carlo Cennamo (tenor sax/keyboards), and Sean Meadows (guitar). (dougscharinmusic.bandcamp.com, retrieved 2026-06-29)

The Mars Volta (2001–2006). Met Cedric Bixler-Zavala and Omar Rodríguez-López during his Royal Trux period. Joined as drummer on the Tremulant EP (GSL54, 2002) and remained for De-Loused In The Comatorium (2003), Frances The Mute (2005), and Amputechture (2006) — the band’s first three studio albums. Departed 2006 at Omar’s request; bassist Juan Alderete later described the split as Theodore being “unhappy.” (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Theodore, as of 2026-06-29)

One Day As A Lion (2008). Duo with Rage Against the Machine vocalist Zack de la Rocha; released a self-titled EP on Anti- Records (July 2008). (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Theodore, as of 2026-06-29)

Other post-TMV work. Played drums on Brandon Boyd (Incubus) solo album The Wild Trapeze (2010). Joined Giraffe Tongue Orchestra in January 2012 (replacing Thomas Pridgen, his Mars Volta successor). Worked on Omar Rodríguez-López solo albums (2015–2017). Drummed on Bright Eyes’ Down in the Weeds, Where the World Once Was (2020) and toured with them through 2022. (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Theodore, as of 2026-06-29)

Queens of the Stone Age (2013–present). Joined in 2013, replacing Joey Castillo. Contributed to the closing track of …Like Clockwork (2013, which reached #1 Billboard 200) before becoming full permanent drummer. Also recorded Villains (2017) and In Times New Roman… (2023). In 2018 performed drums on portions of the Red Dead Redemption 2 video game score with Josh Homme and Michael Shuman. (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Theodore, as of 2026-06-29; articles.roland.com, retrieved 2026-06-29)

Musical Influences

Cites Billy Cobham (Mahavishnu Orchestra) as primary influence. Also Elvin Jones, John Bonham, Neil Peart, Keith Moon, Doug Scharin. Haitian cultural heritage cited as significant to his rhythmic approach. (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Theodore, as of 2026-06-29)
