Mother of J Dilla (James Dewitt Yancey) and Illa J (John Yancey). After J Dilla’s death in February 2006 she became the primary steward of his estate, overseeing posthumous releases, founding the James Dewitt Yancey Foundation, and building the Yancey Media Group / Vintage Vibez Music Group label structure to control release of his archive material.

Biography

Background

Maureen Yancey — known in hip-hop circles as “Ma Dukes” — is the mother of Detroit producer and rapper James Dewitt Yancey (J Dilla) and John Yancey (Illa J). When J Dilla was diagnosed with TTP (a rare blood disorder) and Lupus in 2003, he relocated from Detroit to Los Angeles to live with her. She was a close witness to the last years of his life and the recording sessions that produced much of his posthumous catalog.

Estate Management (2006–present)

J Dilla died on February 10, 2006. Maureen stepped into an active estate management role almost immediately. In May 2006 she co-announced the founding of the James Dewitt Yancey Foundation (later operating as the J Dilla Foundation), with dual missions: raising awareness and funding for Lupus research, and providing arts education to underprivileged youth. The Foundation’s websites (jdillafoundation.org / jamesdyanceyfoundation.org) were linked from the Yancey Media Group’s Discogs presence.

Her first major release credit came the same year: she served as co-executive producer on The Shining (BBE / Okayplayer Records, 2006), alongside Karriem Riggins, Peter Adarkwah, Eddie Bezalel, and Tim Maynor. The album, compiled from tracks recorded during Dilla’s final hospitalized months, was one of the first major posthumous releases and established the estate’s collaborative approach with existing label partners.

Yancey Media Group and Label Structure

Over the following decade Maureen built out a small label infrastructure to manage estate releases:

  • Pay Jay Productions (Discogs ID: 474995) — the primary estate imprint, responsible for a run of 7-inch singles and the landmark The Diary LP (2016). Parent label listed as #212 on Discogs.
  • Yancey Media Group (Discogs ID: 611528) — sublabel of Vintage Vibez Music Group (Discogs ID: 914711). Used alongside Official Ma Dukes on several releases.
  • Official Ma Dukes (Discogs ID: 1451973) — imprint used on releases such as Jay Love Japan (reissue, 2016) and Motor City (2017).
  • Vintage Vibez Music Group (Discogs ID: 914711) — parent entity; also houses Ruff Draft Records (Discogs ID: 398555) as a sublabel.
