David Sheppard

Discogs ID: 530. Realname: David John Sheppard. British musician, music journalist, and author. Active 1992–2025.

Co-founded Second Language Records around 2009 with Glen Johnson and Martin Jensen.

Groups / Projects

David Sheppard has been a member of numerous bands and collaborative projects beyond Piano Magic:

  • Balloon (2) — early 1990s UK indie band (members: Ian Bickerton, David Sheppard, John Moore, Sarah Nixey). Sheppard played guitar, bass, marimba, xylophone, percussion, and backing vocals on the 1992 album Gravity (Dedicated), produced by Michael Brook. Co-produced tracks 2 & 3 on the single Tightrope Walker (1992, Dedicated) alongside Ian Bickerton.
  • State River Widening — London trio formed May 1998 with Keiron Phelan and John Steele. Sheppard played, arranged, wrote, and produced. Self-titled album (1999, Rocket Girl). Also credited on Cottonhead EP (2004, Vertical Form) as composer, guitarist, keyboard player, and woodwind/mallets.
  • Ellis Island Sound — duo with Peter Astor (realname: Peter Astor & Dave Sheppard). Released self-titled LP (1998, All City; 2002, Heavenly), singles on Static Caravan, and The Good Seed (2007, Peacefrog). Later albums Divisions and Regions both 2014 on Village Green.
  • Phelan Sheppard — duo with Keiron Phelan. Four releases: 2 (2000, Rocket Girl), O, Little Stars (2002, Rocket Girl), Harps Old Master (2006, Leaf), Games Of Position / Casino Lisboa (2007, Static Caravan). Sheppard wrote, performed, produced, played guitar, double bass, keyboards, woodwind, percussion, mallets.
  • Smile Down Upon Us — trio with Keiron Phelan and MoomLooo. Smile Down Upon Us (2008, Yacca — Japan). Sheppard played acoustic guitar, bass guitar, glockenspiel, nylon guitar, tenor guitar, organ, percussion.
  • Snow Palms — trio with Christopher Leary and Matt Gooderson. Four albums on Village Green: I n t erv al s (2012), Origin And Echo (2017), Everything Ascending (2019), Land Waves (2020). Sheppard played and composed across multiple releases; on Origin And Echo credited with a vast instrument array including marimba, metallophone, piano, synthesizer, Mellotron, harp, and more, plus producer and Mixed By.
  • Silver Servants — large ensemble including Glen Johnson, Angèle David-Guillou, Frances Castle, Oliver Cherer, Robin Saville, Sharron Kraus, Mark Fry, Nick Franglen, Katie English, and others. Active on Second Language.
  • Mark Fry backing band — credited as “Band” on Fry’s Not On The Radar (2025, Second Language), alongside Angèle David-Guillou, Ian Button, and others.

Solo Work

  • Vertical Land — solo LP released 2015 on Village Green (cat: VGLP009), credited as “David John Sheppard”. Genres: Electronic, Folk, Modern Classical, Ambient, Soundtrack. No additional credits listed (all instruments implied by Sheppard).

Books (Web sources)

Active as a music and culture writer since 1995. Also a lecturer in Music Media Marketing & Communication at BIMM Music Institute. Edited the Saatchi Gallery Magazine: Art and Music, 2008–2018.
