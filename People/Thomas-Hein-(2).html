Overview

Thomas Hein is a British multi-instrumentalist — drums, bass guitar, electronics, and vocals — best known as a former member of the experimental art rock group These New Puritans. His 44 Discogs credits run from 2005 to the 2020s across Art Rock, Experimental, and Post Rock styles, with the densest activity in the 2010s. His principal label relationships are with Angular Recording Corporation and Domino, the homes of These New Puritans’ earlier and later output. His tightest working partnerships are with Jack Barnett, George Barnett, and arranger Graham Sutton — the core creative axis of These New Puritans — alongside vocal contributors Micaela Haslam and Faith Leadbetter. He is also listed as a member of Fogie, a separate project whose nature and timeline relative to These New Puritans is unclear from Discogs alone.

Notes (web)

Fogie

Fogie is a collaborative project between Thomas Hein and Rosalie Cunningham — singer, guitarist, and multi-instrumentalist best known as the founder of the psychedelic rock band Purson. The Bandcamp page describes them as “cosmically entwined friends” who “spent many years indulging in the recreational creation of songs and sounds,” based in Southend-on-Sea, UK. The project pre-dates Hein’s departure from These New Puritans: their sole known release, the 7” single Gimme Mustard / Trout Vapour, came out on 18 August 2014 — while Hein was still an active member of TNP (he left after Inside the Rose in 2019). Fogie is therefore a concurrent side project rather than a post-TNP successor.

Personnel on the release:

  • Thomas Hein — vocals, drums, piano, tapes
  • Rosalie Cunningham — vocals, guitars, bass, keyboards

The release appeared on Machine Elf Records (matching the Machine Elf Records credit already in the Discogs table above for 2014). Genre tags on Bandcamp: progressive rock, glam rock, pop rock, psychedelic; press reception noted “vintage production values,” “trippy dippy psych,” and “a true 60s vibe.” The 7” is listed as sold out; no further releases from Fogie are documented on Bandcamp or Discogs as of August 2026.
