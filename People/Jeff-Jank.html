Jeff Jank

Discogs ID: 346738
Role: Art director, designer, illustrator
Employer: Stones Throw Records (in-house art director)

Stones Throw Records’ primary in-house art director and designer. Credited on 309 Stones Throw releases on Discogs — virtually the entire catalog from the early 2000s through the 2020s. Roles include Art Direction, Design, Illustration, Layout, Sleeve, Cover art. His illustration style (cartoonish, often humorous) became the visual signature of the label. Also credited as “Other” coordinator on 72 releases.

Notable design credits include Madvillainy (2004), Donuts (2006), Champion Sound (2003), A Strange Arrangement (2009), Toeachizown (2009), Mister Mellow (2017).
