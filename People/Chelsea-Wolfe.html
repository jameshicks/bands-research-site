Chelsea Wolfe

Discogs ID: 2078470 Real name: Chelsea Joy Wolfe Born: November 14, 1983, Sacramento, California Alias: Wolf (62) Labels: Sargent House (2011–2025, 115 releases); Loma Vista (2024) Groups: BloodMoon (2) (6190597); Mrs. Piss (7917263); Red Host (6918797); Sister Crayon (1942316); Wild Eyes (2) (2340533); Winter Trees (2775540)

American singer-songwriter spanning dark folk, doom metal, post-punk, and industrial. Primary artist on Sargent House — 115 releases over 14 years, the label’s highest volume act. Left amid the 2023 SH abuse controversy, releasing a public statement alluding to negative experiences.

Primary collaborator: Ben Chisholm (bass, synth, programming, producer) across most SH albums. Lars Stalfors co-produced Pain Is Beauty (2013). Her Ἀποκάλυψις (2011, 10 pressings) was her SH debut; Abyss (2015, 24 pressings) is the most-pressed release in the entire SH catalog.

Guest vocal on Russian Circles Memorial (SH 111, 2013) — the beginning of a long cross-collaborative relationship with the label’s other anchor acts.

More recent album She Reaches Out To She Reaches Out To She (2024, Loma Vista) produced by Dave Sitek.
