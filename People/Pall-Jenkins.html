Pall Jenkins

Vocalist, guitarist, multi-instrumentalist, producer. San Diego, California. Real name: Pall A. Jenkins. Discogs ID: 220085. Known aliases: Paulo Zappoli (used on Amore Del Tropico), Mr. Tube, The Sandman (5). Name variations on releases include Pall, Paul Jenkins, Pall A. Jenkins, Pall Mall, Pall Arthur Jenkins.

Front man of Three Mile Pilot (1991–1997) and The Black Heart Procession (1997–present). Recurring lyrical and vocal collaborator with The Album Leaf / Jimmy LaValle across three albums. Member of Ugly Casanova (2002), Blacksoulwater, Dark Sarcasm (early San Diego hardcore), and Mr. Tube And The Flying Objects (formed 2006). (Source: Discogs artist page; Wikipedia/Pall_Jenkins.)

Early Career — Dark Sarcasm and Three Mile Pilot (1991–1997)

Jenkins’ earliest documented project is Dark Sarcasm, a short-lived hardcore band from San Diego featuring Chris Hatcher, Easy-D, Jenkins, and Sammy Lopez. Discogs notes this was a precursor band before Three Mile Pilot and The Black Heart Procession. The only known release is the Lego Facilities compilation (1992, Gravity (2)).

Three Mile Pilot formed in San Diego in 1991 with Jenkins (vocals, guitar), Armistead Burwell Smith IV (bass, backing vocals — later of Pinback), and Tom Zinser (drums). Tobias Nathaniel joined as a core collaborator. The band released on Gravity (2) and Headhunter Records / Cargo Music. Their debut 7” Nà Vuccà Dò Lupù (1992, Negative Pulse Records) featured Jim French on saxophone.

Key Three Mile Pilot releases:

  • The Chief Assassin To The Sinister (1994, Cargo Music / Headhunter Records)
  • Another Desert, Another Sea (1997, Cargo Music / Headhunter Records)
  • Songs From An Old Town We Once Knew (compilation, 2000)
  • The Inevitable Past Is The Future Forgotten (reunion album, 2010, Temporary Residence Limited)

Jenkins was also credited on Blacksoulwater (alongside Glen Galloway and Kevin Branstetter), whose only Discogs entry is a 2003 Italian split release on Homesleep.

The Black Heart Procession (1997–present)

The Black Heart Procession co-founded 1997 by Pall Jenkins and Tobias Nathaniel after Three Mile Pilot’s hiatus. The duo’s sound centres on piano, organ, Moog, saw, waterphone, and Jenkins’s baritone. Debut released on Headhunter Records; subsequent albums on Touch And Go (albums 2–The Spell) and Temporary Residence Limited (Six, Blood Bunny / Black Rabbit, The Waiter Chapters I–VIII). See The Black Heart Procession for full discography.

Jenkins also did artwork, layout, and illustration for most BHP releases — credits include “Black Ink And Words” (album 2), “Red Artwork, Bat, Ship” (Another Desert, Another Sea).

Era 3 — Collaborations (2002–present)

Ugly Casanova (2002)

Member of Ugly Casanova — Isaac Brock’s (Modest Mouse) side project, also including Joe Plummer, Brian Deck, Tim Rutili (Califone), and others. Contributed written-by credits to Sharpen Your Teeth (2002, Sub Pop).

The Album Leaf (2004–2010)

Contributed lyrics, vocals, saw, and guitar to The Album Leaf across three albums:

  • In A Safe Place (2004, Sub Pop): performer [With The Help Of]
  • Into The Blue Again (2006, City Slang): lyrics (tr.2, 8); backing vocals (tr.8)
  • A Chorus Of Storytellers (2010, Sub Pop): recorded by [vocals, additional tracking]

Castanets (2004)

Performer credited as “Rattle, Hum” on Castanets’ Cathedral (2004, Asthmatic Kitty Records / Sounds Familyre). The Cathedral release is a Castanets (Raymond Raposa) album; other performers include Rafter Roberts, Liz Janes, and Liz Janes.

J. Mascis

  • Tied To A Star (2014, Sub Pop) — guitar, vocals
  • Elastic Days (2018, Sub Pop) — vocals

Mr. Tube And The Flying Objects (2006–2015+)

Co-formed Mr. Tube And The Flying Objects in 2006 (per Wikipedia) with Brad Lee (3) (trumpet, bass, multi-instrumentalist). The ensemble is a large horn-and-rhythm collective. Members also include John Paul Labno and Scott Mercado (2). Released Listen Up (2005, Sweet Nothing Records), No Wrong, No Rights (2015, Collectors Classics Organization / Stereo Disguise Recording Laboratories). Jenkins credited for vocals, guitar, keys, percussion, artwork, and production on No Wrong, No Rights. Additional art on that album by Rick Froberg.

Solo — Built In Sun (2015)

Pall Jenkins and Joe Plummer released Built In Sun (2015, Joyful Noise Recordings / People In A Position To Know) as a duo. Also featuring Richard Swift (2) (bass, guitar, sounds). Produced by Joe Plummer; mixed by Jenkins, Plummer, and Swift.

Systems Officer (2024)

Contributed lyrics to In Our Code (2024, Go-Home Records) by Systems Officer — Armistead Burwell Smith IV’s (Zach Smith from Three Mile Pilot/Pinback) project. Jenkins wrote lyrics for track B5.
