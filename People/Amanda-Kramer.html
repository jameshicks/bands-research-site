Amanda Kramer

Amanda Kramer

Jun 27, 20266 min read

Amanda Kramer

Discogs ID: 295320 Born: New York, New York, 1961 Origin: USA Instrument(s): Keyboards, Piano, Vocals

American musician and keyboardist. Best known as a member of Information Society (on their 1988 debut album), The Golden Palominos (three albums across 1989–1994), and The Psychedelic Furs (the 2020 reunion album Made Of Rain and onward). She has also contributed keyboard and piano work as a session player across three decades, working with artists including 10,000 Maniacs, Steve Kilbey, and The Church.

Biography

Early Career and Information Society (1988)

Kramer appears on the self-titled debut album by Information Society (1988, Tommy Boy), credited as “Performer” alongside core members Kurt Harland and James Cassidy, with Paul Robb handling the bulk of the programming. Information Society were a Minneapolis-born synthpop/electro act who had relocated to New York; the debut yielded the US Top 3 hit “What’s On Your Mind (Pure Energy)“. The extent of Kramer’s live vs. studio role with the band is not fully documented on Discogs, though she is listed in the group’s official member roster.

The Golden Palominos (1989–1994)

Kramer’s most sustained early credit is with The Golden Palominos, the New York avant-garde/art-rock collective helmed by drummer Anton Fier. The Palominos were a rotating cast of downtown NYC musicians — their roster at various points included Bill Laswell, John Zorn, Arto Lindsay, Bootsy Collins, Nicky Skopelitis, Lori Carson, Syd Straw, Knox Chandler, and Bernie Worrell, among others.

Kramer appeared on three consecutive Palominos records:

  • A Dead Horse (1989, Celluloid) — uncredited vocals on tracks 2, 3, 5, and 7
  • Drunk With Passion (1991, Venture/UK) — keyboards and vocals across most of the album; the lineup here also featured Richard Thompson on guitar and Carla Bley on Hammond organ
  • This Is How It Feels (1993, Restless Records) — keyboards; the album also featured Lori Carson on vocals and writing, and Bootsy Collins on guitar

She continued with Pure (1994, Restless Records) — keyboards; a more stripped-back Palominos record centered on Lori Carson’s vocals and co-written with Nicky Skopelitis.

The Palominos context placed Kramer firmly within the downtown New York experimental/art-rock scene of the late 1980s and early 1990s.

Session Work and Film Scoring (1993–1994)

In 1993, Kramer contributed piano and pump organ to 10,000 Maniacs’ landmark MTV Unplugged (Elektra, 1993), one of the most commercially successful releases in the MTV Unplugged series. She played alongside the band’s regular members and Natalie Merchant, covering acoustic-format versions of the Maniacs’ catalog. (A 1994 release ID 4335302 is a variant pressing.)

Also in 1993, she provided backing vocals on composer Blake Leyh’s Four Film Scores (Luminous Records), a collection of original soundtracks. The following year, Kramer and Leyh co-released Wintermass (1994, Luminous Records) — the only release on Discogs credited to Kramer as a primary/lead artist, with both listed as co-producers and co-performers.

Later Session Credits (2001–2023)

Kramer contributed keyboards and vocals to the The Golden Palominos archival release Etc (Lost Songs, Tunes 1996–2000) (2001, XIII Bis/The Establishment, France), gathering unreleased material from the mid-to-late 1990s period.

In 2009 she is credited on keyboards for Finale – The Last Mantaray & More Show (Fremantle Home Entertainment, UK), a live document of a Mantaray stage production.

Her connection to Steve Kilbey and The Church spans two albums:

  • Man Woman Life Death Infinity (The Church, 2017, Unorthodox) — additional keyboards and backing vocals
  • Sydney Rococo (Steve Kilbey solo, 2018, Golden Robot Records) — piano
  • Eros Zeta And The Perfumed Guitars (The Church, 2023, Communicating Vessels) — piano on the closing track 14 (“End Piano”)

The Psychedelic Furs – Made Of Rain (2020–)

Kramer joined the reformed The Psychedelic Furs lineup that recorded Made Of Rain (2020, Cooking Vinyl), the band’s first studio album in 29 years. The album was co-produced by Richard Fortus, Tim Palmer, and the band, with Kramer on keyboards alongside Richard Butler (vocals), Tim Butler (bass), Paul Garisto (drums), Richard Good (guitar), and Mars Williams (saxophone). Kramer is a continuing member of the Furs’ touring and recording lineup.
