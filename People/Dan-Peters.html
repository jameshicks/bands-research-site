Overview

Dan Peters (born August 18, 1967) is a Seattle drummer who entered the city’s underground rock scene in 1983 and became best known as the anchor of Mudhoney, sharing over 300 release credits with core members Steve Turner and Mark Arm across a catalogue that runs from their 1988 Sub Pop debut through the 2020s. Before Mudhoney became his primary home, Peters was active in early Seattle bands including Bundle of Hiss, Feast, and Love Battery, and had a brief stint with Nirvana during the 1990 transition between Chad Channing and Dave Grohl. Sub Pop has been his dominant label relationship throughout, with additional appearances on Reprise, Glitterhouse, and Au Go Go.
