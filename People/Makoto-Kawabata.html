Makoto Kawabata

Discogs ID: 115177
Active: 1980s–present
Origin: Japan
Role: Guitar, producer, bandleader

Core organiser and guitarist of Acid Mothers Temple & The Melting Paraiso UFO. Produced and engineered 11 releases for Alien8 Recordings across the AMT catalog. Also the principal creative force across all AMT variant lineups.
