Robert Millis

Discogs ID: 275325 Real name: Robert Millis

Seattle-based experimental musician, filmmaker, and sound artist. Core member of Climax Golden Twins alongside Jeffery Taylor. Also active in AFCGT, Animist Orchestra, Liver And Bacon, Messenger Girls Trio, Seattle Phonographers Union, Eye Music, Box Of Chocolates, and Disposable God Squad.

Name variations: Cecil B DeMillis, Rob Millis, R. Millis, RMillis, r millis.

Design / Layout Credits (Sublime Frequencies and affiliates)

Millis contributed LP design and/or layout to numerous Sublime Frequencies releases (2007–2014), including the Guitars From Agadez series (Vols. 1–3), Treeg Salaam, Folk And Pop Sounds Of Sumatra Vol 1, Princess Nicotine: Folk And Pop Sounds Of Myanmar (Burma) Vol 1, Eat The Dream: Gnawa Music From Essaouira, and Bush Taxi Mali. He also did layout for the Secret Museum of Mankind series (Vols. II, III, Central Asia) on Outernational Records/Yazoo, and package design on Au Clair De La Lune (Parlortone) and layout on Excavated Shellac: Strings (Parlortone).
