Overview

Thomas Bartlett (born October 13, 1981) is a New York-based keyboard player and producer who has accumulated over 700 Discogs credits since 2000, making him one of the more densely connected figures in the American indie rock milieu. He is a core presence in The National’s extended orbit — Aaron Dessner, Bryce Dessner, Rob Moose, Ben Lanz, Matt Berninger, and both Devendorfs all rank among his top collaborators — while Sufjan Stevens and the Asthmatic Kitty world account for a second major strand of his work. His own project, Doveman, is a flexible ensemble built around his inner circle of collaborators; he is also a member of The Gloaming, the acclaimed Irish-traditional fusion group, as well as Crispi Tritone and Parthenope.
