Terry Riley

Terry Riley

Aug 20, 20262 min read

Terry Riley

Discogs ID: (to confirm)

American minimalist composer; cited as a primary reference point for Emeralds’ sound.

Best known for In C (1964) and A Rainbow in Curved Air (1969). His influence on synthesizer-based drone and repetitive structures is a direct line to the Emeralds approach.
