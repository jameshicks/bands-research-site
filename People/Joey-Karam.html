Joey Karam

Discogs ID: 590759 Real name: Joseph Karam Alias: Joey Juvenile Active: late 1990s–present Origin: San Diego, California

Keyboardist and multi-instrumentalist. Best known as the synthesizer player for The Locust, where his harsh electronic textures are central to the band’s hyper-compressed grindcore/noise-punk hybrid. Also played in Le Shok, Murder City Devils, and several smaller San Diego projects.

The Locust (1998–present)

Karam joined The Locust as their full-time keyboardist, adding a harsh synth dimension to the band’s sub-minute song structures. Stable recording lineup: Karam (keys), Justin Pearson (bass/vocals), Gabe Serbian (drums/vocals), Robert Bray (guitar/vocals). David Astor appeared on the debut only.

The band released three full-lengths and a string of EPs, all on Gold Standard Laboratories and later Anti- (Epitaph):

Le Shok

San Diego punk/garage band, active late 1990s–early 2000s. Karam was one of seven members. Discogs ID 831769.

Murder City Devils

Seattle punk band (Discogs ID 252856). Karam is the reunion-era keyboardist, replacing original keyboardist Leslie Hardy who was part of the original 1996–2001 lineup. He plays keys in the current active lineup alongside Spencer Moody (vocals), Dann Gallucci (guitar), Derek Fudesco (bass), and Coady Willis (drums). Confirmed on the 2024 self-released single Hill And 6th Street B/W Pele’s Feet.
