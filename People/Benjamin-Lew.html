Benjamin Lew

Discogs ID: 5107
Real name: Serge Benjamin Henri Leon Mathot
Role: Composer, electronics musician
Active: 1980–2019 (on Discogs)
Born: Liège, Belgium
Died: 18 February 2025
Top labels: Crammed Discs, Made To Measure

sources: discogs

Belgian composer and electronics musician based in Liège. One of the central figures in the early Crammed Discs catalog and a key contributor to the Made To Measure ambient/composition series. His work sits at the intersection of minimalist electronics, chamber music, and sound poetry. He was a founding member of the duo Orient Et Mécanique alongside Alinovsky. His most enduring collaborations were with Steven Brown of Tuxedomoon (on Douzième Journée and A Propos D’Un Paysage), with Samy Birnbach (on When God Was Famous), and repeatedly with Marc Hollander and Gilles Martin as producers and engineers across the Crammed/Made To Measure ecosystem.

Early Work

His first documented release was “Peking Hotel - China” (1980), a 7” sound poetry single on S.L. Records (cat: SHOL 2963 A) — classified as Non-Music / Sound Poetry. This predates the Crammed Discs relationship by two years and hints at his roots in experimental and spoken-word forms.

He was a member of the duo Orient Et Mécanique (Discogs ID: 198562) alongside Alinovsky, though no releases under that name appear in the primary discography — it may have predated or overlapped with his solo work.

Crammed Discs and Made To Measure (1982–1989)

The core of Lew’s output was made for Crammed Discs and its Made To Measure sub-series. Engineer and co-producer Gilles Martin (alias Nigel Starmil) was his most consistent studio partner — appearing on 33 of his releases, across essentially everything from 1982 onward.

Douzième Journée: Le Verbe, La Parure, L’Amour (1982)

  • Cat#: CRAM 020 (Crammed Discs); CD reissue MTM 15 CD (Made To Measure, 1988)
  • Primary artists: Benjamin Lew / Steven Brown
  • Personnel: Lew (synthesizer, drum machines, tape, written-by, producer); Brown (saxophone, piano, organ, written-by, producer); Marc Hollander (percussion, bass clarinet, written-by tracks A4/B2, producer); Gilles Martin (engineer, producer); Claude Stassart (layout/cover)
  • Lew’s debut on Crammed. A minimalist electronics/chamber music collaboration, with text-and-music construction at its center (“Le Verbe, La Parure, L’Amour” = speech, adornment, love). Brown’s saxophone and organ textures interweave with Lew’s synthesizer and tape work. 12 pressings across Belgium and other territories make it one of the label’s more widely circulated early records.

Made To Measure Vol. 1 (1984)

  • Cat#: first Made To Measure compilation (Crammed Discs / Normal, Germany)
  • Primary artists: Minimal Compact, Benjamin Lew, Aksak Maboul, Tuxedomoon
  • Lew’s contribution: Track A5, composed and produced solo; Gilles Martin co-produced A1–A5 and B1–B9 across the compilation
  • Also features Blaine L. Reininger, Peter Principle (producer, Tuxedomoon tracks), Samy Birnbach, Vincent Kenis, Malka Spigel, and Jeannot Gillis. The compilation established Made To Measure’s aesthetic template — ambient, cinematic, chamber-influenced.

A Propos D’Un Paysage (1985)

  • Cat#: MTM 16 / CRAM 038 (dual catalog on Crammed Discs and Normal)
  • Primary artists: Benjamin Lew / Steven Brown
  • Personnel: Lew (electronics, mixed by); Brown (mixed by); Marc Hollander (mixed by); Gilles Martin (recorded by, mixed by)
  • Second collaboration with Brown. More purely textural than Douzième Journée — the title (“Regarding a Landscape”) describes its ambient character. Lew handles all electronics; Brown contributes to the mix alongside Hollander and Martin.

Nebka (1988)

  • Cat#: MTM 17 (Made To Measure)
  • Primary artist: Benjamin Lew (solo)
  • Personnel: Lew (electronics, composed by, producer); Marc Hollander (clarinet, percussion, co-composed tracks 12/14); Steven Brown (co-composed track 1); Blaine L. Reininger (violin); Claudine Steenackers (cello); Gilles Martin (engineer, producer)
  • Named after the ancient Egyptian pharaoh Nebka. Lew’s solo entry in the Made To Measure series, featuring an ensemble of acoustic instruments woven into the electronics. Hollander, Brown, and Reininger contribute specific tracks rather than playing throughout.

When God Was Famous / When God Was Famous (A Tribute To Poetry) (1989)

  • Cat#: MTM 19 (Made To Measure / Crammed Discs)
  • Primary artists: Samy Birnbach & Benjamin Lew
  • Personnel: Lew (electronics, composed by [selected and set to music], producer); Birnbach (vocals, composed by [selected and set to music], producer); Peter Principle (guitar); Michel Berckmans (oboe, bassoon); Aurelia Boven (cello); Gilles Martin (engineer, producer)
  • Lew’s collaboration with DJ and writer Samy Birnbach. Poetry set to music — the “composed by” credit reads “Selected And Set To Music,” indicating text from existing poets adapted for the record. Birnbach sings; Lew provides the electronic backdrop. Peter Principle (Tuxedomoon’s bassist) plays guitar. Two distinct pressings exist: the original LP (When God Was Famous) and a subsequent version (When God Was Famous (A Tribute To Poetry)) with the same credits.

Collaborative and Session Work

Les Nouvelles Musiques De Chambre #1 (1988)

  • Label: Sub Rosa (Belgium)
  • Primary artists: Benjamin Lew / Controlled Bleeding
  • Personnel (Lew’s side, tracks A1–A3): Lew (producer, recorded by, mixed by, written-by); Gilles Martin (producer, recorded by, mixed by). Controlled Bleeding’s side (track B) handled by Paul Lemos / Joe Papa.
  • An early Sub Rosa release pairing Lew with US industrial/noise group Controlled Bleeding. The split format means their contributions are largely separate.

Searching For Contact (Steven Brown, 2004)

  • Label: LTM (4) (Belgium)
  • Lew credited as Performer and Producer on track 17 only — a late, isolated collaboration with Brown on Brown’s solo album.

The Lonely Crowd (various artists compilation, 2019)

  • Label: Minimal Wave (USA & Europe)
  • Lew credited as Voice — appearance on a retrospective minimal wave/synth compilation.

Later Work

Le Personnage Principal Est Un Peuple Isolé (2019)

  • Label: Stroom (2) (Belgium)
  • Primary artist: Benjamin Lew (solo)
  • Compiled by Ziggy Devriendt and Brecht Van Dingenen; remastered by Mathieu Savenay. This appears to be a late-career retrospective or archival compilation on the Belgian Stroom label — released 37 years after his Crammed debut. Two pressings.

Connections

  • Crammed Discs — central label for his 1982–1989 output
  • Made To Measure — ambient/cinematic sub-series of Crammed; Lew contributed three entries (MTM 15 CD, MTM 17, MTM 19)
  • Steven Brown — most significant creative partner; Brown was the singer/multi-instrumentalist to Lew’s electronics
  • Tuxedomoon — Brown and Reininger’s main band; the Crammed Discs connection brought Tuxedomoon members into Lew’s orbit
  • Marc Hollander — Crammed founder; appeared as musician and producer on Lew’s records
  • Gilles Martin — the consistent engineer-producer behind Lew’s studio output
  • Samy Birnbach — co-wrote and co-produced When God Was Famous; also involved in Made To Measure Vol. 1
  • Peter Principle — Tuxedomoon bassist, brought in for guitar on When God Was Famous
  • Blaine L. Reininger — Tuxedomoon violinist, session player on Nebka
  • Michel Berckmans — Belgian woodwind player, recurring on When God Was Famous
  • Sub Rosa — Belgian experimental label; Les Nouvelles Musiques De Chambre #1 was an early Sub Rosa release
  • Aksak Maboul — Marc Hollander’s other main project; co-primary on Made To Measure Vol. 1
  • Minimal Compact — Israeli post-punk group; co-primary on Made To Measure Vol. 1
