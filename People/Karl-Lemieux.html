Karl Lemieux

Montreal filmmaker and visual artist. Discogs artist ID: 2482562. Long-time collaborator with Godspeed You Black Emperor! providing 16mm film projections for live performances and album artwork (burnt 16mm frames) from the band’s 2012 reunion onward. Credited as a full band member (“God’s Pee Was”) on ‘Allelujah! Don’t Bend Ascend (2012).
