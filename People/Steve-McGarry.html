Steve McGarry

Born 24 January 1953, Wythenshawe, Manchester. Graphic designer and cartoonist/illustrator; one of the central visual figures of the late-1970s Manchester punk scene, and subsequently one of Britain’s most widely-published cartoonists.

Identity confirmed (2026-08-05): The Steve McGarry credited for sleeve design on The Panik’s “It Won’t Sell!” is the same Steve McGarry who became the cartoonist/illustrator known for strips including Biggles, Kid, Stringers, and Buster Bloodvessel in the British national press. Sources: Manchester Digital Music Archive; downthetubes.net interview, 12 Apr 2016.

Manchester punk scene (1976–1979)

McGarry was at the centre of the 1976 Manchester punk scene as a sleeve and logo designer. In his own words (MDMA): “I knocked out the logo … that’s my customary scrawl, which we used on everything from Joy Division to Slaughter & The Dogs. I designed the sleeve for ‘It Won’t Sell’ by The Panik.”

He served as in-house designer for venues The Oaks and Rafters, which hosted early performances by major acts. He also fronted his own band, Steve McGarry’s First Offence, whose members included Toby Toman (later of Primal Scream) and Donald Johnson (later of A Certain Ratio). He was not a member of The Panik.

McGarry and Rob Gretton were childhood schoolmates at St. Bede’s — a 1965 school photograph in the MDMA shows both in the front row (MDMA artefact 6128). This pre-existing relationship explains Gretton commissioning McGarry for both The Panik sleeve and the Joy Division release.
