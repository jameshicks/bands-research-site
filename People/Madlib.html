Madlib

Discogs ID: 16508
Real name: Otis Jackson, Jr.
Origin: Oxnard, California, USA

American producer, MC, multi-instrumentalist, and DJ. One of the most prolific and influential figures in underground hip-hop. Primary recording artist on Stones Throw Records since 1999; appeared first via his crew Lootpack (Soundpieces: Da Antidote!, STH 2019, 1999), then under a vast range of aliases.

Aliases / Pseudonyms (selected)

Quasimoto (looped/pitched-up MC alter-ego), Beat Konducta, Yesterdays New Quintet, Sound Directions, The Last Electro-Acoustic Space Jazz & Percussion Ensemble, Monk Hughes & The Outer Realm, DJ Rels, Joe McDuphrey Experience, Ahmad Miller, Young Jazz Rebels — and many more. Discogs lists 40+ aliases.
