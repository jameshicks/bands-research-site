Alejandra Deheza

American musician and vocalist. Also known as Alley Deheza, A. E. Deheza. Born October 18, 1978, Guatemala. Twin sister of Claudia Deheza. Discogs ID: 285565. Instagram: @alleyestela.

Member of On!Air!Library! (2000–2004) and School Of Seven Bells (2007–2016). Moved to New York City in 1999 originally intending to be a poet; became a musician by accident. One of the central voices of the mid-2000s Brooklyn dream-pop/shoegaze scene.

Background

Father: Bolivian former opera singer; mother: Costa Rican. Raised in South Florida from age nine — a scene Alejandra describes as unusually eclectic: “into goth and also really into hip-hop, freestyle, or Miami bass” simultaneously. Parents were intensely religious. Alejandra and Claudia moved to New York City in 1999; Alejandra arrived with ambitions of being a poet, bringing years of writing.

On!Air!Library! (2000–2004)

Shortly after arriving in NYC, Alejandra was mistaken for a musician and offered an opening slot on a gig bill. She called Claudia and told her they had a month to write songs. That is how On!Air!Library! began. The band name came from an African documentary in which an old man hosted a radio show called “The On Air Library.”

Third member Phillip Wann joined; Jason Hammons was also involved in the early period. OAL signed to Arena Rock Recording Co. in October 2002. Released a split with The Album Leaf (2003) and a self-titled LP (2004) before disbanding in 2005.

See On!Air!Library! for full discography.

Meeting Benjamin Curtis / School of Seven Bells (2007–2016)

Alejandra met Benjamin Curtis in 2004 while both On!Air!Library! and Secret Machines were opening for Interpol on tour. (“It was seriously like lightning hit me a thousand times.“) The three decided to end their commitments to their old bands, move into a shared space, and build a home recording studio together. School Of Seven Bells formed in 2007 in Brooklyn, with Claudia Deheza as the third member.

Alejandra and Curtis were romantically involved for approximately five years, breaking up around the time of Disconnect from Desire (2010), but remained close collaborators and friends.

Claudia left the band in October 2010. The band continued as a duo until Curtis died of T-cell lymphoblastic lymphoma on December 29, 2013 (age 35, Memorial Sloan-Kettering). Alejandra completed the posthumous fourth album SVIIB (2016) from demos Curtis had written before and during his illness. She subsequently relocated to Los Angeles.

See School Of Seven Bells for full discography.

Risil (Non Meters Volume One, 2009, Important Records IMPREC218) is a collaborative project of Scott Herren, Roberto Carlos Lange, Benjamin Curtis, and others; Alejandra contributes vocals and flute. The Prefuse 73 track C4 (“The Only Guitar To Die Alone”) on The Only She Chapters (2011, Warp WARPLP208) was produced by Roberto Carlos Lange and also features Benjamin Curtis on guitar.

Lauren Flax — “Out of Reality” (2021, 2MR) is the only confirmed post-SVIIB release: an elegiac synth track co-written by Flax, Deheza, and Adele Stein; remixed by Skream.

Alejandra also has minor acting credits: Introducing (2012), The Sun Thief (2013), I Will Paint Your Spirit (2013).
