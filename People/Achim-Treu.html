Overview

Achim Treu is a Berlin-based German musician, composer, and producer working across experimental, leftfield, synth-pop, and abstract territories. He has been a member of Dauerfisch, Der Plan, and several smaller projects, with recorded work stretching back to 1985 on the Dauerfisch Produktion imprint. His closest collaborative ties are with Dauerfisch and Ufo Hawaii (11 shared credits each) and with Andre Abshagen (10 credits). His label history runs from Bungalow and Eleganz in the late 1990s and early 2000s through to Compost Records and Numero Group in the 2020s.
