Günter Pauler

Discogs ID: 463753
ANV: G.P.

German mastering and lacquer-cutting engineer. Cut the lacquer for Curlew’s North America (1985, Moers Music). Associated with Pauler Acoustics, Stockdorf, Germany — one of the leading European lacquer-cutting facilities.
