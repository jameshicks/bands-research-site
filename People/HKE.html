HKE

Active: 2014–present
Based: UK (per label geography on most releases)
Labels: Dream Catalogue (primary home, 2018–present), Olde English Spelling Bee (2016–2017), BLCR Laboratories, self-released

HKE is the primary recording alias of David Russo, a prolific vaporwave, ambient, and electronic producer operating under an enormous number of monikers. He is best known under the HKE name for two releases on Olde English Spelling Bee — Omnia (OESB-99, 2016) and the double-LP Dragon Soul (OESB-200, 2017) — and for his ongoing output on Dream Catalogue, where he has released under both HKE and his alias Hong Kong Express.

Overview

Russo began releasing music in 2014, initially under the Hong Kong Express alias on Dream Catalogue, a UK-based digital/cassette label run in the orbit of the vaporwave and lo-fi electronic scenes. The HKE name emerged alongside Hong Kong Express as a parallel identity, with the two aliases sharing identical Discogs real-name attribution (David Russo) and overlapping alias lists.

The scale of his alias practice is exceptional even for the vaporwave scene, where pseudonymous release under multiple names was common. Discogs documents over 80 known aliases for the artist under the main ID, ranging from Japanese-script monikers (ニューシティ, チェスマスター, 中空マスク) to English-language personas (Hong Kong Express, Sandtimer, Sleep Center™, DARKPYRAMID, Silk Demon, Vaporsleuth, Zero Lucid). Several of these (especially Sandtimer) have their own release histories as distinct Discogs artist entries but share the same alias list, confirming shared identity.

His music spans vaporwave (sample-based, heavily processed, nostalgic), ambient techno, IDM, and darker experimental electronic territory. The Dragon Soul LP (2017) typifies his approach: side-length ambient pieces alongside harder-edged tracks, a cosmological/spiritual naming vocabulary (Dragon Blood, God Power, Eternal Return), released on vinyl with etched runouts bearing phrases like “DRAGONS ARE REAL.”

Key Releases — Detail

Gateway アセンション (2015)

Label: Baro Records (BARO50) | Format: Digital (MP3) | With: Telepath テレパシー能力者

A split release structured so each artist contributed a side: HKE contributed tracks 1–3 (“From Dust,” “Spiral,” “Light Patterns”), Telepath contributed tracks 4–6. Styles: Ambient, Broken Beat, Tech House, Techno, Vaporwave. One of the primary collaborations linking the two artists, who have 7 shared releases in total on Discogs.

Omnia (2016)

Label: Olde English Spelling Bee (OESB-99) | Format: Digital (MP3, 12 tracks)
Styles: Ambient, Broken Beat, IDM, Vaporwave

Tracks: Dream / Scorpion Queen / Hand In Throat / Sphere / Odium / Paralysis / Zero / Omni / As Foretold / Requiem / Restore / Awake. No additional personnel credited on Discogs. 3 pressings/editions.

Dragon Soul (2017)

Label: Olde English Spelling Bee / Series 200 (OESB-200) | Format: 2xLP (double vinyl, 12 tracks across 4 sides)
Styles: Ambient, Experimental, IDM, Techno

Tracks: Dragon Blood / Inverted Sandtimer / Dream Angel // Transcended Master HKE / Alone Together / Playful Souls // Dragon Circle / Eternal Return / God Power // Future Life / Love / Fire.

The first release on the Series 200 sublabel imprint. The lacquer was cut by Carl Saff (credited on Discogs, though this is a manufacturing-chain credit). Vinyl runouts are etched: Side A “OESB-200-A HKE SAFF,” Side B “TRASCENDED MASTER SAFF,” Side C “DRAGON SOUL SAFF,” Side D “DRAGONS ARE REAL SAFF.” Track B2 (“Inverted Sandtimer”) directly references the Sandtimer alias.

HKE x ZOMBY (2022)

Label: Dream Catalogue | Format: Digital

Collaboration with Zomby (Justin Moulds), the UK electronic artist known for minimal techno and jungle/grime-adjacent work on labels including XL Recordings. 3 Discogs pressings.

The SQ777 Series (2017)

In 2017 HKE released a seven-volume series under the “SQ777” banner, each volume issued on a different small label across multiple countries (Netherlands, US, Canada, Ukraine, UK, Norway). The releases were: SQ777-1: No Man Is God (BLCR Laboratories, Netherlands), SQ777-2: Heel Aesthetic (H.V.R.F. Central Command, US), SQ777-3: Emotionless Passion (Kudatah, Canada), SQ777-4: High Kinetic Energy (Antifur, Ukraine), SQ777-5: Modern Esoteric (House of the Leg, UK), SQ777-6: 16-Cell (SHUT_INS, US), SQ777-7: Sacred Code (Encrypted) (Asura Revolver, Norway). Each entry has 2–3 Discogs pressings. The multi-label dispersal pattern — one international label per volume — is characteristic of the vaporwave micronetwork release model.

Connection to 2814

The Discogs page for 2814 (ID: 4417063) credits members as “t e l e p a t h テレパシー能力者” and “Hong Kong Express” — the latter being the confirmed Russo alias (Discogs ID 4004566, realname: David Russo). This confirms HKE/David Russo as one half of 2814, the vaporwave/ambient duo whose 新しい日の誕生 (Dream Catalogue, 2015) is among the most widely documented vaporwave releases. The Pillar / New Sun release (Arcola, 2018) is also credited to 2814 and listed in both the 2814 and HKE Discogs discographies.
