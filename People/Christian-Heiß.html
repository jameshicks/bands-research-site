Oct 22, 20264 min read

Christian Heiß

Discogs ID: 629253
Alias: Taison Heiß (also credited as “Taison”, “C. Heiß”, “Heiß”, “Christian Heiss”)
Origin: Munich, Germany (originally Weilheim area)

Keyboardist and electronic musician. Core member of Lali Puna, credited with keys/computer from Faking The Books (2004) onward. Also a member of IDM/electronic project Portmanteau (as Taison) and Dolby Buster. Session and mixing work for Saroos (Alien Transistor, 2006).

Career

Weilheim / Munich Background

Portmanteau’s Discogs profile places Heiß’s roots as “Ex-Weilheim now in Munich,” linking him to the Weilheim scene associated with The Notwist, Markus Acher, and the Alien Transistor label orbit. His Taison alias appears on Portmanteau releases and on some Saroos credits.

Lali Puna

Lali Puna is a Munich electronic-pop band founded by Valerie Trebeljahr after her earlier outfit L.B.Page dissolved. The original lineup included Trebeljahr, Markus Acher, Christoph Brandner, and Florian Zimmer. After lineup changes the band settled into a trio: Trebeljahr (vocals, keyboard, computer), Heiß (keyboard, computer), and Brandner (drums).

Faking The Books (2004, Morr Music 044 mm): Compositions credited variously to Markus Acher, Valerie Trebeljahr, and (on “Left Handed”) Max Punktezahl. Performers: Heiß, Christoph Brandner, Markus Acher, Valerie Trebeljahr. Guest: Osamu Nambu (violin on “Alienation”), Sebastian Hess (cello on “Crawling By Numbers”). Produced, recorded, and mixed by Mario Thaler; mastered by Chris Blair.

Our Inventions (2010, Morr Music MM098): Heiß co-wrote most tracks with Trebeljahr (and some with Acher); also served as recording engineer and performer. Mixed by Oliver Zülch; mastered by Sean Magee. Notable guest: Yukihiro Takahashi (YMO) featured on “Out There” with additional production by Tomohiko Gondo.

Two Windows (2017, Morr Music morr 156): Heiß co-wrote all tracks with Trebeljahr. Notable guests: Dntel (Jimmy Tamborello) — additional performer on “The Frame”; Mary Lattimore — harp feature on “Bony Fish”; Radioactive Man — additional on “Head Up High.” One track (“The Bucket”) is a Kings of Leon cover. Mastered by Frank Arkwright; co-produced and mixed by Mario Thaler.

Saroos (2006)

Heiß played keyboards on track B4 (“One”) of Saroos’s self-titled debut LP (2006, Alien Transistor N16) and mixed several tracks (A2, A3, B1, B2, B4, B5) under his “Christian ‘Taison’ Heiß” credit. Saroos is a Weilheim/Munich electronic/hip-hop group whose members include Florian Zimmer and Christoph Brandner (both ex-Lali Puna) as writers, plus Max Punktezahl (bass, guitar). The album was mastered by Bo Kondren.

Portmanteau

Portmanteau is a Munich IDM/electronic project (Discogs ID 103597) in which Heiß appears as “Taison.” The profile identifies three members: Zetto (Christian Ziegler / Generalmidi, keys/laptop), Taison (Heiß, keys/laptop, ex-Schweisser), and Greulix (Gerald Schrank, drums, ex-Schweisser). Releases:

  • Cosy (2002, self-released) — Heiß as composer, producer, mixer
  • Akja (2008, BSC Music / Biegen & Brechen)

Dolby Buster

Dolby Buster (Discogs ID 978664) is a duo of Heiß and Michael Schwaiger. No releases visible in Discogs beyond the group listing.
