Ben Lurie

Guitarist, bassist, and producer. Born 13 June 1968 in London, England. Best known as a member of The Jesus And Mary Chain from 1989 to 1998. Discogs artist ID: 324262.

Overview

Ben Lurie was born in London and moved to Australia aged four. In high school he formed Sons Of Sorrow with bassist Warwick Yuen and drummer Paul Zezula, releasing the album Nobody Should Dream in 1988 on Rampant Releases (Australia). He returned to London at twenty and joined The Jesus And Mary Chain initially as a session guitarist for the Automatic (1992) tour, progressing to a regular session and then full band role. He played bass and guitar on the key JAMC records Stoned & Dethroned (1994) and Munki (1998), and contributed bass and guitar to singles and EPs through the mid-to-late 1990s.

After JAMC dissolved in 1998, Lurie co-founded Freeheat with Jim Reid, alongside ex-The Gun Club members Nick Sanderson and Romi Mori. The band released a single, “Don’t Worry, Be Happy” (Hall Of Records, 2000), the EP Retox (Outafocus Recordings, 2002), and a later music-only credit on “Back On The Water” (2006). Freeheat’s Discogs primary output is limited but they were active live in the early 2000s.

Outside JAMC and Freeheat, Lurie worked as a producer and engineer. He co-produced The Parkinsons’ debut album A Long Way To Nowhere (Fierce Panda, 2002) with Jim Reid. He engineered and mixed Trash Heart (2005) for Riff Random. He co-produced, co-wrote, and performed on Sister Vanilla’s Little Pop Rock (P-Vine Records, 2005; UK release 2007) alongside Jim Reid, William Reid, and Linda Reid, and co-produced the Sister Vanilla single “Can’t Stop The Rock / What Goes Around” (Chemikal Underground, 2007). Wikipedia also credits him with producing records for Tompaulin, though this is not reflected in Discogs credits.

Lurie moved back to Australia in 2004 and works as a graphic designer. According to Wikipedia (sourced from a 2007 interview), he was active in music work through at least that period despite the relocation.

Name variations on Discogs: B. Lurie, Ben Laurie, BenLurie, Benluri, benlurie.
