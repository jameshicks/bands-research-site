Money Mark

Discogs ID: 3880 Real name: Mark Ramos-Nishita (also as Discogs 252213) Aliases: The Freeway Keyboardist Active: early 1990s–present

Keyboard player and musician. 85 releases on Mo Wax (1994–2006). Known as a collaborator and touring keyboardist for the Beastie Boys. Melodica (1996, MWSSLP001, 19 pressings) is his most-pressed Mo Wax release.
