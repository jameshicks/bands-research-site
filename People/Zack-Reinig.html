Producer, recording engineer, and mixing engineer based in the US. Operating under the banner of EM Recording, Reinig worked across the indie rock, post-rock, and singer-songwriter scenes of the Pacific Northwest and wider US throughout the late 1990s and 2000s. His name appears on Discogs under two common variants: Zach Reinig and Zack Reining.

Career Overview

Reinig’s earliest documented credit is as assistant engineer on Crystal Lewis’s gospel album (Hymns) My Life (1995, Metro One), a large-budget CCM session — likely a formative studio apprenticeship. The same year he handled full production duties on Stray Dog Blues (1995, Stray Dog Productions) — recorded, mixed, mastered, and produced.

By 1999 Reinig was working in proximity to some of the era’s key indie rock projects. He served as assistant engineer on Built To Spill’s Keep It Like A Secret (Virgin, 1999) — the album produced by Doug Martsch and Phil Ek — a significant session at the intersection of Pacific Northwest guitar rock and major-label indie. On the same label circuit he was second engineer on The Black Heart Procession’s 2 (Touch And Go, 1999), produced by Ryan Hadlock.

The early 2000s saw Reinig step up to full recorder/mixer credits. He recorded and mixed The Black Heart Procession’s Three (Touch And Go, 2000) alongside Ryan Hadlock and the band themselves. On Amore Del Tropico (Touch And Go, 2002) he served as sole mixing engineer — the band’s most orchestrated album, featuring cello, upright bass, trumpet, violin, and choral vocals. This is his highest-profile documented credit.

In 2002 Reinig also recorded several tracks for Pinback’s More Or Less Live In A Few Different Places tour EP, working alongside Pall Jenkins and Armistead Burwell Smith IV (Pinback members who also handled some live recording). That same year he produced and mixed records for Jen Wood (Traveling Through Roots, self-released) and an unnamed project under the Woodson Lateral imprint (The Way The Wind Whips The Water).

From 2004 onwards Reinig’s discography diversifies. He engineered and mixed City Of The Stars (2004) for Seattle heavy rock band Akimbo (Seventh Rule Recordings) and co-produced / mixed Young Days (2004) for Hint Hint on Suicide Squeeze. In 2006 he mixed Viva Voce’s Get Yr Blood Sucked Out (Full Time Hobby) alongside the band; engineered and mixed the debut self-titled record for Sera Cahoone (self-released); and co-produced, mixed, and engineered …And We Shall Call Him Joseph (2006, Sad Robot) for Hypatia Lake alongside Dave Hillis.

His last documented Discogs credit is as co-producer of Sera Cahoone’s Only As The Day Is Long (Sub Pop, 2008), her Sub Pop full-length debut — a folk-country record that received wide critical attention.
