Overview

Christopher Uhe is a German vocalist, guitarist, songwriter, and producer working in indie rock, lo-fi, and experimental styles across a career stretching back to 1989. He has been a member of Flowerpornoes, Floor (7), Evonike, Great Tuna!, and Julia Hummer & Too Many Boys, with label relationships ranging from Glitterhouse Records and Columbia in the mid-1990s to Staatsakt. and Earsugar in the mid-2000s through 2010s. His 59 credits place him in sustained orbit with a cluster of Berlin experimental figures — Schneider TM, Rashad Becker, and Dirk Dresselhaus each appear across multiple shared releases, suggesting deep scene ties beyond any single project.
