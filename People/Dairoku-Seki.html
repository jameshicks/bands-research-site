Dairoku Seki

Drummer. Born Tokyo, Japan. Real name: 関大陸 (Seki, Dairoku) / ダイロク. Discogs artist ID: 706417. Founding member of post-rock/screamo band Envy (2) (1992–2018), one of Japan’s most internationally recognised experimental hardcore acts. Departed alongside founding guitarist Masahiro Tobita in February 2018 when Envy restructured its lineup. Also active in the Tokyo hardcore adjacent projects Dove (12), Speak For Myself, Smelling Cunts, and Storm Of Void.

Name variations credited on Discogs: D.Seki, Dairoku, Dauroku, Seki Dairoku.

Overview

Seki was a central figure in Envy’s sound across their entire formative and internationally successful period — from the self-released 1996 debut through the Temporary Residence Limited era that ended with Atheist’s Cornea (2015). His drumming underpins every significant Envy release, including the breakthrough All The Footprints You’ve Ever Left And The Fear Expecting Ahead (2001), the Level Plane debut A Dead Sinking Story (2003), and the TRL/Rock Action LPs Insomniac Doze (2006) and Recitation (2010). He and Tobita were the only two Envy founding members to leave the band; their departure was announced February 6, 2018, when the band announced a new lineup. Hiroki Watanabe (of Heaven In Her Arms) replaced Seki on drums.

Alongside Envy, Seki was active in several concurrent Tokyo projects. Dove (12) was a concurrent early band sharing members with the Envy orbit. Speak For Myself — a split 2000 release with Dove — was a further Tokyo hardcore project. Storm Of Void (2013, Sonzai Records) was an international collaboration with UK guitarist Stanley George Bodman.
