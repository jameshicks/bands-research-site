Overview

David Friedman is an American conductor, vocal arranger, music director, and composer whose career centres on the intersection of Broadway musical theatre and Hollywood animation. He is best known as the vocal arranger and conductor on the core Disney Renaissance soundtracks of the early-to-mid 1990s — Beauty and the Beast, Aladdin, Pocahontas, and The Hunchback of Notre Dame — and for his recurring role on Broadway cast recordings adapting those same properties for the stage.

On Discogs he carries approximately 352 release credits on Walt Disney Records and 105+ shared credits with Stephen Schwartz, making him one of the most persistent behind-the-scenes figures on the Disney musical canon.

Beyond Disney he has worked as producer and songwriter under his own Midder Music imprint, and composed music for the 2013 Broadway show Scandalous (with Kathie Lee Gifford as lyricist). He also released a songwriter album, Listen to My Heart: The Songs of David Friedman (2003, Midder Music), as the sole primary artist under his own name.

Disney Renaissance Work (1991–1998)

Friedman’s role on Disney’s animated feature soundtracks was consistent across the peak years of the Disney Renaissance: vocal arrangements, conducting the vocal recording sessions, and vocal contracting (assembling and managing the choral/session singers). He worked under the creative authority of Alan Menken (music) and the executive oversight of Chris Montan (Walt Disney Records).

Primary credits: Menken, Ashman, and Tim Rice (Howard Ashman died in 1991; Tim Rice wrote the additional songs). Orchestrations again by Danny Troob; Botnick on recording and mastering.

Discogs release ID: 1913343 — Walt Disney Records, 58 pressings

Credits: Arranged By [Vocals], Conductor, Contractor [Vocal Contractor]. Primary credits: Menken and Stephen Schwartz (Schwartz’s first Disney film as lyricist). Additional orchestrations by Michael Starobin and Douglas Besterman alongside Danny Troob.

Credits: Arranged By [Vocal Arrangements By], Contractor [Vocal Contractors]. Primary credits: Menken and Schwartz. Songs conducted by Jack Everly (not Friedman on this film); orchestrations by Michael Starobin and Danny Troob.

Broadway Cast Recordings

Beauty and the Beast Broadway OBC (1994)

Discogs release ID: 6797878 — Walt Disney Records (Germany), 22 pressings

The Broadway production conductor was Michael Kosarin; Friedman handled the vocal/choral side. Orchestrations by Danny Troob and Michael Starobin; produced by Alan Menken and Bruce Botnick.

Composer Credits

Scandalous Broadway OBC (2013)

Discogs release ID: 11521745 — Shout! Factory

Friedman is credited as Music By (alongside David Pomeranz), with Kathie Lee Gifford as lyricist. The show starred Carolee Carmello as evangelist Aimee Semple McPherson. This is Friedman’s only Broadway show where he is the primary musical author rather than a behind-the-scenes arranger/conductor.

Aladdin and the King of Thieves (1996)

Discogs release ID: 12316750 — Walt Disney Records, 3 pressings

Credits: Chorus, Words By, Music By — a direct-to-video Disney sequel for which Friedman contributed original songs as composer and lyricist, alongside his usual choral role.
