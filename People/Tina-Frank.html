Tina Frank

Austrian graphic designer and media artist. Born 1970, Tulln, Austria. Discogs artist ID: 316348. Professor of graphic design at the University of Art and Design in Linz. Long-term visual collaborator with Mego and Editions Mego; one of the defining designers of the Vienna experimental electronic music scene from the mid-1990s onward.

Website: http://www.tinafrank.net

Overview

Frank’s entry into record sleeve design came through the mid-1990s Vienna electronic underground, working almost exclusively for Mego and its circle from the label’s earliest releases. Her first credited Mego sleeve was Fridge Trax (1995) by General Magic & Pita. She went on to design a substantial portion of the label’s catalog through its 2005 closure, and then continued without break into Editions Mego when Peter Rehberg relaunched the operation in 2006. By Discogs count she is the third-most-prolific designer on Editions Mego (30 releases), behind Stephen O’Malley (82) and Dave Coppenhall (38).

Beyond sleeve work, Frank has been active in digital real-time visualisation, video art, and multimedia — areas she began developing concurrently with her print/design work in the mid-1990s. Her practice spans print, corporate design, signage systems, and music visualisation. She has also been a member of the artist groups Chicks On Speed, Esognomig, and Skot (2).

The Discogs record credits her in several roles beyond “Design” and “Cover”: Layout, Artwork, Typography/Typesetting, and — on the Oren Ambarchi/Fennesz/Pimmon/Rehberg/Rowe collaboration Afternoon Tea (Black Truffle, 2009) — Photography.

Career in Stages

1994–1995: Earliest Credits

Frank’s first Discogs credit is This Is Loop Part 1 (1994, Labworks Germany), under the role “Graphics.” Her first Mego credits follow in 1995: Fridge Trax (General Magic & Pita) and UFO Beobachtungen ‘93-‘95 (Pita), both credited as “D’sign,” and Instrument by an unnamed artist (Artwork/Cover). Simultaneously she was working for the German label Virtual Recordings — Acid Files #3 (1995), Subway 26 (1995), and the Virtual Label Compilation - 1st Issue (1995, cover) — giving her a dual footing in the Vienna and German experimental techno/electronic worlds.

1996–1999: Core Mego Visual Identity

Through the second half of the 1990s Frank was the dominant designer for Mego:

  • Seven Tons For Free (1996, Pita) — Cover
  • Amore Motore (… Autobahn) (1996, Mego) — Cover
  • Hotel Paral.lel (1997, Fennesz) — Design
  • Ich Schnitt Mich In Den Finger (1997, Mego) — Cover
  • Plays (1998, Mego) — Artwork/Cover
  • Get Out (1999, Pita) — Cover

Outside Mego during this period: Faßt (1997, Rehberg & Bauer, Touch) — shared design credit with Jon Wozencroft; Post.Art (1997, Funkstörung, Chocolate Industries) — uncredited design; Viennatone (1997, various, !K7) — design alongside inwirements; Concorde+ (1999, Cheap) — Cover and Design; Submission (1999, Breakin’ Records) — Artwork.

The Viennatone compilation (a portrait of the Vienna electronic scene) is a significant item — Frank’s design contextualised the city’s sound alongside the Mego aesthetic.

2000–2005: Peak Mego Era

The period 2001–2005 represents the densest concentration of her most-documented work:

  • Chicks On Speed Will Save Us All! (2000, Chicks On Speed / Chicks On Speed Records) — credited as “Web Mistress” — the only credit in this role across her Discogs listing; her group membership (she is listed as a Chicks On Speed member) makes this a band rather than purely freelance context.
  • Endless Summer (2001, Fennesz, Mego) — Cover. Mego 035; the label’s canonical release internationally, blending guitar noise with melodic ambient. Mastered by Street Romance; photography by Hertha Hurnaus.
  • Asuma (2001, Ilpo Väisänen, Mego) — Cover.
  • I’m Happy, And I’m Singing, And A 1, 2, 3, 4 (2001, Mego) — Cover (credited “Animals For Emma”).
  • Reconnaissance (2001, Oren Ambarchi & Martin Ng, Staubgold) — Cover shared with Inwirements.
  • A Taste Of… (2002, Mego) — Cover.
  • Floppy Nails (2002, Mego) — Cover.
  • Electric Electric (2003, Mego) — Cover.
  • Sun Pandämonium (2003, Hecker, Mego) — Cover; shared design credit with Florian Hecker.
  • Hysteria (2004, Quecksilber) — Cover concept.
  • Triste (2005, Southern Lord) — Cover Design.
  • Resurrection River (2005, Vainio Väisänen Vega / VVV, Mego) — Sleeve Design, shared with Normal (4). One of Mego’s final numbered releases. Artists: Mika Vainio, Ilpo Väisänen, Alan Vega.

2006–2015: Editions Mego and Wider Scene

When Editions Mego launched, Frank continued as a primary visual collaborator:

  • Acid In The Style Of David Tudor (2009, Editions Mego) — Typography/Typesetting.
  • Trahnie (2009, Editions Mego) — Cover.
  • Magic & Return (2009, Fenn O’Berg, Editions Mego) — Artwork. The Fenn O’Berg lineup: Fennesz, Jim O’Rourke, Peter Rehberg.
  • The Crackle Of My Soul (2009, Editions Mego) — Design (finalised by).
  • Afternoon Tea (2009, Ambarchi/Fennesz/Pimmon/Rehberg/Rowe, Black Truffle) — Photography (“Oz Pics”). Frank’s only photography credit on Discogs; the release also features design by Stephen O’Malley.
  • 3 Track 12” (2010, Editions Mego) — Typography/Typesetting.
  • Bye And Bye I’m Going To See The King (2010, Heaven And, Staubgold) — Design, shared with Elvira Stein.
  • Z-Rated (2010, Rotorelief) — Cover and Design.
  • 2/8 Bregman 4/8 Deutsch 7/8 Hecker 1/8 Hõller (2011, Presto!?) — Layout.
  • Fa 2012 (2012, Editions Mego) — Layout (uncredited).
  • RETRO-2038 (2013, COH, Editions Mego) — Design.
  • Cutting The Edge (2009, Chicks On Speed, Chicks On Speed Records) — Design (support).
  • Bécs (2014, Fennesz, Editions Mego) — Cover Design. “Bécs” is Hungarian for Vienna; mastered by Rashad Becker.

2016–2025: Continued Activity

Frank has remained active on Editions Mego into the 2020s:

  • Get In (2016, Pita, Editions Mego) — Artwork.
  • Music Vol. (2016, CoH, Editions Mego) — Cover.
  • Get On (2019, Pita, Editions Mego) — Artwork.
  • Ferrum (2020, Electric Indigo, Editions Mego) — Artwork.
  • Akkosaari (2021, Johannes Auvinen, Editions Mego) — Cover.
  • Uploading The Human (2023, UniCAT Recordings) — Artwork.
  • Dracones (2025, Hüma Utku, Editions Mego) — Design.
