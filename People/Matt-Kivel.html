Matt Kivel

Discogs ID: 2641324 Real name: Matt Kivel Active: 2011–present Based: Texas (US) Genres: Folk, Indie Folk, Ambient, Acoustic Primary Labels: Olde English Spelling Bee, Woodsist, Driftless Recordings, Cascine, pyl records

US singer-songwriter based in Texas. Began as a guitarist in indie and garage-pop bands — most notably Princeton (4) and Gap Dream — before pivoting to a solo career around 2013. His solo work pairs muted acoustic songwriting and understated vocals with shimmering ambient synths. His debut full-length Double Exposure (2013, Olde English Spelling Bee) received an 8.0 from Pitchfork. Subsequent albums moved through folk, art-rock, and ambient registers, featuring notable collaborators including Paul Oldham, Alasdair Roberts, Bonnie Prince Billy (Will Oldham), and Robin Pecknold (Fleet Foxes). Also performs as part of Sleeping Bags (2) (with brother Jesse Kivel) and the loose collective superteam.

Biography

Early Bands (2011–2012)

Kivel first appears on Discogs as a member of Sleeping Bags (2), a project shared with his brother Jesse Kivel that released material on Easter Everywhere (2011). Around the same period he played guitar in Princeton (4) — an indie-pop group whose two members are Matt and Jesse Kivel — and appeared on their Remembrance Of Things To Come (2012, Rallye Label, Japan) in an orchestral arranger and performer capacity. He also played bass on Kids In LA (2013, Rallye Label), another Princeton-adjacent release.

A second concurrent affiliation was as guitarist in Gap Dream, a garage-pop band. Around 2013 he left both bands to focus on solo material.

Solo Debut: Double Exposure (2013)

Kivel’s first major solo statement, Double Exposure (2013, Olde English Spelling Bee, cat# OESB-77, also licensed to Burger Records), was produced and engineered by Mark Nieto, with mixing and mastering by Paul Oldham. The album drew on limited-run cassette releases he had begun circulating earlier. Pitchfork rated it 8.0. Cover painting by Max Markowitz; photography by Olivia Hemaratanatorn.

Days Of Being Wild (2014)

Second album Days Of Being Wild (2014, Woodsist) was produced, recorded, and mixed by Paul Oldham, with additional recording and mixing by Jarvis Taveniere (Woods). Mastered by Timothy Stollenwerk. Artwork again by Max Markowitz; photography by Olivia Hemaratanatorn. The Discogs profile states the album was recorded with “Palace Brothers member Paul Oldham over the course of one summer.”

The same year Kivel released the collaborative EP Kivel And Woulfe (2014, Apollonian Sound) with Tim Woulfe.

Janus (2015/2016)

Third album Janus (Driftless Recordings, cat# none on promo CDr; main pressing labelled 2015–2016) was recorded in Glasgow, Scotland and produced by Alasdair Roberts, Scottish folk musician. The album is credited as Folk/Art Rock on Discogs. Ten tracks including “Janus,” “Violets,” “Pyrrha,” “Prime Meridian,” “Orpheus.”

Fires On The Plain (2016)

Fourth album Fires On The Plain (2016, Driftless Recordings, cat# DFS024, double LP) is one of the most ambitious entries in his catalog — a sprawling 26-track release across four sides, mixing songs, instrumental interludes, and field recordings. Produced by The Tonys (3); recorded by Chris Votek; mixed by Kenny Gilmore; mastered by Paul Oldham. Artwork by Max Markowitz; layout/design by Patrick McDermott (2) (also known as North Americans).

Vocal collaborators on specific tracks:

  • Bonnie Prince Billy (Will Oldham) — lead and harmony vocals on “Forgiveness” (B3)
  • Robin Pecknold (Fleet Foxes) — lead and harmony vocals on “Permanence” (B4); harmony vocals on “Candy” (B8)
  • Jesse Kivel — backing vocals and percussion on “Velveteen” (A6)
  • Sophia Knapp — lead vocals and electric guitar on the instrumental interlude “V” (C1)
  • Emily Hunt Kivel — backing vocals on “Other Shore” (C6) and “Gaudette” (C7)

Instrumental contributors: Chris Kallmyer and Chris Votek (co-credited on the seven instrumental interludes), Andrew Lessman (drums), Gilles O’Kane (bass/guitar/effects), Patrick McDermott (guitar), Frank Silva (3) (saxophone), Gregory Uhlmann (guitar), Ben Usen (piano/organ), Joe Santa Maria (saxophone/clarinet), Caleb Miller (guitar), Graham Chapman (4) (acoustic bass), Mike Lockwood (drums).

Last Night In America (2019)

Fifth album Last Night In America (2019, Cascine / pyl records). Kivel recorded and performed all parts himself. Mixed by Jarvis Taveniere; mastered by Andrew Maury. Cover design and typography by Clare Byrne; painting by Louise Sheldon. AllMusic gave the album 4/5 and selected it as an Album Pick.

that day, on the beach (2020)

Ambient instrumental album that day, on the beach (2020, Pyl Records). Kivel composed and performed. Mixed by Al Carlson; mastered by Andrew Maury. Artwork by Louise Sheldon. A departure from his songwriting albums — purely instrumental.

The same year he appeared as vocalist and recording collaborator on Solid Love (2020, Whatever’s Clever), a release with shared primary credits.

Bend Reality ~ Like A Wave (2022)

Full-band album Bend Reality ~ Like A Wave (2022, pyl records). Kivel played acoustic and electric guitar, vocals, and percussion. Band personnel: Eamon Fogarty (baritone guitar, fretless bass, acoustic guitar, organ, synthesizer, piano), Adam Brisbin (bass), Santiago Dietche (bass, harmony vocals, percussion). Engineered by Don Cento. Bonnie Prince Billy (Will Oldham) guests on vocals on tracks A3, B3, and B4. Kivel also released a single “find love” featuring Bonnie “Prince” Billy in summer 2022.
