Darren Walters

Darren Walters

Discogs ID: 927810 Role: Label co-founder Active: ~1990–2011

Co-founded Jade Tree with Tim Owen in Wilmington, Delaware, circa 1990. Operated the label through its full run until wind-down around 2010–2011.

Background and pre-Jade Tree history

Walters grew up in Wilmington, Delaware, which he describes as roughly 25 minutes from Philadelphia. He discovered punk rock around 1985 after his best friend returned from military school with punk records; the two began attending shows together and watched films like Suburbia and The Decline of Western Civilization. He was drawn into the broader D.C./Maryland/Virginia hardcore scene, regularly making the roughly 70-minute drive to shows there. perfectlines.wordpress.com, 2009

His father played accordion and saxophone; as a kid he moved through AM pop (Shaun Cassidy, Kiss), arena rock, and 1980s New Wave before landing on hardcore. udel.edu/PR/Messenger 2003

Hi-Impact Records and Straight Away

Operating on DIY principles, Walters fronted a punk band called Straight Away and founded his first label, Hi-Impact Records, in 1989, releasing 45 rpm vinyl singles focused on straight edge hardcore. One Hi-Impact release reportedly sold 7,000 copies. udel.edu/PR/Messenger 2003 He was running Hi-Impact while enrolled at the University of Delaware as an English major (intending to become a teacher). perfectlines.wordpress.com, 2009

Meeting Tim Owen and Axtion Packed Records

Walters met Tim Owen through a mutual friend — Owen had attended a Washington, D.C. show with that friend and later co-founded Axtion Packed Records with him. Wikipedia’s Jade Tree article gives 1987 as the year Walters and Owen were first introduced at a D.C. punk show; Owen was then a student at Rochester Institute of Technology. Axtion Packed, like Hi-Impact, focused on straight edge hardcore 45s. [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jade_Tree_(record_label)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jade_Tree_(record_label), as of 2025-10-23)

Founding Jade Tree

When both Hi-Impact and Axtion Packed ran into difficulties around the same time, Walters and Owen decided to “combine forces.” Owen proposed creating “a label that wouldn’t be defined by anything” — no genre restrictions, no format rules. The two borrowed money from their parents and co-founded Jade Tree Records in 1990, modeling it on Touch & Go Records (Chicago) and Dischord Records (Washington, D.C.). perfectlines.wordpress.com, 2009; [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jade_Tree_(record_label)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jade_Tree_(record_label), as of 2025-10-23)

Note: Wikipedia names a third collaborator, Carl Hedgepath, as co-founder of Axtion Packed alongside Owen; the interview sources do not name Hedgepath in connection with Jade Tree’s founding. Whether he had any role in Jade Tree is unconfirmed.

Post-founding

During Jade Tree’s growth years Walters remained in Delaware, where he worked in education focusing on disadvantaged students. [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jade_Tree_(record_label)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jade_Tree_(record_label), as of 2025-10-23)
