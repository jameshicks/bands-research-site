Davey Lane

Born David Daniel Lane, January 31, 1981. Australian guitarist, singer, producer, and songwriter. Lead guitarist of You Am I from 1999 onward; frontman of The Pictures; member of The Saints (2) late-period lineup; rhythm guitarist and co-lead guitarist in charity supergroup The Wrights. Also credited as a member of The Jack, Charles Jenkins & The Zhivagos, The Stems, The Fame-Beats, and Tim Rogers & The Twin Set. Discogs artist ID: 444450.

Biography

Early Career and You Am I (1999–)

Lane joined You Am I in 1999 as lead guitarist, replacing an earlier lineup slot. He debuted on the live EP Saturday Night, ‘Round Ten (1999, BMG/Ra Records) and has remained the band’s primary lead guitarist through every subsequent studio album. You Am I — built around vocalist, guitarist and primary songwriter Tim Rogers (2), bassist Andy Kent, and drummer Russell Hopkinson — had already established themselves as one of Australia’s most significant guitar-rock acts through the mid-1990s. Lane’s entry came at the start of their post-peak major-label phase; the band moved from BMG through EMI and eventually to their own YAI Records imprint.

Studio albums with You Am I in which Lane is credited (all as lead guitar/performer):

  • Damage (2000, BMG/RCA) — lead guitar
  • Dress Me Slowly (2001, BMG Australia/RCA) — guitar
  • Deliverance (2002, BMG Australia/RCA) — performer
  • Convicts (2006, EMI) — self-produced with Greg Wales; saxophonists Adam Simmons and Peter Knight (2) on horns
  • Dilettantes (2008, EMI) — produced by Greg Wales; strings arranged by Melanie Robinson
  • Porridge & Hotsauce (2015, Inertia/YAI Records) — band self-produced; Lane credited guitar, vocals, piano and co-writer on tracks 6 and 9
  • The Lives Of Others (2021, Caroline Australia) — band self-produced; Lane co-recorded and co-wrote tracks A6 and B3
  • Tines Of Stars Unfurled (2023, Universal Music Australia/Virgin Music Australia)

Lane is a recurring co-writer on You Am I material alongside Tim Rogers (2).

The Wrights (2005) — Charity Supergroup

In 2005 Lane participated in The Wrights, an Australian supergroup assembled for a charity single in aid of Boxing Day Tsunami relief. The lineup brought together Lane, Bernard Fanning (Powderfinger), Nic Cester (Jet), Phil Jamieson (Grinspoon), Pat Bourke, and Mark Mahar. Their recording of Harry Vanda and George Young’s “Evie Parts 1, 2 & 3” was produced by Harry Vanda and Glenn Goldsmith and performed at WaveAid at the Sydney Cricket Ground on January 29, 2005. Lane plays rhythm guitar on Part 1 and lead guitar on Part 3.

Solo Work

Lane has released solo material under his own name:

  • Atonally Young (2014, Field Recordings (6)) — debut solo album; Lane plays vocals, guitar, bass guitar, and keyboards throughout. Produced by Tony Buchen, with drums by Brett Wolfenden, piano/synth by James Fleming (2), and backing vocals from Fraser A. Gorman. Tim Rogers co-wrote track 3. Mastered by Leon Zervos.
  • I’m Gonna Burn Out Bright (2017, Field Records) — solo single/release
  • Don’t Bank Your Heart On It (2020, Field Recordings) — produced and written by Lane
  • Finally, A Party Record (2025, Cheersquad Records & Tapes) — produced by Lane and Robert Muiños (who also recorded and mixed); drums by Sam Raines, bass by Luke Hodgson; mastered by Jordan Power; liner notes by Tim Rogers. Lane is sole songwriter, lead vocalist, and multi-instrumentalist.

The Pictures

The Pictures is Lane’s primary band vehicle outside You Am I, featuring Brett Wolfenden (drums) and Luke Thomas (4) (guitar/bass). The band formed in Melbourne in 2000 and was active through 2022. Lane is vocalist, guitarist, and principal songwriter throughout. Key releases: self-titled debut EP (2000), a run of singles and EPs on self-released and Illustrious Artists imprints (2001–2005), compilation The Fantastic Sounds Of The Pictures (2007), debut album Kicking Indifference (2008/2009), and the final single “I Can’t Hold It Back” (2022). Early releases co-produced with Simon Knight.

Note: Dirty, Dirty (2012, Shock (2) (label)) is a Jim Keays album on which Lane and Wolfenden appear as session musicians — it is not a The Pictures release.

Session and Production Work

Lane has worked as engineer, producer, and session guitarist for other artists:

  • Songs For Slim (2013, New West Records) — engineer credit on this Slim Dunlap tribute
  • Slow Gum (2015, Milk!/Marathon Artists/House Anxiety Records) — electric guitar, backing vocals, recorded by and mixed by Lane on this Courtney Barnett-adjacent Australian release
  • This Situation (2016, Surfin’ KI Records) — mixed by Lane
  • Anything Could Happen (2017, Fat Possum Records) — lead guitar on this US release
  • Radio Sketches (2021, Silver Stamp Records) — producer, performer, recorded by
  • Somebody Else (2022, Cheersquad Records & Tapes) — producer, written-by
  • 1967: Vacations In The Past (2024, Tiny Ghost Records) — multi-instrumental contributions (backing vocals, keyboards, bongos, guitar, reverse guitar, piano)
  • Jimmy Barnes — My Criminal Record (2019, Bloodlines) — guitar and backing vocals

The Saints (2) — Late-Period Membership

Lane is listed as a member of The Saints (2) on Discogs, alongside the core surviving history of the band around founder and sole constant member Chris Bailey. His most documented contribution is on Long March Through The Jazz Age (2025, Fire Records) — a posthumous Saints release (Bailey died April 2022) assembled from recordings spanning Bailey’s later career. Lane is credited as “additional contributions” musician playing guitar, electric guitar, and ukulele. Other additional contributors on this release include Sean Carey (guitar, banjo, mellotron), Clare Kahn (cello), Shannon Stitt (piano, organ), Andy Judd (2) (piano, organ, mellotron), and Simon Ferenci (trumpet). Core performer credit on the album: Bailey himself on vocals, bass, and acoustic/electric guitar. Drums and percussion: Peter Wilkinson (2). Engineered and mixed by Sean Carey. Mastered by Kathy Naunton.
