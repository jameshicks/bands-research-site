Olivier Alary

French composer, multi-instrumentalist, and electronic musician based in Montreal. Real name: Olivier Alary. Discogs ID: 226452. Also records and releases as Ensemble (Discogs ID: 432). Discogs also lists a separate alias: Hearing Is Our Concern (no discography entries). Member of groups Chlorgeschlecht (ID: 162986) and Danzen Jetzt (ID: 147195).

Overview

Alary began working under the Ensemble alias in early 2000. The project initially consisted of Alary alone with rotating guest vocalists and musicians; it expanded into an official trio with Johannes Malfatti and Darcy Conroy for the 2011 LP Excerpts. His output spans electronic/glitch (Rephlex), post-rock-adjacent ambient (FatCat Records), modern classical (130701, Line), and collaborations across the Björk orbit — co-writing “Desired Constellation” on Björk’s Medúlla (2004) and contributing to the Triumph Of A Heart single B-side (2005). The Björk Utopia (2017) co-production credit cited in some sources is not confirmed by Discogs credits on any pressing; the connection likely derives from his prior co-writing relationship.

Beyond Ensemble, his solo catalog under his own name released on 130701 (FatCat’s modern-classical sublabel): Fiction / Non-Fiction (2016), a remix EP with Ian William Craig (2017), and u,i (2020, co-billed with Malfatti). He also released the solo Pieces For Sine Wave Oscillators (Line, 2017) and Apparitions (Vol. 1) (Line, 2023).

Ensemble: Project History

Formation and Rephlex Debut (2000–2001)

The first release under the Ensemble name was Sketch Proposals (Rephlex, 2000; cat. 101 cd), produced, programmed, and performed entirely by Alary, with Chanelle Kimber (ID: 666087) contributing vocals and electronics. The sleeve was credited jointly to Ensemble and Karex (ID: 1327630). Genres: Abstract / Experimental / Glitch — early IDM-adjacent territory consistent with the Rephlex roster.

In 2001, Ensemble appeared on the Canadian compilation Volt-AA (02) La Grille / The Grid (Oral, Canada) alongside Alexandre Burton (ID: 1353974) and Deadbeat (Scott Monteith, ID: 15056).

Chlorgeschlecht / Side Projects (2003)

Under the group Chlorgeschlecht (ID: 162986), Alary contributed to Unyoga (Deco, France, 2003) alongside Johannes Malfatti and Alex Kloster (ID: 1093804). This appears to be when the Alary–Malfatti working relationship solidified; both are credited for “Music By, Voice.”

Björk Connection: Medúlla and Triumph Of A Heart (2004–2005)

Alary co-wrote track 8, “Desired Constellation,” on Björk’s Medúlla (Polydor, 2004; Discogs release ID 315356). He is credited “Written-By” on that track. The album is otherwise almost entirely vocal-based, arranged and produced by Björk with co-production by Mark Bell on tracks 3, 7, and 9.

In 2005, Alary contributed a B-side to Björk’s “Triumph Of A Heart” single (One Little Indian, 2005; release ID 392939): track 2 is credited “Written-By” to Alary.

FatCat Phase: Guest Vocalists and the Ensemble Identity (2006)

The “Disown, Delete” 7” (FatCat Records, 7FAT24, 2006) marked the first FatCat release. The A-side was co-composed and co-produced by Alary and Chan Marshall (Cat Power), with Marshall singing and Johannes Malfatti providing orchestral arrangements. The B-side “Carmine” features Tamar Amir (ID: 594038) on vocals. Erik Hove (ID: 370463) plays saxophones; Ryan Morey mastered.

The self-titled LP Ensemble (FatCat, FATCD44, 2006) expanded the guest-vocalist model across eight tracks:

Surrounded / Voltaïc: Björk Live (2006, 2009)

Alary is credited “Programmed By” and “Written-By” on Björk’s live release Surrounded (Polydor, 2006) and as “Written-By” on Voltaïc (Polydor, 2009) — both live documents likely including “Desired Constellation.”

FatCat Digital and Trio Formation (2010–2011)

Envies D’Avalanches (FatCat digital, DS7FAT80, 2010) — a two-track digital single, both tracks produced by Alary and Malfatti. The B-side, “Food For Thought,” is a cover of the UB40 song.

Excerpts (FatCat, FATCD95, 2011) — the first full-length where Darcy Conroy (ID: 1586501) appears as co-composer on tracks 2, 7, 9, and 11, signaling her formal entry into the group. Malfatti co-composed tracks 2, 7, 8, 9, and 11. Engineers: David Bryant (ID: 219835), Paul Johnston (ID: 273167), Tim Gowdy (ID: 2312536). Mastered by Ryan Morey. Sleeve design: Marc Alary. Artwork: Carlos & Jason Sanchez (ID: 11640662). Styles: Ambient / Post Rock.

Note: Discogs lists Excerpts release ID 2543579 as 2010 under the Ensemble primary catalog, and ID 2965869 as 2011 under Alary’s person credits; both refer to the same master (ID: 348784).

Solo Output on 130701 and Line (2015–2023)

Fiction / Non-Fiction (130701, CD13-24P, 2016) — solo LP under own name. Genres: Classical / Stage & Screen / Modern.

Pieces For Sine Wave Oscillators (Line, 2017; release ID 10450235) — solo release, cover image by Daniel Palacios (3) (ID: 6911452).

Remixes (130701, 2017; release ID 10231836) — co-billed with Ian William Craig (ID: 3954633).

u,i (130701, DA13-42, 2020) — co-billed with Johannes Malfatti. Features Kristina Koropecki (cello), Erik Hove (flute, clarinet), Daniel Beilschmidt (organ), John Corban (violin), Hyunjung Ko (vocals), Julián Alary García (vocals — likely family member). Released as WAV files.

Apparitions (Vol. 1) (Line, 2023; release ID 27143349) — solo, no additional credits in Discogs.

Guest guitar credit on Empire Of Time (Forward Music Group, 2015) by Find The Others (ID: 3681889) — track B5, alongside Erik Hove (sax) and Johannes Malfatti (2) (viola) on the same track.
