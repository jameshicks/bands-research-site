From Discogs

A native Texan who began his musical life at the age of seven playing classical piano. Currently resides near Santa Fe, New Mexico, USA (previously lived in Seattle, Washington). Operates the record label 7d Media. His interest in music grew through various instruments: electric bass, electric and acoustic guitar, keyboards, and ultimately the touch guitar. Trey’s web sites promote him as “Touch Guitar Freak, King Crimson Alumnus, Multi-Media Storytell, Music Coach”.

Trey has toured and recorded with Sunday All Over The World, Toni Childs, The Robert Fripp String Quintet, David Sylvian, Vernon Reid, Michael Brook, Eric Johnson (2), John Paul Jones, Toyah, David Hykes of The Harmonic Choir, Security Project, and notably King Crimson.

In 1992 Trey was asked to join David Sylvian & Robert Fripp in a collaborative project that grew from a three-piece into a fully-fledged rock band — touring Japan and Europe in both configurations. The band released "" and a live album, "". During this period Trey also recorded his first solo album "", released on Discipline Records (which was renamed Discipline Global Mobile / DGM in 1995).

Trey was a member of King Crimson from the ‘Double Trio’ formation in 1994 until the ‘Double Duo’ concluded touring on November 19th 2003. During the late 1990s his role in the ‘Double Duo’ formation of King Crimson (Belew, Fripp, Gunn and Mastelotto), helped evolve a new and unique instrument — the Warr Guitar. Overview

Texas-born multi-instrumentalist who began on classical piano at age seven and worked through electric bass, guitar, and keyboards before adopting touch guitar — specifically the Warr Guitar, an instrument he helped evolve during the late-1990s King Crimson “Double Duo” formation — as his defining voice. He was a member of King Crimson from the 1994 “Double Trio” lineup through the conclusion of “Double Duo” touring in November 2003, accruing over 600 shared credits with Robert Fripp and nearly 500 with Pat Mastelotto. His wider network includes David Sylvian (a 1992 collaborative project that toured Japan and Europe), Tony Levin, Bill Bruford, and David Bottrill, and he has participated in Gordian Knot, KTU, and the Deep Energy Orchestra. He operates the 7d Media label from New Mexico; his catalogue runs across Discipline Global Mobile, Panegyric, and Inner Knot, spanning the 1990s into the 2020s.
