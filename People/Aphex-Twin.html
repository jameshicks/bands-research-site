Aphex Twin

Discogs ID: 45
Real name: Richard David James
Born: 18 August 1971, Limerick, Ireland (raised in Cornwall, England)
Genres: Electronic, IDM, ambient techno, acid, drill’n’bass
Labels: Mighty Force, R&S / Apollo, Warp Records, Rephlex Records (co-founder)

Richard David James — releasing primarily as Aphex Twin — is the central figure of 1990s British electronic music and one of the most influential producers in the IDM/experimental electronic tradition. Raised in Lanner, Cornwall; later relocated to London. Co-founded Rephlex Records (ID 107) in 1991 with Grant Wilson-Claridge from a P.O. Box in Redruth, Cornwall. Signed to Warp Records in 1993 — his arrival on Warp aligned with the label’s pivot from bleep techno toward the longer-form “Artificial Intelligence” aesthetic it helped define.

Label History

Mighty Force / early independents (1991)

Debut: Analogue Bubblebath (1991, Mighty Force). Established the “Analogue Bubblebath” series of DIY acid records before RDJ had a major deal.

R&S Records / Apollo (1992–1993)

Selected Ambient Works 85–92 (1992) released on Apollo, the ambient sublabel of Belgium’s R&S Records (catalog AMB LP 3922). Artwork uncredited on release but attributed to Paul Nicholson (2) (alias Number 3, London), who also designed the classic Aphex Twin logo c.1991–1992. Xylem Tube EP (R&S, 1992).

Warp Records (1993–present)

First Warp release: On EP (WAP39, 1993) — sleeve design by The Designers Republic. Warp relationship continued across 30+ years with only occasional gaps. Also released Polygon Window — Surfing On Sine Waves on Warp’s Artificial Intelligence series (1992/1993).

Rephlex Records (1991–2014, co-founder)

See Rephlex Records. Co-founded 1991 with Grant Wilson-Claridge. Label served as home for Caustic Window, GAK, and Bradley Strider releases, as well as the broader Rephlex roster (DMX Krew, Cylob, Leila, Bogdan Raczynski, µ-Ziq, Luke Vibert). Closed 2014 after 713 releases.

Key Discography

Selected Ambient Works 85–92 (1992)

Apollo AMB LP 3922. Debut full-length; recording dates span 1985–1992. Defines the melodic ambient techno mode. Artwork: Paul Nicholson (2) (uncredited on release).

On EP (1993)

Warp WAP39. First Warp release. Sleeve: The Designers Republic.

Selected Ambient Works Volume II (1994)

Warp WARP LP 21. Double LP of pure ambient texture — departure from the melodic approach of Vol. I.

GAK EP (1994)

Warp. Under GAK alias.

…I Care Because You Do (1995)

WARP CD30. RDJ credited as “Music By Everything By.” Contains “Ventolin” single. Also includes “Icct Hedral” — the base composition later orchestrated by Philip Glass for the Donkey Rhubarb EP.

Donkey Rhubarb EP (1995)

Warp WAP63. Contains “Icct Hedral (Philip Glass Orchestration)” (8:05) as track 3. Glass took RDJ’s composition and arranged it for orchestra; writing credit shared between Richard D. James and Philip Glass. Production credits: Kurt Munkacsi, Michael Riesman, Anne Pope (recording/mix engineer); Rich Costey additional engineer. RDJ in a 1995 interview (The Lizard Magazine): “I just did a track for one of his projects. It wasn’t quite what I imagined it would be.” 2017 reissue added bonus track “icct hedral [phillip glass dry version+bonus DAT glitches]” (7:55). Michael Riesman — the same conductor who led Aukso/Marek Moś on Warp Works & Twentieth Century Masters (2006) — appears here first as producer.

Richard D. James Album (1996)

WarpCD43. Drill’n’bass period; compressed, hyper-rhythmic. Mastered at Mayking.

Come To Daddy EP (1997)

WAP94. UK Singles Chart #36. Design: The Designers Republic. Image: Chris Cunningham (2). Lacquer cut: Paul Solomons (B-side). Chris Cunningham directed the video.

Windowlicker (1999)

WAP105. UK Singles Chart #16. Design: The Designers Republic. Cover: Chris Cunningham (2). Lacquer cuts: Porky (5) (A-side), Simon Davey (B-side).

Drukqs (2001)

WARPCD92. Double CD, 30 tracks. Mixes programmed drum compositions with prepared piano pieces (“Avril 14th,” “Nanou2”). Artwork: photographs by RDJ only.

Warp Works & Twentieth Century Masters (2006)

Collaborative live performance release. Conducted by Michael Riesman (Philip Glass Ensemble conductor); performed by Aukso chamber orchestra / Marek Moś. Connects the electronic and classical strands of RDJ’s catalog.

Syro (2014)

WARPLP247. First album under the Aphex Twin name in 13 years. Grammy Award: Best Dance/Electronic Album, 2015. Mastered by Beau Thomas (ID 499136). Artwork: The Designers Republic (credited as “MITDR”). Photography: Anastasia Rybina (“puckography”).
