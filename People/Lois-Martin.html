Overview

Lois Martin (29 October 1952, York, PA – 19 November 2025, Manhattan, NY) was a New York-based violist who accumulated 264 recorded credits from 1981 onward, working across modern classical, contemporary jazz, and film score contexts. Her closest collaborators by far are Philip Glass, Michael Riesman, and Kurt Munkacsi — all central to the Glass organisation — suggesting a long and central role in that world. She also appears in ensemble settings ranging from the Crosby Street String Quartet and the Gil Evans Project to the Brian Landrus Orchestra and the Chris Potter Underground Orchestra, with credits spread across Nonesuch, Columbia, Sony Classical, Verve, and Blue Note.
