Overview

John Van Deusen is a multi-instrumentalist — vocals, bass, guitar, percussion, and keyboards — based in Anacortes, Washington, best known as a member of The Lonely Forest alongside close collaborators Braydn Krueger and Nicholas Wilbur. He is also affiliated with Buffet and Telephone Friends. His solo catalogue is modest: a handful of releases scattered across the 2000s, 2010s, and 2020s on a rotating cast of indie imprints including Trans Records, DevilDuck Records, Tooth & Nail Records, and Asthmatic Kitty Records.
