Overview

Katie Jane Garside is an English vocalist, songwriter, lyricist, and visual artist. She is the creative core of two successive British rock projects — Daisy Chainsaw (formed 1989, dissolved 1993) and Queenadreena (formed 1999, active through approximately 2008) — both built around a permanent axis with guitarist Crispin Gray. Her vocal range spans childlike whispers to harsh scream-singing; her live performances in the early years were noted for extreme theatricality and occasional self-harm onstage, a quality that attracted comparisons to Courtney Love (who reportedly cited Garside as one of the “first true riot grrrls”) while Garside herself never claimed the riot grrrl affiliation.

After leaving Daisy Chainsaw in 1993 she retreated into seclusion in the Lake District, resuming activity only when she reconnected with Gray in 1999. Following Queenadreena’s dissolution she pivoted to acoustic, folk-influenced work with her partner Chris Whittingham under the names Ruby Throat (2007–c.2018) and then Liar, Flower (2019–present). She has also maintained a visual art practice and published a poetry collection.
