Overview

Eric Charles Radcliffe (born 3 December 1950, Basildon, Essex) was an English recording engineer and producer who became a defining presence in early British synth-pop through his ownership of Blackwing Studios in London. He engineered and produced landmark early-1980s work for Depeche Mode, Yazoo, and Fad Gadget, cementing a close working relationship with Daniel Miller and the broader Mute Records orbit. Beyond his studio role he appears as a member of The Assembly, Vince Clarke’s short-lived 1983 project with Feargal Sharkey, suggesting a creative involvement that went beyond pure engineering. His catalogue spans more than 1,500 Discogs credits across synth-pop, new wave, experimental, and house, continuing under Mute into the 2020s.
