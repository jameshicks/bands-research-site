Dave Sitek

Dave Sitek

Oct 19, 20266 min read

Dave Sitek

Full name David Andrew Sitek. Born 6 September 1972. Guitarist, producer, multi-instrumentalist, painter, and photographer. Co-founder of TV On The Radio, based in Brooklyn, NY. Discogs artist ID: 260933.

Biography

Early career and TV on the Radio (2001–2014)

Sitek co-founded TV On The Radio in Brooklyn in the early 2000s alongside Tunde Adebimpe and Kyp Malone. He served as the band’s primary producer across all studio albums — Young Liars EP (2003, Touch And Go), Desperate Youth, Blood Thirsty Babes (2004), Return To Cookie Mountain (2006, 4AD/Touch And Go), Dear Science (2008, Interscope/DGC), Nine Types Of Light (2011, Interscope), and Seeds (2014, Harvest). His production approach layered sampler manipulation, multi-tracked guitars, synthesizers, and dense vocal processing into a sound that defined mid-2000s Brooklyn art-rock.

Alongside the band’s own output, Sitek began producing for other artists from 2002 onward, quickly becoming one of the more sought-after alternative producers of the decade.

Outside production (2002–present)

From 2002, Sitek built a substantial outside discography spanning indie rock, post-punk, soul, and art-pop:

  • Daniel Higgs — Here Is Night, Brothers, Here The Birds Burn (2002, Jagjaguwar) — produced, mixed, recorded
  • Yeah Yeah Yeahs — Fever To Tell (2003, Polydor/Dress Up) — produced and mixed; also additional production on Show Your Bones (2006) and produced several tracks on Cool It Down (2022, Secretly Canadian)
  • Liars — They Were Wrong, So We Drowned (2004, Mute) — produced, mixed; also Dance Mother-era material (Telepathe, see below)
  • Celebration — self-titled (2005, 4AD) — produced, mastered; vocalist Katrina Ford later appeared on Maximum Balloon LP
  • Foals — Antidotes (2008, Transgressive Records) — full album producer; Chris Coady engineered; Stuart Bogie and others contributed brass
  • Scarlett Johansson — Anywhere I Lay My Head (2008, ATCO Records) — produced, recorded, and mixed this Tom Waits covers album; Sitek played guitar and sampler on most tracks; David Bowie guested on vocals on “Falling Down” and “Fannin Street”; Jaleel Bunton, Nick Zinner, and Martin Perna contributed
  • Telepathe — Dance Mother (2009, V2/Cooperative Music) — produced
  • CocoRosie — Grey Oceans (2010, Souterrain Transmissions) — co-producer
  • Holly Miranda — The Magician’s Private Library (2010, XL Recordings) — produced; Holly Miranda also guested on the Maximum Balloon album the same year
  • Ximena Sariñana — self-titled (2011, Warner Bros.) — produced
  • Icky Blossoms — self-titled (2012, Saddle Creek) — produced, mixed, recorded; played bass, drum machine, hi-hat, and percussion
  • Lissy Trullie — self-titled (2012, Downtown Music) — produced
  • Lianne La Havas — Is Your Love Big Enough? (2012, Warner Bros.) — produced one track only (“Forget”)
  • Beady Eye — BE (2013, Columbia) — produced; this was Liam Gallagher’s post-Oasis band; Sitek also played sampler, flute, recorder, and processing
  • Holly Miranda — Holly Miranda (2015, Dangerbird Records) — produced; written-by credits shared
  • Weezer — Weezer (Black Album) (2019, Crush Music/Atlantic) — produced and engineered the full album; also engineered several singles around it (“Can’t Knock The Hustle,” “High As A Kite,” “Living In L.A.,” “California Snow”)
  • Wavves — Hideaway (2021, Fat Possum Records) — produced, engineered, and mixed; played additional guitar and synth on most tracks
  • Chelsea Wolfe — She Reaches Out To She Reaches Out To She (2024, Loma Vista) — produced; mixed by Shawn Everett

Other notable credits include mixing for The Rakes (A Call And Response, 2006, Deltasonic), producing Aviv Geffen (2009, Mars Records), co-producing Tom Morello’s The Atlas Underground (2018, BMG), and guitar on Massive Attack’s Heligoland (2010, Virgin).

Maximum Balloon (2010)

Sitek released a solo album as Maximum Balloon in 2010 on DGC. It functioned as a star-studded collaborative LP with a different vocalist on each track:

Other groups

Sitek became a member of Jane’s Addiction (Discogs ID: 35740) at some point after the TVOTR period. He also participated in The Neverly Boys (ID: 6281378) and WitchGang (ID: 14580922).
