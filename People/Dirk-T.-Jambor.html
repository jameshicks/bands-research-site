Dirk T. Jambor

Discogs ID: 1206241 Name variations: Dirk Jambor, Dirk T.Jambor

Mastering engineer based in Germany. Affiliated with True Track Recording Studio (Facebook: truetrackrecording). Active on record from 2004 through at least 2019.

Biography

Jambor is a German mastering engineer whose Discogs credit history spans 2004–2019, all releases either issued in Germany or with a German-market component, with one exception: Thomas Truax’s Songs From The Films Of David Lynch (2009, UK & US). The True Track Recording Studio affiliation is the only facility information available from the Discogs database; no city or region is confirmed in the structured data (the Sony DADC matrix-runout hypothesis from previous research remains unverified at the Discogs level).

His credits cover a notably wide range of German-market genres: punk/hardcore (Wizo), medieval/folk-rock (Die Streuner), hip-hop (Curse (3), La Resistance (2)), R&B/pop (Cassandra Steen, Sarah Connor), Eurodance (DJ BoBo), rock (Wrecking Company, Jansen (2)), and contemporary jazz (Clemens Gutjahr Trio). The Ray Wilson Upon My Life (2019) credit is partial — he mastered specific tracks on a multi-mastering-engineer release, alongside Bob Ludwig and others.

On the Curse (3) Freiheit (2008) release Jambor is credited specifically as “Mastering Vorbereitet Von” (mastering preparation) alongside Sascha Bühren (final mastering), suggesting he sometimes operates at the pre-mastering / preparation stage rather than the final pass.

The 2018 Jansen (2) release Die Unschuld Der Gebäude is the only credit where his role is listed as “Engineer” rather than “Mastered By,” suggesting occasional broader studio work.

Scene Context

Jambor’s work is firmly anchored in the German domestic market. His label associations span:

  • Indie/punk: Hulk Räckorz (Wizo)
  • Medieval/dark folk: Emmuty Records, Totentanz (Die Streuner)
  • German hip-hop: Alles Real Records, Al Dente Recordz (Curse (3), La Resistance (2))
  • Major-label German pop: X-Cell Records / Universal / Warner Central Europe (Sarah Connor, Cassandra Steen, DJ BoBo)
  • Jazz: Double Moon Records (Clemens Gutjahr Trio)
  • Independent UK/US crossover: Psycho Teddy / SL Records (Thomas Truax)

The breadth of genres — spanning punk, medieval folk, hip-hop, mainstream pop, Eurodance, and jazz — is more consistent with a studio taking work-for-hire jobs than a specialist engineer in a single scene. True Track Recording Studio appears to have operated as a general-purpose mastering facility serving a wide client base.

Key Session Co-Credits

On releases where Jambor mastered, recurring engineers and producers in the German market include:
