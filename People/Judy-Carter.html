Biography

Judy Carter is a UK vocalist and contributor best known for her work with Family Fodder, the eclectic post-punk collective active from around 1975. She is listed as a member of Family Fodder on Discogs and is credited on their two key early-period LPs, Monkey Banana Kitchen (1980) and Greatest Hits (1981). She appears both as a vocalist and — unusually — as a co-creator of the original cover artwork for Monkey Banana Kitchen, alongside bandmates Felix Fiedorowicz and Ian Hill.

Beyond Family Fodder, Carter is a member of People In Control, the production collective associated with the Family Fodder inner circle, and her Discogs profile lists a second group affiliation: People In Control (alongside Charles Bullen, Ian Hill, Martin Harrison, and Rick Wilson). People In Control operated primarily as a production entity — they are credited as producers on Monkey Banana Kitchen (1980) and also on releases outside the Family Fodder orbit, including “Te Deum” (1979, Small Wonder Records).

Carter’s credit trail extends to 2015, when she contributed voice to Side B of Life At The Water’s Edge by Officer!, a French avant-rock group, issued on Blackest Ever Black. This places her within a wider network of overlapping UK and European post-punk and experimental figures including Mick Hobbs and Alig, both of whom also appear on that record.

No biographical details — birthdate, nationality beyond probable UK, other non-musical activities — are documented in Discogs. Her alias Judy Jude does not appear to have been used as a primary artist credit on any release in the database.
