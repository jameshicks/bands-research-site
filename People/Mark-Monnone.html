Overview

Melbourne-based songwriter, bassist, guitarist, and visual artist, best known as a core member of jangle-pop act The Lucksmiths, with whom he amassed the bulk of his 171 Discogs credits stretching back to 1993. His primary collaborators—Marty Donald and Tali White—appear across virtually his entire catalogue, with releases through Candle Records, Matinée Recordings, and Fortuna Pop! defining the band’s peak years in the 1990s and 2000s. Beyond The Lucksmiths he has appeared in Last Leaves, Bart & Friends, and Tangible Excitement!, and records under the Monnone Alone alias. He also operates the Melbourne indie imprint The Lost And Lonesome Recording Co. (trading as Lost & Lonesome), which has remained active into the 2020s.
