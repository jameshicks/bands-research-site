Overview

Melanie Campbell is a drummer and core member of Frog Eyes, the Victoria, BC art-rock group led by Carey Mercer, with whom she shares 25 credits — the bulk of her 32-credit Discogs footprint spanning 1999 through the mid-2020s. Her catalogue sits across indie rock, experimental, and art-rock territory, anchored by Soft Abuse in the 2000s and early 2010s and Paper Bag Records from 2015 onward. Credits shared with Spencer Krug and Daniel Bejar place her within the overlapping Victoria/Vancouver scene that produced Swan Lake alongside Frog Eyes. Her output is fairly steady across four decades, with a cluster of five releases in the 2000s, four in the 2010s, and three in the 2020s.
