Overview

Christopher Imes Prescott is an American musician from San Diego, California, active from 1991 into the 2020s as a drummer, guitarist, and vocalist, and also as a recording and mixing engineer through his Belly Of The Whale studio in San Diego. He is best known as a member of No Knife and Pinback, with additional stints in Fishwife, Hemlock, and the Montalban Quintet. His catalogue falls across indie rock, punk, math rock, and hardcore, with core label relationships at Goldenrod Records and Headhunter Records through the 1990s and Dim Mak Records in the early 2000s. His most frequent collaborators — Gar Wood (24 shared releases), Rob Crow, Matt Ohlin, and Zach Smith — point to a sustained presence in the San Diego underground.
