David Immerglück

Discogs ID: 426044
Also known as: “Immy”, “Immer”, “Bindi Boy”; alias listed on Discogs as Ravi Oli
Born: May 3, 1961, Berkeley, California
Role: Multi-instrumentalist (guitars, pedal steel, mandolin, bass, banjo, backing vocals)

Bay Area multi-instrumentalist and longtime collaborator of Counting Crows. A friend of Adam Duritz from the early San Francisco scene, Immerglück played session on all Counting Crows albums from the 1993 debut through the late 1990s but initially declined full membership, citing prior commitments to Camper Van Beethoven and Monks of Doom. He joined as a permanent member in 1999 ahead of This Desert Life. He plays acoustic, electric, and slide guitars, pedal steel, mandolin, banjo, and bass, and contributes backing vocals.

His primary-artist Discogs credits are sparse: one live release co-billed with Jim Bogios (Jimmy / Immy - Live At Rockwood Music Hall, NYC, 2012, Alchemikal Artz).
