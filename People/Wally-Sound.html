Overview

Mark McClellan, credited as Wally Sound, is the owner and operator of The Wally Sound, a recording studio in Oakland, California. His 217 production, engineering, and mixing credits stretch back to 1978 and cut across punk, psychedelic rock, indie rock, and folk rock. His deepest ties are to the Camper Van Beethoven orbit — David Lowery, Greg Lisher, Victor Krummenacher, and Chris Pedersen all rank among his top collaborators, with Pitch-A-Tent and Future Farmer Recordings the labels anchoring that work. A parallel strand runs through the UK Subs, where Charlie Harper, Nicky Garratt, and Alvin Gibbs appear repeatedly across New Red Archives and Cleopatra releases. He also carries an unusually large body of archival work alongside reissue producer Alec Palao for Big Beat Records and BGP, suggesting The Wally Sound served as a studio of record for Ace Records’ reissue programmes as well. Beyond the studio, McClellan performs as a member of JJ-180, The Realtors, and The Wellsprings Of Hope.
