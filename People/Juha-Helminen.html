Juha Helminen

Finnish guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter from Tampere. Real name: Juha Pekka Helminen. Born: 14 April 1958. Died: 10 March 2013. Discogs artist ID: 431917

Biography

Early Career and Kollaa Kestää (1977–early 1980s)

Helminen was a co-founder of Kollaa Kestää in Tampere in 1977, alongside guitarist/vocalist Kai Kivi and drummer Jyrki Siukonen. Bassist Mikko Nevalainen joined in spring 1978 to complete the classic lineup. The band were a central act of the Finnish punk and new wave scene that coalesced around Poko Rekords and Johanna in the late 1970s.

Helminen played guitar and sang throughout the band’s recordings. His first documented recording was the debut EP Kollaa Kostaa (Poko Rekords, 1978, cat. PIMP 014), a four-track 7” produced by Mikko Syrjä (Pantse Syrjä) that opened with a Finnish-language cover of The Adverts’ “One Chord Wonders.” The debut LP Jäähyväiset Aseille (Johanna, 1979, cat. JHN 2004) is the band’s defining statement: 14 tracks, self-produced, with Helminen credited for guitar, vocals, and also cover artwork (shared with Siukonen). The album features Lora Logic on tenor saxophone and backing vocals.

The single “Kirjoituksia Kellarista” b/w “Kuumat Raiteet” (Poko Rekords, 1979, PIS 029) followed the LP the same year.

In 1984, Poko Rekords issued the compilation Neljän Pennun Ooppera (cat. FORMULA 8), a 12” mini-album drawing from the band’s earlier material. Helminen is credited on guitar and vocals.

Cenci (1980)

After Kollaa Kestää’s activity wound down, Helminen formed Cenci with Kai Kivi and Jyrki Siukonen — three of the four core Kollaa members. The only release on Discogs under this name is a 7” single, “Night That Is Almost Afraid Of Me” / “Between Hatred And Pity” (Johanna, 1980, cat. JHNS 118). Both songs are credited as Music By and Words By Cenci collectively. The release is English-language, a departure from the Finnish of the Kollaa recordings.

Punk Lurex OK (1993–2012)

Helminen played guitar in Punk Lurex OK, a punk band from Tampere founded in 1993. The lineup also included Siukonen on drums, Riitta Suojanen and Tiina Wesslin (bass, vocals), Tepi Pulkkinen (vocals), and Sami Päivärinta (drums). The band recorded for Hiljaiset Levyt and the German label Teenage Rebel Records.

Helminen’s credits across Punk Lurex OK releases:

  • Tappajan Silmät (1994, 7” single, Hiljaiset Levyt, HIKS-042) — guitar. The A-side is a Finnish-language version of The Adverts’ “Gary Gilmore’s Eyes” (written by TV Smith).
  • Veljet, Siskot! (1995, CD EP, Hiljaiset Levyt, HILL-021) — guitar, backing vocals; and a painting credit for the artwork. He also co-composed “Viisisataa Päivää” with Riitta Suojanen, with words by Siukonen and Suojanen.

The band’s full discography spans to at least 2012 (Puolesta Ja Vastaan, Kämäset Levyt), though Helminen’s specific credit on later releases is not detailed in the Discogs data.

Visual Art Credits

Beyond his musical roles, Helminen had a recurring visual art practice. He contributed cover artwork to Jäähyväiset Aseille (1979) and the 2010 reissue single “Musti, Sotakoira” (Combat Rock Industry), and an original painting is credited on the Veljet, Siskot! EP sleeve. He is also credited as photographer on the 2011 release Varjoina Kuljemme Kuolleiden Maassa (Spinefarm Records, 9 pressings), which is unrelated to his own bands — the connection to that release is unclear.

Death

Juha Helminen died on 10 March 2013, aged 54.
