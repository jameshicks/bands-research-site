Jerry Garcia

Jerome John Garcia; guitarist, vocalist, songwriter. Discogs artist ID: 259913

Founding member and lead guitarist of the Grateful Dead. Extensive solo career alongside the band; also led the Jerry Garcia Band (Discogs ID: 481382). Died August 1995. Posthumous archival releases — both solo and Jerry Garcia Band — were extensively mastered by Fred Kevorkian (30+ release entries for Garcia, 19 for the Jerry Garcia Band).

Great American String Band

Member of The Great American String Band alongside David Nichtern, David Grisman, Taj Mahal, Richard Greene, and Buell Neidlinger. Nichtern also appeared on the posthumous Garcia anthology (Rhino, 2005) — guitar on track 20 alongside David Grisman (mandolin) on the same track.

Posthumous Archival Label Infrastructure

After Garcia’s death in August 1995, his archive was released across three distinct label vehicles:

Jerry Made

Label used for the “Pure Jerry” live archive series (2004–2009). A Garcia-estate-adjacent imprint. Releases include Pure Jerry: Bay Area 1978 and eight additional volumes documenting Jerry Garcia Band and solo performances. (Wikipedia: Pure Jerry: Bay Area 1978, as of 2025-07-15)

Round Records (re-launched)

The original Round Records was founded in 1973 by Jerry Garcia and Ron Rakow as a companion to Grateful Dead Records, for solo and side-project releases by Dead members. It was distributed by United Artists Records and folded in early 1976. Key original releases: Garcia (1974), Reflections (1976), Old & In the Way (1975).

Round Records was re-launched circa 2013 by the Garcia family/estate (operated by Jerry’s daughters) as the vehicle for archival reissues and posthumous releases going forward. Re-launch releases include Before The Dead (2018, a box set of Garcia’s earliest known performances) and the Might As Well: A Round Records Retrospective compilation. (jerrygarcia.com; JamBase)

ATO Records

Independent label founded in 2000 by Dave Matthews and manager Coran Capshaw. Starting in 2013, ATO became the primary label for the “Garcia Live” series of archival concert releases. Garcia Live Volume One (released February 19, 2013) was the first of a multi-volume series continuing through at least Volume 22 (scheduled 2026). (Wikipedia: Garcia Live Volume One, as of 2025-07-15; ATO Records)

Conflict: “Jemp Records” reference

The previous text in this file (derived from Discogs label data) stated that posthumous Garcia releases were issued “via the Round Records (3) / Jemp Records labels.” This is an error. JEMP Records (Discogs ID: 72582) is exclusively the band-owned label of Phish, named for the initials of Phish’s four members (Jon, Ernest/Trey, Mike, Page), founded 2005 in Burlington, Vermont. No web source — including Wikipedia’s Jerry Garcia discography, the official jerrygarcia.com, deaddisc.com, or AllMusic — shows any connection between JEMP Records and Jerry Garcia. The Discogs credit associating Garcia releases with “Jemp Records” appears to be a data error (possibly a mislabeled label credit on a specific Discogs release entry). The correct label names for posthumous Garcia archival output are Jerry Made, Round Records (re-launched), and ATO Records.
