Overview

Daniel Bejar (born 1972) is a Canadian musician and the founding, only consistent member of Destroyer, his primary vehicle for over 25 years of indie, folk, art, and pop-rock songwriting. He is also a member of The New Pornographers, the Vancouver-rooted indie supergroup, and has participated in smaller collaborative projects including Swan Lake (2) and Hello Blue Roses. His Discogs catalogue runs to more than 310 credits from 1998 onward, anchored chiefly to Merge Records across two and a half decades, with significant releases also on Matador and Dead Oceans. His longest-tenured production collaborators are John Collins and David Carswell, both Vancouver-based engineers who have shaped the Destroyer sound across multiple eras.

Swan Lake — Formation

Swan Lake grew out of pre-existing personal and professional relationships among all three members. Spencer Krug and Carey Mercer had the longest prior connection: Krug had been an on-and-off member of Mercer’s band Frog Eyes since their 2002 debut, and the two had been roommates. Bejar and Krug had also lived together at “the E.18th house for a year,” though Bejar initially knew Krug mainly as an enthusiast of Charles Ives, Texas Swing, and ska rather than as a songwriter; he later learned of early Sunset Rubdown four-track recordings from that period. radiofreecanuckistan.blogspot.com, 2006

The immediate catalyst was Bejar’s Your Blues (2004) touring band. Bejar enlisted Frog Eyes as his backing band to perform the synth-heavy album material live; Krug was subsequently recruited to join a later iteration of that touring band, including a European leg. Playing together as Bejar’s live unit planted the seed for a proper collaborative project. Before a name was chosen, Mercer referred to it informally as “The Songwriters Project.” The band officially formed in Victoria, BC in early 2006. last.fm Swan Lake biography; Exclaim! “Three Men on Swan Lake”

Beast Moans was recorded partly in a summer cottage town and partly in Victoria, at a house Krug and Mercer knew well. Bejar contributed four songs while most of the arrangement and production work was handled by Krug and Mercer in Victoria after Bejar returned to Vancouver. radiofreecanuckistan.blogspot.com, 2006
