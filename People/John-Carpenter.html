John Carpenter

Discogs ID: 32433 Born: January 16, 1948, Carthage, New York Role: Filmmaker, composer, musician Label: Sacred Bones (2015–present)

Director of Halloween (1978), The Thing (1982), Christine (1983), Big Trouble in Little China (1986), They Live (1988), In the Mouth of Madness (1994), and many others. Has composed his own film scores throughout his career. Sacred Bones released his first non-soundtrack album Lost Themes (SBR-123, 2015, 27 pressings) — the label’s most commercially successful LP to that point. Followed by Lost Themes II (2016, 19 pressings), Lost Themes III: Alive After Death (2021, 16 pressings), Lost Themes IV: “Noir” (2024, 12 pressings), plus numerous Halloween film score releases and anthology compilations.

Co-composers on all Sacred Bones material: son Cody Carpenter (ID 2032334) and son-in-law Daniel Davies (ID 1358248).

Sacred Bones — origin of the relationship

Sacred Bones founder Caleb Braaten initiated the relationship, not Carpenter. The connection came through a shared entertainment attorney representing both Carpenter and filmmaker David Lynch (with whom Sacred Bones had already begun working). The attorney proactively contacted Braaten and suggested he might have ideas for Carpenter. Braaten asked whether Carpenter had any unused film-score material; the answer was no. Carpenter then mentioned he had been making music with his son Cody Carpenter in between playing video games, and sent Braaten a CD-R of that material. That CD-R became Lost Themes (2015). Per Braaten: “He said no but offered that he had been making music with his son in between playing video games. I was intrigued, so he sent a CD-R of a bunch of music that became [2015’s] Lost Themes.”

The Billboard 10th-anniversary piece (paywalled, 2017) has been cited as naming the shared attorney as “Mae Ho,” but this has not been confirmed from a directly fetchable source — treat as unverified until confirmed.
