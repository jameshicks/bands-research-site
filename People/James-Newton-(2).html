James Newton (2)

Discogs ID: 268361
Born: May 1, 1953, Los Angeles, California
Real name: James Weldon Newton
Also credited as: J. Newton, James W. Newton Jr., Newton, James Newton Ensemble

American jazz flutist and composer, one of the leading post-free jazz flautists. Played flute on Norris Jones’s debut solo album Artistry (Of The Cosmos, OTC 801, 1979). Active from the mid-1970s through the 2000s across free jazz, contemporary classical, and world music contexts.
