Mitch Easter

American guitarist, drummer, and record producer. Born 1954, Winston-Salem, North Carolina. Discogs artist ID: 228959.

Owner and operator of Drive-In Studio, then Let It Be Studio, in North Carolina. Best known for producing early R.E.M. recordings (Murmur, Reckoning, Chronic Town EP). Member of Sneakers (12) with Chris Stamey and Will Rigby in the 1970s. Longtime studio collaborator of Stamey — 21 shared Discogs releases, appearing as guitarist, drummer, and engineer/mixer on Stamey solo albums from It’s Alright (1987) through Euphoria (2015) and the Big Star’s Third project (2017). Also a drummer in the Big Star’s Third live ensemble.
