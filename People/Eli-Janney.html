Eli Janney

Eli Janney

Oct 30, 20267 min read

Eli Janney

Discogs ID: 23765
Performing alias on Discogs: Silas Greene (ID: 971311 — a separate entry used for his performing credits with GVSB)
Name variations: DJ EJ, E. Janney, Eli “Laï Laï” Janney, Mr. EJ, Ely Janney, Éli Janney
Website: elijanney.com

American producer, mixer, and engineer, born in Washington, DC. Active from 1986 through the 2010s.

Overview

Eli Janney began his career at Inner Ear Studios under Don Zientara starting in 1986 — the same year he played drums in Rain (18), a DC emo/post-hardcore group whose only release, La Vache Qui Rit (1990, Peterbilt Records), was produced by Ian MacKaye (uncredited). That DC period also connected him to Swiz, the straight-edge hardcore band for whom Janney served as engineer and co-producer on their entire catalog (1987–1993) across Hellfire and Sammich Records. He is credited on Down (1987), Swiz (1988), Hell Yes I Cheated (1989), and the later posthumous compilations No Punches Pulled and Rejects (both 1993, Jade Tree / THD Records).

In the early 1990s Janney relocated to New York City and became a full member of Girls Against Boys (GVSB), playing bass, keyboards, sampler, and providing second vocals. His Discogs performing credits with GVSB appear under the alias Silas Greene (Discogs entry 971311), which is used specifically for his band-member role on Tropic Of Scorpio (1992) and Venus Luxure No.1 Baby (1993), while his engineering and production work on those same and later records appears under his own name. Across the GVSB catalog through the mid-1990s he wore both hats: player and engineer/mixer on House Of GVSB (1996, Touch And Go) and You Can’t Fight What You Can’t See (2002, Jade Tree). He also co-produced Freak*on*ica (1998, DGC), the band’s major-label album, though production on that record was led by Nick Launay.

Beyond GVSB, Janney ran a substantial independent production and engineering career. He produced Brainiac’s Hissing Prigs In Static Couture (1996, Touch And Go), crediting himself as “Mr. EJ.” He co-produced Motion City Soundtrack’s Even If It Kills Me (2007, Epitaph) alongside Adam Schlesinger. He mixed three tracks on Holy Fuck’s breakthrough LP (2007, Young Turks), with the remaining tracks mixed by David Newfeld. He worked extensively within the Dischord-adjacent DC scene through the early 1990s, engineering releases for Ted Niceley-produced projects, Nation of Ulysses, Shudder To Think, Soulside, and others.

He is also listed as a member of Edsel (DC post-hardcore) and Americruiser on Discogs, and is credited on the Hedwig and the Angry Inch film soundtrack (2001, keyboards).

Silas Greene — Alias Disambiguation

Discogs carries a separate artist entry for Silas Greene (ID: 971311) with no profile and only two credits, both GVSB releases from 1992–1993 where it appears as “Mr. Silas Greene” in the band member list. This is Eli Janney’s stage name for his performing role in GVSB — not an independent entity. The alias field on Janney’s own Discogs profile (23765) confirms this: “Silas Greene” is listed there as an alias. The Girls Against Boys Discogs page also includes “Silas Greene” as a listed member, which initially created the impression of a distinct fifth member. Janney’s engineering/production credits on the same releases always appear under his real name.
