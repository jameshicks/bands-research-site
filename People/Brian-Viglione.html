Brian Viglione

Discogs ID: 575534
Born: 16 May 1979, Greenville, New Hampshire, USA
Also credited as: Brian “Viggie” Viglione, Brian Vaglione, Brian Vigloine

American drummer. Co-founder and drummer of The Dresden Dolls, the Boston “Brechtian punk cabaret” duo he formed with Amanda Palmer around 2000. After the Dolls’ indefinite hiatus from September 2008, Viglione became one of the more prolific session and touring drummers in the American indie/alt underground, contributing to projects spanning punk cabaret, gothic rock, folk-punk, hardcore, German indie-pop, and contemporary jazz.

The Dresden Dolls Era (2000–2008)

Viglione met Amanda Palmer in Boston and co-founded The Dresden Dolls around 2000, performing as a duo with Palmer on piano/vocals and Viglione on drums. Their debut self-titled LP (2003, 8ft. Records / Important Records) was produced by Martin Bisi and became the defining statement of the “Brechtian punk cabaret” aesthetic. The follow-up Yes, Virginia… (2006, Roadrunner Records) was produced by Paul Q. Kolderie and Sean Slade, saw Viglione playing drums, percussion, vocals, bass, and guitar. A B-sides/rarities companion, No, Virginia…, followed in 2008.

The band went on indefinite hiatus in September 2008. Reunion activity resumed in the 2010s, including live dates and the 2020 self-released recording I’m Going To Go Back There Someday (on which Viglione was uncredited on multiple instruments).

Post-Hiatus Session and Band Work (2008–present)

Nine Inch Nails — Ghosts I-IV (2008)

Viglione was one of three named special guests on Trent Reznor’s Ghosts I-IV (2008, The Null Corporation), alongside Adrian Belew and Alessandro Cortini. Produced by Reznor, Atticus Ross, and Alan Moulder.

black tape for a blue girl — 10 Neurotics (2009)

Viglione produced, mixed, and arranged drums/percussion on black tape for a blue girl’s 10 Neurotics (Projekt, 2009), working with label head Sam Rosenthal as co-producer and co-mixer. Drums were recorded at Martin Bisi’s studio in Brooklyn. Vocal post-production was handled by Rosenthal and Steve Roach.

Botanica (2)

Member of Botanica (2), a New York band that also included Paul Wallfisch (piano/vocals), David Sims (bass, ex-Jesus Lizard), Abby Travis (bass), and Keith Crupi (drums). Discography: Malediction (1999, Checkered Past Records), With All Seven Fingers (2002), Who You Are (2010), What Do You Believe In (2012).

Franz Nicolay — Luck And Courage (2010)

Drummed on Luck And Courage (Decor Records, 2010) by Franz Nicolay (also a member of The World/Inferno Friendship Society), alongside Ken Thomson (saxophone), Yula Be’eri (bass/vocals), and Maria Sonevytsky (piano/vocals).

Phillip Boa & The Voodooclub — Loyalty (2012)

Served as co-producer and multi-instrumentalist (drums, percussion, guitar, bass, piano, synthesizers, backing vocals, marimba) on Phillip Boa & The Voodooclub’s Loyalty (Cargo Records, 2012). Co-produced with Phillip Boa and David Vella; mixed by Ian Grimble; mastered by Frank Arkwright. Loyalty was a significant record in the Boa catalog, Viglione’s most expansive studio contribution outside of the Dresden Dolls.

Morning Glory — War Psalms (2014)

Credited as “Brian ‘Viggie’ Viglione” on drums/percussion for War Psalms (Fat Wreck Chords, 2014) by Morning Glory (Ezra Arrow Kire).

Thomas Truax — Jetstream Sunset (2015)

Drummed on Thomas Truax’s Jetstream Sunset (Psycho Teddy!, 2015). Mixed by Markus Maria Jansen; mastered by Noah Mintz.

Violent Femmes — We Can Do Anything (2016) & Happy New Year EP (2015)

Joined Violent Femmes as touring/session drummer. Credited on drums, percussion, and vocals for:

  • Happy New Year (Add It Up Productions, 2015): also credited on guitar, artwork, and vocals
  • Memory b/w You Move Me (single, 2016)
  • We Can Do Anything (Add It Up Productions / [PIAS], 2016): produced by Jeff Hamilton (2); mixed by John Agnello

Not credited on the subsequent Hotel Last Resort (2019, [PIAS]).
