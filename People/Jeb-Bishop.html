Jeb Bishop

Trombonist, improviser. Chicago, IL. Discogs ID: 254016.

Overview

Jeb Bishop is one of the most widely-credited improvisers in the US experimental and free jazz scene. His Discogs record spans 1987–2024 across free jazz, experimental rock, post-rock, and free improvisation contexts. Core affiliations include Chicago’s Atavistic/Okka Disk axis (Ken Vandermark, Peter Brötzmann Chicago Tentet) and Thrill Jockey’s experimental roster (Tortoise, The Sea And Cake, Stereolab). He has his own leadership discography on Balance Point Acoustics and Astral Spirits.

Early Credits (Chicago/Midwest, 1987–2000)

Active as session trombonist from at least 1987 (Evil I Do Not To Nod I Live, Pallindrome Productions). Contributing to Merge, Okka Disk, Drag City, Atavistic, Skin Graft, and Thrill Jockey through the 1990s. Also: guitar, bass, and keyboards appear in early credits, suggesting multi-instrumentalism before trombone became primary.
