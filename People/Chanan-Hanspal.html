Chanan Hanspal

Jazz guitarist and composer, UK. Discogs artist ID: 511523. Listed in the Discogs group_member table as a member of 23 Skidoo.

Name variations on record: C. Hanspal, Chanan Haspal, Chanon Hanspal, Hanspal.

Background

Composer/guitarist of Welsh Indian extraction, born in Wales. Worked in the music industry from 1993. Academic: Senior Lecturer in Music and Course Leader for the BA (Hons) Music Performance degree at Anglia Ruskin University (ARU), Cambridge. PhD on the orchestral works of Frank Zappa (University of South Wales); MMus from the University of New South Wales, Sydney; PGCE (University of Greenwich); Associate Diploma in Performing Arts (Jazz), Western Australian Conservatorium. (source)

He relocated to Sydney in 2003 to pursue his MMus, returning to the UK for his PhD. He also wrote a monthly rock guitar column for Guitarist Magazine during his doctoral period. (source)

What the DB Establishes

Discogs describes him as a “jazz guitarist and composer who played in 23 Skidoo.” He has a Bandcamp presence (chananhanspal.bandcamp.com) but no releases under his own name in the DB.

His Discogs release credits are entirely outside 23 Skidoo — no 23 Skidoo release in the DB carries his name. His role, period of involvement, and which recordings (if any) he appears on remain unconfirmed from the DB alone.

Slowly (1994)

Both 1994 credits are with Slowly — a UK acid jazz/funk production outfit built around the Kedros brothers (Caspar Kedros and Darius Kedros). Hanspal played guitar across most tracks on Ming (an EP or mini-LP) and contributed guitar and co-writing credits on the “Rites Of Spring / Understanding The Peace” single. Other contributors on these releases include Steve Dub (associated with The Chemical Brothers / Boys Own camp), Pascal’s Bongo Massive (percussion), and D-Zine (2) (scratches/vocals) — placing these firmly in the mid-1990s UK acid jazz / trip-hop crossover scene.

The Chill Out Label connection is consistent with the funk-influenced direction the Turnbull brothers were exploring during the same period via Ronin Records.

Imaani (2014)

A songwriter credit on track 7 of Standing Tall by Imaani (UK R&B/soul vocalist, notable as the UK’s Eurovision entry in 1998 with “Where Are You?”). Co-written with Kevin Pitts. Hanspal did not perform on the album.

23 Skidoo Connection

Listed as a group member in the Discogs database but carries no printed-sleeve release credits for the band. The 2000 self-titled album (Virgin/Ronin) credits “23 Skidoo Are” as Fritz Catlin, Johnny Turnbull, Alex Turnbull, and Sketch Martin only — Hanspal is absent from the sleeve.

However, his ARU staff profile states he “performed with the experimental group 23 Skidoo, contributing to their self-titled 1999 Virgin Records release and performing at WOMAD Festival (2000) and La Scala, London (2000).” (source) This establishes his involvement as:

  • Period: c.1999–2000 (the reunion/reformation era, not the original 1979–1987 run)
  • Role: Guitarist — playing live and contributing to the 2000 album sessions
  • Live dates confirmed: WOMAD Festival, Reading (July 2000); La Scala, London (2000)
  • Recording context: He is described as having contributed to the 2000 Virgin release, though he does not appear in the printed sleeve credits; his contribution may have been uncredited session work or material that didn’t make the final cut

Conflict: The ARU page says “1999 Virgin Records release” — but the 2000 self-titled LP (CDV 2912) was released July 2000, not 1999. The recording sessions would have taken place in 1999; the ARU page likely refers to the recording/session year rather than the release date.

The stylistic fit is coherent: the 2000 album blended electronica, future jazz, and downtempo elements (featuring Pharoah Sanders, Roots Manuva, Deckwrecka), and Hanspal’s jazz-rock fusion background and prior work with Roots Manuva place him squarely in that orbit.
