Overview

Sara Savery (real name Sara Savery Trojaborg) is a Danish artist working across vocals, bass, drums, production, and lyrics. She is a member of Ghost Society and People Press Play, and also records under the one-off alias Life On The Screen. Her catalogue spans the late 1990s to the early 2010s — concentrated in the 2010s — in styles ranging from Downtempo and IDM to Shoegaze and Ethereal, with releases on Dead People’s Choice and A:larm Music among others. Her most frequent collaborators include Tobias Wilner, Jonas Bjerre, Lasse Herbst, and Hannah Chloë Savery Bertram.
