Overview

Los Angeles-based bassist, producer, arranger, and songwriter born in San Pedro, California in 1967. With 361 total credits spanning from 1992 onward, Bryan has operated as a core figure in a West Coast indie and folk-rock production circle, logging more than 90 shared releases with drummer Jay Bellerose and over 60 each with engineer Ryan Freeland and Aimee Mann — a collaboration density that points to an ongoing studio partnership rather than session work alone. His heaviest label footprint is at SuperEgo Records, with additional work for Nonesuch, International Anthem, and Verve. He appears as a member of Jeff Parker & The New Breed, bridging the LA indie-pop world with Parker’s Chicago jazz orbit.
