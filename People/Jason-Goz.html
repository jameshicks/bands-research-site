Jason Goz

Jul 19, 20262 min read

Jason Goz

Discogs ID: 815905

London-based mastering and lacquer cutting engineer. Most frequent credit on Hyperdub releases. 41 shared releases with Burial in Discogs — the single most common collaborator by release count, though primarily in a technical capacity.

Provided uncredited mastering on Burial’s debut 12” South London Boroughs (2005, HDB001) and uncredited lacquer cutting on Street Halo (2011) and Four Walls / Paradise Circus (Vinyl Factory VF034, 2011).
