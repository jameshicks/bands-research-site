DJ Spooky

Discogs ID: 3214 Real name: Paul D. Miller (Discogs ID: 15495)

American DJ, producer, writer, and multimedia artist born 1970 in Washington, D.C. A key figure in the downtown New York experimental and hip-hop-adjacent electronic scene of the 1990s–2000s.

Co-credited with Matthew Shipp on Optometry (2002, Thirsty Ear), a landmark of the Blue Series that placed free jazz piano and bass alongside laptop electronics and turntables. Shipp served as art director on the release. Also collaborated with Shipp on the Blue Series Continuum.
