Biography

Early career — Sweden in the 1980s

Sten Urban Sandell emerged from the Swedish free improvisation scene in the early 1980s. His first documented Discogs credit is a 1980 piano appearance on the Lokomotiv Konkret album (Urspår), followed by his debut as sole leader, Damp (1985, Bauta Records) — a solo/duo recording on which he performed prepared piano, grand piano, synthesizer, percussion, and gong, alongside guitarist Sören Runolf. Sandell produced the session himself with engineer Hans Isgren.

Throughout the 1980s–early 1990s he worked within the Swedish improvised-music infrastructure centered on small independent labels and the Fylkingen venue in Stockholm.

GUSH (3) — long-running trio

The ensemble that would define the largest arc of Sandell’s career is GUSH (3), a free improvisation trio completed by tenor/soprano saxophonist Mats Gustafsson and drummer Raymond Strid. Their debut recording, Tjo Och Tjim (1990, Dragon), launched an association that has now run more than three decades. GUSH released through Dragon, Bead Records, Atavistic, and Qbico before returning to Swedish contexts with The March (2015, Konvoj Records) and their 30th-anniversary document GUSH 30 Kraków 2018 (2023, Not Two Records) and Afro Blue (2024, Trost Records). Sandell is credited as co-composer and co-producer across the group’s catalog.

Songs Between Cities And Waterholes / Broader Stockholm Scene

In 1993, Sandell appeared on the self-titled release by Songs Between Cities And Waterholes (Bauta Records) — a collective project also featuring bassist Niklas Billström, vocalist/violinist Lach’n Jonsson, and drummer Chris Cutler (of Henry Cow / Recommended Records). His credits include piano, keyboards, voice, and percussion. This project illustrates his connections beyond jazz into the broader experimental-rock/improv borderland.

Gubbröra — 2004 PSI session

In 2004, Sandell co-led Gubbröra (psi label) with Swedish guitarist David Stackenäs and the British trio of Evan Parker, Barry Guy, and Paul Lytton. Sandell played piano, voice, and electronics. The recording was made in London and released by psi (Martin Davidson’s label). This is Sandell’s clearest documented connection to the British free improvisation lineage; Parker would become a recurring collaborator.

Nilssen-Love collaborations — Townorchestrahouse and beyond

Sandell’s collaboration with Norwegian drummer Paal Nilssen-Love is documented from at least 2005, when Townorchestrahouse was released on Clean Feed (Lisbon). The record is credited primarily to Nilssen-Love but lists Sandell as co-composer of all compositions and co-producer alongside Parker and Ingebrigt Håker Flaten. Recorded and engineered by Thomas Hukkelberg, it is a three-movement work (Town / Orchestra / House) totalling approximately seventy minutes.

The duo relationship crystallized with Jacana (2014, Rune Grammofon, cat. RCD 2159), a co-leadership document. Three tracks — “Curvature” (23:10), “Kauri” (17:00), “Jacana” (4:20) — were recorded, mixed, and mastered by Hukkelberg. Sleeve design by Kim Hiorthøy. Sandell is credited for piano and voice; Nilssen-Love for drums and percussion; both share composing and producing credits alongside label head Rune Kristoffersen.

Sandell also appears on the Sten Sandell Trio (Discogs artist ID: 629697), a separate group entity that issued Standing Wave (2001, Sofa), Flat Iron (2004, Sofa), Oval (2007, Intakt Records), Strokes (2007, Clean Feed), and Face Of Tokyo (2009, PNL). He is further listed as a member of the Nilsson Sandell Duo (Discogs ID: 5268149).

Fire! Orchestra

Sandell appears on two Fire! Orchestra albums on Rune Grammofon: Exit! (2013) and Echoes (2023). Fire! Orchestra is Mats Gustafsson’s large-ensemble project. On Exit! he played piano and electronics within a brass/reeds/strings/percussion ensemble of approximately 25 musicians including Raymond Strid, Mats Gustafsson, David Stackenäs, Fredrik Ljungkvist, Johan Berthling, Mariam Wallentin, and Sofia Jernberg.

Evan Parker Electro-Acoustic Ensemble

Sandell appears as pianist and synthesist on the Evan Parker Electro-Acoustic Ensemble’s Warszawa 2019 (2021, Fundacja Słuchaj!), recorded live in Warsaw. The ensemble includes Parker, Paul Lytton, Richard Barrett, Paul Obermayer, and others.

Voices In Between Voices — 2023

Voices In Between Voices (2023, Sonic Transmissions Records) is a co-leadership recording with Norwegian vocalist Tone Åse. Sandell plays grand piano and voice; Tone Åse contributes voice and electronics. The album was produced by Tone Åse and Ståle Storløkken (of Supersilent), mixed by Storløkken, and mastered by Helge Sten (Deathprod). Sandell also provided the cover artwork and a poem (in Swedish) for the sleeve.

Academic and institutional recognition

Sandell holds a Doctor of Philosophy in Fine Arts in Musical Performance and Interpretation from the University of Gothenburg (2013) and is a member of the Royal Academy of Music in Sweden. He received the Royal Academy of Music’s Jazz Award in 2012, with the citation describing him as “one of Swedish improvised music’s most brilliant, captivating and independent voices.”

Compilation appearance

He appears on the Evan Parker-related compilation Compositions And Collaborations | 1972–2022 (2023, ReR Megacorp), a document of Parker’s extended compositional universe.
