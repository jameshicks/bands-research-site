Overview

Rachel Davies is a violinist, vocalist, and songwriter active in indie rock and folk from 1996. Her heaviest recorded output falls in the 2000s across Scopitones and Manifesto, both labels closely tied to David Gedge’s post-Wedding Present project Cinerama—she shares 28 co-credits with Gedge and recurs alongside Dare Mason, Abigail Trundle, Sally Murrell, and Guy Davis in that circle. She is also a member of Stick In The Wheel, the contemporary English folk ensemble.
