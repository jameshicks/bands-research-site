Guy Marc Hinant

Belgian musician, curator, and co-founder of Sub Rosa. Discogs artist ID: 511877. Based in Brussels.

Overview

Guy Marc Hinant co-founded Sub Rosa with Frédéric Walheer in the early 1980s. Within the label he has been the primary compiler, curator, and executive producer, credited on over 80 releases in roles including “Compiled By”, “Curated By”, “Liner Notes”, “Edited By”, and “Executive-Producer”. He is also a performer: member of Pseudo Code, Kosmose, and Waving Ondulata.

His most significant editorial project is the ongoing An Anthology Of Noise & Electronic Music series on Sub Rosa, an a-chronological multi-volume survey of recorded experimental and electronic music.
