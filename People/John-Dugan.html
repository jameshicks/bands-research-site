Overview

John Dugan is a drummer who has been a member of Chisel, Edsel, Exit Verse, Chicago Stone Lightning Band, and Colour (6), accumulating 62 Discogs credits since 1991. His work runs across indie rock, punk, emo, and mod styles; his most frequent credited partners are Ted Leo and his Chisel bandmates, reflecting the band’s central place in his catalogue. Gern Blandsten released the bulk of his mid-1990s output (five works, 1994–1997), while Numero Group appearances in 2022–2023 point to archival reissue activity.

Notes (web)

Chisel — Numero Group reissues and 2023 reunion

What prompted the reissues: In early 2022, Numero Group acquired the rights to Chisel’s catalogue. Around 14 March 2022, the band’s digital discography was pulled from streaming services; Numero Group then announced they were responsible and would be remastering and reissuing it. The rollout ran throughout 2022–2023: All My Kin (May 2022), Innocents Abroad (July 2022), Hip Straights (August 2022), Live on WPTS, 1997, and finally an expanded double-LP reissue of the 1997 album Set You Free with five bonus tracks and liner notes by JJ Skolnik. No specific statement from the band or label about why Numero Group picked up the catalogue (beyond the standard archival-reissue framing) was found in the sources checked. [source: https://stereogum.com/2206359/chisel-announce-more-reunion-shows/news (Stereogum, retrieved 2026-11-14); https://www.stereogum.com/2210037/chisel-reunion-shows-east-coast-set-you-free-reissue/news/ (Stereogum, retrieved 2026-11-14)]

Live reunion: Yes. The reissue campaign was accompanied by a reunion. Chisel — Ted Leo (vocals), Chris Norborg (bass), and John Dugan (drums) — had not performed since 1997, a gap of roughly 26 years. They first reunited for the Numero Twenty festival (celebrating Numero Group’s 20th anniversary) on 18 February 2023 at the Palace Theatre, Los Angeles. Additional 2023 dates:

  • 16 February 2023 — Chicago, IL (The Empty Bottle)
  • 20 February 2023 — San Francisco, CA (Great American Music Hall, Noise Pop 30)
  • May 2023 — brief Northeast run: Connecticut, Massachusetts, Pennsylvania, Washington DC, New York

Total reunion dates: approximately nine shows across February–May 2023. [source: https://stereogum.com/2206359/chisel-announce-more-reunion-shows/news; https://www.stereogum.com/2210037/chisel-reunion-shows-east-coast-set-you-free-reissue/news/; http://events.noisepop.com/events/2023/2/20/chisel-tickets (Noise Pop event listing, retrieved 2026-11-14)]
