Victor Simonelli

Discogs ID: 5314 Real name: Victor Jon Simonelli Origin: Brooklyn, New York City Active: 1986–present Labels (owned): West Side Records (4), Bassline Records, Stellar Records, Brooklyn Trax, Big Big Trax, Unkwn Rec’s

DJ, producer, and label operator from Brooklyn. One of the most prolific figures in the NYC underground house and garage scene of the early-to-mid 1990s, working under more than twenty aliases and running a cluster of interrelated labels. His early career passed through Nu Groove Records and a large body of remix/edit work before he built his own label infrastructure around Bassline Records and its sublabels. Key collaborators include Mac Quayle, Tommy Musto, Mike Dunn, and Tony Humphries. Father of Immanuele Simonelli.

Biography

Early Career: Studio Engineer and Edit Work (1986–1990)

Simonelli began as an engineer at Shakedown Sound Studios in the late 1980s. His earliest Discogs credits are as an editor and remixer on twelve-inch singles from 1986 onward — edits on releases for Blue Chip (2) (1986), Criminal Records (1987–1989), Fourth Floor Records (1988–1990), and Nu Groove Records (1989–1990). His edit credits on Nu Groove’s Looney Tunes - Volume One (1989) and Looney Tunes II (1990) place him inside one of the formative catalogs of New York house and deep house. He also served as producer, arranger, and co-writer for releases on Vendetta Records (2) and Minimal Records (2) in 1989–1991, where he mixed and programmed tracks including “Move To The Beat” (1989, Vendetta) and “Why Can’t We See” (1991, Minimal).

Nu Groove Records and the Groove Committee Era (1991–1992)

Under the alias Groove Committee (Discogs ID: 12415), Simonelli released two key twelves on Nu Groove Records:

  • “I Want You To Know” (1991, Nu Groove NG-085) — produced, mixed, arranged, and written by Simonelli; sides labeled “A1 / Rain On Me / Lets Groove It.”
  • “Dirty Games” (1992, Nu Groove NG-103) — produced and mixed under the “Groove Committee II” variant; includes “Just Play The Music” and “I’ve Got To Feel It” (the latter co-written with engineer Kazuhiko Gomi).

Nu Groove was one of the premier addresses for New York deep house at this point, connected to the broader Fourth Floor Records circle and feeding the Zanzibar and Sound Factory Bar circuit.

Concurrent with Groove Committee, he produced as Solution on E Legal — “Givin’ It All I Got” (1992) — and remixed for Sub-Urban, beginning a relationship with that label that continued through the mid-1990s.

Building the Bassline Empire (1993–1996)

By 1993 Simonelli had shifted from being a hired producer to an executive producer and label boss. His own infrastructure consisted of Bassline Records (parent label, Discogs ID: 2548) with sublabels Big Big Trax (ID: 7531) and Brooklyn Trax (ID: 2674).

Across 1993–1996 he held executive producer credit on dozens of releases for Bassline and Big Big Trax, most of them deep house or garage house twelve-inches with gospel-inflected vocals — the sound he associated with the New York underground. Titles include:

  • “Do You Feel Me” (1993, Bassline BLR-01) — as NY’s Finest (ID: 64890); the opening release on the label, engineered and programmed by Mac Quayle
  • “Agitate It” (1993, Bassline) — executive producer
  • “Make It Happen (For Yourself)” (1995, Bassline) — 6 pressings
  • “Oh Happy Day” (1995, Bassline) — produced and mixed
  • “You Gotta Know” (1995, Bassline) — 4 pressings
  • Force 1 (The Club Is Here)** (1995) — compilation LP on Big Big Trax / Koch Dance Force, his first large-scale artist compilation

Big Big Trax sublabel releases ran in parallel: “BoomBaata” (1994), “Don’t You Ever Stop” (1994), “The Aqua Booty Theme” (1995), “Vol. 2” (1995, 4 pressings), “Past Present & Future” (1996, 2 pressings), “Big Big Trax Vol. 1” (1996 compilation LP).

Bassline - The Comp’ (1996) was a DJ-mix compilation mastermixed by Simonelli himself, documenting the label’s catalog to that point.

His connection to Tony Humphries materialised through “Take Home The Club” (1997, Bassline), a Humphries DJ-mix CD on the label, for which Simonelli served as editor. They also share credits on the Italian compilation Vanity X (1996). Discogs tracks three shared releases between them.

Primary Releases Under the Victor Simonelli Name (1993–1999)

While much of his output appeared under aliases or as executive producer, several releases carried his own name:

  • “I Can’t Help Myself” (1993, Vibe (3) VIBE 5) — as “Victor Simonelli Presents International Connection”; produced, mixed, arranged; engineer James T. Alfano
  • “I’ve Been Changed” (1993, Playtime Records (4) PT 002-93) — as “Victor Simonelli Presents Inner Faith”; club mix / dub / instrumental / acapella; engineer Mac Quayle; executive producer Dino “Blade” Bellafiore; styles: Deep House, Garage House
  • “The EP” (1993, One Records OR12007) — as “Victor Simonelli Presents Sound Of One”; tracks “I Know A Place” (mix engineer Tommy Musto) and “Doing” (additional production Angel Moraes); executive producers Eddie Colon and Roger Sanchez
  • “Essential Funk From Brooklyn” (1995, Hot Productions) — primary release, producer and writer; with Lenny Dee
  • “Vindaloo… The Morning After!” (1994, Sub-Urban / X:treme Records) — DJ mix album; 2 pressings
  • “Sound Of Simonelli Sampler Volume One” (1996, Sound Proof Recordings, UK) — double-12” sampler covering Solution, Inner Faith, and remix work
  • “Nothing Stays The Same” (1997, Equal Records EU001) — as MD + VS + LR (with Mike Dunn and Luis Radio); produced with Maurizio Clemente; Italian release, 8 pressings
  • “Rushin To Meet You” (1999, Equal Records) — as primary artist; presenter, producer, mixed by; with Luca Cucchetti and Stefano Di Carlo

Alias Activity — Key Names

Groove Committee (ID: 12415) — Deep house alias, active 1991–2023. Core Nu Groove releases plus later work on Unkwn Rec’s and Vinyl Solution.

Solution (ID: 28306) — “Givin’ It All I Got” (1992, E Legal); “Feels So Right” (1993, Undercontrol, 15 pressings); “Was That All It Was” (1993, Doghouse Records); “The E.P.” (1997, West Side Records (4)). Also credited as “Soulution.”

NY’s Finest (ID: 64890) — Primarily the Bassline Records vehicle for house vocals; “Do You Feel Me” (1993) as the flagship.

Inner Faith (ID: 34313) — Spiritual/gospel-house vehicle; “I’ve Been Changed” (1993, Playtime) as the key release; revived for “I Can’t Wait Till Tonight” (2013, Traxx Underground).

Creative Force (ID: 29834) — Used on Sub-Urban releases; “It’s So Good” (1993, Sub-Urban) with multiple UK pressings.

Voices Of Faith — “Voices Of Faith EP” (1994, UMM, Italy); includes production by Jazz-N-Groove on one track.

Latin Impressions — Primarily the West Side Records (4) Latin house series; Latin Impressions Part I (1998) was a split release with Michele Chiavarini.

21st Century Body Rockers — Italian releases on Stellar Records from 2000 onward.

Other aliases Discogs lists under his name: A Church, A D.J. & A Sampler; Africaribbean Connections; Brooklyn’s Own; Cloud 9 (5); Ebony Soul; Instant Exposure; Interaction (5); International Connection; Lucky 13 And The Downtown Scene; Street Players; Total Exposure; Underground Circuit (2); VJS Inc.

West Side Records and Italian Period (1997–2003)

From the late 1990s Simonelli moved toward his own imprint West Side Records (4) (ID: 2474608) for solo and alias releases. Key titles:

  • “Latin Impressions Part I” (1998, WSR 004) — with Michele Chiavarini
  • “Tracks From The Other Side” (1999, West Side) — 3 pressings
  • “Living On The Frontline” (2002, West Side) — executive producer, producer, mixed by, arranged by

His Italian footprint deepened through Stellar Records (ID: 4951) and Equal Records:

  • “We Gonna House It Up” (2000, Stellar) — 6 pressings; with Mr. Mike
  • “21st Century Body Rockers Vol. 1” (2000, Stellar) — 5 pressings
  • “Bateria — Latin Impressions” (2003, Flux Recordings, UK) — 4 pressings

Later Work (2006–present)

“I Know You Want Me” (2006, West Side Records (4)) — under Solution; produced, arranged, mixed. “Tracks From The Other Side EP 2” (2012, West Side). In 2012 he released The First 20 Years (Unkwn Rec’s) as a retrospective compilation, and “TMVS Vol 1” (2018, Unkwn Rec’s) documented further catalog. In 2020 he appeared on “The Answer” (Black Book), a release that also credits Chris Lake, Armand Van Helden, and Arthur Baker. The 2023 pair The Early Years Vol 1 and Vol 2 (Unkwn Rec’s, UK vinyl) compiled early material.
