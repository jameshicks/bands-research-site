Christoph Amann

Discogs artist ID: 367202
Also credited as: Amann, C. Amann, Ch. Amann, Christof Amann, Christoff Amann, Christoph, Christoph Amman, Christoph Ammann, Christoph Amonn, Christophe Ammann
Studio: Amann Studios, Vienna (Discogs label ID: 274688)
Website: amannstudios.com

Viennese mastering, mixing, and recording engineer, operating since the early 1990s through his own facility, Amann Studios (also referred to on early releases as “Christof Amann Tonstudio”). One of the most prolific technical collaborators in the Vienna experimental and contemporary-classical scenes: his credits span Mego-era electronica, the Erstwhile improv network, Kairos contemporary-classical releases, Trost Records jazz/avant-garde, and the Morr Music indie-electronic catalogue.

His credit range is wide — mastering-only on some records, full Recorded By / Mixed By / Mastered By / Producer on others. At the most involved end, he is effectively a co-producer; at the other, a finishing engineer. The studio he founded and runs is the through-line: Amann Studios appears as a credited location on dozens of releases across all these contexts.

Amann is also a performer. He played bass on The Pirates (10)‘s 1992 debut and is a member of both Big Sip and The Pirates (10).

Amann Studios

Discogs lists Amann Studios (Wien) as a label/recording studio entity (ID 274688) founded by Amann, with no parent label or sublabels. The studio name appears in recording credits throughout his career — e.g. “Recorded By [Amann Studios]” on Staubgold’s The Golden Years (2012). It functions as a commercial studio serving the broader Vienna scene and beyond, not as a releasing entity.

As Performer: Big Sip and The Pirates (10)

The Pirates (10) (Discogs ID: 2069868) — Viennese alternative rock/core band. Members: Christoph Amann (bass), Doris Knecht, Edgar Zwischenbrugger, Thomas Madersbacher. Released The Pirates (1992, Sacro Egoismo), on which Amann is also credited as mixer and songwriter. One of the earliest appearances in his Discogs credit history.

Big Sip (Discogs ID: 1706200) — Austrian band. Members: Christoph Amann and Martin Tusch. No releases currently indexed on Discogs; band appears to be active but without a catalogued discography.

Contemporary Classical — Kairos (2000s–2020s)

Amann is the primary mastering engineer for Kairos, the Austrian contemporary-classical label — his largest single-label relationship (30+ release credits). Key releases include works by Bernhard Lang, Unsuk Chin, Klangforum Wien, Bernhard Gander, and Friedrich Cerha; also the score for Veronika Franz & Severin Fiala’s Goodnight Mommy (Olga Neuwirth, 2016).
