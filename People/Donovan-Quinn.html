Donovan Quinn

US singer-songwriter, guitarist, and multi-instrumentalist. Discogs artist ID: 473740. Primary alias: Verdure (Discogs ID: 657762). Also known by name variations “Quinn” and “dq.”

Bandcamp: https://donovanquinn.bandcamp.com/

Biography

Early work — Jewelled Antler and cassette culture (2001)

Quinn’s earliest documented release is I Dreamt She Rode On A Pink Gazelle & Other Dreams (2001, Jewelled Antler), credited to The Skygreen Leopards. The label was a small SF-area collective releasing limited cassettes and CDRs in the early 2000s. This release has 19 pressing/edition entries on Discogs, suggesting heavy documentation of variant copies.

The Skygreen Leopards — core years (2001–2014)

Quinn co-founded The Skygreen Leopards with Glenn Donaldson (and, at times, Shayde Sartin). The band’s aesthetic was lysergic, folk-inflected psych — often recorded in a lo-fi home manner with a vast array of instruments. Quinn’s credited instrumentation on One Thousand Bird Ceremony (2004, Soft Abuse) alone runs to 6-string guitar, 12-string guitar, 5-string banjo, bouzouki, Jew’s harp, accordion, drums, Hammond organ, chord organ, tambourine, maracas, mandolin, tin whistle, harmonica, recorder, dulcimer, Echoplex effects, and portable turntables.

The band signed to Jagjaguwar for their most widely-distributed period, releasing three albums:

  • Life & Love In Sparrow’s Meadow (2005)
  • Disciples Of California (2006)
  • Gorgeous Johnny (2009)

Disciples Of California added Jasmyn Wong on drums, Jason Quever on organ/piano, and Wymond Miles on pedal steel. Quinn played guitar, vocals, piano, and electric piano; Donaldson handled guitar, vocals, organ, and bowed banjo.

The band returned for a final album, Family Crimes (2014, Woodsist), before going quiet. A career retrospective compilation, The Jingling World Of The Skygreen Leopards, appeared on Soft Abuse in 2018.

Verdure — solo alias (2004)

Under the Verdure alias, Quinn released The Telescope Dreampatterns (2004) on Camera Obscura (Australian label). Credits include Derek Monypeny on guitar and Jennifer Modenessi on percussion. Quinn handled all composition and performance.

Donovan Quinn & The 13th Month (2008–2010)

A “small rotating cast of players” (per Discogs) centered on Quinn, releasing on Soft Abuse. Members at various points included:

  • Hélène Renaut — piano, violin, backing vocals
  • Jason Quever — guitar, keyboards, piano, cello, backing vocals, producer
  • Jess Roberts — backing vocals
  • Karl Bauer — drums
  • Nick Marcantonio — bass

Releases:

  • Donovan Quinn & The 13th Month (2008, Soft Abuse) — produced by Jason Quever; Quinn wrote, recorded, and sang
  • Your Wicked Man (2010, Soft Abuse) — produced by Quever; Quinn, Marcantonio, and Quever performing

The Brain Band (2007)

A one-off cassette release (The Brain Band, 2007, Fuck It Tapes) uniting Quinn with Glenn Donaldson, Elisa Ambrogio, and Karl Bauer.

The Fuckaroos

Listed as a group on Quinn’s Discogs page (artist ID: 2670120), but no releases appear in the database.

New Bums (2013–2021)

An acoustic folk duo with Ben Chasny (aka Six Organs of Admittance). Low-key and intimate in approach — Chasny and Quinn are the only performers credited. Released on Drag City and self-released. See New Bums.

Lyrics credits (2011–2022)

Quinn contributed lyrics to several releases outside his own projects:

  • Papercuts (2)‘s Fading Parade (2011, Sub Pop) — Written-By credit; Papercuts is Jason Quever’s main project
  • Papercuts (2)‘s Parallel Universe Blues (2018, Slumberland Records) — Written-By
  • Comets On Fire’s Life Among The Savages (2014, Memphis Industries) — Lyrics By
  • Papercuts (2)‘s Past Life Regression (2022, Slumberland Records) — Lyrics By (tracks A3, A4, A5, B4)
