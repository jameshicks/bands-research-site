Aug 23, 20263 min read

Jeff Mangum

Discogs ID: 351055
Real name: Jeffrey Nye Mangum
Role: Vocalist, guitarist, songwriter
From: Ruston, Louisiana

Principal songwriter and vocalist of Neutral Milk Hotel, the most celebrated act of the Elephant Six Collective. In the Aeroplane Over the Sea (Merge, 1998) is one of the most acclaimed indie rock albums of the decade. Largely withdrew from public activity after 1998; re-emerged for sporadic live performances and reunion shows from c.2011.

Appears on Elf Power’s Arena Rock releases When The Red King Comes (1997) and A Dream In Sound (1999) as vocalist/performer.

Aliases (Discogs): Korena Pang, Milk (50), The Long Warm Wall of Alfred Snouts

Other group memberships (Discogs confirmed): The Olivia Tremor Control (252619), Elf Power (347302), Major Organ And The Adding Machine (351114), Black Swan Network (538181), The Elephant Six Orchestra (609301)

Aliases — Notes (web)

Milk / Milk (50)

Before adopting the Neutral Milk Hotel name, Mangum recorded home cassettes as Milk (c.1987–1990), self-released to friends. Titles include Pygmie Barn in E minor (1989) and Beauty (December 1990). The Discogs disambiguator “(50)” is a database numbering convention distinguishing him from other “Milk” entries, not a separate project name. neutralmilkhotel.org

Korena Pang

A pseudonym Mangum uses for experimental sound collage work, distinct from his NMH songwriting. The name first appears in the thank-you credits of On Avery Island (1996). Mangum described recording a full sound collage album under this name in 1999 but chose not to release it: “I made a record of montage sounds in ‘99 under the name Korena Pang, but it was never put out because it didn’t do it for me.” Wikipedia (as of 2026-08-22)

Confirmed releases and credits as Korena Pang:

  • “excerpt from Dogbirthed Brother in Eggsack Delicious” — track on AUX Vol 1: Experimental Sound from Athens, GA (Orange Twin Records, April 2006). This is an edited version of a longer piece Mangum debuted on WFMU in 2002. neutralmilkhotel.org; Wikipedia (as of 2026-08-22)
  • A Hawk and a Hacksaw self-titled album (2002) — credited as Korena on vocals (tracks 3, 7, 10) and violin (track 3). Discogs release 1113272; neutralmilkhotel.fandom.com
  • Guignol — Angela, David and the Great Neapolitan Road Issue (2003) — credited as Korena Pang. Guignol members on this record include Jeremy Barnes, Laurence Coleman, and Aaron Moore. neutralmilkhotel.org

Mangum also did visual art collaboration under this name: a circus-poster collage painting titled “Nine Hundred Ninety Nine Million Black Holes to Pop Into” with artist Beth Sale (August 1999). neutralmilkhotel.org

The Long Warm Wall of Alfred Snouts

A second experimental pseudonym, used interchangeably with Korena Pang for the same body of sound-collage work. The specific credit appeared on an early mix of the WFMU piece — the original broadcast version (summer/fall 2002) was titled “To Animate the Body with the Cocoon of the Her Unconscious Christ the Mother Removes Her Death Body of 1910 Only To Be Reborn in the Same Spirit as a School of Blow Fish Believing in the Coming of the Milk Christ” and credited to The Long Warm Wall of Alfred Snouts; the later edited version released on AUX (2006) used Korena Pang instead. neutralmilkhotel.org; Wikipedia (as of 2026-08-22)

WFMU Shows (2002)

Mangum hosted several shows on WFMU (New Jersey freeform radio) in 2002, billed as “Jefferson.” Programming drew on musique concrète, European folk, environmental recordings, drone work, and free jazz — the sound collage work that produced the Korena Pang/Alfred Snouts material emerged from this period. indyweek.com (paywalled; details confirmed via secondary sources)
