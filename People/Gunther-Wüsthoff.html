Gunther Wüsthoff

German saxophonist, synthesizer player, and electronics performer. Co-founding member of Faust (Polydor/Virgin era, 1971–74) and part of the Werner Diermaier–led Faust (54) faction from 2022. Wüsthoff came from the Hamburg pre-group Nukleus — alongside Jean-Hervé Peron and Rudolf Sosna — which was merged with Campylognatus Citelli by producer Uwe Nettelbeck to form Faust around 1970. After the band’s dissolution in 1975 he was largely absent from the Faust story until a 2022 reunion with Werner Diermaier for the Daumenbruch album.

Overview

Wüsthoff is credited on the four canonical Faust releases: the 1971 Polydor debut, So Far (1972), The Faust Tapes (1973, uncredited performer), and Faust IV (1973, credited for cover design rather than performance on that specific pressing). On So Far he is listed as co-writer alongside all other members — the album credits “all titles written” collectively to the band and to Wüsthoff individually. He also co-wrote the cover design for Faust IV with Nettelbeck.

During the Wümme sessions period, Wüsthoff played saxophone on the Slapp Happy album Sort Of (1972, issued 1980 as Acnalbasac Noom), which was also produced by Nettelbeck with the Faust rhythm section (Peron, Diermaier). He is credited as “Recorded By” on the Japanese reissue of Sort Of (2005, Arcàngelo), indicating he also operated recording equipment during those sessions. He also appeared as a featured performer on Anthony Moore’s solo album Pieces From The Cloudland Ballroom (1971, Polydor), also produced by Nettelbeck.

After a decades-long absence from Faust releases, Wüsthoff returned for the Patchwork 1971–2002 compilation (2002, Staubgold) credited as featuring, and then substantially re-engaged from 2022 onward: credited performer on the 2021 box set 1971–1974 (Bureau B), co-designer of the Punkt. cover (2022, Bureau B — the previously unreleased 1975 Munich recordings), and performer and Spieluhr (musical box) player on Daumenbruch (2022, Eroto Tox Decodings / Faust (54)) and Blickwinkel (2024, Bureau B / Faust (54)).

His sole solo release in the Discogs database is [to|digi]tal (2020, Bureau B, cat. BB348) — an LP on which he is credited for music, text, production, and artwork.

According to Wikipedia (Faust band article), Wüsthoff was “absent from the band for 49 years” before the 2022 reunion with Diermaier.
