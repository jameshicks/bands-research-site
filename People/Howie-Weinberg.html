Howie Weinberg

Discogs ID: 254262
ANVs: HW, H.W., Howard Weinberg, “Howie The Beast Weinberg”, and numerous initials-only variants

New York mastering engineer. Apprenticed at Masterdisk beginning 1977, eventually becoming a senior mastering engineer there. In 2011 he departed to set up Howie Weinberg Mastering as an independent operation. One of the most prolific mastering engineers in Discogs: over 19,700 release credits spanning 1971–2026, dominated by Mastered By (20,468 credits) and Lacquer Cut By (1,208 credits), with an additional 210 Remastered By credits.

His client base cuts across virtually every genre and label tier: major-label pop and rock, Def Jam hip-hop, SST/Sub Pop/Touch and Go indie-punk, Matador/Drag City underground, jazz (Nonesuch, Verve, Blue Note), metal, and 1980s downtown NYC post-punk and no-wave (99 Records, ZE Records, Celluloid, Enjoy Records, Rough Trade US).

His early hip-hop work was concentrated at Profile Records, Def Jam Recordings, and the pioneering Enjoy Records and Celluloid labels. The Celluloid connection (159 credits) tied him to the downtown NYC hip-hop and no-wave overlap of the early 1980s.
