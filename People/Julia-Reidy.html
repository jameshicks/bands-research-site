Julia Reidy

Julia Reidy

Sep 27, 20266 min read

Julia Reidy

Discogs ID: 3932086
Real name / release credit: Jules Reidy
Born: Sydney, Australia
Based: Berlin

Australian guitarist, composer, and improviser. Works primarily with 12-string acoustic guitar alongside electronics and sampling, developing a practice that sits across experimental acoustic music, electroacoustic composition, and drone. Core solo catalogue on Black Truffle (Australia/Berlin) and Editions Mego (Austria); more recent work on Shelter Press (France) and Thrill Jockey (US). Also a member of the large Berlin improvising collective Splitter Orchester.

Early Period — Sydney / Not On Label (2015–2017)

Reidy’s first documented credit appears on the 2015 CDr Chorals (Signals From Arkaim), where she contributes guitar and voice. Her first proper solo release, All Is Ablaze (2016), was self-released on Not On Label, credited with guitar, arrangement, and performance. Sydney guitarist and producer Owen Roberts appears on that record alongside Dean Roberts (mastering/additional guitar) and Carl Saff (mastering). The release shows an early alliance with the Sydney improv/experimental community before her relocation to Berlin.

Dawning On (2017, Slip, UK) was her first release on a dedicated imprint. A solo CDr featuring three long-form pieces (“Surrounds Outlast” at 28:55, “Something I Could Do” at 11:37, “Upwelling” at 19:10), all performed on 12-string guitar. Discogs genres list it under Jazz / Experimental / Free Improvisation — the extended durations signalling an improvisatory approach rooted in the guitar’s sustain and resonance.

Berlin Period — Slip, Black Truffle, Editions Mego (2019–2020)

Brace, Brace (2019, Slip, 12” 45rpm) marked a consolidation of collaborators who would become central to her discography: Joe Talia (mastered by) and Suze Whaites (artwork) both appear here, as they do on the subsequent Black Truffle albums. Sam Slater handled recording supervision and mixing.

In Real Life (2019, Black Truffle, BT051) was her Black Truffle debut — a 12” LP with two long-form sides (“Crystal Bones” / “Adulare”). Genres on Discogs: Electronic / Musique Concrète.

Vanish (2020, Editions Mego, EMEGO288) — a two-sided LP (“Clairvoyant” 18:22 / “Oh Boy” 15:18). Genres: Electronic / Musique Concrète. Nik Colk Void handled layout (credited as Nik Void); Suze Whaites again on artwork; Joe Talia mixed and mastered.

Vestige One (2020, Amplify 2020) — a co-release under the Amplify festival umbrella, self-released.

Duo and Ensemble Work (2023–2024)

Neutral Star (2023, Miasmah) — collaboration with Berlin quartet The Pitch (2). A double-sided LP (two ~25-minute movements: “Endless ≠ Limitless” / “Neutral Star”). Genres: Electronic / Jazz / Abstract / Electroacoustic / Minimal.

Trances (2023, Shelter Press, SP147) — solo LP of two long-form sides (“Non-Linear Stages”), each ~22 minutes. Genres: Electronic / Rock / Ambient / Drone / Electroacoustic / Experimental. Mixed by James Ginzburg; mastered by Stephan Mathieu; photography by Camille Blake.

Ghost/Spirit (2025)

Ghost/Spirit (2025, Thrill Jockey, Thrill-618-X) — her Thrill Jockey debut, released under the name Jules Reidy. Fourteen tracks across two sides; Discogs genre: Electronic / Ambient. A notable departure in approach: Reidy incorporates samples from collaborators Judith Hamann, Morten Joh, Sara Neidorf, Weston Olencki, and Andreas Dzialocha, building composite pieces from others’ recorded material.
