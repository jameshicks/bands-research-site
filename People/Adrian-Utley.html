Adrian Utley

Born: 27 April 1957, Northampton, England Discogs artist ID: 206478 Role: Guitarist, keyboardist, producer, arranger, film composer

Overview

Adrian Francis Utley is a British guitarist and producer best known as a founding member and primary instrumentalist of Portishead, the Bristol trip-hop group he formed with Geoff Barrow and Beth Gibbons in the early 1990s. Within Portishead he plays guitar, bass, and keyboards and co-produces and co-writes almost all of the band’s material. He also functions as musical director for the band’s live orchestral performances, having arranged and conducted the string and horn sections for the Roseland NYC Live concert (1998) alongside arranger Nick Ingman.

Beyond Portishead, Utley has had a consistent parallel career as a session guitarist and record producer. He played guitar on Alain Bashung’s acclaimed Fantaisie Militaire (Barclay, 1998) alongside the core Bashung session band of Simon Edwards, Martyn Barker, and producer Ian Caple. He appeared as a performer on Beth Gibbons’s Candy Says (2003) charity single for the Velvet Underground song, joined her for several tracks on Marianne Faithfull’s Before The Poison (Naïve, 2004) alongside PJ Harvey and Rob Ellis, and played guitar on Massive Attack’s Heligoland (Virgin, 2010).

As a producer Utley has worked extensively with UK indie acts. He produced and engineered three releases for The Coral via Geoff Barrow (including The Invisible Invasion, Deltasonic, 2005), co-produced Too Bright (Matador, 2014) for Perfume Genius with Ali Chant, and produced The Underside Of Power (Matador, 2017) for Algiers also with Ali Chant. He has also worked as a solo composer: Arcadia (Invada, 2018) — his most prominent primary-credited release on Discogs — is the score for the Nick Abrahams documentary film, co-composed with Will Gregory (Goldfrapp, Will Gregory Moog Ensemble).

Utley leads Adrian Utley’s Guitar Orchestra, a Bristol-based ensemble of approximately fifteen guitarists that recorded a performance of Terry Riley’s In C for Invada in 2013 (mixed by Utley and Ali Chant). He is also a member of the groups Stonephace, This I Dig, and 137 (2), and participates in The Will Gregory Moog Ensemble.

Production & Engineering Summary

Utley’s production work clusters around:

  • Portishead core: co-producer on all three albums; engineer on Third.
  • The Coral (Deltasonic, 2005): produced and engineered The Invisible Invasion, In The Morning, Something Inside Of Me with Geoff Barrow mixing.
  • Perfume Genius (Matador, 2014): co-produced Too Bright with Ali Chant at likely Bristol studios.
  • Algiers (Matador, 2017): co-produced The Underside Of Power with Ali Chant.
  • Versari (T-Rec, 2013): produced, engineered, and mixed French band’s Ostinato while also performing on it.
  • Zoo (Caramel Beurre Salé, 2016): produced and mixed.
  • Various one-off productions: Freedom I Love (2016), Flit (2016), Palimpsest (2017), Planet (i) (2021), Heat Ray (2024).
