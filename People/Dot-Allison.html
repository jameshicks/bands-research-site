Overview

Dorothy Elliot Allison (born 17 August 1969, Edinburgh) is a Scottish singer, songwriter, and producer who came to prominence as the vocalist and co-writer of One Dove in the early 1990s, a project anchored by Ian Carmichael, Jim McKinven, and Andrew Weatherall. After One Dove she built a solo catalogue through the late 1990s and 2000s across Heavenly, Arista, and Concrete, working across downtempo, trip-hop, synth-pop, and alternative rock — with Death In Vegas (Richard Fearless and Tim Holmes) serving as a major production home. A quiet 2010s was followed by a renewed presence in the 2020s via Sonic Cathedral and SA Recordings.
