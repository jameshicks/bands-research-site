Robert Turman

Oct 20, 20262 min read

Robert Turman

Discogs ID: 242660

American experimental/noise musician. Key artist on Hanson Records (14 primary credits, 2008–2025 — second only to Aaron Dilloway in longevity). Collaborated with Dilloway on Blizzard (Hanson Records, 2009; Turman: tape, Dilloway: synthesizer). 9 shared releases with Dilloway.

Co-founded NON in 1978 with Boyd Rice; left 1979, after which NON became Rice’s solo vehicle.
