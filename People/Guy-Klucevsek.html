Overview

Guy Klucevsek (1947–2025) was a Manhattan-born composer and accordionist who spent decades expanding the instrument’s solo repertoire, both through his own compositions and by commissioning more than 50 works from composers including John Zorn, Fred Frith, Alvin Lucier, William Duckworth, and Somei Satoh. His catalogue, stretching from the early 1980s through the 2020s, sits at the intersection of experimental, contemporary classical, minimal, and free improvisation styles. He was a central member of Accordion Tribe, an international all-accordion collective, and worked extensively within the downtown New York scene, logging deep collaborative histories with Greg Cohen, Bill Frisell, Wayne Horvitz, Bobby Previte, and Joey Baron. He died on May 22, 2025, in Staten Island.
