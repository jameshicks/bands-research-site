Svend Asmussen

Discogs ID: 95459 Real name: Svend Harald Christian Asmussen Born: 28 February 1916, Copenhagen, Denmark Died: 7 February 2017

Danish jazz violinist. One of the foremost jazz violinists in European history, active from the 1930s into the 2000s. Co-founded The Swe-Danes with Alice Babs and Ulrik Neumann in 1958.
