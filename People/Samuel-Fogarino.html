Samuel Fogarino

American drummer. Born August 9, 1968, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Discogs ID: 273947. Name variations: Sam Fogarino, Sam Fog, Fog.

Drummer of Interpol (New York City post-punk/art rock band, formed 1997). Session drums on On!Air!Library!‘s 2004 LP (tracks 1, 7, 9, credited as “Sam Fogarino”). Also a member of EmptyMansions, Holy Terrors, Magnetic Morning, The Ton-Ups, and Freel.

On!Air!Library! collaboration

On!Air!Library! was a NYC post-rock/experimental trio — Claudia Deheza, Alejandra Deheza, and Phillip Wann — signed to Arena Rock Recording Co. from 2002. Fogarino played drums on tracks 1, 7, and 9 of their self-titled debut LP (released April 6, 2004, on ARRCO/Pretty Activity). The other guest drummer on the album was Brad Conroy of The Boggs (tracks 3, 6, 7). The album was produced by Steve Rivette, Aaron Shoblaske, and the band; tracking was split between Wann’s bedroom and studio sessions with Rivette.

Two documented connections between Fogarino/Interpol and On!Air!Library! predate or coincide with the session:

  1. Shared label orbit. Interpol contributed the track “A Time to Be So Small” to This Is Next Year: A Brooklyn-Based Compilation (Arena Rock Recording Co., July 2001) — the same label that signed On!Air!Library! in 2002. This put Interpol and Arena Rock in contact a full year before OAL’s label deal, making a label-facilitated introduction plausible. Wikipedia — This Is Next Year

  2. 2004 US tour together. Interpol and On!Air!Library! (with The Secret Machines) toured the US in fall 2004, culminating in a two-night stand at Hammerstein Ballroom, New York, November 11–12. Concert Archives; Billboard The album was released April 6, 2004 — before the tour, so the session predated the tour. However, the tour may have been arranged partly because the bands were already acquainted.

No interview or liner-note source has been found that explains the specific personal introduction or how Fogarino came to be booked for the session rather than a regular OAL touring drummer. The Arena Rock label connection (2001–2002 overlap) remains the most concrete documented link prior to the session. Wikipedia’s OAL article (as of 2026-07-31) does not mention Fogarino at all.
