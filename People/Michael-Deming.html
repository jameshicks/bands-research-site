Overview

Michael Deming is a producer, audio engineer, and musician who has been active in indie and alternative rock since the early 1990s, accumulating over 300 credits across genres including folk rock, country rock, and psychedelic rock. He is a member of Lilys, Pernice Brothers, and ¡Action!, placing him at the intersection of several intersecting indie scenes. His most sustained creative partnership is with Thom Monahan (69 shared releases), and he has been a recurring presence on records by Beachwood Sparks, Kurt Heasley, and Joe Pernice. His label work spans Sub Pop — his longest-running relationship, stretching from 1995 through 2023 — alongside ché, Sire, and spinART.
