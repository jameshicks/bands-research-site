Noel Kupersmith

Bassist, vibraphonist. Real name: Noel E. Kupersmith. Discogs ID: 135338. Name variations: Kupersmith, NK, Noel, Noel Kuppersmith, Noël.

Chicago-based bassist active primarily in jazz and experimental music. Original bassist of Chicago Underground Trio (with Rob Mazurek and Chad Taylor) on the first three records (1999–2003); also a member of the Chicago Underground Quartet (Discogs ID: 40217).
