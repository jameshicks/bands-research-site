Joe Lally

Joseph Francis Lally (born December 3, 1963, Silver Spring, Maryland). Bassist, vocalist, songwriter, label owner. Discogs artist ID: 518383. Best known as the founding bassist of Fugazi; also runs Tolotta Records.

Name variations on record: Joe, Joseph Lally, Lally, Lilly.

Biography

Pre-Fugazi (to 1987)

Born in Silver Spring, Maryland. Little documented in Discogs about his pre-Fugazi years. He was embedded in the Washington DC hardcore scene orbiting Dischord Records before Fugazi’s founding.

Fugazi (1987–2003)

Lally was present from Fugazi’s first show (September 3, 1987, Wilson Center, Washington DC — documented in the Fugazi Live Series). With Ian MacKaye, Guy Picciotto, and Brendan Canty, he remained the band’s bassist through their entire active run.

Side projects during Fugazi years

Decahedron / The Black Sea — Lally played bass in this project with Shelby Cinca, Jason Hamacher, and Jake Brown. Discogs lists The Black Sea (ID 576447) and Decahedron (ID 1023043) as aliases of each other. Released The Black Sea EP (2002, Lovitt Records).

Sevens (ID 758393) — lesser-documented DC band; also included Bobby Sullivan, Christopher Farrall, Joshua Larue, Mark Sullivan.

Tolotta Records — Lally founded this label (Discogs label ID 61461). Executive-produced releases on it: The Obsessed (2000 reissue) and Spirit Caravan’s Elusive Truth (2001), plus Jug Fulla Sun (1999) and So Mortal Be / Undone Mind (2002). The first Tolotta release was 777 by Sevens (1994), with Lally on bass.

Ataxia (2004)

Lally played bass and contributed vocals and songwriting on Automatic Writing (2004, Record Collection) under the group name Ataxia, alongside John Frusciante (guitar, synth, voice, producer) and Josh Klinghoffer (drums, synth, voice). All songs credited to Lally, Frusciante, and Klinghoffer. A second record, AW II, followed in 2007 (Record Collection). Both engineered by Ryan Hewitt; Frusciante produced.

Solo work (2004–2011)

Lally spent time in Rome and recorded his solo albums partly with Italian collaborators.

The Messthetics (2018–present)

Lally co-founded The Messthetics with Brendan Canty (drums) and Anthony Pirog (guitar). An instrumental post-punk / jazz-inflected trio, releasing on Dischord then moving to Impulse! for collaborative records with saxophonist James Brandon Lewis.

Coriky (2020)

Coriky is the trio of Lally, Ian MacKaye, and Amy Farina (drums). Self-titled album released 2020 on Dischord (cat. Dischord 190). Recorded and mixed by Coriky and Don Zientara; mastered by T.J. Lipple. Songs credited to Coriky collectively.

Open Questions

— this is a James Brandon Lewis Trio album, not a Messthetics-led record. The Messthetics (Lally, Canty, Pirog) appear only on the final track "Fear Not" as a featured ensemble. Engineered by Martin Bisi (tracks 1–10) and Shahzad Ismaily (track 11). Mixed and mastered by Christopher Hoffman. No producer credit on Discogs, Bandcamp, Anti- Records site, Apple Music, AllMusic, or in press interviews (Free Jazz Collective, DownBeat, Magnet, Tape Op #163). The album appears to carry no formal producer credit — consistent with self-directed jazz recordings. Tape Op #163 interview with Lewis and The Messthetics ([tapeop.com](https://tapeop.com/interviews/163/messthetics-and-james-brandon-lewis-tonal-park)) discusses the "Fear Not" session at Shahzad Ismaily's Figure 8 Studio but does not name a producer for the wider record.
