Kevin Wilkinson

English drummer and session musician. Real name Kevin Michael Wilkinson. Born June 11, 1958, Stoke-on-Trent. Died July 17, 1999. Discogs artist ID: 379225.

Overview

Prolific session and band drummer who worked across a wide range of post-punk and new wave acts in the 1980s–90s. His association with the Robert Fripp circle came via The League Of Gentlemen (2), where he replaced the original drummer Johnny Toobad in late 1980.
