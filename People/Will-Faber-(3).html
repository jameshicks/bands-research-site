Will Faber (3)

Discogs ID: 5022024 Active: 2016–2025 (Discogs span) Based: Chicago, IL Roles: Multi-instrumentalist — guitar, flute, bells, ngoni, voice, violin, synth, saw (bowed metal), zither, percussion

Overview

Will Faber is a Chicago-based multi-instrumentalist whose work spans free improvisation, avant-garde jazz, folk, and experimental music. He is a core member of two trios that overlap with Ben Lamar Gay’s orbit: El Is A Sound Of Joy (with Gay and Rob Frye) and I See Lightning Trio (same three members, separate entity). He appears on Gay’s landmark debut LP Downtown Castles Can Never Block The Sun (2018) and the 2025 follow-up Yowzers, both on International Anthem Recording Company.

Alongside his collaborative work, Faber has a self-released solo discography of CDr and cassette recordings on Not On Label, documenting solo guitar in just intonation, fretless 12-string acoustic guitar, and solo electric guitar — a quieter, less Discogs-visible strand of his practice.

Bio by Project / Era

El Is A Sound Of Joy (2016–present)

El Is A Sound Of Joy is a trio of Faber, Ben Lamar Gay, and Rob Frye (Discogs ID 5022025). Their only release under this name on Discogs as primary artist is Plus (2016, No Index, IDX1003), a CD with two extended-form pieces (“Breathe Easier / Northwest Passage / Flood Map Magic” and “An Earthquake Prediction Is Confirmed / Mangrove Recombination / Flood Map Resurrection”). The full lineup on Plus is larger than the trio — it also includes Brooks Johnson (2) (bass), Stephen Ptacek (percussion, cello, bells, voice), Ritwik Banerji (saxophone), Alex Inglizian (synth, electric piano, violin, electronics), and Joshua Sirotiak (tuba).

A second CDr EP, Ephemerides (2017, Not On Label), is credited to El Is A Sound Of Joy and documents a similar large-group improvisation: Faber on flute, guitar, and percussion; Gay on cornet, synth, voice, and percussion; Frye on bass clarinet, flute, percussion, saxophone (also mixed and mastered); Steve Ptacek on percussion; Alex Inglizian on synth, drum machine (also mixed and mastered); and Bridget Bancroft on voice and percussion.

A third CDr EP, El Is A Sound Of Joy With Avreeayl Ra (undated), documents a session with Chicago percussionist Avreeayl Ra (real name Arthur O’Neil). Faber plays guitar and flute; Gay plays cornet, voice, and bells; Frye plays saxophone, flute, bass clarinet, and bells; Inglizian adds keyboards and electronics; Steve Ptacek adds percussion. Avreeayl Ra performs on percussion. The music is categorized under Abstract, Avant-garde Jazz, Free Funk, Free Jazz, and Experimental.

I See Lightning Trio (undated)

I See Lightning Trio (Discogs ID 7937954) is a separate but identically-membered trio — Gay, Frye, and Faber — with one self-released CDr EP on Not On Label. The instrumentation is more austere and folklike than El Is A Sound Of Joy: Faber plays guitar, ngoni, zither, flute, bells, percussion, and voice; Gay plays banjo, bells, percussion, and voice; Frye plays guitar, mountain dulcimer, bells, percussion, and voice. The fourteen short track titles (“knotted, looped back over and tied,” “Banjo as drum,” “Brass bowed struck and rubbed to ring”) suggest a conceptual concern with timbre and physical gesture. Styles include Appalachian Music, Free Improvisation, Abstract, and Ambient.

Solo Work (2017–2020)

Faber maintains a parallel self-released body of work:

  • Fretless 12-String Guitar (2017, CDr EP, Not On Label) — Four pieces for fretless 12-string acoustic guitar, composed and recorded by Faber alone. Track titles engage world-music idiom and folk tradition (“Kalyani,” “Jiu Kuang [Wine Madness] / Down In The River To Pray,” “Como Hills,” “Flutes [composed as wind lines and empty lines]”). Styles: Abstract, Appalachian Music, Experimental, Free Improvisation, Ethereal.

  • Folded Hills (2018, CDr album, self-released) — Nine-track album for fretless 12-string acoustic guitar. Eight of the nine compositions are credited to Faber; the exception is “Fireflies,” credited to Ben Lamar Gay, and “Balladesque,” credited to Ernest “Khabeer” Dawkins. The inclusion of a Gay composition suggests the album is a document of the broader circle rather than purely solo work, even if performed solo.

  • Deer Creek Tunnel (2020) Acoustic Guitar In Just Intonation (2020, CDr, Not On Label) — A long-form solo guitar record (nine tracks, 77 minutes total) built around a single technique: acoustic guitar in just intonation. Track titles follow a time-of-day arc (“Morning,” “Rain,” “Afternoon,” “Night”).

  • Grounds And Skies (2019) Solo Electric Guitar (2020, cassette, Not On Label) — Solo electric guitar; recorded by Alex Inglizian. Pieces interweave original composition with standards (“Love In Outer Space” by Sun Ra, “Blue Skies” by Irving Berlin). This cassette is the only solo Faber release with an outside credit, suggesting it may have been recorded at Experimental Sound Studio.

Ben Lamar Gay Collaborations (2018, 2025)

Faber is credited on two Ben Lamar Gay solo albums on International Anthem Recording Company:

  • Downtown Castles Can Never Block The Sun (2018) — Faber plays guitar, synth, and flute on tracks B6 and B7, alongside Joshua Sirotiak (tuba, flute) and Tommaso Moretti (drums, flute), with recording and mixing by Richard Malstrom. The album is a compilation spanning many recording sessions and collaborators; Faber’s contribution is two of the album’s final tracks.

  • Yowzers (2025) — Faber plays guitar, bells, voice, and ngoni across the full album. The core band for Yowzers is Faber, Rob Frye (flute, bass clarinet), Tommaso Moretti (drums, percussion, voice), and Matthew Davis (4) (tuba, piano, bells, voice), with additional voice from Ayanna Woods, Tramaine Parker, and Ugochi Nwaogwugwu. Sequenced by Scott McNiece; recorded and mixed by Dave Vettraino; mastered by David Allen (10).
