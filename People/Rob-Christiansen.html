Rob Christiansen

Audio engineer and editor active in the Washington D.C. area, mid-1990s. Discogs artist ID: 281125.

Recurring engineer on Shute Records releases. Recorded Jenhitt’s Music For The Royal Fireworks (1995) and Prototype Sound Series: Number One (1996), mixed / edited the Velocity Girl / Chisel split 7” (Shute No. 8, 1997), and mixed tracks on Jenhitt’s Prototype Sound Series: Number One.
