Bill Siddons

American music manager, born 1948. Discogs artist ID: 1609317. Best known as personal manager of The Doors (1968–1972); subsequently ran a long-career management practice across jazz, rock, and metal under successive firm names — Siddons & Associates, Crosslight Management, and Roxcore Management.

Overview

Siddons took over management of The Doors in 1968 at around age 19, reportedly hired after working as a roadie for the band. He steered them through Jim Morrison’s legal troubles, the L.A. Woman sessions, and Morrison’s death in Paris (July 1971). He continued managing the three surviving members through their post-Morrison albums and then moved into a broader management career that spanned jazz-fusion (John Klemmer), singer-songwriters (Dirk Hamilton), hard rock (Chequered Past), the CSN catalog (managing Crosby & Nash), industrial metal (KMFDM), and guitar rock (Jerry Cantrell, Doyle Bramhall II).

He was co-founder of Siddons & Associates (with Barbara Rose, Discogs ID: 2962621) and later of Roxcore Management (Discogs ID: 6843544) and The Core (Discogs ID: 6770973, with Brian Frank).

Career by Era

The Doors Period (1968–1972)

Siddons appears in the Discogs credits for the final three Doors studio recordings:

  • L.A. Woman (1971, Elektra) — Management [Personal Management]. The last studio album with Jim Morrison; produced by Bruce Botnick and The Doors. Session players included Jerry Scheff (bass) and Marc Benno (rhythm guitar).
  • Other Voices (1971, Elektra) — Management [Personal]. Post-Morrison album by the surviving trio (Ray Manzarek, Robby Krieger, John Densmore), again produced by Botnick.
  • Hot Wacks (1972, Elektra) — Management. Second post-Morrison album by The Wackers (a separate band also managed by Siddons alongside John Frankenheimer).

Siddons & Associates — Jazz and Singer-Songwriter (1974–1982)

After The Doors dissolved, Siddons managed a cluster of ABC Records artists:

  • John Klemmer — managed across at least four albums: Fresh Feathers (1974, ABC), Touch (1975, ABC), Barefoot Ballet (1976, ABC), Lifestyle (Living And Loving) (1977, ABC). Klemmer was the firm’s most consistent jazz client during this period.
  • Dirk Hamilton — Meet Me At The Crux (1978, Elektra), credited as “Bill Siddons And Associates.” LA singer-songwriter; session work on the album included Clydie King and The Waters on backing vocals.
  • John Guerin — Alias I (1977, ABC). Drummer-led jazz session.
  • Blue And Gray (1981, MCA) — unidentified primary artist; Personal Management credit.
  • Cowboys & Englishmen (1982, MCA) — Management credit.

Crosslight Management — CSN and Supergroups (1982–1984)

Siddons appears in management credits on several major-label releases under the Crosslight Management banner:

  • Crosby, Stills & Nash — Daylight Again (1982, Atlantic); Allies (1983, Atlantic). Credited specifically for Crosby & Nash’s management (Stephen Stills had separate representation — Jeff Wald was also credited on Daylight Again).
  • The Perfect Stranger (1982, Elektra) — Management.
  • What Goes Around… (1983, Atlantic) — Management.
  • Chequered Past — Chequered Past (1984, EMI America) — Management [Crosslight Management], alongside Peter Golden. Supergroup featuring Steve Jones (Sex Pistols), Clem Burke (Blondie), Nigel Harrison (Blondie), Tony Sales, and Michael Des Barres.
  • Inamorata (1984, Atlantic); Jukebox (1984, Motown); Circle (1985, MCA) — Management credits on various acts.

CSN / Crosby & Nash Management (1990s)

Siddons maintained a long relationship with David Crosby and Graham Nash:

  • Live It Up (1990, Atlantic) — CSN Management.
  • CSN box set (1991, Atlantic) — Management [Crosby & Nash].
  • Carry On (2002, Atlantic) — Management [Crosby & Nash].
  • After The Storm (1994, Atlantic) — Management [Personal Management, Crosby & Nash].
  • Thousand Roads (1993, Atlantic) — Management [For Siddons And Associates].
  • Hero (1993, Atlantic) — Management.

Siddons & Associates — Diverse Roster (1990s–2000s)

  • Madonna — Erotica (1992, Epic) — Management [North American Representation]. Siddons handled North American management for this release only.
  • Sure Thing (1992, Epic) — Management [USA].
  • Gravity’s Rainbow (1993, Chrysalis); Somebody’s Baby (1993, Chrysalis) — Management for unidentified acts.
  • I Don’t Know Why I Act This Way (1995, Island) — Management.
  • Wither Blister Burn + Peel (1996, Columbia) — Management.
  • Story Of Life (1999, N-Coded Music) — Executive Producer credit.
  • Jerry Cantrell — Degradation Trip (2002, Roadrunner); Degradation Trip Volumes 1 & 2 (2002, Roadrunner) — Management [For Siddons & Associates]. Alice in Chains guitarist’s solo work.

Roxcore Management / The Core (2007–2018)

  • KMFDM — Tohuvabohu (2007, KMFDM Records/Metropolis) — Management, credited alongside The Core (8). Industrial metal act.
  • The Book (2008, Urbnet Records) — Management.
  • Doyle Bramhall II — Shades (2018, Provogue) — Management [For Roxcore Management]. Credited alongside Roxcore Management entity. Blues-rock guitarist associated with Eric Clapton.
  • Daylight Again (1983, MCA Home Video) — Executive Producer. VHS/video release of the CSN concert film.

2025 Archival Credit

  • The Doors: 1967–1971 Immersed (2025, Doors Music Company/Elektra/Rhino) — Management [Personal Management ‘L.A. Woman’]. Archival box set restating his original credit.
