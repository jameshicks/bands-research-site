Overview

Ryan Morey is a Montreal-based mastering engineer who operates Ryan Morey Mastering, accumulating nearly 900 Discogs credits dating back to 2003. He is deeply embedded in the Quebec indie ecosystem, with sustained work across Indica Records, Slam Disques, Audiogram, La Tribu, Secret City Records, Constellation, and Bonsound Records. His closest collaborators include Jace Lasek, Howard Bilerman, and Gus Van Go, alongside a cluster of Arcade Fire–affiliated musicians — Pietro Amato, Tonio Morin-Vargas, Thierry Amar, Sarah Neufeld, Timothy Kingsbury, and William Butler — pointing to his role as the go-to mastering engineer for a wide swath of Montreal and Canadian indie output. His credits span indie rock, folk, punk, and alternative rock.
