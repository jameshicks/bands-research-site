Jesse Kivel

Musician from Maine. Discogs artist ID: 1770585. Primary vehicle is Kisses, a synth-pop/disco-leaning duo with Zinzi Edmundson, active 2009–2015. Also a member of Princeton (4), Sleeping Bags (2), and Mt. Si. Solo releases on his own New Feelings imprint from 2017. Brother of Matt Kivel.

Overview

Jesse Kivel emerged out of the early-2010s LA indie scene, running in overlapping circles with his brother Matt Kivel. His best-known project is Kisses, a duo he formed with Zinzi Edmundson around 2009. Kisses traded in a particular strain of synth-pop inflected by 1980s dance music, disco, and West Coast sun — warmly produced, hooky, and stylistically a little out of step with the blog-era indie mainstream, which may have limited their profile domestically while earning them a dedicated audience in Japan through the Rallye Label.

The debut Kisses release, The Heart Of The Nightlife (2009), was a self-released digital record with Jesse credited for Music By and Recorded By — essentially a home production, with Andrew Maury co-producing one track and handling the mix. Early singles and EPs appeared on the UK label This Is Music between 2010 and 2011. The full-length Kids In LA (2013) was a significant step up in production: it was produced by Pete Wiggs (of Saint Etienne) and Tim Larcombe, mixed by Andrew Maury, and mastered by Heba Kadry, with Matt Kivel contributing bass. Kids In LA was released in Japan on the Rallye Label (cat# RYECD156); US distribution was patchwork. A second album, Rest In Paradise (2015), followed on Hit City USA / Rallye Label (cat# RYECD-228).

Alongside Kisses, Jesse appeared on releases by other projects. Sleeping Bags (2) released an LP on Easter Everywhere in 2011. Princeton (4) — the Kivel brothers’ band — had a release through Rallye Label in Japan in 2012 (Remembrance Of Things To Come). Later he co-founded the LA trio Mt. Si with Michael David (2) (also known as Studio Paradiso) and Sarah Chernoff; they released 911 (2018) on Cascine, mixed by Alex Epton.

From 2017 Jesse began releasing under his own name on New Feelings, his own imprint (new-feelings.com). The EP Content (2017, NF 006) was produced and mixed by Jeff Brodsky, with Victoria Bergsman among the performers. The full solo LP Infinite Jess (2020) was produced and mixed by Joey Genetti, with a large ensemble of LA musicians including Sam Wilkes (2), Sarah Chernoff, Michael David (2), Jacob Mann (2), Kale Stiles, Jeff Brodsky, and others.

Discogs lists his years active as 2009–2023.
