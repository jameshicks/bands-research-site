Mike Cooper

British guitarist, vocalist, and sound artist. Discogs artist ID: 246642.

Overview

Veteran of the UK free improvisation and experimental scenes. Known for slide guitar, lap steel, pedal steel, viola caipira, and electronic processing. Co-member with Viv Dogan Corringham of National Gallery (2) (resonator guitar duo/trio). Extensive collaborative discography.
