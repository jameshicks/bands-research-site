Joe McPhee

Discogs ID: 18813
Origin: USA

American free jazz multi-instrumentalist (tenor saxophone, pocket trumpet, valve trombone). Long career in avant-garde jazz, associated with Hat Hut/hatOLOGY labels. Member of the Peter Brötzmann Chicago Tentet. Appeared alongside Mars Williams in that context.

Flow Trio / ESP-Disk’ (2021)

Co-primary artist on Flow Trio’s Winter Garden (2021, ESP-Disk’, cat. ESP 5040). Credited as tenor saxophone and co-composer. The release is billed as “Flow Trio With Joe McPhee.” Produced by Steve Holtje; cover painting by Yuko Otomo.

Additional Credits (from Tomeka Reid research)

Co-led Let Our Rejoicing Rise (2022, Corbett vs. Dempsey) with Tomeka Reid; produced by John Corbett, recorded by Alex Inglizian. Also a member of The Margots (with Ken Vandermark, Tomeka Reid, Tim Daisy) — contributed voice and a poem to Pescado (2013, Okka Disk). Appeared on Reid’s Of Things Beyond Thule volumes.
