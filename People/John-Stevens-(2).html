John Stevens (2)

Discogs ID: 292493 Real name: John William Stevens Born: June 10, 1940, Brentford, England Died: September 13, 1994, London, England Instruments: Drums, percussion, cornet Alias: Bréno T’fordo Active: 1960s–1994

British drummer and bandleader; one of the founding figures of British free jazz and free improvisation. Formed the Spontaneous Music Ensemble (ID: 207925) in 1965–66, which became one of the first and most influential British free improvisation collectives. The SME’s approach — minimal, interaction-focused, chamber-scale improvisation — was a formative environment for both Evan Parker and Derek Bailey.
