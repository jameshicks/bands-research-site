Mauro Pezzente

Discogs ID: 723021
Real name: Mauro Pezzente
Based: Montreal, Quebec, Canada
Instruments: Bass guitar, electric bass

Bassist and founding member of Godspeed You Black Emperor!. Present on all studio recordings. Also a member of Maica Mia (ID: 2805492). The Hotel2Tango space (later a recording studio) was originally occupied by Pezzente and his partner Kiva Stimac as a gallery/living space (Gallery Quiva) from 1995.
