Quentin Stoltzfus

Philadelphia-based songwriter, musician, engineer, and producer. Discogs artist ID: 348156. Alias: Light Heat.

Overview

Stoltzfus is best known as the core songwriter and driving force behind Mazarin, a Philadelphia indie-pop group with releases on Victoria Records, Rocket Girl, and Bella Union. He also records under the alias Light Heat (released on Ribbon Music, 2013). His studio involvement in the Philadelphia indie scene brought him into the Relay (3) orbit — he co-contributed additional recording to both of Relay’s 2006 Bubble Core releases alongside Mickey Walker.
