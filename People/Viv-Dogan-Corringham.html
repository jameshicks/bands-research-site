Viv Dogan Corringham

Real name: Vivienne Dogan Corringham. British vocalist, composer, and sound artist, currently based in New York. Discogs artist ID: 924071. Website: vivcorringham.org.

Name variations on record include: Viv Corringham, Vivienne Corringham, Vivienne Dogan-Corringham, Vivienne Doğan-Corringham, V. Dogan Corringham, Viv D. Corringham, and others.

Biography

Viv Corringham has worked internationally since the early 1980s, active across music performance, audio installation, and soundwalks. She is described as “a vital force in improvised music since the late 1970s” and “a vocalist of stunning virtuosity.” Her practice explores relations between voice, place, and walking — responding with sung improvisations to recorded and live soundscapes.

Her career begins in the early 1980s with UK all-female experimental and post-punk contexts, including the all-female band Tour De Force (5) (c. 1980) and the experimental outfit WITS. Through the 1980s and 1990s she worked extensively in world and ethnically inflected music, including with the Greek-music group Talking To Charos (1991) and the Middle Eastern/belly dance ensemble Noor Shimaal (based in Fairbanks, AK). She sang on several ARC Music releases combining Turkish folk, Middle Eastern, and world fusion idioms.

Her connection to free improvisation deepened through collaborations with Mike Cooper, Lol Coxhill, Steve Beresford, and Max Eastley — figures central to the London and European free improv scenes. The Monkey Puzzle Trio with Charles Hayward and Nick Doyne-Ditmas developed a more song-oriented but still experimental space; the trio recorded two albums for Slowfoot Records (2010, 2014).

She holds an MA in Sonic Art with Distinction from Middlesex University, London, and a BA in Theatre Design from Nottingham Trent University. She studied Deep Listening with composer Pauline Oliveros and is a certified Deep Listening teacher. She has received McKnight Composer Fellowships in 2006 and 2012.

Her ongoing project Shadow-walks — audio installations and soundwalks presented in gallery and public contexts — has been performed in 18+ countries, from New York to Istanbul to Hong Kong.

From the 2010s onward she has released predominantly on UK labels Linear Obsessional Recordings and Flaming Pines, the latter specialising in field recording and landscape-based sound art. Her 2024–25 Soundwalkscapes series on Flaming Pines is a direct distillation of the Shadow-walks practice: all tracks composed and photographed by her.

V-Human and Family Fodder Connection

Viv Corringham collaborated with Alig (John Pearce of Family Fodder) as the duo V-Human (Discogs ID 69181). Recordings made for this project — dating approximately to the late 1990s / early 2000s — were eventually compiled and released as the Family Fodder album Foreverandever + Sex Works (Jungle Records, FREUDCD119, 2016). She is credited as vocalist (tracks 1–10) on that release. Some of the original recordings had earlier appeared on an obscure Italian release under Alig’s alter-ego “Johnny Human.” (source: jungle-records.net)
