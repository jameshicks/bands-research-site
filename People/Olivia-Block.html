Overview

Olivia Block is a Chicago-based musician, media artist, and composer who combines chamber instrumentation, field recordings, and digital synthesis. She began her career in Austin, Texas in the 1990s, playing in bands including The Thorns and The Marble Index, before developing a practice that extends to site-specific installations and scores for performance and film. With over 100 Discogs credits spanning three decades, she has released on labels including Room40, Entr’acte, and Sedimental, and is a frequent collaborator with figures from the Chicago experimental scene such as Alex Inglizian, Kyle Bruckmann, and Jon Mueller.
