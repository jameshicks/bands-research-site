Notes (web) — Lali Puna collaboration

The collaboration centres on the track “Out There.” It originated on Takahashi’s 22nd solo album Page By Page (Universal Music Japan, TOCT-26798, released 11 March 2009), where it appears as track 1. That album was produced in collaboration with Tomohiko Gondo (also a member of pupa) and was conceived as an internationally collaborative, electronics-oriented project — described in promotional copy as “the conclusion of the group’s devotion to Elektronica.” Takahashi recruited a roster of international guests with whom he had existing or admired relationships: Steve Jansen (England, described as a friend), Cornelius / Keigo Oyamada (Tokyo, frequent collaborator since the Sketch Show tour), amiina (Iceland), ATOMtm (Chile), and Lali Puna (Germany). The album also includes a track titled “Valerie” — almost certainly named for Valerie Trebeljahr, Lali Puna’s lead singer and lyricist, suggesting a closer personal connection than a one-off feature. (CDJapan product page; White Noise Records description)

A version of “Out There” then appeared on Lali Puna’s Our Inventions (Morr Music MM098, April 2010) — credited as featuring Yukihiro Takahashi, with additional production by Tomohiko Gondo. Reviews of Our Inventions describe it as “a collaboration with Yellow Magic Orchestra’s Yukihiro Takahashi” and note it “sounds more than anything like a prototypical Lali Puna composition,” suggesting the Morr Music version was substantially reshaped by Lali Puna from the Page By Page original. (Textura review; Drowned in Sound archived review)

What is not documented in any public source found: who initiated the contact between Takahashi and Trebeljahr, how they communicated, or the precise sequence of how the two album versions were produced relative to each other. No interview with either party addresses this directly. The Page By Page liner notes (not publicly transcribed) may contain this information.
