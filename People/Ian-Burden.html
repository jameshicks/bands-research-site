Ian Burden

Bassist and keyboardist; member of The Human League (joined c. 1980) and The Musical Janeens before that. Born Ian Charles Burden. Discogs artist ID: 466320.

Overview

Burden appears on Bouquet Of Steel (1980, Aardvark, STEAL 2) as co-songwriter on the Musical Janeens track “Glen Miller And His Contemporary Intimacies Meets The Musical Janeens Uptown With A Packet Of Jellies And A Caribbean Monolith” (A7), credited as “Ian Burdon.” His presence on this Sheffield compilation marks one of the last documented moments before he joined The Human League, where he remained through their commercial peak.

The Musical Janeens also carried an alias, Graph (2), on Discogs.
