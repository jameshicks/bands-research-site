Paquito D’Rivera

Cuban-American saxophonist and clarinetist, born 4 June 1948 in Havana. Discogs artist ID: 303413.

Leader of the The United Nation Orchestra. Major figure in Latin jazz; defected from Cuba in 1980. Multiple Grammy winner. Long association with Dizzy Gillespie’s orbit; recorded prolifically for Chesky Records, Columbia, Heads Up International, Half Note, and Sunnyside.

The Paquito D’Rivera Quintet — Core Band Members

The working band that D’Rivera has led since the late 1980s/early 1990s. As of the most recent published lineup, members are Oscar Stagnaro (bass), Diego Urcola (trumpet/trombone), Mark Walker (drums/percussion), and Alex Brown (piano). source: paquitodrivera.com/bio/band/, as confirmed Wikipedia rev. 2026-05-27

Oscar Stagnaro — Bass

Peruvian bassist. Joined D’Rivera’s group in 1990 (Berklee profile says “since 1990”; the Berklee Latin Jazz Program article says they “had performed together since 1992” — minor discrepancy, likely reflecting different project start dates vs. first touring credit). Associate professor at Berklee College of Music since 1987 or 1988 (sources vary by one year). paquitodrivera.com/bio/band/

Stagnaro co-founded and served as academic director of the Paquito D’Rivera/Berklee Latin Jazz Program, an intensive summer program launched July 2014 at Berklee. D’Rivera served as artistic director. Stagnaro described D’Rivera as “a great inspiration and a very generous guy” and noted that D’Rivera conveyed professional ethics through example on the bandstand rather than through instruction. source: college.berklee.edu, 2014

Key shared recordings with D’Rivera include Live at the Blue Note (Quintet, Latin Grammy winner 2001), Jazz Meets the Classics, and the Panamericana Suite (MCG Jazz, 2010 — two Grammy Awards). paquitodrivera.com/bio/band/

Diego Urcola — Trumpet / Trombone

Argentine trumpeter and trombonist, born Buenos Aires. Joined D’Rivera’s quintet in 1991. Three-time Grammy nominee; performs with Jimmy Heath and the Dizzy Gillespie Alumni All-Star Big Band. Described by D’Rivera’s site as a “disciplined technician” combining classical and jazz proficiency. paquitodrivera.com/bio/band/

The Quintet won the Latin Grammy Award for Best Latin Jazz Album for Live at the Blue Note (2001), with Urcola as a member. Wikipedia, rev. 2026-05-27

In 2020, Urcola led his own date — Diego Urcola Quartet featuring Paquito D’Rivera, El Duelo (Sunnyside Records, released 18 September 2020) — a piano-less quartet conceived after the two performed together at Uruguay’s Punta Del Este Jazz Festival. Urcola’s stated aim was to showcase “the more uncompromised, freer side of D’Rivera’s improvisational abilities, which are rarely heard on his own recordings.” Personnel: Urcola (trumpet/flugelhorn), D’Rivera (alto saxophone/clarinet), Eric Doob (drums), Hamish Smith (bass); produced/engineered by Luis Bacque. The release notes described their history as “almost thirty years” of collaboration. diegourcola.bandcamp.com/album/el-duelo; latinjazznet.com

Pernell Saturnino — Percussion

Percussionist born in Curaçao. Studied at the Foundation Institution of Music (1982–1986), then at Berklee College of Music from 1988. Began performing and recording with D’Rivera in 1995 and continued to the present (as of Saturnino’s published bio). Grammy Award winner (2008); appeared on sixteen Grammy-nominated recordings, six of which won. bnkcuracao.com/audio/pernell-saturnino/

Shared recordings with D’Rivera (per Saturnino’s bio): Portrait of Cuba, Island Story (Caribbean Jazz Project), Live at Manchester Guild, Live at the Blue Note, The Clarinetist, Big Band Time (WDR Big Band), Mosaic, Calle 54, Funk Tango, and Panamericana Suite (MCG Jazz, 2010). Also listed on Trombone Man (Juan Pablo Torre) as a shared project. bnkcuracao.com/audio/pernell-saturnino/

Note: Saturnino is not mentioned in the D’Rivera Wikipedia article (as of rev. 2026-05-27) and is not listed as a formal quintet member on D’Rivera’s official site — his role appears to be as a frequent/recurring sideman rather than a fixed-band position.
