Barry Andrews

Keyboardist, vocalist, composer. Real name Barry William Andrews. Co-founder and sole continuous member of Shriekback. Discogs artist ID: 64092.

Aliases/alt. names: Stic Basin (Discogs alias).

Overview

Andrews was a founding member of XTC, appearing on their 1978 debut White Music and contributing to early recordings before departing in 1979. He subsequently co-founded Shriekback in 1981–82 in Kentish Town, London, alongside Dave Allen (ex-Gang of Four) and Carl Marsh. Andrews is the sole member present on every Shriekback release across both the 1980s run and the 2002-onward continuation.

As the band’s primary songwriter and keyboardist, Andrews drove the musical direction across all eras: dense art-funk in the Y Records period, tribal-dance on Big Night Music, ambient-electronic on Sacred City, and increasingly abstract/atmospheric work on the self-released 2010s–2020s output.
