Overview

Jeff Pezzati is a Chicago vocalist best known as the frontman of Naked Raygun, one of the city’s defining punk acts, and The Bomb. Before settling into that vocal role he was Big Black’s original bass player in the early 1980s, placing him at the start of two foundational Chicago underground acts. His catalogue spans over four decades — from early Ruthless Records and Homestead releases in the mid-1980s through Quarterstick Records and Touch And Go work, with recent output on Audio Platter and No Idea Records. His core creative circle is anchored by longtime Naked Raygun bandmates John Haggerty, Pierre Kezdy, and Eric Spicer, alongside producer Iain Burgess who shares credits across the bulk of his recorded work.
