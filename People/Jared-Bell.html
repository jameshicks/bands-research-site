Overview

Jared Bell is a graphic designer and musician, a member of Lymbyc Systym alongside Michael Bell. His catalogue is concentrated in the 2010s, with 24 of his 28 selected releases appearing in that decade, the majority on Western Vinyl between 2009 and 2018, with earlier work on Mush from 2007 to 2009. Beyond the band, Bell built a substantial working relationship with composer Christopher Tignor, sharing ten credits — matching his credit count with Michael Bell outside of Lymbyc Systym itself. His output spans ambient, post-rock, and experimental territory, with recurring appearances by engineers John Congleton and Josh Bonati.
