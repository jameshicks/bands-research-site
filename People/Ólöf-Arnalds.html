Ólöf Arnalds

Discogs ID: 448904
Born: 4 January 1980, Iceland
Active: 2002–present
Instruments: Vocals, violin, stroh violin, guitar, charango, koto, viola, xylophone

Icelandic singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist rooted in the Reykjavík independent music scene of the early 2000s. She studied violin and classical singing from 1988 to 2002, then composition and new media at the Iceland Academy of the Arts (2002–2006). She was a touring member of múm for roughly five years from 2003 before launching a solo career, and has released six albums spanning Icelandic-language folk troubadour work, English-language indie folk, and more recent material on Bella Union. Key collaborators across her career include bassist Skúli Sverrisson, Sigur Rós members Kjartan Sveinsson and Davíð Þór Jónsson, múm’s Gunnar Örn Tynes, and Björk, who contributed uncredited backing vocals to Innundir Skinni (2010). (Wikipedia)
