Richard Bishop was born in Saginaw, Michigan in 1960. He is best known for his 26 year involvement with the band Sun City Girls and as an experimental solo guitarist as Sir Richard Bishop whose work often reflects the shadow worlds of India, the Middle East, North Africa, and other points along the Gypsy trail. He also plays a variety of other instruments including piano. Brother of Alan Bishop.

— Discogs artist profile, ID 316007

Overview

Richard Bishop (born 1960, Saginaw, Michigan) spent 26 years as a core member of Sun City Girls alongside his brother Alan Bishop — their 59 shared releases make Alan his closest recorded collaborator. His substantial solo catalogue as Sir Richard Bishop draws on acoustic and electric guitar traditions from India, the Middle East, and North Africa, with his output concentrated in the 2000s and 2010s across Drag City (11 releases, 2007–2025) and Unrock (9 releases, 2012–2025). Beyond the Sun City Girls axis — which also includes Charlie Gocher (50 shared releases) and Scott Colburn (39) — Bishop has worked in ensemble contexts including Rangda (with Ben Chasny and Chris Corsano), Clandestine Trio, and Paris 1942.
