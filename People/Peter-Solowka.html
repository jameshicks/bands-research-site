Overview

Peter Solowka is a guitarist, mandolin player, and songwriter who built his catalogue across two distinct projects from the mid-1980s onward. He is best known as a founding member of The Wedding Present, where his 108 shared credits with David Gedge and Keith Gregory place him at the core of the band’s early indie-rock output on Reception, Strange Fruit, and Cooking Vinyl. He later co-founded The Ukrainians with Len Liggins, a folk-rock band that grafted Ukrainian traditional music onto indie-punk energy and released through Koka Records, Omnium, and Cooking Vinyl across the 1990s and 2000s. His 143 total credits span Guitar, Mandolin, Backing Vocals, and Written-By roles, with Amelia Fletcher and Chris Allison among his broader collaborators outside those two bands.
