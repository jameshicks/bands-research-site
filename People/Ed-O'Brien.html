Ed O’Brien

Discogs ID: 365794 Real name: Edward John O’Brien Born: 15 April 1968, Oxford, UK Solo alias: EOB

English guitarist and songwriter. Co-founding member of Radiohead (guitar, effects, backing vocals, 1985–present). Solo career as EOB from 2019. Also played with On A Friday (pre-Radiohead, 1985–1991).
