David Dewaele

Belgian musician, producer, and DJ from Ghent. Discogs artist ID: 430762. One half of the Dewaele brothers duo at the core of Soulwax (ID: 34128) and 2 Many DJ’s (ID: 48919), with his older brother Stephen Dewaele (ID: 435059). Co-runs the Deewee label and studio in Ghent. Son of Marc Dewaele (Discogs ID: 1046281). Also credited as “Davido Sulwacce.”

Overview

David Dewaele is a Belgian musician, producer, and DJ who operates almost exclusively in tandem with his older brother Stephen. Together they are the engine of Soulwax — as songwriters, multi-instrumentalists, producers, and mixing engineers on every studio album the band has released, from the grunge-tinged Much Against Everyone’s Advice (1998) through the electronic isolation of From Deewee (2017) and the 2025 LP All Systems Are Lying. As 2 Many DJ’s they pioneered the mash-up bootleg format with the As Heard On Radio Soulwax series (2002–2012), reworking hundreds of licensed and unlicensed tracks into multi-hour DJ megamixes.

Beyond Soulwax, the brothers founded the Deewee label and recording studio in Ghent in the mid-2010s, which functions as both creative hub and imprint. From Deewee they have produced records for Charlotte Adigéry, Bolis Pupul, Marie Davidson, and James Righton, among others. David is the primary guitarist in Soulwax’s live incarnation; on studio records he and Stephen divide instruments broadly and are credited jointly.

The individual Discogs artist page for David Dewaele carries only one release in his own name — the 2023 score Original Music For ‘Lullaby For Scavengers’ By Kim Noble (credited “Stephen & David Dewaele”) — because virtually all releases appear under Soulwax or the 2 Many DJ’s or alias project names.
