Oct 27, 20262 min read

Warren Ellis

Discogs ID: 256751
Born: 14 February 1965, Ballarat, Victoria, Australia
Instruments: Violin, viola, piano, mandolin, bouzouki

Australian multi-instrumentalist. Core member of Dirty Three (1992–present). Joined Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds in 1996; co-founded Grinderman with Nick Cave. Extensive film soundtrack work: Mustang (2015), Django (2017), Gauguin (2017), This Train I Ride (2020), She Walks In Beauty (2021). Solo releases include 3 Pieces For Violin (2002, King Crab Records). Married to Delphine Ellis (née Ciampi — her photographs appear on Dirty Three sleeve art). Former groups: Blackeyed Susans, STM (2), Kim Salmon And The Surrealists, Charlie Marshall And The Body Electric.

Key Credits with Blackeyed Susans

All Souls Alive (1993, Torn & Frayed TORN CD5) — violin, organ, accordion, piano, harpsichord on 8 of 10 tracks. Reveal Yourself (2009, Liberation Music retrospective) — violin, accordion, piano on the All Souls Alive era disc.
