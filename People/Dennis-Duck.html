Dennis Duck

Discogs ID: 232626 Real name: Dennis Mehaffey Aliases: Dennis Mehaffey (also listed separately as artist 727382)

Drummer and founding member of The Dream Syndicate (1981–1989), where he played alongside Karl Precoda, Kendra Smith, and Steve Wynn. Also a member of Rainy Day (1984), the Paisley Underground covers collective. Separately active in Smegma, appearing on 27+ shared releases including Mirage (2010) and 40 Years Of Smegma (2015).
