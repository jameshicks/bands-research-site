Neil Halstead

Discogs ID: 51139
Real name: Not listed (Discogs name variations: Neal Halsted, N. Halstead, etc.)
Born: 7 October 1970, Reading, Berkshire, England
Active: 1989–present
Genre: Shoegaze, Dream Pop, Folk Rock, Indie Rock, Ambient

English singer-songwriter and guitarist. Primary vocalist and songwriter for Slowdive (1989–1995, 2014–present); co-founder of Mojave 3 (1995–~2008); solo recording artist (2001–present); member of Black Hearted Brother and several smaller projects. Responsible for writing the bulk of the Slowdive and Mojave 3 catalogs.

Career Overview

1989–1990: The Pumpkin Fairies / Slowdive Formation

Halstead’s first documented recording credit is under The Pumpkin Fairies — a Reading-based group that self-released a single in 1989 (Not On Label; 29 pressings on Discogs). This was the direct precursor to Slowdive; Discogs lists The Pumpkin Fairies as an alias of Slowdive. Halstead played guitar and co-wrote material.

Slowdive (as a name) began releasing on Creation Records in 1990 — a self-titled debut EP (master ID 9493).

1991–1995: Slowdive

The band’s classic lineup: Neil Halstead (vocals/guitar), Rachel Goswell (vocals/guitar), Nick Chaplin (bass), Simon Scott (drums — from 1990), Christian Savill (guitar). Additional drummers: Adrian Sell (1989), Neil Carter (1990), Ian McCutcheon (1994–1995).

Creation dropped Slowdive after Pygmalion’s disappointing sales and the label’s shift toward Britpop. The band dissolved in 1995.

1995–~2008: Mojave 3

From Slowdive’s ashes, Halstead, Rachel Goswell, and Ian McCutcheon formed Mojave 3. Ivo Watts-Russell of 4AD heard their demo and signed them. The band began recording as “Mojave” but added the “3” after discovering a US band had the name; some early promos still read “Mojave” with a sticker correction.

Core lineup: Neil Halstead (vocals/guitar), Rachel Goswell (vocals), Ian McCutcheon (drums). Extended live/studio members: Simon Rowe (guitar), Christopher Andrews (piano), Alan Forrester (keyboards/vocals, from Out Of Tune era), Raymond Richards (various strings).

2001–present: Solo Work

Concurrent with late Mojave 3 activity, Halstead began a solo recording career on 4AD, then moved to Brushfire Records (Jack Johnson’s label) for subsequent albums.

2013–2014: Black Hearted Brother

Black Hearted Brother — a side project with Mark Van Hoen, Nick Holton, and Paul Blewett, described as “Spacerock/Light Entertainment fusion.” Released Stars Are Our Home (2013, Slumberland Records).

2014–present: Slowdive Reformation

Slowdive announced their reformation in 2014 and toured extensively before releasing two new albums. Halstead remained primary songwriter and producer.

Live / Touring Notes

Bernard Butler connection — Halstead as Butler’s guitarist (summer 1998)

The Discogs biography for Mojave 3 states that Bernard Butler (ex-Suede guitarist) “toured with Mojave 3.” Web research finds this claim is likely inverted: Halstead played guitar for Butler, not Butler for Mojave 3.

What the sources say:

  • Wikipedia’s article on Butler’s debut solo album People Move On (Creation Records, 1998) states: “The shows marked the appearance of Neil Halstead of Mojave 3 as Butler’s touring guitarist, replacing [Andy] Miller, who was busy with Dodgy.” The cited source is Butler’s own archived website (bernardbutler.com/Latest.html, archived by Wayback Machine, 27 Jan 1999). This places Halstead in Butler’s live band during summer 1998 festival dates (T in the Park, Glastonbury). [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_Move_On, as of 2026-07-13]

  • The October 1998 UK Butler headline tour (Cardiff 13 Oct → London Shepherds Bush Empire 28 Oct) is documented on Butler’s own archived news page. Manchester University MDH, 21 Oct 1998, appears in that itinerary. Concert Archives logs “Bernard Butler / Mojave 3” at Manchester University MDH on 21 Oct 1998 — which matches Butler’s headline tour date exactly, strongly indicating Mojave 3 were the support act on that night, not touring members of Butler’s band. [source: https://www.concertarchives.org/concerts/bernard-butler-mojave-3; Bernard Butler news archive https://web.archive.org/web/19981205072411/http://bernardbutler.com/Latest.html]

  • The Wikipedia article on People Move On does not specify whether Halstead continued with Butler’s band beyond the summer festivals into the October UK tour. The archived butler.com news page covering the October 1998 tour makes no mention of Halstead or Mojave 3 in its own band.

  • Neil Halstead’s Wikipedia page and the Mojave 3 Wikipedia article make no claim that Butler played with Mojave 3. The Halstead Wikipedia article notes only that Halstead contributed vocals to Butler’s Friends and Lovers (1999, Creation Records) track “Has Your Mind Got Away?” — a studio guest credit, not a live arrangement. [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Halstead, as of 2026-07-13]

Assessment: The Discogs Mojave 3 band bio claim that “Bernard Butler toured with Mojave 3” is not corroborated by any source found. The documented reality is the reverse: Halstead deputised as Butler’s live guitarist at summer 1998 festivals. Mojave 3 as a band appear to have supported Butler on at least one UK tour date (Manchester, 21 Oct 1998). The Discogs bio claim is likely a garbled or misattributed version of this genuine but inverted connection.
