Joe Foster

Discogs ID: 275385
Real name: Joseph James Foster
Aliases: Joe Foster & The Devil, Joe-From-Hendon, Slaughter Joe, The Slaughter King
Origin: Hendon, London, UK

Producer, musician. Founding member of Television Personalities; performer and co-producer on The Painted Word (1984, Illuminated Records). Co-founded Creation Records with Alan McGee and Edward Ball; left Creation in 1985 but returned later. Founded Kaleidoscope Sound (1986). Had production, A&R, and executive coordination roles at Poptones (founded by Alan McGee in 1999) but was not a co-founder. Founded Rev-Ola, PoppyDisc. Solo releases as Slaughter Joe. Member of Biff Bang Pow!, Teenage Filmstars, Razorcuts, The Playboy Revolutionary. Runs Poppy Productions, controlling licensing/publishing for himself, McGee, and associated artists.

Poptones

Poptones (Discogs label ID: 1925; parent: Poptones Ltd.) was founded by Alan McGee in 1999 after the demise of Creation Records, and wound down in summer 2007. Named after a Public Image Ltd song. Foster was not a co-founder; he contributed as producer, A&R, executive coordinator, and liner notes writer across at least 19 individual release credits on the label. 433 releases in the Discogs catalog.

The Hives were the label’s biggest commercial success; Poptones licensed their UK releases from Sweden’s Burning Heart Records, breaking them in the UK with Your New Favourite Band (2002).
