Johanna Fateman

Musician, writer, and art critic. Discogs artist ID: 526049. Real name: Johanna Rachel Fateman.

Overview

New York-based co-founder of Le Tigre with Kathleen Hanna and Sadie Benning in 1998. Contributed keyboards, electronics, and vocals throughout Le Tigre’s original run (1998–2005) and reunion (2023–). After Le Tigre’s hiatus she co-founded Men (2) with JD Samson. Active as an art critic and writer outside music.
