Overview

Mick Linehan is a guitarist who was a member of both Alternative TV and The Lines, two UK acts central to the late-1970s post-punk scene. His recorded credits run from 1978 — when Alternative TV appeared on Deptford Fun City Records — through to the 2010s, with a concentration in the 1980s across punk, new wave, art rock, and experimental styles. Recurring collaborators include Alternative TV figurehead Mark Perry, Grant Showbiz, and the collective Here & Now, and his catalogue spans labels from Red Records in the early 1980s through to Acute Records and Grey Scale into the 2010s.
