Clare Moore

Discogs artist ID: 429429 Real name: Clare Moore Nationality: Australian Role: Drummer, percussionist, vocalist, co-writer, producer Partner/collaborator: Dave Graney (husband) Active: 1981 – present (Discogs credits span 1982–2024)

Overview

Clare Moore is an Australian drummer who has been Dave Graney’s primary musical partner since the early 1980s. She has played drums and/or percussion on virtually every Graney recording across all his band names. She also shares co-writing and production credits on much of the output, and has released recordings under her own name.

She was a founding member of The Moodists (from the Sputniks Adelaide group), and has continued through Dave Graney & The Coral Snakes, The Dave Graney Show, and Dave Graney And The Lurid Yellow Mist / MistLY.
