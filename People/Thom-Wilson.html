Overview

Thom Wilson was an American record producer and engineer based at The Village Recorder in West Los Angeles, who began his career engineering soft rock in the mid-1970s before becoming a central figure in US punk production from the early 1980s onward. He produced The Offspring’s first three albums and accumulated over 1,150 credits, with his catalogue distributed across Epitaph, Better Youth Organization, and Alternative Tentacles — the backbone labels of Southern California punk. His collaborator network centers on the full Offspring lineup, Dead Kennedys members including East Bay Ray and Rikk Agnew, and mastering engineer Eddy Schreyer. Wilson died on February 8, 2015.
