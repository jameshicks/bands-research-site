Bill Gilonis

Discogs ID: 180277
Active: 1980–present
Origin: UK (b. 1958; a.k.a. “Billy Rebel”)

British guitarist, multi-instrumentalist, producer, and engineer. Co-founded The Work and Woof Records with Tim Hodgkinson in 1980. Relocated to Zurich in 1993 where he continued producing and engineering.

Production and Engineering Credits (selected)

Per his Discogs profile, Gilonis worked as producer, engineer, or musician with: Robert Wyatt, News from Babel (Chris Cutler, Lindsay Cooper, Zeena Parkins, Dagmar Krause), David Thomas, Peter Blegvad, Ut, Lindsay Cooper Film Music Group, Hail. Post-Zurich: produced Swiss bands including No Secrets in the Family, The Jellyfish Kiss, and Lödig.

Notable publications: co-authored book Lost in Translation with Alex Julyan (Lost & Found Publishing, 2009).
