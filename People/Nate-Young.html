Nate Young

Discogs ID: 475551

American noise/experimental musician. Co-founder and vocalist/electronics of Wolf Eyes, alongside Aaron Dilloway and John Olson. Runs solo projects under aliases including Betrayor, Lone Wolf (9), Jean Street, Murder Dog, and others. Co-recorded The Gag File with Aaron Dilloway (Dais Records, 2017). 10 shared releases with Dilloway in the Discogs catalog.
