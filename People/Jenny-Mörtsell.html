Overview

Jenny Mörtsell (born December 6, 1976) is a Swedish illustrator and musician whose stage persona Rozzetta originated as an Italian-disco art project during her studies at Konstfack, where she launched herself as a disco artist for her final degree work. Her catalogue is sparse but spread across three decades — a 2004 debut, a 2010s run on Slender Means Society and Marriage Records, and a 2022 appearance on GALTTA Media — with credits spanning vocals, lyrics, production, and cover art. Her deepest professional relationship is with Parenthetical Girls, with whom she shares eight releases, alongside recurring ties to Paul Alcott, Jherek Bischoff, and Zac Pennington.
