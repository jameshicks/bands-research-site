Overview

James van Leuven is a musician, engineer, and producer whose credits span drums, keyboards, percussion, and production across indie rock and lo-fi contexts. Active from at least 1995 through the late 2000s, he is a member of Automaton Adventure Series, Plan B, Sub Debs, and Sun Breaks. His catalogue divides evenly between the 1990s and 2000s, with releases spread across small independent imprints — Galleon Key Recordings appearing most frequently — alongside a one-off for K Records. His closest recorded collaborators include Bill Jones, Chris Duryee, and producer Phil Ek, pointing to a presence in the Pacific Northwest indie underground.
