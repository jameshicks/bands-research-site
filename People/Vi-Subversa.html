Vi Subversa

Vocalist, guitarist, lyricist. Born Frances Sokolov Sansom, 20 June 1935; died 19 February 2016. Discogs artist IDs: 420468 (Vi Subversa), 16129248 (Frances Sokolov Sansom).

Overview

Lead voice, guitarist, and primary lyricist of Poison Girls, the Brighton anarcho-punk band active from 1977 through the mid-1990s. Vi Subversa was already 40 when she gave her first public performance at Sussex University in 1975 as part of The Body Show, and was 44 when the band’s debut single appeared in 1979 — an unusual entry point into punk. Born of East-European Jewish parents, she spent two years in Israel in the late 1950s before returning to the UK.

Her lyrics were written explicitly from a radical feminist perspective, distinguishing Poison Girls within the predominantly male anarcho-punk scene. She is featured in the documentary film She’s a Punk Rocker.

Her children — Pete Fender (born Daniel Sansom, 1964) and Gem Stone (born Gemma Sansom, 1967) — both became musicians in the same anarcho-punk network, as members of Fatal Microbes and Rubella Ballet.

Vi was also a member of the Lenya Hobnoobs Theatre Company (founded 1987), the radical gay theatre co-operative that made recordings on the Xntrix label.
