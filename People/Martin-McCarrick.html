Martin McCarrick

Role: Cellist, multi-instrumentalist
Discogs ID: 243573

Cellist who performed on This Mortal Coil’s It’ll End In Tears (4AD, CAD411, 1984) — credited on “Kangaroo”. Also a member of Siouxsie and the Banshees and The Creatures.

Core member of Marc And The Mambas and Marc Almond’s Willing Sinners touring/recording band throughout the mid-1980s. Credited on cello, accordion, dulcimer (yang t’chin), and keyboards across nearly all Marc Almond solo releases 1983–1989, including Torment And Toreros (1983), Stories Of Johnny (1985), The Stars We Are (1988), and Jacques (1989). Also credited on string arrangements.

Willing Sinners to Siouxsie and the Banshees transition (1986–1987)

The transition is sequential, not overlapping in any active recording sense.

Mother Fist and Her Five Daughters (Marc Almond with the Willing Sinners, released 6 April 1987) was recorded in Summer 1986 at Milo Studios and Abbey Road, London. McCarrick played cello, accordion, yang t’chin, keyboards, and handled cello arrangements — his last substantial Willing Sinners studio work. Wikipedia — Mother Fist and Her Five Daughters, as of 2026-07-18

He became a full-time Banshee in May 1987 according to his Wikipedia article — roughly a month after Mother Fist was released and several months after it was recorded. Wikipedia — Martin McCarrick, as of 2026-07-18

The new Banshees quintet (with McCarrick and guitarist Jon Klein) was publicly unveiled in July 1987 with the single “Song from the Edge of the World” (released 13 July 1987). The List of Siouxsie and the Banshees members Wikipedia article gives July 1987 as the lineup announcement date, consistent with the May 1987 join date in the McCarrick article (recruited May, publicly debuted July). Wikipedia — List of Siouxsie and the Banshees members, as of 2026-07-18

Conclusion: There was no genuine overlap. McCarrick finished recording Mother Fist in Summer 1986 before the Willing Sinners as a band were effectively done, joined the Banshees in May 1987 (after Mother Fist was released), and made his public Banshees debut in July 1987. He continued to appear on Marc Almond solo credits through 1989 (The Stars We Are, Jacques) as a session contributor, but the Willing Sinners band entity ended with Mother Fist.

Note: McCarrick’s Wikipedia article does not mention the Willing Sinners by name — it refers only to Marc and the Mambas and subsequent Marc Almond collaborations. The “May 1987” join date comes from that article; the “July 1987” lineup announcement comes from the Banshees personnel article. These are not a conflict — they describe different events (recruitment vs. public debut).
