Martin Belmont

Discogs ID: 327615 Born: 1948 Origin: UK

British guitarist (acoustic, electric, six-string bass, banjo). Founding member of Ducks Deluxe (1972–75) and subsequently The Rumour (Graham Parker’s backing band). Later member of Graham Parker & The Goldtops.

Overview

Belmont’s career began in the London pub-rock scene with Ducks Deluxe. After that band dissolved in 1975, he joined The Rumour as part of the band assembled around Graham Parker. He is a member of multiple Rumour-adjacent groups across his career.
