Bruce Tovsky

Engineer and producer. Discogs ID: 426265. Member of The Idiot Orchestra.

Overview

Long-term technical collaborator with the Liquid Liquid circle. Credited as Sound Advisor on the 1981 Liquid Liquid EP and Recording Engineer on Successive Reflexes — both on 99 Records. Later provided digital restoration for the 1997 Mo Wax Liquid Liquid reissue, and served as Reissue Producer on the 2008 Domino Slip In And Out Of Phenomenon compilation. His involvement spans the entire arc from the band’s original releases to their reunion.

Also credited as a live recording engineer (Ombud, Artiscope Music, Denmark, 2008) and for computer work on MCD (Target 15, Denmark, 2012).
