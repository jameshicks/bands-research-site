RM Hubbert

Glasgow-based guitarist and songwriter. Real name: Robert M Hubbert. Also known as “Hubby.” Discogs artist ID: 2514952.

Overview

RM Hubbert is a Glasgow guitarist primarily known for solo fingerstyle guitar records on Chemikal Underground (2010–2016) and collaborative albums with Aidan Moffat on Rock Action Records (2018–2019). Before his solo career, he played guitar (uncredited) in the noise/experimental group Thermoderm (1997) and was a member of El Hombre Trajeado, a Chemikal Underground-associated Glasgow group active into the 2000s. He is also credited as recording engineer on The Yummy Fur’s Piggy Wings (Rock Action, 2019) and contributed acoustic guitar to Remember Remember’s The Quickening (Rock Action, 2011).

Aliases in Discogs: “Hubby” (alias artist entry); name variations include “Hubbert,” “RM ‘Hubby’ Hubbert.”

Groups and Early Career

Thermoderm (1997)

Hubbert was a member of Thermoderm, a short-lived Glasgow experimental/noise group on Soul Static Sound. The sole release — the self-titled Thermoderm LP (1997) — credited him uncredited for guitar and engineering. Other members: Jason Boyce (drums, artwork), Martin Allen (keyboards), Stuart Henderson (bass). Full page: Thermoderm.

El Hombre Trajeado (late 1990s–2000s)

Hubbert was a member of El Hombre Trajeado (Discogs ID: 63565), a Glasgow group on Chemikal Underground whose catalog spans 1997–2016. Other members: Ben Jones (29) (Discogs ID: 4564760) and Stevie Jones (Discogs ID: 653708). Stevie Jones also appears as a recording engineer on Hubbert’s Thirteen Lost & Found (2012). El Hombre Trajeado released on Flotsam & Jetsam, Guided Missile, Lost Dog Recordings, and ultimately Chemikal Underground (Fast Diagonal, 2016).
