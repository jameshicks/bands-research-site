Craig Stewart (2)

Austin, Texas record label executive and musician. Co-founded Emperor Jones in 1995 and ran it through at least 2008; previously co-ran Trance Syndicate Records with his husband King Coffey. Member of Austin psych-rock band Rubble. Discogs artist ID: 757464. Passed away December 18, 2023, aged 53.

Career

Craig Stewart was an Austin-based independent music figure whose work ran across two complementary label operations and one band. The Discogs profile records him as a “record label executive” who “ran the label Trance Syndicate with his husband King Coffey and co-founded the imprint Emperor Jones in 1995.” He also worked for the Austin music conference SXSW at some point in his career.

Trance Syndicate Records (early 1990s–1995)

Before Emperor Jones, Stewart was involved in running Trance Syndicate Records (Discogs ID: 33260), the Austin noise-rock and psychedelic label founded by King Coffey. Trance Syndicate distributed through the Touch And Go network and released records by Sixteen Deluxe, Cherubs, Ed Hall, and others. Stewart’s sole documented production credit from this period is Sixteen Deluxe — Backfeed Magnetbabe (1995, Trance Syndicate Records, cat. TR-37CD), where he is credited as Producer. That album also features mastering by Jerry Tubb (and Kevin Whitley).

Emperor Jones (1995–c.2008)

Stewart co-founded Emperor Jones in 1995 (its first release, For Richer, For Poorer by My Dad Is Dead, is dated 1995). Coffey is described on Discogs as “said to have also been involved with the label,” suggesting Stewart was the primary day-to-day operator. Emperor Jones ran under the tagline “Chicken soup for your third eye” and focused on lo-fi, post-rock, and underground genres. Its 116-release catalog (through a sublabel, Emperor Jones Records) spans the full range of the US and international underground in that era. For a complete history of the label’s releases and roster, see Emperor Jones.

Stewart’s only formally credited role on an Emperor Jones release in the Discogs database is Alastair Galbraith — Cry (2000, EJ-32CD), where he is listed as Mastered By. His broader role as label operator was almost certainly not captured in individual release credits.

Mastering credit outside Emperor Jones

In 2005 Stewart appeared as Mastered By on M. Night Shyamalan’s (Furniture Records, 2005), suggesting he had some mastering capability or access to equipment independent of his label work.

Rubble

Stewart was a member of Rubble, a psych-rock band from Austin, Texas (Discogs artist ID: 320413). Rubble’s only Discogs-documented release is a self-titled CDr (2005, limited edition, Not On Label, 8 tracks), with no individual member credits listed on the release. Other members included Shawn McMillen, Ralph E. White (Bad Livers fiddler), Jeffrey Coffey, Bobby Baker, and Matt Turner.
