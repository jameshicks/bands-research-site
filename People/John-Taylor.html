John Taylor

Discogs ID: 90540
Real name: Nigel John Taylor
Origin: Birmingham, England
Born: 1960

Bassist and co-founder of Duran Duran. Solo LP Feelings Are Good And Other Lies (1997, Revolver Records 3, REV XD 215) featured Rick Boston as performer alongside Roy Hay (Culture Club), Steve Jones (Sex Pistols), and John Shanks. Produced by Hein Hoven.
