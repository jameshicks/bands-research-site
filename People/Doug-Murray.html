Doug Murray

Musician from Austin, Texas. Discogs artist ID: 447983. Twin brother of Greg Murray (2).

Overview

Doug Murray played guitar (and piano) in early Austin punk bands. He and his twin brother Greg were both in Terminal Mind and then The Skunks, where Doug played guitar and piano in the later lineup (1980–1982). On the 1982 Skunks LP he is the primary songwriter alongside Greg and Jesse Sublett.
