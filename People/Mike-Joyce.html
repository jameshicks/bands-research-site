Mike Joyce

Discogs ID: 338774
Real name: Michael Adrian Paul Joyce
Born: June 1, 1963, Manchester, England
Role: Drummer
Groups: The Smiths, Buzzcocks (briefly), Victim (4)

English drummer. Played drums for The Smiths (1982–1987) across all four studio albums. Had brief stints in early Manchester punk/post-punk outfits including Buzzcocks and Victim before The Smiths. Won a 1996 court case against Morrissey and Johnny Marr for unpaid live performance royalties. Later worked as a DJ for XS Manchester radio.

Post-Smiths Activity (1988 onward)

Morrissey solo sessions (1989)

Immediately after The Smiths split, Joyce (alongside Andy Rourke and Craig Gannon) played on early Morrissey solo singles produced by Stephen Street:

  • “Interesting Drug” (1989, Sire/Reprise) — Drums, with Rourke on bass, Gannon and Neil Taylor on guitar, and Kirsty MacColl on vocals
  • “The Last Of The Famous International Playboys” (1989, Sire/Reprise) — Drums
  • The Value Of Ignorance (1989, PolyGram Music Video) — appears in Morrissey video release

Buzzcocks (1991)

Joyce appeared as performer on the Buzzcocks Alive Tonight E.P. (1991, Planet Pacific) alongside Pete Shelley and Steve Diggle — confirming a live/session role with the band around that period.

Julian Cope — Peggy Suicide (1991)

Played drums on three tracks (tracks 5, 8, 18) of Julian Cope’s double album Peggy Suicide (Island Records, 1991), one of several drummers on that sprawling record.

Victim (4) — Empty Men (2014)

Drummed on one track (B2) of a single by Victim (4) (Spit Records), his pre-Smiths Manchester punk band — a reunion session credit.

Vinny Peculiar — The Fall And Rise Of Vinny Peculiar (2006)

Played drums and percussion on the full album by Vinny Peculiar (OnSong). The album also featured former Smiths guitarist Craig Gannon.

Intastella / Jah Wobble — The Rise And Fall Of A Northern Dubstar (2017)

Credited for Vocals / Spoken Word on this release by Intastella featuring Jah Wobble (Northern Quarter label). Other contributors included Nick McCabe (The Verve), John Robb, and John Cooper Clarke.

A Certain Ratio — Loco Live At Hope Mill Studios (2022)

Voice credit (spoken contribution on track B4) on this live album by Manchester post-punk act A Certain Ratio (Mute Records).

The Mighty Wah! — Songs Of Strength & Heartbreak (2025)

Played drums on the Wah- album Songs Of Strength & Heartbreak (Chrysalis Catalogue, 2025), led by Pete Wylie.

Smiths reissues and compilations

Joyce has continued to receive credits on ongoing Smiths reissue and compilation releases across WEA/Warner/Rhino through the 1990s–2020s, including the Singles Box (2008), The Queen Is Dead deluxe reissue (2017), and various Strange Fruit/BBC sessions releases.
