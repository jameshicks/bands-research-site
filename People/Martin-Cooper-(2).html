Overview

Martin Cooper (born Martin Handsley Cooper, 1 October 1958, Liverpool) is an English musician and painter best known as a multi-instrumentalist member of Orchestral Manoeuvres in the Dark (OMD), contributing saxophone, keyboards, and vocals across more than 560 credited works stretching from 1980 to the present. His closest working relationships are with OMD’s core duo of Paul Humphreys and Andy McCluskey, alongside long-standing bandmates Malcolm Holmes and Brian Tench. Outside OMD he is also a member of The Listening Pool. His catalogue maps directly onto OMD’s shifting label home — Dindisc and Epic in the early 1980s, A&M through the mid-to-late decade, and Virgin across multiple eras up to 2025.
