Martin Perna

Discogs ID: 189109
Real name: Martín Perna
Origin: Brooklyn, NY
Aliases: Jack Zapata; Martin Antibalas; Martin Ogbede

Multi-instrumentalist — primarily baritone saxophone, flute, tenor saxophone, bass clarinet, plus percussion, guitar, bass, organ, and vocals. Career began in 1996 at Desco Records, recording and performing with The Soul Providers (who later became the Sharon Jones & The Dap-Kings). Founded Antibalas (inspired by his work with The Daktaris) and Ocote Soul Sounds. Official member of TV On The Radio. Extensive session work across jazz, funk, rock, hip-hop, and Latin music.

Career

Desco Records and The Soul Providers (1996–c.2000)

Entered the Brooklyn soul/funk scene via Desco Records, the independent label that incubated the Dap-Kings orbit. Credited (uncredited on label) on Let’s Get A Groove On (1998, Desco Records) playing baritone saxophone with The Soul Providers.

The Daktaris (late 1990s)

Work with The Daktaris (Discogs ID 62308) — a Desco/Dap-Kings-adjacent Afrobeat project — directly inspired Perna to found Antibalas. Full discography, both on Desco Records:
