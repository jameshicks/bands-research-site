Clive Solomon

UK music industry executive and label co-founder. Real name: Clive Trevor Solomon. Discogs artist ID: 1909264.

Co-founded Fire Records with Johnny Waller in 1984–1985, and remained an owner and senior figure at the label through to approximately 2002, when James Nicholls took over day-to-day operations. As of the Discogs profile for Fire Records Of London Ltd., Solomon holds a 90% stake in the corporate entity. His Discogs credits show a role across the label’s full lifespan — from early production and liner notes through to executive production and compilation research on reissue campaigns in the 2010s and 2020s.

Overview

Clive Solomon’s earliest traceable Discogs credits predate Fire Records: in 1982 he contributed sleeve notes to Mood Six’s single “Hanging Around” (on EMI) and served as executive producer on Jed Dmochowski’s “Sha La La” (on Whaam! Records). These suggest he was already operating in a label/A&R capacity in the UK independent sector before Fire Records was established.

Fire Records was founded in 1984–1985 (Wikipedia dates to 1985) and released its first catalog items — 1000 Mexicans’ “Diving For Pearls” (FIRE 1, 1984) and The Blue Aeroplanes’ “Action Painting” (FIRE 2, 1985) — within its opening year. Solomon co-ran the label with Waller throughout the late 1980s and early 1990s, a period that saw Fire sign Pulp, Spacemen 3, Television Personalities, The Blue Aeroplanes, Close Lobsters, The Parachute Men, and The Perfect Disaster, among others.

Johnny Waller died in October 1996 in a bicycle accident in London, leaving Solomon to continue the label. In the early-to-mid 1990s, Fire’s roster expanded to include US indie acts such as Neutral Milk Hotel, Built To Spill, Gigolo Aunts, and Everclear. By the late 1990s, James Nicholls was increasingly involved; he took over day-to-day operations around 2002. The Fire Records Of London Ltd. corporate record lists the company as jointly owned by Solomon and Gerald Solomon until 30 November 2020, at which point sole majority ownership passed to Clive Solomon alone.

Solomon’s creative involvement continued into the 2010s: he co-compiled (with Dave Henderson) “The First Rock And Roll Record” (Famous Flames, 2015), and served as executive producer and co-researcher on Fire’s three-volume Television Personalities singles retrospective Some Kind Of… (2019–2020).
