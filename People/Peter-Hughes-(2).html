Peter Hughes (2)

Bassist and multi-instrumentalist; long-running member of The Mountain Goats (2002–2023). Also a core member of Nothing Painted Blue (with Franklin Bruno) and leader or co-leader of several Inland Empire / LA indie projects. Discogs artist ID: 367972.

Overview

Peter Hughes is primarily known as the bassist who anchored The Mountain Goats from their shift into a full-band lineup on Tallahassee (2002) through Jenny From Thebes (2023) — a run of over twenty years and more than fifteen studio albums. His role expanded well beyond bass across those records: he is credited playing electric and upright bass, guitar, harmony vocals, keyboards, vibraphone, percussion, shaker, and drums depending on the album.

Before the Mountain Goats, Hughes was a fixture of the Southern California cassette underground, centered on the Shrimper and Scat Records scenes of the early-to-mid 1990s. He played bass and sang in Nothing Painted Blue, Franklin Bruno’s power-pop band, appearing on their key Scat Records LPs; he fronted the solo cassette project Party Of One (Discogs: 113874); co-led the indie-rock band Diskothi-Q (from Upland, CA) with Kevin Hughes and Kevin Trapp; and recorded with Furniture One (with Joel Huschle) for Shrimper. He is also listed as a member of The Cicadas (3), a free/avant project with trombonist Jeb Bishop and Wendy Allen that released El Pulpo (2017, Arbouse Recordings) alongside John Davis (2).

His name variations on Discogs include “Hardcore Hughes,” “P. Hughes,” “Peter H.,” and “Peter Peter Hughes.”

Projects

The Mountain Goats

Hughes joined the Mountain Goats as bassist for Tallahassee (2002, 4AD), the album that marked John Darnielle’s move from home-recording to full studio production. From that point he appeared on every studio album through Jenny From Thebes (2023, Merge), though his specific credited instruments varied:

The Goths credit “Words By” is notable — one of the few instances where Hughes receives a writing credit on a Mountain Goats record.

The Mountain Goats Discogs page (artist ID: 95417) notes Hughes’s departure from the lineup in 2024.

Nothing Painted Blue

Nothing Painted Blue is Franklin Bruno’s long-running project, originally from Los Angeles (formed 1987). Hughes was a regular member for their key Scat Records LPs of the 1990s, playing bass with backing vocals alongside drummer Kyle Brodie and Bruno on vocals/guitar/organ.

Placeholders (1994) was the band’s debut CD on Scat; The Monte Carlo Method (1998) — named for the mathematical modeling concept, reflecting Bruno’s background in mathematics — was their final major release.

Hughes’s connection to Bruno also extended to the The Extra Lens / The Extra Glenns project: he contributed sleeve artwork to Martial Arts Weekend (2002, Absolutely Kosher), the Extra Glenns’ only full-length, credited as “P. Hughes.” He later appeared as electric bassist on several tracks of Another (2012, Shrimper), a 12” release credited to The Human Hearts (Discogs: 1933779), Franklin Bruno’s project on Shrimper, alongside vocalists Jenny Toomey and Laura Cantrell.

Diskothi-Q

Diskothi-Q was an indie rock band from Upland, CA (Discogs: 352777), comprising Peter Hughes, Kevin Hughes, and Kevin Trapp. They were part of the Shrimper / Sonic Enemy cassette ecosystem.

ReleaseYearLabelFormat
Delicious, Homemade1992Sonic EnemyCassette
The Wandering Jew1994Shrimper (SHR 55)LP
Lane Discipline1995X-Mas Records (X-MAS 009)7”

The Wandering Jew (1994, Shrimper) is the band’s primary vinyl document — an 11-track LP recorded by Bob Durkee. Notable: track A2 (“Tulsa Imperative”) is credited as Written-By to The Mountain Goats, an early Darnielle/Hughes crossover in the credits. The Lane Discipline 7” includes covers: “Heartland Feeling” (written by Beck) and “Javelin” (written by Simon Joyner).

Diskothi-Q also appeared on The Wandering Jew split comp, and Hughes appeared on the Shrimper compilation Fantasy Band (1994) credited as Party Of One (uncredited).

Party Of One

Party Of One (Discogs: 113874, realname: Peter Hughes) is Peter Hughes’s solo project. Its only formal Discogs release is a split cassette.

ReleaseYearLabelNotes
Pick Yr Monologue (split cassette w/ Delly Daktel)1992Sonic EnemySide A: Party Of One (8 tracks); Side B: Delly Daktel (10 tracks)

Tracks include “Going To Pomona” — a “Going To…” title that predates Hughes joining the Mountain Goats and echoes Darnielle’s longstanding song-naming convention (or may reflect shared scene vernacular). Hughes also appeared on the Shrimper comp Fantasy Band (1994) in a Party Of One capacity, uncredited.

The Cicadas (3)

The Cicadas (3) (Discogs: 7324646) is a project comprising Peter Hughes, trombonist Jeb Bishop, and Wendy Allen.

ReleaseYearLabelNotes
El Pulpo (with John Davis (2))2017Arbouse Recordings (arbou048)CD

El Pulpo is credited jointly to John Davis (2) (of The Folk Implosion / Deluxx Folk Implosion) and The Cicadas (3). Produced by John Davis (2) and Scott Solter, mastered by Matt Pence. The pairing of Bishop (a Chicago jazz/improv trombonist with extensive free jazz credits including the Vandermark 5, Peter Brötzmann Chicago Tentet, and Exploding Star Orchestra) with Hughes and the Folk Implosion’s Davis suggests a cross-genre meeting point.
