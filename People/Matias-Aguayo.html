Matias Aguayo

Born 1973 in Santiago de Chile; based in Cologne/Berlin. DJ, producer, vocalist, and label founder. Discogs artist ID: 208373. Real name: Matías Aguayo.

Chilean-German figure in European electronic music, active since the late 1990s. Co-founded Closer Musik with Dirk Leyers, released through Kompakt for a decade, then launched Cómeme in 2009 as his own Berlin-based imprint. Known for a voice-led, rhythmically playful approach to techno and minimal that draws on Latin percussion, folklore, and a distinctive theatrical vocal style. Also operates as alias The Don (8).

Background

Aguayo grew up in Chile and relocated to Germany, eventually settling in Cologne — the hub of the Kompakt network. In the late 1990s he contributed to Closer Musik alongside Dirk Leyers, a duo whose spacious, melodic minimal techno landed on Kompakt in 2000–2002. He began releasing under his own name on Kompakt in 2005 with Are You Really Lost, produced together with Marcus Rossknecht (Roccness).

His profile widened during the Kompakt era but the decisive move came in 2009 when he founded Cómeme out of Berlin (address: Kurfürstenstraße 27, 10785 Berlin). Distributed initially through Kompakt and later Above Board Distribution, the label became a home for a Latin American and European underground crossing techno, cumbia, and tropical dance forms.

He has also performed as part of Matias Aguayo & The Desdemonas, a live formation with Gregorio Gómez, Henning Specht, and Matteo Scrimali.

Sound and Style

Aguayo’s production sits in minimal techno but tilts consistently toward the voice — his own falsetto/baritone phrasing and spoken-word style runs through most solo records. Where contemporaries kept vocals sparse or processed, Aguayo made them a structural element: chants, interjections, cumbia-inflected rhythmic patter. His 2009 LP Ay Ay Ay and 2013 LP The Visitor both foreground this; Rollerskate (2009) is a lighter pop-adjacent take on the same sound.

The Cómeme label crystallised a scene mixing Berlin residency culture with South American (especially Chilean and Argentine) producers: Rebolledo, DJs Pareja, Daniel Maloso, Ana Helder, Diegors, Philipp Gorbachev. Later years brought the South African footwork producer DJ Spoko into the orbit for the Dirty Dancing split (2017).

Label Work: Cómeme

Discogs label ID: 149918. Founded Berlin, 2009. Distributed by Kompakt initially, then Above Board Distribution.

Aguayo is credited as founder and director. The label launched with a South American catalogue number series and built a roster mixing Chilean, Argentine, Brazilian, German, and Eastern European producers.
