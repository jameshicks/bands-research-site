Jim White (2)

Discogs ID: 350560
Born: 1962, Melbourne, Victoria, Australia
Instruments: Drums, percussion

Australian drummer, based in Chicago since the late 1990s. Core member of Dirty Three (1992–present). Prolific session player. Past/concurrent groups: Venom P. Stinger, Blackeyed Susans, Crime & The City Solution, Boxhead Ensemble, Tren Brothers, Xylouris White (with Giorgos Xylouris), The Hard Quartet. Session work with Will Oldham, Cat Power, Nina Nastasia, Smog, White Magic.

Tren Brothers

With Dirty Three guitarist Mick Turner, forms Tren Brothers (1998–2009) — instrumental duo. Plays drums and percussion on all six Tren Brothers releases; co-writes all material with Turner. On Blue Trees (2007), White is credited on tracks 1–6 only (the Tren Brothers half); the remaining eight tracks are Turner solo. The 2009 split 7” “Sometimes” marks the group’s last release. See Tren Brothers for full discography.

Key Credits with Blackeyed Susans

All Souls Alive (1993, Torn & Frayed TORN CD5) — drums on 7 of 10 tracks. Reveal Yourself (2009, Liberation Music retrospective) — drums on the All Souls Alive era disc (CD1-8 to CD1-13). Discogs: 3 shared releases total (422723).

Xylouris White

Forms Xylouris White with Cretan lute player George Xylouris. All five studio albums produced by Guy Picciotto: Goats (2014, Other Music Recording Co., OM-015-1), Black Peak (2016, Bella Union, BELLA570V), Mother (2018, Bella Union), The Sisypheans (2019, Drag City, DC758), The Forest In Me (2023, Remote Control, RCON165LP). See Xylouris White for full article.

Crime & The City Solution

Jim White is an official member of Crime & The City Solution (Discogs ID 36663). Earliest documented credit: “You’ve All Got A Home To Go To” (1985, Major Records, Australia) — drums. Also “Hey Believer” (1994, Glitterhouse Records, Germany). The 2023 album The Killer (Mute) shows White co-writing music on two tracks (A2, A4) alongside Simon Bonney and Matthew Smith. Most recent active credit on the band’s newest LP to date.

PJ Harvey

Three albums credited: The Letting Go (2006, Domino, drums + electric piano), White Chalk (2007, Island Records, drums + percussion — musician credit), and Jukebox (2008, Matador, drums). This is one of his most prominent session relationships beyond Australian circles. PJ Harvey

Nick Cave & Warren Ellis

Drums on The Proposition (Original Soundtrack, 2005, Mute) — the score for John Hillcoat’s Australian film. Full Bad Seeds/Ellis rhythm section credit alongside Martyn Casey on bass and Warren Ellis on violin. Nick Cave
