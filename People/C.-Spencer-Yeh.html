Overview

C. Spencer Yeh (real name Chih-Fu Spencer Yeh) is an experimental musician originally from Cincinnati, Ohio, now based in New York City, working primarily with violin, electronics, and voice. He is a core participant in Burning Star Core and has contributed to several collaborative ensembles including Ben Hall’s She Shells String Trio and the CKY Trio. His 254 Discogs credits are concentrated in the 2000s and 2010s, spanning noise, free improvisation, drone, and abstract music, with releases on What The…? Records, Important Records, Hospital Productions, and Helicopter among others. His most frequent collaborators include Ben Hall, Robert Beatty, and Don Dietrich.
