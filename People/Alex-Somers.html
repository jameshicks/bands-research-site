Alex Somers

Discogs ID: 1369297 Real name: Alex Kendall Somers Origin: USA

American artist, composer, and record producer. Jónsi’s partner. Co-produced and mixed Sigur Rós’s Valtari (2012) and Kveikur (2013). Also mixed and engineered on the Heima documentary release.

Collaborative projects:

  • Riceboy Sleeps (Discogs: 1369298) — ambient project with Jón Þór Birgisson; Riceboy Sleeps LP (XL Recordings, 2009)
  • Jónsi & Alex (Discogs: 1503138) — alternate alias for the same duo
  • Liminal (10) (Discogs: 6475374) — project with Jónsi and Paul Corley; Liminal Remixes (Krúnk, 2017)
  • Parachutes (Discogs: 1081730) — separate project

Also composed the Black Mirror: Hang the DJ soundtrack (2017, self-released) and co-created Liminal Remixes (2017).

Connections

  • Sigur Rós — co-producer/mixer from 2012
  • Jón Þór Birgisson — partner; Riceboy Sleeps / Jónsi & Alex
  • Valgeir Sigurðsson — fellow Icelandic scene producer
  • Julianna Barwick — produced, mixed, and engineered Nepenthe (2013, Dead Oceans); sessions in Iceland with Amiina and a teen choir
