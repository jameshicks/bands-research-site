Chris Carter (2)

Discogs ID: 83832

UK electronic musician; member of Throbbing Gristle and Chris & Cosey / Carter Tutti. Credited for “Post Production Processing” on Lustmord’s Heresy (SOL-9, 1990, Soleilmoon Recordings); also credited for CD transfer and processing on A Document Of Early Acoustic & Tactical Experimentation (1991, Dark Vinyl Records).
