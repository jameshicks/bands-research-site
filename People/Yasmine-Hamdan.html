Yasmine Hamdan

Born: 1976, Beirut, Lebanon Based: Paris, France Discogs ID: 493606 Name variations: Y. Hamdan, ياسمين حمدان Labels: Crammed Discs, Kwaidan Records, Hamdanistan Records (own imprint), AZ Groups: Soap Kills, Yas (5)

Overview

Yasmine Hamdan is a Lebanese vocalist and songwriter based in Paris. She first came to prominence as co-founder of Soap Kills, a Lebanese indie electro-pop duo formed in Beirut in October 1997 with producer Zeid Hamdan. Soap Kills were among the earliest electronic/alternative acts to emerge from Lebanon, releasing self-produced cassettes and CDs in the late 1990s and early 2000s before disbanding in 2005. After the group dissolved, Hamdan moved to Paris and worked on a French techno-pop project, Yas (5), produced by Mirwais Ahmadzaï (known for his production on Madonna’s Music and American Life albums), releasing three singles on AZ Records (2008–2009).

Her solo career proper began with an eponymous debut EP on Kwaidan Records in 2012, followed by her first full solo album Ya Nass (2013, Crammed Discs), produced by Marc Collin (founder of Nouvelle Vague). The record drew critical attention as a sophisticated Arabic-language art-pop statement, pairing Hamdan’s voice with electronic arrangements rooted in Lebanese and pan-Arab song traditions. A second album, Al Jamílat (2017), was released on Hostess Entertainment in Japan and other markets through Crammed Discs; a reworking, Jamilat Reprise (2018, Crammed Discs / Hamdanistan Records), added new contributions from producers Leo Abrahams and Luke Smith (3), with poetry by Palestinian poet Mahmoud Darwish (محمود درويش) as lyrics. A third album, I Remember I Forget (2025, Crammed Discs), continues the partnership with the label.

In the film world, Hamdan is best known for performing the song “Hal” in the 2013 Jim Jarmusch vampire film Only Lovers Left Alive — a track credited solely to her on the soundtrack LP (ATP Recordings, 2014). She also appears as a vocalist on one track of lutenist Jozef Van Wissem’s It Is Time For You To Return (2014, Crammed Discs), a release that also features Jim Jarmusch on guitar.

Soap Kills (1997–2005)

Soap Kills was co-founded by Yasmine Hamdan and Zeid Hamdan (also a Lebanese producer and multi-instrumentalist, with own projects including Zeid And The Wings and Bedouin Burger). The duo operated entirely outside Lebanese mainstream industry channels, self-releasing on cassette and CD. Their profile:

  • Formed: October 1997, Beirut
  • Disbanded: 2005
  • Style: Indie electro-pop, ambient, alternative
