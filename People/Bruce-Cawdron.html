Bruce Cawdron

Discogs ID: 422824
Also credited as: Trois Pistoles (alias)
Based: Montreal, Quebec, Canada
Instruments: Drums, marimba, vibraphone, glockenspiel, percussion

Percussionist and member of Godspeed You Black Emperor! from approximately 1997 onward. Also a founding member of Esmerine. Present across GY!BE recordings from F♯ A♯ ∞ through the post-reunion era; credited alongside Aidan Girt as a second drummer. Also credited on Exhaust releases and member of Set Fire To Flames.
