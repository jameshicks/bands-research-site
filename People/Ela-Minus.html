Ela Minus

Discogs ID: 4802432
Real name: Gabriela Jimeno Caldas
Born: Bogotá, Colombia, February 11, 1990. Based in Brooklyn, NY.
Also credited as: Gabriela Jimeno; Ella Minus (name variation)

Colombian electronic musician, vocalist, producer, and engineer. Works primarily in solo electronic production — techno-adjacent with pop structures. All records self-produced and self-engineered. Primary label home is Domino, with the collaborative ♡ on Domino sublabel Smugglers Way.
