Overview

Alfa Anderson (1946–2024) was an American singer and educator from Augusta, Georgia, best known as a lead vocalist of Chic. Her work alongside Nile Rodgers, Bernard Edwards, and fellow vocalist Luci Martin placed her at the center of the group’s late-1970s disco and funk output on Atlantic Records, and her 700-plus credits across a career beginning in 1974 reflect deep ties to the core Chic circle—Tony Thompson, Fonzi Thornton, and Bob Clearmountain among them. Beyond Chic, she lent her voice to the Luv You Madly Orchestra, Next Step (4), and Voices of Shalom.
