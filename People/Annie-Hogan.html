Real name: Ann Margaret Hogan
Discogs ID: 220007
Also credited as: Ann Hogan, Anni Hogan, Anne Hogan, A. Hogan, Anne (Hunk) Hogan
Born: 1961, Birkenhead (Liverpool), UK

Overview

Annie Hogan is an English composer, pianist, keyboardist, performer, and DJ. She grew up in Birkenhead, Merseyside, and became one of the most consistent creative presences in the British post-punk / dark cabaret world of the 1980s. She is best known as the anchor instrumentalist of Marc Almond’s successive bands — Marc And The Mambas, The Willing Sinners, and La Magia — spanning 1982 to approximately 1990, playing piano, harpsichord, vibraphone, marimba, organ, and synthesizer across essentially every significant Marc Almond record of that decade. On The Stars We Are (1988) she graduated to full co-producer and arranger alongside Almond and Billy McGee; she also holds co-writing credits on tracks from that album. Outside the Almond orbit she worked with Nick Cave, Lydia Lunch, Barry Adamson, Foetus (JG Thirlwell), Budgie, Zeke Manyika, and Jarboe’s AEAEA collective. Her solo catalog spans from the 1984 Doublevision EP to the 2019 Cold Spring LP Lost In Blue. More recently she has collaborated extensively with Scanner (Robin Rimbaud) and with Martin Bowes of Attrition, and co-produced with Dave Ball (Soft Cell).
