Trey Pollard

Discogs ID: 2894782 ANV: Trey Based: Richmond, VA Website: treypollard.com

Arranger, conductor, co-producer, engineer, guitarist, and pedal steel player based in Richmond, VA. One of the central creative figures at Spacebomb Records, working across virtually its entire catalog from the label’s founding releases onward. Best known outside the Spacebomb circle as the orchestral arranger behind several notable indie records of the 2010s–2020s, his string and wind arrangements are a defining sound of the Spacebomb aesthetic.

Biography

Pollard is a Richmond-based multi-instrumentalist and arranger whose professional identity is inseparable from Spacebomb Records, the studio/label founded by Matthew E. White. He appears on the label’s first significant release — White’s Big Inner (2012) — as strings arranger and conductor, and has remained one of the operation’s most consistent contributors ever since.

His skill set is unusually broad for a single collaborator: he arranges and conducts orchestral strings and winds, engineers and mixes records at Spacebomb Studios, co-produces albums, and plays pedal steel guitar and guitar as a session player. On Natalie Prass’s debut (2015) he wore all of these hats simultaneously — co-producer, engineer/mixer, strings arranger/conductor, horns arranger (on one track), and pedal steel player.

The Spacebomb house band and string section orbit closely around him. Recurring string players across his arrangements include Amber Blankenship (cello), Treesa Gold (violin/contractor), Anna Bishop (violin), Ellen Cockerham-Riccio (violin), Schuyler Slack (cello), Stephanie Barrett (cello), Kim Ryan (viola), Molly Sharp (viola), and Adrian Pintea (violin). Engineer Adrian Olsen is the other half of the Spacebomb studio team alongside him on many sessions.

Beyond Spacebomb, Pollard has been engaged as a string arranger by artists on Jagjaguwar (Foxygen, Hang, 2017), Secretly Canadian (Faye Webster, I Know I’m Funny Haha, 2021; Car Therapy Sessions, 2022), Dead Oceans (More Photographs, 2023), Matador (Valentine, 2021), 4AD/Helado Negro (Far In, 2021), Domino (Shintaro Sakamoto, K Bay, 2021), ATO Records, BMG, Heavenly, and New West Records. This positions him as one of the more in-demand string arrangers working in the American indie sphere across this period.

His only primary-credited release on Discogs is Antiphone (2018, Spacebomb Records) — a composed orchestral work produced by Matthew E. White.

The Spacebomb Strings Pool
