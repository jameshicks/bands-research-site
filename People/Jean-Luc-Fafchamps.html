Overview

Jean-Luc Fafchamps (born 1960, Brussels) is a Belgian composer and pianist with 66 Discogs credits spanning contemporary classical, experimental, and modern styles. His career stretches from 1984 through the 2020s, with the most concentrated activity in the 1990s and 2010s; Sub Rosa is his primary label home, with six works across 1990–2024. He is a member of three ensembles — Babel Ensemble, Ictus, and Le Bureau Des Pianistes — and his most frequent credited collaborator is Morton Feldman, sharing seven releases, alongside Ictus colleagues Jean-Luc Plouvier and Georges-Elie Octors.
