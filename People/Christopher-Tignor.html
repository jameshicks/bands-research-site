Overview

Christopher Tignor is a New York-based violinist and composer working across experimental, modern classical, and post-rock. He has been a member of three bands — Disassembler, Slow Six, and Wires Under Tension — and his solo catalogue spans from 2004 through the 2010s, concentrated on Western Vinyl, the label that has anchored his releases since 2007.
