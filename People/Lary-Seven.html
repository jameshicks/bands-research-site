Lary Seven

Real name: Lary Gawoski. Born in Buffalo, NY. Multi-instrumentalist, recording engineer, and multimedia artist. Discogs artist ID: 226532.

Also credited as: Larry Seven, Larry 7, Lary 7, 7, Seven. Alias: Larry Lamé (Discogs artist ID: 891048 — used notably on Swans photography credits).

A former student of avant-garde artist Tony Conrad, Lary Seven has been a fixture in the New York experimental underground since the late 1970s. He co-founded Plastikville Records and is the studio operator behind Plastikville Studios. He works predominantly with vintage and analogue equipment, including modified household appliances, and steers away from digital tools. His credits span musician, engineer, producer, and photographer across the Swans ecosystem, Jarboe’s solo work, and a wide range of New York experimental projects.
