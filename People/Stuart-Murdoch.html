Stuart Murdoch

Scottish songwriter, vocalist, and multi-instrumentalist. Discogs artist ID: 325969. Real name: Stuart Lee Murdoch.

Overview

Founder and primary songwriter of Belle & Sebastian. Also conceived and led God Help The Girl, a film and recording project (2009 album on Matador; 2014 film). His Discogs contributor credits show photography, liner notes, and piano credits across the Belle & Sebastian catalog, plus production work on the God Help The Girl soundtrack and related releases. Credited on Animal Magic’s Biggest Bluest Hi-Fi (2001) as string arranger.
