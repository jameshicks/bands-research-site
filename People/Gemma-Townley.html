Overview

Gemma Townley (later Gemma Malley) is a British multi-instrumentalist — cello, vocals, keyboards, and bass — and songwriter best known as a member of Trembling Blue Stars, the indie pop project led by Bobby Wratten. Her recording activity is concentrated in the 1990s, anchored by four releases on Sarah Records between 1992 and 1995, with her closest repeat collaborators being Blueboy and Paul Stewart alongside scene regulars Keith Girdler, Mark Cousens, and Harvey Williams. Work slowed markedly after the 1990s — a single credit in the 2000s — before a modest return in the 2020s through Precious and Aquavinyle Records. Her range of roles across cello, keyboards, and bass reflects the hands-on, low-budget aesthetic of the British indie pop underground she was part of.
