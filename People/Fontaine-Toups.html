Overview

Fontaine Toups is a vocalist, bassist, and guitarist active in indie rock since 1992, most closely associated with Air Miami — a project she shared with Teenbeat label founder Mark Robinson — and with the extended Versus circle, logging 45 shared credits alongside Richard Baluyut and 34 alongside Ed Baluyut. Her credits span Containe, Flower, Saturnine, and a self-named project, The Fontaine Toups, across labels including Teenbeat, Caroline Records, and Merge Records. With 53 Discogs credits from 1992 through at least 2026, her work sits squarely in the 1990s Washington D.C.–adjacent indie and post-hardcore underground.
