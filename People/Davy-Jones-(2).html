David, known as Davy by most of his friends, was born in 1954 in Chester, PA, and lived there until age 13. His musical interest started young when he began music lessons. He later found his true love in playing electric guitar, and was an accomplished guitarist by the time he was in high school. Via a stint in the army, he eventually he ended up in Austin Texas in the late 1970s, where he lived for 35 years. In Austin, Texas, Jones played guitar in punk/New Wave groups The Next and Ideals, and was a founding member and long-term guitarist in cowpunk band The Hickoids, rejoining the group when they reunited in 2006. Other bands he played with included Dicks, Big Foot Chester, Gay Sportscasters, Mannish Ideals, and Diamond Smugglers. Jones passed away from lung cancer on November 25, 2015.

Overview

David Lawrence Jones (1954–2015), known as Davy, grew up in Chester, Pennsylvania and arrived in Austin, Texas in the late 1970s after a stint in the army, where he would remain for some 35 years. He played guitar in Austin punk and New Wave outfits including the Next and Ideals before co-founding cowpunk band the Hickoids, the group most associated with his name; he rejoined them when they reunited in 2006 and continued working with them until near the end of his life. His credits stretch from 1980 into the 2010s across labels including Toxic Shock, Saustex Media, and Ghost Highway Recordings, with Jeff Smith (3), Rice Moorehead, and Walter Daniels among his most frequent collaborators. Jones died of lung cancer on November 25, 2015.
