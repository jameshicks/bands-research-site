Omar Rodriguez-Lopez

Discogs ID: 274511
Real name: Omar Alfredo Rodríguez-López
Born: September 1, 1975, Bayamón, Puerto Rico (grew up El Paso, Texas)
Role: Guitarist, multi-instrumentalist, producer, filmmaker, label founder
Labels (as artist): Gold Standard Laboratories → Rodriguez Lopez Productions → Ipecac Recordings → Clouds Hill
Management: Raw Power Management (Los Angeles)

Co-founder of At The Drive-In and The Mars Volta; co-managed Gold Standard Laboratories (2001–2007); founded Rodriguez Lopez Productions (2007–2016). Elder brother of Marcel Rodriguez-Lopez and Marfred Rodriguez-Lopez. One of the most prolific underground guitarists of the 2000s, with ~90 credited releases spanning solo work, The Mars Volta, and numerous ensembles.

Biography

Origins: Puerto Rico, El Paso, Early Bands (1975–1993)

Born September 1, 1975 in Bayamón, Puerto Rico; family relocated to El Paso, Texas during childhood. Met Cedric Bixler-Zavala in the early 1990s through mutual friend Paul Hinojos. First documented band: Startled Calf (1992, El Paso). Joined At The Drive-In in 1994, initially on bass, switching to guitar by 1996.

At The Drive-In (1994–2001)

ATDI formed in El Paso in 1993; Omar joined 1994. Canonical lineup: Cedric (vocals), Omar (guitar), Jim Ward (guitar), Paul Hinojos (bass), Tony Hajjar (drums). ATDI released Acrobatic Tenement (1996, Fearless), In/Casino/Out (1998, Fearless), Vaya EP (1999, GSL), and Relationship Of Command (2000, Interscope/Grand Royal; produced by Ross Robinson).

In 1998, Omar co-founded De Facto (2) with Cedric and Jeremy Michael Ward — an experimental dub reggae trio performing improvised jams after ATDI shows. De Facto released Megaton Shotblast (GSL45, 2001) on Gold Standard Laboratories.

Band dissolved in 2001; Jim Ward, Paul Hinojos, and Tony Hajjar continued as Sparta.

The Mars Volta and GSL Co-Ownership (2001–2007)

After ATDI’s dissolution, Omar and Cedric co-founded The Mars Volta and co-managed Gold Standard Laboratories from 2001. Omar served as primary producer throughout the TMV discography. Key TMV releases:

  • Tremulant EP (GSL54, 2002) — debut; produced by Alex Newport
  • De-Loused In The Comatorium (GSL75, 2003) — produced by Rick Rubin; John Frusciante, Flea guests
  • Frances The Mute (GSL95, 2005) — Omar-produced; Jeremy Ward’s journal as lyrical source
  • Amputechture (GSL126, 2006) — final GSL release; Frusciante full credited member

GSL closed October 29, 2007.

Ipecac Recordings Flood (2016–2017)

In 2016–2017, Omar released 23 solo albums on Ipecac Recordings (co-founded by Mike Patton) under a dedicated catalog sub-series with the prefix IPC-ORL (IPC-ORL1 through IPC-ORL23). All 23 are digital-only releases (WAV and MP3 formats; no vinyl or CD). The series ran concurrently with the tail end of Rodriguez Lopez Productions and represents the largest single-label batch of solo releases in Omar’s discography.

The recurring production team across the documented albums overlaps with the RLP/TMV era: Chris Common (engineer, mix, master), Jon Debaun (engineer), Lars Stalfors and Marcel Rodriguez-Lopez (on Weekly Mansions, ORL9), and Deantoni Parks as co-producer on Sworn Virgins (ORL1). Birth of a Ghost (ORL21) is an outlier — a full orchestral production with a separate conductor (Christian Celaya), arranger, and string/wind ensemble, suggesting it was purpose-recorded rather than pulled from a backlog. Most of the other 13 albums carry no additional credits in Discogs, consistent with solo home recordings or minimally documented sessions. Whether the series constitutes primarily archival/backlog material or new recordings is not fully answerable from Discogs data alone.

Rodriguez Lopez Productions (2007–2016)

After GSL’s closure, Omar co-founded Rodriguez Lopez Productions with Sargent House (Cathy Pellow, Marc Jetton) in Los Angeles. The label ran 30+ catalog entries (RLPxxx sequence) and distributed two films. Art director: Sonny Kay. Sublabel: RLP Europe (ID 167095). Succeeded as Omar’s primary home by Ipecac Recordings (2014–2017) then Clouds Hill (2021–present).

Omar Rodriguez Lopez Group (2007–present)

Omar Rodriguez Lopez Group — experimental/psychedelic/prog-rock/fusion ensemble founded 2007, conceived at the back cover of the Omar/Damo Suzuki GSL 12”. Rotating cast with documented lineup eras:

  • 2007 formation: Juan Alderete de la Pena (bass), Marcel Rodriguez-Lopez (keys/perc), Thomas Pridgen (drums), Cedric Bixler-Zavala (vocals), Adrian Terrazas (reeds), Money Mark (keys)
  • 2009–2010 golden era: Deantoni Parks (drums), Mark Aanderud (keys), Lars Stalfors (sound manipulation), Ximena Sariñana (vocals)
  • Current (since 2018): Audrey Johnson (drums), Virginia Alves (vocals), Leo Genovese (piano)

Five official releases (2007–2024) on GSL, RLP, Sleepwell, and Clouds Hill.

Other Projects and Collaborations

  • De Facto (2) (1998–2002) — with Cedric Bixler-Zavala and Jeremy Michael Ward; experimental dub reggae; Megaton Shotblast (GSL45, 2001)
  • El Grupo Nuevo De Omar Rodriguez Lopez — ensemble project, distinct from ORLG
  • Bosnian Rainbows (2013) — with Nicci Kasper, Deantoni Parks, Juan Alderete de la Pena

  • Antemasque (2014–2015) — with Cedric Bixler-Zavala, Flea, and Travis Barker
  • Kimono Kult — Omar with Ximena Sariñana

Personal: Dated Mexican singer/actress Ximena Sariñana 2008–2011.

Filmmaking

Concurrent with music production, Omar developed a filmmaking practice. Films distributed through Rodriguez Lopez Productions. Specific titles not fully documented in Discogs.

Reunion and Recent Work (2022–present)

TMV reunited June 2022; The Mars Volta (Clouds Hill CH300, 2022) marked a pop-accessible new direction. Followed by Que Dios Te Maldiga Mi Corazon (Clouds Hill, 2023) and Lucro Sucio; Los Ojos Del Vacio (Clouds Hill, 2025). Omar continues releasing solo and ORLG work on Clouds Hill.
