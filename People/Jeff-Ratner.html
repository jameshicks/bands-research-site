Overview

Jeff Ratner is a bassist primarily known for his membership in the ambient/neo-classical ensemble Bing And Ruth and the folk-rock group Langhorne Slim & The Law. He plays bass, double bass, electric bass, and keyboards across styles spanning ambient, folk rock, and neo-classical, with credits running from 2006 through the 2020s. His most frequent collaborators — Jeremy Viner, Brian Bender, David Moore, and Mike Effenberger — are core figures in the Bing And Ruth circle, and his work has appeared on labels including 4AD and Rvng Intl.
