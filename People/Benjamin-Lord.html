Overview

Benjamin Wayne Lord is a drummer and multi-band member rooted in the Louisville emo and hardcore scene of the 1990s. He has played in Falling Forward, Elliott, 3 Inch Porn Stars, Family in Mourning, and Kilowatthours, with his closest collaborators being Christopher Higdon and Jonathan Mobley (16 shared releases each), suggesting sustained partnerships across several of those projects. His catalogue is heavily concentrated in the 1990s — six releases between 1994 and the early 2000s across Initial Records, Doghouse, Slamdek, and Revelation Records — with at least one later credit on Temporary Residence Limited as recently as 2023.
