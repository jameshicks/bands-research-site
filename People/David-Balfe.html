David Balfe

Discogs ID: 101351
Real name: David Balfe
Born: 1958, Liverpool
Aliases: A Chameleon, Milk (10), Dave Balfe
Groups: Dalek I Love You, Big In Japan, The Teardrop Explodes, Lori & The Chameleons, The Chameleons (2), Radio Blank…, The Turquoise Swimming Pools, Whopper, DBM (8)

Liverpool musician, producer, and label founder. Co-founder of Zoo Records with Bill Drummond; later founder of Food.

Early Career (1977–1978)

Balfe co-founded Dalek I (with Alan Gill) in November 1977. He left in 1978 to join Big In Japan, playing bass on their sole single From Y To Z And Never Again (Zoo Records CAGE 001, 1978).

Zoo Records and The Teardrop Explodes (1978–1983)

In 1978–79, Balfe co-founded Zoo Records with Drummond in Liverpool. As The Chameleons (2) (jointly with Drummond), he produced:

  • The Teardrop Explodes — Sleeping Gas (CAGE 003, 1979; Balfe also engineered)
  • Echo & The Bunnymen — The Pictures On My Wall (CAGE 004, 1979)
  • Lori & The Chameleons — Touch (SIR 4025, 1979; written by Drummond, Balfe, and Kev Ward)

Balfe also produced the Expelaires single To See You / Frequency (CAGE 007, 1979) under his own name.

In 1979 he replaced Paul Simpson as keyboardist in The Teardrop Explodes, playing on Kilimanjaro (Mercury, 1980), Treason (Zoo, 1980), and Wilder (Mercury, 1981). He contributed arrangements and organ/piano/synthesizer to the posthumous Teardrop collection Everybody Wants To Shag… The Teardrop Explodes (Fontana, 1990). He played keyboards on the Teardrop’s final Zoo-era archival releases through to Culture Bunker 1978–82 (Universal, 2023).

Food Records (1983–1994)

After The Teardrop Explodes disbanded in 1983, Balfe set up Food (Food Records, Discogs ID: 6124), which went on to sign Blur (co-producing their debut Leisure, 1991), Jesus Jones, and others. The label operated under Food Ltd. / Parlophone Records Ltd. EMI took complete control of Food in 1994. Andy Ross was his co-director at Food.

Later Work

Post-Food, Balfe moved into freelance writing and directing.
