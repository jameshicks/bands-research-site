Brad Hallen

US bassist. Discogs artist ID: 345060.

Overview

Bassist for Pastiche (2) in the 1980–1982 five-piece lineup. After Pastiche dissolved in 1982, went on to join Roomful of Blues, with whom he has recorded extensively from the 1980s through the 2020s. Also appears as a session and touring bassist across blues, rock, and roots records.
