Overview

Adam Peters is a cellist, keyboardist, pianist, and arranger/orchestrator whose 648 Discogs credits stretch back to 1984. He is a member of Echo & The Bunnymen and worked extensively with the band’s core circle — sharing credits with Will Sergeant, Les Pattinson, Ian McCulloch, and Pete De Freitas — alongside regular Bunnymen producer Gil Norton (149 shared credits). In parallel, Peters built an equally deep working relationship with The Triffids, with over 100 shared credits each with David McComb and the band collectively, as well as Jill Birt, Evil Graham Lee, and Alsy MacDonald. His work spans Indie Rock, Alternative Rock, New Wave, and Folk Rock, and appears across labels including Korova, Island Records, Domino, and Parlophone.
