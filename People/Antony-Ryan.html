Overview

Antony Ryan is a British electronic musician and mastering engineer, best known as co-founder of the ambient electronics duo ISAN alongside Robin Saville. After nearly twenty years composing, producing, and performing as half of ISAN, Ryan transitioned into professional mastering and mixing work, operating under the studio name RedRedPaw (also appearing as the alias Redredpaw Mastering on Discogs).

ISAN — Formation and Early Years (1996–2001)

Ryan and Saville formed ISAN — full name Integrated Services Analogue Network — in 1996. Their earliest releases were spread across a cluster of UK independent labels. The 1997 cassette/single Damil 85 (Wurlitzer Jukebox) appears to be their first indexed release. The following year they released material on Bad Jazz, Tugboat Records, and Earworm, and contributed to a split on Atomic Recordings.

The pivotal move came in 1999 when ISAN became one of the founding artists on Morr Music, the Berlin label run by Thomas Morr. Salamander (MM003, 1999) was the duo’s Morr debut — composed, performed, and produced by Ryan and Saville, with guest track B5 co-credited to Charles Atlas (the rotating collective that included Piano Magic’s Charles Wyatt). The same year they placed releases on Elefant Records (Spain) and Liquefaction Empire.

The early Morr period produced several key releases: Lucky Cat (MM021, 2001), the live document Salle D’ISAN (MM022, 2001), and Exquisite Honeyed Tart (Static Caravan, 2001). All core credits list Ryan and Saville as composers, performers, and producers; cover art across the Morr era was frequently handled by Jan Kruse (2), who became a consistent visual collaborator.

ISAN — Core Morr Music Period (2002–2010)

The duo remained on Morr for a sustained run of albums and EPs through the 2000s:

  • Clockwork Menagerie (MM032, 2002)
  • Meet Next Life (MM046, 2004)
  • Plans Drawn In Pencil (MM065, 2006)
  • Trois Gymnopedies (A Number Of Small Things, 2006) — reinterpretations of Satie’s Gymnopédies
  • Glow In The Dark Safari Set (MM096, 2010)

The Plans Drawn In Pencil cover was designed by Human Empire (humanempire.com). Glass Bird Movement (2016) and Lamenting Machine (2019) were later Morr albums, with mastering on Glass Bird Movement handled by Rashad Becker, who also cut lacquers for earlier releases including Salamander, Salle D’ISAN, and Meet Next Life.

A notable outside credit: ISAN remixed Maps & Diagrams on Delius Remixed (False Industries, 2013).

ISAN — Later Period and Other Labels (2011–2024)

From 2011 onward the duo’s releases diversified across smaller labels while retaining Morr for major albums:

  • Eastside (Kesh Recordings, 2011)
  • Descette (Secret Furry Hole, 2012 / 2013)
  • 150416 (Home Normal, 2016)
  • Nothing Picked And Placed Nowhere (Where It’s At Is Where You Are, 2020)
  • The Man In The Mountain (Company Of Corkbots, 2020) — Ryan credited as Electronics
  • Nine Simple Works (self-released, 2019)
  • ISAN Workshop #1 (self-released, 2020)
  • Demonstration Tapes (Morr Music, 2022) — final Morr release indexed on Discogs
  • Winter Chimes (self-released, 2024)

Seavault (Side Project)

Ryan co-founded Seavault with Simon Scott (Discogs artist ID 499484). The project had a web presence at seavault.net and issued one release: Mercy Seat (A Number Of Small Things, 2007). The Discogs entry also lists the name variation “Seafault.”

Mastering Work (RedRedPaw)

From approximately 2010–2011 onward, Ryan began taking on mastering credits under his own name and the RedRedPaw studio. His mastering client list visible on Discogs is extensive, spanning UK electronic, ambient, and experimental labels including:

  • Morr Music (ongoing: Plus by various artists, 2018; Build A Diorama by Robin Saville, 2020; Hallway Waverider 2022)
  • Wayside And Woodland Recordings / Epic45 — mastered Through Broken Summer (2018), Sun Memory (2019), Cropping The Aftermath (2020), We Were Never Here (2020); ISAN also contributed a remix to Epic45’s Monument EP (2014, W&W022)
  • Clay Pipe Music — multiple releases from 2013 onward
  • Second Language — from 2012 onward (Life Has Not Finished With Me Yet, The Cloisters, Tyneham House)
  • Castles in Space — prolific ongoing relationship from 2019 onward
  • Where It’s At Is Where You Are — from 2015 onward
  • Wayside And Woodland Recordings — ongoing
  • Ghost Box — from 2021 onward (Entangled Routes, Vaganten, The Path, Nova, The Thorn, The Carrier, A Letter From TreeTops, The Endless Echo)
  • Thrill Jockey — Captain Of None (Joan of Arc, 2015), A Flame My Love A Frequency (Circuit des Yeux, 2017), Another Kind Of Revolution (Ryley Walker, 2019), The Tunnel And The Clearing (Ryley Walker, 2021), Le Jour Et La Nuit Du Réel (2023)
  • Lo Recordings — from 2011 onward
  • Sonic Cathedral — from 2020 onward
  • Polytechnic Youth — from 2021 onward
  • Werra Foxma Records — multiple 2022–2023 releases

He has also remastered archival material for Tenorio Cotobade (Spain, 2024–2025), a label specialising in vintage electronic reissues.
