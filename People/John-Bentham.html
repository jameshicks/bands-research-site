Overview

John Bentham (born William John Bentham) was a music manager, producer, and film director active from the early 1980s. He managed punk acts One Way System and Zanti Misfitz, and co-founded three music video and media companies: Jettisoundz Video, Visionary Communications, and Screen Edge. His 80 credits run from 1981 across labels including Anagram Records, Cherry Red, and Captain Oi!, with a consistent core of collaborators — Tom Couch, David Ross, Gaz Buckley, and Gavin Whyte — appearing across the bulk of his output. Beyond punk, his catalogue extends into Space Rock and Psychedelic Rock through work with Hawkwind and Graham Bonnet.
