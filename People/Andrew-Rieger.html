Overview

Andrew Rieger is a guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter primarily known as a core member of Elf Power, the Athens, Georgia indie and psychedelic rock band that has been a fixture of the Elephant Six collective since the mid-1990s. He has accumulated over 100 Discogs credits spanning three decades, with roles ranging from Guitar and Vocals to Bass and Written-By. His most sustained label relationship is with Orange Twin Records, where he appears on ten releases between 2001 and 2025, alongside a long-running association with the Elephant Six Recording Co. His closest collaborators are fellow Elf Power members and Elephant Six affiliates — Laura Carter, Heather McIntosh, Dave Wrathgabar, Derek Almstead, and Will Cullen Hart among the most frequent. Beyond Elf Power, he has also appeared as a member of Major Organ And The Adding Machine and Platypus Pug.

Notes (web)

Elf Power label transition: Arena Rock → Sugar Free → Orange Twin

Arena Rock (full name: Arena Rock Recording Company, “ARRCO”) did not fold or close. It was founded in 1995 in Brooklyn, NY by Greg Glover and Dan Ralph, relocated to Portland, OR in 2004, and remains active as of 2023 with a Bandcamp presence. The label losing Elf Power was not a collapse event. (Wikipedia — Arena Rock Recording Company, as of 2026-10-10; Bandcamp)

The move away from Arena Rock appears to have been a deliberate step toward independence. The sequence:

  1. 1997–1999 — Arena Rock / Elephant Six: When the Red King Comes (1997) and A Dream in Sound (1999) were issued on Arena Rock Recording Co. / Elephant Six. (Trouser Press; Wikipedia — Elf Power)
  2. 2000 — Sugar Free Records: The Winter Is Coming (Oct 2000) was released on Sugar Free Records / Elephant 6 — the first release without Arena Rock involvement. No interview source explains why the band moved. (Wikipedia — The Winter Is Coming)
  3. 1999–2001 — Orange Twin founded: Laura Carter and Andrew Rieger founded Orange Twin Records around 1999, reportedly after finding a 1960s album in a thrift store. The label was established “originally as a fundraiser for the Orange Twin Conservation Community,” a 155-acre nature preserve near Athens, GA. (Wikipedia — Orange Twin Records, as of 2026-10-10; Trouser Press)
  4. 2001+ — Orange Twin: Elf Power’s subsequent releases moved to their own Orange Twin label. Nothing’s Going to Happen (a covers collection, 2002) and Walking With the Beggar Boys (2004) are the first Orange Twin credits per Trouser Press.

No published interview or press piece found gives an explicit reason for leaving Arena Rock specifically. The move is consistent with a broader pattern of Elephant Six-affiliated bands seeking direct ownership of their releases; the founding of Orange Twin as a conservation fundraiser suggests the band’s motivation was primarily about owning the vehicle rather than dissatisfaction with Arena Rock.
