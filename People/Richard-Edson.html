Richard Edson

Drummer, trumpeter, and actor. Born 1954, New Rochelle, New York. Discogs ID: 261880.

Name variations on record: Edson, R. Edson, R. Edison, R.Edson, Richie Edson, Richard Smith.

Overview

Richard Edson occupies a specific niche in downtown NYC music history: he was present at multiple foundational moments across adjacent scenes in the early 1980s before transitioning to a film career. He was the original drummer of Sonic Youth (1981–82), appearing on their self-titled debut EP on Neutral Records (1982) alongside Thurston Moore, Kim Gordon, and Lee Ranaldo — before Bob Bert replaced him, and before Steve Shelley joined for the band’s classic run. At the same time or slightly before, he played trumpet on Liquid Liquid’s self-titled 1981 debut EP on 99 Records, one of the canonical documents of the NYC post-punk/dance scene. He also played in Konk (the Afrobeat-influenced post-punk funk group, formed 1980) on trumpet and percussion.

His presence across Liquid Liquid, Sonic Youth, Konk, and the Glenn Branca Ensemble in the years 1981–83 makes him a minor but genuine connective figure of the downtown NYC avant-garde scene. He went on to play drums for Lydia Lunch on In Limbo (1984) and Drowning In Limbo (1989), trumpet for The Offs (San Francisco punk-ska, relocated to New York) on First Record (1984), and later contributed to Tito & Tarantula and the group Alterboys.

From the mid-1980s he became better known as an actor, appearing in Jim Jarmusch’s Stranger Than Paradise (1984), John Hughes’s Ferris Bueller’s Day Off (1986, as the parking garage attendant), and Spike Lee’s Do the Right Thing (1989). His Discogs footprint continues to show up sporadically through 2025.

The Offs were a San Francisco punk-ska band (later NYC) active from the late 1970s through at least 1984, with members including Don Vinil (vocals), Billy Hawk (guitar), Bob Steeler (drums), and Eric Peterson. Their LP First Record (1984) features cover art by Jean-Michel Basquiat, produced by Roland Young.

Alterboys had no releases in Discogs. Other members: Chris Gill (5), J.C. Garrett, Richard Kelly.
