Craig Scanlon

Discogs ID: 359750
Name variations: Craig Scanlan
Origin: Manchester, England

Guitarist and co-writer. Member of The Fall 1979–1995 — one of the band’s longest-serving members. Credited on nearly all Fall releases from Dragnet (1979) through Cerebral Caustic (1995). Prolific co-writer alongside Mark E. Smith and Stephen Hanley across the Rough Trade, Kamera, and Beggars Banquet eras. No substantial solo or side-project Discogs presence identified beyond The Fall.
