Ted Milton

UK saxophonist, vocalist, poet, and puppeteer. Founder and continuous frontman of Blurt. Discogs artist ID: 117052. Real name: Ted Milton. Brother of Jake Milton.

Overview

Active from 1979 to present. Formed Blurt c.1979–1980. Also leads Ted Milton And The Blurt Big Band, Ted Milton & The BoBonus Beats, Elegiac (2), and The Odes (2). Has appeared as guest saxophonist on releases for Playgroup (2002), Grand Drive (2003), and others.

Name variations: Milton, T Milton, T. Milton, Ted ‘Blurt’ Milton, Ted Milton And Mr. Pugh’s Puppet Theatre.

First recorded credit: Confessions Of An Aeroplane Farter b/w I Don’t Want To Go Poo-Poo! (1979, Echt! label) — vocals and acoustic guitar (uncredited).
