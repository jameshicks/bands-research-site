Overview

Mapstation is the primary solo alias of Stefan Schneider — bassist and co-founder of To Rococo Rot, and former member of Kreidler. Schneider was born in 1961, studied Fine Arts at the Düsseldorf Art Academy (1988–1993), and turned to electronic music in the 1990s. As Mapstation, he works in minimalist electronic music with a loose, rhythmic quality that draws on dub, ambient, and microhouse textures.

The Mapstation catalog spans from 2000 to the present. The core body of work was released on Staubgold (Berlin, 2000–2009) and the related Berlin label ~scape (run by Stefan Betke, a.k.a. Pole), with later releases on Bureau B and his own TAL (4) imprint (talmusic.com). Across these releases, Schneider develops a consistent voice: looped electronics, melodica, acoustic guitar, and synthesizers, often augmented by session drummers and wind players.

The second alias Spiegelmotiv (Discogs ID: 2860942) has only one release documented in Discogs — a self-released 12” EP from 2012 — and its alias links confirm it is the same person as Mapstation and Stefan Schneider.

Connections

  • Stefan Schneider — Mapstation is Schneider’s solo electronic alias; same Discogs realname, same alias links
  • To Rococo Rot — Schneider’s main band (1995–2014); all three members (Robert Lippok, Ronald Lippok, Stefan Schneider) ran parallel solo projects during TRR years
  • Kreidler — Schneider’s pre-TRR band (Düsseldorf); Thomas Klein (drums) is also a Kreidler member; the two groups share the same Düsseldorf electronic scene root
  • Staubgold — anchor label for Mapstation’s core 2000–2008 catalog (6 releases: staubgold 11, 29, 38, 85, and others); also released To Rococo Rot’s Kölner Brett (2001) and Taken From Vinyl (2006)
  • ~scape — released Distance Told Me Things To Be Said (2006) and The Africa Chamber (2009); label run by Stefan Betke (Pole), who mastered The Africa Chamber
  • Bureau B — released My Frequencies, When We (2021); also the main label for Stefan Schneider’s other solo work as “Stefan Schneider” (primary credits)
  • TAL (4) — Schneider’s own imprint (talmusic.com); released Present Unmetrics (2019) and Schneider’s solo LP Tiergarten (2017)
  • Martin Brandlmayr — appears on two albums; co-wrote Version Train (2003); also shared Autistic Daughters/Trapist/Staubgold scene context
  • Stefan Betke — mastered The Africa Chamber; his ~scape label was co-home for Mapstation from 2006
  • Annie Whitehead — trombone on two Mapstation records; veteran UK jazz-improv scene, not from the Düsseldorf/Berlin electronic world — an unusual crossover credit
  • Paul Wirkus — Polish percussionist with own Staubgold discography; the Forest Full Of Drums collaboration gives equal billing
  • Ekkehard Ehlers — Staubgold labelmate; alias Betrieb appears as co-primary on New Motion / Gravitation (2003, Combination Records)
  • Bernd Jestram — core technical collaborator across early catalog; has almost no primary releases of his own; his Staubgold/Berlin engineering credits span TRR, Mapstation, and other Kitty-Yo-adjacent projects
  • Sam Prekop, John McEntire — Split (2019) places Mapstation in dialogue with the Chicago post-rock/electronica circle (The Sea and Cake, Tortoise)
  • Spiegelmotiv — second Mapstation alias; one documented release (2012 self-released EP); alias links in Discogs confirm same artist
