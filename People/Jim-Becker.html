Jim Becker

Chicago-based multi-instrumentalist, singer, and composer. Discogs artist ID: 340579. Real name: James Becker. Active from at least the mid-1990s as a session player and band member across the Chicago experimental, folk, and indie-rock scenes.

Overview

Jim Becker is one of the more quietly essential figures in Chicago’s post-millennium indie and experimental music ecosystem. He plays accordion, fiddle, banjo (fretless and standard), guitar, mandolin, violin, bass, piano, organ, pedal steel, and more — often several instruments on a single record. He joined Califone as a full member from Quicksand/Cradlesnakes (2003) onward, and his expanding instrumental palette is central to the layered acoustic-electronic textures of Heron King Blues (2004) and Roots & Crowns (2006). He is also a long-standing member of Boxhead Ensemble, the Chicago-based ensemble led by Michael Krassner, and co-led the duo Lanzón with Joe Adamik.

Beyond those anchors, Becker has contributed to records by Iron & Wine, Bonnie “Prince” Billy, Iron & Wine / Ben Bridwell, Langhorne Slim, Mind Over Mirrors, and dozens of other artists — typically as a strings/banjo specialist brought in to add acoustic texture. He also released Interkosmos (2009) as a co-primary credit alongside Colleen Burke on Shrug Records.
