Steve Diggle

Guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter. Born 7 May 1955, Manchester, England. Discogs artist ID: 395283. Co-founder of Buzzcocks, joining July 1976 initially as bassist before switching to lead guitar when Steve Garvey joined in late 1977. Prolific songwriter on the UA albums alongside Pete Shelley. Led Flag Of Convenience and related projects during the Buzzcocks hiatus years. Continued to lead Buzzcocks after Shelley’s death in December 2018.

Overview

Diggle was recruited to the nascent Buzzcocks in July 1976 by Pete Shelley and Howard Devoto, originally to play bass alongside Shelley on guitar. He played bass on the landmark Spiral Scratch EP (New Hormones, January 1977), produced by Martin Hannett. When Garvey joined as permanent bassist in late 1977, Diggle shifted to second guitar — a role he held throughout the classic United Artists period and all subsequent lineup configurations.

During the UA years (1977–1981), Diggle shared songwriting duties with Shelley across all three studio albums and contributed several prominent singles including “Harmony in My Head” (1979), produced by Hannett, and tracks on each LP. His guitar is credited on the right-hand channel on A Different Kind of Tension (1979), with Shelley on the left — a literal spatial division of their dual-lead approach.

After the band’s 1981 dissolution, Diggle kept the name alive through a series of side projects: Flag Of Convenience (later listed as Buzzcocks F.O.C.), Steve Diggle & The Flag Of Convenience, and eventually Steve Diggle & The Revolution Of Sound. He also recorded under his own name from 1981 onward with solo EPs and albums. When Buzzcocks reunited fully from 1992, Diggle resumed the second-guitar role through to Shelley’s death. Post-2019 he has led the band as principal songwriter, vocalist, guitarist, and producer on Sonics in the Soul (Cherry Red, 2022) and Attitude Adjustment (Cherry Red, 2026).

Discogs name variations include: Diggle, Diggles, S Diggle, S. Diggle, S.Diggle, Stephen Diggle.
