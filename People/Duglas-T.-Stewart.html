Overview

Duglas T. Stewart is a Glasgow-based singer, songwriter, and guitarist who has been the central figure of BMX Bandits since the mid-1980s. His recorded output stretches from early releases on 53rd & 3rd in 1986 through Creation Records in the 1990s to later work on Rev-Ola, Elefant Records, and the Glasgow-rooted Past Night From Glasgow imprint, accumulating over 200 credits across four decades. The collaborator map around him reads as a cross-section of the Scottish indie scene: Norman Blake of Teenage Fanclub and Eugene Kelly of The Vaselines both appear as recurring partners, alongside long-standing BMX Bandits associates Francis Macdonald, Joe McAlinden, and Duncan Cameron. Stewart also works under the Duglas T. Stewart & Co. and Yeongene monikers alongside the main band.

Notes (web)

BMX Bandits and Creation Records — departure timeline

Multiple sources agree that BMX Bandits left Creation Records in 1997, not 1999. Their last release on the label was the album Theme Park (1996). The departure therefore predates Alan McGee’s resignation from Creation (1999) and the label’s closure (also 1999, with one final release in January 2000) by roughly two years — the band was not caught up in McGee’s exit or the label’s folding.

The Discogs label span of 1992–1999 for this entry likely reflects compilation appearances or licensing activity rather than active signed releases; the active period on Creation ends with Theme Park in 1996.

No source consulted gives an explicit stated reason for the split. The AllMusic biography and TweeNet both note simply that the band left in 1997 and subsequently embarked on a first American mini-tour with an expanded lineup that included Stevie Jackson of Belle & Sebastian.
