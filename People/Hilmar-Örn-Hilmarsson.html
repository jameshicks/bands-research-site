Hilmar Örn Hilmarsson

Discogs ID: 145298
Origin: Iceland

Icelandic composer, musician, and current high priest of the Ásatrú religious society in Iceland. Co-wrote “Life Transmission” lyrics for Þeyr’s debut 7” (1981). Played keyboards, bass, and programming on Höfuðlausnir (Gramm, 1988) by Megas, alongside Björk Guðmundsdóttir and Guðlaugur Kristinn Óttarsson. Not to be confused with Hilmar Örn Agnarsson (bassist in Þeyr, different surname).

Sigur Rós Collaborations

Composed and co-produced the Angels of the Universe film soundtrack (FatCat / Krúnk, 2001) with Sigur Rós; Sigur Rós contributed performances. Also collaborated on Odin’s Raven Magic (Krúnk, 2020) with Sigur Rós and rímur-singer Steindór Andersen, a work first performed at the Reykjavík Arts Festival 2002. 20 shared Discogs entries with Sigur Rós.

Frostbite (2) and H3ÖH (1993)

In 1993 Hilmar co-founded the one-off electronic project Frostbite (2) with Einar Örn Benediktsson, releasing The Second Coming (One Little Indian TPLP666 CD). Hilmar handled instruments and engineering (credited as “HOH”), with mixing shared with Ken Thomas. The same year the duo collaborated with Andrew McKenzie (The Hafler Trio) under the alias H3ÖH (combining HOH’s initials and the H3/Hafler Trio shorthand) to produce The Hafler Trio Bootleg (Ash International, 1993).

Discogs also lists a shared alias Kali (63) (ID 10788817) for the Einar Örn / Hilmar pairing, with no associated releases in the DB.
