Gary Langan

Gary Langan

Oct 03, 20264 min read

Gary Langan

Discogs ID: 89908

British engineer and programmer; co-founding member of The Art Of Noise at ZTT Records. Primary engineer on early FGTH and Art of Noise productions at Sarm Studios.

Co-writer on all Art of Noise releases. Left the group in 1987, leaving Anne Dudley and J.J. Jeczalik to continue until 1990. Remained connected to the group as a silent partner and returned to record their final Hammersmith Odeon concert.

Departure from Art of Noise (1986–1987)

Context: the ZTT split

Langan, Dudley, and Jeczalik departed ZTT Records in 1985, reportedly acrimoniously, and signed with China Records under Derek Green — taking the Art of Noise name with them after winning a court battle. ZTT had placed an injunction preventing them signing to any UK label; they went through the courts and won. Langan later said the court case had left him “beaten up.” (superdeluxeedition.com; superdeluxeedition.com)

The specific break with Dudley and Jeczalik

Langan’s departure from Art of Noise itself came in 1987 — after In Visible Silence and the Paranoimia single, the last release to feature him as a full trio member. The immediate trigger was a disagreement over touring. Dudley and Jeczalik wanted a conventional American college sit-down tour; Langan opposed this and proposed what he called “THE ART OF NOISE Plays Manhattan” — a simultaneous multi-venue performance using VJs and Electronic News Gathering cameras and microwave links to broadcast between four or five clubs at the same time. When the others rejected the concept, the split came:

“We had a difference of opinion which happens in every walk of life. […] The other two were swayed in the other direction and I went ‘I don’t agree!’ […] At the same time, I was due to go off to Europe with SPANDAU BALLET for their ‘Through The Barricades’ tour. So we parted company at that point.” — Gary Langan (electricityclub.co.uk)

He had been producing Spandau Ballet’s Through the Barricades album in Munich before the tour commitment provided the exit. (electricityclub.co.uk; theartofnoiseonline.com)

The Classic Pop retrospective on In Visible Silence describes “Paranoimia” as “the last track released by the group as a trio as Gary jumped ship soon after for a production career.” (classicpopmag.com)

Post-departure connection

Langan’s exit was not a clean break. He described his status afterward as “remaining connected to them as a silent partner” and returned to record what he described as “a final concert at the Hammersmith Odeon.” (electricityclub.co.uk; theartofnoiseonline.com)

Approximately 1992, Jeczalik and Langan had discussed reforming the group with Dudley and together travelled to Cuba to source new material, per Jeczalik’s account to the ZTT fanzine Outside World. (theartofnoiseonline.com)

By 2013, all five original Art of Noise members (Langan, Dudley, Jeczalik, Trevor Horn, Paul Morley) reunited at the 19:Eighties concert at London’s Queen Elizabeth Hall. (superdeluxeedition.com)

In 2014, Langan was publicly enthusiastic about a full reunion:

“What I’d really like, my wish for the future, is that somehow the Art of Noise gets back together again and we go on tour, and we do it properly.” — Gary Langan (superdeluxeedition.com)

He also noted at that point that Jeczalik had recently retired from teaching and “was looking for something to do,” and that serious talks had begun in spring 2014 with a possible 2015 touring timeline. No tour materialised as of the writing of these sources.

JJ Jeczalik, reflecting on the trio dynamic, observed: “The third person in a group brings together any disparate elements” — implying Langan’s absence had affected the group’s equilibrium. (classicpopmag.com)

Langan himself expressed no regrets:

“Sure, made some bad decisions, gone down some wrong avenues but as long as you learn from it, no regrets really.” — Gary Langan (electricityclub.co.uk)

He also participated in promotional work for the archival box set And What Have You Done With My Body, God? alongside the other original members. (theartofnoiseonline.com)
