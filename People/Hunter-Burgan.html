Overview

Hunter Burgan is an American bassist born 14 May 1976 in Grass Valley, California, best known as the long-serving bassist for AFI, with whom he accumulates the bulk of his 344 total credits alongside Davey Havok, Adam Carson, and Jade Puget. His catalogue spans punk, hardcore, alternative rock, and goth rock, running from 1996 through the mid-2020s, chiefly on Nitro Records and later Interscope. Outside AFI he has been a member of Matt Skiba And The Sekrets, Las Gatas Beach Club, LEKTRON, and Badical Turbo Radness, and carries a conspicuous 128 shared credits with Nick 13, pointing to deep involvement in the overlapping goth-punk and psychobilly circles around Hellcat and Nitro.
