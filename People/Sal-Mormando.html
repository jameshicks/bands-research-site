Overview

Sal Mormando is a New Jersey-based audio engineer and co-owner of Kaleidoscope Sound, accumulating over 130 Discogs credits from 1994 onwards across engineering, recording, mixing, and mastering roles. His work spans Alternative Rock, Indie Rock, Punk, Math Rock, and Contemporary Jazz. He has an unusually dense cluster of credits with the Patti Smith circle — Smith herself, Lenny Kaye, Jay Dee Daugherty, Oliver Ray, and Tony Shanahan each share 11 releases with him — suggesting Kaleidoscope Sound served as a home base for her late-1990s and 2000s studio work. He also logged significant work with mastering engineers Greg Calbi and Bob Ludwig, producer-engineer John Agnello, and metal producer Machine.
