Franz Hautzinger

Austrian composer and trumpeter specializing in quartertone trumpet, free improvisation, and electroacoustic music. Born 1963 in Burgenland, Austria. Discogs artist ID: 231889. Partner of Isabelle Duthoit.

Studied trumpet and composition at the Graz Academy of Music and the Vienna Conservatory. Active since the late 1980s across the European improvised music scene, Middle Eastern collaborations, and North American free jazz contexts. His signature instrument is the quartertone trumpet — a custom horn capable of pitches between the Western chromatic semitones — which he developed as both solo and ensemble vocabulary from around 1998 onward.

Name variations on record: F. H., F. Hautzinger, Frank Hautzinger, Franz Hauzinger, Hautzinger.
