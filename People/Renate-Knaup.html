Renate Knaup

Real name: Renate Aschauer-Knaup
Born: 1 July 1948
Discogs ID: 321564
Alias: Henriette Kroetenschwanz (stage alias used on some Amon Düül II releases)
Roles: Vocalist, tambourine

Lead vocalist of Amon Düül II through the band’s classic period (1969–1974), returning for the 1981 and 1995 reunions. The only woman in the band’s classic lineup. Her voice became increasingly central from Carnival in Babylon (1972) onwards as the band moved toward more structured songs. She appeared on Phallus Dei (1969) in a minimal capacity and is credited as “Henriette Kroetenschwanz” as a guest vocalist on Tanz Der Lemminge (1971).

After departing Amon Düül II in 1974, Knaup joined Popol Vuh, the Munich spiritual/ambient group led by Florian Fricke.
