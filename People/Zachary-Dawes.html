Zachary Dawes

Discogs ID: 2736386 Real name: Zachary Dawes Born: July 2, 1985 (USA) Role: Bassist, engineer, technician, producer Active: 2008–present Associated acts: Mini Mansions, The Last Shadow Puppets, Slang Chickens

Biography

Zachary Dawes (born July 2, 1985) is an American bassist, engineer, and producer based in Los Angeles. He is best known as a founding member of Mini Mansions and as the touring/session bassist for The Last Shadow Puppets on their 2016 reunion album.

Early Career and Slang Chickens

Dawes’ earliest Discogs credit is a 2008 release under Slang Chickens (Discogs ID: 1926984), released on Psychedelic Judaism, a small LA imprint that also appears in Mini Mansions’ early orbit. His name variation “Zach Dawes” is used consistently across Mini Mansions credits; “Zachary Dawes” appears on TLSP and his later solo/production work.

Mini Mansions (2009–present)

Dawes co-founded Mini Mansions in Los Angeles in 2009 alongside Mikey Shuman (also bassist in Queens of the Stone Age) and Tyler Parkford. The band self-released an EP in 2009 (Not On Label), then signed to Ipecac Recordings (Josh Homme’s label) for their 2010 self-titled debut LP (Mini Mansions, Rekords Rekords, cat: IPC122). The album was mixed in part by Josh Homme (tracks 3–5) and Biff Dawes (tracks 8, 10, 12 — likely a family member).

Their second album, The Great Pretenders (2014–2015, Capitol Records / Electromagnetic Recordings (2)), was produced by Michael Harris (10) with T-Bone Burnett as executive producer and John Congleton mixing. The album featured Alex Turner on “Vertigo” and Brian Wilson on “Any Emotions.” Dawes is credited as “Zach Dawes, Performer” throughout.

The “Vertigo” single (2015, Electromagnetic Recordings (2), cat: MANSIONS4) was the catalyst for the Mini Mansions / Alex Turner collaboration, with Turner, Shuman, Parkford, and Dawes all sharing songwriting credits. T-Bone Burnett served as executive producer.

Their third record, Works Every Time EP (2018, Fiction Records, cat: MANSIONS5), was produced by Cian Riordan with Dawes on bass guitar and keyboards. A fourth LP, Guy Walks Into A Bar… (2019, Fiction Records), continued with Riordan producing, featuring guest appearances from Alison Mosshart and T-Bone Burnett.

Dawes is also credited as producer on Mini Mansions’ Flashbacks: A Collection of B-Sides from The Great Pretenders (2016) and as both performer and writer on their catalog throughout.

The Last Shadow Puppets (2016)

Dawes is listed as a full member of The Last Shadow Puppets on Discogs alongside Alex Turner, Miles Kane, and James Ford. His primary TLSP credit is Everything You’ve Come To Expect (2016, Domino, cat: WIGLP371X), where he is credited as Performer alongside Turner, Kane, and Ford. The album was produced by James Ford, mixed by Tchad Blake, and features orchestrations by Owen Pallett. Matt Helders (Arctic Monkeys) contributes backing vocals on several tracks.

The Dream Synopsis EP (2016, Domino) also credits Dawes as Performer, confirming his continued involvement in TLSP beyond the main album.

Session and Production Work

Dawes has an extensive sideman and engineering résumé outside his main bands:

  • Engineering/assistant mix: Gregg Allman’s Low Country Blues (2011, Rounder Records, 2nd engineer); Diana Krall’s Glad Rag Doll (2012, Verve Records, assistant mix engineer); Coen Brothers’ Inside Llewyn Davis OST (2013, Nonesuch, assistant engineer + guitar/equipment technician)
  • Bass: Lisa Marie Presley’s Storm & Grace (2012, Universal Republic); The Hunger Games Songs from District 12 compilation (2012, Universal Republic); Brian Wilson’s No Pier Pressure (2015, Capitol Records); Sara Watkins’ Young In All The Wrong Ways (adjacent credits); Sharon Van Etten’s Remind Me Tomorrow (2019, Jagjaguwar); Bedouine’s Bird Songs of a Killjoy (2020, Spacebomb); Beady Eye’s BE (adjacent credits)
  • Elton John’s The Diving Board (2013, Mercury) — credited as Mixed By
  • Unloved – Guilty Of Love (2016, Unloved Records) — Bass
  • Tim Heidecker – Fear Of Death (2020, Spacebomb Records) — Bass (alongside Natalie Mering / Weyes Blood, Jonathan Rado)
  • Sharon Van Etten – Remind Me Tomorrow (2019, Jagjaguwar) — Bass, Synth
  • Bebe Rexha – Better Mistakes-adjacent / Sunflower (2021, Atlantic) — Bass
  • Lana Del Rey – Did You Know That There’s A Tunnel Under Ocean Blvd (2023, Interscope/Polydor) — Producer (alongside Jack Antonoff and Drew Erickson)

The Lana Del Rey production credit marks a significant shift from session bassist to credited co-producer on a major-label flagship release.

Open Questions

  • Dawes’ exact departure from Mini Mansions is not documented in Discogs; his last credit with the band is Guy Walks Into A Bar… (2019). Whether he remains an active member or has exited is unclear from Discogs alone.
  • The “Biff Dawes” credit on the 2010 Mini Mansions debut (mixer, tracks 8/10/12) is almost certainly a family member but is unconfirmed — web research would clarify.
  • The Golden Echo (Kimbra, 2014, Warner Bros.) lists Dawes as “Written-By” — unclear if this is a co-write or a sample credit; the release has only one pressing in Discogs and no tracklist detail attributing the role to specific tracks.
  • Dawes has his own website (zacharydawes.com) and Instagram; solo/production work post-2024 is not in Discogs.
