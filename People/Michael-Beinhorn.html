Michael Beinhorn

Full name: Michael James Beinhorn Role: Keyboards, electronics, producer Discogs ID: 92477

Overview

American musician and producer. Core member of Material 1979–1983, alongside Bill Laswell and Fred Maher. In Material he handled keyboards, synthesizers, tape manipulation, electronics, and vocals — a versatile multi-instrumentalist in the downtown avant-garde tradition.

After the Material period he transitioned into major-label rock production and became a Grammy-winning producer. Production credits include Red Hot Chili Peppers, Soundgarden, Hole, Marilyn Manson, Soul Asylum (Grave Dancers Union, 1992, Columbia — triple platinum; Grammy for “Runaway Train”), and others — a significant career pivot from the downtown avant-garde into the mainstream rock production world of the 1990s. On Grave Dancers Union he also played glockenspiel and celesta, and co-arranged strings on the closing track with Pirner and Sonny Kompanek.
