Joey Pinter

New York guitarist and bassist. Discogs artist ID: 553750. Instagram: @joeypinter.

Overview

Joey Pinter is a New York guitarist and multi-instrumentalist active in the NYC underground since at least 1980. He is best known as a member of Knots (2) — the short-lived NYC punk band that released one single on their own Ideal Records in 1980 — and as a founding member of The Waldos, the band Walter Lure assembled after The Heartbreakers (2) dissolved following Johnny Thunders’s death in 1991. Pinter played guitar on both of the Waldos’ 1990s releases (Crazy Little Baby and Rent Party) alongside former Knots bandmate Tony Coiro. In 1980 he also played guitar and bass on Walter Steding’s debut LP on Red Star Records, produced by Chris Stein of Blondie. His duo Pleasure Pirates, with vocalist/guitarist Ricki Kirk, released an LP on Rawhide Records in 1988. He released a solo album on CD Baby in 2015 and is credited as a songwriter on releases by Los Pepes (4) (2019) and The Flytraps (2020), with the Flytraps credit co-attributed to Knots (2).

Key Release Notes

Walter Steding LP (1980, Red Star Records)

Pinter played guitar and bass across the album alongside drummer Jerry Ryan (4) (also of Knots). Engineered by Bob Blank; produced by Chris Stein (Blondie). Photography by Edo Bertoglio (front sleeve) and George DuBose (back). The record is experimental/electronic in character and sits in the lower Manhattan post-punk art scene — Steding was an Andy Warhol associate. This shared personnel with Knots (Ryan + Pinter) suggests the Knots/Steding sessions were part of the same downtown NYC social circuit.

Hidden Treasures (1988, Rawhide Records)

Pleasure Pirates LP. Four tracks: “Love Don’t Come Easy,” “Trapped,” “Mugger’s Song,” “New York, New York.” Both Pinter and Ricki Kirk are credited as lead vocals and electric guitar — no other personnel listed in Discogs. Cat# R9 1963.

Crazy Little Baby (1991, Baylor Records)

Waldos debut 7”, BAY 6. Lineup: Walter Lure (guitar, vocals), Pinter (guitar, backing vocals), Tony Coiro (bass, vocals), Charlie Sox (drums, backing vocals). Produced by David Eng and The Waldos; engineered by John Ficarrotta.

Rent Party (1994, Sympathy For The Record Industry)

Waldos debut LP, SFTRI 269. Lineup: Lure, Pinter (guitar), Coiro (bass, vocals), Jeff West (4) (drums). No producer listed in Discogs individual credits. Reissued twice by Jungle Records (UK).

Joey Pinter (2015, CD Baby)

Self-titled solo album, 17 tracks, CD format. Genres: Rock / Punk, Rock & Roll. No personnel credits listed in Discogs beyond Pinter as primary artist. Cat# 888295255905.

Songwriter Credits (2019–2020)

  • Positive Negative by Los Pepes (4): Pinter credited Written By on track B6 only. Los Pepes is a UK punk band (Gods Candy / Spaghetty Town Records).
  • Wild Card by The Flytraps: Pinter and Knots (2) credited Written-By on track B6. The Flytraps are a US band (Burger Records / Outro Records, 2020). This is the only Discogs credit where Knots (2) the band entity receives a songwriting attribution.
