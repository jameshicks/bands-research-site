Özün Usta

Discogs ID: 655581
Name variation: Ozun Usta
Based: Istanbul, Turkey
Active: 2004–present (documented)

Istanbul-based multi-instrumentalist — percussion, double bass, and vibraphone. Member of the Istanbul free-improvisation group Konstrukt and the international collective Karkhana. Also member of Etnik Orchestra and Yuri Skies. Her instrument palette shifts across projects: predominantly percussion and hand drums in early Konstrukt work, double bass in Karkhana recordings, and drums in the Elephantine project with Maurice Louca.

Key Release Notes

Dolunay (2011, Re:konstruKt): Konstrukt’s collaboration with Peter Brötzmann. Özün Usta credited on percussion and also jointly on mixing/mastering with Tansu Kaner — unusual dual creative-technical role.

Vibrations Of The Day (2011, Re:konstruKt): Konstrukt with Marshall Allen (Sun Ra Arkestra). Özün Usta plays percussion, drums, and vibraphone — broadest instrumental spread of any Konstrukt session.

Eklisia Sunday (2013, Not Two Records): Konstrukt + Peter Brötzmann, Korhan Futacı, Korhan Argüden. Özün Usta on double bass, djembe, gong, and bells.

Nafas (2016, Omlott): Core Karkhana lineup — Özün Usta (double bass), Umut Çağlar (bendir/percussion), Sharif Sehnaoui (drums), Maurice Louca (organ/synth), Sam Shalabi (oud), Mazen Kerbaj (trumpet/synth). Recorded by Fadi Tabbal.

Elephantine (2019, Sub Rosa) / Moonshine (2023, Sub Rosa): Maurice Louca’s large-ensemble project. Özün Usta contributes drums and percussion across both releases alongside a rotating international cast.
