Overview

Clarence Anicholas Clemons Jr. (January 11, 1942 – June 18, 2011) was a saxophonist from Norfolk, Virginia whose four-decade partnership with Bruce Springsteen & The E Street Band made him one of rock’s most recognisable horn players. His catalogue, concentrated in the 1980s through 2000s, spans pop rock, classic rock, and arena rock, with the bulk of his releases appearing on Columbia and CBS — the same labels that carried Springsteen’s output — reflecting how thoroughly the two careers were intertwined. Beyond the E Street axis he led Clarence Clemons And The Red Bank Rockers, contributed to Artists United Against Apartheid and Jersey Artists For Mankind, and accumulated credits alongside the full E Street core of Garry Tallent, Danny Federici, Max Weinberg, Roy Bittan, and Steve Van Zandt. He also appeared as a member of Dutch rock outfit Herman Brood & His Wild Romance, a transatlantic connection that sits at some distance from his main career.
