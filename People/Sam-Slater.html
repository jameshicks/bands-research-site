Discogs ID: 5170725
Active: c. 2013–present
Based: England
URL: https://www.sssslater.eu/

English composer, producer, and sound artist working across film score, concert music, and experimental recorded work. His career intersects two overlapping worlds: the Icelandic Bedroom Community axis (centred on Jóhann Jóhannsson, Hildur Guðnadóttir, and Valgeir Sigurðsson) and the British experimental/electronic scene touching labels like Slip, Type Records, and Invada. He is also a member of the feedback percussion ensemble OSMIUM (10).

Overview

Slater began accumulating credits around 2013, initially as an engineer and sound designer in proximity to the Bedroom Community circle. By 2016 he was engineering Valgeir Sigurðsson’s live electronics for Jóhann Jóhannsson’s European touring, and that year handled mixing for Shapednoise’s Deafening Chaos Serenity on Type Records. A sustained collaboration with Hildur Guðnadóttir followed: he co-produced her Emmy-nominated score for Chernobyl (HBO, 2019) and co-produced the soundtrack album for Joker (2019, WaterTower Music), which won Guðnadóttir the Academy Award for Best Original Score.

His solo debut Wrong Airport Ghost appeared in 2018 on Bedroom Community, and his second solo record I Do Not Wish To Be Known As A Vandal followed in 2022 on the same label, with contributions from Guðnadóttir (halldorophone, cello), James Ginzburg (mix), Valgeir Sigurðsson (mastering), and JFDR (vocals).

In 2022 he also co-composed Battlefield 2042 (Official Soundtrack) (Lakeshore Records) with Guðnadóttir as primary co-composer.

Connections

  • Jóhann Jóhannsson — engineered live electronics on Jóhannsson’s 2016 European tour; programmed and mixed the Variations portion of Englabörn & Variations (Deutsche Grammophon, 2018, posthumous).
  • Hildur Guðnadóttir — closest ongoing collaborator; Slater co-produced Chernobyl and Joker scores, and Guðnadóttir played on Slater’s I Do Not Wish To Be Known As A Vandal.
  • Valgeir Sigurðsson — Bedroom Community founder and recurring collaborator; mastered Slater’s solo record.
  • Ben Frost — assisted sound design on The Centre Cannot Hold (Mute, 2017).
  • Julia Reidy — mixed and supervised recording on Brace, Brace (Slip, 2019).
  • James Ginzburg — mixed I Do Not Wish To Be Known As A Vandal; Ginzburg runs the Subtext label and is Slater’s closest mixing collaborator on that record.
