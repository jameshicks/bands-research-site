Andy Barker

Real name: Andrew Barker
Born: 1968 — per Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Barker)
Died: November 6, 2021
Role: DJ, programmer, producer
Discogs ID: 35261

Manchester-based DJ, programmer, and electronic music producer. Best known as a member of 808 State from 1989 until his death in 2021. Before joining 808 State, he and Darren Partington performed together as the Spinmasters, DJing on Sunset FM and Kiss FM in Manchester.

Early Career — The Spinmasters and Hit Squad MCR

Andy Barker and Darren Partington operated as a DJ duo called the Spinmasters on Manchester’s Sunset FM and Kiss FM pirate radio stations in the late 1980s. Through that scene they connected with the wider Manchester acid house network centred on Eastern Bloc Records.

In 1988 they appeared as part of Hit Squad MCR, a Manchester collective combining the full early 808 State lineup with MCs and DJs. Hit Squad MCR released one 12” — Wax On The Melt (Eastern Bloc Records, EASTERN 1, 1988) — a three-track white-label split featuring breakbeat and electro material.

808 State (1989–2021)

Gerald Simpson departed 808 State after Quadrastate (1989, Creed Records) to pursue A Guy Called Gerald solo work. Barker and Partington stepped in, completing the classic lineup: Graham Massey, Martin Price, Barker, and Partington. This quartet recorded the ZTT debut Ninety (1989) and the international breakthrough singles “Pacific” and “Cübik.”

Martin Price left in late 1991 (over a US tour), leaving a trio of Massey, Barker, and Partington. From Gorgeous (1993) onward, Barker carried explicit production and writing credits on 808 State records.

When Darren Partington was jailed in January 2015 for drug dealing offences, Barker and Massey continued as a duo. They remained active: Initial Granada Report (self-released, 2019), Transmission Suite (self-released, 2019), and ESP: The 808 State Effect (ZTT, 2021) all appeared in the latter period. Barker co-wrote and co-produced ZTT singles Pump and The Only Rhyme That Bites 99 in 2022 — both released posthumously.

Andy Barker died on November 6, 2021. He was found unresponsive on Peel Moat Road, Heaton Moor, Manchester, just after 3 pm. Greater Manchester Police made a public appeal to trace his last movements; he had last been seen on York Road around 2 pm heading toward Brownsville Road, and was seen again at 2:48 pm with a white bandage around his head — police sought the person who may have administered that first aid. No suspicious circumstances were found. The band’s and family’s statement described the cause as “a short period of illness”; no specific medical cause was ever publicly disclosed. Graham Massey has continued 808 State work since.
