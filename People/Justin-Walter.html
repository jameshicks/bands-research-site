Overview

Justin Walter is a jazz musician, composer, and trumpeter from Ann Arbor, Michigan, best known for his work with the Electronic Valve Instrument (EVI), a rare wind-controlled analog synthesizer. A member of NOMO and Bambi Kino Duo, his solo and ensemble output spans ambient, experimental, and indie rock territory, concentrated in the 2010s. His catalogue has appeared primarily on Polyvinyl Record Company and Kranky, with close collaborators including Fred Thomas, Elliot Bergman, Warren Defever, and Erik Hall — figures central to the Ann Arbor and Detroit indie and experimental underground.
