Overview

Rodney Graham (1949–2022) was a Canadian artist and musician from Abbotsford, British Columbia, active from the early 1980s through the 2010s. He was a member of several Vancouver-area bands — UJ3RK5, Bamff, Blanche Whitman, the Rodney Graham Band, and Volumizer — working across indie rock, folk rock, art rock, and avant-garde styles. His catalogue straddles the indie and art worlds: releases appeared on institutional imprints such as Dia Center For The Arts, Kunsthalle Wien, Kunstverein München, and JRP|Ringier alongside indie labels including Matador, Merge Records, and Loudhailer. His closest collaborators were producer John Collins and engineer David Carswell, and he shared credits with multiple members of The New Pornographers circle, including Daniel Bejar, A.C. Newman, and Neko Case.
