Marcus Schmickler

German electronic musician and composer. Discogs artist ID: 11330. Based in Cologne. Also records as Pluramon, Piethopraxis, and other aliases.

Member of MIMEO (Music In Movement Electronic Orchestra). On Queue (GROB, 1998) credited for mastering. On Electric Chair + Table (GROB, 1999) credited for computer, synthesizer, and as studio editor (disc 1). Also recorded as R/S with Peter Rehberg (One (Snow Mud Rain), Erstwhile, 2007; USA, Pan, 2011).
