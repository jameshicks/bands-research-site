Adriana Kaegi

Vocalist, choreographer, songwriter, and designer. Born in Switzerland; moved to New York City at age 18. Co-founder of Kid Creole And The Coconuts alongside (then-husband) August Darnell and Coati Mundi. Discogs ID: 203878.

Overview

Adriana Kaegi was one of the founding Coconuts vocalists and a core member of the Kid Creole And The Coconuts touring and recording unit from the project’s inception. She is credited on the group’s first three studio albums — Off The Coast Of Me (1980), Fresh Fruit In Foreign Places (1981), and Tropical Gangsters (1982) — as well as the live film Live In Concert “At The Ritz” New York (1982) and subsequent studio albums Doppelganger (1983) and In Praise Of Older Women And Other Crimes (1985). Her contributions extended beyond singing: on Private Waters In The Great Divide (1990) she is credited for backing vocals, choreography, costume design, and jewel design, reflecting a broader creative role within the group’s theatrical presentation.

Kaegi was married to August Darnell from approximately 1978 to 1991. Beyond Kid Creole, she was a member of the vocal trio Boomerang (4) alongside fellow Coconut Cheryl Poirier and Perri Lister, which released one album on Atlantic in 1986. She co-wrote at least one track credited on the Elbow Bones And The Racketeers retrospective New York At Dawn (2012), and released two solo recordings in 2009 and 2011 under her own name (credited as “Adriana” on the 2009 release Tag), self-producing the latter.
