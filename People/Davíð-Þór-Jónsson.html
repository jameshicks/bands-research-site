Davíð Þór Jónsson

Discogs ID: 505659
Pianist, instrumentalist, composer, conductor, arranger, and producer. Born 1978 in Seyðisfjörður, Iceland. Also performs under the abbreviated initials DÞJ and the anglicized form David Thor Jonsson. A prolific figure in the Reykjavík music scene, he has worked across jazz, chamber music, avant-pop, and experimental contexts, contributing to an unusually wide range of Icelandic and international projects. He is primarily known outside Iceland as co-producer (with Kjartan Sveinsson) of Ólöf Arnalds’s second album Innundir Skinni (One Little Indian, 2010).

Note on the Sigur Rós connection: Wikipedia’s article on Innundir Skinni describes him as a “then Sigur Rós member,” and the Ólöf Arnalds Wikipedia article similarly refers to him alongside Kjartan Sveinsson as producers “then of Sigur Rós.” However, his Discogs artist page does not list Sigur Rós in his groups, and Sigur Rós’s own Discogs entry does not include him in the member list (which lists Jónsi Birgisson, Georg Hólm, Kjartan Sveinsson, Orri Páll Dýrason, and Ágúst Ævar Gunnarsson). His relationship to Sigur Rós appears to have been as a touring or associated musician rather than a formal full member; this should be verified against a primary source.
