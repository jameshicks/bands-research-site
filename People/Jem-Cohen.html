Jem Cohen

Award-winning filmmaker, photographer, and graphic designer. Born 1962. Based in Washington DC and New York City. Discogs artist ID: 704870. Also credited as J. Cohen, Jem A. Cohen, Rt. Hon. Jem Cohen.

Long-term visual collaborator with Fugazi, credited on every Fugazi studio album from their first 7” (1989) through The Argument (2001). Made the feature documentary Instrument (1999). Wide-ranging photography credits across Warner Bros., Constellation, Ex Records, and other labels.

Overview

Jem Cohen works primarily in film — observational portraits of urban landscapes, blending 16mm, Super 8, and video formats. His music work spans sleeve design, photography, film direction, and production. The Fugazi connection is the deepest and longest-running: starting with co-designing the Song #1 b/w Joe #1 sleeve in 1989, he contributed graphic concept, assemblage, photography, and text across virtually every major Fugazi release for over a decade, developing a distinctive cut-and-paste aesthetic that became part of the band’s visual identity.

Beyond Fugazi and Dischord Records, Cohen has shot photography for R.E.M. across multiple Warner Bros. albums (1988–2003), directed a concert film for The Ex (2007), co-produced Vic Chesnutt’s North Star Deserter (2007) with Efrim Menuck and Guy Picciotto, and produced Promised Land Sound’s debut for Paradise Of Bachelors (2013).

All Dischord releases above are Dischord Records. The In On The Kill Taker credit specifies “Graphic Concept” and “Assemblage,” the terms Cohen and the band used for the collage-based layouts. Jason Farrell is credited as “Cover Mechanic” on several of these — he handled the physical production/layout side. Don Zientara engineered most of the underlying records.

Instrument (1999): Feature documentary on Fugazi spanning 1987–1999. Cohen directed and shot the film; co-credited with Fugazi as the primary release. The soundtrack album on Dischord has Cohen credited for Cover Stills (film frames). Brendan Canty is a co-director on the later Ashes Of American Flags (see below) but is not listed as co-director on Instrument in the Discogs record — Cohen is the sole director credited there.

The North Star Deserter production credit is notable: it’s one of Cohen’s few explicitly listed producing roles, and it brought together key members of the Montreal post-rock scene with the Fugazi/DC orbit (Picciotto) around Chesnutt’s late work.

The Ex / Building A Broken Mousetrap (2007)

Cohen directed Building A Broken Mousetrap, a concert film of The Ex, released on Ex Records and Touch And Go in 2007. He is credited as Director, Camera Operator (16mm and DV), Film Editor, Film Producer (“A Film By”), Design, Photography, and Liner Notes — an unusually comprehensive solo-auteur credit set for a live release. Co-filmmaker Matt Boyd shared camera and editing credits; audio mixed and mastered by Andy Moor (2) and Colin McLean. This parallels his Instrument work in scope: a long-form documentary of an experimental band by someone embedded in their world.

Ashes Of American Flags (2009)

Ashes Of American Flags (2009, Nonesuch) is a Wilco concert documentary. The Discogs credit on the US pressing (release 5813454) lists Directors as Brendan Canty and Cristoph Green — Cohen is not credited on that pressing. The stub note about Cohen co-directing appears to be based on external sources or a different pressing; it is not confirmed by Discogs data. Canty and Green are also credited as Film Producers. Design by Cristoph Green; photography by Zoran Orlic.
