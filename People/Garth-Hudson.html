Garth Hudson

Eric Garth Hudson (born 2 August 1937, Windsor, Ontario, Canada; died 21 January 2025, Woodstock, New York) was a Canadian multi-instrumentalist — organ, accordion, piano, synthesizer, clavinet, soprano and tenor and baritone saxophone, trumpet — and the principal keyboard architect of The Band. The only classically trained member of the group, Hudson was hired by Ronnie Hawkins on condition that he also teach music theory to the rest of the Hawks; he went on to define the sound of The Band’s recordings through his distinctive Hammond organ swells, Lowrey organ work, and his layered approach to texture and orchestration. After The Band’s formal end at The Last Waltz in 1976 (and again through the reunions of the 1980s and 1990s) Hudson remained one of the most in-demand session players in the Woodstock orbit, playing on records ranging from Bob Dylan and Neil Young archival sets to alt-country records (Neko Case) and indie rock (Mercury Rev). His only official solo album, The Sea To The North (2001), was released on his own Breeze Hill imprint. Discogs artist ID: 252931. Also credited as Campo Malaqua (Discogs ID: 4998756).

Campo Malaqua

Hudson used the alias “Campo Malaqua” on at least two releases:

  • 2nd Right 3rd Row (Eric Von Schmidt, 1972, Poppy) — accordion
  • Sweet Dreams And Quiet Desires (artist: Campo Malaqua, 1973, Avalanche) — organ
  • Sweet Dreams And Quiet Desires / The Second Album (2013 reissue, Real Gone Music)

The 1973 album is credited to Campo Malaqua as the primary artist (Discogs ID 4998756), suggesting this was an intentional pseudonym, possibly to manage contractual obligations.

Research Queue

  • What instruments does the sleeve of Ringo (1973, Apple) list for Hudson? Discogs says “Accordion” but his contribution to a Ringo Starr record has never been fully documented in secondary sources.
  • The Discogs credit on Ringo Starr And His All-Starr Band (1990, EMI) lists Hudson under “Guitar” — is this a data error on the sleeve or a transcription error? Hudson is not known as a guitarist.
  • What tracks specifically does Hudson appear on in Sweet Dreams And Quiet Desires (1973, Campo Malaqua)? Discogs only lists “Organ” with no track listing.
  • The Over The Edge (2000, Breeze Hill) credit lists Hudson as “Organ; Producer [Produced Solo]; Synthesizer” — was this a solo production credited entirely to him, or a group session? No other performers are listed in the Discogs entry.
  • Beautiful Noise (1976, Neil Diamond / CBS) — what tracks does Hudson appear on? Discogs lists only the release credit, not track-level attribution.
  • Hirth From Earth (1975, Hirth Martinez, Warner Bros.) — Discogs credits Hudson on Electric Piano, Upright Piano, Organ [Lowry]. Was this a full-album contribution or partial?
  • The credit for Northern Lights-Southern Cross (1975) lists Hudson as “Engineer [Mixing]” rather than as a performer — did he mix the record as well as play on it, or is this purely an engineering credit?
  • On The Call (1982) and Modern Romans (1983) Hudson is credited with synthesizer and saxophone — was this a sustained working relationship with the band or two separate one-off sessions?
  • Kent State (1981 film soundtrack, RCA) — did Hudson compose or only perform keyboard parts? Discogs lists “Keyboards” with no further detail.
  • What is the full track listing of The Sea To The North (2001), and which tracks feature Levon Helm vs. the other drummers (Dan Brubeck, Gary Burke, Scott Musick)?
