Floating Points

Real name: Sam Shepherd (also credited as SS, S. Shepherd, Samuel Thomas Shepherd)
Discogs ID (Sam Shepherd / real name): 1544337
Discogs ID (Floating Points / performing alias): 1385084
Origin: Manchester, UK; based in London
Active: 2009–present
Labels: Eglo Records → Pluto (4) → Ninja Tune / Luaka Bop

Electronic musician, DJ, producer, and multi-instrumentalist. Co-founded Eglo Records as its first artist in 2009. Known for bridging deep house, jazz, and orchestral composition; leads the Floating Points Ensemble (16-piece live group). Also a member of A Pulse Train, a modular-synth collective.

Early Career & Eglo Records (2009–2014)

Sam Shepherd launched Floating Points with a debut 7” — “For You” (2009, Eglo LGOO1) — and followed quickly with the Vacuum EP (2009, Eglo EGL002). Both releases were co-released with early Eglo partner Jamie Mack. Artwork on these early releases by Kay Shin. The Shadows EP (2011, Eglo EGLO22, 2×12”) marked a breakthrough, featuring Fatima Bramme Sey on vocals (“Myrtle Avenue”) and a string section on “Sais” (Charlotte Bonneton, Magda Pietraszewska, Mariko McTier, Qian Wu, Tsze Yenn Yong). Mastering/lacquer on early Eglo releases by John Dent.

The Floating Points Ensemble debuted in 2010 with Post Suite / Almost In Profile (Ninja Tune, recorded at Abbey Road), winning the Gilles Peterson Worldwide Award for Best BBC Radio 1 Maida Vale Session that year.

Additional early Eglo singles: Peoples Potential (2010), Marilyn (2011), Danger (2011), King Bromeliad (2014), Nuits Sonores / Nectarines (2014).

Elaenia and the Live Band (2015–2017)

Elaenia (2015, Luaka Bop / Pluto LB0085LP / FPLP01) — debut LP. Written, recorded, and mixed entirely by Sam Shepherd; mastered by John Dent, lacquer by Matt Colton. Artwork by Jason Evans (2) and Sam Shepherd. Styles: Future Jazz, Ambient, Leftfield.

Track-level personnel (Discogs):

  • “Silhouettes (I, II & III)”: Susumu Mukai (bass), Joe Zeitlin (cello), Tom Skinner (drums), Matt Kettle (2) (viola), Edward Brenton + Qian Wu (violin), Layla Rutherford + Rahel Dessalegne (voice)
  • “For Marmish”: Joe Zeitlin (cello), Leo Taylor (drums), Alex Reeve (guitar), Matt Kettle (2) (viola), Edward Brenton + Qian Wu (violin)
  • “Peroration Six”: Susumu Mukai (bass), Joe Zeitlin (cello), Leo Taylor (drums), Alex Reeve (guitar), Matt Kettle (2) (viola), Edward Brenton + Qian Wu (violin)
  • Solo tracks (“Nespole,” “Elaenia,” “Argenté,” “Thin Air”): Sam Shepherd on all instruments

The live band that toured Elaenia and recorded Kuiper (2016) and Reflections - Mojave Desert (2017) comprised:

  • Leo Taylor (drums; alias Leo Invisible)
  • Susumu Mukai (bass; alias Zongamin)
  • Alex Reeve (guitar)
  • Matthew Kirkis (guitar, ARP Odyssey; alias Kirkis)

Kuiper (2016, Pluto FP02, 12”): “Kuiper” (A-side, with full band) / “For Marmish Part II” (B-side, solo). Styles: Broken Beat, Contemporary Jazz, Krautrock. Sam Shepherd plays Buchla 200e, Oberheim OB-B, ARP Odyssey, Korg Volca Beats, Fender Rhodes, vibraphone, vocals. String player Anisa Arslanagic on “Kuiper” A-side; tape op Jack Sugden. Lacquer cut by John Dent (as “Jonz”).

Ratio (Deconstructed Mixes) (2017, Pluto FPL03V, 12”): Two long-form DJ tools — “Ratio (Pt. II Organ)” (18:58) and “Ratio (Pt. II Dub)” (7:44). Styles: Ambient, Deep House, House. Lacquer cut by Matt Colton. Also released as full mix (FPL03D) and radio edit (FPL03R) digital singles.

Reflections - Mojave Desert (2017, Pluto RE001DX) — live LP + DVD-Video. Recorded in Mojave Desert. Full live band; Sam Shepherd on Buchla 200e, EMS Synthi, Rhodes Chroma, Oberheim OB-8, Hammond organ, Fender Rhodes, Telinga Parabolic Microphone.

Marhaba (2015, Border Community 46BCD) — collaborative EP with Maleem Mahmoud Ghania and James Holden. Floating Points performs on “Mimoun Marhaba” only; the other three tracks pair Ghania with Holden.

Crush (2019) and LateNightTales (2019)

Crush (2019, Ninja Tune ZEN259X) — second LP. Styles: Dubstep, House, UK Garage. Tracks: “Falaise,” “Last Bloom,” “Anasickmodular,” “Requiem for CS70 And Strings,” “Karakul,” “LesAlpx,” “Bias,” “Environments,” “Birth,” “Sea-Watch,” “Apoptose PT1 / PT2.” Credits sparse on Discogs — no additional personnel listed.

Also curated/mixed a volume of the LateNightTales compilation series (2019, multiple pressings: LP UK, US Club Ed., Japan on Beat Records, digital).

Promises with Pharoah Sanders & the LSO (2021)

Promises (2021, Luaka Bop LBOP097) — collaborative LP with Pharoah Sanders and the London Symphony Orchestra. Single continuous composition in 9 movements across two vinyl sides.

  • Composed and arranged by Sam Shepherd
  • Conducted by Sally Herbert
  • Pharoah Sanders — tenor saxophone, voice
  • Sam Shepherd — piano, harpsichord, celesta, Fender Rhodes, Oberheim 4-Voice, OB-Xa, Solina String Ensemble, Therevox ET-4.3, EMS Synthi, ARP 2600, Buchla 200e
  • Recorded at Lyndhurst Hall (LSO sessions); engineer John Prestage, assisted by Ashley Andrew-Jones and Gianluca Massimo
  • Mixed by Sam Shepherd, assisted by Tim Pennells
  • Lacquer cut by Chris Bellman (Bernie Grundman Mastering)
  • Cover painting Congress by Julie Mehretu
  • Executive producers: Eric Welles-Nyström and Yale Evelev (Luaka Bop)

Cascade (2024) and Recent Work

Cascade (2024, Ninja Tune ZEN303, 2×LP) — third LP. Styles: Ambient, House, Techno. Guest appearances:

  • Dan Snaith (Caribou) — drums on “Fast Forward”
  • Stella Mozgawa — drums on “Afflecks Palace”
  • Hikaru Utada — voice on “Tilt Shift / Ablaze”
  • Miriam Adefris — harp on “Ocotillo”
  • Hannah Ekholm — voice on “Afflecks Palace”
  • Mastered by Matt Colton; artwork by Akiko Nakayama; design by Matthew Cooper (2)

Instruments across album: Yamaha CS-80, CS70, Rhodes Chroma, Buchla 200e, Therevox ET-4.3, ARP 2600, Sequential Circuits Pro One, PPG Wave 2.2, Soma Pulsar, Roland TR-808, TR-8S, Korg MS-20, Modosc-ID700, Nord Modular G2, Solina String Ensemble, Clavichord.

Ambery (2025, Atlantic) — featuring Fred again.. (Fred Gibson). Also credited on the single “Facilita / Ambery” with Fred again.., Dan Snaith, and Menor Teteu.

Lazarus (2025) — original series soundtrack, Milan / [Adult Swim].

A Pulse Train

Member of A Pulse Train (ID 5637257), a modular synth collective on Tactus Tempus. Sam Shepherd plays Yamaha CS80. Other members: Dan Snaith (Caribou), James Shaw (3), Jason Borys, Jeremy Greenspan (Junior Boys), Joe Goddard (Hot Chip), Ryan Smith (12), Sasha Lewis (2).
