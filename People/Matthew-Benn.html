Matthew Benn

Discogs ID: 5423591 Also known as: MB, Matt Benn Alias: Xam (6) (Discogs ID: 4762738) — solo credit as Xam (6) within XAM Duo Role: Bassist, recording engineer, synthesizer

Leeds-based bassist and recording engineer. Member of Hookworms across all three studio LPs (2013–2018), Holodrum, Remover, and XAM Duo (as Xam (6), with Christopher Duffin). Also works as engineer/producer for external artists.

Engineering / Production work

Beyond his performance credits, Benn has established a consistent role as recording engineer and mixer:

  • Hookworms — from Microshift (2018) onward, he shared recording duties alongside Matthew Johnson (12)
  • Nape Neck — engineered both their 2020 debut and the 2022 Look Alive EP (Leeds indie band)
  • Holodrum — recorded and mixed the 2022 self-titled LP
  • XAM Duo — recorded and mixed Xam Duo II (2022)
  • Drahla — recorded and mixed Angeltape (2024, Captured Tracks), alongside co-producer Christopher Duffin
