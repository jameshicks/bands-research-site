Dai Fujikura

Japanese-British contemporary classical composer. Born in Osaka, Japan; resident in the UK for over 20 years. Discogs artist ID: 1597167. Name variations: Daï Fujikura, Fujikura, 藤倉大.

Overview

Fujikura studied composition in London with Edwin Roxburgh (Discogs: 2585229), Daryl Runswick (Discogs: 321583), and George Benjamin (Discogs: 212272). His compositional aesthetic centres on forms and patterns drawn from the natural world — synchronized movement, geometric precision — realized through advanced extended playing techniques that cause textures to develop, transmute, and fragment.

His Discogs footprint sits primarily in the contemporary classical world: orchestral and chamber works released on Japanese labels (Commmons, Sony Music Labels/Sony Records Int’l) alongside European new-music labels. His jazz-adjacent appearance — as performer rather than composer — consists of two specific projects with Norwegian producer Jan Bang.

Scene Connections

Contemporary classical / new music: The bulk of Fujikura’s discography is squarely in the international contemporary music circuit — works performed by the Tokyo Metropolitan Symphony, Nagoya Philharmonic, BBC Symphony, London Sinfonietta, International Contemporary Ensemble, Ensemble Modern, Prague Modern, and Ensemble Nomad. His Japanese label home has been Commmons and Sony Music Labels/Sony Records Int’l. In the European new-music world he connects to the NMC Jerwood Series and to compilations featuring Gérard Grisey, Luciano Berio, Olga Neuwirth, Unsuk Chin, Anna Thorvaldsdottir, Bruno Mantovani.

Jan Bang / Jazzland / Punkt world: Fujikura crossed into this orbit twice. First as a sampled/electronics contributor to Jan Bang’s Narrative From The Subtropics (Jazzland, 2013) — a curated album that drew from sources including Arve Henriksen, Nils Petter Molvær, Sidsel Endresen, Tigran Hamasyan, and Eivind Aarset. Second as co-primary artist on The Bow Maker (Punkt Editions, 2022), where he assembled, edited, and mixed alongside Jan Bang as producer. The Punkt connection is organisational (Punkt Editions is the label of the Punkt festival, Kristiansand) as well as sonic.

David Sylvian / Samadhisound: A single credit — string arrangement for track 10 of Sylvian’s Manafon (2009), a major late Sylvian album featuring Keith Rowe, Fennesz, Evan Parker, Toshimaru Nakamura, John Tilbury.
