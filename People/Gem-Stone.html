Gem Stone

Bassist, vocalist. Real name: Gemma Sansom. Alias: Samantha Dog. Discogs artist ID: 695199

Overview

Founding bassist and vocalist of Rubella Ballet (1979). Also a member of Fatal Microbes. Daughter of Vi Subversa of Poison Girls, and sister of Pete Fender. A core part of the original Rubella Ballet lineup on the debut single Ballet Dance (1982) and the Ballet Bag cassette, and returned for the 2008 retrospective compilation Anarchy In The U.V.

Also credited as a member of the Lenya Hobnoobs Theatre Company. She provided additional vocals on Poison Girls’ Chappaquiddick Bridge (Crass Records, 1980) and Where’s The Pleasure (Xntrix, 1982), and backing vocals on the 2015 Persons Unknown single (All The Madmen Records).
