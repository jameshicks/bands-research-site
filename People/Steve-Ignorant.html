Oct 23, 20269 min read

Steve Ignorant

Discogs ID: 288071 Real name: Steve Williams Born: 1957, Stoke-on-Trent (grew up in Dagenham, East London) Alias: Grant Mitchell (3) (Discogs ID: 1656831 — confirmed same person; the Grant Mitchell entry lists Steve Ignorant as its alias)

Singer and artist. Co-founder and lead vocalist of anarchist punk collective Crass (1977–1984). After Crass disbanded he worked with a range of post-punk projects and returned to performing Crass material live from 2007. In 2007 he moved to the Norfolk coast and began volunteering as a lifeboatman in Sea Palling. He is also a sculptor and a traditional Punch and Judy performer, working under the name Professor Ignorant. (Wikipedia)

Early Life and Crass

Ignorant (born Steven Williams) grew up in Dagenham, East London, later living at Dial House — the self-sustaining anarchist-pacifist intentional community in Epping Forest, Essex, established in 1967. There he met Penny Rimbaud, one of Dial House’s founders, and the two co-founded Crass in 1977. (Wikipedia)

With Crass, Ignorant served as primary vocalist and a central creative force across the band’s seven-year run. The group operated on the Crass Records imprint (later also via Small Wonder Records) and became the defining band of anarcho-punk. Crass stopped performing in 1984.

Post-Crass and Collaborations (1984–2005)

After Crass, Ignorant contributed vocals to several allied bands:

Conflict (2)

Appears as vocalist on at least two Conflict (2) albums: Turning Rebellion Into Money (Mortarhate Records, 1987) and The Final Conflict (Mortarhate Records, 1988). Conflict (2) was the Eltham anarcho-punk group fronted by Colin Jerwood; Ignorant is listed on the Discogs member roster alongside Jerwood. Discogs also credits him (contributor role) on earlier Conflict compilation appearances.

Nightmare Culture (1985)

Performed on “Killy Kill Killy (A Fire Sermon)” (Side A) on the Current 93 / Sickness of Snakes split Nightmare Culture (L.A.Y.L.A.H. Antirecords, LAY 14, Belgium). Credited as one of the “93 Current 93 May Be” ensemble alongside David Tibet, Boyd Rice, John Balance, Steven Stapleton, John Murphy, and others. Note: the Sickness of Snakes B-side (Boyd Rice, John Balance, Peter Christopherson) is a separate lineup; Ignorant appears only on the Current 93 A-side.

Schwartzeneggar

Member alongside Andi Tuck, Ben Corrigan, Bob Butler (2), and Mark Pickstone (who also appears in Conflict). Some of Ignorant’s Stratford Mercenaries-era material credits music “by Schwartzeneggar.”

Stratford Mercenaries

Members: Steve Ignorant, Ed Da Fed, Gary (19), Philip Barker. Released:

  • Live In The U.S.A. 1997 (self-released, 1997)
  • Sense Of Solitude (Southern Records, 2000)

Crass Agenda

A later-period project reuniting several Crass members alongside jazz musicians. Members include Penny Rimbaud, Gee Vaucher, Pete Wright, N.A. Palmer, Eve Libertine (credited as Bronwen Jones (2)), J J Ratter, plus jazz players Julian Siegel, Gene Calderazzo, Ingrid Laubrock, Dylan Bates, and others. Released Penny Rimbaud’s HOW? (Babel / Exitstencil Press, 2004).

Live Crass Performances (2007–2011)

In November 2007 Ignorant performed Crass’s The Feeding of the 5000 in full at Shepherd’s Bush Empire, backed by guest musicians including Graham Butt (a.k.a. Gizz Butt), Bob Butler (2), and Spikey T. Smith. The performances generated public comment from Penny Rimbaud, who acknowledged but described them as a “betrayal of the Crass ethos.” (Wikipedia)

By 2010, after a joint Rebellion Festival appearance with Rimbaud, Ignorant received Rimbaud’s public blessing to perform Crass material. He also published his autobiography The Rest Is Propaganda (Southern Records / co-written with Steve Pottinger, 2010).

On 19 November 2011 he performed “The Last Supper” at Shepherd’s Bush Empire, joined on stage by Rimbaud and Eve Libertine — the last time he performed Crass songs live until 2020. The 2011 show is documented as The Last Supper, Shepherd’s Bush Empire 19 November 2011 (Discogs).

The 2007 show is documented as The Feeding Of The 5000 (Shepherd’s Bush Empire 24-25 November 2007).

Steve Ignorant With Paranoid Visions (2013–2021)

The most active phase of Ignorant’s post-Crass solo output was a run of LPs and EPs recorded with Irish anarcho-punk band Paranoid Visions (Discogs ID: 522797). Core Paranoid Visions players across these releases include vocalist/lyricist Deko Dachau (Discogs ID: 1868940), guitarist P.A. Jesu (Discogs ID: 522799), and contributions from Aoife Destruction, Steo (3), and others. Harvey Birrell (Discogs ID: 73361) mastered most of the records.

Slice Of Life (4) (2013–present)

Slice Of Life (4) (also credited as “Steve Ignorant’s Slice Of Life”) was formed while Ignorant, Carol Hodge (3), and Pete Wilson (3) were stranded in Sydney after a tour. The band debuted live in Wellingborough, UK, on 25 October 2013. Lucas Martin joined on upright bass in 2015. Line-up: Steve Ignorant, Carol Hodge (3), Pete Wilson (3), Pete Rawlinson, Lucas Martin. (Wikipedia)

The Steve Ignorant Band (Discogs ID: 10416286) is a related vehicle with overlapping membership (Carol Hodge (3), Jay Bagnall, Pete Rawlinson, Pete Wilson (3)).

Later Projects and Collaborations
