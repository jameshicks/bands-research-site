Connection to Dining Out Records

Via A Certain Ratio and Martha Tilson: Tilson moved from the Dining Out Records scene to Factory / ACR, where Wilson was manager.

Key Collaborator: Martin Hannett

Wilson brought Martin Hannett in as Factory’s in-house producer from the label’s first releases (1978). Hannett produced Joy Division, New Order, A Certain Ratio, Section 25, The Durutti Column, and OMD for Factory through 1981. The two had a severe falling-out when Hannett sued Factory in 1982 over unpaid royalties. Wilson later reconciled with Hannett sufficiently to bring him back to produce Happy Mondays’ Bummed in 1988.

Key Collaborator: Mike Pickering / T-Coy / Haçienda

Mike Pickering was central to the Haçienda’s transformation from loss-making post-punk venue into the epicentre of UK house music. He had been a member of Quando Quango (a Factory-signed dance act) and joined the Haçienda as a resident DJ, running the Nude Friday night from approximately 1985–86 onwards (sources vary on the exact start year; see conflict note below). Nude played early Chicago and Detroit house records before the acid house explosion, and was reportedly consistently 400 under capacity — i.e. turning people away — by 1987. (Wikipedia: Mike Pickering; British Culture Archive)

T-Coy was a Manchester house trio formed by Pickering together with Simon Topping (also of A Certain Ratio and formerly of Quando Quango) and keyboardist/arranger Richie Close, after Quando Quango dissolved around 1986. The name stands for “Take Care Of Yourself.” They blended Chicago house with Latin percussion, funk, and jazz-dance influences — an approach sometimes tagged “Northern House.” Their releases came out on Deconstruction Records:

(LTM Recordings biography; Discogs master: T-Coy — Cariño)

Conflict — Nude night start year: The existing vault note (sourced from Wikipedia/Haçienda article) says Pickering co-hosted Nude “from 1986.” The LTM Recordings T-Coy biography says “from 1984 onwards.” Search results reference 1985 as another possibility. The FAC51 official history lists a Nude night in 1985. The 1984 date in LTM appears to be the earlier Pickering DJ residency before Nude was branded; 1985–86 is the more widely corroborated start for Nude specifically. Treat as unresolved pending a primary source.

Conflict — Cariño release year: Discogs release pages list the original 12” as 1987. LTM biography says “June 1988.” The 1987 date appears on both Discogs release entries (r22597, r164345) and in secondary sources calling it “an all-time UK acid house classic from 1987.” LTM’s June 1988 date may refer to a repress. Discogs 1987 preferred.
