Oct 14, 20263 min read

Simon Fisher Turner

Discogs ID: 50237

UK composer and musician, closely associated with filmmaker Derek Jarman (scored The Garden, Blue, and other Jarman films). Also a key early figure at Él: his sound-sketch LP Caravaggio 1610 (Sound Sketches For Michele Of The Shadows) (ACME 6, 1986) was one of the label’s signature early albums, and he served as producer on other Él releases (14 producer credits on the label overall). 5 releases on Soleilmoon Recordings (2011–2024). Key Soleilmoon title: The Great White Silence (SOL 176 CD, 2011). Also released Savage Songs Of Brutality And Food… (SOL 190 CD, 2020) with Isabella Fisher Turner and Jasper Fisher Turner.

Peter Christopherson’s posthumous Soleilmoon release (Live At L’Étrange Festival 2004 — The Art Of Mirrors (Homage To Derek Jarman), SOL 196 CD, 2018) also carries the Jarman connection.

Additional Credits

  • Member of Kendall Turner Overdrive with Paul Kendall — released Displaced Links (1997, Mute / Parallel Series)
  • Remixed Derek Jarman’s The Last Of England (OST) (1987, Mute) with Paul Kendall
  • Members: also Deux Filles (Discogs ID: 123672), Jeremy’s Secret, Rhythmic Shudder, The Hangovers
  • Alias “Claudine Coule” appears as a vocalist on the The Last Of England OST

Tape compilations

  • A Can Of Worms — The Tapeworm TTW#100, 2017
