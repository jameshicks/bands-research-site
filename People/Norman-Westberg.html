Norman Westberg

Guitarist. Born June 27, 1958, Detroit. Based in New York City. Active c.1982–present. Discogs artist ID: 269726.

Longest-serving guitarist in Swans, present from the debut Filth (1983) through dissolution (1997) and in the reformed band from 2010 onward. One of only two members (with Michael Gira) to span both eras of the band. Since the early 2010s he has maintained an active solo career in ambient/drone guitar, releasing extensively through Australian label Room40 and Swiss imprint Hallow Ground, with mastering consistently handled by Lawrence English.

Background

Westberg grew up in Detroit and relocated to New York City in the early 1980s. He joined Swans around 1982, appearing on the debut album Filth (recorded 1982, released 1983 on Neutral Records/Zensor). He remained with Swans through the entire original run (1983–1997), and re-joined when Michael Gira reformed the band in 2010.

Swans (1982–1997, 2010–present)

Westberg’s guitar work spans the full arc of Swans’ sound — from the crushing, repetitive riff structures of early records (Filth, Cop, Greed, Holy Money) to the more textural and acoustic-inflected playing of the transitional period (Children Of God, The Burning World), through the expansive atmospheric work of the final original-era albums (White Light From The Mouth Of Infinity, Love Of Life, The Great Annihilator, Soundtracks For The Blind). He is listed with guitar, electric guitar, and acoustic guitar credits across nearly every release, and also contributed banjo on “Saved” (1989), bass on “Can’t Find My Way Home” (1989), and occasional photography credits.

Solo Discography

Westberg began issuing solo material in 2012. His solo work is long-form drone and ambient guitar — typically improvised or composed pieces in the 10–30 minute range, released on vinyl, CDr, cassette, and digital formats.

Side Projects & Bands

The Heroine Sheiks (c.2000–2008)

Post-punk/noise rock band featuring Westberg on guitar alongside vocalist Shannon Selberg, drummer John Fell, bassist George Porfiris, and keyboardist Scott Hill/Eric Eble. Released at least two records during Westberg’s tenure:

  • Rape On The Installment Plan (2000, Reptilian Records) — full LP
  • Best Enemies / Oucha (2001, Rubric Records) — CD single

Sugartime (1991)

NYC band. Westberg contributed vocals and guitar on the 7” single Girlcrash b/w Psych-Mail (1991, Ringers Lactate), recorded at Wharton Tiers’ studio.

Crippled And The Burnout (1993)

NYC post-punk band. Westberg played guitar on the 7” New York (1993, American Gothic Records).

Five Dollar Priest (2008)

Spanish-released LP Five Dollar Priest (Bang! Records, 2008). Wide cast including James Chance (saxophone), Jon Spencer (theremin), Matt Verta-Ray (slide guitar), Bob Bert (drums). Westberg on guitar.

Gin Mill (1994)

Westberg contributed guitar on track “Ex-Patriot” (B1) on the 12” EP Patron (1994, Wreck-Age Records).

Sulfur

NYC-based band. Westberg is listed as a member. Members include Dan Joeright (drums), David Ouimet, Nick Heathen, and others. Discogs artist ID: 292921.

Scarcity Of Tanks

Large-ensemble experimental/free music collective. Members include Jim Sauter, Tom Herman, John Colpitts (of Oneida), Weasel Walter, Scott Krauss, and others. Discogs artist ID: 652848.

ALN (2016)

Trio with Algis A. Kizys (bass, vocals) and Lynn Wright (guitar). Released Something In The Water (2016, Sacred Phrases, cassette). Long-form drone/ambient. Discogs artist ID: 5470815.

A/N (2) (2024)

Duo with Algis A. Kizys. Released Blindspot (2024, Secret Records (9)). Alternative rock/hard rock. Discogs artist ID: 14593705.

Everyday Life Activities / Westberg/Udupa/Mazurkiewicz (2025)

Three-way collaboration with Giridhar Udupa and Jacek Mazurkiewicz. LP + bonus EP Everyday Life Activities (2025, Don’t Sit On My Vinyl! / Gusstaff Records, Poland). Avant-garde jazz/experimental.

Crisis (8) — “The Hollowing” (1997)

Guest guitarist credited as “Vomit Guitar” on tracks 1, 3, and 10 of the Crisis (8) album The Hollowing (1997, Metal Blade Records).

Mirror Queen — “Verdigris” (2017)

Guest guitar on track “Sorrow’s End/Dark Kiss Of The Sun” (A3) on Mirror Queen’s LP Verdigris (2017, Tee Pee Records).
