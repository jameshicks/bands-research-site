Mike Green (9)

Discogs artist ID: 1385065. Real name Michael Barkley Green (also credited as Michael Green, Michael Greene, Michael “Mikey G” Green, Michael Barley Green, Michael Berkley Green). American drummer and vocalist from Wabash, Indiana, based in Los Angeles. Stage name Marc Samson (alias ID: Marc Samson (2)) — used as his frontman persona in Fan Fiction (7).

Biography

Mike Green is an LA-based session drummer who operates primarily in the indie-pop/soft-rock orbit of mid-2000s and 2010s Los Angeles. His Discogs footprint runs from 2006 to 2019, spanning a compact but varied slate of sessions: from folk duos to comedy records to art-pop projects.

His most notable credit is drums on Headless Heroes’ The Silence of Love (2008), where he shared the drum chair with Joey Waronker — a project assembled by producer/engineer Hugo Nicolson and featuring an all-star West Coast cast including Josh Klinghoffer, Gus Seyffert, Leo Abrahams, Woody Jackson, and Dirty Pretty Strings. The Headless Heroes project was Waronker-adjacent and Green’s most high-profile placement in the Discogs record.

The same year (2008) he drummed and sang backing vocals on Willoughby’s I Know What You’re Up To (Sargent Records) alongside Waronker and Seyffert again — suggesting a recurring West Coast session circle. He also contributed drums to Obi Best’s Capades (Social Science Recordings, 2008), a record produced by Alex Lilly with Bram Inscore.

Green’s backing vocal credit on Sia’s Some People Have Real Problems (Hear Music, 2008) — again alongside Waronker and Seyffert — places him firmly in a tight-knit LA session network that orbited the Joey Waronker/Gus Seyffert axis in that period.

Later credits include drums on Tim Heidecker & Wood’s Some Things Never Stay The Same (Little Record Company, 2013) — a collaboration with Tim Heidecker — and percussion/backing vocals on several smaller records through 2019.

As Marc Samson, Green leads Fan Fiction (7), a Los Angeles soft rock band billed as being from “Prism City” (a fictionalised LA). The band also features keyboardist “Jeff Honeycutt” (real name Tyler Cash). Their debut LP Anticipated Hits was self-released in 2022 on vinyl.
