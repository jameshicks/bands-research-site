Overview

Fran Gayo is a songwriter from Gijón, Spain, whose creative life extends well beyond music into cinema — as a programmer and festival director — and poetry. His discographic footprint runs from 1994 into the late 2000s, concentrated across lo-fi, shoegaze, and chanson, with most releases on Acuarela and Darla Records. He was a member of both Cambaral and Mus, contributing clarinet, autoharp, melodica, and choir vocals, with Mónica Vacas and Luigi Navarro among his closest recurring collaborators.
