Johannes Malfatti

Composer, arranger, musician. Discogs ID: 279461. Alias: Yoyush (no Discogs ID listed). French-based; closely associated with Olivier Alary.

Overview

Core member of Olivier Alary’s Ensemble project (ID: 432), credited from the 2006 FatCat phase onwards. Provided orchestral arrangements and mixing on the “Disown, Delete” 7” and Ensemble LP (2006), co-composed and produced Envies D’Avalanches (2010) and Excerpts (2011), and co-composed much of the Excerpts track listing. Also part of Chlorgeschlecht (ID: 162986) alongside Alary and Alex Kloster, documented on Unyoga (Deco, 2003).

In 2020, co-billed with Alary on u,i (130701, DA13-42) — an album of chamber/electronic pieces featuring Kristina Koropecki (cello), Erik Hove (flute, clarinet), Daniel Beilschmidt (organ), John Corban (violin), Hyunjung Ko (vocals), and Julián Alary García (vocals).

Also credited as Johannes Malfatti (2) (ID: 4613911 — viola) on Empire Of Time (Find The Others, Forward Music Group, 2015), indicating Discogs splits the credit between two IDs.
