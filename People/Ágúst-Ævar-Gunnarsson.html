Ágúst Ævar Gunnarsson

Discogs ID: 509724 Origin: Iceland Role: Drums, graphic design Name variations on Discogs: A. Gunnarsson · Agust Aevar · Agust Aevar Gunnarsson · Agust Gunnarsson · Ágúst Aevar Gunnarsson · Augst Gunnarsson · August Aevar Gunnarsson · August Gunnarsson · Gunnarsson · Ágúst Gunnarsson · Ágúst Ævar

Founding drummer of Sigur Rós. One of the three members who formed the group in Reykjavík in January 1994. Present on the debut album Von (1997). Departed from Sigur Rós around 1999 — after or during the sessions for Ágætis Byrjun — and was replaced by Orri Páll Dýrason. Went on to study and practice graphic design.

Biography

Formation of Sigur Rós (1994)

Jón Þór Birgisson (Jónsi; vocals, guitar), Georg Hólm (bass), and Ágúst Ævar Gunnarsson (drums) formed Sigur Rós in January 1994 in Reykjavík. The band name means “Victory Rose” in Icelandic, named after Jónsi’s newborn sister, Sigurrós Elín Birgisdóttir.

Von (1997)

The three-piece recorded the debut album Von (Icelandic for “hope”) in 1997, releasing it on Smekkleysa (cat. SM 67 CD). All production duties — recording, editing, mixing, mastering, arrangement, design, and layout — were credited collectively to Sigur Rós as a band entity. The three members are individually named on the sleeve as “Sigur Rós Er” (Sigur Rós is): Ágúst Ævar Gunnarsson, Georg Hólm, and Jón Þór Birgisson. The album runs twelve roman-numeral-titled tracks across approximately 71 minutes; styles span ambient, shoegaze, experimental, and abstract.

The Von lineup thus represents the only Sigur Rós studio album on which Ágúst is a full performing and composing member.

Outside work: design credit for Pornopop’s Blue (1997)

In the same year as Von, Ágúst contributed design (Hönnun) and images (Myndir) to Blue, a self-released album by Icelandic group Pornopop (Discogs: 756325). This indicates an active involvement in the Reykjavík independent music scene beyond his drumming role in Sigur Rós, and an early orientation toward visual/graphic work.

Keyboard additions and departure (1998–1999)

Kjartan Sveinsson joined Sigur Rós on keyboards in 1998, expanding the group to a four-piece. The band then recorded Ágætis Byrjun with producer Ken Thomas. Ágúst is credited as songwriter (“All Songs Written By”) and performer (“Performed By”) on the 2019 twentieth-anniversary box set Ágætis Byrjun (A Good Beginning) (Krúnk), confirming compositional involvement on that album. However, the original 1999 pressing carried no individual credits and Ágúst departed around this time. Discogs documentation on the original release (ID 2115975) lists no individual performer credits at all.

According to the band’s own Discogs profile, the band “finished” in 1999 at release ID 2115975 — that is, it records that Ágúst’s tenure concluded with or around that release. He subsequently studied graphic design.

Orri Páll Dýrason replaced him on drums for all subsequent Sigur Rós recordings.

Post-Sigur Rós: graphic design and continued songwriting credits

After leaving the band, Ágúst shifted to visual work:

  • 2001 — Co-designed the cover of Motorlab #3 (Kitchen Motors / ÓMI) alongside Kristín Björk Kristjánsdóttir (Kristín Björk). Motorlab #3 featured Pan Sonic and Barry Adamson as primary artists, with liner notes and production contributions from Jóhann Jóhannsson. This placed Ágúst in the orbit of Kitchen Motors, the Reykjavík experimental arts organization that was central to Iceland’s avant-garde scene in the early 2000s.

As a songwriter, Ágúst continued to be credited on Sigur Rós releases where songs from the Von/Ágætis Byrjun era were performed or compiled:

  • 2007 — Hljómalind (EMI / Krúnk, 7” single): Written-By credit on the B-side, “Starálfur (Live Acoustic Version).” The A-side “Hljómalind” carries a Written-By credit to Orri Páll Dýrason (the post-Ágúst drummer).
  • 2011 — Inni (Krúnk, live album/film): Written-By credit on songs traceable to his compositional era.
  • 2018 — Klukk: Sigur Rós In Bells (Krúnk, digital): bells arrangement of Sigur Rós repertoire by the Bjöllukór Tónlistarskóli Reykjanesbæjar choir; Ágúst credited as Written-By for track 3, “Sæglópur.”
  • 2019 — Ágætis Byrjun (A Good Beginning) (Krúnk, 20th anniversary box set): Full songwriter and performer credit on the 1999 album tracks included in this box.

Design Work Context — Motorlab #3 (2001)

A collaborative Reykjavík project pairing Pan Sonic and Barry Adamson, produced under the Kitchen Motors imprint. Ágúst co-designed the cover and supplied photographs alongside Kristín Björk Kristjánsdóttir. Other contributors included Jóhann Jóhannsson (liner notes, mixing, production) and Magnús Blöndal Jóhannsson (brainwave artwork). This places Ágúst within the same network of Reykjavík experimental artists that produced the Kitchen Motors catalog.
