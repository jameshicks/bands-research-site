Jay-Z

Rapper, producer, and entrepreneur from Brooklyn, New York. Real name: Shawn Corey Carter. Discogs artist ID: 21742. Also known as HOV, Jigga, Shawn Carter, The Carter Administration.

Overview

One of the most commercially successful hip-hop artists of all time. Co-founded Roc-A-Fella Records and later Roc Nation. Worked with 9th Wonder on The Black Album (2003, Roc-A-Fella) — facilitated via Young Guru — and the Black American Gangster (2013) mixtape project. Eight shared Discogs releases.
