Eric Emm

Real name: Eric M. Topolsky Discogs ID: 658496 Name variations: Emm, Eric, Eric Topolsky, Erich Emm, M Eric Topolsky, M. Eric Topolsky

American multi-instrumentalist, bassist, producer, and engineer. Pittsburgh origin, later Brooklyn-based. Known primarily for his bass work in Don Caballero and Storm And Stress during the math-rock era, and as co-founder of Brooklyn indie-electronic duo Tanlines.

Era 1 — Pittsburgh (1996–2001)

Vehicle Flips — Ompompanoosuc (1996, Numeric Records/WPTS)

Emm’s earliest Discogs credit, as drummer (under his real name M. Eric Topolsky) on Vehicle Flips’ 1996 release. A Pittsburgh band outside the math-rock circuit.

Storm And Stress (1997–2000)

Emm joined Storm And Stress — Ian Williams’s experimental/free-jazz project alongside drummer Kevin Shea — replacing founding bassist George Draguns prior to the debut LP. Credited as M. Eric Topolsky.

The second Storm And Stress LP, recorded by Jim O’Rourke, pushed into more abstracted free-jazz territory than the debut.

Don Caballero — American Don (2000)

Emm entered the Don Caballero orbit through his Storm And Stress connection to Williams. When Pat Morris left for the second time following What Burns Never Returns (1998), Williams recruited Emm. By the time of American Don (2000, Touch And Go), Mike Banfield had also departed, leaving the band as a trio.

Era 2 — Production and Engineering (2004–2010)

After Don Caballero, Emm relocated to Brooklyn and shifted toward production and engineering. Much of this work was done in partnership with Joshua Ryan under the name The Brothers (8).

The Brothers (8) — Emm and Joshua Ryan — were a Brooklyn-based production team. Their most prominent credit was mixing multiple tracks on !!!‘s Myth Takes (2007, Warp), one of the band’s best-reviewed albums.

Golan Globus (Discogs 95487) was a separate side project with Joshua Ryan, described on Discogs as Joshua Ryan “directing his attention to alternative rock.” Two members: Emm and Ryan.

Era 3 — Tanlines (2008–present)

In 2008 Emm co-founded Tanlines in Brooklyn with Jesse Cohen (percussion). The duo play electronic-tinged indie rock — Emm on guitar, bass, vocals, and keyboards; Cohen on percussion and production.
