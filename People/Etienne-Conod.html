Etienne Conod

Swiss composer, musician, and producer. Discogs artist ID: 288314.

Overview

Etienne Conod is the founder and owner of Sunrise Studios (label ID: 265366) in Switzerland, and of the Sunrise (2) label (ID: 101045). He appears throughout the early Stephan Eicher catalog as engineer and co-producer, and is a recurring figure in the Swiss new wave/pop scene documented by Off Course Records.

In the 1988 compilation, the split is clear: Bahlman produced tracks 1–9 (the 1983 Les Chansons Bleues material), while Conod produced and arranged tracks 10–13 (the Souvenir/Noise Boys material), with Urs Steiger also co-producing. Conod also engineered the earlier recording sessions (1983), making him the likely studio-side contact who may have facilitated the Bahlman production.
