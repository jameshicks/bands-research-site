Dominique Diebold

Discogs ID: 765114
French producer and distributor. Co-founder and director of Recommended Records France, AAA, and Ayaa — three successive incarnations of the same Reims-based label/distributor operation. Active 1983–2020 per Discogs. Name variation: D.D. Also credited as a musician (drums, production co-credit) on Nimal’s 1987 self-titled debut.
