David Thomas (2)

Discogs ID: 180278 Real name: David Lynn Thomas Alias: Crocus Behemoth Born: June 14, 1953, Miami, FL Died: April 23, 2025, Brighton and Hove, East Sussex, England

American vocalist, songwriter, and musician. Core creative force behind Pere Ubu from their 1975 Cleveland origins through to his death in 2025. Also led Rocket From The Tombs and a long series of solo ensembles: David Thomas And The Pedestrians (1981–85), David Thomas And His Legs, David Thomas And Foreigners, David Thomas And The Wooden Birds, David Thomas And Two Pale Boys, The Pale Orchestra. Recorded and produced prolifically into his final years; last known release 2025.

Key projects

  • Pere Ubu — from 1975; albums on Rough Trade, Fontana, Tim/Kerr, Fire Records
  • Rocket From The Tombs — pre-Pere Ubu Cleveland proto-punk band, also featuring Peter Laughner
  • David Thomas And The Pedestrians — see that page for detail
  • David Thomas And The Wooden Birds — 1986–1987; two Rough Trade LPs (Monster Walks The Winter Lake, Blame The Messenger) and the Yugoslav cassette April U Beogradu (Nikad Robom, 1986)
  • Mirror Man (Act 1: Jack & The General) (Cooking Vinyl, 1999) — conducted The Pale Orchestra
  • Solo catalog — extensive on Hearthan, Cooking Vinyl, Glitterhouse, Cherry Red

Production Credits

  • The Pagans — produced “Not Now No Way” (1979, Drome DR5) and co-produced Buried Alive (1986, Treehouse Records (2)). Direct Cleveland underground cross-pollination between Pere Ubu and the Pagans axis.
