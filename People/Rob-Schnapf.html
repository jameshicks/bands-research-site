Rob Schnapf

Los Angeles-based record producer and audio engineer. Discogs artist ID: 258719.

Overview

Rob Schnapf is one of the most consistent presences in American indie and alternative record production since the early 1990s. Based in Los Angeles, he is co-founder of Bong Load Records (Discogs label ID: 18535) alongside Tom Rothrock (Discogs artist ID: 184359) — the label that broke Beck and served as the platform for several key early-decade alt-country and indie records. Schnapf worked roughly ten years at Sunset Sound (Discogs label ID: 269580), one of LA’s legendary recording studios, and later co-founded The Shop studios with Tom Rothrock (Discogs label ID: 276235). He currently runs his own studio operation under a separate imprint (Discogs label ID: 472976, not yet indexed in DB).

Schnapf’s signature work spans two dense collaborations: a years-long creative partnership with Beck beginning with Mellow Gold (1994) and extending through Odelay (1996), and an equally sustained relationship with Elliott Smith, producing XO (1998), Figure 8 (2000), and mixing the posthumous From A Basement On The Hill (2004). He also mixed the debut self-titled album by Foo Fighters (1995). His production style tends toward warm analogue fidelity with space for idiosyncratic arrangement; he frequently appears in guitarist or multi-instrumentalist roles on records he produces. From the 2000s onward his discography diversifies considerably — The Vines, Saves The Day, Guided By Voices, Cass McCombs, Kurt Vile, X (the LA punk band), and dozens of others. He produced two albums for French singer-songwriter H-Burns — Night Moves (2015, producer credit) and Kid We Own The Summer (2017, mix credit) — both released on Vietnam (2) / Because Music, marking one of the more unusual US–France connections in his catalog.

Name variations on Discogs include “R. Schnapf”, “Rob Schanpf”, “Rob Schnaff”, “Rob Schnapp”, “Rob Schnappf”, and several others — all credits are consolidated under artist ID 258719.

Selected Recurring Roles

Beyond producing, Schnapf regularly contributes as a performer on records he makes:

  • Guitar — credited on Highly Evolved (Vines), Lemon Cotton Candy Sunset (2017), Other You (2021), Red Ribbon (2024), ALPHABETLAND (X, 2020), Got No Shadow (1998)
  • Mellotron — Heretics (2015), Kuts (2015)
  • Synthesizer/Moog — Put Your Ghost To Rest (2006)
  • Keyboards, Percussion — Seizures (2009)

He is also credited with handclaps on the Vines’ Highly Evolved UK pressing (2002).

Key Artist Relationships

Tom Rothrock (Discogs ID: 184359) — Schnapf’s most consistent production partner. They co-produced and co-mixed across the bulk of the Beck and Elliott Smith catalogs: Mellow Gold, Odelay (partial), XO, Figure 8, the Foo Fighters debut mix. Also co-founded Bong Load Records together. They parted as a regular production team sometime after 2000.

Beck — relationship runs from 1993 (Mayday, Bong Load 7”) through Mellow Gold (1994), various singles (Loser, Beercan, Pay No Mind), Odelay (1996, one track), and continued singles. Schnapf was central to Beck’s early breakthrough period on Bong Load.

Elliott Smith — Schnapf’s most intensive creative partnership. Produced and recorded XO (1998) and Figure 8 (2000) as co-producer with Smith and Rothrock; mixed Either/Or (1997); worked on Smith’s early DreamWorks singles (Baby Britain, Waltz #2, Son Of Sam); mixed the posthumous From A Basement On The Hill (2004) alongside Joanna Bolme.

The Vines — produced two albums (Highly Evolved, 2002; Winning Days, 2004) and several associated singles for Capitol/EngineRoom, representing his most sustained non-Beck/Smith relationship of the 2000s.

H-Burns (Discogs artist ID: 782702) — French singer-songwriter (real name Renaud Brustlein). Schnapf produced Night Moves (2015) and mixed Kid We Own The Summer (2017), both released on Vietnam (2) / Because Music. Kid We Own The Summer was recorded by Denis Clavaizolle and Jean-Pierre Maillard in France; Schnapf’s role on that album was limited to mix.

X (5) — the original LA punk band (Exene Cervenka, John Doe, Billy Zoom, D.J. Bonebrake). Schnapf produced and engineered their 2020 reunion album ALPHABETLAND on Fat Possum, also contributing additional guitar.

Cass McCombs — recurring mixing and production work from 2016 onward (Cody, (Watch My Moves), Other You).

Cat Power — engineered and mixed Wanderer (2018) and Covers (2022), both on Domino.

Kurt Vile — recorded and mixed B’lieve I’m Goin Down… (2015, Matador), credited as “Mant Sounds” for the mix.
