Overview

Deakin (Joshua Caleb Dibb) is a guitarist, vocalist, and multi-instrumentalist, best known as a member of Animal Collective, the experimental psychedelic group whose catalogue runs from 2003 through the mid-2020s primarily on Domino and Paw Tracks. His 69 Discogs credits are dominated by work alongside AC bandmates Avey Tare, Geologist, and Panda Bear, with engineers Scott Colburn, Alan Douches, and Nicolas Vernhes appearing consistently across the collective’s output. He issued solo material under the My Animal Home imprint between 2016 and 2018, touching styles including Folk Rock and Post Rock beyond his group work.
