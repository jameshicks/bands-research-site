Overview

Alex Kapranos (born Alexander Paul Kapranos Huntley, 20 March 1972, Almondsbury, South Gloucestershire) is the guitarist and lead vocalist of Glasgow indie rock band Franz Ferdinand, whose catalogue on Domino Records runs from 2003 to the present. Before Franz Ferdinand he played in The Blisters and The Amphetameanies, with his Discogs credits reaching back to 1998. Beyond the main band he has appeared in FFS — the joint project with Sparks — and the multi-act supergroup BNQT, with his closest recurring collaborators being Franz Ferdinand bandmates Nick McCarthy, Robert Hardy, and Paul Thomson, alongside longtime producer Tore Johansson.

FFS collaboration — origins and timeline

The FFS pairing was initiated by Sparks. After hearing Franz Ferdinand’s “Take Me Out” on the radio in 2004, the Mael brothers looked up the band, found an interview in which Kapranos named Sparks as a major influence, and called him to propose writing together — specifically pitching a song called “Piss Off” for Franz Ferdinand to record. A meeting at a West Hollywood coffee shop followed, but nothing was recorded immediately because Franz Ferdinand’s touring schedule was too heavy. (Flood Magazine, 2015; NME feature, 2015)

The collaboration was revived by a chance street encounter in San Francisco in April 2013: Kapranos, searching for a dentist, ran into the Mael brothers, ended up at their gig that evening, and — in his own words — “did that thing musicians do: ‘Hey, you know, we should work together.‘” (Flood Magazine, 2015)

From that 2013 meeting the two bands wrote remotely, exchanging material by email between Glasgow and Los Angeles for roughly 18 months, before convening at RAK Studios in London for three weeks of live recording. The self-titled FFS album was released on Domino in June 2015. (Flood Magazine, 2015)

Timeline in brief:

  • 2004 — Sparks reach out after hearing “Take Me Out”; first meeting in West Hollywood; no recording results
  • April 2013 — Chance encounter in San Francisco reignites project
  • 2013–2015 — Remote songwriting (email, Glasgow ↔ Los Angeles) for ~18 months; recording at RAK Studios, London
  • June 2015 — FFS released on Domino Records; extensive tour follows, concluding in Oakland, November 2015

Pre-FF bands and the Franz Ferdinand founding narrative

Kapranos played in Glasgow acts the Blisters (later the Karelia), the Amphetameanies, and the Yummy Fur through the late 1990s while working as a bartender at The 13th Note and promoting at the Kazoo Club and the 99p Club (booking early Delgados and Mogwai gigs). He has described that period as one when “being in a band didn’t buy me my beans on toast.” (Wikipedia, Alex Kapranos article, as of 2026-10-09)

The founding of Franz Ferdinand is attributed not to those bands but to a separate context: Kapranos and Bob Hardy were working together in the kitchen of Groucho St Jude’s on Bath Street, Glasgow, and the band grew out of that friendship and the idea of playing parties. (Glasgow World, snippet only — page returned 403; corroborated by Wikipedia, Franz Ferdinand article, as of 2026-10-09)

No interview or feature found explicitly frames the Blisters or Amphetameanies as direct precursors to or a stepping stone into Franz Ferdinand. The accounts treat them as contemporaneous Glasgow-scene activity rather than a sequential build toward FF. The connection is biographical (same person, same city, overlapping years) but is not narrated as causal in any source checked.
