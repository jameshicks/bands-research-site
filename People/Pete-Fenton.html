Overview

Pete Fenton was a UK-based guitarist and early member of Siouxsie & the Banshees, dismissed mid-performance at London’s Dingwalls on 19 May 1977. His Discogs credits are concentrated in the late 1970s and are dominated by Banshees-related releases shared with Kenny Morris, Siouxsie Sioux, Steven Severin, and John McKay, primarily on Polydor. Beyond the Banshees he is associated with The Heroes (13) and The Jump, with scattered credits extending through the 1980s and into the 2000s. His roles span guitar, songwriting, keyboards, and production.

Dingwalls dismissal — May 1977

On 19 May 1977 Fenton was fired mid-performance at London’s Dingwalls. Siouxsie Sioux physically disconnected his guitar by pulling the lead out during a song. The immediate trigger, as she later described it, was an unsolicited extended guitar solo: “He’d gone into this terrible wailing guitar solo in the middle of a song, so I said, ‘That is fucking shit’ and pulled the lead out of his guitar.” A backstage fight followed, and Dingwalls management reportedly told the band they would never play there again. https://punk77.co.uk/band/siouxsie-and-the-banshees/

The broader reason given by Siouxsie for the incompatibility was stylistic: “Fenton didn’t really fit in. He had an orange lead on his guitar and that really annoyed me. He was a real rock guitarist, always trying to put licks into songs and pulling funny faces when he played. We spent most of the time trying to make him forget what he’d learned.” https://punk77.co.uk/band/siouxsie-and-the-banshees/

The quotes attributed to Siouxsie are widely reproduced across Banshees fan sources and are consistent in wording, suggesting a common origin. The most plausible primary source is Mark Paytress, Siouxsie & The Banshees: The Authorised Biography (Sanctuary, 2003), an oral history compiled from band-member interviews; this has not been confirmed by direct access to the book. https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/159689.Siouxsie_the_Banshees

Firsthand accounts. No published account from Fenton himself about the dismissal has been located. The fan-site forum at thebansheesandothercreatures.co.uk has a thread titled “Pete Fenton” (via Tapatalk) but it returned a 403 on access and its contents could not be checked. No statement from Steven Severin or Kenny Morris about the specific Dingwalls incident has surfaced in web-accessible sources.

John McKay replaced Fenton on guitar; his first gig with the band was at London’s Vortex on 11 July 1977. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_McKay_(guitarist) (Wikipedia, as of 2026-10-23)
