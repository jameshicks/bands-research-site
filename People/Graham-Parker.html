Graham Parker

Discogs ID: 289622 Real name: Graham Thomas Parker Born: November 18, 1950 Origin: UK

British rock singer-songwriter and guitarist. Active 1976–present.

Overview

Veteran UK pub-rock/new wave artist who rose to prominence fronting Graham Parker And The Rumour (formed London, 1975). The Rumour — built from former members of Brinsley Schwarz — were a key live draw bridging the pub-rock scene to new wave. They recorded five albums together until splitting in 1980.

Parker continued as a solo artist and with various backing configurations (Graham Parker And The Shot, Graham Parker And The Fact, and others) before reuniting with The Rumour in 2011. He later formed Graham Parker & The Goldtops for recordings in 2018–2023.

Parker’s alias on Discogs is Tex Skerball.
