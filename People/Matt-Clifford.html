Matt Clifford

Discogs ID: 261457 Name variations: Clifford, Clifford Matt, Matthew Clifford

British multi-instrumentalist, programmer, producer, and arranger. Based in the UK, Clifford has worked across three decades as a session player and close associate of Mick Jagger and The Rolling Stones, contributing keyboards, orchestrations, French horn, flugelhorn, harpsichord, virginal, and drum programming to some of the Stones’ most commercially prominent records. He is also credited as a co-writer and producer on Jagger solo material and played keyboards on the prog-rock supergroup album Anderson Bruford Wakeman Howe (1989) with Jon Anderson, Bill Bruford, Rick Wakeman, and Steve Howe. His only primary-credited release as a lead artist is The Return of the Living Dead (Original Motion Picture Score) (2021), issued as a lathe-cut LP co-credited with Francis Haines (2) and Robert Randles.

Key Credits & Releases

“Continental Drift” (B4) is the most distinctive credit: Clifford supplied full orchestration alongside the Moroccan ensemble Master Musicians Of Jajouka (led by Bachir Attar, Discogs ID: 143423) and the Burkinabe group Farafina. The album was produced by The Glimmer Twins and Chris Kimsey (Discogs ID: 85730), with Christopher Marc Potter as recording engineer.

Anderson Bruford Wakeman Howe (1989)

Clifford contributed keyboards, programming, orchestrations, vocals, and backing vocals to the self-titled album by the Yes splinter group. He also participated as part of the J.M.C. Singers vocal group alongside Jon Anderson and Chris Kimsey. The album was produced by Jon Anderson and Chris Kimsey; other contributors included Tony Levin (bass), Milton McDonald (rhythm guitar), and Tessa Niles (backing vocals).

Mick Jagger — Wandering Spirit (1993)

Clifford played virginal, harpsichord, and provided string arrangements and conducting. The album also featured Flea, Lenny Kravitz, Billy Preston, Benmont Tench, Courtney Pine, and Rick Rubin as producer.

Mick Jagger — Goddess in the Doorway (2001)

Clifford produced tracks 2, 7, 8, 10, and 12 — the majority of the album. Also contributed written-by and arrangement credits. Marti Frederiksen and Jerry Duplessis/Wyclef Jean handled the remaining tracks.

Mick Jagger / The Rolling Stones — “Gotta Get a Grip / England Lost” (2017)

Clifford’s most comprehensive Jagger solo credit: co-wrote, produced, played keyboards, and handled engineering and programming on the double A-side single. Drums: Charlie Watts; guitar: Ron Wood.

The Rolling Stones — Hackney Diamonds (2023)

Keyboards throughout. The album was produced by Andrew Watt (3) (Discogs ID: 3821409). Steve Jordan played drums on the main sessions; Charlie Watts plays on two pre-recorded tracks.

The Rolling Stones — Living in a Ghost Town (2020)

Keyboards and producer (alongside Don Was), plus flugelhorn and French horn. Mixed by Cenzo Townshend.
