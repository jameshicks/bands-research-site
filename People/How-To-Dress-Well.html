How To Dress Well

Discogs ID: 1905156 Real name: Tom Krell (also credited as Tom Krell, ID 2040179) Origin: Boulder, Colorado Active: 2009–2024 Alias: HTDW Labels: Lefse Records (2010); Acéphale / Weird World (2) (2012–2016); Domino (2018–2023); Sargent House (2024)

Solo project of Tom Krell, singer-songwriter and producer from Boulder, Colorado. One of the early voices of what critics called “lo-fi R&B” or “hauntological R&B” — a bedroom-recorded approach to soul and pop influenced by 90s R&B, noise, and ambient music. The project began as a self-released blog-era tape in 2009 and moved through a series of label relationships and sonic evolutions before the final album in 2024.

Krell maintains two Discogs entries: How To Dress Well (1905156, primary artist) and Tom Krell (2040179, credits persona for production and songwriting).

Era Overview

2009–2010 — Bedroom / Blog Era

The Eternal Love (2009, self-released) circulated as a digital tape — Discogs shows 41 pressings but master_id=0 makes this a data artifact; actual distribution was digital/blog-era. Signed to Lefse Records for Love Remains (2010, 8 pressings), which established the defining sound: fragmented, heavily treated vocals over murky, reverb-drenched production evoking 90s R&B structures through a lo-fi lens. Self-produced; mixed by Nick Atamaniuk (2040178); mastered by Carl Saff; lacquer by David Cheppa (408405). Nick Andre (536239) and Matt Shaw (6) (4416336) contributed additional recording.

Ready For The World EP (2010, Lefse, 3 pressings) followed in the same year.

2011–2013 — Breakthrough / Weird World Era

Moved to Weird World (2) (a UK label with apparent XL Recordings ties — Rodaidh McDonald’s profile links xlrecordings.com). And It Was U EP (2012, 6 pressings) preceded the critical breakthrough: Total Loss (2012, 14 pressings — most-pressed proper album).

Total Loss credits:

  • Produced By: Tom Krell; Rodaidh McDonald (primary); Ezekiel Honig (track A1); Forest Swords (track A2)
  • Mixed By: Rodaidh McDonald
  • Mastered By: Guy Davie (369860)
  • Strings (A5, B3): Minna Choi

The album’s distributed production model — different producers for individual tracks — was unusual and brought notable outside voices into the project. Forest Swords and Ezekiel Honig each produced one track.

2014–2016 — Pop Expansion

What Is This Heart? (2014, Weird World (2), 11 pressings): Rodaidh McDonald again as co-producer; Guy Davie mastered and cut lacquer; Zackery Michael (3450820) cover photography; Oval-X Eternal (3878735) design. The album moved toward more direct pop songwriting while retaining the spectral vocal processing of earlier work.

The One (2015, Adidas Originals / Yours Truly, 2 pressings) — a brand tie-in release, co-credited with TĀLĀ (Jasmin Tadjiky, ID 4221667).

Care (2016, Weird World (2), 9 pressings): Tom Krell as executive producer / lyrics; Andrew Dawson (370017) mixed; Chris Gehringer (326440) mastered; Ben Tricklebank (5282729) artwork and photography. The most polished and commercially accessible record of the Weird World period.

2018–2024 — Domino / Late Period

Moved to Domino for The Anteroom (2018, 6 pressings). Production credit shifted entirely to Krell and Joel Ford (alias Airbird, ID 636796), who co-produced and mixed; Dave Cooley (219192) mastered; Josh Clancy (2512873) artwork. Michael Silver (CFCF, ID 1123451) is a significant creative collaborator across two albums. On What Is This Heart? (2014), Silver sent Krell an instrumental demo modelled on Massive Attack’s “Paradise Circus” that became the track “What You Wanted.” On I Am Toward You (2024), the collaboration was more immersive: Silver stayed at Krell’s LA home for 30 days during pandemic-era recording (pre-vaccine, ~early 2020), adding guitar to ~21 songs Krell had built from samples. Key tracks shaped by Silver include “New Confusion” (early synth), “Song in the Middle” (designed as “the spatial experience of walking into a venue or a rave, or even a church”), and “On It and Around It” and “A Secret Within the Voice” (both originated from Silver’s guitar sketches). Trayer Tryon (3862009) also contributed instrumentation and mixing across I Am Toward You.

What Remains (Remixes) (2023, Domino, 3 pressings).

I Am Toward You (2024, Sargent House, 3 pressings): Krell written-by; Trayer Tryon (3862009) mixed; Adam Badí Donoval (5931421) and Zac Emerson (7669359) mastered; Josh Clancy artwork again. Label move to Sargent House for the final release.

Also appeared on LuckyMe compilation ANTH01 (2021) as writer/producer.
