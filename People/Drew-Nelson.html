Overview

Drew Nelson (real name Drew Piraino) is a Brooklyn-based bassist and multi-instrumentalist, identified in Vibrant Light’s Discogs profile as the founder of Psychic Troubles Tapes. His recorded output runs from 1997 to 2005, with an early hardcore chapter on Ferret Music through Torn Apart giving way to a post-rock phase on Temporary Residence Limited with Sonna — the latter frequently recorded by Steve Albini. Beyond those two anchors he also played in The Big Huge and Vibrant Light, drawing on styles ranging from math rock and post-rock to folk rock and downtempo.
