Jeremy Gara

Montreal drummer. Discogs artist ID: 187292.

Drummer of Arcade Fire from Neon Bible (2007) onward, replacing Howard Bilerman. Also a member of Clark The Band (ID: 2293508), Kepler (ID: 153148), and Weights & Measures (ID: 823950).
