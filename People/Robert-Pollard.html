Overview

Robert Pollard is an American rock musician and singer-songwriter from Dayton, Ohio, born October 31, 1957, best known as the founder and primary creative force behind Guided By Voices. His output — over 1,500 Discogs credits spanning from 1986 — works across indie rock, lo-fi, garage rock, and power pop, with the 2000s his most prolific decade and a sustained run continuing through the 2010s. He has released material through Matador, Merge, and his own Guided By Voices Inc. and Fading Captain Series imprints, the latter serving as the main outlet for solo and side-project work from the late 1990s onward. His closest recurring collaborators — Tobin Sprout, Todd Tobias, Jim Pollard, and Doug Gillard — each share over 150 credits with him.
