Overview

Andrew Brant Stack is a Baltimore-based multi-instrumentalist, producer, composer, and remix artist best known as one half of indie-rock duo Wye Oak alongside Jenn Wasner, with whom he shares 79 documented collaborative credits. He plays drums, bass, guitar, and keyboards — often simultaneously in Wye Oak’s live performances — and has been professionally active since 2006. The bulk of his recorded output appeared on Merge Records across 2008–2024, with additional work on City Slang, Sub Pop, and 4AD; a 2024 4AD credit marks a departure from the long Merge relationship. Beyond Wye Oak he is credited as a member of Caleb Stine And The Brakemen, EL VY, Errant Strike, Lambchop, and Noble Lake.
