Carlo Cennamo

Discogs ID: 351388
Name variations: CC, Carlo Cenamo

Saxophonist (primarily alto, also tenor and bass clarinet) based in the US. Active from at least 1998 through 2008 in the post-rock/experimental wing of the DC-adjacent and Chicago scenes. Long-running member of HiM (the Doug Scharin-led dub-influenced post-rock collective) and of Orso (Phil Spirito’s “carny rock / chamber folk” ensemble). Appears as a session guest on releases for Slowdime Records, FatCat Records, Perishable Records, and others.

Groups

HiM

Discogs ID: 13240

Cennamo is a listed member of HiM alongside Doug Scharin, Adam Pierce, Griffin Rodriguez, Fred Erskine, Joshua Larue, Julie Liu, Phil Spirito, Jon Theodore, Joe Goldring, Matt Schneider, Christian Dautresme, Neil Turpin, and Dave Pavkovic. The group released on FatCat Records and Bubble Core Records.

Mice Parade / Mokoondi (2001, FatCat Records): Cennamo plays saxophone on tracks 4–6 alongside Adam Pierce (who handles nearly all instruments including additional sax on those same tracks), with recording by Peter Katis and mixing split between Pierce and Katis. The album also features Doro Tachler on violin (tracks 7, 11, 12).

The Boom (3): The Boom’s two Slowdime releases (Any Day Of The Night, Movin’ Out) form part of a tight DC-scene cluster — Fred Erskine plays guitar/trumpet on both, Joshua Larue adds trombone/keyboards, Juan Carrera engineers, and Christopher Farrall drums.

The Sorts: Two Slowdime appearances. The Hawaiian Bronco also credits Fred Erskine as guest trumpeter. Contemporary Music (1999) includes Joshua LaRue (guitar, synthesizer) and Vin Novara.

Kerosene 454: At Zero produced by J. Robbins, engineered at Inner Ear by Don Zientara.

Just A Fire (Light Up, 2004, Sickroom Records): Cennamo and Fred Erskine both appear as guests; Erskine plays guitar, trumpet, bass, and vocals across the record.

Icy Demons (Miami Ice, 2007, Obey Your Brain): saxophone on track 3 only, alongside guests Jeff Parker (guitar, track 5), Joshua Abrams (upright bass, track 4), and Heather McIntosh (cello/vocals, track 3).
