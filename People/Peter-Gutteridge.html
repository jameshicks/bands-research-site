Peter Gutteridge

Discogs ID: 363005 Born: 19 May 1961, Dunedin, New Zealand Died: 15 September 2014, Auckland, New Zealand

New Zealand musician. Associated with the Dunedin Sound; co-founding guitarist of The Chills (phase 1, 1980) and co-founder of Sneaky Feelings. Also a member of The Clean, The Great Unwashed, Snapper, and The Puddle. Solo releases on Flying Nun and related labels. (Source: Discogs; Wikipedia)
