Fabrizio Moretti

Brazilian-American drummer, born 2 June 1980 in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Drummer and co-founder of The Strokes; co-founder of Little Joy; member of machinegum. Discogs artist ID: 430108. Also credited as “Fab Moretti,” “F. Moretti,” “F.Moretti,” and “Moretti.”

Biography

Early Career and The Strokes (2000–present)

Moretti came up through New York City’s early-2000s garage-rock revival as the drummer for The Strokes, joining fellow members Julian Casablancas (vocals), Nick Valensi (guitar), Albert Hammond Jr. (guitar), and Nikolai Fraiture (bass). The band self-released a demo cassette/CD in 2000 before signing to RCA in the UK and releasing their debut single “The Modern Age” on Rough Trade in 2001.

The Strokes’ debut album Is This It (RCA/BMG, 2001) was produced by Gordon Raphael, with all songs written by Julian Casablancas. Moretti is credited as drummer (as “Fab Moretti”). The album became one of the defining records of the early 2000s indie rock moment and was pressed across more than 50 configurations worldwide.

Room On Fire (RCA, 2003), again produced by Gordon Raphael, continued the band’s run. Moretti played drums; Julian Casablancas again holds all writing credits. Additional engineering by Toshikazu Yoshioka.

First Impressions of Earth (RCA/Sony BMG, 2005) saw the band co-produce with David Kahne (bulk of the album) and Gordon Raphael (tracks 4, 8, 9). For the first time, non-Casablancas songwriting credits appeared: Moretti is credited “Music By” on track 13; Nick Valensi on track 7; Nikolai Fraiture on track 9. Mixed by Andy Wallace.

After a longer hiatus, Angles (RCA/Sony Music, 2011) brought in Gus Oberg as engineer/producer (9 of 10 tracks) and Noah Georgeson for mixing, with Joe Chiccarelli producing and recording select tracks. The band co-produced the full album. Moretti drums throughout (credited as “Fab Moretti”).

Comedown Machine (RCA, 2013) and the single “All The Time” (RCA, 2013) carry Moretti as co-credited Art Direction alongside Brett Kilroe — a visual/design role alongside his drumming. He also contributed illustration to EP No. 01 (RCA, 2012).

The New Abnormal (Cult Records / RCA, 2020) was produced by Rick Rubin, with Jason Lader engineering and mixing most tracks. Artwork by Jean-Michel Basquiat. Moretti credited on drums.

The band also contributed a track to the Future Present Past EP (Cult Records, 2016), on which Moretti holds a “Written-By” credit.

Little Joy (2008)

In 2008 Moretti co-founded Little Joy with Brazilian guitarist/vocalist Rodrigo Amarante (ex-Los Hermanos) and American vocalist/guitarist Binki Shapiro (real name: Jordana Shapiro). The self-titled debut album was released on Rough Trade.

Moretti’s instrumental contributions on the record are unusually broad for a drummer: drums, guitar, piano, bass, percussion, melodica, and backing vocals. He also holds Lyrics By credits on tracks 1–10, and Music By credits on tracks 1–7 and 10, making him a primary songwriter alongside Amarante.

Also in 2008, Little Joy members (Moretti, Amarante, Shapiro) appeared together as performers on Whatwave (Affairs of the Heart, 2011), a release by The Dead Trees produced by Noah Georgeson.

machinegum (2019)

Moretti is a member of machinegum, a trio with Ian Devaney (2) and Steve Marion. The group released Conduit (Frenchkiss Records, 2019). No additional personnel credits are listed in the Discogs record for this release.

Session / Guest Work

  • Ryan Adams — Love Is Hell Pt. 1 (Lost Highway, 2003): Moretti credited as drums. This was a guest appearance during the same period as The Strokes’ Room On Fire.
  • Nation of Language — Introduction, Presence (self-released, 2020): Moretti drummed on two tracks — “Indignities” and “Sacred Tongue.” The release also features Ian Richard Devaney (his machinegum bandmate) as a core member of Nation of Language.
