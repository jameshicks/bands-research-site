Dustin Kensrue

Born November 18, 1980. American vocalist, guitarist, and songwriter. Discogs artist ID: 411212. Lead singer, guitarist, and primary lyricist of Thrice; solo artist; also recorded under the band name The Modern Post.

Real name: Dustin Michael Kensrue. Name variations on credits: D, D. Kensrue, Dustin, Dustin Michael Kensrue, Kensrue.

Biography

Early Career — Thrice and the Post-Hardcore Scene (2000–2006)

Kensrue co-founded Thrice in Orange County, California in the late 1990s. He is the band’s vocalist, guitarist, and sole lyricist throughout their catalog. The band’s debut full-length, Identity Crisis (2000, Greenflag Records), credited him with Vocals and Guitar. They subsequently signed to Sub City Records, releasing The Illusion Of Safety (2002), where Kensrue is credited: Lyrics By; Musician; Vocals, Guitar.

The Artist In The Ambulance (2003, Island Records / Sub City Records) was the band’s major-label breakthrough. Kensrue’s lyrical contributions are consistently credited separately from the band’s collective music writing across all Thrice releases. The EP Stare At The Sun (2003, Island) and the single All That’s Left (2003, Mercury) followed quickly.

The double-LP concept record Vheissu (2005, Island / Sub City) marked a significant artistic expansion; Kensrue is credited Liner Notes and Lyrics By. The DVD release If We Could Only See Us Now (2005) added a Cameraman [Additional Footage] credit.

Alchemy Index Era and Vagrant Transition (2007–2012)

After leaving Island Records, Thrice signed to Vagrant Records. The ambitious four-volume Alchemy Index series was split into two releases:

  • The Alchemy Index Vols. I & II: Fire & Water (2007) — Kensrue: Lyrics By, Design [Art Design]; engineered and produced by Teppei Teranishi
  • The Alchemy Index Vols. III & IV: Air & Earth (2008) — same credits; compiled as the complete The Alchemy Index box set (2009)

Beggars (2009, Vagrant) was produced by Teppei Teranishi and Thrice collectively; mixed by David Schiffman; Kensrue credited Lyrics By on tracks 1–7 and 9–10.

Major / Minor (2011, Vagrant) saw Kensrue credited Vocals, Guitar, Written-By alongside the full band. Produced and mixed by David Schiffman. The Anthology compilation (2012, Staple Records / Workhorse Music Group) collected earlier work.

First Hiatus and Solo Work (2007–2015)

During the Alchemy Index period, Kensrue launched a solo career alongside his Thrice commitments:

Please Come Home (2007, Equal Vision Records, EVR133A) His first solo album — acoustic folk-rock recorded and produced with Teppei Teranishi, who also plays Organ and Piano. Additional drums and electric guitar by Chris Jones (5). Kensrue plays Vocals, Acoustic Guitar, Harmonica, Bass, and Percussion; credited as co-producer and sole writer. Mastered by Gene Grimaldi. Genres: Acoustic, Country Rock, Emo, Folk Rock, Indie Rock.

Tracklist: I Knew You Before / Pistol / I Believe / Please Come Home / Blood & Wine / Consider The Ravens / Weary Saints / Blanket Of Ghosts

This Good Night Is Still Everywhere (2010, Vagrant Records, VR636) A Christmas/holiday LP released on vinyl (limited edition, numbered). Features a mix of originals and traditional carols: “Christmas (Baby Please Come Home),” “Fairytale Of New York,” “Hark! The Herald Angels Sing,” “O Come O Come Emmanuel,” “O Holy Night,” and originals including “This Good Night Is Still Everywhere” and “This Is War.” Genres: Country Blues, Holiday.

The Water & The Blood (2013, BEC Recordings, BEV000004) A 12-track worship/liturgical LP on vinyl. Kensrue is sole primary artist; released on BEC Recordings (a Christian music label). Tracks include: Rejoice / Rock Of Ages / Suffering Servant / My One Comfort / God Is Good / Grace Alone / The Voice Of The Lord / It’s Not Enough / Come Lord Jesus / O God / It Is Finished / Psalm 145. No additional credits on this Discogs entry.

Carry The Fire (2015, Vagrant Records / Staple Records / Workhorse Music Group, WH60) Second proper solo rock LP. Kensrue serves as sole producer and engineer; mixed by Brian Eichelberger. Vinyl LP, 45 RPM, limited edition. Tracks: Ruby / Back To Back / Gallows / There’s Something Dark Inside Of Me / Death Or Glory / In The Darkness / Of Crows And Crowns / Juggernaut / What Beautiful Things / Carry The Fire. Genre: Acoustic Rock.

The Modern Post (2015)

Under the name The Modern Post — a project with Kensrue as sole listed member — one album was released:

Lowborn King (2015, BEC Recordings) The only release credited to The Modern Post (Discogs artist ID: 4755013). No additional personnel listed on the Discogs entry.

Thrice Return and Post-Hiatus Releases (2016–present)

After a hiatus, Thrice reconvened and signed to Epitaph Records:

To Be Everywhere Is To Be Nowhere (2016, Vagrant Records / BMG) Kensrue credited Band, Lyrics By. Produced and engineered by Eric Palmquist; Teppei Teranishi recorded guitars, bass, and keys. Band members explicitly credited: Dustin Kensrue, Teppei Teranishi, Eddie Breckenridge, Riley Breckenridge. Art direction by Chris King (4).

Palms (2018, Epitaph) Kensrue: Lyrics By. Produced by Eric Palmquist and Thrice collectively. Mixed by John Congleton. Teppei Teranishi recorded guitars, bass, and keys. Mastered by Greg Calbi.

Horizons/East (2021, Epitaph) Kensrue: Lyrics By. Engineered by Teppei Teranishi; mixed by Scott Evans; mastered by Alan Douches.

Desert Dreaming (2024, Vagrant Records / BMG Rights Management) Solo release; Kensrue credited as primary artist, no additional credits listed on Discogs.

Horizons/West (2025, Epitaph, Europe) Thrice release; Kensrue credited Guitar, Vocals, Lyrics By.
