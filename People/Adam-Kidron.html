Adam Kidron

Discogs IDs: 143041 (Adam Kidron); 384484 (Skipper Kidron / The Skipper — same person, alternate credit name) Origin: British Active: 1979–present Profile: British-born record producer and serial entrepreneur. Came up as an engineer in the Rough Trade orbit 1979–82, working on foundational post-punk records; moved into production and delivered full albums for Scritti Politti, Orange Juice, Pere Ubu, Ian Dury, Rip Rig & Panic, David Thomas, Nina Hagen, and Lizzy Mercier Descloux. Pivoted to executive roles in the 1990s: Mercury Records film soundtracks, then CEO of Urban Box Office (reggaeton/urban Latino) and Yonder Music.

1979–1980: Rough Trade engineer

Earliest credit is tape op on Patrik Fitzgerald’s Grubby Stories (Polydor/Small Wonder, 1979). By that year he was engineering Rough Trade releases:

Later career

Became CEO of Urban Box Office, a reggaeton and urban Latino label. Later CEO of Yonder Music. Both roles represent a decisive departure from independent music production.
