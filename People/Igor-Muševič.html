Igor Muševič

Slovenian drummer. Discogs artist ID: 1088499. Name variation: Muševič. Original drummer in Begnagrad during the band’s first active period (1975–1978). Also credited as co-composer on later Begnagrad recordings.

Career Overview

Igor Muševič is documented primarily through his role as the original drummer in Begnagrad, the Slovenian avant-garde / chamber-folk group formed in Ljubljana in the mid-1970s. According to the Begnagrad Discogs profile, the band operated in two distinct periods — 1975–1978 (original lineup: Muševič, Vlatko Špindler, Bratko Bibič, Bogo Pečnikar) and 1981–1983 (reconstituted with Aleš Rendla on drums and Nino De Gleria on bass in place of Muševič and Špindler).

Despite departing the lineup by 1978, Muševič reappears on both recordings released under the Begnagrad name in the 1990s — the cassette Jodlovska Urška (1990, Nikad Robom) and the CD Tastare (Theoldwones) (1993, Bess - PRO MARKET) — with the original-era rhythm section reunited (Muševič + Špindler). On Tastare he is also credited as a co-writer on six of the thirteen tracks, indicating compositional involvement, not just a session appearance.

The Discogs database does not document any solo releases or primary-credited projects by Muševič outside of Begnagrad. His credit history is limited to the two Begnagrad recordings listed below.
