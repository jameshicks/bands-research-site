Charlie Collins

Sheffield saxophonist and multi-instrumentalist. Discogs artist ID: 401046.

Co-founder of The Box (3) (from Clock DVA ashes, 1981–1985) and co-founder of Flex 13 (with Peter Hope, from 1994). Credited on saxophone and clarinet across The Box catalog, and as co-writer/producer on Flex 13. Also a member of The Impossible (with Hope and Jon Wood) and Collins/Hope/Infanti.

Clock DVA Involvement

Collins was a member of Clock DVA during their early period, credited on their first two studio albums:

ReleaseYearRole
Thirst1981Saxophone, Flute
White Souls In Black Suits1982Saxophone, Flute, Percussion, Bells (Ice & Go Go)

He also appears on the 2012 archival compilation Horology - DVAtion 78/79/80 (Vinyl-on-Demand), credited on tracks K1–L with Saxophone and Flute — material from the 1978–1980 period. This places him in the band from the earliest pre-label recordings through the first two Fetish/Italian Records releases, before the group dissolved into The Box (3).
