David Roback

Oct 18, 20262 min read

David Roback

Discogs ID: 256622
Instrument: Guitar, production
Scene: Paisley Underground

Los Angeles guitarist and producer. Co-founder of Rain Parade; later formed Opal with Kendra Smith, then Mazzy Star with Hope Sandoval. Produced the Rainy Day LP (Llama Records, 1984), the primary cross-scene document of the Paisley Underground. Member of the Rainy Day collective alongside Karl Precoda, Kendra Smith, Dennis Duck, and members of the Bangles and Three O’Clock.
