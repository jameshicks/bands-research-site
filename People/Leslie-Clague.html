Leslie Clague

Photographer based in Washington, DC. Discogs ID: 2465296. Active in the DC hardcore/punk scene from the early 1980s onward; credited on releases spanning 1983–2026.

Co-created Banned in DC: Photos and Flyers from the DC Punk Underground 1979–85 (1988) with Cynthia Connolly and Sharon Cheslow — the primary photographic document of the first wave of DC punk. Her name occasionally appears as “Leslie Claque” (variant spelling on at least one release).

Overview

Clague was embedded in the DC hardcore scene from its earliest documented years. Her photographic work appears on core Dischord Records releases — Minor Threat, Embrace (2), Rites Of Spring, Dag Nasty — as well as on material outside the Dischord orbit: a Bad Brains retrospective on Caroline Records, a Chalk Circle (2) retrospective on Mississippi Records / Post Present Medium, and more recent reissue work for King Face and Void (12).

The Banned in DC book (book, not a release) was published in 1988 by Sassy Press and co-authored with Connolly and Cheslow; it compiled photographs and xeroxed flyers from the 1979–1985 period. The three co-creators drew on their own archives and those of participants across the scene. This is the work she is most widely cited for outside Discogs.

Her Discogs credits run from 1983 to 2026 — four decades of association with DC punk documentation. She occasionally shares photography credit with other scene photographers: Cynthia Connolly, Amanda MacKaye, and Jim Saah appear alongside her on specific releases.

Notes on Selected Credits

Subject To Change / Out Of Step (Minor Threat): The 1983 Subject To Change EP is her first Discogs credit; she supplied insert photography. The 1986 Out Of Step pressing is a later reissue variant that reused her photography credit.

Take It Back (Dag Nasty, 1986): Her only non-photography credit — listed for Graphics and Layout [Poster], indicating direct involvement in the visual production of the release.

King Face (1987): Self-released EP by King Face, a DC band produced by Ian MacKaye. Photography shared with Amanda MacKaye. Artwork by Cynthia Connolly.

Reflection (Chalk Circle, 2011): A retrospective compilation on Mississippi Records and Post Present Medium of DC post-punk band Chalk Circle (2), whose members included Sharon Cheslow. Clague is one of several photographers credited; Sharon Cheslow compiled the audio and handled much of the design.

King Face (2025): A full-length by King Face on Atomic Action Records, produced by J. Robbins. Clague’s photography carries forward from the 1987 EP — nearly 40 years of association with the band.

Live 1982 (Void, 2026): Posthumous live release on Outer Battery Records; her name appears as “Leslie Claque” in the credits (name-variation on file). Primary photography credit goes to Jim Saah; Clague’s photos appear on the inner sleeve.
