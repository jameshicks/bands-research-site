Rusty Egan

Born: 19 September 1957, London (Irish descent) Role: Drums, DJ, producer

Drummer for Rich Kids (1977–1978) and co-founder of Visage (1978). A central figure in the New Romantic movement as resident DJ at Billy’s club and then the Blitz in London.

DJ Career

  • Billy’s (Soho, 1978–1979): Tuesday Bowie/Roxy Music nights with Steve Strange; this is where Visage coalesced
  • Blitz (Covent Garden, 1979–1981): Resident DJ with Steve Strange; the defining Blitz Kids / New Romantic venue. Played Kraftwerk, Yellow Magic Orchestra, Eno, Ultravox, Landscape.
  • Camden Palace (1982–): Co-opened with Steve Strange and Kevin Millins
  • Owned The Cage record store, King’s Road

Shock (2) Credit

Co-produced and co-wrote “R.E.R.B.” (B-side of Shock (2)‘s debut 7” on RCA, 1980) alongside Richard James Burgess. Also arranged “Angel Face.” Reissued “R.E.R.B.” on his own Blitz Club Records label in 2010. Egan’s connection to Shock reflects the Blitz Club axis: Shock performed at the club while Egan was its resident DJ.

Other Credits

  • Drums on remixed “Yellow Pearl” by Phil Lynott — used as BBC Top of the Pops theme, 1981–1986
  • Solo album: Welcome to the Dancefloor (2017)
