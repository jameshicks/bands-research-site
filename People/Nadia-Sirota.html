Nadia Sirota

American violist, conductor, producer, and broadcaster. New York, NY. Active c. 2006–present. Discogs artist ID: 674198. Name variation on Discogs: Naidia Sirota.

Overview

Nadia Sirota is a central figure in the New York new-music scene — a specialist violist whose career spans solo artistry, ensemble work, orchestral contracting, production, and advocacy. She is associated primarily with Bedroom Community as a solo and collaborative artist, with the Wordless Music Orchestra as contractor and performer, and with the new-music chamber ensemble yMusic. Her viola playing appears on recordings by Nico Muhly, Ben Frost, Daníel Bjarnason, Valgeir Sigurðsson, Björk, Sufjan Stevens, and many others across the indie-classical and experimental pop spectrum.

She is also a broadcaster: she has hosted music programmes and podcasts (including work for New Amsterdam Records), and has become one of the more visible advocates for contemporary classical and new-music causes in the US.

Wordless Music Orchestra — Holy Ground (2010)

On the 2010 MONO live album Holy Ground: NYC Live With The Wordless Music Orchestra (Temporary Residence Ltd., release ID 2254308), Sirota served in a dual capacity:

  • Contractor for the Wordless Music Orchestra — the organizing/logistics credit for assembling the ensemble
  • Violist — performing in the ensemble

The orchestra was produced by Ronen Givony (founder of the Wordless Music series) and conducted by Jeffrey Milarsky. Other violists: Andi Hennenway, John Pickford Richards, Miranda Seilaff. Cellists included Clarice Jensen, Mike Block, and others. The recording/mix was handled by Matt Bayles.

Tessellatum (2017)

Tessellatum (HVALUR29, Bedroom Community, 2017; release ID 10844174) is a collaboration between Irish composer Donnacha Dennehy (composed), Sirota (viola, producer), and Liam Byrne (2) (viola da gamba). Valgeir Sigurðsson mixed; Paul Evans (7) recorded; Sam Slater engineered; Steven Mertens directed the accompanying film. This is one of Sirota’s most compositionally involved credits — she is listed as both producer and performer/violist at the primary level.

First Things First (2009)

First Things First (New Amsterdam Records, 2009; release ID 1761484) is Sirota’s earliest primary credit as a recording artist. Co-produced with Judd Greenstein (New Amsterdam co-founder). Engineered/edited by Ryan Streber; mixed and mastered by Dan Bora.

Scene Connections

  • Bedroom Community — Solo label home 2013–2017; also a recurring session violist on many BC records from 2006 onward (Muhly, Valgeir, Bjarnason). One of the few BC artists who is both a solo artist and a permanent part of the label’s session pool.
  • Wordless Music Orchestra (Discogs ID: 1638996) — Contractor and performer; the Wordless Music series was a NYC concert series programming post-minimalist and experimental orchestral music in rock venues. Ronen Givony organized; Sirota was instrumental in making it happen logistically.
  • yMusic (Discogs ID: 3602036) — NYC new-music sextet; her ensemble credits via yMusic appear on Dirty Projectors’ Swing Lo Magellan (2012), Ben Folds’ So There (2015), Paul Simon’s In The Blue Light (2018), and others.
  • ACME (American Contemporary Music Ensemble, Discogs ID: 517947) — NYC chamber ensemble she is a member of.
  • New Amsterdam Records — Her first primary release (First Things First, 2009); also production credits there; associated with Judd Greenstein and the New Amsterdam new-music advocacy circle.
  • NYC indie-classical network — Sirota’s viola appears on records by The National, Arcade Fire, Dirty Projectors, Sufjan Stevens, Neko Case, and others — the extended orbit where indie rock and new classical overlap in NYC.
  • Film/TV work (2020s) — Increasing screen-credit appearances: Everything Everywhere All at Once (A24, 2023), Pachinko (Apple TV+, 2023), The Marvels (2023), Joker: Folie À Deux (2024), Transformers One (2025).
