Stuart Baker

Discogs ID: 281906
Name variations: S Baker, S. Baker, S.Baker, STUBAK
Label founder, compiler, producer, and mix artist. UK.

Overview

Stuart Baker is the founder and owner of Soul Jazz Records, the London-based independent label and record shop that since 1992–93 has become one of the world’s leading reissue and compilation imprints. Baker is the primary compiler behind the vast majority of Soul Jazz’s compilation output — spanning reggae, funk, soul, no wave, Latin, Brazilian, Krautrock, acid house, punk, hip-hop, jungle, afrobeat, dancehall, and jazz. His Discogs credit count exceeds 140 releases across three-plus decades. He also records under the Souljazzsoundsystem group alias.

Baker’s compilatory method is distinctive: each project is framed as a documentary argument — genre history, geography, or a specific cultural moment — accompanied by extensive sleeve notes, often with original interviews. He consistently co-compiles and co-credits in-house collaborator Adrian Self (who also handles sleeve design), but the curatorial direction across the catalog is plainly Baker’s.

Soul Jazz Records

Soul Jazz Records (Discogs label ID: 27182; parent: Soul Jazz Records Ltd., ID: 364250) was founded in London and has released over 1,400 editions since 1993. It operates through a web of sublabels that mirror Baker’s compilation series:

The label’s deepest ongoing relationship is with Studio One — Baker has compiled more than 20 Studio One compilations from 2001 onward, covering rocksteady, ska, dub, funk, dancehall, lovers rock, and more, licensed with the cooperation of Clement “Coxsone” Dodd and his estate.

Top artists on the label’s roster by release count include ESG (20 releases, 2002–2007), Jackie Mittoo (24), Sound Dimension (21), Dub Specialist (15), Arthur Russell (10), A Certain Ratio (10), The Skatalites (10), and Trees Speak (26, 2020–2025).

Core recurring production/mastering team: Duncan Cowell and Pete Reilly (mastering, on nearly every compilation); Adrian Self (design, co-compiler); Angela Scott (legal/licensing); Pierce Smith (reissue production).

Souljazzsoundsystem

Baker is a member of Souljazzsoundsystem (Discogs ID: 536021), a DJ/compilation collective used as the house alias for Soul Jazz’s Jamaican music compilation series (1998–2009). The Discogs collective realname field lists nine members: Stuart Baker, Scott Bethell, Pete Reilly, Karen Tate, Abi Clarke, Karl Shale, Dean Atkins, Jon Burnip & Kris Jones — a broader pool than the six individually credited on 200% Dynamite! (1999): Baker, Christine Indigo, Ethan Reid, Hayley Willis, Pete Reilly, and Scott Bethell. The collective released eight compilations on Soul Jazz Records, all in the Jamaican music/dub/jungle space.
