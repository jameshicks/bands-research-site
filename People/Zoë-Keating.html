Overview

Zoë Clare Keating is a Canadian-American cellist and composer based in Vermont. Born in Guelph, Ontario, she began playing cello at age eight and studied at Sarah Lawrence College in New York. Before committing to music full time, she worked as an information architect, with projects at Perspecta, Inc., the Research Libraries Group, and the Database of Recorded American Music. She transitioned fully to music around 2005. (Wikipedia)

Keating is best known for her solo recordings, in which she uses live electronic sampling and loop layering to build dense, rhythmically complex cello textures entirely in real time — all sixteen parts on One Cello x 16 are her own. She is also known as a past member of the cello rock band Rasputina, where she served as second chair cellist from approximately 2002 to 2006. Her broader output spans film scores, television, dance commissions, and guest appearances with artists including Amanda Palmer, DJ Shadow, Tarentel, and John Vanderslice. She has been a vocal advocate for artist data transparency, notably publishing her streaming revenue figures publicly to help other musicians understand their earnings.

She is also a member of AEAEA, Inu (4), and Michael Talbott And The Wolfkings.

Film & Television Scoring (selected, from Wikipedia)

Keating has composed extensively for screen. Key credits include:

  • The Conspirator (2011, dir. Robert Redford) — cello soloist alongside composer Mark Isham
  • The Returned (A&E, 2014–2015) — co-composed with Jeff Russo
  • Manhattan (WGN America, Season 2, 2016) — co-composed with Jeff Russo
  • Oslo (HBO, 2021) — co-composed with Jeff Russo; Emmy-nominated for Outstanding Music Composition
  • The Edge of All We Know (2020, documentary about black holes)
  • White Lotus (HBO) — “We Insist” and “Legions (War)” used in Season 2

Her song “Lost” is the theme for the podcast On Being.
