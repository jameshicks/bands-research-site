T.J. Lipple

Mastering engineer, drummer, multi-instrumentalist. Washington DC area. Discogs artist ID: 471514. Member of Aloha (2), Edison (9), and Ulf Magnet. Recurring mastering credit across Dischord Records from the early 2000s onward — one of the label’s primary mastering engineers of the post-Chad Clark era.

Overview

T.J. Lipple operates in two distinct but related capacities on record. As a performer, he is the drummer (and vibraphone, marimba, trombone, keyboards player) for Aloha (2), the Cleveland/DC chamber-pop band on Polyvinyl Record Company. As an engineer, he is one of the most consistent mastering presences in the DC independent ecosystem, credited on well over 100 releases across Dischord Records, Polyvinyl, Jagjaguwar, Sister Polygon Records, and a wide range of indie imprints.

His mastering work for Dischord spans from the early 2000s through at least 2026, appearing on releases by Joe Lally, The Messthetics, Coriky, and archival reissues of foundational DC hardcore material. He has also produced, engineered, and mixed records independently of his mastering work.

Lipple’s name appears in Discogs under several variations: T J Lipple, T. J. Lipple, T.J Lipple, TJ Lipple, TJ Lippett, T.J. Lippett, Tj Lipple, tj.

As a Performer — Aloha (2)

Lipple is a member of Aloha (2) (Discogs ID: 647609), a chamber-influenced post-rock band from Cleveland, Ohio, active on Polyvinyl Record Company from 1999 onward. The band’s lineup also includes Tony Cavallario (guitar, vibraphone), Matthew Gengler (guitar), Cale Parks (bass, keyboards), and Eric Koltnow.

Within Aloha, Lipple plays drums, vibraphone, marimba, mellotron, keyboards, and trombone — an unusually broad role that reflects the band’s approach to multi-layered orchestration within a rock format. He also produced, recorded, and mixed Aloha’s records in-house, including taking sole production credit on Light Works (2007, Polyvinyl).

As Mastering Engineer — Dischord Records

Lipple has one of the highest mastering credit counts in the Dischord catalog — 72 credits according to the label’s Discogs entry, second only to Chad Clark (109 credits) among dedicated mastering engineers. He began appearing on Dischord releases in the early 2000s and is active at least through 2026.
