Overview

Alannah Currie (born 1957, Auckland, New Zealand) is a singer and songwriter primarily known as a core member of Thompson Twins alongside Tom Bailey and Joe Leeway, with whom she accumulated the bulk of her 1,565 Discogs credits across a synth-pop and new wave catalogue anchored on Arista from 1981 onward. After Thompson Twins she continued working with Bailey in Babble and appeared in Feedback Max and The Anti-Heroin Project. She married Jimmy Cauty in 2011 and later left music to work as an artistic furniture upholsterer.

Alannah Currie and Jimmy Cauty — relationship and creative overlap

Relationship timeline. Currie’s marriage to Tom Bailey ended in 2004 after 23 years together (they had been a couple since the Thompson Twins era and married in 1991; children Jackson, born 1987, and Indigo, born 1992). Currie has been quoted: “Tom and I were together for 23 years. Somewhere along the way, we had become good friends rather than lovers. Then, in 2004, I fell crazily in love with Jimmy [Cauty, formerly one half of the KLF, now an artist] and took the plunge by moving back to England.” — quote cited by FamousFix as sourced to The Guardian (c. 2008): https://www.famousfix.com/topic/alannah-currie-and-jimmy-cauty. The specific Guardian article URL and date were not recovered in this research pass. They married in 2011.

How they met. No source located gives the specific circumstances or mutual connection through which Currie and Cauty first met. The only dateable fact is that 2004 marks both Currie’s divorce from Bailey and the start of her relationship with Cauty, coinciding with her move back to London from New Zealand. The quote’s framing (“fell crazily in love with Jimmy”) implies a sudden personal encounter rather than a professional introduction, but no primary source has been found confirming the setting or intermediary.

Thompson Twins / KLF creative overlap. No evidence of any direct creative collaboration between the Thompson Twins circle and the KLF/Cauty scene during either group’s active years was found. The KLF’s Wikipedia list of creative associates contains no Thompson Twins personnel. The two scenes operated in roughly the same London pop/post-punk/dance space of the 1980s but appear not to have intersected professionally.

Post-marriage creative collaboration — “Year of Rage” (2019). The clearest documented creative overlap is Cauty’s involvement in the music video for “Year of Rage” by Deux Furieuses (Currie’s duo with Ros Cairney), released July 2019 on Xtra Mile Recordings as part of the album My War is Your War. The video was shot at Currie’s Doyce Street studio in London; director Dan Donovan led the shoot while Cauty handled second-camera duties capturing street protest scenes and flares. Currie also made a cameo appearance in the video and provided art direction rooted in her “Sisters of Perpetual Resistance” activist collective. Sources: Deux Furieuses official blog post https://www.deuxfurieuses.com/post/2019/07/03/making-year-of-rage-video (retrieved 2026-11-05); Louder Than War interview https://louderthanwar.com/an-interview-with-deux-furieuses/ (retrieved 2026-11-05).
