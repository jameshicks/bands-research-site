David Kikoski

Discogs ID: 486649
Born: 1961, Milltown, NJ, USA
Also known as: Dave Kikoski

American jazz pianist and composer. Based in New York City. Active since the mid-1980s as both a prolific sideman and a solo leader. Core member of the Mingus Big Band, the Roy Haynes Group, and the Soulbopband; closely associated with Randy Brecker, Jeff “Tain” Watts, Seamus Blake, and Al Foster.

Overview

Kikoski emerged in the mid-1980s New York scene as a sideman, appearing on early Criss Cross Jazz dates and Japanese Denon recordings before leading his first album in 1989. His playing spans post-bop and hard bop tradition with a strong compositional voice; he writes many of his own originals for leader dates while remaining in demand as a tasteful, technically accomplished accompanist.

He became a regular in the Mingus Big Band orbit, recording multiple Dreyfus Jazz releases across the late 1990s and 2000s. He has appeared on an unusually large number of Criss Cross Jazz dates — both as leader and sideman — making him one of the most-documented pianists in that label’s catalogue.
