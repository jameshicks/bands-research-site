Overview

Bernd Friedmann, also known as Burnt Friedman, is a German musician and producer born in 1965 in Coburg, working across electronica, dub, and jazz under a variety of project names including Drome, Flanger, and Cyclopean. He founded his own imprint Nonplace in 2000 — now home to the bulk of his catalogue (44 releases through 2024) — and Risque in 2016. His output runs from 1991 to the present, with the most prolific decade being the 2010s (11 releases) and sustained activity continuing into the 2020s; his most frequent collaborators are mastering engineer Rashad Becker, saxophonist Hayden Chisholm, and the late Can drummer Jaki Liebezeit, with whom he maintained a long-running duo. He has also worked with David Sylvian and Atom™.
