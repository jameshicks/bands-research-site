Overview

Jim Fourniadis is a recording engineer, producer, and guitarist active from at least 1987, centred on the New York hardcore and indie-punk scene of the late 1980s and early 1990s. He accumulated over 130 credits, with Vital Music accounting for the bulk of his studio work (18 releases, 1988–1996), and appearances on Alternative Tentacles, Shimmy Disc, and New Alliance Records rounding out the picture. His most sustained collaborative relationship is with James Kavoussi (23 shared credits), and he appears repeatedly alongside the Reagan Youth circle — Dave Insurgent, Paul Cripple, and Roy Edroso — suggesting a central engineering role for that band and its alumni. As a performer he plays in Rats Of Unusual Size, W.O.O., Gawk, and Acidental Potatochip.
