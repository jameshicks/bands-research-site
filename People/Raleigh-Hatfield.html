Overview

Raleigh Hatfield is a multi-instrumentalist — guitar, keyboards, percussion, and voice — and a member of Elf Power, the Athens, Georgia band central to the Elephant Six Collective. Their Discogs catalogue runs from 1995 to at least 2020, totalling 22 credits across indie rock, psychedelic rock, and lo-fi styles. Releases appeared primarily on Elephant Six Recording Co., Arena Rock, and Sugar Free Records. Their collaborator list maps the Elephant Six network closely: Elf Power bandmates Andrew Rieger and Laura Carter lead the count, followed by Will Cullen Hart, Scott Spillane, Jeff Mangum, and Bryan Helium, with producer Dave Fridmann also appearing across several shared credits.
