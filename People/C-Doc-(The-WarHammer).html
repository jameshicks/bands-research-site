David “C-Doc” Snyder is a producer, musician, emcee, and filmmaker. After years of making tapes of solo music for friends, he reached out to Chuck D of Public Enemy who began an internet collaboration with him and a number of other emcees. This group became the world’s first virtual rapp squad: The Impossebulls.

As the chief producer for The Impossebulls’ first two albums, C-Doc was also given the opportunity to remix songs for Public Enemy. This eventually led to Doc producing the epic song “Superman’s Black in the Building” for the New Whirl Odor album. Since then, C-Doc has produced and remixed numerous other songs for PE.

Collaborating with long time friend and fellow Impossebull Tirade on the 2006 LOWdown album groundZERO allowed Doc to fully indulge his love of early 90’s Hiphop. LOWdown followed up that album with an EP, dumpTRUCK, that was recently re-released in an extended edition through blocSonic.

Overview

David “C-Doc” Snyder is a producer, emcee, and filmmaker who reached out to Chuck D of Public Enemy after years of making solo recordings for friends — an outreach that led to The Impossebulls, recognized as the world’s first virtual rap squad. As chief producer on the group’s first two albums, he earned a place in the broader PE creative orbit, producing “Superman’s Black in the Building” for their New Whirl Odor album and contributing numerous remixes. His work spans boom bap, conscious, and jazzy hip-hop; a major parallel thread is LOWdown, a duo project with longtime collaborator Tirade that channeled early-90s hip-hop aesthetics. Activity from 2000 onward — 432 Discogs credits — is anchored primarily through The SpitSLAM Record Label Group and BlocSonic, with Chuck D, Marcus J Gilligan, and Tirade as his most consistent collaborators.
