Victoria Cheong

Musician, DJ. Discogs ID: 1951079. Also records as New Chance and DJ New Chance. Member of Hvywtr.

Primary collaborator with Chandra Oppenheim on the 2019 Blue Stage Sessions (Third Man Records) 7” and the 2022 Part Time Punks Session lathe-cut EP (We Are Time). Credited on both as vocals and melodica.
