Philip Glass

Born: January 31, 1937, Baltimore, Maryland Role: Composer, pianist Discogs artist ID: 20691 Groups: The Philip Glass Ensemble, Mabou Mines

Overview

Philip Glass is an American composer and pianist, one of the central figures in American musical minimalism alongside Steve Reich and La Monte Young. Born in Baltimore, he studied flute and later composition at the Peabody Conservatory, then at the University of Chicago and Juilliard (with Vincent Persichetti). In Paris he studied with Nadia Boulanger and also encountered the work of Darius Milhaud. A formative turning point came in 1966 when he worked as a music copyist for Ravi Shankar on the Chappaqua film project, absorbing Indian rhythmic principles that fundamentally altered his compositional approach.

In autumn 1968 Glass founded the Philip Glass Ensemble to perform his music, and in 1971 launched Chatham Square Productions (label ID: 48788) to release it. Early works — Music With Changing Parts (1971), Music In Similar Motion / Music In Fifths (1973), Music In Twelve Parts (1974–76) — established his additive rhythmic process and his break from conventional European serialism. The 1976 opera Einstein On The Beach, co-created with theater director Robert Wilson (2), brought international attention and is considered a landmark of 20th-century opera. Glassworks (CBS, 1982), designed as an accessible introduction to his music, became a genuine crossover LP.

From the mid-1980s onward Glass worked prolifically across film scoring (Koyaanisqatsi, Mishima, The Hours), opera (Satyagraha, Akhnaten), and symphony. He simultaneously maintained a presence in the downtown NYC music and art scenes, producing Polyrock’s debut album (RCA Victor, 1980) and contributing to pop/rock crossovers including Songs From Liquid Days (CBS, 1986), which featured lyrics by Paul Simon, Suzanne Vega, David Byrne, and Laurie Anderson. In 2002 he co-founded Orange Mountain Music with Kurt Munkacsi as the primary archive and catalog label for his work.

His principal long-term collaborators are Kurt Munkacsi (engineer/co-producer, ensemble member from 1970), Michael Riesman (musical director and conductor), and Jon Gibson (2) (founding Ensemble member, composer). Publisher: Dunvagen Music Publishers (Discogs label ID 266896).
