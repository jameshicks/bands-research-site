Katie Gately

Oct 28, 20265 min read

Katie Gately

Discogs artist ID: 1665317 Real name: Katie Gately Origin: Los Angeles, CA, USA Genres: Electronic, Experimental, Noise, Ambient, Techno Active: 2009–present Labels: Public Information, Blue Tapes, FatCat Records, Tri Angle, Houndstooth

Sound designer and music producer. Self-produces and writes all her material. Emerged in the early 2010s through tape and small-label releases before signing to Tri Angle for her 2016 debut LP Color. Moved to Houndstooth for the 2020 LP Loom and the 2023 double-album Fawn / Brute. Also a member of the Seattle Phonographers Union field recording collective (active 2009).

Career Overview

Early Period (2009–2013)

Gately’s earliest documented release is as part of the Seattle Phonographers Union, a field-recording collective, appearing on a 2009 release on Mimeomeme / and/OAR. Her first solo output arrived in 2013: the self-titled 12” mini-album on Public Information (cat. PUBINF011) and the cassette single Pipes on Blue Tapes (cat. Blue Eight). Both are entirely self-produced and self-mixed, establishing her template of dense layered electronics with processed vocals. The Public Information LP features cover artwork by Austin Irving.

Split / Early Label Period (2013–2015)

In 2014 she contributed one side of Split Series #23 on FatCat Records (cat. 12FAT093, 12”/EP, limited), split with Tlaotlon; her track “Pivot” is produced and mixed by herself. The same year she appeared on a lathe-cut 7” split with drone/tape artist Prayer (Joe Houpert) on FET Press (cat. fet 008), contributing the track “FAR”. She also appeared on the Blue Tapes compilation Sonic Blue Two (2014) as a featured artist.

Tri Angle Period (2016)

Color (2016, Tri Angle, cat. TRIANGLE38, LP) is her debut full-length and primary statement. All material is written, produced, and mixed by Gately. Mastered by Heba Kadry; artwork by Tomory Dodge; design and layout by Collin Fletcher. Tracklist: “Lift”, “Tuck”, “Sift”, “Rive”, “Frisk”, “Sire”, “Color”. Genres: Electronic, Pop / Ambient, Experimental. The release connected her firmly to the Tri Angle label’s wider roster of avant-club and dark electronic artists — The Haxan Cloak, Vessel (5), Holy Other, Balam Acab, Evian Christ.

Serpentwithfeet Collaboration (2018)

Gately produced six tracks (1, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11) on Serpentwithfeet’s debut LP Soil (2018, Tri Angle / Secretly Canadian, release ID 12092769), and has a sole writing credit on track 1. She shares production duties with Clams Casino (tracks 2, 4, 5, 7) and mmph (tracks 6, 9). Executive producer: Robin Carolan (also A&R and management). Mastered by Heba Kadry.

Houndstooth Period (2020–2023)

Loom (2020, Houndstooth, cat. HTH121, LP/Limited Edition Stereo) is her second full-length. Written, produced, performed, and partially mixed by Gately; additional mixing by Jason Agel. Mastered by Matt Colton. Artwork by Andrew Kenji Thorpe. Tracklist: “Ritual”, “Allay”, “Waltz”, “Bracer”, “Rite”, “Tower”, “Flow”, “Rest”. Styles: Experimental, Techno. A digital single Flow also appeared on Houndstooth in 2020.

Fawn / Brute (2023, Houndstooth, cat. HTH170, LP) is a double-sided album structured in two halves: “Fawn” (Side A: “Seed”, “Howl”, “Fawn”, “Cleave”, “Peeve”, “Scale”) and “Brute” (Side B: “Meat”, “Brute”, “Chaw”, “Tame”, “Melt”). Styles: Abstract, Industrial.

Scene Connections

Gately’s Tri Angle period placed her within a loose scene of UK/US avant-electronic artists on that label: The Haxan Cloak, Holy Other, Balam Acab, Evian Christ, Vessel (5), Rabit (2), Sd Laika, Lotic, Roly Porter, Forest Swords. The label was run by Robin Carolan.

Her move to Houndstooth (Fabric Records’ sister label) in 2020 placed her alongside artists such as Floating Points and others in the experimental/club-adjacent space.

Her early career overlaps with the Seattle experimental/field-recording scene via the Seattle Phonographers Union.
