Oct 02, 20262 min read

Kendra Smith

Discogs ID: 273189
Instrument: Bass, vocals
Scene: Paisley Underground

Co-founder of The Dream Syndicate (1981–c.1983) alongside Steve Wynn, Karl Precoda, and Dennis Duck. Also a member of Rainy Day (1984) and later Opal (with David Roback). Known for her bass work on The Days Of Wine And Roses (1982) and the self-titled EP (1982); also took a vocal spotlight on that LP.
