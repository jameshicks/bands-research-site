Susan Lyall

Guitarist and vocalist. New York City. Discogs artist ID: 848238.

Overview

Susan Lyall was a guitarist and vocalist in Band Of Susans, appearing on the band’s first two releases — the 1987 EP Blessing And Curse and the 1988 debut LP Hope Against Hope. She was part of the original founding lineup built around the conceit of multiple Susans: the guitar section comprised Lyall, Susan Tallman, and Susan Stenger (on bass), alongside Robert Poss on guitar/vocals, Ron Spitzer on drums, and Alva Rogers on vocals.

Her specific contribution was guitar and vocals on all tracks from that original period. By the band’s next LP, Love Agenda (1989), Lyall and Tallman had both departed and were replaced by Karen Haglof and Page Hamilton. Lyall is also retroactively credited on the Band Of Susans retrospective compilation Wired For Sound: 1986–1993, where her tracks from the early period are collected on disc one alongside later material.

Discogs lists no aliases, solo work, or other projects for Susan Lyall beyond Band Of Susans.
