R. Stevie Moore

Discogs ID: 220114
Real name: Robert Steven Moore
Born: 1952, Nashville, Tennessee
Based: New Jersey (later New York)

Lo-fi multi-instrumentalist and prolific self-releasing artist. First act on Cuneiform Records: What’s The Point?!! (RUNE 1, 1984) — the label’s debut release, making Moore the foundational entry in the Cuneiform catalog. Moore had already been self-releasing cassettes and albums for years before this.

Leicester Connections

Issued LP Verve (HAM 15, 1985) on Hamster Records And Tapes, Terry Burrows’ Leicester-based label. He contributed vocals to The Chrysanthemums’ debut LP Is That A Fish On Your Shoulder Or Are You Just Pleased To See Me? (1987), singing on “The Holocaust Parade.” In the 2000s, Moore and Burrows collaborated as The Yung - Moore Show, releasing Yung & Moore Versus The Whole Goddam Stinkin’ World (Orgone Records ORGONE1, 2006) — a 15-track album compiled by Burrows.
