WestBam

Discogs ID: 6092 Real name: Maximilian Lenz Aliases: Beatbox Rocker, Mr. Y, WB Loops, Von Sternhagel

German techno, rave, and electro DJ/producer. One of the foundational figures of German rave culture; long associated with the Love Parade, Berlin, and the Mayday raves. Collaborated with Richard Butler on You Need The Drugs (Vertigo Berlin, 2013) — a release that spawned at least 11 pressings/editions including remixes by Thomas P. Heckmann (DJ Hell), Kobosil, and Public Possession (&ME).

Label Affiliations

Low Spirit Recordings (Discogs ID: 1382)

WestBam co-founded Low Spirit Recordings in 1986 together with his brother Fabian Lenz (DJ Dick), William Röttger, Klaus Jankuhn, and Sandra Molzahn (per Discogs artist profile). The label is a sublabel of the parent company Low Spirit Recordings GmbH (Discogs ID: 417166).

Low Spirit Recordings has 17 sublabels in Discogs, including:

  • Amtrax
  • Electric Kingdom
  • Fire Recordings
  • Freundschaft Musik
  • Loud & Slow
  • Nextfire Recordings
  • Road Rage Records
  • Westbam Disco Riot Record

WestBam’s discography on Low Spirit Recordings runs from 1988 (“Disco Deutschland”) through at least 2003, covering his core rave/techno period. Key releases on the label: The Cabinet (1989), The Roof Is On Fire (1990), Wizards Of The Sonic (1994), Agharta - The City Of Shamballa (1998). Several releases were co-issued with major labels — Polydor (1989, 1991), BMG (1997), Urban (1993, 2022).

Later Label History

After the Low Spirit core years, WestBam moved through:

  • Motor Music / BMG (2001, 2016–2017)
  • Bass Planet (his own imprint, 2008–2013)
  • Vertigo Berlin (2013)
  • Embassy One (2020–2022)
  • Müller Records (2025)
