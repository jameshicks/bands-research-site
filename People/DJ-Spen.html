DJ Spen

Real name: Sean Stafford Spencer Born: Baltimore, Maryland Discogs ID: 1497 Also known as: Sean Spencer (alias), SP3N, Spen Active: 1994–present

Baltimore-based house DJ, producer, and label head. One of the central architects of the soulful/gospel house sound that came out of Baltimore in the mid-1990s via Basement Boys Records. Longtime member of The Basement Boys production collective. Ran Code Red Recordings (2004–2011), then co-founded Quantize Recordings (2011–present) with his wife Kelly Tribble Spencer and former Basement Boys collaborator Thommy Davis.

Biography

Baltimore Origins — The Basement Boys Era (1994–2002)

DJ Spen came up in Baltimore as part of The Basement Boys, a large production collective that also included Teddy Douglas, Thommy Davis, Maurice Fulton, Neal Conway, Jay Steinhour, Kris Klayton (= Karizma), Victor Williams, and Wayne Cooper. The group ran Basement Boys Records out of Baltimore, which became one of the defining American soulful/gospel house labels of the late 1990s.

His primary vehicle on the label was “DJ Spen Presents Jasper Street Co.” — a long-running series of releases credited to himself and Jasper Street Co. (a Philadelphia-based vocal house group). The string of Basement Boys Records releases ran from catalog number BB002 (1995) through the early 2000s and established his signature approach: tightly arranged gospel-inflected vocals, live instrumentation (bass, guitar, horns, keyboards), and meticulous studio production.

He also worked with fellow Basement Boy Karizma under the group name DJ Spen & Karizma (Discogs ID 14216), releasing on BBR and its catalog from the late 1990s onward. The “DJ Spen & Karizma” configuration was Spen (Sean Spencer) and Karizma (Kris Klayton/Chris Theadore Clayton).

Other notable Basement Boys Records artists he produced or was associated with: Kenny Bobien, Teddy Douglas, Tracy Hamlin, Tawanna, Michelle Weeks, Those Guys, Soul Pie, Ann Nesby.

DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz (early 2000s)

After departing from the main Basement Boys collective in the early 2000s, Spen formed DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz — a group combining him with The MuthaFunkaz (Gary Hudgins, Gary Deane, Irvin Madden). This configuration released on Defected Records (UK):

Gabryelle (2005, Defected) was a significant UK release in the soulful house market, with credits including Gary Hudgins, Gary Deane, Irvin Madden, DJ Technic (Glenn Brand), and DJ Spen himself.

Quantize Recordings Era (2011–present)

In 2011, Spen co-founded Quantize Recordings (Discogs ID 404562) with his wife Kelly Tribble Spencer and Thommy Davis. Quantize has grown into one of the most prolific active soulful/gospel house labels, with 643+ releases cataloged on Discogs. Its sublabel structure includes:

  • unquantize (ID 883581) — deeper/underground releases
  • Solid Ground Recordings (ID 168462)
  • Reelsoul Musik (ID 1213103)
  • Room Control (ID 36375)
  • QU3 (ID 2944631)

Quantize also became an important label for other artists including Tracy Hamlin, David Anthony (18), Michele Chiavarini, Tasha Larae, Marc Evans, John Morales, Diephuis, and many others. The label consistently releases remixes from the broader gospel/soulful house network.

Remix and International Work

Throughout his career Spen has been a prolific remixer. His discography (when including all credits, not just primary releases) runs to several hundred entries. He has appeared on UK labels including Black Vinyl Records (Disco Dreams volumes, 1999–2000), Defected, Soul Heaven Records, SuSU (Southport Weekender compilations), MN2S, and KneeDeep USA. International collaborators include Italian house producers (Antonello Ferrari, Aldo Bergamasco) and European DJs (Diephuis, Ezel, Neil Pierce).

Connections

  • The Basement Boys — Baltimore production collective Spen was part of from the mid-1990s through the early 2000s. Other members include Teddy Douglas, Thommy Davis, Karizma, and Maurice Fulton.
  • Jasper Street Co. — Philadelphia-based gospel/house vocal group that served as Spen’s primary collaborating act on Basement Boys Records.
  • Quantize Recordings — Spen’s current label home and business; the central node for contemporary soulful house.
  • Tony Humphries — Shared releases; both key figures in the soulful house tradition.
  • Ten City / Byron Stingily — remixed “Devotion” (Ultra Records, 2021, reunion era); co-credited on It’s All Jesus (Spiritually Sound Records, 2004, “DJ Spen Presents Byron Stingily”)
