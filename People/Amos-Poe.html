Amos Poe

Discogs ID: 2234748
Born: September 30, 1949, Tel Aviv, Israel
Died: December 25, 2025
Active: c. 1975–2022
Role: Film director, screenwriter, cinematographer

NYC-based director and screenwriter, born in Israel. Central figure in the CBGB-era underground cinema movement — his Super 8 documentary work of the mid-to-late 1970s constitutes one of the primary visual records of the New York punk scene.

The Blank Generation (1976)

Shot in 1975–76 at CBGB on Super 8 by Poe and Ivan Kral (then guitarist with Patti Smith Group), The Blank Generation captured live performances at the club before the scene had any significant commercial recognition. The film documents:

Robert Fripp — Exposure (1979)

Poe contributed “Vtr Images” (video artwork) to Robert Fripp’s solo album Exposure (EG/Polydor, EGLP 101, 1979). The artwork credits also include Steve Sprouse (colorist) and Chris Stein (design, photography). The project — produced by Fripp and featuring Brian Eno, Peter Gabriel, Phil Collins, Peter Hammill, Daryl Hall, and others — sits outside the NYC punk scene proper but illustrates the overlapping social networks of the period.
