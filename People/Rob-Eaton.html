Overview

Rob Eaton is a New York-based producer, engineer, and mixer whose career dates to 1982 and spans more than 1,500 credits across contemporary jazz, fusion, pop rock, and synth-pop. He is most closely associated with Pat Metheny and the Pat Metheny Group, functioning as a primary technical collaborator across their catalogue from the mid-1980s through the 2010s, with concentrated label ties at Metheny Group Productions, Nonesuch, Warner Bros., and Geffen. His session work also places him alongside Nile Rodgers, Jimmy Bralower, and other New York figures, with The Power Station cited as a key base of operations. In addition to his studio career, he is a member of Dark Star Orchestra, the long-running Grateful Dead tribute act.
