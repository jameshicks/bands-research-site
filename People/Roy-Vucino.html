Roy Vucino

Guitarist, vocalist, and multi-instrumentalist from Québec, Canada. Discogs artist ID: 1113164. Also credited as Choyce (ID 1836816) and The Warden.

Overview

Vucino is the founding leader and sole constant member of Red Mass, a Montreal psych/garage/experimental collective active since 2008. He has also been a member of at least sixteen other bands in the Montreal underground. He records under his own name, as Choyce, and occasionally as The Warden.
