Biography

Daniel Lippel is an American classical and contemporary guitarist whose career spans solo performance, chamber music, commissioning, and improvisation. He has been called an “exciting soloist” by the NY Times, a “versatile and skillful guitarist” by Time Out New York, and a “modern guitar polymath” by Guitar Review.

Lippel co-founded New Focus Recordings (Discogs label ID: 641358), a label specializing in contemporary American composed music, which has become his primary recording home and one of his most sustained artistic commitments. His debut solo album Resonance appeared on New Focus in 2004 (catalogue FCR101, credited as “Daniel Lippel”), featuring works by Mario Davidovsky, Nils Vigeland, Elliott Carter, Soonjung Suh, Judah E. Adashi, and Peter Gilbert (3), with mastering by Ryan Streber. A second solo Bach album, J.S. Bach | BWV 998, 1003, 1010, followed in 2005, produced by Peter Gilbert (3) with engineering by Ryan Streber.

He is a member of two ensembles documented on Discogs. Flexible Music (members: Lippel on guitar, Eric Huebner on piano, Haruka Fuji on percussion, Tim Ruedeman on saxophone) released FM in 2009 on New Focus Recordings and Sustenance in 2007 (also New Focus). The group also appeared on the Albany Records compilation Terror And Memory (2011), where Lippel is specifically credited as “Guitar [Flexible Music].” International Contemporary Ensemble (ICE; Discogs ID: 2002936) is a large new-music ensemble of which Lippel is a listed member alongside Claire Chase, Tyshawn Sorey, and many others. His ICE credits include Abandoned Time (2008, New Focus, co-primary credit alongside ICE, where he also served as producer and wrote liner notes), Ice by Dai Fujikura (2013, Kairos, guitar and editing producer), and Æsopica by Marcos Balter (2016, New Focus/Tundra, guitar), and Dinosaur Scar by Du Yun (2018, Tundra, editing producer).

Beyond these ensemble homes, Lippel has appeared widely as a guest performer and contributor. He played guitar on Cory Smythe’s Circulate Susanna (2018, Pyroclastic Records) — a detuned acoustic guitar and electronics part alongside vocalist Sofia Jernberg, with mixing by Ron St. Germain; Lippel also co-wrote one track as an improvisation. He contributed guitar to Scott L. Miller’s Raba (2018, New Focus), a piece for guitar, saxophone (Laura Cocks), and Ensemble U:. He played guitar on …Through Which The Past Shines…: Works By Nils Vigeland And Reiko Füting (2018, New Focus), a chamber album featuring Nils Vigeland and Reiko Füting. In 2016 he recorded Steve Reich’s Electric Counterpoint with the composer (co-primary credit; New Focus), playing all guitar and electric bass parts.

His solo discography on New Focus also includes Mirrored Spaces (2019) and Against Method (2020, on which he is also listed as supervisor of text). In 2021 he produced and performed on Aufs Lautenwerk, a J.S. Bach lute-works album co-released on New Focus and Microfest, with recording/mixing by John Schneider (5) and mastering by Ryan Streber.

Lippel has served as producer or editing producer on multiple New Focus releases beyond his own: Abandoned Time (ICE, 2008), The Art of Fugue (2017), American Romantics II (2017, production advisor), Against Method (2020), Assemblage Chamber (2022, also contributing additional sounds), Entanglements (2022, session and executive producer), and Giga Concerto (2021, guitar).

His connection to Mice Parade (Adam Pierce’s project) extends across multiple albums: he appears on the self-titled Mice Parade (2007, FatCat), What It Means To Be Left-Handed (2010, FatCat), Candela (2012/2013, P-Vine/FatCat), and Lapapọ (2022), consistently in a performer/guitarist role.

He has contributed liner notes to several releases including SEAMUS Records compilations Music from SEAMUS, Vol. 29 (2020) and Music From SEAMUS Vol. 30 (2021) and Music From SEAMUS, Vol. 31 (2022). He also wrote liner notes for Azica’s Infusion (2016).

Other notable appearances: Subject (2015, Tzadik, guitar); Seven Responses (2017, Innova Recordings, guitar); Letting Time Circle Through Us (2017, New World Records, acoustic guitar); Off Brand (2020, Panoramic Recordings/New Focus, nylon string guitar); Tendrils (2020, Exit Stencil Recordings, guitar); Oblivion (2023, New Focus, electric guitar).
