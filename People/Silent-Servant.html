Silent Servant

Discogs ID: 651504 (note: an earlier vault entry had 1995139 — 651504 is the correct ID) Real name: John Juan Mendez Origin: Los Angeles, California Died: January 18, 2024 Aliases: El Guapo (12), Jasper, Juan Mendez, Silent Editions Name variations: SS, Silent Servants

Los Angeles industrial / techno producer. Active from 2006. Core member of the Sandwell District collective. Died January 18, 2024 at his Los Angeles home alongside Luis Vasquez (The Soft Moon) and Mendez’s partner Simone Ling (ID 10685491) — cause of death: fentanyl, cocaine, ketamine, and ethanol.
