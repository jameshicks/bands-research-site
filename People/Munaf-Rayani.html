Munaf Rayani

Guitarist and co-founder of Explosions In The Sky (Austin, TX). Discogs artist ID: 471901. Active in Discogs records from 2001 to 2023. Also a member of the Austin side project Grizzly Adams Family.

Career

Munaf Rayani was born in Midland, Texas. He co-founded Explosions In The Sky in 1999, initially under the name Breaker Morant, alongside Mark T. Smith (guitar), Michael James (bass), and Christopher Hrasky (drums). The band relocated to Austin and signed to Temporary Residence Limited for their proper debut in 2001. Rayani has remained one of the band’s two guitarists across their entire existence — a stable four-piece lineup that has not changed in over twenty-five years.

His first credit in the Discogs database is a guest appearance on The American Analog Set’s Know By Heart (Tiger Style, 2001), appearing alongside Ben Gibbard (Death Cab for Cutie) and other Austin and broader indie-rock community figures. This reflects the close-knit Austin post-rock and indie scene of the early 2000s in which Explosions In The Sky came up.

The bulk of Rayani’s documented work is as co-writer and performer on Explosions In The Sky’s studio albums and film scores. The band’s breakthrough came with The Earth Is Not A Cold Dead Place (2003), which established their international reputation. Over the following two decades they contributed scores to several major films — most notably the Friday Night Lights OST (2004), directed by Peter Berg, which brought them to a large mainstream audience — and returned to standalone studio albums through End (2023).

A notable side appearance: Rayani is listed in the live crew credits on the Mono (7) release Holy Ground: NYC Live With The Wordless Music Orchestra (Temporary Residence Limited, 2010), suggesting a working or touring-adjacent relationship with the Japanese post-rock band.

Beyond Explosions In The Sky, Rayani is a member of Grizzly Adams Family, an Austin-based project also featuring Bobby Lockhart, Brad Bell (2), Brooks Harlan (of Jawbox / Office Of Future Plans), and Carlos Torres (11). No Grizzly Adams Family releases are catalogued in the Discogs database as of 2025.
