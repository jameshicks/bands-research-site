Overview

Elisa Rodrigues (born 29 September 1986, Cascais, Portugal) is a Portuguese jazz vocalist, songwriter, and lyricist whose career divides into two distinct strands. Her Portuguese work — jazz and bossa nova, developed independently from the late 2000s onward — is the primary strand; her credits with These New Puritans are a significant but time-limited collaboration (2012–2015) on that band’s Field of Reeds album and subsequent touring. She began singing in the Coro Pequenos Cantores do Estoril at age eight (1994), discovered jazz at fifteen, studied at Michel Giacometti music school from 2003, and joined jazz vocal project ELLE in 2007. Despite holding a degree in Fashion Design from UTL, she pursued music full-time. Her Portuguese labels (JACC Records, Casete, Sintoma Records, Sons Em Trânsito) reflect her domestic jazz/bossa nova career rather than any overlap with the TNP art-rock world; JACC Records in particular is a Coimbra-based label dedicated to jazz and creative music (founded 2010 within Jazz ao Centro Clube). Her 20 Discogs credits span 2011 into the 2020s, with the TNP work on Infectious Music being the only UK-facing portion of her output.

Notes (web)

Portuguese career strand vs. TNP involvement: These are clearly separate strands. Rodrigues is a Portuguese jazz/bossa nova singer who was recruited by These New Puritans for Field of Reeds (2013) as an outside collaborator, then toured with them internationally (Barbican, Hollywood Bowl). The Portuguese label credits — JACC Records, Casete, Sintoma Records, Sons Em Trânsito — are all part of her domestic jazz career, which predates the TNP work (Heart Mouth Dialogues is 2011) and continued after it (As Blue As Red, 2018, via Sons Em Trânsito under exclusive licence to Universal Music Portugal). Wikipedia’s TNP article describing her as a “Portuguese fado and jazz singer” is slightly imprecise — her work is primarily jazz and bossa nova rather than fado proper, per her own site (“exploring her jazz & bossa nova roots”) and press coverage. Source: elisarodrigues.pt

JACC Records: Founded 2010 in Coimbra within Jazz ao Centro Clube, a non-profit dedicated to “promotion and dissemination of jazz and creative music.” Publishes debut recordings alongside established musicians. Heart Mouth Dialogues was among its early releases. Source: jacc-records.com

Sons Em Trânsito: Portuguese booking/management agency and label representing artists from the Portuguese music scene; As Blue As Red (2018) was released under Sons Em Trânsito with exclusive licensing to Universal Music Portugal. Source: universalmusic.pt

Festival da Canção 2017: She was selected by Noiserv for the Festival da Canção 2017 but was replaced before the competition aired following a car accident. Source: festivaiscancao.wordpress.com — she was later a candidate again for FC2020.
