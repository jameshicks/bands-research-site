David Gamson

Keyboardist, producer. Discogs ID: 47970.

Keyboardist and co-producer of Scritti Politti alongside Green Gartside and Fred Maher during the band’s mid-1980s pop phase (Cupid & Psyche 85, Rough Trade, 1985). Produced Adele Bertei’s “When It’s Over” / “Every Little Bit Hurts” (Chrysalis CHS 2907, 1985) alongside Maher and John Potoker. The Scritti Politti connection brought the high-gloss pop production aesthetic of that band directly into Bertei’s solo career.

Production & Writing Credits Outside Scritti Politti

Gamson’s non-SP work spans mainstream R&B, pop, and soul from the mid-1980s through the 2010s, with a heavy concentration on Warner/Reprise-family artists and the Maverick/Paisley Park orbit.

Key observations:

  • Chaka Khan is his single biggest non-SP client (1986–1997), spanning at least five projects across Warner/Reprise/MCA
  • Me’Shell NdegéOcello’s Plantation Lullabies (Maverick, 1993) is his most critically noted non-SP production; he also produced much of Peace Beyond Passion (1996)
  • Roger Troutman of Zapp was a significant 1988–1992 collaborator (Reprise/Warner)
  • Maxwell’s Urban Hang Suite (Columbia, 1996) — Gamson played drums and keys
  • His songwriting credit on Kesha’s “Animal” (2010) and Kelly Clarkson’s “Stronger” (2012) shows his reach into mainstream pop
  • Late career: Hannah Diamond (PC Music, 2022–23) signals continued activity into the hyperpop-adjacent sphere

Web Sources

Green Gartside backing vocals on Adele Bertei “When It’s Over” (Chrysalis, 1985)

Status: Confirmed — multiple independent web sources agree that Green Gartside sang backing vocals on Adele Bertei’s “When It’s Over” (Chrysalis CHS 2907, 1985), produced by Gamson, Fred Maher, and John Potoker.

Sources and what each one says:

  • Post-Punk Monk review (2025-12-16): “With backing vocal, for good measure, by Green Gartside his good self.” Describes his part as “closer to a duet in how it had been structured.” Does not quote liner notes directly. https://postpunkmonk.com/2025/12/16/record-review-adele-bertei-when-its-over-12/
  • myvinyldreams blog (2025-06-27): Credits Gartside explicitly as “backing vocals” — “a bit of collab with Scritti Politti with Green Gartside doing backing vocals.” https://myvinyldreams.wordpress.com/2025/06/27/adele-bertei-when-its-over-12-re-ups/
  • Wikipedia (Adele Bertei article): States Gartside provided “guest vocals” on the track. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele_Bertei (as of 2026-07-17)
  • YouTube upload titles: Both the Dance Mix and Metal Mix uploads are titled “Adele Bertei feat. Green Gartside”, consistent with a featured/prominent vocal credit. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eV9Cky3arN8 / https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q8M0lkge9WQ
  • Lost Pop Treasures blog (2010): Notes Gartside as providing “featured vocals.” http://lostpoptreasures.blogspot.com/2010/09/adele-bertei-when-its-over-1985.html

Caveat: No source quotes the actual sleeve/label credit verbatim from the physical record. The exact wording on the Chrysalis pressing (e.g., “backing vocals”, “guest vocals”, or “featured”) is not confirmed from primary documentation. Discogs release pages (r156808, r4520775) were inaccessible (403) during this research pass — those would be the best place to find the literal liner note text.
