Ches Smith

Discogs ID: 398334 Origin: Sacramento, California Aliases: Ches Smith’s Congs For Brums

American drummer and percussionist active in experimental rock, jazz, and free improvisation. Member of Secret Chiefs 3, Xiu Xiu, 7 Year Rabbit Cycle, Marc Ribot’s Ceramic Dog, Tim Berne’s Snakeoil, Mary Halvorson Octet, Trevor Dunn’s Trio-Convulsant, and dozens of other groups.

Overview

One of the most in-demand session and ensemble drummers connecting West Coast experimental rock with New York avant-jazz. Played drums on Secret Chiefs 3’s Xaphan (Book Of Angels Volume 9) (2008, Tzadik). Played drums and vibraphone on 7 Year Rabbit Cycle’s Ache Hornes (2006, FPS013). Released Congs for Brums as a solo record on Free Porcupine Society (FPS015, 2006). Bandcamp active under solo name and Pyroclastic Records.
