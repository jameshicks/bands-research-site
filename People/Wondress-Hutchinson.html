Wondress Hutchinson

Real name: Wondress F. Hutchinson Born: 20 June 1964 Died: 1 May 2021 (age 56) Also known as: Wondress Discogs ID: 125480 Active: 1989–2017 Primary labels: Capitol Records, EMI Electrola, Basement Boys Records, Dejavoo Records, Quantize Recordings

American vocalist working across electronic dance, hip-house, jazz-fusion, and soulful house. Best known for two UK Top 10 hits with Mantronix in 1989–1990, then for her work with Jasper Street Co. and DJ Spen on Basement Boys Records in the late 1990s, and a later house career into the 2010s alongside DJ Joio.

Capitol / EMI Period (1989–1995)

Hutchinson first appeared as the featured vocalist on Mantronix’s Got To Have Your Love (1989, Capitol Records, cat. 12CL 559), a UK 12” in the hip-house and breakbeat styles. Credited as “Wondress” on the sleeve, she sang lead and backing vocals, with production by Kurtis Mantronik (Kurtis el Khaleel). The single reached the UK Top 10.

She returned on Take Your Time (Remix) (1990, Capitol Records, cat. 12CLX 573), again as a primary co-credit. The A-side “Beat You Down Mix” credits her as lead vocalist; the B-sides are remixes of Got To Have Your Love by Steve “Silk” Hurley, where she appears as a backing vocalist. Both releases were pressed across multiple territories (UK, Germany, Italy, Europe, US).

A reissue compilation Got To Have Your Love / Take Your Time appeared on Old Gold (2) in 1992 (4 pressings).

In 1995 she contributed backing vocals to track 8 (“Let’s Say Goodbye”) of Spyro Gyra’s album Love & Other Obsessions (GRP Records, GRD 9808). This was a session credit alongside vocalists Deniece Williams and Barrington Henderson among others; her role was limited to that one track.

Basement Boys / Soulful House Period (1997–2003)

By the late 1990s, Hutchinson had moved into the Baltimore-rooted soulful and garage house world, working with DJ Spen and Jasper Street Co. on Basement Boys Records. She is listed as a full member of Jasper Street Co. on their Discogs page (one of 21 credited members).

Solid Ground (1997, Basement Boys Records BB015)

Solid Ground is a DJ Spen presents Jasper Street Co. 12” (cat. BB015). Hutchinson is credited as Vocals and Written-By alongside an ensemble cast. The track was produced and arranged by Josane D’Shadeauxman and Sean Spencer (DJ Spen’s real name), with live bass (Vince Loving), guitar (Sukarno Richardson), and a large vocal collective including Tracy Hamlin, Karizma, Starr Adkins, Fruity, and others.

Love Changes (1998, Basement Boys Records BBR016)

Love Changes (cat. BBR016) is the follow-up 12” on Basement Boys, again DJ Spen presents Jasper Street Co. Hutchinson is credited as Lead Vocals and Backing Vocals, as well as Written-By (anv “Wonderess Hutchinson” on this release). Side B carries a Tommy Musto remix of Solid Ground.

Shaggy — Strength Of A Woman (2003)

Hutchinson contributed backing vocals (tracks 1–2) to Shaggy’s Strength Of A Woman promo CD (Big Yard Music Group / MCA Records, 2003, UK). She is credited as “Wondress Hutchinson For Diva Express,” indicating she was operating under or affiliated with a vocal group / ensemble called Diva Express at that time.

Later House Career (2013–2017)

Hutchinson re-emerged as a primary credited artist alongside Spanish producer DJ Joio on two house releases:

  • Love The Music (2013, Dejavoo Records DV072, Spain) — digital EP (WAV), produced by DJ Joio (credited as JoioDJ). Includes Earl Tutu & John Khan and Sandro Valentino remixes.
  • Music (2017, Quantize Recordings QTZ147, US) — digital EP (MP3), billed as DJ Spen Presents DJ Joio feat. Wondress Hutchinson. Hutchinson credited as Vocals and Written-By. Produced and arranged by DJ Joio; presented and mixed by DJ Spen.

The 2017 Music release marks her last Discogs-documented credit.
