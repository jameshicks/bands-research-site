Dave Wright (2)

UK saxophonist. Discogs artist ID: 259046.

Second saxophonist in the original Essential Logic lineup alongside Lora Logic.

Overview

Dave Wright played tenor saxophone, backing vocals, and cowbell on Essential Logic’s key early recordings, giving the band its distinctive twin-saxophone sound. He appears on Beat Rhythm News – Waddle Ya Play? (Rough Trade, 1979) and on “Wake Up” (Virgin, 1979). He also appeared on Logically Yours (2022), the career retrospective.
