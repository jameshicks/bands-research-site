Harold Faltermeyer

German composer, keyboardist, arranger, and producer. Born Hans Hugo Harold Faltermeier, October 5, 1952, in Munich. Discogs artist ID: 36628. Real name: Hans Hugo Harold Faltermeier.

Best known for the “Axel F” theme from Beverly Hills Cop (1984) and the Top Gun Anthem (1986), both of which won him Grammy Awards. A core session player and arranger in the Giorgio Moroder Munich circle from the late 1970s, Faltermeyer later built a parallel career as a Hollywood soundtrack composer and pop producer. His 2022 return to the Top Gun: Maverick score — co-credited alongside Hans Zimmer and Lady Gaga — confirmed the durability of his signature themes.

Background and the Munich Circle (1972–1978)

Faltermeyer’s earliest Discogs credits begin in 1972 as an engineer and arranger on German children’s records (Intercord, Fontana) and light-entertainment material on Polydor and Ariola. By the mid-1970s he had moved into production and arrangement work for German pop acts on Ariola.

The pivotal connection was Giorgio Moroder. By 1977–78 Faltermeyer was a full member of the Moroder studio core — alongside Keith Forsey (drums/percussion) and engineer Jürgen Koppers — working out of Munich’s Musicland Studios. His credited roles on Moroder productions in this period ranged from keyboards and synthesizer to engineer and arranger.

The Battlestar Galactica album (1978, Casablanca) is notable as a full production credit shared with Moroder — Faltermeyer is listed as engineer, producer, mixer, keyboardist, arranger, and co-writer on the original compositions side.

Also in this orbit: Pete Bellotte (Moroder’s writing partner on the Summer records), and Sylvester Levay (keyboards on The Wanderer).

Donna Summer and Laura Branigan (1979–1984)

Beyond the individual albums, Faltermeyer appears throughout Donna Summer’s Casablanca catalog in arranging, keyboard, and engineering roles. He was part of the team on No More Tears (Enough Is Enough), Dim All The Lights, Sunset People, Walk Away, and the On The Radio hits compilation.

His first significant American production credit outside the Summer circle came with Laura Branigan. On her 1984 album Self Control (Atlantic, master 102038), he is credited as additional synthesizer and engineer on multiple tracks — the album became one of Branigan’s biggest sellers.

Beverly Hills Cop and “Axel F” (1984)

The Beverly Hills Cop soundtrack (1984, MCA Records) was Faltermeyer’s breakthrough as a Hollywood composer. He wrote the original score (Music By credit on the soundtrack LP). The main theme, “Axel F,” was released as a single and charted at #3 in the US in 1985. Producers of the film were Don Simpson and Jerry Bruckheimer.

Alongside the score, the soundtrack included Glenn Frey’s “The Heat Is On” — written and co-produced by Faltermeyer and Keith Forsey (produced jointly). This single reached #2 on the Billboard Hot 100.

Following Beverly Hills Cop, Faltermeyer became the go-to composer for the Simpson-Bruckheimer action aesthetic.

The Top Gun score won Faltermeyer a second Grammy. The “Top Gun Anthem” became one of the defining cues of 1980s action cinema. On Top Gun, he is credited as Music Score alongside the various pop songs contributed by other artists.

The Running Man (1987, Arnold Schwarzenegger vehicle) was a full solo score credit — Faltermeyer as primary artist and producer.

The Pet Shop Boys relationship is significant: Faltermeyer co-produced their 1990 album Behaviour alongside Neil Tennant and Chris Lowe. The album also featured orchestral arrangements by Angelo Badalamenti and Alexander Balanescu.

Kim Wilde was another consistent partnership: Square Rooms (1984), Cold Dresses (1984), Heart Over Mind (1987), I Come Undone (1987), Passion (1988), Beat Of Love (1991).

Beverly Hills Cop II and Return (1987–1990s)

The Beverly Hills Cop II score (1987 film) was not released at the time; it appeared in expanded form as Beverly Hills Cop II (Music From The Motion Picture) in 2016 via La-La Land Records, with Faltermeyer credited as composer, producer, and performer of the original music.

Other late-1980s/1990s productions:

  • Willy Bogner’s Fire, Ice & Dynamite (1990) — produced, arranged, keyboards
  • Artists United For Nature “Yes We Can” (1989, Virgin) — performer, producer, written-by (charity single group including Faltermeyer)
  • Frankie (ZDF TV series soundtrack, 1995, BMG) — keyboards, produced, arranged

Top Gun: Maverick (2022)

In 2022, Faltermeyer returned to score Top Gun: Maverick alongside Hans Zimmer and Lady Gaga. The Top Gun: Maverick (Music From The Motion Picture) (2022, Interscope/Paramount) credits Faltermeyer, Zimmer, and Gaga equally as primary artists; the score section credits them jointly as Music By. Also listed as Technical Assistant: Florian Faltermeyer (artist ID 8078227) — presumably Harold’s son.

The Beverly Hills Cop: Axel F Netflix film (2024) also used his original Beverly Hills Cop themes (La-La Land Records release).
