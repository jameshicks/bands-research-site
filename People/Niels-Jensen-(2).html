Swedish actor, singer, songwriter, artist and film director, born on May 8, 1964, in Faaborg, Denmark.

Niels Jensen was born in Denmark but soon moved to Lund, Sweden where he at an early age started exploring his many artistic talents. His first exhibition was in Stockholm in 1996. Since then, Niels has exhibited in cities like Barcelona, Shanghai, and Copenhagen, to name a few.

Besides painting , Niels has also been involved in both music and acting. Niels has released numerous albums as well as participated in feature films and theatrical productions. He has also produced music videos for artists like Bo Kaspers Orkester, Jacques Werup, Monostar, Patrik Isaksson and Uno Svenningsson. In 2004, he directed a first short film: Tänk. His art is characterized by an urban tempo and a playful hand.

Niels lives and works in Mallorca since 2007, together with the Spanish artist Bego Riba.

Overview

Born 8 May 1964 in Faaborg, Denmark, Niels Jensen grew up in Lund, Sweden, and developed an unusually broad creative practice spanning music, acting, visual art, and film direction. His recording catalogue runs from the late 1970s through the mid-2000s, densest in the 1980s, working across synth-pop, new wave, and industrial on labels including Axiom Records, Stranded Rekords, Amalthea, and ITM Records. As a musician he has been a member of Barn, Viperas, Niels Projector, and Niels & The New York Street Percussionists, with his closest studio partners including Calle Hansson, Johan Gustavsson, Jonas Hellborg, and — notably for a Swedish independent scene figure — Bernie Worrell, with whom he shares credits on three releases. Outside of recording, he directed music videos for Bo Kaspers Orkester, Uno Svenningsson, Patrik Isaksson, and others, and has had visual art exhibitions in Stockholm, Barcelona, Shanghai, and Copenhagen; since 2007 he has lived in Mallorca.
