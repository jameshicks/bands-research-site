Chrislo Haas

Discogs ID: 342901
Real name: Christian Ludwig Haas
Born: 16 November 1956, Aichach
Died: 23 October 2004, Berlin (alcohol abuse)

German musician. Replaced Kurt Dahlke in Deutsch Amerikanische Freundschaft in 1979, playing synthesizer, tape, and saxophone through the Die Kleinen Und Die Bösen era (Mute, 1980). 22 Discogs credits with DAF.

Outside DAF, key member of Liaisons Dangereuses — the Düsseldorf EBM/electro duo (with Krishna Goineau and Beate Bartel). Also in Der Plan, CHBB, Crime & The City Solution, Minus Delta T, Les Diaboliques, Armed Response.
