Mama Bär

Real name: Andrea Katharina Ingeburg Hjuler (née Göthling) Discogs artist ID: 535617 Active: 2002 – present Based: Flensburg, Germany Primary labels: Der Schöne Hjuler-Memorial-Fond, Psych.KG, Malmlund Records

Sound and visual artist; wife and constant collaborator of Kommissar Hjuler (Detlev Hjuler). Together they form the primary creative partnership at the heart of the Flensburg-based mail-art/sound-art network. Records primarily under her own name and as part of:

  • Kommissar Hjuler Und Frau (Discogs ID 526739) — most frequent joint credit with Hjuler
  • Kommissar Hjuler & Mama Baer (Discogs ID 2058684)

Also credited under name variations: Mama Baer, Mama Bear, Das Snorkfräulein, Snorkfräulein, Frau Mama Baer, Ibu Beruang, Madre Osa, Mor Bjørn, f84.5_asperger_mama_baer, and others. The “Snorkfräulein” alias appears in several project names (Der Kommissar & Das Snorkfräulein, Noqoìlpì & Das Snorkfräulein Beim Blowjob).

She holds 172 primary release credits on Psych.KG alone, and shares virtually all of Kommissar Hjuler’s group project memberships (GP&PLS, Karen Eliot, The Kommissar Hjuler Lectures, The NO!art Performance, Penispals, Tuulen Laulu, Sentimental Fools, Love Gun (2), CoBraMaBaPa, JaDaMaBaPa, Foltergaul & Seine Fesselknechte, Sanity Vampires, Pink Floyd (3), and many others).

Their joint website and Bandcamp operate under the name Asylum Lunaticum (asylum-lunaticum.de / asylumlunaticum.bandcamp.com). The NO!art movement connection (Boris Lurie, Dietmar Kirves) is documented in shared Psych.KG releases.

Has a Wikipedia page (linked from Discogs): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mama_Baer
