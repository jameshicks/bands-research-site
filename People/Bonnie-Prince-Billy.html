Bonnie “Prince” Billy

Stage name of Will Oldham. Singer-songwriter. Born December 15, 1970, Louisville, KY. Discogs ID: 66089 (note: previously listed as 60323 — corrected).

Has recorded under several aliases including Palace, Palace Brothers, Palace Songs, Palace Music, and Bonnie “Prince” Billy. Primarily acoustic/folk/country-inflected singer-songwriter work, released largely through Drag City. Collaborated with Tortoise on The Brave And The Bold (2006, Overcoat Recordings) — a covers album recorded together.

Film & Soundtrack Work

Oldham began as an actor before pivoting to music. He has continued to take acting roles intermittently, predominantly in independent and art-house films.

The Brave and the Bold — Collaboration with Tortoise (2006)

Origins

The project originated through Overcoat Recordings head Howard Greynolds, who wanted Will Oldham to record a cover of Bruce Springsteen’s “Thunder Road.” Oldham agreed on the condition that Tortoise serve as his backing band. What began as a single-song arrangement expanded into a full ten-song covers album.

The “Thunder Road” choice was partly personal: Tortoise drummer John Herndon noted that the track “was chosen partly because the fellow that runs Overcoat Recordings, Howard Greynolds, loves The Boss.” (source: patricksisson.com/tortoise-deep-covers)

The album was recorded in two-week sessions at Soma Studios, 2003–2004, with John McEntire serving as producer/engineer. Released January 24, 2006 on Overcoat Recordings.

Recording

Tortoise guitarist Jeff Parker described the sessions: “It was really natural. Will is something else. His stamina is crazy. That dude would sing all day.” (source: patricksisson.com/tortoise-deep-covers)

Song Selection

Multiple press accounts note the covers were chosen with care — “each was chosen with care and reverence with no sense of irony” (PopMatters). One blog account states Oldham solicited fan suggestions for the tracklist, though he and Tortoise made the final selections; this is unconfirmed by a primary source and should be treated with caution.
