Overview

Tom Van Buskirk is a multi-instrumentalist — percussion, synthesizer, electric piano, and tambourine — best known as a member of Javelin and Lucky Dragons. He began recording in 2006, placing releases on indie imprints including States Rights Records and Marriage Records before Javelin moved to David Byrne’s Luaka Bop for their 2010–2013 output. His catalogue sits at the intersection of experimental, synth-pop, and indie pop, with five selected releases spread across the 2000s and early 2010s. His most frequent collaborators are Annelise Grimm and George Langford, each sharing six releases — alongside Luke Fischbeck (of Lucky Dragons) and figures such as Arrington De Dionyso and Bryce Goggin.

Notes (web)

Luaka Bop signing

Javelin’s signing to Luaka Bop was label-initiated, not band-initiated. In a September 2010 interview, Tom Van Buskirk stated: “They got in touch with us! We had given Jamz n Jemz to friends, and had one terrific book/art/music store in Chicago (Golden Age) carrying our stuff. It made its way into the hands of these consummate organizations, and we really love working with both of them.” The same answer covered both Luaka Bop and Thrill Jockey Records, indicating both labels reached out after encountering the self-distributed Jamz n Jemz. jambands.com, 2010-09-16

No source confirms whether David Byrne was personally involved in the signing decision. The Brightest Young Things site ran an interview that explicitly asked “When Javelin signed to Luaka Bop, did it involve a big sit down and/or soul-signing-away with David Byrne?” — but the page is now login-gated and the answer is not accessible. brightestyoungthings.com (login-gated as of 2026-11-10)

A Monster Fresh video-interview piece (undated, circa 2010) adds one further detail: the CD-R reached Luaka Bop through “one of the many friends whom they had given a copy of their CD-R to” — specifically described as someone doing graphic design work for the label — who then “passed it on to the folks in charge.” The piece confirms no direct Byrne involvement: Javelin “had already found themselves signed” before learning of the connection. monsterfresh.com
