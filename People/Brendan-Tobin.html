Brendan Tobin

NYC-based guitarist and recording engineer active in the heavy post-rock / noise rock underground.

Discogs ID: 1187739 Alias: Bunny (3) — Discogs ID: 624597 (used on earlier Made Out Of Babies releases)

Biography

Made Out Of Babies (2003–2008)

Tobin’s main public presence through the mid-2000s was as guitarist in Made Out Of Babies, the New York noise rock / post-hardcore band fronted by Julie Christmas. He appears on their full Discogs catalog under the alias Bunny (3) for the first two albums, switching to his real name for The Ruiner.

  • Trophy (2005, Neurot NR036) — guitar credited as “Bunny (3).” Recorded and mixed by Joel Hamilton, also producer. The 12-song debut established the band’s abrasive noise rock approach with Christmas’s confrontational vocals over squalling, heavily distorted guitar.
  • Coward (2006, Neurot NR046) — recorded by Steve Albini at Electrical Audio, Chicago. Mastered by John Golden. Still credited as “Bunny (3).”
  • Triad (2006, Neurot NR-044) — three-way split LP with Red Sparowes and Battle Of Mice. Made Out Of Babies contribute “Proud To Drown” and “Gunt.” Tobin credited as guitar (“Bunny (3)”) on these two tracks.
  • The Ruiner (2008, The End Records TE106) — first Discogs credit as Brendan Tobin rather than Bunny (3). Produced jointly by Andrew Schneider, Brady (6), and the band. Tobin also handled additional recording. Guest pedal steel by Greg Burns (2) (Red Sparowes) on track 3, “Invisible Ink” — an early sign of crossover between the two bands.

Red Sparowes (2008–2010)

Tobin joined Red Sparowes as guitarist for their final two releases, replacing Josh Graham who had stepped back from the band.

  • Aphorisms EP (2009, Sargent House SH016) — 12” 45RPM, three tracks. Personnel: Tobin + Bryant Clifford Meyer + Andy Arahood (guitars), Greg Burns (2) (bass/pedal steel), Dave Clifford (drums). Recorded and mixed by Toshi Kasai; mastered by Pete Lyman; artwork/design by Sonny Kay.
  • The Fear Is Excruciating, But Therein Lies The Answer LP (2010, Sargent House/Conspiracy Records) — Tobin is not individually credited in the Belgian pressing’s liner notes; the record’s credits list the band collectively as performers. Emma Ruth Rundle joined this lineup; vocals appear in the mix as texture (not foregrounded). Recorded/mixed by Toshi Kasai; mastered by Brian Gardner.

Tobin also provided cover/band photography for the Sargent House A Day Of Nights (Neurot, 2006) — an early photo credit in the scene.

Bad Powers (2012)

After the Red Sparowes and Made Out Of Babies periods, Tobin co-founded Bad Powers with Made Out Of Babies bandmates Cooper (6) (bass) and Matthew Egan (drums), plus Megan Tweed. The band released one self-titled album (2012, The End Records TE287-2). Tobin handled all recording and mixing, and shared photography credit with Sandra Coan. The album is classified as Indie Rock / Industrial on Discogs; mastered by Josh Bonati.

Julie Christmas — The Bad Wife (2010)

Tobin contributed guitar to two tracks on Julie Christmas’s solo debut The Bad Wife (2010, Rising Pulse Records RP004): “Headless Hawks” (with Cooper (6) on bass, Matthew Egan on drums) and “The Wigmaker’s Widow” (also co-written with Christmas). The album was primarily a John LaMacchia vehicle, with Andrew Schneider producing/mixing.

Low Estate (2015–2017)

Tobin is a member of Low Estate, a black metal / hardcore band on Flenser Records. Lineup: Tobin (bass, recording), Geoff Garlock (guitar), Christopher Todd (3) (drums), Jimmy Hubbard (vocals). Releases:

  • Blood Horizon (2015, self-released)
  • Covert Cult Of Death (2017, Flenser Records FR83) — Sanford Parker (mixed), Brad Boatright (mastered). Guest vocalists: Zachary Lipez (track B1), Dwid Hellion (B2 — Integrity vocalist), David Castillo (11) (B4). Artwork by Thomas Hooper (2).

The Drowning Season

Tobin is listed as a member of The Drowning Season (Discogs ID: 654260) alongside Matt Slowikowski and Michael “Chewka” Marchewka. The band has releases from 2002 through 2013 on Morphenic Release. Tobin does not appear as a credited performer in any individual Drowning Season release in the Discogs database — his membership may predate or postdate the documented recordings, or he appears under an uncredited role.
