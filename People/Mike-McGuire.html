Overview

Mike McGuire is an electronic music producer and engineer whose work spans experimental, ambient, and melodic house. A member of On An Ocean Amp, he worked across a range of indie and electronic imprints including Morr Music, FatCat Records, and Bevlar, with credits running from the mid-1990s through at least 2010. His production collaborations include repeated work with Gregory And The Hawk, Italian producer Populous, and Rob Laakso, among others.
