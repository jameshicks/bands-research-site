Mark Leithauser

Discogs ID: 1951168
Role: Hand-lettering / typography / visual artist
Years active (Discogs): 1995–2019

Graphic artist specialising in hand-lettering and typography. Appears across two distinct bands — Jonathan Fire*Eater and The Walkmen — that share personnel (three JFE members founded The Walkmen) and share the Leithauser surname with vocalist Hamilton Leithauser. Anna Leithauser (photographer, same Discogs surname cluster) is credited on several Walkmen releases. The nature of the family connection is not documented in Discogs but the overlap of surname and bands is consistent across roughly two decades of credits.

Role Description

Across all eight credited appearances the work is consistently hand-lettering or handwriting in a visual/packaging context — never engineering, production, or performance. The role varies in Discogs terminology (“Artwork By [Hand Lettering]”, “Typography [Handlettering By]”, “Artwork [Handwriting By]”, “Lettering [Handlettering]”) but refers to the same speciality. On Wolf Songs For Lambs Mark is listed alongside a separate designer credit (Reiner Design Consultants, NYC) and separate illustration credit, placing him as the lettering contributor within a broader design team.

On the 2019 Third Man reissue of Tremble Under Boom Lights his original hand-lettering credit from the 1996 EP is preserved and explicitly relabelled “Lettering [Handlettering],” confirming the artwork was retained for the reissue.

Anna Leithauser is credited on the same Little House Of Savages single (2004) as Mark (she: Photography; he: Logo Illustration). She also photographed the front cover of Bows + Arrows (2004) and appears as “Anna Leithauser McIntosh” on Live! @ Café Carlyle (2020, Hamilton Leithauser). Her alias “McIntosh” matches Hugh McIntosh (Discogs ID 1380477), who is credited as a performer on Black Hours (2014) — suggesting she married into that name. The web of credits implies a family unit across the two bands rather than coincidence, but Discogs carries no explicit relationship data.
