Overview

An American jazz bassist and composer born in Omaha, Nebraska on October 21, 1953, Marc Johnson accumulated over 1,300 credits across a career stretching from 1975 into the 2020s. His catalogue is heaviest in the 1980s and 1990s and spans contemporary jazz, post-bop, modal, and fusion — recorded across Blue Note, ECM, Concord Jazz, GRP, and Verve, among others. His deepest collaboration is with Bill Evans (305 shared releases), and he also worked extensively with Peter Erskine, Joe LaBarbera, Joey Baron, and Bill Frisell. He is married to Brazilian jazz pianist and singer Eliane Elias, with whom he shares 153 credits.
