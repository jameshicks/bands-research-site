Overview

Juan Gomez is a Caribbean-born musician who relocated to the United States at age five and became a fixture of the Los Angeles Free Music Society (LAFMS) experimental scene. In 1976 he co-founded The Monitors with Dennis Duck — his earliest known band — before appearing on record from 1978 onward across groups including Airway, Clay Allison, Doo-Dooettes, El Trio Primero, and Human Hands. His catalogue of 51 credits spans Experimental, New Wave, Art Rock, and Noise, with key label relationships at LAFMS, Happy Squid Records, and Cortical Foundation. Dennis Duck remains his closest long-term collaborator, with 25 shared releases, followed by Rick Potts and the broader Potts/Recchion circle.
