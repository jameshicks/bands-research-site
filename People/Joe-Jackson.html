Joe Jackson

British singer-songwriter and pianist. Born 1954, Burton upon Trent, England. Discogs artist ID: 75280.

Recorded Night And Day (1982, A&M Records), with cover photography by George DuBose. The album was recorded in New York and connected Jackson to the downtown NYC scene. Also: Wasn’t Tomorrow Wonderful? — credited to Was (Not Was) separately.
