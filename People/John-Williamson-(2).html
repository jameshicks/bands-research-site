Overview

John Robert Williamson (b. 28 December 1963, Ilford, Essex) was the bass guitarist in the indie pop group McCarthy throughout the band’s existence from 1985 to 1990. Before joining McCarthy he had already been working with future bandmate Tim Gane, contributing to Gane’s industrial-music imprint Black Dwarf under the pseudonym Jake Ray — releasing “Trips On A Pleasure Beach” through that project. His catalogue runs mainly across the 1980s (six releases) with a tail into the 1990s and 2000s, centred on Midnight Music and September, the two labels that housed the bulk of McCarthy’s output; a handful of later credits on Cherry Red (1996–2007) and a 2020 appearance on Matador suggest ongoing involvement with McCarthy reissues or retrospective releases.
