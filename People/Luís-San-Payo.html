Overview

Luís San-Payo is a Portuguese drummer with 57 Discogs credits stretching back to 1985, working across experimental, new wave, indie rock, and art rock. He has been a member of Pop Dell’Arte, Rodrigo Leão & Cinema Ensemble, Los Tomatos, Irmãos Catita, and Croix Sainte, and also takes occasional producer and composer credits. His most sustained collaborations are with Amândio Bastos (23 shared releases) and João Peste (17), placing him at the core of the Portuguese indie and experimental scene. His label history runs from early releases on Alliance Records through major-label appearances on Ariola, BMG Portugal, and Columbia, with continued activity on Ama Romanta and Phonograma into the 2020s.
