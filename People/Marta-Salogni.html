Biography

Marta Salogni is a London-based producer, mixing engineer, and recording engineer. She first appears in Discogs credits in 2014, initially as an assistant or additional engineer on sessions at London commercial studios — RAK Studios, Strongroom (Air Lyndhurst Studios group), and Dean Street Studios. Through 2015–2016 she accumulated assistant mixing credits on major-label releases including work for Glass Animals (How To Be A Human Being, Harvest, 2016), ANOHNI & The Johnsons (Hoxna/Ricochet, 2016), and Blossoms (Blossoms, Virgin EMI, 2016).

By 2017 she was working as full mixing engineer in her own right. Her first significant lead credits appeared on Björk’s Utopia (One Little Indian, 2017), where she mixed eight of the fourteen tracks (splitting duties with Heba Kadry). The same year she mixed Alex Cameron’s Forced Witness (Secretly Canadian) and worked on Warpaint’s Heads Up follow-up Heads Up sessions.

By 2018–2019 she was operating as a full producer and lead mixer, with production credits on The Orielles (Disco Volador, Heavenly, 2020), Tirzah sessions (Domino), and mixing credits across a wide client roster spanning 4AD, Mute, DFA, Secretly Canadian, and Young Turks artists.

Her studio, Studio Zona (named after Andrei Tarkovsky’s film Stalker), became her primary base for production work.

Her signature recurring partnership is with mastering engineer Heba Kadry: the pair appear together on an unusually consistent string of releases from 2019 onward — Holly Herndon’s Proto, Ela Minus’s Acts Of Rebellion, Westerman’s A Jackal’s Wedding, Warpaint sessions, English Teacher’s This Could Be Texas, Sharon Van Etten’s Sharon Van Etten & The Attachment Theory, and more.

In 2022–2023 she became the in-house engineer/mixer for Depeche Mode’s late-period recordings with producer James Ford: she mixed and engineered Memento Mori (Columbia, 2023) and the Ghosts Again single (2023), along with subsequent remix releases.

Alongside her studio work, she released Music For Open Spaces (Hands In The Dark, 2023) as a primary artist — a collaborative album with Tom Relleen (of Tomaga) credited as performer, writer, and producer.

Salogni is also credited as an immersive mix engineer, having worked on Björk’s Cornucopia Live (2025) and Ela Minus’s Día (2025) in that capacity.
