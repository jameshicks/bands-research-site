Bill Callahan

Discogs ID: 265577 (Bill Callahan solo); Smog project ID: 56147
Real name: William Rahr Callahan
Origin: Silver Spring, Maryland → Chicago → Marfa, Texas
Active: 1988–present (as Smog through 2005, then solo as Bill Callahan from 2007)
Labels: Drag City (primary, throughout); Domino (European releases as Smog, 1996–2005)

Singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist; lo-fi, folk, country-influenced. Born June 3, 1966 in Silver Spring, Maryland. Also uses aliases “Bill Smog” and “Johnny Appleseed.” Member of The Sundowners (2).

Early work as Smog was home-recorded and released on Disaster Records (3), then Drag City from 1992. After 2005 retired the Smog name and released under his own name. John Congleton recorded multiple solo albums:

  • Sometimes I Wish We Were An Eagle (2009, Drag City)
  • Apocalypse (2011, Drag City)
  • Gold Record (2020, Drag City)

Key collaborators across both Smog and solo eras: Jim O’Rourke, Jim White (2), Joanna Newsom, Steve Albini, Ken Champion.
