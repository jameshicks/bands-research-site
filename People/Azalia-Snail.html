Name variations: A. Snail, Az. Snail, Azaila Snail

Overview

Azalia Snail is an American experimental musician and songwriter with an extensive solo discography released across multiple decades. She is known for lo-fi bedroom recordings, omnichord, and layered vocal textures. She was a member of King Missile IV (2014–2015) and its successor project You, Me and This Fuckin’ Guy (alongside John S. Hall) until 2020, playing omnichord, percussion, and backing vocals. She is also a member of LoveyDove (Discogs ID: 4591370) — the Los Angeles band through which she first connected with Hall — and has been part of Physics (3), S!t!o!i!n!k!, Snail Meets West, and Volume (5). Her solo catalog is distributed via Silber Media and Powertool Records. (Wikipedia: Azalia Snail; Discogs)
