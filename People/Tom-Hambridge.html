Tom Hambridge

American blues, rock, and country producer, drummer, singer, and songwriter. Discogs artist ID: 1241834. Real name: Thomas Jay Hambridge. Born 20 December 1960 in Buffalo, New York.

Overview

Hambridge is one of the most prolific producers working in blues and blues-adjacent rock, active from the early 1980s through the present. Based in Nashville, he has built a decades-long track record producing for Alligator Records, Silvertone Records, and Ruf Records, and has worked extensively as a session drummer and mixer in addition to his production and songwriting roles.

His career arc has three overlapping threads:

  1. Performer — drums, percussion, and vocals on his own releases and as a session contributor. His band T.H. & The Wreckage (with Jim Scoppa and Steven Paul-Perry) documented his work as a frontman/drummer. His NOLA Sessions album (2018) was issued under his own name on Artisanship Music Nashville.
  2. Producer/engineer — from Susan Tedeschi’s breakthrough debut Just Won’t Burn (1998) through three consecutive Christone “Kingfish” Ingram albums (2019–2021) and beyond, he has produced a large portion of the last quarter-century’s notable blues releases. He typically handles mixing and mastering himself, often alongside regular collaborator Michael Saint-Leon.
  3. Songwriter — credited as a writer on recordings by Buddy Guy, Joe Louis Walker, Mike Zito, and others.

Biography

Early Career (1983–1997)

Hambridge appears in Discogs from 1983, credited with drums and vocals on Rockophiles (Pure And Easy Records). This is the earliest confirmed entry in his discography. Details of his activity in the 1980s and early 1990s beyond this remain sparse in the Discogs record.

Production Breakthrough: Susan Tedeschi (1998)

The landmark early credit is Susan Tedeschi’s debut Just Won’t Burn (Tone-Cool Records, 1998), which Hambridge produced (with Ducky Carlisle as assistant producer and Tedeschi as associate producer). The album was a commercial and critical success and established Hambridge as a serious blues-rock producer. Toby Mountain mastered.

Expanding Roster: 2003–2010

During this period Hambridge’s production and session work expanded significantly across blues and rock:

  • 2003 — session drums on an Eagles Records release; guest harmony vocals on Buddy Guy’s Vicious Cycle (Sanctuary).
  • 2004 — produced and mixed Bobby Blue Bland’s I’m A Bluesman (Virgin America). Also appeared on Columbia and Slow Moving Records sessions.
  • 2008 — produced and mixed Skin Deep for Silvertone/Sony/Zomba, marking the beginning of a sustained relationship with the Silvertone imprint.
  • 2010 — produced and mixed Living Proof (Silvertone/Jive), and produced Midnight Flyer (Stax).

George Thorogood, Buddy Guy, and Capitol (2011–2015)

In 2011 Hambridge produced and co-mixed George Thorogood & The Destroyers’ 2120 South Michigan Ave. for Capitol Records, handling drums and percussion as an additional musician alongside Kevin McKendree (piano/organ) and Tommy McDonald (bass). He shared mixing credit at The Switchyard studio with Michael Saint-Leon.

His sustained relationship with Buddy Guy produced two high-profile Silvertone/RCA albums:

  • Born To Play Guitar (2015) — produced and co-mixed; Ducky Carlisle engineered; recorded partly at The Switchyard, Nashville (Saint-Leon) and Sacred Cat Studios, CA.
  • The Blues Is Alive And Well (2018) — produced and co-mixed (with Saint-Leon); engineered at Blackbird Studios, Nashville (Carlisle).
  • Buddy Guy’s Living Proof (2010) — produced and mixed.

He also co-produced Chicago Plays The Stones (Raisin’ Music Records, 2017), specifically the Buddy Guy guitar and vocal session.

Alligator Records Residency (2012–2025)

Hambridge became a go-to producer for Alligator Records, handling several notable signings:

  • Joe Louis Walker — Hellfire (2012, drums/percussion/producer/mixed) and Hornet’s Nest (2014, full producer/mixer/mastered/drums). On Hornet’s Nest, the Muscle Shoals Horn Section appeared alongside pianist Reese Wynans.
  • James Cotton — Cotton Mouth Man (2013, drums/producer/mixed/mastered). Co-mixed with Saint-Leon; engineered by Jim Cooley.
  • Marcia Ball — The Tattooed Lady And The Alligator Man (2014, producer/mixed/mastered). Features Delbert McClinton on harmonica and Terrance Simien on accordion.
  • Christone “Kingfish” Ingram — Kingfish (2019, producer/mixed/mastered). Zach Allen engineered at Blackbird; Saint-Leon handled additional recording and mixing/mastering. This debut launched Ingram as the most-hyped young blues artist of the 2010s.
  • Christone “Kingfish” Ingram — 662 (2021, producer/drums/mixed/mastered on tracks 1–13; Nick Goldston produced/mixed track 14). Kenny Greenberg and Bob Britt on guitar; Tommy MacDonald and Glenn Worf on bass; Marty Sammon on organ/piano.
  • A Bluesman Came To Town (2021) — produced this Alligator release.
  • Selwyn Birchwood — Exorcist (2023, producer). Saint-Leon handled mixing and mastering.

Ruf Records Work (2014–2025)

Hambridge built an additional production relationship with Germany’s Ruf Records:

  • Ragged & Dirty (2014) — produced
  • Don’t Look Back (2015) — produced/mixed/mastered
  • Make Blues Not War — Mike Zito (2016) — drums/harmony vocals/producer/mixed/mastered; Kevin McKendree on keys; Rob McNelley on guitar; Tommy MacDonald on bass; Saint-Leon engineered and mixed.
  • Ride Or Die (2016) — co-produced/mixed/mastered; drums
  • Real Gone (2023) — drums/backing vocals/percussion/mixed/mastered/producer
  • Money & Power (2025) — drums/percussion/backing vocals/mixed/mastered/producer

Keb’ Mo’ (2022)

Hambridge produced eight tracks on Keb’ Mo''s Good To Be… (Rounder Records, 2022), sharing production credit with Keb’ Mo’ himself and Vince Gill. He played drums on tracks 2 and 9. Notable guests included Marcus Miller, Victor Wooten, Nathan East, Christone “Kingfish” Ingram, Darius Rucker, and Kristin Chenoweth.

Ongoing Activity (2020–2025)

Hambridge has remained continuously active:

  • Blues Comin’ On (Cleopatra Blues, 2020) — drums/percussion
  • Empty Promises (Alligator, 2020) — composed, lyricist, mixed, produced
  • Holler If You Hear Me (Blind Pig Records, 2021) — executive producer/mixed/mastered
  • Living In A Burning House (Alligator, 2021) — featured performer, mixed/mastered/produced
  • Good To Be… (Rounder, 2022)
  • The Blues Don’t Lie (Silvertone/RCA, 2022) — mixed/produced
  • Ain’t Done With The Blues (Silvertone/RCA, 2025) — backing vocals/bongos/drums/executive producer/mixed/percussion
  • Down The Hatch (Quarto Valley Records, 2025) — primary artist
  • Hard Road (Red Zero Records, 2025) — produced
  • Money & Power (Ruf Records, 2025) — full performer/production role
