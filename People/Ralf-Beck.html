Overview

Ralf Wolfgang Xaver Beck — also credited as Harry Kane — is a German producer, writer, and mixing engineer active since at least 1987, with 89 total credits spanning trance, house, Krautrock, and experimental music. He is one half of the late-1990s trance act Nalin & Kane alongside Andry Nalin, and a core member of the krautrock/experimental project Die Wilde Jagd, his most sustained recent collaboration with 16 shared credits alongside Sebastian Lee Philipp (18 shared). His label relationships shifted from the trance-era imprints Shampoo and Kosmo Records in the mid-to-late 1990s toward Bureau B, where he has accumulated five works between 2015 and 2023. The Discogs entry clarifies that he is distinct from Ralph Armand Beck, the German trance producer known as Taucher.
