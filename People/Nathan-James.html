Nathan James

Discogs ID: 503287 Name variations: Nathan Young Studio: The Vault Mastering Studios (Vault Mastering), Chandler, AZ (metro Phoenix); attended sessions available in New York Website: vaultmastering.com

Senior mastering engineer and owner of The Vault Mastering Studios in Chandler, Arizona. Active across a wide range of genres — jazz, classical, indie rock, experimental, post-rock, punk, metal, and pop — with credits on labels including Blue Note, Mexican Summer, Joyful Noise Recordings, VHF Records, Roadrunner Records, TVT Records, and Atlantic. Career began as a technical assistant and editor at classical labels (Telarc, Startime International) around 2001–2003 before establishing Vault Mastering.

Note: Two other individuals named Nathan James appear in Discogs — a vocalist (Inglorious / Ten) and a photographer. Vocal credits on Scorpions Revisited (2015) and photography on Tickets To My Downfall (2021) appear to be those separate people, not this mastering engineer.

The Telarc period is notable — Telarc was one of the most technically respected labels in classical and jazz recording, known for its audiophile-grade pressings and direct-to-disc productions. Working there gave James a grounding in high-resolution audio that shaped his later mastering practice.

The Blue Note credit (Rising Son, 2014) is significant — mastering for Blue Note, even on a single release, signals the level of trust major jazz imprints place in a studio. The album was produced by vocalist José James and had A&R oversight from Don Was (then president of Blue Note).
