Josephine Wiggs

Discogs ID: 259175
Real name: Miranda Cordelia Susan Josephine Wiggs
Born: 26 February 1963, Letchworth, Hertfordshire, UK
Role: Bassist, cellist, vocalist, guitarist, multi-instrumentalist, producer

English indie/alternative musician. Best known as the bassist and cellist for The Breeders on their first two studio albums (Pod, 1990; Last Splash, 1993), and as leader of the Josephine Wiggs Experience. Equally active as a producer and session player across the 4AD and Grand Royal orbits.

Early Career — The Perfect Disaster (1983–1991)

Before The Breeders, Wiggs played in The Perfect Disaster, a UK indie band based in Hertfordshire. She is listed among the rotating cast of musicians around the group’s constant member Phil Parfitt. The Perfect Disaster released material on Fire Records throughout the late 1980s, including:

ReleaseYearLabel

Wiggs contributed bass, double bass, cello, piano, and vocals across these releases. She also produced several of them, and co-produced the Mood Elevators E.P. (1989). Notably, Jon Mattock (later her bandmate in Honey Tongue and the Josephine Wiggs Experience) was also a member of The Perfect Disaster.

The Breeders (1989–1994, 2017)

Wiggs joined The Breeders — co-founded by Kim Deal and Tanya Donelly — as bassist and cellist for their debut album. She remained through the band’s commercial peak, departing sometime after the Last Splash cycle.

Last Splash became the band’s commercial breakthrough — the “Cannonball” single (1993, 4AD/Rough Trade) charted and received heavy alternative radio play. Wiggs contributed across multiple instruments, including cello and double bass.

Label: 4AD/Rough Trade (Germany) — the lineup matches Last Splash, with Wiggs on bass and vocals.

2017 Reunion — “Wait In The Car” (2017)

Label: 4AD

In 2017, The Breeders reformed the Last Splash-era lineup. The “Wait In The Car” 7” split the recording between Wiggs (bass on side A) and Kim Deal (bass on side B), with Jim MacPherson on drums and Kelley Deal on guitar/vocals for side A. This led to the All Nerve album (2018), though Discogs credits Wiggs on bass for the All Nerve sessions as well.

All Nerve (2018)

Label: 4AD — Josephine Wiggs credited on bass.

Honey Tongue (1992)

A duo with Jon Mattock, Honey Tongue released the Nude Nudes EP (1992, Playtime Records), with Wiggs contributing vocals, guitar, bass, and cello, and handling design, production, and writing. Their only release under this name.

The Josephine Wiggs Experience (1995–1996)

Wiggs formed the Josephine Wiggs Experience with Jon Mattock (drums, keyboards) and Audu Obaje (guitar). The project released one album on Grand Royal:

Production and Session Work (1995–2001)

Kostars — Hey Cowboy (1995) and Klassics With A “K” (1996)

Label: Grand Royal
Kostars were a duo of Jill Cunniff (Luscious Jackson) and Vivian Trimble (Luscious Jackson), with Kate Schellenbach on drums. Wiggs produced Klassics With A “K” (1996, GR) and engineered/mixed Hey Cowboy (1995) — a direct link between the Grand Royal world of the Breeders orbit and Luscious Jackson.

Ultra Vivid Scene (contributor)

Wiggs is listed as a member of Ultra Vivid Scene (Kurt Ralske’s 4AD project) in Discogs’s group membership data. The UVS discography spans 1988–1992 on 4AD; the specific releases and her role are not fully detailed in Discogs’s additional credits, but the connection places her in the extended 4AD circle alongside UVS’s run of albums (Ultra Vivid Scene 1988, Joy 1967–1990 1990, Rev 1992).

Spacemen 3 — Playing With Fire (1989/reissue 1999)

Label: Space Age Recordings
Wiggs is credited with cello on this release (reissued 1999). The core personnel are J. Spaceman (Jason Pierce) and Sonic Boom (2) (Pete Kember). Her cello appearance connects the 4AD/Fire Records world to the Spacemen 3 / Spiritualized orbit.

Dusty Trails (2000)

See below.

Amy Ray — Stag (2001)

Label: Daemon Records
Wiggs plays bass and keyboards on track 4, alongside Kate Schellenbach on drums and glockenspiel on the same track. Amy Ray is one half of the Indigo Girls. Joan Jett also appears on that track.

Dusty Trails (2000–2001)

A duo with Vivian Trimble (formerly of Luscious Jackson and Kostars). Released one self-titled album on Atlantic Records (2000). Wiggs handled electric bass, upright bass, guitar, drums, bongos, percussion, and backing vocals; Trimble handled lead vocals, keyboards, accordion, acoustic guitar. Wiggs also produced and engineered/mixed the record. The duo wrote most material jointly, with Jill Cunniff contributing lyrics to one track.

Solo Work (2019)

We Fall (2019)

Label: The Sound Of Sinners
Wiggs’s first release credited solely to her own name (as primary artist). Jon Mattock performs alongside her. She handled all writing, recording, mixing, and front cover photography. The record is on her own imprint, The Sound Of Sinners.
