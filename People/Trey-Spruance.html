Trey Spruance

Discogs ID: 265224
Full name: Preston Lea Spruance III (“Trey” = nickname for a third)
Born: 1969
Origin: Eureka, California
Aliases: Scummy, Millers Higher Life, Neck Head, The Enemy (9), Uncooked Meat Prior To State Vector Collapse, Yürt, t=486/666

Guitarist, multi-instrumentalist, composer, and label founder. Co-founded Mr. Bungle with Mike Patton and Trevor Dunn in Eureka, CA in 1985. Briefly joined Faith No More in 1994–95. Since Mr. Bungle’s first hiatus, the primary focus has been Secret Chiefs 3 — a sprawling ensemble project drawing on surf, metal, Middle Eastern music, Sufi cosmology, and film scores, for which he composes, produces, and plays most instruments. Also founder of Web Of Mimicry (Felton/San Francisco, CA).

Eureka origins: The Raging Wrath tapes (1985–1991)

Spruance, Patton, and Dunn formed Mr. Bungle at Eureka High School in 1985. The band spent years circulating cassette demos — the earliest surviving, Goddammit I Love America!!! (1988), OU818 (1989) — before being signed to Warner Bros. / Slash via the attention of John Zorn, who had heard the band and offered to produce their debut. Spruance played guitar on the original 1986 home-recording The Raging Wrath Of The Easter Bunny tape (Ladd-Frith, also credited with trumpet and video games).

Mr. Bungle — Warner Bros. years (1991–2000)

Three studio albums on Warner Bros. / Slash, each a stylistic departure from the last.

Mr. Bungle (1991, Slash / Reprise)

Produced by John Zorn. The band’s major label debut: funk metal, ska, hardcore, noise, and cartoon chaos in rapid succession. Spruance credited as “Scummy” on guitar. Core lineup: Spruance (guitar), Mike Patton (vocals), Trevor Dunn (bass), Danny Heifetz (drums), Bär McKinnon (tenor saxophone, clarinet), Theobald Brooks Lengyel (saxophone, keyboards), David Shea (turntables).

Disco Volante (1995, Warner Bros.)

Spruance contributed original artwork but is listed in Discogs only for “Original Artwork By” — he was already disengaging during this period due to his Faith No More commitment and its aftermath. The record was largely written and recorded without him.

California (1999, London / Slash)

Full return. Co-wrote several tracks (co-wrote “None Of Them Knew They Were Robots,” “Golem II: The Bionic Vapour Boy,” and “Pink Cigarette”). Credited with 2nd engineering, additional engineering, production strategy, and band member. Orchestral textures: Eyvind Kang, William Winant (timpani, mallets), Timb Harris (trumpet), Carla Kihlstedt, Marika Hughes (cello). Engineered by Billy Anderson.

Faith No More (1994–1995)

After Jim Martin left Faith No More in 1993, Spruance was brought in for King For A Day Fool For A Lifetime (1995, Reprise / Slash). His tenure was short; he departed before the album’s touring cycle, citing conflicting demands with Secret Chiefs 3 (then in its early phases). He is listed on the album for guitar and musician credits. Remaining FNM lineup on that record: Mike Patton (vocals), Billy Gould (bass), Mike Bordin (drums), Roddy Bottum (keyboards). Mixed and produced by Andy Wallace.

Secret Chiefs 3 (1996–present)

Spruance’s primary project since the late 1990s. An ensemble with a rotating cast of musicians organized around Spruance’s compositions; sub-groups operating under alias names each correspond to different genre registers:

Web Of Mimicry (label)

Spruance founded Web Of Mimicry (Discogs ID 13412; parent: Mimicry Records, ID 124048), based initially in Felton, CA and later San Francisco. Releases include all Secret Chiefs 3 studio albums from Book M onward, as well as releases by Asva and others. Also distributed/released Path Of Most Resistance (2007) and Satellite Supersonic Vol. 1 (2010) compilations.

Asva (2005–2008)

Contributed guitar, bells (tubular), and piano to Asva’s Futurist’s Against The Ocean (2005, Dos Fatales / Web Of Mimicry) and guitar (baritone and electric) to What You Don’t Know Is Frontier (2008, Southern Records). Asva was the drone-doom project of G. Stuart Dahlquist, with William Winant also appearing on recordings.

Mr. Bungle reunion (2019–present)

Mr. Bungle reunited in 2019 in a revised lineup: Spruance, Patton, Dunn (original core) plus Scott Ian (Anthrax, rhythm guitar) and Dave Lombardo (Slayer, drums). This lineup re-recorded the 1986 demo as The Raging Wrath Of The Easter Bunny Demo (2020, Ipecac Recordings, IPC226LP) — a full-length thrash/hardcore recording with guest covers of Stormtroopers of Death, C.O.C., and Anthrax. The Night They Came Home (2021, Ipecac) followed.

Other credits

  • Guitar solo on Kayo Dot Stained Glass EP (2010, Hydra Head Records)
  • Guitar, keyboards, electronics on Plainfield — a side project (Discogs artist ID 266769)
  • Pop-O-Pies, Faxed Head, Commode Minstrels In Bullface: assorted San Francisco experimental affiliations
