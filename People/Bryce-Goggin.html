Bryce Goggin

Record producer, mixing engineer, and recording engineer based in Brooklyn, NY. Discogs artist ID: 254826. Owner and operator of Trout Recording (Discogs label ID: 288076), his Brooklyn studio. Active from 1989 to the present, with a career spanning indie rock, alternative, noise rock, art rock, jazz, and folk. Best known for his production work with Phish, Pavement, The Lemonheads, Swans, and Joan As Police Woman, but his credit list runs to well over 150 releases across three-plus decades.

Name variations in Discogs: B. Goggin, BG, Brice Goggin, Bryce “Gaugwin” Goggin, Bryce Gogan, Goggin.

Career Overview

1989–1992 — Assistant Engineer and Early Credits

Goggin’s earliest Discogs credits are as an assistant or secondary engineer, placing him in major New York studios at the end of the 1980s. He was assistant engineer on RCA projects — including multi-artist releases in Europe — and worked at Gambit Studios (where he is specifically credited on the 1989 Come (2) release Hex, as engineer and mixer). He also worked at Baby Monster Studios, a downtown Manhattan studio that appears repeatedly in his early credits, including as engineer on Swans’ White Light From The Mouth Of Infinity (1991, Young God Records) and the Swans compilation Body To Body Job To Job (1991).

Other early-career credits include engineering on Michael Gira’s and Jarboe’s projects for Young God Records, and work on alternative and noise-rock records for Enemy Records and Alternative Tentacles. He handled mix engineering on Foetus / JG Thirlwell’s Datacide (1990, Enemy Records) and was part of the crew on the Ramones’ Mondo Bizarro (1992, Chrysalis) as assistant engineer at Baby Monster.

1992–1995 — Rise as Mixer and Co-Producer

By 1992–1993 Goggin was receiving lead mixing credits. He mixed Pavement’s Crooked Rain, Crooked Rain (1994, Matador Records) and Wowee Zowee (1995, Matador) — the records that anchor his reputation in the Matador/indie-rock world. He also recorded and mixed Pavement’s Brighten The Corners (1997) and contributed to multiple Pavement reissue/expanded editions.

In the same period he produced, engineered, and mixed the Lemonheads’ If I Could Talk I’d Tell You (1996, Atlantic) and produced Car Button Cloth (1996, Atlantic/Tag Recordings), continuing a relationship with Evan Dando’s band. He co-produced Chavez’s debut Gone Glimmering (1995, Matador Records) alongside John Agnello and Bob Weston.

He also engineered on Dinosaur Jr.’s Where You Been (1993, Blanco Y Negro) and Without A Sound (1994), appearing in the orbit of the Blanco Y Negro / Reprise / Sire distribution network through which many of his alt-rock clients released.

1995–2002 — Full Producer Credits, Spacehog and Lemonheads Era

Goggin’s clearest run as lead producer is 1995–2002. Highlights:

  • Spacehog — produced and mixed Resident Alien (1995, Sire), The Chinese Album (1997, Sire/Warner), The Hogyssey (implied by credits), and In The Meantime (1996, Sire); a deep, multi-album relationship covering their full Sire tenure.
  • Phish — produced Farmhouse (2000, Elektra) co-producing alongside Trey Anastasio, with John Siket recording and mixing; co-produced the Phish live track Heavy Things (2000); produced Round Room (2002, Elektra) solo, recording and mixing himself.
  • Trey Anastasio — produced Anastasio’s solo debut (2002, Elektra).
  • Come (2) — long relationship; engineered Hex (1989), continued through multiple releases.
  • Nada Surf — mixed Harmacy (1996, Sub Pop) and The Proximity Effect (2010).
  • Evan Dando — mixed and recorded multiple Lemonheads and solo releases through the 1990s–2000s.
  • Sebadoh — worked on projects in the mid-1990s.

He also produced or mixed for Poster Children, The Amps (Kim Deal’s side project), Bettie Serveert, and smaller indie acts through this era.

2002–2010 — Expanding Palette: Angels of Light, Antony, Joan As Police Woman

Through the 2000s Goggin extended his work deeper into the downtown/experimental New York world. Key credits:

  • Angels of Light (Michael Gira’s post-Swans project) — mixed The Angels Of Light Sing “Other People” (2005, Young God Records), We Are Him (2007), Parplar (2008). His Young God relationship is one of the longest in his career, spanning Swans in 1991 to Angels of Light in 2008+.
  • ANOHNI / Antony And The Johnsons — worked on The Crying Light (2009, Secretly Canadian) as engineer and mixer; contributed to Swanlights (2010, Rough Trade) as tracking engineer and mixer. These are among his highest-profile pop credits outside of the rock world.
  • Joan As Police Woman — produced, mixed, and engineered Real Life (2006, [PIAS]), To Survive (2008, Reveal Records/[PIAS]), To Be Loved (2008, Reveal), The Ride (2006, Reveal), Eternal Flame (2006, Reveal), Christobel (2006, Reveal), Stranger Things (2007, Fantasy), The Deep Field (2011, Play It Again Sam/[PIAS]). He is effectively her house producer across an extended run of albums.
  • The Apples In Stereo — co-produced and engineered New Magnetic Wonder (2007, Yep Roc/Simian) alongside Robert Schneider; mixed it as well. Also worked on Energy (2007) and Travellers In Space And Time (2010).
  • Page McConnell — engineered, mixed, and contributed additional production to McConnell’s 2007 solo album on Legacy Records.

2010–Present — Ongoing Production, Phish Return, and New Acts

  • Phish — returned to produce Evolve (2024, ATO/Jemp Records) alongside Vance Powell, handling recording duties; a bookend to his early-2000s Phish productions.
  • Joan As Police Woman — continues; Damned Devotion (2018, Play It Again Sam) has him as additional co-producer.
  • Akron-Family — mixed their self-titled (2005, Young God) and Love Is Simple (2007).
  • Hello Mary — produced and mixed Hello Mary (2023, Frenchkiss Records), showing continued engagement with new indie acts.
  • Sub Pop — recorded and engineered You’re Weird Now (2025, Sub Pop), continuing active recording work.
  • Young God Records — engineered The Seer (2012, Swans) as additional recording engineer; mixed My Father Will Guide Me Up A Rope To The Sky (2010, Young God) and A Beautiful EP credits.
  • Various mixing credits through the 2010s–2020s for Barbès Records artists, Bella Union releases, Svart Records releases, and Fire Records.

Trout Recording

Goggin owns and operates Trout Recording (Discogs label ID: 288076), a recording studio located in Brooklyn, NY. The studio name appears in several credits from the 2000s onward — for example, Akron-Family’s 2007 Love Is Simple is credited “Mixed By [Trout]” and We Are Him (2007) similarly notes “Recorded By [Trout].” The studio URL is troutrecording.com. No Discogs releases are formally registered under the label, suggesting it functions as a production company / credit entity rather than a releasing imprint.
