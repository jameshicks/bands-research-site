Steve Arrington

American funk singer, drummer, and producer. Born Steven Ralph Arrington, March 5, 1956, Dayton, Ohio. Discogs artist ID: 53259.

Overview

Arrington got his start with the Young Mystics. After they disbanded he moved to San Francisco, where he connected with the musicians who would become Slave — a union of the Young Mystics and Black Satin Soul. He joined Slave in 1978, initially as a background vocalist before becoming lead vocalist and co-lead creative voice on hits including “Just A Touch Of Love” (1979, #9 R&B), “Watching You” (1980, #6 R&B), “Snap Shot” (1981, #6 R&B), and “Wait For Me” (1981).

Arrington left Slave after the Show Time album (1981) — his last credited Slave LP — and formed Steve Arrington’s Hall Of Fame, taking Charles Carter and Sam Carter with him. He recorded for Kongflather and Atlantic, with his greatest success being the 1985 LP Dancin’ In The Key Of Life (Top 10 R&B title track; “Feel So Real” Top 20).

He experienced a religious conversion in 1986 and stepped away from pop music by 1991 to pursue spirituality. He returned to recording in 2009 with the self-released Pure Thang, collaborated with Dam-Funk in 2013, and released an album on Stones Throw in 2021.

(Source: Discogs artist 53259, Wikipedia “Steve Arrington”)
