Woolfy

Discogs ID: 333615
Real name: Simon James (3)

UK balearic/deep house artist. Second-most prolific act on Rong Music with 11 releases (2005–2009), including The Warehouse (RONG 016, 2006) and Odyssey / 1987 / Two Far Gone (RONG 021, 2007). Contributed to This Is Rong Music compilation. Two Japan-only LPs on the RONG LP series: If You Know What’s Good For Ya!! (RONG LP002, 2006).
