Bob Dylan

Born Robert Allen Zimmerman, 24 May 1941, Duluth, Minnesota. Singer-songwriter, musician. Discogs artist ID: 59792

Overview

Foundational figure in American popular music. Anton Fig played drums on Empire Burlesque (CBS, 1985, one track) and Knocked Out Loaded (CBS, 1986). Both are mid-career Columbia albums.

Connections

  • Anton Fig — session drummer, 1985–1986
  • Al Kooper — historic collaborator (1960s “Like a Rolling Stone” session)
  • Danny Clinch — photographer on “Love And Theft” (2001), Fragments (The Bootleg Series Vol. 17) (2023), and 80+ releases total
  • George Harrison — “If Not for You” appears on All Things Must Pass (Harrison recorded it); co-founded Traveling Wilburys (1988–1990)
