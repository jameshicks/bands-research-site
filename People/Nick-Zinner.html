Nick Zinner

Discogs ID: 189110 (note: an earlier vault entry listed 403841 — corrected here) Full name: Nicholas Joseph Zinner.

American guitarist and co-founder of Yeah Yeah Yeahs (with Karen O and Brian Chase). Also active as a solo artist and in multiple side projects.

Played guitar on “Don’t Push Me” on Ghostigital’s Division Of Culture And Tourism (2012, Smekkleysa). Played guitar on Scarlett Johansson’s Anywhere I Lay My Head (2008, ATCO), produced by Dave Sitek.
