Charlie Haden

American double bassist. Born 6 August 1937, Shenandoah, Iowa; died 11 July 2014, Los Angeles. Discogs artist ID: 253592

Overview

One of jazz’s most influential bassists, with roots in Ornette Coleman’s first Atlantic quartet. Recorded extensively for ECM Records from 1976 onwards — 185 releases in Discogs tie him to the ECM/Eicher relationship. Prominent in Pat Metheny’s 80/81 (1981) alongside Dewey Redman, Jack DeJohnette, and Michael Brecker. Led the Liberation Music Orchestra.
