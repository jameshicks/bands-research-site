Andrew Watt (3)

Real name: Andrew Wotman. Discogs artist IDs: 3821409 (primary), 5573675 (alias entry “Andrew Wotman”). American record producer, guitarist, and songwriter from New York City. Active as a credited musician and producer from 2014 onward. One of the most commercially prominent rock-adjacent producers of the late 2010s–2020s.

Name variations on Discogs: Adrian Wats, Andrew Watts, WATT, Watt, Watt Andrew, watt.

Former member of California Breed (guitar, vocals; left 2015).

Biography

Early Career / California Breed (2014–2015)

Watt’s first significant Discogs credit is as guitarist and vocalist on California Breed’s self-titled debut (2014, Frontiers Records / Ward Records). California Breed was a supergroup featuring Glenn Hughes and Jason Bonham; Watt departed after the one album to pursue solo songwriting and production work.

Around the same time he co-wrote “Home To Mama” (Justin Bieber / Cody Simpson, 2014, Def Jam) — an early pop writing credit.

Songwriter Phase (2017–2018)

Between 2017 and 2018 Watt racked up co-writing credits on a series of major pop and pop-country singles that charted internationally:

  • “It Ain’t Me” (Kygo / Selena Gomez, 2017) — co-wrote and played guitar
  • “Señorita” (Shawn Mendes / Camila Cabello, 2019) — co-wrote, guitar, keyboards, bass, backing vocals
  • “Havana” (Camila Cabello, 2017) — co-writing credit
  • “Let Me Go” (Alesso / Hailee Steinfeld / Florida Georgia Line, 2017) — producer, guitar, bass, backing vocals; credited under both “Andrew Watt (3)” and “Andrew Wotman”
  • “Wolves” (Selena Gomez / Marshmello, 2017)
  • “Anywhere” (Rita Ora, 2017) — producer, backing vocals, guitar, keyboards
  • “Eastside” (Benny Blanco / Halsey / Khalid, 2018)
  • “Girls” (Rita Ora, 2018)

Producer Breakout (2019–2020)

By 2019 Watt was producing full albums for rock legacy artists while continuing to work across pop:

  • Lana Del Rey — Norman Fucking Rockwell! (2019) — producer and mixer on track 5 (alongside Jack Antonoff who handled most of the album); engineer Paul LaMalfa, who recurs across Watt productions
  • Blink-182 — Nine (2019) — producer, guitars, instrumentation, vocals, written-by on track 9; working alongside John Feldmann
  • Ozzy Osbourne — Ordinary Man (2020) — full album producer; also played guitar, bass guitar (on some tracks), keyboards, backing vocals; co-wrote all tracks. Guest performers: Slash (guitar on “Straight to Hell” / “Ordinary Man”), Duff McKagan (bass), Chad Smith (drums), Tom Morello (guitar on “It’s a Raid” and one other), Charlie Puth (keyboards), Elton John (on title track). Engineer: Paul LaMalfa
  • Dua Lipa — Future Nostalgia (2020) — producer, guitar, keyboards, programmed by, tambourine, backing vocals on the title track; mixing handled by others for the full album
  • Miley Cyrus — Plastic Hearts (2020) — executive producer (album executive producer credit); label RCA

Ozzy Osbourne, Iggy Pop, Eddie Vedder, Pearl Jam (2021–2023)

  • Metallica Reimagined — “Nothing Else Matters” (2021, Blackened) — primary artist alongside Miley Cyrus, Elton John, Yo-Yo Ma, Robert Trujillo, Chad Smith; this Metallica cover appeared on the The Metallica Blacklist compilation
  • Eddie Vedder — Earthling (2022) — producer (full album); also arranged strings and horns (alongside David Campbell), label Republic Records / Seattle Surf Co. Engineer: Paul LaMalfa, Marco Sonzini; mixing: Serban Ghenea
  • Ozzy Osbourne — Patient Number 9 (2022) — full album producer; mixed by Alan Moulder; mastered by Randy Merrill; engineer Paul LaMalfa
  • Iggy Pop — Every Loser (2023) — full album producer and executive producer; also art direction, additional photography; mixed by Alan Moulder; cover artwork by Raymond Pettibon; label Atlantic / Gold Tooth
  • The Rolling Stones — Hackney Diamonds (2023) — sole producer (all tracks); mixed most tracks (Serban Ghenea mixed A1–B5, Watt mixed B6 alongside Paul LaMalfa); written-by credits on A1–A3; label Polydor / Rolling Stones Records. Co-produced alongside Don Was and The Glimmer Twins per the brief, though Discogs credits Watt as the album producer — Was and the Glimmer Twins are credited elsewhere in the Rolling Stones production history
  • Pearl Jam — Dark Matter (2024) — full album producer (credited as “Watt”); also played guitar, piano, keyboards; backing vocals on B3; co-written with the full Pearl Jam lineup. Mixed by Serban Ghenea. Label Republic Records

2024–2025

  • Lady Gaga / Bruno Mars — “Die With a Smile” (2024) — producer, written-by; Interscope / Streamline
  • Post Malone — “Disease” (2024) — producer; also played drums, guitar, percussion, bass, synthesizer; composed and wrote lyrics; Interscope
  • Post Malone — F-1 Trillion (referenced via “Abracadabra” track 2025) — bass, drum, electric guitar, keyboards, written-by, producer on “Abracadabra” (2025, Interscope)
  • Lady Gaga — Mayhem (2025) — extensive instrumentation credits: acoustic guitar, bass, drum machine, drums, electric guitar, guitar, keyboards, percussion, synth; Interscope / Streamline. Also worked on “The Dead Dance” (Ozzy Osbourne, 2025)
  • Elton John / Brandi Carlile — Who Believes In Angels? (2025) — producer; additional polaroid photography; EMI / Rocket Entertainment
