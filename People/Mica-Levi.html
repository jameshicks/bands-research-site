Mica Levi

Discogs artist ID: 2576890 Real name: Micaela Levi (also Micaela Rachel Levi) Active: 2009–present Origin: England, UK Aliases / credited as: Micachu, Meeks (7) Groups: Micachu And The Shapes, Kwesachu, Good Sad Happy Bad, Curl (9), M.A.T.H.E.S.

English musician, composer, and producer. Born and raised in Surrey; classically trained. Best known as Micachu (frontperson of Micachu And The Shapes) and as a film composer (credits include Under The Skin, Jackie, Spencer, Monos, Zola). As Mica Levi, she is the primary producer and co-writer behind Tirzah’s Domino Records albums. Won the European Film Award for Under The Skin (2014). The Micachu and Mica Levi entries are separate Discogs IDs (1172814 and 2576890 respectively) but refer to the same person.
