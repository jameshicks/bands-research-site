Overview

Katherina Bornefeld is a German-born drummer and vocalist based in the Netherlands. She joined The Ex in 1984 as their fourth drummer, replacing Geurt van Gisteren, and has been a continuous member ever since — appearing on every Ex recording from Pokkeherrie (1985) onward. Over four decades she has been part of one of the most sustained anarcho-punk-to-free-improvisation trajectories in European experimental music.

On early releases she is credited as “Kat” or “Katrin” on Pokkeherrie (1985) and as “Katrin Ex” from 1936, The Spanish Revolution (1986) through the 2000s; the full name Katherina Bornefeld appears on credits from the mid-2000s onward, though both IDs coexist in Discogs. Her drumming style accommodates a wide range of approaches across The Ex’s output — from the direct post-punk attack of the 1980s, through the free-improv hybrid of the Tom Cora collaborations, to the polyrhythmic work on later recordings informed by the band’s engagements with Ethiopian musicians.

Beyond The Ex, Bornefeld has been a member of Ex Orkest (the Ex’s large-ensemble project), Katjonband (a duo with Jon Langford (2) of The Mekons / Waco Brothers), 3Musketiere (an earlier German ensemble including her sister Sigrid Bornefeld), and the Ig Henneman Tentet (Dutch free-improvisation chamber group).

Before The Ex, Bornefeld appeared on Abflug (1983), a cassette-only release credited to 3Musketiere, a German trio with Sigrid Bornefeld (violin) and Io (41) (bass). This predates her joining The Ex by a year.
