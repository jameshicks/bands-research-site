Alain Johannes

American multi-instrumentalist, songwriter, producer. Discogs artist ID: 246473

Guest on Motorpsycho’s The Tower (2017), credited for flute, guitar (cigar box and messenger guitar), and vocals. Known for work with Queens of the Stone Age, Them Crooked Vultures, Chris Cornell, and his own projects (Eleven, QOTSA collaborations). Also produced Jimmy Eat World’s Damage (2013, Exotic Location Recordings / RCA).
