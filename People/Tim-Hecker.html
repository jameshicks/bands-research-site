Tim Hecker

Discogs ID: 59906
Real name: Timothy D. Hecker
Alias: Jetone (ID 171337)
Active: 2001–present
Origin: Montreal, Canada / Los Angeles, USA
Genre: Electronic, Ambient, Drone, Noise, Modern Classical

Electronic musician and sound artist. One of the central figures in the post-2000 ambient/drone canon; began on the Montreal underground via Alien8 Recordings and its sublabel Substractif, then moved through Kranky (primary home from 2006 onward), a one-off on 4AD (Love Streams, 2016), his own imprint Sunblind Music (2018–2019), and film/TV work (Lakeshore Records, Milan Records). Known for dense, processed organ and guitar drones, latterly incorporating Japanese gagaku instruments and choral arrangements.

Alias: Jetone

Early recordings under the name Jetone predate the Tim Hecker name on Discogs. No Jetone releases identified on Alien8 or Substractif — the alias predates the label relationship. The Haunt Me, Haunt Me Do It Again 2001 LP was released under the Tim Hecker name.

Connections

  • Alien8 Recordings — 11 releases, central artist in the label’s electronic/ambient strand
  • Substractif — debut LP 2001 (Haunt Me, SUBSF03)
  • Kranky — primary home from 2006; Ravedeath, 1972 (2011) was career breakthrough
  • Ben Frost — engineer and collaborator, Ravedeath recorded at Hallgrímskirkja in Reykjavik where Frost was based
  • Jóhann Jóhannsson — co-wrote choral arrangements on Love Streams; both operated in Iceland/Reykjavik orbit
  • Daniel Lopatin — Instrumental Tourist (2012) on Software (2); both central figures in 2010s electronic ambient
  • Acid Mothers Temple & The Melting Paraiso UFO — label-mates on Alien8; shared Alien8 era 2002–2011
  • Set Fire To Flames — label-mates; overlapping Montreal underground network
