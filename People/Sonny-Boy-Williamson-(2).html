Sonny Boy Williamson (2)

Blues harmonica player and vocalist. Born Aleck or Alex Ford (also known as Rice Miller), Glendora, Mississippi, c.1912; died 1965. Discogs artist ID: 287849.

Overview

Not to be confused with Sonny Boy Williamson I (John Lee “Sonny Boy” Williamson), with whom he shares a name but is a distinct artist. Sonny Boy Williamson II broadcast on the King Biscuit Time radio show from Helena, Arkansas, and became one of the preeminent Chicago blues harmonica masters. His Chess and Checker recordings (209 releases on Discogs) include the influential Down And Out Blues (Checker, 1959). Songwriting credited under his own name on approximately 102 Chess releases.
