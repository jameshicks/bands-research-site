J. Spaceman

Discogs ID: 195231 Real name: Jason Pierce

British guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter. Co-founder of Spacemen 3 and leader of Spiritualized.

Collaborated with Matthew Shipp on SpaceShipp and approximately 4 shared releases. The connection bridges Shipp’s free jazz world with British psychedelic and space rock.
