Pete Phipps

UK drummer. Discogs artist ID: 292027. Real name variants: Peter William Phipps.

Overview

Pete Phipps is a British drummer with credits across glam rock, post-punk, and new wave. His longest-running association was with The Glitter Band, and he has played sessions and band slots with a wide range of UK acts from the mid-1970s through the 2000s. He drummed on PERFECT ZEBRAS’ Mixing With Wildlife LP (1982) alongside fellow drummer Jeff Seopardie, as part of a session arranged by producer Barry Anthony Andrews.
