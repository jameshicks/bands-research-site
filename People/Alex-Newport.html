Alex Newport

Grammy-nominated record producer, mixing engineer, and musician. Born in Wolverhampton, UK. Discogs artist ID: 245398. Alias: Texan L. Power.

Overview

Newport began his career as a guitarist and vocalist in the UK metal/noise scene of the early 1990s, co-founding Fudge Tunnel (Nottingham) and Nailbomb (with Max Cavalera of Sepultura). He moved into production during the mid-1990s, relocating first to New York and then to Los Angeles. By the late 1990s he had established himself as a go-to engineer and producer for post-hardcore and experimental rock, building a long association with At The Drive-In and its offshoots. His most concentrated run of production work spans roughly 1998–2013, covering acts on Gold Standard Laboratories, Ipecac, Epitaph, Anti-, Sub Pop, and Saddle Creek. He owns and operates Future Shock Studio (also credited as Future Shock) in Los Angeles. His engineering credits at named studios include Catherine North Studios, Hyde Street Studios, Prairie Sun Recording Studios, and Blackbird Studio.
