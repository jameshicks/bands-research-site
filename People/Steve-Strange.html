Steve Strange

Born: Steven John Harrington, 28 May 1959, Newbridge, Wales Died: 12 February 2015, aged 55 (heart attack, Egypt) Role: Vocals, nightclub promoter, style icon

Lead vocalist and figurehead of Visage (1978–2015). A central architect of the New Romantic movement through his DJ partnership with Rusty Egan at Billy’s (1978–1979) and the Blitz (1979–1981).

Club Nights & New Romantic Scene

  • Billy’s (Soho, 1978–1979): Tuesday Bowie/Roxy Music nights with Rusty Egan; these club nights were where Visage coalesced
  • Blitz (Covent Garden, 1979–1981): The iconic New Romantic club; Strange and Egan as hosts/DJs; gave rise to the term “Blitz Kids”
  • Camden Palace (1982–): Co-opened with Egan and Kevin Millins
  • Brief cameo in David Bowie’s “Ashes to Ashes” video (1980)

Connection to Rich Kids

Strange was not a member of Rich Kids but met Rusty Egan on the King’s Road in 1978. Egan and Midge Ure began collaborating with Strange during Rich Kids’ final months, leading to Visage.
