Mark Sultan

Canadian garage rock / lo-fi musician. Also records as BBQ. Based in Montreal. Discogs artist IDs: 607369 (Mark Sultan), 1035334 (BBQ (2)).

Real name: Marco Antonio Pepe.

Overview

Mark Sultan is a prolific Canadian one-man-band artist operating in the lo-fi garage rock tradition. He records under his own name and as BBQ (and many other aliases — see below), releasing primarily on In The Red Recordings, BOMP!, Norton Records, and his own Sultan Records imprint. His 2011 releases Livin’ My Life and Whatever I Want both feature production and engineering contributions from Toronto’s Nyles Miszczyk.
