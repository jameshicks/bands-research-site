Jeff McGovern

Oct 18, 20263 min read

Jeff McGovern

Discogs ID: 994689
Bassist, vocalist, multi-instrumentalist. Core member of The Scene Is Now (1984–c. 1988); earlier appearance on Mofungo tracks on the Tape #1 cassette (1980), alongside future Scene Is Now bandmates Philip Dray and Chris Nelson (5).

Overview

Jeff McGovern surfaces in the downtown NYC experimental scene at the turn of the 1980s. His first documented Discogs appearance is on Mofungo’s tracks on the cassette compilation Tape #1 (1980, Not On Label), where he plays bass guitar, vocals, maracas, drums, and melodica on specific tracks — a broad multi-instrumental role that recurs across his later work. That cassette was shared with Information (the direct predecessor to The Scene Is Now) and Blinding Headache, placing McGovern at the node where the proto-TSIN circle overlapped with Mofungo.

By 1984 McGovern was a named performer in The Scene Is Now, appearing on their debut 7” 1150 Lbs. / Treaty Stone (Lost Records (9)) alongside Chris Nelson (5), Philip Dray, and Dick Champ. He continued as a credited band member and co-engineer through Burn All Your Records (1985) and Total Jive (1986), and as performer on Tonight We Ride (1988). He is not credited on the later TSIN albums Songbirds Lie (2004) or Magpie Alarm (2011), which feature a reshuffled lineup including Sue Garner and Greg Peterson.

A peripheral 1979 credit places McGovern on Elementary Particles (Living Legend Records (4)), the debut Mofungo 7”, and a 1982 sleeve credit (photographic negatives) on the State of the Union various-artists compilation (Zoar Records), produced by Elliott Sharp.
