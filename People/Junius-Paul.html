Overview

Junius Paul is an American jazz bassist whose credits, beginning in 2008 and spanning nearly a hundred sessions, range across double bass, bass guitar, and percussion, with occasional composition work. The shape of his catalogue is defined by the International Anthem Recording Company, where nine releases between 2015 and 2025 place him at the center of that label’s closely interconnected Chicago circle — Makaya McCraven, Jeff Parker, Marquis Hill, Joel Ross, Tomeka Reid, and Brandee Younger are among his most frequent collaborators. His work crosses into contemporary, free, and avant-garde jazz as well as fusion, and he has appeared as a band member in Kahil El’Zabar’s Ritual Trio, the Ernest Dawkins New Horizons Ensemble, Corey Wilkes & Abstrakt Pulse, and the Greg Spero Quartet, among others. Beyond International Anthem, his credits extend to Delmark, Nonesuch, XL Recordings, and Pi Recordings.
