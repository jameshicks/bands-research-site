Billy Mutter

Bassist. Discogs artist ID: 1250359. Based in Virginia (Richmond area). Active at least 2005–2011 across two bands: The Silent Type and Memorial (2).

Biography

The Silent Type (2005)

Billy Mutter appears in the Discogs record from 2005 onward, credited as bassist on The Silent Type’s full-length Of Writing / Of Violence (self-released, 2005). The Silent Type was an indie/post-rock/folk band from Richmond, Virginia active c. 2000–2006. Mutter joined an expanded lineup that included vocalist/guitarist Nathan Altice, drummer Jared Laine, pianist/violinist Amber Blankenship, and engineer Jason Laferrera. His earliest known credit on any release.

No credits are documented for the band’s earlier releases (The Silent Type EP, 2000; Gregor Samsa split, 2001; Scarlet And Pure, 2003), where bass was handled by Billy Bennett.

Memorial (2) (2008–2011)

Mutter is a full member of Memorial (2) (Discogs artist ID: 3282493), an American emo/melodic hardcore band described in Discogs as “American band.” The group also included Jared Laine (drums), Chris Dowlan (guitar), and additional guitarists/vocalists Pete Appleby and Brendan Murphy. Memorial (2) had a notably more aggressive sound than The Silent Type — genres on Discogs are Emo, Hard Rock, and Melodic Hardcore.

Mutter is credited on two Memorial (2) releases:

  1. The Creative Process / Berlin (Flight Plan Records, 2008, FPR-005) — 12” vinyl album; Mutter credited for Bass and Backing Vocals.
  2. Mile High City (Run For Cover Records, 2011, RFC024) — 12” vinyl; Mutter is a primary band member (no individual credit breakdown in Discogs).

Memorial (2) also appeared on the UK three-way split CD Elemae • Memorial • Soon (Engineer Records, 2008, ign109), alongside Elemae and Soon (13). The band contributed three tracks: “Who Are We To Say?”, “If It Helps”, and “Munich.”
