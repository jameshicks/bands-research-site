Ryan McGuire

Discogs ID: 476394
Also credited as: R. M.
Origin: USA
Active: 2006–2023 (per Discogs credits)

American bassist, contrabassist, vocalist, composer, and improviser. Founder and bandleader of avant-metal group Ehnahre. Long-time member of Kayo Dot. Plays both electric bass and upright/double bass; also credited with keyboards, saxophone, percussion, bowed glass, and voice across various projects. Runs the label and platform Painted Throat Music (paintedthroatmusic.bandcamp.com).

Biography

McGuire emerged in the mid-2000s as part of the experimental rock and avant-metal scenes centered in Boston and New York. He is listed among the core members of Kayo Dot (Discogs ID 476407), Toby Driver’s New York-based ensemble, appearing on their 2006 Robotic Empire double LP Dowsing Anemone With Copper Tongue — one of the defining releases of the American chamber-metal scene. The same year, he contributed upright bass to the track “Providence By Gaslight” on Daughters’ Hell Songs (Hydra Head Records), alongside fellow Kayo Dot members Forbes Graham (trumpet) and Mia Matsumiya (violin).

In parallel with his Kayo Dot work, McGuire co-founded or joined several overlapping ensembles. Calliope Quartet (Discogs ID 1108635) linked him with Forbes Graham, Ricardo Donoso, and Nathan Ahlers; their only Discogs entry is Musiké Téchne (2008, self-released). The Epicureans (Discogs ID 1177478) is a trio with Ricardo Donoso and David Gross. He also appears in Closers (Discogs ID 7720384) and The Epicureans.

His main vehicle since the late 2000s has been Ehnahre, an avant-metal/doom group he founded or co-leads with a shifting but overlapping lineup. Ehnahre debuted with The Man Closing Up (Improvisations On Themes From Guillevic) (2008, Sound Devastation Records), a two-piece core of McGuire (bass, double bass, voice, percussion) and Tom Malone (2) (drums, guitar), augmented by a wide circle of guests including Forbes Graham, Greg Kelley, Jonah Jenkins, and J Mark Inman. Subsequent Ehnahre records on Crucial Blast, Kathexis, and self-released through Painted Throat Music extended the project through 2023.

In 2013, McGuire released Civilian as a solo artist (self-released), playing double bass on eleven tracks and bowed glass on one, self-recording and mixing; mastered by Kevin Frenette.

His 2023 release The Clock Shaken From The Wall (Xenoglossy Productions) credits the band name Painted Throat — a duo of McGuire (bass, double bass, vocals, saxophone, noises, mastered by) and Joshua Carro (drums, percussion, electronics, mixed by) — suggesting an ongoing project distinct from Ehnahre.

The Discogs profile also notes two undocumented albums: Scented Leaves From a Chinese Jar (2006) and additional work with ensembles Dilettante, Dieslow, and the Forbes Graham Quartet, none of which have Discogs releases.
