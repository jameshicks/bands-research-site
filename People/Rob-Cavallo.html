Rob Cavallo

Discogs ID: 253667 Real name: Robert Siers Cavallo Born: March 21, 1963, Washington, D.C., USA Role: Record producer, musician, music executive Also credited as: Cavallo, R. Cavallo, Rob, Rob Cauallo, Rod Cavallo

American record producer and music executive. Best known for producing Green Day’s entire major-label run from Dookie (1994) through Saviors (2024), with the exception of 21st Century Breakdown (2009, produced with Butch Vig). He also co-produced Jawbreaker’s Dear You (1995, DGC), his most prominent connection to the underground punk world. Beyond punk/rock, Cavallo’s discography spans the Goo Goo Dolls, My Chemical Romance, Paramore, Phil Collins, Stevie Nicks, Fleetwood Mac, Dave Matthews Band, and a series of Disney soundtracks. A multi-instrumentalist, he frequently contributed piano and guitar to sessions he produced. He served as Vice President of A&R and later Chairman and CEO of Warner Bros. Records, and is currently Chief Creative Officer at Warner Music Group.

Career Overview

Early Warner years and A&R (1992–1993)

Cavallo’s earliest Discogs credits are executive producer and A&R credits on Reprise Records releases from 1992: Black Sabbath’s Dehumanizer (A&R), Dio’s Strange Highways (A&R manager, 1994 release), and several smaller Reprise acts. These suggest he had an A&R function at Warner/Reprise before establishing himself as a hands-on producer.

His first major production credit is The Muffs’ self-titled debut (1993, Warner Bros.) — co-produced with the band and David Katznelson. Jerry Finn was an assistant engineer on the session, which was an early connection between the two men. Also in 1993 Cavallo produced “Big Mouth / Do The Robot” for what became a pattern of punk-adjacent Reprise-orbit signings.

Green Day and the Dookie breakthrough (1994–1996)

Dookie (1994, Reprise) established Cavallo as the producer for the Reprise punk wave. He co-produced with the band and co-mixed with Finn and the band. Engineer Neill King was on the session. The album became one of the best-selling rock records of the decade. Cavallo, Finn, and Green Day immediately followed with the Insomniac album (1995), with Kevin Army engineering and Finn mixing; this became a recurring crew configuration.

In parallel, 1995 saw Cavallo produce Jawbreaker’s Dear You (DGC) — co-produced with the band, engineered by Neill King, mixed by Finn. The record is Jawbreaker’s major-label debut and his only recorded connection to the Bay Area underground punk scene. Also in 1995: The Muffs’ Blonder and Blonder (Reprise, co-produced with Finn), the Angus film soundtrack (Reprise, executive and album producer), and production for smaller Reprise acts including Ash (“Jack Names The Planets” / “Kung Fu” mix credits) and several singles.

Consolidation and label expansion (1997–2003)

Nimrod. (1997, Reprise) brought in Chris Lord-Alge as mixer for the first time, a collaboration that would persist through Saviors in 2024. The session engineer was Ken Allardyce. Cavallo also played no credited instrument here but served as producer with the band. Also notable from 1997: L7’s The Beauty Process: Triple Platinum (Reprise/Slash) — co-produced with the band and Joe Barresi; L7 was one of Cavallo’s non-punk major-label Reprise clients.

A key widening of Cavallo’s practice came via the Goo Goo Dolls. He produced A Boy Named Goo (1995, Warner Bros.) and then Dizzy Up The Girl (1998, Warner Bros.), on which he also played as a musician. The singles “Iris” and “Slide” off Dizzy Up The Girl were major commercial hits. He continued with the Goo Goo Dolls through Gutterflower (2002) and Broadway / Platinum Play EP tracks (1998–2004).

Other significant credits from this era:

  • Phil Collins: Testify (2002, Face Value/Warner) — produced and credited “Realization”
  • Fleetwood Mac: Say You Will (2003, Reprise) — produced; Stevie Nicks solo Goodbye Alice In Wonderland (2006, Atlantic) — produced and played guitar
  • Eric Clapton: Clapton Chronicles (1999, Duck/Reprise) — produced; Gift of Screws (2008, Reprise/Warner Bros.)
  • Disney soundtracks: Tarzan (1999), Brother Bear (2003), Treasure Planet (2002) — all produced Phil Collins tracks or soundtrack packages
  • Rent (2005, Warner Bros.) — produced the original motion picture soundtrack, arranged by Cavallo
  • Michelle Branch: Hotel Paper (2003, Maverick) and Are You Happy Now? (2003)

Warner Bros. Records is the dominant label across all these years; Reprise (a WB sublabel) handles the Green Day and related punk-rock work.

My Chemical Romance and post-punk production (2006–2012)

The Black Parade (2006, Reprise) is Cavallo’s most critically significant non-Green Day credit. He co-produced with My Chemical Romance; Chris Lord-Alge mixed. He also contributed acoustic piano to the album. Ted Jensen mastered (as on most of his 2000s Green Day records). The same core crew — engineer Doug McKean, Lord-Alge mixing, Jensen mastering — recurs across the Paramore and Green Day work of this period.

Brand New Eyes (2009, Fueled By Ramen) by Paramore: produced by Cavallo with the band co-credited. Chris Lord-Alge mixed; Doug McKean engineered. Tracks featuring Taylor York, Josh Farro, and Hayley Williams.

Green Day trilogy ¡Uno!, ¡Dos!, ¡TRÉ! (2012, Reprise) — all three produced with the band.

Danger Days: The True Lives Of The Fabulous Killjoys (2010, Reprise) by My Chemical Romance — produced.

Wider range and later career (2007–2024)

  • Kid Rock: Rock N Roll Jesus (2007, Atlantic) — co-produced and recorded/mixed
  • Avril Lavigne: The Best Damn Thing (2007, RCA) — produced
  • Dave Matthews Band: Big Whiskey and the GrooGrux King (2009, RCA) — produced; also played organ and piano
  • Adam Lambert: For Your Entertainment (2009, RCA) — produced and played guitar
  • Shinedown: Amaryllis (2012, Atlantic) — produced
  • Tegan and Sara: Heartthrob (2012, Vapor/Warner Bros.) — produced
  • Lindsey Buckingham: Solo Anthology (2018, Rhino) — produced
  • Beth Hart: War In My Mind (2019, Provogue) — produced; also A Tribute To Led Zeppelin (2022, Provogue) where he played guitar
  • Green Day Saviors (2024, Reprise) — returned after a 12-year gap; Chris Lord-Alge mixed, Ted Jensen mastered, Chris Dugan engineered

Across all eras, recurring names in his sessions include Doug McKean (engineer), Ted Jensen (mastering), and Chris Lord-Alge (mixer). Mike Fasano appears as drum tech on Green Day sessions from Insomniac through Saviors.

Connections

  • Green Day — primary 30-year production relationship; produced every studio album except 21st Century Breakdown (2009); also played piano on American Idiot and contributed to Saviors
  • Jawbreaker — produced Dear You (1995) alongside the band; the album was engineered by Neill King and mixed by Jerry Finn; the most significant connection between Cavallo and the underground punk world
  • Jerry Finn — mixer on Dookie, Insomniac, Dear You, and Blonder And Blonder; Finn’s early career was built within Cavallo’s production orbit before he became an independent producer; both appear on The Muffs’ 1993 debut
  • Chris Lord-Alge — mixer from Nimrod. (1997) onward; shared credits on Green Day, My Chemical Romance, and Paramore albums
  • My Chemical Romance — produced The Black Parade (2006) and Danger Days (2010)
  • Paramore — produced Brand New Eyes (2009)
  • The Muffs — produced self-titled debut (1993, Warner Bros.) and Blonder And Blonder (1995)
  • Stevie Nicks — produced Goodbye Alice In Wonderland (2006); Cavallo played additional guitar
  • Phil Cook — (not related; different artist)
  • DGC — the Jawbreaker connection; label home of Dear You
  • Ted Jensen — mastering engineer on most major productions from the 2000s onward
