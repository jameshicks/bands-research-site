Oct 20, 20263 min read

Matt Norelli

Discogs ID: 597211 Instrument: Drums Location: San Francisco Bay Area, California

Drummer active primarily in the Bay Area from the early 1980s through at least 2015. Best known as the original drummer of American Music Club, playing on their first two releases. His Discogs name variations include “Matt Morelli,” “Nerelli,” and “Norelli,” suggesting some contemporaneous sleeve inconsistencies. Discogs lists him as a member of four groups: American Music Club (ID 92581), HUGElarge (ID 4992713), Ironics (2) (ID 7772563), and Paw Paw Blowtorch (ID 1843846).

Overview

Norelli’s earliest documented credit is the 1982 Ironics (2) 7” single Mayday / Misdemeanors on Age Of Music — a San Francisco punk/post-punk outfit that also featured future AMC member Daniel Pearson on guitar and vocals. This places Norelli and Pearson together as pre-AMC collaborators.

He then drummed on AMC’s full debut LP The Restless Stranger (1985, Grifter Records) — the complete band lineup on that record — and returned for most tracks on Engine (1987, Grifter Records / Zippo Records), AMC’s second LP. On Engine he shares drum duties with two other players (Dave Scheff and Tim Vaughan), suggesting sessions were split across different sessions or dates. After Engine, AMC’s drum chair passed to Tim Mooney, who became the band’s long-running drummer through the major-label era.

Norelli surfaces again much later with a 2015 self-titled record by HUGElarge on HWY 61 Records, where he plays “Cocktail Drumset” — a stripped-back trap kit configuration. HUGElarge features Robert Malta on vocals, guitar and bass, with Karl Derfler recording, mixing and mastering.

The band Paw Paw Blowtorch appears in his Discogs group memberships but has no releases in the database.
