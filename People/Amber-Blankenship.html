Amber Blankenship

Discogs ID: 510112
Real name: Amber Blankenship Frye
ANV: Blankenship
Based: Richmond, Virginia, USA

American cellist, pianist, and violinist. A member of the Richmond post-rock scene from the early 2000s, active across three overlapping groups — Gregor Samsa (3), The Silent Type, and Planar — before transitioning to session and ensemble work via the Spacebomb Records house band in the 2010s.

Groups

Gregor Samsa (3)

Post-rock band from Richmond, VA, formed 2000, fronted by Champ Bennett. Amber plays cello and violin. Noteable for large shifting membership and the involvement of several musicians who also worked in adjacent projects.

Key Release Notes

Red Sparowes / Gregor Samsa — Robotic Empire (2005)

Split 7”/10”; Red Sparowes on the A-side, Gregor Samsa on B-side (tracks B1, B2). Gregor Samsa lineup: Champ Bennett (vocals, guitar), Billy Bennett (drums), Jason Laferrera (bass, recorded), Nikki King (2) (vocals, string arrangements), Jon Sullivan (upright bass), Amber Blankenship (cello, violin). Red Sparowes side features Bryant Clifford Meyer, Josh Graham, Greg Burns (2), Mike Gallagher (2), Dana Berkowitz. Artwork by Josh Graham; mastered by John Golden.

Miasma — The Black Dahlia Murder / Metal Blade Records (2005)

Amber is credited with cello on this melodic death metal album. An anomalous session among her otherwise indie/post-rock/folk credits — one of the few death metal albums to feature her. Produced by Andreas Magnusson; executive produced by Brian Slagel; mastered by Alan Douches.

Big Inner — Matthew E. White / Domino + Spacebomb (2013)

Debut album by Richmond singer-songwriter Matthew E. White, released on Domino internationally. Amber is listed as “Cello [The Spacebomb Strings]” alongside a large ensemble of Richmond musicians. Strings arranged and conducted by Trey Pollard. Marks her entry into the Spacebomb Records orbit.

Natalie Prass — Natalie Prass / Spacebomb + Caroline (2015)

Debut album by Richmond singer-songwriter Natalie Prass. Matthew E. White produced; Trey Pollard co-produced, arranged strings, and engineered. Amber credited as cello alongside Naomi Steckman and Stephanie Barrett. Mastered by Gene Paul.
