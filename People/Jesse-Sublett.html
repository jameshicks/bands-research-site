Jesse Sublett

Austin, Texas bassist, vocalist, author, and visual artist. Born May 15, 1954, in Johnson City, Texas. Discogs artist ID: 1026232. Married to Lois Richwine.

Overview

Jesse Sublett is a central figure in the early Austin punk and new wave scene. After attending Southwest Texas State University in San Marcos — where he formed his first band, Nasty Habit (9), with classmates Stanley Gilbert and Gary Dry — he moved to Austin in 1974. He and Eddie Muñoz formed the hard rock/blues band Jellyroll in 1975, which became the direct precursor to The Skunks.

Sublett co-founded The Skunks with Muñoz and Billy Blackmon in mid-1977. The band debuted at Raul’s club in February 1978 and became one of Austin’s earliest and most visible punk acts. Sublett remained the constant throughout the band’s entire original run (1977–1982), serving as bassist and lead vocalist across all their releases on Skunks Records, Rude Records, and Republic Recording.

After The Skunks dissolved he led the New Wave act Flex (44) — also formed in Austin in the 1980s — and then the pop-rock outfit Secret Six. He moved to Los Angeles in 1987, continuing to play music on the West Coast. His L.A. period brought session work with singer-songwriter Carla Olson, notably appearing on her 1990 live album Live (with ex-Rolling Stone Mick Taylor) and her 1993 studio album Within An Ace. He subsequently returned to Austin.

Beyond music Sublett is known as a true crime author and visual artist. His name variations in Discogs: “J. Sublett,” “Jesse Sublet,” “Sublett,” “j. sublett.”

The album connects the Austin scene (Sublett, his Skunks-era bandmates Hemmert and Callins) with British rock royalty: Mick Taylor (ex-Rolling Stones) and Ian McLagan (ex-Small Faces / Faces), both of whom had relocated to Austin.
