Chris Richards (4)

Washington, D.C. musician. Real name: Christopher Paul Richards. Discogs artist ID: 897979. Also records as Ris Paul Ric.

Guitarist/vocalist. Played in The Elusive (1997–1998) before co-founding Q And Not U (1998–2005) with John Davis (11). Later played in Paint Branch. Also associated with TK Echo and the Tilden Shirtwaist Fire.
