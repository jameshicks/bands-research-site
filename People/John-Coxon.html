John Coxon

Guitarist, producer, multi-instrumentalist. UK. Full name: John Richard Timothy Coxon. Discogs artist ID: 53270. Also credited as “Jonah (6)”, and under name variations: J. Coxon, Jon Coxon, Coxen.

Co-founder of Spring Heel Jack with Ashley Wales. One of the most versatile British session and production figures of the 1990s–2010s, moving across pop production (Betty Boo, Everything But The Girl), post-rock and indie (Disco Inferno, Spiritualized), free jazz and improvised music (Evan Parker, John Tchicai), and experimental electronics. Also co-runs the Treader label with Ashley Wales. Member of About Group, Trance Map, King John.

Early Career: Pop Production (1990–1993)

Coxon’s first documented Discogs credits are as a writer and producer on pop records for Betty Boo (born Alison Clarkson) on Rhythm King / WEA. He co-wrote and produced tracks on Boomania (1990) and Grrr! It’s Betty Boo (1992), including “Doin’ The Do” and “Where Are You Baby?“. He also contributed production and songwriting to Marc Almond’s My Hand Over My Heart (WEA, 1991) and worked as co-producer on Love And Life: A Journey With The Chameleons (Cardiac Records, 1991).

In 1993 he produced the self-titled debut of folk duo Pooka (Elektra, 1993), playing bass guitar alongside producing duties.

Indie and Post-Rock Production (1994–1996)

By 1994 Coxon had pivoted decisively toward independent and experimental production. His most notable credits of this period are for Disco Inferno on Rough Trade:

  • Second Language EP (1994) — produced by Coxon; engineered by Mads Bjerke.
  • Technicolour (1995) — recorded by Coxon and Mads Bjerke; mixed by Bjerke. Disco Inferno produced and wrote the record themselves; Coxon’s role was recording.

He also produced several Rough Trade singles for his own outfit at the time — these appear in the discography as primary Spring Heel Jack credits, but Coxon was writing and producing under his own name on tracks such as Oceola / Double Edge, The Sea Lettuce, Lee Perry, There Are Strings (all Rough Trade, 1994–95).

Treader Label and Free Jazz Collaborations (2004–2008)

Coxon co-founded Treader with Ashley Wales. The label is focused on improvised and experimental music. Key releases Coxon produced or co-produced on Treader:

Evan Parker With Birds (trd001) is built around field recordings of birds combined with Parker’s soprano and tenor saxophone; Coxon and Wales composed and produced the soundscape layer alongside archival bird recordings.

John Tchicai With Strings (trd005) features John Tchicai on bass clarinet, alto saxophone, and voice, with Coxon playing electric guitar, piano, sampler, harpsichord, cymbals, and singing bowls, and co-composing and producing alongside Ashley Wales. Mark Sanders plays drums on selected tracks.
