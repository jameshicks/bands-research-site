American bassist, vocalist, recording engineer, and mastering engineer. Member of indie pop band Suddenly, Tammy! (1992–1996); best known in studio contexts as the primary mastering engineer for Definitive Jux from roughly 2000 through 2010 — credited on at least 44 releases in the label’s catalog.

Career Overview

1991–1996: Indie Recording and Suddenly, Tammy!

Heitmueller’s earliest Discogs credits are as a recording engineer and producer for the early-1990s New York indie scene. He co-produced and engineered In The Presence Of Nothing (1992) by Lilys (Slumberland Records / spinART Records, Slumberland 20 / Spart-2), working alongside Jay Sorrentino; both also contributed additional vocals to the record. This session placed him squarely in the spinART Records and Slumberland Records orbit.

He then co-founded — or was a principal member of — Suddenly, Tammy!, alongside Beth Sorrentino (vocals) and Jay Sorrentino. The band released three records on spinART (1992–1993) before signing to Warner Bros., where they released their major-label debut (We Get There When We Do.) (1995). Heitmueller is credited on the Warner Bros. material as co-producer and engineer, and played bass throughout.

2000–2010: Mastering at Definitive Jux

From around 2000 Heitmueller shifted primarily to mastering, becoming the house mastering engineer for Definitive Jux — El-P’s New York underground hip-hop label. He mastered the majority of the label’s output during its peak period, including records by Aesop Rock, Cage (2), The Perceptionists, Antipop Consortium, Camu Tao, and Murs.

During the same period he also mastered for Warp Records (at least two releases: Arrhythmia by Antipop Consortium in 2002, and Now Soon Someday in 2004), and for a range of other independent labels.

His mastering credit alongside mixer Joey Raia is a recurring pairing throughout the Def Jux catalog.

Suddenly, Tammy! — Band Profile

Suddenly, Tammy! were an indie pop trio formed in New York: Beth Sorrentino (vocals), Jay Sorrentino (bass/production), and Ken Heitmueller (bass/production). All three are credited on production and writing. The band signed to spinART Records for their early output and then to Warner Bros. (1995), but appear to have dissolved after the Warner period without further releases.
