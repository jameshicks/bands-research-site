Overview

Bill Million (born William James Clayton) is a New Jersey guitarist and co-founder of The Feelies, the post-punk/indie rock band whose catalogue runs from 1979 to the present across Rough Trade, A&M Records, and Bar/None Records. He has also worked in The Trypes and Yung Wu, two side projects sharing membership with The Feelies. His closest collaborator by far is bandmate Glenn Mercer (173 shared releases), but his 33 shared credits with Peter Buck mark an unexpected cross-scene connection outside the core New Jersey circle.

Departure from The Feelies and the Dormant Years (1991–2008)

Leaving the band (1991)

A few months after the July 1991 release of Time for a Witness, Million abruptly left The Feelies, triggering an indefinite hiatus that was formally treated as a breakup by 1992. Several pressures converged:

  • Health and insurance. Million had health problems and no health coverage; the band could not afford to self-finance touring, and touring itself had become a physical ordeal for him — in particular the smoke at venues. (Salon.com interview with Glenn Mercer, 2016)
  • Family and financial pressure. His wife’s family was based in Florida and had been pressing him to find stable work. His father-in-law offered him a steady job there, and Million took it. (OndaRock monograph, via search snippet)
  • Economic dead-end. As Mercer put it: “Bill couldn’t afford health insurance…. Just the feeling that we were knocking ourselves out and just treading water, not going anywhere really.” (Salon.com, 2016)

The band played their final show in July 1991 before Million’s departure made the hiatus official.

The dormant years in Florida (1991–2008)

Million relocated to Florida and stepped away from music entirely. The only specific detail on record about his work during these years comes from a mid-1990s interview with Mercer and Dave Weckerman: Million was working changing locks at the Magic Kingdom (Walt Disney World). (furious.com Feelies interview, circa 1996) No other employment or musical activity during this period is documented in available sources.

The other Feelies members continued playing in various configurations in New Jersey throughout the 1990s, but Million remained absent from music. His schedule still required travelling from Florida once the band resumed activity after 2008. (Relix, “Spotlight: The Feelies”)

2008 reunion

The reunion came when Sonic Youth — specifically at Thurston Moore’s instigation — invited The Feelies to open for them on 4 July 2008 at Battery Park, Manhattan. Million returned from Florida for the show. The band then performed Crazy Rhythms in full at All Tomorrow’s Parties later in 2008 and subsequently focused on new material. (Wikipedia, The Feelies, as of 2026-11-05; Relix)
