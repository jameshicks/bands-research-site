Overview

Roy Paci (born Rosario Paci) is a Sicilian trumpet player, arranger, conductor, and bandleader whose work spans Mediterranean brass, ska, reggae, world music, and free jazz. Based in Augusta, Sicily, he has been one of the most active Italian brass musicians in international contexts since the early 1990s.

He is best known internationally as the trumpeter and co-musical director of Manu Chao’s live touring outfit Radio Bemba Sound System, appearing on their Radio Bemba Sound System live album (Virgin, 2002), Siberie M’Etait Contéee (Radio Bemba, 2004), and La Radiolina (Because Music, 2007). That association ran across more than a decade of touring.

As a bandleader he runs Roy Paci & Aretuska, a world-music combo combining calypso, swing, and Sicilian folk, which released five studio albums between 2001 and 2017 on Etnagigante and V2. As a session and featured player he has appeared on Italian pop and rock records across three decades, as well as on major international releases by Mike Patton (Mondo Cane, Ipecac, 2010) and The Ex (Catch My Shoe, 2010; Enormous Door with Brass Unbound, 2013).

He co-founded Banda Ionica in 1997 with Fabio Barovero of Mau Mau (2), a large Sicilian brass ensemble rooted in Holy Week and funeral march traditions. He is also a member of Mau Mau (2) itself (Turin’s Afro-Latin band), Brass Unbound (the Dutch/international brass collective), Conjura, Trionacria, and the large Amsterdam ensemble Ex Orkest.
