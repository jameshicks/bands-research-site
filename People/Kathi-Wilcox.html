Kathi Wilcox

Bassist, guitarist, vocalist. Born November 19, 1969. Discogs artist ID: 438736. Real name: Kathi Lynn Wilcox. Married to Guy Picciotto (Fugazi).

Biography

Early Years — Olympia and Bikini Kill (1990–1993)

Wilcox was a founding member of Bikini Kill, the Olympia, WA punk/riot grrrl group formed in 1990 with Kathleen Hanna (vocals), Tobi Vail (drums), and Billy Karren (guitar). She played bass throughout the band’s existence.

Bikini Kill released their first cassette, Revolution Girl Style Now, self-released in 1991. Their first Kill Rock Stars EP appeared in 1992, recorded by Ian MacKaye and Don Zientara at Inner Ear Studios, with additional tracks recorded by Pat Maley. The self-titled EP was co-released by Kill Rock Stars and Dischord Records.

Their first full-length, Pussy Whipped (1993, Wiiija Records UK / Kill Rock Stars US), was produced and engineered by Stuart Hallerman. The 1992 split LP Yeah Yeah Yeah Yeah / Our Troubled Youth with Huggy Bear appeared on Kill Rock Stars and Catcall.

During the same period, Wilcox was a founding member of Frumpies — a loose collective also including Karren, Vail, Molly Neuman, and Michelle Mae (Bratmobile). Their 1993 cassette Babies and Bunnies (Kill Rock Stars) credited all members as “Frumpies Equal,” with Wilcox contributing vocals on several tracks. Recording by the band collectively and Slim Moon.

Bikini Kill — Mid-Period (1993–1997)

The band released the New Radio 7” (1993, Kill Rock Stars) and the LP Reject All American (1996), the latter produced and recorded by John Goodmanson. Wilcox played bass and drums on the 1995 single I Like Fucking / I Hate Danger.

Bikini Kill broke up in 1997. A compilation, The Singles, appeared in 1998 (Kill Rock Stars), crediting Wilcox with backing vocals and bass.

Frumpies Retrospective (1998)

Frumpie One-Piece (1998, Kill Rock Stars) compiled Frumpies recordings. On this release Wilcox is credited with guitar and vocals, alongside Karren (guitar), Vail (guitar, vocals), and Molly Neuman (drums). Recording credits go to Donna Dresch (tracks 1–7, 15, 17–20) and Slim Moon (tracks 8–12).

The Casual Dots (2003–present)

Wilcox formed The Casual Dots with Christina Billotte (vocals, guitar — formerly of Slant 6, Autoclave) and Steve Dore (drums). Their self-titled debut LP (2004, Kill Rock Stars) was produced and mixed by Guy Picciotto and engineered/mixed by Don Zientara at Inner Ear Studios. Wilcox plays guitar (not bass) in this group; Billotte handles lead vocals and guitar.

Their second LP, Sanguine Truth (2022, Ixor Stix), again produced by Guy Picciotto and mixed by him, engineered by Don Zientara, mastered by John Golden. Billotte also did the cover photograph. The band self-produced as well. A notable 18-year gap between records.

Guest / Session Work (2000s–2010s)

In 2003, Wilcox provided vocals on “Temple” (released 2003 on Sunnyside Up, Germany) by Aura Project (2) feat. Mikem, Freddy Fader & Scotty. A one-off session, not connected to the DC/Olympia scene.

Wilcox sang backing vocals on Fugazi’s final album The Argument (2001, originally; the Discogs entry is a 2016 reissue pressing on Dischord Records). She contributed “second backing vocals” on track 3 alongside Bridget Cross (tracks 3 and 5). Picciotto’s connection likely explains the collaboration.

She also did photography credits: photos for Bikini Kill’s The Future of What compilation (1995, Kill Rock Stars) and label photos for Unwound’s I Want Some (1999, K Records).

The Julie Ruin (2013–present)

Wilcox rejoined Kathleen Hanna in The Julie Ruin (not to be confused with Hanna’s earlier solo project of the same name). The band also includes Sara Landeau (guitar), Carmine Covelli (drums), and Kenny Mellman (keyboards). Wilcox plays bass.

  • Run Fast (2013, The Julie Ruin label): Wilcox on bass and co-songwriter. Mixed variously by Eli Crews, James Murphy (LCD Soundsystem), and Kathleen Hanna.
  • Hit Reset (2016, Hardly Art): co-songwriter credit alongside all members. Produced by Eli Crews and the band.
  • I’m Done 7” (2016, Hardly Art): Wilcox credited as “The Julie Ruin Are.”

Wilcox also received a co-songwriter credit on the song Villain (2016, Hardly Art), credited to The Julie Ruin’s Hit Reset sessions.

Bikini Kill Reunion (2019–present)

Bikini Kill reunited in 2019 for live performances with the classic lineup: Wilcox, Hanna, Vail, and Karren. The 2014 reissues of Yeah Yeah Yeah Yeah (Bikini Kill Records) carry additional credits — Wilcox credited with “Band; Bass; Liner Notes” — and a 2015 release of Revolution Girl Style Now (Bikini Kill Records) lists her as Performer.

Lesser-Known Projects

Panax (Discogs ID: 1887551): trio with Bridget Cross (Unrest, Velocity Girl) and Doug Bailey. On Teen Beat. One 7” single documented: Love Will Rule Our Party (1999, Teenbeat TEENBEAT 280), recorded by Guy Picciotto. Discogs notes they recorded three songs total.

Pigeons (Discogs ID: 1411345): trio with Bridget Cross and Guy Picciotto. No Discogs-catalogued releases.

Star Sign Scorpio (Discogs ID: 715526): solo or small project. Only Wilcox listed as member on Discogs. No Discogs-catalogued releases.
