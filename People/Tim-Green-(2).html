Tim Green (2)

Recording engineer, producer, guitarist, multi-instrumentalist. Real name: Tim Green. Discogs artist ID: 302885. Alias: Concentrick. Studio: Louder Studios (San Francisco → Grass Valley, CA).

Overview

Tim Green is a Seattle-born engineer and musician who became a central figure in the early 1990s Kill Rock Stars ecosystem and the Olympia/DC-adjacent underground. He was a guitarist and member of The Nation of Ulysses (Dischord Records, 1990–1992), the influential DC post-punk group, and appears on their releases as both performer and occasional engineer. After NOÜ he drifted toward production work and settled in San Francisco, where he established Louder Studios.

His Kill Rock Stars work spans 1992–1999 and includes the early riot grrrl catalog — Bratmobile’s Pottymouth, Heavens To Betsy’s These Monsters Are Real, Excuse Seventeen — as well as repeated Unwound sessions. He also engineered several K Records sessions (Alien I.D., She’s Real) and produced the Sleater-Kinney self-titled LP (1995) co-released on Villa Villakula and Chainsaw / Matador.

In the 2000s he shifted toward Bay Area work at Louder Studios, recording and mixing acts including Red Sparowes, Pelican, and (uncredited) some metal and heavy rock sessions.

Studio Notes

Tim Green’s studio profiles on Discogs specify: “Producer/Engineer at Louder Studios, originally located in San Francisco, and now in Grass Valley, California, USA. Also recorded many artists at [l704141] (DC area) and Inner Ear Studios, Washington, D.C.” His early KRS engineering was likely done on location or at Seattle-area spaces; by the mid-2000s he was consistently working out of Louder Studios.
