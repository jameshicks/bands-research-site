Overview

Jordan Koop is a Vancouver-area recording and mixing engineer who has been working full-time since 2003, accumulating credits on over 350 albums primarily by Pacific Northwest and Canadian artists. He owns and operates The Noise Floor, a retreat recording studio on Gabriola Island, BC. His discographic footprint spans 2002–2024 and covers a wide range of indie, punk, noise-rock, folk, and experimental work.

Beyond the engineer role, Koop has musician credits — he played drums in Twin Crystals, a Vancouver noise/psych group active from the mid-2000s into the early 2020s, and appears as a member in Spencer Krug’s Discogs artist entry. For the Spencer Krug album Fading Graffiti (2021), he played electric guitar, engineered, mixed, and co-produced. He also played guitar on Orville Peck’s Sub Pop debut Pony (2019) and on a 2016 release by Spencer Krug.

This Cold Night (recurring)

Koop mixed — uncredited — a long run of releases for Chase Morledge’s project This Cold Night including 90° (2021), Are We Not Immortal Yet?? (2022), Pick Up A Phone, Restrospective XXIII, and Harpy’s Song (all 2023), and Gasping For Air, I Am The Weed, Or So I Thought, and Just Terrified (all 2024). On several of these he also played guitar and produced (uncredited).
