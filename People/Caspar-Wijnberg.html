Caspar Wijnberg

Dutch bassist and keyboardist; member of The Saints (2) Netherlands-era lineup and of Chris Bailey’s working band Chris Bailey & The General Dog. Appeared on Spit The Blues Out (2000), Bone Box (2005), Nothing Is Straight In My House (2005), and Imperious Delirium (2006). Discogs artist ID: 767198.
