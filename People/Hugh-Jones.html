Hugh Jones

Hugh Jones

Oct 06, 20266 min read

Hugh Jones

Born London, England. UK record producer and engineer. Discogs artist ID: 65366. Member of The Turquoise Swimming Pools (band project).

Overview

Hugh Jones is one of the defining producers of British post-punk and new wave. Starting as an apprentice engineer at IBC Studios, London, in the early 1970s — his first major credit is Chick Corea’s Light As A Feather (Polydor, 1973) — he moved to Rockfield Studios in Monmouthshire, Wales in 1978, which became his base for the next decade. A mid-1980s stint in Los Angeles preceded his return to Rockfield.

His peak period ran 1980–1993, during which he produced or engineered some of the most significant records to come out of the UK post-punk scene: The Sound (2)‘s From The Lions Mouth (Korova, 1981), Echo & The Bunnymen’s Heaven Up Here (Korova, 1981) and the self-titled 1983 album, Modern English’s After The Snow (4AD, 1982), That Petrol Emotion’s Manic Pop Thrill (Demon, 1986), two albums for Died Pretty (1991, 1993), and three Kitchens Of Distinction records (1990–1992). He also co-produced The Damned’s Strawberries (Bronze, 1982) and produced early albums for Del Amitri and The Icicle Works. A notable studio performer as well as technician: on After The Snow he contributed backing vocals and keyboards.

He continued working into the 2000s–2010s with ongoing Echo & The Bunnymen work (Siberia 2005, Stormy Weather 2005) and The Bluetones, and produced the 2023 film soundtrack The Wonder for the Accidental label.

Research Queue

  • Heaven Up Here (1981) carries competing role attributions on Discogs across pressings — some list Jones as sole producer, others as “Producer In Association With” alongside the band. Sleeve credits on the original Korova UK pressing would clarify the precise billing.
  • Strawberries (The Damned, 1982) — Discogs credits The Damned themselves as primary Producer and Jones as Co-producer on 7 of 10 tracks (A1, A4, B1–B4, B6). The remaining 3 tracks appear to have had a different arrangement — confirm from the original Bronze sleeve who handled production on those tracks and why Jones’s involvement was partial.
  • Me. In Time (The Charlatans, 1991) — Jones credited as Producer, Engineer on Discogs. Was this a standalone single only or did this production work lead to further sessions that were abandoned?
