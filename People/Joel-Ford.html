Jun 29, 20267 min read

Joel Ford

Discogs ID: 636796
Alias: Airbird (Discogs ID: 2219204)
Labels: Software (2), Driftless Recordings, Mexican Summer, Domino
Active: c. 2005–present
Origin: USA

American producer, musician, creative director, and A&R. Co-founder of Driftless Recordings (label ID 621501). Best known as one half of Ford & Lopatin and Games (3) with Daniel Lopatin (Oneohtrix Point Never), and as a member of Tigercity and Ejecta / Young Ejecta (with Leanne Macomber). His solo alias is Airbird.

Note: the stub previously mentioned “Desire” with Italians Do It Better connections — no evidence of this appears in the Discogs database. Likely a confusion with another artist.

Biography

Tigercity (c. 2005–2008)

Joel Ford’s first documented band was Tigercity, a synth-pop duo with Aynsley Powell. The group released a self-titled EP on Forge Records (2005) and the full-length Pretend Not To Love on Strange Feeling (2008). The album credits show an expanded live lineup including Andrew Brady, Bill Gillim, and Aynsley Powell as performers, with production split between the band and Al Carlson. Tigercity is listed on Discogs as a two-member act (Ford and Powell); the additional performers appear to have been live or session contributors.

Games / Ford & Lopatin (2010–2011)

Ford began collaborating with Daniel Lopatin under the name Games (3). Their debut release Everything Is Working appeared on Hippos In Tanks in 2010, followed by That We Can Play (also Hippos In Tanks, 2010). Both releases credit the duo simply as performers (“Games Is”).

The project renamed to Ford & Lopatin for the 2011 album Channel Pressure (Software SFT-001), the inaugural release on the Software (2) sublabel of Mexican Summer that Ford and Lopatin co-ran. The album was engineered by Al Carlson, mixed by Scott Herren (credited as Guillermo Scott Herren — Prefuse 73), and mastered by Joe LaPorta. Ford and Lopatin share “Music By” credit. Ford & Lopatin also released a Channel Pressure Remixes EP, singles (“Emergency Room”, “World Of Regret”, “Snakes / Flying Dream”), and the compilation World Complete (Ford credited as Producer).

Ford’s Airbird alias appeared in 2011 on a split cassette with Megafortress (“Airbird / Megafortress” on The Curatorial Club, TCC016), with an uncredited composition credit. The Curatorial Club was founded by Michael McGregor (3) of the Chocolate Bobka blog; it also released the Games (3) Spend The Night With compilation (TCC008) and Lopatin’s Chuck Person’s Eccojams Vol. 1 (TCC011). The Airbird project was confirmed as a Joel Ford alias on the Discogs entry for the 2012 LP Trust (Software), which Ford fully produced, engineered, and mixed.

Software (2) label era and OPN Replica (2011)

Ford’s most notable non-Ford & Lopatin production credit from this period is on Daniel Lopatin’s Replica (Mexican Summer / Software, 2011) — the landmark Oneohtrix Point Never album. Ford is credited as Producer alongside Lopatin and Al Carlson (who also mixed and engineered). This makes Ford a co-architect of one of the defining releases of early-2010s experimental music.

Ejecta / Young Ejecta (2013–2020)

Ford formed Ejecta with vocalist Leanne Macomber, releasing Dominae (Driftless Recordings, 2013) — produced by Ford, mastered by Josh Bonati. The group also released “Jeremiah (The Denier)” (Happy Death, UK, 2013). Under pressure from a cease-and-desist from another artist named Ejecta, the duo renamed to Young Ejecta by end of 2014. As Young Ejecta, they released The Planet (Driftless Recordings, 2015), the EP Build A Fire / The Planet EP (Driftless, 2017), and Ride Lonesome (Driftless, 2020). Ford co-wrote and produced Ride Lonesome; mastered by Heba Kadry.

Autre Ne Veut — Anxiety (Software, 2013)

Ford contributed additional production, drum machine programming, and engineering to Autre Ne Veut’s Anxiety (Software / Secretly Canadian, 2013). Lopatin also contributed keyboards and additional production; Al Carlson handled primary production and mix. This triangulates a tight working cluster at the Software label.

Ford also played bass and sang on Autre Ne Veut’s The Flower Lane (Domino, 2013).

Broader production career (2013–present)

Beyond the Software orbit, Ford moved into broader indie and pop production:

  • How To Dress Well — The Anteroom (Domino, 2018): Ford sole producer and mixer (“All Songs And Sounds Produced By”), co-credited with Tom Krell; mastered by Dave Cooley.
  • Alex Cameron — Forced Witness (Secretly Canadian, 2017): additional engineering and additional production alongside Jonathan Rado and Jacknife Lee.
  • Jacques Greene — Fantasy (LuckyMe, 2022): additional production and mixing.
  • North Americans — Long Cool World (Third Man Records, 2023): co-producer and mixer.
  • Teen Daze — Interior (Cascine, 2021): mixed by Ford; also plays bass guitar on tracks 5 and 10.
  • Roped In — (Third Man Records, 2020): mixed, mastered, and engineered by Ford.

yes/and (2021)

Ford formed the project yes/and with Meg Duffy (Hand Habits), releasing a self-titled album on Driftless Recordings (2021). Both credited as composers.
