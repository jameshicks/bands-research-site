Trish Keenan

Vocalist, songwriter, and co-founder of Broadcast. Born Patricia Anne Keenan, 28 September 1968, Winson Green, Birmingham, UK. Died 14 January 2011, Warwick, UK, of pneumonia. Discogs artist ID: 403878.

Biography

Keenan attended Archbishop Grimshaw Roman Catholic school in Birmingham. After school she worked in various catering jobs; at 21 she moved to the bohemian enclave of Moseley, where she formed a folk duo called Hayward Winters. She met James Cargill at a 1960s psychedelic revival club in Birmingham. The pair bonded over shared interests and formed a band called Pan Am Flight Bag, which played two gigs before regrouping as Broadcast in 1995.

As Broadcast’s lead vocalist, Keenan was the public face of a group that drew on BBC Radiophonic Workshop, Ennio Morricone, and John Barry alongside 1960s psych and kosmische influences. The band was frequently associated with Stereolab in their early years and debuted on the Stereolab-run Duophonic Super 45s label before moving to Warp Records.

On returning from Broadcast’s first Australian tour in December 2010, Keenan was diagnosed with pneumonia and hospitalised. She died on 14 January 2011, aged 42.

Work And Non Work (1997, Warp WARP CD52) — Discogs ID: 3195

Compilation collecting the band’s 1996 singles and EP output. Self-engineered on most tracks; additional engineers: Gavin Monaghan, Paul Tipler, Jon Cotton, Steve Leather. Sleeve by Julian House.

The Noise Made By People (2000, Warp/Tommy Boy TBCD1413) — Discogs ID: 3194

Debut full-length. Drums split between Keith York (most tracks) and Steve Perkins (2) (tracks 8, 11, 12). Mixed by Broadcast and Steve Francis (3). Artwork and photography by Julian House.

Haha Sound (2003, Warp WARPCD106) — Discogs ID: 161417

Drums by Neil Bullock (tracks 1–12, 14) and Phil Jenkins (track 13). Mastered by Nick Webb. Artwork and sleeve by Julian House. Written and recorded by Broadcast.

Tender Buttons (2005, Warp WARPCD136) — Discogs ID: 502075

The most minimal Broadcast album — credited solely to Keenan and Cargill as producers, performers, engineers, and co-writers. Former members Roj Stevens (feedback on “Black Cat”) and Tim Felton (fuzz guitar on “Corporeal”) each contribute to a single track. Artwork uncredited but by Julian House.

Investigate Witch Cults Of The Radio Age (2009, Warp WARPCDD189) — Discogs ID: 1944554

Joint release with The Focus Group (Julian House’s solo project). 23-track mini-album, digital format. Collage-heavy, deep in library music and radiophonic electronics.

Outside Broadcast

  • Uncredited vocals on “Who’s Ready To Get High” (1992, Top Charlie Records) — pre-Broadcast appearance.
  • Liner notes for the Valerie And Her Week Of Wonders OST reissue (2006, Finders Keepers Records).
  • Prefuse 73 — Broadcast (with Keenan on vocals) appeared on Surrounded By Silence (2005); Keenan has a Written-By credit on The Only She Chapters (2011, Warp), released posthumously.
