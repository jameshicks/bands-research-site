Brian Baker

Discogs ID: 289257 Real name: Brian Baker (full: Baker James Brian) Alias: Dale Nixon (2) (also a Discogs artist ID 624277 — used as photographer credit on Fake Names LP) Active: 1981–present Origin: Washington, DC

Overview

Brian Baker is a guitarist and bassist who has been a participant in the Washington DC hardcore scene since its earliest days and has remained active in punk for over four decades. He was the original bassist in Minor Threat (1981–83), contributing to the band’s entire Dischord catalog. After Minor Threat dissolved he switched instruments to guitar, first joining Government Issue briefly in 1983–84 before co-founding Dag Nasty with Dave Smalley in 1985. Dag Nasty’s two Dischord Records LPs — Can I Say (1986) and Wig Out At Denkos (1987) — became touchstones for melodic hardcore. He joined Bad Religion in 1994, appearing on every subsequent studio album through the 2020s; the band’s Atlantic and Epitaph releases gave him a second extended career in major-label and major-indie punk. He also has a notable production credit history for DC-scene bands in the mid-1980s, and is one of the founding members of Fake Names (2019–present).

Beyond the main narrative: Baker played guitar on the Meatmen’s Dutch Hercules EP (Touch and Go, 1984) alongside Lyle Preslar, and co-produced several Government Issue and Fountain Of Youth releases throughout 1983–86.

Production Credits (summary)
