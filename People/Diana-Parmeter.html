Overview

Diana Parmeter is an American cellist whose recorded credits run from 1993 to at least 2016, concentrated in post-rock and experimental contexts. She is a member of Sinister Luck Ensemble and has contributed cello as a session or featured player on releases from Temporary Residence Limited, Minty Fresh, Atavistic, and several smaller imprints. Her collaborator list clusters around two distinct scenes: Chicago-area string players Susan Voelz and Alison Chesley, and the Japanese post-rock band Mono and their close associates — Takaakira Goto, Tamaki Kunishi, Yasunori Takada, and Yoda — recorded largely with Steve Albini.
