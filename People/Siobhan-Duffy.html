Siobhan Duffy

Discogs ID: 358331
NYC-based drummer, vocalist, and occasional saxophonist. Active primarily 1991–2010. Her primary creative home was God Is My Co-Pilot, on whose releases she appears more than any non-core member (42 credited appearances across pressings/editions). Through her marriage to Michael Gira she also became a significant contributor to Angels of Light, credited on every studio album of that project. She appears on Discogs under several name variations: Siobhan D., Siobhan N. Duffy, Siobhan Duffy Gira, Siobhan N. Duffy Gira, and the initialism SD. She is also noted in the Discogs profile as the singer with The Gunga Din.

The Lives of Charles Douglas session connects Siobhan Duffy to a broader downtown NYC circle: the album was produced by Moe Tucker (of the Velvet Underground), with Kurt Ralske (Ultra Vivid Scene) on guitars and engineering. Ralske also engineered The Gunga Din’s Introducing, suggesting an overlapping milieu. Charles Douglas (2) also produced the Manish Kalvakota (2000) album on which she also appears.

Her appearance on Madrugada’s The Nightly Disease (2001, Virgin) — a Norwegian alternative-rock band — is the most geographically and stylistically distant credit and likely a one-off via NYC recording sessions.
