Howie Gano

Louisville, Kentucky recording engineer, co-producer, and composer. Discogs ID: 323888.

Also credited as: H. Gano, Howell Gano, Howell Hunt Gana, Howell Hunt Gano, Howey Gano, Howie “Indomitus” Gano.

Overview

Originally a drummer, Gano enrolled in the University of Louisville School of Music after high school and completed three years in the Music Composition Program. He became the defining production figure in Louisville’s independent rock and hardcore scene of the mid-1980s through mid-1990s, working primarily out of Sound on Sound, Kentucky (Discogs label ID: 696949), which he owned. He later owned and operated Louisville Recording Arts (Discogs label ID: 1010088; louisvillerecordingarts.com) and also worked at Mom’s Recording Studio (Discogs label ID: 795580) in the late 1980s and early 1990s, freelancing additionally at Audiovisions (Discogs label ID: 395759).

His two major institutional relationships define the arc of his early career: he was the consistent engineer and co-producer across all of Squirrel Bait’s studio recordings (1985–1987), and he served as de facto house engineer for Self-Destruct Records across essentially its entire catalog run (1986–1993). Beyond those anchor relationships, he worked with a wide range of Louisville and regionally connected acts — post-punk, hardcore, experimental, jazz-inflected rock — making him the most networked production figure in the Louisville scene of that era.

He later extended his work into the 1990s and 2000s with prog-rock outfit French TV (multiple albums on Pretentious Dinosaur Records), as well as session contributions to Bitch Magnet’s 2011 retrospective box set and archival reissues. On at least one French TV release (Virtue in Futility, 1994) he appears as a performer — playing keyboards and drum machine and contributing a scream vocal — showing his role moved beyond pure engineering on longer-term collaborations.

Self-Destruct Records — Full Catalog Footprint

Gano engineered or co-produced the majority of Self-Destruct Records’ 50-release catalog. The releases he is confirmed credited on span:

  • SD No. 1 — Solution Unknown, Taken For Granted E.P. (1986) — Engineer, Producer
  • SD 2 — Big Deal (12), Very Mysterious… E.P. (1987) — Engineer
  • SD 3 — Louisville Sluggers I (1988, Various) — Engineer
  • SD 5 — Kinghorse, Brother Doubt / Freeze (1989) — Co-producer, Engineer
  • SD 6 — Undermine, Wrong In Me (1990) — Engineer
  • SD 8 — Undermine, My Wire (1990) — Engineer
  • SD 9 — Dybbuk (2), Dybbuk (1991) — Engineer
  • SD 11 — Erchint, Blaze (1992) — Engineer
  • SD 12 — Louisville Sluggers II (1992, Various) — Engineer
  • SD 13.5 — Enkindel / Erchint, Toy (1992) — Engineer
  • SD 15 — Dybbuk (2), Breakfast T (1992) — Engineer, Mixed By
  • SD 16 — Louisville Sluggers III (1993, Various)
  • SD 18 — Evergreen (5), Evergreen (1992) — Engineer
  • SD 19.5 — Lather (4), Lather (1993) — Engineer
  • SD 21 — Plunge, Plunge (1993) — Engineer, Producer

The Kinghorse single (SD5) also features Mike Bucayu on bass — illustrating how Bucayu bridged his role as SDR’s in-house producer/A&R and Gano’s role as house engineer, with both men working together across the bulk of the catalog.

Key Collaborator: Mike Bucayu

Mike Bucayu (Discogs artist ID: 1413980) is the most frequent co-credit in Gano’s catalog. He appears as producer on:

  • Solution Unknown Taken For Granted E.P. (1986)
  • Solution Unknown Karen (1987; Gano not credited on this one — Don Zientara engineered)
  • Big Deal (12) Very Mysterious… E.P. (1987)
  • Kinghorse Brother Doubt / Freeze (1989) — also plays bass
  • Undermine Wrong In Me (1990) and My Wire (1990)
  • Dybbuk (2) Dybbuk (1991) and Breakfast T (1992)
  • Erchint Blaze (1992)
  • Evergreen (5) Evergreen (1992)
  • Plunge Plunge (1993)
  • Dybbuk (2) Breakfast T (1992)

Bucayu served as the Self-Destruct Records-side producer on nearly every session Gano engineered there. He also appears as a guitarist on Solution Unknown’s debut and on the Antman (5) single (SD7), where Gano is not credited.

Research Queue

  • The Squirrel Bait profile mentions a July 1984 pre-EP Sound on Sound session — confirm whether Gano is credited on any tape or document from this session, or only on the 1985 EP proper.
  • Louisville Sluggers III (SD16, 1993) has no additional credits in Discogs — confirm whether Gano engineered it (consistent with his work on I and II) or whether another engineer handled it.
  • Confirm whether Self-Destruct No. 4 (Karen by Solution Unknown, 1987) was recorded at Sound on Sound by Gano or elsewhere — Don Zientara is credited as engineer on that release, suggesting an out-of-town session (Inner Ear Studio, DC), but the relationship between the two sessions is unclear.
  • Antman (5) Hate / Say It To Me (SD7, 1991) is the one Self-Destruct release in Gano’s era where he is not credited but Bob Hunter (4) and Bryan Manley (2) are — confirm whether this was recorded at a different studio.
  • Confirm whether the “Transferred By” credit on Elysian Field (Gear Fab Records, 1999) refers to a tape transfer/restoration role for an archival release, or a different usage of that role.
