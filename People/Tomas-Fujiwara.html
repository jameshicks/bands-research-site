Tomas Fujiwara

American jazz drummer, vibraphonist, composer, and bandleader. Born 1977 in Boston; based in Brooklyn. Discogs artist ID: 1165312. Real name: Tomas A. Fujiwara.

Biography

Fujiwara is a central figure in the Brooklyn-based avant-jazz scene that coalesced in the 2000s and 2010s. He came up through the New York improv ecosystem and became a first-call drummer for a dense network of bandleaders on the creative-music left: Taylor Ho Bynum, Mary Halvorson, Michael Formanek, Tomeka Reid, and Ingrid Laubrock among them.

His own bandleading career spans several distinct projects. The earliest Discogs-documented leader credit is a 1998 self-released recording under the ensemble Unified Quest. By the mid-2000s he was a member of Ideal Bread (the Steve Lacy repertory group that also included Taylor Ho Bynum), and by 2014 he had formed Thumbscrew (3) — the organ-trio-like piano-free guitar/bass/drums unit with Mary Halvorson and Michael Formanek — which has become his most visible co-led project, releasing four albums on Cuneiform Records (2014, 2021, 2022, 2024).

His large-ensemble project Tomas Fujiwara’s Triple Double fields two guitars, two trumpets/cornets, and two drummers — an unusual, compositionally driven format — and has released records on Firehouse 12 Records (Triple Double, 2017; March, 2022). His small-group leader work under Tomas Fujiwara Trio recorded Pith (2023) for Out Of Your Head Records, a trio with Tomeka Reid on cello and Patricia Brennan on vibraphone.

Fujiwara also plays vibraphone in addition to drums — an uncommon double — and is credited on vibraphone on several releases including March (2022) and Multicolored Midnight (2022).

He appears on the inaugural releases of several small but significant labels: Firehouse 12 Records, Out Of Your Head Records, and continues to record for Cuneiform into the mid-2020s.

Triple Double Personnel (key releases)

Triple Double (2017, Firehouse 12): Taylor Ho Bynum (cornet), Ralph Alessi (trumpet), Mary Halvorson (guitar), Brandon Seabrook (guitar), Gerald Cleaver (drums), Tomas Fujiwara (drums). Produced by Fujiwara; engineered by Nick Lloyd.

March (2022, Firehouse 12): Same core lineup — Ho Bynum, Alessi, Halvorson, Seabrook, Cleaver, Fujiwara. Produced by Fujiwara and Nick Lloyd.

Thumbscrew Personnel

Mary Halvorson (guitar), Michael Formanek (double bass), Tomas Fujiwara (drums). All three compose; the band self-produces. Recorded by Jon Rosenberg (2014 debut).

Tomas Fujiwara Trio (Pith, 2023)

Tomeka Reid (cello), Patricia Brennan (vibraphone), Tomas Fujiwara (drums, composed by, produced by). Recorded, mixed, and mastered by Nick Lloyd. On Out Of Your Head Records.

Living By Lanterns (New Myth / Old Science, 2012)

Taylor Ho Bynum (cornet), Mary Halvorson (guitar), Tomeka Reid (cello), Greg Ward (2) (alto saxophone), Ingrid Laubrock (tenor saxophone), Joshua Abrams (bass), Mike Reed (2) (drums, electronics, composed by, arranged by), Nick Butcher (electronics), Jason Adasiewicz (vibraphone), Tomas Fujiwara (drums). On Cuneiform Records.
