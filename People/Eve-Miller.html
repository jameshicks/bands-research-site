Overview

Eve Miller is a US cellist who has been active across indie rock, post-rock, and early music since 1994. She is a core member of Rachel’s, the Louisville post-rock/modern classical ensemble, working regularly alongside Christian Frederickson, Jason B. Noble, and Rachel Grimes; her 91 Discogs credits are anchored to Quarterstick Records, Polyvinyl Record Company, and Altitude Records. Beyond Rachel’s she has played with indie act Matt Pond PA and with Tempesta Di Mare, an early-music ensemble, demonstrating a range from rock-adjacent strings to historically informed performance. Her roles span cello, violoncello, strings, vocals, and backing vocals.
