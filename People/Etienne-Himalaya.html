Etienne Himalaya

Discogs ID: 544292
French art-rock musician from Reims. Name variations: Etienne Himalayamour, Etienne Himnalanoy, Himalaya, E. Himalaya, E.H.

The dominant compositional voice in Look De Bouk — credited as primary composer on the majority of tracks across all three Ayaa releases. Also handled recording and mixing from …Avec Des Coussins Bleus (1988) onward, and designed the sleeve for Lacrimae Rerum (1985) with Kwettap Ieuw.

Instruments

Drums, percussion, keyboards, voice, melodica, toy instruments, radio sounds.
