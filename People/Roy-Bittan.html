Roy J. Bittan (born July 2, 1949) is an American keyboardist, best known as a member of Bruce Springsteen’s E Street Band, which he joined on August 23, 1974. Bittan, nicknamed The Professor, plays the piano, organ, accordion and synthesizers. Aside from his membership in Bruce Springsteen’s band, Bittan has played on several dozen albums as a session musician primarily for notable singer-songwriters, and rock and pop artists. Some of the prominent musicians with whom he has recorded include Jon Bon Jovi, David Bowie, Jackson Browne, Tracy Chapman, Chicago, Catie Curtis, Dire Straits, Peter Gabriel, Ian Hunter, Meat Loaf, Stevie Nicks, RVIVR, Bob Seger, Celine Dion, Nelly Furtado, Patty Smyth, Jim Steinman, and Bonnie Tyler.

Overview

Roy Bittan (born July 2, 1949), nicknamed “The Professor,” is an American keyboardist who joined Bruce Springsteen’s E Street Band in August 1974 and has remained a cornerstone of that lineup ever since, playing piano, organ, accordion, and synthesizers. Beyond the E Street Band, he has amassed nearly 4,000 credits as a session musician, appearing on albums by artists as varied as David Bowie, Dire Straits, Peter Gabriel, Meat Loaf, Stevie Nicks, Jon Bon Jovi, and Celine Dion. His studio work clusters around the Columbia label, while a substantial body of live release credits through Nugs.net documents the E Street Band’s touring output from 2014 onward. His closest collaborators are the other E Street Band members — Max Weinberg, Garry Tallent, Steve Van Zandt, Clarence Clemons, and Danny Federici — along with long-running production partners Jon Landau, Chuck Plotkin, Jimmy Iovine, and Bob Clearmountain.
