Biography

Background and Pure Morning (1993–1997)

Ade Blackburn (Adrian Everett Blackburn) is the founding vocalist and guitarist of Liverpool post-punk band Clinic. Before Clinic, he was a member of Pure Morning (2) (Discogs ID 291961), formed in Crosby, near Liverpool in 1993. Pure Morning had all four future Clinic members: Blackburn on guitar/vocals, Hartley (Clinic) (Jonathan Christopher Hartley) on guitar, Brian Campbell on bass, and Carl Turney on drums. The band released six records between 1994 and 1996 on small UK labels Amulet Records (2) and Radar Records (5), culminating in the LP Two Inch Helium Buddah (1996). In 1997 they became Clinic.

Clinic (1997–present)

When Pure Morning became Clinic in 1997, Blackburn remained the central vocalist and co-writer. The band adopted the visual identity they are best known for: white surgical scrubs and faces hidden by surgical masks, maintained as a strict policy throughout their live and press appearances. Clinic’s debut single “I.P.C. Subeditors Dictate Our Youth” (1997, Aladdins Cave Of Golf) made John Peel’s end-of-year Festive Fifty. After signing to Domino Records in 1999, the band built a substantial catalog across seven studio albums (2000–2021), plus EPs, singles, compilations, and B-side collections.

On Discogs, Blackburn’s individual credits on Clinic releases are specifically listed as: Guitar, Keyboards, Vocals — the creative and performance core, distinct from Hartley’s Clarinet, Guitar, Keyboards. On the 2019 album Wheeltappers And Shunters, Blackburn and Hartley are explicitly named as the written-by credits (credited as “Blackburn”), signaling continued co-authorship with Hartley as the core songwriting unit.

Higher Authorities (2016)

Blackburn and Hartley formed a parallel project, Higher Authorities (Discogs ID 4999280), together — notably without Campbell and Turney. Their single Neptune (2016, Domino) was produced by Higher Authorities themselves, with additional production and additional mix by Adrian Sherwood and engineering by Dave McEwen. The project remains a duo at the Discogs level, with no further releases documented in the DB.

Outside Credits

Blackburn appeared as mixer (credited simply as “Ade”) on The Curfew Tower (2013, Product Records (4)), a limited edition compilation LP. Co-mixers on that release included Hartley, Paul Sullivan (5), and Sam Wiehl — suggesting the project had a shared Liverpool/Clinic adjacent context.
