Leroy Glover

Electric guitarist and bassist. Member of ESG. Discogs artist ID: 501761. Name variations: L. Glover, L.Glover.

Overview

Leroy Glover is best known as a bassist and guitarist associated with ESG, the South Bronx funk/post-punk group led by the Scroggins sisters. His Discogs footprint is entirely ESG-related across five releases spanning 1987–2007. According to a WABE news profile linked on his Discogs page, he is based in Atlanta, where he works as a mail carrier and plays guitar.

Note: Discogs ID 501761 has been misassigned several arranger/conductor credits from the 1960s–70s (Smash Records, Epic, Mercury, RCA Victor) that belong to Leroy Glover (2), a separate jazz arranger. Those credits are not Leroy Glover the bassist.
