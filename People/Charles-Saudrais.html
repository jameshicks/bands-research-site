Charles Saudrais

Discogs ID: 298945
Role: Drums / Percussion
Name variations on Discogs: C. Saudrais, Ch. Saudrais, Charle Saudrais, Claude Saudrais

French jazz drummer. Active on the Paris jazz scene from at least 1956 through the late 1970s, appearing on sessions with major American and European jazz figures. His Free Jazz credit (1965) is his only known free-jazz outing; the bulk of his career is mainstream/post-bop drumming.

Career Context

Saudrais was a working Paris-based session and band drummer from the mid-1950s. He appeared on sessions with visiting American musicians — Zoot Sims, Chet Baker, Bobby Jaspar, Pepper Adams, Ted Curson, Anita O’Day, Ben Webster — as well as French leaders like Henri Renaud, Barney Wilen, and Georges Arvanitas. His longest recurring association was with the Futura Records orbit and with pianist Georges Arvanitas, with whom he recorded into the late 1980s. The 1965 Free Jazz percussion credit is an outlier in an otherwise mainstream jazz career. By 1988 he had a primary-artist release (Bird Of Paradise, Carrere), suggesting some late-career visibility under his own name.
