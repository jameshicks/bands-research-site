Wale

Rapper from Washington, D.C. Real name: Olubowale Akintimehin (also Ralph Folarin / Wale Folarin). Discogs artist ID: 932352.

Overview

Nigerian-American rapper who collaborated with 9th Wonder on Back To The Feature (2009, Allido Records). Three shared Discogs releases. Known for blending go-go rhythms and conscious lyricism; later signed to Rick Ross’s Maybach Music Group.
