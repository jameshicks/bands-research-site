Anthony Charles Wakeford is a British neofolk musician, the founder and sole constant member of Sol Invictus. He began as bassist in the Surrey punk band Crisis (2) (1977–1980), co-founded Death in June with Douglas Pearce and Patrick Leagas in 1980, and formed Sol Invictus in 1987 after being expelled from Death in June. He has also collaborated extensively with Steven Stapleton (Nurse With Wound), David Tibet’s Current 93, and Boyd Rice, and runs his own label, Tursa, which has released the majority of Sol Invictus’s catalog since 1990. Wakeford has been open about his early-1980s National Front membership, which he later described (2007) as “probably the worst decision of my life.” (Wikipedia)

Career Overview

Crisis (1977–1980)

Wakeford was bassist for Crisis, a Surrey punk band aligned with the Rock Against Racism and Anti-Nazi League movements. After Crisis disbanded in 1980 Wakeford co-founded Death in June with Douglas Pearce and Patrick Leagas. He was fired from Death in June in early 1984 for “bringing his right-wing leanings into the group” (he had joined the National Front).

Above The Ruins (1984–1986)

After his dismissal from Death in June, Wakeford formed the post-punk project Above The Ruins (Discogs ID: 98515) with Gareth Smith. They released the nine-song demo cassette Songs of the Wolf (1984, Grimnir) and contributed to the No Surrender white-nationalist compilation (1985). The band disbanded shortly after.

Sol Invictus (1987–2023)

In 1987 Wakeford distanced himself from far-right politics and formed Sol Invictus — named from a pre-Christian cult — as a neofolk and post-industrial project. The founding lineup included Ian Read and Karl Blake (Lemon Kittens, Shock Headed Peters). Sol Invictus was the sole constant vehicle for Wakeford’s music for 36 years. He ran three successive self-owned labels for the project: S.V.L. Records → Cerne → Tursa (formed 1990, distributed by World Serpent until that company dissolved in the 2000s, then by Cold Spring). Tursa released 83 releases across the catalog. Wakeford describes the music as “folk noir.” (Wikipedia)

Ian Read left in 1991 to form Fire + Ice; the lineup subsequently expanded to include violinist Matt Howden, cellist Sarah Bradshaw, pianist David Mellor, trumpet player Eric Roger (Gaë Bolg), and singer Sally Doherty, among others.

Other Projects

  • L’Orchestre Noir (Discogs ID: 182963) — Wakeford’s chamber/orchestral side project; released Cantos (1997) and 11 (1999) on Tursa.
  • HaWthorn (Discogs ID: 225005) — duo with Matt Howden; released The Murky Brine (2004).
  • Duo Noir (Discogs ID: 395139) — live duo format with Matt Howden.
  • The Wardrobe (Discogs ID: 395881) — duo with Andrew Liles; released Cups In Cupboard (2005) and A Sandwich Short (2006) on Tursa.
  • The Triple Tree (Discogs ID: 909449) — duo with Andrew King; Ghosts (2008).
  • Grey Force Wakeford (Discogs ID: 1088312) — trio with Nick Grey and Kris Force; Marble Heart (2008).
  • Orchestra Noir (Discogs ID: 1011330) — related orchestral project.
  • Boyd Rice And Friends (Discogs ID: 1273505) — member; appeared on Music, Martinis And Misanthropy (1990, bass guitar) and In The Shadow Of The Sword (1992, performer).
  • Crisis — rejoined the band for a new touring lineup in 2017.
