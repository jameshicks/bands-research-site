Sep 26, 20268 min read

Michio Kurihara

Discogs ID: 375510
Real name: 栗原道夫 (Kurihara Michio)
Name variations: Kuri, Kurihara, Kurihara Michio, M.Kurihara, Michio, 栗原, 栗原ミチオ

Tokyo-based guitarist, best known for his contributions to the Japanese underground psychedelic scene. Core member of White Heaven and Ghost (2), two of the most significant bands associated with the P.S.F. Records underground. Has collaborated extensively with Boris (3) — appearing on Rainbow (2006), Cloud Chamber (2008), and numerous other recordings — and with Damon & Naomi, appearing on several albums and live documents spanning the 2000s–2020s. Also a member of drone/ambient supergroup Ensemble Pearl. Released his first solo album, Sunset Notes, in 2005 on Pedal Records.

Key Releases — Personnel Detail

Sunset Notes (2005, Pedal Records — PDL-0502)

Kurihara’s debut solo LP, credited in both Japanese (栗原ミチオ) and Roman script. Produced by You Ishihara; engineered by Souichirou Nakamura (also credited for arrangements). Cover photography by Naomi Yang.

Rainbow (2006, Pedal Records — PDL-0601)

Co-credited to Boris With Michio Kurihara. Co-produced and recorded by both Boris and Kurihara. Personnel: Michio Kurihara (guitar), Takeshi (guitar, bass, vocals), Wata (guitar, vocals, glockenspiel), Atsuo (percussion). Design and photography by FangsAnalSatan (Atsuo alias). The release that established Kurihara as a standing Boris collaborator.

Cloud Chamber (2008, Pedal Records)

Second Boris With Michio Kurihara co-credited LP. Recorded, edited, and mastered by Souichirou Nakamura. Same Boris lineup as Rainbow.

Smile -Live At Wolf Creek- (2008, Daymare Recordings / Diwphalanx Records)

Boris live recording featuring Kurihara on guitar. Engineered by Noel Ford; mixed/mastered by Souichirou Nakamura; recorded by Mell Dettmer. Kurihara credited as additional guitar alongside the core Boris trio.

Damon & Naomi With Ghost (2000, Sub Pop)

Joint release credited to Damon & Naomi With Ghost. Personnel: Masaki Batoh (acoustic guitar), Damon Krukowski (acoustic guitar, drums, vocals, engineer, mixer, producer), Naomi Yang (bass, harmonium, vocals, design, photography, producer), Michio Kurihara (electric guitar), Kazuo Ogino (keyboards, arrangements). Produced collectively.

Ensemble Pearl (2012, Drag City)

One self-titled album from the drone/ambient supergroup. Performers: Atsuo, Bill Herzog, Michio Kurihara, Stephen O’Malley (Sunn O)))), plus Eyvind Kang and Timb Harris on track 3. Mixed by Randall Dunn; mastered by Bob Weston; recorded by Masato Suzuki.

A Sky Record (2021, self-released)

Third Damon & Naomi With Kurihara co-credited release. Kurihara plays electric guitar and co-engineers. Mixed by Damon Krukowski; mastered by Alan Douches.

Ghost (2) — Key Discography

Ghost (2) was Kurihara’s primary long-running group alongside vocalist/guitarist Masaki Batoh. Formed Tokyo 1984; disbanded 3 August 2014. Most releases on Drag City (US) from the mid-1990s onward.

White Heaven — Key Discography

White Heaven was a Tokyo psych rock group formed by vocalist/guitarist You Ishihara in 1985; split 1997. Kurihara was the lead guitarist. Some members went on to form The Stars (3).
