Overview

Curtis Mayfield was a Chicago-born singer, guitarist, songwriter, and producer who co-founded the R&B vocal group The Impressions in the late 1950s and became one of soul music’s defining voices. During the mid-1960s he established the soul imprints Mayfield Records and Windy C, then left The Impressions in 1970 to pursue a solo career, co-founding the Curtom label alongside Eddie Thomas. His catalogue peaks sharply in the 1970s across soul, funk, and soundtrack work—Curtom alone accounts for 118 releases and remained his primary creative home—before tapering into the 1980s and 1990s. Recurring collaborators include arranger Johnny Pate and producers Roger Anfinsen and Rich Tufo, with The Impressions remaining a frequent presence throughout.
