Shelby Cinca

Shelby Serban-Alex Cinca (also Shelby Serban-Alexandro Cinca). Guitarist, vocalist, designer. Washington DC / Swedish scene. Discogs artist ID: 576444. Best known as the co-founder and guitarist/vocalist of Frodus (1993–1999) and Decahedron / The Black Sea (2002–2004).

Aliases on Discogs: Loki & The Improbable Solution, Prof. Yaya, Reno (11), Triobelisk, catbeats. Name variations: Cinca Shelby, S. Cinca, SC, Shelby, Shelby C., Shelby Serban-Alexandro Cinca.

Overview

Cinca co-founded Frodus with Jason Hamacher in Washington DC in 1993. Through the 1990s Frodus became one of the more visible post-hardcore / math-rock bands out of DC, signing to Tooth & Nail and then releasing their final album on Fueled By Ramen. After Frodus dissolved in 1999, Cinca — alongside Hamacher — joined Joe Lally of Fugazi to form The Black Sea (later Decahedron) on Lovitt Records.

Beyond performing, Cinca is a prolific designer and art director. He ran design and art direction for numerous releases under the Passkontroll.com studio name (credited as a Discogs entity, ID: 3756661) and under the alias Prof. Yaya (ID: 1248761). His design work spans punk / hardcore labels including Lovitt Records, Victory Records, Burning Heart Records, Relapse Records (Dillinger Escape Plan), and Lujo Records.

In parallel, Cinca developed a music project based in Sweden called Frantic Mantis (with Håkan Johansson and Per Stålberg of Division of Laura Lee) and completed work with The Cassettes (Buddyhead Records, 2006). He also recorded under IDM/Game Boy music aliases including catbeats, using Nanoloop software, and released experimental/electronic music under his own name and as Triobelisk.
