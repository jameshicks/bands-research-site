John Reed

British music journalist, archivist, and compilation producer. Discogs artist ID: 405780

Overview

John Reed is a British music journalist and archival compilation specialist with a career spanning four decades across some of the UK’s most prolific reissue and archival labels. He served as research editor for Record Collector from 1989 to 1998, then moved into label roles: Sanctuary Records Group (1998–2006), 395 Music Ltd. (2013–2015), and Cherry Red (2008 to present). His credit trail on Discogs runs from 1986 (management credit on Elton John’s Leather Jackets) through to at least 2026, and encompasses over 200 releases across roles including Compiled By, Coordinator, Project Manager, Product Manager, Liner Notes, Licensing, and Executive Producer.

Reed is best known in independent music circles for curating and compiling Cherry Red’s large-format punk and post-punk archival box set series — beginning with Action Time Vision (2016) and continuing through To The Outside Of Everything (2017), Optimism/Reject (2019), Moving Away From The Pulsebeat (2024), and others in the series. These projects established him as the principal curator of the UK independent punk record in physical archival form. He is repeatedly co-credited alongside licensing specialist Eddy Ball on these compilations.

Reed’s earlier career at Sequel Records and Castle Music (both Sanctuary Records imprints) produced substantial work across Moody Blues reissues, Northern Soul, Immediate Records anthologies, funk and reggae at Trojan Records, Mod and Freakbeat scene compilations, and UK psych/folk. He also wrote liner notes prolifically in the 1990s–2000s era, contributing to reissues for the Jam, Style Council, Small Faces, and Moody Blues among others.

Career Phases

Record Collector Era (pre-1998)

Active as a music journalist with liner note credits from 1991 onwards on Strange Fruit, Big Beat, See For Miles, BGO, Rev-Ola, and Fire Records releases. Notable early credits include liner notes for New Season – The Peel Sessions (Inspiral Carpets, Strange Fruit, 1991), Mighty Baby (Big Beat, 1994), and liner notes/sleeve notes for multiple Small Faces and Immediate Records packages.

Sanctuary/Castle Music/Sequel Era (1998–2006)

Heavy output as Coordinator and Compiler for Castle Music and Sequel Records, covering:

  • Mod/Freakbeat: The Mod Scene, The Freakbeat Scene, The Beat Scene, The Psychedelic Scene (all Deram, 1998); MODrophenia series
  • Northern Soul: The Northern Soul Scene (Deram, 1998)
  • Immediate Records: Out Of Time – The Immediate Anthology, The Darlings Of Wapping Wharf Launderette (both Sequel, 1999); The First Cut (The Immediate Anthology) (Castle, 2001)
  • Reggae/Trojan: Extensive executive production and product management credits at Trojan Records including Tighten Up reissues (2002), Punky Reggae Party (2002), The Mighty Trojan Sound (2003), Jah Love Rockers (2004), and many others
  • Soul/Funk: Curtom Club Classics series (Sequel, 1999); Invictus Club Classics (Castle, 1999 and 2002)
  • UK Psych/Folk: Gather In The Mushrooms (The British Acid Folk Underground 1968–1974) (Castle, 2004)

Additional coordination work for the Kinks (The Kinks Are The Village Green Preservation Society deluxe, Sanctuary, 2004), Motörhead, CCR, and Elkie Brooks among many others.

Earmark (Italy) (2004–2005)

Several reggae/dub project credits on the Italian label Earmark, including Forward Jah Jah Children (2004) and Dawning Of A New Era (2005).

Zonophone/EMI Era (2007)

Three notable psych/Mod compilations for Zonophone (EMI): Ah Feel Like Ahcid (American Psych, 2007), Insane Times (British Psych, 2007), Take My Tip (Mod, 2007).

Cherry Red / Grapefruit Era (2008–present)

Reed’s longest and most high-profile phase. Core output includes:

Indie-pop and post-punk box sets (Cherry Red):

  • Scared To Get Happy (A Story Of Indie-Pop 1980–1989) (2013) — personally conceived and annotated
  • C86 reissue (2014) — credited with conception and licensing
  • Still In A Dream: A Story Of Shoegaze 1988–1995 (2016) — conceived and compiled
  • Action Time Vision (A Story Of Independent UK Punk 1976–1979) (2016) — compiled, coordinated, licensing
  • To The Outside Of Everything (A Story Of UK Post Punk 1977–1981) (2017) — compiled, coordinated, licensing
  • C89 (2018)
  • Optimism/Reject (UK D-I-Y Punk and Post-Punk 1977–1981) (2019) — compiled, produced
  • The Sun Shines Here (The Roots Of Indie-Pop 1980–1984) (2021)
  • Music For New Romantics (2022) — compiled and curated
  • C91 (2022) — compilation producer
  • Dave Brock Presents… This Was Your Future (2022) — conceived and compiled
  • Moving Away From The Pulsebeat (Post-Punk Britain 1977–1981) (2024) — compiled
  • Musik Music Musique 1979 (The Roots Of Synth Pop) (2026) — compiled

Grapefruit Records (Cherry Red sublabel for psych/folk/60s):

  • A Strange Affair (The Sixties Recordings) (2013) — conceived and compiled
  • Love, Poetry And Revolution (2013) — product management
  • I’m A Freak, Baby… (2016) — project management
  • Let’s Go Down And Blow Our Minds (2016)
  • Dust On The Nettles (2015)
  • Multiple Byzantium, Belfast Gypsies, and 1960s act retrospectives through 2021–2022

Other Cherry Red-adjacent:

  • Creation Artifact: The Dawn Of Creation Records 1983–1985 (2015) — compiled alongside Belvedere Sacramento, with liner notes foreword by Alan McGee
  • Everyone’s Going Triple Bad Acid, Yeah! (The Complete Membranes 1980–1993) (2017)
  • Joe Meek’s Tea Chest Tapes series (2022) — concept, curation, coordination
