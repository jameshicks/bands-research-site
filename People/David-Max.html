David Max

Guitarist, bassist, engineer, and vocalist. New Jersey-based musician active from the early 1990s. Discogs artist ID: 211249

Known name variations: D. Max, Dave Max, David Maxxx, David XXX Maxxxx, Max

Overview

David Max is a multi-instrumentalist whose career spans indie rock (as bassist/guitarist in Tadpoles), a pivotal two-year run as guitarist in Psychic TV / PTV3 (2007–2008), and scattered engineering and session work. He plays guitar, bass, synthesizer, and has occasional vocal credits. His longest-running association is with Tadpoles, a New Jersey band he joined around 1992 and which remained active into the 2020s.

Groups

  • Tadpoles — Discogs ID: 536547. New Jersey indie/psych rock band; David Max played bass and contributed guitar on their four studio albums (1992–1999) and a 2025 EP. Core members also include Nick Kramer, Todd Parker, and Mike Audino.
  • Psychic TV — Discogs ID: 17926. Max joined PTV3 for two albums in 2007–2008 (see below).
  • Sons of the Void — Discogs ID: 4919821. Max is the only credited member on Discogs; no further release information in the database.

Connections

  • Max’s entry into PTV3 connected his New Jersey indie-rock world to Genesis P-Orridge’s long-running experimental project. His engineering credit on Hell Is Invisible places him among the producers rather than purely as a sideman.
  • The Cheval Sombre and Spectrum session work in 2009 — right after his PTV3 tenure ended — suggests Nick Kramer remained a connector to the psych/drone milieu around Sonic Boom.
  • The Tadpoles record Make Believe (2025), mixed by Sonic Boom, brings the two threads together three decades later.
  • Dorothy Max Prior appears in the PTV/Psychic TV orbit in the vault; the shared surname “Max” may be coincidental — no relationship confirmed in Discogs data.
