Steve Reid (2)

Discogs ID: 292561 Real name: Steve Reid Born: 29 January 1944, The Bronx, New York City, USA Died: 13 April 2010, New York City, USA (aged 66) Active: 1960s–2010

American jazz and free-funk drummer. Session drummer for Motown in the 1960s; later played with James Brown, Fela Kuti, Miles Davis, Ornette Coleman, and Sun Ra. Founded his own label Mustevic Sound Records in the mid-1970s, releasing a string of self-produced free-jazz/funk records. Late-career collaboration with Kieran Hebden (Four Tet) produced six albums on Domino (2006–2008), introducing him to a new generation.

Death

Reid died on 13 April 2010 in New York City, aged 66, after a battle with cancer. Multiple contemporaneous reports and his Wikipedia article specify the cause as throat cancer. (Wikipedia, as of 2026-06-29; TwentyFourBit, April 2010; Something Else Reviews, April 2010)

The illness was extended rather than sudden — the TwentyFourBit piece, citing Domino Records, reports he “died last night after a battle with cancer.” No specific diagnosis date has surfaced in press coverage; the search summary suggests 2009 but no primary source confirms this.

Kieran Hebden’s statement at the time (via TwentyFourBit / Domino): “Steve was one of my great friends and the most wonderful musician I have ever encountered. The music and adventures we shared have been some of the most happy and meaningful experiences I’ve ever had.”
