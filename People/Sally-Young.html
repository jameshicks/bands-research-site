Sally Young

Guitarist, bassist, drummer, violinist, pianist, and vocalist. Co-founder of Ut (formed December 1978, NYC). Later a member of Quint (4). Discogs artist ID: 531897

Overview

Sally Young is one of the three founding members of Ut, the NYC No Wave / post-punk trio that also included Nina Canal and Jacqui Ham. The band was founded in December 1978 and operated on a strict rotating-instrument and rotating-vocal policy: Young plays guitar, bass, drums, violin, piano, and vocals across the Ut discography, shifting roles track-by-track. She is also a credited songwriter on multiple Ut recordings.

Discogs lists no real name or aliases for Young beyond name-variation spellings (“S. Young,” “Sally,” “Young,” “S”). She has no solo discography entry outside her two bands.

After Ut’s active period wound down (the band went on extended hiatus from around 1991), Young became a member of Quint (4), a UK-based ensemble. The band released a 7” single on Southern Records in 1995, engineered by Steve Albini, and followed it with a full-length album, Time Wounds All Heals (10 tracks), on Egypt Records in 1998.

Quint (4)

Quint (4) (Discogs: 1899804) was a UK band that released a 7” single, “Blueprint To A Blackout / Sawtooth,” on Southern Records (cat. 18533-7) in 1995, engineered by Steve Albini, and a full-length CD album, Time Wounds All Heals (Egypt Records, cat. Egypt 1, 1998). Genres: Rock / Alternative Rock / Noise. See Quint (4) for the full article.
