Overview

Jim Jupp is the co-founder of Ghost Box, the British label that became the focal point of the hauntological music movement. He records primarily as Belbury Poly and The Belbury Circle, working as composer, synthesizer player, and producer across styles including Experimental, Ambient, and Folk. His Discogs-visible catalogue runs from the 2010s into the 2020s, with Ghost Box accounting for the large majority of his output; he has also appeared on Castles in Space and Static Caravan. His most surprising credit thread runs through Polydor, where he collaborated with Paul Weller across several 2020 releases — an unexpected reach beyond the hauntological underground.
