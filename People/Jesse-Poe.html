Percussionist, multi-instrumentalist, producer, and engineer. Best known as the leader and founder of Tanakh, a Richmond, Virginia musical collective formed in 2000. Also one half of the electronic/house duo Jac N’ Jill (with Jacob Poe). His work spans experimental neofolk, post-rock, drone, and house music, and he has appeared as a session player, engineer, and composer across a wide range of projects.

Biography

Early Work and Tanakh (2000–2004)

Jesse Poe founded Tanakh in Richmond, Virginia in 2000. The collective drew on a large rotating cast of performers and operated with Poe as producer and conceptual center, while the lineup expanded and contracted across recordings. Their earliest documented release is an untitled CDr split with Six Organs Of Admittance (Outer Orbit, 2001), followed by the debut full-length Villa Claustrophobia (Alien8 Recordings, 2002), a sprawling experimental/folk work recorded and produced by Poe himself alongside contributions from David Lowery, Ned Oldham, Mick Turner, Jessica Billey, and others.

In parallel with Tanakh’s emergence, Poe appeared as a session player on Bonnevill’s Pelican (Bella Union / Instinct Records, 2001), contributing percussion to the track “Ides.” He also contributed recording and saw to The Iditarod’s The Ghost, The Elf, The Cat And The Angel (Bluesanct, 2002).

Tanakh’s second full-length, Dieu Deuil (Alien8 Recordings, 2004), featured a large ensemble including Darius Jones (alto saxophone), Jim White (guitar, keys, percussion), Dan Calhoune, Curtis Fye, Michele Poulos, Brian Jones (7), and others. Poe’s credited roles on Dieu Deuil were expansive: vocals, guitar, harmonium, singing bowls, bowed glockenspiel, contact mics, electronics, percussion, field recordings, production, and songwriting. The same year, Tanakh released an untitled two-disc drone/experimental set (Alien8 Recordings, 2004), co-produced with Ronan Chris Murphy and mixed by Murphy.

Engineering and Studio Work (2008–2012)

Poe shifted into studio engineering roles in the late 2000s. He is credited as engineer on Last Of The Good Guys (Decca, 2008) and as tracking engineer on It’s About Pride (2012). He also holds a “Recorded By [Studio 19]” credit on The Runner ([PIAS] Belgium, 2008).

Jac N’ Jill (2015)

Jac N’ Jill is a deep house / electronic duo formed with Jacob Poe. They released two EPs on Radda Records in 2015: Crazy Deep EP (RADDA008) and Blue Morning EP (RR012), the latter featuring remixes by Art Of Hot, Jeromy Nail, and Jonathan Cates.

Ghost Songs and Later Work (2019–2020)

In 2020, Poe co-released Ghost Songs (self-released WAV, 2 tracks) as a joint credit with Alan Davidson (4) (also known as Kitchen Cynics). The release — two folk songs, “Babe On The Shore” and “When Father Died” — is credited as written and performed by both.

In 2019, Poe received a composition credit on “Komma” from Present Company by the Peter Hess Quartet (Diskonife Records, 2019), a jazz recording also featuring Tomas Fujiwara, Brian Drye, and Adam Hopkins.
