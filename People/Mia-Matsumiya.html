Mia Matsumiya

Discogs ID: 476397
Name variations: M Matsumiya, Mia Matsumi

Violinist, vocalist, and multi-instrumentalist. Core member of Kayo Dot and co-founder (with Toby Driver) of the duo Tartar Lamb / Tartar Lamb II. Also a contributor to Gregor Samsa (3), Maudlin Of The Well, and Daughters, as well as film score work on Varèse Sarabande.

Biography

Matsumiya is a New York-based violinist who has been a fixture in the avant-rock / compositional-metal scene associated with Toby Driver since at least 2004. Her earliest documented Discogs credit is as both performer and photographer on Driver’s solo release In The L..L..Library Loft (2005, Tzadik) — a live document from 2004 sessions featuring the pre-Kayo Dot lineup, recorded while the band was transitioning from Maudlin Of The Well.

She became an established Kayo Dot member through the band’s run of Hydra Head and Robotic Empire releases, playing violin and increasingly broader instrumentation (voice, organ, synthesizer) as the band’s sound shifted from doom-inflected rock toward atmospheric and jazz-influenced territory. Her most tonally expansive role is on Blue Lambency Downward (2008, Hydra Head), where she contributes violin, voice, organ, and synth (“knobs”) alongside producer Randall Dunn’s sound-design work.

In parallel, she and Driver formed Tartar Lamb in 2006 as a guitar-violin duo. Their debut Sixty Metonymies (2007, Ice Level Music) was augmented by Tim Byrnes on trumpet and Andrew Greenwald on percussion. The project returned in 2011 as Tartar Lamb II (same members, new name) with Polyimage Of Known Exits (2011, Ice Level Music).

Outside the Kayo Dot orbit, Matsumiya contributed to Gregor Samsa (3)‘s Rest (2007, Talk To Me Records) and Over Air (2009, The Kora Records) as a performer, and appeared as a guest violinist on Maudlin Of The Well’s Part The Second (2009, self-released). She played violin on track “Providence By Gaslight” of Daughters’ Hell Songs (2006, Hydra Head), where Forbes Graham contributed trumpet to the same track.

Her film score credits include violin soloist work on The Killing Room (2009) and The Lazarus Project (2009), both released on Varèse Sarabande.

Personnel context — Key releases

Dowsing Anemone With Copper Tongue (2006, Robotic Empire)

Kayo Dot lineup: Toby Driver (guitar, piano, clarinet, perc., voice), Greg Massi (guitar), John Carchia (guitar), Ryan McGuire (bass), Tom Malone (2) (drums), Forbes Graham (trumpet, euphonium), Kyouhei Sada (guitar, keys, effects), Mia Matsumiya (violin, perc.). Lyrics mostly by Jason Byron.

Blue Lambency Downward (2008, Hydra Head)

Driver solo + ensemble. Produced by Randall Dunn. Charlie Zeleny (drums), Hans Teuber (clarinets, sax, flute), Skerik (tenor/bari sax, vibraphone), Dave Abramson (malletophone), Colin Marston (drums recording).

Sixty Metonymies (2007, Ice Level Music)

Tartar Lamb core: Driver (guitar, bass, vocals), Matsumiya (violin), Tim Byrnes (trumpet), Andrew Greenwald (percussion, computer). Produced and recorded by Randall Dunn.
