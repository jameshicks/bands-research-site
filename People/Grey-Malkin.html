Grey Malkin

Discogs ID: 5855611
Location: Scotland (East Lothian / Edinburgh area, per 2020 interview)

British folk/psychedelic musician. One of Alan Davidson (4)‘s most frequent collaborators, with 12 shared releases — shared titles include “Kilgour’s Tale”, “The Wonder Room”, and “Babby’s Ghost / An Encounter By Moonlight”. Part of the UK wyrd-folk and freak-folk underground.

Biography

Real name deliberately withheld; the artist performs under a pseudonym taken from The Grey Malkin, the second album by his own project The Hare and the Moon. In a 2021 interview he noted that “someone thought I was actually called Graham Malkin, which caused some hilarity” — clarifying that is not his name either. (African Paper interview, 2021)

Based in Scotland (self-described as preferring solitude, “often happiest in my own company, or away walking alone in the woods or by the coastline”). (African Paper interview, 2021; Feral Strumpet interview, 2020)

Self-described as having limited instrumental proficiency (“can’t play guitar, mandolin, or keyboards properly, even after all these years”) but prolific as a collaborator and composer. Interests include folklore, Victorian and Edwardian ghost stories (Arthur Machen, Robert Aickman), traditional ballads, and folk horror cinema (The Wicker Man, The Owl Service, Children of the Stones). Also works in photography, painting, and writing; contributes to Moof Magazine and Rituals & Declarations zine. (Feral Strumpet interview, 2020)

Projects

  • The Hare and the Moon — primary project, active c. 2009–2017; Grey Malkin wrote and recorded the music, with a separate singer providing vocals. (Heathen Harvest interview, 2016)
  • Ashtoreth & Grey Malkin — trilogy of albums: Pilgrim, Hermit, Heretic. (African Paper interview, 2021)
  • Embertides — collaboration with David Colohan. (Wyrd Britain, 2018)
  • Widow’s Weeds, Meadowsilver, The Black Swan Triad, Amanda Votta & The Spectral Light — further collaborative projects. (African Paper interview, 2021)
  • The Haunted Cabinar — collaboration with Kentin Jivek. (Heathen Harvest interview, 2016)
  • Alan Davidson (4) — 12 shared Discogs releases. (Discogs)
