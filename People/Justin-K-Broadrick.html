Justin K Broadrick

Discogs ID: 79600 Real name: Justin Karl Michael Broadrick Born: 15 August 1969, Birmingham, UK Active: 1986–present

Musician, multi-instrumentalist, and producer. Founding member of Godflesh (1988) and Jesu (2003). Played guitar on Side A of Napalm Death’s Scum (1987) before either project existed. One of the central architects of industrial metal and drone/shoegaze. Runs own imprint Avalanche Recordings. Operates under many aliases (see below).

Career

1986–1988 — Grindcore origins

At 16, Broadrick played guitar on Side A of Napalm Death’s Scum (Earache, MOSH 3, 1987; 59 pressings) — the record that defined grindcore. He wrote and performed all guitar/lyrics for Side A (tracks A1–A12) before leaving the band; Side B was recorded by an entirely different lineup (Bill Steer, Lee Dorrian, Jim Whitely). Head Of David are credited as producing Side A.

Before and after Napalm Death, Broadrick played in Fall Of Because and began experimenting with industrial textures in Birmingham’s underground scene.

1988–2002 — Godflesh

Formed Godflesh in Birmingham with bassist G.C. Green (ID 222391). The project used a drum machine in place of a live drummer, creating a locked-groove mechanical heaviness. Paul Neville (ID 69603) appeared as second guitarist on some early material.

Selfless (1994) is the most structurally diverse Godflesh album: drum programming plus live drums (Bryan Mantia, ID 1452758), mastered by Bob Ludwig (ID 271098). Songs Of Love And Hate (1996) features cover photography by Andrés Serrano (ID 771802).

Godflesh broke up in 2002; reformed 2009. Return albums: A World Lit Only By Fire (Avalanche, 2014; 8 pressings), Post Self (Hospital Productions, 2017; 7 pressings), Purge (Avalanche, 2023; 5 pressings).

2003–present — Jesu

Founded Jesu as a post-Godflesh project, pivoting from industrial metal toward drone, shoegaze, and ambient textures. Members: Broadrick (all instruments/production), Ted Parsons (ID 65679, drums), Dermot Dalton (ID 69916, bass on selected tracks).

Avalanche Recordings

Broadrick’s self-run label for solo and Godflesh/Jesu/JK Flesh releases. Located in Birmingham. Also used for ambient/experimental releases under Pale Sketcher and other aliases.
