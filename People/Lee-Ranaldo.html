Lee Ranaldo

Born Leonard Mark Ranaldo, February 3, 1956, Glen Cove, Long Island, New York. Guitarist, composer, vocalist, producer, visual artist, and writer. Co-founder of Sonic Youth. Discogs artist ID: 40978.

Overview

Ranaldo moved to New York City in the late 1970s and entered the downtown experimental scene through Glenn Branca’s guitar orchestra, performing on Branca’s landmark The Ascension (1981). Shortly after, together with Thurston Moore and Kim Gordon, he co-founded Sonic Youth, which became one of the most influential alternative rock bands of the 1980s and 1990s. Throughout Sonic Youth’s run (1981–2011) Ranaldo maintained a parallel and prolific solo career spanning noise, drone, spoken word, free improvisation, and indie rock, releasing records on Blast First, Table Of The Elements, Matador Records, and Mute, among others. He has also worked extensively as a producer and recording engineer for other artists, and is a noted visual artist and poet.

Ranaldo’s solo alias on Discogs is Lazy Eight (also the name of his own imprint, used for demo/alternate-mix releases). His most prominent side project is Text Of Light, a free-improvisation ensemble that scored and performed live to avant-garde films; he also performs under his own name in quartet format as Lee Ranaldo And The Dust.

History by Era

1980–1983: Glenn Branca and the Downtown Scene

After arriving in New York, Ranaldo joined Glenn Branca’s guitar orchestra. His first major recorded credit is The Ascension (99 Records, 1981), alongside Branca, David Rosenbloom, and Ned Sublette on guitar; Jeffrey Glenn on bass; Stephan Wischerth on drums; produced by Ed Bahlman of 99 Records; engineered by James Farber. He also appears on Symphony No. 1 (Tonal Plexus) (ROIR, 1983), where he is credited as co-producer alongside Branca and Anne DeMarinis, and on Symphony No. 3 (Gloria) (Les Disques Du Crépuscule, 1983). His participation in the Branca Ensemble concluded around 1983; he is also credited on the later Symphony No. 2 (The Peak Of The Sacred) (Atavistic, 1992), recorded May 14, 1982. He was also credited “Arranged By” on a 1981 release on the Dutch label Kremlin (Februari - April ‘81).

Thurston Moore first encountered Ranaldo performing with Branca at the Noise Fest (June 1981). Shortly after, Moore, Ranaldo, and Kim Gordon formed Sonic Youth.

1981–2011: Sonic Youth

Ranaldo was a core member of Sonic Youth across its entire active span. His Discogs credits with the band include performance on every major release from the debut Sonic Youth EP (Neutral Records, 1982) through The Eternal (Matador, 2009). He contributed guitar and vocals, co-wrote material, handled some photography, and occasionally assisted with recordings. Key milestones:

  • Sonic Youth EP (Neutral Records, 1982)
  • Confusion Is Sex (Neutral Records, 1983)
  • Bad Moon Rising (Blast First / Homestead, 1985)
  • EVOL (Blast First, 1986)
  • Sister (Blast First, 1987)
  • Daydream Nation (Blast First, 1988) — Ranaldo co-wrote all songs
  • Goo (DGC, 1990)
  • Dirty (DGC, 1992)
  • Experimental Jet Set, Trash And No Star (DGC, 1994)
  • A Thousand Leaves (DGC, 1998)
  • NYC Ghosts & Flowers (Geffen, 2000)
  • Murray Street (DGC, 2002)
  • Sonic Nurse (Geffen, 2004)
  • Rather Ripped (Geffen, 2006)
  • The Eternal (Matador, 2009)

Sonic Youth disbanded in 2011 following the separation of Moore and Gordon.

1987–2011: Early Solo and Collaborative Work

Ranaldo released his debut solo album From Here To Infinity (Blast First, 1987), a spoken-word/noise collage LP with artwork by Savage Pencil. A retrospective East Jesus (Some Recordings 1981–1991) (Blast First, 1994) collected earlier solo material.

Outside his own records, Ranaldo produced Babes In Toyland’s Fontanelle (Reprise, 1992) with Kat Bjelland. He also produced or recorded for several Australian acts via Ra Records during the early-to-mid 1990s: Sound As Ever (1993), Berlin Chair (1994), Hi Fi Way (1995, You Am I) among others.

2004: Text Of Light

Text Of Light is a free-improvisation ensemble Ranaldo co-founded with Alan Licht, Christian Marclay, William Hooker, Tim Barnes, Ulrich Krieger, and Gregor Asch (DJ Olive). The group performed live soundtracks to avant-garde films (Stan Brakhage, Andy Warhol, Jack Smith, etc.). Discogs ID: 288448.

2009: Bloomington Collaboration / Ambient Releases

Bloomington, Indiana … Autumn (Streamline, 2009) saw Ranaldo working with Christoph Heemann and Kevin Spencer (2). He continued releasing solo ambient/drone material throughout this period.

2012–2017: Lee Ranaldo And The Dust; Matador and Mute

Following Sonic Youth’s dissolution, Ranaldo formed Lee Ranaldo And The Dust with Steve Shelley (drums), Alan Licht (guitar), and Tim Luntzel (bass). Discogs artist ID: 3489021.
