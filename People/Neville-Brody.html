Neville Brody

Role: Graphic designer, art director Born: 1957, London Active: Late 1970s–present

Connection to This Network

Neville Brody shared a squat with Tom Heslop of 23 Skidoo, and through this connection became design director at Fetish Records — which signed 23 Skidoo and became one of the key post-punk/industrial labels.

23 Skidoo’s members also helped with sleeve finishing for Dining Out Records — placing Brody one degree from the Dining Out scene.

Design Work at Fetish Records

Brody’s Fetish work was among his earliest professional output — pre-digital, hand-crafted using wooden carvings, plaster hands, paper cut-outs, film overlays, and PMT (photo-mechanical transfer) prints. Type was set professionally. Aesthetic: Dadaism and Pop Art, stark geometric forms, unconventional typography, limited colour palettes.

Significance

Brody’s Fetish work established his reputation before The Face brought him mainstream recognition. His method — hand-carved and hand-assembled before photographing — was entirely pre-digital and built on Dadaist/Pop Art principles. The squat connection to Tom Heslop is the origin of his entire professional career.

  • Modern Romance — designed the sleeve for “Walking In The Rain” (WEA, 1983) and “Just My Imagination (Running Away With Me)” (Atlantic, 1983); one of several non-Fetish commercial commissions during his The Face years

Touch / Wozencroft Connection

Brody’s relationship with Jon Wozencroft (Jon Wozencroft) runs across two decades of shared Discogs credits. Early cluster (1983–1990): Touch compilations (Touch Travel, 1984; Meridians 2, 1983), The Hafler Trio’s A Thirsty Fish (Touch, 1987), the Greenpeace Breakthrough compilation (Melodiya, USSR, 1989), and Wire’s Manscape (Mute, 1990). Later cluster (2008–2022): Brody provides art direction and layout for Wire’s Pinkflag catalogue alongside Wozencroft’s photography, covering Object 47, Red Barked Tree, Change Becomes Us, the 2018 catalogue reissues (Pink Flag, Chairs Missing, 154), Mind Hive, and PF456 Redux — totalling 20 shared titles across both clusters.

The FUSE project (1990, co-founded by Brody and Wozencroft) is a typographic research and publishing project, with Wozencroft serving as editor. Brody’s Discogs credits do not include a FUSE-specific entry; the project exists as a publishing imprint rather than a record label.

Key Connections

  • 23 Skidoo — Tom Heslop’s squat-mate; entry into Fetish Records; designed all key early releases
  • Fetish Records — design director, c.1980–1983
  • Throbbing Gristle — designed Five Albums for them on Fetish
  • Cabaret Voltaire — designed Stephen Mallinder’s solo LP Pow-wow; also Manscape (Mute, 1990) with Wozencroft
  • The Hafler Trio — typography on A Thirsty Fish (Touch, TO:9, 1987, with Jon Wozencroft); Discogs credit on A Thirsty Fish. The Hafler Trio also released Kuklos (Touch, TOC:2, 1988) as a soundtrack to Wozencroft’s book documenting Brody’s graphic language.
  • Jon Wozencroft — Touch/FUSE co-collaborator; 20 shared Discogs credits spanning 1983–2022
  • Wire / Pinkflag — joint art direction with Wozencroft from Object 47 (2008) through 2021 catalogue reissues
  • The Face magazine — art director 1981–86 (visual bridge to the entire post-punk/new wave world)
