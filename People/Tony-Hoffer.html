Tony Hoffer

Record producer, mixer, and engineer. Real name: Anthony Hoffer. Born Memphis, Tennessee. Discogs artist ID: 66626.

Overview

Tony Hoffer is a Los Angeles-based producer and mixer whose career spans the late 1990s through the 2020s. He broke through as a co-producer and engineer on Beck’s Midnite Vultures (1999) and went on to handle mixing for AIR’s 10,000 Hz Legend (2001) and co-production of Phoenix’s Alphabetical (2004) — credits that put him at the center of the early-2000s French-adjacent indie/alternative circuit. Through the mid-2000s he was the go-to producer for a run of British indie debuts: The Fratellis’ Costello Music (2006), Belle & Sebastian’s The Life Pursuit (2006) and Write About Love (2010), The Kooks’ Inside In / Inside Out (2005) and Konk (2008), and Supergrass’s Life On Other Planets (2002) and So Much For The City (with The Thrills, 2003). He is the house mixer for Dangerbird Records — credited on over 80 Dangerbird releases — where he produced Fitz And The Tantrums’ More Than Just A Dream (2013) and served as executive producer on Eulogies’ Here Anonymous (2009). His mixing work with Depeche Mode (Sounds Of The Universe, 2009; Junk, 2016; DSVII, 2019; Memento Mori-era singles) represents his longest relationship with a single major act. He also mixed M83’s Hurry Up, We’re Dreaming. (2011) and multiple Metric albums in the 2010s–2020s.

Before his production career, Hoffer played guitar in the Los Angeles bands Last Carousel and This Great Religion alongside Justin Meldal-Johnsen from approximately 1988 to 1993. Discogs confirms two limited-edition 7”s on Intermission Records (1990, 1991) and a self-released cassette (1993) under the This Great Religion name; no releases found under Last Carousel. He also appeared with Meldal-Johnsen and drummer Matt Gunnell on the 1992 Japanese Victor mini-album by Nanaki And The Spice, arranged and produced by David Campbell.

Name variations on Discogs: Anthony Hoffer, Hoffer, T. Hoffer, Tonny Hoffer, Tony Holfer, Tony Miami Hoffer.

Recurring Studio Collaborators

The engineer Todd Burke (Discogs ID: 324194) appears consistently across Hoffer’s productions from approximately 2003 through 2011 — So Much For The City (The Thrills, 2003), The Life Pursuit (Belle & Sebastian, 2006), Costello Music (The Fratellis, 2006), Konk (The Kooks, 2008), Write About Love (Belle & Sebastian, 2010), Hurry Up, We’re Dreaming. (M83, 2011; Hoffer’s mixing sessions). Burke is effectively Hoffer’s primary tracking engineer across this British indie and French act cluster.

Steven Rhodes (Discogs ID: 469244) appears as engineer on So Much For The City (2003) and Swoon (2009), among others.

Jason Gossman (Discogs ID: 299546) appears as assistant engineer and mixing assistant on Swoon and Konk.

Career Overlap with Justin Meldal-Johnsen

Hoffer and Justin Meldal-Johnsen share roots in the LA alternative rock band Last Carousel / This Great Religion (c. 1988–1993). Their paths intersect again across the Beck albums (Midnite Vultures, Guero), and both worked on M83’s Hurry Up, We’re Dreaming. (2011) — Meldal-Johnsen as producer and engineer, Hoffer as mixer.
