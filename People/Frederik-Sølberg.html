Overview

Frederik Sølberg is a Danish musician whose primary project is Rough Days For Diamond Trade — documented on Discogs as an alias for him — which expands to a trio live, with Thomas Stück on synths and programming and Laura Ratschau on bass. His catalogue is sparse: one release in the 2000s and two in the 2010s, spread across indie rock, shoegaze, and downtempo. Beyond Rough Days For Diamond Trade he is a member of Lake Placid and Lasse & Lasse, with his tightest working relationship being Lasse Herbst (8 shared releases); he also shares credits with Cæcilie Trier, Jannis Noya Makrigiannis, and Jonas Bjerre. His releases landed on a range of small Danish imprints as well as the Michigan-based Ghostly International.
