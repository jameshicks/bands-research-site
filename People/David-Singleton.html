David Singleton

Producer, engineer, digital editor. UK. Discogs artist ID: 276130. Also releases music under the alias The Vicar (2).

Overview

Co-founder of Discipline Global Mobile alongside Robert Fripp. Singleton is the primary technical and production architect of the DGM operation: he functions as digital editing engineer, mastering engineer (SADiE system), mixing consultant, and label manager. Credited on approximately 319 DGM releases as producer and 237 as engineer. Also credited on Fripp’s soundscape projects as “Soundscape Assemblage And Compositor.”

Also a member of the project Tone Probe with Fripp, the production entity credited on many DGM compilations and reissue productions.

Key Credits (DGM)

Editing, mastering, and production across virtually the entire DGM catalog — from early 1990s Fripp soundscape recordings through King Crimson’s 50th Anniversary series. Frequently co-credited alongside Robert Fripp as “A Tone Probe Production.”
