Overview

Joe Potts is an American multi-instrumentalist active in experimental, noise, and free improvisation circles since 1976. He is a member of several Los Angeles-based groups — most notably Le Forte Four, Airway, Extended Organ, and No Hope Orchestra — and his releases appear predominantly on the Los Angeles Free Music Society label, signalling deep roots in that city’s avant-garde scene. His closest collaborators include Rick Potts and Tom Potts alongside Tom Recchion, Chip Chapman, and Dennis Duck, all recurring figures in the L.A. experimental underground. With 51 total credits reaching into the 2020s, he has remained a working presence across noise, musique concrète, and abstract improvisation for nearly five decades.
