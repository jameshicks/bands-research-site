Rachel Ware

Discogs artist ID: 689778
Name variations on record: Rachel, Raaachel, Rachel Ware Zooi
Groups: The Mountain Goats (member 1991–1996), The Bright Mountain Choir

Rachel Ware was the co-member of The Mountain Goats during the band’s earliest cassette-era recordings (1991–1996), performing alongside John Darnielle and sharing co-writing credit on a substantial portion of that period’s output. Her name appears on every Mountain Goats release from 1992 through 1996. Beyond the Mountain Goats she has no documented Discogs credits outside that orbit.

Biography

Early Mountain Goats Era (1991–1996)

Rachel Ware was part of the Mountain Goats from the outset of the band’s recording life. The Discogs profile for The Mountain Goats lists her as one half of the core 1991–1996 lineup alongside John Darnielle, with Amy Piatt, Rosanne Lindley, and Sarah Arslanian noted as occasional members across the same period.

The earliest release on which she is named is Songs For Petronius (1992, Shrimper), where she is credited under the band designation “The Mountain Goats Are” alongside Darnielle, Piatt, Lindley, and Arslanian — all listed by first name only (her credit reads “Rachel”). The same five-person collective credited on that release also appears on Chile De Árbol (1993, Ajax Records (2)) and Hot Garden Stomp (1993, Shrimper).

By the 1994 releases — Beautiful Rat Sunset (Shrimper) and Zopilote Machine (Ajax Records) — the credit formulation shifts to “Performer [We Are]”, maintaining the same collective. On Zopilote Machine, The Bright Mountain Choir (Discogs ID: 1012113) is additionally credited as “Recorded By” alongside the Mountain Goats themselves — the Bright Mountain Choir being a Discogs artist entity whose members are identical to the early Mountain Goats occasional lineup: Ware, Piatt, Lindley, and Arslanian.

Nine Black Poppies (1995, Emperor Jones) is the release on which Ware’s co-writing role is most explicitly documented. She and Darnielle are both credited as “Performer [The Mountain Goats Are]”, and both are credited “Written-By” specifically for tracks 1 to 8. This is the clearest Discogs evidence of her as a genuine creative contributor, not merely a performer. The ninth track on Nine Black Poppies uses text by poet Norman Dubie, which accounts for the scoped track range on the co-writing credit.

Photography for Nine Black Poppies is credited to Jason Ware — the same surname as Rachel, suggesting a family connection, though this is not documented in Discogs beyond the credit.

Songs For Peter Hughes (1995, Sonic Squid) credits both Darnielle and Rachel Ware as Performers. Ware is absent from the written-by credit on that EP (songs are credited to Darnielle, with one track co-written by Jonas “Joker” Berggren of Ace of Base).

Sweden (1996, Shrimper) is the last Discogs release on which Rachel Ware appears as Performer. Written-by credits on Sweden are attributed to The Mountain Goats as a collective entity rather than to individual members.

After 1996 she does not appear on any further Discogs release, Mountain Goats or otherwise. The band’s next documented period (2002 onward) featured Peter Hughes (2) as Darnielle’s primary collaborator.

The Bright Mountain Choir

Discogs ID: 1012113
Members (per Discogs): Amy Piatt, Rachel Ware, Rosanne Lindley, Sarah Arslanian.

The Bright Mountain Choir is the name under which the same women who appeared as occasional/collective members of the early Mountain Goats are grouped as a distinct Discogs artist entity. Its sole Discogs release credit is as “Recorded By” on Zopilote Machine (1994, Ajax Records). There is no separate discography for the Bright Mountain Choir as a primary credited act — it functioned as a recording collective or backing unit for that specific release rather than as an independently releasing band.
