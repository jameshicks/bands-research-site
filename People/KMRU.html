KMRU

Nairobi-born, Berlin-based Kenyan sound artist and electronic musician. Real name: Joseph Kamaru Mwangi. Discogs artist ID: 7020237. Grandson of Joseph Kamaru (Discogs ID: 3382075), the influential Kenyan Benga, gospel, and political musician. Active from at least 2017; primary labels include Editions Mego, Warp Records, Touch, Byrd Out, Seil Records, and Injazero. Works across ambient, experimental electronics, field recording, and sound art.

Overview

KMRU is among the most internationally visible African experimental electronic artists of his generation. Beginning with self-released work around 2017, he broke through to European experimental label circuits with the Erased EP (Byrd Out, 2019) and Peel (Editions Mego, 2020), the latter drawing widespread critical attention. His work draws on field recording, synthesis, and ambient construction, often engaging with themes of place, memory, and perception. He bears a notable family lineage: his grandfather Joseph Kamaru was a major figure in Kenyan popular music from the 1970s onward — a Benga and gospel artist, political activist, and prolific recording artist.

His trajectory from self-release and the Nairobi-based Dagoretti label to Warp Records (the Rapture To Rupt collaboration with Seefeel, 2021) and Touch (Natur, 2024) represents one of the more striking trans-continental arcs in recent experimental music. He is also notable for the depth of his collaborative relationships with European producers and engineers, including Stephan Mathieu (mastering on Peel, Dissolution Grip, and Kin) and Fennesz (co-writer on track A2 of Kin, 2026).

Biography by Era

2017 — Self-Released Beginnings

KMRU first appeared on Discogs as a primary artist with Dadar (2017), a self-released digital work issued in Germany. Limited data in the Discogs DB; the release represents his earliest documented activity.

2019 — European Debut

Erased EP (Byrd Out, BYR017, 2019) was his first release on a UK/European label. Design by G.S-L Studio (George Stewart-Lockhart, Discogs ID: 5885467), the primary design house for Byrd Out under Andrew Weatherall. The connection to Byrd Out — a label otherwise rooted in the Weatherall circle — placed KMRU in an eclectic roster alongside free jazz (Evan Parker), dub (Mad Professor), and acid techno (Hardfloor).

2020 — Breakthrough Year

2020 saw a concentrated burst of releases across several labels:

  • Drawing Water (self-released, Kenya) — digital album
  • Jar (Seil Records, Germany) — first appearance on that label
  • Opaquer (Dagoretti, Kenya, cat# DG30/BLB131) — vinyl LP, the most substantial Kenya-based release; recorded, composed, and produced by KMRU (credited as “K M R U”); artwork by Kevin Karash (as Kevin Karanja); mastered by Jim Roll
  • Peel (Editions Mego, Austria) — the release that drew European critical notice; produced and written by KMRU; mastered by Stephan Mathieu
  • Saal (self-released) — digital

The Peel / Editions Mego connection was a landmark: the label, run by Peter Rehberg (Pita) in Vienna, was and remains the central platform for post-digital experimental and electroacoustic music.

2021 — Collaborative Expansion

  • Dialogue (A-TON, Germany, sub-label of Ostgut Ton) — compilation featuring KMRU alongside Luke Slater (Planetary Assault Systems), Anthony Child (Surgeon), Lady Starlight, Speedy J, and Tom Moth; mixed by Luke Slater, mastered by Andreas Kauffelt (Subtonal)
  • Logue (Injazero, UK) — solo album; produced and written by KMRU; mastered by Joshua Eustis (Sons Of Magdalene); art direction by Sine Buyuka, artwork by Logor Oluwamuyiwa, design by Özge Cöne
  • Rapture To Rupt (Warp Records, UK) — split/collaborative release with Seefeel; his most prominent label association to that point

2022 — Multiple Fronts

  • Imperceptible Perceptible (Longform Editions) — solo release; minimal additional credits in the DB
  • Limen (Subtext, Germany, sub-label of Multiverse Ltd.) — solo album; a notable appearance on one of the more rigorous UK-rooted experimental imprints
  • Epoch (Seil Records, Germany) — second Seil album; KMRU credited for both music and artwork
  • Why Is It They Say A City Like Any City? (Karlrecords, Germany) — listed as contributor (Field Recording, Synthesizer), not primary; an important documentary-style release
  • Temporary Stored (self-released) — digital

2023 — Return to Byrd Out, OFNOT Debut

  • Dissolution Grip (OFNOT, Germany) — solo; produced and written by KMRU; KMRU also credited (as Joseph Kamaru) for artwork; mastered by Stephan Mathieu, layout/design by Joe Gilmore
  • Glim (self-released) — digital
  • KMRU / Hibotep (Byrd Out, BYR040, UK) — split with Hibotep (Hibo Elmi); artwork (uncredited) by KMRU; mastered (uncredited) by Dotan Cohen; design by Oliver Bancroft
  • Stupor (Other Power, Worldwide) — a label sublabeled under We Jazz (Finland)

2024 — Touch and Phantom Limb

  • Disconnect (Phantom Limb, UK) — collaboration with Kevin Martin (The Bug), billed as KRM & KMRU; mastered by Stefan Betke (Pole); artwork by Simon Fowler (3)
  • Natur (Touch, UK) — solo; produced, mixed, and written by KMRU; mastered by Simon Scott (INDEX (42)); photography and design by Jon Wozencroft
  • Temporary Stored II (OFNOT, Germany) — second OFNOT release; features credits by Aho Ssan, Bhavisha Panchia, Jessica Ekomane, Nyokabi Kariuki, and Lamin Fofana
  • forge (Seil Records, Germany) — third Seil album

2026

  • Kin (Editions Mego, Germany) — return to Editions Mego; produced, mixed, composed, and photographed by KMRU; mastered by Stephan Mathieu; design and layout by Nik Colk Void; track A2 co-produced and co-written by Fennesz
