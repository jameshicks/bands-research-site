Overview

Dale Morningstar is a Toronto-based recording engineer, producer, and multi-instrumentalist whose studio, The Gas Station, became a central node in the Toronto indie/underground scene from the early 1990s onward. As an engineer and producer he has worked across a remarkable range of Toronto and Canadian artists — from queercore and art-punk (Fifth Column, hHead) to post-rock (Godspeed You Black Emperor!), country-adjacent indie (Neko Case, Great Lake Swimmers, Thrush Hermit), and the broader CanRock world (Barenaked Ladies, the Tragically Hip/Gord Downie). He is also a performing musician — a member of The Dinner Is Ruined Band and The Country Of Miracles — and has one solo release from 1988 on D.A.M. Records (2).

Biography by Era

Early Work and the 1988 Solo Release

Morningstar’s earliest Discogs credit is his own self-titled debut (1988, D.A.M. Records (2)), on which he played guitar, sang, engineered, and produced. Session players included John Press (3) on bass and horns, Terry Pudealek on bass, and Stan Szymkow on horns. The release circulated as a cassette and later as a CD-R; it has 57 pressings/variant entries on Discogs, suggesting sustained bootleg/reissue attention.

By 1992 he was working as an engineer for hire. His first Discogs credits outside his own recordings are:

  • Change Of Heart, Smile (1992, Cargo Records) — Recorded By (alongside producer Michael Phillip Wojewoda)
  • The Dinner Is Ruined Band (his own band), Love Songs From The Lubritorium (1993, A&M/Raw Energy) — Producer, Recorded By, Layout, Words By

The Gas Station Years — Mid-1990s Peak

The mid-1990s represent the densest cluster of notable sessions, most run through The Gas Station:

  • Fifth Column, 36c (1994, K Records) — Engineer on tracks 2 and 9 (Walter Sobczak handled the other tracks)
  • hHead, Fireman (1994, self-released) — Engineer on tracks 1–9, alongside L. Stu Young; Brendan Canning on bass
  • The Inbreds (2), Kombinator (1994, Tag Recordings) — Engineer (alongside Lewis Melville; Dave Clark (5) produced)
  • The Dinner Is Ruined Band, Worm Pickers Brawl (1994, Gas Racket Records) — Producer, Engineer, Written-By (most tracks), Vocals
  • The Inbreds (2), Winning Hearts (1998, Murderecords) — Engineer (Dave Clark (5) produced)
  • Sackville, These Last Songs (1998, Mag Wheel Records) — Recorded By, Mixed By
  • Thrush Hermit, Clayton Park (1999, Sonic Unyon) — Recorded By, Mixed By (album features Joel Plaskett)

Two assistant engineering credits in the mid-1990s are notable for the company they place him in:

  • Barenaked Ladies, Born On A Pirate Ship (1996, Reprise) — Engineer (Assistant); principal engineer/producer was Michael Phillip Wojewoda
  • Veda Hille, Spine (1996, self-released) — Engineer (Additional)
  • Rheostatics (via Lay It Down), Lay It Down (1996, Geffen) — Recorded By (Additional)
  • Fresh Gasoline (by an unnamed act, Quarterstick, 1996) — Studio Assistance

Post-Rock and Cross-Country Sessions (1999–2003)

The highest-profile individual credit in the Discogs data is:

  • Godspeed You Black Emperor!, Slow Riot For New Zerø Kanada E.P. (1999, Kranky) — Recorded By [With], credited under the name “Saint Dale Morningstar.” The EP credits Godspeed themselves as primary recording entity, with Morningstar as co-recorder.

Other credits from this period:

  • Neko Case (as Neko Case & Her Boyfriends), Furnace Room Lullaby (2000, Bloodshot Records) — Recorded By [Bed Tracks] on tracks 2, 4, 8, 11; main engineer/producer was Darryl Neudorf
  • Yat-Kha, Aldyn Dashka (2000, Yat-Kha’s label, Europe) — Engineer; cross-cultural Tuvan throat-singing project
  • Gordon Downie (as Coke Machine Glow), Coke Machine Glow (2001, Wiener Art Records) — Songwriter credit (unusual; this may indicate a more direct collaboration)
  • Julie Doiron, Heart And Crime (2002, Jagjaguwar) — Recorded By, Mixed By (co-mixed with Julie Doiron)
  • Do Make Say Think, Battle Of The Nudes (2003, Zoë Records) — Producer

Band Memberships

The Dinner Is Ruined Band (Discogs ID: 1761017)

Members: Dale Morningstar, Dave Clark (5), Al Kelso. Also known as Dinner Is Ruined / The Dinner Is Ruined. Released at least two records: Love Songs From The Lubritorium (1993, A&M/Raw Energy) and Worm Pickers Brawl (1994, Gas Racket Records).

The Country Of Miracles (Discogs ID: 1677172)

Members: Atom Egoyan, Chris Walla, Dale Morningstar, Dave Clark (5), Gordon Downie, John Press (3), Josh Finlayson, Julie Doiron. The group backed Gordon Downie on The Grand Bounce (2010, Universal Music Canada). Chris Walla (of Death Cab for Cutie) produced and mixed that record.

Open Questions
