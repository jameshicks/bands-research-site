Nina Hagen

Discogs ID: 53800

German vocalist and punk-operatic performer. Adam Kidron produced a large portion of In Ekstasy (CBS, 1985, 38p) — tracks 1–3, 5–7, 9–11 — and the single “Universal Radio” (Columbia, 1985, 17p).
