Kenny Bobien

Real name: Kenneth O’Neal Bobien Discogs ID: 5363 Location: New Jersey / New York area Active: 1989 – present (Discogs credits span 1989–2024) Former spouse: Su Su Bobien (1990s); married Stephanie Cooke March 1999

US gospel-house vocalist. One of the most distinctive voices in American soulful and gospel house, Bobien built his career from the mid-1990s onward across a tightly connected network of Baltimore and New Jersey-area labels — Soundmen On Wax, Basement Boys Records, BPM King Street Sounds, and Strictly Rhythm — while also maintaining a long session and backing-vocal career in R&B and pop that predates his solo house output by several years. His 1998 recording Let Me Show You (Soundmen On Wax), produced by B.O.P. and remixed by DJ Spen & Karizma, is regarded as a landmark of gospel-influenced house music, deploying Gary Hudgins on piano, organ and synth with Irvin Madden on bass alongside DJ Spen & Karizma on drums and remixes. The 1999 Soundmen On Wax CD Blessed (A Gospel Dance Theory) compiles his key early Soundmen On Wax tracks alongside productions from Big Moses, Kerri Chandler, Jovonn, and B.O.P.. On Discogs his name appears under numerous variants including K. Bobien, Kenneth O’Neal Bobien, and K.Bobien (likely typos and regional pressing variations).

Early Career — Session and Backing Vocal Work (1989–1995)

Before his solo house output, Bobien was active as a session vocalist, backing singer, and occasional songwriter across R&B and dance:

  • Stephanie Mills — A Girl Like Me (MCA / 4th & Broadway, 1989) — backing vocals
  • Side Effect — Always There (Capitol Records, 1989) — backing vocals; this is the track later sampled and reworked as a house classic
  • Mantronix — Foundation (Atlantic, 1989) — backing vocals (Europe release)
  • Def Con 4 — Def Con 4 (Reprise Records, 1990) — backing vocals and lead vocals; this is one of his own group projects (he is a listed Discogs member of Def Con 4, alongside other group credits)
  • Movin’ Records releases (Newark, NJ) — backing and lead vocals on two releases: Nowhere To Run (1989) and Rejoice (1992); Movin’ Records is a foundational New Jersey sound label connected to the Club Zanzibar scene in Newark
  • Barbara Tucker — Beautiful People (Positiva, 1994, UK) — backing vocals on the house classic; 16 pressings on Discogs
  • Barbara Tucker — I Get Lifted (Positiva, 1994, UK) — backing vocals; 9 pressings
  • various R&B and soul sessions 1991–1995 across Atlantic, Arista, SBK Records, and independent labels

Also credited as a songwriter on early releases including I’ll Do Anything (SBK Records, 1989) and Still Waiting (Profile Records, 1989).

Solo Emergence — Shelter Records and King Street Sounds (1996–1998)

Bobien’s solo house career launched in 1996 with the B.O.P.-produced Stand Up on Shelter Records (3), co-written and co-produced with Paul Scott and Shank Thompson; Su Su Bobien (his then-wife) and Eddie Stockley contribute backing vocals. Executive producer: Freddy Sanon.

From 1997 he recorded for BPM King Street Sounds (an imprint of King Street Sounds), beginning with Brighter Days (Remix) (1997), a prominent co-credit with Big Moses and Moise Laporte. Engineered by Rob Rives and Phil Pagano; mastered by Rick Essig; executive-produced by Hisa Ishioka (Japanese A&R). Several King Street Sounds releases followed:

  • Back To Life (1998, BPM King Street Sounds)
  • Walk Into The Light (1998, BPM King Street Sounds) — written by Bobien
  • Love’s Been Right Here (2000, BPM King Street Sounds) — written and co-produced by Bobien

In 1998 he was also produced by Masters At Work (Louie Vega and Kenny “Dope” Gonzalez) for Rise Above The Storm on MAW Records — the MAW imprint on which Masters At Work released artist productions; written by Bobien, Vega, and C. Gonzalez.

Soundmen On Wax Era (1998–2005)

The central chapter of Bobien’s discography is his sustained relationship with Soundmen On Wax (Discogs label ID: 1152; parent: Movement Factory LLC; 155 releases), the New Jersey-area label run by Big Moses (Moise Laporte) and Shuji Hirose.

Let Me Show You (1998)

Release ID: 71600. The landmark single. Produced by B.O.P. (credited as B.O.P. Entertainment). Personnel:

  • Gary Hudgins — piano, organ, synth (credited as “Gary Hudge”)
  • Irvin Madden — bass (credited as “Irvine Madden”)
  • DJ Spen & Karizma — drums; also remix (entire A, B1, B2, C1, C2 sides)
  • Wayne Cooper — guitar

The DJ Spen & Karizma remix made this a fixture in gospel-house DJing. The Discogs master (ID 0 — single-pressing entry) has 30 pressings logged across the catalog.

I Can’t Give You Anything / Blessed (1999)

US 12” (release ID 4340). Co-produced by Big Moses and Bobien. Engineer: Moise Laporte. Written by Bobien and Moise Laporte. Part of the same Soundmen On Wax run.

Father (The Frankie Feliciano Remixes) (2000)

Release ID 71603. Bobien as vocalist, writer, and producer. Frankie Feliciano provides the “Ricanstructed” remix and mix. Additional credits: John Kevin Jones (guitar), Jimmy Clissett (harmonica on B and C2), Albert “Sterling” Menendez (additional keyboards). Executive producer: Shuji Hirose.

Superficial People (The Shelter Remixes) (2001)

Release ID 23209. Bobien as primary artist with background vocals, production, and lead vocals. Additional credits include Byron Burke, Byron Stingily, Mark Ingram, Karian Taylor, Timmy Regisford, Orbert C. Davis, all associated with the Shelter Records circle — the Chicago soul-house axis.

Dance For Life - The Remixes (2001)

Release ID 53724. Bobien as producer and vocalist; Big Moses as producer; Moise Laporte as engineer. A&R by Lancelot (7) and Shuji Hirose.

Set Them Free (Remixes) (2005)

Release ID 690679. Bobien as backing vocals, album version producer, and lead vocals. Last major Soundmen On Wax credit.

Basement Boys Records (1999–2001)

Bobien released two singles on Basement Boys Records (BBR, the Baltimore label run by DJ Spen and Teddy Douglas):

  • U Gave Me Love (1999, release ID 23419) — produced and mixed by Jay Steinhour and Teddy Douglas; Irvin Madden on bass; written by Bobien, Steinhour, and Douglas; engineered by Jim “Bonzai” Caruso
  • A Feelin / You Gave Me Love (2001, release ID 22342) — reissue / compilation of the above material

How I Feel and the Basement Boys Production (2001)

How I Feel (Purple Music, Switzerland, 2001; release ID 153347) — co-produced by Bobien and The Basement Boys; Gary Hudgins on keyboards; Irvin Madden on bass; DJ Spen & Karizma on additional drum programming; mix engineered by Jim “Bonzai” Caruso; written by Bobien and Teddy Douglas; executive producer Shuji Hirose. Demonstrates how the Soundmen On Wax, Basement Boys, and King Street Sounds production circles overlapped directly.

Goin Up Yonder and Gospel Standard Recordings (1997)

Goin Up Yonder (1997, Equip Records, release ID 63716; 32 pressings on Discogs). Produced by George Sykes and Christina Hayes. Bobien handles both lead and backing vocals alongside Eddie Stockley and Deneen Jones. Written by Walter Hawkins (original lyrics). The Walter Hawkins gospel standard is recontextualized here as a house track, an approach central to Bobien’s work.

I Shall Not Be Moved / Underground Ministries (1999)

I Shall Not Be Moved (1999, Flatline Records (2), release ID 62239) — credited to Underground Ministries featuring Kenny Bobien. Produced by Holland Tunnel Project and James Perry; executive produced by Backroom Productions. Multiple remix pressings with additional credits including Carlin Taylor, Keisha Hall, Edwin Lugo, Harold Martin Jnr., Kevin Stancill, and Thomas Davis.

It Feels Good — Strictly Rhythm (2002)

It Feels Good (2002, Strictly Rhythm, release ID 71438) — credited to Team Factor featuring Kenny Bobien. Produced by the Italian house duo Pastaboys. Written by Duane Harden, Cristiano Rinaldi, Davide Santandrea, and Vincent Montana Jr. Duane Harden provides the lyrics. Eight pressings logged — one of Bobien’s most-pressed Discogs entries outside King Street Sounds.

Research Queue

  • The brief describes Let Me Show You as “Baltimore gospel-house” — the release is on Soundmen On Wax, a New Jersey-area label, while the producer B.O.P. and the DJ Spen & Karizma team are Baltimore-based. Confirm whether the recording was made in Baltimore or New Jersey, and which scene claimed it.
  • I Shall Not Be Moved credits a producer “Dwayne ‘Spen’ Richardson” on the Blessed CD track “If You Love Somebody Set Them Free” — confirm whether this is the same person as DJ Spen (Sean Spencer) or a different person sharing the “Spen” nickname.
  • Bobien married Stephanie Cooke in March 1999 per Discogs profile — confirm whether this is the same Stephanie Cooke who appears as a collaborator on Soundmen On Wax/BBR releases (Discogs ID 4179, 8 shared releases).
