Stephen Morris

Drummer, percussionist, programmer, synthesizer. Born 28 October 1957, Macclesfield, Cheshire. Discogs ID: 33927. Real name: Stephen Paul David Morris.

Founding member of Joy Division (drums, percussion) and New Order (drums, keyboards, programming). One of the core architects of post-punk rhythm: his drumming on Joy Division’s records — partly shaped by Martin Hannett’s studio treatments — established a template of controlled, martial precision that influenced a generation. With New Order he integrated live drums with machines and synthesizers, central to the band’s evolution from post-punk into the electronic/dance crossover of the 1980s and beyond.

Married to Gillian Gilbert; together they formed The Other Two, a side project releasing on Factory Records and Factory Too in the 1990s–2000s.

Note: Stephen Morris is not a member of Freebass (9) — that project (2010–2017) featured Peter Hook, Andy Rourke, and Gary Mounfield (Mani). The association may be a confusion with Hook’s post-New Order activities.

Biography by Era

Pre-Joy Division: Warsaw / Stiff Kittens (1977–1978)

Morris was recruited by Ian Curtis, Bernard Sumner, and Peter Hook into what would become Joy Division, then performing as Warsaw (later also listed as Stiff Kittens). The An Ideal For Living EP (1978, Enigma) was their debut release — Morris credited on drums. Warsaw-era material is also documented on the posthumous collection Warsaw (1983, Stiff Kittens label, Australia) and various bootleg/archival releases.

Joy Division (1978–1980)

Recorded two studio albums for Factory Records:

  • Unknown Pleasures (1979) — produced by Martin Hannett
  • Closer (1980) — produced by Martin Hannett

Plus key singles: “Transmission,” “She’s Lost Control,” “Love Will Tear Us Apart,” “Atmosphere,” “Ceremony.” Morris and the band are co-writers on all studio material. Peel Sessions (BBC) recorded in 1979 and 1980, released on Strange Fruit from 1986 onward.

Ian Curtis died 18 May 1980. The surviving three — Morris, Sumner, Hook — continued as New Order, with Gillian Gilbert added on keyboards.

Singles with writing credits include “Blue Monday,” “Confusion,” “Thieves Like Us,” “The Perfect Kiss,” “Bizarre Love Triangle,” “True Faith,” “Regret,” “Crystal,” “Krafty,” “Restless,” “Be A Rebel.”

Morris produced or co-produced several New Order releases uncredited; Discogs credits him explicitly as producer on some singles (e.g., “Too Crazy Cowboys” 1984, Shellshock 2023 reissue).

New Order split from Peter Hook acrimoniously around 2007; Hook did not participate in Music Complete (2015) or subsequent releases. Tom Chapman (Discogs ID: 719377) joined as bassist.
