Based: California, USA
URL: theparlourtrick.com

California-based violinist, vocalist, and thereminist, also active as a composer, arranger, and sound designer. Yayanos has operated across an unusually wide stylistic range — from experimental cabaret and dark folk to black metal — appearing as a session player on indie and post-punk recordings in the early 2000s and later as a core member and co-composer in her own ongoing projects. She is perhaps best known under the alias The Harpy and as co-founder of The Parlour Trick alongside Dan Cantrell. Her additional instrument — the Stroh violin, an amplifying-horn variant rarely seen in contemporary recording — appears on several of her Feminazgul credits.

The Parlour Trick

Duo project with pianist/accordionist Dan Cantrell. The project’s website is theparlourtrick.com. Two releases documented on Discogs:

  • A Blessed Unrest (2012) — Both Yayanos and Cantrell credited as composers, arrangers, and multi-instrument performers across all tracks. Engineer: Dan Rathbun (tracks 2–6, 8–11); Mastered By: Myles Boisen.
  • Wandering Room (2016) — No additional personnel listed in Discogs.

Revue Noir

Dark cabaret ensemble on Projekt also featuring Sam Rosenthal (producer/engineer), Nicki Jaine (vocals, piano, guitar), and Tim Karsten (drums). Yayanos credited with violin, theremin, and string arrangement on both releases.

AEAEA

All-female experimental collective. Members: Jarboe, Annie Hogan, Kris Force, Meredith Yayanos, Zoë Keating. One studio release documented on Discogs: Drink The New Wine (2014, Twilight Records (2), Argentina). Produced and mixed by Kris Force.

Feminazgul

Asheville, North Carolina black metal band. Primary member: Margaret Killjoy. Current line-up also includes Laura Beach (2) (lead vocals) and Meredith Yayanos. Yayanos plays violin, Stroh violin, theremin, and contributes vocals and arrangement across all releases. The alias “The Harpy” appears in the 2021 split credit (“Harpy Choir”).
