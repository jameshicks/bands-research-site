Charlie McIntosh

Discogs ID: 286932
Name variations: Charles McIntosh, C. McIntosh, C. McIntosh Esq.

Producer and engineer. The in-house engineer/producer for ché Records and its sister label Cheree Records throughout the early-to-mid 1990s. His career is effectively a map of the ché label’s output. No biographical information is documented on Discogs.

The Disco Inferno relationship is the most sustained: McIntosh was their primary producer from the debut 7” in 1991 through D. I. Go Pop in 1994 — the entire ché and first three Rough Trade releases. On The Last Dance EP (1993) he split duties with Michael Johnson (who produced the A1/B1 tracks); McIntosh took A2/B2.

Animals That Swim (ché CHÉ 5, 1993) — indie rock band also on ché; 50 Dresses is their second single.

Urusei Yatsura — Scottish noise-pop band who were also on ché and eventually signed to Sire Records (US major). McIntosh recorded only track 3 (“Sucker”) on the Kewpies Like Watermelon EP; John A. Rivers handled the other three.
