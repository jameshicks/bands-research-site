King Dude

Solo project of Thomas Jefferson Cowgill (also known as TJ Cowgill). Discogs artist ID: 1759378.

Released Tonight’s Special Death (CDR033, 2010) on Disaro — a self-recorded and self-mixed CDr; track 6 uses lyrics by Robert Frost.

Released Who Taught You How To Love (2016, Dais Records) — a co-credited split 7” with Drab Majesty. Both co-wrote both sides (Cowgill: lyrics; Drab Majesty: lyrics and music). Also on Dais Records catalog (14 releases, 2011–2016).
