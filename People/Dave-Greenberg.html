Overview

Dave Greenberg is a New York-based producer and engineer, and the owner of Rockin’ Reel Recording Studios. With over 1,400 Discogs credits spanning Pop Rock, Classic Rock, Synth-pop, Disco, and Soul, he became a central figure in the Dire Straits recording circle: his most frequent collaborators include Mark Knopfler, Neil Dorfsman, Guy Fletcher, John Illsley, Terry Williams, and Alan Clark, alongside Bob Clearmountain and Nile Rodgers. His credits run across major labels — RCA, Columbia, Def Jam, EMI, and Mercury — reflecting broad work across mainstream rock, pop, and R&B from the early 1980s onward.
