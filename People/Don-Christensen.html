Don Christensen

Drummer, musician, recording engineer, executive producer, painter. Born 1948, Nebraska. New York City. Now based in (reportedly) New York. Discogs IDs: 291051 (Don Christensen), 222300 (Implog alias).

Solo alias: Implog (also written impLOG). Primary musical identity 1978–1982 was as drummer in the core of the NYC No Wave scene. From the mid-1980s onward, became the in-house engineer and executive producer for Philip Glass and Orange Mountain Music. Now works as a painter.

Biography

New York / No Wave (1978–1983)

Christensen arrived in the NYC downtown scene in the late 1970s, becoming the drummer in The Contortions — James Chance’s abrasive funk-punk outfit, one of the four bands documented on Brian Eno’s landmark No New York compilation (1978, Antilles). He appears on the Contortions’ tracks on that record, and on their debut album Buy (1979, ZE Records). He was simultaneously in James White & The Blacks, the Contortions’ sister project under the James Siegfried / James Chance name.

The same tight social circle included Jody Harris (guitar) and George Scott (2) (bass), both of whom would die young (Scott died of a heroin overdose in 1980). Christensen, Harris, Scott, and Pat Irwin formed The Raybeats in 1979 — an instrumental neo-surf combo, moving away from No Wave’s abrasion toward cleaner melodic territory. Scott’s death led to lineup changes; the Raybeats continued without him.

Implog (1980–1982)

Christensen’s solo project, operated under the alias impLOG. Released only two records:

  1. “Holland Tunnel Dive” 12” (1980, Infidelity (2) / Lust-Unlust Music, JMB-231) — Two tracks. Side A (“Holland Tunnel Dive”) features tenor saxophone by Paul Colin. Side B (“On B’Way”) features guest guitar solo by Jody Harris and backing vocals by Danny And The Notones; “On B’Way” is a rearrangement of a Mann/Weil/Leiber & Stoller song. Christensen produced the record himself; engineered by Greg Curry. Matrix runout reads “More Noise From Lust/Unlust.”

  2. “Breakfast / She Creatures” 7” (1982, Log Records, F/W 21519) — Two tracks, both written and performed entirely by Christensen. Co-produced with Dan Dryden. Sparse, no guest musicians.

After this second release, Implog went dormant for over two decades. Christensen reactivated the Implog alias briefly in 2005 to contribute a remix for another artist.

Engineering and Philip Glass (1984–present)

By the mid-1980s Christensen had transitioned to studio engineering. His connection to Philip Glass’s organization became his primary professional identity for decades:

  • Engineer on Glass’s Songs From Liquid Days (1986, CBS) — notable for vocalist collaborations with Linda Ronstadt, Suzanne Vega, Paul Simon
  • Engineer on Akhnaten (1987, CBS) — credited “Engineered By [NYC]”
  • Engineer + drums on Dance Pieces (1987, CBS)
  • Engineer on Powaqqatsi (1988, Nonesuch)
  • Engineer + various instruments on It’s Only A Movie (1983, Shanachie) — drums, drum machine, piano, synthesizer, fuzz guitar, bells, slit drum, marching drum, cowbell, and more
  • Co-produced a Bush Tetras session: Too Many Creeps (1980, 99 Records) — pre-Glass, from his No Wave years
  • From the late 1990s: executive producer and producer at Orange Mountain Music (Glass’s own label) for dozens of releases including Koyaanisqatsi (2009 reissue), The Fog Of War soundtrack (2003), Book Of Longing (2007), and ongoing Glass catalog releases through 2024

The Lost Philip Glass Sessions (2013, Orange Mountain Music) credits him for drums, percussion, vocals, front cover design, and written-by — indicating musical authorship, not just engineering.

Later Work

  • Christmas Evil (You Better Watch Out) (2014, Death Waltz Recording Company) — film soundtrack, primary credit
  • Donnie And Laurie / Patchwork (2018, Unseen Worlds) — primary credit; connection unclear
  • Rhythm And Paranoia: The Best Of Bush Tetras (2021, Wharf Cat Records) — producer; returning to his No Wave roots
  • I Really Want To Bomb You: 1972–1984 (2022, Modern Harmonic) — drums; likely a No Wave compilation
  • Duka 1 1979–1988 (2024, Modern Harmonic) — bass, drums, electronics, percussion, written-by
