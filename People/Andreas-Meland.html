Andreas Meland

Norwegian music executive, label manager, and musician. Born 1979. Discogs artist ID: 97328. Real name: Andreas Risanger Meland. Credited variously as “Andreas R. Meland”, “Andreas Risanger Meland”, and “Meland”.

Staff at Grappa Musikkforlag AS (Norway’s leading independent music group); holds responsibility for Hubro, Grappa’s contemporary jazz and experimental sublabel. Previously ran the Safe As Milk label. Also credited on releases from Odin, another Grappa sublabel. Active in several Norwegian noise/experimental music groups as a performer.

Career Overview

Meland began his career as a musician and label operator — he co-founded or ran Safe As Milk (a small Norwegian label active from the late 1990s), where he also performed as part of the group Düplo. He released a mastering credit as early as 2002 on Waffel Records.

By 2011 his executive-producer credits appear on Hubro releases, marking his transition into his primary role: overseeing the artistic output of Hubro and adjacent Grappa sublabels. His first Hubro executive-producer credit is 1982 (2)‘s debut Pintura (2011). From that point through at least 2021 he is present on virtually every significant Hubro release in an executive-producer, producer, or co-producer role — a span of over a decade.

He is also active as a musician. His performing groups include:

  • Düplo (Discogs: 15379) — with Gaute Sortland and Jon Øvstedal; their 2000 album Slingringsmonn was released on Safe As Milk
  • Bokfink (Discogs: 693486) — with Dag-Are Haugan
  • Røyskatt (Discogs: 727058) — with Sten Ove Toft (real names credited as “Sten Ove Toft, Andreas Meland”)
  • Sort Mel (Discogs: 501436)
  • Norwegian Noise Orchestra (Discogs: 284789) — large-ensemble noise collective

On the 2021 Nils Økland album Glødetrådar (Hubro), Meland is credited both as producer and as a vocalist (track 7).
