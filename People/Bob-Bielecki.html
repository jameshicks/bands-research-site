Overview

Bob Bielecki is a sound designer and audio engineer whose practice is rooted in the experimental use of technology within electronic arts and performance. Beginning in the mid-1970s, he forged a long-running collaboration with Laurie Anderson — his most frequent partner across 145 shared credits — contributing to the design of custom instruments and interfaces used in her performances and installations, while also working with La Monte Young during the same period. His 235-credit catalogue spans experimental, avant-garde, minimal, and modern classical styles across labels including Giorno Poetry Systems, Warner Bros., and Gramavision, with Leanne Ungar and Roma Baran among his next most frequent collaborators. Since 1997 he has been Visiting Assistant Professor of Music at Bard College’s Milton Avery Graduate School of the Arts.
