Gunnar Örn Tynes

Discogs ID: 624414 Born: 5 March 1979, Iceland Name variations: G. Tynes, Gunnar, Gunnar Orn Tynes, Gunnar Tynes, Gunnari Erni Tynes, Tynes

Icelandic guitarist, songwriter, producer, and engineer. One of the four founding members of múm (1997), alongside twin sisters Kristín Anna Valtýsdóttir and Gyða Valtýsdóttir and Örvar Þóreyjarson Smárason. Also a founding member of Andhéri (with Örvar), which released the cassette Fallegir Ósigrar on Smekkleysa in 1997. Beyond múm, Tynes has worked extensively as a producer, mixer, and engineer across the Icelandic and international indie-experimental scenes, with credits ranging from Benni Hemm Hemm (2005) to Ólöf Arnalds (2014) and major film scores for Hildur Guðnadóttir (Chernobyl, Joker). He is associated with the Reykjavík label/venue Mengi.
