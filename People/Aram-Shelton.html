Aram Shelton

American saxophonist and composer, born 14 September 1976. Based in Chicago (and later the San Francisco Bay Area), Shelton is a central figure in the post-AACM free-jazz and creative-music scenes across both coasts. He plays alto saxophone and bass clarinet as his primary instruments, and also handles mixing and production on many of his own releases. Discogs artist ID: 525577.

Biography

Shelton’s recorded work begins around the early 2000s in Chicago, where he moved in overlapping circles with the new wave of improvisers around Locust Music, Delmark Records, and a loose constellation of small labels. His earliest traceable Discogs credit is a 1997 saxophone/keyboards contribution to a No Idea Records release, but his profile solidifies with the Chicago free-jazz scene of 2001–2006.

He was a founding member of Dragons 1976 (Discogs ID: 1954092), a trio with Jason Ajemian (bass) and Tim Daisy (drums). Their sole album On Cortez (2004, Locust Music) is the band’s central document: Shelton on alto, Ajemian on bass (and photography), Daisy on drums, recorded by Devin Davis.

Around the same period, Shelton participated in Who Cares How Long You Sink (Discogs ID: 6669870), the large experimental folk/freeform jazz ensemble founded by Jason Ajemian in 2001. The group operated with a rotating cast of 30+ musicians devoted to slow, tide-like musical motion — material played to inhale/exhale pacing rather than an external clock. Their self-titled debut appeared on Lucky Kitchen in 2004; Shelton played alto saxophone alongside, among others, Jeff Parker, Kyle Bruckmann, Tim Daisy, Matt Bauder, Ernst Karel, and Josh Berman.

He was also a member of Grey Ghost (Discogs ID: 394835), a Chicago group that released a self-titled recording in 2002.

By the mid-2000s, Shelton was a regular presence in the Fast Citizens family of bands — groups organized around the “Fast Citizens” name by different leader/composers. He appears on Keefe Jackson’s Fast Citizens debut Ready Everyday (2006, Delmark Records), with Anton Hatwich, Fred Lonberg-Holm, Josh Berman, Frank Rosaly, produced by Robert G. Koester.

The quartet Arrive (Discogs ID: 2360679) — Shelton, Jason Adasiewicz (vibraphone), Jason Roebke (bass), Tim Daisy (drums) — released Live At Elastic (2008, Singlespeed Music), with compositions credited to Shelton.

Shelton also appears on two albums under Jason Adasiewicz’s Rolldown project (Discogs ID: 3939070): Rolldown (2008, 482 Music) and Varmint (2009, Cuneiform Records), both with the same quintet core of Adasiewicz/Shelton/Roebke/Berman/Frank Rosaly.

He led his own ensembles on two Delmark releases: Aram Shelton’s Fast Citizens (Discogs ID: 3796601) released Two Cities in 2009, featuring Anton Hatwich, Fred Lonberg-Holm, Josh Berman, Frank Rosaly, and Keefe Jackson, produced by Robert G. Koester; Aram Shelton Quartet (Discogs ID: 3310706) released These Times in 2010, with Hatwich, Marc Riordan, and Keefe Jackson.

His West Coast connections deepened with the Cylinder project (Discogs ID: 562901), a co-led quartet with Darren Johnston (trumpet), Lisa Mezzacappa (bass), and Kjell Nordeson (drums/percussion). Cylinder (2011, Clean Feed) credits Shelton as alto sax, clarinet, bass clarinet, and mixer. The same Shelton/Nordeson pairing also appears on Aram Shelton Quartet’s Continental Drift (2020, Clean Feed), with Larry Ochs (tenor/sopranino sax), Mark Dresser and Scott Walton sharing bass duties, and Nordeson on drums.

His trio projects include the Ton Trio series: Ton Trio (Discogs ID: 3795427) released The Way (2009, Singlespeed Music), Shelton handling composition, engineering, mixing, and mastering alongside Kurt Kotheimer and Sam Ospovat; Ton Trio II (Discogs ID: 3686993) released On And On (2014, Singlespeed Music), with Scott Brown (bass) and Alex Vittum (drums).

Steel Bridge Trio (Discogs ID: 4572347) released Different Clocks (2015, Relay Recordings), co-led with Tim Daisy (drums/vibraphone/composer) and Safa Shokrai (bass). Gold Age (Discogs ID: 5177812) released a self-titled LP in 2016 (Singlespeed Music), with Shokrai, Britt Ciampa, and Mark Clifford (3).

The Dirty Snacks Ensemble (Discogs ID: 5813761) released Tidy Universe in 2016 (Recollect Records), an ensemble credit featuring Shelton on alto and bass clarinet.

Shelton’s most recent primary-credited release in the database is Multiring (2020, Astral Spirits), a duo album with Danish-based musician Christian Rønn (Würlitzer piano, effects). Both artists co-wrote and co-mixed; Rønn also mastered.

A notable one-off credit: Shelton appears as an “Additional Musician” on Bonnie ‘Prince’ Billy and Matt Sweeney’s The Brave And The Bold (2006, Domino Records).

He has also been active in the Bay Area avant scene through Broken Trap Ensemble, Flockterkit, Wiener Kids, Kyle Bruckmann’s Degradient, and other projects, as well as the video-game music world via the Valve Studio Orchestra on Fight Songs: The Music Of Team Fortress 2 (2017, Ipecac Recordings / Valve).
