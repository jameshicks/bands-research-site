Mark Doffman

Drummer and percussionist. Discogs artist ID: 360280. Active in post-punk and experimental music from the early 1980s through at least 2015.

Overview

Mark Doffman is primarily documented as a drummer and percussionist across several projects in the UK post-punk and experimental music scenes. He first appears on record with Family Fodder on their 1980 debut Monkey Banana Kitchen, and remained associated with the group across multiple releases spanning more than three decades. Beyond Family Fodder, Doffman contributed drums to the French-based jazz/world collective Non Stop Cargo (1991), the UK experimental ensemble Metalworks (1994), and British bass-baritone Willard White’s The Paul Robeson Legacy (2002). He also plays metallophone alongside drums, and in the Sunday Girls (Director’s Cut) recording (2015) adds vocals and vibraphone to his credited instruments.

No realname, aliases, or group memberships are recorded in the Discogs artist entry. His name variations on the database are “Doffman” and “Mark” (sleeve abbreviations).

Outside Family Fodder

Non Stop Cargo — Holy War (1991)

  • Label: Integral Songs (France)
  • Doffman plays drums on this LP alongside an ensemble including Thierry Jaquelin (alto/soprano sax, flute), Max Cornelly (bass), Djon Mota (guitar), Solan (guitar, vocals), Izzy Postill (keyboards, backing vocals), Djanuno Dabo (percussion, vocals), Adam Pretty (tenor/soprano sax), Guillermo Fellove (trumpet), and Catherine Macé (vocals, tenor/alto sax).
  • Non Stop Cargo appears to be a French-based multi-instrumental collective with a world/jazz orientation. Discogs ID: 4362611.

Metalworks — Parrot Soup (1994)

  • Label: KEDA Records (UK)
  • Doffman plays metallophone and drums on all 13 tracks of this LP. Metalworks is an experimental ensemble whose core membership includes Jan Steele (metallophone, soprano sax, recorder), Janet Sherbourne (metallophone, voice, drums), and Mark Lockett (metallophone, voice, drums). Also on the record: Elizabeth Acons, Luke Steele (2), Nick Gray (4), Rachel Hewitt (metallophone, voice), and Kuljit Bhamra (tabla, tambourine). Co-produced by Janet Sherbourne and Mark Lockett.
  • Metalworks Discogs ID: 1782688.

Willard White — The Paul Robeson Legacy (2002)

  • Label: Linn Records (UK)
  • Doffman plays drums on this tribute to Paul Robeson by British bass-baritone Sir Willard White. The session band includes Geoff Gascoyne (bass), Richard Bolton (guitar), Neal Thornton (piano, keyboards, percussion), and Guy Barker (trumpet).
  • Willard White Discogs ID: 798940.
