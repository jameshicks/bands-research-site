Anders Remmer

Danish electronic musician and producer. Discogs ID: 114164.

Overview

Anders Remmer is one-third of Future 3 (with Thomas Knak and Jesper Skaaning). The same trio also operates as D.A.W.N. (Discogs ID: 11657) and System (Discogs ID: 9977). His primary solo alias is Dub Tractor (Discogs ID: 2491), active since the mid-1990s. Other aliases include Cph Jet, Jet, and Sølvpil.

Name variations: A. Remmer, A.Remmer, Remmer.
