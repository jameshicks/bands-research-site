Steve Roach

American ambient/electronic musician. Discogs artist ID: 12974. Based in Arizona. The most prolific artist on Projekt Records (282 primary-credited releases, 1995–2026).

Overview

Pioneering figure in American ambient and electronic music since the early 1980s. Roach’s foundational works include Structures From Silence (1984) and Dreamtime Return (1988), both of which were later reissued on Projekt (PROJEKT119 and PROJEKT175 respectively). He joined the Projekt roster with The Lost Pieces (1995, PROJEKT 13) and Well Of Souls (1995, PROJEKT 60 — the first of multiple collaborative albums with Vidna Obmana / Dirk Serries).

His output on Projekt has been extraordinary in volume: solo albums, collaborative albums, live recordings, and multi-disc box sets. He also operates his own Timeroom Editions imprint for some releases. Key collaborative partners on Projekt include:

  • Vidna Obmana / Dirk Serries — Well Of Souls (1995), Cavern Of Sirens (1997), Ascension Of Shadows (1999), InnerZone (2002), Spirit Dome – Live Archive (2009)
  • Byron Metcalf — multiple shamanic percussion collaborative albums
  • Erik Wøllo — Stream Of Thought (2009), Nightbloom (2010), The Road Eternal (2011)
  • Mark Seelig — flute-based collaborative works
  • Vir Unis — Body Electric (1999)
  • Roger King — Dust To Dust (1998)
  • Jeffrey Fayman / Momodou Kah / Robert Fripp — Trance Spirits (2002)
  • Michael Stearns — Beyond Earth & Sky (2021)

Also released on Klanggalerie and other labels.
