Ollie Olsen

Discogs ID: 104770

Australian musician, songwriter, and electronic artist; co-founder of Young Charlatans with Rowland S. Howard and Jeffrey Wegener (1977–1978).

Key figure in Melbourne post-punk and later electronic music. After Young Charlatans, moved into experimental and industrial electronic work. The Young Charlatans’ 1978 recordings were officially released as 1978 (Eminent Vinyl, 2026).
