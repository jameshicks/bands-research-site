Terrie Ex

Discogs IDs: 254022 (as Terrie Ex), 1403136 (as Terrie Hessels) Real name: Terrie Hessels Active: 1979–present Dutch guitarist; founding member of The Ex (1979–present), duo partner of Ab Baars and Han Bennink, member of free improv quartet Lean Left.

Overview

Terrie Ex (real name Terrie Hessels) is a founding guitarist of the Dutch post-punk and experimental band The Ex, active continuously from the band’s formation in Amsterdam in 1979. He has appeared on every major Ex release across more than four decades, playing a central role in the band’s evolution from anarcho-punk toward free improvisation, post-rock, and African music crossovers.

Alongside The Ex, Terrie has pursued an extensive career in free improvisation. His most prominent outside unit is Lean Left, a free-improv quartet founded around 2010 with Ex bandmate Andy Moor (2), Chicago reed player Ken Vandermark, and Norwegian drummer Paal Nilssen-Love. Lean Left has released eight albums across Smalltown Superjazzz, Kollaps, Unsounds, Trost Records, and PNL, from The Ex Guitars Meet Nilssen-Love/Vandermark Duo Volume 1 (2010) through Medemer (2020).

His solo and duo catalog appears on Terp Records, the label he co-runs from Amsterdam. Key duo partnerships include: Ab Baars (three albums: Hef 2000, Shifting Sands 2016, and a release listed under the Terrie, Andy & Friends unit), Han Bennink (The Laughing Owl, 2000), Jaap Blonk (Thirsty Ears, 2018), Ken Vandermark (Scaffolding, 2019), and Kaja Draksler (The Swim, 2020).

Terrie also contributed to Double Tandem, a quartet with Ab Baars, Ken Vandermark, and Paal Nilssen-Love — notably absent from the Cement (2012, PNL) recording credits as a primary artist, though the project otherwise overlaps with the Lean Left personnel. He appears on Baro 101 (Terp Records, 2012), the Terp Ethiopian series release with Mats Gustafsson, Paal Nilssen-Love, and Mesele Asmamaw, credited as liner notes author.

He was also a guitarist on Original Silence’s The Second Original Silence (Smalltown Superjazzz, 2008), a large free-improv ensemble including Thurston Moore, Jim O’Rourke, Mats Gustafsson, Paal Nilssen-Love, and Massimo Pupillo.

On record Terrie is credited under multiple name variants: Terrie, Terrie (T), Terry Ex, Terri, Terrie Ex, Terrie Hessels.

Terp Records (Improv. Series)

Terrie operates Terp Records from Amsterdam. The label has 64 releases on Discogs and runs three sublabels: African Series (AS), Improv. Series (IS), and Other Series (OS). His own duo and group recordings occupy the IS catalog alongside releases by Lena Hessels and others. Key IS releases:
