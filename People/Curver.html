Curver

Discogs ID: 61291 Real name: Birgir Örn Thoroddsen Origin: Iceland

Icelandic musician, producer, engineer, and visual artist. One half of Ghostigital with Einar Örn Benediktsson. Website: glatkistan.com

Active in the Reykjavík experimental scene from the early 1990s alongside acts including Stilluppsteypa. Released a debut album Haf on Smekkleysa (1994). source A 2005 Grapevine retrospective on his compilation Sær 1991–1994 names Stilluppsteypa as contemporaries in the same pre-commercial Reykjavík experimental wave. source
