Tolan McNeil

Canadian multi-instrumentalist, songwriter, recording engineer, and producer based in British Columbia. Discogs artist ID: 634751. Active from approximately 1987 to 2022. A central figure in the Vancouver/BC indie and alternative-country scene, McNeil wears multiple hats: guitarist and vocalist in several bands, steel guitar/lap steel session player, independent recording engineer, and occasional producer. He is most closely associated with Stephen McBean (Black Mountain, Pink Mountaintops) through shared early bands, and with Carolyn Mark for whom he served as a key collaborator and producer. He also carried out occasional mastering work for the indie underground (Soft Abuse, Absolutely Kosher) in the mid-2000s. Known aliases/ANVs include “Milky T McNeil”, “Tolan ‘Bunky’ McNeil”, “Tolan ‘Snot Finger’ McNeil”, and “T.M.”

Overview

Punk and Hardcore Roots (1987–1990)

McNeil entered the Canadian underground as a member of Mission Of Christ, the Saskatoon/BC speed-metal-inflected hardcore band that also included Stephen McBean. The band released a split 7” on Final Notice Records / Synthetic Beliefs in 1987 (Mission Of Christ / Fratricide, catno FN001/SB001). McNeil then played guitar and vocals in Onionhouse, another McBean vehicle that released a 7” on Final Notice Records (FN7002) in 1990 — a grunge/punk/melodic hardcore record produced by Ian Ashcroft. A third early-career group was Pigment Vehicle (Saskatoon, late 1980s), which released the cassette Hockey-Night in Saskatoon (1988) on Not On Label; McNeil was a member per Discogs though he is not credited on that specific release.

Solo Recording and Country/Folk Pivot (1998–2002)

By the late 1990s McNeil had shifted toward country-inflected rock and alt-folk. His guitar and vocal work on Pigment Vehicle’s full-length Murder’s Only Foreplay When You’re Hot For Revenge (Sudden Death Records, 1998, SD-004) shows a math-rock/hardcore side. Around 2000 he self-released Give ‘Er (Not On Label), playing essentially all instruments (vocals, electric guitar, electric bass, drums) and recording most of it himself — the only outside engineer on one track was Scott Henderson. The album is a self-produced country-rock statement and represents his clearest turn as a solo artist.

The Carolyn Mark Years and Mint Records (2000–2005)

McNeil became a key collaborator of Carolyn Mark and the Room-Mates, the BC-based country/folk scene centered on Mint Records (12). He contributed across multiple releases:

  • The Other Women (Carolyn Mark, 2000, Mint Records, MRD-?): sequencing and pre-mastering.
  • Terrible Hostess (Carolyn Mark and the Room-Mates, 2002, Mint MRD-061): bass, guitar, backing vocals, overdub recording, man chorus (with Carey Mercer, Ford Pier, Garth Johnson, others).
  • A Tribute To Nashville (Carolyn Mark, 2002, Mint MRD-054): producer, guitarist, bassist, pianist, steel guitar, and vocalist (he sang “I’m Easy” on track 15, credited as “Tolan ‘Bunky’ McNeil”). Featured an extraordinary cast including Neko Case, A.C. Newman, Dallas Good, Travis Good, Kelly Hogan, and The Corn Sisters.

He also engineered and co-produced The Black Monk (2002, Mint Records) and contributed to The Pros And Cons Of Collaboration (2004, Mint MRD-?): guitar, recording, backing vocals.

Session Work and Engineering (2003–2007)

Through the mid-2000s McNeil was a busy session player and independent recording engineer across the BC indie scene:

  • Lap steel / pedal steel: Shapes And Sizes (self-titled, Asthmatic Kitty, 2006, AKR024); Beautiful House (2005, Boompa Records) as additional engineer and pedal steel.
  • Mastering: Light Flows The Putrid Dawn by Blackout Beach (Soft Abuse, 2004); Kauai (2004, The Hive); The Road To New Orleans (2005, Labelman); The Bloody Hand (2006, Absolutely Kosher); The Aaargh! Annual Year Two (2007, Aaargh! Records).
  • Recording engineer: Notorious Lightning And Other Works (2005, Soft Abuse and Merge Records); Shapes And Sizes self-titled (2005–2006); Welcome Kings! (2007, Kill Devil Hills); Through Flames (2007, Switchblade Valentines self-release).
  • Sequencing: Tears Of The Valedictorian (2007, Absolutely Kosher; also Ruminance 2008 edition).

Pink Mountaintops and Jagjaguwar (2009)

McNeil’s most prominent major-label credit: he engineered and performed electric guitar, pedal steel, and mandolin on Outside Love by Pink Mountaintops (Jagjaguwar, 2009, JAG136), Stephen McBean’s solo vehicle. Other players on the record included Kathryn Calder, Sophie Trudeau, Josh Wells, Jesse Sykes, and Amber Webber.

Later Work (2011–2022)

McNeil continued engineering and performing sporadically through the 2010s and into the 2020s:

  • Black Valley Gospel (2011, Bad Vibes): recording.
  • The Stars Fall Shooting Into Twangsville and The Chaser (Secret Emchy Society, 2017 and 2020): lead guitar and backing vocals.
  • Silence In Grave + Realms Of Evil (Mission Of Christ archival compilation, Supreme Echo, 2019, SE19/SE20): appeared as guitarist on select early tracks and as an interviewee.
  • Gold Country Country Gold (Broken Clover Records, 2022): backing vocals, lead guitar, engineer, producer, mixer — his most comprehensive production credit in years.
