Robert Wheeler

Synthesizer player and thereminst. Discogs artist ID: 647469. Also known as Rob Wheeler, Wheeler.

Overview

Robert Wheeler is an American musician best known as the long-term synthesizer player and thereminst of Pere Ubu, the Cleveland avant-rock band led by David Thomas (2). He joined Pere Ubu in 1994–95 for the Ray Gun Suitcase sessions, taking over the electronic texture role previously held by Allen Ravenstine, and remained a core member through 20 Years in a Montana Missile Silo (2017) and the posthumous live record By Order of Mayor Pawlicki (Live in Jarocin) (2020). His instrument palette across that run centered on EML ElectroComp synthesizers, theremin, and Ensoniq EPS sampler, with later credits expanding to include a Grendel Drone Commander, Korg iMS-20, SNM Cacophoner II, and iPad. Wheeler has also held writing credits on multiple Pere Ubu albums, signalling a compositional as well as performance role within the band.

Before Pere Ubu, Wheeler was a member of Home And Garden, a Cleveland post-punk/art-rock project formed by former Pere Ubu members Scott Krauss and Tony Maimone after the band’s first dissolution in 1982. His only known solo release is a collaborative 12” with Allen Ravenstine on the Blue Jet Corporation label (2013), returning him to shared ground with his predecessor in the EML synthesizer chair.

According to Wikipedia, Wheeler has played synthesizer and theremin with Pere Ubu since 1994, and is listed among the band’s past members. David Thomas, Pere Ubu’s sole constant member, died on April 23, 2025.

Projects

Home And Garden

Home And Garden was a Cleveland group formed c.1982 by ex-Pere Ubu members Scott Krauss (drums) and Tony Maimone (bass), alongside Wheeler (EML synthesizer, Ensoniq Mirage), Jim Jones (3) (guitar, synthesizer), Michele Temple (bass guitar), and Jeff Morrison. Their album Melville (1986, After Hours Records) is Wheeler’s earliest Discogs credit. The group also included Diane Duncan and had ties to the wider Cleveland post-punk community. Wikipedia notes that “Krauss and Maimone formed Home and Garden” after Song of the Bailing Man (1982) dissolved the original Pere Ubu lineup. Discogs lists Home and Garden with artist ID 175175.

Farm Report (with Allen Ravenstine)

In 2013 Wheeler and Ravenstine released a collaborative 12” 45 RPM single on the Blue Jet Corporation label (cat. EML-101), titled Farm Report. Both sides feature both musicians on synthesizer (ElectroComp); the engineer was Bob Doidge. The catalog number EML-101 is an explicit nod to the EML ElectroComp 101 synthesizer central to both men’s work in Pere Ubu. This is Wheeler’s only solo/co-primary Discogs credit outside of group projects.

Pere Ubu (1994–2019)

Wheeler joined in time for the Ray Gun Suitcase (1995) sessions and remained on every studio album through The Long Goodbye (2019) and the 2020 live release. His run spans the Tim/Kerr, Glitterhouse, Cooking Vinyl, Fire Records, and Cherry Red eras of the band.
