Neil Hagerty

Real name: Neil Michael Hagerty Discogs artist ID: 263601 Born: US Role: Guitarist, vocalist, songwriter, producer

American guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter. Best known as co-founder of Royal Trux (with Jennifer Herrema). Also records under his full name Neil Michael Hagerty. After Royal Trux, led The Howling Hex through the 2000s–2010s.

Biography

Early Career: Pussy Galore (1985–1990)

Hagerty entered the music world in Washington, D.C., joining Pussy Galore (2) (Discogs ID: 252968) shortly after its formation by Jon Spencer and Julie Cafritz in 1985. The band debuted on Shove Records and Adult Contemporary Recordings with the Feel Good About Your Body EP (1985), quickly becoming a key noise/punk act in the D.C. scene before relocating to New York.

Hagerty played guitar across Pussy Galore (2)‘s core catalog on labels including Shove Records, Product Inc. (UK), and Rough Trade:

After the NY relocation, Bob Bert (of Sonic Youth) and Cristina Martinez also joined the band. The group disbanded in 1990. Spencer went on to form the Jon Spencer Blues Explosion; Cafritz joined Kim Gordon in Free Kitten; Hagerty continued into Royal Trux.

Royal Trux (1987–2001; reunion 2015–2019)

Hagerty co-founded Royal Trux (Discogs ID: 65828) with Jennifer Herrema in 1987. The duo began self-releasing on their own Royal Records (8) before finding a home on Drag City (US) and later Domino (UK/Europe).

Twin Infinitives (1990) is regarded as the duo’s most radical statement — double-LP noise collage, produced by Hagerty and Herrema with engineer Greg Freeman.

Thank You (1995) was produced by David Briggs (Neil Young’s long-time producer) with a full band lineup including Dan Brown (2) on bass and Chris Pyle on drums. Hagerty and Herrema were credited as “Executive Consultants” rather than producers, indicating the full studio-band setup Virgin imposed.

Accelerator saw Hagerty credited with an extraordinary range of instruments: guitar, vocals, kazoo, bass, MIDI controller, keyboards, tin whistle/pennywhistle, percussion, harmonica, and haegum (Korean bowed fiddle).

Reunion (2015–2019):

  • 2017 — Platinum Tips + Ice Cream (Domino / Drag City)
  • 2019 — White Stuff (Fat Possum Records); Quantum Entanglement (Fat Possum)

As of 2019, Hagerty stated he left Royal Trux again after hearing White Stuff and not approving of the final product — the album was written through remote file-swapping between Hagerty and Herrema.

Adam & Eve (4)

Adam & Eve (4) (Discogs ID: 256468) is a production alias shared by Hagerty and Herrema. Credited as producer on Royal Trux’s Pound For Pound (2000, Drag City).

Weird War (2002–2005)

Weird War (Discogs ID: 327643) — Washington D.C. funk/punk/gospel ensemble formed ~2002. Members: Ian Svenonius (vocals), Michelle Mae (bass), Alex Minoff, Sebastian Thomson, Jessica Espeleta, Steve McCarty, Blake Brunner, and Hagerty. Also operated as Scene Creamers. Hagerty-Toth Band

Hagerty-Toth Band (Discogs ID: 4364929) — collaborative project with James Toth (of Wooden Wand). Released Qalgebra (2015, Three Lobed Recordings). Band also included Darin Gray (bass) and Eric Van Leuven (drums, also of The Howling Hex).
