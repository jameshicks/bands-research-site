Aug 13, 20262 min read

Lynn Mabry

Discogs ID: 234997
Origin: USA

Vocalist. Backing vocalist on Junie Morrison’s Columbia LP Bread Alone (1980) and co-writer on “Seaman First Class (Jock Rock)” and “Funky Parts.” Also a vocalist on Funkadelic’s One Nation Under A Groove (1978) and other P-Funk recordings.

Lynn Mabry was a backing vocalist in the P-Funk collective, credited on Parliament’s Funkentelechy Vs. The Placebo Syndrome (1977), Motor Booty Affair (1978), and GloryHallaStoopid (1979). Also a member of Brides Of Funkenstein (with Dawn Silva) on Atlantic Records.

Notable arcs: She was a backing vocalist on the Talking Heads concert film Stop Making Sense (1984), had a sustained association with Japanese pop (Namie Amuro’s Avex Trax tours 1996–2000), worked with Eric Clapton (2010) and appeared on both the Rocketman soundtrack and Elton John’s Jewel Box box set (2019–2020). Her most recent sustained collaboration is with Sheila E. on the Stiletto Flats Music label (2017, 2022), where she also holds producer and arranger credits.
