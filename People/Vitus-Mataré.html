Vitus Mataré

Producer, multi-instrumentalist (keyboards, flute, guitar, vocals). Los Angeles. Discogs artist ID: 271558. Active in records from 1977 onward.

Overview

Mataré is the principal producer behind The Urinals (including their 100 Flowers era), credited on all their core releases from the 1978 debut EP through the 1996 Negative Capability compilation. He also plays keyboards and flute across a number of records and is a listed member of Trotsky Icepick.

His production work ranges across LA punk, SST Records acts, and independent California releases. Among notable credits: produced the earliest Urinals EPs; produced and played on several Trotsky Icepick LPs; produced SST Records artists including Würm (Kill Tunes, 1986), Saint Vitus (Fuck, 1987), October Faction (Mystery Spot, 1987), Slovenly (We Shoot For The Moon, 1989); engineer/producer on Minutemen’s Double Nickels On The Dime reissue remix credit (1987); produced Fundamental label releases including Savage Republic (Jamahiriya, 1988), and Inca Eyeball (Aphonia, 1988); produced Sex Beat 81 (Lolita, France, 1984) and several European acts.
