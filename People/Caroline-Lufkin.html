Overview

Caroline Lufkin is a vocalist, songwriter, and producer whose work spans indie-electronic pop and ambient territory. Born in Okinawa to a US military family, she grew up moving frequently before graduating from college in Boston, Massachusetts. She later returned to Japan, where her sister — J-pop artist Olivia (6) (Olivia Lufkin, signed to Avex Trax) — was already an established pop star. During this period Caroline did commercial session work in Japan, including a cover of Minnie Riperton’s “Loving You” for a telephone company.

Not wanting to pursue J-pop, she relocated to Los Angeles to make music on her own terms. She wrote “Where’s My Love,” researched the indie label Temporary Residence Limited, and sent founder Jeremy DeVine a link to her recording. He signed her for a single; her debut album followed on the same label. Her brother Jeffrey Lufkin (alias: Eli Walks) is a core production and programming collaborator on her solo records.

She has been a member of Mice Parade (the project of Adam Pierce) since 2006, contributing vocals on the Candela album (2012) and appearing on Lapapọ (2022).
