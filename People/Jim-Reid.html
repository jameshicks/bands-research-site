Jim Reid

Born James McLeish Reid, 29 December 1961, Glasgow, Scotland. Primary vocalist and co-songwriter of The Jesus And Mary Chain, which he co-founded with brother William Reid in East Kilbride c.1983. Discogs artist ID: 53883.

Overview

Jim Reid is the vocalist and lead co-songwriter of The Jesus And Mary Chain, the Scottish band he co-founded with brother William Reid around 1983 in East Kilbride. The band relocated to London in 1984, signed to Creation Records for their debut single, and moved to Blanco Y Negro (WEA) for the entirety of their original run (1985–1998). Together the brothers wrote and produced all seven JAMC studio albums through Munki (1998), with Jim taking lead vocal duties on the majority of tracks and sharing production credits across the full catalog.

After JAMC’s 1999 dissolution — following an onstage altercation between Jim and William — Jim formed Freeheat with JAMC guitarist Ben Lurie and members of The Gun Club (Romi Mori and Nick Sanderson). Freeheat released one single (“Don’t Worry, Be Happy”) on Hall Of Records in 2000 and were active until approximately 2003, when Sanderson’s declining health became a factor (he died in 2008).

Outside JAMC, Jim appeared as a guest vocalist on Death In Vegas’s The Contino Sessions (1999), co-wrote and co-produced Linda Reid’s Sister Vanilla project, and released a small number of solo recordings. He also co-wrote a cover of Bob Dylan’s track on his 2006 solo EP Dead End Kids. The JAMC reunion in 2007 brought Jim and William back together, with subsequent studio releases Damage and Joy (2017, Artificial Plastic Records) and Glasgow Eyes (2024, Fuzz Club Records) both bearing his sole songwriter credit.

Dead End Kids (2006) credits Jim Reid as primary artist and producer alongside Philip King (co-producer) and Mark Crozer (recorded by). Track 2 is a Bob Dylan cover. This appears to be Jim’s only standalone solo EP release on Discogs.

The 2003 “I Can See Clearly Now” is a collaboration with German electronic act Seelenluft (released in Austria on Klein Records) — Jim is co-primary artist.

The 2024 release Never Understood The Jesus And Mary Chain is a spoken word / reading credited to both William and Jim Reid jointly.
