Overview

Leigh Gorman is an English bassist born 11 December 1961 in London, best known as one of the founding members of Bow Wow Wow. He came up through Adam And The Ants before Malcolm McLaren recruited the backing band — Gorman alongside Dave Barbarossa and Matthew Ashman — to form Bow Wow Wow with vocalist Annabella Lwin. His 272 documented credits run from 1980 into the 2020s, taking in new wave and synth-pop through to downtempo, future jazz, and house; major label homes across his career include RCA, EMI, and ATCO Records. Beyond Bow Wow Wow he is credited as a member of Soho (2), Chiefs Of Relief, and Fantastic Planet, and holds production and engineering credits alongside performance work.
