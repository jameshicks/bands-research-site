Mitchell Froom

Discogs ID: 55974

American record producer and musician. Produced American Music Club’s Mercury (1993, Virgin). Prolific producer of the 1980s–1990s indie/alternative sphere; worked with Elvis Costello, Crowded House, Los Lobos, Richard Thompson, Sheryl Crow, Paul McCartney. Long-term collaborator with engineer Tchad Blake.

Tchad Blake Partnership

The Froom / Blake pairing was the defining creative duo of this era — Froom produced, Blake engineered and mixed. Key shared albums (both credited):

  • Crowded House: Woodface (1991), Temple Of Low Men, compilations
  • Los Lobos: Colossal Head (1996), This Time (1999), The Town And The City (2006)
  • Elvis Costello: Brutal Youth (1994)
  • Sheryl Crow: self-titled (1996), The Globe Sessions (1998)
  • Soul Coughing: Ruby Vroom (1994), El Oso (1998)
  • Richard Thompson: Rumor And Sigh, Mirror Blue
  • Suzanne Vega: Nine Objects Of Desire (1996)
  • Bonnie Raitt: Fundamental (1998), Silver Lining (2002)
  • Ron Sexsmith: Other Songs (1997), Whereabouts (1999)
  • Neil Finn: Try Whistling This (1998), One All (2002)
  • Paul Simon: Surprise (2006)
  • Cibo Matto: Viva! La Woman (1996)
