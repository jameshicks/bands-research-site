Overview

Fredrik Nilsen is a Los Angeles-based photographer and musician, active since 1977 and a core figure in the Los Angeles Free Music Society (LAFMS). He has performed and recorded with multiple LAFMS-affiliated groups — Airway, Bpeople, Creatures’ Lives, Doo-Dooettes, and Extended Organ — taking on roles as bassist, engineer, and compiler as well as performer. His heaviest label relationship is with Cortical Foundation, where he appears across nine releases between 1996 and 2003. Unusually for a figure rooted in the Los Angeles experimental underground, his credit count of 245 includes extensive shared work with R.E.M. members Peter Buck, Michael Stipe, and Mike Mills — most likely reflecting photography commissions on major-label releases via American Recordings and Warner Bros.
