Chris Nelson (5)

Discogs ID: 618200
Alias: W (32)
Guitarist and graphic designer. Core member of Information, No Group, Mofungo, and The Scene Is Now. Associated with NO Magazine, the No Wave fanzine published at NYU.

Overview

Chris Nelson is credited as graphic designer for NO Magazine and is associated with early currency of the term “no wave” in print. He appears on guitar and vocals on Information’s tracks on the No Magazine #4 flexidisc (1979) and on guitar and trumpet across Information’s tracks on the Tape #1 cassette (1980). He continued through The Scene Is Now into the 2000s and 2010s.

Note: Discogs carries two “Chris Nelson” entries connected to this scene — Chris Nelson (4) (ID 486583, linked to The Scene Is Now and a 1996 trombone credit) and Chris Nelson (5) (ID 618200, linked to Information, Mofungo, No Group, and The Scene Is Now). Whether these are the same individual remains unresolved.
