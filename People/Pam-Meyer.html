Pam Meyer

Nationality: American Role: Visual artist, sleeve designer, musician, jeweller Active: c.1979–1984 (music); 1984–present (jewellery, as ALCHEMY)

Role in This Network

Pam Meyer is a key social hub connecting the London Dining Out scene, the Berlin art world, the Manchester post-punk scene, the New York underground, and the anarcho-punk world — all through personal connections. She is also one of the few figures in this network who remains fully traceable and professionally active.

Artwork Details

  • “Zero Zero One” sleeve (Twilight Zoners, Zip Records, 1979): Limited numbered edition of 30, using an altered image from a printed advertisement
  • Multiple other Twilight Zoners sleeves — exact titles TBC
  • Magazines — artwork for unspecified publications
  • Rubber stamp disk covers — sold in NYC and DC-area shops
  • Volume: The International Discography of the New Wave (2nd ed.) — contributed artwork; this is a key archival publication of the post-punk era

ALCHEMY Jewellery

Operating since 1984 under the name ALCHEMY (Instagram: @alcpam). Creates limited-production jewellery and gift items:

  • Pewter castings of industrial/machine-age artifacts
  • Materials: gears, levers, antique keys, 45rpm record inserts (a nod to her music past), and other found objects
  • Finishes: antique silver, antique brass, antique copper
  • Trained under noted metalsmith Heikki Seppa
  • Worked from Brooklyn loft for many years; now based in Hudson Valley, NY
  • Carried in museum shops and galleries across US and Canada

The use of 45rpm record inserts in her jewellery is a quiet autobiographical flourish.
