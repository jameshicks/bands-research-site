Jacqui Ham

Guitarist, bassist, drummer, vocalist. Co-founder of Ut. Discogs artist ID: 531900

Overview

Jacqui Ham is one of the three founding members of Ut, the No Wave / post-punk trio formed in New York City in December 1978 alongside Nina Canal and Sally Young. Ut’s founding premise was explicitly anti-hierarchical: all three members played all instruments and rotated the role of vocalist and director from song to song. Ham is credited across Ut’s catalog playing guitar, bass, and drums, and as a songwriter on tracks spread across their 1982–1989 recordings.

Ham is credited as a songwriter on the 1982 live cassette Ut Live Nov 1981 (tracks A1 and A5), and on the 1987 double-LP In Gut’s House (tracks A1, A3, C6, D9). On Confidential (1985) she plays guitar and sings on B1. On Griller (1989) she plays bass on tracks 1, 6, 8, and 11; guitar on tracks 2–5, 7, and 10; and drums on track 9.

After Ut went on hiatus in 1991, Ham went on to co-found Dial with Rob Smith and Dom Weeks — a noise rock trio that released Infraction (1996) and Noise Opera (2016) on the CEDE label. She plays guitar and provides vocals on Infraction and is credited as lyricist (Words By) for that record. The band has a fourth member Alex Brandau credited on Discogs.

Ham’s name variations in Discogs include: Ham, J, J Ham, J. Ham, Jacqui, Jaqui.
