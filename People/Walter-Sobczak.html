Walter Sobczak

Discogs ID: 213804. Toronto-based recording engineer, producer, and bassist, active from the early 1980s to the present. Began his career as a bassist in the Toronto post-punk band Sturm Group, then pivoted into studio work at Wellesley Sound Studios, where he rose from assistant engineer to full engineer through the late 1980s. Over the 1990s he built a production and engineering practice spanning Canadian hip-hop, alternative rock, riot grrrl/queercore, industrial metal, and reggae-fusion, making him one of the more genre-fluid figures in Toronto’s independent recording scene. He was a co-founder and bassist/producer of the Virgin/Attic-signed reggae-metal hybrid band Raggadeath (1993–1997). Since 2000 he has worked primarily out of The Studio at Puck’s Farm (2000–2012) and Revolution Recording in downtown Toronto.

Name variations on Discogs: Sobczak, Voltah, W. Sobczak, Walter, Walter N. Sobczak, Walter Sobchak, Walter Sobozak, Walter Sobzcak, Walter Soeczak.

Overview

Early Career: Sturm Group and Wellesley Sound Studios (1983–1989)

Sobczak entered the Toronto music world in the early 1980s as the bassist for Sturm Group, a post-punk outfit that operated until 1989. Their 1989 EP Grind (Amok Records) was among his last performing credits before his studio career took over. While still playing with Sturm Group, Sobczak began work as an assistant engineer at Wellesley Sound Studios in downtown Toronto — one of the city’s premier commercial studios at the time. Credits from this period include assistant engineering on the Dirty Dancing soundtrack (for which he received an RIAA multi-platinum sales award) and on Michie Mee’s debut single for First Priority Music/Atlantic (the Michie Mee And LA Luv recording Victory Is Calling / On This Mic, 1988) and the First Priority Family compilation The First Priority Music Family Basement Flavor (1988).

Full Engineer: Maestro, Dream Warriors, Organized Rhyme, BNL (1989–1993)

By 1989–1990 Sobczak had moved up to full engineer credits. Key recordings from this window:

  • Symphony In Effect (1989) — Maestro Fresh-Wes’s landmark debut, one of the first Canadian hip-hop albums to break mainstream (Sobczak received a CRIA gold/platinum award for this)
  • Do Or Die (1988) — Scott B. Sympathy debut on Maze Music
  • Neil Yonge Street (1990) — Scott B. Sympathy follow-up (Smokeshow Records); engineer, mixer, and co-producer
  • Barenaked Ladies (The Sandwich E.P.) (1991) — the self-released cassette/EP by Barenaked Ladies that launched the band’s career; Sobczak is the engineer of record
  • Check The O.R. (1992) and Huh!? Stiffenin Against The Wall (1992) — both Organized Rhyme albums on A&M, where he co-produced and engineered

He also received a CRIA award for his work on Dream Warriors (the album Subliminal Simulation, 1995, Pendulum Records, co-producer credit) and on the Club Cutz Vol. 4 compilation.

Raggadeath and 1990s Independent Work (1993–1997)

In the mid-nineties Sobczak became a founder, writer, bassist, keyboard player, and producer/engineer for Raggadeath, a Toronto reggae-metal fusion band that released records on Virgin Music Canada and later Attic (the Canadian independent). Key Raggadeath credits: the EP Why Ask Why (1995, Virgin Music Canada), The Family Worship EP (1995, Fringe Product), One Life (1995, Virgin), Dance With The Devil (1997, Attic/TWA Records), and the self-titled album Raggadeath (1997, Attic). On all of these Sobczak served as producer, engineer, mixer, bassist, and keyboard programmer. The band broke up in 1997.

Alongside Raggadeath he continued engineering outside work in this period: Fifth Column’s 36c (1994, K/Denon) — engineer, mixer, and co-producer; Monster Voodoo Machine’s State Voodoo / State Control (1994, D-Tribe Records) — engineer and producer; Dream Warriors’ Subliminal Simulation (1995, Pendulum Records) — co-producer; and Michie Mee’s first single work continued through this era as well.

Puck’s Farm and Later Work (2000–present)

From 2000 to 2012 Sobczak was chief engineer at The Studio at Puck’s Farm, a residential analog studio outside Toronto. Since then he has worked at Revolution Recording in downtown Toronto. Credits from this period become sparser in Discogs but include:

  • Echoes (2009) — Jenn Grant on Six Shooter Records; engineer
  • Walkin’ The Wire (2011) — Rattlesnake Choir on Cousin Jeb Records; engineer, mixer, masterer, producer
  • Orbit (2018) — Avi Granite 6 on Pet Mantis Records; engineer
  • Goodnight Riverdale Park (2019) — Suitcase Sam on Curve Music; engineer and producer
