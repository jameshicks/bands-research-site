Overview

Don Joyce (1944–2015) was a core member of the experimental/sound-collage collective Negativland and the longtime host of their radio programme Over the Edge on KPFA. His credited roles — tape, noises, performer, and mixing — reflect a practice built around appropriation and collage rather than conventional musicianship. His catalogue runs from 1986 through posthumous reissues in 2020, released primarily through Seeland and SST Records, with Mark Hosler, Richard Lyons, Chris Grigg, and David Wills as his closest collaborators.
