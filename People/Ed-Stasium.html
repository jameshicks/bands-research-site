Ed Stasium

Record producer, engineer. Full name: Edward Stasium, Jr. Discogs artist ID: 241495.

American producer and engineer with a long career across rock, punk, and alternative. Best known for his work with the Ramones, Living Colour, and Talking Heads. Also known under aliases including Fred Stadium (used as engineer credit on some sessions).
