Debsey Wykes

Discogs ID: 228256

Singer and musician; backing vocalist on multiple Saint Etienne albums (42 shared releases).

Contributed additional vocals to So Tough (1993), Tiger Bay (1994), and Good Humor (1998). Sang on “Who Do You Think You Are” and “Former Lover” (Tiger Bay). Also a member of Dolly Mixture.
