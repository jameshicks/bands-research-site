Jerry Busher

US drummer, multi-instrumentalist, and audio engineer based in Washington DC. Discogs artist ID: 534692. Name variations: “Jerry”, “Jerry B.”

DC scene lifer who circled Dischord Records from the late 1980s onward. Played drums in Fidelity Jones and Las Mordidas in the early-to-mid 1990s, then became a touring percussionist with Fugazi from approximately 1998 to 2002. After Fugazi’s hiatus he joined French Toast (with James Canty) and Garland Of Hours (with Brendan Canty), while also taking on engineering and production work. Also a credited member of All Scars, The Spinanes (session), Fidelity Jones, Las Mordidas, Deathfix, Elevator (5), and Shadow Riot.

Biography

Late 1980s–Early 1990s: Fidelity Jones & Las Mordidas

Busher appeared on the Dischord Records roster as drummer for Fidelity Jones (Dischord ID 309595), a DC band featuring vocalist Tomas Squip, guitarist Andy Charneco, and bassist Dug Birdzell. He played on both their records: Piltdown Lad (1989) and Venus On Lovely (1990), both engineered by Don Zientara at Inner Ear.

In 1994 he was drumming for Las Mordidas (Discogs ID 1336213), a Dischord/Compulsiv Music band with Dug Birdzell on bass and Chris Thomson on vocals. Their single Surrounded / K.I.T.A. (1994) appeared on both Dischord and Compulsiv Music.

Mid-1990s: All Scars & Connections to Fugazi Circle

Busher became a member of All Scars (Discogs ID 813376), a band whose lineup included Brendan Canty (guitar, keyboards), Chuck Bettis (vocals, percussion, trumpet), and Dug Birdzell (bass). He played drums and trumpet on their releases:

  • Early Ambient (1997, Dischord Records / Slowdime) — drums, trumpet
  • Introduction To Humanity (1999, Slowdime) — drums, trumpet, maracas

The 1999 session also featured James Canty (keyboards) and Amy Farina (vocals). All Scars was effectively a side project of the Dischord/Fugazi circle.

Also in 1997 he played trumpet on the Blonde Redhead single Symphony Of Treble / Kasuality (Touch And Go, 1997), produced and recorded by Guy Picciotto.

1998: The Spinanes — Arches And Aisles

Busher is credited on drums and percussion across nine tracks of Arches And Aisles (Sub Pop, 1998) by The Spinanes, the project of vocalist/guitarist Rebecca Gates. The album also featured Joanna Bolme and was mixed in part by John McEntire (Tortoise). Busher played drums alongside a large ensemble spread across multiple recording locations.

1998–2002: Fugazi Touring

Busher joined Fugazi as a touring live-tech/percussionist, adding second drums and percussion behind core drummer Brendan Canty. He is credited on:

  • End Hits (1998, Dischord) — photography (back cover, “Away” shot)
  • The Argument (2001, Dischord) — “Technician [Live Tech/Percussion]”

His period with Fugazi covered the End Hits and The Argument touring cycles. The Argument credit is his primary documentary Discogs connection to the band, where he appears alongside the four core members (Ian MacKaye, Guy Picciotto, Brendan Canty, Joe Lally) and live sound engineers Joey Picuri and Nick Pellicciotto.

2001–2006: French Toast

Busher became a full member of French Toast (Discogs ID 292022), a Dischord band with James Canty and Ben Gilligan. He is credited as songwriter, multi-instrumentalist (vocals, guitar, bass, drums, tape, keyboards), and artist on both their records:

ReleaseYearLabelBusher’s role
In A Cave2005Dischord RecordsArtwork, Songwriter
Ingleside Terrace2006Dischord RecordsVocals, Guitar, Bass, Drums, Tape, Keyboards; Artwork; produced by Brendan Canty

Ingleside Terrace was produced by Brendan Canty, who also plays on the record alongside Busher and James Canty.

2004: John Frusciante — DC EP

Busher played drums on John Frusciante’s DC EP (2004, Record Collection), an unusual session produced by Ian MacKaye and engineered by Don Zientara. The EP is an outlier in Busher’s discography — Frusciante’s solo satellite project, recorded in DC.

2007 and Beyond: Garland Of Hours, Dust Galaxy, Engineering

After French Toast, Busher joined Garland Of Hours (Discogs ID 1816428), a large-ensemble DC post-rock group co-led by Brendan Canty and also including Mary Timony, Amy Domingues, Devin Ocampo, Jonah Takagi, Brandon L. Butler, and Stefan Bauschmid.

Also in 2007 he appears on a track (A2) on Mother Of Illusion (2007, Eighteenth Street Lounge Music) by Dust Galaxy (Discogs ID 781787), a project by Rob Garza (Thievery Corporation) and James Canty, alongside a large international ensemble.

In 2012 he played pocket trumpet on track B1 of The Odds (2012, Dischord) by The Evens (Ian MacKaye and Amy Farina).

In 2014 he produced, engineered, mixed, played percussion, and sang backing vocals on Angel Eyes (El Rey Records) — an unidentified artist credited to his engineering/production work.

In 2021 he appeared as a guest drummer on Faltered Pursuit (Sleeping Giant Glossolalia) by Hyrrokkin x Merzbow, a noise/experimental collaboration, alongside Chuck Bettis and Andrea Parkins.

In 2023 he appeared as a guest on Scream (2)‘s DC Special (Dischord, 2023), a live album with a large DC guest list including Ian MacKaye, Joe Lally, Dave Grohl, and Brian Baker.
