Overview

Gerard Julien is a New York-based engineer, mixer, and recording engineer whose catalogue spans 1990 through the 2020s, anchored primarily in the Jive Records orbit of boom-bap, jazzy hip-hop, and New Jack Swing. His 179 Discogs credits place him squarely inside the A Tribe Called Quest recording circle — 123 shared releases with the group and 139 with Bob Power, making him one of the most recurrent technical figures in that scene. Pete Christensen, Eric Gast, and Tim Latham each share over 150 credits with him, suggesting a tight studio unit that followed the same sessions across a decade of Jive releases.
