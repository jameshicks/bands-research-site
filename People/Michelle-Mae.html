Michelle Mae

Real name Michelle Mae Orr. American musician, singer, and spiritualist based in Washington, D.C. Bassist and vocalist active from 1993 onward; best known as a founding member and bassist of The Make-Up (1995–2000, reunions 2012–2013, 2017, 2019–present) and Weird War (2002–2005). Earlier associated with Frumpies (1993). Also credited as Michelle Munition (Discogs ID: 6434893) — that listing covers her appearance on the Frumpies Babies and Bunnies EP (1993); the two IDs are confirmed aliases of the same person.

Discogs IDs: 544553 (Michelle Mae) · 6434893 (Michelle Munition)
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Mae

Overview

Michelle Mae’s recording career begins in Olympia, WA with Frumpies — a lo-fi punk supergroup spun out of Bikini Kill and Bratmobile and assembled by Tobi Vail, Kathi Wilcox, Billy Karren, and Molly Neuman. According to Wikipedia, she joined the Frumpies in time to record “Tommy Slich” for the Babies and Bunnies EP (1993), and appears on the Alien Summer Nights EP (also 1993, on Chainsaw Records). Her Discogs credit on Alien Summer Nights lists her under her birth name, Michelle Orr.

She moved to Washington, D.C. and joined The Make-Up in 1995 alongside vocalist Ian Svenonius (ex-Nation of Ulysses), guitarist/organist James Canty, and drummer Steve Gamboa. The Make-Up released four studio albums, two live albums, and a singles compilation before dissolving in 2000. Mae played bass and contributed vocals throughout; on Sound Verite (1997) she also played electric piano.

After the Make-Up dissolved, Mae, Svenonius, and guitarist Alex Minoff formed Weird War (briefly known as Scene Creamers). The group released four albums on Drag City between 2002 and 2005, with Mae playing bass, guitar, clavinet, and percussion and co-writing songs. The Illuminated by the Light LP (2005) was the last studio Weird War album.

Mae is also listed as a member of Witchypoo, an Olympia supergroup, and Scene Creamers (the interim identity of Weird War). No primary-credited solo releases appear under either of her Discogs IDs. Discogs notes her spiritualist work through her website thesacredexpanse.com.

A posthumous Make-Up live album, Untouchable Sound (2006, Drag City/Sea Note), credited her on bass guitar and backing vocals. The Make-Up continued to perform sporadically in reunion configurations from 2012 onward.

Alias Distinction: Michelle Mae vs. Michelle Munition

  • Michelle Mae (ID 544553): primary Discogs entry; covers all Make-Up, Weird War, and Scene Creamers releases.
  • Michelle Munition (ID 6434893): covers only Babies and Bunnies (Kill Rock Stars, 1993) — the Frumpies EP recorded before her more permanent Frumpies credit under “Michelle Mae.” The two IDs are confirmed aliases of the same person (each ID lists the other as an alias).
