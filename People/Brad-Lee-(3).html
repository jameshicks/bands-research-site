Overview

Brad Lefkowits is a San Diego-based multi-instrumentalist, recording engineer, and label operator who works primarily on bass and trumpet but ranges across guitar, keys, drums, and vocals. He owns Loud + Clear Records and runs Stereo Disguise Recording Laboratories, a San Diego studio that has hosted much of his production work. His 64 Discogs credits begin in 2004 and cluster around ambient, downtempo, and indie rock; he has been a member of both Mr. Tube And The Flying Objects and The Album Leaf, with Jimmy LaValle — The Album Leaf’s principal — among his most frequent collaborators alongside Matt Resovich and Dave Lebleu.
