Messenger Douglas

Birmingham-based reggae vocalist and producer. Discogs artist ID: 946270. Name variation: R. Douglas. Active from at least 1998 (Jamaica-recorded single) through to 2021 (session backing vocals in Spain). Best documented as a track-level producer on Overproof Sound System’s album Pull It Up! (2012), and as the primary artist on the Humal Records 7” “Opportunity” (1998).

Overview

Messenger Douglas is a Birmingham-based reggae artist whose Discogs footprint is modest but spans across multiple decades and contexts. His earliest credit is as the primary artist on “Opportunity” / “Version” (1998), a 7” released on the UK/Jamaica reggae imprint Humal Records (RMVS 004 / RMVS 007), produced by Errol Ruddock (aka King Spears / Starky) and engineered by Errol Taylor (2). By 2012 he appears as a producer on three tracks of Overproof Sound System’s Pull It Up! album, co-producing with Groove Corporation (credited as G. Corp). In 2021 he contributed backing vocals to track B1 on Balance, a roots reggae album by Ras Tweed, released on A-Lone Productions (Spain), and backed by the Lone Ark Riddim Force.

External presence: Friendly Fire Music roster page, Facebook, and an archived MySpace page all list him under the “Messenger Douglas” name.

Note on R. Douglas credit discrepancy

Messenger Douglas carries “R. Douglas” as a name variation. A separate Discogs artist — R. Douglas (ID: 1098472), realname Eric Smallwood, member of Ambassadors Of Soul — is the credited producer of the Kingstep 12” (2009, EHR 1203). These are two distinct entries in the DB. The Overproof Sound System file has historically attributed the Kingstep production to Messenger Douglas on the basis of the shared “R. Douglas” name variation; however, Discogs credits that release to artist ID 1098472, not 946270. The two may or may not be the same individual — this is unresolved in Discogs data and would require a primary source (sleeve scan or label confirmation) to settle.
