Charlotte Gainsbourg

British-born French actress and singer (born 21 July 1971, London). Daughter of Serge Gainsbourg and Jane Birkin. Half-sister of Lou Doillon. Longtime partner of actor/director Yvan Attal. Discogs ID: 180664. Member of Les Enfoirés.

Overview

Charlotte Gainsbourg began recording as a teenager, duetting with her father Serge Gainsbourg on the provocative “Lemon Incest” (1984) and making her debut album Charlotte For Ever (1986), with Serge writing all music and lyrics. After a long hiatus focused on her acting career, she returned with 5:55 (2006), produced by Nigel Godrich with music entirely written and performed by AIR. IRM (2009), produced by Beck Hansen, became her international breakthrough — Beck played most instruments on the album and co-wrote across the tracklist. Stage Whisper (2011) compiled live recordings from the IRM tour. Her fourth studio album Rest (2017), produced predominantly by SebastiAn, addressed the deaths of her father and her half-sister Kate Barry; the album includes contributions from Danger Mouse (Brian Burton), Daft Punk’s Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo, and a Paul McCartney cover (“Hey Hey We Are the Monkees” writing credit listed as “Hey Joe” equivalent). She has also appeared as a featured artist on Soundwalk Collective’s Peradam (2020) alongside Patti Smith.
