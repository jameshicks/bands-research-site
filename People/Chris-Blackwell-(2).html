Overview

Christopher Frederic Blackwell is a British drummer and composer best known for his extensive collaboration with Robert Plant, with whom he shares over 200 credits spanning the late 1980s through the 2000s. He served as Plant’s touring and recording drummer alongside bandmates Phil Johnstone, Doug Boyle, Charlie Jones, and Tim Palmer, appearing on releases through Es Paranza Records and Atlantic. Alongside his work with Plant he has composed music for television, film, and advertising, and has also contributed to The Cartoons (3). He should not be confused with the Island Records founder of the same name.
