Tim Hurley

Chicago-based multi-instrumentalist (bass, drums, guitar, synthesizer, lap steel, berimbau, organ, vocals). Discogs artist ID: 568412. Name variations: T. Hurley, T Hurley, Timothy Hurley, TimH.

Core member of Red Red Meat (1993–1996) and early Califone (1998–2000). After leaving Califone he continued as one-half of Sin Ropas (with Danni Iosello) through at least 2016, with records on Perishable Records, Sad Robot, Shrug Records, and Jealous Butcher Records. Also a member of Squash Blossom, Loftus, and Jaap Boots Zonder Kleren.

Sin Ropas (2000–2016)

Duo of Tim Hurley and Danni Iosello. Discogs artist ID: 487275. Active across six releases spanning 2000–2016 — the longest-running project in Hurley’s documented discography. Hurley plays performer, songwriter, and handles recording. Brian Deck appears on Three Cherries (additional engineering) and Mirror Bride (mixing).
