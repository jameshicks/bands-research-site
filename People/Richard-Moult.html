Overview

Richard Moult is an English musician, artist, and poet, originally from Newcastle and now living in remote rural north-west Scotland. His output divides between the experimental psych-folk collective United Bible Studies and solo work for piano and small classical ensembles, the latter characterised by stark, haunting simplicity drawn from his interests in landscape, myth, and redemption. His recorded catalogue is concentrated in the 2010s, spread across small specialist imprints including Fluid Audio, Fort Evil Fruit, and Time Released Sound. His most constant collaborator is David Colohan (22 shared releases), with further sustained work alongside Michael Tanner, Áine O’Dwyer, and Alison O’Donnell.
