Overview

Lenny Franchi began as a bass player before transitioning into studio work as an assistant to Martin Lascelles at De-Mix Studios in London. He subsequently built a freelance career as an engineer, producer, and programmer across indie rock, trip-hop, downtempo, and experimental music, active from at least 1995. His deepest on-record relationship is with Tricky, with whom he shares 31 credits, placing him at the centre of the post-Bristol trip-hop scene of the late 1990s; he also racked up significant work with Björk, Mogwai, and My Bloody Valentine. His credits cluster most heavily around Hut Recordings and Virgin across the late 1990s and 2000s.
