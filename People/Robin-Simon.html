Oct 14, 20262 min read

Robin Simon

Discogs ID: 237652 Real name: Robert Simon Origin: UK

British guitarist. Member of Ultravox 1977–1979 (credited on Systems Of Romance), then joined Magazine 1979–1980 (bridging the gap after John McGeoch left for Siouxsie & The Banshees), credited on the live album Play (1980). Also played on Magazine studio albums The Correct Use of Soap and Magic, Murder and the Weather (The Garden and The Golden Section are Magazine albums on Virgin). Later associated with Visage (Steve Strange) and John Foxx projects.
