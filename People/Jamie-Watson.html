Jamie Watson

Jamie Watson

Oct 04, 20261 min read

Jamie Watson

Discogs ID: 267796

Overview

Edinburgh-based producer and engineer. Produced and/or recorded several Dog Faced Hermans releases from 1989–1993. Broader credits span Scottish indie and broader UK scenes, including work for The Vaselines (Sub Pop releases), Dum-Dum (53rd & 3rd), and various Edinburgh/Scottish acts on Avalanche Records and DDT Records. Also contributed writing credits to Elastica (2) (Fine Friend, 4AD; Slow Buildings, 4AD/Warner Bros.).
