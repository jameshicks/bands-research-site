Jim O'Rourke

Jim O'Rourke

Sep 27, 20262 min read

Jim O’Rourke

Discogs ID: 3550
Role: Producer, engineer, musician, composer
Origin: Chicago, IL (later Tokyo)

Prolific musician, composer, producer, and engineer at the intersection of avant-garde, rock, and electroacoustic music. Recorded Storm And Stress’s Under Thunder & Fluorescent Lights (2000, Touch And Go). Extensive production and engineering career spanning Sonic Youth, Gastr del Sol (co-founder), Wilco (Yankee Hotel Foxtrot), and many others. Also a solo recording artist across several labels.

Key Credits

  • Sonic Youth — full member 1999–2005; co-produced NYC Ghosts & Flowers (2000), mixed Murray Street (2002) and Sonic Nurse (2004)
  • Melt-Banana — mixed Scratch Or Stitch (1995, Meldac); also co-wrote track 19
  • Gastr Del Sol — co-founder and primary creative force
  • Wilco — produced Yankee Hotel Foxtrot
  • Loose Fur — trio with Jeff Tweedy and Glenn Kotche; O’Rourke mixed, engineered, and played multi-instruments on both albums

Groups

  • Gastr Del Sol (co-founder)
  • Sonic Youth (member 1999–2005)
  • Brise-Glace
  • Fenn O’Berg (with Fennesz, Peter Rehberg) — multiple Editions Mego releases
  • Loose Fur (with Jeff Tweedy, Glenn Kotche)
  • Illusion Of Safety

Editions Mego

15 primary credits on Editions Mego (2009–2020): solo release I’m Happy, And I’m Singing And A 1, 2, 3, 4 (EDITIONS mego 50, 2009), multiple Old News issues, and the Fenn O’Berg series (Magic & Return eMego 3154, 2009; In Stereo eMEGO 103, 2010; Live In Japan Part One eMEGO 110.1, 2010; Live In Japan Part Two eMEGO 110.2, 2010; In Hell eMEGO141, 2012). O’Rourke is credited as “Edited By” on In Hell in addition to Performer. Also collaborated with Kassel Jaeger for Wakes On Cerulean (EMEGO 223, 2017) and In Cobalt Aura Sleeps (EMEGO 272, 2020). The Fenn O’Berg trio became inactive with Peter Rehberg’s death in July 2021.

Further Collaborations

  • Cor Fuhler — F-O’R (Conundrom, 2009); duo of piano/EMS Putney/crackle box + organ/EMS Synthi A/computer

Tape compilations

  • Assemblage — PBK Recordings, 1990
  • Patience Is A Virtue — Sound Of Pig SOP 275, 1990
