Phil Manzanera

Born Philip Targett-Adams, 31 January 1951, London. British guitarist. Discogs artist ID: 10351.

Overview

Core member of Roxy Music throughout its run (1972–1983). Close collaborator of Brian Eno from Roxy Music onward — played on Here Come The Warm Jets (1974), Taking Tiger Mountain (By Strategy) (1974); the two founded the 1976 supergroup 801 (with Robert Wyatt, etc.) which released 801 Live. Manzanera also released solo albums and produced.
