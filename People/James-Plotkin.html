James Plotkin

Discogs ID: 12544 Role: Mastering engineer, producer, bassist Active: ~1988–present

79 mastering/production credits across Hydra Head Records catalog. Also a prolific musician — bass guitarist in Khanate (drone/doom, HH) and prior groups. Known for experimental electronics and guitar work as well. Has 11 shared releases with Expo ‘70 as mastering engineer, including Beguiled Entropy (2012, Blackest Rainbow), America Here & Now Sessions (2016, Essence Music), and Evolution (2021).

Mastered all three primary Emeralds (2) studio albums: What Happened (No Fun Productions, 2009), Does It Look Like I’m Here? (Editions Mego, 2010), and Just To Feel Anything (Editions Mego, 2012). 24 shared releases with Emeralds in total.

Split 7” with Pimmon on FatCat Records Split Series #8 (2000) — Plotkin produced his own tracks (A1–A3); Pimmon’s side (B1–B3) mastered by Mandy Parnell.

9 shared releases with Dirk Serries, primarily as mastering engineer on Tonefloat releases including Microphonics and Unseen Descending And Lamentations (Consouling Sounds, 2015).
