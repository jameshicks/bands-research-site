Elisa Ambrogio

US experimental musician. Discogs artist ID: 200414.

Guest on Six Organs of Admittance’s Asleep On The Floodplain (2011, Drag City). Member of Magik Markers. Also in 200 Years and Basalt Fingers with Ben Chasny. Appears on Donovan Quinn’s Honky Tonk Medusa (2012, Northern Spy) as liner notes writer; member of The Brain Band with Quinn, Glenn Donaldson, and Karl Bauer (2007, Fuck It Tapes).
