Overview

Anna de Bruin is a violinist active since at least 2012, with 45 recorded credits spanning an unusually wide stylistic range — from classical and film soundtrack work to indie rock, post-rock, and electronic contexts. She is a member of both the London Contemporary Orchestra and the London Mozart Players, and her closest collaborators (Laura Moody, Hazel Correa, Daisy Vatalaro, Simmy Singh) suggest sustained ensemble activity within those circles. Her label credits reach from classical imprints like Hyperion and Linn Records into more unexpected territory: Ed Banger Records in 2016 and 4AD in 2023, pointing to consistent session work outside the orchestral sphere across both decades of her recorded career.
