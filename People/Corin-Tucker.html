Corin Tucker

Born: 9 November 1972, Eugene, Oregon, USA
Role: Vocalist, guitarist, songwriter
Discogs artist ID: 350407 (also credited as “Kissy,” ID: 621348, on Cadallaca releases)
Active: 1992–present

American vocalist and guitarist from Eugene, Oregon. Best known as co-founder of Sleater-Kinney (1994–2006, reunited 2014–present) alongside Carrie Brownstein. Before Sleater-Kinney she co-founded the riot grrrl duo Heavens To Betsy (2) (1992–1994) with Tracy Sawyer. Other projects include Cadallaca (1998), The Corin Tucker Band (2010–2012), and the indie-rock supergroup Filthy Friends (2016–2019).

Early Career: Heavens To Betsy (1992–1994)

Tucker formed Heavens To Betsy in Olympia, Washington with drummer/bassist Tracy Sawyer. The duo was a defining act of the early riot grrrl scene, rooted in the DIY culture surrounding K Records and Kill Rock Stars. Tucker played guitar and sang; Sawyer handled drums and bass. Their first release was a split 7” with Bratmobile (“My Secret / Cool Schmool,” 1992, K Records). The debut EP These Monsters Are Real (1993, Kill Rock Stars) was recorded by Molly Neuman and Tim Green (2). Their sole full-length, Calculated (1994, Kill Rock Stars), was engineered by John Goodmanson and self-produced by the band — Tucker and Sawyer’s first encounter with Goodmanson, who would later produce four Sleater-Kinney albums.

Sleater-Kinney (1994–2006, 2014–present)

Tucker co-founded Sleater-Kinney (Discogs ID: 218269) with Carrie Brownstein in Olympia in 1994, initially as a side project while both had other bands. The band took its name from Sleater-Kinney Road in Lacey, WA. First drummer was Laura Macfarlane; Janet Weiss joined in 1997 for Dig Me Out, forming the classic trio. The band released early records on Chainsaw Records (Donna Dresch’s label), moved to Kill Rock Stars from 1997 onward, and signed to Sub Pop for The Woods (2005). After an indefinite hiatus announced in 2006, the band reformed in 2014. Weiss departed in 2019; Sleater-Kinney continues as a Tucker–Brownstein duo.

Tucker’s vocal style — a wide-interval yowl capable of extreme dynamic range — is central to the band’s sound. She writes the majority of the band’s lyrics and melodic content.

Cadallaca (1998)

Tucker recorded as “Kissy” (Discogs alias ID: 621348) in the trio Cadallaca (Discogs ID: 320268), alongside Sarah Dougher (vocals, organ) and STS (9) (drums). Introducing Cadallaca (1998, K Records) was produced by Calvin Johnson and recorded by Lawrence Crane. A lighter, more playful project compared to Sleater-Kinney, placing Tucker alongside the K Records world she’d inhabited since the Heavens To Betsy years.

The Corin Tucker Band (2010–2012)

During Sleater-Kinney’s hiatus Tucker formed The Corin Tucker Band (Discogs ID: 1978306), a full group rather than a solo vehicle. The core lineup for both albums: Tucker (vocals, acoustic and electric guitar), Seth Lorinczi (guitars, bass, keyboards — also produced), Sara Lund (drums, percussion — also of Unwound), Douglas Jenkins (cello), and Julianna Bright (vocals). Mike Clark (4) replaced Jenkins and Bright on Kill My Blues, which John Goodmanson mixed.

Filthy Friends (2016–2019)

An indie-rock supergroup based in Portland, Oregon. Members: Tucker (vocals), Peter Buck (guitar; R.E.M.), Scott McCaughey (keyboards), Kurt Bloch (guitar; The Fastbacks), Linda Pitmon (drums), and William Rieflin (bass, mixed; also R.E.M., Ministry). Both albums on Kill Rock Stars; Tucker and Buck the primary songwriters.
