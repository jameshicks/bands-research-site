William Tyler

US guitarist and songwriter. Discogs artist ID: 287719. Alias: The Paper Hats.

Collaborated with Ben Chasny on Parallelogram (2015, Three Lobed Recordings), a split/collaborative release credited to both artists and Six Organs of Admittance. Tyler is known for his fingerstyle guitar work and releases on Merge Records.
