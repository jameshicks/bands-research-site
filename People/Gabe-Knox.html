Gabe Knox

Discogs ID: 6868410 Origin: Toronto, Canada Role: Electronic musician, composer, producer Active: 2015–present Bandcamp: https://gabeknox.bandcamp.com/ Label home page: https://www.aumegaproject.com/artists/gabe-knox/

Toronto-based electronic musician working in the intersections of Krautrock, synthwave, ambient, and experimental electronics. Released the LP ABC (2019) on UK label Polytechnic Youth (cat. PY72), which is one of the very few Canadian artists on that roster. Also one half of the Toronto synth-pop/minimal duo Analytica alongside David Lush.

Biography

Gabe Knox began releasing electronic music in 2015 via the German netlabel Aumega Project, issuing a trilogy of digital EPs (EP:A, EP:B, EP:C) between 2015 and 2018. These were filed under genres including Ambient, Krautrock, Experimental, Post Rock, and Synthwave — situating Knox in the Berlin/motorik electronics tradition from the outset.

In 2019 Knox broke onto vinyl with ABC, a nine-track LP released on Crouch End, North London’s Polytechnic Youth — a label devoted to minimal synth and DIY electronics in the 1978–82 tradition. The album was Knox’s first vinyl release and his most prominent international statement. Nyles Miszczyk (Toronto engineer and multi-instrumentalist) contributes synth on track A3 (“Speak & Spell”); the album was mastered by Noah Mintz at Lacquer Channel Mastering in Toronto. Both collaborators confirm a Toronto-based production circle. The tracklist on ABC draws titles from across technology and modernist architecture — “Glacial Drift,” “Cité Radieuse,” “Das Auto,” “Esperanto” — in keeping with the label’s conceptual-electronic aesthetic.

Also in 2019, Knox mixed the Robin Hatch album T.O.N.T.O. (Robin Records, 2021), a Toronto experimental electronics record featuring Leland Whitty, Eric Slick, Joseph Shabason, Nick Thorburn and others — placing Knox inside a broader Toronto experimental-music network.

Through 2020 Knox released several more records on Polytechnic Youth: a 7” single (MusiComputer, PY98, 2020), a split 7” with Polytechnic Sound Archive (Youth Disco 45, PY127, 2020), and a 10” mini-album (Cosmic Motorik Adventures & Machine Language Music, PY114, 2020). In 2022 he contributed side A to a split LP with Pulse Lovers (Mat Handley, credited as “Pulsliebhaber”; PY 154), performing drum machine, synthesizer, organ and pedals on three tracks.

Concurrently with his solo work, Knox co-founded the Toronto duo Analytica with David Lush. Analytica released two LPs on Ice Machine Records: the self-titled Analytica (IMach003, 2020) — which includes a cover of a Vince Clarke track (B1, “Reason Man”) — and Strategy Of Tension (iMach008, 2024), a minimal electronics LP. Ice Machine Records is a sublabel of Suction Records.
