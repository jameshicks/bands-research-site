Andy Mackay

British musician (saxophone, oboe, synthesizer). Born Andrew Edwin Mackay, 23 July 1946, Lostwithiel, Cornwall. Core member of Roxy Music from 1971 through their 1982 breakup and subsequent reunion tours. Discogs artist ID: 267770.

Overview

Mackay’s saxophone and oboe playing were defining elements of Roxy Music’s sound throughout the band’s existence. He also contributed some songwriting credits (Country Life, Siren). After Roxy’s 1976 hiatus, he released solo material and formed The Explorers (2) with Phil Manzanera.
