Overview

Úlfur Eldjárn is an Icelandic musician whose output ranges from teen pop and electronic music through jazz, classical, and avant-garde. Though best known as a member of the synth ensemble Apparat Organ Quartet, he has also performed with Kósý, Trabant, Kanada, and Funkstrasse across a career stretching back to the mid-1990s. Alongside his recording work, he has composed extensively for theatre, television, and film, including the documentary series Opnun on Icelandic visual art and the internationally released film InnSæi. His most frequent collaborator on record is Jóhann Jóhannsson with 25 shared credits, and his broader network connects him to Amiina, Bang Gang, Bardi Johannsson, and Björk.
