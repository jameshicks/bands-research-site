Overview

Treiops Treyfid is a multi-instrumentalist — guitar, bass, and vocals — whose recorded output runs from a single late-1980s credit through a concentrated run of 1990s releases, with one further entry in the 2010s. Their primary association is with the Washington, D.C. indie/experimental band Pitchblende, with whom they share more credits than any other act; Scott DeSimon, Charles Bennington, A. Justin Chearno, and Patrick Gough are the tightest recurring collaborators. They also appear as a member of Technostress and Triptic Of A Pastel Fern. Across the catalogue — indie rock, art rock, experimental, and abstract — labels include Jade Tree, Poison Plant, Teenbeat, and a single Dischord Records credit, placing Treyfid squarely in the D.C. and adjacent underground scenes of the 1990s.
