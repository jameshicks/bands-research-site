Laurie-Rae Chamberlain

British multi-media visual artist, active mid-1970s and 1980s. Discogs artist ID: 684232. Name variations: L. R. Chamberlain, Laurie Rae Chamberlain, Laurie Rae Chamberlaine, Laurie Ray Chamberlain, Laurie-Rae Chamberlaine. British Museum collection entry: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/term/BIOG196711

Overview

Laurie-Rae Chamberlain was a British multi-media artist who specialised in xerography (photocopier-based image-making) as a fine-art and commercial practice. Her work sits at the intersection of post-punk DIY visual culture and the early 1980s London avant-garde. She is best known for her xerographic sleeve art on releases clustered around David Cunningham’s Piano label and its associated circle — most prominently the The Flying Lizards debut LP (Virgin, 1979), This Heat’s Deceit (Rough Trade, 1981), and the Michael Nyman / Greenaway soundtrack The Draughtsman’s Contract (Charisma, 1982).

Beyond xerography she is also credited as an engineer on This Heat’s Health And Efficiency (1980) and its 1998 reissue, suggesting hands-on involvement at Cold Storage studio during the band’s recording sessions, not merely a visual contributor. She additionally worked in photography, credited for still photography on Adam and the Ants’ “Cartrouble” single (1980) and electrophotography on releases by Mama’s Boys and The Comsat Angels (both 1984). Her work for the Nyman/Greenaway compilation (Venture, 1989) extended her xerographic contribution to that body of work into the late 1980s.

The Discogs record also shows a 2010 RPM retrodisc reissue of the Flying Lizards catalogue explicitly crediting her original xerographic sleeve work for both the debut LP and Fourth Wall, confirming the visual identity she established for those records has been consistently attributed to her in retrospective contexts.

Credits Notes

  • Engineer credits (This Heat): On Health And Efficiency (1980) and its 1998 reissue, Chamberlain is listed alongside This Heat’s regular Cold Storage engineers (Chris Blake, Chris Gray, Geoffrey Zipper, Jack Balchin, Peter Bullen, Phil Clarke). This is not a Chamberlain-as-artist credit — she participated in the actual recording sessions as an engineer.
  • Electrophotography (1984): The Mama’s Boys and Comsat Angels credits are listed as “Photography By [Electrophotography]” — a term that describes photocopier/xerographic process applied to photographic imagery rather than conventional photography.
  • 2006 reissue (Out Of Cold Storage): Her xerographic artwork for Deceit is re-credited here specifically as “Artwork [Colour Xerography]”, confirming continuity with the original 1981 sleeve attribution.
  • 2010 reissue: Credited twice on the Flying Lizards / Fourth Wall compilation — once for each original album sleeve — making the attribution explicit and distinct from David Cunningham’s separate sleeve credits.

Connections

  • David Cunningham — central recurring collaborator; produced all Flying Lizards and Nyman/Piano releases she worked on; co-credited on Flying Lizards sleeves
  • The Flying Lizards — artwork on debut LP (1979), Move On Up (1980), Fourth Wall (1981), Lovers & Other Strangers (1981); work re-credited on 2010 reissue
  • This Heat — engineer on Health And Efficiency (1980); artwork on Deceit (1981); re-credited on Out Of Cold Storage (2006) and Health & Efficiency / Repeat (1998)
  • Michael Nyman — xerographic artwork on self-titled Piano LP (1981, layout credit), The Draughtsman’s Contract (1982), and Nyman/Greenaway Soundtracks (1989)
  • Piano — the David Cunningham-run label that anchored the Flying Lizards / Nyman circle where most of her work was centred
  • Adam And The Ants — still photography on “Cartrouble” single (Do It Records, 1980)
  • The Comsat Angels — electrophotography on “You Move Me (One Good Reason)” single (Jive, 1984)
  • Mama’s Boys — electrophotography on debut LP (Jive, 1984)
