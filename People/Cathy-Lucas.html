Biography

Cathy Lucas is a London-based musician, producer, and composer active across a broad range of indie, experimental, and electronic contexts. She is best known as a founding member and the principal creative force behind Vanishing Twin, the London experimental pop group she has led since 2015. She also records under the alias Orlando (32).

Early career — Fanfarlo and My Sad Captains (2005–2014)

Lucas’s recorded career began in the mid-2000s as a member of two UK indie bands. Fanfarlo (Discogs ID: 547158) — a London indie-pop group also including Simon Aurell, Leon Beckenham, Justin Finch, Amos Memon, and Mark West (2) — released a string of singles on Fortuna Pop! and Moshi Moshi Records before signing to Atlantic / Canvasback, releasing Reservoir (2009) and Rooms Filled With Light (2012). Lucas is credited as written-by on Fanfarlo material and performed across both albums, contributing violin and other parts. The band’s final releases (Let’s Go Extinct, 2014, New World Records) conclude this phase.

In parallel, Lucas was a member of My Sad Captains (Discogs ID: 953228), a London indie trio completed by Leon Dufficy and Dan Davis (13), who released on Stolen Recordings (2009–2011) and Bella Union (2014–2017).

During this period she also contributed violin as a session player: she appears on three tracks (5, 6, 8) of The Horrors’ fourth album Luminous (XL Recordings, 2014), alongside the band and producer Craig Silvey.

Orlando (32) — experimental electronics (2014–2015)

Under the alias Orlando (32) (Discogs ID: 4739255), Lucas released two records on Spanish cassette label Ram Tapes:

  • Earth Moon Earth And Other Round Trips (2014, Ram Tapes) — co-credited to Orlando (32) & Tom Furse (of The Horrors; Discogs ID: 1552892); real name Thomas Furse Cowan.
  • Play Time. Music For Video Games (2015, Ram Tapes, Spain) — split LP co-credited to Orlando (32) / Tomaga; additional credits include Israel Pinilla and Pablo Peiró on the Tomaga side.

The Orlando (32) alias is described on Discogs as “experimental electronic pop.” No further releases under this alias are documented in the DB.

Vanishing Twin (2015–present)

Lucas is the founder and core member of Vanishing Twin (Discogs ID: 5229537), a London-based group whose shifting membership has included Valentina Magaletti (drums), Susumu Mukai (bass/synthesizer; also known as Zongamin, Discogs ID: 530035), Elliott Arndt (Discogs ID: 5312075), and Philip Baerwalde / Man From Uranus (Discogs ID: 3647557). On the 2024 Sub Pop single Life Drummer / Inarisan / Non Guardare, the credited lineup is Lucas, Mukai, and Magaletti.

Lucas takes primary writing, production, and mixing responsibilities across the Vanishing Twin catalog. She co-produced Choose Your Own Adventure (2016, Soundway) with Malcom Catto (Discogs ID: 87301), and is sole producer credit on The Age Of Immunology (2019, Fire Records), where she also mixed tracks A1, A5, and B1 (Catto mixed the remainder). On Ookii Gekkou (2021, Fire Records), she mixed track B2. She also co-credited as Written-By and Recorded By on the EP In Piscina ! (2020, Fire Records).

Lucas additionally designed artwork for the debut EP Choose Your Own Adventure (2016), alongside Magaletti. The Choose Your Own Adventure design credit reflects her involvement in the band’s visual identity from the outset.

Innerspace Orchestra

Lucas is listed as a member of Innerspace Orchestra (Discogs ID: 4922991), alongside Rose Elinor Dougall (Discogs ID: 1400097) and Tom Cowan (2) / Tom Furse (Discogs ID: 1273915 / 1552892). No releases for this group are documented in the Discogs DB.

Collaboration with Tomaga and guest credits (2021)

Lucas contributed to Intimate Immensity (2021, Hands In The Dark), the posthumous final album by Tomaga. She is credited on track 4 as Written By, Performer, Producer, and Vocals — the track’s sole songwriting credit alongside Tomaga members Tom Relleen and Valentina Magaletti on the rest of the album. The connection between Lucas and Tomaga was already established through the Play Time split (2015) and through the shared Vanishing Twin membership of Magaletti.
