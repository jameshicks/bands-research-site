Andrew Halbreich

Harmonica, guitar, vocals. New York City. Discogs artist ID: 1243438. Real name: Andrew David Halbreich. Name variations: A. Halbreich, A.D. Halbreich, Halbreich.

Overview

Andrew Halbreich was a multi-instrumentalist active in New York City’s post-punk scene from 1980 to 1984, playing harmonica, guitar, and vocals in two successive groups anchored by Robert Poss and Ron Spitzer: Tot Rocket And The Twins (1980–1981) and Western Eyes (2) (1984). These groups were the direct precursors to Band Of Susans, and the Halbreich–Poss–Spitzer triangle is among the earliest documented iterations of that circle. All three participated in all three Tot Rocket recordings and in the Western Eyes LP; Halbreich did not continue into Band Of Susans itself.

Beyond his band work, Halbreich contributed cover artwork to the John Valby comedy LP Dr. Dirty In Pixieland (1982, Gemsbok GB-644), and by 2014 he is credited as Music Administrator on the Machete Kills soundtrack LP (Music On Vinyl MOVLP1019), issued on Robert Rodriguez’s soundtrack. He also founded and runs Shrub Music, an Austin, Texas publishing company, suggesting a later move from NYC to Texas.
