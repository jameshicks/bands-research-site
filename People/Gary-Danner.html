Overview

Gary Danner is a German musician and producer, primarily known as a founding member of Station Rose, the Frankfurt-based art-music project he has run alongside Elisa Rose — their 16 shared-release collaboration dwarfs any other in his catalogue. Active since 1981, his output spans experimental, psychedelic, ambient, techno, and synth-pop across three decades, with the bulk of his recorded work concentrated in the 1990s. His label history links him to the Düsseldorf avant-garde via Ata Tak and to Der Plan members Frank Fenstermacher and Kurt Dahlke, both of whom share seven releases with him; later work has appeared on Bureau B and Ata Tak’s successor context. Outside Station Rose he has been part of Die Nervösen Vögel, Gary Texas, and The Vogue.
