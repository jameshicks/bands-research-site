Matt Sweeney

American guitarist, vocalist, and producer. Discogs artist ID: 247495. Based in New York. Known for hard-rock guitar work, a long-running duo with Will Oldham, and session/production credits spanning indie rock, country, and hip-hop.

Career Overview

Matt Sweeney came up in the New York indie-rock underground of the early 1990s. His first significant band was Wide Awake, who released Last American Virgin (1989, Twin/Tone Records) and Laid (1991, City Slang / EFA) — Sweeney is credited on guitar and vocals on both. By 1993 he had co-founded Chavez with Clay Tarver, James Lo, and Scott Masciarelli, the band that became his defining early-career statement.

After Chavez, Sweeney’s work diversified into session playing, production, and a series of collaborative projects — most prominently the long-running duo Superwolf with Will Oldham (as Bonnie “Prince” Billy), membership in Zwan, appearances on The Desert Sessions, and, more recently, The Hard Quartet. As a session guitarist he has appeared on records by Johnny Cash, Adele, the Dixie Chicks (as The Chicks), Queens Of The Stone Age-adjacent projects, Calexico, and many others.

Zwan (2001–2003)

Discogs artist ID: 277868. Supergroup formed around Billy Corgan (Smashing Pumpkins). Members included Sweeney (guitar, vocals), Corgan, David Pajo, Paz Lenchantin, and Jimmy Chamberlin. Released Mary Star Of The Sea (2003, Martha’s Music / Reprise Records). Sweeney co-wrote several tracks and was credited on arranged-by for orchestral passages. See Zwan.

Discogs artist ID: 105346. Duo of Matt Sweeney and Will Oldham (as Bonnie “Prince” Billy). Named after their debut album.

The Desert Sessions (2019)

Discogs artist ID: 283792. Josh Homme’s long-running Joshua Tree session series. Sweeney appeared on Desert Sessions Vol. 11 & 12 (2019, Matador / Rekords Rekords).

Endless Boogie (2004–present, associate member)

Discogs artist ID: 654261. New York extended jam outfit founded by Paul Major. Sweeney is listed as a member alongside Spencer Sweeney, Andrew W.K., Harry Druzd, Jesper Eklow, and others. He appears as a performer on Full House Head (2010, “Key Additional Action” guitar), Long Island (2013), Vibe Killer (2017, “Endless Boogie Be”), and on the Jerome / Rolex split 7” (2020, Wharf Cat Records, as “EB Collective,” alongside Stephen Malkmus). He co-produced Long Island with Jesper Eklow and co-produced Admonitions (2021) with Isak Sjöholm and Eklow.

Probot (2025)

Discogs artist ID: 164773. Dave Grohl’s metal project. Sweeney appeared on the 2025 reissue/version (Southern Lord) credited with additional guitar.

The Hard Quartet (2024)

Discogs artist ID: 14902339. Supergroup: Sweeney, Stephen Malkmus (Pavement/Jicks), Jim White (2) (Dirty Three), and Emmett Kelly (Cairo Gang / Ty Segall). Debut LP The Hard Quartet (2024, Matador). Sweeney co-produced and contributed artwork.

See The Hard Quartet.
