Josh Graham

Visual artist, guitarist, multi-instrumentalist, and director based in the US. Discogs artist ID: 419645. Aliases: IIVII (Discogs ID: 4604542) and Suspended In Light (Discogs ID: 2293681) — two separate Discogs entries, both listing realname “Josh Graham.”

Overview

Josh Graham operates simultaneously across music and visual art. He is best known as the long-running live visual artist and touring guitarist for Neurosis, and as co-founder of two significant post-metal projects: Red Sparowes (Los Angeles, 2003) and Battle of Mice (New York, c. 2005). Both projects were anchored in the Neurot Recordings orbit and connected Graham to a tight network of musicians around the Bay Area and NYC heavy underground.

His visual work — operating under the name Suspended In Light (suspendedinlight.com) — spans album artwork, art direction, film production, and design across a remarkably wide range of clients: in addition to the underground metal world, he has done major commercial design work for Soundgarden (multiple reissues and live releases), Megadeth (Dystopia, 2016), Chris Cornell, Eagles of Death Metal, Wolfmother, Weezer, and Hole (Celebrity Skin reissue, 2025), all primarily through UMe/Universal.

Since 2015 Graham has been the primary creative force behind the solo electronic/ambient project IIVII, releasing records on Belgian label Consouling Sounds. An earlier ambient project, The (Fallen) Black Deer (with Greg Burns (2)), released one record on UK label Latitudes in 2008. He also remained active in the drone/experimental project MGR (led by Mike Gallagher (2) of Isis) and in Blood & Time, a supergroup with Neurosis members Scott Kelly and Noah Landis.

Connections

  • Red Sparowes — original guitarist and visual artist; wrote conceptual narrative for Every Red Heart Shines Toward The Red Sun alongside Julie Christmas
  • Neurosis — touring guitarist and live visual media artist; this connection explains Graham’s access to Neurot Recordings and the tightly linked post-metal underground
  • Battle of Mice — co-founded with Julie Christmas; Graham wrote all music and designed all releases
  • A Storm of Light — his primary solo/band project 2008–2018; co-produced with Joel Hamilton
  • IIVII — ambient/electronic alias active from 2015, exclusively on Consouling Sounds (Belgium)
  • Suspended In Light — visual art brand running parallel to all musical activity; used for design credits across underground and major commercial releases
  • MGR — member alongside Mike Gallagher (2) (Isis)
  • Blood & Time — supergroup with Neurosis members Scott Kelly and Noah Landis
  • The (Fallen) Black Deer — ambient duo with Greg Burns (2)
  • Joel Hamilton — key production collaborator across Battle of Mice and A Storm of Light
  • Soundgarden — major commercial design client via UMe; Graham designed/directed visuals for the full Soundgarden catalog reissue campaign (2010–2019) and original work including the King Animal (2012) album cover
