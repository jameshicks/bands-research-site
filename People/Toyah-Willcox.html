Overview

Toyah Ann Willcox is an English singer, actress, and broadcaster. She fronted the Birmingham post-punk band Toyah (3) from 1977 to 1983 before continuing under her own name. Her peak commercial period ran from 1981 to 1983: she scored 13 UK Top 40 singles, including “It’s a Mystery” and “I Want to Be Free,” and multiple charting albums on Safari Records. Post-band she moved through EG Records/Virgin, self-produced and co-produced solo work, and collaborated closely with members of the King Crimson orbit, notably her husband Robert Fripp and Trey Gunn. She has written two books, appeared in over 40 stage plays, made 10 feature films, and presented hundreds of television programmes.

Notable production credit: Toyah Willcox co-produced Ieya (1980) and is listed as producer/arranger on The Blue Meaning (1980), indicating an early hands-on role in the studio beyond vocals and lyrics.

Producer on Anthem (1981) was Nick Tauber (who also worked with Japan, Ultravox, and others). Engineer was Phil Harding.

The Changeling (1982) was produced by Steve Lillywhite, marking a stylistic shift toward a more produced new-wave sound.

Solo: EG Records / Portrait / Virgin (1985–1991)

After the band dissolved in 1983, Toyah continued under her own name, signing to EG Records (an imprint distributed by Virgin). This era includes more self-directed and co-produced work.

Ophelia’s Shadow (1991) is the most closely connected album to the King Crimson orbit. It was co-written and produced by Toyah, with Trey Gunn and Paul Beavis as core players and Robert Fripp contributing guitar and mixing to “Brilliant Day” and “Lords Of The Never Known.” Keith Tippett plays piano on “Lords Of The Never Known.”

The 1986 appearance on the King Crimson-related The Lady Or The Tiger? (Editions EG, master ID: 1007) credits Toyah Willcox as “Voice.”

Sunday All Over The World (1988–1991)

Sunday All Over The World (Discogs ID: 670919) was a project formed from 1988–1991, originally touring as Fripp Fripp in 1988, then renaming. Members:

  • Robert Fripp — guitar
  • Toyah Willcox — vocals
  • Trey Gunn — Chapman Stick, bass
  • Paul Beavis — drums
