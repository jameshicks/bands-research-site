Connie Burg

Guitarist, vocalist, multi-instrumentalist. Also known as: China Burg (stage name during Mars), Don Burg, Lucy Hamilton (the name under which she is indexed on Discogs). Grew up in Ohio. Discogs ID: 101941 (listed as Lucy Hamilton, realname Connie Burg; aliases: China Burg, Constanza Burg, Don Burg).

Attended Eckerd College, St. Petersburg, Florida, where she met Mark Cunningham and Arto Lindsay. All three relocated to New York together in 1974 — part of the Eckerd College → No Wave pipeline that also included Bobby Swope and Liz Swope.

Core member of Mars (founded December 1975 as China, renamed 1977). Had never played guitar before joining; developed a wholly original atonal style that became a defining element of Mars’ sound. The band name “China” — which gave her the stage name China Burg — was abandoned in 1977 when a commercial act held the name.

Mars formed after she met Nancy Arlen at a Lucinda Childs dance workshop; she and Arlen then introduced Cunningham and Sumner Crane, and the four formed the band immediately.

Later played bass clarinet — learned it for Sumner Crane’s opera John Gavanti, then continued in subsequent projects.
