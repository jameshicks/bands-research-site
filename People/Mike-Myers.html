Mike Myers

Comedian, actor, writer, and film producer, born May 25, 1963, Scarborough, Ontario, Canada. Discogs artist ID: 461114 (credited as “Mike Myers (3)”; Discogs alias “Austin Powers”). Real name: Michael John Myers.

Best known as an SNL cast member (1989–1995), creator of the Wayne’s World and Austin Powers characters, and a recurring presence across his own film soundtracks both as a performer and executive producer. His Discogs footprint is surprisingly substantial: track-level vocal credits, songwriting, soundtrack EP production, Broadway cast appearances, and a credited role on a major documentary film.

Overview

Myers came up through Toronto’s Second City improv scene before joining Saturday Night Live in 1989. His two major character creations — Wayne Campbell (Wayne’s World) and Austin Powers — both generated substantial music spin-offs: Wayne’s World made him a co-writer on an official 12” single; Austin Powers gave rise to Ming Tea, the fictional in-universe band that made actual studio recordings.

On Discogs, he appears under three band groupings: Ming Tea (Austin Powers franchise band), Saturday Night Live Cast (ensemble credit), and Wayne & Garth (the Wayne’s World duo, with Dana Carvey).

Austin Powers: International Man Of Mystery — Original Soundtrack (1997)

Myers is credited as Executive-Producer [Soundtrack Album], co-credited with Demi Moore, Jennifer Todd, Suzanne Todd, and Mitchell Leib.

Myers credited as Voice [Uncredited] on tracks 2–6, 8, and 9. The single is a promo-only novelty/dialogue release tied to the first Austin Powers film. The main BBC track and eight Austin Powers character dialogue/comedy tracks make up the disc.

Various — Maverick, cat# 9362-47348-2 (Europe, CD)

A separate “More Music” volume (Maverick, 1999; Discogs release ID: 1569963, Japan pressing) also carries Myers as Executive-Producer [Soundtrack].

Austin Powers In Goldmember (Music From & Inspired By The Motion Picture) (2002)

Various — Maverick, cat# 9362-48310-2 (Europe, CD)
Discogs release ID: 946334

Myers credited as Executive-Producer [Soundtrack]. Ming Tea returns here with “Daddy Wasn’t There” (track 11) — credited as Ming Tea feat. Austin Powers. Myers is Written-By on that track, co-written with Matthew Sweet (who also produced it). Myers also appears as primary artist on several dialogue/vocal tracks: “9 To 5,” “Stop Hitting Yourself (Dialog),” “More Than Words” (with Manu Narayan), “The Joker,” “My Name Is Guru Pitka (Dialog),” “Guru Lineage (Dialog).”

Various — Angel Records, cat# 33757 (US, 2xCD)

Myers credited as Performer alongside Bernadette Peters, Carol Burnett, Nathan Lane, Lillias White, and Tom Wopat. Sherry! was a short-lived Broadway musical based on the George S. Kaufman / Moss Hart play The Man Who Came to Dinner; music by Laurence Rosenthal, lyrics by James Lipton.

Bohemian Rhapsody (2019)

Various / 20th Century Fox / Regency — Discogs release ID: 13275012
