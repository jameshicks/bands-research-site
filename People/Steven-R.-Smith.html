Steven R. Smith

Steven R. Smith

Oct 22, 20268 min read

Steven R. Smith

Discogs ID: 99956 Born: Fullerton, California Based: San Francisco, CA (later Los Angeles, CA) Active: 1995–present Genres: Experimental, psych-folk, drone, dark ambient, avant-garde

Multi-instrumentalist, composer, and visual artist (woodcuts) associated with the SF Bay Area experimental and psych-folk underground. Operating almost entirely under a succession of solo project names since the mid-1990s, Smith is one of the most prolific figures in the American underground of his era. His primary label home has been Soft Abuse since the late 2000s; earlier work appeared on Jewelled Antler and Emperor Jones.

Instruments: electric guitar, baritone guitar, hurdy gurdy, synth (Moog), percussion, piano, electric piano, organ, fiddle (spike fiddle), psaltery (baritone psaltery), violin, oud, cello (spike cello), bouzouki, harmonium, harpsichord, melodica, banjo, flute, tape, accordion, bulbul tarang, bass, drums, bowed cymbal, timpani, xaphoon (reeds), bells, field recordings.

Name variations: S.R. Smith, S. R. Smith, SR Smith, SRS Website: worstward.com / worstward.bandcamp.com

Career Overview

Early Period — Autopia / Darla / 3 Acre Floor (1995–2001)

Smith was active in the Bay Area from the mid-1990s, releasing on the micro-label Autopia (self-distributed) and through Darla Records as a solo artist and as a member of Mirza (alongside Glenn Donaldson, Brian Lucas, and Mark Williams). Mirza’s Anadromous (Bliss Out Vol. 7) (Darla, 1997) and Iron Compass Flux (Darla, 1998) are among the earliest documents of this scene. Solo work from this period includes the cassette Log The Man Dead (Autopia, 1995), Autumn Is The End (Autopia, 1997; reissued Darla 1998), From Ashes Come (3 Acre Floor, 1999), and Slate Branches (3 Acre Floor / Little Brother Records, 2000). Smith also made woodcut artwork for several releases in this period, a practice he continued for years.

He was also a founding member of Thuja, the long-running SF drone-folk collective that included Glenn Donaldson, Loren Chasse, Greg Bianchini, and Rob Reger. Thuja released on tUMULt, Jewelled Antler, and Important Records.

Jewelled Antler Era — Hala Strana (2002–2007)

Smith became part of the Jewelled Antler collective centred around San Francisco in the early 2000s. His key release from this period is Kohl (Jewelled Antler #15, 2002) — a solo guitar and woodcut record that included a piece written by Roscoe Mitchell (track 7). He also released Antimony (Foxglove, 2004) and Crown Of Marches (Catsup Plate, 2005) in this window.

His main project during this era was Hala Strana (Discogs ID: 363491) — a vehicle for dense Balkan- and East European-inflected folk music played on an array of acoustic instruments. Hala Strana’s self-titled debut appeared on Emperor Jones in 2003 alongside Fielding, a 2xCDr on Jewelled Antler the same year. Heave The Gambrel Roof (Music Fellowship, 2007) marked the project’s peak before Smith pivoted to new aliases.

The Anchorite (Important Records, 2006) was a key solo release from this window, reissued on Root Strata in 2008.

Ulaan Series — Soft Abuse Era (2008–2018)

From 2008 onwards Smith concentrated on a family of “Ulaan” project names (all Mongolian/Central Asian geographic or faunal references), releasing predominantly on Soft Abuse and his own imprint Worstward Recordings.

Ulaan Khol (Discogs ID: 1067934) was the primary vehicle from 2008–2015. The project produced a sequence of six albums on Soft Abuse — I (SAB 026, 2008), II (SAB 032, 2008), III (SAB 038, 2010), La Catacomb (SAB 054, 2011), Salt (SAB 076, 2015) — plus Ending/Returning (Immune, 2013). The sound is predominantly slow-moving, reverb-heavy electric guitar drone with percussion and occasional organ.

Ulaan Markhor (Discogs ID: 2736227) ran concurrently from 2012–2018, with the self-titled debut (SAB 056, 2012) and Spiral Horns, Black Onions, Et Al. (SAB 069, 2014) on Soft Abuse, and two further releases on Worstward Recordings. Helm (Soft Abuse SAB 087, 2018) was the last entry.

Ulaan Passerine (Discogs ID: 3302388) and Ulaan Janthina (Discogs ID: 8078055) are smaller project names from this cluster. Ulaan Passerine released on Brave Mysteries (2013) and Worstward Recordings; Ulaan Janthina produced Ulaan Janthina (Part I) and (Part II) on Worstward Recordings in 2020.

Other releases from this period: Westering (Important Records, 2009), The Line Across (Alt.Vinyl, 2010) — a split/collaboration with Gareth Davis — Floor Of The Sky (Burnt Toast Vinyl, 2011), Hymnal (Cassauna, 2016) with Dirk Serries.

Solo and Late Period — Soft Abuse / Worstward / Eiderdown (2019–present)

From 2019, Smith released primarily under his own name and through Worstward Recordings alongside continued output on Soft Abuse. The Soft Abuse solo run includes A Sketchbook Of Endings (SAB 090, 2019), Spring (SAB 100, 2022), and Olive (SAB 110, 2024).

He has also collaborated with the 43 Odes collective (Discogs ID: 7259076), contributing guitar, percussion, psaltery, fiddle, cello, bulbul tarang, and Moog to the group’s releases on Eiderdown Records: 43 Odes (2019) and HWN UN AMN (2023).

Other late-period credits include: In The Spires (Cold Moon Records, 2021), In Vivo (IIKKI, 2021, with psaltery and cello), The Air’s Chrysalis Chime (Cardinal Fuzz / Feeding Tube Records, 2022, drums).

Scene Connections

  • Jewelled Antler collective — Smith was a core participant in this early-2000s SF collective alongside Glenn Donaldson, Thuja, The Blithe Sons, and others. The collective operated as a micro-community distributing CDr and cassette works.
  • Soft Abuse roster — Smith’s long-term label home is a nexus of the US underground folk/experimental scene; other key artists on the label include Pumice, Frog Eyes, Donovan Quinn, and Blackout Beach.
  • Thuja — The SF drone-folk collective he co-founded with Glenn Donaldson is one of the era’s most distinctive collaborations.
  • Mirza — Earlier SF group also featuring Glenn Donaldson; documented on Darla Records’ “Bliss Out” compilation series.
  • Worstward Recordings — Smith’s own label (WST001 onward), used from 2011 for solo and project releases.
