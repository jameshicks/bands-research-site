Gary Siperko

Guitarist, composer, and multi-instrumentalist. Discogs artist ID: 1919265. Associated with the Cleveland avant-rock scene; member of Pere Ubu, Rocket From The Tombs (reunion lineup), Legendary Shack Shakers, and The Mofos. Active as a session player and touring musician from at least the 2000s onward.

Name variations on credits: G. Siperko, Siperko.

Overview

Gary Siperko is a professional touring and recording guitarist whose stylistic range covers rock, classical, blues, jazz, garage, rockabilly, country, roots, Latin, and experimental music. His primary association is with the post-punk/avant-rock axis centered on David Thomas (2) and the Cleveland underground. He joined Pere Ubu and appeared on their mid-2010s through early 2020s records, and he is also credited on Rocket From The Tombs’ Black Record (2015), the reunion album of the proto-punk band that preceded Pere Ubu. Separately, he has toured and recorded with country-punk act Legendary Shack Shakers and contributed session work to Whiskey Daredevils releases.

According to his Discogs profile, Siperko has done session work with Mary Lorson of Madder Rose / St. Low, though no Discogs release captures that credit in the database.
