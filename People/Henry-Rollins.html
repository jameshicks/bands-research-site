Henry Rollins

Vocalist, writer, spoken word performer. Born Henry Garfield, Washington DC, 1961. Discogs artist ID: 18844.

Overview

Joined Black Flag as vocalist in 1981, replacing Dez Cadena. Present on most of the Rollins-era Black Flag records that carry Raymond Pettibon’s artwork: My War (1984), Slip It In (1984), Loose Nut (1985), In My Head (1985), The Process of Weeding Out (1985). After Black Flag dissolved (1986) formed Rollins Band. Also active as author and spoken word performer.

Solo debut Hot Animal Machine (1987, Texas Hotel Records, cat. TEXAS HOTEL 2) featured guitarist Chris Haskett, bassist Bernie Wandel, and drummer Mick Green (2); produced by Haskett and Rollins, engineered by Geoff Clout; cover art by Mark Mothersbaugh (DEVO). The spoken word record Big Ugly Mouth followed on Texas Hotel in 1987. Hot Animal Machine… Plus (Texas Hotel, 1988/1992) compiled the debut with the Drive By Shooting EP material. Solo spoken word album Sweatbox also on Texas Hotel (1989).

Appears on Mike Watt’s Ball-Hog Or Tugboat? (Columbia, 1995) — the cover of which carries a Pettibon ink drawing.

“Henrietta Collins” is a Rollins alias used for the pre-Rollins Band project Henrietta Collins And The Wifebeating Childhaters.
