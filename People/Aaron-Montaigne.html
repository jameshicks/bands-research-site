Overview

Aaron Montaigne Jones is a drummer and vocalist whose career is rooted in San Diego’s early-1990s post-hardcore scene. He played across several bands closely tied to Gravity Records — Heroin (3), Antioch Arrow, Dangerous Boys Club, Decapitated Lovers, and End Of The Line — with the bulk of his catalogue clustered in the 1990s. After a long quiet period, he resumed recording in the 2010s and 2020s, with releases appearing on Dais Records, Three One G, and Southern Lord. His most consistent collaborators across that span include Matt Anderson (2), Scott Bartoloni, and Ron Johnson (14).

Return to recording: 2009–2024 biographical narrative

Aaron Montaigne’s return to active recording in the 2010s was not connected to San Diego hardcore reunion activity. It followed two distinct new-band formations, both remote from San Diego.

Dangerous Boys Club (Portland, OR, 2009–c.2013) After his US Army service sobered him from a late-1990s heroin addiction, Montaigne settled in Portland, Oregon. There he reunited with Mac Mann — his former Antioch Arrow bandmate, subsequently of Get Hustle — and they co-founded Dangerous Boys Club around 2009. The full lineup added Mark Burden (bass; ex-Silentist / Glass Candy) and Sam Ott (electronic drums; Year Future). The band’s sound drew from Roxy Music, Suicide, the Misfits, Soul Side, and David Bowie — dark-wave / synth-punk territory far removed from the San Diego post-hardcore of the 1990s. DBC released VRIL in 2010 on Fast Weapons, then Pris in April 2013 on Dais Records (catalog DAIS 037). The 2013 Dais Records appearance (Pris) is this DBC record; Montaigne is credited on vocals. Three One G also promoted the band; a news post on the label’s site notes the connection between Aaron and DBC explicitly. [https://threeoneg.com/news/antioch-aaron-dbc] [https://www.discogs.com/release/4457321-DBC-Pris] [https://www.tinymixtapes.com/chocolate-grinder/premiere-dangerous-boys-club-pris]

Note: per a search-result snippet, DBC’s music was subsequently removed from the Dais Records website following controversial statements Montaigne made in interviews. This is unconfirmed from a primary source and should be treated as a weak/provisional claim.

VR SEX (Los Angeles, 2017–c.2022) VR SEX was formed in Los Angeles in 2017 by Andrew Clinco of Drab Majesty (performing as Noel Skum). Montaigne joined as a core member, performing vocals and drums under the alias Z. Oro; the third member was Brian Tarney of Guardian (as Mico Frost) on synths and electric bass. The debut LP Human Traffic Jam was written and demoed by Clinco in Athens, Greece in winter 2017, recorded at Figure 8 studios with producer Ben Greenberg, and released 10 May 2019 on Dais Records. Rough Dimension (2022, Dais / Three One G; Montaigne credited for additional vocals) followed; Hard Copy (2024, Dais; Montaigne as Performer) is the most recent release. [https://post-punk.com/drab-majestys-deb-demure-dons-the-guise-of-noel-scum-for-vr-sexs-debut-lp-human-traffic-jam/] [https://stereogum.com/2272237/vr-sex-fires-singer-band-now-on-hiatus/news]

2026 Antioch Arrow reunion (separate) Wikipedia’s Antioch Arrow article records a reunion beginning in 2026, with a Numero Group reissue of the complete discography and a compilation (Your Hearts Belong to Us) featuring unreleased material. This postdates the 2010s–2020s recording activity described above and is not a cause of it. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antioch_Arrow]

Summary of Dais Records thread Montaigne’s three Dais credits — Pris (2013, DBC), Rough Dimension (2022, VR SEX additional vocals), and Hard Copy (2024, VR SEX) — all flow from these two new-band formations in Portland and Los Angeles, not from any San Diego scene reunion.
