Overview

Matt Berninger (born February 13, 1971) is an American singer-songwriter who fronts and writes lyrics for the indie rock band The National, whose core collaborative circle — Aaron and Bryce Dessner, Bryan and Scott Devendorf, and producer Peter Katis — accounts for the bulk of his 416 Discogs credits. He expanded his output in 2014 by co-founding the side project EL VY with Brent Knopf of Ramona Falls and Menomena. In 2020 he released his debut solo album, Serpentine Prison. His catalogue clusters in the 2010s and 2020s across 4AD and Brassland, with lyricist Carin Besser a recurring collaborator throughout.
