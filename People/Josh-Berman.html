Josh Berman

American cornetist and composer based in Chicago. A central figure in the city’s post-2000 free jazz and experimental improvisation scene, Berman leads his own trio and ensemble (Josh Berman Trio, Josh Berman & His Gang) and appears as a sideman across a wide swath of Chicago improvised music, ranging from the Exploding Star Orchestra to Michael Zerang & The Blue Lights. His primary releases as a leader have come out on Delmark Records and Astral Spirits. Discogs artist ID: 531837. Name variations: Berman; Joshua Berman.
