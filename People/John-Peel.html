Born: 30 August 1939 in Heswall, on the Wirral Peninsula, near Liverpool, England, UK. Died: 25 October 2004 in Cuzco, Peru.

John Peel is best known as a British radio DJ - especially for his work on BBC Radio 1 & 4, and occasionally TV (e.g. Top Of The Pops).

After completing his military service in Britain in 1962, John began working for WRR radio in Dallas. At the time, with The Beatles success peaking, John used his Liverpool connections to claim acquaintance with the group. He was in Dallas when John F Kennedy was shot and was at the press conference just before Lee Harvey Oswald was shot.

For the next three years he moved to various radio stations in the US, among them KOMA in Oklahoma City and KMEN outside Los Angeles. He returned to Britain in 1967 and joined Radio London with the celebrated show The Perfumed Garden. However, John was most well-known after joining BBC Radio 1 from the beginning in 1967. His first show, Top Gear, established him with the late night slot, but through the years until his death, he moved around the schedules reasonably regularly, often much to his chagrin. During the early part of his career at Radio 1, he also co-founded Dandelion Records.

Overview

John Peel (born John Robert Parker Ravenscroft, 1939–2004) rose from US regional radio in the early 1960s to become the most widely credited DJ in British broadcasting history, hosting programmes on BBC Radio 1 from its launch in 1967 until his death in Cuzco, Peru. He co-founded Dandelion Records and was among the first British broadcasters to give sustained airtime to punk, reggae, and hip-hop before those genres crossed into the mainstream. His 494 Discogs credits — as presenter, host, narrator, producer, and occasional vocalist — are dominated by late-1960s folk-rock and blues-rock acts; his most heavily shared credits are with the full Pentangle lineup, Mike Vernon, Shel Talmy, and Gus Dudgeon. His recorded legacy is spread across BBC Transcription Services, Strange Fruit, and Cherry Red alongside his own Dandelion Records imprint.
