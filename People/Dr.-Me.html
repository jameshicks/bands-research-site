Dr. Me

Discogs artist ID: 2533526
Type: Design studio / creative collective
Origin: Manchester, UK
Also credited as: DR.ME, Dr.Me, DR+ME, D.R. ME, Dr Me, Dr-Me

Overview

DR.ME is a Manchester-based creative studio formed by Ryan Doyle (DR) and Mark Edwards (ME), collaborating across design, illustration, and art direction. From roughly 2011 onwards they served as the primary visual identity house for Tri Angle, designing sleeves for the majority of that label’s key releases. Their work spans dark electronic music, indie rock, and occasional high-profile pop commissions — most notably art direction for Mick Jagger’s 2017 single Gotta Get A Grip / England Lost (Polydor). As of Discogs records their output runs from 2009 to at least 2026.

The name is a portmanteau: Ryan Droyle + Mark Edwards = DR.ME.

Label Relationships

Tri Angle Records (primary client, 2012–2016)

Dr. Me designed the majority of Tri Angle’s mid-catalog — at minimum 8 confirmed releases, credited as “Design” or “Design, Artwork.” Their visual language (stark photography, minimal typography, heavy negative space) became strongly associated with Tri Angle’s identity during its critical peak years 2012–2016. The Tri Angle credits summary lists them at 20 releases across the label’s catalog, making them the label’s dominant design voice alongside Collin Fletcher (11 releases).

Memphis Industries (recurring client, 2012–2021)

Four confirmed credits across a decade: Dutch Uncles Fester (2012), Out Of Touch In The Wild (2013), O Shudder (2015), and a 2021 release. Memphis Industries (Discogs ID: 4729) is a London indie label; Dr. Me’s work with them runs parallel to and slightly overlapping their Tri Angle period.

Key Sleeves

Held — Holy Other (Tri Angle, 2012)

Holy Other’s debut album. Credits: Holy Other (written/produced), Dr. Me (design, credited as “D.R. ME”), Matt Colton (mastered), Riyo Nemeth (photography). One of Dr. Me’s earliest major profile credits and the first landmark sleeve in their Tri Angle run.

Excavation — The Haxan Cloak (Tri Angle, 2013)

The Haxan Cloak’s (Bobby Krlic) second album. Dr. Me credited as “Dr.Me” for design; Matt Colton mastered; Cody Cobb photographed. Widely regarded as one of Tri Angle’s definitive releases. 4 pressings documented.

Duga-3 — Evian Christ (Tri Angle, 2013)

Evian Christ (Joshua Leary). Dr. Me credited for “Design, Artwork” — one of two credits where they handled both roles. Mastered by Sebastian Gainsborough (Vessel).

Third Law — Roly Porter (Tri Angle, 2016)

Roly Porter’s compositionally formal ambient/orchestral album. Dr. Me design alongside photography by Suren Manbelyan and performance credits for Paul Jebanasam (guitar) and Cynthia Millar (ondes martenot). Matt Colton mastered.

Gotta Get A Grip / England Lost — Mick Jagger (Polydor, 2017)

The highest-profile single commission: Mick Jagger’s 2017 double A-side. Credited “Design, Art Direction” — Dr. Me’s only known major-label work for a legacy rock artist.

Acts Of Rebellion — Ela Minus (Domino, 2020)

Ela Minus’s debut album on Domino Recording Company. Dr. Me credited “Design, Layout.” Mastered by Heba Kadry, mixed by Marta Salogni.

Prism Of Pleasure — Elkka (Ninja Tune, 2024)

Elkka’s debut album on Ninja Tune. Dr. Me credited for “Design.” One of their most recent major-label commissions.
