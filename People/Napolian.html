Napolian

Real name: Ian Christian Evans

Origin: Los Angeles, CA, USA

Name variations: Napoleon; ナポリアン
Alias: Ian Evans (6) (Discogs ID: 2603502)

Electronic music producer and songwriter based in Los Angeles. Napolian made a series of eclectic releases across the 2010s moving from vaporwave-adjacent cassette experiments through R&B-inflected synth-pop, hallucinatory hip-hop/house hybrids, and eventually into more pop-oriented Warp Records territory. He passed away on June 30, 2021, at the age of 29.

Biography

Early Work — Self-Released and Beer On The Rug (2011)

Napolian first appeared in 2011 with a self-released digital EP, Guns & Synths (Not On Label), a four-track set tagged vaporwave — an early marker of the bedroom-electronic micro-scenes forming online at the time. The tracks featured guest vocalists Garfield (18) and Tairiq on the opening track.

That same year he co-released a split cassette with Computer Dreams on Beer On The Rug (BOTR007). The A-side collected four Napolian tracks (including “Hoobangin ‘96” and “Olympia”); the B-side belonged entirely to Computer Dreams. Mastering by Josh Thomas (2); design/layout by Paul Browning (2). The release was tagged Downtempo / Experimental / Vaporwave.

Software Recording Co. — Rejoice and Incursio (2012–2014)

The pivotal relationship of Napolian’s early career was with Software (2) (Software Recording Co.), the New York-based imprint operating under the Mexican Summer umbrella. His debut vinyl release was Rejoice (2012, SFT 009), a six-track 12” EP at 45 RPM. The lead track “False Memories” featured vocals from Garfield (18) and Tairiq; the EP’s credits list Napolian himself under his alias Ian Evans (6) as Producer and Composed By, with mastering by Paul Gold and artwork/layout by Christian Oldham (who records as Info Warrior and Megazord). Genre: Synth-pop.

Incursio (2014, SFT016) was his full-length debut — a 15-track CD on Software, stretching across Electronic, Hip Hop, House, and Experimental. Garfield (18) appeared again on “Principalities.” The expanded sonic palette showed Napolian moving away from purely atmospheric production toward hip-hop structure. Credits on Incursio also include engineers Niko The Ikon (2 shared releases), Paul Gold (mastering, 2 shared releases), Joe LaPorta / JL (2) (mastering, 2 shared releases), Farbod Kokabi, and Henrik von Sivers / Van Rivers.

Mr. Foolish with Airbird — Cascine (2015)

Mr. Foolish (2015, CSN067) was a co-billed LP with Airbird (Joel Ford) on Cascine. A limited edition vinyl LP, ten tracks across two sides. Genres: Electronic / Hip Hop; Styles: Abstract, Hip Hop, House, Leftfield. No additional production credits beyond the two primary artists are listed on Discogs. This was Napolian’s only Cascine release and his sole co-billed album.

Warp Records — Applaud (2019)

By 2019 Napolian had a track on Warp Records, the Sheffield electronic landmark. “Applaud” (WARPDD293D) was a single credited jointly to Yves Tumor, Hirakish, and Napolian — the three are all listed as written-by, with Yves Tumor and Hirakish sharing vocal/lyrics credits and Downplay (2) listed as producer. Genre: Electronic / Pop; Style: Indie Pop. The release was issued out of the UK.
