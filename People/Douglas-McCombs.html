Douglas McCombs

Bassist and guitarist. Chicago, IL. Discogs ID: 56273.

Co-founded Tortoise (as Mosquito, 1990) with John Herndon. Also member of Eleventh Dream Day (joined 1982), Brokeback (started 1999, initially solo then expanded), and Pullman (since 1997, with Bundy K. Brown). Worked briefly with The For Carnation in 1995.

Eleventh Dream Day Involvement

McCombs played bass on Eleventh Dream Day releases spanning 1987–2015 — essentially the full arc of the band’s recorded output after their self-released 1984 debut (on which he does not appear). He is a core band member, not a session player; the 2015 LP Works For Tomorrow (Thrill Jockey) credits him as “Eleventh Dream Day Is” alongside bassist role.
