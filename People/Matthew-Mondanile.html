Matthew Mondanile

American guitarist, songwriter, and producer. Born February 8, 1985, Ridgewood, New Jersey. Discogs artist ID: 1217376. Real name: Matthew A. Mondanile. Known for solo project Ducktails and as a former member of Real Estate (2). Also ran Future Sound Recordings.

Overview

Mondanile is the sole creative principal behind Ducktails, a project active from 2007 through at least 2023. He was simultaneously a member of the New Jersey jangle-pop band Real Estate (2), which he joined at the band’s formation (c. 2009) and left — was fired — in early 2016. His work spans home-recorded lo-fi cassette releases through full studio productions recorded in New York, Antwerp, and Athens, Greece.

Known name variations on releases: M. Mondanile, Matt Mondanile, Matthew A. Mondanile, Matthew Mondanile III, Matthew Mondanilee, Mondanile, Saint Matthew.

Departure from Real Estate

On 25 May 2016, Real Estate announced Mondanile had left to “focus his creative energy on Ducktails.” The real reason — withheld for over a year — was that he was fired in February 2016 following “allegations of unacceptable treatment of women.” In October 2017 Real Estate made a public statement confirming the firing; Spin published accounts from seven women with specific allegations. Ducktails shows were subsequently cancelled and Domino dropped him.

Sources: Billboard, Oct 2017 · Spin, Oct 2017 · Stereogum, Oct 2017

Greece Period

From early 2018, Mondanile split time between Antwerp, Belgium and Athens, Greece while writing Watercolors. Athens became his stated base; he described learning Greek and taking classical piano lessons. He recorded at Diskex studio in Athens with engineer Sergios Voudris (Voyage Limpid Sound). Watercolors liner notes list recording locations as Ridgewood NJ, Antwerp, and Athens (March 2018–May 2019). Subsequent releases Impressions (2021) and The Cradle (2023) are credited as Greece on Discogs. The Athens circle included drummer Kostas Stergiou, saxophonist Dimitris Karayiannis, vocalist Yani Martinelli, and mastering engineer Anestis Psaradakos.

Sources: Watercolors Bandcamp · Wikipedia (Ducktails), as of 2026-05-28
