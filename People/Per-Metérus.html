Per Metérus

Discogs ID: 2001664 Real name: Per Axel Metérus Also credited as: Pelle Metérus, P. Metérus, Pelle Meterus, Pelle Metréus, Pelle Metérnus, Pelle Méterus, Per Metréus, Per Metéus Born: 3 November 1934, Uppsala, Sweden Died: 4 December 2010, Sollentuna, Sweden Role: Graphic designer, layout artist, visual artist

Overview

Per Metérus was a Swedish graphic designer who worked primarily in the Swedish record industry from the mid-1960s through the late 1980s. His credits on Discogs span more than 280 release entries, overwhelmingly in the roles of “Layout” and “Design,” on releases issued by RCA Victor, Swedish Society Discofil, Telestar, Decca, and Viking (3). He is best known internationally for his cover and album design work on Lee Hazlewood’s Swedish-period records (1971–1976), particularly Requiem For An Almost Lady (1971) and 13 (1972), both on Viking (3). His designs for those albums have remained in print through reissues on Smells Like Records (1999) and Light In The Attic (2017), where his credit is reproduced in full.

Beyond the Hazlewood albums, Metérus was a workhorse of the Swedish mainstream LP industry: he handled layout for folk, jazz, classical, pop, children’s records, and compilation series across the major labels operating in Sweden. The sheer volume of his output — the DB shows “Layout” credits on roughly 220 release entries and dedicated “Design” credits on ~60 more — indicates a staff or semi-staff relationship with RCA’s Swedish operation and several other labels over at least two decades.

Era and Context

Metérus’s earliest Discogs credit is from 1965 (I Våra Hjärtan, Telestar). His output peaks through the 1970s, the decade when Swedish RCA and subsidiary labels like Viking (3) and Swedish Society Discofil were producing significant volumes of LP releases. Viking (3) was a sublabel of Grammofon AB Electra, which itself was the Swedish RCA licensee. This infrastructure explains why Metérus’s name appears on both the Viking (3) Hazlewood pressings and the subsequent RCA Victor Hazlewood albums (20th Century Lee, 1976).

He continued working into the late 1980s and sporadically into the 1990s. His last datable Discogs credit is a photography credit on Kaipa’s The Decca Years 1975–1978 compilation (Inside Out Music, 2005), where archive photos were used.

Lee Hazlewood Connection

Metérus’s most internationally cited work is the run of Hazlewood albums on Viking (3) and RCA Victor Sweden. He worked on all four of Hazlewood’s primary studio/solo releases during the artist’s Swedish residency period:

On 13, Torbjörn Axelman shot the front cover photograph and Bengt Af Geijerstam the back cover; Metérus handled the overall cover construction and design. On Requiem For An Almost Lady, the cover painting was by Ardy Strüwer; Metérus again handled the layout and design. The 20th Century Lee session also included photography by “Martin” (artist ID 50905 on Discogs — identity unclear).

Metérus is credited on a total of 21 unique releases where Lee Hazlewood is also credited, spanning original Swedish pressings through multiple reissues. His design credit has been preserved intact on the 1999 Smells Like Records reissues (SLR 038 / SLR 040) and the 2017 Light In The Attic editions (LITA 142 / LITA 162).

Photography Credits

Metérus is credited for photography on a handful of releases:

  • Dans I Sommarkväll (1982, RCA) — “Photography By [black&white], Layout”
  • Agrell / Linde / Carlstedt / Börtz / Larsson (1988, Swedish Society Discofil) — “Photography By”
  • The Decca Years 1975–1978 (Kaipa, 2005, Inside Out Music) — “Photography By” (archival)

This suggests he occasionally functioned as both photographer and designer on some Swedish Society Discofil projects.
