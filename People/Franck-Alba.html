Overview

Franck Alba (born Franck Jean Alba) is a French multi-instrumentalist whose credits span guitar, bass, and keyboards across indie rock, art rock, and experimental circles, with work dating back to 2003. He is best known as a member of Piano Magic, where his collaboration with the band’s central figure Glen Johnson is his most extensive — 42 shared releases across more than two decades. Beyond Piano Magic he has participated in Fireflies (5), Statues In Fog, and Theory Of Ghosts, with Second Language serving as his most consistent label home (10 releases, 2012–2026). His recurring partners — Jerome Tcherneyan, Alasdair Steer, and Angèle David-Guillou — point to a tightly networked post-rock and leftfield scene, and five shared credits with Andrew Weatherall add a further connection into the broader UK experimental underground.

Weatherall Connection

Alba’s five shared Discogs credits with Andrew Weatherall came through Nina Walsh, not through the Piano Magic orbit. The route was as follows:

Alba met Walsh through Antenna Studio’s ‘Cafe Thing’ sessions in Crystal Palace — a six-month run of four-hour improvised sets involving local poets and musicians — independent of his Piano Magic work. [source: https://louderthanwar.com/exclusive-new-video-from-fireflies/] The two formed Fireflies (5) together, releasing their first 7” (“Freeze Me”) in 2015 on Moine Dubh, the imprint that Walsh ran with Weatherall. Walsh had been Weatherall’s long-time creative partner since the late 1980s (co-running Sabres of Paradise and her Sabrettes label and club), and the pair reunited as the Woodleigh Research Facility from 2013 until Weatherall’s death in 2020.

Alba’s entry into the Weatherall orbit followed from his Fireflies partnership with Walsh:

  • He is credited on Weatherall’s 2016 solo album Convenanza (Rotters Golf Club) as performer and co-composer of “Ghosts Again”. [source: Discogs release credit (r8026977); personnel confirmed via search-result snippet — franckalba.com also lists Convenanza among his credits]
  • He contributed guitar to the Woodleigh Research Facility, performing live with the project. [source: https://franckalba.com/]
  • He is credited on WRF’s ALL IS NOT LOST (2021) — the track “Alchemy” (written by Walsh and Alba, guitar and bass by Alba). [source: https://woodleighresearchfacility.bandcamp.com/track/alchemy]
  • He collaborated with Walsh and poet Michael Smith on the WRF release Headland (December 2021), contributing “echoey delay” guitar textures. [source: https://woodleighresearchfacility.bandcamp.com/album/headland-michael-smith-nina-walsh-franck-alba]

There is no evidence that Piano Magic — the band itself or its core figure Glen Johnson — was the intermediary. The connection runs Alba → Antenna Studio improv scene → Walsh → Weatherall/WRF.

The label overlap visible in the vault (two Rotters Golf Club credits in Alba’s Labels table) reflects Fireflies and/or WRF releases on Weatherall’s imprint.
