Barry Harris (2)

Discogs ID: 277632

American jazz pianist. Born December 15, 1929, Detroit, Michigan. Died December 8, 2021, New York City.

One of the foremost bebop pianists and educators, Harris was closely associated with the Detroit school of bebop. He played piano on Benny Golson’s The Other Side Of Benny Golson (Riverside, 1959). Later renowned as a teacher through his Jazz Cultural Theatre in New York. 15 shared releases with Golson.
