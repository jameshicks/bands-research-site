James Murphy

Origin: New Jersey, USA Active: late 1990s–present

Producer, musician, and co-founder of DFA Records with Tim Goldsworthy. Front man and main creative force of LCD Soundsystem.

Discogs ID

153534 (James Jeremiah Murphy). Groups in Discogs: LCD Soundsystem (38948), The DFA (64961), Pony (2) (406320), Falling Man (3) (4285882), The Speedking Trio (262413).

Early Engineering Credits

Credited as Recorded By on June Of 44’s debut LP Engine Takes To The Water (Quarterstick Records, 1995, QS32CD) — one of the earliest known engineering credits for Murphy, predating LCD Soundsystem and DFA by nearly a decade. The band (Mueller, Meadows, Erskine, Scharin) were part of the Louisville/Quarterstick post-rock network.

Also credited as Recorded By on the Evergreen (5) self-titled LP (Hi-Ball Records, 1996, cat# HBLP-0996), alongside Nicolas Vernhes. Both credits are from roughly the same period of obscure New York engineering work.

Soulwax Connection

Soulwax remixed LCD Soundsystem’s “Daft Punk Is Playing At My House” in 2007 (DFA / EMI). Nancy Whang of LCD Soundsystem appeared on Soulwax’s Any Minute Now-era material (NY Excuse / Nite Version).

Arcade Fire

Co-produced and mixed several tracks on Reflektor (Sonovox / Merge, 2013), working alongside Markus Dravs. Mixed tracks 2-2 and 2-6; produced and recorded additional tracks. 16 shared Discogs release credits with Arcade Fire.

LCD Soundsystem

Full article: LCD Soundsystem (Discogs ID: 38948). Murphy is founder, primary songwriter, vocalist, drummer, and producer. Produced debut LCD Soundsystem (2005) as The DFA; took sole producer credit from This Is Happening (2010) onward. Co-mixed all three studio albums with Dave Sardy.

Other Discogs group credits: Pony (2) (406320), Falling Man (3) (4285882), The Speedking Trio (262413), The DFA production alias (64961).
