Austrian drummer and percussionist, born 1949 in Gutau, Austria. Founding member of Faust and one of the defining drummers of the Krautrock era. His drumming — combining motorik repetition with free jazz–influenced outbursts and a preference for metal percussion and industrial sound objects — has been described as “the motor and heartbeat of Faust’s sonic anarchy” (Deutschlandfunk Kultur). One of the very few krautrock musicians to have remained continuously active from the genre’s origins in the early 1970s through to the present. (Wikipedia: Werner “Zappi” Diermaier)

Overview

Diermaier grew up in Austria; his father was a musician who took him to rehearsals from age seven. In the late 1960s he moved into northern Germany’s underground music scene and formed the trio Campylognatus Citelli with Hans Joachim Irmler (keyboards) and Arnulf Meifert (percussion). Around 1969–70, producer and journalist Uwe Nettelbeck merged Campylognatus Citelli with the Hamburg group Nukleus — comprising Jean-Hervé Peron, Rudolf Sosna, and Gunther Wüsthoff — to form Faust for Polydor Records. The musicians were relocated to a former schoolhouse at Wümme, near Bremen, converted into a private recording studio.

After Faust’s dissolution circa 1975, Diermaier lived privately but remained musically active. He and Peron reunited Faust for concerts in 1990 and 1992, launching the band’s second period. In 1997 Peron departed; Diermaier remained with the Irmler-led lineup. In 2004 he had a falling-out with Irmler (“In 2004, I had trouble with Jochen Irmler. Jean-Hervé and me got together again”) and chose to reunite with Peron, co-founding the FaUSt faction (Discogs: Faust (7), ID 988814). Diermaier co-led that entity until 2021. From around 2022 he appeared on releases attributed to Faust (54) (ID 11519270), a Berlin-based configuration of Faust-adjacent musicians including Dirk Dresselhaus (Schneider TM), Gunther Wüsthoff, and Jochen Arbeit.
