Alex Sadkin

Producer and engineer. Key figure at Island Records’ Compass Point Studios in Nassau, Bahamas during the early 1980s. Discogs artist ID: 30415.

Overview

Sadkin was the defining in-house producer and engineer at Compass Point, working closely with Chris Blackwell on Island’s most commercially and critically significant early-1980s releases. His engineering and production work shaped the Compass Point sound — a hybrid of Caribbean rhythms, post-punk, and sophisticated studio craft.
