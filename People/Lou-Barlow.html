Singer/songwriter and bassist. 60 releases on JNR 2011–2022, primarily as solo artist and through Sebadoh, Folk Implosion, and Sentridoh. Warrants own “Lou Barlow Artist Enabler Club” subscription sublabel. 26 performance credits across JNR catalog including bass, guitar, vocals, drum machine, and electronics.

Folk Implosion

The Folk Implosion (Discogs ID: 297458) — Barlow + John Davis (2) (ID 251936), later expanded. Active 1993–2003, reformed 2022–present.

Also credited as Folk Implosion (ID 16648) — an earlier/alternate Discogs entity covering the same band’s pre-1996 catalog. Name variations on Discogs: N.F.I., NFI, New Folk Implosion, Volksweise.

Natural One (1995)

The single “Natural One” (London Records, LOCDP 382) appeared on the Kids (1995) film soundtrack. Written by Barlow and John Davis (2) (with Wally Gagel co-credited on track 1). Produced by Wally Gagel (track 1) and Tim O’Heir (tracks 2–4). Mastered by Bob Ludwig. Styles: Breaks, Downtempo, Trip Hop.

On the Kids soundtrack proper (release ID 139913), Barlow is credited both as Folk Implosion (with John Davis) and as Deluxx Folk Implosion (with John Davis, Bob Fay, Mark Perretta).
