Kai Blankenberg

Discogs ID: 279938 Location: Düsseldorf, Germany Roles: Mastering engineer; bassist/keyboardist in Subterfuge (5)

Mastering engineer and musician. Owner of Skyline Tonfabrik (Düsseldorf, founded 2009), one of Germany’s most active mastering facilities. Active as a mastering credit since at least 1993, with 600+ Discogs credits spanning indie, rock, electronic, metal, and pop — mostly German-market releases, with a significant body of work for Russian rock band Алиса. Separately, a performing member of Subterfuge (5), a Düsseldorf indie pop band active since 1991, where he plays bass and keyboards.

Skyline Tonfabrik

Skyline Tonfabrik GmbH (Behrenstrasse 62, 40233 Düsseldorf) is Blankenberg’s mastering studio. Discogs profile: founded 2009. The studio offers e-mastering, stem-mastering, test-mastering, audio restoration, and surround services.

Skyline Tonfabrik is the follow-up to an older Skyline Studios, Düsseldorf (Discogs ID 1317740) — described as a recording and mixing facility. Blankenberg’s earliest Discogs credits (1993–2008) predate the 2009 company; he operated out of the original Skyline Studios before founding Tonfabrik. The two should not be confused.

Subterfuge (5) — Musician

Subterfuge (5) is an indie pop band from Düsseldorf, active since 1991, described on Discogs as “indie pop from Düsseldorf, Germany.” Blankenberg plays bass, keyboards, and organ; also credited as producer and writer. His brother Tom Blankenberg is also in the band.
