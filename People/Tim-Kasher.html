Overview

Tim Kasher (born August 19, 1974) is an Omaha, Nebraska singer, guitarist, and producer, best known as the frontman of Cursive and The Good Life — both long-running fixtures of Saddle Creek Records. His catalogue spans indie rock, emo, post-hardcore, art rock, and folk, with the heaviest output concentrated in the 2010s. A small core of collaborators runs through the bulk of his work: Matt Maginn and Mike Mogis each appear on well over a hundred releases alongside him, with engineer Doug Van Sloun and drummer Clint Schnase close behind. Before Cursive he was active in Commander Venus and Slowdown Virginia, and he has also recorded under March Hares and TRC 112.
