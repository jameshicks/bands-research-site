Overview

Cédric Pin is a French musician working primarily as a keyboardist and programmer, associated with Piano Magic, Future Conditional, and Statues In Fog. His catalogue of 58 credits spans from 2003 to at least 2025, with his closest working relationship being the 31-credit collaboration with Glen Johnson — the driving force behind Piano Magic. Earlier work appeared on Green UFOs and Important Records around 2005–2007; his more recent output since 2013 has been concentrated on Second Language. Alongside keyboard and electric piano duties, he also takes songwriter credits, suggesting a compositional role beyond mere session work.
