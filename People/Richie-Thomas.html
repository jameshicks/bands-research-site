Richie Thomas

Drummer and saxophonist. Full name Richard David Thomas. Discogs artist ID: 141743 (listed as “Richard Thomas (2)”; credited on releases as “Richie Thomas”, “R. Thomas”, “Ritchie Thomas”, “Richie Thomas ‘Caught With The Dat Running’”). UK.

Overview

Richard Thomas is a British drummer and saxophonist who has been a persistent figure across the UK post-punk and 4AD universe from the early 1980s onward. He was a full member of Dif Juz from their debut recordings in 1981 through to their 1999 archival release, and from around 1990 to 1992 he drummed for The Jesus And Mary Chain during the Honey’s Dead recording period. His saxophone and drum work also appears on key 4AD releases — the Cocteau Twins’ Victorialand (1986) and The Moon And The Melodies (1986), and the This Mortal Coil compilation Filigree & Shadow (1986). Beyond the 4AD circle he has worked with Butterfly Child, The Hope Blister, Moose (2), The Wolfgang Press, and The Future Sound Of London, as well as French pop and the April March / Bertrand Burgalat axis. In the 2010s–2020s he has performed and recorded as half of Lost Horizons alongside Simon Raymonde.
