Cliff Saffer

Saxophonist, clarinettist. Manchester. Discogs artist ID: 441390. Real name: Clifford Saffer. Name variations: Clif Saffer, CS, Saffer.

Overview

Core reed player (saxophone, clarinet) of Swamp Children (1980–1982) and Kalima. Credited on the So Hot LP (Factory Benelux FBN 21, 1982) as playing saxophone and clarinet. No solo work or other groups found in Discogs beyond Swamp Children and Kalima.
