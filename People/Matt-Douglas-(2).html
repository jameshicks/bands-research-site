Matt Douglas (2)

Multi-instrumentalist (woodwinds, saxophone, clarinet, flute, keyboards, guitar, accordion, piano, vocals) and songwriter. Member of The Mountain Goats since 2015–16. Based in Raleigh, North Carolina. Discogs artist ID: 1227500.

Name variations on Discogs: Douglas, M. Douglas, M.D., Matt Douglas, Matthew Douglas.

Biography

Matt Douglas studied at NYU before winning a Fulbright Scholarship to Central Europe, where he researched Hungarian folk music — a period he has described as the point when he began writing his own songs. After returning to the United States he settled in Raleigh, NC, where he became part of that city’s working indie and Americana music scene.

In Raleigh he formed The Proclivities with guitarist Chris Boerner and drummer Matt McCaughan (Discogs: 818926). The Proclivities released one album, Predispositions (2006, self-released). Around the same time Douglas began extensive session and touring work — most prominently on Josh Ritter’s The Historical Conquests Of Josh Ritter (2007, Victor Records / Sony BMG), on which he played alto saxophone, baritone saxophone, bass clarinet, and backing vocals. The Ritter connection was significant: both Ritter and Douglas were embedded in the same touring-musician ecosystem, and Ritter has been listed among the artists Douglas has shared stages with.

In early 2009 Douglas joined Caitlin Cary (ex-Whiskeytown violinist) for the annual Love Hangover benefit shows in Raleigh. That relationship developed into an ongoing songwriting partnership: they released one album together as Small Ponds — Caitlin Cary & Matt Douglas Are The Small Ponds (2010, Last Chance Records).

Also in Raleigh, Douglas formed the instrumental power trio The Hot At Nights with longtime collaborator Chris Boerner (8-string guitar) and drummer Nick Baglio (Discogs: 5184823). The Hot At Nights released Nice Talk (2011, Chris Boerner Music) and Glaciers (2018, The Foreign Exchange Music).

Throughout 2015 Douglas toured with The Mountain Goats as a multi-instrumentalist promoting Beat The Champ. In 2016 he was formally admitted as a permanent fourth member. His presence on that album was distinctive for its role: he is credited specifically as arranger of all woodwinds — not merely a session player but a creative collaborator shaping the horn/wind textures of the record. In 2015–2016 he was also recruited as the “Sad Sax Of Shit” for Bon Iver’s 22, A Million — a backing-band credit under that alias that illustrates the breadth of his session profile.

As a permanent Mountain Goat, Douglas’s credited instrumental range has expanded album by album: from woodwinds and arranger duties on Beat The Champ and Goths, to guitar, keyboards, accordion, and backing vocals on Getting Into Knives, to strings-and-horns arrangement credit on Jenny From Thebes. On Goths (2017) — the band’s no-guitars record — he also co-produced the vinyl-only remix disc alongside John Darnielle. He continues to work in Raleigh as a performer, teacher, and songwriter.

Notes:

  • On Beat The Champ the band is credited as “The Mountain Goats Are: John Darnielle, Jon Wurster, Peter Hughes (2)” — Douglas is listed separately as an additional credit rather than a named member, reflecting his initial touring/session status.
  • Goths (2017) removed electric guitars entirely; Douglas’s keyboard and vocal parts were central to the album’s orchestral-pop texture. The vinyl remix disc (credited “Remix: John Darnielle, Matt Douglas”) constitutes a formal co-production credit.
  • Jenny From Thebes (2023) was produced by Trina Shoemaker and is the last album to feature Peter Hughes (2) in the lineup.
  • Through This Fire Across From Peter Balkan (2025, Cadmean Dawn) marks the post-Hughes era, with Cameron Ralston on bass.
  • Also credited on The Jordan Lake Sessions: Volumes 1 And 2 (2020, self-released), playing keyboards, guitar, saxophone, and piano.
  • Also credited on the Merge Records release Aquarium Drunkard’s Lagniappe Session (2018) and Hex Of Infinite Binding (2018 EP).
  • Also contributed to AF (Acoustic Foolish) (2019, Merge Records) — listed in “Recorded By [Saxophone]” credit.
  • Also credited on O Come All Ye Faithful (2021 holiday release, Merge Records): saxophone, flute, additional recording.
  • Also credited on Let the Light of the World Open Your Eyes (Alive at Spacebomb Studios) (2020, Merge Records): tenor saxophone, alto saxophone.
