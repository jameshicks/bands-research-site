Tom Mallon

Discogs ID: 251881 Born: 24 December 1956 Died: 9 January 2014 (Glioblastoma Multiforme, diagnosed July 2012) Real name: Tom Mallon-McCorgray

San Francisco recording studio owner, producer, engineer, and musician. Founded Grifter Records in 1984. Produced and performed on all three early American Music Club albums; was a member of AMC 1986–1989 (guitar, then briefly bass). Also played with Toiling Midgets (1991–1997, 2007–) and Negative Trend (2008–).

Built his first studio in a one-bedroom apartment in the Haight in 1976 with a four-track, expanding to two full studios over a career spanning nearly 35 years.

Earlier / Scene Work

Before Grifter, Mallon was active in the Bay Area punk and hardcore underground as an engineer and producer from 1980 onward: credits on Crucifix (1981), FU82 (1982), Capitol Punishment (1983), and releases on Alternative Tentacles, Subterranean Records, and Fowl Records.
