Arone Dyer

Singer, musician, composer, and voice talent. Discogs artist ID: 170604. Website: aronedyer.com.

Co-founder of Buke and Gase (with Aron Sanchez), the experimental duo whose name derives from the custom instruments she and Sanchez built — the buke (bass ukulele) and the gass (guitar-bass hybrid). Also a founding member of Mistresses (with Ryan Oslance) and the voice project Dronechoir. Active as a solo/collaborative artist since at least 1998; earlier pre-Buke and Gase projects include Hominid (2) (with Sanchez, Ram Gabay, and Ian Savage).

Primary Projects

Buke and Gase / Buke and Gass

Dyer and Aron Sanchez formed under the name Buke and Gass (Discogs canonical name; also credited as Buke And Gase), an experimental pop duo based in Hudson, New York. Dyer’s roles within the duo: buke (the custom bass ukulele she plays), vocals, lyrics, percussion, and co-production. Sanchez handles the gass, drums/drum programming, engineering, mixing, and mastering.

Mistresses

Duo with Ryan Oslance (Discogs: 1839272). Released Define “Relationship” (2019, Joyful Noise Recordings). On that record: Dyer — synth, vocals, music by, lyrics by, additional mixing (tracks A5/B1/B2/B4), recorded synth & vocals (B1/B2). Other contributors: Greg Saunier (Deerhoof) — drums, mixed by, recorded by (multiple tracks); Lily Konigsberg — backing vocals (B1).

Solo & Collaborative Work

Jump (1998)

Solo debut. CD on Mocha Moose (cat# MM98AD01). 11-track pop album. Sparse Discogs credits — no additional contributors listed. Genres: Pop.

Arone x s t a r g a z e (2023)

Co-primary release with Stargaze (4) (s t a r g a z e, a Berlin-based contemporary classical ensemble conducted by André de Ridder). Released on Brassland and Transgressive Records; 2 pressings. No additional Discogs credits listed beyond the two primary artists. This is Dyer’s most recent Discogs entry.
