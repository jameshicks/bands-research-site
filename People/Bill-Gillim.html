Bill Gillim

Discogs ID: 1497330 Origin: USA Active: 2008–present

US musician and composer. His primary project is Megafortress, an electronic solo vehicle active since around 2008, but he has also recorded as a vocalist, performer, and bandmember on releases across the New York indie/electronic underground. He plays electronic wind instrument and is a member of Ex-Corp (Discogs artist ID: 17351419).

Megafortress

Megafortress (Discogs artist ID: 2219205) is Gillim’s main project, listed on Discogs with Michael Barron (2) as a secondary member. The project spans from early cassette-era releases through a 2025 retrospective.

Tigercity and early network

Gillim performed on Tigercity’s Pretend Not To Love (2008, Strange Feeling), alongside Andrew Brady, Aynsley Powell, and Joel Ford. The release was produced by Al Carlson and Tigercity.

Other credits

  • Vocalist on Noah Wall’s Hèloïse (2011, self-released) — track A5
  • Remix collaborator on David Van Tieghem’s Fits & Starts (2013, Rvng Intl. / The Sound Of White Columns), as Megafortress
  • Electronic wind instrument on Ex-Corp’s Assemblage (2025, Cached Media)

Ex-Corp

Ex-Corp (Discogs artist ID: 17351419) is a group whose 2025 debut Assemblage credits Gillim (electronic wind instrument), Benjamin Felton (guitar), John Crouch (drums), Daniel Klag (samples/guitar), Dan Goldberg (synthesizer), and Mike Walters (synthesizer/piano). Layout and design by Matthew Sage.
