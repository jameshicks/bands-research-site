Nick Valensi

American guitarist, born 1981. Lead guitarist and one of two guitarists of The Strokes, alongside Albert Hammond Jr.. Discogs artist ID: 430107.

Overview

Nick Valensi is best known as lead guitarist of The Strokes, with whom he has played on every studio album from formation (1998) through the present. On Angles (2011) his Discogs credit distinguishes him specifically as “Lead Guitar” while Hammond Jr. is listed as “Rhythm Guitar” — the clearest formal statement of their division of roles in the band. Outside The Strokes he has done scattered session work and led the side project CRX (4), which released one album (New Skin, 2016) on Columbia, produced by Josh Homme.

The Strokes

Valensi is present on all Strokes studio releases. His individual contributions within the band’s collective songwriting process became visible beginning with First Impressions Of Earth (2005), when individual members started receiving per-track “Music By” credits rather than blanket band credits.

Songwriting Credit — “Ask Me Anything”

On First Impressions Of Earth, Valensi received an individual “Music By” credit (credited as “N.Valensi”) for track 7, “Ask Me Anything” — a notably sparse, spoken-word track in which Casablancas delivers vocals over a minimal guitar figure. Fabrizio Moretti received a “Music By” credit for track 13 (“Evening Sun”) and Nikolai Fraiture for track 9 (“Killing Lies”), suggesting this was the moment the band began acknowledging per-member composition contributions, at least for tracks where one member’s contribution was especially distinct. All other tracks on that album are credited to “J.Casablancas” (Words By, Music By).

On the Future Present Past EP (2016), Valensi is credited “Written-By” on tracks 1 and 4, alongside Casablancas and Fabrizio Moretti on those tracks.

Session Credits

Valensi has a small number of non-Strokes session appearances:

Sia — Clap Your Hands single (2010)

Guitar on this single, produced by Greg Kurstin. Mixed by Kurstin and Dave Trumfio, mastered by Brian Gardner. (Discogs release ID: 3301941)

Sia — We Are Born (2010)

Guitar on Sia’s album We Are Born, again produced by Greg Kurstin (Monkey Puzzle Records / Jive). Released 2010. (Discogs release ID: 2328423). The connection likely runs through the David Kahne / Strokes overlap — Kahne produced First Impressions Of Earth for The Strokes and subsequently pulled Valensi into sessions.

Renée Fleming — Dark Hope (2010)

Guitar on tracks 1, 4, 5, 11 of opera soprano Renée Fleming’s Dark Hope album on Decca. The album was produced, engineered, arranged, and largely performed by David Kahne — who also played bass, keyboards, and guitar on other tracks. Valensi appears alongside guitarist Rusty Anderson (Paul McCartney’s long-time touring guitarist). (Discogs release ID: 9875469)

Little Joy — Little Joy (2008)

Backing vocals on track 7 of the self-titled debut by Little Joy (Fabrizio Moretti’s project with Rodrigo Amarante and Binki Shapiro). Released on Rough Trade. (Discogs release ID: 1547505)

Ringo Starr — Crooked Boy (2024)

Guitar on this single by Ringo Starr, written and produced by Linda Perry. (Discogs release ID: 30453641)
