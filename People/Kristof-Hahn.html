Kristof Hahn

German guitarist, lap steel player, composer, and translator. Born 6 February 1959 in Idar-Oberstein, Germany; grew up in Saarbrücken. Based in Berlin. Discogs artist ID: 421740.

Also credited as Christoph Hahn (Discogs ID: 286490 — a separate but un-merged entry with no further data) and Justice Hahn (Discogs ID: 1265104 — his solo alias, listed as an alias of artist 421740).

Overview

Hahn studied classical guitar in his youth, then moved to electric guitar under the influence of Ike Turner, Dick Dale, Chris Spedding, and Glenn Branca. He moved to West Berlin in 1980 and landed a job at Germany’s first punk record store, Zensor, while studying political science. He co-founded and played in several early-1980s Berlin garage/post-punk bands, most notably Die Goldenen Vampire (later The Legendary Golden Vampires) and The Nirvana Devils.

He joined Swans in 1989 for the Burning World tour and played on White Light from the Mouth of Infinity (1991). He left in 1992 after the birth of his first child, then joined Michael Gira’s follow-up project Angels of Light from its inception in 1998. When Swans reformed in 2010, Hahn was part of the new lineup from the start, contributing lap steel and guitar through to the 2016 dissolution and again from 2023 onward. His approach to lap steel is non-traditional — long drones that function as either textural background or prominent foreground element.

In 2017 he joined Pere Ubu after contributing steel guitar to their album 20 Years in a Montana Missile Silo. He also released a solo instrumental album (Solo Etudes I, 2016) on Eroto Tox Decodings and leads the Berlin rock’n’roll-noir ensemble Sultans Of Gedankenbrain.

Beyond music, Hahn works extensively as a literary translator from English to German (Russell Brand, John Peel, Hubert Selby Jr., Gay Talese, Richard Laymon, Simon Kernick, Christopher Moore).

Research Queue

  • Wikipedia credits Hahn with film soundtracks for German director Robert Schwentke (Eierdiebe/The Family Jewels, 2003) and Austrian director Marko Doringer (Mein Halbes Leben, 2008; Naegel mit Köpfen, 2013) — none of these appear in Discogs. Were these commercially released or only used in film?
  • Koolkings (Shocked & Amazed, 1991) is listed on Discogs as a German release on Marat Records. Wikipedia says it was made with “American musician/producer Alex Chilton.” Confirm whether this is the Alex Chilton of Big Star / Box Tops and what his exact role was.
  • The “Justice Hahn” 10” Down by Love (1989) has a Discogs entry but Wikipedia also lists a second solo 10” Ragged but Right (1991) — this does not appear in Discogs. Confirm whether this was released.
  • Confirm the exact years of Hahn’s three stints in Swans: the initial membership ended in 1992 per Wikipedia (birth of first child); reformed era ran 2010–2017 per Wikipedia; and resumed in 2023. Were there any interim Swans live appearances between 1992 and 2010?
