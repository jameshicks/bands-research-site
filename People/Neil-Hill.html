Neil Hill

Co-founder of SPK. Discogs artist ID: 807874. Real name: Stephen Neil Hill.

Overview

Neil Hill (credited as NE/H/IL, Ne/H/iL, or Skorne; born c. 1956, died February 1984) co-founded SPK in Sydney in 1978 with Graeme Revell, both working at Callan Park Hospital’s psychiatric ward. He was the primary vocalist and electronics operator on SPK’s early noise recordings, most notably No More (1979) and Leichenschrei (1982). When Revell relocated SPK to London circa 1980, Hill remained in Australia; the separation was de facto rather than the result of any documented falling-out. Hill continued working in Sydney under the SoliPsiK project name. He is not credited on SPK’s transitional or later London-period records. He died by suicide in early February 1984.

Notes (web)

Departure from SPK (~1980)

Hill did not formally depart SPK through any documented resignation or rupture. When Revell relocated the band to London (following an invitation from Genesis P-Orridge of Throbbing Gristle / Industrial Records), Hill stayed in Australia and did not join the move. One secondary source characterises this as Hill “refusing the UK move,” resulting temporarily in two simultaneous SPK-related entities — London SPK (Revell, Sinan Revell, David Virgin) and Sydney SPK. source

Revell, in a 2025 interview, described mutual respect with Hill and said they “never had any arguments.” He characterised Hill as “an interesting character but very self-destructive,” noting an obsessive, nihilistic temperament heavily influenced by Yukio Mishima. source

Hill also declined to participate in Revell’s 1982 USA tour, though he had performed with an Australian SPK lineup at the Brickworks venue in Sydney. source

The Pantograph Punch (2024/25) notes: “Ne/H/il never joined Revell in the UK, and his suicide is one of the parts of the SPK mythos that’s been subject to the most uncertainty and rumour.” source

Post-SPK activity: SoliPsiK (1981)

The only documented post-separation release is a 7” single by SoliPsiK, an Australian continuation project on M Squared Records:

SoliPsiK — “See-Saw / Chambermusik” (7”, M Squared M-2009, November 1981) Members: Kitka (Kit Katalog), Sushi (Margaret Hill), Charlyiev (Paul Charlier), Skorne (Neil Hill). Both tracks co-written by Charlier and Hill. Recorded in Sydney (home recording with M Squared studio overdubs/mix). Sound described as abrasive industrial with AKS synthesiser; A-side incorporates Japanese military song-like phrases. source source

No further releases under the Skorne alias or SoliPsiK name have been identified in web research. Wikipedia confirms “no post-SPK activity” for Hill outside this single, and a Discogs artist page for Neil Hill (ID 807874) exists but contains no additional solo releases per search results (not fetched directly — blocked domain).

Death

In early February 1984, just before his 28th birthday, Hill died by suicide. His wife Margaret Hill (née Nikitenko) died two days later from complications of anorexia. source source

Revell, then in England, later said: “I was not completely shocked when given the bad news,” describing long-running concern about Hill’s self-destructive tendencies. The Pantograph Punch article notes SPK were “unflappably, even inexcusably glib” about Hill’s death in contemporaneous interviews. source source
