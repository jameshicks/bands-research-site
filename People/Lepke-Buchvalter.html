Lepke Buchvalter

Discogs ID: 618083
Aliases: Gus Coma, Yakko Banovic
Name Variations: Lepke, Lepke B., Lepke Buchvater, Lepke Buchwalter, Lepke Buchwater, Lepke Bukwater
Origin: UK (London-connected)

London-associated experimental / sound collage musician and collaborator in the post-punk DIY underground. A recurring presence in the It’s War Boys network around Jim Welton, appearing on multiple releases from 1981 onward under his own name and the aliases Gus Coma and Yakko Banovic. Co-founder of Die Trip Computer Die (with Welton/Xentos) and member of Milk From Cheltenham, The Just Measurers, Murphy Working Stiffs, and Modern Shit.
