Jul 04, 20261 min read

Ted Jensen

Mastering engineer. Discogs ID: 259758. 480 shared credits with The Rolling Stones, including mastering Some Girls (1978) and Steel Wheels (1989).

Mastered Mission Of Burma’s reunion LPs ONoffON (2004) and The Obliterati (2006) on Matador Records. One of the most prominent mastering engineers in rock — long-tenured at Sterling Sound, New York. Credits span decades of major and independent releases.

Key Credits (Sigur Rós)

Mastered all major Sigur Rós albums from Takk… (2005) through Kveikur (2013) — over 100 Discogs releases in their catalog. Also mastered many of their singles, EPs, and international pressings. Credits include Takk… (2005), Hoppípolla, Með Suð Í Eyrum Við Spilum Endalaust (2008), Valtari (2012), Kveikur (2013).
