Sherree Lawrence

Discogs ID: 596012
Name variations: S.Lawrence, Sheree Lawrence
Role: Organist, pianist, synthesizer, keyboards, vocals

Organist and keyboardist; core member of The Deep Freeze Mice (Leicester, 1979–1989) and later a performer with The Creams. One of three members constant throughout the Deep Freeze Mice’s entire active decade alongside Alan Jenkins (guitar, vocals) and Mick Bunnage (bass).

Deep Freeze Mice (1979–1989)

Lawrence was one of the founding members of The Deep Freeze Mice, the New Wave art-rock trio (later quartet with a rotating drummer seat) from Leicester. She appears on all nine studio records the band issued across two label eras, playing organ on the first releases and expanding her palette to piano, synthesizer, and occasional lead vocals as the band’s sound evolved.

On the early Mole Embalming Records LPs (1979–1983) she is credited as “Performer” on the debut and as organist from Teenage Head In My Refrigerator (1981) onward — indicating the organ was already present from the start. By the Cordelia Records era her credits expanded consistently: organ and piano from 1984, keyboards on Hang On Constance (1985), synthesizer added on the final LP The Tender Yellow Ponies Of Insomnia (1989). Vocal credits appear on I Love You Little BoBo (1984), Rain Is When The Earth Is Television (1986), War, Famine, Death, Pestilence And Miss Timberlake (1987), and the final album (1989).

The Creams (1994–)

After the Deep Freeze Mice ended in 1989, Lawrence appears as a performer with The Creams, a larger ensemble around Alan Jenkins also including Alison Mackinder, Blodwyn P. Teabag (Discogs ID: 490643), Ariadne Metal-Cream Pie (Discogs ID: 490642), Robyn Gibson, and others. The Creams also released material under the alias “Jody And The Creams” (Discogs ID: 365162).

Her credit on ie (1994, Alan Jenkins and The Creams / Cordelia Records + Raffmond) — a double LP — is specific: “Vocals [Bloodwyn & The Answering Machine],” indicating a character or voice role in what appears to be a concept work. Her credit on Lords Of The Gromet Canning Factory (1998, Jody And The Creams / Pink Lemon Records, Discogs ID: 2965950) is listed simply as “Performer.”
