Dave Allen

Bassist, producer, label founder. Real name David Geoffrey Allen. Born 23 December 1955. Died 5 April 2025. Discogs artist ID: 842383.

Co-founder and original bassist of Gang of Four, active with the band from their 1977 formation until 1981. Subsequently co-founded Shriekback with Barry Andrews (ex-XTC) and Carl Marsh. Later led King Swamp (Virgin Records, 1988–1990) and co-founded Low Pop Suicide. Founded the Seattle-based imprint World Domination Recordings in the early 1990s, distributed through Capitol Records. Continued performing and recording with Shriekback into the 2020s.

Name variations on Discogs: Dave Allan, David Allen, Allen, D. Allen, Warrior, Allen David Geoffrey, David G. Allen, and others.

Biography

Gang of Four (1977–1981)

Allen formed the Leeds art-school group alongside guitarist Andy Gill, vocalist Jon King, and drummer Hugo Burnham. The band signed to EMI via the Fast Product single route. Allen played bass on the debut LP Entertainment! (1979) — co-produced with Rik Walton and Rob Warr — and the follow-up Solid Gold (1981), on which all four members shared a producer credit alongside engineer Jimmy Douglass. He departed in 1981 shortly after Solid Gold was completed, before the band shifted toward a more funk-inflected direction on Songs of the Free.

Shriekback (1982–1992, 2002–2025)

Allen, Barry Andrews (keyboards, ex-XTC), and Carl Marsh (guitar/vocals) formed Shriekback in 1982, releasing early material through Y Records — the label connected to Bob Last and the Gang of Four’s post-punk orbit. The early records (Care, 1983; Jam Science, 1984) were dense art-funk hybrids. The band signed to Arista Records and then Island Records, where Oil and Gold (1985) and Big Night Music (1986) brought wider visibility. Allen wrote or co-wrote most of the catalog alongside Andrews and Marsh.

By the late 1980s Allen had stepped back from Shriekback’s active touring lineup but retained songwriting credits. He rejoined for the 1992 album Sacred City on World Domination Recordings and periodically thereafter. From the 2000s Shriekback operated largely as a self-releasing entity (Malicious Damage; self-released) with Allen as a consistent participant. Final recordings appeared in 2025 — Monument and Heaven to Deinze — released shortly before his death on 5 April 2025.

King Swamp (1988–1990)

Allen co-founded King Swamp with vocalist Walter Wray, guitarist Steve Halliwell, drummer Martyn Barker, and guitarist Dominic Miller (subsequently Sting’s long-term guitarist). The band released two LPs on Virgin Records: King Swamp (1988) and Wiseblood (1990). Allen handled bass, production, and arrangements, and co-wrote all material. The sound was melodic post-punk with soul-inflected vocals.

Low Pop Suicide (c. 1992–1994)

A Los Angeles-based band co-founded by Allen with Rick Boston, Melle Steagal, and Jeff Ward. Released material through Capitol Records and World Domination Recordings: The Disengagement EP (1992) and On the Cross of Commerce (1993). Allen played bass and produced.

World Domination Recordings (1992–c. 1996)

Allen founded World Domination Recordings in Seattle, distributed through Capitol Records (label ID: 4935; 296 releases). The label released or co-released material by Low Pop Suicide, Shriekback, The Elastic Purejoy, Stanford Prison Experiment, and others. Allen served as A&R, executive producer, and direct producer across much of the catalog. The label folded around 1996 when the Capitol relationship ended; Allen subsequently ran a related entity, World Domination Music Group, operating out of Canada via True North.

The Elastic Purejoy and The Clutter of Pop (1994–1996)

Allen’s most explicitly solo-credited project. The Elastic Purejoy (1994, World Domination Recordings) featured Allen as principal writer, lyricist, and musician. The Clutter of Pop (1996, True North) was credited to Dave Allen & The Elastic Purejoy, with Allen on keyboards, guitar, synthesizer, and voice, alongside collaborators Tracy Chisholm and Fred Mills (3).

Other Groups

Allen was also a member of Noise Abroad (Discogs ID: 86322) — the four-piece with Ian Caple, Luc Van Acker, and Brian Nevill that released “Vent That Spleen” (EMI Belgium, 1983). See the Noise Abroad page for details. Also member of Faux Hoax (Discogs ID: 1250529) and The Crash Baptists (2) (Discogs ID: 4633317). Activity dates and scope for the latter two are not fully documented in Discogs.
