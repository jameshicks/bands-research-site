Dave Vettraino

Discogs ID: 1791717 Name variations: Dave V, Dave Vetraino, Dave Vettrino, David Vettraino

Engineer, mixer, producer, and guitarist based in Chicago, Illinois. Runs Public House Sound Recordings (Discogs label ID: 714216), his own studio imprint. A member of the Chicago band The Hecks. The dominant production figure behind much of the International Anthem Recording Company catalog from 2015 onward, frequently paired with co-engineer/mixer David Allen (10).

Career Overview

Vettraino’s documented Discogs credits begin in 2009 as an assistant engineer on sessions for Secretly Canadian and Joyful Noise Recordings, working alongside other engineers in a support role. By 2014–2015, he had stepped up to primary recording and mixing credit, with early independent production work for Chicago indie-rock acts on Manic Static (Ne-Hi, Bathroom At The Beach) and the beginnings of his relationship with International Anthem Recording Company, where he recorded and mixed Makaya McCraven’s In The Moment (2015).

From 2017 onward, Vettraino became one of IARC’s central studio collaborators, engineering and/or mixing a remarkable proportion of the label’s catalog across jazz, improvised music, and avant-soul genres. His name appears alongside David Allen (10) on nearly every major IARC release of the period — the two are credited jointly on Makaya McCraven’s Universal Beings (2018), In These Times (2022), Rob Mazurek / Exploding Star Orchestra’s Dimensional Stardust (2020) and Lightning Dreamers (2023), Jaimie Branch’s entire Fly Or Die trilogy, and much more.

Vettraino’s relationship with Ben Lamar Gay is particularly close: he has served as engineer, mixer, and co-producer on Gay’s solo releases at IARC — Open Arms To Open Us (2021) and Yowzers (2025) — and appears on at least 11 shared releases across Gay’s discography.

He is also an active performer: guitarist in Chicago post-punk/rock band The Hecks (on Trouble In Mind), and occasionally credited as bassist, synthesizer player, or organist on recording sessions (notably Dehd’s Harm’s Way, 2024, on Carpark Records, where he plays organ and produces).

His studio, Public House Sound Recordings, has a Discogs label presence (ID: 714216) with a small number of releases; it appears to operate primarily as his recording facility rather than a release label.

Studio / Facility

Vettraino runs Public House Sound Recordings in Chicago (Discogs label ID: 714216; website: publichousesoundrecordings.com). The label imprint has only two release credits on Discogs, suggesting it functions primarily as a recording facility/studio brand rather than a release vehicle. Exact location within Chicago is not documented in Discogs data.

The Hecks

Vettraino is a member of The Hecks, a Chicago rock band that released a self-titled album on Trouble In Mind in 2016 (Discogs ID: 9122709 / master 1066144). He is credited with guitar and vocals.

Open Questions

Resolved

  • “Careful!” (Sub Pop, 2023): Primary artist is Deeper (6), a Chicago indie rock band. Vettraino co-produced and mixed the record and plays bass on one track and synth on another. This is his deepest single-session playing contribution outside The Hecks.
  • “There Is Only Love And Fear” (2023, IARC): Primary artist is Bex Burch, a percussionist/composer based in London. Vettraino is credited as Executive-Producer alongside David Allen (10) and Scott McNiece — indicating IARC/Vettraino-side executive involvement in an outside-originated project, not a production-from-scratch role. He also engineered the bulk of the sessions.
