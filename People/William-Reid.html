William Reid

Born William Adam Reid, 28 October 1958, Glasgow (raised in East Kilbride), Scotland. Co-founder and guitarist of The Jesus And Mary Chain, brother of Jim Reid. Records solo material under the aliases Lazycame (Discogs ID: 879145), CNYK (Discogs ID: 942845), and Ravenscraeg (Discogs ID: 942844). Discogs artist ID: 232797.

Overview

William Reid is the guitarist, co-songwriter, and occasional vocalist of The Jesus And Mary Chain. Along with brother Jim Reid, he founded the band in East Kilbride around 1983 and developed its defining sonic signature: melodic, Spector-influenced pop melodies submerged in extreme guitar feedback and drone. William takes lead vocals on selected JAMC tracks — notably “Blues from a Gun” and “Head On” (both from Automatic, 1989) and on tracks across Darklands (1987).

William’s role across the band’s original run (1983–1999) was guitarist, co-producer, and co-writer throughout. From Automatic onward he also shared vocal duties explicitly on-record. He is additionally credited with cover concept on Automatic and artwork on Glasgow Eyes (2024).

After the original JAMC split in 1999, William recorded solo material under the alias Lazycame, releasing the EP Taster (1999, Hot Tam), the mini-LP Yawn! EP (2000, Guided Missile), the album Saturday The Fourteenth (2000, Hot Tam), and the album Finbegin (2001, Hall Of Records). All three aliases — Lazycame, CNYK, and Ravenscraeg — appear on the Taster EP as distinct track credits, suggesting they represent stylistic sub-personae within the same solo project. Dick Meaney (long-running JAMC engineer) engineered and assisted across all Lazycame releases.

He was also co-producer and co-writer on Linda Reid’s (Sister Vanilla) album Little Pop Rock (P-Vine Records, 2005), alongside Jim Reid and Ben Lurie.

William contributed a self-designed sleeve and cover photograph to the 1998 JAMC single “Tired of Fucking” (Creation Records), credited under name variations “W.A.R.” (sleeve) and “William R.” (photo).

Solo Work — Lazycame / CNYK / Ravenscraeg

All solo Discogs credits appear under the Lazycame artist page (ID: 879145). The aliases CNYK and Ravenscraeg have no independent release records on Discogs — they appear only as track-level credits within the Taster EP, suggesting they are sub-personae within the Lazycame project rather than separate projects with their own catalogs.
