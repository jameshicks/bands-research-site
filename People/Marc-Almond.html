Marc Almond

Real name: Peter Mark Sinclair Almond
Born: 9 July 1957, Southport, Lancashire, England
Discogs ID: 32915
Aliases on Discogs: Raoul Revere, Suraya Ahmed

British vocalist, songwriter, and performer. Co-founder of Soft Cell with Dave Ball; frontman of Marc And The Mambas; leader of La Magia and The Willing Sinners in the mid-to-late 1980s. One of the most prolific and stylistically wide-ranging voices to emerge from the post-punk/synth-pop milieu of 1980–82 — moving freely from electronic cabaret to torch song, chanson, and orchestral pop. Awarded an OBE in 2018.

Early Career: Soft Cell (1980–1984)

Almond met Dave Ball at Leeds Polytechnic in the late 1970s. As a synth-and-vocals duo they appeared on the Mutant Moments E.P. (1980, A Big Frock Rekord), Almond credited on “Vocals, Other [Synthetic Scratch].” Soft Cell signed to Some Bizzare and broke commercially with their cover of “Tainted Love” (1981, Some Bizzare) — Almond on vocals and percussion, Ball on electronics, produced by Mike Thorne. The single reached UK #1 and remained on the UK chart for 43 weeks.

Soft Cell’s debut album Non-Stop Erotic Cabaret (1981, Some Bizzare) was produced by Mike Thorne. It established the duo’s formula: Ball’s minimal synthesizer and drum machine bed, Almond’s expressionistic vocals, Vicious Pink (credited as Vicious Pink Phenomena) on backing vocals. The follow-up Non-Stop Ecstatic Dancing (1982) was an all-dancefloor rework EP. The Art Of Falling Apart (1983, Some Bizzare) retained Thorne as producer and added John Gatchell (trumpet) to the sound. This Last Night In Sodom (1984, Some Bizzare/Sire) was their final studio album before the duo split.

Almond also appeared as uncredited vocalist on Matt Johnson’s “Discipline” (Flexipop, 1982).

Marc And The Mambas (1982–1984)

Running concurrently with Soft Cell, Marc And The Mambas was Almond’s more experimental side project — looser, more orchestral, more theatrically decadent. Core members: Annie Hogan (piano, keyboards, marimba, vibraphone), Billy McGee (double bass, keyboards, arrangements), Martin McCarrick (cello, accordion, keyboards), Lee Jenkinson (guitar, bass), Steve Sherlock (saxophone, flute). The ensemble also drew in The Venomettes (Anne Stephenson and Gini Hewes/Gini Ball on violin, with McCarrick and McGee) for string arrangements.

Fun City / Sleaze (1982) and the double LP Torment And Toreros (1983, Some Bizzare) were the key releases. Torment And Toreros was co-produced by Almond and Flood, with Matt Johnson on guitar. The Mambas never formally disbanded; they simply became absorbed into Almond’s solo touring band, The Willing Sinners.

Solo Career: Mid-1980s — La Magia / The Willing Sinners

From 1984 Almond recorded as a solo artist, retaining much of the Mambas personnel in a touring/recording band called The Willing Sinners (Hogan, McGee, McCarrick, Richard Riley on guitar, Steven Humphreys on drums, Martin Ditcham percussion, Audrey Riley and Jane West on backing vocals, Enrico Tomasso trumpet). A smaller studio unit, La Magia, centred on Hogan, McGee, and Humphreys.

“Something’s Gotten Hold Of My Heart” — a duet with Gene Pitney (originally from 1989 single, Parlophone/Some Bizzare) — reached UK #1 in January 1989.

Gene Pitney Duet and Parlophone / Capitol Period (1988–1991)

After signing to Parlophone (UK) / Capitol Records (US), Almond’s commercial reach expanded. The Stars We Are album (1988) contained “Tears Run Rings” and led to mainstream exposure. Enchanted (1990, Capitol) and Tenement Symphony (1991, Some Bizzare/WEA) continued in an orchestral pop mode. The 1991 Soft Cell retrospective Memorabilia — The Singles (Mercury) ran alongside.

Absinthe, Stranger Things, and Later Work (1993–2010s)

Almond recorded a French chanson album, Absinthe (1993, Some Bizzare), produced by Almond with arrangements, then moved through XIII BIS Records (France) for Stranger Things (2001) and Heart On Snow (2003). He also released orchestral/theatrical works including Feasting With Panthers (2011, Strike Force Entertainment) and The Velvet Trail (2015, Cherry Red).
