Discogs artist ID: 2080075

Role: Graphic designer and illustrator
Active: 2005–present (Discogs-documented)
Social: facebook.com/dltdesign · instagram.com/dltdesigner

Graphic designer and illustrator; long-running house designer for Rock Action Records (Mogwai’s own imprint) and a frequent collaborator with FatCat Records and its sublabel 130701. The visual identity of Rock Action Records — from the early-to-mid 2000s through the present day — is largely DLT (3)‘s work. Discogs credits exceed 190 individual releases across more than two decades, spanning design, layout, illustration, artwork, art direction, typography, and photography.

Real name not confirmed on Discogs; the alias Dave Thomas (15) appears on the artist entry but no further information is attached to that alias.
