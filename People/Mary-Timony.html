Mary Timony

American singer-songwriter, guitarist, keyboardist, bassist, and violist. Born 1970 in Washington, D.C. Real name: Mary Bozana Timony. Discogs artist ID: 218270.

Central figure in the DC/Boston indie underground from the early 1990s onward. Founded and fronted Helium (3) (Matador, 1993–1998), recorded three solo albums for Matador (2000–2005), co-founded Wild Flag with Carrie Brownstein, Janet Weiss, and Rebecca Cole (2011), and has led Ex Hex since 2014. Also active in Autoclave (2) (early career), Hammered Hulls (Dischord), and as a recurring guest guitarist with Garland Of Hours.

Biography

Early Years — Autoclave (2), 1991–1992

Timony came up in the DC punk and post-punk scene in the early 1990s. Her first documented recorded work is with Autoclave (2) (Dischord/K), a short-lived DC band whose lineup included Christina Billotte (bass, vocals), Nikki Chapman (guitar), and Melissa Berkoff (drums). Timony played guitar and sang. The band’s 7” Go Far (1991, Dischord/K) was engineered and mixed by Brendan Canty and Barrett Jones. All recorded material was later collected on the self-titled compilation Autoclave (1997, Dischord/Mira Records).

Helium, 1993–1998

Timony founded Helium (3) in Boston, the band that became her primary vehicle through the mid-1990s. Core lineup: Timony (guitar, vocals), Ash Bowie (bass, accordion, various), and Shawn Devlin (drums). Early releases also featured Brian Dunton on bass.

Helium appeared on the 1995 compilation Wasps’ Nests (London Records).

Solo Work on Matador, 2000–2005

After Helium’s dissolution, Timony recorded three solo albums for Matador, refining an increasingly elaborate, fantasy-inflected approach to guitar-based songwriting.

ReleaseYearNotes
Mountains2000Timony and Christina Files handle all instruments. Mixed by Eric Masunaga and Bob Weston

Ex Hex2005Lookout! Records. Timony: guitar, vocals, keyboards. Devin Ocampo: drums, bass. Produced by Brendan Canty. Recorded by Canty and Don Zientara

Mountains (2000) is the first release credited solely to Mary Timony as primary artist. The 2005 EP/album Ex Hex — released under her own name on Lookout! Records, produced by Brendan Canty — served as a precursor name to the later band.

The Mary Timony Band (Discogs ID: 1423826) also appears in her discography around this era — the 2007 release The Shapes We Make (Kill Rock Stars) credits Devin Ocampo and has Timony on guitar.

Wild Flag, 2011

Wild Flag formed around 2010 as a supergroup drawing from the DC/Pacific Northwest indie underground. Members:

  • Carrie Brownstein (guitar, vocals) — from Sleater-Kinney
  • Janet Weiss (drums, vocals) — from Sleater-Kinney
  • Rebecca Cole (keyboards, piano, vocals)
  • Mary Timony (guitar, vocals)

The self-titled LP Wild Flag (2011, Merge Records) was recorded by Chris Woodhouse, with mixing split between Woodhouse, Dave Fridmann (track A1), and Tucker Martine (tracks A4, B4). All songs credited to the band collectively.

Ex Hex, 2014–present

Ex Hex is Timony’s ongoing power-pop trio, active from 2014. Current lineup:

  • Mary Timony (guitar, vocals)
  • Betsy Wright (bass, guitar, vocals) — also in The Marked Men
  • Laura Harris (2) (drums) / Jonah Takagi (bass, guitar, engineer)

Rips was recorded with Mitch Easter, revisiting his role as Timony’s collaborator from The Magic City era.

Hammered Hulls, 2019–2022

Hammered Hulls is a DC punk outfit on Dischord. Timony plays bass — a departure from her primary role as guitarist. Core lineup: Mark Cisneros (guitar), Alec MacKaye (vocals), Chris Wilson (drums), Timony (bass). Both albums recorded by Don Zientara and produced/recorded at Inner Ear by Ian MacKaye (Careening, 2022).
