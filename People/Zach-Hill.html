Zach Hill

Drummer, producer, multi-instrumentalist. Born 1979, Sacramento, California. Co-founder of Hella and Death Grips; one of the most prolific and technically extreme drummers in experimental rock. Discogs ID: 509222.

Bio

Sacramento origins — Hella (2001–2013)

Zach Hill formed Hella with guitarist Spencer Seim in Sacramento in 2001. The band debuted as a two-piece with the self-released Leather Diamond (2001) and the 5 Rue Christine LP Hold Your Horse Is (2002), a landmark of math rock — drum and guitar interplay at the edge of human tempo. Early Hella was a strict duo; later records expanded the lineup. They released through Suicide Squeeze, Narnack, and eventually Ipecac Recordings (There’s No 666 In Outer Space, 2007, IPC 83), which added vocalists, bass, and saxophone to the core guitar-drum chassis. A reunion LP, Tripper, appeared on Sargent House in 2011.

Solo work (2007–2010)

Hill ran parallel solo projects throughout Hella’s run. Volume 2 (2007, Rock Is Hell Records) was a drum-solo LP. Astrological Straits (2008, Anticon ABR0086) — a double LP — is the richest document of his solo practice: it pulled in Les Claypool on bass (title track), Chino Moreno (Deftones) on vocals, No Age’s Dean Spunt and Randy Randall, and collaborator Jr. Thompson throughout. Face Tat (2010, Sargent House SH047), produced with Andy Morin, brought in Greg Saunier (Deerhoof), Nick Reinhart (Tera Melos), No Age again, Scott Herren (Prefuse 73), and Devendra Banhart.

Marnie Stern

Hill was the drummer on Marnie Stern’s first three albums: In Advance Of The Broken Arm (2007, Kill Rock Stars) — where he also co-arranged and co-produced — and the self-titled Marnie Stern (2010, Kill Rock Stars). These records wove his blast-beat and polyrhythmic drumming into Stern’s tapping-guitar compositions.

Death Grips (2010–present)

Hill co-founded Death Grips in Sacramento in 2010 with vocalist Stefan Burnett (MC Ride) and producer Andy Morin; he drums, co-produces, and co-writes lyrics across all releases. Death Grips defined a decade of industrial hip-hop: free mixtape Exmilitary (2011, Grindcore Karaoke) → major-label debut The Money Store (2012, Epic) → the band leaked No Love Deep Web to spite Epic and got dropped → launched their own label Third Worlds → Government Plates (2013) → The Powers That B (2015) → Bottomless Pit (2016) → Year Of The Snitch (2018). Morin departed c. 2023; Hill and Burnett announced a new album in November 2025.

Other projects

Hill’s groups list is unusually long for a drummer: The Ladies (with Spencer Seim, Temporary Residence, 2006), Bygones, Chll Pll, Goon Moon, Team Sleep (Chino Moreno’s side project), Crime In Choir, El Grupo Nuevo De Omar Rodriguez Lopez, Risil (with Roberto Carlos Lange and Benjamin Curtis), Nervous Cop, Diamond Watch Wrists, and more.

Drumgasm (2013, Jackpot Records 3) was a drum-improvisation LP recorded with Matt Cameron (Soundgarden/Pearl Jam) and Janet Weiss (Sleater-Kinney), recorded by Lawrence Crane.
