Overview

Karine Charff is a vocalist and keyboardist whose recorded work is built almost entirely around Amp (also credited as A.M.P. Studio), the British ambient-shoegaze project. Her discographic activity runs from 1996 through the mid-2010s and is weighted toward the 1990s — six releases in that decade against two each in the 2000s and 2010s. Her closest collaborators are Amp and Richard Amp (22–27 shared releases), with Richard Walker, Gareth Mitchell, Matt Elliott, and Donald Ross Skinner also recurring in the credits. The catalogue spans Kranky, Darla Records, Enraptured, and the band’s own Ampbase imprint.

Notes (web)

Matt Elliott’s involvement in Amp’s sessions

Matt Elliott (Third Eye Foundation) had a pre-existing connection with Richard Walker (Richard Amp) through the early-1990s Bristol/London project the Secret Garden; Walker left that circle in 1992 to found Amp. After Amp’s mid-1990s hiatus, Elliott joined a regrouped lineup alongside Charff and Matt Jones (3) (Crescent) for the sessions that produced a string of 7” singles in 1995 and the debut album Sirènes (1996). Sources describe his contributions as guitarist, drummer, programmer, and viola player on these early Amp records. en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Elliott_(musician) (as of 2026-11-24); en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amp_(band) (as of 2026-11-24); search-result snippet from bristolarchiverecords.com (fetched 2026-11-24, page itself returned 425).

Sirènes (1996) was co-released in the UK on both Wurlitzer Jukebox and Elliott’s own imprint Linda’s Strange Vacation, confirming an active label-level stake in the release. Elliott’s debut Third Eye Foundation album Semtex was also released on Linda’s Strange Vacation the same year (1996), indicating the two projects overlapped directly. By 1997 Elliott and Jones had left Amp to pursue their own projects, which is consistent with Discogs credits showing no Elliott involvement on Astralmoonbeamprojections or Stenorette (both 1997–98). en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semtex_(album) (as of 2026-11-24).
