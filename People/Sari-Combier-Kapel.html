Overview

Sari Combier-Kapel is a vocalist who appears as a guest on Mice Parade’s Lapapọ (2022), contributing additional vocals to the track “Could This Be Anywhere.” Discogs holds no biography, no aliases, and no other credits for her beyond this single appearance — her broader background is not yet documented in the database.

Context: Lapapọ (2022)

Lapapọ is a Mice Parade album produced and recorded by Adam Pierce (the project’s sole consistent member) with co-recording/mixing by Jeremy Backofen. Mastering by Alan Douches. The album is structured as a series of guest-vocalist vignettes; Sari Combier-Kapel is one of six vocalists credited across the record:
