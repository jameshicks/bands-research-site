Pete Wiggs

Discogs ID: 438192
Real name: Peter Stewart Wiggs

Co-founder and core member of Saint Etienne; primary synthesizer and programming player.

Also a member of Cola Boy, St. Etienne Daho, 50 Year Void, and The Séance. With Bob Stanley, wrote the majority of Saint Etienne’s catalog.
