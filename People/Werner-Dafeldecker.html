Werner Dafeldecker

Austrian composer, double bassist, and guitarist. Born 1964, Vienna. Discogs artist ID: 146254. Co-founder of Polwechsel (1993); active in the Vienna/Berlin free improvisation and electroacoustic scenes from the late 1980s onward. In addition to performance, he has worked extensively as recording engineer, mixer, and mastering engineer across the broader experimental and new-music world.

Overview

Dafeldecker’s career divides into three overlapping streams: ensemble improvisation (anchored by Polwechsel, which he has helmed across nearly thirty years), collaborative chamber/electroacoustic work with a rotating cast of Vienna- and Berlin-based improvisers, and — running alongside both — a substantial body of work as a recording and mastering engineer serving the wider scene he inhabits.

His instrument on record expands well beyond double bass: on various releases he is credited for guitar (acoustic and electric), electronics, analogue electronic processing, tape delay, computer, voice, and walkie-talkie. The bass is his spine, but his studio craft and compositional voice matter as much.

The early Vienna years (late 1980s–mid-1990s) ran through Extraplatte and Durian Records, the latter becoming the home for Polwechsel’s first two canonical albums and several of Dafeldecker’s own chamber collaborations. By the late 1990s he was active on Charhizma (the celebrated 1999 sextet record with Christof Kurzmann, Fennesz, Jim O’Rourke, Kevin Drumm, and Martin Siewert) and GROB. From the 2000s onward, Staubgold and Ni-Vu-Ni-Connu have been central homes, the latter in particular hosting a dense cluster of recent Polwechsel releases (the Embrace suite, 2023).

His mastering work deepens his embeddedness in the international experimental music network. He has mastered records for artists across Poland (Monotype, Instant Classic, Lado ABC), Russia (Mikroton), Japan (Ftarri, Meenna), Australia (Room40), Germany (Zarek, Umlaut, Trouble In The East, Gagarin), and Luxembourg (Ni-Vu-Ni-Connu).

Polwechsel

Polwechsel (Discogs artist ID: 65735) is the ensemble with which Dafeldecker is most strongly identified. The group’s membership has shifted over the years; the Discogs listing shows: Burkhard Beins, Burkhard Stangl, John Butcher, Martin Brandlmayr, Michael Moser, Radu Malfatti, and Dafeldecker.
