Overview

Ed Fotheringham (Edwin Judah Fotheringham) is an Australian-born illustrator and musician who moved from Sydney to Seattle, Washington, where he became embedded in the city’s grunge scene primarily through his visual work. His 398 credits stretch back to 1987 and are dominated by typography, calligraphy, and painting for Mudhoney and their immediate circle — Steve Turner, Dan Peters, Mark Arm, Matt Lukin, and Conrad Uno account for the bulk of his shared-release count — mostly through Sub Pop. His visual reach extends well beyond the grunge world: he also has significant credits on Verve Records releases including Diana Krall albums, alongside Jack Endino-produced records. As a performer he has played in several bands including Bush Pig, Icky Joey, Love + Respect, and The Sad And Lonely(s).
