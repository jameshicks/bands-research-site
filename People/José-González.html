Aug 08, 20268 min read

José González

Real name: José Gabriel González
Born: July 31, 1978, Angered, Sweden (of Argentine descent)
Role: Singer, songwriter, guitarist
Discogs ID: 190821
Active: 1999–present

Biography

Early Life and Pre-Veneer (1999–2002)

José Gabriel González was born in Angered, a suburb of Gothenburg, Sweden, to Argentine immigrant parents. He grew up in Gothenburg and became part of the local independent music community in the late 1990s. His earliest documented Discogs appearance is as a band member of Only If You Call Me Jonathan, a Czech-registered project whose EP September Malevolency (1999, Day After Records) and compilation appearance Red Friend (2000) predate his solo work. He also formed Renascence during this early period.

By 2003 he had begun recording under his own name, self-producing stripped-down acoustic folk guitar recordings at home in Gothenburg.

Veneer and the Heartbeats Moment (2003–2005)

González’s debut album Veneer was released in 2003 on Gothenburg’s Imperial Recordings (4) (cat. IMP007CD). He wrote, produced, recorded, played, and sang everything himself — the credits list him solely for guitar, vocals, percussion, production, and lyrics (barring a trumpet appearance by Stefan Sporsén on “Broken Arrows”). The sole outside songwriter credit is The Knife (Karin and Olof Dreijer), credited for “Music By, Lyrics By” on the track “Heartbeats,” a cover of a song from their 2003 album Deep Cuts.

Cover art and sleeve design for Veneer were handled by Elias Araya, González’s longtime visual collaborator and future Junip bandmate. Mastering and mixing were done by Mikko Hellsing.

Veneer was released to modest attention in Sweden and Northern Europe. The turning point came in 2005 when his acoustic version of “Heartbeats” was licensed for a Sony Bravia television advertisement in the UK. The ad, which featured coloured balls cascading down a San Francisco street, became a major cultural moment; it brought both González and The Knife to international attention and drove heavy reissue and licensing activity on Veneer across multiple territories (the album eventually racked up 41 pressing variants in Discogs).

The single “Crosses / Heartbeats” appeared on the Swedish label Kass Recordings in 2004, followed by Put Your Hand On Your Heart (Imperial Recordings, 2004), a cover of Kylie Minogue’s “Hand On Your Heart.” The Stay In The Shade EP (Imperial, 2004) and Australian Tour EP (Imperial/Shock, 2005) extended the Veneer campaign into new territories.

In Our Nature and International Breakthrough (2006–2009)

For the second album González signed with Peacefrog Records (UK), which distributed through Atlantic in some markets. In Our Nature (2007, Peacefrog PFG114CD) was again written, produced, recorded, and performed largely by González himself. Key credits:

  • Backing vocals: Yukimi Nagano (also co-wrote “The Nest”)
  • Percussion: Erik Bodin
  • Synthesizer: Håkan Wirenstrand
  • Mixed by: González, Don Alsterberg, Kalle Gustafsson Jerneholm, Mattias Nyberg
  • Mastered by: Hans Olsson (Hans Olsson Brookes)
  • Artwork: Elias Araya

Track 5 “Teardrop” is a cover of Massive Attack’s song (co-written by Andrew Vowles, Elizabeth Fraser, Grant Marshall, and Robert Del Naja). Track 7 “Time To Send Someone Away” was co-written with Mattias Bergqvist. Track 8 “The Nest” was co-written with Yukimi Nagano.

The singles from this period include “Down The Line” (Peacefrog, 2007), “Killing For Love” (Imperial, 2007), and “Teardrop” (Peacefrog, 2007). “Down The Line” was mastered by Mandy Parnell and mixed by Michael H. Brauer. In Our Nature Remixes (Imperial, 2008) collected remixes including one by Todd Terje.

A documentary film, The Extraordinary Ordinary Life Of Jose Gonzalez (Svenska Filminstitutet, 2010), was directed by Mikel Cee Karlsson and Erik Hemmendorf.

Junip — Parallel Group Project (2000–2013)

Alongside his solo work, González has long been a core member of the band Junip, a trio also comprising Elias Araya (drums) and Tobias Winterkorn (keyboards). The group dates to around 2000 (their single “Straight Lines” appeared on the Kakafoni label that year). Their first substantial release was the Black Refuge EP (2005, Teme Shet), a limited Swedish pressing. They released the EP Fields in 2010 on City Slang, which served as a preview to their 2013 self-titled album Junip (City Slang, SLANG50045LP).

Junip (2013) was produced and recorded by Don Alsterberg, with mixing also handled by Hans Olsson Brookes. The band wrote and performed all instruments collectively.

Vestiges & Claws (2014–2016)

The third solo album Vestiges & Claws (Peacefrog PFG172, released 2015 in most markets) is González’s sparsest record in terms of credits — the Discogs entries list virtually no additional personnel beyond González himself. A limited UK test pressing appeared in 2014. It was released via Peacefrog Records in Europe, Mute Corporation / Imperial Recordings (4) in North America, and was later reissued on Mute.

The album yielded the single “Leaf Off / The Cave” (Imperial/Peacefrog, 2015) and the remix EP Let It Carry You (Remixes) (Peacefrog, 2016).

Film Work: The Secret Life of Walter Mitty (2013)

González appeared prominently on the soundtrack and score for Ben Stiller’s The Secret Life of Walter Mitty (2013), co-credited with composer Theodore Shapiro. González contributed vocals, guitar, and the track “Stay Alive” (released separately on Island Records / Republic Records). Other performers on the soundtrack include Of Monsters And Men. Executive producers on the soundtrack album were Ben Stiller and George Drakoulias.

Local Valley (2021)

Local Valley (City Slang / Imperial Recordings, 2021) marked a stylistic shift toward songs with Spanish and Swedish lyrics alongside English. González self-produced and recorded again; Hans Olsson handled mixing and mastering. Collaborators include:

  • Co-writing / guest vocals on “Swing”: Hannele Fernström (also responsible for album artwork throughout this period and onward)
  • Co-wrote “En Stund På Jorden”: Laleh Pourkarim (Laleh)
  • Co-wrote “Honey Honey”: Stefan Kozalla (DJ Koze)
  • “Line Of Fire” is credited to González, Araya, and Winterkorn — taken directly from the Junip catalog (it appears on Junip, 2013)

Local Valley was designed by Robert Samsonowitz, who has been the main graphic designer on González’s and Junip’s releases from Vestiges & Claws onward. The album was followed by Local Valley Remixes (City Slang / Imperial / Mute, 2023).

Against The Dying Of The Light (2026)

González’s most recent album, released in 2026 on Mute (cat. 1218-1). Self-produced and self-recorded; Hans Olsson mixed and mastered. Artwork by Hannele Fernström, design by Robert Samsonowitz. Thirteen tracks of solo acoustic folk.

Connections

  • The Knife — covered “Heartbeats” from Deep Cuts (2003); became the definitive moment of his career through the 2005 Sony Bravia ad
  • Massive Attack — covered “Teardrop” on In Our Nature (writers Andrew Vowles, Elizabeth Fraser, Grant Marshall, Robert Del Naja)
  • Kylie Minogue — covered “Hand On Your Heart” (2004 single)
  • Yukimi Nagano — of Little Dragon; backing vocals on In Our Nature, co-wrote “The Nest”
  • Junip — González’s ongoing trio with Elias Araya and Tobias Winterkorn
  • Stefan Kozalla (DJ Koze) — co-wrote “Honey Honey” on Local Valley
  • Laleh Pourkarim — co-wrote “En Stund På Jorden” on Local Valley
  • Of Monsters And Men — shared the Walter Mitty soundtrack
  • Jens Lekman — shared a split single; both part of Gothenburg indie scene
  • Ane Brun — shared compilation appearance
  • The Books, Sharon Jones & The Dap-Kings — shared split “Inspiration Information / Cello Song”
  • Zero 7 — González contributed vocals to The Garden (Atlantic, 2006) and Futures
