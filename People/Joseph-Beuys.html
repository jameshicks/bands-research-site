Joseph Beuys

German visual artist, performance artist, and art theorist. Born 1921, died 1986. Professor at the Kunstakademie Düsseldorf from 1961 to 1972. Discogs artist ID: 251144.

Overview

Beuys was one of the most influential figures in post-war European art, known for performance works (Aktionen), sculpture, and a political philosophy of art as social practice (“social sculpture”). His position at the Düsseldorf Kunstakademie made him a central node in the network that produced Krautrock: students including Eberhard Kranemann and Florian Schneider passed through his orbit, and Beuys actively championed Pissoff, the noise/improv group Kranemann co-founded in 1967, organising their performance at the Creamcheese Club.

A 1968 recording of a Creamcheese Club performance — Creamcheese Düsseldorf – 1968 (Rotorelief, 2021) — credits both Beuys and Pissoff as primary artists.
