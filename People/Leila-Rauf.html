Leila Rauf

Multi-instrumentalist, composer, and vocalist. Real name Leila Abdul-Rauf. San Francisco Bay Area. Discogs artist ID: 1225521.

Overview

Leila Rauf (Leila Abdul-Rauf) is a Bay Area musician active across experimental metal, doom, and noise projects. She is a member of Amber Asylum, contributing guitar, piano, synth, and voice on Bitter River (2009), and has sustained membership across a wide range of underground SF/Oakland heavy acts including Bastard Noise, Hammers Of Misfortune, Vastum, and Saros. Her Discogs member credits span over a dozen bands, reflecting a wide-ranging session and project presence in the Bay Area underground.
