Overview

Newcastle-based guitarist and singer/songwriter Billy Gilbert has been active in the UK punk and power pop scene since the early 1980s, recording and performing across a steady spread of decades through to the 2020s. His band history includes Penetration (2), Chelsea (2), 16 Forever, Automatic NE1, and New Kicks, with his closest recurring collaborators drawn from the Toy Dolls circle — Olga, Duncan Redmonds — and Arturo Bassick. Releases have appeared on a scatter of small UK and European punk labels, with Randale Records carrying the most. He currently records under the name The Tonighters.
