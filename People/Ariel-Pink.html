Ariel Pink

Real name: Ariel Marcus Rosenberg. Born June 24, 1978. Los Angeles-based lo-fi/hypnagogic pop artist, widely credited as a progenitor of the hypnagogic pop genre. Long associated with Mexican Summer.

Overview

Ariel Pink spent the bulk of the 2000s–2010s releasing home-recorded cassette pop under the name Ariel Pink’s Haunted Graffiti, building a cult following before signing to Mexican Summer, which brought his work to wider release. His 2010 album Before Today (Mexican Summer) marked a significant commercial and critical breakthrough. He continued releasing on Mexican Summer through the late 2010s. The label relationship ended abruptly in January 2021 following a political controversy.

Mexican Summer Period (approx. 2010–2021)

Key releases on Mexican Summer include Before Today (2010), Mature Themes (2012), pom pom (2014), and Dedicated to Bobby Jameson (2017). The label had planned additional archival vinyl releases in early 2021.

January 6, 2021 Controversy and Label Split

On January 6, 2021, Ariel Pink traveled to Washington, D.C. and attended the pro-Trump rally near the White House that preceded the storming of the U.S. Capitol. He was photographed in D.C. with fellow musician John Maus and director Alex Lee Moyer. Some reports initially placed him inside the Capitol; Pink disputed this.

On January 7 he posted on Twitter: “i was in dc to peacefully show my support for the president. i attended the rally on the white house lawn and went back to hotel and took a nap. case closed.” He also stated: “its true- i dont and never have advocated for violent confrontation or rioting.” (Pollstar, 2021-01-11)

Mexican Summer’s statement, posted to Twitter on January 8, 2021:

“Due to recent events, Mexican Summer and its staff have decided to end our working relationship with Ariel Rosenberg AKA Ariel Pink moving forward.”

(Pollstar, 2021-01-11; Brooklyn Vegan; Rolling Stone, 2021-01-09)

At the time of the split, Mexican Summer had planned to release several Ariel Pink archival vinyl LPs on January 29, 2021 (including Sit N’ Spin and Oddities Sodomies Vol. 3); these releases were cancelled. (Pollstar)

Additional controversies compounded the fallout: inflammatory comments Pink made in a December 2020 podcast resurfaced, and harassment allegations from an ex-girlfriend circulated. He appeared on Tucker Carlson’s Fox News program, stating the loss of label support left him “pretty much destitute and on the street.” (Tablet Magazine; Variety, 2021-01-11)

Post-Mexican Summer Activity

After the split, Pink released music independently and under his own imprints.

Ariel Pink’s Dark Side

Pink formed the group Ariel Pink’s Dark Side with producer Nick Noto, David Stagno, and Chloe Chaidez. Releases on the Dark Side Family Jams label (his own imprint):

  • The Key of Joy Is Disobedience (2022) — released September 2, 2022; initial run of 100 copies on limited red vinyl, also released digitally via Bandcamp (Discogs; Bandcamp)
  • Never Made A Demo, Ever (2023) — details (label, date, personnel) not yet confirmed

Pink also distributed material via Substack during this period.

Return as Ariel Pink

  • With You Every Night (2025) — released September 2025 on LAC Records (catalog LAC001), vinyl LP. Described as a spiritual successor to Dedicated to Bobby Jameson (2017). Received generally positive reviews. (Oxford Student review, 2025-09-13; X post confirming LAC001)
