French composer, multi-instrumentalist, and producer. Studied piano and harmony in a French conservatoire from age five; in her teens sang and played electric guitar in rock bands. Performs and records solo under her own name and as Klima. Joined Piano Magic around 2001 and became one of the project’s most consistent collaborators — 39 shared Discogs releases, credited variously on vocals, keyboards, dulcimer, guitar, piano, glockenspiel, clarinet, harmonium, Moog, and as songwriter. Her status within Piano Magic was always “a recurrent collaborator but not a permanent member per se,” with touring depending on her availability. Alongside the Piano Magic work, she ran the Klima solo project (Peacefrog, Second Language) and from 2013 increasingly moved toward contemporary classical territory under her own name on Village Green. In 2023 she re-orchestrated seven of her compositions for symphonic orchestra for the Force Majeure ballet, now part of the repertoire of Darmstadt’s state ballet and orchestra.

Ginger Ale

French electro-pop band, active in the early 2000s. She sang, played keyboard, and played electric guitar. Releases include Laid Back Galerie (2002). The Klima solo project had been on her mind since 2000 but was delayed partly by her Ginger Ale commitments before she eventually signed with Peacefrog in 2004.

Silver Servants

A Second Language label collective project. In 2010, Second Language invited its artists to Soup Studios in Shoreditch, London to improvise and record an album with one rule: move on after an hour. Glen Johnson and Jerome Tcherneyan (both of Piano Magic), Oliver Cherer, and others were among the assembled cast — the group quickly named themselves Silver Servants (a misheard “Civil Servants”). Eight hours produced eight sketches. Johnson and Cherer polished the recordings over several years; the album Silver Servants was released in 2014 on Second Language (SL030), with a companion Cold Lazarus EP (SL030x).
