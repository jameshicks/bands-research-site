Burial

Discogs ID: 306157
Real name: William Emmanuel Bevan
Alias: William Bevan (Discogs 1975486)
Origin: South London

Electronic recording artist. Music draws on future garage, dubstep, 2-step garage, ambient, and jungle. Releases almost exclusively on Hyperdub, the label founded by Kode9 (Steve Goodman). Identity kept anonymous until 2008. One of the most critically significant British electronic artists of the 2000s–2020s.

Release Notes

Burial (2006, HDBCD001)

Debut album. 13 tracks. Kode9 engineered. The Space Ape (Stephen Samuel Gordon) provides vocals on the track “Spaceape” — an early connection between the two artists on the Hyperdub roster.

Untrue (2007, HDBLP002)

Second and (as of 2025) most recent solo LP. Nine tracks over two vinyl sides. Considered definitive of the “burial sound” — heavily time-stretched and pitch-shifted vocal samples, static noise, rain and crowd textures. Photography by Georgina Cook.

Four Walls / Paradise Circus (2011, The Vinyl Factory VF034)

Released under the credit “Massive Attack vs. Burial.” Side B (“Paradise Circus”) features uncredited vocals by Hope Sandoval. Artwork by Robert Del Naja. Limited numbered 12”.

Ego / Mirror (2011) and Her Revolution / His Rope (2020)

Both on Text Records/XL with Four Tet and Thom Yorke; the two records form a loose trilogy with Moth / Wolf Cub (2009).

Antidawn EP (2022, HDBLP050)

Five-track digital EP; Burial’s most substantial solo release since Untrue. Catalogue number HDBLP050 marks Hyperdub’s 50th LP release.
