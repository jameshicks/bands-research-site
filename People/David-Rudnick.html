David Rudnick

David Rudnick

Discogs artist ID: 2458902 Origin: UK Active: 2009–present Practice described as: Graphic Designer at Terrain URLs: terra.in · davidrudnick.org · void.davidrudnick.org

UK-based graphic designer and visual artist. One of the most distinctive typographic voices in experimental electronic music from the early 2010s through the 2020s. His work appears across post-industrial techno (Perc Trax), witch house/experimental club (Tri Angle), UK underground (ManMakeMusic, Phantasy Sound), and internationally acclaimed albums for Nicolas Jaar, Oneohtrix Point Never, Black Midi, and Evian Christ. Credited both under his own name and uncredited across dozens of releases.

Biography

2009–2011 — Early Work: Phantasy Sound and Boysnoize Records

Rudnick’s earliest Discogs-documented design credit is an uncredited contribution to Erol Alkan’s “Bangkok” (Phantasy Sound, 2009). He was already in proximity to the UK dance underground centered on Phantasy Sound, ManMakeMusic, and adjacent imprints.

His first major credited role came with Spank Rock’s album Everything Is Boring & Everyone Is A F---ing Liar (Boysnoize Records, 2011), where he received credit for Art Direction, Layout Design, and Typography — the most prominent and fully attributed design role in his early career. The project connected him to the Boysnoize Records world (Boys Noize as executive producer and mixer) and established his reputation for typographic work within hip-hop-adjacent electronic contexts.

2012–2013 — ManMakeMusic, Phantasy Sound, and the London Scene

Over 2012–2013 Rudnick became the near-default designer for the ManMakeMusic label, contributing uncredited design work to at least eight releases: Fernweh / Hearts (2011), Gold Language EP (2012), For Her (2013), Laszlo Dancehall EP (2013), Leslie Crowther (2013), Slime (2013), Edits Volume 1 (2013), Erosion Flow EP (2014), and more. He was simultaneously working across Phantasy Sound releases (Knights, 2012; EP 1, 2013; Through Me / Nothing But Pleasure, 2013; Without Armour, 2013).

For the Canadian label Turbo, he contributed design to at least four releases in 2013–2014 — Ghost Systems Rave, Saturday Night Fervor, Trust Your Body, Download EP, and Stronghold EP — spreading his reach into the transatlantic techno/electro circuit.

He also contributed Artwork to the standalone Lemonade release (Phantasy Sound / Because Music, 2012) and graphic design to Temple Fortune EP (Civil Music, 2012).

2014 — Tri Angle and the Evian Christ Collaboration

In 2014 Rudnick contributed Artwork and Typography to Evian Christ’s Waterfall EP (Tri Angle, TRIANGLE22) — his highest-profile credit to that point and the beginning of a recurring visual partnership with Evian Christ. The EP was Evian Christ’s final Tri Angle release, mixed by Joshua Leary and Noah Rubin, mastered by Heba Kadry.

Also in 2014 he contributed Artwork to two releases on WeDidIt Records (US): Core and Void, extending his US reach.

2015 — George FitzGerald and Double Six

Rudnick contributed Art Direction to George FitzGerald’s debut album Fading Love (Double Six (2), 2015) and the associated single Full Circle — two releases on the Domino-distributed Double Six Records imprint. This marked his entry into artist-album level art direction for a broader commercial context.

2016 — Nicolas Jaar’s Sirens

A pivotal credit: Rudnick is listed alongside Nicolas Jaar himself for Graphic Design and Art Direction on Sirens (Other People, 2016) — Jaar’s politically charged second album. Jaar’s father, the artist Alfredo Jaar, contributed cover photography. The Sirens credit represents Rudnick’s first significant co-art-direction with the artist rather than a standalone design commission.

He also contributed uncredited design to Igneous (Perc Trax, 2016) — a split release by Perc (Alistair Wells) and Randomer on Perc Trax.

Also in 2016: uncredited design on “TT / Love” (Young Turks, 2015, one pressing year discrepancy).

2017 — Katy Perry’s Witness

Rudnick received front-and-back cover Art Direction credit on Katy Perry’s album Witness (Capitol Records, 2017) — his most mainstream commission and the widest-distribution work in his Discogs catalog (28 pressings logged). The overall Art Direction was shared with Nicole Frantz; Rudnick held the cover-specific credit. Witness is the outlier in his body of work — pop mainstream against his otherwise experimental-electronic practice.

2018 — Oneohtrix Point Never, Nicolas Jaar, R&S Records

2018 marks Rudnick’s deepest engagement with the Warp Records world:

  • Oneohtrix Point Never (Age Of, Warp Records, 2018): Design credited alongside Jim Shaw’s artwork. OPN’s most ambitious album to date.
  • Oneohtrix Point Never (Love In The Time Of Lexapro, Warp Records, 2018): Design on the EP. Cover painting by Christian Riese Lassen.
  • Nicolas Jaar (Nymphs, R&S Records, 2018): Design — Jaar’s album on the Belgian techno institution R & S Records.
  • Perc Trax (Heavy The Eclipse, Electric Deluxe, 2018): Design on the Perc release for Netherlands label.
  • WeDidIt Records (Nova, 2018): Artwork.

2019 — Black Midi Debut

Rudnick contributed Design to Black Midi’s debut album Schlagenheim (Rough Trade, 2019) — produced by Dan Carey and mixed by Dan Carey as well — alongside engineer Alexis Smith and mastering by Christian Wright. This started the Black Midi design relationship.

He also designed the cover for Dark Morph (The Vinyl Factory, 2019) — a three-pressing release on the London-based label.

2020–2022 — Black Midi Albums and Continued Warp Work

The Black Midi run continued through their entire Rough Trade album sequence:

  • Cavalcade (Rough Trade, 2021) — Design credited solely to Rudnick
  • Hellfire (Rough Trade, 2022) — Design shared with Emiel Penninck and Rani Yasmine Putri

He also contributed Typography to Oneohtrix Point Never’s Again (Warp Records, 2023) — typography specifically, alongside layout by Elijah Funk and artwork by Matias Faldbakken.

Additional 2020–2022 credits: Sweater / 7-Eleven (Rough Trade, 2020); Stellar Drifting (Double Six (2), 2022).

2023 — Evian Christ’s Revanchist

Rudnick designed Evian Christ’s debut album Revanchist (Warp Records, 2023), completing a nine-year arc from the Waterfall EP (2014) to Evian Christ’s proper full-length. CGI photogrammetry by Ruben Fro, retouching by Sathyan Rizzo.

He also contributed Typography to OPN’s Again (Warp, 2023) in the same year.
