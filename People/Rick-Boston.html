Rick Boston

Discogs ID: 280570
Real name: Richard Wallie Boston
Origin: Los Angeles, California, USA
Roles: Guitarist, vocalist, songwriter, producer

Los Angeles-based multi-instrumentalist and record producer. Active from the late 1980s across indie rock, alternative, and major-label pop contexts. His career divides into three broad phases: bandleader/co-founder (Low Pop Suicide, Hand Of Fate), producer-for-hire on major indie and major-label projects (Rickie Lee Jones, Alice Cooper), and ongoing session and collaborative work. Worked for Qualified Sound Technologies, a LA production company.

Career

Late 1980s — Touring Guitarist

First documented credit: guitarist on Belinda Carlisle’s Live! (1988, Virgin Music Video, VVD395) — a VHS concert release featuring her band including Donna de Lory (backing vocals), Bekka Bramlett (backing vocals), Denny Fongheiser (drums), and Eric Pressly (bass).

1990 — Hand Of Fate

Co-founded Hand Of Fate with vocalist/co-producer John Keck, bassist Rick May, and drummer Jerry Angel. Self-titled LP released on Epic (cat. 467735 2, Europe/1990) — New Wave rock. Boston produced and played guitar.

Also credited as Written-By on Walking On A Wire (1990, Chameleon Records 2) — another artist’s release.

1992–1994 — Low Pop Suicide

Core member of Low Pop Suicide (Discogs ID 280569), the LA indie rock band co-founded with Dave Allen (Gang of Four bassist), drummer Jeff Ward, and drummer Melle Steagal. Boston served as guitarist (“psychick guitar” credit on Exit At The Axis), vocalist, producer, and songwriter throughout the band’s Capitol Records / World Domination Recordings period.

The Death Of Excellence was the band’s final LP (12 tracks); co-produced with Michael Vail Blum. Mark Leonard played bass.

Also credited as Additional Musician on The Elastic Purejoy’s The Elastic Purejoy (1994, WDR) — a Dave Allen side project.

1994 — Film Work

Producer and songwriter on The Harvest (1994, True North) — the original motion picture soundtrack for a horror film of the same name. Japan release (3 pressings).

1996 — Solo EP and Freelance

Released Numb (1996, World Domination Recordings, WD0041-2) — his only primary solo credit in Discogs. An 8-track alternative rock EP. Credited with production; recording and mixing by Frank Crank; Jessy Greene plays violin and voice; design by Scott Hyers.

Track overlap with Low Pop Suicide’s catalog: “Humbled,” “Zombie,” “Suicide Ego,” “Life & Death,” “Bless My Body” all appear on The Death Of Excellence. Numb appears to be a re-recording or compilation of those tracks.

Also contributed guitar and mixing (tracks 1, 2, 7) to The Elastic Purejoy / Dave Allen’s The Clutter Of Pop (1996, True North, Canada).

1997 — Rickie Lee Jones, John Taylor

Rickie Lee Jones Ghostyhead (1997, Reprise Records, CDW 46557) — co-produced with Rickie Lee Jones. A 10-track Trip Hop/downtempo album. Engineers: Barry Goldberg (2) and John Nelson; mastered by Tom Baker (3). Also produced the “Firewalker” single (5-mix maxi-CD, Reprise 9 43882-2) with remixes by David Kahne, Mr. Spring, and RH Factor.

John Taylor Feelings Are Good And Other Lies (1997, Revolver Records 3, REV XD 215) — credited as Performer. Taylor’s post-Duran Duran solo LP; other performers include Roy Hay (Culture Club), Steve Jones (Sex Pistols), and John Shanks.

2000 — The Januaries

Member and primary creative contributor to The Januaries (Discogs ID 215014) — guitar, bass, backing vocals, programming, and producer on the self-titled debut (2000, Foodchain Records). Other members: Debbie Diamond, John Nau, Mitch Maker, Petur Smith, Tobias Kroon.

2005 — Alice Cooper

Producer (with Steve Lindsey (4)) and session player on Alice Cooper’s Dirty Diamonds (2005, Spitfire Records, SPITCD257). Co-wrote six tracks including “Woman Of Mass Distraction,” “Dirty Diamonds,” “You Make Me Wanna,” “Stand” (with Xzibit), “Zombie Dance,” and “Six Hours.” Played acoustic and electric guitar, 12-string guitar, arranged horns on the title track. Mastered by Gavin Lurssen; mixed by Dave Way; engineered by Gabe Veltri and Adam Hawkins.

2008–2009

Guitarist on My Kind Of Holiday (2008, TRP Records) and Heaven On Earth (2009, Virgin) — both unidentified primary artists in Discogs data.
