Kimya Dawson

Discogs ID: 534786
Real name: Kimya Camia Dawson
Born: November 17, 1972, USA

American singer-songwriter. Central figure in the New York anti-folk scene alongside Jeffrey Lewis, Adam Green, and others. Primarily acoustic, lo-fi folk with confessional, politically charged, and often absurdist lyrics. Her most widespread public exposure came from The Moldy Peaches’ “Anyone Else But You” appearing in the 2007 film Juno — the song and the film’s soundtrack introduced her to a mainstream audience mid-career. She also has a hip-hop crossover project, The Uncluded, with Aesop Rock.

The Moldy Peaches

The Moldy Peaches (Discogs ID 84911) — the group originally conceived as a pseudonym for Adam Green in 1994. Dawson joined as a full member in 1998; from 1999 they were a full band with a shifting lineup of additional guitarists, drummers, and bassists. The lineup on their only studio album (self-titled, 2000, Rough Trade) consists of Green, Dawson, and Chris Barron. Barron left in 2001; the group broke up 2004. Dawson and Green reunited several times between 2007 and 2011.

Key Moldy Peaches releases:

  • The Moldy Peaches (2000, Rough Trade) — self-titled studio album; includes “Anyone Else But You”
  • Unreleased Cutz & Live Jamz 1994–2002 (2002, self-released)
  • “County Fair / Rainbows” (2002, Rough Trade, UK single)
  • “Anyone Else But You” (2008, Rough Trade) — single re-released on Juno film exposure
  • Origin Story 1994–1999 (2022, ORG Music) — archival compilation

The Uncluded

The Uncluded (Discogs ID 2719519) — hip-hop/folk crossover duo with Aesop Rock (Ian Bavitz, ID 815717). Both members write, record, and perform. James McNew (Yo La Tengo bassist, ID 365541) plays drums on “Delicate Cycle.” Executive-produced by Dawson, Bavitz, Sean Daley (Slug of Atmosphere), and Brent Sayers. Anti-Folk Scene Connections

Dawson’s anti-folk roots connect her to the AFNY (Anti-Folk New York) community documented on AFNY Collaborations Volume 1 (Pro Anti, 2002), which features Jeffrey Lewis, Diane Cluck, and others. Her split with Jeffrey Lewis (Kimya Dawson And Jeff Lewis, 2001) is a key early document. She also collaborated with Matty Pop Chart (Matt Tobey, ID 563157) on multiple K Records releases.

Juno (2007)

The Moldy Peaches’ “Anyone Else But You” (from the 2000 self-titled album) was used in Jason Reitman’s film Juno (2007) and performed by the lead actors. Rough Trade reissued the song as a single in 2008. Several of Dawson’s solo songs appeared on the film’s soundtrack — her highest-profile mainstream exposure. No Juno soundtrack is credited as a primary release in her Discogs discography (it appears as a Various Artists compilation).

Aesop Rock Connection

Aesop Rock (ID 28104) appears as a collaborator across 2 shared releases: The Uncluded work and a Daytrotter session. Dawson also appears on Aesop Rock’s The Impossible Kid (2016, Rhymesayers) on the track “Tuff.”
