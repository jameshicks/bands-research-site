Chris Zane

Chris Zane

Discogs ID: 261842
Origin: New York City, USA
Role: Producer, mixer, engineer, multi-instrumentalist
Base: Gigantic Studios, NYC (credited from at least 2004 onward)

Producer and mixer central to the NYC indie rock scene of the mid-2000s through mid-2010s. Primary production partner of engineer Alex Aldi; together they formed the core team at Gigantic Studios across roughly 2005–2015. Also plays drums and percussion on select sessions. Active from 2001 as an assistant engineer; still active as of 2024–2026 (mixing credits on recent releases). Name variations on releases include “C. Zane”, “Chris ‘Specs’ Zane”, and “Chris Zane (Kele)“.

Biography / Career Overview

Early Work (2001–2004)

Zane entered the industry as an assistant engineer; his first Discogs credit is as “Engineer [Assistant]” on a 2001 release (Sweet & Lowdown, Justin Time). Through 2001–2004 he moved quickly into full engineering and producing roles, working with indie-rock acts on Arena Rock and early Frenchkiss Records releases. The Gigantic Music/Gigantic Studios name appears from at least 2004 (credited on a Bar/None release that year). Early production clients include The Boggs, Human Television, and Cloud Room.

Mid-Period: Frenchkiss Core and NYC Indie (2005–2010)

The core of his reputation was built across these five years, predominantly in the orbit of Frenchkiss Records and associated NYC / UK indie labels. Key partnerships:

  • Les Savy Fav: Produced, recorded, and mixed both Let’s Stay Friends (2007, Frenchkiss) and Root For Ruin (2010, Wichita). Alex Aldi served as 2nd engineer on both.
  • White Rabbits: Produced Fort Nightly (2007, Academy Fight Song); Aldi mixed; Zane also contributed additional percussion.
  • The Walkmen: Recorded and mixed You & Me (2008) alongside second engineer Aldi and co-recorder John Agnello; mixed selected tracks on Lisbon (2010, sessions at Gigantic Studios with Aldi as second engineer).
  • Passion Pit: Produced and mixed Manners (2009, Frenchkiss/Columbia) — Aldi recorded and co-mixed. The relationship carried through Gossamer (2012) and Kindred (2015), the latter with Benny Blanco (3) co-producing select tracks.
  • Tokyo Police Club: Produced one track on Elephant Shell (2008, Saddle Creek).
  • The Hundred In The Hands: Produced and mixed two tracks on the self-titled (2010, Warp); also contributed live drums. Aldi mixed those same tracks.
  • Klang (V2, 2009): Full producer/recorder/mixer credit.
  • Harlem Shakes: Mixed Burning Birthdays (2006) and produced Technicolor Health (2009, Gigantic Music).
  • Transparence (Polyvinyl, 2009): Producer/mixer/percussion.

He also worked in this period with Wall Of Sound acts (Olympic Swimmers, Two Wounded Birds), One Little Indian acts (Lali Puna-adjacent and others), and Wichita acts.

Later Period (2011–2026)

Post-2012 Zane moved increasingly into mixing work alongside continued producing. Notable credits:

  • Passion Pit Kindred (2015, Columbia): Full producer/engineer/drummer credit, still alongside Aldi.
  • DFA Records orbit: Engineer on a 2010 XL/DFA single; producer/engineer on Obsessive Compulsive Frequency Vol. 3 (DFA/Eskuché, 2011); mixed Dumb Disco Ideas and Dynamics (DFA, 2013).
  • Apar (True Panther Sounds, 2013): Co-producer/mixer/percussion.
  • Jinja Safari (Island, 2013): Mixer.
  • Matter (Columbia/Neon Gold, 2016): Producer.
  • Songs For Morning And Evening Times (Smooth Jaws, 2021): Co-producer/mixer.
  • Summerskin (Dew Process, 2022): Co-producer/mixer.
  • Oui, LSF (Frenchkiss, 2024): Mixer — returning to Les Savy Fav’s live/archival release.
  • Whiplash (Nettwerk, 2024): Producer/mixer.
  • Away With The Fairies (2026): Mixing engineer.

Connections

  • Close working relationship with Alex Aldi from approximately 2005–2015; Aldi appears as second engineer or mix co-credit on virtually every major Zane production of that era.
  • Gigantic Music is the label imprint associated with Gigantic Studios; both appear on early-to-mid 2000s releases. The relationship between the studio and the label is unclear from Discogs data alone.
  • Regular mastering chain: Greg Calbi at Sterling Sound for many of the Frenchkiss-era releases.
  • Connected to the DFA orbit through engineering credits (2010–2013) — including work adjacent to LCD Soundsystem / DFA acts.
  • The Hundred In The Hands credit shows him contributing live drums on a Warp release, indicating he functions as a session musician when needed, not only as a studio engineer.
  • Les Savy Fav relationship spans the full arc: Let’s Stay Friends (2007), Root For Ruin (2010), Oui, LSF (2024).

Open Questions

  • Gigantic Studios: Exact founding date, ownership structure, and current operational status unknown from Discogs. The studio name appears as a credit from at least 2004. Requires web research to confirm.
  • Gigantic Music vs. Gigantic Studios: The label imprint “Gigantic Music” appears on releases 2004–2009 (Cloud Room, Human Television, Harlem Shakes, Forts). Relationship to the studio unclear — may be same entity or related.
  • “Chris Zane (Kele)” name variation: Suggests production work with Kele Okereke (Bloc Party) but no specific release surfaces clearly in the credits list under this alias — worth investigating.
  • Discogs profile is minimal (no realname, no bio). Some releases in 2005–2009 may be missing from the DB.
