Niall McMenamin

Recording engineer and assistant producer, based in Glasgow. Discogs artist ID: 2015808. Closely associated with Mogwai and their Rock Action Records label; primary in-house engineer at Castle of Doom, Mogwai’s own Glasgow studio, from at least 2011 onward. Career began in 2009 as assistant engineer on commercial pop and orchestral sessions before pivoting fully into the Glasgow post-rock ecosystem.

Overview

McMenamin’s name first appears on Discogs in 2009, assisting senior engineer Stuart McCredie on two releases by the Glasgow art-pop group Codeine Velvet Club — both on Island Records. Those sessions were produced by the band’s own John Lawler alongside McCredie and were large-scale productions (orchestra, choir, brass); McMenamin was in the assistant seat for both the album Codeine Velvet Club (2009) and the single Vanity Kills (2009).

By 2011 he had moved into Castle of Doom, Mogwai’s studio in Glasgow, and is credited on the Earth Division EP specifically as “Recorded By [At Castle Of Doom]” — his first documented credit in that room. From that point, his association with Paul Savage (Mogwai’s long-standing engineer/producer, also working out of Castle of Doom and Chem 19 studio) becomes the defining professional relationship in his recorded output.

His role deepened across each successive Mogwai project:

  • 2011 (Hardcore Will Never Die, But You Will.) — additional recording and assisted Paul Savage on the mix. Savage held the primary “Recorded By” and “Mixed By” credits; McMenamin was credited alongside him in both.
  • 2013 (Les Revenants, Les Revenants EP) — co-producer and sole recording engineer. This is his most elevated billing in the Mogwai catalog: Savage is absent from the recording credit on these releases, with McMenamin holding both “Producer” and “Recorded By” alongside Mogwai themselves. Tony Doogan handled the mix.
  • 2014 (Rave Tapes) — assistant producer, assistant recording engineer, assistant mix engineer. Savage returns as the senior person in all three roles, with McMenamin explicitly credited as [Assistant] across all of them.

The Les Revenants work is notable in context: Mogwai scored the Canal+ French TV drama of the same name, and both the full LP and the companion EP credit McMenamin as co-producer — a significant step up from the purely technical-assistant role he occupied on Hardcore and would return to on Rave Tapes.

Outside Mogwai’s own recordings, he captured the debut Rock Action EP by Sacred Paws (Six Songs, 2015), again at Castle of Doom (Tony Doogan mixed). His one outlier in Discogs is a “Deacon Blue Album Crew” credit on The Great Western Road (2025, Cooking Vinyl) — an album-crew listing rather than a named engineering role, suggesting a Glasgow session or logistical involvement rather than a primary technical credit on that record.

Name variation on Discogs: Nial McMenanmin (a typo variant, not a separate alias).

Connections

  • Paul Savage — senior engineer/producer at Castle of Doom and Chem 19; McMenamin worked under Savage on Earth Division EP, Hardcore Will Never Die, and Rave Tapes. On Les Revenants, the two shared a co-producer role with Savage absent from the recording credit.
  • Mogwai — primary client across the Castle of Doom years; seven release credits between 2011 and 2014
  • Rock Action Records — Mogwai’s own label, home to all his post-2011 work except the 2025 Deacon Blue album
  • Sacred Paws — recorded their Rock Action debut EP Six Songs (2015); Tony Doogan mixed
  • Tony Doogan — mixed both Les Revenants (LP and EP) and Six Songs; a recurring Glasgow-scene mixer
  • Stuart McCredie — senior engineer on the Codeine Velvet Club sessions, McMenamin’s earliest credited role

Open Questions
