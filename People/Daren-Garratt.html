Overview

Daren Garratt is a drummer and percussionist best known as a member of The Fall, the long-running Manchester post-punk group led by Mark E. Smith, as well as Pram and Marshall Smith (2). His Discogs credits span from 1999 into the mid-2020s, with the bulk of his recorded output concentrated in the 2010s across styles that include post-punk, indie rock, and Krautrock. His recurring collaborators include core Fall members Eleni Poulou, Keiron Melling, Peter Greenway, Dave Spurr, and Alan Apperley, alongside Robert Lloyd of The Nightingales. Cherry Red has been his most consistent label presence, accounting for five releases between 2013 and 2025.
