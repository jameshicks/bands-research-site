Joan As Police Woman

Joan Wasser; singer-songwriter, violinist, and multi-instrumentalist. Discogs artist ID: 542128

NYC-based indie art-pop solo project. Began releasing under the Joan As Police Woman name in 2004; debut album Real Life on Reveal Records / [pias] (2006) established her as a significant figure in UK and European indie. Long-running mastering relationship with Fred Kevorkian across 57+ release entries, 2006 onward. Also collaborated with Tony Allen and Dave Okumu on The Solution Is Restless (2021, [pias]).
