Mats Gustafsson

Discogs ID: 184421 Born: 1964, Umeå, Sweden Role: Saxophonist, improviser, label operator Active: ~1984–present

Swedish free jazz and free improvisation saxophonist. A central figure in European free improvisation; runs the label Omlott. 20 shared Discogs releases with Merzbow. Also a member of The Thing (with Ingebrigt Håker Flaten and Paal Nilssen-Love) and frequent collaborator with Peter Brötzmann, Ken Vandermark, and others. Appeared on The Ex + Brass Unbound’s Enormous Door (2013, Ex Records); 3 shared releases with The Ex.

Rune Grammofon Work

Primary artist on Rune Grammofon as leader of Fire! (33 releases, 2009–2024) and Fire! Orchestra (24 releases, 2013–2023). 59 performer/arranger/conductor credits and 27 writer credits on the label. Notable collaborations recorded for the label: Stones with Colin Stetson (2012), In The Mouth - A Hand with Oren Ambarchi via Fire! (2012), What / If / They Both Could Fly with Evan Parker and Joe McPhee (2013), Actions (Fire! Orchestra + Krzysztof Penderecki, 2020).
