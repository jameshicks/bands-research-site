Brian Hodgson

Discogs ID: 131042 Alias: Nikki St. George Active: 1962–1995 (BBC Radiophonic Workshop); ongoing

British electronic music composer and sound designer. Member of the BBC Radiophonic Workshop (1962–1972; returned as organiser 1977–1995). Created the TARDIS materialisation/dematerialisation sound for Doctor Who by dragging keys along the rusted bass strings of a broken piano and slowing the recording. Co-founded Unit Delta Plus (1966) with Delia Derbyshire and Peter Zinovieff, and subsequently the Kaleidophon studio with Derbyshire and David Vorhaus, which produced White Noise’s debut An Electric Storm (Island, 1968). Also a member of Electrophon (2), Trane (3), and Wavemaker.
