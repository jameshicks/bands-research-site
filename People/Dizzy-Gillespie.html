Dizzy Gillespie

Discogs ID: 64694 Real name: John Birks Gillespie Aliases: “Izzy” Goldberg; B. Bopstein; John Birks; John Kildare

American jazz trumpeter, composer, bandleader, and key co-architect of bebop. Born October 21, 1917, Cheraw, South Carolina. Died January 6, 1993, Englewood, New Jersey.

One of the founders of bebop alongside Charlie Parker, Gillespie led big bands and small groups from the 1940s onward. Benny Golson played tenor saxophone and contributed arrangements in Gillespie’s band 1956–1958, appearing on At Newport (Verve, 1957), Birks’ Works (Verve, 1958), and Dizzy In Greece (Verve, 1957). The Jazztet composition “Are You Real?” was also performed by Gillespie’s groups.
