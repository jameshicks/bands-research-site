Buzzy Feiten

Real name: Howard R. Feiten II. American guitarist, singer/songwriter, producer, arranger, and luthier. Discogs artist ID: 320003.

Feiten joined The Paul Butterfield Blues Band in 1969, playing guitar, piano, organ, and even French horn on Keep On Moving — the band’s move into large-ensemble R&B. After leaving Butterfield around 1971 he became one of the most in-demand session guitarists in New York and Los Angeles across the 1970s and 1980s, appearing on records by Bob Dylan, Stevie Wonder, Gregg Allman, Rickie Lee Jones, Don McLean, Gene Clark, and many others. In the late 1970s he teamed with keyboardist Neil Larsen to form the Larsen-Feiten Band (also recorded as Full Moon (5)), releasing two albums on Warner Bros. He later became widely known in the guitar world as the inventor of the Buzz Feiten Tuning System, a patented compensated-nut intonation system, and as the principal of Supernova Guitar Research. He has also played electric bass and French horn on record.

Name variations on record: Buzz Feiten, Buzz “Stoney” Feiten, Howard “Buzz” Feiten, Howard “Buzzy” Feiten, and numerous misspellings (Faiten, Feiton, Felten, Felton, Fieton).
