Harald Grosskopf

German drummer and electronic musician. Born 1949. Played drums in Wallenstein; associated with Klaus Schulze and the Berlin School / kosmische scene. Discogs artist ID: 99472.

Overview

Grosskopf is best known as the drummer on Klaus Schulze’s early albums and for his 1980 solo album Synthesist (originally on Sky Records), which merged electronic music with acoustic drums. Bureau B has reissued and issued new recordings (24+ entries), active on the label from 2014 to 2024.

Connections

  • Bureau B — primary reissue label
  • Sky Records — original label (Synthesist)
  • Eberhard Kranemann — co-equal collaboration on Krautwerk (2017, Bureau B) as the duo Krautwerk (2)
