Greg Puciato

Discogs ID: 972690 Real name: Gregory John Puciato (born March 27, 1980) Based: Los Angeles, California Roles: Vocalist, songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, label operator

American vocalist and songwriter best known as the frontman of The Dillinger Escape Plan (joined September 2001; band dissolved 2017) and as co-founder of The Black Queen. Post-Dillinger he has operated primarily as a solo artist on his own Federal Prisoner imprint (Los Angeles, founded co-operatively with Jesse Draxler) and is a member of the supergroup Killer Be Killed. He joined Dillinger in a scenario analogous to the young Henry Rollins joining Black Flag — as a fan who sent in an audition tape after the band publicly sought a replacement for original vocalist Dimitri Minakakis. His first recording with the band was a Black Flag cover for a tribute compilation. Across his career he has worked across mathcore, industrial, synth-pop, and solo electronic territory, maintaining notable cross-genre guest credit activity.

Project Notes

The Dillinger Escape Plan: Puciato joined after band parted ways with original vocalist Dimitri Minakakis. Core lineup during Puciato era: Benjamin Weinman (guitar, primary composer), Liam Wilson (2) (bass), and rotating drummers including Chris Pennie and Billy Rymer. Irony Is A Dead Scene (2002, Epitaph) paired the band with Mike Patton as co-vocalist, co-producer, and co-writer. Producer and engineer Steve Evetts handled One Of Us Is The Killer (2013) and Dissociation (2016); Josh Wilbur provided additional engineering on Dissociation and mixed it alongside Kurt Ballou. Alan Douches mastered Irony Is A Dead Scene and One Of Us Is The Killer. Final album Dissociation (2016) was the only Dillinger LP with strings (arranged and performed by SEVEN)SUNS) and featured additional drums from Zach Hill.

The Black Queen: Co-founded with Joshua Eustis (Telefon Tel Aviv, Nine Inch Nails, Puscifer) and Steven Alexander Ryan. All writing, production, mixing, and mastering credited collectively to the band. Justin Meldal-Johnsen served as executive producer on the 2015–2016 EPs and debut LP. Jesse Draxler handled all visual work across the catalog. Infinite Games (2018) was the first release on Federal Prisoner (cat# FP001).

Killer Be Killed: Supergroup with Max Cavalera (Sepultura/Cavalera Conspiracy), Troy Sanders (Mastodon), and Dave Elitch (drums on debut) / Ben Koller (drums on Reluctant Hero). Both albums produced by Josh Wilbur. Juan Montoya (guitar, additional) appears on both records. Reluctant Hero (2020) mastered by Ted Jensen.

Better Lovers: Formed with members of Every Time I Die — Jordan Buckley (guitar), Steve Micciche (bass), Clayton Holyoak (drums) — and producer Will Putney (guitar). Signed to SharpTone. Released EP God Made Me An Animal (2023) and debut LP Highly Irresponsible (2024).

Error (3): Short-lived project with Atticus Ross, Leopold Ross, and Brett Gurewitz (Bad Religion / Epitaph founder). One self-titled album, Epitaph, 2004.

Spylacopa: Formed with Jeff Caxide (ISIS), John LaMacchia (Coheed and Cambria), and Julie Christmas. Released on Rising Pulse Records.
