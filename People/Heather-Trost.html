Heather Trost

Discogs ID: 519783
Name variations: H. Trost, Heather, Heather Troste, Trost
Bandcamp: https://heathertrost.bandcamp.com/

Violinist, violist, and vocalist from Albuquerque, New Mexico. She is the co-leader and co-founder of A Hawk And A Hacksaw alongside drummer Jeremy Barnes, the Eastern-European-folk-inflected project through which she has released the bulk of her work since 2002. She also recorded as a member of early ambient/drone project Opion Somnium with Barnes and Zach Condon before Condon’s career as Beirut took off — Trost subsequently contributed strings to several Beirut albums. As a solo artist she has released records under her own name on LM Dupli-Cation and Third Man Records, working across folk, psych, and chamber pop with an expanding instrumental palette (violin, viola, Stroh violin, Mellotron, ARP synthesizer, Davolisint, harpsichord, autoharp). In 2024 she joined the psychedelic/North African-influenced project Dhana Jeera with Barnes and a wider Albuquerque-connected ensemble.

Connections

  • A Hawk And A Hacksaw — co-leader with Jeremy Barnes; core project since 2002, based in Albuquerque and Budapest; draws on Eastern European and Balkan folk
  • Opion Somnium — pre-Beirut drone/ambient trio with Barnes and Zach Condon; two releases 2002–2004
  • Beirut — Trost contributed violin/viola to Gulag Orkestar (2006), The Flying Club Cup (2007 Russian pressing), and The Rip Tide (2011); early Beirut personnel overlap substantially with A Hawk And A Hacksaw
  • Dhana Jeera — 2024 psychedelic/North African collective; also includes John Fernandes, Jeremy Barnes, Steven Ledbetter, David McDonnell, and others
  • Swans — credited (Stroh violin, violin, viola, fiddle; recording) on Leaving Meaning. (2019, Young God Records)
  • Josephine Foster — collaborated on Blood Rushing (2012, Fire Records); connected through Albuquerque/New Mexico scene
