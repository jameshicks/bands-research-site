Overview

James McAlister is a drummer, percussionist, and composer originally from Tulsa, Oklahoma, now based in Los Angeles, with Discogs credits running back to 1999. He came up playing with Pedro the Lion and Ester Drang before establishing himself as one of the more in-demand players in indie and folk rock, accumulating 376 credits across a range of band and session roles. The dominant association across his career is with Sufjan Stevens (81 shared releases), with Asthmatic Kitty Records as his anchor label for nearly two decades (17 works, 2005–2023). From the mid-2010s onward he increasingly appears in the Aaron Dessner–adjacent orbit — with Rob Moose, Thomas Bartlett, Jonathan Low, and Ben Lanz as recurring partners — on releases for 4AD, Atlantic, and Eastern Glow Recordings through the early 2020s.
