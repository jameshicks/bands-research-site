Steve Hopkins

Keyboardist, programmer, producer. Born 14 May 1951, Manchester. Discogs artist ID: 328080. Alias: The Mercurian.

Overview

Core member and co-producer of The Invisible Girls, the Manchester-based backing ensemble led by Martin Hannett. Hopkins handled keyboards and synthesizer programming, and co-wrote several tracks. His Invisible Girls work includes the Pauline Murray LP Pauline Murray and the Invisible Girls (Illusive, 1980), the singles “Dream Sequences” and “Searching for Heaven” (both Illusive, 1980–1981), and the full John Cooper Clarke catalog (1978–1982).

Also produced Artery (2)‘s releases on Aardvark: Unbalanced / The Slide (STEEL 3, 1980, co-produced with the band) and Cars In Motion (AARD 5, 1981).

Beyond the Invisible Girls, Hopkins played piano on Happy Mondays’ Bummed (Factory, 1988) — part of Martin Hannett’s return engagement at Factory. He also worked with Jilted John (Ed Garrity), Morrissey (Ouija Board, Ouija Board — Sire/Reprise, 1989; piano), and contributed to sessions by Distant Cousins. His Discogs contributor credits run to 30+ releases across 1978–2020. He produced “Love At The Haçienda” (Wicked Frog, 1985) and the Jazz Defektors’ CBS/Sony album (1987). From 1991, Hopkins pursued a career in experimental cold atom physics, eventually joining Durham University’s Atomic and Molecular Physics group. (Source: Wikipedia, “Stephen Hopkins (musician)”, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Hopkins_(musician))

Both as Steve Hopkins and Stephen Hopkins on credits; Discogs uses Steve Hopkins.
