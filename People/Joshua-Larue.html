Joshua Larue

Real name: Joshua LaRue (capital R; Discogs canonical spelling)
Discogs ID: 584096
Also credited as: Josh Larue, Josh LaRue, Joshua La Rue, Joshua “Peg” LaRue, Jose Larue, jLa, JL
Alias on Discogs: Josh Payne (2)

American multi-instrumentalist from Texas. Plays guitar, bass, piano, electric piano, trombone, and drums. Active from the early 1990s onward across a wide range of projects — from Austin garage/blues with Jack O’Fire to post-rock with Rain Like The Sound Of Trains (Dischord), HiM, and Mice Parade.

Overview

LaRue appears across an unusually diverse cross-section of American independent music from the 1990s and 2000s, connecting the Dischord/DC orbit with Austin’s underground and New York-adjacent experimental rock scenes. His multi-instrumental range — trombone and electric piano alongside more standard rock instruments — made him a recurring utility player in ensembles that valued unusual timbres.

He was a founding/early member of Jack O’Fire, an Austin raw blues/garage group led by Tim Kerr; played drums in Rain Like The Sound Of Trains, a short-lived Dischord band; contributed to HiM, the jazz-inflected post-rock collective anchored by Doug Scharin; and was a long-running ensemble member for Mice Parade (Adam Pierce’s project), appearing across at least three releases spanning 2007–2012.
