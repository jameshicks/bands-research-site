Overview

Theo Van Eenbergen is an audio engineer and musician based in Eindhoven, Noord-Brabant, Netherlands, with credits stretching from 1980 through at least 2008. He was a member of both Nasmak — the Dutch experimental/new wave act — and the Rollins Band, where he worked alongside Henry Rollins, Andrew Weiss, Chris Haskett, and Sim Cain; his 33 shared credits with the full Rollins Band lineup indicate sustained involvement across their catalogue. His 175 total credits span production, engineering, mixing, and performance across punk, hardcore, new wave, and experimental music, with his heaviest label associations on Eksakt Records (1986–1989) and Plurex Records (1980–1983), and a later cluster on Southern Lord in 2008. Close recurring collaborators beyond his two bands include Joop Van Brakel and Ian MacKaye.
