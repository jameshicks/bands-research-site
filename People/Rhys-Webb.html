Overview

Rhys Webb is a UK musician, primarily known as the bassist and keyboardist of The Horrors, the Southend-on-Sea post-punk/psychedelic band formed in 2005. On the band’s early Loog-era singles (2006–2007) he is credited under the alias Spider Webb (3) and plays Vox Continental organ and keyboards — before the lineup settled and individual roles were formally credited. By Skying (XL Recordings, 2011) he is credited as Bass and Keyboards, a dual role he carried through the band’s peak XL period. On V (Wolf Tone, 2017) the credit expands to Bass Guitar and Synthesizer, reflecting the album’s synthesizer-heavy production (produced by Paul Epworth). On Night Life (Fiction Records, 2025) he takes the most expansive credit to date: Vocals, Bass, Synth, Guitar, and Programmed By.

Outside The Horrors, Webb co-founded Spider And The Flies with bandmate Tom Furse (credited on the project as “Rhys ‘Spider’ Webb” and “Tomethy Furse” respectively). The duo released two records on Mute Irregulars (2007, 2009) and remixed Radiohead’s “Reckoner” (2008). He also plays bass and backing vocals in The Shadracks (3), a London punk group on Damaged Goods, and contributed a guest keyboards session to The Hypnotic Eye’s “Marianne / Searching” (HYPEYE, 2012).

Early Alias: Spider Webb (3)

On early Horrors 7-inches (2006), Webb is separately credited under the stage name Spider Webb (3) (Discogs ID: 573885), playing Vox Continental organ, keyboards, and on one pressing, guitar. This reflects the band’s pre-contract informal period and the stage names used in their early goth-punk presentation. The alias appears only on 2006 Loog singles and the US Stolen Transmission EP; by Strange House (2007) he is folded into the group credit.
