Shayde Sartin

US musician (San Francisco Bay Area). Discogs artist ID: 599604. Bassist, guitarist, and multi-instrumentalist active from the late 1990s through at least the mid-2020s, associated with the Jewelled Antler / Soft Abuse psych-folk scene and the broader Bay Area indie underground.

Also credited as: S. Sartin, Sartin, Shayde.

Wikipedia: http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shayde_Sartin

Overview

Shayde Sartin is best known as one of the three core members of The Skygreen Leopards, the psych-folk duo / trio built around Donovan Quinn and Glenn Donaldson. His longest-running primary association is as bassist of The Skygreen Leopards (Jagjaguwar / Jewelled Antler era, 2001–2014), but his credits spread across a tightly connected cluster of Bay Area acts: Flying Canyon (with Donaldson and Cayce Lindner, on Soft Abuse), The Fresh & Onlys (post-2008, as multi-instrumentalist alongside Tim Cohen (2) and Wymond Miles), Sonny And The Sunsets (with Sonny Smith, multiple Polyvinyl-era albums), Peacers (Drag City, with Mike Donovan (2)), and Brigid Dawson And The Mothers Network.

He has also contributed as a session player to records by Kelley Stoltz (two Sub Pop albums), Wooden Wand (Kill Rock Stars), and others, and provided uncredited artwork for at least one release. His roles across releases include bass, guitar, baritone guitar, baritone saxophone, arrangement, and instruments broadly.

Key Projects

The Skygreen Leopards (2001–2014)

Discogs ID: 473739. Members: Donovan Quinn, Glenn Donaldson, Shayde Sartin (bass). Released on Jewelled Antler (2001), Soft Abuse (2004), Jagjaguwar (2005–2009), and Woodsist (2014). Sartin is credited on their major-label-adjacent run: Disciples Of California (Jagjaguwar, 2006) and Gorgeous Johnny (Jagjaguwar, 2009), and Family Crimes (Woodsist, 2014).

Flying Canyon (2006)

Discogs ID: 640019. Members: Cayce Lindner (vocals, guitar, songwriter), Glenn Donaldson (drums, vocals), Shayde Sartin (bass). One self-titled album on Soft Abuse (SAB017, 2006). Psychedelic / folk rock.

The Fresh & Onlys (2008–2017)

Discogs ID: 1383206. Members include Tim Cohen (2), Wymond Miles, Kyle Gibson (3), Shayde Sartin. Bay Area garage/psychedelic rock band with a long run on Castle Face, Woodsist, In The Red, Captured Tracks, Sacred Bones, Mexican Summer, and Sinderlyn. Sartin is credited across multiple albums including Play It Strange (In The Red, 2010) and Long Slow Dance (Mexican Summer, 2012).

Sonny And The Sunsets (2009–2023)

Discogs ID: 1426510. Fronted by Sonny Smith. Sartin contributed to Tomorrow Is Alright (Soft Abuse / Secret Seven, 2009), Talent Night At The Ashram (Polyvinyl, 2015), Moods Baby Moods (Polyvinyl, 2016), and Self Awareness Through Macrame (Rocks In Your Head Records, 2023). Active across bass and guitar roles.

Peacers (2015–2021)

Discogs ID: 4414315. Members: Mike Donovan (2), Mike Shoun, Bo Moore (2), Eric Park, Shayde Sartin, Ty Segall. Released on Drag City: Peacers (2015), Introducing The Crimsmen (2017), Blexxed Rec (2021). Sartin credited on bass and guitar.

Brigid Dawson And The Mothers Network

Discogs ID: 9213436. Members include Brigid Dawson, Mike Donovan (2), Mike Shoun, Jeff Tobias, Jason Robira, Jim McHugh, Mikey Young, Peter Kerlin, Shayde Sartin. Released on Rocks In Your Head Records.

Giant Skyflower Band

Discogs ID: 1021162. Members: Glenn Donaldson and Shayde Sartin only. No Discogs releases listed under this name.
