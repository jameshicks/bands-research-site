Gary Thom

Scottish musician (drums, songwriter). Discogs artist ID: 494181. Name variation: “Thom.” Years active (Discogs): 1999–2018.

Overview

Gary Thom is a Glasgow/Scottish indie-pop drummer and songwriter. He is documented as a member of Astrid (3) and California Snow Story, and as the third member of The Company Presents Roy Moller alongside Roy Moller and Stevie Jackson.
