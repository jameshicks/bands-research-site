The Extra Lens

Indie folk/rock duo of John Darnielle (The Mountain Goats) and Franklin Bruno (Nothing Painted Blue). Formerly known as The Extra Glenns. Discogs artist IDs: The Extra Lens = 2000777; The Extra Glenns = 263259.

Overview

The Extra Lens is the current name for a long-running side project between Darnielle and Bruno, two of the more prominent figures in 1990s US indie songwriting. Their collaboration predates the widespread recognition either would gain through their main projects. On Discogs, the duo appear under two distinct artist entries representing the two name periods:

  • The Extra Glenns (1993–c.2002) — 7” era and first album
  • The Extra Lens (2010–present) — renamed for the second album onward

The “Glenns” name was a private joke: on Discogs credits from the 1990s, Darnielle and Bruno appear as Glenn D and Glenn B respectively (aliases both maintain on their own artist pages). The Discogs profile text on the Extra Glenns entry describes them as “something of an underground legend in the ’90s, recording tracks for singles and some compilations.” A show at the 2000 San Francisco Noise Pop Festival drew an unexpectedly large turnout — attendees reportedly traveled from New York specifically for the set — which convinced the duo to continue more actively.

In 2002 Absolutely Kosher Records released Martial Arts Weekend, their first full-length. The project went quiet again before returning in 2010 under the renamed Extra Lens moniker for Undercard on Merge Records.

As The Extra Glenns

Infidelity (1993)

Label: Harriet Records (cat. Harriet 022) · Format: 7” 45 RPM · Discogs ID: 1539531

Three-track single; the earliest documented release by the duo. Darnielle (Glenn D) and Bruno (Glenn B) are both credited for Guitar, Vocals, and Arranged By / Written-By respectively. Mixed by Bob Durkee. The runout engravings read “DAMMIT RICKY” (Side A) and “TAKE YOUR VITAMINS!!!” (Side B).

Martial Arts Weekend (2001/2002)

Label: Absolutely Kosher Records (cat. AK017) · Format: CD · Discogs ID: 1810474

Note: Discogs indexes this at 2001 but the Absolutely Kosher entry and the Extra Glenns profile both place the release in 2002. The CD matrix indicates Rainbo pressing.

The group’s first full-length, recorded after the Noise Pop Festival resurgence. Twelve tracks, all co-written by Darnielle and Bruno (except the cover, see below). “Going To Morocco,” “Going To Michigan,” and “Going To Marrakesh” continue the Mountain Goats “Going To…” song-title convention. “Malevolent Seascape Y” echoes the Infidelity 7“‘s “Malevolent Cityscape X,” suggesting a planned continuity across the catalog.

Track 7, “Memories,” is a cover, credited to L. Cohen (Leonard Cohen) and P. Spector (Phil Spector) — the song originally recorded by The Crystals and famously by Cohen himself.

Peter Hughes — the Mountain Goats bassist, who also appears in Nothing Painted Blue — did the sleeve artwork (credited as “P. Hughes”).

As The Extra Lens

Undercard (2010)

Label: Merge Records (cat. MRG406) · Formats: LP + CD · Discogs IDs: 2526999 (LP), 4324505 (CD) · Master ID: 554339

The duo’s second full-length and first release under the Extra Lens name. Songwriting is split between Darnielle and Bruno: the CD credits list Darnielle as Written-By on tracks A1–A3, A5, B8, B10–B12, and Bruno on A3, A4, A6, A7 (with A3 shared). Track B9 (“In Germany Before The War”) is a cover of the Randy Newman song.

The LP pressing was mastered at Masterdisk. Jon Wurster (Superchunk / The Mountain Goats drummer) plays percussion on “Cruiserweights” (track A2). Matt Houser plays percussion on “Communicating Doors” (A4) and “Ambivalent Landscape Z” (B8). Jean Cook contributes strings to “Tug On The Line” (B10). Mixing was shared between Franklin Bruno and Mitch Rackin; recording by Brian Paulson and Mitch Rackin.

The title “Ambivalent Landscape Z” continues the Malevolent Cityscape / Seascape sequence from the Extra Glenns catalog (X → Y → Z), completing a trilogy across all three releases.

Internal Song-Title Continuity

The duo ran a deliberate series of “Malevolent Landscape” titles across all three releases:

  • “Malevolent Cityscape X” — Infidelity (1993)
  • “Malevolent Seascape Y” — Martial Arts Weekend (2001/2002)
  • “Ambivalent Landscape Z” — Undercard (2010)

(The shift from “Malevolent” to “Ambivalent” on the third entry may be intentional, completing the trilogy with a tonal modulation.)

Martial Arts Weekend also includes three tracks in Darnielle’s “Going To…” convention (“Going To Morocco,” “Going To Michigan,” “Going To Marrakesh”), crosslinking it to the Mountain Goats songwriting tradition.
