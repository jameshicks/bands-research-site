Overview

Liquor Giants are a Los Angeles power pop and indie rock band formed in 1989. Their core catalogue concentrates in the 1990s, with the bulk of their output on Rubber Records (their most durable home, spanning 1991–2005) and a mid-period stint on Matador (1996–1998). The five-piece lineup of Byron Reynolds, D.A. Valdez, Dan Earhart, Joe Katz, and Mark McGroarty draws on power pop, garage rock, and alternative rock. Moses Lawn is their most frequent collaborator, appearing across four releases.
