Apartment (2)

Bristol, UK post-punk band. Discogs artist ID: 946080. Active September 1978 – summer 1980.

Overview

Apartment formed in Bristol in September 1978 and broke up in the summer of 1980. In that roughly two-year window they recorded a self-produced 7-inch single and a body of additional material that went unreleased at the time. Their sole original release was the double A-side “The Car / Winter” (1980) on Bristol’s Heartbeat Records, catalog number PULSE 7. The band self-produced the record alongside co-producer Simon Edwards (4); engineering was handled by David Lord and Glenn Tommey.

The three core members — Alan Griffiths (vocals, guitar), Emil Joachim (drums, also credited as Emil (11)), and Richard White (4) (bass) — are credited across both the 1980 single and the 2009 archival release. Songwriting on the single was shared: “The Car” is credited to all three members (Griffiths/Joachim/White), while “Winter” is credited solely to Griffiths.

A test pressing of “The Car / Winter” on Not On Label also exists in the Discogs database, indicating pre-release or promo copies circulated.

In 2009, Bristol Archive Records compiled the band’s recordings as House Of Secrets (ARC104), a digital album of 15 tracks. A physical CD edition followed in 2016 (ARC261CD, limited edition), which also credits David Lord and Glenn Tommey as engineers with the band as producer. House Of Secrets surfaces tracks including “The Alternative,” “Distractions,” “Shot Down,” “Broken Glass,” “Third Floor,” “I’m X,” “Poison,” “Fact Or Fiction,” “Blank Windscreen,” “Time,” “Stress Factor,” and “Cruel System” — material apparently recorded but not released during the band’s active period.

“The Alternative” was licensed to Cherry Red for inclusion on the 2024 box set Moving Away From The Pulsebeat (Post-Punk Britain 1977-1981) (CRCD5BOX169), a 5-CD, 105-track survey of British post-punk 1977–1981. Apartment appear on disc 2, alongside acts including Joy Division, The Slits, The Raincoats, Au Pairs, and The Pop Group — placing them squarely in the documented Bristol/UK post-punk scene of the period, even if they remained obscure at the time.

Scene Connections

Apartment operated out of Bristol during the same period as The Pop Group, Glaxo Babies, and other acts associated with the city’s post-punk scene. Their label Heartbeat Records was a Bristol-based independent. Engineer David Lord was a significant figure in the Bristol recording community (also a member of Bearz and later a notable producer/engineer).

The 2024 Cherry Red compilation situates Apartment alongside canonical post-punk acts — Joy Division, The Slits, The Raincoats, Gang Of Four, Magazine, Public Image Limited, The Fall, The Cure — confirming retrospective recognition as a legitimate if minor part of that scene.
