Queens Of The Stone Age

Discogs ID: 56168 Formed: 1996/1997, Palm Desert, California, USA Genre: Alternative Rock, Hard Rock, Stoner Rock Labels: Man’s Ruin Records (1997–1998), Loosegroove Records, Interscope Records (2000–2007), Rekords Rekords (2005–2011), Matador Records (2013–present)

Queens Of The Stone Age is an American rock band founded by guitarist and vocalist Josh Homme in Palm Desert, California, following the dissolution of Kyuss in 1995. The band emerged in 1997 with a debut 10” on Man’s Ruin Records and quickly established a signature sound — heavy, groove-driven riffs, psychedelic undertones, and a sardonic wit — that set them apart from the grunge and post-grunge scene of the era. Though Homme has been the sole constant member, QOTSA has always operated through a rotating cast of musicians and collaborators, treating the band more as a vehicle for Homme’s songwriting than as a fixed lineup. Over seven studio albums and nearly three decades, the band has moved through Interscope to Matador, consistently maintained critical acclaim, and connected the California desert-rock underground to mainstream hard rock.
