Headless Heroes

Discogs artist ID: 1298043. Orchestral art-pop collective, Los Angeles / London. Active c. 2008–2009. Website: denofheroes.com (defunct); also at fargorecords.com/headlessheroes (defunct).

Overview

Headless Heroes was a recording project conceived and built by London-born producer and engineer Hugo Nicolson around an ensemble of guest vocalists he called “The Cast.” The concept was cover versions — each track reinterpreting a song from the folk-pop underground (Jackson C. Frank, Vashti Bunyan, Daniel Johnston, Nick Cave, Linda Perhacs, The Jesus and Mary Chain, and others) — arranged in a lush, orchestral style with strings, brass, and layered instrumentation drawing on LA’s session community.

The core instrumentalist roster — Leo Abrahams, Gus Seyffert, Joey Waronker, Josh Klinghoffer, and Woody Jackson — was the same across both full releases. The string section was provided by Dirty Pretty Strings, a London string quartet/ensemble. Trumpet was played by Mike Boldger. The drum chair was shared between Joey Waronker and Mike Green (9).

The executive producer across all three releases was Eddie Bezalel.

Releases appeared on two labels: Names (a sublabel of 679) in the UK, and Fargo (5) (French, a sublabel of Fargo Records) in Europe. The 2009 remix EP carried a dual imprint: Names and Headless Heroes (imprint).

No compilation appearances are documented in Discogs.

The Cast — Vocalists

Discogs credits “Alela Diane” as the sole vocalist on The Silence Of Love (the album-level credit). The tracklist for the album does not carry per-track vocalist credits in Discogs, so the full distribution of vocal performances across tracks cannot be confirmed from the database alone. She is the only vocalist credited at the release level on either album. The North Wind Blew South single and the EP carry no vocalist credits.
