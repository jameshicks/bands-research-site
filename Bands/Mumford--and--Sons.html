Overview

Mumford & Sons are a four-piece folk-rock group from West London, formed in December 2007. Marcus Mumford, Winston Marshall, Ben Lovett, and Ted Dwane each play several instruments — between them covering vocals, guitar, banjo, mandolin, accordion, string bass, and drums — producing a rootsy, old-time sound shaped by influences ranging from country and bluegrass to Crosby, Stills, Nash & Young and Kings of Leon. The catalogue is weighted heavily toward the 2010s, with 28 releases in that decade against 9 in the 2000s and 3 in the 2020s, spanning folk, bluegrass, folk rock, indie rock, and alternative rock. Island Records and the band’s own Gentlemen Of The Road imprint have been their primary release vehicles since 2009, with producer Markus Dravs and engineer Ruadhri Cushnan as the most consistent studio collaborators.
