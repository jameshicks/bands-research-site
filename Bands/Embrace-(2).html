Embrace (2)

Discogs ID: 265295 Active: 1985–1986 Origin: Washington, DC, USA Label: Dischord Records

Short-lived DC post-hardcore band. Ian MacKaye’s first band after Minor Threat dissolved. Played 11 shows total, split before a planned Boston gig while MacKaye was in England. Their one LP was released posthumously in 1987. A key band of the Revolution Summer moment.
