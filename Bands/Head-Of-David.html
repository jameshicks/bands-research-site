Head Of David

Discogs ID: 55960 Origin: Birmingham (Black Country), West Midlands, UK Active: 1986–1991 (disbanded); reunion activity thereafter Genres: Industrial, Noise, Punk, Alternative Rock, Extreme Metal Name variations: H.O.D., H.O.D., H.O.D.I.C.A., H.OD

Overview

Head Of David formed in the early 1980s in the Black Country area of the West Midlands. They released records through Blast First — the noise/experimental imprint run by Paul Smith (52), a Mute subsidiary — with Dutch distribution through Torso and limited US exposure on Mute. Their catalog occupies a position between post-punk industrial noise and extreme metal that made them a formative presence in the British underground of the late 1980s.

The band’s most historically significant association is with Justin K Broadrick, who played drums from 1987 to 1989 — the period spanning the LP, White Elephant, and Dustbowl recordings. Broadrick joined Head Of David after his stint playing guitar on Side A of Napalm Death’s Scum (Earache, 1987), which Head Of David produced. He left Head Of David in 1989 to form Godflesh, turning the drum machine-driven industrial metal he had developed in this context into his primary vehicle. The Dustbowl album (1988), recorded with Steve Albini at the controls, is the clearest document of the Broadrick-era band and has been cited as a proto-industrial metal record predating Godflesh’s debut.

Head Of David split in 1991 following Seed State, the band’s final studio album. Vocalist Stephen R. Burroughs continued as Tunnels Of Āh (Discogs ID: 3547978), while the band has regrouped in various partial configurations. A 1998 Blast First 7” (Bugged / Snake Domain) documents a late reunion single. Vocalist Stephen Burroughs, guitarist Eric Jurenovskis, bassist Dave Cochrane, and drummer Paul Sharp continued playing together after the formal split.

Production Credit: Napalm Death’s Scum

Head Of David are credited as producers on Side A of Napalm Death’s Scum (Earache, MOSH 3, 1987) — one of the defining documents of grindcore. Side A (tracks A1–A12) featured Justin Broadrick on guitar, Nicholas Bullen on bass/vocals, and Mick Harris on drums. Side B was recorded by a completely different lineup and produced by Digby Pearson. Head Of David’s production credit applies only to Side A.
