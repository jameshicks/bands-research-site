The Elusive

Short-lived Washington D.C. post-hardcore / emo band active 1997–1998. Discogs artist ID: 2051404

Overview

The Elusive formed in the summer of 1997 following the breakup of Corm, the previous band of John Davis (11) and several other members. Davis, along with Alex Ficker, Dan Chu, and his brother Ian Chu, launched the project as an explicitly temporary vehicle — their plan from the outset was to tour that summer with only a demo tape to sell. Chris Richards (4) rounded out the lineup, and the band wrote six songs before hitting the road for a run of Midwest dates.

The band split in April 1998, shortly after releasing their sole record. Davis and Richards went on to play together in Q And Not U and later Paint Branch, cementing The Elusive’s status as an early chapter in the DC post-hardcore lineage that those bands would continue. Chris Richards also subsequently performed as a solo artist under the alias Ris Paul Ric.
