The Spacebomb Strings

House string ensemble at Spacebomb Studios, Richmond, VA. Discogs artist ID: 5812131.

The Spacebomb Strings are the recurring string section on Spacebomb Records productions, with arrangements and conducting typically handled by Trey Pollard. Sessions are recorded by Adrian Olsen and produced primarily by Matthew E. White.
