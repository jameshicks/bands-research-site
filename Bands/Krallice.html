Krallice

Discogs ID: 1182892 Origin: New York City, USA Active: c. 2007–present Genres: Black Metal, Avant-Garde, Experimental

Avant-garde black metal quartet formed c. 2007 in New York City. Core lineup: Colin Marston (guitar, bass, Warr guitar), Mick Barr (guitar, bass, vocals), Lev Weinstein (drums), and Nick McMaster (bass, vocals). All studio recordings produced, recorded, mixed, and mastered by Marston at his studio Menegroth, The Thousand Caves in Queens.

Krallice are notable for applying a compositionally rigorous, harmonically dense approach to extreme metal — long interlocking guitar counterpoint structures, jazz-inflected drumming, and a general indifference to genre convention. All four members have substantial parallel projects spanning death metal, noise, experimental composition, and free jazz.

Key Personnel Notes

Colin Marston handled all production across the entire discography — recorded, mixed, and mastered at Menegroth, The Thousand Caves. On Psychagogue (2022) he is credited with keyboards, drums, and vocals in addition to guitar, suggesting an unusually fluid instrument assignment for that session.

Nick McMaster contributed cover artwork to Dimensional Bleedthrough (collage) and co-designed Diotima with Max Hooper Schneider (Discogs ID: 2252167). McMaster also performs in Geryon (3) with Lev Weinstein.

Dave Edwardson (Neurosis, Tribes of Neurot; Discogs ID: 377918) appears on Loüm (2017) as primary co-artist — vocals and synths. Lance Hill (5) recorded synth tracks.

Geryon (3) (Discogs ID: 2896788) — the McMaster/Weinstein duo — split the Wolf/Astomatous LP with Krallice on Gilead Media in 2020, making it a record where half the band essentially appeared twice.

Orphan (10) is credited as sole songwriter on Orphan Of Sickness (2011) — the EP is a tribute/covers release. The Rorschach (2) cover “Traditional” came out as a Decibel Flexi Series single the same year (DB009).
