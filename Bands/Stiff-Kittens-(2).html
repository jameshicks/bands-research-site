Stiff Kittens (2)

Discogs artist ID: 695777

Earliest name associated with the group that became Warsaw (3) and then Joy Division. The name appeared on the flyer for the band’s first gig — a bill with Buzzcocks and Penetration (2) — but the band took the stage as Warsaw. “Stiff Kittens” was not a performing identity so much as a printed label for an unformed outfit; it subsequently attached to a run of unofficial bootleg LPs drawn from Warsaw-era recordings.

Overview

The band that would become Joy Division played their first confirmed live date in 1976 under the name Stiff Kittens, printed on the gig bill. Onstage they introduced themselves as Warsaw — the name under which they performed throughout 1977. According to Discogs, “Stiff Kittens” has been used for some Joy Division bootlegs, which accounts for the two unofficial LP credits under this entity.

Core members at this stage were Ian Curtis (vocals), Bernard Sumner (guitar, keyboards; credited on bootlegs as Bernard Albrecht or B.A. Digham), and Peter Hook (bass). Steve Brotherdale was an early drummer before Stephen Morris joined. An additional early peripheral member, Terry Mason (6) (Discogs ID: 13043235), appears in the Warsaw (3) member list in a management/roadie capacity.
