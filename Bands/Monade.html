Monade

French-language pop/experimental group led by Lætitia Sadier. Released on Drag City in the 2000s. Discogs artist ID: 68350

Overview

Monade was Lætitia Sadier’s side project during and after the later Stereolab years, recording French-language material in a more intimate register than Stereolab. Released albums on Drag City. Considered a complement to Sadier’s solo recordings.
