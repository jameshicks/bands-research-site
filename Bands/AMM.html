AMM

Discogs ID: 12550 Active: 1965–present (various lineups) Origin: London, UK

British free improvisation / noise ensemble. Originated in London in 1965–66; closely associated with the Scratch Orchestra and Cornelius Cardew. Core members across the 1966 recordings: Cardew (piano, cello, electronics), Eddie Prévost (percussion), Keith Rowe (electric guitar, electronics), Lou Gare (tenor sax, violin), Lawrence Sheaff (cello, accordion, clarinet, electronics). AMMMusic 1966 released by ReR Megacorp in 1989 (RēR AMMCD), produced by Peter Jenner et al.
