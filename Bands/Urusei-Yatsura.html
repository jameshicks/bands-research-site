Urusei Yatsura

Discogs ID: 340134
Origin: Glasgow, Scotland
Labels: ché Records, Sire Records

Scottish noise-pop / indie rock band who were part of the ché stable before signing to Sire (US major). The Kewpies Like Watermelon CD EP (Sire / ché, 1996) splits recording duties: tracks 1, 2, 4 recorded by John A. Rivers; track 3 (“Sucker”) recorded by Charlie McIntosh. Writers: Fergus Lawrie (tracks 1, 2, 4) and Graham Kemp (track 3).
