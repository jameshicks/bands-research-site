The Orielles

UK indie/experimental pop band. Discogs ID: 4562442. Based in Halifax, West Yorkshire.

Signed to Heavenly Recordings. Albums include Silver Dollar Moment (2018) and Disco Volador (2020). Disco Volador was produced and mixed by Marta Salogni, mastered by Heba Kadry.
