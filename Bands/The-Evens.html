The Evens

Discogs ID: 292572 Active: 2001–present (intermittent) Origin: Washington, DC, USA Label: Dischord Records Members: Ian MacKaye (guitar/vocals), Amy Farina (drums/vocals)

Stripped-down DC duo formed by Ian MacKaye and Amy Farina (who married). MacKaye plays a baritone guitar; Farina drums and sings. No bass. The sound is spacious and melodic — a deliberate departure from the intensity of MacKaye’s earlier bands (Minor Threat, Fugazi) but still built on direct, political songwriting. All three studio albums recorded and mixed by Don Zientara at Inner Ear Studios and released on Dischord Records.

Amy Farina — Background

Before The Evens, Farina was a member of The Warmers, a DC trio also including Alec MacKaye (vocals) and Juan Carrera (guitar). The Warmers released a 7” (Thin Air / “Occupation: Fish”, Dischord 100.5, 1995) and a self-titled LP (Dischord DIS102CD, 1996), both recorded by Don Zientara; Ian MacKaye produced the LP. Farina also appeared on a Ted Leo / Pharmacists release (credited as drummer, 2001) and contributed artwork silhouettes to Two Headed Coin (1993, Art Monk Construction). After The Evens’ extended hiatus, she joined Ian MacKaye and Joe Lally in Coriky, who released a self-titled LP on Dischord in 2020.
