Fog Lake

Discogs ID: 2764941 Real name: Aaron Powell Active: 2012–present Origin: Newfoundland, Canada Genres: Lo-Fi, Indie Pop, Bedroom Pop, Indie Rock Labels: Orchid Tapes, Birdtapes, Dog Knights Productions, Out Of Breath Records, Z Tapes, Illuminated Paths Aliases: Aaron Powell (2) (Discogs ID: 4608249), Farewell Alaska

Fog Lake is the musical alias of Aaron Powell, a Newfoundland-based Canadian musician. Originally conceived as an ambient project, it evolved into a signature blend of lo-fi bedroom pop and dark, nostalgic indie rock that Powell has called “downer pop.” He is one of the few artists with releases across the full decade-plus run of Orchid Tapes, contributing four albums to the label between 2014 and 2021.

Biography

Origins (2012–2013)

Aaron Powell began recording under the Fog Lake name around 2012, initially as an outlet for ambient and drone recordings made during high school in Newfoundland. The earliest releases — Stay Lost (2012) and Inference (iii) (2012) — were self-released. The project was briefly collected on the There’s A Spirit, There’s A Soul // Stay Lost compilation (2013, Illuminated Paths), an early-career document of the ambient/drone phase.

The first full-length proper, Farther Reaches (January 2013), established a more song-based direction. The Holy Cross EP followed later that year — also self-released, though it received a cassette pressing through Birdtapes — and marked a turn toward acoustic indie pop and lo-fi rock. Notably, Holy Cross included a cover of “Best Love,” credited as written by Kurt Vile.

Orchid Tapes era — first wave (2014–2017)

In February 2014 Powell released Virgo Indigo through Orchid Tapes (OCT034), his first label-backed full-length. Warren Hildebrand (also known as Foxes In Fiction) — the Orchid Tapes founder and house producer — mastered the record. Photography was provided by Daniel Diasgranados (cover) and Heather Nolan (promo). The album’s twelve tracks of hazy, tape-saturated pop placed Powell firmly within the Orchid Tapes aesthetic and became one of the label’s defining early releases.

Victoria Park followed in 2015 on Orchid Tapes (OCT053), issued as a limited-edition numbered cassette. The record moved toward indie rock while retaining the bedroom-recording intimacy. No additional production credits appear in the Discogs entry — Powell handled the full recording himself.

Dragonchaser (2017, OCT064) continued the trajectory, adding folk rock inflections to the lo-fi rock template. Again self-produced with no listed co-credits on the Discogs entry.

Both Virgo Indigo and the Farther Reaches album were licensed to Z Tapes in Slovakia, which issued a split compilation pressing (Virgo Indigo / Farther Reaches, 2015 and 2019 repressings) for the European market.

Expanding roster (2018)

2018 was a prolific year. Captain was released via Dog Knights Productions on vinyl (DK122) for Europe and Stack Your Roster on cassette — marking Powell’s first vinyl release and a stepping-outside of the Orchid Tapes home base. The album retained the lo-fi indie rock sound across ten tracks.

Carousel (2018) was a self-released six-track EP, available digitally. Powell handled all writing and recording himself.

Also in 2018, Powell contributed four tracks to a split cassette EP with Euphoria Again (project of Johnny Klein (2), a.k.a. Johnny Klein (2)), released through Out Of Breath Records (OOB020). Powell produced and mastered his own side; Christian Novelli provided cover photography.

Z Tapes issued a further European compilation pairing, Dragonchaser / Victoria Park, in 2018.

Return to Orchid Tapes — Tragedy Reel (2021)

Tragedy Reel (April 2021, OCT072) represented the most fully-produced Fog Lake record to date. Warren Hildebrand returned as co-producer and mixer. Paul Vroom mastered the album. The record featured a roster of guest contributors across its nine tracks:

  • “Dakota” — Clarke Sondermann (backing vocals), Molly Germer (strings)
  • “Jitterbug” — Warren Hildebrand (backing vocals), Julian Fader (drums)
  • “Latter Day Saint” — Kenny Boothby (backing vocals)
  • “Crocodile” — Emma Pope (2) (backing vocals)
  • “Pity Party” — Molly Germer (strings)
  • “June” — Warren Hildebrand (backing vocals)

Cover photography credited to Jen King (2) (as J. King).

Recent work (2023–present)

Midnight Society (2023) was self-released digitally. Powell again handled writing and recording. Julian Fader contributed drums on two tracks (“Motorcade” and “Getaway”); Jen King (2) contributed bass, backing vocals, and co-writing on “Motorcade” (credited as Jae King).
