Ateleia

American experimental / ambient project. Discogs artist ID: 130727. United States.

Ateleia is the primary solo recording alias of James Elliott (real name James Lanier Elliott Jr.; Discogs ID: 690228), a New York City–based (later Chicago-based) experimental electronic musician. Elliott co-founded the label Antiopic with guitarist David Daniell, and is best known externally as a former member of School Of Seven Bells (bass and electronics) and Bear In Heaven. As Ateleia, Elliott operates in abstract electronic, ambient, and experimental territory; the project’s Discogs-registered releases span 2004–2007.

Connections

  • James Elliott — sole member / composer; Ateleia is his primary experimental alias
  • David Daniell — co-founder of Antiopic; performed guitar/synthesizer on Swimming Against The Moments; collaborated in Project Qua Project
  • School Of Seven Bells — Elliott was a founding member (bass, electronics); Nightly shares the Radium (3) catalog with SVIIB’s debut EP
  • Bear In Heaven — Elliott was a member; Jon Philpot and Sadek Bazaraa (both Bear In Heaven members) appeared on Swimming Against The Moments
  • Benjamin Curtis — School of Seven Bells co-founder; played Guitar and Electronics on Nightly track 3
  • Radium (3) — sublabel of Table Of The Elements; released both Nightly (2007) and the Impala Eardrums sampler (2008)
  • Antiopic — Elliott’s own label; also home to David Daniell, Need Thomas Windham, Dion Workman, Carlos Giffoni, Jim O’Rourke, Lee Ranaldo, Alvin Lucier, Alan Licht, Oren Ambarchi
  • Xeric — sister label to Radium (3) under Table Of The Elements; released Formal Sleep (2007)
  • Push For Night — Elliott’s duo with Oliver Chapoy; 2021 LP on The Tapeworm (UK)
  • David Grubbs (Discogs ID: 56194) — harmonium on Formal Sleep track 3
  • Greg Davis (7) — mastered Nightly track 2; single Discogs credit, identity unknown
