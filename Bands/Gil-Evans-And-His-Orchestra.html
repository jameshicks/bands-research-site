Gil Evans And His Orchestra

Large jazz ensemble led by arranger and composer Gil Evans (born Ian Ernest Gilmore Green, 1912–1988). Discogs artist ID: 59659. One of the most celebrated jazz orchestras of the post-bop era, known for dense, coloristic writing and adventurous programming.

Overview

Gil Evans built an ensemble that served as a recurring laboratory for improvisers from multiple generations. The orchestra’s membership over its decades is enormous — Discogs lists over 150 credited members — spanning jazz mainstream, avant-garde, and rock crossover. Evans is best known for his collaborations with Miles Davis (Miles Ahead, Porgy and Bess, Sketches of Spain), but his own orchestra recordings are equally significant.

David Sanborn Connection

David Sanborn is listed as a member of the Gil Evans Orchestra in Discogs. He appears on several Evans releases:

  • Svengali (Atlantic, 1973)
  • Live At The Royal Festival Hall (RCA Victor, 1979) — alto/soprano/sopranino sax, flute
  • Priestess (Antilles/Polystar, 1983)
  • There Comes A Time (RCA, 1976/reissue)
