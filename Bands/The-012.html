The 012

UK post-punk / experimental band, active 1979–1984 and intermittently thereafter. Discogs artist ID: 575877

Overview

The 012 were a UK experimental post-punk group who released two cassette albums in the early 1980s and one vinyl LP on Flicknife Records before falling into obscurity. The band’s core was guitarist/vocalist Keith Dobson (performing under the alias Kifkif Le Batteur) and drummer José Gross, with bassist Grant Showbiz (Grant Cunliffe) appearing on their key releases. Their 1984 LP Lets Get Proffesional contains “Asbestos Lead Asbestos” (5:29), the track that brought the band renewed attention when it was selected for the 2021 Cherry Red box set Shake The Foundations (Militant Funk & The Post-Punk Dancefloor 1978-1984).

The band also name-varied as 012 on Discogs. Their first release, the 1979 cassette Heart Noise Heart, was shared with The Sell-Outs — another group featuring Keith Dobson — on the Organised Chaos label (a sublabel of Fuck Off Records (2), cat# OUCH 005). The 1981 cassette The White Patterns On Her Dress was issued on Funky Weird Tapes (FW. 001) and featured Grant Showbiz on bass.

Keith Dobson’s later career is notable: after The 012, he was a founding member of World Domination Enterprises, the post-punk / noise-funk band active from the mid-1980s, and also played in Here & Now (3), Planet Gong (2), and The Love Bringers.

A 2021 digital EP Age Of Aquarius (WDEcorp Records) credits The 012 and features the core trio of Kifkif Le Batteur / Keith Dobson (vocals, guitar), José Gross (drums), and Grant Showbiz (bass), suggesting a partial reformation under the WDE umbrella.
