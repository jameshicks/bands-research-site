The Skunks

Discogs artist ID: 321451 (listed as “Skunks (2)” on Discogs; realname: The Skunks)
Active: 1977–1982 (original run); reunion/archival activity 2000–2001
Origin: Austin, Texas, USA
Genres: Punk, Power Pop, New Wave

Overview

The Skunks were one of the earliest and most significant punk bands to emerge from Austin, Texas. Formed in mid-1977, they made their Austin debut at Raul’s club in February 1978 — having already played a series of dates in Dallas and Fort Worth in January that year alongside The Violators (2) (which featured Kathy Valentine prior to her joining The Go-Go’s). In a city not yet known for punk, The Skunks were pioneers.

The original trio was Jesse Sublett (bass, vocals), “Fazz” Eddie Muñoz (guitar), and Billy Blackmon (drums). After recording their debut LP — the self-titled “Black LP” — Muñoz departed for Los Angeles at the end of 1978 and joined The Plimsouls. Jon Dee Graham replaced him on guitar in January 1979, forming what is considered the definitive lineup. In their later phase, twin brothers Doug Murray (guitar) and Greg Murray (drums) also joined the band alongside pianist/guitarist Doug Murray and eventually drummer Bill Mansell.

The band self-released most of their early material on their own Skunks Records imprint. Their 1980 album was released on Rude Records (10) (an Austin-based label associated with Lone Star Studios), and their 1982 LP came out on Republic Recording, a Texas label with offices in Austin and Houston.

The Skunks were inducted into the Austin Music Hall of Fame in 2008. “Earthquake Shake” — the B-side of their first single — later appeared on Punk 45 Vol. 1 - Kill The Hippies! Kill Yourself! (Soul Jazz Records, 2013), cementing their place in the documented history of American punk.

Note on lineup sequence: Blackmon was the drummer throughout the original run (1977–1982). Greg Murray replaced him (or they overlapped) in the final lineup that recorded the 1982 LP, where Greg Murray is credited as the sole drummer. The Discogs “Earthquake Shake” 2000 archival CD lists the core Sublett / Graham / Blackmon trio, suggesting the reunion drew on the 1979–1980 lineup.
