Some Velvet Sidewalk

Lo-fi indie rock / punk band from Olympia, Washington. Discogs artist ID: 267490. Active c. 1988–1997.

Overview

Some Velvet Sidewalk was led by Al Larsen and released primarily through K Records. Members listed on Discogs include Al Larsen, Don Blair, Jennifer Olay, Louise Olsen, Martin Bernier, Paul Schuster, Robert Christie (2), Ryan Baldoz, and Tobi Vail (drums, early period). Vail toured with the band in early 1990.
