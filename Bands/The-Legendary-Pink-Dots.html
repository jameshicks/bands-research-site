The Legendary Pink Dots

Discogs ID: 26205

UK/Netherlands experimental/psychedelic group led by Edward Ka-Spel. Long-running; 54 releases on Soleilmoon Recordings between 1995 and 2025, making them the label’s second-most-prolific act after Muslimgauze. Active since the early 1980s; prolific and consistent across decades.

Large rotating membership including Martijn De Kleer, Niels van Hoorn, Raymond Steeg, Ryan Moore, Patrick Q. Paganini, Phil Knight (2), and many others. Ka-Spel also releases solo material on Soleilmoon (9 entries).

Key Soleilmoon titles: From Here You’ll Watch The World Go By (SOL 28 CD, 1995 — first Soleilmoon album), 9 Lives To Wonder (SOL 30 CD, 1995), Brighter Now (SOL 39 CD, 1996), Nemesis Online (SOL 75 CD, 1998), Crushed Velvet Apocalypse (2001).
