Begnagrad

Discogs ID: 1088500
Legendary Slovenian avant-garde / chamber-folk group from Ljubljana, active in two distinct periods in former Yugoslavia: 1975–1978 and 1981–1983. Their music fuses Central European folk idioms (Slovenian folk songs, polka, waltz, yodelling), jazz-inflected harmony, and an avant-garde compositional sensibility rooted in the Rock In Opposition orbit. Core figure Bratko Bibič (accordion) connects them to the wider international experimental scene via Nimal and later Accordion Tribe.

Jodlovska Urška (1990, Nikad Robom 010)

Cassette album. Genres: Folk, Jazz, Rock / Styles: Avant-garde Jazz, Avantgarde, Folk Rock, Jazz-Rock, Polka, Prog Rock, Swing. Recorded by Boris Romih. Note that despite the 1990 date, this features the original-era rhythm section (Muševič / Špindler) rather than the 1981-era one, and many tracks (“Jara Kaća”, “Kranjska Gora Blues”, “Uspavanka Za Juvana”, “Tolmun”) reappear on the 1993 Tastare CD — suggesting this cassette may capture an earlier live or rehearsal session.

Tracklist: A1 Jara Kaća; A2 Beg Na Grad; A3 Boris Ris Romih Mih; A4 Ta Nova; A5 Uspavanka Za Juvana; A6 Beg Na Grad; B1 Kranjska Gora Blues; B2 Taleva-Panzer Valtz; B3 Zimska; B4 Tolmun; B5 Ta Dolga.

Konzert For A Broken Dance (1990, Ayaa cdt 1190)

CD reissue/remaster released by the French label Ayaa (Reims). This is the release that connected Begnagrad to the international avant-garde circuit: Ayaa also released Nimal’s Dis Tanz (1992) and distributed work by The Work, Etron Fou Leloublan, Fred Frith, Ghédalia Tazartès, and others. Genres: Jazz, Rock / Styles: Avantgarde, Folk Rock, Prog Rock. Cover art by Bogo Pečnikar. Co-produced by Begnagrad and Neven Smolčič. The track titles appear in Slovenian with English or German translations on the CD.

Tastare (Theoldwones) (1993, Bess – PRO MARKET BPM 0001)

CD on the Slovenian label Bess – PRO MARKET. The subtitle “Theoldwones” (= “The Old Ones”) signals retrospective intent; tracks 1–11 were produced by Anđelko Maletić and tracks 12–13 by the band itself. Engineered by Katalin Farkaš (tracks 1–11), Boris Romih (tracks 12–13), and Tine Žitko (tracks 12–13). Mastered by Peter Gruden (tracks 1–11) and Janez Križaj (tracks 12–13). Liner notes by Bratko Bibič. Rights society: DSS (Slovenian). Again features the original-era members (Muševič, Špindler) alongside the 1981-era core, with Romih engineering.

Connections

  • Bratko Bibič — core accordionist; also in Nimal (Swiss RIO collective), Accordion Tribe, Bratko Bibič & The Madleys, Die Wiener Ziehharmoniker
  • Nimal — Bratko Bibič is a shared member; both bands appeared on Ayaa
  • Ayaa — French label (Reims) that released Konzert For A Broken Dance (1990); the same label that distributed Nimal, The Work, Etron Fou Leloublan, Ne Zhdali
  • Chris Cutler — (Discogs ID: 177209) credited on the 1982 LP (single-release shared credit); member of Henry Cow, Art Bears, Cassiber; Recommended Records / RēR network
  • Accordion Tribe — later international accordion ensemble including Bratko Bibič
  • RTV Ljubljana — parent of ZKP RTVL, the Yugoslav state broadcaster label

Open Questions

  • The band’s profile says active 1975–1978 and 1981–1983, but recordings appear in 1990 and 1993 — Discogs does not clarify whether these are archival releases of older material or new studio work; the overlapping tracklists between Jodlovska Urška (1990) and Tastare (1993) suggest much of this material predates the release dates
  • Chris Cutler (Henry Cow / Recommended Records) appears as a co-credit on the 1982 LP — the nature of this connection (distribution deal, liner notes, guest appearance?) is not clear from Discogs alone
  • The Jodlovska Urška cassette features the 1975–78 lineup (Muševič, Špindler), not the 1981–83 one — the reason for this mix-and-match across later releases is unclear
  • Whether Begnagrad ever performed outside Yugoslavia / Slovenia (touring in France, Germany, Switzerland given Ayaa connection)
  • ZKP RTVL (Zavod za Kulturno-Prosvetno Lubilijana) was a sublabel of the state broadcaster RTV Ljubljana — context for how avant-garde music got released on a state label in 1982 Yugoslavia
  • Neven Smolčič (ID 850660) — credited as producer on two releases but no further Discogs profile information available; his role in the group or the Slovenian scene is undocumented
  • Web sources needed to clarify formation story, live history, and any unreleased recordings
