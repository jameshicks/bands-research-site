Violent Femmes

Discogs ID: 87643

Wisconsin folk-punk trio formed in Milwaukee in 1980. Fronted by Gordon Gano (vocals, guitar) with Brian Ritchie (bass) and Victor DeLorenzo (drums). Their self-titled debut (1983) on Slash became a slow-burn college radio classic.
