The Dils

Discogs ID: 263827
Active: 1977–1980
Origin: Carlsbad (San Diego County), California; later San Francisco and Los Angeles

American punk band co-founded and led throughout by brothers Chip Kinman (guitar, vocals) and Tony Kinman (bass, vocals). One of the earliest American punk bands with an explicitly left-wing political stance — sometimes called “California’s The Clash.” “Class War” (1977) is their signature track; it was later covered by Mission Of Burma on ONoffON (2004). The band appeared briefly in the Cheech & Chong film Up In Smoke. After the Dils split, the Kinmans formed Rank & File (cowpunk) and later Blackbird (295001) and Cowboy Nation (1557960).

Member Lineups (Discogs)

Multiple drummers cycled through: Endre Alqover, Rand McNally, Bill Chobotar (Zippy Pinhead), John Silvers. Early lineup also included vocalist Jeff Scott and drummer Joe Nanini. All material written by Kinman and Kinman.
