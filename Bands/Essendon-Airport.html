From Discogs

Essendon Airport was an Australian post-punk group from 1978-83 which explored experimental minimalist, electronic and funk music. They reformed in 2003 and have been performing intermittently since.

The band began as an instrumental duo and an offshoot of experimental music project → ↑ →, in 1978 with Robert Goodge on guitar and David Chesworth on Wurlitzer electric piano along with a home made drum machine.

They later became a four piece adding drums - Paul Fletcher, and saxophone - Ian Cox, and finally a five piece with bass player Barbara Hogarth in 1982. They reformed as a trio in 2003 for a few performances, including original members Robert Goodge and David Chesworth together with pedal steel guitarist Evil Graham Lee. Subsequent performances have also variously included previous members Paul Fletcher, Anne Sanger, and Barbara Hogarth.

Overview

Essendon Airport were an Australian post-punk group formed in 1978 as an offshoot of the experimental project → ↑ →, originally a duo of Robert Goodge on guitar and David Chesworth on Wurlitzer electric piano with a homemade drum machine. They expanded to a five-piece by 1982, adding Paul Fletcher on drums, Ian Cox on saxophone, and Barbara Hogarth on bass, before dissolving in 1983. Their catalogue spans three releases on Innocent (2) from 1980–82 and later work on Chapter Music following a 2003 reformation, with subsequent performances drawing variously on original members including Evil Graham Lee on pedal steel.
