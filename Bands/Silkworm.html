Overview

Silkworm were a US indie rock band who formed in Missoula, Montana in 1987, relocated to Seattle in 1990, and built a catalogue of guitar-driven alternative rock concentrated across the 1990s and into the 2000s on labels including Matador, Touch And Go, C/Z Records, and 12XU. The band’s closest long-term collaborator was Steve Albini, who shares credits on 27 releases, with Heather Whinna and Matthew Barnhart also recurring. The group dissolved in 2005 following the death of drummer Michael Dahlquist; guitarist Joel R. L. Phelps, who had played with the band from its founding through 1994, rejoined the reformed lineup that came together in 2024 for Albini’s memorial and elected to continue performing into 2025.
