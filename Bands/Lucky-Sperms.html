Members: Kim Gordon, Lee Ranaldo, Mike Watt, Steve Shelley

Overview

Lucky Sperms was a short-lived quartet assembled in 1991 around Mike Watt (of Minutemen / fIREHOSE) and three members of Sonic Youth — Kim Gordon, Lee Ranaldo, and Steve Shelley. The group released a single 7”, The Man (catalog number E #15), on Ecstatic Peace!, Thurston Moore’s imprint. Engineered by Wharton Tiers at Fun City Recording in New York; sleeve illustration by Raymond Pettibon. The band is self-credited as producer.

The A-side is a cover of Daniel Johnston’s “Walking The Cow” performed primarily by Watt (guitar, bass, vocals) with Shelley on drums — a Minutemen/SST-adjacent reading of Johnston’s lo-fi folk. The B-side is a medley of two Beatles songs: “Tomorrow Never Knows” / “Glass Onion,” featuring Ranaldo on guitar, Gordon on tambourine, and Watt again on bass and vocals. Thurston Moore is credited on the B-side as “Other [Nada]” — a joke credit indicating he was present but contributed nothing musically.

The A-side matrix inscription — “E-15 A T.MOORE VS. T. MOTTOLA” — is a wry dig at Thurston Moore’s corporate antagonist Tommy Mottola (then president of Sony Music, whose subsidiary DGC Records signed Sonic Youth in 1990). The B-side reads “E-15-B SSSL ECSTATIC BUMMER.” Both labels carry the pressing code U-31366M.

The release is catalogued under Discogs genres Blues, Electronic, Jazz, Rock; styles Art Rock, Noise — a somewhat unruly classification that reflects the release’s one-off, irreverent character.
