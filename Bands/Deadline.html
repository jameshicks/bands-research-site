Deadline

Discogs ID: 9368 Origin: USA Active: 1985; 1991; 2021 (archival release) Label: Celluloid Records (1985); Day Eight Music (1991)

Core project of Bill Laswell and Phillip Wilson, drawing on a large rotating cast from the downtown NYC and funk/avant-garde milieu.

Overview

Deadline was a recording project led by bassist Bill Laswell and drummer Phillip Wilson, operating primarily under the Laswell-affiliated umbrella at Celluloid Records. The group had no fixed lineup: the 1985 debut Down By Law assembled an extraordinary constellation including Manu Dibango, Jaco Pastorius, Bernie Worrell, Olu Dara, Steve Turre, Jonas Hellborg, and Aiyb Dieng. The music combined funk, African rhythms, dub processing, and electronics into a loose, bass-heavy groove aligned with what Laswell and peers were exploring across Material, Time Zone, and related Celluloid projects.

The 1991 CD Dissident (Day Eight Music / Atlantic) shifted toward a heavier, more European-progressive palette, with Jonas Hellborg as producer and co-writer, Jens Johansson on keyboards, Bootsy Collins on bass, Nicky Skopelitis on Fairlight synthesizer, and Oz Fritz as engineer/mixer. This album is Nicky Skopelitis’s primary credit within the Deadline catalogue.

A third release, CBGB (2021), is a self-released archival document of an early live performance, featuring a leaner four-piece of Laswell, Wilson, Robert Quine, Fred Maher, and Michael Beinhorn — closer to the post-punk downtown rock axis.

Dissident (1991, CD)

Personnel: Jonas Hellborg (bass, producer); Bill Laswell (bass, co-producer on tracks 1, 2, 4–6); Bootsy Collins (bass); Bernie Worrell (keyboards); Jens Johansson (keyboards); Nicky Skopelitis (Fairlight synthesizer); Aiyb Dieng (percussion). Tracklist: “Who’s Who” / “Black Swans” / “World Disorder” / “Refrain” / “Heart of Darkness” / “The Stone That Speaks” / “Broken Edge”. Writing credits split between Hellborg (most tracks), Laswell, Johansson, and Skopelitis.

Genres/styles: Electronic, Jazz, Funk / Soul; Ambient, Dub, Electro, Future Jazz, Jazz-Rock.

Connections

  • Bill Laswell — project founder; also led Material, produced for Celluloid Records extensively
  • Nicky Skopelitis — Fairlight synth and co-writer on Dissident; part of Laswell’s core downtown circle
  • Celluloid Records — home label for 1985 releases; Jean Karakos’s NYC imprint central to this scene
  • Day Eight Music — released Dissident 1991
  • Manu Dibango — Cameroonian saxophonist/vocalist; “Makossa Rock” title references his 1972 hit
  • Jaco Pastorius — plays bass on “Makossa Rock” (one of his last recorded appearances)
