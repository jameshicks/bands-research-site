Trash Kit

London post-punk trio. Discogs artist ID: 1976369. Name variation: TK.

Overview

Trash Kit formed in London in 2009, initially a three-piece of Rachel Aggs (guitar, violin, vocals), Rachel Horwood (drums, vocals), and Ros Murray (bass). The band is rooted in the DIY underground of London’s post-punk scene and has maintained a close relationship with promoters and label Upset! The Rhythm throughout their career.

Their music draws on post-punk, no-wave, and African fingerstyle guitar — Aggs’ playing is explicitly informed by African rhythmic patterns and no-wave percussiveness. Horwood’s drumming is described as treating the kit as a lead instrument. Murray brings cello and classical training (she was previously a member of Electrelane) to her bass work, though her credited role in Trash Kit is bass only.

By the time of Horizon (2019), Murray had departed and Gill Partington (also of Halo Halo) took over on bass guitar. The Discogs member list includes both Murray and Partington.

All three studio LPs — Trash Kit (2010), Confidence (2014), and Horizon (2019) — were released on Upset! The Rhythm. Influences cited in the band’s profile include X-Ray Spex, The Ex, and The Raincoats.

Rachel Aggs is the most prolific member across parallel projects: she plays in Sacred Paws, Shopping (3), and Golden Grrrls, and releases solo music under her Discogs name Ray Aggs. Rachel Horwood also plays in Halo Halo, Bas Jan, and Jenny Moore’s Mystic Business, among others.
