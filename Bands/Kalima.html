Kalima

Discogs ID: 12096
Origin: Manchester, UK
Active: 1983–1991; reformed 2001
Genres: Jazz, Latin, Funk
Styles: Jazzdance, Contemporary Jazz, Latin Jazz, Bossa Nova

Manchester jazz-funk and Latin fusion band, formed in 1983 as a direct evolution of Swamp Children (1980–1983). All five Swamp Children founding members carried over. Released exclusively on Factory Records throughout their original run. The name was taken from a track by jazz drummer Elvin Jones. The band shared significant membership overlap with A Certain Ratio — Tony Quigley, Martin Moscrop, Andy Connell, and Jez Kerr all appear in both contexts.

Origins: Swamp Children → Kalima

Swamp Children formed in Manchester in 1980 around Ann Quigley (vocals), Tony Quigley (sax/bass), John Kirkham (guitar), Ceri Evans (keyboards/bass), Cliff Saffer (sax), and Martin Moscrop (drums). They recorded for Factory Records and mutated into Kalima in 1983 without a full lineup break — the same core members simply adopted the new name and direction, moving from a post-punk-inflected sound toward jazz, Latin, and Brazilian influences.

The Swamp Children recordings appear on the Flyaway compilation (Facd 219, 1989) as its first two tracks; the remaining 14 tracks document Kalima’s 1984–1987 output.

After Factory

Kalima broke up in 1991. They reformed in 2001 and released In Spirit (2002) on Kin Records — the only recording from the reunion period in Discogs.

Middle era (1987–1988)

The 1987 single Weird Feelings marked a lineup change — Martin Moscrop departed and David Higgins took over on drums. Other new faces: Matthew Taylor (alto/tenor sax), Martin Hennin (bass), Khairie Gedal (trumpet/flugelhorn), Bernard Moss (flute).

Ceri Evans departure (2026-07-13)

Verdict: Evans departed Kalima in 1986, after the Whispered Words single (April 1986). No more specific month is documented.

LTM Recordings biography (written by James Nice, revised September 2011): https://www.ltmrecordings.com/kalima.html

The biography contains two relevant passages:

  1. Describing the autumn 1986 departures: “that autumn Connell withdrew from both ACR and Kalima, and was followed by Kerr and Moscrop soon afterwards.”

  2. A later retrospective passage quoting Ann Quigley: “just as had Kalima lost their initial innocence with the departure of Martin, Jez, Andy and Ceri in 1986, the core trio of Ann, John and Tony now found that their label too had undergone a sea change.”

The first passage does not name Evans explicitly in the autumn 1986 sequence (it names Connell, Kerr, Moscrop, plus manager Nathan McGough and percussionist Chris Manis). The second passage groups Evans with the same cohort. Both point to 1986 as his departure year; timing within 1986 is unspecified but most likely autumn alongside or shortly after the others.

Wikipedia, Kalima (band) (as of 2026-06-25): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalima_(band)

Corroborates: “Evans, Kerr and Moscrop soon followed” after Connell’s post-Whispered Words withdrawal. Attributes the departures to scheduling conflicts with ACR’s US touring schedule.

Conflict flag: Wikipedia, The Brand New Heavies article (as of 2026-07-13): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Brand_New_Heavies lists Evans as “keyboards (1985–1992)” — suggesting he joined BNH in 1985, a year before his Kalima departure. This implies either (a) Evans was simultaneously in both bands in 1985–1986, (b) the BNH “1985” start date is incorrect, or (c) his Kalima departure was earlier than autumn 1986. The LTM bio is the more authoritative source for the Kalima side; the BNH Wikipedia date is unsourced. Discogs Kalima credits show Evans absent from all released recordings.
