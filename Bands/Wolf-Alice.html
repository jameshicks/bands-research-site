Wolf Alice

Alternative / indie rock band from London, UK. Discogs artist ID: 3153581. Formed in 2010 as an acoustic duo — vocalist-guitarist Ellie Rowsell and guitarist Joff Oddie — before adding drummer Joel Amey and bassist Theo Ellis in 2012 to become a full band. Early singles on Chess Club (2013) gave way to a deal with Dirty Hit, on which they released all three studio albums. The band name derives from Angela Carter’s short story Wolf-Alice, a feral-child variation on Little Red Riding Hood. Three albums across six years chart a move from raw guitar-band noise-pop (My Love Is Cool) through atmospheric post-grunge (Visions of a Life) to orchestrated art-rock (Blue Weekend); a fourth album (The Clearing, 2025) marks a shift to Columbia / RCA. They won the Mercury Prize for Visions of a Life (2017) and again for Blue Weekend (2021).
