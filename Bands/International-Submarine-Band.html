International Submarine Band

Discogs ID: 1005217 Also credited as: Gram Parsons’ International Submarine Band; Int. Submarine Band

Country rock band active c. 1966–1968. Recorded Safe At Home on LHI Records in 1968 (cat# LHI-S-12001) — considered by some music historians to be the first country rock album. The record predates Gram Parsons’s tenure with The Byrds.

Safe At Home (LHI-S-12001, 1968)

Produced by Suzi Jane Hokom. Engineered by Eddie Brackett and Michael Lietz. Mastered by Ken Perry. Tracklist blends Parsons originals with covers of Merle Haggard, Johnny Cash, Arthur “Big Boy” Crudup, Jack Clement, and Barry Goldberg.
