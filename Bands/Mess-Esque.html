Mess Esque

Discogs artist ID: 9257494

Australian indie/folk duo formed in 2020, consisting of Mick Turner (guitar, bass, keyboards, organ, percussion) and Helen Franzmann (vocals, lyrics). Franzmann also records as McKisko. Turner is the guitarist of Dirty Three, giving Mess Esque a natural connection to that band’s atmospheric, sprawling instrumental sensibility — but with Franzmann’s vocals as a central element rather than an afterthought. The duo releases music through Drag City internationally and Milk! Records in Australia.

Note: an earlier vault stub incorrectly listed the members as Mick Turner and Jim White (2). Jim White (2) is Dirty Three’s drummer and collaborates with Turner in Tren Brothers and Boxhead Ensemble, but is not a member of Mess Esque. He does appear as a session drummer on one track (“Jupiter”) on the self-titled album and on the A-side of the 2022 single.
