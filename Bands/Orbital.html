Orbital

Discogs ID: 1279 Origin: Sevenoaks, Kent, England Active: 1989–2004, 2009–present Members: Phil Hartnoll, Paul Hartnoll

British electronic duo; one of the defining acts of UK rave and techno. Named after the M25 orbital motorway. Multiple classic albums on FFRR / Internal / London Records. Collaborated with Zola Jesus on New France (2012, ACP Recordings). Released as a single and EP across multiple pressings: UK/Europe on ACP Recordings, Australia on Liberator Music, Denmark (remixes) on Warner Music Denmark. ACP Recordings is the primary label — not a sublabel or distributor — credited on all main pressings (Discogs master 420026).
