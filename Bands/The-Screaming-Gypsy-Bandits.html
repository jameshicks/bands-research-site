The Screaming Gypsy Bandits

1970s avant-rock group from Bloomington, Indiana, USA. Discogs artist ID: 896646. Name variations: “Screamin’ Gypsy Bandits”, “Screaming Gypsy Bandits.”

Overview

The Screaming Gypsy Bandits were the avant-rock group that Mark Bingham joined after returning from Los Angeles in the mid-1960s. Bingham attended Indiana University in Bloomington and began his own indie label Bar-B-Q Records in parallel with the band. The group’s lineup included future MX-80 Sound members Bruce Anderson and Dale Sophiea, singer/songwriter Caroline Peyton, and jazz bassist Mark Dresser.
