Lee Ranaldo And The Dust

Post–Sonic Youth band led by Lee Ranaldo (guitar, vocals), with Alan Licht (guitar, backing vocals), Steve Shelley (drums, percussion), and Tim Luntzel (bass). Discogs artist ID: 3489021. Name variations: LR And The Dust, Lee Ranaldo & The Dust, Lee Ranaldo Y Los Dust.

Overview

Lee Ranaldo And The Dust formed in 2012 as the touring and recording band behind Lee Ranaldo’s post-Sonic Youth solo work. The Dust’s core lineup — Licht (guitar), Shelley (drums), and Luntzel (bass) — first came together for Ranaldo’s 2012 album Between The Times And The Tides (Matador), which is credited to Lee Ranaldo solo but features all four musicians. The band was formally credited on their second record together, Last Night On Earth (Matador, 2013), and continued with the acoustic-format LP Acoustic Dust (El Segell Del Primavera, 2014), recorded in Barcelona.

The group’s production team was collaborative: Last Night On Earth was produced by Ranaldo, with Licht, Shelley, and engineer Ted Young all sharing co-production credit. Young also served as recording and mixing engineer on the main album. A supplementary release, The Rising Tide (Last Night On Earth Demos And Rehearsals) (Lazy Eight, 2013), documented the sessions.

According to Wikipedia’s article on Last Night On Earth, Ranaldo organized The Dust specifically as a touring and recording unit following Between The Times And The Tides, which had featured a more scattered lineup (Jim O’Rourke, Nels Cline, Bob Bert among others). The Dust provided a stable, band-oriented context for Ranaldo’s songwriting.

Acoustic Dust (2014) took a different approach: the band traveled to Barcelona for a week with producer/multi-instrumentalist Refree (Raül Fernandez Refree), recording a full-band acoustic set of songs from the first two albums plus cover versions of Neil Young, Sandy Denny, and Michael Nesmith. The record was issued by Primavera Sound’s label El Segell Del Primavera and represents the band’s only non-Matador studio release.

Tim Luntzel — Discogs Profile

Tim Luntzel (also credited as Tim Lüntzel, Timothy Bruce Luntzel) has no primary-credited solo releases on Discogs. His contributor credits include bass work across a range of singer-songwriter, jazz, and indie contexts:

  • Conor Oberst / Bright Eyes — I’m Wide Awake, It’s Morning (2005, Saddle Creek); Cassadaga (2007, Saddle Creek)
  • Jenny Scheinman — Jenny Scheinman (2008, Koch Records)
  • Loudon Wainwright III — High Wide And Handsome: The Charlie Poole Project (2009, Proper Records)
  • Norah Jones — The List (2009, Manhattan Records); Slow New York (2006, Manhattan Records)
  • Garland Jeffreys — The King Of In Between / Don’t Call Me Buckwheat sessions (various)
  • Lee Ranaldo — Electric Trim (Mute, 2017) as bajo quinto player
  • Counting Crows — Underwater Sunshine (Or What We Did On Our Summer Vacation) (2012)
  • Everly Brothers tribute — Foreverly (Reprise, 2013, Billie Joe Armstrong / Norah Jones); A Date With The Everly Brothers (Lake Bottom Records, 2013)

His Discogs groups include Lee Ranaldo And The Dust (3489021), Firehorse (3) (2742423), Guaranteed Swahili (1558304), Jim Campilongo Electric Trio (1007421), and The Melvin Sparks Band (328136). Active across NYC jazz, country, and indie scenes.

Ted Young — Discogs Profile

Ted Young (Discogs ID: 494409) is a New York-based recording and mixing engineer with extensive credits at Water Music Studios (Hoboken, NJ) and The Magic Shop (NYC), and at Plum Creek Sound. His credits span hardcore, indie rock, alternative, and country/Americana. Notable sessions include:

  • Sonic Youth — The Eternal (Matador, 2009) as ProTools engineer
  • Lee Ranaldo — Between The Times And The Tides (Matador, 2012) as assistant mix engineer
  • Kurt Vile — Wakin On A Pretty Daze (Matador, 2013); Bottle It In (Matador, 2018)
  • The Hold Steady — Boys And Girls In America (Vagrant, 2006) as assistant mix engineer
  • Neil Nilsson — Son Of Schmilsson (Legacy/RCA, 2006) as mix engineer
  • Lamb Of God — Ashes Of The Wake (Prosthetic/Epic, 2004) as assistant engineer
  • The Rolling Stones — The Rolling Stones In Mono (ABKCO, 2016) as sound restoration; Rock And Roll Circus (ABKCO, 2019) as mixer
  • White Lung — Endless Now (Sub Pop, 2011) as engineer
  • Alan Vega — archival tape transfers (Sacred Bones, 2026)

Young’s work on Last Night On Earth (2013) was his most prominent production co-credit.
