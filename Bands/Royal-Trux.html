Royal Trux

Discogs artist ID: 65828 Active: 1987–2001; reunited 2015–2019 Country: US Genres: Noise Rock, Art Rock, Alternative Rock

Overview

Royal Trux were an American art rock / noise rock duo — Neil Hagerty (guitar, vocals, multi-instrumentalist) and Jennifer Herrema (vocals, production) — formed in 1987 and dissolving in 2001, with a 2015–2019 reunion. Their career is one of the most stylistically restless in 1990s indie rock: from near-unlistenable double-LP noise collage (Twin Infinitives, 1990) to hard rock with a hired full band produced by Neil Young’s producer David Briggs (Thank You, 1995), and back again to blown-out independent recording. They began on Drag City (US) and Domino (UK/Europe), made two albums for Virgin/Hut Recordings during 1995–1997, then returned to Domino and Drag City. Reunion-era recordings were released on Fat Possum Records. In 2019 Hagerty publicly disowned the final album White Stuff, stating it had been composed through remote file-swapping and he did not approve the finished result.

Neil Hagerty had previously played guitar in Pussy Galore (2) (Washington D.C. / New York, 1985–1990). After Royal Trux dissolved, Hagerty led The Howling Hex (2003–2020, on Drag City); Herrema led RTX (2) (2004–2011) and then Black Bananas (2012–).
