Simple Minds

Scottish post-punk / new wave band formed in Glasgow in 1977. Discogs artist ID: 18547

Overview

Simple Minds emerged from the Glasgow post-punk scene in 1977, rising directly from the ashes of Johnny And The Self Abusers, whose sole single was released the same day the band split. The core duo of Jim Kerr (vocals) and Charlie Burchill (guitar) anchored a shifting lineup through twelve studio albums spanning four decades. Their early records — produced by John Leckie and released on the small Scottish label Zoom Records — placed them at the intersection of post-punk, krautrock, and synth-driven new wave. By 1981 they had signed to Virgin and were working with Steve Hillage; by 1982 (with producer Peter Walsh) they achieved the breakthrough of New Gold Dream (81-82-83-84). The shift to stadium rock came with Sparkle In The Rain (produced by Steve Lillywhite, 1983) and Once Upon A Time (produced by Jimmy Iovine and Bob Clearmountain, 1985), the latter containing “Don’t You (Forget About Me)” — co-written and produced by Keith Forsey, the song became a global #1 after its inclusion on The Breakfast Club soundtrack. Since 2005 the band has returned to consistent activity, scoring six consecutive UK Top 40 studio albums through 2022’s Direction Of The Heart.
