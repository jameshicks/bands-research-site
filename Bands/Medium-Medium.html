Medium Medium

From: Nottingham, England Active: 1979–1983; partial reunion activity from late 1980s Labels: Apt Music (self), Cherry Red, Sound (5), Intercord, Trance Records Discogs ID: 33938

Overview

Post-punk funk band from Nottingham. Five-piece at formation, expanded to seven by 1982. Their 1981 single “Hungry, So Angry” is considered a definitive document of British post-punk’s encounter with funk — bass-driven, sax-laced, unrelenting. Produced by Adrian Sherwood in collaboration with Graham Spink. The debut album The Glitterhouse followed on Cherry Red the same year; a second album, Splendid Isolation, was recorded in the Netherlands but never given a UK release.

Frontman and primary lyricist John Rees Lewis died in June 2024.

History

1979: Self-released debut

Formed Nottingham. First release “Them Or Me / Freeze” (Apt Music, 1979) — self-released on their own imprint. Produced by Robin Langridge and Wally Brill. The sleeve carried the stylised typography “mēdium mēdium.” Five-piece: Turton, Ryder, Spink, Lewis, Stone.

1981: Cherry Red and breakthrough

Signed to Cherry Red. “Hungry, So Angry / Nadsat Dream” (Cherry Red CHERRY 24, 1981) — co-produced by Adrian Sherwood and Graham Spink, mastered by Porky (5). “Hungry, So Angry” is the track they’re known for: fast and relentless, with sax and a locked-in funk groove underneath Lewis’s unhinged vocals. The B-side title “Nadsat Dream” references the invented slang of Anthony Burgess’s A Clockwork Orange.

The Glitterhouse (Cherry Red BRED 22, 1981): debut album, self-produced. Recorded at Foel Studio (Dave Anderson’s studio in Wales) with Anderson and Spink engineering. Lyrics divided between Lewis (most tracks) and Ryder (A3, B2). Distributed internationally via Roadrunner, Cachalot, Ariston (Italy), Edigsa (Spain), and others — unusually wide reach for a Cherry Red LP.

Both the single and the album were licensed to Virgin in certain markets.

1982: Splendid Isolation (Netherlands only)

Second album, Splendid Isolation (Sound (5), Netherlands, 1982) — produced by John Kriek and Medium Medium, engineered by Spink and Kriek. Released on a Dutch label and distributed via Intercord in Germany; no UK release. The single “If You Touched Her She’d Smear” (Intercord / Sound 5, 1982) drew from the same sessions, with Les Barrett contributing cover art.

The lack of a UK release for Splendid Isolation remains the central gap in their story.

1988: Reissue activity

The Glitterhouse & Plus (Trance Records / Line, 1988) — reissue of debut album with additional tracks, again credited to Dave Anderson and Graham Spink as producers/engineers. Live In Holland (Trance Records, 1988) appeared alongside it.

Dave Anderson (3) — Foel Studio

Dave Anderson (born 1949, Romford) is the most significant outside presence in the Medium Medium discography. He engineered and produced The Glitterhouse at his own Foel Studio (founded 1973) in Wales. His prior career: member of Amon Düül II (1967–1971), Hawkwind (1971–1972), and later The Groundhogs (1984–2017). He also runs the Demi Monde label. His involvement connects Medium Medium — however tangentially — to the krautrock and space-rock underground of the early 1970s.

Adrian Sherwood Connection

Adrian Sherwood (born 1958, London) produced “Hungry, So Angry / Nadsat Dream” with Graham Spink. Sherwood is the founder of On-U Sound — the label and production approach that bridged UK post-punk with Jamaican dub through the 1980s. His other production work during this period included Cabaret Voltaire’s Code (1987), Tackhead, New Age Steppers, and many others. Medium Medium on Cherry Red and Sherwood on On-U Sound were operating in overlapping circles of British post-punk’s leftfield in 1981.

Cherry Red Context

Cherry Red (founded 1978 by Iain McNay) was one of the key British post-punk independents. Medium Medium were on the label alongside The Monochrome Set, Marine Girls, Felt, and others — a roster running from arch post-punk to jangly indie. The label’s approach (low overhead, wide licensing) explains how The Glitterhouse ended up pressed in Spain, Italy, and the Netherlands simultaneously.
