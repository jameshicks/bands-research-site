Redmoon Theater

Chicago-based experimental theater company. Active from at least the mid-1990s. Known for large-scale spectacle productions combining puppetry, physical performance, and original music. Associated recordings on Garlic Records (2) and Redmoon Records document music composed and performed for specific productions.

No dedicated Discogs artist page found — Redmoon Theater appears on Discogs as a credit in release titles and on the Redmoon Records label (ID: 71645), which issued at least one release. The primary Discogs anchor is composer/percussionist Michael Zerang, who is the credited primary artist on the theater’s recording releases.

Redmoon Theater’s The Ballad Of Frankie And Johnny (1997)

Discogs release ID: 782992
Label: Redmoon Records — cat# #001
Primary credit: Michael Zerang
Format: CD
Genres: Jazz, Pop

Music composed and arranged by Michael Zerang for Redmoon Theater’s Ballad of Frankie and Johnny production. Released on the theater’s own Redmoon Records imprint.

Track 15 (“Hollow”) composed by Shu Shubat, with vocals by Tria Smith. Track 21 (“Lullaby, Good Night”) composed and played on accordion by Tria Smith.

The Earth Sessions Vol. I/II (1997)

Discogs release ID: 1760202
Label: BOXmedia — cat# BOXCD004A/B
Primary credits: Jim Baker & Michael Zerang
Format: 2xCD (Album)
Genres: Electronic, Jazz
Styles: Abstract, Free Improvisation

Dual-volume set: Vol. I is “Music For Piano And Drum Kit,” Vol. II is “Music For Percussion And Electronics.” Not explicitly credited as a Redmoon Theater production on the release, but Redmoon Theater is named in the brief as associated. The cover art is by Knud Karl Lonberg-Holm (Discogs ID: 2421107) — likely related to Fredrick Lonberg-Holm.
