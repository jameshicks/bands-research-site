Happy Go Licky

Washington DC post-hardcore band. Discogs artist ID: 309584. Active 1987–1988.

Transitional outfit sharing the exact lineup of Rites Of Spring — Guy Picciotto, Eddie Janney, Mike Fellows, and Brendan Canty — that operated in the gap between Rites Of Spring’s dissolution and the formation of Fugazi. Released one self-titled LP on Peterbilt Records (1988) and appeared briefly under the name HGL. The band also figures in the 8/2/82 recording (Peterbilt, 1989), though that release is formally credited to Deadline (5).

Biography

Formation and Context (1987)

Happy Go Licky formed in 1987 from the ashes of Rites Of Spring, keeping the full four-piece intact. The DC scene at that moment was in transition: the first wave of Dischord hardcore had given way to a more emotionally charged post-hardcore, and the members were simultaneously circling other configurations — One Last Wish (a short-lived 1986 project featuring Picciotto, Janney, Canty, and Michael Hampton (3)) had already come and gone.

The name change to Happy Go Licky represented a deliberate break in identity rather than a straightforward continuation of Rites Of Spring’s sound, though the lineup was unchanged. Picciotto and Janney shared guitar and vocal duties. Fellows held the bass chair as he had in Rites Of Spring. Canty drove the rhythm section.

Recording and LP Release (1988)

The band recorded a self-titled album released in 1988 on Peterbilt Records (cat. Peterbilt 001), a label Picciotto founded that year. The LP is a 12” vinyl release (33 ⅓ RPM) with six tracks:

Side A: Boca Raton / Suzuki / Pastel Blue Eyes
Side B: Ansol / White Lines / Torso Butter

The matrix runout reads “PETERBILT-001-A 8x349 MASTERDISK” / “PETERBILT-001-B 8x349 MASTERDISK”, placing the lacquer cut at Masterdisk. Discogs catalogs two known pressings. The release is categorized under Rock, with styles Art Rock / Experimental / Punk.

By the time the LP appeared, the band had already disbanded and Picciotto was active in Fugazi (which began in 1987). The LP thus functions partly as documentation of the transitional period rather than an active release.

The 8/2/82 Recording and Deadline (5) (1989)

The second Peterbilt release (Peterbilt 2, 1989) is titled 8/2/82 and is credited on Discogs to Deadline (5), not Happy Go Licky. Canty plays drums on the recording; other personnel include Chris Caron (guitar), Terry Scanlon (bass), Ray Hare (vocals), and backing vocalists including Chipsterwick, Harvestfellows, and Urchin (3). Ian MacKaye and Don Zientara are credited as engineers. Jeff Nelson provided graphic assistance. This is a live recording from August 2, 1982 — predating Happy Go Licky by five years — and shares only the label and Canty’s participation with the Happy Go Licky story. The original brief that associated it with Happy Go Licky appears to reflect Peterbilt catalog context rather than direct band lineage.

Dissolution and Onward (1988–)

Happy Go Licky disbanded in 1988. The four members scattered across several subsequent projects:

  • Picciotto and Canty co-founded Fugazi with Ian MacKaye and Joe Lally
  • Janney joined Untouchables and later Skewbald / Grand Union, Rozzlyn Rangers, Faith (3), and Brief Weeds
  • Fellows went on to Royal Trux, Silver Jews, Air Miami, Government Issue, and Getaway Car
  • Canty additionally participated in The Dambuilders, All Scars, Deathfix, Brief Weeds, The Messthetics, Fake Names, and MC50

The One Last Wish recordings from 1986 — featuring Picciotto, Janney, Canty, and Michael Hampton — were eventually released posthumously in 1999 as 1986 (Dischord DIS 118 / Peterbilt 6), co-produced by Ian MacKaye and the band, engineered by Don Zientara.

Connections

  • Peterbilt Records — Picciotto’s own label, founded 1988; all HGL releases issued here
  • Dischord Records — Peterbilt operates in close association with Dischord (Arlington, VA address; co-releases)
  • Don Zientara — engineer on associated Peterbilt recordings (Deadline, One Last Wish)
  • Ian MacKaye — co-engineer on Deadline 8/2/82; producer of One Last Wish 1986; Fugazi co-founder
