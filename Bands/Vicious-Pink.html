Vicious Pink

UK synth-pop duo formed in Leeds in 1981 by Brian Moss and Josephine “Josie” Warden (also credited as Cece). Discogs artist ID: 47022. Also recorded as Vicious Pink Phenomena (Discogs ID: 87384) — the early-period name; same members, confirmed alias.

Overview

Vicious Pink began performing under the name Vicious Pink Phenomena around 1981–1982, changing to Vicious Pink by the time of their Parlophone signing circa 1983–1984. Their debut single “My Private Tokyo” (1982) was released on The Mobile Suit Corporation (a sublabel of Phonogram) and produced by Dave Ball of Soft Cell. Earlier still, as Vicious Pink Phenomena, they contributed backing vocals to Soft Cell’s landmark debut album Non-Stop Erotic Cabaret (1981).

Their commercial peak came with Parlophone singles “Cccan’t You See” (1984) and “Fetish / Spooky” (1985), both produced by Tony Mansfield. The 1986 compilation LP Vicious Pink (released on Capitol in Canada and EMI elsewhere) gathered their Parlophone material and became their primary album-format release. A late-period single “8:15 To Nowhere” appeared on Belgian label LD Records in 1988, entering New Beat territory.

After a long hiatus, the duo returned in the 2020s with new material on Minimal Wave — the archival/revival label run out of New York — releasing the retrospective/new-material double-LP West View (2022) and the new album Unexpected (2024), the latter featuring Dave Ball as an uncredited guest.

The Two Discogs Entries

Discogs carries two separate entries for this act:

  • Vicious Pink (ID: 47022) — main entry; described as “New Wave Synth-Pop duo formed in Leeds, England in 1981, formerly known as Vicious Pink Phenomena.” Releases from 1984 onward.
  • Vicious Pink Phenomena (ID: 87384) — early-period name. Releases 1981–1983 and occasional later unofficial releases. Name variations include “Robert & Fifi (Vicious Pink Phenomena, The)” and “Robert And Fifi (Vicious Pink Phenomena)” — indicating the “Robert Reevell” / “Fifi Reevell” aliases used by Brian Moss and Josie Warden on the debut single.

Both entries share the same members (Brian Moss / Josie Warden) and alias each other. The distinction is the name used at time of release: Vicious Pink Phenomena for 1981–1983 output, Vicious Pink from 1984 onward. The Soft Cell Non-Stop Erotic Cabaret backing vocals (1981) are credited to Vicious Pink Phenomena.
