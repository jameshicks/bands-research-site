Hole (2)

Alternative rock band formed in Hollywood, Los Angeles, 1989. Discogs artist ID: 105728. The “(2)” is Discogs convention to disambiguate from the JG Thirlwell/Foetus album and other entries.

Overview

Hole was founded by singer-guitarist Courtney Love and lead guitarist Eric Erlandson. The band went through numerous bassist and drummer changes across its lifespan (1989–2002, revived 2009–2012), with the most prominent members being drummer Patty Schemel and bassists Kristen Pfaff (died June 1994) and Melissa Auf Der Maur. Hole released four studio albums spanning two distinct eras and became one of the most commercially successful rock bands of the 1990s fronted by a woman. (Wikipedia)

Their debut LP Pretty on the Inside (1991) was produced by Kim Gordon and Don Fleming and drew comparisons to the noise-rock and punk underground. Their second album Live Through This (1994, DGC Records) fused grunge, punk, and pop-rock; it was released four days after the death of Kurt Cobain, Love’s husband. Celebrity Skin (1998, DGC) marked a commercial shift toward polished alternative rock, co-written in part by Billy Corgan, and sold approximately two million copies worldwide.

A reunion in 2009–2012 produced Nobody’s Daughter (2010, Mercury), recorded largely with a new lineup and produced by Michael Beinhorn and Linda Perry.

Connections

  • Courtney Love — founder, vocalist, guitarist; sole constant member; also released America’s Sweetheart (2004) as solo artist on Virgin/EMI
  • Eric Erlandson — lead guitarist 1989–2002, 2012; only other original surviving member through both eras; Hit So Hard documentary 2012
  • Kristen Pfaff — bassist on Live Through This; died of heroin overdose, June 16, 1994; previously in Janitor Joe
  • Patty Schemel — drummer on Live Through This and Celebrity Skin; subject of documentary Hit So Hard (2011); also appeared with Melissa Auf Der Maur and others post-Hole
  • Melissa Auf Der Maur — bassist from 1994; previously in Montreal; solo albums Auf Der Maur (2004, Capitol) and Out Of Our Minds (2010)
  • Kim Gordon — co-produced debut; Sonic Youth co-founder
  • Don Fleming — co-produced debut; member of B.A.L.L., Dim Stars
  • Paul Q. Kolderie — co-produced Live Through This; Fort Apache Studios; worked on Radiohead Pablo Honey, Morphine, Sugar
  • Sean Slade — co-produced Live Through This with Kolderie; same Fort Apache network
  • Michael Beinhorn — produced Celebrity Skin and Nobody’s Daughter; also produced RHCP Mother’s Milk (1989), Soundgarden Superunknown (1994), Marilyn Manson Mechanical Animals (1998), Ozzy Osbourne Ozzmosis (1995)
  • Billy Corgan — co-wrote several Celebrity Skin tracks; Smashing Pumpkins frontman
  • Kat Bjelland — Babes In Toyland frontwoman; pre-Hole Pagan Babies project with Love; appears on Hole-related compilations
  • J Mascis — mixed “Rock Star” on Live Through This; Dinosaur Jr frontman; ties to Don Fleming
  • Jack Endino — produced Dicknail (Sub Pop, 1991); prolific Seattle grunge producer
  • Linda Perry — produced 2 tracks on Nobody’s Daughter; former 4 Non Blondes frontwoman
  • Kurt Cobain — Courtney Love’s husband (married February 1992, died April 5, 1994); appears in credits of the 2012 Hit So Hard documentary
  • Stevie Nicks — Hole covered her “Gold Dust Woman” (1996 soundtrack)
  • Stuart Moxham — credits on Live Through This era; originally of The Young Marble Giants
  • DGC — primary label 1994–1999
  • City Slang — European singles label across whole first era
  • Caroline Records — released Pretty on the Inside and Ask For It EP
