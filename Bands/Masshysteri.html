Overview

Masshysteri were a punk band from Umeå, Sweden, active from 2007 to 2010. The four-piece — Erik Viklund, Kajsa Bergsten, Robert Pettersson, and Sara Almgren — worked across punk, power pop, garage rock, and post-punk. Their compact run yielded four releases, with three appearing on US label Feral Ward alongside placements on Swedish imprints Ny Våg Records and Nada Nada Discos. Recording sessions consistently involved Henrik Oja and Mats Hammarström, who share credits across nearly the full catalogue.

Feral Ward connection — origin

The most likely explanation for Masshysteri releasing on Feral Ward is a pre-existing label relationship carried over from their predecessor band. Three of Masshysteri’s core members — Robert Pettersson, Sara Almgren, and Erik Viklund — had previously been in The Vicious, also from Umeå. The Vicious released their LP Alienated on Feral Ward (catalog YAN-039, 2006–07), pressed as 12” at 45 RPM, with Swedish co-release on Ny Våg Records. The same label pairing (Feral Ward for the US, Ny Våg for Sweden) reappears on Masshysteri releases, strongly suggesting the arrangement simply continued. No interview or press source has been found that explicitly confirms this or names a person at Feral Ward who championed Masshysteri.

Sources checked: RVA Mag review (rvamag.com, no deal detail), shootmeagain.com artist page (no deal detail), tryburningthisone.blogspot.com post on Alienated (no deal detail), Tumblr Passion On Plastic blog (403 — not fetched). Search results (popsike.com, Discogs snippet) confirm The Vicious / Feral Ward / Ny Våg release facts.

Feral Ward label background: Founded by Yannick Lorrain as The Great American Steak Religion (Ottawa, Canada, 1994), renamed Witch Hunt (2000), then Feral Ward (2002), later operating from Portland, OR. Specialises in hardcore punk and crust. No confirmed personal tie between Lorrain and the Umeå network documented in any source found. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_American_Steak_Religion (checked 2026-11-19)

Dennis Lyxzén as manager: Wikipedia lists Lyxzén as Masshysteri’s record company manager, and search-result snippets confirm he attributed the band’s breakup to internal member conflicts. Lyxzén co-founded Ny Våg Records and Desperate Fight Records, which fits a managerial role overseeing the Swedish side of the releases. No source names him as the person who brokered the Feral Ward deal specifically.
