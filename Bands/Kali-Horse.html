Kali Horse

Discogs ID: 14154151 Origin: Toronto, Ontario, Canada Genre: Ethereal post-psych swamp rock Active: 2018– (originally as Kaleidoscope Horse; renamed Kali Horse c.2020–21) Label: Self-released (Not On Label) Former name: Kaleidoscope Horse (five-piece; released Slow Slow album in 2018 — pre-dates Discogs Kali Horse entry)

Toronto two-piece built around Desiree Das Gupta and Sam Maloney (2). The band’s self-description on Discogs is “ethereal post-psych swamp rock.” Their debut is a dense, layered, largely self-produced digital album with an expanded ensemble of contributing players, almost entirely from the Toronto scene. By 2021 they were already an active live act; by 2025–26 they had expanded to a four-piece for live shows (adding Brandon Bak on drums and Cameron Kirk on bass).

Conflict (Active date): Discogs earliest entry is 2023 (Some Type Of Electric Lagoon). Wavelength Music documents them performing in Feb 2021 under the Kali Horse name, and a Wavelength interview (Feb 2021) references the precursor project Kaleidoscope Horse releasing Slow Slow in 2018. The 2023 Discogs date reflects only the DB’s catalogued release, not formation. [source: https://www.wavelengthmusic.ca/zine/kali-horse-the-wl-interview/]

2099 EP (2025)

Self-released EP, 5 tracks. Released 2025-07-25. Described as a departure from the guitar-forward sound of Some Type Of Electric Lagoon, foregrounding vocal texture and electronic elements. Singles: “The Buyer” (with music video) and “In Blue” (video shot on a Toronto rooftop in winter, two long takes). [source: https://exclaim.ca/music/article/kali-horse-share-2099-ep-music-video-for-the-buyer, https://exclaim.ca/music/article/kali-horse-are-in-blue-in-new-music-video]

Live Activity

Summary

Kali Horse are an active live band with a documented performance history from at least February 2021. Live shows expanded the duo to a four-piece (adding Brandon Bak on drums/production and Cameron Kirk on bass). Their live aesthetic, per a 2021 Wavelength interview, involves theatrical elements: “There’s fog. There’s a demonic voice. There’s some dance tracks, some guitar solos.” [source: https://www.wavelengthmusic.ca/zine/kali-horse-the-wl-interview/]

Das Gupta / The Cross Sea Connection — Clarified

The “orbit of The Cross Sea” description was partially accurate but imprecise. Das Gupta has no musical role in The Cross Sea and is not a member. Her connection is a single photographic credit: she took the photo titled “Anna In The Reeds” that appears on The Cross Sea’s 2026 debut album. The Cross Sea is a separate Toronto project led by Anna Mernieks-Duffield, Kevin McMahon, and Daniel Liss (2).
