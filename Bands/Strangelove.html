Strangelove

Bristol alternative/indie rock band, formed 1991, active 1992–1998. Discogs artist ID: 92568.

Overview

Strangelove formed in Bristol in 1991 around vocalist Patrick Duff and guitarist Julian Pransky-Poole. They released their first two singles on the local Sermon label before signing to Food (an EMI imprint best known for Blur and Jesus Jones), where they released two studio albums and a B-sides/odds-and-ends compilation before disbanding around 1998. Their sound sits in the darker, more melancholic end of mid-1990s Britpop — Patrick Duff’s confessional lyrical style drew regular comparisons to Brett Anderson of Suede.

Their primary production collaborator throughout the Food years was Paul Corkett, who produced, engineered, and mixed the majority of their studio output.

Studio Albums

Time For The Rest Of Your Life (1994)

Label: Food Records. Discogs release ID: 745939 (canonical UK pressing).

The debut album. Produced by Paul Corkett and Strangelove on most tracks; track 2 co-produced with Mike Thorne (with additional production by Corkett); track 9 produced by The Insects (Bob Locke and Tim Norfolk, also known as Startled Insects). Corkett engineered throughout; Donald Ross Skinner contributed organ/Hammond and vibraphone. Bob Locke also played double bass on the record.

Strangelove (1997)

Label: Food Records / Parlophone. Discogs release ID: 915726 (canonical UK pressing).

Third studio album, self-titled. Produced by Paul Corkett and Strangelove. Corkett engineered (credited as “Engineered By, For 140db”); mixing split between Corkett (tracks 1–4, 8, 10, 11) and David Bascombe (tracks 5–7, 9). Mastered by Tim Young. The 140db studio credit is notable — this was Corkett’s own studio setup.

The Sermon label (ID 92880) was a Bristol-based independent that served as Strangelove’s early outlet. Angelo Bruschini (producer on Hysteria Unknown) is the guitarist in The Blue Aeroplanes, making this another direct Bristol scene connection. Donald Ross Skinner (producer on the lead track of Hysteria Unknown and later session musician on Time For The Rest Of Your Life) is known primarily as guitarist for Prolapse.
