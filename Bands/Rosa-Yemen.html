Rosa Yemen

French post-punk / no wave performance art duo. Active 1978–79. Core members: Lizzy Mercier Descloux and D.J. Banes (Didier Esteban). Discogs group ID: 269237.

Overview

Rosa Yemen was a short-lived but historically significant avant-garde project formed in New York City in 1978 by Lizzy Mercier Descloux and Didier Esteban (brother of Michel Esteban, who co-founded ZE Records). The project operated at the intersection of no wave confrontationalism, French conceptualism, and downtown NYC performance art. Their sole self-titled EP — six tracks recorded in 1978 — was released in 1979 on the French label Celluloid Records, engineered by Bob Blank at Blank Tape Studios in NYC.

The group’s name is a wry anagram / portmanteau; its visual identity was handled by Fiction Prod. (artwork) and photographers Lisa Genet (front cover) and Fabio (back cover). Discogs also lists Erik Fitoussi as a member, though his role does not appear in released credits.

The EP predates most of ZE Records’ canonical catalog but exists within the same social orbit: Michel Esteban produced, Bob Blank engineered (he also worked with Aural Exciters, where both Blank and Lizzy were members), and the downtown NYC loft/club scene forms the backdrop.

Scene Connections

  • Lizzy Mercier Descloux went on to a solo career on ZE Records, recording Press Color (1979) and subsequent albums.
  • Bob Blank was the house engineer for much of the ZE Records / downtown NYC scene; his Blank Tape Studios appears across Aural Exciters, James White, and Lizzy’s records.
  • Michel Esteban co-ran ZE Records and Skydog Records (French punk importer), providing the Paris–NYC axis that positioned Rosa Yemen between French post-punk and American no wave.
  • Aural Exciters connects Lizzy and Bob Blank directly; Lizzy was a member.
  • Design credit for Rosa Yemen appears on two unrelated later records in Discogs (credited as “Design” on Gunjogacrayon 2016 and In Focus? 2013) — likely coincidental reuse of the name, not Rosa Yemen the group.

Open Questions

  • What was Erik Fitoussi’s specific role in Rosa Yemen? Partially researched via web (2026-05-24). He was the guitarist in Marie et les Garçons, which recorded Divorce at Blank Tape Studios in early 1979 with Michel Esteban and Bob Blank — the same producer/engineer/studio as Rosa Yemen. Wikipedia’s Marie et les Garçons article states he “played on the debut album by Lizzy Mercier Descloux” (Press Color), but does not name him in connection with Rosa Yemen. The EP was recorded July 1978, before Marie et les Garçons arrived in New York. No primary source (liner notes, interview, review) has been found that names a specific Rosa Yemen role for Fitoussi. Discogs member listing may reflect scene overlap rather than an uncredited EP performance. Gap remains: needs a primary source (liner notes scan, an interview naming him on the EP, or an archived review).
  • The ZE Records UK pressing (Release ID 442764, 1979) is confirmed — see pressing details above. The “ZE Records, 1978” date in older stubs was incorrect; both pressings are 1979.

Notes (web) — Erik Fitoussi research, 2026-05-24

Searched for Fitoussi’s role in Rosa Yemen. Key findings:

  • Erik Fitoussi was the guitarist in Marie et les Garçons (Lyon, formed ~1976), alongside Patrick Vidal (bass), Christian Faye (guitar), and Marie Girard (vocals). He co-wrote most of their material with Vidal. Wikipedia: Marie et les Garçons, as of 2026-05-23
  • Marie et les Garçons travelled to New York in early 1979 to record Divorce at Blank Tape Studios with producer Michel Esteban and engineer Bob Blank — the identical production team and studio used for the Rosa Yemen EP (recorded July 1978) and Press Color. ZE Records site
  • Wikipedia’s Marie et les Garçons article states: “Fitoussi also played on the debut album by Lizzy Mercier Descloux” (i.e. Press Color, 1979). No mention of Rosa Yemen is made. Wikipedia: Marie et les Garçons, as of 2026-05-23
  • The Michel Esteban interview at vol1brooklyn confirms Fitoussi “was from the French band Marie et les Garçons, who were recording their debut album at Blank tape joined, that was the main force” — referring to Press Color sessions, not Rosa Yemen. vol1brooklyn.com, 2015-09-17
  • Rosa Yemen was recorded in July 1978. Marie et les Garçons’ New York sessions were in early 1979. The timeline means Fitoussi was not in NYC during the Rosa Yemen recording.
  • No web source (review, interview, liner notes scan) names Fitoussi as performing on the Rosa Yemen EP itself.

Working hypothesis: Fitoussi’s Discogs group-member listing for Rosa Yemen likely reflects either (a) a data entry error conflating his Press Color contribution with Rosa Yemen, or (b) a very minor uncredited live/performance role in the Rosa Yemen project that postdated the recording. A liner notes scan of the physical Celluloid pressing (CEL 2-6566) would be the cleanest resolution.
