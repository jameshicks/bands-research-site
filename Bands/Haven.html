Overview

Haven were a Manchester indie rock band whose entire recorded output falls within the early 2000s — eleven releases between 2000 and 2004, distributed through Radiate and Virgin. Their sound drew on indie rock, Britpop, and alternative rock, and the band’s catalogue bears an unusually close working relationship with Johnny Marr, who shares credits on a significant portion of their releases. Producer James Spencer was another constant presence throughout their Virgin-era work. A solitary 2007 release on The First Time Records marks their final appearance on Discogs, suggesting a brief reunion or archival issue after the main Virgin run ended.
