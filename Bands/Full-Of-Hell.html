Full Of Hell

Discogs ID: 2408502
Active: 2009–present
Origin: Ocean City, Maryland / Pennsylvania, USA
Genre: Hardcore punk, grindcore, noise, experimental

Hardcore/grindcore band formed 2009, built around vocalist Dylan Walker and guitarist Spencer Hazard. The sound absorbs grindcore blast-beat density, harsh electronics, and noise-as-texture; later records push into death metal and doom territory. Prolific split catalog throughout their early years; collaborations with Merzbow, The Body (3), Nails, Code Orange Kids, and HEALTH (2) anchor their wider reputation.
