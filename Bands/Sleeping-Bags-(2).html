Sleeping Bags (2)

US band. Discogs artist ID: 2123440. Brothers Matt Kivel (ID: 2641324) and Jesse Kivel (ID: 1770585), formed in the early 2010s alongside two additional members: Abe Burns and Mark Nieto. Released a 7” split on Jodie & Victor (2010) and a self-titled LP on Easter Everywhere (2011). The project overlaps almost entirely with Princeton (4), which is also a Kivel brothers vehicle.

Overview

Sleeping Bags (2) is a Los Angeles-adjacent indie/shoegaze band built around the Kivel brothers during a period when both were active in multiple concurrent projects. Jesse Kivel was simultaneously in Kisses (with Zinzi Edmundson) and Princeton (4); Matt Kivel was in Princeton (4) and Gap Dream before pivoting to a solo career around 2013. Sleeping Bags released two records in 2010–2011 and appears to have been dormant since.

The LP Sleeping Bags (2011) was engineered and mixed/mastered entirely in-house by band members Mark Nieto and Abe Burns. Max Markowitz, a recurring collaborator in the broader Kivel orbit (he did artwork on several of Matt Kivel’s solo albums), contributed artwork.
