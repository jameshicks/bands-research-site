Skeleton Crew (2)

American avant-rock / free-improvisation group. Discogs artist ID: 366111. Active 1982–1986, based in New York City. Name variations: S. Crew, Skelton Crew, スケルトン・クルー.

Overview

Skeleton Crew was formed in New York City in 1982 by British guitarist Fred Frith and American cellist Tom Cora. The founding duo was expanded by the addition of Zeena Parkins (harp, keyboards) and Dave Newhouse (of The Muffins); however the core creative and recorded identity of the group was Frith and Cora, with Parkins a full contributing member on The Country Of Blinds (1986) and on subsequent live activity.

The group occupied an unusual space in the early 1980s NYC downtown scene: composed song structures meeting free improvisation, home-made instruments alongside conventional ones, rock energy filtered through avant-garde chamber sensibility. Frith brought guitar, 6-string bass, violin, piano, and Casio; Cora brought cello, bass, accordion, percussion, and “contraptions.” Parkins added organ, electric harp, accordion, and drums on the second LP. Lyrics were often politically pointed.

Their two studio LPs — Learn To Talk (RecRec / Rift, 1984) and The Country Of Blinds (Rift, 1986) — were co-released between the Swiss label RecRec Music and the NYC-based Rift imprint. The group toured Europe extensively during their active years. After dissolving in 1986, their catalog was compiled and reissued on CD by RecRec in 1990 (Learn To Talk / Country Of Blinds, ReCDec 512), and again on ReR Megacorp / Fred Records in 2005.

An unofficial live bootleg, Hamburg, 12.10.82, documents the duo in its early form; additional live recordings appear on compilation and documentary releases.
