Fruit Bats

American indie folk band led by Eric D. Johnson. Discogs artist ID: 226064. Active 2001–2025.

Overview

Chicago-originating band, later relocated to Portland/LA. Thom Monahan is listed as a member in Discogs and produced/engineered Gold Past Life (2019, Merge Records). Top labels: Merge Records, Sub Pop, Jealous Butcher Records.

Connections

  • Thom Monahan — member and producer
  • Merge Records — primary label

Key Early Release

Echolocation (2001, Perishable Records, cat# PER016) — debut album, produced by Brian Deck. Personnel: Eric D. Johnson (all instruments, primary songwriter), Dan Strack (guitar, bass, lap steel, vocals, cello, etc.), John Byce (drums, synthesizer), Jim Becker (fiddle, mandolin), Ben Massarella (percussion), Tim Rutili (slide guitar, vocals, feedback), Gillian Lisee (keyboards, vocals), Charles Kim (pedal steel, bass), Brian Belval (percussion). Mastered by Dan Stout.
