Swell Maps

Discogs ID: 78465
Origin: Solihull / Birmingham, UK
Active: 1972 (formation) / 1977–1980 (recording and live activity)
Genres: Post-punk, DIY, Experimental, Garage Rock
Labels: Rather Records, Rough Trade
Name variations: The Maps, The Swell Maps, Swellmaps, Well Maps

English post-punk and DIY group, formed in Solihull (Birmingham) by brothers Nikki Sudden (Adrian Nicholas Godfrey) and Epic Soundtracks (Kevin Paul Godfrey). One of the earliest and most influential British DIY bands, self-releasing records on their own Rather Records label from 1978. They dissolved in 1980 after two studio albums. A later lineup under Jowe Head (Stephen Bird) performed as Swell Maps C21 before reverting to the Swell Maps name in 2026.

Formation and Early Years (1972–1977)

Discogs places formation as 1972 in Solihull; recording and releasing activity began in 1977–1978. The founding nucleus was the Godfrey brothers — Nikki Sudden (guitar, vocals, primary songwriter) and Epic Soundtracks (drums, percussion, piano, electronics). The lineup was completed with Richard Earl (alias Biggles Books; guitar), David Barrington (alias Phones B. Sportsman; bass/electronics), and John Cockrill (alias Golden Cockrill; guitar) — all adopting pseudonyms. Jowe Head (Stephen Bird; bass, guitar, vocals) was the final core member.

The band were active in the Solihull/Birmingham punk milieu before their first recordings, developing a rough, home-taped aesthetic. Epic Soundtracks was also credited under aliases including Amphibious Landing Craft and Amphibious (2).

Recording and Label Activity (1978–1980)

Rather Records

The band founded Rather Records (ID: 39208) to release their own material. The label’s first release was Steve Treatment’s 5A-Sided 45 (GEAR TWO, 1978); Swell Maps occupied GEAR ONE with their debut single. Rather Records also released material by Cult Figures and The Phones Sportsman Band. Swell Maps releases on the label used the “GEAR” and “TROY” catalog series.

Debut Single: “Read About Seymour” (1978)

Read About Seymour / Full Moon (Rather Records, GEAR ONE, 1978). Core credits: Nikki Sudden (lyrics, music, photography), Epic Soundtracks (drums, artwork), Jowe Head (bass), Phones B. Sportsman (bass/electronics), Dikki Mint and Golden Cockrill as “hangers on.” Engineered and mixed by Mike Kemp.

First Album: A Trip To Marineville (1979)

LabelCat No

Double LP. Engineered by John A. Rivers (credited as “John Rivers”) across most tracks. Songwriting split among all core members. Cover art by Epic Soundtracks, Jowe Head (7” sleeve), and Nikki Sudden (inner sleeve). A sprawling, genre-blurring record mixing punk thrash, tape experiments, and pop.

Dissolution (1980)

The band split in 1980. Nikki Sudden immediately pursued a solo career; Epic Soundtracks pursued both solo work and session drumming; Jowe Head joined Television Personalities; Richard Earl released one record as a solo artist (1981). The Rather Records label issued one further Swell Maps-adjacent release: Nikki Sudden’s solo debut Back To The Start (GEAR 11, 1981).

Labels

Rather Records (Discogs ID: 39208)

The band’s own imprint. Catalog used GEAR and TROY series. Other artists: Steve Treatment, Cult Figures, The Phones Sportsman Band. Label continued into 1981 (Nikki Sudden solo debut). No parent label; independent.

Rough Trade (Discogs ID: 51167)

Distributed and co-released several Swell Maps releases from 1979. Key relationship for the band’s wider UK distribution. See Rough Trade.

Post-Maps Careers

Nikki Sudden

After the split, Nikki Sudden (Adrian Godfrey) pursued a prolific solo and collaborative career from 1981 until his death in 2006. Key label associations: Rather Records (debut Back To The Start, 1981), Abstract Records (1982), Flicknife Records (1983), Glass Records (1984–1988), Creation Records (1986–1990), UFO Records (1991), Glitterhouse Records (Germany, 1996–1999), Alive Records (2000), Secretly Canadian (2002–2006).

Formed Jacobites with Dave Kusworth (1984, Glass Records); the group recorded sporadically through the 1980s–1990s. Solo highlights: Robespierre’s Velvet Basement (1985, Glass), Kiss You Kidnapped Charabanc (1987, Creation), The Jewel Thief (1991, UFO Records), Red Brocade (1999, Glitterhouse), Treasure Island (2004, Secretly Canadian). Final album: The Truth Doesn’t Matter (2006, Secretly Canadian). Died March 26, 2006. A posthumous album, Fred Beethoven (2014, Troubadour), was also released.

Epic Soundtracks

Kevin Godfrey pursued solo work (debut solo 7” Popular Classical, 1981, Rough Trade; Rain, Rain, Rain EP, 1982, Rough Trade) and extensive session drumming through the 1980s–1990s. Session work included These Immortal Souls, Crime & The City Solution, and others. He moved to the US. Key albums: Rise Above (1992, Rough Trade), Sleeping Star (1994, Normal), Debris (1995, Return To Sender), Change My Life (1996, Bar/None), Everything Is Temporary (1999, Innerstate). Also recorded with Nikki Sudden (I Knew Buffalo Bill, 1987, Flicknife). Died November 5, 1997. A posthumous compilation Film Soundtracks appeared in 2017 (Troubadour).

Jowe Head

After the Maps, Jowe Head (Stephen Bird) joined Television Personalities, becoming a long-term musical partner to Daniel Treacy. Solo discography: Pincer Movement (1981, Hedonics Record), Rain, Rain, Rain EP (1982, Rough Trade, shared with Epic Soundtracks), Strawberry Deutsche Mark (1986, Constrictor, Germany), Personal Organizer (1989, Constrictor), and later Widdershins (2019, Easy Action). Later led the continuation project Swell Maps C21 and the revived Swell Maps from 2026.

Richard Earl (Biggles Books)

Released one solo record, The Egg Store Ilk (1981, Pilot Records). Primarily stayed out of the public record thereafter.

David Barrington (Phones B. Sportsman)

Formed The Phones Sportsman Band (released I Really Love You, Rather Records, GEAR NINE, 1980) and later The NoMen and The Independent Piggies (with John Cockrill).

John Cockrill (Golden Cockrill)

Member of The Independent Piggies (with David Barrington).
