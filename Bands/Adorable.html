Adorable

Discogs artist ID: 256674
Formed: Coventry, England, 1991
Disbanded: 1994
Genre: Indie Rock, Shoegaze, Britpop
Label: Creation Records

English indie rock band formed in Coventry out of the ashes of The Candy Thieves. Signed by Alan McGee to Creation Records, they released two studio albums and a run of singles between 1992 and 1994 before dissolving.

Overview

Adorable were a four-piece from Coventry fronted by Polish-born vocalist and guitarist Piotr Fijalkowski (known also as Pete Fij). Signed to Creation Records by Alan McGee, they debuted in 1992 with the single “Homeboy” and followed it quickly with “Sunshine Smile” and “I’ll Be Your Saint” the same year. Their debut album Against Perfection (1993) drew on the shoegaze and indie rock sounds that dominated Creation at the time. The band released a second album, Fake (1994), before breaking up the same year.

Their primary producer for most of the 1992–1993 material was Pat Collier, with Alan Moulder contributing production to “Homeboy” (A-side) and the album track “Homeboy” on Against Perfection. Paul Corkett took over as primary producer for much of the Fake album and the associated 1994 singles.

A posthumous compilation, Footnotes 92–94 (Cherry Red, 2008), collected key tracks from their run.
