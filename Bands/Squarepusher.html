Squarepusher

Performing alias of Tom Jenkinson (born 17 January 1975, Chelmsford, Essex, UK). Electronic musician, bassist, and composer, primarily signed to Warp Records since 1996. Older brother of Andy Jenkinson (aka Ceephax Acid Crew). Jenkinson produces, engineers, and composes all material himself, and performs live bass on many recordings. His work spans drum and bass, acid, jazz-fusion, IDM, and live-band projects through his alter ego Shobaleader One.

Discogs artist ID: 269 (Tom Jenkinson alias). Separate Discogs entry for Tom Jenkinson as an individual: artist ID 268.

Overview

Squarepusher emerged from the mid-1990s UK electronic underground, debuting on Rephlex Records (Richard D. James’s label) with Feed Me Weird Things (1996) — for which James wrote liner notes — before establishing a long primary relationship with Warp Records via the Port Rhombus EP (1996). The characteristic sound of the early period combined high-tempo drum programming with live electric bass improvisation and jazz influences, making Hard Normal Daddy (1997) and Music Is Rotted One Note (1998) defining records of what became known as drill’n’bass or drill and bass. From the early 2000s the work diversified: Go Plastic (2001) moved toward noisier, more aggressive synthesis; Ultravisitor (2004) returned to bass-heavy live performance; Hello Everything (2006) embraced melodic extravagance; Solo Electric Bass 1 (2009) was an unaccompanied bass record. The live-band project Shobaleader One produced the collaborative d’Demonstrator (2010). Later albums Damogen Furies (2015) and Be Up A Hello (2020) explored live hardware synthesis. The most recent release as of 2025 is Stereotype (Warp, 2025), recorded under the alias Stereotype (24).

All music is self-produced. Jenkinson is also credited with artwork/visuals on several releases and co-designed early sleeves alongside The Designers Republic (who created the Burningn’n Tree and Big Loada artwork) and designer Kleber (art on Music Is Rotted One Note, Budakhan Mindphone, Selection Sixteen).

Shobaleader One

Live band formed in the mid-2000s, introduced as a vehicle for performing Squarepusher’s music with a full group. Discogs group ID: 1931427. Members:

  • Tom Jenkinson (bass, vocals) — credited as Squarepusher
  • Strobe Nazard (keyboards)
  • Sten t’Mech (guitar)
  • Arg Nution (guitar)
  • Company Laser (drums) — real name Adam Betts; also records as Colossal Squid

Released Cryptic Motion (Ed Banger Records, France, 2010) and d’Demonstrator (Warp, 2010).

Tom Jenkinson & Friends

Loose collaborative group, Discogs ID 35311. Members listed on Discogs include Tom Jenkinson, Andy Diagram, Andy Jenkinson (Ceephax Acid Crew), Ashley Slater, Ian Cassar, Mick Beck, and Steve Argüelles. Andy Diagram’s involvement has not been independently confirmed beyond the Discogs group listing.
