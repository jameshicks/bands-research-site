To Rococo Rot

Discogs ID: 435
Origin: Berlin, Germany
Active: 1995 – c. 2014
Genres: Post-rock, Electronic, Minimal, Electroacoustic

Berlin trio whose decade-long career traced a careful arc from lo-fi cassette experimentalism through international label affiliations (City Slang, Mute, Domino) to a final orchestral-tinged farewell. Their sound — bass-heavy minimal grooves, dry drum patterns, treated guitar, and electronic texture — became a benchmark of late-1990s German post-rock and continued to evolve through the 2000s without dramatically shifting its aesthetic center of gravity.

The Lippok brothers (born 1966 and close in age) grew up in Berlin; Schneider joined from Düsseldorf, bringing experience from Kreidler’s stripped-down electronic rock. Both Ronald and Stefan simultaneously maintained other active projects throughout the band’s existence.
