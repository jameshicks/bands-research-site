Juventud Juché

Madrid-based Spanish punk/post-punk trio, active since late 2011. The band’s three members — Javier Molina (guitar/vocals), Arturo Hernández (drums), and Luis Fernández (bass) — are the core lineup throughout their catalog. They released their first demo digitally in April 2012 and have been active on the Spanish indie underground, releasing through Sonido Muchacho and allied labels. Their 2016 LP Movimientos, produced by Ian Crause of Disco Inferno, is their most internationally notable release and Crause’s only documented production work outside the UK. Luis Fernández (bass) also plays in Los Punsetes.

Key Personnel (Non-Members)

Bernardo Calvo (ID: 3814827)

Producer on the 2012 EP (uncredited) and Quemadero LP (2013). Full name Bernardo Calvo Fernández; also credited as “Bernardo Tomlover Calvo”. Active across the Spanish indie scene; his Discogs credits include production for Carolina Durante’s self-titled debut (2019, Sonido Muchacho/Universal), Examiga’s Examiga (2018), and La Foto Fantasma’s La Foto Fantasma (2014). Mastered Ian Crause’s The Vertical Axis / The Song of Phaethon (2019, Sonido Costa Verde).

Ojo / Oscar Moreno Gómez (ID: 3025234 / 6006469)

Recorded and mixed the Miedo / En Tu Casa 7” (2015) and recorded Movimientos (2016). Also credited on releases by other artists. Known by the alias Ojo (6) on Discogs; full name Óscar Moreno. Has a Bandcamp at ojomusicorum.bandcamp.com.
