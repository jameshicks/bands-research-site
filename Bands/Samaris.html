Samaris

Icelandic electronic-folk trio from Reykjavík. Discogs artist ID: 2394232.

Overview

Samaris were formed in Reykjavík in January 2011. The trio consisted of singer Jófríður Ákadóttir (vocals), Áslaug Rún Magnúsdóttir (clarinet), and Þórður Kári Steinþórsson (electronics/programming). Their music set early 20th-century Icelandic poetry and folk texts — in particular verses by Steingrímur Thorsteinsson — against spare electronic production, combining processed clarinet with downtempo and dub-influenced rhythms.

They debuted in 2011 with a self-released CDr EP, Hljóma Þú (Not On Label, cat# SAMARIS001), which drew early remix contributions from Icelandic electronic producers Muted (Sigurður Eldjárn) and Futuregrapher. The debut attracted attention on the Icelandic music scene and led to a deal with both 12 Tónar (Iceland) and One Little Indian (UK). Their debut full-length Samaris (2013) was a compilation of the self-released EP tracks plus new material, released in different editions on both labels. The second album Silkidrangar (2014) was recorded with engineer and mixer Friðfinnur ‘Oculus’ Sigurðsson and featured additional clarinet players and a conductor on one track. Black Lights (2016) shifted the sound toward dance-pop and electronic production, with the band self-producing for the first time and mastering by Mandy Parnell.

Jófríður Ákadóttir was simultaneously active in Pascal Pinon (with her twin sister Ásthildur Ákadóttir), releasing Twosomeness on Morr Music in 2013 while Samaris was in full swing. She departed after Black Lights to pursue her solo alias JFDR full-time. Áslaug Rún Magnúsdóttir later relocated to Denmark. Þórður Kári Steinþórsson is also credited on electronic releases under the alias Kosmodod and is a member of Hidden People alongside Kári Guðmundsson.
