Pan Sonic

Discogs ID: 3822
Origin: Finland
Active: 1993–2009 (original run); 2015–2016 (reunion); posthumous releases through 2021
Labels: Sähkö Recordings, Blast First, Mute, Blast First Petite
Name variations: Originally “Panasonic” (1993–1998), renamed due to trademark pressure from Panasonic corporation
Members: Mika Vainio, Ilpo Väisänen, Sami Salo (1993–1996, founding/early member)

Finnish electronic duo known for harsh, minimal, and industrial electronics. Internationally recognised from 1995 onwards following international signing to Blast First (a Mute sub-label). Disbanded 2009; briefly reunited 2015–16 for a film soundtrack. Mika Vainio died 12 April 2017 (on the road, age 53), ending any possibility of further activity. Posthumous releases and archive material continued to appear through 2021.

Biography

Panasonic period (1993–1998)

Mika Vainio and Ilpo Väisänen formed the project in Turku, Finland in 1993, initially with a third member, Sami Salo (alias Hertsi). The trio began releasing on Sähkö Recordings (ID: 985), the experimental techno label run by their friend Tommi Grönlund (Discogs: 99452). Their debut 12” EP — credited as “Panasonic” — appeared on Sähkö in 1994 (catalogue: SÄHKÖ-007), a white-label vinyl of two slow-building, industrial techno pieces (“Muuntaja” / “Murtaja”).

Salo left in 1996 after the Kulma sessions; from that point forward the project was a duo.

In 1995, during a first tour outside Finland, they secured a deal with Blast First (the Mute Records sub-label run by Paul Smith). The debut Blast First release was Vakio (1995, BFFP 118) — a 4-LP box set, engineered by Jari Lehtinen. It received significant international attention. The 1996 album Kulma = Angle (BFFP 132) continued the run, with credits explicitly naming Vainio and Väisänen as composers and Lehtinen as technician.

The Japanese electronics corporation Matsushita (owners of the Panasonic trademark) issued a legal challenge, forcing the name change to “Pan Sonic” in 1998.

Pan Sonic period (1998–2009)

The duo continued on Blast First (now distributed through Mute in various territories) and later on Mute directly.

A (1999) was the first album under the Pan Sonic name, mastered by Denis Blackham, with photography by Finnish artist Jimi Tenor. Aaltopiiri (2000, BFFP166) followed as a vinyl-only double LP on Blast First.

Kesto (234.48:4) (2004, Mute MUTE 9243) was their most ambitious statement: a 4-CD set totalling over four hours of material, its title denoting the exact playing time.

Katodivaihe = Cathodephase (2007, Blast First Petite) and Gravitoni (2010, Blast First Petite PTYT 045) appeared on Blast First Petite, with both albums written entirely by Vainio and Väisänen and mastered by Denis Blackham. Gravitoni ended the Blast First Petite run.

Che (2009, Blast First Petite PTYT 021) was the final release before the initial dissolution: a 10” split with Sunn O))), Alan Vega, and Stephen Burroughs, with Sunn O)))‘s Stephen O’Malley and Greg Anderson (plus Rex Ritter and Joe Preston) contributing to the first track.

The duo disbanded in 2009.

Late releases and posthumous period (2014–2021)

Oksastus (2014, Kvitnu) was recorded for the Ukrainian label Kvitnu, produced by Dmytro Fedorenko (Kotra). It was the last studio album issued during Vainio’s lifetime under the Pan Sonic name.

In 2015, Vainio and Väisänen briefly reunited to compose the soundtrack for the documentary film Atomin Paluu (“Return of the Atom”), released as a Blast First Petite album in 2016.

Mika Vainio died on 12 April 2017.

Posthumous and archival releases continued: Sähkö - The Movie Soundtrack EP (2018, Sähkö Recordings), Remixes (2020, Sähkö Recordings), and Microwaves (2021, Die Schachtel, Italy).

Vainio / Väisänen / Vega (VVV)

A separate group project combining both Pan Sonic members with Alan Vega (of Suicide). Discogs ID: 30938. Releases: Endless (1998, Blast First) and Resurrection River (2005, Mego).
