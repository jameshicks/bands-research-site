Coliseum (2)

Post-hardcore / punk band. Louisville, Kentucky. Discogs artist ID: 279813. Also released as “C.L.S.M.” (alternate name variation used on Infinity Shit, 2023).

Biography

Formation and Debut Era (2003–2006)

Coliseum was founded in November 2003 by guitarist/vocalist Ryan Patterson (ID: 844983), formerly of Black Cross and then-active as label manager of Initial Records. The first lineup assembled around Patterson included Tony Ash (guitar), Keith Bryant (bass), and Matt Jaha (drums). Early releases came out on Patterson’s own Auxiliary Records and on Level Plane Records, a New Jersey post-hardcore label.

The self-titled debut LP (Coliseum, 2004, Level Plane LPR-064) was recorded and mixed by Jason Loewenstein (of Sebadoh), with mastering by Alan Douches, and is co-credited as Written-By to the full band. The record is firmly in the Louisville/Kentucky hardcore tradition: fast, metallic punk with a sludge undercurrent.

In 2004 Coliseum appeared on the Maximum Louisville Split Series Volume I alongside local scene bands, co-released on Auxiliary Records and Initial Records. A Coliseum Demo also circulated on Auxiliary Records the same year.

The Goddamage EP (2005, Manic Ride Records) and the split LP Not Of This World (2005, Level Plane) — a split with Doomriders — extended the early run. Two tracks on Not Of This World are credited as Written-By to Glenn Danzig (tracks 1 and 5), indicating a Misfits/Danzig cover tradition.

The 2006 split/compilation Moral Damage (Destructure Records D014) paired Coliseum with Louisville band Lords (3) and is notable for the personnel: the Coliseum tracks feature the early lineup (Patterson vocals/guitar, Tony Ash guitar, Keith Bryant bass, Matt Jaha drums) with production by Jason Loewenstein and mixing by Evan Patterson (Ryan’s younger brother, of Young Widows — Evan Patterson). The same year brought a split 7” with Young Widows on Relapse Records.

Lineup Shift and No Salvation (2007)

By No Salvation (2007, Reflections Records / Relapse Records), the lineup had shifted significantly: Mike Pascal joined on bass/vocals and Chris Maggio on drums/vocals, replacing Bryant and Jaha. Ryan Patterson remained on vocals/guitar. This album was produced, recorded, and mixed by Kurt Ballou at GodCity Studio — his production is heavier and more metallic than the Loewenstein-produced debut era. Mastered by Nick Zampiello. Drum tech: Alex Garcia-Rivera. The triple-split 7” High On Fire / Coliseum / Baroness (2007, Relapse) placed them in explicitly metal company — High On Fire and Baroness.

Transition to Deathwish and TRL (2009–2010)

The 2009 7” True Quiet / Last Wave (Deathwish) kept the Pascal/Maggio rhythm section and added artwork by John Dyer Baizley of Baroness and recording by Chris Owens. It was their first release on Deathwish (label ID: 74737) and previewed the heavier direction.

Self-released demo recordings as House With A Curse: Demos (2009) circulated online.

The full pivot came with House With A Curse (2010, Temporary Residence Limited, TRR 175), recorded by Kevin Ratterman, mixed by J. Robbins, mastered by Carl Saff. TRL became their primary home through 2013. Written-By credits to Ryan Patterson throughout. The Goddamage EP was reissued by TRL (TRR 186, 2010) alongside the album campaign; a Goddamage (Deluxe Expanded Edition) also appeared (TRR, 2010).

TRL Peak Years (2011–2013)

Carter Wilson joined on bass, replacing Mike Pascal, for the Parasites EP (2011, TRL, TRR 193). The EP documents a transitional lineup: Pascal credited as Performer alongside the new Wilson. Recording split across multiple engineers — J. Robbins, Kevin Ratterman, and Trip Barriger at different sessions. Cover art and illustration by Rick Froberg (Hot Snakes / Drive Like Jehu). Dan Coutant mastered. This EP is the first credit for Coutant, who mastered every Coliseum release through Anxiety’s Kiss.

The split 7” Horror Business / Bullet (2011, TRL/Merge Records, TRR 176) paired them with Superchunk on a Misfits covers (“Horror Business”) and a Buzzcocks cover (“Bullet”), co-released with Merge Records.

The live document Live At The Atlantic: Volume Four (2011, Sound Study Recordings) was recorded at The Atlantic, Toronto, on a bill also featuring Burning Love (2) and Cursed, capturing the band mid-tour.

By Sister Faith (2013, TRL, TRR 219) the lineup was a trio: Patterson, Carter Wilson, and Kayhan Vaziri (bass). Produced, recorded, and mixed by J. Robbins. Mastered by Dan Coutant. Layout and design by Ryan Patterson. A companion Sister Chance EP (2013, No Idea Records) appeared concurrently; UK releases came via Holy Roar Records.

Deathwish Return and Anxiety’s Kiss (2014–2015)

In 2014 the debut album was reissued in a deluxe 2xLP format (Deathwish, DW164) with new artwork by John Dyer Baizley and liner notes by Ryan Patterson. The reissue expanded the tracklist to include split tracks and demos.

Anxiety’s Kiss (2015, Deathwish) was the final studio album, again produced by J. Robbins, mastered by Dan Coutant. Track B2 excerpts lyrics from Edgar Allan Poe’s “The Coliseum” (1833) — a deliberate namecheck. The trio lineup (Patterson, Wilson, Vaziri) was now stable.

Hiatus and Return as C.L.S.M. (2023–2024)

After a long hiatus, the band returned as “C.L.S.M.” (Coliseum, stylized) with Infinity Shit (2023, Equal Vision Records / Auxiliary Records). The album was mixed by Jonah Falco and mastered by Brad Boatright (of From Ashes Rise). Self-recorded: Patterson handled vocals/guitars/bass/synth/sax recording himself; Carter Wilson recorded drums. Lineup remained the same trio (Patterson, Wilson, Vaziri).

In 2024 two more singles appeared self-released on Auxiliary Records: You Might Be Dead and a cover of Pailhead’s “Man Should Surrender,” alongside a 2024 reissue of the debut on Auxiliary Records.

Scene Context

Coliseum occupied a specific Louisville post-hardcore stratum alongside Young Widows (with whom they share Evan Patterson and the Relapse split), The For Carnation, and the broader orbit of Britt Walford and David Pajo-era Louisville experimentation. Ryan Patterson’s dual role as musician and designer gave Coliseum a visual consistency; his design imprint Auxiliary Records / Auxiliary Design (Discogs ID: 370681) handled many releases across the Louisville scene.

The Kurt Ballou production on No Salvation (2007) aligned them with the Deathwish/Converge hardcore metallic orbit. The subsequent pivot to J. Robbins (Jawbox, Burning Airlines) for TRL releases shifted the production aesthetic toward a cleaner, post-punk-informed sound. The final Deathwish era with Anxiety’s Kiss (2015) returned to a heavier register.

The 2011 Superchunk split (Horror Business / Bullet, TRR 176) was an unexpected pairing: both bands covering punk classics (Misfits, Buzzcocks) on a Temporary Residence / Merge co-release.

The 2023 return as “C.L.S.M.” with Jonah Falco (Fucked Up) mixing represents a Canadian hardcore connection largely absent from earlier records.
