Polyorchard

US free improvisation ensemble based in North Carolina. Discogs artist ID: 4466337

Overview

Polyorchard is a fluid-membership free improvisation collective active since at least 2015. The ensemble is convener-led rather than fixed-lineup: a core of David Menestres (double bass, producer, label operator) and Chris Eubank (2) (cello) provides continuity, with Laurent Estoppey (saxophone) appearing across most releases. The group operates its own imprint, Out and Gone Music, which has served as Polyorchard’s primary release vehicle since 2017. All music is credited as spontaneously composed (free improvisation with no pre-written material).

The ensemble’s name reflects its approach: “poly” (many, overlapping) meets “orchard” (an organic, cultivated space for growth) — a loose, branching structure rather than a fixed band.

Discography

All releases on Out and Gone Music except Color Theory In Black And White.

Color Theory In Black And White (2015)

Self-released (Not On Label). Discogs release ID: 6955112. Format unknown from DB.

Lineup: David Menestres (bass), Chris Eubank (2) (cello, tracks 1-1 to 1-4), Laurent Estoppey (tenor sax, alto sax, tracks 2-1 to 2-6), Jeb Bishop (trombone, tracks 2-1 to 2-6), Dan Ruccia (viola, tracks 1-1 to 1-4). Recorded by Dan Lilley. Mastered by Andrew Weathers. Liner notes by Emily Leon. Art by Lincoln Hancock.

Note: Two distinct sub-configurations visible here — a string quartet (cello/viola/bass) on disc 1 and a horn trio (sax/trombone) with bass on disc 2.

Red October (2017)

Out and Gone Music, OG03. Discogs release ID: 10898252 (primary pressing); 15719176 (variant). LP.

Lineup: Laurent Estoppey (saxophone), Jeb Bishop (trombone), Shawn Galvin (percussion), David Menestres (double bass, recorded by, artwork). Mastered by Alex Inglizian. Liner notes by Emily Leon. Music by Polyorchard (tracks A1–A3, B2–B3).

Note: This is the earliest Out and Gone Music release in the DB (catno OG03), suggesting OG01 and OG02 predate this or were non-Polyorchard releases not in Discogs.

Sextet | Quintet (2018)

Out and Gone Music, OG 006. Discogs release ID: 12147290. CD.

Two configurations documented on a single release:

Sextet (tracks 1–3): David Menestres (double bass), Chris Eubank (2) (cello), Jeb Bishop (trombone), Dan Ruccia (viola), Bill McConaghy (trumpet), David Morris (10) (tuba).

Quintet (tracks 4–7): David Menestres, Chris Eubank (2), Jeb Bishop, Dan Ruccia, Jacob Wick (trumpet) — McConaghy and Morris replaced by Wick.

Recorded by Dan Lilley. All music spontaneously composed.

Black Mountain (2019)

Out and Gone Music, OG010. Discogs release ID: 15063190 (primary pressing); 34818104 (variant).

Lineup: Laurent Estoppey (alto sax, baritone sax), Michael Thomas Jackson (clarinet, radio), David Menestres (double bass, objects, binaural recording by, produced by), Dan Ruccia (viola, piano). Cover image by Emily Leon. Mastered by Andrew Weathers.

Note: New member Michael Thomas Jackson (Discogs ID: 596452) joins; Jeb Bishop absent; Menestres produces and records binaural. The “radio” credit for Jackson is unusual — suggests environmental/found-sound integration.

Sommian (2019)

Out and Gone Music, OG011. Discogs release ID: 13755753 (primary pressing); 19809535 (variant).

Lineup: Bob “Crowmeat” Pence (bass clarinet), Charles Phaneuf (clarinet), Michael Thomas Jackson (clarinet), David Menestres (double bass, producer, recorded by), Dan Ruccia (viola).

Note: Three-clarinet front-line with bass and viola. Laurent Estoppey and Jeb Bishop both absent; the ensemble takes a more chamber-like woodwind direction.

Ink (2020)

Out and Gone Music, OG013. Discogs release ID: 15059652.

Lineup: Jeb Bishop (trombone), David Menestres (double bass, recorded by). Liner notes by Emily Leon. Mastered by Andrew Weathers.

Note: The sparsest lineup in the discography — effectively a duo of Bishop and Menestres. Bishop returns after missing Black Mountain and Sommian.

An Excess of Primary Forms (2021)

Out and Gone Music, catno 018. Discogs release ID: 36261007. Digital (FLAC), 10 tracks, ~160 min total. No performer credits listed in Discogs beyond the ensemble name.

Mixed by David Menestres. Mastered by Andrew Weathers. Cover art and mix by David Menestres. Liner notes by Emily Leon. Genres: Electronic, Jazz. Styles: Experimental, Free Improvisation.

Note: No individual performer names attached to this release in Discogs; lineup not reconstructable from DB alone.

Label: Out and Gone Music

Out and Gone Music (Discogs label ID: 1337020) appears to be Polyorchard’s self-operated imprint. Of 10 releases in the DB, 9 are Polyorchard (across pressings/editions); one is Bag Of Tricks Vol 2 (2018, OG07) by James Gilmore (3), a guitarist with Shawn Galvin (percussion) — Galvin also appeared on Polyorchard’s Red October, suggesting a linked musician community. Andrew Weathers masters across both Polyorchard and the Gilmore release.

Scene Connections

Insect And Western Party

Jeb Bishop, Chris Eubank (2), David Menestres, and Dan Ruccia all appear as members of Insect And Western Party, the rotating Eugene Chadbourne-led ensemble. This group also includes Carla Kihlstedt (violin), Ellery Eskelin (saxophone), Brian Ritchie (bass), Gino Robair, and others from the experimental/improv circuit. Polyorchard shares four members with this ensemble, suggesting a networked NC/West Coast improv community centered on Chadbourne’s orbit.

Micro-East Collective

Bill McConaghy and Chris Eubank (2) are both listed as members of Micro-East Collective (Discogs ID: 183413), a collective that appears to share personnel with Polyorchard’s larger rotating cast.

Laurent Estoppey’s European Connections

Laurent Estoppey is Swiss-based and has a parallel career in European improvised music: member of Ensemble Babel, Duo Dilemme, One Plus One (7), and Le Recueil Des Miracles. His 2025 solo album Freedom From The Known is on ezz-thetics. His presence in Polyorchard provides a transatlantic thread.

Jeb Bishop — Chicago / National Scene

Jeb Bishop is one of the most extensively connected members. His discography spans Chicago improv (Tortoise, Stereolab, Jim O’Rourke, Ken Vandermark, Peter Brötzmann Chicago Tentet) through experimental rock (Bonnie “Prince” Billy, The Sea And Cake, Nels Cline) to avant-jazz (Bill Dixon, Exploding Star Orchestra). His own leadership appears on Astral Spirits and Balance Point Acoustics. See Jeb Bishop for full discography.

Andrew Weathers

Mastering engineer across Polyorchard’s catalog (4 of 6 releases) and also appears on Jacob Wick’s Trumpet Trumpet Synthesizer. Based in North Carolina; runs Andrew Weathers Ensemble. His recurring presence is a production constant across the NC experimental scene.

Dan Lilley

Recorded two Polyorchard releases (Color Theory In Black And White, Sextet | Quintet). Discogs ID: 6999300. No other significant credits visible in the DB.
