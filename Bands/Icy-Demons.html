Icy Demons

Discogs ID: 556634 Active: 2004–2023 (confirmed Discogs range; possibly continuing) Origin: Chicago, Illinois, USA Genre: Experimental, avant-garde, jazz-adjacent Labels: Cloud Recordings, Eastern Developments, Obey Your Brain, Polyvinyl Record Company

Icy Demons is an American experimental music project from Chicago, led by multi-instrumentalist and engineer Griffin Rodriguez (also known as Blue Hawaii). The core lineup draws from Chicago’s experimental/post-rock underground, with overlapping membership with Bablicon and a network reaching into post-jazz, chamber pop, and avant-electronics. Active at least from 2004 through the 2023 album Smiles, the group has maintained a small but dense discography across five releases, with an extended cast of collaborators that shifts across each record.

Connections

  • Griffin Rodriguez — co-founder and primary creative force; also Blue Hawaii. Multi-instrumentalist and recording engineer. Also in Bablicon, HiM, Orso.
  • David McDonnell — co-founder; also in Bablicon, Michael Columbia, Dhana Jeera. Credited as “The Diminisher” and “Thousand Rabbits Running” on Icy Demons records.
  • Christopher Powell (Pow Pow) — co-founder; also in Need New Body, Man Man, Lilys, Bent Leg Fatima.
  • Dylan Ryan — drummer; also in Sand (19), Michael Columbia (with McDonnell and Chris Kalis).
  • Bablicon — precursor project sharing Rodriguez and McDonnell; Canterbury/RiO-influenced avant trio 1999–2001.
  • Pit Er Pat — split 7” partner (2005, Polyvinyl).
  • Zach Condon / Beirut — guest on Smiles; Condon and Rodriguez had a long engineering/production relationship.
  • Silvia Bolognesi — Italian jazz bassist; guest on Smiles.
  • Joshua Abrams — Chicago jazz bassist (Natural Information Society); guest on Miami Ice.
  • Jeff Parker — Chicago jazz guitarist (Tortoise); guest on Miami Ice.
  • Heather McIntosh — cellist/composer; guest on Miami Ice.
  • Cloud Recordings — Elephant Six-affiliated Athens label (Jeremy Barnes connection); home for Fight Back! and Smiles.
  • Carlo Cennamo — Chicago multi-instrumentalist; contributes sax to Fight Back! and Miami Ice; also in HiM and Orso with Rodriguez.
  • Michael Columbia — Chicago group sharing McDonnell, Dylan Ryan, and Chris Kalis as members.
