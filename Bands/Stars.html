Stars

Canadian indie pop band. Discogs artist ID: 127607. Based in Montreal, Quebec.

Released on Arts & Crafts Productions (8 releases, 2005–2024). Key album: In Our Bedroom After The War (2007). Known for orchestrated indie pop with male/female vocals (Torquil Campbell and Amy Millan). Amy Millan also released a solo record on A&C (2006). Set Yourself On Fire 20th Anniversary Edition (2024) confirms the long-running A&C relationship.
