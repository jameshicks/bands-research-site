Barrel (3)

Early-to-mid 1990s post-hardcore/post-rock band from Atlanta, Georgia. Discogs artist ID: 2821948. The band’s Discogs profile links to a 2009 blog post documenting their full discography including live recordings (http://beyondfailure.blogspot.com/2009/06/barrel-discography-including-live-at.html), but only one studio LP is currently indexed in Discogs under this artist entry.

Note on prior stub: An earlier version of this page described Barrel (3) as “NYC-based experimental/noise” — this appears to be incorrect. Discogs explicitly identifies them as an Atlanta band, and the member profiles corroborate an Atlanta origin. The NYC/experimental association likely derives from members later relocating (notably David Daniell relocated to NYC and then Chicago), and from the heavy overlap with San Agustin personnel.

Overview

Barrel (3) were a five-piece Atlanta outfit active in the first half of the 1990s. Their sound, as categorized on Discogs, falls under Post Rock and Post-Hardcore. The lineup — David Daniell and Rick Moore (9) on guitar, Andrew Burnes on bass, Scott H. Robbins on drums, and Ali Mahjouri providing vocals and/or guitar — later fed directly into San Agustin (Daniell and Burnes both appear there) and a number of other Atlanta-area and Atlanta-connected projects.

Their sole Discogs-indexed release is Pine And Cotton, Sand (1995), a self-released LP recorded by Geoff Turner and mastered by John Golden.

Connections

  • David Daniell — member; later relocated to NYC then Chicago; central figure in experimental/drone guitar world through Table Of The Elements, Thrill Jockey, and collaborations with Rhys Chatham, Fennesz, and Douglas McCombs
  • San Agustin — post-Barrel experimental guitar trio with Daniell and Burnes (plus Bryan Fielden); Discogs lists one 1997 7” on Old Gold / Samizdat; active 1996–present per Wikipedia
  • Project Qua Project — Daniell/James Elliott duo; no releases indexed in Discogs
  • Apiary (2) — later Daniell quartet with Jason Stein (2), Joseph Clayton Mills, Steven Hess; 2020 release on The Tapeworm
  • Gold Sparkle Band — acoustic avant-garde jazz quartet featuring Andrew Burnes; formed Atlanta, later Brooklyn
  • Rebar (3) — post-Barrel Atlanta project with Burnes, Rick Moore (9), Robbins
  • Old Gold — Atlanta experimental label (43 releases in Discogs) that released the Gold Sparkle Band’s 1994 debut and the San Agustin 1997 7”; likely the label ecosystem around this scene
