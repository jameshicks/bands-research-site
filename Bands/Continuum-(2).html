Overview

Continuum is an ambient/drone duo formed by Steven Wilson and Dirk Serries. Wilson brought his Bass Communion identity — a long-running ambient project running parallel to Porcupine Tree — while Serries brought his Vidna Obmana project, his principal ambient persona across the 1990s and into the 2000s. The project sits squarely in the sustained-tone, slow-evolution zone the two artists already occupied independently, and was conceived explicitly as a meeting of those two practices rather than as a departure from either.

The duo’s structural conceit is the “Construct” — each original album consists of three continuous pieces of roughly equal length (~18–22 minutes each), numbered sequentially across releases. Continuum (2005) contains Constructs I–III; Continuum 2 (2007) contains Constructs IV–VI. The six Constructs therefore form a single large continuous work across the two CDs, which the 2014 triple-LP anthology Continuum I + II makes explicit by collecting all six in sequence across six sides of vinyl.

Alongside the original recordings, the project produced a parallel series of “Recyclings” — vinyl releases on which each original album’s source material is submitted to extended reworking by a single outside collaborator. Volume One gave the Continuum material to Serries (working here in his Vidna Obmana guise) to transform; Volume Two gave the Continuum 2 material to Justin K Broadrick. Both appeared on Tonefloat.

Discography

Original Studio Releases

Continuum (2005, Soleilmoon Recordings, SOL 136 CD)
CD, Limited Edition. Genres: Electronic — Ambient, Drone, Minimal.

Tracklist:

  • Construct I (19:54)
  • Construct II (21:30)
  • Construct III (19:45)

Credits: Bass Communion and Vidna Obmana (as vidnaObmana) — Performer. Photography and design by Lasse Hoile.

The first Continuum release. Wilson and Serries each contributed through their respective project personas rather than under their personal names. Lasse Hoile, the photographer closely associated with Porcupine Tree, handled the visual design.

Continuum 2 (2007, Soleilmoon Recordings, SOL 159 CD)
CD, Limited Edition. Genres: Electronic, Rock — Ambient, Drone, Experimental.

Tracklist:

  • Construct IV (22:45)
  • Construct V (17:29)
  • Construct VI (18:31)

Credits: Dirk Serries — Electric Guitar, Bass Guitar, Drone, Performer [Processing]. Steven Wilson — Electric Guitar, Bass Guitar, Drone, Performer [Processing]. Photography and design by Lasse Hoile.

The sequel and completion of the six-Construct sequence. Unlike the 2005 release, individual member credits are explicit here: both Wilson and Serries played electric guitar, bass guitar, and drone, with both also credited for processing. The instrumentation makes clear that the “ambient” tag conceals a heavily treated guitar-based practice.

Recyclings Series (Vinyl)

The Continuum Recyclings, Volume One (2006, Tonefloat, tf27)
2xLP, Limited Edition. Primary credit: Continuum (2) / Bass Communion & Vidna Obmana.

Four untitled sides, each ~19 minutes. Remix / Recyclings by Vidna Obmana (as vidnaobmana). Graphic design and photography by Carl Glover (4).

The Recyclings concept: the source material of Continuum is handed to Serries under his Vidna Obmana alias for wholesale reworking across four LP sides. The co-credit structure on the sleeve — “Continuum / Bass Communion & Vidna Obmana” — reflects the dual authorship of source and transformation. Note that the catalog number tf27 is later reused on the 2016 promo Construct III (Bass Communion Immersion Mix).

Continuum Recyclings Volume Two (2010, Tonefloat, tf51)
2xLP, Limited Edition. Genres: Electronic, Rock — Ambient, Drone, Experimental.

Four titled sides: One (17:53), Two (18:52), Three (21:51), Four (15:25). Recycled by Justin K Broadrick. Graphic design and photography by Carl Glover (4).

Continuum 2’s source material given to Broadrick (Godflesh, Jesu, Greymachine) for transformation. Broadrick’s involvement connects the project’s ambient wing to his parallel work in heavy music processing and drone.

Anthology and Reissues

Continuum I + II (2014, Tonefloat, TF134)
3xLP, Compilation, Numbered. Genres: Electronic — Ambient, Drone, Experimental, Minimal.
Artwork / Photography by Aleph (10) (i.e., Carl Glover (4)).

All six Constructs collected across six LP sides in sequence:

  • Side A: Construct I (19:54) / Side B: Construct IV (22:45)
  • Side C: Construct II (21:30) / Side D: Construct V (17:28)
  • Side E: Construct III (19:45) / Side F: Construct VI (18:31)

The sequencing — alternating I and II tracks rather than presenting them as two separate albums — frames the six Constructs as a single extended work.

Continuum I / The Continuum Recyclings, Volume I (2016, Tonefloat, TF174)
Box Set, Deluxe Edition, Limited Edition — two CDs (CD reissues of Continuum and Recyclings Vol. 1). Design and photography by Carl Glover (4). Recyclings sides credited to Vidna Obmana (as vidnaObmana) at track level.

Continuum II / The Continuum Recyclings, Volume II (2016, Tonefloat, TF175)
Box Set, Deluxe Edition, Limited Edition — two CDs (CD reissues of Continuum 2 and Recyclings Vol. 2). Design and photography by Carl Glover (4). Recyclings sides credited to Justin K Broadrick (as Justin K. Broadrick) at track level.

Construct III (Bass Communion Immersion Mix) (2016, Tonefloat, tf27 — promo)
CD Promo. A single 60-minute track: Construct III reworked under the Bass Communion Immersion Mix label. Credits: Dirk Serries and Steven Wilson as “Musician.” Appears to be a promotional release tied to the 2016 box set campaign.
