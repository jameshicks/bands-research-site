Biography

Worm Is Green formed in Akranes, Iceland, a small industrial town on the coast outside Reykjavík. The group coalesced around Árni Teitur Ásgeirsson and brothers Bjarni Thor Hannesson and Thorsteinn Hannesson, with Vilberg Rafsteinn Jonsson and later Gudridur Ringsted rounding out the core lineup. The project exists in the orbit of the early-2000s Reykjavík electronic scene that also produced Trabant, múm, and Apparat Organ Quartet, all connected through the TMT Entertainment / Thule Musik label family.

Early Period: TMT Entertainment and Automagic (2003)

Worm Is Green debuted with Automagic (TMT12CD, 2003) on TMT Entertainment, the electronic imprint under the Thule Musik umbrella that also housed Trabant and múm. The album drew on IDM, leftfield electronics, and experimental pop. Production credit was shared across all three founding members; mixing and mastering was handled by Equalizer Axel.

The album’s most notable guest credit is Ragnar Kjartansson — now internationally celebrated as a performance and visual artist — who sang on track 4, “Shine”. Kjartansson was at this time also a member of Trabant (with whom Worm Is Green shared the TMT label), as well as Kanada, Kósý, and The Funerals. A second guest, Birgir Hilmarsson (aka Biggi Hilmars), contributed vocals and synthesizer on track 8, “Outline”.

Around the same period (2002), Worm Is Green contributed a remix to Trabant’s Superman single (TMT07CD, 2002), cementing the two acts’ close connection within the Reykjavík scene.

Push Play and International Moves (2005–2007)

The second album Push Play (DCD025, 2005) was released on Dennis, an Icelandic label, and represented a stylistic expansion into downtempo, electro, trip hop, and synth-pop. No additional personnel are credited on Discogs for this release. The album received a remix 12” two years later — Push Play Remixes (MKX 008, 2007) — issued on the German label Mikrolux. Remixers included Icelandic contemporaries Exos (Laidback Mix), Hermigervill (Re-Assembly), and Bix (Back To Basics Mix), plus Jonfri and John Log. Notably, John Log is an alias for Árni Teitur Ásgeirsson, who thus appears on his own remix record.

Glow and Digital Phase (2009)

Glow (atad003, 2009) marked the band’s move to their own digital imprint ata:digital, founded by Árni Teitur Ásgeirsson. The album brought vocalist Gudridur Ringsted into the credited lineup alongside the original trio. Árni Teitur took on a central production role: programming, synthesizers, vocals, mixing, artwork, concept, and lyrics. Thorsteinn Hannesson contributed drums, Bjarni Thor contributed synthesizer, and mastering was handled by Sigurður Ingvar Thorvaldsson. The Hopeful EP (atad005, 2009) followed the same year with remixes from Ruxpin, Zombie (2), Heartfelt (2), and Hypno (8).

To Them We Are Only Shadows (2014)

The 2014 album To Them We Are Only Shadows (atad011, ata:digital) saw Vilberg Rafsteinn Jonsson contributing bass, with all five core members listed as musicians. Árni Teitur again handled music, artwork, lyrics, and mixing. A companion digital EP, Let’s Just Love My Midi (2014), appeared on the US platform EverythingIsChemical.

ata:empire and Recent Work (2018–present)

Worm Is Green moved to ata:empire (a sublabel of ata:digital) for 2018–2019 singles — Through The Glass (ata015), Close Your Eyes Now, and Please Smile — all credited solely to Árni Teitur Ásgeirsson for music and lyrics. The Vélvild EP (2023) featured collaborations with Vélvild and Huxion. The group has also appeared on five installments of the Icelandic label compilation series Loops, Cuts & Lost Clues (ata:empire, 2016–2024).
