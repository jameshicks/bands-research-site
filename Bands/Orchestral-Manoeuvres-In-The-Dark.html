Orchestral Manoeuvres In The Dark

Merseyside (Wirral) synth-pop duo/group, formed 1978. Discogs artist ID: 9462

Overview

Andy McCluskey and Paul Humphreys-led electronic pop act from the Wirral (Greater Merseyside). Technically from Meols rather than Liverpool proper, but integral to the regional scene. “Bunker Soldiers” (1979) appears on Revolutionary Spirit - The Sound Of Liverpool 1976-1988 (1-12), representing their earliest Zoo Records-adjacent period before they signed to DinDisc. Went on to have sustained international synth-pop chart success (“Enola Gay,” “Electricity,” “So In Love”).
