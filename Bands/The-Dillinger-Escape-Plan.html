The Dillinger Escape Plan

Discogs ID: 372801 Origin: Morris Plains, New Jersey, USA Active: 1997–2017 Genres: Mathcore, experimental metal, hardcore punk

New Jersey mathcore band. Signed to Relapse Records for debut releases; Under The Running Board EP (1998) and Calculating Infinity LP (1999) are their foundational records on the label. 129 total Relapse releases. Final Relapse studio LP: One Of Us Is The Killer (2013). Disbanded 2017.

Key personnel: Greg Puciato (vocals, 2001–2017), Benjamin Weinman (guitar, principal composer), Liam Wilson (2) (bass), Billy Rymer (drums on later records), Chris Pennie (drums on earlier records). Original vocalist: Dimitri Minakakis (departed to focus on graphic arts/design; replaced by Puciato in 2001).

Irony Is A Dead Scene (2002, Epitaph) was co-billed with Mike Patton who co-wrote, co-produced, and performed on the EP. Produced by Weinman, Pennie, and Patton; mixed by Steve Evetts; mastered by Alan Douches.

Miss Machine (2004, Relapse) — Puciato’s first full studio LP with the band.

One Of Us Is The Killer (2013) and Dissociation (2016) both produced and engineered by Steve Evetts; Dissociation additionally mixed by Josh Wilbur and Kurt Ballou; mastered by Alan Douches. Dissociation features additional drums from Zach Hill (track 11) and strings from SEVEN)SUNS.

Post-Relapse: Option Paralysis (2010), One Of Us Is The Killer (2013), Dissociation (2016) all on their own Party Smasher Inc. (co-released with Season Of Mist and Sumerian Records on some editions). Live Infinity (2019) returned to Relapse.
