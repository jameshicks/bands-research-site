Bablicon

Discogs ID: 309256
Origin: Chicago, Illinois, USA
Active: 1996–2002
Labels: Pickled Egg Records, Misra Records
Genre: Avant-fusion, free jazz, post-rock, lo-fi, psychedelia

Multi-instrumental avant-fusion trio formed in Chicago in 1996 by Jeremy Barnes, Griffin Rodriguez, and David McDonnell. All three were multi-instrumentalists, each operating under an alias: Barnes as Marta Tennae, Rodriguez as Blue Hawaii, and McDonnell as The Diminisher. The Discogs profile describes Bablicon’s music as cross-breeding Canterbury prog (Soft Machine, National Health) and RiO (Henry Cow, This Heat) with no-jazz, no-wave, and stoner rock. Wikipedia’s article on the band categorises them under the Elephant Six collective, where their connection ran primarily through Barnes’s prior membership in Neutral Milk Hotel (as drummer, 1996–1998) and his subsequent affiliation with the Athens/Albuquerque E6 circle. The band released four records between 1999 and 2000 and dissolved in 2001–2002 following McDonnell’s departure to study in China and Barnes’s move to France.

Discogs ID: 446527 · Label: Pickled Egg Records (egg 13, UK); Misra Records (001, US) · Format: CD
Country: UK · Master ID: 172564 (4 pressings total)

Full-length debut, 14 tracks, including the title track and “Chunks Of Syrup Amidst Plain Yoghurt” (re-recorded). Label Contexts

Pickled Egg Records (ID: 25555, 110 releases)

UK independent label based in Brighton. Pickled Egg was Bablicon’s primary UK label across all four releases. The label’s roster around 1999–2001 included Volcano The Bear, Pop-Off Tuesday, The Evolution Control Committee, Daniel Johnston, Marshmallow Coast, and Need New Body — placing Bablicon in a broader UK experimental/lo-fi scene. Bablicon toured the UK with Pickled Egg label-mates at the time of In A Different City (Wikipedia).

Misra Records (ID: 41272, 175 releases)

US independent label based in Austin, Texas. Misra handled US distribution of Bablicon’s releases from In A Different City onward and issued The Cat That Was A Dog as their US full-length. Other early Misra roster members included Volcano The Bear, Summer Hymns, The Mendoza Line, Destroyer (4), and later Shearwater and Phosphorescent.

Related Projects and Split-Offs

Per Discogs, Bablicon’s listed related project is Need New Body (Discogs artist ID: 241907), a Philadelphia-based experimental group who also recorded for Pickled Egg Records (Egg 30, Need New Body, 2001; Egg 48, UFO, 2003; Egg 55, Where’s Black Ben?, 2005). Christopher Powell (Pow Pow) of Need New Body later co-founded Icy Demons with Rodriguez and McDonnell, creating a clear personnel bridge between the two projects.
