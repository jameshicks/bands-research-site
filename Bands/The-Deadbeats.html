The Deadbeats

Punk band from Los Angeles, California. Discogs artist ID: 314392. Alias: The Suction Cups (2). Name variations: Dead Beats, Deadbeats.

Overview

The Deadbeats were a first-wave LA punk band active in the late 1970s, best known for “Kill The Hippies” — the song that names Soul Jazz’s compilation Punk 45 Vol. 1 - Kill The Hippies! Kill Yourself!. Their only contemporary release was the four-track 7” Kill The Hippies (Dangerhouse, 1978, cat no. IQ-29), produced by guitarist Geza X (Geza X. Gedeon), who was simultaneously one of the defining producers of the LA punk scene. The single contained “Kill The Hippies,” “Brainless,” “Final Ride,” and “Deadbeat.” Most tracks were written by vocalist Scott Guerin; “Final Ride” and “Mean Mr. Mommy Man” were co-written with Geza X.

The band also appears on the Dangerhouse compilation Yes L.A. (1979) and on the Blank Generation double-LP survey of US/Canadian punk. A live recording from the Whisky a Go Go in 1978 was released by Sympathy For The Record Industry in 1994. In 2025, a new track “Shut Down” appeared on the Artifix Records split EP A Smaller Circle Of Friends alongside the Adolescents and White Flag.

Under the alias The Suction Cups (2), the band issued a single 7” — “F#%k Like A Bunny / G-Spot” (Deep Six Albatross, 2016).
