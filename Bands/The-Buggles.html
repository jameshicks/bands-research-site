The Buggles

Discogs ID: — (search needed)

British synth-pop duo: Trevor Horn (vocals, production) and Geoff Downes (keyboards). Famous for “Video Killed the Radio Star” (1979) — the first video played on MTV (August 1981). Both members joined Yes in 1980 briefly; Horn was Yes vocalist for Drama (1980).

Archival Buggles material appeared on ZTT Records in later years. ZTT holds rights to or distributes some Buggles catalog via Trevor Horn’s control of the label.

Geoff Downes Post-Buggles Career

After the Buggles dissolved, Downes (Discogs ID: 275311) had one of the more prolific post-synth-pop careers in British prog rock. His trajectory:

Yes (1980–1981)

Downes and Trevor Horn both joined Yes in 1980, replacing departing members. Downes played keyboards on Drama (1980, Atlantic) — he co-wrote and played on the album alongside Horn as vocalist. The pairing lasted only one album before the classic Yes lineup reforms pushed them out.

Asia (1982–present)

Downes co-founded Asia (2) in 1981 with John Wetton (bass/vocals), Steve Howe (guitar, ex-Yes), and Carl Palmer (drums, ex-ELP). The “supergroup” was one of the biggest-selling acts of the early 1980s.

Wetton/Downes Collaboration

Downes and John Wetton recorded as a duo: John Wetton/Geoffrey Downes (2001, Stallion Records). Wetton also appears on multiple Asia releases Downes produced.

Downes Braide Association

A project with Chris Braide: Skyscraper Souls (2017, Magical Thinker Records), Halcyon Hymns (2021), Celestial Songs (2023).

Greg Lake’s Ride The Tiger / Rock Aid Armenia

Downes appears on the Smoke On The Water ‘90 charity single (1990, Life-Aid Armenia Records) via Rock Aid Armenia, and contributed to Greg Lake’s Ride The Tiger project.

Yes — Return Appearances

  • Heaven & Earth (2014, Frontiers Records)
