Defunkt

Funk / punk-jazz band. New York City. Founded c. 1979. Led by trombonist Joseph Bowie. Discogs artist ID: 68925.

Defunkt fused free jazz trombone, funk rhythms, and punk aggression — operating in the same downtown NYC milieu as the No Wave scene but with stronger roots in Black avant-garde jazz (via Bowie’s background in the Black Artists Group and the St. Louis loft scene).
