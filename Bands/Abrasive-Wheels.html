Abrasive Wheels

Leeds punk/oi! band formed 1979. Discogs artist ID: 252602. One of the harder-edged acts on the Leeds independent scene, part of the oi!/street punk movement. Released on Riot City Records. On Where Were You- (Independent Music From Leeds 1978-1989) with “Army Song” (CD1, track 22).
