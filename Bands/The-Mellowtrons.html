The Mellowtrons

Solo alias of Lee Walker. Discogs artist ID: 458. UK. Active at least 1996.

Overview

The Mellowtrons is the recording alias of Lee Walker, also a member of Transformer (3). The project released the album Evacuate (1996, Chill Out Label, CHILL CD009) — nine tracks, Electronic / Abstract / Breaks / Leftfield.

Paul Willmott (ex-Disco Inferno, Transformer (3)) co-produced three tracks on Evacuate (tracks 3, 6, 8), documenting an overlap between the Transformer (3) circle and Lee Walker’s solo work.
