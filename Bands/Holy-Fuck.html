Holy Fuck

Discogs ID: 816972 Name variations: Holy F, Holy F***, The Band Origin: Toronto, Canada Active: 2003–present Genre: Electronic rock, noise rock, experimental Labels: Young Turks, Innovative Leisure Records, Last Gang Records, Hand Drawn Dracula, Holy Ef Music

Overview

Toronto-based outfit built around the concept of replicating modern electronic music live using traditional instruments — drums, bass, keyboards, pedals, mixers, toy keyboards, and a 35mm film synchronizer — without laptops or programmed backing tracks. Co-founded by Brian Borcherdt and Graham Walsh.

Core lineup from Latin (2010) onwards: Borcherdt, Walsh, Matt McQuaid, and Matt Schulz. Earlier releases featured a revolving cast including Mike Bigelow, Loel Campbell, Matt Schulz, Glenn Milchem, Kevin Lynn, and Craig James (2).
