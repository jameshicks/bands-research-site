Overview

The Minders are an indie pop band from Denver, Colorado, working across jangle pop, psychedelic rock, and broader indie and pop rock territory. The group — including Hilarie Sidney, Joanna Bolme, Alex Arrowsmith, Jeff Almond, and Joel Burrows — has been active since 1995, with a catalogue concentrated in the 1990s (five releases) and the 2010s (four releases), with a quieter stretch in between. Their label history shows a sustained relationship with the Elephant Six Recording Co. alongside primary homes at Space Cassette and spinART Records. Recurring collaborators include Merisa Bissinger and Rick Benjamin (five shared credits each) and Hutch Harris (three).
