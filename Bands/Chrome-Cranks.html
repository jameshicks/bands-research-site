Chrome Cranks

Discogs ID: 181359
Origin: Formed Cincinnati, Ohio (1988); based in New York City
Active: 1988–c.1998 (original run); reunited 2009, 2012, 2015
Also known as: The Chrome Cranks, The Cranks, Cranks

NYC noise rock / garage punk band. Formed in Cincinnati but relocated to New York, where they became part of the early-1990s underground centered on labels like PCP Entertainment and Crypt Records. Known for raw, blues-infused punk and a revolving cast of downtown NYC collaborators.

Members

The core lineup through most of the 1990s was Peter Aaron (vocals/guitar), William Weber (guitar), Jerry Teel (bass), and a rotating drummer slot (Bob Bert on studio records, Charles Hanson and others live and on some recordings). Several other musicians passed through across the band’s lifespan.

Connections

  • Jerry Teel — bassist; also in Boss Hog and The Honeymoon Killers (2), making him a central node in the NYC underground rock scene
  • Bob Bert — primary studio drummer through the mid-1990s; veteran of Sonic Youth (1982–83) and Pussy Galore (2)
  • Charles Hanson — secondary drummer; founding member of The Normals (2) (New Orleans, 1978), connecting Chrome Cranks to Southern punk history
  • William Weber — guitarist/engineer; also played with GG Allin & The Murder Junkies and Candy Snatchers
  • Peter Aaron — vocalist/guitarist/primary songwriter; real name Peter Wegele
  • Michael Gira — provided artwork for Ain’t No Lies In Blood (2012); frontman of Swans
  • Foetus (JG Thirlwell) — shared a 1994 split 7” (Vice Squad Dick) on PCP Entertainment
  • PCP Entertainment — home label for the bulk of the 1990s catalog; also released Jonathan Fire*Eater
