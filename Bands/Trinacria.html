Trinacria

Discogs ID: 1104103 Active: 2005–present (sporadically) Origin: Norway Genres: Avant-garde metal, experimental, noise, doom metal, black metal Social: myspace.com/trinacriamusic; facebook.com/trinacriamusic

Overview

Trinacria is a Norwegian experimental heavy metal project formed in 2005, uniting members of the progressive/Viking black metal band Enslaved with two leading figures of Norway’s free improvisation and noise scene. The group’s core conceit is a collision of extreme metal — specifically the dense, shape-shifting sound Enslaved had been developing since the early 2000s — with free improvisation, extended electroacoustic technique, and electronics.

The name Trinacria refers to the ancient Greek and Latin name for Sicily (from its three-cornered shape), though the band has not publicly explained the choice. According to Wikipedia, Grutle Kjellson has stated the project was formed in 2005 by himself, Ivar Bjørnson, Arve Isdal, and other musicians.

The lineup brings together Ivar Bjørnson (guitarist and principal songwriter of Enslaved, credited on Discogs under his legal name Ivar Peersen), Grutle Kjellson (bassist and vocalist of Enslaved), Arve Isdal (guitarist of Enslaved, performing here under his alias Ice Dale), Iver Sandøy (drummer and engineer, Enslaved), and bassist Espen Lien (also of Audrey Horne) on the metal side; and Maja S. K. Ratkje (voice, electronics) and Hild Sofie Tafjord (French horn, electronics) representing the experimental wing. Both Ratkje and Tafjord are longstanding collaborators through Spunk and Fe-mail.

Trinacria released their debut album Travel Now Journey Infinitely in 2008 on Indie Recordings, and a digital single “In The Sky” in 2019 on Classroom Records (2).

Note on Ivar Bjørnson / Ivar Peersen: These are two Discogs entries for the same person. Ivar Skontorp Peersen (born 27 November 1977) performs and is widely known under the stage name Ivar Bjørnson. Discogs entry 446847 (“Ivar Bjørnson”) is the primary credited name on Travel Now Journey Infinitely; entry 525276 (“Ivar Peersen”) appears in the Trinacria member list and carries “Ivar Bjørnson” as an alias. Both share the same realname. The stub’s reference to “Ivar Peersen” is correct per the member-list data but his release credit reads “Ivar Bjørnson.”

Labels

Indie Recordings (label ID: 91318)

Norwegian metal and hard rock label, sublabel of Indie Distribution. Release count: 1,108. Home to Enslaved’s Vertebrae (2008) and Axioma Ethica Odini (2010), as well as Satyricon, Wardruna, Kvelertak, Cult Of Luna, Shining (2), and many Norwegian extreme metal acts. Trinacria’s debut was released as INDIE010CD, placed in the same catalog run as Enslaved’s Vertebrae (INDIE023LP). The label is closely tied to the Bergen/Norwegian heavy music scene.

Classroom Records (2) (label ID: 1861215)

Small Italian label (20 releases on Discogs). No parent label. Primarily electronic / dance music — their first release was “Abissi” by Stuffed Squid (2019, CLR001). The Trinacria single (CLR003) appears anomalous given the label’s catalogue. No further context available from Discogs.

Connections

  • Enslaved — source band for Ivar Bjørnson, Grutle Kjellson, Arve Isdal, and Iver Sandøy; one of the most significant Norwegian extreme metal bands (formed 1991 by Bjørnson and Kjellson)
  • Maja S. K. Ratkje — voice, electronics; member of Spunk, Fe-mail, Phantom Orchard; winner of Arne Nordheim Composer’s Prize (2001)
  • Hild Sofie Tafjord — French horn, electronics; member of Spunk, Fe-mail, Zeitkratzer, Lemur (2)
  • Ivar Bjørnson — founding member and principal songwriter of Enslaved; also Borknagar (early albums), BardSpec, Ivar Bjørnson & Einar Selvik (Skuggsjá project)
  • Grutle Kjellson — bassist/vocalist of Enslaved; also guested on Darkthrone (Too Old, Too Cold 2006), Shining (Blackjazz 2010 — “Omen” and “21st Century Schizoid Man”), Vardlokk
  • Arve Isdal — guitarist of Enslaved; also Audrey Horne and I (5); performs as Ice Dale
  • Iver Sandøy — drummer and engineer; Enslaved since at least the Vertebrae era; also Manngard, BardSpec, Oppnedbass; alias Solkongen (2)
  • Espen Lien — bassist; also in Audrey Horne (sharing bandmates with Isdal) and Barbie Bones
  • Jørgen Træen — Bergen-based producer and engineer; extensive credits across Norwegian rock, experimental, and metal (Below The Lights, Enslaved’s Monumension, Jaga Jazzist, Datarock, Kings of Convenience, Röyksopp, and many others); runs Duper Studio in Bergen
  • Indie Recordings — home label for the debut; also released Enslaved’s two albums from the same period
  • Spunk — shared membership through Ratkje and Tafjord; Norwegian free improv quartet on Rune Grammofon
  • Fe-mail — noise duo shared by Ratkje and Tafjord; active c.2000–2006
  • Audrey Horne — shares Arve Isdal and Espen Lien; Bergen hard rock band on Indie Recordings
