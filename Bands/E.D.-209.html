Active: 1988
From: Manchester, England
Genre: Electronic / Acid House

Manchester acid house trio and side project of Martin Moscrop and Donald Johnson, both core members of A Certain Ratio. The third member, Dave Rofe, ran the group’s label and later became a prominent music manager. The name references the robotic enforcement unit from the 1987 film RoboCop.

Metre is the group’s only known release. It appeared on Dun For Money Records, an early Manchester house music label run by Dave Rofe and backed by Rob Gretton — the Joy Division/New Order manager and Factory Records co-founder. The label’s catalog number DFM 003 places it third in the DFM series; the label ran releases from 1988–1990 across six catalog entries, featuring acts including Fifth Of Heaven, Rowetta, and Ashley & Jackson.

ACR / Manchester Connection

E.D.-209 sits at the intersection of Manchester’s post-punk legacy and the late-1980s house music wave. Moscrop and Johnson were already deep into the Factory Records ecosystem through A Certain Ratio; this project placed them in the acid house scene alongside Factory-adjacent infrastructure (Rob Gretton’s backing of DFM Records connects the dots). The Metre 12” has three pressings documented on Discogs, suggesting it circulated beyond a purely private pressing.
