Tones On Tail

UK post-punk / goth / funk band, 1982–1984. Discogs artist ID: 98173

Overview

Tones On Tail began in 1982 as an outlet for Daniel Ash — guitarist and co-founder of Bauhaus — to record more experimental, playful material outside the goth orthodoxy that Bauhaus had come to represent. The first release was a self-titled 12” EP on 4AD (BAD 203, 1982), with Ash joined only by Glenn Campling on bass and keyboards. The duo’s sound blended dub, funk, psychedelia, and minimal synth-pop: a deliberate contrast to Bauhaus’s heavy theatricality.

After Bauhaus dissolved in mid-1983, drummer Kevin Haskins joined as a full member, giving the group a conventional lineup and a more grounded rhythmic base. Their next releases appeared on Situation Two (a Beggars Banquet sublabel) and then on Beggars Banquet itself. The one album, Pop (BEGA 51, 1984), collected their singles alongside new material and stands as the group’s definitive statement: angular guitar, danceable basslines, and Ash’s laconic vocals sitting somewhere between post-punk austerity and outright groove.

The band dissolved following a US tour in late 1984. Ash and Haskins then reunited with Bauhaus bassist David J to form Love And Rockets in 1985. Glenn Campling was not part of that successor group; he later formed Lonestation with Mark Garner.

The name variations “Tones On Tails” and “Tones On Tale” appear on some pressings.

Connections

  • Daniel Ash — co-founder, guitarist, primary songwriter; formed TOT as Bauhaus side project
  • Kevin Haskins — joined 1983; also in Bauhaus, Love And Rockets, Poptone
  • Glenn Campling — co-founder; later formed Lonestation; no solo major-label discography
  • David J — not in TOT, but Bauhaus bassist who joined Ash and Haskins in Love And Rockets after TOT dissolved
  • Bauhaus — parent band; TOT formed during Bauhaus hiatus; reconvened with David J as Love And Rockets
  • Love And Rockets — direct successor; formed 1985 by Ash, Haskins, and David J
  • Poptone — later project (2017–) reuniting Daniel Ash and Kevin Haskins, performing Bauhaus/TOT/Love and Rockets material
  • 4AD — debut label; part of the broader Beggars Group
  • Situation Two — Beggars Banquet sublabel used for early UK releases
  • Natacha Atlas — uncredited model on Pop LP cover (confirmed in Everything! 1998 credits)
  • Caroline Lavelle — cellist on “Performance” single and album track
  • Shake The Foundations (Militant Funk & The Post-Punk Dancefloor 1978-1984) — “Lions” featured on this 2021 Cherry Red compilation
