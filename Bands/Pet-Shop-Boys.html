Pet Shop Boys

British synth-pop duo formed London 1981. Discogs artist ID: 7552

Neil Tennant (vocals) and Chris Lowe (keyboards). One of the best-selling music acts of all time.

Douglas Hart directed the music video for their 2009 single “Did You See Me Coming?” (Astralwerks), from the album Yes.
