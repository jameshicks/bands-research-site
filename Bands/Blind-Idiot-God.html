Blind Idiot God

Instrumental noise-rock / avant-metal trio, formed circa 1982–85 in St. Louis, Missouri, later relocated to Brooklyn, NY. Discogs artist ID: 170038. Name variation: B.I.G.

The band name derives from H. P. Lovecraft’s fiction, where the god Azathoth is described as the “blind idiot god.”

Overview

Blind Idiot God coalesced in St. Louis around guitarist Andy Hawkins, bassist Gabe Katz, and drummer Ted Epstein (Discogs credits place initial formation in 1982; the band’s Discogs profile says mid-1980s). The three moved to Brooklyn after signing to SST Records, secured by Greg Ginn of Black Flag after Ginn heard a self-produced cassette demo circulating in the St. Louis scene. That demo-era cassette is catalogued on Discogs as an untitled 1983 self-release on Not On Label.

Their music fused heavy metal intensity with dub textures, free-improv openness, and compositional influences including György Ligeti and Igor Stravinsky, while also covering The Meters and drawing on hardcore punk energy. The debut was self-produced alongside engineer/co-producer Martin Bisi at BC Studio in Brooklyn. Bill Laswell took the producer chair for the subsequent Enemy Records releases.

After Cyclotron (1992) Epstein departed. The band appeared on Laswell’s Praxis album Sacrifist (1993) as performers, then Hawkins continued under the alias Azonic through the mid-1990s before a BIG reunion in the early 2000s, with Tim Wyskida (of Khanate) replacing Epstein on drums. A second bassist listed on Discogs is Will Dahl, associated with the band’s later activity. The reunion resulted in the double LP Before Ever After (2015) on Hawkins’s own label, Indivisible Music.
