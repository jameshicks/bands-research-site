Grouper

Oct 07, 20264 min read

Grouper

Discogs ID: 688281
Real name: Liz Harris (Elizabeth Anne Harris)
Born: July 15, 1980, West Marin, California
Aliases: Liz Harris (Discogs ID: 543986), Nivhek
Labels: Free Porcupine Society → Type Records → Yellow Electric (own) → Kranky
Active: 2005–present

Solo project of Liz Harris. Ambient, drone, folk, and shoegaze music built on layered guitar and piano, heavy reverb, tape saturation, and vocals buried deep in the mix. One of the most consistently acclaimed artists in experimental American folk/ambient across the 2010s. Also runs Yellow Electric, her own label.

Other active projects: Mirrorring (with Jesy Fortino / Tiny Vipers), Raum (with Jefre Cantu-Ledesma), Slow Walkers (with Lawrence English), Badgerlore, Flash Lights, Helen (36), Visitor (9).

Biography

Origins (West Marin, Portland, 2005)

Born July 15, 1980 in West Marin, California. Early recordings released initially as CDRs and cassettes under the Grouper name from 2005. First commercial release: Way Their Crept (2005, Free Porcupine Society, FPS014), co-mastered by Harris and Pete Swanson. Cover photography throughout the early period by Peter Harris (11).

Breakthrough: Dragging A Dead Deer Up A Hill (2008)

Dragging A Dead Deer Up A Hill (Type Records, TYPE038, 2008; UK press) — the record that established Grouper’s reputation beyond the Pacific Northwest experimental underground. 12 tracks; all songs by Liz Harris; mastered by Timothy Stollenwerk (Stereophonic Mastering). Genres on Discogs: Electronic / Folk, World & Country / Rock; styles: Ambient, Drone, Ethereal, Experimental, Folk Rock, Shoegaze.

Tracklist: “Disengaged,” “Heavy Water/I’d Rather Be Sleeping,” “Stuck,” “When We Fall,” “Traveling Through A Sea,” “Fishing Bird (Empty Gutted In The Evening Breeze),” “Invisible,” “I’m Dragging A Dead Deer Up A Hill,” “A Cover Over,” “Wind And Snow,” “Tidal Wave,” “We’ve All Gone To Sleep.”
