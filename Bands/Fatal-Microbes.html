Fatal Microbes

British anarcho-punk band formed in 1978 in the orbit of Brighton’s Poison Girls / Crass scene. Active 1978–1979; revived for a 2023 Record Store Day reissue. Discogs artist ID: 305750. Name variation: ‘Fatal’ Microbes.

Overview

Fatal Microbes were a short-lived but historically significant anarcho-punk outfit built around Honey Bane (born Donna Tracy Boylan, 4 November 1963), who co-founded the group at age 14. The band’s direct family connection to Poison Girls was structural: Gem Stone (drums) and Pete Fender (guitar, vocals) were the daughter and son of Poison Girls singer Vi Subversa (Frances Sokolov Sansom). This made Fatal Microbes an extension of the same Brighton anarcho-punk household, not merely a parallel act.

The band released a split 12” with Poison Girls in 1979 (“Closed Shop / Violence Grows”, cat. Weeny 3) co-released on Xntrix Records and Small Wonder Records, and a solo 7” EP (“Violence Grows”, Small Wonder cat. “small twenty”) the same year. Both releases were produced by Richard Famous (Richard Swales), Poison Girls guitarist. The 7” was also co-produced by Xntrix (2), a production entity connected to the Poison Girls camp.

The split 12” is a direct document of the Brighton anarcho scene: Side A is Poison Girls material (“Closed Shop”, “Piano Lessons”) with their own personnel, while Side B is Fatal Microbes (“Violence Grows”, “Beautiful Pictures”) with Honey Bane on vocals, Pete Fender on guitar and vocals, Scott Barker on bass and vocals, and Gem Stone on drums. Songwriting on the Fatal Microbes tracks was credited to Bane / Fender / Barker.

After the band dissolved circa 1979, Honey Bane began a collaboration with Crass (recorded as Donna and The Kebabs, released on You Can Be You Records) before being signed to EMI/Zonophone via Sham 69’s Jimmy Pursey, scoring a UK Top 30 single in 1981 (“Turn Me On Turn Me Off”). Gem Stone and Pete Fender (as Dan Sansom) went on to Rubella Ballet, with Dan Sansom later also joining Omega Tribe and working as producer/engineer at Magneto Sound Studios.

In 2023, the “Violence Grows” EP was reissued on 12” vinyl for Record Store Day by You Can Be You Records (cat. YCYX002), credited jointly to Honey Bane and Fatal Microbes. This release added a previously unreleased track, “Violence Grew”.
