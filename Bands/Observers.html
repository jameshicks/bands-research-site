Overview

Observers were a five-piece post-punk/new wave band from Leicester, active around 1980–1981. They are documented in Discogs via two releases: a self-released 7” single on the locally-run The S+T Label and a track on the Leicester scene compilation Where The Hell Is… Leicester. Both releases place them squarely within the city’s small but active independent punk and new wave underground of that era.

The band combined guitar, saxophone, and vocals in a lineup that does not map onto a conventional post-punk template — the saxophone presence (credited to Paul Harding (7) alongside dual-vocal duties) gives the group a slightly more textured sound than a standard three-guitar arrangement. Both known compositions are credited to the Observers collectively.

Their bass player, Steve Pearce, subsequently became one of London’s most active session bassists across three decades of major-label work. Guitarist and vocalist Geoff Sapsford went on to record for Stiff Records and Polydor in the mid-to-late 1980s, and a 2024 Jazz In Britain credit confirms ongoing activity. The other three members leave no further trace in Discogs.

ST 2 — This Age / Suicide (1980)

Discogs ID: 2357180 | Cat: ST 2 | Format: 7” vinyl | Styles: New Wave, Punk

A-side: “This Age” / B-side: “Suicide”. Both tracks written by the Observers (collective credit).

Engineered by Simon Jones (4). Lacquer cut by Porky (5) (George Peckham) — his characteristic “Porky Prime Cut” etchings appear in both runout grooves, with the messages “IN ANY AGE A PORKY PRIME CUT. (ALI)” (side A) and “IT’S A KILLER. A PORKY PRIME CUT. (ALI)” (side B).

Matrix identifiers: ST 2-A (side A label), ST 2-B (side B label).

The S+T Label Context

The S+T Label was a Leicester independent label run by Chris Freer, Kev Reverb (Kevin Bayliss), and associates. It operated on a self-financing model: bands paid for recording; the label covered pressing and production, with profits split 50/50. The Observers’ ST 2 was the second release in the catalog, following the The Amber Squad’s ST 1 (1980) and preceding the Where The Hell Is…Leicester? compilation LP (STAB 1, 1981) and Future Toys’ ST3 (1981).

The free-single packaging of ST 2 with STAB 1 copies (at least a subset) makes the two releases closely linked in terms of physical distribution.

Where The Hell Is…Leicester? Context

The Observers contributed “Crisis” at position B4 on the twelve-band LP Where The Hell Is… Leicester (STAB 1, The S+T Label, 1981). The track is credited to the Observers collectively — no individual credits appear on the compilation. Other bands on the LP include The Deep Freeze Mice (the compilation’s most prolific act), The Amber Squad, Future Toys, and New Age (5).
