Melt-Banana

Tokyo noise-punk duo, formed c. 1991/1992. Discogs artist ID: 190371

Overview

Melt-Banana are one of Japan’s most internationally visible avant-noise bands. Their sound combines hyper-compressed grindcore tempos, Yasuko Onuki’s high-pitched processed vocals, Ichiro Agata’s heavily distorted guitar, and tape-splice song structures that rarely top the one-minute mark. The band emerged from Tokyo’s underground scene in the early 1990s and built a substantial US and European following through relentless touring and records on Skin Graft, Tzadik, and their own A-Zap imprint. By the 2010s they had streamlined to a duo, touring and recording as Yasuko (vocals) and Agata (guitar + programming), without live bass and drums.

Name variations on record: Melt Banana, Melt-Banana Lite, Meltbanana.

Biography

Formation and early recordings (1991–1994)

The band formed in Tokyo around 1991/1992, initially as a quartet. Their self-titled cassette debut appeared in 1993 on Iguana Coax (Japan). The UK label Chocolate Monk released Cactuses Come In The Flocks in 1994 — a collection of short tracks that introduced the band outside Japan. The US debut Speak Squeak Creak (Nux Organization, 1994) put them before the American underground audience. Also in 1994 came the split 7” with God Is My Co-Pilot on HG Fact (cat# HG-024), one of their first Japanese label releases.

Skin Graft era (1995–1997)

The mini-album It’s In The Pillcase (Nux Organization / Skin Graft Records, 1995), with K.K. Null engineering and mixing, was their breakthrough in US underground circles. The full-length Scratch Or Stitch (Meldac, 1995) is the most fully produced early record — Steve Albini recorded it and Jim O’Rourke mixed it, with the four-piece lineup of Agata, Rika Mm’, Sudoh Toshiaki, and Yasuko. Eleventh followed on Slap A Ham Records (1997).

Charlie / Tzadik period (1998–2000)

Charlie (A-Zap Records, 1998) was self-produced. HG Fact issued Dead Spex the same year. In 1999 John Zorn’s Tzadik released MxBx 1998/13000 Miles At Light Velocity, drawn from a series of New York City shows; John Zorn served as executive producer, with Robert Musso engineering and Ikue Mori doing sleeve design. Teeny Shiny (A-Zap, 2000) continued the self-released run.

Cell-Scape and splits era (2001–2007)

Cell-Scape (A-Zap, 2003) marked a consolidation of the group’s sound. During this period the band issued numerous splits: with Discordance Axis (HG Fact, 1995); Three Studies For A Crucifixion (Passacaglia Records, 2001); The Locust on Gold Standard Laboratories (2002); Big D And The Kids Table (Fork In Hand Records, 2002); Narcosis (Speedowax / SuperFi, 2003); Fantômas (Unhip Records, Italy, 2005). The 4-way compilation Erase Yer Head #9 (Pandemonium Records, 1999) included them alongside God Is My Co-Pilot and others.

Bambi’s Dilemma (A-Zap, 2007) was the last full-length with the four-piece band.

Duo era (2009–present)

Initial T. (Init Records, 2009), with art by Aaron Horkey and mastering by Carl Saff, launched the duo configuration. By this point Agata handled all instrumentation with programmed percussion. The split 12” with Young Widows (Temporary Residence Limited, 2009) brought them into the post-metal underground. Fetch (A-Zap, 2013) was their first full-length in six years. A split with Napalm Death appeared on Ipecac Recordings (2016). The most recent record in the DB is 3+5 (A-Zap, 2024).

The two singles compilations 13 Hedgehogs (MxBx Singles 1994–1999) (A-Zap, 2005) and Return of 13 Hedgehogs (MxBx Singles 2000–2009) (A-Zap, 2015) collect their out-of-print 7” catalog.

Both Yasuko and Agata also appear in the side project Onsen-Banana (Discogs: 698768).
