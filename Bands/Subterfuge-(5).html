Subterfuge (5)

Discogs ID: 362196 Origin: Düsseldorf, Germany Genre: Indie pop Active: 1991–present Websites: http://subterfuge.de/ · Twitter @Subterfuge91 · facebook.com/idobirds · instagram.com/sbtfg/

Indie pop band from Düsseldorf, Germany, active since at least 1991. The band runs its own label imprint, I Do Birds Records (Discogs ID: 1071372), named after their 2001 LP; as of the Discogs entry the label has issued two catalog releases: the 2016 reissues of The Legendary Eifel Tapes (IDB 001) and Marc (IDB 002). Core songwriters throughout the band’s history are Thomas Baumhoff and Lars Christian Schmidt, each handling guitar, bass, vocals, and lead writing credits across all eras. Their longest-running external production relationship is with O.L.A.F. Opal, who co-produced tracks on both I Do Birds (2001) and The Legendary Eifel Tapes (2005).

Open Questions

  • The Discogs discography has gaps: no Discogs entry for any release between Subterfuge / Soap (1997) and I Do Birds (2001). Were there singles or EPs during this period not entered in Discogs?
  • Marc appears as both a 1996 and 2001 release on Langstrumpf Records (same master). Which is the actual first release date?
  • Michael Krannich is listed as a Subterfuge (5) member in Discogs but appears only on the Soap (3) side of the 1997 split. Was he ever actually a Subterfuge member, or is this a Discogs data error?
  • The And Again cassette (1992) is listed under “Not On Label” with 14 pressings — the high pressing count is unusual for a self-released cassette EP. Is this a Discogs artifact (duplicate entries)?
  • O.L.A.F. Opal’s connection to Düsseldorf and to the band’s scene is undocumented in Discogs beyond the production credits.
  • The gap between The Legendary Eifel Tapes (2005) and the 2021 singles is at least partially explained by Schmidt’s Quent (2) project (active from 2006) and The Radio Field (from c. 2020), but whether there was any Subterfuge output 2005–2021 not on Discogs is unclear.
  • The 2021 singles (“The Snake Wife,” “The Good Good”) may be digital-only pre-releases leading to Dots. — format not confirmed in Discogs data.
  • Lorenz Naumann’s role as producer on Dots. and other recent material — is he a current full member, or a collaborating producer?
  • Instagram handle @sbtfg suggests ongoing activity; current lineup composition unclear from Discogs alone.
