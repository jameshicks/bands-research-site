Expo ‘70

Discogs ID: 1026268 Also known as: Expo Seventy (live/band configuration) Active: 2003–present Base: Kansas City, Missouri (originally began while based in Los Angeles) Genre: Drone, Ambient, Psychedelic Rock, Space Rock, Experimental

Overview

Expo ‘70 is the long-running experimental project of Justin Wright, a Kansas City-based musician and visual artist who also operates the Sonic Meditations label. Wright began recording as Expo ‘70 around 2003 while living in Los Angeles, and has continued the project since relocating back to the Kansas City metro area.

At its core, Expo ‘70 is Wright’s solo vehicle — guitar and synthesizer-centered drone/ambient music built on improvisation, deep-space tonality, and kosmische influences. Over the years it has expanded into a loose collective configuration involving collaborators such as McKinley Jones (Moog synthesizer), Matt Hill (6) (bass, Korg synth), Aaron Osborne (bass, synth), and Mike Vera (2) (drums). The live/touring version of the project has operated under the name Expo Seventy and has included rotating rhythm section players.

Wright has released prolifically across small international labels (cassette, CDr, vinyl), making Expo ‘70 a representative example of the 2000s–2010s underground drone/psych tape economy. The Discogs catalog spans approximately 40 unique releases (primary credits, master-collapsed) from 2003 through 2021.

Biography

Origins (2003–2008)

Wright’s first documented release as Expo ‘70 was a split CDr with SXBRS (the Peter Kris / Paul Kneejie alias) on Kill Shaman in 2003 (cat# KSR5). Kill Shaman, a US experimental label, would remain the primary home for Expo ‘70’s earliest output through the mid-2000s.

The 2006 CDr Center Of The Earth (KSR16) is one of the earliest fully solo Expo ‘70 statements — four untitled tracks running over 50 minutes, recorded and mixed by Wright with mastering by Pete Lyman. The album established the project’s core aesthetic: long-form, heavily processed guitar drone with no conventional song structure.

The 2006 cassette Exquisite Lust (Kill Shaman, KSR15) and 2007 Mystical Amplification (Kill Shaman) continued building out the catalog in limited editions on CDr and cassette. A 2008 split LP with Rahdunes (Kill Shaman KSR27) — featuring Aaron Coyes and Indra Dunis of Rahdunes — marked one of the first significant collaborative vinyl releases.

Expansion and Tape Circuit Peak (2009–2011)

2009 was the project’s breakout year in terms of volume and international reach. Multiple releases appeared across different countries and labels within a single calendar year:

  • Corridors To Infinity (cassette, Sonic Meditations SM003, US) — featuring McKinley Jones on Moog and Matt Hill (6) on organ; arguably the first fully realized LP-equivalent statement of the collective format
  • Sonic Messenger (2xCD, Beta-lactam Ring Records, US) — the largest-scale release to date, a double album with a full group lineup: Wright on guitar/Roland synth, Matt Hill (6) on bass/Korg/acoustic guitar, J. Ashley Miller on synth/voice/singing bowls/auxiliary percussion, David Williams (27) on singing bowls/gong/percussion; engineered and mixed by J. Ashley Miller
  • Galaxy Of Mysticism (Reverb Worship, UK)
  • Woolgather Visions (Gold Soundz, Norway)
  • Psychic Funeral (Ruralfaune, France)
  • Psychosis (Peasant Magik, US)
  • Awakening (Sloow Tapes, Belgium)
  • Night Flights (Fedora Corpse, US)

This period demonstrated Expo ‘70’s centrality to an international network of micro-labels operating in the cassette/CDr underground, spanning the US, UK, Europe, and Scandinavia.

The 2011 cassette Blackout (Sonic Meditations SM011) documented live performances in Ithaca and Manhattan from October 2009, with Wright on guitar and Realistic Moog alongside Matt Hill (6) on bass/Korg/analog drum machine.

Maturing Sound: Vinyl Era (2012–2015)

Virtually From The Unknown (2012, Sonic Meditations SM045) represents the solidified trio format: Wright (guitar, synth), Aaron Osborne (bass, synth, cymbal), Mike Vera (2) (drums, gong, bowed cymbal). Two extended tracks across ~38 minutes.

Beguiled Entropy (2012, Blackest Rainbow, UK, vinyl LP BRR243) brought the project to a British vinyl audience with five tracks featuring Wright solo (guitar, analog drum machine, synth), recorded by David Moore (5) and mastered by James Plotkin.

Filer À L’Anglaise (2013, Deep Distance, UK) and Closet Full Of Candles (2013, Sonic Meditations) continued the steady cadence.

Solar Drifting (2015, Zoharum, Poland) reached a Polish audience through that label’s limited edition vinyl series. Cleverly Mystique (2015, 5nakefork) and Kinetic Tones (2015, Oaken Palace Records, UK) rounded out a busy mid-decade period.

Essence Music Era and Band Configuration (2016–2021)

America Here & Now Sessions (2016, Essence Music, Brazil, LP ESSWAX02) — credited under the Expo Seventy moniker — marks the full articulation of the live band format. Personnel: Wright (guitar, synth), Aaron Osborne (bass, percussion, cymbal), Mike Vera (2) and David Williams (27) (percussion), J. Ashley Miller (mix, recording), Jon Kraft (recording). Mastered by James Plotkin. This Essence Music relationship continued with Evolution (2021, ESS030, CD), which added Chris Fugitt (drums) and Chad Meise (recording/mixing), and for the first time listed Scott Colburn as mastering engineer.

Mother Universe Has Birthed Her Last Cosmos (2018, Zoharum, Poland; also Sonic Meditations) is a compilation/retrospective release.

Mystic Caravan (2021, Sonic Meditations SM077, vinyl LP) — minimal credits listed; appears to be a return to the band configuration under Expo Seventy styling on the sleeve.
