Overview

Django Django are a London indie rock band formed in 2009, built around drummer-producer Dave Maclean, vocalist-guitarist Vincent Neff, bassist Jimmy Dixon, and synth player Tommy Grace. Their sound blends indie rock with synth-pop, electro, and downtempo textures, with the bulk of their catalogue — twenty releases across the 2010s — issued on Because Music, distributed via Ribbon Music in North America. Mastering duties across the catalogue have consistently gone to Matt Colton, Mandy Parnell, and Marta Salogni, while designer Alexis Smith (5) is the dominant non-musician credit. By 2024 the band had shifted to Another Cheap Thrill for new releases, ending a twelve-year run with Because Music.
