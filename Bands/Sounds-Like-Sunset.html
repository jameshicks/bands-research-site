Overview

Sounds Like Sunset are an Australian shoegaze and dream pop group. Their catalogue falls into two distinct eras — a late-1990s/early-2000s run across small labels including Quietly Suburban Recordings and Modular Recordings, and a return to activity in the 2010s through Tym Records and Simon Tahiti Records. The gap between Invisible (2005) and We Could Leave Tonight (2014) was not a formal breakup: the band continued playing shows and recording intermittently, with the extended gap attributed to members marrying and having children, the departure of original guitarist Andrew Fevre before recording sessions in 2009, and years of backlogged material being gradually worked through (per a 2014 David Challinor interview, happymag.tv). Producer Wayne Connolly appears across three of their releases and is by far their most consistent outside collaborator; they also share credits with fellow Australian acts Iowa (2) and The Lassie Foundation, placing them firmly within that country’s indie and shoegaze community.

Notes (web)

The 2005–2013 gap: no formal breakup

According to a 2014 interview with David Challinor published by Tomatrax (via happymag.tv, https://happymag.tv/interview-with-sounds-like-sunset/), Sounds Like Sunset did not formally break up between Invisible (2005) and We Could Leave Tonight (2014). Challinor described the band as simply inactive on-and-off rather than dissolved: “We’ve been pretty busy. Just doing heaps of shows on & off, recording on & off when we can or when we feel like something’s worthy of being committed to tape or whatever.” He also noted the band had not felt the gap keenly: “It doesn’t really feel like it’s been that long though.”

The primary factors behind the extended gap were personal life changes — “Most of us also ended getting married & having kids too!” — and the departure of original guitarist Andrew Fevre (“Beef”) just before the recording sessions began in 2009. Challinor described this directly: “One thing is this was all done as a 3-piece, ‘cos our good mate & original guitar guy Beef…decided to leave the group just before we started the actual recording part with Wayne in 2009.” (Wayne is Wayne Connolly, their long-standing producer.) A new guitarist, Ben, subsequently joined, returning the band to a four-piece.

Material for We Could Leave Tonight accumulated over many years; Challinor noted that at least one track pre-dated Invisible itself: “One song on the record is actually from before our last album (2005’s Invisible) was recorded, so a lot of these songs have been on the backburner for ages.” The 2014 release was therefore more a gradual finalisation of banked material than a deliberate comeback campaign.
