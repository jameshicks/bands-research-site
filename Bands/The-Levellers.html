Overview

The Levellers are an English folk rock band founded in Brighton in 1988, known for weaving fiddle-led folk instrumentation with alternative rock energy and politically charged songwriting. The core lineup stabilised from 1991 when Simon Friend joined, following the departure of founding member Alan Miles; a relatively stable roster then carried the band across four decades and over 40 releases. Their commercial peak came in the 1990s under China Records (28 works), while their own On The Fiddle Recordings imprint, running since 1991 and still active in 2025, anchored their independent output. Production relationships with Markus Dravs and Jon Kelly shaped the major-label era, and Sean Lakeman’s 22 shared credits reflect deeper ties to the English folk revival circuit.
