Overview

Portastatic originated as a solo vehicle for Mac McCaughan — lead singer and guitarist of Superchunk — based in Chapel Hill, NC, and gradually expanded into a full band that at times included Superchunk’s Jim Wilbur and McCaughan’s brother Matt. The catalogue concentrates in the 1990s and 2000s, spanning indie rock and lo-fi territory with occasional acoustic and avant-garde detours, amounting to roughly a dozen releases. Merge Records has been the consistent home throughout, from the 1993 debut to a 2014 appearance, with scattered releases on smaller US and European imprints alongside. Recurring studio collaborators include producer John Plymale, Zeno Gill, and Annie Hayden, while Peter Holsapple and Laura Cantrell also appear across multiple credits.
