Washed Out

Discogs ID: 1575877 Real name: Ernest Weatherly Greene Jr. Also listed as: Ernest Greene (Discogs ID 1575878) Origin: Georgia, USA Active: 2009–present Genres: Chillwave, lo-fi electronic, synth-pop

Ernest Greene’s chillwave/electronic project. One of the defining acts of the chillwave moment (~2009–2010), Washed Out built a substantial discography across Mexican Summer, Sub Pop, Weird World, and Stones Throw. Greene operates as primary songwriter/producer with a consistent stable of collaborators, most centrally producer Ben H. Allen.

Career Overview

2009 — Origins and Chillwave Emergence

Washed Out emerged from Georgia in summer 2009 with High Times, a cassette on Mirror Universe Tapes (8 pressings). The Life Of Leisure EP followed that same year on Mexican Summer (MEX023) — a digital-only MP3 release. “Feel It All Around” became the de facto signature track of the chillwave genre as it was named by bloggers. The EP’s hazy, bedroom-recorded aesthetic — faded synths, slowed samples, reverbed vocals — defined a micro-genre moment.

2011 — Major Statement: Within And Without

Within And Without (Weird World WEIRD003LP, 2011) is Washed Out’s most-pressed release (23 pressings) and represents the step from blogosphere sensation to proper label debut. Produced with Ben H. Allen, who also contributed bass, guitar, and percussion. Key guests: Caroline Polachek (featured vocalist and co-writer on “You And I”), Bradley Hagen (drums on “Eyes Be Closed”), Heather McIntosh (cello on “Far Away”), Mark Cobb (rototoms on “Eyes Be Closed”). Art direction and design by Jeff Kleinsmith.

2013 — Paracosm

Paracosm (Sub Pop SP 1055, 2013) — 18 pressings, second most-pressed. Again produced with Ben H. Allen. Additional contributors: Bradley Hagen (drums), Robby Handley (bass on 3 tracks), Matthew Stoessel (pedal steel on title track). Cover photography by Sara Cywnar. Design by Jeff Kleinsmith and Sasha Barr. Lacquer cutting split: Joe Lambert (LP), John Golden (7”).

2017 — Mister Mellow (Visual Album)

Mister Mellow (Stones Throw STH2387, 2017) — 11 pressings. A visual album: each of the 12 tracks paired with a short film directed by a different filmmaker, including Greene himself directing “I’ve Been Daydreaming My Entire Life.” Produced by Cole M.G.N., who also mixed and recorded; mastered by Dave Cooley. Design/layout by Jeff Jank; titles design by Bráulio Amado.

2020–present — Continued Output

Purple Noon (Sub Pop SP1365, 2020) — 11 pressings. Produced with Ben H. Allen; drummed by Harold Brown on 5 tracks; mastered by Randy Merrill; photography by Blair Greene. Notes From A Quiet Life (Sub Pop, 2024) — 11 pressings.

Connections

  • Mexican Summer — Origin label; “Feel It All Around” spread via MP3 blogs from MEX023 in 2009
  • Ben H. Allen — The dominant production relationship; shaped the sound of Washed Out’s commercial peak (Within And Without, Paracosm, Purple Noon)
  • Caroline Polachek — Guest on Within And Without “You And I”; also ex-Chairlift; her inclusion signals crossover between chillwave and art-pop worlds
  • Sub Pop — Handled North America for both Within And Without and Paracosm; global home from Purple Noon (2020) onward
  • Weird World — UK/Europe territorial licence for Within And Without (2011) and Paracosm (2013) only; not a label switch — Sub Pop and Weird World ran simultaneously in their respective territories. After Paracosm the deal ended.
  • Stones Throw — Mister Mellow departure into visual album format; Stones Throw known primarily for hip-hop/beat music, unusual signing

Open Questions

  • Why did Mister Mellow land at Stones Throw rather than Sub Pop? Label switch unexplained in Discogs

Answer: The split is strictly by credit type, not by era or label. Washed Out (1575877) holds all primary/performer credits — it is the credited artist on every release header (role = NULL, as_primary = true), plus occasional secondary roles like “Producer” or “Remix” when credited by that name on a guest release. Ernest Greene (1575878) holds the compositional/production contributor credits on those same releases — specifically “Written-By”, “Recorded By”, “Producer”, “Co-producer”, “Performer” (when listed as a named contributor rather than the header act), and “Art Direction”. So on a given release like Within And Without, Washed Out is the primary artist (the name on the cover) and Ernest Greene is the “Written-By / Producer / Art Direction” credit in the liner notes. The two entities are aliases of the same person and share identical real names and URLs on Discogs; this is a standard Discogs split where the project/stage name holds performer credits and the legal/birth name holds the composition and production credits.
