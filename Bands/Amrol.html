Amrol

Discogs ID: 402196
Active: 1993 (single cassette release documented)
Country: Germany
Genre: Rock — Indie Rock, New Wave
Label: Progressive Entertainment

Amrol was a German rock group formed in the early 1990s, notable primarily as an early vehicle for Markus Detmer — the musician and journalist who would later found the experimental label Staubgold (1998). The band released one cassette, Demoral (1993), on the small German tape imprint Progressive Entertainment.

Label: Progressive Entertainment

Progressive Entertainment (Discogs ID: 52629) was a small German cassette imprint active in 1993. No parent label or sublabels are recorded. Its earliest known catalog entry is PE 01 — Lufteeth In Europa by Lufties In Europa (Gregor Lehnick’s solo/alias project), also from 1993. Demoral carries catalog number PE 09, suggesting at least nine releases were issued. No other information about the label is available in Discogs.

Related Projects

Lufties In Europa

Lufties In Europa (Discogs ID: 523875) is the alias of Amrol bassist Gregor Lehnick. The project released Lufteeth In Europa (1993, Progressive Entertainment PE 01) — a nine-track ambient/experimental cassette predating or concurrent with Demoral. The name variation “Gregor ‘Lufti’ Lehnick” appears in his Discogs name variations, suggesting “Lufties” derives from this nickname. Unlike Amrol’s indie rock/new wave sound, Lufteeth In Europa is cataloged as Ambient / Experimental Electronic.

Klangwart

Klangwart (Discogs ID: 42387) — Markus Detmer’s primary ongoing project, formed in Cologne in 1996 with Timo Reuber. 14 releases on Staubgold (2008–2019).

Detmerkugel

Detmerkugel (Discogs ID: 775511) — duo of Markus Detmer and Christof Kugel (Discogs ID: 503865). No releases in Discogs.

Context

Amrol and Lufties In Europa sit at the pre-history of the Staubgold milieu. By 1993, Markus Detmer was already active in the Cologne underground cassette scene alongside Gregor Lehnick; within five years he would launch Staubgold (1998) and develop Klangwart into a sustained experimental project. The Demoral cassette documents the indie rock/new wave phase of that trajectory — guitar-based, sung, and structurally conventional compared to the microsound and electroacoustic work Detmer would go on to release and produce.

Progressive Entertainment as a label connects Amrol directly to Lufties In Europa, suggesting a small Cologne cassette ecosystem circa 1993 with Detmer and Lehnick at its centre.
