UK Decay

Discogs ID: 132793

UK post-punk / deathrock band. Tony Cook (2) engineered three of their releases in 1981 on Fresh Records: For Madmen Only (ID 929680), Hobby For A Day, and Unexpected Guest / Dresden. John Loder co-produced alongside the band.
