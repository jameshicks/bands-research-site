The Pogues

Discogs artist ID: 253234

English Celtic punk / folk band, founded in London in the early 1980s as Pogue Mahone (“kiss my arse” in Gaelic). Founded and fronted by Shane MacGowan.

Overview

Core founding lineup included Shane MacGowan (vocals), Jem Finer (banjo, mandola, saxophone), Spider Stacy (whistle), James Fearnley (accordion), Andrew Ranken (drums), and Cait O’Riordan (bass). Later members included Philip Chevron, Terry Woods, Darryl Hunt, James McNally, and David Coulter.

MacGowan was fired in 1991; the band continued (with Joe Strummer briefly) until 1996. Reformed in 2001; final show August 2014. Finer, Stacy, and Fearnley reconvened for 40th-anniversary shows in 2024–2025.

Philip Chevron died 8 October 2013. Darryl Hunt died 8 August 2022. Shane MacGowan died 30 November 2023. Andrew Ranken died 10 February 2026.
