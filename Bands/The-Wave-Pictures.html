Overview

English indie rock trio from Wymeswold, Leicestershire, formed in 1998 under the name Blind Summit before becoming The Wave Pictures. The core lineup of David Tattersall, Franic Rozycki, and Jonny Helm spans a catalogue concentrated in the 2010s (27 releases out of 40 total), rooted in indie rock, folk rock, and lo-fi styles. Their primary label home was Moshi Moshi Records (2007–2022, 27 works), with additional releases on Fika Recordings, Fortuna Pop!, and a 2026 appearance on Bella Union. Their most frequent collaborators are Stanley Brinks (26 shared releases) and Simon Trought (23 shared releases), alongside recurrent work with Darren Hayman and Clémence Freschard.
