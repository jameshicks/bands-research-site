Overview

Spirits Burning is a San Francisco-based space rock collective founded in 1986 and led by Don Falcone. Rather than a fixed lineup, it operates as a revolving ensemble, drawing in contributors across its catalogue that include Hawkwind veterans Adrian Shaw and Alan Davey, Blue Öyster Cult co-founder Albert Bouchard, and producer Jack Endino. The group spans space rock, psychedelic rock, prog, ambient, and experimental sounds, with eleven albums released from 1999 onward on labels including Voiceprint, Gonzo Multimedia, and Purple Pyramid.
