The Cloud People

DC-area indie rock band. Discogs artist ID: 4734784.

Overview

The Cloud People are a Washington, DC-area indie rock project formed by Bridget Cross, her partner George Kuhar, and Nick Gallas. The band shares two members — Cross and Kuhar — with Maybe It’s Reno, their earlier collaboration. Discogs lists no profile text or URL for the group, suggesting a low-profile or informal existence.

The band has one documented release: Time To Land (2015, Shadow Island Records), a six-track CD album recorded by Guy Picciotto (Fugazi, Rites of Spring) and produced by George Kuhar. Cross and Kuhar take care of most instrumentation; Gallas contributes bass clarinet and clarinet. The album is credited to the USA, Canada, and UK simultaneously, which may reflect a multi-territory self-release or small-label distribution arrangement. Shadow Island Records has only this one release in Discogs.

Cross is credited on the album sleeve (and in Discogs) as “Bridget Cross Kuhar,” confirming her partnership with George Kuhar.
