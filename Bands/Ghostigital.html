Ghostigital

Origin: Reykjavík, Iceland
Active: c. 2003–present
Members: Einar Örn Benediktsson (vocals, programming), Curver (Birgir Örn Thoroddsen — electronics, production)

Overview

Ghostigital is an Icelandic experimental electronic duo comprising Einar Örn Benediktsson — best known internationally as one half of The Sugarcubes — and Curver (Birgir Örn Thoroddsen), a Reykjavík-based musician, producer, and engineer. The project draws on glitch, hip hop, noise, electro, free jazz, and leftfield electronics, and is notable for the breadth of its guest collaborators: across their two main albums the duo brought in Mark E. Smith (The Fall), Katie Jane Garside, David Byrne, Damon Albarn, Alan Vega, Nick Zinner, King Buzzo, Dälek, and others. Einar Örn also appears on Gorillaz’ “El Mañana” (2006, lead vocals).

The Discogs profile notes that m=108842 — an Einar Örn release — is considered the conceptual debut, predating the Ipecac album; Ghostigital activity is documented from at least 2003 (per the retrospective’s subtitle “2003–2013”).

The duo records and performs as a two-piece but expands to a larger ensemble live and in studio, incorporating a recurring set of Reykjavík musicians. Smekkleysa — the label co-founded alongside The Sugarcubes — has been the primary Icelandic outlet for their later releases.
