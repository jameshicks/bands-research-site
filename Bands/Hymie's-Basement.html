Active: 2003–2004 (releases); Discogs years_active listed through 2021

Indie hip-hop / experimental pop duo from Minneapolis, formed by guitarist-producer Andrew Broder (of Fog) and rapper-singer Yoni Wolf (Jonathan Avram Wolf; of cLOUDDEAD and Why?). Released one self-titled LP and a single on Lex Records in 2003–2004, plus a live recording. A meeting point between Minneapolis indie rock production and the Anticon avant-rap world.

Hymie’s Basement (2003)

The sole full-length. Written, produced, and performed by Broder and Wolf. Jeremy Ylvisaker contributes harmony vocals and recorded tracks 2–8 and 10–14. Tom Herbers mixed the album, shot the photographs, and recorded tracks 1, 9, and 15. George Horn mastered. Layout by Wes Winship.

Context

Andrew Broder is a Minneapolis-based multi-instrumentalist and producer. His primary project is Fog, a critically recognized indie/experimental act on Ninja Tune and other labels. He also plays guitar in Why? and has collaborated extensively across the Minneapolis underground — Lateduster, HeatdeatH, The Interferents, Crook & Flail, Pentacene, The Cloak Ox. A 2004 release pairs him directly with George Cartwright of Curlew on Roaratorio — connecting these two worlds directly.

Yoni Wolf is a rapper, singer, and songwriter from Cincinnati, raised in the Bay Area. Co-founded cLOUDDEAD with Doseone (Adam Drucker) and Odd Nosdam (David Madson) — a landmark avant-rap trio on Anticon. Leads Why?, which evolved from a solo project into a full band. Has also recorded as The Pony (2) and under the band credits Why? (9). His brother Josiah Wolf is the drummer of Why?.
