Blackmouth

Dark experimental/industrial trio: Jarboe (vocals, lyrics), John Bergin (composer, mixer, design), Brett Smith (composer, mixer). Active in two phases: 2000 self-titled album on Crowd Control Activities, and 2019 reunion as Jarboe / Bergin / Smith on Stompbox13. Discogs artist ID: 73115. Bandcamp: jarboeberginsmith.bandcamp.com

Overview

Blackmouth emerged at the turn of the millennium from the overlapping circles of Jarboe’s post-Swans world and John Bergin’s Illinois-based industrial/comics scene. The self-titled 2000 album (Crowd Control Activities cat. CCA 021 / Crowded 21) is credited to the group as producer; individual credits list Bergin and Brett Smith as composers and mixers, and Jarboe as vocalist and lyricist. Vocal engineering on the album was handled by Daniel Crowder and Drew West. Cover artwork was by Cedric Victor-De Souza, with design, digital manipulation, typography, and additional photography by Bergin. The CD identifier notes Grinder Tool and Die (BMI) and Jarboe Music (ASCAP) as publishing entities.

The group was dormant for nearly two decades before reconvening in 2019. The reunion release Blackmouth: To Forget Time (Stompbox13, digital EP) credits all three members equally as written-by, performer, mixed by, and producer. Mastering was split: tracks 1–2 by Robert Rich, tracks 3–4 by Bergin. That same year, Stompbox13 also issued a Blackmouth (Deluxe Edition) compiling the original 2000 album with new material; mastering followed the same split.

A separate John Bergin solo release, Blackness Bleeding: The Blackmouth Remixes (Stompbox13, 2015), predated the reunion and collected reworkings of Blackmouth source material remixed solely by Bergin.

Bergin also issued the limited 2000 Grinder Books And Recordings pressing as a numbered box set (2xCD+CDr) — Disc 1 is the original album; Disc 2 (“Blackness Bleeding”) is 13 tracks remixed by Bergin.

The sonic territory across all releases spans ambient, industrial, and darkwave, with Jarboe’s voice as the defining textural element.

Member Projects & Context

Jarboe

Post-Swans solo and collaborative artist. Extensive discography under her own name and The Living Jarboe imprint. Key labels: Young God Records, Translation Loss Records, Consouling Sounds. Other projects: Skin (2), AEAEA, Neurosis & Jarboe (2003). See Jarboe for full discography.

John Bergin

Illinois-based composer, visual artist, and comics creator. Primary solo output under his own name and as C17H19NO3 (alias). Runs Stompbox13 label. Other bands: Trust Obey (with Brett Smith, industrial/noise), Tertium Non Data (with Brett Smith), Western Devils (with Brett Smith), Pigface, Lolo (2), Paved In Skin, Orifice, Camouflaged Abominations, Crime Lords Of 1982. Published graphic novels via Grinder Books; known in comics world alongside Dark Horse work.

Brett Smith

Composer/producer working in dark ambient and industrial. Solo work as Caul (Discogs ID: 100084, active 1994–2014). Caul released extensively on Epiphany Recordings, Malignant Records, Eibon Records, and Aglaia. Also composed the soundtrack From Inside (2012, Lakeshore Records). Shares three band memberships with Bergin: Trust Obey, Tertium Non Data, Western Devils.
