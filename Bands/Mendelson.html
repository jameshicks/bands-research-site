Overview

Mendelson is a French five-piece — Charlie O., Jean-Michel Pirès, Olivier Fejoz, Pascal Bouaziz, and Pierre-Yves Louis — working at the intersection of chanson, indie rock, and experimental art rock since 1997. Their catalogue spans four decades and nine releases, opening on Lithium through the late 1990s, moving to ReC-Son in the mid-2000s, and settling with Ici D’Ailleurs from 2013 to at least 2021. Frequent collaborators include Emmanuel Bacquet, Quentin Rollet, and Meïr Cohen across multiple shared releases.
