Endless Boogie

New York City jam/noise rock collective. Discogs artist ID: 654261. Founded 1997 by record collector Paul Major alongside employees of No Quarter Records. First live performance: 2001. Name taken from the John Lee Hooker album of the same name (a94557 on Discogs). Released six studio LPs, a compilation of early material, several live and self-released recordings, and a split 7” with Weak Signal. All commercial LP releases on No Quarter.

Formation and Background

Endless Boogie began as a loose gathering with no commercial intent — the original idea was simply to jam. Three participants were employees of No Quarter Records; the fourth founding presence was Paul Major, a professional record collector and obsessive digger whose knowledge of 1970s heavy rock and folk informed the band’s aesthetic. There were no plans to record or tour when the group formed. By 2001 they had played their first live show; by 2005 they had committed a lineup to tape.

Paul Major (born Mark Ohe, also known as Paul “Top Dollar” Major) sings and plays guitar. He has also written liner notes for compilations on RidingEasy Records, Anthology Recordings, and Modern Harmonic, and has been associated with the bands Prison (11), Sorcerers (3), The Moldy Dogs, The Tears (10), and Walkie Talkie.

Open Questions

  • “Dodging Dues” cited as a 2021 album in the research brief — not found in Discogs. The 2021 LP is Admonitions. Possible alternate title, promo title, or error.
  • Marko Pezzati / Mark Ohe: two separate Discogs entities (IDs 1057560 and 1074489) both appear to be the same person. The Volume 1 (2005) credit is under ID 2739930 (a third entry). Needs reconciliation.
  • Full recording history of Siberia Live (2005, Dolor Del Estamago) cassette — label is obscure; unofficial nature noted.
  • No Quarter Records founding history and relationship to the three founding members: the Discogs label profile (ID: 44042) has no text bio.
