SQÜRL

Discogs ID: 4431635 Alias / Former name: Bad Rabbit (3) (Discogs ID: 1651288) Formed: 2009, New York City Genre: Experimental rock, psychedelia, drone, noise, country

Overview

SQÜRL is an experimental rock band from New York City, built around filmmaker Jim Jarmusch (electric and resonator guitar, synthesizer, bass, effects) and multi-instrumentalist Carter Logan (electric piano, synthesizer, bass, drums). The band originated in 2009 when Jarmusch and Logan teamed with producer/engineer Shane Stoneback to record an original score for Jarmusch’s film The Limits of Control — releasing that material under the name Bad Rabbit (3). In 2010 they changed the name to SQÜRL.

Their self-description: “an enthusiastically marginal rock band from New York City who like big drums & broken guitars, cassette recorders, loops, feedback, sad country songs, molten stoner core, chopped & screwed hip-hop, and imaginary movie scores.” Early EPs were tracked by Stoneback at Treefort Recording in Brooklyn; later work moved to collaboration with producers Jonathan Kreinik and Randall Dunn and masterer Heba Kadry.

The band’s highest-profile release is the Only Lovers Left Alive soundtrack (2013/2014, ATP Recordings), co-credited with lutenist Jozef Van Wissem, for which Van Wissem won the Cannes Soundtrack Award at the 2013 Cannes Film Festival. Stoneback departed after 2014; since then SQÜRL has continued as a Jarmusch/Logan duo. Van Wissem toured with SQÜRL as a live member on lute in 2015–2016.
