The New Christs

Discogs ID: 637839. Garage rock / post-punk band based in Sydney, New South Wales, Australia. Formed 1981, continuously active through multiple lineup changes into the 2010s. Led throughout by vocalist Rob Younger, also singer of Radio Birdman. The band is characterised by heavy, Stooges-influenced guitar rock rooted in the Sydney underground scene that spawned Radio Birdman, The Saints, and The Celibate Rifles.

Name variation: New Christs (without “The”).

Overview

The New Christs began as a vehicle for Rob Younger following Radio Birdman’s dissolution in 1978. The earliest lineup (1981) featured vocalist Bruce “Cub” Callaway (also in The Saints (2) and No Dance), with Younger credited under his alias Rowdy Yates. The first single “Face A New God” (1981) was produced by Callaway himself (credited as Cab Callaway).

Through the mid-1980s the band cycled through members drawn from the Sydney garage-rock network — notably guitarist Chris Masuak (Radio Birdman), guitarist Richard Jakimyszyn, bassist Tony Robertson (4), and drummer Mark Kingsmill. Two EPs on the French label Sonics Records (“Like A Curse”, 1984; “Born Out Of Time”, 1985) established the band internationally.

The classic 1987–1989 run produced three records on Australian label Citadel (7): the “Dropping Like Flies” EP (1987), album Divine Rites (1988), and album Distemper (1989). This era’s lineup centred on bassist/vocalist Jim Dickson (2), guitarist Charlie Owen, drummer Louis Burdett, and organist Louis Tillett, with engineer/producer Alan Thorne helming the recordings.

The 1990s brought a new lineup featuring guitarist Tony Harper, bassist Christian Houllemare, and drummer Peter Kelly (2). Two albums — Pedestal (1994) and Woe Betide (1995) — appeared on Citadel, followed by Lower Yourself (1997). A further album We Got This! arrived on Laughing Outlaw Records in 2002.

The 2000s–2010s saw a stabilised lineup of Dave Kettley (guitar), Brent Williams (guitar/keyboards), Jim Dickson (bass; returning), and Paul Larsen (drums), which recorded Gloria (2009, Impedance Records), Incantations (2014, Impedance Records / Music Farmers), and a 2025 retrospective The Burning Of Rome: Selected Works.

Scene Connections

The New Christs occupy the core of Sydney’s post-punk / garage-rock underground. The band’s membership network overlaps heavily with:

  • Radio Birdman (268343) — Rob Younger was Radio Birdman’s vocalist; Jim Dickson, Chris Masuak, and Nik Rieth also appear in both bands.
  • The Celibate Rifles (439045) — Kent Steedman (guitar), Paul Larsen (drums), and Nik Rieth (drums) are shared members; the Rifles and the Christs ran parallel throughout the 1980s–90s.
  • The Saints (2) (198097) — Bruce Callaway’s primary affiliation; another foundational Sydney/Australian punk band.
  • No Dance (573209) — Side project featuring Bruce Callaway alongside Damien Lovelock, Louis Tillett, and Brett Myers; overlapping personnel with the Celibate Rifles.
  • The Aints (450870) — Kent Steedman is a member; another Sydney garage-rock outfit.
  • New Race (637853) — Rob Younger’s early post-Radio Birdman project.
  • Louis Tillett — Organ player on 1987–88 recordings; also had a solo career through Citadel.

The Divine Rites (1988) album is widely regarded as the band’s peak, bringing together the tightest lineup (Younger, Owen, Dickson, Burdett, Tillett) with engineer Alan Thorne’s crisp studio work.
