Disease (2)

Sheffield post-punk band, active c. 1980–1981. Discogs artist ID: 711686.

Overview

Disease (2) were a six-piece post-punk group from Sheffield, active at the height of the city’s early-1980s independent scene. Their recorded output is minimal but documents the full arc of a short-lived local band: a track on a scene compilation, a self-released demo cassette, and a self-funded 7” single before dissolving.

Their most prominent appearance is “Psychobin” (track A4) on Bouquet Of Steel (1980, Aardvark, STEAL 2) — the landmark Sheffield compilation compiled by Marcus Featherby that captured fifteen local acts at a single moment. “Psychobin” was produced by Kevin Bacon of The Comsat Angels and written by Jim Darnill. Kevin Bacon also produced two other tracks on the same compilation (Scarborough Antelopes and Veiled Threat), suggesting a brief local production sideline alongside his work with the Comsat Angels.

The following year the band issued a double-A-side 7” — No Future / I Know How (1981, Disease Discs, DD1) — on their own imprint, again produced by Kevin Bacon. A self-released demo cassette also exists in the Discogs catalog (release ID: 20759650), undated, with most track titles unlisted; “I Know How” appears on both the demo and the single.

Graham Gaunt is separately credited on the Aardvark label’s 1981 Artery (2) single Cars In Motion (AARD 5) as cover designer — the only confirmed post-Disease (2) Discogs credit for any member.
