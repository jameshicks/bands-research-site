Overview

Parts & Labor were an American experimental and noise rock band formed in Brooklyn, NY in 2002, working across indie rock, math rock, and noise. They released the bulk of their catalogue through the 2000s — six releases across a range of small labels, anchored by a longer relationship with Jagjaguwar (four releases, 2006–2011) and Brah Records. A single release in the 2010s marks the end of their Discogs footprint, suggesting the band wound down after roughly a decade. Engineers Blair Wells and Paul Gold appear across much of their output, with Dave Fridmann also contributing; drummer Christopher R. Weingarten later became a prominent music journalist.
