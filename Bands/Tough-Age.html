Tough Age

Discogs ID: 3553314 Also known as: Toughage Origin: Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada Formed: late 2012

“Powerhouse pop band” (Discogs). All studio releases on Mint Records (12). Primary producer across the catalog: Jay Arner (Vancouver musician, also in Energy Slime and International Falls).

(guitar, vocals), Penny Clark (guitar, vocals), Lauren Smith (bass, vocals), Chris Martell (drums, vocals), Jason Oliver (3) (guitar). Produced by Jay Arner. Mastered by Julian Marrs. Photos: Joe Klotz, Leonard Whistler. Genres: Pop, Rock; Pop Punk. A tribute to Vancouver band Cub (specifically their track “Hot Dog Day”), continuing a lineage of Vancouver indie pop.

Split Releases

  • Pizza Slice / Giuseppe Pizzeria Split (2021, with Dumb (8)) — split 7” on Mint Records (12), cat. MRS193. Limited Edition, Stereo. Tough Age contributes Side B (“Giuseppe Pizzeria”); Dumb contributes Side A (“Pizza Slice”). Matrix etched: “XEE DUMB - PIZZA SLICE” / “XEE TOUGH AGE”.

Outside Appearances
