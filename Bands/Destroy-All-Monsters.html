Destroy All Monsters

Detroit art-noise-punk collective active 1973–1984 (and periodic reunions), formed around 1973 by Mike Kelley, Cary Loren, Jim Shaw, and Niagara while students at the University of Michigan. Later incorporated Ron Asheton (ex-Stooges), Mike Davis (ex-MC5), and other members. Discogs artist ID: 303051. Name variations: D.A.M., Destroy All Monsters Collective, Ron Asheton’s Destroy All Monsters.

Overview

Started as a loose, avant-garde noise collective generating performances, zines, Super-8 films, and audio assemblages. The original core of Kelley, Loren, Shaw, and Niagara defined the earliest period (1973–1976). After Kelley and Shaw departed for CalArts (Los Angeles), the band continued with Niagara and Loren and later recruited Ron Asheton and Michael Davis, shifting toward a more punk-rock sound. That lineup released singles on IDBI Records (1978), Black Hole Records (1979), and Cherry Red (UK, 1979).
