Hanatarash

Discogs ID: 78093
Real name (founder): 山塚徹郎 (Yamatsuka Tetsurō)
Active: 1983–c.1993 (sporadic returns)
Origin: Osaka, Japan

Japanese noise project founded by Yamatsuka Eye in 1983. Name (ハナタラシ) literally means “sniveler” (person with a runny nose). Infamous for extremely dangerous live performances: Eye used a backhoe to throw junk around venues, cut his own leg with an electric saw mid-show, and severed a dead cat on stage. A 1985 Tokyo show (Superloft) required audience liability waivers; the performance ended before Eye threw a lit molotov cocktail onto a gasoline-soaked stage, costing the venue ¥600,000. Eventually banned from performing anywhere.

Other members: Ikuo Taketani (Discogs ID 562361), Ohmiya Ichi / 大宮イチ (Discogs ID 624708) — actor, appeared only in the LP cover photo session, not a performing member.

Eye went on to found Boredoms while Hanatarash became inactive. The project resurfaced in the 1990s for some releases without the on-stage mayhem.
