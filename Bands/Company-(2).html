Company (2)

Discogs ID: 326370 Name variations: Company 91, Derek Bailey Company, Virtual Company Formed: 1976 by Derek Bailey Dissolved: c. 2001 (final release: Company In Marseille, 2002)

Rotating free-improvisation collective formed and convened by Derek Bailey. Not a fixed-lineup group — rather a framework for collective improvisation with an ever-changing cast of musicians. The annual Company Week (held regularly in the 1980s and into the 1990s) brought together nine or ten musicians for five days of intensive improvisation, often pairing people who had never played together before. One of the defining institutions of European free improvisation.
