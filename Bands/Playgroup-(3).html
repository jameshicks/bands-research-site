Playgroup (3)

UK experimental / post-punk / dub ensemble. Discogs artist ID: 31639. Active 1982–1983 (original releases); reissued 1991. Orbit of On-U Sound and Cherry Red.

Overview

Playgroup (3) — disambiguated in Discogs from Trevor Jackson’s later Playgroup project — was a loose ensemble assembled around Adrian Sherwood’s On-U Sound production practice and signed to Cherry Red. The group released two LPs (Epic Sound Battles Chapter One, 1982; Epic Sound Battles Chapter Two, 1983) and one 12” single (“Going Overdrawn / Forty Winks,” 1983), all produced by Sherwood. A compilation of both LPs was reissued on Cherry Red in 1991.

The group drew from a rotating pool of On-U Sound regulars and post-punk veterans. Core members listed on Discogs include Sean Oliver (bass/guitar, nicknamed “Hogg”), Bruce Smith (drums, from The Pop Group and Rip Rig & Panic), John Waddington (guitar, from The Pop Group), George Oban (bass, from Aswad and New Age Steppers), and Bonjo Iyabinghi Noah (percussion, from African Head Charge).

The name “Machine Gun Hogg & Co.” — an On-U Sound-adjacent project that predates Playgroup (3)‘s debut by a year — is almost certainly connected through Sean Oliver’s “Hogg” nickname: see Machine Gun Hogg & Co. for detail. The track “Hoggs Might Fly” (B6 on Chapter One, co-written by Bruce Smith and Sean Oliver) names the connection explicitly.

Connections

  • Machine Gun Hogg & Co. — earlier On-U Sound project likely sharing Sean Oliver (“Hogg”) and other personnel; “Hoggs Might Fly” on Chapter One names the link explicitly
  • Adrian Sherwood — producer; founder of On-U Sound; also credited as “Adrian Maxwell” (written-by) and “Crocodile” (Chapter Two effects) and “Prisoner (3)” (Chapter Two drums) — multiple aliases on these records
  • Sean Oliver — “Hogg”; bassist; Antiguan/British, died March 1990 aged 27; also in Rip Rig & Panic and New Age Steppers
  • Bruce Smith — drummer; from The Pop Group; later Public Image Limited
  • John Waddington — guitarist; from The Pop Group (died June 2023)
  • George Oban (aka Ras Levi) — bassist; from Aswad; died 2022
  • Cherry Red — label for all releases; also released Wild Paarty Sounds Volume One (1981)
  • On-U Sound — co-released Chapter Two; Sherwood’s label home
  • The Pop Group — Bruce Smith and John Waddington originate here (Bristol post-punk)
  • Rip Rig & Panic — Bruce Smith and Sean Oliver both members
  • New Age Steppers — Sean Oliver, George Oban, and Bonjo Iyabinghi Noah all passed through
  • African Head Charge — George Oban and Bonjo Iyabinghi Noah both members; On-U Sound sister project
  • Shake The Foundations (Militant Funk & The Post-Punk Dancefloor 1978-1984) — 2021 Cherry Red box set featuring Playgroup (3)‘s “Hoggs Might Fly” (Disc 3-16)
