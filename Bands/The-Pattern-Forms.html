Overview

The Pattern Forms is a collaborative project pairing Ed Macfarlane and Edd Gibson of indie-pop band Friendly Fires with Jon Brooks, the solo artist behind The Advisory Circle on the Ghost Box label. The catalogue sits across two eras: a pair of releases on Ghost Box in 2015–2016 and a return in 2022 via Belbury Music, a sister imprint within the same stable, suggesting an ongoing relationship with the Ghost Box world. Stylistically the project moves across ambient, downtempo, synth-pop and experimental folk — considerably more eclectic than either parent act.

How the collaboration formed

No interview or press piece locates the first contact between Jon Brooks and the Friendly Fires members. What multiple sources agree on is the following:

  • The three shared a passion for soundtrack and electronic library music of the 1970s and ’80s. The Ghost Box Greedbag store description of Peel Away the Ivy states the album’s “roots lie in both bands’ shared love of soundtracks and electronic library music of the 70s and 80s” — the same phrasing appears across retailer descriptions, suggesting it originates from Ghost Box’s own press copy. (ghostbox.greedbag.com, accessed 2026-11-09)
  • The first documented output is the double A-side “Fluchtwege” / “The Sacrifice” on the Ghost Box Other Voices singles series (2015), catalogue GBX023/024. This was the project’s public debut, not a private origin point — the collaboration existed before that release.
  • Ghost Box’s Wikipedia article notes the label “has releases by guest artists sometimes collaborating with members of the regular roster,” which is consistent with Ghost Box facilitating the pairing, but the article does not name The Pattern Forms as a specific example of a brokered link. (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghost_Box_Records, as of 2026-11-09)
  • In a 2020 Haunted Generation interview, Brooks mentions The Pattern Forms only in passing: “I’ve done collaborations with Friendly Fires as The Pattern Forms, and we did an album together, that was good fun to do.” No origin story is given. (hauntedgeneration.co.uk, accessed 2026-11-09)
  • After Peel Away the Ivy (2016) the working relationship continued: Ed Macfarlane appears on vocals on “No Way Back” and Edward Gibson co-produced “Scuba” on The Advisory Circle’s Ways of Seeing (Ghost Box, 2018), indicating an ongoing personal as well as professional relationship that extended beyond Ghost Box’s curation. (djfood.org, accessed 2026-11-09)

Summary: No source states whether the connection was initiated by Ghost Box or was a pre-existing personal friendship. The consistent framing — shared library-music taste as the creative glue, with Ghost Box’s Other Voices series as the vehicle for the first release — is compatible with either explanation. The continued work outside the Ghost Box release schedule (Ways of Seeing guest spots; the 2021 self-released The Scenic Route) suggests the relationship became independent of the label relatively quickly.
