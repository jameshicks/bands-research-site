Quint (4)

UK band active mid-to-late 1990s. Two releases: one 7” on Southern Records (1995) and one CD album on Egypt Records (1998). Discogs artist ID: 1899804

Overview

Quint (4) was a London-based ensemble formed around 1994–95, centred on Sally Young — a founding member of the NYC No Wave trio Ut who had relocated to London by the early 1980s. The band released a 7” single, “Blueprint To A Blackout / Sawtooth,” on Southern Records in 1995 (cat. 18533-7), engineered by Steve Albini. Genres: Alternative Rock, Noise. A full-length album, Time Wounds All Heals, followed in 1998 on Egypt Records (cat. Egypt 1), a small UK label with only seven releases in Discogs.

The lineup was unusually textured for the period: two drummers (Graham Newbury and Steve Gilchrist), violin (Pookie Jin), trumpet (Andrew Blick), and dual guitar/trumpet (Ian Watson alongside Young’s vocals and guitar). The 1995 7” credits a five-piece (Young, Sellers, Newbury, Blick, Jin) while the 1998 album uses a slightly different lineup (Young, Sellers, Gilchrist, Watson, Pookie Jin — no Newbury or Blick). Debbie Smith (Curve, Echobelly) is listed in the Discogs member roster but does not appear in either release’s credits; her involvement may have been at rehearsal stage or for live shows.

The Wikipedia article on Ut (citing a Southern Records promotional page) refers to the band as releasing the Time Wounds All Heals album on Southern Records, but Discogs records that album on Egypt Records. The 7” single was on Southern Records; the album moved to Egypt Records.

Steve Albini’s connection to the project echoes his earlier work with Young: he had engineered Ut’s final album Griller (1989, Blast First) some six years before the Quint (4) single.
