UI

New York post-rock group. Co-founded in 1990 by Sasha Frere-Jones and Clem Waldmann. Debuted with Sidelong (18535-1, 1995) on Southern Records; 17 releases through 2002. Recorded and mixed by Greg Frey; mastered by John Loder.

History

Ui co-founded in 1990 by Sasha Frere-Jones and Clem Waldmann, operating as a bridge between NYC punk-funk and post-rock with a dual-bass lineup. The band released three studio albums on Southern Records — Sidelong (1996), Lifelike (1998), and Answers (2003) — and toured internationally.

Frere-Jones stopped performing with Ui in 2003, and the band effectively dissolved around that time, shortly after recording Answers. He joined The New Yorker as pop music critic in January 2004. The remaining members reconvened for a single reunion performance at Primavera Festival (Barcelona) in 2010. Frere-Jones also contributed a remix under the moniker Calvinist in 2011. No further activity has been documented.

The band’s end appears to have been driven primarily by Frere-Jones’s transition to full-time music criticism rather than by any documented conflict or formal announcement. (novembermag.com interview, as of 2026-07-27; Wikipedia: Ui (band), as of 2026-07-27)
