Kwesachu

Discogs ID: 1874692

Kwesachu is a collaborative project between Kwes. (Kwesi Sey) and Mica Levi (credited as Micachu). The name is a portmanteau of their two names. Based in London, the project released two self-produced cassette mixtapes in 2009 and 2012, both self-released on the “Not On Label (Kwesachu Self-released)” imprint. The releases function as curated showcases drawing in a wide circle of London-adjacent artists across hip-hop, indie, experimental pop, and electronic music.

Overview

Both Kwes. and Mica Levi were active in overlapping London music circles in the late 2000s and early 2010s — Kwes. as a producer-songwriter signed to Warp Records, Mica Levi through Micachu And The Shapes on Accidental Records. Kwesachu served as a vehicle for their collaborative production work and for platforming friends and peers.

The project has no formal group membership structure — Discogs lists Kwesi Sey and Mica Levi as its two members. The mixtape format allowed for a fluid, low-pressure environment where tracks could be originals, reworks, or remixes. M.A.T.H.E.S. (an alias configuration around Mica Levi) is also credited as a producer on the first volume.

Genre / Sound Context

Both releases are tagged Electronic / Pop / Experimental on Discogs. The cassette format and mixtape structure situate Kwesachu within DIY London underground practice of the period — lo-fi distribution, collaborative track-sharing, no commercial label. The broad roster reflects the overlapping creative networks of South and East London at the turn of the decade.
