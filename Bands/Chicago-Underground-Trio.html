Chicago Underground Trio

Chicago-based experimental jazz trio led by cornetist Rob Mazurek and drummer/percussionist Chad Taylor, with bassist Noel Kupersmith on the first three albums (1999–2003) and Jason Ajemian on Chronicle (2007). Discogs artist ID: 337034. Name variation: “The Chicago Underground Trio.”

Overview

The Chicago Underground Trio (CUT) emerged from the late-1990s Chicago creative-music scene anchored by Mazurek’s long-running Chicago Underground collective. Founded as a trio in 1999, the group built outward from the Mazurek/Taylor core duo (see Chicago Underground Duo), adding a bassist to extend into a richer textural space. Mazurek plays cornet alongside laptop computers, Moog synthesizer, electric celeste, bamboo flute, gong, and Moogerfooger pedals; Taylor commands a full drum kit plus vibraphone, marimba, mbira, and assorted percussion. The music blends free jazz improvisation with ambient electronics, drone, post-rock weight, and the influence of the AACM’s collective-improvisation tradition.

The group released four studio albums between 1999 and 2007: three on Delmark Records (Possible Cube, Flamethrower, Chronicle) and one on Thrill Jockey (Slon). A reissue of Possible Cube appeared on Delmark in 2022. The Trio also contributed six tracks to Psychic Temple’s Houses of the Holy (2020, Joyful Noise Recordings) as a performance credit. The broader Chicago Underground family — Duo, Quartet, Orchestra, and London Underground — is profiled at the Duo’s page and in the individual member stubs.

The Discogs profile note reads: “See also Chicago Underground Quartet (same group, plus one)” — confirming the Trio’s membership list as a subset of the Quartet’s.

Connections

  • Rob Mazurek — leader and cornetist; co-founder with Taylor; extensive parallel career in solo work, Exploding Star Orchestra, São Paulo Underground, and numerous large ensembles
  • Chad Taylor — co-founder; drums, vibraphone, mbira; sustained partnership with Mazurek across 25+ years
  • Noel Kupersmith — original bassist (1999–2003); also member of Chicago Underground Quartet, Brokeback, Red Krayola, Ted Sirota’s Rebel Souls
