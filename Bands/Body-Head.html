Body/Head

Discogs ID: 2165621 Members: Kim Gordon, Bill Nace Origin: US Active: 2011–present Genres: Noise, Free Improvisation, Drone, Experimental Rock Labels: Matador Records, Ecstatic Peace!, Feeding Tube Records, Open Mouth, Three Lobed Recordings

Biography

Body/Head is a free rock and noise duo formed by Kim Gordon (vocals, guitar) and Bill Nace (guitar). They began performing together in 2011, the same year Sonic Youth went on indefinite hiatus following Gordon’s separation from Thurston Moore.

The duo came out of an improvisational encounter between the two musicians. Their approach is grounded in sustained, feedback-driven guitar interplay — minimal in structure, exploring drone, noise, and free improvisation. Gordon brings vocal contributions over a foundation of interlocking, corroded guitar textures, while Nace operates from a solo improvisation background with roots in Philadelphia’s experimental music scene.

Their first release, the cassette Fractured Orgasm (2011), appeared on Ecstatic Peace! — Thurston Moore’s label, catalog number 22e — establishing the duo in the noise/experimental underground before they had any formal LP. Their debut full-length, Coming Apart (2013, Matador Records), was produced with engineer Justin Pizzoferrato and mastered by John Golden. The record includes a cover of Nina Simone’s “Ain’t Got No” (listed as “Aint”) and a lyric drawn from Traditional sources, alongside Gordon originals. It received significant attention as the first substantial post-Sonic Youth statement from Gordon.

The 7” single The Show Is Over (2014, Matador, OLE-1061-7) followed, with two tracks recorded and mixed again by Pizzoferrato and mastered by Golden, with cover image by artist Christopher Wool.

Live Hassle (2016, Feeding Tube Records, FTR242) documented a live performance — a limited-edition numbered white-label LP, recorded by Ethan Long.

No Waves (2016, Matador, OLE-1121-2) is a live CD recording capturing three extended pieces, including an “Abstract/Actress” running over 23 minutes. Cover art by Raymond Pettibon; design by Mike Zimmerman; mixing engineers Misha Goldman and Scott Pederson; mastered by Carl Saff.

The Switch (2018, Matador, OLE-1361-2) is the second studio LP, engineered and mixed in part by Pizzoferrato, with Body/Head credited as co-mixers and writers. Mastered by Golden. Photography by Vice Cooler.

“come on” e.p. (2023, Open Mouth / Three Lobed Recordings, OM78 / TLR147) is a double-7” EP (four tracks, 45 RPM, limited edition, numbered). Produced by Nace and Seth Manchester; engineered by Vice Cooler and Manchester; mastered by Chuck Johnson. Gordon plays vocals, electric guitar, synth, and field recordings; Nace plays electric guitar, piano, Echoplex tape delay.

Collaborative Projects

Beyond the core duo, Gordon and Nace have recorded under variant names with additional musicians:

Body/Gate/Head (Discogs ID: 3055295) — a one-off performance and recording with Michael Morley (Gate, of The Dead C). Released as Glare Luring Yo (2012, Feeding Tube Records, FTR099): two-sided LP with tracks “One For Alice Bonnard” and “One For Earthworm Jim.” All three credited on guitar, with Gordon also on vocals.

Body/Dilloway/Head (Discogs ID: 10304236) — a collaboration with Aaron Dilloway (Wolf Eyes), released as Body/Dilloway/Head (2021, Three Lobed Recordings, TLR-135X). Digital album (MP3) with three long pieces. All three credited for “Sounds Created” and “Edited By [Manipulated By].” Mastered by Carl Saff.
