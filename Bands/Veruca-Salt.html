Veruca Salt

American alternative rock band formed in Chicago, Illinois in 1992 by vocalist-guitarists Nina Gordon and Louise Post, drummer Jim Shapiro, and bassist Steve Lack. Named after the spoiled character from Roald Dahl’s Charlie and the Chocolate Factory. Discogs artist ID: 28974.

Overview

Veruca Salt built their sound around dual lead vocals and dual rhythm-guitar interplay between Gordon and Post, with each typically writing and singing her own songs while the other contributed backing vocals. The band broke through nationally on the strength of the 1994 single “Seether” (co-written by Gordon and Post), released first on Minty Fresh before the band signed to DGC Records. American Thighs (1994), recorded at Idful Studio in Chicago by Brad Wood, reached Gold certification in both the US and Canada. The follow-up, Eight Arms to Hold You (1997), was produced by Bob Rock and recorded in Maui, Hawaii; it spawned “Volcano Girls,” used as the opening theme for the teen film Jawbreaker (1999).

Gordon departed in 1998 after a falling-out with Post. Post carried the band forward through two more albums — Resolver (2000) and IV (2006) — with shifting lineups before going on indefinite hiatus in 2012. The original four-piece reunited in 2013 and released Ghost Notes (2015), the first album credited to the full original lineup since Eight Arms. Post has continued releasing solo and Veruca Salt material on El Camino Media (via the sub-label Velveteen Records).

Sources: Discogs; Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veruca_Salt).

Notes:

  • Jim Shapiro is Nina Gordon’s brother; he had no prior drumming experience when he joined.
  • Post and Gordon were introduced by mutual friend Lili Taylor.
  • Stacy Jones (formerly Letters to Cleo, later American Hi-Fi) replaced Shapiro post-Eight Arms for touring.
  • After 1998, Post ran the band as her solo project through the Velveteen Records imprint (distribution via Beyond Music).
  • Nicole Fiorentino later joined The Smashing Pumpkins. Kellii Scott returned to Failure.
