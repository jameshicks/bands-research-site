The Flying Burrito Bros

Discogs ID: 285133 Also known as: Flying Burrito Brothers, The Burrito Brothers, The Burritos Active: 1968–present (various lineups) Origin: Los Angeles, California, US

Country rock band co-founded by Gram Parsons and Chris Hillman after both departed The Byrds in 1968. The Parsons/Hillman lineup (1968–1970) recorded the two defining documents of the band’s classic period; the group continued under various configurations after Parsons left in 1970, and the name has been used through the 2000s.

Classic Lineup (1968–1970)

  • Gram Parsons — vocals, rhythm guitar, keyboards
  • Chris Hillman — vocals, guitar, bass, mandolin
  • Sneaky Pete Kleinow — pedal steel guitar (Discogs ID: 322746)
  • Chris Ethridge — bass, piano (early lineup; replaced by Hillman on bass)
  • Michael Clarke — drums (Discogs ID: 639320)
  • Bernie Leadon — guitar (joined for Burrito Deluxe; Discogs ID: 482142)
  • Byron Berline — fiddle (session; Discogs ID: 255181)
