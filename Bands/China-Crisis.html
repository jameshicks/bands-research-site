Overview

China Crisis are a synth-pop and new wave group formed in Liverpool in 1979, blending art-rock sensibilities with melodic pop craft. Their catalogue is heavily weighted toward the 1980s (25 releases) when they recorded primarily for Virgin in the UK, with concurrent US deals on Warner Bros. (1983–1985) and then A&M Records (1986–1989); a smaller wave of releases followed in the 1990s, and the band returned to activity in the 2020s on Last Night From Glasgow. Production throughout their peak years ran through Mike Howlett and the Clive Langer/Alan Winstanley partnership, with Gil Norton also among their close collaborators.
