Doves

Discogs ID: 3855
Name variations: The Doves, The Band
Active: 1998–2010 (hiatus), 2018–present

Alternative rock band from Manchester. Formed when Sub Sub — the dance/house project of the same three members — rechristened itself after a studio fire destroyed most of their equipment. Signed to Heavenly (Discogs label ID confirmed in catalog). Debuted as Doves with the Cedar EP (Casino/Robs Records sublabel, 1998); major label debut album Lost Souls (Heavenly, 2000).

Formation and Transition from Sub Sub

Sub Sub released their final record, “This Time I’m Not Wrong” (with Bernard Sumner) on Robs Records in 1997. Following a studio fire that wiped out most of their equipment, the three members reconvened under the name Doves. Their first release as Doves was the Cedar EP (Casino, 1998 — Casino is a sublabel of Robs Records). They self-recorded and self-mixed the EP; Stuart Warburton plays harmonica. All songs written by Goodwin / J. Williams / A. Williams.

The early Casino releases (1998–1999) include Cedar EP, Sea EP, Here It Comes, and Road Movie (a split with Twisted Nerve / XL Recordings). This transitional period shows the band finding a guitar-oriented sound while retaining full production control.
