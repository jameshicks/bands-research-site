Bonnevill

Discogs ID: 352351
Active: 1999–2001
Members: Mick Turner (guitar), Jessica Billey (violin, piano)
Labels: Temporary Residence Limited, Bella Union, Instinct Records, Naïve

Bonnevill was an instrumental duo formed by Mick Turner (of Dirty Three) and Jessica Billey (violinist, also of Boxhead Ensemble, Pinetop Seven). The project produced two releases — a six-track maxi-CD in 1999 and a full-length album in 2001 — both quiet and chamber-inflected, weaving Turner’s guitar work against Billey’s violin and piano. The name shares the “Bonnevill” spelling (one l) with no apparent reference to the Pontiac or other uses. Both members contributed compositions and cover artwork; Billey’s paintings graced both releases.

Release Notes

Travels In Constants Volume Two — Temporary Residence Limited, 1999

Six tracks: “Tigermoth”, “El Nopal”, “Blue Kite Dog”, “Elsa Sass”, “Bella’s Orchard”, “Tomorrow’s Keys”. Genres: Folk Rock / Indie Rock / Post Rock. Engineered by Michael Krassner (also credited as Mike Krassner; plays piano on the record). Cover paintings by Jessica Billey. Written by Billey and Turner jointly.

Part of the Travels In Constants compilation/series on Temporary Residence Limited (sublabel ID: 348413).

Pelican — Bella Union / Instinct Records / Naïve, 2001

Seven tracks: “Gene”, “Lammy”, “Camoe”, “Ides”, “Midnight Driver”, “Pelican”, “Lullabye”. Genres: Electronic / Rock. Styles: Modern Classical / Post Rock. Composed by both Billey and Turner; cover painting by Billey, tray painting by Turner. Three simultaneous territory releases: UK (Bella Union BELLACD703), US (Instinct Records INS7002-2), France (Naïve BU 30003). The US pressing carries a shorter credits list than the UK/France versions.

Pelican is Part 3 of Bella Union’s Series 7 concept series — seven instrumental albums, seven tracks each, recorded in seven days. [Source: iceburn666.blogspot.com]

Guest appearances on specific tracks:

  • “Camoe”: Joel Silbersher (guitar)
  • “Ides”: Brian Freeman (cello), Jesse Poe (percussion), Brian Paulson (recorded by — credited as “Brian Poulsen”)
