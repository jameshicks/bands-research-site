Bourbonese Qualk

UK experimental / industrial group. Formed Southport, 1979. Discogs artist ID: 48247.

Alias: Recloose Organisation (listed on Discogs as an alias for the group entity).

Overview

Bourbonese Qualk began when Simon Crab and his brother Ted Crabtree started making experimental music in a DIY anarchist spirit in Southport (near Liverpool), 1979. From the start the group was grounded in resistance to authority, collectivist organisation, and stylistic uncompromise. After relocating to London and setting up in a squat, they expanded to a three-piece with Julian Gilbert and Steven Tanza, founded the Recloose Organisation label, and ran the Ambulance Station as a community arts space. Members came and went over two decades, with Crab as the only constant. The group officially disbanded in late 2002 following the death of guitarist/multi-instrumentalist Miles Miles.

Key stylistic moves: early DIY industrial/experimental (1982–85); tribal/industrial crossover (1986–89); techno/hard-trance convergence (1992–93). Their back catalogue has been extensively reissued on Klanggalerie (Vienna) from 2019 onwards.

History

Origins (1979–1981)

Simon Crab and Ted Crabtree (Discogs: 5660842) began working together in Southport in 1979. The name “Bourbonese Qualk” was chosen without a fixed referent. They recorded and released early cassette material, including We Want Peace - Autumn 1981 (Recloose Organisation, 1981).

Recloose era: first lineup (1982–1986)

After moving to a London squat around 1982, Crab was joined by Julian Gilbert (vocals, theatrics) and Steven Tanza (percussion). Together they ran the Ambulance Station as an open cultural space and founded the Recloose Organisation as a collectivist label. The label ran until 1986 and issued 13+ releases by the group and others (Muslimgauze, E.g Oblique Graph, Observation Clinique, Laxative Souls, Pierpaolo Zoppo, various artists).

New lineup and Recloose’s end (1986–1987)

From 1986, the core group was Crab alongside Owen If Rossiter (percussion; Discogs: 478437), multi-instrumentalist/guitarist Miles Miles (Discogs: 442917), and Kif Cole (Discogs: 478436). Andy Wilson (5) (Discogs: 974903) also appears in the Bourbonese Qualk members list.

Recloose Organisation evolved into New International Recordings in 1987. The self-titled Bourbonese Qualk LP (New International Recordings, NIR871, 1987) appeared as the first release under the successor imprint — 15 tracks including “Skin Deep,” “On Your Knees,” “Dream Decade,” “Shock Corridor.” Lineup: Crab and Tanza (transitional credit).

Tribal/industrial peak (1989)

My Government Is My Soul (FünfUndVierzig, 1989) is widely considered the defining statement of the second-phase lineup. Lineup confirmed: Crab (voice, electronics), Miles Miles (guitar, saxophone), Owen Rossiter (drums, percussion), Kif Cole (live sound). The group wrote and produced themselves. Eleven tracks including “Guilt,” “Let It Go,” “Ton Ton Macoute,” “Know Your Enemy,” “Éist.”

Dissolution (late 1990s – 2002)

The group remained active through the 1990s and into the early 2000s, playing any venue — both sides of the Iron Curtain — that would have them. They maintained full control of music, image, and graphics throughout. The band officially disbanded in late 2002 following the death of Miles Miles.
