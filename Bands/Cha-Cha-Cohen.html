Overview

Cha-Cha Cohen were a Glasgow-associated indie group formed in 1994 by three ex-Wedding Present members — Keith Gregory, Paul Dorrington, and Simon Smith — who recruited vocalist Jacqui Cohen (Jaqi Dulany) from New York noise-rock band The Dustdevils. After a 1995 debut single on Hemiola Records, they settled on Chemikal Underground, expanding the lineup with keyboard player Alan Thomas and releasing a self-titled album in 1998. Dorrington departed in 1999 (replaced by Tanya Mellot), and their final album All Artists Are Criminals followed in 2002, before Gregory and Cohen emigrated to Australia and the group dissolved. Their catalogue, concentrated in the 1990s, blends Art Rock and Indie Rock with traces of Trip Hop and Breakbeat.
