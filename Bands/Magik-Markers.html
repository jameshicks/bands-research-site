Overview

Magik Markers are an American noise rock band from Hartford, Connecticut, formed in 2001. The core lineup of Elisa Ambrogio, John Shaw, and Pete Nolan emerged after Leah Quimby departed in 2006. Working across noise, experimental, psychedelic rock, and avant-garde territory, they were most prolific in the 2000s (12 releases that decade) and have continued releasing into the 2020s. Their catalogue is split between their own Arbitrary Signs imprint — home to 8 releases spanning 2002–2024 — and Drag City, which handled 5 releases from 2009–2021; earlier records appeared on Ecstatic Peace!, Not Not Fun Records, and Hospital Productions. Lee Ranaldo and Aaron Mullan are each credited on 6 shared releases, pointing to sustained ties with both the Sonic Youth orbit and the wider underground.
