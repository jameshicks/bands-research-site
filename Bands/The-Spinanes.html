The Spinanes

US indie rock band formed in Portland, Oregon. Discogs artist ID: 362329. Also credited as “Spinanes”, “The Spinnanes”, “Spinnanes.” Active 1992–2000; the core and enduring project of songwriter/vocalist/guitarist Rebecca Gates.

Overview

The Spinanes began as a duo: Rebecca Gates on guitar and vocals, and Scott Plouf on drums. After two singles on the local Portland indie label IMP Records (4), Sub Pop signed them in 1992. Their debut album Manos (1993) reached number one on US college radio charts, driven by the single “Noel, Jonah And Me,” which received MTV 120 Minutes airplay. Rather than building on that momentum commercially, Gates and Plouf largely stepped back from the spotlight, preferring guest appearances and collaborative contributions over touring aggressively.

The second album Strand (1996) was self-produced by the duo and featured a notable guest: Elliott Smith sang backing vocals on two tracks. Shortly after its release, Plouf departed to join Built To Spill as a full member. Gates relocated from Portland to Chicago, where she assembled a new lineup: Joanna Bolme on guitar, bass, organ, and synthesizer, and Jerry Busher on drums. This version of The Spinanes recorded Arches And Aisles (1998) with John McEntire (of Tortoise) producing and mixing several tracks, and with guest appearances from Sam Prekop (The Sea And Cake) and a large ensemble of Chicago/Memphis-scene contributors. The band continued until 2000, when Gates wound things down and began releasing under her own name. In 2000 Merge Records issued Imp Years, a six-track collection of the pre-Sub Pop recordings made for IMP Records.

Members

The Spinanes went through two distinct lineup phases:

Original duo (1992–1996)

  • Rebecca Gates — vocals, guitar, piano, organ, mellotron, plate reverb; songwriter; all releases
  • Scott Plouf — drums, percussion, timpani, guitar (wah-wah); also trumpet on Imp Years

Expanded lineup (1997–2000)

  • Rebecca Gates — vocals, guitar, piano, organ, mellotron, keyboards, bass; songwriter; all releases
  • Joanna Bolme — guitar, bass, organ, Moog synthesizer (Arches And Aisles)
  • Jerry Busher — drums, percussion (Arches And Aisles, most tracks)

Additional members listed on Discogs: Jim Kimball (drums), Kendall Jane Meade — details and release appearances not confirmed in Discogs records.

Discography

Singles and Early Recordings

“Rummy” / “Hawaiian Baby” (1992, IMP Records, IMP008) — 7” single. The band’s debut release on Portland’s local IMP Records (Discogs: IMP Records (4)). Produced by John Moen and The Spinanes. Recorded by Pat Maley and Michael Lastra. Gates on guitar/vocals, Plouf on drums. Two tracks that would later resurface on Imp Years.

The Discogs record also references a second Imp single (“Suffice” / “Halloween Candy”) though it does not appear as a separate release entry — both tracks appear on Imp Years.

Compilation / Archive

Imp Years (2000, Merge Records MRG160) — six-track compilation of recordings made for IMP Records before the Sub Pop deal. Tracks: “Suffice,” “Halloween Candy,” “Rummy,” “Hawaiian Baby,” “Messy Shitty,” “Handful Of Hearts.” Recorded mainly by Pat Maley, with Michael Lastra on select tracks; John Moen recorded/assisted. “Messy Shitty” features additional players: Scott Fox (bass), Sean Croghan (guitar), Ted Miller (xylophone); “Handful Of Hearts” features Pat Maley (harmonica), Gates (piano), Plouf (trumpet), and is the sole Spinanes track credited to John Moen as songwriter (the others are all Gates originals). Released on Mac McCaughan and Laura Ballance’s Merge Records — the same label behind Superchunk.

Production and Studio Notes

Gates took on increasing creative control across the three albums. On Manos, Brian Paulson produced — consistent with Sub Pop’s practice of hiring established indie producers. By Strand, Gates was self-producing with assistance from a roster of collaborators working at different studios. By Arches And Aisles, she held sole Producer credit with Takino as assistant, and personally handled mixing credit on multiple tracks alongside the engineers.

Rebecca Gates Beyond The Spinanes

Gates’s non-Spinanes credits visible in the Discogs record include:

  • Backing vocals on Elliott Smith’s self-titled album (1995, Kill Rock Stars)
  • Design and photography credits on several band-era releases, including art direction on Arches And Aisles
  • Artwork credit on a Drag City release (Chestnut Station, 1998 — marquee logo)
  • Backing vocals on The Gap’s The Gap (2000, Jade Tree)
  • Her solo project Rebecca Gates And The Consortium, which released Ruby Series (2001, Badman Recording Co.) and The Float (2012, 12XU)

Gates also appears (as performer) on the Ben Lee/Elliott Smith–adjacent “Happyness” (2002, Wishing Tree Records) and a Wilco-related project (2003). She is credited with liner notes on the Elliott Smith posthumous release New Moon (2007, Domino) and additional vocals on the Beck Odelay reissue (2008, Geffen). She is also credited as a performer/vocalist on Quasi (2)‘s Quicksand/Cradlesnakes (2003, Thrill Jockey) — drums on that session are Janet Weiss’s.

She was also a member of the Cradle Robbers (Discogs group ID: 1143544), though no releases appear in the Discogs record under that name.
