Carter Tutti

British electronic duo — rebrand of Chris & Cosey for new material. Members: Chris Carter (2) and Cosey Fanni Tutti. Discogs artist ID: 188407.

Overview

Carter Tutti is the name used by Chris Carter and Cosey Fanni Tutti for studio and live work from the 2000s onward, separate from their Chris & Cosey archival catalog. Coolicon was released on The state51 Conspiracy in 2013.
