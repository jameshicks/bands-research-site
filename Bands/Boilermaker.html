Boilermaker

Discogs ID: 632957
Origin: Encinitas, CA
Active: 1993–2000
Genres: Emo, Post-Hardcore, Indie Rock

Indie/emo band from Encinitas, California (San Diego County). Rooted in the mid-1990s San Diego scene alongside Three Mile Pilot, Drive Like Jehu, and Rob Crow’s constellation of projects. Released two full-lengths on Goldenrod Records, two 7”s on Wrenched Records, and a split with Three Mile Pilot before dissolving c. 2000. A 2001 career compilation on Better Looking Records documented most of their catalog; a comprehensive Numero Group box set followed in 2025.

Boilermaker (debut 7”, 1994) — Discogs 1030365

Wrenched Records WR 004. Single-sided 7” at 33⅓ RPM. Two tracks: “Used To Be” / “Beseme Mucho You Fucker.” Lineup credited as: Richard Sanderson (guitar), Rob Crow (guitar), Terrin Durfey (bass/vocals), Timothy James Semple (drums). Cover layout by Timothy James Semple; photography by O’Connell Lingus.

Watercourse (1994) — Discogs 2721775

Goldenrod Records GR28. Debut LP; 10 tracks. Three-piece lineup (Sanderson / Durfey / Semple); George Sanderson (accordion, uncredited, track 10). Produced by Boilermaker and Mark Trombino; engineered by Trombino. Mastered by John Golden. Cover painting by Thomas H. Semple; back cover painting by James Durfey. Track 10 (“Dark Island”) co-written by David Silver (3) and Iain Maclachlan.

In Wallace’s Shadow (1996) — Discogs 2902529

Goldenrod Records GR52. Second LP; 10 tracks. Three-piece; Richard Sanderson also on piano. Guest vocal by Denver Lucas on “Slow Down.” Produced by Boilermaker and Mark Trombino; engineered and mixed by Trombino. Richard Sanderson created the cover painting; James Durfey the back cover.

Boilermaker / Three Mile Pilot split (1998) — Discogs 1046661

Slowdance Records SLOW 001. 7” at 45 RPM. Side A: Boilermaker, “Last On The Drive.” Side B: Three Mile Pilot, “In This Town I Awaken.” Boilermaker recorded by Gar Wood; TMP self-recorded. Photography on each side by Ezra Ace (Boilermaker) and Nick Ott (TMP). Three Mile Pilot lineup: Armistead Burwell Smith IV, Pall Jenkins, Tobias Nathaniel, Tom Zinser.

Leucadia (2001) — Discogs 2805325

Better Looking Records BLR009. Career compilation CD; 17 tracks drawing from Watercourse, In Wallace’s Shadow, the 1998 LP, plus “Whitewash” and “Cruel Heart” (not on prior albums). Mastered by Rafter Roberts. Photography by Ezra Ace Caraeff (cover), Raymond Potes (sunset), Aaron Reagin, Darius Shayegan. Title references the coastal community of Leucadia, also part of Encinitas.

Not Enough Time To Get Anything Halfway Done (2025) — Discogs 32833521

Numero Group NUM 228. Comprehensive box set: 4xLP reissues of Watercourse and In Wallace’s Shadow + LP of the 1998 Boilermaker LP + LP/7” of rarities. Includes previously unissued material (marked “Untitled 1998” on the Leucadia comp). Reissue produced by Adam Luksetich, Ken Shipley, and Rob Sevier for Numero Group. Lacquer cut by Robert Weston (Chicago Mastering Service). Liner notes by Nina Corcoran. Rob Crow contributes backing vocals on Drained Nonsense-era tracks.
