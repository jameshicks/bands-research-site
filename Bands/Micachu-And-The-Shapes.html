Micachu And The Shapes

Discogs artist ID: 8234107 Also credited as: Micachu & The Shapes, Micachu With The Shapes, Michachu, Shapes Origin: London, UK Active: 2009–2015 (as Micachu And The Shapes); renamed Good Sad Happy Bad in 2020 Labels: Rough Trade, Accidental

London noise-pop / experimental rock trio formed around Mica Levi (aka Micachu). Noted for homemade and repurposed instruments, lo-fi textures, and an approach to songwriting that collapsed pop hooks into abrasive, short-form structures. All three studio albums released on Rough Trade (often co-released on Accidental, Matthew Herbert’s label). In 2011 they collaborated with the London Sinfonietta for an orchestral reworking of their material. In 2020 the band continued under the new name Good Sad Happy Bad, with Raisa Khan stepping forward as lead vocalist.

Biography

Formation and Jewellery (2009)

Mica Levi had studied at the Guildhall School of Music and had been releasing material as a solo artist (Micachu) since the late 2000s, including the 2008 single “Golden Phone” on Accidental. The band crystallised around Levi, Khan, and Pell and debuted with Jewellery in February 2009 on Rough Trade / Accidental (RTRADCD500). The album was produced jointly by Levi and Matthew Herbert (credited as Matthew “Lemons” Herbert), who runs Accidental. It is unusual in the Discogs credits in that many tracks list only the “Micachu” artist ID (1172814) rather than the full band ID — reflecting either a recording period that predated the full band lineup or Discogs cataloguing variance.

Jewellery contains 13 tracks (with the UK pressing featuring a 20-minute silence followed by a hidden track, making position 13 a three-part sequence). Track 7, “Ship”, features Man Like Me (Discogs ID 457669) and has a co-writing credit by Johnny Langer (ID 1428491). On the credited tracks, Mica plays electric or acoustic guitar and lead vocals; Raisa plays synthesizer, zither, and percussion; Marc plays drums and percussion.

Around the same time (2009), the band contributed to an untitled CDr split EP (Not On Label) with Let’s Wrestle and The Laurel Collective, each contributing acoustic versions of tracks.

Chopped & Screwed with London Sinfonietta (2011)

The band’s second studio release came out of a live commission with the London Sinfonietta, recorded at Kings Place, London on 1 May 2010. The LP version (Chopped & Screwed (At Kings Place 01-05-10), RTRADLP612) was released on Rough Trade vinyl in 2011; a CDr promo circulated as Chopped & Screwed. The album is credited jointly to Micachu & The Shapes and London Sinfonietta. Nine tracks, all new material, genre-tagged as Electronic / Experimental. There are no individual production or engineering credits on the Discogs entries.

Individual London Sinfonietta players credited on the Discogs collaboration graph include: Timothy Lines, Alexander Piatti, Joan Atherton, Oliver Coates, Adam Wynter, Helen Keen, Paul Silverthorne, Laurent Quenelle, and David Sheppard (2).

Never (2012)

The second proper studio album, Never (Rough Trade / Accidental, RTRADCD642), was released in 2012. Recorded and mixed by Dilip Harris, with Mica Levi also credited as mixer. The album genre crosses Electronic, Rock, and Abstract/Experimental. Track 13, “Nothing”, features Wesley Gonzalez on vocals. Fourteen tracks total, running from minute-long sketches to a near-five-minute closer.

Good Sad Happy Bad (2015)

The final album under the Micachu And The Shapes name, released on Rough Trade vinyl (RTRADLP742) in 2015. Thirteen tracks; no additional production or engineering credits in Discogs beyond the band’s own written-by credit. Genre: Rock / Experimental.

Renaming to Good Sad Happy Bad (2020)

In 2020 the band renamed themselves Good Sad Happy Bad. Raisa Khan took lead vocals; Mica Levi stepped to guitar and occasional vocals; Marc Pell remained on percussion; CJ Calderwood joined as multi-instrumentalist.

Discogs ID Notes

The band has two active Discogs entries for Mica Levi’s persona:

  • Micachu (ID 1172814) — used on Jewellery primary credits and the Micachu solo discography
  • Mica Levi (ID 2576890) — used on production credits and her film-score work
  • Both refer to the same person; they share the realname “Mica Levi” / “Micaela Levi”
