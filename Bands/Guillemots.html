Guillemots

British indie rock band. Formed London, c. 2004. Led by Fyfe Dangerfield. Discogs artist ID: 537102.

Overview

Critically acclaimed indie group known for orchestral arrangements and Dangerfield’s expressive vocals. Hello Land! (CON150, 2012) was released on The state51 Conspiracy.
