Severed Heads

Discogs artist ID: 26212. Australian industrial / electronic group, Sydney. Active from late 1970s. Two releases on Red Flame in 1983–1984, including Since The Accident (RFA 32, 1983) — their UK/Europe pressing on Red Flame. Later associated with Nettwerk Records. One of the early acts in Australian industrial/experimental electronic music.
