Overview

Macha is an Athens, Georgia indie rock and post-rock trio — Joshua McKay, Kai Riedl, and Mischo McKay — whose catalogue of four releases appeared on Jetset Records and Bad News Records between 1998 and 2004, with a Numero Group reissue arriving in 2026. The band’s sound folds experimental textures, field recordings, and ethereal elements into a post-rock framework. Their closest recording relationships were with engineer Wes Martin and, notably, the Dallas group Bedhead — whose members Bubba and Matt Kadane each share six credits with Macha, suggesting a sustained cross-scene collaboration unusual for two bands from different regions.

Notes (web) — Macha / Bedhead collaboration backstory

How the two bands connected

The link between Macha and Bedhead was personal and predated both bands. The core of each group — Bubba Kadane and Matt Kadane (Bedhead’s songwriting brothers) and Joshua McKay and Mischo McKay (Macha’s founding siblings) — grew up together in Wichita Falls, TX and played together in high school. Austin Chronicle review, 16 June 2000

A second, more direct tie: Mischo McKay served as Bedhead’s drummer in the band’s early/formative years before he and Joshua McKay relocated to Athens, GA and founded Macha. Numero Group, “Indescribably Epic: The Tale of Bedhead”

The collaboration therefore did not require any label introduction or touring coincidence; it was a reunion of long-standing friends from a shared hometown.

How the recording came together

Bedhead initiated the project in 1999, shortly before the band disbanded. Matt Kadane and Bubba Kadane mailed an eight-track tape of skeletal, unfinished songs — mostly drum and guitar parts — to the McKays in Athens. The McKays completed the compositions by filling in the remaining spaces (including Javanese zither, Thai xylophone, and vibraphone), producing a set of five tracks that blend Bedhead’s minimalist guitar-slumber-pop with Macha’s gamelan-influenced textures. The liner notes describe it as “a long-distance collaboration.” Austin Chronicle review, 16 June 2000; bedheadband.com

Macha Loved Bedhead — Bedhead Loved Macha was released on Jetset Records on 25 April 2000.
