Odeurs

Discogs ID: 1220022 Also known as: Ramon Pipin’s Odeurs

French satirical new wave / post-punk collective, active primarily in the early-to-mid 1980s. Centred on Ramon Pipin (alias of Alain Ranval, Discogs ID 650247 / 575577).

Members included Klaus Blasquiz, Jean-Philippe Goude, Dominique Esnault, Gérard Prévost, Philippe Vauville, Shitty Télaouine, Laurent De Gaspéris, and others from the Zeuhl / chanson margins.
