ABC

Sheffield new wave / sophisti-pop group. Discogs artist ID: 23035

Overview

Formed in Sheffield in the early 1980s around vocalist Martin Fry and guitarist/keyboardist Mark White. Known for the 1982 debut album The Lexicon of Love (produced by Anne Dudley and Trevor Horn on ZTT’s parent Phonogram). Represented the more polished, orchestrated wing of Sheffield’s post-punk scene.
