Disconnection (2)

Avant-garde / disco / dub act. UK. Discogs artist ID: 425404. Active c. 1976–1984 (by Discogs years-active range).

Part of the Y Records house circle. Released the Bali Ha’i 12” (Y 32, 1982) and contributed “Bali Ha’i” to the label compilation Birth Of The Y (Y 33 1/3, 1982). The group appears to have functioned partly as a Y Records studio project or collective vehicle, not a stable touring band — Angela Jaeger (also of Pigbag and The Drowning Craze) is the sole confirmed member in Discogs, with additional personnel (synthesizers, guitar, strings, vocals) varying by release.

Their 1984 IRS Records single We Love You showed a move toward a lusher pop-orchestral sound with string arrangements and session vocalists, departing from the sparse Y Records house aesthetic.

Context and Y Records Position

Bali Ha’i (Y 32) appears on the Birth Of The Y compilation as track A1 — the opening cut on the label’s own cross-roster showcase. This positioning suggests some degree of label confidence in the track as a representative piece. The Rodgers & Hammerstein source material (a song from a mainstream 1949 American musical) treated in a dub/disco mode is consistent with the label’s broader willingness to collide genres and contexts.

Angela Jaeger’s cross-membership in The Drowning Craze and later Pigbag (from late 1982) suggests Disconnection (2) may have been partly a solo or duo project for her within the Y Records studio context, rather than a full band with a fixed lineup.
