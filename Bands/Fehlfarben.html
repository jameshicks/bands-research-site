Fehlfarben

Discogs ID: 68809
Origin: Düsseldorf, Germany

German NDW band, Düsseldorf. Kurt Dahlke (alias Pyrolator) played keyboards. Known for Monarchie Und Alltag (1980), a key NDW record. Connected to the Ata Tak / Düsseldorf scene. Formed directly out of Mittagspause — core members Peter Hein (vocals), Markus Oehlen (drums), and Thomas Schwebel (guitar) all came from Mittagspause. Several Mittagspause songs (Militürk, Herrenreiter, Ernstfall) were reworked for Fehlfarben records. Xao Seffcheque also a member.
