The Beasts Of Bourbon

Australian rock band. Discogs artist ID: 347188

A loose aggregation of Melbourne underground musicians active from the early 1980s, anchored by vocalist Tex Perkins and featuring members drawn from Kim Salmon And The Surrealists, The Johnnys, and the broader scene. Tony Cohen worked on multiple releases. Other members included Kim Salmon, Spencer P. Jones, Brad Shepherd, Charlie Owen, Boris Sujdovic, and James Baker (2).
