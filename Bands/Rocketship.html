Overview

Rocketship is a bedroom pop project rooted in ’60s melody, shoegaze texture, and psychedelic drone, based around songwriter and guitarist Dustin Reske. The group began around 1993 with a full lineup including keyboardist Heidi Barney, bassist Verna Brock, and drummer Jim Rivas (of Holiday Flyer); by 1996 Reske was largely writing and recording on his own, with rotating performers in support. The catalogue is concentrated in the 1990s across small labels including Cardinal, Drive-In, and I Wish I Was A Slumberland Record, with a single release in the 2000s before a renewed run in the 2010s on Nonstop Co-op and Darla Records; Ellen Osborn joined as vocalist from 2015. Collaborations with Estonian indie-pop outfit Pia Fraus and San Francisco shoegaze act Henry’s Dress point to a wider transatlantic scene network.
