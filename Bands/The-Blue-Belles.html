The Blue Belles

Discogs ID: 3122859 Origin: Philadelphia / Trenton, NJ, USA

Early name for the group that became Patti LaBelle And The Bluebells. Same core lineup: Patti LaBelle, Nona Hendryx, Sarah Dash, Cindy Birdsong. The name shifted to “Patti LaBelle And The Blue Belles” / “Patti LaBelle And The Bluebells” as Patti’s name gained prominence on sleeves.

Naming on Releases

Discogs splits these credits across two artist entries (IDs 3122859 and 403181), and the sleeve anv fields reveal the actual progression:

Pattern: The “The Blue Belles” / “The Blue-Belles” credit (without Patti’s name) appears only on the very earliest 1962 Newtown pressings. By the time of “I Sold My Heart To The Junkman” alternate pressings in 1962, Patti’s name was already being foregrounded on the sleeve. From 1962 onward all pressings use some variant of “Patti/Patty LaBelle And The Blue Belles/Bluebells.” The spelling of “LaBelle” also varied (“Labelle,” “La Belle”) through at least 1963; Atlantic settled on “Patti LaBelle And The Bluebells” from 1965.
