Angels of Light

Post-folk / experimental. New York City. Active 1999–2010. Led by Michael Gira following the dissolution of Swans. Also billed as The Angels Of Light. Discogs artist ID: 278926.

Overview

Angels of Light was Michael Gira’s primary project between the two eras of Swans. Formed in 1997 after he disbanded Swans, and active through 2010 when Swans reformed. Where Swans had been rooted in noise-rock brutalism, Angels of Light leaned toward acoustic folk structures, orchestral texture, and chamber-like arrangements — though Gira’s emotional intensity and lyrical darkness remained constant.

All six studio albums appeared on Gira’s own Young God Records. The ensemble was not a fixed band but a rotating cast of New York collaborators and fellow Young God artists. Key recurring players across the catalog include guitarist Kristof Hahn (also in Swans), multi-percussionist Larry Mullins (alias Toby Dammit; also Swans and Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds), drummer/percussionist Phil Puleo (Cop Shoot Cop, Swans), bassist/multi-instrumentalist Dana Schechter (Bee and Flower, Insect Ark), and drummer-percussionist Thor Harris (Shearwater). The later albums — particularly The Angels Of Light Sing “Other People” (2005) and We Are Him (2007) — saw the Brooklyn band Akron-Family integrated as core instrumentalists, blurring the line between the two projects. Engineer and mixer Martin Bisi (BC Studios) anchored the early recordings; producer-engineer Bryce Goggin took over mixing duties on the last two albums.

Gira was the sole consistent member: singer, acoustic and electric guitarist, sole writer and producer on all releases.
