The Associates

Scottish post-punk / synth-pop duo/group. Active early 1980s. Discogs ID: needs lookup.

Overview

UK post-punk group associated with the early 1980s British new-wave scene. Notable connection to the MatM/Toronto milieu: Martha Ladly (ex-Martha And The Muffins) became a member of The Associates after departing the Muffins c.1981.

Connections

  • Martha Ladly — member post-Muffins
  • Martha And The Muffins — Martha Ladly connection

Other Notes

Lead vocalist Billy MacKenzie died January 1997; his posthumous album Beyond The Sun was released on Nude Records later that year.
