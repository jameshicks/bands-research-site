Electric Callas

New Wave / punk band formed in Lyon, France in 1976. Active approximately 1976–1981. Discogs artist ID: 656093.

Overview

Electric Callas were a Lyon-based post-punk/New Wave group who released two records between 1979 and 1981 — a 12” EP on Pathé and a 12” single on Celluloid — before disbanding. Three of their four members (Marie Girard, Jean-Marc Vallod, and Philippe Girard) had already been active in Marie Et Les Garçons, one of the flagship French post-punk groups associated with Michel Esteban’s SPY Records and then ZE Records. The Celluloid release placed Electric Callas in the orbit of Celluloid Records, the Franco-American label that served as the primary European distribution point for the ZE/downtown New York aesthetic.

The band’s vocalist and primary songwriter was Jangil Callas (real name Jean-Gilles Posada), who also handled graphic design on the debut EP. The “Callas” surname adopted by Jangil is a reference to opera singer Maria Callas; the band name plays on this, inverting the classical connotation with punk electricity.

Compilation appearances confirmed in the Discogs database include Punk 45 - Les Punks The French Connection (Soul Jazz Records, 2016), where “Kill Me Two Times” appears on side B.

Connections

  • Marie Et Les Garçons — three of the four members (Marie Girard, Jean-Marc Vallod, Philippe Girard) were also in this group, Michel Esteban’s flagship signing; Marie Et Les Garçons released on Celluloid and ZE Records
  • Celluloid Records — the Winner single (1981) carries Celluloid’s CEL catalog prefix, situating Electric Callas in the same label network as James Chance, Metal Urbain, and the downtown New York scene
  • Yves Chaland — the Belgian cartoonist designed the logo for Winner; Chaland was a rising figure in the French bande dessinée world at the time (he died in 1990); his visual involvement points to crossover between the French punk scene and the BD milieu
  • SPY Records — indirect connection via the Marie Et Les Garçons members, who had released on SPY before joining Electric Callas
  • Patrick Vidal — Marie Et Les Garçons vocalist; featured on the 2008 re-recording of “W.S.B.” released on the Des Jeunes Gens Mödernes compilation

Web sources

Formation and original lineup

Electric Callas was formed in 1976 around Jean-Gilles Posada (Jangil). The original 1976 lineup included Philippe Girard (drums), Jean-Marc Vallod (guitar), and Frankie Fender (bass). In 1978, Gino Ferrucci replaced the departed Frankie Fender on bass. Marie Girard joined on drums during the later period, after she left Marie Et Les Garçons due to “musical incompatibility” during the Divorce album sessions in early 1979. (rockmadeinfrance.com)

This clarifies the Discogs gap: Vallod and Girard (Philippe) appear to have been in the original 1976–1977 lineup but had departed before the recorded releases of 1979–1981. Marie Girard’s drums credit on Winner (1981) reflects her joining after leaving Marie Et Les Garçons, not simultaneous membership.

Live history and pre-release activity

The band was active and noticed on the live circuit from 1977, described in contemporary press as sometimes “provocative.” A key event: Electric Callas performed at the “Le Rock d’Ici à l’Olympia” festival in Paris on July 8–10, 1978 — a three-day punk showcase that also featured Marie Et Les Garçons, Stinky Toys, Starshooter, Diesel, Bijou, Asphalt Jungle, Guilty Razors, and Les Lou’s. Their performance of “I Wanna Be Your Dog” (Stooges cover) is documented. Following the festival, Pathé Marconi signed them and offered three studio days to record So Chic (released 1979). (bm-lyon.fr via rockmadeinfrance.com; monstres-sacres.blogspot.com)

An earlier compilation appearance exists: “Kill Me Two Times” appeared on the Skydog label compilation Skydog Commando (SGSC 0018, 1978) as track A3. Skydog was Marc Zermati’s label. The liner notes described Electric Callas as “Les Stooges de Lyon.” (dieordiy2.blogspot.com; linflux.com)

Electric Callas also performed at or around the Frigo venue in Lyon, which served as a creative hub for the scene. The Frigo collective later organized the “Nuits bleues” festival (1982) and Radio Bellevue pirate radio. (petit-bulletin.fr)

Compilation appearances and retrospective coverage

“So Chic” was cited in the French music press as “an essential part of the new wave movement” and has appeared on at least 11 CD compilations, including works connected to Étienne Daho’s Frenchy But Chic (1994) column/project and the Agnès B / Born Bad Des Jeunes Gens Mödernes compilation (2008). The 2008 edition included a new recording: “W.S.B. (version 2008)” featuring Patrick Vidal of Marie Et Les Garçons. (senscritique.com; last.fm)

Other documented compilation appearances include La Nuit Punk de l’Olympia (1999 CD reissue, Pathé), Les Plus Grands Succès du Punk, and Les Punks: The French Connection (The First Wave Of French Punk 1977–80). (discogs.com)

A separate single titled “Talk Talk”, credited to Electric Callas with Howie B and JG Callas, is referenced on Last.fm and chartsinfrance.net. Release date unconfirmed; likely 1990s. This indicates at least one mid-period release beyond the two Discogs-catalogued records. (last.fm)

Libération newspaper famously declared Lyon “capitale du rock” in 1978; Electric Callas, Marie Et Les Garçons, and Starshooter are consistently cited as the three groups that earned Lyon that designation. (rockmadeinfrance.com)
