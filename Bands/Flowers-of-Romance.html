Flowers of Romance

Short-lived proto-punk/punk project active summer 1976 to January–February 1977 in London. The band never played live and never recorded. It is remembered chiefly as a rehearsal-room collective that funnelled several members into landmark punk and post-punk groups. The name was suggested by Johnny Rotten and was later adopted for an early Sex Pistols on-stage jam, a Viv Albertine song on The Slits’ Return of the Giant Slits (1981), and a 1981 Public Image Ltd album and its title track.

Note: Not to be confused with the Public Image Ltd album or track “Flowers of Romance” (1981).

Overview

Formed in the summer of 1976 by Jo Faull (guitar) and Sarah “Rouge” Hall (bass) — then girlfriends of Sex Pistols members Steve Jones and Paul Cook respectively — the band rehearsed in squats, including Davis Road and later at Rehearsal Rehearsals (The Clash’s base in Camden). Viv Albertine described it as a “bedroom band”; the group attempted Ramones covers but, by her account, produced no finished songs: “we emerged at the end of it absolutely white, and without one song. Nothing.” (Andrew Gallix blog, drawing on England’s Dreaming by Jon Savage)

The band dissolved in January–February 1977. The proximate cause was Sid Vicious committing to the Sex Pistols (Glen Matlock’s departure was announced 28 February 1977; Vicious had been in talks from at least 13 February 1977) and Kenny Morris simultaneously leaving to rehearse with Siouxsie and the Banshees. Punk 77 cites February 1977 as the dissolution month; Wikipedia gives January 1977 — likely the period when the decision was reached rather than formally announced. See Conflict note below.

Viv Albertine — confirmed

Yes: multiple independent sources confirm Viv Albertine as a core member of the original 1976 lineup, not merely an associate. She is cited as a founding guitarist alongside Jo Faull and Sarah Hall. (Wikipedia, as of 2026-07-10; Punk 77; Andrew Gallix citing England’s Dreaming)

Keith Levene — confirmed

Yes: confirmed member. The Punk 77 article and Wikipedia both list Levene as a guitarist. The Keith Levene vault page already notes his brief participation “late 1976” citing fodderstompf.com and Wikipedia/PIL. (Wikipedia, as of 2026-07-10; Punk 77)

Marco Pirroni — not confirmed as member

Marco Pirroni was apparently arranged to join on bass but the rehearsal never took place because of Sid Vicious’s arrest following the 100 Club glass-throwing incident (September 1976). He is therefore not counted as an actual member. (Punk 77; Sceneroller)

Conflicts

  • Dissolution month: Wikipedia (The Flowers of Romance (British band), as of 2026-07-10) says the band “ended in January 1977”. Punk 77 (The Flowers of Romance) says “February” 1977. The Sex Pistols timeline (Glen Matlock departure announced 28 February 1977; Vicious radio interview claiming audition 13 February 1977) is more consistent with February being the actual break-up month, with January possibly reflecting when internal discussions began. Neither date is contradicted by a primary source.
  • Formation year conflict (context note): The parent brief flagged a discrepancy between “c.1977–78” (in the vault active-dates field) and “c.1976–77” (research notes). Both Wikipedia and Punk 77 confirm formation in mid/summer 1976 and dissolution in January–February 1977 — so the correct active span is 1976–1977, not 1977–78. The “c.1977–78” reference appears to have been an error in an earlier vault entry and is corrected by the current page.
