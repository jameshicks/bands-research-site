M.A.T.H.E.S.

Discogs artist ID: 2071978 Name variations: M.A.T.H.E.S, The M.A.T.H.E.S. Origin: London, UK Type: Experimental alter-ego / alias identity Alias of: Micachu And The Shapes (Discogs ID 8234107) and Good Sad Happy Bad (Discogs ID 8207804)

Overview

M.A.T.H.E.S. is listed on Discogs as an “experimental alter-ego” of Micachu And The Shapes, and is registered as a mutual alias shared by all three names in the Micachu / Good Sad Happy Bad lineage. The membership is identical to the founding Micachu And The Shapes trio: Mica Levi (Discogs ID 2576890), Raisa Khan (Discogs ID 1428490), and Marc Pell (Discogs ID 1428489).

Discogs documentation for this name is sparse. The alias relationship is asserted in both directions — Micachu And The Shapes lists M.A.T.H.E.S. as an alias, Good Sad Happy Bad lists M.A.T.H.E.S. as an alias, and M.A.T.H.E.S. in turn lists both of them as aliases — but there is no released recording credited to M.A.T.H.E.S. as a primary artist.

Interpretation

The M.A.T.H.E.S. alias appears to have been used, at minimum, as a production credit tag for the trio acting collectively — distinct from Mica Levi working as Micachu solo. The name surfaces in 2009, the same year as the Kwesachu mixtape and the Micachu And The Shapes debut Jewellery, suggesting it was part of an early period of name experimentation.

What “M.A.T.H.E.S.” stands for, if anything, is not documented in Discogs. The Discogs profile describes it only as an “experimental alter-ego.” Whether the name was ever used in a live or performance context, or whether any other recordings exist under this alias outside the Discogs database, is unknown from available data.
