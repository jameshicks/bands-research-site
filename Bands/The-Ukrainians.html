The Ukrainians

British band playing Ukrainian folk-influenced punk and rock. Discogs artist ID: 418915 (on Cooking Vinyl 1991–2004, 31 releases).

Formed from members of the Wedding Present scene. Noted for Pisni Iz The Smiths (FRY CD 023, 1992) — Smiths songs performed in Ukrainian — and Vorony (1993). Associated with Cooking Vinyl through the early-to-mid 1990s.
