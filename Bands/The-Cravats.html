The Cravats

Post-punk / DIY band, Redditch, UK. Active 1977–1984 (later reformed). Discogs artist ID: 261869.

Overview

Unconventional post-punk outfit notable for incorporating saxophone heavily into a punk framework. Led by The Shend (vocals) and Svor Naan (bass). Released on Small Wonder Records and their own Dining Out Records. Later released on Crass Records. Featured here with “Situations Vacant”. Reformed periodically; The Shend later had an acting career.
