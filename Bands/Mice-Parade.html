Overview

Mice Parade is the primary solo project of Adam Pierce. The name is an anagram of “Adam Pierce.” On record, Pierce plays nearly all instruments himself — drums, guitar, vibraphone, marimba, mbira, various synthesis, harp (cheng), Rhodes, bass — and typically engineers, produces, and mixes his own material. The project debuted on FatCat Records in 1998 and has released regularly on FatCat and Pierce’s own Bubble Core Records ever since.

Despite being fundamentally a solo project, the Discogs member list credits Caroline Lufkin, Doug Scharin, and Joshua Larue as members — suggesting a live/touring band form has existed alongside the studio recordings.

Per the Discogs artist profile: “Mice Parade is electronic music without electronics. It sounds like electronic music, but in fact hardly any samples are used on his albums. All the instruments on the albums Mokoondi and Ramda, for example, are played live and were recorded in one take. Most of the instruments were played by Adam Pierce himself.”
