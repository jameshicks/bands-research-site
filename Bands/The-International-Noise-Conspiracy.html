The International Noise Conspiracy

Swedish garage punk / rock band from Umeå, Sweden. Discogs artist ID: 258224. Active 1998–2009. Also known as T(I)NC, TINC, The (International) Noise Conspiracy.

Overview

Formed in Umeå in 1998 by Dennis Lyxzén and Lars Strömberg immediately after Refused dissolved. The project was built around a shared fascination with a Phil Ochs quote — “the perfect rock outfit would be a combination of Elvis and Che Guevara” — and melded the soul and garage rock of the mid-1960s with late-70s punk energy and radical leftist politics.

Their sound was a sharp contrast to Refused’s post-hardcore complexity: shorter, garage-inflected songs indebted to the MC5, Stooges, and 60s soul, threaded with sloganeering Situationist politics. They released prolifically across international DIY and indie labels before signing to Epitaph for Survival Sickness (2000) and Burning Heart for A New Morning, Changing Weather (2001) and Armed Love (2004). The final studio album The Cross Of My Calling appeared on Burning Heart in 2008. The band dissolved in 2009.

Members

  • Dennis Lyxzén — lead vocals, harmonica
  • Lars Strömberg — guitar, vocals; co-founder
  • Inge Johansson — bass (also in Against Me!, Totalt Jävla Mörker, Knugen Faller)
  • Ludwig Dahlberg — drums (later drummed for Indochine)
  • Sara Almgren — vocals (also in Masshysteri, INVSN, The Vicious)

Key Release: Survival Sickness (2000)

Discogs release ID: 508394. Produced and mixed by Jari Haapalainen. The core band credited collectively as “The (International) Noise Conspiracy Collective”: Dennis Lyxzén, Inge Johansson, Lars Strömberg, Ludwig Dahlberg, Sara Almgren, plus extended collective members Al Ruel, Axel Stattin, Erik Åsell, Mårten Sahlin, Patrick Daly, Robin Hedberg.

Key Release: A New Morning, Changing Weather (2001)

Discogs release ID: 505854. Produced by Jari Haapalainen. Guest appearances: Björn Yttling (horn), Sven-Eric Dahlberg (Rhodes), Jonas Kullhammar (saxophone), Markus Olsson (saxophone).

Key Release: Armed Love (2004)

Discogs release ID: 419981. Produced by Rick Rubin — a high-profile pairing. Additional musicians: Billy Preston (keyboards, tracks 4–6, 10), Benmont Tench (keyboards, tracks 2, 3, 8, 9), Jonas Kullhammar (keyboards and saxophone), Lenny Castro (percussion). Backing vocals from Charlotte Hatherley and Sam Velde. Mixed by Rich Costey. Photography by Glen E. Friedman. This was the band’s most commercially ambitious record and their most sonically polished.
