Jets To Brazil

Discogs ID: 138072 Active: 1997–2003 Origin: New York

Indie rock band formed by Blake Schwarzenbach and Jeremy Chatelain in 1997 after Jawbreaker’s dissolution. Released three studio albums on Jade Tree, all produced by J. Robbins at Inner Ear Studio and mastered by Alan Douches. Split after a final tour in 2003.

2003 Split

Chris Daly departed the band in fall 2002, shortly after Perfecting Loneliness was recorded. In an interview with No Echo, Daly described his exit as amicable and self-initiated: “It had run its course, and ceased to be fun/rewarding for me by that time.” He cited exhaustion with van touring and wanting “something more stable, grounded, and consistent.” He noted there was no significant conflict: “I left amicably, and they got a replacement drummer. They just stopped and that was it.” [source: https://www.noecho.net/interviews/chris-daly-ressurection-108-texas-is-the-reason-jets-to-brazil]

The remaining members brought in Matt Torrey (ex-MK Ultra) on drums and completed a final tour in summer 2003 supporting Perfecting Loneliness, the dates having been delayed roughly nine months after the album’s October 2002 release. [source: https://www.punknews.org/article/5852/jets-to-brazil-back-on-tour]

From Blake Schwarzenbach’s side, a November 2012 American Songwriter interview identified two additional contributing factors: burnout with the write-record-tour cycle (“tired of the write-record-tour-repeat grind”), and audience behaviour during Jets shows — fans still calling out Jawbreaker requests — which he found frustrating. The Jawbreaker-requests dynamic is independently corroborated by a contemporaneous show review from a June 30, 2003 Cambridge, MA date (The Middle East), which noted audience members shouting for Jawbreaker songs throughout the set. [source: https://toomuchrock.com/shows/jets.html] After the 2003 tour ended, the band stopped without a formal announcement. Schwarzenbach subsequently stepped away from music, earned a graduate degree, and taught English composition and literature at Hunter College, which he described as making him “feel like a legitimate citizen for the first time.” [source: https://americansongwriter.com/blake-schwarzenbach-remembering-to-forget/ — published November 2012]

No official statement from the band or Jade Tree appears to have been issued at the time.

Post-Split

  • Blake Schwarzenbach: Taught at Hunter College (NYC); formed The Thorns of Life (c. 2009), then Forgetters (2011 onward, with former Against Me! drummer George Rebelo).
  • Chris Daly: Reunited with Texas Is The Reason for reunion shows; later joined High Disciple.
  • Jeremy Chatelain: Recorded solo as Cub Country (Future Farmer Recordings 2004; Broken Horse 2005; Cargo Records 2006; Daniel 13 Press 2011; 6131 Records 2013). Also listed in Discogs as member of Fan Modine and Geometry.
