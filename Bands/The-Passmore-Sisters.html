Overview

The Passmore Sisters were a Bradford, England indie rock and pop group formed in 1983 and active until 1988. In that span they released four singles and an LP on Sharp Records, and recorded three BBC Radio 1 sessions — two for the John Peel Show (1985 and 1986) and one for the Janice Long Show (1987). The core lineup paired Martin Sadofski on vocals and Howi Taylor on bass with a rotating guitar slot (Peter Richardson, then Brian E. Roberts) and drums (Adrian Lee, later Robert Grace). Their entire catalogue sits in the 1980s decade, with a sole later credit appearing on Vegetable Records in 2018, some thirty years after the band’s original run.

Notes (web)

2018 Vegetable Records release: nature and circumstances

The Original Rock ‘n’ Roll Chair (Vegetable Records, released 26 October 2018) is an archival compilation, not a reunion or new recordings. It collects the band’s singles and B-sides from 1985–1987 (12 tracks on the digital/streaming release; the CD digipak adds 7 further tracks including early demos and the extended 12” version of at least one track).

The band’s own Bandcamp bio describes being “woken by the internet in 2018”: sustained online interest in their 1980s output — including enquiries from Japan — prompted them to compile the existing recordings for a proper release. Howard Taylor (bass) is quoted in a Backseat Mafia feature (March 2021) saying the album “kept popping its head up occasionally over the years and then the internet arrived and people from Japan were asking about it.” The band reconnected partly via Instagram.

The title comes from an artwork by Pete Richardson (original guitarist) made during his student days in Liverpool — it was not a band name or release title used in the 1980s.

The ℗ & © 2018 credit is held by Vegetable Records (3); the recordings themselves originate from the Sharp Records era.
