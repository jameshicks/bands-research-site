David Thomas And Two Pale Boys

Discogs ID: 1243718
Name variations: David Thomas & The Two Pale Boys · David Thomas & Two Pale Boys · 2pbs
Active: 1996–2004 (studio releases); later live activity documented

Overview

David Thomas And Two Pale Boys was a trio formed by David Thomas (2) — vocalist and co-founder of Pere Ubu — with British trumpeter Andy Diagram and guitarist/electronics musician Keith Moliné. The group served as Thomas’s primary recording and performing ensemble during the late 1990s and early 2000s, operating in parallel with the reformed Pere Ubu.

The band’s approach was deliberately scaled down from Pere Ubu’s “Hollywood blockbuster on a CinemaScope screen” aesthetic. Thomas described the Two Pale Boys projects as “indie arthouse films”: spare, spontaneous, and improvisation-centred. Both Diagram and Moliné made extensive use of MIDI and electronics, giving the trio a broader tonal palette than the instrumentation — trumpet plus guitar — might suggest. Diagram looped his trumpet through harmonizers and echo machines to build layered brass soundscapes; Moliné deployed MIDI guitar, bass, violin, and sampler. Thomas sang, played melodeon, musette, and acted as producer and often mixer and masterer.

The studio albums were recorded self-to-tape by the band members themselves, with mixing and engineering handled at Suma Recording Studio in Painesville, Ohio, by Paul Hamann — Thomas’s long-standing engineer from the Pere Ubu years. Packaging across all four Cooking Vinyl and Glitterhouse albums was handled by John Thompson (17).

Thomas died on 23 April 2025 in Brighton at the age of 71 from kidney disease complications, according to Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Thomas_(musician)).

See also Two Pale Boys (Discogs ID: 291412) — the Diagram/Moliné duo credit used when Thomas is not billed as primary.
