North Sea Radio Orchestra

English chamber-pop / art-rock ensemble based in London. Discogs artist ID: 616338.

Biography

Formation and Early Work (2005–2008)

The North Sea Radio Orchestra (NSRO) formed in London in the early 2000s around guitarist, arranger, and director Craig Fortnam and pianist William D. Drake. Both were already members of Cardiacs — the sprawling, cult British band around Tim Smith (Cardiacs) — and brought that ensemble’s appetite for complex, English-inflected music to a chamber context.

Their debut release, the EP The Flower (2005, Oof! Records), was composed entirely by Craig Fortnam and produced by James Larcombe. It demonstrated the core NSRO aesthetic immediately: guitar and chamber woodwinds setting settings of Victorian and Edwardian poetry. The self-titled debut album followed in 2006, again on Oof! Records, and expanded the palette considerably — with settings of Tennyson, Longfellow, Yeats, Hardy, and William Johnson Cory alongside original Fortnam and Drake compositions. The album credits reveal the early ensemble: Nicola Baigent (clarinet, bass clarinet), Harry Escott (cello), Luke Crookes (bassoon, credited on later releases as Luke Crookes), Brian Wright (violin), Sharron Fortnam (lead vocals and lyrics on some tracks), and a North Sea Chorus that included Kavus Torabi, William D. Drake, Melanie Woods, and Suzy Kirby. Piano duties on the debut were handled by James Larcombe and Ben Davies rather than Drake directly.

The EP The End Of Chimes (2007) and the album Birds (2008) continued on Oof! Records. The End Of Chimes features Craig Fortnam and James Larcombe sharing writing and arranging duties; it was produced by Mark Cawthra, who also engineered the debut album and is a Cardiacs alumnus.

The Household Mark era (2011–2016)

In 2011 NSRO moved to their own imprint, The Household Mark, for I A Moon (cat. THM001). The album was self-produced by Craig Fortnam (who also handled recording) and mastered by Mark Cawthra. The ensemble on this record shows consolidation around a stable core: Fortnam (guitar, voice, organ, percussion), Sharron Fortnam (lead vocals), William D. Drake (absent from the direct credits on this release — piano and organ credited to Ben Davies and James Larcombe), Luke Crookes (bassoon), Harry Escott (cello), Nicola Baigent (clarinet, bass clarinet), Brian Wright (violin, viola), Hugh Wilkinson (percussion), James Larcombe (monosynth, organ, hurdy gurdy), and Dug Parker (voice). Tracklist includes “Morpheus Miracle Maker,” “Heavy Weather,” “Berliner Luft,” and “When Things Fall Apart.”

Dronne followed in 2016, also on The Household Mark. Discogs credits for this release are sparse; no additional personnel are listed beyond the NSRO primary credit.

Folly Bololey and Dark Companion (2019)

Folly Bololey (Songs From Robert Wyatt’s Rock Bottom) (2019, Dark Companion) is the ensemble’s most high-profile release — a complete re-staging of Robert Wyatt’s 1974 landmark Rock Bottom, recorded live in Italy. It was co-credited to North Sea Radio Orchestra, John Greaves, and Annie Barbazza, making all three primary artists.

Craig Fortnam arranged and directed. Annie Barbazza sang lead vocal and played Farfisa organ. John Greaves played bass guitar and sang on several tracks, also contributing spoken word. William D. Drake played piano and Farfisa organ, and sang. Laurent Valéro played violin, viola, recorder, and bass recorder. Cheb Nettles played drums. Luke Crookes (bassoon), Harry Escott (cello), and Nicola Baigent (clarinet, bass clarinet) completed the chamber section. Tommaso Franguelli added vibraphone and percussion.

The record was produced in Italy; executive production by Massimo Marchini (Dark Companion label head). Robert Wyatt contributed liner notes, as did novelist Jonathan Coe.

Special Powers (2025)

Special Powers (2025, Believers Roast) is the most recent NSRO album, moving to a new label with ties to the Canterbury/progressive continuum. All music is composed by Craig Fortnam. The Discogs credit listing is minimal — Fortnam (composed by) and Alberto Callegari (mastered by).

Connections

  • Cardiacs — Craig Fortnam, William D. Drake, Kavus Torabi, Sharron Fortnam, Melanie Woods, and Mark Cawthra are all Cardiacs alumni; the Cardiacs overlap is the single most important background connection for NSRO
  • Knifeworld — Craig Fortnam and Kavus Torabi are both members; Melanie Woods also appears
  • Arch Garrison — Craig Fortnam and James Larcombe; the acoustic duo project running in parallel to NSRO
  • Henry Cow — John Greaves’s original group; connects Folly Bololey to the Canterbury/avant-rock lineage
  • National Health — John Greaves also a member
  • Robert Wyatt — Rock Bottom (1974) was the source material for Folly Bololey; Wyatt contributed liner notes to the 2019 recording
  • Dark Companion — Italian label run by Massimo Marchini; specialises in progressive and chamber music

Name Variations

Discogs lists the name variation “North Sea Radio Orchestra And Chorus” (reflecting the choral dimension of the self-titled LP).
