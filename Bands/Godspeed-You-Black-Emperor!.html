Godspeed You Black Emperor!

Discogs ID: 280442 (also listed on Discogs as “Godspeed You! Black Emperor” — same artist)
Formed: 1994, Montreal, Quebec, Canada
Status: Active (hiatus 2004–2009; reunited 2010)
Genres: Post-rock, experimental, drone
Also known as: GY!BE, GYBE, Thee Godspeed You Black Emperor!, God’s Pee

Instrumental (or near-instrumental) Montreal post-rock collective formed in 1994. Originally a smaller project credited to founding member Efrim Menuck alone, GY!BE expanded into a nine- to ten-piece ensemble — guitars, basses, violin, drums, cello, and 16mm film projections — and became one of the defining acts of the international post-rock scene in the late 1990s and early 2000s. All studio recordings have been released on Constellation Records (Montreal) except two early releases on Kranky (Chicago) and a 1998 split with Fly Pan Am. The band went on hiatus in 2004 and returned in 2010/2012. As of 2024 they remain active.

Note: The 1994 cassette All Lights Fucked On The Hairy Amp Drooling is a solo Efrim Menuck project credited on Discogs as “God Speed You Black Emperor!” (ID: 10789969) — a separate entry. The band proper begins with the 1997 LP.

Connections

  • Constellation Records — primary label throughout career; label was founded in 1997 by Ian Ilavsky and Don Wilkie, not by GY!BE
  • Kranky — Chicago label; co-released Slow Riot and Lift Yr. Skinny Fists
  • Efrim Menuck — founding member; also founded A Silver Mt. Zion and All Hands_Make Light
  • David Bryant — founding member; also of Hiss Tracts and Set Fire To Flames
  • Thierry Amar — bassist; co-founded A Silver Mt. Zion, Black Ox Orkestar; member of Land Of Kush; co-runs Hotel2Tango studio
  • Sophie Trudeau — violinist; also of A Silver Mt. Zion, Set Fire To Flames, Hrsta, Et Sans, The Mile End Ladies String Auxiliary
  • Aidan Girt — drummer; also of Exhaust, Set Fire To Flames; solo as 1-Speed Bike
  • Roger Tellier-Craig — guitarist 1999–2003; also of Fly Pan Am, Set Fire To Flames, Et Sans; solo as Le Révélateur
  • Howard Bilerman — key recording/mix engineer; co-founder of Hotel2Tango with Menuck and Amar; also played drums on Arcade Fire’s Funeral
  • Harris Newman | Grey Market Mastering — mastering engineer for all post-2012 releases; also mastered many other Constellation releases
  • Steve Albini — recorded Yanqui U.X.O. at Electrical Audio, Chicago
  • Jace Lasek — recorded/mixed G_d’s Pee (2021) and No Title (2024); of The Besnard Lakes
  • Karl Lemieux — 16mm film projections collaborator; visual dimension of live shows and artwork (2012–present)
  • A Silver Mt. Zion — sister project founded by Menuck; shares Trudeau, Amar as members
  • Fly Pan Am — shared split 7” (1998); overlapping Montreal scene; Roger Tellier-Craig member of both
  • Set Fire To Flames — Montreal post-rock collective sharing several GY!BE members (Bryant, Trudeau, Aidan Girt, Tellier-Craig)
  • Esmerine — Bruce Cawdron is also a member
  • Exhaust — Aidan Girt is also a member
  • Jessica Moss — early member (1995–1997); later had solo career on Constellation and was in ASMZ and Black Ox Orkestar

Research Queue

  • What was the original 1994 formation story of GY!BE as a collective — was it a deliberate expansion of Menuck’s solo cassette project or a parallel formation?
  • Confirm whether Norsola Johnson departed after Yanqui U.X.O. (2002) or was still active during the hiatus years (2003–2004) before the band stopped performing.
  • Roger Tellier-Craig appeared on Lift Yr. Skinny Fists (2000) and Yanqui U.X.O. (2002) but is absent from the 2012 reunion onward — did he formally leave the band before the hiatus or during it?
  • Slow Riot For New Zerø Kanada E.P. credits Dale Morningstar as “Recorded By (With)” — was this recorded at the Gas Station studio in Toronto (Morningstar’s usual room) or in Montreal?
  • The Yanqui U.X.O. sleeve famously included a diagram connecting major record labels to military/weapons contractors — who created this diagram and was it designed by the band or sourced from a third party?
  • Tim Herzog’s precise role when he joined: was he brought in as a replacement for a departing drummer or as an additional drummer alongside Aidan Girt and Bruce Cawdron?
  • Confirm whether the 2004 Polaris-era hiatus was announced publicly as a hiatus at the time or only recognized retrospectively.
  • ‘Allelujah! Don’t Bend Ascend won the 2013 Polaris Music Prize — was this the first time GY!BE was shortlisted for the Prize, or had they been longlisted in earlier years?
  • Jace Lasek replaced Greg Norman as engineer after Luciferian Towers (2017) — was this a formal arrangement or per-project?
