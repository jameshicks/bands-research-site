Beach Fossils

Discogs ID: 1720433 Active: 2009–present Origin: Brooklyn, New York Principal: Dustin Payseur (real name: James Dustin Payseur) Labels: Captured Tracks (2010–2013); Bayonet Records (2017–present); Numero Group (2025 single)

American indie/dream-pop band formed by Dustin Payseur in Brooklyn, 2009. Began as a solo bedroom project — the debut LP was entirely self-written and self-recorded by Payseur — and expanded into a full band over successive records. One of the founding-era acts on Captured Tracks alongside Wild Nothing and DIIV; moved to Bayonet Records by 2017.

Payseur’s other groups include Fluoride (4305707), Laced (3) (5255783), Nasty Bits (4973277), Shitfather (6765522), and The Snow (3) (3802792).

Notes (web)

Gruesome Flowers (A Tribute To The Wake) (2011, Captured Tracks CT105)

This is a split 7-inch between Beach Fossils and Wild Nothing, not a full compilation. Each band contributes one cover:

  • Beach Fossils — “Plastic Flowers”
  • Wild Nothing — “Gruesome Castle”

Both songs originate from the 1987 EP Something That No One Else Could Bring (Factory Records FAC 178) by The Wake (UK), the Glasgow post-punk group formed in 1981. That is the band being tributed — confirmed as the Factory Records / Sarah Records act, not any other act named The Wake. (Bleep listing; Factory Records catalog entry; Discogs release — as of 2026-06-29)

A second volume, Gruesome Flowers 2: A Tribute To The Wake, followed in 2012 featuring Blouse and Craft Spells, also on Captured Tracks.
