Overview

Keith Richards And The X-Pensive Winos served as the backing band for Keith Richards’ solo career, supporting his two studio albums and touring through the late 1980s and early 1990s. The core lineup included saxophonist Bobby Keys, drummer Charley Drayton, keyboardist Ivan Neville, and vocalist Sarah Dash alongside Richards himself. Their recorded output spans Blues Rock, Rock & Roll, and Classic Rock with elements of Roots Reggae and Folk Rock, and the group released one live album documenting their performances. Producer and engineer Don Smith was the group’s most consistent studio collaborator across their catalog.
