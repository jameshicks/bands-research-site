Future Pilot A.K.A.

Glasgow, Scotland. Discogs artist ID: 1438. Real name: Sushil Kumar Dade. Alias: Sushil K. Dade. Active 1996–2019.

Overview

Future Pilot A.K.A. is the recording project of Glasgow-based Indian musician Sushil Kumar Dade (real name; also credited as Sushil K. Dade). The project is defined by an unusually wide stylistic range: South Asian instrumentation (tabla, esraj, bul-bul tarang), Scottish folk and traditional song, experimental electronics, tape manipulation, and leftfield indie pop — often assembled through a “versus” methodology in which each release pairs Dade with different collaborators. Name variations used in print: Future Pilot AKA, Future Pilot Aka, Future Pilot Art’In Yo San, The Future Pilot AKA.

Dade released primarily through Geographic (a Domino sublabel; label ID 13732) and Creeping Bent (label ID 17333), with early work on Domino Records and later releases on Glass Modern (label ID 1483355). The project’s 1999 double-CD compilation Vs — A Galaxy Of Sound (Sulfur Records) stands as its most wide-ranging document: a survey of duo collaborations spanning Andrew Weatherall / Two Lone Swordsmen, Cornershop, The Pastels, Bill Wells, James Kirk (Orange Juice), Alan Vega, Scanner, Kim Fowley, and many others. The 2006 album Secrets From The Clockhouse (Creeping Bent, BENT 091CD) assembled an extraordinary guest list including Kim Gordon, Thurston Moore, Grant McLennan, Robert Forster, Mike Watt, Damo Suzuki, Stuart Murdoch, and Alasdair Gray.

Dade is also credited as a member of The Pastels (Discogs member list), and John Hogarty (also a Pastels member) appears across multiple Future Pilot A.K.A. releases.

Biography

1996–1998 — Early Releases and the Glasgow Scene

The project’s first appearance on Discogs is a 1996 split release on Via Satellite (label ID 38300) shared with Mount Vernon Arts Lab (Drew Mulholland’s project). This positions Future Pilot A.K.A. early within the Glasgow / Scottish underground network that also fed Creeping Bent.

In 1998, two releases arrived on Domino Records:

  • Hurricane Fighter Plane / The Gates To Film City (1998): A three-way split single pairing Future Pilot A.K.A., The Pastels, and Two Lone Swordsmen (Andrew Weatherall and Keith Tenniswood). “The Gates To Film City” was a full collaboration: Dade edited and added bass, electronics, and percussion over Weatherall/Tenniswood programming.
  • The Bill Wells Octet vs. Future Pilot A.K.A. (1998, Domino/Geographic): A joint LP with Bill Wells’s octet. The Octet’s Discogs lineup includes Allon Beauvoisin, Andy Hamill, Bill Wells, Chick Lyall, Graeme Wilson, John Burgess (2), Lindsay Cooper (2), and Tom Bancroft.

2000–2003 — Geographic Period

Three solo releases on Geographic:

  • Darshan (2000): Produced and directed by Sushil K. Dade; recorded by Raymond McGinley (Teenage Fanclub).
  • Beat Of A Drum (2001, single): Produced and directed by Dade.
  • Tiny Waves, Mighty Sea (2001, double CD): The project’s major early statement. Compiled by David Scott (credited first), with Stephen Pastel and Sushil K. Dade. Vocals: Vinita Dade (tracks 2, 4, 7, 11, 13), Katrina Mitchell (tracks 2, 7, 8), Norman Blake (tracks 3, 7, 9). Recorded by Raymond McGinley. Mastered by Steve Rooke, Stephen Pastel, and Dade. Design by Katrina Mitchell.
  • Salute Your Soul (2003): Engineered by James Chapman (3); produced and written by Sushil K. Dade. Guest credits: Mikey Dread, Nick Webb, James Chapman, Philip Glass (written-by), Gordon Kennedy (2), Matt Cairns, Greg Mitchell, Anurudh Shivkumar Dade, Douglas MacIntyre.

Family Members in the Project

Vinita Dade (vocals, six releases), Anurudh Shivkumar Dade (vocals/percussion, four releases), Radhika Dade (vocals, Orkestra Digitalis) are all family members who appear consistently across the catalog.
