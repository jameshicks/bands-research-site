Soap (3)

Discogs ID: 358818 Origin: Germany Genre: Indie Rock Active: circa 1997 (earliest known release)

German indie rock band. Known primarily from a 1997 split 7” with Subterfuge (5) on Blurr Records, a Düsseldorf-connected German indie label. The band shared personnel with Subterfuge (5) — specifically Daniel Klingen and Michael Krannich, both of whom appear in Subterfuge’s Discogs membership roster and on the Soap side of the split.

Daniel Klingen is the only member with a broader Discogs footprint beyond Soap (3) — he is a full member of Subterfuge (5) (drums, percussion) and has an extensive production/engineering career spanning German indie, rock, and pop into the 2020s.

Michael Krannich is listed as a member of Subterfuge (5) in Discogs, but only appears in performance credits on the Soap side of this split. His Discogs page links him to a second release in 2023 (Don’ts And Dos, credited under “The Radio Field Are” on Less Records / Subjangle). This may reflect overlap between Soap and Subterfuge membership, a Discogs data error, or a later-career reunion/regrouping.

Key Release: Subterfuge / Soap (1997)

Label: Blurr Records (cat# Blurr 12) Format: 7” vinyl, 33⅓ RPM Country: Germany Genres: Rock / Indie Rock GEMA / LC 3200

Split 7” with Subterfuge (5). Subterfuge occupy the A-side (tracks A1–A2); Soap occupy the B-side (B1–B2). Each side was produced and recorded entirely separately, with different producers and engineers for each half.

Daniel Klingen functioned as sole producer, recording engineer, and mixer for all Soap material. Kai Blankenberg — who produced the Subterfuge side — also mastered the entire release at what would become his Skyline studio facility in Düsseldorf. Thomas Baumhoff handled the sleeve for the full 7”.

Label Context: Blurr Records

Blurr Records (Discogs ID: 128638) is a German independent label based in Erkrath (near Düsseldorf), owned by Carsten Johannisbauer. It grew out of a fanzine called Blurr and released approximately 28 catalog items over its active period. The label’s catalog spans indie rock, indie pop, and alternative, with releases from artists including Quest For Rescue, Free Yourself, and Craving. The Subterfuge / Soap split was issued as cat# Blurr 12. The label also runs a sublabel, Kneipe Für Gestaltung (Discogs ID: 941817).
