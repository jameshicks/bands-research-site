Overview

Scott & Rivers is a Japan-based collaborative duo formed by Scott Murphy of Allister and Rivers Cuomo of Weezer. The two connected through a shared deal with Universal Music Group and met in Los Angeles in 2009, subsequently writing and recording original material sung entirely in Japanese. Both share lead vocal duties and guitar, with Japanese session musicians completing the lineup. Their recorded output spans 2013 into 2020, moving from Universal and Sony Japan to the independent tAnkers Inc., covering pop punk, J-pop, and alternative rock.
