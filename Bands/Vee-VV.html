Vee VV

UK indie rock / post-punk group from Blackpool. Discogs artist ID: 198405

Also credited as: Vee V.V. · Vee v.v. · Vee Vee Vee · Vee-V-V

Overview

Vee VV were a Blackpool-based group active from 1982 through at least 1990, cycling through several lineups across that span. Formed in the early 1980s as the post-punk scene shaded into indie rock, they operated on the margins of the UK independent circuit — releasing singles on small Lancashire and German labels before recording their sole LP, Life, Liberty And The Pursuit Of Happiness (1988), on their own Payola imprint. Their track “Keep Beat” appears on Disc 3 of Shake The Foundations (Militant Funk & The Post-Punk Dancefloor 1978-1984) (Cherry Red, 2021), situating them in the militant funk / post-punk dancefloor lineage. A retrospective compilation, Payola (Edils Recordings, 2019), collected material across multiple eras; a new LP, Crackerjack (Payola, 2024), indicates the band reconvened decades later. Producer and BBC Radio DJ Mark Radcliffe produced their 1989 cassette single, an early credit in his production career.

Personnel on Payola Retrospective (2019, track-by-track)

The Payola CD (Edils Recordings, cat# er46) provides the most granular personnel documentation — each track has individual credits matching the lineup periods:

1982–84 lineup tracks (e.g. “Love Canal”, “Keep Beat”, “Hold On”, etc.):

  • Mark Ormrod — vocals
  • Chris Anderton — guitar (+ backing vocals on some)
  • Ian Butterworth — guitar (+ backing vocals on some)
  • Dave Milner (2) — drums
  • Martin Reynolds — bass guitar

1985–86 lineup tracks (e.g. “Don’t Get Down”, “Cinnamon”):

  • Mark Ormrod — vocals
  • Ian Butterworth — guitar
  • Peter Slater — drums
  • Martin Reynolds — bass guitar

1987–88 lineup tracks (e.g. “Fish on Friday”, “Better the Devil”, “Prick up Your Ears”, “Flame On”):

  • Mark Ormrod — vocals
  • Ian Butterworth — guitar (not listed by name on these tracks; Paul Williams fills guitar)
  • Derek Butler — drums
  • Martin Reynolds — bass guitar
  • Paul Williams — guitar (on “Prick up Your Ears”, “Flame On”)

Release-level credits on Payola:

  • Nick Garside — Engineer, Producer (track 14)
  • Mark Radcliffe — Producer (tracks 15–16)
