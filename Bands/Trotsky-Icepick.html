Overview

Trotsky Icepick is a Los Angeles post-punk / art-punk band formed in 1986 by Vitus Mataré and Kjehl Johansen following the dissolution of The Last and 100 Flowers. The band’s name refers to the murder weapon used to assassinate Russian revolutionary Leon Trotsky. Their first release, Poison Summer (1983/1986), appeared under the alias Danny and the Doorknobs on Mataré’s own label, Old Scratch Grammophon; it was reissued in 1989 on SST Records under the banner Trotsky Icepick Presents: Danny And The Doorknobs. Five further LPs followed on Greg Ginn’s SST Records through 1994. After a long hiatus the band returned in 2011 and again in 2019, releasing I Haunted Myself on the newly established Poison Summer Records.

The band draws directly from the wreckage of key early-1980s LA bands: Johansen and Talley-Jones carried over from The Urinals / 100 Flowers; Mataré and Rosewall from The Last; Mike Patton (2) from The Middle Class; Jason Kahn from the Leaving Trains and Downey Mildew; Hunter Crowley later from The Last and The Brian Jonestown Massacre. The lineup shifted considerably across the eight-year SST run, giving the records distinct personnel from album to album while retaining Johansen and Mataré as the constant core.

According to Wikipedia, the band is cited in Martin C. Strong’s The Great Indie Discography (2003) and has received coverage from Robert Christgau and Blurt magazine (Wikipedia: Trotsky Icepick).

Alias: Danny and the Doorknobs

The Danny and the Doorknobs name has a separate Discogs artist entry (ID: 512215) listed as an alias of Trotsky Icepick. The alias was used for the first Poison Summer release (Old Scratch, 1983) and the 1989 compilation In ‘Poison Summer’ (on which both names appear in primary credit: “Trotsky Icepick Presents: Danny And The Doorknobs”). Poison Summer Records issued a new Danny and the Doorknobs LP, Jukebox In The L.A. River, in 2019 alongside the Trotsky Icepick return. The Danny and the Doorknobs Discogs entry includes Tom Watson (Discogs ID: 410609) and Tom Hofer (Discogs ID: 585045) as members not separately listed on the main Trotsky Icepick page.

The 1989 SST reissue of In ‘Poison Summer’ consolidates older Danny and the Doorknobs recordings; the original Old Scratch Poison Summer (SLP-0186) and the 1986 Trotsky Icepick Poison Summer (SLP-0786) are distinct releases credited separately on Discogs.

Per-Album Personnel Notes

Poison Summer (1986, Old Scratch SLP-0786): Kjehl Johansen (guitar, 8-string bass, vocals), Vitus Mataré (guitar, vocals), Jamie Lennon (keyboards, bass), John Frank (3) (drums). Produced by Mataré and Johansen. Mastered by John Golden. Recorded by Eric Westfall. Sleeve design by Felice Mataré.

Baby (1988, SST CD 197): Kjehl Johansen (guitar, vocals), Vitus Mataré (producer, vocals, guitar), John Rosewall (bass, guitar), Jason Kahn (drums). Guest: D.J. Bonebrake (marimba on “Mar Vista Bus Stop” and “And It Goes Like This”). Mastered by John Golden.

El Kabong (1989, SST-246): No per-track credits on Discogs; this entry is a test pressing / white label.

In ‘Poison Summer’ (1989, SST 254): Danny and the Doorknobs recording, produced by Trotsky Icepick. Engineered by Ethan James (Ralph Burns Kellogg). No individual personnel listed on Discogs.

The Ultraviolet Catastrophe (1990, SST 279): Kjehl Johansen (guitar), Vitus Mataré (guitar, vocals), John Rosewall (bass, guitar), Hunter Crowley (drums), John Talley-Jones (vocals).

Carpetbomb The Riff (1993, SST CD 295): Kjehl Johansen (guitar, engineer), Vitus Mataré (guitar, vocals, producer), John Talley-Jones (vocals, lyrics), Mike Patton (2) (bass, lyrics, music), John Glogovac (drums, music). Produced by Trotsky Icepick and Mataré. Mastered by John Golden. Engineer: Kenan Kelley. Includes a cover of “Shaken” written by The Middle Class.

Hot Pop Hello (1994, SST CD 286): No personnel credits on Discogs.

I Haunted Myself (2019, Poison Summer Records PSLP1902): No personnel credits on Discogs.

John Golden (Discogs ID: 265502, alias “The Man Himself”) mastered the majority of the catalog and also cut lacquers. He is a prolific LA mastering engineer across the SST orbit.

Ethan James (real name Ralph Burns Kellogg, Discogs ID: 237678) engineered In ‘Poison Summer’; he was a key engineer/producer at Radio Tokyo studios, associated with the 100 Flowers sessions and other LA independent acts.

D.J. Bonebrake (Discogs ID: 269744) played marimba on two tracks from Baby — he is best known as the drummer for X.

The 1992 split 7” pairs Trotsky Icepick with Vena Cava, covering The Saints’ “This Perfect Day” (written by Chris Bailey and Ed Kuepper). Vena Cava shared members with Trotsky Icepick (John Talley-Jones appears in both).
