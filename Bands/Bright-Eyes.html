Bright Eyes

Indie folk / lo-fi project. Omaha, Nebraska. Discogs ID: 83606.

Project led by Conor Oberst, released on Saddle Creek Records. Split 7” Collaboration Series No. 1 (2002, Better Looking Records) with The Album Leaf: LaValle provided instrumentation; Oberst sang. Known for prolific Saddle Creek output in the early 2000s.
