Overview

The Gunn-Truscinski Duo is an experimental and psychedelic rock unit built around guitarist Steve Gunn and drummer John Truscinski, active since 2010. Their catalogue is compact — four releases in the 2010s and two in the 2020s — all issued through Three Lobed Recordings, with whom they have maintained an exclusive relationship across fifteen years. The project is centred on improvisational interplay between the two players, and its recording pace has coexisted alongside Gunn’s parallel and increasingly prominent solo career. Studio work consistently drew on a tight circle of engineers and producers, with Jason Meagher and Seth Manchester handling several sessions between them.
