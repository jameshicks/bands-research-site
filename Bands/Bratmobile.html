Bratmobile

Discogs ID: 267486
From: Olympia, WA / Eugene, OR
Active: 1991–1994; reunited 1999–2003; active again from 2023
Genres: Riot Grrrl, Punk
Label: Kill Rock Stars

Riot grrrl trio. Lineup: Allison Wolfe (vocals), Molly Neuman (drums), Erin Smith (guitar). 35 releases on Kill Rock Stars.

Debut LP Pottymouth (KRS 208, 1993): tracks 1–14 recorded by Tim Green (2); tracks 15–17 (“Kiss & Ride,” “No You Don’t,” “Queenie”) recorded by Conrad Uno and Pat Maley. “Cherry Bomb” (track 3) is a Joan Jett / Kim Fowley cover, uncredited on the sleeve. Molly Neuman sang lead on “Richard” (track 11).

Later albums: Ladies, Women And Girls (originally 2000 on Lookout! Records, cat. LK252CD; reissued KRS 2023), Girls Get Busy (reissued KRS 2023), Pottymouth (reissued KRS 2014). Peel Session (2025 reissue on KRS 804).

Early Membership Note

Christina Billotte appears as a Bratmobile member on Discogs and is credited as “Band [Bratmobile]” on four tracks (A5, A8–A10) of the 1992 cassette compilation The Embassy Tapes 1990 To 1992 (not-on-label, release ID 5351462), alongside Allison Wolfe, Erin Smith, Jen Smith, and Molly Neuman. Billotte does not appear on any commercial Bratmobile releases. This reflects an early/pre-label period; the extent of her membership is not fully documented in Discogs alone.
