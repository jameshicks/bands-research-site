The Chrysanthemums

Leicester art-pop / indie band co-founded by Alan Jenkins. Discogs artist ID: 294788.

Psychedelic art pop band with a large cult following almost entirely outside the UK. Active from 1987 (overlapping with the final years of The Deep Freeze Mice). Released on Egg Plant (1987–1989), Madagascar (1990–1991), Jarmusic (1995), and returned with Decoy For A Dognapper! (Egg Plant, 2022). Name variations: Chrys&themums, The Chrys&themums, Chrysanthemums. In 2010, German music magazine MusikExpress placed them at number 23 in their list of the most under-rated bands of all time.

The 1997 reconstituted lineup with Vladimir Zajkowiecz (as Martin Howells) operated under the visual-pun name Chrys&themums. The 2022 concept album Decoy For A Dognapper! is based on a single 1969 episode of the children’s cartoon Scooby-Doo.
