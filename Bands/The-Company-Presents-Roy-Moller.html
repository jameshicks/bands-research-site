The Company Presents Roy Moller

Glasgow indie-pop project. Discogs artist ID: 1654753. Also listed under name variation “The Company” (used on the sleeve of their only known release). Years active: 2008 (Discogs-documented).

Biography

The Company Presents Roy Moller is a Glasgow-based indie-pop collaboration built around singer-songwriter Roy Moller and Stevie Jackson, the guitarist and co-songwriter of Belle & Sebastian. The third member is Gary Thom, a drummer and songwriter previously associated with the Scottish indie group Astrid (3) and the Glasgow-Edinburgh act California Snow Story.

Roy Moller (born 3 July 1963, Edinburgh) had released his first solo single as early as 2003 on the German-based Félicité Singles Club and had maintained an active if low-profile career in Scottish indie music before teaming with Jackson. The project name is a slightly formal construction — “The Company Presents Roy Moller” — with the trio crediting themselves as “The Company” in the producer and written-by slots of the release, suggesting the collective was conceived as a presenting vehicle for Moller’s songwriting rather than a standard band-name.

The sole documented release is “Join The Dots” (A-side), issued as a split 7” under the series title Searching For The Now 1 on Slumberland Records (Oakland, CA) in 2008. Catalog number: SLR74. “Searching For The Now” is also the name of a Slumberland sublabel, indicating the split-7” series was a distinct imprint within the Slumberland structure. The flipside was contributed by The Hermit Crabs (2), a Glasgow-formed indie-pop act led by Melanie Whittle, who contributed “Flaxen-Haired Boy.”

The session personnel on “Join The Dots” include: Roy Moller (guitar, synthesizer, vocals, handclaps, footstomps), Stevie Jackson (handclaps, footstomps), Gary Thom (handclaps, footstomps, percussion), Zac Ware (guitar), Mark Ferrie (2) (bass), Ross Wilson (9) (drums), and John Rooney (6) (handclaps, footstomps). Engineering was handled by Colin McGeoch and Ronan Breslin; mastered by Greg Kane (Gregory Kane). Production and songwriting are credited to “The Company” (i.e., the trio). The pressing was handled at Pirates Press (per matrix runout: “74869H1/A PIRATES PRESS SLR74 A”).

Moller and Jackson’s collaboration continued in a second project, The Store Keys, a five-piece that expanded the lineup to include Marco Rea, Lorne Cowieson, and Joe Kane (5) and released a split 7” on The Barne Society (2011).
