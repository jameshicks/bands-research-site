Sing-Sing

Discogs ID: 197242. UK indie pop duo formed 1997, disbanded 2008. Members: Emma Anderson (guitar, vocals; ex-Lush) and Lisa O’Neill (vocals; ex-Locust). Released Feels Like Summer (1998, Bella Union, BELLACD8) — one of the label’s earliest releases.

Formation and early history

Emma Anderson and Lisa O’Neill met through a mutual acquaintance in mid-1997, shortly after Lush had disbanded. O’Neill had previously worked with producer Mark Van Hoen in Locust. In early 1998 the duo recorded a first four-song demo with Justin Welch of Elastica on drums. A second demo — produced with Van Hoen — caught the attention of Simon Raymonde and Robin Guthrie, co-founders of Bella Union and both ex-Cocteau Twins.

Raymonde and Guthrie released “Feels Like Summer” as a Bella Union single in October 1998 (BELLACD8), making it one of the label’s earliest releases — Bella Union had itself only been founded in 1997, initially to release Cocteau Twins’ own material and collaborations. After the Cocteau Twins split, Raymonde and Guthrie (alongside former manager Fiona Glyn-Jones) continued operating the label, with early signings including Dirty Three, Françoiz Breut, and The Czars alongside Sing-Sing. [Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sing-Sing_(band), as of 2026-08-08; Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bella_Union, as of 2026-08-08]

“Feels Like Summer” reached number 26 on the UK Indie Singles chart.

Subsequent releases

After the Bella Union single, Sing-Sing set up their own imprint, Aerial Records, releasing further singles including “I’ll Be” and a re-recorded “Feels Like Summer” in 2000. Their debut album The Joy of Sing-Sing (2001) was released on Alan McGee’s Poptones label. The VPME interview with Anderson notes they had access to Sanctuary’s studio Nomis at the time. A second album, Sing-Sing and I (2005), was part-financed by fans on their own label. The band disbanded on New Year’s Day 2008. [Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sing-Sing_(band), as of 2026-08-08; VPME interview, https://www.thevpme.com/interviews/lit-up-emma-anderson-interview/]
