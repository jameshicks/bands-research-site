Alex G (2)

Discogs ID: 363279 Origin: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, USA Labels: Orchid Tapes (2014), Domino Records (later) Genres: Rock, Lo-Fi, Indie Rock

Solo project of Alex Giannascoli. DSU (OCT042, 2014) was released on Orchid Tapes as a limited vinyl LP — his breakthrough record, leading to a deal with Domino Records. The Orchid Tapes pressing had 9 pressings/editions documented on Discogs. Also contributed “Cards” to the Boring Ecstasy compilation (OCT039, 2014) and “July 27 2015” to Radiating Light (OCT060, 2016).

Prior to the Orchid Tapes period, Alex G released early material on Birdtapes (Brooklyn/Philadelphia), including the SALT cassette (2011) and a split 7” with R.L. Kelly (BRD010, 2013) — the label’s most-pressed release at 9 documented pressings. 11 releases total on Birdtapes. Ronnie Giannascoli (same surname; possible family connection) is credited with cover artwork on one Birdtapes release.
