Wooden Wand

Discogs ID: 315202
Real name: James Jackson Toth
Aliases: DUNZA, Grim Jim, Hassara, James Toth, The Wand, Wand, Wand Jehovah, Wooden Wand Hassara, Wooden Wand Jehovah
Members: Jody Nelson (649474) listed
Genres: Experimental folk, psych, avant-folk

Solo project of James Jackson Toth. Released four records on 5 Rue Christine between 2004 and 2006, often in partnership with The Vanishing Voice: Harem Of The Sundrum & The Witness Figg (GER057, 2004), Buck Dharma (GER051, 2005, with The Vanishing Voice), Gipsy Freedom (GER053, 2006, with The Vanishing Voice), plus one more.

The Vanishing Voice is a separate band — not a Toth alias. Their members include G. Lucas Crane (aka Nonhorse, also Woods (2)), Heidi Diehl, Jarvis Taveniere (also Woods (2)), Pete Nolan (Magik Markers), Jessica Toth (aka Jex Thoth), and Keith Wood (Sunburned Hand Of The Man). Thurston Moore contributed liner notes to XIAO (2004, De Stijl). Outside 5RC, Wooden Wand & The Vanishing Voice released prolifically on Troubleman Unlimited, De Stijl, Time-Lag Records, Weird Forest Records, 23 Productions, and others (2003–2007). The collaboration’s only non-Wooden Wand-credited release is Stone Tablet (2007, Important Records).
