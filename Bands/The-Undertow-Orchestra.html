The Undertow Orchestra

Singer-songwriter supergroup formed in 2006, organized under the Undertow Music Collective management umbrella. Brought together four acclaimed indie/alt-country songwriters — Mark Eitzel, David Bazan, Vic Chesnutt, and Will Johnson — for a short US and European tour in February–March 2006, with one performance (Valentine’s Day, Boston) documented as an unofficial live MP3 release.

Discogs artist ID: 10734949
Name variation: Undertow Orchestra

Biography

2006 — Formation, Tour, and Boston Performance

The Undertow Orchestra was assembled in 2006 by Undertow Music Collective manager Bob Andrews, who brought together four artists on his roster because “they are all fans of each other” and wanted a “band vibe” rather than the standard solo-set co-headlining format. [source: glidemagazine.com/4887/the-undertow-orchestra/] All four members were on the Undertow roster:

  • Mark Eitzel — frontman of American Music Club, solo artist
  • David Bazan — frontman of Pedro the Lion, also Headphones project
  • Vic Chesnutt — Athens, GA singer-songwriter
  • Will Johnson — frontman of Centro-matic, solo artist

A fifth performer, Scott Danbom (Centro-matic bandmate of Will Johnson), joined as pianist and violinist throughout the tour. [source: thecurrent.org/feature/2006/02/21/undertow-orchestra-live-in-studio]

The group had a single rehearsal on February 3, 2006 — eight days before their first show. [source: mattbrundage.com/2006/02/12/snowed-in-with-the-undertow-orchestra/] Rehearsals took place at Vic Chesnutt’s home in Athens, GA.

Tour format: Each member took turns at center stage performing three or four of his own songs, while the other three played as his backing band. For much of the set, the instrument split was Eitzel and Johnson on guitar, Bazan on drums, Chesnutt on bass, and Danbom on keyboards — though Bazan and Johnson swapped positions when Bazan came forward to sing. [source: brooklynvegan.com/the-undertow-or/] Seating at venues was arranged “in true orchestra fashion.” [source: thepitchkc.com/the-undertow-orchestra/]

Tour dates: The tour ran February 8 – March 1, 2006. It opened in Athens, GA (February 8, likely 40 Watt Club — per Last.fm), traveled the eastern US with confirmed stops at Arlington, VA (Iota Club and Cafe, February 11), Boston, MA (Remis Auditorium, Museum of Fine Arts, February 14), and New York City, and concluded March 1 at Atlanta’s Variety Playhouse. The Wikipedia article on Undertow Music additionally claims a European leg, and the phrasing “toured the US and Europe” appears on multiple sources; however, no specific European dates have been independently confirmed during this research. [sources: glidemagazine.com/4887/the-undertow-orchestra/; mattbrundage.com/2006/02/12/snowed-in-with-the-undertow-orchestra/; last.fm/music/Undertow+Orchestra; en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undertow_Music] The band played to mostly sold-out crowds, including audiences who braved 18 inches of snow in New York. [source: last.fm/music/Undertow+Orchestra]

The tour received press coverage from the Chicago Tribune, Boston Herald, and Minnesota Public Radio (The Current). [source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Undertow_Orchestra, as of 2026-06-09] The group performed a live studio session for The Current on February 21, 2006, the day after their Minneapolis show at First Avenue. [source: thecurrent.org/feature/2006/02/21/undertow-orchestra-live-in-studio]

Boston recording: The Remis Auditorium show (February 14, 2006) was recorded and distributed as a free MP3 download. The recording was shared by music blog Aquarium Drunkard as part of their “Live Upload Series” (post VII, March 12, 2006) and also posted by Brooklyn Vegan. [sources: aquariumdrunkard.com/2006/03/12/live-upload-series-vii-undertow-orchestra/; brooklynvegan.com/the-undertow-or/] The Discogs “Unofficial Release” tag is consistent with a fan or blogger distribution rather than a label-sanctioned release. The recording appears to have originated as a freely shared digital download from music blogs, not a commercially pressed release. No evidence of it being a board tape or self-distributed by the band has been confirmed.

The project name mirrors the management entity: Undertow Music Collective was also associated with Undertow Music, a related label imprint.
