Overview

Reuber is the solo recording alias of Timo Reuber, who co-founded the Cologne electroacoustic duo Klangwart with Markus Detmer in 1996. The Reuber alias predates the Klangwart–Staubgold relationship: the first credited Reuber appearance is as a featured artist on the Klangstelle compilation Köln-Olpe (1998), and early engineering credits also surface on the Klangstelle label (mixing and mastering the Töa / Amöbenruh 7” that same year).

The project’s principal run is seven solo albums on Staubgold from 2000 to 2023, making Reuber one of the label’s longest-standing roster relationships. The music spans ambient electronics, Krautrock-influenced repetition, and abstract composition — cataloged on Discogs under Electronic / Ambient / Experimental / Krautrock / Industrial / Berlin-School. A recurring collaborator across the solo albums is Joseph Suchy, who mastered most releases and contributed guitar on Südpol (2007) and as “Ringrichter” on Ring (2010). Timo Reuber is also explicitly credited as “Music By” under his real name on Ruhig Blut (2001), cementing the alias connection on-record.

Outside the solo catalog, Reuber (Timo Reuber) co-produced the two most recent Klangwart albums: Transit (2015) and Bogotá (2019), the latter a large-ensemble studio work recorded with Colombian and Venezuelan musicians.

The alias name was also applied retroactively on Discogs to a 1998 Klangstelle contribution (Köln-Olpe), where the artist appears credited as “Featuring.”

Connections

  • Timo Reuber — the real person behind the alias (confirmed by Discogs realname field and “Music By — T. Reuber” credit on Ruhig Blut)
  • Klangwart — Timo Reuber’s duo with Markus Detmer; the Reuber alias and Klangwart share the Staubgold label and several personnel
  • Staubgold — primary label for all solo output (staubgold 8 through staubgold 132, 2000–2023); label founded by Markus Detmer
  • Klangstelle — pre-Staubgold imprint run by Klangwart; site of first Reuber credit (1998)
  • Joseph Suchy — long-running collaborator: mastered Ruhig Blut, Kintopp, Südpol; played guitar on Südpol and Ring; co-produced Transit with Reuber; also a solo artist on Staubgold (Calabi.Yau, Soundchambers) and member of Burnt Friedman & The Nu Dub Players
  • Markus Detmer — Klangwart co-founder and Staubgold label head; contributed “additional synthetic sounds” on Anna (2000); Reuber produced his two final Klangwart albums

Research Queue

  • Rueckschau (staubgold 132, 2023) is cataloged as a “Compilation” — does it compile tracks from all prior Reuber Staubgold LPs, or does it include unreleased/archival material? Track titles partially overlap with Südpol (“Altweiber”, “Auf Auf”) and Kintopp (“Stadtflucht”, “Für Immer”, “Schlusskuss”), but also include edits (“Kpunkt (Edit)”, “Steppengraskrieger (Edit)”, “Ring (Edit)”). One track “Ruhigblutschlafgut (Remix)” suggests a remix of Ruhig Blut material — who remixed it?
  • The 2017 release Monserrate (Cougouyou Music 8) lists Reuber as producer but Cougouyou Music is a Staubgold sublabel; who is the primary artist on that release, and is it related to the Colombian ensemble work on Bogotá (2019)?
  • Jyrgen Hall is credited on Anna (2000) for bass and one co-write — does he appear elsewhere in the Staubgold or Cologne experimental scene?
  • Hakima Merabet is credited with vocals on Anna (2000) — does she have a broader discography or was this a one-off collaboration?
  • Ruhig Blut’s three tracks run 21+ minutes each — is this a field-recording or drone work, or structured composition? Discogs lists no sub-track detail.
