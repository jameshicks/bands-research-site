Cult Figures

Discogs ID: 294377

UK post-punk group, formed Solihull College of Technology, West Midlands, 1977. Active 1979–c.1980, then reformed c.2016 and active through at least 2026. Released Zip Nolan (Rather Records GEAR FOUR, 1979) and In Love (Rather Records GEAR EIGHT, 1980) on Rather Records, the label co-run by Swell Maps. Top labels: Gare Du Nord Records, Rather Records, CFC (own imprint).

Members (per Discogs)

Alan “Jock” Rodger, Gary Jones (27), Jonathan Hodgson, Martin Hughes (7), Lee McFadden, Fraser Gillespie, Steve May (15), Stuart Hilton (2). Discogs ID: 294377.

Post-1980 Activity (Web Sources)

The band is a revival/reunion, not a continuation. They split c.1980 after two Rather Records singles and approximately 38 years of dormancy. They reformed around 2015–2016.

Producer continuity

The Strange Brew (April 23, 2026) notes the band has worked with “the same producer since the first time around” across their 50-year span — consistent with John A. Rivers (Woodbine Street Studios, Leamington Spa), who engineered/produced “I Remember” (1979-era recording, comp. 2022) and mixed Deritend (2021). source
