Sharkboy (2)

Discogs ID: 291449 Origin: Brighton, UK Active: 1993–1995 Genres: Indie Rock, Pop Rock, Country Rock Label: Nude Records

Biography

Sharkboy (2) were a Brighton-based indie group formed in the early 1990s around vocalist and principal songwriter Avy (full name: Avy Sharkboy, as per her Discogs profile). The Discogs entry notes that the band “became more and more Avy’s solo project” over time, suggesting a gradual shift from a conventional group structure toward a vehicle primarily fronted by her.

The band signed to Nude Records, the London independent label that also housed Suede and would become a prominent address of the mid-1990s UK indie scene. Sharkboy (2) debuted in 1993 with the 12” single “Crystaline” (cat. NUD 7T), produced by Phil Wright (2), who would go on to produce their entire Nude Records output. The sleeve was designed by Brian Cannon and Microdot (4) — the same design partnership responsible for Oasis and other Britpop-era sleeves.

A run of singles followed in 1994: the Matinee EP (released as both a 10-track vinyl LP format and a promo) and the four-track CD single “Razor / Bright Things Lie / Dear Gilda / Show Me Now” (cat. NUD 8CD). The Razor EP showed a broadening palette — it is tagged Alternative Rock, Country Rock, and Indie Rock on Discogs, and Adrian Oxaal’s credits on that release include banjo alongside bass, cello, keyboards, and guitar.

In 1995 Sharkboy (2) released their album, The Valentine Tapes (cat. NUDE 4CD, Nude Records), produced by Phil Wright (2). The album featured guest violin from Dickon Hinchliffe (credited as “Dickon Hinchcliffe” on the sleeve) on tracks 1, 3, and 5 — Hinchliffe was simultaneously a founding member of Tindersticks. Track 4 (“Same Mother Of Pearl”) has an additional drums credit for Steve Hewitt in place of the band’s regular drummer Dil Davies; this Steve Hewitt (Discogs ID: 435459, born Steven James Hewitt, 22 March 1971, Northwich, Cheshire) is confirmed as the same drummer who later played in Placebo and previously in The Boo Radleys, Six By Seven, and Breed (2). Phil Wright (2) plays keyboards, pedal steel guitar, and percussion on the album in addition to producing; he also arranged strings for tracks 1 and 5.

The album was accompanied by a box set series called The Valentine Singles: three individually numbered 7” limited editions, each pairing a new Sharkboy (2) original with a cover version. The three component singles were:

  • “Big Black Jaguar” / “Diamonds Are Forever” (cat. NUD 14S) — B-side written by John Barry and Don Black
  • “My Magnetic Susan” / “Some Misunderstanding” (cat. NUD 16S) — B-side written by Gene Clark
  • “Tiny Seismic Night” / “Je T’Aime” (cat. NUD 17S) — B-side written by Serge Gainsbourg

A cassette-format promo compilation of the box set material also circulated (cat. listed as “none” on Discogs). The Valentine Singles lineup credits Steve Hewitt on drums and percussion throughout (not Dil Davies), and Jem Noble as engineer. Phil Wright (2) plays pedal steel guitar, guitar, and percussion in addition to producing.

A test pressing for “Big Black Jaguar / Diamonds Are Forever” appears on Discogs as a separate entry (id: 13190041).

No Discogs-documented activity follows the 1995 releases until The Black Sessions (2009, Sangatte Records, cat. SANG 0051 CD), a CDr release of a French radio session recorded in Paris. That document is listed as an unofficial release. The setlist draws heavily from the Matinee/Razor-era catalog and includes a cover of Gainsbourg’s “Je T’Aime Moi Non Plus.” Avy’s Discogs profile (as of the DB dump) notes she records and writes as a solo artist under the Sharkboy name and produces visual/audio-visual live pieces; she is also listed as a member of the group Rockhard Garçons.

Open Questions

  • Steve Hewitt / Placebo confirmation: CONFIRMED via Discogs. Steve Hewitt (ID: 435459, born Northwich, Cheshire, 1971) is the Placebo drummer. He drummed on track 4 of The Valentine Tapes and throughout all three Valentine Singles box set singles. His presence on the Valentine Singles is more extensive than the original stub noted — he plays all drums/percussion on those three 7”s, not just one album track.
  • Avy’s full name: Discogs lists her only as “Avy.” Her profile text refers to her as “Avy Sharkboy” and her website is signofthesharkboy.com; no surname is documented in the DB. No separate Discogs presence under a full name found.
  • Nick Wilson (2): Discogs shows no groups or credits outside of Sharkboy (2). His full name is not documented.
  • Gavin Cheyne: No Discogs credits outside of Sharkboy (2). No profile text.
  • Alan Stirner post-Sharkboy: Discogs lists him in Blow Up (ID: 652783), Butterfield (2) (ID: 1297071), and Whirl (2) (ID: 1086282) — none of these have been researched.
  • Matinee catalog confusion: The Matinee release (cat. NUDE 2 LP) appears to be a 10-track vinyl LP format, not a conventional single — it may be a promo album or a limited vinyl release distinct from a standard single. US release via Tristar Music is documented.
  • Why “Sharkboy (2)”: Discogs disambiguation implies at least one other artist using the Sharkboy name. The Discogs note that the band became “more and more Avy’s solo project” suggests the group gradually dissolved into a solo vehicle; Avy’s Discogs page lists ongoing solo activity under the Sharkboy name.
