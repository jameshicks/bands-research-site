Spunk

Norwegian free improvisation group. Discogs artist ID: 33137. Active from at least 1999. All-female improvising ensemble based in Oslo.

Overview

Spunk were among the earliest acts on Rune Grammofon, with their debut Det Eneste Jeg Vet Er At Det Ikke Er En Støvsuger released in 1999 (RCD 2010). They released three primary albums on the label across fifteen years. Nominated for the Spellemannprisen (Norwegian Grammy) for best contemporary album in 2013 with Das Wohltemperierte Spunk.
