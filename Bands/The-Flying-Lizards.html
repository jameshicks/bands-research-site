The Flying Lizards

Art-pop / post-punk group. UK. Discogs ID: 24165. Project of David Cunningham.

Known for their 1979 cover of “Money (That’s What I Want)” (Virgin). Statik Records releases 1984–1985: Dizzy Miss Lizzie (1984, catno TAK 25/12); Sex Machine (1984, catno TAK 19/12, 5 pressings); Top Ten LP (1984, 8 pressings); 16 total releases.
