Nazoranai

Japanese/Australian/American experimental improvisation trio. Discogs artist ID: 2877354. Japanese name: なぞらない (“not to trace / not to imitate”).

Overview

Nazoranai is a free improvisation / experimental rock trio formed in 2011 by Keiji Haino (guitar, vocals, synth), Oren Ambarchi (drums), and Stephen O’Malley (bass). Though the group formally convened in 2011, all three had previously played together during a [[Bands/Sunn O)))] performance in 2006.

The name なぞらない (nazoranai) is a Japanese verb meaning “not to trace” or “not to imitate” — a statement of the group’s improvisational, non-derivative approach. The group draws together three distinct lineages: Haino’s decades-spanning avant-rock and psych practice in Japan, Ambarchi’s percussive experimentalism from Sydney, and O’Malley’s drone-metal architecture (Sunn O))), KTL, Gravetemple) from the USA. In live and recorded performance they blur the boundaries between structured drone, noise, and free jazz.

Their recordings have been issued primarily on Ideologic Organ, the label curated and art-directed by O’Malley himself (launched April 14, 2011). Ideologic Organ was manufactured and distributed by Editions Mego from 2011–2021, and by Shelter Press since 2021. The 2017 third album appeared on the American label W.25th.

Connections

  • Keiji Haino — Japanese avant-garde guitarist, vocalist; decades-long career in experimental rock, noise, and free improvisation
  • Oren Ambarchi — Australian drummer, guitarist; prolific collaborator across experimental and drone music
  • Stephen O’Malley — American guitarist; co-founder of Sunn O))), Gravetemple, KTL; curator of Ideologic Organ
  • [[Bands/Sunn O)))] — O’Malley’s primary band; all three Nazoranai members first played together at a Sunn O))) show (2006)
  • Gravetemple — overlapping membership (O’Malley, Ambarchi); similarly positioned at drone/noise/improv intersection
  • Ideologic Organ — curated by O’Malley; primary label for 2012 and 2014 releases (Discogs label ID: 243287)
  • Editions Mego — manufactured/distributed Ideologic Organ 2011–2021; co-credited on 2012 CD
  • Shelter Press — current parent of Ideologic Organ (from 2021)
  • W.25th — American label that issued the 2017 third album (cat# W25-02)
  • Joe Talia — Australian musician/engineer; mixed all three Nazoranai albums, mastered the 2012 debut
  • Rashad Becker — Berlin-based mastering engineer; cut/mastered 2012 and 2014 releases
