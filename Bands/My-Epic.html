My Epic

Discogs ID: 1951931
Formed: Charlotte, North Carolina, 2005
Genre: Post-rock, experimental rock, Christian rock
Labels: Dreamt Music (2008–2010), Facedown Records (2011–2019), Tooth & Nail Records (2024)
Website: myepicrock.com

Experimental post-rock band founded in Charlotte, North Carolina in 2005. My Epic operate within the Christian rock sphere but draw heavily on post-rock textures, dynamics, and atmospheric production — long instrumental passages, layered guitars, and emotionally weighted lyrics. They signed to Dreamt Music, a sublabel of Facedown Records, for their debut full-length, then graduated to Facedown proper and spent roughly a decade there before moving to Tooth & Nail Records for their 2024 album.

Era Notes

2005–2007: Charlotte origins and self-release

My Epic formed in Charlotte, NC in 2005. Their first release, This Is Rescue (2006), was self-released (Not On Label) and appeared in multiple pressings. No production or personnel credits survive in Discogs for this release.

2008–2010: Dreamt Music era

I Am Undone (2008) launched the band’s label career on Dreamt Music (cat. DM002), with Facedown Records co-distributing (FCD073). The record was produced, engineered, mixed, and mastered by Andrew Prousalis. Amber Blankenship contributed strings; artwork was handled by Dave Quiggle, who would remain the band’s primary visual collaborator through multiple albums. The lineup on I Am Undone was a three-piece: Aaron Stone (2), Jeremiah Austin, and Jesse Stone (2).

Yet (2010) expanded the credits significantly. Matt Goldman (of Smalltown Poets) produced, mixed, and co-wrote on the record — beginning a long working partnership. T.W. Walsh (of Pedro The Lion and Lo Tom) mastered the album. An unusually large group vocal credit listed over a dozen contributors, suggesting community/congregation participation in the recording sessions.

2011–2015: Facedown Records proper

Broken Voice (2011) moved fully to Facedown Records and brought in a wider collaborator network. Producer/mixer Sid Mennon handled production; cellist Christina Brook, violinist Kelly Rathod, and brass player Jesse Clasen added chamber-folk textures. Tanner Morita and Matt Doran joined as performers; Michael Fossenkemper mastered.

Behold (2013) returned Matt Goldman as sole producer, engineer, and mixer. Alex Lowe mastered.

2016–2019: Digital and heavier era

Viscera (2016) was a 5-track digital EP. Discogs has no personnel credits beyond the primary artist listing, suggesting it may have been largely self-produced.

Ultraviolet (2018) listed Nate Washburn and Matt Goldman as co-producers — marking Washburn’s first production credit for the band alongside his role as guitarist. Missy Stone contributed backing vocals. T.W. Walsh returned for mastering.

Violence (2019) was produced by Matt Goldman and mastered by Greg Reierson.

2024: Tooth & Nail Records

Loriella (2024) marked a label move to Tooth & Nail Records. Nate Washburn produced the full record and took on broad multi-instrumental duties (guitar, bass, engineering, mixing). Matt Goldman engineered one track (A5). Alex Camarena and Jesse Stone (2) both appear on drums, suggesting Camarena may have played on some tracks. Tyler Washburn is credited for “Technician, Instrumentation.”

Label Notes

Dreamt Music (Discogs ID 176302) is a sublabel of Facedown Records (ID 67449), itself part of Facedown Records Inc. (ID 544451). Dreamt Music ran from approximately 2008–2010, issuing 7 releases total, including My Epic’s first two LPs and records by Thieves & Liars, Sleep For Sleepers, and Shapes Stars Make. My Epic’s I Am Undone was catalog number DM002; Yet was issued under both Dreamt Music and Facedown labels. After Yet, the band moved directly to the Facedown Records imprint.
