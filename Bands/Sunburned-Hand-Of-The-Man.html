Sunburned Hand Of The Man

US experimental/psychedelic collective. Discogs artist ID: 324139. Also listed as: Sunburned, The Sunburned, The Sunburned Family.

Overview

A large rotating collective based in the New England underground scene, active across psych, noise, and free improvisation. Membership is fluid and extensive.
