Overview

Vainio / Väisänen / Vega — abbreviated VVV — is a collaborative project combining the two Finnish electronic musicians of Pan Sonic with Alan Vega, the Brooklyn-born vocalist and visual artist best known as co-founder of Suicide. The project produced two albums: Endless (1998, Blast First) and Resurrection River (2005, Mego). Both records sit at the intersection of Pan Sonic’s stark industrial minimalism and Vega’s declamatory rockabilly-punk vocal style — a combination that proved one of the era’s most effective cross-genre collisions.

The collaboration grew out of the Vainio/Väisänen orbit around Blast First and the broader European experimental underground during the late 1990s. Vega, who had followed a long arc from Suicide’s confrontational early work through a run of solo records on ZE, Celluloid, and Elektra, found in the two Finns a machine-driven environment that matched his apocalyptic persona. On VVV recordings, Vega’s voice occupies the same register as early Suicide — a rockabilly howl over oscillating electronics — while the Pan Sonic contribution is more stripped and mechanical than on their duo records.

The Three Members

Mika Vainio (1963–2017)

Born 15 May 1963 in Helsinki; died 12 April 2017 in France, aged 53. Full name Mika Tapio Vainio.

Vainio co-founded Pan Sonic (as Panasonic) in 1993 with Ilpo Väisänen and Sami Salo. As a solo artist he operated primarily under the alias Ø and released on a range of labels including Touch, Raster-Noton, Editions Mego, Blast First Petite, and Cosmo Rhythmatic. His early solo work (Onko, Touch, 1997; Ydin, Wavetrap, 1999) established him as a major voice in European minimalist and industrial electronics. Later solo records — Life (…It Eats You Up) (Editions Mego, 2011), Fe₃O₄ - Magnetite (Touch, 2012), Hephaestus (Editions Mego, 2014), Peau Froide, Léger Soleil (Cosmo Rhythmatic, 2015) — consolidated that reputation. He died on the road in France in April 2017, reportedly en route to or from a performance. Posthumous releases continued: Ignis (Cosmo Rhythmatic, 2018), 25082016235210179 (Berlin Atonal Recordings, 2020), and Last Live (Mego, 2021).

Other groups and projects: IBM (with Väisänen), ÄÄNIPÄÄ, Vladislav Delay Quartet, VVE (with Väisänen), Corporate 09, Gagarin-Kombinaatti, Orchestra Guacamole, Kosmos (2), Rancho Relaxo Allstars.

See also: Mika Vainio

Ilpo Väisänen (born 1963)

Born 1963 in Kuopio, Finland. Full name Ilpo Uolevi Väisänen.

Väisänen co-founded Pan Sonic alongside Vainio and has remained active in experimental electronics across multiple projects and aliases. His solo alias output includes work as I-LP-O In Dub, I-LP-ON, IFÖ, Liima, and Piiri. Main solo releases: Moor EP (UPO, 1995), Asuma (Mego, 2001), Syntetisaattori Musiikkia Kuopiosta (Kvitnu, 2016), Brainwave Music No. III (I Shall Sing Until My Land Is Free, 2025).

Other groups: Angel (3), Angelicus, Fistpost, IBM (with Vainio), VVE (with Vainio).

See also: Ilpo Väisänen

Alan Vega (1938–2016)

Born 23 June 1938 in Brooklyn, New York (full name Boruch Alan Bermowitz); died 16 July 2016 in New York City, aged 78.

Vega began his career as a visual artist — his “light sculptures” from discarded electronics were shown at Manhattan galleries including OK Harris during the early 1970s. He co-founded Suicide with Martin Rev around 1970; the duo’s 1977 self-titled debut (produced by Craig Leon and Marty Thau on Red Star Records) is a foundational document in electronic and punk history. Suicide were intermittently active until 2016.

Alongside Suicide activity, Vega built a solo career from 1980 onward. His self-titled debut (PVC/ZE, 1980) and Collision Drive (Celluloid, 1981) extended the rockabilly-electronics fusion. Saturn Strip (Elektra/ZE, 1983), produced by Ric Ocasek, was his major-label moment. His 1990s work — Deuce Avenue (1990), Power On To Zero Hour (1991), New Raceion (1993), Dujang Prang (1995), all on Musidisc — marked a return to machine-driven minimalism. Cubist Blues (Last Call Records, 1996, with Alex Chilton and Ben Vaughn) was a notable diversion.

Vega suffered a stroke in 2012 that curtailed live performance. His final posthumous releases include It (Fader Label, 2017), Mutator (Sacred Bones Records, 2021), After Dark (In The Red, 2021), and Insurrection (In The Red, 2024).

See also: Alan Vega

Label Context

Blast First

Blast First (Discogs: 2500) is a UK label, sub-label of Mute, operated by Paul Smith. It specialised in American noise rock, free jazz, and avant-garde material. Its roster included Sonic Youth, Band Of Susans, Glenn Branca, and — centrally — Pan Sonic, who were on Blast First from Vakio (1995) through Aaltopiiri (2000). Endless (1998, BFFP147) was a natural extension of the label’s ongoing relationship with the Pan Sonic duo.

Mego

Mego (Discogs: 2440) was a Vienna-based experimental label founded in late 1994 by Ramon Bauer, Peter Meininger, and Andreas Pieper. Pita (Peter Rehberg) joined the team in 1995 when Meininger left. The label ran until the end of 2005 and is closely associated with the post-digital “Mego sound” — a glitch-heavy, aggressive approach to electronics that influenced a generation of experimental producers. Resurrection River (MEGO 075, 2005) was released near the end of Mego’s original run. The label was relaunched in January 2006 as Editions Mego, with Rehberg as the primary operator. Mego’s visual universe was closely associated with designer/artist Tina Frank, who worked on VVV’s Resurrection River sleeve.

Context and Connections

The VVV collaboration sits at the convergence of two distinct avant-garde traditions: the Finnish electronic minimalism of Vainio and Väisänen — rooted in Sähkö Recordings, the Blast First/Mute axis, and the European experimental scene centred partly on Vienna’s Mego — and the New York proto-punk/no-wave lineage of Vega, reaching back to Suicide’s art-damaged minimalism of the 1970s. That both traditions shared a commitment to stripped-down, aggressive electronics made the pairing conceptually coherent.

Vega’s connection to Blast First ran separately through Suicide: the label released American Supreme (Blast First/Mute, 2002) and later Station (Blast First, 2007) as a solo album.

Connections within the VVV orbit:

  • Pan Sonic — the primary vehicle for Vainio and Väisänen
  • Suicide — Vega’s primary group with Martin Rev
  • Blast First — released Endless; also home to Pan Sonic 1995–2000
  • Mego — released Resurrection River; relaunched as Editions Mego
  • Sähkö Recordings — Pan Sonic’s founding label (Finnish experimental techno)
  • Jimi Tenor — Finnish musician; guest on Resurrection River
  • Tina Frank — Austrian visual artist; Mego design associate
