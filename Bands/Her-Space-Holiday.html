Her Space Holiday

Discogs ID: 20718
Real name: Marc Paul Bianchi
Aliases: Marc Bianchi (ID 561663), Xoxo Panda
Name variations: HSH, Her Space, 她的太空假期
Active: 1996–2011; returned 2017–present
Origin: San Mateo, California, US
Genres: Indietronic, downtempo, IDM, indie rock, experimental electronic

Solo project of Marc Paul Bianchi. Before Her Space Holiday, Bianchi was active in the California hardcore scene in two bands: Indian Summer and Mohinder. After departing that world he founded the record label Audio Information Phenomena; Train Bridge Recordings then approached him, leading to the first HSH release (Audio Astronomy, 1997). The project began as a bedroom 4-track operation in Northern California.

Around 2001, Bianchi relocated from the Bay Area to Austin, TX. His girlfriend and collaborator Keely played keyboards in early live configurations (she had stepped back from the live show by 2004). Songs are consistently autobiographical — family estrangement, distance, and loss (his grandmother’s death) appear as recurring subjects. Touring included runs with Bright Eyes, The Faint, Pinback, Bob Mould, The American Analog Set, and Lymbyc Systym.

HSH’s self-titled 2011 LP was announced as the final release; Bianchi cited a 2010 Japan performance as the moment he knew the project had run its course (“it just didn’t feel right anymore”). He returned in 2017 and released a comeback EP, Gravity, in 2018 on No More Good Ideas (self-released imprint).
