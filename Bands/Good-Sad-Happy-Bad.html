Good Sad Happy Bad

Discogs artist ID: 8207804 Origin: London, UK Active: 2020–present Former name: Micachu And The Shapes (also recorded as M.A.T.H.E.S.) Label: Textile Records

Overview

Good Sad Happy Bad is a London-based band formed in 2020 when Micachu And The Shapes rechristened themselves under a new name. The renaming marked a genuine shift in internal dynamics: Raisa Khan, previously a backing voice and instrumentalist in the Shapes, stepped forward as primary lead vocalist; Mica Levi moved to guitar and occasional vocals rather than fronting the group; Marc Pell continued on percussion; and new member CJ Calderwood joined as a multi-instrumentalist (saxophone, noises, and various other textures).

The band’s two studio albums under this name — Shades (2020) and All Kinds of Days (2024) — were both released on the French independent label Textile Records (catalog numbers TLP28 and T33 respectively). The music expanded the Shapes’ already eclectic approach into territory that has been classified on Discogs under Rock/Indie Rock (Shades) and Electronic/Rock/Ambient (All Kinds of Days), suggesting a continued willingness to resist genre consolidation.

Origins: Micachu And The Shapes

The predecessor group, Micachu And The Shapes (Discogs ID: 8234107), was active from approximately 2009–2015, releasing on Rough Trade Records and Accidental (label). The core lineup was Mica Levi, Raisa Khan, and Marc Pell. Discography as Micachu & The Shapes:
