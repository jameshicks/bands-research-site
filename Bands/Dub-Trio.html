Dave Holmes and Joe Tomino had a pre-Dub Trio band called Like Yesterday (2004, Bandha Records). Stu Brooks has also recorded as a solo artist and with Fake Legs.

Overview

Dub Trio make mostly live dub music with minimal overdubs. They first toured the NYC area as a nameless group before the descriptive tag “Dub Trio” stuck. Despite the name, their records draw heavily from metal and post-rock: dense guitar, locked bass-drums grooves, and dub echo/delay processing running throughout rather than as an afterthought. All three members contribute keyboards, and Tomino plays melodica across the early records.

Their long-running production partnership with Joel Hamilton (also of A Storm of Light and The Book Of Knots) defined their studio sound from the debut through 2019. Hamilton engineered, mixed, and co-produced all studio records from Exploring The Dangers Of onward.

A notable outside placement: Dub Trio co-wrote “B5” on Mike Patton’s Peeping Tom (3) self-titled album (Anticon, 2006). Patton’s label Ipecac Recordings signed them for Another Sound Is Dying (2008), where Patton wrote lyrics for one track (“Stroke My Smurf,” track 9). The Another Sound Is Dying credit explicitly lists Patton as “Lyrics By” for that track; Joel Hamilton served as executive producer alongside the band.

Joe Tomino is also listed as a member of Battle of Mice and drummed on their 2008 Jesu split.
