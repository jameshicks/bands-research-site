Overview

Slowblow is an Icelandic duo formed in 1994 by Dagur Kári Pétursson and Orri Jónsson, working across experimental, lo-fi, modern classical, ambient, and indie rock. Their six-release catalogue divides evenly between the 1990s and 2000s, moving from the early Icelandic imprint Sirkafúsk to Smekkleysa and the film-distribution label Milan — the latter, alongside credits from Ergis Film Production, A-Film, and Cinéart, points toward significant soundtrack work. They sit firmly within the Reykjavík experimental scene, sharing credits with Sigur Rós, Amiina, Björk, Bang Gang, and Damon Albarn.
