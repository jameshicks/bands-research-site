Overview

Puscifer is an American experimental rock act — spanning alternative, industrial, and downtempo — formed in Los Angeles and launched on record in 2007 by Maynard James Keenan, who describes it as his “creative subconscious” and serves as its only permanent member. Since 2011, Keenan, vocalist/songwriter Carina Round, and multi-instrumentalist Mat Mitchell have formed a settled core trio, supported by a rotating cast that has included Greg Edwards, Jeff Friedl, Gunnar Olsen, and Joshua Eustis. The catalogue — 31 selected releases split roughly evenly across the 2000s and 2010s, then accelerating to 13 in the 2020s — sits primarily on the self-owned Puscifer Entertainment imprint distributed through BMG and Alchemy Recordings. Closest label connections outside that orbit run to A Perfect Circle and, via shared catalogue, Primus; production collaborators include Alan Moulder and Lustmord.
