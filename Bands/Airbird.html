Airbird

Discogs ID: 2219204 (alias of Joel Ford) Active: 2011–2015 (releases on Discogs; broader activity c. 2011–2023 per label credits) Origin: USA Type: Solo alias Labels: The Curatorial Club, Software (2), Cascine

Airbird is the solo electronic alias of Joel Ford, American producer, musician, and co-founder of Driftless Recordings. The alias is confirmed on Discogs as a name variation of Joel Ford (artist ID 636796). Ford is best known as one half of Ford & Lopatin and its earlier incarnation Games (3), both collaborations with Daniel Lopatin (Oneohtrix Point Never). Airbird represents his solo synth-pop and electronic work, running in parallel with those group projects and his production career.

Overview

Airbird emerged in 2011 alongside Ford’s collaborative work on the Software (2) label. The sound sits in synth-pop and house-adjacent electronic music, consistent with the broader Software aesthetic of that period — glossy, melodic, digitally diffuse. The Airbird alias was active primarily 2011–2015, bookended by an early split cassette and a co-billed LP with Los Angeles producer Napolian. All Airbird releases were physical (cassette, 12-inch vinyl, LP).

The split between the Airbird alias and Joel Ford’s name in production credits is clean: Airbird handles the performer/artist role; “Joel Ford” appears as producer, engineer, and composer in additional credits (including on Airbird releases themselves).

Release Details

Airbird / Megafortress (2011, The Curatorial Club TCC016)

Split cassette on The Curatorial Club, a US cassette imprint founded by Michael McGregor (3) of the Chocolate Bobka blog. The A-side is four Airbird tracks; the B-side is four tracks by Megafortress (a separate artist, Discogs ID 2219205, credited to Bill Gillim). Composition credits are listed as “uncredited” on the Discogs entry — Joel Ford (A1–A4) and Bill Gillim (B1–B4) — suggesting no artist names were printed on the physical release. Cover art and layout by Christian Oldham (also uncredited on the release).

Airbird tracks:

  • A1: “Part Of The Game”
  • A2: “Surfer Girl”
  • A3: “Somewhere In A Field”
  • A4: “King For The Night”

City Vs Mountains / Rotating Cloud (2011, Software SFT 003)

Two-track 12” on Software, the third release on the label. Joel Ford produced and contributed music; Daniel Lopatin co-produced; Al Carlson mixed, engineered, and contributed additional production; Joe Lambert mastered.

Tracklist:

  • A: “City Vs Mountains” (5:25)
  • B: “Rotating Cloud” (8:50)

Label Context

Software (2) (sublabel of Mexican Summer) was co-run by Joel Ford and Daniel Lopatin. The two Airbird 12-inches were SFT 003 and SFT 004 — early catalog slots situating Airbird as part of the label’s core identity alongside Ford & Lopatin’s Channel Pressure (SFT 001).

The Curatorial Club was a Brooklyn cassette/tape label. The split with Megafortress (TCC016) was one of multiple releases Ford appeared on through this imprint, including the Games (3) release Spend The Night With.

Cascine is a New York-based label. Mr. Foolish (2015) was Airbird’s only release there; Cascine is also where Ford later appeared as mixer on Teen Daze’s Interior (2021).
