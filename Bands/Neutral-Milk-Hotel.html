Neutral Milk Hotel

Discogs ID: 252434
Formed: 1989, Ruston, Louisiana
Genre: Lo-fi, indie folk, psychedelic pop, noise pop

Lo-fi indie folk band anchored by singer-guitarist-writer Jeff Mangum. One of the central acts of the Elephant Six Collective alongside The Olivia Tremor Control and The Apples In Stereo. Their 1998 album In The Aeroplane Over The Sea — produced by Robert Schneider at Pet Sounds Studio, Athens, Georgia — became one of the most-pressed independent releases in the Discogs dataset (38 pressings to date). Fire Records handled UK/European distribution.

Discography

Everything Is (1993)

Label: Cher Doll Records · Discogs ID: 772950 · 12 pressings

Debut single. Essentially a Jeff Mangum home recording with contributors from the nascent Elephant Six circle: Will Cullen Hart (credited as “W. Cullen,” performs “Postman Corpse”) and Hilarie Sidney (typography on outer edge). Bells by Shani Atwood and Shannon Willis. Sleeve: Mangum and Nancy Ostrander.

UK re-press on Fire Records (BLAZE 79, 1995, ID 536252).

Hype-City Soundtrack (1993)

Label: Elephant Six Recording Co. · Discogs ID: 2098709 · 19 pressings

First release on the Elephant Six Recording Co. imprint — the inaugural catalog entry for the collective. Mangum writes, produces, sings, and plays all instruments except track A5, where Bill Doss plays drums and Shannon Willis plays guitar and vocals.

On Avery Island (1996)

Label: Merge Records / Fire Records · Discogs ID: 420390 · 23 pressings

First full-length. Produced by Robert Schneider (credited “Robert Schnieder” on sleeve). Largely a two-person recording: Mangum plays guitar, drums, voice, bells, xylophone, air organ, Casio, and tapes; Schneider plays home organs, air organs, fuzz bass, xylophone, and arranged the horns. Cover design by Mangum and Lisa Janssen.

In The Aeroplane Over The Sea (1998)

Label: Merge Records · Discogs ID: 368210 · 38 pressings

Second and final studio album. Produced by Robert Schneider. Full five-piece lineup plus an extended horn section and additional players. Art direction by Mangum and Chris Bilheimer; artwork (“Flying Victrola”) by Brian Dewan. Co-writing credits on track 2 split between Mangum, Barnes, Koster, and Spillane; track 5 written by Spillane.

Elephant Six Collective

Neutral Milk Hotel was a founding presence in the Elephant Six Collective, an informal group of bands that emerged from Ruston, Louisiana in the early 1990s and later centered on Athens, Georgia. The collective’s first label release was NMH’s Hype-City Soundtrack (Elephant Six Recording Co., 1993). Core collective bands: The Olivia Tremor Control (Will Cullen Hart, Bill Doss), The Apples In Stereo (Robert Schneider, Hilarie Sidney), Elf Power (Discogs ID 347302 — Jeff Mangum a member).
