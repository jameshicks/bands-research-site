TV On The Radio

Discogs ID: 172047 Origin: Brooklyn, New York Active: 2001–present (extended hiatus post-2014) Genres: Rock, Electronic Styles: Art Rock, Indie Rock, Experimental, Leftfield, Synth-pop Name variations: TVOTR, T.V. On The Radio, TV on the Radio

Overview

TV On The Radio (TVOTR) formed in Brooklyn in 2001 around the creative partnership of vocalist Tunde Adebimpe and multi-instrumentalist/producer Dave Sitek. Guitarist/vocalist Kyp Malone joined early, followed by drummer Jaleel Bunton and bassist/keyboardist Gerard Smith. The group also drew on an extended circle that included flautist/saxophonist Martin Perna (founding member of Antibalas) and trumpeter Todd Simon (also Antibalas, El Michels Affair).

Their sound fused post-punk density, R&B harmonics, noisy guitar loops, and Dave Sitek’s idiosyncratic production — built largely in Brooklyn apartments and home studios before label support arrived. The 2003 Touch And Go EP Young Liars announced them as a distinct voice in mid-2000s indie rock. The EP drew on peers from the interconnected Brooklyn/NYC scene: Nick Zinner and Brian Chase (Yeah Yeah Yeahs), Katrina Ford, Shannon Funchess.

Their label arc ran from a self-released cassette (2002) through Touch And Go, a co-release structure with 4AD, then a major deal with Interscope/DGC for Dear Science (2008), and finally Harvest/Capitol for Seeds (2014). Return To Cookie Mountain (2006) featured a notable studio appearance from David Bowie (vocals on “Province”) and Kazu Makino of Blonde Redhead (“Hours”). Dear Science drew in an expanded brass and string cast including Colin Stetson, Matana Roberts, and Stuart Bogie.

Founding member Gerard Smith died of lung cancer in April 2011, shortly after Nine Types of Light was released. The band went on extended hiatus after Seeds in 2014; Discogs tracks activity through 2024.
