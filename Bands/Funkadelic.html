Funkadelic

Discogs ID: 29923
Origin: Detroit, Michigan, USA
Active: 1968–1981, reunions
Labels: Westbound Records, Warner Bros. Records

George Clinton’s Funkadelic side of the P-Funk collective (alongside Parliament). Junie Morrison joined in 1977, playing keyboards and synthesizer on One Nation Under A Groove (1978, Warner Bros.) and Uncle Jam Wants You (1979). Core members: George Clinton, Bootsy Collins, Bernie Worrell, Eddie Hazel, Michael Hampton, Garry Shider.

Inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame 1997 (as Parliament-Funkadelic).

Parliament and Funkadelic shared virtually all core members and ran simultaneously on different labels throughout the 1970s. See Parliament for full collective personnel tables. Funkadelic was on Westbound (1970–1974) then Warner Bros. (1978–1981); Parliament was on Invictus then Casablanca. Both share the same Discogs aliases list.

Post-1981 Activity

After the Electric Spanking of War Babies (1981, Warner Bros.), Funkadelic went dormant as a recording entity but George Clinton continued working under the name for sporadic releases and live activity.

Reissues and compilations (selected)

The 1989–1993 period saw heavy reissue activity, primarily through Westbound Records (the original Westbound catalog: Funkadelic, Free Your Mind, Maggot Brain, Let’s Take It To The Stage, Standing On The Verge, Tales Of Kidd Funkadelic) and Charly Groove/Charly Records in the UK (One Nation Under A Groove, Uncle Jam Wants You, Hardcore Jollies, etc.). Key compilations: Music For Your Mother (Funkadelic 45s) (1992, Westbound), Motor City Madness – The Ultimate Funkadelic Westbound Compilation (2003, Westbound).

Live releases

  • The Mothership Connection – Live From Houston (1985, Virgin Music Video) — VHS/video release
  • Live At Montreux 2004 (2005, Eagle Records) — confirms continuing live activity

Mothership Reconnection (1998)

Scott Grooves feat. Parliament/Funkadelic, “Mothership Reconnection” (Soma Quality Recordings, SOMA 71). A house/disco record; Daft Punk contributed a remix. Composed by George Clinton, Bootsy Collins, Bernie Worrell. Not a Funkadelic-original release but carries the Funkadelic primary credit.
