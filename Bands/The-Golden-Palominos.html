The Golden Palominos

NYC avant-rock collective, 1981–1996. Discogs artist ID: 41563

No fixed lineup — conceptually a vehicle for drummer/producer Anton Fier to assemble rotating casts of downtown New York and wider avant/rock musicians around a shared groove aesthetic. The name is Fier’s; the membership shifted almost entirely album to album. Output moved from no-wave-adjacent avant-jazz (Celluloid phase, 1983–89) to alternative rock with named vocalists (Restless phase, 1993–96).

Overview

Anton Fier, an alumnus of the no-wave scene and a session drummer for Lydia Lunch, James Chance, and others, formed The Golden Palominos as a studio-first project with Bill Laswell and Celluloid Records founder Jean Karakos behind them. The first album (1983) gathered the cream of the downtown improvised/funk crossover world — John Zorn, Fred Frith, Jamaaladeen Tacuma, Melvin Gibbs, Arto Lindsay, Nicky Skopelitis, and percussionist Aiyb Dieng. By the second album Fier was recruiting rock and pop names (Michael Stipe, John Lydon, Richard Thompson, Jack Bruce, Syd Straw) as track-vocalists. The Celluloid run ended with A Dead Horse (1989); the Restless Records period (1993–96) coalesced around Lori Carson as primary vocalist and a more alt-rock sound. The group issued nothing after Dead Inside (1996).

Connections

  • Anton Fier — founder and only constant; links to Lydia Lunch, James Chance, The Contortions milieu
  • Bill Laswell — recurring bassist/co-producer; see Celluloid Records and Material
  • Jody Harris — guitarist from The Contortions; present throughout Celluloid run
  • John Zorn — downtown NYC axis
  • Fred Frith — Art Bears / Henry Cow lineage
  • Arto Lindsay — no-wave roots (DNA (4)); also solo career
  • Michael Stipe — appearing before R.E.M.’s commercial breakthrough; returns on Drunk with Passion
  • Bob Mould — post-Hüsker Dü; one track on Drunk with Passion
  • Carla Bley — jazz composer/organist; appears on three albums spanning 1985–1991
  • Peter Blegvad — Slapp Happy / Henry Cow lineage; dominant on Blast of Silence
  • Richard Thompson — Fairport Convention alumnus; recurring on late Celluloid and Drunk with Passion
  • Celluloid Records — home for all pre-1991 work
  • Restless Records — 1993–96; parent Rykodisc
