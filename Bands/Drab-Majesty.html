Drab Majesty

Los Angeles darkwave / ethereal wave project. Primary vehicle for Andrew Clinco (performing as alter-ego Deb DeMure), active from 2012. Signed to Dais Records from 2015. Discogs artist ID: 3512996

Overview

Drab Majesty began as a solo bedroom project under the Deb DeMure persona — an androgynous stage alter-ego cultivated by Andrew Clinco. The earliest output (2012) was self-released cassette material. The 2013 split with Eleven Pond on Voltage Control marked the first proper release, and Dais Records picked the project up in 2015.

A second live member, Alex Nicolaou (also credited as Mona D., Discogs IDs: 4996101 / 6318687), handles vocals and keyboards and is listed in the Drab Majesty band roster on Discogs. Nicolaou appears in recording credits on Careless (2015), Modern Mirror (2019), and An Object In Motion (2023).

The project’s sonic signature combines arpeggiating synth textures, gated reverb drums, processed guitar, and DeMure’s detached baritone — drawing comparisons to The Chameleons, Clan of Xymox, and 4AD-era ethereal acts. The Demonstration (2017) is widely considered the breakthrough LP.

Label Context — Dais Records

Dais Records (ID: 93202) is the primary label from 2015 onward. Founded 2007 by Gibby Miller and Ryan Martin (2), based in LA/NYC, distributed by Secretly Distribution. Drab Majesty is the highest-release-count artist in the Dais catalog (141 releases/pressings through 2025).

Key Dais label-mates: Coil, Drew McDowall, Youth Code, VR Sex, Cold Cave, Choir Boy (2), Psychic TV, Genesis Breyer P-Orridge, King Dude, Xeno And Oaklander, ADULT., Youth Code, High-Functioning Flesh.

Scene Connections

  • LA darkwave scene 2010s — overlaps with Cold Showers, Body Of Light, High-Functioning Flesh on Dais Records.
  • Emma Ruth Rundle connection via shared band Marriages and guest vocal on Careless.
  • Rachel Goswell (Slowdive) appears on An Object In Motion — cross-genre shoegaze/ethereal link.
  • Joshua Eustis (Telefon Tel Aviv, Nine Inch Nails, Puscifer, Second Woman) — produced both 2017 and 2019 LPs, key creative partnership.
  • Ben Greenberg (The Men, Uniform (5)) — produced An Object In Motion; NYC noise-rock underground connection.
