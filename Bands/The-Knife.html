The Knife

The Knife

Aug 20, 20267 min read

The Knife

Discogs ID: 87717
Origin: Stockholm, Sweden
Active: 1999–2014 (disbanded November 2014 after final Shaking The Habitual tour shows)
Members: Karin Dreijer · Olof Dreijer
Labels: Rabid Records · Brille Records · Cooperative Music · Mute Records (Fever Ray)

Swedish electronic duo formed by siblings Karin Dreijer (born 7 April 1975, Nacka) and Olof Dreijer (born 27 November 1981). One of the most critically distinctive acts in 2000s European electronic music, self-produced throughout their career, with a noted refusal of mainstream media and a conceptually consistent body of work running from synth-pop to abrasive industrial techno. They operated primarily through their own Rabid Records imprint and disbanded formally in 2014. Karin subsequently continued as Karin Dreijer (also known as Fever Ray); Olof as a solo artist and DJ under aliases including Oni Ayhun.

Name variations on releases include: The Kniφe, The Kniфe (used on the Shaking The Habitual sleeve), K&OD, Knife.

Biography

Formation and Early Period (1999–2002)

The duo formed in 1999 in Stockholm. Olof was still a teenager when they released their debut single “Afraid of You” (Rabid Records, 2000). Their self-titled debut album followed on Rabid Records in 2001, produced entirely by the duo — both credited with accordion, alto saxophone, electric guitar, acoustic guitar, bass guitar, organ, synthesizer, sequencing, sampling, and drum programming, with Karin also handling vocals. Mastering across this period was handled by Henrik Jonsson (2), a consistent figure throughout their catalog.

Karin had previously been a member of Honey Is Cool, releasing several records between 1994–1999.

Deep Cuts Era (2003)

Their second album Deep Cuts (Rabid Records, 2003) expanded the duo’s reach, with mixing largely handled by Christoffer Berg (tracks 1–4, 6–13) alongside The Knife themselves. This album contained “Heartbeats” and “Pass This On,” both of which became cult singles. A cover version of “Heartbeats” by José González achieved wide attention via a Sony Bravia TV advertisement, bringing The Knife retroactive exposure. The duo also scored the Swedish film Hannah Med H the same year, releasing the soundtrack through Rabid Records.

Silent Shout (2006) — Breakthrough

Silent Shout (Rabid Records, 2006) is the duo’s most acclaimed album and a landmark of European electronic music. Self-produced, with mixing by Christoffer Berg (tracks 1–7, 9, 11) and Pelle Gunnerfeldt (tracks 8, 10), and mastered by Henrik Jonsson (2). Artwork by Johan Toorell. The video for the title track was directed by Andreas Nilsson (3).

An accompanying DVD, Silent Shout — An Audio Visual Experience (2006), documented the live show. A UK edition was released through Cooperative Music.

Tomorrow, In A Year (2010) — Opera Collaboration

Tomorrow, In A Year (2010) was an operatic work commissioned by Copenhagen’s Hotel Pro Forma theatre company, led by director Kirsten Dehlholm. Created in collaboration with Mount Sims (credited as Mt. Sims) and PlanningToRock, with live percussion by Hjörleifur Jónsson. Recorded, mixed, and produced jointly by the trio. Mastered by Rashad Becker. The album is based on Darwin’s On the Origin of Species. A “Seeds” EP was also released in 2010, featuring contributions from Jonathan Johansson and Sara Hernández.

Shaking The Habitual (2013) — Final Studio Album

Shaking The Habitual (Brille Records, 2013) — credited on the sleeve as “The Kniфe” — is a double album running over 90 minutes, described as the duo’s most explicitly political work. Mastered by Mandy Parnell. Additional production and live contributions came from Johannes Berglund and Martin Falck. The track “Stay Out Here” featured vocalists Shannon Funchess and Emily Roysdon.

A remix album, Shaken Up Versions (Rabid Records, 2014), followed. Tour-related single/EP releases included A Tooth For An Eye, Raging Lung, and Without You My Life Would Be Boring (all Brille Records, 2013–2014). The Shaking The Habitual Tour ended in November 2014 with the duo’s dissolution.

Live At Terminal 5 (2017)

A posthumous live release documenting a 2013 performance, released on Rabid Records. The live shows involved an extensive cast of dancers and performers including Shannon Funchess, Emily Roysdon, Liv Strömquist, and many others.

Member Solo Projects

Karin Dreijer — Fever Ray

Karin records and performs as Fever Ray (Discogs ID: 1307274). Solo debut album Fever Ray (Rabid Records, 2009). Second album Plunge (Rabid Records, 2017). Third album Radical Romantics (Mute, 2022). Also credited extensively as a vocalist on Röyksopp releases (“What Else Is There?”, Junior, etc.).

Olof Dreijer — Oni Ayhun / DJ Coolof

Olof releases under the aliases Oni Ayhun and DJ Coolof. Solo output includes Souvenir (Rabid Records, 2023) and Rosa Rugosa (Hessle Audio, 2023). Also released Echoes From Mamori (Montehermoso, 2009) and material on Melodies International (2021) and Ad 93 (2024).
