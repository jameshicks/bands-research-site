The Heads

Discogs ID: 67229
Active: 1996
Members: Chris Frantz, Jerry Harrison, Tina Weymouth

Reunion project of three-quarters of Talking Heads, formed without David Byrne. Released one album: No Talking, Just Head (October 8, 1996) on MCA Records, featuring guest vocalists across all tracks. Commercially unsuccessful; no further activity. Originally planned to include live recordings and touring, but Byrne’s legal action curtailed those plans.

Formation and Byrne’s Non-Participation

Weymouth, Frantz, and Harrison spent several years attempting to involve David Byrne in a new Talking Heads project following the band’s effective end after their 1988 album Naked. During that period Byrne repeatedly declined to engage directly — Weymouth told the Hartford Courant in 1996: “We waited eight years. We knew it wasn’t going to happen, when David refused to come to meetings with us. He’d send his lawyer instead.” Frantz told the Orlando Sentinel in 1996: “We tried everything in our power to have David reconsider and join us, but after a certain point, we just gave up.”

The three began informal jam sessions in late 1994, confirmed their chemistry still held, and proceeded to record without Byrne using rotating guest vocalists. The album title No Talking, Just Head was a pointed acknowledgment of the situation.

Byrne subsequently sued the trio, alleging that the band name “The Heads” and the project’s presentation infringed on the Talking Heads trademark — calling it “a pretty obvious attempt to cash in on the Talking Heads name.” The suit was settled out of court. As part of the resolution the band adopted the name “The Heads” rather than anything more directly evoking Talking Heads, and plans for a live album and touring were dropped; only the studio album was released.

Byrne’s own characterisation of the breakdown: “I realized I was not in this as a masochist, and that I don’t need to be whipped and berated.” [source: diffuser.fm/heads-no-talking-just-head/]
