Nine Inch Nails

Industrial rock / electronic project. Founded by Trent Reznor in Cleveland, Ohio, 1988. Discogs artist ID: 3857

Aliases on Discogs: Taxi Driver (10), The Screws (5).

Overview

Nine Inch Nails is Trent Reznor’s principal creative vehicle, running from 1988 to the present. It operates as a de facto solo project in the studio — Reznor writes, performs, programs, and produces everything — with a shifting cast of live members and studio collaborators. The lineup and label situation has changed substantially across several eras.

Atticus Ross became a credited full member from Ghosts I-IV (2008) onward, co-writing and co-producing every NIN studio release since then. Before that, he appeared on With Teeth (2005) and Year Zero (2007) as additional producer / engineer.

The arc breaks into four broad eras: the TVT Records period (1989–1991); the Nothing/Interscope period (1992–2004); the Interscope return with Ross entering (2005–2009); and the self-release Null Corporation era (2008–present, with Columbia/Polydor and Capitol for Hesitation Marks and Bad Witch respectively).

Key Releases & Studio Personnel

Pretty Hate Machine (1989, TVT Records)

Release ID: 145796

Reznor’s debut as NIN. Largely an electronic / synth record, co-produced with John Fryer (who engineered and mixed much of it) and with additional synth programming from Flood (Mark Ellis) and Tim Niemi. Keith LeBlanc engineered and co-mixed several tracks. Chris Vrenna is credited for digital editing and “invaluable assistance” — his first NIN credit. Gary Talpas handled sleeve design.

ProducerTrent Reznor (all tracks), John Fryer (tracks 4–10)
Engineer / MixJohn Fryer, Keith LeBlanc, Ken Quartarone, Kennan Keating
Additional synth programmingFlood, Tim Niemi
Digital editing / assistanceChris Vrenna
Mastered ByBarry Diament (uncredited)
DesignGary Talpas

Broken EP (1992, Nothing Records / Interscope)

Release ID: 116577

First release on Reznor’s own Nothing Records imprint (distributed via Interscope). Co-produced with Flood on several tracks (A2, A3, A6). Additional engineering by Sean Beavan, Paul Kendall, and others. Mastered by Tom Baker (3). Chris Vrenna appears again in an assistant management role. A dog (“Maise”) receives a “Barks & Roars” vocal credit.

The Downward Spiral (1994, Nothing Records / Interscope)

Release ID: 512609

The commercial and artistic breakthrough. Co-produced with Flood (specific tracks: A1, A2, B1–B3, C3, C4). Alan Moulder mixed most of the album (tracks A1–C1, C3–D2) — his first significant NIN credit and the start of a long partnership. Chris Vrenna and Sean Beavan on additional engineering. Charlie Clouser (ID 27509) is credited for “continuity” editing alongside Vrenna and Reznor. Artwork by Russell Mills; mastered by Tom Baker (3).

The Fragile (1999, Nothing Records / Interscope)

Release ID: 4405

Double album; the most elaborate production of the first era. Alan Moulder is credited as Engineer, Mixer, and co-Producer — the most prominent Moulder credit up to this point. Steve Albini recorded supplemental drums. Bob Ezrin supervised “final continuity and flow.” Charlie Clouser and Keith Hillebrandt contributed additional sound design. Dave Ogilvie (Skinny Puppy associate) appears as additional engineer.

With Teeth (2005, Nothing Records / Interscope)

Release ID: 451720

First Atticus Ross credit on a NIN record: “Programmed By, Producer [Additional]” and “Sound Design.” Dave Grohl played drums on tracks 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 10, 11 — a widely noted guest appearance. Jerome Dillon is credited for “additional drums” (programming). Alan Moulder co-produced and engineered.

Year Zero (2007, Interscope Records)

Release ID: 1092575

Concept album. Atticus Ross credited as Engineer and “Produced With” (co-producer) — a step up from With Teeth. Alan Moulder engineered and mixed. Mastered by Brian Gardner. No live drummer; fully programmatic. Art direction by Rob Sheridan (2).

Ghosts I-IV (2008, The Null Corporation)

Release ID: 1303175

First release on Reznor’s own The Null Corporation imprint (ID: 108462); sold as digital download and limited physical editions. Ross is now full co-writer (credited on nearly every track), arranger, programmer, and co-producer — effectively equal partner. Guest “special guests” include Adrian Belew (King Crimson), Alessandro Cortini, and Brian Viglione (The Dresden Dolls). Alan Moulder engineered and mixed.

Hesitation Marks (2013, Columbia / Polydor)

Release ID: 4862637

Return to a major label (Columbia in US, Polydor in Europe). Reznor / Ross / Moulder as the established production trio. Russell Mills again provides artwork. Drum recording by Joe Barresi. The production setup is tighter and more live-feeling than Ghosts.

Bad Witch (2018, Capitol Records / The Null Corporation)

Release ID: 12156667

Six-track mini-album; the third in a trilogy with Not The Actual Events (2016) and Add Violence (2017). Reznor and Ross both credited as full performer, writer, arranger, producer, programmer. Alan Moulder mixed. Mastered by Tom Baker (3). Art direction by 12:01 and Hassan Rahim.
