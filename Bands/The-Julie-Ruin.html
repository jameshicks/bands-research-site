The Julie Ruin

Dance-punk/indie rock band. New York City. Discogs artist ID: 3439059. Formed 2010 by Kathleen Hanna.

Overview

The Julie Ruin is the full-band project Kathleen Hanna assembled in New York City in early 2010, reviving a name she had used as a 1998 solo alias (the solo project is a separate Discogs entry: Julie Ruin, ID 390236). The band rehearses in Greenpoint, Brooklyn and recorded at Oscilloscope and Figure 8 Recording in Brooklyn. The five-piece lineup draws heavily on Hanna’s riot grrrl history: bassist Kathi Wilcox is her co-founding partner in Bikini Kill, while keyboardist Kenny Mellman (of the cabaret duo Kiki And Herb) and guitarist Sara Landeau brought different backgrounds. Carmine Covelli plays drums.

The band previewed live at the Knitting Factory in Brooklyn in December 2010, playing Bikini Kill and Le Tigre songs alongside new material. Their debut LP Run Fast appeared in September 2013, self-released on the band’s own The Julie Ruin imprint (TJR101). Their second album Hit Reset followed in July 2016 on Hardly Art (HAR-096), produced by Eli Crews with the full band co-credited as songwriters.

Genres: Dance-punk, indie rock, electro, punk, synth-pop. The band has been active since 2010 (Wikipedia notes “2010–present”).

Name Disambiguation

  • Julie Ruin (Discogs ID: 390236) — Kathleen Hanna solo alias, active 1998. Released one album: Julie Ruin (Kill Rock Stars, KRS 297, 1998). Entirely Hanna’s bedroom project.
  • The Julie Ruin (Discogs ID: 3439059) — the five-piece band, active 2010–present.

Discogs lists both as separate entities with different IDs, though Julie Ruin’s realname on Discogs is listed as Kathleen Hanna, and Hanna’s own artist page lists “Julie Ruin” as an alias. The Wikipedia article on The Julie Ruin explicitly disambiguates: “This article is about a band. For the earlier Kathleen Hanna solo record, see Julie Ruin.”
