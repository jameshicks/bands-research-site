The Dukes Of Stratosphear

Discogs artist ID: 257163. XTC’s psychedelic alter ego — a 1960s pastiche project recorded under pseudonyms in 1985 and 1987. All four members of XTC participated, crediting themselves with elaborate fake names in keeping with the conceit. The recordings were produced at Townhouse Studios, London, by John Leckie (who used his own pseudonym, Swami Anand Nagara, on the credits). The project ran to two original releases — the 25 O’Clock mini-LP (1985) and the full LP Psonic Psunspot (1987) — plus a US anthology (Chips From The Chocolate Fireball) that combined both. Reissues continued on Andy Partridge’s own Ape House imprint from 2009 through a deluxe 2019 surround-sound box set. In Discogs the Dukes are listed as an alias of XTC; shared aliases on their page include Johnny Japes And His Jesticles, Star Park, The Beech Avenue Boys, and The Three Wise Men.

Research Queue

  • Confirm the recording studio(s) used for Psonic Psunspot (1987) — Townhouse is assumed from the 1985 sessions but sleeve credits on the UK pressing may specify another location.
  • The 25 O’Clock runout groove carries the inscription “TO BE TAKEN TWICE DALI TOWNHOUSE” (side A) and “I CAN SEE FOUR MOLES” (side B) — confirm whether these were cut at Townhouse Studios mastering or elsewhere.
