Eulogies

Discogs ID: 928760

Los Angeles indie rock band active 2007–2011, signed to Dangerbird Records for their entire career. Fronted by Peter Walker (5), who co-founded Dangerbird. Released three full-length albums and a run of EPs/singles across 18 total pressings on the label.

Biography

Formation and Debut (2007)

Eulogies emerged from Los Angeles around 2007, built around Peter Walker (5) — an American singer-songwriter who co-founded Dangerbird Records alongside Jeff Castelaz. The self-titled debut Eulogies (DGB 022, 2007) was co-produced by Walker and Hrishikesh Hirway (of The One AM Radio), recorded by Mike Cresswell, mixed by John Goodmanson, and mastered by Dave Cooley. The 13-track CD on Dangerbird established the band’s compact, melodic indie rock template. The same year the band released a split 7” with Sea Wolf (cat. D8) — “You’re A Wolf” / “If I Knew You” — and a limited picture-disc 7” of “If I Knew You.”

Tempted To Do Nothing EP (2008)

The four-track Tempted To Do Nothing EP (2008) was released in CD, VinylDisc, and promo formats. Tracks from it — “Bad Connection,” “Is There Anyone Here?”, “Tempted To Do Nothing,” “Two Can Play” — carried forward into the subsequent album. Credits are sparse in the DB for this release; producer continuity with Hirway and Walker is assumed based on overlapping tracks appearing on Here Anonymous.

Here Anonymous (2009)

The second full-length, Here Anonymous, appeared in multiple formats: CD (DGB034), a limited numbered double-LP (DGB034V), and an additional CD issue. Co-produced by Peter Walker (5) and Hrishikesh Hirway, with Tony Hoffer serving as executive producer and mixer, and Ryan Hewitt as engineer. Guest credits include Nikki Monninger (of Silversun Pickups) on vocals and Laura Darlington on flute. Peter Walker is the sole credited songwriter (Written-By). A companion EP, Homespun (2009, CD), was also released around this period.

Tear The Fences Down (2010–2011)

The third album was produced by Butch Walker — a significant change in the production chair. It appeared first as a CDr promo in 2010, then in CD (DGB050) and vinyl LP (DGB 050V) editions in 2011, plus a CDr sampler promo. The 10-track album shows no additional credits in the DB beyond Butch Walker as producer, suggesting a tighter, more streamlined studio approach. This was the band’s final release.
