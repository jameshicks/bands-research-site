AEAEA

All-female experimental collective formed in the United States. Discogs artist ID: 3970645. Bandcamp: aeaea.bandcamp.com

Overview

AEAEA is an all-female experimental ensemble bringing together five artists whose individual careers span dark ambient, post-punk, neoclassical, and avant-garde territory. The group centres on vocalist Jarboe (ex-Swans) alongside pianist Annie Hogan (longtime collaborator of Marc Almond), violinist and producer Kris Force (Amber Asylum), violinist and thereminist Meredith Yayanos (The Parlour Trick, Feminazgul), and cellist Zoë Keating (Rasputina). The name AEAEA refers to the mythological island of Circe.

One studio album is documented in Discogs: Drink The New Wine (2014), released on the Argentine label Twilight Records (2). The album was produced, mixed, and mastered by Kris Force, who also created the artwork and contributed painting. Activity beyond that release is documented primarily on Bandcamp, where the group has released additional material.

The project sits at a nexus of several overlapping scenes — post-industrial, neoclassical, dark folk — and draws on each member’s individual background. Jarboe and Kris Force have the longest collaborative thread outside AEAEA, with Force contributing violin and production to multiple Jarboe solo albums (Mahakali, 2008; co-primary on The Path, 2010; The Cut Of The Warrior, 2018). Jarboe and Force also share membership in the large ensemble The Sweet Meat Love And Holy Cult.
