The Walkmen

Discogs ID: 138073
Origin: New York City / Washington DC, USA
Active: 2000–2013; reunion 2022–present
Labels: StarTime International → Record Collection → Gigantic Music / Fat Possum Records / Bella Union

American indie rock band. Formed in 2000 from the intersection of two earlier acts: Jonathan Fire*Eater (whose members included Walter Martin, Paul Maroon, and Matt Barrick) and The Recoys (whose members included Hamilton Leithauser and Peter Bauer). Seven studio albums over thirteen years; hiatus 2013; reunion announced 2022.

Bows + Arrows (2004)

Label: Record Collection (cat. 48680-2)
Format: CD (12 pressings)
Styles: Indie Rock

Self-produced, with “The Rat” (track 2) produced by Dave Sardy and engineered by Greg Gordon. Stuart Sikes engineered and mixed tracks 3–11. Mastered by Fred Kevorkian. Standout single: “The Rat.” Cover photography by Anna Leithauser. Walter Martin credited with “Organ, Pandemonium."

"Pussy Cats” Starring The Walkmen (2006)

Label: Record Collection
Format: CD/LP (6 pressings)
Styles: Indie Rock

Full cover of Harry Nilsson’s 1974 Pussy Cats LP (originally produced by John Lennon). Self-produced and recorded/mixed. Includes string and brass arrangements; Kevin McMahon as engineer/mixer on some tracks.

A Hundred Miles Off (2006)

Label: Record Collection
Format: CD/LP (10 pressings)
Styles: Indie Rock

Band-recorded and self-mixed. Partly recorded with Don Zientara and Kevin McMahon.

You & Me (2008)

Label: Gigantic Music (GTC 17)
Format: LP (Limited Edition; 15 pressings)
Styles: Indie Rock, Blues/Rock

Produced by Chris Zane at Gigantic Studios, NYC. Alex Aldi served as 2nd engineer. 14 tracks including “Dónde Está La Playa,” “In The New Year,” “The Blue Route,” “Canadian Girl.” Mastered by Emily Lazar via Lodge. Also released on other labels including Talitres (Europe).

Lisbon (2010)

Label: Fat Possum Records (FP1228-1)
Format: LP (17 pressings)
Styles: Indie Rock

Split production across three approaches:

  • John Congleton produced, engineered, and mixed 6 tracks (“Juveniles,” “Angela Surf City,” “Victory,” “All My Great Designs,” “Woe Is Me,” “Torch Song”)
  • Chris Zane engineered and mixed 4 tracks (“Blue As Your Blood,” “Stranded,” “While I Shovel The Snow,” “Lisbon”)
  • Band self-produced/mixed 1 track (“Follow The Leader”)

Alex Aldi — 2nd engineer at Gigantic Studios across the Zane-produced tracks. Mastered by Greg Calbi. Lacquer cut by Jason Ward. Notable brass section: Paul Brandenburg (trumpet/horn section leader), plus multiple trumpet and trombone players. String section: Dana Lyn, Leyna Marika Papach, Rachel Golub (violin), Alex Waterman and Clara Kennedy (cello). Guest vocals: Alec Ounsworth (Clap Your Hands Say Yeah) on “All My Great Designs.” Photographs by Luigi Ghirri (cover), Fred Maroon (inside).

Heaven (2012)

Label: Fat Possum Records (FP1273-1)
Format: LP (8 pressings; plus a separate 2-pressing edition)
Styles: Indie Rock

Produced, engineered, and mixed by Phil Ek at Bear Creek Studios, Seattle. 2nd engineer Cameron Nicklaus; Jerry Streeter as 2nd assistant. Mastered by Greg Calbi. Strings on “Heartbreaker” and “Line By Line” by Erika Pierson, Jen Kozel, Jenn Glenn, Victoria Parker. Robin Pecknold (Fleet Foxes) contributes vocals on four tracks: “We Can’t Be Beat” (A1), “Jerry Jr.’s Tune” (B3), “No One Ever Sleeps” (B6), “Dreamboat” (B7). Morgan Henderson (Fleet Foxes) plays additional percussion on “The Love You Love.”
