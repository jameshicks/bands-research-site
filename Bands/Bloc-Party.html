Overview

Bloc Party are a British indie rock band from London, formed in 2003, with a catalogue of over 40 releases concentrated heavily in the 2000s. Their primary label home has been Wichita Records (2004–2024), with early releases distributed through V2 and Vice and later work on Infectious Music. Producers Paul Epworth and Jacknife Lee are the dominant collaborators by shared credit count — 118 and 94 respectively — suggesting a close ongoing working relationship that tracked the band’s progression from guitar-driven indie rock into electro and house. The credited membership includes vocalist Kele Okereke alongside Gordon Moakes, Justin Harris, Harry Deacon, and Daniel Pugsley.
