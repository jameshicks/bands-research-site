Tall Dwarfs

New Zealand rock duo formed 1981, comprising Chris Knox and Alec Bathgate. Discogs ID: 261407. Known for lo-fi psychedelic indie, use of household objects as percussion, and their place in the Dunedin Sound. The Olivia Tremor Control cited them as an influence. In 2005 the two bands toured the US together; Cloud Recordings issued US editions of Weeville (CLD006) and Fork Songs (CLD007) that year.
