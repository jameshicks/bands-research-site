Solution Unknown

Punk/hardcore band from Louisville, Kentucky. Active 1984–1987. Discogs artist ID: 1263451.

Notable as a pre-Slint project connecting David Pajo and Todd Brashear — both later central to Slint — along with personnel orbiting the founding generation of the Louisville underground. The band’s three releases document the transition from early Louisville hardcore (1984 self-released cassette) through the Self-Destruct Records 7” era to a final LP tracked at Inner Ear Studios in Arlington, Virginia with Don Zientara engineering.

The lineup shift between Justice For All (1984) and the 1986 EP is significant: Schmidt moves from bass/vocals to vocals only, Bucayu shifts from bass to guitar, and Chappelle joins on bass. By Karen (1987), Pajo has expanded his role to include lead guitar and additional guitar alongside drums, and Brashear is now on guitar.

The debut is a self-released cassette with 14 tracks on a single side. No recording location or engineer is credited in the Discogs entry. The two confirmed players are Mike Bucayu (Bass, Vocals) and David Pajo (Drums, Guitar) — the other members (Schmidt, Chappelle) are not individually credited in the database. Given the lineup documented on later releases, Schmidt was almost certainly the vocalist.

The band’s most documented release and the only LP. Recorded at Inner Ear Studios in Arlington, Virginia — the primary studio of the Washington DC hardcore and post-hardcore scene — with Don Zientara engineering and co-producing. This is an unusual geographic reach for a Louisville band: traveling to DC’s premier punk studio for a final album suggests both ambition and connections to the DC scene.

By Karen, Pajo is the band’s most multi-credited performer: drums plus two guitar credits (lead guitar and “more guitar”). Brashear’s appearance here — on guitar, before his later role as Slint’s bassist — is his first documented release credit in Discogs. The band co-produced alongside Zientara.

Notable: “Ethan A.D.” (A6) is credited to Ethan Buckler — co-founder of Slint and founding bassist on Tweez. This places the Solution Unknown circle in direct creative contact with both Walford (EP writing credit, 1986) and Buckler (track credit, 1987), the two figures who bracket Slint’s early bass history. “Bad Attitude” (A5) is credited to Malignant Growth, a Louisville-area band.

Connections to the Louisville Scene

Solution Unknown sits at the intersection of the Louisville hardcore underground and the circle that would crystallize as Slint:

  • David Pajo (Drums/Guitar, all releases) — joined Slint as guitarist; Tweez (1989), Spiderland (1991). Went on to Tortoise, Papa M, Zwan, and extensive solo work.
  • Todd Brashear (Karen, 1987) — became Slint’s bassist from Spiderland forward, replacing Ethan Buckler. Spiderland and subsequent Slint activity. Also uncredited bass on Slint’s Spiderland per the Discogs record.
  • Britt Walford (co-writer, “Control,” 1986) — principal Slint drummer; also Squirrel Bait, King Kong (3), The Breeders (as “Shannon Doughton”). His co-writing credit on the Taken For Granted EP predates any formal Slint session.
  • Ethan Buckler (songwriter, “Ethan A.D.,” 1987) — Slint co-founder and original bassist. Named in a track credit on Karen, indicating a direct social/creative connection.
  • Mike Bucayu (Guitar/Bass, all releases) — became a significant presence in the Louisville scene, producing multiple Self-Destruct Records releases in the late 1980s–early 1990s (Undermine, Kinghorse, Dybbuk (2), Antman, Blaze, Evergreen (5)) and playing bass on Kinghorse’s Kinghorse LP (Caroline Records, 1990).
  • Eric Schmidt (Vocals) — appeared on the 1992 Self-Destruct Records release Breakfast T (Dybbuk (2)) as a vocalist, indicating continued involvement in the scene post-Solution Unknown.
  • Don Zientara — engineer/co-producer on Karen; operator of Inner Ear Studios, the primary DC hardcore recording site; engineered records for Fugazi, Minor Threat, and effectively the entire Dischord catalog.
  • Howie Gano — engineer and co-producer on Taken For Granted E.P.; Louisville’s house engineer, also responsible for both Squirrel Bait records (1985 EP and Skag Heaven, 1987).

Open Questions

  • Recording location for Justice For All: Likely recorded in Louisville, possibly with Howie Gano — but no engineer or studio credit exists in the Discogs database. The 1984 date pre-dates the band’s relationship with Self-Destruct Records.
  • Full lineup on Justice For All: Only Bucayu and Pajo are individually credited; Schmidt and Chappelle’s involvement on the cassette is presumed based on later lineup documentation.
  • Todd Brashear’s pre-Karen history: Karen (1987) is his first Discogs-documented credit anywhere. Whether he played with any Louisville bands before joining Solution Unknown is not in the database.
  • Eric Schmidt and Kent Chappelle post-Solution Unknown: Schmidt appears on the 1992 Dybbuk (2) LP (Breakfast T, Self-Destruct); Chappelle has no credits beyond Solution Unknown in Discogs.
  • Britt Walford’s involvement: His co-writer credit on “Control” (1986) is undocumented in terms of how the collaboration happened — whether he played on the session, contributed lyrics by mail, or attended a rehearsal is not in Discogs.
  • “Ethan A.D.” origins: Whether the Ethan Buckler track was a pre-existing Buckler composition covered by Solution Unknown, or a co-written piece, is unclear — the Discogs credit lists Buckler as sole written-by.
  • Mike Bucayu beyond Discogs: His production career at Self-Destruct ran through 1993; later credits thin out after Wholeness Of The Soul (2009, Noise Pollution). Current activity unknown.
  • Karen release quantity: The LP shows only 1 pressing count vs. 2 for the EP — whether this reflects relative rarity or incomplete Discogs data is unclear.
