Levitation (2)

British psychedelic / progressive rock band, formed 1990 in London. Discogs artist ID: 216453.

Founded by Terry Bickers after his departure from The House Of Love in late 1989. Disbanded 1994; two reunion releases on Flashback Records (6) in 2015.
