Tanlines

Discogs ID: 1447282
Members: Eric Emm (guitar, vocals), Jesse Cohen (percussion)
From: Brooklyn, New York
Active: 2008–present
Labels: Young Turks (2008); True Panther Sounds (2010–2015); Merge Records (2023)

Brooklyn indie-electronic duo. Eric Emm brought a math-rock background from Don Caballero and Storm And Stress; Jesse Cohen came from Brooklyn dance-punk act Professor Murder. Tanlines blend guitar, percussion, and synthesizers into a warm, melodic strain of electronic indie pop.

History

Formation and Early Singles (2008–2010)

Tanlines formed in Brooklyn in 2008. Debut release: “New Flowers” b/w “Power Everything” (12”, Young Turks YT020T, 2008) — an early indication of the duo’s electronic/indie sound, released on the London-based Young Turks label (home of The xx). A 2009 split 7” single with SALEM (6), “Babydaddy / S.A.W.” (Fader Label / Southern Comfort, cat# 2469-CORPRO), followed. Side A (“Babydaddy”) is by SALEM (6); Side B (“S.A.W.”, 3:36) is by Tanlines, who are also credited as Producer. Artwork by AJ Fusco. Limited edition 7” at 45 RPM.

A 2010 split release with Memory Tapes (“Real Life”) circulated on Not On Label.

Settings EP (2010)

First release on True Panther Sounds (TRUE-020-1). Self-produced by Tanlines; engineered by Patrick Ford; mastered by Joe LaPorta with assistant mastering by Heba Kadry. Six tracks: “Reinfo,” “Real Life,” “Three Trees,” “Policy Of Trust,” “Bees,” and “Z” — the last featuring Glasser (Cameron Mesirow) on vocals. The EP established the duo’s signature of interlocking percussion, layered guitar, and synthesized textures.

Mixed Emotions (2012)

Debut full-length (True Panther Sounds TRUE-066-2). Eleven tracks: “Brothers,” “All Of Me,” “Green Grass,” “Abby,” “Yes Way,” “Not The Same,” “Lost Somewhere,” “Real Life,” “Rain Delay,” “Cactus,” “Nonesuch.” Mixed by Jimmy Douglass (credited across multiple pressings); mastered by Greg Calbi. Expanded on the Settings sound into a complete album statement.

Highlights (2015)

Second LP (True Panther Sounds TRUE-110-1). Co-produced with Chris Taylor (7) (Grizzly Bear) and Patrick Ford; mixed by Rob Kinelski; mastered by Joe LaPorta. Cover photography by Autumn DeWilde; interior photography by Jenny Hueston. Management: Will Hubbard.

Taylor contributed backing vocals (tracks A5, B1), additional guitar (A3), synth (A2, A4, B2), tambourine and piano (A5), plus co-production credit across multiple tracks. Patrick Ford played piano on “Thinking” (B4). Ten tracks: “Pieces,” “Slipping Away,” “Palace,” “Two Thousand Miles,” “Invisible Ways,” “Bad Situations,” “Running Still,” “Thinking,” “If You Stay,” “Darling Dreamer.”

Hiatus and The Big Mess (2023)

After Highlights the duo went quiet for eight years. The Big Mess (Merge Records MRG828, 2023) marked their return, with production by Peter Katis (known for work with Interpol, The National). Eleven tracks: “The Big Mess,” “Outer Banks,” “New Reality,” “Burns Effect,” “Clouds,” “Unreal,” “Arm’s Length Away,” “Endless Love,” “Speed,” “Hold On,” “The Age Of Innocence.” Written by Eric Emm.
