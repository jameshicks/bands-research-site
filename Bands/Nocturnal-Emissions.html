Overview

Nocturnal Emissions is a long-running UK sound-art project initiated in London in or around 1980 by Nigel Ayers together with Caroline K (Caroline Kaye Walters, ID 96013) and Daniel Ayers (ID 502909). Their self-description: “a sound art project…releasing numerous records and CDs in music styles ranging from electro-acoustic, musique concrète, hybridised beats, sound collage, post-industrial music, ambient and noise music.” The sound work is part of an ongoing multimedia campaign of guerrilla sign ontology utilising video art, film, hypertext, and other documents.

Nigel Ayers is the sole continuous member; “numerous other members have come and gone.” The project is now based in Cornwall. By the 2010s–2020s it operates largely as a Nigel Ayers solo vehicle, occasionally with guest contributors.

Formation and early years (1979–1983)

Nigel Ayers co-ran Sterile Records (ID 9411) with Caroline K from 1979. Nocturnal Emissions coalesced from this milieu; their debut releases appeared on Sterile from 1981. The earliest recordings were raw industrial noise and tape collage, influenced by the post-punk/industrial scene of the time.

Mid-period: Earthly Delights and international licensing (1987–1994)

Following the closure of Sterile, Ayers launched Earthly Delights (ID 11873), which carried the Nocturnal Emissions catalog through the late 1980s and 1990s. This era saw significant stylistic broadening — from industrial noise into ambient, musique concrète, and organic sound design. Jon Wozencroft (Touch label) contributed gamelan sound reprocessing to The World Is My Womb.

Soleilmoon era and compilations (1994–2003)

Nocturnal Emissions had a significant presence on Soleilmoon Recordings (ID 175), with 18 releases documented 1987–2003 (beginning with The World Is My Womb as SOL 7 in 1987). Holy Of Holies (SOL, 1994) and the Befehlsnotstand / Shake Those Chains reissue represent this relationship.

The 1992 Seminal Works compilation (Earthly Delights) provided an overview of early material. The 2008 Vinyl-on-demand box set Lest We Forget — Work In Progress 1979–1988 collated early archive material.

Later period: self-released and small-label work (2005–present)

By the mid-2000s, Nocturnal Emissions moved largely to self-release and boutique labels. A Mannequin (Berlin) retrospective compilation in 2017 — compiled by Alessandro Adriani, designed by Simon Crab, mastered by Rude 66 — brought the early catalog to a new audience. The Minimal Works series (2020–2024) on self-released CDR/download represents an ongoing prolific output.
