Wide Boy Awake

London post-punk/dance band, active 1982–1984. Discogs artist ID: 383814

Overview

Wide Boy Awake were a London duo formed around Kevin Mooney and John Keogh, playing a funk-inflected post-punk style that combined synth electronics with bass-driven grooves. Mooney had previously been the bassist in Adam and the Ants during the Kings of the Wild Frontier era (1980), and the rhythmic sensibility he developed there fed directly into Wide Boy Awake’s dancefloor-oriented sound.

All of the band’s releases appeared on RCA (and its subsidiary RCA Victor) between 1982 and 1984. Debut 12-inch “Chicken Outlaw” / “Slang Teacher” (1982) established their template: punchy electro-funk with angular post-punk edges. The self-titled mini-album Wide Boy Awake (1983, US release) collected five tracks including both sides of the debut single and three further songs. The single “Billy Hyena” (1983/1984 — promo US release 1983, wider release 1984) brought in New York producer François Kevorkian, representing a more club-oriented direction. Two tracks — “Slang Teacher” and “Set Fighter” — were later included on the 2021 Cherry Red box set Shake The Foundations (Militant Funk & The Post-Punk Dancefloor 1978-1984), placing the band squarely in the documented canon of British post-punk dancefloor music.

The brief also mentions CBS and Arista as release labels; Discogs records only RCA/RCA Victor for all Wide Boy Awake releases — this may reflect pre-Discogs demo or label negotiation history not captured in the database.

After Wide Boy Awake dissolved, Mooney and Keogh both appeared in Max (31) (also known as Lomax), a later group also featuring Matthew Ashman (ex-Bow Wow Wow, Adam and the Ants), Leslie Winer, John Reynolds, and Tex Axile.

Connections

  • Adam and the Ants — Kevin Mooney was bassist during the Kings of the Wild Frontier period (1980)
  • Max (31) — post-Wide Boy Awake group uniting Mooney and Keogh with Matthew Ashman, Leslie Winer, John Reynolds, Tex Axile; released on Chrysalis and ZTT
  • Bow Wow Wow — Matthew Ashman (Max (31) co-member) was guitarist
  • Leslie Winer — Max (31) member; poet and artist, later credited as an early figure in trip-hop
  • Nick Glennie-Smith — primary producer of Wide Boy Awake’s early material; later Hollywood film composer (The Rock, We Were Soldiers)
  • François Kevorkian — produced “Billy Hyena”; major New York dance music producer and DJ
  • Trevor Spencer — co-producer with Glennie-Smith; had earlier work with Leo Sayer and in library music (KPM)
  • Shake The Foundations (Militant Funk & The Post-Punk Dancefloor 1978-1984) — 2021 Cherry Red anthology including “Slang Teacher” and “Set Fighter”
  • JoBoxers — shared the Lee Cooper Jeans promo 12” (1983)
