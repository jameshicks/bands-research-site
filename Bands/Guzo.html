Overview

Guzo is the solo project of Madelyn Villano, a violinist and multi-instrumentalist based in Portland, Oregon. Villano is also a member of Smegma — the long-running Los Angeles/Portland free-improvisation collective — and of the duo Giggles (4) alongside Alieta Herrera-Train.

The name “Guzo” appears on three releases between 2013 and 2017, all on Pigface Records, the in-house imprint tied to the Smegma circle. The project is tightly embedded in that network: both of the Guzo split releases are with other members of the Smegma ensemble (Smegma itself; Ace & Duck).

On the 2017 release An Evening In Copenhagen, the Guzo track side credits Madelyn Villano explicitly for Violin, confirming the instrument she brings to the project.

Release Detail

American Girl (2013, Pigface Records 021)

Solo cassette, two untitled sides (A and B). No additional performers credited in Discogs. Genre: Electronic, Experimental. Limited edition format. The first Guzo release and the solo statement of the project before the collaborative splits.

40 Years Of Smegma Tour (2014, pig 024)

Split cassette issued to accompany the 40th anniversary Smegma tour. Side A1 is titled “Guzo” (15:22) — a solo Villano performance. Sides A2 and B1 (“Smegma Pt. 1,” 11:02; “Smegma Pt. 2,” 30:20) document Smegma live.

Credited performers across both sides:

  • Ace Farren Ford — Performer
  • Dennis Duck — Performer
  • Ju Suk Reet Meate — Performer
  • Madelyn Villano (= Guzo) — Performer
  • Rock ‘N’ Roll Jackie (credited as “Rock And Roll Jackie”) — Performer

Genres: Electronic, Rock. Style: Experimental.

An Evening In Copenhagen (2017, cat 029)

Split cassette of two live sets at Jazz House Copenhagen, 2016.

Side A — Ace & Duck:

  • Track A1: “Spitballs Scalped An Adolescent”
  • Track A2: “I Ain’t Gonna Drink No More”
  • Track A3: “Blues By Nature”
  • Track A4: “Trio” (with Mette Rasmussen on Alto Saxophone)
  • Track A5: “Good Ale”

Side B — Guzo:

  • Track B: Untitled (Madelyn Villano: Violin)

Credits on the Ace & Duck side:

  • Ace Farren Ford — Vocals, Alto Saxophone, Harmonica, Recorder
  • Dennis Duck — Drums, Percussion, Vocals
  • Mette Rasmussen — Alto Saxophone (track A4 only)

Guzo side credits Madelyn Villano explicitly for Violin. Genres: Electronic, Rock. Styles: Avantgarde, Experimental.

Other Release: Full-On Rager (2014, Obsolete Units)

Outside the Pigface Records catalog, Guzo appears on a split release with The Tenses titled Full-On Rager (2014, Obsolete Units, release ID 5485507). Credited performers:

  • Ju Suk Reet Meate — Performer (all tracks)
  • Madelyn Villano (= Guzo) — Performer (Side A)
  • Oblivia (3) — Performer (all tracks)

This is the only known Guzo release outside Pigface Records.
