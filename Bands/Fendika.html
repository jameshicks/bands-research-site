Fendika

Discogs ID: 3190879 Website: https://fendika.org/ Based: Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

Fendika is an Ethiopian music ensemble based in Addis Ababa, led by dancer and choreographer Melaku Belay. The group operates a venue in Addis Ababa, tours internationally, and records music rooted in traditional Ethiopian regional styles — Gurage, Wollo, Agaw, Gonder, Gume — brought together with collective improvisation and dance. Internationally they are best known for their collaborations with Dutch post-punk group The Ex and with the Norwegian large-ensemble free-jazz unit Paal Nilssen-Love Large Unit. Their recordings span 7” singles to full-length albums across four different labels.

Key Release Detail

Lale Guma / Addis Hum (2015, EX 140)

7” single, two-track. Fendika performing alongside all four members of The Ex. Credits: Andy Moor (2) (baritone guitar, guitar, mastering, photography); Terrie Ex / Terrie Hessels (guitar); Arnold de Boer (guitar, vocals, mastering); Katherina Bornefeld (drums, vocals); Misale Legesse (kobero drum); Endris Hassen (masinko); Melaku Belay (dance); Zenash Tsegaye (dance); Nardos Tesfaw (vocals). Recorded by Gavin McDowell and Corno Zwetsloot; mixed by The Ex and Corno Zwetsloot. Cover design: Emma Fischer.

Birabiro (2016, AS27CD)

Solo debut album on Terp Records. Eight tracks drawing on Ethiopian regional styles: Gurage, Wollo, Agaw, Gonder, Gume. No additional credits listed on Discogs.

Tracklist: “Yewolaya Weyo (Gurage)” / “Maleda (Wollo)” / “Zelesegna” / “Nargi (Agaw)” / “Musica / Satenaw” / “Ere Gedaye Gedaye / Shellela / Lale Guma (Gonder)” / “Anbessa (Gume)” / “Tezeta”

Ethiobraz (2019, PNL049)

Joint album with Paal Nilssen-Love Large Unit, recorded in Norway and released on Paal Nilssen-Love’s own label PNL. A 10-track session mixing traditional Ethiopian material with the large-ensemble free-jazz sound of Large Unit (21 members). Most tracks credited “Traditional Song From Ethiopia”; “Fluku” by Paal Nilssen-Love; “Capoeira Mata Um” draws on Brazilian capoeira via Celio de Carvalho and Paulinho Bicolor. Produced by Paal Nilssen-Love. Cover design by Lasse Marhaug. Engineered, recorded, mixed, and mastered by Christian Obermayer.

Notable Large Unit members on this session: Terrie Ex (electric guitar), Lasse Marhaug (cover design), Paal Nilssen-Love (drums, producer), Andreas Wildhagen (drums), Mats Äleklint (trombone), Thomas Johansson (trumpet), Per Åke Holmlander (tuba), Julie Kjær (flute, alto saxophone), Klaus Ellerhusen Holm (clarinet, saxophones), Kristoffer Berre Alberts (saxophones), Kalle Moberg (accordion), Jon Rune Strøm (bass), Christian Meaas Svendsen (bass), Ketil Gutvik (electric guitar), Tommi Keränen (electronics), Celio de Carvalho (percussion, berimbau), Paulinho Bicolor (cuica). Fendika members: Melaku Belay (dance, vocal), Zenash Tsegaye (dance), Nardos Tesfaw (vocal), Fassika Hailu (krar), Mesay Abebaye (kobero), Habetamu Yeshambel (masinko).

Gojo (2023, FBL007)

Collaboration with K-Sanchis (also known as Trio Kazanchis), a Swiss trio comprising Steve Buchanan (alto saxophone), Jeroen Visser (baritone and tenor saxophone, harmonium, clarinet), and Marina Iten. Nine tracks; Fendika members contributing: Melaku Belay (vocals), Nardos Tesfaw (vocals), Mesay Abebaye (kebero), Habetamu Yeshambel (masinko), Sentayehu Tadesse (kirar), Robel Solomon (kirar, bass), Emebet Woldetsdik (vocals/backing vocals).

Broader Context: Melaku Belay

Melaku Belay also appeared as dance performer (not under the Fendika name) on:

  • Y’Anbessaw Tezeta (2013, Terp Records) — Getachew Mekuria & The Ex & Friends — credited for dance alongside a larger ensemble produced by The Ex

He is additionally listed as a member of XVIII-21 Le Baroque Nomade (Discogs ID: 8093878), a French baroque ensemble — suggesting international reach beyond the Ethiopian/free-jazz circuit.
