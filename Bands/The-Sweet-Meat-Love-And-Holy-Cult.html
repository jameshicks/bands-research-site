The Sweet Meat Love And Holy Cult

Large experimental folk-rock collective. Discogs artist ID: 3113062. Primarily based in the southeastern United States; some members located in Brazil and Bali at time of formation. Active at least 2007–2013 per Discogs catalog.

Overview

The Sweet Meat Love And Holy Cult is a large collective of songwriters described by Discogs as referencing folk rock, with a southeastern US base and a membership that spread geographically to Brazil and Bali. The ensemble is anchored by two prominent figures from the US underground: vocalist and keyboardist Jarboe (Swans, World of Skin) and composer-violinist Kris Force (Amber Asylum, Culper Ring). The group’s distributed structure — with tracks written individually or in small sub-groups by different members — functions as a collective songwriting pool rather than a conventional band.

Its two Discogs-catalogued releases span six years: the 2007 seven-inch “Introducing The Sweet Meat Love And Holy Cult” (Paradigms Recordings, UK) and the 2013 double-CD album “Mystagogue” (Atavistic, US). The 2007 single was credited to “Jarboe Introducing The Sweet Meat Love And Holy Cult,” positioning Jarboe as the public-facing lead; the 2013 album was credited as “Jarboe Presents The Sweet Meat Love And Holy Cult,” consistent with that framing. Songwriting credits on Mystagogue are distributed across Henry Derek, Jarboe, Brian Castillo, Diana Obscura, Damon Young, Paul Mercer, Nic Le Ban, Jerry Blue, Antic Clay (Blind Michael Bradley), Elizabeth Hummel, and Leo Nazar — essentially the full core membership.

The ensemble’s name appears to have originated from a set of Bandcamp or informal recordings before the Discogs entries; many likely releases remain uncatalogued.

Connections

  • Jarboe — co-anchor of the collective; credited as primary artist on both releases; contributed voice, keyboards, recordings, and songwriting throughout
  • Kris Force — member; mastered “Mystagogue”; violin on both releases; overlapping connection via AEAEA and The Path (2010, Paradigms Recordings)
  • Paradigms Recordings — released the 2007 seven-inch; same label released Jarboe / Kris Force The Path and Amber Asylum’s GardenOfLove
  • Atavistic — released “Mystagogue” (2013); Chicago experimental imprint
  • AEAEA — parallel all-female collective featuring both Jarboe and Kris Force
  • Amber Asylum — Kris Force’s primary project; also appeared on Paradigms Recordings
  • The Changelings — US folk/alternative band featuring Chandler Rentz, Damon Young, and Paul Mercer — three Sweet Meat members came from this group
  • Swans — Jarboe’s foundational group; informs her vocal and experimental approach here
