British Electric Foundation

Sheffield electronic production project. Discogs artist ID: 24220. Also known as B.E.F.

Overview

Production and recording vehicle for Martyn Ware and Ian Craig Marsh, operating alongside (and predating) Heaven 17. Best known for cover-version albums featuring guest vocalists. Formed circa 1980 after both left The Human League.
