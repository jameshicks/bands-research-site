Pales (2)

Discogs ID: 7710561

Berlin-based duo formed by guitarist/composer Julia Reidy and drummer Samuel Hall (2). Both members are Australian-born and relocated to Berlin, where they operate within the city’s improvised and experimental music scene. The group’s sound sits at the intersection of free improvisation and contemporary jazz, built around Reidy’s electric guitar and Hall’s percussion.

Overview

Pales (2) is an improvising duo whose sole Discogs-documented release, Juana Tumble (2016), was issued on Ivory Tower Records (4), an Australian digital imprint. The record is catalogued under Jazz with styles of Contemporary Jazz and Free Improvisation — a guitar-and-percussion duo format with no additional overdubs or studio augmentation listed in the credits. Both members are also active in S P O I L E R, a separate Berlin-based project they share.
