The Chills

Discogs ID: 83084 Origin: Dunedin, New Zealand Active: 1980–1983, 1984–1992, 1994–1996, 1999–2024 (posthumous 2025) Genres: Dunedin sound, jangle pop, indie pop, indie rock Name variations: Chills, Martin Phillipps & The Chills, A Wrinkle in Time (temporary, 1983–1984)

Overview

The Chills are a New Zealand indie rock band formed in Dunedin in 1980, built around founding singer-songwriter and guitarist Martin Phillipps (2 July 1963 – 28 July 2024). Phillipps was the sole constant member through an extraordinary series of lineup changes — sometimes described by local press as “the Curse of the Chills” — cycling through more than twenty musicians across four decades. The band were among the earliest architects of the Dunedin sound: the reverb-drenched, guitar-pop aesthetic associated with Flying Nun and which substantially influenced the indie rock template of the 1990s.

Their earliest recordings appeared on the 1982 Flying Nun compilation Dunedin Double, alongside Sneaky Feelings, The Verlaines, and The Stones. Their debut studio album Brave Words (1987, produced by Mayo Thompson of Red Krayola) reached the top 30 in New Zealand. International breakthrough came with Submarine Bells (1990), released via Slash/London worldwide — it reached #1 in New Zealand and produced their only US chart single “Heavenly Pop Hit” (#17 Billboard Alternative Airplay). A third album, Soft Bomb (1992, produced by Gavin MacKillop), peaked at #3 in New Zealand. After a 19-year hiatus from studio recording, the band regrouped with new members on Fire Records and released four more studio albums between 2015 and 2021. Phillipps died on 28 July 2024; a posthumous album Spring Board: The Early Unrecorded Songs was released in February 2025. (Sources: Discogs; Wikipedia)

Connections

  • Flying Nun — primary label for early career (1982–1994); the label that documented the Dunedin Sound
  • Creation Records — issued Kaleidoscope World (1986) in the UK; Discogs ID 1658
  • Slash — worldwide deal (1990–1992); released Submarine Bells and Soft Bomb
  • Fire Records — UK/worldwide label for the reunion era (2013–present)
  • The Clean — fellow Dunedin Sound band; David Kilgour briefly played with The Chills (1983); Peter Gutteridge was in both bands
  • Sneaky Feelings — shared Dunedin Double compilation (1982)
  • The Verlaines — shared Dunedin Double; Caroline Easther and Alan Haig also played with The Verlaines
  • Peter Gutteridge — founding guitarist (1980); went on to The Clean, Snapper, The Puddle, The Great Unwashed
  • David Kilgour — brief member (1983), phase 6; The Clean; also Pop Art Toasters with Phillipps
  • Jane Dodd — founding bassist (1980–1981); later The Verlaines, Able Tasmans
  • Mayo Thompson — produced Brave Words (1987); Red Krayola / Pere Ubu
  • Pop Art Toasters — Phillipps project with David Kilgour post-Soft Bomb disbandment (1994)
  • The Same (16) — Phillipps’ pre-Chills band (formed 1978)
  • Justin Harwood — bass (1986–1990); later Luna (5)
  • Chris Knox — Toy Love / Tall Dwarfs; Dunedin scene associate; 26 shared releases on Chills catalogue

Documentary

The Chills: The Triumph & Tragedy of Martin Phillipps (2019) — directed by Julia Parnell and Rob Curry. Premiered at SXSW; broadcast on Prime TV New Zealand. Covers the band’s history and Phillipps’ hepatitis C struggle. Discogs ID: 14005664.
