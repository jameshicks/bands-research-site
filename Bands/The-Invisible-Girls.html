The Invisible Girls

Post-punk backing group, Salford, Greater Manchester, UK. Discogs artist ID: 277394. Active 1978–c.1982. Name variation: Invisible Girls.

Overview

The Invisible Girls were a studio and occasional live backing group formed in Salford, Greater Manchester in 1978 to provide a musical backdrop for the recorded output of Salford punk poet John Cooper Clarke. The group’s creative nucleus was Factory Records producer Martin Hannett (bass, synthesizers, co-writing) and keyboardist Steve Hopkins (keyboards, synthesizer programming, co-writing), with a rotating cast of Manchester-scene musicians — including Paul Burgess (10cc drummer), Pauline Murray (ex-Penetration (2) vocalist), Robert Blamire (ex-Penetration (2) bassist), Vini Reilly (The Durutti Column), Pete Shelley (Buzzcocks), Bill Nelson (Be-Bop Deluxe), Wayne Hussey, and Dave Rowbotham — appearing across releases.

The group’s primary association was with John Cooper Clarke’s CBS/Epic catalogue (1978–1982), for which Hannett and Hopkins produced, arranged, and performed. Midway through this run, the Invisible Girls also anchored Pauline Murray’s self-titled 1980 solo LP on the Illusive label — a departure into synth-pop territory — and in 1982 backed Nico on the single “Procession” (1/2 Records). A 2015 retrospective compilation issued by Factory Benelux under the name Martin Hannett and Steve Hopkins: The Invisible Girls collected material from across the group’s lifespan.

Source: Wikipedia (“The Invisible Girls”, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Invisible_Girls); Discogs artist 277394.

Connections

  • John Cooper Clarke — primary vehicle; all four studio LPs (1978–1982)
  • Martin Hannett — co-founder, producer, bass, synthesizer; the sonic architect of the group
  • Steve Hopkins — co-founder, keyboards, co-producer, co-writer; present on all primary releases; subsequently played piano on Happy Mondays’ Bummed (Factory, 1988) and worked with Morrissey
  • Paul Burgess — drums on debut and later work; primary role with 10cc; left mid-career to focus on 10cc
  • Pauline Murray — lead vocalist on self-titled 1980 Illusive LP; ex-Penetration (2); went on to form the Storm with Blamire
  • Robert Blamire — bass on Murray-era releases; co-writer on LP; ex-Penetration (2); later producer of Scars’ Author! Author! and longtime Penetration producer
  • Vini Reilly — guest guitarist on Pauline Murray LP; simultaneously fronting The Durutti Column
  • Dave Rowbotham — guitar on Murray LP; murdered 1991
  • Wayne Hussey — live guitarist for Murray-era Invisible Girls; subsequently joined Dead or Alive; later The Mission
  • Pete Shelley — Buzzcocks guitarist; guest on Disguise In Love and Snap, Crackle & Bop
  • Bill Nelson — Be-Bop Deluxe guitarist; guest on Disguise In Love and Me And My Big Mouth
  • John Maher — Buzzcocks drummer; played on Murray LP and “Searching For Heaven”
  • Chris Nagle — engineer on Murray LP and Me And My Big Mouth
  • Nico (3) — former Velvet Underground vocalist; backed on “Procession” (1982)
  • Illusive — Pauline Murray releases (1980–1981)
  • Factory Benelux — 2015 retrospective compilation
