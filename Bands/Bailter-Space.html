Overview

Bailter Space is a noise rock band from Christchurch, New Zealand, founded in 1986 by Hamish Kilgour and Alister Parker under the name Nelsh Bailter Space, shortened to their current name in early 1988. Their catalogue is concentrated in the 1990s with eleven releases, drawing on noise, post-rock, space rock, and alternative rock. Flying Nun Records anchored their career across the full span from 1988 to 2021, while Matador handled their North American profile from 1992 onward. The 2000s are conspicuously absent from their release history; they returned with new material in the 2010s and again in the 2020s, with producer Nick Roughan appearing as their most consistent long-term collaborator.
