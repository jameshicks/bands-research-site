The Next Step (5)

Los Angeles jazz ensemble. Discogs artist ID: 3616203.

Overview

The Next Step is the core band assembled around saxophonist Kamasi Washington in Los Angeles. The group formed out of the tight-knit circle of musicians who grew up together in the Leimert Park / South Central LA scene — many of them childhood friends and long-time collaborators before any of them achieved wider recognition. The ensemble functions simultaneously as Washington’s backing band and as a collective in its own right, with members who each maintain substantial independent careers.

The group’s existence as a documented recording unit predates Washington’s breakthrough: they self-released Live At 5th Street Dick’s in 2005 and The Proclamation in 2007 — both on Washington’s own imprint, sold at local venues and distributed informally. These early records established the ensemble’s approach: long-form improvisational jazz built on spiritual and post-bop foundations, with dual drums and dual bass (acoustic and electric), keyboards, trombone, and Washington’s tenor saxophone as the lead voice.

When The Epic appeared in 2015 on Brainfeeder, it credited Washington as the primary artist with The Next Step featured — not as a backup unit but as the named ensemble. The record’s commercial and critical success introduced the group to a much broader audience. The instrumentation on The Epic added orchestral strings, a choir, and additional brass, but the core band remained the same personnel from the 2005 and 2007 self-releases.

Notes:

  • Two drummers (Bruner Jr. and Austin) and two bassists (Mosley on acoustic, Thundercat on electric) are a defining structural feature of the ensemble.
  • Patrice Quinn appears on The Proclamation (2007) and is listed as a formal member on the Discogs artist entry; she sings lead vocals and choir on The Epic.
  • Rickey Washington (Kamasi’s father, soprano saxophone) appears on Live At 5th Street Dick’s (track “Askim”) but is not listed as a formal member.
  • Terrace Martin (alto saxophone, credited as “Teris Martin”) appears on the first two tracks of Live At 5th Street Dick’s but is not a core member.

The Epic (2015) — Key Personnel

Released on Brainfeeder (BF050) as a 3×LP box set. Primary credit: Kamasi Washington. All compositions arranged and produced by Washington.

Core band: Kamasi Washington (tenor sax), Ryan Porter (trombone), Cameron Graves (piano/organ/choir), Miles Mosley (acoustic bass), Stephen “Thundercat” Bruner (electric bass), Ronald Bruner Jr. (drums), Tony Austin (drums/lead tracking engineer), Brandon Coleman (keyboards/organ/piano), Patrice Quinn (lead vocals/choir)

Additional performers on The Epic: Dwight Trible (lead vocals/choir), Igmar Thomas (trumpet), Leon Mobley (percussion), Robert Miller (drums, track D4). Orchestral strings (Lucia Micarelli, Paul Cartwright, Neel Hammond, Jen Simone, Tylana Renga Enomoto — violins; Andrea Whitt, Molly Rogers — violas; Artyom Manukyan, Ginger Murphy — cellos). Mixed by Benjamin Tierney; mastered by Stephen Marcussen.
