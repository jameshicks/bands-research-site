Crass Agenda

UK project reuniting members of Crass with jazz musicians. Discogs artist ID: 1951365. Also known as Last Amendment (alias).

Overview

A collaboration that brought together several Crass members — Penny Rimbaud, Gee Vaucher, Pete Wright, N.A. Palmer, Eve Libertine (credited as Bronwen Jones (2)), J J Ratter (Discogs ID: 7107354), and Steve Ignorant — alongside jazz musicians including Julian Siegel (saxophone), Gene Calderazzo (Discogs ID: 479509, drums), Ingrid Laubrock (Discogs ID: 561153, saxophone), Dylan Bates (Discogs ID: 1176577, violin), A-Soma (Discogs ID: 295074), Kate Shortt (Discogs ID: 1518135), Ed Jones (Discogs ID: 251235).
