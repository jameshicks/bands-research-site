Singers & Players

Discogs ID: 40537
Active: 1981–2024
Labels: On-U Sound, Cherry Red
Genre: Reggae, dub, post-punk crossover

Loose collective of 15–20 vocalists, musicians, and DJs assembled by Adrian Sherwood as the first mutation of his On-U Sound roster. Described by Sherwood as “Punks & Rastas, Rebels, Rockers, Instrumentalists and Experimentalists.” Members overlapped extensively with other On-U Sound groups: Dub Syndicate, Creation Rebel, African Head Charge, Playgroup, New Age Steppers. The marquee vocalists were Prince Far I, Bim Sherman, and Mikey Dread. Post-punk crossover figures — Ari-Up (The Slits) and Keith Levene (Public Image Ltd) — appear as instrumentalists on War Of Words.

Name variations: S&P, Singer And Players, Singers + Players, Singers And Players

The On-U Sound Network

Singers & Players existed as a container for Adrian Sherwood’s wider studio collective. The same musicians and vocalists cycled through Creation Rebel, Dub Syndicate, African Head Charge, and New Age Steppers, with Sherwood always at the mixing desk. Key crossover figures:

  • Keith Levene and Ari-Up brought post-punk/punk connections (War Of Words, 1981) — connecting the On-U world to Public Image Ltd and The Slits
  • Dennis Bovell — independent dub production figure who engineered the debut
  • Prince Far I — died September 1983; his shadow runs through all early albums; after his death Congo Ashanti Roy took on more prominent vocal roles
  • Style Scott — drummer who graduated to co-producer credit by Vacuum Pumping (1988)
  • 99 Records — the NYC post-punk/dance label that handled War Of Words for the US market (cat. 99-002LP), connecting On-U Sound to the American No Wave/dance scene alongside Maximum Joy and Bush Tetras
