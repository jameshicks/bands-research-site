Asobi Seksu

Discogs ID: (unknown) Active: 2001–2013 Origin: New York, New York

New York shoegaze/dream-pop band led by vocalist Yuki Chikudate and guitarist James Hanna. Japanese-language band name. Connected to Boris (3) through shared tour/split activity and the overlapping shoegaze-adjacent heavy music community of the mid-2000s.

Lineup History

The band cycled through several members around the core duo of Yuki Chikudate (vocals, keyboards) and James Hanna (guitar). Original rhythm section Glenn Waldman (bass) and Keith Hopkin (drums) departed after touring the debut album (2005). A series of drummers followed — Bryan Greene, Mitch Spivak, Ben Shapiro, Gunnar Olsen — before Larry Gorman settled into the drum seat in 2009. Bassist Haji was replaced by Billy Pavone in 2007. The final stable lineup (Chikudate, Hanna, Pavone, Gorman) held from 2009 until the 2013 hiatus. (Wikipedia — Asobi Seksu, as of 2026-10-03; Last.fm biography)

No member departure immediately preceded the 2013 hiatus — the dissolution was not triggered by anyone leaving the band. The hiatus was a collective decision by the core members.

Hiatus (2013)

The band announced an indefinite hiatus on September 30, 2013, via their Facebook page. The stated reason was not given publicly. The Facebook post read: “Thanks for all the memories guys. Hope to make more down the line. We miss you all.” In the comments the band added: “Saying goodbye for the forseeable future, but we may write for Asobi again in a couple of years… Truly thankful for all of you and for 11 amazing years.” (Gigwise; Wikipedia, as of 2026-08-16)

No specific reason — creative, personal, or logistical — was given in the announcement or in any press coverage found. Financial strain from touring had been a documented issue earlier in their career: a 2009 Kickstarter campaign to fund a UK support slot with White Lies described projected losses of over $10,000 on a single tour, with the band noting “each day we’ll spend more money than we earn.” (Drowned in Sound archive, 2009) Whether this was a factor in the eventual hiatus is not documented. In his only on-record comment about the split, James Hanna said only: “After we disbanded Asobi, I started writing some songs with power chords.” (Philthy Mag interview)

Post-hiatus: Hanna formed fuzz/garage project Youth Worship in 2014 (EP1 on Self Harm Records, 2014; LP1, 2015) and later co-formed electronic-rap duo Kempt with MC Wolfe. Chikudate transitioned to a career in Montessori education; she holds Master’s degrees in Music Education and Early Childhood Education from Columbia University, plus an Early Childhood Montessori Certification, and serves as an Early Childhood Teacher at Love of Learning Montessori School. No public statement from Chikudate links the Montessori career shift directly to the 2013 hiatus decision — the transition timing is inferred, not documented. (Wikipedia — Yuki Chikudate, as of 2026-08-16; BrooklynVegan; Love of Learning Montessori staff page)

Reunion show: On July 23, 2014, the band announced a one-off show opening for Slowdive in Boston on October 26, 2014, at Slowdive’s request. (Wikipedia — Asobi Seksu, as of 2026-08-16)
