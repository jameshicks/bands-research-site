Legs On Earth

Discogs ID: 4342708 Active: c. 1997–2000 (late 1990s) Location: Sacramento / Davis, California, USA Genres: Math Rock, Noise Rock, Alternative Rock Members: Zach Hill · Spencer Seim · Josh Hill (3) · Julian Imsdahl

History

Legs On Earth formed in the Sacramento/Davis area in the late 1990s, coalescing around a group of musicians connected to the regional experimental rock and college scene. The band’s lineup brought together two guitarists — Spencer Seim and Josh Hill (3) — drummer Zach Hill (who is Josh’s cousin), and multi-instrumentalist Julian Imsdahl on bass, vocals, and keyboards.

The band’s sole known document is Lasers and Saviors, released in 1999 on the local independent Para-Sight Records (cat. PSR008). The album was produced by The Paul & Tim Show and engineered by Pauly Kay, with Timothy Riley (credited as “Timbo”) as 2nd engineer. Artwork was handled by Brook Caballaro (Anti-One Eye Art) with cover image by Gary Imsdahl and photography by Alison Schmidt. The interlocking guitar approach — two guitars operating in independent rhythmic layers against Hill’s drumming — prefigures the math-rock language Zach Hill and Spencer Seim would develop more fully in Hella. Lyrics were co-written by Zach Hill (credited on the sleeve as “Zak Hill”) and Julian Imsdahl (credited as “Jules Imsdahl”).

The band had dissolved by around 2000. In 2001, Zach Hill and Spencer Seim formed Hella as a guitar-and-drums duo in Sacramento. Josh Hill (3) later appeared as a guitarist on Hella’s expanded-lineup album There’s No 666 In Outer Space (Ipecac Recordings, 2007). Julian Imsdahl did not follow into Hella; his subsequent projects listed on Discogs are Grandstream and Somewhere In The Middle Of Nowhere.

Para-Sight Records was a small Nevada City, California–based label; the archive URLs on the Discogs artist page for Legs On Earth point to a Wayback Machine capture of the label’s original website, which featured a dedicated page for the band.
