Kathryn Joseph

Scottish singer-songwriter and pianist, originally from Aberdeen, now based in Glasgow. Discogs artist ID: 4199019. Sister of artist [a4272389] (identity not publicly confirmed in Discogs data).

Overview

Kathryn Joseph writes stark, piano-led songs with emotionally concentrated lyrics. Her 2015 debut album Bones You Have Thrown Me And Blood I’ve Spilled — released on her own Hits The Fan Records — won the Mercury Music Prize that year and was produced entirely by Marcus MacKay, with whom she has maintained a long creative partnership. She joined Rock Action Records for her second album in 2018, and has remained there through two subsequent albums and several singles. From 2022 onward she has co-produced with Lomond Campbell, whose modular synthesis and drum programming have become a defining element of the more recent records.

Joseph is also a member of Out Lines, a Glasgow trio with James Graham (of The Twilight Sad) and Marcus MacKay, who released one album, Conflats, on Rock Action in 2017.

She takes an active role in production design across her catalog: co-writing, contributing album artwork, booklet photography (from 2022), and cover photography (2025).
