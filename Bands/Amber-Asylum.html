Amber Asylum

Experimental neoclassical / darkwave / dark ambient project. San Francisco, California, USA. Founded 1990 by Kris Force. Discogs artist ID: 31595.

Overview

Amber Asylum is the primary compositional vehicle of Kris Force (Kris T. Force) — composer, multi-instrumentalist, and producer — operating since 1990 in San Francisco. The project functions as a fluid ensemble platform rather than a fixed band: violin and cello are the dominant instruments, and the music tends toward neoclassical, romantic, and brooding dark ambient textures. Wikipedia describes it as “a highly variable San Francisco-based music group.”

The lineup has shifted across every release, drawing from the Bay Area underground’s intersection of experimental classical, metal, and noise circles. Core recurring players include cellist Jackie Gratz (Grayceon, Giant Squid, Asunder), who appears on releases spanning 1999–2025, and early member Martha Burns (cello). Steve Von Till (Neurosis) is listed as a Discogs member and appears on the 1990 self-titled and The Natural Philosophy Of Love (1997). Later lineups brought in Leila Rauf (Bastard Noise, Vastum, Hammers Of Misfortune), Eric Wood (Man Is The Bastard / Bastard Noise), and Sigrid Sheie (Hammers Of Misfortune, Vhol).

The label trajectory maps the project’s arc: early recordings on Elfenblut (a Misanthropy Records sublabel, UK), then three albums on Release Entertainment (a Relapse Records sublabel) through 2000, two albums on Profound Lore Records (2007–2009), and then from 2015 onward on Prophecy Productions (Germany), which released the 2025 album Ruby Red. The 2005 EP was self-released on Bio-Fidelic.
