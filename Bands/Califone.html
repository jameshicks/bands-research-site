Califone

Experimental rock / alt-country group. Chicago, Illinois. Discogs ID: 197418. Active 1998–present (intermittent).

Led by Tim Rutili (real name Temistocles Hugo Rutili, born 1967/68) and Brian Deck. Evolved directly from Red Red Meat when that band dissolved in the late 1990s. Rutili is also a filmmaker and visual artist.

Both Rutili and Deck played on Ugly Casanova’s Sharpen Your Teeth (Sub Pop, 2002) alongside Isaac Brock.

The Boxhead Ensemble connection: Rutili is a confirmed full member of Boxhead Ensemble. Michael Krassner, Boxhead Ensemble’s music director, co-produced and mixed Califone’s Heron King Blues (2004) and recorded Roots & Crowns (2006). Jim Becker and Wil Hendricks appear in both bands.
