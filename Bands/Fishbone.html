Fishbone

American ska-punk/funk-metal band formed in Los Angeles, CA, 1979. Discogs artist ID: 121386.

Overview

Fishbone emerged from the South Central Los Angeles public school system, where the founding six members met through the LAUSD voluntary busing program in the late 1970s. Signed to Columbia Records in 1985, they released a string of critically acclaimed but commercially underperforming records through the early 1990s. Their hybrid of ska, punk, funk, metal, soul, and reggae was genre-defining but hard to market; they became a live institution while the industry largely passed them over. Internal member departures accelerated after 1993, with the lineup becoming fluid from Chim Chim’s Bad Ass Revenge (1996) onward. Despite decades of instability, Angelo Moore and John Norwood Fisher have kept the band active through the 2000s and 2010s with self-released and small-label recordings.

Name variations on Discogs include “Fishbone & The Familyhood Nextperience” and “Fishbone’s Familyhood Nexperience.”

Key Personnel (by release)

Fishbone EP (1985) — Discogs 376896

  • All six original members; produced by David Kahne
  • Engineer: Kahne; remix: David Leonard

In Your Face (1986) — Discogs 396211

  • Produced + engineered by Kahne; additional keys/guitar by Kahne
  • Engineer: Tchad Blake, Bill Jackson; Mixed by David Leonard
  • All six original members

Truth And Soul (1988) — Discogs 391895

  • Produced + engineered by Kahne; mixed by David Leonard
  • Christopher Dowd credited here as “Charlie Down”; Kendall Jones doubles on bass (track 8)
  • Note: Phillip Fisher on drums/percussion throughout

The Reality Of My Surroundings (1991) — Discogs 391898

  • Band self-produced with Kahne as associate producer; Kahne produced tracks 12, 17, 18
  • Mixed by Michael Brauer (tracks 1, 3, 7, 11, 12, 18) and Thom Panunzio (tracks 2, 4–6, 8, 10, 13, 16)
  • Massive backing vocal cast including Larry Fishburne, Sylvia Massy (as backing vocalist, not engineer), Ron St. Germain recorded live tracks
  • T-Bone David (percussion); Fernando Pullum (trumpet)

Give A Monkey A Brain… (1993) — Discogs 391899

  • Produced by Terry Date and Fishbone (not David Kahne)
  • Engineered by Terry Date; mixed by Andy Wallace; mastered by Howie Weinberg
  • Lineup: all original six + John Bigham
  • Guest: Branford Marsalis (saxophone, “Drunk Skitzo”); Billy “Bass” Nelson (backing vocals/arrangements, “Lemon Meringue”); Damon Wayans (voice sample, “Swim”)

Chim Chim’s Bad Ass Revenge (1996) — Discogs 391902

  • Rowdy Records; co-produced by Dallas Austin and Fishbone
  • Guests: Busta Rhymes (vocals, “Psychologically Overcast”), N’Dea Davenport, Joi (3), Mary Harris

Still Stuck In Your Throat (2006) — Discogs 1387229

  • Produced by John Norwood Fisher + Jimmy Sloan; David Kahne returned as mixer only
  • Lineup: Moore, Fisher, Rocky George, John Steward, Drè Gipson, John McKnight, Curtis Storey
  • Includes cover of Sublime’s “Date Rape” (written by Brad Nowell)

Crazy Glue (2011) — Discogs 3409184

  • Produced by John Norwood Fisher; co-produced/engineered/mixed/mastered by Derek O’Brien
  • Lineup: Moore, Fisher (bass), Rocky George (guitar), Drè Gipson (keys), John Steward (drums), Walter Kibby (trumpet), Jay Armant (trombone)
