Belbury Poly

Discogs ID: 466915 Real person: Jim Jupp Aliases: Eric Zann, Jim Jupp Origin: UK Active: 2005–2023 (on Ghost Box) Label: Ghost Box

Overview

Belbury Poly is the primary recording alias of Jim Jupp, co-founder of Ghost Box (with Julian House). The most prolific artist on the Ghost Box roster with 46 releases between 2005 and 2023. The project draws on British library music, hauntological electronics, and pastoral psychedelia — closely aligned with the broader Ghost Box aesthetic established alongside The Focus Group and The Advisory Circle.

Jim Jupp also records under the alias Eric Zann (another Ghost Box alias, Discogs ID 651069).
