Lingua Ignota

Discogs ID: 5825775 Active: 2017–2023 (as Lingua Ignota); continues as Reverend Kristin Michael Hayter (2023–present) Principal: Kristin Hayter (1386275) Label: Sargent House (2020–2022, 18 releases)

Solo project of Kristin Hayter. Extreme music combining classical composition, metal, and noise. Releases on Sargent House: Sinner Get Ready (SH253, 2021, 12 pressings) was the highest-profile SH release of the era. Left SH amid the 2023 abuse controversy — Hayter had previously cut ties before Cathy Pellow’s resignation and released a public statement.

Training Background

Hayter began singing in church choir at age 8; a teacher noticed her natural vibrato and she progressed to solos and church cantor duties. Her parents started her on classical voice and piano lessons around age 10–11. Her classical vocal training was multi-lingual (specialty German, also Italian, French, Russian, Latin), and through high school she trained with the intention of attending conservatory and studying opera, while also performing in small opera productions. She did not ultimately pursue a conservatory degree — instead she earned a BFA in interdisciplinary creative arts from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago and an MFA in Literary Arts from Brown University (2016), where she presented her Lingua Ignota thesis work. She has cited the classical foundation as technically underpinning her extreme and extended vocal techniques; influences include Diamanda Galás and Cathy Berberian, both known for pushing classical voice into confrontational territory. She also studied traditional Bulgarian polyphony. (Wikipedia, as of 2026-06-25; Austin Chronicle interview, 2019)

Post-SH / Post-Lingua Ignota Activity

In November 2022 Hayter announced plans to retire the Lingua Ignota project in 2023, stating “This music has been excruciating to perform, and I know that what is healthiest for me is to stop performing it.” (Wikipedia, as of 2026-06-26) Final U.S. shows were April 28–30, 2023 at Chicago’s Thalia Hall; a European tour in May 2023 was canceled due to a spinal injury. Final performances: October 13–14, 2023 at London’s Islington Assembly Hall. A live album, The End: Live at Islington Assembly Hall, was released November 3, 2023. (Wikipedia)

New Name: Reverend Kristin Michael Hayter

On August 22, 2023 Hayter announced the Lingua Ignota project was ending and that she would release music under the name Reverend Kristin Michael Hayter. (Stereogum, 2023-08-22)

Debut album: Saved! — Released October 20, 2023 via Perpetual Flame Ministries. 11 tracks. Recorded with longtime collaborator Seth Manchester on 4-track equipment, then deliberately degraded through broken cassette players. Explores evangelical conversion as allegory for “undoing and rebuilding.” (Stereogum; Consequence)

Outtakes release: Saved! The Index — Released February 2, 2024 via Perpetual Flame Ministries. 12 outtakes from the Saved! sessions. (Wikipedia)

New Label: Perpetual Flame Ministries

Co-founded December 2022 with KW Campol of Vile Creature. Described as “a source for music, writing, fine art, and ephemera” building on Hayter’s Lingua Ignota conceptual work. First release was a vinyl reissue of her debut album. (Consequence, 2022-12; Wikipedia)

Guest Appearances (2025)

Guest contributor on Die Close (Blood Vulture, released June 27, 2025, via Two Minutes to Late Night’s Bandcamp). Hayter appears on “Entwined” and the closing track “Die Close: Finale.” (The Soundboard Reviews, 2025-06-25; The Progressive Subway, 2025-08-09)

No further solo releases confirmed as of 2026-06-26.
