Overview

Summer Camp is a lo-fi synth pop duo from Walton-on-Thames, UK, formed by Elizabeth Sankey and Jeremy Warmsley — the pair briefly operated under the name Morgan Waves before settling on their current moniker. The bulk of their output arrived in the 2010s, spanning indie pop and indie rock with occasional ventures into disco, score, and soundtrack territory. Moshi Moshi Records was their primary home, housing 13 of their works between 2010 and 2020. Regular studio collaborators include Ash Gardner, William Bowerman, and Ash Workman.
