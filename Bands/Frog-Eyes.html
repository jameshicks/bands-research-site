Frog Eyes

Canadian art-rock / post-punk band. Discogs artist ID: 831336. Based in Victoria, BC.

Core lineup on the Soft Abuse debut (2003): Carey Mercer (guitar, vocals, painting), Spencer Krug (band member), Melanie Campbell (drums), Michael Rak (bass). Dante Decaro also a member (shared with Wolf Parade).

First release on Soft Abuse: Emboldened Navigator (2003, cat. 001) — the label’s very first catalog entry, a 7”. Mercer and Krug went on to many other projects (Wolf Parade, Carey Mercer’s Blackout Beach solo project, etc.).
