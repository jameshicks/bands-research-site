Bikini Kill

Olympia/Washington DC riot grrrl punk band. Discogs artist ID: 267489. Formed 1990, disbanded 1997, reunited 2019.

Overview

Bikini Kill pioneered the riot grrrl movement, fronted by Kathleen Hanna and formed in Olympia, Washington in 1990. They were central to the feminist punk/DIY underground of the early 1990s, closely tied to the Olympia scene. Released primarily on Kill Rock Stars and (UK) Wiiija Records. Disbanded 1997; reunited 2019 on their own Bikini Kill Records imprint.

Notable Credits / Connections

  • Bikini Kill EP (1992): recorded in part by Ian MacKaye and Don Zientara at Inner Ear Studio
  • Pussy Whipped KRS-218 (1993): cassette; produced and engineered by Stuart Hallerman; “Rebel Girl” is here; “Speed Heart” vocals Kathi Wilcox; “Tell Me So” and “Hamster Baby” vocals Tobi Vail; “Starfish” has Kathleen Hanna on bass; Tammy Rae Carland cover photo (ID: 592080)
  • Reject All American (1996): produced by John Goodmanson
  • Kathi Wilcox later joined The Julie Ruin (with Hanna)
  • Tobi Vail was in a relationship with Kurt Cobain (ca. 1990); Cobain is said to have written “Smells Like Teen Spirit” after Bikini Kill slogans
