The Hives

Swedish garage rock band from Fagersta, Västmanland. Formed 1993. Discogs artist ID: 105733.

Note: The research brief that prompted adjacent research incorrectly identified this band as connected to Lars Göransson (Discogs 461121). That Lars Göransson is an American engineer based in Austin, TX. No Discogs-documented connection between him and The Hives exists.

Overview

All members were born 1977–1978. Led by vocalist Per Almqvist (stage name Howlin’ Pelle Almqvist). Known for matching black-and-white stage costumes, high-energy live performances, and a manufactured mythology around the fictional svengali “Randy Fitzsimmons.” All songs have been credited to “Randy Fitzsimmons” — Discogs confirms this is an alias for guitarist Niklas Almqvist (Nicholaus Arson); Fitzsimmons (Discogs ID 641027) lists his real name as Niklas Almqvist. Broke internationally after Your New Favourite Band (Poptones/Sire, 2001) compiled their early Swedish recordings for UK/US markets.

The “reveal” became the conceptual hook for the 2023 album The Death Of Randy Fitzsimmons.

Career Narrative

Fagersta origins (1993–1996)

Formed in Fagersta, a small industrial town in Västmanland. The 1994 Sounds Like Sushi cassette was self-released (Not On Label). Their first proper release Oh Lord! When? How? (1996) came out on local Sidekicks Records.

Burning Heart era (1997–2001)

Signed to Burning Heart Records, the Örebro-based punk/hardcore label (also home to Refused and The International Noise Conspiracy). Debut LP Barely Legal (1997) was engineered by Pelle Gunnerfeldt, who would become the band’s primary studio collaborator for the next decade. The A.K.A I-D-I-O-T EP (1998) and single “Hate To Say I Told You So” (2000) built momentum in Sweden.

Veni Vidi Vicious (2000) — produced and recorded by Pelle Gunnerfeldt — became the band’s artistic breakout, establishing the propulsive two-guitar attack and wiry garage-punk blueprint. All songs credited to Randy Fitzsimmons. The compilation Your New Favourite Band (2001, Poptones/UK, Sire/US) packaged the best of the early Burning Heart material for international markets and triggered a global garage rock revival moment alongside The Strokes and The White Stripes.

Major label peak (2004–2007)

Tyrannosaurus Hives (2004, Polydor) was self-produced with recording by Pelle Gunnerfeldt. Strings arranged by Björn Yttling (of Peter Bjorn and John). Mixing by Michael Ilbert. The album reached the top 10 in Sweden and charted in the UK and US. Singles “Walk Idiot Walk” and “Two-Timing Touch And Broken Bones” received heavy airplay. The live DVD Tussles In Brussels (2005, Polydor) documented the tour.

The Black And White Album (2007, A&M Octone Records / Interscope) was the band’s most producer-diverse record: tracks produced by Dennis Herring, Pharrell Williams (tracks 4 and 8), Garret Lee (Jacknife Lee, track 5), and Thomas Öberg (Moneybrother, track 14), alongside self-produced cuts. Mixed by Pelle Gunnerfeldt (tracks 3, 5, 9–14) and Serban Ghenea (tracks 1, 2, 4, 7, 8). Mastered by Ted Jensen.

Own label and later work (2010–present)

Founded own imprint Disques Hives, releasing Tarred And Feathered (2010 single) and Lex Hives (2012, Universal distribution). Released two singles on Third Man Records (2019) and a live album there (2020). The Death Of Randy Fitzsimmons (2023, Disques Hives) served as both a new album and the conceptual resolution of the Randy Fitzsimmons mythology. The Hives Forever Forever The Hives (2025, Play It Again Sam) continued the run.
