Soulwax

Belgian electronic-rock band from Ghent, centred on brothers Stephen Dewaele and David Dewaele. Discogs artist ID: 34128. Also operate as 2 Many DJ’s (DJ alias). Founded their own studio, label, and creative infrastructure under the name Deewee.

Overview

Soulwax formed in Ghent in the early 1990s as a guitar-led alternative rock band. Their breakthrough came with Much Against Everyone’s Advice (1998), produced by Dave Sardy, which brought them international attention and a deal with Play It Again Sam Records. After that album the Dewaele brothers pivoted toward electronic music and began operating as 2 Many DJ’s, creating the bootleg/mash-up mix series As Heard On Radio Soulwax (2002), which became widely cited as the defining document of early-2000s mash-up culture. The NYT named it best popular music album of 2002.

The Soulwax name returned to prominence with Any Minute Now (2004, produced with Flood), and Nite Versions (2005), a self-produced remix/rework of the album in a more electronic direction. A long hiatus from full studio albums followed, during which the brothers composed the soundtrack for Felix Van Groeningen’s film Belgica (2016). That same year they launched the Deewee label and studio complex in Ghent, which has become the primary platform for their own work and for a roster of Belgian and international artists.

From Deewee (2017) was the long-awaited return: a 10-track studio album recorded and mixed in the Deewee studio, featuring a full live band including Igor Cavalera on drums. All Systems Are Lying (2025) on Because Music / Deewee followed, again with the core band.

Deewee Label

Deewee (Discogs ID: 901323) is the Dewaele brothers’ own label and studio complex in Ghent, launched 2016. It houses both Soulwax’s primary output and a roster of associated acts including Charlotte Adigéry & Bolis Pupul, Marie Davidson, James Righton, Asa Moto, Bolis Pupul, Die Verboten (another Dewaele project), Klanken, and others. Sublabel: Waffles (ID: 900623).

2 Many DJ’s / The Flying Dewaele Brothers

The Dewaeles’ DJ alias has operated under several names:

  • The Flying Dewaele Brothers (Discogs ID: 39764) — earliest alias, also known as “The Fucking DB”
  • 2 Many DJ’s (Discogs ID: 48919) — primary DJ identity; name variations include 2manydjs, 2 Many DJs
  • The Dee Wees, Klanken, Samantha Fu — further aliases

The As Heard On Radio Soulwax bootleg mix series (14 installments, 2002–2012) pioneered mash-up culture, blending house, rock, disco, and reggae. Pt. 2 (2002) on [PIAS] was commercially released after the bootleg versions and was named the NYT’s best popular music album of 2002.

Other aliases and side projects of the brothers include:

  • Die Verboten (Discogs ID: 1529563) — Deewee label project
  • EMS Synthi 100 (Discogs ID: 7976033) — experimental project using the titular synthesizer
