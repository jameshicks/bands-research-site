Motion City Soundtrack

American rock band from Minneapolis, Minnesota. Discogs artist ID: 319670. Active 1997–2016; reunited 2019–present.

Overview

Motion City Soundtrack was formed in Minneapolis in 1997 by vocalist/guitarist Justin Courtney Pierre and lead guitarist Joshua Cain. The band built their following through relentless touring over several years before landing on Epitaph Records in 2003. Their signature sound — pop-punk and emo underscored by Moog synthesizer and keyboards — set them apart from contemporaries, the Moog idea traceable to Cain hearing the Rentals’ Return of the Rentals (1995) and buying a cheap Moog at a pawn shop.

The stable lineup solidified around 2002: bassist/keyboardist Matthew Taylor and drummer Tony Thaxton joined after being recruited from Submerge during a tour stop in Pennsylvania; keyboardist Jesse Johnson, a co-worker of Cain’s who had never played keyboards before, joined three weeks before the debut album recording. Lyrics were primarily handled by Pierre, whose writing addressed anxiety, substance abuse, alienation, and self-destructive behavior — often autobiographical, reflecting his years-long struggles with alcohol and drugs. Pierre entered rehabilitation after completing Even If It Kills Me (2007).

The band released seven studio albums, the majority on Epitaph. A brief major-label stint (Columbia, 2010’s My Dinosaur Life) ended when the label dropped them; they returned to Epitaph for Go (2012) and Panic Stations (2015). After announcing a breakup in March 2016 and completing a farewell tour, they reunited in 2019. Their seventh album, The Same Old Wasted Wonderful World, appeared in 2025 — their first with the classic lineup since Go. (Source: Wikipedia)

The band name came from a defunct film project created by Cain’s brother Brian. Their three shared influences are Superchunk, Jawbox, and the Pixies; Cain dismissed the pop-punk label, citing ’90s indie acts as the primary reference point.

Member Side Projects

Justin Pierre

Pierre is the primary lyricist and public face of MCS. His side projects and solo work:

  • Farewell Continental (Discogs ID: 2135500) — band on Paper + Plastick. Releases: Farewell Continental EP (2009), EP #2 (2010), ¡Hey, Hey Pioneers! (2011). Produced by Ed Ackerson.
  • In the Drink (2018, Epitaph) — solo album released as “Justin Courtney Pierre.” Produced and engineered by Joshua Cain; mixed by Marc McClusky; mastered by Greg Calbi.
  • The Company We Keep (Discogs ID: 2757852) — another group credit.
  • The Rapture Twins (Discogs ID: 5929832) — group credit.
  • Slide Coaster (Discogs ID: 5206742) — group credit.
  • Alias: Richard Kimble (5) (Discogs).

Pierre also contributed lead vocals to a Universe 2 release (2021).

Joshua Cain

  • Previously in The Saddest Girl Story (Discogs ID: 579880) — the Minneapolis band where Cain first recruited Pierre as singer.
  • Produced Justin Pierre’s solo album In the Drink (2018).
  • On The Same Old Wasted Wonderful World (2025), Cain handled guitar/vocals/keyboard engineering and production alongside O’Keefe.

Tony Thaxton & Matt Taylor

  • Both were previously in Submerge (5) (Discogs ID: 4235335), a Milton, Pennsylvania band. MCS recruited them during a 2001 tour.
  • Thaxton is also in Don’t Stop Or We’ll Die (Discogs ID: 2167046): releases include The Ballad Of Bird And Fox EP (2008) and Dazzle Me (2018).
  • Taylor was also in These Enzymes (Discogs ID: 3272017).

Jesse Johnson

  • Also a member of Johnny Angel (3) (Discogs ID: 1557071).
  • Known for his “Moogstand” live performance move — a handstand on the synthesizer. (Source: Wikipedia)
