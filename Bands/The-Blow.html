Overview

The Blow is the duo of Khaela Maricich and Melissa Dyne, building electro-acoustic pop from modular synths, vintage samplers, and DIY audio gear — the same rig they use to compose, produce, and perform live. Jona Bechtolt is also listed as a member, suggesting the lineup has not always been the same duo. The catalogue is concentrated in the 2000s across K Records and Tomlab, with styles ranging from indie pop and synth-pop to lo-fi and experimental; activity tailed off in the 2010s and resumed with a 2022 release on Hallogallo Tapes. Recurring collaborators include Steve Schroeder, Adam Forkner, Zac Pennington, and Calvin Johnson, whose label K Records anchored the group’s most active period.
