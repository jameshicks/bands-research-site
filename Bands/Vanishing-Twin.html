Overview

Vanishing Twin is a London-based experimental pop group founded in 2015 by Cathy Lucas. The name references Lucas’s identical twin sister absorbed in utero during gestation. The group was originally a quintet: Lucas (vocals, writing, production), Valentina Magaletti (drums), Susumu Mukai (bass, synthesizer; aka Zongamin), Elliott Arndt (flute, percussion; Discogs ID: 5312075), and Philip Baerwalde / Man From Uranus (Discogs ID: 3647557 / 488061; strange sounds, electronics, library music). Arndt departed in late 2019; Baerwalde announced his departure in May 2022. As of the 2024 Sub Pop single, the core lineup is Lucas, Mukai, and Magaletti.

According to Wikipedia, the band’s sound has been described as drawing on psychedelic traditions from around the globe — from tropicália to kosmische rock — and has been compared to Stereolab. The group released four full-length albums between 2016 and 2023, beginning with Choose Your Own Adventure (Soundway, 2016) and culminating in Afternoon X (Fire Records, 2023), which received “universal acclaim” reviews on Metacritic. Their 2019 album The Age Of Immunology received an 8/10 from Pitchfork.

Lucas co-produced the debut LP with Malcom Catto of The Heliocentrics; Catto served as co-producer and mixer across the first three records on Soundway and the Fire Records years through Ookii Gekkou (2021). From The Age Of Immunology (2019) onward, Lucas increasingly took sole production credit. The 2021 album Ookii Gekkou was written and recorded during the COVID-19 lockdown.

Connections

  • Cathy Lucas — founder, songwriter, primary producer; previously in Fanfarlo, My Sad Captains, Innerspace Orchestra; also records as Orlando (32)
  • Valentina Magaletti — drummer; simultaneously in Tomaga, Holy Tongue, Moin (2), UUUU, V/Z, and many solo / collaborative projects
  • Susumu Mukai — bassist/synthesizer; also known as Zongamin; in V/Z with Magaletti, and Holy Tongue; previously in live band for Floating Points
  • Philip Baerwalde / Man From Uranus — electronics and library music (2015–2022); solo releases on Strange Lights and Outer Music; also in Little Princess Orchestra
  • Elliott Arndt — flute and percussion (2015–2019); filmmaker and visual artist
  • Malcom Catto — co-producer and mixer on first four albums; also in The Heliocentrics, Connie Price & The Keystones, Quantic & His Combo Bárbaro
  • Soundway — first label home (2016–2017); known for African music compilations and reissues
  • Fire Records — main label from 2019; London independent active since 1984
  • The state51 Conspiracy — London independent label and distributor; released Tell Me Not Here (2024) on full LP and Singularity 08 lathe-cut on the Singularity Series sublabel
  • Tomaga — connection via Magaletti (member) and Cathy Lucas’s guest credit on Intimate Immensity (2021); also the Play Time split (as Orlando (32), 2015)
  • V/Z — duo of Magaletti and Mukai; Lucas contributed lyrics and vocals to track A3 of Suono Assente (AD 93, 2023)
  • Holy Tongue — shared members Magaletti and Mukai
