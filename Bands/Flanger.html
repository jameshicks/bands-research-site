Flanger

Electronic duo. Members: Bernd Friedmann (Discogs ID: 58045) and Uwe Schmidt, aka Atom Heart (Discogs ID: 519207). Based in the Frankfurt/Cologne German experimental scene.

Discogs artist ID: 6505.

Overview

Flanger released three titles on Ntone in 1999–2000: Flanger E.P. (NTONE 32), Flanger E.P. 2 (NTONE 33), and Templates (ntone cd33, 1999); Midnight Sound (Ntone CD 40, 2000). Styles: Downtempo, Future Jazz.

Members

  • Bernd Friedmann (ID: 58045) — also credited as mastering engineer and drummer on various Ntone releases
  • Uwe Schmidt (ID: 519207) — also known as Atom Heart; prolific German electronic artist
