International Airport

Discogs artist ID: 105123 Country: Scotland (UK) Active: 1998–2004 Genre: Abstract / Post-Rock / Folk Rock / Electronic Labels: All City, Geographic URL: https://www.dominomusic.com/artists/international-airport

Scottish collective centred on Alasdair Roberts, Tom Crossley, and Lorna Gilfedder, active from 1998 to 2004. A parallel project to Appendix Out (which shared Roberts and Crossley), International Airport operated as a larger ensemble drawing on a rotating Glasgow-area cast for its recordings. Their only full LP, Nothing We Can Control (Geographic, 2000), was mixed by John McEntire of Tortoise.

Biography

Origins and Debut (1997–1998)

International Airport began as an offshoot of the Glasgow scene surrounding Alasdair Roberts and Tom Crossley — both of whom were simultaneously active in Appendix Out (formed c.1996). The group’s debut release was a 7” single, Airport Songs (All City, cat. no. “Glasgow”, 1998), pressed in the US and featuring three tracks including a cover of “Una Stanza Vuota” with written-by credits to Carlo Rossi (2) and Ennio Morricone. Sleeve artwork was credited to Annabel Wright (credited as “Aggi (4)”), Stephen McRobbie, and Tom Crossley.

Nothing We Can Control (2000)

The group’s first (and only) full-length album, released on the Glasgow-affiliated Geographic label (cat. GEOG 2CD) in 2000 with a Japanese pressing following in 2001. The album runs 12 tracks across genres — described on Discogs as Abstract / Post Rock / Electronic / Rock — and was mixed by John McEntire (Tortoise, Gastr Del Sol). Personnel on Nothing We Can Control formed the core International Airport collective:

Core performers:

  • Alasdair Roberts (credited as “Ali Roberts”) — chanter, electronics, percussion, piano, violin, vocals, bulbul tarang
  • Tom Crossley — bass, organ, drums, electronics, flute, percussion, guitar, mandolin, melodica, piano, toy clarinet, vocals, xylophone
  • Annabel Wright — bass, piano, vocals
  • Aki Okauchi — bass, cello, guitar, vocals
  • Robbie Wilson (2) — guitar
  • Stephen Aston — guitar, melodica, vocals
  • Cari Anderson — organ, melodica, drums
  • Julie Greene — drums

Guest / additional:

  • Mick Cooke (of Belle & Sebastian) — French horn, flugelhorn (track 11 only)
  • John McEntire — Mixed By
  • Steve Rooke — Mastered By (Japan pressing)
  • Alasdair Cooke — Recorded By
  • Annabel Wright / Julie Greene / Rikke (3) / Tom Crossley — Photography

The album was recorded by Alasdair Cooke and sleeve design was conceived by Annabel Wright and Tom Crossley.

Association! Single and Teenage Fanclub Collaboration (2004)

The group’s most explicitly collaborative release was the Association! / Association single (Geographic, GEOG29CD, 2004), a co-credit with Teenage Fanclub. The CD features four versions of the same track — “Channel Mash,” “Teenage Fanclub Instrumental,” “Played By Teenage Fanclub,” and “Int. Air. Source” — all recorded and mixed by Duncan Cameron (2) and Teenage Fanclub. A promo CDr variant (no cat. no.) circulated under the slightly different title Association.

Reunion Of Island Goose (2004)

International Airport’s second and final album, Reunion Of Island Goose (Geographic, GEOG26CD, 2004), appeared the same year as the Teenage Fanclub single. Discogs credits list no additional personnel on this pressing (credits appear sparse), but the collaborators record shows continued involvement of several Nothing We Can Control contributors. The album comprises 12 tracks with styles spanning Abstract, Downtempo, and Folk Rock — a broader palette than the debut LP. A Japanese pressing also exists (2004).
