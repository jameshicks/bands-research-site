Overview

Marion were an English Britpop band formed in 1993 in Macclesfield, Cheshire. They emerged from the same geographic axis as New Order and The Smiths — Macclesfield / Manchester — and their name variation on Discogs lists “The Program,” which was also the title of their second album. The band signed to London Records after releasing an early single on Rough Trade in 1994, and released two full-length albums during the mid-1990s Britpop peak. Their best-known singles are “Sleep” (produced by Stephen Street) and “Let’s All Go Together” (produced by Al Clay). The band’s second album, The Program (1998), was produced by Johnny Marr. Marion dissolved around 1999. Guitarist Phil Cunningham went on to join New Order in 2001, where he remains a member. The band reformed in 2016 and released two EPs on their own Marion Recordings imprint.

The core songwriting trio throughout was Harding / Cunningham / Grantham. The bassist changed between albums: Julian Phillips played on the 1994 Rough Trade single; Nick Gilbert (also a member of Felt) played on both albums; Phil Cunningham and Tony Grantham are credited as arrangers and co-composers from the earliest release.
