Overview

Nana Grizol is an indie pop and folk punk band from Athens, Georgia, active since 2005. Their catalogue spans three decades, with the bulk of their output — five releases — arriving in the 2010s, and two more in the 2020s. They have worked primarily with Orange Twin Records throughout their career, with individual releases on Don Giovanni Records and Merge Records. The band’s collaborator network includes Neutral Milk Hotel members Julian Koster and Scott Spillane, placing them squarely within Athens’ Elephant 6-adjacent scene.
