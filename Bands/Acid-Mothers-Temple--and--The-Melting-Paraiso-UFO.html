Acid Mothers Temple & The Melting Paraiso UFO

Discogs ID: 53760
Active: 1990s–present
Origin: Japan
Genre: Psychedelic rock, noise, experimental, krautrock

Japanese psychedelic collective centred on guitarist/producer Makoto Kawabata. Top Alien8 artist by release count (12 releases, 2002–2011). Alien8 Recordings served as the primary Canadian/North American home for their output during this period.

Key Alien8 releases:

  • Electric Heavyland (2002, ALIENCD34)
  • Mantra Of Love (2004, ALIENCD44)
  • Lord Of The Underground: Vishnu And The Magic Elixir (2009, ALIEN8LP84)
  • Pink Lady Lemonade (You’re From Inner Space) (2011, ALIENLP90)

Core AMT & The Cosmic Inferno variant also released on Alien8: Starless And Bible Black Sabbath (2006, ALIENCD62).
