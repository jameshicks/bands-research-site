Overview

Brass Unbound is the collective name given to the five-piece brass section assembled to collaborate with Dutch post-punk group The Ex on their 2012 touring and 2013 studio recording project. The group brought together improvisers from Chicago, Sweden, Italy, and the Netherlands — all with strong free jazz and free improvisation credentials — around the long-standing Dutch experimental rock band.

The collaboration is documented on Enormous Door (2013, Ex Records EX 138LP), a co-credited LP on which both The Ex and Brass Unbound share primary artist credit. All songs are co-written and co-arranged by both parties. The Ex self-produced the record; engineer Riccardo Parravicini handled primary recording, with additional capture of Mats Gustafsson’s parts by Andreas Werliin, Arnold de Boer, and Johan Berthling.

Joost Buis is named as a member in the brief (and appears on Y’Anbessaw Tezeta, a related Ex project from 2012), but is not credited on either pressing of Enormous Door in the Discogs database. Discogs lists only four members for the Brass Unbound artist entry: Ken Vandermark, Mats Gustafsson, Roy Paci, and Wolter Wierbos.

Member Contexts

Ken Vandermark (b. USA)

Chicago reed player and composer, winner of the MacArthur “Genius” Fellowship (1999). Leads or has led dozens of ensembles including Vandermark 5, DKV Trio, Territory Band series, Lean Left, and Peter Brötzmann Chicago Tentet. One of the most prolific figures in post-1990 American free jazz. Also appeared on The Ex’s Enormous Door and shares 6 releases with The Ex on Discogs. Primary discography spans labels including Atavistic, Delmark, Smalltown Superjazzz, Intakt, and Corbett vs. Dempsey.

Mats Gustafsson (b. 1964, Umeå, Sweden)

Saxophonist and improviser; runs the Swedish label Omlott. Central figure in European free improvisation. Leads Fire! and Fire! Orchestra (both extensively documented on Rune Grammofon). Long-standing member of The Thing (2) with Ingebrigt Håker Flaten and Paal Nilssen-Love. Frequent collaborator with Peter Brötzmann, Ken Vandermark, and Merzbow (20 shared Discogs releases with Merzbow). As-leader discography spans 1991–2026.

Roy Paci (b. Rosario Paci, Italy/Sicily)

Trumpet player and arranger; real name Rosario Paci. Based in Italy; strongly associated with Mediterranean brass, ska, and world music contexts. Best known internationally as trumpeter in Manu Chao’s Radio Bemba Sound System (appeared on Radio Bemba Sound System live album 2002, La Radiolina 2007, Manu Chao tours). Also performed with Mike Patton on Mondo Cane (Ipecac, 2010) and led his own group Roy Paci & Aretuska. Appeared on The Ex’s Catch My Shoe (Ex Records, 2010) — a prior Ex collaboration before Enormous Door. Extensive Italian pop and rock session credits.

Wolter Wierbos (b. Netherlands)

Dutch trombonist; one of the leading figures in Dutch experimental jazz. Long-serving member of the ICP Orchestra (Instant Composers Pool). Also a member of Bik Bent Braam, Available Jelly, Globe Unity Orchestra, Berlin Contemporary Jazz Orchestra, and the Cecil Taylor European Orchestra. Shares 20 Discogs releases with The Ex. Solo as-leader catalog: Wierbos (DATA Records, 1982), Live In Montreal (1986), Sounds In A Garden (Relay Recordings, 2016).

Joost Buis (b. Netherlands)

Dutch trombonist. Discogs ID: 849244. Active in the Dutch free jazz and improvised music scene since the late 1980s. Credits include trombone work across a wide range of Dutch ensembles, including appearances alongside The Ex on Y’Anbessaw Tezeta (Terp Records, 2012) — an Ethiopian-influenced collaboration that preceded Enormous Door. Also member of Extended (with Rob Daenen). As-leader releases include work on DATA Records, Morvin Records, and Conundrom.
