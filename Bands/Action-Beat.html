Action Beat

Discogs ID: 1233970. UK noise/post-punk band.

Overview

Members per Discogs: Don McLean (2), Peter James Taylor, Stig noise sound system. Primary post-Ex collaborators with GW Sok; co-headlined three releases together: A Remarkable Machine (2014, Ernest Jenning Record Co.), The World Is Fucked, But I Feel Fine (2017, Psych.KG), and a split EP. GW Sok contributes words; Action Beat provides music.
