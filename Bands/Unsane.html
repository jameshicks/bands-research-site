Unsane

Discogs artist ID: 138729
Name variations: Unsane ‘90, Unsane N.Y.C., Unsane NYC
Active: 1988 – 2019 (hiatus); reformed 2021

Overview

Unsane are a noise-rock trio from New York City, formed in 1988. They are built around the permanent core of guitarist and vocalist Chris Spencer, with a lineup history cycling through two bassists and two primary drummers across their run. The original lineup — Spencer, bassist Pete Shore, and drummer Charlie Ondras — recorded the band’s earliest singles and debut album; Ondras died of a drug overdose in 1992, prompting a lineup change. The essential long-running lineup that carried most of the catalog consists of Spencer, bassist Dave Curran, and drummer Vinnie Signorelli (the latter a former member of Swans).

The band recorded at Wharton Tiers’s Fun City Studios and at Martin Bisi’s BC Studio in Brooklyn through the early 1990s, and later with engineers Andrew Schneider and Joel Hamilton. Their Discogs catalog spans early singles on Sub Pop and small indie labels through LPs on City Slang, Matador, and Amphetamine Reptile, and into the 2000s–2010s on Relapse, Ipecac, and Alternative Tentacles. The video for “Scrape” (from Scattered, Smothered & Covered, 1995) received MTV airplay and is the band’s best-known single.

Spencer announced the band had permanently ceased activity in August 2019, then reversed the announcement in August 2021, assembling a new lineup — alongside Cooper (6) and drummer Jon Syverson — focused on performing early material.
