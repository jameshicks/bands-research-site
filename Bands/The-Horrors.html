The Horrors

Discogs ID: 517333
From: London / Southend-on-Sea, England
Active: 2005–present
Genre: Post-punk, psychedelic rock, shoegaze

UK post-punk/psychedelic band. Remixed Memory Tapes’ “Bicycle” as “Bicycle (Horrors Cosmic Dub)” (2009). Known for evolving from garage-punk debut (Strange House, 2007) to shoegaze-influenced Primary Colours (2009, XL Recordings) and beyond.

Connections

  • Cathy Lucas — session violin on Luminous (2014, XL Recordings; tracks 5, 6, 8)
  • Tom Furse — keyboardist (real name Thomas Furse Cowan); also in Innerspace Orchestra with Cathy Lucas
