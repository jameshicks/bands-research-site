Overview

Crippled And The Burnout were a Post-Punk/Punk group whose sole documented output was a single release on the American Gothic label in 1993. The five-member lineup included guitarist Norman Westberg — best known for his long tenure in Swans — alongside Dennis Lucas, Kenny Braun, Michael Duane, and Robert Goss. The band’s catalogue spans a single year, leaving their formation and context largely unrecorded; no Discogs profile exists to fill in the gaps.

Notes (web)

Norman Westberg identity confirmed. The Norman Westberg listed in Crippled And The Burnout is the same person as the long-running Swans guitarist. His Discogs artist page (https://www.discogs.com/artist/269726-Norman-Westberg) lists Crippled And The Burnout among his groups, and a March 2026 blog post (“Lunch with Norm,” flamingpablum.com/2026/03/lunch-with-norm/) describes the project explicitly as featuring “Norman Westberg on guitar” alongside “Michael Duane from the Dustdevils” and being “fronted by a guy named Robert Goss,” calling it “a great, way more abrasive little project” believed to be studio-only. (Source: search snippet from flamingpablum.com, 2026-03 post; page is a Spotify podcast embed with minimal body text — full transcript unavailable.)

Concurrent with Swans. Westberg was an active Swans member through the band’s first dissolution (1997), so his involvement in 1993 places Crippled And The Burnout squarely as a concurrent side project. Swans were active and releasing records throughout 1993 (Restless Records era / Love of Life / White Light from the Mouth of Infinity period).

Robert Goss — founder and label owner. Robert Goss (d. 28 August 2023, Palenville, NY) was the band’s frontman and the founder of American Gothic Records, the label that released New York (1993). His obituary notes he “founded the bands Crippled & The Burnout and The Super Creeps with many friends” and that he had founded American Gothic Records earlier, through which he had released singles for Dee Dee Ramone and Ritchie Stotts of The Plasmatics. He became embedded in New York’s Lower East Side music scene from 1986. The band thus appears to have been Goss’s own vehicle, with Westberg and Michael Duane (of Dustdevils) recruited as collaborators. (Source: obituary at keyserfuneralservice.com/obituaries/robert-goss-jr, accessed 2026-10-28.)

Michael Duane — Dustdevils connection confirmed. Michael Duane was the guitarist and co-founder of the Dustdevils, a New York City noise-rock band active from the mid-1980s through the mid-1990s, among the first artists signed to Matador Records. His involvement in Crippled And The Burnout fits the period when the Dustdevils were still active. (Source: Wikipedia, “Dustdevils,” https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dustdevils, as of 2026-10-28.)

Wikipedia gap. The Norman Westberg Wikipedia article (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Westberg, as of 2026-10-28) does not mention Crippled And The Burnout among his side projects — his listed projects are The Body Haters, Jarboe solo albums, The Heroine Sheiks, NeVAh, Five Dollar Priest, Crisis, and Hidden Rifles.

Release format. The New York release appears on 7” vinyl in at least three variants (white, yellow, and standard) per Discogs records (https://www.discogs.com/Crippled-And-The-Burnout-New-York/master/978234).

Dead end — formation details. No source found describing how or why Westberg and Duane were recruited to the project, where it was recorded, or what studio was used. The flamingpablum.com post is a podcast embed; the transcript is unavailable without subscribing.
