Culture Club

British pop group formed in London, 1981. Discogs artist ID: 9706. Members: Boy George (George O’Dowd, vocals), Jon Moss (drums), Roy Hay (guitar, keyboards), Michael Craig (bass).

Overview

Culture Club were one of the defining acts of the early-1980s UK pop scene, known for Boy George’s androgynous image and a sound drawing on new wave, pop, soul, and reggae. Their run of Virgin singles and albums produced multiple UK and US number ones.
