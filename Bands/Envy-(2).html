Envy (2)

Post-hardcore / screamo / experimental rock band from Tokyo, Japan. Formed 1992, initially as Blind Justice (2), renamed Envy in 1995. Discogs artist ID: 155390.

Biography

Formation and Early Years (1992–2000)

Envy formed in Tokyo in 1992 under the name Blind Justice (2), with a founding lineup of Nobukata Kawai (guitar), Manabu Nakagawa (bass), Masahiro Tobita (guitar), Dairoku Seki (drums), and Tetsuya Fukagawa (vocals). They changed their name to Envy in 1995. All five original members remained together for over two decades — an unusual degree of lineup stability for the genre.

Their early releases came out on HG Fact, the Tokyo label that was a hub for Japanese hardcore and experimental rock. Breathing And Dying In This Place (1996) was their debut; they followed it with a split 7” with Endeavor in 1997 (Envy / Endeavor), From Here To Eternity (1998), and Angel’s Curse Whispered In The Edge Of Despair (1999). A 2000 split 7” (A Split Seven Inch) paired them with US hardcore band This Machine Kills on HG Fact.

International Breakthrough (2001–2005)

All The Footprints You’ve Ever Left And The Fear Expecting Ahead (2001, HG Fact) was the album that brought international attention — thirteen pressings across multiple labels and territories. The album establishes the signature sound: raw screamo hardcore cut with passages of near-ambient melodic guitar and Fukagawa’s spoken-word delivery. Takashi Kitaguchi (known on some credits as “Patch”) recorded it, and Hiromichi Takiguchi mastered it.

A Dead Sinking Story (2003) was their first US release, issued on Level Plane Records. Kitaguchi engineered; Tatsuya Kase mastered. This was the lineup of: Fukagawa (vocals), Kawai (guitar), Tobita (guitar), Nakagawa (bass), Seki (drums), credited here as “Daichi Takasugi” in one variant credit alongside the other four — likely an alias or additional credit.

In 2002, a French label, Waiting For An Angel, issued Our Dreams Walking Their Way - Chapter 1, documenting the band’s European reach well before their Western label home was established.

Compiled Fragments 1997–2003 (2005, Sonzai Records) gathered early material and served as a retrospective bridge to their Western licensing period.

Temporary Residence Limited Era (2006–2016)

Temporary Residence Limited became the band’s primary Western home from 2006 onward. Insomniac Doze (2006, TRR 112) was the TRL debut, co-released by UK label Rock Action Records (a Mogwai-run imprint). Kitaguchi recorded and mixed; “Tucky” mastered the Japan pressing. The album ran to ten pressings. Fukagawa’s lyrics, written in Japanese and accompanied by English translations (by Teruhiro Takenouchi and Koo Takenouchi, the latter also contributing French horn), cement the literary character of the band’s work.

Abyssal (2007, Rock Action Records / TRL, 7 pressings) followed. That same year, a Japan-only companion release, Transfovista, appeared on Sonzai Records via BounDEE.

Two significant split releases appeared in 2008. Envy / Jesu (Daymare Recordings, Japan) paired them with Justin K. Broadrick’s Jesu; Aaron Turner provided artwork and illustrations. The Western split, Thursday / Envy (TRR 146), brought them into collaboration with New Jersey post-hardcore band Thursday; Jeremy deVine handled art direction, design, and layout — his recurring role across TRL releases.

Recitation (2010, TRR 168) marks a high point of the TRL era: Kitaguchi recorded and mixed; “Tucky” mastered again. Yoshiharu Ota did photography. Poet and musician Kaoru Okunuki contributed a voice/poetry reading, underlining the literary dimension of the record. Artist Susan (26) provided artwork.

Invariable Will, Recurring Ebbs And Flows (2013, TRR 200) credits the classic five-piece lineup explicitly by instrument — Kawai and Tobita on guitars, Nakagawa on bass, Seki on drums, Fukagawa on vocals.

Atheist’s Cornea (2015, TRR 259 / Sonzai Records) continued the partnership with TRL while maintaining a Japan-side Sonzai release.

Fukagawa left the band in 2016 (returning 2018).

Lineup Change and Post-2018 Period

Fukagawa rejoined in 2018 alongside three new members: Tsuyoshi Yoshitake (credited as yOshi, of killie) on guitar, Yoshimitsu Taki (of 9mm Parabellum Bullet) on guitar, and Hiroki Watanabe (of Heaven In Her Arms) on drums — replacing the long-standing tandem of Tobita and Seki. The founding rhythm-section anchor, Nakagawa, stayed on bass; Kawai remained on guitar.

Alnair In August (2018, TRR) was the return record, mastered by Rafael Anton Irisarri. Definition Of Impossibility (2019, Pelagic Records) was their sole Pelagic LP, with Daisuke Yamashita recording and mixing; Hiromichi Takiguchi mastered. A live album, Last Wish Live At Liquidroom Tokyo (2020, Pelagic), documented this lineup in concert.

The Fallen Crimson (2020, TRR 341) brought them back fully to TRL, mastered by Josh Bonati. Seimei (2022, TRL / Japan) followed. Eunoia (2024, TRR 436), their most recent studio album, was recorded and mixed by Kitaguchi (as “Takashi ‘Patch’ Kitaguchi”), mastered again by Bonati. Photography by Yoshiharu Ota. English translation by Yuta Nakajima.
