Ghost (2)

Discogs artist ID: 73875
Also known as: Ghost (Japan), The Ghost

Japanese experimental rock and improvisation group, formed in Tokyo in 1984. Disbanded 3 August 2014. Led by vocalist/guitarist Masaki Batoh. From the mid-1990s onward, most releases issued on US label Drag City; earlier work on P.S.F. Records. Michio Kurihara was the electric guitarist throughout the band’s later period. Ghost blend folk, psychedelia, krautrock, and Eastern elements; Batoh is the central compositional figure.
