Overview

Stórsveit Nix Noltes is a Reykjavík-based large ensemble (the name translates roughly as “Nix Noltes’s Orchestra” or “Big Band Nix Noltes”) performing Balkan and Eastern European folk repertoire — primarily Bulgarian and Macedonian dance forms including rachenitsa, krepatka, gankino horo, daichovo, kopanitsa, and pajdusko. The group draws its personnel from the overlapping circles of Icelandic experimental and folk-pop musicians active in the 2000s, most notably members of múm and Benni Hemm Hemm.

The band’s Discogs profile describes them as “Icelandic post-rock and Balkan folk band composed mostly of members of other Icelandic bands such as múm and Benni Hemm Hemm.” Their two known releases (2005, 2007) are entirely instrumental interpretations of traditional Balkan material rather than original compositions.

Notable Personnel Notes

Páll Guðmundsson (Discogs ID: 366646) — Icelandic sculptor born 1959 in Húsafell. His instrument, called the Steinharpa, is a large xylophone-like idiophone built from stones found around his home. He has also performed with Sigur Rós (per Wikipedia). His Discogs credits are limited to Orkiduer Hawaí and a 2012 Icelandic release (Stafnbúi on 12 Tónar).

Ólafur Björn Ólafsson — Drums on Orkiduer Hawaí and contributor to several Benni Hemm Hemm records. Released two solo albums on Hubro (Norway): The Third Script (2017) and Lanzarote (2019).

Áki Ásgeirsson — Trumpet/cornet. Active from at least 1999 (Áki & Starkaður on Geimsteinn) through 2024, primarily within the Reykjavík scene via Radio Bongo / Reykjavík Record Shop releases.
