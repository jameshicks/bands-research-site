The Now (4)

Discogs ID: 1118700 Origin: Peterborough, UK Active: 1977–c.1979 (original run); retrospective releases 2002–2005 Genres: Punk, Post-Punk, Power Pop Labels: The Ultimate Record Label, Raw Records (2), Last Year’s Youth Records

Biography

The Now formed in Peterborough in the first half of 1977, assembled from the wreckage of local band The Faderz. The founding lineup was Mike McGuire (3) (vocals, ex-The Faderz), Steve Rolls (guitar, ex-The Faderz), Paul Farrow (bass, known as “Faz”) and Joe MacColl (drums).

Their debut single “Development Corporations” b/w “Why?” came out in 1977 on The Ultimate Record Label (cat. ULT.401), a small UK imprint. The runout groove carries the message “TO PETER BOROUGH - LUV PORKY”, a personalised inscription from mastering engineer George Peckham (Porky (5)), confirming the single’s Peterborough origins. Songwriting on the debut was credited to McGuire (as “McGuire”) and Steve Rolls (as “Rowles” — his real surname is Stephen David Rowles). Paul Farrow is credited as “Paul Farrer” on the sleeve.

A second single, “Into The 80’s” b/w “Nine O’Clock”, followed in 1979 on Raw Records (2) (cat. RAW 31), a well-distributed London punk imprint. The writing credit here reads “Then” — one of the band’s name variations alongside “Now”. This appears to mark the end of the band’s original active period.

The band remained dormant until the early 2000s when German label Last Year’s Youth Records reissued “Into The 80’s” as a limited-edition EP (2002, LAST 12), followed by the retrospective LP Here Come The Now (2003, LAST BIG 4). The LP drew on material split between two sessions: Side Now featured MacColl on drums; Side Then featured two later/alternate members — Tommy Tomlinson (3) on drums and Nigel Davis on guitar, indicating the band had a second lineup configuration. A further LP of 1978 demos, Into The 80’s! - The 1978 Demos, appeared on Last Year’s Youth sublabel Boot Records (3) in 2005 (LEG-1), divided into “The T.W. Tapes” and “The Trunk Session”.

Wider rediscovery came in 2013 when “Development Corporations” was included on Soul Jazz Records’ Punk 45: There Is No Such Thing As Society — Vol. 2: Underground Punk And Post-Punk In The UK 1977–81, placing the band alongside Television Personalities, Josef K, Swell Maps, The Mekons, The Cravats, and others.

Three of the four original members — Joe MacColl, Steve Rolls, and Paul Farrow — subsequently played in Destructors, a longer-running Peterborough punk act. Mike McGuire went on to Sudden Sway, a post-punk/art-pop group.
