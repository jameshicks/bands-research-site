Badgerlore

Discogs ID: 556018
Active: 1999–2007
Experimental folk-rock collective, US. Founded by Rob Fisk and Ben Chasny; grew into a rotating ensemble drawing from the San Francisco psych-folk and free-improvisation underground.

Overview

Badgerlore began as a duo project between Rob Fisk (founder of Free Porcupine Society, also of Deerhoof, 7 Year Rabbit Cycle, and Common Eider King Eider) and Ben Chasny (Six Organs of Admittance, Comets On Fire). Their earliest release — a limited CDr on Pavilion and White Tapes (1999) — documents the duo in a spare, minimal mode tagged on Discogs under Blues/Electronic with styles “Electric Blues” and “Minimal.”

Over the following years the project expanded. By 2004’s Of Things Too Sorrowful To Be Reminded Of And Things Too Beautiful To Possess (the first release on Fisk’s own Free Porcupine Society), the core duo of Fisk and Chasny was fully established, with Fisk also credited for artwork. The 2005 Stories For Owls introduced a fuller ensemble: Tom Carter (Charalambides) and Pete Swanson (Yellow Swans) joined as performers. That record appeared on both Free Porcupine Society (CD, FPS12) and Yik Yak (LP, white label, limited edition). Both the 2007 albums completed the lineup by adding Glenn Donaldson (of The Skygreen Leopards and the Jewelled Antler collective) and Liz Harris (Grouper). We Are All Hopeful Farmers, We Are All Scared Rabbits was produced by Jeff Hunt and engineered and mixed by Tom Carter himself; it appeared on Xeric (a sublabel of Table Of The Elements). Book Of Dirt, a brief single-track CDr (23:57), came out on Wholly Other the same year.

An undated release, Birdghosts, appears on Ravenhill Cabin with no year or additional credits in the Discogs database; its place in the chronology is unclear.

The band’s profile also includes a split contribution: the 2000 UK release Three Owls Six Ears on Betley Welcomes Careful Drivers is a three-way split with A Warm Palindrome and Feverdreams. Badgerlore contributes one long track (“Beetle Kill Wood Lord,” 19:56); the A Warm Palindrome tracks feature Andy Jarvis (acoustic guitar, organ), Sol Kirkland (electric guitar, bass), Mikarla Jarvis (vocals, cello, ring-modulated flute), Phil Todd (2) (written by, performer, sampler, acoustic guitar, Casio, shortwave receiver), and Joincey (electric guitar, bass, drums, Amiga sampler, amplified egg slicer, pitchshifter).
