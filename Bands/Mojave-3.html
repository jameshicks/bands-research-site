British folk rock / country-inflected band formed from Slowdive — not Lush. Core trio: Neil Halstead (vocals/guitar, primary songwriter), Rachel Goswell (vocals), Ian McCutcheon (drums). Signed to 4AD by Ivo Watts-Russell who heard their demo.

Name: started as “Mojave”; stickered to “Mojave 3” when a US band with the same name was found. 104 Discogs entries.
