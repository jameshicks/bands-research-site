Amon Düül

Origin: Munich, West Germany
Discogs ID: 201946
Genres: Krautrock, experimental rock, acid rock
Years active: 1967–1971, 1980–1989

The original Munich commune-based music collective that preceded and spun off Amon Düül II. Formed by members of a radical art commune in Munich in the late 1960s. The commune split when offered a recording opportunity — the more musically proficient members boycotted and formed Amon Düül II, while the remaining commune members recorded what would later be released as Amon Düül. Recordings were of improvised and collective character, made around 1969, and released later to capitalize on Amon Düül II’s success.

Members include several who went on to Amon Düül II: Chris Karrer, John Weinzierl, Falk-Ulrich Rogner, and Peter Leopold. Other members (Angelika Filanda, Ella Bauer, Rainer Bauer, Klaus Esser, Krischke, Ulrich Leopold) had little or no further documented Discogs activity.
