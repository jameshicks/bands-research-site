Biography

Graham Parker & The Goldtops are a British rock outfit assembled around Graham Parker — the veteran pub-rock singer-songwriter best known for fronting Graham Parker And The Rumour from 1976 to 1980. The Goldtops represent Parker’s most sustained band project of his later career, drawing on a core of seasoned UK musicians to revive the tight, R&B-inflected sound of his classic period.

The band’s nucleus connects directly to The Rumour’s lineage. Martin Belmont (electric guitar) was a founding member of Ducks Deluxe and subsequently joined The Rumour, where he played on all the classic Parker/Rumour albums. Geraint Watkins (piano, organ, melodica) — a Welsh musician born in Abertridwr in 1951 — brings a long career spanning session and band work across the UK roots and pub-rock scene, including stints with Nick Lowe & His Band, The Balham Alligators, and Willie And The Poor Boys. Simon Edwards (bass, backing vocals), best known from Fairground Attraction alongside Roy Dodds (drums), forms a rhythm section with a proven track record across that band and beyond — Edwards also worked with Talk Talk, Billy Bragg & The Blokes, and various UK soul and jazz projects. The brass section of Ray Beavis (tenor saxophone) and Dick Hanson (trumpet) are both members of The Rumour Brass, the horn section reconstituted from the original Rumour touring lineup.

The Goldtops’ debut studio album, Cloud Symbols (2018), was released on 100- Records (2) — a UK independent label whose other signings include Orchestral Manoeuvres In The Dark, Skunk Anansie, Stone Foundation, and We Are Scientists. The album was produced by Parker, Neil Brockbank, and Tuck Nelson, with Nelson also handling recording and mixing. It features the full band lineup — Dodds, Edwards, Belmont, Watkins, Beavis, and Hanson — alongside Parker on vocals, acoustic guitar, and backing vocals. The brass parts were arranged by Beavis. All songs were written by Parker.

In 2019, 100% Records released Squeezing Out Sparks Solo Acoustic 40th Anniversary, a solo acoustic record from Parker marking the 40th anniversary of the landmark 1979 album Squeezing Out Sparks (originally recorded with The Rumour). The 2019 release is credited to Graham Parker only and carries no additional personnel credits in the Discogs record.

Five Old Souls (Live In Southampton With The Rumour Brass) (2021, 100% Records, cat. 100CD111) is the Goldtops’ key live document. Credited to Graham Parker & The Goldtops, it captures the band live with the full brass section (Beavis, Hanson, plus Toby Gucklhorn on trombone). The 17-track set draws heavily on Parker’s back catalogue — including “Heat Treatment,” “Soul Shoes,” “Hotel Chambermaid,” and “White Honey” — alongside covers of “You Left The Water Running” (Dan Penn / Oscar Franck / Rick Hall) and “Hold Back The Night” (Felder/Young/Harris/Baker). Parker introduces the band during track 4.

The Goldtops continued recording into 2023. Last Chance To Learn The Twist (Big Stir Records, BSR-0099, 2023) is a digital-only release (MP3/album) in the Power Pop/Pop Rock style, featuring 13 tracks. Personnel credits are sparse in the Discogs entry but the band is listed as Graham Parker & The Goldtops. The same year saw a double-A-side single, Them Bugs b/w The Ologist Song, also on Big Stir Records.

Parker’s wider solo discography on 100% Records in this period includes Nixon’s Rules (2019, 100S98), a non-Goldtops solo release. His later work moved to Iconoclassic Records (Acid Bubblegum 2023, Deepcut To Nowhere 2024, The Last Rock N Roll Tour 2025), suggesting the Goldtops project may have concluded around 2023.

Open Questions

  • The Discogs Goldtops entity lists Jim Russell (ID 161220) as a member but he does not appear in any release credits — his role is unclear (possibly a recording session that was not released, or a data error).
  • Cloud Symbols is noted in some sources as featuring “The Goldtops” but the Discogs release is credited solely to Graham Parker — the band credit only appears on Five Old Souls and the 2023 releases. Whether the Goldtops name was applied retroactively to Cloud Symbols needs confirmation.
  • Squeezing Out Sparks Solo Acoustic 40th Anniversary carries no personnel credits in Discogs — whether any Goldtops members appear on it is unconfirmed from the DB alone.
  • The stub note mentions a 2021 Five Old Souls on “100% Records” but the Discogs label is specifically “100% Records (2)” (ID 155058), distinct from the Formation Records sublabel “100% Records” (ID 7382) which is a mid-90s house label. These are unrelated entities sharing a similar name.
  • Parker’s move to Iconoclassic Records from 2023 onwards may signal the end of the Goldtops configuration — no Goldtops releases are recorded after Last Chance To Learn The Twist (2023) in the DB.
