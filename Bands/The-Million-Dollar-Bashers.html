The Million Dollar Bashers

Discogs ID: 982142

A large ensemble assembled for the Bob Dylan tribute soundtrack to Todd Haynes’s 2007 film I’m Not There — in which six actors portray different aspects of Dylan’s persona. The name derives from “Million Dollar Bash,” a Dylan / The Band Basement Tapes track. Participants covered Dylan songs for the film’s soundtrack album.

Notes

The “Million Dollar Bashers” name may have been used for live tribute performances as well as the soundtrack recording. The ensemble’s composition — mixing Sonic Youth members, Wilco members, Tom Verlaine, and Robyn Hitchcock — reflects the range of Dylan’s influence across indie and art-rock.
