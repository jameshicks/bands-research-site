Adventures In Stereo

Discogs ID: 343271 Origin: Glasgow, Scotland Active: 1996–2000 Labels: Creeping Bent (1996–1998); Bobsled Records (1998–2000); Underground Sounds (Simon Dine faction, 1997) Genre: Indie pop, indie rock, lo-fi, Britpop

Overview

Adventures In Stereo formed from the ashes of Spirea X, the short-lived 4AD band that had included vocalist Judith Boyle and guitarist/songwriter Jim Beattie (ex-Primal Scream). The duo formed Adventures In Stereo in the mid-1990s with Simon Dine, who created much of the early music. After two acclaimed EPs, Dine split from the group; both parties continued releasing records under the Adventures In Stereo name — Beattie’s via Creeping Bent (Glasgow), Dine’s via Underground Sounds.

The Beattie-led faction is the primary catalog documented here. Their sound was melodic indie pop, drawing on jangly guitars, lo-fi production values, and short song forms (many tracks under 2 minutes on Alternative Stereo Sounds).

Key Release Credits

Adventures In Stereo (1997, Bent 015CD — Discogs ID 411675)

18-track self-titled LP collecting early recordings and unreleased material. Tracklist includes: Underground Sound, Cry Your Love Away, The Attic Walk, Summer High, 13th Floor, Airline, There Was A Time, Runaway, Good Times, Don’t You Worry Little One, When We Go Back, Flipside, When Times Were Young, Close To You, Remain Again, My Buddy Go, Pretty Things, We’ll Meet Again.
