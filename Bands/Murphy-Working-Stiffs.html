Murphy Working Stiffs

Discogs ID: 234093
Aliases: The Murphy Foundation (Discogs ID: 5609008), The Murphy Working Stiffs (name variation)
Origin: UK (London)
Active: c. 1986

UK experimental ensemble active in the London DIY underground. Part of the extended “Murphy” constellation of projects centred around Robert Storey (His Grace The Earl Of Killorglin) and intersecting with the Woof Records / It’s War Boys networks. The group recorded one cassette album, Metamurphosis (1986), under the name The Murphy Foundation on Not On Label; a later edition under the name The Murphy Working Stiffs appeared on the Divine Agency label (Divine Agency release ID: 9933451, Not On Label release ID: 3642271, master ID: 1149128).

Robert Storey is the central figure in the wider Murphy network. His Discogs aliases include His Grace The Earl Of Killorglin (2853476), Bob Hopeless (2), Rob Murphy (5), Thoth (2), and Tito Pueblo. On the Divine Agency pressing of Metamurphosis, His Grace The Earl Of Killorglin is listed as Co-producer alongside Chris Gray (2). Storey’s other groups span Bing Selfish & The Ideals, Murphy No Geisha, Orchestre Murphy, Murphy Love Experience, Up Murphy Street, The Murphy Challengers, The Murphy Federation, The Miners Of Banal, and Croque Madame (2) — constituting essentially his own parallel musical universe of rotating “Murphy” projects.

Emma Peters (Discogs ID: 1255414) contributes strings on the Divine Agency pressing and is also credited on Up Murphy Street’s self-titled album (viola). She is not listed as a formal group member.

Network Context

Woof Records / The Work axis

Bill Gilonis co-founded Woof Records and The Work with Tim Hodgkinson in 1980. His appearances on Woof releases include Slow Crimes (1982) and The Work’s own catalog. Chris Gray (2) has a long parallel career as a recording engineer: his Discogs credit trail runs from 1978 punk sessions (Alternative TV, The Police’s Outlandos d’Amour, Klark Kent) through 1980s production work (The Homosexuals, Ut) into the 1990s and beyond. His presence on Metamurphosis as co-producer alongside Robert Storey (as The Earl Of Killorglin) is his clearest connection to the London experimental underground outside commercial production.

It’s War Boys network

Lepke Buchvalter connects the group directly to the It’s War Boys label and its associated projects. His credit on Metamurphosis for “Vocals, Instruments [Scronics]” uses the same unusual term “Scronics” that appears across his It’s War Boys-adjacent work. He and Chris Gray (2) also appear together in The Just Measurers (Discogs ID: 1210069), which released Flagellation on It’s War Boys in 1983 alongside Jim Welton.

Divine Agency label

Divine Agency (Discogs ID: 690345) is a small UK label with 5 releases and one sublabel (Divine Records (9)). Beyond Metamurphosis, its catalog consists entirely of Robert Storey-connected projects: Murphy No Geisha (Storey and Chris Cornetto, 2003), Nobodies’ Cup of Tea (2009, featuring Storey), Atsuko Kamura’s Kamura Obscura (2014, featuring Storey on guitar and John Glyn on saxophone), and Croque Madame (2)‘s self-titled (2016, also featuring Storey). The label’s Bandcamp URL is divinerecords.bandcamp.com. Divine Agency functions effectively as Robert Storey’s label home.

Up Murphy Street overlap

Up Murphy Street shares substantial personnel with Murphy Working Stiffs: Chris Cornetto, Chris Gray (2), John Glyn, Robert Storey, and Lepke Buchvalter (guest vocal on Up Murphy Street’s “Bald Progress”). The differences: Up Murphy Street’s core is anchored by Mick Hobbs and Bill Gilonis (who produced it), while Murphy Working Stiffs does not include Hobbs. Up Murphy Street also credits Trefor Goronwy (This Heat / The Camberwell Now) and Amos (3) / Jim Whelton (L. Voag / Xentos).

The Murphy Federation and broader Murphy universe

Robert Storey’s other named “Murphy” projects on Discogs include The Murphy Federation (members: Bing Selfish, Chris Gray (2), Storey; Discogs ID: 1642447), Murphy No Geisha (members: Chris Cornetto, Storey; ID: 3827875), Murphy Love Experience (ID: 695364), The Murphy Challengers (ID: 2221220), and The Murphies (ID: 10376017). These form a recurring, overlapping roster rather than a fixed band — a constellation of Murphy-branded projects cycling through the same core contributors.

Open Questions

  • The exact relationship between the two Metamurphosis pressings is unconfirmed: whether the Divine Agency pressing is a reissue or a simultaneous release variant of the 1986 Not On Label cassette. The Not On Label version credits “The Murphy Foundation”; the Divine Agency version credits “The Murphy Working Stiffs.” Both point to master 1149128.
  • No released year is recorded for the Divine Agency pressing in Discogs. Is it contemporary with the 1986 Not On Label cassette, or a later reissue? (The Divine Agency label’s Bandcamp suggests ongoing activity into the 2000s-2010s.)
  • Francois du Plessis (ID: 3827949) and Lorella Giradot (ID: 3827950) have no other Discogs credits. Their biographical backgrounds are unknown.
  • Pete Baker (4) (ID: 3859214) also appears in Lenin Lads (Discogs ID: 2221226) alongside Chris Gray (2), under the alias Pedro Panadero. No other context on Lenin Lads is in the vault.
  • Andi Haller (ID: 1238629) has Austrian connections: he appears on two releases from Vienna’s Nur Sch. Rec! label (1989, 1991) alongside KÖB. The Babyzithers (ID: 1238628) appear to be a Haller-affiliated group. His connection to the London Murphy network is undocumented.
  • Whether any live recordings, additional releases, or radio sessions exist for Murphy Working Stiffs is unknown — the Discogs entry is sparse.
  • Chris Gray (2)‘s name variations (C.D. Greyt, Christopher Greyt, C.D. Grebe) suggest possible use of different spellings across records. Whether he is the same person as any other “Chris Gray” in Discogs is unconfirmed — he is listed as “(2)” disambiguation.
  • The Emma Peters credited on Metamurphosis (strings) and Up Murphy Street (viola) shares a Discogs ID (1255414) — confirming same person — but no further biographical information is available.
