Thin Lizzy

Irish rock band, formed Dublin, 1969. Led by Phil Lynott (bass, vocals). Midge Ure had a brief association with Thin Lizzy before joining Ultravox in 1979.

Personnel Notes

Midge Ure (July 1979 – early 1980)

Midge Ure joined as a temporary replacement guitarist after Gary Moore abruptly departed mid-tour on 17 July 1979, when the band was in the US supporting Journey. Lynott called Ure with one day’s notice; Ure’s first show was at the Fox Theatre in New Orleans.

Role: primarily live/touring, with limited studio contribution.

  • Live: toured the US as second guitarist alongside Scott Gorham from July 1979 through the end of the US leg, then on into a Japan tour in early 1980. During the Japan tour he switched to keyboards (by his own account he had limited keyboard skills at that point).
  • Studio — Black Rose: A Rock Legend (1979): co-writing credit on “Get Out of Here.” Ure himself downplayed his contribution: “I didn’t really add an awful lot to it” and described himself as “a passive participant.” (American Songwriter)
  • Studio — The Continuing Saga of the Ageing Orphans (1979 remix compilation): guitar parts on “Things Ain’t Working Out” and “Dublin.” (Wikipedia — Midge Ure, as of 2026-06-29)

Ure left after the Japan/US tours concluded (early 1980) to focus on Ultravox, which he had already joined in April 1979 — the two commitments overlapped. He continued collaborating with Phil Lynott post-Lizzy: co-wrote “Yellow Pearl” (Lynott solo hit) and produced “Together” on The Philip Lynott Album.

Replacement after Ure: Dave Flett, then Snowy White.
