Sun Kil Moon

American indie/folk-rock project. Formed 2002, San Francisco area. Discogs artist ID: 348551. Name variation on Discogs: Sun Kill Moon.

Founded by Mark Kozelek following the dissolution of Red House Painters. Debut on Vinyl Films (2003); primary label Caldo Verde Records from 2005 onward. Kozelek is the sole constant — all other credited members vary by album.

The Red House Painters rhythm section (Anthony Koutsos — drums, Jerry Vessel — bass, Phil Carney — guitar) carried directly into the early Sun Kil Moon lineup.

Overview

Sun Kil Moon launched as a de facto continuation of Red House Painters, retaining Kozelek’s folk-rock songwriting and long-form song structures. The debut Ghosts Of The Great Highway (2003) names tracks after boxers. Tiny Cities (2005) is an all-covers album of Isaac Brock-era Modest Mouse songs (see below). From 2008 onward the project shifted toward Kozelek as a solo voice, with rotating collaborators assembled per album, culminating in stylistically diverse records ranging from acoustic solo guitar to noise-pop collaboration (Jesu) and Hungarian jazz-funk (Amoeba, 2024).
