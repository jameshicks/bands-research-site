Wolf Eyes

Discogs ID: 164195

US noise/experimental group based in Michigan; one of the central acts of the early 2000s American noise underground.

Core members include Nate Young, John Olson, and (formerly) Aaron Dilloway. Collaborated with Smegma on the split LP The Beast (2003, De Stijl).
