The Jam

Discogs ID: 10384

UK punk/Mod rock band formed in Woking, Surrey, 1972. Signed to Polydor 1977; dissolved by Paul Weller in October 1982 at the height of their commercial success.

Overview

The Jam were the dominant British guitar band of the late punk/post-punk era, regularly reaching the top of the UK singles chart. Rooted in Mod revival aesthetics (sharp suits, Rickenbackers), they were distinguished from contemporaries by Paul Weller’s sharp social-realist songwriting. The band had six UK #1 singles. They never broke through in the United States.

Members

  • Paul Weller — vocals, guitar, principal songwriter
  • Bruce Foxton — bass, vocals
  • Rick Buckler — drums
  • Steve Brookes — guitar (early lineup, left before major label signing)
