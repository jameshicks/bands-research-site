Errors

Electronic / post-rock / synth-rock group from Glasgow, Scotland. Discogs artist ID: 305059. Active on Rock Action Records from 2005 to at least 2015.

Overview

Errors formed in Glasgow in the early 2000s, releasing a self-titled CDr EP on Not On Label in 2004 before signing to Rock Action Records — Mogwai’s Glasgow imprint — for their official debut in 2005. Over the next decade they released three full-length albums and a run of singles and EPs on the label, developing a sound that merged post-rock guitar textures with synthesizers, electronic programming, and dance-inflected rhythms. Their Discogs genres span Electronic, Rock, Electro, Math Rock, Synth-pop, Leftfield, and EBM across different releases, reflecting a progression from skittery indie-electronic in the early period toward a more polished synth-pop and EBM sound by Lease Of Life (2015).

The early records were recorded and mixed partly with John Cummings — Mogwai’s guitarist — who co-recorded and co-mixed the debut album It’s Not Something But It Is Like Whatever (2008) and the Toes single. From the A Rumour In Africa single (2010) onward, recording and mixing credits shift fully to the band themselves (with Simon Ward / Steven Ward listed as engineer across later records).
