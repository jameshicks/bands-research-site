Amon Düül II (also Amon Düül) is a psychedelic/progressive band from Llanfair Caereinion, Powys, Wales, United Kingdom, that was formed in 1981. Not to be confused with Amon Düül, the original band, and Amon Düül II, the first Amon Düül II incarnation.

Please use this profile for the band formed by former Amon Düül II members John Weinzierl and Dave Anderson (3) at Foel Studios (South Wales, UK) in parallel to the official band. They recorded a number of sessions that resulted in four album releases, also featuring musicians from Van Der Graaf Generator, Ozric Tentacles, etc.

Overview

A Welsh psychedelic/progressive outfit formed in 1981 at Foel Studios in Llanfair Caereinion, Powys, by former Amon Düül II members John Weinzierl and Dave Anderson, running in parallel to the official band rather than as a continuation of it. The catalogue is built from four albums recorded in those sessions, drawing in musicians from Van der Graaf Generator and Ozric Tentacles; most releases appeared on Demi Monde, Thunderbolt, and Illuminated Records through the 1980s, with a late addition on Klimt in 2012. Robert Calvert figures across ten shared credits — a Hawkwind-adjacent space-rock connection — alongside Ozric Tentacles’ Ed Wynne and Joey Ozric, and Groundhogs guitarist Tony McPhee.
