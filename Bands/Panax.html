Panax

DC-area indie rock trio, active in the late 1990s. Discogs artist ID: 1887551. Affiliated with Teen Beat Records (catalog URL: teenbeatrecords.com/artists/bridgetkathidoug.html). Members: Bridget Cross, Kathi Wilcox, and Doug Bailey.

Overview

Panax was a short-lived trio that, by the Discogs profile’s own account, “never played a show, but thankfully recorded three songs with Guy Picciotto before they went their separate ways.” The band is documented in Discogs as an artist entity with a Teen Beat website listing, but only one release appears in the database: the 7” single Love Will Rule Our Party (1999, Teenbeat TEENBEAT 280), credited to the three members jointly (Bridget Cross / Kathi Wilcox / Doug Bailey) rather than under the “Panax” band name. The release was recorded by Guy Picciotto (Fugazi, Rites of Spring).

The band is a notable convergence of the DC indie and riot grrrl scenes: Bridget Cross was a core member of Unrest (2) and Air Miami (both on Teen Beat Records); Kathi Wilcox was a founding member of Bikini Kill and later The Casual Dots and The Julie Ruin; and Doug Bailey had ties to The Ropers and (The Sounds Of) Kaleidoscope.

The Discogs artist page URL from Teen Beat Records links to the page for “Bridget, Kathi & Doug” — suggesting the band was also known informally by members’ first names rather than consistently as “Panax.”
