The Rentals

American alternative rock / indie pop band. Discogs artist ID: 260323. Founded 1994 by Matt Sharp while he was still a member of Weezer.

Overview

The Rentals began as Matt Sharp’s side project during the recording and touring cycle of Weezer’s Blue Album. Their debut Return of the Rentals (1995, Maverick) featured Pat Wilson on drums and achieved moderate success with the single “Friends of P.” After Sharp left Weezer in 1998 he focused full-time on the Rentals. The band has had an intermittent release schedule into the 2010s.
