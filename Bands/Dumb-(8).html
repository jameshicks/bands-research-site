Dumb (8)

Discogs ID: 6561234 Also known as: Dumb And Friends Origin: Vancouver, BC, Canada Active: 2015–present Genres: Punk, Post-Punk, Indie Rock

Canadian punk/post-punk band from Vancouver, BC. Known for a prolific run of self-recorded cassettes and digital releases before signing to Mint Records (12) in 2018. The band writes, records, and mixes its own material and has a tightly knit DIY ethos; on later records they brought in outside engineers and mastering engineers while retaining production and songwriting control.
