Overview

The Jah Trio is a backing vocal group composed of Frank Infante and Jimmy Destri — both members of Blondie — alongside Mike Chapman, the producer behind most of Blondie’s late-1970s and early-1980s recordings. Their 14 credits cluster tightly around the Blondie sessions of 1979–1981, with all top collaborators being Blondie-associated artists, and their releases appeared on Chrysalis and RCA. The group’s catalogue spans two decades in miniature — a single release in the 1970s and one in the 1980s — suggesting a studio credit rather than an active performing unit.
