On!Air!Library!

American indie/electronic band. US, active 2000–2004. Discogs ID: 248836. Name variation: On! Air! Library!

The name derives from an African documentary in which an old man hosted a radio show called “The On Air Library.”

Core trio of Alejandra Deheza (vocals, guitar, electronic drums), Claudia Deheza (vocals, guitar, keyboards), and Phillip Wann (vocals, guitar, bass, electronic drums, sampler); Jason Hammons appears in the realname field and likely contributed to the early period. Genres ranged from shoegaze on the debut EP to downtempo electronic and alternative rock by the 2004 LP. The band broke up after the LP; Alejandra and Claudia Deheza went on to form School Of Seven Bells in 2007.

After On!Air!Library!

Both Deheza sisters joined School Of Seven Bells in 2007, with guitarist Benjamin Curtis (ex-Secret Machines, Tripping Daisy). The trio released three albums on Vagrant/Ghostly; Claudia left in 2010; Curtis died of T-cell lymphoblastic lymphoma on December 29, 2013. The final SVIIB album SVIIB (2016) was posthumously completed from Curtis’s demos.

Phillip Wann also appeared in Daylight’s For The Birds.
