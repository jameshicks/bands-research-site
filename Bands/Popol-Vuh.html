Popol Vuh

Origin: Munich, Germany
Discogs ID: 81882
Genres: Krautrock, ambient, spiritual/sacred music, progressive rock, film music

Munich-based krautrock and ambient/spiritual music group led by Florian Fricke, active from the late 1960s through the 1990s. Closely associated with director Werner Herzog, for whose films they provided many soundtracks. Among the most distinctive bands of the German experimental scene, moving from early electronic explorations (Kosmische) toward acoustic and spiritual music.

Strong personnel overlap with Amon Düül II: founding member Holger Trülzsch also guested on Amon Düül II’s debut Phallus Dei; Renate Knaup joined Popol Vuh after departing Amon Düül II in 1974; Daniel Fichelscher (drums, guitar) was a long-term member of both bands simultaneously.

Members (Discogs)

Florian Fricke (founder), Daniel Fichelscher, Holger Trülzsch, Renate Knaup, Al Gromer Khan, Conny Veit, Djong Yun, Frank Fiedler, Klaus Wiese, Ted De Jong, Robert Eliscu, Susan Goetting, Mathias von Tippelskirch, Maya Rose, Guido Hieronymus.
