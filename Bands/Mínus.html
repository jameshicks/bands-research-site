Overview

Mínus are a Reykjavík hardcore/alternative rock band, active since 1998, whose catalogue concentrates in the 2000s across styles ranging from punk and hardcore to indie rock and experimental. Their primary label home has been Smekkleysa (Iceland’s Bad Taste imprint), which has carried their work from 2001 through to 2024 — with international releases through Victory Records in the US and One Little Indian in the UK at different points in their career. Core collaborators run deep into the Reykjavík scene: producer Curver connects across 21 shared releases and vocalist Krummi Björgvins co-credits on 19, alongside scene-mates Amiina, Bang Gang, Apparat Organ Quartet, and Björk through compilation appearances.
