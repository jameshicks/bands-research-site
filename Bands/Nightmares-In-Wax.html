Nightmares In Wax

Liverpool post-punk group, active 1980–1981. Discogs artist ID: 129854

Overview

Nightmares In Wax were a Liverpool post-punk group formed around 1980, the immediate predecessor to Dead Or Alive. The band was led by vocalist Pete Burns (born Peter Jozzepi Burns, 5 August 1959 – 23 October 2016) and managed by Francesco Mellina. Their sole official release was the 7” single Birth Of A Nation (Inevitable (2), 1980, cat. INEV 0002), a three-track vinyl produced by Liverpool DJ and producer Noddy Knowler (Mike Knowler). The single’s A-side “Black Leather” (lyrics Burns, music Healy) became the track most associated with the band’s post-punk identity, later appearing on multiple Liverpool scene compilations. The band’s sound was rawer and more angular than what Dead Or Alive would develop, blending post-punk guitar with early dark-disco tendencies.

The group dissolved into Dead Or Alive around 1981–82, with Burns and keyboardist Martin Healy carrying over directly. A 2023 Cherry Red retrospective, Let Them Drag My Soul Away: Singles, Demos, Sessions And Live Recordings 1979–1982 (CDTRED879), compiled the Nightmares In Wax single alongside Dead Or Alive’s pre-Youthquake output — effectively treating the two names as a continuous arc.

Note: not related to Nightmares On Wax.

Label: Inevitable (2)

Inevitable (2) (Discogs label ID: 721439) was a Liverpool-based independent label, active from 1979 and associated with Inevitable Records Ltd. (parent). Run by two individuals (IDs 1891599 and 85784) as a by-product of the Probe Records shop (label ID 274257). Later entered a distribution deal with RCA for its final releases.
