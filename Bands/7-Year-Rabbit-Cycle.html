7 Year Rabbit Cycle

Discogs ID: 466880 Origin: San Francisco, CA, USA Active: 2002-2006 (Discogs documents releases 2003-2006) Label: Free Porcupine Society (FPS) Genres: Experimental, Noise, Post Rock, Noise-Folk URL: myspace.com/7yearrabbitcycle (defunct)

Overview

7 Year Rabbit Cycle is a San Francisco experimental/noise-folk band co-founded by Rob Fisk and Kelly Goode (credited variously as Kelly Goodefisk). Discogs describes them as a “noise rock band from US.” The group was the founding project of the Free Porcupine Society label, which Rob Fisk established in 2002 with Kelly Goode to release their music. The label subsequently grew into a key outlet for the SF Bay Area experimental underground, releasing records by Xiu Xiu, Deerhoof, Liz Harris (Grouper), Current 93, and Good For Cows.

Both Fisk and Goode are former members of Deerhoof — a central fact connecting 7 Year Rabbit Cycle to the broader Bay Area experimental rock scene of the early 2000s. Xiu Xiu’s Jamie Stewart contributed guitar and vocals to the band, and Deerhoof members John Dieterich and Greg Saunier mixed and mastered the second album. The band’s fluid, collective approach is evident across all three albums: different members take lead vocal duties on individual tracks, and the credited lineup expanded and contracted between releases.

Formation and Context

The Free Porcupine Society label was started in 2002, explicitly to release 7 Year Rabbit Cycle’s debut. Rob Fisk had been a member of Deerhoof (listed in Deerhoof’s Discogs member roster alongside Kelly Goode) and was simultaneously active in multiple experimental projects. The label contact email confirms Fisk as the primary operator. Label copy describes FPS as an outlet for “no wave hermit folk- dirt pirate dance- avant breadmaking field recordings” — language that maps directly onto 7YRC’s aesthetic.

The band drew heavily from overlapping SF Bay Area circles: Xiu Xiu (Jamie Stewart, Miya Osaki, Devin Hoff all connect to both bands), Deerhoof (Dieterich, Saunier as engineers; Fisk and Goode as former members), and the experimental folk underground that would also produce Badgerlore and Common Eider King Eider — both later Rob Fisk projects.

Connections

  • Xiu Xiu — Jamie Stewart and Miya Osaki played in both bands; Devin Hoff bridges both

  • Free Porcupine Society — Founded by Fisk and Goode; grew to document the wider Bay Area experimental scene
