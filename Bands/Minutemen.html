Minutemen

American punk/post-punk/hardcore band, San Pedro, California. Discogs artist ID: 28963. Active c. 1980–1985. Disbanded following the death of guitarist D. Boon in December 1985.

Overview

Core members: D. Boon (guitar, vocals; Discogs ID: 258554), Mike Watt (bass, vocals; Discogs ID: 77192), George Hurley (drums; Discogs ID: 177139). San Pedro-based; associated with the SST Records milieu and the New Alliance Records label.

Known for eclectic politics, brief/dense song structures, jazz and funk influences alongside hardcore speed. Their double LP Double Nickels on the Dime (SST, 1984) — with illustrations by Raymond Pettibon — is widely considered a landmark of American independent music.
