Deutsch Amerikanische Freundschaft

Also known as: D.A.F. / DAF
Discogs ID: 12830
Origin: Düsseldorf, West Germany
Active: 1978–1982 (first run); 1985–1987 (reunion); 2003 (reunion); 2017–2020 (final period)
Genres: Electronic, Electro-punk, NDW, EBM

German electro-punk group formed in 1978, best known as the duo of Gabi Delgado (vocals) and Robert Görl (drums, electronics). Core architects of Neue Deutsche Welle (NDW) and, via the Virgin trilogy, founding forebears of Electronic Body Music (EBM). See also DAF (short-name page).

Transitional lineup (1979–1982)

Kurt Dahlke departed; replaced by Chrislo Haas (synthesizer, tape, saxophone, bass guitar) from 1979. Spelmans and Kemner phased out through 1980; by the Alles Ist Gut era (1981) the band operated as a strict duo of Görl + Delgado.

Career

1978–1979 — Düsseldorf Origins

Formed out of the Rhine/Ruhr punk scene. Delgado had been briefly involved with Mittagspause (as dancer/performer) and later Deutschland Terzett; he was also involved in making “The Ostrich,” the first German punk fanzine (founded by Franz Bielmeier). Görl had prior connections to Der Plan.

Debut LP: Produkt Der Deutsch-Amerikanischen Freundschaft (Warning Records NR. 00001 / WR 001, 1979). All tracks untitled — 22 tracks across two sides of vinyl. Personnel: Görl (drums), Kemner (bass), Spelmans (guitar), Dahlke (synthesizer). Self-released on their own Warning Records (6) imprint.

1980 — London, Mute Records

Band moved to London in 1980 and signed to Mute Records. Haas had replaced Dahlke by this point; Spelmans remained.

1981 — Virgin Trilogy, Part I & II

Signed to Virgin. Settled as a duo: Görl composed all music and played all instruments; Delgado provided all words and vocals. Conny Plank produced all three Virgin LPs.

Alles Ist Gut (Virgin 203 644, 1981):

RolePersonnel

10 tracks. Contains “Der Mussolini” (A2) — DAF’s most covered/remixed track. “Der Räuber Und Der Prinz” (A5), “Rote Lippen” (A3). Defines the NDW/electro-body sound: minimal drum machine, Görl’s hard electronic grooves, Delgado’s provocative German-language yelps.

11 tracks. Sides titled “Light Side” and “Dark Side.” Contains “El Que,” “Absolute Körperkontrolle,” “Verschwende Deine Jugend.” 36+ pressings worldwide.

Singles from the Alles Ist Gut / Gold Und Liebe period: “Der Mussolini” (Virgin, UK), “Der Räuber Und Der Prinz / Rote Lippen” (Germany), “Der Räuber Und Der Prinz / Tanz Mit Mir” (Mute, UK), “Goldenes Spielzeug / Gold Gold Gold” (Virgin, UK), “Als Wär’s Das Letzte Mal / Der Mussolini” (Germany).

1982 — Für Immer and First Split

10 tracks. All written jointly by Görl + Delgado-Lopez. Tracks include “Im Dschungel Der Liebe,” “Ein Bißchen Krieg,” “Kebab Träume,” “Verlieb Dich In Mich.”

The band split shortly before the album’s release in 1982. Additional 1982 singles: Sex Unter Wasser / Knochen Auf Knochen, Verlieb Dich In Mich / Ein Bisschen Krieg, Kebab-Träume / Ein Bisschen Krieg. Live sets from 1982–84 circulated via Live HH 12.12.80 (bootleg, Mutterstolz Records, 32 pressings) and official Live issues.

After split: Gabi Delgado released Mistress (1983, solo debut). Robert Görl released solo material and worked with Der Plan.

1985–1987 — Reunion, Illuminated/Dean Records

Reunited in 1985 under Illuminated Records:

  • Absolute Body Control (Mix Solid) 12” (Illuminated, 1985)
  • Brothers 7” (Illuminated LEV 65, 1985) — self-produced; “Brothers (Mix By DAF)” / “Brothers (Robert’s Mix)”

Moved to Dean Records:

  • 1st Step To Heaven (Dean Records, 1986)
  • Voulez Vous Coucher Avec Moi Ce Soir (Dean Records, 1986)
  • Pure Joy 12” (Dean Records, 1986)
  • The Gun (Dean Records, 1987)

Conny Plank died December 18, 1987.

1988–2002 — Compilations, Sporadic Activity

  • D.A.F. compilation LP (Virgin, 1988) — widely distributed, 9+ pressings
  • Hitz Blitz compilation (JCI, US, 1989)
  • Verschwende Deine Jugend / El Que Remix single (Virgin, 1989)
  • Der Mussolini 12” EP (Mute/The Grey Area, 1998) — reissue compilation
  • Der Sheriff (Anti-Amerikanisches Lied) single (Superstar Recordings, 2002)

Around 1996 Delgado teamed with Wotan Wilke to launch DOS, building on DAF’s legacy with contemporary dance music elements. He also ran Düsseldorf and Berlin club projects including Delkom (with Saba Komossa).

15 tracks. Titled descriptively: “Der Sheriff (Anti-Amerikanisches Lied),” “Du Bewegst Dich (Tanzlied),” “Rock Hoch (Sexlied),” etc. Minimal/EBM aesthetic, self-produced.

2017–2020 — Grönland Records, Final Period

  • Das Ist DAF (Grönland Records, 2017) — major retrospective compilation; mastered by Kai Blankenberg
  • Reworx (Grönland Records, 2018) — remix album
  • Ich Denk An Dich single (Grönland Records, 2020)

Gabi Delgado died of a heart attack on March 22, 2020. The duo had been planning a new studio album.

2021 — Nur Noch Einer

After Delgado’s death, Görl assembled old DAF material from the band’s 1980s work, wrote new lyrics, sang them himself, and released as Nur Noch Einer (Grönland Records, 2021). The title translates as “just one more,” “one last album,” or “down to one.” 15 tracks, including “Erste DAF Probe” (first DAF rehearsal) and “Ein Kind Aus Dem Ratinger Hof” (referencing the key Düsseldorf venue Ratinger Hof).

Connections
