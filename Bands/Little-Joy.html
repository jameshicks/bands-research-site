Little Joy

Brazilian-American indie-pop trio formed 2007–2008 in Los Angeles. Discogs artist ID: 1288662.

Members: Fabrizio Moretti (drums, multi-instrumentalist; The Strokes), Rodrigo Amarante (vocals, guitar, multi-instrumentalist; Los Hermanos (3)), and Binki Shapiro (vocals, guitar, multi-instrumentalist; real name Jordana Shapiro). Discogs also lists Jack Dishel (Discogs ID: 380175) as a member, though his role in the group is not reflected in the album’s credit sheet.

Released one self-titled album in 2008 on Rough Trade (cat no. RTRADLP 485). No second album has been documented in Discogs.

Formation

The trio’s origin is described on their Discogs profile. Amarante and Moretti — coming from separate hemispheres of the indie world — first met at a Portuguese festival in Lisbon, where both had performed. They stayed up talking through the night near the river, floating the idea of making music outside their respective bands.

The concrete opportunity arose when Amarante traveled to Los Angeles to record with Devendra Banhart on his Smokey Rolls Down Thunder Canyon album (2007). During down time from that recording, Amarante and Moretti began meeting to plan their collaboration. Binki Shapiro, a Los Angeles native, was introduced to the pair through mutual friends and became the third member, encouraging them to commit to the project.

The three moved into a house in the Echo Park neighborhood of Los Angeles to demo material. The band name came from the cocktail lounge down the street from their home.

Self-Titled Album (2008)

Little Joy was released in 2008 on Rough Trade, with pressings in the UK, US, France, Australia, Japan, and (in 2009) Brazil via Slap (2). The primary pressing is a UK/US vinyl LP.

Producer: Noah Georgeson produced and mixed the album, and plays slide guitar (tracks 2, 4, 8) and guitar (track 4) as a performer. He is the dominant production voice across the record.

Co-producer, track 1: Thom Monahan co-produced the opening track “The Next Time Around” and is credited as mixer on the album overall.

Co-producer, tracks 2–11: Beau Raymond (Discogs ID: 954840) is credited as co-producer on all tracks except the first.

Mastered By: JJ Golden (Discogs ID: 379566).

Photography: Cover photography by Maria Eugenia Baker. Group and back cover photography by Autumn DeWilde (credited on the sleeve as “Autumn De Wilde”).

Artwork and Design: Brett Kilroe (Discogs ID: 1597984) and Binki Shapiro.

Background: The Devendra Banhart Connection

The circumstances linking Little Joy’s three members run directly through Devendra Banhart’s 2007 sessions. Amarante and Moretti’s preliminary collaboration planning took place during the making of Banhart’s Smokey Rolls Down Thunder Canyon. Noah Georgeson, who produced the Little Joy album, had also recorded that Banhart album — cementing the production relationship before the band formally existed. Banhart later returned the favor by contributing additional vocals to “Don’t Watch Me Dancing” (B4).

This overlap is characteristic of the freak-folk and LA indie circles of the mid-to-late 2000s: Georgeson, Monahan, Banhart, Adam Green, and Nick Valensi are all present on the record as session contributors or guest vocalists.
