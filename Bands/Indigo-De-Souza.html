Overview

Indigo De Souza is an indie rock act from Asheville, North Carolina, centered on the solo artist of the same name. Active since 2018, the catalogue spans two releases in the 2010s and three in the 2020s, moving across indie rock, alternative rock, and indie pop with touches of garage rock and synth-pop. The project released primarily through Saddle Creek from 2019 to 2024 before signing to Loma Vista in 2025. Recurring collaborators include MJ Lenderman (17 shared credits) and Owen Stone (15), alongside producers Josh Bonati, Alex Farrar, and Adam McDaniel.

Notes (web)

Saddle Creek → Loma Vista (2024)

De Souza signed to Loma Vista in September 2024, debuting with the EP Wholesome Evil Fantasy (20 Sep 2024) before the full LP Precipice (2025). No announcement from either party stated specific deal terms or named label-priorities reasoning. De Souza’s own public explanation, given later in the context of a dispute with Loma Vista, was that she left Saddle Creek because she “hoped that their resources and experience would help me grow as an artist and move into the next chapter of my career” — i.e., a straightforward step-up to a better-resourced major-distributed label. No press release from the signing announcement (Sep 2024) quoted her or the label on strategic rationale; it focused on the new EP. clickrollboom.co.uk, 20 Sep 2024

Loma Vista dispute and GoFundMe (Oct 2026)

By October 2026 — roughly two years after signing — De Souza had launched a GoFundMe seeking $90,000 to buy back rights to a completed but unreleased album and exit the Loma Vista deal early. In her public statement she did not name the label explicitly, but press coverage identified it as Loma Vista based on Precipice. Her characterisation of why the relationship failed:

“I felt a centring around business, money, and bureaucracy in a way that I had not experienced before. Things felt harder — harder to work, harder to create, harder to manage my mental health, and harder to stay connected and true to my spirit as an artist and person.”

“I felt like I couldn’t move, and it made me sick. I knew I had made a mistake and that I couldn’t thrive in that kind of work environment.”

“It has been indescribably painful to feel this album just sitting there, finished, while I have been waiting and negotiating its freedom.”

She described the early-termination negotiation as “one of the most demoralizing experiences of my life” and stated she had “offered to repay every cent they invested in the making of this record.” The completed album was written and recorded after Hurricane Helene devastated her North Carolina home in late 2024. As of the campaign launch, $12,548 of the $90,000 goal had been raised. Sources: Paste Magazine, 13 Oct 2026 · Exclaim!, 13 Oct 2026 · Stereogum, 13 Oct 2026

Summary: The original Saddle Creek → Loma Vista move was resource-driven (access to a larger label’s infrastructure) with no known personal or creative falling-out with Saddle Creek on record. The Loma Vista relationship subsequently broke down over what De Souza characterised as a values misalignment around commercialism and control, leading to a public campaign to exit the deal.
