The Parasails

Solo project of Matthew Mondanile (Ducktails). Active 2009. Discogs artist ID: 1499017.

Overview

The Parasails was a short-lived solo side project created by Matthew Mondanile in 2009, running parallel to his primary solo outlet Ducktails. Both projects emerged from the same period of home-recording activity rooted in lo-fi, tape-saturated drone and ambient pop. A 2009 Village Voice piece described it as Mondanile “growing another limb” alongside Ducktails.

The project’s sole documented release is Skylife (2009), issued on El Tule in two formats: a CDr co-released with Future Sound Recordings, and a limited-edition cassette on El Tule alone. Mondanile is credited on the CDr pressing under the playful role “Swimmer” — no other personnel appear.

Skylife is a two-sided record (tracks titled simply “A” and “B” on the CDr; “Untitled” A/B on the cassette), tagged across genres including Ambient, Drone, Experimental, Lo-Fi, Downtempo, and Pop Rock — consistent with the hazy, home-recorded aesthetic Mondanile was developing across all his projects at the time.
