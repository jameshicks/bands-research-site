Sun City Girls

Phoenix, Arizona experimental/avant-garde trio: Alan Bishop (bass/vocals), Richard Bishop (guitar), and Charles Gocher (drums, died 2007). Active from 1981 until Gocher’s death; one of the most prolific and eclectic acts in US underground music, touching psychedelia, world music, noise, and comedy. John Yates handled art direction on Torch Of The Mystics (1993, Tupelo Recording Company).
