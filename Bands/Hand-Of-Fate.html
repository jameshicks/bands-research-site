Hand Of Fate

Discogs ID: 612985
Origin: Los Angeles, California, USA
Label: WTG Records (US, cat. NK-46068); Epic (Europe/UK, cat. 467735 2)
Active: c. 1989–1991

LA AOR/rock band. Members: John Keck (vocals, co-producer), Rick Boston (guitar, co-producer), Rick May (bass), Jerry Angel (drums); also listed: Ricky May and Jerry Angel. One LP: Hand Of Fate (1990). Additional session credits: Marv Kanarek (Linn Drum), Suzie Katayama (cello).

Label Signing Story

The “Epic” credit in many listings is a distribution artefact. The band’s actual US label was WTG Records (cat. NK-46068); Epic handled the European/UK release (cat. 467735 2) because WTG was distributed through the CBS/Epic network.

What WTG Records was: WTG was a Los Angeles-based imprint launched in 1988 by industry veteran Jerry Greenberg as a West Coast sibling to Columbia and Epic within the CBS Records Group. Its name stood for Walter Yetnikoff, Tommy Mottola, and (Jerry) Greenberg — the three CBS/Sony executives behind it. The label operated 1988–c.1993, then folded into Sony Music Entertainment; Greenberg subsequently went on to run MJJ Music for Michael Jackson. Other WTG roster acts included Jason Bonham (Greenberg’s flagship signing), Motörhead, Eighth Wonder (featuring Patsy Kensit), Louie Louie, and Bonham/Jimmy Harnen. (source: interview with Jerry Greenberg, bitememf.com, 2010; WTG Records, Rate Your Music)

Why an LA indie act landed there: Guitarist/co-producer Rick Boston was already an active session player and film-soundtrack composer in Los Angeles before Hand Of Fate. He played guitar on Belinda Carlisle’s 1988 album Heaven on Earth and had soundtrack credits on Police Academy 4, Roxanne, and later Iron Man (2008). That professional foothold in the LA industry placed him in proximity to the CBS/Epic network that ran WTG. No direct interview source has been found describing the specific pitch or A&R introduction, but the most plausible route is Boston’s existing session/production connections feeding into WTG’s West Coast mandate to sign LA-based talent. (Rick Boston, Wikipedia, as of 2026-10-03)
