James Carter Organ Trio

Soul-jazz organ trio led by saxophonist James Carter (3). Discogs artist ID: 1111697. Active from at least 2004; principal live vehicle for Carter’s organ-trio work rooted in Detroit’s jazz scene and Baker’s Keyboard Lounge.

Overview

The James Carter Organ Trio operates in the classic Hammond organ-trio format — saxophone, organ, drums — and has been Carter’s primary vehicle for soul-jazz, hard bop, and post-bop live performance since the mid-2000s. The format connects directly to Carter’s Detroit upbringing and his long association with Baker’s Keyboard Lounge, where organ-trio jazz has been a staple since the club’s founding in 1936. The trio has released four studio and live albums across Half Note Records, EmArcy/Universal, and Blue Note.

The core lineup for its first decade was Carter on saxophones, Gerard Gibbs on Hammond B-3 organ, and Leonard King on drums. For the 2019 Blue Note live album, Alex White (14) replaced King on drums, while Gibbs remained on organ.

Formation Context

Carter had established an organ-trio working relationship before forming this named ensemble. Gerard Gibbs and Leonard King both appeared on Carter’s Live At Baker’s Keyboard Lounge (Warner Bros., 2004), a broader-band date at the Detroit club. The organ-trio configuration appears to have crystallized as a named touring and recording unit from around that period.

Leonard King’s Detroit roots run deep: he was the drummer in The Lyman Woodard Organization (Strata Records, 1975), a foundational Detroit soul-jazz ensemble. This connects the trio directly to the lineage of Detroit jazz that nurtured Carter.
