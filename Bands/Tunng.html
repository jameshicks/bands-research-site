Overview

Tunng are a London-based experimental folk band founded in 2003, whose sound weaves together acoustic folk with electronic and leftfield textures. The core lineup of Mike Lindsay, Martin Smith, Ashley Bates, Becky Jacobs, and Phill Winter produced their most prolific output in the 2000s — 13 releases — with a quieter but continued run into the 2010s and 2020s. Full Time Hobby has been their home since 2006 and remains so as of 2025, with earlier singles and EPs through Static Caravan. Electronic musician Benge towers as their most frequent collaborator at 38 shared credits, a connection that points to the electronic dimension running through their otherwise folk-rooted catalogue.
