Age Of Chance

Leeds indie/dance crossover band, formed 1983. Discogs artist ID: 24172. Known for abrasive sampladelic sound fusing post-punk and hip-hop; had a notable cover of “Kiss” (Prince) in 1987. Active on FON Records and later Virgin. On Where Were You- (Independent Music From Leeds 1978-1989) with “I Don’t Know And I Don’t Care” (CD2, track 16).
