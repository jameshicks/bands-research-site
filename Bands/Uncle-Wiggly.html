Overview

Uncle Wiggly was a New York indie/psychedelic rock band formed in the early 1990s by WFMU personality Wm. Berger together with James Kavoussi and Michael Anzalone of Fly Ashtray; fourth member Jenna McGrath also appears in the lineup. Berger and Kavoussi traded guitar and drums while Anzalone anchored on bass, and all three original members shared songwriting and vocal duties. Their catalogue — five releases clustered across the 1990s — reflects an aesthetic shaped by early Pink Floyd, Krautrock, and SST-era touchstones like the Meat Puppets and the Minutemen. Primary label homes were Shimmy Disc and Nur Sch. Rec!, with additional releases on Teenbeat, Dark Beloved Cloud, and several smaller imprints; frequent studio collaborators included J.Z. Barrell and Jim Fourniadis, each appearing on six releases.
