This Great Religion

Alternative / indie rock band from Los Angeles, CA. Active 1988–1993. Discogs artist ID: 3377831. Originally named Last Carousel; renamed to This Great Religion when vocalist Maria Hall joined the lineup.

The band is primarily notable as the first professional partnership of Justin Meldal-Johnsen (bass) and Tony Hoffer (guitar), both of whom went on to major studio careers in the following decade. Their sole label home was Intermission Records (Whittier, CA), run by Brock Wirtz of the post-rock group Scenic. The band released two limited-edition numbered 7”s on Intermission and a self-released cassette before dissolving in 1993. Three of the four Discogs-listed members — Meldal-Johnsen, Hoffer, and drummer Matt Gunnell — also appeared together on the 1992 Japanese Victor mini-album by Nanaki And The Spice, suggesting the core rhythm section remained active as a working unit even as the band wound down.

No releases have been found on Discogs under the prior name Last Carousel.
