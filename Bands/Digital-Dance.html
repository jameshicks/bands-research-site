Digital Dance

Discogs ID: 141346
Active: 1979–1982 (approx.)
Origin: Brussels, Belgium
Genres: New Wave, Synth-pop, Post-Punk, Coldwave
Labels: Digital Records (3) (own label); Vogue; Dirty Dance; LTM Recordings (2007 retrospective)

Belgian New Wave and coldwave band formed at the intersection of the Brussels punk scene and early European electronic music. Their first release was a 1979 Kraftwerk cover, their own label Digital Records (3) issued the two original releases, and the project dissolved in the early 1980s as members dispersed into a constellation of post-punk and synth projects. A posthumous compilation, Treatment, was issued by LTM in 2007. Central figure Jerry WX died in 2000; the remaining members have remained active in Belgian music for decades.

Connections

  • Kid Montana (ID 148767) appears as a shared project for Lederman, Barbery, Zylbersztajn, Wauquaire, and Lefebvre — effectively the successor vehicle for much of the Digital Dance lineup post-dissolution.
  • Streets (2) (ID 300722) is the band where Lederman first played (from 1977); Zylbersztajn, Wauquaire also passed through it. The Brussels/Belgian punk-to-synth pipeline runs through this band.
  • Play It Again Sam Records: Stephan Barbery designed for PIAS between 1983 and 1990, placing him at the centre of the Belgian independent label scene during its most significant decade.
  • LTM Recordings (UK) issued the 2007 Treatment compilation (LTMCD 2498) — part of LTM’s broader programme of reissuing overlooked European post-punk and new wave.

Note: “guitarist” here refers to his solo instrument (guitar + rhythm box); LTM cites him as bassist within Digital Dance. https://stilll-off.bandcamp.com/album/psychoactive-itch (as of 2026-07-12)
