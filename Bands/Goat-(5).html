Goat (5)

British rock band from Brighton, active 1989–1991. Discogs artist ID: 431308

Signed to Situation Two (a sublabel of Beggars Banquet) in 1989, the band released one debut single before moving up to Beggars Banquet proper, where they issued three further singles and one album, Medication Time (1991). The guitarist Adrian Oxaal subsequently joined Sharkboy (2) and then James, where he has remained through to the present. Singer Marcus Thirtle performed under the stage name Marcus Vandell and later fronted The Vandells. Bassist Mark Browning (2) moved on to The Belltower.

Nota bene: Discogs disambiguates as “Goat (5)” — several other artists share the name.

Bio

Goat were a Brighton rock quartet that signed to Situation Two — the post-punk / indie sublabel operated under the Beggars Banquet umbrella — in 1989. Their debut, the 12” single “Can’t Get By” (cat. SIT 63 T), was self-composed and co-produced by the band alongside engineer Ian Caple, with mastering by BilBo (3) at Damont. The two-sided 12” also featured B-sides “In The Fields” and “Whitewashed.”

For subsequent releases the band moved to Beggars Banquet directly. The 1990 single “Your Love Isa Mine” (BEG 239 T) was produced by Goat alongside veteran rock producer Nick Tauber, with recording and mixing by Barry Clempson and Tauber; a B-side remix of “Kissin Me” was handled by The Compression Twins with engineering by Ian Caple. All four members are credited as co-writers on this release — Oxaal as “Oxall,” Lovejoy as “Lovejoy,” Marcus Thirtle billed as “Vandell,” and Browning as “Browning.”

Also in 1990, the band released the six-track mini-album As You Like (BBL 110), self-produced at John Cornfield’s studio, with photography by Paul Cox (5) and artwork/design by Stiff Weapon and Paula Huntbach. The record was licensed to Vertigo for a Canadian pressing and to Alfa for Japan (1991).

The 1991 singles were both limited-edition formats: “Good Times” (BEG 248P) was a 10” picture disc, with individual performance credits listing Oxaal on guitar, Ambrose Lovejoy on drums, and Marcus Vandell on vocals. It was co-produced by the band and Mark Flannery, and included a cover of “T.V.Eye” (The Stooges). “Everybody Wants To Be There” (BEG 256 T) was a 10” 45 rpm limited edition; the lead track remix was produced by Mark Flannery, with Nick Robbins engineering the remaining tracks.

The album Medication Time (BEGA 119), released in 1991, was co-produced by the band and Mark Flannery. It runs to eleven tracks including studio versions of “Everybody Wants To Be There,” “Good Times,” and “In The Fields” (a returning B-side from the debut). Oxaal is billed as “Adrian ‘Odd’ Oxaal” with credits for “Guitar, Noises, Other [Yellch]” and Songwriter. Photography was by Paul Cox (5). The album was licensed internationally: to SPV GmbH (UK pressing with different cat no.), Alfa (Japan), Rebel Rec. (Germany), and Beggars Banquet (Italy/Worldwide).

No compilations featuring the band are documented in Discogs.

Open Questions

  • As You Like (1990): This six-track mini-album is not mentioned in many standard Goat (5) writeups — it predates Medication Time and appears to be a proper release. Whether it was commercially successful or widely reviewed is not resolvable from Discogs.
  • The “Good Times” 10” picture disc (BEG 248P) is tagged as “Heavy Metal” on Discogs — an unusual genre tag for a Brighton indie act; may reflect the heavier direction the single took or a mis-tagging.
  • Exact recording studio for Medication Time not specified in Discogs credits.
  • Mark Flannery’s studio affiliation: Discogs profile references label ID 266498 but this was not pulled; identity of the facility unknown from DB alone.
  • Barry Clempson and The Compression Twins (on “Your Love Isa Mine”): minor credits only in DB; no further profile information.
  • Koot (2) (Rod Chambers): credited for illustration on “Good Times” and “Everybody Wants To Be There” — hard-rock artwork designer; no further connection to Goat documented.
  • Stiff Weapon (Stephen Webbon / Slim Star, Discogs ID 1828296): artwork on As You Like and Medication Time — further info on this designer not in DB.
