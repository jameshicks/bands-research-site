The Beat (2)

Birmingham ska/2 Tone revival group. Discogs artist ID: 29786. Active 1978/1979–1983. Known as The English Beat in North America and briefly as The British Beat in Australia to avoid confusion with a US pop group.

Overview

The Beat formed in Birmingham in 1978, coalescing around guitarist/vocalist Dave Wakeling and bassist David Steele, with guitarist Andy Cox, rapper/toaster Ranking Roger (Roger Charlery), drummer Everett Morton, and veteran Jamaican saxophonist Saxa (Lionel Augustus Martin). Keyboardist Dave Blockhead (David Wright) joined in 1980.

The group debuted on Two-Tone Records in late 1979 with a cover of “Tears of a Clown,” immediately charting in the UK and establishing them as key players in the Birmingham ska revival alongside The Specials, Madness, and Selecter. They quickly launched their own imprint, Go-Feet Records, distributing through Arista in the UK and licensed to I.R.S. Records for North America. All three studio albums were produced by Bob Sargeant, with Mike Hedges producing one track on Special Beat Service.

The band split in 1983. Cox and Steele formed Fine Young Cannibals (later also 2 Men A Drum Machine And A Trumpet). Wakeling and Ranking Roger (along with Saxa and other personnel) formed General Public. Saxa and Everett Morton formed International Beat. Ranking Roger also joined members of The Specials in Special Beat.

Two touring lineups operated into the 2010s: “The English Beat starring Dave Wakeling” (US-based, Discogs: The Beat (8)) and “The Beat with Ranking Roger” (UK-based, Discogs: The Beat (6), which also featured Ranking Roger’s son). Ranking Roger died 26 March 2019, age 56.
