Neotropic

Electronic artist; real name Riz Maslen. Early artist on Different Drummer, active from the mid-1990s.

Overview

Neotropic (Riz Maslen) was part of the Different Drummer roster alongside Original Rockers / Rockers Hi-Fi. Her track “Bubble Dub” appears on the Bastard Tracks compilation (Different Drummer, CDG0012, 1995). The Different Drummer profile specifically names Neotropic as one of the label’s founding-era acts. She is also credited as Written-By/Producer on “Highway” via Airgoose/Bastard Tracks material.
