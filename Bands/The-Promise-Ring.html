The Promise Ring

Discogs ID: 623225 Active: ~1995–2002 Origin: Milwaukee, Wisconsin

Midwestern emo/indie rock band; most-catalogued artist on Jade Tree (71 entries). Nothing Feels Good (1997) is the label’s most-pressed release at 22 pressings. 30° Everywhere (1996) preceded it. Core members: Davey Von Bohlen (vocals/guitar), Jason Gnewikow (guitar), Scott Beschta, Dan Didier. Formed February 1995 in Milwaukee from members of None Left Standing (Gnewikow, Mangan) and Ceilishrine (Didier, Beschta); von Bohlen joined that spring as replacement for Matt Mangan (who relocated to Indianapolis). First self-released 7” on Foresight Records, May 1995. Cap’n Jazz broke up July 1995, freeing von Bohlen to focus fully on the Promise Ring. Signed to Jade Tree 1995; debut EP Falsetto Keeps Time February 1996. Reunited 2012. [Source: Wikipedia, as of 2026-04-09 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Promise_Ring]
