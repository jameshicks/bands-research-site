Storm And Stress

Also styled: Storm & Stress, Storm&Stress, stormandstress
Discogs ID: 193149
Origin: Pittsburgh, PA (later NYC)
Active: c.1996–2000
Genres: Free jazz, math rock, experimental, noise rock

Free improvisation / math-rock trio whose name derives from the German literary movement Sturm und Drang. Formed around Ian Williams — then also the guitarist in Don Caballero — with drummer Kevin Shea and bassist Eric Emm (real name Eric M. Topolsky). A fourth member, George Draguns, is listed on the Discogs artist page and the band profile credits him as the original bassist, though he receives no LP credits; Emm appears on both studio albums. The band released two LPs and one promo CD on Touch And Go between 1997 and 2000, then dissolved. Williams went on to found Battles; Shea joined a wide orbit of NYC jazz and experimental projects.
