Dead Kennedys

Hardcore punk band. San Francisco, California, USA. Discogs ID: 239092. Formed 1978.

Members: Jello Biafra (vocals), East Bay Ray (guitar), Klaus Flouride (bass), Ted (drums, later D.H. Peligro). Statik Records was their primary UK licensee with 77 releases 1981–1989. Key Statik releases: In God We Trust, Inc. EP (1981, catno STAT EP2, 26 pressings); Bleed For Me (1982, 13 pressings); Halloween (1982); Plastic Surgery Disasters LP (1982, catno STAT LP 11, 29 pressings — Statik’s most-pressed master); Plastic Surgery Disasters / In God We Trust, Inc. CD (1985). Original US releases on Alternative Tentacles.
