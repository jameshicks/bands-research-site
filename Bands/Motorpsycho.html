Motorpsycho

Discogs ID: 70518 Origin: Trondheim, Norway Active: 1989–present Genres: Psychedelic Rock, Progressive Rock, Art Rock, Hard Rock, Noise Rock Labels: Voices Of Wonder (1991–1995), Columbia (1996–2001), Stickman Records (3) (1994–2023), Rune Grammofon (2008–2024), Det Nordenfjeldske Grammofonselskab (2024–present)

Long-running Norwegian progressive/psychedelic band founded in Trondheim in October 1989. The band took its name from a Russ Meyer film while watching a triple-feature in London — “Mudhoney” and “Faster Pussycat” were already taken by other bands; “Motorpsycho” was still available. They are the highest-volume artist on Rune Grammofon with 69 releases (2008–2024), and one of Norway’s most prolific rock acts across three decades. Core membership is Bent Sæther (bass, vocals, primary songwriter) and Hans Magnus Ryan (guitar, vocals); the drummer slot has rotated through five documented members. Helge Sten (Deathprod) co-produced the first several albums and performed on Ancient Astronauts (2022), making him effectively a long-term third voice in the band’s sound.

Biography

Formation and Early Years (1989–1992)

Motorpsycho formed in October 1989 with the lineup of Bent Sæther (vocals, bass), Hans Magnus Ryan — credited as “Snah” — (guitar, vocals), and Kjell Runar Jenssen (drums, credited as “Killer”). Their first release was the cassette Maiden Voyage (1990) on Knallsyndikatet, a privately distributed item that circulated locally in Trondheim. The debut proper, Lobotomizer, followed in 1991 on Voices Of Wonder, recorded with minimal crew (Svein Solberg and Trude Midtgård engineering). Kjell Runar Jenssen departed after Lobotomizer, and Håkon Gebhardt took over on drums.

The early EPs — 3 Songs For Rut and 8 Soothing Songs For Rut (both 1992, Voices Of Wonder) — established the pattern of pairing compact song-based material with more expansive noise-rock excursions.

The Demon Box Era and Deathprod Collaboration (1993–1995)

The triple LP Demon Box (1993, Voices Of Wonder) remains one of Motorpsycho’s most ambitious early statements. It introduced a key creative partnership: Deathprod (Helge Sten operating under his noise/electronics alias) co-produced and contributed noise generation, audio processing, and “audio virus” effects. Lars Lien handled recording alongside Helge Sten (Sten and Lien would remain closely associated with the band through much of the 1990s). Kim Hiorthøy provided artwork — a collaboration that also continued for decades.

The 1994 double album Timothy’s Monster (Harvest) deepened the Deathprod partnership. Its personnel listed five “Motorpsychos” on that album: Bent Sæther, Snah M. Ryan, Håkon Gebhardt, Lars Lien, and Deathprod. Helge Sten and Lars Lien handled mixing in divided portions of the record — a structural approach that gave different sides distinct sonic textures. Kim Hiorthøy did art direction and design.

Major Label Period and Stickman (1994–2007)

The Tussler (1994, dBut) and Blissard (1996, Columbia) moved the band toward more polished, internationally distributed work. Blissard expanded the lineup to a four-piece with Morten Fagervik on keyboards, vibraphone, and rhythm guitar alongside the core trio. Production was shared between Bent Sæther and Deathprod, with Pieter Kloos also credited as producer (“with”), recorded across multiple studios (Atlantis Grammofon AB with Helge Sten and Pieter Kloos engineering; Lydlab and Rodeløkka Studio also used). Stickman Records (3) (Germany) became a key release partner from 1994 onward, often issuing international editions of albums simultaneously with or slightly after Norwegian releases.

Angels And Daemons At Play (1997), Trust Us (1998), and Let Them Eat Cake (2000) continued the run of strong studio albums. The Roadwork live archive series launched in 1999 with Vol. 1 on Stickman, documenting 1998 European dates. The series would eventually reach at least five volumes.

In 2003 Motorpsycho contributed to the Dutch In The Fishtank series (Konkurrent, Konkurrent).

Håkon Gebhardt served as drummer from 1991 until March 2005, when he left to pursue other projects — a 14-year tenure that defined the classic lineup. His departure prompted a period of lineup adjustment.

Black Hole / Blank Canvas (2006, Stickman) and Haircuts (2008, Stickman) appeared in the post-Gebhardt period before the transition to Rune Grammofon.

Rune Grammofon Era — Phase One: Kenneth Kapstad (2008–2016)

Little Lucid Moments (2008, Rune Grammofon, RCD 2073) marked the label debut and introduced Kenneth Kapstad on drums. The album was co-produced by Bent Sæther and Helge Sten (in his own name, not as Deathprod), with Jørgen Træen handling mixing and generating additional sounds. Bob Katz mastered. Kim Hiorthøy continued on cover art — a collaboration now spanning 15+ years.

Child Of The Future (2009), Heavy Metal Fruit (2010), and X-3 (Knuckleheads In Space) (2010, a three-way release with Rune Grammofon) followed.

The Death Defying Unicorn (2012, RCD 2124) is one of the most elaborately orchestrated releases in the catalog. A full co-credit with Ståle Storløkken (keyboards), the album featured the Trondheim Jazz Orchestra and Trondheimsolistene strings. Arrangement credits were shared between the band, Storløkken, and the orchestral leaders. Kenneth Kapstad played drums; Kåre Christoffer Vestrheim (mellotron, gong, additional production) and Kjetil Møster (clarinet, saxophones) appeared as featured performers. The writing credits are notably cross-attributed: Sæther, Ryan, and Storløkken all share song credits throughout the double album.

Still Life With Eggplant (2013, Stickman) appeared on the German label rather than Rune Grammofon — illustrating the dual-label pattern common in this period. Behind The Sun (2014, Rune Grammofon, RLP 3155) continued with Kapstad; Crispin Glover Records (Norway) co-issued the Stay Young / These Are Days single (2014). The live recording En Konsert For Folk Flest (2015, Stickman) documented the Nidarosdomen (Nidaros Cathedral) concert from July 2014.

Here Be Monsters (2016, Rune Grammofon) and Spin, Spin, Spin (2016, Rune Grammofon) appeared in the same year, the latter being an archival/reissue project.

Rune Grammofon Era — Phase Two: Tomas Järmyr (2016–2024)

Tomas Järmyr (Swedish, previously in Zu, Yodok, and numerous Scandinavian experimental projects) joined as drummer in or around 2016–2017. He appears on The Tower (MPLP 301, Rune Grammofon, 2017) — the first MPLP-catalog release — as well as all subsequent studio albums through the 2020s.

The Tower (2017) was partly recorded at Rancho De La Luna (Joshua Tree, California) with Dave Raphael and Hayden Scott engineering; additional overdubs done in-house. Alain Johannes (guitar, cigar box guitar, flute, vocals) guested on several tracks. The album had Wim Bult mastering and Noah Shain mixing. Cover and artwork by Håkon Gullvåg, who became the visual collaborator for several subsequent records.

Begynnelser (Music From An Imagined Play) (2017, Rune Grammofon) was a theatrical soundtrack project.

The All Is One (2020, MPLP 304, Rune Grammofon) expanded the lineup further. Performers on the album included Sæther, Ryan, Järmyr, Reine Fiske (on specific tracks), and Lars Horntveth and Ola Kvernberg (strings/arrangement, on specific tracks). Mastered by Helge Sten; mixed by Andrew Scheps. Peter Deimel engineered at the French studio (tracks A1–A4, D1–D4) and Henning Svoren at a second location (tracks B1–C2).

Kingdom Of Oblivion (2021, MPLP 305, Stickman) featured the Sæther/Ryan/Järmyr/Fiske four-piece as core performers. Mastered by Helge Sten; mixed by Andrew Scheps.

Ancient Astronauts (2022, MPLP 306, Rune Grammofon) returned Helge Sten to a performer credit alongside the four-piece — he also mastered the album, and Deathprod (his noise alias) is credited as producer. Andrew Scheps again mixed.

Yay! (2023, Stickman), Punch (2023, Alternative Tentacles / Crispin Glover Records — a collaboration with Patrik Fitzgerald and Jello Biafra), and Neigh!! (2024, Det Nordenfjeldske Grammofonselskab) indicate the band continuing to operate across multiple label relationships even after the formal Rune Grammofon catalog period ended.

Post-Rune Grammofon: Det Nordenfjeldske Grammofonselskab (2024–present)

The 2024 LP Neigh!! appeared on Det Nordenfjeldske Grammofonselskab — a new label entity that also issued the 2025 film soundtrack Ahsol Caravan (Original Motion Picture Soundtrack) and a self-titled 2025 album. A 2026 release The Gaia II Space Corps appeared on “Not On Label (Motorpsycho Self-released).”

Open Questions

  • Ingvald André Vassbø and Olaf Olsen are listed as Discogs members of Motorpsycho but no specific release credits identified — their roles and periods need clarification (possibly live/touring members or very early lineup).

  • The Motorpsycho Archives and Det Nordenfjeldske Grammofonselskab labels may be fully band-owned entities — not confirmed from Discogs data alone.
