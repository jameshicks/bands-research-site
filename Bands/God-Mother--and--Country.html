God Mother & Country

Short-lived post-punk / synth-pop group. UK. Discogs ID: 996598. Active 1985. Name variation: “GMC”.

Formed by core members of Float Up CP following that band’s dissolution. Discogs credits as members: Bruce Smith (drums), Neneh Cherry (vocals), and Dave Wright (2) (saxophone — the “Dave Flash Wright” also credited on Rip Rig & Panic releases). The “Foot On The Rock” single credits a larger ensemble of performers.
