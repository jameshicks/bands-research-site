Positive Noise

New wave / synthpop band. Glasgow, Scotland. Discogs ID: 293706. Active from July 1979; synths added December 1979.

The first act signed to Statik Records, and the reason the label named itself after the Second City Statik — A Glasgow Compilation on which they appeared. Three brothers — Ross, Fraser (Frazer), and Graham Middleton — with Les Gaff and Russell Blackstock. Key Statik releases: Heart Of Darkness LP (1981, catno stat lp1), Charm single (1981), Give Me Passion single (1981). 79 total Statik releases 1981–1985.
