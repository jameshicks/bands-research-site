Overview

The Belbury Circle is the duo of Jim Jupp and Jon Brooks, two central figures in the Ghost Box Records orbit — Jupp co-founded the label, while Brooks records separately as The Advisory Circle. They released two records on Ghost Box between 2013 and 2017, working in a territory of synth-pop, synthwave, and experimental downtempo. Their catalogue’s most prominent outside connection is John Foxx, with whom they share three credits across their short run.

Notes (web)

John Foxx collaboration

The Belbury Circle’s connection with John Foxx produced two releases and was rooted in acknowledged influence rather than any intermediary introduction: Jupp and Brooks have openly cited Foxx as a key influence on their work, making the pairing a natural one once Ghost Box was in a position to propose it.

Empty Avenues EP (2013) — The first collaboration was a five- or six-track EP titled Empty Avenues, released September 2013 on Ghost Box (catalogue GBX019). It came out as both a CD (six tracks, with a Pye Corner Audio remix of the title track) and a 10” vinyl (five tracks, with a download code). Packaging was designed by Ghost Box co-founder Julian House. Ghost Box and contemporary press described the music as Foxx’s songs “perfectly framed by haunting pastoral electronics, with perhaps a stronger pop sensibility than previous Ghost Box releases.” A reviewer at Post Punk Monk noted the sound drew on library music, early synthesizers, and late-1960s pre-psychedelic pop, with Crumar electric pianos giving it a sepia-toned quality distinct from Foxx’s concurrent work with The Maths. https://postpunkmonk.com/2013/10/03/restless-foxx-stays-busy-apart-from-the-maths/ · The Quietus, 2013

Outward Journeys LP (2017) — Foxx reprised the collaboration on The Belbury Circle’s debut full-length album Outward Journeys, released 3 November 2017 on Ghost Box (CD, LP, cassette, download). He contributed vocals and synth to two tracks: “Trees” and “Forgotten Town.” The album was billed as “The Belbury Circle & John Foxx.” https://music.apple.com/us/album/outward-journeys/1280329256 · https://bleep.com/release/89276-the-belbury-circle-outward-journeys

How the pairing came about — No contemporaneous interview or press piece found (across The Quietus review, the Post Punk Monk piece, the Wikipedia Ghost Box article, and retail pages) gives a first-person account of the introduction. The circumstantial picture is: Jupp and Brooks acknowledged Foxx as a formative influence; Ghost Box was operating in territory (hauntological synthesiser music, British pastoral electronics) adjacent to Foxx’s early solo work and the broader Maths-era output; and the label was at that point (2013) collaborating with outside artists. The connection appears to have been artist-initiated or label-proposed on the basis of shared aesthetic ground rather than through a third-party introduction.
