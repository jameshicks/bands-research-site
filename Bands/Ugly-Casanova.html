Ugly Casanova

Indie rock collective / side project. Discogs ID: 226068. Active 2002 (one album, one 7”).

Originated from home recordings made by Isaac Brock (Modest Mouse) under the alias “Ugly Casanova,” assembled into a full album with producer Brian Deck (Califone, Red Red Meat) and Tim Rutili (Califone, Red Red Meat). Additional contributors include Pall Jenkins (The Black Heart Procession), John Orth, and Tyler Riley. Released entirely on Sub Pop (2002). No subsequent releases under this name.

Context

The Edgar Graham fiction. Ugly Casanova was publicly presented as the project of a mysterious figure named “Edgar E. Graham” — supposedly a savant who appeared at a Modest Mouse Denver show in 1998 and left tapes. More tapes were “discovered” in November 2001, swathed in tape and pelts, and released as Sharpen Your Teeth. Brock later admitted the persona was entirely invented: “a fiction thing that I started awhile back to eliminate myself from the band” — i.e., to avoid interviews. The name “Ugly Casanova” predates the project: the 1997 Modest Mouse EP The Fruit That Ate Itself was credited to a production entity called “Ugly Casanova,” suggesting Brock used it as a publishing vehicle, and he had written a song called “Ugly Casanova” around 1993.

Recorded at Glacial Pace Studios, Oregon — Brock’s own facility. Produced by Brock and Brian Deck. Released May 21, 2002 (Sub Pop SPCD 552).

Brian Deck and Tim Rutili — both core to the Chicago Califone / Red Red Meat orbit — served as primary production and instrumental collaborators, giving Sharpen Your Teeth a lo-fi folk and country texture distinct from contemporary Modest Mouse records. Brock and Pall Jenkins co-wrote and co-sang on two tracks (“Pacifico,” “So Long To The Holidays”); John Orth (Holopaw) co-wrote and sang on four tracks.

2010 activity. Eight new Ugly Casanova songs appeared on the soundtrack to the documentary 180° South: Conquerors of the Useless (2010).

2026. Ugly Casanova announced for Modest Mouse’s 2026 fan cruise — first public activity announcement in ~15 years.
