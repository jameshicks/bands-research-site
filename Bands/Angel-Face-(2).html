Overview

Angel Face (2) is a band whose catalogue spans Punk, Psychedelic Rock, Garage Rock, Hard Rock, and Noise. With a first release in 1985, the group has spread its output across seven separate single-release labels over four decades — from Pacific Production (1985) and Flying Charentaise (1990) through Apachefilm and TransLoveEnergy (both 2004) to Salted City (2010), Cameleon Records (2) (2016), and LUCE E STELLE (2024). The five-member lineup of Denis Alvarez, Eric Tende, Frédéric Cousseau, Henry Flesh, and Hervé Zénouda has no documented collaborators in Discogs beyond their own releases, and no Discogs biography exists to explain the group’s origins or the persistent label-hopping across its long lifespan.
