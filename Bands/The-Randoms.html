The Randoms

Discogs artist ID: 327345 (listed as “Randoms”; name variation “The Randoms”) Origin: Los Angeles, California Active: 1977 Label: Dangerhouse (PT-1) Genre: Punk

Overview

The Randoms were a short-lived Los Angeles punk outfit active in 1977, best remembered for their sole release: the two-sided 7” single “A B C D b/w Let’s Get Rid Of New York” on Dangerhouse (catalog number PT-1). The record was among the first releases on Dangerhouse, the Hollywood punk label co-founded in 1977 by Pat Garrett, Black Randy (John “Black Randy” Morris), and David Brown (21).

The band’s lineup was: Pat Garrett (guitar, vocals), K. K. Barrett (drums), and John Doe (2) (bass) — the latter known at the time by his legal name John Nommensen Duchac. Both Garrett and Barrett had parallel involvement with Black Randy & The Metrosquad, the loose collective that formed the social and logistical core of the early Dangerhouse circle. Duchac would shortly adopt the stage name John Doe and become a founding member of X (5), one of the defining bands of the LA punk scene.

The B-side, “Let’s Get Rid Of New York,” has had the longer afterlife: it was included on the Soul Jazz compilation Punk 45 Vol. 1 - Kill The Hippies! Kill Yourself! (2013), cementing its status as a canonical artifact of West Coast first-wave punk.

Track Credits

Per the Dangerhouse: Complete Singles Collected (2013) tracklist, which is the most fully credited Discogs source:

“A B C D”

  • John Doe (2) — Bass
  • K. K. Barrett — Drums
  • Pat Garrett — Vocals, Guitar
  • Written-By: K. K. Barrett, Pat Garrett

“Let’s Get Rid Of New York”

  • John Doe (2) — Bass
  • K. K. Barrett — Drums
  • Pat Garrett — Vocals, Guitar
  • Written-By: Pat Garrett

Note: The original 1977 7” (Discogs ID 472034) credits “Written-By” on the A-side to Barrett and Garrett but lists only John Doe’s bass credit and Pat Garrett’s “Written-By” on the B-side, with no producer or engineer credits.

Note on Discogs entries: John Doe appears under two Discogs artist IDs — 158024 (“John Doe (2)”) and 1565899 (“John Duchac”) — both listing realname John Nommensen Duchac and identical aliases. The two entries are effectively duplicates and should be treated as the same person.

Connections

  • Dangerhouse — sole label. Co-founded by Pat Garrett, Black Randy, and David Brown (21). Released all Randoms material.
  • Black Randy & The Metrosquad — overlapping membership (Garrett, Barrett, and John Duchac all credited on Black Randy releases)
  • X (5) — John Doe (John Duchac) became a founding member immediately after or concurrent with The Randoms
  • The Dils — Pat Garrett played drums on The Dils’ Dangerhouse single (“198 Seconds of The Dils,” 1977) under the alias Rand McNally
  • Screamers — K.K. Barrett was drummer for the Screamers, the other major Dangerhouse-adjacent act
  • Punk 45 Vol. 1 - Kill The Hippies! Kill Yourself! — “Let’s Get Rid Of New York” included on Soul Jazz’s 2013 survey of American punk 1973–1980
  • Punk 45 - Chaos In The City Of Angels And Devils (Los Angeles) — Dangerhouse and the wider LA punk context documented
