Alkaline Trio

Chicago, Illinois punk/post-punk trio formed 1996. Members: Matt Skiba (guitar/vocals), Dan Andriano (bass/vocals), Derek Grant (drums). Known for melodic punk with dark lyrical themes. Released on independent labels (Jade Tree, Asian Man Records) before major-label tenure on Epic and then Epitaph. Discogs artist ID: search needed.

John Yates (Stealworks) designed Agony & Irony (2008, Epic) and This Addiction (2010, Epitaph).
