The Normals (2)

Punk band from New Orleans, Louisiana. Discogs artist ID: 321458. Also catalogued as “Normals.”

Overview

The Normals formed in New Orleans in the late 1970s around the songwriting partnership of Charles Hanson and David Brewton. Their sole original-era release was the 1978 single “Almost Ready” / “Hardcore” on Lectric Eye Records (cat. EYE 2), produced by Hanson. “Almost Ready” was written by Brewton; the B-side “Hardcore” by Hanson. The single is a key document of New Orleans punk and has been reissued multiple times, including a 2010 pressing on Last Laugh Records (2).

After a long dormancy, the band reconvened and released Vacation To Nowhere in 2011 on Last Laugh Records (2) — a full LP with twelve tracks drawing on material written by all four members. A second reunion release, the three-track EP Demented Breakdown, followed in 2017 on the same label.

Hanson later went on to play in several other bands with national profiles, most notably Chrome Cranks (active early–mid 1990s, on PCP Entertainment and Crypt Records). Chris Luckette also played in The Cold (2), a New Orleans group. Steve Walters (3) played bass in Cowboy Mouth in the early 1990s.
