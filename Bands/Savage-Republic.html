Savage Republic

Discogs artist ID: 121133

Los Angeles post-punk / experimental band, active from the early 1980s. Formed at UCLA. Known for tribal percussion, abrasive guitar textures, and middle-eastern influences.

Overview

Released their debut LP Tragic Figures in 1982 on Independent Project Records (cat: IP-004), co-produced with Jonathan Gold. Bruce Licher was the primary creative force and founded Independent Project Records to release the band’s work.
