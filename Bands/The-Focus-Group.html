The Focus Group

Discogs ID: 445511 Real name: Julian House Origin: UK Active: 2005–present Type: Solo recording project of Julian House Name variations: Focus Group, The Focus Groop Primary label: Ghost Box

Overview

The Focus Group is the recording alias of Julian House — British graphic designer, Ghost Box co-founder, and longtime sleeve artist for Broadcast and Stereolab. The project makes densely layered collage music assembled from radiophonic sources, library records, toy instruments, vintage electronics, and fragments of early British educational film and television sound. The sleeve notes to the first release, Sketches And Spells, describe the project as “a collection of individuals.. an empty church hall, hoofprints, mirrors, echoes, sequences of ordered greenery, televisual images, fonties and ortres” — imagery drawn from House’s collage visual aesthetic.

All solo releases appeared on Ghost Box, the label House co-founded with Jim Jupp (Belbury Poly). The most high-profile releases were two collaborative albums with Broadcast, the first of which — Investigate Witch Cults Of The Radio Age (2009) — was released by Warp Records rather than Ghost Box, marking a rare step outside the label for Focus Group material. A second Broadcast collaboration, Familiar Shapes And Noises, appeared on Ghost Box the following year, shortly before Broadcast vocalist Trish Keenan died in January 2011.

Later Focus Group work included a 2017 LP credited to “The Focus Groop” and close collaboration with Ghost Box labelmate Jon Brooks (The Advisory Circle), who produced the 2013 album Elektrik Karousel and mastered the 2017 record.

The Broadcast Collaborations

The relationship with Broadcast had roots in Julian House’s role as Broadcast’s long-running visual designer — he designed sleeves for all their Warp Records albums from Work And Non Work (1997) through to the Investigate Witch Cults campaign. The musical collaboration grew naturally from this close creative partnership between House and Trish Keenan and James Cargill.

Ghost Box Context

The Focus Group was present at the launch of Ghost Box — Sketches And Spells (GBX002) and Hey Let Loose Your Love (GBX005) were among the earliest releases in 2005. The label is co-run by House and Jim Jupp. The roster as of the early 2020s also includes Belbury Poly, The Advisory Circle, Pye Corner Audio, Beautify Junkyards, and John Foxx. The Focus Group accounts for 23 releases on the label (4th in roster by release count behind Belbury Poly, The Advisory Circle, and Pye Corner Audio).

House has designed sleeve artwork for essentially the entire Ghost Box catalog throughout this period.

Julian House’s Design Career (Selected)

House has been one of the most prolific sleeve designers in UK independent music since the mid-1990s, working extensively as “House@Intro” via the design studio Intro. Significant design credits include:

  • Stereolab (Duophonic Super 45s, 1996–2001 extensively)
  • Broadcast (Warp Records, 1997–2009)
  • Primal Scream (Creation Records, 1997–2004)
  • Oasis (via Beady Eye and Noel Gallagher’s High Flying Birds)
  • Massive Attack (Mute, 1997)
  • The Chemical Brothers (Astralwerks/Virgin, 2005)
  • Plone (Ghost Box reissues)
  • Björk — Film titles for Biophilia Live (2014)
  • Paul McCartney — A Christmas Cornucopia (2010) and later releases
  • John Coltrane estate reissues — A Love Supreme: Live in Seattle (2021, Impulse!)

House also directed the video for Doves’ “There Goes The Fear” (2002, Heavenly) and was co-founder and member of Children Of Alice (with James Cargill and Roj Stevens) in 2017.
