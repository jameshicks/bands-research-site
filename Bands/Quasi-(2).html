Quasi (2)

Portland, Oregon indie rock duo (occasionally trio), formed 1993. Discogs artist ID: 242940. Core members: Sam Coomes (Rocksichord/keyboards/bass/vocals) and Janet Weiss (drums/vocals). Bassist Joanna Bolme joined as a third member from approximately 2007 to 2011.

Overview

Coomes and Weiss were married; Quasi continued after their divorce and remained active for three decades. The band’s sound centers on Coomes’s heavily processed Rocksichord — a keyboard with sustain-less, percussive attack that mimics guitar tones — layered over Weiss’s driving, dynamic drumming and their dual-vocal interplay. The instrumentation is spare by design: two people covering melodic, harmonic, and rhythmic ground that typically requires a full band, with Coomes doing most songwriting and Weiss contributing drums, occasional co-writes, and significant production work from The Sword Of God onward.

Quasi formed in Portland in 1993. Their earliest releases were self-recorded and self-released or put out on small regional labels (Key-Op Records, Red Rover). They signed to Up Records for the US market and Domino for the UK/Europe, a split that defined their early catalog. By 2001 their US home shifted to Touch And Go, then to Kill Rock Stars for American Gong (2010) and Mole City (2013), and finally to Sub Pop for Breaking The Balls Of History (2023).

Members

  • Sam Coomes (Discogs ID: 260123) — Rocksichord, keyboards, bass, vocals; primary songwriter throughout; also recorded and mixed on early releases
  • Janet Weiss (Discogs ID: 320275) — drums, vocals; became co-producer from The Sword Of God (2001); fully self-produced Mole City (2013) with Coomes
  • Joanna Bolme (Discogs ID: 343877) — bass; credited as “Band” on American Gong (2010); her Discogs profile confirms a period of c. 2007–2011; also appeared as recording engineer on track 12 of Field Studies (1999) with her brother Larry Bolme

Connections

  • Sleater-Kinney — Weiss’s primary parallel band from the mid-1990s through at least 2006 and reunions; she drummed for Sleater-Kinney concurrently with Quasi
  • Wild Flag — Weiss co-founded Wild Flag (2011) during a gap in Quasi activity
  • Neil Gust — design assistance on The Sword Of God; Gust was the other member of Heatmiser alongside Elliott Smith
  • Elliott Smith — played bass on Field Studies (1999); his presence on the record reflects the tight Portland/Northwest indie scene
  • Domino — primary UK/European label from 1998 onward; also handled several US Kill Rock Stars records for EU distribution
  • Up Records — early US label; released R&B Transmogrification (1997) and Featuring “Birds” (1998)
  • Touch And Go — US label 2001–2006
  • Kill Rock Stars — US label 2010–2013; also issued the 1998 single “Unto Itself b/w Kiss the Snowman”
  • Sub Pop — Breaking The Balls Of History (2023)
