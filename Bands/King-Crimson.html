King Crimson

Discogs ID: 70828
Active: 1968–present (with hiatuses)
Origin: London, UK
Label: Island Records (early); E.G. Records Ltd. / Editions EG (catalog); various

British progressive rock band, one of the most influential in the genre. Founded and continuously steered by guitarist Robert Fripp. Numerous lineup changes over six decades. Classic lineup (Mark I) featured Greg Lake, Michael Giles, Ian McDonald, and Peter Sinfield. Later eras brought Bill Bruford, John Wetton, Adrian Belew, Tony Levin, and many others.

Back catalog reissued systematically on Editions EG under the EGKC prefix (EGKC 1–10), covering albums from In The Court Of The Crimson King (1969) through Red (1974) and compilations.

Key Members by Era

Mark I (1969–1972): Robert Fripp, Greg Lake, Michael Giles, Ian McDonald, Peter Sinfield Mark III (1973–1974): Fripp, John Wetton, Bill Bruford, David Cross, Jamie Muir Discipline / Mark IV (1981–1984): Fripp, Adrian Belew, Tony Levin, Bill Bruford Double Trio / Mark V (1994–1996): Fripp, Belew, Levin, Bruford, Trey Gunn, Pat Mastelotto Later era (2014–2021): Fripp, Belew, Levin, Mastelotto, Jakko Jakszyk, Mel Collins, Gavin Harrison

DGM Era (1992–present)

From 1992, all King Crimson studio and archival releases moved to Discipline Global Mobile, the label Fripp and Singleton founded. The DGM catalog begins with The Great Deceiver (1992, live 1973–74 material) and the VROOOM EP (DGM 0004, 1994), the first release of the Double Trio era. Major DGM studio albums: THRAK (1995), The ConstruKction Of Light (2000), The Power To Believe (2003). DGM also issued the extensive King Crimson Collectors’ Club (CLUB prefix) series of audience and board recordings, plus the 21st Century, 30th, 40th, and 50th Anniversary series of reissues.
