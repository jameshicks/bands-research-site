Talking Heads

Discogs ID: 22854
Origin: New York City, USA (formed Providence, Rhode Island)
Active: 1975–1991; no full reunion
Labels: Sire Records, EMI (Europe), Warner Bros. Records
Genres: New wave, post-punk, art punk, art pop, funk rock

American rock band formed 1975. Core lineup: David Byrne (guitar, vocals), Tina Weymouth (bass), Chris Frantz (drums), Jerry Harrison (keyboards, guitar). Disbanded 1991; inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame 2002. Eight studio albums on Sire, plus several live and compilation records.

Formation (1975–1977)

Byrne, Frantz, and Weymouth met at the Rhode Island School of Design (RISD). Relocated to New York City and became fixtures at CBGB alongside Television, Patti Smith, and the Ramones. Jerry Harrison joined in early 1977, having previously played in Jonathan Richman’s Modern Lovers. The four-piece lineup held for the band’s entire career.

The Eno Trilogy (1978–1980)

Three consecutive albums with Brian Eno as producer marked the band’s creative peak and their incorporation of African polyrhythm, tape manipulation, and studio-as-instrument methods. My Life In The Bush Of Ghosts (1981, Eno + Byrne, on Sire/EG) grew directly from the Remain In Light sessions.

Fear Of Music (1979, Sire)

Eno as effects/treatments and co-producer. Recorded at Atlantic Studios, Hit Factory, and Record Plant (New York) using the Record Plant Remote Truck. Engineer: Rod O’Brien. Mastered by Greg Calbi. Manager: Gary Kurfirst (throughout band’s career).

Remain In Light (1980, Sire)

Most heavily collaborative record. Eno sole producer. All music credited to the band collectively; Eno and Byrne credited for vocal arrangements. Recorded at Compass Point Studios, Nassau. Engineers: Dave Jerden (primary), Rhett Davies, John Potoker, Steven Stanley (additional). Mixed by Eno, Byrne, Jerden, Potoker. Mastered by Greg Calbi. Graphic design by M&Co.

Guest musicians: Adrian Belew (guitar), Jon Hassell (trumpet, horn arrangements on “The Overload” / B2), Nona Hendryx (voice), Robert Palmer (percussion), Jose Rossy (percussion).

Post-Eno Self-Production (1983–1988)

First self-produced LP without Eno. Recorded by Butch Jones; overdubs/mixing by Alex Sadkin. Mastered by Ted Jensen. Cover by Byrne. All music by the four members; lyrics by Byrne.

Stop Making Sense (1984, EMI / Sire)

Live soundtrack to Jonathan Demme’s concert film. Recorded at the Pantages Theatre, Hollywood. Mixed by Chris Frantz, Jerry Harrison, and Eric Thorngren. Mastered by Ted Jensen and Jack Skinner. Expanded live band: Steve Scales (percussion), Alex Weir (guitar), Bernie Worrell (keyboards), Lynn Mabry and Ednah Holt (backing vocals).

True Stories (1986, EMI)

Soundtrack to Byrne’s directorial film of the same name. Band performed on the record; also featured various Texas musicians.

Naked (1988, Sire / Fly)

Produced by Steve Lillywhite. Recorded in Paris. Heavy use of world music elements: African and Latin percussion, kora, horns. Johnny Marr (guitar on 4 tracks), Mory Kanté (kora), Wally Badarou (keyboards), Lenny Pickett (tenor saxophone, horn arrangements), Robin Eubanks (trombone). Final studio album before disbandment.

Disbandment and The Heads (1991–1996)

The band announced their disbandment in 1991. In 1996, the three remaining members (Harrison, Frantz, Weymouth) formed The Heads and released No Talking, Just Head without Byrne, using a rotating lineup of guest vocalists. The reunion was unsuccessful.

Connections

  • Jerry Harrison — keyboards/guitar; solo albums; joined from Modern Lovers
  • Brian Eno — producer trilogy 1978–1980; co-wrote Remain In Light; Eno/Byrne My Life In The Bush Of Ghosts (1981)

  • Tom Tom Club — Weymouth/Frantz side project; formed 1981 during Remain In Light downtime
  • Sire Records — primary label throughout

  • Bernie Worrell — guest keyboards, Stop Making Sense live band; Parliament-Funkadelic
  • Jon Hassell — trumpet, Remain In Light; fourth world minimalist composer
  • Nona Hendryx — voice, Remain In Light; ex-Labelle

  • M&Co. — graphic design, Remain In Light and Little Creatures
