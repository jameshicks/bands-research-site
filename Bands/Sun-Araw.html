Sun Araw

Discogs ID: 1065935
Real name: Cameron Stallones (Discogs ID: 1390955)
Also known as: Aristocrat P. Child, DJ Si Si Si Gracias
Base: Los Angeles, California
Active: 2008–present
Labels: Not Not Fun Records (2008–2010), Sun Ark Records (2011–present), Thrill Jockey (2011), RVNG Intl. (2012)

Solo project of Cameron Stallones (born Austin, Texas; based Long Beach, California). One of the defining artists of the late-2000s Pacific/LA experimental underground — five LPs on Not Not Fun Records in three years, then a pivot to his own label and a sequence of increasingly ambitious collaborative records culminating in the landmark reggae-experimental crossover Icon Give Thank (RVNG Intl., 2012) with M. Geddes Gengras and The Congos. Performs live under flexible configurations: Sun Araw Duo, Trio, Band VIII, Band IX, Band XV, etc.

Stallones studied film and briefly a “weird Classics program” at college; father and sister are history teachers. Worked full-time at a film archive until approximately November 2011, recording Sun Araw in parallel. Previously a guitarist in Magic Lantern — an NNF-affiliated collective — before splitting off solo material as The Phynx demos. Sun Ark studio is in his Long Beach home. The name: araw is Tagalog for “sun” or “day,” so Sun Araw = “sun sun” / intended by Stallones as a pun on “Sunday” (the Sabbath).

Era I — Not Not Fun (2008–2010)

Sun Araw emerged from Stallones’s solo demos originally intended for Magic Lantern, the NNF collective he was guitaristing for. Those demos became The Phynx. Sun Araw debuted with two 2008 LPs on NNF: The Phynx (NNF112) and Beach Head (NNF140). A third 2008 release, Boat Trip, appeared on Stunned Records. The pace accelerated in 2009: Heavy Deeds (NNF169) — limited vinyl LP, mastered by James Plotkin; runout etchings read “the message is…” (A) / “to keep moving” (B). On Patrol (NNF187) and Sun Ark (NNF183) followed in 2010, with Pete Swanson credited on Sun Ark. Also 2010: Livephreaxxx!!!! on Leaving Records; Major Grotto on Hustle Muscle (Australia).

All writing, performing, and artwork on NNF releases credited solely to Cameron Stallones.

Era II — Sun Ark Records / Thrill Jockey (2011–2012)

Stallones founded Sun Ark Records as his primary release vehicle from 2011 onward. Two major 2011 releases:

Night Gallery (Thrill Jockey, THRILL 266) — collaboration with Eternal Tapestry (Nick Bindeman’s Portland group). Double-length LP of extended psychedelic improvisation: four untitled movements ranging 6–14 minutes. Runout: “Thrill 266 A” / “Thrill 266 B Salt.”

Ancient Romans (Sun Ark SA012) — first Sun Ark LP. Eight-track CD. Mastered by Sonic Boom (Pete Kember); mixed by Matt Koshak and Cameron Stallones; partial engineering by Bobb Bruno on closing track “Impluvium.” Titles reference classical antiquity: Lucretius, Crown Shell, Crete, Lute And Lyre, At Delphi, Fit For Caesar, Trireme, Impluvium.

Era III — Icon Give Thank / RVNG / Duppy Gun (2012)

The pivotal year. Three major releases:

Icon Give Thank (RVNG Intl., FRKWYS09LTD, 2012) — Sun Araw & M. Geddes Gengras Meet The Congos. Limited LP + DVD. Stallones and Gengras traveled to Jamaica in early 2011 to record with the Congos; multiple Jamaica sessions followed. The Congos — Jamaican roots reggae vocal group formed late 1970s (Cedric Myton, Congo Ashanti Roy / Roydel Johnson, Watty Burnett; originally recorded at Lee “Scratch” Perry’s Black Ark Studios) — provide vocals over Stallones and Gengras’s dub-inflected electronic production. Styles: Ambient, Dub, Experimental. Part of RVNG Intl.’s FRKWYS series. Reached #7 on Billboard Top World Albums chart. Connected to the ongoing Duppy Gun project (Stallones, Gengras, Alex Gray, Jamaican collaborators).

The Inner Treaty (Sun Ark SA022, 2012) — LP. Mastered by Sonic Boom; written/performed/produced by Cameron Stallones. Notably: B-side track “The Summum” credits Pharoah Sanders as Written-By — a Coltrane-era free jazz composition covered in a dub/psychedelic context.

Sun Ark Prayer Tapes, Vol. 1 & 2 (Sun Ark Records, 2012) — cassette releases; later compiled and reissued by Brierfield Flood Press (Australia, 2022).

Scene Context

Sun Araw sits at the intersection of several overlapping scenes:

  • Not Not Fun Records core — 2008–2010 NNF cohort: Inca Ore, Yellow Swans, Pocahaunted, Peaking Lights
  • Portland experimental — Night Gallery with Eternal Tapestry (Nick Bindeman et al.)
  • Dub / reggae crossover — Icon Give Thank with The Congos; ongoing Duppy Gun project with M. Geddes Gengras
  • Kosmische / psych lineage — Sonic Boom (Spacemen 3) as mastering engineer signals aesthetic alignment; Laraaji collaboration
  • Sun Ark Records — Stallones’s own label handles most post-2011 output, keeping production and distribution tightly controlled
