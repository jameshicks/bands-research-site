City Center

Discogs Artist ID: 1196329 (listed as “City Center (3)” on Discogs) Origin: New York City, US Active: 2008–2015 (approx.) Genres: Indie, Experimental, Lo-fi, Drone, Folk Labels: Life Like, Type Records, M’lady’s Records, K Records

Overview

City Center was a New York-based duo of Fred Thomas and Ryan Howard, making hazy, lo-fi experimental music that blended drone, folk, and indie pop textures. The project launched in 2008 with a large-run CDR on the short-lived Invisible Friend imprint before graduating to cassette and vinyl releases on Life Like, Type, M’lady’s Records, and ultimately K Records (Olympia). Their blog and Myspace presence (citycenternyc.blogspot.com) reflected the DIY infrastructure of the late-2000s New York underground. The discography is compact — five distinct releases over roughly three years — but the band bridged multiple micro-scenes: the New York/Brooklyn lo-fi world, the Pacific Northwest tape-label circuit, and the UK experimental fringe (via Type Records).

Fred Thomas is one of the most prolific figures in the Detroit/Ann Arbor indie underground, with memberships in well over two dozen bands including Saturday Looks Good To Me, His Name Is Alive, Tyvek, and numerous others. Ryan Howard also performs under the aliases Jackpot (23) and Patient To Patient, and was also a member of Drunken Barn Dance.

Era Breakdown

2008 — Debut

  • January 2008 (CDR, Invisible Friend, 2008) — debut release, US only. A self-produced CDR in a reportedly large edition. No additional personnel credited on Discogs.

2009 — Prolific Year

City Center released four items in a single year across four different labels, establishing their reach across the DIY tape/vinyl underground.

  • Because (7”, PRTY NGG!, 2009) — performers: Fred Thomas, Ryan Howard; cover art by Nick Jaroch. Two pressings.
  • City Center (LP, Type Records, 2009, UK) — the band’s most widely distributed record, on the respected UK electronic/experimental imprint. With guest contributions from cellist Colette Alexander, flautist Leah Paul, and vibraphonist Brian Groux. Three pressings including a UK edition.
  • Cops Don’t Care (7”, M’lady’s Records, 2009) — cover art by Hisham Bharoocha (also of Soft Circle / Black Dice). Two pressings.
  • Grouper / City Center (split, self-released, 2009) — co-released with Grouper (Liz Harris), self-released by both parties.

2011 — Final Studio Record

  • Redeemer (LP, K Records, 2011) — recorded by Chris Koltay. Released on the storied Olympia DIY institution K Records. Two pressings. This appears to be the final official City Center release on Discogs.
