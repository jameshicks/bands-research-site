Muscle Drum (2)

US experimental / synth-pop project. Discogs artist ID: 2619256. Active at minimum 2009–2011. No real name, aliases, or members listed on Discogs.

Overview

Muscle Drum (2) released two records in the 2009–2011 period: a CDr on Disaro that landed near the beginning of that label’s CDr-era run, and a limited vinyl LP on the small Chicago-area imprint Break Up. The project sits at the intersection of synth-pop, psychedelic rock, experimental electronics, and lo-fi punk — genre tags that shift between the two releases, suggesting the project ranged stylistically rather than occupying a fixed sound.

Discogs carries no profile text, no real name, and no member information for this artist. What the DB does show is a consistent relationship with Weasel Walter, who mastered both releases, and the presence of Robert Spector as a credited performer on Fog Hag.

The CDr The Aforementioned (CDR018) is the earliest CDr-prefixed entry in the Disaro catalog. The CDr prefix at Disaro appears to mark a second phase of the label’s output beginning around 2009, when witch-house was cohering as a recognizable internet scene; Muscle Drum (2)‘s placement at CDR018 puts the project at the leading edge of that phase.
