Animal Magic (2)

UK post-punk / dance band, formed Bristol area, active 1982. Discogs artist ID: 376294

Overview

Animal Magic were a Bristol-based post-punk / dance group whose entire known original output consists of two 12”/7” singles released in 1982 on Recreational Records, the label arm of Revolver Records in Bristol. Their sound bridges punk energy with funk and disco rhythms — the genres logged on their 12” (“Disco”, “Punk”) and 7” (“New Wave”, “Post-Punk”) encapsulate the range. Their 12” single “Get It Right” was later selected for disc 3 of the 2021 Cherry Red box set Shake The Foundations (Militant Funk & The Post-Punk Dancefloor 1978-1984), which is how the band first came to wider attention. A 2023 vinyl reissue collected both singles as the Get It Right / Standard Man EP Collection on Lantern Rec. (cat. LANR032).

The band’s Discogs page lists four members: Rob Boswell, Howard Purse, Mark Taylor (15), and James Hill (7). Howard Purse is the most traceable member, appearing in several other Bristol-area projects: Fishfood, Cake (11), and The Family (9).

Recreational Records was launched in 1981 by Revolver Records (Bristol) — a record shop and member of the Cartel independent distribution group. Animal Magic’s “Standard Man” / “Trash That Blad” (SPORT 8) was the label’s final release in 1982. Engineer Steve Street, a central figure in the Bristol independent music scene, worked on “Standard Man”.

Label Context — Recreational Records

Recreational Records (Discogs label ID: 49694) was the in-house label of Revolver Records, Bristol — a record shop and part of the Cartel independent distribution network. Active 1981–1982, 9 releases total. Key artists: Talisman (3) (reggae), Scream And Dance, Ivory Coasters, Mouth, Electric Guitars, The X-Certs. Animal Magic’s two singles were the label’s final releases before it ceased. The label’s contact address was Revolver Records, 1 Berkeley Crescent, The Triangle, Bristol BS8 1HA.
