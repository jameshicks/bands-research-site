The Last Words

Discogs ID: 818409 Name variations: Last Words

Australian punk/new wave band formed c. 1977–1978, with Jeffrey Wegener on drums; other members include Malcolm Baxter, Ricky Leigh Kendall, John Gunn (2), and Andy Groome.

Released Animal World (Wizard Records/Remand Records, 1978) and a self-titled LP (Armageddon Records, 1980), as well as Top Secret (Armageddon Records, 1980). Archival reissues appeared on Papagájův Hlasatel Records in 2021. Wegener’s membership here predates Laughing Clowns and runs concurrent with his time in Young Charlatans.
