Fiction Plane

Rock band from England, formed c. 2000 (originally as Santa’s Boyfriend). Discogs artist ID: 880221. Led by vocalist/bassist Joe Sumner (son of Sting), with guitarist Seton Daunt and drummer Pete Wilhoit as the core trio. Bassist/multi-instrumentalist Dan Brown (7) was also a member and appears on the debut full-length. Active from the early 2000s through at least 2015 across four studio albums.

Overview

Fiction Plane emerged from the UK indie/rock scene of the early 2000s, releasing their debut album Everything Will Never Be OK in 2002–2003 on MCA Records, produced by David Kahne. They followed it with the UK-only Bitter Forces And Lame Race Horses (2005, Everybody’s Records), then broke through in the US market with Left Side Of The Brain (2007, Bieler Bros. Records), produced by Paul Corkett. Their third album Paradiso (2009, XIII Bis Records) was mixed by Ash Howes. A fourth album, Sparks (2010, Roadrunner Records), again produced by Paul Corkett, and a fifth, Mondo Lumina (2015, Verycords), round out their catalog. The band were sometimes credited as “FictionPlane” or “Fictionplane” on releases.
