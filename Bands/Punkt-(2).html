Punkt (2)

Norwegian live-remix project based in Kristiansand. Discogs artist ID: 625413

Members: Arve Henriksen, Erik Honoré, Jan Bang

Bandcamp: https://punktnorway.bandcamp.com/

Biography

Punkt is a Kristiansand-based music festival and recording project built around a single conceptual idea: live remixing. During festival performances, invited musicians play a set on stage while Jan Bang and Erik Honoré — seated at samplers and processing rigs in real time — capture fragments of that live sound and immediately reconstruct them into a simultaneous remix concert happening alongside the original. The audience hears both layers at once: the source performance and its instantaneous shadow.

The project is registered on Discogs under the trio name Punkt (2), with Arve Henriksen (trumpet) as the third permanent member alongside Bang and Honoré. In practice the group name functions as a flexible umbrella: some releases credit the trio directly, others credit Punkt (2) alongside specific featured performers, and later Punkt Editions releases appear under individual member names or collaboration pairings. The festival itself — the Punkt Festival, held annually in Kristiansand — is a broader curatorial entity that reaches well beyond the trio’s recorded output.

All three members are established figures in the Norwegian post-jazz and experimental music community. Henriksen is also a member of Supersilent and has led solo projects on Rune Grammofon and other imprints. Honoré has recorded under his own name and with groups including Velvet Belly, Woodlands, and Dark Star Safari. Bang produces and remixes across the ECM-adjacent Norwegian scene; his solo discography and collaborative output with Eivind Aarset form a substantial thread running through the Punkt Editions catalog.

Punkt — Group vs. Festival Distinction

On Discogs, “Punkt (2)” designates the trio of Henriksen, Honoré, and Bang as a recording and performing entity. The wider Punkt Festival is a separate annual curatorial event in Kristiansand that invites a rotating cast of international and Norwegian artists; those artists perform and are simultaneously live-remixed by Bang and Honoré. The festival predates any fixed trio concept and is the umbrella under which the group name was coined.

The label arm — Punkt Editions — is the most recent formal channel and explicitly describes itself as “an archive of two decades of sonic experimentation” as well as a forward-looking label, issuing vault recordings, new studio work, and solo releases by members. It operates as a sublabel of Bugge Wesseltoft’s Jazzland Recordings.

The earlier Punkt Recordings label (ID 72660) is a minimal entity with only two entries in the database: the two pressings of Crime Scenes (Norwegian 2006, German 2007).

Scene Context

Punkt sits at the intersection of Norwegian post-jazz (the ECM/Jazzland tradition of Molvær, Aarset, Endresen) and European electronic improvisation. The live-remix concept has antecedents in dub and in the real-time processing practices of artists like Brian Eno, but Punkt applies it within a jazz-festival frame with a rotating cast drawn from the Nordic experimental scene. Arve Henriksen’s membership in Supersilent and his ties to the Christian Wallumrød Ensemble locate the project firmly within the post-ECM Rune Grammofon axis. The Punkt Editions label, launched formally in the early 2020s, has expanded outward — commissioning work from composers like Dai Fujikura and featuring Ensemble Modern — suggesting an ambition beyond purely Norwegian-Scandinavian network.
