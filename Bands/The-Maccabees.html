Overview

The Maccabees are a London indie rock band formed in 2004, comprising Felix White, Hugo White, Orlando Weeks, Robert Dylan Thomas, and Rupert Jarvis — though Thomas departed the drummer’s role in 2008. Their catalogue spans two active decades: 12 releases in the 2000s and 10 in the 2010s, with Fiction Records and Polydor as their main homes throughout. The band announced a breakup in August 2016 and played farewell shows in Manchester and London through mid-2017, before reforming in 2025 for All Points East and continuing live work into 2026. Their most sustained production partnership was with Cenzo Townshend, who shares credits on 28 releases, with Markus Dravs and Bob Ludwig also recurring across the catalogue.
