Overview

The Name were a British power pop band rooted in the Peterborough music scene, associated with the mod revival of the late 1970s. They released one single during their active years — “Forget Art Let’s Dance” b/w “Misfits” (1980, Dindisc DIN 14) — produced by two different figures: Side A by Mike Howlett (Gang of Four, Martha and the Muffins producer), Side B by Huw Gower of The Records. A posthumous LP, What’s In A Name?, appeared on Detour Records in 2002, collecting studio recordings and live material.

The band shares its Peterborough punk network with The Now (4) and Destructors: drummer Joe MacColl and guitarist Nigel Davis passed through all three bands. Bassist David Colton and guitarist Ian Graham (2) both went on to Gobblinz.

The band’s website was documented at http://historyofthename.tripod.com.

Scene Connections

The Name sit at the intersection of Peterborough’s mod-revival and punk scenes. Their Discogs member roster overlaps substantially with The Now (4) (1977 Peterborough punk band) and Destructors (longer-running Peterborough punk act active from 1982).

Labels:

  • Dindisc — the single’s label; Virgin subsidiary known for new wave signings (OMD, Martha and the Muffins). Cat. DIN 14 places The Name in Dindisc’s 1980 catalog.
  • Detour Records (3) — reissue/compilation label that compiled the 2002 LP. Cat. DRLP033.

Runout matrix text: The 2002 LP’s runout contains two etched messages: “Whatever happened to the now?” (Side A) and “Where are the Gobblinz?” (Side B). These shout-outs place The Name in explicit dialogue with The Now (4) and Gobblinz as part of a shared Peterborough scene memory.

Production connections:

  • Mike Howlett produced the A-side of the 1980 single — a notable connection given his concurrent work with Dindisc roster acts (Martha and the Muffins) and shortly after with Gang of Four.
  • Huw Gower produced the B-side. Gower was the guitarist of The Records, a British power pop band on Virgin; his involvement reinforces the band’s power pop credentials and Virgin/Dindisc network ties.

  • The What’s In A Name? 2002 LP has no producer or engineer credits on Discogs.

  • Whether “Forget Art Let’s Dance” and “Fuck Art Let’s Dance” (Side A studio, 2002) are the same recording is unclear — the 2002 studio version may predate the 1980 single or be a different recording.
