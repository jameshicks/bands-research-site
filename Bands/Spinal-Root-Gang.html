Spinal Root Gang

Discogs ID: None (no Discogs presence confirmed) Active: c. late 1970s–1982 (two phases; see below) Based: Stockbridge, Massachusetts (origins); New York City (later phase) Genre: No Wave, Post-Punk, Art Rock Labels: None confirmed (demo recordings only)

Overview

Spinal Root Gang was a short-lived band founded by twin brothers Dan and Josh Braun and their father, Saul Braun. The group existed in two distinct phases: an original formation from their high-school years in Stockbridge, Massachusetts, and a later New York City incarnation in 1982 that briefly featured Madonna (then pre-fame) on lead vocals.

The band left no known commercially released recordings. Its primary historical significance is as a node in the Braun twins’ early musical trajectory — which also included Circus Mort (with Michael Gira), T-Venus (with Pat Irwin and Julia Heyward), and The Del-Byzanteens (with Jim Jarmusch) — and as a footnote in Madonna’s pre-pop career. One demo, “We Live in a House,” is documented for the 1982 phase and remains unreleased.

The Braun twins later formed Deep Six (1982) and went on to co-found Submarine Entertainment (2001), a film production and distribution company responsible for several Academy Award-winning documentaries.

History

Phase 1: Stockbridge Origins (c. late 1970s)

Dan and Josh Braun grew up in Stockbridge, Massachusetts, and began playing together at around age 14. Their earliest bands — “Platinum Hardcore” and “Hack Ptooey” — were formed during high school. The first project they considered serious was Spinal Root Gang, which included their father Saul Braun (a freelance writer who played saxophone), as well as Trey Sabatelli, Karen Fish, and Janis Krasnow. No recordings from this phase are documented.

Phase 2: New York City (1982)

After relocating to New York City in 1979, Dan and Josh became embedded in the downtown no wave/post-punk scene. By 1982 they were playing with Michael Gira in Circus Mort and had joined The Del-Byzanteens with Jim Jarmusch. Around this same period, Josh Braun encountered Madonna while she was rehearsing with a group called Emmy at the Music Building in New York. Separately, Saul Braun (the twins’ father) had begun renting a room from Madonna on the Upper East Side.

Saul Braun expressed interest in reviving Spinal Root Gang with Madonna as vocalist. Madonna agreed, on the condition that her friend and roommate Janis Galloway could also participate. The group convened to write and record. The one documented product of this phase is “We Live in a House,” co-written by Josh Braun, Janis Galloway, and Madonna in 1982 (US copyright registration PAu-1-843-482). A demo was recorded with Saul Braun on saxophone and Madonna as the sole vocalist — Galloway was not present for the recording session. The demo has never been commercially released.

Thurston Moore of Sonic Youth later noted this period in a 2018 Guardian interview, describing the band as featuring Madonna “with these two twins, Dan and Josh Braun, who were the first members of Swans” — emphasizing the interconnected history of Madonna and the New York no wave scene.

SoundCloud Presence

A SoundCloud account under the name “Spinal Root Gang” (username: user-87083062, real name listed as “Daniel B,” location: NYC) uploaded one track, “Shake Your Tail” (genre tag: “electro boogie robot funk junk”), posted February 4, 2017. As of 2020, the account had 2 followers and 82 plays on that track. It is unclear whether this account is operated by Dan Braun and represents a continuation of the project, or is an unrelated use of the name.
