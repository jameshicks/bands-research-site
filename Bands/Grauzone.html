Grauzone

Swiss post-punk / new wave band formed in Basel, ~1980. Discogs artist ID: 7549.

Overview

Grauzone were one of the central acts in the early-1980s Swiss underground scene centred on Off Course Records. They released material both on Off Course Records and EMI Electrola / Welt-Rekord, making them the most commercially connected Swiss wave band of the period.

Best known for “Eisbær” (1981), a hypnotic synth-pop track that has been reissued and licensed widely. Also released “Film 2” (1981), which became a cult item in European minimal wave circles.

The group’s only full-length compilation/archive release was Grauzone 1980–1982 (2010, mital-U). A 2019 reissue of “Eisbær” appeared on We Release Whatever The Fuck We Want Records.
