They Might Be Giants

Brooklyn-based alternative/indie pop duo. Discogs artist ID: 159350.

Formed by John Flansburgh and John Linnell in 1982 in Brooklyn, New York. Most prolific artist on Restless Records with 82 releases logged (1987–2003). Self-titled debut (1987, cat 7 72603-2) produced by Bill Krauss. Known for melodic, accordion-driven, eclectic songwriting.
