Alabama 3

UK collective formed in Brixton, London, 1995 (some early Discogs pressings note 1991 for initial activity). Discogs artist ID: 239530. Also known as A3 in the US and Canada, following legal action by US country act Alabama.

Overview

Alabama 3 are a Brixton-based collective founded by Rob Spragg (stage name Larry Love) and Jake Black (stage name Rev. D. Wayne Love). Their sound blends country, blues, gospel, acid house, and spoken word — characterised as the “mischievous miscegenation of Hank Williams, gospel and acid house.” The band began as an underground acid house project and expanded through the 1990s into a sprawling multi-member collective, centred on Brixton but drawing personnel from the UK punk and reggae scenes.

Their 1997 single “Woke Up This Morning” became internationally known as the title sequence theme for HBO’s The Sopranos (1999–2007). The band recorded and released under the name Alabama 3 in the UK and Europe; US releases used the name A3 (name variations on Discogs: A-3, Alabama, Alabama3, a3). They are sometimes listed as “The Ministers At Work” — a formal alias (Discogs ID: 139187) used on certain production credits and early releases.

John Jennings (“Segs”) connection

Bass player and frequent producer John Jennings — known as “Segs,” alias Frank Zappatista on Alabama 3 credits — is a member of The Ruts and Ruts DC, one of UK punk’s most significant rhythm sections. His presence gives Alabama 3 a direct link to the British punk/reggae tradition; he produced and played on La Peste (2000) and several others alongside engineer/producer Steve Dub.

”Woke Up This Morning” and The Sopranos

The track “Woke Up This Morning” (1997, master ID: 112455) was released as a single on Elemental Records in the UK, with US release via Geffen Records. The original UK pressing (release ID: 66604) is credited to Alabama 3, mastered by Maxwell Anandappa. Subsequent pressings appeared through Geffen (US, 1997), Columbia (UK, 2000), and Sony Music Studios (US, 2000). The US single versions credit both “Alabama 3 / The Street Angels Choir 2000” as primary artists. The track interpolates or is indebted to Howlin’ Wolf (Chester Burnett, Discogs ID: 682348) — Burnett appears as a co-writer credit across multiple “Woke Up This Morning” release variants.

The song was adopted by HBO as the title theme for The Sopranos from 1999, bringing the band’s name to a mass US audience and leading to extensive reissue and remix activity. The US market releases use the A3 name due to the pre-existing Alabama trademark.
