Karkhana

Aug 01, 20261 min read

Karkhana

Discogs ID: 4230773
Active: 2015–2021 (documented)
Labels: Unrock, Karlrecords

International experimental improvisation collective combining players from Beirut, Cairo, Istanbul, and Chicago. Described as blending free jazz and psychedelic music with shaabi, tarab, and other Arabic and Turkish musical traditions.
