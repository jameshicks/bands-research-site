Fuck Buttons

UK noise/electronic duo formed in Bristol c. 2004. Members: Andrew Hung and Benjamin John Power (also known as Blanck Mass). Discogs artist ID: 1083695. Self-described genre: “Rainbow Rock.”

Overview

Fuck Buttons formed in Bristol around 2004 and released a self-produced CDr demo in 2006 before signing to ATP Recordings. Their debut album Street Horrrsing (2008) was recorded and mixed by John Cummings (Mogwai) and Tim Cedar (Part Chimp), establishing the band’s dense, looping noise-electronic sound.

The second album Tarot Sport (2009) marked a significant shift: Andrew Weatherall came on board as producer, adding rhythmic propulsion and cleaner structures to the band’s existing drone/noise palette. The album gained a wider profile and the track “Olympians” was later used by Danny Boyle in the opening ceremony of the 2012 London Olympics — an event not documented in the Discogs DB.

The third album Slow Focus (2013) was self-produced, with Nina Walsh and Matt Rowlands credited for initial tracking and Jimmy Robertson as mix engineer. After Slow Focus the band went on indefinite hiatus; no further releases appear in the Discogs DB as of the data dump.

Benjamin John Power continued as Blanck Mass (solo electronic project on Sacred Bones) and also joined Editors as a member. Andrew Hung launched a solo career releasing on Lex Records, and produced artists including Beth Ditto and scored the film The Greasy Strangler (2016).
