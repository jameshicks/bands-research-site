The Fall

Discogs ID: 2228
Origin: Salford / Greater Manchester, England
Active: 1976–2018
Name variations: Fall, The Fall Group, The Fall., The Mighty Fall

Post-punk band formed in Manchester in 1976 by vocalist and sole constant member Mark E. Smith, who died on 24 January 2018. Over four decades The Fall released approximately 34 studio albums, 50+ singles, and well over 50 live and compilation albums. They recorded 24 sessions for John Peel’s BBC Radio 1 show between June 1978 and August 2004 — an unmatched record. The band cycled through well over 30 different lineups; Smith was the only member present for the entire run. Musically rooted in punk, post-punk, krautrock, and rockabilly, the band is defined by Smith’s declamatory spoken-word delivery, deliberately abrasive production choices, and repetitive motorik rhythms.

The band’s career spans roughly five label eras: Step-Forward Records (1978–1981), Rough Trade and Kamera Records (1980–1983), Beggars Banquet (1984–1989), Cog Sinister / Fontana / Phonogram (1990–1996), and a long self-managed/small-label phase from Jet Records through Eagle Records, Action Records, Slogan Records, and finally Cherry Red (2011–2017).

Connections
