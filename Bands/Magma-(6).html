Magma (6)

Discogs ID: 179749 | Founded: 1969, Paris, France | Genre: Progressive Rock / Zeuhl / Jazz-Rock

French progressive rock and fusion band founded in 1969 by drummer Christian Vander. Originators of the “zeuhl” subgenre — a term coined by fans for Magma’s distinctive blend of Coltrane-influenced jazz, heavy rock, and orchestral writing, performed largely in the invented Kobaïan language. Operated in two major phases: 1969–1983 (classic era, multiple lineup shifts) and 1996–present (reunion and new recordings). Several key members later appeared in the Heldon orbit: Patrick Gauthier (keyboards), Klaus Blasquiz (vocals), Jannick Top (bass — Heldon V), and Bernard Paganotti (bass — 1982 live Heldon).

Origins and the Kobaïan Universe

Magma was founded in the summer of 1969 by Christian Vander, who conceived of the band as a vehicle for an elaborate cosmic mythology: the story of inhabitants fleeing a dying Earth to the planet Kobaïa. Vander invented the Kobaïan language for the band’s lyrics — an artificial tongue with its own phonology, deployed across multiple albums in interconnected narrative arcs (the Theusz Hamtaahk trilogy, the Ẁurdah Ïtah / MDK / Köhntarkösz sequence, and the later Ëmëhntëhtt-Ré trilogy). The Discogs profile notes the strong jazz-rock and fusion foundation, with Vander citing John Coltrane as a major influence.

Vander himself used multiple Kobaïan aliases in credits — appearing as “Theïus Bïngöh,” “Zébehn Straïn Dë Geustaah,” and “Dërstühn” on various releases; on Attahk (1978) some player credits are listed entirely under Kobaïan names (Stündëhr, Wurd Gorgo, Kahal Negümüraaht, Dë Wëlëss) making lineup reconstruction from that LP alone difficult.

Phase One: 1969–1983

The Magma Live Bass Transition

Jannick Top played on Mekanïk Destruktïw Kommandöh (1973) and Köhntarkösz (1974) — the two most celebrated Phase One albums — then departed. Bernard Paganotti replaced him in the band by the time of Magma Live (1975). Both Top and Paganotti later appeared in Heldon: Top on Heldon V: Un Rêve Sans Conséquence Spéciale (1976), Paganotti in the 1982 live Heldon configuration.

The Zëss (2019) album is an outlier in the Phase Two catalog: recorded with the City of Prague Philharmonic Orchestra, conducted by Adam Klemens, orchestrated by Rémi Dumoulin. The drum chair was taken by Morgan Ågren rather than Vander himself.

Key Members — Profiles

Christian Vander (Discogs ID: 252641)

Drummer, vocalist, composer, bandleader. Born in France; cited Coltrane as primary influence. Uses multiple Kobaïan aliases in credits: Theïus Bïngöh, Zébehn Straïn Dë Geustaah, Dërstühn. Outside Magma: Christian Vander Quartet, Christian Vander Trio, Offering (post-Magma project, see below), Univeria Zekt, Cruciferius, Vandertop (duo with Jannick Top). Owns/operates Seventh Records.

Stella Vander (Discogs ID: 252644)

Real name Stella Zelcer. Vocalist, percussion. Joined by MDK (1973); Kobaïan alias Tauhd Zaïa. Continued through Phase Two; credited as producer on Zëss (2019) and Kãrtëhl (2022) under her married name Stella Vander Linon. Also a member of Offering.

Klaus Blasquiz (Discogs ID: 252640)

Lead vocalist. Kobaïan alias Klötsz Zaspïaahk / N’Dongo Lumba. Present from debut through Phase One. Also a member of Univeria Zekt (early Magma spin-off), Vandertop (with Christian and Jannick Top), and Odeurs. His Heldon appearance: 1982 live recording.

Jannick Top (Discogs ID: 252645)

Real name Jannick Yves Top. Electric bass. Aliases include Samy Wathson and Wahrgenuhr Reugehlem Esteh. Played on MDK (1973) and Köhntarkösz (1974) — the peak of Phase One’s critical standing. Left Magma and founded Weidorje (with Paganotti and Gauthier). Also played with Space (French synth group), Rosebud, European Music Authority, and Vandertop (duo with Christian Vander). Bass credit on Heldon V (Un Rêve Sans Conséquence Spéciale, 1976).

Bernard Paganotti (Discogs ID: 252648)

Bass. Kobaïan alias Ẁurd Λëmgalaï. Replaced Jannick Top in Magma by Magma Live (1975). Also a founding member of Weidorje (with Top and Gauthier). Heldon member (1982 live configuration). Post-Magma session work was extraordinarily prolific across French variety/pop (Johnny Hallyday, Francis Cabrel, Mylène Farmer, Gipsy Kings, etc.) into the 2000s. Also appeared on Heldon’s Rhizosphere / Live, Paris 1982 reissue (Cuneiform, 1994).

Patrick Gauthier (Discogs ID: 252639)

Keyboards. Member of both Magma and Weidorje. Also a member of Heldon, Lockwood, Schizo (4), and Angle (3). One of the primary figures connecting the Magma orbit to the wider French experimental rock scene.

Laurent Thibault (Discogs ID: 334606)

Bass and producer on the debut LP Magma (1970). Also performed as bass player in Phase One, and later acted as engineer/producer on Attahk (1978). Kobaïan alias Naü Ektïlah. Also a member of Zorgones and The Brain (7).

Giorgio Gomelsky (Discogs ID: 232991)

Producer on Mekanïk Destruktïw Kommandöh (1973), Köhntarkösz (1974), and Üdü Ẁüdü (1976). Gomelsky was the impresario behind the UK Utopia label (Utopia (2), parent: The Gomelsky Eggers Music & Information Company Ltd.) which released Magma Live (1975). Russian-born Swiss impresario also associated with the Rolling Stones’ early management and the Yardbirds.

Related Projects

Weidorje

Post-Magma band founded by Jannick Top circa 1977–78. Members: Top, Bernard Paganotti, Patrick Gauthier, Alain Guillard, Yvon Guillard, Jean-Philippe Goude, Kirt Rust, Michel Ettori. Released one self-titled LP (1978, Cobra label). Functioned as the direct zeuhl offshoot from Magma’s rhythm section, retaining the Kobaïan-influenced heaviness.

Offering (Discogs ID: 3151344)

Christian Vander and Stella Vander’s project active in the mid-1980s through the first reunion phase. “Magma then regrouped under the name Offering making a very different music” (Discogs profile). Members include several Phase Two Magma players: Emmanuel Borghi, Isabelle Feuillebois, Hervé Aknin, Simon Goubert.

Univeria Zekt (Discogs ID: 512636)

Early spin-off/side project. Members overlap almost entirely with the early Magma lineup: Christian Vander, Claude Engel, Francis Moze, François Cahen, Klaus Blasquiz, Teddy Lasry, Yochk’O Seffer.

Vandertop (Discogs ID: 2039730)

Duo of Christian Vander and Jannick Top — also listed as a Magma alias in Discogs.
