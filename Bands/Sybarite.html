History

Sybarite is the solo recording project of Xian Hawkins (born Christian Hawkins), a New York-based multi-instrumentalist. Before launching the project, Hawkins spent much of the mid-1990s touring and recording with Silver Apples, the pioneering late-1960s electronic duo that was reactivated in 1994 with Hawkins joining as a third member alongside founding members Simeon Coxe and Dan Taylor. His contributions to Silver Apples spanned the band’s mid-’90s catalogue on Whirlybird Records, including keyboard work on Beacon (1997) and Decatur (1998), as well as synthesizer and electronics work on the Fractal Flow / Lovefingers single (1996, Enraptured).

Hawkins began writing and recording under the Sybarite name in the late 1990s. The project’s sound sits between downtempo electronic and modern chamber ensemble — melodic, sophisticated, and rhythmically understated. The debut album Meusic (1999, Emanate Records) established the project, credited to Hawkins as performer of all instruments. A string of releases followed in 2001–2002 across multiple labels including Static Caravan (UK), Awkward Silence Recordings (UK), and Temporary Residence Limited (US).

The 2001 EP + ep (Travels In Constants Volume Fifteen) was Sybarite’s entry in Travels In Constants, the ongoing CD singles series run by TRL, which paired each volume with a different artist under the shared catalog number TRR 13. The EP was recorded and mixed by Jacques Cohen, with cover photography by Denise Fasanello. Track 6.2 — “Engaged” — appears in a remix by Lilienthal (Arrow Kleeman), the Belgian-based electronic project that also collaborated with Sybarite on the 2002 split/collaboration release Make Shift Carousel.

Sybarite’s most high-profile releases came in 2002, when 4AD put out two records: Nonument (a full-length, produced and recorded by Hawkins, with additional production from Andrew Ingalls on several tracks) and Scene Of The Crime. Both were designed by Vaughan Oliver / V23 with photography by Tom Skipp and, in the case of Scene Of The Crime, Brooke Williams. This 4AD period represents the project’s highest commercial visibility.

After Cut Out Shape (2006, TRL), Sybarite went quiet before returning with two releases in 2016: Loose Hold and Waver The Absolute, both on TRL, the latter produced and recorded by Hawkins and mastered by Roger Seibel.

Alongside Sybarite, Hawkins co-founded Bell Horses with singer Jenny Owen Youngs.
