The Chameleons

Post-punk band. Middleton (6 miles north of Manchester), UK. Discogs ID: 137234. Formed 1981.

Members: Mark Burgess (vocals), Reg Smithies (guitar), Dave Fielding (guitar), John Lever (drums; d. 2017). Burgess had been in The Clichés; Smithies and Fielding were in Years (3). Recruited Lever from The Politicians (6).

One early single on CBS (“In Shreds”, 1982; no. 42 in John Peel’s Festive Fifty) before CBS dropped them. Signed to Statik Records. Key Statik releases: Script Of The Bridge LP (1983, catno STAT LP 17, 23 pressings; recorded in Rochdale over six weeks); singles “A Person Isn’t Safe Anywhere These Days” and “As High As You Can Go” (1983); What Does Anything Mean? Basically LP (1985). Statik was distributed by Virgin, making The Chameleons ineligible for the UK independent charts — a significant grievance. Left Statik after the second album and signed to Geffen; released Strange Times LP before fragmenting after the sudden death of manager Tony Fletcher in 1987. Reformed 2000; continue as of 2024 with Burgess and Smithies; Lever passed away in 2017.

Production

  • Statik Records era (1983-1985): producer not confirmed from Discogs for Script Of The Bridge; David M. Allen produced singles When Lightning Strikes (1983), A Million Miles Away / Shanty (1984), Distant Fires (1984)
  • Geffen era: Strange Times (1986) produced by David M. Allen; engineer Mark Saunders
  • Retrospective: Return Of The Roughnecks – The Best Of The Chameleons (1997, Dead Dead Good) produced by David M. Allen

Post-Reformation Discography (2000 onwards)

Reformed 2000 with Burgess, Smithies, Fielding, and Lever. Lever passed away in 2017.
