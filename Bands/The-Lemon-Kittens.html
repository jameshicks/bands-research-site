The Lemon Kittens

Discogs artist name: Lemon Kittens
Active: 1978–1982
Origin: Reading, England

Experimental post-punk duo (eventually) of Karl Blake and Danielle Dax, active 1978–1982. Formed by Blake and Gary Thatcher on 5 April 1978; underwent numerous lineup changes before stabilising as a Blake/Dax duo. Described by critic Simon Reynolds as “aggressively absurdist.” Performed approximately 22 concerts total. [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Blake, as of 2026-06-29]

Dax joined c.1979 after Blake placed an advertisement in Industrial News (September 1979); she initially contributed artwork before becoming a core performer (vocals, keyboards, flute, saxophone, bass, co-producer). [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danielle_Dax, as of 2026-06-29]

United Dairies connection

Signed by John Fothergill (NWW co-founder and United Dairies co-operator) for their debut LP We Buy A Hammer For Daddy (UD 02, 1980). The band’s United Dairies association places them at the centre of the Fothergill/Stapleton split: Fothergill’s romance with Danielle Dax caused a permanent rift with Steven Stapleton, resulting in approximately one year of parallel United Dairies operations (c.1980–1981). Stapleton stated he would not have released Cake Beast (UD 07), which appeared under Fothergill’s operation of the label.

The band moved to Illuminated Records for their final LP (1982), losing their United Dairies connection as Stapleton took sole control of the label. [source: https://www.brainwashed.com/nww/words/wire1997.html; https://headheritage.co.uk/unsung/review/1452]
