Venetian Snares

Discogs ID: 12832
Real name: Aaron Funk
Origin: Winnipeg, Canada
Active: 2001–present
Label: Planet Mu (primary, 122 releases)

Canadian electronic musician. Real name Aaron Funk, from Winnipeg. The most prolific artist on Planet Mu by release count (122). Also records as Last Step, Hawerchuk, BeeSnares. Known for extreme-tempo breakcore and drum’n’bass constructions. His Timesig sublabel is operated by Planet Mu. Landmark Planet Mu release: Rossz Csillag Alatt Született (ZIQ111CD, 2005), which applied breakcore production to Hungarian classical orchestral samples and brought wider critical notice.

Aliases: Hawerchuk vs Last Step

Both aliases release exclusively on Planet Mu, but cover distinct stylistic territory:

Hawerchuk (Discogs ID: 215564)

  • 2 releases: Camel Toe / Caned Pees (2004, 12” ZIQ106) and Four Messengers (2006, 12” ZIQ158)
  • Styles: Breakbeat / Breaks / Dubstep — vinyl-only 12” singles aimed at the dancefloor
  • The “Kevin Hawerchuk” name that appears as realname on the Discogs entry is just another alias Funk used; the Written-By credit on Four Messengers is attributed to “K. Hawerchuk”

Last Step (Discogs ID: 286338)

  • 4 releases: You’re A Nice Girl (2005 EP), Last Step (2007 LP, ZIQ149CD), 1961 (2008 2xLP, ZIQ220), Sleep (2012 digital LP, ZIQ303)
  • Styles: Acid / IDM / Electro / Experimental — longer-form album-oriented work
  • Represents a mellower, more melodic side compared to Venetian Snares breakcore; tempo and aggression are dialed back, with acid lines and electro grooves more prominent

In short: Hawerchuk = dancefloor breaks/dubstep 12”s (2004–2006); Last Step = acid/IDM/electro albums (2005–2012); Venetian Snares = the main alias for all breakcore, drum’n’bass, and orchestral works.
