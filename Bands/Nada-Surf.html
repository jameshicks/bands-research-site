Overview

New York alternative rock band formed in 1992, led by guitarist and vocalist Matthew Caws alongside drummer Ira Elliot, bassist Daniel Lorca, and keyboardist Louie Lino. They broke wide with the sardonic power-pop single “Popular” on Elektra in 1996, then rebuilt their following from the early 2000s onward as an indie band, primarily through Barsuk Records and City Slang — a partnership that ran to 2022. Their catalogue of 36 releases spans four decades, densest in the 2000s, with styles ranging from indie rock and power pop through indie pop; key production collaborators across that run include Joe McGinty, Chris Walla, Ric Ocasek, and Tom Beaujour.
