British-based group led by Mark Eitzel (of American Music Club). Assembled around 2013 in the UK, the group recorded a single album at Konk Studios, London — the same studio associated with The Kinks. The lineup was a collaboration between Eitzel and a small circle of UK-based musicians, with Gareth Huw Davies handling production, bass, mixing, and mastering. The sessions yielded 11 studio tracks plus three live recordings from early 2013 UK dates. The original CD appeared in 2013 under the group name; a digital reissue was released in 2021 credited to Mark Eitzel.

Context

The Eitzel Ordeal was a relatively low-key side project within Mark Eitzel’s overall trajectory. By 2013 Eitzel had been a solo artist for nearly two decades after American Music Club’s first breakup (1995), with a string of albums on Warner Bros. and Matador before shifting to self-released work and smaller labels (Decor Records, Merge Records). The Konk Sessions sits between his Don’t Be A Stranger (2012, Decor) and Hey Mr Ferryman (2017, Merge) solo records.

The group appears to have been a one-off project. Discogs lists no further releases under The Eitzel Ordeal name, and no subsequent tours or recordings are documented in the database.
