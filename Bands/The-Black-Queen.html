Overview

The Black Queen is a Los Angeles electronic project formed in 2011 by Greg Puciato (vocalist, The Dillinger Escape Plan), Joshua Eustis (producer/multi-instrumentalist, Telefon Tel Aviv, Puscifer, Nine Inch Nails), and Steven Alexander Ryan (technician/sound designer, associated with Nine Inch Nails, Kesha, and A Perfect Circle). The band formed after Puciato and Eustis met backstage at a Dillinger Escape Plan show and discovered a mutual obsession with electronic music.

The project operates as a creative outlet for sounds and emotional territory outside their respective main bands. Musically they occupy a zone of icy synth-pop, darkwave, and industrial — melodic but tense, built for the dancefloor without conceding the bleakness. All writing, production, mixing, and mastering on their releases is credited collectively to The Black Queen, with Justin Meldal-Johnsen serving as executive producer across the 2015–2016 EP/album run. Jesse Draxler handled artwork and visual design across the full catalog.

The band founded their own imprint, Federal Prisoner (Discogs label ID: 1478886), to release their second album Infinite Games (2018) and all subsequent Puciato solo output.
