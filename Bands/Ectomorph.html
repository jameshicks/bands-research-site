Ectomorph

Discogs ID: 1462
Origin: Detroit, Michigan, USA
Active: 1994–2020
Name variations: The Ectomorph Collective

Detroit electro and techno collective. Founded 1994 by Brendan M. Gillen and Gerald Donald; Donald later departed and was replaced by Erika Sherman. From 2000 onward, also collaborated with DJ Godfather (Brian Jeffries) and Carlos Souffront. Operators of Interdimensional Transmissions (IT), the Detroit label they founded and continue to run.

Ectomorph’s sound sits squarely in the Detroit electro tradition — machine-funk rhythms, sci-fi textures, and locked-groove hardware experimentalism. A characteristic of many releases is that the vinyl contains locked grooves and/or hidden tracks between main cuts.

Interdimensional Transmissions (IT)

Interdimensional Transmissions is the Detroit electro label Ectomorph founded and operates (Discogs ID: 2839). All Ectomorph releases have appeared on it, and the label has released work by associated artists. Sublabels include: Acid Series, Eye Teeth, IT Podcast, Infinite Refractions, Interdimensional Publishing.

The label’s own note: “Please note that most releases contain locked grooves and/or hidden tracks.”

Gerald Donald’s broader context

Gerald Donald (ID 202454) is one of Detroit techno’s most prolific figures. As a founding Ectomorph member, his departure was significant. His many aliases and groups include:

  • Drexciya — with James Stinson (quintessential Detroit electro)
  • Dopplereffekt — with Michaela To-Nhan Bertel (his wife)
  • Der Zyklus
  • Arpanet (alias)
  • Elecktroids
  • Underground Resistance (member)
  • Abstract Thought
  • Many further aliases: Heinrich Mueller, Japanese Telecom, Dataphysix, Glass Domain, Intellitronic, Rudolf Klorzeiger, Xor Gate, Z Therapy
