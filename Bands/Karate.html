Karate

Discogs ID: 196946
Origin: Boston, Massachusetts (Allston)
Active: 1993–2005; reunited 2022–present
Name variations: Karate!
Genres: Post-rock, indie rock, jazz-inflected rock, slowcore

Boston indie/post-rock band formed 1993 by Geoff Farina (guitar/vocals), Eamonn Vitt (guitar/bass), and Gavin McCarthy (drums). Bassist Jeff Goddard joined in 1995, and Vitt shifted to second guitar; Vitt departed in 1997 to pursue a medical career, leaving the classic trio of Farina/Goddard/McCarthy. The most-represented artist on Southern Records with releases across the label’s full lifespan.

Karate’s sound sits at an unusual intersection: the angularity of post-rock, the patience of slowcore, and a deep absorption of jazz phrasing (particularly in Farina’s guitar work) — clean-toned, unhurried, and emotionally introspective. Farina’s voice and writing grew more nakedly lyrical over time, while Andy Hong’s recording approach (from The Bed Is In The Ocean onward) kept everything dry and close.

Farina developed hearing problems after twelve years of live performance and disbanded the group in July 2005. The band reunited in 2022 and signed to Numero Group, releasing new material alongside archival compilations.
