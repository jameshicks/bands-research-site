Throwing Muses

Discogs ID: 81475
Origin: Newport, RI / Boston, MA, USA
Active: 1981–1997; 2003–present
Labels: 4AD, Sire (US), Reprise Records, Rykodisc

American indie rock band founded by step-sisters Kristin Hersh and Tanya Donelly in 1981 in Newport, Rhode Island. First American act signed to 4AD (1986). Donelly left in 1991 to found Belly; Hersh continued and the band persists to the present.

Note on the Discogs profile: The profile states Tanya left “in 1991” bringing Fred Abong with her, and Bernard Georges replaced Fred. Red Heaven (1992) still credits Leslie Langston on bass — this suggests Langston may have played on Red Heaven before Georges fully took over, or the transition happened mid-cycle. The Discogs profile narrative places the shift at “after Leslie left in 1990” for The Real Ramona (Abong’s album), then Tanya/Fred departed in 1991, and Georges stepped in thereafter.
