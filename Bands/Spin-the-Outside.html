Spin the Outside

Discogs artist ID: 8024062 Formed: 2011, New York City Label: Gesch Records (house label, all releases) Bandcamp: spintheoutside.bandcamp.com Facebook: facebook.com/SpintheOutside

NYC-based genre-fluid collective formed by singer/songwriter/multi-instrumentalist Mr. Fussy (real name David Geschwendt) in 2011. The project operates as a loose collective — a rotating cast of players built around a core of Mr. Fussy and vocalist/keyboardist Citizen Zebra — spanning alternative rock, synth-pop, new wave, hip hop, funk, and children’s music across fifteen studio releases (2011–2025), all on Geschwendt’s own Gesch Records imprint.

A press blurb from Chartburst (Jay Wilkinson) calls them “New York’s Spin The Outside… a sonic lake made by Willy Wonka.”

Biography

Formation and Early Records (2011–2013)

Spin the Outside was formed in NYC in 2011 by Mr. Fussy, who serves as songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, producer, engineer, and mixer on every release. The self-titled debut (GES-001, 2011) introduced the core lineup — Mr. Fussy (vocals, bass), Citizen Zebra (vocals, keyboards), Todd Roger (drums), Alfonso Alegria (guitar, strings), and Klaus Traurigsten (synthesizer, trumpet) — with a straightforward alternative rock sound.

Redshift (2012, GES-002) expanded the lineup: Craig Brann contributed guitar, Nicholas Kozak added saxophone, and Nick Morrison (5) took over bass and guitar duties. The Christmas single Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star on the Tree (2012, GES-003) stripped back to the core Fussy/Zebra duo.

Only In Dreamland (2013, GES-005) marked an early genre shift toward synth-pop and new wave. The same year produced Rip Roarin Rap Music (2013, GES-004) — an unambiguous hip hop record featuring MC Dream (3) on lead vocals alongside Fussy and Zebra.

Mid-Period: Solidifying the Sound (2014–2018)

Rise And Fall (2014, GES-006) brought Simon Leander on drums and Melissa Zayas on backing vocals. Craig Brann contributes guitar on track 8. City of Ghosts (2016, GES-007) introduced Nathan Adler on guitar, with Leander returning on drums.

Beloved (2018, GES-008) shifted into electronic/experimental hip hop territory, with guest vocalist Skee (8) on three tracks (lyrics on “Concrete Nights,” “Cold Cut,” “Someone’s Gonna Pay”) and Thomas Ripley contributing piano across the album.

Expanded Lineup and Later Prolificacy (2020–2025)

2020 was a two-record year. Broken (GES-009) reduced back to the Fussy/Zebra core with a new wave/dance-pop/experimental sound. The Perpetual Tedium of Zoe Stardust and the Day Jobs (GES-010) introduced Zoe Stardust on drums and Jeff Berner on electric guitar, acoustic guitar, lap steel, and backing vocals — Berner’s first appearance in the discography. Nick Morrison (5) returned on bass/double bass.

Nocturnes (2021, GES-011) added drummer Jamie Starr (5) and brought Jeff Berner back on guitar, synth, keyboards, and backing vocals. The album spans pop, rock, indie rock, pop rock, and funk.

Booty Pop (2022, GES-014) and Wentworth by the Sea (2022, GES-012) both feature the Fussy/Zebra/Berner trio. The latter is credited as experimental electronic/pop. Note the catalog gap — GES-013 does not appear in the Discogs database.

Age of Animals (2024, GES-015) was the band’s first vinyl release, credited as Rock and Children’s music, featuring Jamie Starr (5) on drums and Thomas Ripley on organ/keyboards. Songs for Sleepwalkers (2025, GES-016) added Johannes Van Zyl on drums, Nick Morrison (5) on bass (track 5), and Jeff Berner contributing songwriting credits alongside the usual roles — the first time Berner appears as a songwriter.
