Overview

The xx are an English indie pop band from Wandsworth, London, formed in 2005, whose stripped-back sound draws on indie rock, pop, and house. The core trio since late 2009 — Romy Madley Croft (vocals, guitar), Oliver Sim (vocals, bass), and Jamie Smith (beats/production/keyboards) — crystallised when founding member Baria Qureshi departed in November of that year citing exhaustion and personal differences. Their catalogue runs from the late 2000s through the 2010s and is primarily released on Young Turks, the XL Recordings imprint that has carried nearly all of their output. Producer Rodaidh McDonald is by far their most consistent collaborator, sharing credits across 91 releases — a working relationship that has defined the sonic character of the band’s recordings.
