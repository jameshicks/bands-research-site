Conflict (2)

British anarcho-punk band. Discogs artist ID: 251960. Based in London.

Overview

One of the key bands on Crass Records, releasing The House That Man Built (1982, cat. 221984/1). Closely aligned with the anarchist politics of Crass. Steve Ignorant of Crass later became a member of Conflict.
