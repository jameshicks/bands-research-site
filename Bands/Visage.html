Visage

Discogs ID: 3479 Origin: London, England Active: 1978–1985; reunion 2002–2015 (ended with Steve Strange’s death)

New wave / synth-pop collective formed in London 1978 by Midge Ure and Rusty Egan — both then still members of Rich Kids — alongside Steve Strange as vocalist. Best known for the single “Fade to Grey” (1980, UK Top 10; No. 1 in Germany and Switzerland).

Wikipedia, as of 2026-06-25

Formation & Rich Kids Connection

Ure and Egan began forming Visage in late 1978 while the Rich Kids were still active, driven by their shared enthusiasm for synthesizer-led music that the rest of Rich Kids rejected. They hosted David Bowie/Roxy Music-themed club nights at Billy’s in Soho alongside Steve Strange (who had recently left the new wave band The Photons). These nights seeded both Visage and the broader New Romantic club scene.

Producer Martin Rushent heard early Visage material at Billy’s and financed further recording toward a deal with his new Radar Records imprint.

Timeline of overlap with Rich Kids:

  • Late 1978: Ure and Egan begin Visage sessions while Rich Kids still active
  • December 1978: Rich Kids dissolve
  • April 1979: Midge Ure joins Ultravox
  • September 1979: Visage debut single “Tar” released on Radar Records
  • 1980: Visage debut album; “Fade to Grey” becomes a hit
