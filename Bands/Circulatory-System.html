Circulatory System

Psychedelic experimental band formed in Athens, Georgia by Will Cullen Hart after the dissolution of The Olivia Tremor Control in 1999. Discogs ID: 359158. Primary artist on Cloud Recordings, with 13 releases between 2001 and 2023.

Members include Will Cullen Hart, John Fernandes, Derek Almstead, Peter Erchick, Eric Harris, Heather McIntosh, and others from the Elephant 6 orbit. Three studio albums: Circulatory System (2001, CLD-001), Signal Morning (2009, CLD010), and Mosaics Within Mosaics (2014, CLD022).
