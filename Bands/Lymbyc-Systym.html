Overview

Lymbyc Systym is an American instrumental duo — Jared Bell and Michael Bell — working across post-rock, ambient, and experimental music. They debuted in 2003 and were most prolific in the 2000s, issuing six records during that decade before continuing with three more in the 2010s; a further release appeared in 2021 on Dark Operative. Their catalogue found its longest homes at Mush Records (2006–2009) and Western Vinyl (2012–2016). Producer John Congleton features prominently across their work with 16 shared credits, and they share an unusually high overlap of 15 credits with post-rock act This Will Destroy You.
