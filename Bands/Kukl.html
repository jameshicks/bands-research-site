Kukl

Discogs ID: 217096 Also known as: K.U.K.L. Origin: Reykjavík, Iceland Active: August 1983–1986

Icelandic avant-garde post-punk group formed in the Reykjavík underground. One of the most internationally visible acts to emerge from Iceland before The Sugarcubes — which formed from Kukl’s core lineup after the band dissolved. Released two studio albums on London’s Crass Records, the anarchist collective label run by Penny Rimbaud and Eve Libertine.

Discography

Söngull (1983)

Label: Gramm (Iceland) — cat. GRAMM 17 Format: 7” Tracks: “Söngull” / “Pökn (Fyrir Byrjendur)” All six members credited. Engineer/mixer: Kjartan Kjartansson (ID 761890). One additional non-member credit: Giðar (ID 5190650) — role unspecified in Discogs. Siggi Baldursson credited under the alias “Tryggur” on this release. The sole Icelandic domestic release; all subsequent records came out on UK or French labels.

The Eye (1984)

Label: Crass Records — cat. 1984/1 Format: LP Tracks: Assassin / Anna / Moonbath / Open The Window And Let The Spirit Fly Free / Dismembered / Seagull / The Spire / Handa Tjolla Engineer: Tony Cook (2). Artwork: Dada Nana. Einar Melax plays bagpipes, bells, harmonium, and strings in addition to keyboards. Matrix inscriptions: “OPEN THE WINDOW…” / “AND LET THE SOUL FLY FREE.” Pressed at MPO. Styles: Experimental, Indie Rock, New Wave, Post-Punk.

À Paris 14.9.84 (1985)

Label: V.I.S.A. (France) — cat. N°003 Format: Cassette Recorded: Live, Paris, September 14, 1984 Tracklist (11 tracks): A1 Seagull / A2 Moonbath / A3 He Hapes / A4 Carlos / A5 The Men On The Cross / A6 The Spire / B1 Anna / B2 Dismembered / B3 Vials Of Wrath / B4 Latin / B5 Pökn For Beginners

Personnel as credited: Birgir Mogensen (bass), Siggi Baldursson / Tryggur (drums), Godkrist (guitar), Einar Melax (keyboards), Björk (vocals), Einar Örn (vocals). French live document; cassette-only release on a small Parisian label.

Holidays In Europe (The Naughty Nought) (1986)

Label: Crass Records — cat. 4. Format: LP Tracks: Outward Flight (Psalm 323) / France (A Mutual Thrill) / Gibraltar (Copy Thy Neighbour) / Greece (Just By The Book) / England (Zro) / Holland (Latent) / Aegean (Vials Of Wrath) / The Homecoming (The Night) Producer: Penny Rimbaud (credited as “Penny Lapsang Rimbaud”). Engineer: Mel Jefferson (ID 149624). Einar Melax: DX7, piano. Guðlaugur Kristinn Óttarsson credited as “Gud Krist.” B1 “England (Zro)” features additional vocals by Jamil Sairah (ID 599909). Matrix inscriptions: “MPO CAT NO 4 A-1 SIMPLY” / “MPO CAT NO 4 B-1 A MASTER WORK.” Styles: Experimental, New Wave, Post-Punk. The band’s final record; dissolved shortly after release.

Note on Sjon: Sjon does NOT appear on the 1986 original pressing. He is credited as “Warmest Thanks” in the liner notes of the 2001 and later CD reissues — an acknowledgment, not a musical or writing credit on the record itself.

Live Manchester England 1984 (2005)

Label: Not On Label (Kukl) — cat. none Format: CDr Recorded: Live, Manchester, England, 1984 Unofficial release; 9 tracks, no additional personnel credits. Circulated long after the band’s dissolution.

Rokk Arnir 1986-04-xx (2006)

Label: Bjork Hub Production — cat. none Format: DVDr (PAL; Unofficial Release; Remastered; Stereo) Recorded: Icelandic television, April 1986 Tracklist (7): Television Introduction / Dismembered / A Mutual Thrill / Masse Critique / Hér Koma Börnin / Vials Of Wrath / Assassin

Personnel: Birgir Mogensen (bass guitar), Siggi Baldursson / Sigtryggur Baldursson (drums), Guðlaugur Kristinn Óttarsson (guitar), Einar Melax (keyboards), Björk Guðmundsdóttir (vocals), Einar Örn Benediktsson (vocals). Video edited by Dan Lynch (8) (ID 6860935).

Rokk Arnir was an Icelandic music television programme. This is a fan-released unofficial DVDr of a Kukl TV appearance from approximately April 1986 — just months before the band dissolved. Released in 2006 on the Bjork Hub Production fan label. Genres: Rock; Styles: Experimental, Indie Rock, Punk.

The 2008 DMM vinyl reissues credit Leon West (ID 799219, alias: Secondson; Welsh musician, member of Goldie Lookin Chain and Beefeaters; Newport hip-hop engineer/producer) as “Consultant” — his exact role in the DMM pressing process is not described in Discogs. West received the same Consultant credit across ~30 OLI DMM vinyl releases in 2007–2009, covering Björk’s full solo catalogue, The Sugarcubes, and others; see his full article for detail. The 2001 Crass/OLI CD pressings of Holidays In Europe credit Sjon as “Other [Warmest Thanks]” in the liner notes.

One Little Indian’s involvement reflects the label’s ownership of Björk’s back catalogue — both solo and Sugarcubes/Kukl. Smekkleysa (Bad Taste, the Sugarcubes’ Icelandic imprint) is credited on at least one OLI pressing of Holidays In Europe.

After Kukl

The dissolution of Kukl in 1986 led directly to the formation of The Sugarcubes (Sykurmolarnir), which carried over Björk, Einar Örn, Siggi Baldursson, and others from the same Reykjavík circle. Sjon — who gave “Warmest Thanks” acknowledgment on Kukl reissues — became a long-term Björk lyrical collaborator through the Sugarcubes era and her solo career.

Einar Örn Benediktsson (b. 29 October 1962, Reykjavík) was active in the Icelandic punk scene before Kukl with Purrkur Pillnikk and has been prolific since. Post-Sugarcubes projects include Ghostigital (with Curver / Birgir Örn Thoroddsen, ID 61291), Grindverk, Frostbite (2), H3ÖH, Ornamental, and Kali (63). He has also composed music for film and pursued a visual art career.
