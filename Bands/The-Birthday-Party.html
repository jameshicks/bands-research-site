The Birthday Party

Post-punk band. Melbourne, Australia / London, UK. Discogs ID: 40247. Active c. 1977–1983.

Led by Nick Cave with Mick Harvey, Rowland S. Howard, Tracy Pew, Phill Calvert. Evolved from The Boys Next Door. Statik Records handled Greek territory pressings in 1982: Junkyard (catno STAT 3107) and Prayers On Fire (catno STAT 3103) — suggesting Statik had a Southern European distribution arrangement. Their main UK releases were on 4AD and Missing Link.

After dissolution in 1983: Nick Cave formed Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds; Rowland S. Howard joined Crime & The City Solution then founded These Immortal Souls (1987–1998). Genevieve McGuckin guested on keyboards on Prayers on Fire (1981) before becoming Howard’s partner and co-founder of TIS. Mick Harvey also went on to Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds. Barry Adamson briefly participated in the post-Birthday Party orbit.
