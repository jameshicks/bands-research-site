Muslimgauze

Discogs ID: 484
Real name: Bryn Jones
Born: June 17, 1961
Died: January 14, 1999
Aliases: Bryn Jones, E.g Oblique Graph

British ethnic electronica and experimental music project. Bryn Jones was the sole creator — the name is a pun on “muslin” (a type of gauze) combined with “Muslim,” reflecting his preoccupation with conflicts throughout the Muslim world, especially the Israel-Palestine conflict. He recorded at an extraordinary pace; Soleilmoon Recordings is his dominant label with 62 catalog entries, the majority released posthumously after his death in January 1999. His partnership with Soleilmoon and sister label Staalplaat was central to the label’s identity through the 1990s.

First Soleilmoon release: Untitled (SOL 2, 1988). Key titles: Vote Hezbollah (SOL 17 CD, 1993), Salaam Alekum, Bastard (SOL 25 CD, 1995), Syrinjia (SOLV 007, 1998 — 10 pressings). Posthumous releases continued through at least 2014.

Collaborated with Bass Communion (Steven Wilson) on Bass Communion v Muslimgauze (SOL 89 CD, 1999), completed after his death.

Death and Estate

Jones was admitted to A&E in Manchester on December 30, 1998, with a rare fungal infection in his bloodstream, became heavily sedated, contracted pneumonia, and died January 14, 1999, aged 37.

He routinely sent lengthy recordings to labels and collaborators with no instructions — which seeded a vast archive spread across multiple parties. Geert-Jan Hobijn of Staalplaat became the “de facto executor” of the work (Bidoun, 2009). No public documentation of formal master ownership has surfaced. By 2009 there were 50+ posthumous releases; at least 180 total Muslimgauze titles existed. The Staalplaat Muslimgauze Archive series was still issuing material as of 2025 (AR 74).

Soleilmoon Recordings appears to have introduced Muslimgauze to Staalplaat (ca. 1988), after which Staalplaat became the primary European vehicle. Posthumous releases continue independently on both labels and on 10+ smaller imprints.
