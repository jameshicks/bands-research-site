Tears For Fears

British synth-pop / art rock duo formed in Bath, England, 1981. Discogs artist ID: 37082. Members: Roland Orzabal and Curt Smith.

Major commercial success with The Hurting (1983) and Songs From The Big Chair (1985). Their ambitious third album The Seeds Of Love (1989, Fontana) marked a creative peak and the beginning of a prolonged hiatus.

Tim Palmer produced several of their 1990s releases after Curt Smith departed to go solo:

  • Elemental (1993, Mercury) — full producer credit; Palmer also played all instruments
  • Raoul And The Kings Of Spain (1995, Epic) — producer
  • Woman In Chains (1991, Fontana, with Oleta Adams) — producer
  • Multiple singles including Break It Down Again (1993), Cold (1993)

Connections

  • Tim Palmer — produced 1991–1995 output including full albums
