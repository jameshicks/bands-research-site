Chastity Belt

Seattle, Washington indie rock band. Discogs artist ID: 3440479. On Hardly Art 2015–2019.

Overview

Chastity Belt formed at Whitman College in Walla Walla, Washington and became one of the key artists on Hardly Art through the mid-2010s. Vocalist and guitarist Julia Shapiro also released solo material on the label. Known for dry, sardonic lyrics delivered over spacious indie rock arrangements. Associated with a Pacific Northwest DIY feminist scene alongside TacocaT.
