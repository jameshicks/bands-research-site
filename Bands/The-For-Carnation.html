The For Carnation

Louisville / Chicago post-rock band. Active 1995–2000. Discogs ID: 135335.

Brian McMahan’s (ex-Slint) post-Slint project. Drew in members from the overlapping Louisville and Chicago experimental scenes. Signed to Matador (early EPs) and Domino / Touch And Go (full-length).
