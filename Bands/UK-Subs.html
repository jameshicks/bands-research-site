UK Subs

Discogs ID: 131370. UK punk band; 13 releases on Jungle Records spanning 1993–2024, primarily on the Fallout Records sublabel (FALL series). Key Fallout releases include Normal Service Resumed (FALL LP 050, 1993), Occupied (FALL LP 052, 1996), and Sub Mission (The Best Of UK Subs 1982-1998) (FALL CD 055, 1999).
