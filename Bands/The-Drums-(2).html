Overview

The Drums are a Brooklyn-based American indie pop and post-punk band founded in 2008, emerging from the short-lived act Elkland (earlier known as Goat Explosion). Their core lineup of Jonathan Pierce, Jacob Graham, Adam Kessler, and Connor Hanwick debuted on record in 2009 and built the bulk of their catalogue across the 2010s, working primarily with Moshi Moshi Records and Island Records before shifting to Anti- from 2017 onward. Their sound spans indie rock, new wave, surf, and synth-pop, and recurring studio collaborators include Geoff Pesche, Johnny Aries, Adam Boose, and Eric Broucek.

Notes (web)

Label change: Island Records / Minor Records → Anti- (2017)

No public statement by Jonny Pierce or any band member has been found explaining why The Drums left Island Records (or Minor Records, which issued Encyclopedia in 2014). No interviews from the 2017 cycle — including pieces in KEXP, Outsmart Magazine, The Skinny, and Northern Transmissions — address the departure from prior labels. The Wikipedia article for Abysmal Thoughts and the Encyclopedia article are likewise silent on the reasons.

What is documented is Pierce’s account of joining Anti-: in a 2017 Northern Transmissions interview, Pierce recalled that Anti- head Andy Kaulkin told him at their first meeting, “here is what we like about you: you’re a fucking weirdo, you’re a loner, you don’t really belong to any group of bands.” Pierce described this as validating his sense of not fitting with other musical communities. (northerntransmissions.com)

The intermediate label shift — from Island to Minor Records for Encyclopedia in 2014 — is also undocumented in public-facing interviews reviewed here.

Conclusion: No public falling-out with Island Records or label-push narrative has been reported. The Anti- signing appears to have been framed by Pierce solely in positive terms (label fit / artistic identity), not as a reaction to difficulties with a prior label. The absence of any Island conflict narrative across multiple 2017 interviews suggests the departure was either amicable or simply not discussed publicly.
