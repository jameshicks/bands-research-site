Machine Gun Hogg & Co.

UK experimental / post-punk / dub project. Discogs artist ID: 134031. Orbit of On-U Sound and Cherry Red, early 1980s.

Overview

Machine Gun Hogg & Co. is a recording project that appeared on the On-U Sound-produced compilation Wild Paarty Sounds Volume One (1981, Cherry Red) with the track “Bed Bound Saga.” The recording was produced by Adrian Sherwood, placing it squarely in the On-U Sound orbit.

The name almost certainly encodes the nickname of bassist Sean Oliver — credited as “Sean ‘Hogg’ Oliver” on Playgroup (3)‘s Epic Sound Battles Chapter One (1982) — making this likely an early or parallel configuration of personnel who would later record as Playgroup (3). The track “Hoggs Might Fly” on Playgroup (3)‘s debut LP (credited to Bruce Smith and Sean Oliver) reinforces this directly.

Discogs lists no standalone discography under the Machine Gun Hogg & Co. name — the project has no profile text, no listed members, and no aliases. Its entire documented footprint is the single track “Bed Bound Saga,” which appeared in 1981 and was reissued on the Shake The Foundations box set in 2021.

Wild Paarty Sounds Volume One — Context

The 1981 Wild Paarty Sounds Volume One (Cherry Red, B RED 24) is the primary and original release vehicle for “Bed Bound Saga.” The LP was produced entirely by Adrian Sherwood and compiled as a snapshot of the On-U Sound orbit — featuring New Age Steppers, Prince Far I & Creation Rebel, Suns Of Arqa, The Chicken Granny, Judy Nylon, and others. Machine Gun Hogg & Co.’s track appeared as A2, the second track on the first side, alongside artists who were core On-U Sound associates.

The Playgroup (3) Connection

Playgroup (3) (Discogs ID: 31639) is the primary named group recording under the same Adrian Sherwood–produced, Cherry Red–released framework in 1982–83. The personnel overlap is direct:

  • Sean Oliver — credited as “Sean ‘Hogg’ Oliver” on Epic Sound Battles Chapter One (Playgroup (3), 1982). The “Hogg” of “Machine Gun Hogg & Co.” is his nickname. Oliver also co-wrote “Hoggs Might Fly” (with Bruce Smith), B6 on Chapter One — a track named explicitly after himself.
  • Bruce Smith — drummer, co-wrote “Hoggs Might Fly.” One of Playgroup (3)‘s listed members.
  • Both projects were produced by Adrian Sherwood and released on Cherry Red.

The most plausible reading: Machine Gun Hogg & Co. was either an early name for a formation that became Playgroup (3), or a named configuration of On-U Sound regulars that overlapped substantially with Playgroup (3)‘s membership. The Discogs database does not cross-link the two entities, and no alias relationship is formally declared.

Connections

  • Playgroup (3) — likely overlapping or alter-ego project; shares the “Hogg” name (Sean Oliver’s nickname), producer (Adrian Sherwood), and label (Cherry Red); “Hoggs Might Fly” names the connection explicitly
  • Adrian Sherwood — producer of Wild Paarty Sounds Volume One; ran On-U Sound; produced all Playgroup (3) releases
  • Sean Oliver — Antiguan/British bassist whose nickname “Hogg” forms the core of the project name; died March 1990 of sickle cell anaemia, aged 27
  • Bruce Smith — drummer; co-wrote “Hoggs Might Fly” with Sean Oliver; also in The Pop Group, Rip Rig & Panic, Public Image Limited
  • Cherry Red — released Wild Paarty Sounds Volume One (1981) and the 2021 reissue compilation
  • On-U Sound — production home for the Wild Paarty Sounds sessions
  • New Age Steppers — On-U Sound ensemble sharing personnel with Playgroup (3) and the Wild Paarty Sounds orbit
  • The Pop Group — Bruce Smith and John Waddington both from this Bristol post-punk band
  • Rip Rig & Panic — Bruce Smith and Sean Oliver both members
  • Shake The Foundations (Militant Funk & The Post-Punk Dancefloor 1978-1984) — 2021 Cherry Red box set where “Bed Bound Saga” reappeared
  • The Chicken Granny — fellow On-U Sound-adjacent project on the same Wild Paarty Sounds LP
