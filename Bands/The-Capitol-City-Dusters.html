The Capitol City Dusters

Post-hardcore band from Washington, D.C. Formed July 1996, disbanded spring 2003. Discogs artist ID: 5669565

Overview

The Capitol City Dusters were a D.C. post-hardcore band active 1996–2003, releasing all of their records jointly on Dischord Records and the smaller local imprint Superbad Records. They operated squarely in the orbit of the Dischord scene — Geoff Turner (of Gray Matter (2), New Wet Kojak, and Three (3)) served as producer/engineer/mixer across the first three releases — but their sound evolved from driving two-chord punk toward more textured indie rock by the time of the final LP Rock Creek (2002). Their catalog also includes two split 7”s, one with D.C. act The Most Secret Method and one pressed in Spain on Bcore for a European tour.

The band went by “The Dusters” in credits throughout their run.
