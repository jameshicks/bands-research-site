Magic Drum Orchestra

Twenty-piece live percussion ensemble; Discogs ID: 3858509. Founded 2005 by Glyn Bush (Bigga Bush) and Ralph Cree. Active on record 2009–2020. Primary labels: Lion Head Recordings and Tru Thoughts.

Overview

Magic Drum Orchestra (MDO) is a Bristol-rooted large-format live percussion group, a deliberate contrast to the studio-electronic, dub-reggae-influenced work of Rockers Hi-Fi. Where Rockers Hi-Fi builds tracks in the studio, MDO puts twenty percussionists on stage. The ensemble was co-founded in 2005 by Glyn Bush — known under his production alias Bigga Bush, and one half of Original Rockers / Rockers Hi-Fi — and Ralph Cree, whose Discogs presence is almost entirely MDO-specific.

The Discogs listing groups MDO under the same alias cluster as Rockers Hi-Fi, recognising that the project is an extension of Bush’s musical identity rather than a wholly separate entity. The group’s genres span Latin, Afrobeat, Samba, Jungle, and Electronic — a percussion-led eclecticism that drew on global rhythmic traditions and tied into the Bristol scene’s longstanding interest in world music and bass music.

The first recorded output, In The Studio (2009), appeared on Lion Head Recordings — the label also home to Bush’s solo work as Bigga Bush and Lightning Head. Later releases moved to Tru Thoughts, the Brighton-based label associated with the downtempo / nu-jazz / bass scene.

Connections to Rockers Hi-Fi and the Bristol Scene

Magic Drum Orchestra is formally listed as an alias of Rockers Hi-Fi in the Discogs database — both entities share the same alias cluster (Airgoose, Lightning Head, Moody White, Original Rockers, Switchblade Sisters, Wavelad). The underlying creative force in both cases is Glyn Bush.

  • Rockers Hi-Fi (ID: 1558) — formed 1991 as Original Rockers in Birmingham; Bush is a founding member
  • Farda P. (ID: 8622) — rapper/vocalist in Rockers Hi-Fi, appears as featured artist on the 2020 MDO EP
  • Lion Head Recordings — Bush’s own imprint, home to his Bigga Bush and Lightning Head solo output as well as MDO
  • Different Drummer — label co-founded by Glyn Bush (per Discogs profile)
  • Tru Thoughts — Brighton label (sublabel of Tru Thoughts Recordings, parent ID: 519331); MDO’s home from 2014 onward; sublabels include Unfold Recordings and Zebra Traffic
