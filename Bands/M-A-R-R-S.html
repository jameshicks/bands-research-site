M-A-R-R-S

UK recording project formed 1987 as a collaboration between Colourbox (Martyn Young, Steven Young) and A.R. Kane (Rudy Tambala, Alex Ayuli). Discogs artist ID: 12590.

Overview

Assembled under the 4AD umbrella as a one-off supergroup meeting. Their single “Pump Up The Volume” / “Anitina (The First Time I See She Dance)” (4th & Broadway, 1987) reached number one in the UK and is widely cited as one of the first sample-based dance tracks to hit the mainstream. CJ Mackintosh (scratching) and Dave Dorrell (performer/drop-ins) also credited. Engineer: Lincoln Fong. Design: Vaughan Oliver.
