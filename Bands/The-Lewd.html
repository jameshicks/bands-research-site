The Lewd

American punk band formed in Seattle, WA in 1977. Discogs artist ID: 413413. Name variations: Lewd.

Overview

The Lewd were one of Seattle’s earliest punk acts, formed in 1977 by J. Sats Beret after he left Ze Whiz Kidz (with Tomato Du Plenty). In late 1979, Beret and bassist Blobbo (Kurdt Vanderhoof) relocated the band to San Francisco. The band has remained active in some form ever since, with the current lineup of J. Sats Beret, Bob Clic, Tony Garay, Mic Rawls, and Rob Vastano.

The most notable aspect of the Lewd’s legacy beyond their own catalog: their membership reads as a quiet thread through Pacific Northwest and California punk history. Blobbo is confirmed by Discogs as an alias for Kurdt Vanderhoof, who would go on to found Metal Church. Drummer Christopher Reece (credited as “Christopher Clark Reece” on American Wino) went on to play with Social Distortion from 1984 to 1993. (The stub’s suggestion of a “Cars connection” is not supported by Discogs; Reece’s documented post-Lewd band is Social Distortion.)

Key Releases — Personnel Detail

Kill Yourself 7” (1979, Scratched Records)

Lineup: J. Sats Beret (vocals, written-by), Blobbo / Kurdt Vanderhoof (bass, performer), Brad Ramels (guitar), Jon Nay (drums). Producer: Marty Frasu. This is the four-piece Seattle lineup that had been operating since formation.

Tracklist: A — “Kill Yourself” (written by Brad Ramels); B1 — “Trash Can Baby” (written by Peter Tabor); B2 — “Pay Or Die” (written by Brad Ramels).

American Wino LP (1982, ICI Records)

The band’s only original-era LP. This is the San Francisco–era lineup: Bob Clic (guitar, vocals), Olga De Volga (bass, vocals), Christopher Reece (drums), J. Sats Beret (vocals). Producer: Clem Fisher. Mixed by Clem Fisher, Steve Brenner, and others. Engineered by Steve Brenner (side A) and Michael Fox (side B).

Full tracklist: “American Wino” / “Justice/Liberty” / “I’m Not Pretty” / “Climate Of Fear” / “Magnetic Heart” / “Suburban Prodigy” / “Beyond Moderation” // “Polluted Brain” / “Fight” / “Mobile Home” / “Cold & Numb” / “Dressed In Black”. Most songs written by Bob Clic and J. Sats Beret; “I’m Not Pretty” by Beret and Kurdt Vanderhoof; “Magnetic Heart” by Olga De Volga.

Kill Yourself … Again (1998, Chuckie Boy)

Compilation spanning the band’s full 1978–1982 history, documenting all lineup phases by track range. Mastered by Kurdt Vanderhoof. Design by Art Chantry and J. Sats Beret. Liner notes by Alan Wright (aka Dr. Weasel), Happy-Tom (of Turbonegro), and Lisa Lindstrom.

Demo-nstrations (2021, Puke N Vomit Records)

Demo compilation LP released in multiple variants. Two lineup periods: tracks A1–B2 feature Peter Tabor (guitar), Mike Davidson (bass), Dave Drury (drums); tracks B3–B7 feature Blobbo / Kurdt Vanderhoof (guitar), Bob Clic (bass), Carl Socko (drums). J. Sats Beret on vocals throughout.

Post-Lewd Trajectories

  • Kurdt Vanderhoof (Blobbo): Went on to found Metal Church, the Seattle/Aberdeen heavy metal band. Born June 28, 1961 in Aberdeen, WA.
  • Christopher Reece (b. July 28, 1959): Drummed for Social Distortion from 1984 to 1993. (The “Cars connection” in the original stub is not supported by Discogs — Social Distortion is his documented post-Lewd band.)
  • J. Sats Beret: Continued leading the Lewd through all subsequent lineups to the present.
