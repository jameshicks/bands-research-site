Overview

Le Volume Courbe is the recording project of Charlotte Marionneau, active since 2001 across indie rock, shoegaze, lo-fi, and pop rock. The catalogue spans three decades evenly — three releases each in the 2000s, 2010s, and 2020s — with Honest Jon’s Records as the most sustained label home. The project draws a remarkably high-profile collaborator pool: Peter Lockett leads with eleven shared credits, and Noel Gallagher, Johnny Marr, and Jason Falkner are each among the eight-plus-credit contributors, pointing to deep ties in the British indie scene. Sour Mash and Ring The Alarm have also featured the project, alongside earlier homes Poptones and Pickpocket.
