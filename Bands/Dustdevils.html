Dustdevils

Noise-rock band formed in Leeds, England, mid-1980s. Led by British guitarist/vocalist Michael Duane and Australian-born singer/bassist Jacqui Dulany. Originally a Leeds outfit recording for the local Rouska label, they relocated to New York City and eventually signed to Matador Records. Discogs artist ID: 109888. Name variations: DUSTdevils, Dust Devil.

Overview

Dustdevils began in Leeds around 1985–86, issuing their debut 12” EP Seeds In The Spoil on the Leeds indie imprint Rouska in 1986. Their sound drew on post-punk, noise-rock, and goth-inflected indie, placing them in the same Leeds ecosystem as The Sisters Of Mercy, The Three Johns, and the Gang Of Four alumni scene documented on the 2023 Cherry Red compilation Where Were You- (Independent Music From Leeds 1978-1989).

After their Leeds-period debut LP Rhenyards Grin (Rouska, 1987) and two further Rouska releases, the band crossed the Atlantic to New York. There they recruited bassist Mark Ibold and drummer David Reid (credited as “Reed”) and recorded the Teen-Beat single …Is Big Leggy (1989), produced by Kramer (2). The full-length Geek Drip followed the same year (Matador/Teen-Beat), with Keith Gregory (of the Wedding Present) appearing on bass. The 1990 LP Struggling Electric And Chemical (co-released on Matador and Teen-Beat) was recorded by Wharton Tiers (uncredited); Ibold and guitarist Rick Nance rounded out the lineup alongside Duane and Dulany. Ibold left shortly after to join Pavement.

A final release, the EP Extant (Matador, 1996), saw Duane and Dulany joined by Matador co-founder Gerard Cosloy on bass and Jon Dale on drums.

Context

The “Seeds In The Spoil” 12” places them in the Leeds post-punk scene of the mid-1980s alongside other Rouska-adjacent acts. The Rouska label was founded by Richard Rouska and operated out of Leeds (Electric Press Factory, 39 Cookridge Street, LS2); the 1988 Dollar Cacophony compilation gathered the label’s roster. After relocating to New York, the band’s co-production relationship with Geoff Clout ended; NYC connections brought in Kramer (2) and the Wharton Tiers / Matador axis.

Michael Duane continued recording after Dustdevils under various projects including Cameras In Cars, Atlantic Drone, Ancients, Crippled And The Burnout, and Dustdevil & Crow.
