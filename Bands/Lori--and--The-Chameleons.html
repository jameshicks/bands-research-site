Lori & The Chameleons

UK studio/conceptual act. Active 1979–1980. Discogs artist ID: 248501.

Not a performing band. A project assembled by Bill Drummond and David Balfe — operating as their production alias The Chameleons (2) — around a vocalist named Lori Lartey (Discogs ID: 717031). Two singles were released, both credited to “Lori & The Chameleons,” on Zoo Records / Sire (1979) and Korova (1980).

Biography / Concept

Drummond and Balfe co-founded Zoo Records in Liverpool in 1978–79, growing out of their shared membership in Big In Japan. Zoo functioned simultaneously as label, management, and production house for the post-punk and new wave acts emerging from Liverpool’s Eric’s club circuit — including Echo & The Bunnymen and The Teardrop Explodes.

As a production alias, Drummond and Balfe used the name The Chameleons (2) across their key Zoo and early Korova output. Lori & The Chameleons was one of several projects released under this umbrella — a vehicle built around a vocalist, Lori Lartey, whose only documented recording work was these two singles.

The first single, “Touch” (1979), was co-written by Drummond, Balfe, and Kev Ward (a Zoo scene associate credited elsewhere with the cover artwork). The B-side “Love On The Ganges” was co-written by the same three. The A-side had a track-level production credit for Mike Bersin alongside the wider Chameleons (2) credit, suggesting a divided or collaborative studio arrangement. The single appeared on Zoo Records with a Sire catalog number (SIR 4025) — Sire co-released several Zoo titles in this period — and later appeared on Korova with additional label credits.

The second single, “The Lonely Spy” (1980), appeared on Korova’s own catalog as KOW 5 — placing it early in Korova’s numbered series, among that label’s founding releases. Drummond and Balfe are the only credited writers; there is no third writer credit and no track-level producer variant. The B-side was “Peru.”

The project released no further material. Drummond and Balfe’s attention had by 1980 shifted to producing the Echo & The Bunnymen debut LP Crocodiles (Korova, 1980) and the first Teardrop Explodes LP Kilimanjaro (Mercury, 1980), both as The Chameleons (2).

Pressings of Touch (Discogs master 211304)

Multiple pressings were issued across the original Zoo/Sire release (1979, UK), and further pressings appeared on Korova and Zoo in 1981 and on Korova/Sire in France (1980) and Italy (1982). The Netherlands pressing (Discogs ID 2339033, 1979) was labeled Zoo Records and carries the same catalog number.

Connections

  • The Chameleons (2) — the Drummond/Balfe production alias under which both singles were produced
  • Big In Japan — the predecessor group from which Drummond and Balfe came; dissolved 1979
  • Zoo Records — Drummond and Balfe’s label; released Touch (1979)
  • Sire — co-released Touch as SIR 4025
  • Korova — released The Lonely Spy (1980, KOW 5); later pressed Touch reissues
  • Echo & The Bunnymen — produced by Drummond/Balfe (as The Chameleons (2)) at the same time; debut LP Crocodiles (Korova, 1980)
  • The Teardrop Explodes — also produced by Drummond/Balfe; Kilimanjaro (Mercury, 1980)
  • Dalek I — David Balfe was a member; also Zoo-affiliated
  • The KLF — Drummond’s major later project (with Jimmy Cauty); see Bill Drummond
  • Food — David Balfe’s post-Zoo label; see David Balfe

Compilation Appearances

No compilations specifically credited to Lori & The Chameleons appear in the Discogs data. “Touch” appears on the Zoo retrospective ‘The Zoo’ Uncaged 1978–1982 (Document, 1990) under the Zoo Records catalog.

Open Questions

  • Lori Lartey’s background prior to and after these recordings is undocumented in Discogs. Her Discogs page (ID 717031) has no other releases listed and no profile text beyond “Lori in Lori & The Chameleons.”
  • Mike Bersin’s precise relationship to the Zoo circle is unclear. His Discogs credits (ID 248502) are primarily as producer on Amazon Records (6) releases (1976) and one French release (1980). He shares his Discogs artist number sequence with Lori & The Chameleons (248501/248502), suggesting he was entered into the DB at the same time. The alias “Mike Burson” is noted but unexplored.
  • Kev Ward (Discogs ID 459091) is described as an “associate of Big In Japan and Zoo Records” but has no further release credits in the Discogs data beyond this single.
  • Whether “Touch” received any chart placement or BBC Radio play is not in the Discogs data.
  • The stylistic intent of the project — whether it was a pop experiment, a parody, or a sincere attempt at a commercial single — is not documented in the DB.
