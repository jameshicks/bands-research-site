Jonathan Fire*Eater

Discogs ID: 266772 Origin: Washington DC (formed 1993); based in New York City Active: 1993–1998 Genre: Indie rock, post-punk, garage rock

Indie rock quintet formed in Washington DC, quickly relocated to New York City. One of the most hyped American underground bands of the mid-1990s, signed to DreamWorks Records for their debut full-length — an unusual major-label deal for the time that generated significant press. The band dissolved in 1998 following their DreamWorks LP’s commercial underperformance. Three of the five members — Matt Barrick, Paul Maroon, and Walter Martin — went on to form The Walkmen. Vocalist Stewart Lupton died May 27, 2018, aged 43.

Lee Hazlewood Connection

JFE covered Lee Hazlewood on at least two occasions:

  1. “The City That Never Sleeps” — Titanic Tours Presents split 7” (1995); Hazlewood’s authorship uncredited on sleeve
  2. “When Prince Was A Kid” — bonus track on 2019 Third Man reissue of Tremble; Hazlewood credited as Written-By
