The Rosebuds

Indie rock band from Raleigh, North Carolina. Discogs artist ID: 723451. Formed 2001. Active on Merge Records and Goodnight Records through the mid-2000s to 2014; final album on Western Vinyl. Core songwriting duo of Ivan Howard (vocals, guitar) and Kelly Crisp (keyboards); surrounding lineup cycled through several NC musicians across eight releases.

Overview

The Rosebuds formed in Raleigh, NC in 2001 around the songwriting partnership of Ivan Howard and Kelly Crisp. Their early records — a 2002 split 7” on Pidgeon English and a debut LP on Merge Records in 2003 — established a template of melodic indie pop drawing on Howard’s guitar work and Crisp’s keyboards. The band’s relationship with Goodnight Records (Raleigh) produced their most discussed early release: a 2004 split 7” with The Close (GNR003). Merge became the band’s primary label through four albums (2003–2011). Their 2007 album Night Of The Furies featured guest work from Justin Vernon and Matt McCaughan (both later of Bon Iver). The 2014 album Sand + Silence on Western Vinyl was produced by Justin Vernon and BJ Burton with a full Bon Iver-adjacent band and functioned as a more stripped-back, collaborative project. Ivan Howard subsequently pursued a solo career under the alias Howard Ivans for Spacebomb Records (2013–2017).

Ivan Howard Solo / Alias Work

Howard also appeared as a session vocalist on multiple releases in the 2009–2021 period:

  • Gayngs — Relayted (2010, Jagjaguwar); Ivan Howard was a member
  • Dead Oceans release (2009) — Vocals
  • Trekky Records (2010) — Vocals
  • Bengals — The Drop Zone (2021) — songwriter, guitar, vocals

Summer Set (Discogs ID: 545113) is a band containing Ivan Howard, Brian Weeks, Jonathan Bass, and several other musicians — a notable overlap with the Rosebuds lineup.

Connections

  • The Close — closest scene collaborators; shared split 7” on Goodnight Records (GNR003, 2004); Ivan Howard sang backup vocals on The Close’s Sun, Burn (2006)
  • Goodnight Records — Raleigh NC label; put out the split (GNR003), Birds Make Good Neighbors (GNT 01), and Night Of The Furies (GNR015)
  • Merge Records — primary label for four LPs (2003–2011); Chapel Hill, NC
  • Western Vinyl — label for Sand + Silence (2014)
  • Brian Paulson — produced and recorded Birds Make Good Neighbors (2005) and contributed recording to Night Of The Furies (2007)
  • Chris Stamey — mixed Life Like (2008); co-produced and recorded Loud Planes Fly Low (2011); NC indie rock veteran (The dB’s)
  • BJ Burton — co-produced Loud Planes Fly Low (2011) and Sand + Silence (2014); key Bon Iver engineer
  • Justin Vernon (Bon Iver) — played guitar and recorded on Night Of The Furies (2007); produced and performed on Sand + Silence (2014); shared Gayngs membership with Ivan Howard
  • Matt McCaughan (Bon Iver, Portastatic, Hiss Golden Messenger) — drums on Night Of The Furies and Sand + Silence
  • Nicholas Sanborn (Sylvan Esso) — bass and keys on Sand + Silence
  • Gayngs — Ivan Howard and Justin Vernon were both members of this Minneapolis collective (2010)
  • Ashley Stove — Bill Alphin (Rosebuds drummer) + Jim Brantley (Rosebuds recording collaborator); NC band on Merge
  • Utah! — shared split 7” (2002, Pidgeon English)
  • Spacebomb Records — Ivan Howard’s solo label home as Howard Ivans (2013–2017)
  • Jim Brantley — recurring recording collaborator across Make Out, Night Of The Furies, and Life Like; also a member of Ashley Stove
