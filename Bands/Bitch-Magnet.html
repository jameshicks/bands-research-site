Bitch Magnet

American post-hardcore / noise rock band. Formed Oberlin College, Ohio, 1986. Discogs artist ID: 297894.

Overview

Bitch Magnet formed at Oberlin College in 1986 and subsequently relocated to North Carolina. The core lineup for most of their catalog was Sooyoung Park (bass, vocals), Jon Fine (guitar), and Orestes Delatorre (drums), with David Galt on second guitar for the Umber-era records. David Grubbs played guitar on early sessions before departing for Bastro. Their sound combined post-hardcore noise with structural complexity; they are credited as an early influence on the broader post-hardcore and math rock scenes that expanded through the 1990s. The band effectively ended in 1991; Fine later joined Don Caballero in 1999.

In 2011 they reunited for live performances and co-coordinated (with Temporary Residence Limited’s Jeremy deVine) a comprehensive retrospective box set reissue of their full catalog.
