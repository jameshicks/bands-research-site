Rockpile

Discogs ID: 313261

British rock band, late 1970s–1980. Core lineup: Nick Lowe (bass, vocals), Dave Edmunds (guitar, vocals), Billy Bremner (guitar), Terry Williams (drums). Closely associated with the Stiff/Radar Records new wave scene.

Shared orbit with Graham Parker And The Rumour through Nick Lowe (who produced the first two Parker/Rumour albums). Both groups were part of the same London pub-rock-to-new-wave scene.
