Overview

The Emotions (2) were a Jamaican vocal group active during the rocksteady and early reggae era of the late 1960s. The original lineup comprised Max Romeo, Lloyd Shakespeare — brother of acclaimed bassist Robbie Shakespeare — and Kenneth Knight, recording around five tracks for producer Blondel Calnek’s Caltone label circa 1967. After Max Romeo departed, the group continued with Audley Rollens and later Milton Henry stepping in, collaborating along the way with scene heavyweights including The Pioneers, Tommy McCook & The Supersonics, and Lloyd Daley.
