The Black Heart Procession

Gothic post-rock / dark chamber pop. San Diego, California. Discogs ID: 294859. Active 1997–present.

Formed in 1997 by Pall Jenkins and Tobias Nathaniel when their prior band Three Mile Pilot went on open-ended hiatus. The core sound: piano, organ, Moog synthesizer, musical saw, waterphone, and Jenkins’s low baritone vocals — dark, cinematic, instrumentally oblique. Self-produced across most of their catalog. Moved from Headhunter to Touch And Go (albums 2 through The Spell, 1999–2006), then to Temporary Residence Limited (Six, Blood Bunny / Black Rabbit, 2009–2010). Activity continued into 2026 with a new single and a reissue.

Amore Del Tropico (2002, Touch And Go — tg232cd) → Amore Del Tropico

Concept album: a noir tropical murder mystery told across all 15 tracks. A significant stylistic departure — bossa nova and Latin-tinged textures alongside the band’s usual gothic palette. Recorded at SDRL (Jenkins’s home studio, built partly for this project). Always conceived as cinematic; a companion film followed in 2004.

The Tropics Of Love (2004, Touch And Go — tg239dvd)

Companion film to Amore Del Tropico. 70-minute DVD. Directed, written, and edited jointly by Pall Jenkins and Matthew Hoyt. Silent film format — no dialogue; the full Amore Del Tropico album serves as the soundtrack. The band starred in it. Each of the 15 tracks becomes a chapter, mapping the film directly to the album’s sequence. Released on Touch And Go two years after the record.

Jimmy LaValle joined BHP as touring bassist following his departure from Tristeza in early 2003. In a Tape Op interview (issue 41), LaValle states: “I had just done a tour with Blackheart Procession [as the bassist] and I invited him to come to Iceland with me” — referring to Jenkins joining him for an Album Leaf Iceland recording session, where Jenkins played guitar, saw, and sang. The Fishtank session documents LaValle as part of the live lineup before his deeper involvement on The Spell.

The Spell (2006, Touch And Go — tg271cd)

Jimmy LaValle has playing credits across six of eleven tracks — piano, bass, organ, and “reverb tank.” LaValle joined as touring bassist first; the studio role on The Spell grew from that touring relationship. The album was written and recorded as a full band rather than the Jenkins/Nathaniel core with overdubbed guests — a deliberate change in method. Jenkins: “This one we wrote on purpose as a full band.”

Beyond Music: inFAMOUS 2 (2011)

BHP contributed one track — “Fade Away” (composed by and performed by BHP) — to the inFAMOUS 2: The Blue Soundtrack (Sony Computer Entertainment America, 2011). The soundtrack’s other contributors include New Orleans funk outfit Galactic and film composer Jim Dooley. A single licensed placement, not a score project.
