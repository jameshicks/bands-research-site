M.V.I.P.

Deep house duo formed in Germany, comprising Matthias Vogt (one half of Motorcitysoul, also a member of -re-jazz-) and Ian Pooley (real name Ian Christopher Pinnekamp), one of Germany’s most prolific house producers. Their sole known release, the Aviator E.P. (2010), appeared on Pooley’s own Pooledmusic label and includes a remix by Motorcitysoul.

Scene Connections

M.V.I.P. sits squarely in the German deep house scene of the late 2000s–early 2010s. Ian Pooley (Pinnekamp) is a Frankfurt-rooted producer who began releasing on Force Inc. Music Works in 1993, later moved through V2 Records, and has operated his own Pooledmusic imprint since the early 2000s — releasing material by himself and a tight circle of collaborators. Matthias Vogt brings the Motorcitysoul side of the equation: that project (with Christian Rindermann) was central to the deep/soulful house movement on labels including Simple Records, STIR15, and Aus Music in the 2004–2013 window. The collaboration is a natural intersection of two German scenes — Pooley’s Frankfurt house lineage and Motorcitysoul’s Cologne/Düsseldorf-adjacent deep house. The Motorcitysoul remix of “MVIP Theme” also appeared on the Motorcitysouled — Selected Remixes 2004–2010 compilation, embedding the track directly into that project’s canon.

Vogt’s other groups include -re-jazz- (jazz-electronic crossover), Matthias Vogt Trio, and Kids In The Streets. Ian Pooley’s alias network spans Ansicht, Bluelite, Ides, Quiet Daze, Silvershower, The Low Frequency Band, and The Modulor, among others.
