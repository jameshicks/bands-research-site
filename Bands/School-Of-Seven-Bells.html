School Of Seven Bells

American indie rock / shoegaze / dream pop band. New York City. Formed 2007; final release 2016. Discogs ID: 828694. Often stylized SVIIB.

Originally a trio: Alejandra Deheza (vocals, guitar), Claudia Deheza (keyboards, vocals), and Benjamin Curtis (guitar, synthesizers, vocals). Claudia left in 2010; Curtis died of T-cell lymphoblastic lymphoma on December 29, 2013. The fourth and final album SVIIB (2016) was completed posthumously from demos Curtis had written during his illness.

The Deheza sisters came from On!Air!Library! (2000–2004); Curtis came from Secret Machines and Tripping Daisy. The three met in 2004 when both OAL and Secret Machines were opening for Interpol on tour; they agreed to dissolve their old bands, move into a shared space, and build a home studio together. Alejandra Deheza and Curtis were also romantically involved for approximately five years.
