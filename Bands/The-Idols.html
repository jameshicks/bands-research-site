Overview

The Idols were a New York punk group formed by drummer Jerry Nolan shortly after his departure from the Heartbreakers in the summer of 1977. They are best known as the backing band for Sid Vicious at his New York-area live dates, a role reflected in their heavy overlap with Sid’s collaborator credits. The band dissolved in late 1979; Steve Dior and Barry Jones subsequently co-founded the London Cowboys, while Jones and Nolan both later worked with Johnny Thunders. Their catalogue is thin — one release on Ork Records from the 1970s and two archival issues on French labels in the 1990s — and their membership included Arthur Kane, the original bassist of the New York Dolls.
