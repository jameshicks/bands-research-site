Le Tigre

Electronic feminist punk / dance-punk band. New York City. Active 1998–2005; reunion touring from 2023. Discogs artist ID: 40310.

Overview

Le Tigre were formed in New York City in 1998 by Kathleen Hanna (formerly of Bikini Kill), Johanna Fateman, and Sadie Benning. Benning — a video artist — left in 2001 and was replaced by JD Samson for all subsequent activity. The band combined punk directness and feminist/queer politics with lo-fi electronics, programmed beats, samples, and a dance-pop sensibility. Their live shows incorporated video, performance art, and multimedia. They went on indefinite hiatus after the This Island touring cycle around 2005–2006 and began touring again in 2023.

Biography

Formation and Self-Titled Debut (1998–1999)

After Bikini Kill disbanded, Kathleen Hanna relocated to New York and began collaborating with Johanna Fateman and video artist Sadie Benning. The trio debuted with the single “Hot Topic” / “Yr Critique” as a 7” on Wiiija Records (UK, WIJ 107, 1999). The debut album Le Tigre followed later that year on Wiiija (WIJ LP 1108) and Mr. Lady in the US (MRLR-07). Produced by Le Tigre and Chris Stamey, it introduced their signature sound: punchy programmed drums, keyboard loops, samples, and Hanna’s deadpan-to-ecstatic vocals. “Hot Topic” name-checked a litany of feminist and queer artists and activists.

From The Desk of Mr. Lady EP and Lineup Change (2000–2001)

The EP From The Desk of Mr. Lady (Mr. Lady, MRLR-14, 2000) bridged the first and second albums. JD Samson appeared as additional vocalist on “Bang! Bang!” and “Mediocrity Rules,” anticipating her full membership. Sadie Benning left in 2001; Samson became the third full member.

The second album Feminist Sweepstakes (Mr. Lady, MRLR-19, 2001) was again produced with Chris Stamey. Guest appearances included Vaginal Davis (as “Dr. Vaginal Creme Davis”) on “Well Well Well,” Tammy Rae Carland on “Tres Bien,” and Melissa York on live drums for “Keep On Livin’.”

The Remix 12” (Mr. Lady, MRLR-24, 2001) featured remixes by Analog Tara, Swim With The Dolphins (Fateman’s alias), The DFA (“Deceptacon (DFA Remix)” — an early high-profile DFA commission), Reid Speed, DJ Ham & Cheese On Rye, and Lauren Flax.

This Island and Major Label Period (2003–2005)

For the third album, Le Tigre signed to Universal Records via Island Records Group and established their own imprint Le Tigre Records (Discogs: 33422), through which all three back-catalogue albums were reissued (LT001–LT003). This Island (Le Tigre Records / Universal, B0003385-02, 2004) was produced by Le Tigre and Nick Sansano, with “Tell You Now” produced by Ric Ocasek. “New Kicks” wove in sampled speeches from the 2003 United For Peace anti-war march, featuring voices of Al Sharpton, Susan Sarandon, Amy Goodman, and Ossie Davis. Guest vocalists on “Viz” included Gretchen Phillips, Harry Dodge, and Murray Hill.

Singles: After Dark (Island/Universal, MCSTD 40411); New Kicks (Universal, TIGRECDP1); TKO / Nanny Nanny Boo Boo (Island/Universal, WMCST 40398, 12”) with a Peaches remix of “TKO” and Junior Senior remixes of “Nanny Nanny Boo Boo.”

Le Tigre also remixed “Standing In The Way Of Control” by The Gossip (L’Age D’Or, 2006) and appeared as guest vocalists on a Chicks On Speed compilation (2002) and a “Wordy Rappinghood” single (2003).

Hiatus and Reunion (2005–present)

After the This Island touring cycle the band went on hiatus. Fateman and Samson subsequently formed Men (2), releasing Talk About Body (IAMSOUND, 2011) and Labor (Men Make Music, 2013). Hanna fronted The Julie Ruin (with Samson and others), releasing Run Fast (2013) and Hit Reset on Hardly Art (2016).

Le Tigre began touring again in 2023. A cassette compilation Great Hits! (Not On Label, 2023) appeared around that time.
