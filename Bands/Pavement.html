Pavement

Indie rock / lo-fi. Stockton, California / New York City. Active 1989–1999, 2010, 2022–present. Discogs ID: 7578.

Core: Stephen Malkmus (vocals, guitar) and Scott Kannberg / Spiral Stairs (guitar). Rhythm section evolved — Gary Young (drums, original; fired 1993), Mark Ibold (bass), Steve West (drums, from 1993), Bob Nastanovich (percussion/keyboards). Formed in Stockton, CA; became associated with the New York downtown scene via their label Matador Records. Debut LP Slanted And Enchanted (OLE 038-2, 1992) is the definitive lo-fi indie rock record of its era. Four more LPs on Matador: Crooked Rain, Crooked Rain (1994), Wowee Zowee (1995), Brighten The Corners (1997), Terror Twilight (1999). Disbanded 1999; reunion tours 2010 and 2022+.
