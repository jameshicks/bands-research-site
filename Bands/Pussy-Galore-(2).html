Pussy Galore (2)

American noise-rock / garage-punk band. Discogs artist ID: 252968.

Overview

Pussy Galore were formed in Washington, D.C. in 1985 by Jon Spencer and Julie Cafritz. Shortly after their debut 7” on Shove Records, Neil Hagerty joined on guitar. The band relocated to New York City, where Bob Bert (then of Sonic Youth) and Spencer’s then-girlfriend Cristina Martinez joined the lineup. The group released four studio albums and several EPs between 1985 and 1990, primarily on Shove Records (US) and Product Inc. (UK, a Mute Records sublabel), plus a notable split 7” on Sub Pop with Tad.

Their sound was confrontational noise-punk: heavily distorted guitars, abrasive production, and a chaotic live reputation. The band disbanded in 1990. Spencer and Martinez went on to form Boss Hog, and Spencer later led The Jon Spencer Blues Explosion. Cafritz joined Kim Gordon in Free Kitten. Hagerty launched Royal Trux with Jennifer Herrema. Bert became the studio drummer for Chrome Cranks and played with numerous NYC acts including Action Swingers.

Key recording collaborators included Wharton Tiers (engineer, several releases), Barrett Jones (engineer, debut and early EPs), Steve Albini (engineer, Dial ‘M’ For Motherfucker), and mastering engineer Chris Gehringer.
