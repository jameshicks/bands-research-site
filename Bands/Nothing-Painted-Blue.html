Nothing Painted Blue

Los Angeles indie/power-pop band led by Franklin Bruno, active 1987–1998 (and sporadically beyond). Discogs artist ID: 608500.

Overview

Nothing Painted Blue formed in Los Angeles in 1987, led by songwriter Franklin Bruno (guitar, vocals). Bruno and drummer Kyle Brodie (Discogs ID: 769328) were the only constant members across the band’s run; the rest of the lineup rotated through several configurations. The band released a debut LP in 1990 and built a decade-long catalog across Jupa Records, Shrimper, Kokopop (a Shimmy-Disc sublabel), Anyway Records, and primarily Scat Records (Cleveland). Bruno and Brodie were previously in a band called Born Leaders.

The band sometimes stylized their name as “N∅thing Painted Blue” or “ØPB,” referencing the empty set symbol from mathematics. Mathematical notation runs throughout their iconography and songwriting: Bruno’s undergraduate degree was in mathematics, tracks are titled “Indices And Surds,” “Epistemophilia,” “Cathexis,” and their final LP was named after the Monte Carlo method. The band wrote for John Darnielle’s “Lab Rat Blues” (credited on Emotional Discipline), and Peter Hughes (2) — later the Mountain Goats’ longtime bassist — played bass on four releases from 1994–1998. Joey Burns of Calexico and Friends of Dean Martinez appeared as a performer on the 1992 and 1993 records.
