Overview

Landscape were a London-based electro/pop/jazz group active from the mid-1970s through the early 1980s, working across synth-pop, new wave, experimental, and fusion styles. The bulk of their catalogue — four releases in the 1970s and eleven in the 1980s — appeared on RCA and RCA Victor, following earlier independent releases on Event Horizon Enterprises and Jaguar. After issuing the Manhattan Boogie-Woogie LP, the original five-piece (Andy Pask, Christopher Heaton, Dick Pearce, Geoff Duckworth, and Greg Lyon) contracted to a trio and continued recording under the name Landscape III. Key studio collaborators across their RCA years included Andy Jackson, Pete Smith, Colin Thurston, and Hugh Padgham.
