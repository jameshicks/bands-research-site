Xiu Xiu

Post-punk / experimental art-rock band, San Jose, California, formed 2001. Discogs ID: 154622. Pronounced “shoo shoo.” Active 2001–present. Jamie Stewart is the sole constant member and primary songwriter; the lineup has rotated continuously around him.

Biography

Formation and 5 Rue Christine period (2001–2006)

Xiu Xiu formed in San Jose in 2001, releasing a self-titled demo/CDR (Tonight And Today, 2001) on their own. Their debut proper, Knife Play (2002), appeared on 5 Rue Christine (cat. GER016) — the Kill Rock Stars sublabel that also housed Hella. The lineup for Knife Play was expansive: Jamie Stewart on vocals, harmonium, guitar and programming, with Cory McCulloch, Ches Smith, Greg Saunier, Yvonne Chen, Lauren Andrews, Jason Albertini, Tally Jones, and a brass section. The album was mastered by George Horn at Fantasy Studios.

A Promise followed in 2003 (5 Rue Christine, GER024), a more stripped-back set co-credited to Jamie Stewart throughout, with guest appearances from Jherek Bischoff, Sam Mickens, and others. The track “Fast Car” is a cover of the Tracy Chapman song.

Fabulous Muscles (2004) was the first album released in Europe on Tomlab (tom 34) — the US edition came later. Cory McCulloch produced; the core unit shrank to Stewart, McCulloch, Jherek Bischoff, Sam Mickens, and Lauren Andrews, with Pete Swanson (of Yellow Swans) and Gabriel Mindel Saloman contributing to “Support Our Troops OH!“.

By La Foret (2005, Acuarela nois1058), Caralee McElroy had joined as a full member. Cory McCulloch again produced; the record featured Devin Hoff, John Dieterich (Deerhoof), Ches Smith, Nick Hennies, and Marika Hughes. A split 7” with Devendra Banhart appeared on 5 Rue Christine the same year.

The Air Force (2006, 5 Rue Christine, GER063) was co-produced by Stewart, McElroy, and Greg Saunier (Deerhoof). Angela Seo (credited as Seo Hyunhye) contributed additional lyrics to two tracks — her first appearance in the Xiu Xiu credits. The album was recorded by Shawn Porter at Math Lab Studio and mastered by Thomas Dimuzio.

Kill Rock Stars years (2008–2010)

After a split EP on Creepshow / States Rights (2006), the band moved to Kill Rock Stars proper. Women As Lovers (2008, KRS484) featured Caralee McElroy, Ches Smith, Devin Hoff, Greg Saunier, and guests including Michael Gira (Swans) guesting on vocals for a cover of Queen/David Bowie’s “Under Pressure.” The album came out in a limited CD+DVD edition with a full live/video disc. Mastered by Thomas Dimuzio.

Dear God, I Hate Myself (2010, Kill Rock Stars, KRS503) was produced by “Thee Fallings Out Brose” (a studio alias) and mixed by Greg Saunier. It marks a turning point in lineup; by this record, McElroy had begun to step back and Angela Seo was becoming a full member.

Polyvinyl period (2012–present)

Always (2012, Polyvinyl PRC-236) was the first album for Polyvinyl. Mixed by John Congleton, produced by Greg Saunier and Xiu Xiu. The credited musicians include Angela Seo, Ches Smith, Devin Hoff, Greg Saunier, Carla Bozulich, John Dieterich, Sam Mickens, and Zac Pennington.

Angel Guts: Red Classroom (2014, Polyvinyl PRC-274) introduced Thor Harris (Shearwater/Swans) on drums and percussion, with Angela Seo on synths and Stewart. Produced and mixed by John Congleton; mastered by Alan Douches.

Plays The Music Of Twin Peaks (2016, Bella Union BELLA543V) — a full-length cover of Angelo Badalamenti’s Twin Peaks soundtrack, released via Bella Union. Personnel: Stewart, Angela Seo, Jherek Bischoff (bass/double bass), Shayna Dunkelman (vibraphone/percussion/drums). Mixed by Greg Saunier.

Forget (2017, Polyvinyl PRC-327) — no track-level credits in Discogs for this pressing.

Girl With Basket Of Fruit (2019, Polyvinyl PRC-373) — produced by Angela Seo and Greg Saunier; Devin Hoff on double bass; John Congleton mixed. Guests include Eugene Robinson (Oxbow), Tara Jane O’Neil.

OH NO (2021, Polyvinyl PRC-426) — a double LP featuring a guest vocalist on almost every track: Sharon van Etten, Chelsea Wolfe, Owen Pallett, Grouper (Liz Harris), Greg Saunier, Haley Fohr (Circuit des Yeux), and others. One track (“One Hundred Years”) is a Cure cover. Produced by Angela Seo, Greg Saunier, and Lawrence English.

Ignore Grief (2023, Polyvinyl POVL469) introduced David Kendrick as a credited co-writer and instrumentalist alongside Stewart and Seo. Styles span darkwave, drone, industrial.

13” Frank Beltrame Italian Stiletto With Bison Horn Grips (2024, Polyvinyl) is the most recent studio LP in the DB.

Mastered: George Horn.

A Promise (2003)

Jamie Stewart — all tracks; Lauren Andrews, Ches Smith, Yvonne Chen, Tally Jones — select tracks; guests: Cory McCulloch, Jherek Bischoff, Sam Mickens, Aaron Russell, Sara Chaney, Korum Bischoff. Cover: “Fast Car” (Tracy Chapman). Mastered: John Golden.

Fabulous Muscles (2004)

Core: Jamie Stewart, Jherek Bischoff, Sam Mickens, Cory McCulloch (producer), Lauren Andrews. Guests on “Support Our Troops OH!”: Pete Swanson, Gabriel Mindel Saloman, Josh Stewart, Sprout Guy. Mastered: John Golden.

La Foret (2005)

Core: Jamie Stewart, Caralee McElroy, Cory McCulloch (producer). Session: Devin Hoff, Ches Smith, John Dieterich, Nick Hennies, Miya Osaki, Sam Mickens (string arr.), Marika Hughes, Ben Goldberg, Alan Wiley.

The Air Force (2006)

Core: Jamie Stewart, Caralee McElroy, Greg Saunier (all three producing). Session: Devin Hoff, Nedelle Torrisi, David Horvitz. Angela Seo (Hyunhye Seo) — additional lyrics on two tracks. Mastered: JJ Golden. Recorded: Shawn Porter.

Women As Lovers (2008)

Core: Jamie Stewart, Caralee McElroy, Ches Smith, Greg Saunier, Devin Hoff. Guests: Michael Gira (vocals on “Under Pressure”), Howard Wiley (sax), Darren Johnston (trumpet), Gabriel Mindel Saloman (DJ set). Mastered: Thomas Dimuzio.

Dear God, I Hate Myself (2010)

Produced by “Thee Fallings Out Brose.” Strings/horn arranged by Jamie Stewart. Mixed by Greg Saunier. Mastered: Brent Lambert.

Always (2012)

Core: Jamie Stewart (engineer), Angela Seo, Greg Saunier (producer), Ches Smith, Devin Hoff, Sam Mickens, John Dieterich, Carla Bozulich, Zac Pennington. Mixed: John Congleton. Mastered: Alan Douches.

Angel Guts: Red Classroom (2014)

Jamie Stewart — vocals, analog synth, drum machine; Angela Seo — analog/microtonal synth; Thor Harris — drums, percussion; Shayna Dunkelman — percussion. Produced/mixed/recorded: John Congleton (and Stewart). Lyrics edited by Angela Seo, John Congleton, Vincent Van Gerven Oei. Mastered: Alan Douches.

Plays The Music Of Twin Peaks (2016)

Jamie Stewart — voice, guitar, synth, noises, drum machine; Angela Seo — piano, synth, cymbal; Jherek Bischoff — producer, bass guitar, double bass; Shayna Dunkelman — vibraphone, percussion, drums, synth. Mixed: Greg Saunier. Mastered: Alan Douches. Written by: Angelo Badalamenti.

Girl With Basket Of Fruit (2019)

Jamie Stewart — vocals, synth, guitar, percussion, viola; Angela Seo — piano, vocals, electronic drums, organ, harmonica (also producer); Greg Saunier — organ (also producer); Devin Hoff — double bass; Ches Smith — percussion; Daniel Brevil, Emmanuel Obi, Elliott Reed — percussion. Guests: Eugene Robinson, Tara Jane O’Neil (recording), Ace Stewart, River Stewart. Mixed: John Congleton. Mastered: Lawrence English.

OH NO (2021)

Produced by Angela Seo, Greg Saunier, Lawrence English. Recorded by Jamie Stewart. Cover: “One Hundred Years” (The Cure). Guests per track: Sharon van Etten, Deb Demure, Haley Fohr, Greg Saunier, Susanne Sachsse, Angus Andrew (Liars), Angela Seo, Owen Pallett, Chelsea Wolfe, Fabrizio Modonese Palumbo, Jonathan Meiburg (Shearwater), Twin Shadow, Alice Bag, Liz Harris (Grouper), Valerie Diaz. Mastered: Alan Douches.

Ignore Grief (2023)

Jamie Stewart — vocals, instruments, written by; Angela Seo — vocals, instruments; David Kendrick — instruments, written by.
