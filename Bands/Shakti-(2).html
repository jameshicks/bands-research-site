Shakti (2)

Discogs ID: 262879 Also known as: Remember Shakti; Shakti With John McLaughlin Type: Indo-jazz fusion group

Indian-Western acoustic fusion group formed ~1975. One of the earliest and most influential “world fusion” acts, combining Indian Carnatic and Hindustani classical traditions with jazz guitar.

Overview

Original lineup (1975–1977): John McLaughlin (guitar), L. Shankar (violin), Zakir Hussain (tabla), R. Raghavan (mridangam), Vikku Vinayakram (ghatam). The original band recorded and toured during 1975–1977, then went dormant.

Revived as “Remember Shakti” in the late 1990s with McLaughlin and Hussain, replacing the original Indian members with: V. Selvaganesh (kanjira/percussion, taking his father Vikku Vinayakram’s role), U. Srinivas (mandolin). Shankar Mahadevan later joined.

In 2023, Shakti released their first studio recording in 46 years, reuniting McLaughlin, Hussain, and Vinayakram from the original lineup alongside new members Ganesh Rajagopalan and Shankar Mahadevan.
