Gap Dream

Discogs ID: 2588580 Origin: United States Active: 2012–2016 Genres: Garage Rock, Psychedelic Rock, Synth-pop, Glam Labels: Burger Records, Third Man Records, Philthy Phonographic Records, Fat Possum Records, Suicide Squeeze

Biography

Gap Dream is primarily the project of Gabe Fulvimar (Gabriel Fulvimar; Discogs ID: 293150), a US musician who writes, records, and produces under the name. Fulvimar is credited as Writer, Recorded By, and Producer on every release — Gap Dream functions as his solo vehicle, with live and session personnel around him rather than a fixed lineup.

The project emerged in 2012. The debut self-titled cassette Gap Dream appeared on Burger Records (cat# BRGR153) — a ten-track album on cassette covering Garage Rock, Glam, and Psychedelic Rock terrain. The same year, the 7” single Ali Baba was released on Suicide Squeeze (cat# S109), produced and recorded by Fulvimar with artwork by Greg Boyd.

In 2013 Gap Dream entered a more prolific phase, issuing three vinyl releases: the 7” Chill Spot b/w Peter’s Brother (Burger Records, BRGR501), co-produced with Bobby Harlow of The Go; a 7” Shine Your Love on Fat Possum Records (FP-1365), also co-produced with Harlow; and the LP Shine Your Light (Burger Records, BRGR555), again co-produced by Fulvimar and Harlow, with mastering by Jim Kissling. Additionally, a one-sided flexi-disc Peter’s Brother (Instrumental Version) appeared as a joint release between Burger Records (BRGR616) and Philthy Phonographic Records (PHIL003).

Also in 2013, Gap Dream released the 7” 58th Street Fingers / Scary Dennis on Third Man Records (TMR 253) — an exclusive limited edition pressing in the Third Man Records Blue Series. This is the band’s only Third Man release.

Matt Kivel played guitar in Gap Dream during this early period. Around 2013 he departed to focus on his own solo career, which began in earnest with Double Exposure (2013, Olde English Spelling Bee). Bobby May (3) (Discogs ID: 5091309), bassist and organist who later joined Death Valley Girls, is also listed as a member on Discogs.

A 2015 split 7” with Part Time (Burger Records / Lolipop Records, BRGR775) contributed one new track, “Strong Love,” written by Fulvimar.

The final Gap Dream full-length, This Is Gap Dream (2016, Burger Records, BRGR900), marked a significant stylistic shift toward Synth-pop and Electronic textures, a departure from the earlier garage-rock sound. The album carries no additional personnel credits on Discogs beyond the primary Gap Dream credit, suggesting Fulvimar recorded it solo.

A 2015 compilation cassette Gap Dream / Shine Your Light (Burger Records, BRGR00957) compiled the self-titled debut and Shine Your Light LP, with Fulvimar credited on instruments and vocals and Bobby Harlow credited for mixing the Shine Your Light material.
