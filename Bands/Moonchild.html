Moonchild

Italian vocal/piano duo project. Primary artists: Annie Barbazza (vocals) and Max Repetti (piano). One album: Moonchild (2018, Manticore). Discogs master ID: 1504720.

Discogs ID Disambiguation

The Discogs page at artist ID 84692 is a mislabeled or merged entry. Its primary data describes a different artist: Lucas James Rodenbush, a San Jose, CA electronic producer active 1999–2002, also known as E.B.E. (2), Entity, and Lucas Rodenbush — connected to labels Solid Grooves and Solid Trax, with collaborators Alexi Delano and Johnny Fiasco. He has nothing to do with the Italian duo.

However, that same Discogs page lists Annie Barbazza (artist ID 4759127) as a “member,” which is how Barbazza’s own Discogs profile links back to “Moonchild” as one of her groups. The Barbazza/Repetti project does not have its own separate dedicated artist page on Discogs — it is represented only via the release credits on Moonchild (Discogs release IDs 12035895 and 12053081, master 1504720), where the primary artists are credited as “Max Repetti / Annie Barbazza.”

For this vault: The operative meaning of “Moonchild” is the Barbazza/Repetti 2018 project. The Lucas Rodenbush alias is a Discogs data artifact. The Discogs artist ID 84692 should not be cited as the project’s ID — use the release/master IDs instead.

Project Origin

The Moonchild concept was created by Greg Lake — bassist and vocalist of King Crimson and Emerson, Lake & Palmer — as a vehicle for a recording project of his own. Lake had revived his Manticore label in 2015 together with Italian producer and label director Massimo Marchini, releasing Live in Piacenza (2017) as his first Manticore release in decades.

When Lake became seriously ill, he redirected the Moonchild project to Annie Barbazza, a young Milanese singer-multi-instrumentalist he had first encountered at a concert in Piacenza, Italy. Lake remained involved as a producer, working alongside Marchini. The album was completed and released in 2018; Greg Lake died on 7 December 2016, before the album’s release — meaning Moonchild was produced during the last months of his life and stands as one of his final recorded works as a producer.

Album: Moonchild (2018)

A duo record conceived as an intimate re-reading of repertoire from across the Greg Lake songbook — pieces originally recorded with King Crimson and ELP, plus material from Lake’s solo career. The format is voice and piano, stripped of the orchestration and prog bombast of the originals.

Label: Manticore

Manticore (Discogs ID: 35662) was originally founded in 1973 by ELP’s production company Manticore Records Ltd. and distributed by Island Records (UK) and Atlantic/Motown (US). The label folded in the late 1970s and its back catalogue was licensed to Castle Communications in 1995.

In 2015, Greg Lake revived Manticore together with Massimo Marchini, releasing his last two albums on the relaunched imprint. After Lake’s death in 2016, Marchini took over the artistic direction. The label is now owned by Lake’s widow, Regina Uta Lake. The parent entity is Manticore Records Ltd. (Discogs label ID: 356887).
