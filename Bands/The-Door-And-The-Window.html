From Discogs

The Door And The Window was a short-lived British art punk DIY band, formed by the duo of Nag (2) and Bendle in London, and active from 1979 to ‘81. Nag and Bendle lacked any musical training or experience, but they nevertheless started by securing a concert gig for T.D.A.T.W., and then booked rehearsal studio to record their debut EP on a simple cassette recorder.

Inspired by Throbbing Gristle, File Under Pop and the ‘do-it-yourself’ aesthetics of UK punk movement, the duo recorded and self-released on 7” through the newly established NB Records (4) label. The edition of 1000 copies arrived from the pressing plant with white labels in plain sleeves, so Nag and Bendle hand-painted all the covers, and managed to distribute the entire stock.

The follow-up 7” was released the same year, distributed by Rough Trade. Vinyl with photocopied cover and folded A4 inlay was packaged in a clear plastic sleeve, with ‘tracklist’ stickers on white labels. Inspired by anarcho-punks Crass, T.D.A.T.W. wrote Do not pay more than 55p on the cover, but the distributor nevertheless managed to sold out the edition of 2000 copies. Production Line charted as a joint Single Of The Week with Ian Dury’s in New Musical Express magazine.

Overview

The Door And The Window were a short-lived London art-punk DIY group active 1979–81, founded by Nag (2) and Bendle without prior musical experience. Drawing on Throbbing Gristle and the British punk DIY ethic, they self-released two hand-packaged 7”s on their own NB Records (4) imprint — the second distributed by Rough Trade and named joint Single of the Week in NME alongside Ian Dury. The lineup later expanded to include Mark Perry (Alternative TV) and Anthony Clough, and the band performed alongside The Pop Group, Scritti Politti, Swell Maps, The Raincoats, and The Instant Automatons before disbanding in 1981. Reissue interest surfaced via Overground Records and, most recently, Rodent Tapes in 2022.
