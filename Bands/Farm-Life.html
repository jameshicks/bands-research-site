History

Farm Life formed in the early 1980s by members who met on a fine art course at Leicester Polytechnic. Original lineup: Andy “Jesus” Mosquera (guitar), Peter Swaine (vocals), Steve Gerrard (2) (guitar), Laurence Wood (saxophone), James Hunt (bass), and Mark Thompson (drums). Steve Gerrard (2) joined after leaving Disco Zombies (he had returned to Leicester). [Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bomb_Party, as of 2026-08-12]

Their track “Susie’s Party” appears on Cheap Tricks in a Box (Dining Out Records, 1982). A second single, “Big Country,” was withdrawn before release from Dan Treacy’s Whaam! label. [Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bomb_Party, as of 2026-08-12]

Dissolution and reformation as The Bomb Party (1984)

Farm Life disbanded in 1984. Three members — Andy “Jesus” Mosquera (switching from guitar to vocals), Steve Gerrard (2) (guitar), and Mark Thompson (drums) — reformed with new bassist Sarah Corina as The Bomb Party, taking their name from Graham Greene’s novella Doctor Fischer of Geneva or the Bomb Party. The reconstitution was not a simple rebrand: the lineup changed (Corina replaced Hunt and Wood; Swaine departed) and Mosquera’s role shifted. They debuted in 1985 with the Ray Gun EP on Abstract Records. [Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bomb_Party, as of 2026-08-12]
