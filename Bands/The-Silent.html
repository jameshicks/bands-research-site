Context: The Leicester Scene

The Where The Hell Is…Leicester? compilation was the central document of the Leicester post-punk underground ca. 1979–1981. Issued on The S+T Label — run by Chris Freer, Paul Betts, Kev Reverb (Kevin Bayliss), and Mick Dawkeye — the LP captured twelve acts from the city across styles: Minimal, New Wave, Post-Punk, Punk, and Reggae-adjacent sounds.

The Silent’s placement at B1 — the lead track of the second side — suggests some standing within the scene, but nothing further can be confirmed from Discogs.
