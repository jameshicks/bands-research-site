Collections Of Colonies Of Bees

Milwaukee, Wisconsin-based experimental post-rock ensemble. Discogs artist ID: 76441. Active 1998–present.

Overview

Collections Of Colonies Of Bees was founded in 1998 by guitarist Chris Rosenau and drummer Jon Mueller, who were both members of the Milwaukee post-rock group Pele. The project began as an outlet for exploring folk and bluegrass instrumentation filtered through electronics, with early releases appearing on the UK label Rosewood Union and then Crouton Music. By 2003–2004 the group had expanded its membership, adding Jon Minor (electronics), Jim Schoenecker (electronics/synthesizer), and signing with Polyvinyl Records for the album Customer. A second expansion in 2006 added Thomas Wincek and Daniel Spack, bringing the lineup to five members for the 2008 album Birds, released on Radium (3) (a sublabel of Table Of The Elements).

Several members — Mueller, Wincek, Rosenau, Spack, and Schoenecker — are simultaneously the core of Volcano Choir, a collaboration with Justin Vernon (Bon Iver) that released two albums on Jagjaguwar (Unmap, 2009; Repave, 2013). Following Giving (2011, Hometapes), Mueller and Wincek departed to pursue solo work and All Tiny Creatures respectively. A post-2012 lineup around Rosenau, Spack, Ben Derickson, Matthew Skemp, and later Marielle Allschwang continued recording, releasing Set (2014, Hometapes) and Hawaii (2018, Polyvinyl). A 2025 release Celebrities (Castle Danger Records) indicates the band is still active.

Former member Nicholas Sanborn later became best known as one half of the electronic pop duo Sylvan Esso, with Amelia Meath.

Sources: Discogs profile (ID 76441); Wikipedia, “Collections of Colonies of Bees” (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collections_of_Colonies_of_Bees); Wikipedia, “Volcano Choir” (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcano_Choir).

Associated Projects

Pele

The direct predecessor. Chris Rosenau (guitar), Jon Mueller (drums), Jon Minor (sampler/guitar), Scott Schoenbeck (bass), Scott Beschta (keyboards), Matt Tennessen (bass). Post-rock/math-rock, Milwaukee; active 1997–2004, reformed 2014. Releases on Star Star Stereo, Sign Language Records, Polyvinyl Record Company. Discogs artist ID: 67739. See Pele.

Volcano Choir

Collaboration between the Collections Of Colonies Of Bees core (Rosenau, Mueller, Wincek, Spack, Schoenecker + Matthew Skemp) and Justin Vernon of Bon Iver. Two albums on Jagjaguwar: Unmap (2009, reaching #92 US Billboard 200), Repave (2013). Wisconsin-based; on hiatus since late 2014. Discogs artist ID: 1579189. See Volcano Choir.

All Tiny Creatures

Thomas Wincek’s primary post-CoCB project, also featuring Ben Derickson and Matthew Skemp. Four albums on Hometapes (2009–2013). Discogs artist ID: 1539239. See All Tiny Creatures.

The Portable Quartet

Rotating ensemble featuring Chris Rosenau, Jon Mueller, Jim Schoenecker, and Thomas Wincek. One release: Take The Train (2005, Crouton). Discogs artist ID: 399659.

Sylvan Esso

Indie-electronic duo featuring former CoCB member Nicholas Sanborn (as one half) alongside Amelia Meath. Multiple albums on Partisan Records and Loma Vista. Discogs artist ID: 3472305. See Sylvan Esso.
