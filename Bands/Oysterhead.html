Oysterhead

American rock supergroup. Discogs artist ID: 347720. Formed 2000; originally active 2000–2001, reformed 2019.

Members: Trey Anastasio (guitar, vocals), Les Claypool (bass, vocals), Stewart Copeland (drums).

Overview

Oysterhead was assembled in early 2000 when Anastasio, Claypool, and Copeland agreed to perform together at the New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival. The three had not previously played together as a unit — they came from three distinct worlds: Phish (Anastasio), Primus (Claypool), and The Police (Copeland). The Jazz Fest performance led directly to studio work, and the band recorded and self-produced their debut and only studio album, The Grand Pecking Order, released on Elektra in September 2001.

The band went on hiatus after the album cycle and did not reconvene until 2019. Discogs documents the original active period through three releases: a 2000 promo/sampler, the debut single “Mr. Oysterhead,” and the full album.

Members — Background

Trey Anastasio (Discogs ID: 305837)

Guitarist, vocalist, and primary songwriter for Phish (formed 1983, Burlington, VT). Also leads the Trey Anastasio Band and has an extensive solo discography on Elektra, ATO, and other labels. Real name: Ernest Giuseppe Anastasio III.

Les Claypool (Discogs ID: 149855)

Bassist and vocalist; founder and constant member of Primus (formed 1984, El Sobrante, CA). Real name: Leslie Edward Claypool. Extensive side project activity: Sausage, Les Claypool’s Frog Brigade, Colonel Claypool’s Bucket Of Bernie Brains, The Claypool Lennon Delirium, and others. Previously played with Blind Illusion. Discogs aliases include “Granddad” and “Lapland Miclovik.”

Stewart Copeland (Discogs ID: 126442)

Drummer; co-founder of The Police (formed 1977, London). Real name: Stewart Armstrong Copeland. Also a film/TV composer and has participated in: Curved Air (pre-Police), Animal Logic, Gizmodrome, and others. Records under alias Klark Kent (3).

Label

Elektra (Discogs ID: 651) — major label; subsidiary of Warner Music Group. Home to Phish during the Elektra era and the label for Oysterhead’s studio output. The Grand Pecking Order carries catalog number 62677-2.
