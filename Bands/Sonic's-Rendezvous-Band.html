Sonic’s Rendezvous Band

Discogs ID: 654623
Active: c.1975–1980
Origin: Detroit, MI

Post-MC5 band led by Fred Smith (guitar). Also included Scott Morgan (vocals/guitar), Gary Rasmussen (bass), Scott Asheton (drums, of The Stooges). Known for the single “City Slang” (1978), largely unreleased during their active period; extensive live reputation. Smith’s most significant post-MC5 project before his marriage to Patti Smith in 1980.
