The Fleshtones

Discogs ID: 255629
Origin: Queens, New York City, USA
Active: 1976–present
Also known as: Thee Roman Gods, Flesh Tones, Los Fleshtones

New York garage rock / “super rock” band formed 1976. One of the longest-running acts in the NYC underground, mixing ’50s–’60s R&B, fuzz guitar, Farfisa organ, rockabilly, and surf into what they call “super rock.” Foundational to the CBGB-era new wave scene and active continuously into the 2020s.

Members

Core / long-running:

  • Peter Zaremba — vocals, keyboards, harmonica, organ, percussion (founding member)
  • Keith Streng — guitar, vocals (founding member)
  • Jan Marek Pakulski — bass, vocals (founding member; to c. 1990)
  • Bill Milhizer — drums, percussion (founding member)
  • Ken Fox — bass, vocals (joined c. 1991, replacing Pakulski; longtime member)
  • Gordon Spaeth — alto saxophone, harmonica (frequent collaborator; died 2005)

Other members (per Discogs):

  • Danny B. Harvey — guitar
  • Fred Smith (7) — (role unspecified)
  • Mitchell Ames — engineer/assistant (also credited on Blast Off!)
  • Robert Burke Warren — bass, vocals (later-period member)
  • Walter Sczesny — (role unspecified)
  • Lenny Calderone II — drums (documented on Blast Off! sessions, c. 1978–1982)

Key Release Personnel

Blast Off! (1982, ROIR)

Produced by Marty Thau; engineered by Larry Alexander. Zaremba: vocals, harmonica, organ, percussion, cover art concept. Streng: guitar/vocals. Pakulski: bass/backing vocals. Lenny Calderone II: drums. Recorded c. 1978.

Roman Gods (1981, I.R.S. Records)

Produced by Richard Mazda. Engineered by David Lichtenstein. Zaremba: vocals, jacket design, artwork. Streng: guitar. Pakulski: bass. Milhizer: drums. Spaeth: alto sax/harmonica. John Weiss: tenor saxophone.

Hexbreaker! (1983, I.R.S. Records)

Produced by Richard Mazda. Engineered by David Lichtenstein. Artwork: Zaremba, Mazda, Carl Grasso, George DuBose. Zaremba: vocals [uncredited], maracas. Spaeth: organ/harmonica/sax. Mazda: tenor saxophone. John Weiss: tenor sax. Mixed by Avi Kipper.

Do You Swing? (2003, Yep Roc Records)

Produced by Zaremba. Engineered/recorded by Rick Miller. Lineup: Zaremba (vocals/harmonica/organ), Streng (guitar/vocals), Fox (bass/backing vocals), Milhizer (drums/percussion).

Face Of The Screaming Werewolf (2020, Yep Roc Records)

Art Direction by Zaremba. Lineup: Zaremba, Streng (guitar/alto vocals/photography), Fox (bass/vocals/photography), Milhizer (drums). Guest: Michael Giblin (guitar, keyboards, chorus), Sara Milonovich (violin), Vibeke Saugestad (piano/chorus).
