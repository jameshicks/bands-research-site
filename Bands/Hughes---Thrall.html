Biography

Formation (1982)

Hughes/Thrall was a short-lived hard rock duo formed in 1982 between British singer-bassist Glenn Hughes and American guitarist Pat Thrall. Hughes had spent the preceding years as the bassist and co-vocalist of Deep Purple (Mark III and Mark IV lineups, 1973–1977) before a period working with Trapeze and beginning a solo career. Thrall arrived from a diverse background: session work with the jazz-fusion group Automatic Man (Island Records, 1976–1977), touring with Go (featuring Stomu Yamashta), stints in the Pat Travers Band (Polydor, 1978–1981), and an appearance on Billy Cobham’s Aura (Atlantic, 1979).

The two formed a songwriting partnership and self-produced the bulk of the album themselves, with Andy Johns brought in to produce several tracks and handle mixing and engineering duties. Rob Fraboni co-produced four tracks (A3, A4, B3, B4). Executive production was credited to Joel Brandes and Ron Domont.

The Album (1982)

The self-titled debut and only studio album, Hughes/Thrall, was recorded in 1982 and released in the US on Boulevard Records (5) (catalog number ARZ 38116). Internationally the album appeared on Epic Records in the UK, Europe, Spain, Greece, and Canada, and on WEA in Japan. The album was credited as a 9-track LP.

Three drummerss shared the session duties: Frankie Banali, Gary Ferguson, and Gary Mallaber — indicating the album was recorded across multiple sessions. Peter Schless played keyboards. Tim Kramer was the main engineer, with David Ahlert, Jim Perkins (2), and Tom Yuill as assistant engineers. Bob Carbone handled original mastering. All songs were co-written by Glenn Hughes and Pat Thrall, except “Hold Out Your Life” which credited Hughes and Thrall but with Thrall’s writing credit only noted through track B1 (per Discogs). The Rock Candy reissue credits confirm the writing as “Words By, Music By” — Hughes and Thrall on tracks 1–7 and 9–11.

A lead single, “Beg, Borrow Or Steal” b/w “Who Will You Run To,” was issued in the US on Boulevard Records (ZS4 03355). A second single, “The Look In Your Eye,” was released in Spain, the Netherlands, and Japan (the Japanese single bore the title 瞳の叫び = The Look In Your Eye).

A US promo sampler EP was also issued on Boulevard Records.

The album’s styles are classified in Discogs as Arena Rock, Classic Rock, and Hard Rock.

Dissolution

The duo did not release a second album. Both Hughes and Thrall moved on to separate projects — Hughes into his extensive solo career on Roadrunner and Steamhammer, and Thrall into session and engineering work (eventually becoming a prominent engineer and producer at Power Station / Avatar Studios, New York, working extensively in pop and hip-hop through the 1990s–2010s).
