Mau Mau (2)

Discogs ID: 583133
Origin: Turin, Italy
Founded: 1991
Genre: African and Latin traditions, world music, ska

Overview

Italian band from Turin. Mixes African and Latin traditions; formed in 1991. One of the leading Italian world music acts of the 1990s. Roy Paci is listed as a member; Fabio Barovero (who later co-founded Banda Ionica with Paci) is also a member. Both Paci and Barovero brought Sicilian brass and folk elements into the band’s sound.
