Konstruktivists

UK industrial / EBM group. Discogs artist ID: 162039.

Overview

Konstruktivists are one of the most extensively documented acts in the Klanggalerie catalogue, with 24 releases spanning 1999 to 2020 — covering both reissues of their 1980s and 1990s material and ongoing new recordings. They are a key figure in the UK industrial and post-industrial underground.
