Overview

The Max Von Mosch Orchestra is a German jazz large ensemble led by saxophonist and composer Max von Mosch. The group’s sole Discogs-documented release is Black Périgord (2011, Embab), a transatlantic production that brought together a core roster of German jazz musicians with several prominent New York-based players. The album’s special guest credit to veteran trombonist Robin Eubanks gives the record a strong connection to the American mainstream jazz tradition.

The orchestra is organized around Max von Mosch, who plays tenor saxophone and clarinet and composed all the material. The ensemble functions as a vehicle for his writing rather than a collective band in the strict sense.

Leadership

Max von Mosch (Discogs ID: 2644375) is described on Discogs as a German saxophonist. He also leads or co-leads two other groups:

  • Max.bab — a smaller ensemble featuring Andreas Haberl, Benedikt Jahnel, Benny Schäfer, and Max von Mosch; the group released Going Home (Embab, 2007) before the orchestra’s debut, suggesting the orchestra grew out of the Max.bab project. Max.bab was also the founding collective behind the Embab label (est. 2006).
  • Francis Drake (4) — a trio/quartet featuring Henning Sieverts and Max Frankl.

His name variation on Discogs is “M. v. Mosch.” His website: maxvonmosch.de.

Label: Embab

Embab (Discogs ID: 533164) was founded in 2006 by members of Max.bab in cooperation with BSC Music GmbH (Münsing, Bavaria, Germany). It specializes in jazz and is distributed by Rough Trade Distribution. Label code: LC 14355. The label is a sublabel of BSC Music. Its documented releases on Discogs are primarily Max von Mosch / Max.bab productions:

  • Going Home (2007, Max.Bab) — cat# 398.9932.2
  • Black Périgord (2011, Max Von Mosch Orchestra with Robin Eubanks) — cat# 398.9935.2

The label appears to function as a house imprint for Max von Mosch’s projects.

Key Connections

  • Philip Dizack — New York trumpeter; member of the orchestra and credited on Black Périgord; also a member of several other New York large ensembles (JC Hopkins Biggish Band, Remy Le Boeuf’s Assembly Of Shadows, The Awakening Orchestra)
  • Robin Eubanks — special guest trombonist on Black Périgord; Philadelphia-born; long association with Dave Holland groups, Art Blakey & The Jazz Messengers, SFJazz Collective, and his own ensembles
  • Linda Oh — New York bassist (Malaysian-Australian); also a member of Imagine (21) alongside Dizack
  • Robert Landfermann — German bassist listed as member; deep ties to the German jazz scene (Pablo Held Trio, Niels Klein Quartett, etc.)
  • Jonas Burgwinkel — German drummer listed as member; extensive German jazz scene connections
  • Michael Dease — American trombonist; also a member of Christian McBride Big Band and Dizzy Gillespie All-Star Big Band
  • Xavier Davis — American pianist; also in Christian McBride Big Band
