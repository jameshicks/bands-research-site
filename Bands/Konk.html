Konk

Post-punk / dance / Afrobeat / funk band. New York City. Formed 1980. Discogs ID: 16804. Active through at least 1989; retrospective on Soul Jazz Records in 2004.

Part of the downtown NYC post-punk dance scene, playing regularly alongside Liquid Liquid, Bush Tetras, ESG, The Peech Boys, and The Lounge Lizards. Their profile notes they “won the attention of both scenes” — post-punk and disco — by blending new wave attitude with the dance crowd’s sensibility. Influences: Afrobeat, jazz, funk, hip hop. Sound described as highly percussive with simple, strong bass lines. Featured in a book by David Byrne on the New York post-punk scene.
