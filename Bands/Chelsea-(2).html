Chelsea (2)

UK punk band formed in London, 1976. Led by vocalist Gene October; one of the first-wave British punk acts. Discogs artist ID: 260917. Website: chelseapunkband.com.

Overview

Chelsea formed in 1976. The original lineup famously included William Broad (later Billy Idol) on guitar and Tony James on bass, both of whom departed before any recordings were made to form Generation X. Gene October retained the Chelsea name and rebuilt the band. “Right to Work” (1977, Step-Forward) is their signature single. The band has maintained a rotating lineup ever since, with October as the sole constant. James Stevenson is the longest-serving guitarist across multiple eras. See Gene October for full biography and discography detail.
