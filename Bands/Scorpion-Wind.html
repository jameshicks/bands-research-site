Scorpion Wind

Discogs ID: 51399
Active: 1996 (recording); 2007 (reissue)
Genre: Neofolk, post-industrial
Members: Boyd Rice, Douglas Pearce, John Murphy

Overview

Scorpion Wind is a one-off neofolk / post-industrial collaboration between Boyd Rice (operating primarily as NON), Douglas Pearce (the driving force behind Death in June), and Australian percussionist John Murphy. The three had circulated in overlapping orbits throughout the 1980s and 1990s — Rice and Pearce had already collaborated on Music, Martinis And Misanthropy (Boyd Rice And Friends, 1990), In The Shadow Of The Sword (NON, 1992), and Wolf Pact (Boyd Rice & Fiends, 2002); Murphy had worked as Pearce’s principal live percussionist for Death in June.

The sole original release, Heaven Sent, appeared in 1996 on Pearce’s own New European Recordings imprint (via its sublabel Twilight Command), catalogued NERO XXXIX. It was issued simultaneously as a double LP and as a CD. The recording drew on additional contributors — Timothy Jenn (guitar, keyboards), Richard Leviathan (percussion), Dave Lokan (engineer/producer on most tracks), and Campbell Finley (trumpet) — making the project rather more expansive than the three-name billing suggests. Two tracks (“Paradise Of Perfection” and “There Is No More Sleep”) were separately produced and mixed by Pearce and Ken Thomas, giving them a slightly different sonic character from the Lokan-produced material.

In 2007, the album was reissued in remastered form on Nerus (NERUS 39), another NER sublabel, with two bonus tracks: a lengthy additional piece (“Funeral For 3”, 13:12) and a brief coda (“A Toast”, 0:14). The Discogs primary credit for the 2007 reissue is listed as “Death In June & Boyd Rice” rather than Scorpion Wind — an apparent cataloguing anomaly on the label’s part.

The project also appears on the 2003 multi-artist CDr Plauen 1997 (Valkrie, unofficial/limited), which documents a 1997 live concert and includes one Scorpion Wind track (“Never”) alongside sets from Death In June / Kapo!, Strength Through Joy, and Boyd Rice solo. This recording was never officially issued.

Thematically, Heaven Sent sits squarely within the mid-1990s neofolk movement: acoustic and semi-electric folk instrumentation layered with tape manipulations, drone, and Rice’s characteristic baritone vocals, touching on esoteric, pagan, and neo-martial imagery shared across all three members’ primary projects. (Wikipedia: Douglas P., Boyd Rice)

Connections

  • Douglas Pearce — core member; founder of Death in June; ran New European Recordings which issued Heaven Sent; previously in Crisis (2) with Tony Wakeford
  • John Murphy | 284808 — core member; percussionist; principal live drummer for Death in June; also active in SPK, Lustmord, KnifeLadder, Dogs Blood Order, Last Dominion Lost, Shining Vril, and dozens of other post-industrial and neofolk projects

  • NON — Rice’s primary project; same label network and scene
