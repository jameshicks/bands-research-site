The Middle Class

Discogs ID: 219590
Origin: Santa Ana, California
Active: approx. 1977–1982
Also credited as: Middle Class

American hardcore punk band from Santa Ana, California. Their debut EP Out of Vogue (Joke Records, 1978) is widely cited as one of the earliest hardcore punk recordings — four tracks clocking under five minutes total, with tempos and aggression that departed sharply from the punk that preceded it. The band appeared in the 2006 documentary American Hardcore.

The Atta brothers (Jeff, Mike, Bruce) are the core of the group. Matt Simon (2) appears in the Homeland LP lineup (1982) as drummer, replacing Bruce Atta. Mike Patton (2) is a distinct person from Faith No More’s Mike Patton (Discogs disambiguation number used throughout).

The 2008 Out of Vogue – The Early Material LP on Frontier is the definitive collection: it gathers the Out of Vogue EP, T&N sessions (three tracks: “Love Is Just A Tool,” “Above Suspicion,” “Archetype”), the Scavenged Luxury EP, and four early demos (“Autistic,” “You Belong,” “No Applause,” “What We Do”).

There is also an undated release 1978–1979 on L.A.P.D. Records and an undated compilation Out of Vogue – Discography Album ‘1978–1980’ on Deny Everything.

Label Notes

Joke Records (2)

Discogs ID: 467174. US hardcore punk label. Only one release documented on Discogs: Out of Vogue by The Middle Class (cat no. 09831 / 19831A). No profile text, no parent label, no sublabels, no URLs. Effectively a one-release vanity/local label. The catalog number discrepancy (09831 vs 19831A) reflects side A/B matrix variants, not separate catalog numbers.

Torture Garden

Discogs ID: 112585. US label; no profile information. One Middle Class release documented: Scavenged Luxury (TG101).
