Adolescents

Orange County, California hardcore punk band, formed 1980. Discogs artist ID: 277723.

Overview

Formed in Fullerton / Orange County in 1980. Original lineup: Tony Adolescent (vocals), Rikk Agnew (guitar), Frank Agnew (guitar), Steve Soto (bass), Casey Royer (drums). Released their self-titled debut (“The Blue Album”) on Frontier Records in 1981 — one of the key documents of early Southern California hardcore. Members overlapped with D.I. and Christian Death (Rikk Agnew).
