Out Lines

Glasgow collaborative trio formed circa 2016–2017. Discogs artist ID: 6082449

Members: Marcus MacKay (producer, writer, recorder), James Graham (vocals, writer; also of The Twilight Sad), and Kathryn Joseph (vocals, writer). The group released one studio album, Conflats (2017), on Rock Action Records — the Glasgow label run by Mogwai — and a handful of promotional singles drawn from that record. No further releases are documented on Discogs.

Overview

Out Lines grew out of the Glasgow independent music scene. Marcus MacKay brought in two of the city’s most distinctive voices: James Graham, lead vocalist of The Twilight Sad, and Kathryn Joseph, who had just won the Scottish Album of the Year Award for her 2015 debut Bones You Have Thrown Me And Blood I’ve Spilled. MacKay himself had previous credits as a producer and engineer across Glasgow indie (The Zephyrs, The Reindeer Section, The P&R Social Club, Our Lunar Activities) and operates the Diving Bell Lounge studio in Glasgow. His production credits include Snow Patrol’s Final Straw (recorded by), multiple Frightened Rabbit releases, and Kathryn Joseph’s subsequent solo records.

Conflats was mixed by Tony Doogan — the Glasgow producer closely associated with Mogwai, Belle & Sebastian, and the broader Scottish indie network — and mastered by Frank Arkwright at Abbey Road. Artwork was handled by designer DLT (3) (Dave Thomas, Discogs ID 2080075) with a credited contribution from Scott Hutchison of Frightened Rabbit, who died in May 2018, the year after the album’s release. Hutchison and James Graham were also both members of The Fruit Tree Foundation.

Members

Marcus MacKay

Discogs ID: 327353. Real name Marcus Mackay. Glasgow-based producer, engineer, and multi-instrumentalist. Operates the Diving Bell Lounge studio. Member of: Out Lines, The Zephyrs, The Reindeer Section, The P&R Social Club, Our Lunar Activities. Extensive production and engineering credits from the mid-1990s onward: Snow Patrol’s Final Straw (Recorded By), The Twilight Sad, Frightened Rabbit, Kathryn Joseph’s From When I Wake The Want Is (2018, Recorded By / Mixed By / Producer), and others. Also credited on Frightened Rabbit’s A Frightened Rabbit EP (2011, Engineer, Mixed By) and Kathryn Joseph’s subsequent records on Rock Action.

James Graham

Discogs ID: 708628. Real name James Alexander Graham. Lead vocalist of The Twilight Sad (formed Kilsyth, Scotland). Also a member of Gentle Sinners and The Fruit Tree Foundation alongside Scott Hutchison.
