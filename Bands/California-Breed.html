California Breed

Discogs ID: 3803640 Active: 2013–2015 Genre: Hard Rock Location: Los Angeles, California (English-American band) Members: Glenn Hughes · Jason Bonham · Andrew Watt (3) · Joey Castillo Primary Label: Frontiers Records

History

California Breed formed in Los Angeles in 2013 as a hard rock supergroup built around the partnership of British vocalist/bassist Glenn Hughes (ex-Deep Purple, Trapeze) and British drummer Jason Bonham (son of Led Zeppelin’s John Bonham; ex-Black Country Communion). The two had previously played together in Black Country Communion, the supergroup organized by Joe Bonamassa around 2009–2013.

The third founding member was American guitarist and vocalist Andrew Watt (3) (real name Andrew Wotman), a New York-born musician then in his early twenties. Watt’s role was guitarist, co-vocalist, and co-songwriter. A fourth member, drummer/percussionist Joey Castillo (ex-Queens Of The Stone Age, Danzig), is listed on the Discogs roster, though he does not appear in the album’s playing credits — Bonham is the sole credited drummer. Castillo’s precise function within the live or recording lineup is unclear from Discogs data.

The debut album California Breed was recorded with producer Dave Cobb — then rising quickly in prominence as a rock and Americana producer — at Cobb’s studio. All three primary members shared songwriting credits. Keyboards were provided by session player Mike Webb (2). Cobb also played percussion on the record. Mixing was handled by Vance Powell, with Eddie Spear as mix assistant. Mastering was done by Pete Lyman. Female background vocals were provided by Kristen Rogers.

The album was released in 2014 on Frontiers Records (Italy) and Ward Records (Japan). It was distributed across multiple territories: Irond (Russia), Icarus Music (Argentina), Soulfood (2) (Germany), and Scarecrow Records (2) (Mexico). The deluxe edition included a DVD with two music videos (“The Way”, “Sweet Tea”) and a 23-minute making-of documentary.

Julian Lennon contributed additional vocals on the track “Breathe.”

Andrew Watt (3) departed in 2015 to pursue a solo songwriting and production career. He would go on to become one of the most commercially prominent rock-adjacent producers of the following decade, working with Ozzy Osbourne, The Rolling Stones, Pearl Jam, Iggy Pop, and many others. The band dissolved following his departure; no second album was recorded or released.

  • Danzig — Joey Castillo was drummer 1996–2004
