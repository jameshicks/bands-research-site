Discogs ID: 506967 Active: 2004 Origin: Chicago, IL Roles: Duo — bass / voice / harp / mandolin / voice

Chicago duo of Jason Ajemian and Josephine Foster, active at least for the recording of their 2004 debut. One release documented on Discogs; the record was later reissued twice.

Biography / Profile

Born Heller is a duo featuring Jason Ajemian (bass, voice) and Josephine Foster (harp, mandolin, voice). Both were Chicago-based in the early 2000s: Ajemian as a working jazz bassist embedded in the post-AACM free-improv scene; Foster as a solo folk-and-psychedelic singer-songwriter who had already self-released two albums. The project sits outside both of their primary lanes — it is a spare, acoustic folk record, intimate and song-focused, built from material largely composed by Foster (8 of 11 tracks), with two tracks attributed to Ajemian and one to both.

The album was recorded in two sessions by two engineers: Devin Davis (tracks 3, 5–8, 11) and Paul Oldham (tracks 1, 2, 4, 9, 10) — a split that likely reflects different sessions at different locations. Paul Oldham’s involvement ties the record into the Louisville / Palace Records orbit; he is the recording-and-production specialist behind the bulk of the Will Oldham / Bonnie “Prince” Billy catalog. Devin Davis is a Chicago multi-instrumentalist associated with the indie-pop and experimental scenes. Mastering was handled by Ernst Karel, then active as an improviser (trumpet, electronics) in Chicago before transitioning later to electroacoustic and ethnographic sound work. The album was released by Locust Music, a Chicago indie label run by Dawson Prater (who also handled design on the record).

The album’s title track does not appear — “Born Heller” is the name of the duo, not a track on the LP. The eleven tracks are concise (most under four minutes) and largely acoustic.
