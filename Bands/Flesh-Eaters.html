Flesh Eaters

Origin: Los Angeles, California
Active: 1977–present (intermittently)
Discogs artist ID: 257142 (as “The Flesh Eaters”; name variations: Flesh Eaters, Flesheaters, The Flesheaters)

LA punk / deathrock band led by Chris D. (Chris Desjardins). Chris D. produced half of The Gun Club’s Fire Of Love (1981) and has remained active with the Flesh Eaters across several decades, often with rotating membership from the broader LA underground.

Known Members (from Discogs)

Chris D. (vocals, lyrics) — core; D.J. Bonebrake — drums; Dave Alvin — guitar (Blasters connection); Steve Berlin — saxophone; Joe Nanini — drums; Dennis Walsh — drums; Scott Lasken — bass; John Curry — guitar; Joe Ramirez — guitar; Tito Larriva — bass; and many others across configurations.
