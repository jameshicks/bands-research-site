Motorcitysoul

Discogs ID: 201351 Members: Christian Rindermann, Matthias Vogt Origin: Frankfurt, Germany Active: 2004–2011 (as recorded duo; members continue individually) Genres: Deep House, Electronic Labels: INFRACom!, STIR15 Recordings, Aus Music, Simple Records, Ibadan, Finale Sessions, BCBtec

Biography

Motorcitysoul is a Frankfurt-based deep house duo formed by Christian Rindermann (Discogs ID: 762609, alias C-Rock) and Matthias Vogt (Discogs ID: 83953, alias DJ Matt). The name pays homage to Detroit’s soul and techno heritage — a deliberate positioning that ran through their career in sound and in collaborations.

Formation and INFRACom! debut (2004–2005)

The duo’s debut album Did You Expect That? (INFRACom!, IC 113-2, 2004) established their base: Vogt as the musicological anchor — keyboards, piano, vocals, arrangements — and Rindermann adding production and engineering. The album is a live-instrument-forward deep house record with contributions from the core -re-jazz- lineup: bassist Andreas Manns, guitarist Hanno Busch, percussionist Heiko Himmighoffen, and vocalist Inga Lühning. Mastering by Mainframe (2) (Frank Marheineke). A follow-up single Written In The Stars (INFRACom!, 2005) retained Lühning on vocals.

STIR15 Recordings period (2006–2007)

The duo moved to the German label STIR15 Recordings for a run of 12-inches. Mbali (Potelea Mbali) (stir1532, 2006) featured Swahili vocalist Onejiru (P.W. Schindler) and became a recurring track in their catalog, later remixed by Jerome Sydenham. Aura (stir1533, 2007) followed, again with Himmighoffen on percussion, remixed by Jimpster.

Between INFRACom! and STIR15 they also appeared on the Lo-Fi Stereo imprint (a STIR15 sublabel) with Housemusic / State Of Mind (2007), co-written with Renato Figoli and Signor Andreoni.

Aus Music (2007)

In 2007 the duo placed two releases on the UK label Aus Music (part of the !K7 Label Group): Kazan (Exit Cube) (AUS0706) and Space Kätzle (AUS0709). Both were mastered by Shane McEnhill at Aus, and Space Kätzle received a remix from Jerome Sydenham (his “Blacktro Penetration” version). These records placed them in the broader European minimal/tech-house circuit.

Simple Records period (2008–2011)

The longest continuous label relationship was with the UK imprint Simple Records (a sublabel of Simple Records Ltd.), where they released four records between 2008 and 2011:

  • Change You feat. Ovasoul7 (SIMPLE0834, 2008) — remixed by Shur-I-kan
  • Solar / Hatohay EP (2008)
  • Vivid (Simple0942, 2009) — remixed twice by Roman Flügel
  • Ushuaia Day (2011)

Mastering across this run was handled by Matt Colton.

Ibadan and the New York connection (2010)

Mbali / Jero (Ibadan, irc099, 2010) was a split release coupling Motorcitysoul/Onejiru’s “Mbali” in a Jerome Sydenham remix with a new track “Jero” co-credited to Jerome Sydenham & Dennis Ferrer. Sydenham’s vocal dub and dubstrumental of “Mbali” took up the A-side; the B-side had Sydenham and Dennis Ferrer delivering vocals on “Jero.” The release consolidated Motorcitysoul’s relationship with the Ibadan world.

Community EP and Finale Sessions (2011)

Community EP (FSDL001 / dl-002, 2011) was co-released on Finale Sessions and DeepLabs, co-credited with Luke Hess. The split 12-inch gave side A to Hess (“Come,” “Unity”) and side B to Motorcitysoul (“Rosenau,” “Ostpark”). Mastering/cut by Mathieu Berthet. The release directly connected a Frankfurt duo with a Detroit-affiliated underground New York label — the aesthetic bridge the stub identified was real.

Motorcitysouled retrospective (2011)

Motorcitysouled — Selected Remixes 2004–2010 (STIR15 Recordings, Stir15 CD8, 2011) compiled a decade of remix work. Tracks remixed include releases by Tiger Stripes (Mikael Nordgren), Solomun & Stimming, Johannes Heil, Dirt Crew, Kruse & Nürnberg, Jimpster alias Clara Hill, Atjazz, and others. The duo also remixed a track for Kids In The Streets (the joint project with Chez Damier). Design and layout by C-Rock, Matthias Vogt, and Volka; photography by Mario Andreya.

BCBtec

Manic Mondays EP (BCBtec 133, 2010) — a digital-only release on BCBtec — also appeared in this period, showing them exploring digital distribution channels alongside physical.

Christian Rindermann (C-Rock)

Rindermann’s role in the duo is primarily production and engineering. He serves as C-Rock on production credits across most Motorcitysoul releases. Outside the duo he is a member of Further (with Neville Attree and Dd Sot) and Kids In The Streets (with Vogt and Anthony Pearson). His solo/alias production work includes credits on STIR15 Recordings (Bassmatic, 2010), Ibadan, and the Motorcitysouled retrospective where he remastered the compilation.

Matthias Vogt

Vogt is a trained jazz pianist and keyboardist, born in Rüsselsheim. His background in jazz is audible in the live-instrument-heavy early Motorcitysoul material. He is a core member of -re-jazz- (the INFRACom! jazz-house ensemble including Andreas Manns, Hanno Busch, Heiko Himmighoffen, Inga Lühning, Oliver Leicht, Jan Stürmer, and Volker Schmidt), for which he plays piano, electric piano (Fender Rhodes), and synthesizer. He also fronts his own jazz trio Matthias Vogt Trio (with Manns and Volker Schmidt) and operates as M.V.I.P. (with Ian Pinnekamp). Solo primary-credit releases include EPs on Polytone Recordings (2010–2019), Lazy Days Recordings (Canada, 2011–2012), Large Records (2011), Anjunadeep (2015–2016), and Flaneurecordings (2022).

Scene Connections

  • Detroit homage: Name and aesthetic explicitly reference Detroit soul/techno heritage. Realized via collaborations with Luke Hess (Detroit-area), Jerome Sydenham (Ibadan/NYC-Detroit axis), and releases on Finale Sessions (Detroit-affiliated NY label).
  • Frankfurt / INFRACom! network: Shared musicians with -re-jazz- — specifically Andreas Manns, Hanno Busch, Heiko Himmighoffen, Inga Lühning. INFRACom! was the base label for both -re-jazz- and Motorcitysoul.
  • Kids In The Streets: Joint project (Rindermann + Vogt + Anthony Pearson) with Chez Damier contribution; Motorcitysouled retrospective includes a remix of a Kids In The Streets track.
  • UK house circuit: Aus Music and Simple Records placed them in the UK minimal/tech-house world; remixers included Roman Flügel, Shur-I-kan, Jimpster.
  • Ibadan axis: Mbali / Jero (irc099) connects to the Jerome Sydenham / Dennis Ferrer world.
  • Motorcitysoul Productions: A registered label imprint (Discogs label ID: 1556934) exists under this name but has no releases in the database.
