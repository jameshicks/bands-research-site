Overview

The Plasmatics were an American punk rock and heavy metal band formed in New York City in 1977 by manager Rod Swenson and vocalist Wendy O. Williams. They became notorious for their incendiary live performances — chainsawing guitars, sledgehammering televisions, and detonating automobiles on stage — which earned them both a devoted following and considerable controversy. Their catalogue runs from 1978 through the early 1980s peak (thirteen releases that decade), spanning styles from raw punk through thrash and hard rock, before resurfacing via reissues and archival releases into the 2000s and beyond. They recorded for Stiff Records early on before moving to Capitol Records in 1982, with producers Jimmy Miller and Ed Stasium handling much of their studio work.
