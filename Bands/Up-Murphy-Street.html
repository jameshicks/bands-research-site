Up Murphy Street

Loose ensemble centred on Mick Hobbs and Bill Gilonis, releasing one album originally in 1986 (UK vinyl) and reissued in 2010 by El Frenzy Productions (Spain). Discogs artist ID: 1642431.

Overview

Up Murphy Street is a large cast project drawing principally on the Officer! / Family Fodder / Murphy Working Stiffs network. The core creative pair — Mick Hobbs and Bill Gilonis — are both central members of Officer! and The Work, and the wider ensemble on the album draws in several recurring collaborators from that same London underground axis.

The album was originally released as a vinyl LP on Not On Label in 1986 (UK; Discogs release ID: 2028127, master ID: 457891), with no personnel credits on that pressing. It was later reissued as a CDr by El Frenzy Productions in Spain around 2010 (Discogs release ID: 3760485), and exists in a further CD pressing on the same label (Discogs release ID: 15922541) with full personnel credits. Genres on Discogs: Rock / Art Rock, Avantgarde, Lo-Fi.

Discogs lists three members on the artist page: Chris Cornetto (602709), Chris Gray (2) (260648), and Robert Storey (2551254) — this is a partial subset of the full credited ensemble. The 1986 original lists no additional credits; all personnel detail comes from the El Frenzy reissue.

Network Context

The ensemble pulls together several overlapping circles:

  • Officer! / The Work axis: Hobbs, Gilonis, and Fiedorowicz are the Officer! core. Gilonis and Hobbs are also The Work co-founders. Chris Gray (2) is a Murphy Working Stiffs member alongside Gilonis. John Glyn is similarly a Murphy Working Stiffs member.
  • It’s War Boys / Welton underground: Lepke Buchvalter and Amos (3) / Jim Whelton (L. Voag / Xentos) both circulate through the It’s War Boys label and its adjacent projects.
  • This Heat orbit: Trefor Goronwy toured with This Heat in 1981–82 and co-founded The Camberwell Now.
  • Bing Selfish orbit: Bing Selfish (executive producer) ran his own ensemble projects (Bing Selfish & The Ideals, which also featured Chris Cornetto), and The Murphy Federation.
  • Robert Storey / His Grace The Earl Of Killorglin is a member of Murphy Working Stiffs and apparently the connecting figure around whom the “Murphy” strand of this network coheres.
