Trembling Blue Stars

UK indie-pop band formed by Bobby Wratten after the dissolution of The Field Mice. Discogs artist ID: 133082.

The principal non-Saint Etienne production client for Ian Catt throughout 1996–2004; Catt produced or engineered every Trembling Blue Stars album on Shinkansen Recordings and their final record The Seven Autumn Flowers (2004) on Elefant Records.
