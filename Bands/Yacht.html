American electro/synth pop band, based in Los Angeles, California. Formerly started as a solo project of Jona Bechtolt, a Portland Oregon based musician, then became a duo with the addition of writer Claire L. Evans in 2009. Y.A.C.H.T. stands for: Young Americans Challenging High Technology.

— Discogs artist profile, ID 408531

Overview

YACHT originated as a solo project by Portland, Oregon-based musician Jona Bechtolt around 2003, then expanded into a duo when writer Claire L. Evans joined in 2009; the name is an acronym for Young Americans Challenging High Technology. Based in Los Angeles, the act operates in electro and synth-pop with experimental and leftfield edges. Their catalogue spans three decades — 8 releases in the 2000s, 12 in the 2010s, and 2 so far in the 2020s — with DFA Records serving as their primary home for over a decade (2008–2019, 14 works). Recurring collaborators include Simon Davey (10 shared releases) and Adam Forkner (9).
