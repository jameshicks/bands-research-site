That Petrol Emotion

Northern Irish/London post-punk and alternative rock band. Active 1985–1994; reunited 2008–2010. Discogs artist ID: 92580

Overview

That Petrol Emotion formed in Derry, Northern Ireland in 1985, initially built around two ex-members of The Undertones — brothers John O’Neill (who went by “Sean” within the band) and Damian O’Neill — plus three former members of Derry bands Bam Bam and The Calling and Derry Hitmakers: guitarist Réamann Ó Gormain (also known as Raymond Gorman), drummer Ciaran McLaughlin, and Seattle-born vocalist Steve Mack. Bassist Brendan Kelly joined later and appears on the band’s final material.

They recorded five studio albums across 1986–1993 on Demon Records, Polydor, Virgin Records, and their own Koogat label, exploring a restless fusion of post-punk, garage rock, alternative rock, and — particularly in the early 1990s — elements of dance and sampling that placed them ahead of the Madchester/baggy curve. John O’Neill departed in 1988 after the second album, following which the band shifted further toward dance-influenced sounds. Following dissolution in 1994, four members reunited briefly in 2008–2010 for live dates and festival appearances, and later formed The Everlasting Yeah (2013).

Name variations: T.P.E., TPE, T. P. E.
