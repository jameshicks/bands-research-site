Group Therapy (7)

UK post-punk band formed in Grimsby, December 1981. Discogs artist ID: 1408212

Overview

Group Therapy (7) were a post-punk band from Grimsby, England, formed in December 1981. Their sole documented release is the 7-inch single “Arty Fact” / “Drug Chic” (1982) on Kamera Records (catalog number ERA 011), produced and engineered by Colin Richardson. The band co-produced the record alongside Richardson.

Per liner notes written by member Bill Brewster for the 2021 compilation Shake The Foundations (Militant Funk & The Post-Punk Dancefloor 1978-1984), the original lineup was: Bill Brewster, Greg Harding, Charlie Porter, Roy Bainton, Phil Dover, and Richard Pardy — described as a “child sax prodigy” who was fifteen at the time and went on to become a professional session and live player.

Their track “Arty-Fact” (4:19) — the A-side of that single — appears on Disc 3 of Shake The Foundations, curated by Brewster himself (alongside John Reed). It is one of the deeper obscurities on the compilation, representing the grassroots end of the post-punk dancefloor scene rather than any chart profile.

Label Context

Kamera Records was a small UK independent label formed by Chris Youle (Discogs artist ID: 1665503), active in the early 1980s with 45 releases in Discogs. Its catalog sits firmly in the British post-punk and new wave space: The Fall released two albums on Kamera (Hex Enduction Hour, 1982; Room To Live, 1982), and the label also issued records by Au Pairs (Sense And Sensuality, 1982), Palais Schaumburg, Allez Allez, and Dancing Did. “Arty Fact” (ERA 011) is a 7-inch single; the label’s ERA prefix was used for its singles series alongside the KAM prefix used for albums.
