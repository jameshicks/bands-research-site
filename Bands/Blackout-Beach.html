Blackout Beach

Solo project (and occasional small ensemble) of Carey Mercer, guitarist and vocalist of Frog Eyes. Discogs artist ID: 1082665. Based in British Columbia, Canada. Active 2004–2013 on record.

Bio

Blackout Beach is Carey Mercer’s outlet for more stripped-back, home-recorded material alongside his work in Frog Eyes. The project began with a 2004 CD on Soft Abuse and continued through a lathe-cut 7” (2007), a full-length CD (2008), and a vinyl LP on Dead Oceans (2011), a self-released digital EP (2012), and a final limited LP back on Soft Abuse (2013). Mercer handles all primary instruments and production; collaborators appear across releases but the project is functionally solo.

Discogs lists Dante Decaro (of Frog Eyes and Wolf Parade) as a member alongside Mercer, but Decaro’s only credited performance on a release is bass on the 2013 LP Blues Trip.
