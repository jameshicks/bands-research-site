Restaurant For Dogs

Post-punk / new wave group active ca. 1980, centred on the same personnel as The League Of Gentlemen (2). Discogs artist ID: 746329. Their sole known recordings were released in 2023 as a self-titled CD/digital album on the Shriekback self-released imprint.

Overview

Restaurant For Dogs was assembled by Barry Andrews (keyboards/vocals) between his departure from XTC (1979) and the formation of Shriekback (1982). The group reunites several musicians who had passed through Robert Fripp’s The League Of Gentlemen (2) in 1980: Andrews, Sara Lee (bass), and Kevin Wilkinson (drums). The Discogs profile describes it as “short-lived” and places it “after XTC and before Shriekback — ca. 1980.”

The 2023 release is a posthumous document of those circa-1980 sessions rather than a new recording: Kevin Wilkinson died in July 1999, which confirms the recordings predate the release by more than two decades. The album was released under the Shriekback self-released banner and compiled by Andrews and Bruce McRae, who also contributed liner notes track-by-track. Carlo Asciutti provided additional liner notes (“Honest Words”) and also designed the cover art.

The music spans post-punk, art-rock, and early new wave territory — lengthy, atmospherically dense tracks (“Stepping Out With The Human Animal” runs 8:32, “The Old Tribe” 8:34) alongside shorter, more direct material. The connection to the Fripp/League of Gentlemen orbit and the overlapping Shriekback membership marks this as a key missing link between XTC-era Andrews and the Shriekback sound.

Two recording locations are documented: The Townhouse (London) and Stable Studios. Nick Launay was at the time one of the most in-demand young engineers in the UK post-punk scene, having worked with Public Image Ltd., the Birthday Party, and others in the same period.

Connections and Scene Context

  • The League Of Gentlemen (2) — Andrews, Sara Lee, and Kevin Wilkinson all played in Fripp’s League of Gentlemen in 1980; Restaurant For Dogs is effectively a parallel or closely adjacent project from the same moment
  • XTC — Andrews departed XTC in 1979, immediately before this group
  • Shriekback — Andrews co-founded Shriekback in 1981–82; Carlo Asciutti and Clare Hirst also appear in Shriekback’s membership
  • Robert Fripp — the League of Gentlemen connection runs through Fripp, though he is not a member of Restaurant For Dogs
  • Gang of Four — Sara Lee went on to work extensively with Gang of Four
  • Nick Launay — engineer; Launay’s involvement places this in the same production world as early-80s UK post-punk

The overlap between Restaurant For Dogs and Shriekback membership (Andrews, Asciutti, Hirst) suggests the group was partly a creative laboratory for ideas that fed into Shriekback’s formation.

Open Questions

  • The exact recording dates within “ca. 1980” are not documented in the Discogs entry — the two recorded-at locations (Townhouse, Stable Studios) and the two engineers suggest possibly more than one session or a longer recording span.
  • Why the recordings remained unreleased for over 40 years is unknown from Discogs data; web sources (Andrews interviews, Shriekback site) would be needed.
  • Bruce McRae’s background outside Restaurant For Dogs and Thee Caretakers is not documented on Discogs.
  • The identity and background of “Alberto ???”/Alberto Hernandez (4) is unclear — the question marks in the credit suggest the compilers could not identify a surname at time of release.
  • Danny Crilly (Discogs ID: 4559068) is credited with “Extra Vocals” but is absent from the Discogs members list; his broader background is not documented.
  • Whether any singles or demos circulated informally before the 2023 compilation is unknown.
