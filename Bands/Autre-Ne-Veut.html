Autre Ne Veut

Solo project of Arthur Ashin (real name Arthur Ashin; also credited as Adley Arthur Atkin). American R&B / art-pop musician based in New York. Discogs artist ID: 1609994. Active from 2010; catalogued on Discogs through 2024. Best known for Anxiety (2013, Software (2) / Mexican Summer), produced by Ashin with additional production from Al Carlson, Joel Ford, and Daniel Lopatin (Oneohtrix Point Never).

Overview

Autre Ne Veut began as a bedroom synth-pop project, debuting in 2010 on the cassette/LP imprint Olde English Spelling Bee (catalog OESB-49). The self-titled debut — ten tracks of hazy, lo-fi R&B — was mixed by Chris Coady and mastered by Carl Saff; Ashin handled all composition himself.

In 2011 Ashin moved to Hippos In Tanks for the Body EP (digital only, catalog G010002665865N), a four-track set self-produced and co-mixed with Al Carlson, who from this point became his primary studio collaborator. The same year he co-headlined a 7” with UK ambient duo Seahawks on the Ocean Moon label (catalog OM001), contributing “Don’s Rainbow” (and a dub version) — a disco-leaning one-off that demonstrates the breadth of his output.

Anxiety (2013, Software (2) SFT 013) is the signature release. Recorded and engineered primarily by Al Carlson, with additional engineering from Joel Ford and assistant engineers Davey Jewell and Zac Meyer, the album brought in a wider cast: Joel Ford on drum machine/drum programming and production; Daniel Lopatin on keyboards and additional production; guitarists Andrew Brady and Joshua Wilson; vocalists Jessica Zambri, Cristi Jo Zambri, and Lee Pinkas. Mastered by Joe LaPorta. Art direction by Thunderhorse; packaging design by Rob Carmichael (2). The album’s closer is a cover of Whitney Houston’s “I Wanna Dance With Somebody.” The LP was pressed as a limited, numbered vinyl edition and co-released with Mexican Summer (the parent label of Software (2)).

The release generated a notable remix 12”: Play By Play (Jacques Greene Remix) (2013, Software (2)), issued in seven pressing variants.

Age of Transparency (2015, Downtown DWT70413) was Ashin’s third LP, released on a double vinyl set with nine tracks. No additional credits are documented in Discogs for this pressing beyond the primary artist.

In 2017 Ashin co-produced the debut LP Young by indie-pop duo Overcoats (Arts & Crafts, A&C133), alongside Nicolas Vernhes; Joe LaPorta again mastered.
