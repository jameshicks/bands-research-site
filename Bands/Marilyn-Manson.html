Marilyn Manson

American industrial metal / hard rock band, formed in Fort Lauderdale, Florida in 1989 as Marilyn Manson & The Spooky Kids. Discogs artist ID: 12208. The band is named after its founder and frontman Brian Hugh Warner (stage name Marilyn Manson; Discogs artist ID: 392594), who is considered the project’s sole constant member and creative director. From roughly 2007 onward the project operates effectively as a solo vehicle, with Warner assembling touring and recording lineups as needed.

The band gained broad notoriety through provocative live performances, elaborate shock-rock aesthetics, and a run of commercially successful albums in the 1990s and 2000s. Their sound draws heavily on industrial rock, glam metal, and gothic rock, shifting across albums from the abrasive noise-industrial attack of Antichrist Superstar (1996) through the arena-glam of Mechanical Animals (1998) and the orchestrated grotesque of The Golden Age Of Grotesque (2003) to the blues-inflected hard rock of The Pale Emperor (2015) and Heaven Upside Down (2017).
