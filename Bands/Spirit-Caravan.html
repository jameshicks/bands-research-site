Spirit Caravan

American doom metal / stoner rock band from Potomac, Maryland. Discogs artist ID: 907855. Active 1996–2002, with a reunion in 2014–2016. Originally named Shine (21) before a name-conflict forced the change; took the new name from a song by predecessor band The Obsessed.

The band was formed by guitarist and vocalist Scott “Wino” Weinrich after The Obsessed dissolved in 1994, with bassist/vocalist Dave Sherman (formerly of Wretched) and drummer/vocalist Gary Isom (formerly of Unorthodox). According to Wikipedia, Weinrich credits Sherman and Isom for encouraging him back into music after a period of inactivity. Their primary label was Tolotta Records, run by Joe Lally of Fugazi, who executive-produced their studio records. They also released on MeteorCity and Tee Pee Records. Recurring engineer/producer across the bulk of the catalog was Chris Kozlowski. Cover art for Jug Fulla Sun was painted by Daniel Higgs of Lungfish.

The band split in 2002. Weinrich went on to Place Of Skulls and founded The Hidden Hand; Sherman founded Earthride; Isom joined Pentagram, Valkyrie, and Nitroseed. A reunion in 2014 used Henry Vasquez (Saint Vitus, Blood Of The Sun) on drums in place of Isom, who was initially involved. In 2016 Weinrich announced the Spirit Caravan lineup would become the new incarnation of The Obsessed, which went on to release Sacred (2017, Relapse Records) and Gilded Sorrow (2024, Ripple Music). Dave Sherman died in 2022. (Wikipedia: Spirit Caravan.)

Their song “Dove-Tongued Aggressor” appeared on the soundtrack to the 2005 video game Tony Hawk’s American Wasteland.

Chris Kozlowski (ID: 477862) is the de facto house engineer across the bulk of the Spirit Caravan / Shine catalog, starting with the pre-rename Powertime demo (1997) and continuing through Elusive Truth (2001). His broader Discogs footprint covers Indestroy (1987), The Obsessed (Standing On The Sun, 1992), Earthride (Something Wicked, 2010), and The Hidden Hand (Divine Propaganda, 2003; Mother Teacher Destroyer, 2004; The Resurrection of Whiskey Foote, 2007) — meaning he tracked the Wino universe across all its phases. He also produced a number of DC/Maryland adjacent acts (Fugazi scene adjacents, Stag (2016, Third Man), a range of 2000s heavy/metal records).

Bruce Falkinburg (ID: 420672) appears in The Obsessed’s Discogs member list and handled recording on the final two Spirit Caravan productions. His primary credits are as engineer/producer across DC-area projects; no primary-artist discography as leader on Discogs.

MeteorCity (release_count: 121) is a New Mexico–based stoner/doom label founded 1997 by Jadd Shickler and Aaron Emmel. It released the Dreamwheel EP (1999) and the posthumous The Last Embrace 2xCD (2003), the latter compiling the Shine tracks and all Spirit Caravan studio material. (Wikipedia: MeteorCity.)

Name change: Shine → Spirit Caravan

A 1999 interview with Wino (Ink 19, October 1999, https://ink19.com/1999/10/magazine/interviews/ckiypx-spirit-caravan) states the change was “due to there being a band already called Shine” — no specific band is named. The article does not identify the other Shine band and makes no mention of a legal threat or cease-and-desist; it frames it simply as a name conflict.

Timeline bracketed by: the band was still performing as Shine at The Bottleneck, Lawrence KS, on May 18, 1998 (Twin Cities Metal blog, http://twincitiesmetal.blogspot.com/2012/08/shine-pre-spirit-caravan-may-18-1998.html), and Jug Fulla Sun was released in 1999 under the Spirit Caravan name. The name change therefore occurred between mid-1998 and early 1999 — most likely late 1998.

Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirit_Caravan, accessed 2026-08-08) says only that “another band of the same name threatened legal action,” without naming that band. The claim that the other Shine was “Arizona-based” appears in some web sources but traces to no primary citation found; treat it as unverified until a named interview or press source confirms it.

Conclusion: The exact identity of the other Shine band is unconfirmed by any primary source found. The name change year is 1998 (based on the May 1998 live date as Shine and 1999 debut as Spirit Caravan).
