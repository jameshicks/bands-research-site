Overview

Geisterfahrer are a Hamburg new wave/post-punk band formed in 1979, working across minimal, experimental, and punk-inflected styles. Their catalogue runs from an early-1980s foundation on Konkurrenz Schallplatten and Phonogram through a later cluster on What’s So Funny About.. (1986–1989), with renewed activity in the mid-2000s on Plastic Frog Records and again around 2020. Their most frequent outside collaborator is Holger Hiller, who shares credits on four releases, pointing to deep roots in the Hamburg experimental underground. Member Matthias Schuster later named his Hamburg recording studio after the band.
