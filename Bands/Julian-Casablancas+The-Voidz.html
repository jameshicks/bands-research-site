Julian Casablancas+The Voidz

American rock band fronted by Julian Casablancas of The Strokes. Also listed on Discogs as The Voidz (2). Discogs artist IDs: 3989546 (Julian Casablancas+The Voidz), 6383079 (The Voidz (2)).

Overview

Formed after Casablancas’ 2009 solo album Phrazes for the Young, the band debuted as Julian Casablancas+The Voidz with Tyranny (2014) and has continued as The Voidz with Virtue (2018) and subsequent releases. The sound is considerably more experimental, psychedelic, and abrasive than The Strokes — dense arrangements, noise rock, post-punk, and synth elements. All releases through Cult Records (3).

Key Credits

Tyranny (2014)

Produced and mixed by Shawn Everett. Additional engineering by Ryan Fagman. Mastered by Brian Gardner, Dave Kutch, and Emily Lazar. Artwork by Warren Fu, Liz Hirsch, El Teneen, and Sam Adoquei (cover painting). Released on Cult Records (3) (catno: CLT017-2).

Virtue (2018)

Produced by Shawn Everett (main), with additional production by Alana Da Fonseca and Chris Tabron. Mixed by Andy Wallace and Shawn Everett (most tracks); Ben Baptie (select tracks). Mastered by Ben Baptie. Cover artwork by Felipe Pantone. 15-track double LP on Cult Records (3) / RCA.
