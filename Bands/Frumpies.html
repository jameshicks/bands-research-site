Frumpies

Lo-fi punk/riot grrrl collective formed in Olympia, Washington, 1992. Discogs ID: 276323. Also credited as “The Frumpies” and “The Frumpies Five.” Active 1992–2000 (original run), with a 2020 vinyl reissue marking the end of catalog activity.

Overview

The Frumpies were a loose supergroup drawn from the core Olympia riot grrrl scene: Tobi Vail, Kathi Wilcox, and Billy Karren (all of Bikini Kill), plus Molly Neuman of Bratmobile, and Michelle Mae (later of The Make-Up and Weird War). The band self-consciously operated as a collective — early releases credit all members as “Frumpies Equal” or “The Frumpies Are” — and leadership rotated through the membership on songwriting and lead vocals.

Their first release, the Alien Summer Nights 7” (Chainsaw Records, 1993), featured the four-piece of Vail, Wilcox, Karren, and Neuman. Michelle Mae joined for the Babies And Bunnies 7” (Kill Rock Stars, 1993), making the group a quintet. The Safety First EP (Wiiija Records, 1994) and Tommy Slich (Lookout! Records, 1995) followed before a hiatus. The band reunited for Eunuch Nights (Kill Rock Stars, 1998), the same year the compilation Frumpie One-Piece (Kill Rock Stars) gathered all prior singles onto CD. The final release was the Frumpies Forever EP (Kill Rock Stars, 2000). Kill Rock Stars reissued Frumpie One-Piece on vinyl in 2020 (KRS-662).

Key engineer and producer throughout was Donna Dresch of Team Dresch, who recorded and produced the Alien Summer Nights and Safety First sessions, and handled recording on much of the Frumpie One-Piece compilation (tracks 1–7, 15, 17–20). Slim Moon (Kill Rock Stars founder) recorded the Babies And Bunnies material (tracks 8–12 on the compilation). “B1 – Planet What” on Tommy Slich has a co-writing credit to The Black Light Panthers (Discogs ID: 1075058). “Tell Me” on Frumpies Forever was co-written by the credit entity Jagger-Richards (Discogs ID: 845581).

Wikipedia (via the offline snapshot) gives the formation year as 1992 and the years-active span as 1992–2000; Discogs records first release year as 1993.

Notes:

  • On Babies And Bunnies (1993), all five members credited as “Frumpies Equal” — Billy Karren under the alias Billy The Stinker (Discogs ID: 6434891), Molly Neuman as Molly Germs (Discogs ID: 6434892), Michelle Mae as Michelle Munition (Discogs ID: 6434893).
  • On Alien Summer Nights (1993) and Eunuch Nights (1998), Michelle Mae is absent; lineup is Vail, Wilcox, Karren, Neuman.
  • On Frumpies Forever (2000), Michelle Mae again absent; credited four-piece.

Connections

  • Bikini Kill — Vail, Wilcox, Karren are all founding Bikini Kill members; the Frumpies emerged directly from that orbit
  • Bratmobile — Molly Neuman’s primary band; formed the same Olympia/DC punk nexus
  • The Make-Up — Michelle Mae joined after leaving the Frumpies
  • Weird War — Michelle Mae’s later band (post-Make-Up)
  • Team Dresch — Donna Dresch produced/recorded multiple Frumpies sessions
  • Kill Rock Stars — primary label (four of seven releases); founded by Slim Moon, who also recorded Babies And Bunnies
  • Wiiija Records — UK release of Safety First (1994)
  • Lookout! Records — released Tommy Slich (1995); Molly Neuman later became a co-owner of the label
  • Chainsaw Records — first release Alien Summer Nights (1993)
  • Donna Dresch — recurring engineer/producer; member of Team Dresch, Dinosaur Jr., Screaming Trees
  • Slim Moon — Kill Rock Stars founder; recorded Babies And Bunnies
  • Tobi Vail — also in Some Velvet Sidewalk, The Go Team, Morgan & The Organ Donors
  • Kathi Wilcox — also in The Casual Dots, The Julie Ruin
  • Billy Karren — also in Snakepit (Discogs ID: 614451)
  • Molly Neuman — also in The Peechees, Love Or Perish; managed The Donnas
