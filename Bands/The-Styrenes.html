The Styrenes

New wave / punk band. Cleveland, Ohio, c. 1977–1979. Discogs artist ID: 529193.

Overview

Cleveland new wave group, c. 1977–1979. Anton Fier is listed as a member; their 1977 single “I Saw You” (Mustard (2) label) is the earliest Discogs credit for Fier. Documented in It Came Out Of Cleveland — 1973–1979 Vol.1: Styrenes (Mono-Tone Records, 2022). Connected to the Electric Eels through John Morton and Paul Marotta (both in Electric Eels and The Styrenes), and Jamie Klimek (Mirrors/Electric Eels).

Compilation Appearances

  • Punk 45 - Extermination Nights In The Sixth City! (Cleveland Ohio) (Soul Jazz Records, 2015) — 2 tracks under different names: “Drano In Your Veins” (as Poli Styrene Jazz Band; written by Paul Marotta) and “Everything Near Me” (as Styrene Money; written by Jamie Klimek).
