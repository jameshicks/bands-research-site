The Static

Oct 05, 20265 min read

The Static

Origin: New York City Active: 1979 Genre: No Wave, Art Rock, Experimental Discogs artist ID: 297535 Name variations: Static, The Statics

NYC No Wave trio formed by Glenn Branca after the dissolution of Theoretical Girls. The Static represents the transitional moment in Branca’s career between his punk-adjacent no-wave band and his later guitar symphony ensemble work. Their recorded output consists of two releases from 1979 — a 7” single and a live cassette — both of which have been reissued.

Biography

The Static formed in 1979 when Branca, having ended Theoretical Girls, assembled a new group with bassist/vocalist Barbara Ess and drummer/vocalist Christine Hahn (No Wave). The lineup was leaner than Theoretical Girls — no keyboards — and placed Branca’s guitar work in starker relief.

Both Ess and Hahn had already been part of the overlapping downtown art/music world. Hahn had been a founding member of Daily Life (alongside Branca and Ess), one of the Branca-affiliated groups that performed at the “Bands at Artists Space” showcase in 1978 alongside the bands later captured on No New York. Ess would go on to co-found Y Pants the same year, and later play in the Glenn Branca Ensemble.

The group played venues including Mudd Club, Max’s Kansas City, The Kitchen, and Artists Space — the core circuit for downtown experimental music in the period. Hahn’s Discogs profile also lists The Kitchen and Artists Space as performance locations for this era.

The band produced two original releases in 1979. The first was a 7” single on their own imprint Theoretical Records — the same label used for the 1978 Theoretical Girls single — with two tracks written by Branca. Both sides were produced by conceptual artist Dan Graham and by Mark Bingham. The second release was a split cassette issued by the British sound-art magazine Audio Arts, documenting a live performance at Riverside Studios in London in 1979. Side A features Dan Graham performing his “Performer/Audience/Mirror” piece; Side B is an untitled set by The Static, with Ess on bass and vocals, Hahn on drums and vocals, and Branca on guitar and vocals. The cassette was edited by Audio Arts founder William Furlong, who also conducted an interview with Dan Graham included as an insert.

After 1979, The Static dissolved. Branca moved toward his guitar ensemble work, releasing “Lesson No. 1” on 99 Records in 1980 and building toward the large-scale guitar symphonies for which he became best known. Ess co-founded Y Pants and continued her photography career. Hahn toured Europe with The Static (per her profile), subsequently collaborated on a magazine/record project in Berlin, and became a founding member of Malaria!.

The 7” was reissued in 1995 by Chicago’s Atavistic (cat# alp3345) as part of their Unheard Music Series of archival No Wave material. The Audio Arts cassette was reissued by Brooklyn-based art-book publisher Primary Information in 2016.

Connections

  • Theoretical Records — self-release imprint shared with Theoretical Girls; only three releases documented

  • Mark Bingham — co-producer of the 7”; New Orleans-based musician/producer, member of Social Climbers

  • Atavistic — Chicago experimental/archival label; reissued the 7” in 1995
