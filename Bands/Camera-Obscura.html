Camera Obscura

Scottish indie pop band, Glasgow. Active from mid-1990s.

Overview

Glasgow-based pop band associated with Elefant Records (Spain) and Merge Records (US). Björn Yttling arranged strings on their album Let’s Get Out Of This Country (2006, Merge) and My Maudlin Career (2009, 4AD), and on several related singles including “Lloyd, I’m Ready To Be Heartbroken,” “If Looks Could Kill,” and “French Navy.” Singer: Tracyanne Campbell (see Tracyanne Campbell).
