ICP Orchestra

Discogs ID: 1669446 Name variations: ICP, Instant Composers Pool, Instant Composers Pool Orchestra, The ICP Orchestra Website: http://www.icporchestra.com

Amsterdam-based free improvisation ensemble, an extension of the Instant Composers Pool (ICP) collective founded by pianist Misha Mengelberg, drummer Han Bennink, and saxophonist Willem Breuker in 1967. The ICP Orchestra is the larger ensemble formation of this collective, documented on the Instant Composers Pool label and others.

Discogs separates ICP (artist ID 65739, the collective/trio entity) from ICP Orchestra (artist ID 1669446). The Orchestra typically involves ten or more musicians. Its first release under the Orchestra name was Live Soncino (ICP 022, 1979).
