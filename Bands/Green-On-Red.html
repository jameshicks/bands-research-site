Green On Red

Discogs ID: 329863. Tucson/Los Angeles desert-rock / Paisley Underground band. Active 1980s–2000s.

Overview

Paisley Underground-adjacent band from Tucson/LA. Members include Dan Stuart, Chuck Prophet, Chris Cacavas, Jack Waterson. Brad Johnson (2) contributed cover art to their 2006 live DVD Valley Fever: Live At The Rialto, Tucson, AZ 9/04/05 (Blue Rose Records).
