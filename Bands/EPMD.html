Overview

EPMD — standing for Erick and Parrish Making Dollars — is an East Coast Hip Hop duo formed by Erick Sermon and Parrish Smith in Brentwood, Long Island in 1987. Their signature monotone delivery and heavy funk sampling set them apart from contemporaries, and their catalogue was most prolific across the late 1980s and 1990s, spanning labels including Fresh Records, Sleeping Bag Records, and eventually Def Jam. In the early 1990s they founded the Hit Squad collective, whose roster included Redman, Das EFX, K-Solo, DJ Scratch, and Hurricane G. The duo dissolved and reformed twice before resuming a sustained partnership after 2006, with longtime production associates Charlie Marotta and Ivan ‘Doc’ Rodriguez among their most frequent collaborators.
