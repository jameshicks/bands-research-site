Overview

Dancer (22) are a Glasgow-based band working in indie rock, post-punk, and indie pop. Their entire catalogue sits in the 2020s — six releases from 2023 through 2025 spread across small UK labels including Gold Mold Records and Meritorio Records, as well as the American Happy Happy Birthday To Me Records. The four-member lineup of Andrew Robert Doig, Christopher Taylor (14), Gavin Murdoch, and Gemma Fleet overlaps heavily with a consistent cluster of collaborators — Ronan Fay (9 shared releases) prominent among them — pointing to an active scene network around Glasgow’s current indie and post-punk community.
