Meatmen

Discogs artist ID: 251802
Origin: Washington, DC / Detroit, USA
Active: c. 1980s
Labels: Homestead Records, Touch And Go

Hardcore punk band fronted by Tesco Vee. DC-connected; their 1985 album War Of The Superbikes was produced by Ted Niceley and Jim Crenca, featuring ex-Minor Threat members Brian Baker and Lyle Preslar.
