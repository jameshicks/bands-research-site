X (5)

Discogs ID: 128581
Origin: Los Angeles, California
Active: 1977–present (intermittently)
Name variations: “The X”, “X The Unheard”

Overview

X formed in Los Angeles in 1977 as one of the first wave of Hollywood punk bands. The classic lineup — John Doe (2) (bass, vocals), Exene Cervenka (vocals), Billy Zoom (guitar), and D.J. Bonebrake (drums) — defined the band’s sound: rockabilly-inflected guitar, dual male-female vocal harmonies, and poetic lyrics rooted in Southern California mythology. Their 1978 debut 7” “Adult Books” appeared on Dangerhouse, the key Los Angeles punk single imprint. The first four studio albums (1980–1983) were produced by Ray Manzarek of The Doors, who also played organ on Los Angeles (1980).

X signed to Elektra in 1982 and broadened their sound on Under The Big Black Sun and More Fun In The New World. Billy Zoom departed before Ain’t Love Grand (1985), with Dave Alvin of The Blasters and Tony Gilkyson taking guitar duties on subsequent recordings. See How We Are (1987) featured Dave Alvin and Tony Gilkyson sharing guitar credit alongside keyboards from Benmont Tench of the Heartbreakers. The band went on hiatus after the 1988 live album. The 1993 reunion album Hey Zeus! on Big Life featured a stable lineup of Cervenka, Doe, Bonebrake, and Gilkyson.

After further sporadic reunion activity — including the acoustic 1995 set Unclogged and a 2005 live album — the original four (Cervenka, Doe, Zoom, Bonebrake) returned in earnest with ALPHABETLAND (2020, Fat Possum), produced by Rob Schnapf. Smoke & Fiction followed in 2024 on Fat Possum. As of the vault date, X remains active as a touring and recording band.

X appeared in Penelope Spheeris’s documentary The Decline of Western Civilization (1981). “We’re Desperate” is included on Punk 45 - Chaos In The City Of Angels And Devils (Los Angeles); “White Girl” appears on Blank Generation (A Story Of U.S. - Canadian Punk & Its Aftershocks 1975-1981).

Notes on member IDs:

  • John Doe (2) appears under two Discogs entries: 158024 (primary credits page) and 1565899 (“John Duchac” — realname John Nommensen Duchac). Both share the same aliases and Wikipedia URL; they are the same person.
  • Billy Zoom’s realname is Stuart Tyson Kindell; Discogs lists “Ty Kindell” (artist 6175559) as his alias — the “Ty Kindell” listed as a band member resolves to Billy Zoom himself.
  • Dave Alvin is credited on See How We Are (1987) as a performer alongside Tony Gilkyson. His Discogs member listing for X, and his appearance in Ain’t Love Grand credits, suggests he contributed as a session/interim player rather than a permanent replacement. He departed to pursue his solo career.
