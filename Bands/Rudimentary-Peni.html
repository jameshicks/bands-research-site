British anarcho-punk band centered on Nick Blinko (vocals/guitar) and his disturbing visual art. No More Pain EP (BOOBOO 09, 2008) released on Southern Records. Farce EP (1982, cat. 221984/2) released on Crass Records — one of the label’s key non-Crass releases.

Nick Blinko — Art and Writing Career

Nick Blinko (born 4 September 1961) has a substantial parallel career as a visual artist and author, operating firmly within outsider art / art brut circles.

Visual Art Practice

Blinko works in pen-and-ink on paper, building microscopically detailed compositions through cross-hatching — thousands of tiny flecks and dashes from which emerge faces, skeletons, religious figures, and fragmented text. He describes cross-hatching as “depression incarnate – depth via the depths.” Sessions typically run four to eighteen hours; he draws without magnifying lenses and has described the practice as erasure of “the daunting world.” His process is complicated by his schizophrenia treatment: gallery owner Henry Boxer (Henry Boxer Gallery, London) has stated that Blinko must “wean himself off his medication to create,” leaving him “open to hallucinations” — “he compromises his sanity to produce his art.” (PM Press blog, 2019)

He has been drawing continuously since childhood. From the mid-1980s his output accelerated. His themes draw on history, time, and critique of orthodoxy — the Church, cruelty, diabolical imagery — rendered in what Raw Vision called a “carnivalesque world.” (Raw Vision issue 110, Spring 2022)

Note on “Pagan Fragments” (Brief Discrepancy)

The research brief listed Pagan Fragments (1999) as a Blinko novel. No evidence of this title was found across Wikipedia, PM Press, Zagava Books, Goodreads, Henry Boxer Gallery, or other sources. The novel in his bibliography is The Primal Screamer (1995). “Pagan Fragments” may be a misremembered or conflated title — flagged for verification.
