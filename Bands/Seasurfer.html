Overview

Seasurfer is a Hamburg-based shoegaze and dream pop band founded by guitarist Dirk Ritter (Dirk Knight) and Berlin-based singer Dorian E. Early digital singles earned them a deal with US label Saint Marie Records, and their 2014 debut LP Dive In was mastered by Karl Skivington of Spotlight Kid and by Robin Guthrie of the Cocteau Twins, whom Ritter credits as a mentor. The lineup shifted significantly mid-decade: Apolonia replaced Dorian E. as lead vocalist, Julia Beyer of Chandeen joined, and the rhythm section drew in Volker Zacharias, known from Cassandra Complex and Girls Under Glass. Their catalogue runs from 2013 to the early 2020s, split between Saint Marie Records and Reptile Music.
