Hickoids

Austin/San Antonio country-punk band. Discogs artist ID: 511068. Also recorded as “The Hickoids” and “The Sickoids.”

Co-founded in Austin in 1984 by vocalist Jeff Smith (3) and guitarist Jukebox (2), built around the concept of “hardcore punk meets hardcore country.” Active 1984–1989, continued with varying lineups until 1991, then resumed regularly from 2006 onward. Part of the same Texas underground that produced Jean Caffeine’s All-Nite Truckstop. Releases from 2011 onward on Jeff Smith (3)‘s own Saustex Media label.
