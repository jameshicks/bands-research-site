Overview

The Vacant Lot were a pop punk and power pop group from Brooklyn, New York, whose entire recorded output — six releases — falls within the first half of the 1990s. Shake The Record Label was their primary home (three releases, 1992–1995), though they also appeared on Sympathy For The Record Industry and Japan’s Toy’s Factory. Founding Dictators member Andy Shernoff figures prominently across six shared credits, placing the band firmly in the broader New York punk lineage, and the Caiati family (Mike, Manny, and Albert) are recurring studio collaborators throughout the catalogue.
