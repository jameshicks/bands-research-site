Pastiche (2)

Boston power pop / punk band, active c. 1976–1982. Discogs artist ID: 1288482. Name variations: Half-Pastiche, Pastiche (The Mini-Band).

Overview

Pastiche was the brainchild of Curtis Naihersey (credited onstage and on releases as Mr. Curt), guitarist and songwriter who assembled the first lineup around 1976. The band cycled through multiple configurations before coalescing into a tight five-piece when vocalist Ken Scales — known for his shaved head and idiosyncratic stage presence — joined, appearing first on the B-side of a Mr. Curt solo single on Euphoria Records (“I’m Going Blind,” 1978).

That lineup entered the 1980 WBCN Rock and Roll Rumble, an annual Boston battle-of-the-bands, and advanced to the finals — where they famously defeated a hard rock trio called France in a contest widely characterized in local press as “New Wave vs. Heavy Metal.” The win raised the band’s profile considerably and supported touring up and down the East Coast. Their 1981 reggae-inflected single “Lock It Up” b/w “Like I Always Do” (on Modern Method Records) became one of the defining local singles of the period. Tracks also appeared on Boston rock compilations (“Psycho Blonde” received area airplay).

In 1982 the band expanded to a seven-piece, a move that backfired — the new lineup fragmented quickly, and the group played its last show in April 1982. Scales and bassist Brad Hallen subsequently formed Adventure Set with other musicians. In 1984, Mr. Curt compiled archival recordings spanning 1977–1983 into a cassette release, Past Tense!! ‘77–‘83, on his own Camaraderie Music Cassettes imprint. The “Flash Of The Moment” 7” was later licensed to Soul Jazz Records for inclusion on Punk 45 Vol. 1 - Kill The Hippies! Kill Yourself! (2013).

Connections

  • Mr. Curt — bandleader, primary songwriter, producer. Outside Pastiche: produced Rhythms & Blues (1982) by Men & Volts on Eat Records; produced and performed on Laughing Academy (1990) by Laughing Academy (Dahlia Records, co-production). Solo 7” on Euphoria Records (1978) uses La Peste (3) as backing band on A-side and Pastiche as backing band on B-side.
  • Brad Hallen — bassist who joined Roomful of Blues after Pastiche. Also documented on Gin (1980, Varulven), Solo Loco (1981, BOMP!), Change No Change (1985, Elektra), Hard (1990, Atlantic), and multiple later blues records through 2024.
  • Modern Method Records — Boston punk/new wave label that issued “Lock It Up.” Their 1981–1985 catalog includes This Is Boston Not L.A. (compilation), The Freeze, The Outlets, and others. Pastiche’s win at the 1980 Rock and Roll Rumble (co-sponsored by WBCN) was the direct link to this label relationship.
  • Soul Jazz Records — reissued “Flash Of The Moment” on Punk 45 Vol. 1 - Kill The Hippies! Kill Yourself! (2013), positioning Pastiche within the broader 1973–1980 American underground punk narrative alongside Pere Ubu, Tuxedomoon, Theoretical Girls, and others.
  • La Peste (3) — Boston band whose members (Peter Dayton, Roger Tripp, Mark Andreasson, Ian Kalinosky) served as Mr. Curt’s backing band on the A-side of his 1978 solo single; Pastiche backed him on the B-side. Contemporaneous Boston punk peers.
  • Roomful of Blues — Brad Hallen’s primary post-Pastiche band.
  • Adventure Set — formed by Ken Scales and Brad Hallen after Pastiche dissolved in 1982. No Discogs presence found.
