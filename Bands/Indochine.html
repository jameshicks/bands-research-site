Indochine

French new wave / rock band, formed in Paris in 1981. Discogs artist ID: 52912.

Overview

Founded by Nicola Sirkis and Dominique Nicolas, with early additions of Dimitri Bodianski and Sirkis’s twin brother Stéphane Sirkis (who died in 1999). The band underwent multiple lineup changes; as of 2013 onward, Nicola Sirkis is the only continuous original member. The band found a renewed commercial profile around 2002 with the album Paradize following guitarist / arranger Olivier Gérard (Oli de Sat) joining the lineup.

Dahlberg joined as drummer circa 2013, one of several Swedish musicians absorbed into the French rock world through his post-TINC network.

Members (Post-2013 Era)

  • Nicola Sirkis — vocals, multi-instruments, producer (founder)
  • Oli de Sat (Olivier Gérard) — guitar, keyboards, bass, synth
  • Boris Jardel — guitar, percussion
  • Marc Eliard — bass
  • Ludwig Dahlberg — drums (joined c. 2013)
