The Charlatans

Manchester/Midlands indie rock band. Discogs artist ID: 49023. Formed 1989 in the West Midlands, relocated to Manchester/Northwich. Core members: Tim Burgess (vocals), Rob Collins (keyboards, died 1996), Mark Collins (guitar), Martin Blunt (bass), Jon Brookes (drums, died 2013). Debut single “Indian Rope” on Dead Dead Good Records (1990); debut LP Some Friendly (Situation Two, 1990). Known for Hammond organ-driven sound that placed them at the edge of Madchester and shoegaze. Long career through 1990s–2010s on Beggars Banquet / MCA / Universal.
