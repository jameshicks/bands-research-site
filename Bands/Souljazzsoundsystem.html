Overview

Souljazzsoundsystem is the collective alias under which the staff and associates of Soul Jazz Records compiled a series of Jamaican music compilations between 1998 and 2009 (with a 2026 reissue). The name is used as a credited compiler entity on eight releases, all on Soul Jazz Records, covering ska, rocksteady, reggae, dancehall, dub, and jungle.

The collective is not a performing or recording group. It functions as a house alias — an in-house editorial team rather than a band — reflecting the label’s sound-system culture influences. The Discogs artist page lists the collective’s realname as “Stuart Baker, Scott Bethell, Pete Reilly, Karen Tate, Abi Clarke, Karl Shale, Dean Atkins, Jon Burnip & Kris Jones,” a larger crew than the six individually credited on 200% Dynamite! (1999). Discogs members list (with artist entries): Stuart Baker, Christine Indigo, Ethan Reid, Hayley Willis, Pete Reilly. Scott Bethell also has a Discogs entry (ID: 2038863) but is not formally linked as a group member on the collective’s page.

Stuart Baker is the label’s founder and the through-line compiler on all eight Souljazzsoundsystem releases. Pete Reilly co-compiled 200% Dynamite! (credited) and mastered the majority of the catalog. He is also the mastering engineer of record across the broader Soul Jazz catalog from the late 1990s through the 2020s.

Series Notes

The % Dynamite! series (100% through 600%) is the collective’s signature project — a run of Jamaican music anthologies tracing ska, rocksteady, early reggae, dancehall, and jungle. The numbering does not indicate chronological sequence of the music covered; each volume focuses on a different facet of Jamaican music’s arc. 400% Dynamite! does not appear in the Discogs DB under this credit — it may not exist or may be uncredited.

Nice Up The Dance (2003) is a dancehall/sound-system set outside the numbered series. Dub Echoes (2009) accompanied a documentary film of the same name directed by Bruno Natal, tracing dub music from Jamaica to the UK and Brazil.

Label Context

Souljazzsoundsystem is one expression of Soul Jazz Records’ identity as a London-based sound-system culture outfit as much as a record label. The name evokes Jamaican sound-system tradition — the collective that owns and operates the sound, selects the music, and controls the event. Soul Jazz’s associated retail operation, Sounds Of The Universe, and its compilation practice both draw on this lineage.

The collective’s Jamaican compilation series runs parallel to Baker’s broader compilation work on the label: he was simultaneously compiling Latin, funk, no-wave, and jazz material under his own name, while the Souljazzsoundsystem credit gathered the reggae-specialist work under a collective umbrella with sound-system connotations.

Soul Jazz Records sublabels relevant to this work include Soul Jazz Studio One Series (391810) and the broader label catalog distributed through Universal Sound (5633).
