German heavy metal band from the town of Solingen, originally assembled in the early 1970s by vocalist Udo Dirkschneider.

Their beginnings can be traced back to the late 1960s, when the band got its start under the name Band X. Accept’s lineup has changed over the years, which has included different singers, guitarists, bassists and drummers. Accept have broken up and reformed multiple times. They first split up in 1989, several months after the release of Eat the Heat (which was their only album with Dirkschneider’s initial replacement David Reece), but reformed in 1992 and released three more albums before disbanding again in 1997. After briefly reuniting in 2005, Accept announced their third reunion in 2009, with former T.T. Quick frontman Mark Tornillo replacing Dirkschneider.

Overview

Accept are a German heavy metal band from Solingen, tracing their origins to the late 1960s under the name Band X and coalescing in the early 1970s around vocalist Udo Dirkschneider. Their catalogue peaks sharply in the 1980s — seventeen releases in that decade — across a succession of labels from the early Brain/Polydor era through Portrait, RCA, and Epic during their US breakthrough years. The band fractured and reformed repeatedly: Dirkschneider departed before 1989’s Eat the Heat, the only Accept album recorded with replacement singer David Reece, and a 1992–1997 reunion gave way to another split before brief activity in 2005. A third reunion in 2009 brought former T.T. Quick frontman Mark Tornillo into the fold, and the Tornillo-era band built a sustained second career on Nuclear Blast across seven releases in the 2010s. Lyricist and collaborator Deaffy (Gaby Hauke) is among the most consistent creative presences across the catalogue, with nearly 450 shared credits.
