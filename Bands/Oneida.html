Overview

Oneida are an American experimental rock band from Brooklyn, New York, active since 1997 with a catalogue of roughly 28 releases spanning psychedelic rock, stoner rock, noise, and experimental indie. Their core lineup — Barry London, Bobby Matador, Hanoi Jane, John Colpitts, and Kid Millions — has remained relatively stable across nearly three decades of output. The majority of their catalogue appeared on Jagjaguwar between 1999 and 2018, with more recent work on Joyful Noise Recordings; producer Nicolas Vernhes and engineer Peter Katis are their most frequent studio collaborators, credited across a combined 33 releases.

Notes (web)

Label transition: Jagjaguwar to Joyful Noise Recordings

In an interview published around March 2018, Bobby Matador explained the split with Jagjaguwar directly:

“Jagjaguwar, our label for like 15 years, has grown to such extent that Oneida’s idiosyncratic approach and sound aren’t really a possibility for them to sell the way they’re currently set up as a business. We parted ways amicably, with a lot of respect for each other.”

On the move to Joyful Noise Recordings, Matador added that the band were “loving working with Joyful Noise, who are living up to what we value: honesty, thoughtful approach to creative community, and hard work.”

The transition was announced publicly around November 2017 with the release of the 7” Town Crier b/w Golden Age of the Pariah — described as the first new, non-collaborative Oneida song in two years and a deliberate introduction to the new label relationship. A full album (Romance) followed in 2018.
