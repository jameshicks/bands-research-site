History

A sprawling, experimental post-punk collective from Kent. Their music incorporated saxophones, violin, synths, piano, and multiple female vocalists alongside the core guitar/bass/drums setup — pushing post-punk into wilder, more open territory.

Released singles on both Dining Out Records and Illuminated Records. The Dark World compilation (2022, Upset The Rhythm) gathers their 1979–81 recordings.

Key cross-band connection: Noel Blanden was a Normil Hawaiians member who later joined Behaviour Red — directly linking the two Dining Out bands.

John Peel Session

Recorded: 20 May 1980, Maida Vale Studios, BBC London
Broadcast: 19 June 1980; 7 July 1980; 2 October 1980 (three broadcasts over six months)

Tracks:

  1. The Beat Goes On
  2. Memories
  3. Levels of Water
  4. I Wanna

The session used the lineup of Guy Smith, Jim Lusted, Nick (guitars/vocals), Brian Kealy (drums), and Sue Leeves (backing vocals). The self-produced demo that led to the session also secured a 7” deal. Session tracks released on Dark World (79–81) (Upset The Rhythm, 2022).
