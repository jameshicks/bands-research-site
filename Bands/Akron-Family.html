Akron/Family

Experimental folk / freak-folk / psychedelic rock band formed 2002 in Williamsburg, Brooklyn, New York. Discogs artist ID: 425610. Filed as Akron-Family to avoid Obsidian wiki-link slash conflict.

Overview

Akron/Family formed around 2002 in the Williamsburg, Brooklyn, social scene centred on the “Gimme! Coffee” coffee shop. Their founding four-piece consisted of Seth Olinsky, Ryan Vanderhoof, Miles Seaton, and Dana Janssen — multi-instrumentalists who all sang. The band began sending demos to Young God Records head Michael Gira in 2003 and signed to the label the following year.

Their 2005 self-titled debut pulled together material recorded in their Brooklyn apartment: field recordings of creaking chairs, thunderclaps and untuned television static woven into psychedelic electronics, guitars and glockenspiel. The Wire compared the result to “loose US scene of folk-derived weirdness” with nods to Chicago post-rock. That same year they recorded and toured as the core backing band for Gira’s Angels of Light project — on The Angels Of Light Sing “Other People” (2005) and We Are Him (2007) — while also issuing a collaborative split LP, Akron/Family & Angels Of Light (Young God, 2005).

Ryan Vanderhoof departed amicably sometime between the completion of Love Is Simple (2007) and the start of the US tour to live in a Buddhist dharma center in the Midwest. The remaining trio — Olinsky, Seaton, Janssen — moved to Dead Oceans for Set ‘Em Wild, Set ‘Em Free (2009) and subsequent releases. The band broke up after the Sub Verses (2013) touring cycle, with members pursuing solo and side projects. Miles Seaton (real name Miles Cooper Seaton) died in a single-car accident in February 2021, aged 41 (Wikipedia).
