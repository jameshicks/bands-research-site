Overview

Seefeel are a British electronic/shoegaze group formed in the early 1990s. Their original lineup — Mark Clifford (guitar, sequencing), Sarah Peacock (vocals), Daren Seymour (bass, electronics), and Justin Fletcher (percussion, programming) — debuted on Too Pure with the More Like Space and Plainsong EPs in 1993. Their debut LP Quique (Too Pure, 1993) established a signature sound that collapsed indie guitar abstraction into dub-inflected ambience and rhythm-machine repetition, earning them quick association with the post-rave electronic underground. They moved to Warp Records for the Fracture / Tied and Starethrough EPs (1994) and the LP Succour (1995), and briefly released the Ch-Vox 12” on Rephlex (1996) before going dormant.

Mark Van Hoen was a member through the early Warp period before launching solo work as Locust. Mark Clifford continued as Disjecta on Warp. Seefeel reformed circa 2008–2010, releasing the Faults EP and self-titled LP on Warp (2010). In 2019 they released archival Peel Session recordings. A prolific second phase followed in 2021 with two full LPs (Reduct, Rapture To Rupt) and the retrospective box Rupt And Flex (1994–96). The 2021 LP Rapture To Rupt was recorded in collaboration with Nairobi-based electronic artist KMRU and released on Warp. Their 2024 LP Everything Squared was mastered by Stefan Betke (Pole).
