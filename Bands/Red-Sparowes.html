Red Sparowes

Los Angeles instrumental post-rock band, formed 2003, active through ~2012. Discogs artist ID: 393288. Name variation: Red Sparows.

Overview

Red Sparowes emerged from the Los Angeles heavy/post-rock underground closely connected to Isis and Neurosis’s orbit. The band is entirely instrumental, structuring albums as long-form conceptual narratives — each track title forming a sentence or paragraph of text. Their albums engage Mao’s Great Leap Forward, authoritarian ideology, and environmental catastrophe as thematic territory. Musically they combine post-rock dynamics with pedal steel guitar, giving them a distinctive desert-tinged quality unusual for the genre.

The band operated across two overlapping label contexts: Neurot Recordings (Scott Kelly / Steve Von Till’s imprint) and Robotic Empire for the first two records, before moving to Sargent House for the final LP.

Neurot Recordings is the label run by members of Neurosis (Scott Kelly, Steve Von Till), which explains the Josh Graham connection — Graham played guitar in Neurosis. This gives Red Sparowes a direct institutional link to the Bay Area experimental metal scene even while being an LA band.

Connections

  • Isis: Bryant Clifford Meyer (guitar, keyboards) and Jeff Caxide (bass) were both members. Mike Gallagher (2) (Isis guitarist) guests on the Gregor Samsa split. The Isis / Red Sparowes orbit also overlaps through Aaron Turner and Hydra Head Records, though Red Sparowes did not release on Hydra Head.
  • Neurosis: Josh Graham was the live visual artist / guitarist in Neurosis. Neurot Recordings is Neurosis’s own label, making it natural that Red Sparowes debuted there.
  • Battle of Mice: Josh Graham co-founded Battle of Mice (with Julie Christmas). The Triad split EP documents all three Neurot-adjacent acts together. Julie Christmas also co-wrote the concept for Every Red Heart Shines Toward The Red Sun.
  • Made Out Of Babies: Brendan Tobin, who joined Red Sparowes for Aphorisms, was also in Made Out Of Babies (alongside Julie Christmas). Both bands appear on Triad.
  • Marriages: Dave Clifford (drums) and Emma Ruth Rundle both went on to form Marriages after Red Sparowes wound down.
  • Emma Ruth Rundle: Joined for the final LP; subsequently became a significant solo artist and member of Marriages. Her connection to Sargent House predates Red Sparowes.
  • Grails: Split partners for Black Tar Prophecies Vol. I. Emil Amos (Grails) later became notable for solo work as Holy Sons.
  • Toshi Kasai: Recorded and mixed both late-period releases. Kasai is also known for his work with the Melvins and Big Business.
  • Sonny Kay: Art director on late-period releases. Sonny Kay is also connected to the At the Drive-In / Sparta / Mars Volta world through his work as an art director and label operator (Gold Standard Laboratories).
  • Tim Green (2): Recorded / mixed Every Red Heart Shines Toward The Red Sun. Green ran Louder Studios in San Francisco and is a member of The Fucking Champs / Nation of Ulysses lineage.

Open Questions

  • Why did Jeff Caxide and Dana Berkowitz leave in 2004? Profile says “relocated” but no further detail available in Discogs.
  • Did Andy Arahood remain in the band through the full Fear Is Excruciating cycle, or did he depart before it? (He is credited on the LP but his Discogs group list only shows Angel Hair and Red Sparowes.)
  • Josh Graham’s active membership status: he is not credited on Aphorisms or The Fear Is Excruciating — was this an official departure or a hiatus? His alias IIVII / Suspended In Light (Discogs ID: 2293681) is a separate entity.
  • No compilation appearances found in Discogs. Possible omission in the DB, or genuinely compilation-averse?
  • 2020 reissues of The Fear Is Excruciating on Sargent House — confirmed purely archival: 10th anniversary reissue (March 20, 2020) in multiple limited vinyl colour variants (Yellow/Blue Cloudy Effect/Blue Splatter; Yellow & Green Galaxy; Clear with beer splatter). No reunion activity.
  • Brendan Tobin alias “Bunny (3)” (ID: 624597) — is this a separate performing project or just a nickname variant? He appears as both on the Triad release’s Made Out Of Babies tracks.
  • Label Conspiracy Records (2) (Belgium) — relationship to Neurot / Sargent House unclear; may be a licensed European distribution deal.
