The Cramps

Origin: Sacramento, California (later New York City)
Active: 1976–2009

American psychobilly / horror punk band led by Lux Interior and Poison Ivy. Kid Congo Powers (Brian Tristan) played guitar with the Cramps in the early 1980s, overlapping with his time in The Gun Club. One of the foundational bands connecting punk energy with rockabilly and blues, directly parallel to what the Gun Club was doing.
