The Dresden Dolls

American “Brechtian punk cabaret” duo from Boston, Massachusetts. Discogs artist ID: 220652. Members: Amanda Palmer (piano, vocals) and Brian Viglione (drums). Active mid-2000 onward; indefinite hiatus from September 2008 while Palmer pursued solo work and Viglione played with other bands; reunion activity in the 2010s.

Overview

The Dresden Dolls formed in mid-2000 in Boston, built around the stripped-down pairing of piano and drums. They coined their own genre tag — “Brechtian Punk Cabaret” — a phrase that nodded to the Weimar-era theatrical reformer Bertolt Brecht, whose alienation effects they saw as an antecedent to their own confrontational, theatrically styled performances. The “Dresden” in the name carries deliberate weight: Dresden porcelain dolls are the fragile, high-craft variety, while the city itself was firebombed at the end of World War II in 1945.

They self-released their debut through Amanda Palmer’s own 8ft. Records imprint (catalog: 8ft. 001) after building a live following in the Boston area. The debut was recorded and produced by Martin Bisi at BC Studio, Brooklyn — a meeting point with the New York experimental underground Bisi had been at the centre of since the 1980s. Bisi also contributed electronics (Memoryman pedal) on several tracks and co-arranged the string section on the closing track “Truce.”

In spring 2005 they were taken on as the opening band for Nine Inch Nails — even without Trent Reznor having seen them live beforehand — a pairing that significantly expanded their audience. They subsequently signed to Roadrunner Records and released their second album Yes, Virginia… (2006) and the companion collection No, Virginia (2008) before entering hiatus.

Post-hiatus, Brian Viglione became a working session and touring drummer with a wide range of acts including Inferno Friendship Society, Violent Femmes, Phillip Boa & The Voodooclub, and black tape for a blue girl. Amanda Palmer developed a prolific solo career under the 8ft. Records banner, latterly working with writer Neil Gaiman.

Connections

  • Amanda Palmer — co-founder; piano, vocals, primary songwriter; continued on 8ft. Records as solo artist, and in projects Evelyn Evelyn, Amanda Palmer & The Grand Theft Orchestra, 8in8
  • Brian Viglione — co-founder; drums; later played with Inferno Friendship Society, Violent Femmes, Phillip Boa & The Voodooclub, black tape for a blue girl, and Botanica (2); session work on Nine Inch Nails Ghosts I-IV (2008)
  • Martin Bisi — producer and engineer of debut; BC Studio, Brooklyn
  • Fred Kevorkian — mastering engineer on debut
  • Nine Inch Nails — The Dresden Dolls were the opening act on NIN’s 2005 tour
  • Edward Ka-Spel — 6 shared releases; appeared on Live At The Roundhouse London (2007); Palmer later collaborated with Ka-Spel (I Can Spin A Rainbow, 2017)
  • Thomas Truax — connection via Brian Viglione, who drummed on Truax’s Jetstream Sunset (2015, Psycho Teddy!)
  • Bertolt Brecht — namesake of the “Brechtian” half of their genre tag
  • Meredith Yayanos — violin on A Is For Accident outtake “Will” (BC Studio, engineered by Bisi)
