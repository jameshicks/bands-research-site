Arctic Monkeys

Discogs artist ID: 391170 Origin: High Green, Sheffield, South Yorkshire, UK Active: 2002–present Label: Domino Records (2005–present) Members: Alex Turner (vocals, guitar), Jamie Cook (guitar), Nick O’Malley (bass, 2006–present), Matt Helders (drums) Former Members: Andy Nicholson (bass, 2002–2006) Management: Wildlife Entertainment (Ian McAndrew, Geoff Barradale)

Indie rock band from Sheffield; one of the defining British guitar acts of the 2000s–2020s. Their 2005 debut Whatever People Say I Am, That’s What I’m Not became one of the fastest-selling debut albums in UK chart history. Over seven studio albums spanning 2006–2022 the band moved from raw post-punk to psychedelic rock, Stax-influenced hip-hop production, lounge-inflected art rock, and orchestral pop, while remaining anchored to Domino Records throughout. Also operate under the alias Death Ramps (Discogs ID 986372), used for side-project and informal releases.

Overview

Formation and Early Years (2002–2004)

Arctic Monkeys formed around 2002 in High Green, a suburb of Sheffield. The four founding members — Alex Turner, Jamie Cook, Andy Nicholson (bass), and Matt Helders — self-distributed demo CD-Rs at early shows, a tactic that built a grassroots following before any label involvement. Recordings from this period circulated as bootlegs and file-sharing downloads; Discogs documents an early self-released CD-R, Beneath The Boardwalk (2004, Not On Label), and the Untitled Session demo that ended up in the Domino catalog as a promo item.

Domino Signing and Debut (2005)

Arctic Monkeys signed to Domino Records in 2005. Their debut single “I Bet You Look Good On The Dancefloor” (RUG 212, October 2005) entered the UK charts at number one. Their debut album Whatever People Say I Am, That’s What I’m Not (WIGCD162 / WIGLP162, January 2006 official release, though some pressings are dated 2005) was produced primarily by Jim Abbiss, with one track (“Mardy Bum”) produced by local Sheffield engineer Alan Smyth. Mixed largely by Barny Barnicott and Jim Abbiss. The album sold over 360,000 copies in its first week in the UK.

The EP Who The Fuck Are Arctic Monkeys? (Domino, 2006) and the single “When The Sun Goes Down” (2005) followed quickly. Also in 2006: “Leave Before The Lights Come On” and a live album Live @ Stubbs BBQ Austin Texas.

Bass player Andy Nicholson left the band in 2006 during touring; he was replaced by Nick O’Malley, formerly of The Dodgems (2).

Favourite Worst Nightmare and Continued Ascent (2007)

Favourite Worst Nightmare (WIGCD188, April 2007) saw James Ford step in as producer and co-mixer alongside Mike Crossey. Alan Moulder mixed the bulk of the album. The record was harder and faster than the debut, opening with “Brianstorm” (a standalone single, April 2007). A live film and album, At The Apollo (Domino, 2008), documented the period.

Humbug and the Homme Collaboration (2009)

Humbug (WIGCD220, August 2009) was a deliberate left turn: recorded partly in Joshua Tree, California with Josh Homme producing seven of the album’s tracks and James Ford producing three others. Alain Johannes engineered the Homme sessions. Mixed by Rich Costey. The album was more psychedelic and American-influenced than its predecessors. “Crying Lightning” was the lead single.

Suck It And See (2011)

Suck It And See (WIGCD258, June 2011), produced entirely by James Ford, marked a return to more melodic songwriting with cleaner production. Mixed by Craig Silvey. Engineer was James Brown (2). “Don’t Sit Down ‘Cause I’ve Moved Your Chair” and “The Hellcat Spangled Shalalala” were the key singles.

AM (2013)

AM (WIGCD317, September 2013) was produced by James Ford (tracks 1, 3–12) and co-produced by Ross Orton across much of the album, with Orton also producing the second track outright. The sound drew on hip-hop rhythms and Stax soul, with Josh Homme contributing backing vocals on tracks 3 and 11. John Cooper Clarke’s poem “I Wanna Be Yours” was set to music as the closing track, credited as co-written by Clarke. Mixed by Tchad Blake. Additional guitar on track 8 by Bill Ryder-Jones; keyboards throughout by James Ford. “Do I Wanna Know?” and “R U Mine?” (released as a standalone single in 2012) were the key cuts.

Tranquility Base Hotel + Casino (2018)

Tranquility Base Hotel + Casino (WIGCD339, May 2018) was co-produced by Alex Turner and James Ford, largely recorded at La Frette, Paris and in Los Angeles. More orchestrated and lounge-influenced than any prior record — piano-led, with Turner composing nearly all parts. Ford co-engineered and handled mixing. The concept album format and self-consciously literary lyrics were a significant departure. “Four Out Of Five” was the lead single.

The Car (2022)

The Car (WIGCD455, October 2022) continued the late-period orchestral direction, produced and mixed by James Ford, with Turner contributing string arrangements alongside Ford and Bridget Samuels. Recorded at various locations including sessions engineered by Anthony Cazade and Loren Humphrey. “There’d Better Be A Mirrorball” and “Body Paint” preceded the album as singles. Matt Helders handled the cover photography.
