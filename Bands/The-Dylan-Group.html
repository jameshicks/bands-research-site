The Dylan Group

Discogs ID: 12536
Alias: Dylan & Adam 1 (Discogs ID: 14708)
Name variation: Dylan Group
Origin: US

Overview

Duo formed by Adam Pierce and Dylan Cristy, operating out of the Bubble Core Records orbit. Released seven primary records on Bubble Core (1997–2000) and one on FatCat Records (1999). Also appeared on early Bubble Core compilations under the alias Dylan & Adam 1 (Discogs ID: 14708). The project ran concurrently with Pierce’s Mice Parade and the HiM collective (Doug Scharin’s group).

Cristy’s instrumental role: vibraphone, percussion, Korg MS20 synthesizer. Pierce: drums, marimba, gamelan, guitar, keyboards, production, engineering on most releases.
