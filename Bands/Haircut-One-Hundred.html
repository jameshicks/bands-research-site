Haircut One Hundred

UK new wave / funk pop band formed in Beckenham, London, c. 1980–1981. Discogs artist ID: 140554. Also credited as “Haircut 100” and “ヘアカット100”.

Overview

Haircut One Hundred formed in Beckenham around 1980–1981, led by Nick Heyward (vocals, guitar). The band were closely associated with the early-1980s UK “new pop” movement — angular funk rhythms, jazz-inflected brass, and polished melodies wrapped in radio-friendly production. Signed to Arista, they debuted with “Favourite Shirts (Boy Meets Girl)” in late 1981 and quickly became one of the most commercially prominent UK acts of 1982. Their debut LP Pelican West (1982) reached #2 on the UK charts and spawned three further hit singles. Heyward departed to go solo in 1983, leaving the remaining members — Graham Jones, Les Nemes, Marc Fox (2), Phil Smith, and Blair Cunningham — to record a second album, Paint And Paint (1984), without him. The band dissolved in 1984. They have reformed intermittently from 2004 and released new material in 2024.

Throughout both albums the primary producer was Bob Sargeant, who also produced The Beat (2) and Fine Young Cannibals. All UK singles and Pelican West were on Arista; the post-Heyward singles and Paint And Paint moved to Polydor.
