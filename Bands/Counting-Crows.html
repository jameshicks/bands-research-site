Overview

Counting Crows is an American rock band from Berkeley, California, formed in 1991 by vocalist Adam Duritz and guitarist/producer David Bryson. Beginning as an acoustic duo playing the Bay Area club circuit, the band expanded to a full lineup before being signed to Geffen/DGC by A&R executive Gary Gersh following a bidding war among nine labels in 1992. Their debut album August And Everything After (1993), produced by T Bone Burnett and mixed in part by Scott Litt, became one of the fastest-selling debuts of its era — seven million copies in the US alone — driven by the hit single “Mr. Jones.” The band received two Grammy nominations in 1994 (Best Rock Performance by a Duo or Group, Best New Artist) and won a 1995 American Music Award for Favorite Alternative Artist.

Their sound draws heavily on Van Morrison, R.E.M., and Bob Dylan, blending Duritz’s confessional lyrics with melodic guitar rock and Gillingham’s wide keyboard palette. The band is also known for its restless, improvisatory live performances in which arrangements change significantly from night to night.

Counting Crows left Geffen in 2009 after 18 years and have since recorded independently and on smaller labels. They have sold more than 20 million albums worldwide. An HBO documentary, Counting Crows: Have You Seen Me Lately?, was released December 2025 covering the band’s early rise and the making of Recovering The Satellites. (Source: Wikipedia)

Notes on David Immerglück: A longtime Bay Area friend of Duritz, Immerglück played session on all early Counting Crows records but initially declined full membership, citing obligations to Camper Van Beethoven and Monks of Doom. He joined permanently in 1999 ahead of This Desert Life. His primary-credited discography as an artist is sparse — one live release with Jim Bogios (Jimmy / Immy - Live At Rockwood Music Hall, NYC, 2012) — but his Discogs contributor footprint is wide. Pre-History and Related Projects

  • The Himalayans — Bay Area band, c. 1990–91. Adam Duritz was a member. Wikipedia notes the 1991 lineup included Dave Janusko, Dan Jewett, Marty Jones, Adam Duritz, and Chris Roldan. Marty Jones (bass player) is the “Mr. Jones” the song refers to.
  • Sordid Humor — San Francisco band active 1987–1991; Adam Duritz was a member. Also included Tom Barnes and Toby Hawkins. Wikipedia notes they were on Oedipus Records and Capricorn Records.
  • Adam Duritz solo: Released All My Bloody Valentines (2011, AMBV), a covers album with Facebook-crowd-sourced artwork. Discogs ID: 277507. Only two primary-credited releases on Discogs.
  • David Immerglück with Camper Van Beethoven — reason he initially declined full membership; still active with CVB. Also a member of Monks of Doom.
