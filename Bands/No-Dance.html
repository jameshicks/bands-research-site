No Dance

Discogs ID: 573209. Sydney, Australia. Active 1984. Acoustic side-project formed around the core of Damien Lovelock (vocalist of The Celibate Rifles), with Brett Myers (Died Pretty), Louis Tillett (Wet Taxis), and Bruce Callaway (The New Christs, The Saints (2)). Released one EP, Carnival Of Souls, in 1984 on Hot Records (2). Genres: acoustic, indie rock (Discogs), situated in the Sydney post-punk underground.

Overview

No Dance was convened as an acoustic studio project, not a touring band. The four members each had ongoing primary bands in the Sydney scene — Lovelock and Steedman in The Celibate Rifles, Myers in Died Pretty, Tillett in Wet Taxis and his own solo work, Callaway in The New Christs and earlier in The Saints. The project was described on Discogs as “an Australian acoustic studio band formed as a side project.”

The EP was produced and engineered jointly by David Connor (a prolific Hot Records studio engineer through 1983–89) and Kent Steedman — guitarist of The Celibate Rifles, here credited in a production role alongside his bandmate Lovelock.
