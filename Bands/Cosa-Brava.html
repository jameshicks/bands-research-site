Cosa Brava

Discogs artist ID: 1748582

Bay Area avant-garde ensemble led by Fred Frith, active 2009–2012 with a reunion recording released in 2024. Formed around Frith’s circle of West Coast improvisers and composers, Cosa Brava operates in the space between avant-rock, free improvisation, and chamber music — composed structures giving way to collective improvisation, with strong melodic instincts running underneath the noise. The band released two albums on Intakt Records during their active period, and a third archival/live set on Klanggalerie over a decade later.

The name means “rough thing” or “difficult thing” in Italian — apt for a group that sounds simultaneously accessible and genuinely strange.
