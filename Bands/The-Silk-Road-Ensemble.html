The Silk Road Ensemble

Multi-cultural contemporary world music collective founded by cellist Yo-Yo Ma in 2000 as part of the Silkroad arts organization. Discogs artist ID: 377374. Also credited as “Silk Road Ensemble,” “Silkroad Ensemble,” and “The Silkroad Ensemble.”

The ensemble is a flexible, rotating collective of musicians from Central Asia, the Middle East, East Asia, Europe, and the Americas, convened to explore and fuse musical traditions along the historical Silk Road trade routes. In July 2020, Rhiannon Giddens took over from Yo-Yo Ma as artistic director. Primary labels: Sony Classical (2001–2007), Masterworks (3) (2013–2016), In A Circle Records (2017–2023), Nonesuch (2024).

Overview

Origins and the Silkroad Project (1998–2001)

Yo-Yo Ma first convened the ensemble in 1998 as part of the Silkroad Project, a non-profit arts initiative exploring how cultures interact across trade routes. The formal Silk Road Ensemble emerged in 2000, premiering at the Tanglewood Festival. The debut album Silk Road Journeys (Sony Classical, 2001) — co-credited with Yo-Yo Ma — was produced by Steve Epstein and engineered by Richard King, who would both recur across the ensemble’s early catalog. The record mixed traditional folk melodies from China, Mongolia, Persia, and Armenia with Western chamber writing, featuring instruments as varied as the pipa, shakuhachi, duduk, kamancheh, tabla, and bowed strings.

Sony Classical Era (2002–2008)

The second album, Silk Road Journeys: Beyond The Horizon (Sony Classical, 2004), continued the multicultural chamber format with a similar lineup. The Japanese-market companion Enchantment (Sony Classical, 2004) presented the ensemble in a more intimate acoustic setting, with detailed per-track instrumentation documenting the full breadth of the collective’s members: Wu Man (pipa), Kayhan Kalhor (kamancheh), Sandeep Das (tabla), Kojiro Umezaki (shakuhachi), Wu Tong (sheng, bawu, xun, vocals), Alim Qasimov (vocals, daf), Gevorg Dabaghyan (duduk), Rauf Islamov (kamancheh), Siamak Aghaei (tombak, santur), Shane Shanahan (multiple percussion), Colin Jacobsen (violin), Ljova (viola), and others.

New Impossibilities (Sony Classical, 2007) was a co-production with the Chicago Symphony Orchestra and conductor Miguel Harth-Bedoya. Traditions And Transformations: Sound Of Silk Road Chicago (CSO Resound, 2008) documented a Chicago residency featuring works by Lou Harrison, Ernest Bloch, Sergei Prokofiev, and Byambasuren Sharav; it was produced and mixed by David Frost and mastered by Richard King.

Masterworks Era and the Grammy (2013–2016)

A Playlist Without Borders (Masterworks, 2013) marked a shift in both label and production style. Produced by Jesse Lewis and Jesse Brayman, it was recorded and mixed by Jody Elff. Artistic advisors for the project were listed as Kojiro Umezaki, Johnny Gandelsman, Mark Suter, and Jeff Beecher, reflecting the ensemble’s increasingly member-driven structure. The liner notes were contributed by composer Osvaldo Golijov.

Sing Me Home (Masterworks, 2016) explored American folk traditions — Appalachian, gospel, blues, and immigrant music — and won the Grammy Award for Best World Music Album. Rhiannon Giddens was among the performers contributing to the project’s American vernacular strand.

Leadership Transition and Later Work (2017–present)

The ensemble contributed to the soundtrack of Ken Burns’s documentary The Vietnam War (In A Circle Records, 2017), with Yo-Yo Ma still co-credited. Falling Out Of Time: A Tone Poem In Voices (In A Circle Records, 2020) was a vocal and chamber piece composed and adapted by Osvaldo Golijov, performed without Yo-Yo Ma as the primary credit — marking the ensemble’s independent trajectory. In July 2020, Rhiannon Giddens became artistic director.

Phoenix Rising (Silk Road Project, Inc., 2023) was a live album produced and engineered by Jody Elff, with artwork by Kevork Mourad and art direction by Giddens. American Railroad (Nonesuch, 2024) was co-credited with Rhiannon Giddens and brought together musicians from across the ensemble’s current roster, including Wu Man, Sandeep Das, Kaoru Watanabe, Haruka Fuji, Mazz Swift, and Michi Wiancko, alongside guests such as Pura Fé and Francesco Turrisi.
