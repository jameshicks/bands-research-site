Overview

Orso is a US-based band formed in 1996 and led by Phil Spirito, previously of Rex (Southern Records), HiM (the Doug Scharin collective), and Loftus. The ensemble’s sound centres on prominent banjo, tenor guitar, and tenor banjo alongside chamber instruments — viola, cello, saxophone, glockenspiel — and an experimental approach that incorporates typewriters, toilets, electronics, and other non-musical sound sources. The core lineup through the Perishable years featured Griffin Rodriguez (bass, keyboards), Carlo Cennamo (alto saxophone), Julie Liu (viola, violin), Brian Deck (drums, producer), Ben Massarella (drums, producer), and Gillian Lisee (piano, organ, vocals). The band released three albums on Perishable Records (1998–2004), one on Contraphonic (2008), and a large body of self-released material extending through 2022.

Orso shares considerable personnel overlap with HiM and the broader Chicago post-rock circle: Spirito, Rodriguez, Cennamo, and Liu all appear on HiM records, while Deck and Massarella are the Red Red Meat / Califone rhythm section. Tim Rutili (Califone) contributes guitar and vocals on Long Time By (2000) and Ask Your Neighbor (2008). The band was also credited as “Orso Action” on Califone’s Heron King Blues (2004, Thrill Jockey), providing additional music and performance.
