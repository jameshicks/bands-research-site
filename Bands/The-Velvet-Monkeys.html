The Velvet Monkeys

American noise/garage rock band, Washington D.C. / New York City. Discogs artist ID: 154053.

Overview

Led by Don Fleming, The Velvet Monkeys were active from the early 1980s with a DC punk core before expanding into a sprawling NYC-connected lineup. Their 1990 album Rake (Rough Trade) is the best-known release and features an all-star noise-rock cast: Thurston Moore, J Mascis, Julie Cafritz, Jay Spiegel, John Hammill, and Malcolm Riviera. Fleming is the constant throughout; the band also appears on the 1991 Sub Pop single “Rock The Nation” b/w “Why Don’t We Do It In The Road?“.
