Loose Fur

Chicago supergroup of Jeff Tweedy, Jim O’Rourke, and Glenn Kotche. Discogs artist ID: 466436. Active 2000–2006.

Overview

Loose Fur formed in May 2000 when Wilco frontman Jeff Tweedy invited multi-instrumentalist Jim O’Rourke to play with him at Chicago’s Noise Pop festival. During rehearsals for the May 14 show at the Double Door, O’Rourke brought along drummer Glenn Kotche. The three worked up new songs that later appeared on their self-titled debut. The collaboration proved generative beyond the band itself: Kotche subsequently became Wilco’s permanent drummer, and O’Rourke mixed Wilco’s landmark Yankee Hotel Foxtrot (2002).

The band released two albums on Drag City — a self-titled LP (2002) and Born Again In The USA (2006) — before disbanding. The self-titled was issued in Japan on P-Vine Records in 2002 ahead of the US Drag City pressing.
