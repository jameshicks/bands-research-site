Flow Trio

Discogs ID: 1534954
NYC free jazz trio: Louis Belogenis (tenor/soprano saxophone), Joe Morris (bass), and Rashid Bakr (drums, also credited as Charles Downs). Active from at least 2009. Released on ESP-Disk’ and Ayler Records.

Members

  • Louis Belogenis — tenor saxophone, soprano saxophone
  • Joe Morris — bass
  • Rashid Bakr (Charles Downs) — drums

Note on drummer’s name: The drummer is credited as “Charles Downs” on all Flow Trio releases, but his primary Discogs identity is Rashid Bakr (ID: 323659), with Charles Downs listed as an alias/real name. He is the same person who drums in Other Dimensions In Music and has worked with Cecil Taylor and Billy Bang.
