Magic Lantern

Discogs ID: 977519
Origin: Long Beach, California
Active: 2007–c.2010
Labels: Not Not Fun Records (NNF103, NNF137-LP, NNF190), URCK Records
Genres: Psychedelic Rock, Drone, Surf, Prog Rock

Psychedelic rock band from Long Beach and Whittier, California. Core trio of Philip French, William Giacchi, and Cameron Stallones began jamming in the mid-2000s, inspired by psychedelic rock including German and Japanese 1970s styles (Krautrock lineage explicitly cited). Expanded to five-piece with Chip Knechtel (drums) and Gavin Fort (bass). Released home-studio CD-R demos before performing at the Tee Pee Records showcase in August 2007 and recording with Bobb Bruno in summer 2008.

Closely intertwined with Sun Araw: Stallones was Magic Lantern’s guitarist; Sun Araw’s debut The Phynx (NNF112, 2008) grew from demos originally intended for Magic Lantern. Broke up shortly after Platoon (2010) — no precise date found. Bobb Bruno — who recorded and mixed Platoon — is also a member of Best Coast (and Pocahaunted), placing Magic Lantern at the exact intersection of the Long Beach DIY psychedelic and the indie-pop scenes that would diverge sharply after 2010.

Key Personnel Beyond the Band

Bobb Bruno — recorded and mixed Platoon. Member of Best Coast (with Bethany Cosentino) and Pocahaunted (Amanda Brown’s NNF project). His presence as engineer places Magic Lantern at the crossroads of the Long Beach psychedelic underground and the nascent indie-pop that would become Best Coast.

James Plotkin — mastered High Beams and Platoon; also mastered Sun Araw’s Heavy Deeds and various other NNF releases.

Scene Context

Magic Lantern were a hub of the Long Beach / LA psychedelic underground in the NNF years (2007–2010). Members scattered into multiple post-band trajectories:

  • Cameron Stallones → Sun Araw (solo, Sun Ark Records; Icon Give Thank with The Congos)
  • Bobb Bruno → Best Coast (Domino Records; mainstream indie) and continued session/engineering work
  • The Hop-Frog Kollectiv split situates them in a West Coast improv/experimental world adjacent to but distinct from the NNF noise crowd

The Long Beach home studio (Sun Ark) that Stallones built for Magic Lantern recordings became the infrastructure for all subsequent Sun Araw work.
