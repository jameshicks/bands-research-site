Loftus

Chicago-based collaborative supergroup. Discogs ID: 487274. One self-titled LP on Perishable Records, 1998. Catalog number: PER003. Format: CD.

Overview

Loftus was a one-off collective session drawing eight musicians from the overlapping Chicago underground networks centred on Red Red Meat, Rex, HiM, Tortoise, and Gastr Del Sol. The LP was released the same year Red Red Meat dissolved and Califone debuted — Loftus functions as a snapshot of the entire extended Perishable Records social circle working together in one place, without any single composer credit appearing in the DB.

The record appeared between two otherwise-separate sub-networks: the Red Red Meat core (Rutili, Deck, Hurley, Massarella) and the Rex/HiM axis (Scharin, Spirito, Harvey), with Bundy K. Brown bridging both sides through his ties to Tortoise, Gastr del Sol, Pullman, and his production work on Rex’s 3 (1997, with Deck). The Dan Ryan Ditch reference in track 5 is a Chicago-specific landmark — a nod to the expressway drainage channel on the South Side.

Lineup Networks

The eight performers cluster into two overlapping groups:

Red Red Meat / Califone axis: Rutili, Deck, Massarella, Hurley — the core of both bands active 1990–2000 on Perishable and Sub Pop.

Rex / HiM axis: Scharin, Spirito, Harvey — the central three members of Rex (Southern Records, 1995–1997). Scharin and Spirito both appear on HiM records; Harvey later leads the Curtis Harvey Trio and appears on Pullman.

Bridge: Bundy K. Brown links both axes — he co-produced Rex’s 3 (1997) with Brian Deck, is a founding member of Tortoise and Gastr del Sol, and had worked with the Perishable circle through Pullman. He is the only person on this record who crosses cleanly between both sub-networks.

Connections

  • Red Red Meat — Rutili, Deck, Hurley, Massarella all from RRM
  • Califone — same core members; Loftus appeared the same year as Califone’s debut
  • Rex — Scharin, Spirito, Harvey; Rex’s final record (3, 1997) was co-produced by Brown and Deck
  • HiM — Scharin and Spirito both in HiM; Sworn Eyes (Perishable, 1999) appeared the year after
  • Tortoise — Bundy K. Brown (founding bassist)
  • Gastr Del Sol — Bundy K. Brown (member until 1998 dissolution)
  • Pullman — Brown and Curtis Harvey both members
  • Perishable Records — PER003; the label’s third catalogued release
