Ital (3)

Discogs ID: 2150668
Real name: Daniel Martin-McCormick
Aliases: Relaxer (2), Sex Worker

Electronic producer based in New York. Initiated 100% Silk with the founding release SILK001 (Ital’s Theme, 2011); appeared again on SILK010 (Only For Tonight, 2011) — 5 releases total on the label through 2012. Also collaborated with LA Vampires on Streetwise (NNF235, 2011, Not Not Fun Records).

The Ital name was used for minimal/house-oriented work; Relaxer (2) and Sex Worker are aliases associated with different sonic contexts.

Project Status / Post-2016 Activity

The Ital alias appears to have been retired around 2016–2017 in favour of a new alias, Relaxer (2), under which Martin-McCormick has released consistently from 2017 onward. Multiple current artist bios describe him as “fka Ital”:

  • Resident Advisor biography (as of 2025): “the project of Daniel Martin-McCormick, fka Ital” — lists Concealer (Planet Mu), Coconut Grove (Avenue 66), Hedonic Fatigue (Climate of Fear), EPs Licking and Candy (Lovers Rock Recordings), and a 2024 work Break as Relaxer. No Ital releases post-Tower B. (ra.co/dj/relaxer/biography)
  • Unsound festival 2025 bio: “fka Ital”; current project listed as Relaxer. (unsound.pl)
  • First Floor / Substack profile (2024–25): states he adopted the Relaxer identity in 2016, subsequently exploring “techno, ambient, sound design, freaky bass mutations and hybridized drum & bass.” Historical Ital work described as helping launch “lo-fi outsider house” of the early 2010s before “morphing into something more slippery.” (firstfloor.substack.com)
  • Creative Independent interview, 20 Feb 2025: describes him as “fka Ital” but contains no direct quote from Martin-McCormick explaining the transition or retiring the Ital name. (thecreativeindependent.com)

No post-2016 releases under the Ital name have been identified across Discogs, RA, Bandcamp, or web searches. The Discogs “years_active through 2017” entry is not corroborated by any 2017 release; the trailing year likely reflects the nominal winding-down period rather than an actual release.

Additional post-2016 context:

  • Co-director of Sustain-Release festival, 2017–2021. (ra.co/dj/relaxer/biography)
  • Founded label Climate of Fear (year listed in searches as 2017). (relaxerelaxer.bandcamp.com)
  • Black Eyes reunited 2023, with reissue of self-titled debut and first live shows in ~19 years.
