Jasper Street Co.

Discogs ID: 2105 Also known as: Jasper Street Company (and many spelling variants — see below) Origin: Philadelphia, PA Active: 1995 – present (2019–2021 releases on Nervous Records / Vega Records) Genre: Gospel House, Garage House, Deep House

Overview

Jasper Street Co. is a Philadelphia-based gospel-oriented house music vocal group formed in the mid-1990s. Their name comes from the address of The Basement Boys’ recording studio in Baltimore — 510 Jasper Street. The group’s lineup has shifted throughout the years, with Thomas “Fruity” Roberts, Jr. (credited as Fruity) and Vonita White (also as Vonita Jenkins) documented as the longest-serving members. Other vocalists who appear across their catalog include Tracy Hamlin, Wondress Hutchinson, Martin Wilson, Sandy Hamlett, Rodney Hamlett, Starr Adkins, LaTasha Spencer, Josane D’Shadeauxman, Vonita Jenkins, and April Lovelist, among others.

The group is primarily associated with DJ Spen (Sean Stafford Spencer) of The Basement Boys, who presented or co-produced most of their releases from 1995 to 2003 on Basement Boys Records. Their sound blends gospel vocal traditions — choir-style backgrounds, devotional lyrics, testifying leads — with the deep, garage-inflected house productions that define the Baltimore/Philadelphia underground of that era.

DJ Spen Collaboration

DJ Spen (real name Sean Stafford Spencer, Discogs ID: 1497) is the central figure behind Jasper Street Co.’s catalog. The “DJ Spen Presents Jasper Street Co.” branding appears on the group’s releases from the 1995 debut A Feelin’ (BB002) through the 1999–2000 singles. Spen is credited as producer, mixer, arranger, and sometimes vocalist across these releases.

The Discogs collaborator graph shows 48 shared releases between DJ Spen and Jasper Street Co. — the largest single overlap in their respective catalogs. After the “Presents” era wound down circa 2000, later Jasper Street releases (e.g. Another Day, Got Me Going) list Spen in remixer or background roles rather than the headline “Presents” format.

The group’s name itself derives from the Basement Boys studio address (510 Jasper Street, Baltimore), cementing the structural connection between the group and Spen’s production home.

Ann Nesby Collaboration

Praisin His Name (2002, BBR-052) is a joint release crediting “Jasper Street Co. featuring Ann Nesby.” Ann Nesby (Discogs ID: 41641, real name Lula Ann Nesby) is a Chicago-based R&B/gospel singer and former lead singer of Sounds Of Blackness (until 1995). On this release she takes lead vocals; Jasper Street Co. members (Tracy Hamlin, Fruity, Martin Wilson, Sandy Hamlett, Vonita Jenkins, April Lovelace, Biblical Jones) provide backing vocals. Production by DJ Spen & Karizma (as the DJ Spen & Karizma duo). The Discogs collaborator graph shows five shared releases between Ann Nesby and Jasper Street Co.
