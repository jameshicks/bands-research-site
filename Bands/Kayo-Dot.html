Kayo Dot

Avant-garde progressive/chamber metal band. Discogs artist ID: 476407

Overview

New York-based avant-prog/chamber metal ensemble led by Toby Driver, who also fronted maudlin of the Well. Mia Matsumiya is also a member. Both performed on Gregor Samsa (3)‘s Rest (2008) and Over Air (2009), creating a notable link between the Richmond chamber post-rock scene and Kayo Dot’s New York avant-metal world.
