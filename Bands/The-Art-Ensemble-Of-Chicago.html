The Art Ensemble Of Chicago

AACM free jazz collective, formed Chicago 1969. Discogs artist ID: 97538.

Overview

Grew out of The Roscoe Mitchell Art Ensemble (precursor, mid-1960s). Core members at formation: Roscoe Mitchell (reeds), Lester Bowie (trumpet), Malachi Favors (bass), Joseph Jarman (reeds), Don Moye (drums/percussion, joined 1970). The group was affiliated with the AACM (Association for the Advancement of Creative Musicians), formed in Chicago in 1965 by Muhal Richard Abrams and others.

Phillip Wilson was an early member of the precursor Roscoe Mitchell Art Ensemble before the group coalesced into the AEC and relocated to Paris in 1969.

The group moved to Paris 1969–1971 and recorded extensively for BYG Actuel and other European labels before returning to the US.
