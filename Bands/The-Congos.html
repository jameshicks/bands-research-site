The Congos

Discogs ID: 10577
Origin: Jamaica
Active: c.1977–early 1980s; reformed mid-1990s–present

Jamaican roots reggae vocal group. Formed in the second half of the 1970s by Cedric Myton and Congo Ashanti Roy (Roydel Johnson); Watty Burnett added to handle deeper chords. Recorded at Lee “Scratch” Perry’s Black Ark Studios. Split in the early 1980s; reformed mid-1990s.

Collaborated with Sun Araw and M. Geddes Gengras on Icon Give Thank (RVNG Intl., FRKWYS09LTD, 2012) — experimental dub/ambient crossover that placed their vocal style over electronic production. 6 shared Discogs releases with Sun Araw.

Members: Cedric Myton, Congo Ashanti Roy (Roydel Johnson), Watty Burnett, Devon Russell, Kenroy Fyffe, Lindberg Lewis
