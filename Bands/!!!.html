!!!

Pronounced: “Chk Chk Chk”
Discogs ID: 207714
Origin: Sacramento, California (later New York City)
Active: 1996–present
Genres: Dance-punk, post-punk, funk

Dance-punk collective formed Sacramento 1996, evolved from punk band The Yah Mos. Originally an 8-piece with a horn section, percussion-heavy arrangements, and overlapping vocals. Relocated to New York City circa 2006. Core constant: vocalist Nic Offer and guitarist/bassist Tyler Pope, who also both served in sister project Out Hud simultaneously. Three members (Nic Offer, Tyler Pope, Justin Van Der Volgen) bridged both bands during Out Hud’s run.

Label arc: self-released CDR (1997) → Gold Standard Laboratories (2000–2003) → Touch And Go + Warp Records (2003–2005) → Warp exclusively (2007–present).

Jerry Fuchs — Death and “The Hammer”

Death (November 8, 2009)

Jerry Fuchs (born December 31, 1974; full name Gerhardt Fuchs) died at Bellevue Hospital, New York City, at approximately 3:30–3:33 a.m. on Sunday, November 8, 2009, aged 34. Wikipedia: Jerry Fuchs, as of 2025-06-27

Circumstances: Fuchs and another man (later identified in press as Alessi) became trapped in a malfunctioning freight elevator at 338 Berry Street, Williamsburg, Brooklyn, while attending a benefit event — The Uniform Project charity fundraiser raising money for education for underprivileged children in India. The building at 338 Berry Street had outstanding code violations on record with the NYC Department of Buildings, including a citation for “Failure to Maintain Elevator.” After approximately 20 minutes, a party organiser and a person associated with the building helped pry the elevator doors open between floors. Alessi jumped first and landed safely; Fuchs jumped next and initially landed, but the hood of his sweatshirt caught on the elevator mechanism and jerked him back, pulling him into the shaft. He fell the equivalent of approximately five stories. Splice Today, November 2009; Village Voice, November 10, 2009; Wikipedia

Birth year note: The Arthur Magazine memorial (arthurmag.com, November 2009) gives 1974–2009; at least one early press report cited 1975. Wikipedia gives December 31, 1974 as the confirmed birth date.

Other band affiliations at time of death: Turing Machine, The Juan MacLean, Maserati, and live/session work with LCD Soundsystem and MSTRKRFT.

”The Hammer” on Strange Weather, Isn’t It? (2010)

“The Hammer” is track 9 (closing track, 4:54) on Strange Weather, Isn’t It?, released August 24, 2010 on Warp. It is the sole track on the album featuring Fuchs on drums; the remaining tracks use Paul Quattrone (tracks 1, 3, 6–8) and Guy Licata (tracks 1–5). Wikipedia: Strange Weather Isn’t It, as of 2025-06-27

The track was recorded before Fuchs’ death on November 8, 2009; the album was completed and released posthumously in 2010, making this Fuchs’ final studio credit with !!!. Contemporary reviews single out “The Hammer” as an album highlight — Consequence of Sound called it “one of the best songs !!! has ever written,” noting “a thick synth melody” and “clarifying boldness.” Consequence of Sound, August 2010

No band or label statements found regarding the specific circumstances of completing “The Hammer” after Fuchs’ death. Nic Offer, in a 2010 interview with Fact Magazine, acknowledged the loss but declined to elaborate: “so much of what you say just turns into this mechanical thing that you spit out, and I don’t want my memories of him to turn into a soundbite that I can’t feel anymore.” Fact Magazine, May 2010

Posthumous credits on other bands: Fuchs also appeared posthumously on Maserati’s Pyramid of the Sun (2010) and Turing Machine’s What Is The Meaning Of What (2012). Wikipedia: Jerry Fuchs
