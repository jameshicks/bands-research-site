Golden Shoulders

Discogs ID: 1075317 Origin: Sacramento, California Active: 2002–2019 Primary format: CD (2002–2004), 7” (2005–2006), LP (2019)

Overview

Indie-pop/rock project led by Sacramento vocalist, guitarist, and multi-instrumentalist Adam Kline. Golden Shoulders is notable for its rotating cast of collaborators drawn from the early-2000s Sacramento/Bay Area indie scene — including Joanna Newsom, Josh Klinghoffer (later of Red Hot Chili Peppers), Neal Morgan, and Deathray members Todd Roper and Dana Gumbiner. Kline recorded, mixed, and primarily wrote all releases himself.

Four official records across 17 years: two Doppler Records full-lengths (2002, 2004), a UK lathe-cut 7” (2005), a Welcome Home Records EP (2006), and a self-released LP in 2019.

Second CD. 12 tracks. Produced by Dana Gumbiner and Golden Shoulders (credited). Adam Kline: arranged, rhythm guitar, piano, percussion, vocals, photography; also credited as producer. Dana Gumbiner: engineer, mixed by, vocals. Tim Pratt: mastered by.

Design: Adam Kline and Keara Fallon. Photography: Adam Kline, Alison Schmidt, Keara Fallon.

Twenty Bees 7” — Twenty Bees, 2005 (BEE17)

UK lathe-cut 7”. 5 tracks: “Let My Burden Be,” “1995 Instrumental,” “A Short And Fleeting Thing” (side A); “Golden Soldiers,” “Seventeen Bees” (side B). No personnel credits in Discogs. Limited edition.

Kin — Welcome Home Records, 2006 (HOME 003)

7” EP. 5 tracks including two labelled as demos: “Be Warned (Demo)” and “There’s No Reason (Demo).” Tracks: “Friends And Family,” “Daughters,” “Golden Rules,” “Be Warned (Demo),” “There’s No Reason (Demo).” No personnel credits in Discogs.

LP; self-released. 14 tracks. Released after a 13-year gap since Kin.
