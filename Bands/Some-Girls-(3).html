Some Girls (3)

Discogs ID: 446202
Origin: San Diego, California
Active: ~2001–2007
Labels: Deathwish Inc., Three One G, Epitaph Records

Noise-punk / hardcore band formed around Justin Pearson and Wes Eisold, both operating under aliases (Pearson as himself; Eisold credited as Chrome Jesus). Active roughly 2001–2006, with six releases across three labels culminating in a major-distributed album on Epitaph. Part of the overlapping San Diego underground centred on Pearson and The Locust’s extended network.

Key Releases

All My Friends Are Going Death (Deathwish, 2004)

CD, 15 listed tracks plus a hidden medley at position 69 (tracks 16–68 silent). Grindcore/hardcore/punk. Includes a cover of “No Fun” (The Stooges, written-by credit). Mastered/mixed by Paul Miner, Sal (2), and Steven Kitchens. Artwork by D. Sant and Nick Lennen; design by Jacob Bannon.

Tracklist (main): “All My Friends Are Going Death” / “Gonna Set My Soul On Fire” / “Now Only Memories Run On Railway Tracks” / “The Rains” / “His N’ Hers” / “Up To Our Hips” / “Sex And Glue” / “Red Cuts Through Black Hearts” / “Some Girls Have All The Fuck” / “Queens Without Kings” / “Blues Singer” / “Aligula” / “Pale Pink Vodka Veins” / “No Fun” (Stooges) / “Up To Our Hips Again.”

The DNA Will Have Its Say (Three One G, 2005)

7-track mini-CD (Minimax format, enhanced). Experimental/punk. Recorded by Sal Gallegos; mixed by Dan Maier; design by Nick Pritchard. Title track features additional vocals by Karen O (Yeah Yeah Yeahs).

Tracklist: “I Need Drugs” / “A Sick Cult” / “Me & My Blasphemy” / “The DNA Will Have Its Say” (feat. Karen O) / “Don’t Hate Me Just ‘Cause I Hate You” / “Nazi Rodeo” / “Hot Air Balloons.”

Heaven’s Pregnant Teens (Epitaph, 2006)

14-track album (enhanced CD, includes “Warm Milk” video). Produced and recorded by Alex Newport. Experimental/hardcore/noise. Includes a cover of “Religion II” (Public Image Ltd, written-by credit). Gabe Serbian contributes additional musicianship on closing track “Deathface” (9:09). Photography and design by Nick Pritchard and Chris Woo.

Tracklist: “Beautiful Rune” / “Hot Piss” / “Dead In A Web” / “Warm Milk” / “You’ll Be Happier With Lower Standards” / “Ex Nuns/Dead Dogs” / “Totally Pregnant Teens” / “Bone Metal” / “Marry Mortuary” / “Religion II” (PiL) / “Skull’s Old Girlfriends” / “Retard And Feathered” / “Deathface” (feat. Open Questions

Dissolution

The band’s breakup was announced on October 23, 2007, roughly 21 months after Heaven’s Pregnant Teens was released (January 2006). No extended public statement was issued by any member; the announcement came through a brief note on Deathwish Inc.’s site: “Word from various members of Some Girls is that they have officially and quietly put the band to rest.” (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Some_Girls_(California_band), as of 2026-10-10, citing the Punknews article of 2007-10-23)

Justin Pearson, in a 2009 interview, gave the only on-record member explanation: “It was time as the band had run its course.” (deadlegpress.wordpress.com)

Wes Eisold has spoken in later interviews about preferring solo/full-control projects (Cold Cave) over collaborative band structures, saying he “eventually stopped doing American Nightmare and Some Girls and wanted to make [his] own music.” No single interview contains a specific explanation for the Some Girls dissolution beyond this general preference for solo work. (Search-result snippet only; source interview not directly verified — treat as background context, not a citable direct quote.)

In summary: no documented specific trigger (creative differences, label difficulties, member conflicts) has been stated publicly. The available record is consistent with a quiet, mutual winding-down once the Epitaph album cycle concluded.
