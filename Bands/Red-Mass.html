Red Mass

Montreal psych/garage/experimental collective built around guitarist and singer Roy Vucino (a.k.a. Choyce, Discogs artist ID: 1113164). Active since 2008. Discogs artist ID: 1592519. Releases span Alien8 Recordings, Slovenly Recordings, No Coast, Goodbye Boozy Records, and a string of European garage imprints. The lineup is fluid — Vucino is the sole constant — but longtime participants include bassist/electronic musician Jean-Sébastien Truchy, vocalist Hannah Lewis, drummer Riccardo Lucchesi, and multi-instrumentalist Susil Sharma.

Overview

Red Mass debuted in 2008 with a 12” EP on Psychic Handshake Recordings (PSY-002), recorded by Choyce and Montreal musician Sultan Chalabi. A second 7” the same year on Wallride Records (“Too Many Parties”) established the project’s low-fi, high-velocity aesthetic. The early period (2008–2010) is characterised by a cluster of split and EP releases: a split 10” with German punk band Strange Attractor on P. Trash Records (2009), a 10” EP on Red Lounge Records (Germany), a 12” EP on Semprini (Canada), and a 10” on Rococo Records (2010) — all self-recorded by Choyce.

The split 7” on Alien8 Recordings with Duchess Says (2009, cat. no. not listed on Discogs) placed Red Mass within the Montreal experimental underground alongside the label’s broader post-punk and noise-rock roster.

Through 2011–2015 the band continued releasing singles and EPs on a mix of Italian (Goodbye Boozy Records), Canadian (Mammoth Cave Recording Co., Zaxxon Records), and American (Certified PR Records, Solid Sex Lovie Doll Records) labels. The 2012 single “Television Personalities” b/w “Kill It From The Inside” on Mammoth Cave (MCR024) and the 2014 “White Nights” 7” on Zaxxon (ZR007) are representative of this phase.

The 2016 EP Rouge N.2 on Slovenly Recordings (702-168), a single-sided 12” recorded and mixed by Vucino and mastered by Tim Warren (2), stands out as a particularly concise statement: seven tracks on one side, featuring contributions from Hannah Lewis, Ariane Gruet-Pelchat, and the duo Pouf, with French-language material (“Saletté,” “Noir Et Blanc,” “Confession D’un Chacal”) alongside English tracks.

Kilrush Drive (2019, No Coast NC 33) is the most production-heavy Red Mass record to date, with three producers: Jace Lasek of The Besnard Lakes, Martin Bisi (BC Studio, Brooklyn — Sonic Youth, Swans, Foetus), and Mingo L’Indien (Martin Leblanc of Les Georges Leningrad). Mixing by Sébastien Perry. The album stretches across electronic, darkwave, synth-pop, and post-punk, and is co-produced by both Vucino and Hannah Lewis.

Volume 1. Sweet Blasphemy (2022, Goodbye Boozy Records, cassette) continues the collaborative ethos: guest drumming from King Khan, backing vocals from Hannah Lewis and Bloodshot Bill, production and all instruments by Vucino, with Annie-Claude Deschênes (Duchess Says) contributing beats to one track. A cover of material by Slates (2) appears on one song.

Roy Vucino’s parallel activity across the Montreal underground scene is extensive — he has been a member of at least sixteen other projects, including CPC Gangbangs, Pypy, Les Sexareenos, Honey & Lies, The Cheating Hearts, The Del-Gators, and Les Enfants Sauvages.
