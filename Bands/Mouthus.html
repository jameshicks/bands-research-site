Mouthus

Brooklyn-based noise/experimental duo of Brian Sullivan and Nate Nelson, active 2002–2009. Discogs artist ID: 460035.

Overview

Mouthus formed in Brooklyn in 2002. Their sound sits within the US underground noise and drone-adjacent tradition — untitled tracks, largely improvised or loosely structured, recorded in a lo-fi register. They self-released on their own imprint Our Mouth Records alongside appearances on major underground labels of the period: Ecstatic Peace!, Troubleman Unlimited, Olde English Spelling Bee, Three Lobed Recordings, Load Records, and Important Records (2).

Nate Nelson is also a member of Religious Knives and Lower Dens; Brian Sullivan operates under the alias Eskimo King and has projects including Spectre Folk, United Waters, and numerous others. Both members also appear in Spirit Of The Positive Wind, Beard-o, and White Rock.

Key Releases — Detail

Slow Globes (2005, Troubleman Unlimited, TMU 154)

LP. Six untitled tracks (A1–A3, B1–B3). No additional credits listed. Genres: Experimental, Noise.

Mouthus (2006, Olde English Spelling Bee, OESB-02)

LP, limited and numbered. Two untitled sides. Mastered at Masterdisk (matrix: “FOR KIRSTEN”). One of OESB’s earliest vinyl releases.

For The Great Slave Lakes (2006, Three Lobed Recordings, TLR 029)

CD, limited edition. Seven tracks including the 42-minute closer “Compound My Eyes.” Styles: Industrial, Noise.

Saw A Halo (2007, Load Records, Load 103)

CD. Seven titled tracks. Produced by Mouthus and Samara Lubelski. The most widely distributed Mouthus release; Load Records was a significant Providence-based noise/experimental imprint.

Crippled Rosebud Binding (2006, Music Fellowship, MF-17)

2×LP split with Sunroof! and Double Leopards. Side A: Double Leopards ”…” (14:30). Side B: Mouthus “God Of Moth” (15:45). Sides C–D: Sunroof! tracks (with Marcia Bassett on guitar and Matthew Bower) and a three-way collaborative closing track “Haunches.”
