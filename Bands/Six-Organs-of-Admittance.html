Six Organs of Admittance

Solo guitar project of Ben Chasny. Discogs artist ID: 274469. Active 1998–present.

Overview

Six Organs of Admittance is the primary solo vehicle of Northern California guitarist Ben Chasny. Though nominally a solo project, it has ranged across radically different modes depending on era: lo-fi drone-folk on cassette and CDr, electric psych rock with full bands, solo acoustic minimalism, and algorithmic composition via the Hexadic System. Chasny is the sole constant — all writing and the bulk of recording is credited to him throughout, with collaborators brought in as the work demands.

The project name derives from the Tibetan Book of the Dead. Alternate forms used on releases include “6 Organs Of Admittance,” “Electric Six Organs Of Admittance,” and simply “Six Organs.”

Biography by Era

Origins — Cassette Underground (1998–2001)

Chasny launched the project in 1998 on the small Pavilion label with a self-titled cassette (ID: 788041). Two further Pavilion albums followed in quick succession — Dust and Chimes (1999) and Nightly Trembling (2000). These early releases were self-recorded, acoustic-guitar-led home recordings distributed on cassette and CDr through the underground psych/folk tape network. The debut Pavilion release lists no additional credited personnel beyond Chasny.

An important early split/cassette, Somewhere Between Her Shoulder And God (2000), appeared on the tiny Warm Freedom Of Tongue label. The Manifestation (2000, Ba Da Bing!) marked the first release with notable outside names: David Tibet (David Tibet) of Current 93 participated as a performer alongside Jennifer Juniper Stratford and Utrillo Kushner.

Songs From The Entoptic Garden Volume Two (2001, Time-Lag Records) was a split with Charalambides (Charalambides).

Holy Mountain Period (2002–2004)

Dark Noontide (2002) was the first album on Holy Mountain (label ID: 40094), the San Francisco heavy psychedelic imprint. The album is all-Chasny — “All Sounds Made By Ben Chasny” — a solo electric and drone record. Holy Mountain had already issued a repress of Dust and Chimes (2000). For Octavio Paz (2003, Time-Lag Records) continued the solo approach: Chasny played electric and acoustic guitar throughout, self-recorded.

Also during this stretch, Chasny appeared as guitarist on The Wicker Image (1996 recording, issued Holy Mountain, 2002-era), establishing his connection to that label’s broader psych-rock community.

Drag City / Full Band Breakthrough (2005–2008)

School Of The Flower (2005) was the Drag City debut and a significant expansion of the project. Chris Corsano played drums, percussion, and organ across the full album — the first time a major collaborator appeared on a Six Organs LP. The title track included a Gary Higgins (Gary Higgins (2)) cover. Bill Skibbe and Jessica Ruffins recorded the album; Nick Webb mastered. Steve Quenell’s paintings appeared on the cover, beginning a long visual collaboration.

The Sun Awakens (2006, Drag City) introduced producer Tim Green (2) as a key studio collaborator. Further guest players included Al Cisneros (Al Cisneros), Liz Harris (Liz Harris / Grouper), Pete Swanson (Pete Swanson), and Stephanie Volkmar. Green also co-produced the follow-up, Shelter From The Ash (2007, Drag City), a more electric-rock album.

The 2008 release RTZ on Drag City functions as a retrospective compilation of the early catalog.

Orchestral Expansion — Luminous Night (2009–2011)

Luminous Night (2009, Drag City) is the most elaborate Six Organs studio production. Randall Dunn produced and played Korg Poly Six and piano; Matt Chamberlain played drums and percussion; Eyvind Kang contributed viola; Hans Teuber played flute; Tor Dietrichson played tabla; Dave Abramson contributed handclaps. Chasny played guitar, bass, vocals, keyboards, electronics, and buried/excavated 4-track tape.

Asleep On The Floodplain (2011, Drag City) returned to a simpler approach — Chasny credited with “Instruments” throughout; some tracks mixed by Randall Dunn or Mell Dettmer, others by Chasny himself. Elisa Ambrogio appeared as a guest.

Comets On Fire Parallel / Extended Universe

Throughout this period, Chasny was also the guitarist in Comets On Fire, an electric psych-rock band that also recorded for Sub Pop (Blue Cathedral, 2004; Avatar, 2006). The Comets’ rhythm section — Noel Von Harmonson, Ethan Miller, Utrillo Kushner — began to appear on Six Organs work as well.

The Lost Electric Six Organs Of Admittance Album (2009, Silver Currant) documents a more electric/band-oriented mode, with Von Harmonson, Ethan Miller, and Utrillo Kushner all credited.

Parsons’ Blues (2012, Drag City) featured Miller and Von Harmonson again.

Ascent (2012, Drag City) is the most explicitly “band” Six Organs record: Chasny and Tim Green (2) co-produced; Ethan Miller (Ethan Miller) and Noel Von Harmonson (Noel Von Harmonson) on guitars; Ben Flashman on bass; Utrillo Kushner on drums.

Hexadic System (2015–2017)

Hexadic (2015, Drag City) introduced a compositional system Chasny developed and documented separately (the Hexadic System, a method for deriving compositions from a deck of cards). The album was recorded with Charlie Saufley (bass), Rob Fisk (Rob Fisk, bass), and Noel Von Harmonson (drums); recorded by Eric Bauer. A companion Hexadic II appeared the same year. Parallelogram (2015, Three Lobed Recordings) was a split/collaboration with William Tyler (William Tyler).

Burning The Threshold (2017, Drag City) marked a return to solo recording — “All Sounds By Ben Chasny.”

Hermit Hut / Recent Work (2019–present)

Chasny launched the Hermit Hut label (ID: 732429) for smaller / cassette-format releases. Losing Tracks Of Time (2019) and the Paz Bonus (3 Song EP) (2019) appeared there, with Chasny self-recording and mixing throughout.

Companion Rises (2020, Drag City) is another solo album — Chasny credited for all sounds, recording, mixing, and photography.

The Veiled Sea (2021, Three Lobed Recordings) was a collaborative release.

Jinxed By Being (2024, Drag City) is a full collaboration with UK producer Shackleton (Sam Shackleton), both co-writing and co-producing.

Time Is Glass (2024, Drag City) is a solo record — Chasny performing, writing, recording, and photographing.
