Holopaw

Indie folk / country rock band. Gainesville, Florida. Discogs ID: 86454. Active 2001–2013.

Founded in Gainesville by vocalist John Orth and a shifting cast of collaborators. Signed to Sub Pop for the first two albums; later self-released and moved to smaller imprints. Known for a spare, bucolic sound — fingerpicked guitars, pedal steel, trumpet, fiddle — with Orth’s laconic vocals at the center. Gained wider attention through John Orth’s participation in Ugly Casanova’s Sharpen Your Teeth (Sub Pop, 2002), recorded just before Holopaw’s own debut.

Key Connections

Isaac Brock / Ugly Casanova loop: Brock moved to Gainesville FL around 1999–2000 for his girlfriend (a Gainesville native). Orth — born Jacksonville, FSU fine arts grad — was already embedded in the local scene. They met there. When Brock assembled the Ugly Casanova sessions in 2001 at his home in Cottage Grove, Oregon, he invited Orth out. Orth co-wrote and sang on four tracks of Sharpen Your Teeth (2002, Sub Pop). Sub Pop’s own Holopaw artist page confirms the causal chain: the band “came to our attention by way of Isaac Brock (he and Holopaw’s John Orth co-wrote some of our favorite songs on the Ugly Casanova record).” Brock played them the demo and the signing followed. Brock then guested on the Holopaw debut (2003) — vocals on “Igloo Glass,” mandolin on “Pony Apprehension.” Brian Deck produced both records; same engineer team (Colin Studybaker, Laurent Bichara) both sessions.

Nate Walcott — trumpet on Holopaw debut; long-running string arranger and multi-instrumentalist for Bright Eyes (Conor Oberst’s band). Another node connecting Holopaw to the broader Sub Pop / indie folk network.

Fredrick Lonberg-Holm — Chicago-based cellist and improv musician; plays cello on three tracks of the debut.

Dave Lebleu — vibraphone on Quit +/Or Fight; also bass/guitar player in The Album Leaf’s live band. A quiet link between the Holopaw and Album Leaf circles.

Daedelus (Alfred Darlington) — LA electronic producer; bass clarinet on “Curious” (Quit +/Or Fight).
