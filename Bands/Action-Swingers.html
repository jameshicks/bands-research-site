Action Swingers

Garage punk / indie rock band, New York City. Discogs artist ID: 935196. Active late 1980s–1998. Wikipedia: Action Swingers.

Overview

Formed in the late 1980s by singer/guitarist Ned Hayden, the only constant member throughout the band’s history. Julie Cafritz (ex-Pussy Galore (2)) co-founded the band as lead guitarist. Initial lineup also included Johan Kugelberg (drums) and Don Fleming (production, additional drums). Bob Bert (drums, ex-Sonic Youth / Pussy Galore) and Pete Shore (bass; also Unsane) joined for the self-titled debut LP (1991, Primo Scree). J Mascis played drums on two early tracks. Cafritz left in early 1992 to form Free Kitten with Kim Gordon. The band continued with shifting lineups through 1998 (Caroline Records, Wiiija Records, Reptilian Records). A Peel session was recorded at BBC Radio 1 in March 1992.

Wikipedia (AllMusic/Erik Hage): “incendiary garage punk.” NYT: “punk without pretences. Brutally elemental and patently obnoxious.”
