Honey Ltd.

Sep 27, 20265 min read

Honey Ltd.

Discogs ID: 370286 Also known as: The Mama Cats (pre-LHI); Eve (7) (post-LHI) Active: 1968–1969 (LHI period) Label: LHI Records Genre: Sunshine pop, soul, psychedelic rock, vocal

Female vocal quartet from Detroit, signed to Lee Hazlewood Industries, Inc.’s LHI Records imprint. Protegées of Lee Hazlewood. Released one LP and four singles for LHI before dissolving. Their complete LHI recordings were reissued by Light In The Attic in 2013.

Biography

The group originated in Detroit in the mid-1960s. Friends Laura Polkinghorne (later Laura Creamer) and Marsha Jo Temmer met sisters Alexandra (“Sandy”) and Joan Sliwin at Wayne State University. The four began singing together, initially performing covers of Motown hits coming out of their hometown. By 1967 they had cohered into a group and came under the orbit of producer Punch Andrews, who named them the Mama Cats.

In the summer of 1967 they spent two weeks in Los Angeles and were drawn to the city. In January 1968 all four pooled resources and relocated, staying on Temmer’s grandmother’s floor and hitchhiking to their audition with Lee Hazlewood at 9000 Sunset Boulevard. Hazlewood signed them to LHI Records, renamed them Honey Ltd., and served as primary producer.

Their debut single, “Come Down / Tomorrow Your Heart” (LHI-1208, 1968), was arranged and conducted by Ian Freebairn-Smith, with Hazlewood producing. The self-titled LP Honey Ltd. (LHI-12002, 1968) followed — eight tracks of sunshine pop, soul, and psychedelic rock, with sleeve illustration by Frank Morton (3).

A second single, “Louie, Louie” (LHI-45-1216, 1968), was a promotional-only pressing co-produced by Hazlewood and Donnie Owens with arrangement and conducting by Jack Nitzsche. The 1969 singles brought in a second producer: Mike Post handled the A-side of both “Eli’s Coming” (LHI-3) and “Silver Threads And Golden Needles / No You Are” (LHI-12), while Hazlewood continued to produce the B-sides and the group’s own compositions.

The group dissolved by 1969. All four members subsequently recorded and performed together as Eve (7). Laura Polkinghorne (as Laura Creamer) went on to a solo career, joined Bob Seger And The Silver Bullet Band and Twisted Brown Trucker Band-related projects, and recorded well into the 2000s.

Connections

  • Jack Nitzsche — arranger/conductor on the “Louie, Louie” promo single
  • Donnie Owens — co-produced “Louie, Louie” alongside Hazlewood
  • Mike Post — produced both 1969 A-sides
  • Frank Morton (3) — sleeve illustration/painting for the LP
