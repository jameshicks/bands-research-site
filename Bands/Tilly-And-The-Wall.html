Overview

Tilly And The Wall were an indie pop group from Omaha, Nebraska, active 2001–2013. Their five-piece lineup — three vocalists (Derek Pressnall, Kianna Alarid, Neely Jenkins), keyboardist Nick White, and tap dancer Jamie Williams standing in for a drummer — gave the band a distinctive sound rooted in indie rock and folk. Their catalogue skewed heavily toward the 2000s (14 releases), with their final three releases arriving in the early 2010s before the group went quiet. They were closely tied to the Omaha scene: producer Mike Mogis appears on 21 shared credits and the band released material on Team Love Records and Moshi Moshi Records across most of their career before a brief stint on Merge Records near the end.
