Fucked Up

Discogs ID: 168843 Origin: Toronto, Ontario, Canada Formed: 2001 Genre: Hardcore punk, post-hardcore Labels: Deranged Records (2002–2006), Matador Records (2008–2014), Merge Records (2010, 2018–2023), Tankcrimes, Fucked Up Records (self-released), Sub Pop (2025–)

Canadian hardcore punk band formed in Toronto in 2001. Known for increasingly ambitious, long-form concept albums that expand well beyond genre norms, and for members who credit themselves under pseudonyms (see lineup table below). Active continuously through 2025, with the membership largely stabilizing around the core five of Damian Abraham, Mike Haliechuk, Jonah Falco, Sandy Miranda, and Josh Zucker.

Year Of The… Singles Series

An ongoing series of long-form 12” singles tied to the Chinese Zodiac — each typically 15–20+ minutes long, often featuring guest performers. Titles include: Year Of The Dog (2006, Blocks Recording Club), Year Of The Pig (2007, What’s Your Rupture?), Year Of The Rat (2009), Year Of The Ox (2010, Merge), Year Of The Tiger (2012, Matador), Year Of The Dragon (2014, Tankcrimes), Year Of The Hare (2015, Deathwish), Year Of The Snake (2017, Tankcrimes), Year Of The Horse (2021, Fucked Up Records / Tankcrimes), Year Of The Goat (2025, Fucked Up Records / Tankcrimes).
