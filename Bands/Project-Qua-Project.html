Project Qua Project

Experimental duo of David Daniell (guitar) and James Elliott (electronics / bass). New York City. Discogs artist ID: 187058. No releases indexed in Discogs.

The project represents the intersection of Daniell’s long-form electric guitar practice and Elliott’s experimental electronics work. Both members co-founded the NYC avant-garde label Antiopic, which released experimental and electronic music from 2002 through 2011. No recordings by Project Qua Project appear in the Discogs database as of the research date.

Context — Member Activity

David Daniell

Daniell is a New York-based guitarist whose Discogs credits span 1995–2020. His primary solo catalogue appeared on Table Of The Elements (Sem, 2002, AN002; Los Jacintos and The Hideout, 2008) and Thrill Jockey (Sycamore with Douglas McCombs, 2009; Knoxville with Fennesz and Tony Buck, 2010; Versions with McCombs, 2012). He served as concertmaster on Rhys Chatham’s 200-guitar ensemble piece A Crimson Grail (Nonesuch, 2010). Additional engineering and photography credits at Table Of The Elements throughout the 2000s. Member of Apiary (2) (The Tapeworm, 2020).

James Elliott

Elliott is an American experimental electronic musician who records solo material under the aliases Ateleia, James Conduit, Arclike, and Totem Test. He was a founding member of School Of Seven Bells (bass, electronics; 2007 debut EP period), and played bass and synthesizer in Bear In Heaven through their first album Red Bloom of the Boom (Hometapes, 2007) and returned for I Love You, It’s Cool (Dead Oceans, 2012). He currently records as Push For Night with Oliver Chapoy (The Tapeworm, 2021). According to the Wikipedia article on Bear in Heaven, Elliott left Bear in Heaven after the Red Bloom recordings to focus on School of Seven Bells and his solo project Ateleia.

Antiopic (shared label)

Both Daniell and Elliott co-founded and ran Antiopic (label ID 11925), a New York City avant-garde, electronic, and experimental label active from 2002 to at least 2011. The label released works by Daniell, Elliott (as Ateleia), as well as Carlos Giffoni, Jim O’Rourke, Lee Ranaldo, Alvin Lucier, Dion Workman, Ultra-Red, Alan Licht, Oren Ambarchi, and Tetuzi Akiyama among others. Full catalog: 20 releases in Discogs.
