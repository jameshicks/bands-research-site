Eyes

Discogs ID: 310822
Also known as: The Eyes
Origin: Los Angeles, California
Active: late 1970s

Short-lived Hollywood punk band notable for the musicians who passed through. Charlotte Caffey (later of The Go-Go’s) and D.J. Bonebrake (later of X (5)) both appear in the Discogs member list, alongside Joe Nanini and Joe Ramirez (both also in Black Randy & The Metrosquad and Randoms). “TAQN” is included on Punk 45 - Chaos In The City Of Angels And Devils (Los Angeles).

Members (Discogs)

Charlotte Caffey, D.J. Bonebrake, David Brown (21), Jimmy Leach (2), Joe Nanini, Joe Ramirez.

Connections
