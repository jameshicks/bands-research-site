The Cooper Temple Clause

UK post-punk/indie rock band formed in Reading, early 2000s. Disbanded April 2007. Also known as TCTC. Discogs artist ID: 138071

Overview

The Cooper Temple Clause formed in Reading in the early 2000s and released three studio albums across their career. Their first two records — See This Through And Leave (2002) and Kick Up The Fire, And Let The Flames Break Loose (2003) — came out on Morning Records, with Paul Corkett producing the debut and Dan Austin taking the lead on the second. For the third album, Make This Your Own (2006), the band moved to Sequel Records (a subsidiary of Castle Music Ltd.) and worked with producers Chris Hughes and Mark Frith. The band announced their split in late April 2007, with the Head EP released shortly before as their final output.

Name variations in use: Cooper Temple Clause, TCTC.
