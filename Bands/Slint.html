Slint

Louisville, KY post-hardcore / post-rock band. Active 1986–1994; reunions 2005, 2007, 2014. Discogs ID: 56206.

Formed out of the Louisville hardcore scene — several members came directly from Squirrel Bait. Known for unconventional song structures, dynamic soft/loud tension, and largely spoken-word vocals. Their second album Spiderland (1991) is widely cited as a landmark of post-rock and a direct influence on bands ranging from Mogwai to Slipknot. The band dissolved almost immediately after completing it.

Biography

Formation and Louisville Context (1986–1988)

Slint coalesced in Louisville in 1986 around Brian McMahan and Britt Walford, who had already played together in Squirrel Bait (the city’s most prominent teen hardcore act, which also included future Gastr del Sol / Drag City figure David Grubbs). David Pajo and Ethan Buckler rounded out the early lineup. All four were teenagers when the band began.

The Louisville underground circuit — small venues, cassette culture, regional touring — gave Slint their early exposure. King Kong (3), a loose collective project involving McMahan, Walford, Pajo, and Buckler, ran parallel to Slint in this period and shared the same social network.

Tweez and Steve Albini (1989)

The debut album Tweez was recorded in 1988 and released in 1989 on Jennifer Hartman Records (Louisville), engineered by Steve Albini under one of his pseudonyms, “Some Fuckin’ Derd Niffer.” The album features the original lineup: McMahan and Pajo on guitars, Buckler on bass, and Walford on drums. Track titles are given as first names (Ron, Carol, Charlotte, etc.) — reportedly the names of band members’ parents. Guest vocalist Edgar Blossom appears on the track “Warren.”

Shortly after Tweez, Ethan Buckler departed and was replaced on bass by Todd Brashear.

Spiderland and Dissolution (1990–1991)

Spiderland was recorded in late 1990 at River North Studios, Chicago, engineered by Brian Paulson. Unusually, all four musicians are credited as “uncredited” on the original pressing — their names appear in Discogs credits but the original LP sleeve does not list them by role. The cover photograph was taken by Will Oldham, then a Louisville-scene figure before his own recording career. The album was released on Touch And Go in March 1991 (catno T&G LP#64).

The band effectively disbanded upon completing Spiderland. Accounts suggest significant psychological strain during and after the recording process. McMahan and Walford moved to Chicago; Pajo eventually joined Tortoise.

Posthumous Releases (1994–2000)

A 10” EP (two untitled tracks, known informally as Untitled or the “Glenn / Rhoda” EP after the UK promo cassette title) was released on Touch And Go in 1994 (catno TG132), engineered by Albini with Brian Paulson mixing. This material is believed to date from recording sessions around the time of Spiderland. The UK promo cassette version, titled Glen / Rhoda, was released the same year.

“Live Songs” (1997) is a self-released unofficial cassette on the band’s own imprint. Los Fabulosos (2000, Lady Butcher LBCD001) is an unofficial limited CD compiling live recordings from the Tweez-era.

Reunions (2005, 2007, 2014)

The band reunited for live performances in 2005, then again in 2007 — the 2007 tour dates included the debut of a new composition titled “King’s Approach,” performed alongside the Spiderland set and the Untitled 10” material.

The 2014 reunion was the most substantial: it accompanied the release of a major Spiderland box set (Touch And Go) containing a remastered LP, the Bonus Tracks LP of previously unreleased studio outtakes and demos, and a new documentary film about the band’s origins and the Louisville scene. Tracks on the Bonus Tracks LP include “Brian’s Song (Post-Spiderland Demo),” a live cover of Neil Young’s “Cortez The Killer” (Chicago 1989), 4-track vocal demos of “Washer,” “Nosferatu,” and “Pam,” and Evanston riff tape recordings of “Good Morning, Captain” and “Nosferatu Man.”

Later Activity (2019–2024)

In 2019 Slint issued a Record Store Day release, Breadcrumb Trail / Good Morning, Captain (Touch And Go TG366LP), a 45 RPM EP featuring alternate mixes of two Spiderland tracks. The 2024 Tweez 35th Anniversary Edition (Touch And Go) included a companion release, Tweez (Tweethan Mix), and a standalone single Hippie Chick (Tweethan Mix).
