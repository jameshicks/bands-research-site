Biffy Clyro

Scottish alternative rock trio from Kilmarnock. Discogs artist ID: 269159.

Overview

Biffy Clyro formed in Kilmarnock, Scotland in 1995. The band consists of Simon Neil (vocals, guitar) and twin brothers James Johnston (bass, vocals) and Ben Johnston (drums, vocals). They emerged from the post-hardcore underground and spent the first decade of their career on Beggars Banquet, building a devoted cult following with a series of dense, technically complex albums before crossing over to mainstream British rock success with Puzzle (2007) and Only Revolutions (2009), the latter debuting at number one in the UK.

The band relocated to 14th Floor Records (a Warner subsidiary) for Puzzle and have remained within the Warner umbrella since. Their name variations on Discogs include “Biffo Clyro”, “Biffy C.”, “Biffy Crylo”, “Clyro”, and “Screwfish”. Former alias / band name: Screwfish (pre-1995).

Touring members include Mike Vennart (additional guitar, 2010–present), formerly of Oceansize, and Richard A Ingram aka Gambler (keyboards, 2012–present), also formerly of Oceansize.
