History

Origins: Killsadie and Botch (2001–2002)

TAAS emerged from two dissolved Pacific Northwest bands. Killsadie was a Minneapolis post-hardcore band (1997–2001) whose alumni went on to form Minus The Bear, Pretty Girls Make Graves, and TAAS — vocalist Steve Snere and drummer Erin Tate carried directly over. Brian Cook (2) came from Botch, the influential Tacoma mathcore band. The band formed in Seattle in late 2001/2002, debuting with the self-released The Blue Rose EP (2002).

Jade Tree years (2003–2004)

The debut LP This Is Meant To Hurt You (Jade Tree, 2003) was recorded with a five-piece lineup including Joe Preston (2) and Jesse Robertson alongside the eventual core of Cook, Frederiksen, and Snere. Matt Bayles produced, engineered, and mixed; Alan Douches mastered. Erin Tate replaced the departed members for Oxeneers Or The Lion Sleeps When Its Antelope Go Home (Jade Tree, 2004) — the band’s most-pressed album (13 pressings). Bayles again produced; additional musicians included Dave Knudson (guitar, credited as additional musician — his Botch connection to Brian Cook), Ben Verellen (Harkonen, bass), and Demian Johnston (guitar).

Hydra Head and lineup shift (2005–2006)

Like A Virgin (Hydra Head, 2005) was a split with Harkonen — Seattle’s other leading mathcore act — produced by Jack Endino (the original Seattle grunge producer, Nirvana, Mudhoney). Erin Tate left to join Minus The Bear as a founding member; Chris Common took over on drums. Easter (Second Nature Recordings, 2006) was the first record with Common on drums, and he doubled as producer and mixer — beginning what became a long production relationship.

Final records and dissolution (2007–2010)

Gold Diggers / Pink Mammoth (Hydra Head, 2008) — a collaborative EP/split with Pelican (2) — preceded the final LP Tail Swallower And Dove (Suicide Squeeze, 2008). Chris Common produced, engineered, and mixed; Ed Brooks mastered. A split 7” with Russian Circles on Sargent House appeared the same year — a natural pairing given Brian Cook’s parallel membership in both bands. The band broke up in January 2010.

Reunion and archival activity (2014, 2022)

A reunion 7” (Sex Beat / Energy Drink And The Long Walk Home, Suicide Squeeze, 2014) marked a brief return. In 2022 Suicide Squeeze released Duct Tape & Shivering Crows — a compilation collecting rarities and out-of-print material (6 pressings).
