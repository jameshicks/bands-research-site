Garland Of Hours

Washington DC experimental/chamber-folk band, active approximately 2000–2012. Discogs artist ID: 1816428

Led by Amy Domingues (vocals, cello, piano, keyboards), with a rotating cast anchored by DC scene veterans Brendan Canty and Jerry Busher. The project began as Domingues’s solo vehicle and expanded into a fluid ensemble, touching baroque pop, folk, and post-rock across three albums. Closely linked to the Fugazi extended circle but with a distinctly quieter, more chamber-oriented aesthetic.

Overview

According to the Discogs profile, Garland Of Hours formed in 2000 when Amy Domingues began performing keyboard and cello-based songs with a rotating cast of friends, including Brendan Canty, Jerry Busher, Brandon Butler, and Mary Timony. The project appeared in the DC music documentary Burn to Shine before releasing its self-titled debut in 2003. Two subsequent albums followed: The Soundest Serum (2007) and Lucidia (2012).

The group is effectively Domingues’s songwriting project — she engineered and mixed the 2007 album herself and handled most of the multi-instrumental and production work throughout. The lineup varied significantly from record to record.
