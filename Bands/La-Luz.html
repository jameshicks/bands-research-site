La Luz

Seattle-based surf/indie rock band. Discogs artist ID: 2943627 (La Luz (2)). Active on Hardly Art 2013–present.

Overview

La Luz is a surf-inflected indie rock band founded in Seattle, Washington. Led by guitarist and vocalist Shana Cleveland, the band is the flagship and longest-running artist on Hardly Art. Their sound draws on reverb-heavy surf rock, close harmonies, and psychedelic touches. The band has gone through multiple lineup changes since formation but Cleveland has remained the constant.
