Knee Deep

Discogs ID: 261 Members: Sebastian Döring (Sebastian Döring) & Thorsten Freese (Thorsten Freese) Origin: Hamburg, Germany Active: late 1990s – 2010 (primary discography); ongoing label operations Genre: Funky house, soulful house, deep house Labels (own): Kneedeep Recordings (founded 2003), KneeDeep USA (sublabel) Also credited as: Kneedeep, KD, Knee Deep Bros., Knee Deep Brothers, Upper Class, Notus

Overview

Knee Deep is a Hamburg-based house music duo formed by Sebastian Döring and Thorsten Freese (AKA Toddie) in the late 1990s through Hamburg’s club scene. They built their early reputation as remixers — working with major-label pop and dance acts — and as underground producers for the Hamburg labels Records Of Interest (R.O.I.) and Trax Of Interest (T.O.I.), both associated with local house keystone Mousse T. Their most internationally prominent release is “Gotta Have House (Do You Remember)” (2008), a co-production with Baltimore house figure DJ Spen featuring vocalist Biblical Jones on the Knee Deep-operated KneeDeep USA label, with Code Red Remixes also issued on Code Red Recordings.

In 2003 they founded Kneedeep Recordings (Hamburg), with the American sublabel KneeDeep USA later added for US market releases. They also produced Ministry of Sound compilation mixes in the Testament of House series (2003–2005), bringing them to a broader European audience.

Sebastian Döring operates under multiple aliases: Superbird, TBA, and formerly Lovebirds. Earlier he had worked under the Kiez Kidz project with Daniel Klein (AKA Gucciman). Both Döring and Freese co-run the parallel projects Upper Class and Notus (and are listed as aliases of the Knee Deep entity on Discogs, Discogs alias IDs unresolved).

Formation and Early Years (late 1990s – 2002)

Sebastian Döring had already established himself as a remixer under the alias TBA by the time he and Freese met in Hamburg’s club scene. Together with Kid Alex and Daniel Klein, they strengthened the R.O.I. and T.O.I. labels, which were backed by Mousse T.

Their first primary release as Knee Deep (credited “Knee Deep Bros.”) was “The Groove” (Urban, 1999) — a significant early original. The early output also included “Nassau Rules” (Records Of Interest, 2002) and “Nightbirds” (Hustle, 2002).

They quickly built a remix portfolio across European pop and dance:

  • Michael Jackson’s “Rock With You” — Knee Deep Remixe (Echo / Universal, 1998)
  • Shabulle’s “Tha Music” (Urban, 1999)
  • Afterlife’s “(Mucho Mambo) Sway” (House Tempo, 2000)
  • Afro Medusa’s “Pasilda” (Rulin Records, 2000)
  • “Corcovado (Knee Deep Mixes)” (Virgin, 2002)
  • Various Sony Soho Square remixes including “Feel So Good”, “Main Vein”, “I Love Every Little Thing About You” (all 2002)
  • Soulsearcher, ATFC, Olav Basoski, Astrotrax, Johnny Corporate, David Morales, Full Intention, Craig David, Jamiroquai (name-checked in Discogs profile)

Their track “I’ll Be There For You” (Fluential, 2000) was another primary outing, featuring producer credits alongside vocalist contributions.

Label Founding and US Expansion (2003–2008)

In 2003 they co-compiled “Testament Of House” for Ministry of Sound Germany — a showcase for their DJ mix style. A Second Prophecy (2004) and Third Prophecy (2005) followed, cementing their relationship with the Ministry of Sound Germany operation.

Kneedeep Recordings was formally established in Hamburg in 2003. The label’s address: Neue Groeningerstr. 10, 20457 Hamburg. KneeDeep USA (Discogs ID 124381) was added as a US-market sublabel under it.

“Gotta Have House” — The DJ Spen Collaboration (2008)

The most internationally prominent release in the Knee Deep catalog. The track originated as a Knee Deep & DJ Spen co-production featuring vocalist Biblical Jones and additional vocals from DJ Spen, Kelly Tribble Spencer, and Kyle Tribble.

Original release:

  • Gotta Have House (Do You Remember) (KneeDeep USA, 2008)
  • Primary: Kneedeep & DJ Spen feat. Biblical Jones
  • Producers: DJ Spen & Knee Deep
  • Written-By: Gary Ahrens (= Gary Deane), Rodney Hamlett, Sean Spencer (= DJ Spen), Sebastian Döring

Code Red Remixes:

  • Gotta Have House (The Code Red Remixes) (Code Red Recordings, CODE20, 2008)
  • Primary: Knee Deep & DJ Spen (also pressed as DJ Spen / Knee Deep)
  • Additional Producers: DJ Spen & Thommy Davis, Gary Hudgins, Irvin Madden
  • Vocals: Biblical Jones, DJ Spen, Kelly Tribble Spencer, Kyle Tribble
  • Keyboards: Gary Hudgins, Irvin Madden

The master (Discogs master ID 139293) has 10 pressings across the two release configurations — notable for house 12”s.
