The Boom (3)

US post-rock/jazz-rock trio active in 1998, based in the DC-area orbit. Released two albums and one split 7” on Slowdime and Big Top Records (2), all in 1998. The “(3)” disambiguates them on Discogs from other acts of the same name. Discogs artist ID: 1615906.

Overview

The Boom (3) emerged from the same cluster of musicians who populated the late-1990s Slowdime Records scene centred on Washington, DC. The core trio — Fred Erskine (guitar, trumpet, vocals), Booker T. Sessoms III (bass), and Joshua Larue (trombone, keyboards/electric piano) — was rounded out on both albums by drummer J. Carrier and saxophonist Carlo Cennamo. The group shared personnel with The Sorts (LaRue and Erskine; Farrall), HiM (Erskine, LaRue, and Cennamo), and Hoover (3) (Erskine, Farrall). Both full-lengths were engineered by recurring DC-scene figures: Juan Carrera recorded and mixed the debut, while Don Zientara (Inner Ear Studio) recorded Movin’ Out. The split 7” with The Sorts on Big Top Records (2) features a congas cameo by Jon Theodore, who would later drum for The Mars Volta and Queens Of The Stone Age.

The group’s two Slowdime albums are listed as Jazz/Rock and Alternative Rock/Post-Punk respectively on Discogs, reflecting a sound that mixed the horns-and-trombone ensemble writing typical of Slowdime-era post-rock with funkier rhythmic material. No activity is documented after 1998.
