Drive-By Truckers

Discogs ID: 493270
Origin: Florence, Alabama / Athens, Georgia
Active: 1996 – present
Labels: Soul Dump Records · Ghost Meat Records · Lost Highway · New West Records · ATO Records
Name variations: DBT, Drive By Truckers, The Drive-By Truckers

Alternative country / Southern rock band formed in 1996. Co-led by singer-guitarist Patterson Hood and singer-guitarist Mike Cooley, both originally from the Muscle Shoals / Florence, Alabama area. The band relocated early to Athens, Georgia, where it has been based since. Jason Isbell was a member 2001–2007, departing to pursue a solo career. The band’s long-running producer and engineer is David Barbe, who has helmed nearly every studio album.

Biography

Formation and early years (1996–2000)

Patterson Hood and Mike Cooley had been playing together since the late 1980s in the pre-cursor band Adam’s House Cat, active in the Muscle Shoals area. Drive-By Truckers proper formed in 1996 and released their debut 7” — Nine Bullets / Bulldozers And Dirt (1997, Soul Dump Records) — with a lineup of Hood, Cooley, John Neff (pedal steel), Matt Lane (3) (drums), Barry Sell (mandolin), and Adam Howell (bass/vocals). The independent double album Gangstabilly (1998, Ghost Meat Records) followed, then Pizza Deliverance (1999, Ghostmeat/Soul Dump), both produced by David Barbe. The live album Alabama Ass Whuppin’ (2000, Second Heaven Records) documented this period. During these years the rhythm section rotated frequently; Brad Morgan came on as permanent drummer around this time and Earl Hicks contributed bass.

Southern Rock Opera and the breakthrough (2001–2002)

Southern Rock Opera (2001, Soul Dump Records; 2002, Lost Highway) is a double-album concept record meditating on the mythology and contradictions of Southern rock, structured around Lynyrd Skynyrd’s career and plane crash. Patterson Hood wrote the bulk; Mike Cooley contributed several songs; Rob Malone (2) co-wrote two tracks. Personnel featured additional vocalists Kelly Hogan, Anne Richmond Boston, Amy Pike, and Jyl Freed. David Barbe produced and mixed; Dick Cooper (3) co-produced and recorded. The album was initially self-released and then picked up by Lost Highway (a Universal imprint) for a 2002 major-label reissue, giving the band their first national distribution. Artwork by Wes Freed, who became the band’s regular visual artist.

Jason Isbell joined as a third guitarist and songwriter around 2001, in time for the Southern Rock Opera touring cycle.

Decoration Day and The Dirty South — the Isbell era (2003–2005)

Decoration Day (2003, New West Records, NW6047) marked the band’s New West debut. Jason Isbell contributed the title track and “Outfit” as full songwriter credits; the album also featured Spooner Oldham on electric piano and John Neff on pedal steel throughout. Shonna Tucker appeared on bass for the first time. David Barbe produced, recorded, and mixed; Glenn Schick mastered.

The Dirty South (2004, New West Records, NW6058) deepened the historical storytelling approach. Isbell wrote four tracks solo (“The Day John Henry Died,” “Danko / Manuel,” “Never Gonna Change,” “Goddamn Lonely Love”). Barbe produced, with John Agnello co-mixing. The permanent lineup at this point: Hood (vocals/guitar), Cooley (vocals/guitar), Isbell (vocals/guitar), Morgan (drums), Tucker (bass/vocals), Neff (pedal steel/guitar).

A Blessing And A Curse and English Oceans — transitional lineups (2006–2014)

A Blessing And A Curse (2006, New West Records) was the last studio album with Isbell, who left the band in 2007. Following Isbell’s departure, Isbell released the solo album Sirens Of The Ditch (2007, New West Records) and formed Jason Isbell And The 400 Unit.

Brighter Than Creation’s Dark (2008, New West Records, NW6135) was the band’s first without Isbell. The lineup expanded: Hood, Cooley, Morgan, Tucker, Neff, and Jay Gonzalez (keyboards/guitar, full name Jason Gonzalez, joining circa 2008). Spooner Oldham played organ and piano throughout; Shonna Tucker contributed three songwriting credits and lead vocals on “I’m Sorry Huston” and “Home Field Advantage.” David Barbe produced/engineered/mixed; Greg Calbi mastered. Nineteen tracks.

The Big To-Do (2010, ATO Records, ATO0084) — the band’s first on ATO Records. Credits on this pressing list no additional performers beyond the core band.

Go-Go Boots (2011, ATO Records / Play It Again Sam in Europe) followed quickly.

Shonna Tucker departed the band following Go-Go Boots (2011). Matt Patton joined as bassist, credited as a full member from English Oceans onward.

English Oceans (2014, ATO Records, ATO0226LP) — a deliberate return to the dual-guitar/dual-songwriting model. Hood and Cooley each wrote exactly half the tracks. The lineup: Hood, Cooley, Morgan, Jay Gonzalez (keyboards/guitar), Matt Patton (bass), and Michael Patterson (additional guitar). Drew Vandenberg engineered; Barbe produced and mixed; Calbi mastered. Horns arranged by Jay Gonzalez; George Davidson on tenor sax; Adam Courson on trumpet.

American Band and the political turn (2016–2020)

American Band (2016, ATO Records, ATO0335) was explicitly political — addresses gun violence, race, and American identity. Recorded at Sam Phillips Recording in Memphis with Matt Ross-Spang engineering; Barbe producing and mixing; Calbi mastering.

The Unraveling (2020, ATO Records) and The New OK (2020, ATO Records) were recorded and released in close succession, both addressing the political moment. Both produced by David Barbe; The Unraveling tracked partly at Sam Phillips Recording (Matt Ross-Spang recording and mixing several tracks); The New OK similarly split between Sam Phillips and Chase Park Transduction (Athens). Guest on The Unraveling: Cody Dickinson (washboard). Guest on The New OK: Mike Mills (of R.E.M.) backing vocals on “Maria’s Awful Disclosure.”

Welcome 2 Club XIII (2022)

Welcome 2 Club XIII (2022, ATO Records, ATO0601) brought guests Margo Price and Mike Mills (backing vocals), Scott Danbom (fiddle), Jeremy Ivey (harmonica), and Randall Bramblett (tenor saxophone). Produced by David Barbe; Matt Ross-Spang tracked and mixed several tracks.

Note on Matt Pence

The research brief listed Matt Pence as co-producer of Floor It!!! (2020, New West) and recorder of The Straight Hits! (2018, Mute). Discogs shows both of these as other artists’ albums, not DBT releases: Floor It!!! is by The Texas Gentlemen (Discogs 5715080); The Straight Hits! is by Josh T. Pearson (Discogs 149226) on Mute. Pence’s connection to DBT’s orbit is via Jason Isbell: he played drums on Jason Isbell And The 400 Unit (2009, Lightning Rod Records). The Matt Pence page already documents these correctly.
