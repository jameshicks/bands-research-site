The Will Gregory Moog Ensemble

Discogs ID: 4604672 Active: 2015–present Top labels: Mute, Society of Sound Music Leader: Will Gregory

UK ensemble of synthesizer players and wind instrumentalists, led by Will Gregory (of Goldfrapp). Members play vintage Moog synthesizers and related analogue instruments alongside electronic wind instruments. Adrian Utley (Portishead) is a member.
