The Present Moment

Electronic / darkwave / witch-house project based in the United States. Discogs artist ID: 1936775. Name variation: TPM. Active 2010–2022 per Discogs. The project is essentially the vehicle of Scott Milton (producer, musician, drummer, Los Angeles), who is listed as both a member and as an alias on Discogs. Philipp Münch is the other principal collaborator, credited as member and co-writer/producer on the early releases.

Overview

The Present Moment sits at the intersection of witch-house and darkwave electronics. Their debut The High Road (2010) appeared on Houston’s Disaro — one of the foundational witch-house imprints — placing them squarely in that early-internet era scene. Subsequent releases moved through European industrial/EBM channels (Desire Records (4) / Mannequin) and later self-released or through smaller US labels. The collaborative axis shifted over the years: early records were co-written and produced with Philipp Münch (Nuremberg-based electronics veteran, Synapscape); later albums brought in Los Angeles-based collaborators.

The project name “The Present Moment” on Discogs is also listed as an alias for Scott Milton individually, and Scott Milton’s own Discogs entry lists the band as an alias — indicating Discogs treats it as a near-solo vehicle.

Scene Context

The Present Moment emerged directly out of the Houston witch-house scene centered on Disaro. The High Road appeared at CDR 028 in 2010 alongside label-mates White Ring, oOoOO (3), Ritualz, and King Dude — the core Disaro witch-house roster. The collaboration with Philipp Münch (a prolific figure in European industrial/EBM via Synapscape, Ars Moriendi, The Rorschach Garden, etc.) gave the early records a transatlantic dimension, connecting the US witch-house internet scene to the European body-music underground. Later releases on Mannequin (Italy) and Young And Cold Records (Netherlands) reinforced those European industrial/darkwave ties.
