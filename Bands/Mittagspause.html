Mittagspause

Discogs ID: 178954
Also known as: MiPau
Origin: Düsseldorf, Germany
Active: c. 1978–1980
Genres: Punk, New Wave, Post-Punk

One of the earliest German punk bands, Mittagspause formed in Düsseldorf around 1978 and operated on the crest of the Rhine/Ruhr punk scene that fed directly into Neue Deutsche Welle (NDW). Their name translates as “lunch break.” The band released a pair of hard-to-find 7-inch singles on the local Pure Freude and Rondo labels, then broke up around 1980. Key members subsequently formed or joined Fehlfarben, one of the most important NDW bands, and several Mittagspause songs were reworked and appeared on Fehlfarben records.

Career Narrative

Formation and Scene Context (c. 1978)

Mittagspause emerged from the Düsseldorf punk underground in the late 1970s. Düsseldorf was already home to Krautrock innovators (Kraftwerk, Neu!, La Düsseldorf), but Mittagspause were part of a harder, scrappier scene that drew on British punk while developing a distinctly German voice. Gabi Delgado (later DAF) was associated with the scene and co-wrote “Militürk” (credited as “Delgado-Lopez” on the EP), though he was not a full member of the performing lineup.

The band’s vocalist, Peter Hein, performed under the alias “Janie Jones” (a Clash-referencing alias), establishing a sardonic, irreverent tone matched by the band’s clipped, aggressive sound.

Recordings (1979)

The band’s primary studio output dates entirely to 1979. They released two 7-inch records:

“Herrenreiter / Paff” (1979, Rondo / fit 2) — A-side “Herrenreiter” (their signature track) backed with “Paff,” a cover of the folk standard “Puff (The Magic Dragon)” by Yarrow/Lipton/Oldörp. The single was produced by Franz Bielmeier and mixed by Bernd Schmidt. Cover art by Franz and Heike Bielmeier.

“Mittagspause” EP (1979, Pure Freude / CK-1) — A limited-edition double-7-inch (four sides) containing 11 tracks. This is the more expansive document of the band’s sound. Lineup: Markus Oehlen (drums), Franz Bielmeier (guitar), Thomas Schwebel (guitar), Peter Hein (vocals). Produced by Bernd Schmidt and Mittagspause collectively. Writing credits across the EP go to Bielmeier, Oehlen, Hein, Schwebel, with “Militürk” (B2) credited jointly to Bielmeier and Gabi Delgado. “Innen Stadt Front” (B3) written by Peter Stiefermann.

Tracks on the EP include “Testbild,” “3x Nordpol,” “Intelnet,” “X-9200,” “Militürk,” “Innen Stadt Front,” “Deutschland,” “Derendorf,” “Überblick,” “In Der Tat,” and “Ernstfall.” Several of these — “Militürk,” “Herrenreiter,” “Ernstfall” — later resurfaced in reworked versions on Fehlfarben recordings.

Live Recording (1979–80)

“Zensor 20.10.1979” (1980, Not On Label) — A two-sided cassette of untitled live material, recorded at the Zensor venue on 20 October 1979. Unofficial release; no credits beyond the band.

Break-up and Aftermath (c. 1980)

Mittagspause dissolved around 1980. Peter Hein, Markus Oehlen, and Thomas Schwebel went directly into Fehlfarben, which became one of the defining bands of Neue Deutsche Welle — especially their 1980 debut Monarchie Und Alltag. Several Mittagspause compositions were refashioned for Fehlfarben records. Gabi Delgado and Robert Görl had by this point crystallized Deutsch Amerikanische Freundschaft (DAF), which ran in parallel.
