Overview

Vox Populi! is a French ethno-industrial ensemble founded in 1982, working across experimental, ambient, industrial, minimal, and abstract territory. Their catalogue is heavily weighted toward the 1980s, with nine releases in that decade, before a long pause and then renewed activity in the 2010s and 2020s via Nostalgie De La Boue, Emotional Rescue, and Platform 23 Records. The core membership — Arash Khalatbari, Ariel Kyrou, Axel Kyrou, Chester Harlan, and Francis Manne — recurs across releases, with the Vox Man Records imprint serving as the band’s own outlet from their earliest years through to 2014. Close collaborative relationships with related projects Les Plantes and Vox 231 suggest a broader constellation of overlapping entities around the same principals.
