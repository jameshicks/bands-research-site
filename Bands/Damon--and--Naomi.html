Overview

Damon & Naomi are an American dream pop / indie folk duo formed in 1991 in Boston, Massachusetts, by Damon Krukowski (drums, guitar, vocals) and Naomi Yang (bass, keyboards, vocals), both formerly of Galaxie 500. After Galaxie 500 dissolved in 1991 when guitarist Dean Wareham quit, Krukowski and Yang released a handful of tracks they had already recorded — initially under the alias Pierre Étoile on Rough Trade (UK, 1991). Producer Kramer (2) then persuaded them back into the studio, and the debut LP More Sad Hits appeared on Kramer’s Shimmy Disc in 1992.

The duo has simultaneously maintained a parallel band, Magic Hour (1993–1996), a psychedelic rock quartet co-led with Wayne Rogers and Kate Biggar of Crystalized Movements. From 1998’s Playback Singers onward, all recording was done at their own Kali Studios in Cambridge, Massachusetts, and the duo self-produced. Their longest-running external collaborator is Japanese guitarist Michio Kurihara — a member of Ghost (2) — who has appeared on recordings from 1999 through 2021. In 2005 they launched their own imprint, 20|20|20, which has since served as the primary release vehicle for their catalog and selected reissues (including the Galaxie 500 back catalog and a Michio Kurihara solo LP).

Damon Krukowski is also a published poet, essayist, and journalist (Pitchfork) and hosted the 2017 Radiotopia podcast Ways of Hearing. Naomi Yang is an active graphic designer (responsible for all Damon & Naomi and Galaxie 500 packaging), photographer, and filmmaker — her short film Fortune (2013) has screened internationally, and she has directed music videos for Waxahatchee, Julia Holter, Marissa Nadler, Lee Ranaldo, and Steve Gunn, among others. Both studied at Harvard (Krukowski BA 1985, Yang BA 1986); they set up independent book publisher Exact Change in 1989. (Source: Wikipedia, Damon Krukowski; Naomi Yang (musician))

Pierre Étoile

Pierre Étoile is a pre-Damon & Naomi alias, credited on Discogs to Damon & Naomi (and listing only Damon Krukowski as a member on the alias page). The sole primary release is the 1991 UK EP In The Sun on Rough Trade (master ID 226420, release ID 547706) — tracks recorded before Galaxie 500’s final split. A 1997 Elefant Records edition (Pierre Étoile, cat ID 1808776) collected these tracks with a Spanish label credit; the 1997 release also credited the alias as producer and listed Jon Lupfer as engineer.

Connections

  • Damon Krukowski — co-founder; drums, guitar, vocals; Harvard-educated poet and writer; born September 6, 1963
  • Naomi Yang — co-founder; bass, keyboards, vocals; graphic designer and filmmaker; born September 15, 1964; daughter of photographer John Yang
  • Galaxie 500 — predecessor band (1987–1991); Wareham, Krukowski, Yang; releases on Rough Trade
  • Magic Hour — concurrent side project (1993–1996) with Wayne Rogers and Kate Biggar
  • Ghost (2) — collaborative LP (2000, Sub Pop); ongoing connection through Michio Kurihara and Masaki Batoh
  • Michio Kurihara — primary recurring collaborator; electric guitar on With Ghost, The Earth Is Blue, Within These Walls, False Beats, A Sky Record; also appeared on split 7” (1999) and live documents
  • Masaki Batoh — Ghost (2) leader; acoustic guitar on Damon & Naomi With Ghost (2000) and False Beats & True Hearts (2011)
  • Kramer (2) — produced first two LPs; head of Shimmy Disc; later controversial dispute over Wondrous World original masters
  • Shimmy Disc — debut label; Kramer’s imprint
  • 20|20|20 — own label, launched 2004/2005; home of all post-Sub Pop releases and Galaxie 500 back catalog
  • Rough Trade — released Pierre Étoile EP (UK, 1991)
  • Exact Change — independent book publisher founded 1989 by Krukowski and Yang; specializes in 19th–20th century avant-garde literature
