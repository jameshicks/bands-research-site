Maybe It’s Reno

DC-area indie rock project credited to Bridget Cross and George Kuhar, released under the Teenbeat label in 2008. Discogs artist ID: 1964872. Discogs profile: http://www.teenbeatrecords.com/artists/maybeitsreno.html

Overview

Maybe It’s Reno is primarily Bridget Cross’s songwriting vehicle — the Discogs profile states the group is “Bridget Cross and George Kuhar sometimes with Bridget’s Unrest bandmates Phil Krauth and Mark Robinson.” The one documented release, the self-titled album (Teenbeat, April 22, 2008), features Mark Robinson as producer and player on tracks 1–7, alongside Phil Krauth (drums, mellotron) on the same tracks. Tracks 8–10 substitute George Kuhar on guitar/keys and Jordan Strudel on drums, with Kuhar also recording those tracks. All songs are written by Bridget Cross.

Wikipedia’s entry on Unrest (2) frames the album as almost equating to another Unrest reunion: “all of the songs are written by Cross and she also performs all of the vocals.” The project sits squarely within the Teen Beat Records / Teenbeat ecosystem that Mark Robinson built and has operated since 1985.

George Kuhar is also Cross’s partner; Cross’s Discogs name variation “Bridget Cross Kuhar” attests to this. Kuhar’s Discogs footprint is limited to Maybe It’s Reno and The Cloud People.
