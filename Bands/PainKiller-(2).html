PainKiller (2)

Discogs ID: 69896
Active: 1991–present (with hiatuses)
Origin: New York / Birmingham
Genre: Free jazz, grindcore, noise, dub, experimental

Power trio: John Zorn (saxophone, voice), Bill Laswell (bass), Mick Harris (drums, voice, programming). Formed 1991; one of the most confrontational ensembles in the Zorn/Laswell orbit, colliding blast-beat extremity with dub bass and free-jazz improvisation.

Formation and Concept

PainKiller emerged from the downtown NYC / early-90s noise underground. Zorn and Laswell had overlapping circles (Laswell produced for Celluloid; Zorn recorded for Elektra Nonesuch), and Mick Harris — then exiting Napalm Death — brought grindcore drumming into a free-jazz frame. The name was borrowed from the Judas Priest album. Their early records on Earache placed them in the metal catalog but sounded like nothing else on the label.

Discography

Guts Of A Virgin (1991)

Label: Earache — MOSH 45
Format: 12” LP (45 rpm), later CD
Genres: Free Jazz, Grindcore, Noise, Experimental
12 tracks. Debut album; the purest statement of the blast-dub-sax collision. No guests; core trio only.

Buried Secrets (1992)

Label: Earache — MOSH 62CD
Guests: Justin K Broadrick + G.C. Green (Godflesh) on tracks 5 & 10
Follow-up studio record. Broadrick and Green contribute the industrial weight they brought to Godflesh; their tracks are among the heaviest on the record.

Rituals (Live In Japan) (1993)

Label: Toy’s Factory
Guests: Keiji Haino (guitar, vocals); Buckethead (guitar); Kevin Sharp (vocals, on select tracks)
Live document of the Japanese touring run. Haino’s raw guitar and voice extend the noise palette; Buckethead’s cameo is an anomaly in the PainKiller catalog.

Execution Ground (1994)

Label: Subharmonic — SD 7008-2
Format: 2CD
Engineers: Oz Fritz (main), Robert Musso (ambient pieces)
Management: Axiom/Ambient (Bill Murphy, Peter Wetherbee); Material Inc. (Tracy McKnight)
Major stylistic pivot. Five long tracks built from dub textures, bass processing, and atmospheric density — far less grindcore attack than the Earache records. The dub and ambient direction points toward where Laswell would take the Axiom imprint.

Collected Works (1998)

Label: Tzadik
Anthology pulling from across the catalog, including tracks featuring Keiji Haino and Koichi Makigami (vocals on “Marianne”).

Guts Of A Virgin & Buried Secrets (1998)

Label: Earache
Reissue compiling the two Earache studio albums onto one release.

Talisman (Live In Nagoya) (2002)

Label: Tzadik — TZ 7342
Three long tracks. Core trio; no guests listed. Among the most focused live recordings in the catalog.
