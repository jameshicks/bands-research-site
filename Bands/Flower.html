Flower was a New York City indie rock band (June 1986 – 1990) formed by guitarist Richard Baluyut (later of Versus and Whysall Lane), singer/bassist Ian James (later of Cell and French), drummer Rob Hale (also later of Versus), and keyboardist Yosh Najita (formerly of synth band Groan Box). After recording the Crash EP with Kramer at Noise New York studios, Najita and Hale were replaced by Richard’s brother Edward Baluyut (also later of Versus) on guitar and Andrew Bordwin (later of Ruby Falls) on drums, and Richard began sharing lead vocal duties. Their final album Hologram Sky was not released domestically in the US, and Ian James was briefly replaced by Fontaine Toups before the band dissolved in 1990.

Overview

Flower was a New York City indie rock band active from 1986 to 1990, founded by guitarist Richard Baluyut, singer/bassist Ian James, drummer Rob Hale, and keyboardist Yosh Najita (formerly of synth band Groan Box). After recording the Crash EP with producer Kramer at Noise New York studios, the lineup shifted substantially: Najita and Hale departed, replaced by Richard’s brother Edward Baluyut on guitar and Andrew Bordwin on drums. The band released two records across their run — spanning the late 1980s and early 1990s — on labels including B Records, Bear Records, and Semaphore; their final album Hologram Sky went unreleased in the US. Richard Baluyut, Edward Baluyut, and Rob Hale all later formed Versus, while Ian James went on to Cell and French.
