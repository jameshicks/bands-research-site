The The

Discogs ID: 28646
Origin: London, England
Active: 1979–present (sporadic; long hiatus 2000–2017)
Members: Matt Johnson (sole constant member)
Labels: Some Bizzare, Epic, Nothing Records

Post-punk / art rock project of Matt Johnson. Ensoulment (2024) is their first studio album since Naked Self (2000), marking a return after a nearly 25-year hiatus. Produced by Matt Johnson and Warne Livesey, recorded at Real World Studios in Bath, UK. Livesey is a returning collaborator who produced Infected (1986) and Mind Bomb (1989).

Ensoulment — Livesey Reconnection (web research, 2026-10-20)

Question: How did Matt Johnson reconnect with Warne Livesey for Ensoulment after a ~25-year gap — was it a deliberate choice to return to a trusted collaborator, and was there any intermediary?

Findings: No single interview provides a detailed account of the mechanics of the reconnection (who called whom, whether there was an intermediary), but multiple sources from the Ensoulment press campaign establish the following:

  • Johnson and Livesey had maintained contact over the intervening years — Johnson is quoted in RUSSH magazine (2024) describing Livesey as someone “with whom he had kept in touch for some time.” [source: RUSSH interview]
  • The search engine summary for the BrooklynVegan interview (which returned a 403 on direct fetch) indicates the two had seen each other when Livesey visited England or when Johnson toured Canada, and had “spoken over the years about working together again.” The BrooklynVegan piece appears to be the primary source for this level of detail. [snippet only — BrooklynVegan article 403’d; cite as: BrooklynVegan — not directly verified]
  • Livesey has been based in Toronto, Canada since the late 1990s (Wikipedia notes he relocated there, first working with the Matthew Good Band from 1997). [source: Warne Livesey — Wikipedia, as of 2026-10-20]
  • The choice to work together again reads as deliberate and relationship-driven — Johnson chose Livesey specifically for what he described as the album’s “cohesion,” citing Livesey’s history on Mind Bomb and Infected. Johnson is quoted: “it was great getting Warne back in. Yeah, it was very easy. We picked up very quickly.” [snippet from search results; original source likely the BrooklynVegan or RUSSH interview]
  • No intermediary is mentioned in any source found. All accounts frame it as a direct reconnection between Johnson and Livesey.
  • The Ensoulment sessions were recorded at Real World Studios, Bath, UK — Livesey would have travelled from Toronto.

What was NOT found: No interview with Warne Livesey himself about Ensoulment surfaced. The Current (thecurrent.org) interview with Johnson (published 2024-10-30) does not mention Livesey at all. FLOOD magazine interview likewise does not discuss Livesey. Spin, Yahoo/NME, 48hills, and Lyndsanity interviews checked — none address the reconnection in detail.

Sources checked (this pass):

  • RUSSH — Matt Johnson interview — “kept in touch for some time”; no intermediary mentioned
  • BrooklynVegan interview — 403 on fetch; search snippet indicates most detailed account of the reconnection
  • The Current, thecurrent.org, 2024-10-30 — no mention of Livesey
  • FLOOD magazine — no mention of Livesey
  • American Songwriter — no detail on reconnection
  • Warne Livesey — Wikipedia — confirms Toronto base; no narrative of Ensoulment reconnection
  • Lyndsanity, 48hills, Yahoo/NME, Popdose — no relevant detail

Notes (web)

Correction (verified 2026-10-05): An earlier draft of this page incorrectly stated that Gigi’s Recovery (2023, Human Season Records) was The The’s reunion album, produced by John Congleton. That album is in fact by The Murder Capital — a separate band entirely. The name “Human Season Records” is also The Murder Capital’s label, not Matt Johnson’s. John Congleton has no known production credits for The The or Matt Johnson (confirmed via Wikipedia’s John Congleton article and search across music press). The question “How did Congleton come to produce the reunion album?” is therefore based on a false premise.
