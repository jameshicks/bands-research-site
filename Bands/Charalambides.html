Charalambides

Discogs ID: 147840 Members: Tom Carter, Christina Carter, Heather Leigh Murray (from 2000), Jason Bill (1993–1996) Origin: Houston, Texas (formed 1991); relocated to Austin, Texas (1997) Active: 1991–2011 (at least; final album 2011) Genres: Experimental Folk, Drone, Free Folk, Psychedelic Labels: Siltbreeze, Kranky, Beta-lactam Ring Records, Wholly Other, Eclipse Records (3), Time-Lag Records

Biography

Formation and Houston Years (1991–1996)

Charalambides was formed in Houston, Texas in 1991 by Tom Carter and Christina Carter (née Madonia). The duo performed extended guitar and voice compositions and improvisations, rooted in drone and experimental folk but informed by free jazz and improvised music. Their early work was self-released on cassette: Our Bed Is Green (1993) and Historic 6th Ward (1994) came out on their own imprint.

From 1993 to 1996 the group expanded to a trio with the addition of guitarist Jason Bill, who appears on Historic 6th Ward (1994) and Market Square (1995). The period of this trio configuration aligned with Charalambides’ breakthrough into wider distribution via the Philadelphia underground label Siltbreeze, which released Union (1994, SB26), Market Square (1995, SB46/47), and Houston (1998, SB 67). The Siltbreeze releases brought Charalambides into contact with the broader American underground noise/experimental scene.

Austin Relocation and Late 1990s (1997–2000)

The Carters relocated to Austin, Texas in 1997. By 2000, the lineup had expanded again with the addition of steel guitarist Heather Leigh Murray (formerly of Ash Castles On The Ghost Coast), who became a recurring presence on recordings into the 2000s. Murray’s pedal steel, psaltery, and voice added a further textural layer to the group’s sound.

Peak Activity and Label Alignment (2001–2007)

The early 2000s represent the most concentrated period of Charalambides’ recorded output and label relationships:

  • Branches (2001, Eclipse Records (3)) — studio album
  • Songs From The Entoptic Garden Volume Two (2001, Time-Lag Records, Time-Lag 002) — split with Six Organs of Admittance (Ben Chasny’s project); appeared on the second installment of the Time-Lag label’s compilation series
  • Ana / Kata (2002, Beta-lactam Ring Records, mt029) — all three core members (Tom Carter, Christina Carter, Heather Leigh Murray) on guitar and voice
  • Untitled (2002, Wholly Other) — split with Scorces (a Christina Carter side project also involving Murray)
  • Joy Shapes (2004, Kranky, krank068) — first Kranky album; trio lineup; Tom Carter on electric guitar, acoustic guitar, lap steel guitar, chimes, and wind wand; Christina Carter on electric guitar, voice, and bells; Heather Leigh Murray on pedal steel, psaltery, and voice; Jerry Tubb (Jerry Tubb) mastered at Terra Nova Mastering, Austin
  • Glowing Raw (2006, Wholly Other)
  • Likeness (2007, Kranky, krank113) — Tom Carter and Christina Carter credited with music; Christina Carter credited with arranged/written lyrics (adapting traditional and original texts)
  • Strangle The Wretched Heavens (2007, Wholly Other) — notable for crediting Jason Bill alongside the Carters under the “Charalambides” designation, suggesting a reunion of the 1993–1996 trio configuration for this material

Jason Bill is also credited on a separate Discogs entry, Migrantes, indicating he later formed or was part of an Arizona duo by that name.

Final Album and Hiatus (2011)

Exile (2011, Kranky, KRANK 158) is the last Charalambides album in the Discogs catalog, their third and final Kranky release. No subsequent Charalambides albums are documented on Discogs. Tom Carter and Christina Carter continued active solo careers after this point.

Scene Connections

Charalambides occupied a specific position in the American experimental underground of the 1990s–2000s often labeled “free folk” or “psych freak-folk.” Their connections include:

  • Six Organs of Admittance — shared Time-Lag release; both in the free folk continuum
  • Badgerlore — Tom Carter was a member of this Free Porcupine Society supergroup alongside Ben Chasny, Rob Fisk, Pete Swanson, and Liz Harris
  • Siltbreeze — Philadelphia underground label connecting them to noise rock and lo-fi avant-garde scenes in the mid-1990s
  • Kranky — Chicago label; shared roster with Low, Stars of the Lid, Tim Hecker — the ambient/drone side of American indie
  • Bardo Pond — Philadelphia heavy psych/drone; shared Terrastock festival appearance
  • Liz Harris / Grouper — shared compilation appearance; Liz Harris also in Badgerlore with Tom Carter
  • Scorces — side project of Christina Carter and Heather Leigh Murray; released on Wholly Other
  • The Mike Gunn — early Houston psych band containing Tom Carter; pre-Charalambides context
  • Primordial Undermind — international psych improv group; Tom Carter is a member
