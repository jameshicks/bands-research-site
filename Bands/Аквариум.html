Аквариум

Discogs ID: 163453 Also known as: Aquarium, БГ-Бэнд Origin: Leningrad / St. Petersburg, USSR / Russia

Russian rock band led by Борис Гребенщиков. One of the founding acts of the Leningrad underground rock scene. Featured alongside Алиса, Кино, and Странные Игры on the Red Wave compilation (Big Time Records, US, 1986). 17 releases on AnTrop (1987–2023).

Key Members

  • Борис Гребенщиков — vocals, guitar, principal songwriter
  • Сергей Курёхин — keyboards (also associated as Pop Mechanics)
  • Александр Титов (ID: 374358) — bass guitar
  • Всеволод Гаккель (ID: 678199) — cello
  • Андрей Романов (ID: 529615) — flute, vocals
  • Петр Трощенков (ID: 529590) — drums
