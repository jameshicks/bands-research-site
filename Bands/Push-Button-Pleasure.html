Push-Button Pleasure

UK experimental electronic duo formed c. 1982 by Terry Burrows and Mark Beaumont (3). Discogs artist ID: 165193. Name variations: Push Button Pleasure, Pushbutton Pleasure. All releases appeared on Burrows’ own Hamster Records And Tapes imprint. Active 1982–1987. The duo is notable partly for its overlap with Burrows’ alias Jung Analysts, which shared the debut LP and was billed as ”(‘Jung Analysts’ Branch Of) Push-Button Pleasure” on that release.

Overview

Push-Button Pleasure was one of several projects run by Terry Burrows through Hamster Records And Tapes, the small UK independent label he founded. The duo paired Burrows with Mark Beaumont (3), who wrote, composed, and performed on the cassette releases. Their output sits squarely in the UK DIY experimental/electronic underground of the early 1980s — short-run cassettes and vinyl LPs, self-released with absurdist humour evident in the track and album titles.

The debut release, the vinyl LP (The Penal Collada Effect) / A Leading Surgeon Speaks! (HAM 008, 1982), is a split between two sides of the Push-Button Pleasure alias: Side A is credited to the ”(‘Jung Analysts’ Branch Of) Of Push-Button Pleasure” — which on Discogs is the Jung Analysts entry (ID 164878, realname Terry Burrows) — while Side B carries tracks credited to Push-Button Pleasure proper. The running matrix messages carry Porky Prime Cut lacquer inscriptions, indicating cutting at George Peckham’s facility.

Two cassette albums followed in 1982: Bring Me The Leg Of Arthur Askey!! (Even) (HAM 05) and Let Icons Be Icons (Ham 006). Both credit Burrows and Beaumont as writers, composers, and performers.

Activity appears to have paused after 1982. The final release under this name, The Last Dissonance (HAM 19, 1987), is a vinyl LP that credits Push-Button Pleasure as both performer and writer — no separate Beaumont credit appears, suggesting Burrows may have been working solo or that Beaumont’s involvement was uncredited. The 1987 release has a soundtrack/theme dimension (it is tagged Electronic / Stage & Screen / Experimental / Soundtrack / Theme on Discogs).

Mark Beaumont (3)‘s other group was The Spawn (2) (Discogs 4043809), who appeared on Hamster in 1989. His aliases include Berbel Nobodius, Enski Boski, Melvyn Z Pad, and Penal Colada — the last of which is also a track title on the debut LP.
