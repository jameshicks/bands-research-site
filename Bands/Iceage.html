Rønnenfelt: Visual Art & Publications

Poetry book — Sunken Heights (2023)

Rønnenfelt’s debut poetry collection, Sunken Heights, was published by Anthology Editions on July 18, 2023 (ISBN 978-1-944860-57-8). The 48-page hardcover presents the poems in both the original Danish and Rønnenfelt’s own English translation. The book was designed in collaboration with Elizabeth Peyton and features original artwork by her. It is distributed through the Mexican Summer shop and listed at Printed Matter. anthology.net · printedmatter.org/catalog/62892 · Mexican Summer shop

Relationship with Elizabeth Peyton

Rønnenfelt first met Elizabeth Peyton at an Iceage concert in Leipzig in 2012. She subsequently invited him to sit for portraits — sessions described as “long afternoons drinking wine and eating almonds and chocolate while the artist painted.” One resulting work, Fire (Elias) (2014), is a watercolor on paper (16 × 12 in). A portrait of Rønnenfelt was shown in Peyton’s 2019 exhibition Aire and Angels at the National Portrait Gallery, London, where it appeared alongside a 1575 portrait of Elizabeth I and a canvas of Kurt Cobain. They later collaborated on a short film premiered by AnOther Magazine, filmed at 222 The Bowery, New York. anothermag.com · giornopoetrysystems.org

Artforum — Top Ten (May 2021)

Rønnenfelt contributed a “Top Ten” list to Artforum, published May 11, 2021, in which he wrote about (among other things) the paintings of Stanley Spencer and expressed a long-held wish to visit the Sandham Memorial Chapel in Hampshire. This marks him as a recognised art-world voice — Artforum carries him as an author. The list is also available as a video on the Artforum site. artforum.com/lists/elias-bender-ronnenfelt/
