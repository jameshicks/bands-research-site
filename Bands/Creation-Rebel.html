Creation Rebel

Dub group. UK. Discogs ID: 85421. Active 1978–present.

Associated with Adrian Sherwood and the On-U Sound world. Pre-On-U Sound history goes back to Sherwood’s Hitrun label (1978).

Members (per Discogs)

Clifton “Bigga” Morrison, Desmond “Fatfingers” Coke, Eric “Fish” Clarke, Eskimo Fox, Keith Logan, Lincoln Valentine Scott, Peter Stroud (2), Tony Phillips (2), Veral Rose.
