Black Flag

American hardcore punk band, Los Angeles. Discogs artist ID: 253278.

Formed by guitarist Greg Ginn in Hermosa Beach, CA, c. 1976–1978. One of the central bands of American hardcore punk and a key act in establishing the SST Records imprint. Known for their prolific touring, confrontational live performances, and Raymond Pettibon’s cover art. Original vocalist: Keith Morris (through 1979, who left to form Circle Jerks). Best-known lineup: Henry Garfield (later Henry Rollins) on vocals (1981–1986). Other vocalists included Ron Reyes and Dez Cadena.

Members included: Keith Morris, Dez Cadena, Ron Reyes, Henry Garfield (Henry Rollins), Greg Ginn, Chuck Dukowski (Gary McDaniel), Bill Stevenson, Robo (Roberto Valverde), Kira Roessler, and others.

Connected to Raymond Pettibon (cover art from 1979 through 1988 retrospectives), Glen E. Friedman (photography), Ed Colver (photography), SST Records (label), and Spot (house producer/engineer). Vocalist history: Keith Morris (1976–1979) → Ron Reyes → Dez Cadena → Henry Rollins (1981–1986).
