Spoon

Indie rock. Austin, Texas. Active 1993–present. Discogs ID: 33287.

Core duo: Britt Daniel (vocals, guitar) and Jim Eno (drums). Formed 1993 in Austin. Signed to Matador Records for debut Telephono (OLE 201-1, 1996) and the Soft Effects EP (1997). Spoon then chose to leave Matador — Britt Daniel felt the label viewed their sound as too commercial — and signed to Elektra Records in February 1998 (the album had already been recorded before the deal was finalised). Elektra released A Series Of Sneaks (1998) then dropped them after their A&R advocate Ron Laffitte departed. Signed to Merge for Girls Can Tell (2001) — breakthrough. Returned to Matador for Gimme Fiction (OLE 660-2, 2005), Ga Ga Ga Ga Ga (2007), Transference (2010), They Want My Soul (2014). One of Matador’s most commercially successful 2010s acts. 156 total releases on Matador.

Note — corrected narrative (2026-07-27): The vault previously stated “Matador dropped them after a second single.” This is inaccurate. Multiple sources confirm Spoon voluntarily left Matador for Elektra. Britt Daniel stated directly: “I was pretty bummed out when we left Matador and made a record for this major label” (Relix interview, https://relix.com/articles/detail/spoon_further_on_down_the_road/). The Wikipedia article for A Series of Sneaks corroborates: Daniel felt Matador viewed their sound as too commercial; the band agreed to join Elektra in fall 1997 and signed in February 1998. It was Elektra, not Matador, that dropped them.
