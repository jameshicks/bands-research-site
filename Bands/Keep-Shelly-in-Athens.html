Keep Shelly in Athens

Electronic/dream pop/chillwave duo from Athens, Greece, formed 2010. The only constant member is producer RPR (also known as P. Dreamweaver). Vocalists have rotated: Sarah P. (2010–early 2014), Myrtha (March 2014–2016), and Jessica Bell (October 2016–present). In 2017 the duo founded their own imprint Athenian Aura Recordings. The band name is a pun on Κυψέλη (Kypseli), a neighbourhood of Athens.

Discogs artist ID: 2047758

Overview

Keep Shelly in Athens emerged from the early-2010s chillwave/dream pop internet scene, self-releasing their debut EP In Love With Dusk (2010) on Forest Family Records before attracting international label attention. Their 2011 12” Campus Martius E.P. on Planet Mu introduced them to a UK electronic audience. Debut full-length At Home (2013, Cascine) marked their mainstream chillwave peak, featuring the classic RPR + Sarah P. lineup. After Sarah P.’s departure in early 2014, Myrtha fronted the darker, more synth-driven Now I’m Ready (2015, Friends Of Friends). Jessica Bell joined for Philokalia (2017, Plancha), the most textured and ambient-leaning album. The 2023 album Among Wolves and 2026’s V were self-released on Athenian Aura Recordings, showing a return toward indie-pop and shoegaze territory.

Connections
