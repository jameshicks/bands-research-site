Newtown Neurotics

Discogs ID: 306151. UK punk band; 19 releases on Jungle Records spanning 1985–2005, on both the Jungle (JUNG series) and Fresh Records (14) (FREUD series) imprints. Key releases include Repercussions (FREUD 7, 1985), Kickstarting A Backfiring Nation (FREUD 10, 1986), Is Your Washroom Breeding Bolsheviks? (FREUD 19, 1988), and the retrospective 45 Revolutions Per Minute - Singles 1979-1984 (FREUD31, 1990).
