History

Formed 1977 at Leicester University by Andy Ross, Johnny Hawkins, Geoff Dodimead, and Andy Fullerton. Dave Henderson (formerly of the Blazers) joined as additional vocalist.

Relocated to London. Recorded for Dining Out Records (Henderson’s own label).

After splitting, Henderson and Ross formed Club Tango.

Reunited for live shows in 2011 and 2018.
