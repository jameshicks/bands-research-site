Till The Old World’s Blown Up And A New One Is Created

Vienna-based electro-acoustic trio. Discogs artist ID: 1244950.

Overview

A project uniting three central figures of the Vienna electroacoustic scene: Werner Dafeldecker (double bass, tape delay, computer), Martin Brandlmayr (drums, percussion, vibraphone, computer), and Fennesz (electric guitar, acoustic guitar, computer). The ensemble name comes from a line attributed to a utopian/revolutionary context and is used both as project name and as the title of their sole recording.

The album is a two-disc set: a single long-form piece on CD1 (34:34) and three shorter edits on CD2. All three members contributed “additional recordings” to the source material; the editing, arranging, and mixing fell respectively to each member per track. The source material was recorded by Christoph Amann. Artwork for the original release was handled by re-p.org.

A 2012 vinyl LP edition on M=Minimal re-credited the record under the three core members’ names (Brandlmayr - Dafeldecker - Christian Fennesz) and brought in Berlin duo Christian Borngräber and Jens Strüver as executive producers and visual designers. Stefan Betke (Pole) cut the vinyl at Scape Mastering; Nicholas Bussmann handled the CD mastering for that edition as well.
