Paramore

Discogs artist ID: 609563

Alternative/modern rock band from Franklin, Tennessee, formed in 2004. Signed to Fueled By Ramen for their first five albums; moved to Atlantic Records for This Is Why (2023). The current lineup is Hayley Williams (vocals), Taylor York (guitar), and Zac Farro (drums). The band went through several lineup changes — most notably the departure of guitarist Josh Farro and bassist Jeremy Davis — but Williams and York have been the creative constant across all eras. Their production lineage runs from early punk-pop records with David Bendeth and Mike Green to the polished pop of Rob Cavallo, the eclectic self-titled record with Justin Meldal-Johnsen, and the Carlos De La Garza-dominated run from Paramore (2013) through This Is Why (2023).

Connections

  • Hayley Williams — vocalist, core creative force; solo albums Petals For Armor (2020, Atlantic) and Flowers For Vases / Descansos (2021); collaborated with B.o.B on “Airplanes” (2010); guest vocals on American Football, Chvrches, Wolf Alice
  • Justin Meldal-Johnsen — producer/bassist across massive discography (Beck, Garbage, M83, Wolf Alice, Tegan and Sara); produced Paramore (2013) and After Laughter (2017); also played bass on After Laughter
  • Carlos De La Garza — engineer on Paramore (2013); co-engineer on After Laughter (2017); lead producer This Is Why (2023); also worked extensively with Paramore’s Fueled By Ramen label-mates
  • Ken Andrews — mixed and sang backing vocals on Paramore (2013); best known as frontman of Failure; active session and mix engineer
  • Ted Jensen — mastered all records from The Summer Tic EP through Paramore (2013); Sterling Sound, NYC
  • Dave Cooley — mastered After Laughter (2017)
  • Rob Cavallo — produced Brand New Eyes (2009); major label producer known for Green Day
  • David Bendeth — produced, mixed, and performed on Riot! (2007)
  • Josh Farro — co-founding guitarist; wrote on all early albums; departed 2010; alias FARRO; no significant solo Discogs releases found post-departure
  • Zac Farro — co-founding drummer; left 2010, returned 2017; records independently as Halfnoise (since 2012, on Congrats Records)
  • Jeremy Davis — bassist on first four albums (2004–2015 with gaps)
  • Taylor York — guitarist and increasingly central producer; produced most of Paramore (2013) self-titled, co-produced After Laughter, co-produced This Is Why
  • Manny Marroquin — mixed This Is Why (2023); Grammy-winning mixer
