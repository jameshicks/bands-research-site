Butthole Surfers

American experimental / psychedelic noise-rock band founded in San Antonio, Texas in 1981 by Gibby Haynes and Paul Leary. Discogs artist ID: 18838. Alias: 100 Foot Worm Makes Own Food.

Overview

The Butthole Surfers emerged from San Antonio and relocated to Austin, Texas, becoming one of the most confrontational and genuinely strange bands of the American underground. Formed in 1981 by vocalist Gibby Haynes (Gibson Jerome Haynes) and guitarist Paul Leary (Paul Leary Walthall), the group built its reputation on increasingly abrasive live performances — multiple projections, strobe lights, burning effigies, dual drummers — and a body of recordings that refused to stay in any recognisable lane, mixing noise rock, psychedelia, hardcore, and electronic manipulation.

Their early catalog (1983–1988) was released through Alternative Tentacles and Touch And Go, yielding the most influential sequence of records: Psychic… Powerless… Another Man’s Sac (1984), Rembrandt Pussyhorse (1986), Locust Abortion Technician (1987), and Hairway To Steven (1988). A move toward major-label territory — first through Rough Trade (Piouhgd, 1991) then Capitol Records (Independent Worm Saloon, 1993; Electriclarryland, 1996) — brought the band a commercial peak with the unexpected MTV staple “Pepper” in 1996. Their final studio album, Weird Revolution (Hollywood Records/Surfdog, 2001), did not repeat that success, and the band subsequently entered an extended hiatus. They maintained a self-release operation through their own label Latino Bugger Veil.

Core long-term members: Gibby Haynes (vocals), Paul Leary (guitar), King Coffey (drums, as Jeffrey Coffey), and Jeffrey Pinkus (bass, 1986–1994 and 2008–present). Teresa Taylor (“Teresa Nervosa”) drummed 1983–1989 and briefly in 2008–2009 before her death. Kramer (2) (Mark Kramer) appeared briefly in 1985 and toured with the band. The band has carried numerous other short-tenure members through its 40+ year history.

Connections

  • Gibby Haynes — co-founder, vocalist; side projects include The Jackofficers, Revolting Cocks, and the band P (with Johnny Depp)
  • Paul Leary — co-founder, guitarist; became a prolific producer (Sublime, Meat Puppets, Daniel Johnston, Supersuckers)
  • King Coffey — drummer; founded Trance Syndicate Records and co-involved with Emperor Jones
  • Jeffrey Pinkus — bassist; also in The Jackofficers, Daddy Longhead, Honky (2), Melvins (Hold It In 2014)
  • Teresa Taylor — drummer 1983–1989; also in Meat Joy; appeared in Linklater’s Slacker
  • Kramer (2) — brief member 1985; founder of Shimmy Disc; produced Galaxie 500, Bongwater, Ween
  • Melvins — close Austin/underground adjacency; Leary and Pinkus both performed on Hold It In (2014); 2012 split release with the Surfers
  • Emperor Jones — King Coffey involved; Austin-scene connection
  • Trance Syndicate Records — King Coffey’s label, released Good King Wencenslaus (1994) and associated Austin underground acts
  • The Jackofficers — Haynes/Pinkus electronic side project
  • John Paul Jones — produced Independent Worm Saloon; Led Zeppelin connection
