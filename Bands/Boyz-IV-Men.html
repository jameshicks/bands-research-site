Boyz IV Men

Experimental/electronic trio from the US. Active c. 2009–2012. Discogs artist ID: 2144988. Not to be confused with the R&B group Boyz II Men.

Members: Josh Hill (3), Aaron Rodriguez, Trevor Goosen.

Overview

Boyz IV Men were a Soundcloud-era experimental group whose one known physical release, the 12” EP Sweat (2010), landed on Turn Up — a small San Francisco-area independent that also released The Sandwitches and Sonny Smith in the same period. The group self-produced the record alongside Tim Green (2), who also recorded it at his Louder Studios facility. The EP sits at the intersection of indie rock, new wave, and experimental electronics — Discogs genres/styles: Electronic, Rock / Experimental, Indie Rock, New Wave.

The group’s web presence included an archived site at boyzivmen.com and a roster page at turnuprecords.com; a SoundCloud account (soundcloud.com/boyz-iv-men) and a Myspace page (archived 2009) are also documented on Discogs.

Josh Hill (3) is a guitarist/drummer/keyboardist with roots in the Sacramento math-rock scene — cousin of Zach Hill (Hella, Death Grips) and a member of several related groups. Trevor Goosen was also a member of Scene Index (2004–2006). Aaron Rodriguez’s other projects are not documented on Discogs; his URLs on Discogs point to aronmark.com and an Instagram at @aaron_mark_.
