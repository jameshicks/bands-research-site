The Everlasting Yeah

Post-punk / alternative rock band formed 2013, London/Derry. Discogs artist ID: 4116532

Overview

The Everlasting Yeah was formed in 2013 by four members of the final That Petrol Emotion lineup: Damian O’Neill (guitar/vocals), Réamann Ó Gormain (guitar/vocals), Ciaran McLaughlin (drums/percussion/vocals), and Brendan Kelly (bass/vocals). The reunion came roughly three years after the 2008–2010 TPE live revival, and predates any further Undertones activity for Damian O’Neill.

The band operates on their own label, Infinite Thrill (Infinite Thrill, Discogs label ID: 767286), a UK imprint created specifically for the group. As of the Discogs data, the label has issued exactly two releases — both Everlasting Yeah LPs — with catalog numbers INTH001 LP and INTH002 LP.

Their debut LP Anima Rising (2014) was self-produced and engineered by Andy Ramsay, the Stereolab drummer who runs Press Play Studios in London. The second LP Staying Cool Staying Free With The Everlasting Yeah (2024) brought in co-producer Grant Strang. A decade separated the two records; no singles or EPs appear in the Discogs database between or around them.
