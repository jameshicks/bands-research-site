People In Control

Discogs ID: 205581
Also credited as: The People In Control
Type: Production collective
Origin: UK
Active: c. 1979–1984 (documented release credits)

Overview

People In Control is a shared production credit used by a loose collective centred on the Family Fodder inner circle in London during the post-punk period. It is not a performing band. The name functions as a collective alias covering a group of musicians — primarily Charles Bullen, Ian Hill, Judy Carter, Martin Harrison, and Rick Wilson — who worked together across Family Fodder and related projects during the late 1970s and early 1980s.

The collective’s documented production work spans 1979 to 1984, bookended by the Frank Sumatra single “Te Deum” (Small Wonder Records, 1979) and a single-track contribution to Hermine Demoriane’s Lonely At The Top LP (Salomé Discs, 1984). Their most significant credit is as producer on Family Fodder’s debut album Monkey Banana Kitchen (Fresh Records, 1980), where the credit appears as “The People In Control.” A 2006 LTM CD reissue of Hermine Demoriane’s The World On My Plates (retitled The World On My Plates Bis) attributes “Instrumentation By [Unspecified]” to them on three bonus tracks — recordings that were likely made in the early 1980s and had not previously appeared on the original 1982 Crammed Discs LP.

The collective reflects a practice common in London experimental/post-punk circles of the period: rather than individual producer credits, a group of collaborators pooled under a shared name. Charles Bullen’s simultaneous membership in This Heat — one of the period’s most rigorous experimental acts — situates People In Control firmly within the overlap between the DIY/post-punk underground (Small Wonder, Fresh Records) and the avant-garde wing (Southern Studios, This Heat’s tape-heavy approach).

Monkey Banana Kitchen (1980)

Artist: Family Fodder
Label: Fresh Records (14) (Discogs ID: 344059)
Catalog: FRESH LP 3
Format: LP
Discogs release ID: 564234
Genres: Rock / Avantgarde, New Wave

People In Control are credited here under the variant name “The People In Control” as Producer. The album was recorded across four London studios — Berry Street (16-track), Elephant (8-track), Mobile (16-track), and Vineyard (24-track) — with separate engineers for each: Dave Hunt (Berry St.), Colin Lay (Elephant), Geoffrey (14) (Mobile), Keith Hancock (Vineyard).

Lonely At The Top (1984) — track B2 only

Artist: Hermine (3) (Hermine Demoriane, Discogs ID: 537592)
Label: Salomé Discs
Catalog: LOM 002
Format: LP
Discogs release ID: 472128
Genres: Electronic / Downtempo

People In Control are credited as Producer, Arranged By on track B2 (“La Valse De 99 Ans”) only. The remaining tracks are produced by Cass Davies, Dominic Weeks, and (on B1) Martin Pig / Stephen Kent. This indicates People In Control contributed to a single track on what is otherwise a different production team’s record.

Other notable personnel on the album include Tim Hodgkinson (baritone saxophone, and engineer on B2–B4), Felix Fiedorowicz (bassoon), and Ian Hill (accordion). John Fryer recorded most of the album. Tim Young mastered.

The World On My Plates Bis (2006) — bonus tracks only

Artist: Hermine (3) (Hermine Demoriane)
Label: LTM Recordings (LTM (4), Discogs ID: 495079)
Catalog: LTMCD 2473
Format: CD; Album; Reissue; Remastered
Discogs release ID: 1320038

This is a CD reissue of Hermine Demoriane’s 1982 Crammed Discs album The World On My Plates, expanded with three bonus tracks (7, 8, 13). People In Control are credited as “Instrumentation By [Unspecified]” specifically on tracks 7, 8, and 13. The same tracks are produced by David Cunningham (The Flying Lizards) and engineered by Dave Hunt. Rod Melvin plays piano/organ on these bonus tracks; Simon Brint plays bass and saxophone.

The original 1982 LP carried no People In Control credit — the collective’s contribution is specific to the bonus material, suggesting these recordings are from the early 1980s and were unreleased until this reissue. LTM coordinator James Nice wrote the liner notes.

Connections

  • This Heat — via Charles Bullen, who was simultaneously a This Heat member during the 1979–1981 period
  • Small Wonder Records — released “Te Deum” (TEENY 1), the earliest documented People In Control production credit
  • Fresh Records (14) — released Monkey Banana Kitchen (FRESH LP 3), the collective’s primary production credit
  • LTM Recordings — issued The World On My Plates Bis (LTMCD 2473, 2006) containing the final documented People In Control credit
  • Hermine Demoriane — artist on two of the four credited releases; herself part of the broader London experimental/post-punk milieu
  • David Cunningham — co-produced the bonus tracks on The World On My Plates Bis where People In Control receive instrumentation credit; Cunningham also produced Family Fodder’s “Savoir Faire” single (1980) alongside Charles Bullen
