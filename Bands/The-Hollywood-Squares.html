The Hollywood Squares

Los Angeles punk duo, formed 1977. Discogs artist ID: 1984683.

Overview

The Hollywood Squares were a Los Angeles punk duo formed in 1977, consisting of Eddie Vincent (2) (songwriter) and Bob Schell (2). They are best known for their debut single “Hillside Strangler!” (1978), a two-song 7-inch released in a very limited pressing on their own Square Records label (cat# SQ 001). The A-side title referenced the Hillside Strangler murders, a series of killings that occurred in the Los Angeles area in 1977–78. The single quickly sold out and became a sought-after collector’s item, appearing in numerous bootleg pressings over the years — most notably the Killed By Death #1 compilation (1988/1989). In 2006, the band released Hillside Strangler! Restrangled, a compilation of their recordings including previously unreleased material, on Bluside Records (US, CD) and Rave Up Records (Italy, LP). All recorded material was produced by Ron Hitchcock. The original single was officially reissued in 2020 by Soul Jazz Records (cat# SJR 408-5).

A note on membership: an earlier stub listed “Spider Cobb (bass) and Tad Malone (drums)” as the band’s members. Discogs lists only Eddie Vincent (2) and Bob Schell (2) as members, and Bob Schell (2)‘s own Discogs alias entry includes “Spider Cobb” — suggesting “Spider Cobb” may be an alias or stage name used by Schell, but this is not confirmed by Discogs member data alone.
