Relay (3)

Philadelphia-based post-rock quartet active in the mid-2000s. Discogs artist ID: 643926

Overview

Relay is a Philadelphia four-piece that built its sound around careful studio experimentation — humming bass, shuffly drums, glitchy keyboards, and dissonant pedal-treated guitar collapsed into what the band’s own Discogs profile calls “iridescent sonorous reduction.” Their two commercial releases both landed in 2006 on Bubble Core Records: the Type / Void EP (BC-49) and the full-length Still Point Of Turning (BC-050). A self-released CD-R EP, pre(ep), preceded these in 2005.

The band connects to Bubble Core Records partly through Jeff Zeigler, who had engineered and mixed Swirlies’ Cats Of The Wild: Volume Two (Bubble Core, 2003) before Relay signed there. Additional recording on both releases brought in Mickey Walker and Quentin Stoltzfus, both rooted in the Philadelphia indie scene and connected through Mazarin. Three of the four members — Zeigler, Ian M. Fraser, and Mikele Edwards — later reunited in Arc In Round.

Connections

  • Bubble Core Records — released Type / Void and Still Point Of Turning in 2006; label founded by Adam Pierce (Mice Parade)
  • Jeff Zeigler — multi-instrumentalist; built Uniform Recording studio in Philadelphia; prolific engineer/producer for Secretly Canadian, Matador, and Drag City artists post-Relay
  • Ian M. Fraser — composer working in computer music, generative structures, stochastic processes; later worked as Keroaän (with Reed Evan Rosenberg)
  • Mikele Edwards — keyboards/vocals; appeared on Aquila Rose (2) Say Speaker Speak (2005) and later provided vocals/arrangement on Kurt Vile’s Childish Prodigy (Matador, 2009)
  • Arc In Round — post-Relay project shared by Zeigler, Fraser, and Edwards, plus Matt Ricchini; released self-titled LP on La Société Expéditionnaire (2012)
  • Mickey Walker — additional recording on both 2006 releases; member of Lilys, Mazarin, and Swirlies
  • Quentin Stoltzfus — additional recording on both 2006 releases; songwriter and member of Mazarin, Therisphere, and Two Projects To Harness The Surge Of The Tides; alias Light Heat
  • Alan Douches — mastered Still Point Of Turning; recurring Bubble Core mastering engineer
  • Swirlies — shared label (Bubble Core) and shared engineer (Zeigler); Swirlies member Mickey Walker appears on Relay records
  • Kohoutek — Philadelphia psychedelic improv band; Zeigler listed as synth player
  • Don Devore — guest guitarist on Type / Void track “Venture Complete” (Phase Lock Loop credit); member of Lilys, The Icarus Line, Ink & Dagger
