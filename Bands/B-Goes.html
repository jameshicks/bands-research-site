B Goes

Swiss alternative rock group, led by Andi Hoffmann (Andreas Hoffmann). Discogs artist ID: 2207250. Also credited as B-Goes and B-GOES on releases and pressings.

Overview

B Goes was formed in Switzerland in 1989 by Andi Hoffmann, who assembled a core group of Swiss musicians for a recording project. The group released entirely on the Swiss independent By The Way Records from 1990 through 2002, across a catalog of eight distinct releases plus several additional pressings and formats. Early releases featured a stable lineup that included Alig (John Pearce, accordion), Andi Hug (drums), Chessy Weaver (vocals), and Jüre Schmidhauser (bass).

Around 1993 Hoffmann relocated to New Orleans, where he rebuilt the group around American musicians. The mature sound is described as “organic rock” rooted in New Orleans musical culture — drawing on cajun, calypso, reggae, swamp pop, hip-hop, and hi-life — with a strong emphasis on vocal harmonies. The 1995 album 937 Dante Street (BTW22CD) was produced with New Orleans engineer and producer Dave McNair, and its title explicitly references the New Orleans address. The 2002 album Living In The Big Wide World featured a fully American ensemble including guitarist Anders Osborne (New Orleans blues-rock), guitarist Roberto Luti (2), bassist Thomas McDonald, drummer Cori Walters, and Hammond organist Trevor Brooks, among others.

The 1995 material was also licensed to the US market on the Ocean Music label as Dante Street (OM2007, 1996). A 1996 release, Mississippi Love, was credited jointly to Andi Hoffmann & B Goes and appeared on the French label Musidisc (cat. 119202), expanding the group’s reach beyond Switzerland. Hoffmann is the sole constant — writer, vocalist, guitarist, and producer across the entire catalog.

Label: By The Way Records

Swiss independent label. Discogs label ID: 241639. No parent label; no sublabels. 25 releases total on Discogs — all by B Goes. The label appears to have been Hoffmann’s own vehicle. Catalog numbers run BTW 2 through BTW 31CD (1990–2004), with sequential assignment across formats. No label URL or profile text on Discogs.

Connections

  • Andi Hoffmann (ID: 1653313) — founder, vocalist, guitarist, producer, songwriter; sole consistent member. Has two primary credits on Discogs: Mississippi Love (1996, Musidisc) and Living In The Big Wide World (2002, By The Way Records), both as “Andi Hoffmann / B Goes.”
  • Alig (ID: 236307, also John Pearce / John Pearce (2) ID: 1837976) — early member; accordion. Member of Family Fodder (UK post-punk collective). His Discogs pages use two separate IDs: 236307 (stage name “Alig”) and 1837976 (birth name “John Pearce (2)”), which are mutual aliases.
  • Andi Hug (ID: 987965) — drums; member of Patent Ochsner (major Swiss alternative rock band), Kadash, Ho Orchestra, Honeypump, Mixtüür, Die Bürger Von Calais, Stop The Shoppers, Ugly Bluz; one of Switzerland’s most active drummers across multiple projects.
  • Chessy Weaver (ID: 1453530) — vocals; also listed as Jessy Weaver-Lauener; also member of Phon Roll and Honeypump (overlapping with Andi Hug).
  • Jüre Schmidhauser (ID: 345738) — bass; also member of Züri West (major Swiss dialect-rock band), Mãozinha, Stop The Shoppers (also overlapping with Andi Hug), The Four Million Views, and Naiare.
  • Mick Hobbs (ID: 307762) — contributed voice on Travel As Far As I Can (1992); British avant-rock musician associated with The Work, Family Fodder, Officer!; connecting B Goes to the UK post-punk underground.
  • Dave McNair (ID: 360608) — produced and engineered 937 Dante Street (1995) and Poor Folks (1995); New Orleans-based producer/engineer/mastering engineer with very extensive discography spanning rock, soul, and Americana.
  • Anders Osborne (ID: 352411) — guitar on Living In The Big Wide World (2002); New Orleans blues-rock singer-songwriter with extensive solo career on Alligator Records, 550 Music/OKeh, Shanachie, and Back On Dumaine Records.
  • Mauro Zompicchiatti (ID: 274147) — co-producer and bassist on There Ain’t No Ice-Cream… (1991), Travel As Far As I Can (1992), Kisses & Dreams (1992); active in Swiss music from the 1980s (Polo Hofer / Schnoutz Records circle).
  • Christian Brantschen (ID: 482474) — piano/accordion/organ on three early 1990s releases; also member of Patent Ochsner, connecting B Goes further to the central Swiss alternative rock scene.
  • By The Way Records (ID: 241639) — sole label throughout entire catalog; Hoffmann’s own imprint.
