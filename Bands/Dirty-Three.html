Dirty Three

Discogs ID: 65791
ANV: Dirty 3, The Dirty 3, The Dirty Three
Origin: Melbourne, Victoria, Australia
Active: 1992 – present (with gaps)
Labels: Touch And Go, Bella Union, Anchor & Hope, Scuzz Productions, Torn & Frayed, Poon Village

Australian instrumental rock trio: Warren Ellis (violin, viola, piano, mandolin, bouzouki), Mick Turner (guitar, bass, organ), and Jim White (2) (drums, percussion). All compositions are trio credits throughout. Known for long-form, emotionally intense pieces driven by Ellis’s violin over Turner’s resonant guitar textures and White’s loose, dynamic drumming. One of the defining acts of post-rock’s chamber-music wing.

Formation and Early Years (1992–1995)

Formed in Melbourne in 1992, emerging directly out of the dissolution of Venom P. Stinger — Turner and White had both been in that band, and Ellis came from a brief stint in Blackeyed Susans. The initial recordings appeared on small Australian imprints: a self-titled cassette on Scuzz Productions (1993, 17 pressings) and then a vinyl self-titled on Torn & Frayed (1994, 18 pressings). The Sad & Dangerous LP (1994) was their first US release, on the Australian-run Poon Village imprint.

Key Release Notes

Horse Stories — Touch And Go (1996)

First Touch And Go LP. Engineered by Nick Atkins; mixed by Paul Corkett. Mick Turner’s painting on the front cover — his visual art practice runs alongside the band throughout its career. All compositions credited to the three members collectively.

Ocean Songs — Touch And Go (1998)

Most widely pressed Dirty Three album (23 pressings). Co-produced by the band and Steve Albini. David Grubbs plays piano on B1, B2, and C2. Mick Turner: guitar, melodica, loops; Warren Ellis: violin, viola. A landmark of the genre.

Whatever You Love, You Are — Touch And Go (2000)

Recorded and mixed by Lincoln Fong. Design by David Babbitt and Mick Turner. Second-most pressed album (20 pressings).

In The Fishtank 7 — Konkurrent (2001)

Collaboration with Low (Alan Sparhawk, Mimi Parker, Zak Sally) for the Dutch Konkurrent “In The Fishtank” series — recordings made in a fixed location with limited time. Engineered and mastered by Zlaya Hadzich. All compositions credited jointly.

She Has No Strings Apollo — Touch And Go (2002)

Produced and mixed by F. Lor (also credited as Fabrice Laureau). Adam Rhodes recorded and mixed additional tracks. Delphine Ciampi (Warren Ellis’s wife) photographed. Mick Turner’s paintings used as artwork.

Cinder — Anchor & Hope / Touch And Go (2005)

Double album. Recorded and mixed by Casey Rice. Warren Ellis plays violin, viola, Irish bouzouki, piano, and mandolin. Guest vocalists and writers: Chan Marshall co-wrote track 10; Lajkó Félix and Sally Timms appear. Mark Soul also credited.

Love Changes Everything — Anchor & Hope / Remote Control (2024)

First album in twelve years. Mixed and engineered by Nick Huggins; lacquer cut by JJ Golden. Released in Australia and New Zealand on Remote Control, internationally on Anchor & Hope.

Members’ Other Projects

Warren Ellis

Joined Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds in 1996 (same year as Horse Stories); co-founded Grinderman with Nick Cave. Extensive film soundtrack work: Mustang (2015), Django (2017), Gauguin (2017), This Train I Ride (2020), She Walks In Beauty (2021). Solo releases: 3 Pieces For Violin (2002, King Crab Records). Married to Delphine Ellis (née Delphine Ciampi). Former groups: Blackeyed Susans, STM (2), Kim Salmon And The Surrealists, Charlie Marshall And The Body Electric.

Mick Turner

Founded Anchor & Hope in 1996. Extensive solo catalogue on Drag City: Tren Phantasma (1997), Marlan Rosa (1999), Moth (2002), Seven Angels (2002, Three Lobed Recordings), Blue Trees (2007), Don’t Tell The Driver (2013). Visual artist — his paintings appear on multiple Dirty Three covers. Other groups: Venom P. Stinger, The Moodists, Fungus Brains, Boxhead Ensemble, Tren Brothers, Bonnevill, Mess Esque.

Jim White (2)

Live-in Chicago since the late 1990s; prolific session drummer. Past/concurrent groups: Venom P. Stinger, Blackeyed Susans, Crime & The City Solution, Boxhead Ensemble, Tren Brothers, Xylouris White, The Hard Quartet. Session work with Will Oldham, Cat Power, Nina Nastasia, Smog, White Magic.
