The Beatings (5)

Discogs ID: 2712606 Also known as: The Beatin’s Origin: Washington DC, USA Active: c. 2009–2010 Members: Stewart Lupton, Carole Wagner Greenwood Bandcamp: stewartluptonthebeatins.bandcamp.com

Duo/small ensemble formed by Stewart Lupton (formerly of Jonathan Fire*Eater) with Carole Wagner Greenwood. Genres: Blues, Folk, Rock. Released one 12” EP and one digital single before going inactive.

Overview

The Beatings (5) was Lupton’s primary project after The Child Ballads (mid-2000s). While the band is nominally a duo between Lupton and Greenwood, A Little Give And Take reads more as a Lupton solo production — he played and produced virtually all the instruments and mixed the album himself. Greenwood’s contribution is nonetheless substantial: she is credited on six instruments plus additional recording duties and cover design.

The DC scene connection runs deep: Brendan Canty (Fugazi) contributed additional recordings, and Cynthia Connolly (DC photographer and Dischord-associated figure) played banjo. The repertoire on A Little Give And Take draws heavily on American folk and country sources (Bob Nolan, Elizabeth Cotten, Dimitri Tiomkin) with additional lyrics credited to “The Beatin’s.”
