The American Analog Set

Indie rock / slowcore / dream pop band, founded 1995 in Fort Worth, Texas; based in Austin, Texas. Discogs artist ID: 64767. Active approximately 1995–2005, reunion activity 2024.

Also credited as: AmAnSet, Amanset, American Analog, American Analog Set.

Overview

The American Analog Set were a central act in the late-1990s Austin slowcore scene, blending hushed vocals, repetitive melodic figures, and minimalist arrangements into a sound sometimes called “lo-fi post-rock” or “dream pop.” Core songwriter and vocalist Andrew Kenny anchored the band throughout its run. The group released their first records on Emperor Jones (1996–1999), then moved to Tiger Style for their best-known album Know By Heart (2001), before signing to Morr Music for their final studio album Set Free (2005). A career retrospective box set, New Drifters, appeared on Numero Group in 2024.

Biography

Formation and Early Years (1995–1996)

The band formed in Fort Worth, Texas in 1995 before relocating to Austin. Their earliest release was the 7” single “Diana Slowburner II” (Emperor Jones, 1996, cat. EJ-03), a lo-fi guitar-and-organ sketch that set the template for everything that followed. The debut LP The Fun Of Watching Fireworks appeared later that year (Emperor Jones EJ04LP), an eight-track vinyl record recorded with the same languid, reverb-washed approach: slow tempos, layered organ, Andrew Kenny’s half-whispered vocals.

From Our Living Room To Yours and Mid-Period Emperor Jones (1997–1999)

1997 saw three releases: the 7” Magnificent Seventies (EJ-12?), the split/EP Late One Sunday & The Following Morning on Darla Records, and the full-length From Our Living Room To Yours (Emperor Jones EJ14LP). The album featured the same core band and was tagged with styles Indie Rock, Lo-Fi, Post Rock, and Slowcore — the clearest articulation of their aesthetic to that point.

The 1999 CD The Golden Band (Emperor Jones EJ28CD) marked a slight shift toward cleaner digital recording while retaining the hushed dynamics. The album closed with a cello performance by Leslie Sisson on “Will The Real Danny Radnor Please Stand?” — Sisson later became a full member of The Wooden Birds, Andrew Kenny’s next band. A companion 7” single The Only Living Boy Around also appeared in 1999.

Know By Heart and Tiger Style (2001)

The band’s most acclaimed album, Know By Heart (Tiger Style TS-018, 2001), recorded and mixed by the band themselves, was mastered by Jerry Tubb — who went on to master nearly every subsequent AmAnSet release. The record featured notable guest appearances: Ben Gibbard (of Death Cab For Cutie and The Postal Service), Munaf Rayani (of Explosions In The Sky), Laura Krause, and Shawn O’Keefe (also listed as a band member). Artwork by Craig McCaffrey, Aki Nagasaka, and Lizelle Villapando.

The album’s twelve tracks included “Punk As Fuck,” “Like Foxes Through Fences,” and the title track. It cemented the band’s reputation on the indie circuit and connected them firmly to the Austin post-rock scene through the Explosions In The Sky association.

Updates, Promise Of Love, and Wind-Down (2002–2005)

Updates (Tiger Style TS-026, 2002) was a 12” remix EP pulling apart Know By Heart material: Her Space Holiday remixed “Aaron & Maria” and “Know By Heart”; Styrofoam remixed “The Postman” and “We’re Computerizing And We Just Don’t Need You Anymore.” Mastered again by Jerry Tubb; art by Craig McCaffrey.

Promise Of Love (2003, cat. 5846172) was released on the European Labels imprint, marking the band’s wider distribution push into Europe. Eight tracks, mastered by Jerry Tubb, artwork by Craig McCaffrey.

Everything Ends In Spring (Ruined Potential Records, 2005) was a US release, followed closely by Set Free (Morr Music MM057CD, 2005), the final studio LP, released initially in Germany. The album included a cover of “JR” written by Codeine — an explicit acknowledgment of a key slowcore antecedent. Set Free was listed in both Electronic and Rock genres with an Indie Rock style marker.

Compilations and Archival Releases (2001–2024)

Through The 90s: Singles And Unreleased (Emperor Jones EJ42CD, 2001) compiled early singles, B-sides, and live recordings. Leslie Sisson appeared on “It’s All About Us”; Shawn O’Keefe on “Mellow Fellow / Gone To Earth (Live).”

Hard To Find: Singles And Unreleased 2000-2005 (Hometown Fantasy, 2009) gathered the second-era rarities in digital format.

The 2024 New Drifters box set (Numero Group cat. 229) was a major reissue project: a 5-LP box collecting The Fun Of Watching Fireworks, From Our Living Room To Yours, The Golden Band, and a disc of rarities titled Nine Legend Road, all remastered. Produced by Rob Sevier and Ken Shipley of Numero Group; mastered by Jessica Thompson.
