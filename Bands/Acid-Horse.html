Acid Horse

Discogs ID: 28524 Active: 1989 Origin: Chicago, IL, USA / Sheffield, UK sources: [discogs, web]

One-off industrial/electronic collaboration between members of Cabaret Voltaire and Ministry, recorded under pseudonyms to circumvent label contract restrictions. A meeting of Sheffield post-industrial electronics and Chicago’s industrial metal scene.

Live Performances

No evidence of any live performances exists. All available sources — Wikipedia, AllMusic, the Ministry fan archive (prongs.org), and a 2020 interview with Stephen Mallinder — treat Acid Horse as a strictly one-off studio project.

Mallinder confirmed the secretive nature in a 2020 interview: “No it could only be a one off. In fact to this day we’ve never owned up to it really. We were in the studio in Chicago with Marshall Jefferson recording tracks for ‘Groovy, Laidback & Nasty’ and did some moonlighting with Al (Jorgensen) and Chris (Connelly) to do ACID HORSE. EMI had paid for the trip to record with Marshall so would have taken a dim view of us doing a bit on the side, hence we were credited under pseudonyms on the release.” [source: https://www.electricityclub.co.uk/stephen-mallinder-interview/ — as of 2026-07-12]

The project’s context (covert studio session while under EMI contract, pseudonyms specifically to avoid label discovery, no subsequent releases or band activity) makes any live performance highly implausible. No concert listings, reviews, or references to Acid Horse gigs appear in any source consulted.
