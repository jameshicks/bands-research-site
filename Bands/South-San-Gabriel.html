South San Gabriel

Discogs ID: 445919
Origin: Denton, Texas
Labels: Tight Spot Records, Munich Records, Misra Records

Quieter, more introspective side project of Centro-Matic, featuring the same four members: Will Johnson, Matt Pence, Scott Danbom, Mark Hedman. Name first appeared on Centro-Matic’s album South San Gabriel Songs/Music (2000). First standalone release: split 7” with Okkervil River (2002, Tight Spot Records).
