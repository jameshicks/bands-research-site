Tad

Grunge / heavy rock. Seattle, Washington. Active 1988–1999. Discogs ID: not confirmed — 83 releases on Sub Pop.

Led by Tad Doyle (vocals, guitar). One of Sub Pop’s heaviest early signings — darker and more metallic than Mudhoney. Debut single “Daisy / Ritual Device” (SP19, 1988); debut LP God’s Balls (SP27/GRCD 51, 1989), produced by Jack Endino. Second LP 8-Way Santa (SP89, 1991). Toured with Nirvana in Europe, 1989 — one of the earliest grunge European tours. Sub Pop’s cult band: critically admired but commercially overshadowed by Nirvana and Soundgarden.
