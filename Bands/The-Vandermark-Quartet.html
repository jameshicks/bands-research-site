The Vandermark Quartet

Discogs ID: 2290189
Active: 1993–1994
Origin: Chicago, Illinois, USA
Label: Platypus Records (2)

Chicago free jazz / avant-garde quartet led by Ken Vandermark, active in the early 1990s before the formation of the Vandermark 5. The group represents Vandermark’s first sustained recording project as a leader and documents his early Chicago scene work.

Note on lineup: The Discogs stub for this band originally listed Fredrick Lonberg-Holm as a member, but the actual recorded lineup does not include him. The fourth voice was Daniel Scanlan (violin, guitar, cornet). Lonberg-Holm is a close collaborator of Vandermark’s but was not part of this group.

Label Context

All three releases came out on Platypus Records (2) (Discogs ID: 367262), a small Chicago imprint active 1993–97. The catalog numbers PP 001 and PP 002 suggest the Quartet’s two albums were the label’s primary output. This is distinct from Atavistic and Okka Disk — the labels most associated with Vandermark’s later work. The Quartet predates his Atavistic period entirely.

Context in Vandermark’s Catalog

The Vandermark Quartet is Ken Vandermark’s first documented recording project as a leader. The band dissolved around 1994–95, and by 1997 he had formed the Vandermark 5 on Atavistic — with a largely different roster (Fredrick Lonberg-Holm, Jeb Bishop, and others). Kent Kessler carried over from the Quartet into the Vandermark 5, making him the primary continuity between the two projects. Michael Zerang remained an active collaborator in the Chicago scene (DKV Trio context, Peter Brötzmann Chicago Tentet) but did not join the Vandermark 5 proper.

The Quartet’s music sits at the intersection of free jazz, jazz-rock, and avant-garde post-bop — stylistically broader than the later Vandermark 5, which leaned more squarely into free improvisation and European jazz traditions.
