Chris McGregor’s Brotherhood Of Breath

Discogs artist ID: 202310
Also known as: Brotherhood Of Breath
Active: early 1970s–1990s (continuing posthumously under the name)
Origin: London, UK

Large jazz ensemble led by Chris McGregor, uniting South African exile musicians with core figures from the British free improvisation scene. The Brotherhood represents the fullest realization of the fusion between South African township jazz and European free improvisation, a sound rooted in McGregor’s writing and the combined energies of a 10–15 piece band.

History

The Brotherhood grew out of the Blue Notes community that McGregor had brought to London. After the Blue Notes disbanded as a working group, McGregor assembled a larger ensemble drawing on both the exiles and British players he had connected with in the London scene. The result was a band that could swing hard, play free, and draw on South African melodic and rhythmic sources simultaneously.

Their first release — and the first release on Ogun — was Live At Willisau (1974, OG 100), recorded at the Willisau Jazz Festival in Switzerland.
