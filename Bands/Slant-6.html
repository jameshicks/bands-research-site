Slant 6

All-female punk/indie rock trio from Washington, DC. Discogs artist ID: 307923. Active 1992 to November 1995. All releases on Dischord Records, produced with Ian MacKaye and engineered by Don Zientara at Inner Ear Studio. Name variations: “Slant Six,” “Slant6.”
