Boxhead Ensemble

Chicago-based experimental/chamber ensemble. Discogs ID: 147341. Active 1997–present (intermittent). Led by music director Michael Krassner. Associated with the Chicago post-rock and improvised-music underground.

Primary label: Atavistic (early releases 1997–1999), then Hired Hand (2014, 2017). Also released on Astral Spirits / Monofonus Press (2015) and Jealous Butcher Records (2017).

Overview

A rotating ensemble convened by Michael Krassner drawing on Chicago’s experimental underground. The membership list on Discogs is very large (30+ credited members over the years), covering figures from Tortoise, Wilco, Dirty Three, and the Chicago free-jazz and post-rock scenes. Lineups shift per release.

Tim Rutili (of Red Red Meat and Califone) is a confirmed member, listed as a current member on Wikipedia (as of 2026-04-25). His earliest confirmed involvement is the September 2001 European tour (“Stories, Maps and Notes from the Half-Light”), not the Atavistic-era studio records. He does not appear in the credited lineups for Dutch Harbor (1997), The Last Place to Go (1998), or Quartets (2003). Studio credits confirmed: The Unseen Hand (2014), Here: Chicago Sessions (2017), Ancient Music (2016/2023).

Krassner also worked as producer/engineer for Califone (Heron King Blues, 2004; Roots & Crowns, 2006), creating a clear production bridge between the two projects.

Notes (web)

  • Electric Guitar (2017): Described by the label as “the Boxhead Ensemble boiled down to its core” — in practice this is Krassner recording solo electric guitar. Track titles are dedications to past collaborators (Vandermark, Lonberg-Holm, etc.), not performance credits. No ensemble members confirmed on this record. (Jealous Butcher)
  • Here: Chicago Sessions recording context: sessions recorded 2008–2011 at Electrical Audio, Chicago, commissioned for Braden King’s Armenia road movie Here. The music was ultimately cut from the film; released separately in 2017. A separate Armenia release (Dark Companion Records, 2024) drew on different Boxhead Ensemble material for the same film’s MoMA premiere. (Dusted review by Bill Meyer, May 2017)
  • Ancient Music appeared first as a limited CDr in 2016 (Discogs ID: 11338339), then as an expanded CD on Jealous Butcher Records in July 2023. (Discogs)
