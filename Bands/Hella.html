Hella

Math rock band, Sacramento, California. Formed 2001 by Zach Hill (drums) and Spencer Seim (guitar). Operated as a duo through 2005, expanded to a full band for There’s No 666 In Outer Space (2007), then contracted back to the duo for Tripper (2011). Discogs ID: 218092. Name variations: Hella!!, !!A⅃⅃ƎH.

Biography

Origins and the Duo Period (2001–2004)

Hella formed in Sacramento when Spencer Seim and Zach Hill began playing as a guitar-and-drums two-piece. Their debut, Leather Diamond (2001), was self-released on CDr — three tracks establishing the format: densely interlocking guitar figures against Hill’s hyperactive drumming kept within compact song lengths despite the technical extremity.

The band signed to 5 Rue Christine — the math/experimental sublabel of Kill Rock Stars — for their first proper vinyl, Falam Dynasty (7” 33RPM, 2002, cat. ger022), followed immediately by the debut full-length Hold Your Horse Is (CD, 2002, cat. ger018). Engineered and mixed by Aaron Prellwitz (Galen Baudhuin, assistant engineer), mastered by George Horn. The track “The D.Elkan” appears to be a nod to future member Dan Elkan.

In 2003 the band released on two further labels: the Suicide Squeeze 12” EP Bitches Aint Shit But Good People (S-026) featured Seim on guitar and programming, Hill on drums and artwork, with Dan Elkan contributing guest vocals on the closing track “D. Elkan Sings Republic Of R+R.” Narnack Records issued Total Bugs Bunny On Wild Bass (12” 45RPM EP, NCK 7006) the same year — a more electronics-forward release with lacquer cut by JJ Golden.

2004 brought three more releases. Acoustics (CD EP, Toad Records Japan, TOAD 002) was an explicitly acoustic duo set. The first full LP, The Devil Isnt Red (Suicide Squeeze S-031), was recorded and mixed by Jr. Thompson and Tony Cale, mastered by JJ Golden, with cover painting by Zach Hill. Also in 2004 Hella contributed “Stephen Hawking Has A Posse” (recorded by Jr. Thompson) to a split 7” with Four Tet on Ache Records, Canada (ACHE013).

Church Gone Wild / Chirpin Hard (2005)

Released on Suicide Squeeze (S 044), this two-disc set packages separate solo works by each member. Disc 1, Church Gone Wild (59 minutes, 12 movements), is Spencer Seim’s composition — performed, written, and recorded by him alone, with cellist Nick Julian appearing on movements 11 and 12. Disc 2, Chirpin Hard (46 minutes, 12 tracks), is Zach Hill’s — produced and mixed by Hill and Jr. Thompson, engineered by Thompson and Tony Cale. The two discs are formally distinct solo works released under the band name. Discogs credits only Hella as primary; no other acts credited. Note: the brief that spawned this research described this as a split with Xiu Xiu — that is not supported by the Discogs release record.

Also in 2005: Concentration Face & Homeboy (5 Rue Christine, GER044) — a CD+DVD combining four new studio tracks (drums recorded by Jr. Thompson, mixed by Seim) with Japan tour footage directed and edited by R.T. Thomas.

Expanded Lineup and Ipecac Period (2006–2007)

For There’s No 666 In Outer Space (Ipecac Recordings IPC 83 — recorded 2006, released 2007), Hella expanded significantly beyond the duo. The album brought in vocalist Aaron Ross (2), second guitarist Josh Hill (3) (Zach Hill’s cousin), and bassist/keyboardist Carson McWhirter. Guest contributors included saxophonist Skerik (tracks 1, 6, 11), cellist Nick Julian (tracks 1, 4, 5), and additional vocalists Greg Moore (2) and Thom Moore (2) (tracks 2, 4, 6, 7). The album was issued on Ipecac Recordings, Mike Patton’s label. Jonathan Hischke is listed as a band member on the Discogs artist page but does not appear in the release credits.

Hiatus and Tripper (2011)

After the expanded lineup period the band went quiet for several years. Tripper (Sargent House SH064, 2011) returned to the core duo. Produced, engineered, and mixed by Andy Morin (co-producer with Hella), with recording split between Morin and Seim. Issued by Sargent House on LP. Styles: Math Rock, Avantgarde, Noise, Post Rock, Thrash.

Later Activity (2013)

Santa’s Little Hella (Joyful Noise Recordings, JNR125.11, 2013) — a single-sided limited-edition flexi-disc 7” 45RPM. The only known release after Tripper.
