Music Revelation Ensemble

American free funk / free jazz ensemble. Discogs artist ID: 341074. Led by guitarist and vocalist James Blood Ulmer. Active from c.1980.

Overview

The Music Revelation Ensemble was James Blood Ulmer’s primary vehicle for exploring harmolodics (Ornette Coleman’s theoretical framework) in a funk and free jazz context. Core members included Ulmer on guitar/vocals, David Murray on tenor saxophone, Ronald Shannon Jackson on drums, and Amin Ali on electric bass. The ensemble occupied the intersection of no-wave, free funk, and loft jazz.
