Manitoba

Recording alias of Dan Snaith (real name: Daniel Victor Snaith; Discogs ID: 438883), used for his early electronic releases on Leaf from 2000 to 2003. Following a threatened lawsuit from punk singer Handsome Dick Manitoba (Richard Blum, of The Dictators), Snaith retired the name and relaunched the project as Caribou from 2005. Discogs artist ID: 517. Based in UK (released on Leaf); originally from Dundas, Ontario, Canada.

Other aliases used by Dan Snaith: Daphni, Lb Lynam, Caribou.

Overview

Dan Snaith signed to Leaf in 2000 and released all of his Manitoba material on that label across a run of EPs and two full albums. The music drew on IDM, Breaks, and Leftfield electronics alongside more abstract and experimental textures, and attracted early comparisons to the work of his friend and sometime collaborator Four Tet (Kieran Hebden). The 2003 album Up In Flames was the culmination of the Manitoba period, earning wider critical notice and the slightly awkward press tag “folktronica.”

In 2004, Richard Blum — better known as Handsome Dick Manitoba, singer of the New York punk band The Dictators — threatened legal action over the use of the name. Snaith changed the alias to Caribou and has operated under that name (and the DJ alias Daphni) ever since.

Name Change: Manitoba → Caribou

In 2004, Richard Blum — performing as Handsome Dick Manitoba — threatened to sue over the use of the name “Manitoba.” Blum had been performing as Handsome Dick Manitoba since his time fronting New York punk band The Dictators (active from the mid-1970s). Snaith agreed to change his alias and adopted the name Caribou, under which he released The Milk Of Human Kindness (Leaf, 2005). The Manitoba alias has not been used since.

Dan Snaith’s Broader Career Context

Under the Manitoba alias Snaith also appeared in:

  • A Pulse Train (group, Discogs ID: 5637257)
  • Caribou Vibration Ensemble (group, Discogs ID: 4085257) — jazz-influenced ensemble featuring, among others, Marshall Allen; active around 2010–2014

Post-Manitoba aliases:

  • Caribou — primary artist name from 2005 onward; albums on Leaf, Domino, Merge Records, City Slang through to Honey (Merge, 2024)
  • Daphni — DJ/club alias (Jiaolong label)
  • Lb Lynam — occasional alias
