Transmission (12)

New York City experimental duo — later a trio — active in the early 1980s and documented on a 2006 EP. Discogs artist ID: 2957655.

Transmission was formed by drummer Jonathan Kane and Tunisian-born saxophonist Daniel Galli-Duani around 1981–1982, running concurrently with the formation of Swans. Both Kane and Galli-Duani were founding participants in the earliest Swans configuration; the Discogs profile for the band notes that Transmission “was one-half of the original version of the Swans” and that much of their material “evolved into Swans tracks, including the standout ‘Weakling’.” Michael Gira was reportedly fond enough of Transmission’s live performances to mix some of their shows. Kane’s own timeline on Wikipedia confirms that Transmission formed alongside Swans in 1981–1983 (Wikipedia, “Jonathan Kane”).

Although active in the early 1980s, Transmission’s only official release appeared decades later: a six-track EP titled Transmission (2006) on Radium (3) (catalog number TOE-CD-804), the sublabel of Table Of The Elements run by Jeff Hunt. The 2006 recording added Sal Palazzo on bass (appearing on track 5, “Surrender”). Styles on Discogs: Experimental, No Wave, Space Rock.

Members

Both core members of Transmission are also listed as members of Swans on Discogs.

Jonathan Kane (born November 4, 1956, New York City) — drummer, composer. Co-founder of Swans and previously a member of Circus Mort (with Gira). A longtime collaborator of Rhys Chatham, serving as the sole drummer on Chatham’s large guitar-orchestra works from 1988 onward. Solo artist on Radium (3) (February, 2005; I Looked At The Sun, 2006; Jet Ear Party, 2009). Attended Berklee College of Music 1977–79; began professional career in blues bands in 1974.

Daniel Galli-Duani (b. 1941, Tunisia) — saxophonist. Credited on the 1982 Swans EP (“Swans,” Labor Records, LAB-17) as Daniel Galliduani. His Discogs credits span 1982–2025, though outside the Swans debut, the Transmission EP, and a 1994 vocal/lyrics credit on Future Primitive (Wildcat!), his Discogs presence is sparse. He also appears on Filth (L.P.#1, E.P.#1) 1982/83 (Young God Records, 1990) and the 2015 Mute/Young God Filth reissue.

Sal Palazzo — bassist, appearing only on track 5 of the 2006 EP. His sole Discogs credit is this release (year active: 2006, ID: 1357960). Does not meet the multi-project threshold for a stub.
