The Mekons

Discogs ID: 263823
Origin: Leeds, UK
Active: 1977–present

Long-running Leeds post-punk/country-punk collective. Formed at the University of Leeds, 1977. Co-led throughout by Jon Langford (2) and Tom Greenhalgh. Other long-term members: Sally Timms (vocals), Susie Honeyman (fiddle), Steve Goulding (drums), Lu Edmonds (bass). Title track “Where Were You?” on Where Were You- (Independent Music From Leeds 1978-1989) (CD1, track 1 — their 1978 debut single gives the compilation its name). Released New York on ROIR (A154, 1987). Key albums include Fear And Whiskey (Sin, 1985) and The Mekons Rock ‘n’ Roll (Twin/Tone, 1989). Among the most prolific and critically respected UK indie bands of the 1980s–1990s.

Long-term US home: Quarterstick Records (1993–2015, 34 releases). Key Quarterstick releases include I ♥ Mekons (compilation, QS19CD, 1993), Retreat From Memphis (QS26CD, 1994), Fear And Whiskey reissue (QS80CD, 2002). Producer on Quarterstick sessions: “Ducky” (credited); engineer Dave Trumfio.
