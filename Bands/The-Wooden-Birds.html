The Wooden Birds

Indie pop/folk band from Austin, Texas, active 2008–2012. Discogs artist ID: 1429462

Side project of Andrew Kenny, frontman of The American Analog Set. Released two full-length albums — Magnolia (2009, Morr Music) and Two Matchsticks (2011, Barsuk Records) — before dissolving after Kenny relocated from Austin.

Context and Origin

The Wooden Birds emerged in the wake of The American Analog Set’s extended hiatus. AmAnSet, the slowcore/post-rock outfit Andrew Kenny had led since the mid-1990s in Fort Worth and Austin, Texas, released their final studio album Set Free in 2005 on Morr Music and went quiet. The Wooden Birds began around 2008 as Kenny’s active vehicle during that interval, retaining the same Austin scene and some of the same collaborators.

The band’s name and identity signaled a departure from AmAnSet’s drone-heavy minimalism toward more conventional song structures, acoustic textures, and folk-pop production — though Kenny’s quietly melodic voice and plainspoken lyrical style carried over intact. Morr Music, the Berlin indie label that had released AmAnSet’s Set Free, put out Magnolia in 2009, maintaining the label relationship. For the second album the band moved to Barsuk Records in Seattle, home of Death Cab For Cutie — a shift that brought Ben Gibbard in as a guest.

Sound

The Wooden Birds occupy the intersection of indie folk, acoustic pop, and low-key indie rock. Kenny’s songwriting foregrounds melody and lyric over texture; the arrangements are spare but warm, with guitar, organ, and a modest rhythm section. Magnolia leans toward indie rock production (Discogs genre: Rock / Indie Rock). Two Matchsticks is broader in scope — Discogs tags it Acoustic, Indie Rock, Pop Rock — and more polished, co-mixed with Louie Lino. The Montague Street EP offers acoustic reworkings of four Magnolia tracks, stripping them to their essentials.

Morr Music (Discogs ID: 196, Berlin) also released The American Analog Set’s Set Free (2005), making it the natural home for Kenny’s follow-up project. Barsuk Records (Discogs ID: 37418, Seattle) is home of Death Cab For Cutie, which likely facilitated the Ben Gibbard guest appearance on Two Matchsticks.

Connections

  • The American Analog Set — direct predecessor; Kenny, Wingo, and label relationships carry over; AmAnSet went on hiatus 2005, resumed with New Drifters (2024, Numero Group)
  • Ben Gibbard (Death Cab For Cutie, The Postal Service) — guest on Two Matchsticks; shares the Barsuk label home
  • David Wingo — musician on both albums, also active in Ola Podrida and as a film composer
  • Leslie Sisson — core performer on both records; later fronted Moving Panoramas
  • Matthew Pond (Matt Pond PA) — guest performer on Two Matchsticks
  • Morr Music — Berlin label connecting AmAnSet and Wooden Birds catalogs
  • Barsuk Records — Seattle indie label, connecting the project to the Death Cab orbit

Andrew Kenny Solo and Post-Wooden Birds

  • Home: Volume V (2003, Post-Parlo Records, Discogs ID: 4641795) — solo release predating Wooden Birds
  • Two Step (2015, Hometown Fantasy label, Discogs ID: 31827776) — film score under Kenny’s own name; includes piano version of “Long Time To Lose It” from Two Matchsticks
  • The American Analog Set New Drifters (2024, Numero Group) — Kenny plays guitar, percussion, organ, marimba, vibraphone, and handles recording and mixing
