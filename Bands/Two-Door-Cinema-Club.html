Overview

Two Door Cinema Club are a Northern Irish indie rock band formed in 2007 in Bangor and Donaghadee, County Down, comprising Alex Trimble (vocals, guitar, synths), Kevin Baird (bass, vocals), and Sam Halliday (guitar, vocals). Their sound blends indie rock with synth-pop and electro inflections, and their catalogue is concentrated in the 2010s, with 14 releases in that decade following four in the late 2000s. Production partnerships define the records: Jacknife Lee and Matt Bishop (3) each share 50 credits with the band, while Robin Schmidt, Mike Crossey, and Philippe Zdar also appear prominently. Their label history runs from early tastemaker homes at Kitsuné Music and Cooperative Music through Parlophone and on to Glassnote and [PIAS] in the 2019–2025 period.
