Dag Nasty

Discogs ID: 293162 Active: ~1985–1988; reunions thereafter (through at least 2025) Origin: Washington, DC, USA Labels: Dischord Records (early); Giant Records (2) (later)

DC melodic hardcore band. Formed by Brian Baker (ex-Minor Threat) and Dave Smalley. Part of the Revolution Summer milieu. A proto-melodic-hardcore act whose approach influenced the 1990s melodic punk/HC movement. Multiple vocalists across their catalog.
