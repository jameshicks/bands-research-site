The School (2)

Cardiff-based Welsh indie-pop band formed in 2007. Discogs artist ID: 1104173. Signed to Madrid’s Elefant Records for their entire commercial career, releasing three studio albums between 2010 and 2015. Core songwriting by vocalist Liz Hunt (and, partly, Simon Stone). Named variously — member Simon Stone (2) performs under the alias Simon Love, which gives the band’s name a double meaning.

Formation and Early Years (2007–2009)

The School formed in Cardiff in 2007. Their earliest Discogs entry is a self-released four-track CDr EP, also titled The School (2007, Not On Label), containing the songs “Summer’s Here,” “Shoulder,” “Valentine,” and “All I Wanna Do” — all of which would resurface on later records, indicating these were foundational demos. No production credits are attached to the CDr.

Contact with Elefant Records came quickly. By 2008 the band appeared on the Spanish label with the double-A-side single “All I Wanna Do / Valentine” (ER-1131-S, Spain) — their first professionally produced release, recorded and produced by Ian Catt. The single documented an early lineup clearly: Ryan Cox (6) on bass, Rob Jones (10) on drums, Ryan (22) on glockenspiel and percussion, Ceri Frost on electric guitar and backing vocals, Liz Hunt on keyboards, lead vocals, and songwriting, Steph Doble on electric violin, and Rosie (9) on acoustic guitar and backing vocals. Artwork was by Luis Calvo (2), mastering by Xavier Alarcón — both recurring Elefant names. Simon Stone (2) received a co-writing credit on the A-side.

Loveless Unbeliever (2010)

The debut album, Loveless Unbeliever (Elefant Records, ER-1151LP, 2010), was released on vinyl LP and drew together material developed since the band’s formation. Produced and recorded by Ian Catt, mastered by Andy Le Vien, with photography by Alison Wonderland (2) and artwork by Luis Calvo (2). The LP was a thirteen-track collection running well under forty minutes in total, with virtually every track built from a streamlined verse-chorus construction — an intentional homage to the economy of early-1960s girl-group pop and the jangle-pop tradition.

Reading Too Much Into Things Like Everything (2012)

The second album, Reading Too Much Into Things Like Everything (Elefant Records, ER-1166LP, 2012), was a twelve-track LP released in a limited edition vinyl pressing across Europe. The Discogs credits entry for this release carries no additional personnel, suggesting the Elefant submission was minimal — personnel beyond the primary artist credit are not documented in the DB for this record. The album title is characteristically wordy and self-aware, in keeping with the band’s affectionate awkwardness.

Wasting Away And Wondering (2015)

The third and most recent studio album, Wasting Away And Wondering (Elefant Records, ER-1201LP, 2015), was released as a limited numbered vinyl LP in Spain. It is the most fully documented of the three records in terms of personnel credits, and shows a somewhat evolved lineup from the debut. Production and recording shifted from Ian Catt to Rob Jones (10) — who here performs as a musician (drums, trumpet, saxophone) in addition to producing and recording the album.

The producer shift to Rob Jones is significant. Jones (Discogs artist ID: 1617387) operates under the aliases Gene Dudley, Sailor Hawkins, The Gene Dudley Group, and The Voluntary Butler Scheme — the latter being his primary recording project (a one-man-band indie act). Stephen Black (ID: 2074408) appears on saxophone; his other credits are not captured here. The 38-second instrumental “My Heart’s Beating Overtime” functions as an interlude separating the two halves.

Genre: Pop; style: Indie Pop — reflecting a somewhat lighter, more direct categorisation than the debut.

Connections and Context

The School’s Elefant Records placement puts them within a network of UK and European indie-pop acts that coalesced around the label from the late 1990s onward. Ian Catt’s involvement on the debut connects them directly to the Saint Etienne production lineage and to Trembling Blue Stars via Catt’s decade-long Shinkansen work. Catt had been producing for Elefant since 2000 and The School’s Loveless Unbeliever fits neatly into that strand of his career.

The Liz Hunt / Simon Stone (2) / Simon Love connection gives the band a link to The Loves, a UK pop act on which both appear. Simon Stone’s alias “Simon Love” is likely the source of the band name — The School of Love, or simply Love/The School — though this is inferred from the DB data rather than documented in writing.

Rob Jones (10) as producer of the third album represents a shift toward a more self-contained, band-produced mode. His Voluntary Butler Scheme project is adjacent UK indie-pop territory.
