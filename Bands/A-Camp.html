A Camp

Swedish indie pop side project led by Nina Persson (of The Cardigans). Discogs artist ID: 41384.

Overview

Members: Nina Persson (vocals), Nathan Larson (bass, various), Niclas Frisk (piano, guitar, harmonica). Based in Stockholm. Released on Stockholm Records.

The debut self-titled album (2001) was produced by Mark Linkous of Sparklehorse, who also contributed sampler, guitar, vocals, and Chamberlin synthesizer. A large ensemble was assembled for the record, including Jonathan Donahue (saw), Joan Wasser (violin), Jane Scarpantoni (cello), Julia Kent (cello), and others.
