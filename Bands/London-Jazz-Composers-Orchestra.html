London Jazz Composers Orchestra

Discogs ID: 1311998 Also known as: Barry Guy London Jazz Composers Orchestra, London Jazz Composers’ Orchestra

Large-scale British free improvisation orchestra organized by bassist Barry Guy. One of the major large-ensemble vehicles of European free improvisation, active from the early 1970s. First recording Ode (Incus, 1972). Later recorded for FMP, Intakt Records, and others through Double Trouble Two (1998).
