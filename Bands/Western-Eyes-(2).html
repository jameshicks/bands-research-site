Western Eyes (2)

NYC rock group, 1984. Discogs artist ID: 13722052.

Overview

Western Eyes (2) was a New York City band active in 1984, sharing the same core membership as the earlier Tot Rocket And The Twins: Robert Poss (guitar, lead vocals), Ron Spitzer (bass, lead vocals), and Andrew Halbreich (guitar, harmonica, vocals). The group recorded one release — a self-titled LP on Trace Elements Records, produced by Nicolas Collins. It represents the direct precursor to Band Of Susans, which Poss formed two years later; Susan Tallman is even credited as “Cultural Attaché” on the Western Eyes LP, bridging this group to the later band.
