Epic45

UK ambient-pop duo formed in Wheaton Aston, Staffordshire, England in 1999. Discogs artist ID: 197560. Core members: Ben Holton (guitar, vocals, synthesizer, lyrics, photography) and Rob Glover (guitar, bass, electronics, sampler). Centred on the Wayside And Woodland Recordings label, which Holton and Glover co-run; the label is the primary home for their output from 2008 onward. Wider Discogs name variation: “Epic 45”.

The duo describe their music as ambient pop. Their recordings draw on field recordings, tape manipulation, sampled textures, and conventional guitar-and-drums instrumentation, layered into atmospheric slow-builds. Photography and visual design — mostly by Holton — are integral to each release.
