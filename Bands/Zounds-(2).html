Note on “(2)” designation: Discogs labels this band “Zounds (2)” because an unrelated artist (Discogs ID 87382, member: Dominic Sol Perez-Silva) holds the bare “Zounds” entry. There is no “Zounds (1)” — the unqualified entry is simply a different, unrelated act. The “(2)” is a Discogs-internal disambiguation only and does not reflect any common-use numbering. The vault filename should remain Zounds (2).md to match the Discogs entry; when referring to the band in prose, use “Zounds” without qualification.

UK post-punk/anarcho band from Reading, England. Formed 1977, broke up late 1982, later reuniting in 2001. “The Curse Of Zounds” (Rough Trade, 1981, 10p) — Adam Kidron credited as engineer and producer.
