The Kills

Discogs artist ID: 202601 Origin: UK/USA Active: 2000–present Label: Domino Records Members: Alison Mosshart (vocals), Jamie Hince (guitar)

Blues-punk/garage rock duo. Mosshart is British-American; Hince is British. Signed to Domino from debut. Discography on Domino:

  • Keep On Your Mean Side (2003, WIGCD 124)
  • No Wow (2004, WIGCD 149)
  • Midnight Boom (2008)
  • Blood Pressures (2011)
  • Ash & Ice (2016)

Mosshart is also vocalist of Dead Weather (with Jack White). Hince married Kate Moss (2011, divorced 2015). 223 release entries on Domino.
