Tren Brothers

Discogs ID: 952391
Also known as: Tren Bros
Active: 1998–2009
Origin: Australia
Genre: Instrumental rock, post-rock, experimental

Australian instrumental duo formed in 1998 by Mick Turner (guitar, melodica, harmonica, keyboards, loops) and Jim White (2) (drums, percussion) — the guitarist and drummer of Dirty Three. The group operates as a side project and creative outlet distinct from Dirty Three, with a quieter, more abstract character. Their recordings are sparse, spacious, and largely wordless, with Turner’s unhurried guitar figures set against White’s characteristic open-kit playing. The project released sporadically across a decade, accumulating a small but cohesive catalog on Drag City, Secretly Canadian, Western Vinyl, Chapter Music, and Inertia Music.

The name variation “The Marquis De Tren” appears in Discogs as a Mick Turner alias, suggesting Turner occasionally operated under an extended version of the project name.

Context

Tren Brothers operates clearly in the orbit of Dirty Three — same two members, similar instrumental palette, overlapping labels (Drag City, the Dirty Three’s US home from the mid-1990s). The aesthetic is stripped back relative to Dirty Three: White’s drums are more open and less dramatic; Turner’s guitar is unhurried, often looped or melodica-driven. Several recurring engineers (Greg Norman, Michael Krassner) also appear in the broader Australian post-rock and Chicago indie scenes.

The Blue Trees compilation is the most accessible entry point and the only full-length, though its mixed nature (half Tren Brothers tracks, half Turner solo) means it functions more as a survey than a unified album. The Dirty Three’s broader family of collaborators and labels connects the group to the Australian underground (Chapter Music, Inertia) and the US post-rock/indie world simultaneously.

J. Walker (2) is also known as Machine Translations, suggesting connection to the Melbourne indie scene.
