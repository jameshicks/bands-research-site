Quix-o-tic

(Obsidian filename sanitized: * → -. Full Discogs name: Quixotic. Name variation: “Quixotic.“)

Washington, DC alternative/indie band. Discogs artist ID: 558111. Active 1997–2002. Described as blending R&B and doo-wop with a gothic outlook. All releases on Ixor Stix except the final album (Mortal Mirror) on Kill Rock Stars.
