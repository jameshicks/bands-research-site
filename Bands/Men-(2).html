Men (2)

Electronic / dance-punk group. New York City. Active 2009–2013. Discogs artist ID: 1406836. Also known as MEN; alias listing: JD Samson & MEN.

Overview

Men formed in New York around 2009 out of the hiatus of Le Tigre, anchored by JD Samson and Michael O’Neill (5) as the consistent core, with Johanna Fateman, Ginger Brooks Takahashi, Emily Roysdon, and Tami Hart all credited as contributors at various points. The project inherited Le Tigre’s queer feminist politics and dance-floor sensibility but pushed into harder, more rock-inflected territory — less sample-collage, more live guitars and physicality. Two albums appeared: Talk About Body (IAMSOUND Records, 2011) and Labor (Men Make Music, 2013), after which activity ceased.

The Discogs page lists the current core as JD Samson and Michael O’Neill, with Fateman, Takahashi, Roysdon, and Tami Hart designated “former contributors.”

Biography

Origins and Self-Released EP (2009)

Men began as a self-released CDr EP simply titled Men (Not On Label, 2009), containing three tracks: “Off Our Backs,” “Credit Card Babie$,” and “Simultaneously” — all three of which would appear in reworked form on later releases. The lineup at this stage drew on overlapping circles from Le Tigre and NYC queer/art communities.

Early Singles and Label Pickup (2010)

Two singles brought the band to wider attention. “Off Our Backs” appeared as a 7” on IAMSOUND Records (Europe, cat. 88697806237, 2010) with a radio edit on the A-side and a Jeppe “Money Is A Major Issue” remix on the B-side — connecting the band to the Scandinavian electro-pop axis. “Credit Card Babie$” appeared as a 12” on UK label Trouble Records (3) (cat. DUDE017, 2010), with remixes by Lauren Flax, Stereogamous, and Barletta — placing them in the NYC queer dance underground. Men also collaborated with Brazilian electro act CSS on a split 7” Rip Off / City Grrrl (CSS (5) label, cat. CSS-666, 2011): CSS remixed “Rip Off” (with a Julie Cafritz-associated rework credited) and Men remixed CSS’s “City Grrrl.”

Talk About Body (2011)

The debut album Talk About Body (IAMSOUND Records, cat. IAM 045, 2011) was recorded and produced primarily by the band and Jordan Lieb (aka Black Light Smoke). Mixed by Eric Broucek; mastered by Simon Davey. The credited lineup on the sleeve lists JD Samson, Ginger Brooks Takahashi, and Michael O’Neill (5) as “Men Is”; Johanna Fateman receives additional production credits on several tracks; Emily Roysdon contributes lyrics to four tracks and additional vocals to three. Track-level credits indicate sampling of a Carolyn Brandy composition on “Off Our Backs” (sample of “Temaya Sister of the Fishes”); and “My Family” is based on a Joan Armatrading / Pam Nestor composition. Cover artwork by Anne de Vries; design by Ginger Brooks Takahashi. Band portrait photography by Cass Bird.

Genres tagged on Discogs: Electronic, Pop; styles: Electro, Synth-pop. The album’s 12 tracks run from clattering dance-punk (“Take Your Shirt Off,” “Credit Card Babies”) to slower, more textured pieces.

Labor (2013)

Labor (Men Make Music label, cat. none, 2013) appeared as an LP. The core credited as “Men core member” on Discogs is JD Samson and Michael O’Neill only — no Fateman, Takahashi, or Roysdon credit. The album leans heavily on guest co-production: each track credits a different collaborator as performer/co-producer: XXXchange (Alex Epton) on “Power Strobe,” “Neon Poles,” and “Fucked Up”; Yuksek on “All The Way Thru”; Alex Suarez (of Cobra Starship) on the majority of remaining tracks; and Jordan Lieb on “Next” and “I Don’t Care.” Mastered by Sarah Register (of Talk Normal). Genre: Electronic, Pop; style: Dance-pop. The label “Men Make Music” appears to be the band’s own imprint (Discogs label ID 1212814, parent listed as ID 212).

After Labor the band ceased releasing material under this name. JD Samson continued under her own name and with other projects; a 2018 release under her name appeared on YouTooCanWoo.

Open Questions

  • Johanna Fateman’s involvement post-Talk About Body: Discogs credits her only on Talk About Body (no credit on Labor). Whether she departed the band or simply stepped back from recording is not documented in Discogs.
  • Tami Hart’s specific releases: listed as a former contributor on the Discogs artist page but does not appear in track-level credits on either album in the DB.
  • Emily Roysdon’s departure timing: credited on Talk About Body but absent from Labor.
  • Labor release logistics: catalog number listed as “none” on Discogs; the Men Make Music label (ID 1212814) shows parent label ID 212, which may be a generic Discogs placeholder. Unclear if this was a fully self-distributed release.
  • Live band lineup: Discogs does not document touring/live configurations, which may have differed from recording personnel.
  • Any activity after 2013: Discogs shows no releases under Men (2) post-Labor. JD Samson has releases under her own name (2018, YouTooCanWoo). No reunion activity confirmed in DB.
