Experimental noise, drone, and stoner/space-rock group from Los Angeles. Described on Discogs as “wide-ranging stoner and space-rock band.” One of the founding acts and most prolific artists on Not Not Fun Records — 28 NNF releases (2005–2024), more than any other NNF artist.

Is Robedoor Britt Brown’s Primary Project?

Yes. Of Britt Brown’s performing projects, Robedoor has the most releases (28 on NNF alone), the longest continuous run (2005–2024), and the widest distribution across labels. His other groups (Pocahaunted, Weirdo/Begeirdo, etc.) are secondary. Robedoor is the project most consistently associated with him on NNF.

Sound and Evolution

Robedoor began in the 2005–2007 period as a heavy noise/drone act releasing small-run CDrs and tapes on micro-labels (Chondritic Sound, Sycophanticide, Tanzprocesz, Arbor). Titles like Grendel Glare, Greater Heresy, Ritual Heirs, and Rancor Keeper signal a blackened noise-drone aesthetic rooted in the Pacific/LA underground scene of the mid-2000s.

By the LP era (2009–2011) — Raiders (NNF167), Bored Fortress (NNF202), Too Down To Die (NNF237) — the group expanded toward slower, more textured stoner and space-rock, with multiple pressings indicating commercial traction within the experimental underground.

Primal Sphere (2013, Hands In The Dark) and New Age Sewage (2017) mark a later, more refined phase with European label distribution. Negative Legacy (2019, Deathbomb Arc) extended the catalog with a LA noise-adjacent label.

Drunk On Poison: 2005–2008 (2020, Deathbomb Arc) is a retrospective compilation covering the early tape/CDr era.

Hidden Ascension (2024) is a reissue of the 2006 Ruralfaune release, co-issued by Music Fellowship and NNF — Britt Brown handled design.
