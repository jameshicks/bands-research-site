Neptune (5)

Experimental / industrial noise rock ensemble founded 1994 in Boston, Massachusetts; relocated to Portland, Oregon. Discogs artist ID: 682980. Wikipedia: Neptune (American band).

Overview

Neptune was initiated by sculptor and musician Jason Sanford as a project built around hand-fabricated instruments made from scrap steel, found objects, and industrial salvage. The band is described in its own Discogs profile as having wrenched “its sound spatter on self-built instruments to often confounded audiences around the world” — a line that captures the core aesthetic. Instruments evolved from purely percussive and metallic origins to incorporate oscillators, synthesizers (homemade and commercial), and electronics, while retaining an emphasis on constructed or modified gear.

The band has operated with at least eleven lineups across its thirty-year history. The core creative nucleus from roughly 2000 onward — Sanford, Mark Pearson (3), and Daniel Paul Boucher — reactivated together in 2023 after a period of dormancy and recorded a new LP (Play Some Music, Sleeping Giant Glossolalia, 2026). Long-serving recording engineer and multi-instrumentalist Kevin Micka — also credited under the alias Animal Hospital — was central to the sound of the 2005–2013 output.

Neptune has shared stages with The Ex, Mission of Burma, Lightning Bolt, The Flaming Lips, Blonde Redhead, Melt-Banana, Charles Hayward, Liars, Black Dice, James Chance & the Contortions, Gang Gang Dance, Six Finger Satellite, Wolf Eyes, Deerhunter, Arab on Radar, and Parts & Labor (per Wikipedia). As of 2024 the band had played over 700 shows.

Discogs genres: Electronic, Rock. Styles: Experimental, Industrial, Abstract, Noise, Krautrock, Dark Ambient, Math Rock (varies by release).
