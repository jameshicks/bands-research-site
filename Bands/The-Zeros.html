The Zeros

Chicano punk band from Chula Vista, California. Discogs artist ID: 261758. Name variations: The Zeroes, The Zero’s, Zeros.

Overview

The Zeros formed in Chula Vista (San Diego area) in 1976 and are widely cited as one of the first Chicano punk bands. Core members were Javier Escovedo (lead guitar, lead vocals), Hector Penalosa (bass), Karton Chenelle / Baba Chenelle (drums), and Robert Alan Lopez (rhythm guitar, vocals) — the latter better known later as El Vez, the “Mexican Elvis.” Victor Penalosa (Hector’s brother) also played with the band at various points.

The band self-produced their debut single “Don’t Push Me Around / Wimp” (BOMP!, 1977, Bomp 110), one of the first punk singles released on BOMP! Records. Their second single “Wild Week-End / Beat Your Heart Out” (BOMP!, 1978, Bomp 118) was co-produced with Greg Shaw (2) — the label’s founder and driving curatorial force. A third single “They Say That (Everything’s Alright) / Getting Nowhere Fast” (Test Tube Records, 1980) appeared shortly before the band’s initial breakup.

The band were active 1976–1980, reformed in the early 1990s, toured Spain in 2007, reunited for a West Coast tour in 2009, and undertook a brief East Coast tour in 2010. The most recent release under the name is a 2024 co-release LP between BOMP! and Munster Records.

Javier Escovedo is a brother of singer-songwriter Alejandro Escovedo.

Research Queue

  • The Discogs member credit for “Kevin Chanel” (ID: 5088009) and “Karton Chenelle” (ID: 549089) suggest two separate people, but the sleeve credits on actual releases only list Karton/Baba Chenelle as drummer — confirm whether Kevin Chanel is the same person as Karton Chenelle or a distinct member from a different era.
  • Robert Alan Lopez / El Vez left The Zeros before their final single (he does not appear on the 1980 Test Tube recordings) — confirm what year he departed and when he joined Catholic Discipline.
  • The 1992 “I Don’t Wanna” single credits Richard Blitz as both engineer and producer, while the Right Now! album credits “Richard ‘Blitz’ Livoni” as recorded-by — confirm these are the same person (Discogs has both artist ID 2084637 “Richard Blitz” and 1343619 “Richard Livoni”).
