Refused

Swedish hardcore punk / post-hardcore band from Umeå, Sweden. Discogs artist ID: 219574. Active 1991–1998, reunited 2012, then permanently from late 2014. Disbanded permanently 21 December 2025.

Overview

Refused formed in Umeå in 1991 around vocalist Dennis Lyxzén and drummer David Sandström, both already active in the local hardcore scene via Final Exit. They released a string of albums through the Swedish label Startrec and Burning Heart Records before their landmark 1998 album The Shape Of Punk To Come, which would become one of the most influential records in post-hardcore. The band split immediately after its release following mounting internal tensions, infamously walking off stage mid-tour. The split itself became legendary — the live bootleg album Refused Are Fucking Dead (2006) documents the chaos.

A 2012 reunion for festival touring ended in a second hiatus. They returned permanently in late 2014, releasing Freedom (2015) and War Music (2019) on Epitaph. The band’s final show was in Umeå on 21 December 2025.

Key Release: The Shape Of Punk To Come (1998)

Discogs release ID: 381102. Recorded and produced by Andreas Nilsson, Eskil Lövström, Pelle Henricsson, and the band themselves. The record incorporated jazz, electronic, and free music elements into hardcore punk, with upright double bass (Jakob Munck), cello (Magnus Björklund), violin (Torbjörn Näsbom), and melodica alongside the core band. Layout and art direction: Dennis Lyxzén and José Saxlund.

Key Release: Songs To Fan The Flames Of Discontent (1996)

Discogs release ID: 369562. Produced by Pelle Gunnerfeldt, recorded by Pelle Henricsson and Eskil Lövström. The album that broke the band internationally in post-hardcore circles.

Key Release: War Music (2019)

Discogs release ID: 14271650. Self-produced by David Sandström and Kristofer Steen; vocals produced by Patrik Berger (tracks 1, 2, 4–7, 9, 10). Mixed by Martin “Konie” Ehrencrona. Mastered by Magnus Lindberg.
