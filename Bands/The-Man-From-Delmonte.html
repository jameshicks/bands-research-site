The Man From Delmonte

Manchester guitar-pop band. Discogs artist ID: 300953. Name variations: “Man From Delmonte”, “The Man From Del Monte”.

Overview

Manchester-based indie guitar-pop band closely associated with Bop Cassettes, on which they released a run of singles in 1989–1990. One of the more enduring acts from the Bop Cassettes stable; still active as of 2025 (self-released “Better Things”). Contributing act on the Manchester North Of England compilation (1988), their earliest Discogs-documented appearance.
