Overview

An Australian rock group formed in Perth in 1997 and later based in Melbourne, The Drones draw on blues, garage, and psychedelic rock with a sprawling range of cited influences stretching from Van Morrison and Townes Van Zandt to Bad Brains and Ornette Coleman. Their catalogue is weighted heavily toward the 2000s — 11 releases that decade against 4 in the 2010s — with core output appearing on Bang! Records and ATP Recordings. Gareth Liddiard (vocals, guitar) and Fiona Kitschin (bass) anchored the long-running lineup, with Dan Luscombe joining on lead guitar in 2007; drummer Mike Noga served from 2004 to 2014 and died on 27 August 2020. Frequent collaborators include Aaron Cupples, Loki Lockwood, and producer Burke Reid.
