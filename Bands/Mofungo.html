Mofungo

Discogs artist ID: 254916
Alternative rock / No Wave / post-punk group from New York City. Active c. 1979–1993. Central figures in the downtown NYC experimental rock scene alongside Information and The Scene Is Now.

Overview

Mofungo formed in New York in 1979, emerging from the No Wave ferment around CBGB and the downtown loft scene. Their debut, Elementary Particles (7”, Living Legend Records (4), 1979; cat. LL 001), was produced by Chris Stamey of The dB’s and mixed by Jim Jordan at Masterdisk. Four tracks — “At The Shop,” “Ya-Da,” “5 Gs,” “Gato Perdido” — capture the raw early lineup of Jeff McGovern, Jim Posner, Robert Sietsema, and Willie Klein.

In 1980 they appeared on Tape #1 (cassette, Not On Label), a three-band split shared with Information and Blinding Headache, documenting an overlapping downtown NYC scene; Mofungo contribute eleven tracks. The cassette features an expanded lineup with Rick Brown, Gary Larson, Philip Dray, Kym Bond, and Seth Gunning on organ.

The early 1980s saw a run of self-produced and independently released records: End Of The World (cassette, 1981, Mofungo Music), produced by Carol Parkinson; El Salvador (7”, Rough Trade, 1982; RT 103); and Out Of Line (LP, Zoar Records, 1983; ZOAR 13). Frederick Douglass (LP, Coyote Records / Twin/Tone, 1985; TTC 8555) introduced the collaboration with Elliott Sharp, who produced and performed. Two further LPs appeared on Lost Records (9) — Messenger Dogs Of The Gods (1986; TTL8675) and the promo EP Lemmings (1986; TTL8694p) — before the band signed to SST Records for Bugged (1988; SST 191 / SST CD 191) and Work (1989; SST 240). Both SST albums were produced by Elliott Sharp and engineered by Martin Bisi at BC Studio (Bugged) and Steve McAllister (Work). An 8-song demo, Lay Some More Eggs, circulated in 1985.

The SST albums show Mofungo expanding into jazz-rock territory — Work is tagged Jazz/Rock and Avantgarde in Discogs, with covers of Sonny Boy Williamson and Blind Alfred Reed sitting alongside originals, and a D. Boon tribute (“Themselves”). Robert Sietsema handled bass, vocals, album design, and photography; Chris Nelson (5) played drums and vocals; Willie Klein guitar and vocals; Elliott Sharp guitar and saxophone.

Discogs lists the band’s existence as 1979–1993.

Release Credits Detail

Elementary Particles (1979)

Lineup: Jeff McGovern, Jim Posner, Robert Sietsema, Willie Klein (all Performer). Produced by Chris Stamey; mixed by Jim Jordan.

Tape #1 (1980) — Three-band split

Mofungo tracks (6–10, 22–28): Robert Sietsema (guitar/bass/drums), Willie Klein (guitar), Jim Posner (guitar/bass/drums), Jeff McGovern (bass/vocals/maracas/drums), Seth Gunning (organ). Co-artists: Information (members Philip Dray, Rick Brown, Gary Larson, Chris Nelson (5)); Blinding Headache (Jim Posner, Willie Klein, Rick Brown).

End Of The World (1981) — Cassette

Robert Sietsema (bass/guitar), Jeff McGovern (drums/vocals), Willie Klein (guitar/vocals), Seth Gunning (organ/synthesizer), A.C. Chubb (saxophone). Produced and recorded by Carol Parkinson; Ann DeMarinis recorded track B6.

El Salvador (1982) — 7”

Robert Sietsema (bass/guitar), Jeff McGovern (drums/vocals), Willie Klein (guitar/vocals), A.C. Chubb (alto saxophone, B2), Dave Sewelson (baritone saxophone, B2), Seth Gunning (organ/synthesizer). Recorded by Carol Parkinson; mixed by Chris Stamey (A, B1) and Jeff McGovern (B2).

Out Of Line (1983)

Core band: Robert Sietsema, Willie Klein, Jeff McGovern, A.C. Chubb. Guests: Chris Nelson (5), Elliott Sharp, Phillip Johnston, Dave Sewelson. Self-produced. Engineer: Cherina Mastrantones.

Frederick Douglass (1985)

Chris Nelson (5) (vocals/drums/guitar/trombone), Willie Klein (vocals/guitar), Robert Sietsema (bass/vocals), Philip Dray (drums/guitar/organ/vocals), Elliott Sharp (sax/organ). Produced by Elliott Sharp; engineered by Martin Bisi (A side) and Steve McAllister (B side).

Messenger Dogs Of The Gods (1986)

Chris Nelson (5) (vocals/drums/guitar/keyboards), Willie Klein (vocals/guitar), Robert Sietsema (vocals/bass/guitar/keyboards), Elliott Sharp (guitar/saxophone/keyboards/Producer), Heather Drake (vocals). Engineered by Martin Bisi at BC Studio.

End Of The World, Part 2 (1987)

Chris Nelson (5), Willie Klein, Robert Sietsema, Elliott Sharp (Performer + Producer). Engineered and recorded by Martin Bisi.

Bugged (1988) — SST CD 191

Chris Nelson (5), Willie Klein, Robert Sietsema, Elliott Sharp (Performer + Producer). Engineer: Martin Bisi. Art Direction: Robert Sietsema. Back cover art: E. A. Seguy. Includes covers of traditional material and originals; “Backward Christian Soldiers” co-written by Barbara Barg, Joe Chassler, and Mike Sappol.

Work (1989) — SST 240

Robert Sietsema (bass/vocals/design/photography), Chris Nelson (5) (drums/vocals), Willie Klein (guitar/vocals), Elliott Sharp (guitar/saxophone/producer/design). Engineered by Steve McAllister. Artwork: Hugo Gellert. Covers: Sonny Boy Williamson (“Fattenin’ Frogs For Snakes”), Blind Alfred Reed (“Money Craving Folks”), D. Boon (“Themselves”).
