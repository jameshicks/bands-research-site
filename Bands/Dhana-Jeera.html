Discogs ID: 14532253

Active: 2024–
Genres: Folk, Psychedelic Rock; described on Discogs as merging psychedelic and North African influences

Overview

Dhana Jeera is a large-ensemble psychedelic folk collective that released a self-titled debut LP in 2024 on Cloud Recordings, the Athens, Georgia–based label co-founded by John Fernandes and Will Cullen Hart. The group draws its core membership from two overlapping constellations: the Albuquerque duo A Hawk And A Hacksaw (Jeremy Barnes and Heather Trost) and the Chicago avant-ensemble Bablicon (Barnes and David McDonnell), with additional members from the wider New Mexico and Athens underground.

The name and sonics reflect an interest in North African and psychedelic traditions. The self-titled debut (11 tracks, released as FLAC) was engineered collectively by Barnes, John Fernandes, Steven Lance Ledbetter, and Roan O’Reilly, mixed by Barnes and Ledbetter, and mastered by Derek Almstead — a long-running Elephant 6 affiliate whose credits span of Montreal, Circulatory System, and others. Artwork was handled by Jeremy Kiran Fernandes, who also contributed the bulk of the compositions (Music By, Lyrics By) and played guitar, keyboards, clarinet, and hand percussion. The album’s tracklist: “Blushing Sky,” “Snake Of Light,” “Neptune Rising,” “Moon Baby,” “Black Mountain Lion,” “Ghost Dance,” “Mother Taurus,” “Portail Vert” (lyrics by Hamlin Jackson), “Sequoia,” “Joy And Sorrow,” “Sea To Dust.”

Cloud Recordings is a significant context for this release: the label’s catalog runs directly from Elephant 6’s inner circle (Circulatory System, The Olivia Tremor Control, A Hawk And A Hacksaw, Dark Meat, Icy Demons) through to 2025 releases by Organically Programmed and Jardin Botanique + John Fernandes, making Dhana Jeera a natural fit for its roster.

Connections

  • A Hawk And A Hacksaw — Barnes and Trost are the duo’s core; A Hawk And A Hacksaw began on Cloud Recordings (cld 002, 2002) before moving to Leaf and LM Dupli-Cation
  • Bablicon — Barnes and McDonnell co-founded this Chicago avant-trio (1996–2002) alongside Griffin Rodriguez; psychedelic/free-jazz roots feed directly into Dhana Jeera’s sonics
  • The Olivia Tremor Control — John Fernandes was a member; OTC’s Black Foliage was reissued on Cloud Recordings (CLD004)
  • Circulatory System — Cloud Recordings’ flagship artist; John Fernandes also contributed to Signal Morning and Mosaics Within Mosaics
  • Icy Demons — David McDonnell / Griffin Rodriguez project that also appears on Cloud Recordings (CLD035, Smiles, 2023)
  • Immaterial Possession — Jeremy Kiran Fernandes’ other Discogs-documented project; also on Cloud Recordings (CLD030LP, 2020)
  • Cloud Recordings — the label home connecting nearly every member’s wider discography; co-founded by John Fernandes and Will Cullen Hart in 2001
  • Derek Almstead — mastering engineer, Elephant 6 affiliate; a recurring figure across Cloud Recordings’ catalog since the early 2000s
