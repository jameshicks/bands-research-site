King (Coventry)

Formed: Coventry, 1982 Split: 1986 Discogs artist ID: 89292 Discogs name: “King” (not disambiguated)

Overview

British new wave / pop group formed in Coventry in 1982, fronted by Paul King. Signed to CBS; had a major UK hit with “Love & Pride” (1984/85). Split 1986; Paul King continued as a solo artist on CBS then Siren Records into 1990. Co-managed throughout their career by Perry Haines and Dolphin Lovers Ltd.

Management

Co-managed by Perry Haines and Dolphin Lovers Ltd. — both credited on Steps In Time (1984) and Bitter Sweet (1985) LPs and subsequent releases.

Rod Pearce (Fetish Records founder) is documented in the vault as co-manager; Discogs credits list Perry Haines and “Dolphin Lovers Ltd.” rather than Rod Pearce by name — Dolphin Lovers Ltd. may be the company through which Pearce operated.

Production

Steps In Time and Bitter Sweet produced by Richard James Burgess (of Landscape / Shriekback production fame). Engineers on the debut included Flood, David Bascombe, Andy Jackson, and Phill Brown.

Formation and Pre-History

King evolved from the Reluctant Stereotypes, a Coventry rock-ska band that Paul King had joined after graduating from Coventry Drama School and a stint with the West Midlands Police Cadets. The Stereotypes appeared on The Old Grey Whistle Test, where they caught the attention of Perry Haines, who approached Paul King with both modelling work and an offer to manage his next project. [coventrygigs.blogspot.com https://coventrygigs.blogspot.com/2014/11/king-rise-and-fall-of-this-coventry-band.html]

After the Stereotypes disbanded, Paul King, Tony Wall (bass), and Colin Heanes (drums) formed Raw Screens in 1982. The Raw Screens evolved into King by summer 1982 after losing trumpet player Lynn Thompson. The “Multi Tone” concept — a play on Coventry’s Two Tone scene — was jointly developed by Paul King and Perry Haines. [King (new wave band) Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_(new_wave_band), as of 2026-06-29; coventrygigs.blogspot.com]

Conflict: Wikipedia states King formed in 1983; the Coventry-specific blog sources and the Paul King Wikipedia article indicate the Raw Screens precursor formed in 1982 and relaunched as King in 1983. The vault entry “formed 1982” above is consistent with the precursor band; the official King group dates from 1983.

Paul King biographical note: born in Galway, Ireland; moved to Coventry with family at a young age. [Wikipedia: Paul King (VJ) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_King_(VJ), as of 2026-06-29]

Full Lineup

Beyond the four primary Discogs-listed members, drums rotated through: Colin Heanes (original, replaced February 1983), John Hewitt (1983–1985), and Adrian Lillywhite (touring/recording only, not in promotional materials). [coventrygigs.blogspot.com]

“Love & Pride” also reached #55 on the US Billboard Hot 100 (September 1985). “Alone Without You” peaked at #14 in New Zealand. [Wikipedia: King (new wave band); Wikipedia: Alone Without You (King song) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alone_Without_You_(King_song), as of 2026-06-29]

Both albums went Gold: Steps in Time peaked at UK #6; Bitter Sweet at UK #16. Total: two Gold albums, five top 30 singles, two top 10 singles within approximately one year. [Wikipedia: King (new wave band)]

“Love & Pride“‘s breakthrough was driven by a live performance on BBC’s Saturday Superstore, after which the re-release was issued and climbed to #2 (held for three weeks in February 1985). [Wikipedia]

Visual Identity / Fashion

Perry Haines’s fashion input included introducing King to Doc Martens and persuading the brand to produce multi-coloured boots specifically for the group — resulting in a Dr. Martens sponsorship. Paul King’s visual signature (cockatoo haircut, spray-painted Doc Martens) became the band’s public image. [coventrygigs.blogspot.com]

Split and Aftermath

The band split in 1986 after a tour of Japan. The record label preferred a Paul King solo album over a third band album, effectively ending the group. [Wikipedia: King (new wave band)]

Paul King went on to release the solo LP Joy (1987, CBS), produced by American producer Dan Hartman. He subsequently became a VJ for MTV and later returned to Ireland. Perry Haines continued as his manager through the Siren Records solo period (through at least 1990, per Discogs credits). [Wikipedia: Paul King (VJ)]

Dolphin Lovers Ltd.

No source identifies who owns or operates Dolphin Lovers Ltd. Rod Pearce and Perry Haines are the documented co-managers; Dolphin Lovers Ltd. appears as the company entity on Discogs credits. [see Perry Haines.md]
