Art Objects

Post-punk band. Bristol, England. Active 1978–1981. Discogs artist ID: unknown (see Discogs release 2354898 for “Bagpipe Music”).

Direct precursor to The Blue Aeroplanes: three core members — Gerard Langley, John Langley, and Wojtek Dmochowski — regrouped as the Blue Aeroplanes in 1981.

Gerard and John Langley are brothers. Wojtek Dmochowski was a dancer (non-musician) who remained with the project into the Blue Aeroplanes era.

The initial 1978 lineup was a trio: Gerard Langley, Wojtek Dmochowski, and Jonjo Key. The full six-piece formed through 1979, with John Langley (drums) and Bill Stair (bass) joining by mid-1979 and Robin Key (guitar) completing the lineup shortly after. Wikipedia, as of 2026-06-25

History

Formed 1978 in Bristol. Debuted at Bristol’s Ashton Court Festival, summer 1978.

Released two singles (both 1980) and one album (1981) before disbanding. After the split, Gerard Langley, John Langley, and Wojtek Dmochowski played a handful more gigs under the Art Objects name before re-emerging as The Blue Aeroplanes in 1981 — first performing under that name at the King Street Art Gallery, Bristol. Scene Connections

  • Bristol post-punk scene: Shared the Heartbeat Records label with Glaxo Babies and The Transmitters, central acts in the Bristol post-punk cluster of 1979–1981.
  • Fried Egg Records: Both singles appeared on Fried Egg Records, another Bristol indie label documenting the local post-punk/new wave scene (1979–1980); their “Hard Objects” EP charted on independent charts during the NME strike period.
  • The Blue Aeroplanes: Direct continuation; the same three principals (Gerard Langley, John Langley, Wojtek Dmochowski) formed the core of the Blue Aeroplanes from 1981. Nick Jacobs (ex-Exploding Seagulls, Southampton) joined them there.
  • Cherry Red Records: The 2007 Bagpipe Music reissue connected the band’s archival profile to this well-documented UK independent catalogue.
