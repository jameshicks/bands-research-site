Nimal

Swiss international avant-folk / Rock In Opposition collective, active 1987–1993. Discogs artist ID: 366096. Also known as Nimal Group.

Overview

Nimal was a Swiss-centred international ensemble occupying the fringe of the Rock In Opposition movement and closely related to Debile Menthol. The band’s music blends folk elements (hurdy gurdy, bouzouki, accordion) with avant-garde composition and a rhythmic energy that tilts toward folk-rock, art-rock, and occasional humour. The group was led by Swiss multi-instrumentalist Jean-Maurice Rossel (“Momo”) and drew in players from Slovenia, France, Switzerland, the US, and the UK.

American cellist Tom Cora was a core member, contributing cello, vocals, bass, and co-production on the debut LP; his presence places Nimal squarely within the transatlantic experimental network linking Zürich’s RecRec scene to the NYC downtown avant-garde. Slovenian accordionist Bratko Bibič became increasingly central by the second album and the Dis Tanz CD. US drummer Pippin Barnett — also a Curlew member — provided the rhythmic backbone across all three studio records.

The band released two studio LPs on RecRec Music (Zürich) and a third CD on the French label Ayaa, bookended by a promo cassette split with Ne Zhdali also on Ayaa.

Tracklist: A1–A4 La Semaine Des Quatre Jeudis (suite); A5 Dimanche; A6 Animal Triste; B1 La Marelle; B2 Au Zoo; B3 Un Drame; B4 Le Tram; B5 Maligne.

Voix De Surface (1990, RecRec 31)

Second LP on RecRec. Composition credit goes collectively to Nimal (group). Mixed in two sessions: A1–B2 mixed by Bratko Bibič and François Dietz; B3–B5 mixed by Cédric Vuille and Jean-Vin Huguenin. Recorded by François Dietz (A1–B2) and Flavio Vigne (B3–B5). Cover by Isabelle Ayuso; design by Diego Zweifel. Genres: Folk, Rock / styles: Art Rock, Prog Rock.

Tracklist: A1 Une Lucie; A2 In Tenda; A3 Sale Temps, Mais Les Couleurs Sont Belles; A4 Tarotska; B1 Le Soleil; B2 La Marelle; B3 Le 4ème Jeudi De La Semaine; B4 James River; B5 Maligne.

Song credits: Rossel wrote A1, B1, B2, B3, B5; Tom Cora wrote A2, B4; Jean-Vin Huguenin wrote A3; Bratko Bibič wrote A4.

Dis Tanz (1992, Ayaa cdt 1292)

CD on French label Ayaa. Tom Cora is absent. Rossel, Bibič, and Barnett are the core trio, with additional players per track. Mixed by Bibič, Rossel, and Nino De Gleria; recorded by Rossel. Cover by Isabelle Ayuso. Genres: Folk, Jazz, Rock / styles: Avantgarde, Contemporary Jazz, Folk.

14-track album; most compositions by Rossel or Bibič.

Notable per-track personnel:

  • André Schenk (bass), Daniel Spahni (drums) on “Juillet”
  • Nino De Gleria (bass) on “Grand Carré”, “Au Nord”, “Od Tukaj Do Zdaj”, “Assauts”, “Ba(a)L”
  • Cédric Vuille and Pierre Kaufmann (clarinets) on “Grand Carré”
  • Shirley Anne Hofmann (euphonium) on “Ein Warmer Schnee-Kuss”
  • Gilles Rieder (drums) on “Assez D’assauts!”

Demo Tape Ayaa January 93 (1993, Ayaa)

Promo/sampler cassette split with French ensemble Ne Zhdali. Side A: Ne Zhdali tracks from their Rhinoceros And Other Forms Of Life. Side B: Nimal tracks drawn from the Dis Tanz CD (Opa!, Juillet, Fragile Part I & II, Pain Sec, Last Call For Summer 90, Grand Carré, Ein Warmer Schnee-Kuss, Au Nord).
