The Orb

British ambient house / electronic act, formed London c.1988. Discogs artist ID: 54286 (on Cooking Vinyl 2004–2025, 108 releases — the label’s second most-prolific artist by release count).

Pioneered ambient house / electronic music in the late 1980s and early 1990s. Core member: Alex Paterson (Duncan Alexander Robert Paterson). Other members have included Thomas Fehlmann, Jimmy Cauty, Kris Weston, Andy Hughes (2), Fil Le Gonidec, Tim Bran, and others. Joined Cooking Vinyl in 2004 and have remained one of the label’s most active acts. The sublabel Orbscure (Discogs ID: 2115592) was created by Cooking Vinyl specifically for The Orb’s releases.

Thomas Fehlmann joined as a part-time member after befriending Alex Paterson through his Teutonic Beats label. He is co-producer, mixer, and writer on most major Orb albums from Orbus Terrarum (1995) onward.
