Charge

UK punk band. Discogs artist ID: 221833. Name variation: Charge [Except Stu. P. Didiot].

Overview

Released Kings Cross / Brave New World / Gods Kids (1981) on Test Pressings (cat. tp3), self-produced alongside engineer Peter J. Reynolds. Written by Stu Lunn. Members included Stu P. Didiot, Martyn Watts, Iain MacLaughlan, Dave Griffiths (2), and Moose (16). The band maintained a website at stu-p-didiot.com.
