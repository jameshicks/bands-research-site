Prefuse 73

Electronic producer / beatmaker project of Scott Herren (Guillermo Scott Herren). Based Atlanta / New York. Discogs ID: 3722. Active 2000–present. Core label: Warp Records (2001–2011), then Lex Records (2018–2024) and Temporary Residence Limited (2015).

Prefuse 73 pioneered “glitch-hop”: hip-hop sample vocabulary processed through extreme digital fragmentation, rapid edits, and textural abstraction. The Warp albums feature a wide cast of guest MCs and singers alongside purely instrumental tracks.

Bio

Early period / Schematic era (1997–2000). Herren’s earliest releases came under the Delarosa & Asora alias on Schematic (Miami), an imprint associated with the laptop/glitch scene. Backsum EP (1999) and Agony Part 1 (2000) document this work. Savath & Savalas — a softer, acoustic-influenced project co-created with Eva Puyuelo Muns — debuted simultaneously on Chicago’s Hefty Records with Folk Songs For Trains, Trees And Honey (2000).

Warp signing and breakthrough (2000–2003). The Prefuse 73 alias launched with the Estrocaro EP (2000, co-released Chocolate Industries / Warp). Vocal Studies + Uprock Narratives (2001, WARPCD83) established the template: chopped-up breakbeats with MC cameos from Aesop Rock, MF Doom, Mikah 9, Sam Prekop, and Dose One, held together by instrumental passages of disintegrating hip-hop. One Word Extinguisher (2003, WARPCD105) pushed further into the fragmented aesthetic, with track-producer credits going to Dabrye, Daedelus, and Tommy Guerrero.

Guest-saturated mid-period (2004–2007). Savath & Savalas moved to Warp for Apropa’t (2004, WARPCD115), a Catalan-language folk-electronics record credited to Herren and Eva Puyuelo Muns with backing from Tortoise members John McEntire and John Herndon. Back as Prefuse 73, Surrounded By Silence (2005, WARPCD129) is the densest collaborator release: El-P, Ghostface Killah, Aesop Rock, GZA, Masta Killa, The Books, Trish Keenan (Broadcast), Café Tacuba, and the Deheza sisters all appear. Security Screenings (2006, WARPCD140) is sparer, with Kieran Hebden (Four Tet) and Babatunde Adebimpe among the few guests. Preparations (2007, WARPLP158) introduced a “players” credit — School Of Seven Bells, John Stanier, Roberto Carlos Lange, and Tobias Lilja — moving toward live-instrumentation integration.

Later Warp years (2009–2011). Everything She Touched Turned Ampexian (2009, WARPLP179) is a dense 29-track LP with Dimlite, Zach Hill, Claudia Deheza, and The Gaslamp Killer. The Only She Chapters (2011, WARPLP208) is an all-female-vocalist project: Zola Jesus, Trish Keenan, Angel Deradoorian, Shara Worden (My Brightest Diamond), Alejandra Deheza, and Niki Randa, with Benjamin Curtis on guitar and Roberto Carlos Lange co-producing C4.

Post-Warp (2015–present). Three releases on Temporary Residence Limited in 2015 (Rivington Não Rio, Forsyth Gardens, Every Color Of Darkness). Moved to Lex Records with Sacrifices (2018) and Fudge Beats (2019). Self-released The Failing Institute Of The Contras and The Failing Institute Of The Sampled Source in 2020. New Strategies For Modern Crime Vol. 1 (Lex Records, 2024) is the most recent.

Pattern Notes

  • Remix-heavy: A large proportion of comp appearances are remixes he contributed (Clouddead, Telefon Tel Aviv, Beans, Towa Tei, Roy Ayers-era material).
  • Label hubs: Chocolate Industries and Ninja Tune account for many appearances; Warp samplers throughout the 2001–2005 period; Impulse! (jazz) shows his crossover reach.
  • Geographic spread: Japanese-market editions (P-Vine, Beat Records, Hyde Out) frequently licensed his tracks for separate pressings; France (Trax, Wagram) and Germany (Juice, Kyo) also prominent.
  • Festival comps: Sónar (2003, 2004), All Tomorrow’s Parties (2001), La Route Du Rock (2003), Domino Festival (2003, 2005) all included him.
