Lack Of Knowledge

UK anarcho-punk/post-punk band from south-east England, active from the early 1980s. Discogs artist ID: 261284. Also known as LOK, L.O.K.

Overview

Lack of Knowledge were part of the Crass Records / anarcho-punk orbit in the early 1980s. Their LP Grey (1984, Crass Records) was produced by Penny Rimbaud of Crass at Southern Studios. The band reunited to record White Science (2004, Alternative Records (3)). Notably, both Tony Barber and Philip Barker went from LOK directly into the reunited Buzzcocks lineup in 1992, forming the rhythm section.
