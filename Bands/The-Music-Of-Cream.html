The Music Of Cream

Discogs ID: 6791910
Active: c. 2016–present (50th anniversary tour documented 2018)
Genre: Blues Rock, Psychedelic Rock
Country: UK / US

Cream tribute trio whose concept rests entirely on lineage: two of the three members are sons of original Cream (2) members, and the third is the son of one of Cream’s most closely associated engineers. The band performs the full Cream catalog in a live setting.

Overview

The Music Of Cream was formed as a tribute to Cream (2) (1966–68) — the power trio of Eric Clapton (guitar), Jack Bruce (bass), and Ginger Baker (drums), widely regarded as one of the foundational bands of British blues-rock and psychedelic rock. Two of Cream’s members’ sons and the son of a key engineer carry the catalog forward:

  • Kofi Baker (drums) — son of Ginger Baker
  • Malcolm Bruce (bass/keyboards/vocals) — son of Jack Bruce
  • Will Johns (2) (guitar/vocals) — son of engineer/producer Andy Johns, and nephew by marriage of Eric Clapton

The family triangle is almost exact: Kofi stands in for Ginger; Malcolm for Jack; and Will — though not a blood relative of Clapton — carries a direct personal connection via his father’s decades of work and his aunt/uncle relationship with Eric Clapton. Kofi Baker and Malcolm Bruce had collaborated together since at least 1989 (in The Now (11) and later Lost City), making them the established nucleus to which Will Johns was added.

Member Backgrounds

Kofi Baker has the most extensive Discogs-documented career of the three. His lineage work began as early as 1989 (The Now (11) with Malcolm Bruce) and continued through the early Cream Experience and into The Music Of Cream. Outside tribute work, he is known for the 1995 fusion record Abstract Logic (Day Eight Music) with Jonas Hellborg and Shawn Lane, and for his years with the LA fusion group OHM: alongside ex-Megadeth guitarist Chris Poland. His father Ginger Baker died 6 October 2019.

Malcolm Bruce plays guitar, keyboards, and sings. His Discogs credits span collaborations with Jack Bruce (including the 1994 Cities Of The Heart transcription credit and the 2014 Silver Rails sessions where he contributed guitar and pre-production), Corky Laing (of Mountain), and an appearance on The X-files (A 20 Year Retrospective) (1998). He and Kofi Baker are long-time collaborators, having played together in The Now (11) (1989), Lost City (1993), and now The Music Of Cream. His father Jack Bruce died 25 October 2014.

Will Johns (2) is the son of Andy Johns — the highly regarded British engineer/producer who worked with Led Zeppelin (IV, Houses of the Holy), The Rolling Stones (Exile on Main St.), and Van Halen, among many others. Andy Johns died 7 April 2013. Will is also the nephew by marriage of Eric Clapton, making his role in a Cream tribute act more than coincidental. His Discogs-documented solo production credit is Hooks & Lines (2012, Big Bear Records, UK) where he is credited as producer; he also has a Will Johns Band project (Discogs ID: 4372395).

The Original Band: Cream (2)

Cream (2) (Discogs ID: 229621) was active 1966–1968. Members:

  • Eric Clapton — guitar
  • Jack Bruce — bass, vocals
  • Ginger Baker — drums

Key albums: Fresh Cream (1966), Disraeli Gears (1967), Wheels Of Fire (1968, studio + live double), Goodbye (1969, posthumous). The band reunited for four nights at the Royal Albert Hall in May 2005 — their first performances together in 37 years.

Cream are considered foundational to blues-rock, heavy rock, and psychedelic rock. Their influence on the power trio format (no rhythm guitarist) is extensive.
