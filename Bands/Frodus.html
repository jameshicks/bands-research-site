Frodus

Washington DC post-hardcore / math-rock band. Discogs artist ID: 397463. Also recorded as “Frodus Sound Laboratories,” “The Frodus Triad,” “Frodus Conglomerate International,” and several reunion-era aliases (FDIC, Frodus Escape Plan). Active 1993–1999, reunion 2009–2010.

Overview

Frodus was formed in 1993 by vocalist/guitarist Shelby Cinca and drummer Jason Hamacher. The band cycled through numerous bassists over their six-year run. Their name comes from the last episode of The Monkees TV series (“Mijacogeo,” a.k.a. “The Frodis Caper”). Known for a dense mixture of math rock and hardcore punk with esoteric, dissonant lyrical themes; described by critics as one of the most influential post-hardcore bands of the 1990s (Wikipedia).

Their final album, And We Washed Our Weapons In The Sea (2001, Fueled By Ramen), is considered by the band their best-recorded work. It was recorded in 1999 but took two years to release after an earlier label (MIA Records) shut down.

Cinca and Hamacher went on to form The Black Sea / Decahedron with Joe Lally of Fugazi in 2002–2003.

For the 2009 reunion, the band brought in Liam Wilson (The Dillinger Escape Plan) on bass and Jake Brown for select dates. The reunion was organized around a reissue of Conglomerate International on Gilead Media and framed as a response to the bank bailouts. Soundlab 1 (2010, Lovitt Records) documented the reunion recording session.
