Electronic

Synth-pop / electronic / dance. Manchester. Formed 1989. Discogs ID: 4868.

Supergroup formed by Bernard Sumner (Joy Division, New Order) and Johnny Marr (The Smiths). First signed to Factory Records; later moved to Parlophone. Active 1989–1999; on indefinite hiatus thereafter.

Notable collaborators: Neil Tennant (Pet Shop Boys), Karl Bartos (ex-Kraftwerk).
