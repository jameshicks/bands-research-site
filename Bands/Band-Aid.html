Band Aid

Charity supergroup assembled 1984 by Bob Geldof and Midge Ure to record “Do They Know It’s Christmas?” (November 1984, Mercury). The single raised funds for the 1984–85 Ethiopian famine. Ure co-wrote the song with Geldof and produced the session. Discogs artist ID: 136704
