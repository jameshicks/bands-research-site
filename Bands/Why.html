Why?

Experimental hip-hop / indie rock band, Cincinnati OH, 2003–present. Discogs artist ID: 55554.

Biography

Origins: Yoni Wolf Solo (2003)

Why? began as a solo recording alias for Yoni Wolf (real name Jonathan Avram Wolf), son of a Cincinnati rabbi and art-book editor. Wolf had been active in the Anticon orbit since the late 1990s — he co-formed the live improv group Apogee with Dose One and his brother Josiah Wolf in 1997, and appeared on early Anticon releases under the name “Greenthink” alongside Dose One before settling on the Why? moniker.

The first Why? full-length, Oaklandazulasylum (2003), was released on Anticon under the alias Why? (9) (Discogs ID: 5113839) to distinguish it as a solo effort. It presented Wolf’s bedroom-produced blend of sample collage, confessional rapping, and melodic song fragments, with only occasional contributions from Josiah Wolf and Odd Nosdam. The same year, the 12” EP The Early Whitney appeared under the main Why? credit and already featured a live-band lineup: Yoni, Josiah, Nosdam, and Doug McDiarmid (performing under his alias “Early Dissmissal”).

Band Formation and the Anticon Peak (2005–2009)

In 2005 Why? officially expanded into a full band. Yoni recruited long-time friends Doug McDiarmid (guitar/bass/keys), Matt Meldon (guitar, ukulele), and his older brother Josiah Wolf (drums). Together they recorded Elephant Eyelash (2005, Anticon ABR0055), produced under the collective alias “The Wolf Bros.” (Josiah and Yoni) and credited live performance to “Miss Ohio’s Nameless Girls And Ghost Serenaders.” Elephant Eyelash marked the transition from hip-hop collage toward hook-driven indie rock while keeping Wolf’s oblique, confessional lyrics. Guest contributions included Dose One (Adam Drucker) on backing vocals and Jel (Jeffrey Logan) on MPC drums.

Alopecia (2008, Anticon ABR0080 / Tomlab TOM112) is widely considered the band’s breakthrough. Produced by The Wolf Bros. and mixed by Eli Crews, it expanded the core lineup to include Andrew Broder (of Fog) and Mark Erickson (also a Fog member), alongside a large cast of additional performers including Odd Nosdam, Dose One, Jeremy Ylvisaker, Nedelle Torrisi, and Liz Hodson. The album was co-released in the UK/Europe on Tomlab and received significant crossover attention for its balance of rap cadence and baroque-pop melody.

Eskimo Snow (2009, Anticon ABR0098) followed immediately — recorded in the same sessions as Alopecia but shelved as a separate, more introspective LP. Recorded by Tom Herbers and initially mixed by Mark Nevers before Eli Crews completed the mix. Songwriter credits on several tracks were shared across Yoni, Josiah, Doug McDiarmid, and Matt Meldon.

Transition Period (2012–2013)

Mumps, Etc. (2012, Anticon / City Slang Slang50028P) was the band’s last record for Anticon and marked a rawer, pared-down direction. The Discogs entry credits only Jonathan Wolf (2) as performer/recorder, suggesting a largely solo studio effort despite live touring with the full band. The accompanying Sod In The Seed EP appeared the same year. Why? then moved to Joyful Noise Recordings for the Bangs and Golden Tickets EPs in 2013.

Joyful Noise Era (2017–present)

After a quieter mid-decade period (Yoni Wolf released solo and production work under his own name), Why? returned with Moh Lhean (2017, Joyful Noise JNR222). The album brought a warmer, more orchestrated sound with flute, cello, double bass, and synthesizers alongside the core band of Yoni Wolf, Josiah Wolf, Doug McDiarmid, Matt Meldon, and Ben Sloan (drums). Guests included Aaron Weiss, Ryan Lott (Son Lux), and Liz Wolf. The record was produced, recorded, and mixed by The Wolf Bros. in-house.

Aokohio (2019, Joyful Noise) was released digitally — a dense, fragmentary 19-track album. The We Are Real EP followed the same year. A live album, Live At Third Man Records (2018), documented the touring band on Third Man Records.

The Well I Fell Into (2024, Waterlines WL002) marked the debut of Why?’s own imprint. The album restored Andrew Broder to the core lineup alongside Yoni, Josiah, and Doug McDiarmid, and featured an unusually broad guest roster: Ada Lea, Gia Margaret, Lillie West, Macie Stewart, Montaigne, Serengeti, Adam Schatz, and Emily Hope Price. Production was shared between The Wolf Bros., Brian Joseph, Broder, McDiarmid, and Josh Berg. A companion Demos LP was released on Joyful Noise in 2025.
