Ludus

Discogs ID: 121146. Post-punk band from Manchester. Active 1978–1984. Centred around vocalist/artist Linder Sterling (Linda Mulvey). Released six records on New Hormones and one on Interior Music (Belgium, 1987). Morrissey is a documented fan.

Graham Revell / SPK Connection

Graham Revell (Discogs ID 738191) played soprano saxophone on Ludus recordings 1982–84. He is also the founder and leader of SPK, the Australian/UK industrial group. The two projects sit at opposite ends of the post-punk spectrum aesthetically, but the link is explicable through geography and a shared social network.

SPK’s London base. SPK relocated from Sydney to London in 1980 and remained UK-based through at least 1984. They recorded their debut Information Overload Unit in a Vauxhall squat; by 1982 their expanded London lineup included Brian Williams (Lustmord), John Murphy (later Death in June), and Derek Thompson. Revell was therefore living in London and embedded in the UK experimental/industrial milieu during the exact period he appears on Ludus records. Wikipedia – SPK (band), as of 2026-07-06.

The Dave Formula conduit. The most likely link between Revell and Linder is Dave Formula (ex-Magazine keyboardist), who joined Ludus in 1982 as producer and arranger following a recommendation reportedly connected to the Manchester post-punk network. The Les Disques du Crépuscule biography states: “In order to achieve a richer, more sophisticated sound, Linder, Ian and Dave Formula were joined by bassist Paul Cavanagh, drummer Roy O’Shea and sax players Lee Buick and Graham Revell.” Formula’s role as assembler of the expanded lineup suggests he may have sourced or introduced Revell, though no published source confirms this directly. Les Disques du Crépuscule – Ludus biography; LTM Recordings – Ludus biography.

Scope of Revell’s involvement. Wikipedia’s Graeme Revell article notes he “has also done string arrangements for Ludus,” implying the relationship extended beyond saxophone performance to compositional/arrangement work, though no specific release is cited there. Wikipedia – Graeme Revell, as of 2026-07-06.

What is not documented. No published interview from Revell or Linder Sterling explains the introduction or how the collaboration was initiated. The Quietus interview with Linder (2017) was inaccessible during this research pass (403). No Revell interview (Side-Line 2025, Outsideleft, Pantograph Punch) mentions Ludus. The specific mechanism of introduction remains undocumented in any source consulted.
