Toupidek Limonade

Discogs ID: 141113
Also known as: TPDK, TPDK LMND, Toupidek, Toupidek Limonade et Jérôme Tagu
Origin: France (Reims / Rennes orbit)
Active: c. 1998–2012
Genre: Experimental / Avantgarde / Abstract / Art Rock
Label: In-Poly-Sons (primary), co-released with Gazul Records

Overview

Toupidek Limonade is a French experimental group centred on the trio of Denis Tagu, Jean Caël, and Kwettap Ieuw. The band grew out of the same tight Reims avant-garde cluster that produced Look De Bouk and Hellebore — Tagu and Ieuw are both Look De Bouk members, and Caël and Tagu both appear in Hellebore. Their records sit squarely in the RIO-adjacent French experimental idiom: song-based but abstract, with dense polyrhythm, unexpected instrumentation, and a consistent willingness to quote, parody, or rework canonical composers (Gainsbourg, Pergolesi, Alfred Jarry, folk sources).

The group released two albums on In-Poly-Sons in co-release with Gazul Records, both in 2000. A third and fourth release followed on In-Poly-Sons in 2001 and 2003 (as compilation and split material). Denis Tagu is the label manager of In-Poly-Sons, which makes Toupidek Limonade in effect an in-house act. The band’s Discogs entry uses the variant name “Toupidek Limonade et Jérôme Tagu” on at least one release, suggesting Tagu’s role was occasionally foregrounded in credits.

The 2000 album Il Y A Des Nuits Et Des Nuits has the alias TPDK LMND in the credits (for the group’s own arranged by, mixed by, and recorded by roles), and features a remix by Etienne Himalaya — the fourth Look De Bouk member — on track 13, tying the two bands together via a direct collaboration.

Release Details

Il Y A Des Bulles Dans La Banquise (1998)

In-Poly-Sons IPS 0198 / Gazul Records GA 8616.AR | CD | France
Discogs Release ID: 4023259
Genres: Electronic, Rock | Styles: Abstract, Art Rock, Avantgarde

The debut album. 17 tracks, heavily songwriter-divided: Jean Caël wrote the majority (10 tracks), Denis Tagu wrote 6, and one track (“Berlin Bin Bin”) is co-credited to Caël and Kwettap Ieuw. Etienne Himalaya appears here as a guest: playing trumpet on track 5 (“Trott’in”) and sharing a co-written credit with Tagu on track 14 (“Eléonore”). Himalaya also handled recording.

Il Y A Des Nuits Et Des Nuits (2000)

In-Poly-Sons IPS 0300 / Gazul Records GA 8633.AR | CD (Reissue) | France
Discogs Release ID: 4022928
Genres: Electronic, Rock | Styles: Abstract, Avantgarde, Experimental

The second album, catalogued as a “reissue” in Discogs (suggesting earlier material re-presented or repackaged). 14 tracks. The record pulls in several guest performers beyond the core trio: Daniel Koskowitz (Hellebore / NEO Museum) plays percussion on the four “Valse D’Ubu Cocu” movements and mixed the record alongside the group. Antoine Gindt (Hellebore) recorded the sessions and plays guitar on track 8. Etienne Himalaya contributes a remix of track 13 (“Laissons Les Nez Faire Titi”).

Songwriting sources are notably varied: Jean Caël again dominates originals, but the record also includes Serge Gainsbourg (“Laisse Béton Les Moss”), Alfred Jarry (the four “Valse D’Ubu Cocu” pieces, written with Denis Tagu), and Pergolesi (informing “Nounours ‘Sax De Couchage’”). One track credits the writer simply as “Folk.”

Scene Context

Toupidek Limonade sits at the intersection of two axes in the French experimental scene:

  1. The Reims cluster — the same core group of musicians that made Look De Bouk and Hellebore. Denis Tagu, Kwettap Ieuw, Jean Caël, Daniel Koskowitz, Antoine Gindt, Alain Casari, and Etienne Himalaya all appear in multiple combinations across these bands.

  2. The In-Poly-Sons label network — Denis Tagu’s position as label manager means Toupidek Limonade is effectively the label’s “house band.” The label’s orbit also includes Klimperei, Pierre Bastien, Guigou Chenevier, and The Work, all sharing the RIO-adjacent, uncommercial ethos that In-Poly-Sons describes explicitly in its label profile.

The two Toupidek albums co-released with Gazul Records position them within the Gazul / Musea distribution network for French experimental music — the same network that carried the Look De Bouk 2001 compilation.

Open Questions

  • No founding date or context documented for how Toupidek Limonade formed as distinct from the Look De Bouk nucleus — Discogs profile only says “From France.”
  • The variant name “Toupidek Limonade et Jérôme Tagu” implies Denis Tagu’s given name may be Jérôme (with “Denis” a stage/professional name) — not confirmed by any Discogs data.
  • No releases documented after 2000 in the primary discography. The stub noted “active c. 1998–2012” — the source of the 2012 end date is not in the Discogs data retrieved.
  • Whether Il Y A Des Nuits Et Des Nuits (Discogs: “Reissue”) is a reissue of the 1984 Ayaa release Il Ya Des Nuits (ID 1327262) or simply a Discogs misclassification needs clarification.
