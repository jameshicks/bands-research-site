The Messthetics

Washington DC instrumental power trio. Discogs artist ID: 6370465. Active 2018–present.

Members: Joe Lally (bass), Brendan Canty (drums), Anthony Pirog (guitar). From 2024 onward, the core trio has expanded into an ongoing collaboration with tenor saxophonist James Brandon Lewis.

Formation and Background

The Messthetics began in 2016 when Brendan Canty and Joe Lally — the rhythm section of Fugazi — reconvened with guitarist Anthony Pirog. Fugazi had been on indefinite hiatus since 2002–2003; the pair had remained in DC and active in various projects (Canty in Deathfix, Garland Of Hours, and extensive production work; Lally in Ataxia, solo records on Dischord Records, and Tolotta Records, and later Coriky).

Pirog, a DC-area jazz guitarist active in the local improv and experimental scenes, brought a sharply different vocabulary to the rhythm section: his background runs through free jazz, experimental electronics, and chamber-oriented work. The combination was deliberate — Canty and Lally wanted to take the power-trio form into different harmonic and rhythmic territory, with Pirog functioning as both lead voice and structural organizer rather than a post-hardcore guitarist.

The Discogs profile quotes their founding intent as seeking to “re-imagine the power trio, bringing into the 21st Century a form that may have reached near perfection with [Black Sabbath’s Black Sabbath] on the very first day of the 1970s.”

They released their debut LP in 2018 on Dischord Records, staying with the label for a second LP before moving to Impulse! for the James Brandon Lewis collaborations.

Members

Joe Lally — Bass

Joseph Francis Lally (born December 3, 1963, Silver Spring, Maryland). Founding bassist of Fugazi (1987–2003), active in the DC hardcore and post-hardcore scene since the mid-1980s. Full biography at Joe Lally.

Brendan Canty — Drums

Born March 9, 1966, Teaneck, New Jersey. Drummer for Rites Of Spring (1984–1986), then Fugazi (1987–2003). Also a film director (co-directed the Fugazi documentary Instrument with Jem Cohen, 1999; and the Wilco film Ashes Of American Flags, 2009) and producer (Ted Leo, The Thermals, Bob Mould). Full biography at Brendan Canty.

Anthony Pirog — Guitar

Guitarist, multi-instrumentalist, and composer. Discogs artist ID: 2726356. Based in Washington DC. Active on record since 2006. Top labels: Cuneiform Records, Sonic Mass Records, AGS Recordings.

Pirog’s primary duo project is Janel & Anthony — a duo with cellist Janel Leppin (who also arranged cello on the Messthetics debut). Their Where Is Home (2012, Cuneiform Records) is the flagship release of that project. He also records as the Anthony Pirog Trio (ID 3328015), Anthony Pirog Sextet (ID 3328013), and The Anthony Pirog Ensemble (ID 2919060), and appears in BeepHonk, Five Times Surprise, Skysaw, and Six Six, among others.

Guest appearance on Eye Of I (2023, Anti- — cat. 7950-2)

Eye Of I is a James Brandon Lewis Trio album, not a Messthetics album — but The Messthetics (Lally, Canty, Pirog) appear as a featured unit on the closing track “Fear Not” (track 11), alongside engineer/multi-instrumentalist Shahzad Ismaily (synthesizer / Moog), who also engineered that track. The Messthetics and the James Brandon Lewis Trio are both credited as arrangers of “Fear Not.”

This marks the first documented recording intersection of The Messthetics and James Brandon Lewis. The album includes a Donny Hathaway cover (“Someday We’ll All Be Free”) and a Cecil Taylor piece (“Womb Water”), with liner notes by Thurston Moore. Mixed and mastered by cellist Christopher Hoffman. Engineered by Martin Bisi (tracks 1–10).

The James Brandon Lewis Trio lineup on this record: Lewis (tenor saxophone), Christopher Hoffman (cello, loops/pedals), Max Jaffe (drums, sensory percussion). Cornet on tracks 2 and 10: Kirk Knuffke.

James Brandon Lewis — Collaborator Profile

US tenor saxophonist and composer. Discogs artist ID: 4002378. Born 1983 in Buffalo, NY; based in New York. Full page at James Brandon Lewis.

Studied at the Buffalo Academy for the Visual and Performing Arts, then Howard University, then CalArts (MFA 2010), where he studied with Charlie Haden and Wadada Leo Smith. Has worked with Dave Douglas, Joshua Redman, Aruán Ortiz, Chad Taylor, Brad Jones, Hank Roberts, Tony Malaby, and Marilyn Crispell.

Groups and ensembles: James Brandon Lewis Trio (ID 5841584), James Brandon Lewis Quartet (ID 8187118), Red Lily Quintet (ID 9379258), Heroes Are Gang Leaders (ID 6117274), Cliffs (3) (ID 14828512), Mendoza Hoff Revels (ID 13652470), Orientation Of We (ID 8181919), Tenor Triage (ID 17357539).
