Buke and Gase

Experimental pop duo from Hudson, New York. Discogs canonical name: Buke And Gass (ID: 1831696); also credited as Buke And Gase, Buke & Gass. Members: Arone Dyer (buke, vocals, lyrics, percussion, co-production) and Aron Sanchez (gass, drums/drum programming, engineering, mixing, mastering).

The name derives from the custom instruments the duo built: Dyer’s buke (a bass ukulele) and Sanchez’s gass (a guitar-bass hybrid). Both members were previously in Hominid (2) together.
