The Asphodells

UK electronic duo formed 2012. Members: Andrew Weatherall and Tim Fairplay. Discogs artist ID: 2733213. Released primarily on Rotters Golf Club (ID 4928). Active 2012–2014.

Overview

The Asphodells were a project formed by Andrew Weatherall (Discogs ID 2657) and Tim Fairplay (Discogs ID 659308), two figures who had already worked together on Weatherall’s solo album A Pox On The Pioneers (2009), where Fairplay played guitar and bass. The duo name references the asphodel, a flower associated in Greek mythology with the underworld and the dead — consistent with Weatherall’s longstanding interest in pastoral mysticism, dub, and kosmische aesthetics.

Their core output is a single studio album, Ruled By Passion, Destroyed By Lust (2012), and a companion remixes collection, Remixed (2013), both on Weatherall’s own Rotters Golf Club label. A further 12-inch single, Before Your Eyes / Velo (2014), appeared on the Telophase label as a collaboration with Friendly Fires. There is also a self-titled limited CDr (2013, Not On Label), a DJ/compilation mix document with no original material.

Sonically, The Asphodells occupied the same terrain as Weatherall’s broader late solo work: kosmische-influenced techno with substantial live instrumentation (guitar, baglama, melodica, live bass), filtered through his dub and house sensibility. The project ran parallel to Weatherall’s solo work under his own name and The Woodleigh Research Facility (with Nina Walsh).

Context: Asphodells Within Weatherall’s Career

The Asphodells emerged in the period between Weatherall’s last Two Lone Swordsmen activity and the late solo albums Convenanza and Consolamentum (both 2016). They sit chronologically after his second solo album A Pox On The Pioneers (2009, Rotters Golf Club) and before his pairing with Nina Walsh in The Woodleigh Research Facility (first release 2015).

For Tim Fairplay, The Asphodells were his first high-profile collaborative project, following his first solo EP (2011, Astro Lab Recordings). His post-Asphodells output continued to parallel Weatherall’s aesthetic interests — kosmische, dark electronic, live instrumentation — across his own solo discography and the Dungeon Module label (from 2021).

  • Tim Fairplay — co-founder; played guitar/bass on Weatherall’s A Pox On The Pioneers before forming the duo

  • Two Lone Swordsmen — Weatherall’s preceding duo (with Keith Tenniswood); ended 2007; direct predecessor in terms of label and aesthetic
  • The Woodleigh Research Facility — Weatherall’s subsequent project with Nina Walsh; succeeded the Asphodells on Rotters Golf Club
  • Battant — Tim Fairplay and Andy Baxter’s other band; Baxter played bass and baglama on the Asphodells album
  • Noel Summerville — mastering engineer across Rotters Golf Club releases; appears on Ruled By Passion and Remixed
  • Julian House — sleeve design on Before Your Eyes / Velo; Ghost Box founder; connected to Belbury Poly (who appear on the self-titled CDr)
  • Friendly Fires — joint primary credit on Before Your Eyes / Velo (2014, Telophase)
  • Telophase — label for Before Your Eyes / Velo (2014)
