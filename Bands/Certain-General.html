Certain General

Influential NYC post-punk / no wave band. Discogs artist ID: 443013. Name variation: “Certain Generals.”

Biography

Certain General was formed in late 1980 in Manhattan’s East Village by Parker Dulany, Phillip Gammage, Marcy Saddy, and Russell Berke. The original four-piece quickly became fixtures at downtown NYC venues including CBGB, Danceteria, and Club 57, performing alongside figures from the broader downtown art scene including Keith Haring and Jean-Michel Basquiat. Wikipedia, as of 2025-08-13

The original lineup recorded the debut EP Holiday of Love (Labor, cat. LAB-18) in 1982 — a five-track 12” mini-album produced by Peter Holsapple of the The dB’s and mixed by Michael Gira of Swans (then also a Labor Records act). Talk About Pop Music, 2015 The EP placed them firmly in the NYC post-punk / no wave milieu. Berke departed in 1983 and was replaced by Joe Lupo, who had previously been the band’s roadie. Wikipedia, as of 2025-08-13

After a brief dissolution, the band reconstituted around the core pair of Parker Dulany and Phillip Gammage and recorded November’s Heat (1984), released exclusively in France on L’Invitation Au Suicide (ID 7). The record was produced and recorded by Jim Tothe and mixed by Steve Ett (Steven Ettinger). French press named it album of the year and the band of the year — making Certain General one of the very few American acts with a genuine French cult following in this period. The four-piece lineup on the record was Dulany (vocals, guitar), Lupo (5) / Joe Lupo (vocals, bass, guitar, keyboards), Marcy Saddy (vocals, drums, percussion), and Gammage (vocals, guitar, harmonica); all four shared writing credits.

The mid-1980s saw upheaval in the lineup. By 1985 the band had regrouped in a substantially different configuration. These Are The Days (LAS ID 24, 1985) was produced by Sprague Hollander and Kevin Tooley, mixed by Andy Heermans, with Dulany credited for lyrics, art direction, and vocals. Far Away In America - The Live Side (LAS, 1985) was a split LP with Band Of Outsiders (Discogs ID 851918), a fellow LAS act active 1981–2013. The 7” Will You / Poison In The Pond (LAS ID 124, 1986) rounded out the label relationship — six releases in total, the deepest that LAS committed to any single American band.

In the late 1980s the band signed briefly to Barclay (France), releasing two records in 1988: the CD album Cabin Fever (Barclay 837 415-2) produced by Sprague Hollander, and the single I Lose Myself (also Hollander). The acoustic French tour in autumn 1987 by Dulany and Hollander was reportedly the catalyst for Barclay’s interest. soul-kitchen.fr interview, 2018 The 1990 LP Jacklighter (Barclay 843 100-1) marked the most high-profile outside involvement: tracks were co-produced and played on by Lloyd Cole, with additional production from Fred Maher and Gavin MacKillop. Cole also played harmonica and performed backing vocals on the single “Baby Are You Rich?”, and Blair Cowan — his Commotions bandmate — played accordion and Hammond organ on several cuts. The Lloyd Cole involvement came through a pre-existing planned touring arrangement: Barclay had scheduled Certain General to open for Lloyd Cole on a European and American tour in 1990, but pulled them from the tour one week before it was to start. soul-kitchen.fr interview, 2018 Matthew Sweet contributed backing vocals on one track (“She’s Got Mine”); Sweet’s connection to the band runs through Drew Vogelman, who also drummed for Sweet. soundbetter.com profile By this point the band’s lineup had narrowed to Dulany (vocals), Hollander (guitar, pedal steel, organ, harmonica, backing vocals), Philip Ames (bass), and Vogelman (drums/percussion).

The band entered hiatus after Barclay dropped them following Jacklighter, reconvening in 1999 when the original members (Dulany, Gammage, Saddy, Berke) reunited. Wikipedia, as of 2025-08-13 Post-reunion releases include Signals from the Source (1999), Closer to the Sun (2000), Live at the Public Theatre (2001, featuring guest Julee Cruise), An Introduction to War (2020, Sourmash USA Records, a 2CD set with studio sessions and live recordings from CBGB and City Gardens 1981–83) Bandcamp, Stolen Car (2010/2011, Le Maquis label), and Live it Down (2013). The band toured France in spring 2016. Wikipedia, as of 2025-08-13 Dulany and Gammage remained the main constants and songwriters across all incarnations. Robert Christgau described their sound as “rolling, ambitious, somewhat mannered art pop that is totally original.” Wikipedia, as of 2025-08-13 They have also been described as “the bridge between Television and Radiohead.” Bandcamp

Black Maria — Crammed Discs (1990, release ID 438093)

Black Maria is a self-titled album by the Belgian/French band Black Maria, released on Crammed Discs. Track 7 is credited “Composed By — Certain General” and “Composed By — Parker Dulany.” This is a song placement credit, not a full band appearance on the record. The album was recorded at Studio Acces Digital, Rouen, August 1990. Discogs r438093

Holiday of Love — Production Credit Note

The 1982 EP was produced by Peter Holsapple and mixed by Michael Gira of Swans (then also on Labor Records). Talk About Pop Music, 2015 Some sources (e.g. Trouser Press) incorrectly describe Gira as a co-producer; the liner notes credit is mixer only.

Open Questions

  • Exact lineup on Far Away In America - The Live Side (LAS, 1985) and Will You / Poison In The Pond (LAS, 1986) — Discogs credits remain sparse. The Sourmash full album (1984) used the lineup with Berke (bass), Saddy (drums), Dulany and Gammage, but this needs confirmation for the LAS-only live-side release.
  • What is the full catalog count on LAS? The stub says 6 releases but Discogs primary entries show 4 distinct masters (ID 7, split, ID 24, ID 124) — the remaining two may be singles or pressings not captured as separate masters.
  • Signals from the Source (1999) and Closer to the Sun (2000) label and full credits are not confirmed — Wikipedia lists them but without label or personnel detail.
  • The An Introduction to War (2020) Discogs master ID is not yet in the vault.
