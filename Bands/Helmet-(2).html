Helmet (2)

Alternative metal / post-hardcore band, New York. Discogs artist ID: 87645

Overview

Prominent 1990s alt-metal band founded by guitarist/vocalist Page Hamilton in New York City. Known for down-tuned, precise, riff-driven post-hardcore with a metronomic rhythm section. John Stanier was the drummer throughout the classic period (1989–1997); the classic lineup also included Henry Bogdan on bass and Peter Mengede (later Rob Echeverria, then Chris Traynor) on second guitar.
