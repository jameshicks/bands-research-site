King Kong (3)

Louisville, Kentucky experimental / rock collective. Discogs ID: 318171. Active 1988–2007+.

Founded in 1988 by Ethan Buckler, the original bassist of Slint. King Kong began as a vehicle for the same social orbit that produced Slint — the debut 7” features the full early Slint lineup — and evolved over nearly two decades into a shifting ensemble anchored by Buckler, cycling through a long list of Louisville and Chicago scene players. Eight studio releases on three labels; loosely bluesy, lo-fi, and eclectic in style, with a humorous streak that sits outside the post-rock canon most of its members are better known for.

Connections

  • Slint — all four founding members (Buckler, McMahan, Pajo, Walford) of the original King Kong lineup were members of Slint; the Movie Star 7” predates Spiderland and is effectively a Slint-lineup recording under a different name
  • Squirrel Bait — pre-Slint Louisville hardcore; McMahan, Walford, Grubbs, and Schuler all passed through
  • Evergreen (5) — Britt Walford connection; same Louisville underground
  • The For Carnation — Brian McMahan’s post-Slint band
  • Tortoise — David Pajo’s post-Slint base in Chicago
  • The Anomoanon — Willy MacLean is a core member
  • Royal Trux — Neil Hagerty produced The Big Bang (2002)
  • Wilco orbit — Mikael Jorgensen engineered The Big Bang before joining Wilco

Amy George / Amy Greenwood / Amy Partin Ritchie — Name Note

One person appears under three distinct names across the King Kong discography:

  • Amy George (Discogs 1212865) — Funny Farm (1993)
  • Amy Greenwood (Discogs 928226) — Me Hungry (1995), Kingdom Of Kong (1997)
  • Amy Partin Ritchie (Discogs 1914311) — Buncha Beans (2007)

The Discogs profile for King Kong (3) notes the full chain: “Amy Ritchie (formerly Amy Partin Ritchie, Amy Partin, Amy Greenwood, Amy George (maiden name)).” She was a vocalist with the band from approximately 1992–2024.
