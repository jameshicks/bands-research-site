Silver Jews

Discogs artist ID: 65825 Active: 1989–2009 Labels: Drag City (primary); Domino Records (UK/European) Primary member: David Berman (lyrics/vocals); Stephen Malkmus and Bob Nastanovich (early collaborators)

American indie/country-rock project led by David Berman. Berman died August 2019. Key Domino releases: Starlite Walker (1994), The Natural Bridge (1996), American Water (1998), Bright Flight (2001), Tanglewood Numbers (2005).

American Water features heavy contributions from Pavement’s Stephen Malkmus.
