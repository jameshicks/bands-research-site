Spacehopper (5)

Glasgow, Scotland. Discogs artist ID: 4837490. The “(5)” disambiguation is Discogs-assigned. Active on Creeping Bent 1995–1997, with three releases across two catalog entries. Genre: Indie Rock.

Overview

Spacehopper were a four-piece Glasgow band formed in 1990. Their Discogs profile lists the lineup as Dave Spacehopper (guitar/vocals), Sarah Spacehopper (guitar), Dave Bass (bass), and Bryn (drums) — the surnamed aliases “Spacehopper” and “Bass” appear to be band-adopted pseudonyms rather than legal surnames.

They were among Creeping Bent’s earliest signings, with their debut Milkmetal (Bent 002, 1995) being one of the very first vinyl releases on the label (following only Bent 001, a performance event). Their output on Creeping Bent is small but covers both sides of the label’s early format strategy: a 10” EP debut and a split 7”, with a further track appearing on the CD edition of Singles Club #1.

No Discogs aliases, group memberships, or associated projects are recorded for any of the members.

Label: Creeping Bent / Singles Club — Cat No: BENT019CD
Format: CD Single

Spacehopper contributed “Mars Bonding” (3:11) as track 4 on the CD edition of Singles Club #1. The 7” vinyl edition of Singles Club #1 (Bent 019, cat no. “bent 019”) was a separate release — an Adventures In Stereo / The Leopards split — and does not include Spacehopper. The CD-only bonus track was their final documented Creeping Bent appearance.

Notes on personnel:

  • Stephen Lironi (Discogs ID: 102018) — Creeping Bent’s key in-house producer; ex-Jazzateers, Altered Images; also frontman of The Revolutionary Corps Of Teenage Jesus.
  • Hodge (4) (Discogs ID: 3098797) — No further profile on Discogs; an apparent Glasgow session/studio figure. Produced both the B-sides of Milkmetal and Spacehopper’s side of the Bent 006 split.
  • Larry Primrose (Discogs ID: 318265) — Engineer and producer; worked at Sirocco Studio and Shabby Road Studios, Kilmarnock.
  • Graeme Wilmington (Discogs ID: 829763) — Realname Graeme Crawford Wilmington; member of Close Lobsters (Paisley jangle-pop, Fire Records). Wilmington’s appearance as engineer on Milkmetal is a notable connection between the Creeping Bent scene and the earlier Scottish indie scene.

Open Questions

  • Real identities of members: The names Dave Spacehopper, Sarah Spacehopper, Dave Bass, and Bryn are likely pseudonyms. No real names recorded on Discogs.
  • Formation context: Discogs profile says the band started in Glasgow in 1990, but their first release was 1995 — five years unaccounted for. What were they doing before Creeping Bent?
  • Hodge (4): No profile information on Discogs beyond credits. Who is this producer, and what other work did they do?
  • Post-1997 activity: No releases after Singles Club #1 (1997). Whether the band disbanded or simply stopped recording is not documented.
  • Larry Primrose’s studios: Described as working at Sirocco Studio and Shabby Road Studios, Kilmarnock — neither of which appears elsewhere in the Creeping Bent catalog documentation. Was he a Glasgow-area independent engineer?
