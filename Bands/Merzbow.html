Merzbow

Discogs ID: 12551 (artist), real name entry: Masami Akita (Discogs ID 58248) Origin: Tokyo, Japan Active: 1979–present Real name: Masami Akita

Japanese noise artist. One of the most prolific recording artists of any genre. Relapse Records issued Venereology (1994), one of the most extreme noise records in the Relapse catalog. Later: Merzbow + Boris Gensho (2016) and 2R0I2P0 (2020) on Relapse. The Desolation House sublabel of Relapse focused on ambient/dark ambient and may have hosted further Merzbow-adjacent material.

Lineup History

  • 1979–1989: Duo — Masami Akita and Kiyoshi Mizutani
  • 1989–1991: Solo — Masami Akita only (Mizutani departs)
  • 1991–1999: Trio — Masami Akita + Reiko Azuma (electronics, voice) + Tetsuo Sakaibara (aka Bara) (voice, dance)
  • 1999–present: Solo — Masami Akita only

Name derived from Kurt Schwitters’s “Merzbau”; intended to be pronounced the same way.

Early Label: Lowest Music & Arts

From 1980–1984, Merzbow released cassettes on Akita’s self-operated imprint Lowest Music & Arts (Discogs label ID 16449), out of Machida-shi, Tokyo. The label became ZSF Produkt in 1983 to accommodate releases by other artists. The full catalog was reissued in 2012 by German label Vinyl-on-demand as the Lowest Music & Arts 1980–1983 box set (10 LPs + 7”, cat VOD108).

Key Collaborations (partial)

  • Pan Sonic — V (2003, Les Disques Victo VICTO cd 089); a single 68-minute track; music by Masami Akita, Mika Vainio, and Ilpo Väisänen

Boris + Merzbow Collaborations

The Boris/Merzbow partnership spans two decades and multiple labels. The known Relapse releases are Gensho (2016), 2R0I2P0 (2020), and Relapse reissues of earlier albums in 2023. Non-Relapse collaborative releases:
