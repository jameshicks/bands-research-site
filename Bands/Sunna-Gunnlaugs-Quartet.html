Sunna Gunnlaugs Quartet

Icelandic-American contemporary jazz quartet. Discogs artist ID: 5652662.

Overview

The Sunna Gunnlaugs Quartet was the vehicle through which Icelandic pianist and composer Sunna Gunnlaugs (born Sunna Gunnlaugsdóttir, 11 May 1970) expanded her working trio into a saxophone-led quartet format. While her standard unit throughout the early 2000s was a piano/bass/drums trio, the Quartet brought in New York tenor saxophonist Ohad Talmor alongside bassist Matt Pavolka and her long-standing drummer Scott McLemore — both Pavolka and McLemore being New York-based players, giving the group its Icelandic-American character.

The Quartet’s sole documented release is Live In Europe (2003, Sunny Sky Records, Sunny Sky 722), a live CD recorded during a European tour. It is the only Discogs-listed entry for the band as a named entity. The Quartet appears to have been a touring configuration rather than a long-term recording project; Sunna’s main ongoing ensemble at this period remained the trio format, and the saxophonist slot (Ohad Talmor) was not carried into subsequent albums. The rhythm section of McLemore and Pavolka did not continue as a regular unit either — later trio recordings returned to Drew Gress or Eivind Opsvik on bass before eventually settling on Þorgrímur Jónsson.

The Quartet sits within a broader cluster of Sunna Gunnlaugs-led ensembles catalogued separately on Discogs: the solo recordings, the Sunna Gunnlaugsdóttir Trio, and this Quartet entry.
