Stilluppsteypa

Discogs ID: 70861
Origin: Reykjavík, Iceland
Members: Sigtryggur Berg Sigmarsson, Helgi Thorsson

Icelandic experimental electronic duo. Part of the Reykjavík avant-garde scene. Released material on various European experimental labels; associated with the broader network of Icelandic electronic and noise artists.

Contributed a remix used as the intro for “G.H.O.S.T.I.G.I.T.A.L.” on Ghostigital’s Division Of Culture And Tourism (2012, Smekkleysa).

Kitchen Motors Connection

Kitchen Motors was a Reykjavík-based record label, think tank, and artist collective founded in 1999, focused on experimental and avant-garde work. Stilluppsteypa contributed the track “Demon Jukebox” (2:55) to the Kitchen Motors Family Album (2006, 12 Tónar, cat: 12T027) — a compilation documenting artists who had worked with the collective since its founding. (Wikipedia, as of 2026-07-04) (Discogs)

The compilation’s framing (“artists and bands who have worked with Kitchen Motors since it started in 1999”) implies a working relationship with the collective, but the Wikipedia article does not describe Stilluppsteypa as a member or core affiliate — the nature of the prior relationship (commissioned project, live event, residency, etc.) is not documented in available sources.

Other contributing artists on the same compilation include Múm, Jóhann Jóhannsson, Amiina, Mugison, Slowblow, Benni Hemm Hemm, Apparat Organ Quartet, and Kira Kira — placing Stilluppsteypa within the same documented network of Reykjavík experimental and avant-garde artists.

Collaboration — Hildur Gudnadóttir & BJ Nilsen

Stilluppsteypa released Second Childhood (2007, Quecksilber) in collaboration with Icelandic cellist Hildur Gudnadóttir and Swedish sound artist BJ Nilsen. Recorded and mixed in Stockholm 2006, with additional sessions in Berlin and Reykjavík. Seven tracks, 56 minutes. (Discogs release 1068600) (bjnilsen.info)

This is a second documented Icelandic collaboration (beyond Curver/Ghostigital), with Hildur Gudnadóttir being a prominent figure in Icelandic experimental/contemporary music.

Scene Connections — Curver

Curver (real name: Birgir Örn Thoroddsen) is an Icelandic musician, producer, and visual artist, best known as one half of Ghostigital (with Einar Örn Benediktsson). He was active in the Reykjavík experimental scene from the early 1990s; a 2005 Grapevine retrospective on his 1991–1994 compilation Sær explicitly names Stilluppsteypa alongside Curver as contemporaries who “sounded like nothing heard in the local music scene before.” source

Documented joint release: Stilluppsteypa and Curver released a collaborative 7” EP titled Inside AM (b/w “Make Star Shine”) on Fire Inc. (catalog: f-2, 1994, Reykjavík). This is the primary documented collaboration between the two. source: RateYourMusic

Later connection via Ghostigital: Stilluppsteypa contributed a track used as the intro for “G.H.O.S.T.I.G.I.T.A.L.” on Ghostigital’s Division Of Culture And Tourism (2012, Smekkleysa), indirectly linking the two acts again through Curver’s Ghostigital project.
