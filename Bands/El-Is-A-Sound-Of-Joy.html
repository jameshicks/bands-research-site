El Is A Sound Of Joy

Discogs ID: 5022025 Based: Chicago, IL Members: Ben Lamar Gay, Rob Frye, Will Faber (3) Active: at least 2016–2017 (Discogs span); undated EP also exists

Overview

El Is A Sound Of Joy is a Chicago-based free improvisation / avant-garde jazz ensemble with three core members — cornetist/multi-instrumentalist Ben Lamar Gay, multi-woodwind player Rob Frye, and guitarist/multi-instrumentalist Will Faber (3). The group functions as an extended ensemble in practice, regularly pulling in additional players. Their recorded output consists of three self-released/small-label CDr releases.

The name does not appear to have a stable lineup — Plus featured eight musicians, Ephemerides featured six, and the Avreeayl Ra EP featured six (including guest percussionist Avreeayl Ra). Recurring expanded members include Alex Inglizian (synth, electronics) and Stephen Ptacek (percussion).

Distinct from I See Lightning Trio (Discogs ID 7937954), which is an identically-membered trio (Gay, Frye, Faber) but documents a more spare, folk-inflected approach.
