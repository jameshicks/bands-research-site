F/i

Milwaukee experimental/space rock group. Discogs artist ID: 94864. Name variations: F-i, F/I. Alias: The Surfin’ Führers. Websites: sixthstation.com/fi, furious.com/perfect/milwaukee.html.

Overview

Released two cassettes (Y and Zombie, both 1985) and contributed tracks to the Mirage compilation on Cause And Effect. Member Richard Franecki also released solo material on the label (Test Pattern, 1985).

Members (Discogs)
