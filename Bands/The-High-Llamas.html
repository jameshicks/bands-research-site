The High Llamas

London-based baroque/orchestral pop group formed 1992 by Sean O’Hagan. Discogs artist ID: 2815

Overview

The High Llamas are Sean O’Hagan’s primary vehicle, emerging after his departure from Microdisney (1988) and a solo record titled High Llamas (1990). The group name was taken from a Victorian hot-air balloon image in a magazine. O’Hagan assembled a large rotating cast of London musicians — players from the orchestral, jazz, and indie worlds — to realise productions drawing on late-1960s Brian Wilson (especially Smile-era Beach Boys and Van Dyke Parks), French pop (Gainsbourg, Nino Ferrer), and British orchestral pop.

Their aesthetic is consistent: intricate string and brass arrangements, Vox organ, vibraphone, upright piano, Moog synthesizer, glockenspiel, detailed multi-part vocal harmonies, and an embrace of exotica and 1970s library music colourations. Later albums moved toward extended groove-based pieces and electronic textures without abandoning the orchestral palette.

Long-term label home is Drag City (from Can Cladders, 2007 onward). Earlier catalog was issued on Target Records (4) (Gideon Gaye, 1994), Alpaca Park (the mid-to-late 1990s run), and V2 (co-releases, 1998–2000). Sean O’Hagan was also a member of Stereolab 1993–1994 and a recurring string/brass arranger on their albums through 2008.
