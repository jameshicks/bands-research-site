Overview

Les Slugs are a Belgian punk band who write and perform entirely in French, mixing punk and grindcore with post-punk and chanson elements. They debuted in 1991 on Sket Records before moving to their long-term home Aredje, which released their work from 1993 through 2019. Their catalogue has an unusual shape: one release in the 1990s, then silence until three further records appeared in the 2010s. Their closest recorded collaborator is fellow Belgian act Agathocles, with three shared releases pointing to a shared DIY grindcore scene.
