Red House Painters

American alternative/slowcore rock group. Formed 1989, San Francisco, CA. Disbanded 2001. Discogs artist ID: 82311.

Led by Mark Kozelek (vocals, guitar). Signed to 4AD in 1992; final album on Sub Pop (2001). One of the premier slowcore/indie rock groups of the 1990s.
