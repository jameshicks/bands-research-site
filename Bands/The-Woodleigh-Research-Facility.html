Notes

After Weatherall’s death in February 2020, Walsh appears to continue the project — Phonox Nights (2023) and Vous Du EP (2024) remain credited to The Woodleigh Research Facility with both names in the DB. Whether the Weatherall credits reflect pre-recorded material or posthumous attribution is unclear from Discogs alone.

Post-Weatherall Activity (Web sources)

Andrew Weatherall died 17 February 2020. The project’s post-2020 output divides into two distinct streams:

1. Archival WRF releases (duo material, released posthumously under The Woodleigh Research Facility name)

All post-2020 releases credited to The Woodleigh Research Facility are archival — material recorded with Weatherall before his death, curated and released by Walsh afterward.

  • Phonox Nights (December 2023, Facility 4) — described by retailers as “the last album conceptualised and completed by Nina Walsh and Andrew Weatherall at her Facility 4 studio before his February 2020 passing.” Named after the Brixton club where the material was road-tested. Sleeve notes by Kris Needs. source
  • Vous Du EP (2024, Byrd Out) — also posthumous archival material. source (Discogs)
  • Apparently Solo series (multiple volumes, 2022 onwards, WRF Bandcamp) — Walsh’s “dispatches from her colossal vault of previously unheard W.R.F. recordings.” Archival duo recordings. source
  • Maximum Vaultage series (Vol. 1–12+, launched February 2025, WRF Bandcamp) — twelve three-track EPs released monthly throughout 2025, drawn from finished mixes in the duo’s “for sale” folder. Walsh described the process as “an extremely healing experience.” Sleeve notes by Kris Needs. source

2. New solo productions (under Facility 5 / Nina Walsh name)

Walsh has also made new solo music, clearly distinguished from the archival WRF strand by a separate label identity:

  • All Is Not Lost (November 5, 2021, Facility 5) — described as her “spectacular maiden voyage” under the new Facility 5 name, recorded after relocating from south London to Dorset. Instruments: keyboards, guitar, arcane machinery, voice. Explicitly credited as solo Walsh, not WRF. source
  • Sail We Must (March 14, 2025, Facility 5 / ninawalsh.bandcamp.com) — further solo work on Facility 5. source

The Facility 5 Bandcamp bio states: “Since Andrew’s 2020 passing all actually solo productions are by Nina Walsh, marked by the new Facility5 name & logo.” source

Walsh’s public statements

In a 2026 interview with Iconic Underground Magazine, Walsh discussed the aftermath of Weatherall’s death and described the experience of revisiting archival recordings as “an extremely healing experience.” She also fought to ensure Weatherall’s name remained on Phonox Nights, stating: “It cost me quite dearly mentally and financially to make sure Andrew’s name was on the album because I had to fight and fight and fight.” Walsh is also publishing an autobiography in two parts (via writer Kris Needs), the first covering the Sabres era. A documentary adaptation is reportedly under discussion. source

Summary

The WRF name is reserved for archival duo material (pre-2020 recordings); Walsh’s new solo compositions appear under Facility 5 / her own name. The Bandcamp page for The Woodleigh Research Facility continues to serve as the primary archive delivery mechanism, with the Maximum Vaultage series (launched February 2025) being the most active ongoing strand as of mid-2026.
