Kreidler

Discogs ID: 405
Origin: Düsseldorf, Germany

Düsseldorf minimal/electronic rock group. Key member Andreas Reihse plays keyboards. The Düsseldorf base places them in proximity to the post-krautrock scene around Klaus Dinger and La! NEU? — Reihse was also a member of La! NEU? and appeared on Japandorf’s 2000!. Stefan Schneider (bass) was a member before leaving to co-found To Rococo Rot in Berlin (1995).
