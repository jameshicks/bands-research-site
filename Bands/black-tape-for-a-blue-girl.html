black tape for a blue girl

US ethereal/darkwave/ambient band founded and led by Sam Rosenthal. Discogs artist ID: 84056. The founding and flagship act of Projekt Records; active 1986–present.

Overview

black tape for a blue girl is the primary recording project of Sam Rosenthal, who launched it alongside his Projekt label. The band name is consistently lowercase. The sound spans 4AD-influenced ethereal pop, spoken-word, dark ambient, and neoclassical territory. It has been the most continuously present act on Projekt across its entire history (139 releases on Discogs, 1986–2025).

Key collaborators have cycled through across the band’s career; Rosenthal has used various vocalists, instrumentalists, and guest performers on different albums. Rosenthal himself appears as As Lonely As Dave Bowman (ambient alias) and Nova Cat Purring Sounds.
