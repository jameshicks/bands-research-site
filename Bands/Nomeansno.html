Oct 30, 20264 min read

Nomeansno

Discogs ID: 133641
Origin: Victoria, British Columbia, Canada
Active: 1979–2016
Genres: Punk, post-punk, jazz-punk, hardcore

Influential Canadian post-punk/jazz-punk band (primarily the Wright brothers: Rob and John). All Roads Lead To Ausfahrt (2006) on Southern Records. Long association with Alternative Tentacles.

Southern Records connection

Nomeansno’s relationship with Southern Records developed in the early 2000s, primarily driven by European distribution concerns. Their connection appears to predate the full move: a February 2000 quote from Tom Holliston (guitarist) referenced the “SHAME” EP being released on “Southern records, UK,” suggesting Southern was handling at least some UK/European releases before the band formally left Alternative Tentacles. [source: no-means-no.de/nmn-news.html]

In April 2002, Nomeansno parted ways with Alternative Tentacles after roughly 15 years. The split was described as amicable — the band simply wanted to control their records, and distribution in Europe (their primary market) had become a key concern. [source: livevictoria.com forum, https://livevictoria.com/index.php?action=browse_messageboard&current_board=16&current_subject=216515&expand_user_info=216580] By July 2002, Southern Records was listed in their own biography as the band’s home for re-releasing the back catalogue. [source: no-means-no.de/nmn-news.html]

The arrangement was a distribution deal rather than a traditional signing: Nomeansno released material on their own Wrong Records imprint, with Southern handling manufacturing and distribution for UK/Europe. This covered back-catalogue reissues (e.g. Wrong reissued 2004, Mama CD debut 2004, Small Parts Isolated and Destroyed and 0+2=1 reissued January/February 2007) and the final studio album All Roads Lead To Ausfahrt (2006), which came out on Wrong Records / AntAcidAudio (North America) and Southern Records (Europe). [sources: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Roads_Lead_to_Ausfahrt (as of 2026-10-30); en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomeansno (as of 2026-10-30)]

Southern Records was founded by John Loder (who ran Southern Studios) and had an existing model of distributing North American indie/punk labels in Europe — they had formal relationships with Dischord, Touch & Go, Ipecac, and Constellation, among others. Nomeansno’s move to Southern fits that pattern: a band seeking European reach connecting with a label that functioned as a hub between American independent music and European audiences. No source names a specific personal introduction or individual at Southern who brokered the deal. [source: drownedinsound.org label focus on Southern Records, https://www.drownedinsound.org/archive-label-focus-18-southern-records-3478970/]

The relationship later soured: on 14 December 2023, the band posted publicly that Southern Records “ha[d] been selling their records without payment to the band for years and are not an authorized source” of their recordings. [source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Records (as of 2026-10-30)]

Note on the brief: The research brief described All Roads Lead To Ausfahrt as a “1992 album.” This is incorrect — the album was released 22 August 2006. The vault entry above already had the correct date (2006). No Nomeansno release from 1992 appears to have involved Southern Records; their 1992-era catalogue was on Alternative Tentacles.
