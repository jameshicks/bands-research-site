L7

Discogs ID: 227850

Los Angeles punk/grunge band formed in 1985. Members: Donita Sparks, Suzi Gardner, Jennifer Finch, Dee Plakas. Released several albums on Slash including Bricks Are Heavy (1992, produced by Butch Vig) and Hungry for Stink (1994).
