The Jazz Composer’s Orchestra

Free jazz / avant-garde large ensemble, USA, founded by Michael Mantler and Carla Bley. Also credited as Jazz Composer’s Orchestra, Jazz Composers Orchestra. Discogs artist ID: 309383. The associated label was JCOA Records.

Overview

The Jazz Composer’s Orchestra was a large-scale collective founded in New York in the mid-1960s. It served as a vehicle for extended orchestral free jazz and was closely tied to the JCOA (Jazz Composer’s Orchestra Association) record label, which distributed records outside the mainstream industry. The roster drew on virtually every major figure in the New York free jazz scene.
