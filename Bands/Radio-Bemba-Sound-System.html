Radio Bemba Sound System

Discogs ID: 1583991
Also known as: Radio Bemba
Type: Live touring band for Manu Chao

Overview

Radio Bemba was initially an alias used by Manu Chao for secret shows in the 1990s; the name was then used for his touring band in the 2000s. The ensemble performed Manu Chao’s catalog live and appears on two officially documented releases.
