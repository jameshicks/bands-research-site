Inca Ore

Discogs ID: 922265
Real name: Eva Saelens (also credited as Eva Pox; Discogs ID: 3217358)
Active: 2005–c.2009 (primary release window)
Base: Portland, Oregon
Labels: Collective Jyrk, Not Not Fun Records
Genres: Experimental, avant-folk, noise, psychedelic; Discogs tags include Abstract, Noise, Poetry, Therapy

Solo project of Eva Saelens (Eva Pox), a Portland-based experimental musician operating in the Pacific Northwest underground of the mid-2000s. Closely embedded in the scene around Pete Swanson, Adam Forkner (White Rainbow), Honey Owens (Valet), and Nick Bindeman (Tunnels) — many of whom shared the Jackie O Motherfucker extended orbit. Five shared Discogs releases with Grouper (Liz Harris), including a direct split (2007).

Era I — Collective Jyrk (2005)

Debut release Brute Nature Vs. Wild Magic (2005, Collective Jyrk, CDr) established the Inca Ore sound: DIY home recording, layered voice, psychedelic drift, raw production. Recorded by Eva Saelens; mixed by Adam Forkner and Honey Owens; mastered by Pete Swanson.

The same year, Collaborations (2005, Collective Jyrk / Decomposition) — a compilation — credited Inca Ore for voice contributions.

Era II — Self-Released / Not Not Fun (2007–2009)

A self-released split with Grouper (2007) connected Inca Ore to the Portland ambient underground and the broader Not Not Fun Records ecosystem.

Birthday Of Bless You (2008, Not Not Fun Records, NNF107) was Inca Ore’s vinyl debut — a limited LP. Entirely self-produced, self-recorded, and self-written; mastered and sequenced by Pete Swanson. One track (“Silver Wings”) uses lyrics by country songwriter Merle Haggard. Discogs styles: Abstract, Avantgarde, Experimental.

Silver Sea Surfer School (2009, Not Not Fun Records, NNF175) — second LP, no additional credits listed beyond Inca Ore as primary artist. Styles: Abstract, Noise, Poetry, Therapy — the “Therapy” tag distinctive in the catalog.

Scene Context

Inca Ore was embedded in the mid-2000s Portland experimental underground, a loose scene that included:

  • Jackie O Motherfucker — extended collective whose members (Adam Forkner, Honey Owens, Nick Bindeman) all appear on Inca Ore releases
  • Pete Swanson — Portland noise artist and frequent technical collaborator (also one half of Yellow Swans with Gabriel Mindel Saloman)
  • Not Not Fun Records — Los Angeles label (Amanda Brown / Britt Brown) that served as the key distribution point for this wing of West Coast experimental music; Inca Ore’s two LPs are both NNF releases
  • Grouper — direct split partner; Liz Harris and Eva Saelens shared five Discogs releases total

The combination of home recording, layered voice, psychedelic drift, and hand-pressed CDr / limited vinyl situates Inca Ore firmly in the DIY freak-folk / noise-adjacent experimental scene that flourished on the West Coast c.2004–2010.
