Decahedron

Discogs artist ID: 1023043. Also recorded as The Black Sea (Discogs ID: 576447). Post-hardcore / noise-rock trio, Washington DC. Active 2002–2004. Name variation on record: Decadroid.

Overview

Decahedron formed in 2002 under the name The Black Sea, with Shelby Cinca (guitar/vocals, formerly of Frodus) and Jason Hamacher (drums, also of Frodus) recruiting Joe Lally (bass, then still active in Fugazi) and guitarist/bassist Jake Brown. The band released their debut EP as The Black Sea on Lovitt Records in 2002, then renamed to Decahedron and released their sole full-length, Disconnection_Imminent, on Lovitt in 2004. Lally left after these two releases; the band continued without him for a period but did not issue further full-lengths. Discogs lists The Black Sea (576447) and Decahedron (1023043) as mutual aliases.

The project bridged the post-hardcore/math-rock scene orbiting Lovitt Records and Frodus with Lally’s longer-running work in the DC underground. The connection to Fugazi brought extra attention, though the sound was its own: angular, noise-inflected guitar rock with denser texture than Frodus’s earlier work.

According to Wikipedia’s Frodus article, after Lally left following Disconnection_Imminent, he was replaced by Jonathan Ford (of Unwed Sailor and Roadside Monument). Ford subsequently left in 2004, and Jake Brown (formerly of Moments In Grace and Twothirtyeight) then joined. The band suspended operations in 2005 following the release of an EP titled 2005 — this release does not appear in Discogs under the Decahedron entry. Jake Brown went on to work across multiple projects under the Computer Club Records orbit and also works under the alias ISYA.

Note on Discogs coverage gap: The post-Lally period of Decahedron (2004–2005 with Jonathan Ford and then Jake Brown) does not appear to be documented in Discogs. The 2005 EP mentioned by Wikipedia is not in the Decahedron Discogs entry. Jake Brown (Discogs ID: 2800476) is credited as a member on the Decahedron page, suggesting his tenure is recognized, but no release credits appear for him with the band.
