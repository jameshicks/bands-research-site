The Controllers

Punk rock band from Los Angeles, California. Discogs artist ID: 372541. Name variation: The Controllers.

Overview

The Controllers formed in July 1977 in Los Angeles and disbanded in 1979, reuniting briefly in 1999 and again in the early 2000s. They were part of the first wave of LA punk centred on The Masque club in Hollywood, contemporaries of The Germs, The Dils, and other bands recorded and released by What Records? founder Chris Ashford. Their two What Records? singles — the split 7” Don’t Talk To Me / (The Original) Neutron Bomb / Victims (1978) and their own Neutron Bomb / Killer Queers (1978) — are their most-documented original-era releases. A third single, Slow Boy (1979), appeared on Siamese Records. The band left behind no original full-length LP; their anthology The Controllers (Bacchus Archives, 2000) collected the singles and additional studio and live material.

Connections

  • What Records? — Chris Ashford’s label released both original 1978 singles; the label’s catalog runs Germs → Dils → Controllers → Skulls → Eyes as WHAT 01–03, a snapshot of the core Masque scene
  • The Germs — labelmates on What Records? (WHAT 01, “Forming”)
  • The Dils — labelmates on What Records? (WHAT 02, “I Hate The Rich”)
  • The Eyes (LA) / The Skulls (3) — shared the WHAT 03 split EP; The Eyes co-written by Charlotte Caffey (later of The Go-Go’s)
  • The Gears — Kidd Spike and Steve Swailes are also members
  • Skull Control — Kidd Spike is also listed as a member
  • Charlie Quintana — drummed on 1982 live recordings; went on to the Cruzados, Izzy Stradlin & the Ju Ju Hounds, Social Distortion (session), and other significant work
  • Soul Jazz Records — reissued “Neutron Bomb” in 2013–14 on the Punk 45 series alongside The Pagans
  • Bibbe Hansen / Al Hansen — Slow Boy EP photography (Bibbe) and co-writing credit (Al); Al Hansen was a Fluxus artist and Bibbe’s father; Beck’s family connection (Bibbe is Beck’s mother)

Open Questions

  • Mike Alessi appears in the Discogs member roster but has no credited role on any specific release and no independent Discogs artist page with credits — his specific role and era within the band is undocumented in the database.
  • Gaye Austin and Hillary Dillary are named in the profile as 1977 drummers but appear on no credited releases — no independent Discogs presence found.
  • The The Controllers Bacchus Archives LP (BA1148) side B includes tracks by Kaos (14) and Skull Control — relationship between these acts and the Controllers is not explained in the Discogs record.
  • “Forming” on What Records? is listed as WHAT 01, “I Hate The Rich” as WHAT 02, the split EP as WHAT 03, Neutron Bomb as WHAT 04 — WHAT 05 appears missing from the catalog; not documented in Discogs.
  • Whether the band played The Masque regularly or had other documented live history beyond the Jan. 1982 Cathay De Grande recording is not in Discogs.
  • The 1999 reunion is noted in the profile but no releases tied to it appear in Discogs (the 1996 demo on Another Sunny Day may predate the formal reunion date).
