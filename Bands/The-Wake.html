The Wake

Scottish post-punk/indie band. Discogs artist ID: 12091. Released on Factory Records, Factory Benelux. Associated with the Factory label group. Members include Caesar (Gerard McNulty), Carolyn Allen, Steven Allen, Bobby Gillespie (early).

Overview

Reunited for Harmony LP (Factory Benelux, 2013), on which Wilf Smarties produced tracks 14 and 15. Other producers on that album: Chris Nagle, John Leckie, Oz (3), Barry Andrew.
