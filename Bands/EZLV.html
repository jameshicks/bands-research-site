EZLV

Discogs ID: 2093723 Name variation: EZLV (also appears as “Ezlv”) Active: 2011–2013 Origin: Montréal, Canada

DJs and producers duo from Montréal. Real names not documented on Discogs. Released two digital EPs in 2011 and a split 12” vinyl in 2013 (the label debut for House Of Silk). All three releases mastered by fellow Montréal producer Pheek (Jean-Patrice Rémillard), or by Anders Peterson (on the House Of Silk 12”). Styles span Deep House, Tech House, House, and Minimal.

No compilations on Discogs. No further activity documented after 2013.
