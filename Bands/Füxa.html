Overview

Füxa is the space rock project of Randall Nieman, active since 1995 with a rotating lineup that has included Eric Morrison, Jesse Percival, Ryan Anderson, and Tom Meade. The catalogue is weighted toward the 1990s (10 releases) with a quieter 2000s stretch (3 releases) followed by a sustained return in the 2010s (5 more). Mind Expansion and Rocket Girl have each carried 8 credits across nearly three decades, making them the twin constants in an otherwise shifting release history. The collaborator network is unusually wide for the genre: Richard Formby and Stephen Lawrie anchor a UK shoegaze axis, while Dean Wareham and Britta Phillips, Mark Refoy (ex-Spiritualized), Martin Rev (Suicide), and Stereolab all share credits, mapping a project that ranged well beyond its ambient/space rock core.

Notes (web) — 2000s gap and 2010s return

Why output slowed after 2002: In an interview with David Bourgoin at Progress Report (progress-report.co.uk/Interviews/Fuxa/Fuxa.htm — page returning 521 as of 2026-11-20, content summarized in search snippets), Nieman stated that soon after releasing Supercharged (2002) he joined Sonic Boom (Pete Kember)‘s touring group Spectrum for approximately five or six years. During this period Spectrum toured extensively in America and Europe (2007–2011 documented). He also did work with The Telescopes, Martin Rev, and others through his own label Mind Expansion. The Rocket Girl artist page corroborates this framing directly: “after a few years consumed by other projects, fuxa returns full steam.” https://rocketgirl.co.uk/artists/13

The only Füxa release between Supercharged (2002) and the 2010s burst was the Where The Sky Is Low / Electric Sound Of Summer 7” on Mind Expansion (2005), consistent with Nieman maintaining minimal Füxa output while active in Spectrum.

The 2010s return: The first marker of renewed activity was the Split 7” on Rocket Girl (2011), followed by Electric Sound Of Summer LP (2012) — described by Rocket Girl as a “return full steam” — and then three further Rocket Girl releases in 2013 alone (Dirty D, Frequencies For Physical, Mental And Spiritual Healing, Sun Is Shining). The Drowned in Sound review of Electric Sound Of Summer noted it was “his first long player since” The Modified Mechanics Of This Device (2001), an 11-year gap. http://dis11.herokuapp.com/releases/17086/reviews/4145114

Founding member Ryan Anderson’s return is part of the 2013 picture: he appeared on “Berzap” on Dirty D, described as his first Füxa recording since In Flight Audio (1999). Search snippet source: progress-report.co.uk interview via Google snippet, not directly fetched.
