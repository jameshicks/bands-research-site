Preoccupations

Post-punk band from Calgary, Alberta, Canada. Discogs artist ID: 5224523 (Preoccupations); 3664488 (Viet Cong (2)). Formed 2012, originally as Viet Cong. Name changed to Preoccupations in 2016 amid controversy over the original name.

Members: Matt Flegel (bass, vocals), Scott Munro (guitar, synth), Daniel Christiansen (guitar), Mike Wallace (drums). All four members also played together previously in Women.

History

Women and Pre-History

Preoccupations grew directly out of Women, a Calgary post-punk/noise-pop quartet active c. 2007–2012. Women’s members included Matt Flegel, Mike Wallace, Pat Flegel (Matt’s sibling, later known as Pat Flegel / Cindy Lee), and Chris Reimer (who died in 2012). Daniel Christiansen was not in Women but joined Flegel and Wallace for the new band. Scott Munro — active in several Calgary experimental ensembles including Ghostkeeper, Bent Spoon Duo, Bent Spoon Trio, and The Musk Cup — rounded out the lineup.

Matt Flegel and Mike Wallace were also briefly in Veritas, a band listed on Discogs as an alias of Women, alongside Pat Flegel.

Viet Cong Era (2012–2016)

The band formed under the name Viet Cong (Discogs: Viet Cong (2) to disambiguate from another artist). Their earliest release was a self-titled cassette (2013, Not On Label) with six tracks, later released digitally under the Throw It Away EP on their own. “Continental Shelf” appeared as a free digital single on Jagjaguwar in 2014, signalling the label relationship that would anchor their catalog.

The self-titled debut album Viet Cong (January 2015, Jagjaguwar, cat. JAG260) drew wide critical attention. The album runs seven tracks, culminating in the side-long “Death” (11:17). Styles on Discogs: Alternative Rock, Post-Punk. The album was released internationally via Jagjaguwar and domestically through Flemish Eye.

The name attracted protests over its perceived disrespect to Vietnamese communities. By mid-2016 the band had formally changed their name to Preoccupations.

Preoccupations Era (2016–present)

Self-Titled (2016) — Preoccupations (Jagjaguwar, JAG290). Produced by Graham Walsh (of Holy Fuck), Matt Flegel, and Scott Munro; recorded and mixed by Walsh. Walsh also plays keyboards on “Forbidden” and “Fever.” Guest vocalists: Dan Boeckner (Wolf Parade / Handsome Furs / Divine Fits) on “Memory”; Julie Fader on “Anxiety” and “Memory”; Jessie Stein on “Memory.” Inside artwork by Pat Flegel. Mastered by Paul Gold. A standalone single, “Degraded,” was also released on Jagjaguwar the same year.

Tracklist: Anxiety / Monotony / Zodiac / Memory / Degraded / Sense / Forbidden / Stimulation / Fever.

Cassette (2017) — Live/archival cassette release on Jagjaguwar (master shared with the Viet Cong cassette). Credits list the full four members as Performers, with Matt Flegel and Scott Munro as Producers and Munro handling recording/mixing. Notably includes a cover of Bauhaus’s “Spy in the Cab” (credited as Songwriter: Bauhaus). Pat Flegel (as Patrick Flegel) did the cover art. Mastered by Paul Gold.

Telemetry At Howe Bridge (2018) — Split 7” with Protomartyr (2) on Domino, released as a Record Store Day exclusive. Two tracks, one per band. The Discogs credit entry for this release contains no creative credits beyond the two primary artists — the label note in the brief about Sonny Diperri as shared producer is not confirmed by Discogs credits on this specific release. Sonny Diperri’s connection to both bands likely comes through their respective studio work rather than this split.

New Material (2018) — Second LP under the Preoccupations name (Jagjaguwar, JAG320). No additional production credits on Discogs. Tracklist: Espionage / Decompose / Disarray / Manipulation / Antidote / Solace / Doubt / Compliance. Genre: Indie Rock on Discogs.

Arrangements (2022) — Third LP (Flemish Eye, FLCR052-LP), returning to the Canadian indie label. Produced by Matt Flegel and Scott Munro; mixed by Graham Walsh; mastered by Mikey Young (Eddy Current Suppression Ring, Total Control). Mike Wallace (credited as Michael Wallace, Discogs ID 567656 here) gets a co-producer credit. Track “Advisor” features cello by Rashaad Jones and guitar by Thomas B Martin. Tracklist: Fix Bayonets! / Ricochet / Death of Melody / Slowly / Advisor / Recalibrate / Tearing up the Grass.

Ill At Ease (2025) — Fourth LP (Born Losers Records, blr124). Produced and recorded by Flegel and Munro; mixed by Marcos Muniz, who also adds drum programming to “Sken” and piano/synth to “Krem2.” Mike Wallace returns as additional producer. Drums on A3–A4 and B1–B4 recorded by Drew Riekman. Additional guitars by Alana Devito. Guest vocals on “Focus” by Marlaena Moore. Mastered by Mikey Young. Tracklist: Focus / Bastards / Ill At Ease / Retrograde / Andromeda / Panic / Sken / Krem2.
