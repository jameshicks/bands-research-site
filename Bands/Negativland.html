Negativland

Bay Area (California) experimental/culture-jamming group. Discogs artist ID: 24120. Own label: Seeland.

Overview

Known for audio collage, found-sound manipulation, and media culture critique. Jamcon ‘84 (recorded 1984, released 1986) was co-released on Cause And Effect and their own Seeland label (Seeland cat# 004). Members on that release include Don Joyce, Mark Hosler, Richard Lyons, David Wills, Ian Allen, Chris Grigg.
