Sex Pistols

UK punk band, London, 1975–78. Discogs artist ID: 31753.

Overview

Assembled and managed by Malcolm McLaren from his King’s Road boutique SEX circle. Lineup: John Lydon (aka Johnny Rotten, vocals), Steve Jones (guitar), Paul Cook (drums), Glen Matlock (bass, later replaced by Sid Vicious). Released “Anarchy in the U.K.” (EMI, 1976), “God Save the Queen” (A&M then Virgin, 1977), and the album Never Mind the Bollocks, Here’s the Sex Pistols (Virgin, 1977). Disintegrated in January 1978 after the US tour.

Connections

  • Malcolm McLaren — manager
  • John Lydon — vocalist
  • Glen Matlock — original bassist
  • Public Image Limited — Lydon’s post-Pistols band
  • Rich Kids — Matlock’s post-Pistols band

Flowers of Romance connection

The name “Flowers of Romance” was suggested by John Lydon and used by the short-lived 1976 rehearsal band Flowers of Romance, which included Sid Vicious. The name was later used for on-stage Pistols jams.
