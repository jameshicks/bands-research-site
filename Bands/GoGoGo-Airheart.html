GoGoGo Airheart

Discogs ID: 125431

Origin: San Diego, California
Active: 1996–2006
Final show: May 22, 2006, The Casbah, San Diego
Labels: Wheat Records · Vinyl Communications · Ideal Weight · Overcoat Recordings · Gold Standard Laboratories (GSL) · Sympathy For The Record Industry · Flapping Jet Records

San Diego post-punk band. Over a decade they moved from an experimental, dub-inflected sound (debut CD, 1997) through art-punk (1998) and angular post-punk with jazz touches (2000) to a leaner, more direct indie rock approach (2002, 2005). Connected to the GSL/Three One G San Diego underground but occupying an idiosyncratic position — too melodic for grindcore, too abrasive for indie rock.

Members

Personnel shifted across eras. The core throughout was Ashish Vyas (bass, vocals) and Mike Vermillion (guitar, vocals). Drumming duties rotated; violin and keyboards appeared in specific periods.

Era by Era

1996–1998: Early Period (Vinyl Communications / Ideal Weight)

The band’s first recording appeared on a 1996 split 7” with Carpet on Wheat Records 001 — an obscure limited white label. Carpet’s lineup included Luke Jenner, who would later front The Rapture.

The debut LP Gogogoairheart (1997) was released on Vinyl Communications (CD only) and sits apart from the rest of the catalog: tagged Ambient, Dub, Experimental, and Post Rock on Discogs, it reflects an earlier, more diffuse sound. The lineup at this point included Andy Robillard on drums, Teri Hoefer on violin, Michael Henning, and The Spacewürm as an additional recording participant. Production was shared between the band, Andy Ward (14), and The Spacewürm.

Love My Life…Hate My Friends (1998, Ideal Weight IDL001) is the first LP recorded with Rafter Roberts, who would become a recurring collaborator. Rafter recorded, mixed, and contributed “additional tonalities” to several tracks. The lineup stripped to Robillard, Vyas, Vermillion, and Hoefer.

1999–2000: GSL Alignment

The Things We Need EP (1999, Overcoat Recordings OC-02) introduces a new lineup configuration: Jimmy LaValle (later The Album Leaf) plays drums and electric piano, Jay Hough contributes drums on one track, and Jason Crane adds trumpet — an element that persisted into the next LP. Benjamin White appears as a full member.

Out Every Window The Snap Of Envy & Greed (2000) was their first release on Gold Standard Laboratories (co-released with Ideal Weight). The album cover of The Pop Group’s “Trap” signals their post-punk reference points. Jay Hough is now the primary drummer; Benjamin White handles keyboards, piano, synth, and guitar. Rafter Roberts recorded, mixed, and mastered — and played lap steel on the closing track “For Your Will.”

2002–2005: Full GSL Period

Exitheuxa (2002, GSL56) is the most documented release: full per-track personnel credits show the mature four-piece lineup (Vyas, Hough, White, Vermillion) with Andy Robillard handling drums on three specific tracks (“Sit And Stare,” “Last Goodbye,” “Move Along”). Sonny Kay — GSL founder — handled layout and design. The album was recorded by Rafter Roberts and Ansen Caires.

Rats! Sing! Sing! (2005, GSL) was the final LP, mixed by Carlos De La Garza. The band played their last show May 22, 2006 at The Casbah in San Diego.

Labels

Gold Standard Laboratories (GSL)

Gold Standard Laboratories — Discogs ID 12131. Founded 1993 in Boulder, CO by Sonny Kay; relocated to San Diego 2001, then LA; closed October 29, 2007. Co-owned from 2001 by Omar Rodríguez-López of The Mars Volta. GSL was the defining label of the San Diego avant-punk underground; other acts included The Locust, !!!, The VSS, Arab On Radar.

GGGAH appeared on GSL from 2000 (Out Every Window, GSL31) through Rats! Sing! Sing! (2005).

Vinyl Communications

Vinyl Communications (Discogs label ID: 637) — San Diego indie label. Released the debut CD Gogogoairheart (VC 122, 1997).

Ideal Weight

Ideal Weight (Discogs label ID: 122380) — small San Diego imprint. Released Love My Life…Hate My Friends (IDL001, 1998) and co-released Out Every Window (iw005, 2000) with GSL.

Scene Context

GoGoGo Airheart emerged from the same San Diego underground that produced The Locust, Drive Like Jehu, Rocket From The Crypt, and Tristeza. Several members had overlapping affiliations: Jimmy LaValle played in The Locust, Swing Kids (2), The Crimson Curse, and Tristeza, all while maintaining The Album Leaf as a solo ambient project. John Reis (Rocket From The Crypt, Drive Like Jehu, Hot Snakes, Swami Records) recorded demos with the band in 2004 — reflecting the tight interconnection of the San Diego scene. Sonny Kay was simultaneously running GSL, fronting The VSS, and later Year Future.

The 1996 split with Carpet links the band to the New York scene: Luke Jenner, later of The Rapture, was in Carpet at this point.
