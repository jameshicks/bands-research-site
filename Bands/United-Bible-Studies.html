Overview

United Bible Studies is an Irish experimental and improvisational folk ensemble that has been recording since 2003. Built around a core of Alison O’Donnell, David Colohan, Dominic Cooper, Graeme Lockett, and Ivan Pawle, the group has maintained a steady output across three decades, with four releases each in the 2000s and 2010s and continued activity into the 2020s. Their early work appeared primarily on Deserted Village, while later releases have spread across a succession of smaller imprints — Deep Water Acres, Golden Pavilion Records, Pariah Child, and Yoshiwara Collective. Vocalist and multi-instrumentalist Áine O’Dwyer and Gavin Prior are by far their most frequent collaborators, each appearing on more than ten shared releases, reflecting a close-knit Irish experimental folk community centred around the group.
