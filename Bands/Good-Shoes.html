Overview

Good Shoes are an English indie-rock band from Morden, South London, active from 2005. Their catalogue leans toward indie rock and Britpop, with the bulk of their output — eight releases — concentrated in the 2000s and a further three in the 2010s. They were primarily signed to Brille Records, which issued eleven of their works between 2005 and 2010, having briefly debuted on Young And Lost Club. Their most frequent collaborators were Swedish producers Per Sunding and Tore Johansson, who together share twenty-five credits with the band — a notable Scandinavian connection for a South London act.

Notes (web) — Per Sunding / Tore Johansson production partnership

Good Shoes travelled to Malmö, Sweden to record their debut album Think Before You Speak (Brille Records, 26 March 2007) with Per Sunding at Tambourine Studios. The album was recorded in nineteen days. Per Sunding (also known for work with The Cardigans and The Concretes) produced the record; Tore Johansson served as mixer on the album and on earlier singles. The two had co-produced at least one pre-album single together: on “The Photos On My Wall” (2006, Brille Records), Sunding is credited as producer and recording engineer with Johansson as co-producer and mixer; on the “Morden” single (2007, Brille Records), Sunding recorded and produced while Johansson handled mixing. record-producers.com lists “The Photos On My Wall” explicitly as a Johansson co-production/mix credit.

The route to Tambourine Studios runs through the studio’s own history. Sunding co-founded Tambourine Studios in Malmö in 1991 as a member of the band Eggstone, with Johansson (who had produced Eggstone’s 1991 EP at a state-funded studio) providing initial training before the team learned hands-on. Once Eggstone’s 1992 album raised the studio’s profile, Tambourine began attracting international acts. Wikipedia’s Tambourine Studios article (as of 2026-10-30) names Good Shoes alongside Saint Etienne and Tom Jones as international artists who recorded there. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tambourine_Studios

The Dummy Mag feature on the band (dmy.co, pre-2007) confirms: “The band travelled even farther afield to record their forthcoming debut album, Think Before You Speak, flying to Sweden to work with producer Per Sunding (The Cardigans, The Concretes).” The same piece notes the band flew to Sweden “so many times” for the sessions. https://dmy.co/news/good-shoes-the-good-stuff/

Which albums/recordings the partnership covered: On the basis of Discogs credits (13 shared with Sunding, 12 with Johansson) and the sources above, the partnership spans the 2006 pre-album singles period through at least Think Before You Speak (2007). Wikipedia’s article on the second album No Hope, No Future (2010) lists no producer credits and no Swedish recording location; Discogs credits for that album do not prominently feature Sunding or Johansson in the same count, though the overall shared-credit totals suggest some continued involvement across the later singles. No interview or press source found explicitly states whether they returned to Tambourine for the second album — this remains unconfirmed.

How the partnership came about: No interview source found spells out who made the initial introduction (e.g. Brille Records A&R, an agent, or a referral from another band). The most likely route is Brille Records or a UK indie network connecting Good Shoes with Tambourine Studios on the strength of Sunding’s work with The Cardigans and The Concretes — but this is inference, not a cited statement.
