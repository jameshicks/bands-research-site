The B-52’s

New wave / party rock band formed in Athens, Georgia, 1976. Discogs artist ID: 242415.

Debut LP (1979, Island Records) photographed by George DuBose — cover became iconic. Managed by Gary Kurfirst; debut produced by Chris Blackwell. Members included Fred Schneider, Kate Pierson, Cindy Wilson, Ricky Wilson, Keith Strickland.
