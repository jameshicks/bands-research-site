Screamers

Discogs artist ID: 65390 Origin: Los Angeles, California Active: 1977–1981 (approximately)

Overview

The Screamers were a Los Angeles punk band notable for performing and recording without guitars — their sound was built around synthesizers, drums, and vocals. They are one of the few significant LA punk acts to never officially release a studio album during their active years; their reputation rested entirely on live performances and bootleg/demo recordings. K. K. Barrett was the drummer.
