The Coral

Discogs artist ID: 138082

Indie rock / psychedelic band from West Kirby, Merseyside, UK. Formed late 1990s. Signed to Deltasonic Records for their first five albums (2002–2007), later moved to Ignition Records (2) and Modern Sky UK / Run on Records.

Bill Ryder-Jones — Departure Details

The 2005 illness and the 2008 permanent departure were both driven by escalating mental health problems rather than creative differences or a desire for solo work. The sequence, drawn from multiple interviews Ryder-Jones has given across 2023–2024:

  • 2005 temporary withdrawal: Ryder-Jones stopped touring due to a stress-related illness — Wikipedia’s characterisation. Interviews clarify this as the onset of severe agoraphobia and panic attacks that began around age 21. The immediate trigger for the temporary break was reportedly an eight-hour panic attack during recording sessions in South Wales. The Quietus, 2024
  • Underlying causes: He has cited a traumatic childhood — specifically the death of his older brother Daniel (aged nine, cliff fall, family holiday in Wales, 1991) which left him with PTSD — combined with heavy drug use during the band’s early years as the root causes. Big Issue, 2024
  • 2008 permanent departure: He rejoined the band after 2005 but “it made me ill and had to give that up again.” In his own words (All Things Loud interview): “I had a nervous breakdown when I was 21. It’s as simple as that, really.” The formal announcement came 9 January 2008. All Things Loud
  • Aftermath: Post-departure, his condition worsened to the point where he shared a bed with his mother for almost a year. He has described being “very agoraphobic” with an undiagnosed dissociative disorder. He attempted university but could not manage it. Recovery only began in his late twenties. Domino Records founder Laurence Bell signing him after discovering solo recordings on MySpace is credited with reversing “a terrifying downwards spiral.” Big Issue, 2024
  • Not creative differences: No interview source cites disagreements with band members or a drive toward solo work as causes. The departure was health-driven.
