Lean Left

Discogs ID: 1821754
Genre: Free Improvisation / Free Jazz
Active: 2010–present
Country: Netherlands / Norway / US

Overview

Lean Left is a transatlantic free-improvisation quartet formed around 2010, pairing the two guitarists of Dutch post-punk and experimental band The Ex — Terrie Ex (Terrie Hessels) and Andy Moor (2) — with Chicago reedist Ken Vandermark and Norwegian drummer Paal Nilssen-Love. The name derives from the debut release’s track listing and the band’s connection to the Ken Vandermark / Paal Nilssen-Love duo (who had already recorded Milwaukee Volume together in 2009 on Smalltown Superjazzz), with Vandermark and Nilssen-Love representing the “duo” half that the “Ex Guitars” meet on that first disc.

The group sits at the intersection of several scenes: The Ex’s anarcho-punk-to-free-improv trajectory; the Chicago-to-Nordic axis that Vandermark’s network defines; and Nilssen-Love’s Nordic and international free jazz circuit. All four members self-produce, and the records document live or studio improvisations rather than composed pieces. The Café Oto sessions (2012, Kollaps) are notable for featuring guest improvisers — Ab Baars on Day One, Steve Noble on Day Two — expanding the lineup to a temporary quintet.

Post-punk rhythmic intensity, noise-rock guitar overload, and extended saxophone/clarinet vocabulary coexist in the group’s sound. Among Lean Left’s non-obvious credits: Lasse Marhaug mixed and co-produced Medemer (2020, PNL), and comedian/author Stewart Lee contributed sleeve notes to Live At Café Oto (Unsounds, 2012).

Connections

  • The Ex — Terrie Ex and Andy Moor are core members; Lean Left is the improv offshoot of The Ex’s international network
  • Ken Vandermark — Chicago reedist; the Vandermark / Nilssen-Love duo predates Lean Left (Milwaukee Volume, 2009, Smalltown Superjazzz)
  • Paal Nilssen-Love — Norwegian drummer; also in The Thing (2), Boneshaker (3), Paal Nilssen-Love Large Unit
  • Terrie Ex — The Ex guitarist since 1979; also runs Terp Records
  • Andy Moor (2) — The Ex guitarist since 1990; founding member Dog Faced Hermans; Unsounds label
  • Smalltown Superjazzz — released first two discs; sublabel of Smalltown Supersound
  • Unsounds — Amsterdam label; released Live At Café Oto (CD) and Live At Area Sismica; Andy Moor is closely associated
  • Kollaps — Munich-based label; released the two Café Oto LPs with Ab Baars and Steve Noble guests
  • Trost Records — Vienna; released I Forgot To Breathe (2017 LP)
  • PNL — Paal Nilssen-Love’s self-run label; released Medemer (2020)
  • Ab Baars — Dutch saxophonist / ICP Orchestra member; guest on Café Oto Day One; also in Double Tandem with Terrie Ex, Andy Moor, Ken Vandermark, Paal Nilssen-Love
  • Steve Noble — London drummer; guest on Café Oto Day Two
  • Colin McLean — sound engineer; recorded and mastered Volumes 1 and 2; mix advisor on Live At Area Sismica; also credited on Live At Café Oto (CD)
  • Lasse Marhaug — Norwegian noise artist and producer; mixed/mastered Medemer (2020)
  • John Chantler — recorded both Café Oto sessions (Day One CD and the Unsounds CD)
  • Stewart Lee — comedian / author; sleeve notes on Live At Café Oto (Unsounds CD)
  • Double Tandem — related group with overlapping personnel (Ab Baars, Vandermark, Nilssen-Love, Terrie Ex)
  • The Ex Guitars — guitar sub-unit of The Ex (Terrie Ex, Andy Moor, Arnold de Boer)
  • Joakim Haugland — co-producer on Volumes 1 and 2; associated with Smalltown Superjazzz
