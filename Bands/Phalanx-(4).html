Phalanx (4)

American free jazz / jazz-funk ensemble. Discogs artist ID: 915919. Formed around the pairing of guitarist James Blood Ulmer and tenor saxophonist George Adams, with two distinct lineups across their studio run (1986–1988) and a live reunion document (2013).

Overview

Phalanx formed in the mid-1980s as a vehicle for the Ulmer–Adams collaboration, bringing together two figures whose backgrounds overlapped at the intersection of free jazz and blues-rooted groove. Ulmer’s harmolodic guitar approach — learned directly from Ornette Coleman — anchored the group’s rhythmic logic, while Adams contributed a blues-drenched, emotionally extroverted tenor sound honed through years with Charles Mingus, Gil Evans, and the George Adams–Don Pullen Quartet.

The band had two distinct lineups. The debut album Got Something Good For You (1986, Moers Music) featured the funk-oriented rhythm section of Amin Ali (electric bass) and Calvin Weston (drums) — both long-running Ulmer associates from his earlier Rough Trade and CBS recordings. The subsequent DIW recordings (Original Phalanx, 1987; In Touch, 1988) replaced that section with Rashied Ali (drums) and Norris Jones / Sirone (double bass), shifting the character toward more open avant-garde terrain. The DIW sessions were produced by Kazunori Sugiyama and recorded in Japan.

A live album, Phalanx Live (2013, American Revelation Music), documents a later reunion of the original (Amin Ali / Calvin Weston) lineup, captured for Radio Bremen and edited by Michael Mustafa Ulmer.

Members — Extended Profiles

  • James Blood Ulmer (Discogs ID: 149965) — harmolodic guitarist, vocalist, composer. Trained under Ornette Coleman. Also led Music Revelation Ensemble and numerous solo albums on Rough Trade, Columbia, DIW, and In+Out Records.
  • George Adams (Discogs ID: 336123) — tenor saxophonist, flutist, vocalist. George Rufus Adams, 1940–1992. Longtime member of the Charles Mingus band; later co-led the George Adams - Don Pullen Quartet with pianist Don Pullen. Also worked with Gil Evans, the Fatback Band, and Mingus Dynasty.
  • Amin Ali (Discogs ID: 353840) — electric bassist. Core Ulmer sideman from 1980 onward (Are You Glad To Be In America?, Free Lancing, Black Rock on Rough Trade/CBS). Also credited as associate producer on Black Rock (1982).
  • Calvin Weston (Discogs ID: 353841) — drummer. Also a core Ulmer sideman from 1980 (Are You Glad To Be In America? through Black Rock). Later played with Ornette Coleman’s Prime Time (Of Human Feelings, 1982) and with Lounge Lizards (1991–1998). Led own session Mirakle (2000, Tzadik).
  • Norris Jones / Sirone (Discogs IDs: 417149 / 297037) — bassist. Co-founder of The Revolutionary Ensemble; member of Cecil Taylor Unit (1978–80). Performed as “Sirone” from the early 1970s.
  • Rashied Ali (Discogs ID: 254546) — drummer. Coltrane’s drummer during the final period (1965–67). Later led Prima Materia and the Rashied Ali Quintet on his own Survival Records label.
