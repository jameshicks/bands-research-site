Mantronix

Discogs ID: 3165

New York hip-hop/electronic group formed around producer Kurtis Mantronik (Kurtis el Khaleel), active from the mid-1980s.

Their 1989 single Got To Have Your Love featuring vocalist Wondress Hutchinson reached the UK Top 10, as did the follow-up Take Your Time in 1990, both on Capitol Records.

Members include Bryce Wilson, Kurtis Khaleel, and others across different lineup periods.

Key Engineer

Andy Heermans is credited as engineer and session player (bass, guitar) on The Incredible Sound Machine (1991, Capitol Records), and as remix/mix engineer on “Got To Have Your Love” (1989) and “Take Your Time” (1990). He worked across the full Capitol Records period of the group.
