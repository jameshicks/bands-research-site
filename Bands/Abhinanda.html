Overview

Abhinanda were a straight-edge hardcore band from Umeå, Sweden, whose active run stretched from 1992 to 1998, with the bulk of their catalogue — five releases — landing on Desperate Fight Records during the 1990s alongside appearances on Genet and Locomotive Music. The band is embedded in the dense Umeå hardcore scene through shared membership with Jonas Lyxzén and recurring production credits from Pelle Henricsson and André Jacobsson, engineers central to that scene’s sound. After a 2004 hometown reunion and a 2010 Stockholm show and Japan tour (tied to a Japanese compilation), they re-formed in 2022 with a new drummer, Jejo Perkovic, and have played sporadically since. Two further releases appeared in the 2020s, suggesting a low-key return to activity.
