The Vanishing Voice

Discogs ID: 362883
Active: 2003–2013 (per Discogs years active)
Base: New York / US underground
Genres: Experimental folk, psych-rock, avant-folk, free improv
Also known as: Vanishing (alias), Vanishing Voice, Voice

The Vanishing Voice is a collective/ensemble drawn from the New York psychedelic and experimental underground. They are a distinct band — not a Wooden Wand alias — though Wooden Wand (James Jackson Toth) is their most consistent collaborator, appearing on the majority of their releases. The group has an open, rotating membership model, with a core of four members and a rotating cast of additional performers.

No profile text exists on Discogs. No connection to Tzadik Records or the downtown avant-garde scene (John Zorn circle) found in the data.

Key Release: XIAO (2004)

The largest documented ensemble recording. Released on De Stijl. Liner notes by Thurston Moore. Performers credited:

  • G. Lucas Crane (“Glucas ‘Nonhorse’ Crane”)
  • Heidi Diehl (“Heidi ‘H Reality’ Diehl”)
  • Jarvis Taveniere (“Steven Taveniere”)
  • Satja Sai Baba / Jessica Toth (“Satya Sai”)
  • Jeffrey Lewis (“Jeff Lightning Lewis”)
  • Adira Amram
  • DM Seidel
  • Gabriel Walsh (2)
  • Tovah O’Rourke
  • Ursula Damm
  • Wooden Wand (James Jackson Toth)

Tracks include “Paper Trail Blues,” “Caribou Christ In The Great Void,” “Weird Wisteria Tangles Carrion Christ But Intends No Harm,” “Lions In Love.”

Key Release: Stone Tablet (2007)

The only release solely credited to The Vanishing Voice (no Wooden Wand co-credit). Released on Important Records (imprec142, limited edition LP). Tighter four-piece lineup: G. Lucas Crane, Heidi Diehl, Jarvis Taveniere, Pete Nolan. Styles tagged as Krautrock and Psychedelic Rock, suggesting the group moved toward a more focused sound on this record. Tracks: “Stone Tablet,” “Oblisk,” “Peace Symbols,” “Last Evil.”
