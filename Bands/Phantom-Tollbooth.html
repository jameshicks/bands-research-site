Phantom Tollbooth

Discogs artist ID: 103771

American underground rock band, New York City. Active from 1985 through at least 2003. Core lineup: Dave Rick (guitar, vocals), Gerard Smith (2) (bass, vocals), Jon Coats (drums). Released on Homestead Records and Off Records.

Overview

Phantom Tollbooth were part of the NYC indie/underground scene of the mid-to-late 1980s. Their records were produced with strong DIY sensibility — the 1986 EP and debut were self-produced (with Chris Xefos); One-Way Conversation (1987) was produced with Martin Bisi at BC Studio; Power Toy (1988) was produced by Spot (the SST house producer); and Daylight in the Quiet Zone (1990) was self-produced by Jon Coats. A reunion LP, Beard of Lightning (2003), appeared on Off Records with Daniel Rey mixing and Robert Pollard (Guided by Voices) as a guest vocalist.
