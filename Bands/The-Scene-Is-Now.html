Oct 18, 20266 min read

The Scene Is Now

Discogs artist ID: 994693
Also known as: TSIN, Scene Is Now
New York City post-punk / avant-garde band. Active c. 1984–2020. Direct successor to Information.

Overview

The Scene Is Now formed in New York City in 1984 from the core of Information, the no-wave-adjacent band built around the NO Magazine circle at NYU. The link is direct: Information members Chris Nelson (5) and Philip Dray carried over into TSIN from the start, while Jeff McGovern — who had appeared on Mofungo tracks on the shared Tape #1 cassette (1980) alongside those same players — joined as a founding member.

The band’s description on Discogs, “New York City-based avant-garde jug band from the 1980s,” captures the irreverent self-definition that runs through their catalog. Their 1980s releases on Lost Records (9) — a small NYC imprint at 361 Canal Street, later 346 E 13th St — run from the debut 7” 1150 Lbs. / Treaty Stone (1984) through three LPs culminating in Tonight We Ride (1988). The label was a parent of Precision Records (5) and also put out Mofungo, making it the physical hub of the overlapping TSIN/Mofungo circle. The four 1980s releases produced by the band themselves (with outside production assistance from Elliott Sharp on part of Total Jive) were engineered variably by McGovern, Robert Sietsema, Carol Parkinson, and Martin Bisi.

After Tonight We Ride (1988), the lineup shifted. McGovern does not appear on the band’s subsequent records. The 2004 reunion album Songbirds Lie (Tongue Master Records) brought a reconstituted lineup: Sue Garner on bass, Robert Dennis (2) on drums, Greg Peterson (3) and Steven Levi as additional performers, alongside returning members Dray and Chris Nelson (4). The 2011 follow-up Magpie Alarm (Tongue Master Records) credits Sue Garner as engineer and co-mixer as well as performer, and introduces Cheryl Kingan in a guest vocal role. Both later albums were released on a UK label, Tongue Master Records, which champions American and British independent music in small editions.

A 1995 Bar/None Records compilation, The Oily Years (1983–1993), gathered 27 tracks spanning the full 1980s–early 1990s run, with Tony Maimone, Will Rigby, Frank Slattery, Lianne Smith, and others appearing across the later unreleased tracks alongside the core members.

The Discogs name variations “Scene Is Now” and “TSIN” are both used in credits; the Bandcamp URL confirms continued online presence into the 2010s.
