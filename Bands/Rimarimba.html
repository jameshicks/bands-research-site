Project of Robert Cox, a UK composer and multi-instrumentalist. Rimarimba began as a trio with Davy Walklett and Lenny Bove, releasing two limited-edition vinyl LPs on Cordelia Records (Leicester) in 1985. A third LP came out in 1987 on Terry Burrows’ Hamster Records And Tapes. After a long hiatus, Cox resumed releasing under the Rimarimba name in the 2010s and 2019, including a major archival box-set on the US label Freedom To Spend and two new releases.

Overview

Rimarimba emerged from the mid-1980s UK home-recording underground. Its two Cordelia LPs — On Dry Land (Ericat 005) and Below The Horizon (Ericat 008) — were limited-edition pressings within the ERICAT vinyl series run by Alan Jenkins of The Deep Freeze Mice, which simultaneously documented the Leicester DIY scene and Jenkins’ connections to the broader cassette underground. The Discogs genre/style tags across both LPs are Electronic / Experimental, Minimal.

On Dry Land is an 17-track LP (11 tracks side A, 6 side B) with no track durations logged in Discogs. Below The Horizon runs to six titled tracks on Side A plus a single 21-minute side-long piece, “Bebag,” on Side B. A third LP, In The Woods (1985, Unlikely Records), was released the same year as the Cordelia titles; Chicago Death Excretion Geometry (1987, Hamster Records And Tapes) followed two years later, co-produced by Terry Burrows (Yukio Yung / Jung Analysts).

Cox revived the project decades later. The US label Freedom To Spend issued The Rimarimba Collection (2018), a 4-LP/8-LP box compiling the three 1985 LPs, an unreleased 1985 cassette (Light Metabolism Number Prague), and additional material — with liner notes by Jon Dale and Cox, mastering by Josh Bonati. Two additional releases followed in 2019: the double cassette Beware Of Falling Bicycles (Unlikely Records, URT120) — a Robert Cox solo recording across two cassettes — and Sea, Shore, See, Sure?, a 62-minute single-piece file release on the Australian label Longform Editions.

Lenny Bove is also a member of Fifty Foot Hose and Tripod Jimmie.

Scene Context

Rimarimba’s two Cordelia LPs sit squarely within the mid-1980s UK home-recording scene documented by the ERICAT series. The Neuron Music 12” (Ericat 016, 1986) places Rimarimba alongside The Deep Freeze Mice, Mr. Concept, and Jung Analysts — a capsule of the Cordelia circle. The connection to Terry Burrows (who also ran Hamster Records And Tapes and later co-founded The Chrysanthemums with Jenkins) placed Rimarimba at the crossroads of the Leicester and broader UK experimental cassette networks.

The 2018 Freedom To Spend reissue brought Rimarimba renewed international attention, framing the 1985 recordings within the context of sound art and minimal electronics reissue culture.
