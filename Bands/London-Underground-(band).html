London Underground (band)

UK post-punk / dub / funk group, 1980–1983. Discogs artist ID: 132714. One of the earliest bands associated with Adrian Sherwood’s On-U Sound label, releasing two 7” singles and a 10” on the label before moving to a full-length LP in 1983. Name variations on Discogs: “London U.”, “London. U”.

Overview

London Underground formed around 1980 and were among the first acts signed to On-U Sound — appearing on the label’s inaugural single alongside New Age Steppers in 1980. Their sound sits at the intersection of post-punk, dub, and experimental funk, with a fluid lineup centered on a core of four members: Martin Frederix (guitar, bass, vocals; also credited as Martin Harrison), Pete Holdsworth (vocals, melodica), Dan Sheals (drums), and Kendall Ernest (bass). Doctor Pablo (Peter Stroud) played keyboards, melodica, and guitar across all the later releases. Kishi Yamamoto handled sleeve design and played Chinese lute on the LP.

The band released a one-sided debut single in 1980 (New Age Steppers on the A-side), then a Situation Two single in 1981, followed by a 10” EP and two 1983 releases on On-U Sound — the LP At Home With The London Underground and the mini-album Current Affairs Session — before ceasing activity. Adrian Sherwood produced all releases; from the 1983 material onward, Frederix co-produced several tracks. The 1989 CD Compilation 80–84 gathered the LP and mini-album tracks.

The track “Dreams Are Better” appears on the 2021 compilation Shake The Foundations (Militant Funk & The Post-Punk Dancefloor 1978-1984) (Disc 3). That title does not appear in Discogs’ track listings for the band’s own releases, suggesting it was either an unreleased or non-Discogs-catalogued recording.

Release Detail

Fade Away / Learn A Language (1980, On-U Sound)

A split 7” that was On-U Sound’s very first release (catalogue number ON-Usound 1.). The A-side “Fade Away” belongs to New Age Steppers and is a cover of a Junior Byles composition. The AA-side “Learn A Language” is London Underground’s debut recording. Produced by Adrian Sherwood (credited as “A. Sheerwood”). Engineered uncredited by John Walker (2). Mastering by Eddy Gorecki.

Train Of Thought / All Too Many (1981, Situation Two)

The only release on the Beggars Banquet subsidiary Situation Two (SIT 9). Kishi Yamamoto did sleeve design and photography. All writing credited to London Underground.

Watching West Indians In The Cold (1982, On-U Sound)

10” EP with three tracks: “Strange Things” (4:07), “Conspiracy” (4:00), “Why Do Fat Men Have Such Skinny Thoughts?” (4:23). Produced by Adrian Sherwood. Sleeve by Kishi Yamamoto.

Between The Lines / Rise And Fall (1983, On-U Sound)

7” produced jointly by Sherwood and the band. Core lineup on this single: Kendall Ernest (bass), Martin Frederix (bass, guitar, keyboards), Dan Sheals (drums), Doctor Pablo (keyboards, guitar), Pete Holdsworth (vocals, lyrics). Mastered by Kevin Metcalfe.

At Home With The London Underground (1983, On-U Sound — ON-U LP 22)

8-track LP. Producer credits split: Sherwood handles A1, B1, B4; Frederix handles A2–A4, B2–B3. Band lineup: Martin Frederix (guitar, bass, vocals), Dan Sheals (drums, whistle, guitar), Doctor Pablo (melodica, piano, guitar), Pete Holdsworth (vocals, melodica), Kip (bass, guitar), Kishi Yamamoto (sleeve, Chinese lute on “Moving Mountains”). Guest credits include Chris (saxophone on “Great Expectations”), Roland (110) (saxophone on “You Don’t Know”), Helen (52), Jude (44), Sandie (6) (backing vocals on “Great Expectations”, “Watch Your Step”), and Bob Andy (written-by credit on “You Don’t Know”). Mastered by Kevin Metcalfe.

Current Affairs Session (1983, On-U Sound — On-U LP 28 m)

Mini-album with 7 tracks (one untitled bass section, then A1–A3 / B1–B3). Full lineup: Kendall Ernest (bass, guitar), Martin Frederix (bass, guitar, keyboards), Dan Sheals (drums, whistle, percussion), Doctor Pablo (keyboards, guitar, melodica), Pete Holdsworth (vocals). Produced by Sherwood and the band. Mastered by Kevin Metcalfe.

Compilation 80–84 (1989, On-U Sound — ON-U CD7)

CD compilation drawing all 14 tracks from At Home With The London Underground and Current Affairs Session. Reiterates the same personnel as the two 1983 LPs. Mastered by Kevin Metcalfe.
