Blanck Mass

Discogs ID: 2307335 Active: 2011–present Member: Benjamin John Power Labels: Rock Action Records (debut 2011), Sacred Bones (2015–2022), Weirding Way Records (2021–), Invada Records (2023–), Lakeshore Records (2026–), Mexican Summer (single contribution, ca. 2020)

Solo project of Benjamin Power, one half of Fuck Buttons. Self-titled debut released on Rock Action Records in 2011 (ROCKACT58LP). Electronic / noise / industrial-influenced music. Sacred Bones output: Dumb Flesh (SBR-136, 2015, 10 pressings), World Eater (SBR-174, 2017, 12 pressings), Animated Violence Mild (SBR-220, 2019, 12 pressings), In Ferneaux (SBR-267, 2021, 8 pressings), Ted K (SBR-286, 2022, 11 pressings) — score for the film Ted K. 83 Discogs releases on Sacred Bones through 2022.

Post-Sacred Bones, Blanck Mass has shifted increasingly toward film and TV scoring work. Invada Records (Geoff Barrow’s Bristol label) has been the primary outlet for soundtrack releases since 2023. Lakeshore Records co-released the Crime 101 score in 2026. Weirding Way Records released Mind Killer (2021). Mexican Summer lists Blanck Mass as a roster artist, likely from a single contribution to their Looking Glass digital singles series (ca. 2020). No post-Sacred Bones non-soundtrack studio album confirmed as of August 2026.
