Sham 69 are an English punk band that formed in Hersham in 1975.

Although not as commercially successful as many of their contemporaries, albeit with a greater number of chart entries, Sham 69 has been a huge musical and lyrical influence on the Oi! and streetpunk genres. The band allegedly derived their name from a piece of football-related graffiti that founder Jimmy Pursey saw on a wall, which originally said Walton and Hersham ‘69 but had partly faded away.

Overview

Sham 69 are an English punk band from Hersham, Surrey, formed in 1975 by Jimmy Pursey — the name reportedly taken from football graffiti that originally read “Walton and Hersham ‘69” before part of it faded away. Though they fell short of the commercial heights of some punk contemporaries, the band racked up a substantial run of chart entries and became a foundational influence on the Oi! and street-punk movements. Their catalogue is heaviest in the 1970s and 1980s, with Polydor as the primary original label; since the 1990s a succession of reissue imprints — Dojo, Castle Music, Captain Oi! — has kept the back catalogue continuously in print through to the mid-2020s.
