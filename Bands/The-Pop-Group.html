The Pop Group

Post-punk / funk / dub band. Bristol, UK. Discogs ID: 33932. Formed 1977, disbanded 1980. Reformed 2010.

One of the defining British post-punk acts — combined dub, free jazz, funk, and no wave noise with explicitly political lyrics. Managed by Disc O’Dell and released on his Y Records alongside Radar Records and Rough Trade. Both Mark Stewart and John Waddington died in 2023, ending the band’s second phase.

Formation

Formed in Bristol in 1977 around vocalist Mark Stewart. Original core: Gareth Sager (guitar, sax, keys), Simon Underwood (bass), Bruce Smith (drums), John Waddington (guitar). Dan Catsis (bass, ex-Glaxo Babies) joined later and played on the second album.
