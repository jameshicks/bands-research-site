Assorted Images

UK graphic design company, based in London. Discogs artist ID: 1585695. Founded in 1977 by Malcolm Garrett, who served as its design director until 1994. On Discogs the collective is also registered as a company label (label ID: 148862), with a sublabel “Ai (2)“.

Overview

Assorted Images is the design collective established by Malcolm Garrett in 1977 — his Discogs profile dates its formal founding to 1983, but the collective’s first credited work appears from 1977 onward (the Buzzcocks single “Orgasm Addict”). The name was a deliberate typographic conceit: credits almost always appeared in a stylised form as “Assorted iMaGes” (with a capitalized M, G and alternating mixed case), and the collective routinely used one-off variant names — almost always an adjective beginning with “A” followed by “Images” — as specific-job aliases. Discogs lists over 60 such name variations, from “Angular Images” to “Auto iMaGes” to “StOLeN iMaGeS, Copenhagen.” The company could also be credited as “Ai” or ”@.”

Assorted Images defined the look of British post-punk and new wave from the late 1970s through the mid-1980s. Their work for Buzzcocks on the United Artists label — particularly the Love Bites and A Different Kind of Tension album sleeves — established a dense, typographically adventurous approach that set the template for the era. The collective went on to become the primary design house for Simple Minds, Duran Duran, Culture Club, and dozens of other acts on Virgin, EMI and allied labels through the early-to-mid 1980s. Their involvement with Virgin Records was especially extensive, spanning the label’s peak commercial period from around 1981 to 1989.

The Discogs profile notes the company was “based in Central London.” Malcolm Garrett’s own profile describes it as begun “in 1983” — this appears to refer to a formal incorporation or relaunch rather than the start of credited work, which began in 1977. He left Assorted Images in 1994 and subsequently co-founded AMX Digital, later renamed, before becoming Creative Director at Images (a separate venture) and Artistic Director at Design Manchester.

Buzzcocks Factual Note

The Discogs record does not credit Assorted Images on Another Music In A Different Kitchen (1978). That sleeve is credited individually to Malcolm Garrett. The Assorted Images credit on Buzzcocks begins with the “Orgasm Addict” single (1977) and runs through the UA singles and albums.

Connections

  • Malcolm Garrett — founder and design director, 1977–1994; the creative engine of the collective
  • Buzzcocks — earliest and most historically significant client; established the Assorted Images typographic aesthetic
  • Simple Minds — longest-running major client (1981–2012 in Discogs); entire Virgin-era visual identity
  • Duran Duran — major client 1981–1984; debut album through Seven And The Ragged Tiger
  • Culture Club — primary design house for peak commercial period
  • Honey Bane — Zonophone singles designed by collective
  • Radar Records — extensive work on Radar’s Manchester-era post-punk catalog
  • United Artists Records — Buzzcocks UA period

A Note on Barney Bubbles

There is a conflation risk in the vault: Barney Bubbles (Colin Fulcher) also used “Assorted Images” as a Discogs alias on some credits — particularly Radar Records work. Bubbles and the Malcolm Garrett collective are separate entities. Bubbles died in 1983; his work for Radar predates the Garrett collective’s Radar credits and reflects his own independent use of the name. When Discogs credits “Assorted Images” on Radar-label releases, attribution should be checked against the individual release to determine whether Bubbles or Garrett’s collective is the correct party.
