Television Personalities

Discogs ID: 83074
Origin: London, UK
Formed: 1977/1978
Aliases: Teen ‘78 (ID 1241445), The Gifted Children (ID 549392)
Genres: Post-Punk, Indie Rock, Lo-Fi, Mod, Psychedelic

English post-punk band formed in London by Daniel Treacy (born Daniel Joseph Treacy). One of the most persistently DIY and eclectic British acts of the indie era — their output ranges from cheap, clattering three-chord punk-pop to sprawling psychedelia, drone, and neo-psychedelia, with only Treacy’s songwriting as a constant through dozens of lineup changes. Early records on Kings Road and Rough Trade established the template; a long partnership with Fire Records from 1989 continued through to 2025.

Key early associates: Edward Ball (Whaam! Records co-founder, later The Times, Biff Bang Pow!), Joe Foster (later co-founder of Creation Records), and later Jowe Head (bass/multi-instrumentalist, 50+ shared releases) and producer Phil Vinall (55+ shared releases, key from Privilege onward).

Discography by Era

Origins and Early Singles (1978–1981)

“Where’s Bill Grundy Now?” EP (1978, Kings Road) — Debut self-release, four tracks including “Part-Time Punks.” White label pressing. Mastered by John Martin (13) (uncredited); plated by Maxwell Anandappa. The title song and “Part-Time Punks” became defining TVP statements — a mocking, affectionate take on punk as fashion.

“Smashing Time!” (1980, Rough Trade) — Rough Trade single.

“I Know Where Syd Barrett Lives / Arthur The Gardener” (1981, Rough Trade) — another Rough Trade single flagging the psychedelic/cult reference obsessions that would run throughout TVP.

…And Don’t The Kids Just Love It (1981, Rough Trade ROUGH 24) — debut LP. Lineup: Daniel Treacy (vocals), Edward Ball (guitar/bass), Mark Sheppard.

Whaam! Records Period (1982–1983)

“Mummy Your Not Watching Me” (1982, Whaam! Records) / “They Could Have Been Bigger Than The Beatles” (1982, Whaam!) — released on Edward Ball’s co-founded label Whaam! Records.

“Biff, Bang, Pow! / A Picture Of Dorian Gray” (1983, Creation Artifact) — flexi-disc on Creation Artifact, the early Creation Records imprint run by Alan McGee and Edward Ball. “Biff, Bang, Pow!” is a Creation original (written by Eddie Phillips and Kenny Pickett of The Creation); “A Picture Of Dorian Gray” written by Treacy. Establishes direct TVP–Creation link — Edward Ball was McGee’s best friend, and Biff Bang Pow! (a Creation band) included both McGee and Ball.

The Painted Word (1984)

The Painted Word (1984, Illuminated Records JAMS 37) — second LP. Lineup: Daniel Treacy, Joe Foster, Dave Musker, Mark Flunder; with Ingrid Weiss, David Walmsley, Martin Daley as additional performers. Produced by Treacy and Foster. Styles: Art Rock, Indie Rock, Lo-Fi, Psychedelic. 14 tracks.
