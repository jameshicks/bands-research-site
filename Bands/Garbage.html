Garbage

Alternative rock band. Discogs artist ID: 12332. Formed 1993–1994, Madison, Wisconsin, USA.

Overview

Garbage formed in Madison, Wisconsin when producers Butch Vig (drums, programming, loops, effects) and Steve Marker (guitar, keyboards, samples, loops) — co-founders of Smart Studios — and musician Duke Erikson (guitar, keyboards, bass, six-string bass) began experimenting with drum-machine and sample-heavy rock. The three had prior connections through Wisconsin bands Spooner (Vig and Erikson) and Fire Town (Vig and Erikson). Marker was a roadie for Fire Town.

The fourth member, Scottish vocalist Shirley Manson, was recruited after Marker spotted Angelfish — Manson’s then-band — on MTV’s 120 Minutes. After an initial failed audition in Madison, Manson returned for a second pass that produced skeletal versions of several songs. The band officially formed in 1994 and released their self-titled debut in 1995. All four members share songwriting and production credit across the entire catalog.

Garbage operated through Mushroom (UK, Europe, Australia) and Almo Sounds (US) for their first three albums, moved to Geffen Records for Bleed Like Me (2005), then launched their own Stun Volume imprint in 2012 for full creative control. Their 2021 album No Gods No Masters and 2025 album Let All That We Imagine Be The Light came out through Infectious Music / Stun Volume. The band hiatus from roughly 2005 to 2010 was followed by a sustained reunion that has continued to the present.

Discogs lists two aliases for the band: Angelfish (6) and Puff (17). These appear to be Discogs alias-link artifacts pointing to the pre-Garbage Angelfish lineup and an early working name; they are not separate significant recording entities.

Shirley Manson: Pre-Garbage

Goodbye Mr. Mackenzie

Goodbye Mr. Mackenzie — Scottish rock band, Discogs ID: 86995, formed Edinburgh 1981. Members included Martin Metcalfe (vocals/guitar), Rona Scobie (vocals/keyboards), Manson (vocals), Derek Kelly (drums), Big John Duncan (guitar), Fin Wilson (bass). Released on The Precious Organisation, Capitol Records (UK and US), Parlophone, Radioactive, and their own Blokshok label. Active ~1983–1995. Also issued material as Angelfish (2) (Discogs ID: 251965), Isa And The Filthy Tongues, and The Filthy Tongues.

Connections

  • Butch Vig — drummer, co-founder, co-producer; also one of the most prominent rock producers of the 1990s (Nirvana, Smashing Pumpkins, Sonic Youth, Foo Fighters, Green Day)
  • Shirley Manson — vocalist; formerly Goodbye Mr. Mackenzie / Angelfish (2)
  • Steve Marker — guitarist; co-founded Smart Studios with Vig
  • Duke Erikson — guitar, keyboards; formerly Spooner and Fire Town with Vig
  • Goodbye Mr. Mackenzie — Manson’s prior band (Discogs ID: 86995)
  • Angelfish (2) — Manson in a Goodbye Mr. Mackenzie offshoot that led to her Garbage recruitment
  • Fire Town — pre-Garbage band: Vig and Erikson (Discogs ID: 1459037); Atlantic, 1987–1989
  • Spooner — pre-Garbage band: Vig and Erikson (Discogs ID: 125625); BOAT Records / Mountain Railroad, 1979–1990
  • Smart Studios — recording studio founded by Vig and Marker; home of Garbage’s early recording
  • Billy Bush — primary engineer across Garbage’s catalog from Version 2.0 onward; also engineer/producer for Silversun Pickups, AFI, Against Me!, Green Day (extended)
  • Emily Lazar — mastering engineer on Bleed Like Me, Not Your Kind Of People, Strange Little Birds
  • Howie Weinberg — mastered Garbage (1995) and Version 2.0 (1998)
  • Scott Hull (2) — mastered Garbage (1995), Version 2.0 (1998), Beautifulgarbage (2001)
  • Dave Grohl — drummed on “Bad Boyfriend” (Bleed Like Me, 2005)
  • Justin Meldal-Johnsen — recurring session bassist (Beautifulgarbage, Not Your Kind Of People, Strange Little Birds, No Gods No Masters)
  • Eric Avery — bass sessions on Not Your Kind Of People, Strange Little Birds, No Gods No Masters
  • Matt Chamberlain — drums on select tracks of Beautifulgarbage and Not Your Kind Of People
  • Stéphane Sednaoui — band photographer on debut and Version 2.0
  • Heba Kadry — mastered No Gods No Masters (2021)
  • David Arnold — composer; co-produced “The World Is Not Enough” (1999 Bond theme) with Garbage
  • UNKLE — remixed “The World Is Not Enough” (1999); James Lavelle and Richard File credited
