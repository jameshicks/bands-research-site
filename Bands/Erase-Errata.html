Erase Errata

San Francisco post-punk / no-wave group, formed 1999. Discogs artist ID: 80777.

Overview

Erase Errata were a San Francisco-based quartet active from 1999 into the 2010s, operating in the intersection of no-wave, post-punk, and art-rock. They formed in San Francisco in 1999 and quickly became fixtures of the Bay Area underground, releasing their debut 7” on Inconvenient Press & Recordings in 2000. Their early releases placed them alongside Numbers, Black Dice, and others in a loose network of post-no-wave acts with ties to the Tigerbeat6 and Troubleman Unlimited scenes.

The classic lineup — Jenny Hoyston (vocals, trumpet, guitar/keyboards), Bianca Sparta (drums), Ellie Erickson (bass), Sara Jaffe (guitar) — recorded their debut LP Other Animals (2001, Troubleman Unlimited) and the live document At Crystal Palace (2003, Blast First / Mute). By Night Life (2006, Kill Rock Stars), the lineup had contracted to a trio (Hoyston, Sparta, Erickson), with Sara Jaffe departing. A decade-long dormancy followed before the band reconvened for Lost Weekend (2015, Under The Sun). They also released a split 7” with Sonic Youth in 2003 on Narnack Records, and participated in the Dancing Machine remix LP in 2003 (Troubleman Unlimited). A sample from their track “Marathon” appeared on Le Tigre’s self-titled LP.

Discogs lists the band’s styles as Art Rock, No Wave, and Post-Punk (Rock).
