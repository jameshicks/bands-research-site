Jesus Jones

UK alternative/indie-dance band, formed late 1980s. Discogs artist ID: 10422

Overview

Led by Mike Edwards (vocalist, multi-instrumentalist, primary songwriter and self-producer from Doubt onward). Food Records’ first significant chart act. Broke internationally with “Right Here, Right Now” (1990, from Doubt), which reached number one in the US. Released four studio albums on Food, 1989–1997.
