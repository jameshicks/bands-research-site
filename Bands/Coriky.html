Coriky

Genres: Rock; Alternative Rock, Indie Rock

Washington DC trio of Ian MacKaye (guitar/vocals), Joe Lally (bass/vocals), and Amy Farina (drums/vocals). Formed in 2015; first show 2018. Self-titled debut LP (2020, Dischord DIS190) recorded and mixed by Don Zientara and the band at Inner Ear Studios — among the final sessions before the studio closed in October 2021. All three members sing; all songs credited collectively to the band.

Biography

Formation (2015–2018)

Coriky formed in 2015, drawing together three figures already deeply embedded in the DC post-hardcore lineage. Ian MacKaye had co-founded Dischord Records in 1980, led Minor Threat and Fugazi across two decades, and more recently recorded as a duo (with Amy Farina) in The Evens. Joe Lally was Fugazi’s bassist throughout its 1987–2002 run and had since recorded solo records on Dischord, collaborated with John Frusciante and Josh Klinghoffer in Ataxia, and co-founded the instrumental trio The Messthetics (also 2018). Amy Farina had been MacKaye’s partner in The Evens since 2001 and is his partner in life as well.

Despite forming in 2015, the band did not play their first show until 2018 — consistent with the members’ long history of deliberate, unrushed project development. Discogs’ band profile notes simply: “Formed in 2015, Coriky did not play their first show until 2018. They have recorded one album. They hope to tour.”

Recording and Debut (2018–2020)

The band recorded their debut with Don Zientara at Inner Ear Studios in Arlington, Virginia — the same studio and engineer that had served as the primary recording home for Dischord Records since 1980. Zientara’s studio had been the site of virtually every major record in MacKaye’s and Lally’s careers: from the Teen Idles’ first EP through all seven Fugazi studio albums and the Evens records. By the time Coriky recorded there, Inner Ear was in its final years of operation; the studio closed on October 1, 2021.

The album Coriky was released in 2020 on Dischord (cat. DIS190CD for the CD pressing). Before the album’s release, a digital single — “Clean Kill” — was issued in 2020 on Dischord, also recorded with Zientara. “Clean Kill” appears as the album’s opening track.

Cover mechanics (sleeve design) for the album were handled by Jason Farrell — Dischord’s long-standing graphic designer who had served in the same capacity for Fugazi’s releases and dozens of other Dischord records. Mastering was by T.J. Lipple, who had also mastered Lally’s solo album Why Should I Get Used To It (2011) and the Messthetics albums.

All songs on Coriky are credited collectively to the band (all songs by Coriky). Artwork credit is also given to the band collectively.

Context within the DC Lineage

Coriky represents the convergence of three post-Fugazi trajectories. MacKaye and Lally are the sole carry-overs from Fugazi’s core lineup active together in a new recording project. Farina links Coriky to The Evens, making it an expansion of that duo by one — the addition of Lally’s bass and the consequent shift in dynamic and register. The three-vocalist format (all members sing) has no direct precedent in MacKaye’s prior bands; Fugazi split lead vocals between MacKaye and Picciotto, while The Evens was a more symmetrical dual-vocal arrangement.

The band does not have a second album as of the Discogs data available. Discogs also has no live releases documented.
