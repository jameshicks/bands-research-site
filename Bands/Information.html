Information

Discogs artist ID: 613506 (as “Information (2)“)
New York No Wave band. Active c. 1979–1980. House band of NO Magazine. Later evolved into The Scene Is Now.

Overview

Information formed around NO Magazine (“Instant Artifact of the New Order”), a No Wave fanzine published out of NYU. The band served as the magazine’s house act and contributed tracks to the flexidisc packed with NO Magazine #4 (1979). Their Discogs entry lists three members: Chris Nelson (5) (guitar, trumpet), Philip Dray (electric piano, vocals), and Rick Brown (drums, vocals). Jim Sclavunos (steel drums, saxophone) played on the same flexidisc tracks but is not listed as a member of the Information Discogs entry — his involvement was likely a session contribution, consistent with his simultaneous membership in Beirut Slump.

The flexidisc (release ID 5773873) contains four tracks across two bands. Information contribute tracks A1 “Translator” and A2 “New Alphabet” (titled “Alphabet” in some sources). The B-side track “We Like Boys Who Say No” (not “I Like Boys Who Say No”) is credited to No Group — a separate act comprising Sclavunos, Dray, Nelson, and Peter Dellolio alongside Annene Kaye (credited as “Childbride”) and Seth Goldstein. Chris Stamey of The dB’s co-produced the Information tracks A1 and A2. B1 “Well Oh Well” is by NNB.

The band’s second documented appearance is the cassette compilation Tape #1 (1980, Not On Label), shared with Mofungo and Blinding Headache. Information contribute twelve tracks to this release, including “Let’s Compromise,” “Nozzles,” “What A Knucklehead,” “Zombie Moire,” and others. The lineup overlaps with the flexidisc: Dray (electric piano), Brown (drums), and Nelson (guitar, trumpet), with Gary Larson also on guitar/bass and Jeff McGovern appearing on bass/vocals for Mofungo tracks, foreshadowing his later role in The Scene Is Now.

Chris Nelson was the graphic designer for NO Magazine and is associated with early use of the term “no wave.” Discogs lists two Chris Nelson entries connected to this circle: Chris Nelson (4) (ID 486583) and Chris Nelson (5) (ID 618200). The (5) entry is the one listed as a member of Information (2) and also appears in Mofungo, No Group, and The Scene Is Now. The (4) entry links only to The Scene Is Now and carries a trombone credit on a 1996 Matador release, suggesting these may be two different people or a data split — this is unresolved in Discogs.

Information dissolved around 1980. Nelson and Dray re-emerged in The Scene Is Now, which began releasing in 1984 on Lost Records (9).
