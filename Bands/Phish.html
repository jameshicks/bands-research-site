Phish

American rock and jam band from Burlington, Vermont. Discogs artist ID: 252354. Alias: Sci-Fi Soldier.

Overview

Phish formed at the University of Vermont in 1983. The core quartet — Trey Anastasio (guitar, lead vocals), Mike Gordon (bass, vocals), Jon Fishman (drums, percussion, vocals), and Page McConnell (keyboards, vocals) — solidified in 1986 after the departure of founding guitarist Jeff Holdsworth. The band is known for long-form musical improvisation, genre blending, and a devoted fan following built largely through relentless live touring.

They released their debut cassette Junta independently in 1989 and signed to Elektra Records in 1992, releasing seven studio albums on the label through 2004. After twenty years of near-continuous activity, they broke up in August 2004. They reunited in March 2009 and have recorded and toured steadily since, releasing albums on their own Jemp Records imprint and most recently on ATO Records.

A parallel strand of activity since the early 2000s: an extensive archival live-release program — first under the Livephish.com / Livephish.com download label (distributed through Elektra), then physically and digitally through Jemp Records. The program spans dozens of concert recordings from 1989 to the present.

Livephish / Jemp Archive Program

The digital download archive launched c. 2001 under the Livephish.com label (Discogs ID: 137005; parent: Elektra). After the hiatus, archival physical and download releases continued under Jemp Records (Discogs ID: 72582, 240 total releases on Discogs). The program covers concert recordings from 1989 onward. The original stub reference to “Round Records (3)” is incorrect — Round Records (3) is the Jerry Garcia family label; Phish’s archive label is Jemp Records / Livephish.com.
