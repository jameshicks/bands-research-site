English Teacher

UK indie / post-punk band from Leeds. Discogs ID: 8380779. Active since 2020 (earlier as Frank, 2018).

Biography

Origins (2018–2020)

English Teacher began as a dream pop project called Frank, formed in Leeds in 2018. The core lineup — Lily Fontaine (vocals, guitar, synth), Lewis Whiting (lead guitar, synth), Nicholas Eden (bass), and Douglas Frost (drums) — played their first gig under the English Teacher name in 2020. Leeds was a natural home: a city with a well-established indie and post-punk infrastructure, strong DIY venues, and a University of Leeds music scene that has fed bands from Gang of Four onward.

Early Self-Released Work (2021–2022)

The band’s first two Discogs entries are self-released digital singles. The R&B single (2021, Not On Label) was their first catalogued release — a two-track digital file single. Co-songwriter credits on the B-side “Wallace” include James Sanger (notable session songwriter) alongside members Douglas Frost and Nicholas Eden.

Their first vinyl release was the Polyawkward EP (2022, Nice Swan Records, NSWN055), a five-track 12” EP recorded with engineers Christoph Skirl and Theo Verney. Styles on Discogs list it as Alternative Rock / Indie Rock / Math Rock. Nice Swan was a mid-tier UK indie imprint with an active 2019–2024 catalog; it served as a stepping stone to a major deal.

Signing to Island Records & Breakthrough (2022–2024)

English Teacher signed to Island Records ahead of their debut album. The first Island release was the live document Live From BBC Maida Vale (2024, Island Records, cat. 7514177), a limited 10” EP capturing five tracks including a cover of LCD Soundsystem’s “New York, I Love You But You’re Bringing Me Down.”

The debut full-length This Could Be Texas followed in 2024 (Island Records, cat. 5876414 / 506431). It was produced and mixed by Marta Salogni and mastered by Heba Kadry — Salogni’s recurring production partnership with Kadry extending to their English Teacher work. The album spans fourteen tracks on vinyl (plus a CD-only “Pool Sessions” bonus set), with styles catalogued as Alternative Rock / Post-Punk.

The band won the Mercury Prize 2024 for This Could Be Texas — a notable outcome for a debut album.

Remix Album (2025)

This Could Be A Remix Album (2025, Island Records) collected thirteen reworkings of album tracks plus one new track (“Albert Road”), with remixes by Daniel Avery, Water From Your Eyes, SHERELLE, Baxter Dury, Working Men’s Club, Max Cooper, Matt Maltese, and others. The remix album is digital-only (FLAC) and shows the band’s reach across indie, club, and electronic scenes.
