James

Discogs ID: 12094. Manchester indie rock band, formed early 1980s. Originally on Factory Records, then Fontana/Mercury for their commercial peak, and later BMG/Infectious Music/Virgin Music for the reform era.

Andy Diagram Era (1989–92; 2007–present)

Andy Diagram joined during the Gold Mother (1990) sessions and remained through Seven (1992). His processed trumpet — through harmonizer, fuzz, and wah pedals — became a signature element. He rejoined in 2007 for the ongoing reformation and has appeared on all subsequent studio albums.

Key releases with Diagram: Gold Mother (1990), James (1991), Seven (1992), Hey Ma (2008), La Petite Mort (2014), Girl At The End Of The World (2016), Living In Extraordinary Times (2018), All The Colours Of You (2021), Be Opened By The Wonderful (40 Years Orchestrated) (2023), Nothing But Love // The Definitive Best Of (2025).

Research Queue

Late-1990s Onward (Whiplash era +)

Whiplash (1997, Fontana) brought in Adrian Oxaal on guitar/cello and Stephen Hague as producer, with Brian Eno providing “Frequent Interference And Occasional Co-production” and keyboard/synth parts. The Best Of compilation followed in 1998 on Fontana/Mercury worldwide.

After the 2001 hiatus and 2007 reformation, the ongoing lineup added Chloe Alper (vocals/percussion) and Debbie Knox-Hewson (drums). Post-reformation studio albums:

  • Hey Ma (2008)
  • La Petite Mort (2014)
  • Girl At The End Of The World (2016)
  • Living In Extraordinary Times (2018, Infectious Music) — produced by Charlie Andrew; Brian Eno on synth programming
  • All The Colours Of You (2021, Virgin Music) — produced by Jacknife Lee
  • Be Opened By The Wonderful (40 Years Orchestrated) (2023, Virgin Music) — orchestrated by Joe Duddell with ORCA22 and Manchester Inspirational Voices choir
  • Yummy (2024, Virgin Music)

2025: Nothing But Love // The Definitive Best Of (EMI/Mercury/Universal) — all current members contributed liner notes.
