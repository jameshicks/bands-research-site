The Police

Overview

The Police were a British rock band formed in London in 1977, originally comprising Stewart Copeland (drums), Gordon “Sting” Sumner (vocals/bass), Henry Padovani (guitar), and later Andy Summers (guitar) — Padovani departing early, leaving the classic trio. Emerging from the late-1970s punk and new wave scene, they developed a distinctive sound that blended reggae-inflected rhythms, post-punk urgency, and polished pop craft, placing them at the centre of British rock’s mainstream crossover moment. Their catalogue is concentrated in the 1980s (27 releases vs. 13 in the 1970s) and released almost entirely through A&M Records, with Nigel Gray and Hugh Padgham as their primary producers across the studio run. The band dissolved in 1986 and reunited for a world tour in 2007–2008; their work has since been extensively reissued through Universal, Polydor, and PolyGram imprints.
