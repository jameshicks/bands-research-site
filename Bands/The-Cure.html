The Cure

UK post-punk / goth rock band formed 1976 in Crawley, West Sussex. Discogs artist ID: 28972. Led throughout by vocalist/guitarist Robert Smith. One of the definitive acts of the British alternative music scene, active from the late 1970s to the present.

Overview

Core commercial and artistic peak ran 1984-1992, during which all studio albums were co-produced with David M. Allen. UK label was Fiction Records (founded by Chris Parry); US releases on Elektra.
