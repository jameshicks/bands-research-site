Three Mile Pilot

Post-rock / indie rock band. San Diego, California. Discogs ID: 252907. Formed 1991, active 1991–1997 (initial run), reunited 2009–2012. Name variations: 3mp, 3-Mile Pilot, 3 Mile Pilot, TMP.

Biography

Formation and Early Period (1991–1993)

Three Mile Pilot formed in San Diego in 1991 around singer-guitarist Pall Jenkins and multi-instrumentalist Armistead Burwell Smith IV (later known as Zach Smith, later of Pinback). Drummer Tom Zinser and occasional collaborator Jim French (saxophone and various wind instruments) filled out the early lineup. Tobias Nathaniel (piano, bass) became a recurring member, though he appears less consistently than the core trio.

The debut LP Nà Vuccà Dò Lupù (1992) was released on Negative Pulse Records — a small San Diego label — recorded with engineer Jeff Forrest. The record featured Jim French on saxophone and exotic wind instruments on two tracks (B1, B3), establishing the band’s early identity as something angular, rhythmically off-kilter, and deliberately oblique.

Core Years (1994–1997)

The Chief Assassin To The Sinister (1994, Cargo/Headhunter, HED-030) was the band’s commercial breakthrough and remains their best-known record. Discogs-tagged genres include Experimental, Indie Rock, and Math Rock — one of the most accurate triple-classifications in that era’s San Diego scene. Smith played bass, vocals, piano, and cello; Zinser drums; Jenkins guitar and vocals. Jim French returned on horns (tr.4). The 8-minute title track stands as a career peak.

Between 1994 and 1997 the band released a run of 7”s and split releases on various small labels: Circumcised / Nussun (1994, Cargo/Headhunter), Starcontrol Out (1995, Negative Records (2)), Piano Plus / Piano Minus (1996, Penny Farthing Records (2)), Red Sensing (1996, Outer Universe Research Records), and split releases with Boilermaker and A Minor Forest.

Another Desert, Another Sea (1997, Cargo Music/Headhunter, HED-078) was the final full-length of the initial run — a 13-track album produced by Steve Fisk, John Goodmanson, Mark Trombino, and the band itself. Tobias Nathaniel appears on two tracks (“The Year Of No Light,” “Ruin”). Rick Froberg (of Drive Like Jehu / Hot Snakes) contributes backing vocals on “Ruin.” Steve Fisk plays theremin on “South.”

Later in 1997 the band also released a self-titled mini-album LP on Gravity (2) (gravity 31), which included a cover of Brian Eno’s “By This River” performed by Jenkins and Nathaniel.

Hiatus and Aftermath (1997–2008)

Three Mile Pilot went on open-ended hiatus in 1997. Jenkins and Nathaniel immediately founded The Black Heart Procession, taking the piano-centric, atmospherically dark elements of TMP’s later work forward into a new direction. Both Smith and Zinser joined Pinback with Rob Crow — this is the direct personnel lineage: Smith is the Armistead Burwell Smith IV / Zach Smith identity listed as Pinback’s co-founder alongside Rob Crow, and Zinser is also a documented Pinback member. Smith and Zinser also formed Neighborhood Watch together.

In 2000 Headhunter/Cargo Music released Songs From An Old Town We Once Knew (HED-083), a 2xCD retrospective compilation gathering tracks from across the catalog. The compilation credits confirm the three-person “is Three Mile Pilot” core: Smith, Jenkins, Zinser (with Nathaniel and Jim French credited as “at times on these recordings”).

Reunion Period (2009–2012)

The band reunited and signed to Temporary Residence Limited. Three releases emerged:

  • Planets (2009, TRL, TRR-158) — 7” single; lineup: Jenkins, Smith, Zinser
  • The Inevitable Past Is The Future Forgotten (2010, TRL, TRR166) — 2xLP, 12 tracks; lineup: Jenkins (credited as Pall Jenkins), Zinser, Zach Smith (7) [= Smith]; Tobias Nathaniel plays piano on tr.C1, Matt Resovich violin on tr.C2
  • Maps (2012, TRL, TRR192LP) — 12” EP, single-sided, 5 tracks; lineup: Paulo Zappoli [= Jenkins], Zinser, Zach Smith (7)

The reunion material is tagged Indie Rock and tends toward quieter, more atmospheric territory than the early run.
