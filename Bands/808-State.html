808 State

Discogs ID: 318
Origin: Manchester, UK
Active: 1988–present
Labels: Creed Records (1988–1989), ZTT Records (1989–2021), Circus Records (2002)

Manchester electronic group, central to the UK acid house and techno explosion. Named after the Roland TR-808 drum machine. Formed 1988 at Eastern Bloc Records, Martin Price’s Manchester record shop, which doubled as a hub for the city’s emerging rave scene. Aliases include Lounge Jays, MacMillan & Wife, Two Fat Ladies And A Duck.

Membership History

1988 (founding trio): Martin Price (co-founder, Creed Records owner) + Graham Massey (ex-Biting Tongues, Factory Records) + Gerald Simpson
1989 onwards: Gerald Simpson departs → becomes A Guy Called Gerald; replaced by the Spinmasters: Andy Barker + Darren Partington
Late 1991: Martin Price leaves (refused US tour); forms Switzerland, manages Kaliphz (Rochdale rappers)
2015: Darren Partington jailed January 2015 (18 months, drug dealing); Massey and Barker continue as duo
2021: Andy Barker dies 6 November 2021; Massey continues

People and Labels: Background

Martin Price ran Eastern Bloc Records in Manchester’s Northern Quarter, which was the city’s key import and specialist record shop during the late 1980s acid house explosion. He also ran Creed Records as the label arm. His departure from 808 State (over the US tour) was a significant moment — he subsequently formed Switzerland and moved into management (notably Kaliphz).

Graham Massey was the musical anchor throughout, carrying the group from the Biting Tongues post-punk background into acid house, techno, and beyond. Massey’s aliases include Big Muff Bass, Massonix, and Prof Vernon World. He was also part of Homelife and Beach Surgeons, and contributed to other ZTT projects.

Gerald Simpson (A Guy Called Gerald) had already produced “Voodoo Ray” (1988) under his own name while a founding member of 808 State. His departure was amicable; both 808 State and A Guy Called Gerald continued on parallel tracks through the acid house era.

Andy Barker and Darren Partington (the Spinmasters) were DJs on Sunset FM and Kiss FM (Manchester) before joining 808 State. Both also recorded as Hit Squad MCR.

Live

808 State headlined the homecoming concert at the 2002 Commonwealth Games in Manchester — 40,000 people. One of the landmark moments of Manchester’s post-Hacienda cultural reclamation.
