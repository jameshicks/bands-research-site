Penetration (2)

Punk/post-punk band from Ferryhill, County Durham, UK. Formed 1976, disbanded 1979, reformed c.2001. Discogs artist ID: 256243.

Overview

Penetration formed in Ferryhill, County Durham in 1976, founded by vocalist Pauline Murray (born 8 March 1958, Durham) and bassist Robert Blamire (born 1956). They were one of the few significant punk acts to emerge from the North East of England. Signed to Virgin Records, they released two studio albums — Moving Targets (1978) and Coming Up For Air (1979) — plus a run of singles beginning with “Don’t Dictate” (1977), which is widely regarded as a UK punk classic. The band disbanded in 1979. Murray went on to record with The Invisible Girls (the Martin Hannett–led Manchester collective), and Blamire produced the Scars’ LP Author! Author! (PRE, 1981). The band reformed around 2001 with several new members; the reformed lineup released singles on Damaged Goods in 2008–2010 and two albums on the Polestar label in 2015 and 2017.
