Out Hud

Discogs ID: 59963
Also known as: Outhud
Origin: Bay Area, California (later New York City)
Active: 1996–2005
Genres: Electronic, dance-punk, experimental

Electronic/dance-punk quintet formed Bay Area 1996, later based in New York City. Defined by the collision of live drums/cello/bass with DJ mixing — Justin Van Der Volgen occupied the “mixer” slot rather than a conventional instrumental role. Three members — Nic Offer, Tyler Pope, and Justin Van Der Volgen — simultaneously belonged to !!! throughout Out Hud’s existence, making the two bands essentially twin projects from the same core personnel pool.

Label arc: Red Alert Works (debut 7”, 1998) → Gold Standard Laboratories (1998–1999) → 5 Rue Christine (2000, GER007) → Kranky (2002–2005) + !K7 Records (2005 singles). Disbanded 2005.
