x_x (X Blank X)

Cleveland proto-punk / experimental group. Active c. 1975–1982, with archival releases continuing through 2018. Discogs artist ID: 443901.

Name variations: X Blank X, Ex Blank Ex, X-Blank-X, X-X, X Pagans X, X( )X, X_____X.

Overview

x_x formed in Cleveland, Ohio around the mid-1970s, emerging from the same underground as Electric Eels, Pere Ubu, Mirrors, and Rocket From The Tombs — a scene that is now recognized as one of the earliest nodes of American punk and no wave. The band was led by John Morton (guitarist and primary vocalist of the Electric Eels), and the Discogs member list shows a rotating cast of Cleveland scene veterans.

The group released two 7” singles on Drome Records in 1979 and 1980 (the only original releases in their discography), then surfaced intermittently on compilations across the 1990s through 2010s, culminating in a 2014 archival LP (X Sticky Fingers X) on Full Contact Records and a 2015 live/archival CD EP (Albert Ayler’s Ghosts Live At The Yellow Ghetto) on Smog Veil Records. A 2018 7” (Not Now No Way, My Mind’s Eye Records) — credited as “X Pagans X” — was their most recent release.

Their track “Your Full Of Shit” appeared on the Soul Jazz Punk 45: Kill The Hippies! Kill Yourself! (2013), credited as “X Blank X,” and two tracks (“Approaching The Minimal With Spray Guns” and “A”) appeared on Punk 45: Extermination Nights In The Sixth City! (2015), the Cleveland-specific volume of the same series.

Scene Context

x_x was embedded in one of the most significant American proto-punk scenes. Their immediate contemporaries on the Cleveland underground included:

  • Electric Eels — John Morton and Anton Fier were also Electric Eels members; the Eels are cited as a direct influence on no wave and American punk
  • Pere Ubu — also included Anton Fier at various points; the defining Cleveland art-rock band
  • Mirrors — shared members Craig Bell and Michael Weldon; another early Cleveland proto-punk act
  • Rocket From The Tombs — Craig Bell was a member; the proto-punk supergroup that split into Pere Ubu and the Dead Boys
  • The Pagans — covered by x_x on the 2018 single; fellow Cleveland punk band
  • The Styrenes — John Morton was a member; later Cleveland new wave act
  • Jazz Destroyers — James Ellis was a member; appeared on the same Punk 45: Extermination Nights compilation

The Punk 45: Extermination Nights In The Sixth City! LP (Soul Jazz, 2015) is the most comprehensive document of this scene, placing x_x alongside Electric Eels, Pere Ubu, Mirrors, Rocket From The Tombs, Pagans, Human Switchboard, Defnics, Broncs, and The Styrenes.
