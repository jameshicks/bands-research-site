US hardcore/powerviolence band. Discogs artist ID: 216899.

Notes (web)

Swirlies split — how it came about

The Swirlies / Dropdead release listed in Discogs as Fast Forward (6) is a cassette, not a 7”. It was a show tape: roughly 25–30 home-dubbed copies were given away at a Dropdead + Swirlies in-store performance at Fast Forward Records, Providence, RI on May 4, 1991. Fast Forward was a record store (Providence) that hosted in-store shows; it is the same entity as the Discogs label “Fast Forward (6)“. The tape was not a commercial release — copies were free at the door.

The connection was geographic rather than a shared ongoing label relationship. Swirlies were a Boston-area band; this was their second-ever live appearance (their first live show had been January 25, 1991). No published source explains the specific personal or booking relationship that brought the two bands onto the same bill. The Chicago Reader (2024 preview piece) describes it only as “an in-store show.” [https://chicagoreader.com/music/concert-preview/swirlies-sleeping-village/]

The Swirlies Bandcamp page for their half of the tape states: “Side Swirlies of a split cassette created for the Dropdead + Swirlies show at Fast Forward Records (Providence, RI). About 25 to 30 copies were given away at the show (May 4, 1991 at 10pm).” [https://swirlies.bandcamp.com/album/side-swirlies-from-the-1991-dropdead-swirlies-split-tape]

The exhaustive Swirlies discography at rcarchives.com confirms: “25 to 30 home dubbed copies — free giveaway for a show at Fast Forward Records, Providence, RI in May 1991” and notes “sound quality is atrocious, to a level far beyond the first demo, as these were dubs of dubs of bad recordings.” [https://rcarchives.com/swirlies/discography/numbers-damon01.html]

Summary: The pairing reflects a shared DIY/indie-network booking at a Providence record store, not a formal label co-release. The specific person or connection who arranged the double bill is not documented in any available source.
