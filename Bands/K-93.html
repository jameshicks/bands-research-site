K÷93

One-off supergroup project formed c. 2020 by Peter Hook (ex-Joy Division / New Order), Jaz Coleman, and Geordie Walker (both of Killing Joke). They released a single self-titled EP/mini-album on Cadiz Entertainment in 2021. Discogs artist ID: 8993392.

Overview

K÷93 brought together three post-punk veterans whose careers overlapped without ever formally crossing: Hook, the melodic-bass architect of Joy Division and New Order; and Coleman and Walker, the singer/composer and guitarist who have been the core of Killing Joke since 1978. The project’s name encodes the personnel — K (Killing Joke), Hook (Hook), and 93 as a numerological/occult reference that fits with Coleman’s well-documented interest in Thelema and esoteric philosophy.

The release is minimal — three tracks on a limited 10” EP with no producer credit listed. It sits squarely in a post-punk / industrial tradition shared by all three members’ primary bands.

Formation and Context

No producer is credited on the release, suggesting the three performed and self-produced the sessions. Coleman wrote the sleeve notes alongside Hook. The earliest pressing (a white-label test pressing, cat. CADIZ12198) appeared in 2020, with the numbered limited edition vinyl and digital release following in 2021.

The project has no documented follow-up or live activity in Discogs. Killing Joke remained active concurrently; Hook continued Peter Hook And The Light operations. The EP appears to be a self-contained one-off.
