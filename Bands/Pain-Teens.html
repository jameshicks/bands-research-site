Pain Teens

Noise rock / experimental rock. Houston, Texas. Active 1985–1995. Discogs artist ID: 138726.

Overview

Pain Teens were formed in Houston, Texas in 1985 by vocalist/lyricist Bliss Blood (real name Lisa Kay Peterson) and guitarist/multi-instrumentalist Scott Ayers. Beginning as a punk-aligned band, they evolved into a chaotic hybrid of noise rock, tape loops, found sound, sampling, and exotic instrumentation. Early output appeared on their own Anomie Records imprint; from 1990 they released four LPs on Trance Syndicate Records, the Austin label run by King Coffey of the Butthole Surfers.

Drummer Frank Gary Martin and bassist Kirk Carr joined in 1990, forming the classic lineup for the Trance Syndicate period. Scott Ayers produced and engineered all releases. The band dissolved in 1995; Blood moved to New York City that year. Kirk Carr died of cancer in May 2008.
