bdrmm

Hull-based dreampop / shoegaze band, pronounced “bedroom”. Discogs artist ID: 7255372. Founded 2016.

Overview

bdrmm are a shoegaze and post-punk group from Hull, UK, formed in 2016. The band’s core creative unit centres on Ryan Smith (guitar, vocals) and Jordan Smith (bass, synth, production, artwork), with Joe Vickers (guitar), Conor Murray (bass), Luke Irvin (drums), and Danny Hull (synth, vocals) rounding out the lineup on various releases. The band began as a self-releasing act before signing with Sonic Cathedral for their debut album Bedroom (2020) and associated EPs and singles. From 2023 onward they moved to Rock Action Records for their second and third albums.

Their sound is consistently anchored by the production and recording work of Alex Greaves, who has been involved from the earliest self-released recordings (2018) through to Microtonic (2025). Greaves records, produces, mixes, and co-writes across their catalog, making him the primary sonic architect alongside the band’s own writing. Mastering shifted from Heba Kadry (on Bedroom and surrounding Sonic Cathedral-era releases) to Frank Arkwright at Abbey Road (I Don’t Know, 2023) and Dick Beetham (Microtonic, 2025).
