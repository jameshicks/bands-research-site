New Age Steppers

Post-punk dub collective. UK. Discogs ID: 23698. Active 1980–present.

Produced by Adrian Sherwood. Began on Statik Records (1980–81) before migrating fully to On-U Sound. 45 On-U Sound releases total.

Members (per Discogs)

Ari-Up (Ariane Forster; The Slits), Bruce Smith (The Pop Group, PiL), Mark Stewart, Keith Levene (PiL), George Oban, Eskimo Fox, Neneh Cherry, Sean Oliver, Jarrett Tomlinson, Lincoln Valentine Scott.

Track-level vocal notes (web-sourced): The Discogs sleeve credit is album-wide (“Ari, Viv, Mark”) without track breakdown. Multiple review sources confirm: Mark Stewart sings on A4 “Crazy Dreams And High Ideals” (he wrote the track; it was “later re-versioned with the Maffia”); Ari Up handles reggae interpretations including A1 “Fade Away” and B3 “Love Forever”; Vivien Goldman sings B4 “Private Armies” (which she wrote). Notes

The collective operated with a rotating cast rather than a fixed lineup. Core constants across all releases: Adrian Sherwood (producer) and Ari-Up (vocal lead). The 1981 debut LP drew heavily from the UK post-punk world (The Pop Group, The Slits, PIL, The Raincoats); by Foundation Steppers (1983) the sound shifted toward Jamaican session players.

Mark Stewart’s involvement was concentrated at the founding moment — the self-titled debut LP (1981). He is credited as “Mark” in the Vocals section of that LP, confirms lead vocals on A4 (“Crazy Dreams And High Ideals” — his own composition), and has an uncredited songwriting credit for the same track. He does not appear on subsequent NAS studio releases, having launched Mark Stewart And The Maffia as his primary On-U Sound vehicle from 1982 onward. His 1994 Massive Hits Vol. 1 credit is archival (material drawn from the 1981 LP sessions).

Sherwood and Stewart had known each other since the mid-1970s, meeting as teenagers at Revolver Records in Bristol, where Sherwood delivered reggae imports weekly. After The Pop Group split in 1981, Stewart moved to London and entered the On-U Sound orbit, with NAS participation preceding the Maffia project. (Caught by the River; Test Pressing / Sherwood interview, as of 2026-06-27.)

The project bridges On-U Sound’s post-punk reggae world and the wider UK post-punk network via Ari-Up (The Slits), Bruce Smith and Mark Stewart (The Pop Group), and Keith Levene (PiL).
