Pumice

New Zealand experimental folk/lo-fi project led by Stefan Neville (Discogs artist ID: 75434). Active since 1994, based in New Zealand. Pumice is effectively a one-person operation — Neville writes, performs, records, and produces nearly everything himself, typically at home on his own equipment. Releases span cassette, vinyl, and CD across a range of underground labels; the project’s longest-running home is Soft Abuse (US), which has issued the bulk of the catalog from 2006 onward.

Members listed on Discogs: Stefan Neville (core), Jade Farley, Jon Arcus, Kaaterama Morehu.

Overview

Pumice emerged from Christchurch/New Zealand in the early 1990s, with the first release Yi-Jun appearing on local label Plop (2) in 1994. Through the late 1990s Neville self-released prolifically on his own imprint Stabbies And The Rocket Recordings, issuing a stream of cassettes, CDrs, and small-run vinyl in New Zealand — Walkden Fisher (1995), Poise (1996), Platter One (1997), I’ll Take No Chance Near A Volcano (1997), Let’s Treat The Pig’s Head As Dip (1998), Platter 4 (1999), and A Window Into An Egg (2001) among them. The recordings from this period are rough-hewn and cassette-oriented, grounded in lo-fi home recording but displaying the melodic instinct and quietly surreal sensibility that would define later work.

International exposure arrived gradually in the early 2000s. A 2001 reissue of I’ll Take No Chance Near A Volcano on Australian label Celebrate Psi Phenomenon extended the reach beyond NZ. The 2003 cassette Sexual on PseudoArcana and the 2004 LP Raft on Last Visible Dog / Stabbies And The Rocket Recordings brought wider underground attention. The 2005 split with Pewt’r Trust on Ultra Eczema (Belgium) and the Worldwide Skull CDr on Audiobot placed Pumice within the global underground network.

The Soft Abuse relationship began in 2006 with Yeahnahvienna (SAB014), a cassette/download EP recorded by Neville. The first LP on the label, Pebbles (2007, SAB022), was mastered by Pete Swanson (of Yellow Swans) and established the pattern that would continue for over 15 years: Neville alone on all instruments and recording, with minimal outside contributions. Quo (2008) followed, with mastering by Roy Martyn.

A 2009 split 7” on Soft Abuse paired Pumice’s side B with Grouper (Liz Harris) on side A (SAB035). Persevere (2009, Soft Abuse SAB037) appeared the same year.

PUNY (2012, SAB050) marked a return after a quiet period. A 2013 three-way split Realistic Pillow on Belgian label (K-RAA-K)³ gathered Pumice with Mad Nanna and Kraus (2) and Slug Guts. Land (2014, SAB066) was another solo-recorded LP.

Puddles (2015, SAB071) was the most collaborative album to that point — Neville played all main tracks (acoustic guitar, electric guitar, vocals, tape, organ, drums, loops, bass, synth, piano) but enlisted Matt De Gennaro (clavichord, organ, guitar, violin, electric piano on tracks 3 and 8), Hermione Johnson (piano), Pat Kraus (synth on track 1), Claire Mahoney (vocals), Indira Neville (vocals), Lucy Danko (vocals), Ponzo Danko (vocals), and Leighton Craig (vocals, clarinet).

Platelets (2018, SAB085) saw Jade Farley contributing violin and keyboards on the A-side alongside Neville’s guitar, recorder, and accordion. Worldwide Welts (2019) appeared on Dubbed Tapes. Table (2020, SAB093) again brought in Jade Farley (violin, guitar, vocals, keyboards) and had mastering by Sean McCann (4). Worldwide Table (2021) was another Dubbed Tapes release.

Phylis (2022, SAB105) was the most recent major LP on Soft Abuse — Neville again performed everything (bass, ukulele, vocals, sampler, drums, organ, tape, guitar, synth) and handled production and artwork; Jade Farley contributed violin, keyboards, guitar, synth, melodica, vocals, drums, and organ throughout; Hermione Johnson played piano on one track; Sean McCann (4) mastered. The most recent entry in the DB is Miserable Poison (2024) on Swedish label Fördämning Arkiv.

Neville operates under numerous other project names — see Stefan Neville for the full list of groups and aliases including The Renderers, Sunken, The Aesthetics (2), Ohm (11), and others.
