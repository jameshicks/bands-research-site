Sweep The Leg Johnny

Discogs ID: 323833
Origin: Chicago, Illinois
Active: c.1992–present
Genres: Post-rock, jazz-rock, experimental

Chicago experimental rock/jazz band. 10 releases on Southern Records 1999–2021 — one of the label’s most consistently present acts across its lifespan.

Notes (web) — Chicago post-rock scene connections

Researched 2026-07-27. Sources: Wikipedia (as of 2026-07-27), Discogs release pages, Thrill Jockey artist page.

Summary: No direct connection to Tortoise or Thrill Jockey Records is documented in any source. The indirect links to that scene are real but mediated.

Indirect Thrill Jockey link — A Minor Forest split

The clearest scene adjacency is a 1998 split 7-inch with A Minor Forest on Tree Records’ Post Marked Stamps series (no. 8, limited to 1,800 copies). A Minor Forest was a San Francisco math-rock band on Thrill Jockey (released Flemish Altruism, 1996, and Inindependence, 1998 on that label) — the same label as Tortoise. The split was recorded at Kingsize Soundlabs in Chicago (STLJ’s side) and Lucky Cat in San Francisco (A Minor Forest’s side). [source: https://www.discogs.com/release/798105-A-Minor-Forest-Sweep-The-Leg-Johnny-Post-Marked-Stamps-No-8; A Minor Forest / Thrill Jockey confirmed at https://www.thrilljockey.com/artists/a-minor-forest]

This places STLJ in a direct collaboration with a Thrill Jockey act, but does not put STLJ on Thrill Jockey themselves — they were never signed to or released on that label.

No documented Tortoise connection

No source (Wikipedia, AllMusic, Discogs release credits, Rate Your Music, the STLJ Sick Room Records pages) documents a shared release, shared member, co-headlining tour, or compilation appearance linking STLJ and Tortoise. Both are Chicago-based and contemporaneous, but the connection does not appear in any primary or secondary source found.

Sick Room Records — STLJ members’ own label, post-rock roster

In 2001, STLJ members Mitch Cheney and Steve Sostak co-founded Sick Room Records (with Ryan Duncan) in Chicago. The label ran 2001–2017 and released approximately 73 acts across indie/post/math rock, noise rock, and punk, including fellow STLJ-adjacent acts Rumah Sakit and Just A Fire (Chris Daly’s post-STLJ band with members of June of 44 and Hoover). The Sick Room roster does not overlap with Thrill Jockey’s roster in any documented way. [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sick_Room_Records, as of 2026-07-27]

June of 44 / Hoover orbit (member connection, not scene-label)

Chris Daly (guitar) joined Just A Fire after STLJ’s 2002 dissolution — a band with members of June of 44 and Hoover, both post-hardcore/post-rock acts. This is a personnel connection to the broader experimental rock scene, not to the Tortoise/Thrill Jockey axis specifically. [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweep_the_Leg_Johnny, as of 2026-07-27]

Other split partners

STLJ’s other split releases (Streganona 1995, Cowpers 1998, Rumah Sakit 2000/2003, Haymarket Riot 2002) show no further Thrill Jockey or Tortoise connections in any source found.
