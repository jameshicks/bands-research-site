NEU!

German duo: Michael Rother (guitar, bass, electronics) and Klaus Dinger (drums, guitar, voice). Formed in Düsseldorf 1971 after both left early Kraftwerk. Discogs artist ID: 12636.

Overview

NEU! are foundational figures of Krautrock and motorik music. Rother and Dinger met in the Düsseldorf avant-garde circle — both briefly played in early Kraftwerk before departing and founding NEU! in 1971. Their three studio albums (1972–1975), all produced and engineered by Conny Plank, established the “motorik” beat (Dinger’s unwavering 4/4 pulse, also called the “Apache beat”), a signature approach to guitar texture and drone, and a studio practice that influenced post-punk, shoegaze, krautrock, and electronic music globally.

The group had minimal commercial success during its existence but is retrospectively considered one of the founders of krautrock and a major influence on subsequent rock and electronic music. Early member Eberhard Kranemann (alias Fritz Müller) appeared on the debut album sleeve photography and on 1972 live recordings. The 1975 album NEU! ‘75 expanded the lineup for its B-side, bringing in Thomas Dinger and Hans Lampe (2) on additional drums — the B-side’s harder punk-adjacent attack contrasting sharply with the ambient drift of Side A. After NEU! ‘75 Rother departed; the band dissolved as an active unit.

A contested reunion in 1985–86 produced recordings released later as the ‘86 single (Grönland, 2010) and the Neu! 4 Japan CD (Captain Trip, 1995), the latter disputed by Rother. The 2009 1972 Live CDr documented early live material including Kranemann and bassist Uli Trepte. A 50th anniversary box set appeared in 2022.

Connections

  • Conny Plank — produced and engineered all three studio albums; widely described as the band’s unofficial third member
  • Michael Rother — co-founder; after NEU! formed Harmonia with Dieter Moebius and Hans-Joachim Roedelius, then pursued solo career (Sky Records, then Grönland)
  • Klaus Dinger — co-founder; after NEU! formed La Düsseldorf with his brother Thomas Dinger and Hans Lampe (2); died 21 March 2008
  • Kraftwerk — both Rother and Dinger briefly played in early Kraftwerk before departing to form NEU!
  • Eberhard Kranemann — early member; played in NEU!‘s 1972 live lineup (lap steel); photographed the debut sleeve under his Fritz Müller alias
  • Thomas Dinger — Klaus’s brother; drums on NEU! ‘75 Side B; co-founded La Düsseldorf
  • Hans Lampe (2) — assistant engineer on Neu! 2; drums on NEU! ‘75 Side B; co-founded La Düsseldorf
  • Harmonia — Michael Rother’s post-NEU! collaboration with Cluster members Moebius and Roedelius
  • La Düsseldorf — Klaus Dinger’s post-NEU! band
  • La! NEU? — late Dinger project (Discogs ID: 204114)
  • Hallogallo 2010 — Michael Rother touring project (Discogs ID: 1936529)
  • Brain — primary label for the three studio albums; sublabel of Metronome
  • Uli Trepte — bassist on the 1972 live recording (also known from Guru Guru); Discogs ID: 233355
