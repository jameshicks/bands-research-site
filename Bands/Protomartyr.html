Protomartyr

Post-punk band from Detroit, Michigan, USA. Discogs artist ID: 2978786 (listed as “Protomartyr (2)”). Active c. 2010–present. Core label homes: Hardly Art (2014–2015), Domino (2017–2023); debut releases on Urinal Cake Records and Doubles Tapes (2012).

Overview

Protomartyr formed in Detroit around 2010, coalescing around vocalist Joe Casey, guitarist Greg Ahee, bassist Scott Davidson, and drummer Alex Leonard. Kevin Boyer (guitar, vocals — also of Tyvek) appears on their earliest releases. The band began with a self-released cassette and two singles on Urinal Cake Records before signing to Hardly Art (a Sub Pop subsidiary) for their second and third studio albums. Their move to Domino in 2017 marked a step up in profile and production scope, bringing in outside producers and guest musicians for the first time. By Ultimate Success Today (2020) the band was incorporating free-jazz musicians and chamber players alongside its core post-punk framework. Formal Growth In The Desert (2023), produced by Jake Aron and Greg Ahee himself, is their most recent studio album.
