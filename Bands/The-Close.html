The Close

Discogs ID: 884182
Origin: United States
Active: 2000–2006
Genres: Indie Rock
Labels: Moodswing Records, Goodnight Records

Indie rock band active in the early 2000s US indie scene. Released two full-lengths and several splits/EPs across Moodswing Records and Goodnight Records. Self-recorded early releases; later work engineered by Mark Rains and mastered by Glenn Schick. Opened for Karate (the Boston post-rock band). Most closely associated with The Rosebuds, with whom they shared a split 7” on Goodnight Records (2004).

Post-dissolution

The band dissolved in 2006/2007 (see conflict note below). Known post-band activities:

Brooks Meek — formed The Meeks Family, an Atlanta-based indie folk/country project, in winter 2008, initially collaborating with Jonathan Bradley (formerly of Iron and Wine). Their debut album Thirty One Years of Lullaby (c. 2010) included a guest duet, “Lips for Your Kissin’”, with former Close bandmate Theresa Fedor. Meeks has described the project as his response to abandoning touring after The Close broke up and choosing home life and parenthood. The Meeks Family continued as a rotating ensemble and was still performing as of May 2025 (Atlanta show with Subsonics). (themeeksfamilymusic.com; Creative Loafing Atlanta, c. March 2010)

Theresa Fedor — appeared as a guest vocalist on The Meeks Family’s debut album (duet with Brooks Meeks on “Lips for Your Kissin’”). No further post-Close musical projects found on the web or Discogs. (Creative Loafing Atlanta, c. March 2010)

Dustin Nigro — was bass player for Gentleman Jesse & His Men (Atlanta power-pop) from 2005 to 2008, overlapping with the final years of The Close. He left Gentleman Jesse amicably after the band’s 2008 full-length. Discogs also shows guitar credits on 2011–2012 releases (specific projects not confirmed by web search). (rateyourmusic.com/artist/gentleman_jesse_and_his_men; Discogs credits per trawler)

Keefe Justice — Discogs shows drum credits on releases in 2022 and 2023. Specific projects not confirmed by web search. (Discogs credits per trawler)

Conflict — dissolution year

The vault header and Discogs list the band as active 2000–2006 (final release Sun, Burn was 2006). However, themeeksfamilymusic.com (About page) and Creative Loafing Atlanta both describe The Close as disbanding in 2007. The discrepancy may reflect when Sun, Burn was released vs. when the band formally stopped performing. (themeeksfamilymusic.com; Creative Loafing Atlanta)
