Japandorf

Discogs ID: 7320433
Name variations: Prejapandorf
Origin: Japan / Düsseldorf, Germany
Active: c. 2000–2017 (releases)
Label: Grönland Records

Japanese-rooted group operating at the intersection of the Düsseldorf krautrock legacy and the Japanese experimental scene. All four members have sustained ties to Düsseldorf: Kazuyuki Onouchi and Miki Yui are based there; Satoshi Okamoto links via his sub-tle. project; Masaki Nakao co-performs and writes lyrics across both LPs.

The group’s defining relationship is with Klaus Dinger — drummer and co-founder of NEU! and La Düsseldorf, and a driving force in La! NEU?. Dinger’s 1996 Tokyo concerts with La! NEU? appear to have seeded the connection. He died on 21 March 2008 — his Discogs bio explicitly states he died “while preparing the album ‘Japandorf’.” Both Japandorf releases were therefore posthumous collaborations, assembled and released under the stewardship of Kazuyuki Onouchi and Miki Yui.

Discography

Klaus Dinger + Japandorf (2013)

Label: Grönland Records (LPGRON124)
Format: LP
Genres/Styles: Electronic, Rock / Krautrock

Posthumous debut LP. Recorded before Dinger’s 2008 death; released 2013 on Grönland. Eleven tracks including “Immermannstraße,” “Sketch No. 1_b,” “Udon,” “Kittelbach Symphony,” “Spacemelo,” “Cha Cha 2008 (Edit),” and “Andreaskirche.” Two pressings.

Credits across the tracklist distribute music composition among Dinger, Onouchi, and Okamoto; lyrics/words by Nakao, Yui, and Dinger. Dinger plays guitar, bass, and vocals on various tracks. Onouchi handles drums, mix, and recording. Miki Yui plays synth and vocals; Okamoto piano and keyboards.

2000! (2017)

Label: Grönland Records (LPGRON170)
Format: LP (Record Store Day; Limited Edition)
Credit: “Klaus Dinger + Prejapandorf” (early name variant)
Genres/Styles: Electronic, Rock / Krautrock

Recordings from June–September 2000, released for Record Store Day 2017. Three pressings. Track titles document their sessions precisely: “June 2. 2000! Lilienthal Studio,” “Mayday 2000!,” “Untitled, September 12. 2000!” Seven tracks.

Expands the circle considerably: Andreas Reihse (Kreidler, La! NEU?) plays keyboards on four tracks; Viktoria Wehrmeister (La! NEU?, Superbilk, Toresch) contributes vocals and words throughout; Thea Djordjadze, Georgian-German visual artist and Düsseldorf Academy alumna, sings on two tracks; Maki Umehara sings on the opening track; Shinichi Mizoguchi photographs.

Context

Japandorf sits inside the extended Düsseldorf post-krautrock ecosystem seeded by Dinger. The overlapping La! NEU? personnel (Reihse, Wehrmeister) on 2000! confirms the group emerged directly from Dinger’s 2000-era La! NEU? activity. The Japan connection — Onouchi, Nakao, Yui, and Okamoto are all Japanese — echoes the cultural cross-pollination Dinger pursued in Tokyo in 1996.

The name shift from “Prejapandorf” (2000!) to “Japandorf” (the 2013 LP and group name) reflects the chronological inversion of the releases: the 2000! recordings came first but were released second; the group name crystallized only after the material was edited for posthumous release.

Both LPs were mastered by Kai Blankenberg at Skyline Tonfabrik in Düsseldorf.
