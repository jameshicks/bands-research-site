History

Formed in Manchester in 1977. Signed to Factory Records from the outset — all early recordings produced by Martin Hannett (Factory’s house producer). Opened for Talking Heads on their 1979 UK tour, which reportedly influenced Talking Heads toward a funkier sound.

In September 1980, after returning from a US tour with New Order, they recruited Martha Tilson as a co-vocalist — she had just come from the London post-punk scene.

Sextet (Factory Records, Jan 1982) was their first self-produced album and featured Tilson prominently. I’d Like To See You Again (1982) was made after her departure.

Notes on the discography

  • Good Together featured guest vocals from Bernard Sumner and Shaun Ryder on “Good Together” and Denise Johnson throughout; produced in split: some tracks with Bob Kraushaar, others with Julian Mendelsohn.
  • acr:mcr (1990) is a remix/compilation album on A&M — not to be confused with a later release. Guest: Bernard Sumner on “Won’t Stop Loving You” remix. Denise Johnson featured prominently.
  • Up In Downsville includes a Jon Dasilva remix of “27 Forever” and an M People remix of “Mello”.
  • Looking For A Certain Ratio… is a remix compilation on Creation with contributions from Electronic (Sumner/Marr), Graham Massey (808 State), Sub Sub, Way Out West, The Other Two, and others. Liner notes by Tony Wilson.
  • acr:set is an authoritative career retrospective on Mute covering 1979–2008; liner notes by Dave Haslam.
  • ACR Loco features Denise Johnson, Gabe Gurnsey (Factory Floor), Maria Uzor, and Eric Random among contributors.
  • 1982 features vocalist Ellen Beth Abdi prominently and Andrew Meecham (Bizarre Inc) on one track.
  • It All Comes Down To This — produced by Dan Carey (Wet Leg, Black Midi); the core trio of Kerr/Johnson/Moscrop only.

Key Connections

  • Martha Tilson — the Dining Out bridge (Occult Chemistry → ACR)
  • Martin Hannett — produced all early Factory recordings
  • Tony Wilson — Factory Records founder / ACR manager
  • New Order — toured together (US, 1980)
  • Talking Heads — ACR opened for them (1979 UK tour)
  • Simon Topping → T-Coy (with Mike Pickering, later Haçienda DJ / M People)
  • Andy Connell → Swing Out Sister
  • Barry Adamson (Magazine, Bad Seeds) — collaborated on “Dirty Boy” (Mute, 2018 single; also Dirty Boy & Shack Up Remixed, Mute, 2019)
  • Martin Moscrop → Kalima (formerly Swamp Children)
  • Tony Quigley — also core member of Swamp Children and Kalima
  • Swamp Children — Manchester Factory band with heavy ACR overlap; ACR featured uncredited on Taste What’s Rhythm B2 (FBN 16, 1982); Simon Topping co-produced Little Voices (FAC 49, 1981)
  • Denise Johnson — member 1990–2020; also recorded with Primal Scream and New Order; died 27 July 2020
