13 & God

Transatlantic experimental hip-hop collective formed in 2004. A collaboration between members of Themselves and Subtle (from Anticon, Bay Area California) and members of The Notwist (from Weilheim, Germany). Releases co-issued on Anticon and Alien Transistor. Discogs artist ID: 276028.

Overview

13 & God grew out of a meeting between the Anticon collective and the Weilheim scene that orbited The Notwist and their own label, Alien Transistor. The project brought together Dose One (Adam Drucker) and Jel (Jeffrey Logan) — the core of Themselves — with The Notwist’s Markus Acher, Micha Acher, and Martin Gretschmann (best known under his solo alias Console). Dax Pierson (Subtle) and Jordan Dalrymple are also credited as full members, making for a seven-piece transatlantic ensemble.

The debut self-titled album (2005) was preceded by the “Men Of Station” 12” single, both co-released simultaneously on Anticon and Alien Transistor. The album melds abstract hip-hop production — chopped loops, beatmaking — with the Weilheim post-rock and electronic idiom that defined The Notwist records like Neon Golden. Gretschmann handled mixing. Remixes on the “Men Of Station” single came from Anticon labelmates Why? and Alias (3), plus Hrvatski.

A live album, Live In Japan, was released in 2008 on the Japanese label Art Union, documenting the band’s touring activity.

The second album Own Your Ghost followed in 2011, six years after the debut — a long gap explained in part by Dax Pierson’s serious injury in a tour bus accident in January 2005, which left him paralyzed from the chest down. Despite this, Pierson is credited as a performer on Own Your Ghost. Production on the second album was more elaborate: Oliver Zülch (of the Weilheim/Munich scene) served as additional producer and recording engineer alongside Jay Pellicci; a small chamber ensemble contributed saxophone, bass clarinet, trombone, viola, and violin; and Lyrics were co-written by Drucker and Markus Acher. Adam Drucker also handled artwork for the record. A companion European pressing (Oldage) was issued on Alien Transistor the same year.

The project appears to have been dormant since 2011.
