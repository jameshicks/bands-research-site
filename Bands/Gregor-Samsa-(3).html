Gregor Samsa (3)

Gregor Samsa (3)

Sep 28, 20268 min read

Gregor Samsa (3)

Discogs ID: 391694
Active: 2000–2009 (reunion/reissue activity 2025)
Origin: Richmond, Virginia, USA
Genres: Post-rock, chamber pop, ambient
Name variations: GS
Name source: Gregor Samsa is the protagonist of Franz Kafka’s The Metamorphosis.

Richmond post-rock/chamber pop ensemble fronted by Champ Bennett. Distinguished by a large rotating cast of contributors — string players, upright bass, drums — and carefully titled releases whose names encode their total runtimes (27:36, 55:12, etc.). Core membership centred on the Bennett family (Champ, Billy, and Nikki) with an extended circle that at its peak included Toby Driver and Mia Matsumiya of Kayo Dot.

Biography

Formation and Early Releases (2000–2002)

The band formed in Richmond around 2000. An initial lineup included Champ Bennett alongside his brother Billy Bennett, future wife Nikki King (2) (born Nikki King, later Nikki King Bennett), Amber Blankenship (strings), Jason Laferrera (bass/engineer), Nathan Altice, Nick Wurz, Earl Yevak, and Tony Thaxton.

A split CDr with The Silent Type appeared in 2001 as a self-released limited edition. Notably, Amber Blankenship was simultaneously a member of The Silent Type, connecting the two acts. Nathan Altice and Nick Wurz also had ties to The Silent Type.

The 2002 self-titled EP on Iodine Recordings (cat: IO-06) set an early template: three tracks titled simply “O,” “OO,” and “OOO [Dm0],” mastered by Matthew Azevedo (who also records as Retribution Body). Personnel were billed collectively with last names only on sleeve.

27:36 and European Exposure (2003–2004)

The 27:36 EP (Iodine IOD 10, 2003) contained three untitled tracks and became the first release to reach a European audience via the Luxembourg label Own Records, followed by German repackaging on Mi Amante / Riptide Recordings as 27:36 + 2DOT (with additional material, pre-production by Alan Weatherhead, post-production by Colin Decker, and direct metal mastering by Hendrik Pauler of Stockfisch Records). This German pressing went to four pressings across 2004–2006.

Robotic Empire Split (2005)

A 12” split with Red Sparowes on Robotic Empire (ROBO-044) brought the band into the orbit of the Los Angeles post-rock and post-metal world. GS contributed two tracks to side B: “Young & Old / Divine Longing” (mixed by Brian Paulson) and ”+ Rock Song.” Jason Laferrera recorded the GS tracks; Nikki King (2) arranged strings; Josh Graham designed the artwork. The split was pressed at least five times through 2006.

The Kora Records Era: 55:12 (2006)

55:12 (The Kora Records TKR 04) was the band’s first full-length album; the title encodes its total runtime of 55 minutes and 12 seconds. Self-produced (Gregor Samsa credited as producer), engineered by Jason Laferrera. Eight named tracks including “Makeshift Shelters,” “Even Numbers,” and “Young & Old” (a re-recording of the split track). European pressings issued on Own Records.

Rest and the Expanded Ensemble (2007–2008)

Rest (The Kora Records TKR 011) represented the largest ensemble to that point. The core recording featured Champ Bennett as engineer and Alan Weatherhead as mixer. New faces included Toby Driver and Mia Matsumiya (both of Kayo Dot), Cory Bise, Jeremiah Klinger, Debra Wassum, Andrew Miller, filmmaker Rick Alverson, and Alex Aldi (engineer/performer). Mastering by Mark Christensen.

The album received unusually wide physical distribution: first issued in South Korea on Talk To Me Records (2007), then in Poland on Gusstaff Records and in Europe on Own Records (both 2008), with four separate US pressings on The Kora Records through 2008. Tracks include “The Adolescent,” “Ain Leuh,” “Jeroen Van Aken,” and “Du Meine Leise.”

Over Air and Dissolution (2009)

Over Air (The Kora Records TRK 015) was an enhanced, limited, numbered CD featuring alternate versions/mixes of Rest tracks (tracks 1–6 credited to Cory Bise, Jeremiah Klinger, Mia Matsumiya, Nikki King, The Brothers Bennett, and Toby Driver), produced and recorded by Dutch producers Berry Kamer and Maurice Woestenburg. Champ Bennett and Billy Bennett appear uncredited.

Two bonus collaborative tracks rounded out the disc: “XXX” with Tiempo (Champ Bennett, Earl Yevak, Nikki King) and “Lean” with Robert Donne. Video content included a Paris 2008 live film directed by Rick Alverson and a music video for “Jeroen Van Aken” filmed by Benlx.

No further releases appeared until 2025.

Return: 40:13.5 (2025)

A 12” EP titled 40:13.5 appeared on Iodine Recordings (IOD-27), marking a return to the original label. Issued in two pressings, the five tracks are titled O, OO, OOO, OOOO, OOOOO — consciously echoing the 2001–2002 releases. Listed on Discogs as a compilation/remastered release.

Connections

  • Red Sparowes — shared a 12” split (Robotic Empire, 2005); Josh Graham and Bryant Clifford Meyer from Red Sparowes later appeared in other projects
  • Kayo Dot / Toby Driver — Driver and Matsumiya performed on Rest (2008), linking GS to the avant-prog world
  • The Silent Type — split partners (2001); Amber Blankenship was simultaneously a member of both bands; Nathan Altice and Nick Wurz also in both
  • Tiempo — side project of Champ Bennett, Earl Yevak, and Nikki King; contributes “XXX” track on Over Air
  • The Brothers Bennett — credited unit consisting of Champ and Billy Bennett; appears on Over Air
  • Iodine Recordings — Philadelphia indie label (home to Hopesfall, Off Minor); GS were among their most exported acts via European licensing
