What? Noise

Sound engineering collective and occasional live act. Formed late 1980s, based at Strawberry Studios and Yellow Two Studios, Stockport. Discogs artist ID: 159347.

Also known as: What Noise, What Noise?

Members: Chris Nagle, Julia Adamson, Tim Harris.

Overview

What? Noise were three sound engineers — Tim Harris, Julia Adamson, and Chris Nagle — working out of Strawberry Studios and Yellow Two Studios in Stockport in the late 1980s. Alongside their professional studio work producing and engineering records for other acts, the trio constituted a performing and recording group in their own right. They wrote and self-produced all their material.

Live, they played stylophone, guitars, and whirly sound hoses while programming drums and sequencers using C Lab software (later marketed as Logic Pro) on Atari computers, producing finished masters at their studio base. The combination of unconventional sound sources, digital sequencing and studio-grade production values gave the group its industrial/leftfield character.

Their brief but concentrated live presence peaked between 1989 and 1990: a UK support tour with Fishbone in 1989, and a university circuit tour in 1990 alongside Finitribe (then promoting their Fat album on One Little Indian / Finiflex). The Finitribe tour connection is directly reflected in What? Noise’s own Fat album being released on Brave Records — a sublabel of One Little Indian. Manchester concerts included performances with Dub Sex and Flatback Four.

The group released one original single and one original LP during their active period (1989–1990). Two later CDr releases on Julia Adamson’s own Invisiblegirl Records compiled archival What? Noise material: a 2006 reissue doubling the Vein EP with a companion Lose And Win EP, and a 2010 four-track CDr titled Nuance.

Release Notes

Vein (1989, Cut Deep)

What? Noise’s debut: a 12” 45 RPM single on the Cut Deep label (cat. cut 12002). Genres: Electronic / Rock; styles: Indie Rock, Industrial. The pressing was cut at MPO (matrix etchings “MPO CUT 12 002 A1” / “MPO CUT 12 002 B1”). The spine carries catalogue reference “02-17540” and a pan-European distribution code “EFA MS 17510 02”, indicating German distribution through EFA. Written-By and Producer credited to What? Noise.

Fat (1990, Brave Records)

What? Noise’s only LP. Ten tracks, vinyl LP, cut at Townhouse using DMM (Direct Metal Mastering) — matrix etchings “MPO BND 8 LP A¹ TOWNHOUSE DMM” / “MPO BND 8 LP B¹ TOWNHOUSE DMM”. Label: Brave Records (BND 8 LP), a sublabel of One Little Indian. Additional credits: sleeve design by Paul Khera; management by Paula Greenwood. Written-By and Producer: What? Noise. Genres: Electronic / Rock; styles: Indie Rock, Industrial, Leftfield. This LP is the same Fat that Finitribe were touring to promote in 1990 per the Discogs profile — though Finitribe released their own Finitribe Fat on One Little Indian directly; the naming coincidence and shared label parentage is notable.

Connections

  • Chris Nagle — engineer/producer; Factory Records / Hannett era 1979–1981; Biting Tongues Compressor (1987); The Charlatans Some Friendly (1990)
  • Julia Adamson — first female engineer at Strawberry Studios; later The Fall (1995–2001); runs Invisiblegirl Records
  • Martin Hannett — Nagle’s primary earlier collaborator; shared Strawberry Studios infrastructure
  • Factory Records — Nagle’s institutional home predating What? Noise; several Factory-adjacent acts recorded at What? Noise’s Stockport studios
  • Biting Tongues — Nagle produced Compressor (FAC 188, 1987); performed in the same Manchester milieu
  • Dub Sex — shared Manchester concert bills; Chris Nagle mixed Dub Sex’s 1987 self-titled release (Skysaw Records)
  • Finitribe — shared One Little Indian label umbrella; toured together 1990
  • Fishbone — What? Noise supported on UK tour, 1989
  • The Charlatans — Nagle produced their debut LP Some Friendly (1990) contemporaneously with What? Noise’s Fat
  • One Little Indian — parent of Brave Records; label home for Fat (1990)
