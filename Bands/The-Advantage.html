The Advantage

Discogs ID: 652023
Alias: Generic (2)
Origin: Nevada City / Sacramento, California
Active: 1998–present
Genres: Math rock

Sacramento-area math rock band specializing in covers of NES (Nintendo Entertainment System) 8-bit video game music. Formed 1998 in Nevada City. The band originally included Nick Rogers (2) and an uncredited member, with Spencer Seim on drums; they re-formed as a stable quartet and released their debut on 5 Rue Christine in 2004.

The key cross-pollination: Spencer Seim (drums) and Carson McWhirter (bass) are simultaneously the rhythm section of Hella — both bands were active simultaneously from the Sacramento scene on the same label.
