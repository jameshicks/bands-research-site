Overview

The Black Keys are a blues rock duo formed in Akron, Ohio in 2001, comprising Dan Auerbach on guitar and vocals and Patrick Carney on drums. Beginning with a raw, lo-fi approach on independent labels — Alive Records and Fat Possum in the early 2000s, then Epitaph — they amassed a prolific catalogue across two decades before crossing into mainstream success in the 2010s, their busiest release period. Recurring partnerships with producer Danger Mouse and engineer Tchad Blake shaped the more polished, groove-heavy sound of their later work, and Nonesuch became their primary label home with 35 catalogue entries. Auerbach also runs Easy Eye Sound, which appears across a handful of the band’s more recent releases.
