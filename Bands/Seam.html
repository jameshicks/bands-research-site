Seam

Alternative/indie rock band. Formed Chapel Hill, North Carolina, 1990 (or 1991 per some pressings); relocated to Chicago, IL after debut. Discogs artist ID: 330670. Active 1990–1998; briefly reunited 2006.

Overview

Seam was founded in Chapel Hill by Sooyoung Park (vocals, guitar) — previously of Bitch Magnet — with bassist Lexi Mitchell (who had co-founded an earlier band, the Lilies, with Stephen Immerwahr at Oberlin College) and drummer Mac McCaughan (also of Superchunk, co-founder of Merge Records). Their debut LP Days Of Thunder (Homestead Records, 1991) was a 7” single pressed in the US; Headsparks followed in 1992 on City Slang as Seam’s first full CD album.

After the debut, Park and Mitchell moved to Chicago. McCaughan departed; Bundy K. Brown (guitar) and John McEntire (drums) — both of the forming Tortoise — briefly joined for the Kernel EP (1993, Touch And Go / City Slang / Trash Flow Records). Brown and McEntire then returned to Tortoise; Craig White (guitar) and Bob Rising (drums) replaced them for the second LP The Problem With Me (Touch And Go, 1993). For the third LP Are You Driving Me Crazy? (Touch And Go, 1995), Park assembled an entirely new lineup: Reg Shrader (guitar), William Shin (bass), and Chris Manfrin (drums). This final lineup — minus Shrader, who was replaced by John Lee in 1999 — also recorded The Pace Is Glacial (Touch And Go, 1998). Park/Shin/Manfrin/Lee reunited to play Touch And Go’s 25th Anniversary Party in 2006.

Production throughout was handled primarily by Brad Wood, Chicago’s key indie engineer of the period (also: Exile In Guyville, Sunny Day Real Estate, Veruca Salt). The later albums were recorded and mixed by Brian Paulson (The Pace Is Glacial) and Wood (Are You Driving Me Crazy?). City Slang served as Seam’s European home throughout; ché Records issued one UK single in 1995.

Mitchell and Park co-wrote “New Year’s,” which Codeine recorded for Frigid Stars LP (Glitterhouse/Sub Pop, 1990) — establishing the Oberlin-to-Chicago axis that runs through Seam’s early biography.
