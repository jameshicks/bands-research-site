The Flying Hearts

Loose ensemble / compositional vehicle. New York City. Active approximately 1975–1979. Discogs artist ID: 1260921

Overview

The Flying Hearts were not a fixed band but a rotating collective assembled by Arthur Russell as his primary vehicle for loft-music and minimalist composition during the mid-to-late 1970s. The name appears on concert flyers and organizational materials from that period rather than on commercially released records — their sole document, Instrumentals, was released under Russell’s name alone, first in 1984 and again in 2017.

Russell used the ensemble to pursue a strain of downtown minimalism that differed from the Reich/Glass school: slower tempos, more tonal ambiguity, a willingness to blur loft jazz, chamber music, and rock instrumentation. Players were drawn from the interconnected worlds of No Wave, Talking Heads, Glass ensemble alumni, and the loft jazz scene. The lineup shifted across sessions; no single configuration was canonical.

The Discogs artist page lists a core membership drawn from Russell’s regular collaborators of the period: Arthur Russell, Jerry Harrison, Rhys Chatham, Peter Gordon, David Van Tieghem, Ernie Brooks, Peter Zummo, and Jon Gibson (2). Release credits extend this further to Bill Ruyle, Jon Sholle, Larry Saltzman, Glen Lomoro, Garrett List, Andy Paley, and Julius Eastman (conducting). David Byrne is associated with the project in historical accounts but does not appear in the Discogs credits for Instrumentals.

The Instrumentals Sessions (1975–1979)

The recordings were made across multiple sessions between 1975 and 1979. The 2017 Audika reissue credits the material as “Instrumentals (1975)” for the core Volume 1/Volume 2 content, with two additional pieces — “Sketch For ‘The Face of Helen’” and “Reach One (With Two Fender Rhodes)” (1973) — appended.

The 1984 pressing (Instrumentals, 1974 - Vol. 2) released only the Part I / Part II / Part III material corresponding to sessions dated June 23, 1977 and May 10, 1978 in its tracklisting. The 2017 Audika reissue (Instrumentals, cat. AU-1016-1) expanded the program to a double LP covering both “Volume 1” (10 parts, all Flying Hearts ensemble) and “Volume 2” (Parts I–III with ensemble, plus the CETA Orchestra material conducted by Julius Eastman, plus the “Face of Helen” sketch and “Reach One”).

Label Context

The 1984 original appeared on Another Side, a sublabel of Les Disques du Crépuscule (Discogs label ID 11143, parent 4718). The Crépuscule catalog number follows the TWI matrix system typical of the label. The Instrumentals material was positioned alongside the label’s avant-garde and post-punk catalogue in an era when Crépuscule was actively documenting New York downtown experimentalism.

The 2017 reissue was produced by Steve Knutson for Audika Records, the label he founded specifically to preserve Arthur Russell’s catalog.

Notes and Discrepancies

  • The Discogs Flying Hearts artist page (ID 1260921) lists only “Ballad Of The Lights” (2010) in its own discography. Instrumentals is credited to Arthur Russell as primary artist, not to The Flying Hearts as a band.
  • The Discogs Flying Hearts member list omits Jon Sholle, Bill Ruyle, Jon Gibson (2), and others who appear in the actual release credits — the listed membership (Russell, Harrison, Chatham, Gordon, Van Tieghem, Brooks, Zummo, Larry Saltzman) appears to be incomplete or drawn from a subset of sessions.
  • David Byrne is noted in some historical accounts as a participant but does not appear in either the 1984 or 2017 Discogs release credits.
  • The title “1974” in the 1984 LP title (Instrumentals, 1974 - Vol. 2) appears inconsistent with session dates listed on the tracks (1977, 1978). The 2017 Audika reissue gives the material a “(1975)” date. This discrepancy is unresolved in the Discogs data.
  • Glen Lomoro appears as “Glen Lomaro” (ANV) on the 2017 pressing tracklist.
