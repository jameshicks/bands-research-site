The Wedding Present

Leeds indie rock band formed 1985. Discogs artist ID: 243285. Led by David Gedge (vocals/guitar). Emerged from the Leeds scene into national prominence via Reception Records and later RCA. Known for melodic guitar-driven indie and prolific single output (famously charted 12 singles in 1992). On Where Were You- (Independent Music From Leeds 1978-1989) with “You Should Always Keep In Touch With Your Friends” (CD2, track 18).
