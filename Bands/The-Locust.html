The Locust

Noise-punk / grindcore / avant-garde group from San Diego, California. Active 1994–2022 (Discogs lists 1996–2025 based on release dates). Discogs artist ID: 125425.

Known for ultra-short, frenetic songs, insect costumes, harsh synthesizer textures layered over grindcore riffing, and confrontational stage presence. The band stood at the intersection of San Diego hardcore, power electronics, and experimental noise, and were central to the Gold Standard Laboratories / Three One G scene.

Biography by Era

Origins (1994–1996)

Formed in San Diego, California around 1994, with the earliest Discogs release date of 1996. The initial lineup included Justin Pearson (bass/vocals), David Astor (drums), Gabe Serbian (guitar), and Bobby Bray (guitar). Jimmy LaValle (later of The Album Leaf) also appeared in early lineups. Keyboard player Joey Karam joined, giving the band its signature synth-noise attack on top of punishing grind riffs.

Emerged from the same San Diego underground that produced Swing Kids (2), The Crimson Curse, and the early Three One G roster. First release: a split 5” picture disc with Jenny Piccolo on Three One G (1996, cat. #4).

Gold Standard Laboratories era (1996–2003)

The bulk of the band’s catalog came out on Gold Standard Laboratories (GSL), the San Diego/Boulder label run by (co-owned after 2001 by The Mars Volta’s Omar Rodríguez-López). Key releases:

  • 1996 split 10” with Man Is The Bastard on King Of The Monsters
  • 1997 self-titled 7” (GSL 09) — debut proper
  • 1998 self-titled LP (GSL 15, 45 RPM) — 15 tracks, grindcore/hardcore
  • 2000 split 7” with Arab On Radar (GSL 29)
  • 2000 remix 12” Well I’ll Be A Monkey’s Uncle (GSL 32) — remixes by Bastard Noise / Eric Wood, Kid606, Christoph De Babalon, and others; exposed the band to the breakcore/IDM world
  • 2001 Flight Of The Wounded Locust 7” (GSL 41)
  • 2002 split 7” with Melt-Banana (GSL 48)

The band’s visual identity — full-body insect costumes designed to resemble locusts — became established in this period. Songs rarely exceeded 90 seconds.

Major-label push (2003)

Plague Soundscapes (2003, Anti-, cat. 86667-2) was the band’s first full-length on a larger label (Anti- is an Epitaph imprint). 23 tracks, total runtime ~20 minutes. Produced without individual credit on the Discogs entry. This was their most widely heard album and received coverage in mainstream music press.

Ipecac / late period (2005–2007)

Safety Second, Body Last EP (2005, Ipecac Recordings, IPC 61) — produced/engineered by Alex Newport. Marked a slight turn toward more labyrinthine song structures alongside the blasts.

New Erections (2007, Anti-, 86748-2) — the final studio album, produced by Alex Newport. 11 tracks. Guest vocals by Wes Eisold on track 4. The Discogs entry lists the four-piece lineup: Justin Pearson, Joey Karam, Gabe Serbian, Bobby Bray.

Later releases and compilation (2010–2025)

The Peel Sessions (2010, Radio Surgery, 003) — BBC Peel Sessions material on vinyl, 16 tracks.

Molecular Genetics From The Gold Standard Labs (2012, Anti-, 87172-2) — 44-track compilation drawing from the GSL catalog and early material; includes tracks from both self-titled releases and singles. Reissued/repressed in 2025 on Anti-.

The band reportedly disbanded in 2022 per profile notes (“officially disbanded in 2022”), though some activity continued through 2025 per Discogs credits.
