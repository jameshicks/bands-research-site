Institut Für Feinmotorik

Discogs ID: 32884
ANV: Institut Fuer Feinmotorik
Alias: Gunther Zuckmayr
URL: http://www.institut-fuer-feinmotorik.net

German experimental/turntablist group active from the mid-1990s onward. The project works in the gap between minimal electronic music, sound art, and what might be called applied object-noise: six to eight “empty” turntables — carrying no signal-bearing vinyl but instead manipulated lock grooves, bare paper labels, or nothing at all — are supplemented with rubber bands, adhesive tape, cardboard, wire, and miscellaneous detritus to generate acoustic signals without conventional gain-stage input. The group’s Discogs profile describes this as “the acoustic sphere beyond usual gain signals,” in which “elements of noise containing no original sense emerge to significant structures.” One of the foundational acts of the Staubgold aesthetic, appearing as the label’s fifth release in 1998 and returning for a second album in 2002.

Connections

  • Staubgold — primary label; two studio albums (1998 and 2002); the debut Wenig Information: Kein Titel (staubgold 5) is a foundational Staubgold release
  • Joseph Suchy — mastered Penetrans (2002); Cologne-based guitarist and house engineer for much of the early Staubgold catalog
  • Asmus Tietchens — veteran Hamburg experimental/avant-garde composer; wrote liner notes for Abgegriffen (2011)
  • Thomas Brinkmann — appeared alongside IFF on the 1999 Verschiedene: Cd-Projekt compilation; Brinkmann is a recurrent figure in the German minimal techno/turntablist scene
  • Florian Meyer — member; also records as Don’t DJ; member of The Durian Brothers with Marc Matter
  • Marc Matter — member; also records as Voiceover (2); member of The Durian Brothers
  • Marriage Records (US) — released Abgegriffen (2011); an American label reaching into the European experimental scene
  • SWR — German public broadcaster; awarded the Karl-Sczuka-Preis for radio art in 2011 for Die 50 Skulpturen
  • Karl-Sczuka-Preis — annual SWR prize for radio art/Hörspiel; 2011 winner
