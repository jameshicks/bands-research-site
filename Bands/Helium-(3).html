Helium (3)

American indie rock band formed in 1992 in Boston, Massachusetts, disbanded 1998. Discogs artist ID: 198669. Primary vehicle for Mary Timony as songwriter, guitarist, and vocalist, signed to Matador Records for their full studio run. The band’s catalog spans art-punk and guitar-noise to orchestrated psychedelia, bookended by a 2017 box-set retrospective.

Overview

Helium formed around Mary Timony after her time in DC band Autoclave (2). The earliest documented recording is a 1992 7” on Warped Records, with Brian Dunton on bass and Shawn Devlin on drums. By 1993 they had released the Hole In The Ground EP on Boston’s Pop Narcotic label, produced by Adam Lasus, which brought them to Matador’s attention. From Pirate Prude (1994) onward, all releases came out on Matador.

The classic Helium lineup — Timony, Ash Bowie (bass, accordion), and Shawn Devlin (drums) — solidified by The Dirt Of Luck (1995). Brian Dunton held the bass role on Pirate Prude and appeared on Pat’s Trick; Bowie took over fully from The Dirt Of Luck and appears on all subsequent records.

The final album, The Magic City (1997), marked a shift toward denser orchestration, with Mitch Easter co-producing and contributing mandolin, pedal steel guitar, slide guitar, backing vocals, and guitar. Chris Stamey provided technical assistance on the same sessions. The band dissolved in 1998.

A retrospective box set, Ends With And, was released by Matador in 2017 (OLE-1108-1), alongside newly remastered LP reissues of The Dirt Of Luck and The Magic City & No Guitars.

Connections

  • Matador Records — label for all studio albums (1994–1997) and reissues
  • Pop Narcotic — debut EP label (1993)
  • Adam Lasus — producer and engineer on Hole In The Ground, Pirate Prude, Pat’s Trick (assist), and The Dirt Of Luck (co-producer); also recorded as “Adam ‘Red’ Lasus” and used the alias Red (40) as engineer credit
  • Mitch Easter — co-producer and session musician on The Magic City; previously produced R.E.M.’s early records; also produced Ex Hex’s Rips (2014)
  • Chris Stamey — technical assistance on The Magic City; member of The dB’s; Stamey and Easter previously bandmates in Sneakers (12)
  • Mary Timony — founder, primary songwriter, guitarist, vocalist; continued solo on Matador, then via Wild Flag, Ex Hex, Hammered Hulls
  • Ash Bowie — bassist from The Dirt Of Luck onward; accordion on The Magic City
  • Shawn Devlin — drummer throughout
  • Brian Dunton — original bassist; departed before The Dirt Of Luck
