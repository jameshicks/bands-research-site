The Obsessed

American doom metal band from Potomac, Maryland. Discogs artist ID: 311946. Alias: Spirit Caravan (Discogs lists them as the same entity, reflecting the 2016 lineup-continuity transition). Led throughout by guitarist and vocalist Scott “Wino” Weinrich (ID: 327269).

The Obsessed are one of the foundational American doom metal acts, pre-dating most of the genre’s canonical landmarks. Formed in the late 1970s/early 1980s in the Maryland suburbs of Washington DC, they built a following in both the Maryland heavy underground and DC’s hardcore punk scene before Weinrich departed to join Saint Vitus in 1986. A Hellhound Records deal prompted a full reunion in 1990, resulting in three studio albums through 1994. After a 2016 permanent reunion the band signed to Relapse Records and has since released two more studio albums. (Sources: Discogs artist 311946; Wikipedia: The Obsessed; Wikipedia: Scott Weinrich.)

History

Formation and Early Period (1976–1986)

The band formed originally as Warhorse in 1976 in Potomac, Maryland, with Weinrich on guitar. By the late 1970s it was known as the Leather Nunz before settling on The Obsessed, with a lineup of Weinrich (guitar/vocals), Mark Laue (bass), and Dave Flood / “Dave The Slave” (drums), plus early members including guitarist/vocalist John Reese and later vocalists Vance Bockis and Norman Lawson.

In 1983 they released the “Sodden Jackal” 7” (three tracks, Invictus Records GI/104 — the Discogs primary release title is The Obsessed). The lineup at this point was Weinrich (guitar/vocals), Mark Laue as “Professor Dark” (bass), and Dave The Slave (drums). A track, “Concrete Cancer,” appeared on Metal Blade’s Metal Massacre VI compilation; the band also self-released a demo of that track in 1984. In 1985 they recorded a full album at Ambient Studios, College Park, Maryland, with drummer Ed Gulli replacing Flood — but could not secure a release deal and broke up shortly after.

Saint Vitus Period / Tape Trading (1986–1989)

Weinrich moved to California to join Saint Vitus, recording three studio albums (Born Too Late 1986, Mournful Cries 1988, V 1990), a live album, and an EP. Meanwhile the 1985 Obsessed tape circulated in tape-trading circles. German doom label Hellhound Records — which had signed Saint Vitus — offered to release it.

First Reunion: Hellhound and Columbia (1990–1995)

The 1985 recordings were released as The Obsessed (Hellhound, 1990). To support the release Weinrich reformed the band with drummer Greg Rogers and bassist Scott Reeder (who replaced Danny Hood, killed in a motorcycle accident shortly after joining). Weinrich then left Saint Vitus and made The Obsessed his full-time band.

Lunar Womb followed in 1991 (Hellhound), produced collectively by Weinrich, Reeder, Rogers, and Mathias Schneeberger (engineer). Scott Reeder subsequently left to join Kyuss, replaced by Guy Pinhas. The Pinhas lineup signed to Columbia Records. The Church Within was released in 1994, produced by the band and MC Snoob (Mathias Schneeberger), with Joshua Sarubin as Columbia executive producer. Columbia pulled promotion as the album was being released; it was a commercial failure. The band soon dissolved and Weinrich went through a period of substance abuse.

A final 7” EP, Altamont Nation (Bong Load Records, 1995), closed out the era; it was produced by Rob Schnapf and Tom Rothrock.

Aftermath and Anthology (1996–2010)

In 1999 Southern Lord released Incarnate, an odds-and-ends anthology spanning 1983–1994, compiled by Greg Anderson and Stephen O’Malley. It drew on multiple eras and producers (Pat Lydon, Rob Schnapf, Tom Rothrock, Scott Reeder, Melvins/Dale Crover for one track). Doom Records (2) also issued live recordings from 1992 (Live At The Wax Museum) and archival material.

Weinrich formed Shine (later Spirit Caravan) and The Hidden Hand, and rejoined Saint Vitus in reunion capacity from 2009. Tolotta Records issued a reissue of the 1985/1990 album with live 1984 bonus tracks in 2000.

Permanent Reunion (2016–present)

In February 2016 Weinrich announced that the Spirit Caravan lineup would adopt The Obsessed name. The reunion lineup featured Spirit Caravan bassist Dave Sherman (4) and drummer Brian Costantino. Sacred was released in April 2017 on Relapse Records (the band’s first studio album in 23 years), produced by the trio. For Gilded Sorrow (Ripple Music, 2024) the lineup had shifted to Weinrich, guitarist Jason Taylor (7), bassist Chris Angleberger, and drummer Brian Costantino; Taylor and Frank Marchand co-produced with Weinrich.

In July 2025 Brian Costantino departed and was replaced by Bob Pantella (Monster Magnet / The Atomic Bitchwax). As of June 2026, a new album titled Live Fast – Love Hard – Die Free is in recording for a tentative 2027 release. (Wikipedia: The Obsessed.)
