Foxygen

Indie rock duo from Los Angeles. Discogs ID: 2769573. Active 2007–2022 on Jagjaguwar.

Members Jonathan Rado and Sam France. Hang (2017, Jagjaguwar) featured orchestral arrangements written and conducted by Trey Pollard, with the Spacebomb string/brass pool; arrangements recorded by Adrian Olsen.
