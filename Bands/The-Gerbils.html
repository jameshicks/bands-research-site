The Gerbils

Discogs ID: 351425 Genres: Indie Rock, Power Pop Years active: 1993/1995–1999, 2001 (tour), 2004–2006 Origin: Ruston, Louisiana → Athens, Georgia, US

The Gerbils were an American indie rock / power pop band, part of the Elephant Six Collective. They formed in Ruston, Louisiana — where founding members Scott Spillane, John D’Azzo, and Will Westbrook were college roommates — and relocated to Athens, Georgia. The band’s active years were punctuated by hiatuses: they played a farewell show in 1999, reunited to tour with Elf Power in 2001 following the release of The Battle of Electricity, and again became active in 2004. Guitarist Will Westbrook died on 11 December 2006, and the band disbanded permanently. They released two full-length albums and three singles across their run. Per Wikipedia, a cellist named Penny Burbank was an early member, though she does not appear in Discogs credits.
