Label (reissue): Soleilmoon Recordings

Overview

Sickness of Snakes was a one-off collaboration between Boyd Rice (NON), Geoff Rushton (John Balance of Coil), and Peter Christopherson (also of Coil, and formerly Throbbing Gristle). Their only recording appeared in 1985 as the B-side of a split mini-LP with Current 93 on the Belgian label L.A.Y.L.A.H. Antirecords (catalog LAY 14). The A-side, a 12-minute piece credited to “93 Current 93,” draws on largely the same personnel pool.

The project predates Coil’s fully autonomous phase and sits at the intersection of the early UK industrial/post-industrial scene and the American noise/provocateur strand represented by Boyd Rice. Rice was the sole surviving member at the time of the 2013 Soleilmoon reissue — both Balance (d. November 13, 2004) and Christopherson (d. November 24, 2010) had died in the intervening years — and he wrote the liner notes for the reissue, making him the de facto curator/archivist of the project’s legacy.

The 2013 Soleilmoon pressing (SOL 180) is a single-sided 12” reissuing only the Sickness of Snakes material from the original split, not the Current 93 side.

The Original Split: Current 93 Connection

The 1985 vinyl presents a significant cross-pollination. Side A is credited to Current 93 but its performers overlap extensively with Sickness of Snakes — Boyd Rice and John Balance appear on both sides of the record. The credit “93 Current 93 May Be” signals this was a loose collective rather than the defined Current 93 lineup. David Tibet (Current 93’s primary figure), Steve Ignorant (Crass), and Steven Stapleton (Nurse With Wound) also performed on Side A, marking this record as a gathering-point for the early UK experimental underground.

Soleilmoon Context

The 2013 Soleilmoon reissue (SOL 180) arrived in a cluster of Coil-adjacent posthumous releases on the label. Charles Powne (ID 970976), the label’s owner, contributed insert photography to the 2013 pressing and is the same person credited in the Soleilmoon company credits. Boyd Rice wrote the liner notes, placing himself as the project’s surviving spokesman. The pressing is single-sided, limited, and numbered — a format Soleilmoon used for archival/collectible reissues.

L.A.Y.L.A.H. Antirecords

The original Belgian label L.A.Y.L.A.H. Antirecords (ID 10713, catalog prefix LAY) released the original split in 1985. The catalog number LAY 14 places this near the middle of the label’s run. The label name is a reference to the Thelemic/Crowleyite “Liber AL” tradition shared by much of the surrounding scene.

Open Questions

  • Recording dates for the Sickness of Snakes tracks — the 1985 release date does not mean 1985 recording; session date unknown
  • Who specifically compiled and licensed the 2013 Soleilmoon reissue — Boyd Rice’s liner notes suggest his involvement, but the legal/archival process is undocumented in Discogs
  • Boyd Rice’s specific role in the sessions beyond “performer” — guitar, noise, voice?
  • Whether “The Pope Held Upside Down” omits Boyd Rice’s writing credit deliberately or by error (only Balance and Christopherson are listed as Written-By for that track)
  • Whether there are any unreleased or circulating recordings beyond the three B-side tracks
  • Full context of the Crowley/Thelema connection between label name (L.A.Y.L.A.H.) and the band’s personnel (all of whom were involved with occult-adjacent networks)
