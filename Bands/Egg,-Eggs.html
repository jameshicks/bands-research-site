Egg, Eggs

US free rock / experimental ensemble. Discogs artist ID: 2055209.

Overview

A loose collective that recorded two albums for Feeding Tube Records: Albino Bigot (2011) and Vole Crusher (2012). Personnel overlaps significantly with Sunburned Hand Of The Man and related Massachusetts/New England experimental circles.
