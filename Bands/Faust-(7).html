Overview

Faust (7) is the post-2004 continuation of the krautrock group led by Jean-Hervé Peron, with drummer Werner “Zappi” Diermaier participating until 2021, alongside rotating English and French collaborators including Amaury Cambuzat, Geraldine Swayne, Gunther Wüsthoff, and James Hodson. This incarnation is distinct from the parallel Faust (6) entity led by Hans Joachim Irmler, representing the division of the original band into two separate ongoing projects. The catalogue spans three decades — eight releases in the 2000s, seven in the 2010s, and two in the 2020s — with Bureau B and Dirter Promotions as the principal label homes. The group has been notably collaborative, sharing credits across multiple releases with Keiji Haino, Nurse With Wound, Family Fodder, Kommissar Hjuler Und Frau, and producer Johann Scheerer.
