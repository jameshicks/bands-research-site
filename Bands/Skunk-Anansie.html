Skunk Anansie

UK alternative rock / hard rock band. Formed London, 1994. Discogs artist ID: 11394. Disbanded April 2001; reunited 2009, still active as of 2025.

Overview

Skunk Anansie formed in London in early 1994, debuting at the Splash Club in March of that year. The name blends “skunk” (added, per the band, “to make the name nastier”) with Anansi, the spider-man figure from Akan folk tradition. The founding lineup was vocalist Skin (Deborah Anne Dyer), guitarist Martin “Ace” Kent, bassist Richard “Cass” Lewis, and drummer Robbie France. France was replaced by Mark Richardson during 1995, after Richardson met the band at the Kerrang! Awards ceremony where they were voted Best New British Band.

Signed to One Little Indian in 1994, the band released three studio albums in the OLI era: Paranoid & Sunburnt (1995), Stoosh (1996), and Post Orgasmic Chill (1999). Stoosh crossed over to major-label distribution via Virgin Records and became the band’s commercial peak in the UK. The band switched fully to Virgin for Post Orgasmic Chill (1999), which was produced and mixed by Andy Wallace. They headlined the Pyramid Stage at Glastonbury in June 1999.

The band split in April 2001. After a reunion confirmed in late 2008, they returned with the best-of Smashes and Trashes (2009) and toured extensively. Their 2009–present run has been on independent labels — Ear Music (Wonderlustre, 2010), the band’s own imprint working with 100% Records (Black Traffic, 2012), Spinefarm Records (Anarchytecture, 2016), and FLG/Frontiers for the seventh studio album The Painful Truth (2025).

Principal songwriters across the catalog: Skin and Len Arran (Discogs: 662791), with band members Ace, Cass, and (from Post Orgasmic Chill) Mark Richardson also credited as co-writers. Len Arran appeared on stage as acoustic guitarist on the 2013 live album An Acoustic Skunk Anansie Live In London. The band have sold approximately five million records worldwide and were named in the Guinness Book of British Hit Singles & Albums (2004) as one of the most successful UK chart acts between 1952–2003.

Wikipedia notes the band is often grouped with the “Britrock” movement rather than Britpop, given their harder sound and explicitly political / feminist-rage content. Skin has described the band as “clit-rock.” (Source: Wikipedia, “Skunk Anansie.“)

Members’ Other Projects

Skin (Deborah Anne Dyer, Discogs: 11539)

Solo career on EMI and V2: Fleshwounds (2003, EMI), Fake Chemical State (2006, V2). Electronic / club work: “Licking Cream” (1999, Epic/TVT), “Carmen Queasy” with Maxim (2000, XL Recordings), guest vocals on numerous dance releases. Solo EPs on Mood Records (4): Breed EP (2015). Continued Italian market releases through 2018–2019.

Martin “Ace” Kent (Discogs: 494421)

Solo album Still Hungry as Ace Sounds (not detailed on Discogs as primary credits; Wikipedia notes it featured Shingai Shoniwa of Noisettes and Skye from Morcheeba). Also in The Screaming Ameebas (Discogs: 2489858). Guitar tutor at Brighton Institute of Modern Music.

Richard “Cass” Lewis (Discogs: 494418)

Played bass on Gary Moore’s Scars (2002, Sanctuary) under the group credit Scars (7) (Discogs: 3706786). Also in Dark City (2) (Discogs: 340903, Virgin 1985–1986) and The Bojangles (Discogs: 425412).

Mark Richardson (5) (Discogs: 494420)

Joined Feeder after the death of their original drummer Jon Lee; extensive catalog with Feeder on Echo label from 1996 onward. Previously in Little Angels (Polydor). Also in Sunna (Discogs: 3853), B.L.O.W. (Discogs: 760715).

Robbie France (Discogs: 923383)

Drummer before Mark Richardson. Substantial pre-Skunk Anansie career: UFO (5), Diamond Head (2), Wishbone Ash, Alphaville, Ellis Beggs & Howard. Died 14 January 2012 in Spain, aged 52.
