Sacred Paws

Glasgow/London indie-rock duo. Discogs artist ID: 3033385. Active 2015–2025, primarily on Rock Action Records.

Overview

Sacred Paws is the project of Eilidh Rodgers (drums, vocals) and Rachel Aggs (guitar, bass, vocals). Both members had prior bands in the Glasgow indie underground — Eilidh through Golden Grrrls, Rachel through Golden Grrrls, Trash Kit, and Shopping (3). Sacred Paws formed in that milieu and released all their music on Rock Action Records (the Mogwai-founded Glasgow label), with US co-releases on Merge Records for the later albums.

Their sound is built around Rachel Aggs’ choppy, angular guitar lines — indebted to post-punk and Afrobeat — set against Eilidh Rodgers’ driving drum parts, with close two-part vocal harmonies throughout. The mood is deliberately joyful and energetic, setting them somewhat apart from the more austere Glasgow post-punk tradition. Later albums added a recurring brass section and string arrangements.

All Rock Action recordings were produced by Tony Doogan, the Glasgow producer most closely associated with the label (Mogwai, Arab Strap, The Delgados).

The Discogs member list also includes Jack Mellin (The Pastels, Spinning Coin) as a live and session contributor, and Moema Meade (Dog Legs, As Ondas) as a member. Ray Aggs (Discogs ID: 12747824) is Rachel Aggs’ primary Discogs entry; ID 2109538 is an earlier duplicate.
