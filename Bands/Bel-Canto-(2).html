Bel Canto (2)

Discogs ID: 26219
Note: Not to be confused with Bel Canto Orchestra (different entity in vault)
Active: 1987–2005 (on Discogs)
Top label: Crammed Discs

Norwegian dream-pop/ethereal trio formed in Bergen. Primary members: Anneli Drecker (vocals), Geir Jenssen (electronics — later of Biosphere), Nils Johansen. One of the most prolific artists on Crammed Discs with 46 releases. Marc Hollander played additional keyboards and percussion on Birds Of Passage (1990) and produced it. Known for lush atmospheric pop bridging post-punk and ambient influences.
