Band Of Outsiders

NYC post-punk band. Discogs artist ID: 851918. Active approximately 1981–2013.

Overview

Band Of Outsiders evolved from the late-1970s CBGB act The Limit, self-releasing the single “Done Away” in 1981. Singer/guitarist Marc Jeffrey was a central figure. Their sound “compared favorably to both Television” and southwestern American acts of the era. Trouser Press The band co-released Far Away In America (Sourmash Records SM-101L, 1984) with Certain General — each band contributed two live and two studio tracks. The L’Invitation Au Suicide release Far Away In America - The Live Side (1985) is the live-tracks portion of that collaboration.
