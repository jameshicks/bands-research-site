Perfect Zebras

UK post-punk / dance band active 1982–1983. Discogs artist ID: 773566

Overview

Perfect Zebras were a British post-punk/new wave group who released two albums and several singles on the independent Focus Records (3) in 1982, before moving to Polydor for a second LP and further singles in 1983. All of their releases were produced by Barry Anthony Andrews (alias Benny Leopard), who also worked with Pete Phipps’s group The Secret (3). The band’s lineup centred on Alan Jarry (vocals), Paul Robertson (guitars), Terry Stephens (saxophone, flute), and Dennis Smith (bass) — Smith having previously played with mod acts Secret Affair and Advertising.

The track “What Dance Is This?” (4:14) first appeared as track A3 on the 1983 LP Zebra (Focus Records FOLP 2 / Polydor). It was licensed almost four decades later for the Cherry Red 3xCD box set Shake The Foundations (Militant Funk & The Post-Punk Dancefloor 1978-1984) (2021), which placed the band alongside A Certain Ratio, 23 Skidoo, Simple Minds, and Medium Medium. The Mixing With Wildlife LP (1982) was recorded partly at Townhouse Studios (indicated by the matrix runouts FOLP.1 A1 TOWNHOUSE).

Discogs lists no other compilations featuring the band, and no post-1983 activity.
