Cassiber

Discogs ID: 217844 Active: 1982–1992 Origin: Germany / UK

Avant-rock/free improvisation group. Core members: Chris Cutler (drums, electronics, voice), Heiner Goebbels (keyboards, sampler, bass, computer), Christoph Anders (vocals, keyboards, guitar), and Alfred Harth (saxophones, trumpet, trombone, clarinet, cello) — the original quartet 1982–1985; continued as trio after Harth’s departure. Albums released on ReR Megacorp. A Face We All Know (1990, ReR CCD) incorporates texts by Thomas Pynchon and Rainald Goetz.

Rolf Riehm — membership status and the “departure” question

Multiple independent sources confirm that Rolf Riehm (15 June 1937 – 3 January 2026) was not a member of Cassiber. The question of his “departure” therefore does not apply in the conventional sense.

His connection to the band’s circle comes from a single pre-Cassiber project: in December 1980, Alfred Harth assembled an ensemble including Heiner Goebbels, Christoph Anders, drummer Paul Lovens, Rolf Riehm (composer/oboist), and trombonist-violinist Annemarie Roelofs for the JAPO release Es herrscht Uhu im Land (1981). That project is documented as a direct precursor to Cassiber’s formation in 1982 — sources describe it as the work whose “musical program led to more focused work in the group Cassiber.” Riehm did not follow into the band; Cassiber formed as a quartet of Harth, Goebbels, Anders, and Chris Cutler.

Wikipedia’s Cassiber article, the Squidco liner-note description for The Cassiber Box (2013), and the Rolf Riehm Wikipedia obituary all list the same four-person core and make no mention of Riehm as a Cassiber member. His own Wikipedia article identifies only one band association: the Sogenanntes Linksradikales Blasorchester (1976–1981). The claim in the existing vault description that Riehm was a Cassiber guitarist appears to be an error — possibly carried over from his involvement on Es herrscht Uhu im Land.

The actual departure that changed the band’s direction was Alfred Harth’s. Harth left Cassiber in 1985 (some sources say 1986) to form avant-rock groups Gestalt et Jive and Vladimir Estragon. After his departure the band continued as a trio (Cutler, Goebbels, Anders). The Free Jazz Collective’s review of The Cassiber Box notes the shift post-Harth toward “more written stuff” rather than improvisational approaches. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassiber (checked 2026-11-22); https://www.freejazzblog.org/2014/03/cassiber-cassiber-box-rer-2013.html; https://www.squidco.com/miva/merchant.mvc?Screen=PROD&Store_Code=S&Product_Code=34264&Category_Code=NEWRCK; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Riehm (checked 2026-11-22)

Conflict note: The existing vault description lists “Rolf Riehm (guitar)” as a Cassiber member. No web source or Discogs credit (per the trawler’s note above) supports this. The most likely explanation is that his name migrated from the Es herrscht Uhu im Land ensemble into Cassiber’s member list in error. The description line should be corrected to remove Riehm from the members list.
