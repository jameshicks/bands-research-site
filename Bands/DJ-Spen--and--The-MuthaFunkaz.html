DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz

Discogs ID: 483952 Also known as: DJ Spen & The Muthafunkaz, DJ Spen & Muthafunkaz, DJ Spen And The MuthaFunkaz, DJ Spen Presents The MuthaFunkaz, Spen & The MuthaFunkaz Members: DJ Spen (Sean Spencer), Gary Hudgins, Gary Deane, Irvin Madden Active: 2004–2022 (on Discogs; based on earliest and latest primary credits)

Combined project of DJ Spen with The MuthaFunkaz — the Baltimore house production collective of Gary Hudgins (keyboards), Gary Deane (executive producer, mastering, songwriter), and Irvin Madden (bass, engineer). The group released on Code Red Recordings (DJ Spen and Deane’s own Baltimore imprint) and on Defected (UK), where they were most internationally prominent.

Key Releases — Credits Detail

Rock The Bellz (Code Red Recordings, 2004) — DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz as primary producers. First release under the combined name.

Gabryelle (Defected, 2005) — presented as “DJ Spen Presents DJ Technic”; Gary Deane, DJ Spen, and Gary Hudgins (as “Gary Hudge”) are co-producers; DJ Technic (Glenn Brand) is the primary producer and mixer. Despite the credit structure, this became the flagship release for the DJ Spen / MuthaFunkaz axis in the UK market.

Holy Ghost (Code Red Recordings, 2005) — Gary Deane and Sean Spencer as executive producers; Gary Hudgins on keyboards and organ solo; Irvin Madden on bass and guitar; Kelly Tribble Spencer among the vocalists.

It’s So Easy (Code Red Recordings, 2007) — DJ Spen & The MuthaFunkaz feat. Ann Nesby; Gary Hudgins on keyboards and vocal arrangements; Irvin Madden on additional keyboards and backing vocals; Erin Madden on backing vocals.

Given Me Joy (Defected, 2008) — presenter credit; Marc Evans as vocalist; Gary Hudgins arranged backing vocals, keyboards, and strings; Irvin Madden on bass, guitar, and backing vocals.

Reach Out For Love (Defected, 2008) — presenter credit; Marc Evans on lead vocals; The MuthaFunkaz as producer and arranged by; Gary Hudgins on keyboards and string arrangements; Irvin Madden on keyboards (additional), guitar, percussion, and backing vocals.
