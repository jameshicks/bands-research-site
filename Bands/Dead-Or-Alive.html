Dead Or Alive

UK post-punk/new wave/pop band, Liverpool. Discogs artist ID: 46720

Overview

Liverpool group fronted by Pete Burns. Began as a dark post-punk act before shifting dramatically toward hi-NRG pop. “I’m Falling” (from their post-punk period) appeared on Moving Away From The Pulsebeat (Post-Punk Britain 1977-1981) (2024). Later known for the 1985 hit “You Spin Me Round (Like a Record).”

Compilation Appearances

  • Moving Away From The Pulsebeat (Post-Punk Britain 1977-1981) — “I’m Falling”
  • Revolutionary Spirit - The Sound Of Liverpool 1976-1988 — track 4-5 “Misty Circles” (pre-ZTT era); Cherry Red CRCDBOX39, 2018

Connections
