Flux Of Pink Indians

Discogs artist ID: 213077
Active: c. 1979–1987 (formed as The Epileptics / The Licks; active as Flux from c. 1980)
Origin: Hertfordshire, UK
Genre: Anarcho-punk; hardcore punk; dub/funk (later)

Anarcho-punk band from Hertfordshire who grew out of the earlier punk group The Epileptics (also known as The Licks). The name variations on Discogs include “A Flux Of Pink Indians” and “Flux O Pink Indians.” Their debut EP Neu Smell (1981) appeared on Crass Records — one of the earliest releases on that label by a non-Crass act — placing them at the centre of the UK anarcho-punk scene around Crass and Dial House. Over three LPs they moved through hardcore punk, experimental noise-and-melody, and finally a mixture of dub, funk, protest song, and percussion. Bassist Derek Birkett co-founded One Little Indian in 1985, the label that would release their final LP and grow into one of the UK’s most prominent independent labels of the 1990s.

History

Origins: The Epileptics / The Licks (c. 1978–1980)

Flux Of Pink Indians grew out of The Epileptics (Discogs ID: 265403; also known as The Licks), a Hertfordshire punk band whose members included Derek Birkett (bass), Colin Latter (vocals), Kev Biscoe (later credited as Kevin Hunter), and Richard Coveney. The Epileptics released the Nineteen Seventies E.P. (1980, Spiderleg Records) and Last Bus To Debden (1981, Spiderleg). A joint cassette, Live And Studio Recordings 1978/1979/1980, was self-released by both The Epileptics and Flux Of Pink Indians together, documenting the transitional period.

The band began operating under the Flux Of Pink Indians name around 1980, releasing a self-distributed cassette simply titled 1980 (Spiderleg Tapes, SPIDLE ONE), containing six tracks including “Tube Disaster” and “Sick Butchers.”

Neu Smell and Crass Records (1981)

In 1981 the band released the Neu Smell EP on Crass Records (cat. no. 321984/2), making them one of the earliest non-Crass acts to appear on the label. The EP’s lineup: Colin Latter (vocals, credited as “Colsk (The Terrible) Latter”), Derek Birkett (bass), Sid Attion (drums, credited as “Sid”), Andy Smith (4) (guitar), and Neil Puncher (guitar). Produced by Penny Rimbaud (Crass drummer; real name Jeremy John Ratter). Engineered by John Loder at Southern Studios.

Strive To Survive (1983)

The debut LP, Strive To Survive Causing Least Suffering Possible (1983, Spiderleg Records), was the band’s hardest-edged statement — full hardcore punk. By this point the lineup used alias credits throughout: Derek Birkett as “Colonel Blimp (3)” (bass), Colin Latter as “Hugh ‘Man’ Bean” (vocals), Martin Wilson (2) as “Len Fairclough” (drums), and Kevin Hunter credited as “Git R. Hero” (guitar). Produced by Penny Rimbaud, credited as “Shaw Ram Buoy.” Engineered by Simaen Skolfield.

The Fucking Cunts Treat Us Like Pricks (1984)

The second LP, The Fucking Cunts Treat Us Like Pricks (1984, Spiderleg Records), moved toward more experimental territory — noise, political lyrics, and melody. Discogs credits for this release carry no individual personnel beyond the band.

Uncarved Block (1986)

The third and final LP, Uncarved Block (1986), was the first release on One Little Indian (co-founded by Derek Birkett and Tim Kelly, both ex-Flux). Credited simply as “Flux” on the release. An ambitious production bringing in an extensive cast of guest collaborators: Adrian Sherwood (featured; producer/dub mixer), Style Scott (Dub Syndicate drummer; featured), Bonjo Iyabinghi Noah (African Head Charge percussionist; credited as “Bonjo I”), Kenny Wellington, Brian Pugsley, Ray Shulman (ex-Gentle Giant; featured), Louise Bell (credited as “Lu”), Martin Wilson (2) (as “Martin”), Coal Bunker (alias of Colin Latter), and others. The record blended protest songs, dub, funk, and percussion — a sharp stylistic departure from the early hardcore material.

Retrospectives

One Little Indian continued to release Flux material after the band’s activity wound down:

  • Neu Smell / Taking A Liberty (1987, One Little Indian) — EP compilation
  • Strive To Survive & Neu Smell (1989, One Little Indian) — double-LP compilation
  • The Fucking Cunts Treat Us Like Pricks & Taking A Liberty (1989, One Little Indian) — compilation
  • Not So Brave (1997, Overground Records) — major retrospective LP compiling tracks from across the catalog; personnel credits span multiple lineups
  • Live Statement (2002, Overground Records) — live recording
  • Fits And Starts (2003, Dr. Strange Records, US) — compilation shared with The Epileptics
  • Angry Songs And Bitter Words (2003, Ruptured Ambitions) — compilation
