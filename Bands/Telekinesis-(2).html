Overview

Telekinesis is a Seattle-based indie rock project operating as the primary alias of Michael Benjamin Lerner, who writes and performs the bulk of the material himself, with a live lineup that has included Chris Staples, David Broecker, and Jonie Broecker. The catalogue spans 2009 to 2019 — nine releases in total, with the weight in the 2010s — anchored at Merge Records (seven works) and supplemented by four releases through Morr Music for European reach. Producer Chris Walla shares credits across sixteen releases, a notably sustained relationship, and Matthew Caws appears on eight, reflecting the project’s deep ties to the Merge Records orbit.

Notes (web)

How Lerner met Chris Walla / origin of the debut production

Lerner and Chris Walla were introduced through Jason McGerr — Death Cab for Cutie’s drummer, who had given Lerner drum lessons from around age 10. The two first met in person at McGerr’s wedding (reported as 2005). (Bothell Reporter, 2011-03-16)

After that they crossed paths briefly backstage at a couple of Death Cab shows, but the connection that led directly to the record was a MySpace message: Walla heard the songs Lerner had posted there and reached out unprompted to say he liked them. At the first show of Death Cab’s Narrow Stairs tour (2008), Walla approached Lerner and said: “Hey, I want to make your record. What do you think?” (Doubtful Sounds interview, 2009-05-28)

Walla also played a key role in getting the debut signed: after recording, he sent the album to Merge Records alongside Lerner’s lawyer (who also worked with Arcade Fire). (Stereogum Turntable Interview)

The Bothell Reporter article additionally notes that Walla produced “both of Telekinesis’ albums” — this conflicts with Discogs credits and Wikipedia, which list Walla producing only the debut Telekinesis! (2009), not subsequent records. The Bothell Reporter piece is from 2011, by which point only 12 Desperate Straight Lines (2010) had been released, so “both albums” may simply refer to those two. Discogs credits for 12 Desperate Straight Lines should be checked to resolve this.

Conflict note: Discogs shows Walla on 16 releases total for the Telekinesis catalogue, but the extent of his production role vs. other credit types (mixing, engineering, guest instruments) across later records is not confirmed from web sources reviewed here.
