The Raybeats

Instrumental neo-surf / post-punk combo. New York City. Active 1979–c.1983. Discogs ID: 512911.

Founded in 1979 by George Scott III, who recruited Don Christensen and Jody Harris (both from James Chance & the Contortions) and Pat Irwin (from 8 Eyed Spy, where Scott was also playing at the same time). Scott was running both bands simultaneously before his death. The Raybeats shifted away from No Wave abrasion toward cleaner melodic surf-influenced instrumental rock. After Scott’s death on 5 August 1980, Danny Amis replaced him on bass; the band recorded the Roping Wild Bears EP (1981) with Amis and continued into the early 1980s.
