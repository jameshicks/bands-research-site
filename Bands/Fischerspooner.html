Fischerspooner

Sep 27, 20267 min read

Fischerspooner

Discogs ID: 9086 Origin: New York, USA (members met in Chicago at art school) Active: 1998–2019 Members: Warren Fischer · Casey Spooner

American electroclash duo. Warren Fischer (music, programming) and Casey Spooner (vocals, lyrics) met at art school in Chicago and relocated to New York City, forming Fischerspooner in 1998. Their sound combined synthpop, electro, post-punk, musical theatre, and cabaret. The duo split in 2019 over personal and creative differences.

Biography

Formation and Early Releases (1998–2001)

Fischer and Spooner met as art students in Chicago before establishing themselves in New York’s late-1990s downtown art and performance scene. Their earliest output was a 1999 self-released cassette/CD called Bootleg (Not On Label), followed by the debut single “Emerge” on Serotonin Records (2000) and an early EP on For Those Who Know Records (2000) — both low-profile US pressings. A further early single, “Tone Poem,” appeared on Flapping Jet Records (2000).

Producer Nicolas Vernhes (Rare Book Room studio, Brooklyn) co-produced the band from their earliest recordings. The self-titled For Those Who Know EP already credits Fischer as Music By and Spooner as Vocals/Lyrics By, with Vernhes as co-producer — the core creative arrangement that would persist through the Capitol years.

Electroclash Breakthrough: #1 (2001–2002)

The debut album #1 was initially released in 2001 on their own FS Studios imprint. The record was co-produced with Nicolas Vernhes and featured vocalist contributions from Cindy Greene and Lizzy Yoder, with photography by Roe Ethridge and design by Scott Hug. Track A2, “Emerge,” contained a co-write credit for Colin Newman of Wire.

#1 circulated largely through DJ culture and word-of-mouth before Ministry Of Sound picked it up for a major international release in 2002. The single “Emerge” became the defining track of the electroclash movement, receiving wide licensing and DJ play. MOS also released “The 15th” and subsequent singles. The label issued approximately 11 Fischerspooner releases across this period, including remixed editions and limited formats.

The band were also early signatories to International Deejay Gigolo Records, the Munich-based label run by DJ Hell that was central to the electroclash scene.

Capitol Records: Odyssey (2003–2006)

Fischerspooner signed to Capitol Records for their second album Odyssey (2005). The album was co-produced by Fischer and involved an expanded creative circle: songwriter and producer Linda Perry co-wrote three tracks, and Mirwais Ahmadzaï (known for his work with Madonna) contributed to several songs. Mastering was handled by Ted Jensen. The record was art directed by Gareth Hague with design input from Fischer and Spooner themselves.

A tie-in DJ mix compilation, Time Out Presents The Other Side: New York (Breakbeat Science/Deaf Dumb & Blind, 2005), was credited to Fischerspooner as a DJ mix.

Capitol also released the single “Never Win” (2005, FS Studios/Capitol) and the UK-only “A Kick In The Teeth (Tiefschwarz Remix)” (EMI, 2005). The German label International Deejay Gigolo issued “We Need A War” (2006), which also involved Mirwais and Jimmy Harry as co-writers/producers.

Kitsuné and Entertainment (2007–2010)

The band moved to French label Kitsuné Music for a pair of French-market releases: the single “Danse En France” and the EP The Best Revenge (both 2008). These were produced with a team including Aubrey Ford, Dave Way, Jeff Saltzman, Ian Pai, and Gabriel Espinosa — a Los Angeles-oriented production group.

Their third studio album, Entertainment (2009), was released on Lo Recordings in the UK. The Lo Recordings period also yielded the singles “Supply & Demand” (including an Autoerotique remix) and “We Are Electric” (LoEB, 2009). The Entertainment album featured an extensive visual/fashion credit list including designers Alexander McQueen, Nicola Formichetti, and fashion artist K8 Hardy.

Hiatus and Return: Sir (2017–2019)

After a lengthy hiatus, Fischerspooner returned with the single “Have Fun Tonight” (2017, Ultra Music), followed by the full album Sir (2018, Ultra Records). Sir was produced by Michael Stipe of R.E.M. and mixed by Stuart White. The album also featured collaboration with producer BOOTS and songwriter Andy Lemaster. The band split permanently in 2019.
