Overview

Henry’s Dress was an American noise pop band formed in Albuquerque, NM in 1993 by AV Linton (later of The Aislers Set) and Matt Hartman — who swapped between vocals, guitar, and drums — alongside bassist Hayyim Sanchez; they relocated to San Francisco before disbanding in 1996. Their catalogue of six 1990s releases spans indie rock, shoegaze, and punk-tinged styles, the bulk of it on Slumberland Records, placing them squarely in the Bay Area indiepop scene of the mid-1990s. Shared releases with Tiger Trap, Rose Melberg, Rocketship, and Sleater-Kinney reflect the tight web of that scene, and Dub Narcotic Sound System appears among their closer collaborators as well. A seventh release surfaced on The Breadcrumb Trail in 2000, four years after the band dissolved.
