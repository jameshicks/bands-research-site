HiM

Discogs ID: 13240
Name variation: H.I.M.
Note: Not the Finnish metal band HIM (Ville Valo). This is a US experimental/post-rock collective.

Experimental/post-rock group centred on Doug Scharin (drums). Also a credited member project for Adam Pierce. Released on FatCat Records (Our Point Of Departure, FATLP12, 2000; New Features, FATLP15, 2001). US releases of Sworn Eyes (1999, PER009) and Our Point Of Departure (2000, PER012) were on Perishable Records; the latter was simultaneously issued by FatCat in the UK (FATCD12) and licensed to Polydor Japan (PCD-24032).
