Indie rock band from Dallas, Texas, active from 1991 to 1998.

Whilst Bedhead have split, the songwriting duo of brothers Kadane have since formed The New Year.

On many (all?) Bedhead releases there is a longer catalogue number, e.g. TG225BH06RJN1244:16 for the album . The true meaning of these numbers has not been revealed by the band. They seem to at least contain the label catalog number and total duration.

Overview

Bedhead were a Dallas, Texas indie rock group active from 1991 to 1998, known for a quiet, deliberate sound that drew on slowcore, post-rock, and shoegaze. Their catalogue is concentrated in the mid-1990s — six releases across that decade, primarily on Trance Syndicate Records — before the band dissolved in 1998. After the split, brothers Matt and Bubba Kadane carried on as The New Year. Bedhead’s work has found a sustained second life through Numero Group, which has issued or reissued seven releases from 2014 onward, suggesting the band’s reputation has grown well past their active years. Their most frequent collaborator across the catalogue is Adam Wiltzie, who shares eleven credits with the band.
