The Lickerish Quartet

Los Angeles power-pop group, active 2020–2022. Discogs artist ID: 7889428.

A reunion of three former Jellyfish (2) members — Roger Joseph Manning Jr. (keyboards, vocals), Eric Dover (guitar, vocals), and Tim Smith (5) (bass, vocals) — who had not played together since 1994. The Discogs profile notes the band formed in 2017, though their first release appeared in 2020. The trio self-produced throughout, handling all arrangements, recordings, and instrumental and vocal performances. Their recurring session drummer was Jeremy Stacey. The project issued three four-track EPs under the series title “Threesome” (Vols. 1–3) and one full-length compilation album, Fables From Fearless Heights (2022), which gathered all twelve EP tracks plus bonus material for a Japan-only release on Sony Records Int’l. The group was not a Jellyfish reunion proper — founding vocalist/drummer Andy Sturmer was not involved.

According to Wikipedia’s Jellyfish article (sourced from a 2020 PopMatters interview with Manning), Manning noted that Sturmer “remains uninterested in any kind of post-Jellyfish activity.” The Lickerish Quartet name has no Discogs disambiguation entry and no known aliases or alternate artist entries.

Labels

Label Logic (Discogs ID: 1499247) — a small US label with only 3 releases in the Discogs database, one of which is Threesome Vol. 1. The other releases are by Vintage Trouble and Sasha’s Bloc. Very limited catalog; likely a project-specific or management-affiliated imprint.

Lojinx (Discogs ID: 30860) — a UK-based power-pop and pop-rock independent, founded in the early 2000s. Catalog of 175 releases (as of this research). Key roster includes Fountains Of Wayne (Sky Full Of Holes, 2011), They Might Be Giants (multiple releases from 2013–2018), The Posies (Solid States, 2016), Brendan Benson (What Kind Of World, 2012; You Were Right, 2013), Ken Stringfellow (Danzig In The Moonlight, 2012), Mike Viola (multiple releases), Graham Gouldman (Modesty Forbids, 2020; I Have Notes, 2024), and Butch Walker (multiple releases). A natural home for The Lickerish Quartet given its power-pop orientation. Threesome Vol. 2 (LJX124CD) and Threesome Vol. 3 (LJX129CD) are on Lojinx.

Sony Records Int’l — Fables From Fearless Heights was released as a Japan-market compilation on Sony Records Int’l (cat. SICX 30124), gathering all three EPs plus two bonus covers.

Background: The Jellyfish Connection

All three members appeared on Jellyfish (2)‘s second album Spilt Milk (1993, Charisma/Virgin):

  • Manning co-founded Jellyfish in 1989 with Andy Sturmer and was the band’s keyboardist, co-writer, and co-producer throughout its existence.
  • Tim Smith joined for the Spilt Milk sessions and touring period (1992–1994).
  • Dover joined as touring guitarist for the Spilt Milk tour (1993–1994), replacing Jason Falkner who left before the second album was completed.

After Jellyfish dissolved in 1994, Manning and Dover immediately reunited in Imperial Drag (1995–1997), a glam-pop quartet signed to Columbia/Work. Manning also produced the first album by Tim Smith’s project Umajets (Demolotion, 1995/1997). So all three members were in contact and collaborating within a year of Jellyfish’s breakup, well before The Lickerish Quartet formed. (Wikipedia’s Jellyfish article places the Lickerish Quartet’s formation in 2017, though Discogs shows the first release in 2020.)

Member Profiles

Roger Joseph Manning Jr. (Discogs ID: 8591)

Full profile: Roger Joseph Manning Jr.

Post-Jellyfish, Manning has been one of LA’s most prolific session keyboardists, with credits spanning Beck (Mutations, Sea Change, Hyperspace), Blink-182, Morrissey, The Killers, The Mars Volta, Paramore (string arrangements), and dozens more. Solo records include The Land Of Pure Imagination (2006), Catnip Dynamite (2008), and Glamping (2018). Aliases: Malibu, Uli Nomi.

Eric Dover (Discogs ID: 670462)

Full profile: Eric Dover

Born 19 January 1967, Jasper, Alabama. Dover’s most substantial pre-Lickerish credits are with Jellyfish (Spilt Milk tour), Imperial Drag (co-founder, lead vocals/guitar, 1995–97), and Alice Cooper (The Eyes of Alice Cooper, 2003; Dragontown, 2001 — guitar and backing vocals). He also appeared on the Slash’s Snakepit debut (It’s Five O’Clock Somewhere, 1995, Geffen) and Glen Campbell’s Ghost On The Canvas (2011). His Discogs groups include Slash’s Snakepit, Halloween Jack (2), Sextus, Love Bang, and Tommy’s Rocktrip.

Tim Smith (5) (Discogs ID: 533106)

Full profile: Tim Smith (5)

Tim Smith (full name: Timothy Christopher Smith) has been a core member of Sheryl Crow’s touring and recording band since 1996, appearing on Sheryl Crow And Friends: Live From Central Park (1999), C’mon, C’mon (2002), The Globe Sessions (1998), and numerous other Crow releases. He also co-produced the Umajets debut Demolotion (1997, Clearspot) and has recorded with Noel Gallagher’s High Flying Birds and The Producers (6).
