The Mercury Program

Discogs ID: 322989
Origin: Gainesville, Florida
Active: 1997–present (sporadic)
Genres: Post-rock, jazz, chamber pop

Instrumental post-rock quartet formed Gainesville FL, August 1997. Original trio: Tom Reno (guitar), Sander Travisano (bass), Dave Lebleu (drums). Whit Travisano joined as 4th member (electric piano, vibraphone) after the self-titled debut on Boxcar Records (1999). From then on released exclusively on Tiger Style (12 releases, 2000–2003).

Signature sound: guitar + vibraphone + electric piano + drums — notably no dedicated bass on most recordings. Known for crediting themselves with intentionally deadpan technical language (“Level Adjustments And All Other Preparations For Production Performed By” for engineering, “Triangular Department” for triangle/percussion). Produced by Hank McAfee (“Binary Conversion And Anger Management By”).
