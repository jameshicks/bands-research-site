Trumans Water

Experimental rock and noise-pop band from San Diego, California. Formed 1991. Discogs artist ID: 259618. Name variations: TW, Truman’s Water, Trumans.

Overview

Trumans Water emerged from San Diego in 1991 as part of the post-Sonic Youth wave of US underground bands pushing noise, dissonance, and post-punk into increasingly fractured directions. The band built a dense early catalog on Homestead Records, Elemental Records (UK), and several smaller cassette/7” imprints between 1992 and 1995 before a brief stint on Runt (1996) and then two albums on Emperor Jones (Austin) in 1998 and 2001. A single on Sub Pop appeared in 2000. Later activity included the 2010 LP on Asthmatic Kitty Records, a 2012 split, and a 2019 LP on Novitic Industries. Their active recorded years on Discogs span 1992–2022.

The core membership revolved around Glen Galloway and the Branstetter brothers (Kevin and Kirk), with a notably fluid lineup through the 1990s that cycled through multiple drummers.

Biography

Formation and early years (1991–1993)

Trumans Water formed in San Diego in 1991. Their debut material appeared in 1992: the Laugh Light’s Lit EP (Drunken Fish Records, DF-03) and the debut album Of Thick Tum on Homestead Records (HMS192-2, CD; HMS192-1, LP), recorded by Bill Day. The Our Scars Like Badges EP (Homestead, HMS190-7) also came out in 1992. The Of Thick Tum lineup credited on Discogs does not list individual members by name on the LP itself.

1993 was the band’s most prolific year: they released three major albums and a clutch of singles and EPs across both US and UK labels. Godspeed The Punchline (Homestead, HMS209-2) featured the lineup of Glen Galloway (guitar, vocals), Kirk Branstetter (guitar, vocals), Kevin Branstetter (bass, vocals), Ely Moyal (drums), and Mike Mooridian (drums, vocals) — a two-drummer configuration. Spasm Smash XXXOXOX Ox & Ass was issued on Elemental Records in the UK (ELM 9CD/LP) and on Homestead in the US (HMS199-2), with artwork by John Patrick Salisbury and recording by Bill Day. The 10 X My Age EP (Elemental, ELM 14CD) also appeared in 1993 and was later issued in multiple pressings. UK label Elemental Records became a key international outlet during this period.

Other 1993 releases: the Godspeed The Static 7” (Drunken Fish, DF-10), Godspeed The Vortex split (Negative Records / The Way Out Sound), Hey Fish (Drunken Fish, DF-06), Godspeed The Hemorrhage (Homestead, HMS193-1). The band also appeared on the compilation Ask For Disorder (Justice My Eye/Elevated Loin; Dutch East India Trading).

The Peel Sessions (Strange Fruit, SFRCD133, 1995) documented three separate BBC sessions spanning different lineup configurations: tracks 1–5 featured Glen Galloway, Kirk Branstetter, Kevin Branstetter, and Ely Moyal, produced by Mike Robinson; tracks 6–11 featured Mike Mooradian on drums, produced by Mike Engles; tracks 12–16 featured Kevin Cascell (drums, piano, vocals), Kevin Branstetter (guitar, piano, vocals), and Kirk Branstetter (guitar, piano, vocals), produced by Mike Robinson.

Mid-period (1994–1997)

1994 brought more releases: the Santee Busbill 7” (Destroy All Music, D.A.M.3), the Skyjacker single (Sympathy For The Record Industry, SFTRI 255), Spazz Rockdance Inferno (Clawfist, UK), Jubileeeee (The Way Out Sound), and Couch Of The Spastics (Chocolate Monk, UK). Japanese editions of Of Thick Tum and Godspeed The Punchline appeared on Seven Seas / King Records in 1994.

The 1995 full-length Milktrain To Paydirt (Homestead, HMS221-2) was recorded by Tim Green (2) and assembled/produced by Kevin Branstetter, Kevin Cascell, and Kirk Branstetter, with artwork by Kevin Cascell. This album appears to mark the transition point where Kevin Cascell joined as a more central member.

In 1996, Action Ornaments (Runt, runt28) introduced a lineup of Kevin Branstetter, Kevin Cascell, and Kirk Branstetter as performers, with Greg Paul on mixing and Ryan Foster on mastering. Brendan Bell recorded the album. The same year saw Cough Forth Such Dilemmas on Union Pole (UP40). 1997 releases included Apistogramma (Justice My Eye/Elevated Loin, #006), “Great Flood” E.P. (Footprint, Belgium), and No Dead Space (Union Pole, UP63). The The First Dead Man Of Diluvia 7” appeared on Wäntage USA (Wantage 002) in 1995.

Emperor Jones period (1998–2001)

Fragments Of A Lucky Break (Emperor Jones, EJ23CD, 1998) was the first full Emperor Jones LP. The lineup was Glen Galloway, Kevin Branstetter, Kevin Cascell, and Kirk Branstetter, recorded by Greg Paul and mastered by Ryan Foster. A UK edition appeared on Infinite Chug (CHUG 10, 1998); a split single with I’m Being Good on Infinite Chug (CHUG 11, 1998) appeared simultaneously.

The Miss Spaceship single (Sub Pop, SP519, 2000) was a notable anomaly — Sub Pop at the time was still associated with grunge/indie-rock mainstream territory. The lineup on Miss Spaceship was Kevin Branstetter (guitar, vocals), Kirk Branstetter (guitar, vocals), Kevin Cascell (drums), and Dylan Rice-Leary (bass) — a new bassist replacing Glen Galloway. Recorded by Greg Paul, mastered by John Golden.

The self-titled Trumans Water (Emperor Jones, ej38cd, 2001; also Delboy Records, Belgium, db007) was their second and final Emperor Jones album. Discogs credits for this release list no additional personnel beyond the band itself.

Later activity (2003–present)

The Singles 1992-1997 (No Sides, NS 07, 2003) compiled early single material. The same year brought You Are In The Line Of Fire And They Are Shooting At You (Homesleep, Italy) and The Artishock Man (Ultra Eczema, Belgium).

Rosolina Mar Meet Trumans Water (Robot Radio Records, Italy, 2007) was a collaborative album with Italian musicians including Fabio Magistrali, Manuele Fusaroli, Nicola Fontana, Enrico Zambon, Bruno Vanessi, Luca Zenari, and Andrea Belfi.

O Zeta Zunis (Asthmatic Kitty / Justice My Eye/Elevated Loin, AKR073 / JME/EL #9, 2010) reunited the band with a lineup of Kevin Branstetter, Kirk Branstetter, Mike Coumatos, and John Schier. Mixed by David Barrett (2), Scott Thiessen, and the band; mastered by Andrew Clare and Kevin Branstetter.

Trumans Water / Birdbath split (Ingue Records, ING010, 2012), Lotions & Creams (Ghoulhouse Records, GH-007, 2014), and Live In Oslo (Handmade Records, HMR023, 2017 — with John Sherman) followed. The Oslo recording included Jarle Steinhovden and Kenneth Amundsen as additional performers/engineers.

Chèvre Au Lait (Novitic Industries, 2019; reissued Tenzenmen, Australia, 2021) brought the lineup of Kevin Branstetter, Kirk Branstetter, Mike Coumatos, and Geoff Soule. Reissues of Of Thick Tum and O Zeta Zunis appeared on Tenzenmen in 2021. An Action Ornaments reissue appeared on Improved Sequence Records (Italy) in 2022.

Peel Sessions (Strange Fruit, 1995)

Three distinct sessions with different lineups:

Session 1 (tracks 1–5): Glen Galloway (guitar, vocals), Kirk Branstetter (guitar, backing vocals), Kevin Branstetter (bass, backing vocals), Ely Moyal (drums). Produced by Mike Robinson; engineered by Mike Robinson and Tim Durham.

Session 2 (tracks 6–11): Lineup included Mike Mooradian (drums, vocals). Produced and engineered by Mike Engles and George Thomas (2).

Session 3 (tracks 12–16): Kevin Branstetter (guitar, piano, vocals), Kirk Branstetter (guitar, piano, vocals), Kevin Cascell (drums, piano, vocals). Dean Pritchard (exhaust pipe). Produced by Mike Robinson; engineered by Julian Markham.
