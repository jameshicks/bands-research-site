Overview

Panthers were a New York City indie rock band formed in 2000, led by vocalist Jayson Green alongside guitarists Justin Chearno and Kip Uhlhorn, bassist Geoff Garlock, and drummer Jeffrey Salane. Their entire catalogue runs across nine releases between 2002 and 2008, touching on alternative rock, punk, disco, and leftfield sounds. Vice Records was their main outlet, releasing five of those records between 2003 and 2007, with European licensing through City Slang; their final releases in 2008 appeared on DFA and Permanent Vacation, suggesting a late shift in direction before activity ceased. Drummer Jerry Fuchs and producer Alex Newport were among their closest studio collaborators, appearing across much of the catalogue.

Notes (web)

2008 label shift: DFA and Permanent Vacation

The DFA and Permanent Vacation releases in 2008 are both the same remix single — “Goblin City (Holy Ghost! / Run Roc Remix)” — not a new studio record. “Goblin City” was a track on the 2007 Vice Records LP The Trick; after the album cycle the track was licensed to Permanent Vacation (Munich) for a remixes-only 12” (released July 4, 2008, catalog PV009) and to DFA for the North American pressing (September 2008, catalog DFA2206). The DFA connection follows naturally from the remix: Holy Ghost! were a DFA act at the time, and DFA routinely released singles by DFA-affiliated remixers. Permanent Vacation handled the European side. No interview or press source documents a deliberate label switch away from Vice; the structure is consistent with a standard post-album remix licensing deal rather than a new signing. Sources: Beatport listing, Permanent Vacation label; Permanent Vacation Bandcamp; Discogs master 143228 (snippet only — Discogs not fetched directly).

Disbanding

No formal disbandment announcement has been located. Multiple sources list Panthers’ years of activity as 2000–2008, with Goblin City (the remix single) as the final release. Jayson Green confirmed in a later interview that “that band ending” but gave no specific date or circumstances: “That band ended, I was in another band with our mutual friend Justin Cherno called Panthers. That band ending and then I looked up, I had no discernible skills.” (Jayson Green – Five Rules, Substack, accessed 2026-11-10). After Panthers, Green fronted Violent Bullshit and later Jayson Green & The Jerk (DFA, 2024); Kip Uhlhorn co-founded Cloudland Canyon with Simon Wojan. The band appears to have simply stopped rather than issuing a formal split announcement. The blog (((((OPENmind/SATURATEDbrain))))) also lists 2000–2008 as the active years. (openmindsaturatedbrain.blogspot.com, April 2015).
