Jesse Garon & The Desperadoes

Edinburgh indie-pop group, active late 1980s. Discogs artist ID: 243276

Douglas Hart produced two of their releases: The Adam Faith Experience (1987, Velocity Records (2)) and the Billy The Whizz E.P. (1987, Narodnik). Their lineup included Angus McPake (bass), Margarita Vasquez-Ponte (drums), Bruce Hopkins (guitar), John Robb, Andrew Tully (guitar/vocals), and Fran Schoppler (vocals).
