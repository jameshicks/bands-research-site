The Vibrators

UK punk band. Discogs artist ID: 263832

Overview

London punk group formed 1976, among the earliest UK punk acts to secure a major label deal (Epic). Best known for Pure Mania (1977, Columbia/CBS) and V2 (1978, Epic). Long-running band with numerous lineup changes; core identity associated with guitarist Ian Carnochan (Knox, alias Miss Directed) and vocalist/guitarist Pat Collier (early period).

Key Members (from Discogs)

  • Ian Carnochan (Knox / Miss Directed) — guitar, vocals. See Ian Carnochan
  • Jonathan Edwards (C. Slug / Eddie Edwards) — drums (1977–79)
  • Greg Van Cook (Miss Guided) — guitar (late 1970s)
  • Pat Collier — bass, vocals (early period; also a producer)
