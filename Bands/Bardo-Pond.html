Bardo Pond

Bardo Pond are an American heavy psychedelic / drone rock band formed in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania in 1991. They are one of the defining acts of American underground psych, operating at the intersection of noise rock, space rock, shoegaze, and free improvisation. Their sound — elongated, feedback-drenched guitar drones, Isobel Sollenberger’s flute and keening vocals, and a rhythm section that ranges from locked groove to free pulse — has been compared to Pink Floyd, Spacemen 3, and My Bloody Valentine. The band name was given by bassist Clint Takeda, drawn from the Tibetan Book of the Dead. They formed post-university as improvisers before learning to write songs proper, and have maintained continuous activity for over three decades. (Wikipedia)

The core lineup of brothers Michael and John Gibbons (guitars), Isobel Sollenberger (flute, vocals), and Clint Takeda (bass) has been the constant. Drum duties were held by Joe Culver through the first decade, Ed Farnsworth replacing him around 2001, and Jason Kourkounis settling in the seat by the 2010s. Aaron Igler (synth/electronics) is listed as a current member in their Discogs profile.

Discogs name variations: BP, Bardo, Brando Pound (the last used as producer credit on several Matador releases). Discogs ID: 31590.
