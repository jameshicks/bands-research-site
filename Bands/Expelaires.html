First formed in 1978, this Leeds based bands nucleus consisted of Craig Adams, David Wolfenden (later of Red Lorry Yellow Lorry and The Mission) and Grape (Grape (2), later of 3,000 Revs and Groovin’ With Lucy).

After supports to Echo & The Bunnymen and the The Teardrop Explodes they signed a one single deal for “about £400” with Zoo Records. The first single “To See You” was released in September 1979 and featured Grape (vocals), Wolfie (Guitar), Craig (Guitar/Keyboards), Mark Copson (bass) and Carl “Tich” Harper (Darren Carl Harper, drums).

Their upcoming band status earned them an invitation to record a session for John Peel. The session comprised of 4 tracks “Nasty Media”, “Its Alright Mother”, “Frequency” and “The Dashboard (killed me)“.

A few months after this, also due to the limited success of the single, things started to go wrong: first, Copson quit, to be replaced by David Harper (later of Five Or Six) and Craig, too, left not long after the release of their second single “Sympathy” in January 1980. The song will appear in the compilation “Hicks From The Sticks” released the same year.

Overview

A Leeds post-punk group formed in 1978 around Craig Adams, David Wolfenden, and vocalist Paul “Grape” Gregory, whose members went on to shape the city’s post-punk landscape — Wolfenden joining Red Lorry Yellow Lorry and then The Mission, David Harper joining Five Or Six. After supporting Echo & The Bunnymen and The Teardrop Explodes, they released two singles: “To See You” on Zoo Records (1979) and “Sympathy” on Rockburgh Records (1980), the latter appearing on the “Hicks From The Sticks” compilation. Personnel turnover and Craig Adams’ departure brought the original run to a close, though the band reformed in 2012 and returned to recording with the EP “Sick Of Lies” in 2014.
