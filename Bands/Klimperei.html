Klimperei

Discogs ID: 137265
French duo formed by Christophe Petchanatz and Françoise Lefebvre (also credited as Françoise Petchanatz). Active from at least 1987 into the 2020s, with the duo format operative through the mid-2000s and the project continuing as Christophe Petchanatz’s solo vehicle thereafter. Core aesthetic: toy instruments, piano, flute, small percussion, home recording — described by the Discogs profile as “expressionist theater for puppets,” comparable to Pascal Comelade, Yann Tiersen, and Nino Rota. Two releases on Ayaa (Reims, France) mark the peak of their early-90s visibility; preceded by cassette releases on German and French underground labels. Klimperei is also listed as a member entity of KPA (6).

Biography

Origins and cassette years (1987–1990)

The earliest documented Klimperei release is Brötchen Et Petits Pains (1987, Individual Pop, Germany, cat IP 001), a split cassette with Los Paranos featuring cover drawings by Éric Watier. The same year produced When Memories Began To Fade… (Underground Productions, France, SUB 9), a 45-track cassette containing covers of Brian Eno, Gong/Daevid Allen, Lennon-McCartney, Karl Jenkins, and Ravel alongside originals — a snapshot of Klimperei’s eclectic, defiantly amateur aesthetic. Both releases place the group firmly in the European cassette-culture underground of the late 1980s.

Gris-Nez (1990, SHM Tapes, Germany) was compiled by Guido Erfen with cover design again by Éric Watier. Au Jardin Botanique (1991, SPH, Portugal, SPH019) followed the same year on yet another European underground imprint, confirming a wide network of tape-trading contacts across France, Germany, and Portugal.

Ayaa period and commercial breakthrough (1991–1995)

Tout Seul Sur La Plage En Hiver (1991, Ayaa, CDT 0590) was the duo’s first CD release, produced by Ayaa (label head Dominique Diebold). The 28-track album includes one track co-composed and co-recorded with Laurent Fauconnet (“Retour De Grèce”). Genres on Discogs: Electronic, Rock; styles: Avantgarde, Experimental. At the same time Vilain Chien appeared on Underground Productions (SUB 24), continuing the cassette line.

Les Plus Belles Valses (1994, Trümmer Kassetten, Germany, TP 033) was a cassette compilation drawing on existing material. The second and final Ayaa CD, Blumenfabrik (1995, cdt 1094), credits Christophe and Françoise Petchanatz explicitly as composers and performers, with cover art by Jacques Greco and liner notes by Japanese critic Atsushi Sasaki; produced again by Ayaa. Its 35 tracks consolidate the approach: short, melancholy, chamber-scaled pieces in a naive-classical mode.

Ayaa itself (based in Reims) was a label and distributor run by Dominique Diebold; its broader catalog includes Étron Fou le Loublan, Ghédalia Tazartès, Fred Frith / Lol Coxhill, Lars Hollmer, and Ne Zhdali — situating Klimperei in a curated French avant/eclectic milieu.

Post-Ayaa and label diversification (2000–2011)

Alice Au Pays Des Merveilles (2000, Gazul Records / In-Poly-Sons, GA 8642.AR / IPS 0600) is a 44-track double-concept album setting Lewis Carroll’s Alice in Wonderland and Through the Looking-Glass plus The Hunting of the Snark to music. Composed by Christophe and Françoise Petchanatz; text translation by Dominique Quélen; cover painting by Alfreda Benge (wife of Robert Wyatt); mastered by Denis Tagu.

Serieux (2002, Novel Cell Poem, Japan, NCP-14) is a 38-track compilation/overview on a Japanese label, with several tracks co-composed and recorded by Laurent Fauconnet and design/illustration by Pirocchio.

Mondocane (2007, Acidsoxx Musicks, US, XX032) is a collaborative CD with Palo Alto (French experimental duo). Klimperei overdubbed music onto Palo Alto’s compositions; mixed by Christophe Petchanatz; mastered by Norscq. Also features percussion by Claire Lamy; sleeve notes by Jacques Barbéri.

Les Tableaux D’Une Exposition D’Après Modeste Moussorgski, 1874 (2011, In-Poly-Sons, IPS 0312) is a solo Klimperei LP adapting Mussorgsky’s Pictures at an Exhibition, arranged, recorded, and mixed entirely by Christophe Petchanatz. One track features Lola Petchanatz (presumably daughter of Christophe).

Recent work (2021–2024)

Rainbow De Nuit (2021, Marionette, Marionette15) is a digital collaborative album with David Fenech — both credited as writers and producers, with percussion by Denis Tagu on three tracks and mastering by Noel Summerville.

Cirque Klimperei 1988–2022 (2024, Time Released Sound, TRS113) is a 2LP career retrospective on the US ambient/drone label Time Released Sound, mastered by Taylor Deupree, spanning 34 years of the project. Music credited solely to Christophe Petchanatz.

Scene Context

Klimperei belongs to a loosely connected late-1980s/90s European cassette underground that produced naive, lo-fi, and toy-instrument music alongside more consciously avant-garde work. Their Ayaa releases put them alongside Ghédalia Tazartès, Étron Fou le Loublan, Fred Frith, and Lars Hollmer. The crossover with the cassette-culture nodes in Germany (Individual Pop, SHM Tapes, Trümmer Kassetten) and Portugal (SPH) reflects the pan-European tape-trading network of the era. The Mondocane collaboration with Palo Alto and the 2021 work with David Fenech connect the project to a later generation of French experimental musicians. The 2024 retrospective on Time Released Sound (a US label associated with ambient/drone packaging) marks Klimperei’s absorption into an international collector market.
