Grinderman

Side project of Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds, formed in London in 2006. An intentionally raw, high-voltage vehicle for four Bad Seeds members to strip back the orchestration and play loud, confrontational rock — described by Cave as “a way to escape the weight of The Bad Seeds.” Active 2006–2011. Two studio albums, one remix album, multiple singles. All releases on Mute (Europe/UK/Australia) and Anti- (US).

The band was originally called Mini Seeds during early rehearsals. The final name comes from Memphis Slim’s song “Grinder Man Blues,” which Cave reportedly began singing spontaneously at one of the first rehearsals.

Overview

Grinderman was conceived as a deliberately limited, spontaneous unit — no outside writers, no orchestral arrangements. Cave and Ellis handled the bulk of the experimental texture; the sound sits in garage rock, blues rock, and noise, with Cave’s lyrical obsessions (desire, failure, the grotesque, mortality) running harder and more feverish than in much Bad Seeds work. Producer Nick Launay worked on both albums, recording the bulk of the debut at Assault & Battery (London) and State of the Ark (Richmond) studios.

The debut attracted immediate critical attention for its abrasive directness. The second album, Grinderman 2 (2010), expanded the palette slightly — more studio texture, denser production — while staying faithful to the band’s confrontational posture. The remix collection Grinderman 2 RMX (2012) gathered contributions from Robert Fripp, Josh Homme, Andrew Weatherall, Barry Adamson, UNKLE, Factory Floor, A Place To Bury Strangers, Nick Zinner, and others.

No formal dissolution announcement was ever made; Cave and Ellis moved heavily into film scoring work after 2011 and Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds resumed activity with Push the Sky Away (2013).

Label Details

  • Mute (Europe, UK, Australia): All releases catalogue STUMM / MUTE / 12MUTE prefix. Label code LC05834 throughout. Parent label at time of release: Mute Records.
  • Anti- (US): Distributed/released under Epitaph umbrella. Cat# range 86861 → 87198.
  • BMG: Reissues from 2018 onward reflect Mute’s acquisition by BMG; later pressings carry both BMG and Mute credits.
  • Australian pressings: Mute (2) / Virgin distribution.
  • Japan: Hostess Entertainment Unlimited (album 1); Beatink Inc. (album 2).

Connections

  • Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds — all four members; Grinderman was an active complement rather than a break, running in parallel 2006–2011
  • Dirty Three — Warren Ellis’s other long-running project
  • The Birthday Party — Cave’s earlier Australian post-punk band
  • Nick Launay — produced both studio albums; long-standing Cave associate (also worked with Bad Seeds, Midnight Oil, INXS, Yeah Yeah Yeahs, Arcade Fire)
  • Robert Fripp — contributed guitars to “Super Heathen Child” on the Heathen Child 12” and Grinderman 2 RMX
  • Josh Homme — remixed “Mickey Mouse And The Goodbye Man”; credited as “Joshua Homme”
  • Andrew Weatherall — remixed “Heathen Child” for Grinderman 2 RMX
  • Barry Adamson — remixed “Palaces Of Montezuma”; Bad Seeds alumnus, longstanding Cave collaborator
  • Factory Floor — remixed “Evil” (‘The Michael Cliffe House’ remix)
  • UNKLE — “Hyper Worm Tamer” collaboration; also on Grinderman 2 RMX
  • The National — Matt Berninger featured on “Evil (Silver Alert Remix)”
  • Tom Hingston Studio — design/art direction across entire discography
