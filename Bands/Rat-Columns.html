Overview

Rat Columns is an Australian indie rock and post-punk duo formed in 2011, comprising David West and Matt Bleyle. Their discography runs across two decades — six releases in the 2010s and two more in the early 2020s — with a notable mid-decade moment when the band placed singles on Blackest Ever Black, Adagio830, Upset! The Rhythm, and Slumberland Records, four distinct international labels, within a short window between 2014 and 2018. Mikey Young, the Melbourne producer and musician known from Eddy Current Suppression Ring and Total Control, appears on seven of their releases, making him their closest ongoing collaborator. Their later work returned to a consolidated setup on Tough Love Records.
