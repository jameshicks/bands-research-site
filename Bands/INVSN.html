INVSN

Swedish post-punk / darkwave band from Umeå. Also known as Invasionen. Discogs artist ID: 1973114. Alias: The Lost Patrol.

Overview

Dennis Lyxzén’s ongoing band after The International Noise Conspiracy. Also includes Sara Almgren from TINC. The Discogs entry uses the name “Invasionen” with “INVSN” as a name variation.
