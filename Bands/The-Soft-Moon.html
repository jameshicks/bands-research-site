The Soft Moon

Discogs ID: 1775650 Active: 2009–2024 Origin: Oakland, California (founded); Los Angeles (later) Principal: Luis Vasquez (real name: Jose Luis Vasquez; May 30, 1979 – January 18, 2024) Labels: Captured Tracks (2010–2016); Sacred Bones (2018–2022); aufnahme + wiedergabe (remixes, Germany)

American neo-post-punk / dark electronic project founded in 2009 as a solo project by Luis Vasquez. Born in Los Angeles to a Cuban immigrant mother and a Mexican father; based in Oakland through the early years, later Los Angeles. Vasquez died January 18, 2024 in Los Angeles at age 44. His body was found at the home of Silent Servant (John Juan Mendez), alongside Mendez and Mendez’s partner Simone Ling — all three died from the combined effects of fentanyl, cocaine, ketamine, and ethanol. Silent Servant has releases on Sacred Bones.

The Soft Moon began on Captured Tracks in 2010 and was one of the label’s darker outliers alongside the broader lo-fi/dream-pop CT aesthetic. The debut LP established a motorik, heavily reverbed post-punk/industrial sound. After Deeper (2015), the project moved to Sacred Bones for Criminal (2018) and the final album Exister (2022).

Members beyond Vasquez listed on Discogs: Keven Tecon (1328293) and Matteo Vallicelli (2543659) — both in live band capacity. Justin Anastasi (1739468) credited as live band member on Zeros.

Vasquez was also a member of Lumerians (1856895).

Notes (web)

Mike Simonetti / RCRD LBL remix (2011)

The remix We Are We (Mike Simonetti’s 1992 Trance-Formation Mix) was posted on RCRD LBL on July 18, 2011 (Hype Machine listing, retrieved 2026-10-20). No interview or press piece found that directly states how the collaboration came about or who initiated it.

What can be established from web sources:

  • Mike Simonetti co-founded Italians Do It Better with Johnny Jewel in 2006 and ran Troubleman Unlimited before that. He left IDIB in 2013. (Wikipedia, Italians Do It Better, as of 2026-10-20)
  • Simonetti had a pre-existing friendship with Captured Tracks founder Mike Sniper: Sniper’s band Blank Dogs released material on Simonetti’s Troubleman Unlimited label in 2008, before Captured Tracks signed The Soft Moon. (Northern Transmissions, retrieved 2026-10-20); (Tiny Mix Tapes, retrieved 2026-10-20)
  • This friendship later deepened enough that Simonetti and Sniper co-founded 2MR (2 Mikes Records) together in 2013–2015. (Billboard, retrieved 2026-10-20)
  • RCRD LBL was a free-download music blog/platform, not an imprint of IDIB or Captured Tracks; it frequently hosted label-commissioned remixes for promotion.

Probable mechanism (inferred, not directly sourced): The Simonetti–Sniper friendship, established via the Troubleman/Blank Dogs connection, makes it plausible that the remix was arranged informally between the two labels’ principals in mid-2011. No contemporaneous interview confirms this. No statement from Vasquez or Simonetti about the commission has been found in accessible web sources.

Sources checked, no relevant result: hartzine.com Mike Simonetti interview (2013, no mention of Soft Moon or RCRD LBL); Wayback-blocked RCRD LBL; allmusic.com (blocked); rateyourmusic.com (blocked).

aufnahme + wiedergabe

Discogs label ID: 234423. Berlin-based independent label founded in 2011 by Philipp Strobel (Discogs artist ID 2628451; also records as NecroPhil (2)) and Gabriel Brero (Discogs artist ID 2149265; also records/performs as La Fete Triste). Julia Meusel joined in 2013. The label focuses on post-punk, dark electronic, EBM, and industrial-adjacent music. Sublabels: Tapetopia (1719699) and Wiedergaben (1561080).

The Soft Moon appeared on aufnahme + wiedergabe for two releases: Deeper Remixed Vol. 1 (2016, co-released with Captured Tracks) and Criminal Remixed (2018, 10 pressings). These were the label’s primary point of contact with the project — The Soft Moon’s studio albums remained on Captured Tracks and Sacred Bones.
