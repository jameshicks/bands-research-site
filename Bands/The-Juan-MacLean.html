The Juan MacLean

Discogs ID: 53081 Active: 2002–2020 Origin: New York, USA

Electronic/house project of Juan MacLean (Discogs 427182). One of the original DFA Records acts, with 128 releases on the label (2002–2020). Vocalist Nancy Whang (also LCD Soundsystem) featured prominently across the catalog. House-leaning side of the DFA aesthetic, in contrast to LCD Soundsystem’s rock/dance hybrid.
