Against Me!

Punk/rock band. Gainesville, Florida.

Overview

Florida punk band led by Laura Jane Grace (Tom Gabel pre-transition). Butch Vig produced two albums: New Wave (2007, Sire) and White Crosses (2010, Sire). Singles from the Vig-produced period include “Thrash Unreal,” “White People For Peace,” “I Was A Teenage Anarchist,” “Last Of The American Girls.”
