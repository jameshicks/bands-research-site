The Comsat Angels

Sheffield post-punk/new wave band. Discogs artist ID: 286952.

Members: Steve Fellows, Kevin Bacon, Andy Peake, Mik Glaisher, Simon Anderson (2), Terry Todd. Also recorded as Dream Command.

Contributed “Ju Ju Money” to Aardvark’s Bouquet Of Steel compilation (1980), produced by Kevin Bacon. Bacon also produced two other tracks on that compilation (for Scarborough Antelopes and Veiled Threat) and produced both of Disease (2)‘s other recordings — the undated demo cassette and the 1981 single No Future / I Know How on Disease Discs.
