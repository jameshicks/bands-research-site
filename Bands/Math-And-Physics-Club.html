Overview

Math and Physics Club were a Seattle indie pop band whose catalogue ran from 2005 to 2019, when they announced their disbandment on December 16 of that year. Across five releases, their sound spans indie pop, indie rock, and pop rock — one record in the 2000s and four in the following decade. Their primary label throughout was Matinée Recordings, which released five of their records across their entire active span, with additional releases on Fika Recordings, Dufflecoat Records, and Jigsaw Records. The core lineup included Andrew Hall (the most credited member), Charles Bert, Ethan Jones, James Werle, and Kevin Emerson, with Barry Corliss and Bob Schwenkler each sharing credits across five releases alongside engineer Martin Feveyear.
