Four Tet

Electronic / post-rock / IDM / deep house project of Kieran Miles David Hebden (born 2 September 1977, Putney, London, UK). Active from 1997 to present. Discogs artist ID: 3543. Also records under his own name Kieran Hebden (Discogs ID: 60342). Label boss of Text Records (his own imprint).

Biography

Fridge era and early Four Tet (1994–2000)

Kieran Hebden formed the instrumental post-rock trio Fridge (Discogs ID: 30361) in Putney in 1994 alongside Adem Ilhan (Discogs ID: 632452) and Sam Jeffers (Discogs ID: 413632). Fridge released a series of EPs on Output Records through 1997–1999 — Anglepoised, Ceefax, Semaphore, Sevens And Twelves, Lign — before moving to Go! Beat for further EPs. They eventually signed to Domino, releasing the full-lengths Happiness (2001) and The Sun (2007).

Alongside Fridge, Hebden began releasing as Four Tet on Output from 1998. The Thirtysixtwentyfive EP (1998, Output, OPR14) was his debut; Dialogue and Misnomer followed in 1999. A notable early release was a split 12” with Stefan Betke’s Pole project (Pole v Four Tet EP / Four Tet v Pole EP, 2000, Leaf, DOCK 20), placing Four Tet early within the European minimal/glitch electronic sphere.

Domino era — Pause and Rounds breakthrough (2001–2007)

Four Tet signed to Domino around 2001. Pause (2001, Domino, DOMUS02CD) was the debut full-length — abstract downtempo electronics, immediately recognisable for its chopped folk-sample aesthetic and intimate scale. Rounds (2003, Domino, WIGCD126) brought wider critical attention: its fusion of broken beats with acoustic instruments (guitar, banjo, flute) and melodic warmth defined what became known as the “folktronica” sound. It remains his signature record.

As Serious As Your Life (2003, Domino) was a standalone single. My Angel Rocks Back And Forth (2004) preceded the second full album, Everything Ecstatic (2005, Domino, WIGCD154), which shifted towards faster tempos and more fragmented rhythmic structures. A compilation mix for LateNightTales (2004) and the DJ-Kicks series (!K7, 2006) demonstrated his profile as a DJ and curator alongside his production work.

During this period Hebden began a deep collaboration with veteran jazz/free-funk drummer Steve Reid (ID: 292561 — Steve Reid (2)). The joint releases on Domino — The Exchange Session Vol. 1 and Vol. 2 (both 2006), Tongues (2007), The Sun Never Sets (2007), and Daxaar (Recorded In Africa) (2007) — were issued under the “Kieran Hebden & Steve Reid” credit, with Hebden as part of the Steve Reid Ensemble. These sessions are among the most distinctive collaborations in his catalogue: live free jazz meeting electronic production.

Ringer (2008, Domino, RUG295CDP) was a four-track mini-album (c.31 min) marking a turn towards minimal techno and abstract electronics at longer duration — “Ringer” runs almost ten minutes.

Text Records / Domino crossover (2009–2012)

Hebden launched Text Records (Discogs ID: 11205) as his own imprint around 2009, initially for joint releases with Burial (William Emmanuel Bevan, Discogs ID: 306157). The Burial/Four Tet EPs are among the most celebrated electronic records of the decade:

  • Moth / Wolf Cub (2009, Text Records, TEXT-006)
  • Ego / Mirror (2011, Text Records, TEXT010) — also features Thom Yorke (Discogs ID: 4854)
  • Nova (2012, Text Records, TEXT 013)
  • Her Revolution / His Rope (2020, XL Recordings, XL1106T) — also features Thom Yorke
  • Nova/Moth (2022, Text Records, TEXTDDD18)

There Is Love In You (2010, Domino) was his last album for Domino, introducing strong house and techno influences (“Love Cry”, “Sing”, “Plastic People”). Its successor, Pink (2012, Text Records, TEXT018), was released as a digital-only download directly through his own label — eight long tracks in the ambient/deep house/IDM vein.

Text Records self-release era (2013–present)

From 2013, Four Tet released exclusively via Text Records or self-released, operating entirely outside major-label infrastructure.

Beautiful Rewind (2013, Text Records, TEXT025) brought a denser, more energetic sound with UK garage and jungle influences; daughter Tallulah Hebden provides vocals on “Ever Never.” 0181 (2013, Text Records) was a standalone EP. Morning/Evening (2015, Text Records) was radical in format: two side-long tracks totalling 40 minutes, built around Indian classical music influences.

New Energy (2017, Text Records, TEXT046J) — 14 tracks, ambient house, widely praised. Sixteen Oceans (2020, Text Records, TEXT051) — 16 tracks, warmly received. Three (2024, Text Records, TEXT056) — eight tracks, deep house / ambient / downtempo.

Hebden also worked as a producer and collaborator outside his own projects: additional production on Bloc Party’s Silent Alarm (2005); production contributions to releases by Ellie Goulding, Skrillex, Fred again.., DaBaby, and Nelly Furtado; a remix for The xx (VCR, 2010, Young Turks); and close creative ties with Floating Points (Sam Shepherd, ID: 1544337), with whom he shares credits on Roseland and the Melodies Record Club 001 release.

Ongoing live performances include two Alexandra Palace shows (May 2019; May 2023), documented as self-released recordings.
