Fine Young Cannibals

Birmingham pop/soul trio formed 1984. Discogs artist ID: 4711. Active 1984–1996, with reunions and anniversary releases through 2025.

Alias on Discogs: The Rare Grooves.

Overview

Fine Young Cannibals were formed in Birmingham, England in 1984 by guitarist Andy Cox and bassist/keyboardist David Steele — both recently departed from ska/pop group The Beat (2) — alongside vocalist Roland Gift, who had previously fronted Hull post-punk group Akrylykz. The trio’s name was taken from the 1960 film All the Fine Young Cannibals.

The band signed to London Records and released their self-titled debut album in 1985, a record that blended soul influences with post-punk arrangements, produced largely by Cox, Steele, and Gift themselves with contributions from Robin Millar and Mike Pela. Their second album, The Raw & the Cooked (1988–89), became a transatlantic smash: it reached number one in both the UK and the US, driven by singles “She Drives Me Crazy” and “Good Thing,” both US number ones in 1989. The band went on hiatus after the subsequent tour, with members pursuing solo and side projects. A compilation, The Finest, appeared in 1996. FYC released a 40th-anniversary retrospective, FYC40, in 2025 on London Records.

In the US the albums were released on I.R.S. Records (distributed by MCA Records).

Connections

  • The Beat (2) — Andy Cox and David Steele were founding members; Saxa (saxophonist) also appeared on FYC debut
  • Akrylykz — Roland Gift’s pre-FYC group; credited on their 1980 Red Rhino single (Discogs: 766656)
  • Red Rhino Records — Roland Gift’s earliest label credit via Akrylykz (RED 2, 1980)
  • Buzzcocks — Pete Shelley wrote “Ever Fallen In Love,” which FYC covered on The Raw & The Cooked
  • Talking Heads — Jerry Harrison (Talking Heads guitarist) produced “Ever Fallen In Love”
  • David Z. — Minneapolis producer (worked with Prince circle) who produced key tracks on The Raw & The Cooked
  • Jimmy Somerville — backed vocals on “Suspicious Minds”; also appeared in Fine Young Cannibals (same era, same Birmingham/UK soul-pop scene)
  • Anton Corbijn — photographed debut album cover
  • Jools Holland — played piano on The Raw & The Cooked
