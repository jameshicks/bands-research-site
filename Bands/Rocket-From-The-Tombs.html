Rocket From The Tombs

Discogs ID: 252899
Origin: Cleveland, Ohio, USA
Active: 1974–1975, 2003–present (reformations)

Proto-punk band from Cleveland, important as the direct precursor to both Pere Ubu and Dead Boys. Members Richard Lloyd (of Television) and Peter Laughner were central figures. David Thomas went on to Pere Ubu; Stiv Bators and Cheetah Chrome to Dead Boys.

Compilation Appearances

  • Punk 45 - Extermination Nights In The Sixth City! (Cleveland Ohio) (Soul Jazz Records, 2015) — “Life Stinks” (3:26), written by Peter Laughner.

Reunion Lineup

When the band reformed in 2003–2004, the reunion lineup consisted of:

  • David Thomas (2) — vocals
  • Craig Bell — bass, vocals
  • Cheetah Chrome — guitar, vocals
  • Richard Lloyd — guitar (produced, mixed, and recorded Rocket Redux)
  • Steve Mehlman — drums

This lineup recorded Rocket Redux (2004, Glitterhouse Records), produced by Richard Lloyd, with mastering by Emily Lazar. A second album, Barfly (2011, Fire Records), followed with the same core lineup plus The Snot-ettes on backing vocals. Gary Siperko is also listed as a Discogs member of the reunion band.
