The Belltower

American alternative rock / shoegaze band from New York City, active 1991–1995. Discogs artist ID: 274942.

Overview

The Belltower formed in New York City around 1991, combining shoegaze textures with American indie-pop songwriting. They released their debut material in the UK on Ultimate, a Dedicated Records imprint that also housed Chapterhouse, before signing to EastWest Records America for their US full-length Popdropper (1992). The band is notable in retrospect for its alumni: Jody Porter and Adam Schlesinger went on to co-found Fountains Of Wayne, while Britta Phillips joined Luna (5) in 2001 and subsequently formed Dean & Britta with Dean Wareham.

Key Release: Popdropper (1992)

The band’s only full-length, released in the US on EastWest Records America. Jody Porter is credited as sole producer on most tracks; Terry Bickers (House of Love, Levitation) co-produced tracks 6, 7, and 9. Primary songwriter throughout is Porter, with Phillips contributing one music credit (track 6) and several lyrics. Britta Phillips sings lead on tracks 1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9–11; Porter sings on 2, 5, 8.

Key Release: Exploration Day E.P. (1991)

UK debut on Ultimate. Produced jointly by Terry Bickers and the band (tracks 1–3); engineered by Mike Silverston; mixed by Chris Dickie (tracks 2, 4). Core lineup of Porter, Phillips, Browning, and Dmytryszyn.

Key Release: In Hollow (1991)

Self-produced mini-LP/EP on Ultimate, UK. Engineered by Bob Pridden (The Who’s longtime sound engineer) and Chris Dickie; mixed by Anjali Dutt. Art direction by Cally (ID 284049).

UK Scene Connections

The Belltower toured with British shoegaze acts and were lumped into the same scene despite being American. The Special Tour Single (1991, Not On Label) was shared with Chapterhouse and Five Thirty, placing them squarely in that UK alternative milieu. Terry Bickers — who had just departed The House Of Love and was forming Levitation (2) — produced the debut EP and several Popdropper tracks, cementing this transatlantic connection.
