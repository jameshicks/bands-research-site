1982 (2)

Discogs ID: 2456262 Country: Norway Label: Hubro Active: 2009–2017 (based on release dates) Bandcamp: https://1982hubro.bandcamp.com

Norwegian chamber-folk trio: Nils Økland (Hardanger fiddle, violin), Sigbjørn Apeland (harmonium, organ, piano), and Øyvind Skarbø (drums, percussion). The group blends Norwegian folk idioms — particularly the drone-heavy timbre of the Hardanger fiddle — with free improvisation and an unhurried, meditative sensibility. Skarbø doubles as producer across most releases. All studio work recorded by Davide Bertolini. Visual identity consistent across releases via the design studio Yokoland.

The debut (2009) appeared on NORCD; from Pintura (2011) onward, all releases are on Hubro, the Norwegian jazz/experimental imprint under Grappa Musikkforlag AS.
