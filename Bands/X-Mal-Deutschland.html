X Mal Deutschland

Discogs ID: 58788
Origin: Hamburg, Germany
Active: 1980–1992
Labels: 4AD, Phonogram

German post-punk / goth band. Anja Huwe (vocals), Fiona Sangster (keyboards), Manuela Zwingmann (guitar), Rita Simon (bass), Caro May (drums). Fetisch (CAD302, 1983) on 4AD; influential on UK goth and European post-punk.

Lineup History

Formed 1980 in Hamburg as an all-female band:

Original lineup (1980–1981):

  • Anja Huwe — vocals
  • Manuela Rickers — guitar
  • Fiona Sangster — keyboards
  • Rita Simon (2) — bass
  • Caro May — drums

First change (~1981): Rita Simon replaced by Wolfgang Ellerbrock (bass).

Second change (1982): Caro May replaced by Manuela Zwingman (drums). This lineup recorded Fetisch (1983) and the early 4AD singles.

Third change (1984): Manuela Zwingmann left; replaced by Peter Bellendir (drums). This proved the longest-running lineup, recording Tocsin (1984), Matador (1986), and Viva (1987).

Post-Viva collapse (1987–1988): Manuela Rickers, Fiona Sangster, and Peter Bellendir all departed. Anja Huwe and Wolfgang Ellerbrock continued with entirely new personnel: Frank Ziegert (guitar, ex-Abwärts), Henry Staroste (keyboards, producer), and Curt Cress (drums, studio). This late lineup recorded Devils (1989, Metronome) in a more mainstream pop direction; the band dissolved shortly after.
