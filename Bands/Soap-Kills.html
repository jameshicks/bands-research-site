Soap Kills

Discogs ID: 493604 Name variations: SoapKills, soapkills, الصابون يقتل Formed: October 1997, Beirut, Lebanon Disbanded: 2005 Genre: Indie electro-pop, ambient, electronic, alternative Members: Yasmine Hamdan, Zeid Hamdan

Overview

Soap Kills were one of the first Lebanese independent electronic/alternative acts, formed in Beirut in October 1997. The duo — vocalist Yasmine Hamdan and producer/multi-instrumentalist Zeid Hamdan — operated entirely outside the Lebanese mainstream music industry, self-releasing their records without label distribution. Their music blended Arabic song idioms with ambient electronics and indie-pop arrangements. The band disbanded in 2005 after three studio albums, all released on cassette/CD in Lebanon. A posthumous best-of compilation was issued by Crammed Discs in Belgium in 2015.
