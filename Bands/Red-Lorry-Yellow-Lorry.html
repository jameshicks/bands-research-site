Red Lorry Yellow Lorry

Post-punk / goth rock band. Leeds, England. Active c. 1982–1990. Discogs artist ID: 146588.

The most prolific act on Red Rhino Records with 45 releases on the label between 1983 and 1988. Core lineup: Chris Reed (guitar, vocals), David Wolfenden (guitar), Leon Phillips (bass), Chris Oldroyd (drums). Self-produced on key albums including Paint Your Wagon (REDLP 65, 1986).
