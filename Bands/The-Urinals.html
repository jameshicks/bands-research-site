The Urinals

The Urinals

Oct 05, 20265 min read

The Urinals / 100 Flowers

Los Angeles punk and post-punk band. Discogs artist IDs: 177624 (The Urinals / Urinals), 368935 (100 Flowers). Also operated briefly as Chairs of Perception.

Overview

The Urinals formed in 1978 at UCLA’s Dykstra Hall dormitory as a five-piece parody of punk rock. The founding lineup included Delia Frankel (vocals), Steve Willard (guitar), Kevin Barrett (toy drums), Kjehl Johansen (toy organ), and John Jones — later John Talley-Jones — (bass). Their early EPs on Happy Squid Records, all produced by Vitus Mataré, established them as one of the most tightly minimal acts in the first wave of LA punk: sub-two-minute songs, rudimentary instrumentation, acidic brevity.

By 1982 the core trio of Talley-Jones, Barrett, and Johansen had shed the founding members and renamed themselves 100 Flowers, invoking Chairman Mao’s “Let a Hundred Flowers Bloom” campaign. The name change accompanied a deliberate sonic shift: where the Urinals had been raw and aggressively simple, 100 Flowers incorporated psychedelia, funk, industrial elements, and more layered production. They remained on Happy Squid and continued working with Mataré, adding studio engineers Ethan James (real name Ralph Burns Kellogg) and Randy Burns as key collaborators. 100 Flowers released three records before disbanding in 1984.

The band reformed in 1996 as The Urinals and released a career retrospective compilation, Negative Capability…Check It Out!, on Amphetamine Reptile Records. A 2003 LP of new material, What Is Real And What Is Not, followed on Warning Label. A further revival album, Next Year At Marienbad (2015, Happy Squid), added Rob Roberge on guitar/vocals. The 100 Flowers catalog received renewed attention via reissues on Superior Viaduct (2013) and In The Red Recordings, and a new 100 Flowers single appeared on Spacecase Records in 2019 with an expanded retrospective, Drawing Fire +, on In The Red in 2020.

Talley-Jones and Johansen also co-founded the longer-running side project Trotsky Icepick (alongside Vitus Mataré as a member), and Talley-Jones is associated with 17 Pygmies, Radwaste, Danny and the Doorknobs, Vena Cava, and Strikeslip.
