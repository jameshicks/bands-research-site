No Joy

Shoegaze / noise rock band formed in Montreal, Canada in late 2009 by Jasamine White-Gluz and Laura Lloyd. Discogs artist ID: 1919643. Active 2009–present (as of 2025). Primary labels: Mexican Summer, Joyful Noise Recordings, Hand Drawn Dracula.

Overview

No Joy emerged from Montreal’s indie scene in 2009, initially a duo of Jasamine White-Gluz and Laura Lloyd. Their sound sits at the intersection of shoegaze, noise rock, and dream pop — heavy guitar distortion and feedback layered over melodic vocals. The band signed early to Brooklyn-based Mexican Summer, releasing their debut full-length Ghost Blonde in 2010. Over the following decade the lineup expanded and contracted, with White-Gluz as the constant core; from around 2016 onward, No Joy operates effectively as her solo project with collaborators. The 2018 split LP with Sonic Boom (Pete Kember of Spacemen 3) marked a notable crossover moment. Their 2025 album Bugland was co-produced with experimental producer Fire-Toolz (Angel Marcloid).

Biography by Era

Formation and Early Releases (2009–2011)

Jasamine White-Gluz and Laura Lloyd formed No Joy in late 2009 in Montreal. Their debut release was the No Summer EP (2010, Mexican Summer), recorded and mixed by Graham Van Pelt (Miracle Fortress). The debut full-length Ghost Blonde (2010, Mexican Summer) followed quickly, mixed by Sune Rose Wagner (The Raveonettes) and mastered by Paul Gold. The early lineup included both White-Gluz and Lloyd alongside a shifting live band. A split 7” with Marnie Stern (“Your Kids Are Going To Love This / He Cried”, 2011) appeared on Associated Electronic Recordings.

Expanding the Lineup: Negaverse and Wait To Pleasure (2012–2013)

The Negaverse EP (2012, Mexican Summer) documents a fuller band lineup: Garland Hastings (drums, artwork), Casey Scheibling (bass), alongside White-Gluz (vocals, guitar) and Lloyd (guitar). Mixed again by Sune Rose Wagner, mastered by Paul Gold. The band then entered a productive run with producer and mixer Jorge Elbrecht (of Lansing-Dreiden, Violens): the Pastel And Pass Out EP (2013) and full-length Wait To Pleasure (2013, Mexican Summer) were both produced and mixed by Elbrecht. The Hare Tarot Lies EP also appeared in 2013 on Mexican Summer.

More Faithful and Arts & Crafts (2015)

More Faithful (2015) was released on Arts & Crafts Productions — a label shift from Mexican Summer. Credits confirm the lineup as White-Gluz, Lloyd, Garland Hastings, and Michael Farsky, all credited as Songwriter/Performer, with Jorge Elbrecht returning as producer. Engineers on the album were Davey Jewell and Rob Laakso; mastering by Heba Kadry. This remains the clearest documentation of a four-person band configuration.

Post-Lloyd Era: Drool Sucker and Creep EP (2016)

Laura Lloyd’s last credited appearance in the Discogs record is More Faithful (2015). By Drool Sucker (2016, Topshelf Records), the credited performers are White-Gluz, Garland Hastings, and Michael Farsky. This release brought in Toronto producers Brian Borcherdt (Holy Fuck) and Graham Walsh as co-producers, mixers, and engineers; mastered by Carl Saff. The Creep EP (2016, Grey Market) also appears from this period.

Joyful Noise Years and Sonic Boom Split (2018)

No Joy signed to Joyful Noise Recordings for a run of releases in 2018. Blue Room Sessions (2018, Third Man Records) credited White-Gluz (vocals, guitar), Jorge Elbrecht (vocals, guitar), and Jamie Thompson (drums). The split LP with Sonic Boom (No Joy / Sonic Boom, 2018, Joyful Noise) saw White-Gluz and Pete Kember (Sonic Boom) share writing and production duties. Two more 2018 singles appeared on Joyful Noise: Obsession (Radio Edit) and Silent Night / There’s Always Tomorrow.

Motherhood (2020)

Motherhood (2020) was released jointly on Hand Drawn Dracula (cat. HDD084) and Joyful Noise Recordings (cat. JNR 340). The album is credited entirely to No Joy with no named performers in the Discogs record — Discogs lists no additional credits beyond the band. Tracklist: Birthmark / Dream Rats / Nothing Will Hurt / Four / Ageless / Why Mothers Die / Happy Bleeding / Signal Lights / Fish / Primal Curse / Kidder. Genre tags: Electronic, Rock; styles: Indie Pop, Indie Rock, Shoegaze.

Bugland and Collaboration with Fire-Toolz (2025)

Bugland (2025, Hand Drawn Dracula) is a full collaborative album with experimental producer Fire-Toolz (Angel Marcloid). Credits: White-Gluz (vocals, lyrics, guitar, bass, synth, piano), Angel Marcloid/Fire-Toolz (guitar, keyboards, VST drums, electronics, bass; also producer and mixer), Heba Kadry (mastering). This release marks a sharp stylistic departure, aligning No Joy’s shoegaze foundations with Fire-Toolz’s dense electronic/noise-pop production.
