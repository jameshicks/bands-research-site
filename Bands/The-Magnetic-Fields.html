The Magnetic Fields

Discogs artist ID: 41095. US indie pop group led by songwriter Stephin Merritt, Boston/New York. Formed early 1990s. Distant Plastic Trees (1991) — their debut album proper — was released in the UK/Europe on Red Flame (RFCD 3), making Red Flame one of the earliest non-American labels to release Merritt’s work. Vocals on the record by Susan Anway. Later famous for the triple-LP 69 Love Songs (1999, Merge Records).
