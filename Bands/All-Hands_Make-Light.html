All Hands_Make Light

Montreal duo of Efrim Menuck and Ariel Engle, formed c. 2021. Discogs artist ID: 10018471.

Overview

All Hands_Make Light is a collaborative project between Efrim Menuck (founding member of Godspeed You Black Emperor! and A Silver Mt. Zion) and Ariel Engle (member of Broken Social Scene, solo project La Force (2), and duo AroarA). The pairing brings together two of Montreal’s most prominent experimental and post-rock figures — Menuck from the post-rock / drone / political-noise axis, Engle from the indie-pop / art-song axis — into a project that sits closer to Menuck’s aesthetic world: slow, heavy, drone-inflected rock, with Engle’s vocals as the central melodic anchor over Menuck’s textural “Noises.”

The duo debuted with a self-released cassette and digital LP in 2021, then moved to Constellation Records (the Montreal label most associated with Menuck’s other projects) for their second album Darling The Dawn (2023). The second album expands the core duo with session contributions from a tight constellation of Montreal collaborators.
