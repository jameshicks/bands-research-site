Chumbawamba

Discogs ID: 59549
Origin: Burnley / Leeds, England
Active: 1982–2012
Genres: Anarcho-punk, pop-punk, folk, new wave

British anarcho-punk collective turned pop provocateurs. Three releases on Southern Records 1992–1994, including Shhh (18515-1, 1992). Best known internationally for “Tubthumping” (1997, EMI/Republic).
