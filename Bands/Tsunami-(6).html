Tsunami (6)

Indie rock band from Arlington, Virginia, formed 1990. Discogs artist ID: 305876. (The “(6)” disambiguation distinguishes this from other artists named Tsunami on Discogs.)

Overview

Tsunami formed in late 1990 when housemates Jenny Toomey and Kristin Thomson decided to play a New Year’s party. They recruited drummer John Pamer and bassist Andrew Webster (from the band Bricks and Jenny’s earlier group Geek). The band was central to the DC/Arlington indie scene of the early 1990s and released most of their catalog on Simple Machines, the label co-run by Toomey and Thomson.

Franklin Bruno (of Nothing Painted Blue) is listed as a member on Discogs, though his specific credits on their releases are not documented in the DB. The connection reflects the broader DC/LA indie network and Simple Machines ties: Bruno released A Bedroom Community (1995) on Simple Machines.

The band released a late comeback LP, Loud Is As, on Numero Group in 2024.
