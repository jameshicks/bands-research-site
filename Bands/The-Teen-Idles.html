The Teen Idles

Discogs ID: 271699 Active: 1979–1980 Origin: Washington, DC, USA Genres: Hardcore Punk Label: Dischord Records

The Teen Idles were a Washington, DC hardcore punk band active 1979–1980, notable as one of the earliest bands in the DC hardcore scene and as the founding act behind Dischord Records. Formed from high school classmates, the band released a single EP — Minor Disturbance (Dischord 1, 1980) — before breaking up, but their brief existence seeded the entire DC hardcore ecosystem. Members Ian MacKaye and Jeff Nelson went on to form Minor Threat and continue running Dischord to this day.

Overview

The Teen Idles grew out of an earlier band called The Slinkees, which featured the same core of MacKaye, Nelson, and Geordie Grindle alongside vocalist Mark Sullivan. When Sullivan left for college, Strejcek stepped in as vocalist and the group rechristened themselves The Teen Idles.

Through 1979 and 1980 they played regularly in the DC area. In the summer of 1980 they traveled to California for two shows, and the LA hardcore scene — particularly the practice of slamdancing — made a strong impression. They returned to DC having absorbed those influences, and are credited with importing them into the nascent local scene, which in turn called itself “harDCore.”

The band broke up in late 1980 under a combination of pressures: tension between Grindle and Nelson (partly driven by Grindle’s born-again Christian girlfriend’s disapproval of the band), and MacKaye’s frustration with Strejcek’s performances and his own desire to be the vocalist. Rather than let the money they had saved from shows sit idle, the members pooled it to press their own EP. They founded Dischord Records specifically to release it, making Minor Disturbance E.P. catalog number Dischord 1.

After the split, Grindle stepped away from music. Strejcek helped MacKaye and Nelson with Dischord initially but was edged out — partly due to an incident in which he left a box of Teen Idles records sitting on a hot car engine, destroying them. Strejcek went on to form Youth Brigade (2) (DC’s Youth Brigade, not to be confused with the LA band), which was short-lived; he subsequently left the scene. MacKaye and Nelson formed Minor Threat and continued building Dischord into a defining institution of American independent punk.

In 1996, to mark Dischord’s 100th release, the label issued the Anniversary EP (Dischord 100) — previously unheard Teen Idles demo recordings drawn from home tapes, including material from the Slinkees-era.

Connections

  • Dischord Records — founded by MacKaye and Nelson specifically to release Minor Disturbance E.P.; the band’s EP is Dischord 1
  • Minor Threat — formed by MacKaye and Nelson after the Teen Idles split; direct continuation
  • The Slinkees — direct predecessor band; same members minus Strejcek, plus Mark Sullivan (4) on vocals (Discogs ID: 1316410)
  • Youth Brigade (2) — DC’s Youth Brigade, formed by Strejcek post-Teen Idles; appeared alongside Teen Idles on the Four Old 7”s comp
  • Ian MacKaye — bass, lyricist; co-founded Dischord; went on to Minor Threat, Fugazi, The Evens
  • Jeff Nelson — drums, graphics; co-founded Dischord; went on to Minor Threat
  • Skip Groff — producer of Minor Disturbance; ran Yesterday and Today Records in DC, an early supporter of the scene
  • Don Zientara — engineer; ran Inner Ear Studio, the recording home of much of the DC hardcore scene
  • State Of Alert (ID: 271698) — Henry Garfield’s (Henry Rollins) band; appeared on same Four Old 7”s comp on Dischord
