Sentridoh

Solo alias of Lou Barlow (Louis Knox Barlow). Discogs artist ID: 287365.

Overview

Sentridoh is the primary cassette solo alias used by Lou Barlow — bassist/guitarist of Dinosaur Jr. and co-founder of Sebadoh — for his most stripped-back home recordings. The Sentridoh name was used on Shrimper and a handful of other small labels for releases spanning the early 1990s to mid-2000s.

Key Shrimper appearances include Losers (SHR 12, 1991), Most Of The WORST And Some Of The Best Of Sentridoh (SHR 36, 1992), Lou B’s Wasted Pieces ‘87–‘93 (SHR 48, 1993, credited to “Lou Barlow / Sentridoh”), and The Original Losing Losers ‘82–‘91 (SHR 67, 1995).
