Overview

Spoons are a Canadian New Wave band formed in 1979 in Burlington, Ontario, whose catalogue spans synth-pop, post-punk, and pop rock across four decades. Their output was heaviest in the 1980s, with Ready Records serving as their primary label from 1981 all the way through 2021 — a continuity that bracketed a mid-decade detour through the Anthem and Mercury imprints. Producer John Punter is their dominant collaborator, appearing across nearly half their releases, while Nile Rodgers’ credit count of 24 shared releases points to a substantial production or remix involvement well beyond a single session.
