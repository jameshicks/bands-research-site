Death Grips

Experimental hip-hop / industrial group, Sacramento, California. Formed 2010. Members: Stefan Burnett (MC Ride, vocals/lyrics), Zach Hill (drums/production/lyrics), Andy Morin (keyboards/production/lyrics). Discogs ID: 2225756.

Defined a decade of post-genre aggressive music — punk, hip-hop, noise, industrial collapsed into one. Known for confrontational releases, deliberately leaking No Love Deep Web to spite Epic Records in 2012, and running their own imprint Third Worlds.

Upcoming / In Progress

Untitled album (announced November 2025)

On 5 November 2025, Burnett and Hill posted a carousel of studio photos on the Death Grips Instagram with the caption: “The writing and recording of our next album is underway. We’re looking forward to the new Death Grips record.” The post was signed by Burnett and Hill only — Andy Morin was not mentioned, consistent with his c. 2023 departure. source source source

Background: In February 2025 leaked texts attributed to Morin claimed the group was “over”; Burnett and Hill responded in April 2025 with a public statement: “Despite rumour and hearsay, we remain active as Death Grips.” source
