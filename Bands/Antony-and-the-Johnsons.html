Antony and the Johnsons

New York-based chamber-pop / avant-folk group. Discogs artist ID: 185134. Active 2000–2014 (revived as Anohni And The Johnsons from 2023). Led by ANOHNI (then credited as Antony Hegarty).

Overview

Antony And The Johnsons emerged from the downtown New York cabaret and experimental music scene in the late 1990s. The lineup was fluid — centered on ANOHNI’s voice and piano with a rotating cast of strings, reeds, and rhythm players. Early releases on Durtro (David Tibet’s UK imprint) reached a small audience; the project broke internationally when I Am A Bird Now (2005) won the UK Mercury Prize.

The group’s sound: spare piano, cello and string arrangements, minimal rhythm, and ANOHNI’s high, operatic, gender-fluid voice. Lyrical themes include trans identity, grief, environmental mourning, and spiritual longing.
