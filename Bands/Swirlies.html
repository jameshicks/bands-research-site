Swirlies

Discogs ID: 136975
Also known as: The Yes Girls (alias); The Swirlies (name variation)
Origin: Boston, MA, US
Active: 1990–present (intermittently)
Genres: Lo-fi, shoegaze, indie rock, experimental

Overview

Swirlies formed in Boston in 1990 around the core of guitarist/vocalist Damon Tutunjian and guitarist/vocalist Seana Carmody, with Andy Bernick (bass) and Ben Drucker (drums) completing the original lineup through 1993. The band became a fixture of the early-1990s US indie underground — releasing primarily through Taang! — and was distinctive for combining shoegaze noise and Moog/Mellotron texture with a deliberately lo-fi, cassette-culture aesthetic. Their 1993 debut full-length Blonder Tongue Audio Baton is the canonical statement of this sound. Membership was always fluid; by the later 1990s the core had contracted to Tutunjian plus rotating collaborators including Christina Files and Anthony De Luca, and by the 2003 record Cats Of The Wild: Volume Two the band had become almost a collective vehicle, crediting over a dozen performers including future solo star Kurt Vile, Adam Pierce (later of Mice Parade), Kevin Shea, and Rob Laakso. The band has released records sporadically into the 2010s–2020s on their own Sneaky Flute Empire imprint.

The Yes Girls alias appears on a 2000 Sneaky Flute Empire / Pehr compilation (Damon Andy Rob Ron: The Yes Girls) that documents that smaller-lineup iteration.
