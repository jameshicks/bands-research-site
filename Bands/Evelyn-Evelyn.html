Evelyn Evelyn

American conceptual music duo. Discogs artist ID: 1763364. Also listed as “Evelyn and Evelyn Neville.”

Overview

Evelyn Evelyn is a fictional duo project by Amanda Palmer and Jason Webley, portraying conjoined twin sisters named Eva and Lyn Neville. The project began in September 2007 with the EP Elephant Elephant (11 Records). The conceit involves an invented backstory of twin sisters raised in a circus, told through music, a graphic novel, and tour performances. The full LP Evelyn Evelyn was released on March 30, 2010, co-released between 11 Records and 8ft. Records (cat: XI-13). A companion graphic novel illustrated by Cynthia von Buhler was published by Diamond Comic Distributors in 2011.
