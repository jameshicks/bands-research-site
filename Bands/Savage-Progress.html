Overview

Savage Progress were a short-lived British synthpop group, active only between 1982 and 1984, with a lineup including Andrew Edge, Carol Isaacs, Glynnis, Ned Morant, and Rik Kenton. Their six 1980s releases appeared on 10 Records and Virgin, drawing from synth-pop, new wave, and disco. The band worked closely with Danny Hyde (32 shared credits) and Wix (18 shared credits), both of whom would go on to prominent careers in British music. A solitary release on Findlaters in 2008 marks their only post-1980s catalogue entry.
