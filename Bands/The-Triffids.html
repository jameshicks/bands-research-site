The Triffids

Australian rock band, Perth, Western Australia. Discogs artist ID: 256846

Overview

The Triffids were one of the most acclaimed Australian bands of the 1980s, led by vocalist and songwriter David McComb. The band was also the origin point for several members of Blackeyed Susans: Phil Kakulas (bass), Evil Graham Lee (pedal steel), and David McComb all moved between or formed the Blackeyed Susans after The Triffids dissolved (officially 1989).

Key Members (Blackeyed Susans cross-connections)

  • David McComb — vocalist, songwriter; Triffids founder; died 1999
  • Phil Kakulas — bass
  • Evil Graham Lee — pedal steel guitar
