Marriages

Discogs ID: 2741503 Active: 2012–2015 (per Discogs credits) Origin: Los Angeles, California Members: Emma Ruth Rundle (guitar, vocals), Dave Clifford (drums), Greg Burns (2) (bass, synthesizer, art design), Andrew Clinco (drums/percussion on Salome)

Post-rock/shoegaze trio formed by three Red Sparowes members after that band wound down. Released on Sargent House. Emma Ruth Rundle subsequently launched a prominent solo career on Sargent House.

A fourth credited member on Salome is Andrew Clinco (drums, percussion, guitar on one track), suggesting a lineup change between the two LPs. Marriages also worked with Andrew Clinco and Fred Sablan (guest guitar on Salome track B4).
