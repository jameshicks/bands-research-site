Best Coast

Discogs ID: 1536084
Origin: Los Angeles / Long Beach, California
Active: 2009–present
Genres: Indie Rock, Surf Pop, Lo-Fi
Labels: Mexican Summer, Wichita Recordings, Harvest (2), Concord Records, Art Fag Recordings, Jewel City
Members: Bethany Cosentino (vocals, guitar), Bobb Bruno (guitar, bass, drums, production)

Surf-pop / indie rock duo from Los Angeles, formed 2009. Bethany Cosentino is the primary songwriter and vocalist; Bobb Bruno is the primary instrumentalist and co-arranger. Live drumming has been handled by rotating members (Ali Koehler, Garrett Ray, Brady Miller). The band’s sound evolved from home-recorded lo-fi cassette pop (2009–2010) through polished LA studio rock (2012–2015) to a more direct power-pop production style (2020).

Bethany Cosentino was previously in Pocahaunted (with Amanda Brown (2), on Not Not Fun Records, 21 releases 2006–2010); Bobb Bruno was also in Pocahaunted and Magic Lantern. The shift from Pocahaunted/NNF to Best Coast/Mexican Summer traces the exact moment the Long Beach psychedelic underground crossed into the indie mainstream (~2009–2010).

Biography

2009 — Origins and First Releases

Best Coast launched with a run of limited 7” singles and cassettes, all recorded and performed by Cosentino and Bruno in a deliberately lo-fi home-recording style. The debut 7” Best Coast (Art Fag Recordings AF007, 2009) paired “Sun Was High (So Was I)” with “So Gone” and a Lesley Gore cover (“That’s The Way Boys Are”). Design by Cosentino and Roy Tatum; Bruno played all instruments except vocals. Other early singles came out on Group Tightener (Make You Mine), Black Iris Music (When I’m With You), and Blackest Rainbow (Where The Boys Are).

2010 — Crazy For You and Mexican Summer

Debut LP Crazy For You (Mexican Summer MEX 053, 2010) established the core Best Coast sound: Cosentino on vocals and guitar; Bruno on guitar, bass, drums; Lewis Pesacov as engineer, mixer, and additional instrumentalist (guitar, bass, keyboards); Garrett Ray on additional percussion; Michael Harris (10) on additional engineering. 12 tracks, limited first pressing on vinyl. Simultaneously released “Tough Guys / Up All Night” on French label Atelier Ciseaux and the Summer Is Forever split 7” with Wavves on Mexican Summer (MEX 079, 2011).

2011 — Summer Is Forever EP

Summer Is Forever (Mexican Summer, 2011) — a 6-track EP following the debut, still in the lo-fi register. Cosentino credited as Written-By.

2012 — The Only Place

Second LP The Only Place (Mexican Summer MEX 109, 2012) marked a significant production shift. Jon Brion produced and played multiple instruments (percussion, keyboards, twelve-string guitar, lap steel guitar, bass viol). Greg Koller engineered and mixed; Patricia Sullivan mastered; assistant engineers Chandler Harrod and Jake Gorski; Eric Caudieux edited. Bobb Bruno: drums, guitar, bass. Bethany Cosentino: vocals, guitar, songwriter. Design by Dan Schechter; illustration by Jess Rotter. 11 tracks.

2013 — Fade Away EP

Fade Away (Jewel City JWCY001VL1, 2013) — 7-track mini-album. Produced, engineered, and mixed by Wally Gagel. Bobb Bruno: guitar, bass. Bethany Cosentino: vocals, guitar, keyboards, songwriter. Brady Miller (2) on drums; Ricky Cosentino on drums (tracks A3, A4); Brett Mielke on lap steel and 12-string; Xandy Barry on piano and additional engineering. Pete Lyman mastered.

2015 — California Nights

Third LP California Nights (Harvest, 2015). Wally Gagel returned as producer, engineer, and mixer; Brady Miller (2) on drums, guitar, percussion, and keyboards; Bobb Bruno: guitar, bass, keyboards; Bethany Cosentino: guitar, vocals, songwriter. Seth Olansky and Vincent Silva as assistant producers/engineers/mixers. Howie Weinberg mastered. Released on Harvest (Capitol/Universal). 12 tracks.

2016 — Summer Is Forever 2 EP

Summer Is Forever 2 (Ghost Ramp, 2016) — companion EP to the Wavves collaboration strand. Also a “Late 20s / Bigger Man” single on Harvest Records.

2018 — Self-Released Material

Best Kids (2018) — self-released.

2020 — Always Tomorrow

Fourth LP Always Tomorrow (Concord Records CRE01185, 2020). Producers: Justin Meldal-Johnsen and Carlos De La Garza (who also engineered, mixed, and contributed instrumentation). Bobb Bruno: guitar, bass, keyboards. Bethany Cosentino: vocals, guitar, keyboards, percussion. Kiel Feher on drums; Zac Rae on keyboards. 11 tracks. Mastered by Dave Cooley (one credit; Alan Moulder mixed at least one track). John Congleton also credited.
