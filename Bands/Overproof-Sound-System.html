Overproof Sound System

Bristol-based dub/reggae sound system collective. Discogs artist ID: 104474. Active 2002–2012 (primary release window). The core production unit is Brian Nordhoff and Roberto Cimarosti of Groove Corporation, credited here as G. Corp. Extended membership includes Ras Tweed (Kevin Tweed), Michael Madden (2) (Juggla), and R. Adams.

Name variations on record: Over Proof Sound System · Over-Proof-Sound-System · Overproof Soundsystem · Overproof Soundsytem · The Overproof Soundsystem.

Overview

Overproof Sound System is a sister project to Groove Corporation, using the same core production team (Nordhoff + Cimarosti as G. Corp) but framed as a fully-assembled sound system with additional vocalists and toasters. Where Groove Corporation releases tend toward studio-crafted dub and electronic hybrids, the Overproof Sound System output is more directly rooted in reggae/dancehall riddim culture — sound system “dub plates,” vocal cuts over live-sounding rhythms, and roots-flavoured album work.

All early releases appeared on Different Drummer (Birmingham). Later output moved to self-production via Elephant House Recordings and, for the sole album, Collision: Cause Of Chapter 3 (a sublabel of Echo Beach, Germany).

Release Notes

Watch What You Put Inna… EP (2002, DIFCD21)

Six versions of the debut track. Remixes split between G.Corp (Radio Version, Version, Dub Version) and Different Drummer in-house production (Different Drummer Radio Version, Version, Instrumental). No additional session credits logged in Discogs beyond the primary artist.

Nothing To Proove EP (2004, DIFEP27)

Vinyl-only 4-tracker. Producer/recorded by Groove Corporation as G. Corp. Tracks: “The Plea” / “Kunta Kinte” / “Get With It” / “Herb Dub.”

Nothing To Proove (2004, DIFCD27)

12-track CD album, the expanded version of the EP material plus new cuts. Notable track-level vocal credits:

  • Track 2 “Live It Up Right” — vocals by Kenijah Boothe (Discogs ID: 845815)
  • Track 9 “The Plea” — backing vocals by Jackie Deane (Discogs ID: 78318)
  • Track 12 “The Herb” — vocals by Cheshire Cat (Discogs ID: 11533)

The album title is also spelled “Nothing To Prove” but the Discogs primary entry uses “Proove” (DIFCD27/DIFEP27 both use this spelling).

The Model (2008, BANG05)

Self-released 12” (Not On Label, catalogue number BANG05). A-side is a dub reggae cover of Kraftwerk’s “Das Modell” — the A1/A2 writing credits go to Kraftwerk members Karl Bartos, Ralf Hütter, and lyricist Emil Schult. The B-side revisits “Watch What You Put Inna” with Nordhoff, Cimarosti, Tweed, Stevens, and Madden as writers.

Kingstep (2009, EHR 1203)

The only release on Elephant House Recordings (Discogs ID: 145132) — likely the group’s own Bristol imprint (note alignment with Groove Corporation’s “Elephant House” studio brand). Discogs credits R. Douglas (ID: 1098472, realname Eric Smallwood) as producer — a separate entry from Messenger Douglas (ID: 946270), though both carry “R. Douglas” as a name variation. Whether these are the same individual is unresolved; see the Messenger Douglas page for details. G. Corp is credited for additional production and mixing. B-side remix by Unitone HiFi, a New Zealand–based dub/reggae outfit.

Pull It Up! (2012, CCT3026-2)

18-track CD album on Echo Beach’s Collision: Cause Of Chapter 3 sublabel (Germany). Genres: Electronic, Reggae; styles: Dancehall, Dub, Reggae. G. Corp produced and mixed the full album; Messenger Douglas produced tracks 14 (“Unity”), 15 (“King Step”), and 16 (“Mama”), with G. Corp on additional production and mixing. Track 9 “War Must Cease” features The Myspace Choir — a large ensemble credit listing: C. Kelly (5), E. Sze Kit Ng, G. Douglas (4), I. Costa, Jaffa (9) (Jeffrey Wright), Jim Megarry, K. Charnock, L. Filce, M. Gibbs, Ninety (as Ninety (9T)), M. Mullins, S. Altaf, S. Morrison (3), S. Mullins, S. Pellarin. Artwork by Ninety.
