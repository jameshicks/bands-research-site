Wire

British post-punk band. Formed London, 1976. Members include Colin Newman, Graham Lewis, Bruce Gilbert, and Robert Gotobed. Discogs artist ID: 21323.

Overview

Pioneer post-punk group whose debut trio of albums — Pink Flag (1977), Chairs Missing (1978), 154 (1979) — are widely regarded as foundational. Known for radical stylistic reinvention across each release. Disbanded 1980, reformed 1985, and have continued in various configurations since.

State51 released reissues of Pink Flag and Chairs Missing in 2006. Wire maintain their own label Wire-Sound.
