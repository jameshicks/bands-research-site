Chain And The Gang

Washington DC punk/soul band led by Ian Svenonius, active 2009–2021. Discogs ID: 1447437

Overview

Long-running project with a rotating lineup anchored by Svenonius and James Canty. Released primarily on K Records and In The Red Recordings. Name variations: CHAIN, Chain & The Gang.

Key Members (various lineups)

  • Ian Svenonius — vocals, primary songwriter
  • James Canty — guitar
  • Brian Weber, Katie Alice Greer, Veronica Ortuño, Anna Nasty, Fiona Campbell among rotating members
