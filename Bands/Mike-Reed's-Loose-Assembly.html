Overview

Mike Reed’s Loose Assembly was a Chicago-based jazz quintet led by drummer and composer Mike Reed (2) (born Bielefeld, Germany, 1974; Chicago-based since 1997). The group released three albums on 482 Music between 2007 and 2010, operating at the intersection of structured composition and open improvisation. The core lineup — Greg Ward (2) (alto saxophone), Jason Adasiewicz (vibraphone), Joshua Abrams (bass), and Tomeka Reid (cello) — remained consistent across all three records. The final album, Empathetic Parts (2010), featured AACM legend Roscoe Mitchell as a co-billed collaborator. The ensemble occasionally appears credited as “Loose Assembly” on individual track compositions.
