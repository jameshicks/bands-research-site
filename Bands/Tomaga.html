Tomaga

Discogs ID: 3433688
Based: London, UK
Active: 2013–2020
Genre: Experimental electronic, avant-garde, improvised music
Members: Tom Relleen, Valentina Magaletti

Overview

Tomaga was a London-based experimental electronic duo consisting of Tom Relleen (synthesizer, bass guitar, organ, mbira, cello, electronics, drum machine, vibraphone, phonography, found objects; producer and recording engineer) and Valentina Magaletti (drums, prepared drums, percussion, metalwork, vibraphone, voice). They released four studio albums and several EPs primarily on Hands In The Dark between 2014 and 2021 (including posthumous releases following Relleen’s death on 23 August 2020). Their sound combined rhythmic improvisation with dense synthesizer textures, found objects, and occasional contributions from brass, strings, and electronics guests.

Marta Salogni served as mixing engineer from Extended Play 1 (2019) onward, including the posthumous Intimate Immensity (2021) and the Salogni/Relleen collaborative LP Music For Open Spaces (2023). Chris Potter mastered the first two HITD albums; Sarah Register mastered Intimate Immensity.

Relleen also co-ran Negative Days (with others), which issued early Tomaga digital releases and EPs, and ran All Time Low separately. The debut Sleepy Jazz For Tired Cats (2013) appeared on Bunkland Records, a small UK imprint, before the pair moved to Hands In The Dark for the duration of their studio career.

Tom Relleen — Other Projects

Beyond Tomaga, Relleen was active in several parallel projects documented in the Discogs DB:

  • The Oscillation — bass player in Demian Castellanos’s London psychedelic/krautrock band; multiple albums on Cardinal Fuzz, All Time Low, and Rocket Recordings
  • Papivores — duo with Agathe Max; Death And Spring (Hands In The Dark, 2019); mixed by Salogni
  • Autotelia — duo with Demian Castellanos (The Oscillation); I (Rocket Recordings, 2020); mixed by Salogni
  • Staran Wake (Discogs group ID: 12722726) — no releases documented in DB
  • All Time Low — Relleen’s own label; released The Oscillation material and compilations
  • Negative Days (Discogs ID: 780449) — co-ran label; issued early Tomaga EPs and co-releases with HITD
  • Co-founder of Phonica Records — London record shop (not a label)
  • Posthumous: Music For Open Spaces (2023, Hands In The Dark) — credited to Marta Salogni & Tom Relleen; both as writer, performer, producer; Relleen also credited for “Internal Map” artwork
