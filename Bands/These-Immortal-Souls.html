These Immortal Souls

Australian post-punk band formed in London in 1987, led by Rowland S. Howard following the dissolution of Crime & The City Solution. Discogs artist ID: 284765.

Overview

These Immortal Souls were formed when Rowland S. Howard — formerly of The Boys Next Door, Young Charlatans, The Birthday Party, and Crime & The City Solution — broke away to lead his own group. The band was founded in London in 1987 (Discogs gives 1986 as a founding year per Genevieve McGuckin’s profile), and initially operated out of the UK. Howard’s partner and co-founder Genevieve McGuckin (Vox Continental organ, piano) joined from the outset, as did Howard’s brother Harry Howard (bass) and Epic Soundtracks (drums, percussion; real name Kevin Godfrey), who was simultaneously playing in Crime & The City Solution and Swell Maps.

The band released two studio albums on Mute Records — Get Lost (Don’t Lie) (1987) and I’m Never Gonna Die Again (1992) — before dissolving in July 1998. The five-year gap between albums, plus the band’s relocation from London to Melbourne in the mid-1990s, contributed to their relative obscurity compared to Howard’s earlier work with The Birthday Party.

Epic Soundtracks died in November 1997, before the band’s official dissolution in 1998. An additional member listed on Discogs is Craig Williamson (drums; ID 667631), who presumably served as a later drummer after Epic. A second listed member, Craig Williams (4) (ID 1180676), may represent the same individual under a variant name.

Spencer P. Jones (Spencer P. Jones; guitar) is also listed by Howard’s Discogs profile as an additional member who played with the band.
