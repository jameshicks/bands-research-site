White Hills

New York City-based psychedelic space-rock band, formed c. 2005. Core duo: Dave W. (guitar/vocals; real name Dave Weinberg) and Ego Sensation (bass/vocals; real name Lynnea Scalora). Discogs artist ID: 424983.

Overview

White Hills emerged from the New York underground in the mid-2000s, self-releasing material on their own White Hills Music imprint before moving to Thrill Jockey for their wider-exposure period (2009–2017 and again 2025). The band describe themselves as “Motorik Fuzz Art-Rock” but have been tagged variously as Krautrock, Industrial, Psychedelic, Goth, and Post-Punk. Dave W. and Ego Sensation form the permanent creative core; the band has cycled through numerous drummers and additional players over the years, with Kid Millions (John Colpitts, also of Oneida) serving as a key early percussionist. The group recorded extensively at Martin Bisi’s BC Studio in Brooklyn across multiple albums. White Hills also appear in Jim Jarmusch’s film Only Lovers Left Alive (2013). Their own label, Heads On Fire, launched around 2021 and has anchored their most recent releases.

Name variation on Discogs: Black Valleys (White Hills).
