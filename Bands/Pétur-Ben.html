Overview

Pétur Ben is the solo project of Pétur Þór Benediktsson, an Icelandic musician born in 1976 in Reykjavik. Active since 2004, the catalogue spans indie rock, post-punk, experimental and acoustic styles across three decades, with the core output concentrated in the 2010s. Early releases appeared on Reykjavik’s 12 Tónar, while later work reached international labels — 4AD in 2019 and One Little Independent Records in 2022 — reflecting a broadening profile. His most consistent collaborator is Birgir Jón Birgisson across 14 shared releases; he has also operated as part of Pétur Ben & Eberg and holds membership in Kippi Kaninus.
