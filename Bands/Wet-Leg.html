Wet Leg

Discogs artist ID: 9611545 Origin: Isle of Wight, UK Active: 2019–present Label: Domino Genres: Indie Rock, Post-Punk

British indie rock duo formed by Rhian Teasdale and Hester Chambers on the Isle of Wight. Their debut single “Chaise Longue” (2021) went viral and announced one of the more striking British debuts of the decade; their self-titled debut album (2022) was shortlisted for the Mercury Prize. Second album Moisturizer followed in 2025 on Domino. Their live band expands the duo to a five-piece.

History

Rhian Teasdale and Hester Chambers met at Isle of Wight College while studying music, roughly ten years before they formed Wet Leg. The band was founded in 2019. Their first public release, “Chaise Longue,” appeared in June 2021 — a deadpan, hook-driven post-punk song that spread rapidly online and established the dry wit that would define the project. Two more singles followed in 2021: “Wet Dream” and the double A-side “Too Late Now / Oh No.”

The self-titled debut album Wet Leg was released on Domino in April 2022 and was recorded in multiple sessions with producer Dan Carey, who handled the bulk of the album (tracks A1 and A4–B6), with “Chaise Longue” produced by Jon McMullen and “Angelica” produced by Joshua Mobaraki (both also members of the live band). The album was mixed by Alan Moulder and mastered by Matt Colton at Metropolis. It was shortlisted for the 2022 Mercury Prize.

In 2022, Soulwax contributed a remix of “Too Late Now,” released as a two-track single.

The second album, Moisturizer, arrived in 2025 on Domino. Dan Carey returned as producer; Alan Moulder again mixed; Matt Colton again mastered. The expanded live lineup — Teasdale, Chambers, Mobaraki, Henry Holmes (drums), and Ellis Durand (bass) — played on the record. Hester Chambers contributed artwork (reverse sleeve) alongside cover artist Iris Luz.
