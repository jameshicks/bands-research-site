Leathermouth

American hardcore punk band. Discogs artist ID: 1663721. Also typeset as LeATHERMØUTH. Formed 2007, New Jersey. Active 2007–2010; one-off reunion tour May 2013. Side project of Frank Iero (My Chemical Romance).

  • Genres: Hardcore punk, post-hardcore
  • Label: Epitaph Records (Epitaph, ID: 61599)
  • Active: 2007–2010 (reunion 2013)
  • Country: US

Overview

Leathermouth was formed in 2007 by a group of Frank Iero’s friends — the band existed before Iero joined. When the original vocalist failed to produce lyrics, Iero stepped in as lead singer. The project gave him an outlet for aggressive, fast hardcore far outside My Chemical Romance’s arena-rock sound. They released one studio album, XØ (January 27, 2009, Epitaph), then disbanded in 2010. A one-off reunion tour occurred on May 19, 2013.

The MCR-to-Leathermouth crossover is specifically a Frank Iero–James Dewees axis: both men are members of MCR (Iero as rhythm guitarist, Dewees as touring keyboardist), Leathermouth, and Death Spells — a digital hardcore project they formed together. Dewees brought his experience from Coalesce and The Get Up Kids into the rhythm section.

The XØ album was self-produced by the band alongside Steve Oyolla and mastered by George Marino. It was released on Epitaph Records, putting the band alongside the label’s long-running catalog of punk and hardcore artists.

Reissue History

XØ has been pressed multiple times since its original 2009 release (15 pressings total across the master). Significant reissue waves occurred in 2018 (US and international) and 2023–2024 (multiple US vinyl variants). The album has remained in print, suggesting sustained catalog demand on Epitaph.

Scene Connections

Leathermouth sits at the intersection of several orbits:

  • MCR orbit: Iero’s hardcore outlet while MCR was at commercial peak. Dewees’s presence ties in the MCR touring band.
  • Post-hardcore / Kansas City hardcore: Dewees brought his Coalesce and Get Up Kids pedigree directly into the rhythm section.
  • Epitaph Records: Places the band alongside Bad Religion, Rancid, Alkaline Trio, and the label’s broader punk catalog.
  • Death Spells: Iero and Dewees reconvened for Death Spells, releasing Nothing Above, Nothing Below (2016, Vagrant Records) — a digital hardcore project that extends the aggressive sonic territory of Leathermouth.
  • Iero solo trajectory: Post-MCR, Iero continued releasing under his own name — Frank Iero And The Patience (Parachutes, 2016), Frank Iero And The Future Violents (Barriers, 2019; Heaven Is A Place, This Is A Place, 2021), and L.S. Dunes (2022).
