Overview

Bob James Trio is a jazz ensemble led by pianist Bob James, whose catalogue stretches from 1963 into the 2020s in distinct, widely spaced clusters rather than as a continuous working band. The earliest output — on Mercury and ESP-Disk’ — sits in free jazz and avant-garde territory, placing James alongside figures like Gordon Mumma and Robert Ashley before a gap of roughly three decades. When the trio re-emerged in the 1990s it operated in a post-bop and contemporary jazz mode, drawing on a rotating cast of prominent rhythm-section players — Christian McBride, Brian Blade, and Billy Kilson among them — and releasing through Warner Bros., Verve, and eventually Evosound into the 2020s.
