Skin (2)

Also known as The World Of Skin / World Of Skin / The World Of Skin (Swans). Discogs artist ID: 149804.

Side project of Michael Gira and Jarboe (both of Swans), with additional members Clinton Steele and Steve McAllister. Active c.1987–1990. Name variants were used interchangeably across releases — the Discogs artist page carries “The World Of Skin,” “World Of Skin,” and “The World Of Skin (Swans)” as name variations.

Overview

Skin (2) grew directly out of the mid-1980s Swans orbit, with the first two albums recorded in the wake of Swans’ 1986 live album Public Castration Is A Good Idea. Per the Discogs profile, these recordings directly informed Swans’ subsequent studio record Children Of God (1987). Where Swans was dense, abrasive, and physically imposing, Skin worked in a quieter, more intimate register: piano-led compositions, Jarboe’s voice as the primary sonic surface, and chamber string arrangements alongside Gira’s drum programming and textural work. The combination of Jarboe’s melodic sensibility with Gira’s lyrical bleakness produced a body of work that reads as a confessional counterpart to Swans’ maximalism.

The initial releases — three Product Inc. twelve-inch singles and one LP in 1987–1988 — were recorded across multiple UK studios (Blackwing, Chocolate Factory, Konk Studios, Worldwide Studios) with engineers John Fryer, Paul Kendall, Roop Caulson, and Dave Powell. A US compilation (The World Of Skin, 1988, Product Inc. / Rough Trade) gathered material from these sessions in a slightly different configuration. The final full album, Ten Songs For Another World (1990), appeared on Young God Records — Gira’s own imprint — and introduced Clinton Steele (guitar) and Steve McAllister (drums, bass programming, recording/mixing) as full collaborators. Jarboe and Gira are the only constants across all releases.

The project is credited on Ten Songs For Another World under the primary name “The World Of Skin,” which was also the name used on the 1997 Atavistic/Young God reissue of Children Of God / World Of Skin. After the 1990 album, Skin (2) ceased activity; Swans continued until their first dissolution in 1997.
