Dog Faced Hermans

Discogs ID: 304715 Origin: Edinburgh, Scotland (later Amsterdam, Netherlands) Active: 1986–1995 Genres: Post-Punk, Anarcho-Punk, Experimental Labels: Demon Radge Records, Konkurrel, Alternative Tentacles Name variations: DFH, DGH, The Dog Faced Hermanns, The Dogfaced Hermans

Overview

Dog Faced Hermans were a Scottish post-punk/anarcho-punk band formed in Edinburgh in 1986 and dissolved in 1995, spending much of their later years based in Amsterdam. The core lineup — vocalist and trumpeter Marion Coutts, guitarist Andy Moor (2), bassist Colin McLean, and drummer/saxophonist Wilf Plum — remained stable across their full run, with Gert-Jan Blom joining later as live sound engineer and additional contributor. They self-produced most of their work and were closely allied with the European anarcho/DIY punk network centred on Amsterdam’s Konkurrel imprint and its parent Konkurrent. Their U.S. releases came through Alternative Tentacles (Jello Biafra’s label), embedding them in the North American post-hardcore circuit as well. Andy Moor left the group around 1990 to join The Ex, though Discogs credits confirm he remained on records through at least Hum Of Life (1993) and Those Deep Buds (1994). The band appeared on the At Bimhuis (1991–2015) compilation alongside The Ex.

Labels

Demon Radge Records

Discogs ID: 68739. The band’s primary UK label, with 8 releases total (16 across all pressings). Dog Faced Hermans were essentially the label’s main act — the only other credited artist is Good And Gone (Methil Box, 1987, RADGE 2). The label’s catalog is almost entirely Dog Faced Hermans releases: Unbend (RADGE 1), Live Action & Increasing, Everyday Timebomb, Too Much For The Red Ticker, Humans Fly / Every Day Timebomb, Hum Of Life, and a 2022 reissue of Bella Ciao / Miss O’Grady.

Konkurrel

Discogs ID: 42395. Dutch DIY label, sublabel of Konkurrent (parent: Konkurrent). 134 releases total. Co-released most of the band’s records from 1990 onward alongside Demon Radge. The Konkurrel roster was the heart of the European DIY circuit: Victims Family, Negazione, Nomeansno, De Kift, Verbal Assault, Snuff, False Prophets, Rhythm Activism, Social Unrest, and others. Dog Faced Hermans were among the most represented artists on the label.

Alternative Tentacles

Discogs ID: 10389. Jello Biafra’s US label; 2,031+ releases. Released Those Deep Buds (1994) and Bump And Swing (1995). Connection to the label places Dog Faced Hermans alongside a vast catalog of North American hardcore and punk.

  • Konkurrent — parent of Konkurrel; the Amsterdam DIY label network the band operated within
