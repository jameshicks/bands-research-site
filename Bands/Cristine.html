Cristine

London-based band, formed 2005/2006 by Stephane Bejean (full name Stéphane Bejean-Lebenson, a Parisian who relocated to London). Discogs artist ID: 414890

Released the debut single “Piggy In The Middle / I Freak” (Cassette Records, 2005) as Simian Mobile Disco vs. Cristine — connecting them to the then-emerging Simian Mobile Disco. Second single “Cross The Line / Use” followed on Mute Irregulars/Mute (2007).

Members: Stephane Bejean (vocals/writing), Robert Schultzberg (drums), Tree Carr (guitar/vocals), Douglas Hart (bass — joined 2007).

Notes (web)

Simian Mobile Disco collaboration

The debut 12” “Piggy In The Middle / I Freak” (catalog: C-102) was released on Cassette Records in 2005 as a split/versus single: Simian Mobile Disco’s “Piggy In The Middle” (plus a Punks Jump Up remix) on the A-side, and Cristine’s “I Freak” (plus a Springmine Disaster remix) on the B-side. The release predates SMD’s breakout “Hustler” (Kitsuné, 2006) and sits among their very earliest releases as a duo, alongside “The Count” and “Pulse.” (Wikipedia — Simian Mobile Disco, as of 2026-10-14; EIL.com product listing; Discogs release 523177)

Origin of collaboration — not found. Extensive web search (music press, Last.fm biography, EIL/RareVinyl/Amazon listings, Grokipedia, Wikipedia) turned up no interview, press piece, or first-hand account explaining how Cristine and Simian Mobile Disco came to record together or who brokered the Cassette Records deal. The Last.fm biography for Cristine and the Robert Schultzberg Grokipedia entry both omit any mention of the SMD connection entirely. (Last.fm — Cristine; Grokipedia — Robert Schultzberg)

Additional biographical detail (web)

  • Stéphane Bejean-Lebenson is the sole consistent member/songwriter; the band is his solo project backed by rotating musicians. (Last.fm — Cristine)
  • Robert Schultzberg (drums, ex-Placebo) joined in 2007 — after the debut SMD single, so he was not part of the original SMD collaboration. (Grokipedia — Robert Schultzberg)
  • In 2009 Cristine toured the UK with Primal Scream and RTX on the NME Rock n’ Roll Riot Tour. (Last.fm — Cristine)
