Milk From Cheltenham

Discogs ID: 618090
Origin: UK (Cheltenham area implied by name)
Active: 1983; reconvened for Alga Marghen reissues 2005–2008
Genres: Experimental, Art Rock, Post-Punk
Labels: It’s War Boys (1983), Alga Marghen (2005, 2008)

Experimental/art rock trio from the UK, active primarily in 1983 as part of the It’s War Boys cassette network centred on Jim Welton (aka L. Voag, Xentos, and many other aliases). Members: Lepke Buchvalter, Salamander (6), and Victorr Lounge. Released one cassette, Triptych Of Poisoners (It’s War Boys £3, 1983), which was later reissued as a CD by Italian avant-garde label Alga Marghen in 2005 with expanded credits and additional performers. A second Alga Marghen release, Will Make You Ill (2008), is credited to Modern Shit but features Buchvalter and Welton (as Xentos Bentos) as performers.

The group’s name is unexplained in any documented source. The Cheltenham reference may indicate the actual location of some members — distinct from Welton’s North London base — or may be purely deadpan.

The It’s War Boys Context

Milk From Cheltenham was one of approximately 13 documented releases on It’s War Boys, the micro-label founded and run by Jim Welton from London N5 in the early 1980s. The label’s catalogue ran on a pound-sign numbering system (£1, £2, etc.) with most material credited to aliases. Triptych Of Poisoners is catalogue item £3, one of the earliest entries.

Other significant It’s War Boys releases overlapping with the Milk From Cheltenham personnel:

  • £1 — C’est Fab (Nancy Sesay And The Melodaires, 1982) — also features Victorr Lounge and Welton (as Amos (3))
  • £5 — Flagellation (The Just Measurers, 1983) — Welton, Buchvalter, Chris Gray (2)
  • £21 — Will Make You Sick (Modern Shit, undated) — Welton + Buchvalter; reissued as Will Make You Ill by Alga Marghen

The label’s full intended catalogue ran to at least £31. Items £7, £8, £12, £14–16, £19–20, £22–31 have no corresponding Discogs entries; several are described in the label’s own Discogs profile as lost or “presumed erased.”

The Alga Marghen Reissues

Alga Marghen (Discogs ID: 9805) is an Italian experimental/avant-garde archival label with 258 releases documented on Discogs, run by Emanuele Carcano (ID: 223843). Their catalog centers on acoustic and electronic avant-garde, Fluxus, sound poetry, and improvised music — primarily documentation of European and American experimental music from the 1960s–1980s. The Milk From Cheltenham and Modern Shit reissues appear within the label’s “TES” subseries (the catalog numbers use “TES” as a component), alongside releases by artists including Hermann Nitsch, Ghédalia Tazartès, and Tom Johnson.

The decision to reissue an obscure 1983 UK DIY cassette under that imprint suggests Carcano or an intermediary had a connection to the It’s War Boys network — Xentos/Welton’s involvement as Producer on the 2005 reissue supports this being at least partly Welton-driven rather than entirely third-party.
