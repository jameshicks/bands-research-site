Green Day

Punk rock trio from Oakland/Berkeley, CA. Discogs artist ID: 251593. Active 1987–present (formed as Sweet Children in 1987, renamed Green Day c. 1989). Former alias on Discogs: Sweet Children (3).

John Kiffmeyer (stage name Al Sobrante) was the original drummer and is credited as executive producer on Kerplunk!. Tré Cool replaced him in 1990; Kiffmeyer had been a member of Lookouts with Tré before Green Day.

Overview

Green Day grew out of the 924 Gilman Street punk collective in Berkeley. Their early records on Lookout! Records placed them at the center of Bay Area pop-punk; signing to Reprise in 1993 and releasing Dookie (1994) made them the commercial face of the punk mainstream crossover alongside The Offspring. Their producer through the major-label era was Rob Cavallo, who co-produced every studio album from Dookie through ¡Uno! (2012) and Saviors (2024), with one exception (21st Century Breakdown, produced with Butch Vig).

Billie Joe Armstrong runs Adeline Records (founded late 1990s), which released Pinhead Gunpowder records and other Bay Area punk. He also co-founded the Foxboro Hot Tubs and The Network (both with Dirnt and Tré Cool) and released Foreverly (2013) with Norah Jones as a solo duo project.

Side Projects

Billie Joe Armstrong

  • Pinhead Gunpowder (Discogs ID: 297105) — punk band active 1991–present; releases on Lookout! and Adeline Records. Goodbye Ellston Avenue (1997), Shoot The Moon (1999), Compulsive Disclosure (2003), still releasing in 2022–2024 on 1-2-3-4 Go! Records.
  • Foxboro Hot Tubs (Discogs ID: 1167336) — garage-rock project with Dirnt and Tré Cool; Stop Drop And Roll!!! (2008, Jingle Town Records/Warner).
  • The Network (2) (Discogs ID: 443218) — new-wave/synthpunk project with Dirnt and Tré Cool, released anonymously; Money Money 2020 (2003, Adeline Records), Money Money 2020 Part II: We Told Ya So (2020, Joe Robot Records).
  • The Longshot (Discogs ID: 6420472) — BJA’s solo-fronted project; The Longshot EP (2018, Crush Music), Love Is For Losers (2018).
  • Billie Joe Armstrong (solo) — No Fun Mondays (2020, Reprise) — covers album; Foreverly (2013, Reprise) — album of Everly Brothers songs recorded with Norah Jones.

Mike Dirnt

  • The Frustrators (Discogs ID: 2189190) — melodic punk side project; Bored In The USA (2000), Achtung Jackass (2002), Griller (2011), all on Adeline or Dr. Strange Records.
  • Foxboro Hot Tubs — shared with BJA and Tré.
  • The Network (2) — shared with BJA and Tré.
  • Previously played with Screeching Weasel (Discogs credit).

Tré Cool

  • Lookouts (Discogs ID: 365601) — pre-Green Day punk band from Mendocino County; also featured Lawrence Livermore (Lookout! Records founder).
  • Foxboro Hot Tubs — shared.
  • The Network (2) — shared.
  • Dead Mermaids (Discogs ID: 4925042) — side project.
  • Previously played with Samiam per Discogs credits.
