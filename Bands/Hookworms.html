Hookworms

Discogs ID: 2405393 Active: 2011–2018 Origin: Leeds, UK Genres: Psychedelic Rock, Space Rock, Shoegaze, Indie Rock, Synth-pop Labels: Sun Ark Records (2011), Gringo Records (2012–2013), Weird World (2013–2014), Domino Recording Company (2018)

Hookworms were an indie/psychedelic rock band from Leeds, UK. They operated under a semi-anonymous initials-only convention for most of their career — members were credited as MJ, MB, JN, JW, SS, and EG. They disbanded on 31 October 2018 following public allegations of sexual and mental abuse against frontman and primary member MJ (Matthew Johnson (12)).

Biography

Formation and Early Recordings (2011–2012)

Hookworms formed in Leeds around 2011. Their debut release was a self-titled cassette on the US label Sun Ark Records (SA014, 2011), limited edition, featuring four tracks: “Medicine Cabinet,” “Teen Dreams,” “I Have Some Business Out West,” and “Resolution.” Recording was handled by Matthew Johnson (12) (MJ), who operated Suburban Home studio in Kirkstall, Leeds. Mastering was by Carl Saff.

The following year they released a split 7” with Leeds drone-rock duo Kogumaza, “Form And Function / Ursids” (Gringo Records, WAAT048, 2012), mastered by Tom Woodhead. This established their relationship with Gringo Records (2), a Leeds-based independent label that would release their debut album.

They also released two live cassettes in 2012: Live Vol. 1 and Live Volume 2.

Pearl Mystic and Gringo Records (2013)

Pearl Mystic (2013) was their debut LP, released on Gringo Records (WAAT051) and also pressed by Weird World (WEIRD032CD), the Domino sublabel. The album was recorded by MJ at Suburban Home and mastered by Carl Saff. The lineup for this record was:

Also in 2013: the double A-side single “Radio Tokyo / On Returning” (Too Pure), featuring artwork by Anna Peaker.

The Hum and Weird World (2014)

By The Hum (2014), the lineup had changed: EG (5) had departed, and Jonathan Nash (2) (JN) joined on guitar/drums. The album was released on Weird World (WEIRD040CD), cementing the band’s relationship with that label (a Domino sublabel). Recording and mixing by MJ; mastering by Alex Wharton. Sleeve and design by Idiot’s Pasture (Discogs ID 3497172).

The album also appeared with a Velvet Underground connection: the Discogs entry credits Lou Reed, Sterling Morrison, and John Cale in shared-release context — likely a compilation or limited promo pressing that appeared alongside their material.

Supporting the album, they released the promo single “On Leaving” (Weird World, WEIRD053CDP, 2014) and the live release Live Vol III (Weird World, 2014).

The Gap and Microshift (2015–2018)

Between The Hum (2014) and Microshift (2018), there was a four-year gap during which no new Hookworms studio material was released, though MJ continued extensive production work at Suburban Home for other Leeds and UK artists (see MJ’s Production Work below).

Microshift (2018) was released on Domino Recording Company directly (WIGLP423, not via the Weird World sublabel). It marked a shift in sound toward synth-pop alongside psychedelic rock, and introduced several outside contributors. The lineup on Microshift is:

Dissolution (October 2018)

On 31 October 2018, Hookworms announced their split following public allegations of sexual and mental abuse against MJ (Matthew Johnson). The Discogs profile notes this directly. The announcement effectively ended the band. No public response from MJ on Discogs record.

Related Projects (Post-Hookworms)

Several members formed or joined new projects after the dissolution:

  • Holodrum (Discogs ID 8905651) — features JW (Jonathan Wilkinson), JN (Jonathan Nash), MB (Matthew Benn), Sam Shipstone (SS), and Christopher Duffin (the Microshift saxophonist), plus Emily Garner and Steve Nuttall. Effectively a successor grouping of Hookworms members.
  • Remover (Discogs ID 5167143) — features MJ (Matthew Johnson), JN (Jonathan Nash), MB (Matthew Benn). MJ’s other ongoing band.
  • Family Scraps (Discogs ID 5509377) — features MJ.
  • XAM Duo (Discogs ID 5411529) — features Matthew Benn (as Xam (6)).

Jonathan Nash (2) is also involved in a wide range of Leeds bands: Cowtown, Brown Owl, Nope (2), Runners (3), The Dragon Rapide, Tigers!
