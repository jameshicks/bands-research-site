Overview

Bästard was a French experimental noise rock band from Lyon, formed in 1993 out of the breakup of Deity Guns. Active through the mid-1990s with seven releases — primarily on Semantic and then Ici D’Ailleurs — the group disbanded in 1997, leaving a catalogue spanning post rock, noise, and abstract experimentation. They reunited in 2005 for a single performance at Le Confort Moderne marking that venue’s 20th anniversary, then reconstituted under the name Zëro in 2006. Recurring collaborators across their releases include Andy Bryant, Remko Schouten, and Yann Tiersen.
