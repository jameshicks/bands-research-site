Soft Cell

Leeds-formed synth-pop duo, active 1980–1984 (reformed 2001–2003, again 2017–2024). Discogs artist ID: 12196

Marc Almond (vocals) and Dave Ball (electronics/production). Met at Leeds Polytechnic. Best known for “Tainted Love” (1981, UK #1, Some Bizzare). On Where Were You- (Independent Music From Leeds 1978-1989) with “A Man Can Get Lost” — one of their earliest recordings. All studio albums released on Some Bizzare.
