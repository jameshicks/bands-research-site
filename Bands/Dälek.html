Dälek

Discogs ID: 26511
Origin: Newark, New Jersey
Active: c.1996–present
Genres: Experimental hip-hop, noise, drone

Newark experimental hip-hop duo: MC dälek (Will Brooks) and Oktopus (Alap Momin). Two releases on Southern Records (2004, 2009). Known for dense, abrasive production layering hip-hop over noise/industrial textures. Also released on Ipecac Recordings (Mike Patton’s label). In 2004 collaborated with Faust on Derbe Respect, Alder (Klangbad / Staubgold) — a cross-genre meeting of krautrock and avant-garde hip-hop.
