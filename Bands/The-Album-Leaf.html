The Album Leaf

Solo project / live band of Jimmy LaValle. San Diego, California (later Los Angeles). Discogs ID: 94612. Active 1999–present.

Began as a one-man home recording project in 1999, influenced by German ambient/psych (Can, Cluster, Harmonia), Brian Eno, electronic music, and traditional mariachi. Evolved into a full live band by the time of A Chorus Of Storytellers (2010). Signature elements: Rhodes piano, field recordings, textured electronics, orchestral strings, and (on later records) vocals.

Era 1 — Self-Released / Small Label Debut (1999–2001)

First records released on small US indie labels. An Orchestrated Rise To Fall (1999, Music Fellowship) was a self-recorded debut. In An Off White Room (February 2001, Troubleman Unlimited) followed as an EP (4–5 tracks): “Project Loop,” “Glisten,” “Six AM,” an ~23-minute ambient track of bird sounds titled “Off White Room,” and a bonus Kraftwerk cover “Computer Love.” The EP title referred to the color of LaValle’s home studio — his living room. One Day I’ll Be On Time (2001, Tiger Style) was the first full LP to draw wider attention — Rhodes piano, field recordings, Rafter Roberts engineering on select tracks.

Era 2 — Sigur Rós Connection / Sub Pop Breakthrough (2002–2006)

Sigur Rós discovered The Album Leaf after One Day I’ll Be On Time and took LaValle on two US tours and a European tour. This exposure led to the In A Safe Place sessions in Iceland at Sigur Rós’s Sundlaugin studio. Several Sigur Rós members participated. Birgir Jón Birgisson (Biggi, Sigur Rós’s recording engineer) co-produced and mixed. Released on Sub Pop in the US, City Slang in Europe.

A 7” split with Bright Eyes (Collaboration Series No. 1, 2002, Better Looking Records) featured Conor Oberst on vocals.

Seal Beach (2003, Acuarela/Spain) and a split LP A Lifetime Or More (2003, Rykodisc UK / Arena Rock US) also appeared in this window. The split, On!Air!Library! / The Album Leaf, gives LaValle three tracks (Another Day / Essex / Lamplight); On!Air!Library! take the remaining five.

Era 3 — Sub Pop / Live Band Formation (2010)

A Chorus Of Storytellers (2010, Sub Pop) marked the arrival of a settled live lineup: Drew Andrews (guitar/vocals), Matt Resovich (violin), Tim Reece (drums), Luis Hermosillo (bass). Several tracks co-written lyrically with Pall Jenkins. Recorded at Bear Creek Studio, Seattle by Ryan Hadlock; mixed by Birgir Jón Birgisson and LaValle.

Era 4 — Self-Release / Relapse / Eastern Glow (2012–present)

After A Chorus Of Storytellers, LaValle began self-releasing material via his own Eastern Glow Recordings imprint: Forward/Return (2012), film soundtracks, and compilation tracks. Between Waves (2016, Relapse Records) was the first Album Leaf album recorded as a full-band effort from the ground up, with the settled lineup (LaValle, Brad Lee, Dave Lebleu, Matt Resovich, Tim Reece). The album was self-produced entirely by LaValle and the band.

Future Falling (2023, Eastern Glow) continued the self-release path. Lines In A Leaf (2025, Numero Group / Tree Records) is an archival release.
