Kahil El’Zabar’s Ritual Trio

Chicago free jazz trio led by Kahil El’Zabar. Active 1986–2008. Discogs artist ID: 322784. Also credited as “Ritual Trio,” “Kahil El’Zabar Ritual Trio,” “Kahil El’Zabar’s The Ritual,” and “Kahil El’Zabar’s The Ritual Trio.”

Overview

Founded in 1986, the Ritual Trio was El’Zabar’s primary small-group vehicle across more than two decades of recording. It drew its founding membership directly from the Art Ensemble Of Chicago: El’Zabar on drums, percussion, and kalimba; Lester Bowie on trumpet; and Malachi Favors on bass. That lineup recorded The Ritual (Sound Aspects, 1986) and Sacred Love (Sound Aspects, 1988), with Donald Garrett added on clarinet and percussion for the latter.

The earliest guest credit went to Billy Bang (violin) on Another Kind Of Groove (Sound Aspects, 1987), where Bang joined Favors as co-billed primary artists alongside El’Zabar. Bang would return as a featured guest in 1995 and 2005.

The group’s second major phase began with its move to Delmark Records in 1994. The core trio stabilized into El’Zabar / Favors / Ari Brown (tenor and soprano saxophone, piano) — a lineup that persisted through Renaissance Of The Resistance (1994), Big Cliff (1995), Jitterbug Junction (1997, on CIMP), Conversations (1999), and Africa N’da Blues (2000). Archie Shepp guested on Conversations (tenor saxophone, piano on track 3); Pharoah Sanders guested on Africa N’da Blues (tenor saxophone) and again on Ooh Live! (2008).

For Live At The River East Art Center (Delmark, 2005), Favors was replaced on bass by Yosef Ben Israel, with Billy Bang again as special guest on electric violin.

Magg-Zelma (I Am Digital Media, 2008) is credited as a Ritual Trio recording featuring Lester Bowie on trumpet — but given that Bowie died in 1999 and this release shares the same Discogs master as The Ritual (1986), it is almost certainly archival or previously unreleased material from the original Sound Aspects–era sessions, issued posthumously.

Junius Paul (bass) is listed in the Discogs members roster but does not appear in the verified track-level credits for any release in the discography; his Ritual Trio credit likely reflects a later touring or unrecorded configuration.

Open Questions

  • The Ritual (1986) is not in the Discogs discography for artist ID 322784 — it is credited to Lester Bowie / Kahil El’Zabar / Malachi Favors individually rather than to the Ritual Trio entity. This is a known Discogs credit inconsistency.
  • Junius Paul’s specific Ritual Trio recording dates are unconfirmed in Discogs; the connection may be live/touring only.
  • Alika Rising At Leverkusener Jazztage (1990) credits Ari Brown but the stub listed it as a Sound Aspects trio record — this is the earliest documented appearance of Ari Brown in the lineup (predating the Delmark move by four years).
  • No Discogs data on the relationship between Magg-Zelma and The Ritual beyond the shared master ID; archival provenance unconfirmed without web research.
