Exploding Star Orchestra

Large-format free-jazz and improvised-music ensemble based in Chicago, led by Rob Mazurek (cornet, electronics, composer, director). Active since approximately 2007, ESO operates as a rotating collective drawn from the Chicago creative-music scene, with membership expanding and contracting across releases. The ensemble sits at the intersection of free jazz, avant-garde improvisation, and electronics, and has released records on Thrill Jockey, Delmark Records, Rogueart, Cuneiform Records, and International Anthem Recording Company. Discogs artist ID: 721090.

Also credited as “Exploding Star Electro Acoustic Ensemble” and “Rob Mazurek - Exploding Star Orchestra” — both are name variations on the same Discogs entry, not separate pages.

Overview

Exploding Star Orchestra is Mazurek’s vehicle for large-ensemble composition that integrates his visual-art practice (paintings, lithographs, video scores) with free jazz and electronics. The core Chicago personnel — including Nicole Mitchell, Jeff Parker, John Herndon, Matthew Lux, Matt Bauder, Jeb Bishop, Jason Adasiewicz, Mike Reed (2), Damon Locks, and Jim Baker — recur across the early records, with Brazilian collaborators (Mauricio Takara, Guilherme Granado) and New York improvisers joining later releases. By the 2020 Dimensional Stardust record on International Anthem / Nonesuch, the ensemble had expanded to include Jaimie Branch, Craig Taborn, Tomeka Reid, and Angelica Sanchez.

A key collaborator on the first two Thrill Jockey albums was John McEntire, who served as both marimba / tubular bells player and producer/engineer. The 2008 record brought in free-jazz legend Bill Dixon as co-headliner. The 2013 Matter Anti-Matter LP (Rogueart) featured AACM pillar Roscoe Mitchell.

Connections

  • Rob Mazurek — founder, director, cornet; also leads Chicago Underground Trio, Chicago Underground Duo, Mandarin Movie, and many other projects
  • Bill Dixon — free-jazz trumpet legend; co-headlined 2008 Thrill Jockey LP; Dixon composed two of three pieces
  • Roscoe Mitchell — AACM pillar; co-headlined Matter Anti-Matter (2013, Rogueart)
  • Nicole Mitchell — flutist / composer; appears on most ESO records; leads Nicole Mitchell’s Black Earth Ensemble
  • Jeff Parker — guitarist; long-running ESO member; also Tortoise and solo on International Anthem Recording Company
  • John McEntire — producer and marimba player on first two LPs; also Tortoise, The Sea And Cake
  • Damon Locks — vocalist, visual artist, electronics; recurs from 2008 onward; also leads Black Monument Ensemble
  • Jason Adasiewicz — vibraphonist; early ESO core; also Mike Reed’s Loose Assembly
  • Chad Taylor — drummer; later ESO recordings; also Chicago Underground Trio
  • Jaimie Branch — trumpet; Dimensional Stardust (2020); her own fly or die series on International Anthem Recording Company
  • Craig Taborn — pianist / synthesist; Lightning Dreamers and Live At The Adler; active in Roscoe Mitchell projects
  • Tomeka Reid — cellist; Dimensional Stardust and Live At The Adler; also Artifacts (5), Mike Reed’s Loose Assembly
  • Angelica Sanchez — pianist; active in ESO since 2015; also Tri-Centric Orchestra
  • Dave Vettraino | Engineer and producer on three IARC-era ESO records; central to International Anthem Recording Company infrastructure
  • Mauricio Takara — Brazilian percussionist / cavaquinho; joins ESO from 2013; also in São Paulo Underground with Mazurek
  • International Anthem Recording Company — home label since 2020; also releases Jeff Parker, Jaimie Branch, Makaya McCraven
  • Thrill Jockey — home for first two releases, 2007–2008

Research Queue
