Deep Puddle Dynamics

Discogs ID: 55548
Active: c. 1998–2000
Label: Anticon

Anticon supergroup. Members: Dose One (Adam Drucker), Alias (3) (Brendon Whitney), Slug (Sean Daley of Atmosphere), Sole (Tim Holland). Their LP The Taste Of Rain… Why Kneel (1999, Anticon) is a foundational document of the label’s early aesthetic — collage-heavy, atmospheric, and lyrically dense. One of several projects that defined the initial Anticon identity before its artists moved toward more individual paths.

Formation

Sole (Tim Holland) and Alias (3) (Brendon Whitney) were both from Maine and had previously recorded together as The Live Poets — the group released What’s It All About in 1996 on 45 Below Records alongside Moodswing9. Sole and Alias knew Doseone and Slug only through their recorded music initially. [source: Wikipedia/Sole_(hip-hop_artist) as of 2026-08-23]

All four came together at an Aceyalone concert, where their mutual admiration led to the decision to collaborate. As Sole put it: “We all loved each-other’s music and wanted to do a project… we said, Fuck it, let’s all just come to Minneapolis.” [source: http://wernervonwallenrod.blogspot.com/2023/10/the-lost-deep-puddle-dynamics-interview.html — blog post reprinting a 1999 interview originally published by The Source]

They assembled for an intensive week-long recording session at Slug’s Minneapolis home circa 1998. The album was largely completed in approximately three days of actual recording time within that week. During the session, Slug went through significant personal upheaval — he severed ties with a close friend and attended his grandfather’s funeral; he channeled these events into verse while driving to the funeral, producing what became “June 26th, 1998,” a track the group considered the album’s culmination. [source: same 1999 interview via wernervonwallenrod.blogspot.com]

Sole described the experience as “life-changing”; all members characterized themselves as emotionally humbled by the result. Production was handled by Jel, DJ Mayonnaise, Alias (3), DJ Abilities, and Ant (of Atmosphere). [source: same 1999 interview]

The collaboration also played a role in the founding of Anticon: the project brought three of the label’s eventual co-founders (Sole, Doseone, Alias) together with Slug, and the creative momentum contributed to the collective formalizing as a label in 1998. [source: https://www.kqed.org/arts/13979349/anticon-history-doseone-jel-sole-alias-clouddead-odd-nosdam-oakland]
