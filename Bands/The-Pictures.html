The Pictures

Australian rock band from Melbourne, active 2000–2022. Frontman and primary creative force: Davey Lane (lead guitarist of You Am I). Discogs artist ID: 315887.

Overview

The Pictures formed in Melbourne around 2000 as Davey Lane’s primary vehicle outside You Am I. The band operated in Australian indie rock, releasing a string of singles and EPs through the 2000s before issuing their debut album Kicking Indifference in 2008–2009. A final single, “I Can’t Hold It Back”, appeared in 2022, more than a decade after the band’s last album-length release.

The core lineup that carried the band from 2002 onward was: Davey Lane (vocals, guitar), Brett Wolfenden (drums), and Luke Thomas (4) (bass, guitar). Earlier releases (2000–2001) featured Leigh White on bass and at times a different drum configuration, with Simon Knight co-producing.

Note on Dirty, Dirty (2012)

The 2012 Shock (2) release Dirty, Dirty (Discogs release 5835013) is a Jim Keays album, not a The Pictures release. Davey Lane and Brett Wolfenden appear as session musicians — Lane on electric guitar, acoustic guitar, stylophone, and backing vocals; Wolfenden on drums, percussion, and backing vocals. Jim Keays (1947–2014) was the frontman of Australian 1960s–70s rock act Masters Apprentices. The album’s producer and bassist was Ted Lethborg.
