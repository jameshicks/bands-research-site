Text (2)

Swedish experimental/punk collective formed in Umeå. Discogs artist ID: 1197200.

Overview

TEXT (stylised in capitals) is a side project formed by members of Refused: drummer/vocalist David Sandström, guitarist/producer Jon Brännström, and guitarist Kristofer Steen. They operate outside the hardcore framework of Refused, working in abstract electronic composition, ambient, and experimental rock with elements of spoken word and dialogue.

The band released one album, Text (2000), on Umeå imprint Demon Box Recording and Desperate Fight Records (cat# box01 / dfr25), later reissued in the US (2001) on Buddyhead (cat# BH004). A single, Sound Is Compressed; Words Rebel And Hiss, preceded the album on the same Swedish labels (2000, cat# box02 / dfr026).

Despite the brief’s framing suggesting a 2004 Burning Heart Records release and a lineup including Dennis Lyxzén and Sara Almgren, Discogs records no such release; Text (2)‘s complete catalogue is two entries from 2000/2001, and neither Lyxzén nor Sara Almgren appears in any credit. The band is a Refused spinoff centred on Sandström, Brännström, and Steen.

Note on naming: Discogs disambiguates this band as “Text (2)” to distinguish it from an unrelated artist (Text, Discogs ID 526377, a solo US project by Zach Miller). For vault purposes this page uses the Discogs-disambiguated title.
