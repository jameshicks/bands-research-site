Brainiac

Discogs artist ID: 20019. Alternative/art rock band from Dayton, Ohio. Active 1990s. Released on Touch And Go.

Overview

Dayton, Ohio band known for angular, electronics-infused rock. Produced by Eli Janney on their final studio album Hissing Prigs In Static Couture (1996, Touch And Go) — Janney credited as “Mr. EJ.” The band’s trajectory was cut short by the death of frontman Tim Taylor (5) in 1997.
