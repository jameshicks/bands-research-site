The Lo Yo Yo

UK avant-rock / post-punk collective, active 1984–1986 with reissues through 2025. Discogs artist ID: 750296. Name variation: Lo Yo Yo. A loose ensemble centred on Mick Hobbs and John Pearce (Alig), with strong links to the British experimental underground — notably Family Fodder and This Heat — and to the Swiss cassette label Calypso Now.

Overview

The Lo Yo Yo was conceptualized by John “Alig” Pearce in 1984 after his primary group Family Fodder went dormant. The band was based in London, UK. Their name derives from the Captain Beefheart song “Low Yo Yo Stuff” from the 1972 album Clear Spot. [source: https://www.last.fm/music/The+Lo+Yo+Yo/+wiki (as of 2025-05-27); https://theloyoyo.bandcamp.com/album/self-titled]

The group released their self-titled home demo cassette (recorded on an eight-track reel to reel) and a split cassette with Belgian collective Look De Bouk (Double Dog Dare, Summer ‘84), both in 1984, via the Swiss label Calypso Now and on a self-released edition. The following year they released the mini-album Extra Weapons on Floppy Discs (2), a UK independent (recorded at Cold Storage), with Charles Bullen (of This Heat) co-producing alongside the band. A live cassette documenting a December 1985 performance in Würzburg, Germany was released in 1986. The band was active for approximately two years.

The core membership — Hobbs, Alig (John Pearce), Carrie Brooks, Joey Stack, and Steve Wright (6) — overlapped heavily with Family Fodder. Musical style blended post-punk with dub and reggae elements, featuring Alig’s bass, Carrie Brooks’ post-punk drumming, Annie Hunt’s cello and vocals, and Mick Hobbs’ guitar. The Extra Weapons lineup expanded to include Annie Hunt (cello, glockenspiel), Rick Wilson (percussion), and Bob Vanderbob (saxophone, whistle). For the 1984 self-titled home demo, additional players included Felix Fiedorowicz (bassoon) and Tony (138) (cello). Joey Stack’s lyrics were notably socialist-leaning. Approximately ten additional musicians contributed across the band’s run. [source: https://theloyoyo.bandcamp.com/album/self-titled; https://continuo.wordpress.com/2012/06/11/the-lo-yo-yo-extra-weapons/]

The 2025 Concentric Circles reissue documents the original 1984 home demo tape. About half of those demo songs were later re-recorded for Extra Weapons, but the demo versions are described as radically different. The release is listed with a September 5, 2025 date (catalog: CNTR.011); Mick Hobbs died 3 January 2025, making this a posthumous release for him. Whether the reissue project was underway before his death is not confirmed by available sources. [source: https://upcomingvinyl.com/record/lo-yo-yo-the-lo-yo-yo; https://theloyoyo.bandcamp.com/album/self-titled]

After a long dormancy the catalog received renewed attention: Klanggalerie (Vienna) issued a compilation remaster of Extra Weapons and Double Dog Dare, Summer ‘84 in 2022 (remastered by Martin Bowes), and Staubgold (Berlin) reissued Extra Weapons on vinyl in 2023. Concentric Circles (Portland, US) reissued the self-titled home demo on LP in 2025.
