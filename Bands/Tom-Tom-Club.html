Tom Tom Club

Discogs ID: (to confirm)
Origin: New York City / Nassau, Bahamas
Active: 1981–present
Members: Tina Weymouth, Chris Frantz

Side project of Tina Weymouth and Chris Frantz of Talking Heads. Formed 1981 during downtime between the Remain In Light recording sessions and subsequent touring. Dance/funk/pop sensibility. Debut single “Genius Of Love” (1981) became a major dance hit and has been extensively sampled.
