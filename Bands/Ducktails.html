Ducktails

Solo project of Matthew Mondanile (Matthew A. Mondanile). Discogs artist ID: 1217375. US lo-fi / dream-pop project active from 2007; Mondanile was simultaneously a member of Real Estate (2). The project moved from home-recorded cassette releases through increasingly polished studio LPs, signing to Domino in 2013.

Overview

Ducktails began as a solo home-recording project by Matt Mondanile, a New Jersey-based guitarist best known as a member of Real Estate (2). The earliest material — issued on CDr and cassette through small labels like Future Sound Recordings and Not Not Fun Records — landed in the lo-fi/psych-adjacent cassette underground of 2007–2009, alongside contemporaries such as James Ferraro, Julian Lynch, and White Fence.

The project’s critical breakthrough came with Landscapes (2009) on Olde English Spelling Bee (OESB-40) and the self-titled LP on Not Not Fun Records, both of which placed Ducktails alongside the tape-underground acts that were beginning to attract broader attention. III: Arcade Dynamics (2011) on Woodsist marked a step toward cleaner production, with contributions from Real Estate (2) members and Jarvis Taveniere mixing.

The Domino period (2013–2015) produced two full-length LPs — The Flower Lane and St. Catherine — with full-band studio arrangements, outside producers, and guest credits from Daniel Lopatin (Oneohtrix Point Never) and Julia Holter. After St. Catherine (2015), Mondanile departed Real Estate (2) and later relocated to Greece, where the project entered a new phase under his own New Images imprint, releasing a string of records with a shifting cast of Greek musicians.

Mondanile has also worked under aliases and in other groups including Traum Ecke, Egyptian Sports Network, The Parasails, and Predator Vision.

Scene Connections

Ducktails emerged from the same cassette underground as Not Not Fun Records and Olde English Spelling Bee — a loose network of home-recordists operating in the late 2000s blog era. Key overlapping artists in this orbit include James Ferraro, Julian Lynch, LA Vampires, and Monopoly Child Star Searchers.

The Woodsist connection (shared with Real Estate (2)) placed Ducktails alongside Woods (2), Kevin Morby, Crystal Stilts, and Wavves in the lo-fi rock cluster of 2009–2011.

The move to Domino in 2013 marked an alignment with the indie mainstream: Domino also housed Real Estate (2)‘s records from this period, and the The Flower Lane credits show significant crossover between the two projects.

The post-2017 New Images phase, based partly in Greece, brought in a different set of collaborators — Greek musicians Sergios Voudris, Kostas Stergiou, Dimitris Karayiannis, and Yani Martinelli — alongside Finnish drummer Jaakko Eino Kalevi on Impressions.

Web Sources

Departure from Real Estate

On 25 May 2016, Real Estate issued a public announcement that Mondanile had left the band to “focus his creative energy on Ducktails.” The actual circumstances were not disclosed at that time. In October 2017 — over a year later — the remaining members confirmed to press that he had been fired in February 2016 following “allegations of unacceptable treatment of women” brought to their attention. According to reporting, a newly added drummer gave the founding members an ultimatum that led to Mondanile’s removal. In October 2017 Spin published accounts from seven women detailing specific allegations. Subsequent to the October 2017 reporting, Ducktails shows were cancelled and Domino dropped him.

Sources: Billboard, 2017 · Stereogum, 2017 · Spin, 2017 · The Fader, 2017 · NME, 2017

Compilation Releases

All three Discogs-flagged compilations are archival collections of previously unreleased material, issued on cassette (limited hand-risograph printed, ~100 copies each) then later on vinyl and digital:

  • Daffy Duck in Hollywood (April 2017): 15 tracks recorded 2013–2016 in Los Angeles during the St. Catherine and Jersey Devil sessions. Originally cassette; remastered 2022 vinyl edition (300 copies). Bandcamp · Discogs
  • Hummingbird Babysitter (June 2017): 15 tracks, second cassette compilation of unused 2013–2016 LA material, distributed alongside Daffy Duck in Hollywood during a May 2017 European tour. Bandcamp · Fact Mag, 2017 · Discogs
  • Hard Rock Cafe Chernobyl (September 2019): Third archival compilation; previously unreleased material from 2014–2018. Discogs vinyl pressing 2021

Greece Relocation

Mondanile began spending time in Athens in early 2018 while writing Watercolors. He also lived in Antwerp, Belgium (with his girlfriend) for part of that writing period, but made regular trips back to Athens to record at Diskex studio with engineer Sergios Voudris (of Voyage Limpid Sound). By the time of promotion for Watercolors (2019) he described Athens as his base, noting he was learning Greek and taking classical piano lessons. The Watercolors liner notes list recording locations as Ridgewood NJ, Antwerp, and Athens (March 2018–May 2019). Greek collaborators on the record: Sergios Voudris (engineer, co-mixer, vocals, bass, organ/Rhodes), Kostas Stergiou (drums), Dimitris Karayiannis (saxophone), Yani Martinelli (vocals, guitar, keyboards), mastering by Anestis Psaradakos at Athens Mastering.

Sources: Wikipedia (Ducktails), as of 2026-05-28 · Watercolors Bandcamp

Side Projects

  • Egyptian Sports Network: Duo of Mondanile and Spencer Clark (Monopoly Child Star Searchers, The Skaters, Vodka Soap). One known album: Interstitial Luxor (Pacific City Sound Visions, June 2013). Concept: interstitial television music for a fictional Egyptian sports network; described as a “zero-gravity television network transmitting from the Olympic Space Station.” Discogs · Norman Records review
  • The Parasails: Solo Mondanile side project, active c. 2009. Described at the time as a second solo limb alongside Ducktails. Village Voice, 2009
  • Predator Vision: Pre-Ducktails band from Mondanile’s time at Hampshire College in Massachusetts. Included future Real Estate (2) drummer Etienne Pierre Duguay. Described as psychedelic rock / drone / noise rock. Wikipedia (Matt Mondanile), as of 2026-05-28
  • Traum Ecke: Listed as a Mondanile project on Discogs (ID: 3289323) but no web coverage found confirming composition or style.
