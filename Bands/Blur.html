Blur

UK rock band, formed London 1988 (originally as Seymour). Discogs artist ID: 7551

Overview

Formed as Seymour by Damon Albarn (vocals), Graham Coxon (guitar), Alex James (2) (bass), and Dave Rowntree (drums). Signed to Food by Andy Ross in March 1990, renamed Blur. Became the label’s flagship act and one of the defining bands of the Britpop era. Produced six studio albums on Food, 1991–1999.
