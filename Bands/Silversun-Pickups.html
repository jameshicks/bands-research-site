Silversun Pickups

Indie rock / shoegaze band from Los Angeles, California. Discogs artist ID: 579616. Active 2000–present. Also known as “The Silversun Pickups” (name variation on some releases).

Overview

Silversun Pickups formed in Los Angeles around 2000, coalescing around the core four-piece of Brian Aubert (guitar/vocals), Nikki Monninger (bass/backing vocals), Joe Lester (keyboards/sounds), and Christopher Guanlao (drums/backing vocals). The band is named after a store near USC in Los Angeles. An early lineup (pre-2005) featured a different bassist — Kennedy (3) (Discogs ID: 416996) appears on guitar on two tracks of the Pikul EP — and Elvira Gonzalez on drums; the current lineup has been stable since approximately 2005.

Their first release was the 7” single “Kissing Families / All The Coyotes” (2002, Sea Level Records), followed by the debut EP Pikul (2005) on Dangerbird Records, where they remained through their first four full-lengths. From 2015 onward they released on New Machine Recordings (Discogs ID: 901074), effectively their own imprint. Managed throughout their peak period by Q Prime Inc.

The band occupies a distinct niche in 2000s alt-rock: densely layered guitar textures, shoegaze-adjacent dynamics, and Aubert’s high-register vocals over Monninger’s melodic bass work. Producers Dave Cooley (first two albums), Jacknife Lee (third and fourth), and Butch Vig (fifth and sixth) each shaped a distinct sonic chapter.

In 2019 Silversun Pickups and Butch Vig formed a recorded project together called SSVU (Discogs ID: 12142663; full name: “Silversun Vigups”), formalizing the creative relationship begun with Widow’s Weeds.
