Overview

Electroscope was a duo of Gayle Brogan and John Cavanagh, active 1996–2000, who set out to synthesise the approaches of Joe Meek and The United States Of America across a remarkably prolific run of 104 releases in just four years — spanning experimental, ambient, indie rock, and psychedelic territory. Their catalogue spread across a constellation of small imprints, with Wurlitzer Jukebox and Boa Records as their main homes and appearances on around a dozen others. Their closest outside working relationship was with Mount Vernon Arts Lab, with whom they share three releases. After the partnership ended, Brogan continued recording as Pefkin and Cavanagh as Phosphene (2); the two eventually reconvened as Electroscope, working with VCS3 synthesis, a 1950s radio microphone, and layered vocals.
