The Diagram Brothers

Discogs ID: 363710

Active: 1979–1982
Location: Manchester, UK
Genres: Post-Punk, New Wave, Experimental
Labels: Construct Records (2) (1980), New Hormones (1981–1982), Outatune (1982)

Manchester post-punk band, 1979–1982. Not actually brothers — they adopted “Diagram” pseudonyms, with the sole exception being Andy Diagram who used his real surname. Their music sits in the skewed end of British post-punk: angular guitar, deadpan vocals, sardonic/absurdist lyrics. Closely associated with the New Hormones label orbit.

Biography

Formation (1979–1980)

Formed in Manchester c. 1979. The founding lineup drew from the same loose scene as New Hormones and Dislocation Dance. Their debut single, We Are All Animals (Construct Records, 1980), was self-produced on one side (B2, “I Would Like To Live In Prison”) and engineered by John Brierley on the other two tracks, with Nigel Holland co-producing those. The single was released on Construct Records (2) (catalog CON1) rather than New Hormones, though the band was already part of the New Hormones social world.

New Hormones Period (1981–1982)

The band released three records on New Hormones in quick succession:

  1. Bricks / Postal Bargains (ORG 9, 1981) — 7” single, produced by Stuart James, Mike Hinc, and the band; engineered by John Brierley; sleeve design by Richard Boon.

  2. Some Marvels Of Modern Science (ORG 17, 1981) — LP, 14 tracks. Produced by Colin Richardson, Peter Wright (2), Richard Boon, and the band. Eric Random (credited as Eric Random Diagram) contributed ambient noise to the track “Litter”. Artwork by Maggie Diagram; logo design by Bryn Jones (5).

  3. Discordo 45 (ORG 21, 1982) — 10” EP, 4 tracks. No producer or engineer listed in Discogs. Tracks: “Discordo” / “My Dinner” / “Fondue Soiree” / “Cherry Blossom”.

German EP and Dissolution (1982)

The band’s final release was the German E.P. (Outatune, Out 8207, 1982), released in Germany. It included a German-language version of “I Didn’t Get Where I Am Today By Being A Right Git” (from the LP), plus “Discordo” and “Bricks”. The band dissolved shortly after, in 1982.

Peel Sessions

The BBC Peel Sessions — recorded during the active period — were eventually compiled and released on CD by LTM Recordings (LTMCD 2558, 2011) as The Peel Sessions. The 13-track set covers most of the catalog, including some tracks not on the original studio releases (“Hey Dad!”, “Tracey”, “The Expert”, “You’ll Never Walk Alone”, “You’ve Got To Pick A Pocket Or Two”).

Connections

  • Andy Diagram → James: After the Diagram Brothers dissolved, Andy Diagram joined James as trumpeter, appearing on Gold Mother (1990), Seven (1992), and subsequent albums; he remains in the band’s current lineup. He also co-founded Spaceheads with Richard Harrison.
  • Andy Diagram → Dislocation Dance: Andy Diagram was simultaneously in Dislocation Dance (1980–83) and The Diagram Brothers; his trumpet appears on Dislocation Dance releases alongside the New Hormones credits.
  • Diagram Brothers → Eric Random And The Bedlamites (ORG 18): Fraser Reich (shakuhachi) and Lawrence Fitzgerald (violin) played on Eric Random’s ORG 18 release (1982), which also featured Stephen Mallinder and Richard H. Kirk of Cabaret Voltaire.
  • Bryn Jones (5) connection: The Bryn Jones (5) who designed the LP logo is listed with Discogs ID 2400693 — a different entity from Muslimgauze (Discogs ID 484, real name Bryn Jones, b. 1961, Salford). The Muslimgauze entry does not reference any early design work, and the ORG 17 credit is under a distinct Discogs artist ID. The connection is coincidence of name rather than confirmed identity.
  • New Hormones extended family: All five Diagram members circulated within the tight New Hormones social world. Fraser Reich also contributed artwork to Eric Random’s Dow Chemical Company (ORG 11, 1981).
  • Construct Records (2): The debut single appeared on a separate small Manchester label. No further releases are associated with Construct Records (2) on Discogs beyond this single.
  • LTM reissues: LTM Recordings released the Peel Sessions CD in 2011 and had previously handled reissues of the New Hormones catalog.
