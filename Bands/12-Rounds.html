12 Rounds

UK electronic rock group. Discogs artist ID: 170295. Active c.1994–1998 (on record); never officially disbanded. Originally formed under the name 12 Rounds With Jesus (appeared on NME promotional cassette Past Forward).

Notes

  • Signed to Polydor mid-1990s; Kirk Hellie departed before the US period
  • Dropped by Polydor; recorded third album in basement, 1997
  • Trent Reznor heard recordings and signed them to Nothing Records
  • Third album (with Trent Reznor in-studio) recorded but never released due to collapse of Nothing Records
  • Atticus Ross and Claudia Sarne married; band went on indefinite hiatus but never disbanded
  • Kirk Hellie returned to the circle: credited on Broken City score (2014) and Shōgun (2024) alongside Atticus/Leopold/Sarne
  • Barry Adamson played bass on the Pleasant Smell Nothing Records single — linking the 12 Rounds and Adamson/Mute circles

Connections
