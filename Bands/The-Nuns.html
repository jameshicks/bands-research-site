Oct 22, 20262 min read

The Nuns

Discogs ID: 244089

San Francisco punk band active from 1977; one of the city’s earliest punk groups.

Played at the Sex Pistols’ final concert at Winterland (January 1978) and released a single on 415 Records in 1978 before their self-titled LP on Posh Boy/BOMP! in 1980. Members included Alejandro Escovedo (guitar), Jeff Olener (vocals), Jennifer Anderson (keyboards), Richie Detrick (bass), and Kevin Foley (drums).
