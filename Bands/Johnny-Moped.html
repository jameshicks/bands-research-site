Johnny Moped

UK punk band. Discogs artist ID: 295132

Overview

British punk group active c. 1976–1979, associated with the Chiswick and Vaultage 78 label. Known for a raw, chaotic punk sound. “Incendiary Device” (sleeve design by Barney Bubbles) appeared on Punk 45 Vol. 2 - There Is No Such Thing As Society (Soul Jazz, 2013). “No One” appeared on Action Time Vision (A Story Of Independent UK Punk 1976-1979) (Cherry Red, 2016).
