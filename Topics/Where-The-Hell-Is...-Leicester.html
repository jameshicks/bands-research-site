Format: LP, Compilation

Year: 1981
Country: UK
Discogs ID: 1635367
Genres: Electronic, Rock
Styles: Minimal, New Wave, Post-Punk, Punk

Overview

Twelve-band compilation LP documenting the Leicester post-punk underground, issued by The S+T Label — co-founded by Chris Freer, Paul Betts, Kev Reverb (Kevin Bayliss), and Mick Dawkeye. The only release with the STAB catalog prefix (vs. the ST-numbered singles). No engineer, producer, or sleeve credits appear in Discogs for the LP itself.
