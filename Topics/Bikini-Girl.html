Bikini Girl (Magazine)

Type: Art / music fanzine Based: Lower East Side, New York City Published: 1978–1990 Editor/Publisher: Lisa B. Falour

Connection to Dining Out Records

Occult Chemistry’s track “Water” appeared on a flexi disc inserted into an issue of Bikini Girl in 1980, pressed in New York. This is the first concrete evidence of the London Dining Out scene’s direct reach into the New York underground art/music world — and may reflect Martha Tilson’s own New York connections, given that she was likely American.

Notes

Bikini Girl was a key publication of the Lower East Side art/music underground, running for over a decade and covering the post-punk, no-wave, and avant-garde scenes. Running from 1978 to 1990 gives it a long span across punk, no wave, post-punk, and early indie.

Lisa B. Falour — Background and Scene Connections

Lisa B. Falour published under multiple names: Lisa Baumgardner (her byline on the zine) and Deena Schwartzbaum. She died in January 2015, aged 57. A 2023 retrospective, “De-Constructing Lisa,” was curated by Susan Hannaford, Kai Eric, and John Mozzer, featuring the Bikini Girl fanzines, personal artifacts, letters, and films. [source: tarotworldtour.wordpress.com/2015/03/05/remembering-lisa-falour/ as of 2026-06-26]

Club 57 (Irving Plaza basement, NYC): Falour was described as “a major figure of Club 57” — the East Village underground venue/arts collective that overlapped with the early-1980s no wave and post-punk scenes. A commenter at the memorial post notes: “To me, Club 57 was Lisa Baumgardner.” Her bondage art film, made as a Hunter College graduate project (filmed at School of Visual Arts), was screened at Club 57 open film nights. [source: tarotworldtour.wordpress.com/2015/03/05/remembering-lisa-falour/ as of 2026-06-26]

Mudd Club: The zine documented the Mudd Club scene with photographs — including an image of David Byrne (Talking Heads) in conversation with Klaus Nomi — and at least one issue covered Club Mudd directly. [source: Etsy listing description for Bikini Girl Nos. 2–3–4, etsy.com/listing/1623860074, retrieved 2026-06-26]

Music coverage documented in the zine:

  • Review of No New York (the Brian Eno-produced No Wave compilation, 1978) appeared in at least one issue [source: Etsy listing description, retrieved 2026-06-26]
  • Interview with David Byrne (Talking Heads) [source: Etsy listing description, retrieved 2026-06-26]
  • A Burroughs reading review appeared in Vol. 1 Issue 4 (possibly Nova Convention-related) [source: realitystudio.org/bibliographic-bunker/william-burroughs-and-bikini-girl/ as of 2026-06-26]
  • Interviews with Gerard Malanga (Velvet Underground associate / Warhol Factory) and literary figures including Lynne Tillman [source: multiple web sources, retrieved 2026-06-26]

Factory/Warhol circle: Factory superstar Ondine presented a film at a screening hosted by Falour under the Bikini Girl aegis, placing her in the Warhol-adjacent downtown orbit. [source: search result aggregate, retrieved 2026-06-26]

Note on source quality: Most detail above comes from secondhand sale listings (Etsy) and a memorial blog. No direct liner-note or archived interview source was found confirming specific band affiliations beyond the above. The No New York review and David Byrne interview are sourced only from a commercial listing description — treat as plausible but unverified until an actual issue can be examined.
