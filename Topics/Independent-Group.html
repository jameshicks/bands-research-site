The Independent Group

Active: Winter 1952–53 and 1953–54 Based at: ICA (Institute of Contemporary Arts), London Significance: Precursor to British Pop Art

What It Was

The Independent Group (IG) was a loose collective of artists, architects, and critics who met informally at the ICA in two sessions. Not a manifesto-driven movement but a series of debates — they challenged the modernist establishment’s disdain for popular culture by treating American movies, science fiction, advertising, car design, and pop music with the same intellectual seriousness previously reserved for high art.

Their core intervention: mass culture as legitimate aesthetic material.

This Is TomorrowWhitechapel Gallery, August 1956Watershed exhibition; Turnbull showed Sun Gazer

This Is Tomorrow directly seeded British Pop Art of the early 1960s (David Hockney, Peter Blake, Allen Jones, etc.).

Eduardo Paolozzi’s 1947 collage I Was a Rich Man’s Plaything — made before the IG existed — contained the word “pop” in a speech bubble and is now held by Tate as “the initial standard bearer of Pop Art.”

The ICA Thread: From Pop Art to Industrial Music

The ICA is the institutional thread connecting the Independent Group to post-punk:

YearEventSignificance
1946ICA founded (Roland Penrose, Peter Watson, Herbert Read)Explicit mission: space for artists/writers/scientists outside the Royal Academy
1952–54Independent Group meetingsConceptual root of British Pop Art
1956This Is TomorrowPublic flowering of IG ideas
1968ICA moves to Nash House, The MallStill its current location
1976COUM Transmissions: Prostitution exhibitionCosey Fanni Tutti’s pornographic magazine images; House of Commons debate; Tory MP called them “wreckers of Western civilization.” Immediately afterwards, Genesis P-Orridge, Cosey Fanni Tutti, and Peter Christopherson formed Throbbing Gristle — founding act of industrial music.
1977–90ICA active post-punk programmingThe Fall, experimental acts, performance art

The through-line: The ICA nurtured anti-establishment, high-culture-meets-mass-culture provocation with the IG in the early 1950s → the same building hosted the birth of industrial music in 1976 → the same milieu that Alex and Johnny Turnbull grew up adjacent to, their father being a core IG member. When TG then produced 23 Skidoo’s debut album, the loop was explicitly closed.
