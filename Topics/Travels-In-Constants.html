Travels In Constants

Long-running single-artist EP/mini-album subscription series on Temporary Residence Limited, running from 1999 to at least 2015. Each volume is credited to one artist and released under the unified catalog number TRR 13 (later individual catalog numbers from Vol. 24 onward). All volumes are limited-edition CDEPs or mini-albums. Matrix runouts use the code TICxx to encode the volume number, which resolves several volumes whose titles do not include a volume number.

No compilation in the traditional sense — each volume is a discrete solo release. The series spans post-rock, folk, ambient, experimental, drone, electronic, and neo-classical.

Label ID (Discogs): 9952. Series cat# spine: TRR 13 (Vols. 1–23), TIC24 (Vol. 24), no cat# (Vol. 25 digital).

There are no Discogs entries currently documenting Vols. 6, 8, and 9 under “Travels In Constants” titles — they appear only by matrix code.

Contributing Artists

Artists appear in volume order. Existing vault pages linked; stubs noted.

  • By Water — Vol. 1 (1999). One-track 17-minute piece “Staring Running Water”. Discogs artist ID: 1125214.
  • Bonnevill — Vol. 2 (1999). 6-track EP. Members include Jessica Billey (violin, artwork) and Mick Turner (guitar). Discogs artist ID: 352351.
  • Tarentel — Vol. 3 (1999). 2-track ambient/experimental. Recorded by Scott Solter. Discogs artist ID: 64725.
  • Drona Parva — Vol. 4 (1999). 3-track drone/ambient suite. Performed by Nemo Bidstrup. Discogs artist ID: 378572.
  • David Pajo (as Papa M) — Vol. 5 (1999). Single untitled track, 13:43. Features Will Oldham (lyrics). Discogs Papa M ID: 30391.
  • Sonna — Vol. 6 (2000). Released as The Eventual Bow; identified by matrix code TIC6. Members: Chris Mackie, Drew Nelson, Jeremy DeVine, Jim Redd, Trevor HollAnd. Discogs artist ID: 77636.
  • Bonnie “Prince” Billy — Vol. 7 (2000). Released as More Revery. Covers of Bill Withers, John Holt, John Phillips, PJ Harvey, The Renderers, Tim McGraw. Recorded by The Rove Unit; engineering by Paul Oldham. Discogs artist ID: 66089.
  • Paul Newman (2) — Vol. 8 (2000). Self-titled, three untitled tracks. Matrix: TIC008. Discogs artist ID: 267684.
  • Low — Vol. 9 (2000). Released as The Exit Papers (A Soundtrack By Low). Six untitled tracks. Mastered by Tom Herbers. With string players Alicia Mattsen (viola), Heidi Olson, Rebecca Olson (violins). Discogs artist ID: 10824.
  • Cerberus Shoal — Vol. 10 (2001). Two tracks; remixes by Caleb Mulkerin and Tom Rogers. Mastered by Steve Drown. Discogs artist ID: 327051.
  • Rumah Sakit — Vol. 11 (2001). 4-track EP. Recorded by Robert Weston (Bob Weston). Performers: Jeff Shannon, John Baez, Kenseth Thibideau, Mitch Cheney. Discogs artist ID: 132030.
  • Mogwai — Vol. 12 (2001). 3-track EP including a Papa M cover (“Arundel”). Compiled by John Disco. Recorded by Kevin Lynch and Michael Brennan Jr. Discogs artist ID: 2813.
  • Appendix Out — Vol. 13 (2001). 5-track EP with spoken word (reading from The Golden Bough by Sir James Frazer). Features bagpipes by Donald WG Lindsay, extra singing by Gareth Eggie. Design by Alasdair Roberts. Discogs artist ID: 105126.
  • Songs: Ohia — Vol. 14 (2001). Single untitled 17:53 piece. This recording also tied to the Songs: Ohia master that spawned later Temporary Residence reissues (master 1405402). Discogs artist ID: 337276.
  • Sybarite — Vol. 15 (2001). 7-track EP titled + ep. Project of Xian Hawkins; includes Lilienthal remix. Mixed/recorded by Jacques Cohen. Discogs artist ID: 2261.
  • At Right Angles — Vol. 16 (2002). 5-track EP. Written by Nathan Salsburg (tracks 1, 3–5) and Bert Lyons/Paul Rauen (track 2). Discogs artist ID: 334715.
  • Kilowatthours — Vol. 17 (2004). 5-track EP, all untitled. Discogs artist ID: 190406.
  • Colophon (2) — Vol. 18 (2004). Love Loops. 13-track album. Music by Jefre Cantu-Ledesma. Discogs artist ID: 308933.
  • The Drift (2) — Vol. 19 (2005). Know Certain Future. Members: Trevor Montgomery (bass/sampler), Rich Douthit (drums/bells), Danny Grody (guitar/Wurlitzer), Jeff Jacobs (trumpet/flugelhorn). Discogs artist ID: 399480.
  • Eluvium — Vol. 20 (2005). Single 33-minute track “Behind Your Trouble”. Personnel: Eric Macey and Matthew Cooper. Discogs artist ID: 138187.
  • Explosions In The Sky — Vol. 21 (2005). The Rescue. 8-track EP structured as “Day One” through “Day Eight”. Discogs artist ID: 190408.
  • Mono (7) — Vol. 22 (2007). The Phoenix Tree. 4-track EP. Recorded/mixed by Steve Albini. String arrangements by Takaakira Goto and Susan Voelz. Mastered by John Golden. Discogs artist ID: 208568.
  • Christopher Flores — Vol. 23 (2007). Testing The Blues. 7-track electronic/glitch album. Discogs artist ID: 249235.
  • Adam Wiltzie — Vol. 24 (2015). 4 parts of “The Endless Battle Of The Maudlin Ballade” plus “Last Tango In Dendermonde”. Budapest Art Orchestra conducted by Pejtsik Péter. Mixed/mastered by Francesco Donadello. Cat# TIC24. Discogs artist ID: 93848.
  • Prefuse 73 — Vol. 25 (2015). Digital-only EP (10 MP3 tracks). Features Sam Dew on “Infrared”. Discogs artist ID: 3722.

Series Notes

  • All physical volumes pressed at Cinram (Canada) through at least Vol. 23. Matrix code format: TICxx followed by Cinram job code.
  • Vol. 12 (Mogwai) includes a cover of a Papa M song (“Arundel”), creating a cross-volume reference.
  • Vol. 18 (Colophon) is the only volume released as a full-length album rather than an EP or maxi-single.
  • Vol. 19 (The Drift) is described as a “Mini-Album”.
  • Vol. 23 (Christopher Flores) is also released as a full album.
  • Vol. 24 (Adam Wiltzie) has a separate self-release edition (Discogs ID: 7157545) as well as TRL and European pressings.
  • Vol. 25 (Prefuse 73) is digital-only; no physical pressing documented in Discogs.
  • The series appears to have gone dormant after 2015; no Vol. 26 or later documented on Discogs.

Connections

  • Temporary Residence Limited — the parent label. Jeremy DeVine founded the label and the series simultaneously.
  • Songs: Ohia — Vol. 14 release is connected to a later LP reissue on TRL (master 1405402, issued 2018 as Travels In Constants LP, cat# TRR246 LP).
  • Eluvium — also has full album releases on TRL beyond the TIC series.
  • Explosions In The Sky — The Rescue (Vol. 21) is a standalone soundtrack EP; companion to their main TRL output.
  • Mono (7) — The Phoenix Tree (Vol. 22) is a significant release featuring the Budapest string players that MONO worked with on orchestral recordings.

Open Questions

  • Vols. 6, 8, and 9 are confirmed by matrix codes but not titled as TIC volumes on their covers or in their Discogs entries. It is unclear whether they were officially marketed as part of the series or simply share the manufacturing code.
  • No documentation in Discogs for any volumes beyond 25. Whether the series officially ended or simply stopped being documented is unclear.
  • Vol. 6 (Sonna, The Eventual Bow) is listed with “none” catno in some DB entries — unclear whether it carried TRR 13 on the sleeve.
  • The series concept (subscription model? open series?) is not documented in Discogs metadata — requires web sources to confirm.
  • Songs: Ohia’s 2018 LP Travels In Constants (TRR246 LP) — the relationship between this LP and Vol. 14 (same master ID 1405402) needs clarification: is the LP a reissue of Vol. 14, or does it contain different material?
